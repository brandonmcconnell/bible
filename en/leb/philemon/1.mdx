## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, to Philemon, our dear friend and fellow worker,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> and to Apphia our sister, and to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church in your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Thanksgiving and Prayer for Philemon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I thank my God, always making mention of you in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> because I hear about your love and faith which you have toward the Lord Jesus and for all the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> I pray that the fellowship of your faith may become effective in the knowledge of every good thing that is in us for Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> For I have great joy and encouragement from your love, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, brother.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Appeal for Onesimus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> Therefore, although I have great confidence in Christ to order you to do what is proper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> instead I appeal to you because of love, since I am such a one as Paul, now an old man and also a prisoner of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I am appealing to you concerning my child whom I became the father of during my imprisonment, Onesimus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> Once he was useless to you, but now he is useful to you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “both to you”">a</a></sup>and to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> whom I have sent back to you himself, that is, my heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> whom I wanted to keep with me, in order that he might serve me on behalf of you during my imprisonment for the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> But apart from your consent, I wanted to do nothing, in order that your good deed might be not as according to necessity, but according to your own free will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> For perhaps because of this, he was separated from you for a time, in order that you might have him back forever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> no longer as a slave, but more than a slave, a beloved brother, especially to me, but how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Request to Philemon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> If therefore you consider me a partner, receive him as you would me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> But if in anything he has caused you loss or owes you anything, ⸤charge this to my account⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “this to me charge to my account”">b</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I, Paul, write this with my own hand: I will pay it back, lest I mention to you that ⸤you owe me even your very self besides⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “even yourself to me you owe besides”">c</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yes, brother, I ought to have some benefit of you in the Lord; refresh my heart in Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> Confident of your obedience, I am writing to you because I know that you will do even beyond what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> At the same time also, prepare a guest room for me, for I hope that through your prayers I will be restored to you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings and Benediction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, greets you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> and so do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>
