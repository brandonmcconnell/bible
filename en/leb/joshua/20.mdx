## Cities of Refuge Are Established

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 7:10; Joshua 13:1–7; Joshua 6:2"> And Yahweh spoke to Joshua, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:2–13; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Numbers 35:11–24"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>saying, ‘Appoint for yourselves cities of refuge, of which I spoke to you through the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Anyone who kills a person by accident or unintentionally<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “by not knowing”">b</a></sup>may flee there; they will be for yourselves a refuge from the avenger of blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:7; Ruth 4:1–2; Hebrews 6:18"> The killer will flee to one of these cities, stand at the entrance of the gate of the city, and ⸤state his case to the elders of that city⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “he will speak his words in the ears of the elders of that city”">c</a></sup>and they will take him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “they will gather him”">d</a></sup>into the city and give him a place, and he will dwell among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 35:12; Numbers 35:25"> And if the avenger of blood pursues after him, they will not hand over the killer into his hand, because he killed his neighbor unintentionally, and ⸤he did not hate him previously⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “he did not hate him since yesterday and the day before that”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:12; Hebrews 9:26; Numbers 35:24–25"> The killer will stay in that city until he stands before the congregation for the trial, until the death of the one who is the high priest in those days. Then ⸤the killer will return⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the killer will return and go”">f</a></sup>to his city and to his house, to the city from which he fled.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:11; 1 Chronicles 6:76; Luke 1:39"> So ⸤they set apart⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “consecrated”">g</a></sup>Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali, Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and Kiriath Arba<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “the city of Arba”">h</a></sup>(that is, Hebron) in the hill country of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:78; Joshua 21:36; Joshua 21:27"> Beyond the Jordan east of Jericho, they appointed Bezer in the wilderness on the plateau, from the tribe of Reuben, Ramoth in Gilead, from the tribe of Gad, and Golan in the Bashan, from the tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 35:15; Joshua 20:4; Joshua 20:6"> These were the cities designated for all the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>and for the foreigners<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “foreigner”">j</a></sup>dwelling among them, for anyone that kills a person unintentionally to flee there, and not die by the hand of the avenger of blood, ⸤until there is a trial⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “until he stands”">k</a></sup>before the congregation.</span>
</div>
