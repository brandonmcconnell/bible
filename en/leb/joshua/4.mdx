## The Israelites Make a Memorial

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:2; Joshua 3:17"> After all the nation finished crossing the Jordan, Yahweh said to Joshua,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 3:12; Numbers 1:4–15; 1 Kings 18:31"> “Take twelve men from the people, ⸤one man from each tribe⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “man one man one from tribe”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 4:19–20; Joshua 3:13; Joshua 4:8"> and command them, saying, ‘Take for yourselves twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan where the priests’ feet stood firmly, and bring them over with you, and set them up in ⸤the place where you will camp tonight⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the lodging place where you will lodge tonight”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 4:2; Mark 3:14–19"> So Joshua summoned the twelve men whom he had appointed from the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>one from each tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Joshua said to them, “Cross over before<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “the presence”">d</a></sup>the ark of Yahweh your God to the middle of the Jordan, and each one of you lift up a stone on your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">e</a></sup>shoulder, according to the number of the tribes of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Joshua 4:21; Psalm 44:1"> so that this may be a reminder<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “sign”">g</a></sup>among you. When your children ask in the future, saying, ‘⸤What do these stones mean to you⸥?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “What are these stones for you?”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 16:40; Exodus 28:12"> you will say to them that the waters of the Jordan were cut off ⸤from before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “the presence of”">i</a></sup>the ark of the covenant of Yahweh. When it crossed the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan were cut off. These stones will be as a memorial for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>for eternity.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 4:2–5; Joshua 1:16–18; Joshua 4:20"> Thus the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>did as Joshua commanded. They took twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan as Yahweh told Joshua, according to the number of the tribes of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup>and they carried them over with them to ⸤the camp⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the place of overnight lodging”">m</a></sup>and they put them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:12; Judges 1:26; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> Then Joshua set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan, in the place where the feet of the priests carrying the ark of the covenant stood, and they are there to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 3:13; Psalm 119:60; Proverbs 27:1"> The priests carrying the ark remained standing in the middle of the Jordan until everything that Yahweh commanded Joshua to tell the people was finished, according to all that Moses commanded Joshua. And the people hastily crossed over.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 3:8; Joshua 4:18; Joshua 3:17"> And it happened, when all the people had finished crossing, the ark of Yahweh and the priests crossed over in front of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “before the presence”">n</a></sup>the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–32; Joshua 1:14"> The children of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh crossed over armed before the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>as Moses told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; Jeremiah 39:5; Jeremiah 52:8"> About forty thousand armed for fighting crossed over before the presence of Yahweh to the plains of Jericho for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; 2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Kings 3:28"> On that day Yahweh exalted Joshua ⸤in the sight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “in the eyes”">p</a></sup>of all Israel, and they respected him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “feared him”">q</a></sup>as they respected<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “feared”">r</a></sup>Moses all the days of his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then Yahweh said to Joshua, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Exodus 25:16–22; Joshua 3:3–6"> “Command the priests carrying the ark of the testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="That is, the ark of the covenant">s</a></sup>⸤to come up⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “and let them come up”">t</a></sup>from the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 8:16–18; Acts 16:35–39; Acts 16:23"> So Joshua commanded the priests, saying, “Come up from the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; 1 Chronicles 12:15; Joshua 3:13"> And it happened that when the priests carrying the ark came up from the middle of the Jordan, and the soles of the priests’ feet ⸤touched dry land⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “were raised from the ground to dry land”">u</a></sup>the waters of the Jordan returned to their place and flowed over all its banks as before.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:9; Micah 6:5; Exodus 12:2–3"> And the people came up from the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month, and they camped in Gilgal on the eastern edge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="That is, border">v</a></sup>of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:3; Joshua 4:8"> And those twelve stones that they took from the Jordan, Joshua set up in Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; Psalm 145:4–7; Psalm 105:2–5"> And he said to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">w</a></sup>“When your children ask in the future ⸤their parents⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “their fathers”">x</a></sup>‘⸤What is the meaning of these stones⸥?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “What are these stones?”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Isaiah 51:10; Isaiah 44:27"> you will let your children know by saying, ‘Israel crossed this Jordan on dry ground.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Isaiah 43:16; Isaiah 63:12–14"> For Yahweh your God dried up the waters of the Jordan before you, until you had crossed, just as Yahweh your God did to the ⸤Red Sea⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sea of reeds”">z</a></sup>which he dried up before us until we had crossed over,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 106:8; Psalm 89:13; 1 Kings 8:42–43"> so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the hand of Yahweh is strong, so that you may fear Yahweh your God ⸤forever⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “all the days”">aa</a></sup></span>
</div>
