## The Last of the Land Is Divided

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; 1 Samuel 1:3; Joshua 21:2"> The entire congregation of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>assembled at Shiloh, and they set up there the tent of meeting, and the land was subdued before them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “in their presence”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And seven tribes remained among the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>who had not been apportioned their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 18:9; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 10:4"> And Joshua said to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>“⸤How long⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “Until when”">e</a></sup>will you be slack about going to take possession of the land that Yahweh, the God of your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “fathers”">f</a></sup>has given you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 18:6; Joshua 4:2; Joshua 18:3"> Provide three men ⸤from each tribe⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “according to each tribe”">g</a></sup>and I will send them so that they may begin to go through the land and write a description of it ⸤according to their inheritance⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “according to the mouth of their inheritance”">h</a></sup>and let them come to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 16:1–10; Joshua 19:1–9; Joshua 15:1–12"> They will divide it among themselves into seven portions; ⸤Judah will maintain its border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “Judah will stand on its border”">i</a></sup>from the south, and ⸤the house of Joseph will maintain its border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the house of Joseph will stand on its border”">j</a></sup>from the north.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 13:33; Joshua 13:8–31; Numbers 18:20"> The Levites among you have no portion, for their inheritance is the priesthood of Yahweh; Gad, Reuben, and the half-tribe of Manasseh received their inheritance beyond the Jordan to the east, which Moses Yahweh’s servant gave to them.”</span>
</div>

## The Allotment of Benjamin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 16:1–10; Joshua 15:1–8; Deuteronomy 10:1–22"> And the allotment of the tribe of Benjamin came up according to their families, and the border of their allotment fell<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “went out”">q</a></sup>between the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “sons”">r</a></sup>of Judah and the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “sons”">s</a></sup>of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 16:1; Joshua 7:2; Joshua 2:1"> Their northern border began at the Jordan and went up to the slope of Jericho on the north and continued into the hill country to the west; ⸤it ends⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “the goings out of it were”">t</a></sup>at the wilderness of Beth Aven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Joshua 16:2–3; Joshua 10:11"> The border passes on from there to Luz, to the slope of Luz to the south (that is, Bethel); then the border goes down to Ataroth Addar to the mountain that is south of Lower Beth-Horon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:9; 1 Chronicles 13:5–6; 1 Samuel 7:1–2"> Then the border changes direction and turns to the western side southward, from the mountain that ⸤is opposite⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “against the face of”">u</a></sup>Beth-Horon to the south. ⸤It ends⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the goings out of it were”">v</a></sup>at Kiriath Baal (that is, Kiriath Jearim), a town belonging to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “sons”">w</a></sup>of Judah. This is the western side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:9"> The southern side begins on the outskirts of Kiriath Jearim, and the border continues to the west to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 23:10; Joshua 15:7–8; Isaiah 30:33"> the border goes down to the foot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “to the edge”">x</a></sup>of the mountain, which is opposite the Valley of Ben Hinnom,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “valley of the son of-Hinnom”">y</a></sup>which is in the valley of Rephaim to the north; then it goes down the valley of Hinnom to the slope of the Jebusites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">z</a></sup>to the south, and then it goes down to En Rogel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 15:6"> It changes direction from the north, and it continues to En Shemesh; it goes out to Geliloth, which is opposite the ascent of Adummim, and it goes down to the stone of Bohan, son of Reuben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 15:6; Joshua 15:61"> It passes on to the slope opposite the Arabah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="A dry region that runs south of the Sea of Galilee along the Jordan Valley">aa</a></sup>to the north, and it goes down to the Arabah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="A dry region that runs south of the Sea of Galilee along the Jordan Valley">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Joshua 15:2; Numbers 34:3"> The border passes on to the slope of Beth-hoglah to the north and ⸤ends⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “the goings out of it were”">ac</a></sup>at the north bay of the Salt Sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="That is, the Dead Sea">ad</a></sup>at the south end of the Jordan. This is the southern border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Jordan forms its border on the eastern side. This is the inheritance of the tribe of Benjamin, its borders that surrounds them, according to their families.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 6:1; Joshua 18:19"> Now the towns of the tribes of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “sons”">ae</a></sup>of Benjamin, according to their families, were Jericho, Beth-hoglah, Emek Keziz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 15:6; 1 Kings 12:29–32; Joshua 18:18"> Beth Arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:17"> Avvim, Parah, Ophrah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 2:26; Joshua 21:17; Isaiah 10:29"> Kephar Ammoni, Ophni, and Geba; twelve cities and their villages.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 9:17; 1 Kings 3:4–5; Joshua 10:2"> Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 10:17; Ezra 2:25; Joshua 9:17"> Mizpeh, Kephirah, Mozah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:14; Joshua 15:8; Judges 20:4–5"> Zela, Haeleph, Jebus (that is, Jerusalem), Gibeah, and Kiriath; fourteen cities and their villages. This is the inheritance of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “sons”">af</a></sup>of Benjamin according to their families.</span>
</div>
