## The Allotment of Ephraim and Manasseh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 8:15; 2 Kings 2:19–21"> The allotment of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “sons”">a</a></sup>of Joseph went from the Jordan by Jericho, at the waters of Jericho to the east, into the wilderness, going up from Jericho into the hill country to Bethel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:13; Genesis 28:19; 2 Samuel 16:16"> it continues from Bethel to Luz, and it passes along to the territory of the Arkites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “Arkite”">b</a></sup>at Ataroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 10:33; 2 Chronicles 8:5; Joshua 18:13"> Then it goes down, to the west, to the territory of the Japhletites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “Japhletite”">c</a></sup>up to the territory of Lower Beth-horon, then to Gezer, and ⸤it ends⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the goings out of it were”">d</a></sup>at the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 17:14"> And the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “sons”">e</a></sup>of Joseph, Manasseh and Ephraim, received their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 18:13; Joshua 16:2"> This was the border of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “sons”">f</a></sup>of Ephraim according to their families: the border of their inheritance to the east was Ataroth Addar, up to Upper Beth-horon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 17:7; Joshua 18:1"> The border continues to the sea; from Micmethath to the north, the border turns to the east to Taanath Shiloh, and it passes along it from the east to Janoah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:28; Joshua 6:26; Joshua 3:16"> Then it goes down from Janoah to Ataroth and to Naarah; it touches Jericho and ends at the Jordan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 17:8–9; Joshua 16:3–6; Joshua 12:17"> from Tappuah the border goes to the west, to the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">g</a></sup>of Kanah, and ⸤it ends⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the goings out of it were”">h</a></sup>at the sea. This is the inheritance of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “sons”">i</a></sup>of Ephraim according to their families,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 17:9"> with the cities that were set apart for the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “sons”">j</a></sup>of Ephraim in the midst of the inheritance of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “sons”">k</a></sup>of Manasseh, all the cities and their villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:29; Deuteronomy 7:1–2; 1 Kings 9:16"> But they did not drive out the Canananites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">l</a></sup>who were dwelling in Gezer, and so the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">m</a></sup>live in the midst of Ephraim to this day, but they became forced laborers.</span>
</div>
