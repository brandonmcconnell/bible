## The Allotment of the Other Half-Tribe of Manasseh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:29; Genesis 41:51; Genesis 50:23"> Then the allotment was made for the tribe of Manasseh, because he was the firstborn of Joseph. To Makir, the firstborn of Manasseh, the father of Gilead, ⸤were allotted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and there was to him”">a</a></sup>Gilead and Bashan, because he was a warrior.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “a man of war”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:18; Numbers 26:29–32; Judges 6:11"> An allotment was made for the remaining descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “sons”">c</a></sup>of Manasseh, according to their families: For the children of Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida—these were the male descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “sons”">d</a></sup>of Manasseh son of Joseph according to their families.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 26:33; Numbers 36:2–11"> But Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Makir, son of Manasseh, had no sons, only daughters. These are the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Numbers 27:5–7; Galatians 3:28"> They came before Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders, saying, “Yahweh commanded Moses to give an inheritance to us among our kinsmen.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “among our brothers”">e</a></sup>Therefore, according to the ⸤command of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “mouth of Yahweh”">f</a></sup>he gave them an inheritance among the kinsmen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “brothers”">g</a></sup>of their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 17:2–3; Numbers 32:30–42; Joshua 13:29–31"> Thus ten shares fell to Manasseh, besides the land of Gilead and Bashan, which is beyond the Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> because the daughters of Manasseh received an inheritance among his sons. And the land of Gilead was allotted to the remaining descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “sons”">h</a></sup>of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:21; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 24:32"> The border of Manasseh was from Asher to Micmethath, which is opposite Shechem;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “which faces Shechem”">i</a></sup>then the border goes to the south, to the inhabitants of En Tappuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 16:8; Joshua 15:34; Joshua 12:17"> The land of Tappuah ⸤belonged to Manasseh⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “was to Manasseh”">j</a></sup>but Tuppuah on the border of Manasseh ⸤belonged to the descendants of Ephraim⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “was to the children of Ephraim”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 16:3; Joshua 19:29; Joshua 16:8–9"> Then the border goes down to the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">l</a></sup>of Kanah to the south of the wadi. These cities belong to Ephraim among the cities of Manasseh. The border of Manasseh is north of the wadi, and ⸤it ends⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the goings out of it were”">m</a></sup>at the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The south is Ephraim’s, and the north is Manasseh’s; the sea is their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “its”">n</a></sup>border; Asher touches the north and on the east Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:29; 2 Kings 9:27; Judges 5:19"> In Issachar and Asher, Manasseh had Beth-shean and its villages, Ibleam and its villages, the inhabitants of Dor and its villages, the inhabitants of En-dor and its villages, the inhabitants of Taanach and its villages, the inhabitants of Megiddo and its villages; the third is Napheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 1:27–28; Exodus 23:29–33; Romans 6:12–14"> But the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “sons”">o</a></sup>of Manasseh were not able to take possession of these towns; the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">p</a></sup>were determined to live in this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Philippians 4:13; Judges 1:35"> And it happened, when the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>grew strong, they put the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">r</a></sup>to forced labor but never drove them out completely.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Tribes of Joseph Object

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:34–37; Genesis 48:19; Genesis 48:22"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “sons”">s</a></sup>of Joseph spoke with Joshua, saying, “Why have you given us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “to me”">t</a></sup>one allotment and one share as an inheritance? We are many people, which Yahweh has blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; Genesis 14:5; Luke 12:48"> And Joshua said to them, “If you are many people, go up to the forest and clear a place there for yourselves in the land of the Perizzites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">u</a></sup>and Rephaim, since the hill country of Ephraim is too narrow for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 4:12; Judges 4:3; Judges 1:19"> And the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “sons”">v</a></sup>of Joseph said, “The hill country is not enough for us, and all of the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">w</a></sup>living in the land of the valley have chariots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">x</a></sup>of iron, those in Beth-shean and its villages, and those in the Jezreel Valley.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Joshua said to the house of Joseph, to Ephraim and Manasseh, “You are many people and have great power; you will not have one allotment only;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Romans 8:31; Numbers 14:6–9"> the hill country will be yours. Even though it is a forest, you will clear it, and it will be yours to its farthest borders. You will drive out the Canaanites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">y</a></sup>even though they have iron chariots and are strong.”</span>
</div>
</div>
