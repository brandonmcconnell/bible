## The Gibeonites Act with Cunning

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 23:23"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And it happened”">a</a></sup>when all the kings who were beyond the Jordan in the hill country and in the Shephelah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “lowlands”; a geographical region on the western edge of the hills of Judea">b</a></sup>and on all the coast of the great sea toward Lebanon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “white mountain”">c</a></sup>—the Hittites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">d</a></sup>the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">e</a></sup>the Canaanites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">f</a></sup>the Perizzites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">g</a></sup>the Hivites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">h</a></sup>and the Jebusites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">i</a></sup>—heard of this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Joel 3:9–13; Psalm 2:1–2"> they gathered themselves together to fight with one accord against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “with”">j</a></sup>Joshua and Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:17; 2 Samuel 21:1–2"> But the inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua did to Jericho and Ai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Matthew 10:16; Mark 2:22"> and they acted on their part with cunning: they went and prepared provisions,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The Hebrew is difficult here. Some ancient manuscripts read, “they sent out a delegation/an envoy”">k</a></sup>and took worn-out sacks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “sackcloths”">l</a></sup>for their donkeys and old wineskins that were torn and mended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Deuteronomy 33:25; Deuteronomy 29:5"> The sandals on their feet were patched and old, their clothes were old, and their food was dry and crumbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; 2 Kings 20:14; Joshua 9:9"> And they went to Joshua at the camp at Gilgal and said to him and to the men of Israel, “We have come from a far land; so then ⸤make a covenant with us⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “cut for us a covenant”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 11:19; Judges 2:2; Exodus 34:12"> And the men of Israel said to the Hivites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">n</a></sup>“Perhaps you are living among us; how can we ⸤make a covenant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “cut a covenant”">o</a></sup>with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:11; 2 Kings 10:5; Joshua 9:11"> They said to Joshua, “We are your servants.” And Joshua said to them, “Who are you, and from where do you come?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15; Joshua 9:24; Isaiah 55:5"> And they said to him, “Your servants have come from a very far land because of the name of Yahweh your God; we have heard of his reputation, of all that he did in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:24–35; Deuteronomy 1:4; Joshua 12:4"> and of all that he did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan—to Sihon king of Heshbon and to Og king of Bashan, who was in Ashtaroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 9:8; Esther 8:17; Joshua 1:11"> So our elders said to us and all the inhabitants of our land, ‘Take in your hand provisions for the journey, and go to meet them, and say to them, “We are your servants; so then ⸤make a covenant with us⸥.” ’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “cut for us a covenant”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 9:4–5"> This is our bread; it was hot when we took it from our houses as provisions on the day we set out to come to you. But now, look, it is dry and crumbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> These are the wineskins that we filled new, but look, they have burst; and these are our clothes and sandals that have worn out from the very long journey.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Numbers 27:21; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14"> So the leaders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “men”">q</a></sup>took from their provisions, but ⸤they did not ask direction from Yahweh⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of Yahweh they did not ask”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:2; Deuteronomy 20:10–11; Joshua 11:19"> And Joshua made peace with them, and ⸤he made a covenant with them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “he cut a covenant with them”">s</a></sup>to allow them to live happily, and the leaders of the congregation swore an oath to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 12:19"> And it happened that at the end of three days, after they made a covenant with them, they heard that ⸤they were their neighbors⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “they were near them”">t</a></sup>and living among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 2:25; Joshua 18:25–28; Joshua 18:14"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">u</a></sup>set out and went to their cities on the third day (their cities were Gibeon, Kephirah, Beeroth, and Kiriath Jearim).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 15:4; 2 Samuel 21:7; Ecclesiastes 5:6"> But the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup>did not attack them, because the leaders of the congregation had sworn to them by Yahweh the God of Israel. And all the congregation murmured ⸤against their leaders⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “against the leaders of the congregation”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Joshua 9:20; Ecclesiastes 9:2"> But all the leaders of the congregation said, “We have sworn to them by Yahweh the God of Israel, and so we cannot touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 20:25; 1 Timothy 1:10"> This we will do to them: we will let them live so that wrath will not be on us because of the oath we swore to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:11; Joshua 9:27; Joshua 9:23"> And the leaders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “men”">x</a></sup>said to them, “Let them live.” So they became woodcutters and water carriers for all the congregation, just as the leaders had said to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 9:16; Genesis 3:13–14"> And Joshua summoned them and said, “Why have you deceived us saying, ‘We are very far from you’ when you are living among us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 9:21; Genesis 9:25–26; Joshua 9:27"> Therefore you are cursed; some of you will always be slaves as woodcutters and water carriers for the house of my God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:15–17; Deuteronomy 7:1–2; Exodus 23:31–33"> And they answered Joshua and said, “Because it was told with certainty to your servants that Yahweh your God commanded Moses his servant to give to you all the land and to destroy all the inhabitants of the land before you, so we were very afraid for our lives because of you, and so we did this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 16:6; Judges 10:15; Jeremiah 26:14"> So then, look, we are in your hand; do with us whatever seems good and right in your eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So he did this to them: he saved them from the hand of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">y</a></sup>and they did not kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5"> And that day Joshua made them woodcutters and water carriers for the congregation and for the altar of Yahweh, to this day, in the place that he should choose.</span>
</div>
