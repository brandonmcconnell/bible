## The Israelites Are Circumcised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 2:9–11; Numbers 13:29; 1 Kings 10:5"> And it happened, when all the kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan to the west, and all the kings of the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">a</a></sup>who were by the sea heard that Yahweh dried up the waters of the Jordan in front of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>until they crossed over, their hearts melted, and ⸤there was no courage left in them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a spirit was no longer in them”">c</a></sup>because of the presence of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Deuteronomy 10:16; Exodus 4:25"> At that time Yahweh said to Joshua, “Make for yourself knives of flint, and circumcise the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>a second time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Genesis 17:23–27"> So Joshua made knives of flint, and he circumcised the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>at the hill of the foreskins.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “Gibeath-haaraloth”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Deuteronomy 2:16"> This is the reason why Joshua circumcised all the people: all the males who went out from Egypt, all the warriors, died in the wilderness as they went out from Egypt ⸤on the journey⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “on the road”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8–9; Hosea 6:6–7; Matthew 12:7"> For all the people who left were circumcised, but all the people born in the wilderness ⸤on the journey⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “on the road”">i</a></sup>after they left from Egypt were not circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:23; Deuteronomy 2:14; Hebrews 3:11"> For forty years the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>traveled in the wilderness until all the nation, the warriors that left Egypt, perished, because they did not listen to the voice of Yahweh. To them Yahweh swore that they would not see the land that he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “Yahweh”">k</a></sup>swore to their ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>to give to us, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 14:31; Deuteronomy 1:39"> And it was their children whom he raised in their place that Joshua circumcised, for they were uncircumcised, because they had not been circumcised ⸤on the journey⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “on the road”">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Israelites Celebrate Passover in Canaan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 34:25"> And it happened, when all the nation had finished circumcising, they remained where they were in the camp until they recovered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 4:19; 1 Samuel 17:26; Genesis 34:14"> And Yahweh said to Joshua, “Today I have rolled away the disgrace of Egypt from you.” Therefore, the name of that place is called Gilgal<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “rolling”">n</a></sup>to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:1–5; Ezekiel 12:3"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>camped at Gilgal, and they kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month, in the evening, on the plains of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 23:14; Exodus 13:6–7; Leviticus 23:6"> On the next day after the Passover, on that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “this”">p</a></sup>very day, they ate from the produce of the land, unleavened cakes and roasted corn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 16:35; Isaiah 65:13–14; Proverbs 13:22"> And the manna ceased the day after, when they started eating the produce of the land, and there was no longer manna for the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>They ate from the crop of the land of Canaan in that year.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Commander of Yahweh’s Army Appears Before Joshua

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 18:2; Numbers 22:23; Numbers 22:31"> And it happened, when Joshua was by Jericho, ⸤he looked up⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “he lifted up his eyes”">r</a></sup>and he saw a man standing ⸤opposite him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “against him”">s</a></sup>with his sword drawn in his hand. And Joshua went to him and said, “Are you with us, or with our adversaries?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 55:4; Genesis 17:3; Revelation 19:10–14"> And he said, “Neither. I have come now as the commander of Yahweh’s army.” And Joshua fell on his face to the earth, and ⸤he bowed down⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “he worshiped”">t</a></sup>and said to him, “What is my lord commanding his servant?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Acts 7:32–33; 2 Peter 1:18"> The commander of Yahweh’s army said to Joshua, “Take off your sandals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “sandal”">u</a></sup>from your feet,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “foot”">v</a></sup>for the place where you are standing is holy.” And Joshua did so.</span>
</div>
