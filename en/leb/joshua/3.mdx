## The Israelites Cross the Jordan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 2:1; Mark 1:35; Numbers 25:1"> Joshua rose early in the morning, and they set out from Acacia Grove.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “Shittim”">a</a></sup>And they came up to the Jordan, he and all the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>and they spent the night there before they crossed over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:10–11"> At the end of the three days the officers passed through the midst of the camp,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; 2 Samuel 6:13; Deuteronomy 31:25"> and they commanded the people: “When you see the Levitical priests carrying the ark of the covenant of Yahweh your God you must set out from your place and go after it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 89:7; Hebrews 12:28–29; Exodus 3:5"> But there will be a distance between you and it of about two thousand cubits in measurement.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, about 1 km">c</a></sup>Do not come near it, so that you may know the way that you must go, for you have not passed on this way ⸤before⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from yesterday three days before”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Leviticus 20:7; Joel 2:16"> And Joshua said to the people, “Sanctify yourselves, because tomorrow Yahweh will do wonders in your midst.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:2–3; Numbers 14:15; Micah 2:13"> And Joshua said to the priests, “Take up the ark of the covenant and cross over ⸤ahead of the people⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “before the presence of the people”">e</a></sup>And they took up the ark of the covenant and went ⸤ahead of the people⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “before the presence of the people”">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:14; Joshua 1:5; 1 Chronicles 29:25"> Then Yahweh said to Joshua, “This day I will begin exalting you ⸤in the sight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the eyes”">g</a></sup>of all Israel, that they may know that I was with Moses, and I will be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 3:3; Joshua 3:17; 2 Chronicles 29:15"> You will command the priests carrying the ark of the covenant, saying, ‘At the moment that you come to the edge of the waters of the Jordan, you will stand still in the Jordan.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 12:8"> And Joshua said to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>“Come here, and hear the words of Yahweh your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Exodus 33:2; Psalm 44:2–3"> Joshua said, “By this you will know that the living God is in your midst, and he will certainly drive out the Canaanites ⸤from before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “from your presence”">i</a></sup>and the Hittites, Hivites, the Perizzites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, and the Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 6:5; Zechariah 4:14; Psalm 24:1"> Look! The ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “world”">j</a></sup>is about to cross over ahead of you into the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 4:2; Joshua 4:9; Joshua 4:4"> So then, take twelve men from the tribes of Israel, ⸤one from each tribe⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “one man for the tribe”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 15:8; Psalm 78:13; Joshua 3:15–16"> When the soles of the feet of the priests carrying the ark of Yahweh, Lord of all the earth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “world”">l</a></sup>rest in the waters of the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan will be cut off ⸤upstream⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “going down from above”">m</a></sup>and they will stand still in one heap.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 7:44–45; Joshua 6:6; 1 Corinthians 1:24–25"> And it happened, when the people set out from their tents to cross over the Jordan, the priests carrying the ark of the covenant were ⸤ahead of the people⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “before the presence of the people”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:15; Joshua 4:18; Joshua 3:13"> When those carrying the ark came up to the Jordan, and the priests carrying the ark dipped their feet in the edge of the water (the Jordan was flowing over its banks during all the days of harvest),</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 3:13; 1 Kings 4:12; 1 Kings 7:46"> the waters flowing down from above stood still; they stood up in one heap very far from Adam, the city that is beside Zarethan, while the waters flowing down to the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="That is, the Dead Sea">o</a></sup>⸤were completely cut off⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “they were completed they were cut off”">p</a></sup>and the people crossed over opposite Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:22; Exodus 14:29; Psalm 66:6"> And the priests carrying the ark of the covenant of Yahweh stood firmly on the dry land in the middle of the Jordan while all Israel crossed on dry ground, until all the nation finished crossing the Jordan.</span>
</div>
