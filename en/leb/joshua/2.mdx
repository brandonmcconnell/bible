## Spies View the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; James 2:25; Matthew 1:5"> Then Joshua son of Nun secretly sent two men from Acacia Grove<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “Shittim”">a</a></sup>as spies, saying, “Go, view the land, especially Jericho.” So they went, and entered the house of a prostitute whose name was Rahab, and ⸤spent the night⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “lay down”">b</a></sup>there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:13; Daniel 4:35; Proverbs 21:30"> The king of Jericho was told, “Look, some men from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>have come here tonight to search out the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:3; Genesis 42:9–12; Acts 12:6"> And the king of Jericho sent for Rahab, saying, “Bring out the men who came to you, the ones who have entered your house, for they have come to search out the whole land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 6:19; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 2 Samuel 16:18–19"> But the woman took the two men and hid them. And she said, “Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; Ezekiel 47:12; Revelation 21:25"> And when it was time to shut the gate ⸤for the night⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in the darkness” or “at dark”">d</a></sup>the men left, and I do not know where they went. Chase after them quickly, for you may catch up to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; James 2:25; Colossians 3:3"> (But she had taken them to the roof and had hidden them ⸤in the stalks of flax⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in the flaxes of the plant stalk”">e</a></sup>that she ⸤had spread out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “had been arranged”">f</a></sup>on the roof.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 2:5; Judges 12:5"> So the men chased after them on ⸤the way to the Jordan⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the road of the Jordan”">g</a></sup>at the fords; and they shut the gate behind the pursuers that had gone out after them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Before ⸤they went to sleep⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “they lay down”">h</a></sup>she came up to them on the roof</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 2:11"> and said to the men, “I know that Yahweh has given you the land, and that dread of you has fallen on us, and that all the inhabitants of the land melt away in fear because of your presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Exodus 15:14–16; Exodus 14:21–31"> For we have heard how Yahweh dried up the waters of the ⸤Red Sea⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sea of reeds”">i</a></sup>before you when you went out from Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites that were beyond the Jordan, Sihon and Og, whom you utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Joshua 5:1; Joshua 7:5"> We heard this, and our hearts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “heart”">j</a></sup>melted, and ⸤no courage was left in anyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “a spirit no longer stood in anyone”">k</a></sup>because of your presence. For Yahweh your God is God in the heavens above and on the earth below.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 2:18; Joshua 9:18–20; 2 Timothy 1:16–18"> So then please swear to me by Yahweh, because ⸤I have shown loyalty to you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “I have done with you a loyal love”">l</a></sup>and ⸤you will also show loyalty⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “you will do … loyal love”">m</a></sup>⸤to my family⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “with the house of my father”">n</a></sup>You must give me a sign of good faith,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “faithfulness”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and you will spare my father and mother, my brothers and sisters, and all that belongs to them; you will deliver our lives from death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Judges 1:24–25; Genesis 24:49"> And the men said to her, “⸤Our lives for yours⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “Our lives in place of yours to die”">p</a></sup>If you do not report this business of ours, ⸤we will show you loyalty and faithfulness⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “we will do with you loyal love and faithfulness”">q</a></sup>when Yahweh gives us the land.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 2 Corinthians 11:33; Joshua 6:20"> Then she lowered them with a rope through the window, as her house was on the outer side of the wall, and she was residing in the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:25; 1 Samuel 23:14; Psalm 11:1"> And she said to them, “Go to the mountain, so that the pursuers will not find you, and hide yourselves there three days until the pursuers return, and afterward you may go ⸤on your way⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to your way”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 30:2; 2 Samuel 21:7; Genesis 24:3–8"> The men said to her, “We will be released from this oath of yours that you made us swear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 6:23; Joshua 2:21; Joshua 2:12–13"> When we come to the land, you must tie this scarlet cord in the window through which you let us down, and you must gather your father and mother, and your brothers, and your whole family to your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 27:31; 1 Kings 2:36–42; Exodus 12:13"> If anyone goes outside the doors of your house, ⸤they will be responsible for their own death⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “his blood will be on his head”">s</a></sup>and we will be innocent. Anyone who will be with you in the house, ⸤we will be responsible for their death⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “his blood will be on our head”">t</a></sup>⸤if a hand is laid on them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “if a hand will be against him”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 11:13"> But if you report this business of ours, we will be released from your oath that you made us swear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 2:5; Joshua 2:18; Matthew 7:24"> And she said, “According to your word it will be.” Then she sent them away, and they went, and she tied the scarlet cord in the window.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:10–12; 2 Samuel 17:20; Psalm 32:6–7"> They departed and came to the mountain, and they stayed there three days until the pursuers returned. The pursuers searched ⸤all along the way⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “on all of the road”">v</a></sup>but did not find them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The two men returned and went down from the mountain, and they crossed over and came to Joshua son of Nun, and they told him everything that happened to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Joshua 1:8; Joshua 2:9–11"> They said to Joshua, “Surely Yahweh has given all the land into our hand; also, all the inhabitants of the land melt away in fear because of our presence.”</span>
</div>
