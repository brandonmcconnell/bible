## Joshua Addresses the Israelites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:9; Deuteronomy 1:38; Deuteronomy 31:23"> After the death of Moses the servant of Yahweh, Yahweh said to Joshua son of Nun, the assistant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “servant”">a</a></sup>of Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:7; Numbers 27:16–21"> “My servant Moses is dead. Get up and cross the Jordan, you and all this people, into the land that I am giving to them, to the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 14:9; Titus 1:2"> Every place that the soles of your feet will tread, I have given it to you, as I promised<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “spoke”">c</a></sup>to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21; Deuteronomy 3:25"> From the wilderness ⸤and the Lebanon⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “this Lebanon”; “Lebanon” in Hebrew means “white mountain”">d</a></sup>up to the great river, the river Euphrates, all of the land of the Hittites, and up to ⸤the great sea in the west⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the great sea of the setting sun”">e</a></sup>will be your territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Matthew 28:20"> No one will stand before you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “in your presence”">f</a></sup>all the days of your life. Just as I was with Moses, so will I be with you; I will not fail you, and I will not forsake you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:9"> Be strong and courageous, for you will give the people this land as an inheritance that I swore to their ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup>to give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:13; Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:8"> Only be strong and very courageous ⸤to observe diligently the whole law⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to keep and to act according to the whole law”">h</a></sup>that Moses my servant commanded you. Do not turn aside from it, to the right or left, so that you may succeed ⸤wherever you go⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “in all that you go”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 119:15; Psalm 19:14; Colossians 3:16"> The scroll of this law will not depart from your mouth; you will meditate on it day and night so that ⸤you may observe diligently all that is written⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to keep and to act according to all that is written”">j</a></sup>in it. For then you will succeed in your ways and prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Jeremiah 1:7–8; Joshua 1:6–7"> Did I not command you? Be strong and courageous! Do not fear or be dismayed, for Yahweh your God is with you ⸤wherever you go⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “in all that you go”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Joshua commanded the officers of the people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1; Hosea 6:2; 2 Kings 20:5"> “Pass through the midst of the camp and command the people: ‘Prepare your provisions;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “provision”">l</a></sup>⸤in⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “for in still”">m</a></sup>three days you are to cross the Jordan to go possess the land that Yahweh your God is giving you to possess.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–22"> To the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh Joshua said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–28; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 22:1–4"> “Remember the word that Moses Yahweh’s servant commanded you, saying, ‘Yahweh your God is giving rest to you, and he is giving<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “he gave”">n</a></sup>you this land.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Exodus 13:18; Revelation 17:4"> Your wives, your little children, and your livestock, they will remain in the land that Moses gave to you beyond the Jordan. ⸤All of the best fighting men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “All the mighty warriors of the troop”">o</a></sup>will cross armed in front of your families; they will help you</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:4; Numbers 32:17–22; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> until Yahweh gives rest to your families as well as to you. They will take possession of the land that Yahweh your God is giving to them. Then you will return to your own land and take possession of it, the land that Moses the servant of Yahweh gave you beyond the Jordan ⸤to the east⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to the sunrise”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Deuteronomy 5:27; 1 Peter 2:13–15"> And they answered Joshua, saying, “All that you have commanded us we will do, and wherever you will send us we will go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; 1 Kings 1:37; Joshua 1:9"> Just as we obeyed Moses, so will we obey you. Only may Yahweh your God be with you, as he was with Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:9; Ezra 10:4"> Whoever ⸤rebels against your orders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “defiles against your mouth”">q</a></sup>and does not obey your words according to what you commanded us will be put to death. Only be strong and courageous.”</span>
</div>
</div>
