## The Sin of Achan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 22:20; Ecclesiastes 9:18; 1 Chronicles 2:6–7"> But the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>broke faith concerning the devoted things.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “consecrated possessions ”">b</a></sup>Achan son of Carmi son of Zabdi son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took from the devoted things;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “thing” or “consecrated possession”">c</a></sup>and ⸤Yahweh’s anger was kindled⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the nose of Yahweh became hot”">d</a></sup>against the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Hosea 4:15; 1 Samuel 14:23"> Now Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is near Beth Aven, east of Bethel, and he said to them, “Go up and spy out Ai.” And the men went up and spied out Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11–12; Luke 13:24; 2 Peter 1:5"> And they returned to Joshua and said to him, “Do not let all the people go up and attack Ai; only two or three thousand men should go up because they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, the people of Ai">f</a></sup>are few. Do not make all the people weary up there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:25; Leviticus 26:17; Deuteronomy 32:30"> So about three thousand from the people went up there, and they fled before the men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 2:11; Joshua 2:9; Leviticus 26:36"> The men of Ai killed about thirty-six of them, and they chased them from the gate up to Shebarim and killed them on the slope. And the hearts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “heart”">g</a></sup>of the people melted and became like water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 37:29; Genesis 37:34; Revelation 18:19"> And Joshua tore his clothes and fell to the ground on his face before the ark of Yahweh until the evening, he and the elders of Israel; and they put dust on their heads.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “head”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5; 2 Kings 3:10; Matthew 17:20"> And Joshua said, “Ah, my Lord! Why did you bring this people across the Jordan to give us into the hand of the Amorites to destroy us? If only we had been content and stayed beyond the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:5–6; Ezra 9:10; Habakkuk 2:1"> Please, my Lord! What can I say after ⸤Israel has fled from its enemies⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “Israel has turned its neck before its enemies”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 32:12; Psalm 83:4; Ezekiel 36:22–23"> The Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land will hear of this, and they will surround us and cut off our name from the land. What will you do, for your great name?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; Exodus 14:15; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And Yahweh said to Joshua, “Stand up! ⸤Why⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “For what this”">j</a></sup>have you fallen on your face?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Hebrews 4:13; Joshua 6:17–19"> Israel has sinned and ⸤transgressed my covenant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “crossed my covenant”">k</a></sup>that I commanded them. They have taken from the devoted things;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “thing” or “from the consecrated possession”">l</a></sup>they have stolen and acted deceitfully, and they have put them among their belongings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 5:4–5; Isaiah 59:2; Joshua 6:18"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>were unable to stand before their enemies; ⸤they fled from their enemies⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “they turned their neck before their enemies”">n</a></sup>because they have become a thing devoted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “consecrated”">o</a></sup>for destruction. ⸤I will be with you no more⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “I will not do again to be with you”">p</a></sup>unless you destroy the devoted things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “thing” or “consecrated possession”">q</a></sup>from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; Joshua 3:5; Matthew 7:5"> Get up, sanctify the people, and say, ‘Sanctify yourselves for tomorrow. Thus says Yahweh the God of Israel: “There are devoted things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “thing” or “consecrated possession”">r</a></sup>in your midst, O Israel. You will be unable to stand before your enemies until you remove the devoted things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “thing” or “consecrated possession”">s</a></sup>from your midst.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Jonah 1:7; Joshua 7:17–18"> In the morning you will come forward, ⸤tribe by tribe⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “according to your tribes”">t</a></sup>and the tribe that Yahweh will select by lot will come forward by clans, and the clan that Yahweh selects by lot will come forward by families, and the family that Yahweh selects by lot will come forward one by one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 1 Samuel 14:38–39; Judges 20:6"> The one caught with the devoted things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “thing” or “consecrated possession”">u</a></sup>will be burned with fire, he and all that belongs to him, because ⸤he transgressed the covenant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “he crossed the covenant”">v</a></sup>of Yahweh, and because he did a disgraceful thing in Israel.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 119:60; Joshua 3:1; Genesis 22:3"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “And”">w</a></sup>Joshua rose early in the morning and brought forward Israel, ⸤tribe by tribe⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “according to its tribes”">x</a></sup>and the tribe of Judah was selected by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; 1 Chronicles 2:4–7; Genesis 38:30"> And he brought forward the clans of Judah and selected the clan of the Zerahites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “Zerahite”">y</a></sup>by lot. Then he brought forward the clan of the Zerahites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “Zerahite”">z</a></sup>one by one, and Zabdi was selected by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 32:23; Acts 5:1–10; 1 Samuel 14:42"> He brought forward his family, one by one, and Achan son of Carmi son of Zabdi son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was selected by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:5; Jeremiah 13:16; John 9:24"> And Joshua said to Achan, “My son, please, give glory to Yahweh the God of Israel, and give him a doxology in court.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Some interpret “make a confession”">aa</a></sup>Tell me, please, what you have done; do not hide it from me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 38:18; Job 33:27; Genesis 42:21"> And Achan answered Joshua and said, “It is true. I have sinned against Yahweh the God of Israel, and this is what I did:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:15; Psalm 119:37; 1 John 2:15–16"> I saw among the spoil a beautiful robe from Shinar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="“Shinar” refers to Babylonia">ab</a></sup>two hundred shekels of silver, and one bar of gold that weighed fifty shekels; I coveted them and took them. They are hidden in the ground inside my tent, and the silver is under it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent; and there they were, hidden in his tent, and the silver was under it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And they took them from the tent and brought them to Joshua and all the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ac</a></sup>And they spread them out before the presence of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:27; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Joshua 7:26"> Then Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan son of Zerah, the silver, the robe, the bar of gold, his sons and daughters, his cattle and donkeys, his sheep, his tent, and everything that was his, and they brought them to the valley of Achor.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Hebrew “trouble”; a valley in the Jericho region">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; 1 Chronicles 2:7; Leviticus 24:14"> And Joshua said, “Why did you bring us trouble? Yahweh will bring you trouble on this day.” And all Israel stoned them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">ae</a></sup>with stones;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Hebrew “stone”">af</a></sup>and they burned them with fire after they stoned them with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 65:10; Hosea 2:15; Deuteronomy 13:17"> Then ⸤they placed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “they raised up”">ag</a></sup>on top of him a great pile of stones that remains to this day. And Yahweh ⸤turned from his burning anger⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “turned from his burning nose”">ah</a></sup>and thus the name of that place to this day is called the valley of Achor.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “trouble”; a valley in the Jericho region">ai</a></sup></span>
</div>
