## The Land Allotted West of the Jordan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 34:17–29"> These are the territories that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>inherited in the land of Canaan, which Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the families of the tribes of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>gave as an inheritance to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 33:54; Numbers 34:13; Numbers 26:55–56"> Their inheritance was by lot, just as Yahweh commanded through the hand of Moses, for the nine tribes and the half-tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:14; Joshua 13:8; Joshua 13:32–33"> For Moses had given an inheritance of the two tribes and the half-tribe beyond the Jordan, but to the Levites he gave no inheritance among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 48:5; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2; Numbers 35:2–8"> For the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “sons”">c</a></sup>of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim, and they did not give a plot of ground to the Levites in the land, only cities to live in, with their pastureland for their flocks and for their goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 21:2; Numbers 35:1–2"> Just as Yahweh commanded Moses, so the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>did; and they allotted the land.</span>
</div>

## Caleb Receives Hebron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Numbers 13:6; Numbers 14:24"> Then the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “sons”">e</a></sup>of Judah came to Joshua at Gilgal; and Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite said to him, “You know the word that Yahweh said to Moses the man of God at Kadesh Barnea concerning you and me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:6; Numbers 13:26–33; Numbers 14:6–10"> ⸤I was forty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “I was a son of forty years ”">f</a></sup>when Moses Yahweh’s servant sent me from Kadesh Barnea to spy out the land, and I returned ⸤with an honest report⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “with a word just as was with my heart”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 13:31"> My companions who went up with me made the hearts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “heart”">h</a></sup>of the people melt, but I remained true to Yahweh my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 1:3; Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:22–24"> And Moses swore on that day, saying, ‘Surely the land that your foot has trodden on will be an inheritance to you and your sons forever, because you remained true to Yahweh my God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:33–34; Numbers 14:30; Joshua 11:18"> So then, look, Yahweh has kept me alive just as he promised these forty-five years,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “year”">i</a></sup>from the time that Yahweh spoke this word to Moses while Israel ⸤wandered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “went”">j</a></sup>in the wilderness. Now look, today ⸤I am eighty-five years old⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “I am a son of eighty-five years ”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Psalm 103:5; Deuteronomy 31:2"> Today I am still strong, just as on the day that Moses sent me; as my strength was then, so now also is my strength for war ⸤and for daily activities⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “for going out and for coming in ”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:33; Philippians 4:13; Numbers 13:33"> So now give me this hill country that Yahweh spoke of on that day, for you heard on that day that the Anakites were there, with great and fortified cities. Perhaps Yahweh is with me, and I will drive them out just as Yahweh promised.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “said”">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:20; Joshua 22:6; 1 Chronicles 6:55–56"> And Joshua blessed him and gave Hebron to Caleb son of Jephunneh as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 14:8–9; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Thus Hebron became the inheritance of Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite to this day, because he remained true to Yahweh the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 11:23; Genesis 23:2; Joshua 15:13"> And the name of Hebron formerly was Kiriath Arba;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “the city of Arba”">n</a></sup>Arba was the greatest person among the Anakites. And the land rested from war.</span>
</div>
</div>
