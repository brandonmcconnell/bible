## Joshua’s Conquest of the North

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 11:10; Psalm 2:1–4; Judges 4:2"> And it happened, when Jabin king of Hazor heard this, he sent to Jobab king of Madon, to the king of Shimron, to the king of Acshaph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Judges 1:27; Joshua 17:11"> and to the kings who were in the north in the hill country, in the Arabah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A dry region that runs south of the Sea of Galilee along the Jordan valley">a</a></sup>south of Kinnereth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Sea of Galilee">b</a></sup>in the Shephelah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “lowlands”; a geographical region on the western edge of the hills of Judea">c</a></sup>and in Naphoth Dor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “the heights of Dor”">d</a></sup>in the west,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:3; Joshua 18:26; Genesis 31:49"> to the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">e</a></sup>in the east and west, the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">f</a></sup>the Hittites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">g</a></sup>the Perizzites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">h</a></sup>and the Jebusites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">i</a></sup>in the hill country, and the Hivites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">j</a></sup>⸤at the foot of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “under”">k</a></sup>Hermon in the land of Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 7:12; 1 Samuel 13:5; Genesis 32:12"> They came out, they and all their armies with them, as a great army like the sand on the seashore, with very many horses and chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 118:10–12; Revelation 16:14; Isaiah 8:9"> And all these kings joined forces, and they came and camped together by the waters of Merom to fight with Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 10:8; 2 Samuel 8:4; Judges 20:28"> And Yahweh said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid because of their presence, for tomorrow at this time I will hand them over slain to Israel; you will hamstring their horses and burn their chariots with fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 10:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> So Joshua, and ⸤all the fighting men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “all the people of war”">l</a></sup>with him, came against them suddenly at the waters of Merom, and ⸤they attacked them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “they fell upon them”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:6; Joshua 19:28; Zechariah 9:2"> And Yahweh gave them into the hand of Israel, and they struck them and pursued them up to Great Sidon and Misrephoth Maim, and eastward up to the valley of Mizpeh. And they struck them until they left behind no survivor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 11:6; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> And Joshua did to them as Yahweh commanded him; he hamstrung their horses and burned their chariots with fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 11:1; Judges 4:2"> Then Joshua turned back at that time, and he captured Hazor and struck its king with the sword, because Hazor formerly was the head of all these kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 10:40; Deuteronomy 20:16"> He struck all the people that were in it with ⸤the edge of the sword⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword”">n</a></sup>utterly destroying them. ⸤There was no one left who breathed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “No one was left over of any breath”">o</a></sup>and he burned Hazor with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Deuteronomy 7:2; Numbers 33:50–53"> And Joshua captured all the cities of these kings, and all their kings, and he utterly destroyed them with ⸤the edge of the sword⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword”">p</a></sup>as Moses the servant of Yahweh commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:18"> Israel did not burn the cities standing on their mounds,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “mound”">q</a></sup>except Hazor alone, which Joshua burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:9; Joshua 8:27"> And all the spoil and livestock of these cities the ⸤Israelites⸥ took as booty; they struck the people with ⸤the edge of the sword⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword”">r</a></sup>until they had destroyed them—they left behind no one who breathed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 1:7; Exodus 34:11–13; Acts 20:20"> Just as Yahweh commanded Moses his servant, so Moses commanded Joshua, and Joshua did; he left nothing undone that Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Review of Joshua’s Conquests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 10:41; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 12:8"> So Joshua took all this land: the hill country, all the Negev,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="An arid region south of the Judaean hills">s</a></sup>all the land of Goshen,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="A southern region; the name means “on the mountains”">t</a></sup>the Shephelah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “lowlands”; a geographical region on the western edge of the hills of Judea">u</a></sup>the Arabah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="A dry region that runs south of the Sea of Galilee along the Jordan valley">v</a></sup>and the hill country of Israel and its Shephelah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “lowlands”; a geographical region on the western edge of the hills of Judea">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Joshua 11:3; Joshua 13:5"> from Mount Halak that rises to Seir and to Baal Gad in the valley of Lebanon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “white mountain”">x</a></sup>⸤at the foot of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “under”">y</a></sup>Mount Hermon; he captured all their kings, struck them, and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 14:7–10; Joshua 11:23"> For many days Joshua made war with all these kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 9:3–27"> There was not a city that made peace with the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">z</a></sup>besides the Hivites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">aa</a></sup>and the inhabitants of Gibeon—⸤all were taken in battle⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “the all they took in the battle”">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 4:21"> For it was Yahweh that ⸤hardened their hearts⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “made their hearts strong”">ac</a></sup>to meet Israel in war in order to utterly destroy them without mercy, that they would destroy them just as Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:2; Numbers 13:22–23; Joshua 15:13–14"> At that time Joshua came and exterminated the Anakites from the hill country, from Hebron, Debir, Anab, and from all the hill country of Judah, and from all the hill country of Israel; Joshua utterly destroyed them with their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:4; 1 Chronicles 18:1; Joshua 15:46"> None of the Anakites were left in the land of the ⸤Israelites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ad</a></sup>some remained only in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 21:44–45; Numbers 26:52–55; Joshua 14:1–15"> Joshua took all the land according to all that Yahweh had spoken to Moses; and Joshua gave it as an inheritance to Israel, according to their tribal divisions, and the land rested from war.</span>
</div>
</div>
