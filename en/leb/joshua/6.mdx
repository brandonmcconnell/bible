## The Battle of Jericho

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 2:24; Joshua 2:9–14; Psalm 127:1"> Now Jericho was shut up inside and out because of the presence of the ⸤Israelites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>no one was going out or coming in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Joshua 8:1; Joshua 2:24"> And Yahweh said to Joshua, “Look, I am giving Jericho into your hand, its king and the soldiers of the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; Numbers 14:9; Joshua 6:7"> You will march around the city, all the warriors circling the city once; you will do so for six days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:17; Revelation 16:1; 1 Kings 18:43"> And seven priests will bear seven trumpets of rams’ horns before the ark. On the seventh day you will march around the city seven times, and the priests will blow on the trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hebrews 11:30; Exodus 19:19"> And when they blow long on the horn of the ram, when you hear the sound of the trumpet, all the people will shout with a great war cry, and the wall of the city will fall flat,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “in its place”">b</a></sup>and ⸤the people will charge, each one straight ahead⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the people will go up, each before him”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:1; Deuteronomy 20:2–4; Joshua 3:3"> So Joshua son of Nun summoned the priests and said to them, “Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests carry the trumpets of the rams’ horns before the ark of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:13; Joshua 6:3; Joshua 1:14"> And he said<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “they said”">d</a></sup>to the people, “Go forward and march around the city, and let the armed men pass before the ark of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 32:20; Joshua 6:3–4"> ⸤And when Joshua spoke⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “And it happened the moment of the saying of Joshua”">e</a></sup>to the people, the seven priests carrying the seven trumpets of rams’ horns before the presence of Yahweh went forward and they blew the trumpets; and the ark of the covenant of Yahweh followed behind them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:13; Numbers 10:25; Isaiah 58:8"> And the armed men went before the priests who blew the trumpets, and the rear guard came after the ark, while they were blowing the trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:49; Matthew 12:19; Isaiah 42:2"> But Joshua commanded the people, saying, “You will not shout, and you will not let your voice be heard; a word will not go out from your mouth until the day I say to you ‘Shout!’ Then you will shout.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the ark of Yahweh went around the city, ⸤circling once⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “circling one occurrence”">f</a></sup>and they came into the camp and spent the night in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 3:1; Hebrews 11:7–8; Genesis 22:3"> Then Joshua got up early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; Matthew 24:13; 1 Chronicles 15:26"> The seven priests carrying the seven trumpets of the rams’ horns before the ark of Yahweh went on continually, and they blew on the trumpets. And the armed men went before them, and the rear guard came after the ark of Yahweh, while the trumpets blew continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 6:15; Joshua 6:11; Joshua 6:3"> And they marched around the city once on the second day, and they returned to the camp. They did this for six days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 119:147; 2 Peter 1:19; Matthew 28:1"> Then on the seventh day they rose early at dawn, and they marched around the city in this manner seven times. It was only on that day that they marched around the city seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Joshua 6:5; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23"> And at the seventh time the priests blew on the trumpets, and Joshua said to the people, “Shout! For Yahweh has given you the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:17; James 2:25; Micah 4:13"> The city and all that is in it will be devoted to Yahweh; only Rahab the prostitute and all who are with her in the house will live, because she hid the messengers whom we sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 5:21; Ephesians 5:11; Joshua 7:1"> As for you, keep away from the things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “thing”">g</a></sup>devoted to destruction so that you do not take them and bring about your own destruction, making the camp of Israel an object for destruction, bringing trouble upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:20; Matthew 27:6; Mark 12:41"> But all of the silver and gold, and the items of bronze and iron, are holy to Yahweh, and they must go to Yahweh’s treasury.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:30; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Joshua 6:5"> So the people shouted, and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, the priests">h</a></sup>blew on the trumpets. And when the people heard the sound of the trumpet, they raised a great shout, and the wall fell flat. ⸤The people charged, each one straight ahead into the city⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “The people went up, each before him”">i</a></sup>and they captured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 137:8–9; Jeremiah 48:18; Joshua 11:14"> And they utterly destroyed ⸤by the edge of the sword⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “by the mouth of the sword”">j</a></sup>all who were in the city, both men and women, young and old, ox, sheep, and donkey.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:17; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> Then Joshua said to the two men who spied on the land, “Go to the prostitute’s house and bring out from there the woman and all who belong to her, just as you swore to her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; Genesis 18:24; Acts 27:24"> So the young men who were spies went and brought Rahab and her father and mother, her brothers, and all who were with her. And they brought all her family out and set them outside the camp of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 8:28; Revelation 17:16; 2 Kings 25:9"> And they burned the city and all that was in it with fire; they put only the silver and gold, and the items of copper and iron, into the treasury of the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Matthew 1:5; Judges 1:24–25"> But Joshua spared Rahab the prostitute ⸤and her family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “and the house of her father”">k</a></sup>and all who were with her, and she has lived in the midst of Israel until this day, because she hid the messengers whom Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 16:34; Acts 19:13; Numbers 5:19–21"> And Joshua swore at that time, saying, “Cursed is anyone before Yahweh who gets up and builds Jericho, this city. At the cost of his firstborn he will lay its foundation, and at the cost of his youngest he will set up its gates.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 9:3; Joshua 1:5; Matthew 28:20"> So Yahweh was with Joshua, and his fame was in all the land.</span>
</div>
</div>
