## Ai Is Destroyed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 7:18; Deuteronomy 1:21"> Then Yahweh said to Joshua, “Do not fear or be dismayed. Take ⸤all the fighting men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “all the people of war”">a</a></sup>with you ⸤and go up immediately to Ai⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “get up and go up to Ai”">b</a></sup>Look, I am giving into your hand the king of Ai, his city, and his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 6:21; 2 Chronicles 20:22"> You will do to Ai and its king that which you did to Jericho and its king; you may take only its spoils<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “spoil”">c</a></sup>and livestock as booty for yourself. Set for yourself an ambush against the city from behind it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Matthew 25:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:2"> So Joshua and ⸤all the fighting men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “all the people of war”">d</a></sup>went up immediately to Ai. Joshua chose thirty thousand of the best fighting men and sent them by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:29; Joshua 8:16; Judges 20:36"> And he commanded them, saying, “Look, you are to lay an ambush against the city from behind. Do not go very far from the city and be ready.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 7:5; Matthew 10:16; Judges 20:31–33"> Then I and all of the people who are with me will approach the city. And when they go out to meet us ⸤as before⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “as that at the first occasion ”">e</a></sup>we will flee from them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “before their presence”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 20:32; Exodus 15:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> They will come out after us until we draw them away from the city, because they will think, ‘They are fleeing from us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “before our presence”">g</a></sup>⸤as before⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “as that at the first occasion ”">h</a></sup>So we will flee from them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “before their presence”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31; 2 Kings 5:1; Joshua 8:1"> Then you will rise up from the ambush and take possession of the city, for Yahweh your God will give it into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Joshua 1:16; Joshua 1:9"> And when you capture the city you will set it on fire as Yahweh commanded. Look, I have commanded you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 7:2; Genesis 12:8; Ezra 2:28"> So Joshua sent them out, and they went to the place of the ambush, and they sat between Bethel and Ai, to the west of Ai; but Joshua spent the night ⸤with the people⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “in the middle of the people”">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 6:12; Psalm 119:60; Genesis 22:3"> Joshua rose early in the morning and mustered the people, and he went up with the elders of Israel before the people of Ai.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “before the presence of the people of Ai”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> ⸤All the fighting men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “All the people of war”">l</a></sup>who were with him ⸤went up and drew near before the city⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “went up, drew near, and came before the city”">m</a></sup>and camped north of Ai; there was a valley between him and Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 8:2–3"> And he took about five thousand men and set them in ambush between Bethel and Ai, to the west of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:12; Joshua 8:8"> So they stationed the forces; all the army was north of the city while ⸤the rear guard was west⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “while its rear guard was west of the city”">n</a></sup>But Joshua went that night to the middle of the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:39; Judges 20:34–36; Deuteronomy 1:1"> When the king of Ai saw this, the men of the city hurried and rose early and went out to meet Israel for battle—he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="That is, the king">o</a></sup>and all his army—to the meeting place before the Arabah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="A dry region that runs south of the Sea of Galilee along the Jordan Valley">p</a></sup>He did not know that there was an ambush for him behind the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 18:12; Joshua 16:1; Judges 20:36"> Then Joshua and all Israel acted like they were beaten before them, and they fled ⸤in the direction of the wilderness⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the way of the wilderness”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 20:31; Judges 20:36–39; Joshua 8:5–6"> All of the people who were in the city were called to pursue after them. As they pursued after Joshua, they were drawn away from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 5:13; Joshua 8:3; Isaiah 19:11–13"> Not a man remained in Ai or Bethel who had not gone out after Israel; they left the city open and pursued after Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 8:26; Job 15:25; 1 Samuel 17:41"> And Yahweh said to Joshua, “Stretch out the sword<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “spear”">r</a></sup>that is in your hand to Ai, because I will give it into your hand.” And Joshua stretched out the sword that was in his hand to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The moment he stretched out his hand, those in the ambush stood up quickly from their place and ran. And they went into the city and captured it, quickly setting the city ablaze with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 76:5; Job 11:20; Genesis 19:28"> Then the men of Ai looked behind them, and they saw smoke from the city rising to the sky; ⸤they had no power to flee this way or that⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “it was not in their hands to flee here and here”">s</a></sup>and the people fleeing the wilderness turned around to the pursuers.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “pursuer”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had captured the city and that the smoke of the city was rising; they returned and struck down the men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 10:28; Joshua 6:21"> Then the others from the city came out to meet them, ⸤and they found themselves surrounded by Israel⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “they were in the middle of Israel”">u</a></sup>⸤some on one side, and others on the other side⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “these from these, and these from this”">v</a></sup>And they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="That is, Israel">w</a></sup>struck them down until no survivor or fugitive was left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:8; Joshua 10:17; Revelation 19:20"> But they captured the king of Ai alive, and they brought him to Joshua.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 11:10–14; Joshua 10:30–41; Numbers 21:24"> When Israel finished slaughtering all the inhabitants of Ai in the open field, in the wilderness where they pursued them, and when all of them had fallen by ⸤the edge of the sword⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword”">x</a></sup>until they all had perished, all Israel returned to Ai and attacked it with ⸤the edge of the sword⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> All the people that fell on that day, both men and women, were twelve thousand—all the inhabitants of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:11–12; Joshua 8:18"> For Joshua did not draw back his hand that was stretched out with the sword until he had utterly destroyed all the inhabitants of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; Numbers 31:26; Psalm 50:10"> Only the livestock and the spoil of that city Israel took as booty for themselves, according to the word of Yahweh that Joshua commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:16; Jeremiah 49:2; 2 Kings 19:25"> So Joshua burned Ai and made it an everlasting heap of rubbish, a desolate place until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 7:26; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:22–23"> The king of Ai he hanged on a tree until the time of evening, and as the sun went down Joshua commanded them, and they brought down his dead body from the tree. Then they threw it at the entrance of the gate of the city, and they raised over it a great heap of stones that remains to this day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel Renews the Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:4–6; Exodus 20:24; Genesis 12:7–8"> Then Joshua built an altar on Mount Ebal for Yahweh the God of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–7; Exodus 20:24–25; Joshua 8:34–35"> as Moses Yahweh’s servant commanded the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">z</a></sup>as it is written in the scroll of the law of Moses: “an altar of unhewn<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “whole”">aa</a></sup>stones on which no one has ⸤wielded⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “waved over them”">ab</a></sup>an iron implement.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="See Exod 20:25">ac</a></sup>And they offered burnt offerings on it and sacrificed fellowship offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:2–3; Deuteronomy 27:8"> And there Joshua wrote on the stones a copy of the law of Moses, which he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="That is, Moses">ad</a></sup>had written, in the presence of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:29; Deuteronomy 31:12; Deuteronomy 31:9"> Then all Israel, ⸤foreigner as well as native⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “as the alien as the native”">af</a></sup>with the elders, officials, and judges stood ⸤on either side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “from this and from this”">ag</a></sup>of the ark before the priests and the Levites, who carried the ark of the covenant of Yahweh. Half of them stood in front of Mount Gerizim, and the other half in front of Mount Ebal, as Moses Yahweh’s servant had commanded before to bless the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:2–3; Nehemiah 13:1; Deuteronomy 31:10–12"> And afterward he read all the words of the law, the blessings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Hebrew “blessing”">ah</a></sup>and the curses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “curse”">ai</a></sup>according to all that was written in the scroll of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:12; Nehemiah 8:2; Jeremiah 26:2"> There was not a word from all that Moses commanded that Joshua did not read before the assembly of all Israel, and the women, the little children, and the traveling foreigners<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “foreigner”">aj</a></sup>among them.</span>
</div>
