## The Eastern Tribes Return

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 32:18–33; Deuteronomy 29:7–8"> Then Joshua summoned the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:12–18; Deuteronomy 3:16–20; Numbers 32:20–29"> and he said to them, “You have observed all that Moses Yahweh’s servant commanded you, and you have listened<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “you have obeyed”">a</a></sup>to my voice in all that I have commanded you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:23–27"> you have not forsaken your kinsmen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “brothers”">b</a></sup>these many days, up to this day, and you have observed the obligation of the command of Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Numbers 32:18; Deuteronomy 3:20"> So then, Yahweh your God has given rest to your kinsmen,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “brothers”">c</a></sup>just as he promised them; so then, turn and go to your tents to the land of your possession, which Moses Yahweh’s servant gave to you beyond the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; Exodus 20:6; Romans 8:28"> Only be very careful to observe the commandment and law that Moses Yahweh’s servant commanded you, to love Yahweh your God, to walk in all his ways, to keep his commandments, to hold fast to him, and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “inner self”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 14:13; Luke 24:50; Genesis 47:7"> And Joshua blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–12; Numbers 32:33; Joshua 13:29–31"> And to the half-tribe of Manasseh Moses had given a possession in Bashan, but to the other half Joshua had given a possession with their kinsmen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “brothers”">e</a></sup>beyond the Jordan to the west; and when Joshua sent them to their tents and blessed them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 31:27; Deuteronomy 8:9–14; Proverbs 3:16"> he said to them, “Return to your tents with much wealth, and with very much livestock, with silver, gold, copper, iron, and with very much clothing; divide the war-booty of your enemies with your kinsmen.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “brothers”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Numbers 32:26; Numbers 32:1"> So the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “sons”">g</a></sup>of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh returned home and departed with the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan, to go to the land of Gilead to the land of their possession, which they had acquired ⸤according to the command of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “on the mouth of Yahweh”">i</a></sup>through the hand of Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:26–27; Genesis 31:46–52; Joshua 4:5–9"> And they came to the region of the Jordan that is in the land of Canaan, and the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “sons”">j</a></sup>of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh built there an altar on the Jordan, ⸤a large and imposing altar⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “a large altar to appearance”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:12–14; Leviticus 17:8–9; Joshua 2:7"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup>heard it said that the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “sons”">m</a></sup>of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh had built an altar next to the land of Canaan, in the region of the Jordan, on the side belonging to the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 20:1–11; Deuteronomy 13:15; Galatians 4:17–18"> When the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>heard of it, the whole congregation of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">p</a></sup>gathered at Shiloh to go up against them for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 25:7; Psalm 106:30–31; Exodus 6:25"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>sent to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “sons”">r</a></sup>of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, to the land of Gilead, Phinehas the priest son of Eleazar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:4; Exodus 18:25"> and ten leaders with him, ⸤one leader for each⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “one leader, one leader”">s</a></sup>⸤family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “of the house of father”">t</a></sup>from each of the tribes of Israel; and each one was the head of ⸤his family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “house of their fathers”">u</a></sup>among the clans of Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “thousands of Israel”">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:3; 1 Samuel 15:23; Deuteronomy 30:17"> They came to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “sons”">w</a></sup>of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, to the land of Gilead, and they spoke with them, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:13–14"> “Thus says all the congregation of Yahweh: ‘What is this treachery that you have committed against the God of Israel by turning away today from following Yahweh, by building for yourselves an altar to rebel today against Yahweh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–18; Psalm 106:28–29; Deuteronomy 4:3–4"> Is not the sin of Peor ⸤enough for us⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “too little for us”">x</a></sup>from which we have not cleansed ourselves today, and for which a plague came to the congregation of Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Joshua 22:16; Joshua 7:11"> that you must turn today from following Yahweh? If you rebel today against Yahweh, tomorrow he will be angry with all of the congregation of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Acts 10:14–15"> if, however, the land of your property is unclean, cross over to the land of Yahweh’s property, where Yahweh’s tabernacle resides,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “stands”">y</a></sup>and take possession among us. But you must not rebel against Yahweh or against us by building for yourselves an altar other than the altar of Yahweh our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 7:1; Joshua 7:5; Joshua 7:18"> Did not Achan son of Zerah commit treachery with devoted things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “consecrated possession”">z</a></sup>and wrath fell on all the congregation of Israel? ⸤And he alone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “And he is one man that”">aa</a></sup>did not perish because of his iniquity.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 16:1; 1 Peter 3:15"> And the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “sons”">ab</a></sup>of Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh spoke with the heads of the clans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “thousands”">ac</a></sup>of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 8:39; Psalm 44:21; Deuteronomy 10:17"> “Yahweh, God of gods! Yahweh, God of gods knows. And let Israel itself know, if it was in rebellion or treachery against Yahweh, do not spare us this day</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16; Deuteronomy 18:19; Ezekiel 3:18"> for building for ourselves an altar to turn away from Yahweh, or if it was to offer burnt offerings,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Hebrew “offering”">ad</a></sup>grain offerings,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Hebrew “offering”">ae</a></sup>or fellowship offerings on it, may Yahweh himself take vengeance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 4:6; Deuteronomy 6:20; Genesis 30:33"> But in fact, we have done this because of anxiety, because of a reason, saying, ‘In the future your children may say to our children, ‘⸤What is the relationship between you and Yahweh the God of Israel⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “What is to you and to Yahweh the God of Israel”">af</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 12:16; Ezra 4:2–3; 1 Samuel 26:19"> Yahweh has made the Jordan a border between us and you, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “sons”">ag</a></sup>of Reuben and Gad; you have no portion in Yahweh.’ So your children may put an end to our children worshiping<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “seeing”">ah</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So we said, ‘Let us build immediately for ourselves an altar, not for burnt offerings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “offering”">ai</a></sup>or for sacrifices;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “sacrifice”">aj</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 24:27; Genesis 31:48; Deuteronomy 12:17–18"> instead, it is a witness between us and you, and between our generations after us for performing the serving of Yahweh in his presence with our burnt offerings, sacrifices, and fellowship offerings; so that your children may not say in the future to our children, “You have no portion in Yahweh.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Ezekiel 43:10–11; Hebrews 8:5"> And we thought, if they say to us and to our children in the future, we can say, ‘Look at this replica of the altar of Yahweh, which our ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “fathers”">ak</a></sup>made, not for burnt offerings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “offering”">al</a></sup>or sacrifices;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Hebrew “sacrifice”">am</a></sup>rather, it is a witness between us and you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 24:16; Deuteronomy 12:13–14; Joshua 22:23"> Far be it from us to rebel against Yahweh, to turn today from following Yahweh, to build an altar for burnt offerings,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Hebrew “offering”">an</a></sup>grain offerings,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “offering”">ao</a></sup>or sacrifices,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Hebrew “sacrifice”">ap</a></sup>instead of the altar of Yahweh our God that is before his tabernacle.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:33; Esther 1:21; Proverbs 15:1"> Phinehas the priest, the leaders of the congregation, and the heads of the clans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “thousands”">aq</a></sup>of Israel who were with him heard the words that the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “sons”">ar</a></sup>of Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh spoke, and ⸤they were satisfied⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “it was good in their eyes”">as</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Leviticus 26:11–12; Isaiah 12:6"> Phinehas the priest, son of Eleazar, said to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “sons”">at</a></sup>of Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh, “Today we know that Yahweh is among us, because you have not committed this treachery against Yahweh. Therefore you have rescued the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">au</a></sup>from the hand of Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 22:12–14; Proverbs 25:13"> And Phinehas the priest, son of Eleazar, and the leaders returned from the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Or “sons”">av</a></sup>of Reuben and Gad, from the land of Gilead, to the land of Canaan to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">aw</a></sup>and ⸤they gave them their report⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Literally “they brought back a word to them”">ax</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:20; Luke 2:28; Daniel 2:19"> ⸤The report satisfied the Israelites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Literally “The report was good in the eyes of the children of Israel”">ay</a></sup>they blessed God, and they did not speak of going up for battle against them to destroy the land in which the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Or “sons”">az</a></sup>of Reuben and Gad were living.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Or “dwelling”">ba</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 22:27; Matthew 4:10; Isaiah 43:10"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Or “sons”">bb</a></sup>of Reuben and Gad called the altar Witness, “Because,” they said, “it is a witness between us that Yahweh is God.”</span>
