## The Sun Stands Still at Gibeon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Joshua 2:24; Exodus 15:14–16"> he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “they”">a</a></sup>became very afraid because Gibeon was a very large city, ⸤like one of the royal cities⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “like one of the cities of the kingship”">b</a></sup>and because it was larger than Ai, and all its men were mighty warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:9; Micah 1:13; Joshua 10:1"> So Adoni-Zedek king of Jerusalem sent word to Hohman king of Hebron, to Piram king of Jarmuth, to Japhia king of Lachish, and to Debir king of Eglon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 9:15; Joshua 10:1; Isaiah 8:9–10"> “Come up and help me, and let us attack Gibeon, because it has made peace with Joshua and the ⸤Israelites⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

Help us, for all the kings of the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">g</a></sup>who dwell in the hill country have gathered against us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 8:1; Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 8:14"> So Joshua went up from Gilgal, he and ⸤all the fighting men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “all the people of the war”">h</a></sup>with him, ⸤all the best warriors⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “all the mighty warriors of the army”">i</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 18:14; Joshua 16:3; Joshua 16:5"> And Yahweh threw them into panic before Israel, who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">l</a></sup>struck them with a great blow at Gibeon and pursued them by the way of the ascent of Beth-horon and struck them as far as Azekah and Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:2; Judges 5:20; Psalm 18:12–14"> And as they were fleeing from Israel, they were on the slope of Beth-horon, and Yahweh threw huge stones from the heavens on them as far as Azekah; and more died by the hail stones than those whom the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>killed by the sword.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; Exodus 14:14; Joshua 10:42"> There has not been a day like this before it or after, that Yahweh listened to the voice of a man; for Yahweh fought for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 10:43; Joshua 10:6"> And Joshua returned and all Israel with him to the camp at Gilgal.</span>

## The Kings of the Amorites Are Killed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And it was told to Joshua, saying, “The five kings were found hidden in the cave at Makkedah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Job 21:30; Amos 9:1"> And Joshua said, “Roll large stones against the mouth of the cave, and set men in front of it to guard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 18:37–41; Jeremiah 8:14; Isaiah 9:14–15"> But do not stay there; pursue after your enemies and attack them from the rear. Do not allow them to go into their cities, for Yahweh your God has given them into your hand.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 11:7; Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 57:4"> and all the people returned to the camp safely<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “in peace”">r</a></sup>to Joshua at Makkedah. ⸤No one spoke⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “No one moved his tongue”">s</a></sup>against the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:32"> And Joshua said, “Open the mouth of the cave, and bring to me those five kings from the cave.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:1; Joshua 10:5; Joshua 10:3"> And they did so, and brought him these five kings from the cave, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Romans 16:20; Revelation 2:26–27"> And when they brought these kings to Joshua, Joshua called all the men of Israel and said to the commanders of ⸤the fighting men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “the men of war”">u</a></sup>who had gone with him, “Come near, put your feet on the necks of these kings.” So they came near and put their feet on their necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10"> And Joshua said to them, “Do not be afraid or dismayed! Be strong and bold, for thus Yahweh will do to all your enemies whom you are about to fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 8:29; 2 Samuel 21:9; Matthew 27:25"> And after this Joshua struck them down and killed them, and he hanged them on five trees. And they were hanging on the trees until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:29; Deuteronomy 21:22–23; Joshua 7:26"> And it happened ⸤at the time of sunset⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “at the time of the going of the sun”">v</a></sup>Joshua commanded, and they took them down from the trees and threw them into the cave where they had hidden themselves, and they put large stones against the mouth of the cave, which are there to this very day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 7:16; Deuteronomy 20:16–17"> Joshua captured Makkedah on that day, and he struck it and its king with ⸤the edge of the sword⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword”">w</a></sup>he utterly destroyed it and everyone that was in it. He did not leave behind a survivor. So he did to the king of Makkedah just as he did to the king of Jericho.</span>
</div>

## Joshua’s Conquest of the South

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Yahweh also gave it into the hand of Israel, and its king and all the people in it he struck with ⸤the edge of the sword⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword”">x</a></sup>He left in it no survivor. <span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Joshua 11:16; Joshua 15:51; Deuteronomy 9:23"> Joshua struck them from Kadesh Barnea to Gaza, and all the land of Goshen up to Gibeon;</span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 10:15; Joshua 4:19; 1 Samuel 11:14"> And Joshua returned, and all Israel with him, to the camp at Gilgal.</span>
