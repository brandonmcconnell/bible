## Joshua’s Farewell Address

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 13:1; Joshua 21:44; Joshua 11:23"> And it happened, after ⸤a long time⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “many days”">a</a></sup>after Yahweh had given rest to Israel from all their surrounding enemies, and after Joshua was old and ⸤well-advanced in years⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “he went into the days”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; Deuteronomy 31:28; 1 Chronicles 28:1"> Joshua summoned all Israel, their elders, heads, judges, and officials, and he said to them, “I am old and ⸤well-advanced in years⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “I went into the days”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Joshua 10:42; Joshua 10:14"> and you have seen all that Yahweh your God has done to all these nations ⸤for your sake⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “because of your presence”">d</a></sup>for Yahweh your God is fighting for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 13:6–7; Joshua 18:10; Joshua 13:2"> Look! I have allotted to you these remaining nations as an inheritance for your tribes, from the Jordan, with all the nations that I have cut off, to the Great Sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean">e</a></sup>⸤in the west⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to the setting of the sun”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:23; Joshua 13:6; Exodus 33:2"> And Yahweh your God will push them ⸤back before you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from your presence”">g</a></sup>and drive them ⸤out of your sight⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “from your face”">h</a></sup>and you will possess their land, just as Yahweh your God promised to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Deuteronomy 28:14; Proverbs 4:26–27"> Be very strong to observe carefully all that is written in the scroll of the law of Moses so as not to turn aside from it, to the right or left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Psalm 16:4; Deuteronomy 7:2–3"> so as not to go among these remaining nations with you; ⸤do not profess⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “do not mention”">i</a></sup>the name of their gods, and do not swear by them, serve them, or ⸤bow down to them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “bow yourselves down to them”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 22:5; Deuteronomy 13:4"> But hold fast to Yahweh your God, just as you have done up to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 11:23; Joshua 23:5"> Yahweh has driven out before you great and strong nations; and as for you, ⸤nobody has withstood⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “a man has not stood in your presence”">k</a></sup>you to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Leviticus 26:8; Deuteronomy 3:22"> One of your men put to flight a thousand, for Yahweh your God is fighting for you, just as he promised you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 22:5; Deuteronomy 6:5–12; Proverbs 4:23"> Take utmost care for the sake of your life to love Yahweh your God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Ezekiel 18:24; Zephaniah 1:6"> for if indeed you turn back and join these remaining nations ⸤among you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “with you”">l</a></sup>and you intermarry with them, ⸤marrying their women and they yours⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “you go into them and they into you”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Numbers 33:55; Exodus 23:33"> know for certain that Yahweh your God ⸤will not continue to drive out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “will not drive out again”">n</a></sup>these nations from before you; they will be for you a snare and a trap, a whip on your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from this good land that Yahweh your God has given to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 21:33; 1 Kings 2:2; Leviticus 26:3–13"> Look! ⸤I am about to die⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “I am going today on the way of all the earth”">o</a></sup>and you know in all your hearts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “heart”">p</a></sup>and souls<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “soul”; or “inner self”">q</a></sup>that not one thing ⸤failed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “fell”">r</a></sup>from all the good things that Yahweh your God promised concerning you; everything ⸤has been fulfilled⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “has come out ”">s</a></sup>⸤not one thing failed⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “not one thing fell from it”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Leviticus 26:14–46; Judges 3:8"> But just as all the good things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “thing”">u</a></sup>came to you that Yahweh your God promised, so will Yahweh bring to you all the bad things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “things”">v</a></sup>until he has destroyed you from this good land that Yahweh your God has given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:25–26; 2 Kings 24:20"> If you transgress the covenant of Yahweh your God, which he commanded to you, and you go and serve other gods and bow down to them, ⸤Yahweh’s anger will be kindled⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “Yahweh’s nose will become hot”">w</a></sup>against you, and you will perish quickly from the good land that he has given to you.”</span>
</div>
</div>
