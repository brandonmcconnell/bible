<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 13:11; 1 Samuel 27:8; Joel 3:4"> This is the remaining land: all the regions of the Philistines, and all of the Geshurites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “Geshurite”">c</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 11:8; Joshua 23:13; Judges 2:21–23"> all the inhabitants of the hill country, from the Lebanon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “white mountain”">o</a></sup>up to Misrephoth Maim, and all the Sidonians. <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 12:4; Joshua 14:3–4; Numbers 21:23–35"> all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Ashtaroth and Edrei—he was left over from the survivors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “survivor”">z</a></sup>of the Rephaim; these Moses had defeated and driven out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 23:12–13; Joshua 13:11; Numbers 33:55"> But the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">aa</a></sup>did not drive out the Geshurites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “Geshurite”">ab</a></sup>or the Maacathites;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Hebrew “Maacathite”">ac</a></sup>Geshur and Maacah live among Israel to this day.</span>

## Reuben’s Inheritance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “sons”">ag</a></sup>of Reuben according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Joshua 12:2; Isaiah 16:7–9"> Their territory was from Aroer, which was on the edge of the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">ah</a></sup>of Arnon, and the city that is in the middle of the valley, and all the plateau by Medeba;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 22:41; Numbers 32:38; Numbers 21:19"> Heshbon and its cities that are on the plateau; Dibon, Bamoth Baal, Beth Baal Meon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 21:23; Joshua 21:36–37; 1 Chronicles 6:78–79"> Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 32:37–38"> Kiriathaim, Sibmah, and Zereth Shahar on the hill of the valley;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 12:3; Deuteronomy 4:46; Ezekiel 25:9"> Beth Peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth Jeshimoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Deuteronomy 3:10; Deuteronomy 2:30–36"> all of the cities of the plateau, and all the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">ai</a></sup>who reigned in Heshbon and whom Moses defeated with the leaders of Midian, Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the princes of Sihon who dwelled in the land.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the border of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “sons”">ak</a></sup>of Reuben was the Jordan and its banks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “bank”">al</a></sup>This was the inheritance of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “sons”">am</a></sup>of Reuben according to their families, the cities, and their villages.</span>

## The Half-Tribe of Manasseh’s Inheritance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Moses gave an inheritance to the half-tribe of Manasseh; it was for the half-tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “sons”">at</a></sup>of Manasseh according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; Deuteronomy 3:13–15; Numbers 32:39–41"> Their territory was from Mahanaim, all Bashan, all the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all the settlements<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Or “tent villages”">au</a></sup>of Jair, which are in Bashan, sixty cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 12:4; Numbers 32:39–40"> and half of Gilead, with Ashtaroth, Edrei, and the cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan; these were allotted to the children of Makir son of Manasseh, for half of the children of Makir according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> These are the territories that Moses gave as an inheritance on the desert-plateau of Moab, beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 18:7; Joshua 13:14; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> But to the tribe of Levi Moses did not give an inheritance; Yahweh the God of Israel, he is their inheritance, just as ⸤he promised them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “he said to them”">av</a></sup></span>
