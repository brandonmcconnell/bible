## Joshua Recounts Their History

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 23:2; 1 Samuel 10:19; Genesis 33:18–19"> And Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem; he summoned the elders of Israel, their heads, their judges, and their officials, and they presented themselves before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:15; Ezekiel 16:3; Genesis 31:30"> And Joshua said to all the people, “Thus says Yahweh the God of Israel: ‘⸤Long ago⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “from ancient”">a</a></sup>your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “fathers”">b</a></sup>—Terah the father of Abraham and the father of Nahor—lived beyond the river,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Euphrates">c</a></sup>and they served other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Genesis 21:2–3; Acts 7:2–3"> I took your ancestor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “father”">d</a></sup>Abraham from beyond the river<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, the Euphrates">e</a></sup>and led him through all the land of Canaan, and I increased his offspring; I gave him Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Deuteronomy 2:5; Genesis 25:24–26"> and to Isaac I gave Jacob and Esau. To Esau I gave the hill country of Seir to possess, but Jacob and his children went down to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 7:1–12; Psalm 105:26–36"> And I sent Moses and Aaron, and I plagued Egypt with what I did in its midst; and afterward I brought you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Hebrews 11:29; Isaiah 63:12–13"> When I brought out your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “fathers”">f</a></sup>from Egypt, you came to the sea, and the Egyptians pursued after your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup>with chariots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">h</a></sup>and horsemen to the ⸤Red Sea⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sea of reed”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Exodus 14:10; Exodus 14:20"> They cried out to Yahweh, and he put darkness between you and the Egyptians, and he brought the sea over them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="That is, the Egyptians">j</a></sup>and covered them; your own eyes saw what I did in Egypt. Then you lived in the wilderness for many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Psalm 135:10–11; Nehemiah 9:22"> And I brought you to the land of the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">k</a></sup>who lived beyond the Jordan; they fought you, and I gave them into your hand; you took possession of their land, and I destroyed them ⸤before you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “in your presence”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Micah 6:5; Numbers 22:2"> Then Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, set out and fought against Israel, and he sent and summoned Balaam son of Beor to curse you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 24:5–10; Isaiah 54:17"> but I was not willing to listen to Balaam, and he richly blessed you. So I rescued you from his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 4:10–12; Joshua 3:14–17; Psalm 114:5"> and you crossed the Jordan and came to Jericho. And the citizens of Jericho, the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">m</a></sup>the Perizzites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">n</a></sup>the Canaanites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">o</a></sup>the Hittites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">p</a></sup>the Girgashites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “Girgashite”">q</a></sup>the Hivites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">r</a></sup>and the Jebusites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">s</a></sup>fought against you, and I gave them into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Exodus 23:28; Psalm 44:3–6"> I sent before you the hornet and they drove out before you two kings of the Amorites;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">t</a></sup>but not by your sword or bow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Deuteronomy 8:7; Joshua 21:45"> I gave to you a land that you have not labored on, and cities that you have not built, and you live<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “dwell”">u</a></sup>in them; you eat from vineyards and olive groves that you have not planted.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Israelites Promise to Serve Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Deuteronomy 10:12; Genesis 35:2"> “So now, revere Yahweh and serve him in sincerity and faithfulness; remove the gods that your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “fathers”">v</a></sup>served beyond the river<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="That is, the Euphrates">w</a></sup>and in Egypt, and serve Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; John 6:67–68; Ruth 1:15–16"> But if it is bad in your eyes to serve Yahweh, choose for yourselves today whom you want to serve, whether it is the gods that your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “fathers”">x</a></sup>served beyond the river,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="That is, the Euphrates">y</a></sup>or the gods of the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">z</a></sup>in whose land you are living; but as for me and my household, we will serve Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 10:38–39; 1 Samuel 12:23; Romans 6:2"> And the people answered and said, “Far be it from us that we would forsake Yahweh to serve other gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 63:7–14; Isaiah 46:4"> for Yahweh our God brought us and our ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “fathers”">aa</a></sup>from the land of Egypt, from the house of slavery, and did these great signs before our eyes. He protected us along the entire way that we went, and among all the peoples through whose midst we passed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 4:2; Exodus 10:2; Psalm 116:16"> And Yahweh drove out all the people before us, the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="“Hebrew “Amorite”">ab</a></sup>who live<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “dwell”">ac</a></sup>in the land. We will serve Yahweh, for he is our God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 5:16; Exodus 23:21; Psalm 99:9"> But Joshua said to the people, “You cannot serve Yahweh, for he is a holy and jealous God; he will not forgive your transgressions or your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–27; Ezekiel 18:24; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> If you forsake Yahweh and serve foreign gods, he will turn and bring disaster to you; he will destroy you after he has done good to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 44:5; Deuteronomy 5:27–28"> And the people said to Joshua, “No, we will serve Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 119:173; Psalm 119:11; Job 15:6"> And Joshua said to the people, “You are witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen for yourselves to serve Yahweh.” And they said, “We are witnesses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16–18; Joshua 24:14; Hebrews 12:28–29"> He said, “Remove the foreign gods that are in your midst, and incline your hearts to Yahweh the God of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Deuteronomy 5:27–29"> And the people said to Joshua, “We will serve Yahweh our God, and we will listen to his voice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:38; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Exodus 15:25"> So Joshua ⸤made a covenant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “cut a covenant”">ad</a></sup>with the people on that day, and he established for them a statute and a judgment at Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Genesis 35:4; Joshua 4:20–24"> Then Joshua wrote these words in a scroll of the law of God, and he took a large stone and set it up there under a large tree, which is at the shrine of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 22:34; Joshua 22:27–28"> And Joshua said to all the people, “Look, this stone will be a witness against us, for it has heard all the words of Yahweh that he spoke with us. It will be as a witness against you, so that you do not deny your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 2:6"> Then Joshua sent the people away to their inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:8; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; Deuteronomy 34:5"> After these things Joshua son of Nun servant of Yahweh died; ⸤he was one hundred and ten years old⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “a son of one hundred and ten years”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:50; Judges 2:9; 2 Samuel 23:30"> They buried him in the territory of his inheritance, at Timnath-Serah, which is in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 2:7; Deuteronomy 31:13; Deuteronomy 11:2"> Israel served Yahweh all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders who lived long after Joshua, and who had known all the work that Yahweh did for Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Joshua 24:28–31 is repeated in Judges 2:6–10">af</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 50:25; Genesis 33:19; Exodus 13:19"> The bones of Jacob, which the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ag</a></sup>had brought out from Egypt, they buried at Shechem, in a piece of land that Jacob had bought from the children of Hamor, the father of Shechem, for one hundred pieces of money;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Hebrew kesitah">ah</a></sup>it became an inheritance for the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “sons”">ai</a></sup>of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 22:13; Exodus 6:25; Isaiah 57:1–2"> And Eleazar son of Aaron died; and they buried him in Gibeah in the hill country of Ephraim, which had been given to his son Phinehas.</span>
</div>
</div>
