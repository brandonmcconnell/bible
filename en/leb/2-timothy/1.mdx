## Timothy’s Spiritual Heritage

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Hebrews 13:8; Acts 23:1"> ⸤I am thankful⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “I have thankfulness”">a</a></sup>to God, whom I have served with a clear conscience ⸤as my ancestors did⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “from ancestors”">b</a></sup>, when ⸤I remember you constantly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “I have remembrance concerning you constantly”">c</a></sup>in my prayers night and day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 1:8; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–1"> longing to see you as I remember your tears, so that I may be filled with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Acts 16:1; 2 Timothy 3:15"> ⸤remembering the⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “receiving a remembrance of the”">d</a></sup>sincere faith in you, which lived first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am convinced that is in you also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8"> for which reason I remind you to rekindle the gift of God that is in you through the laying on of my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:18; Galatians 5:22"> For God has not given us a spirit of cowardice, but of power and love and self-discipline.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Exhortation to Share Paul’s Suffering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 1:16; Mark 8:38; 1 Peter 4:13–15"> Therefore, do not be ashamed of the testimony about our Lord, nor me his prisoner, but suffer along with me for the gospel, according to the power of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; Titus 3:4–5; Ephesians 1:3–4"> who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works but according to his own purpose and grace that was given to us in Christ Jesus ⸤before time began⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “before times eternal”">e</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:2; Romans 16:26; 2 Peter 3:18"> but has now been disclosed by the appearing of our Savior Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “our Savior Christ Jesus”">f</a></sup>who has abolished death and brought to light life and immortality through the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:7–8; Acts 9:15"> for which I was appointed a proclaimer and an apostle and a teacher,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; Jude 1:24; 1 Peter 4:19"> for which reason also I suffer these things. But I am not ashamed, because I know in whom I have believed, and I am convinced that he is able to guard what I have entrusted until that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 1:9; 2 Timothy 3:14; Philippians 4:9"> Hold fast to the pattern of sound words which you heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:17; Romans 8:13; 1 Timothy 6:20"> Guard the good deposit through the Holy Spirit who lives in us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:16; Philippians 2:21; Acts 19:10"> You know this, that all those in Asia turned away from me, among whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:19; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he refreshed me many times, and was not ashamed of my imprisonment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 28:30–31"> but when he was in Rome, he diligently sought me and found me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:12; 1 Timothy 1:3"> May the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord in that day! And how much he served me in Ephesus you know very well.</span>
</div>
</div>
