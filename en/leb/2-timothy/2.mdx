## Exhortation to Be Strong in Grace

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Ephesians 6:10; 2 Timothy 1:7"> You, therefore, my child, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13–14; 1 Timothy 4:6; 1 Timothy 6:12"> and the things which you have heard from me in the presence of many witnesses, entrust these things to faithful people who will be competent to teach others also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2–3; James 1:12; 2 Timothy 4:5"> Suffer together with me as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 8:14; 2 Peter 2:20"> No one who serves as a soldier is entangled in the activities of everyday life, so that he may please the one who enlisted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Revelation 3:11; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> And also if anyone competes he is not crowned unless he competes according to the rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; 1 Corinthians 9:7–11; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> The farmer who works hard must be the first to receive a share of the crops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:5; 1 John 5:20; Luke 24:45"> Consider what I am saying, for the Lord will grant you understanding in all these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Trustworthy Saying

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Romans 2:16; 1 Corinthians 15:11–20"> Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, a descendant of David according to my gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Acts 28:31; 2 Timothy 1:8"> in connection with which I suffer misfortune to the point of ⸤imprisonment⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “bonds”">a</a></sup>as a criminal, but the word of God is not bound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 2:3"> Because of this, I endure all things for the sake of the chosen, in order that they also may obtain salvation which is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:8; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:5"> The saying is trustworthy:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">For if we died with him, we will also live with him;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Matthew 10:33; Revelation 20:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">if we endure, we will also reign with him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">if we deny him, he also will deny us;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 3:3; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">if we are unfaithful, he remains faithful—⸤he cannot deny himself⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “he is not able to deny himself”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “because he is not able to deny himself”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## A Worker Approved to God and Not Ashamed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:9–11; 2 Timothy 2:23; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Remind people of these things, solemnly urging them before the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “before God”">d</a></sup>not to dispute about words. This is in no way beneficial and leads to the ruin of the hearers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Timothy 4:6"> Make every effort to present yourself approved to God, a worker having no need to be ashamed, guiding the word of truth along a straight path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:14"> But avoid pointless chatter, for it will progress to greater ungodliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; Nahum 3:15; James 5:3"> and their message ⸤will spread⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “will experience spreading”">e</a></sup>like gangrene, among whom are Hymenaeus and Philetus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12; 1 Timothy 1:19; Hebrews 3:10"> who have deviated concerning the truth by saying the resurrection has already taken place, and they are upsetting the faith of some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 10:14; 1 Corinthians 8:3; Nahum 1:7"> However, the solid foundation of God stands firm, having this seal: “The Lord knows those who are his,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Num 16:5">f</a></sup>and “Everyone who names the name of the Lord must abstain from unrighteousness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:21–23; 1 Peter 2:5; 1 Timothy 3:15"> Now in a great house there are not only gold and silver vessels, but also wooden and earthenware ones, some of which are for honorable use, and some of which are for ordinary use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:17; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> Therefore, if someone cleanses himself from these things, he will be a vessel for honorable use, set apart, useful to the Master, prepared for every good work.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Peter 3:11; 1 Corinthians 6:18"> But flee from youthful desires, and pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace, in company with those who call upon the Lord from a pure heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; Titus 3:9; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But avoid foolish and uninformed controversies, because you know that they produce quarrels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Titus 3:2; James 1:19–20; 1 Timothy 3:2–3"> And the slave of the Lord must not quarrel, but be kind toward everyone, skillful in teaching, tolerant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 11:18; Galatians 6:1; 1 Timothy 2:4"> correcting those who are opposed with gentleness, seeing whether perhaps God may grant them repentance to a knowledge of the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; Psalm 124:7"> and they will come to their senses again and escape from the trap of the devil, being held captive by him to do his will.</span>
</div>
</div>
