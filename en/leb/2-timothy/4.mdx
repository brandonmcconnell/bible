## Paul’s Charge to Timothy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Timothy 5:21"> I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is going to judge the living and the dead, and by his appearing and his kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Timothy 4:15–16; Titus 2:15"> preach the word, be ready in season and out of season, reprove, rebuke, exhort, with all patience and instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 Timothy 3:1–6; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> For there will be a time when they will not put up with sound teaching, but in accordance with their own desires, they will accumulate for themselves teachers, ⸤because they have an insatiable curiosity⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “feeling an itching in the ear”; “because” is supplied as a component of the participle (“feeling an itching”) which is understood as causal">a</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; 1 Timothy 1:4; Acts 7:57"> and they will turn away from the hearing of the truth, but will turn to myths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 1:13; 2 Timothy 2:3"> But you, be self-controlled in all things, bear hardship patiently, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your ministry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; Philippians 2:17; 2 Peter 1:14–15"> For I am already being poured out as a drink offering, and the time of my departure is imminent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Hebrews 12:1–2"> I have fought the good fight, I have completed the race, I have kept the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:12; Revelation 2:10; 1 Peter 5:4"> Finally, the crown of righteousness is reserved for me, that the Lord, the righteous Judge, will award to me on that day, and not only to me, but also to all who have loved his appearing.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Instructions to Timothy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 2 Timothy 4:21"> Make haste to come to me quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 2 Timothy 1:15; Colossians 4:14–15"> For Demas deserted me, because he loved the present age, and went to Thessalonica. Crescens went to Galatia; Titus went to Dalmatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 12:25"> Luke alone is with me. Take along Mark and bring him with you, because he is useful to me for ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21; Acts 20:4; Titus 3:12"> But I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:11; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 16:11"> When you come, bring the cloak that I left behind in Troas with Carpus, and the scrolls, especially the parchments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:6; Psalm 28:4"> Alexander the metalworker did me much harm; may the Lord pay back to him according to his deeds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Philippians 3:2; Matthew 10:16–17"> against whom you also be on guard, because he vehemently opposed our words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:60; John 16:32; Mark 14:50"> At my first defense, no one came to my aid, but they all deserted me; may it not be counted against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Philippians 1:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:37"> But the Lord helped me and strengthened me, so that through me the proclamation might be fulfilled and all the Gentiles might hear, and he rescued me from the lion’s mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 121:7; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; Jude 1:24–25"> The Lord will rescue me from every evil deed, and will save me for his heavenly kingdom, to whom be the glory ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to the ages of the ages”">b</a></sup>. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings and Benediction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19; Romans 16:3–4"> Greet Prisca and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 19:22; Philippians 2:26–27"> Erastus remained in Corinth, but Trophimus I left behind in Miletus because he was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 4:22; 3 John 1:14"> Make haste to come before winter. Eubulus and Pudens and Linus and Claudia and all the brothers greet you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philemon 1:25; Galatians 6:18; Colossians 4:18"> The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you.</span>
</div>
</div>
