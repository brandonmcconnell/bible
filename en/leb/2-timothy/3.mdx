## Difficult Times Ahead in the Last Days

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> But know this, that in the last days difficult times will come,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:21; Romans 1:29–31; Jude 1:16"> for people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boasters, arrogant, slanderers, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, unholy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; Romans 1:31; Matthew 10:21"> hardhearted, irreconcilable, slanderous, without self-control, savage, with no interest for what is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; 1 Timothy 3:6; Jude 1:4"> traitors, reckless, conceited, loving pleasure rather than loving God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Titus 1:16; Matthew 23:27–28"> maintaining a form of godliness, but denying its power. Avoid these people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 3:17; Titus 3:3"> For from these are those who slip into houses and captivate foolish women loaded down with sins, led by various kinds of desires,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 2:4"> always learning and never able to come to a knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:8–11; Revelation 2:20; 1 Timothy 6:5"> And ⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “in the manner in which”">a</a></sup>Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so also these oppose the truth, people corrupted in mind, disqualified concerning the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Exodus 7:12; Jeremiah 28:15–17"> But they will not progress to a greater extent, for their folly will be quite evident to everyone, as also the folly of those two was.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Value of the Scriptures

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11; Titus 2:7; 2 Peter 1:5–7"> But you have faithfully followed my teaching, way of life, purpose, faith, patience, love, endurance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 34:19; Acts 14:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28"> persecutions, and sufferings that happened to me in Antioch, in Iconium, and in Lystra, what sort of persecutions I endured, and the Lord delivered me from all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:12–16; 1 Peter 2:20–21"> And indeed, all those who want to live in a godly manner in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1; 2 Peter 2:20; Titus 3:3"> But evil people and imposters will progress to the worse, deceiving and being deceived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 2 Timothy 2:2; 2 Timothy 1:13"> But you continue in the things which you have learned and are convinced of, because you know from whom you learned them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 119:98–99; Luke 24:45; Proverbs 22:6"> and that from childhood you have known the holy writings that are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Hebrews 4:12; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> All scripture is inspired by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, for training in righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:21; Ephesians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> in order that the person of God may be competent, equipped for every good work.</span>
</div>
</div>
