## Samson Defeats the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 38:17; Genesis 29:21; Luke 15:29"> After a while, at the time of the wheat harvest, Samson visited his wife with a ⸤young goat⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “kid of goat”">a</a></sup>He said, “I want to go to my wife’s private room.” But her father would not allow him to enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 14:20; Judges 14:16; Genesis 38:14"> Her father said, “I really thought that you hated her, so I gave her to your companion. Is not her younger sister more beautiful than she? Please, ⸤take her instead⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “let her be in the place of her”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 14:15"> And Samson said to them, “This time, as far as the Philistines are concerned, when I do something evil I am without blame.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:15; Psalm 63:10; Lamentations 5:18"> And Samson went and captured three hundred foxes, and he took torches. He turned them tail to tail, and he put one torch between two tails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:30; Exodus 22:6"> He set fire to the torches and let the foxes go into the standing grain of the Philistines, and he burned both the stacks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “stack”">c</a></sup>of sheaves and the standing grain, up to the vineyards<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “vineyard”">d</a></sup>of olive groves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 14:15; 1 Thessalonians 4:6; Proverbs 22:8"> And the Philistines said, “Who has done this?” And they said, “Samson the son-in-law of the Timnite, because he took his wife and gave her to his companion.” And the Philistines went up and burned her and her father with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 14:4; Romans 12:19; Judges 14:19"> Samson said to them, “If you want to behave like this, I swear I will not rest unless I have taken revenge on you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Isaiah 63:6; Isaiah 25:10"> And ⸤he gave them a thorough beating⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “he struck them hip and thigh with a great blow”">e</a></sup>and he went down and stayed in the cleft of the rock of Etam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 15:17; Judges 15:19; Judges 15:14"> Then the Philistines came up and encamped in Judah, and they overran Lehi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the men of Judah said, “Why have you come up against us?” And they said, “To bind Samson; to do to him just as he did to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> Then three thousand men from Judah went down to the cleft of the rock of Etam, and they said to Samson, “Do you not know that the Philistines are ruling over us? What is this that you have done to us?” And he said to them, “Just as they did to me, so I have done to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Acts 7:25; 1 Kings 2:25"> They said to him, “We have come down to bind you and give you over into the hand of the Philistines.” And Samson said to them, “Swear to me that you will not attack me yourselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They said to him, “No, we will only bind you and give you into their hand; we will certainly not kill you.” So they bound him with two new ropes, and they brought him up from the rock.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 14:19; Judges 3:10; Psalm 118:11"> As he came up to Lehi, the Philistines came shouting to meet him; and the Spirit of Yahweh rushed on him, and the ropes that were on his arms became like flax that has burned with fire, and his bindings melted from his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Leviticus 26:8; Judges 3:31"> And he found a fresh jawbone of a donkey; he reached down and took it and killed one thousand men with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Samson said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“With the jawbone of the donkey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">heap upon heap;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the jawbone of the donkey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I struck dead one thousand men.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And it happened, when he finished speaking he threw the jawbone from his hand; and he called that place Ramath Lehi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, “Hill of the Jawbone”">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:7–8; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 8:4"> And he was very thirsty, and he called to Yahweh and said, “You gave this great victory into the hand of your servant, but now I must die of thirst and fall into the hand of the uncircumcised?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 45:27; Psalm 34:6"> So God split the hollow place that is at Lehi, and water came out from it; and he drank, and his spirit returned, and he was revived. Thus he called its name ⸤The Spring of Ha-Qore⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the spring of the one who called” or “En-hakkore”">g</a></sup>which is at Lehi to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 16:31; Judges 13:5"> And he judged Israel in the days of the Philistines twenty years.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
