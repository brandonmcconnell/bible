## Micah’s Idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 18:2; Joshua 17:14–18; Judges 10:1"> There was a man from the hill country of Ephraim; his name was Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:25; Ruth 3:10; 1 Samuel 23:21"> And he said to his mother, “The eleven hundred pieces of silver that were taken from you, and about which you also pronounced a curse ⸤in my hearing⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “In my ears”">a</a></sup>are with me; I took it.” And his mother said, “Blessed be my son by Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:4; Exodus 20:23; Leviticus 19:4"> He returned the eleven hundred pieces<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “piece”">b</a></sup>of silver to his mother, and his mother thought, “I will certainly consecrate to Yahweh the pieces<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “piece”">c</a></sup>of silver from my hand for my son to make an idol of cast metal; now then, I will return them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">d</a></sup>to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6–7; Jeremiah 10:9–10"> When he returned the pieces<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “piece”">e</a></sup>of silver to his mother, his mother took two hundred pieces<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “piece”">f</a></sup>of silver, and she gave it to the smith, and he made it into an idol of cast metal; and it was in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 8:27; Genesis 31:19"> The man Micah had for himself ⸤a shrine⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a house of god”">g</a></sup>and he made an ephod and teraphim, and ⸤he appointed one of his sons⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “he filled the hand of one of his sons”">h</a></sup>who became a priest for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Proverbs 14:12; Judges 21:25"> In those days there was no king in Israel, and each one did what was right in his own eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1; Ruth 1:1–2"> There was a young man from Bethlehem in Judah, from the clan of Judah; he was a Levite and was dwelling as a foreigner<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “alien”">i</a></sup>there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:10–11; Judges 17:11; Joshua 24:33"> And the man went from the town of Bethlehem in Judah to live as a foreigner<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “alien”">j</a></sup>wherever he could find a place. And he came to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, to continue his journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Micah said to him, “From where do you come?” And he said to him, “I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah; I am going to dwell as a foreigner<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “alien”">k</a></sup>wherever I can find a place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:19; Judges 18:19–20; 1 Timothy 6:10"> And Micah said to him, “Stay with me and be to me a father and a priest, and I will give to you ten pieces<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “piece”">l</a></sup>of silver a year, a set of clothes, and your food.” So the Levite went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Levite agreed to stay with the man; and the young man became as one of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 18:30; Numbers 16:8–10; Numbers 16:5"> So Micah ⸤appointed the Levite⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “filled the hand of the Levite”">m</a></sup>and the young man became a priest for him; and he was in the house of Micah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Proverbs 14:12; John 16:2"> And Micah said, “Now I know Yahweh will make me prosperous, because the Levite has become my priest.”</span>
</div>
