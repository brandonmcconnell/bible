## Jephthah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32; Judges 6:12; 2 Kings 5:1"> Jephthah the Gileadite was a mighty warrior; he was the son of a prostitute, and ⸤Gilead was his father⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “Gilead fathered Jephthah”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Proverbs 2:16; Deuteronomy 23:2"> Gilead’s wife also bore for him sons; and the sons of his wife grew up and drove Jephthah away, and they said to him, “You will not inherit the house of our father because you are the son of another woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 9:4; 1 Samuel 22:2; 2 Samuel 10:8"> So Jephthah fled from the presence of his brothers, and he lived in the land of Tob. And ⸤outlaws⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “unprincipled/worthless men”">b</a></sup>gathered around Jephthah and went with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 10:9"> After a time the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">c</a></sup>made war with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 118:22–23; Acts 7:35–39; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> When the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">d</a></sup>made war with Israel, the elders of Gilead went to bring Jephthah from the land of Tob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And they said to Jephthah, “Come and be our commander, so that we may make war against the ⸤Ammonites⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 3:9; Genesis 45:4–5; Isaiah 60:14"> Jephthah said to the elders, “Did you not shun me and drive me out from the house of my father? Why do you come to me now when you have trouble?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 10:18; 1 Kings 13:6; Exodus 10:17"> And the elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, “That being so, we have now returned to you, that you may go with us ⸤to fight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to make war”">f</a></sup>against the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">g</a></sup>and become for us as head of all the inhabitants of Gilead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 32:20–29"> So Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, “If you bring me back ⸤to fight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to make war”">h</a></sup>against the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">i</a></sup>and Yahweh gives them ⸤over to me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “before me”">j</a></sup>will I be your head?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5; Genesis 31:50; Jeremiah 29:23"> And the elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, “Yahweh will be a ⸤witness⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “hearer”">k</a></sup>between us; we will act according to your word.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 10:17; 1 Samuel 10:17; Judges 20:1"> So Jephthah went with the elders of Gilead, and the people made him head and commander over them. And Jephthah spoke all his words before Yahweh at Mizpah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26; Matthew 18:15–16; Proverbs 25:8–9"> And Jephthah sent messengers to the king of the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">l</a></sup>saying, “What is between you and me that you have come to me to make war against my land?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 32:22; Numbers 21:24–26; Deuteronomy 2:37"> And the king of the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">m</a></sup>said to Jephthah’s messengers, “Because Israel took my land from the Arnon up to the Jabbok and the Jordan when they came up from Egypt; so then, restore it peacefully.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:11; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> Once again Jephthah sent messengers to the king of the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Numbers 21:27–30"> and he said to him, “Thus says Jephthah, ‘Israel did not take the land of Moab or the land of the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:25; Deuteronomy 1:40; Numbers 20:1"> because when they came up from Egypt, Israel went through the wilderness to the ⸤Red Sea⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sea of reed”">p</a></sup>and went to Kadesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 20:14–21; Deuteronomy 2:4–9; Numbers 20:1"> Israel sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, “Please let us cross through your land,” but the king of Edom would not listen. And they also sent messengers to the king of Moab, but he was not willing. So Israel stayed in Kadesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:1–8; Numbers 22:36; Numbers 21:4–13"> Then they traveled through the wilderness, went around the land of Edom and Moab, and came to ⸤the east⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “from rise of sun”">q</a></sup>side of the land of Moab, and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">r</a></sup>encamped beyond the Arnon; and they did not go into the territory of Moab because the Arnon was the border of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 2:26–36; Deuteronomy 3:1–17"> Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">s</a></sup>king of Heshbon; and Israel said to him, ‘Please let us cross through your land ⸤to our country⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “to our place”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 13:15–32; Numbers 21:23–25; Psalm 136:17–21"> But Sihon did not trust Israel to cross through his territory, so Sihon gathered all his people and then encamped at Jahaz; and he made war with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Yahweh, the God of Israel, gave Sihon and all his people into the hand of Israel, and they defeated them; and Israel occupied all the land of the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">u</a></sup>inhabiting that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36"> They occupied all the territory of the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">v</a></sup>from the Arnon up to the Jabbok, and from the wilderness up to the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> So then Yahweh, the God of Israel, has driven out the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">w</a></sup>from before his people Israel, and you want to possess it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Psalm 78:55; Micah 4:5"> Do you not possess what Chemosh your god gave you to possess? Whoever Yahweh our God has driven out before us, we will possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 6:5; Joshua 24:9–10; Deuteronomy 23:3–4"> So then, are you any better than Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab? Did he ever quarrel with Israel, or did he ever make war against them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 12:5; Judges 10:8"> When Israel lived in Heshbon and its villages, and in Aroer and its villages, and in all the towns that are ⸤along the Arnon⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “on the hands of the Arnon”">x</a></sup>for three hundred years,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “year”">y</a></sup>why did you not recover them at that time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:15; Genesis 16:5; 1 Samuel 24:12"> I have not sinned against you; but you are the one who is doing wrong by making war against me. ⸤Let Yahweh judge⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “Let Yahweh the judge, judge”">z</a></sup>today between the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">aa</a></sup>and the ⸤Ammonites⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 14:11; Proverbs 16:18"> But the king of the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">ac</a></sup>did not listen to the message that Jephthah sent to him.</span>

## Jephthah Makes a Vow

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> And the Spirit of Yahweh came upon Jephthah, and he passed through Gilead and Manasseh. He passed through Mizpah of Gilead, and from Mizpah of Gilead he passed through to the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11; Ecclesiastes 5:1–2"> And Jephthah made a vow to Yahweh, and he said, “If indeed you will give the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">ae</a></sup>into my hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 66:13–14; Leviticus 27:28–29; 1 Samuel 1:28"> whatever<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “whoever”">af</a></sup>comes out from the doors of my house to meet me when I return safely from the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">ag</a></sup>will be Yahweh’s, and I will offer it as a burnt offering.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:4; Judges 2:18; Judges 3:10"> And Jephthah crossed over to the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">ah</a></sup>to make war against them; and Yahweh gave them into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:17; Deuteronomy 2:36"> And he defeated them with a very great blow, from Aroer as far as Minnith, twenty towns,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “town”">ai</a></sup>up to Abel Keramim. And the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">aj</a></sup>were subdued before the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ak</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:4; Exodus 15:20; Judges 10:17"> Jephthah came to Mizpah, to his house, and behold his daughter came out to meet him with tambourines and dancing. She was his only child; he did not have a son or daughter except her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 14:7–9; Genesis 37:29–30; Genesis 42:36–38"> And the moment he saw her, he tore his clothes and said, “Ah! My daughter, you have caused me to bow down, and you have become my trouble. ⸤I made an oath⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “I have opened wide my mouth”">al</a></sup>to Yahweh, and I cannot take it back.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:19; Philippians 2:30; Romans 16:4"> She said to him, “My father, ⸤you made an oath⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “you have opened wide your mouth”">am</a></sup>to Yahweh. Do to me according to what has gone out from your mouth, since Yahweh gave vengeance to you against your enemies, the ⸤Ammonites⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">an</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 1:25; 1 Samuel 1:6"> And she said to her father, “Let this thing be done for me: grant me two months so that I may go wander<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “down”">ao</a></sup>on the mountains and lament over my virginity, I and my companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And he said, “Go.” He sent her away for two months, and she went with her friends, and she lamented over her virginity on the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 66:3; Judges 11:31; Leviticus 27:28–29"> At the end of the two months she returned to her father, and he did to her according to his vow; and ⸤she did not sleep with a man⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “she did not know a man”">ap</a></sup>And it became an annual custom in Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Kings 9:25; Judges 5:11"> for the daughters of Israel to go and lament the daughter of Jephthah the Gileadite for forty days of the year.</span>
</div>
