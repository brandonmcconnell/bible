## Gideon Pursues Zebah and Zalmunna

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:41; Judges 12:1–6; Job 5:2"> The men of Ephraim said to him, “What is this thing you have done to us, not calling us when you went to fight against the Midianites?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “Midianite”">a</a></sup>And they quarreled with him severely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Philippians 2:2–3; James 3:13–18"> And he said to them, “What have I done now in comparison with you? Are not the gleanings of Ephraim better than the grape harvest of Abiezer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Judges 7:24–25; John 4:37"> God has given into your hand the commanders of Midian, Oreb, and Zeeb. What have I been able to do in comparison with you?” And their ⸤anger⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “spirit”">b</a></sup>against him subsided when ⸤he said that⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “he said this thing/word”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> Then Gideon came to the Jordan, crossing it with the three hundred men who were with him, weary and pursuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Psalm 60:6; 1 Samuel 25:18"> He said to the men of Succoth, “Please give loaves of bread to the people who are ⸤following me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “at my feet”">d</a></sup>for they are weary, and I am pursuing Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 20:11; Philippians 2:21; Proverbs 18:23"> The officials of Succoth said, “Is the hand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “palm”">e</a></sup>of Zebah and Zalmunna in your hand now, that we should give bread to your army?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 7:15"> Gideon said, “Well then, when Yahweh gives Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand, I will trample your flesh with the thorns and briers of the wilderness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:30–31; 1 Kings 12:25"> He went from there to Penuel, and he spoke similarly to them; and the men of Penuel answered him just as the men of Succoth answered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 8:17; 1 Kings 22:27–28"> And he said also to the men of Penuel, saying, “When I return ⸤safely⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “in peace”">f</a></sup>I will tear down this tower.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 20:25; Judges 20:2"> Now Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor, and their armies with them, about fifteen thousand men remained from the entire army<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “camp”">g</a></sup>of the people of the east; those that fell in battle were one hundred and twenty thousand ⸤swordsmen⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sword-drawing men”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:42; Judges 18:27"> And Gideon went up the route of those who dwell in tents on the east of Nobah and Jogbehah, and he attacked the army<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “camp”">i</a></sup>when it was off its guard.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “unsuspecting”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 83:11; Amos 2:14; Job 12:16–21"> And Zebah and Zalmunna fled, and he pursued them and captured the two kings of Midian, Zebah and Zalmunna, and he routed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “he frightened” or “he threw into panic”">k</a></sup>the entire army.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “camp”">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then Gideon son of Jehoash returned from the battle ⸤by way of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “from”">m</a></sup>the ascent of Heres.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “pass of Heres”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 1:24–25; 1 Samuel 30:11–15"> He captured a young man ⸤from Succoth⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “from the men of Succoth”">o</a></sup>and questioned him. The young man listed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “wrote down”">p</a></sup>for him the commanders of Succoth and its elders, seventy-seven men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:6–7"> He came to the men of Succoth, and he said, “Here is Zebah and Zalmunna, about whom you taunted me, saying, ‘Is the palm<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “hand”">q</a></sup>of Zebah and Zalmunna in your hand now, that we should give food to your weary men?’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:7; Ezra 2:6; Proverbs 10:13"> He took the elders of the city and the thorn bushes and briers of the wilderness, and ⸤he trampled⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “he taught,” but see v. 7">r</a></sup>the men of Succoth with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 8:9; 1 Kings 12:25"> He broke down the tower of Penuel, and he killed the men of the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:6; Jude 1:16; Psalm 89:12"> And he said to Zebah and Zalmunna, “What type were the men whom you killed at Tabor?” And they said, “⸤They were like you⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally, “like you, like them”">s</a></sup>each one of them had the appearance of the sons of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He said, “They were my brothers, the sons of my mother. As Yahweh lives, if you had kept them alive I would not kill you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 149:9; 1 Samuel 15:33; Joshua 10:24"> And he said to Jether, his firstborn, “Get up, kill them.” But the boy did not draw his sword, for he was afraid because he was still a boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 83:11; Judges 8:26; Isaiah 3:18"> Zebah and Zalmunna said, “Get up yourself, and strike us, for as is the man, so is his power.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “strength”">t</a></sup>So Gideon got up and killed Zebah and Zalmunna, and he took the crescent ornaments that were on the necks of their camels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:15; Judges 9:8–15; 1 Samuel 12:12"> The men of Israel said to Gideon, “Rule over us, both you and your sons, and your sons’ son, for you have delivered us from the hand of Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 10:19"> But Gideon said to them, “I will not rule over you, and my son will not rule over you; Yahweh will rule over you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:13; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 37:28"> And Gideon said to them, “⸤Let me make a request of you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “Let me ask from you a request”">u</a></sup>that each of you give to me an ornamental ring from his plunder.” (They had ornamental rings of gold, because they were Ishmaelites.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They said, “We will gladly give them,” and they spread out a garment, and everyone threw there an ornamental ring of his plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 18:16; John 19:2; Revelation 18:12"> The weight of the ornamental rings of gold that he requested was one thousand seven hundred shekels of gold, apart from the crescents, pendants, and purple garments that were on the kings of Midian, and apart from the pendants that were on the necks of their camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:14; Psalm 106:39"> Gideon made an ephod out of it, and he put it in his town in Ophrah, and all Israel prostituted themselves to it there, and it became a snare to Gideon and his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 3:11; Judges 3:30"> And Midian was subdued before the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup>and they did not again lift up their head, and the land rested for forty years in the days of Gideon.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Gideon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 7:1; 1 Samuel 12:11"> Jerub-Baal son of Joash lived in his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 9:5; Judges 9:2; 1 Kings 11:3"> Now Gideon had seventy sons, ⸤his own offspring⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “from his own loins”">w</a></sup>for he had many wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 9:18; Genesis 22:24; Genesis 16:15"> His concubine who was in Shechem also bore for him a son, and ⸤he named him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “he set his name”">x</a></sup>Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Judges 6:24; Job 5:26"> And Gideon son of Joash died at a good old age, and he was buried in the tomb of Jehoash his father, in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “Abiezrite”">y</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 9:46; Judges 2:19; Judges 9:4"> And it happened, as soon as Gideon died, the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">z</a></sup>returned and prostituted themselves after the Baals, and they made for themselves Baal-Berith as god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 78:42; Psalm 106:21; Psalm 78:11"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">aa</a></sup>did not remember Yahweh their God, who had delivered them from the hand of their enemies from all around,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Judges 9:5; Judges 9:16–19"> nor did they show favor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “loyalty”">ab</a></sup>to the house of Jerub-Baal (that is, Gideon) in accordance with all the good that he did for Israel.</span>
</div>
