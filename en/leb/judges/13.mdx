## Samson’s Parents

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:9; Judges 6:1; Romans 2:6"> And again, the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>did evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and Yahweh gave them into the hand of the Philistines forty years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 19:41; Luke 1:7; Joshua 15:33"> There was a certain man from Zorah, from the tribe of the Danites, and his name was Manoah; his wife was infertile and did not bear children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Luke 1:11; Judges 6:11–12"> And an angel of Yahweh appeared to the woman, and he said to her, “Behold, you are infertile and have not borne children, but you will conceive and bear a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Judges 13:14; Numbers 6:2–3"> So then, be careful and do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 6:5; 1 Samuel 1:11; 1 Chronicles 18:1"> because you will conceive and bear a son. No razor ⸤will touch⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “will go upon”">b</a></sup>his head, because the boy will be a ⸤Nazirite of God⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “consecrated of God” or “devoted of God”">c</a></sup>⸤from birth⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from the womb”">d</a></sup>And it is he who will begin to deliver Israel from the hand of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 13:17–18; 1 Samuel 9:6; Matthew 28:3–4"> And the woman came and told her husband, saying, “A man of God came to me, and his appearance was like the appearance of an angel of God, very awesome. I did not ask him from where he came, and he did not tell me his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 13:4"> And he said to me, ‘Look, you will conceive and bear a son, so then, do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean, for the boy will be a Nazirite of God from birth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “the womb”">e</a></sup>until the day of his death.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:6; Job 34:32; Proverbs 3:5–6"> Then Manoah prayed to Yahweh and said, “Excuse me, my Lord, please let the man of God whom you sent again come to us and teach us what we should do concerning the boy who will be born.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 65:2; Matthew 7:7–11"> And God listened to the voice of Manoah, and an angel of God came again to the woman; she was sitting in the field, but Manoah her husband was not with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:28–29; John 1:41–42"> The woman quickly ran and told her husband, and she said to him, “Look! The man who came to me the other day appeared to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So Manoah got up and went after his wife, and he came to the man and said to him, “Are you the man that spoke to the woman?” And he said, “I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 22:6; Ephesians 6:4; Genesis 18:19"> And Manoah said to him, “Now ⸤when your words come true⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “when your words will come”">f</a></sup>what will be the boy’s ⸤manner of life⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “measure”">g</a></sup>and work?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the angel of Yahweh said to Manoah, “Let the woman be attentive to all that I said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 2:5; Judges 13:4; 2 Thessalonians 3:4"> She should not eat of anything that comes from the vine, or drink wine or strong drink, or eat anything unclean; she should keep all that I commanded.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 6:18–19; Genesis 18:3–5; Judges 13:3"> And Manoah said to the angel of Yahweh, “⸤Please stay⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “Please, let us hold you back”">h</a></sup>and let us prepare a ⸤young goat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “kid of goat”">i</a></sup>for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 13:23; Judges 6:20; Judges 6:26"> The angel of Yahweh said to Manoah, “If you keep me, I will not eat your food, but if you prepare a burnt offering for Yahweh, you can offer it (for Manoah did not know that he was an angel of Yahweh).”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 32:29"> And Manoah said to the angel of Yahweh, “What is your name so that when your words come true we may honor you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 32:29; Isaiah 9:6; Judges 13:6"> But the angel of Yahweh said to him, “Why do you ask my name? It is too wonderful.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 6:19–21; 1 Kings 18:30–38"> And Manoah took the ⸤young goat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “kid of goat”">j</a></sup>and the grain offering, and he offered it to Yahweh on the rock, to the one who performs miracles.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “the one who works wonders”">k</a></sup>And Manoah and his wife were watching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; 1 Chronicles 21:16; 2 Kings 2:11"> And when the flame went up toward the heaven from the altar, the angel of Yahweh went up in the flame of the altar to heaven while Manoah and his wife were watching. And they fell on their faces to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 6:22; Hosea 12:4–5; Judges 13:16"> The angel of Yahweh did not appear again to Manoah and his wife, and then Manoah knew that he was a messenger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “angel”">l</a></sup>of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; Deuteronomy 5:26; John 1:18"> And Manoah said to his wife, “We will certainly die because we have seen God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 25:14; 1 Corinthians 12:21; John 15:15"> But his wife said to him, “If Yahweh wanted to kill us he would not have taken<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “accepted”">m</a></sup>from our hand the burnt offering and the grain offering, or shown us all these things, or now announced to us things such as these.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 1:80; 1 Samuel 3:19; Hebrews 11:32"> The woman bore a son, and she called him Samson; the boy grew big, and Yahweh blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Joshua 15:33; John 3:34"> And the Spirit of Yahweh began to stir him in the camp of Dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol.</span>
</div>
</div>
