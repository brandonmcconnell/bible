## Gideon’s Three Hundred Men

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Genesis 12:6"> Then Jerub-Baal (that is, Gideon) rose early, and all the army that was with him. They were camped beside the spring of Harod;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “En-Harod”">a</a></sup>the camp of Midian was north of the hill of Moreh, in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> And Yahweh said to Gideon, “The troops that are with you are too many for me to give Midian into their hands; Israel will boast, saying, ‘My hand has delivered me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Revelation 17:14; Revelation 21:8"> So then, please proclaim in the ⸤hearing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “ears”">b</a></sup>of the troops, saying, ‘Whoever is fearful and trembling, let him return and depart from the Mount of Gilead.’ ” About twenty-two thousand troops returned, and ten thousand remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 33:16; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Samuel 14:6"> And Yahweh said to Gideon, “There are still too many troops; bring them down to the water, and I will sift through them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “test them”">c</a></sup>for you there. For whomever I say to you, ‘This one will go with you,’ he will go with you; and for all whom I say to you, ‘This one will not go with you,’ he will not go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So he brought down the troops to the water, and Yahweh said to Gideon, “You must separate everyone who laps up the water to drink with his tongue like a dog from those ⸤who kneel⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “who kneels to his knees”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The number of those lapping up the water with their hand to their mouth was three hundred men; all the rest of the troops kneeled to drink the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:6; Isaiah 41:14–16; Judges 7:18–22"> And Yahweh said to Gideon, “I will deliver you with the three hundred men lapping up the water; I will give Midian into your hand, so let the other troops go, each to his own place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 6:20; Numbers 10:9; Joshua 6:4"> So they took their provisions and their trumpets into their hand, and he sent all the men of Israel, each one, to his tent; but three hundred of the men he kept; the camp of Midian was below him in the valley.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 46:2–3; Judges 3:28; Acts 18:9–10"> And that night Yahweh said to him, “Get up; go down against the camp, for I have given it into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–14; Judges 4:8–9"> But if you are afraid, go down to the camp with Purah your servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Judges 7:13–15; Isaiah 35:3–4"> and you will hear what they say; and afterward ⸤you will have courage⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “your hands will be strong”">e</a></sup>and you will go down against the camp.” Then he went down with Purah his servant to the outpost of the armed men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “edge of the men lined up in array”">f</a></sup>that were in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:10; Judges 6:5; Joshua 11:4"> Now the Midianites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “Midianite”">g</a></sup>Amalekites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “Amalekite”">h</a></sup>and all the people of the east were lying in the valley, like a great multitude of locusts; their camels were without number, as numerous as the sand that is on the shore of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; Isaiah 41:14–15; Judges 4:9"> When Gideon came, a man was recounting a dream<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “telling a dream”">i</a></sup>to his friend, and he said, “Behold, ⸤I had a dream⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “I dreamed a dream”">j</a></sup>a round loaf of barley bread was tumbling into the camp of Midian, and it came up to the tent, it struck it, and it fell and turned it upside down so that the tent fell.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6–7; Joshua 2:9; Joshua 2:24"> His friend answered him and said, “This cannot be anything except the sword of Gideon son of Jehoash, a man of Israel; God has given Midian and the entire camp into his hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–6; Genesis 40:8; 2 Chronicles 20:18–19"> When Gideon heard the recounting of the dream<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “telling of the dream”">k</a></sup>and its interpretation, he bowed down and returned to the camp of Israel; and he said, “Get up, for Yahweh has given the camp of Midian into your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7"> He divided the three hundred men into three companies,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “divisions”">l</a></sup>and he put trumpets and empty jars in everyone’s hand, with torches inside the jars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; Matthew 16:24; Hebrews 13:7"> And he said to them, “Watch me and do the same. When I come to the edge of the camp, ⸤do just as I do⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “just as I will do, you must do”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:15–17; 1 Samuel 17:47"> When I and all who are with me blow on the trumpet, you must also blow on the trumpets and surround the camp, and you must say, ‘To Yahweh and to Gideon!’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 2:9; Exodus 14:24; Matthew 25:6"> So Gideon and the hundred men who were with him came to the edge of the camp at the beginning of the middle night-watch, when they had just finished setting up the guards, and they blew on the trumpets and smashed the jars that were in their hands.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “hand”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 27:13; 2 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> When three companies<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “divisions”">o</a></sup>blew on the trumpets and broke the jars, they held in their left hand the torches and in their right hand the trumpets for blowing, and they cried, “A sword for Yahweh and for Gideon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:17; Proverbs 28:1; 2 Kings 7:6–7"> And each stood ⸤in his place⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “under him”">p</a></sup>all around the camp, and all the camp ran, and they cried out as they fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 4:12; Joshua 6:20; Joshua 6:4"> When they blew the three hundred trumpets, Yahweh set the sword of each one against his neighbor throughout the whole camp, and the camp<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “army”">q</a></sup>fled as far as Beth Shittah toward Zererah, up to Abel Meholah, the border by Tabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 6:35; 1 Samuel 14:21–22"> And the men of Israel were called from Naphtali, from Asher, and from all of Manasseh, and they pursued after Midian.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 3:27–28; Philippians 1:27; Romans 15:30"> And Gideon sent messengers throughout all the hill country of Ephraim, saying, “Come down to oppose<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “meet”">r</a></sup>Midian, and capture from them the waters up to Beth Barah and the Jordan.” He called out all the men of Ephraim, and they captured the waters up to Beth Barah and the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 10:26; Judges 8:3–4; Psalm 83:11–12"> And they captured the two commanders of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb, and they killed Oreb at the rock of Oreb, and they killed Zeeb at the wine press of Zeeb, while they chased Midian; and they brought the heads<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “head”">s</a></sup>of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon from beyond the Jordan.</span>
</div>
</div>
