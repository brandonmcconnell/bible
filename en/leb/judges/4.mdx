## Deborah and Barak

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Judges 3:12; Judges 3:7"> And again the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>did evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and Ehud died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 11:1; Judges 4:16; Judges 4:13"> So Yahweh sold them into the hand of Jabin king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor. The commander of his army was Sisera, and he was living in Harosheth Haggoyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 1:19; Psalm 106:42; Judges 3:9"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>cried to Yahweh, as he had nine hundred iron chariots,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">c</a></sup>and he oppressed the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>⸤cruelly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “with force”">e</a></sup>for twenty years.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “year”">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Luke 2:36; 2 Kings 22:14"> Now at that time Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lappidoth, was judging<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “leading”">g</a></sup>Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:19; Jeremiah 31:15; Exodus 18:26"> And she used to sit under the palm tree of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim; and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>went up to her for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Judges 8:18; Hebrews 11:32"> She sent and called for Barak son of Abinoam from Kedesh Naphtali and said to him, “Has not Yahweh the God of Israel commanded you? ‘Go, ⸤march to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “take the lead at”">i</a></sup>Mount Tabor, and take ten thousand men from the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">j</a></sup>of Naphtali and Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 18:40; Ezekiel 38:10–16; Psalm 83:9–10"> ⸤I will draw out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “I will draw to you”">k</a></sup>Sisera, the commander of Jabin’s army, with his chariots and troops, to the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">l</a></sup>of Kishon, and I will give him into your hand.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–14; Matthew 14:30–31"> Barak said to her, “If you go with me, I will go; but if you do not go with me, I will not go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 2:14; 1 Samuel 2:30; Judges 4:17–22"> She said, “Surely I will go with you; however, there will be no glory for you in ⸤the path you are taking⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “the way you are going”">m</a></sup>for Yahweh will sell Sisera into the hand of a woman.” And Deborah stood up and went with Barak to Kedesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:18; Judges 5:15; Judges 4:14"> Barak summoned Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh; and ⸤they went up behind him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “they went up in his feet”">n</a></sup>ten thousand men, and Deborah went up with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Joshua 19:33; Numbers 10:29"> And Heber the Kenite was separated from the other Kenites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “Kenite”">o</a></sup>that is, from the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">p</a></sup>of Hobab the father-in-law of Moses. And ⸤he was encamped⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “he spread his tent”">q</a></sup>at Elon-bezaanannim, which is near Kedesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 89:12; Jeremiah 46:18; Joshua 19:12"> When they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="The subject is not specified in Hebrew">r</a></sup>reported to Sisera that Barak son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 4:2–3; Judges 4:7"> Sisera summoned all his chariots—all nine hundred chariots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">s</a></sup>of iron—and the entire army that was with him from Harosheth Haggoyim to the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">t</a></sup>of Kishon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; 2 Samuel 5:24; Isaiah 52:12"> And Deborah said to Barak, “Get up! This is the day that Yahweh has given Sisera into your hand. Has Yahweh not gone out before you?” So Barak went out from Mount Tabor with ten thousand men following him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 10:10; Psalm 83:9–10; Judges 5:20–21"> And Yahweh threw Sisera and all his chariots and army into confusion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “routed Sisera and all his chariots and army”">u</a></sup>before ⸤the edge of Barak’s sword⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword before Barak”">v</a></sup>and Sisera dismounted from his chariot and fled on foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Leviticus 26:7–8; James 2:13"> But Barak pursued after the chariots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">w</a></sup>and army as far as Harosheth Haggoyim, and all of Sisera’s army fell to ⸤the edge of the sword⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword”">x</a></sup>no one was left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 69:22; Isaiah 57:21; Amos 5:19–20"> Sisera fled on foot to the tent of Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, because there was peace between Jabin king of Hazor and the house of Heber the Kenite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 6:19"> And Jael came out to meet Sisera, and she said to him, “Turn aside, my lord; turn aside to me and do not be afraid.” So he turned aside into her tent, and she covered him ⸤with a blanket⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “with a covering/rug”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:25–26; Genesis 24:43; Isaiah 41:17"> And he said to her, “Please, give me a drink of water, because I am thirsty.” So she opened a skin vessel of milk and gave him a drink and covered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 2:3–5; 2 Samuel 17:20"> And he said to her, “Stand at the doorway of the tent, and if anyone comes and asks you, and says, ‘Is there anyone here?’ You must answer, ‘No.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 5:26–27; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Judges 3:21"> But Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, took in her hand a tent peg and a hammer, and she went softly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “secretly”">z</a></sup>to him and drove the peg into his temple, and it went through into the ground; he was fast asleep since he was exhausted, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:10–15; 2 Samuel 17:3"> And behold, Barak was pursuing Sisera, and Jael went out to meet him, and she said to him, “Come, and I will show you the man whom you are seeking.” And he came with her and saw that Sisera was lying dead with the peg in his temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:24; Psalm 18:47; Psalm 18:39"> On that day God subdued Jabin king of Canaan before the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:12"> And the hand of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ab</a></sup>⸤pressed harder⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “went harder”">ac</a></sup>and harder on Jabin king of Canaan, until they destroyed Jabin king of Canaan.</span>
</div>
