## Some Nations Remain in the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:21–22; Deuteronomy 7:22; Proverbs 17:3"> These are the nations that Yahweh left, to test Israel by them (that is, to test all those who ⸤had not experienced⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “did not know”">a</a></sup>any of the wars of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 3:5; Genesis 3:7; 1 Corinthians 9:26–27"> in order that the generations of Israel would know war, to teach those ⸤who had not experienced it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “who had not known it”">b</a></sup>before):</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Joshua 13:5; Judges 10:7"> the five rulers of the Philistines, all the Canaanites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">c</a></sup>the Sidonians,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “Sidonian”">d</a></sup>and the Hivites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">e</a></sup>living on Mount Lebanon, from Mount Baal Hermon up to Lebo-Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 3:1; Judges 2:22; Exodus 15:25"> They were left for testing Israel, to know whether they would keep the commands of Yahweh that he commanded their ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “fathers”">f</a></sup>through the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 106:34–38; Genesis 10:15–18; Exodus 3:8"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>lived in the midst of the Canaanites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">h</a></sup>the Hittites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “Hittite”">i</a></sup>the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">j</a></sup>the Perizzites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">k</a></sup>the Hivites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “Hivite”">l</a></sup>and the Jebusites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Exodus 34:16; 1 Kings 11:1–5"> And they took their daughters as wives for themselves, and they gave their daughters to their sons, and they served their gods.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “worshiped their gods”">n</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Othniel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:6; 1 Kings 18:19"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>did evil in the eyes of Yahweh. They forgot Yahweh their God, and they served the Baals and the Asheroth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Asheroth are cultic poles set up next to an altar symbolizing the goddess Asherah">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Habakkuk 3:7; Judges 4:9"> And ⸤the anger of Yahweh was kindled⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the nose of Yahweh became hot”">q</a></sup>against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of Cushan-Rishathaim, the king of Aram Naharaim; and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">r</a></sup>served Cushan-Rishathaim eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Judges 1:13; Psalm 22:5"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">s</a></sup>cried out to Yahweh, and Yahweh raised up a deliverer for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">t</a></sup>who delivered them, Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 14:19"> And the spirit of Yahweh came upon him, and he judged Israel. He went out to war, and Yahweh gave Cushan-Rishathaim king of Aram into his hand, and ⸤he prevailed over⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “his hand was strong over”">u</a></sup>Cushan-Rishathaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 8:28; Joshua 11:23"> So the land rested forty years. Then Othniel son of Kenaz died.</span>

## Ehud

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:9; Judges 2:19; Judges 2:14"> And again the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup>did evil in the eyes of Yahweh. So Yahweh strengthened Eglon king of Moab against Israel, because they did evil in the eyes of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 34:3; Judges 5:14"> He gathered to himself the ⸤Ammonites and Amalekites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon and Amalek” or “children of Ammon and Amalek”">w</a></sup>and he went and defeated Israel, and they took possession of the city of palms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Deuteronomy 28:40; Leviticus 26:23–25"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">x</a></sup>served Eglon king of Moab eighteen years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Psalm 78:34; Isaiah 36:16"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">y</a></sup>cried out to Yahweh, and Yahweh raised up for them a deliverer, Ehud son of Gera, a Benjaminite and ⸤a left-handed man⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “a man bound by his right hand”">z</a></sup>And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">aa</a></sup>sent a tribute to Eglon king of Moab ⸤through him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “in his hand”">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:8; Revelation 2:12; Psalm 149:6"> Ehud made for himself a short, ⸤two-edged⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “with two mouths”">ac</a></sup>sword (a cubit in length), and he fastened it under his clothes on his right thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20; Job 15:27; Jeremiah 50:11"> Then he presented the tribute to Eglon king of Moab. Now Eglon was a very fat man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When Ehud<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="That is, Eglon">ad</a></sup>had finished presenting the tribute, he sent away the people who carried the tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 4:20; Judges 3:20; Acts 23:18–19"> But he turned back from the sculptured stones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “Pesilim”; some translations translate the phrase as a proper name">ae</a></sup>that were near Gilgal, and he said, “I have ⸤a secret message⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “a word of secrecy”">af</a></sup>for you, O king.” And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">ag</a></sup>said, “Silence!” So all those standing in his presence went out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:7; Amos 3:15; Psalm 29:1"> and Ehud came to him while he was sitting alone in his cool upper room. And Ehud said, “I have a ⸤message from God⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “word of God”">ah</a></sup>for you.” So he got up from his seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Job 20:25; 1 Samuel 15:33"> Then Ehud reached with his left hand for the sword on his right thigh, and he thrust it into his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="That is, Eglon’s">ai</a></sup>stomach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the handle also went in after the blade, and the fat closed over the blade because he did not draw back the sword from his stomach; and it went protruding out the back.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="The Hebrew is uncertain; some translations have “and the dirt/entrails came out”">aj</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Ehud went out the vestibule, and he closed the doors of the upper room and locked them behind him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:3"> After he left, his servants returned. When they saw that the doors of the upper room were locked, ⸤they thought⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Hebrew “they said”">ak</a></sup>“Surely he is ⸤relieving himself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “covering his feet”">al</a></sup>in the cool inner room.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they waited so long they became embarrassed because he did not open the doors of the upper room. So they took the key and opened the doors, and there their lord was lying on the ground dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Ehud escaped while they delayed. He passed by the sculptured stones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “Pesilim;” some translations to translate the phrase as a proper name">am</a></sup>and escaped to Seirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 6:34; 1 Samuel 13:3; Judges 5:14"> And when he arrived he sounded the trumpet in the hill country of Ephraim, and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">an</a></sup>went down from the hill country with him leading them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 12:5; Judges 7:9; Judges 7:15"> And he said to them, “Follow after me! Yahweh has given Moab your enemies into your hand.” So they went down after him, and they captured the fords of the Jordan toward Moab; and they did not allow anyone to cross over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 3:17; Psalm 17:10"> And they struck Moab at that time, about ten thousand men, ⸤all strong and able men⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “all fat and men of strength”">ao</a></sup>no one escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 3:11; Judges 5:31"> And Moab was subdued on that day under the hand of Israel. And the land rested eighty years.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Hebrew “year”">ap</a></sup></span>
</div>
