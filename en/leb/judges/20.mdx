<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:22; Judges 19:25–26; Genesis 19:4–8"> The lords of Gibeah rose up against me and surrounded the house at night. They intended to kill me, and they raped my concubine, and she died.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jonah 1:7; Nehemiah 11:1; Joshua 14:2"> So then, this is what we will do to Gibeah: we will go up against her by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> We will take ten men of one hundred from all the tribes of Israel, and one hundred of one thousand, and one thousand of ten thousand, to bring provisions to the troops, to repay them when they come to Gibeah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “Geba”">i</a></sup>of Benjamin for all the disgraceful things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “thing”">j</a></sup>they did in Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the men of Israel were gathered to the city as one body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “man”">k</a></sup>united.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Deuteronomy 20:10; Romans 12:18"> Then the tribes of Israel sent men throughout all the tribes of Benjamin, saying, “What is this wickedness that happened among you?</span>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 8:14; Isaiah 47:11; Job 21:13"> Then ten thousand well-trained<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Or “choice/chosen”">bc</a></sup>men from all Israel came out against Gibeah, and the battle became fierce;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="Or “the battle became heavy”">bd</a></sup>they did not know that disaster was about to ⸤close in⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-be" id="fnref-be" data-fn="Literally “touching”">be</a></sup>on them.</span>
