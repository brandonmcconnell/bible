## Israel Disobeys Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Genesis 17:7–8; Leviticus 26:42"> And the angel of Yahweh went up from Gilgal to Bokim and said, “I brought you up from Egypt, and I brought you to the land that I had promised to your ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fathers”">a</a></sup>I said, ‘I will never break my covenant with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2–4; Jeremiah 2:36; Ezra 9:1–3"> And as for you, do not ⸤make a covenant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “cut a covenant”">b</a></sup>with the inhabitants of this land; break down their altars.’ But you did not listen to my voice. ⸤Why would you do such a thing⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “What is this thing you have done?”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 23:13; Numbers 33:55; Deuteronomy 7:16"> Now I say, I will not drive them out from before you; they will ⸤become as thorns⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “become snares;” some ancient manuscripts read “be adversaries”">d</a></sup>for you, and their gods will be a trap for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Proverbs 17:10; Zechariah 12:10"> And as the angel of Yahweh spoke these words to all the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>the people ⸤wept bitterly⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “lifted up their voices and they wept”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:9; Genesis 35:8; Joshua 7:26"> And they called the name of this place Bokim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="“Bokim” means “weepers”">g</a></sup>and there they sacrificed to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joshua Dies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 24:28–31; Joshua 22:6"> And Joshua sent the people away, and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>went each to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">i</a></sup>own inheritance to take possession of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Joshua 24:31; 2 Chronicles 24:2"> And the people served Yahweh all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders who outlived Joshua, who saw all the great work Yahweh had done for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:29–30"> And Joshua son of Nun, servant of Yahweh, died ⸤at the age of one hundred and ten years⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a son of one hundred and ten years”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 19:50; Joshua 24:30"> They buried him within the border of his inheritance in Timnah-heres, in the hill country of Ephraim north of Mount Gaash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:12; Titus 1:16; Exodus 5:2"> Moreover, that entire generation was gathered to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">k</a></sup>ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>and another generation grew up after them who did not know Yahweh or the work he had done for Israel.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Judges 2:6–10 is repeated in Joshua 24:28–31">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel Worships the Baals

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 4:1; Judges 6:1; Judges 3:7"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">n</a></sup>did evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and ⸤they served⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “they worshiped”">o</a></sup>the Baals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 6:14–15"> They abandoned Yahweh the God of their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “fathers”">p</a></sup>who brought them out from the land of Egypt. They ⸤followed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “went after”">q</a></sup>other gods from the gods of the people who were around them; and they bowed down to them, and they provoked the anger of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Psalm 106:36; Judges 3:7"> They abandoned Yahweh, and they served Baal and the Ashtaroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 17:20; Psalm 106:40–42; Deuteronomy 32:30"> So ⸤the anger of Yahweh was kindled⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “The nose of Yahweh became hot”">r</a></sup>against Israel, and he gave them into the hand of plunderers; and they plundered them, and he sold them into the hand of their enemies from all sides. They were unable to withstand their enemies any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Joshua 23:15–16; Leviticus 26:14–46"> ⸤Whenever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “At all that”">s</a></sup>they went out, the hand of Yahweh was against them to harm them, just as Yahweh warned, and just as Yahweh had sworn to them. And ⸤they were very distressed⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “it was very cramped for them”">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 13:20; Psalm 106:43–45; 1 Samuel 12:11"> Then Yahweh raised up leaders,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “judges”">u</a></sup>and they delivered them from the hand of their plunderers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Deuteronomy 9:12; Judges 2:7"> But they did not listen to their leaders,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “judges”">v</a></sup>but lusted after other gods and bowed down to them. They turned away quickly from the way that their ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “fathers”">w</a></sup>went, who had obeyed the commandment of Yahweh; they did not do as their ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Joshua 1:5; Exodus 2:24"> And when Yahweh raised leaders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “judges”">x</a></sup>for them, Yahweh was with the leader,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “judge”">y</a></sup>and he delivered them from the hand of their enemies all the days of the leader,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “judge”">z</a></sup>for Yahweh was moved by their groaning because of their persecutors and oppressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Judges 4:1; Matthew 23:32"> But when the leader<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “judge”">aa</a></sup>died they relapsed and acted corruptly, more than their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “fathers”">ab</a></sup>following other gods, serving them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “worshiping them”">ac</a></sup>and bowing down to them. They would not give up their deeds or their stubborn ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Joshua 23:16; Exodus 32:10–11"> ⸤So the anger of Yahweh burned⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “So the nose of Yahweh became hot”">ad</a></sup>against Israel, and he said, “Because this people transgressed my covenant that I commanded their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “fathers”">ae</a></sup>and have not obeyed my voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 23:13; Judges 2:3; Judges 3:3"> I will not again drive out anyone from before them from the nations that Joshua left when he died,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Deuteronomy 8:16; Deuteronomy 13:3"> in order to test Israel whether or not they would observe the way of Yahweh, to walk in it just as their ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “fathers”">af</a></sup>did.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> So Yahweh left those nations; he did not drive them out at once, and he did not give them into the hand of Joshua.</span>
</div>
</div>
