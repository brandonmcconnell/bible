## Samson Marries

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; Joshua 19:43; 1 John 2:16"> And Samson went down to Timnah, and he saw a woman in Timnah from the daughters of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 34:4; Genesis 21:21; Genesis 24:2–3"> He went up and told his father and mother, and he said, “I saw a woman in Timnah from the daughters of the Philistines; so then, take her for me as a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 15:18; 1 Samuel 17:36; 1 Samuel 14:6"> But his father and mother said to him, “Is there not a wife among the daughters of your relatives, or among all our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “my”">a</a></sup>people, that you must take a wife from the uncircumcised Philistines?” But Samson said to his father, “Take her for me because ⸤she pleases me⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “she is right in my eyes”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Psalm 115:3; Judges 13:1"> His father and mother did not know that this was from Yahweh; he was seeking for an occasion against the Philistines. Now at that time the Philistines were ruling<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “having control”">c</a></sup>in Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Samson and his father and mother went down to Timnah, and they came to the vineyards of Timnah, and suddenly a young lion came roaring to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6"> And the Spirit of Yahweh rushed upon him, and he tore the lion apart as one might tear apart a male kid goat (⸤he was bare-handed⸥).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “there was nothing in his hand”">d</a></sup>But he did not tell his father and mother what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then he went down and talked to the woman, and ⸤she pleased Samson⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “she did what was right in the eyes of Samson”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Genesis 29:21"> And he returned after awhile ⸤to marry her⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to take her”">f</a></sup>and he turned aside to see the carcass of the lion, and there was a swarm of wild honey bees in the body of the lion, and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:25–30; Proverbs 25:15"> He scraped it out into his hands, and he went on, eating it as he went. And he went to his father and mother and gave some to them, and they ate it. But he did not tell them that he had scraped the honey from the body of the lion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 29:22; Revelation 19:9; Esther 1:7–22"> His father went down to the woman, and Samson prepared there a feast, as young men were accustomed to doing this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; 1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 10:23"> When they saw him, they took thirty companions, and they were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1; Ezekiel 17:2; Genesis 45:22"> And Samson said to them, “Let me tell you a riddle. If you can fully explain it to me within the seven days of the feast, and find it out, I will give to you thirty linen garments and thirty festal garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> But if you are unable to explain it to me, you must give me thirty linen garments and thirty festal garments.” So they said to him, “Tell your riddle; let us hear it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:2–4; 1 Kings 17:6; Romans 8:37"> He said to them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“From the eater came out food,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">From the strong came out sweet.”</span><br />But they were unable to explain the riddle for three days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 16:5; Judges 15:6; Proverbs 6:26"> When it was the fourth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “seventh”; other ancient translations have “fourth”">g</a></sup>day, they said to Samson’s wife, “Entice your husband and tell us the riddle, or we will burn you and your father’s house with fire. Have you invited us to rob us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 16:15; Genesis 2:24"> And Samson’s wife wept before him, and she said, “You must hate me; you do not love me. You told the riddle to ⸤my people⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the sons/children of my people”">h</a></sup>but you have not explained it to me.” He said to her, “I have not explained it to my father and mother. Why should I explain it to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 16:16; Job 2:9; Judges 16:13"> She wept before him the seven days of their feast; and it happened, because she nagged him, on the seventh day he explained it to her, and she told the riddle to ⸤her people⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the sons/children of her people”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The men of the city said to him on the seventh day before the sun went down,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What is sweeter than honey?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">What is stronger than a lion?”</span><br />And he said to them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If you had not plowed with my heifer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you would not have found out my riddle.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> And the Spirit of Yahweh rushed on him, and he went down to Ashkelon. He killed thirty men from them, and he took their belongings, and he gave festal garments to the ones that explained the riddle. ⸤He was angry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “his nose was hot”">j</a></sup>and he went up to his father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 15:2; John 3:29; Psalm 55:12–13"> And Samson’s wife was given to his companion who was his best man.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “friend”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
