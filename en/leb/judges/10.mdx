## The Philistines and Ammonites Afflict the Israelites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:16; Judges 3:9; Joshua 15:48"> After Abimelech, Tola son of Puah son of Dod, a man of Issachar, rose up to deliver Israel; and he was living at Shamir in the hill country of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he judged Israel twenty-three years. And he died and was buried in Shamir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 32:29; Genesis 31:48"> After him Jair the Gileadite rose up, and he judged Israel twenty-two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:41; Judges 5:10; Deuteronomy 3:14"> And he had thirty sons who would ride on thirty donkeys, and they had thirty towns that are in the land of Gilead that they called Havvoth Jair until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Jair died and was buried in Kamon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:33; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> And again, the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>did evil in the eyes of Yahweh. They served the Baals, the Ashtaroth, the gods of Aram, Sidon, Moab, and the gods of the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">b</a></sup>and Philistines; they abandoned Yahweh and did not serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Deuteronomy 32:16–22; 1 Samuel 12:9–10"> And ⸤the anger of Yahweh burned⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the nose of Yahweh became hot”">c</a></sup>against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of the Philistines and the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 30:13; Judges 10:5"> They crushed and oppressed the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>in that year; for eighteen years they crushed all the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>who were beyond the Jordan, in the land of the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">g</a></sup>which is in Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:1–2; Deuteronomy 28:65; 2 Chronicles 15:5"> The ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">h</a></sup>crossed the Jordan to fight also against Judah, Benjamin, and the house of Ephraim; and Israel was very distressed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:10; Psalm 107:28; Psalm 107:13"> Then the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>cried out to Yahweh, saying, “We have sinned against you; we have abandoned our God and served the Baals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 3:31; Numbers 21:21–25; Exodus 14:30"> And Yahweh said to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>“Did I not deliver you from the Egyptians,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “from Egypt”">k</a></sup>the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">l</a></sup>from the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">m</a></sup>and from the Philistines?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “Philistine”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 6:3; 2 Chronicles 26:6–7; Psalm 106:42–43"> And when the Sidonians, the Amalekites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “Amalekite”">o</a></sup>and the Maonites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “Maonite”">p</a></sup>oppressed you, you cried out to me, and I delivered you from their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:13; 1 Chronicles 28:9; Judges 2:12"> Yet you have abandoned me and served other gods. Therefore I will no longer deliver you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37–38; 1 Kings 18:27–28"> Go and cry out to the gods whom you have chosen; let them deliver you in the time of your trouble.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:26; Proverbs 28:13"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>said to Yahweh, “We have sinned; ⸤do to us accordingly as you see fit⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “do to us according to the good in your eyes”">r</a></sup>only please deliver us this day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; Psalm 106:44–45; Ezekiel 18:30–32"> So they removed the foreign gods from their midst and served Yahweh; and ⸤he could no longer bear⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “his soul was short with”">s</a></sup>the misery of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Genesis 31:49; Judges 11:11"> And the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">t</a></sup>were summoned,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “were called to arms”">u</a></sup>and they camped in Gilead. And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup>gathered and camped at Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 11:11; Judges 12:7; Judges 1:1"> The people, the ones commanding Gilead, ⸤said to each other⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “said each to his neighbor”">w</a></sup>“Who is the man that will begin to fight against the ⸤Ammonites⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">x</a></sup>He will be as head over all the inhabitants of Gilead.”</span>
</div>
</div>
