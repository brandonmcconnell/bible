## Israel Continues Its Conquest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:18; Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:28"> After the death of Joshua, the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>inquired of Yahweh, saying, “Who will go up first for us against the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">b</a></sup>to fight against them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 2:3"> And Yahweh said, “Judah will go up. I hereby give the land into his hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:11; Joshua 19:1; Judges 1:17"> And Judah said to Simeon his brother, “Go up with me into my allotment, and let us fight against the Canaanites; then I too will go with you into your allotment.” And Simeon went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:8; Exodus 23:28–29; Joshua 11:6–8"> And Judah went up, and Yahweh gave the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">c</a></sup>and the Perizzites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">d</a></sup>into their hand, and they defeated ten thousand men at Bezek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> At Bezek they came upon Adoni-bezek, and they fought against him and defeated the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">e</a></sup>and the Perizzites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “Perizzite”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Adoni-bezek fled, but they pursued after him; they caught him and cut off ⸤his thumbs and big toes⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the thumbs of his hands and feet”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:33; Revelation 13:10; Luke 6:37–38"> Adoni-bezek said, “Seventy kings with ⸤their thumbs and big toes⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the thumbs of their hands and feet”">h</a></sup>cut off used to pick up scraps under my table; just as I have done, so God has repaid to me. And they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 1:21"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">i</a></sup>of Judah fought against Jerusalem, and they captured it, ⸤put it to the sword⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “they struck it with the mouth of the sword”">j</a></sup>and ⸤set the city on fire⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the city they sent away with fire”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:13–20; Joshua 11:21; Joshua 10:36"> Afterward the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">l</a></sup>of Judah pursued to fight against the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">m</a></sup>who were living in the hill country, the Negev,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="An arid region south of the Judean hills">n</a></sup>and the Shephelah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="A geographical region associated with an area of low country on the western edge of the Judaean hills.">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 14:15; Jeremiah 9:23"> And Judah went against the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">p</a></sup>living in Hebron (the former name of Hebron was Kiriath Arba). And they defeated Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 10:38–39"> And from there they went to the inhabitants of Debir (the former name of Debir was Kiriath Sepher).</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:16–17; 1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 18:23"> And Caleb said, “Whoever attacks Kiriath Sepher and captures it, I will give to him Acsah my daughter as a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 3:9"> Othniel son of Kenaz, the younger brother of Caleb, captured it, and he gave to him Acsah his daughter as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:18–19"> When she came to him, she urged him to ask her father for a field. As she dismounted from the donkey, Caleb said to her, “⸤What do you want⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “What is for you?”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Peter 3:9"> And she said to him, “Give me ⸤a gift⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “blessing”">r</a></sup>you have given me the land of the Negev,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="An arid region south of the Judaean hills">s</a></sup>and give me also a spring of water.” And Caleb gave to her the upper and lower spring.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Judges 1:11–15 is almost identical to Joshua 15:13–19">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:29–32; Judges 4:11; 1 Samuel 15:6"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">u</a></sup>of the Kenite, Moses’ father-in-law, went up with the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">v</a></sup>of Judah from the city of palms into the wilderness of Judah, which is in the Negev<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="An arid region south of the Judaean hills">w</a></sup>near Arad. And they went<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “he went”">x</a></sup>and settled with the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:3; Joshua 19:4"> And Judah went with his brother Simeon, and they defeated the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">y</a></sup>inhabiting Zephath; they utterly destroyed it, so he called the name of the city Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; Judges 3:3; Joshua 15:45–47"> Judah captured Gaza and its territory, Ashkelon and its territory, and Ekron and its territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; Judges 1:2; Psalm 60:12"> And Yahweh was with Judah, and he took possession of the hill country, but they could not drive out the inhabitants of the plain because they had chariots of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13–14; Numbers 14:24"> They gave Hebron to Caleb just as Moses said, and he drove out the three sons of Anak from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 15:63; Judges 19:10–12; Judges 1:8"> But the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">z</a></sup>of Benjamin did not drive out the Jebusites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “Jebusite”">aa</a></sup>who lived in Jerusalem, so the Jebusites have lived among the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">ab</a></sup>of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 1:19; 2 Kings 18:7; Numbers 1:32"> Likewise, the house of Joseph went up against Bethel, and Yahweh was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 35:6; Genesis 48:3"> And the house of Joseph spied out Bethel (the former name of the city was Luz).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 2:12–14; 1 Samuel 30:15"> And when the spies saw a man leaving the city,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “going out from the city”">ac</a></sup>they said to him, “Please show us the entrance of the city, and we will deal kindly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “do a loyal love”">ad</a></sup>with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:22–25"> So he showed them the entrance of the city, and they struck the city with ⸤the edge of the sword⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword”">ae</a></sup>but they let go the man and all his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6; 2 Chronicles 1:17"> And the man went to the land of the Hittites, and he built a city and named it Luz; this is its name to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 17:11–13; Jeremiah 48:10; Psalm 106:34–35"> Manasseh did not drive out Beth-Sean and its towns, or Taanach and its towns, or the inhabitants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Hebrew “inhabitant”">af</a></sup>of Dor and its towns, or the inhabitants of Ibleam and its towns, or the inhabitants of Megiddo and its towns; the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">ag</a></sup>were determined to live in this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And it happened, when Israel grew strong, they put the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">ah</a></sup>to forced labor, but they never totally drove them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:16"> Ephraim did not drive out the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">ai</a></sup>living in Gezer, so the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">aj</a></sup>lived in their midst in Gezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:15"> Zebulun did not drive out the inhabitants of Kitron or Nahalol, so the Canaanites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">ak</a></sup>lived in their midst and became subjected to forced labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–30"> Asher did not drive out the inhabitants of Acco, Sidon, Ahlab, Aczib, Helbah, Aphik, or Rehob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 106:34–35"> so the Asherites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “Asherite”">al</a></sup>lived in the midst of the Canaanites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">am</a></sup>the inhabitants of the land, for they did not drive them out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–38; Judges 1:30; Psalm 18:24"> Naphtali did not drive out the inhabitants of Beth Shemesh or Beth-anath, but lived in the midst of the Canaanites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Hebrew “Canaanite”">an</a></sup>the inhabitants of the land; the inhabitants of Beth Shemesh and Beth-anath became forced labor for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:1"> The Amorites pressed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “forced”">ao</a></sup>the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">ap</a></sup>of Dan to the hill country, and they did not allow them to come down to the plain;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Joshua 19:42; Judges 12:12; Joshua 10:12"> the Amorites were determined to live in Har-heres,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “Mount Heres”">aq</a></sup>in Aijalon, and in Shaalbim, but the hand of the house of Joseph was heavy on them, and they became subjected to forced labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 34:4; Joshua 15:2–3"> The border of the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">ar</a></sup>ran from the ascent of Akrabbim from Sela and upward.</span>
</div>
