## Samson and Delilah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:47; Ezra 9:1–2; Genesis 38:16–18"> Samson went down to Gaza; there he saw a prostitute and ⸤had sex with her⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “went into her”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 118:10–12; 1 Samuel 23:26; Acts 9:24"> The Gazites were told, “Samson has come here,” so they surrounded the place and lay in ambush for him all night at the city gate. They kept silent all night, saying, “We will wait until the morning light, and then we will kill him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Psalm 107:16; Isaiah 63:1–5"> But Samson lay until the middle of the night; he got up in the middle of the night and took hold of the doors of the city gate and the two door posts, tore them loose with the bar, put them on his shoulders, and carried them up to the top of the hill that is ⸤in front of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “on the face of”">b</a></sup>Hebron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Proverbs 22:14; Nehemiah 13:26"> After this he fell in love with a woman in the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">c</a></sup>of Sorek, and her name was Delilah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Judges 14:15; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> And the rulers of the Philistines came up to her and said, “Entice him and find out what makes his strength so great, and how we can overpower him, so that we may bind him up in order to subdue him; each of us will give you eleven hundred pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21; Proverbs 6:26; Proverbs 22:14"> So Delilah said to Samson, “Please tell me what makes your strength so great, and with what can you be tied up to subdue you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:2–3; Romans 3:8; 1 Samuel 27:10"> Samson said to her, “If you tie me up with seven fresh bowstrings that are not dried up, I will become weak like everyone else.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26"> So the rulers of the Philistines brought up to her seven fresh bowstrings that were not dried up, and she tied him up with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 58:9"> The ambush was sitting in wait for her in an inner room. And she said to him, “The Philistines are upon you Samson!” And he snapped the bowstrings just as flax fiber snaps when it comes close to fire. And the secret of his strength remained unknown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 16:13; Luke 22:48; Judges 16:7"> Delilah said to Samson, “Look, you have mocked me and told me lies. Please tell me how you can be bound.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:5; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 13:3"> He said to her, “If they tie me tightly with new ropes that have not been used, I will become weak and be like everyone else.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So Delilah took new ropes and tied him up with them, and she said to him, “The Philistines are upon you, Samson!” (The ambush was sitting in an inner room.) But he snapped them from his arms like thread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Delilah said to Samson, “Until now you have mocked me and told lies to me. Tell me how you can be bound.” And he said to her, “If you weave seven locks of my head with warp-threads.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Many modern translations include an additional phrase found in the Greek translation: “and fasten it with a pin, then I will become weak and be like everyone else. So while he slept, Delilah took the seven locks of his head and wove them”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 106:43; Ezra 9:13–14"> She fastened it with the pin and said to him, “The Philistines are upon you, Samson!” And Samson woke up from his sleep and tore loose the loom pin of the web and the warp-threads.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “warp-thread”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 14:16; 1 John 2:15–16; John 14:21–24"> And she said to him, “How can you say, ‘I love you,’ when your heart is not with me? You have mocked me these three times, and you have not told me how your strength is so great.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 7:21–23; Luke 18:5; Mark 14:24"> And because she nagged him day after day with her words, and pestered him, ⸤his soul grew impatient to the point of death⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “his inner self grew tired/impatient to death”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 7:5; Judges 13:5; Proverbs 12:23"> So ⸤he confided everything to her⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “he told her all his heart”">g</a></sup>and he said to her, “A razor ⸤has never touched⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “has never gone up”">h</a></sup>my head, for I am a ⸤Nazirite of God⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “consecrated of God” or “devoted of God”">i</a></sup>⸤from birth⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “from the womb of my mother”">j</a></sup>If I am shaved my strength will leave me, and I will become weak, like everyone else.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Judges 16:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> Delilah realized that ⸤he had confided in her⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “he had told her all his heart”">k</a></sup>so she sent and called the rulers of the Philistines, saying, “Come up one more time, for ⸤he has confided in me⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “he has told me all his heart”">l</a></sup>And the rulers of the Philistines came up, and they brought the money ⸤with them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “in their hand”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 7:26–27; Proverbs 7:21–23; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And she put him to sleep on her lap; then she called the men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “man”">n</a></sup>and shaved off seven locks of his head. Then she began to subdue him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “humiliate him”">o</a></sup>and his strength went away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 14:42–43; 1 Samuel 16:14; Joshua 7:12"> And she said to him, “The Philistines are upon you, Samson!” And he woke up from his sleep and said, “I will go out just like every other time and shake myself free,” but he did not know that Yahweh had left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 2:19; Proverbs 14:14; Isaiah 47:2"> And the Philistines seized him, gouged his eyes, and brought him to Gaza. They tied him up with bronze shackles, and he became a grinder ⸤in the prison⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “in the house of the prisoners”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 107:13–14; Psalm 106:44–45; Leviticus 26:44"> But the hair of his head began to grow back after it had been shaved off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–5; Proverbs 24:17; Psalm 35:15–16"> The rulers of the Philistines had gathered to sacrifice a great sacrifice to Dagon their god and to rejoice. And they said, “Our god has given Samson our enemy into our hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 5:4; Daniel 5:23; Judges 15:16"> And the people saw him, and they praised their god, for they said, “Our god has given into our hand those who hate us, devastate our land, and have ⸤killed many of us⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “made numerous our slain”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 19:6; Judges 9:27"> After awhile, when their hearts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “heart”">r</a></sup>were merry, they said, “Call Samson and let him entertain us.” And they called Samson ⸤from the prison⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “from the house of the prisoner”">s</a></sup>and ⸤he entertained them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “he made sport before them”">t</a></sup>And they made him stand between the pillars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then Samson said to the servant who was holding him by his hand, “Position me so that I can touch the pillars on which the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “temple”">u</a></sup>rests, so I can lean on them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:8; 2 Samuel 11:2; Joshua 2:8"> And the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “temple”">v</a></sup>was full of men and women, and all of the rulers of the Philistines were there—about three thousand men and women were on the roof watching the performance of Samson.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Samson’s Revenge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:15; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalm 143:12"> And Samson called to Yahweh and said, “My Lord Yahweh, remember me! Please give me strength this one time, O God, so that I can repay with one act of revenge to the Philistines for my eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And Samson reached out and held two of the middle pillars on which the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “temple”">w</a></sup>was resting, and he leaned on them, one on his right and one on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Matthew 16:25; Matthew 24:38–39"> And Samson said, “Let me die with the Philistines.” And he pushed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “he caused to bend”">x</a></sup>with all his strength, and the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “temple”">y</a></sup>fell on the rulers and all of the people who were with him. And the dead whom he killed in his death were more than those he killed in his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 15:20; Judges 13:25; Judges 13:2"> His brothers and ⸤his whole family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “all the house of his father”">z</a></sup>came down and picked him up; and they brought him up and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol in the tomb of Manoah his father; he judged Israel twenty years.</span>
</div>
