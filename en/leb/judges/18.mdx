## The Tribe of Dan Seeks Territory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 17:6; Judges 19:1; Judges 1:34"> In those days there was no king in Israel. And in those days the tribe of the Danites was seeking territory for itself to live in, because until that day it had not been allotted territory among the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 2:1; Judges 18:11"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">a</a></sup>of Dan sent from the whole number of their clan five ⸤capable men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons of physical strength”">b</a></sup>from Zorah and Eshtaol to spy out the land and to explore it. And they said to them, “Go, explore the land.” And they went to the hill country of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, and they spent the night there.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “they lodged there”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 12:6; Genesis 27:22; Matthew 26:73"> While they were with the house of Micah, they recognized the voice of the young Levite, and they turned aside there and said to him, “Who brought you here? What are you doing in this place, and ⸤what is your business here⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “What is for you here?”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 17:10; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 28:21"> And he said to them, “Micah did such and such for me and hired me, and I became his priest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:10; Isaiah 30:1; 1 Kings 22:5"> And they said to him, “Please inquire of God that we may know whether our journey that we are going on will be successful.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 22:15; Psalm 33:18"> And the priest said to them, “Go in peace. Yahweh is in front of you on the journey you want to go on.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:27–28; 1 Samuel 3:13"> And the five men went and came to Laish, and they observed the people who were living according to the customs of the Sidonians, quiet and unsuspecting, and lacking nothing in the land, and possessing restraint.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or perhaps “prosperity”; the Hebrew of this word is uncertain">e</a></sup>And they were far from the Sidonians and had no word with anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 18:11; Judges 18:2; Judges 13:2"> They came to their relatives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “brothers”">f</a></sup>at Zorah and Eshtaol, and their relatives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “brothers”">g</a></sup>said to them, “What do you report?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 2:23–24; Numbers 13:30; Joshua 18:3"> And they said to them, “Come, let us go up against them; for we have seen the land, and it is very good. Will you do nothing? Do not hesitate to go, to enter, to possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Judges 18:7; Deuteronomy 2:29"> When you go you will come to an unsuspecting people, and the land ⸤is spread out on all sides⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “is wide of hands”">h</a></sup>God has given a place into your hands where there is no lack of anything that is on the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 18:16"> Six hundred men from the clan of the Danites from Zorah and Eshtaol, armed with weapons of war, set out from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 15:60; 2 Chronicles 1:4"> They went up and encamped at Kiriath Jearim in Judah. Therefore they called this place Camp of Dan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is “Mahaneh-dan”">i</a></sup>to this day; it is west of Kiriath Jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 24:30; Judges 19:1; Judges 18:2–3"> From there they crossed over to the hill country of Ephraim, and they came to the house of Micah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:3–4; 1 Samuel 14:28"> And the five men that went out to spy out the land (that is, Laish) responded and said to their relatives,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “brothers”">j</a></sup>“Do you know that there are in these houses an ephod, teraphim, and an idol of cast metal? So then, consider what you must do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 43:27; 1 Samuel 17:22; John 14:27"> So they turned to that direction, and they came to the house of the young Levite, the house of Micah, and ⸤they greeted him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “they asked for him in peace”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:11"> And six hundred men from the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">l</a></sup>of Dan, armed with their weapons of war, were standing at the entrance of the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 17:4–5; Judges 18:2"> And the five men that went to spy out the land went up, and they entered there and took the carved divine image, ephod, teraphim, and the molten image. The priest was standing at the entrance of the gate with the six hundred men armed with the weapons of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When these went to Micah’s house, they took the divine carved image, ephod, the teraphim, and the molten image, and the priest asked them, “What are you doing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 21:5; Judges 17:10"> And they said to him, “Keep quiet! Put your hand on your mouth and come with us and be for us a father and a priest. Is it better being a priest for a house of one man or being a priest for a tribe and clan in Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Ezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 30:15"> ⸤The priest accepted the offer⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “It was good in the heart of the priest”">m</a></sup>and he took the ephod, teraphim, and molten image and went along with the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And they turned<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “resumed their journey”">n</a></sup>and went and put the little children, the livestock, and the valuable property in front of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> When they were at a distance from the house, Micah and the men who were in the houses that were near the house of Micah cried out, and they overtook the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">o</a></sup>of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 114:5; 2 Samuel 14:5; 1 Samuel 11:5"> And they called to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">p</a></sup>of Dan, who turned around to face them, and they said to Micah, “What is the matter with you that you assembled together?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18–19; Isaiah 44:18–20; Psalm 115:8"> He said, “You took away my gods that I had made, and the priest, and then you go away. What is now left for me? How can you say to me, ‘What is the matter?’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 3:5; 1 Samuel 30:6"> And the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">q</a></sup>of Dan said to him, “You should not let your voice be heard among us, so that ⸤ill-tempered men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “men of bitter soul”">r</a></sup>⸤will not attack you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “will not fall on you”">s</a></sup>⸤and take your life⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “and you will lose your life”">t</a></sup>and the lives of your household.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">u</a></sup>of Dan went their way. When Micah saw that they were stronger than him, he turned to return to his house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:7; Judges 18:10"> And they took what Micah had made, and his priest, and they came to Laish, to a quiet and unsuspecting people, and ⸤they put them to the sword⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “they put them to the mouth of the sword”">v</a></sup>and burned the city with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; Judges 18:7; Numbers 13:21"> There was no deliverer, because it was far from Sidon, and ⸤they had had no dealings with anyone⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “a thing was not for them with anyone”">w</a></sup>It was in the valley that belonged to Beth-rehob, and they rebuilt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “built”">x</a></sup>the city and lived in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Genesis 14:14; 1 Kings 15:20"> And they called the name of the city Dan, after Dan their ancestor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “father”">y</a></sup>who was born to Israel; but the former name of the city was Laish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:10–11; Judges 13:1; Judges 17:5"> And the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">z</a></sup>of Dan set up for themselves the carved divine image, and Jonathan son of Gershom, son of Manasseh,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Other ancient versions read “Moses”">aa</a></sup>he and his sons were priests for the tribe of the Danites until the time of the captivity of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 1:3; Judges 19:18"> So they set up for themselves the carved divine image that Micah had made, all the days that the house of God was in Shiloh.</span>
</div>
</div>
