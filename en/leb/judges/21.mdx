## A Decision Is Made About the Tribe of Benjamin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Mark 6:23; Acts 23:12"> The men of Israel had sworn at Mizpah, saying, “None of us will give his daughter to Benjamin as a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 20:26; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 30:4"> And the people of Bethel came and sat there until evening before God, and they lifted their voices and ⸤wept bitterly⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “they cried a great weeping”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 74:1; Joshua 7:7–9; Isaiah 63:17"> And they said, “Why, Yahweh, God of Israel, has it happened today in Israel that one tribe is lacking from Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:25; 1 Kings 8:64; Psalm 78:34–35"> On the next day the people rose early, and they built there an altar and sacrificed burnt offerings and fellowship offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:1"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>said, “Who in the assembly has not come up from all the tribes of Israel to Yahweh?” For a solemn oath was taken concerning whoever did not come up to Yahweh at Mizpah, saying, “He will certainly be put to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 21:15; Judges 20:23; Luke 19:41–42"> But the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>had compassion for Benjamin, their relatives,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “his brother”">d</a></sup>and they said, “Today one tribe is cut off from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; 1 Samuel 14:28–29"> What will we do for them—for the ones being left over—for wives? For we have sworn by Yahweh not to give to them our daughters as wives.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–3; 2 Samuel 2:5–6"> They asked, “Which one is there from the tribes of Israel who did not come up to Yahweh at Mizpah?” And behold, no one came from Jabesh-gilead to the camp, to the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The people were counted, and no one was there from the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:23; Judges 21:5; 1 Samuel 11:7"> And the congregation sent there twelve thousand men from the troops, and they commanded them, saying, “Go, strike the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead with ⸤the edge of the sword⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of the sword”">e</a></sup>and the women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 31:17–18; Deuteronomy 2:34"> This is the thing you will do: ⸤you will destroy⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “you will devote to destruction”">f</a></sup>every man and ⸤every woman who had sex with a man⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “every woman who knows the bed of a man”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremiah 7:12; Psalm 78:60"> And they found among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead four hundred young virgins<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “virgin”">h</a></sup>who had not ⸤had sex with a man⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “known a man, as far as the bed of a male”">i</a></sup>and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 20:47; Deuteronomy 20:10; Ephesians 2:17"> Then the congregation sent and spoke all this to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">j</a></sup>of Benjamin who were at the rock of Rimmon, and ⸤they assured them they would not be hurt⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “they proclaimed peace to them”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 21:12; 1 Corinthians 7:2; Judges 20:47"> And Benjamin returned at that time, and they gave to them the women whom they kept alive from Jabesh-gilead; but they were not enough for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 21:6; 1 Chronicles 15:13; Isaiah 58:12"> And the people felt sorry for Benjamin because ⸤Yahweh weakened the tribes of Israel⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “Yahweh make a breach with the tribes of Israel”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So the elders of the congregation said, “What should we do for the remaining ones for wives, since the women from Benjamin have been destroyed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 26:55; Numbers 36:7"> And they said, “There must be a remnant for Benjamin, so that a tribe will not be blotted out from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 11:35"> But we cannot give them wives from our daughters.” (For the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>swore, saying, “Cursed be anyone who gives a wife to Benjamin.”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:10; Deuteronomy 16:1; Deuteronomy 16:13"> And they said, “Look, the annual feast of Yahweh is in Shiloh, which is to the north of Bethel, ⸤east⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “rising of the sun”">n</a></sup>of the main road that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They instructed the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">o</a></sup>of Benjamin, saying, “Go, lie in ambush in the vineyards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:34; Exodus 15:20; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> and watch and look; when the daughters of Shiloh dance in the dances, come out from the vineyards and seize for yourselves a wife from the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 21:1; Judges 21:18; Judges 21:14"> And if their fathers or their brothers complain to us, we will say to them, ‘Allow us to have them, because we did not capture a wife for each man in the battle, and because you did not give them to them, ⸤or else⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “as the time”">p</a></sup>you would have been guilty.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:48"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">q</a></sup>of Benjamin did likewise, and they took wives for each of them from the dancers whom they seized, and they went and returned to their territory, and they rebuilt the cities and they lived in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">r</a></sup>dispersed from there at that time according to tribe and family; and they went out from there, each one to their own territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8; Judges 17:6; Proverbs 3:5"> In those days there was no king in Israel; each one did what was right in his own eyes.</span>
</div>
</div>
