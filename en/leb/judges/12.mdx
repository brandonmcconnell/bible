## Tribal Conflict Between Gilead and Ephraim

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 8:1; James 4:1–2; James 3:16"> The men of Ephraim were called to arms, and they crossed over to Zaphon and said to Jephthah, “Why did you cross over and make war against the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">a</a></sup>and why did you not call us to go with you? We will burn down your house over you with fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:12–33"> And Jephthah said to them, “I and my people were engaged in great conflict with the ⸤Ammonites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">b</a></sup>I called you, but you did not deliver me from their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 28:21; Job 13:14"> I saw that you would not deliver us; ⸤I risked my own life⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “I put my life in my hand”">c</a></sup>and I crossed over to the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">d</a></sup>and Yahweh gave them into my hand. Why have you come up to me this day to fight against me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10; Deuteronomy 3:12–17; Proverbs 15:1"> Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead, and he made war with Ephraim; and the men of Gilead defeated Ephraim because they said, “You are fugitives of Ephraim, you Gileadites, in the midst of Ephraim and Manasseh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 22:11; Joshua 2:7"> Then Gilead captured the fords of the Jordan from Ephraim, and whenever a fugitive of Ephraim said, “Let me cross over,” the men of Gilead said to him, “Are you an Ephraimite?” When he said, “No,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 69:2; Psalm 69:15; Mark 14:70"> they said to him, “Please say Shibboleth,” and if he said, “Sibboleth”—because he could not ⸤pronounce it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “speak it”">e</a></sup>correctly—they grabbed him and executed him at the fords of Jordan. At that time forty-two thousand from Ephraim fell.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Jephthah judged Israel six years. Then Jephthah the Gileadite died, and he was buried in one of the cities of Gilead.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Micah 5:2; 1 Samuel 16:1; Matthew 2:1"> After him Ibzan from Bethlehem judged Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Judges 12:14"> He had thirty sons. He gave his thirty daughters away in marriage outside his clan and brought in from outside thirty young women for his sons. He judged Israel for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “And”">f</a></sup>Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> After him Elon the Zebulunite judged Israel, and he judged Israel ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:13; 1 Chronicles 6:69; Joshua 19:42"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “And”">g</a></sup>Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried in Aijalon in the land of Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After him Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite judged Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 5:10; Judges 10:4"> He had forty sons and thirty grandsons that rode on seventy male donkeys. He judged Israel for eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 5:14; Judges 3:13; Judges 3:27"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “And”">h</a></sup>Abdon the son of Hillel the Pirathonite died and was buried in Pirathon, in the land of Ephraim in the hill country of the Amalekites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “Amalekite”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
