## The Midianites Oppresses Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26–29; Judges 2:19–20; Judges 2:13–14"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>did evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and Yahweh gave them into the hand of the Midianites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “Midianite”">b</a></sup>for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:38; 1 Samuel 13:6; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> The hand of the Midianites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “Midianite”">c</a></sup>prevailed over Israel; because of the presence of the Midianites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “Midianite”">d</a></sup>the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>made for themselves hiding places that were in the mountains, caves, and strongholds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 6:33; Judges 3:13; Judges 7:12"> And whenever Israel sowed seed, the Midianites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “Midianite”">f</a></sup>Amalekites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “Amalekite”">g</a></sup>and the people of the east would come up against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:51; Micah 6:15"> They would camp against them and destroy the produce of the land ⸤as far as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “until your coming to”">h</a></sup>Gaza; they left no produce in Israel, or sheep, ox, or donkey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 8:10; Jeremiah 46:23"> For they, their livestock, and their tents would come up like a great number of locusts; they and their camels could not be counted; they came into the land and devoured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Jeremiah 5:17; Psalm 50:15"> Israel was very poor because of the presence of the Midianites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “Midianite”">i</a></sup>and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>cried out to Yahweh.</span>

## The Angel of Yahweh Calls Gideon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 17:2; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 13:3"> The angel of Yahweh came and sat under the oak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “terebinth”">r</a></sup>that was at Ophrah that belonged to Jehoash the Abiezrite; and Gideon his son was threshing wheat in the winepress to hide it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “keep it away”">s</a></sup>from the Midianites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “Midianite”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Luke 1:28; Judges 2:18"> The angel of Yahweh appeared to him and said to him, “Yahweh is with you, ⸤you mighty warrior⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “strong/mighty of power”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 77:7–9; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Isaiah 59:1–2"> Gideon said to him, “Excuse me, my lord. If Yahweh is with us, why then has all this happened to us? Where are all his wonderful deeds that our ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “fathers”">v</a></sup>recounted to us, saying, ‘Did not Yahweh bring us up from Egypt?’ But now Yahweh has forsaken us; he has given us into the palm of Midian.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:5–9; Hebrews 11:34; 1 Samuel 12:11"> And Yahweh turned to him and said, “Go in this your strength, and you will deliver Israel from the palm of Midian. Did I not send you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:21; Exodus 3:11; Exodus 4:10"> He said to him, “Excuse me, my lord. How will I deliver Israel? Look, my clan is the weakest in Manasseh, and I am the youngest in my father’s house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Exodus 3:12; Acts 11:21"> And Yahweh said to him, “But I will be with you, and you will defeat Midian ⸤as if they are one man⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “as one man”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 86:17; Exodus 33:13; Isaiah 7:11"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="That is, Gideon">x</a></sup>said to him, “Please, if I have found favor in your eyes, ⸤show me a sign⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “make a sign for me”">y</a></sup>that you are speaking with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 13:15; Genesis 18:3; Genesis 18:5"> Please, do not depart from here until I come back to you and bring out my gift and set it out before you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “in your presence”">z</a></sup>And he said, “I will stay until you return.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 18:6–8; Judges 13:15–19; Leviticus 2:4"> And Gideon went and prepared ⸤a young goat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “a kid of goat”">aa</a></sup>and unleavened cakes from an ephah of flour; he put meat in a basket, and the broth he put in a pot, and he brought them to him under the oak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “terebinth”">ab</a></sup>and presented them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 13:19; 1 Kings 18:33–34"> The angel of God said to him, “Take the meat and the unleavened cakes and put them on this rock; pour the broth over it.” And he did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; 1 Kings 18:38; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> Then the angel of Yahweh reached out the tip of the staff that was in his hand, and he touched the meat and the unleavened cakes; and fire went up from the rock and consumed the meat and the unleavened cakes. And the angel of Yahweh went ⸤from his sight⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “from his eyes”">ac</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; Exodus 33:20; Deuteronomy 5:26"> And Gideon realized that he was the angel of Yahweh; and Gideon said, “Oh, my lord Yahweh! For now I have seen the angel of Yahweh face to face.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; John 20:26; Genesis 43:23"> And Yahweh said to him, “Peace be with you. Do not fear; you will not die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; Exodus 17:15; Judges 8:32"> And Gideon built there an altar to Yahweh, and he called it “Yahweh is peace.” To this day it is still in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Hebrew “Abiezrite”">ad</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; Judges 3:7; Acts 5:29"> Now on that same night Yahweh said to him, “Take the bull of the cattle that belongs to your father, and a second bull seven years old, and pull down the altar of Baal that belongs to your father, and cut down the Asherah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="An Asherah is a cultic pole set up next to an altar symbolizing the goddess Asherah">ae</a></sup>that is beside it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Samuel 24:18; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> and build an altar to Yahweh your God on the top of this stronghold in the proper arrangement, and take a second bull and offer it as a burnt offering with the wood of the Asherah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="An Asherah is a cultic pole set up next to an altar symbolizing the goddess Asherah">af</a></sup>that you will cut down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; John 2:5; Deuteronomy 4:1"> Gideon took ten men from his servants, and he did just as Yahweh told him;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “spoke to him”">ag</a></sup>and because he was too afraid of his ⸤father’s family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “father’s house”">ah</a></sup>and the men of the city to do it during the day, he did it during night.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gideon Destroys the Altar of Baal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When the men of the city got up early in the morning, look, the altar of Baal and the Asherah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="An Asherah is a cultic pole set up next to an altar symbolizing the goddess Asherah">ai</a></sup>that was beside it were cut down, and the second bull was offered on the altar that had been built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> ⸤And they said to one another⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “And each man to his neighbor said”">aj</a></sup>“Who did this thing?” So they searched and inquired, and they said, “Gideon son of Jehoash did this thing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 26:9; Philippians 3:6; Jeremiah 50:38"> And the men of the city said to Jehoash, “Bring out your son so that he may die, for he has pulled down the altar of Baal and cut down the Asherah that was beside it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:11; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 5:11"> But Jehoash said to all who stood against him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “who arrayed against him”">ak</a></sup>“Will you contend for Baal? Will you rescue him? Whoever contends for him will be put to death by the morning. If he is a god, let him contend for himself because ⸤his altar has been pulled down⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “because he has pulled down his altar”; the subject in Hebrew has not been specified">al</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:11; Judges 7:1; 2 Samuel 11:21"> Thus, on that day he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="That is, Gideon">am</a></sup>was called Jerub-Baal, ⸤which means⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “saying”">an</a></sup>“Let Baal contend against him,” because he had pulled down his altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 17:16; Judges 6:3; Romans 8:35–39"> Then all the Midianites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “Midianite”">ao</a></sup>Amalekites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Hebrew “Amalekite”">ap</a></sup>and the people of the east gathered together and crossed the Jordan; and they camped in the valley of Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 3:27; 2 Chronicles 24:20; Judges 3:10"> So the Spirit of Yahweh ⸤took possession of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “clothed”">aq</a></sup>Gideon, and he blew on the trumpet, and the Abiezrites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Hebrew “Abiezerite”">ar</a></sup>were called to follow him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:6–12; Judges 4:6"> He sent messengers throughout all Manasseh, and they were also called to follow him; and he sent messengers throughout Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali, and they went up to meet them.</span>
</div>

## Gideon Tests Yahweh With the Fleece

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Judges 6:14; Matthew 16:1; Judges 6:17–20"> Then Gideon said to God, “In order to see that you will deliver Israel by my hand, just as you have said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hosea 14:5; Matthew 10:5–6; Matthew 15:24"> I will place a fleece of wool on the threshing floor. If there is dew on the fleece only, and all of the ground is dry, I will know that you will deliver Israel by my hand, just as you have said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7"> And it was so. He arose early the next day and squeezed the fleece, and he wrung out dew from the fleece, a full drinking bowl of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 18:32; Isaiah 50:2; Psalm 107:33–35"> And Gideon said to God, “⸤Do not let your anger burn⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “Do not let your nose become hot”">as</a></sup>against me; let me speak once more. Please let me test once more with the fleece; let the fleece be dry, and let there be dew on the ground.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And God did so that night; only the fleece was dry, and dew was on all the ground.</span>
</div>
