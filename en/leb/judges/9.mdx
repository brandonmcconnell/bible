<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 11:1–2; 2 Chronicles 21:4; Judges 6:24"> And he went to his father’s house at Ophrah, and he killed his brothers, the sons of Jerub-Baal, seventy men, on one stone. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:9; 1 Kings 12:20; 2 Kings 12:20"> All the lords of Shechem and Beth-Millo gathered, and they went and made Abimelech as king, near the oak<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “terebinth”">h</a></sup>of the pillar that is at Shechem.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 8:22–23; Daniel 4:10–18; 2 Kings 14:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">“The trees went certainly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to anoint a king over themselves.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And they said to the olive tree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Rule over us.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 29:7; Acts 4:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the olive tree replied,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Should I stop producing my oil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">which by me gods and men are honored,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">to go sway over the trees?’</span></span>

## The Downfall of Shechem and Abimelech

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Abimelech ruled over Israel three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 19:14; Isaiah 19:2"> And God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the lords of Shechem, and the lords of Shechem dealt treacherously with Abimelech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 2:32; Psalm 7:16; Esther 9:25"> so that the violence done to the seventy brothers of Abimelech would be avenged and their blood be placed on Abimelech their brother, who killed them, and on the lords of Shechem, ⸤who helped⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “who strengthened his hands”">p</a></sup>to kill his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 8:12–13; Proverbs 1:11–12; Joshua 8:4"> And the lords of Shechem set for him ambushes on the top of the mountains, and they robbed all who passed by them along the road; and it was reported to Abimelech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:8; Genesis 19:7"> And Gaal son of Ebel and his relatives came, and they crossed over into Shechem, and the lords of Shechem ⸤gave him confidence⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “put confidence into him”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 9:4; Judges 9:46; Psalm 109:17"> They went out into the field and harvested their vineyards and trod them, and they ⸤held a festival⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “made a rejoicing”">r</a></sup>And they went into the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “house”">s</a></sup>of their god, and they ate and drank and cursed Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 34:2; Genesis 34:6; 1 Samuel 25:10"> Then Gaal son of Ebed said, “Who is Abimelech, and who are we of Shechem that we should serve him? Is he not the son of Jerub-Baal, and is not Zebul his chief officer?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “overseer”">t</a></sup>Serve the men of Hamor the father of Shechem. Why should we serve him?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="That is, Abimelech">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:4; 2 Samuel 2:14–17; Isaiah 36:8–9"> If only this people ⸤were in my command⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “were in my hand”">v</a></sup>Then I would remove Abimelech, and I would have said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “he said”; the translation “I would have said” follows the LXX">w</a></sup>‘Increase your army and come out!’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> When Zebul the commander of the city heard the words of Gaal son of Ebed, ⸤he became angry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “his nose became hot”">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and he sent messengers to Abimelech in Tormah, saying, “Look, Gaal son of Ebed and his relatives are coming to Shechem, and they are stirring up the city against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Psalm 36:4; Proverbs 1:11–16"> So then, get up by night, you and the army that is with you, and lie in ambush in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:7; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Leviticus 25:26"> And in the morning at sunrise, get up and rush the city; and look, when he and the troops who are with him come out to you, ⸤you must act according to whatever opportunity offers itself⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “you must do to him just as it will find your hand”">y</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> So Abimelech and all the army that was with him got up by night, and they lay in ambush against Shechem in four divisions.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “companies”">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Gaal son of Ebed went out and stood at the entrance of the city gate, and Abimelech and the army that was with him got up from the ambush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 8:24; Ezekiel 7:7"> When Gaal saw the army, he said to Zebul, “Look, people are coming down from the top of the mountains!” And Zebul said to him, “⸤The shadows of the mountains look like people to you⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “the shadows of the mountains you are seeing as men”">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:14"> ⸤And Gaal spoke again⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “And he did again again Gaal to speak”">ab</a></sup>and said, “Look, people are coming down from Tabbur-erez, and one division<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “company”">ac</a></sup>is coming from the direction of Elon-meonenim.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “the Sorcerer’s Oak”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Judges 9:28–29; 2 Kings 14:8–14; 2 Samuel 2:26–27"> And Zebul said to him, “Where then ⸤is your boast⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “is your mouth”">ae</a></sup>you who said, ‘Who is Abimelech that we should serve him?’ Is this not the army that you rejected? Please, go out now and fight against them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Hebrew “against him”">af</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> So Gaal went out before the lords of Shechem and fought against Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Kings 20:18–21; 1 Kings 20:30"> And Abimelech chased him, and he fled before him; many fell slain up to the entrance of the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Judges 9:28; Judges 9:30"> So Abimelech resided at Arumah, and Zebul drove out Gaal and his relatives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “brothers”">ag</a></sup>from living in Shechem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> On the next day the people went out to the field; and it was reported to Abimelech,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> so he took the army and divided them into three divisions,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “companies”">ah</a></sup>and he laid an ambush in the field. And he saw the people were coming out from the city, and he arose against them and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; Judges 9:15; Judges 9:20"> Then Abimelech and the divisions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “companies”">ai</a></sup>that were with him dashed out and stood at the entrance of the city gate, and the two divisions dashed out against all who were in the field, and they killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Deuteronomy 29:23; James 2:13"> Abimelech fought against the city all that day, and he captured the city and killed the people that were in it; then he broke down the city and sowed it with salt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Judges 8:33; Judges 9:4; 1 Kings 8:26"> When all the lords of the tower of Shechem heard, they went to the vault<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “cellar”">aj</a></sup>of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “house”">ak</a></sup>of El-Berith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> It was told to Abimelech that all the lords of the tower of Shechem had gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalm 68:14; Proverbs 1:11–12; Judges 7:17–18"> So Abimelech went up Mount Zalmon, he and all his army that were with him, and Abimelech took the ax<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “axes”">al</a></sup>in his hand and cut down a bundle of brushwood, and he lifted it and put it on his shoulder. And he said to the army that was with him, “What you have seen me do, quickly do also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; James 3:16; Judges 9:20"> So the whole army cut down each one branch for himself and followed Abimelech, and they put them against the vault<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “cellar”">am</a></sup>and set the vault ablaze with fire on those inside, so that all the men of the tower of Shechem died, about a thousand men and women.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Then Abimelech went to Thebez, and he encamped against Thebez and captured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> But there was a strong tower in the middle of the city, and all the men, women, and lords of the city fled there and shut themselves in; and they went up to the roof of the tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Judges 9:48–49; 2 Kings 14:10; 2 Kings 15:16"> Abimelech came up to the tower and fought against it, and he came near the entrance of the tower to burn it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:21; Job 31:3; Jeremiah 49:20"> But a certain woman<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “But one woman”">an</a></sup>threw an upper millstone on Abimelech’s head and cracked open his skull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:4–5"> He called quickly to the young man carrying his weapons, and he said to him, “Draw your sword and kill me, so that they will not say of me, ‘A woman killed him.’ ” So the young man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “his young man”">ao</a></sup>stabbed him, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:16; 2 Samuel 20:21–22; 1 Kings 22:35–36"> When the men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead, each one went to his home.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “place”">ap</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Psalm 94:23; Judges 9:24; Proverbs 5:22"> So God repaid<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “returned”">aq</a></sup>the wickedness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “evil”">ar</a></sup>that Abimelech committed against his father by killing his seventy brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Judges 9:20; Joshua 6:26; 1 Kings 16:34"> And God also repaid all the wickedness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Or “evil”">as</a></sup>of the men of Shechem on their heads, and the curse of Jotham son of Jerub-Baal fell on them.</span>
