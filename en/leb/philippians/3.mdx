## Warning Against Evil Workers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 5:11; Philippians 4:4; James 1:2"> Finally, my brothers, rejoice in the Lord. To write the same things to you is not troublesome to me, but is a safeguard for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13; Titus 1:16; Revelation 22:15"> Beware of the dogs, beware of the evil workers, beware of the mutilation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This term is a wordplay on the Greek word “circumcision”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Deuteronomy 30:6; Romans 7:6"> For we are the circumcision, the ones who worship<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “who serve”">b</a></sup>by the Spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “in the Spirit”">c</a></sup>of God and boast in Christ Jesus and do not put confidence in the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:18–22"> although I could have confidence even in the flesh. Righteousness Through Christ<br />If anyone else thinks to put confidence in the flesh, I can do so more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; Romans 11:1; Acts 23:6"> ⸤circumcised on the eighth day⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “with respect to circumcision it was on the eighth day”">d</a></sup>, from the nation of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew born from Hebrews, according to the law a Pharisee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 1:13–14; Acts 8:3; Acts 26:9–10"> according to zeal persecuting the church, according to the righteousness in the law being blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 14:33; Matthew 16:26; Matthew 13:44–46"> But whatever things were gain to me, these things I have considered loss because of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; Romans 8:18; Philippians 3:7"> ⸤More than that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “but on the contrary”">e</a></sup>, I even consider all things to be loss because of the surpassing greatness of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord, for the sake of whom I have suffered the loss of all things, and consider them dung, in order that I may gain Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Galatians 2:16"> and may be found in him, not having my righteousness which is from the law, but which is through faith in Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “through Christ’s faithfulness”">f</a></sup>the righteousness from God on the basis of faith,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “on the basis of his faithfulness”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 8:17; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> so that I may know him and the power of his resurrection and the fellowship of his sufferings, being conformed to his death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Revelation 20:5; Acts 23:6"> if somehow I may attain to the resurrection from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:11–12; 2 Peter 1:5–8; Psalm 138:8"> Not that I have already received this, or have already been made perfect, but I press on if indeed I may lay hold of that for which also I was laid hold of by Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Christ Jesus”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:62; Hebrews 12:1–2; Philippians 3:12"> Brothers, I do not consider myself to have laid hold of it. But I do one thing, forgetting the things behind and straining toward the things ahead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; 1 Peter 5:10; Revelation 3:21"> I press on toward the goal for the prize of the upward call of God in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Imitate Paul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 7:17; James 1:4–5; Proverbs 2:3–6"> Therefore as many as are perfect, ⸤let us hold this opinion⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “let us think this”">i</a></sup>, and if you think anything differently, God will reveal this also to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:6; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 2:4–5"> Only to what we have attained, to the same hold on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Philippians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16; 1 Peter 5:3"> Become fellow imitators of me, brothers, and observe those who walk in this way, just as you have us as an example.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17; Isaiah 8:11; Galatians 6:12"> For many live, of whom I spoke about to you many times, but now speak about even weeping, as the enemies of the cross of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 16:18; Romans 8:5–7; 2 Peter 2:3"> whose end is destruction, whose God is the stomach, and whose glory is in their shame, the ones who think on earthly things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19; Colossians 3:1–3; 2 Corinthians 4:18–1"> For our commonwealth exists in heaven, from which also we eagerly await a savior, the Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 3:2; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 15:42–56"> who will transform our humble body to be conformed to his glorious body, in accordance with the power that enables him even to subject all things to himself.</span>
</div>
