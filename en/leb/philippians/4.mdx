<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> I know how both ⸤to make do with little⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to be humbled”">d</a></sup>and I know how ⸤to have an abundance⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to abound”">e</a></sup>.
