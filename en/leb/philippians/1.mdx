## Thanksgiving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:2–3; Philemon 1:4–5"> I give thanks to my God upon my every remembrance of you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “upon your every remembrance of me ”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:2; Romans 1:9; 2 John 1:4"> always in my every prayer for all of you, making the prayer with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:14–15; Philippians 1:7; Hebrews 3:14"> because of your participation in the gospel from the first day until now,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 4:12"> convinced of this same thing, that the one who began a good work in you will finish it until the day of Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3; Philippians 1:5; Colossians 4:18"> just as it is right for me to think this about all of you, because I have you in my heart, since both in my imprisonment and in the defense and confirmation of the gospel all of you are sharers of grace with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:4"> For God is my witness, that I long for all of you with the affection of Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Prayer for Spiritual Maturity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:12; Colossians 1:9; 2 Peter 1:5–6"> And this I pray: that your love may abound still more and more in knowledge and all discernment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> so that you may approve what is superior, in order that you may be sincere and blameless in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “for,” or “until”">b</a></sup>the day of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; John 15:8; John 15:4–5"> having been filled with the fruit of righteousness which comes through Jesus Christ to the glory and praise of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Progress of the Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Romans 8:28; Acts 11:19–21"> Now I want you to know, brothers, that ⸤my circumstances⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the things with me”">c</a></sup>have happened instead for the progress of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:9; Ephesians 3:1; Philippians 1:7"> so that my imprisonment in Christ has become known in the whole praetorium<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “praetorian guard,” or “imperial guard”">d</a></sup>and to all the rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 6:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 2:2; Luke 1:74"> and most of the brothers, trusting in the Lord because of my imprisonment, dare even more to speak the word without fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:17–18; Philippians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> Some even because of envy and strife preach Christ, but some also because of good will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 4:1–2; Psalm 69:26"> The latter do so from love, knowing that I am appointed for the defense of the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:11–12; Philippians 2:3; Philippians 1:7"> The former proclaim Christ from selfish ambition, not sincerely, thinking to raise up affliction in my imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 9:50; Mark 9:38–40; 1 Corinthians 15:11"> ⸤What is the result?⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “for what”">e</a></sup>Only that in every way, whether in pretense or in truth, Christ is proclaimed, and in this I rejoice. But also I will rejoice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Acts 16:7; 2 Corinthians 1:11"> for I know that this will turn out to me for deliverance through your prayer and the support of the Spirit of Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Isaiah 54:4"> according to my eager expectation and hope, that I will be put to shame in nothing, but with all boldness, even now as always Christ will be exalted in my body, whether through life or through death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; 2 Corinthians 5:8; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> For to me to live is Christ and to die is gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Galatians 2:20; Philippians 1:24"> But if it is to live in the flesh, this is fruitful work for me, and which I will prefer I do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:8; Psalm 17:15; Revelation 14:13"> But I am hard pressed ⸤between the two options⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “by the two”">f</a></sup>, having the desire to depart and to be with Christ, ⸤for this is very much better⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “for this is more better by much”">g</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 16:7; Philippians 1:22; Philippians 1:25–26"> But to stay on in the flesh is more necessary for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 2:24; 2 Corinthians 1:24; Ephesians 4:11–13"> And because I am convinced of this, I know that I will remain and continue with all of you for your progress and joy in the faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:14; 2 Corinthians 5:12; Philippians 3:3"> so that what you can be proud of may increase in Christ Jesus because of me through my return again to you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Stand Firm for the Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; 1 Corinthians 16:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> Only lead your lives in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ, so that whether I come and see you or am absent I hear ⸤your circumstances⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the things concerning you”">h</a></sup>, that you are standing firm in one spirit, with one soul contending side by side for the faith of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Matthew 10:28"> and not letting yourselves be intimidated in anything by your opponents, which is a sign of destruction to them, but of your salvation, and this from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13; Acts 5:41"> because to you has been graciously granted on behalf of Christ not only to believe in him, but also to suffer on behalf of him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:2; Acts 16:19–40; 2 Timothy 4:7"> having the same struggle which you saw in me and now hear about in me.</span>
</div>
</div>
