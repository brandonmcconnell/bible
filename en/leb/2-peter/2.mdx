## The Rise and Fall of the False Teachers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; Jude 1:4; Deuteronomy 13:1–3"> But there were also false prophets among the people, as there will be false teachers among you also, who will bring in ⸤destructive heresies⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “heresies of destruction”">a</a></sup>, even denying the Master who bought them, thus bringing on themselves swift destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Jude 1:10; 1 Peter 2:12"> And many will follow their licentious ways, because of whom the way of truth will be reviled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; Deuteronomy 32:35; Romans 16:18"> And in greediness they will exploit you with false words, whose condemnation from long ago is not idle, and their destruction is not asleep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jude 1:6; Matthew 25:41; Romans 8:32"> For if God did not spare the angels who sinned, but held them captive in Tartarus with chains of darkness and handed them over to be kept for judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 3:6; Jude 1:14–15"> and did not spare the ancient world, but preserved Noah, a proclaimer of righteousness, ⸤and seven others⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “eighth”">b</a></sup>when he brought a flood on the world of the ungodly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; Luke 17:28–30; Deuteronomy 29:23"> and condemned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to destruction, reducing them to ashes, having appointed them as an example for those who are going to be ungodly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 19:16; Genesis 19:29; Genesis 19:7–8"> and rescued righteous Lot, worn down by the way of life of lawless persons in licentiousness</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 119:158; Ezekiel 9:4; Psalm 119:136"> (for that righteous man, as he lived among them day after day, was tormenting his righteous soul by the lawless deeds he was seeing and hearing),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 34:15–19; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 3:10"> then the Lord knows how to rescue the godly from trials and to reserve the unrighteous to be punished at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “until”">c</a></sup>the day of judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jude 1:16; Jude 1:10; Jude 1:18"> and especially those who go after the flesh in defiling lust<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in lust of defilement,” translated here as an attributive genitive">d</a></sup>and who despise authority.<br />Bold and arrogant, they do not tremble in awe as they blaspheme majestic beings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jude 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Psalm 104:4"> whereas angels, who are greater in strength and power, do not bring against them a demeaning judgment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “a demeaning judgment from the Lord”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jude 1:10; Jeremiah 12:3; Galatians 6:8"> But these persons, like irrational animals born only with natural instincts for capture and killing, blaspheming ⸤about things⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “with reference to which”">f</a></sup>they do not understand, in their destruction will also be destroyed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 13:13; Romans 2:8–9; Hebrews 2:2–3"> being harmed as the wages of unrighteousness. Considering reveling in the daytime a pleasure, they are stains and blemishes, carousing in their deceitful pleasures when they feast together with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 2:3; 2 Peter 2:18; Ephesians 2:3"> having eyes full of desire for an adulteress and unceasing from sin, enticing unstable persons, and having hearts trained for greediness. Accursed children!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Jude 1:11; Deuteronomy 23:4–5"> By leaving the straight path, they have gone astray, because they followed the way of Balaam the son of Bosor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Although some English versions use “Beor” here, this is due to harmonization with the Old Testament; the vast majority of Greek manuscripts read “Bosor” here">g</a></sup>who loved the wages of unrighteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 22:21–33; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Luke 16:11"> but received a rebuke for his own lawlessness: a speechless donkey, speaking with a human voice, restrained ⸤the prophet’s madness⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the of the prophet madness”">h</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:12–13; Ephesians 4:14; Jeremiah 14:3"> These people are waterless springs and mists driven by a hurricane, for whom the gloom of darkness has been reserved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20; Jude 1:15–16; Romans 13:13"> ⸤For by speaking high-sounding but empty words⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for speaking pompous words of emptiness”">i</a></sup>, they entice with desires of the flesh and with licentiousness those who are scarcely escaping from those who live in error,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:34; Romans 6:16–22; Galatians 5:13"> promising them freedom although they themselves are slaves of depravity. For to whatever someone succumbs, by this he is also<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “also”">j</a></sup>enslaved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–27; Hebrews 6:4–8; Philippians 3:19"> For if, after they have escaped from the defilements of the world through the knowledge of the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “of our Lord”">k</a></sup>and Savior Jesus Christ, and they are again entangled in these things and succumb to them, the last state has become worse for them than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Hebrews 6:4–6; James 4:17"> For it would have been better for them not to have known the way of righteousness than having known it, to turn back from the holy commandment that had been delivered to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 26:11"> The statement of the true proverb has happened to them, “A dog returns to its own vomit,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A paraphrased quotation from Prov 26:11">l</a></sup>and “A sow, after washing herself, returns to wallowing in the mud.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The source of this quotation is uncertain">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
