## Job’s Blameless Behavior

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:35; Job 30:30; Isaiah 1:6"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">y</a></sup>Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “the accuser,” or “the adversary”">z</a></sup>went out from ⸤Yahweh’s presence⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “the faces of Yahweh”">aa</a></sup>and he inflicted Job with loathsome skin sores from the sole of his foot up to the crown of his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 42:6; Ezekiel 27:30; Jonah 3:6"> ⸤So⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">ab</a></sup>he took for himself a potsherd with which to scrape himself, and he sat in the midst of the ashes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 2:5; Job 2:3; Malachi 3:14"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">ac</a></sup>his wife said to him, “Are you still persisting in your blamelessness? ⸤Curse⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “Bless”—negative meaning by context">ad</a></sup>God and die.”</span>
</div>

Indeed, should we receive the good from God, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “and”">af</a></sup>not receive the evil?” In all this, Job did not sin with his lips.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Job 42:11; Jeremiah 49:7"> Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">ag</a></sup>Job’s three friends heard of this calamity that had come upon him. So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">ah</a></sup>each set out from his own place: Eliphaz the Temanite and Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite. And they met<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “they agreed”">ai</a></sup>together to come to console him and to comfort him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Ezekiel 27:30; Nehemiah 9:1"> Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">aj</a></sup>they lifted up their eyes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “they gazed”">ak</a></sup>from afar, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">al</a></sup>they did not recognize him, so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">am</a></sup>they raised their voice, and they wept, and each man tore his outer garment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “robe”">an</a></sup>and threw dust on their heads ⸤toward the sky⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “in the air”; literally “to the heavens”">ao</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 50:10; Nehemiah 1:4; Ezekiel 3:15"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">ap</a></sup>they sat with him on the ground for seven days and seven nights, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">aq</a></sup>no one spoke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “there was no speaking”">ar</a></sup>a word to him because they saw that ⸤his⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “the”">as</a></sup>suffering was very great.</span>
</div>
