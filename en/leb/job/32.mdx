<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 22:21; Job 40:8; Job 27:2"> So Elihu the son of Barakel the Buzite, from the clan of Ram, ⸤became angry⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the nose of Elihu became hot”">a</a></sup>⸤He became angry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “His nose became hot”">b</a></sup>at Job because he justified himself rather than God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 8:6; Job 24:25; Job 22:5–30"> and ⸤he became angry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “his nose became hot”">c</a></sup>at his three friends because they had not found an answer, and they had declared Job guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Proverbs 18:13"> Thus Elihu had waited for Job ⸤to speak⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in words”">d</a></sup>because ⸤they were older than he⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “they were older than he as far as days”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 32:2; Exodus 32:19"> When Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of the three men, ⸤he became angry⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “his nose became hot”">f</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Job 12:12; Hebrews 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I thought,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “said”">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Let days</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Compare the idiom of “as far as days” in vv. 4 and 6">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">speak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let many years teach wisdom.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 38:36; Job 33:4; 2 Timothy 3:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Truly, it is a spirit in a human being,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the breath of Shaddai teaches them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Job 12:20; Ecclesiastes 4:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is not ⸤the aged⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “many in days ”">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who are wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">o</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is not the elders who understand justice.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I say, ‘Listen to me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let me also declare my knowledge myself.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 29:21; Job 5:27; Proverbs 28:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Look, I waited for your</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Plural">p</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">words;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I listened for your</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Plural">q</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">insight,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until you searched out ⸤what to say⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “words,” or “sentences”">r</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:3; 1 Timothy 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I directed my attention to you;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Plural">s</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and, look, there is no one for Job who refuted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">answering his words among you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">So do not say, ‘We have found wisdom;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let God refute him, not a man.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">t</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he did not direct his words</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “sentences,” or “what he said”">u</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will not answer him with your</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Plural">v</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">words.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:24–25; Matthew 22:46; Job 29:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">“They are dismayed; they no longer answer;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">⸤they have nothing to say⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “words fail from them,” or “sentences fail from them,” or “what to say fails from them”">w</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 1:19; Job 13:5; Amos 5:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I have waited because they do not speak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they stand there and no longer answer.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 35:3–4; Job 33:12; Job 32:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I myself will answer my share also;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I myself will declare my knowledge also,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 39:3; Acts 4:20; 2 Corinthians 5:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">for I am full of words;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “sentences,” or “what to say”">x</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">⸤the spirit within me⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “the spirit of my stomach”">y</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">urges me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Look, my internal organs are like unopened wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like new wineskins it</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Antecedent is “wine”">z</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is ready to burst open.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 8:6–7; Job 21:3; Job 13:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let me speak ⸤that I may find relief⸥;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “and let it find relief for me”">aa</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let me open my lips, and let me answer.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Job 13:8; Matthew 22:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤Please let me not show partiality to anyone⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “Not please I will lift up face of a man”">ab</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let me not show flattery to human beings,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 12:2–3; 1 Thessalonians 2:5; Proverbs 29:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">for I do not know how ⸤to flatter⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “I will flatter”">ac</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">else my Maker would ⸤soon⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “like little”">ad</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">put an end to me.</span></span>
