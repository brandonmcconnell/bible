<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:9; Romans 11:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he will carry out ⸤what he appoints for me⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “my portion,” or “my allotment”">q</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and many things like these are with him.</span><br /></span>
