## Job’s Character and Greatness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:11; Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20"> There was a man in the land of Uz whose<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">a</a></sup>name was Job. That<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “And that”">b</a></sup>man was blameless and upright and God-fearing and turning away from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 127:3–5; Job 42:13; Psalm 128:3"> And seven sons and three daughters were born to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 29:25; Job 42:12; Judges 6:3"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">c</a></sup>his livestock came to be seven thousand sheep and goats and three thousand camels and five hundred pairs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “pair”">d</a></sup>of oxen and five hundred female donkeys, and he had very many slaves, and that man was greater than all the people of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Psalm 133:1"> And his sons used to go and hold a feast ⸤at each other’s house⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “at the house of a man,” or “at the house of each”">e</a></sup>on his day, and they would send, and they would invite their three sisters to eat and to drink with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 42:8; Genesis 8:20; 1 Samuel 16:5"> ⸤Then when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “And it would happen that,” or “And it would happen when”">f</a></sup>the days of the feast had run their course, ⸤Job would send⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “and Job would send”">g</a></sup>and he would sanctify them. Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">h</a></sup>he would arise early in the morning and offer burnt offerings according to the number of all of them, because Job thought,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “said”">i</a></sup>“Perhaps my children have sinned and ⸤cursed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “blessed”— negative meaning by context">j</a></sup>God in their heart.” This is what Job used to do ⸤all the time⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “always”; literally “all the days”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:1; Job 38:7; Zechariah 3:1"> And it happened ⸤one day⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the day”">l</a></sup>⸤that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “and”">m</a></sup>the sons of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “the heavenly beings,” or “the angels”">n</a></sup>came to present themselves before Yahweh, and Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “the accuser,” or “the adversary”">o</a></sup>also came into their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Revelation 12:12–17; Matthew 12:43"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">p</a></sup>Yahweh said to Satan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “the accuser,” or “the adversary”">q</a></sup>“From where have you come?”<br />Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">r</a></sup>Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “the accuser,” or “the adversary”">s</a></sup>answered Yahweh and said, “From roaming on the earth and from walking about in it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:3; Job 1:1; Numbers 12:7–8"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">t</a></sup>Yahweh said to Satan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “the accuser,” or “the adversary”">u</a></sup>“Have you ⸤considered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “set your heart”">v</a></sup>my servant Job? Indeed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “For,” or “Because”">w</a></sup>there is no one like him on the earth—a blameless man and upright and God-fearing and turning away from evil.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 4:8; Job 2:10"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">x</a></sup>Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “the accuser,” or “the adversary”">y</a></sup>answered Yahweh and said, “Does Job fear God for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 34:7; Zechariah 2:5; 1 Peter 1:5"> Have you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Emphatic personal pronoun could be translated “you yourself”">z</a></sup>not put a fence around him and his household and around ⸤all that belongs to him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “all that is his”">aa</a></sup>⸤on every side⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “from around”">ab</a></sup>You have blessed the work of his hands, and his livestock<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “possessions”">ac</a></sup>has increased in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:5; Job 19:21; Revelation 16:11"> But,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">ad</a></sup>on the other hand, stretch out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “please”">ae</a></sup>your hand and touch ⸤all that belongs to him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “all that is his”">af</a></sup>and see ⸤whether⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “if not”">ag</a></sup>he will ⸤curse⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “bless”— negative meaning by context">ah</a></sup>you to your face.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:31–32; John 3:35–36; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">ai</a></sup>Yahweh said to Satan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “the accuser,” or “the adversary”">aj</a></sup>“Look, ⸤all that belongs to him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “all that is his”">ak</a></sup>is in your ⸤power⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “hand”">al</a></sup>Only do not stretch out your hand ⸤against⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “to,” or “unto”">am</a></sup>him.” So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">an</a></sup>Satan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “the accuser,” or “the adversary”">ao</a></sup>went out from Yahweh’s ⸤presence⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “faces of”">ap</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Adversary’s Attack on Job’s Possessions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Proverbs 27:1; Job 1:4"> And then there was one day when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">aq</a></sup>his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in their firstborn brother’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:13; Jeremiah 51:31; 1 Samuel 4:17"> And a messenger came to Job and said, “The oxen were plowing, and the female donkeys were feeding ⸤beside them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “on their hands”">ar</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Job 6:19; Isaiah 45:14"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">as</a></sup>the Sabeans ⸤attacked⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “fell upon them ”">at</a></sup>and they took them, and they slew the servants ⸤by the edge of the sword⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “with the mouth of the sword”">au</a></sup>But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">av</a></sup>I escaped, even I alone, to tell you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:12; Genesis 19:24; 1 Kings 18:38"> While this one was still speaking, ⸤another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “this”">aw</a></sup>came and said, “The fire of God fell from the heavens, and it blazed up against the sheep and goats and against the servants, and it consumed them. But I escaped, even I alone, to tell you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 11:28; Habakkuk 1:6; Genesis 11:31"> While this one was still speaking, ⸤another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Literally “this”">ax</a></sup>came and said, “The Chaldeans formed three divisions, and they made a raid on the camels, and they carried them away, and they struck your servants ⸤by the edge of the sword⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Literally “with the mouth of the sword”">ay</a></sup>but I escaped, even I alone, to tell you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 1:4; Job 1:13; Job 27:14"> At the time<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Hebrew “Until”">az</a></sup>this one was speaking, ⸤another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Literally “this”">ba</a></sup>came and said, “Your sons and your daughters were eating and drinking wine in their firstborn brother’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 42:36; Luke 13:1–5; Judges 16:30"> And behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Or “Look”">bb</a></sup>a great wind came from across the desert, and it struck the four corners of the house ⸤so that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Literally “and”">bc</a></sup>it fell upon the young people, and they died. But I escaped, even I alone, to tell you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; 2 Samuel 12:16–20; Genesis 37:34"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">bd</a></sup>Job arose and tore his outer garment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-be" id="fnref-be" data-fn="Or “robe”">be</a></sup>and shaved his head; ⸤then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">bf</a></sup>he fell upon the ground and he worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:15; 1 Timothy 6:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">bg</a></sup>he said, “Naked I came out from my mother’s womb, and naked I will return there. Yahweh gives, and Yahweh takes. Let Yahweh’s name be blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 1:12; Job 2:10; 1 Peter 1:7"> In all this, Job did not sin and did not ⸤charge God with wrongdoing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Literally “give unseemliness to God”">bh</a></sup></span>
</div>
