<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:9; Ezekiel 14:9; 1 Kings 22:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Strength and sound wisdom are with him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the deceived and the deceiver are his.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “for him”">t</a></sup><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:21; Psalm 116:16; 2 Chronicles 33:11–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He loosens the fetters of kings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he binds a loincloth on their loins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Isaiah 37:36–38; Revelation 17:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">He leads priests away stripped,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he overthrows ⸤the members of ancient families⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “the constant”">u</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 32:9; Job 17:4; Job 12:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">He deprives the trustworthy of speech,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he takes away the discretion of elders.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 107:40; Isaiah 23:9; 2 Kings 9:34–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">He pours contempt on noblemen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he loosens the girdle of the mighty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“He uncovers mysteries out of darkness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he brings deep shadow to the light.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 9:3; Isaiah 26:15; Isaiah 60:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">He makes the nations great, then</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">v</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he destroys them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he expands the nations, then</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">w</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he guides them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 107:40; Job 12:20; Hosea 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">He strips away the insight of the heads of the earth’s people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he makes them wander in ⸤a pathless wasteland⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “in a wasteland not a way”">x</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 5:14; Psalm 107:27; Isaiah 19:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">They grope in the dark ⸤without⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “and not”">y</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">light,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he makes them stagger like a</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">z</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">drunkard.</span></span>
