<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:3; Job 1:15–19; Psalm 6:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Surely now he has worn me out;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Singular">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have devastated all my company.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 20:25; Job 6:4; Lamentations 2:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">His archers surround me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he slashes open my kidneys, and he does not have compassion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he pours out my gall on the ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 15:8; Lamentations 3:3–5; Joel 2:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He breached me ⸤breach upon breach⸥;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “breach upon the faces of breach”">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he rushes at me like a warrior.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; Psalm 7:5; Psalm 75:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I have sewed sackcloth on my skin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I have inserted ⸤my pride⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “my horn”">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the dust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:34; Psalm 102:3–5; Lamentations 1:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">My face is red because of weeping,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and deep shadows are on my eyelids,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Psalm 66:18–19; Job 27:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤although⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “upon,” or “because”">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">violence is not on my hands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and my prayer is pure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Ezekiel 24:7; Genesis 4:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“O earth, you should not cover my blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let there be no place</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “do not let it become a place”">o</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for my cry for help.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 11:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">So now look, my witness is in the heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he who vouches for me is in the heights.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Psalm 142:2; Job 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">My friends scorn me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my eye pours out tears to God,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; Romans 9:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">and it argues</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “but may someone argue”">p</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for a mortal with God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and as ⸤a human⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a son of man”">q</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for his friend.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 14:10; Job 14:14; Job 14:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, after ⸤a few years⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “years of number”">r</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">then</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">s</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will go the way from which I will not return.</span></span>
