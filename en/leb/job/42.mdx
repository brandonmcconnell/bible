## Job’s Repentance and Restoration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">a</a></sup>Job answered Yahweh and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27; Genesis 18:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I know that you can do all things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and any scheme from you will not be thwarted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 131:1; Psalm 139:6; Job 38:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Who is this darkening counsel without knowledge?’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I uttered, but</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I did not understand;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">⸤things too wonderful for me⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “wonderful things from me”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but I did not know.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 40:7; Job 38:3; Genesis 18:30–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Hear and I will speak;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will question you, then</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">inform me.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; Romans 10:17; John 12:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">By the ear’s hearing I heard of you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">now my eye has seen you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; James 4:7–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I despise myself</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and repent in dust and ashes.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 11:5–6; Job 32:2–3; Job 32:5"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">f</a></sup>after Yahweh spoke these words to Job, Yahweh said to Eliphaz the Temanite, “⸤My wrath has been kindled⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “My nose became hot”">g</a></sup>against you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Singular">h</a></sup>and against the two of your friends, for you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Plural">i</a></sup>have not spoken to me what is right as my servant Job has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:1; James 5:16; Psalm 103:10"> ⸤So then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “And now,” or “Therefore”">j</a></sup>take for yourselves seven bulls and seven rams, and go to my servant Job and offer a burnt offering for yourselves. And my servant Job will pray for you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Plural">k</a></sup>for ⸤I will certainly accept his prayer⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “if his face I will lift up,” or “indeed his face I will lift up” (“if” used as an oath particle)">l</a></sup>so that it will not be done with you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Plural">m</a></sup>according to your folly, for you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Plural">n</a></sup>have not spoken to me what is right as my servant Job has.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Acts 9:6; Acts 10:33"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">o</a></sup>Eliphaz the Temanite and Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite went and did just as Yahweh had told them, and ⸤Yahweh accepted Job’s prayer⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “Yahweh lifted up the face of Job”">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:3; Job 8:6–7; Proverbs 22:4"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">q</a></sup>Yahweh returned Job’s fortune when he prayed to him on behalf of his friends. Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">r</a></sup>Yahweh increased ⸤all that Job had twice as much as before⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “all which for Job to twice”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 19:13–14; Proverbs 16:7"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">t</a></sup>all his brothers and all his sisters and all those who had known him ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “to faces”">u</a></sup>came to him, and they ate bread<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “food”">v</a></sup>with him in his house and showed sympathy to him and comforted him for all the disaster<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “evil”">w</a></sup>that Yahweh had brought upon him. Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">x</a></sup>each one gave to him one piece of money, and each one gave to him one ornamental ring of gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 8:7; Ecclesiastes 7:8; Proverbs 10:22"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">y</a></sup>Yahweh blessed Job’s latter days more than his beginning. ⸤Thus he had⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “And it came to be for him,” or “And it was for him”">z</a></sup>fourteen thousand sheep and goats and six thousand camels and a thousand pair of oxen and a thousand female donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 1:2; Psalm 107:41; Psalm 127:3"> ⸤And he had⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “And it came to be for him,” or “And it was for him”">aa</a></sup>seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he called the name of the first Jemimah and the name of the second Keziah and the name of the third Qeren-Happuk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Psalm 144:12; Acts 7:20"> And beautiful women were not found in all the land like Job’s daughters, and their father gave to them an inheritance in the midst of their brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 17:6; Psalm 128:6; Genesis 50:23"> And Job lived after this one hundred and forty years, and he saw his sons and his ⸤grandsons⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “the sons of his sons”">ab</a></sup>for four generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Genesis 15:15; Job 5:26"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">ac</a></sup>Job died old and full of days.</span>
</div>
