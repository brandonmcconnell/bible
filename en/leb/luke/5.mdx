## Jesus Calls His First Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; Mark 3:9"> Now it happened that while the crowd was pressing around him and hearing the word of God, he was standing beside the lake of Gennesaret,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Another name for the Sea of Galilee">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:19; Matthew 4:21"> and he saw two boats there beside the lake, but the fishermen had gotten out of them and were washing their nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; Matthew 13:1–2; John 1:41–42"> And he got into one of the boats, which was Simon’s, and asked him to put out from the land a little. And he sat down and began to teach the crowds from the boat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 21:6; Matthew 17:27"> And when he stopped speaking, he said to Simon, “Put out into the deep water and let down your nets for a catch.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Luke 6:46–48"> And Simon answered and said, “Master, although we worked hard through the whole night, we caught nothing. But at your word I will let down the nets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; John 21:6–11; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> And when they did this, they caught a very large number of fish, and their nets began to tear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Philippians 4:3; Acts 11:25"> And they signaled to their partners in the other boat to come and help them, and they came and filled both the boats so that they began to sink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:5; Job 42:5–6; Revelation 1:17"> And when he saw it, Simon Peter fell down at Jesus’ knees, saying, “Depart from me, Lord, because I am ⸤a sinful man⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “a man, a sinner”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Psalm 8:8; Mark 9:6"> For amazement had seized him and all those who were with him at the catch of fish that they had caught,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Matthew 13:47; Ezekiel 47:9–10"> and so also were James and John, the sons of Zebedee, who were business partners with Simon. And Jesus said to Simon, “Do not be afraid! From now on you will be catching people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 4:20; Luke 5:28; Matthew 19:27"> And after they brought their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">c</a></sup>boats to the land, they left everything and followed him.</span>
</div>

## A Leper Cleansed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 50:15; Luke 17:16; Matthew 8:2–4"> And it happened that while he was in one of the towns, ⸤there was⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “behold”">d</a></sup>a man ⸤covered with leprosy⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “full of leprosy”">e</a></sup>And when he saw Jesus, he fell down on his face and begged him, saying, “Lord, if you are willing, you are able to make me clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; John 4:50–53; Hosea 14:4"> And extending his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">f</a></sup>hand he touched him, saying, “I am willing; be clean.” And immediately the leprosy went away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 17:14; Mark 1:44"> And he ordered him, “Tell no one, but go and show yourself to the priest and bring the offering for your cleansing just as Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:30–31; Luke 12:1; Mark 1:45–2"> But the report about him spread even more, and large crowds were gathering to hear him and to be healed of their illnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–36; Matthew 14:23"> But he himself was withdrawing in the wilderness and praying.</span>
</div>

## A Paralytic Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Matthew 11:5"> And it happened that on one of the days as he was teaching, Pharisees and teachers of the law were sitting there who ⸤had come⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “were having come”">g</a></sup>from every village of Galilee and Judea and from Jerusalem, and the power of the Lord was there in order for him to heal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 2:3–12; Matthew 9:2–8; John 5:5–6"> And behold, men came carrying on a stretcher a man who was paralyzed, and they were seeking to bring him in and place him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts Some manuscripts include the pronoun “him” after “place”">h</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Since Greek routinely omits direct objects when they are clear from context, the pronoun is not necessary here in the Greek text, but it must be supplied in the English translation">i</a></sup>before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 2:4; Matthew 10:27; Matthew 24:17"> And when they did not find a way to bring him in because of the crowd, they went up on the roof and let him down through the roof tiles with the stretcher into the midst of them, in front of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 14:9; Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5"> And when he saw their faith, he said, “Friend, your sins are forgiven you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Mark 2:6–7; Luke 7:49"> And the scribes and the Pharisees began to reason, saying, “Who is this man who speaks blasphemies? Who is able to forgive sins except God alone?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:38; Isaiah 66:18; Psalm 139:2"> But Jesus, perceiving their thoughts, answered and said to them, “Why are you reasoning in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 2:9; Matthew 9:5"> Which is easier to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven you,’ or to say, ‘Get up and walk?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Isaiah 53:11; Luke 8:54"> But in order that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins,” he said to the one who was paralyzed, “I say to you, ‘Get up and pick up your stretcher and go to your home.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 9:24; Luke 5:13; Psalm 107:20–22"> And immediately he stood up before them, picked up what he had been lying on, and went away to his home, glorifying God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Matthew 12:23; Luke 7:16"> And amazement seized them all, and they began to glorify God. And they were filled with fear, saying, “We have seen wonderful things today!”</span>
</div>

## Levi Called to Follow Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 2:13–22; Matthew 9:9–17; John 1:43"> And after these things, he went out and saw a tax collector ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “by name”">j</a></sup>Levi sitting at the tax booth, and he said to him, “Follow me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 5:11; Matthew 19:22–27; 1 Kings 19:19–21"> And leaving everything behind, he got up and began to follow him.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 2:15; Luke 15:1; 1 Corinthians 10:27"> And Levi gave a great banquet for him in his house, and there was a large crowd of tax collectors and others who were reclining for the meal with them.</span>

## On Fasting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 9:14–17; Mark 2:18–22; John 3:25"> And they said to him, “The disciples of John fast often and make prayers—likewise also the disciples of the Pharisees—but yours are eating and drinking!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 3:29; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> So he said<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “So Jesus said”">l</a></sup>to them, “You are not able to make the ⸤bridegroom’s attendants⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons of the bridal chamber”">m</a></sup>fast as long as the bridegroom is with them, are you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Acts 14:23; Acts 3:21"> But days will come, and when the bridegroom is taken away from them, then they will fast in those days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 9:16–17; Mark 2:21–22; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> And he also told a parable to them: “No one tears a patch from a new garment and puts it on an old garment. Otherwise, he will have torn the new also, and the old will not match the patch that is from the new.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 9:13; Psalm 119:83; Joshua 9:4"> And no one pours new wine into old wineskins. Otherwise, the new wine will burst the wineskins, and it will be spilled and the wineskins will be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 8:8–13; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ezekiel 36:26"> But new wine must be put into new wineskins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Mark 7:7–13; Jeremiah 6:16"> And no one after drinking old wine wants new, because he says, ‘The old is just fine!’ ”</span>
