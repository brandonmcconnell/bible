## The Parable of the Lost Sheep

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15; Matthew 9:10–13; Luke 5:29–32"> Now all the tax collectors and the sinners were drawing near to hear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:11; Luke 19:7; Luke 7:39"> And both the Pharisees and the scribes were complaining, saying, “This man welcomes sinners and eats with them!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So he told them this parable, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Ezekiel 34:11–12; Matthew 18:12–14"> “What man of you, having a hundred sheep and losing one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine in the grassland and go after the one that was lost until he finds it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Luke 19:9; Isaiah 46:3–4"> And when he has found it, he places it on his shoulders, rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 15:10; Luke 15:24; 1 Peter 2:25"> And when he returns to his home, he calls together his friends and neighbors, saying to them, ‘Rejoice with me, because I have found my sheep that was lost!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 5:32; Luke 15:10; Luke 15:32"> I tell you that in the same way, there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous people who have no need of repentance.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Lost Coin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:12; John 10:16"> Or what woman who has ten drachmas, if she loses one drachma, does not light a lamp and sweep the house and search carefully until she finds it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:6–7"> And when she has found it, she calls together her friends and neighbors, saying, ‘Rejoice with me, because I have found the drachma that I had lost!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 15:7; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 33:11"> In the same way, I tell you, there is joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner who repents.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Lost Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–31"> And he said, “A certain man had two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:16–17; Mark 12:44; Luke 15:30"> And the younger of them said to his father, ‘Father, give me the share of the property that is coming to me.’ So he divided his assets between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Jeremiah 2:5; Proverbs 21:17"> And after not many days, the younger son gathered everything and went on a journey to a distant country, and there he squandered his wealth by living wastefully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; Amos 8:9–12; Hosea 2:9–14"> And after he had spent everything, there was a severe famine throughout that country, and he began to be in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> And he went and hired himself out to one of the citizens of that country, and he sent him into his fields to tend pigs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:19–21; Lamentations 4:5; Hosea 12:1"> And he was longing to fill his stomach with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “to stuff himself with”">a</a></sup>the carob pods that the pigs were eating, and no one was giving anything to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28; Ephesians 2:4–5; Luke 15:18–19"> “But when he came to himself, he said, ‘How many of my father’s hired workers have an abundance ⸤of food⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “of bread”">b</a></sup>and I am dying here from hunger!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 John 1:8–10; Luke 15:21"> I will set out and go to my father and will say to him, ‘Father, I have sinned against heaven and ⸤in your sight⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “in the sight of you”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 42:6; Luke 5:8; Psalm 84:10"> I am no longer worthy to be called your son! Make me like one of your hired workers.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 103:10–13; Psalm 86:5; Psalm 86:15"> And he set out and came to his own father. But while he was still a long way away, his father saw him and had compassion, and ran ⸤and embraced him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “fell on his neck”">d</a></sup>and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 51:4; Luke 15:18–19; Jeremiah 3:13"> And his son said to him, ‘Father, I have sinned against heaven and ⸤in your sight⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in the sight of you”">e</a></sup>I am no longer worthy to be called your son!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Revelation 6:11"> But his father said to his slaves, ‘Quickly bring out the best robe and put it on him, and put a ring on his finger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “hand,” but this is a metonymy of whole (“hand”) for part (“finger”)">f</a></sup>and sandals on his feet!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Matthew 22:2–14; Psalm 63:5"> And bring the fattened calf—kill it and let us eat and celebrate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5; Ezekiel 34:16; Luke 19:10"> because this son of mine was dead, and is alive again! He was lost and is found!’ And they began to celebrate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 150:4; Jeremiah 31:4; Luke 15:11–12"> “Now his older son was in the field, and when he came and approached the house, he heard music and dancing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And he summoned one of the slaves and asked what these things meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 9:17; Philemon 1:16; Acts 22:13"> And he said to him, ‘Your brother has come, and your father has killed the fattened calf because he has gotten him back healthy.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jonah 4:9; 1 Samuel 18:8; Jonah 4:1–4"> But he became angry and did not want to go in. So his father came out and began to implore him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:11–12; Zechariah 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> But he answered and said to his father, ‘Behold, so many years I have served you, and have never disobeyed your command! And you never gave me a young goat so that I could celebrate with my friends!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Luke 15:12–13; Luke 15:32"> But when this son of yours returned—who has consumed your assets with prostitutes—you killed the fattened calf for him!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Romans 9:4; Romans 11:1"> But he said to him, ‘Child, you are always with me, and ⸤everything I have belongs to you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “all my things are yours”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–10; Luke 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> But it was necessary to celebrate and to rejoice, because this brother of yours was dead, and is alive, and was lost, and is found!’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
