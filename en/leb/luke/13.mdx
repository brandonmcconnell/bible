<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:16; Isaiah 5:1–4; Galatians 5:22"> And he told this parable: “A certain man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard, and he came looking for fruit on it and did not find any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:19; Luke 3:9; John 15:6"> So he said to the gardener, ‘Behold, ⸤for three years⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “three years from which”">a</a></sup>I have come looking for fruit on this fig tree and did not find any. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 15:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:15; Hebrews 6:8"> And if indeed it produces fruit in the coming year, so much the better, but if not, you can cut it down.’ ”</span>

## A Woman with a Disabling Spirit Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Luke 4:44; Luke 4:15–16"> Now he was teaching in one of the synagogues on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Luke 8:27; Luke 13:16"> And behold, a woman was there who had a spirit ⸤that had disabled her⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “of weakness”">c</a></sup>for eighteen years, and she was bent over and not able to straighten herself up completely.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “at all”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 107:20; Matthew 8:16; Luke 6:8–10"> And when he saw her, Jesus summoned her and said to her, “Woman, you are freed from your disability!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 107:20–22; Mark 16:18; Mark 5:23"> And he placed his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">e</a></sup>hands on her, and immediately she straightened up and glorified God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:9; Matthew 12:2; John 9:14–16"> But the ruler of the synagogue, indignant because Jesus had healed on the Sabbath, answered and said to the crowd, “There are six days on which it is necessary to work. Therefore come and be healed on them, and not on the day of the Sabbath!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 14:5; John 7:21–24; Acts 13:9–10"> But the Lord answered and said to him, “Hypocrites! Does not each one of you untie his ox or his donkey from the feeding trough on the Sabbath and lead it away to water it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 2:27; 2 Timothy 2:26; Luke 19:9"> And this woman, who is a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan bound ⸤eighteen⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “ten and eight”">f</a></sup>long years—is it not necessary that she be released from this bond on the day of the Sabbath?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Psalm 132:18; Isaiah 45:24"> And when he said these things, all those who opposed him were humiliated, and the whole crowd was rejoicing at all the splendid things that were being done by him.</span>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Matthew 9:35; Mark 10:32–34"> And he was going throughout towns and villages, teaching and making his journey toward Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:14; Matthew 22:14; John 21:21–22"> And someone said to him, “Lord, ⸤are there only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “if”">h</a></sup>a few who are saved?” And he said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 7:13–14; 2 Peter 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27"> “Make every effort to enter through the narrow door, because many, I tell you, will seek to enter and will not be able to,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 25:10–12; Luke 13:27; Matthew 7:21–23"> when once the master of the house has gotten up and shut the door, and you begin to stand outside and knock on the door, saying, ‘Lord, open the door for us!’ And he will answer and say to you, ‘I do not know where you are from!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 58:2; Titus 1:16; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Then you will begin to say, ‘We ate and drank in your presence, and you taught in our streets!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 6:8; Matthew 25:41; Matthew 7:22–23"> And he will reply, saying to you, ‘I do not know where you are from! <span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:11–12; Matthew 22:13; Matthew 25:30"> In that place there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth, when you see Abraham and Isaac and Jacob and all the prophets in the kingdom of God, but yourselves thrown outside!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 43:6; Isaiah 66:18–20; Isaiah 49:6"> And they will come from east and west, and from north and south, and will recline at the table in the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 20:16"> And behold, some are last who will be first, and some are first who will be last.”</span>
