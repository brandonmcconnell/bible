## Rejected at Nazareth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 13:15; Acts 7:42"> And the scroll of the prophet Isaiah was given to him, and unrolling the scroll he found the place where it was written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:5; Psalm 146:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">“The Spirit of the Lord is upon me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of which he has anointed me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to proclaim good news to the poor.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has sent me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to proclaim release to the captives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and recovery of sight to the blind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to send out in freedom those who are oppressed,</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 4:25–26; Acts 3:18; Acts 2:16–18"> And he began to say to them, “Today this scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 13:54–56; Psalm 45:2; Acts 6:10"> And they were all speaking well of him, and were astonished at the gracious words that were coming out of his mouth. And they were saying, “Is this man not the son of Joseph?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:46–53; Matthew 11:23–24; Mark 2:1–12"> And he said to them, “Doubtless you will tell me this parable: ‘Physician, heal yourself!’ Whatever we have heard that took place in Capernaum, do here in your hometown also!”</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–24; Matthew 11:21; Obadiah 1:20"> And Elijah was sent to none of them, but only to Zarephath in the region of Sidon, to a woman who was a widow.</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 5:33; 2 Chronicles 24:20–21; Jeremiah 38:6"> And all those in the synagogue were filled with anger when they heard these things.</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:39; John 8:59; John 18:6–7"> But he passed through their midst and went on his way.</span>

## Jesus Teaches and Heals Many in Capernaum

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 1:21–28; Matthew 4:13; Acts 13:50–2"> And he came down to Capernaum, a town of Galilee, and was teaching them on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 4:36; Matthew 7:28–29; John 6:63"> And they were astounded at his teaching, because ⸤he spoke⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “his word was”">j</a></sup>with authority.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:23"> And in the synagogue there was a man who had the spirit of an unclean demon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “an unclean demonic spirit”">k</a></sup>and he cried out with a loud voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 1:24; James 2:19; Matthew 8:29"> “Ha! ⸤Leave us alone⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “what to us and to you”">l</a></sup>Jesus the Nazarene! Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are—the Holy One of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:39; Matthew 8:26"> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be silent and come out of him!” And after throwing him down in their midst, the demon came out of him without hurting him at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 4:32; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 12:22–23"> And amazement came upon them all, and they began to talk with one another, saying, “What word<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “command”">m</a></sup>is this? For he commands the unclean spirits with authority and power, and they come out!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 1:45; Luke 4:14; Isaiah 52:13"> And news about him went out into every place of the surrounding region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:29–31; John 11:22"> And after he set out from the synagogue, he went into Simon’s house. And Simon’s mother-in-law was afflicted with a high fever, and they asked him on behalf of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Luke 4:35; Luke 8:24"> And he stood over her and rebuked the fever, and it left her. And immediately she got up and began to serve them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:32–34; Matthew 8:16–17; Mark 5:23"> Now as the sun was setting, all who had those who were sick with various diseases brought them to him, and placing his hands on every one of them, he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:34; James 2:19; Luke 4:34–35"> And demons also were coming out of many, crying out and saying, “You are the Son of God!” And he rebuked them and did not permit them to speak, because they knew that he was the Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “Messiah”">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–38; John 4:34"> And when it was day, he departed and went to an isolated place. And the crowds were seeking him, and came to him and were trying to prevent him from departing from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 1:38–39; 2 Timothy 4:2; John 9:4"> But he said to them, “It is necessary for me to proclaim the good news of the kingdom of God to the other towns also, because I was sent for this purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:39; Luke 4:15"> And he was preaching in the synagogues of Judea.</span>
