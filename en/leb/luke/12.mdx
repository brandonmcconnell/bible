## The Parable of the Rich Landowner Who Was a Fool

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Timothy 6:5; Ezekiel 33:31"> Now someone from the crowd said to him, “Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 2:3; Romans 2:1; Romans 9:20"> But he said to him, “Man, who made me a judge or an arbitrator over you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5; 1 Timothy 6:6–10; Psalm 119:36–37"> And he said to them, “Watch out and guard yourselves from all greediness, because not even when someone has an abundance ⸤does⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “is”">c</a></sup>his life consist of his possessions.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:12–14; Hosea 2:8; Genesis 41:47–49"> And he told a parable to them, saying, “The land of a certain rich man yielded an abundant harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:42; Isaiah 58:7; Romans 12:13"> And he reasoned to himself, saying, ‘What should I do? For I do not have anywhere I can gather in my crops.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:15; James 4:15; Luke 12:21"> And he said, ‘I will do this: I will tear down my barns and build larger ones, and I will gather in there all my grain and possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; James 4:13–15; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> And I will say to my soul, “Soul, you have many possessions stored up for many years. Relax, eat, drink, celebrate!” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 27:8; Jeremiah 17:11; 1 Timothy 6:7"> But God said to him, ‘Fool! This night your life<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The same Greek word can be translated “soul” or “life” depending on the context">d</a></sup>is demanded from you, and the things which you have prepared—whose will they be?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Luke 12:33; James 2:5"> So is the one who stores up treasure for himself, and who is not rich toward God!”</span>
</div>

## Anxiety

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 13:5"> And he said to his disciples, “For this reason I tell you, do not be anxious for your life, what you will eat, or for your body, what you will wear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 2:6; Proverbs 13:8; Job 2:4"> For life is more than food, and the body more than clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:31; Psalm 147:9; Luke 12:7"> Consider the ravens, that they neither sow nor reap; to them there is neither storeroom nor barn, and God feeds them. How much more are you worth than the birds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:27; Matthew 5:36; Luke 19:3"> And which of you by being anxious is able to add an hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “a cubit” (the literal meaning); most scholars understand this to refer figuratively to an “hour” of life here, though some take it as a literal measurement of height">e</a></sup>to his life span?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Psalm 39:6; Ecclesiastes 7:13"> If then you are not even able to do a very little thing, why are you anxious about the rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 6:28–30; Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 10:1–13"> Consider the lilies, how they grow: they do not toil or spin, but I say to you, not even Solomon in all his glory was dressed like one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Isaiah 40:6; Matthew 6:30"> But if God clothes the grass in the field in this way, although it is here today and tomorrow is thrown into the oven, how much more will he do so for you, you of little faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 6:31; Luke 12:22; Luke 22:35"> And you, do not consider what you will eat and what you will drink, and do not be anxious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 6:32; Matthew 6:8; Ephesians 4:17"> For all the nations of the world seek after these things, and your Father knows that you need these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Hebrews 13:5; Psalm 34:9"> But seek his kingdom and these things will be added to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:28; 1 Peter 1:3–5; Revelation 1:6"> “Do not be afraid, little flock, because your Father is well pleased to give you the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; Acts 4:34–35; Acts 2:45"> Sell your possessions and give charitable gifts. Make for yourselves money bags that do not wear out, an inexhaustible treasure in heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “in the heavens”">f</a></sup>where thief does not approach or moth destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Matthew 6:21; Philippians 3:20"> For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.</span>
</div>
</div>

## On the Alert for the Master’s Return

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jude 1:20–21; James 5:7–8; Revelation 3:20"> And you, be like people who are waiting for their master when he returns from the wedding feast,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or perhaps simply “feast”">h</a></sup>so that when he comes back and knocks, they can open the door for him immediately.</span>

<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Revelation 3:3; Revelation 16:15"> But understand this, that if the master of the house had known what hour the thief was coming, he would not have left his house to be broken into.</span>

## A Faithful Slave and an Unfaithful Slave

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 13:37; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8"> And Peter said, “Lord, are you telling this parable for us, or also for everyone?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 24:45–46; 1 Peter 5:1–4; Hebrews 13:17"> And the Lord said, “Who then is the faithful wise manager whom the master will put in charge over his servants to give them their food allowance at the right time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Blessed is that slave whom his master will find so doing when he comes back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 22:29–30; Matthew 24:47; Luke 19:17–19"> Truly I say to you that he will put him in charge of all his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 24:48–50; Revelation 18:7–8; Philippians 3:18–19"> But if that slave should say ⸤to himself⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “in his heart”">i</a></sup>‘My master is taking a long time to return,’ and he begins to beat the male slaves and the female slaves and to eat and drink and get drunk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 12:40; Matthew 24:51; Psalm 11:5"> the master of that slave will come on a day that he does not expect and at an hour that he does not know, and will cut him in two and assign his place with the unbelievers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 4:17; John 9:41; John 12:48"> And that slave who knew the will of his master and did not prepare or do according to his will ⸤will be given a severe beating⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “will be beaten much”">j</a></sup></span>

## Not Peace, But a Sword of Divisiveness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Luke 12:51–52; Matthew 3:10–12"> “I have come to bring fire on the earth, and how I wish that it had been kindled already!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 12:27–28; John 19:30; John 7:6–8"> But I have a baptism to be baptized with, and how I am distressed until it is accomplished!</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:34–36; Luke 12:49; Matthew 24:7–10"> Do you think that I have come to grant peace on the earth? No, I tell you, but rather division!</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Acts 14:1–4; John 15:18–21; Micah 7:5–6"> For from now on there will be five in one household, divided three against two and two against three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Matthew 10:21–22; Matthew 24:10"> They will be divided, father against son and son against father, mother against daughter and daughter against mother, mother-in-law against her daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law against mother-in-law.”</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 16:2–4; 1 Kings 18:44–45"> And he also said to the crowds, “When you see a cloud coming up in the west, you say at once, ‘A rainstorm is coming,’ and so it happens.</span>
