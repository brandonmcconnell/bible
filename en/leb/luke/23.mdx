## Jesus Brought Before Pilate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Matthew 27:11–14; Luke 22:66"> And the whole assembly of them rose up and brought him before Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 19:12; Luke 23:14"> And they began to accuse him, saying, “We have found this man misleading our nation and forbidding us to pay taxes to Caesar, and saying he himself is Christ, a king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 1:49; 1 Timothy 6:13; Luke 22:70"> And Pilate asked him, saying, “Are you the king of the Jews?” And he answered him and said, “You say so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:22"> So Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, “I find no basis for an accusation against this man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:14; John 1:43; John 2:11"> But they insisted, saying, “He incites the people, teaching throughout the whole of Judea and beginning from Galilee as far as here.”</span>

## Jesus Brought Before Herod

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> Now when Pilate heard this, he asked if the man was a Galilean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> And when he found out that he was from the jurisdiction of Herod, he sent him over to Herod, who was also in Jerusalem in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> And when Herod saw Jesus, he was very glad, for he had been wanting to see him for a long time, because he had heard about him and was hoping to see some miracle performed by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> So he questioned him ⸤at considerable length⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “with many words”">a</a></sup>but he answered nothing to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> And the chief priests and the scribes were standing there vehemently accusing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; Psalm 69:19–20"> And Herod with his soldiers also treated him with contempt, and after mocking him and dressing him in glistening clothing, he sent him back to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; Psalm 83:4–6"> And both Herod and Pilate became friends with one another on that same day, ⸤for they had previously been enemies of one another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “for they had previously existed being at enmity with each other”">b</a></sup></span>

## Pilate Releases Barabbas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> So Pilate called together the chief priests and the rulers and the people</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> and said to them, “You brought me this man as one who was misleading the people, and behold, when I examined him before you, I found nothing in this man as basis for the accusation which ⸤you are making⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “you are accusing”">c</a></sup>against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But neither did Herod, because he sent him back to us. And behold, nothing deserving death ⸤has been done⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “is having been done”">d</a></sup>by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> Therefore I will punish him and release him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Many of the most important Greek manuscripts lack v. 17 , “Now he was obligated to release for them at the feast one person.”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> But they all cried out in unison, saying, “Take this man away, and release for us Barabbas!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> (who had been thrown in prison because of a certain insurrection that had taken place in the city, and for murder).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> And Pilate, wanting to release Jesus, addressed them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts explicitly state “them”">f</a></sup>again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> but they kept crying out, saying, “Crucify! Crucify him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> So he said to them a third time, “Why? What wrong has this man done? I found no basis for an accusation ⸤deserving death⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “of death”">g</a></sup>in him. Therefore I will punish him and release him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Psalm 57:4; Psalm 22:12–13"> But they were urgent, demanding with loud cries that he be crucified. And their cries prevailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> And Pilate decided that their demand should be granted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> And he released the one who had been thrown into prison because of insurrection and murder, whom they were asking for, but Jesus he handed over to their will.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Is Crucified

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> And as they led him away, they seized Simon, a certain man of Cyrene, who was coming from the country, and placed the cross on him, to carry it behind Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> And a great crowd of the people were following him, and of women who were mourning and lamenting him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 5:8; Song of Solomon 1:5"> But turning to them, Jesus said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for me, but weep for yourselves and for your children!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> For behold, days are coming in which they will say, ‘Blessed are the barren, and the wombs that did not give birth, and the breasts that did not nurse!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> Then they will begin to say to the mountains, ‘Fall on us!’ and to the hills, ‘Cover us!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> For if they do these things ⸤when the wood is green⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “in the green wood”">h</a></sup>what will happen ⸤when it is dry⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “in the dry”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> And two other criminals were also led away to be executed with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> And when they came to the place that is called “The Skull,” there they crucified him, and the criminals, the one on his right and the other on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> 〚But Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing.”〛<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Many important manuscripts lack v. 34a , “But Jesus said, ‘Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing.’ ”">j</a></sup>And they cast lots to divide his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalm 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> And the people stood there watching, but the rulers also ridiculed him, saying, “He saved others; let him save himself, if this man is the Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “Messiah”">k</a></sup>of God, the Chosen One!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalm 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> And the soldiers also mocked him, coming up and offering him sour wine</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and saying, “If you are the king of the Jews, save yourself!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> And there was also an inscription over him, “This is the king of the Jews.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> And one of the criminals who were hanged there reviled him, saying, “Are you not the Christ? Save yourself—and us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> But the other answered and rebuked him, saying, “Do you not even fear God, because you are undergoing the same condemnation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> And we indeed justly, for we are receiving ⸤what we deserve⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “things worthy”">l</a></sup>for what we have done. But this man has done nothing wrong!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> And he said, “Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalm 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> And he said to him, “Truly I say to you, today you will be with me in paradise.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Dies on the Cross

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> And by this time it was about the sixth hour, and darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> because the light of the sun failed. And the curtain of the temple was torn apart down the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalm 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> And Jesus, calling out with a loud voice, said, “Father, into your hands I entrust my spirit!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 31:5">m</a></sup>And after he said this, he expired.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> Now when the centurion saw what had happened, he began to praise God, saying, “Certainly this man was righteous!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> And all the crowds that had come together for this spectacle, when they saw the things that had happened, returned home beating their breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalm 38:11; Luke 23:55"> And all his acquaintances, and the women who had followed him from Galilee who saw these things, stood at a distance.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Is Buried

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> And behold, a man ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “by name”">n</a></sup>Joseph, who was a member of the council,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “a member of the Sanhedrin”">o</a></sup>a good<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and a good”">p</a></sup>and righteous man</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> (this man was not consenting to their plan and deed), from Arimathea, ⸤a Judean town⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a town of the Jews”">q</a></sup>who was looking forward to the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> This man approached Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> And he took it down and wrapped it in a linen cloth and placed him in a tomb cut into the rock where no one had ever been placed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> And it was the day of preparation, and the Sabbath was drawing near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> And the women who had been accompanying him from Galilee followed and saw the tomb and how his body was placed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> And they returned and prepared fragrant spices and perfumes, and on the Sabbath they rested according to the commandment.</span>
