<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> And an angel of the Lord stood near them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and ⸤they were terribly frightened⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “they were afraid with great fear”">c</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

## Jesus in the Temple at Twelve Years Old

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> And his parents went every year to Jerusalem for the feast of the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And when he was twelve years old, they went up according to the custom of the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> And after the days were completed, while they were returning, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem. And his parents did not know it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalm 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalm 42:4"> but believing him to be in the group of travelers, they went a day’s journey. And they began searching for him among their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">p</a></sup>relatives and their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">q</a></sup>acquaintances,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and when they did not find him, they returned to Jerusalem to search for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> And it happened that after three days they found him in the temple courts, sitting in the midst of the teachers and listening to them and asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalm 119:99"> And all who heard him were amazed at his insight and his answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> And when they saw him, they were astounded and his mother said to him, “Child, why have you done this to us? Look, your father and I have been searching for you anxiously!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> And he said to them, “⸤Why⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “what is it that”">r</a></sup>were you searching for me? Did you not know that it was necessary for me to be in the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “things” (= business)">s</a></sup>of my Father?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> And they did not understand the statement that he spoke to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> And he went down with them and came to Nazareth, and was submitting to them. And his mother treasured all these things in her heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> And Jesus was advancing in wisdom and stature and in favor with God and with people.</span>
</div>
