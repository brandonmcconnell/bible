## A Man Suffering from Edema Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:2; Luke 20:20; Psalm 62:4"> And it happened that when he came to the house of a certain one of the leaders of the Pharisees on a Sabbath to eat ⸤a meal⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “bread”">a</a></sup>they were watching him closely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And behold, a certain man was in front of him, suffering from edema.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 12:10; Mark 3:4; Luke 13:14–16"> And Jesus answered and said to the legal experts and Pharisees, saying, “Is it permitted to heal on the Sabbath, or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Matthew 21:25–27"> But they remained silent. And he took hold of him and healed him, and sent him away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 13:15; Exodus 23:4–5; Matthew 12:11–12"> And he said to them, “Who among you, if your son or your ox falls into a well<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “cistern”">b</a></sup>on the day of the Sabbath, will not immediately pull him out?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 20:40; Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10"> And they were not able to make a reply to these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Guests at the Wedding Feast

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Luke 11:43; Matthew 23:6"> Now he told a parable to those who had been invited when he noticed how they were choosing for themselves the places of honor, saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7"> “When you are invited by someone to a wedding feast,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or perhaps simply “a feast”">c</a></sup>do not recline at the table in the place of honor, lest someone more distinguished than you has been invited by him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Proverbs 11:2; Proverbs 3:35"> and the one who invited you ⸤both⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “and him”">d</a></sup>will come and say to you, ‘Give the place to this person,’ and then with shame you will begin to take the last place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 25:6–7; Proverbs 15:33; 1 Samuel 15:17"> But when you are invited, go and recline at the table in the last place, so that when the one who invited you comes, he will say to you, ‘Friend, move up higher.’ Then it will be an honor to you in the presence of all those who are reclining at the table with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 18:14; Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 29:23"> For everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, and the one who humbles himself will be exalted.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Great Banquet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:46; James 2:1–6; Luke 6:32–36"> And he also said to the one who had invited him, “When you give a dinner or a banquet, do not invite your friends or your brothers or your relatives or wealthy neighbors, lest they also invite you in return, and repayment come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 14:21; Isaiah 58:10; Proverbs 14:31"> But whenever you give a banquet, invite the poor, the crippled, the lame, the blind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Acts 24:15; John 5:29"> and you will be blessed, because they are not able to repay you. For it will be paid back to you at the resurrection of the righteous.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; Luke 22:30; Luke 13:29"> Now when one of those reclining at the table with him heard these things, he said to him, “Blessed is ⸤everyone who⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “whoever”">e</a></sup>will eat bread in the kingdom of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 22:2–14; Revelation 22:17; Isaiah 55:1–7"> But he said to him, “A certain man was giving a large banquet and invited many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:27–29; Matthew 10:1–4; Matthew 22:3–4"> And he sent his slave at the hour of the banquet to say to those who have been invited, ‘Come, because now it is ready!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 17:26–31; Luke 18:24"> And they all ⸤alike⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “by one”">f</a></sup>began to excuse themselves. The first said to him, ‘I have purchased a field, and ⸤I must⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “I have necessity”">g</a></sup>go out to look at it. I ask you, consider me excused.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And another said, ‘I have purchased five yoke of oxen, and I am going to examine them. I ask you, consider me excused.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:5; 1 Corinthians 7:33; Luke 14:26–28"> And another said, ‘I have married a wife, and for this reason I am not able to come.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 14:13; Matthew 11:5; Luke 14:24"> And the slave came and reported these things to his master. Then the master of the house became angry and said to his slave, ‘Go out quickly into the streets and alleys of the city and bring in here the poor and crippled and blind and lame!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 14:2; Ephesians 3:8; 1 Timothy 2:5–6"> And the slave said, ‘Sir, what you ordered has been done, and there is still room.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:9–10; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:47–48"> And the master said to the slave, ‘Go out into the highways and hedges and press them to come in, so that my house will be filled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Acts 13:46; John 8:24"> For I say to you that none of those persons who were invited will taste my banquet!’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Cost of Discipleship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:24–27; Luke 12:1"> Now large crowds were going along with him, and he turned around and said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; John 12:25; Philippians 3:8"> “If anyone comes to me and does not hate his own father and mother and wife and children and brothers and sisters, and furthermore, even his own life, ⸤he cannot be⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “he is not able to be”">h</a></sup>my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 9:23–25; Mark 8:34–37"> Whoever does not carry his own cross and ⸤follow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “come after”">i</a></sup>me ⸤cannot be⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “is not able to be”">j</a></sup>my disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 24:27; Matthew 10:22; Joshua 24:19–24"> For which of you, wanting to build a tower, does not first sit down and calculate the cost to see if he has enough ⸤to complete it⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “for completion”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> ⸤Otherwise⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “so that lest”">l</a></sup>after he has laid the foundation and is not able to finish it, all who see it will begin to ridicule him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 6:11; 2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 3:11–14"> saying, ‘This man began to build and was not able to finish!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 25:8; 2 Kings 18:20–22"> Or what king, going out to engage another king in battle, does not sit down first and deliberate whether he is able with ten thousand to oppose the one coming against him with twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 12:20; James 4:6–10; Luke 12:58"> But if not, while the other is still far away, he sends an ambassador and asks ⸤for terms of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the things with reference to”">m</a></sup>peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; 1 John 2:15–16; Luke 5:28"> In the same way, therefore, every one of you who does not renounce all his own possessions ⸤cannot be⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “is not able to be”">n</a></sup>my disciple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6; Mark 9:49–50"> “Now salt is good, but if salt becomes tasteless, with what will it be made salty?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Luke 9:44; Revelation 2:11"> It is usable neither for the soil nor for the manure pile; they throw it out. The one who has ears to hear, let him hear!”</span>
</div>
</div>
