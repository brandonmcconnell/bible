## A Centurion’s Slave Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:5–13; Luke 7:1–10; Matthew 7:28–29"> After he had finished all his statements in the hearing of the people, he entered into Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2–3; Genesis 24:27; John 4:46–47"> Now a certain centurion’s slave, who was esteemed by him, ⸤was sick⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was having badly”">a</a></sup>and was about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 8:5; John 4:47; Luke 9:38"> So when he heard about Jesus, he sent Jewish elders to him, asking him that he would come and cure his slave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; Matthew 10:11; Luke 7:6–7"> And when they came to Jesus, they began imploring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive here (“began imploring”)">b</a></sup>him earnestly, saying, “He is worthy that you grant this for him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:3–9; 1 Kings 5:1"> because he loves our nation and he himself built the synagogue for us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 5:24; Genesis 32:10; James 4:6"> So Jesus went with them. Now by this time he was not far away from the house, and the centurion sent friends, saying to him, “Lord, do not trouble yourself, for I am not worthy that you should come in under my roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 107:20; Psalm 33:9; Luke 4:36"> For this reason neither did I consider myself worthy to come to you. But say the word and my slave must be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Acts 23:17; Acts 22:25–26"> For I also am a man placed under authority, who has soldiers under me, and I say to this one, ‘Go!’ and he goes, and to another one, ‘Come!’ and he comes, and to my slave, ‘Do this!’ and he does it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Romans 3:1–3; Matthew 8:10"> And when Jesus heard these things, he marveled at him, and turning around to the crowd that was following him, he said, “I tell you, not even in Israel have I found such great faith!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:50–53; Matthew 8:13; Matthew 15:28"> And when they returned to the house, those who had been sent found the slave healthy.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Widow’s Son Raised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 10:38"> And it happened that on the next day he went to a town called Nain, and his disciples and a large crowd went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 8:42; Luke 8:52; 1 Kings 17:9"> And as he approached the gate of the town, behold, a man who had died was being carried out, his mother’s only son, and she was a widow. And a large crowd from the town was with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 103:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalm 86:15"> And when the Lord saw her, he had compassion for her and said to her, “Do not weep!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:25; John 11:43–44; Acts 9:40–41"> And he came up and touched the bier, and those who were carrying it stopped. And he said, “Young man, I say to you, get up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; 2 Kings 4:32–37; 1 Kings 17:23–24"> And the dead man sat up and began to speak, and he gave him to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:39; Luke 1:68"> And fear seized them all, and they began to glorify God, saying, “A great prophet has appeared among us!” and “God has visited to help his people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 6:14; Matthew 9:26"> And this report about him went out in the whole of Judea and in all the surrounding region.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Question from John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:26; Matthew 11:2–19"> And his disciples reported to John about all these things. And summoning a certain two of his disciples, John</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; John 4:25; Genesis 22:18"> sent them to the Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A number of significant manuscripts read “Jesus”">c</a></sup>saying, “Are you the one who is to come, or should we look for another?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:19"> And when the men came to him, they said, “John the Baptist sent us to you, saying, ‘Are you the one who is to come, or should we look for another?’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:10; Psalm 90:7–9; Hebrews 12:6"> In that hour he healed many people of diseases and suffering and evil spirits, and he granted sight to many blind people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> And he answered and said to them, “Go and tell John what you have seen and heard: the blind receive sight, the lame walk, lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and the deaf hear”">d</a></sup>the dead are raised, the poor have good news announced to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Romans 9:32–33"> And whoever is not offended by me is blessed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; John 1:23; Luke 1:80"> And when the messengers of John had departed, he began to speak to the crowds concerning John: “What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; Esther 8:15; Esther 1:3"> But what did you go out to see? A man dressed in soft clothing? Behold, those who are in splendid clothing and luxury are in the royal palaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:76; John 5:35; Luke 20:6"> But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I tell you, and even more than a prophet!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2"> It is this man about whom it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Behold, I am sending my messenger before your face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who will prepare your way before you.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Mal 3:1; cf. Mark 1:2 ; Matt 11:10">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17; Matthew 11:11; Colossians 1:25–27"> I tell you, there is no one greater among those born of women than John, but the one who is least in the kingdom of God is greater than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 3:12; Luke 7:35; Acts 18:25"> (And all the people, when they heard this—even the tax collectors—affirmed the righteousness of God, because they had been baptized with the baptism of John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 13:34; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Matthew 22:35"> but the Pharisees and the legal experts rejected the purpose of God for themselves, because they had not been baptized by him.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Lamentations 2:13; Mark 4:30"> “To what then shall I compare the people of this generation, and what are they like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Jeremiah 5:3–5; Proverbs 17:16"> They are like children sitting in the marketplace and calling out to one another, who say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘We played the flute for you and you did not dance;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we sang a lament and you did not weep.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Mark 1:6; John 8:52"> For John the Baptist has come not eating bread or drinking wine, and you say, ‘He has a demon!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:36; John 12:2"> The Son of Man has come eating and drinking, and you say, ‘Behold, a man who is a glutton and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–36; Matthew 11:19; Hosea 14:9"> And wisdom is vindicated by all her children.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Sinful Woman Anoints Jesus’ Feet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:5–6; Luke 14:1"> Now one of the Pharisees asked him to eat with him, and he entered into the house of the Pharisee and reclined at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:6–13; Matthew 21:31"> And behold, a woman in the town who was a sinner, when she learned that he was dining in the Pharisee’s house, brought an alabaster flask of perfumed oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalm 126:5–6; Psalm 51:17; Genesis 18:4"> and standing behind him at his feet weeping, she began to wet his feet with her tears and was wiping them with the hair of her head and was kissing his feet and anointing them with the perfumed oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Luke 15:2; Mark 7:21"> Now when the Pharisee who invited him saw this, he spoke to himself, saying, “If this man were a prophet, he would have known who and what kind of woman this is who is touching him, that she is a sinner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 16:19; Luke 5:31; Ezekiel 33:31"> And Jesus answered and said to him, “Simon, I have something to say to you.” And he said, “Teacher, say it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 7:47; Romans 5:20; Matthew 18:28"> “There were two debtors who owed a certain creditor. One owed five hundred denarii and the other fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 9:18–19; Colossians 3:13; Romans 3:24"> When they were not able to repay him, he forgave the debts of both. Now which of them will love him more?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalm 116:16–18; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 7:47"> Simon answered and said, “I suppose that it is the one to whom he forgave more.” And he said to him, “You have judged correctly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Judges 19:21; Genesis 18:4"> And turning toward the woman, he said to Simon, “Do you see this woman? I entered into your house. You did not give me water for my feet, but she wet my feet with her tears and wiped them with her hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:5; 2 Samuel 19:39; Romans 16:16"> You did not give me a kiss, but from the time I entered, she has not stopped kissing my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalm 23:5; Ecclesiastes 9:8; Matthew 6:17"> You did not anoint my head with olive oil, but she anointed my feet with perfumed oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 John 4:19; 1 John 3:18; Isaiah 1:18"> ⸤For this reason⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “on account of which”">f</a></sup>I tell you, her sins—which were many—have been forgiven, for she loved much. But the one to whom little is forgiven loves little.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5; Luke 5:20"> And he said to her, “Your sins are forgiven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 5:20–21; Matthew 9:3; Mark 2:7"> And those who were reclining at the table with him began to say among themselves, “Who is this who even forgives sins?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Luke 8:48; Luke 18:42"> And he said to the woman, “Your faith has saved you. Go in peace.”</span>
</div>
