<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:25; Mark 2:23–28; Matthew 12:1–8"> Now it happened that on a Sabbath he went through the grain fields, and his disciples were picking and eating the heads of grain, rubbing them in their hands.</span>

## A Man with a Withered Hand Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 3:1–6; Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 4:31"> Now it happened that on another Sabbath he entered into the synagogue and was teaching, and a man was there, and his right hand was withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Psalm 38:12; Luke 13:14"> So the scribes and the Pharisees were watching closely<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “were watching him closely”">a</a></sup>to see if he would heal on the Sabbath, in order that they could find a reason to accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 42:4; Matthew 9:4; Luke 5:22"> But he knew their thoughts and said to the man who had the withered hand, “Get up and stand in the middle,” and he got up and stood there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 3:4; Matthew 12:12–13; Luke 14:3"> And Jesus said to them, “I ask you whether it is permitted on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save a life or to destroy it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:6–7; Mark 3:5; John 5:8"> And after looking around at them all, he said to him, “Stretch out your hand,” and he did, and his hand was restored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 2:1–2; Acts 5:33; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> But they were filled with fury, and began discussing with one another what they might do to Jesus.</span>
