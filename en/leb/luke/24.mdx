<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> and they were conversing with one another about all these things that had happened.</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> And he said to them, “What things?” So they said to him, “The things concerning Jesus the Nazarene, a man who was a prophet, powerful in deed and word before God and all the people,</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> But also some women from among us astonished us, who were at the tomb early in the morning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and when they did not find his body, they came back saying they had seen even a vision of angels, who said that he was alive!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> And some of those with us went out to the tomb and found it like this, just as the women had also said, but him they did not see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> And he said to them, “O foolish and slow in heart to believe in all that the prophets have spoken!</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> And beginning from Moses and from all the prophets, he interpreted to them the things concerning himself in all the scriptures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> And they drew near to the village where they were going, and he acted as though he was going farther.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> And they urged him strongly, saying, “Stay with us, because it is getting toward evening, and by this time the day is far spent.” And he went in to stay with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> And it happened that when he reclined at the table with them, he took the bread and gave thanks, and after breaking it, he gave it to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> And their eyes were opened, and they recognized him, and he became invisible to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> And they said to one another, “Were not our hearts burning within us while he was speaking with us on the road, while he was explaining the scriptures to us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> And they got up that same hour and returned to Jerusalem and found the eleven and those with them assembled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> saying, “The Lord has really been raised, and has appeared to Simon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> And they began describing ⸤what happened⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the things”">g</a></sup>on the road, and how he was recognized by them in the breaking of the bread.</span>
