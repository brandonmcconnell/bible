## The Parable of the Dishonest Manager

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Matthew 25:14–30; James 4:3"> And he also said to the disciples, “A certain man was rich, who had a manager. And charges were brought to him that this person was squandering his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:9–10; 1 Peter 4:5; Revelation 20:12"> And he summoned him and said to him, ‘What is this I hear about you? Give the account of your management, because you can no longer manage.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Isaiah 10:3; Proverbs 18:9"> And the manager said to himself, ‘What should I do, because my master is taking away the management from me? I am not strong enough to dig; I am ashamed to beg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; Jeremiah 4:22; James 3:15"> I know what I should do, so that when I am removed from the management they will welcome me into their homes!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:41–42; Matthew 18:24"> And he summoned each one of his own master’s debtors and said to the first, ‘How much do you owe my master?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:10; Luke 16:12; Luke 16:9"> And he said, ‘A hundred measures of olive oil.’ So he said to him, ‘Take your promissory note and sit down quickly and write fifty.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:11–12; Luke 20:9; Luke 20:12"> Then he said to another, ‘And how much do you owe?’ And he said, ‘A hundred measures of wheat.’ He said to him, ‘Take your promissory note and write eighty.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5; Ephesians 5:8; Proverbs 6:6–8"> And the master praised the dishonest manager, because he had acted shrewdly. For the sons of this age are shrewder than the sons of light with regard to their own generation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “kind”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 19:21; 1 Timothy 6:17–19; Daniel 4:27"> And I tell you, make friends for yourselves by means of unrighteous wealth, so that when it runs out they will welcome you into the eternal dwellings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:11–12; Matthew 25:21; Luke 19:17"> “The one who is faithful in very little is also faithful in much, and the one who is dishonest in very little is also dishonest in much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 12:33; James 2:5; Proverbs 8:18–19"> If then you have not been faithful with unrighteous wealth, who will entrust to you the true riches?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 19:13–26; Matthew 25:14–29; Job 1:21"> And if you have not been faithful with what belongs to another, who will give you your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Matthew 6:24; James 4:4"> No domestic slave is able to serve two masters, for either he will hate the one and love the other, or he will be devoted to one and will despise the other. You are not able to serve God and money.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hypocrisy, Law, and the Kingdom of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:35; Luke 20:47; Jeremiah 6:13"> Now the Pharisees, who were lovers of money, heard all these things, and they ridiculed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Proverbs 21:2; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> And he said to them, “You are the ones who justify themselves in the sight of men, but God knows your hearts! For what is considered exalted among men is an abomination in the sight of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 11:9–14; Matthew 21:32; Acts 3:24–25"> “The law and the prophets were until John; from that time on the kingdom of God has been proclaimed, and everyone is urgently pressed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The verb is translated here as a passive; some English versions translate the verb as active (“forces their way into it”)">b</a></sup>into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:18; Luke 21:33; 1 Peter 1:25"> But it is easier for heaven and earth to pass away than for one stroke of a letter of the law to become invalid.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Rich Man and Lazarus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49; Amos 6:4–6; Luke 18:24–25"> “Now a certain man was rich, and dressed in purple cloth and fine linen, feasting sumptuously every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:2; 1 Samuel 2:8; James 2:5"> And a certain poor man ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “by name”">c</a></sup>Lazarus, covered with sores, lay at his gate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 15:27; Mark 7:28; John 6:12"> and was longing to be filled with what fell from the table of the rich man. But even the dogs came and licked his sores.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Psalm 49:16–19; James 1:11"> Now it happened that the poor man died, and he was carried away by the angels to ⸤Abraham’s side⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the bosom of Abraham”">d</a></sup>And the rich man also died and was buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Luke 16:28; Matthew 18:9"> And in Hades he lifted up his eyes as he was in torment and saw Abraham from a distance, and Lazarus ⸤at his side⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in his bosom”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 4:10; Revelation 22:1; Revelation 7:16–17"> And he called out and said, ‘Father Abraham, have mercy on me, and send Lazarus so that he could dip the tip of his finger in water and cool my tongue, because I am suffering pain in this flame!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:24; 1 John 2:15; Psalm 17:14"> But Abraham said, ‘Child, remember that you received your good things during your life, and Lazarus likewise bad things. But now he is comforted here, but you are suffering pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 3:36; Psalm 49:14; Psalm 50:22"> And in addition to all these things, a great chasm has been established between us and you, so that those who want to cross over from here to you are not able to do so, nor can they cross over from there to us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> So he said, ‘Then I ask you, father, that you send him to my father’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:40; Psalm 49:12–13; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> for I have five brothers, so that he could warn them, in order that they also should not come to this place of torment!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:21; Luke 24:27; Luke 16:16"> But Abraham said, ‘They have Moses and the prophets; they must listen to them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 16:9–11; Luke 16:24; Luke 13:5"> And he said, ‘No, father Abraham, but if someone from the dead goes to them, they will repent!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Acts 19:8; John 11:43–53"> But he said to him, ‘If they do not listen to Moses and the prophets, neither will they be convinced if someone rises from the dead.’ ”</span>
</div>
