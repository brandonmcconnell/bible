## Jesus and Zacchaeus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:1–27; Luke 18:35; 2 Kings 2:18–22"> And he entered and traveled through Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 18:24–27; 2 Chronicles 17:5–6"> And ⸤there was⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “behold”">a</a></sup>a man ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “called by name”">b</a></sup>Zacchaeus, and he was a chief tax collector, and he was rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 9:7–9; John 12:21"> And he was seeking to see Jesus—who he was—and he was not able to as a result of the crowd, because he was short in stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; Luke 5:19; 1 Kings 10:27"> And he ran on ahead and climbed up into a sycamore tree so that he could see him, because he was going to go through that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; John 14:23; Hebrews 13:2"> And when he came to the place, Jesus looked up and said to him, “Zacchaeus, come down quickly, because it is necessary for me to stay at your house today!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:29; Acts 16:34; Acts 16:15"> And he came down quickly and welcomed him joyfully.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:6; Numbers 5:7; Luke 18:22–23"> And Zacchaeus stopped and said to the Lord, “Behold, half of my possessions, Lord, I am giving to the poor, and if I have extorted anything from anyone, I am paying it back four times as much!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; Luke 13:16; Romans 4:16"> And Jesus said to him, “Today salvation has come to this house, because he too is a son of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Matthew 1:21; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> For the Son of Man came to seek and to save those who are lost.”</span>
</div>

## The Parable of the Ten Minas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> Now while they were listening to these things, he went on and told a parable, because he was near Jerusalem and they thought that the kingdom of God was going to appear immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> Therefore he said, “A certain nobleman traveled to a distant country to receive for himself a kingdom and to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; John 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> And summoning ten of his own slaves, he gave them ten minas<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A Greek monetary unit equal to 100 drachmas">c</a></sup>and said to them, ‘Do business ⸤until I come back⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in which time I am coming back”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> But his citizens hated him, and sent a delegation after him, saying, ‘We do not want this man to be king over us!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; Psalm 2:4–6"> And it happened that when he returned after receiving the kingdom, he ordered these slaves to whom he had given the money to be summoned to him, so that he could know what they had gained by trading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> So the first arrived, saying, ‘Sir, your mina has made ten minas more!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> And he said to him, ‘Well done, good slave! Because you have been faithful in a very small thing, ⸤have authority⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “be having authority”">e</a></sup>over ten cities.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> And the second came, saying, ‘Sir, your mina has made five minas.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> So he said to this one also, ‘And you be over five cities.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; James 4:17"> And another came, saying, ‘Sir, behold your mina, which I had put away for safekeeping in a piece of cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 John 4:18"> For I was afraid of you, because you are a severe man—you withdraw what you did not deposit, and you reap what you did not sow!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> He said to him, ‘⸤By your own words⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from your own mouth”">f</a></sup>I will judge you, wicked slave! You knew that I am a severe man, withdrawing what I did not deposit and reaping what I did not sow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> And why did you not give my money to the bank, and I, when I returned, would have collected it with interest?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> And to the bystanders he said, ‘Take away from him the mina and give it to the one who has the ten minas!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> And they said to him, ‘Sir, he has ten minas.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 John 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> ‘I tell you that to everyone who has, more will be given. But from the one who does not have, even what he has will be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Psalm 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> But these enemies of mine who did not want me to be king over them—bring them here and slaughter them in my presence!’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Triumphal Entry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> And after he had said these things, he traveled on ahead, going up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> And it happened that when he drew near to Bethphage and Bethany, to the hill<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “mountain”">g</a></sup>called the Mount of Olives, he sent two of the disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> saying, ‘Go into the village in front of you, in which as you enter you will find a colt tied, on which ⸤no person has ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “no one of men ever”">h</a></sup>sat, and untie it and bring it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 24:1; Psalm 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> And if anyone asks you, ‘Why are you untying it?’ you will say this: ‘The Lord has need of it.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 22:13"> So those who were sent went and found it just as he had told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And as they were untying the colt, its owners said to them, ‘Why are you untying the colt?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 10:35; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> So they said, ‘The Lord has need of it.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; Matthew 21:7; John 12:14"> And they brought it to Jesus, and throwing their cloaks on the colt, they put Jesus on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Matthew 21:8"> And as he was going along, they were spreading out their cloaks on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; Matthew 21:1"> Now as he was drawing near by this time to the descent from the Mount of Olives, the whole crowd of the disciples began rejoicing to praise God with a loud voice for all the miracles that they had seen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 13:35; Psalm 118:22–26"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed is the king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the one who comes in the name of the Lord!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 118:26, with “the king” added as a clarification">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Peace in heaven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and glory in the highest!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; Matthew 23:13; Acts 4:16–18"> And some of the Pharisees from the crowd said to him, “Teacher, rebuke your disciples!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; Matthew 3:9"> And he answered and said, “I tell you that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “that”">j</a></sup>if these keep silent, the stones will cry out!”</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">
