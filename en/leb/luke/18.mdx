## The Parable of the Unjust Judge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 4:2; Romans 12:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:17"> And he told them a parable to show that they must always pray and not be discouraged,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalm 8:1–4; Luke 18:4"> saying, “There was a certain judge in a certain town who did not fear God and did not respect people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; Jeremiah 5:28; Deuteronomy 27:19"> And there was a widow in that town, and she kept coming to him, saying, ‘Grant me justice against my adversary!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12–13; Luke 16:3; Luke 12:17"> And he was not willing for a time, but after these things he said to himself, ‘Even if I do not fear God or respect people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Luke 18:39; Judges 16:16"> yet because this widow is causing trouble for me, I will grant her justice, so that she does not wear me down in the end by her coming back!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 7:13"> And the Lord said, “Listen to what the unrighteous judge is saying!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35–37; 2 Peter 3:9; Luke 11:13"> And will not God surely ⸤see to it that justice is done⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “carry out the giving of justice”">a</a></sup>to his chosen ones who cry out to him day and night, and will he delay toward them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23–26; 2 Peter 3:8–9; Matthew 24:9–13"> I tell you that ⸤he will see to it that justice is done⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “he will carry out the giving of justice”">b</a></sup>for them soon! Nevertheless, when the Son of Man comes, then will he find faith on earth?”</span>
</div>

## The Parable of the Pharisee and the Tax Collector

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> And he also told this parable to some who trusted in themselves that they were righteous, and looked down on ⸤everyone else⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the rest”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 21:31–32; 1 Kings 8:30; 2 Kings 20:5"> “Two men went up to the temple to pray, one a Pharisee and the other a tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; Revelation 3:17"> The Pharisee stood and prayed these things with reference to himself: ‘God, I give thanks to you that I am not like other people—swindlers, unrighteous people, adulterers, or even like this tax collector!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Matthew 23:23–24; Matthew 9:14"> I fast twice a week; I give a tenth of all that I get.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 John 1:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:15"> But the tax collector, standing far away, did not want even to raise his eyes to heaven, but was beating his breast, saying, ‘God, be merciful to me, a sinner!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> I tell you, this man went down to his house justified rather than that one! For everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, but the one who humbles himself will be exalted.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> But Jesus called them to himself, saying, “Allow the children to come to me, and do not forbid them, ⸤for to such belongs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “for of such is”">d</a></sup>the kingdom of God.</span>

## A Rich Young Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> And a certain ruler asked him, saying, “Good Teacher, by doing what will I inherit eternal life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 15:14–16; James 1:17; Hebrews 7:26"> And Jesus said to him, “Why do you call me good? No one is good except God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Romans 13:9"> You know the commandments: ‘Do not commit adultery, do not murder, do not steal, do not give false testimony, honor your father and mother.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 20:12–16 ; Deut 5:16–20">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 3:6; Romans 10:2–3; Mark 10:20–21"> And he said, “All these I have observed from my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “my”">f</a></sup>youth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 12:33; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Matthew 19:21"> And when he heard this, Jesus said to him, “⸤You still lack one thing⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “one still lacking to you”">g</a></sup>Sell all that you have, and distribute the proceeds to the poor—and you will have treasure in heaven—and come, follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> But when he heard these things he became very sad, because he was extremely wealthy.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; James 2:5–7; Mark 10:23–27"> And Jesus took notice of him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts add “becoming very sad”">h</a></sup>and said, “How ⸤difficult it is for⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “with difficulty”">i</a></sup>those who possess wealth to enter into the kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 23:24"> For it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter into the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 13:23"> So those who heard this said, “And who can be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37"> But he said, “What is impossible with men is possible with God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 10:28; Philippians 3:7; Matthew 19:27"> And Peter said, “Behold, we have left ⸤all that is ours⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “our own”">j</a></sup>and followed you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> And he said to them, “Truly I say to you that there is no one who has left house or wife or brothers or parents or children on account of the kingdom of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Job 42:10; Revelation 2:17"> who will not receive many times more in this time and in the age to come, eternal life.”</span>

## Jesus Predicts His Death and Resurrection a Third Time

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; Psalm 22:1–31"> And taking aside the twelve, he said to them, “Behold, we are going up to Jerusalem, and all the things that are written by the prophets with reference to the Son of Man will be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> For he will be handed over to the Gentiles and will be mocked and mistreated and spit on,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Luke 24:7; Matthew 27:63"> and after flogging him they will kill him, and on the third day he will rise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32; Luke 24:25"> And they understood none of these things, and this saying was concealed from them, and they did not comprehend the things that were said.</span>
</div>

## A Blind Man Healed at Jericho

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:29–34; Mark 10:46–52; Luke 16:20–21"> Now it happened that as he drew near to Jericho, a certain blind man was sitting on the side of the road begging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 21:10–11; Luke 15:26"> And when he heard a crowd going by, he inquired what this meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; John 19:19; John 12:35–36"> And they told him, “Jesus the Nazarene is passing by.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> And he called out, saying, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; Psalm 141:1; Luke 18:15"> And those who were in front rebuked him, that he should be silent, but he cried out ⸤even more loudly⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “by much more”">k</a></sup>“Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:31–34; Mark 10:48–52"> So Jesus stopped and ordered him to be brought to him. And when he approached, he asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5–15; Matthew 20:21–22; Philippians 4:6"> “What do you want me to do for you?” And he said, “Lord, that I may regain my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 8:3; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19"> And Jesus said to him, “Regain your sight! Your faith has saved you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalm 146:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> And immediately he regained his sight and began to follow him, glorifying God. And all the people, when they saw it, gave praise to God.</span>
