## Sin, Forgiveness, Faith, and Service

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:7; Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 11:19"> And he said to his disciples, “It is impossible for causes for stumbling not to come, but woe to him through whom they come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 9:42; Matthew 18:14; Matthew 18:3–6"> It would be better for him if ⸤a millstone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a stone belonging to a mill”">a</a></sup>is placed around his neck and he is thrown into the sea than that he causes one of these little ones to sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Matthew 18:21; Matthew 18:15–17"> “Be concerned about yourselves! If your brother sins, rebuke him, and if he repents, forgive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 18:21–22; Romans 12:20"> And if he sins against you seven times in a day, and seven times he returns to you saying, ‘I repent,’ you must forgive him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Philippians 4:13"> And the apostles said to the Lord, “Increase our faith!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; Matthew 21:21; Mark 9:23"> So the Lord said, “If you have faith like a mustard seed, you could say to this mulberry tree, ‘Be uprooted and planted in the sea,’ and it would obey you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:11; Luke 13:15; Luke 14:5"> “And which of you who has a slave plowing or shepherding sheep who comes in from the field will say to him, ‘Come here at once and recline at the table’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:37; Genesis 43:16; 2 Samuel 12:20"> Will he not rather say to him, ‘Prepare something that I may eat, and dress yourself to serve me while I eat and drink, and after these things you will eat and drink.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He will not ⸤be grateful⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “have gratitude”">b</a></sup>to the slave because he did what was ordered, will he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–17; Job 22:2–3; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Thus you also, when you have done all the ⸤things you were ordered to do⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “things that were ordered to you”">c</a></sup>say, ‘We are unworthy slaves; we have done what we were obligated to do.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ten Lepers Cleansed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:51–52; John 4:3–4"> And it happened that while traveling toward Jerusalem, he was passing through ⸤the region between⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “through the midst”">d</a></sup>Samaria and Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Luke 5:12; 2 Kings 7:3"> And as he was entering into a certain village, ten men met him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts explicitly state “him”">e</a></sup>—lepers, who stood at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Matthew 9:27"> And they raised their voices, saying, “Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:14; Leviticus 13:1–46; 2 Kings 5:14"> And when he saw them he said to them, “Go and show yourselves to the priests.” And it happened that as they were going, they were cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 30:11–12; Psalm 103:1–4; Psalm 118:18–19"> But one of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back, praising God with a loud voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 8:5–25; Matthew 10:5; Revelation 19:10"> And he fell on his face at his feet, giving thanks to him. And he was a Samaritan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; Psalm 106:13; Genesis 3:9"> So Jesus answered and said, “Were not ten cleansed? And where are the nine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 50:23; Matthew 15:24–28; Psalm 106:13"> Was no one found to turn back and give praise to God except this foreigner?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Luke 8:48; Mark 10:52"> And he said to him, “Get up and go your way. Your faith has saved you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Coming of the Kingdom of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 18:36; Luke 19:11; Daniel 2:44"> Now when he was asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God would come, he answered them and said, “The kingdom of God does not come with ⸤things that can be observed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “observation”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 12:28; Romans 14:17; Mark 13:21"> nor will they say, ‘Behold, here it is!’ or ‘There!’ For behold, the kingdom of God is in your midst.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Coming of the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 5:35; Matthew 9:15; John 8:21–24"> And he said to the disciples, “Days will come when you will desire to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you will not see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–26; Luke 17:21"> And they will say to you, ‘Behold, there!’ ‘Behold, here!’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “ ‘Behold, there!’ or ‘Behold, here!’ ”">g</a></sup>Do not go out or run after them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Matthew 24:30; Malachi 4:1–2"> For just as the lightning shines forth, flashing from one place under heaven to another place under heaven, so the Son of Man will be in his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> But first it is necessary for him to suffer many things, and to be rejected by this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:24; Genesis 7:7–23"> And just as it was in the days of Noah, so also it will be in the days of the Son of Man—</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–3; Luke 16:19–23"> they were eating, they were drinking, they were marrying, they were being given in marriage, until the day Noah entered into the ark, and the flood came and destroyed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49–50; Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:1–5"> Likewise, just as it was in the days of Lot—they were eating, they were drinking, they were buying, they were selling, they were planting, they were building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; Zephaniah 2:9; Jude 1:7"> But on the day that Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire and sulphur from heaven and destroyed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Revelation 1:7"> It will be ⸤just the same⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “according to the same”">h</a></sup>on the day that the Son of Man is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 21:21; Mark 13:14–16; Jeremiah 45:5"> On that day, whoever is on the housetop and his goods are in the house must not come down to take them away. And likewise the one who is in the field must not turn back</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 19:26; Hebrews 10:38–39; 2 Peter 2:18–22"> Remember Lot’s wife!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:35–37; Matthew 10:39; John 12:25"> Whoever seeks to preserve his life will lose it, but whoever loses it will keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 24:40–41; 2 Peter 2:9; Malachi 3:16–18"> I tell you that in that night there will be two in one bed; one will be taken and the other will be left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5; Judges 16:21"> There will be two women grinding at the same place; one will be taken and the other will be left.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A few manuscripts add v. 36 (with some variations): “There will be two in the field; one will be taken and the other will be left.”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 24:28; Job 39:29–30; Revelation 19:17–18"> And they answered and said to him, “Where, Lord?” So he said to them, “Where the dead body is, there also the vultures will be gathered.”</span>
</div>
