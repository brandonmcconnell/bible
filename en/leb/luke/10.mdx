## The Seventy-Two Appointed and Sent Out

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:7–13; Luke 9:52; Numbers 11:24–26"> And after these things, the Lord also<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “also”">a</a></sup>appointed seventy-two others and sent them out two by two before ⸤him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “his presence”">b</a></sup>into every town and place where he was about to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 9:36–38; John 4:35–38; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9"> And he said to them, “The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few. Therefore ask the Lord of the harvest that he send out workers into his harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Acts 20:29; Ezekiel 2:3–6"> Go! Behold, I am sending you out like lambs in the midst of wolves!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:35; 2 Kings 4:29; Luke 10:4–12"> Do not carry a money bag or a traveler’s bag or sandals, and greet no one along the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Matthew 10:12–13; Isaiah 57:19"> And into whatever house you enter, first say, “Peace be to this household!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16; Ephesians 5:6"> And if a son of peace is there, your peace will rest on him. But if not, it will return to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Matthew 10:10–11; Galatians 6:6"> And remain in the same house, eating and drinking ⸤whatever they provide⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the things from them”">c</a></sup>for the worker is worthy of his pay. Do not move from house to house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; Matthew 10:40; Luke 10:10"> And into whatever town you enter and they welcome you, eat ⸤whatever is⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the things”">d</a></sup>set before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; John 3:5; Luke 10:11"> and heal the sick in it, and say to them, “The kingdom of God has come near to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 10:14; Acts 13:51"> But into whatever town you enter and they do not welcome you, go out into its streets and say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:9; Acts 13:51; Acts 13:26"> “Even the dust of your town that clings to our feet we wipe off against you! Nevertheless know this: that the kingdom of God has come near!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “has come”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:24; Lamentations 4:6"> I tell you that it will be more bearable on that day for Sodom than for that town!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1–18; Joel 3:4–8; Ezekiel 26:1–26"> Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the miracles that were done in you had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 15:22–25; Romans 2:1; Luke 12:47–48"> But it will be more bearable for Tyre and for Sidon in the judgment than for you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Matthew 4:13; Luke 13:28"> And you, Capernaum, will you be exalted to heaven? No! You will be brought down to Hades!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:20; Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> The one who listens to you listens to me, and the one who rejects you rejects me. But the one who rejects me rejects the one who sent me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Mark 16:17; Luke 9:1"> And the seventy-two returned with joy, saying, “Lord, even the demons are subject to us in your name!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 12:7–9; Isaiah 14:12; Revelation 9:1"> So he said to them, “I saw Satan falling like lightning from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 91:13; Mark 16:18; Romans 16:20"> Behold, I have given you the authority to tread on snakes and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy, and nothing will ever harm you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 20:15; Revelation 20:12; Hebrews 12:23"> Nevertheless, do not rejoice in this, that the spirits are subject to you, but rejoice that your names are inscribed in heaven.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Rejoices and Prays

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3; Matthew 13:11–16; Matthew 11:25–27"> At that same time he rejoiced in the Holy Spirit and said, “I praise you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that you have hidden these things from the wise and intelligent and have revealed them to young children. Yes, Father, for this was pleasing before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:27; John 1:18; John 6:44–46"> All things have been handed over to me by my Father, and no one knows who the Son is except the Father and who the Father is except the Son, and anyone to whom the Son wants to reveal him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17"> And turning to the disciples, he said privately, “Blessed are the eyes that see the things which you see!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> For I tell you that many prophets and kings desired to see the things which you see, and did not see them, and to hear the things which you hear, and did not hear them.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Good Samaritan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–39; Luke 18:18; Matthew 19:16–19"> And behold, a certain legal expert stood up to test him, saying, “Teacher, what must I do so that I will inherit eternal life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Isaiah 8:20; Galatians 3:21–22"> And he said to him, “What is written in the law? How do you read it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 12:30–31; Matthew 22:37–40; Deuteronomy 6:5"> And he answered and said, “You shall love the Lord your God from all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your strength, and with all your mind,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 6:5">f</a></sup>and your neighbor as yourself.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 19:18">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:11; Leviticus 18:5; Matthew 19:17"> And he said to him, “You have answered correctly. Do this and you will live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Romans 4:2; Leviticus 19:34"> But he, wanting to justify himself, said to Jesus, “And who is my neighbor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 88:4; Luke 18:31; Lamentations 2:12"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “and”">h</a></sup>Jesus replied and said, “A certain man was going down from Jerusalem to Jericho, and fell into the hands of robbers, who both stripped him and beat him. After inflicting blows on him, they went away, leaving him half dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Psalm 69:20; Jeremiah 5:31"> Now by coincidence a certain priest was going down on that road, and when he saw him, he passed by on the opposite side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 27:10; Acts 18:17; Psalm 109:25"> And in the same way also a Levite, when he came down to the place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “who happened by the place, when he came up to him”">i</a></sup>and saw him, passed by on the opposite side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 8:50; Jeremiah 38:7–13; Luke 7:13"> But a certain Samaritan who was traveling came up to him and, when he saw him, had compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 12:20; Psalm 147:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> And he came up and bandaged his wounds, pouring on olive oil and wine, and he put him on his own animal and brought him to an inn and took care of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 19:17; Luke 14:13; Romans 16:23"> And on the next day, he took out two denarii and gave them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “he took out and gave two denarii”">j</a></sup>to the innkeeper, and said, “Take care of him, and whatever you spend in addition, I will repay to you when I return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 7:42; Matthew 21:28–31; Matthew 17:25"> Which of these three do you suppose became a neighbor of the man who fell among the robbers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 3:16–18; Micah 6:8; 1 Peter 2:21"> So he said, “The one who showed mercy to him.” And Jesus said to him, “You go and do likewise.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Martha and Mary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:19–20; John 11:1–5; John 12:1–3"> Now as they traveled along, he entered into a certain village. And a certain woman ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “by name”">k</a></sup>Martha welcomed him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Most manuscripts add some form of the location such as “into her house” but there is considerable variation in the exact wording, so the shorter reading is preferred">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Luke 8:35; John 12:3"> And ⸤she had⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “this was”">m</a></sup>a sister named Mary, who also sat at the feet of Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “of the Lord”">n</a></sup>and was listening to his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:27; Luke 12:29; Matthew 14:15"> But Martha was distracted with much preparation, so she approached and said, “Lord, is it not a concern to you that my sister has left me alone to make preparations? <span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Luke 21:34; Matthew 6:25–34"> But the Lord answered and said to her, “Martha, Martha, you are anxious and troubled about many things!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalm 27:4; Psalm 16:5–6; John 10:27–28"> But few things are necessary, or only one thing,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “But one thing is necessary”">o</a></sup>for Mary has chosen the better part, which will not be taken away from her.”</span>
