## Jesus’ Authority Challenged

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–27; Luke 19:47–48; Acts 6:12"> And it happened that on one of the days while he was teaching the people in the temple courts and proclaiming the gospel, the chief priests and the scribes approached together with the elders</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Mark 11:28–33; John 2:18"> and said, saying to him, “Tell us, by what authority you are doing these things, or who is the one who gave you this authority?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Luke 22:68; Colossians 4:6"> And he answered and said to them, “I also will ask you a question, and you tell me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 15:18; Matthew 11:7–19; Matthew 17:11–12"> The baptism of John—was it from heaven or from men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:34; John 1:15–18; John 1:30"> And they discussed this with one another, saying, “If we say ‘From heaven,’ he will say, ‘Why did you not believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; John 10:41"> But if we say, ‘From men,’ all the people will stone us to death, because they are convinced that John was a prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; 2 Peter 3:3; Isaiah 41:28"> And they replied that they did not know where it was from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4–5; Matthew 21:27; Mark 11:33"> And Jesus said to them, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I am doing these things.”</span>
</div>

## The Parable of the Tenant Farmers in the Vineyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:33–46; Mark 12:1–12; Isaiah 5:1–7"> And he began to tell the people this parable: “A man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “A certain man”">a</a></sup>planted a vineyard, and leased it to tenant farmers, and went on a journey for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Nehemiah 9:26; Matthew 21:34–36"> And at the proper time he sent a slave to the tenant farmers, so that they would give him some of the fruit of the vineyard. But the tenant farmers sent him away empty-handed after beating him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 10:1; Hebrews 11:36–37; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> And he proceeded to send another slave, but they beat and dishonored that one also, and sent him away empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he proceeded to send a third, but they wounded and threw out this one also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; John 3:16–17; Jeremiah 36:7"> So the owner of the vineyard said, ‘What should I do? I will send my beloved son; perhaps they will respect him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Hebrews 1:2; Acts 2:23"> But when the tenant farmers saw him, they began to reason with one another, saying, ‘This is the heir. Let us kill him so that the inheritance will become ours!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:12; Mark 12:6–9; Matthew 21:37–40"> And they threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. What then will the owner of the vineyard do to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:41"> He will come and destroy those tenant farmers and give the vineyard to others.” And when they heard this, they said, “May this never happen!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 118:22; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> But he looked intently at them and said, “What then is this that is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The stone which the builders rejected,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">this has become ⸤the cornerstone⸥.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the head of the corner”">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; Zechariah 12:3; Daniel 2:44–45"> Everyone who falls on that stone will be broken to pieces, and the one on whom it falls—it will crush him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 19:47–48; Luke 20:14; Matthew 21:45–46"> And the scribes and the chief priests sought to lay their hands on him at that same hour, and they were afraid of the people, for they knew that he had told this parable with reference to them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paying Taxes to Caesar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 11:54; Matthew 22:15–22; Matthew 27:2"> And they watched him closely and sent spies who pretended they were upright, in order that they could catch him in a statement, so that they could hand him over to the authority and the jurisdiction of the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4–5; Matthew 26:49–50; Matthew 22:16"> And they asked him, saying, “Teacher, we know that you speak and teach rightly, and do not ⸤show partiality⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “receive face”">c</a></sup>but teach the way of God in truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:37; Mark 12:14–17; Acts 5:37"> Is it permitted for us to pay taxes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “the tribute tax”">d</a></sup>to Caesar or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 22:18; 1 Corinthians 3:19; Luke 5:22"> But seeing through their craftiness, he said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 18:28; Acts 11:28; Acts 26:32"> “Show me a denarius! Whose image and inscription does it have?” And they answered and said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “And they said”">e</a></sup>“Caesar’s.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 13:6–7; Mark 12:17; 1 Peter 2:13–17"> So he said to them, “Well then, give to Caesar the things of Caesar, and to God the things of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Proverbs 26:4–5; Titus 1:10"> And they were not able to catch him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts explicitly state “him”">f</a></sup>in a statement in the sight of the people, and astonished at his answer, they became silent.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Question About Marriage and the Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; 1 Corinthians 15:12; Mark 12:18–27"> Now some of the Sadducees—who deny ⸤that there is a resurrection⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “resurrection not to exist”">g</a></sup>—came up and asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Genesis 38:8; Genesis 38:11"> saying, “Teacher, Moses wrote for us if someone’s brother dies having a wife, and this man is childless, that his brother should take the wife and ⸤father⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “raise up”">h</a></sup>descendants for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20; Jeremiah 22:30"> Now there were seven brothers, and the first took a wife and died childless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the second,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> and the third took her, and likewise also the seven did not leave children and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Hebrews 9:27"> Finally the woman also died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 22:24–28; Mark 12:19–23"> Therefore in the resurrection, the woman—whose wife will she be? For the seven had her as wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 16:8; Luke 17:27; Hebrews 13:4"> And Jesus said to them, “The sons of this age marry and are given in marriage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; John 5:29; Daniel 12:2–3"> but those who are considered worthy to attain to that age and to the resurrection from the dead neither marry nor are given in marriage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Philippians 3:21; Matthew 22:30"> for they are not even able to die any longer, because they are like the angels and are sons of God, because they are sons of the resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Mark 12:26–27; Acts 7:30–32"> But that the dead are raised, even Moses revealed in the passage about the bush, when he calls the Lord the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 6:10–11"> Now he is not God of the dead, but of the living, for all live to him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 23:9; Mark 12:28–34; Matthew 22:34–40"> And some of the scribes answered and said, “Teacher, you have spoken well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 22:46; Mark 12:34; Proverbs 26:5"> For they no longer dared to ask him anything.</span>
</div>
</div>

## David’s Son and Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 12:35–37; Matthew 1:1; Matthew 22:41–45"> But he said to them, “In what sense do they say that the Christ is David’s son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalm 110:1; Acts 2:34–35; Hebrews 1:13"> For David himself says in the book of Psalms,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Sit at my right hand,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 19:27; Psalm 110:1; Revelation 19:14–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">until I make your enemies</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a footstool for your feet.” ’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 110:1">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:31–35; Matthew 1:23"> David therefore calls him ‘Lord,’ and how is he his son?”</span>

## Warning to Beware of the Scribes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Mark 12:38–39; Mark 8:34"> And while all the people were listening, he said to the disciples,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “to his disciples”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:43; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7"> “Beware of the scribes, who like walking around in long robes and who love greetings in the marketplaces and the best seats in the synagogues and the places of honor at banquets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 12:40; James 3:1; Titus 1:16"> who devour the houses of widows and pray lengthy prayers for the sake of appearance. These will receive more severe condemnation!”</span>
