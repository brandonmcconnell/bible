## The Parable of the Sower

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 4:1–9; Matthew 13:2–9"> And while a large crowd was gathering and they were going to him from town after town, he spoke by means of a parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; James 1:23–24; Mark 4:15"> “The sower went out to sow his seed, and while he was sowing, ⸤some seed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “some of which”">b</a></sup>fell on the side of the path and was trampled under foot, and the birds of the sky devoured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Luke 8:13; Mark 4:5–6"> And other seed fell on the rock, and when it came up, it withered, because it did not have moisture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Luke 21:34"> And other seed fell in the midst of the thorn plants, and the thorn plants grew up with it and choked it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 2:7"> And other seed fell on the good soil, and when it came up, it produced a hundred times as much grain.” As he said these things, he called out, “The one who has ears to hear, let him hear!”</span>
</div>

## The Parable of the Sower Interpreted

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:14–20; James 1:21"> Now the parable means this: the seed is the word of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–14; Revelation 12:9; James 1:23–24"> and those beside the path are the ones who have heard. Then the devil comes and takes away the word from their heart, so that they may not believe and be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 6:4; John 5:35; Proverbs 12:12"> And those on the rock are those who receive the word with joy when they hear it, and these do not have enough root, who believe for a time and in a time of testing fall away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> And the seed that fell into the thorn plants—these are the ones who hear and as they go along are choked by the worries and riches and pleasures of life, and they do not bear fruit to maturity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Galatians 5:22–26; Colossians 1:10"> But the seed on the good soil—these are the ones who, after hearing the word, hold fast to it with a noble and good heart, and bear fruit with patient endurance.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Matthew 10:26; Mark 4:22"> For nothing is secret that will not become evident, and nothing hidden that will never be known and come to light.</span>

</div>

## Calming of a Storm

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–27; Mark 4:35–41; Mark 6:45"> Now it happened that on one of the days both he and his disciples got into a boat, and he said to them, “Let us cross over to the other side of the lake.” And they set sail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 93:3–4; Psalm 124:2–4; Psalm 148:8"> and as they were sailing, he fell asleep. And a storm of wind came down on the lake, and they were being swamped and were in danger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 107:25–29; Luke 4:39; Psalm 104:6–9"> And they came and woke him up, saying, “Master, master! We are perishing!” So he got up and rebuked the wind and the billowing waves of water and they ceased, and it became calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 8:26; Proverbs 30:4; Mark 4:40–41"> And he said to them, “Where is your faith?” But they were afraid and were astonished, saying to one another, “Who then is this, that he commands even the winds and the water and they obey him?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Demon-possessed Gerasene Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 5:1–20; Matthew 8:28–34"> And they sailed to the region of the Gerasenes, which is opposite Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 5:2–5; 1 Samuel 19:24; Numbers 19:16"> And as he got out on the land, a certain man from the town met him who had demons and for a considerable time had not worn clothes and did not live in a house, but among the tombs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; 2 Peter 2:4; Mark 5:6–8"> And when he saw Jesus, he cried out, fell down before him, and said with a loud voice, “⸤What do I have to do with you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “what to me and to you”">d</a></sup>Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg you, do not torment me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:3–5; Luke 9:39; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For he had commanded the unclean spirit to come out of the man. (For it had seized him many times, and he was bound with chains and shackles and was guarded, and breaking the bonds he would be driven by the demon into the deserted places.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 26:53; Luke 8:2; Matthew 8:29"> So Jesus asked him, “What is your name?” And he said, “Legion,” because many demons had entered into him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Philippians 2:10–11; Job 2:5"> And they began imploring him that he would not order them to depart into the abyss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3; Revelation 20:7"> Now there was a large herd of pigs feeding there on the hill, and they implored him that he would permit them to enter into those pigs. And he permitted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; John 8:44; Luke 8:22–23"> So the demons came out of the man and entered into the pigs, and the herd rushed headlong down the steep slope into the lake and were drowned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 5:14; Matthew 8:33; Matthew 28:11"> And when the herdsmen saw what had happened, they fled and reported it in the town and in the countryside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:39; Acts 22:3; 1 John 3:8"> So they went out to see what had happened, and they came to Jesus and found the man from whom the demons had gone out sitting there clothed and in his right mind, at the feet of Jesus, and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 4:24"> And those who had seen it reported to them how the man who had been demon-possessed had been healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:28; Luke 10:10–11"> And all the people of the surrounding region of the Gerasenes asked him to depart from them, because they had been seized with great fear. So he got into the boat and returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:13–16; Mark 5:18–20; Psalm 145:3–12"> And the man from who the demons had gone out was begging him to stay with him, but he sent him away, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 17:15–18; 1 Timothy 5:8; Deuteronomy 10:21"> “Return to your home and tell all that God has done for you.” And he went away, proclaiming throughout the whole town all that Jesus had done for him.</span>
</div>

## A Woman Healed and a Daughter Raised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 5:21; Mark 6:20"> Now when Jesus returned, the crowd welcomed him, because they were all waiting for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 5:22–43; Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8"> And behold, a man ⸤who was named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to whom the name”">e</a></sup>Jairus came, and this man was a ruler of the synagogue. And he fell down at the feet of Jesus and began imploring him to come to his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 7:12; Luke 8:45; Job 1:18–19"> because ⸤he had⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “there was to him”">f</a></sup>an only daughter, about twelve years old, and she was dying.<br />Now as he was going, the crowds were pressing against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 5:25–26; Matthew 9:20–22; Leviticus 15:25–33"> And a woman who was ⸤suffering from hemorrhages⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “with a flow of blood”">g</a></sup>for twelve years (who, although she had spent all her assets on physicians, was not able to be healed by anyone)</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Mark 5:27–28; Deuteronomy 22:12; Acts 19:12"> came up behind him and touched the edge of his cloak, and immediately ⸤her hemorrhaging⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the flow of her blood”">h</a></sup>stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 5:30–32; Luke 9:13; Luke 5:5"> And Jesus said, “Who is the one who touched me?” And when they all denied it, Peter said, “Master, the crowds are pressing you hard and crowding you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 5:17; 1 Peter 2:9"> But Jesus said, “Someone touched me, because I know power has gone out from me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 5:33; Luke 17:15–16; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> And when the woman saw that she did not escape notice, she came trembling and falling down before him. In the presence of all the people, she told for what reason she had touched him, and that she was healed immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 9:22; Hebrews 4:2"> And he said to her, “Daughter, your faith has saved you. Go in peace.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:35–43; Isaiah 7:12; Luke 7:6"> While he was still speaking, someone came from the synagogue ruler’s house, saying, “Your daughter is dead! Trouble the Teacher no longer!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 11:25; Mark 5:36"> But Jesus, when he heard this, replied to him, “Do not be afraid! Only believe, and she will be healed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Mark 14:33; Matthew 6:5–6; 1 Kings 17:19–23"> Now when he came to the house, he did not allow anyone to enter with him except Peter and John and James and the father and mother of the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 23:27; John 11:4; Zechariah 12:10"> And they were all weeping and mourning for her, but he said, “Do not weep! For she is not dead, but is sleeping.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Luke 16:14; Isaiah 53:3; John 11:39"> And they began laughing at him, because they knew that she was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; John 11:43; Mark 1:31"> But he took her hand and called, saying, “Child, get up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 11:44; 1 Kings 17:21–23; Mark 5:43"> And her spirit returned, and she got up immediately, and he ordered something to be given to her to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Luke 5:14"> And her parents were astonished, but he ordered them to tell no one what had happened.</span>
</div>
