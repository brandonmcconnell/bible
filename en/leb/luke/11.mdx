## Ask, Seek, Knock

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:1–8"> And he said to them, “Who of you will have a friend, and will go to him at midnight and say to him, ‘Friend, lend me three loaves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> because a friend of mine has come to me on a journey, and I do not have anything to set before him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 13:25; Galatians 6:17; Luke 7:6"> And that one will answer from inside and say, ‘Do not cause me trouble! The door has already been shut and my children are with me in bed! I am not able to get up to give you anything.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Colossians 4:12; Romans 15:30"> I tell you, even if he does not give him anything after he gets up because he is his friend, at any rate because of his impudence<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “shamelessness”; some translate as “persistence” based on the context, though this is not the normal meaning of the word">a</a></sup>he will get up and give him whatever he needs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 11:24; John 16:23–24; Matthew 21:22"> And I tell you, ask and it will be given to you; seek and you will find; knock and it will be opened for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:3; Lamentations 3:54–58; Psalm 31:22"> For everyone who asks receives, and the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks it will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 7:9; Isaiah 49:15"> But what father from among you, if his son will ask for a fish, instead of a fish will give him a snake?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Luke 10:19; Ezekiel 2:6"> Or also, if he will ask for an egg, will give him a scorpion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:11; Isaiah 44:3–4; Romans 8:32"> Therefore if you, although you are evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will the Father from heaven give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A House Divided Cannot Stand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 12:22–24; Matthew 9:32–34; Mark 7:32–37"> And he was expelling a mute demon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “a demon, and it was mute”">b</a></sup>Now it happened that when the demon came out, the man who had been mute spoke, and the crowds were astonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 9:34; Matthew 12:24–30; John 8:48"> But some of them said, “By Beelzebul the ruler of demons he expels demons!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 6:30; 1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12"> And others, in order to test him, were demanding from him a sign from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 3:23–27; Matthew 12:25–29; Isaiah 19:2–3"> But he, knowing their thoughts, said to them, “Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste, and ⸤a divided household⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “house against house”">c</a></sup>falls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 12:26; Matthew 12:31–34; Luke 11:15"> So if Satan also is divided against himself, how will his kingdom stand? For you say that I expel demons by Beelzebul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 9:49; Job 15:6; Romans 3:19"> But if I expel demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your sons expel them? For this reason they will be your judges!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 8:19; Matthew 12:28; Luke 10:9"> But if I expel demons by the finger of God, then the kingdom of God has come upon you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:27; Matthew 12:29"> When a strong man, fully armed, guards his own palace, his possessions are ⸤safe⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in peace”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–25; Isaiah 53:12; 1 John 3:8"> But when a stronger man attacks him and conquers him, he takes away his full armor in which he trusted and distributes his plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:30; Luke 9:50; Mark 9:40"> The one who is not with me is against me, and the one who does not gather with me scatters.</span>
</div>
</div>

## An Unclean Spirit Returns

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 12:43–45; 1 Peter 5:8; Job 2:2"> “Whenever an unclean spirit has gone out of a person, it travels through waterless places searching for rest, and does not find it. It says,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Then it says”">e</a></sup>‘I will return to my house from which I came out.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; 2 Peter 2:10–19; Jude 1:8–13"> And when it arrives it finds the house swept and put in order.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 5:14; Matthew 12:45; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> Then it goes and brings along seven other spirits more evil than itself, and they go in and live there. And the last state of that person becomes worse than the first!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 1:42; Luke 1:48; Luke 23:29"> Now it happened that as he said these things, a certain woman from the crowd raised her voice and said to him, “Blessed is the womb that bore you, and the breasts at which you nursed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 6:47–48; Luke 8:21; Psalm 112:1"> But he said, “On the contrary, blessed are those who hear the word of God and follow it!”</span>

## The Sign of Jonah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Mark 8:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> And as the crowds were increasing, he began to say, “This generation is an evil generation! It demands a sign, and no sign will be given to it except the sign of Jonah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 12:40–42; Jonah 1:17; Jonah 3:2–10"> For just as Jonah became a sign to the Ninevites, so also the Son of Man will be to this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; 1 Kings 10:1–13; Romans 2:27"> The queen of the south will rise up at the judgment with the people of this generation and condemn them, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and behold, something greater than Solomon is here!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Luke 11:31; Jonah 3:5–4"> The people of Nineveh will stand up at the judgment with this generation and condemn it, because they repented at the proclamation of Jonah, and behold, something greater than Jonah is here!</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:16–17; Mark 4:21–22; Matthew 5:15–16"> “No one after lighting a lamp puts it in a cellar or under a bushel basket, but on a lampstand, so that those who come in can see the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:22–23; Acts 26:18; Jeremiah 5:21"> Your eye is the lamp of the body. When your eye is sincere, your whole body is full of light also. But when it is evil, your body is dark also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 16:25; 2 Peter 1:9; James 3:13–17"> Therefore pay careful attention that the light in you is not darkness!</span>

</div>
</div>

## Pharisees and Legal Experts Denounced

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 7:36; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23; Luke 14:1"> And as he was speaking, a Pharisee asked him ⸤to have a meal⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “that he would have a meal”">f</a></sup>with him, and he went in and reclined at table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:2–3; Mark 7:2–5; John 3:25"> And the Pharisee, when he saw it, was astonished that he did not first wash before the meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 30:12; Genesis 6:5"> But the Lord said to him, “Now you Pharisees cleanse the outside of the cup and of the dish, but your inside is full of greediness and wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 12:20; 1 Corinthians 15:36; Matthew 23:26"> Fools! Did not the one who made the outside make the inside also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 12:33; James 1:27; Titus 1:15"> But give as charitable giving the things that are within, and behold, everything is clean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Luke 18:12; Proverbs 21:3"> “But woe to you, Pharisees, because you pay a tenth of mint and rue and every garden herb, and neglect justice and love for God! But it was necessary to do these things without neglecting those things also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38–39; Luke 14:7–11"> Woe to you, Pharisees, because you love the best seat in the synagogues and the greetings in the marketplaces!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 23:27–28; Psalm 5:9; Acts 23:3"> Woe to you, because you are like unmarked graves, and the people who walk over them do not know it!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 9:40; Matthew 22:35; Jeremiah 6:10"> And one of the legal experts answered and said to him, “Teacher, when you say these things, you insult us also!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Isaiah 10:1; Matthew 23:2–4"> So he said, “Woe to you also, legal experts, because you load people with burdens hard to bear, and you yourselves do not touch the burdens with one of your fingers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Acts 7:51; Matthew 23:29–33"> Woe to you, because you build the tombs of the prophets, and your fathers killed them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="James 5:10; Matthew 23:31; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> As a result you are witnesses, and you approve of the deeds of your fathers, because they killed them and you build their tombs!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A large number of later manuscripts add the words “their tombs” here, with variations of wording; although the words are not likely to be original, it is necessary to supply them in keeping with English style">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; Ephesians 4:11"> For this reason also the wisdom of God said, ‘I will send to them prophets and apostles, and some of them they will kill and persecute,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Revelation 18:20–24; 2 Kings 24:4"> so that the blood of all the prophets that has been shed from the foundation of the world may be required of this generation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Matthew 23:35; Hebrews 11:4"> from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah, who perished between the altar and the temple building.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the house,” here a reference to the temple">h</a></sup>Yes, I tell you, it will be required of this generation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Luke 11:45–46"> Woe to you, legal experts, because you have taken away the key to knowledge! You did not enter yourselves, and you hindered those who were entering!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; Jeremiah 20:10; Luke 20:20"> And when he departed from there, the scribes and the Pharisees began to be terribly hostile, and to question him closely about many things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 12:13; Matthew 22:15; Matthew 22:35"> plotting to catch him with reference to something ⸤he might say⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “from his mouth”">i</a></sup></span>
