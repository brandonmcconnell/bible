## The Genealogy of Jesus Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Romans 1:3; Galatians 3:16"> The book of the genealogy of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:8; Hebrews 11:17–18; Genesis 46:8–27"> Abraham became the father of Isaac, and Isaac became the father of Jacob, and Jacob became the father of Judah and his brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:29–30; 1 Chronicles 4:1"> and Judah became the father of Perez and Zerah by Tamar, and Perez became the father of Hezron, and Hezron became the father of Aram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Although the Greek text reads “Aram,” many English versions substitute the Old Testament form of the name, “Ram” (cf. 1 Chr 2:9 ; Ruth 4:19 ), here and in the following verse">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10–12; Numbers 7:12; Numbers 2:3"> and Aram became the father of Amminadab, and Amminadab became the father of Nahshon, and Nahshon became the father of Salmon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Ruth 1:16–17; James 2:25"> and Salmon became the father of Boaz by Rahab, and Boaz became the father of Obed by Ruth, and Obed became the father of Jesse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 17:12; 2 Samuel 12:24–25"> and Jesse became the father of David the king.<br />And David became the father of Solomon by the wife of Uriah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1–15; 1 Kings 14:31; 1 Kings 11:43–24"> and Solomon became the father of Rehoboam, and Rehoboam became the father of Abijah, and Abijah became the father of Asa,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Greek “Asaph,” alternately spelled “Asa” in many English versions here and in the following verse (cf. 1 Chr 3:10 )">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:11; 2 Chronicles 26:1–23; 2 Kings 8:16"> and Asa became the father of Jehoshaphat,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Greek “Josaphat”; alternately spelled “Jehoshaphat” in many English versions">c</a></sup>and Jehoshaphat became the father of Joram, and Joram became the father of Uzziah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:1–13; 2 Chronicles 26:21; 2 Chronicles 27:1–32"> and Uzziah became the father of Jotham, and Jotham became the father of Ahaz, and Ahaz became the father of Hezekiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:33–27; 1 Kings 13:2; 1 Chronicles 3:13–15"> and Hezekiah became the father of Manasseh, and Manasseh became the father of Amon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The earliest and best Greek manuscripts read “Amos,” but many English versions use the Old Testament form of the name here, “Amon” (cf. 2 Kgs 21:18 )">d</a></sup>and Amon became the father of Josiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:20; Jeremiah 52:28–30; Daniel 1:2"> and Josiah became the father of Jechoniah and his brothers, at the time of the deportation to Babylon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:17; Ezra 3:2; Luke 3:27"> And after the deportation to Babylon, Jechoniah became the father of Shealtiel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Greek “Salathiel,” but many English versions use the Old Testament form of the name here, “Shealtiel” (cf. Ezra 3:2 )">e</a></sup>and Shealtiel became the father of Zerubbabel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and Zerubbabel became the father of Abiud, and Abiud became the father of Eliakim, and Eliakim became the father of Azor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and Azor became the father of Zadok, and Zadok became the father of Achim, and Achim became the father of Eliud,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and Eliud became the father of Eleazar, and Eleazar became the father of Matthan, and Matthan became the father of Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:17; John 4:25; Luke 3:23"> and Jacob became the father of Joseph, the husband of Mary by whom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Greek relative pronoun is feminine gender and thus refers only to Mary, not Joseph">f</a></sup>was born Jesus, who is called Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 1:11–12; 2 Kings 24:14; Matthew 11:2"> Therefore all the generations from Abraham to David are fourteen generations, and from David to the deportation to Babylon are fourteen generations, and from the deportation to Babylon to the Christ are fourteen generations.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Birth of Jesus Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Luke 1:27–38; Job 14:4"> Now the birth of Jesus Christ occurred in this way. His mother Mary had been betrothed to Joseph, but before they came together, she was found ⸤to be pregnant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to have in the womb”">g</a></sup>by the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; John 8:4–5"> So Joseph her husband, being righteous and not wanting to disgrace her, intended to divorce her secretly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Luke 1:19; Matthew 1:18"> But as he was considering these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, “Joseph, son of David, do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife, for what has been conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:23; Acts 4:12; John 1:29"> And she will give birth to a son, and you will call his name ‘Jesus,’ because he will save his people from their sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Matthew 2:23; 1 Kings 8:15"> Now all this happened in order that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet would be fulfilled, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6–7; Psalm 46:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, the virgin ⸤will become pregnant⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “will have in the womb”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will give birth to a son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will call his name Emmanuel,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 7:14">i</a></sup><br />which is translated, “God with us.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="An allusion to Isa 8:8 , 10 in the Greek OT (LXX)">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:14; James 2:21–26; John 2:5–8"> And Joseph, when he woke up from sleep, did as the angel of the Lord commanded him, and he took his wife</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Exodus 13:2; Matthew 1:21"> and ⸤did not have sexual relations with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “did not know”">k</a></sup>her until she gave birth to a son. And he called his name Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>
