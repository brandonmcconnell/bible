## A Paralytic Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Luke 8:37; Matthew 8:18"> And getting into a boat, he crossed over and came to his own town.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A reference to Capernaum">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:1–12; Luke 5:18–26; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> And behold, they brought to him a paralytic lying on a stretcher, and when Jesus saw their faith, he said to the paralytic, “Have courage, child, your sins are forgiven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Luke 5:21; Leviticus 24:16"> And behold, some of the scribes said to themselves, “This man is blaspheming!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:8; Matthew 12:25; Revelation 2:23"> And knowing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “perceiving”">b</a></sup>their thoughts, Jesus said, “Why do you think evil in your hearts?</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Matthew 9:5; John 20:21–23"> But in order that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins,” then he said to the paralytic, “Get up, pick up your stretcher and go to your home.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he got up and went to his home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Matthew 15:31; Acts 4:21"> But when the crowds saw this, they were afraid and glorified God who had given such authority to men.</span>

## On Fasting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:33–39; Mark 2:18–22; Matthew 6:16"> Then the disciples of John<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, John the Baptist">g</a></sup>approached him, saying, “Why do we and the Pharisees fast often, but your disciples do not fast?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; John 3:29; Revelation 21:2"> And Jesus said to them, “⸤The bridegroom’s attendants⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the bridal chamber”">h</a></sup>are not able to mourn as long as the bridegroom is with them. But days are coming when the bridegroom is taken away from them, and then they will fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; John 16:12; Psalm 125:3"> But no one puts a patch of unshrunken cloth on an old garment, for its patch pulls away from the garment, and the tear becomes worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 9:4; Job 32:19; Psalm 119:83"> Nor do they put new wine into old wineskins. ⸤Otherwise⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “but if not”">i</a></sup>the wineskins burst and the wine is spilled, and the wineskins are destroyed. But they put new wine into new wineskins and both are preserved.”</span>
</div>

## A Woman Healed and a Daughter Raised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:41–56; Mark 5:22–43; Matthew 8:2"> As he was saying these things to them, behold, one of the rulers came and knelt down before him, saying, “My daughter has just now died, but come, place your hand on her and she will live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Galatians 6:9–10; Matthew 8:7"> And Jesus and his disciples got up and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 8:43–56; Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56"> And behold, a woman who had been suffering with a hemorrhage twelve years approached from behind and touched the edge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “tassel”">j</a></sup>of his cloak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 5:26–33; Luke 8:45–47; Acts 19:12"> for she said to herself, “If only I touch his cloak I will be healed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 15:28; Matthew 9:2"> But Jesus, turning around and seeing her, said, “Have courage, daughter! Your faith has healed you.” And the woman was healed from that hour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; Mark 5:35–40; Luke 8:49–51"> And when Jesus came into the ruler’s house and saw the flute players and the disorderly crowd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:10; John 11:11–13; Matthew 27:39–43"> he said, “Go away, because the girl is not dead, but is sleeping.” And they ridiculed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 9:27; Mark 5:41; Luke 8:54"> But when the crowd had been sent out, he entered and took her hand, and the girl got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 1:45"> And this report went out into that whole region.</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Mark 10:52; Matthew 8:13"> Then he touched their eyes, saying, “According to your faith let it be done for you.”</span>

<div className="bp">
