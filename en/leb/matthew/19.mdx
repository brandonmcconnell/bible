## On Divorce

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 10:40; Matthew 19:1–9; Matthew 7:28"> And it happened that when Jesus had finished these statements, he went away from Galilee and came into the region of Judea on the other side of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:15; Mark 6:55–56; Matthew 15:30–31"> And large crowds followed him, and he healed them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 10:2; Hebrews 3:9; Matthew 16:1"> And Pharisees came up to him in order to test him, and asked if it was permitted for a man to divorce his wife for any cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:23; Genesis 1:27; Genesis 5:2"> And he answered and said, “Have you not read that the one who created them from the beginning made them male and female</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 2:21–24; 1 Corinthians 7:4; 1 Corinthians 7:2"> and said, ‘On account of this a man will leave his father and his mother and will be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh’?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 2:24">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Mark 10:9; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14"> So then, they are no longer two but one flesh. Therefore what God has joined together, man must not separate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 5:31; Mark 10:4"> They said to him, “Why then did Moses command us to give a document—a certificate of divorce—and to divorce her?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 2:13–14; Genesis 2:24; Mark 10:5"> He said to them, “Moses, with reference to your hardness of heart, permitted you to divorce your wives, but from the beginning it was not like this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:32; Luke 16:18; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> Now I say to you that whoever divorces his wife, except on the basis of sexual immorality, and marries another commits adultery, and whoever marries her who is divorced commits adultery.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “and whoever marries her who is divorced commits adultery”">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32–35; Proverbs 19:13–14; Proverbs 21:19"> The disciples<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “His disciples”">c</a></sup>said to him, “If this is the case of a man with his wife, it would be better not to marry!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 7:35; 1 Corinthians 7:7–9"> But he said to them, “Not everyone can accept this saying but those to whom it has been given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32–38; 1 Corinthians 9:5; Isaiah 56:3–4"> For there are eunuchs who were born as such from their mother’s womb, and there are eunuchs who were made eunuchs by people, and there are eunuchs who have made themselves eunuchs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="An understood repetition of the term from earlier in the verse">d</a></sup>for the sake of the kingdom of heaven. The one who is able to accept this, let him accept it.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 10:13–16; Luke 18:15–17; Matthew 18:2–5"> Then children were brought to him so that he could lay his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">e</a></sup>hands on them and pray, but the disciples rebuked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 18:3; Luke 18:16–17; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> But Jesus said, “Allow the children, and do not forbid them to come to me, ⸤for to such belongs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “for of such is”">f</a></sup>the kingdom of heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 10:16; 2 Timothy 3:15; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> And he laid his hands on them and traveled on from there.</span>

## A Rich Young Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 10:17–30; John 3:15; Jude 1:21"> And behold, someone came up to him and said, “Teacher, what good thing must I do so that I will have eternal life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Luke 10:26–28; Galatians 3:11–13"> And he said to him, “Why are you asking me about what is good? There is one who is good. But if you want to enter into life, keep the commandments!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Exodus 20:12–17; Mark 10:19"> He said to him, “Which ones?” And Jesus said, “Do not commit murder, do not commit adultery, do not steal, do not give false testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 20:12; Matthew 22:39"> honor your father and your mother, and love your neighbor as yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Mark 10:20–21; Philippians 3:6"> The young man said to him, “All these I have observed. What do I still lack?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 10:21; Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33"> Jesus said to him, “If you want to be perfect, go, sell your possessions and give the proceeds to the poor—and you will have treasure in heaven—and come, follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 13:22"> But when the young man heard the statement, he went away sorrowful, because he was one who had many possessions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Matthew 13:22; Mark 10:23"> And Jesus said to his disciples, “Truly I say to you that with difficulty a rich person will enter into the kingdom of heaven!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:44; Luke 18:25; Mark 10:24–25"> And again I say to you, it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than a rich person into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “to enter into”">g</a></sup>the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 10:13; Matthew 24:22; Romans 11:5–7"> So when the disciples heard this, they were extremely amazed, saying, “Then who can be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Luke 1:37; Job 42:2"> But Jesus looked at them and said to them, “With human beings this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 3:8; Luke 18:28; Luke 5:11"> Then Peter answered and said to him, “Behold, we have left everything and followed you. What then will there be for us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:28–30; 1 Corinthians 6:2–3; Matthew 25:31"> And Jesus said to them, “Truly I say to you that in the renewal of the world,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The words “of the world” are supplied as a clarification of “renewal”">h</a></sup>when the Son of Man sits on his glorious throne, you who have followed me—you also will sit on twelve thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:29–30; Mark 10:29–30; Philippians 3:8"> And everyone who has left houses or brothers or sisters or father or mother or wife<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “or wife”">i</a></sup>or children or fields on account of my name will receive a hundred times as much, and will inherit eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 20:16; Luke 13:30; Mark 10:31"> But many who are first will be last, and the last first.</span>
</div>
</div>
