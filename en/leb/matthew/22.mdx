## The Parable of the Wedding Celebration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 20:1–16; Matthew 12:43–45; Luke 14:16"> And continuing, Jesus spoke to them again in parables, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 19:7–9; Matthew 13:24; Ephesians 5:24–32"> “The kingdom of heaven may be compared to a man—a king—who gave a wedding celebration for his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; John 5:40; Luke 14:15–17"> And he sent his slaves to summon those who had been invited to the wedding celebration, and they did not want to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 13:46; Matthew 21:36; John 6:50–57"> Again he sent other slaves, saying, ‘Tell those who have been invited, “Behold, I have prepared my dinner; my oxen and fattened cattle have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding celebration!” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 2:3; Luke 14:18–20; 1 John 2:15–16"> But they paid no attention and went away—this one to his own field, that one to his business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 15:19–20; John 16:2–3; Acts 7:51–57"> And the others, seizing his slaves, mistreated them and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 19:27; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Luke 21:24"> And the king was angry and sent his troops and destroyed those murderers and burned their city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:5; Acts 13:46; Revelation 3:4"> Then he said to his slaves, ‘The wedding celebration is ready, but those who had been invited were not worthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 14:21–24; Proverbs 1:20–23; Mark 16:15–16"> Therefore, go out to the places where the roads exit the city and invite to the wedding celebration as many people as you find.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Revelation 19:6–9; 2 Corinthians 12:21"> And those slaves went out into the roads and gathered everyone whom they found, both evil and good, and the wedding celebration was filled ⸤with dinner guests⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “with those reclining at table”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But when the king came in to see the ⸤dinner guests⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “ones reclining at table”">b</a></sup>he saw a man there not dressed in wedding clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:9; Job 5:16; Matthew 20:13"> And he said to him, ‘Friend, how did you come in here, not having wedding clothes?’ But ⸤he could say nothing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “he was silent”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Matthew 25:30; Matthew 24:51"> Then the king said to the servants, ‘Tie him up ⸤hand and foot⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “feet and hands”">d</a></sup>and throw him into the outer darkness. In that place there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Luke 13:23–24; Matthew 7:13–14"> For many are called but few are chosen.”</span>
</div>

## Paying Taxes to Caesar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Mark 12:13–17; Psalm 2:2"> Then the Pharisees went ⸤and consulted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “took counsel”">e</a></sup>so that they could entrap him with a statement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 3:6; Mark 8:15; Mark 12:14"> And they sent their disciples to him with the Herodians, saying, “Teacher, we know that you are truthful and teach the way of God in truth, and ⸤you do not care what anyone thinks⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “it is not a care to you concerning anyone”">f</a></sup>⸤because you do not regard the opinion of people⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “because you do not look at the face of men”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 2:1; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 17:25"> Therefore tell us what you think. Is it permitted to pay taxes to Caesar or not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:6; Luke 5:22; John 2:25"> But because he knew their maliciousness, Jesus said, “Hypocrites! Why are you testing me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Revelation 6:6; Matthew 18:28"> Show me the coin for the tax!” So they brought him a denarius.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 20:24"> And he said to them, “Whose image and inscription is this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:7; Proverbs 24:21; Acts 4:19"> They said to him, “Caesar’s.” Then he said to them, “Therefore give to Caesar the things of Caesar, and to God the things of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 12:12; Matthew 22:46; Luke 20:25–26"> And when they heard this, they were astonished, and they left him and went away.</span>
</div>

## A Question About Marriage and the Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Mark 12:18–27; Acts 23:6–8"> On that day Sadducees—who say there is no resurrection—came up to him and asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Luke 20:28; Ruth 1:11"> saying, “Teacher, Moses said if someone dies without having children, his brother is to marry his wife and ⸤father⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “raise up”">h</a></sup>descendants for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:29–33; Mark 12:19–23; Hebrews 9:27"> Now there were seven brothers with us. And the first died after getting married, and because he did not have descendants, he left his wife to his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So also the second and the third, up to the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And last of all the woman died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> In the resurrection, therefore, whose wife of the seven will she be? For they all had her as wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 20:9; Romans 15:4; Luke 1:37"> But Jesus answered and said to them, “You are mistaken, because you do not know the scriptures or the power of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 20:34–36; Mark 12:24–25; Matthew 24:38"> For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels of God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “of God”">i</a></sup>in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 12:3; Matthew 21:16; Matthew 21:42"> Now concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read what was spoken to you by God, who said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Mark 12:26–27; Luke 20:37–38"> “I am the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob”?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 3:6">j</a></sup>He is not the God of the dead, but of the living!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 12:17; Mark 6:2; John 7:46"> And when the crowds heard this, they were amazed at his teaching.</span>

## The Greatest Commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 12:28–31; Matthew 25:3–5; Matthew 12:14"> Now when the Pharisees heard that he had silenced the Sadducees, they assembled at the same place.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “they assembled together”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 7:30; Luke 10:25–37; Luke 11:52"> And one of them, a legal expert, put a question to him to test him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 12:28–33; Matthew 5:19–20; Luke 11:42"> “Teacher, which commandment is greatest in the law?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 12:29–30; Luke 10:27; Deuteronomy 6:5"> And he said to him, “ ‘You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 6:5">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> This is the greatest and first commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 12:31; Luke 10:27–37; Leviticus 19:18"> And the second is like it: ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 19:18">m</a></sup></span>
