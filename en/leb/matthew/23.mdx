## Seven Woes Pronounced on the Scribes and Pharisees

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Luke 20:45–46; Matthew 15:10–20"> Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and to his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 20:46; Mark 12:38; Malachi 2:7"> saying, “The scribes and the Pharisees sit on the seat of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Titus 1:16; Romans 2:19–24"> Therefore do and observe everything that they tell you, but do not do as ⸤they do⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “their deeds”">a</a></sup>for they tell others to do something and do not do it themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:46; Acts 15:10; Galatians 6:13"> And they tie up heavy burdens<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “burdens that are heavy and hard to bear”">b</a></sup>and put them on people’s shoulders, but they themselves are not willing with their finger to move them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 6:1–16; John 12:43; Philippians 2:3"> And they do all their deeds in order to be seen by people, for they make their phylacteries broad and make their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">c</a></sup>tassels long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:9; Luke 11:43–54; Mark 12:38–39"> And they love the place of honor at banquets and the best seats in the synagogues</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 1:38; John 1:49; John 3:26"> and the greetings in the marketplaces and to be called ‘Rabbi’ by people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 1:12–13; 1 Peter 5:3"> But you are not to be called ‘Rabbi,’ because one is your teacher, and you are all brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:1; Hebrews 12:9; Malachi 1:6"> And do not call anyone your father on earth, for one is your heavenly Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And do not be called teachers, because one is your teacher, the Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Galatians 5:13; John 13:14–15"> And the greatest among you will be your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; James 4:6; Proverbs 29:23"> And whoever exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 11:52; Matthew 23:29; Matthew 23:23"> “But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees—hypocrites!—because you shut the kingdom of heaven before people! For you do not enter, nor permit those wanting to go in to enter.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The most important Greek manuscripts omit v. 14 , “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees—hypocrites!—because you devour widows’ houses and for show you pray long prayers! Therefore you will receive the greater condemnation.”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Acts 13:10; Acts 13:43"> “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees—hypocrites!—because you travel around the sea and the dry land to make one convert, and when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a son of hell as you are!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; Matthew 5:33–35; James 5:12"> “Woe to you, blind guides, who say, ‘Whoever swears by the temple, it is nothing! But whoever swears by the gold of the temple is bound by his oath.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The phrase “by his oath ” is not in the Greek text but is implied">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 23:19; Numbers 16:38–39; Psalm 94:8"> Fools and blind people! For which is greater, the gold or the temple that makes the gold holy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And, ‘Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing! But whoever swears by the gift that is on it is bound by his oath.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The phrase “by his oath ” is not in the Greek text but is implied">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Exodus 30:29"> Blind people! For which is greater, the gift or the altar that makes the gift holy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore the one who swears by the altar swears by it and by everything that is on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 26:8; 1 Kings 8:13; 2 Chronicles 6:2"> And the one who swears by the temple swears by it and by the one who dwells in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:34; Psalm 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> And the one who swears by heaven swears by the throne of God and by the one who sits on it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Micah 6:8; Hosea 6:6"> “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees—hypocrites!—because you pay a tenth of mint and dill and cumin, and neglect the more important matters of the law—justice and mercy and faithfulness! It was necessary<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “But it was necessary”">g</a></sup>to do these things ⸤while not neglecting those⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “and those not to neglect”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:24; Matthew 27:6–8; Matthew 15:2–6"> Blind guides who filter out a gnat and swallow a camel!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:39–40; Matthew 15:19–20; Mark 7:4–13"> “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees—hypocrites!—because you cleanse the outside of the cup and the dish, but inside they are full of greed and self-indulgence!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Hebrews 10:22"> Blind Pharisee! First clean the inside of the cup and the dish,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “and the dish”">i</a></sup>so that the outside of it may become clean also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 11:44; Acts 23:3; Numbers 19:16"> “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees—hypocrites!—because you are like whitewashed tombs which on the outside appear beautiful, but on the inside are full of the bones of the dead and of everything unclean!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Psalm 51:6; Matthew 23:5"> In the same way, on the outside you also appear righteous to people, but inside you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 11:47–48; Acts 2:29"> “Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees—hypocrites!—because you build the tombs of the prophets and decorate the graves of the righteous,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Matthew 23:34–35; Matthew 21:35–36"> and you say, ‘If we had lived in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partners with them in the blood of the prophets!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 7:51–52; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Joshua 24:22"> Thus you testify against yourselves that you are descendants of those who murdered the prophets!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Numbers 32:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:16"> And you—fill up the measure of your fathers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 5:22"> Serpents! Offspring of vipers! How will you escape from the condemnation to hell?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Matthew 13:52; Colossians 1:28"> For this reason, behold, I am sending to you prophets and wise men and scribes. Some of them you will kill and crucify, and some of them you will flog in your synagogues and will pursue from town to town,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20–22; Zechariah 1:1; Hebrews 11:4"> so that upon you will come all the righteous blood shed on the earth from the blood of righteous Abel up to the blood of Zechariah son of Barachiah, whom you murdered between the temple and the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 24:34; Mark 13:30–31"> Truly I say to you, all these things will come upon this generation!</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lament over Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Ruth 2:12; Psalm 91:4"> “Jerusalem, Jerusalem, who kills the prophets and stones those who are sent to her! How many times I wanted to gather your children together ⸤the way⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “in the manner in which”">j</a></sup>a hen gathers her young together under her wings, and you were not willing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:5; Luke 13:35; Luke 21:24"> Behold, your house has been left to you desolate!</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalm 118:26; Matthew 21:9; Romans 11:25"> For I tell you, you will never see me from now on until you say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Blessed is the one who comes in the name of the Lord!’ ”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 118:26">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
