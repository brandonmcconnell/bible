## The Parable of the Workers in the Vineyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 13:47; Matthew 13:24; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> “For the kingdom of heaven is like a man—the master of the house—who went out early in the morning to hire workers for his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:26; Ecclesiastes 12:1; Matthew 22:19"> And after coming to an agreement with the workers for a denarius per day, he sent them into his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; Acts 2:15; Matthew 20:6–7"> And going out about the third hour, he saw others standing idle in the marketplace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:11; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Luke 19:7–10"> And to those people he said, ‘You also go into the vineyard, and I will give you whatever is right.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24–26; Acts 10:3; Mark 15:33–34"> So they went. Going out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “And going out”">a</a></sup>again about the sixth and ninth hour he did the same thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; John 9:4; Acts 17:21"> And about the eleventh hour he went out and found others standing there and said to them, ‘Why are you standing here the whole day unemployed?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–12; Hebrews 6:10; John 9:4"> They said to him, ‘Because no one hired us.’ He said to them, ‘You go also into the vineyard.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:15; 1 Peter 4:10; Romans 2:6–10"> And when evening came, the owner of the vineyard said to his manager, ‘Call the workers and pay them their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">b</a></sup>wages, beginning from the last up to the first.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 20:6–7; Luke 23:40–43; Matthew 20:2"> And when the ones hired about the eleventh hour came, they received a denarius apiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And when the first came, they thought that they would receive more, and they also received a denarius apiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Luke 19:7; Acts 22:21–22"> And when they received it, they began to complain<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive here (“began to complain”)">c</a></sup>against the master of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:5–6; 2 Corinthians 11:23–28; Luke 12:55"> saying, ‘These last people worked one hour and you made them equal to us who have endured the burden of the day and the burning heat!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:50; Matthew 22:12; Romans 9:14–15"> But he answered one of them and said, ‘Friend, I am not doing you wrong. Did you not come to an agreement with me for a denarius?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:2; Luke 15:31; Matthew 6:6"> Take what is yours and go! But I want to give to this last person the same as I gave<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The words “I gave ” are an implied repetition from the verb earlier in the verse">d</a></sup>to you also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:15–24; Deuteronomy 15:9; Jonah 4:1–4"> Is it not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Or is it not”">e</a></sup>permitted for me to do whatever I want with what is mine? Or is your eye evil because I am generous?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Mark 10:31; Matthew 22:14"> Thus the last will be first and the first last.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Predicts His Death and Resurrection a Third Time

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 10:32–34; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 16:13"> And as Jesus was going up to Jerusalem, he took the twelve disciples by themselves and said to them on the way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Acts 2:23; Matthew 27:1"> “Behold, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be handed over to the chief priests and scribes, and they will condemn him to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–31"> and will hand him over to the Gentiles to mock him and flog him and crucify him, and on the third day he will be raised.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Request by the Mother of James and John

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 8:2"> Then the mother of the sons of Zebedee came up to him with her sons, and kneeling down she asked something from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 10:36–37; Esther 5:3"> And he said to her, “What do you want?” She said to him, “Say that these two sons of mine may sit one at your right hand and one at your left in your kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 26:42; Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:39"> But Jesus answered and said, “You do not know what you are asking! Are you able to drink the cup that I am about to drink?” They said to him, “We are able.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:9; Matthew 25:34; Acts 12:2"> He said to them, “You will indeed drink my cup, but to sit at my right hand and at my left is not mine<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “this is not mine”">f</a></sup>to grant, but is for those for whom it has been prepared by my Father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Philippians 2:3; Mark 10:41"> And when the ten heard this, they were indignant concerning the two brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:25–27; Matthew 20:25–28; John 13:12–17"> But Jesus called them to himself and said, “You know that the rulers of the Gentiles lord it over them, and those in high positions exercise authority over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 10:43; Mark 9:35; Mark 10:45"> It will not be like this among you! But whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:26; Mark 9:33–35; Matthew 18:4"> and whoever wants to be most prominent among you must be your slave—</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Hebrews 9:28; Titus 2:14"> just as the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Two Blind Men Healed at Jericho

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:46–52; Luke 18:35–43; Matthew 9:27–31"> And as they were going out of Jericho, a large crowd followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 10:46; Isaiah 42:16; Matthew 21:9"> And behold, there were two blind men sitting beside the road. When they heard that Jesus was passing by, they called out, saying, “Lord, have mercy on us,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Have mercy on us, Lord”">g</a></sup>Son of David!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:13; Colossians 4:2; Matthew 7:7–8"> And the crowd rebuked them so that they would be quiet. But they called out all the more, saying, “Lord, have mercy on us,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Have mercy on us, Lord”">h</a></sup>Son of David!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:37; Philippians 4:6; Matthew 20:21"> And Jesus stopped, called them, and said, “What do you want me to do for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 119:18; Ephesians 1:17–19"> They said to him, “Lord, that our eyes be opened!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15–16; Matthew 9:29; Acts 26:18"> And having compassion, Jesus touched their eyes, and immediately they received their sight and followed him.</span>
</div>
