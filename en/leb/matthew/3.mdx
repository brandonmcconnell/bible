## John the Baptist Begins His Ministry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–6; John 1:6–8; Luke 1:76"> Now in those days John the Baptist came preaching in the Judean wilderness</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:17; Mark 1:15; Daniel 2:44"> and saying, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven has come near!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 3:1; John 1:23"> For this is the one who was spoken about by the prophet Isaiah, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The voice of one crying out in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Prepare the way of the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">make his paths straight.’ ”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 40:3">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4; Mark 1:6"> Now John himself had his clothing made from camel’s hair and a belt made of leather around his waist, and his food was locusts and wild honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Luke 3:7; John 3:23"> Then Jerusalem and all Judea and all the district around the Jordan were going out to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:13–16; Acts 2:38–41"> and they were being baptized by him in the Jordan River as they confessed their sins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Romans 5:9"> But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said to them, “Offspring of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 3:8; Acts 26:20; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> Therefore produce fruit worthy of repentance!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:8; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:27–29"> And do not think to say to yourselves, ‘We have Abraham as father.’ For I say to you that God is able to raise up children for Abraham from these stones!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:6; Luke 3:9; Matthew 7:19"> Already now the ax is positioned at the root of the trees; therefore every tree not producing good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 4:4; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> I baptize you with water for repentance, but the one who comes after me is more powerful than I am, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 13:30; Luke 3:17"> His winnowing shovel is in his hand, and he will clean out his threshing floor and will gather his wheat into the storehouse, but he will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Baptism of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:32–34; Matthew 2:22; Mark 1:9–11"> Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan to John in order to be baptized by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 3:3–7; Acts 1:5–8; John 13:6–8"> But John tried to prevent him, saying, “⸤I need⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “I have need”">b</a></sup>to be baptized by you, and do you come to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:34; John 15:10; Psalm 40:7–8"> But Jesus answered and said to him, “Permit it now, for in this way it is right for us to fulfill all righteousness.” Then he permitted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; Mark 1:10; Luke 3:21–22"> Now after he was baptized, Jesus immediately went up from the water, and behold, the heavens opened<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “opened to him”">c</a></sup>and he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove coming<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and coming”">d</a></sup>upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Psalm 2:7; Matthew 17:5"> And behold, there was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The words “there was ” are not in the Greek text, but are supplied to make a complete sentence in English">e</a></sup>a voice from heaven saying, “This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased.”</span>
</div>
