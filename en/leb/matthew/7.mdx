## The Sermon on the Mount: On Judging Others

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 6:37; Romans 2:1–2; James 4:11–12"> “Do not judge, so that you will not be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 2:13; Mark 4:24; Luke 6:38"> For by what judgment you judge, you will be judged, and by what measure you measure out, it will be measured out to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 8:7–9; Galatians 6:1; Luke 6:41–42"> And why do you see the speck that is in your brother’s eye, but do not notice the beam of wood in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Or how will you say to your brother, ‘Allow me to remove the speck from your eye,’ and behold, the beam of wood is in your own eye?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:42; Psalm 51:9–13; Matthew 23:13–28"> Hypocrite! First remove the beam of wood from your own eye and then you will see clearly to remove the speck from your brother’s eye!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 9:7–8; Proverbs 23:9; Matthew 10:14–15"> “Do not give what is holy to dogs, or throw your pearls in front of pigs, lest they trample them with their feet, and turn around and tear you to pieces.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sermon on the Mount: Ask, Seek, Knock

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 3:22; John 15:7"> “Ask and it will be given to you; seek and you will find; knock and it will be opened for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 81:10; Psalm 81:16; Luke 23:42–43"> For everyone who asks receives, and the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks it will be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13"> Or what man is there among you, if his son will ask him for bread, will give him a stone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Or also if he will ask for a fish, will give him a snake?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 11:11–13; James 1:17; Romans 8:32"> Therefore if you, although you are evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father who is in heaven give good things to those who ask him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:31; Romans 13:8–10; Galatians 5:13–14"> Therefore in all things, whatever you want that people should do to you, thus also you do to them. For this is the law and the prophets.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sermon on the Mount: The Narrow Gate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 13:24–25; Proverbs 16:25; John 14:6"> “Enter through the narrow gate, because broad is the gate and spacious is the road that leads to destruction, and there are many who enter through it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 13:23–30; Matthew 22:14; Matthew 16:24–25"> because narrow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “how narrow”">a</a></sup>is the gate and constricted is the road that leads to life, and there are few who find it!</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sermon on the Mount: Recognizing False Prophets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 4:1; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Matthew 24:11"> “Beware of false prophets who come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inside are ravenous wolves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:43–45; Matthew 12:33; James 3:12"> You will recognize them by their fruits: they do not gather grapes from thorn bushes or figs from thistles, do they?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The negative construction in Greek anticipates a negative answer here, indicated by “do they ”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 12:33–35; Galatians 5:22–24; Ephesians 5:9"> In the same way, every good tree produces good fruit, but a bad tree produces bad fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; 1 John 3:9–10"> A good tree is not able to produce bad fruit, nor a bad tree to produce good fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Hebrews 6:8; John 15:2–6"> Every tree that does not produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:16; Acts 5:38"> As a result, you will recognize them by their fruits.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sermon on the Mount: False Followers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46; James 1:22"> “Not everyone who says to me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ will enter into the kingdom of heaven, but the one who does the will of my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:25–27; 1 Corinthians 13:1–2; Malachi 3:17–18"> On that day many will say to me, ‘Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name, and expel demons in your name, and perform many miracles in your name?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Psalm 6:8; Luke 13:25"> And then ⸤I will say to them plainly⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “I will declare to them”">c</a></sup>‘I never knew you. Depart from me, you who practice lawlessness!’</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sermon on the Mount: Two Houses and Two Foundations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:47–49; John 13:17; 1 Corinthians 3:10–11"> “Therefore everyone who hears these words of mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 1:12; Psalm 125:1–2; 1 Peter 1:7"> And the rain came down and the rivers came and the winds blew and beat against that house, and it did not collapse, because its foundation was laid on the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:9; Luke 6:49; James 2:20"> And everyone who hears these words of mine and does not do them will be like a foolish man who built his house on the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 13:19–22; 2 Peter 2:20–22; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> And the rain came down and the rivers came and the winds blew and beat against that house, and it collapsed, and its fall was great.”</span>
</div>
</div>
