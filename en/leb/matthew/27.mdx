## Pilate Releases Barabbas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:38–40; Luke 23:16; John 19:16"> Now at each feast, the governor was accustomed to release one prisoner to the crowd—the one whom they wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 15:7; Acts 3:14; Luke 23:25"> And at that time they had a notorious prisoner named Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Although many manuscripts omit “Jesus” here, it is so hard to explain why a scribe would have added it that the reading is probably original">e</a></sup>Barabbas.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="“Barabbas” means “son of the father” in Aramaic">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:15; 1 Kings 18:21; Mark 15:9–12"> So after they had assembled, Pilate said to them, “Whom do you want me to release for you—Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Although many manuscripts omit “Jesus” here, it is so hard to explain why a scribe would have added it that the reading is probably original">g</a></sup>Barabbas or Jesus who is called Christ?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Acts 5:17; Mark 15:10"> (For he knew that they had handed him over because of envy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Proverbs 29:1; Matthew 27:24"> And while he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent a message to him, saying, “⸤Have nothing to do with that righteous man⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “nothing to you and to that righteous man”">h</a></sup>for I have suffered much as a result of a dream today because of him.”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:14–15; John 19:15–16; John 18:40"> But the chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowds that they should ask for Barabbas and put Jesus to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So the governor answered and said to them, “Which of the two do you want me to release for you?” And they said, “Barabbas!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–3; Isaiah 49:7; Acts 13:38"> Pilate said to them, “What then should I do with Jesus, the one who is called Christ?” They all said, “Let him be crucified!”</span>

</div>

You see to it!”</span>

<div className="bp">
