## The Abomination of Desolation

<div className="bt">

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Mark 13:15–16; Matthew 10:27"> The one who is on his housetop must not come down to take things out of his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the one who is in the field must not turn back to pick up his cloak.</span>

## The Unknown Day and Hour

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 13:32; Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:44"> “But concerning that day and hour no one knows—not even the angels of heaven nor the Son—except the Father alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:1–7; 2 Peter 2:5"> For just as the days of Noah were, so the coming of the Son of Man will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 17:26–28; Romans 13:13–14; Luke 14:18–20"> For as in the days<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “those days”">l</a></sup>before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day Noah entered into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:20; Matthew 24:37; Acts 13:41"> And they did not know anything until the deluge came and swept them all away. So also the coming of the Son of Man will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 17:34–37; 2 Peter 2:7–9; 2 Peter 2:5"> Then there will be two men in the field; one will be taken and one left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Isaiah 47:2; Luke 17:35"> Two women will be grinding at the mill; one will be taken and one left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 21:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:6; Matthew 25:13"> Therefore be on the alert, because you do not know what day your Lord is coming!</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 12:39; Matthew 24:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–6"> But understand this: that if the master of the house had known what watch of the night the thief was coming, he would have stayed awake and would not have let his house be broken into.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 25:10; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 25:13"> For this reason you also must be ready, because the Son of Man is coming at an hour that you do not think he will come.</span>
</div>

## A Faithful Slave and an Unfaithful Slave

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Revelation 16:15; Luke 12:43; Luke 12:37"> Blessed is that slave whom his master will find so doing when he comes back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 25:23; Matthew 25:21; Luke 12:37"> Truly I say to you that he will put him in charge of all his possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 18:32; Isaiah 32:6; Luke 12:45"> But if that evil slave should say ⸤to himself⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “in his heart”">m</a></sup>‘My master is staying away for a long time,’</span>
