<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Psalm 55:20–21; Psalm 28:3; 2 Samuel 3:27"> Now the one who was betraying him had given them a sign, saying, “The one whom I kiss—he is the one. Arrest him!”</span>
