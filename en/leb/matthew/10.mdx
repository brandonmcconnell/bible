## The Twelve Commissioned and Sent Out

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:1–6; Mark 3:13–15; Mark 6:7–13"> And summoning his twelve disciples, he gave them authority over unclean spirits, so that they could expel them and could heal every<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “every kind of”">a</a></sup>disease and every<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “every kind of”">b</a></sup>sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21; Acts 1:13; Luke 5:10"> Now these are the names of the twelve apostles: first Simon who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother, James<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and James”">c</a></sup>the son of Zebedee, and John his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14"> Philip, and Bartholomew, Thomas, and Matthew the tax collector, James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:14; Matthew 26:47; John 13:2"> Simon the Zealot,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the Cananean,” but according to BDAG 507 s.v., this term has no relation at all to the geographical terms for Cana or Canaan, but is derived from the Aramaic term for “enthusiast, zealot” (see Luke 6:15 ; Acts 1:13 )">d</a></sup>and Judas Iscariot—the one who also betrayed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 4:9; Acts 22:21–23; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> Jesus sent out these twelve, instructing them saying, “Do not go on the road to the Gentiles, and do not enter into a city of the Samaritans,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Isaiah 53:6"> but go instead to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Matthew 3:2; Luke 9:60"> And as you are going, preach, saying, ‘The kingdom of heaven has come near!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 3:6; Luke 10:9; Acts 4:30"> Heal those who are sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, expel demons. Freely you have received; freely give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:35; Luke 9:3–5; Luke 10:4–12"> Do not procure gold or silver or copper for your belts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–12; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> Do not take a traveler’s bag for the road, or two tunics, or sandals, or a staff, for the worker is deserving of his provisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Job 31:32; Judges 19:16–21"> And into whatever town or village you enter, inquire who in it is worthy, and stay there until you depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:6; Luke 10:5–6; Acts 10:36"> And when you enter into the house, greet it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 10:6; Psalm 35:13; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> And if the house is worthy, let your peace come upon it, but if it is not worthy, let your peace return to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 10:10–11; Acts 18:6; Nehemiah 5:13"> And whoever does not welcome you or listen to your words, shake off the dust from your feet as you are going out of that house or that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A repetition of “that” is supplied in English; the single Greek term is understood to modify both “house” and “town”">e</a></sup>town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; 1 John 4:17; John 15:22–24"> Truly I say to you, it will be more bearable for the region of Sodom and Gomorrah on the day of judgment than for that town!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Persecution of Disciples Predicted

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:3; Philippians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> “Behold, I am sending you out like sheep in the midst of wolves. Therefore be wise as serpents and innocent as doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Matthew 23:34; Acts 26:11"> But beware of people, because they will hand you over to councils, and they will flog you in their synagogues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:25–27; Matthew 8:4; Psalm 2:1–6"> And you will be brought before both governors and kings because of me, for a witness to them and to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 4:12; Mark 13:11–13; 2 Timothy 4:17"> But whenever they hand you over, do not be anxious how to speak or what you should say, for what you should say will be given to you at that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; Luke 12:12"> For you are not the ones who are speaking, but the Spirit of your Father who is speaking through you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:16–17; Micah 7:5–6; Mark 13:12–13"> “And brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his children, and children will rise up against parents and have them put to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:13; James 1:12; Revelation 2:10"> and you will be hated by everyone because of my name. But the one who endures to the end—this one will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:28; Acts 8:1; Mark 13:26"> And whenever they persecute you in this town, flee to another, for truly I say to you, you will never finish going through the towns of Israel until the Son of Man comes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 15:20; Luke 6:40; John 13:16"> “A disciple is not superior to his teacher, nor a slave superior to his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Matthew 12:24; Matthew 9:34"> It is enough for the disciple that he become like his teacher, and the slave like his master. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebul, how much more the members of his household?</span>
</div>
</div>

## Fear God Rather Than People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Mark 4:22; Jeremiah 1:8"> “Therefore do not be afraid of them, because nothing is hidden that will not be revealed, and nothing secret that will not become known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 5:20; John 16:13; John 16:1"> What I say to you in the dark, tell in the light, and what you hear in your ear, proclaim on the housetops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:4–5; Isaiah 8:12–13; Revelation 2:10"> And do not be afraid of those who kill the body but are not able to kill the soul, but instead be afraid of the one who is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 12:6–7; Psalm 104:27–30"> Are not two sparrows sold for a penny?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally, “an assarion,” a Roman coin worth about 1/16 of a denarius">f</a></sup>And one of them will not fall to the ground ⸤without the knowledge and consent⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “without”; the phrase “the knowledge and consent” is implied when this term is used of God">g</a></sup>of your Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 12:7; 1 Samuel 14:45; Acts 27:34"> And even the hairs of your head are all numbered!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Psalm 8:5; Matthew 6:26"> Therefore do not be afraid; you are worth more than many sparrows.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 3:5; Romans 10:9–10; Luke 12:8–9"> “Therefore everyone who acknowledges me before people, I also will acknowledge him before my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:12; Luke 9:26; Luke 12:9"> But whoever denies me before people, I also will deny him before my Father who is in heaven.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Not Peace, But a Sword of Divisiveness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 12:49–53; Acts 14:4; Acts 13:45–50"> “Do not think that I have come to bring peace on the earth! I have not come to bring peace, but a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Matthew 10:21; Luke 21:16"> For I have come to turn a man against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Micah 7:6; Jeremiah 12:6; Psalm 41:9"> And the enemies of a man will be the members of his household.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="An allusion to Mic 7:6">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 14:26; Matthew 22:37; Philippians 3:7–9"> The one who loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me, and the one who loves son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 14:27; Mark 8:34; Matthew 16:24"> And whoever does not take up his cross and follow me is not worthy of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 12:25; Mark 8:35–36; Luke 17:33"> The one who finds his life will lose it, and the one who loses his life because of me will find it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## On Rewards

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:20; Luke 9:48; Luke 10:16"> “The one who receives you receives me, and the one who receives me receives the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="3 John 1:5–8; Hebrews 6:10; 2 Kings 4:16–17"> The one who receives a prophet in the name of a prophet will receive a prophet’s reward, and the one who receives a righteous person in the name of a righteous person will receive a righteous person’s reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10; Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:41–42"> And whoever gives one of these little ones only a cup of cold water to drink in the name of a disciple, truly I say to you, he will never lose his reward.”</span>
</div>
