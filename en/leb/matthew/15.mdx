## Human Traditions and God’s Commandments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Matthew 23:2; Luke 5:21"> Then Pharisees and scribes came to Jesus from Jerusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 11:38; Mark 7:2; Colossians 2:20–23"> “Why do your disciples break the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands when they eat ⸤a meal⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “bread”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:23; Titus 1:14; Colossians 2:8"> So he answered and said to them, “Why do you also break the commandment of God because of your tradition?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Leviticus 20:9; Exodus 21:17"> For God said, ‘Honor your father and your mother,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 20:12 ; Deut 5:16">b</a></sup>and ‘The one who speaks evil of father or mother ⸤must certainly die⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “let him die the death”">c</a></sup>.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 21:17 ; Lev 20:9">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Acts 5:29; Matthew 23:16–18"> But you say, ‘Whoever says to his father or his mother, “Whatever ⸤benefit you would have received⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “you would have been benefited”">e</a></sup>from me is a gift to God,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Jeremiah 8:8; Mark 7:13"> need not honor his father,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Most later manuscripts add “or his mother”">f</a></sup>and you make void the word of God for the sake of your tradition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 7:6; Matthew 23:23–29; Acts 28:25–27"> Hypocrites! Isaiah correctly prophesied about you saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Ezekiel 33:31; Acts 8:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘This people honors me with their lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but their heart is far, far away from me,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; Mark 7:7; 1 Timothy 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">and they worship me in vain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">teaching as doctrines the commandments of men.’ ”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 29:13">g</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Defilement from Within

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 7:14–15; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 24:45"> And summoning the crowd, he said to them, “Hear and understand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Matthew 15:18–20; James 3:5–8"> It is not what goes into the mouth that defiles a person, but what comes out of the mouth—this defiles a person.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:13–14; 1 Corinthians 10:32–33; Galatians 2:5"> Then the disciples came and said to him, “Do you know that the Pharisees were offended when they heard this saying?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:2; Isaiah 60:21; John 15:6"> And he answered and said, “Every plant that my heavenly Father did not plant will be uprooted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:39; Isaiah 56:10; Matthew 23:16–24"> Let<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “Depart from”">h</a></sup>them! They are blind guides of the blind. And if the blind guide the blind, both will fall into a pit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:36; Mark 4:34; John 16:29"> But Peter answered and said to him, “Explain this parable to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:9; Mark 7:18; Mark 9:32"> But he said, “Are you also still without understanding?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:45; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Matthew 7:19–20"> Do you not understand that everything that enters into the mouth goes into the stomach and is evacuated into the latrine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34; James 3:6–10"> But the things that come out of the mouth come from the heart, and these defile the person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 7:21–23; Galatians 5:19–21; Jeremiah 17:9"> For from the heart come evil plans, murder, adultery, sexual immorality, theft, false witness, abusive speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:3–4; Ephesians 5:3–6; 1 Corinthians 6:18–20"> These are the things that defile a person. But eating with unwashed hands does not defile a person!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Canaanite Woman’s Great Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 7:24–30; Matthew 11:21–23; Judges 1:31"> And departing from there, Jesus went away to the region of Tyre and Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Mark 7:25–26; Matthew 17:15"> And behold, a Canaanite woman from that district came and cried out, saying, “Have mercy on me, Lord, Son of David! My daughter is severely possessed by a demon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 14:15; Mark 10:47–48; Lamentations 3:8"> But he did not answer her a word. And his disciples came up and asked him, saying, “Send her away, because she is crying out after us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:5–6; Romans 15:8; Jeremiah 50:6–7"> But he answered and said, “I was not sent except to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 32:26; Matthew 20:31; Matthew 14:33"> But she came and knelt down before him, saying, “Lord, help me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Matthew 7:6; Philippians 3:2"> And he answered and said, “It is not right to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 3:8; Luke 16:21; Job 40:4–5"> So she said, “Yes, Lord, for even the dogs eat the crumbs that fall from their master’s table.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 8:13; Psalm 145:19"> Then Jesus answered and said to her, “O woman, your faith is great! Let it be done for you as you want.” And her daughter was healed from that hour.</span>
</div>

## Many Others Healed in Galilee

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; John 6:1; Matthew 5:1"> And departing from there, Jesus went along the Sea of Galilee, and he went up on the mountain and was sitting there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:17–19; Acts 5:15–16; Matthew 14:35–36"> And large crowds came to him, having with them the mute, blind, lame, crippled,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “the lame, blind, crippled, mute”">i</a></sup>and many others, and they put them down at his feet, and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 18:43; Luke 17:15–18"> So then the crowd was astonished when they saw the mute speaking, the crippled healthy, and the lame walking, and the blind seeing, and they praised the God of Israel.</span>

## The Feeding of Four Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 27:33; Luke 12:29–30; Matthew 9:36"> And Jesus summoned his disciples and said, “I have compassion on the crowd, because they have remained with me three days already and do not have anything to eat, and I do not want to send them away hungry lest they give out on the way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 6:37; Matthew 14:15; Mark 8:4–5"> And the disciples said to him, “Where in this desolate place ⸤can we get⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “for us”">j</a></sup>so much bread that such a great crowd could be satisfied?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 16:9–10; Luke 24:41–42; John 21:9–10"> And Jesus said to them, “How many loaves do you have?” So they said, “Seven, and a few little fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Mark 6:39–40; John 6:10"> And commanding the crowd to recline for a meal on the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 22:19; Romans 14:6; John 6:11"> he took the seven loaves and the fish and after he had given thanks, he broke them and began giving<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive here (“began giving”)">k</a></sup>them to the disciples, and the disciples gave them to the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 8:19–21; Matthew 16:9–10; Matthew 15:33"> And they all ate and were satisfied, and they picked up what was left over of the broken pieces, seven baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Now those who ate were four thousand men, in addition to women and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Mark 8:10; Matthew 14:22"> And after he sent away the crowds, he got into the boat and went to the region of Magadan.</span>
</div>
