## A Centurion’s Slave Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:1–10; Acts 27:43; Acts 10:1–33"> Now when he entered Capernaum, a centurion approached him, appealing to him</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 9:18–19; Luke 7:6; Mark 5:23–24"> And he said to him, “I will come and heal him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 107:20; Luke 7:6–7; Psalm 33:9"> And the centurion answered and said, “Lord, I am not worthy that you should come in under my roof. But only say the word and my slave will be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:35–36; Titus 2:9; Colossians 3:22"> For I also am a man under authority who has soldiers under me, and I say to this one, ‘Go!’ and he goes, and to another one, ‘Come!’ and he comes, and to my slave, ‘Do this!’ and he does it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation (see the parallel in Luke 7:8 ).">c</a></sup>.”</span>

## Demon-possessed Gadarenes Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Mark 5:1–20; Acts 10:38"> And when he came to the other side,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="That is, the other side of the Sea of Galilee">k</a></sup>to the region of the Gadarenes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Many manuscripts read “Gergesenes”; others read “Gerasenes” (see Luke 8:26 )">l</a></sup>two demon-possessed men coming from among the tombs met him, very violent, so that no one was able to pass by along that road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Luke 4:34; James 2:19"> And behold, they cried out, saying, “⸤What do you have to do with us⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “what to us and to you”">m</a></sup>Son of God? Have you come here to torment us before the time?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="That is, before the appointed time of judgment">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:8; Leviticus 11:7; Mark 5:11"> Now a long way from them a large herd of pigs was feeding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 20:1–2; Luke 8:30–33; Mark 5:12"> So the demons implored him, saying, “If you are going to expel us, send us into the herd of pigs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 5:13; Acts 2:23; Revelation 20:7"> And he said to them, “Go!” So they departed and went into the pigs, and behold, the whole herd rushed headlong down the steep slope into the sea and drowned in the water.</span>
