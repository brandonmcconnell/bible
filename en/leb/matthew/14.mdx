## Herod Kills John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Mark 8:15; Luke 9:7–9"> At that time Herod the tetrarch heard the report about Jesus</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; John 10:41; Mark 8:28"> and he said to his servants, “This is John the Baptist! He has been raised from the dead, and for this reason miraculous powers are at work in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 4:12"> For Herod, after arresting John, bound him and put him in prison on account of Herodias, the wife of his brother Philip,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; Mark 6:18"> because John had been saying to him, “It is not permitted for you to have her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 11:9; Mark 6:19–20"> And although he wanted to kill him, he feared the crowd, because they looked upon him as a prophet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 6:21–23; Genesis 40:20; Hosea 1:5–6"> But when Herod’s birthday celebration took place, the daughter of Herodias danced in the midst of them and pleased Herod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6; Esther 7:2"> Therefore he promised with an oath to give her whatever she asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 6:24; Proverbs 29:10; Proverbs 1:16"> And coached by her mother, she said, “Give me the head of John the Baptist here on a platter!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:12–16; 1 Samuel 14:28; Mark 6:26"> And although the king was distressed, because of his oaths and his ⸤dinner guests⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “those reclining at table with”">a</a></sup>he commanded the request to be granted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 17:12; Matthew 21:35–36; Luke 9:9"> And he sent orders and had John beheaded in the prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Genesis 49:7; Ezekiel 16:3–4"> and his head was brought on a platter and given to the girl, and she brought it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:2; Matthew 27:58–61"> And his disciples came and took away the corpse and buried it, and went and told Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Feeding of Five Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:10–17; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 6:30–44"> Now when Jesus heard it, he withdrew from there in a boat to an isolated place by himself. And when the crowds heard it, they followed him by land from the towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17"> And as he got out, he saw the large crowd and had compassion on them and healed their sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 8:3; Mark 6:35–36"> Now when it was evening, the disciples came to him saying, “The place is desolate and the hour ⸤is late⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “has passed away”">b</a></sup>Release the crowds so that they can go away into the villages and purchase food for themselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; 2 Corinthians 8:2–3; Ecclesiastes 11:2"> But Jesus said to them, “They do not ⸤need⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “have need”">c</a></sup>to go away. You give them something to eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Mark 6:37–38; Luke 9:13"> And they said to him, “We do not have anything here except five loaves and two fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> So he said, “Bring them here to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 8:6–7; Luke 24:30; 1 Samuel 9:13"> And he commanded the crowds to recline for a meal on the grass. Taking the five loaves and the two fish and looking up to heaven, he gave thanks. And after breaking them, he gave the loaves to the disciples, and the disciples gave them to the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 16:12; Mark 6:42–44; Matthew 16:8–10"> And they all ate and were satisfied, and they picked up what was left over of the broken pieces, twelve baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 9:8–11; Acts 4:4"> Now those who ate were about five thousand men, in addition to women and children.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Walks on the Water

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45–51; John 6:15–21; Matthew 15:39"> And immediately he made the disciples get into the boat and go ahead of him to the other side, while he sent away the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 6:46; Matthew 26:36"> And after he sent away the crowds, he went up on the mountain by himself to pray. So when evening came, he was there alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:18; Mark 6:48; Matthew 8:24"> But the boat was already many stadia distant from the land, being beaten by the waves, because the wind was against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 9:8; John 6:19; Mark 6:48"> And in the fourth watch of the night he came to them, walking on the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:49–50; Revelation 1:17; Luke 1:11–12"> But the disciples, when they saw him walking on the sea, were terrified, saying, “It is a ghost!” and they cried out in fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Revelation 1:17–18; John 16:33"> But immediately Jesus spoke to them, saying, “Have courage, I am he! Do not be afraid!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:68; Romans 12:3; Matthew 26:33–35"> And Peter answered him and said, “Lord, if ⸤it is you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “you are” he">d</a></sup>command me to come to you on the water!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Mark 9:23; Acts 3:16"> So he said, “Come!” And getting out of the boat, Peter walked on the water and came toward Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Lamentations 3:54–57; 2 Timothy 4:16–17; Mark 14:38"> But when he saw the strong wind, he was afraid. And beginning to sink, he cried out, saying, “Lord, save me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 1:6–8; Psalm 138:7; Genesis 22:14"> And immediately Jesus extended his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">e</a></sup>hand and caught him and said to him, “You of little faith! Why did you doubt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 107:29–30; Mark 4:41; John 6:21"> And when they got into the boat, the wind abated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:27; Psalm 2:7; Romans 1:4"> So those in the boat worshiped him, saying, “Truly you are the Son of God!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Many Healed at Gennesaret

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 6:53–56; Luke 5:1; John 6:24–25"> And after they had crossed over, they came to land at Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Mark 6:55; Mark 1:28–34"> And when the men of that place recognized him, they sent word into that whole surrounding region, and they brought to him all those ⸤who were sick⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “having badly”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Mark 3:10; Exodus 28:33–43"> And they were imploring him that they might only touch the edge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “tassel”">g</a></sup>of his cloak, and all those who touched it were cured.</span>
</div>
