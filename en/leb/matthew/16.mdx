## The Signs of the Times

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:16; Matthew 16:6; Acts 4:1"> And when the Pharisees and Sadducees came to test him, they asked him to show them a sign from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:54–56"> So he answered and said to them, “When evening comes you say, ‘It will be fair weather because the sky is red,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 12:56; 1 Chronicles 12:32; Matthew 11:5"> and early in the morning, ‘Today it will be stormy weather, because the sky is red and darkening.’ You know how to evaluate correctly the appearance of the sky, but you are not able to evaluate the signs of the times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 11:29–30; Matthew 12:39–40; Jonah 1:17"> An evil and adulterous generation seeks for a sign, and a sign will not be given to it except the sign of Jonah!” And he left them and went away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Beware the Leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 8:13–14; Matthew 15:39"> And when the disciples arrived at the other side,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the other side of the Sea of Galilee">a</a></sup>they had forgotten to take bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:1; Galatians 5:9; Matthew 16:12"> And Jesus said to them, “Watch out for and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 8:16–18; Matthew 15:16–18; Acts 10:14"> So they were discussing this among themselves, saying, “It is because we did not take bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:30; Matthew 14:31; Matthew 8:26"> But knowing this, Jesus said, “Why are you discussing among yourselves that you did not take bread,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “you do not have bread”">b</a></sup>you of little faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 14:17–21; John 6:9–13; Luke 9:13–17"> Do you not yet understand or do you not remember the five loaves for the five thousand, and how many baskets you took up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:34–38; Mark 8:17–21; Mark 8:5–9"> Or the seven loaves for the four thousand and how many baskets you took up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 16:6; Luke 12:56; Mark 8:21"> How do you not understand that I did not speak to you about bread? But beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Acts 23:8; Matthew 15:4–9"> Then they understood that he did not say to beware of the leaven of bread, but of the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter’s Confession at Caesarea Philippi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:18–20; Mark 8:27–29; Daniel 7:13"> Now when Jesus came to the region of Caesarea ⸤Philippi⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “of Philip”">c</a></sup>he began asking his disciples, saying, “Who do people say that the Son of Man is?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:15; Matthew 14:2; Malachi 4:5"> And they said, ⸤Some⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “those on the one hand”">d</a></sup>say John the Baptist, but others Elijah, and others Jeremiah or one of the prophets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 9:20; Matthew 13:11; Mark 8:29"> He said to them, “But who do you say that I am?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; John 11:27; 1 John 4:15"> And Simon Peter answered and said, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 6:45; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 11:25–27"> And Jesus answered and said to him, “Blessed are you, Simon son of Jonah, because flesh and blood did not reveal this to you, but my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 2:19–22; 1 Corinthians 3:9–11; Isaiah 28:16"> And I also say to you that you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not overpower it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:18; Isaiah 22:22; Revelation 1:18"> I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven, and whatever you bind on earth will be bound<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “will have been bound”">e</a></sup>in heaven, and whatever you release on earth will be released<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “will have been released”">f</a></sup>in heaven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Luke 9:21; John 1:41"> Then he commanded the disciples that they should tell no one that he was the Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Predicts His Death and Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 24:6–7; Matthew 20:17–19; Matthew 17:22–23"> From that time on Jesus began to show his disciples that he must go to Jerusalem and suffer many things from the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised on the third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 8:32; John 13:6–8; Matthew 16:16–17"> And Peter took him aside and began to rebuke him, saying, ⸤God forbid⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “merciful to you”">g</a></sup>Lord! This will never happen to you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 8:5–8; Colossians 3:2; Philippians 3:19"> But he turned around and said to Peter, “Get behind me, Satan! You are a cause for stumbling to me, because you are not intent on the things of God, but the things of people!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Taking Up One’s Cross to Follow Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:38; Luke 14:27; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> Then Jesus said to his disciples, “If anyone wants to come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Mark 8:35; Revelation 12:11"> For whoever wants to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life on account of me will find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 8:36–37; Job 27:8; Luke 9:25"> For what will a person be benefited if he gains the whole world but forfeits his life? Or what will a person give in exchange for his life?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Psalm 62:12; Romans 2:6"> For the Son of Man is going to come in the glory of his Father with his angels, and at that time he will reward each one according to ⸤what he has done⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “his activity”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:27; Mark 9:1; John 8:52"> Truly I say to you, that there are some of those standing here who will never experience death until they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom.”</span>
</div>
