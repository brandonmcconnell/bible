## The Question About Who Is Greatest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 9:33–37; Luke 9:46–48; Matthew 20:20–28"> At that time the disciples came up to Jesus, saying, “Who then is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:13–14; Mark 9:36–37; 1 Kings 3:7"> And calling a child to himself, he had him stand in their midst</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 10:14–15; 1 Peter 2:2; Matthew 19:14"> and said, “Truly I say to you, unless you turn around and become like young children, you will never enter into the kingdom of heaven!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Mark 10:43"> Therefore whoever humbles himself like this child, this person is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:37; John 13:20"> and whoever welcomes one child such as this in my name welcomes me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 9:42; Luke 17:1–2; Matthew 18:10"> But whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in me to sin, it would be better for him that ⸤a large millstone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a millstone of a donkey”">a</a></sup>be hung on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “around”">b</a></sup>his neck and he be drowned in the depths of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:1; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 26:24"> Woe to the world because of causes for stumbling, for it is a necessity that causes for stumbling come; nevertheless, woe to the person through whom the cause for stumbling comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:29–30; Philippians 3:8–9; Romans 13:12"> And if your hand or your foot causes you to sin, cut it off and throw it from you! It is better for you to enter into life crippled or lame than, having two hands or two feet, to be thrown into the eternal fire!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Matthew 18:8; Matthew 5:29"> And if your eye causes you to sin, tear it out and throw it from you! It is better for you to enter into life one-eyed than, having two eyes, to be thrown into fiery hell!</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Lost Sheep

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 91:11; Psalm 34:7; Hebrews 1:14"> “See to it that you do not despise one of these little ones, for I tell you that their angels in heaven constantly see the face of my Father who is in heaven.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Many later Greek manuscripts include vs. 11 , “For the Son of Man came to save those who are lost.”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:12; Luke 15:4–7; Ezekiel 34:16"> What do you think? If ⸤a certain man has⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “there are to a certain man”">d</a></sup>a hundred sheep, and one of them wanders away, will he not leave the ninety-nine on the hills and go and look for the one that wandered away?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Luke 15:5–10; James 2:13"> And if he happens to find it, truly I say to you that he rejoices over it more than over the ninety-nine that did not wander away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:39–40; 2 Peter 3:9"> In the same way it is not the will ⸤of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">e</a></sup>your Father who is in heaven that one of these little ones perish.</span>

## Confronting a Brother Who Sins Against You

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Luke 17:3–4; Galatians 6:1"> “Now if your brother sins against you, go correct him between you and him alone. If he listens to you, you have gained your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1; John 8:17"> But if he does not listen, take with you in addition one or two others, so that by ⸤the testimony⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the mouth”">f</a></sup>of two or three witnesses every matter may be established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Corinthians 5:9–7; 2 Thessalonians 3:14–15"> And if he refuses to listen to them, tell it to the church. But if he refuses to listen to the church also, let him be to you as a Gentile and a tax collector.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:19; 2 Corinthians 2:10; John 20:23"> “Truly I say to you, whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you release on earth will be released in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; 1 John 5:14–16; Mark 11:24"> Again, truly I say to you that if two of you agree on earth about any matter that they ask, it will be done for them from my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4; Revelation 21:3; Matthew 28:20"> For where two or three are gathered in my name, I am there in the midst of them.”</span>
</div>

## The Parable of the Unforgiving Slave

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 17:3–4; Matthew 18:15"> Then Peter came up to him and said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Then Peter came up and said to him”">g</a></sup>“Lord, how many times will my brother sin against me and I will forgive him? Up to seven times?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:13; Mark 11:25; Matthew 6:14–15"> Jesus said to him, “I do not say to you up to seven times, but up to seventy times seven!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “seventy-seven times”">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; Romans 14:12; Matthew 3:2"> “For this reason the kingdom of heaven may be compared to a man—a king—who wanted to settle accounts with his slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 38:4; Psalm 130:3–4; Luke 16:5"> And when he began to settle them, someone was brought to him who owed ten thousand talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Nehemiah 5:5; Luke 7:42"> And because he did not have enough to repay it, the master ordered him to be sold, and his wife and his children and everything that he had, and to be repaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:29; Luke 7:43; Matthew 8:2"> Then the slave threw himself to the ground and began to do obeisance to him, saying, ‘Be patient with me, and I will pay back everything to you!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 145:8; Nehemiah 9:17; Psalm 86:15"> So the master of that slave, because he had compassion, released him and forgave him the loan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Ezekiel 45:9; Deuteronomy 15:2"> But that slave went out and found one of his fellow slaves who owed him a hundred denarii, and taking hold of him, he began to choke him, saying, ‘Pay back everything that you owe!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 18:26; Matthew 6:12; Philemon 1:18–19"> Then his fellow slave threw himself to the ground and began to implore him, saying, ‘Be patient with me and I will repay you!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 22:27; 1 Kings 21:27–29"> But he did not want to, but rather he went and threw him into prison until he would repay what was owed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Hebrews 13:3; Mark 3:5"> So when his fellow slaves saw what had happened, they were extremely distressed, and went and reported to their master everything that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26; Romans 3:19"> Then his master summoned him and said to him, ‘Wicked slave! I forgave you all that debt because you implored me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Colossians 3:13; Matthew 6:12; Ephesians 4:32–2"> Should you not also have shown mercy to your fellow slave as I also showed mercy to you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:25–26; James 2:13; Luke 12:58–59"> And because he was angry, his master handed him over to the merciless jailers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “torturers”">i</a></sup>until he would repay everything that was owed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; James 2:13; Luke 6:37–38"> So also my heavenly Father will do to you, unless each of you forgives his brother from your hearts!”</span>
</div>
