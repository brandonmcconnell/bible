## Jesus Is Raised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 16:1–8; John 20:1–10; Matthew 27:56"> Now after the Sabbath, at the dawning on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to view the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:26; John 20:12–13; Mark 16:3–5"> And behold, there was a great earthquake, for an angel of the Lord descended from heaven and came up and rolled away the stone and sat down<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “was sitting”; here “sat down” reflects an ingressive nuance (beginning of a process or entry into a state) in the translation of the imperfect verb">a</a></sup>on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 9:3; Acts 1:10; Mark 16:5"> Now his appearance was like lightning and his clothing white as snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Daniel 10:7; Matthew 27:65–66"> And the guards trembled from the fear of him and became like dead men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:17–18; Hebrews 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> But the angel answered and said to the women, “Do not be afraid, for I know that you are looking for Jesus, who was crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:6; Matthew 12:40; Luke 24:6–8"> He is not here, for he has been raised, just as he said. Come, see the place where he was lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Luke 24:22–24; Matthew 28:10"> And go quickly, tell his disciples, ‘He has been raised from the dead, and behold, he is going ahead of you into Galilee. You will see him there.’ Behold, I have told you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 2:11; Mark 16:8; Luke 24:36–41"> And they departed quickly from the tomb with fear and great joy, and ran to tell his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 28:17; John 20:14–17; Matthew 14:33"> And behold, Jesus met them, saying, “Greetings!” And they came up and took hold of his feet and worshiped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:29; John 20:17; Matthew 28:7"> Then Jesus said to them, “Do not be afraid! Go tell my brothers that they should go to Galilee, and there they will see me.”</span>

## The Guards Report the Body Stolen

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:65–66; Matthew 28:4"> And while they were going, behold, some of the guard of soldiers went into the city and reported to the chief priests everything that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:3–4; Acts 4:5–22; Matthew 27:1–2"> And after they had assembled with the elders and had taken counsel, they gave a rather large sum of money to the soldiers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64"> telling them, “Say ‘His disciples came during the night and stole him while we were sleeping.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 12:19"> And if this matter is heard before the governor, we will satisfy him and ⸤keep you out of trouble⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “make you free from care”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 27:8; Matthew 26:15"> So they took the money and did as they were told, and spread abroad this report among the Jews until this very day.</span>

## The Great Commission

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:32; Matthew 28:10; Matthew 28:7"> So the eleven disciples proceeded to Galilee, to the mountain which Jesus had designated for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 45:11; Psalm 2:12; John 5:23"> And when they saw him, they worshiped him, but some doubted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:35; 1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 11:27"> And Jesus approached and spoke to them, saying, “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 2:38–39; Acts 1:8"> Therefore, go and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,</span>
