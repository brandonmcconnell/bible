## The Parable of the Sower

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:4–10; Matthew 13:1–15; Mark 4:1–12"> On that day Jesus went out of the house and was sitting by the sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the Sea of Galilee">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 5:3; Matthew 4:25; Mark 4:1"> And large crowds gathered close around him, so that he got into a boat to sit down, and all the crowd was standing on the shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 4:2–9; Mark 4:13; Psalm 78:2"> And he spoke many things to them in parables, saying, “Behold, the sower went out to sow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:18–19"> and while he was sowing, ⸤some seed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “some of which”">b</a></sup>fell on the side of the path, and the birds came and devoured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Ezekiel 36:26; Zechariah 7:12"> And other seed fell on the rocky ground, where it did not have much soil, and it sprang up at once because it did not have any depth of soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:21; James 1:11–12; Colossians 1:23"> But when the sun rose it was scorched, and because it did not have enough root, it withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 4:18–19; Jeremiah 4:3–4; Genesis 3:18"> And other seed fell among the thorn plants, and the thorn plants came up and choked it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:23; Luke 8:15; Genesis 26:12"> But other seed fell on the good soil and produced grain,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “fruit,” describing here the grain harvested from the healthy plants; in contemporary English this would more naturally be expressed by terms like “grain” or “crop”">c</a></sup>this one a hundred times as much and this one sixty and this one thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:7"> The one who has ears, let him hear!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Reason for the Parables

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 4:33–34; Mark 4:10"> And the disciples came up and said to him, “⸤Why⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “because of what”">d</a></sup>do you speak to them in parables?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 19:11"> And he answered and said to them, “To you it has been granted to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to those people it has not been granted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:29; Mark 4:24–25; Luke 8:18"> For whoever has, to him more will be given, and he will have an abundance. But whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2; Jeremiah 5:21; Isaiah 42:18–20"> For this reason I speak to them in parables, because seeing they do not see, and hearing they do not hear, nor do they understand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; 2 Corinthians 3:14"> and with reference to them the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled that says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“⸤You will listen carefully⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “with hearing you will hear”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will never understand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ⸤you will look closely⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “seeing you will see”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will never perceive.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Mark 4:12; Zechariah 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the heart of this people has become dull,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and with their ears they hear with difficulty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they have shut their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that they would not see with their eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and hear with their ears</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and understand with their heart</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and turn, and I would heal them.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 6:9–10">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Ephesians 1:17–18; Matthew 16:17"> But your eyes are blessed because they see, and your ears because they hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:56; Hebrews 11:13; 1 Peter 1:10–12"> For truly I say to you that many prophets and righteous people longed to see what you see, and did not see it, and to hear what you hear, and did not hear it!</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Sower Interpreted

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:11–12; Luke 8:11–15; Mark 4:13–20"> “You, therefore, listen to the parable of the sower:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 8:11–15; Matthew 13:38; Hebrews 2:1"> When anyone hears the word about the kingdom and does not understand it, the evil one comes and snatches away what was sown in his heart. This is what was sown on the side of the path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; Psalm 106:12–13; John 5:35"> And what was sown on the rocky ground—this is the one who hears the word and immediately receives it with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 2 Peter 1:8–9; Ephesians 3:17"> But he does not have a root in himself, but ⸤lasts only a little while⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “is temporary”">h</a></sup>and when affliction or persecution happens because of the word, immediately he falls away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:17; Luke 8:14; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> And what was sown into the thorn plants—this is the one who hears the word, and the anxiety of this world<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “of the world”">i</a></sup>and the deceitfulness of wealth choke the word and it becomes unproductive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 1:11; Matthew 12:33; John 15:16"> But what was sown on the good soil—this is the one who hears the word and understands it, who indeed bears fruit and produces, this one a hundred times as much, and this one sixty, and this one thirty.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Weeds Among the Wheat

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 25:1; Matthew 13:47; Matthew 20:1"> He put before them another parable, saying, “The kingdom of heaven may be compared to a man who sowed good seed in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; 2 Peter 2:1; Acts 20:30–31"> But while his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">j</a></sup>people were sleeping, his enemy came and sowed darnel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A weed that looks similar to wheat but has poisonous seeds">k</a></sup>in the midst of the wheat and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 4:26–29"> So when the ⸤wheat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “grass,” “hay,” but in this context referring to the good plants as opposed to the weeds">l</a></sup>sprouted and yielded grain, then the darnel appeared also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Corinthians 6:1; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20"> So the slaves of the master of the house came and said to him, ‘Master, did you not sow good seed in your field? How then does it have darnel?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:3–7; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; Luke 9:49–54"> And he said to them, ‘An enemy has done this!’ So the slaves said to him, ‘Then do you want us to go and gather them?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> But he said, “No, lest when you gather the darnel you uproot the wheat together with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 3:12; 1 Timothy 5:24; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> Let both grow together until the harvest, and at the season of the harvest I will tell the reapers, “First gather the darnel and tie it into bundles to burn them, but gather the wheat into my storehouse.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 16:17–20; Matthew 13:11; Matthew 9:28"> Then he left the crowds and came into the house, and his disciples came to him saying, “Explain the parable of the darnel in the field to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–7; Matthew 13:41; Luke 10:16"> So he answered and said, “The one who sows the good seed is the Son of Man,</span>

<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 13:39"> Thus just as the darnel is gathered and burned<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “burned up”">p</a></sup>with fire, so it will be at the end of the age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Matthew 18:7; 2 Peter 2:1–2"> The Son of Man will send out his angels and they will gather out of his kingdom all the causes of sin and those who do lawless deeds,</span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Matthew 11:15; Luke 12:32"> Then the righteous will shine like the sun in the kingdom of their Father.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="An allusion to Dan 12:3">r</a></sup>The one who has ears, let him hear!</span>
