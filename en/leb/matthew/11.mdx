## A Question from John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 10:38; Luke 8:1"> And it happened that when Jesus had finished giving orders to his twelve disciples, he went on from there to teach and to preach in their towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Matthew 14:3; Matthew 9:14"> Now when John<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, John the Baptist">a</a></sup>heard in prison the deeds of Christ, he sent word by his disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:27; Malachi 3:1; Deuteronomy 18:15–18"> and said to him, “Are you the one who is to come, or should we look for another?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Jesus answered and said to them, “Go and tell John what you hear and see:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> the blind receive sight and the lame walk, lepers are cleansed and the deaf hear, and the dead are raised, and the poor have good news announced to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 26:31"> And whoever is not offended by me is blessed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:6; John 5:35; Mark 1:3–5"> Now as these were going away, Jesus began to speak to the crowds concerning John: “What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; 2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4"> But what did you go out to see? A man dressed in soft clothing? Behold, those who wear soft clothing are in the houses of kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:76; Matthew 14:5; Matthew 21:24–26"> But why did you go out? To see a prophet?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “But what did you go out to see? A prophet?”">b</a></sup>Yes, I tell you, and even more than a prophet!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 4:5"> It is this man about whom it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Behold, I am sending my messenger before your face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who will prepare your way before you.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Mal 3:1; cf. Mark 1:2">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 7:28; Ephesians 3:8; Matthew 3:11"> Truly I say to you, among those born of women there has not arisen one greater than John the Baptist. But the one who is least in the kingdom of heaven is greater than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 16:16; Luke 13:24; Ephesians 6:11–13"> But from the days of John the Baptist until now, the kingdom of heaven is treated violently, and the violent claim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “seize”">d</a></sup>it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:17–18; Romans 3:21; Acts 13:27"> For all the prophets and the law prophesied until John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Matthew 17:10–13; Mark 9:11–13"> and if you are willing to accept it, he is Elijah, the one who is going to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:8; Matthew 13:9; Mark 4:23"> The one who has ears, let him hear!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 7:31–35; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 23:36"> “But to what shall I compare this generation? It is like children sitting in the marketplaces who call out to one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 28:9–13; Isaiah 30:29; Jeremiah 9:17–20"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘We played the flute for you and you did not dance;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we sang a lament and you did not mourn.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Matthew 3:4; John 10:20"> For John came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, ‘He has a demon!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:10–11; Luke 15:1–2; Luke 19:7"> The Son of Man came eating and drinking, and they say, ‘Behold, a man who is a glutton and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’ But wisdom is vindicated by her deeds.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Many Greek manuscripts, including most later ones, read “children”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Woes Pronounced on Unrepentant Towns

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:13–15; Jeremiah 8:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> Then he began to reproach the towns in which the majority of his miracles had been done, because they did not repent:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:6–7; Acts 12:20; Luke 9:10"> “Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the miracles done in you had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:24; Matthew 10:15; Hebrews 10:26–31"> Nevertheless I tell you, it will be more bearable for Tyre and for Sidon on the day of judgment than for you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; John 4:46–54; Genesis 13:13"> And you, Capernaum, will you be exalted to heaven? No, you will be brought down to Hades! For if the miracles done in you had been done in Sodom, it would have remained until today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:22; Mark 6:11"> Nevertheless I tell you that it will be more bearable for the region of Sodom on the day of judgment than for you!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Yoke That Is Easy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 21:16; Psalm 8:2; Matthew 16:17"> At that time Jesus answered and said, “I praise you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because you have hidden these things from the wise and intelligent, and have revealed them to young children.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or perhaps “to the childlike,” or “to the innocent”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Job 33:13"> Yes, Father, for to do so ⸤was your gracious will⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “was pleasing before you”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:6–9; Matthew 28:18; Luke 10:22"> All things have been handed over to me by my Father, and no one knows the Son except the Father, and no one knows the Father except the Son and ⸤anyone to whom⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to whomever”">h</a></sup>the Son wants to reveal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 7:37; John 6:37"> Come to me, all of you who labor and are burdened, and I will give you rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; 1 John 2:6; Philippians 2:7–8"> Take my yoke on you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:3; John 16:33; Philippians 4:13"> For my yoke is easy to carry and my burden is light.”</span>
</div>
</div>
