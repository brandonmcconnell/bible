## The Temptation of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Exodus 34:28; 1 Kings 19:8"> and after he had fasted forty days and forty nights, then he was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:1–5; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; Numbers 11:4–6"> And the tempter approached and said to him, “If you are the Son of God, order that these stones become bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Luke 4:4; John 6:63"> But he answered and said, “It is written, ‘Man will not live on bread alone, but on every word that comes out of the mouth of God.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 8:3">a</a></sup></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 91:11–12; Hebrews 1:14; Psalm 34:7"> and said to him, “If you are the Son of God, throw yourself down! </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:16; 1 Corinthians 10:9; Psalm 78:18"> Jesus said to him, “On the other hand it is written, ‘You are not to put the Lord your God to the test.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 6:16">e</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Public Ministry in Galilee

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 1:14; Luke 3:20; Matthew 14:3"> Now when he heard that John ⸤had been arrested⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “had been handed over”">g</a></sup>he withdrew into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:46; Mark 1:21; Matthew 11:23"> And leaving Nazareth, he went and lived in Capernaum by the sea, in the region of Zebulun and Naphtali,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1–2; Matthew 2:23; Luke 24:44"> in order that what was spoken by the prophet Isaiah would be fulfilled, who said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:1; 2 Kings 15:29; Joshua 21:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">⸤toward the sea⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the way of the sea”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the other side of the Jordan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Galilee of the Gentiles</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “nations”; the same Greek word can be translated “nations” or “Gentiles” depending on the context">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">—</span></span>

## Jesus Calls His First Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 1:40–42; Matthew 10:2; Luke 6:14"> Now as he was walking beside the Sea of Galilee, he saw two brothers, Simon called Peter and his brother Andrew, throwing a casting net into the sea (for they were fishermen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:17–18; John 12:26; Matthew 16:24"> And he said to them, “⸤Follow me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “come behind me”">k</a></sup>and I will make you fishers of people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:28–31; Matthew 19:27; Luke 18:28–30"> And immediately they left their nets and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 1:19–20; Matthew 10:2; Mark 3:17"> And going on from there, he saw two other brothers, James the son of Zebedee and his brother John, in the boat with their father Zebedee, mending their nets, and he called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 9:59–60; Deuteronomy 33:9–10"> And immediately they left the boat and their father and followed him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Teaching, Preaching, and Healing throughout Galilee

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 9:35; Mark 1:39; Acts 10:38"> And he went around through all of Galilee, teaching in their synagogues and proclaiming the good news of the kingdom and healing every disease and every sickness among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:6; Matthew 17:15; Luke 5:15"> And a report about him went out ⸤throughout⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “in the whole of”">l</a></sup>Syria, and they brought to him all those ⸤who were sick⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “having badly”">m</a></sup>with various diseases and afflicted by torments, demon-possessed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and demon-possessed”">n</a></sup>and epileptics and paralytics, and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 6:17; Mark 5:20; Mark 3:7–8"> And large crowds followed him from Galilee, Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and from the other side of the Jordan.</span>
