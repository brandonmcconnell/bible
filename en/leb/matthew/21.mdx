<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–2; Acts 17:25; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> And if anyone says anything to you, you will say, ‘The Lord ⸤needs them⸥,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “has need of them”">a</a></sup>and he will send them at once.”</span>

## The Cleansing of the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 2:14–17; Luke 19:45–47; Mark 11:15–18"> And Jesus entered the temple courts and drove out all those who were selling and buying in the temple, and overturned the tables of the money changers and the chairs of those who were selling doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:11; Isaiah 56:7; Luke 19:46"> And he said to them, “It is written, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 56:7">e</a></sup>but you have made it a cave of robbers!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 3:1–9; Acts 10:38; Matthew 11:4–5"> And the blind and the lame came up to him in the temple courts and he healed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 21:9; Luke 19:39–40; Isaiah 26:11"> But when the chief priests and the scribes saw the wonderful things that he did, and the children shouting in the temple courts and saying, “Hosanna to the Son of David!” they were indignant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 8:2; Matthew 11:25; Luke 19:39–40"> And they said to him, “Do you hear what these children are saying?” So Jesus said to them, “Yes, have you never read, ‘Out of the mouths of children and nursing babies you have prepared for yourself praise’?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 8:2">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:18; Mark 11:11; Luke 24:50"> And leaving them, he went outside of the city to Bethany and spent the night there.</span>
</div>

## A Barren Fig Tree Cursed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 4:2; Hebrews 4:15; Matthew 12:1"> Now early in the morning, as he was returning to the city, he was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 15:6; Mark 11:14; Titus 1:16"> And seeing a single fig tree by the road, he went to it and found nothing on it except leaves only. And he said to it, “May there be no more fruit from you ⸤forever⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to the age”">g</a></sup>and the fig tree withered at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Mark 11:20–21; James 1:10–11"> And when they saw it, the disciples were astonished, saying, “How did the fig tree wither at once?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 1:6; Matthew 17:20; Mark 11:22–23"> And Jesus answered and said to them, “Truly I say to you, if you have faith and do not doubt, you will do not only what was done to the fig tree, but even if you say to this mountain, ‘Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,” it will happen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; 1 John 5:14–15; Mark 11:24"> And whatever you ask in prayer, if you believe, you will receive.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Authority Challenged

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 4:7; Mark 11:27–33; 1 Chronicles 24:1–19"> And after he arrived at the temple, the chief priests and the elders of the people came up to him while he was teaching, saying, “By what authority are you doing these things? And who gave you this authority?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Colossians 4:6; Matthew 10:16"> And Jesus answered and said to them, “I also will ask you one question. If you tell the answer to me, I also will tell you by what authority I am doing these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 3:20; Mark 1:1–11; John 3:18"> From where was the baptism of John—from heaven or from men?” And they began to discuss this among themselves, saying, “If we say ‘From heaven,’ he will say to us, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Matthew 14:5; John 5:35"> But if we say, ‘From men,’ we are afraid of the crowd, because they all look upon John as a prophet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 1:18–22; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10; Luke 20:7–8"> And they answered and said to Jesus, “We do not know.” And he said to them, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I am doing these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Two Sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Matthew 20:1; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> “Now what do you think? A man had two sons. He approached<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “And he approached”">h</a></sup>the first and said, ‘Son, go work in the vineyard today.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28–32; Matthew 3:2–8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> And he answered and said, ‘I do not want to!’ But later he changed his mind and went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Ezekiel 33:31; Matthew 23:3"> And he approached the second<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “the other”">i</a></sup>and said the same thing. So he answered and said, ‘I will, sir,’ and he did not go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 7:29; Luke 7:37–50; Matthew 7:21"> Which of the two did the will of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">j</a></sup>father?” They said, “The first.” Jesus said to them, “Truly I say to you that the tax collectors and the prostitutes are going ahead of you into the kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 7:37–50; Zechariah 7:11–12; Luke 7:29–30"> For John came to you in the way of righteousness and you did not believe him, but the tax collectors and the prostitutes did believe him. And when you saw it, you did not even change your minds later so as to believe in him.</span>
</div>

## The Parable of the Tenant Farmers in the Vineyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 12:1–12; Luke 20:9–19"> “Listen to another parable: There was a man—a master of a house—who planted a vineyard, and put a fence around it, and dug a winepress in it, and built a watchtower, and leased it to tenant farmers, and went on a journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 20:10–19; Isaiah 5:4; Zechariah 1:3–6"> And when the season of fruit drew near, he sent his slaves to the tenant farmers to collect his fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Matthew 5:12; Hebrews 11:36–37"> And the tenant farmers seized his slaves, one of whom they beat, and one of whom they killed, and one of whom they stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 22:4"> Again, he sent other slaves, more than the first ones, and they did the same thing to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 3:16; John 3:35–36; Zephaniah 3:7"> So finally he sent his son to them, saying, ‘They will respect my son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 20:14; Genesis 37:18–20; Mark 12:7–8"> But when the tenant farmers saw the son, they said among themselves, ‘This is the heir. Come, let us kill him and have his inheritance!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 2:23; James 5:6; Luke 22:52–54"> And they seized him and threw him out of the vineyard and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Mark 12:9; Luke 20:15–16"> Now when the master of the vineyard arrives, what will he do to those tenant farmers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Matthew 21:43; Acts 28:28"> They said to him, “He will destroy those evil men completely and lease the vineyard to other tenant farmers who will give him the fruits in their season.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalm 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11"> Jesus said to them, “Have you never read in the scriptures,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The stone which the builders rejected,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">this has become ⸤the cornerstone⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the head of the corner”">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This came about from the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it is marvelous in our eyes’?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 118:22–23">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 21:41; Matthew 8:11–12; 1 Peter 2:9"> For this reason, I tell you that the kingdom of God will be taken away from you and will be given to a people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “nation”">m</a></sup>who produce its fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Peter 2:8; Daniel 2:34–35"> And the one who falls on this stone will be broken to pieces, and the one on whom it falls—it will crush him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 20:19; Luke 11:45; Matthew 12:12"> And when the chief priests and the Pharisees heard his parables, they knew that he was speaking about them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Matthew 21:26; Luke 7:16"> and although they wanted to arrest him, they were afraid of the crowds, because they looked upon him as a prophet.</span>
</div>
