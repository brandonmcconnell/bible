## The Transfiguration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Matthew 26:37; Mark 5:37"> And after six days Jesus took along Peter and James and John his brother, and led them up on a high mountain by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 104:2; Matthew 28:3; Luke 9:29"> And he was transfigured before them, and his face shone like the sun, and his clothing became bright as the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Mark 9:4; Malachi 4:5"> And behold, Moses and Elijah appeared to them, talking with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Luke 9:33; John 14:8–9"> So Peter answered and said to Jesus, “Lord, it is good that we are here! If you want, I will make here three shelters, one for you and one for Moses and one for Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; Matthew 12:18; Deuteronomy 18:15"> While he was still speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them, and behold, a voice from the cloud said, “This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased. Listen to him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 26:14; 2 Peter 1:18; Daniel 8:17"> And when the disciples heard this, they fell down on their faces and were extremely frightened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 10:18; Matthew 14:27; Daniel 8:18"> And Jesus came and touched them and said, “Get up and do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:36; Mark 9:8; Acts 12:10–11"> And when they lifted up their eyes they saw no one except him—Jesus alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Mark 9:9–13; Matthew 17:23"> And as they were coming down from the mountain, Jesus commanded them saying, “Tell no one the vision until the Son of Man is raised from the dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; John 1:21"> And the disciples asked him, saying, “Then why do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 1:16–17; Malachi 4:6"> And he answered and said, “Elijah indeed is coming, and will restore all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3–12; Matthew 16:21; Acts 13:24–28"> But I say to you that Elijah has already come, and they did not recognize him, but did with him whatever they wanted. In the same way also the Son of Man is going to suffer at their hands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 11:14"> Then the disciples understood that he had spoken to them about John the Baptist.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Demon-possessed Boy Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 9:14–29; Mark 1:40; Acts 10:25–26"> And when they came to the crowd, a man approached him, kneeling down before him</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Job 1:10–19; Mark 9:20–22"> and saying, “Lord, have mercy on my son, because he has seizures and suffers severely, for often he falls into the fire and often into the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Luke 9:40; Acts 19:15–16"> And I brought him to your disciples, and they were not able to heal him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Matthew 6:30; Mark 16:14"> And Jesus answered and said, “O unbelieving and perverse generation! ⸤How long⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “until when”">a</a></sup>will I be with you? ⸤How long⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “until when”">b</a></sup>must I put up with you? Bring him here to me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Mark 1:34; Mark 5:8"> And Jesus rebuked him, and the demon came out of him and the boy was healed from that hour.</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 17:23"> Now as they were gathering in Galilee, Jesus said to them, “The Son of Man is going to be betrayed into the hands of men,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or possibly “of people,” in a generic sense, although the reference here may be to the specific group responsible for Jesus’ arrest, where it is unlikely women were present">e</a></sup></span>

## Paying the Double Drachma Temple Tax

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 38:26; Exodus 30:13; Mark 9:33"> Now when they arrived in Capernaum, the ones who collected the double drachma tax<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This refers to the half-shekel annual tax paid by Jewish males to support the temple; over time the name of the coin commonly used to pay the tax came to be used for the tax itself">f</a></sup>came up to Peter and said, “Does your teacher not pay the double drachma tax?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:21; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 22:17"> He said, “Yes.” And when he came into the house, Jesus spoke to him first, saying, “What do you think, Simon? From whom do the kings of the earth collect tolls or taxes—from their own sons, or from foreigners?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And when he said, “From foreigners,” Jesus said to him, “Then the sons are free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> But so that we do not give offense to them, go out to the sea, cast a line with a hook, and take the first fish that comes up. And when you open its mouth, you will find a four-drachma coin. Take that and give it to them for me and you.”</span>
</div>
