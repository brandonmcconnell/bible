## The Sermon on the Mount: Charitable Giving

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; John 12:43; Hebrews 6:10"> “And take care not to practice your righteousness before people to be seen by them; ⸤otherwise⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “but if not”">a</a></sup>you have no reward from your Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Matthew 6:16; 2 Corinthians 9:6–15"> Therefore whenever you practice charitable giving, do not sound a trumpet in front of you, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets, in order that they may be praised by people. Truly I say to you, they have received their reward in full!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 7:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:44"> But you, when you practice charitable giving, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing,</span>

## The Sermon on the Mount: How to Pray

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:10–11; Matthew 6:2; Luke 18:13"> And whenever you pray, do not be like the hypocrites, because they love to stand and pray in the synagogues and on the corners of the streets, in order that they may be seen by people. Truly I say to you, they have received their reward in full!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 14:23; Isaiah 65:24"> But whenever you pray, enter into your inner room and shut your door and pray to your Father who is in secret, and your Father who sees in secret will reward you.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 12:50"> <span className="pl pl-1">May your kingdom come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">may your will be done</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on earth as it is in heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 30:8; Luke 11:3; Matthew 4:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give us today our daily bread,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Mark 11:25; Ephesians 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">and forgive us our debts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as we also have forgiven our debtors.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 7:14–17; Matthew 26:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">And do not bring us into temptation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but deliver us from the evil one.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “evil”; most later Greek manuscripts add the phrase “for yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory forever, amen”">c</a></sup></span>

## The Sermon on the Mount: How to Fast

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3–5; Matthew 9:14–15; Daniel 9:3"> “Whenever you fast, do not be sullen like the hypocrites, for they make their faces unrecognizable in order that they may be seen fasting by people. Truly I say to you, they have received their reward in full!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ruth 3:3; Ecclesiastes 9:8; 2 Samuel 12:20"> But when you are fasting, ⸤put olive oil on your head⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “anoint your head”">d</a></sup>and wash your face</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Matthew 6:4; 2 Corinthians 10:18"> so that you will not be seen by people as fasting, but to your Father who is in secret, and your Father who sees in secret will reward you.</span>
</div>

## The Sermon on the Mount: Anxiety

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; 1 Peter 5:7; Romans 8:32"> “For this reason I say to you, do not be anxious for your life, what you will eat,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts add “or what you will drink”; other later manuscripts add “and what you will drink”">h</a></sup>and not for your body, what you will wear. Is your life not more than food and your body more than clothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 10:29–31; Psalm 147:9; Luke 12:24–32"> Consider the birds of the sky, that they do not sow or reap or gather produce into barns, and your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not worth more than they are?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:25–26; Ecclesiastes 3:14; Psalm 39:5–6"> And who among you, by being anxious, is able to add one hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “cubit”">i</a></sup>to his life span?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; Philippians 4:6; Luke 12:27"> And why are you anxious about clothing? Observe the lilies of the field, how they grow: they do not toil or spin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:20–22; 2 Chronicles 9:4–6; 1 Peter 3:2–5"> but I say to you that not even Solomon in all his glory was dressed like one of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:31; Luke 12:28; 1 Peter 1:24"> But if God dresses the grass of the field in this way, although it is here today and tomorrow is thrown into the oven, will he not do so much more for you, you of little faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Luke 12:29; Matthew 4:4"> Therefore do not be anxious, saying, ‘What will we eat?’ or ‘What will we drink?’ or ‘What will we wear?,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 6:8; Luke 12:30; Ephesians 4:17"> for the pagans seek after all these things. For your heavenly Father knows that you need all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 12:31; Matthew 5:6; John 6:27"> But seek first his kingdom and righteousness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “the kingdom of God and his righteousness”">j</a></sup>and all these things will be added to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 6:25; John 14:27; Hebrews 13:5–6"> Therefore do not be anxious for tomorrow, because tomorrow will be anxious for itself. ⸤Each day has enough trouble of its own.⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sufficient for the day its trouble”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
