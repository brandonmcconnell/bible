## The Sermon on the Mount: Salt and Light

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 14:34–35; Mark 9:49–50"> “You are the salt of the earth. But if salt becomes tasteless, by what will it be made salty? It is good for nothing any longer except to be thrown outside and trampled under foot by people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; John 8:12; Ephesians 5:8–14"> You are the light of the world. A city located on top of a hill cannot be hidden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:33; Mark 4:21; Luke 8:16"> nor do they light a lamp and place it under a basket, but on a lampstand, and it shines on all those in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Titus 2:7; John 15:8"> In the same way let your light shine before people, so that they can see your good works and glorify your Father who is in heaven.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sermon on the Mount: The Law and the Prophets Fulfilled

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Luke 16:17; Galatians 4:4–5"> “Do not think that I have come to destroy the law or the prophets. I have not come to destroy them but to fulfill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 16:17; Isaiah 40:8; 1 Peter 1:25"> For truly I say to you, until heaven and earth pass away, not one tiny letter or one stroke of a letter will pass away from the law until all takes place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; James 2:10–11; Matthew 28:20"> Therefore whoever abolishes one of the least of these commandments and teaches people to do so will be called least in the kingdom of heaven, but whoever keeps them and teaches them, this person will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 10:2–3; Philippians 3:9; Romans 9:30–32"> For I say to you that unless your righteousness greatly surpasses that of the scribes and Pharisees, you will never enter into the kingdom of heaven.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sermon on the Mount: Anger Toward Others

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:17; Exodus 20:13; Numbers 35:30–34"> “You have heard that it was said to the ⸤people of old⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “ancients”">a</a></sup>‘Do not commit murder,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 20:13 ; Deut 5:17">b</a></sup>and ‘whoever commits murder will be subject to judgment.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:14–15; 1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 2:9"> But I say to you that everyone who is angry at his brother will be subject to judgment, and whoever says to his brother, ‘Stupid fool!’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Greek “Raca,” a term of verbal abuse involving lack of intelligence">c</a></sup>will be subject to the council, and whoever says, ‘Obstinate fool!’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Perhaps with the idea of obstinate, godless foolishness; some take the word to be a Greek transliteration of the Hebrew word for “rebel” (Deut 21:18 , 20 )">d</a></sup>will be subject to fiery hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 11:25; Leviticus 6:2–6; Matthew 5:24"> Therefore if you present your gift at the altar and there remember that your brother has something against you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:16; Romans 12:17–18; Matthew 18:15–17"> leave your gift there before the altar and first go be reconciled to your brother, and then come and present your gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 12:58–59; Proverbs 25:8; Hebrews 3:13"> ⸤Settle the case quickly with your accuser⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “be making friends quickly with your accuser”">e</a></sup>while you are with him on the way, lest your accuser hand you over to the judge, and the judge to the officer, and you be thrown into prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:34; Luke 12:59; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> Truly I say to you, you will never come out of there until you have paid back the last penny!</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sermon on the Mount: Adultery and Lust

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:18; Proverbs 6:32; Exodus 20:14"> “You have heard that it was said, ‘Do not commit adultery.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 20:14 ; Deut 5:17">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 John 2:16; James 1:14–15; Proverbs 6:25"> But I say to you that everyone who looks at a woman to lust for her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 8:13; Mark 9:43–48; Colossians 3:5"> And if your right eye causes you to sin, tear it out and throw it from you! For it is better for you that one of your members be destroyed than your whole body be thrown into hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:43; Matthew 5:29; 1 Peter 2:8"> And if your right hand causes you to sin, cut it off and throw it from you! For it is better for you that one of your limbs be destroyed than your whole body go into hell.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Sermon on the Mount: Divorce

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Matthew 19:7"> “And it was said, ‘Whoever divorces his wife must give her a certificate of divorce.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 24:1">g</a></sup></span>

</div>

<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 24:19–20; Deuteronomy 19:21; Exodus 21:22–27"> “You have heard that it was said, ‘An eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 21:24 ; Lev 24:20">l</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:26; Matthew 27:32; Mark 15:21"> And whoever forces you to go one mile,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="A Roman mile was originally a thousand paces, but was later fixed at eight stades (1,478.5 meters)">o</a></sup>go with him two.</span>

## The Sermon on the Mount: Love for Enemies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Psalm 139:21–22; Galatians 5:13–14"> “You have heard that it was said, ‘Love your neighbor’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 19:18">p</a></sup>and ‘Hate your enemy.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="An allusion to Deut 23:3–6">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–28"> But I say to you, love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalm 145:9; Luke 6:35; Acts 14:17"> in order that you may be sons of your Father who is in heaven, because he causes his sun to rise on the evil and the good, and he sends rain on the just and the unjust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 6:32–35; 1 Peter 2:20–23; Matthew 9:10–11"> For if you love those who love you, what reward do you have? Do not the tax collectors also do the same?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:20; Luke 6:32; 1 Peter 2:20"> And if you greet only your brothers, what are you doing that is remarkable? Do not the Gentiles also do the same?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15–16; James 1:4; Leviticus 19:2"> Therefore you be perfect as your heavenly Father is perfect.</span>
