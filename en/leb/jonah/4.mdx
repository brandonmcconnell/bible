## Jonah Is Angry at Yahweh’s Compassion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 15:28; Jonah 4:9; Jonah 4:4"> And this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">a</a></sup>⸤was greatly displeasing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “was displeasing with great displeasure”">b</a></sup>to Jonah, and ⸤he became furious⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “it was hot for him”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 86:15; Exodus 34:6–7; Numbers 14:18–19"> And he prayed to Yahweh and said, “O Yahweh, was this not ⸤what I said⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “my word”">d</a></sup>while I was in my homeland? Therefore ⸤I originally fled⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “I did the first time to flee”">e</a></sup>to Tarshish, because I knew that you are a gracious and compassionate God, slow to anger ⸤and having great steadfast love⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “and great of steadfast love”">f</a></sup>and one who relents concerning calamity.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, calamity sent by God as judgment">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:4; Ecclesiastes 7:1; Numbers 11:15"> And so then, Yahweh, please take my life from me, because for me death is better than life!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Jonah 4:9; Micah 6:3"> And Yahweh said, “⸤Is it right for you to be angry⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “Rightfully is it hot for you”; some take this to mean “Are you so very angry?” (Targum Jonah 4:4; see also JPS, NEB, NET)">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; 1 Kings 19:13; Jeremiah 17:15–16"> And Jonah went out from the city and sat down east of the city, and he made for himself a shelter there. And he sat under it in the shade, ⸤waiting to see⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “until he would see”">i</a></sup>what would happen with the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 103:10–14; Isaiah 39:2; Esther 5:9"> And Yahweh God appointed a plant,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Probably a castor oil plant, though some have suggested some type of gourd plant">j</a></sup>and he made it grow up over Jonah to be a shade over his head, to save him from his discomfort. And Jonah ⸤was very glad⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “was glad with great joy”">k</a></sup>about the plant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 1:12; Job 1:21; Psalm 102:10"> So God appointed a worm ⸤at daybreak⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “at the coming up of the dawn”">l</a></sup>the next day, and it attacked the plant, and it withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Psalm 121:6; Ezekiel 19:12"> ⸤And when the sun rose⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “and it happened at the rising of the sun”">m</a></sup>God appointed a scorching east wind, and the sun beat down on Jonah’s head and he grew faint. ⸤And he asked that he could die⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “and he asked his soul to die”">n</a></sup>and said, “My death is better than my life!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 5:2; Job 18:4; Judges 16:16"> So God said to Jonah, “⸤Is it right for you to be angry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “rightfully is it hot for you”; some take this to mean “Are you so very angry?” (Targum Jonah 4:4; see also JPS, NEB, NET)">o</a></sup>about the plant?” And he said, “⸤It is right for me to be angry enough to die⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “Rightfully it is hot for me until death”">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; Genesis 17:12"> But Yahweh said, “You are troubled about the plant, for which you did not labor nor cause it to grow. ⸤It grew up in a night and it perished in a night⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “Which was a son of a night and as a son of a night it perished”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 18:33; Jonah 1:2; Luke 15:28–32"> And should I not be concerned about Nineveh, the great city, ⸤in which there are⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “which there are in it”">r</a></sup>more ⸤than one hundred and twenty thousand⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “than two ten myriad” (12 x 10,000)">s</a></sup>people who do not know right from left,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="“hand ” is often supplied, but it is not clear just what deficiency is meant by this expression, which occurs only here in biblical Hebrew">t</a></sup>plus many animals?”</span>
</div>
