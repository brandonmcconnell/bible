## The People of Nineveh Repent at Jonah’s Proclamation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jonah 1:1; John 21:15–17"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jonah a second time, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:7; Jeremiah 1:17; Jonah 3:3"> “Get up! Go to Nineveh, the great city, and proclaim to it the message that I am telling you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–29; Psalm 80:10; Genesis 22:3"> So Jonah got up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “set out”">a</a></sup>and went to Nineveh according to the word of Yahweh. Now Nineveh was ⸤an extraordinarily great city⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “a great city to God” or “a great city to the gods,” a disputed phrase which may refer to God’s estimate or ownership of Nineveh, to the presence of many idols, or an idiom referring to the size of Nineveh (this translation takes the last view)">b</a></sup>—a journey of three days across.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This phrase may also refer to a journey on which business was done, so that “three days” is the total elapsed time">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:22; Jeremiah 18:7–10; 2 Kings 20:1"> And Jonah began to go into the city a journey of one day, and he cried out and said, “Forty more days and Nineveh will be demolished!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “overthrown”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Matthew 12:41; Joel 2:12–17"> And the people of Nineveh believed in God, and they proclaimed a fast and put on sackcloth—from the greatest of them ⸤to the least important⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to the smallest of them”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The King’s Proclamation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:6–10; Esther 4:1–4; Daniel 9:3"> And the news reached the king of Nineveh, and he rose from his throne and removed his royal robe, put on sackcloth, and sat in the ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:15–16"> And he had a proclamation made, and said,<br />“In Nineveh, by a decree of the king and his nobles:<br />“No human being or animal, no herd or flock, shall taste anything! They must not eat, and they must not drink water!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; Isaiah 55:6–7; Ezekiel 18:21–24"> And the human beings and the animals must be covered with sackcloth! And they must call forcefully to God, and each must turn from his evil way and from the violence that is in his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “their”">f</a></sup>hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:22; Joel 2:13–14; Psalm 106:45"> Who knows? God may relent and change his mind and turn ⸤from his blazing anger⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from the heat of his anger”">g</a></sup>⸤so that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">h</a></sup>we will not perish.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:8; 1 Kings 21:27–29; Luke 15:20"> And God saw their deeds—that they turned from their evil ways—and God changed his mind about the evil that he had said he would bring upon them, and he did not do it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
