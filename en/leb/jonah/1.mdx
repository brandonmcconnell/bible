## Jonah Disobeys Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Luke 11:29–30; 2 Kings 14:25"> And the word of Yahweh came to Jonah the son of Amittai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Genesis 10:11; Isaiah 58:1"> “Get up! Go to the great city Nineveh and cry out against her, because their evil has come up ⸤before me⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to my face”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 139:7–12; Genesis 4:16; Joshua 19:46"> But Jonah set out to flee toward Tarshish from ⸤the presence of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “from to face of”">b</a></sup>Yahweh. And he went down to Joppa and found a merchant ship going to Tarshish, and paid her fare, and went on board her to go with them toward Tarshish from ⸤the presence of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “from to face of”">c</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 107:23–31; Matthew 8:24–27; Exodus 10:19"> And Yahweh hurled a great wind upon the sea, and it was a great storm on the sea, and the merchant ship ⸤was in danger of breaking up⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “threatened to be broken up”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Acts 27:18–19; 1 Kings 18:26"> And the mariners were afraid, and each cried out to his god. And they threw the ⸤contents⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “objects”">e</a></sup>that were in the merchant ship into the sea to lighten it for them. And meanwhile Jonah went down into the hold of the vessel and lay down and fell asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Psalm 107:28–29; Ephesians 5:14"> And the captain<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “commander”">f</a></sup>of the ship approached him and said to him, “Why are you ⸤sound asleep⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in a deep sleep”">g</a></sup>Get up! Call on your god! Perhaps your god<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the god,” with the article indicating previous reference">h</a></sup>will take notice of us and we won’t perish!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Joshua 7:13–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> And they said ⸤to one another⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “each to his friend/companion”">i</a></sup>“Come, let us cast lots so that we may know on whose account this disaster has come on us!” And they cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; 1 Samuel 30:13; Joshua 7:19"> So they said to him, “Please tell us whoever is responsible that this disaster has come upon us! What is your occupation? And from where do you come? What is your country? And from which people are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 146:5–6; Psalm 95:5–6; Nehemiah 9:6"> And he said to them, “I am a Hebrew, and I fear Yahweh, the God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Job 27:22; Joshua 7:25"> Then the men ⸤were greatly afraid⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “were afraid with a great fear”">j</a></sup>and they said to him, “What is this you have done?” because they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the men” but this is redundant in English">k</a></sup>knew that he was fleeing ⸤from the presence of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “from before the face of”">l</a></sup>Yahweh (because he had told them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:1–6; Micah 6:6–7; 1 Samuel 6:2–3"> So they said to him, “What shall we do to you so that the sea may quiet down for us?” because the sea ⸤was growing more and more tempestuous⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “going/growing and storming”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:17; 1 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 7:12"> And he said to them, “Pick me up and hurl me into the sea so that the sea may quiet down for you, because I know that on account of me this great storm has come upon you all.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Job 34:29"> But the men rowed hard to bring the ship<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Here the direct object is supplied from context">n</a></sup>back to the dry land, and they could not do so because the sea ⸤was growing more and more tempestuous⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “going/growing and storming”">o</a></sup>against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 115:3; Psalm 135:6; Ephesians 1:11"> So they cried out to Yahweh, and they said, “O Yahweh! Please do not let us perish because of this man’s life, and ⸤do not make us guilty of innocent blood⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “do not give innocent blood on us”">p</a></sup>because you, O Yahweh, did what you wanted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:24; Psalm 93:3–4; Psalm 107:29"> And they picked Jonah up and hurled him into the sea, and the sea ceased from its raging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 50:14; Genesis 8:20; Daniel 6:26"> So the men feared Yahweh ⸤greatly⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “with a great fear”">q</a></sup>and they offered a sacrifice to Yahweh and ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “vowed”">r</a></sup>vows.</span>
</div>
</div>
