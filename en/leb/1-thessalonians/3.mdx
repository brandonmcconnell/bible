## Paul Sends Timothy to Thessalonica

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 17:15; 1 Thessalonians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:17"> Therefore when we could bear it no longer, we determined to be left behind in Athens alone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:22–23; Ephesians 6:21–22; Philippians 2:19–25"> and we sent Timothy, our brother and fellow worker for God in the gospel of Christ, in order to strengthen and to encourage you about your faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Romans 5:3; Acts 9:16"> so that no one would be shaken by these afflictions. For you yourselves know that we are appointed for this,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; 2 Corinthians 8:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 2:2"> for indeed when we were with you we told you beforehand that we were about to be afflicted, just as indeed it happened, and you know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 1:13–14; Matthew 4:3; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> Because of this, I also, when I could endure it no longer, sent in order to know your faith, lest somehow the tempter tempted you and our labor should be in vain.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Timothy’s Positive Report

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 52:7; Proverbs 25:25"> But now, because Timothy has come to us from you and has brought good news to us of your faith and love, and that ⸤you always think kindly of us⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “that you have kindly remembrance of us always”">a</a></sup>, desiring to see us just as also we desire to see you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 John 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> because of this, brothers, we have been comforted because of you in all our distress and affliction through your faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Revelation 3:11"> because now we live, if you stand firm in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 71:14–15; Psalm 96:12–13; 2 Corinthians 9:15"> For what thanks can we repay to God concerning you, because of all the joy with which we rejoice because of you before our God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:11; Philippians 1:25"> night and day praying beyond all measure that we may see your face and complete what is lacking in your faith?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Matthew 6:32; 2 Thessalonians 2:16"> Now may our God and Father himself and our Lord Jesus direct our way to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 John 4:7–16; Matthew 22:39"> and may the Lord cause you to increase and to abound in love for one another and for all, just as also we do for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; Ephesians 5:27"> so that your hearts may be established blameless in holiness before our God and Father at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all his saints.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “his saints. Amen.”">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
