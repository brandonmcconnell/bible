## Paul’s Approach to Ministry in Thessalonica

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 15:2; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> For you yourselves know, brothers, our reception with you, that it was not in vain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 16:22–24; Acts 4:13; Acts 17:2–9"> but after we had already suffered and been mistreated in Philippi, just as you know, we had the courage in our God to speak to you the gospel of God amid much opposition.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 4:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> For our exhortation is not from error or from impurity or with deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:10; Romans 8:27; Galatians 2:7"> but just as we have been approved by God to be entrusted with the gospel, thus we speak, not as pleasing people but God, who examines our hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 1:9; 2 Corinthians 12:17; Acts 20:33"> ⸤For never⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “for neither ever”">a</a></sup>did we come with a word of flattery, just as you know, nor with a pretext of greediness (God is witness),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 1:10; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; John 5:41"> nor seeking glory from people, neither from you nor from others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:11; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> ⸤Although we could have insisted on our own importance⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “being able to be with a claim of importance”">b</a></sup>as apostles of Christ, yet we became infants in your midst, like a nursing mother cherishes her own children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 3:16; 2 Corinthians 12:15; Philippians 2:17"> Longing for you in this way, we determined to share with you not only the gospel of God but also our own souls, because you had become dear to us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; 2 Corinthians 11:9; Acts 18:3"> For you remember, brothers, our labor and hardship: working by night and day in order not to be a burden to any of you, we proclaimed to you the gospel of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> You are witnesses, and so is God, how devoutly and righteously and blamelessly we became to you who believe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:14–15; Hebrews 13:22; 1 Timothy 5:21"> just as you know how we treated each one of you, like a father his own children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; 1 John 2:6; 1 John 1:6–7"> exhorting and consoling you and insisting that you live in a manner worthy of God, who calls you to his own kingdom and glory.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Thessalonians’ Response to Paul’s Message

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Romans 10:17; 1 Peter 1:23"> And because of this we also give thanks to God constantly, that when you received God’s word ⸤that you heard⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “of hearing”">c</a></sup>from us, you accepted it not as the word of men, but as it truly is, the word of God, which also is at work in you who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:4; Hebrews 10:33–34; Hebrews 5:7–8"> For you became imitators, brothers, of the churches of God which are in Judea in Christ Jesus, because you also suffered the same things at the hands of your own people, just as they themselves did also at the hands of the Jews,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:12; Luke 11:48–53; Acts 7:52"> who killed both the Lord Jesus and the prophets, and who persecuted us, and who are not pleasing to God and are opposed to all people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:13; Acts 14:19; Mark 16:16"> hindering us from speaking to the Gentiles in order that they may be saved, so that they always fill up their sins. But wrath has come upon them to the end.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “has come upon them at last”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Desire to Revisit the Thessalonians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Corinthians 5:3; 1 Thessalonians 3:6"> But when we were made orphans by separation from you, brothers, for ⸤a short time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the time of an hour”">e</a></sup>(in face, not in heart), we were even more eager with great desire to see your face,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 15:22; Romans 1:13; Revelation 2:10"> because we wanted to come to you—I, Paul, ⸤on more than one occasion⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “both once and twice”">f</a></sup>—and Satan hindered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Philippians 2:16"> For who is our hope or joy or crown of boasting? Is it not even you, in the presence of our Lord Jesus at his coming?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:14; 1 Corinthians 11:7; Proverbs 17:6"> For you are our glory and joy.</span>
</div>
