## Thanksgiving for the Thessalonian Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Ephesians 1:15–16; Philemon 1:4"> We give thanks to God always concerning all of you, making mention constantly in our prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:13; Revelation 2:19; Galatians 5:6"> because we remember your work of faith and labor of love and steadfastness of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ in the presence of our God and Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Titus 3:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> knowing, brothers dearly loved by God, ⸤that he has chosen you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the choice of you”">a</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Mark 16:20; 1 Corinthians 2:2–5"> because our gospel did not come to you with word only, but also with power and with the Holy Spirit and with much certainty, just as you know what sort of people we became among you for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 1 Corinthians 4:16; Acts 13:52"> And you became imitators of us and of the Lord, receiving the word in much affliction, with the joy of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12"> so that you became an example to all those who believe in Macedonia and in Achaia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Romans 10:14–18; Isaiah 52:7"> for from you the word of the Lord has sounded forth, not only in Macedonia and Achaia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and in Achaia”">b</a></sup>but in every place your faith toward God has gone out, so that we have no need to say anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Galatians 4:8–9"> For they themselves report about us, what sort of welcome we had with you, and how you turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:9; Isaiah 25:8–9; Romans 5:9–10"> and to await his Son from heaven, whom he raised from the dead, Jesus, the one who delivers us from the coming wrath.</span>
</div>
</div>
