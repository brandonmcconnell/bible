## Appeal for Continuing Sanctification

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 2 Peter 3:18"> Finally therefore, brothers, we ask you and appeal to you in the Lord Jesus that, just as you have received from us how it is necessary for you to live and to please God, just as indeed you are living, that you progress even more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Ezekiel 3:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:10"> For you know what commands we gave to you through the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> For this is the will of God, your sanctification: that you abstain from sexual immorality;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:9; Romans 12:1; Hebrews 13:4"> that each of you know how to possess his own vessel in sanctification and honor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Acts 17:30–31; Ephesians 4:17–19"> not in lustful passion, just as also the Gentiles who do not know God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; Exodus 20:17; Romans 12:19"> not to transgress and to exploit his brother in the matter, because the Lord is the one who avenges concerning all these things, just as also we told you beforehand and testified solemnly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Peter 2:9–12"> For God did not call us to impurity, but in holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Luke 10:16; John 12:48"> Therefore the one who rejects this is not rejecting man, but God, who also gives his Holy Spirit to you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Affirmation of Continuing Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Romans 12:10; 1 John 3:23"> But concerning brotherly love, I do not have need to write to you, for you yourselves are taught by God to love one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:12; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> for indeed you are practicing it toward all the brothers in all of Macedonia. But we urge you, brothers, to progress even more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:6; Ephesians 4:28; 1 Peter 3:4"> and to aspire to live a quiet life, and to attend to ⸤your own business⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “your own things”">a</a></sup>and to work with your hands,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “your own hands”">b</a></sup>just as we commanded you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; 1 Peter 2:12; Romans 12:17"> so that you may live decently toward those outside, and may have need of nothing.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Comfort of the Lord’s Return

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 4:15; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> Now we do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, concerning those who have fallen asleep, so that you will not grieve as also the rest, who have no hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Philippians 3:20–21; Isaiah 26:19"> For if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, thus also God will bring those who have fallen asleep through Jesus together with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–53; 2 Corinthians 4:14; 1 Thessalonians 2:19"> For this we say to you by the word of the Lord, that we who are alive, who remain until the Lord’s coming, will not possibly precede those who have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:51–52; Revelation 1:7; Matthew 24:30–31"> For the Lord himself will descend from heaven with a shout of command, with the voice of the archangel and with the trumpet of God, and the dead in Christ will rise first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:52; Revelation 11:12; John 14:3"> Then we who are alive, who remain, will be snatched away at the same time together with them in the clouds for a meeting with the Lord in the air, and thus we will be together with the Lord always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Luke 21:28; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> Therefore comfort one another with these words.</span>
</div>
</div>
