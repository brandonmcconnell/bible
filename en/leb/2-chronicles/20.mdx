## The Prayer of Jehoshaphat

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 83:5–8; Isaiah 16:6; 2 Chronicles 32:1"> And it happened that afterward, the ⸤Moabites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Moab”">a</a></sup>the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">b</a></sup>and some of the Meunites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Hebrew term is difficult; it may also mean “Ammonim”">c</a></sup>came against Jehoshaphat for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 14:7; 1 Samuel 23:29; Joshua 15:62"> And they came and reported it to Jehoshaphat, saying, “A great multitude from beyond the sea, from Aram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “Syria”">d</a></sup>is coming against you. Now behold, they are in Hazazon Tamar” (that is, En Gedi).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; 2 Chronicles 19:3; 1 Samuel 7:6"> Then Jehoshaphat was afraid and set his face to seek Yahweh. And he called for a fast through all Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 50:15; Psalm 60:10–12; Psalm 69:35"> And Judah assembled to seek after Yahweh; even from all the cities of Judah they came to seek Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 19:15–19; 2 Chronicles 6:12–13; 2 Chronicles 34:31"> Then Jehoshaphat stood in the assembly of Judah and Jerusalem in the house of Yahweh before the new courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Daniel 4:17"> And he said, “O Yahweh, God of our ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>are you not God in heaven? Now you rule in all the kingdoms of the nations, and in your hand are power and might, and there is none who can resist against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8; James 2:23; Psalm 44:2"> O, our God, did you yourself not drive out the inhabitants of this land before your people Israel and give it to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “seed”">f</a></sup>of Abraham your friend forever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:10; 2 Chronicles 2:4"> And they lived in it and built for you a sanctuary for your name in it, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:28–30; 2 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Kings 8:33"> ‘If disaster, a sword, punishment, disease, or famine come upon us, we shall stand before this house and before you—for your name is in this house—and cry out to you out of our trouble, then you will hear and save us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 2:9"> And now, look, the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “sons”">g</a></sup>of Moab and Ammon and Mount Seir whom you did not allow Israel to come against when they came from Egypt, from whom they turned aside and did not destroy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 83:3–12; Judges 11:23–24; Jeremiah 18:20"> now behold, they are rewarding us by coming to dispossess us from your possession which you gave us as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 121:1–2; Psalm 25:15; Psalm 141:8"> O our God, will you not judge them, for there is no power in us before this great multitude that is coming against us. Now we do not know what we shall do, for our eyes are upon you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 10:1; Acts 21:5; Deuteronomy 29:10"> And all Judah was standing before Yahweh, and also their little children, their wives, and their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:1; 2 Chronicles 24:20; Acts 10:31"> Then the Spirit of Yahweh came upon Jahaziel, the son of Zechariah, the son of Benaiah, the son of Jeiel, the son of Mattaniah, the Levite from the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “sons”">h</a></sup>of Asaph, in the midst of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 14:13–14"> And he said, “Listen, all Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem and King Jehoshaphat, thus says Yahweh to you: ‘Do not be afraid and do not be dismayed before this great multitude, for the battle is not yours, but God’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Tomorrow go down against them. Behold, they are coming up the ascent of Ziz. And you will find them at the end of the valley facing the desert of Jeruel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–14; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Isaiah 41:10"> There will be no need for you to fight in this battle. Take your stand! Stand and see the deliverance of Yahweh among you, O Judah and Jerusalem! Do not fear and do not be dismayed. Tomorrow go out before them and Yahweh will be with you.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; 2 Chronicles 7:3; Genesis 24:26"> Then Jehoshaphat bowed his face<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “nostrils”">i</a></sup>down to the ground, and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem fell before Yahweh to worship Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:16–22; 1 Chronicles 16:42; 1 Chronicles 16:5"> Then the Levites, from the ⸤Kohathites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons of the Kohathites”">j</a></sup>and from the ⸤Korahites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons of the Korahites”">k</a></sup>stood up to praise Yahweh the God of Israel with an exceedingly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “above”">l</a></sup>loud voice.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God Delivers Judah and Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 8:31; John 14:1; John 11:40"> And they rose up early in the morning and went out to the desert of Tekoa. And when they went out, Jehoshaphat stood and said, “Hear me, O Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem! Believe in Yahweh your God, and you shall be established! Believe in his prophets, and you shall succeed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 107:1; Psalm 106:1; 2 Chronicles 5:13"> And when he had taken counsel with the people, he appointed singers to Yahweh who were giving praise to the splendor of his holiness when they went out before the army. And they said, “Give thanks to Yahweh, for his loyal love is everlasting!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:20; Isaiah 19:2; Ezekiel 38:21"> And ⸤when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “on time” or “at the time”">m</a></sup>they began with singing and praise, Yahweh set ambushes against the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “sons”">n</a></sup>of Ammon, Moab, and Mount Seir who were coming against Judah. And they were defeated</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Ezekiel 35:2–3"> when the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “sons”">o</a></sup>of Ammon and Moab rose up against the inhabitants of Mount Seir to destroy and demolish them. And when the inhabitants of Seir had made an end of them, each helped to destroy his comrade.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36; Psalm 110:6; 1 Chronicles 5:22"> And Judah came against the watchtower of the wilderness. And they looked at the crowd, and behold, corpses were lying on the ground. There was none who had escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:19–20; 2 Kings 7:9–16; Numbers 31:15"> When Jehoshaphat came with his people to plunder their booty, they found among them in abundance possessions, corpses, and precious items. And they took plunder for themselves ⸤until they could carry no more⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “until not carrying”">p</a></sup>And they were three days in plundering the booty, for it was abundant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 17:15; Genesis 32:20; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> And on the fourth day, they assembled in the Valley of Berakah, for there they blessed Yahweh. Therefore the name of that place is called the Valley of Berakah up to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:43; Hebrews 6:20; Psalm 30:1"> And all the men of Judah and Jerusalem returned, and Jehoshaphat was at their head in returning to Jerusalem with joy, for Yahweh caused them to rejoice over their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 14:2–3; Psalm 57:8; Psalm 92:3"> And they came to Jerusalem with harps, stringed instruments, and trumpets to the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; 2 Kings 7:6; Exodus 23:27"> And the fear of God came upon all the kingdoms of the earth when they heard that Yahweh had fought against the enemies of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Samuel 7:1"> So the kingdom of Jehoshaphat was at rest, since his God gave rest to him all around.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The End of Jehoshaphat’s Reign

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 22:41–44"> So Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah. He was thirty-five years old when he began to reign. And he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Azubah, the daughter of Shilhi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:2–5; 1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11–13"> And he walked in the way of his father Asa and did not turn aside from it, to do what was right in the eyes of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:14"> Only the high places were not removed. The people still had not yet set their heart on the God of their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “fathers”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 16:1; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 16:7"> Now the rest of the words of Jehoshaphat, from the first to the last, behold, they are written in the chronicles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “words”">r</a></sup>of Jehu the son of Hanani, which are recorded in the scroll of the kings of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48–49; 2 Kings 1:2–16"> And afterward Jehoshaphat the king of Judah joined with Ahaziah the king of Israel, who acted wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 10:22; 2 Chronicles 9:21; 1 Kings 9:26"> He joined with him to build ships to go to Tarshish. And they built the ships in Ezion Geber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 2 Chronicles 9:21"> Then Eliezer the son of Dodavahu of Mareshah prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying, “Because of your joining with Ahaziah, Yahweh will break down your works.” And the ships were destroyed and were not able to go to Tarshish.</span>
</div>
</div>
