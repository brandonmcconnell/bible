## The Reign of Jehoram

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 22:50; 2 Kings 8:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> And Jehoshaphat slept with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fathers”">a</a></sup>and was buried with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “fathers”">b</a></sup>in the city of David, and Jehoram his son reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:6; 2 Chronicles 23:2; 2 Chronicles 24:5"> Now he had brothers, the sons of Jehoshaphat: Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah. All these were the sons of Jehoshaphat, king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:23; Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 2 Chronicles 11:5"> And their father gave to them many gifts of silver, gold, and valuables, with fortified cities in Judah, but he gave the kingdom to Jehoram, for he was the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:5; 2 Chronicles 21:17; Genesis 4:8"> When Jehoram ascended to the kingdom of his father, he strengthened himself and murdered all his brothers with the sword, and even some of the princes of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 8:17–22"> Jehoram was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; 1 Kings 12:28–30; 2 Kings 8:18"> And he walked in the way of the kings of Israel as the house of Ahab had done, for the daughter of Ahab was his wife. And he did evil in the sight of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; Psalm 132:17–18; 2 Samuel 7:12–17"> But Yahweh was not willing to destroy the house of David on account of the covenant that he had ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “cut”">c</a></sup>with David and since he had promised<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “said”">d</a></sup>to give a lamp to him and to his descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “sons”">e</a></sup>⸤forever⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “all the days”">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:10; 2 Chronicles 20:22–23; Genesis 27:40"> In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah, and they set up a king of their own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Jehoram passed over with his commanders and all the chariots with him, and when night came he struck Edom who was all around him and the commanders of his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Jeremiah 2:13; 2 Chronicles 13:10"> So Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah until this day. Then Libnah also revolted at that time from under his hand, because he had forsaken Yahweh, the God of his ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 20:5; 1 Kings 11:7; Leviticus 17:7"> Moreover, he made high places in the hill country of Judah, and he enticed the inhabitants of Jerusalem to be unfaithful, and he led Judah astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:2–5; 2 Chronicles 17:3–4; 2 Kings 2:1"> And a letter from Elijah the prophet came to him, saying, “Thus says Yahweh, the God of David your father: ‘Because you have not walked in the ways of Jehoshaphat your father or in the ways of Asa, the king of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:4; 2 Chronicles 21:11; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> but have walked in the way of the kings of Israel and have enticed Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem to be unfaithful like the unfaithfulness of the house of Ahab, and have also murdered your brothers of the house of your father who were better than you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Exodus 20:5; Hosea 5:11"> behold, Yahweh is inflicting a great plague on your people, your children, your wives, and all your possessions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; Acts 1:18; Deuteronomy 28:67"> and you yourself will be afflicted with great illness, with sickness in your bowels, until your bowels come out on account of the illness, day by day.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:11; 2 Chronicles 33:11; 1 Kings 11:14"> And Yahweh stirred up the spirit of the Philistines and the Arabs who were ⸤near⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “at the hand of”">h</a></sup>the Cushites against Jehoram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:1; 2 Chronicles 22:6; 2 Chronicles 25:23"> And they came up against Judah and invaded it and carried away all the possessions found in the house of the king, and also his sons and his wives, so that no son was left to him except Jehoahaz his youngest son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:15; Acts 12:23; 2 Kings 9:29"> And after all this Yahweh afflicted him in his bowels with an illness for which there was no cure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Jeremiah 34:5"> And it happened that ⸤after many days, at the end of two years⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for days from days and as the time went out to the end for two days”">i</a></sup>his bowels came out because of his illness, and he died in terrible agony. And his people did not make a fire for him like the fire for his ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “fathers”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18; 2 Chronicles 28:27; Jeremiah 22:28"> He was thirty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem. And he departed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “went”">k</a></sup>with no one’s regret. And they buried him in the city of David, but not in the burial sites of the kings.</span>
</div>
</div>
