## The End of Asa’s Reign

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; 2 Chronicles 15:5; 1 Kings 12:27"> In the thirty-sixth year of the reign of Asa, Baasha the king of Israel went up against Judah. And he built Ramah in order not to allow anyone to go out or come in to Asa, king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 12:18; 2 Chronicles 28:21; 2 Kings 18:15"> Then Asa brought out silver and gold from the storehouses of the house of Yahweh and the house of the king, and he sent them to Ben-Hadad, king of Aram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “Syria”">a</a></sup>who lived in Damascus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 15:4; 2 Chronicles 18:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> “There is a covenant between me and you, as there was between my father and your father. Look, I am sending you silver and gold. Go, break your covenant with Baasha, king of Israel, that he might withdraw from me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Chronicles 17:12; 1 Kings 15:20"> And Ben-Hadad listened to King Asa, and he sent commanders of his troops that were with him against the cities of Israel. And they struck Ijon, Dan, Abel-Maim, and all the storage cities of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And it happened that when Baasha heard this, he stopped building Ramah and ceased his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; Joshua 15:38; Isaiah 10:29"> Then King Asa took all of Judah, and they carried away the stones of Ramah and its timber with which Baasha had been building, and he built Geba and Mizpah with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 16:1; Psalm 146:3–6"> And at that time Hanani the seer came to Asa king of Judah and said to him, “On account of your reliance upon the king of Aram,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “Syria”">b</a></sup>and since you did not rely on Yahweh your God, therefore the troops of the king of Aram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “Syria”">c</a></sup>escaped from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:3; 2 Chronicles 13:16; 2 Chronicles 16:7"> Were not the Cushites and Libyans a mighty army with very abundant chariots and horsemen? And because of your reliance on Yahweh he gave them into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Proverbs 15:3; Jeremiah 16:17"> For the eyes of Yahweh roam throughout all the earth to strengthen those whose heart is fully devoted to him. You have been foolish in this, for from now on you will have wars.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:26; Acts 16:23–24; 2 Samuel 12:13"> Then Asa was angry with the seer and put him in ⸤the prison⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the house of the stocks”">d</a></sup>for he was enraged with him concerning this. And Asa oppressed some of the people at that time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 35:27"> Now behold, the words of Asa from the first to the last, behold, they are written in the scroll of the kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; 2 Chronicles 28:22; 1 Chronicles 10:14"> And in the thirty-ninth year of his reign, he fell ⸤severely⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “until his illness unto severity”">e</a></sup>ill in his feet. But even in his illness he did not seek Yahweh, but only among the healers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24"> And Asa slept with his ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “fathers”">f</a></sup>And he died in the forty-first year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 50:2; 2 Chronicles 21:19; Jeremiah 34:5"> And they buried him in his burial site, which had been cut out for him in the city of David. And they laid him on the bier which they had filled with all kinds of spices made by the perfumers as a fragment ointment. And they made a great fire in his honor.</span>
</div>
</div>
