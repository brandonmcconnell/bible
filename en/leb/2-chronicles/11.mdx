## Rehoboam’s Kingdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 12:21–24; Psalm 33:10; Psalm 33:16"> When Rehoboam came to Jerusalem he assembled the house of Judah and Benjamin, one hundred and eighty thousand ⸤chosen warriors⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “chosen makers of war”">a</a></sup>to fight against Israel to restore the kingdom to Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 2 Chronicles 8:14"> But the word of Yahweh came to Shemaiah, the man of God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 49:28; Revelation 7:4–8"> “Say to Rehoboam, son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Chronicles 10:15–16"> ‘Thus says Yahweh: “Do not go up, and do not fight against your brothers. Return, each man to his house, for this matter has come from me.” ’ ” So they obeyed the words of Yahweh and turned back from going against Jeroboam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; Isaiah 22:8–11"> And Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem, and he built cities as strongholds in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:5; Amos 1:1; 1 Chronicles 4:32"> He built Bethlehem, Etah, Tekoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:58"> Beth-Zur, Socoh, Adullam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 54:1; Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 23:19"> Gath, Mareshah, Ziph,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 15:39; Joshua 15:35; Joshua 10:5"> Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Joshua 15:33; Joshua 14:14"> Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron, fortified cities that are in Judah and in Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:19; Isaiah 22:10–11; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> And he strengthened the fortifications and put commanders in them, along with stores of food, olive oil, and wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; 2 Chronicles 26:14–15; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> And in all the cities he put shields and spears, and he greatly strengthened them. So he had Judah and Benjamin.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Priests and Levites Come to Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Now the priests and the Levites who were throughout all Israel took their stand with him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, Rehoboam">b</a></sup>from all their territories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 35:2–5; 1 Kings 12:28–33"> For the Levites left their pasturelands and their property and came to Judah and to Jerusalem, for Jeroboam and his sons had rejected them from serving as priests to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:33"> And he appointed his own priests for the high places and for the goat idols and for the bull calves that he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; Acts 11:23; Psalm 84:5–7"> Then after them, from all the tribes of Israel, those who set their heart to seek Yahweh, the God of Israel, came to Jerusalem to offer to Yahweh, the God of their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 2 Chronicles 1:1–12; 2 Chronicles 7:17–19"> And they strengthened the kingdom of Judah and made Rehoboam, the son of Solomon, secure for three years, for they walked in the way of David and Solomon for three years.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rehoboam’s Harem and Family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Chronicles 27:18; 1 Samuel 17:13"> Then Rehoboam took to himself as a wife Mahalath, the daughter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This follows the reading tradition (Qere) ; the Kethib reading has “son”">d</a></sup>of Yerimot son of David, and of Abihail the daughter of Eliab the son of Jesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And she bore to him sons: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2; 2 Chronicles 11:21; 2 Chronicles 12:16"> And after her he took Maacah the daughter of Absalom, who bore to him Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 8:30; Deuteronomy 17:17; 1 Kings 11:3"> And Rehoboam loved Maacah the daughter of Absalom more than all his wives and concubines (for he took eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and he fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:15–17; 1 Chronicles 29:1; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> And Rehoboam appointed Abijah the son of Maacah as chief and crown prince over his brothers, in order to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Genesis 25:6; Luke 16:8"> And he dealt wisely and distributed some of his sons throughout all the land of Judah and Benjamin, through all the fortified cities, and gave abundant provisions to them and obtained many wives for them.</span>
</div>
</div>
