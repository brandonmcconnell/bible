## Jehoshaphat’s Alliance with Ahab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:5; 2 Chronicles 21:6; 2 Chronicles 19:2"> Now Jehoshaphat had much wealth and honor, and he became son-in-law to Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 1:9"> And ⸤after some years⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “at the end of years”">a</a></sup>he went down to Ahab in Samaria. And Ahab slaughtered many sheep and cattle for him and for the people who were with him, and urged him to go up against Ramoth-Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 22:4; 2 Kings 3:7; 2 John 1:10–11"> And Ahab the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, king of Judah, “Will you go up with me to Ramoth-Gilead?” And he answered him, “I am as you are, and my people are as your people with you in war.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:2; 2 Samuel 5:19; 2 Samuel 2:1"> Then Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, “Seek ⸤first of all⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “as the day”">b</a></sup>the word of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3; Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> Then the king of Israel gathered the prophets, four hundred men. And he said to them, “Shall we go to Ramoth-Gilead to battle, or shall we cease?” And they said, “Go up, since God will give it into the hand of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 22:7–9; 2 Kings 3:11–13"> Then Jehoshaphat said, “Is there not here another prophet of Yahweh that we might inquire of him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Micah 2:7; 1 Kings 21:20"> Then the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “There is yet one man by whom to seek Yahweh, but I hate him, because he never prophesies good concerning me, but ⸤always⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “all his days”">c</a></sup>disaster. He is Micaiah the son of Imlah.” Then Jehoshaphat said, “Let not the king say thus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 22:9; Isaiah 39:7; 2 Chronicles 18:25–26"> Then the king of Israel called to a eunuch and said, “Quickly bring Micaiah the son of Imlah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9; Daniel 7:9; Matthew 19:28"> Now the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat the king of Judah were each sitting on his throne, clothed in robes. And they were sitting at the threshing floor at the entrance of the gate of Samaria, and all the prophets were prophesying before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; Zechariah 1:18–21; Jeremiah 28:2–3"> And Zedekiah the son of Kenaanah made for himself horns of iron, and he said, “Thus says Yahweh: ‘With these you will gore Aram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “Syria”">d</a></sup>to their destruction.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 24:24–25; 2 Chronicles 18:33–34; Jude 1:16"> And all the prophets prophesied thus, saying, “Go up to Ramoth-Gilead and triumph! Yahweh will give it into the hand of the king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 2:6; Psalm 10:11; Micah 2:11"> Then the messenger who went to call Micaiah said to him, “Behold, the words of the prophets are good with one voice to the king. Please let your word be as one with them and speak good.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 22:35; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18–20"> But Micaiah said, “As Yahweh lives, only what my God has said, that will I speak.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:15; Lamentations 4:21; Amos 4:4–5"> And when he had come to the king, the king said to him, “Micaiah, shall we go to Ramoth-Gilead to war or shall I cease?” And he said, “Go up and triumph! They shall be given into your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 26:63; 1 Samuel 14:24; 1 Kings 22:16"> But the king said to him, “How many times shall I make you swear that you speak nothing except the truth in the name of Yahweh?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:5–6; Numbers 27:17"> Then he said, “I saw all Israel scattered upon the mountains like sheep that had no shepherd. And Yahweh said, ‘These have no masters; let them return to his own house in peace.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:7; 1 Kings 22:18; Proverbs 29:1"> Then the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “Did I not say to you he would not prophesy good concerning me, but only disaster?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Isaiah 6:1–5; 1 Kings 22:19–23"> Then Micaiah said, “Therefore hear the word of Yahweh: I saw Yahweh sitting upon his throne with all the host of heaven standing on his right and on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 11:5"> And Yahweh said, ‘Who will entice Ahab the king of Israel that he would go up and fall at Ramoth-Gilead?’ And one said this, and another said that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:6; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> Then a spirit came forth and stood before Yahweh and said, ‘I will entice him.’ Then Yahweh said to him, ‘By what means?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:44; 1 Kings 22:21–22; 1 John 4:6"> Then he said, ‘I will go forth and will be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.’ And he said, ‘You will entice him and will also succeed. Go out and do so.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Ezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16"> So now, behold, Yahweh has put a spirit of deception into the mouths of these your prophets. Yahweh has spoken disaster against you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Jeremiah 20:2; 2 Chronicles 18:10"> Then Zedekiah the son of Kenaanah came near and struck Micaiah on the cheek and said, “⸤Which way⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “Where is this the way”">e</a></sup>did the Spirit of Yahweh come from me to speak to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:21–22; Isaiah 26:20; Jeremiah 28:16–17"> Then Micaiah said, “Behold, you will see on that day when you go into a private room to hide yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:8; 2 Chronicles 34:8; Jeremiah 38:6–7"> Then the king of Israel said, “Take Micaiah and return him to Amon the commander of the city and to Jehoash the son of the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:10; Psalm 80:5; Luke 23:2"> and say, ‘Thus says the king: “Put this one into the prison house, and let him eat ⸤a meager ration of bread and water⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “bread a short ration and water a short ration”">f</a></sup>until I return in peace.” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:45–46; Amos 9:10; Micah 1:2"> And Micaiah said, “If you surely return in peace Yahweh has not spoken with me.” Then he said, “Hear, peoples, all of them!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Ahab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 22:29–33"> Then the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went up to Ramoth-Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:8; Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 26:25"> And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “I will disguise myself and go into battle, but you, wear your garments.” So the king of Israel disguised himself, and they went to war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:11; Deuteronomy 1:17; 1 Kings 20:42"> Now the king of Aram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “Syria”">g</a></sup>had commanded the commanders of his chariots,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">h</a></sup>saying, “Do not fight with the small or the great, but only with the king of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:14; Psalm 118:13; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And it happened that when the commanders of the chariots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">i</a></sup>saw Jehoshaphat, then they said, “It is the king of Israel!” And they turned against him to fight. Then Jehoshaphat cried out and Yahweh helped him. God drove them away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 22:33"> And it happened that when the commanders of the chariots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">j</a></sup>saw that it was not the king of Israel, they turned back from following him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:23; 2 Samuel 15:11; 1 Kings 22:34–35"> Now a certain man drew the bow ⸤at random⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “in his innocence”">k</a></sup>and struck the king of Israel between the soldering of his breastplate armor. Then he said to the chariot driver, “⸤Turn around⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “Turn away your hand”">l</a></sup>get me away from the war camp, for I am wounded.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 13:21; 2 Chronicles 18:27; Proverbs 28:17"> And the battle went on that day, and the king of Israel was propped up in the chariot facing Aram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “Syria”">m</a></sup>until the evening. And he died at the time of the going down of the sun.</span>
</div>
