## Solomon Blesses the People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 18:8–11; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 20:21"> Then Solomon said, “Yahweh has decided<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “said”">a</a></sup>to dwell in the deep gloom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4–6; Psalm 135:21; 1 Chronicles 28:6"> But I have built for you an exalted house, even a place for you to dwell in forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:14; 2 Chronicles 29:29; 1 Kings 8:55–61"> Then the king turned around<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “turned his face”">b</a></sup>and blessed all the assembly of Israel, and all the assembly of Israel were standing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 72:18–19; Matthew 24:35; Psalm 68:32–35"> And he said, “Blessed be Yahweh, the God of Israel, who spoke with his mouth with David my father, and with his hands he has fulfilled it, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:24; 1 Kings 8:16; 1 Samuel 13:13–14"> ‘From the day that I brought my people out of the land of Egypt I did not choose a city among all the tribes of Israel to build a house in order for my name to be there. Nor did I choose a man to be leader over my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:4; Psalm 132:13; 2 Chronicles 12:13"> But I have chosen my name to be there in Jerusalem, and I have chosen David to be over my people Israel.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:1; 1 Kings 5:3; 1 Chronicles 22:7"> Now, it was in the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of Yahweh, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Mark 14:8; 1 Kings 8:18–21"> But Yahweh said to David my father, ‘Because it was in your heart to build a house for my name, you did well that it was in your heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12–13; 1 Chronicles 17:4; 1 Chronicles 17:11–12"> but you yourself will not build the house, but your son who will proceed from your loins; he himself will build the house for my name.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Chronicles 17:11"> Now Yahweh has fulfilled his word that he spoke. I have risen in the place of David my father and am seated on the throne of Israel as Yahweh has spoken. I have built the house for the name of Yahweh, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:10; 2 Chronicles 5:7; 1 Kings 8:21"> And I have there the ark in which is the covenant of Yahweh that he ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “cut”">c</a></sup>with the ⸤Israelites⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon’s Prayer to Dedicate the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 63:4; Isaiah 50:10; Job 11:13"> Then Solomon stood before the altar of Yahweh before all the assembly of Israel and spread out his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 8:54; Nehemiah 8:4; Psalm 95:6"> (For Solomon had made a platform of bronze five cubits long and five cubits wide and three cubits high, and placed it in the midst of the outer court.) And he stood upon it, then knelt down on his knees before the whole assembly of Israel and spread out his hands to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5"> And he said, “O Yahweh, God of Israel, there is none like you, a God in the heavens and on the earth, keeping the covenant and loyal love with your servants who walk before you with all their heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9–10; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 8:24"> that you have kept for your servant David my father what you spoke to him. You spoke with your mouth, and by your hand you have fulfilled it on this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; John 15:14–15; 2 Chronicles 7:18"> And now, O Yahweh, God of Israel, keep for your servant David, my father, what you have spoken to him, saying, ‘⸤You shall not lack⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “There shall not be cut off”">e</a></sup>a man to sit on the throne of Israel before me, if only your sons keep their way, to walk in my law as you have walked before me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:5; Isaiah 41:17; 2 Chronicles 6:4"> And now, O Yahweh, God of Israel, let your word that you have spoken to your servant David be confirmed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:6; Isaiah 66:1; Psalm 139:7–10"> “But will God indeed dwell with humankind upon the earth? Look, the heavens and ⸤the highest heavens⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the heavens of the heavens”">f</a></sup>cannot contain you! Surely then this house that I have built will not contain you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:28; Luke 18:1–7; John 17:20"> But turn to the prayer of your servant and to his plea, O Yahweh my God, to hearken to the pleading and to the prayer that your servant is praying before you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 34:15; Daniel 6:10; Psalm 121:5"> that your eyes may be open day and night to this house, to the place that you have promised to place your name there, that you may hear the prayer that your servant has prayed to this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Micah 7:18; Daniel 9:19; Isaiah 43:25"> And listen to the pleas of your servant and your people Israel when they pray toward this place, that you yourself might hear from the place of your dwelling, from the heavens, that you might hear and forgive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 5:19–22"> “If a man sins against his neighbor and ⸤makes him swear an oath⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “places upon him an oath to swear an oath”">g</a></sup>and comes with an oath before your altar in this house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; Proverbs 17:15; Jeremiah 51:56"> then may you yourself hear from the heavens and act and judge your servants, to repay the guilty by bringing his way upon his head and to vindicate the righteous by giving to him according to his righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; 2 Kings 17:7–18; Joshua 7:8"> “And if your people Israel are defeated before an enemy because they have sinned against you and they repent and confess your name and pray and plead before you in this house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:6–13; Ezra 1:1–6; Genesis 13:15"> then may you yourself hear from the heavens and forgive the sin of your people Israel and again bring them back to the land that you gave to them and to their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “fathers”">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; Deuteronomy 11:17; Leviticus 26:19"> “When the heavens are stopped up and there is no rain because they have sinned against you and they pray to this place and confess your name and turn back from their sin when you humble them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35–36; Jeremiah 5:24; Zechariah 10:1"> then may you yourself hear from the heavens and forgive the sin of your servants and your people Israel, when you teach them the good way in which they should walk, and give rain upon your land that you have given to your people as an inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 9:3–11; Joel 2:25; Ruth 1:1"> “If there is a famine in the land; if there is disease, blight, mildew, locust, and caterpillar; if it happens that his enemy lays siege to him in the land and his gates; any kind of plague, any kind of disease,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalm 50:15; Psalm 32:2–6; 2 Chronicles 6:12–13"> then any prayer, any plea that is made by any person and by all your people Israel, each one who knows his own affliction and his own anguish and who spreads out his hands to this house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; 1 Samuel 16:7; Revelation 2:23"> then may you yourself hear from the heavens, your dwelling place, and forgive and give to each according to all his ways, since you know the heart of the children of humankind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Job 28:28; Psalm 128:1"> that they may fear you and walk in your ways all the days that they are alive upon the face of the land that you have given to our ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “fathers”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–7; 1 Kings 8:41–43; Matthew 8:10–11"> “And also, with respect to the foreigner, he who is not of your people Israel, but comes from a distant land, for the sake of your great name and your powerful hand and outstretched arm, when he comes and prays to this house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; 1 Kings 8:16; Revelation 11:15"> then may you yourself hear from the heavens, from your dwelling place, and do according to all that the foreigner asks of you, so that all peoples of earth will know of your name and fear you, as do your people Israel, and that they may know that your name ⸤possesses⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “is called over”">j</a></sup>this house that I have built.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:6–13; 2 Chronicles 18:31; 2 Chronicles 20:4"> “When your people go out to battle against their enemy according to the way that you have sent them, and they pray to you toward this city that you have chosen and the house that I have built for your name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Psalm 9:3–4; Jeremiah 5:28"> then may you hear their prayer and their pleas from the heavens and uphold their case.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2; 1 John 1:8–10"> When they sin against you—for there is no person who does not sin—and you become angry with them and place them before their enemy that they carry them away as captives to a land far or near,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:18–20; Daniel 9:5–11; Isaiah 64:6–12"> but ⸤if they repent⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “if they turn back to their heart”">k</a></sup>in the land where they were taken captive and ⸤repent⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “turn back”">l</a></sup>and beg you for mercy in the land of their captivity, saying, ‘We have sinned and acted perversely and done wickedly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11–13; Daniel 6:10; Daniel 9:3–4"> and we return to you,’ with their whole heart and with all their inmost being in the land of their captivity where they took them captive, and if they pray toward their land that you have given to their ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “fathers”">m</a></sup>and the city that you have chosen and to the house that I have built for your name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Micah 7:18–20; 2 Chronicles 6:35; Psalm 25:18"> then may you hear their prayer and their pleas from the heavens, from your dwelling place, and uphold their case and forgive your people who sinned against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:15; Psalm 116:2; Nehemiah 1:6"> Now, O my God, please let your eyes be open and your ears attentive to the prayer of this place!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 1 Chronicles 28:2; Psalm 132:8–10"> “And now arise, O Yahweh God, to your resting place, you and the ark of your strength! O Yahweh God, let the priests be clothed with salvation, and let your holy ones rejoice in your goodness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Psalm 132:1; Isaiah 61:1"> O Yahweh God, do not turn away the face of your anointed one! Remember the loyal love of your servant, David!”</span>
</div>
</div>
