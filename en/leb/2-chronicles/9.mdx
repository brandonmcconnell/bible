## Solomon and the Queen of Sheba

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; 1 Kings 10:1–13"> Now the queen of Sheba heard of the fame of Solomon, and she came to Jerusalem to test Solomon with hard questions, with much wealth and camels, bearing spices and much gold and precious stones. And she came to Solomon and told him all that was ⸤on her mind⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “in her heart”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:15; 1 Kings 3:12; Mark 4:34"> And Solomon ⸤answered all her questions⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “made known to her all her words”">b</a></sup>Not a word was hidden from Solomon that he did not answer for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–4; 1 Kings 10:3; 1 Kings 6:1–7"> When the queen of Sheba saw the wisdom of Solomon and the house he had built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 12:37; 1 Kings 4:22–23; Psalm 143:7"> and the food of his table, and the position of his officers, the duty of his servants and their clothing, his cupbearers and their clothing, and his burnt offerings that he offered at the house of Yahweh, there was no longer any breath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “spirit”">c</a></sup>in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 10:6"> And she said to the king, “The word which I heard in my land concerning your words and your wisdom is true,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 10:7; 2 Chronicles 9:5; 1 Kings 4:34"> but I did not believe their words until I came and saw with my own eyes. And behold, half the greatness of your wisdom was not reported to me; you surpass the rumors that I had heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 27:4; Proverbs 3:14; Proverbs 10:21"> Blessed are your men, and blessed are these your servants who stand before you regularly and hear your wisdom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11; 1 Chronicles 29:23; Isaiah 32:1–2"> Blessed be Yahweh your God who took delight in you, to put you on his throne as king for Yahweh your God! Because your God loved Israel, he established him forever and has put you over them as king to do justice and righteousness!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:1; Psalm 72:10; Psalm 72:15"> And she gave to the king one hundred and twenty talents of gold and a very great amount of spices and precious stones. And there were no spices such as those that the queen of Sheba gave to King Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:18; 1 Kings 10:11; 1 Kings 10:22"> Moreover the servants of Hiram and the servants of Solomon who brought gold from Ophir brought algum wood and precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 10:12; 1 Chronicles 23:5; Psalm 92:1–3"> And the king made steps for the house of Yahweh and the house of the king, and lyres and harps for the singers from the algum wood. And there was not seen anything like them before in the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:13; Ephesians 3:20; Psalm 20:4"> Now King Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba every desired thing she asked, besides what she had brought to the king. Then she turned and went to her land, she and her servants.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon’s Great Wealth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 72:15; Psalm 68:29; 1 Kings 10:14–28"> And it happened that the weight of the gold that came to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 68:29; Psalm 72:10"> besides the gold and silver the traders and merchants brought, and all the kings of Arabia and the governors of the land brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> And King Solomon made two hundred shields of beaten gold; six hundred shekels of beaten gold went into each shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2"> And he made three hundred small shields of beaten gold; three hundred shekels went into each small shield. And the king put them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 10:18–20; Psalm 45:8; Revelation 20:11"> And the king made a large ivory throne and overlaid it with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Revelation 5:5; Numbers 23:24"> And the throne had six steps, and a footstool of gold was attached to the throne. And there were armrests ⸤on each side⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from this and from that”">d</a></sup>And by ⸤the seat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the place of sitting”">e</a></sup>were two lions standing beside the armrests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Revelation 21:12"> And twelve lions were standing there on each of the six steps ⸤on each end⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from this and from that”">f</a></sup>And there was nothing like it made in all the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 10:21; Esther 1:7; 2 Chronicles 9:27"> And all the drinking vessels of King Solomon were of gold, and all the vessels of the House of the Forest of Lebanon were of pure gold. Silver was not valued as anything in the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Job 39:13; 1 Kings 10:22"> For the ships of the king went to Tarshish with the servants of Huram.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="This is the spelling in Hebrew, though many translations have “Hiram”">g</a></sup>Once ⸤every three years⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “for three years”">h</a></sup>the ships of Tarshish came carrying gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:12; Psalm 89:27; 1 Kings 4:30–31"> Thus King Solomon was greater than all the kings of the earth in wealth and wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:5; James 3:17; Proverbs 2:6"> And all the kings of the earth sought the face of Solomon to hear his wisdom that God put in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 10:25; Job 42:11; Psalm 72:10"> And each of them brought his gifts, objects of silver, objects of gold, cloaks, myrrh, spices, horses, and mules, as an amount year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 4:26; 1 Kings 10:26; 2 Chronicles 1:14"> And Solomon had four thousand stalls for horses and chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen. And he stationed them in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 4:21; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 4:24"> And he ruled over all the kings from the River<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, the Euphrates">i</a></sup>to the land of the Philistines, to the boundary of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:15–17; Isaiah 9:10; 1 Kings 10:27–29"> And the king made silver in Jerusalem as stones, and he made cedar as the sycamore trees that are in the Shephelah for abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:16; 1 Kings 10:28; Isaiah 31:1"> And the horses were imported from Egypt and from all lands for Solomon.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Solomon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:29; 1 Kings 11:41–43; 1 Kings 11:29"> As for the remainder of the words of Solomon from the first to the last, are they not written in the chronicles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “words”">j</a></sup>of Nathan the prophet and in the prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite, and in the visions of Iddo the seer, concerning Jeroboam the son of Nebat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 11:42–43"> And Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 1:21"> And Solomon slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “fathers”">k</a></sup>and they buried him in the city of David his father. And Rehoboam his son reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
