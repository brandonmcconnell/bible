## The Ark Installed in the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 22:14; 2 Samuel 8:11"> When all the work that Solomon did for the house of Yahweh was finished, Solomon brought the holy objects of David his father: the silver, the gold, and all the objects he had put into the storehouses of the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12; 2 Chronicles 1:4; Numbers 10:33"> Then Solomon assembled all the elders of Israel and all the heads of the tribes, the leaders of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">a</a></sup>for the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>to Jerusalem in order to bring up the ark of the covenant of Yahweh from the city of David, which is Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:2; 2 Chronicles 7:8–10; Leviticus 23:34–36"> And all the men of Israel assembled before the king at the feast that is in the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 8:3; Joshua 3:6; Joshua 6:6"> And all the elders of Israel came, and the Levites took up the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6; 2 Chronicles 1:3; 1 Kings 8:4"> And they brought up the ark, the tent of assembly,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “meeting”">c</a></sup>and all the holy objects that were in the tent; the Levitical priests brought them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:21; 2 Samuel 6:13; 1 Chronicles 16:1–2"> And King Solomon and the whole community of Israel that was assembled before him were before the ark, sacrificing sheep and cattle that could not be counted nor numbered for abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 132:8; 1 Kings 6:23–28; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> Then the priests brought up the ark of the covenant of Yahweh to its place in the inner sanctuary of the house, into ⸤the most holy place⸥, underneath the wings of the cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:12–15; Numbers 4:6; Exodus 37:3–5"> The cherubim were spreading their wings over the place of the ark, so that the cherubim were covering the ark and over its poles from above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8–9"> But the poles were so long that the ends of the poles from the ark were seen from before the inner sanctuary, but they could not be seen from the outside. And they are there until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; 2 Chronicles 6:11; Deuteronomy 10:2–5"> There was nothing in the ark but the two stone tablets that Moses had put inside at Horeb on which Yahweh had ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “cut”">d</a></sup>a covenant with the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>when they went out from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:4; 2 Chronicles 29:5; Job 1:5"> And when the priests came out of the sanctuary (for all the priests who were present<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “who were found”">f</a></sup>sanctified themselves without keeping their divisions)</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:24; Psalm 68:25; 1 Chronicles 6:39"> and all the Levitical singers—Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, their sons, their brothers, dressed in fine linen, with cymbals, harps, and stringed instruments—they were standing to the east of the altar. And with them outside were one hundred and twenty priests who were trumpet blowers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 3:11; Jeremiah 33:11; 2 Chronicles 7:3"> And it was the primary duty of the trumpeters and singers to make themselves heard with one voice, to praise and give thanks to Yahweh. And when a sound from the trumpets, cymbals, and other instruments of song was raised to Yahweh—for he is good, because his loyal love is everlasting—then the house, the house of Yahweh, was filled with a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 40:35; 1 Kings 8:11; Isaiah 6:1–4"> And the priests were not able to stand to minister because of the cloud, for the glory of Yahweh filled the house of God.</span>
