## The Furnishings of Solomon’s Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 8:64; Ezekiel 43:13–17; 1 Kings 9:25"> And he made an altar of bronze, twenty cubits was its length, and twenty cubits was its width, and ten cubits was its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 3:5; Zechariah 13:1; Exodus 30:18–21"> Then he made the sea of molten metal, from brim to brim it was ten cubits, completely round. And it was five cubits in height, and ⸤its circumference measured⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and it measured a line all around it”">a</a></sup>thirty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 10:14; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> Under it were figures of oxen all around it, ten cubits high, encircling the sea all around. The oxen were in two rows ⸤cast as one piece with it⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “molten with its casting”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 28:19–20; Revelation 21:14; Mark 16:15"> It was standing upon twelve oxen, three facing north, and three facing west, and three facing south, and three facing east. The sea was set upon them from above, and all their hindquarters faced ⸤inward⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “into the house”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:26"> And its thickness was a handbreadth, and its brim was like the working of the lip of a cup, the blossom of a lily. And it held three thousand baths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:38; Exodus 30:18–21; 1 Kings 7:40"> And he made ten basins. And he set five on the south and five on the north in which to wash; they washed off the work of the burnt offering in them, but the sea was for the priests to wash therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; 1 Kings 7:49; John 8:12"> And he made ten golden lampstands according to their custom, and he set them in the temple, five on the south and five on the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; Malachi 1:12; Exodus 25:23–30"> And he made ten tables and placed them in the temple, five on the south and five on the north. And he made a hundred drinking bowls of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:36; 2 Kings 21:5; 1 Kings 7:12"> And he made the courtyard of the priests and the great outer courtyard and the doors for the outer court. And he overlaid their doors with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 7:39"> And he set the sea at the southeast corner of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:40–51; 1 Kings 7:14"> And Huram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This is the spelling in Hebrew, though many translations have “Hiram”">d</a></sup>made the pots, the shovels, and the drinking bowls. So Hiram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The spelling (“Hiram”) is different here than in the previous occurrences">e</a></sup>finished making the work that he made on the house of God for King Solomon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 7:41; 2 Chronicles 3:15–17"> the two columns, the bowls, and the two capitals on top of the columns, and the two latticeworks to cover the two bowls of the capitals that were on top of the columns,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 7:42; Jeremiah 52:23"> and the four hundred pomegranates for the two latticeworks, two rows of pomegranates for the latticework, to cover the two bowls of the capitals that were before the columns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 7:27–43"> And he made the water carts, and he made the basins on the water carts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and the one sea and the twelve oxen underneath it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Kings 7:13–14; 2 Chronicles 4:11"> And Huram-abi made for King Solomon the pots, the shovels, the three-pronged meat forks, and all the utensils of polished bronze for the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:46"> The king cast them in the plain of the Jordan, in the clay soil between Succoth and Zeredah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:47; Jeremiah 52:20; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> Solomon made all these utensils in great abundance, for the weight of the bronze could not be determined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 9:13; Daniel 5:2–3; Exodus 25:23–30"> So Solomon made all the objects that were in the house of God: the altar of gold; the tables upon which was the bread of the presence;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:7; Exodus 25:31–37; 1 Kings 6:5"> the lampstands, and the lamps for burning according to the custom before the inner sanctuary, of solid gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 6:29; Exodus 37:20; 1 Kings 6:35"> and the blossoms, the lamps, and the tongs that were of solid gold;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; 2 Kings 25:14; Jeremiah 52:18"> and the snuffers, the drinking bowls, the dishes, and the firepans, of solid gold; and the entrance to the house, the inner doors to ⸤the most holy place⸥, and the doors to the house of the temple were of gold.</span>
