## The Reign of Amaziah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 14:1–6"> Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he became king. And he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Jehoaddan from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:14; James 4:8; Acts 8:21"> And he did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh, only not with a fully-devoted heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25–26; 2 Kings 14:5–22; Genesis 9:5–6"> And it happened that as his reign was strengthened, he killed his servants who had killed his father the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; Ezekiel 18:20; 2 Kings 14:5–6"> But he did not put their children to death, according to what is written in the law, in the scroll of Moses, where Yahweh commanded, saying, “Fathers shall not be put to death on account of the sons, and the sons shall not be put to death on account of the fathers, but each shall be put to death for his own sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 1:3; 2 Chronicles 17:14–18; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> Then Amaziah assembled the men of Judah and set them by ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the house of the fathers”">a</a></sup>under commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds for all Judah and Benjamin. And he counted them from twenty years old and upward, and found them to be three hundred thousand chosen for going out to battle, able with spear and shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he hired from Israel one hundred thousand strong, mighty warriors for one hundred talents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:2; 1 Kings 12:28; Hosea 9:13"> But a man of God came to him, saying, “O king, do not let the army of Israel go with you, for Yahweh is not with Israel, all the Ephraimites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:11; 2 Chronicles 20:6; Philippians 4:19"> But even still, you yourself go, do! Be strong for the battle else God will make you stumble before the enemy! For there is power with God to help and to cause stumbling.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:22; Deuteronomy 8:18"> And Amaziah said to the man of God, “Now what should I do with the one hundred talents that I have given to the troops of Israel?” And the man of God said, “⸤Yahweh is able to give⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “There is to Yahweh to give”">b</a></sup>to you more than this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; 2 Samuel 19:43; Proverbs 29:22"> Then Amaziah dismissed the troops that came to him from Ephraim to go to their home.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “their place”">c</a></sup>And ⸤they became very angry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “their noses became very hot”">d</a></sup>with Judah, and they returned to their home ⸤in great anger⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in hotness of nose”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 14:7; Psalm 60:1; 2 Samuel 8:13"> Then Amaziah strengthened himself, and leading his army, he went to the Valley of Salt. And he struck down ten thousand men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “sons”">f</a></sup>of Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:10; 1 Chronicles 20:3; 2 Samuel 12:31"> Now the ⸤troops⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the troop”">g</a></sup>captured ten thousand others alive, and they brought them to the top of the rock and threw them from the top of the rock. So all of them were smashed to pieces.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:5; 1 Kings 16:29; 1 Kings 9:17"> As for the ⸤troops⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons of the troop”">h</a></sup>Amaziah had sent back from going with him to war, they raided the cities of Judah from Samaria to Beth-Horon. And they struck down three thousand of them and plundered much booty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:23; Deuteronomy 7:25; Deuteronomy 7:5"> And it happened that after Amaziah came back from killing the Edomites, he brought the gods of the sons of Seir, and he stood them up for himself as gods and bowed down before them and made smoke offerings for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 96:5; 2 Chronicles 25:11–12; 2 Chronicles 25:7"> So ⸤Yahweh became very angry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “Yahweh’s nose became very hot”">i</a></sup>with Amaziah and sent a prophet to him, and he said to him, “Why have you sought the gods of the people who could not deliver their own people from your hand?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:20–21; Jeremiah 29:26; 2 Chronicles 18:25"> And it happened that while he was speaking to him, he said to him, “Have we appointed you as a counselor to the king? Stop—⸤why should you be killed⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “why shall he strike you?”">j</a></sup>So the prophet stopped and said, “I know that God has determined to destroy you, because you have done this and have not listened to my advice.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:8–14; 2 Chronicles 25:13; Proverbs 20:3"> Then Amaziah king of Judah took counsel, and he sent to Joash the son of Jehoahaz the son of Jehu, king of Israel, saying, “Come,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="This translation follows the Qere reading">k</a></sup>⸤let us face one another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “let us look at faces”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 1 Kings 4:33; Psalm 80:13"> And Joash the king of Israel sent to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, “The thorn bush that is in Lebanon has sent to the cedar which is in Lebanon, saying, ‘Give your daughter to my son as wife.’ But a wild animal of the field that was in Lebanon passed by and trampled the thorn bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:16; 2 Chronicles 32:25; Proverbs 18:6"> Look, you say that you have struck Edom, and your heart has lifted you up to boast. Now remain at your home. Why stir up disaster that you fall, you and Judah with you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 12:15; 1 Peter 2:8"> But Amaziah did not listen, for it was from God that he might give them into the hand of enemies, because they sought the gods of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19–20; 1 Samuel 6:9; Joshua 21:16"> So Joash the king of Israel went up, and he and Amaziah the king of Judah ⸤faced one another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “looked at one another’s faces”">m</a></sup>at Beth-Shemesh, which belongs to Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:10; 2 Chronicles 28:5–6; 1 Kings 22:36"> And Judah was defeated before Israel, and each man fled to his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; Nehemiah 8:16; 2 Chronicles 26:9"> And Joash the king of Israel captured Amaziah the king of Judah, the son of Joash, the son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-Shemesh. And they brought him to Jerusalem and broke down the wall of Jerusalem from the Gate of Ephraim to the Corner Gate, four hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:15; 2 Chronicles 12:9; 2 Kings 14:14"> Then with all the gold and silver, all the objects found in the house of God with Obed-Edom, the storehouses of the house of the king, and ⸤the hostages⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the pledges”">n</a></sup>he returned to Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 14:17–22"> And Amaziah the son of Joash, king of Judah, lived after the death of Joash the son of Jehoahaz, the king of Israel, fifteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:34; 2 Kings 14:15"> Now the remainder of the words of Amaziah, from the first to the last, behold, are they not written in the scroll of the kings of Judah and Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:25; Joshua 10:31; 2 Kings 14:19"> And from the time that Amaziah turned away from Yahweh, they had plotted a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem. And he fled to Lachish, but they sent after him to Lachish and killed him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 14:20"> And they carried him on the horses and buried him with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “fathers”">o</a></sup>in the city of Judah.</span>
</div>
</div>
