## Abijah and Civil War

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:16; 1 Kings 15:1–8"> In the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam, Abijah reigned over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:20; 1 Kings 15:6–7; Judges 19:16"> He reigned for three years in Jerusalem. And the name of his mother was Micaiah the daughter of Uriel from Gibeah.<br />Now there was war between Abijah and between Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:12–13; 1 Chronicles 21:5; 1 Samuel 17:1–3"> And Abijah joined in the battle with an army of four hundred thousand ⸤battle-hardened warriors⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “mighty warriors of battle”">a</a></sup>chosen men, and Jeroboam put the battle in order against him with eight hundred thousand chosen men, an army of mighty warriors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 18:22; 2 Chronicles 15:2; Genesis 10:18"> Then Abijah stood up on top of Mount Zemaraim that is in the hill country of Ephraim and said, “Hear me, O Jeroboam and all Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 2:13; Mark 9:49–50"> Do you not all know that Yahweh the God of Israel gave the kingdom to David over Israel forever, to him and to his sons by a covenant of salt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 11:26; 1 Kings 12:20; 2 Chronicles 10:19"> But Jeroboam the son of Nebat, a servant of Solomon the son of David, rose up and rebelled against his master,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 9:4; 2 Chronicles 12:13; Acts 17:5"> and worthless men, ⸤sons of wickedness⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons of Belial”">b</a></sup>were gathered about him and became hostile against Rehoboam the son of Solomon, when Rehoboam was a boy and gentle of heart and was not strong enough to stand before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; 2 Chronicles 11:15; Luke 19:14"> So now you yourselves are considering that you are strong before the kingdom of Yahweh which is in the hand the sons of David, since you yourselves are a great multitude and you have bull calves of gold that Jeroboam has made for idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14–15; Jeremiah 2:11; Exodus 29:35"> Did you not drive out the priests of Yahweh, the sons of Aaron, the Levites? Have you not made for yourselves priests like all the peoples of the lands? All who come ⸤to be consecrated for priestly service⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to fill his hand” (a Hebrew idiom for ordination)">c</a></sup>with a young bull or seven rams becomes a priest of what is not a god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 2 Chronicles 11:16–17; Zechariah 13:9"> But as for us, Yahweh is our God, and we have not forsaken him. The priests serving Yahweh are the sons of Aaron, the Levites, according to their service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Leviticus 24:2–9; Exodus 29:38"> They offer burnt offerings to Yahweh ⸤every morning⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in the morning, in the morning”">d</a></sup>and ⸤every evening⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in the evening, in the evening”">e</a></sup>sweet spices of incense, and the rows of bread upon the pure table. And the golden lampstand with its lamps they keep kindled ⸤every evening⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “in the evening, in the evening”">f</a></sup>for we are keeping the requirement of Yahweh our God, but you yourselves have forsaken him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:39; Romans 8:31; Numbers 10:8–9"> And look, God is with us at the head, and his priests have the trumpets for sounding blasts against you, O sons of Israel. Do not fight against Yahweh, the God of your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup>for you will not succeed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 8:4; Joshua 8:9; Proverbs 21:30"> Then Jeroboam sent around an ambush to come behind them. So they were in front of Judah, but the ambush was behind them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:11; Exodus 14:10; Joshua 8:20"> When Judah turned, then behold, the battle against them was in front and behind, and they cried to Yahweh, and the priests blew on the trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:12; Joshua 11:8; Psalm 118:4–7"> Then the men of Judah shouted, and it happened that when the men of Judah were shouting, then God defeated Jeroboam and all Israel before Abijah and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; Judges 11:21; Joshua 10:12"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>fled from before Judah, and God gave them into their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:6; 2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 13:12"> And Abijah and his people struck a great blow against them, and the dead from Israel that fell were five hundred thousand chosen men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; 2 Chronicles 14:11; Nahum 1:7"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>were subdued at that time, and the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “sons”">j</a></sup>of Judah were victorious, for they relied upon Yahweh the God of their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “fathers”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:8; Joshua 15:9; Joshua 10:39"> And Abijah pursued after Jeroboam and took cities from him: Bethel with its villages, Jeshanah with its villages, and Ephron with its villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:38; 1 Kings 14:20; Acts 12:23"> And Jeroboam did not regain strength again in the days of Abijah. And Yahweh plagued him and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 9:5; 2 Samuel 5:12–13; Judges 10:4"> But Abijah became strong, and he took to himself fourteen wives and fathered twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29; 2 Chronicles 12:15; 2 Chronicles 24:27"> Now the rest of the words of Abijah and his ways and his words are written in the story of the prophet Iddo.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
