## Solomon Builds the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:18; Genesis 22:14; 1 Chronicles 22:1"> Then Solomon began to build the house of Yahweh in Jerusalem, on Mount Moriah, where Yahweh had appeared to David his father, at the place that David had established, on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1"> And he began to build in the second month of the fourth year of his reign.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Chronicles 28:11–19"> Now these were the measurements of Solomon for building the house of God: the length in cubits by the former measurement was sixty cubits, and the width was twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:23; Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> ⸤The length of the portico that was in front was the same as the front of the width of the house⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “The length at the front of the portico the front of the width of the house”">a</a></sup>twenty cubits. And its height was one hundred and twenty cubits. And he overlaid it on the inside with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:15–17; 1 Kings 6:21–22"> And the great house itself he covered with cypress wood, then he overlaid it with pure gold. And he put on it palm tree images and ornate chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:8; Revelation 21:18–21"> Then he overlaid the house with precious stone as decoration. (Now the gold was the gold of Parvaim.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20–22; Exodus 26:29; Ezekiel 7:20"> And he overlaid the house with gold—the beams, the thresholds, the walls, and the doors. And he carved cherubim upon the walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; 1 Kings 6:19–20; 1 Kings 6:16"> Then he made ⸤the most holy place⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the house of the holy of the holies”">b</a></sup>Its length ⸤was equal to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “at the front of”">c</a></sup>the width of the house: twenty cubits. And its breadth was twenty cubits. And he overlaid it with six hundred talents of fine gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11"> And the weight for the nails was fifty shekels of gold. And he overlaid its upper rooms with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23–28; Exodus 25:18"> And he made two sculpted wood cherubim in ⸤the most holy place⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the house of the holy of the holies”">d</a></sup>and he overlaid them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The length of the outstretched wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits; one wing of five cubits touched the wall of the house, and the other wing of five cubits was touching the outstretched wing of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the wing of five cubits of the other cherub was touching the wall of the house, and its other wing five cubits long touching the wing of the first<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “other”">e</a></sup>cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:20"> The wings of these cherubim extended twenty cubits, and they were standing on their feet, their faces toward the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:3; Hebrews 10:20; Matthew 27:51"> And he made the curtain of blue and purple and crimson and fine linen, and put cherubim on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–24; Jeremiah 52:20–23"> And at the front of the house he made two columns, thirty-five cubits high, and the capital on their top was five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 6:21"> And he made decorative chains in the inner sanctuary and put them on top of the columns. And he made one hundred pomegranate ornaments, and put them on the chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:21"> And he erected the columns in front of the temple, one on the south and one on the north. He called the name of the southern one Jakin, and the name of the northern one Boaz.</span>
</div>
