## Hezekiah Celebrates Passover

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 2 Chronicles 11:13; Hosea 5:4"> Then Hezekiah sent to all Israel and Judah, and he also wrote letters to Ephraim and Manasseh, to come to the house of Yahweh in Jerusalem to make a Passover feast to Yahweh the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 9:10–11; 1 Chronicles 13:1–3; 2 Chronicles 30:13"> Now the king and his princes, and all the assembly in Jerusalem had taken counsel to make the Passover feast in the second month—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; Exodus 12:6; Exodus 12:18"> but they were not able to make it at that time, for the priests had not consecrated themselves ⸤in sufficient numbers⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “for enough”">a</a></sup>and the people had not been assembled in Jerusalem—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:4"> and the plan seemed right in the eyes of the king and in the eyes of all the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Leviticus 23:2; Esther 8:8–10"> So they let the decree stand, ⸤to make a proclamation⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to spread a voice”">b</a></sup>throughout all Israel, from Beersheba to Dan, to come to make a Passover feast to Yahweh the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:31; Esther 8:14; 2 Kings 15:19"> And the runners went with the letter from the hand of the king and his princes throughout all Israel and Judah according to the command of the king, saying, “O sons of Israel, return to Yahweh, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, that he may turn again to the remnant of you who escaped from the hand of the kings of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:8; Ezekiel 20:13–18; Zechariah 1:3–4"> Do not be like your fathers and like your brothers, who acted unfaithfully before Yahweh, the God of their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>so that he made them as a desolation, as you see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 32:9; 2 Chronicles 29:10; Deuteronomy 10:16"> Now, do not stiffen your neck as your fathers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “ancestors”">d</a></sup>but give a hand to Yahweh and come to his sanctuary, which he has consecrated forever, and serve Yahweh your God that his fierce anger may turn away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 55:7; Micah 7:18; Psalm 86:5"> For when you return to Yahweh, your brothers and your children will find compassion before their captors and return to this land, for Yahweh your God is gracious and compassionate, and he will not turn away his face from you if you return to him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; 2 Chronicles 30:6; Nehemiah 2:19"> And it happened that the runners were passing from city to city in the land of Ephraim and Manasseh, and up to Zebulun, but they were laughing at them and mocking them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:18; 2 Chronicles 30:21; 2 Chronicles 30:25"> Only men from Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun humbled themselves and came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 24:7; Philippians 2:13"> The hand of God was also upon Judah, to give them one heart to obey the command of the king and the princes concerning the word of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:2; Psalm 84:7"> So many people gathered in Jerusalem to hold the Feast of Unleavened Bread in the second month; it was a very great assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:24; 2 Samuel 15:23; 2 Chronicles 15:16"> And they rose up and removed the altars that were in Jerusalem. And they removed all the incense altars and threw them away in the Wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “valley”; a wadi is a valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">e</a></sup>Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 5:11; Exodus 19:22"> And they slaughtered the Passover lamb on the fourteenth day of the second month. And the priests and the Levites were disgraced, so they consecrated themselves and brought burnt offerings into the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; 2 Chronicles 35:15; 2 Kings 11:14"> Then they stood at their positions according to custom, according to the law of Moses the man of God. The priests were sprinkling the blood from the hand of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 35:3–6; Exodus 12:6"> For there were many in the assembly who did not consecrate themselves, so the Levites were over the killing of the Passover sacrifices for all who were not clean, to consecrate them to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:28; James 5:15–16; Daniel 9:19"> For a majority of the people, many from Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun, had not cleansed themselves. But they ate the Passover sacrifice ⸤otherwise than prescribed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “in not as it is written”">f</a></sup>but Hezekiah had prayed for them, saying, “May the good Yahweh make atonement unto</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; Proverbs 23:26; Job 11:13"> everyone whose heart is set to seek God, Yahweh the God of his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup>though not according to the cleansing for the sanctuary.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; James 5:15–16; Psalm 103:3"> And Yahweh listened to Hezekiah, and he healed the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:15; Exodus 13:6; Deuteronomy 12:12"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>who were found in Jerusalem kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days with great joy. And the Levites and the priests were offering praise to Yahweh day by day, with powerful instruments to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:6; Nehemiah 9:3; Isaiah 40:1–2"> And Hezekiah spoke to the heart of all the Levites ⸤who were skilled at service⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “who were showing good understanding”">i</a></sup>to Yahweh. So they ate the feast seven days, offering peace offerings and giving thanks to Yahweh, the God of their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “fathers”">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Chronicles 7:9"> Then the whole assembly decided to make seven more days of feasting, so they made seven more days with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8; Ephesians 4:8"> for Hezekiah the king of Judah had ⸤provided⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “lifted up”">k</a></sup>one thousand bulls and seven thousand sheep for the assembly, and the princes ⸤provided⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “lifted up”">l</a></sup>one thousand bulls and ten thousand sheep for the assembly. And many priests consecrated themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:11; 2 Chronicles 30:18; 1 Chronicles 16:10–11"> And the whole assembly of Judah, the priests and the Levites, all the assembly who had come from the land of Israel, and those living in Judah rejoiced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:8–10"> And there was great joy in Jerusalem, for from the days of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel, there was nothing such as this in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:15; Psalm 68:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> Then the priests and the Levites arose and blessed the people, and their voice was heard, and their prayer came to his holy dwelling place in the heavens.</span>
</div>
</div>
