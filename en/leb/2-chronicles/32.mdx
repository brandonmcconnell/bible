## Sennacherib’s Invasion of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:13–37; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Chronicles 20:1–2"> After these things and these acts of faithfulness, Sennacherib the king of Assyria came, and he came against Judah. And he encamped against the fortified cities and planned to break them down for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 9:53; Luke 9:51; 2 Kings 12:17"> When Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib had come and that his face was set for battle against Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 24:6; Proverbs 20:18; Proverbs 15:22"> he took counsel with his commanders and his mighty warriors to block off the waters of the springs that came from outside the city, and they helped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:30; 2 Kings 18:9; 1 Kings 19:21"> Then many people were gathered, and they blocked off all the springs and the river that flowed through the midst of the land, saying, “Why should the kings of Assyria come and find much water?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:23; 1 Kings 9:24; 2 Kings 25:4"> Then he strengthened himself and built up all the walls that were broken down, and raised towers upon them and another wall ⸤outside⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “toward the street”">a</a></sup>And he strengthened the Millo of the city of David and made much weaponry and small shields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22; Nehemiah 8:16"> And he appointed commanders for battle over the people and gathered them to himself into the public square of the gate of the city. And he spoke to their hearts, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 6:16; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 6:10"> “Be strong! Be courageous! Do not fear and do not be dismayed before the king of Assyria and before all the crowd that is with him, for there are more with us than with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; 2 Chronicles 20:17; Isaiah 41:10"> With him is the arm of flesh, and with us is Yahweh our God, to help us and to fight our battles.” And the people took confidence with the words of Hezekiah, king of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Joshua 10:31; Isaiah 36:2"> After this Sennacherib the king of Assyria sent his servants to Jerusalem (now he and all his armies with him were against Lachish) to Hezekiah king of Judah and to all of Judah that was in Jerusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:19; Isaiah 36:4"> “Thus says Sennacherib the king of Assyria: ‘On what are you relying that you are dwelling in siege works in Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 27:43; Psalm 11:1–3; Isaiah 36:18"> Is not Hezekiah urging you to give you up to die by starvation and thirst, saying, “Yahweh our God will save us from the hand of the king of Assyria”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; Deuteronomy 12:13–14; Deuteronomy 12:26–27"> Has not Hezekiah himself removed his high places and his altars and said to Judah and to Jerusalem, saying, “You must bow down before one altar and upon it you must make offerings”?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:33–35; Isaiah 44:8–10; Daniel 4:30"> Do you not know what I have done, I and my ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “fathers”">b</a></sup>to all the peoples of the lands? Were the gods of the nations of all the lands at all able to save their land from my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 10:11–12; Exodus 15:9–11; Isaiah 42:8"> Who among all the gods of those nations whom my ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>utterly destroyed was able to save his people from my hand, that your God will be able to save you from my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 5:2; Daniel 3:15; Isaiah 36:18"> So now, do not let Hezekiah deceive you. Do not let him urge you according to this. Do not put trust in him, for no god of any nation and kingdom has been able to save his people from my hand and from the hand of my ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “fathers”">d</a></sup>Surely then your God will not save you from my hand!’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 73:9; John 15:21; Job 15:25–26"> And still more his servants said against Yahweh God and against Hezekiah his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; 2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:28–29"> And he wrote letters to treat Yahweh the God of Israel with contempt and spoke against him, saying, “As the gods of the nations of the earth who did not save their people from my hand, so likewise the God of Hezekiah will not save his people from my hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; 2 Kings 18:26–28; 1 Samuel 17:26"> Then they called with a great voice in Judean to the people of Jerusalem who were upon the wall to frighten them and terrify them, so that they could take the city captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 19:18; Isaiah 2:8; Jeremiah 32:30"> And they spoke about the God of Jerusalem as about the gods of the peoples of the earth, the works of the hands of humankind.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh’s Deliverance of Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 91:14–15; Isaiah 37:14–20; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> Then King Hezekiah and Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet, prayed concerning this. And they cried to the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 37:36–38; Psalm 76:5; Psalm 18:50"> Then Yahweh sent an angel, and he destroyed every mighty warrior of strength, commander, and officer in the camp of the king of Assyria. And he returned with shamed face to his land and went into the house of his god. And some of the offspring of his loins fell upon him there with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4–5; Psalm 18:48–50; 2 Thessalonians 3:5"> So Yahweh saved Hezekiah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem from the hand of Sennacherib, king of Assyria, and from the all their enemies, and gave them rest all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:5; Matthew 2:11; 2 Chronicles 1:1"> And many brought tribute to Yahweh, to Jerusalem, and precious things to Hezekiah king of Judah, so that he was exalted in the eyes of all the nations thereafter.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Rest of Hezekiah’s Reign

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–8; 2 Kings 20:1–11; Isaiah 38:21–22"> In those days Hezekiah fell ill unto death, and he prayed to Yahweh. And he answered him and gave him a sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:18; 2 Chronicles 26:16; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> But Hezekiah did not reciprocate according to the benefit placed upon him, because his heart became proud. So wrath was upon him and upon Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18–19; 2 Chronicles 34:27–28; 2 Chronicles 33:12"> But Hezekiah humbled himself with respect to the arrogance of his heart, he and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that the wrath of Yahweh did not come upon them in the days of Hezekiah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:25–31; 2 Chronicles 1:12; Proverbs 10:22"> And Hezekiah had very much wealth and honor, and he made storehouses for himself for silver, gold, precious stones, spices, small shields, and all sorts of desirable objects;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; 2 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Kings 4:26"> and storage buildings for the yield of grain, new wine, and olive oil; and animal stalls for all kinds of animals, and animals and herds for animal stalls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:12; Proverbs 10:22; 1 Samuel 2:7"> And he made cities for himself, and livestock of sheep and abundant cattle, for God had given to him very abundant possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 1:33; Isaiah 22:9–11; Psalm 1:1–3"> And this same Hezekiah blocked off the flow of the waters of the upper Gihon, and directed them down the west side of the city of David. And Hezekiah prospered in all his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; Proverbs 17:3; Genesis 22:1"> And thus in the matter of the envoys of the commanders of Babylon who had been sent to him to seek the sign that had happened in the land, God forsook him, to test him and to know all that was in his heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 36:1–22; 2 Kings 18:1–20; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21"> Now the remainder of the words of Hezekiah and his loyal love, behold, they are written in the visions of Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet, upon the scroll of the kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; 1 Kings 1:21; 2 Chronicles 16:14"> And Hezekiah slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>and they buried him in the upper part of the burial sites of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “sons”">f</a></sup>of David. And all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem did him honor at his death. And Manasseh his son became king in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>
