## Josiah Observes the Passover

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:3; 2 Kings 23:21–23"> Josiah kept<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “made”">a</a></sup>the Passover to Yahweh in Jerusalem. And they slaughtered the Passover sacrifice on the fourteenth day of the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2; 1 Chronicles 22:19; 2 Chronicles 29:5–11"> And he set the priests at their posts and encouraged them in the service of the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:26; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 33:10"> And he said to the Levites who were teaching all Israel, who were sanctified to Yahweh, “Put the holy ark in the house which Solomon the son of David, king of Israel, built. There is no need to carry it on your shoulders. Now serve Yahweh your God and his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 23:1–26; Nehemiah 11:10–20"> And prepare yourselves according to ⸤your families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the house of your fathers”">b</a></sup>according to your working groups, according to the decree<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “writing”">c</a></sup>of David king of Israel and according to the decree<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “writing”">d</a></sup>of Solomon his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 134:1; Psalm 135:2; Ezra 6:18"> And stand in the sanctuary according to the clans of ⸤your families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the house of your fathers”">e</a></sup>for your brothers, the ⸤lay people⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons of the people”">f</a></sup>and a part of the ⸤family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “house of the father”">g</a></sup>of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; Hebrews 9:13–14; 2 Chronicles 29:15"> And slaughter the Passover lamb and consecrate yourself and prepare for your brothers to do according to the word of Yahweh by the hand of Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 8:63; 2 Chronicles 30:24; Isaiah 32:8"> Then Josiah provided for the ⸤lay people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons of the people”">h</a></sup>sheep, lambs, and young goats, all for the Passover for all who were found there, to the sum of thirty thousand, along with three thousand bulls. These were the possession of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:31–33; Jeremiah 29:25–26; 1 Chronicles 24:4–5"> And his officials contributed a freewill offering to the people, to the priests, and to the Levites. Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel, commanders of the house of God, gave to the priests two thousand six hundred Passover lambs and three hundred bulls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:21–28; Jeremiah 3:10; Isaiah 1:10–15"> Conaniah also, and Shemaiah and Nethanel his brothers, and Hashabiah and Jeiel and Jehozabad, commanders of the Levites, contributed to the Levites five thousand Passover lambs and five hundred bulls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:16; Ezra 6:18; 2 Chronicles 35:4–5"> And when the work had been completed, the priests stood at their positions and the Levites in their working groups according to the command of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:34; Hebrews 9:21–22; 2 Chronicles 29:22–24"> And they slaughtered the Passover lamb, and the priests sprinkled the blood from their hand, and the Levites flayed the sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 3:9–11; Leviticus 3:3; Leviticus 3:5"> And they set aside the burnt offerings to give them to the clans of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “house of the fathers”">i</a></sup>for the ⸤lay people⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons of the people”">j</a></sup>to offer to Yahweh according to what is written in the scroll of Moses. And thus they did with the bulls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:8–9; 1 Samuel 2:13–15; Deuteronomy 16:7"> Then they roasted the Passover lamb in the fire according to the ordinance. And they boiled the holy offerings in pots, in kettles, and in dishes and brought it quickly to the ⸤lay people⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons of the people”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:2–4"> And afterward they prepared for themselves and for the priests, since the priests, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “sons”">l</a></sup>of Aaron, were busy with offering the burnt offerings and the fat until night. So the Levites prepared for themselves and for the priests, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “sons”">m</a></sup>of Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–7; 1 Chronicles 26:12–19; 1 Chronicles 9:17–19"> And the singers, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “sons”">n</a></sup>of Asaph, were at their stations according to the decree<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “command”">o</a></sup>of David and Asaph and Heman and Jeduthun, the seer of the king. And the gatekeepers were at ⸤each gate⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “gate to gate”">p</a></sup>They did not need to depart from their service, for their brothers the Levites had made preparations for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So all the service of Yahweh was prepared on that day to keep<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “make”">q</a></sup>the Passover and to sacrifice burnt offerings on the altar of Yahweh, according to the command of King Josiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:15–20; 2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 23:15"> And the Israelites who were present kept<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “made”">r</a></sup>the Passover at that time, and the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:5; 2 Kings 23:21–23; 2 Chronicles 30:26–27"> And there was no Passover like it kept<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “made”">s</a></sup>in Israel since the days of Samuel the prophet. And none of the kings of Israel kept<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “made”">t</a></sup>a Passover like that which Josiah and the priests and the Levites, and all of Judah and Israel who were present, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, kept.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “made”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> In the eighteenth year of the reign of Josiah this Passover was kept.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “made”">v</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of Josiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 23:29–30; Jeremiah 46:2–12"> After all of this that Josiah had prepared for the temple,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “house”">w</a></sup>Neco the king of Egypt went up to make war at Carchemish on the Euphrates, and Josiah went up to meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:19; 2 Kings 18:25; Isaiah 36:10"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="That is, Neco">x</a></sup>sent messengers to him, saying, “⸤What is there between us⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “What to me and to you”">y</a></sup>O king of Judah? I am not against you yourself this day, but against the house making war against me. And God has commanded me to make haste. Stop opposing God, who is with me that he will not destroy you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 5:19; 2 Chronicles 18:29; 1 Kings 22:30"> But Josiah did not turn his face from him, but he disguised himself to fight with him. He did not listen to the words of Neco from the mouth of God, and he went out to fight in the plain of Megiddo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 22:34; 2 Kings 9:24; Genesis 49:23"> And the archers shot King Josiah, and the king said to his servants, “Take me away, for I am badly wounded.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 12:11; 2 Kings 23:30; Psalm 36:6"> So his servants took him out of the chariot and transported him in his second chariot and brought him to Jerusalem. And he died and was buried in the burial sites of his ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “fathers”">z</a></sup>And all Judah and Jerusalem mourned over Josiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Lamentations 4:20; Jeremiah 22:10; Job 3:8"> And Jeremiah uttered a lament for Josiah, and all the male and female singers have spoken in their laments about Josiah to this day. And they made them a requirement in Israel, and behold, they are written in the laments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:20; 2 Chronicles 32:32"> Now the remainder of the words of Josiah and his loyal love for what is written in the law of Yahweh</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:22; 2 Chronicles 25:26; 2 Kings 10:34"> and his words, from the first to the last, behold, they are written in the scroll of the kings of Israel and Judah.</span>
</div>
</div>
