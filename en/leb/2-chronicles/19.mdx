## Jehoshaphat’s Reforms

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:31–32"> Then Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned to his house in peace to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 139:21–22; 2 John 1:10–11; James 4:4"> And Jehu son of Hanani the seer went out to meet him. And he said to King Jehoshaphat, “Should you be a help to the wicked and love those who hate Yahweh? On account of this, wrath has come against you from Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:12; Ezra 7:10; 1 Kings 14:13"> Nevertheless some good things have been found in regard to you, for you burned the Asherahs out of the land and set your heart to seek God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:15–17; 1 Samuel 7:3–4; 2 Chronicles 29:10–11"> And Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem and returned and went out among the people from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim and brought them back to Yahweh, the God of their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fathers”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–20; 2 Chronicles 19:8; 1 Peter 2:13–14"> And he appointed judges in the land and in all the fortified cities of Judah, city by city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Ecclesiastes 5:8"> And he said to the judges, “Consider what you are doing, for you are not judging for a man but for Yahweh. Now he is with you in speaking justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Genesis 18:25; Romans 2:11"> So now, let the fear of Yahweh be upon you all. ⸤Be careful what you do⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “Keep and do”">b</a></sup>for there is neither wickedness nor ⸤showing partiality⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “lifting faces”">c</a></sup>and taking bribes with Yahweh our God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:8–9; Exodus 18:19–26; Deuteronomy 17:8–13"> Moreover, Jehoshaphat appointed in Jerusalem some of the Levites and the priests and heads of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">d</a></sup>of Israel as judges and to decide legal disputes for Yahweh. And they sat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “lived”">e</a></sup>in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; 2 Samuel 23:3; Deuteronomy 1:16–17"> And he commanded them, saying, “Thus you must do in the fear of Yahweh in faithfulness and with a whole heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:8–13; Ezekiel 33:6; Ezekiel 3:18–21"> Any legal dispute that comes before you from your brothers who live in their cities concerning bloodguilt, law, commandment, statutes, and justice, you must warn them, so that they do not incur guilt before Yahweh, and that wrath may not come upon you and your brothers. Thus you must do that you do not incur guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:8; Psalm 18:25–26; 2 Timothy 2:1"> And behold, Amariah the chief priest is over you in all matters of the word of Yahweh, and Zebadiah the son of Ishmael, the governor of the house of Judah, in all matters of the king, and the Levites shall be before you as officials. Be strong and do well! May Yahweh be with the upright.”</span>
</div>
