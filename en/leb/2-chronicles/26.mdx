## The Reign of Uzziah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:1; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Chronicles 33:25"> Then all the people of Judah took Uzziah (now he was sixteen years old) and made him king in the place of his father Amaziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 16:6; 2 Chronicles 25:28; 2 Chronicles 25:23"> He built Elath and returned it to Judah after the king slept with his ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fathers”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 15:2–3; Amos 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> Uzziah was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem fifty-two years. And the name of his mother was Yecoliah of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:2"> And he did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh, according to all that Amaziah his father had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 24:2; 1 Chronicles 22:13"> And he began to seek<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “was seeking”">b</a></sup>God in the days of Zechariah who was teaching in visions of God. And ⸤whenever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “in the days”">c</a></sup>he sought Yahweh God made him have success.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29; 1 Samuel 5:6; 1 Samuel 5:1"> And he went out and made war against the Philistines, and he broke down the wall of Gath and the wall of Jabneh and the wall of Ashdod, and he built cities in Ashdod and among the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16; 1 Chronicles 5:20; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> And God helped him against the Philistines and against the Arabs who were living in Gur-Baal and Meunim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; 2 Chronicles 17:11; Genesis 19:38"> And the Ammonites gave tribute to Uzziah, and his fame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “name”">d</a></sup>went out to the boundary<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to coming in”">e</a></sup>of Egypt, for he became ⸤very strong⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to above”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:13; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Kings 14:13"> And Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the Corner Gate, at the Valley Gate, and at the Angle, and he strengthened them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 26:18–21; Isaiah 29:17; 2 Kings 3:4"> And he built towers in the desert and hewed many cisterns, for he had much livestock, both in the Shephelah and on the plain, along with farmers and vinedressers in the hills and in the fertile lands, for he loved the soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2"> And Uzziah had troops ⸤trained and ready for war⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “making war, going out to fight”">g</a></sup>in divisions according to the number of their enrollment at the hand of Jeuel the scribe and Maaseiah the officer, under the hand of Hananiah of the king’s commanders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The whole number of the heads of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">h</a></sup>for mighty warriors of strength was two thousand six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:5; 2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> And under their hand were army troops numbering three hundred and seven thousand five hundred who could make war with power and strength to help the king against an enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 20:16; 1 Samuel 17:49"> And Uzziah prepared small shields, spears, helmets, breastplates, bows, and slingstones for the whole army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 31:4; Matthew 4:24; 2 Chronicles 2:7"> And he made siege machines in Jerusalem designed by skillful men to be set upon the towers and upon the corners to shoot arrows and large slingstones. And his fame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “name”">i</a></sup>went out far, for he was helped marvelously, for he was strong.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:25; 2 Chronicles 25:19; Proverbs 16:18"> But on account of his strength his heart grew proud unto destruction. And he acted unfaithfully against Yahweh his God and went into the temple of Yahweh to offer an incense offering on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:10; 1 Chronicles 26:6; 1 Chronicles 12:28"> And Azariah the priest, along with eighty strong priests with him, went in after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 30:7–8; Numbers 18:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> And they stood against King Uzziah and said to him, “It is not for you, Uzziah, to burn incense to Yahweh, but it is for the priests, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “sons”">j</a></sup>of Aaron, who are consecrated to burn incense. Go out of the sanctuary, for you have acted unfaithfully. There will be no honor for you from Yahweh God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 12:10; 2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> Then Uzziah, his censer in hand to burn incense, became angry. And when he became angry with the priests, then leprosy appeared on his forehead in front of the priests in the house of Yahweh at the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Esther 6:12; Leviticus 14:34"> When Azariah the chief priest and all the priests turned to him, behold, he was leprous in the forehead. So they rushed him away from there, and he also hastened to go out, for Yahweh had smitten him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:15; 2 Kings 7:3"> So King Uzziah was leprous until the day of his death, and he lived in a separate house as a leper, for he was cut off from the house of Yahweh. And Jotham his son was over the house of the king, judging the people of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 6:1; 2 Chronicles 9:29"> Now the remainder of the words of Uzziah, from the first to the last, Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, wrote them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:27; 2 Chronicles 21:20; 2 Chronicles 26:18"> And Uzziah slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “fathers”">k</a></sup>and they buried him with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>in the burial field which belonged to the kings, because, they said, “He was leprous.” And Jotham his son reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>
