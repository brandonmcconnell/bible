## Asa’s Religious Reforms

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 20:14; Numbers 24:2"> Now Azariah the son of Oded—the Spirit of God came upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:8; 2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> And he went out before Asa and said to him, “Hear me, O Asa and all of Judah and Benjamin! Yahweh is with you while you are with him. And if you will seek him he will be found by you. But if you forsake him he will forsake you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9; Leviticus 10:11; 2 Chronicles 17:8–9"> Now Israel has been without the true God many days, and without a teaching priest, and without law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Deuteronomy 4:29–30; 2 Chronicles 15:15"> but at its trouble he returned to Yahweh, the God of Israel. They sought him, and he was found by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Psalm 121:8; 1 Samuel 13:6"> And in those times there was no peace for the one going out and the one coming in, for great tumults were upon all the inhabitants of the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 21:22–24; Matthew 24:7; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> Nation was crushed by nation, and city was against city, for God threw them into confusion by all sorts of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Joshua 1:7; Joshua 1:9"> But as for you, be strong and let not your hands be weak, for there is reward for your labor.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:12; 2 Chronicles 13:19; 2 Chronicles 4:1"> And when Asa heard these words, the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage and removed the vile idols from all the lands of Judah and Benjamin, and from the cities that he had taken in the hill country of Ephraim, and he repaired the altar of Yahweh that was in front of the portico of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:16; Acts 9:31; Zechariah 8:21–23"> And he gathered all Judah and Benjamin and those sojourning with them, from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon, for many had deserted to him from Israel when they saw that Yahweh his God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 8:9"> And they were gathered at Jerusalem in the third month of the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:13–15; Numbers 31:28–29; Numbers 31:50"> And they sacrificed to Yahweh on that day from the war booty they brought back: seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 24:14; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Deuteronomy 10:12"> And they entered into a covenant to seek Yahweh, the God of their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fathers”">a</a></sup>with all their heart and with all their inmost being,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “soul”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Psalm 115:13; 1 Kings 18:40"> but all who will not seek Yahweh the God of Israel should be killed, from young to old, from men to women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:13; Psalm 81:1–4; Nehemiah 10:29"> And they took an oath to Yahweh with a great voice, with shouting, with trumpets, and with horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:7; 2 Chronicles 15:12; Isaiah 26:8"> And all Judah rejoiced over the oath, for they swore with all their heart. And they sought him with their whole desire, and he was found by them, and Yahweh gave rest to them all around.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:13–24; 1 Kings 15:10; 2 Kings 23:15"> And also Maacah, the mother of Asa, the king removed her from being queen, because she had made a repulsive image for Asherah. And Asa cut down her repulsive image, and he crushed and burned it at the Wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “valley”; a wadi is a valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">c</a></sup>Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:4; 2 Kings 12:3; 1 Kings 11:4"> But the high places were not removed from Israel. Nevertheless, the heart of Asa was fully devoted all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26; 1 Kings 15:14–15"> And he brought the holy objects of his father and his own holy objects into the house of God—silver and gold and vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 15:31; 1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 15:16–17"> And there was no war until the thirty-fifth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
</div>
