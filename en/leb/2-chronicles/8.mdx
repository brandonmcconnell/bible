## Solomon’s Accomplishments and Deeds

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 9:10–28"> And it happened that at the end of twenty years in which Solomon had built the house of Yahweh and his own house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 9:11–18"> Solomon also built the cities that Huram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This is the spelling in Hebrew, though many translations have “Hiram”">a</a></sup>had given to him, and he settled the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 1 Chronicles 18:3; 2 Samuel 8:3"> And Solomon went to Hamath-Zobah and laid siege upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:17–19"> And he built Tadmor in the desert, and he built all the storage cities in Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:24; Joshua 16:5; 2 Chronicles 14:7"> He also built Upper Beth-Horon and Lower Beth-Horon, fortified cities with walls, gates, and bars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 2 Chronicles 8:4; 2 Chronicles 1:14"> Baalath and all the storage cities that belonged to Solomon; all the cities for chariots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">c</a></sup>and cities for horses; and any desire of Solomon that he desired to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and in all the land of his dominion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 15:18–21; Deuteronomy 7:1; 1 Kings 9:20–22"> All the people who remained from the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites who were not from Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:21; 1 Kings 4:6"> from the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">d</a></sup>who were left over after them in the land, whom the Israelites had not destroyed, Solomon conscripted them as forced labor until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–12; Exodus 19:5–6; Galatians 4:31"> But from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>Solomon did not give as servants for his work, but they were men of war, his commanders of his officers, and commanders of his chariots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “chariot”">f</a></sup>and his horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 9:23; 1 Kings 5:16; 2 Chronicles 2:18"> And these were the commanders of the garrisons that belonged to King Solomon, two hundred and fifty who exercised authority over the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 3:1; 1 Kings 7:8; 1 Kings 9:24"> And Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharaoh from the city of David to the house that he had built for her, for he said, “My wife shall not dwell in the house of David, king of Israel, for those places to which the ark of Yahweh has come are holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:1; 2 Chronicles 15:8; John 10:23"> Then Solomon offered burnt offerings to Yahweh on the altar of Yahweh that he had built before the portico,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 29:38–42; Ezekiel 46:3–15"> ⸤as was the daily requirement⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “with respect to the word of the day, by day”">g</a></sup>to offer up according to the commandments of Moses for Sabbaths, new moon festivals, and the three ⸤annual⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “occurrences in the year”">h</a></sup>feasts: the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the Feast of Weeks, and the Feast of Booths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:17; Ezra 6:18; Nehemiah 12:36"> And in accord with the ordinance of David his father he appointed working groups of priests for their service, Levites for their responsibilities for praise and cultic service before the priests ⸤according to the daily requirement⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “according to the word of the day, by day”">i</a></sup>and the gatekeepers in their working groups, gate by gate. For thus David the man of God had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Chronicles 9:29; 1 Kings 7:51"> And they did not turn aside from the commandment of the king concerning the priests and the Levites concerning anything, and concerning the treasury rooms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; 1 Kings 6:7"> And Solomon accomplished all the work from the day the foundation stone was laid for the house of Yahweh until its completion, and the house of Yahweh was finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:22; Deuteronomy 2:8; Numbers 33:35"> Then Solomon went to Ezion-Geber, and to Elath, on the edge of the sea in the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:10; 2 Chronicles 9:13; Ecclesiastes 2:8"> And Huram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="This is the spelling in Hebrew, though many translations have “Hiram”">j</a></sup>sent to him, by the hand of his servants, ships and servants knowledgeable of the sea. And they went with the servants of Solomon to Ophir, and from there they collected four hundred and fifty talents of gold and brought it to King Solomon.</span>
</div>
