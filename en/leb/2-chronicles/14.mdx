## The Reign of Asa in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:31; 1 Kings 2:10; 1 Kings 14:31"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2 Chronicles 14:1–15 in the English Bible is 13:23–14:14 in the Hebrew Bible">a</a></sup>And Abijah slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “fathers”">b</a></sup>and they buried him in the city of David. And Asa his son became king in his place. In his days the land was at rest ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:11; 2 Chronicles 31:20; 1 Kings 15:14"> Asa did that which was good and right in the eyes of Yahweh his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:14"> He removed the foreign altars and high places, broke down the stone pillars, and cut down the Asherahs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:19; Psalm 119:10; Psalm 101:2–8"> And he commanded Judah to seek Yahweh, the God of their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>and to keep the law and the commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:4; 2 Chronicles 34:7"> And he removed the high places and the incense stands from all the cities of Judah, and the kingdom had rest under<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “before”">d</a></sup>him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:15; 1 Chronicles 22:9; Job 34:29"> And he built fortified cities in Judah, for the land was at rest. Under<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “With”">e</a></sup>him there was no war in those years, for Yahweh had given rest to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; 2 Chronicles 14:6; Jeremiah 29:12–14"> And he said to Judah, “Let us build these cities and surround them with walls, towers, gates, and bars while the land is before us, because we have sought Yahweh our God. We have sought and he has given rest to us on every side.” So they built and had success.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:3; 2 Chronicles 17:14–19; 2 Chronicles 11:1"> And Asa had an army of three hundred thousand from Judah, bearing shields and spears, and two hundred and eighty thousand from Benjamin who carried shields and drew bows. All of these were mighty warriors of strength.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:8; 2 Chronicles 12:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:8"> Zerah the Cushite came out against them with an army of a thousand thousands and three hundred chariots. And he came to Mareshah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4"> And Asa went out before him, and a battle was put in order in the valley of Zepah at Mareshah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:19; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; Psalm 22:5"> Then Asa cried out to Yahweh his God. And he said, “O Yahweh, there is none with you to help between the great and the powerless. Help us, O Yahweh our God, for we rely upon you, and in your name we have come against this multitude. O Yahweh, you are our God! Let no man prevail against you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:15; Psalm 136:17–18; Deuteronomy 28:7"> So Yahweh defeated the Cushites before Asa and before Judah, and the Cushites fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:19; Genesis 26:1; Genesis 20:1"> Then Asa and the army<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “people”">f</a></sup>with him pursued them to Gerar. And many from the Cushites fell, for they had no deliverance, for they were shattered before Yahweh and before his army. And they carried away much war booty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:10; Genesis 35:5; 2 Chronicles 20:29"> And they struck all the cities around Gerar, for the fear of Yahweh was upon them. And they plundered all the cities, for there was much plunder in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:30–47; Numbers 31:9; 1 Samuel 30:20"> And they also struck the tents of the herdsmen and carried away many sheep and camels. Then they returned to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
