## The Reign of Manasseh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 21:1–18; 1 Chronicles 3:13; Isaiah 3:12"> Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:3; Deuteronomy 18:9; 2 Kings 17:11"> And he did evil in the eyes of Yahweh according to the detestable things of the nations whom Yahweh drove out before the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:21; Deuteronomy 17:3; 2 Chronicles 31:1"> And ⸤he rebuilt⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “returned and built”">b</a></sup>the high places that Hezekiah his father broke down, and he set up altars for the Baals, made Asherahs, and bowed down to the host of heaven and served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:16; 1 Kings 8:29; Deuteronomy 12:11"> And he built altars in the house of Yahweh, of which Yahweh had said, “In Jerusalem my name shall be forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:9; Jeremiah 32:34–35; Ezekiel 8:7–18"> And he built altars to all the host of heaven in the two courtyards of the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Leviticus 20:6; 2 Chronicles 28:3"> And he himself burned his sons in the fire in the Valley of Ben-Hinnom. And he practiced sorcery and divination, and he engaged in witchcraft and dealt with mediums and spiritists. And he did much evil in the eyes of Yahweh, to provoke him to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:4; 2 Chronicles 7:16; 1 Kings 11:13"> And he placed the carved image of the idol that he had made in the house of God, of which God had said to David and to Solomon his son, “In this house and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen from all the tribes of Israel, I will put my name forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:10; Deuteronomy 5:31–1; Leviticus 8:36"> And I will ⸤never⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “not continue to”">c</a></sup>remove the foot of Israel from upon the land that I appointed to your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “fathers”">d</a></sup>if only you will take care to do all that I have commanded them, all the law, the regulations, and the judgments by the hand of Moses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 24:3–4; Proverbs 29:12; 2 Chronicles 33:2"> And Manasseh seduced Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem to do evil more than the nations that Yahweh destroyed before the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Manasseh’s Repentance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Jeremiah 44:4–5; Zechariah 1:4"> And Yahweh spoke to Manasseh and to his people, but they would not listen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36; 2 Chronicles 36:6; Nehemiah 9:32"> So Yahweh brought the commanders of the army of the king of Assyria upon them, and they took Manasseh captive with hooks, and they bound him with bronze fetters and brought him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 10:3; Psalm 50:15; 2 Chronicles 32:26"> And when he was in distress he entreated Yahweh his God and greatly humbled himself before the God of his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “fathers”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:20; Daniel 4:25; Matthew 7:7–8"> and prayed to him. And God responded to him and heard his plea and let him return to Jerusalem to his kingdom. And Manasseh knew that Yahweh was God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:3; 1 Kings 1:33; Nehemiah 3:3"> Then afterward he built an outer wall for the city of David west of the Gihon in the valley, and for the entrance into the Gate of the Fishes. And it encircled the Ophel and raised it very high. Then he placed strong commanders in all the fortified cities of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:3–7; Hosea 14:1–3; 2 Kings 21:7"> And he removed the foreign gods and the carved image from the house of Yahweh and all the altars which he built on the mountain of the house of Yahweh and in Jerusalem, and he threw them outside the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:4; 1 Kings 18:30; 2 Chronicles 33:9"> And he restored the altar of Yahweh and offered sacrifices of peace offerings and thank offerings upon it. And he commanded Judah to serve Yahweh, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:12; 1 Kings 22:43; 2 Kings 15:4"> Nevertheless, the people offered at the high places, but only to Yahweh their God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:19; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13; Amos 7:12"> Now the remainder of the words of Manasseh and his prayer to his God and the words of the seers who spoke to him in the name of Yahweh, the God of Israel, behold, they are in the words of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 9:11; 1 John 1:9; Daniel 5:22"> And his prayer, and how he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, God">g</a></sup>responded to him, all his sin and his unfaithful acts, and the places where he built the high places and set up Asherahs and idols before he humbled himself, behold, they are written in the words of the seers.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Amon’s Reign

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 1:10; 2 Chronicles 32:33; 2 Kings 21:18–25"> And Manasseh slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “fathers”">h</a></sup>and they buried him in his house. And Amon his son became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:13–15; Luke 12:19–20; 2 Chronicles 33:1"> Amon was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 21:20; Ezekiel 20:18; 2 Kings 21:1–11"> And he did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh as Manasseh his father had done. And Amon sacrificed to all the idols that Manasseh his father had made, and he served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:12; 2 Chronicles 33:19; Jeremiah 7:26"> And he did not humble himself before Yahweh, as Manasseh his father humbled himself, but Amon himself multiplied his guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:22; 2 Chronicles 25:27–28; 2 Chronicles 24:25–26"> And his servants conspired against him and killed him in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; 2 Chronicles 34:1; 2 Chronicles 36:1"> But the people of the land struck down all who conspired against King Amon. And the people of the land made his son Josiah king in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>
