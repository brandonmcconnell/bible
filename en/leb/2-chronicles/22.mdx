## The Reign of Ahaziah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Kings 8:24–29; 2 Chronicles 36:1"> And the inhabitants of Jerusalem made Ahaziah his youngest son king in his place, for the band of men who had come with the Arabs to the camp had murdered all the older sons. And Ahaziah the son of Jehoram, the king of Judah, reigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 16:28; 2 Kings 8:26; 2 Chronicles 21:6"> Ahaziah was forty-two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Septuagint reads “twenty-two” here">a</a></sup>years old when he became king, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. And the name of his mother was Athaliah, the daughter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some translations render the term “granddaughter”">b</a></sup>of Omri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 27:12–13; Genesis 6:4–5; Judges 17:4–5"> He also walked in the ways of the house of Ahab, for his mother was his counselor for doing wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; 2 Chronicles 24:17–18; Proverbs 19:27"> And he did evil in the eyes of Yahweh as the house of Ahab had done, for they were his counselors to his destruction after the death of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 8:28–29; 2 Chronicles 19:2; Psalm 1:1"> He also walked in their counsel and went with Jehoram the son of Ahab, king of Israel, to war against Hazael king of Aram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “Syria”">c</a></sup>at Ramoth-Gilead. And the Arameans wounded Joram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 9:15; 2 Kings 8:29; 2 Kings 10:13–14"> And he returned to be healed in Jezreel because of the wounds he had suffered at Ramah, when he made war against Hazael king of Aram.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “Syria”">d</a></sup>And Azariah the son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to see Jehoram son of Ahab in Jezreel for he was sick.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:15; 2 Kings 9:21; Malachi 4:3"> But the downfall of Ahaziah was from God, and intended to come to Joram. And when he came, he went out with Jehoram to Jehu the son of Nimshi, whom Yahweh had anointed to destroy the house of Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 10:10–14"> And it happened that when Jehu was judging the house of Ahab, he found the princes of Judah and the sons of the brothers of Ahaziah who ministered to Ahaziah, and he killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:17; 2 Kings 9:27–28; 2 Chronicles 21:20–1"> And he searched for Ahaziah and captured him. And he was hiding in Samaria. And they brought him to Jehu and put him to death. And they buried him, for they said, “He is the descendant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “son”; Ahaziah was Jehoshaphat’s grandson">e</a></sup>of Jehoshaphat, who sought Yahweh with his whole heart.” And the house of Ahab had no one to exercise power over the kingdom.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Reign of Athaliah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 11:1–3; 2 Chronicles 22:2–4"> Now when Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and murdered all the ⸤royal descendants⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “seed of the kingdom”">f</a></sup>of the house of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 15:4; 2 Kings 11:2; Isaiah 65:8"> But Jehosheba the daughter of the king took Joash, the son of Ahaziah, and kidnapped him away from the sons of the king who were to be put to death. And she put him and his nurse in a private bedroom. So Jehosheba the daughter of King Jehoram, the wife of Jehoiada the priest, because she was a sister of Ahaziah, hid him from before Athaliah so that she did not put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 12:8; Psalm 73:18–19; Jeremiah 12:1"> And he was hidden with them in the house of God six years, but Athaliah reigned over the land.</span>
</div>
</div>
