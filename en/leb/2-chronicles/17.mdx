## Jehoshaphat Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24; Ephesians 6:10; Matthew 1:8"> And Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his place, and he strengthened himself against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:8; 2 Chronicles 11:5; 2 Chronicles 11:11–12"> And he put troops in all the fortified cities of Judah, and put garrisons in the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that Asa his father had taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 22:2; Isaiah 41:10; 2 Timothy 4:22"> And Yahweh was with Jehoshaphat, because he walked in the former ways of David his father and did not seek after the Baals,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 12:28; Luke 1:6; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> but he sought after the God of his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fathers”">a</a></sup>and walked in his commandments and not according to the works of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:1; Matthew 6:33; Psalm 127:1"> So Yahweh established the kingdom in his hand. And all Judah gave tribute to Jehoshaphat, and he had much wealth and honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; 2 Chronicles 15:17; 2 Chronicles 20:33"> And his heart was courageous in the ways of Yahweh. Moreover, he removed the high places and the Asherahs from Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3; Nehemiah 8:7–8; Ecclesiastes 12:9–10"> In the third year of his reign he sent his officials, Ben-Hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah, to teach in the cities of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:1–6; 2 Chronicles 19:8; Malachi 2:7"> and with them the Levites Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-Adonijah; and with them the priests Elisham and Joram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 5:46; Romans 3:2; Acts 8:40"> And they taught in Judah, and the scroll of the law of Yahweh was with them when they went around in all the cities of Judah, and they taught the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:14; Exodus 15:14–16; Proverbs 16:7"> And the fear of Yahweh was upon all the kingdoms of the lands surrounding Judah, and they did not make war against Jehoshaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:8; 2 Chronicles 9:14; 2 Samuel 8:2"> And some of the Philistines brought a gift and silver to Jehoshaphat as tribute. The Arabians also brought him seven thousand seven hundred sheep and seven thousand seven hundred goats.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:2–6; 2 Chronicles 14:6–7; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> And Jehoshaphat ⸤grew greater and greater⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “was going and becoming great to the height”">b</a></sup>and he built fortresses and storage cities in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:25–31; 2 Chronicles 26:10–15"> And he had many supplies in the cities of Judah and mighty warriors for battle in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1; Genesis 15:5; Numbers 1:18"> Now these were their enrollment by the house of their ancestors:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>of Judah, the commanders of thousands: Adnah, the commander, and with him were three hundred thousand mighty armed warriors;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and at his side,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “hand”">d</a></sup>Jehohanan the commander, and with him were two hundred and eighty thousand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 5:9; Judges 5:2; 1 Chronicles 29:9"> and at his side,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “hand”">e</a></sup>Amasiah the son of Zicri, who had made a freewill offering to Yahweh, and with him were two hundred thousand mighty armed warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:8; 2 Samuel 1:21–22"> And from Benjamin: Eliada, a powerful mighty warrior, and with him were two hundred thousand armed with bow and shield;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and at his side,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “hand”">f</a></sup>Jehozabad, and with him were one hundred and eighty thousand armed for war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 17:2; 2 Chronicles 11:12; 2 Chronicles 11:23"> These were serving the king, besides those whom the king had placed in the fortified cities throughout all Judah.</span>
</div>
