## The Reign of Jotham

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:12; Isaiah 1:1; Matthew 1:9"> Jotham was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. And the name of his mother was Jerusha the daughter of Zadok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 15:34–35; Acts 5:13; 2 Chronicles 26:4"> And he did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh, according to all that his father Uzziah had done—only he did not go into the temple of Yahweh. But the people still acted corruptly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Nehemiah 3:26–27; Jeremiah 20:2"> He built the upper gate of the house of Yahweh, and ⸤he did much restoration⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “he built much”">a</a></sup>on the wall of Ophel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:5–10; 2 Chronicles 26:9–10; Joshua 14:12–13"> And he built cities in the hill country of Judah, and he built forts and towers in the wooded places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 11:4–33; 2 Samuel 10:1–14; Jeremiah 49:1–6"> Now he fought with the king of the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">b</a></sup>and prevailed against them. And the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">c</a></sup>gave to him in that year one hundred talents of silver and ten thousand measures of wheat and ten thousand measures of barley. This is what the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">d</a></sup>rendered to him, as well as in the second and third year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:5; 2 Chronicles 19:3"> And Jotham strengthened himself, for he established his ways before Yahweh his God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:22–23; 2 Kings 15:36; 2 Chronicles 32:32–33"> Now the remainder of the words of Jotham and all his wars and his ways, behold, they are written upon the scroll of the kings of Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:1"> He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 15:38"> And Jotham slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>and they buried him in the city of David. And his son Ahaz became king in his place.</span>
</div>
