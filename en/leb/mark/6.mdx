## Rejected at Nazareth

<div className="bp">

## Herod Kills John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Mark 6:14–29; Luke 23:7–12"> And King Herod heard it, because his name had become known. And they were saying, “John, the one who baptizes, has been raised from the dead, and because of this these miraculous powers are at work in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; Malachi 4:5; Matthew 21:11"> But others were saying, “He is Elijah,” and others were saying, “He is a prophet like one of the prophets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; Matthew 27:4; Luke 9:9"> But when Herod heard it, he said, “John whom I beheaded—this one has been raised!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 11:2; Luke 3:1"> For Herod himself had sent and arrested John and bound him in prison because of Herodias, the wife of Philip his brother, because he had married her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; 1 Kings 22:14"> For John had been saying to Herod, “It is not permitted for you to have your brother’s wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Ephesians 4:26–27; Genesis 39:17–20"> So Herodias held a grudge against him and was wanting to kill him, and was not able to do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5; 2 Chronicles 24:15–22"> For Herod was afraid of John, because he knew him to be a righteous and holy man and protected him. And when he listened to him, he was greatly perplexed, and yet he listened to him gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 2:18; Genesis 40:20; 1 Peter 4:3"> And a suitable day came when Herod, on his birthday, gave a banquet for his courtiers and military tribunes and the most prominent men of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esther 1:10–12; Matthew 14:6; Daniel 5:2"> And when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Here “when ” is supplied as a component of the temporal genitive absolute participle (“came in”)">c</a></sup>the daughter of Herodias herself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="In place of “the daughter of Herodias herself” some manuscripts have “his daughter Herodias”">d</a></sup>came in and danced and pleased<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “danced, she pleased”">e</a></sup>Herod and ⸤his dinner guests⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “those reclining at table with him ”">f</a></sup>the king said to the girl, “Ask me for whatever you want, and I will give it to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esther 7:2; Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6"> And he swore to her,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts add “at length”">g</a></sup>“Anything whatever you ask me for I will give you, up to half my kingdom!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 31:31; Ezekiel 19:2–3; Genesis 27:8–11"> And she went out and said to her mother, “What should I ask for?” And she said, “The head of John the baptizer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Numbers 7:19–89; Numbers 7:13"> And she came in immediately with haste to the king and asked, saying, “I want you to give me the head of John the Baptist on a platter immediately.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:24–25; Matthew 14:9; Matthew 27:3–5"> And although he was deeply grieved, the king, because of his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">h</a></sup>oaths and ⸤dinner guests⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “those who were reclining at table”">i</a></sup>did not want to refuse her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 14:10–11"> And immediately the king sent an executioner and ordered him to bring his head. And he went and beheaded him in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he brought his head on a platter and gave it to the girl, and the girl gave it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 14:12; 2 Chronicles 24:16; Matthew 27:57–60"> And when his disciples heard this, they came and took away his corpse and placed it in a tomb.</span>

## The Feeding of Five Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 9:10; Luke 10:17; Matthew 10:2"> And the apostles regathered to Jesus and reported to him everything that they had done and that they had taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 3:20; John 6:1; Mark 1:45"> And he said to them, “You yourselves come privately to an isolated place and rest for a short time.” For those who were coming and going were many, and they did not even have time to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 8:2–9; Mark 6:45; Mark 4:36"> And they went away in the boat to an isolated place by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 6:2; Matthew 15:29–31; Mark 6:54–55"> And many people saw them leaving and recognized them, and ran there together by land from all the towns, and arrived ahead of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Luke 9:11; Numbers 27:17"> And getting out of the boat he saw the large crowd and had compassion on them, because they were like sheep ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “not having”">j</a></sup>a shepherd, and he began to teach them many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:5–15; Luke 9:12–17; Matthew 14:15–21"> And the hour had already become late when his disciples came up to him, saying, “The place is desolate and the hour is already late.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 3:21; Mark 5:31"> Send them away so that they can go into the surrounding farms and villages and purchase something to eat for themselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 15:32–33; Mark 8:2–3"> But he answered and said to them, “You give them something to eat.” And they said to him, “Should we go and purchase bread for two hundred denarii and give it to them to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:34; Mark 8:5; John 6:9"> And he said to them, “How many loaves do you have? Go look!” And when they found out, they said, “Five, and two fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 Corinthians 14:33; Matthew 15:35"> And he ordered them all to recline in groups on the green grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:14–15"> And they reclined in groups, by hundreds and by fifties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Mark 7:34; Luke 9:16"> And taking the five loaves and the two fish and looking up to heaven, he gave thanks and broke the loaves and gave them to his disciples so that they could set them before them. And he distributed the two fish to them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 9:17; Mark 8:8–9; Matthew 15:37–38"> And they all ate and were satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 8:19–20"> And they picked up the broken pieces, twelve baskets full, and of the fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And those who ate the loaves were five thousand men.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalm 9:10; Philippians 3:10"> And as they were getting out of the boat, people recognized him immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 2:1–3; Mark 3:7–11"> They ran about through that whole region and began to carry around those ⸤who were sick⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “who were having badly”">m</a></sup>on stretchers, wherever they heard that he was.</span>

And all those who touched it were healed.</span>
