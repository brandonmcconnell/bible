## A Demon-possessed Gerasene Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Matthew 8:28–34; Mark 4:35"> And they came to the other side of the sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the Sea of Galilee">a</a></sup>to the region of the Gerasenes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Most later manuscripts read “Gadarenes,” while other manuscripts read “Gergesenes” here">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:23; Isaiah 65:4; Mark 5:8"> And as he was getting out of the boat, immediately from the tombs a man with an unclean spirit went to meet him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Luke 8:29; Daniel 4:32–33"> who ⸤lived⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “had his dwelling”">c</a></sup>among the tombs. And no one was able to bind him any longer, not even with a chain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 3:7–8"> because he had often been bound with shackles and chains, and the chains had been torn apart by him, and the shackles had been shattered. And no one was strong enough to subdue him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:7–8; 1 Kings 18:28; John 8:44"> And during every night and day among the tombs and on the mountains he was crying out and cutting himself with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:19; Psalm 66:3; Luke 4:41"> And when he saw Jesus from a distance, he ran and knelt down before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; Acts 16:17; Matthew 26:63"> And crying out with a loud voice he said, “⸤What have I to do with you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “what to me and to you”">d</a></sup>, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I implore you by God, do not torment me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 1:25; Mark 9:25–26; Acts 16:18"> (For he was saying to him, “Come out of the man, unclean spirit!”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 8:30; Mark 5:15; Matthew 26:53"> And he was asking him “⸤What is your name⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “what name to you”">e</a></sup>And he said to him, “My name is Legion, because we are many.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Mark 5:13"> And he was imploring him many times that he would not send them out of the region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 11:7–8; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> Now a large herd of pigs was there at the hill feeding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Job 1:10–12; Luke 22:31–32"> and they implored him, saying, “Send us to the pigs so that we may enter into them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; 1 Peter 3:22; Job 2:6"> And he permitted them. And the unclean spirits came out and entered into the pigs, and the herd—about two thousand—rushed headlong down the steep slope into the sea and were drowned in the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 8:34; Matthew 8:33"> And their herdsmen fled and reported it in the town and in the countryside, and they came to see what it was that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Samuel 6:20–21"> And they came to Jesus and saw the demon-possessed man sitting there clothed and in his right mind—the one who had had the legion—and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And those who had seen it described to them what had happened to the demon-possessed man, and about the pigs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 1:24; Luke 8:37"> And they began to urge him to depart from their region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:38–39; Philippians 1:23–24; Mark 5:17"> And as he was getting into the boat, the man who had been demon-possessed began to implore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive here (“began to implore”)">f</a></sup>him that he could go with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 66:16; John 4:29; Isaiah 38:9–20"> And he did not permit him, but said to him, “Go to your home to your people and tell them all that the Lord has done for you, and that he has had mercy on you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:31; Matthew 4:25"> And he went away and began to proclaim in the Decapolis all that Jesus had done for him, and they were all astonished.</span>

## A Woman Healed and a Daughter Raised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 4:1; Luke 8:40"> And after Jesus had crossed over again in the boat to the other side, a large crowd gathered to him, and he was beside the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8; Mark 5:35–36"> And one of the rulers of the synagogue came—Jairus by name—and when he saw him, he fell down at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Luke 4:40; Mark 16:18"> And he was imploring him many times, saying, “My little daughter ⸤is at the point of death⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “has finally”">g</a></sup>Come, lay your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">h</a></sup>hands on her, so that she will get well and will live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 5:31; Mark 3:20; Luke 7:6"> And he went with him, and a large crowd was following him and pressing around him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:25–27; Luke 8:43–44; Matthew 9:20–22"> And there was a woman who was ⸤suffering from hemorrhages⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “with a flow of blood”">i</a></sup>twelve years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22; Psalm 108:12"> And she had endured many things under many physicians, and had spent ⸤all that she had⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “all with her”">j</a></sup>and had received no help at all, but instead ⸤became worse⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “came back for the worse”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:12; Matthew 14:36; Acts 5:15"> When she heard about Jesus, she came up in the crowd behind him and touched his cloak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> for she was saying, “If I touch just his clothing, I will be healed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Psalm 30:2; Mark 3:10"> And immediately her ⸤hemorrhage stopped⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “flow of blood was dried up”">l</a></sup>and she realized in her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">m</a></sup>body that she was healed of her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">n</a></sup>suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Luke 5:17"> And immediately Jesus, perceiving in himself that power had gone out from himself, turned around in the crowd and said, “Who touched my clothing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:45; Luke 9:12"> And his disciples said to him, “You see the crowd pressing upon you, and you say ‘Who touched me?’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And he was looking around to see the one who had done this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:47; Luke 1:29; Luke 1:12"> So the woman, frightened and trembling, knowing what had happened to her, came and fell down before him and told him the whole truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 8:48; Matthew 9:22; Luke 7:50"> But he said to her, “Daughter, your faith has healed you. Go in peace and be well from your suffering.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 8:49; John 11:39; Mark 5:22"> While he was still speaking, they came from the synagogue ruler’s house saying, “Your daughter has died. Why trouble the Teacher further?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 8:50; Matthew 9:28–29; John 11:40"> But Jesus, ignoring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “overhearing”">o</a></sup>⸤what was said⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the report that was spoken”">p</a></sup>told the ruler of the synagogue, “Do not be afraid—only believe!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 14:33; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And he did not allow anyone to follow along with him except Peter and James and John, the brother of James.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:23–24; Luke 8:52–53; Mark 5:22"> And they came to the house of the ruler of the synagogue and saw a commotion, and people weeping and wailing loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 20:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14"> And when he entered, he said to them, “Why are you agitated and weeping? The child is not dead, but is sleeping.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 9:24–25"> And they began laughing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive here (“began laughing”)">q</a></sup>at him. But he sent them all out and took along the father and mother of the child, and those who were with him, and went in to where the child was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Luke 7:14–15; Luke 8:54–55"> And taking hold of the child’s hand, he said to her, “Talitha koum!” (which is translated, “Little girl, I say to you, get up!”),</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 4:41; Acts 3:10–13; Mark 7:37"> and immediately the girl stood up and began walking around<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive here (“began walking around”)">r</a></sup>(for she was twelve years old). And ⸤immediately they were utterly and completely astonished⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “immediately they were astonished with great astonishment”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:43"> And he commanded them strictly that no one should learn of this, and said to give her something to eat.</span>
</div>
</div>
