## A Man with a Withered Hand Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 6:6–11; Mark 1:21"> And he entered into the synagogue again, and a man who had a withered hand was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 37:32; Luke 14:1; Luke 6:7"> And they were watching him closely to see if he would heal him on the Sabbath, in order that they could accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 1:28–30; Isaiah 42:4; Luke 6:8"> And he said to the man who had the withered hand, “Come into the middle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:9; Matthew 12:10–12; Hosea 6:6"> And he said to them, “Is it permitted on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save life or to kill?” But they were silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 6:10; Ephesians 4:18; Romans 11:25"> And looking around at them with anger, grieved at the hardness of their hearts, he said to the man, “Stretch out your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">a</a></sup>hand.” And he stretched it out, and his hand was restored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:16; Mark 12:13; Matthew 12:14"> And the Pharisees went out immediately with the Herodians ⸤and began to conspire⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “began to give counsel”; the imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive (“began to”)">b</a></sup>against him with regard to how they could destroy him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Heals Crowds by the Sea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 4:25; Luke 6:17; Matthew 12:15"> And Jesus went away with his disciples to the sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Sea of Galilee">c</a></sup>and a great crowd from Galilee followed him. And from Judea</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Isaiah 34:5; Matthew 11:21"> and from Jerusalem and from Idumea and the other side of the Jordan and around Tyre and Sidon a great crowd came to him because they heard all that he was doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 5:30; John 6:15"> And he told his disciples that a small boat should stand ready for him because of the crowd, so that they would not press upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56; Matthew 4:23"> For he had healed many, so that all those who ⸤were suffering from diseases⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “had suffering”">d</a></sup>pressed about him in order that they could touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 1:23–24; Luke 8:28"> And the unclean spirits, whenever they saw him, were falling down before him and crying out, saying, “You are the Son of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:16; Mark 1:25; Mark 1:34"> And he warned them strictly that they should not make him known.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Selection of the Twelve Apostles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 9:1; Luke 6:12–16; Matthew 10:1–4"> And he went up on the mountain and summoned those whom he wanted, and they came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Luke 9:1–6; John 15:16"> And he appointed twelve,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts add “whom he also named apostles”">e</a></sup>so that they would be with him and so that he could send them out to preach</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and to have authority to expel demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:42; Acts 1:13; Matthew 16:16–18"> And he appointed the twelve.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Most Greek manuscripts omit the phrase “and he appointed the twelve”">f</a></sup>And to Simon he gave the name Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Mark 14:33; Jeremiah 23:29"> and James the son of Zebedee and John the brother of James (and he gave to them the name Boanerges, that is, “Sons of Thunder”),</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Mark 2:14; Matthew 9:9"> and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Zealot,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the Cananean,” but according to BDAG 507 s.v., this term has no relation at all to the geographical terms for Cana or Canaan, but is derived from the Aramaic term for “enthusiast, zealot” (see Luke 6:15 ; Acts 1:13 )">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 6:71; John 13:2; Acts 1:16–25"> and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A House Divided Cannot Stand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 6:31; John 4:31–34; Mark 9:28"> And he went home, and the crowd gathered again, so that they were not even able to eat a meal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 26:24; John 10:20; Mark 3:31"> And when ⸤his family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally those “close to him”">h</a></sup>heard this, they went out to restrain him, for they were saying, “He has lost his mind!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:1; Matthew 10:25; Matthew 9:34"> And the scribes who had come down from Jerusalem were saying, “He is possessed by Beelzebul!” and “By the ruler of the demons he expels the demons!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 11:17–23; Matthew 12:25–30; Matthew 13:34"> And he called them to himself and was speaking to them in parables, “How can Satan expel Satan?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 11:14; Isaiah 19:2–3; 2 Samuel 20:6"> And if a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom is not able to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="James 3:16; Psalm 133:1; Galatians 5:15"> And if a house is divided against itself, that house will not be able to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if Satan has risen up against himself and is divided, he is not able to stand, but is at an end!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–26; Matthew 12:29; Luke 11:21–23"> But no one is able to enter into the house of a strong man and plunder his property unless he first ties up the strong man, and then he can thoroughly plunder his house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; 1 John 5:16"> “Truly I say to you that all the sins and the blasphemies will be forgiven the sons of men, however much they blaspheme.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 12:31–32; Luke 12:10; 2 Thessalonians 1:9"> But whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit does not have forgiveness ⸤forever⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">i</a></sup>but is guilty of an eternal sin”—</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 10:20; Mark 3:22"> because they were saying, “He has an unclean spirit.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Mother and Brothers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:19–21; Matthew 12:46–50"> And his mother and his brothers arrived, and standing outside, they sent word to him to summon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And a crowd was sitting around him, and they told him, “Behold, your mother and your brothers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some manuscripts add “and your sisters”">j</a></sup>are outside looking for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 3:21; Deuteronomy 33:9; Mark 6:3"> And he answered them and said, “Who is my mother or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and”">k</a></sup>my brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 12:49–50; Luke 11:27–28; Romans 8:29"> And looking around at those who were sitting around him in a circle, he said, “Behold, my mother and my brothers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:17; Matthew 7:21; 1 John 3:22–23"> For whoever does the will of God, this person is my brother and sister and mother.”</span>
</div>
