## The Parable of the Tenant Farmers in the Vineyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 13:34; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> And he began to speak to them in parables: “A man planted a vineyard, and put a fence around it, and dug a trough for the winepress, and built a watchtower, and leased it to tenant farmers, and went on a journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> And he sent a slave to the tenant farmers at the proper time, so that he could collect some of the fruit of the vineyard from the tenant farmers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> And they seized him and beat him and sent him away empty-handed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And again he sent to them another slave, and that one they struck on the head and dishonored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> And he sent another, and that one they killed. And he sent many others, some of whom they beat and some of whom they killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> He had one more, a beloved son. Last of all he sent him to them, saying, ‘They will respect my son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> But those tenant farmers said to one another, ‘This is the heir. Come, let us kill him and the inheritance will be ours!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> And they seized and killed him and threw him out of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> What<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “What then”">a</a></sup>will the owner of the vineyard do? He will come and destroy the tenant farmers and give the vineyard to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> Have you not read this scripture:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The stone which the builders rejected,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">this has become ⸤the cornerstone⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the head of the corner”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">This came about from the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it is marvelous in our eyes’?”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 118:22–23">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> And they were seeking to arrest him, and they were afraid of the crowd, because they knew that he had told the parable with reference to them. And they left him and went away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paying Taxes to Caesar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> And they sent some of the Pharisees and the Herodians to him so that they could catch him unawares in a statement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> And when they came, they said to him, “Teacher, we know that you are truthful and ⸤you do not care what anyone thinks⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “it is not a care to you concerning anyone”">d</a></sup>⸤because you do not regard the opinion of people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “because you do not look at the face of men”">e</a></sup>but teach the way of God in truth. Is it permitted to pay taxes to Caesar or not? Should we pay or should we not pay?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> But because he knew their hypocrisy, he said to them, “Why are you testing me? Bring me a denarius so that I can look at it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> So they brought one. And he said to them, “Whose image and inscription is this?” And they said to him, “Caesar’s.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> And Jesus said to them, “Give to Caesar the things of Caesar, and to God the things of God!” And they were utterly amazed at him.</span>

## A Question About Marriage and the Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> And Sadducees—who say there is no resurrection—came up to him and began to ask<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive here (“began to ask”)">f</a></sup>him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> “Teacher, Moses wrote for us that if someone’s brother dies and he leaves behind a wife and does not leave a child, that his brother should take the wife and ⸤father⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “raise up”">g</a></sup>descendants for his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> There were seven brothers, and the first took a wife. And when he died, he did not leave descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the second took her, and he died without leaving descendants. And the third likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the seven did not leave descendants. Last of all the woman also died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurrection, when they rise, ⸤whose⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “who of them”">h</a></sup>wife will she be? For the seven had her as wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Jesus said to them, “Are you not deceived because of this, because you do not know the scriptures or the power of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> For when they rise from the dead, they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> Now concerning the dead, that they are raised, have you not read in the book of Moses ⸤in the passage about the bush⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “at the bush”">i</a></sup>how God spoke to him, saying, ‘I am the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob’?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 3:6">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He is not God of the dead, but of the living. You are very much mistaken!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Greatest Commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> And one of the scribes came up and heard them debating. When he saw that he answered them well, he asked him, “Which commandment is the most important of all?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Jesus answered, “The most important is, ‘Listen, Israel! The Lord our God, the Lord is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> And you shall love the Lord your God from your whole heart and from your whole soul and from your whole mind and from your whole strength.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 6:4–5 ; Josh 22:5">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> The second is this: ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 19:18">l</a></sup>There is no other commandment greater than these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> And the scribe said to him, “That is true, Teacher. You have said ⸤correctly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “in accordance with truth”">m</a></sup>that he is one and there is no other except him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> And to love him from your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">n</a></sup>whole heart and from your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">o</a></sup>whole understanding and from your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">p</a></sup>whole strength, and to love your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">q</a></sup>neighbor as yourself, is much more than all whole burnt offerings and sacrifices.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> And Jesus, when he saw that he had answered thoughtfully, said to him, “You are not far from the kingdom of God.” And no one dared to put a question to him any longer.</span>

## A Poor Widow’s Offering

<div className="bp">
