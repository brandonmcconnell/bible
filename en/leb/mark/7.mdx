## Human Traditions and God’s Commandments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:14; Mark 7:13; Colossians 2:8"> (For the Pharisees and all the Jews do not eat unless they wash their hands ⸤ritually⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “with the fist”; although the exact meaning of the phrase is uncertain, there is general agreement it has to do with the ritual or ceremonial nature of the handwashing">b</a></sup>thus holding fast to the traditions of the elders.</span>

## Defilement from Within

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:10; Isaiah 6:9; Proverbs 8:5"> And summoning the crowd again, he said to them, “Listen to me, all of you, and understand:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Leviticus 11:42–47; Titus 1:15"> There is nothing outside of a person that is able to defile him by going into him. But the things that go out of a person are the things that defile a person.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Most later manuscripts add v. 16 , “If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear.”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Mark 9:28"> And when he entered into the house away from the crowd, his disciples asked him about the parable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 15:16–17; Hebrews 5:11; Isaiah 28:9–10"> And he said to them, “So are you also without understanding? Do you not understand that everything that is outside that goes into a person is not able to defile him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 15:17; 1 Corinthians 6:13; Colossians 2:21–22"> For it does not enter into his heart but into his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">t</a></sup>stomach, and goes out into the latrine”—thus declaring all foods clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 3:6; James 1:14–15; James 4:1"> And he said, “What comes out of a person, that defiles a person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 8:7–8; James 4:1–3"> For from within, from the heart of people, come evil plans, sexual immoralities, thefts, murders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 20:15; Matthew 6:23; 1 Peter 2:15"> adulteries, acts of greed, malicious deeds, deceit, licentiousness, ⸤envy⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “the evil eye”">u</a></sup>abusive speech, pride, foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 7:15; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 3:17"> All these evil things come from within and defile a person.”</span>

## A Syrophoenician Woman’s Great Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> And from there he set out and went to the region of Tyre. And when he entered into a house, he wanted no one to know, and yet he was not able to escape notice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> But immediately a woman whose young daughter was possessed by an unclean spirit, when she heard about him, came and fell down at his feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> Now the woman was a Greek—a Syrophoenician by nationality—and she was asking him that he would expel the demon from her daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> And he said to her, “Let the children be satisfied first, for it is not right to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> But she answered and said to him, “Lord, even the dogs under the table eat the children’s crumbs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> And he said to her, “Because of this statement, go! The demon has gone out of your daughter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> And when she went to her home, she found the child lying on the bed and the demon gone.</span>

## A Man Deaf and Unable to Speak Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> And again he went away from the region of Tyre and came through Sidon to the Sea of Galilee, within the region of the Decapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> And they brought to him a man who was deaf and had difficulty speaking, and they were imploring him that he would place his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">v</a></sup>hand on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> And he took him away from the crowd by himself and put his fingers into his ears, and after spitting, he touched his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> And looking up to heaven, he sighed and said to him, “Ephphatha!” (that is, “Be opened!”).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalm 33:9; Mark 2:12"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “And immediately”">w</a></sup>his ears were opened and ⸤his difficulty in speaking was removed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “the bond of his tongue was loosened”">x</a></sup>and he began to speak normally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> And he ordered them that they should say nothing, but as much as he ordered them not to, they proclaimed it even more instead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Mark 5:42; Exodus 4:10–11"> And they were amazed beyond all measure, saying, “He has done all things well! He even makes the deaf hear and the mute speak!”</span>
