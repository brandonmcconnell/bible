## Jesus Taken to Pilate

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 23:1–5; Luke 22:66"> And as soon as morning came, after formulating a plan, the chief priests, with the elders and scribes and the whole Sanhedrin, tied up Jesus, led him away, and handed him over to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Matthew 27:11–14; 1 Timothy 6:13"> And Pilate asked him, “Are you the king of the Jews?” And he answered him and said, “You say so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Mark 15:5; John 18:29–31"> And the chief priests began to accuse<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive here (“began to accuse”)">a</a></sup>him of many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 19:10; Matthew 27:13; Matthew 26:62"> So Pilate asked him again, saying, “Do you not answer anything? See how many charges they are bringing against you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Zechariah 3:8; Psalm 71:7"> But Jesus did not answer anything further, so that Pilate was astonished.</span>

## Pilate Releases Barabbas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:39–40; Acts 25:9; Acts 24:27"> Now at each feast he customarily released<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as customary here (“customarily released”)">b</a></sup>for them one prisoner whom they requested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:18–19; Matthew 27:16; Luke 23:25"> And the one named Barabbas<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Barabbas” means “son of the father” in Aramaic">c</a></sup>was imprisoned with the rebels who had committed murder in the rebellion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the crowd came up and began to ask him to do as he customarily did<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as customary here (“customarily did”)">d</a></sup>for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:14–16; John 19:4–5; John 18:39"> So Pilate answered them, saying, “Do you want me to release for you the king of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Titus 3:3; Genesis 4:4–6"> (For he realized that the chief priests had handed him over because of envy.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:14; John 18:40; Hosea 5:1"> But the chief priests incited the crowd so that he would release for them Barabbas instead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 9:6–7; Zechariah 9:9"> So Pilate answered and said to them again, “Then what do you want me to do with the one whom you call the king of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they shouted again, “Crucify him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:41; Luke 23:23–24; Isaiah 53:3"> And Pilate said to them, “Why? What evil has he done?” But they shouted even louder, “Crucify him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:1; Matthew 27:26; Isaiah 50:6"> So Pilate, because he wanted ⸤to satisfy⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to make sufficient”">e</a></sup>the crowd, released for them Barabbas. ## Jesus Is Mocked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:9; Matthew 27:27–31; John 18:28"> So the soldiers led him away into the palace (that is, the governor’s residence) and called together the whole cohort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 27:28–30; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> And they put a purple cloak on him, and after weaving a crown of thorns they placed it on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:2; Genesis 37:10"> And they began to greet him, “Hail, king of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Isaiah 53:3–5; Luke 23:11"> And they repeatedly struck<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as iterative here (“repeatedly struck”)">f</a></sup>him on the head with a reed, and were spitting on him, and ⸤they knelt down⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “bending the knees”">g</a></sup>and did obeisance to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:16; Matthew 27:31"> And when they had mocked him, they stripped him of the purple cloak and put his own clothes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “his clothes” in place of “his own clothes”">h</a></sup>on him, and they led him out so that they could crucify him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Is Crucified

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Luke 23:26; Romans 16:13"> And they forced a certain man who was passing by, Simon of Cyrene (the father of Alexander and Rufus), who was coming from the country, to carry his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 27:33–44; John 19:17–27; Luke 23:27–38"> And they brought him to the place Golgotha (which is translated “Place of a Skull”).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; John 19:28–30; Luke 23:36"> And they attempted to give him wine mixed with myrrh, but he did not take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 23:34; John 19:23–24; Matthew 27:35–36"> And they crucified him and divided his clothes among themselves by casting lots for them to see who should take what.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 19:14; Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45"> Now it was the third hour when they crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Matthew 2:2; John 19:18–22"> And the inscription of the charge against him was written, “The king of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 23:32–33; Matthew 27:38; John 19:18"> And with him they crucified two robbers, one on his right and one on his left.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Most later Greek manuscripts add v. 28 (a quotation from Isa 53:12 ) after v. 27 , “And the scripture was fulfilled that says, ‘And he was counted with the lawless ones’ ”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Psalm 109:25; Lamentations 1:12"> And those who passed by reviled him, shaking their heads and saying, “Aha! The one who would destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> save yourself by coming down from the cross!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:23–24; Matthew 27:41–43; Luke 23:35–37"> In the same way also the chief priests, along with the scribes, were mocking him to one another, saying, “He saved others; he is not able to save himself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Luke 23:39–43; Zechariah 9:9"> Let the Christ, the king of Israel, come down now from the cross, so that we may see and believe! Even those who were crucified with him were reviling him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Dies on the Cross

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Luke 23:44–45; Amos 8:9–10"> And when the sixth hour came, darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 22:1; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> And at the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, “Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?” (which is translated, “My God, my God, ⸤why⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “for what reason ”">j</a></sup>have you forsaken me?”)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 22:1">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:47–49; Mark 9:11–13; Matthew 17:11–13"> And some of the bystanders, when they heard it, said, “Behold, he is summoning Elijah!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalm 69:21; Mark 15:23; John 19:28–30"> And someone ran and filled a sponge with sour wine, put it on a reed, and gave it to him to drink, saying, “Leave him alone! Let us see if Elijah is coming to take him down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 19:30; Luke 23:46; Matthew 27:50"> But Jesus uttered a loud cry and expired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–23; Luke 23:45; Hebrews 9:3–12"> And the curtain of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Luke 23:47–48; Matthew 27:43"> And when the centurion who was standing opposite him saw that he expired like this, he said, “Truly this man was God’s Son!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> And there were also women observing from a distance, among whom were Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the younger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or perhaps “the short,” referring to stature">l</a></sup>and Joses,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="This name appears in Matt 27:56 as “Joseph”">m</a></sup>and Salome,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> who used to follow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as customary here (“used to follow”)">n</a></sup>him and serve him when he was in Galilee, and many other women who went up with him to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>

## Jesus Is Buried

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42; John 19:31"> And when it was already evening, since it was the day of preparation (that is, the day before the Sabbath),</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 2:38; Luke 2:25; Luke 23:51"> Joseph of Arimathea, a prominent member of the council who was also himself looking forward to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “waiting for”">o</a></sup>the kingdom of God, came acting courageously and went in to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 19:31–37"> And Pilate was surprised that he was already dead, and summoning the centurion, asked him whether he had died already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 27:58; John 19:38; Mark 15:39"> And when he learned of it from the centurion, he granted the corpse to Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 22:16"> And after purchasing a linen cloth and taking him down, he wrapped him in the linen cloth and placed him in a tomb that had been cut from the rock. And he rolled a stone over the entrance of the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 15:40; Mark 16:1; Luke 23:55–2"> Now Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses saw where he was placed.</span>
