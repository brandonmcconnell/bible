## A Paralytic Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:47; Luke 5:18; Luke 18:35–38"> And when he entered again into Capernaum after some days, it became known that he was at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Mark 1:45; Psalm 40:9"> And many had gathered, so that there was no longer room, not even at the door, and he was speaking the word to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:18–26; Matthew 9:1–8; Matthew 4:24"> And they came bringing to him a paralytic, carried by four of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 5:19; Deuteronomy 22:8"> And when they were not able to bring him to him because of the crowd, they removed the roof where he was. And after digging through, they lowered the stretcher on which the paralytic was lying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; James 5:15; Mark 2:9–10"> And when Jesus saw their faith, he said to the paralytic, “Child, your sins are forgiven.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:21–22; Mark 8:17; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> Now some of the scribes were sitting there and reasoning in their hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Luke 5:21; Micah 7:18"> “Why does this man speak like this? He is blaspheming! Who is able to forgive sins except God alone?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 139:2; Acts 8:22; Luke 5:22"> And immediately Jesus, perceiving in his spirit that they were reasoning like this within themselves, said to them, “Why are you considering these things in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 5:22–25; Matthew 9:5"> Which is easier to say to the paralytic, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say ‘Get up and pick up your stretcher and walk’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Matthew 9:6–8; John 5:20–27"> But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins,”—he said to the paralytic—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 1:41; John 5:8–10; John 6:63"> “I say to you, get up, pick up your stretcher, and go to your home.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 9:33; Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:16"> And he got up and immediately picked up his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">a</a></sup>stretcher and went out in front of them all, so that they were all amazed and glorified God, saying, “We have never seen anything like this!”</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:27–32; Luke 6:15; Matthew 9:9–13"> And as he was passing by, he saw Levi the son of Alphaeus sitting at the tax booth, and he said to him, “Follow me!” And he stood up and followed him.</span>
</div>

## On Fasting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:12; Matthew 6:18; Matthew 9:14–17"> And John’s disciples and the Pharisees were fasting, and they came and said to him, “⸤Why⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “for what” reason">h</a></sup>do the disciples of John and the disciples of the Pharisees fast, but your disciples do not fast?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 25:1–10; Genesis 29:22; Judges 14:10–11"> And Jesus said to them, “The ⸤bridegroom’s attendants⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the bridal chamber”">i</a></sup>are not able to fast while the bridegroom is with them, are they?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The negative construction in Greek anticipates a negative answer here, indicated in the translation by the phrase “are they ”">j</a></sup>As long a time as they have the bridegroom with them, they are not able to fast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Revelation 21:9; Isaiah 54:5"> But days will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them, and then they will fast in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:16; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Isaiah 57:16"> No one sews a patch of unshrunken cloth on an old garment. ⸤Otherwise⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “but if not”">k</a></sup>the patch pulls away from it—the new from the old—and the tear becomes worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:17; Psalm 119:83; Luke 5:37–38"> And no one puts new wine into old wineskins. ⸤Otherwise⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “but if not”">l</a></sup>the wine will burst the wineskins and the wine is destroyed and the wineskins too. But new wine is put into new wineskins.”</span>
</div>

## Plucking Grain on the Sabbath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–8; Deuteronomy 23:24–25; Luke 6:1–5"> And it happened that he was going through the grain fields on the Sabbath, and his disciples began to make their way while plucking off the heads of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:10; Hebrews 12:3; Matthew 12:2"> And the Pharisees began to say<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive here (“began to say”)">m</a></sup>to him, “Behold, why are they doing what is not permitted on the Sabbath?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 10:26; Matthew 22:31"> And he said to them, “Have you never read what David did when he had need and he and those who were with him were hungry—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; Leviticus 24:5–9; 1 Samuel 21:1"> how he entered into the house of God in the time of Abiathar the high priest and ate the bread of the presentation, which it is not permitted to eat (except the priests) and also gave it to those who were with him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 2:16; Luke 6:9; Deuteronomy 5:14"> And he said to them, “The Sabbath was established for people, and not people for the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 12:8; Revelation 1:10; John 5:17"> So then, the Son of Man is lord even of the Sabbath.”</span>
