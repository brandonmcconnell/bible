<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 9:28–36; Isaiah 33:17; Matthew 17:1–8"> And after six days, Jesus took along Peter and James and John, and led them to a high mountain by themselves alone. And he was transfigured before them,</span>

## A Demon-possessed Boy Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:37–42; Matthew 17:14–20; Luke 11:53–54"> And when they came to the disciples, they saw a large crowd around them and scribes arguing with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:30; Mark 9:2–3"> And immediately the whole crowd, when they saw him, were amazed, and ran up to him and greeted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 5:30–32; Mark 8:11"> And he asked them, “What are you arguing about with them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Luke 11:14; John 4:47"> And one individual from the crowd answered him, “Teacher, I brought to you my son who has ⸤a spirit that makes him mute⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “a mute spirit”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 11:23; Matthew 17:16"> And whenever it seizes him, it throws him down and he foams at the mouth and grinds his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">e</a></sup>teeth and becomes paralyzed. And I told your disciples that they should expel it, and they were not able to do so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; John 20:27; Numbers 14:27"> And he answered them and said, “O unbelieving generation! ⸤How long⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “until when”">f</a></sup>will I be with you? ⸤How long⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “until when”">g</a></sup>must I put up with you? Bring him to me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:26; Luke 4:35; John 8:44"> And they brought him to him. And when he saw him, the spirit immediately convulsed him, and falling on the ground, he began to roll around, foaming at the mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:33; Acts 4:22; Mark 5:25"> And he asked his father how long it was since this had been happening to him. And he said, “From childhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:22–28; Mark 5:19; Matthew 20:34"> And often it has thrown him both into fire and into water, in order that it could destroy him. But if you are able to do anything, have compassion on us and help us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:40; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:23"> But Jesus said to him, “If you are able! All things are possible for the one who believes!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 17:5; Hebrews 12:2; Ephesians 2:8"> Immediately the father of the child cried out and said, “I believe! Help my unbelief!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:18; Luke 9:42; Mark 9:15"> Now when Jesus saw that a crowd was running together, he rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, “Mute and deaf spirit, I command you, come out of him, and enter into him no more!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:20; Mark 1:26; Mark 9:18"> And it came out, screaming and convulsing him greatly, and he became as if he were dead, so that most of them said, “He has died!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 3:7; Mark 1:41"> But Jesus took hold of his hand and raised him up, and he stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Mark 7:17; Matthew 15:15"> And after he had entered into the house, his disciples asked him privately, “Why were we not able to expel it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 5:15; Daniel 9:3; Matthew 17:20"> And he said to them, “This kind can come out by nothing except by prayer.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts add “and fasting”">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
