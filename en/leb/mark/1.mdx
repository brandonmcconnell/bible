## John the Baptist Begins His Ministry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 5:11–12; Matthew 4:3; Matthew 3:17"> The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts add “the Son of God”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:10; Luke 1:76"> Just as it is written in the prophet Isaiah,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I am sending my messenger before your face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who will prepare your way,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 3:3; Luke 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">the voice of one shouting in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Prepare the way of the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">make straight his paths!’ ”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 40:3">b</a></sup></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:3–4; Matthew 3:6; Acts 13:24–25"> John was there baptizing in the wilderness, proclaiming<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and proclaiming”">c</a></sup>a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–42; Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:5–6"> And all the Judean region and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem went out to him and were being baptized by him in the Jordan River, confessing their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Leviticus 11:22; Matthew 3:4"> And John was dressed in camel’s hair and a belt made of leather around his waist, and he ate locusts and wild honey.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:5; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 19:4–6"> I baptized you with water, but he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.”</span>

## Jesus Calls His First Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42; John 6:8"> And as he was passing by along the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew, Simon’s brother, casting a net into the sea (for they were fishermen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 5:10; Matthew 4:19–20; Acts 2:38–41"> And Jesus said to them, “⸤Follow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “come after”">f</a></sup>me and I will make you become fishers of people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:28–30; Luke 5:11; Mark 10:28–31"> And immediately they left their nets and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 5:37; Acts 1:13; Mark 10:35"> And going on a little farther, he saw James the son of Zebedee and his brother John, and they were in the boat mending the nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26; Mark 10:29"> And immediately he called them, and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired men and went away after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:2; Mark 1:39; Matthew 4:23"> And they went into Capernaum and immediately on the Sabbath he began to teach in the synagogue.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “he went into the synagogue and began to teach”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## A Man with an Unclean Spirit Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54; Luke 4:32"> And they were amazed at his teaching, because he was teaching them like one who had authority, and not like the scribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Mark 7:25; Mark 1:34"> And so then there was a man in their synagogue with an unclean spirit, and he cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; James 2:19; Acts 3:14"> saying, “⸤Leave us alone⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “what to us and to you”">h</a></sup>Jesus the Nazarene! Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are—the Holy One of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 1:34; Luke 4:41; Acts 16:17"> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be silent, and come out of him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:26; Mark 9:20; Luke 11:22"> And after convulsing him and crying out with a loud voice, the unclean spirit came out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:31; Mark 7:37; Luke 9:1"> And they were all amazed, so that they began to discuss with one another, saying, “What is this? A new teaching with authority! He even commands the unclean spirits and they obey him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 9:31; Luke 4:37; Mark 1:45"> And the report about him then went out everywhere in the whole surrounding region of Galilee.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 4:38–39; Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:21"> And so then he departed from the synagogue and came into the house of Simon and Andrew with James and John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:5; Mark 5:23; John 11:3"> Now Simon’s mother-in-law was lying down, suffering with a fever, and at once they told him about her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:41; Psalm 116:12; Mark 5:41"> And he came and raised her up by taking hold of her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">i</a></sup>hand, and the fever left her, and she began to serve them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 8:16; Mark 3:2; Luke 4:40"> Now when it was evening, when the sun had set, they began bringing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The imperfect tense has been translated as ingressive here (“began bringing”)">j</a></sup>to him all those ⸤who were sick⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “who were having badly”">k</a></sup>and those who were demon-possessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Acts 13:44"> And the whole town was gathered together at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 3:12; Matthew 4:23"> And he healed many ⸤who were sick⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “who were having badly”">l</a></sup>with various diseases and expelled many demons. And he did not permit the demons to speak, because they knew him.</span>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Luke 5:16; Luke 22:39–46"> And getting up early in the morning while it was very dark, he departed and went to a deserted place, and there he was praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Simon and those who were with him searched diligently for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:19; Mark 1:5; Zechariah 11:11"> And they found him and said to him, “Everyone is looking for you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:43; John 17:8; Isaiah 61:1–3"> And he said to them, “Let us go elsewhere, into the neighboring rural towns, so that I can preach there also, because I have come out for this very reason.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:21; Luke 4:43–44"> And he went into all Galilee preaching in their synagogues and expelling demons.</span>

## A Leper Cleansed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 5:12–14; Matthew 8:2–4; Leviticus 13:1–14"> And a leper came to him, entreating him and kneeling down, saying<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and saying”">m</a></sup>to him, “If you are willing, you are able to make me clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:15"> And becoming angry,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “having compassion”">n</a></sup>he stretched out his hand and touched him, and said to him, “I am willing; be made clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 15:3; Mark 5:29; John 4:50–53"> And immediately the leprosy went away from him and he was made clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:30; Mark 7:36; Luke 8:56"> And warning him sternly, he sent him away at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 14:1–32; Luke 17:14; Matthew 8:4"> And he said to him, “See to it that you say nothing to anyone, but go, show yourself to the priest and bring for your cleansing the things which Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Matthew 9:31; John 6:2"> But he went out and began to proclaim it freely and to spread abroad the account, so that he was no longer able to enter publicly into a town. But he was staying outside in deserted places, and they were coming to him from all directions.</span>
</div>
