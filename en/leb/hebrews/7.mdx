## The Greatness of Melchizedek

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:18–20; Psalm 57:2; Hebrews 6:20"> For this Melchizedek, king of Salem, priest of the most high God, who met Abraham as he was returning from the slaughter of the kings and blessed him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This verse contains a number of quotations from Gen 14:17–19">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 18:21; Psalm 85:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:17"> to whom also Abraham apportioned a tenth of everything<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 14:20">b</a></sup>—in the first place, his name is translated “king of righteousness,” and then also “king of Salem,” that is, “king of peace”;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:23–28; Exodus 6:20–27"> without father, without mother, without genealogy, having neither beginning of days nor end of life, but resembling the Son of God—he remains a priest for all time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; Acts 2:29; Acts 7:8–9"> But see how great this man was, to whom Abraham<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “even Abraham”">c</a></sup>the patriarch gave a tenth from the spoils!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30–33; 2 Chronicles 31:4–6; Numbers 18:21–32"> And indeed those of the sons of Levi who receive the priesthood have a commandment to collect a tenth from the people according to the law, that is, from their brothers, although ⸤they are descended from Abraham⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “coming forth from the loins of Abraham”">d</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Hebrews 7:3–4; Genesis 13:14–17"> But the one who did not trace his descent from them collected tithes from Abraham and blessed the one who had the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:27; Genesis 48:15–20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> Now without any dispute the inferior is blessed by the more prominent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20; Hebrews 5:6; Revelation 1:18"> And in this case mortal men receive tithes, but in that case it is testified that he lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Genesis 14:20; Hebrews 7:4"> And, ⸤so to speak⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “as if to say a word”">e</a></sup>, even Levi, the one who receives tithes, has paid tithes through Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Hebrews 7:5; Genesis 46:26"> For he was still in the loins of his father when Melchizedek met him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Superiority of Jesus to Melchizedek

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 8:7; Galatians 2:21; Galatians 4:9"> Thus if perfection was through the Levitical priesthood, for on the basis of it the people received the law, what further need is there for another priest to arise according to the order of Melchizedek and not said to be according to the order of Aaron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 66:21; Ezekiel 16:61"> For when the priesthood changes, of necessity there is a change of the law also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 17:5"> For the one about whom these things are spoken belongs to another tribe from which no one has officiated at the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Isaiah 11:1; Luke 3:33"> For it is evident that our Lord is a descendant of Judah, a tribe with reference to which Moses said nothing concerning priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 110:4; Hebrews 7:17–21; Hebrews 7:3"> And it is still more clear, if another priest according to the likeness of Melchizedek arises,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Colossians 2:20; Hebrews 7:28"> who has become a priest not according to a law of physical requirement, but according to the power of an indestructible life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 110:4; Hebrews 5:6; Hebrews 7:21"> For it is testified,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are a priest ⸤forever⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">according to the order of Melchizedek.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 110:4 (see also Heb 5:6 ; 6:20 )">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:3; Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:9"> For on the one hand a preceding commandment is set aside because of its weakness and uselessness</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 9:9"> (for the law made nothing perfect), but on the other hand there is the introduction of a better hope through which we draw near to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:6; Psalm 110:4"> And by as much as this was not without an oath (for these on the one hand ⸤have become priests⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “are having become priests”">h</a></sup>without an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 6:16–18"> but he with an oath by the one who said to him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Lord has sworn and will not change his mind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘You are a priest ⸤forever⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">’ ”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 110:4 (see also Heb 5:6 ; 6:20 ; 7:17 )">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">),</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:25; Genesis 44:32; Hebrews 8:6–12"> by so much more<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “so much more also”">k</a></sup>Jesus has become the guarantee of a better covenant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10–11; 1 Chronicles 6:3–14; Hebrews 7:8"> And indeed many ⸤have become⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “are having become”">l</a></sup>priests, because they were prevented by death from continuing in office,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 12:34; Hebrews 7:28; Isaiah 9:6–7"> but he, because he continues ⸤forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">m</a></sup>, holds the priesthood permanently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 8:34; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Therefore also he is able to save completely those who draw near to God through him, because he always lives in order to intercede on their behalf.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:14–15; 1 Peter 2:22"> For a high priest such as this indeed is fitting for us, holy, innocent, undefiled, separated from sinners, and having become exalted above the heavens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:14"> who does not ⸤need every day⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “have necessity every day”">n</a></sup>like the former high priests to offer up sacrifices for his own sins and then for the sins of the people, because he did this once for all when he offered up himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 7:21; Hebrews 1:2"> For the law appoints men as high priests who have weakness, but the statement of the oath, after the law, appoints a Son, who is made perfect ⸤forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">o</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
</div>
