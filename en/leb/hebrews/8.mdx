## The Mediator of a New and Better Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Revelation 3:21"> Now this is the main point in what has been said: we have a high priest such as this, who sat down at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 33:7; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 9:23–24"> a minister of the sanctuary and of the true tabernacle which the Lord set up, not man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Hebrews 5:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For every high priest is appointed in order to offer both gifts and sacrifices; therefore it was necessary for this one also to have something that he offers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11–15; Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 17:12–13"> Now if he were on earth, he would not even be a priest, because there are those who offer the gifts according to the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Colossians 2:17; Hebrews 10:1"> who serve a sketch and shadow of the heavenly things, just as Moses was warned when he was about to complete the tabernacle, for he says, “See to it that you make everything according to the pattern which was shown to you on the mountain.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 25:40">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 7:22; Galatians 3:16–21; Hebrews 12:24"> But now he has attained a more excellent ministry, by as much as he is also mediator of a better covenant which has been enacted upon better promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:18"> For if that first covenant had been faultless, occasion would not have been sought for a second.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16–17; Hebrews 12:24"> For in finding fault with them he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, days are coming, says the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I will complete a new covenant with the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and with the house of Judah,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:32; Galatians 4:24; Mark 8:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">not like the covenant which I made with their fathers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the day I took hold of them by my hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to lead them out of the land of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they did not continue in my covenant</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I disregarded them, says the Lord.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–28; Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 11:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this is the covenant that I will decree with the house of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after those days, says the Lord:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am putting my laws in their minds</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will write them on their hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will be ⸤their⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to them for”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be ⸤my⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to me for”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they will not teach each one his fellow citizen</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and each one his brother, saying, ‘Know the Lord,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they will all know me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the least of them to the greatest.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Isaiah 43:25; Romans 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I will be merciful toward their wrongdoings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will not remember their sins any longer.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 7:11–12"> In calling it new, he has declared the former to be old. Now what is becoming obsolete and growing old is near to disappearing.</span>
