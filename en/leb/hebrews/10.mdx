## Christ’s One Sacrifice for Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Hebrews 9:11; Colossians 2:17"> For the law, possessing a shadow of the good things that are about to come, not the form of things itself, is never able ⸤year by year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “according to year”">a</a></sup>by means of the same sacrifices which they offer without interruption to make perfect those who draw near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:19; Psalm 103:12"> For otherwise, would they not have ceased to be offered, because the ones who worship, having been purified once and for all, would no longer have any consciousness of sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 23:27–28"> But in them there is a reminder of sins ⸤year by year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “according to year”">b</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Isaiah 1:11–15; Micah 6:6–8"> For it is impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 40:6–8; Isaiah 1:11; Isaiah 7:14"> Therefore, when he came into the world, he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sacrifice and offering you did not want,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a body you prepared for me;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:10; Matthew 3:17; Psalm 147:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">you did not delight in whole burnt offerings and offerings for sins.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 40:7–8; John 4:34; John 6:38"> <span className="pl pl-2">Then I said, ‘Behold, I have come—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the roll of the book it is written about me—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to do your will, O God.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5; Mark 12:33"> When he says above,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sacrifices and offerings and whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you did not want, nor did you delight in,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Various phrases from the quotation of Ps 40:6 in Heb 10:5–6 are repeated here">c</a></sup><br />which are offered according to the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 12:27–28; Hebrews 10:7"> then he has said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I have come to do your will.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A repetition of Ps 40:8 from Heb 10:7; many later manuscripts add “O God,” making the quotation conform to Heb 10:7 more closely">d</a></sup><br />He takes away the first in order to establish the second,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:14; Hebrews 10:12; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> by which will we are made holy through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 28:3; Hebrews 10:4"> And every priest stands every day serving and offering the same sacrifices many times, which are never able to take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Romans 8:34"> But this one, after he had offered one sacrifice for sins for all time, sat down at the right hand of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:13; Psalm 110:1; Luke 20:43"> from now on waiting until his enemies are made a footstool for his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For by one offering he has perfected for all time those who are made holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 3:6"> And the Holy Spirit also testifies to us, for after saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 8:8–12; Romans 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">“This is the covenant that I will decree for them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after those days, says the Lord:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am putting my laws on their hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will write them on their minds.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Jer 31:33">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Hebrews 8:12"> He also says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Their sins and their lawless deeds I will never remember again.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Jer 31:34">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:2; Hebrews 10:14"> Now where there is forgiveness of these, there is no longer an offering for sin.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hold Fast the Confession of Our Hope

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 4:16"> Therefore, brothers, since we have confidence for the entrance into the sanctuary by the blood of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:6; John 10:9; Hebrews 9:3"> by the new and living way which he inaugurated for us through the curtain, that is, his flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:14–16; Hebrews 8:1"> and since we have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="These words are an implied repetition from v. 19 for clarity">g</a></sup>a great priest over the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; John 3:5; Ephesians 5:26"> let us approach with a true heart in the full assurance of faith, our hearts sprinkled clean from an evil conscience and our bodies washed with pure water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> Let us hold fast to the confession of our hope without wavering, for the one who promised is faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 John 3:18; Hebrews 13:1"> And let us think about ⸤how to stir one another up to love⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “one another for the stirring up of love”">h</a></sup>and good works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 2:42; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Matthew 18:20"> not abandoning ⸤our meeting together⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the meeting of ourselves”">i</a></sup>, as is the habit of some, but encouraging each other, and by so much more as you see the day drawing near.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Serious Warning Against Continuing Deliberate Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 6:4–6; John 9:41"> For if we keep on sinning deliberately after receiving the knowledge of the truth, there no longer remains a sacrifice for sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Hebrews 2:3; Zephaniah 1:18"> but a certain fearful expectation of judgment and a fury of fire that is about to consume the adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:2–13"> Anyone who rejected the law of Moses dies without mercy on the testimony of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 6:6; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> How much worse punishment do you think the person will be considered worthy of who treats with disdain the Son of God and who considers ordinary the blood of the covenant by which he was made holy and who insults the Spirit of grace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35–36; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> For we know the one who said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Vengeance is mine, I will repay,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 32:35">j</a></sup><br />and again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Lord will judge his people.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 32:36">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Luke 12:5; Hebrews 12:29"> It is a terrifying thing to fall into the hands of the living God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4; Hebrews 12:4; 2 Timothy 2:3–13"> But remember the former days in which, after you were enlightened, you endured a great struggle with sufferings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:9; Philippians 4:14; Hebrews 11:36"> sometimes being publicly exposed both to insults and to afflictions, and sometimes becoming sharers with those who were treated in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:16; Matthew 5:11–12"> For you both sympathized with the prisoners and put up with the seizure of your belongings with joy because you knew that you yourselves had a better and permanent possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Galatians 6:8–10; Hebrews 3:6"> Therefore do not throw away your confidence, which has great reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1; Psalm 37:7; James 5:7–11"> For you have need of endurance, in order that after you have done the will of God, you may receive what was promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Habakkuk 2:3–4; Luke 18:8"> For yet<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“a very, very little while,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the one who is coming will come and will not delay.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Galatians 3:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But my righteous one will live by faith,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and if he shrinks back, my soul is not well pleased with him.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A quotation from Hab 2:3–4">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 John 5:5; 1 Peter 1:5; Mark 16:16"> But we are not among those who shrink back to destruction, but among those who have faith to the preservation of our souls.</span>
</div>
</div>
