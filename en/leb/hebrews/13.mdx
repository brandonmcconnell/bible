## Concluding Ethical Instructions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 Peter 3:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Brotherly love must continue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 25:35; Genesis 18:1–3; 1 Peter 4:9"> Do not neglect hospitality, because through this some have received angels as guests without knowing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:36; Hebrews 10:34; Romans 12:15"> Remember the prisoners, as though you were fellow-prisoners; remember the mistreated, as though you yourselves also are being mistreated in the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Proverbs 5:15–23; Galatians 5:19"> Marriage must be held in honor by all, and the marriage bed be undefiled, because God will judge sexually immoral people and adulterers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:6"> Your lifestyle must be free from the love of money, being content with what you have. For he himself has said, “I will never desert you, and I will never abandon you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 31:6 , 8">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 118:6–9; Isaiah 41:10; Romans 8:31"> So then, we can say with confidence,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Lord is my helper, I will not be afraid.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and I will not be afraid”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What will man do to me?”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 118:6">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 13:17; Philippians 3:17"> Remember your leaders, who spoke the word of God to you; ⸤considering the outcome of their way of life⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “of whom considering the outcome of the way of life”">d</a></sup>, imitate their faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 3:6; James 1:17; Revelation 1:8"> Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and ⸤forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to the ages”">e</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:3–5; Ephesians 5:6"> Do not be carried away by various and strange teachings, for it is good for the heart to be strengthened by grace, not by foods by which those who participate have not benefited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 1 Corinthians 10:20; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> We have an altar from which those who serve in the tabernacle do not have the right to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:27; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:11"> For the bodies of those animals whose blood is brought into the sanctuary by the high priest for sins are burned up outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12–14; John 19:17–18; Acts 7:58"> Therefore Jesus also suffered outside the gate, in order that he might sanctify the people by his own blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:26; 1 Peter 4:14–16; Acts 5:41"> So we must go out to him outside the camp, bearing his reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; Colossians 3:1–3; Hebrews 11:12–16"> For here we do not have a permanent city, but we seek the city that is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 107:21–22; Psalm 50:14; Romans 12:1"> Therefore through him let us offer up a sacrifice of praise ⸤continually⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “through all”">f</a></sup>to God, that is, the fruit of lips that confess his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:10; Romans 12:13"> And do not neglect doing good and generosity, for God is pleased with such sacrifices.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:17; Hebrews 13:7"> Obey your leaders and submit to them, for they keep watch over your souls as those who will give an account, so that they can do this with joy and not with groaning, for this would be unprofitable for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:1; Colossians 4:3"> Pray for us, for we are convinced that we have a good conscience, and want to conduct ourselves commendably in every way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philemon 1:22; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:31–32"> And I especially urge you to do this, so that I may be restored to you more quickly.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Benediction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:11; Hebrews 10:22; Psalm 23:1"> Now may the God of peace, who brought up from the dead our Lord Jesus, the great shepherd of the sheep, by the blood of the eternal covenant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:17; Philippians 4:13"> equip you with every good thing to do his will, carrying out in us what is pleasing before him through Jesus Christ, to whom be the glory ⸤forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to the ages”">g</a></sup>.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “forever and ever” (literally, “to the ages of the ages”)">h</a></sup>Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Conclusion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:1; 1 Peter 5:12; 2 Corinthians 6:1"> Now I urge you, brothers, bear with my word of exhortation, for indeed I have written to you ⸤briefly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “through few words”">i</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philemon 1:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Timothy 1:8"> Know that our brother Timothy has been released, with whom I will see you, if he comes quickly enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17; Acts 18:2"> Greet all your leaders and all the saints. Those from Italy greet you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; 2 Timothy 4:22; Titus 3:15"> Grace be with all of you.</span>
</div>
</div>
