## The Earthly Ministry of the Old Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Hebrews 9:10–11; Hebrews 8:2"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Now even”">a</a></sup>the first covenant had regulations for worship and the earthly sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 40:4; Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 26:35"> For a tent was prepared, the first one, in which were the lampstand and the table and the presentation of the loaves, which is called the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 26:31–33; Exodus 40:3; Matthew 27:51"> And after the second curtain was a tent called the holy of holies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 17:10; 2 Chronicles 5:10; Exodus 40:3"> containing the golden incense altar and the ark of the covenant covered on all sides with gold, in which were a golden jar containing the manna and the rod of Aaron that budded and the tablets of the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 16:2; Numbers 7:89; Exodus 25:17–22"> And above it were the cherubim of glory overshadowing the mercy seat, about which it is not now possible to speak in detail.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Exodus 27:21; Luke 1:8–11"> Now these things having been prepared in this way, the priests enter into the first tent ⸤continually⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “throughout all”">b</a></sup>as they accomplish their service,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 16:34; Leviticus 5:18; Hebrews 10:19–20"> but only the high priest enters into the second tent once a year, not without blood, which he offers on behalf of himself and the sins of the people committed in ignorance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 14:6; Hebrews 3:7; Hebrews 10:19–22"> The Holy Spirit was making this clear, that the way into the holy place was not yet revealed, while the first tent was still in existence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:21; Hebrews 5:1; Romans 5:14"> which was a symbol for the present time, in which both the gifts and sacrifices which were offered were not able to perfect the worshiper with respect to the conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:15; Colossians 2:16; Leviticus 11:2–47"> concerning instead only food and drink and different washings, regulations of outward things imposed until the time of setting things right.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Heavenly Ministry of the New Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Hebrews 8:1–2"> But Christ has arrived as a high priest of the good things to come. Through the greater and more perfect tent not made by hands, that is, not of this creation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Hebrews 9:15"> and not by the blood of goats and calves, but by his own blood, he entered once for all into the most holy place, obtaining eternal redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 19:2–21; Leviticus 16:14–16; Acts 15:9"> For if the blood of goats and bulls and the ashes of a young cow sprinkled on those who are defiled sanctify them for the ritual purity of the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 Peter 1:19; 1 John 1:7"> how much more will the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit offered himself without blemish to God, cleanse our consciences from dead works to serve the living God?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 3:24–26; Romans 8:30; Romans 8:28"> And because of this, he is the mediator of a new covenant, in order that, because a death has taken place for the redemption of transgressions committed during the first covenant, those who are the called may receive the promise of the eternal inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:22; Romans 5:12"> For where there is a will, it is a necessity for the death of the one who made the will to be established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 48:21; John 14:27; Galatians 3:15"> For a will is in force concerning those who are dead, since it is never in force when the one who made the will is alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 24:3–8; Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 9:22"> Therefore not even the first covenant was ratified without blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 24:5–11; Numbers 19:6; Hebrews 10:4"> For when every commandment had been spoken by Moses to all the people according to the law, he took the blood of calves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “calves and goats”">c</a></sup>with water and scarlet wool and hyssop and sprinkled both the scroll itself and all the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 24:8; Matthew 26:28; Hebrews 13:20"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“This is the blood of the covenant that God has commanded for you.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 24:8">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 8:19; Exodus 29:36"> And likewise he sprinkled both the tabernacle and all the utensils of service with the blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 6:7; Leviticus 4:35"> Indeed, nearly everything is purified with blood according to the law, and apart from the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–21; Hebrews 10:4; Hebrews 9:14"> Therefore it was necessary for the sketches of the things in heaven to be purified with these sacrifices, but the heavenly things themselves to be purified with better sacrifices than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:11–12; Hebrews 9:23"> For Christ did not enter into a sanctuary made by hands, a mere copy of the true one, but into heaven itself, now to appear in the presence of God on our behalf,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 9:14"> and not in order that he can offer himself many times, as the high priest enters into the sanctuary ⸤year by year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “according to year”">e</a></sup>with blood not his own,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; 1 Peter 1:20; Hebrews 1:2"> since it would have been necessary for him to suffer many times from the foundation of the world, but now he has appeared once at the end of the ages for the removal of sin by the sacrifice of himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:7; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> And ⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “in as much as”">f</a></sup>it is destined for people to die once, and after this, judgment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Titus 2:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Romans 6:10"> thus also Christ, having been offered once in order to bear the sins of many, will appear for the second time without reference to sin to those who eagerly await him for salvation.</span>
</div>
</div>
