## God’s Full and Final Revelation in the Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> Although God spoke long ago in many parts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “portions”">a</a></sup>and in many ways to the fathers by the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Colossians 1:16–18"> in these last days he has spoken to us by a Son, whom he appointed heir of all things, through whom also he made the world,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “the universe”; literally “the ages”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> who is the radiance of his glory and the representation of his essence, sustaining all things by the word of power.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “by the word of his power. When he had made purification for sins, he sat down”">c</a></sup>When he had made purification for sins through him, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> having become by so much better than the angels, by as much as he has inherited a more excellent name than theirs.</span>
</div>
</div>
