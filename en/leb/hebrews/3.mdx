## The Superiority of Jesus to Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 4:14–15"> Therefore, holy brothers, sharers in a heavenly calling, consider Jesus, the apostle and high priest of our confession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; 1 Timothy 3:15; Ephesians 2:22"> who was faithful to the one who appointed him, as Moses also was in his household.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “in all his household”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; 1 Peter 2:5–7; Zechariah 6:12–13"> For this one is considered worthy of greater glory than Moses, inasmuch as the one who builds it has greater honor than the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 1:2; Esther 2:10"> For every house is built by someone, but the one who built all things is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; Hebrews 3:2; Exodus 14:31"> And Moses was faithful in all his house as a servant, for a testimony to the things that would be spoken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:35"> but Christ was faithful as a son over his house, whose house we are, if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “if indeed”">b</a></sup>we hold fast to our confidence and the hope we can be proud of.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Serious Warning Against Unbelief

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> Therefore, just as the Holy Spirit says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today, if you hear his voice,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of testing in the wilderness,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">where your fathers tested me by trial</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and saw my works</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalm 67:2"> <span className="pl pl-2">for forty years.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I was angry with this generation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I said, ‘They always go astray in their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they do not know my ways.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> <span className="pl pl-2">As I swore in my anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘⸤They will never enter⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “if they will enter”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">into my rest.’ ”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 95:7b-11">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> Watch out, brothers, lest there be in some of you an evil, unbelieving heart, with the result that you fall away from the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> But encourage one another ⸤day by day⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “by each day”">e</a></sup>, as long as it is called “today,” so that ⸤none of you become hardened⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “not anyone of you be hardened”">f</a></sup>by the deception of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> For we have become partners of Christ, if indeed we hold fast the beginning of our commitment steadfast until the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalm 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> ⸤while it is said⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the saying”">g</a></sup>,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today, if you hear his voice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 95:7b-8">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> For who, when they heard it, were disobedient? Surely it was not all who went out from Egypt through Moses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> And with whom was he angry for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose dead bodies fell in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> And to whom did he swear they would not enter into his rest, except those who were disobedient?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:5; John 3:36; Mark 16:16"> And so we see that they were not able to enter because of unbelief.</span>
</div>
</div>
