## A High Priest Like Melchizedek

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:3; Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 7:27"> For every high priest taken from among men is appointed on behalf of people in the things relating to God, in order that he can offer both gifts and sacrifices on behalf of sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> being able to deal gently with those who are ignorant and led astray, since he himself also is surrounded by weakness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 16:6"> and because of it he is obligated to offer sacrifices for sins ⸤for himself also, as well as for the people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “as for the people so also for himself”">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 16:5"> And someone does not take for himself the honor, but is called by God, just as Aaron also was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 2:7; John 8:54; Acts 13:33"> Thus also Christ did not glorify himself to become high priest, but the one who said to him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are my Son, today I have begotten you,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 2:7">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:21"> just as also in another place he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are a priest ⸤forever⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">according to the order of Melchizedek,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 110:4">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:32–39; Luke 22:41–44; John 17:1"> who in the days of his flesh offered up both prayers and supplications, with loud crying and tears, to the one who was able to save him from death, and he was heard as a result of his reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Hebrews 10:5–9; John 4:34"> Although he was a son, he learned obedience from what he suffered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:15"> and being perfected, he became the source of eternal salvation to all those who obey him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5–6; Hebrews 6:20"> being designated by God a high priest according to the order of Melchizedek.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Advanced Teaching Hindered by Immaturity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; 2 Peter 3:16"> Concerning this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “which”">e</a></sup>⸤we have much to say and it is difficult to explain⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “great for us the message and hard to explain to say”">f</a></sup>, since you have become sluggish in hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 6:1; 1 Corinthians 3:1–3; 1 Peter 2:2"> For indeed, although you ought to be teachers ⸤by this time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “because of the time”">g</a></sup>, you have need of someone to teach you again the beginning elements of the oracles of God, and ⸤you have need of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “you are having need of”">h</a></sup>milk, not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and not”">i</a></sup>solid food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Peter 2:2; 2 Timothy 3:16"> For everyone who partakes of milk is unacquainted with the message of righteousness, because he is an infant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ephesians 1:18"> But solid food is for the mature, who because of practice have trained their faculties for the distinguishing of both good and evil.</span>
</div>
