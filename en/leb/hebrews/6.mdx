## A Serious Warning Against Falling Away

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 9:14"> Therefore, leaving behind the elementary message about Christ, let us move on to maturity, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and faith in God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:13–57; Acts 17:31–32; Romans 6:3–5"> teaching about baptisms and laying on of hands, and resurrection of the dead and eternal judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 16:7; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> And this we will do, if God permits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–29; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Matthew 5:13"> For it is impossible concerning those who have once been enlightened, and have tasted the heavenly gift, and become sharers of the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:3; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> and have tasted the good word of God and the powers of the coming age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:21; Hebrews 10:29; Hebrews 6:4"> and having fallen away, to renew them again to repentance, because they have crucified again for themselves the Son of God and held him up to contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 10:12; Isaiah 44:3; Psalm 65:9–13"> For ground that drinks the rain that comes often upon it, and brings forth vegetation usable to those people ⸤for whose sake⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “for the sake of whom”">a</a></sup>it is also cultivated, shares a blessing from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:17–18; John 15:6; Luke 13:7–9"> But if it produces thorns and thistles, it is worthless and near to a curse, whose end is for burning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:39; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Hebrews 6:4–6"> But even if we are speaking in this way, dear friends, we are convinced of better things concerning you, and belonging to salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; Hebrews 13:16; Galatians 6:10"> For God is not unjust, so as to forget your work and the love which you demonstrated for his name by having served the saints, and continuing to serve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–8; Hebrews 3:6; Romans 5:2–5"> And we desire each one of you to demonstrate the same diligence for the full assurance of your hope until the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 10:36; 1 John 2:25"> in order that you may not be sluggish, but imitators of those who inherit the promises through faith and patience.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Reliability of God’s Promise

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 22:15–18; Luke 1:73; Jeremiah 22:5"> For when God made a promise to Abraham, since he had no one greater to swear by, he swore by himself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Deuteronomy 1:10; Nehemiah 9:23"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Surely ⸤I will greatly bless⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “blessing I will bless”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you, and ⸤I will greatly multiply⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “multiplying I will multiply”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 12:2–3; Romans 4:17–25; Exodus 1:7"> And so, by persevering, he obtained the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 31:53"> For people swear by what is greater than themselves, and the oath for confirmation is the end of all dispute for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Proverbs 19:21; Isaiah 55:11"> In the same way God, because he wanted to show even more to the heirs of the promise the unchangeableness of his resolve, guaranteed it with an oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2; 1 Samuel 15:29"> in order that through two unchangeable things, in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have taken refuge may have powerful encouragement to hold fast to the hope set before us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 43:5; Romans 5:5–10; Hebrews 9:7"> which we have like an anchor of the soul, both firm and steadfast, and entering into the inside of the curtain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14; Hebrews 3:1; Hebrews 5:6"> where Jesus, the forerunner for us, entered, because he became a high priest ⸤forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to the age”">d</a></sup>according to the order of Melchizedek.</span>
</div>
</div>
