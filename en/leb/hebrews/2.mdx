## Warning Not to Neglect Salvation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 8:15; 2 Peter 1:12–13; Proverbs 7:1–2"> Because of this, it is all the more necessary that we pay attention to the things we have heard, lest we drift away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Galatians 3:19; Hebrews 10:28"> For if the word spoken through angels was binding and every transgression and act of disobedience received a just penalty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 12:25; Hebrews 5:9"> how will we escape if we neglect so great a salvation which had its beginning when it was spoken through the Lord and was confirmed to us by those who heard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:3; Ephesians 1:5; Mark 16:20"> while God was testifying at the same time by signs and wonders and various miracles and distributions of the Holy Spirit according to his will.</span>
</div>

## The Son’s Humiliation and Suffering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 2 Peter 3:13; Hebrews 6:5"> For he did not subject to angels the world to come, about which we are speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 8:4–8; Psalm 144:3; Job 7:17–18"> But someone testified somewhere, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What is man, that you remember him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or the son of man, that you care for him?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 8:5; Philippians 2:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made him for a short time lower than the angels;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you crowned him with glory and honor;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Several important manuscripts add “and placed him over the works of your hands” to the end of v. 7">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 13:3; 1 Corinthians 15:27; Psalm 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-2">you subjected all things under his feet.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 8:4–6">b</a></sup><br />For in subjecting all things,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “subjecting all things to him”">c</a></sup>he left nothing that was not subject to him. But now we do not yet see all things subjected to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 2:7–9; Revelation 5:9; 1 John 4:9–10"> but we see Jesus, for a short time made lower than the angels, because of the suffering of death crowned with glory and honor, so that apart from God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “so that by the grace of God”">d</a></sup>he might taste death on behalf of everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Acts 5:31; Luke 24:46"> For it was fitting for him for whom are all things and through whom are all things in bringing many sons to glory to perfect the originator of their salvation through sufferings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:10; Hebrews 13:12; Hebrews 10:14"> For both the one who sanctifies and the ones who are sanctified are all from one, for which reason he is not ashamed to call them brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 22:22; Psalm 40:10; John 18:20"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will proclaim your name to my brothers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the midst of the assembly I will sing in praise of you.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 22:22">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17–18; Isaiah 12:2; Psalm 91:2"> And again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will trust in him.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 8:17">f</a></sup><br />And again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I and the children God has given me.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 8:18">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; Romans 8:3; Colossians 2:15"> Therefore, since the children share in blood and flesh, he also in like manner shared in these same things, in order that through death he could destroy the one who has the power of death, that is, the devil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:15; 2 Timothy 1:7; Luke 1:74–75"> and could set free these who through fear of death were subject to slavery throughout all their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:16; Romans 4:16–25"> For surely he is not concerned with angels, but he is concerned with the descendants of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:14–2; Philippians 2:7–8; Hebrews 2:14"> Therefore he was obligated to be made like his brothers in all respects, in order that he could become a merciful and faithful high priest in the things relating to God, in order to make atonement for the sins of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Hebrews 4:15–16; Jude 1:24"> For in that which he himself suffered when he was tempted, he is able to help those who are tempted.</span>
</div>
</div>
