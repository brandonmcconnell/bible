## The Rest that Remains for the People of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Hebrews 4:9; Proverbs 28:14"> Therefore let us fear, while there remains a promise of entering into his rest, that none of you appear to fall short of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Romans 10:16–17; Hebrews 11:6"> ⸤For we also have had the good news proclaimed to us⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “for we are also having had the good news proclaimed”">a</a></sup>, just as those also did, but the message ⸤they heard⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “of hearing”">b</a></sup>did not benefit them, because they were not united with those who heard it in faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 95:11; Matthew 11:28–29; 1 Peter 1:20"> For we who have believed enter into rest,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “that rest”">c</a></sup>just as he has said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“As I swore in my anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘⸤They will never enter⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “if they will enter”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">into my rest.’ ”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 95:11">e</a></sup><br />And yet these works have been accomplished from the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Exodus 31:17; Genesis 2:1–2"> For he has spoken somewhere about the seventh day in this way: “And God rested on the seventh day from all his works,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 2:2">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 95:11; Hebrews 4:3; Hebrews 3:11"> and in this passage again, ‘⸤They will never enter⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “if they will enter”">g</a></sup>into my rest.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 95:11">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:43; Hebrews 3:18–19; Acts 13:46–47"> Since therefore it remains for some to enter into it, and the ones to whom the good news was proclaimed previously did not enter because of disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 95:7; Hebrews 3:7–8; Hebrews 3:15"> again he ordains a certain day, today, speaking by David after so long a time, just as had been said before,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today, if you hear his voice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">do not harden your hearts.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 95:7b-8 (see also Heb 3:7–8 , 15 )">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:15; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 12:9"> For if Joshua had caused them to rest, he would not have spoken about another day after these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:10; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:14–17"> Consequently a sabbath rest remains for the people of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Hebrews 4:3–4; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For the one who has entered into his rest has also himself rested from his works, just as God did from his own works.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–30; Luke 13:24; Hebrews 3:18–1"> Therefore, let us make every effort to enter into that rest, in order that no one may fall in the same pattern of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:29; Isaiah 55:11; Ephesians 6:17"> For the word of God is living and active and sharper than any double-edged sword, and piercing as far as the division of soul and spirit, both joints and marrow, and able to judge the reflections and thoughts of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:5; Psalm 33:13–15; Proverbs 15:3"> And no creature is hidden in the sight of him, but all things are naked and laid bare to the eyes of him to whom ⸤we must give our account⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “our account”">j</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Our Great High Priest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; Hebrews 12:2; Hebrews 9:12"> Therefore, because we have a great high priest who has gone through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast to our confession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Isaiah 53:4–5"> For we do not have a high priest who is not able to sympathize with our weaknesses, but who has been tempted in all things in the same way, without sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:19–23"> Therefore let us approach with confidence to the throne of grace, in order that we may receive mercy and find grace to help in time of need.</span>
</div>
