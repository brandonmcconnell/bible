## The Example of Jesus’ Suffering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Philippians 2:16"> Therefore, since we also have such a great cloud of witnesses surrounding us, putting aside every weight and ⸤the sin that so easily ensnares us⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the easily ensnaring sin”">a</a></sup>, let us run with patient endurance the race that has been set before us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:7; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:23–24"> fixing our eyes on Jesus, the originator and perfecter of faith, who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross, disregarding the shame, and has sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Revelation 2:3"> For consider the one who endured such hostility by sinners against himself, so that you will not grow weary in your souls and give up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Revelation 2:13; Hebrews 10:32–34"> You have not yet resisted to the point of shedding your blood as you struggle against sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:11–12; Job 5:17–18; Revelation 3:19"> And have you completely forgotten the exhortation which instructs you as sons?<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“My son, do not make light of the Lord’s discipline,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or give up when you are corrected by him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12; Psalm 119:75"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord disciplines the one whom he loves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and punishes every son whom he accepts.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Prov 3:11–12">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Deuteronomy 8:5; Proverbs 13:24"> Endure it for discipline. God is dealing with you as sons. For what son is there whom a father does not discipline?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6; 1 Peter 5:9–10; Psalm 73:1"> But if you are without discipline, in which all legitimate sons have become participants, then you are illegitimate and not sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Numbers 27:16; Isaiah 42:5"> Furthermore, we have had ⸤our earthly fathers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the flesh of our fathers”">c</a></sup>who disciplined us, and we respected them. Will we not much rather subject ourselves to the Father of spirits and live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:4; Leviticus 11:44–45; Titus 2:14"> For they disciplined us for a few days according to what seemed appropriate to them, but he does so for our benefit, in order that we can have a share in his holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:3–5; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Hebrews 12:5–6"> Now all discipline seems for the moment not to be joyful but painful, but later it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness for those who are trained by it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Serious Warning Against Refusing God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; Hebrews 12:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:14"> Therefore strengthen your slackened hands and your weakened knees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 4:26–27; Isaiah 35:3; Galatians 6:1"> and make straight paths for your feet, so that what is lame will not be dislocated, but rather be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Peter 1:15–16; 1 Thessalonians 4:7"> Pursue peace with everyone, and holiness, without which no one will see the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:12; Deuteronomy 29:18; Galatians 5:4"> Take care that no one falls short of the grace of God; that no one growing up like a root of bitterness causes trouble, and by it many become defiled;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 25:31–34; 1 Corinthians 6:15–20; Revelation 22:15"> that no one be a sexually immoral or totally worldly person like Esau, who for one meal traded his own birthright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; Matthew 25:11–12; Matthew 7:23"> For you know that also afterwards, when he wanted to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, because he did not find an occasion for repentance, although he sought it with tears.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:11; Exodus 24:17; Exodus 20:18"> For you have not come to something that can be touched, and to a burning fire, and to darkness, and to gloom, and to a whirlwind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Deuteronomy 4:12"> and to the noise of a trumpet, and to the sound of words which those who heard begged that not another word be spoken to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 19:12–13; Exodus 19:16; Galatians 3:10"> For they could not endure what was commanded: “If even an animal touches the mountain, it must be stoned.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 19:12–13">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 19:16; Deuteronomy 9:19; Isaiah 6:3–5"> And the spectacle was so terrifying that Moses said, “I am terrified and trembling.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 9:19">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 4:26; Philippians 3:20; Revelation 14:1"> But you have come to Mount Zion, and to the city of the living God, to the heavenly Jerusalem, and to tens of thousands of angels, to the festal gathering</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="James 1:18; Hebrews 11:40; Luke 10:20"> and assembly of the firstborn who are enrolled in heaven, and to God the judge of all, and to the spirits of righteous people made perfect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Hebrews 11:28; Hebrews 11:4"> and to Jesus, the mediator of a new covenant, and ⸤to the sprinkled blood⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to the blood of sprinkling”">f</a></sup>that speaks better than Abel’s does.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Deuteronomy 30:17; Hebrews 2:1–3"> Watch out that you do not refuse the one who is speaking! For if those did not escape when they refused the one who warned them on earth, much less will we escape, if we reject the one who warns from heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Isaiah 13:13; Exodus 19:18"> whose voice shook the earth at that time, but now he has promised, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yet once more I will shake not only the earth but also heaven.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Hag 2:6">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:31; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:10–11"> Now the phrase “yet once more” indicates the removal of what is shaken, namely, things that have been created, in order that the things that are not shaken may remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 13:15; Daniel 2:44"> Therefore, since we are receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us be thankful, through which let us serve God acceptably, with awe and reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Deuteronomy 9:3; Hebrews 10:27"> For indeed our God is a consuming fire.</span>
</div>
</div>
