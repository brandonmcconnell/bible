## An Oracle Against Edom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8–9; Jeremiah 31:3; Deuteronomy 10:15"> “I have loved you,” says Yahweh, but you say, “How have you loved us?” “Is Esau not Jacob’s brother?” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">d</a></sup>Yahweh. “I have loved Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Obadiah 1:10; Ezekiel 35:3–4; Jeremiah 49:16–18"> but Esau I have hated. I have made his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, “Esau’s”">e</a></sup>mountain ranges a desolation, and given his inheritance to the jackals of the desert.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 35:9; Ezekiel 11:10"> If Edom says, “We are shattered, but we will return and rebuild the ruins,” Yahweh of hosts says this: “They may build, but I will tear down; and they will be called a territory of wickedness, and the people with whom Yahweh is angry forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:7; Psalm 58:10–11; Ezekiel 38:16"> Your eyes will see this, and you will say, “Yahweh is great beyond the borders<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “boundary”">f</a></sup>of Israel.”</span>
</div>

## An Oracle Against the Temple Priesthood

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:46; Isaiah 1:2; John 13:13–17"> “A son honors his father, and a slave his master;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “masters”">g</a></sup>but if I am a father, where is my honor, and if I am a master,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “masters”">h</a></sup>where is my reverence?” says Yahweh of hosts to you, O priests, ⸤who despise⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “despisers of”">i</a></sup>my name. “But you say, ‘How have we despised your name?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:27–32; Malachi 1:8"> You are presenting defiled food on my altar! But you ask, ‘How have we defiled you?’ By saying that the table of Yahweh is despised!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Leviticus 22:19–25; Hosea 8:13"> When you offer a blind animal for sacrifice, is that not wrong?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “is there no wrong?”">j</a></sup>And when you offer the lame and the one who is ill, is that not wrong?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “is there no wrong?”">k</a></sup>Present it, please, to your governor! Will he be pleased with you? ⸤Will he show you favor⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “Will he lift your face?”">l</a></sup>says Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 32:11; Jeremiah 27:18; 1 Peter 1:17"> So then, ⸤implore the favor of God⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “appease, please, the face of God”">m</a></sup>so that he will be gracious to us. “⸤This is what you have done⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “From your hand was this”">n</a></sup>⸤Will he show favor to any of you⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “will he lift up from you a face”">o</a></sup>says Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Amos 5:21–24; Jeremiah 6:20"> “Who also among you will shut the temple doors so that you will not kindle fire in vain on my altar? I take no pleasure in you,” says Yahweh of hosts, “and ⸤I will not accept⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “I will not take pleasure in”">p</a></sup>an offering from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 54:5; Isaiah 66:19–20"> From the rising of the sun to its setting, my name is great among the nations, and in every place incense is being presented to my name, and a pure offering. For my name is great among the nations,” says Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 1:6–8; Malachi 2:8; Amos 2:7"> “But you are profaning it by saying the table of the Lord is defiled, and its fruit—its food<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="The Hebrew here is difficult; “its food” may function to explain “its fruit”">q</a></sup>—is despised!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:3; Leviticus 22:19–23; Mark 14:37–38"> And you say, ‘Look! This is a weariness,’ and you sniff with disdain at it,” says Yahweh of hosts. “And you bring the stolen, the lame, and the one that is sick—this you bring as the offering! Should I ⸤accept it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “take pleasure in it”">r</a></sup>from your hand?” says Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 5:1–10; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> “Cursed is the one who cheats, who has in his flock a male and vows it, but instead sacrifices a blemished one to the Lord! For I am a great king,” says Yahweh of hosts, “and my name is awesome among the nations.”</span>
</div>
