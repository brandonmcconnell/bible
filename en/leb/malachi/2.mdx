## Yahweh Hates Faithlessness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 100:3; Malachi 2:11; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> ⸤Do we not all have⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “Is for all of us not”">o</a></sup>one father? Has not one God created us? Why then are we unfaithful ⸤to one another⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “each man to his brother”">p</a></sup>profaning the covenant of our ancestors?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “fathers”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–18; Ezra 9:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> Judah has been faithless, and a detestable thing has been done in Israel and in Jerusalem. Judah has profaned the sanctuary of Yahweh which he loves, and has married the daughter of a foreign<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “pagan”">r</a></sup>god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:29; Leviticus 20:3; Ezekiel 24:21"> May Yahweh cut off from the tents of Jacob the man who does this, the one who is awake and who answers, and the one who brings an offering to Yahweh of hosts!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–15; Jeremiah 14:12; Psalm 78:34–37"> And this you do ⸤as well⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “a second time”">s</a></sup>you cover the altar of Yahweh with tears, with weeping and with groaning, because he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Yahweh">t</a></sup>⸤no longer regards the offering⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “there is no longer turning to the offering”">u</a></sup>or accepts it with favor from your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Isaiah 54:6"> But you ask, “⸤For what reason⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “Because of what?”">v</a></sup>Because Yahweh stands as a witness between you and the wife of your youth, against whom you have been unfaithful, even though she is your marriage partner and ⸤your wife by covenant⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “the wife of your covenant”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 19:4–6; Malachi 2:14; Mark 10:6–8"> ⸤Did not one God make them?⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “Not one did he make”">x</a></sup>But a remnant of the spirit is his.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="The meaning of this sentence is difficult; literally “And a remnant of the spirit to him”">y</a></sup>And what does the one God desire? An offspring of God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “a godly offspring”">z</a></sup>⸤You must be attentive to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “You must keep watch on”">aa</a></sup>your spirit, and you must not be unfaithful to the wife of your youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Matthew 5:31–32; Mark 10:2–12"> “For I hate divorce,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “sending away”">ab</a></sup>says Yahweh, the God of Israel, “and he who covers his clothing with violence,” says Yahweh of hosts. “⸤You must be attentive to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “You must keep watch on”">ac</a></sup>your spirit and you must not be unfaithful.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> You have wearied Yahweh with your words, but you ask, “With what have we wearied him?” You have done so ⸤when you say⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “at your saying”">ad</a></sup>“Everyone who does evil is good in the ⸤sight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “eyes”">ae</a></sup>of Yahweh, and in them he delights,” or “Where is the God of justice?”</span>
</div>
