## Yahweh’s Messenger is Coming

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2–3"> “Look! I am going to send my messenger,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “my angel”">a</a></sup>and he will prepare<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “clear”">b</a></sup>the way ⸤before me⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “before my face”">c</a></sup>And the Lord whom you are seeking will come suddenly to his temple, and the messenger of the covenant, in whom you are taking pleasure—look!—he is about to come,” says Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Isaiah 4:4; 1 Corinthians 3:13–15"> And who can endure the day of his coming? And who is the one who can stand ⸤when he appears⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “at his appearing”">d</a></sup>For he is like a refiner’s fire, like launderers’ alkali.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “lye,” or “soap”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:25; Proverbs 17:3; Zechariah 13:9"> He will sit as a refiner and purifier of silver; he will purify the children of Levi, and he will refine them like gold and like silver, and they will present to Yahweh offerings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “offering”">f</a></sup>in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Isaiah 56:7; Zechariah 8:3"> And the offering of Judah and Jerusalem will be pleasing to Yahweh, like in the days of old and like in former years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 5:8–9; Hebrews 13:4; Deuteronomy 5:11"> “Then I will approach you for judgment, and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers and against the adulterers, and against those who swear ⸤falsely⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “as far as the falseness”">g</a></sup>and against the oppressors of the hired worker with his wages, the widow and the orphan, and ⸤the abusers of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “those pushing aside”">h</a></sup>the alien,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “sojourner”">i</a></sup>and yet do not fear me,” says Yahweh of hosts.</span>
</div>
</div>

## An Overflowing Blessing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Hebrews 13:8; James 1:17"> “For I, Yahweh, have not changed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “I do not change”">j</a></sup>and you, O children of Jacob, have not perished.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “are not destroyed”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:3; Romans 10:21; Romans 10:3"> From the days of your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>you have turned aside from my rules, and have not kept<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “observed”">m</a></sup>them! Return to me and I will return to you,” says Yahweh of hosts, “but you say, ‘How shall we return?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Matthew 22:21; Mark 12:17"> Will a human dare to rob God? Yet you are robbing me! And you say, ‘How have we robbed you?’ In the tithes and the contributions!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–19; Haggai 1:6–11; Haggai 2:14–17"> You are being cursed with a curse, for you are robbing me, the whole nation of you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; Matthew 6:33; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Bring the whole tithe to ⸤the storehouse⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “the house of the treasure”">n</a></sup>so that there will be food in my house, and test me please in this,” says Yahweh of hosts, “if I will not open for you the windows of heaven, and pour forth for you ⸤an overflowing blessing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally, “a blessing up to not enough”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:14; Joel 2:22; Zechariah 8:12"> I will rebuke the devourer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="NRSV, JPS interpret this word as “locust”">p</a></sup>for you; it will not destroy the fruit of your soil; your vine in the field will not be unfruitful,” says Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 61:9; Isaiah 62:4; Zechariah 8:23"> “And all the nations will call you blessed, because you will be ⸤a land in which one takes joy⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a land of desire”">q</a></sup>says Yahweh of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 2:17; Isaiah 37:23; Romans 9:20"> “Your words have been harsh<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “strong”">r</a></sup>against me,” says Yahweh. “But you say, ‘How have we spoken together against you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3; Zephaniah 1:12; Psalm 73:8–13"> You have said, ‘It is useless<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “vain”">s</a></sup>to serve God! What is the gain if we keep<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “observe”">t</a></sup>his requirements, and if we walk as mourners ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">u</a></sup>Yahweh of hosts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Jeremiah 12:1–2; Malachi 2:17"> And now we are calling the arrogant blessed! Not only do those who do wickedness prosper; they also test God and they escape!’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Book of Remembrance is Written

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; Psalm 56:8; Malachi 4:2"> Then those who revered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “fear”">v</a></sup>Yahweh spoke ⸤with one another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “each with his friend/companion”">w</a></sup>And Yahweh listened attentively<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “gave heed”">x</a></sup>and heard, and a scroll of remembrance was written ⸤before him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to the face of him”">y</a></sup>of those who revere<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “fear”">z</a></sup>Yahweh and ponder his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Deuteronomy 7:6; 2 Corinthians 6:18"> “They will be mine,” says Yahweh of hosts, “on the day that I am acting, my treasured possession. I will have compassion on them as a man has compassion on his son who serves him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 25:46; Psalm 58:10–11; Genesis 18:25"> You will return and see the difference between the righteous and the wicked, between the one who serves God and the one who does not serve him.</span>
</div>
</div>
