## Those Who Have Wealth but Do Not Enjoy It Are Pitiful

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13"> Here is another misfortune that I have seen under the sun, and it is prevalent among humankind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:19; Psalm 17:14; Psalm 73:7"> God gives a man wealth, possessions, and honor, so that he lacks nothing his heart desires; yet God does not enable him to enjoy it—instead someone else ends up enjoying it. This is vanity—indeed, it is a grievous ill!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 3:16; Jeremiah 22:19; Ecclesiastes 4:3"> Even if a man fathers a hundred children and lives many years so that the days of his years are many, if his heart<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “his soul”">a</a></sup>is not satisfied with ⸤his prosperity⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the good”">b</a></sup>and ⸤he does not receive a proper burial⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “and also there is no burial for him”">c</a></sup>I deem the stillborn better than him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 109:13"> For he comes into vanity and departs into darkness, and his name is shrouded in darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 58:8; Job 14:1; Job 3:10–13"> He has neither seen nor known the sun, yet he has more rest than him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Ecclesiastes 3:20; Job 30:23"> Even if a man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">d</a></sup>lives a thousand years twice, if he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">e</a></sup>does not enjoy ⸤prosperity⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “good”">f</a></sup>⸤both suffer the same fate⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “are not the all going to the same place?”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## One Must Learn to Be Content with What One Has

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 6:27; Proverbs 16:26; 1 Timothy 6:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">All of a man’s toil is for his mouth—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">yet his appetite is never satisfied.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:1; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Psalm 101:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">So do the wise really have an advantage over fools?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤Can the poor really gain anything by knowing how to act in front of others⸥?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “What is there for the poor knowing how to conduct themselves before the living?”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; Job 31:7; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤Better to be content with what your eyes see</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than for your soul to constantly crave more⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “Sight of the eyes is better than wandering of desire”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This also is vanity and chasing wind!</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## It is Futile for Humans to Complain about God’s Irresistible Will

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 9:32; Isaiah 45:9–10; Job 40:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whatever is—it was already determined,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤what will be—it has already been decided⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “and his name is known what he is man”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">As for man, he cannot argue</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">against what is more powerful than him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:16; Ecclesiastes 5:7; Ecclesiastes 2:3–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤Increasing words only multiplies futility⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “Where there are numerous words, it makes numerous vanity”">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">how does that profit anyone?</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Future is Inscrutable to Humans

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 4:14; Job 14:2; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> For who knows what is good for a man in his life during the few days of his fleeting life, which are fleeting as a shadow? For who can tell anyone what will happen ⸤in the future⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “after him”">l</a></sup>under the sun?</span>
