## No One Knows the Future

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:14; Ecclesiastes 9:12; Proverbs 24:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Surely no one knows what will be,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so who can tell anyone what will happen?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 89:48; Job 14:5; Hebrews 9:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Just as no one can control the wind to restrain the wind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so also no one can control the day of his death.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Just as no one is discharged in time of war,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so wickedness will not deliver the wicked.</span></span>

## The World Marred by Oppression and Injustice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:8; Ecclesiastes 4:7–8; Ecclesiastes 5:13"> I saw all this as I applied my heart to all the deeds done under the sun: ⸤sometimes those in authority harm others⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sometimes one man domineers another man to his harm”">c</a></sup></span>

</div>

## Although Evil is Not Punished Swiftly, God Does Eventually Punish Sinners

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; Psalm 50:21–22; Romans 2:4–5"> Because sentence against an evil deed is not carried out quickly, the heart of ⸤humans⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the man”">d</a></sup>fills up within them to do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 37:11; 2 Peter 2:9; Luke 1:50"> Although the sinner does evil a hundred times and prolongs his life, yet I also know that it will be good for those who fear God—because they fear ⸤his presence⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from before his face”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:12; Isaiah 3:11; Job 14:2"> But it will not go well with the wicked, and they will not prolong their days, like the shadow; because there is no fearing ⸤God’s presence⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from before the face of God”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:15; Ecclesiastes 2:14; Malachi 3:15"> There is a vanity that happens on earth: sometimes the righteous suffer what the wicked deserve, and sometimes the wicked receive what the righteous deserve. I said, “This also is vanity!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Humans Should Enjoy the Life That God Gives to Them

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 5:18; Ecclesiastes 3:12–13"> So I recommend enjoyment. For there is nothing better for man under the sun than to eat and to drink and to rejoice. This will accompany him in his toil the days of his life that God gives to him under the sun.</span>
</div>
</div>

## No One Can Discover the Rhyme and Reason for Things

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13; Psalm 127:2; Ecclesiastes 2:23"> I applied my mind to know wisdom and to understand the business that is done on earth—how neither day nor night one’s eyes see sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:11; Psalm 73:16; Romans 11:33"> Then I saw all the work of God—man is not able to discover the work that is done under the sun. Although man may toil in seeking, he cannot find it. Even if a wise man claims that he knows it, he cannot find it.</span>
</div>
