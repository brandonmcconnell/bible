## Advice to the Young: Life is Short and Then You Die

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Lamentations 3:27; Ecclesiastes 11:8; Psalm 71:17–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember your Creator in the days of your youth—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">before the days of trouble come</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the years draw near when you will say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“I find no pleasure in them!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; 1 Samuel 4:18; 1 Samuel 4:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Before the sun, the light, the moon, and the stars darken</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the clouds return after the rain.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 90:9–10; Psalm 102:23; 2 Samuel 21:15–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the guards of the house tremble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the men of strength are bent;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the grinders cease because they are few,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those looking through the windows see dimly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:10; 2 Samuel 19:35; Revelation 18:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the doors on the street are shut,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when the sound of the grinding mill is low;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">one rises up to the sound of the bird,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the daughters of song are brought low.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:13; Jeremiah 9:17–20; Job 30:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are afraid of heights,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and terrors are on the road.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The almond tree blossoms,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the grasshopper draws itself along, and desire fails</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because man goes to his eternal home,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the mourners go about in the streets.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Before the silver cord is snapped</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the golden bowl is broken;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the jar at the foundation is broken,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the wheel at the cistern is broken.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Ecclesiastes 3:20–21; Psalm 146:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the dust returns to the earth as it was,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the breath returns to God who gave it.</span></span>

## Epilogue

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 4:32; Proverbs 1:1; 1 Kings 10:8"> The Teacher<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “Qohelet”">b</a></sup>was full of wisdom, and he taught the people with knowledge. He carefully considered many proverbs and carefully arranged them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:5; 1 Timothy 1:15; Proverbs 16:21–24"> The Teacher<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “Qohelet”">c</a></sup>sought to find delightful words,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “words of delight”">d</a></sup>and he wrote<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The MT reads the term passively, “what is written,” but an alternate textual tradition reads, “and he wrote”">e</a></sup>what is upright—truthful words.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 2:37; Hebrews 4:12; Ezekiel 34:23"> The words of the wise are like cattle goads; the collections of the sages are like pricks inflicted by one shepherd.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “The owner of collections are given by one shepherd”">f</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Romans 2:16; Matthew 12:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">For God will bring every deed into judgment,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">including every secret thing, whether good or evil.</span></span>
