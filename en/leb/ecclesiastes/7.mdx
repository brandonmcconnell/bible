<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; Proverbs 14:29; Psalm 126:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The end of a matter is better than its beginning;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤better to be slow to anger than hot-headed⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “one who is long of spirit is better than one who is high of spirit”">a</a></sup></span>
