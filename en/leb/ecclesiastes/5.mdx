<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:19; Hebrews 12:28–29; Proverbs 15:8"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Ecclesiastes 5:1–20 in the English Bible is 4:17–5:19 in the Hebrew Bible">a</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">Guard your steps when you go to the house of God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">draw near to listen rather than to offer a sacrifice of fools,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">for they do not know that they are doing evil.</span></span>

## There is Never Enough Money to Satisfy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 6:24; Luke 12:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoever loves money is not satisfied with money,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and whoever loves wealth is not satisfied with profit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This also is vanity!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Habakkuk 2:13; Psalm 119:36–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">When prosperity increases,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those who consume it increase.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">⸤So its owner gains nothing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">except to see his wealth before it is spent⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “And what gain has its owner but to see it with his eyes?”">d</a></sup></span>

## Hoarding Wealth Can Backfire

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:1–2; Luke 18:22–23; Genesis 19:14"> There is a grievous evil which I have seen under the sun: wealth ⸤hoarded⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “kept ”">e</a></sup>by its owner to his harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Haggai 2:16–17; Proverbs 23:5"> That wealth was lost in a bad venture. Although he has borne a child, ⸤he has nothing to leave to him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “he has nothing in his hand”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 49:17; Job 1:21; 1 Timothy 6:7"> Just as he came from his mother’s womb naked, ⸤he will depart⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “return to go”">g</a></sup>just as he came; he will take nothing with him for his toil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:29; Ecclesiastes 1:3; John 6:27"> This also is a grievous illness. Exactly as he came, so he will go. What profit does he gain for all his toil for the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 127:2; Ezekiel 4:16–17; Psalm 90:7–11"> Also, he eats in darkness all his days; he is frustrated in much sickness and resentment.</span>

## If You Have Wealth, Enjoy It as God Enables

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> Look! I have discovered what is good and fitting: to eat and to drink and ⸤to enjoy⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to see goodness”">h</a></sup>all the fruit of the toil with which one toils under the sun during the number of the days of his life that God gives to him—for this is his lot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “fate”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Ecclesiastes 3:13; Ecclesiastes 6:2"> This indeed is a gift of God: everyone to whom God gives wealth and possessions, he also empowers him ⸤to enjoy them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to eat from it”">j</a></sup>to accept his lot, and to rejoice in the fruit of his toil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:21–24; Isaiah 65:13–14; Psalm 37:16"> For he does not remember the brief days of his life, for God keeps his heart preoccupied with enjoyment of life.</span>
