## Enjoy Life to the Fullest within the Auspices of the Fear of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:11; Matthew 5:45; Proverbs 15:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">The light is sweet,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it is pleasant for the eyes to see the sun.</span></span>
