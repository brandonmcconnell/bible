## Qohelet Introduces His Quest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:1; 1 Kings 4:1–19"> I, the Teacher,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “Qohelet”">h</a></sup>was king over Israel in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:10; Genesis 3:19; 1 Timothy 4:15"> I applied my mind to seek and to search by wisdom all that is done under the heavens.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="MT reads “under the heavens,” which is supported by LXX; however, several versions (Syriac Peshitta, Aramaic Targum, Latin Vulgate) as well as the Cairo Geniza manuscript read, “under the sun,” cf. 1:3, 9, etc.">i</a></sup>It is a grievous task God has given to ⸤humans⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the man”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I saw all the works that are done under the sun. Look! Everything is vanity and chasing wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:12–13; Isaiah 40:4; Ecclesiastes 3:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">What is twisted cannot be straightened,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The MT reads the active “to be straight”; however, the BHS editors suggest the passive “to be straightened,” which is supported by LXX, which reflects a passive form">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and what is lacking cannot be counted.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="The MT reads “to be numbered”; however, the BHS editors suggest “to be supplied,” comparing 1:15b with similar wording in the Babylonian Talmud: “May the Almighty replenish your loss” (b. Berachot 16b)">l</a></sup></span>
</div>

I have become great and have increased in wisdom more than anyone who ⸤has preceded⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “before me”">n</a></sup>me over Jerusalem. ⸤I have acquired a great deal of wisdom and knowledge⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “And my mind has seen much wisdom and knowledge”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ecclesiastes 1:13–14"> So ⸤I dedicated myself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “So I gave my heart”">p</a></sup>to learn about wisdom and to learn about delusion and folly. However, I discovered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “I knew”">q</a></sup>that this also is chasing wind.</span>
