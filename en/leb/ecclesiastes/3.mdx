## God Has Ordained the Ebb and Flow of Human Activities

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:17; Matthew 16:3; Ecclesiastes 8:5–6"> For everything there is an appointed time, a time for every matter under heaven:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 9:27; Job 14:5; Matthew 15:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to bear</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “a time to be born”; MT reads the active form “to bear children ”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a time to die;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to plant and a time to root up what is planted;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 6:1–2; 1 Samuel 2:6; Jeremiah 45:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to kill and a time to heal;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to break down and a time to build up;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Psalm 30:5; James 4:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to weep and a time to laugh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to mourn and a time to dance;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:25; Joshua 10:27; Joshua 4:3–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to throw away stones and a time to gather stones;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to embrace and a time to refrain from embracing;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–8; Luke 9:24–25; Mark 8:35–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to seek and a time to lose;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to keep and a time to throw away;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Micah 7:5; Lamentations 3:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to tear and a time to sew;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time to be silent and a time to speak;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Ephesians 5:28–29; Luke 14:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">a time to love and a time to hate;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time for war and a time for peace.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:3; Ecclesiastes 5:16; Proverbs 14:23"> What does the worker gain in his toil?</span>
</div>
</div>

## No One Understands God’s Mysterious Plan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:13–14; Genesis 3:19; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I have seen the busyness God gives to ⸤humans⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the man”">b</a></sup>to preoccupy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “to be busy”">c</a></sup>them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Romans 1:19–20; Job 11:7"> He has made everything suitable in its time. He also has put ⸤the past⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “eternity”">d</a></sup>in their hearts, yet no one can grasp what God does from the beginning to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 37:3; Ecclesiastes 3:22; 1 Timothy 6:18"> So I realized that there is ⸤nothing better⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “no good”">e</a></sup>for them than to ⸤rejoice and enjoy themselves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to rejoice and to do good”">f</a></sup>during their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 128:2; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Ecclesiastes 2:24"> And for anyone to eat and drink, that is, ⸤to enjoy the fruit of all his toil⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to see good in all his toil”">g</a></sup>this also is a gift of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; Psalm 33:11; Romans 11:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know everything God does endures forever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nothing can be added to it, and nothing can be taken from it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">for God so acts that humans</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “they”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-3">might stand in awe before him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:9–10; Ecclesiastes 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">What is—it already was,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and what will be—it already is,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">for ⸤God will do what he has done.⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “God seeks what is pursued”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Mysterious Plan Allows Injustice to Exist in the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Psalm 94:21–22"> I saw something else under the sun: instead of justice there was evil; instead of righteousness there was wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 16:27"> So I said ⸤to myself⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “in my heart”">j</a></sup>“God will surely judge the righteous and the wicked, for he has appointed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The MT reads שָׁם “there,” but repointing to שָׂם, “he has appointed,” makes better sense">k</a></sup>a time of judgment for every deed and every work.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 73:22; Psalm 49:12; Hebrews 9:27"> I said to myself concerning ⸤humans⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the man”">l</a></sup>“God sifts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “tests”">m</a></sup>them in order to show<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The MT reads active “to see,” but causative “to show” is reflected by LXX, Syriac Peshitta, and Latin Vulgate">n</a></sup>them that they are like beasts.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 49:12; Psalm 49:20; Psalm 89:47–48"> For the fate of ⸤humans⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the man”">o</a></sup>and the fate of the beast is ⸤the same⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “is one”">p</a></sup>The death of the one is like the death of the other, for ⸤both are mortal⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “and one breath is for all”">q</a></sup>Man has no advantage over the beast, for both are fleeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Job 34:15; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Both go to one place—both came from dust and both return to dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; John 14:3; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> For no one knows whether the spirit of a human ascends to heaven and whether the spirit of the beast descends to the ground!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:11–12; Ecclesiastes 10:14"> So I concluded that there is nothing better for a person than to enjoy the fruit of his labor, for this is his lot in life. ⸤For no one knows what will happen in the future.⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “For who can bring him to see in what will be after him?”">r</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
