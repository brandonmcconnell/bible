<div className="bp poetry">

## People Need Balance in Their Approach to Labor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> I also realized that all of the toil and all of the skillful work that is done—it is envy between one man and ⸤another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “his friend”">b</a></sup>This also is vanity and chasing wind!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 6:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤The fool refuses to work with his hands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so he has nothing to eat except his own skin⸥!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “The fool folds his hands and eats his flesh”">c</a></sup></span>

## Wealth without Someone with Which to Enjoy It is Futile

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Psalm 78:33; Zechariah 1:6"> I turned again and saw another vanity under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Proverbs 27:20; Luke 12:20"> Sometimes a man is all alone with no companion; he also has neither son nor brother. Yet there is no end to all his toil, and his eye is not satisfied with wealth. He laments, “For whom am I toiling and depriving ⸤myself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “his soul”">d</a></sup>of pleasure?” This also is vanity—it is an unhappy business!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Friends and Family Can Help One Another in Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:17; Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 12:18–21"> Two are better than the one, for they enjoy a better reward for their toil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:18; Isaiah 35:3–4"> For if one falls, his companion may help him up. But pity the one who falls and there is ⸤no one⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “there is not a second”">e</a></sup>to help him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 1:1–4"> Also if two lie together, ⸤they can keep each other warm⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “and it will be warm for them”">f</a></sup>But how can one person be warm?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 3:16–17; Ephesians 4:3; 2 Samuel 23:9"> ⸤Although an assailant may overpower one person, two may withstand him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “And even though he will prevail against him, the one; the second, they will withstand opposite him”">g</a></sup>A threefold cord ⸤is not easily broken⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “will not be broken in haste”">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## One Must Be Willing to Listen to Counsel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:16; 2 Chronicles 16:9–10; Proverbs 19:1"> A poor but wise youth is better than an old but foolish king who no longer knows ⸤how to receive advice⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “how to be warned”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 113:7–8; Genesis 41:14; Job 5:11"> For he came out of the prison house to reign, ⸤since he was born poor in his kingdom⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “for all in his kingdom he was born poor”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:6"> I saw all the living who move about under the sun with the youth; the second who will stand in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; 1 Kings 1:40; Ecclesiastes 2:17"> There is no end to all the people, to all who were before him. Yet the later generation will not rejoice in him, for this also is vanity and chasing wind!</span>
