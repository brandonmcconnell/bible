## The Same Fate—Death—Awaits Everyone

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:3; John 10:27–30; 1 Peter 1:5"> So all this I laid to my heart, and I concluded<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “examined”">a</a></sup>that the righteous and the wise, as well as their deeds, are in the hand of God. So no one knows anything that will come to them, whether it will be love or hatred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Malachi 3:5"> The same fate comes to everyone:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the righteous and to the wicked,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the good and to the wicked,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Several versions (Greek, Syriac, Latin) insert “and to the bad”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the clean and to the unclean,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to those who sacrifice and to those who do not sacrifice.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">As with the good man, so also to the sinner;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as with those who swear an oath, so also those who fear oaths.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11; Ecclesiastes 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is the ⸤injustice⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “evil”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that is done under the sun:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the same fate comes to everyone.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Also the hearts of ⸤humans⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the man”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">are full of evil;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">delusion is in their hearts during their lives, and then they die.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Death Deprives Humans of Everything in Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:7–12; Isaiah 38:18; Luke 16:26–29"> Whoever is joined<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Kethib reads “chosen,” but the Qere as well as all the versions and numerous medieval Hebrew manuscripts read “joined”">e</a></sup>to all the living has hope. After all, even a live dog is better than a dead lion!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 26:14; Ecclesiastes 1:11; Psalm 6:5"> For the living know that they will die, but the dead do not know anything. They no longer have a reward, and even the memory of them is forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 146:3–4; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Job 3:17–18"> What they loved and hated, as well as what they desired, has already perished. They no longer have any share in what is done under the sun.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Enjoy Life While It Lasts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Deuteronomy 12:7; Ecclesiastes 10:19"> Go—eat your food with joy, and drink your wine with a merry heart! For God already has approved your deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:14; Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8"> Always be clothed in white garments, and never let your head lack oil!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; Ecclesiastes 2:10; Malachi 2:15"> Enjoy life with the wife whom you love all the days of your vain life which he gives you under the sun, because this is your lot in life and in the toil with which you toil under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 5:16; Romans 12:11"> Whatever your hand finds to do—do it with all your might; for in Sheol—where you are going—no one works, plans, knows, or thinks about anything.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Injustice of Time and Chance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; Proverbs 21:30–31; Lamentations 3:37–38"> I looked again and saw under the sun that the race does not belong to the swift, the battle does not belong to the mighty, food does not belong to the wise, wealth does not belong to the intelligent, and success<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “favor”">f</a></sup>does not belong to the skillful, for time and chance befalls all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Luke 21:34–36; Ecclesiastes 8:5–7"> For man does not know his time. Just as fish are caught in a cruel net and like birds who are seized in a snare, so also ⸤humans⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the man”">g</a></sup>are ensnared at a cruel time when it falls suddenly upon them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Wisdom—Although Vulnerable—is Superior to Power

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:16; Ecclesiastes 7:15; Ecclesiastes 6:1"> I have also seen this example of wisdom under the sun, and it seemed great to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:15–22; 2 Kings 6:24–20"> There was a small city with few people in it. A great king came and besieged it, building great siege works against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:13; Genesis 40:23; Esther 6:2–3"> Now, a poor wise man was found in it, and he delivered the city by his wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Proverbs 24:5; Proverbs 21:22"> So I concluded that wisdom is better than might, yet the wisdom of the poor is despised, and his words are not heard.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Wisdom—Although Vulnerable—is Superior to Folly

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 3:17–18; Proverbs 28:23; James 1:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The words of the wise are heard in peace</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">more than the shouting of a ruler is heard among the fools.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 7:11–12; Ecclesiastes 9:16; Joshua 7:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wisdom is better than weapons of war,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but one sinner destroys much good.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
