## The Call of Ezekiel to Speak God’s Words

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 10:11; Ezekiel 3:17; Ezekiel 4:1"> And he said to me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">a</a></sup>stand on your feet, so that I can speak with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:24; Numbers 11:25–26; Ezekiel 3:12"> And the Spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “a spirit”">b</a></sup>came into me as he was speaking to me, and it set me on my feet, and I heard the one speaking to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:25; 1 Samuel 8:7–8; Deuteronomy 9:24"> And he said to me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">c</a></sup>I am sending you to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>to nations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “a people”">e</a></sup>who are rebelling, who rebelled against me, they and their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “fathers”">f</a></sup>they transgressed against me ⸤until this very day⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “until the day the this”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:7; Isaiah 48:4; Jeremiah 5:3"> And the children<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “sons”">h</a></sup>are ⸤impudent⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “hard of face”">i</a></sup>and ⸤stubborn⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “strong of heart”">j</a></sup>and so I am sending you to them, and you must say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:33; Ezekiel 3:27; Matthew 10:12–15"> And they, whether they listen or whether they fail to listen, for they are a rebellious house, they will know that a prophet was in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:8; Isaiah 51:12; 1 Peter 3:14"> And you, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">k</a></sup>you must not be afraid of them, and you must not be afraid of their words, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “although”">l</a></sup>briers and thorns are with you, and you are sitting among scorpions. You must not be afraid of their words, and you must not be dismayed because of their ⸤looks⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “faces”">m</a></sup>for they are a rebellious house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Ezekiel 3:17; Jeremiah 1:7"> And you must speak my words to them whether they listen or whether they fail to listen, for they are rebellious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 10:9; Jeremiah 15:16; Isaiah 50:5"> And you, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">n</a></sup>hear what I am speaking to you: you must not be rebellious like the house of rebellion. Open your mouth and eat what I am giving to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Revelation 5:1–5; Daniel 10:10"> And I looked,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “saw”">o</a></sup>and look! There was a hand stretched out to me, and look! In it was ⸤a scroll with writing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “a scroll of a book”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Isaiah 3:11; Revelation 9:12"> And he rolled it out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “he spread it out”">q</a></sup>⸤before me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to the face of me”">r</a></sup>and it was written on the front and back, and there were written on it laments and mourning and wailing.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “woe”">s</a></sup></span>
</div>
