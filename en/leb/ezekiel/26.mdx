## Ezekiel Prophesies Against Tyre

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Jeremiah 39:2"> And it was in the ⸤eleventh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “one ten”">a</a></sup>year, on the first day of the month, the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “was”">b</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to say”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1–18; Ezekiel 36:2; Joel 3:4"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">d</a></sup>because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “because that”">e</a></sup>Tyre said concerning Jerusalem, ‘Ah! The gates of the peoples are broken; it has swung open to me; I shall be filled, for it lies in ruins!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:42; Isaiah 5:30; Micah 4:11"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘Look! I am against you, Tyre, and I will bring up against you many nations like the stirring up of the sea ⸤stirring up its waves⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “with respect to its waves”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 23:11; Amos 1:10; Ezekiel 26:9"> And I will destroy the walls of Tyre, and they will demolish its towers, and I will scrape away its earthen dirt from it, and I will make it into ⸤a bare rock⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to barrenness of rock”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 27:32; Ezekiel 25:7"> It will become a place for spreading out dragnets in the midst of the sea; for I have spoken,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">h</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh. ‘And it will become as plunder for the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:46; Ezekiel 26:8; Jeremiah 49:2"> and its daughters who are in the field with the sword, they will be killed; and they will know that I am Yahweh.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 7:12; Daniel 2:37; Ezekiel 23:24"> For thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I am bringing to Tyre Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon, from the north, the king of kings, with horse and with chariot and with horsemen and his assembly and many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:22; Jeremiah 52:4; Jeremiah 32:24"> Your daughters he will kill in the field with the sword, and he will place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “give”">i</a></sup>against you siege works, and he will build against you a siege ramp, and he will raise against you a shield,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:15"> and the thrust of his battering ram he will direct against your walls, and your towers he will break down with his weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15; Ezekiel 27:28; Jeremiah 47:3"> From the abundance of his horses he will cover you with their fine dust; at the sound of horseman and wheel and chariot your walls will shake, at his coming into your gates like the entrance of a city that is being broken through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:28; Isaiah 26:5; Habakkuk 1:8"> With the hooves of his horses he will trample all of your streets. He will kill your people with the sword, and ⸤your strong stone pillars⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the stone pillars of strength your”">j</a></sup>will tumble down to the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 23:8; 2 Chronicles 32:27; Zechariah 9:3–4"> And they will plunder your wealth, and they will loot your merchandise, and they will break down your walls, and the houses of your delight they will break down, and your stones and your timbers and your earthen dirt they will cast into the midst of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Isaiah 23:16; Isaiah 14:11"> And, I will put an end to the noise of your songs, and the sound of your lyres will not be heard any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 1:4; Job 12:14; Numbers 23:19"> And I will make you into ⸤a bare rock⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to barrenness of rock”">k</a></sup>a place for the spreading out of dragnets. You will not be built again, for I, Yahweh, I have spoken,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">l</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:35; Jeremiah 49:21; Ezekiel 26:18"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh to Tyre, “Will not the coastlands shake from the sound of your downfall, at the groaning of the wounded, ⸤at people being killed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “at being killed a killing”">m</a></sup>in the midst of you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:10; Jonah 3:6; Job 8:22"> And all the princes of the sea will go down from their thrones, and they will remove their robes, and their ⸤beautiful garments⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the garments of beautiful finished cloth”">n</a></sup>of finished cloth they will take off. With terror they will be clothed, and on the ground they will sit, and they will tremble ⸤continually⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “for moments”">o</a></sup>and they will be appalled over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:12; Ezekiel 19:1; 2 Samuel 1:25–27"> And they will raise a lament over you, and they will say to you,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘How you have been lost who was inhabited from</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “on”">p</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the seas;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the city that was praised, that was strong.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is located on the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its inhabitants imposed their terror ⸤on all of its inhabitants⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to all of its inhabitants”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:15; Isaiah 23:5–7; Ezekiel 27:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now the coastlands will tremble</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">at the day of your downfall,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the islands that are in the sea</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will be horrified because of your departure.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 26:3; Daniel 11:40"> For thus says the Lord Yahweh: “When I make you a desolate city, like the cities that are not inhabited, when bringing up over you the deep, the ⸤great surging waters⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the waters the many”">r</a></sup>will cover you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 27:13; Luke 10:15; Zechariah 2:8"> And I will bring you down with those who are going down to the grave,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “pit”">s</a></sup>⸤an ancient people⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “a people of eternity/endless ages”">t</a></sup>and I will cause you to dwell in the world<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “land”">u</a></sup>of the depths, in the ruins from of old with those who are going down to the grave,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “pit”">v</a></sup>so that you will not be inhabited and ⸤have a place⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “and I will give beauty in the land of the living”">w</a></sup>in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:36; Ezekiel 28:19; Revelation 18:21"> Sudden terrors I will bring on you, and you shall no longer exist; and you will be sought, and you will not be found again ⸤forever⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “to eternity/endless ages”">x</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">y</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
</div>
