## A Valley of Dried Bones; A Renewed Nation of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:22; Ezekiel 1:3; Acts 8:39"> The hand of Yahweh was upon me, and he brought me by the Spirit of Yahweh, and he ⸤let me rest⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “placed me”; literally “set me”">a</a></sup>in the midst of the valley, and it was full of bones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 141:7; Ezekiel 37:11; Deuteronomy 11:30"> And he led me ⸤all around⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “all around, all around”">b</a></sup>over them, and look, very many on the surface of the valley, and look, very dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:21; Deuteronomy 32:39"> And he said to me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">c</a></sup>can these bones live?” And I said, “Lord Yahweh, you know.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 5:25; John 5:28–29; Matthew 21:21"> And he said to me, “Prophesy to these bones, and you must say to them, to the dry bones, ‘Hear the word of Yahweh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:14; Psalm 104:29–30; Genesis 2:7"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh to these bones: “Look! I am bringing into you breath, and you will live!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:27; Isaiah 49:23; Joel 3:17"> And I will lay on you sinews, and I will let flesh come upon you, and I will cover you over with skin, and I will put breath into you, and you will live, and you will know that I am Yahweh.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:2; 1 Kings 19:11–13; Jeremiah 13:5–7"> And I prophesied ⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “as that”">d</a></sup>I had been commanded, and there was a sound at my prophesying, and look! A rattling, and they came together—the bones! Bone to its bone!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And I looked, and indeed, sinews were on them, and flesh went up,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “appeared”">e</a></sup>and skin covered over<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “on”">f</a></sup>them ⸤upward⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from to above”">g</a></sup>but breath was not in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:8; Song of Solomon 4:16; Psalm 104:30"> And he said to me, “Prophesy to the breath; prophesy, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">h</a></sup>and you must say to the breath, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh ⸤from the four winds⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “from four winds”">i</a></sup>“Come, O spirit and breath, on these dead ones, so that they may live!” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 11:11; Psalm 104:30; Revelation 20:4–5"> And I prophesied ⸤as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “like what”">j</a></sup>he commanded me, and the breath came into them, and they became alive, and they stood on their feet, a very, very large group.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1; Psalm 77:7–9; Ezekiel 39:25"> And he said to me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">k</a></sup>these bones are all of the house of Israel; look! they are saying, ‘Our bones are dried up, and our hope is destroyed; ⸤we are cut off as far as we are concerned⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “we are cut in two for us”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; Hosea 13:14; 1 Thessalonians 4:16"> Therefore prophesy, and you must say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I am opening your graves, and I will bring you up from your graves, my people, and I will bring you to the land of Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:6; Ezekiel 16:62; Psalm 126:2–3"> And you will know that I am Yahweh when I open your graves when I bring you up from your graves, my people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Joel 2:28–29; Ezekiel 36:36"> And I will put my breath into you so that you may live, and I will cause you to rest on your soil, and you will know that I, Yahweh, I have spoken, and I will act!” ’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">m</a></sup>Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “was”">n</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to say”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:9; 2 Chronicles 10:19; 2 Chronicles 10:17"> “And you, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">p</a></sup>take for yourself ⸤a piece of wood⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a piece of wood one”">q</a></sup>and write on it, ‘For Judah and for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">r</a></sup>his associates,’ and take another piece of wood, and write on it, ‘For Joseph, the piece of wood of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “for”">s</a></sup>Ephraim and all of the house of Israel his associates.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 1:11; Jeremiah 50:4; Isaiah 11:13"> And join them one to the other with respect to you as one piece of wood, so that they may become one in your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 12:9; Ezekiel 17:12"> ⸤When⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “as that”">t</a></sup>⸤your people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “the children of your people”">u</a></sup>say to you, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “to say”">v</a></sup>‘Will you not inform us as to what these actions mean for you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 10:6; Ezekiel 37:16–17; Ephesians 2:13–14"> Then speak to them, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I am taking the piece of wood for Joseph that is in the hand of Ephraim and the tribes of Israel its associates, and I will put them on it, the piece of wood of Judah, and I will make them into one piece of wood, so that they be one in my hand.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:3; Numbers 17:6–9; Hosea 12:10"> And the pieces of wood on which you wrote will be in your hand before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:24; Jeremiah 29:14; Jeremiah 23:8"> And speak to them, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I am taking the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">w</a></sup>from among ⸤the nations to which they went⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “which they went there”">x</a></sup>and I will gather them from ⸤everywhere⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “all around”">y</a></sup>and I will bring them to their own soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24; Ezekiel 34:23–24; Jeremiah 3:18"> And I will make them into one nation in the land on the mountains of Israel, and ⸤they will all have one king as their king⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “and king one he will be for all of them to king”">z</a></sup>and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="According to the reading tradition (Qere )">aa</a></sup>will not again be two nations and will not again divide into two kingdoms again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 21:3–4; Ezekiel 36:28–29; 1 John 1:9"> And they will not defile themselves again with their idols and with vile idols and with all of their transgressions, and I will save them from all of their apostasy ⸤by which they sinned⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “which they sinned by them”">ab</a></sup>and I will cleanse them and they will be for me as a people and I, I will be for them as God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:23–24; Hosea 3:5; Ezekiel 37:25"> “ ‘ “And my servant David will be king over them, and one shepherd will be for all of them, and in my regulations they will go, and my statutes they will observe, and they will do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Amos 9:15; Ezekiel 37:24; Ezekiel 28:25"> And they will dwell on the land that I gave to my servant, to Jacob, ⸤in which your ancestors dwelled⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “which they dwelt in it your fathers”">ac</a></sup>and they will dwell on it, they and their children and the children of their children ⸤forever⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “until eternity” or “until unlimited time”">ad</a></sup>and my servant David will be a leader for them ⸤forever⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “to eternity” or “to endless ages”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Jeremiah 30:19; Ezekiel 34:25"> And I will make with them a covenant of peace; ⸤an everlasting covenant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “a covenant of eternity”">af</a></sup>it will be with them. And I will establish them, and I will cause them to increase, and I will put my sanctuary in the midst of them ⸤forever⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “to eternity” or “to unlimited ages”">ag</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; John 1:14; Ezekiel 37:23"> And my tabernacle will be with them, and I will be for them as God, and they will be to me as a nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17–19; Ezekiel 20:12; Exodus 31:13"> And the nations will know that I, Yahweh, am consecrating Israel when my sanctuary is in the midst of them ⸤forever⸥.” ’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “to eternity” or “to unlimited ages”">ah</a></sup></span>
