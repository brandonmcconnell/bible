## The Allotment of the Land and the Gates of the City

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:15–17; Joshua 19:40–47; Revelation 7:4–8"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “Now”">a</a></sup>these are the names of the tribes: At the end of the north, ⸤along the way to Hethlon⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to the side of the way of Hethlon”">b</a></sup>from Lebo-hamath to Hazar Enan on the boundary of Damascus ⸤beside Hamath northward⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “northward to the side of Hamat,”">c</a></sup>there will be one portion for Dan from the eastern border to the ⸤west⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sea”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–31; Genesis 30:12–13"> And next to the territory of Dan, from ⸤the eastern border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the side of the east">e</a></sup>up to ⸤the western border⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the side of the sea/west”">f</a></sup>one portion for Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–39; Genesis 30:7–8"> And next to the territory of Asher, from ⸤the eastern border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the side of the east”">g</a></sup>and up to ⸤the western border⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the side of the west/sea”">h</a></sup>one portion for Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–11; Joshua 13:29–31; Genesis 48:14–20"> And next to the territory of Naphtali, from ⸤the eastern border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the side of the east”">i</a></sup>up to ⸤the western border⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the side of the sea/west”">j</a></sup>one portion for Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 17:8–10; Joshua 17:14–18; Joshua 16:1–10"> And next to the territory of Manasseh, from ⸤the eastern border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the side of the east”">k</a></sup>up to ⸤the western border⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the side of the sea/west”">l</a></sup>one portion for Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 13:15–21; Genesis 29:32; Genesis 49:3–4"> And next to the territory of Ephraim, from ⸤the eastern border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the side of the east”">m</a></sup>and up to ⸤the western border⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the side of the sea/west">n</a></sup>one portion for Reuben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:1–63; Joshua 19:9; Genesis 29:35"> And next to the territory of Reuben, from ⸤the eastern border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the side of the east">o</a></sup>up to ⸤the western border⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the side of the sea/west">p</a></sup>one portion for Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:1–6; Isaiah 12:6; Isaiah 33:20–22"> And next to the territory of Judah, from ⸤the eastern border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the side of the east">q</a></sup>up to ⸤the western border⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the side of the sea/west">r</a></sup>shall be located the contribution that you set apart, twenty-five thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="That is, 8.33 miles">s</a></sup>in width, and its length just like one of the portions from ⸤the eastern border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “the side of the east">t</a></sup>up to ⸤the western border⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “the side of the sea/west">u</a></sup>and the sanctuary shall be in the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The contribution that you shall set apart for Yahweh, its length shall be twenty-five thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="That is, 8.33 miles">v</a></sup>and its width ten thousand cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="That is, 3.5 miles; cf. 48:13 and 45:1">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:4; Ezekiel 44:28; Matthew 10:10"> And to these shall be ⸤the holy district⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “the contribution of the holiness”">x</a></sup>to the priests northwards twenty-five thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="That is, 8.33 miles">y</a></sup>and westwards its width ten thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="That is, 3.5 miles">z</a></sup>and eastwards its width ten thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="That is, 3.5 miles">aa</a></sup>and southwards its length twenty-five thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="That is, 8.33 miles">ab</a></sup>and the sanctuary of Yahweh shall be in the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:46; Ezekiel 44:10; Matthew 24:45–46"> To the priests, the ones being consecrated from the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “sons”">ac</a></sup>of Zadok, who took care of my service and who ⸤did not go astray⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “who not they went astray”">ad</a></sup>⸤when the Israelites went astray⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “at going astray the children of Israel”">ae</a></sup>⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “as that”">af</a></sup>the Levites went astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 27:21; Ezekiel 45:4"> And it will be for them a special gift from the contribution of the land ⸤as a most holy object⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “as a holiness of holinesses”">ag</a></sup>next to the territory of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:3; Deuteronomy 12:19; Ezekiel 45:5"> And the Levites ⸤alongside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “to corresponding”">ah</a></sup>the territory of the priests shall have twenty-five thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="That is, 8.33 miles">ai</a></sup>in length and in width ten thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="That is, 3.5 miles">aj</a></sup>its ⸤full length⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “all/every of its length ….”">ak</a></sup>twenty-five thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="That is, 8.33 miles">al</a></sup>and its full width, ten thousand cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="That is, 3.5 miles">am</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Leviticus 27:9–10; Exodus 22:29"> And they shall not sell any part from it, and shall not exchange, and shall not transfer the best of the land, for it is holy to Yahweh.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And a pastureland shall be for the city northwards, two hundred and fifty cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="That is, 150 yards">az</a></sup>southwards two hundred and fifty cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="That is, 150 yards">ba</a></sup>and eastwards two hundred and fifty cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="That is, 150 yards">bb</a></sup>and westwards two hundred and fifty cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="That is, 150 yards">bc</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 9:27; Ezra 2:43–58; Ezekiel 45:6"> And the rest in its length ⸤alongside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="Literally “to corresponding”">bd</a></sup>⸤the holy district⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-be" id="fnref-be" data-fn="Literally “the contribution of the holiness”">be</a></sup>ten thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="That is, 3.5 miles">bf</a></sup>eastwards and ten thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="That is, 3.5 miles">bg</a></sup>westwards; and it shall be ⸤alongside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Literally “to corresponding”">bh</a></sup>⸤the holy district⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bi" id="fnref-bi" data-fn="Literally “the contribution of the holiness”">bi</a></sup>and its yield<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bj" id="fnref-bj" data-fn="According to the reading tradition (Qere ); cf. BHS">bj</a></sup>shall be as food for the workers of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:6; 1 Kings 4:7–23; Nehemiah 11:1–36"> And the workers of the city from all the tribes of Israel shall cultivate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 21:16; Hebrews 12:17"> All of the contribution, twenty-five thousand by twenty five thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bk" id="fnref-bk" data-fn="That is, 8.33 square miles">bk</a></sup>square, you shall set apart as ⸤the holy district⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bl" id="fnref-bl" data-fn="Literally “the contribution of the holiness”">bl</a></sup>along with the property of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:22; Ezekiel 37:24; Ezekiel 48:8"> And the rest shall be to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bm" id="fnref-bm" data-fn="Or “for”">bm</a></sup>the prince, ⸤on both sides⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bn" id="fnref-bn" data-fn="Literally “from this and from this”">bn</a></sup>⸤of the holy district⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bo" id="fnref-bo" data-fn="Literally “of the contribution of the holiness”">bo</a></sup>and of the property of the city ⸤extending from⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bp" id="fnref-bp" data-fn="Literally “to in front of”">bp</a></sup>twenty-five thousand cubits of the contribution up to the boundary of the east, and westwards ⸤extending⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bq" id="fnref-bq" data-fn="Literally “to in front of”">bq</a></sup>twenty-five thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-br" id="fnref-br" data-fn="That is, 8.33 miles">br</a></sup>to the boundary of the sea ⸤alongside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bs" id="fnref-bs" data-fn="Literally “to corresponding”">bs</a></sup>the tribal portions. They shall be for the prince, and it shall be ⸤the holy district⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bt" id="fnref-bt" data-fn="Literally “the contribution of the holiness”">bt</a></sup>and the sanctuary of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bu" id="fnref-bu" data-fn="Or “house”">bu</a></sup>will be in its middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And also the property of the Levites and the property of the city will be in the midst of the property which is for the prince; between the territory of Judah and between the territory of Benjamin the land will be for the prince.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 18:21–28; Ezekiel 48:1–7; Genesis 35:16–19"> And for the remainder of the tribes: From the side on the east up to the side on the west, Benjamin one portion;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 19:1–9; Genesis 29:33; Genesis 49:5–7"> and next to the territory of Benjamin, from the side on the east up to the side of the sea, Simeon one portion;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 19:17–23; Genesis 30:14–18"> and next to the territory of Simeon, from the side of the east up to the side of the sea, Issachar one portion;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 19:10–16; Genesis 30:19–20"> and next to the territory of Issachar, from the side of the east up to the side of the sea, Zebulun one portion;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 13:24–28; Genesis 30:10–11"> and next to the territory of Zebulun, from the side of the east up to the side of the sea, Gad one portion;</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:16; Revelation 21:16; Ezekiel 48:32–35"> And these shall be the exits of the city: From<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bz" id="fnref-bz" data-fn="Or “on” or “along”">bz</a></sup>⸤the north side⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ca" id="fnref-ca" data-fn="Literally “the side of the north”">ca</a></sup>four thousand and five hundred cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-cb" id="fnref-cb" data-fn="That is, 1.5 miles">cb</a></sup>by measurement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 21:12–13; Isaiah 26:1–2; Revelation 21:21"> And the gates of the city shall be according to the names of the tribes of Israel: three gates to the north, the gate of Reuben, one; the gate of Judah, one; the gate of Levi, one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And on the east side, four thousand five hundred cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-cc" id="fnref-cc" data-fn="That is, 1.5 miles">cc</a></sup>and three gates, and so the gate of Joseph, one; the gate of Benjamin, one; the gate of Dan, one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And on the south side, four thousand five hundred cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-cd" id="fnref-cd" data-fn="That is, 1.5 miles">cd</a></sup>by measurement, and so three gates, the gate of Simeon, one; the gate of Issachar, one; the gate of Zebulun, one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And on the west<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ce" id="fnref-ce" data-fn="Or “sea”">ce</a></sup>side, four thousand five hundred cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-cf" id="fnref-cf" data-fn="That is, 1.5 miles">cf</a></sup>and their gates three, the gate of Gad, one; the gate of Asher, one; the gate of Naphtali, one.</span>
