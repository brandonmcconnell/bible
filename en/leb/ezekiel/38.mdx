<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was”">a</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to say”">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:8; Ezekiel 39:1–10; Ezekiel 29:3"> And you must say, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I am against you, Gog, the head<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “chief”">e</a></sup>leader of Meshach and Tubal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:4; Daniel 11:40; Ezekiel 38:15"> and I will turn you around, and I will place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “give”">f</a></sup>hooks in your cheeks, and I will bring you out and all of your horses and horsemen ⸤fully armed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “clothed of fullness”">g</a></sup>all of them, a great crowd, holding a shield,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “buckler”">h</a></sup>and small shield, and holding<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “wielding”">i</a></sup>swords, all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:10; Genesis 10:6; Ezekiel 30:5"> Persia, Cush, and Put are with them, all of them, with a small shield and helmet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:14; Genesis 10:2–3; 1 Chronicles 1:5–6"> Gomer and all of its troops, Beth Togarmah, the remote areas of the north, and with all of its troops and many peoples with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:8; Joel 3:9–12; Isaiah 37:22"> Be ready, and prepare ⸤yourselves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “for you”">j</a></sup>you and all of your assembly,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “battalions” or “hoards”">k</a></sup>the assembling<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “assemblies”">l</a></sup>around you, and you must be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “serve”">m</a></sup>for them as a guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Isaiah 24:22; Ezekiel 38:11–12"> After many days you will be mustered; ⸤in the last years⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “final years”; literally “in the last of the years”">n</a></sup>you will come to a restored land from the sword, gathered from many peoples on the mountains of Israel which were as ⸤permanent ruins⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “ruins continually/permanently”">o</a></sup>but from peoples it was brought out, and they will dwell ⸤in safety⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “with confidence/assurance/trust”">p</a></sup>all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 28:2; Jeremiah 4:13; Joel 2:2"> And you will advance like a storm; you will come, and you will be like a cloud ⸤covering⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to cover”">q</a></sup>the land, and all of your troops and many nations along with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 36:4; Micah 2:1; Psalm 83:3–4"> “ ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “and it will be”">r</a></sup>on that day, things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “thoughts”">s</a></sup>will come up on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “in”">t</a></sup>your mind, and you will devise evil plans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 2:4–5; Proverbs 3:29–30; Jeremiah 49:31–32"> And you will say, ‘I will go up against a land of open country; I will come<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “go”">u</a></sup>to the people being at rest ⸤in safety⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “with confidence”">v</a></sup>all of them dwelling without a wall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “walls”">w</a></sup>and crossbars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “crossbar”">x</a></sup>and ⸤without doors⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “and doors there are not for them”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; Ezekiel 38:8; Ezekiel 29:19"> to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “for”">z</a></sup>loot loot,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “war-booty”; this word is chosen because of the alliteration in Hebrew">aa</a></sup>and to plunder<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “seize”; this word is chosen because of the alliteration in Hebrew">ab</a></sup>plunder, ⸤to assail⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “to bring back your hand against”; NJPS, “to turn your hand against”">ac</a></sup>inhabited ruins and a people gathered together from various peoples and who are acquiring livestock and goods and dwelling at the center of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:15; Ezekiel 27:22–23; Ezekiel 32:2"> Sheba and Dedan and traders of Tarshish and all its strong lions,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “all of its violent leaders”">ad</a></sup>they will ask you, ‘To<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “for”">ae</a></sup>loot loot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “war-booty” this word is chosen because of the alliteration in Hebrew">af</a></sup>are you coming? To plunder<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “seize”; this word is chosen because of the alliteration in Hebrew">ag</a></sup>plunder have you summoned your assembly, to take away silver and gold, to take livestock and goods, to loot great amounts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Hebrew “amount”">ah</a></sup>of loot?” ’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:8; Zechariah 2:5; Jeremiah 23:6"> “Therefore prophesy, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">ai</a></sup>and you must say to Gog, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Will you not realize<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="LXX reads, “stir up/rouse yourself”">aj</a></sup>on that day when my people Israel are dwelling ⸤in safety⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “with confidence/trust”">ak</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:2; Ezekiel 38:6; Revelation 20:8"> and so you will come from your place, from the remote areas of the north, you and many people with you, ⸤horsemen⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “riders of horsemen”">al</a></sup>all of them, a great crowd and a vast army,</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; Revelation 11:13; Revelation 16:20"> And in my passion, in the fire of my wrath, I spoke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “I asserted”">ap</a></sup>that ⸤certainly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “if not”">aq</a></sup>on that day a great earthquake will be on the land of Israel.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Haggai 2:22; Judges 7:22; Ezekiel 14:17"> And I will call against him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Or “summon against him” (cf. NJPS)">ax</a></sup>in all of my mountains a sword,’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">ay</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, ‘And the sword of each person will be against his brother.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 9:16; Revelation 19:1–6; Ezekiel 38:16"> And so I will exalt myself, and I will show myself holy, and I will make myself known before the eyes of many nations, and they will know that I am Yahweh.’ ”</span>
