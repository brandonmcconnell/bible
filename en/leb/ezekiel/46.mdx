<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 66:23; Ezekiel 44:1–2; Exodus 20:9"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “The gate of the inner courtyard facing east must be shut on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “during”">a</a></sup>the six of the days for work, but on the day of the Sabbath it must be opened and on the day of the new moon it must be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:3; Ezekiel 46:8; 1 Chronicles 29:10–12"> And the prince shall come by way of the portico of the gate from the outside, and he shall stand at the doorframe of the gate and the priests shall prepare his burnt offering and his fellowship offerings, and he shall bow down at the threshold of the gate. Then he shall go out, but the gate shall not be closed until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:10; John 10:9; Hebrews 10:19–22"> And the people of the land will bow down at the doorway of that gate on the Sabbaths and on the new moons ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">b</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:17; Numbers 28:9–10"> And the burnt offering which the prince will offer to Yahweh on the Sabbath day must be six male lambs without defect and a ram without defect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:24; Ezekiel 46:7; Deuteronomy 16:17"> And the grain offering he will give shall be an ephah ⸤for each ram⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to the ram”">c</a></sup>and for the male lambs the grain offering shall be ⸤as much as he wants to give⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the gift of his hand”">d</a></sup>and a hin of olive oil ⸤for each ephah⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to the ephah”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1"> And on the day of the new moon he will offer a bull, ⸤a calf⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “son of cattle,” or “son of the herd”">f</a></sup>without defect and six male lambs and a ram; they must be without defect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5"> And an ephah for each bull and an ephah for the ram he must provide as a grain offering, and also for the male lambs ⸤as much as he can afford⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “as that it stretches out his hand”">g</a></sup>and a hin of olive oil for each ephah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Ezekiel 44:1–3; Colossians 1:18"> And when the prince comes, on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “by”">h</a></sup>the way of the portico of the gate he shall come and by this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “his”">i</a></sup>way he shall go out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; 2 Peter 2:20–21"> And when the people of the land come ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">j</a></sup>Yahweh at the festivals, the one coming by way of the gate of the north to bow down<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “to worship”">k</a></sup>must go out by the way of the gate of the south, and the one coming by way of the gate of the south must go out by the way of the gate of the north, so he must not return by the way of the gate by which he came,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “came by it”">l</a></sup>but before it he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="According to the reading tradition (Qere )">m</a></sup>shall go out opposite it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 42:4; 1 Chronicles 29:20; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> And the prince will be in the midst of them; he will come in when they come in, and when they go out he will go out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5; Ezekiel 46:7; Numbers 15:1–41"> And at the festivals and at the appointed times, the grain offering will be an ephah with a bull and an ephah with the ram and with the male lambs, ⸤as much as he wants to give⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the gift of his hand”">n</a></sup>and a hin of olive oil ⸤for each ephah⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to the ephah”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1–2; Ezekiel 45:17; Ezekiel 44:3"> And when the prince makes a freewill offering, a burnt offering, or a fellowship offering as a freewill offering to Yahweh, the gate facing east will be open for him. And he must offer his burnt offering and his fellowship offering ⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “as that”">p</a></sup>he does on the day of the Sabbath, and then he must go out and the gate will shut ⸤after he goes out⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “after his going out”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; John 1:29; Exodus 12:5"> And a male lamb, ⸤a yearling⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “a son of his year”">r</a></sup>without defect, he must provide as a burnt offering ⸤daily⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “for the day”">s</a></sup>for Yahweh; ⸤every morning⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “in the morning in the morning”">t</a></sup>he must provide it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 28:5"> And a grain offering he must provide in addition to it ⸤every morning⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “in the morning in the morning”">u</a></sup>a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">v</a></sup>sixth of an<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">w</a></sup>ephah and a third of a hin of olive oil to moisten the finely milled flour as a grain offering to Yahweh ⸤as a perpetual statute⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “regulations of everlasting continually”">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1–10; Numbers 28:6; Exodus 29:42"> So they must provide the male lamb and the grain offering and the olive oil ⸤every morning⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “in the morning in the morning”">y</a></sup>⸤as a regular burnt offering⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “as burnt offering of regularity/continuity”">z</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Galatians 4:7; Genesis 25:5–6"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “If the prince gives a gift to any one from among his sons, it is his inheritance and it will belong<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “be”">aa</a></sup>to his sons; it is their possession as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Luke 19:25–26; Galatians 4:30–31"> And if he give a gift from his inheritance to one from among his servants, then it will belong<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “be”">ab</a></sup>to him until the year of release,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="That is, the Year of Jubilee">ac</a></sup>and then it will return to the prince. His inheritance with respect to his sons will be only for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:8; 1 Kings 21:19; Ezekiel 22:27"> But the prince shall not take from the inheritance of the people to rob<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “oppress”">ad</a></sup>them, separating them from their property; from his own property he shall bestow an inheritance on his sons, so that my people will not be driven away, any one, from his property.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:9; Ezekiel 44:4–5; Ezekiel 40:44–46"> Then he brought me through the entrance which was on the side of the gate ⸤to the holy chambers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “to the chamber of the holiness”">ae</a></sup>to the priests; the chambers were facing northwards and, look, there was a place at the extreme end westwards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:13; Ezekiel 44:29; Ezekiel 44:19"> And he said to me, “This is the place where the priests will boil the guilt offering and the sin offering, and where they shall bake the grain offering in order not to bring them out to the outer courtyard to make the people holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And he brought me to the outer courtyard and led me past the four corners of the courtyard; and look, ⸤there was a courtyard in each corner of the courtyard⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “a courtyard in the corner of the courtyard, a courtyard in the corner of the courtyard”">af</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In the four corners of the courtyard were small<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Hebrew uncertain">ag</a></sup>courtyards forty cubits in length and thirty cubits in width, each with ⸤the same measurements⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “measurement one”">ah</a></sup>for the four of them with corners.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “being cornered”">ai</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And a row was all around inside<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “in”">aj</a></sup>them, and cooking-places were made under the rows of stones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Possibly some kind of stone ledge">ak</a></sup>all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:20; John 21:15–17; 1 Peter 5:2"> And he said to me, “These are the houses for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “of”">al</a></sup>cooking, where the ones serving the temple shall cook the sacrifices of the people.”</span>
</div>
