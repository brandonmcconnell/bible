## The Prince, the Levites, and the Priests Described

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 42:14"> And he brought me back by the way of the outer gate of the sanctuary that is facing east, and it was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2–4; Exodus 24:10; Isaiah 6:1–5"> And Yahweh said to me, “This gate will be shut. It shall not be opened, and ⸤no one⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and a man not”">a</a></sup>will go through it, for Yahweh, the God of Israel, has entered it, and it will be shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Ezekiel 34:24; Ezekiel 37:25"> The prince, he may sit in it to eat food ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">b</a></sup>Yahweh; he will come from the way<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “direction”">c</a></sup>of the portico of the gate and by means of its way he will also go out.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Revelation 1:17; Ezekiel 3:23"> Then he brought me by the way of the gate of the north to the front of the temple,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “house”">d</a></sup>and I looked,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “saw”">e</a></sup>and look! The glory of Yahweh filled the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “house”">f</a></sup>of Yahweh, and I fell on my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:4; Ezekiel 43:10–11; Deuteronomy 12:32"> And Yahweh said, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">g</a></sup>set your heart<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “pay close attention” or “listen closely”">h</a></sup>and look with your eyes and with your ears hear ⸤everything⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “all of which/what”">i</a></sup>I am saying to you concerning all the statutes of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “house”">j</a></sup>of Yahweh and concerning all its laws,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="According to the reading tradition (Qere ); cf. BHS">k</a></sup>and you must listen with your heart concerning the entrance of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “house”">l</a></sup>with all of the exits of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:9; Ezekiel 45:9; 1 Peter 4:3"> And you must say to the rebellious, to the house of Israel, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Enough for you, house of Israel, of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “from”">m</a></sup>all of your detestable things!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 9:26; Ezekiel 44:9"> At<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “in”">n</a></sup>your bringing ⸤foreigners⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “children/people of a foreign land”">o</a></sup>who are uncircumcised of heart and uncircumcised of flesh to be in my sanctuary to profane it, my temple,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “house”">p</a></sup>⸤as you offered my food, fat, and blood⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “in/at your offering my food and fat and blood”">q</a></sup>so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “and”">r</a></sup>you broke my covenant by all of your detestable things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:14; Numbers 18:3–5; Ezekiel 40:45–46"> And you did not observe the responsibility of my sanctuary, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “and”">s</a></sup>you appointed them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="That is, foreigners">t</a></sup>as the keepers of my responsibility in my sanctuary for you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 44:7; Zechariah 14:21"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Every ⸤foreigner⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “child of a foreign land”">u</a></sup>uncircumcised of heart and uncircumcised of flesh shall not come into my sanctuary—not any of the ⸤foreigners⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “child of a foreign land”">v</a></sup>who are in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 48:11; 2 Kings 23:8–9"> ⸤But⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “For if”">x</a></sup>the Levites who removed themselves from me ⸤at the going astray of Israel⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “when Israel went astray”">y</a></sup>who went astray from me and went after their idols, as a result they will bear their guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 16:9; 2 Chronicles 29:34; 2 Chronicles 30:17"> Then they will be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “may be”">z</a></sup>in my sanctuary, those serving in my sanctuary, as sentries<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “guards”">aa</a></sup>at the gates of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “house”">ab</a></sup>and as those serving the temple;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “house”">ac</a></sup>they will slaughter the burnt offering and the sacrifice for the people, and they will stand ⸤before them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “to the face of them”">ad</a></sup>to serve them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 16:10–16; Psalm 106:26; Ezekiel 14:3–4"> ⸤because⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “because that”">ae</a></sup>they used to serve them ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">af</a></sup>their idols and they were for the house of Israel like a stumbling block of iniquity. Therefore ⸤I swore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “I raised my hand”">ag</a></sup>concerning them,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">ah</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, “that they will bear their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:3; 2 Kings 23:9; Ezekiel 16:61"> And they shall not approach me to serve as a priest for me and to come near to all of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “any of”">ai</a></sup>my holy objects, ⸤to the most holy objects⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “to the holy objects of the holy objects”">aj</a></sup>and they will bear their disgrace and the results of their detestable things they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:28–32; Numbers 18:4"> And I will appoint them as the keepers of the responsibility of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “toward”">ak</a></sup>the temple, for all of its work<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “tasks”">al</a></sup>and ⸤everything⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “all”">am</a></sup>which will be done in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Ezekiel 48:11; Ezekiel 44:7"> But the Levitical priests, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “sons”">an</a></sup>of Zadok, who cared for the responsibility of my sanctuary ⸤when the Israelites went astray⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “at going astray the children of Israel”">ao</a></sup>from me, they will approach me to serve me, and they will stand ⸤before me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “to the face of me”">ap</a></sup>to offer to me fat and blood,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">aq</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:22; Malachi 1:12; Numbers 18:7–8"> “They shall come to my sanctuary, and they shall approach my table to serve me, and they will observe my requirement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Exodus 28:43; Exodus 39:27–29"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “and it will happen”">ar</a></sup>⸤when they come⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “at their coming”">as</a></sup>to the gates of the inner courtyards, they shall put on their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">at</a></sup>inner linen garments and not wear wool garments ⸤when they serve⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “at their serving”">au</a></sup>in the gates of the inner courtyard and ⸤inside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “and toward”">av</a></sup>the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; 1 Corinthians 11:4–10; Isaiah 3:20"> Linen turbans shall be on their head and linen undergarments shall be on their waists; they shall not gird themselves with material causing perspiration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:20; Leviticus 6:27; Leviticus 6:10–11"> When they go out to the outer courtyard to the people, they must take off their garment in which they were serving, and they must place them ⸤in the holy chambers⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “in the chambers of the holiness”">aw</a></sup>and they must put on other garments, so that they will not make the people holy with their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:14; Numbers 6:5; Leviticus 21:5–24"> And their head they shall not shave, or long hair they shall not let grow; short they shall clip their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 10:9; Titus 1:7–8; 1 Timothy 5:23"> And no priest shall drink wine when they come into the inner courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Leviticus 21:13–14; 1 Timothy 3:2"> And a widow or divorced woman they shall not take for themselves as wives, ⸤but only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Literally “but if”">ax</a></sup>a virgin from the offspring of the house of Israel, or the widow who is a widow for a priest they may take.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Leviticus 10:10–11; 2 Timothy 2:24–25"> And they will teach my people the difference between what is holy and what is unholy, and the difference between unclean and clean they must show them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Ezekiel 22:26; 2 Chronicles 19:8–10"> And at a legal dispute they themselves shall stand to judge; employing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Or “with”">ay</a></sup>my judgments they shall judge it, and with respect to my laws and my statutes. All my festivals they shall keep and my Sabbaths they shall consecrate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:16; Leviticus 22:4; Matthew 8:21–22"> And ⸤near a dead person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Literally “and to a dead person of a man”">az</a></sup>he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="That is, the priest">ba</a></sup>shall not come to be defiled, ⸤but only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Literally “but if”">bb</a></sup>for a father and for a mother and for a son and for a daughter, for a brother and for a sister ⸤who was not married⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Literally “who not she was for a man”">bc</a></sup>may they defile themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13–14; Numbers 19:11–14; Numbers 6:10–21"> And, after his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="That is, the priest">bd</a></sup>cleansing, they shall count for him seven days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 6:9–11; Ezekiel 44:17; Leviticus 4:3–35"> and then on the day of his coming into the sanctuary to the inner courtyard to serve in the sanctuary, he shall offer his sin offering,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-be" id="fnref-be" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">be</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:33; Numbers 18:20; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> And thus it will be to them as regards inheritance, that I am their inheritance, and so you shall not give to them property in Israel; I am their property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 27:21; Leviticus 27:28"> The grain offering and the sin offering and the guilt offering, they themselves may eat them, and also all the consecrated possessions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="Hebrew “possession”">bf</a></sup>in Israel will be theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:35–37; 2 Chronicles 31:10; Deuteronomy 18:4"> And also what is first of all of the firstfruits of everything, and of all of the contribution of everything from all of your contributions, to the priest ⸤it belongs⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="Literally “it shall be”">bg</a></sup>and what is first of your dough you shall give to the priest, so that a blessing may rest on your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Exodus 22:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> Any dead body or mangled carcass from the birds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Hebrew “bird”">bh</a></sup>or from the animals,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bi" id="fnref-bi" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">bi</a></sup>the priests shall not eat.”</span>
</div>
