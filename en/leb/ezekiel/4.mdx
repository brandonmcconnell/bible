## Siege of Jerusalem Illustrated on a Brick

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 1:2–9; Amos 3:2; Ezekiel 12:3–16"> “Now,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">b</a></sup>take for yourself a brick, and you must put it ⸤before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to the face of you”">c</a></sup>and you must portray on it a city, Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:22; Jeremiah 6:6; Luke 19:42–44"> And you must build against it siege works, and you must build against it a bulwark, and you must heap against it a siege ramp, and you must set up against it camps and put against it a battering ram all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:6; Isaiah 8:18; Isaiah 20:3"> And take for yourself a plate of iron, and you must place it as a wall of iron between you and the city, and you must set your face against it, and it must be ⸤under siege⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in the siege”">d</a></sup>and you must lay the siege against it; it is a sign for the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 16:22; Leviticus 10:17; Numbers 18:1"> And you, lie down on your left side, and you must put<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “you will put”">e</a></sup>the guilt of the house of Israel on it. You will carry their guilt the number of days that you will lie on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Isaiah 53:6"> And I will give to you the years of their guilt according to the number of the days, three hundred and ninety days, and you must bear the guilt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “punishment”">f</a></sup>of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Daniel 12:11–12; Daniel 9:24–26"> When you have completed these, then you must lie a second time on your right side; and you must bear the guilt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “punishment”">g</a></sup>of the house of Judah forty days, a day for each year, a day for each year I give it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 6:2"> And toward the siege of Jerusalem you must set your face and your bared arm; then you must prophesy against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:25"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “and”">h</a></sup>look! I will put on you cords, and you may not turn yourself from one side to your other side until you complete the days of your siege.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ezekiel’s Strange Defiled Meal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Isaiah 28:25; Ezekiel 4:16"> “And you, take for yourself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “you”">i</a></sup>wheat and barley and beans and lentils and millet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “sorghum”">j</a></sup>and spelt, and you must put them in one vessel, and you must make them for yourself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “you”">k</a></sup>into a food during the number of days that you are lying on your side; three hundred and ninety days you shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 45:12; Deuteronomy 28:51–68"> And your food that you will eat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “will eat it”">l</a></sup>will be according to weight; twenty shekels for each day ⸤at fixed times⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “from time to time”">m</a></sup>you shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; John 3:34; Ezekiel 4:16"> And ⸤an amount of water⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “water by amount”">n</a></sup>you shall drink, a sixth of a hin; ⸤at fixed times⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “from time to time”">o</a></sup>you shall drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:12; Genesis 18:6"> And as a bread-cake of barley you shall eat it, and ⸤with human excrement⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “with human dung of the excrement of the human”">p</a></sup>you shall bake it before their eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 1:8; Hosea 9:3–4"> And Yahweh said, “Thus shall the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>eat their unclean food among the nations ⸤where I will scatter them⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “which I will scatter them there”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 10:14; Ezekiel 20:49; Ezekiel 9:8"> And I said, “Ah, Lord Yahweh! Look! I have not been defiling myself, and a dead body and mangled carcass I have not eaten from my childhood until now, and ⸤unclean meat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “flesh of unclean meat”">s</a></sup>has not come into my mouth!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he said to me, “See I will give you ⸤cattle manure⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “the manure of cattle”">t</a></sup>in the place of the feces of a human, and you may prepare your food on it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Isaiah 3:1; Leviticus 26:26"> And he said to me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">u</a></sup>look, I am going to break the ⸤supply⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “staff”">v</a></sup>of bread in Jerusalem, and they will eat bread by weight, ⸤anxiously⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “and with worry”">w</a></sup>and ⸤rationed water⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “and water by amount”">x</a></sup>and they will drink with horror,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Ezekiel 33:10"> so that they will lack food and water, and they will be appalled ⸤with one another⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “a man and his brother”">y</a></sup>and they will waste away because of their guilt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “punishment”">z</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
