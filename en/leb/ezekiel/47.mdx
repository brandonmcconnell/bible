## River of the Temple, Division of the Land and Its Boundaries

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Zechariah 14:8; Joel 3:18"> And he brought me back to the doorway of the temple and, look! There was water coming out from under the threshold of the temple eastward, because the face of the temple was eastwards; and the water was coming down from under the south side of the temple, from south of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:4; Ezekiel 44:2"> And he brought me out by the way of the north gate and he led me around by the ⸤way leading to the outside of the outer gate⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “way of the outside to the gate of the outside”">a</a></sup>by way of the gate facing east, and look, there was water trickling from the south side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Acts 10:45–46; Acts 11:16–18"> As the man went eastward a measuring line was in his hand, and he measured a thousand ⸤cubits⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in the cubit”; about 1,750 feet">b</a></sup>And he led me through on into the water; it was ⸤ankle deep⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “water of ankles”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 1:6; Romans 15:19; Acts 19:10–20"> And he measured a thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="About 1,750 feet">d</a></sup>and he brought me over into the waters and it was ⸤knee deep⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “water of knees”">e</a></sup>And he measured a thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="About 1,750 feet">f</a></sup>and he brought me over into the waters and it was ⸤waist deep⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “water of waist”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Habakkuk 2:14; Matthew 13:31–32"> And he measured a thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="About 1,750 feet">h</a></sup>and it became a stream that I was not able to cross, because the water rose, ⸤waters a person could swim in⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “water of swimming”">i</a></sup>and became a stream that could not be crossed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:5; Ezekiel 40:4; Zechariah 4:2"> And he asked me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">j</a></sup>did you see this?” And he made me go, and he brought me back along the bank of the stream.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:12; Revelation 22:2; 2 Kings 2:13"> ⸤When I returned⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “At my returning”">k</a></sup>then look! On the bank of the river were many trees ⸤on both sides⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “from this and from this”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Deuteronomy 3:17; 2 Kings 2:19–22"> And he said to me, “These waters are going out to the eastern region, and they go down to the ⸤Jordan Valley⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “Arabah”">m</a></sup>and they come to the sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Clearly refers to the Dead Sea">n</a></sup>and flow into the sea where ⸤they issue out⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “being brought out/are issued out”">o</a></sup>and the waters in the sea will be healed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “made fresh”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 6:63; John 4:14"> And then every living creature with which it teems,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “swarms”">q</a></sup>⸤to every place the waters come⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to all of which they come there the water”">r</a></sup>it will live; and very many fish will live because these waters came there, and so the water will be healed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “fresh”">s</a></sup>and everything will be alive ⸤everywhere that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “all that”">t</a></sup>⸤the stream will come⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “that it will come there the stream”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Psalm 104:25; Numbers 34:6"> And then fishers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “fishermen”">v</a></sup>from En Gedi and on up to En Eglaim will stand beside it, a sea-girt drying yard for nets; their fish for the dragnets will be ⸤of every kind⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “to the”">w</a></sup>like the fish of the great sea,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean">x</a></sup>and very many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Revelation 22:11; Jeremiah 17:6"> But its marshes and its swamps will not be cured, for they were given for salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 22:2; Psalm 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8"> And along the stream will go up on its banks ⸤from both sides⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “from this and from this”">y</a></sup>every tree ⸤producing food⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “of food”">z</a></sup>its leaf will not wither and it will not cease producing its fruit. ⸤Every month⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “for its months”">aa</a></sup>it will bear early fruit, for its waters are going out from the sanctuary, and its fruit will be as food, and its leaf for healing.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:4–6; Jeremiah 3:18; Numbers 34:2–12"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “This is the boundary by which you shall distribute the land for ⸤the twelve⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “two ten”">ab</a></sup>tribes of Israel: Two shares shall be for Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5–6; Genesis 12:7; Ezekiel 48:29"> And you must take possession of it, each one a share like his brother, of this land which ⸤I swore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “I raised my hand”">ac</a></sup>to give it to your fathers, and so this land shall fall to you as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 34:8; Ezekiel 48:1"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “Bow”">ad</a></sup>this is the boundary of the land: ⸤On the north side⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “to the side of the north”">ae</a></sup>from the great sea by the way of Hethlon ⸤until you come to Zedad⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “to the arriving at Zedad”">af</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:1; Numbers 13:21; 2 Samuel 8:8"> Hamath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “Lebo-Hamath to Zeded” (cf. NRSV, NJPS)">ag</a></sup>to Berothath to Sibraim, which is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “are”">ah</a></sup>between the boundary of Damascus and the boundary of Hamath, on to Hazer Hatticon, which is on the boundary of Hauran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:1; Numbers 34:9"> And so the boundary will be from the sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean">ai</a></sup>to Hazar Enan at the boundary of Damascus northwards and the boundary of Hamath to the north, and this is the boundary on the side of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Genesis 31:23; Genesis 31:47"> ⸤And the eastern boundary⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “and the side of the east”">aj</a></sup>will be between Hauran and Damascus, and from between Gilead and the land of Israel along the Jordan River from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “to”">ak</a></sup>the boundary on the eastern sea to Tamar. And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “Now”">al</a></sup>⸤this is the border on the east⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “and this is the side of the east”">am</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:28; Deuteronomy 32:51; Numbers 34:5"> ⸤And on the south side the border⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “and the side of the south to south”">an</a></sup>will run from Tamar up to the waters of Meribot Kadesh and along the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="That is, the Wadi of Egypt">ao</a></sup>to the Great Sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean">ap</a></sup>And this is ⸤the boundary to the south⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “and this is the side of south to the south”">aq</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Ezekiel 48:1"> ⸤And on the west side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “and the side of the west”">ar</a></sup>the Great Sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean">as</a></sup>shall be the boundary up to opposite Lebo-Hamath. ⸤This is the western boundary⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “this is the side of the west”">at</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> You shall divide this land for yourselves according to the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Acts 11:18"> And then you shall allocate it as an inheritance for yourselves and for the aliens dwelling as aliens in the midst of you who have children in the midst of you, and so they shall be to you just like full citizens among the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">au</a></sup>With you they shall be allotted with an inheritance in the midst of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And then in the tribe in which an alien who is with you dwells, there you shall give his inheritance,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">av</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
