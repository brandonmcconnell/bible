## Jerusalem a Useless Vine

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:2; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 24:8–10"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: ⸤Just like⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “As that”">j</a></sup>the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “wood”">k</a></sup>which I gave to the fire for fuel, so I have given the inhabitants of Jerusalem.</span>
