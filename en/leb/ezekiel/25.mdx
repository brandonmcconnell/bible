## Judgment upon Various Gentile Nations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was”">a</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to say”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:1–6; Amos 1:13–15; Ezekiel 6:2"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">c</a></sup>set your face toward the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">d</a></sup>and prophesy against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalm 70:2–3; Proverbs 17:5"> and you must say to the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">e</a></sup>‘Hear the word of the Lord Yahweh! Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Because of your saying, ‘Ah!’ to my sanctuary when it was profaned, and to the land of Israel when it was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “became”">f</a></sup>desolate, and to the house of Judah when they went into the exile,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:33; 1 Kings 4:30"> therefore look! I am giving you to the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “children”">g</a></sup>of the East as a possession, and they will set up their encampments in you, and they will make in you their dwelling places; ⸤they themselves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “they, they”">h</a></sup>will eat your fruit and ⸤they themselves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “they, they”">i</a></sup>will drink your milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; 2 Samuel 12:26; Isaiah 17:2"> And I will make Rabbah as a pasture of camels and the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">j</a></sup>as a haunt of flocks, and they will know that I am Yahweh.” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:8; Obadiah 1:12; Zephaniah 2:10"> For thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Because of clapping your hand and stamping with your foot, and because you rejoiced in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “to”">k</a></sup>yourself with all of your malice over the land of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:4; Ezekiel 25:16; Ezekiel 6:14"> therefore look! I stretched out my hand against you, and I will give you as plunder<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="According to the reading tradition (Qere )">l</a></sup>to the nations, and I will cut you off from the peoples, and I will destroy you from the countries, and I will wipe you out, and you will know that I am Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 15:1–9; Jeremiah 25:21; Jeremiah 48:1–47"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Because of Moab and Seir saying, ‘The house of Judah is like all of the nations,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:23; Joshua 13:17; Joshua 13:19–20"> therefore, look! I am opening the side of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “the flank”">m</a></sup>Moab from the cities on its frontier, the glory of the land: Beth Jeshimoth, Baal Meon, and Kiriathaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:32; Psalm 83:3–6; Ezekiel 25:4"> I will give it to the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “children”">n</a></sup>of the East in addition to the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">o</a></sup>as a possession, so that the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">p</a></sup>will not be remembered among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Ezekiel 30:19; Psalm 149:7"> And on Moab I will execute punishments, and they will know that I am Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7–22; Ezekiel 25:8; Psalm 137:7"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Because of the doings of Edom in avenging himself with vengeance for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “toward”">q</a></sup>the house of Judah, and so they became very guilty and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “since”">r</a></sup>they avenged themselves on them,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:8; Malachi 1:3–4; Jeremiah 25:23"> therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: “so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “and”">s</a></sup>I will stretch out my hand against Edom and I will cut off from it both human and animal, and I will make it a ruin from Teman and Dedan; they will fall by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:14; Genesis 27:29; Hebrews 10:30–31"> And I will exact my vengeance on Edom by the hand of my people Israel, and they will do in Edom according to my anger and according to my rage, and they will know my vengeance,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">t</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:6; Isaiah 14:29–31; Ezekiel 25:12"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Because of the ⸤acting⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “doing”">u</a></sup>of the Philistines in vengeance, so that they avenged themselves relentlessly with malice in themselves for destruction ⸤with everlasting hostility⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “with hostility of ongoing ages/eternity”">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:14; Zephaniah 2:4–15; Ezekiel 25:13"> therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘Look! I am stretching out my hand against the Philistines, and I will cut off the Kerethites, and I will destroy the remainder of ⸤the seacoast⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “the coast of the sea”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:11; Ezekiel 25:14; Ezekiel 5:15"> And I will execute on them great vengeance with punishments of rage, and they will know that I am Yahweh when I exact my vengeance on them!”</span>
</div>
</div>
