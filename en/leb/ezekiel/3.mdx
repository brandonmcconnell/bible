<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; Isaiah 33:19; Jonah 3:2–4"> For you are sent to the house of Israel, not to a people ⸤of obscure speech⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “obscure of lip”">e</a></sup>and ⸤of a difficult language⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “heavy/thick of tongue”">f</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Ezekiel 2:4; 1 Samuel 8:7"> And the house of Israel, they are not willing to listen to you, for they are not willing to listen to me, for all of the house of Israel is hard of forehead, and they are hard of heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:18; Acts 7:51–56; Micah 3:8"> But look, I have made<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “set”">j</a></sup>your face hard ⸤against⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to correspond to ”">k</a></sup>their faces and your forehead hard ⸤against⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to correspond to ”">l</a></sup>their forehead.</span>
