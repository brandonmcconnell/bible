<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 3:16; Ezekiel 11:24; Acts 7:56"> And it was in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, on the fifth day of the month, and I was in the midst of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “among”">a</a></sup>the exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">b</a></sup>by the ⸤Kebar River⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “river of Kebar”">c</a></sup>The heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 24:12–15; Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> On the fifth day of the month—it was the fifth year of the exile of the king Jehoiachin—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 3:22; 2 Kings 3:15"> the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “was”">d</a></sup>clearly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “certainly”">e</a></sup>to Ezekiel the son of Buzi, the priest, in the land of the Chaldeans at the ⸤Kebar River⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “river of Kebar”">f</a></sup>and the hand of Yahweh was on him there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:27; Ezekiel 8:2; Jeremiah 23:19"> And I looked,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “saw”">g</a></sup>and look! A storm wind was coming from the north, a great cloud, and fire flashing back and forth, and brightness around and ⸤within it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “for it”">h</a></sup>and from its midst<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “middle”">i</a></sup>it was like the outward appearance of amber stone from the midst<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “middle”">j</a></sup>of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:6–8; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 6:6"> And from its midst<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “middle”">k</a></sup>was the likeness of four living creatures, and this was their appearance: ⸤a human form⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “this their appearance the likeness of a human to them”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7–8; Ezekiel 1:15"> and ⸤each had four faces⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “and four faces to each”">m</a></sup>and ⸤each of them had four wings⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “and four wings to each for them”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:15; Daniel 10:6; Psalm 104:4"> And their legs were straight legs, and the sole of their feet was like the sole of the foot of a calf, and they were sparkling like the outward appearance of polished bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:21; Ezekiel 10:11; Isaiah 6:6"> And under their wings were ⸤human hands⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “hand his a man”">o</a></sup>on their four sides. And their faces and their wings for the four of them were as follows:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:11–12"> ⸤their wings were touching one another⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “their wings were touching each her sister”">p</a></sup>⸤each of them went straight forward⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “they did not turn at their going”">q</a></sup>⸤without turning right or left⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “each toward the side of his face they went”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:14; Revelation 4:7; 1 Corinthians 9:9–10"> The likeness of their faces was the face of a human in front, and the face of a lion on the right ⸤of each of them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “of the four of them”">s</a></sup>and the face of an ox on the left ⸤of each of them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “of the four of them”">t</a></sup>and the face of an eagle ⸤for each of them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “of the four of them”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 6:2; Ezekiel 1:23; Ezekiel 10:19"> So were their faces; their wings were spread out ⸤upward⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “from to above”">v</a></sup>each had two touching one another and two covering their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 10:22; Hebrews 1:14"> And each ⸤went straight forward⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “toward the side of his face they went”">w</a></sup>⸤wherever the spirit went⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “towards which it was there the spirit”">x</a></sup>they went, and ⸤they did not turn as they went⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “they did not turn at their going”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Psalm 104:4; Matthew 28:3"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “And”">z</a></sup>the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning coals of fire, like the appearance of torches. It<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="That is, the fire">aa</a></sup>was moving to and fro between the living creatures, and ⸤the fire was very bright⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “brightness was for the fire”">ab</a></sup>and lightning was going out from the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Zechariah 4:10; Mark 13:27"> And the living creatures ⸤were speeding to and fro⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “sped forth and returned”">ac</a></sup>like the appearance of lightning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:9; Ezekiel 10:9; Ezekiel 1:19–21"> And I saw the living creatures, and look! A wheel was on the earth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “ground”">ad</a></sup>beside ⸤each of the living creatures that had four faces⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “the living creatures for the four of his/its faces.” The LXX has “for the four of them.”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Ezekiel 10:9–11; Exodus 39:13"> The appearance of the wheels and their construction was like the appearance of beryl,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “chrysolite,” or “topaz”; this stone is difficult to identify exactly">af</a></sup>and ⸤they all looked alike⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “likeness was one for the four of them”">ag</a></sup>and their appearance and their construction was ⸤like a wheel within a wheel⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “as that it was a wheel in the midst of a wheel”">ah</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:9; Ezekiel 1:12; Isaiah 55:11"> When they moved, they went toward their four sides; they did not ⸤veer at all as they went⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “turn away when they went”">ai</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:12; Revelation 4:6; Revelation 4:8"> And their rims ⸤were high and awesome⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “height was for them and awesomeness was for them”">aj</a></sup>and ⸤all four of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “of the four of them”">ak</a></sup>their rims were full of eyes all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 10:19; Psalm 103:20"> And at the going of the living creatures, the wheels next to them also went, and ⸤when the creatures were lifted from the ground⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “and at the lifting up of the creatures from the earth”">al</a></sup>the wheels also rose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:12; Ezekiel 10:17; 1 Corinthians 14:32"> ⸤Wherever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “Toward which”">am</a></sup>the spirit went they would go there, and the wheels rose, for the spirit of the living creatures<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Hebrew “creature”">an</a></sup>was in the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:17; Ezekiel 1:19–20; Romans 8:2"> At their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="That is, the living creatures">ao</a></sup>going, they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="That is, the wheels">ap</a></sup>go, and at their standing, they stood, and at their being lifted up from on the earth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “ground”">aq</a></sup>the wheels rose,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “were lifted up”">ar</a></sup>for the spirit of the living creatures<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Hebrew “creature”">as</a></sup>was in the wheels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Ezekiel 1:26; Revelation 4:6"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “And”">at</a></sup>the likeness above the heads of the living creatures was an expanse like the outward appearance of ⸤awesome⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “terrible”">au</a></sup>⸤ice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “frozen water”">av</a></sup>spread out above their heads ⸤upward⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “from to upward”">aw</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 4:18; Ezekiel 1:11–12; Psalm 89:7"> And under the expanse their wings were stretched out straight ⸤one toward the other⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Literally “each towards its sister”">ax</a></sup>each had two wings covering them, and each had two wings covering their bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 1:15"> And I heard the sound of their wings like the sound of many waters, like the voice of Shaddai,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Often translated “Almighty”">ay</a></sup>and ⸤when they moved⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Literally “at their going”">az</a></sup>there was a sound of tumult like the sound of an army; ⸤when they stood still⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Literally “at their standing”">ba</a></sup>they lowered their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:22"> And there was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Or “it was”">bb</a></sup>a sound from above the expanse that was above their heads, ⸤and when they stood⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Literally “at their standing”">bc</a></sup>they lowered their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:1; Exodus 24:10; Revelation 1:13"> And from above the expanse that was above their heads there was the likeness of a throne, ⸤looking like a sapphire⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="Literally “the appearance of a stone of sapphire”">bd</a></sup>and above the likeness of the throne was a likeness similar to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-be" id="fnref-be" data-fn="Or “like”">be</a></sup>the appearance of a human on it, but ⸤above it⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="Literally “from to above”">bf</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Ezekiel 8:2; Hebrews 12:29"> And I saw something like the outward appearance of amber, something like the appearance of fire, with a covering around it, from the likeness of his loins and ⸤upward⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="Literally “to above”">bg</a></sup>And from the likeness of his loins and ⸤downward⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Literally “to down”">bh</a></sup>I saw something like the appearance of fire, and ⸤it was radiant all around⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bi" id="fnref-bi" data-fn="Literally “brightness was for it around”">bi</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 10:1; Revelation 4:3; Genesis 17:3"> Like the appearance<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bj" id="fnref-bj" data-fn="Or “likeness”">bj</a></sup>of a bow that is in the cloud on ⸤a rainy day⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bk" id="fnref-bk" data-fn="Literally “the day of the rain”">bk</a></sup>such was the radiance<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bl" id="fnref-bl" data-fn="Or “brightness”">bl</a></sup>around it; thus was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of Yahweh. And I saw, and I fell on my face, and I heard a voice speaking.</span>
