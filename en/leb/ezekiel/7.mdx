## Punishment for Abominations Throughout the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was”">a</a></sup>to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 8:2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:3"> “And you, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">b</a></sup>thus says the Lord Yahweh to the land of Israel: ‘The end comes, the end on the four corners of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 33:20"> Now the end is on you, and I will send my anger on you, and I will judge you according to your ways, and I will bring on you all your detestable things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 11:21; Ezekiel 5:11"> And my eye will not take pity on you, and I will not show compassion for your ways; on you I will bring your detestable things; they will be in the midst of you, and you will know that I am Yahweh.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Kings 21:12–13; Ezekiel 5:9"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘Look! Disaster after disaster is coming!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Ezekiel 7:3; 2 Peter 2:5"> The end comes, comes the end! It has awakened against you! Look! It comes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 7:12; Isaiah 22:5"> Doom is coming against you, the dweller<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “inhabitant”">c</a></sup>of the land; the time comes, the day is near, panic and not joy is on the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:8; Ezekiel 14:19; Ezekiel 20:8"> ⸤Soon⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “now from near”">d</a></sup>I will pour out my rage on you, and I will fully vent my anger on you, and I will judge you according to your ways, and I will bring on you all of your detestable things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:13; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 9:13"> And my eye will not take pity, and I will not show compassion. According to your ways I will ⸤deal with you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “on you I will bring”">e</a></sup>and your detestable things will be in the midst of you, and you will know that I am Yahweh who strikes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “smites”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:5; James 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Look! The day is coming; doom goes out, the staff blossoms, pride sprouts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Ezekiel 7:23; Isaiah 59:6–8"> Violence has grown to become a staff of wickedness; ⸤none⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “not any ”">g</a></sup>from them will remain, and ⸤none⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “not any ”">h</a></sup>from their abundance nor from their wealth;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “noise,” or “multitude”; the same word appears in each of the next three verses">i</a></sup>and prominence will not be among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13–14; 1 Corinthians 7:29–31; Ezekiel 7:5–7"> The time has come, the day has arrived; let not the buyer rejoice, and let the seller not mourn, for anger is on all their multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:24–28; Leviticus 25:31; Job 15:25"> For the seller will not return to the merchandise ⸤while they are still alive⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “while still is in the life, their life”">j</a></sup>for the vision is about all of its multitude; it will not change, and a man because of his guilt will not be able to hold onto his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 51:27"> They shall blow on the trumpet and prepare ⸤everything⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the all”">k</a></sup>but there is no one going to the battle, for my anger is on all of their multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Ezekiel 5:12; Lamentations 1:20"> The sword ⸤is outside⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “is in the outside place”">l</a></sup>and the plague and the famine ⸤are inside⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “are from the house">m</a></sup>the one who is in the field will die by the sword, and the one who is in the city, famine and plague will devour him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Isaiah 38:14; Ezra 9:15"> And if their survivors will escape, they will be on the mountains, like the doves of the valley, all of them groaning, each because of his guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Hebrews 12:12; Jeremiah 6:24"> All of the hands will hang limp, and all of ⸤the knees will be wet with urine⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the knees will flow with water”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Isaiah 15:2–3; Isaiah 3:24"> And they will wear sackcloth, and horror will cover them, and on all of the faces will be shame, and baldness on all of their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Zephaniah 1:18; Luke 12:19–20"> Their silver they will discard on the streets, and filth will be their gold; their silver and their gold will not be able to rescue them on the day of the wrath of Yahweh. They will not satisfy their ⸤hunger⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “selves,” or “desire”">o</a></sup>and their stomachs they will not fill, for their guilt will be their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">p</a></sup>stumbling block.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judgment for the Profanation of the Temple and Sanctuary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:15–16"> “ ‘And the beauty of its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “their”">q</a></sup>ornament ⸤he made into a prideful thing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to a prideful thing he made it”">r</a></sup>and made with it their detestable things and their vile idols; therefore I will make it into an impure thing for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Psalm 74:2–8; 2 Chronicles 36:18–19"> And I will give it into the hand of strangers as plunder and to the wicked people of the earth as spoil, and they will defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Psalm 74:18–23; Psalm 74:10–11"> And I will turn my face from them, and they will defile my treasured place, and violent ones will enter and defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:9; Jeremiah 27:2; Ezekiel 11:6"> Make a chain for the land; it is full of ⸤bloody crimes⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “the judgment of blood”">s</a></sup>and the city is full of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Psalm 106:41; Ezekiel 21:31"> And I will bring the worst of the nations, and they will take possession of their houses, and I will put to an end the pride of the mighty ones, and their sanctuaries will be defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15–16; Isaiah 57:21; Lamentations 4:17–18"> Anguish comes, and they will seek peace, and there will be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Ezekiel 14:1; Micah 3:6"> Calamity upon calamity will come, and rumor will be upon rumor. And they will seek a vision from a prophet, but instruction will perish from the priest and counsel from the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 7:4–8; Ezekiel 26:16"> The king will mourn, and the prince will be dressed with despair, and the hands of the people of the land will tremble. According to their way I will do to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “deal with”">t</a></sup>them, and according to their judgments I will judge them, and they will know that I am Yahweh.’ ”</span>
</div>
