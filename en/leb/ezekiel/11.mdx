## Judgment On Evil Rulers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 11:13"> And the Spirit lifted me up, and it brought me to the eastern gate, the one facing east, of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “house”">a</a></sup>of Yahweh. And look, there were twenty-five men in the doorway of the gate, and I saw Jaazaniah the son of Azzur in the midst of them, and Pelatiah the son of Benaiah, the commanders of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Jeremiah 5:5; Psalm 2:1–2"> And he said to me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">b</a></sup>these are the men ⸤who devise mischief⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the ones devising mischief”">c</a></sup>and who are offering bad counsel in this city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:27; Ezekiel 12:22; Ezekiel 11:7–11"> who are saying, “The building of houses is not near; the city is the pot, and we are the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46–47; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 3:2–15"> Therefore prophesy against them! Prophesy, son of man!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 16:17; Revelation 2:23"> And the Spirit of Yahweh fell on me, and he said to me, “Say, ‘thus says Yahweh: “This is what you think, house of Israel, and I myself know them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">e</a></sup>the thoughts of your spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 22:27; Ezekiel 22:12"> You made your slain ones numerous in this city, and you have filled its streets with slain ones.” ’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “slain one”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:3–13; Micah 3:2–3; 2 Kings 25:18–22"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Your slain ones whom you put in its midst, they are the flesh, and it is the pot, and I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">g</a></sup>will bring you out from its midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:24; Isaiah 24:17–18; Job 3:25"> The sword you have feared, and the sword I will bring against you!” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">h</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Psalm 106:41; Deuteronomy 28:36"> “And I will bring you out from its midst, and I will give you into the hand of strangers, and I will execute judgments against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 52:9–10; 2 Kings 25:19–21"> By the sword you will fall at the border of Israel; I will judge you, and you will know that I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7–10"> It will not be as a pot to you, and so you would be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “will be”">i</a></sup>in the midst of it as flesh, for at the border of Israel I will judge you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 8:14"> And you will know that I am Yahweh, whose<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “which”">j</a></sup>rules you did not follow, and whose<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “my”">k</a></sup>regulations you did not do, but according to the regulations of the nations that are around you, you acted.” ’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “did”">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 9:8; Acts 5:5"> And it happened that ⸤as I was prophesying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “at my prophesying”">m</a></sup>Pelatiahu the son of Benaiahu died! And I fell on my face, and I cried with a loud voice, and I said, “Ah, Lord Yahweh, you are making a complete destruction of the remnant of Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “was”">n</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “to say”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Isaiah 65:5; John 16:2"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">p</a></sup>your brothers, your brothers, the men of your redemption, and all of the house of Israel, all of it, ⸤who said concerning the inhabitants of Jerusalem⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “which they said concerning them the inhabitants of Jerusalem”">q</a></sup>‘They are far from Yahweh, therefore to us this land was given as a possession.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Psalm 90:1"> Therefore say, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Though I have removed them far away among the nations and though I have scattered them among the countries, yet I was a sanctuary to them for a little while in the countries ⸤to which they have gone⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “which they came there”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Jeremiah 3:18; Ezekiel 34:13"> Therefore say, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “And I will assemble you from the peoples, and I will gather you from the countries to which you were scattered among them, and I will give the land of Israel to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 42:7–8"> And when they come there, then they will remove all of its vile idols and all of its detestable things from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 31:33; Psalm 51:10"> And I will give to them one heart, and a new spirit I will give<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “put”">s</a></sup>in their inner parts. And I will remove their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">t</a></sup>heart of stone from their body, and I will give to them a heart of flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:11; Psalm 105:45; Jeremiah 30:22"> so that they may walk in my statutes, and they will keep my regulations, and they will do them, and they will be to me a people, and I myself will be to them as God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Jeremiah 1:16"> But to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “and to”">u</a></sup>the heart of their abominations and the detestable things their heart is going. I will bring their way on their head,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">v</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:19; Ezekiel 1:19–20"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “And”">w</a></sup>the cherubim lifted up their wings, and their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “the”">x</a></sup>wheels were beside them, and the glory of the God of Israel ⸤was over them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “from to above”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 14:4; Ezekiel 8:4; Ezekiel 10:4"> And the glory of Yahweh went up from the midst of the city, and it stood still on the mountain that is to the east of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:12; Ezekiel 8:3; Ezekiel 11:1"> And the Spirit lifted me up, and it brought me to Chaldea, to the exiles,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">z</a></sup>in the vision by the spirit of God; and the vision that I had seen ⸤left me⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “went up from being upon me”">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:4; Ezekiel 3:27; Ezekiel 3:17"> And I spoke to the exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">ab</a></sup>all of the words of Yahweh that he had shown me.</span>
</div>
