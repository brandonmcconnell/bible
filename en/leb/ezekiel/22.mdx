The princes of Israel, each one according to his strength,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “influence”">h</a></sup>they are in you ⸤for the shedding of blood⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to shedding of blood”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Exodus 22:21–22; Leviticus 20:9"> They have treated father and mother with contempt in you; ⸤they violated⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “they did with extortion”">j</a></sup>the alien with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “by”">k</a></sup>extortion; in the midst of you they mistreated the orphan and widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:38–39; Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 20:13"> You despised my holy objects; my Sabbaths you profaned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 4:14; Leviticus 19:16; Hosea 4:10"> Slanderous men are ⸤among you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “in you”">l</a></sup>to shed blood, and they, ⸤among you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “in you”">m</a></sup>eat upon the mountains; they do wickedness in the midst of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 18:19; Leviticus 18:7–8; Ezekiel 18:6"> They uncover the nakedness of a father among<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “in”">n</a></sup>you; they violate a woman unclean of menstruation among<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “in”">o</a></sup>you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; 2 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:17"> And a man does a detestable thing with the wife of his neighbor, and a man defiles his daughter-in-law in wickedness, and a man sexually violates among<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “in”">p</a></sup>you his sister, the daughter of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:25; Ezekiel 23:35; Psalm 106:21"> They take a bribe among<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “in”">q</a></sup>you in order to shed blood; and you take usury, and you make gain from your neighbors by extortion, and so you have forgotten me, ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">r</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:17; Isaiah 33:15; Amos 2:6–8"> And look! I strike my hand for your ill-gotten gain that you have made and at your blood that was in the midst of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Ezekiel 17:24; Ezekiel 24:14"> Can your heart endure,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “hold up”">s</a></sup>or can your hands be strong at the days in which I am dealing with you? I, Yahweh, I have spoken, and I will act!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:8; Ezekiel 22:22; Deuteronomy 4:27"> I will scatter you among the nations, and I will disperse you through the countries, and I will purge your uncleanness from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Psalm 83:18; Ezekiel 7:24"> And I will be profaned by you before the eyes of the nations, and you will know that I am Yahweh.” ’ ”</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Ezekiel 24:3–6; Micah 4:12"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘Because all of you have become as silver dross, therefore look! I am gathering you to the midst of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:11–12; Isaiah 54:16; Ezekiel 24:13"> like the gathering of silver and bronze and iron and lead and tin to the middle of a furnace to blow fire on it for melting. Thus I will gather in my anger and in my rage, and I will ⸤deposit⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “I will put/place”">w</a></sup>you, and I will melt you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:7; Nahum 1:6; Psalm 50:3"> And I will gather you, and I will blow on you with the fire of my wrath, and so I will melt you in the midst of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 20:33; Hosea 5:10"> Thus you will be melted like the melting of silver in the midst of a furnace, and you will know that I, Yahweh, I have poured out my rage on you.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “was”">x</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to say”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14–16; Jeremiah 2:30; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">z</a></sup>say to her, ‘You are a land not cleansed; it is not rained upon in the day of indignation.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 2:34; Ezekiel 13:19"> The conspiracy of its prophets in the midst of her is like a roaring lion that is tearing prey. They devour people, and they take wealth and treasure; they make its widows numerous in the midst of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Ezekiel 22:8"> Its priests treat my law violently, and they profane my holy objects; they do not distinguish between a holy object and what is unholy, or between the clean and the unclean. They do not teach the difference, and they hide their eyes from my Sabbaths, and so I am profaned in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:13; Isaiah 1:23; Ezekiel 22:6"> Its officials are like wolves tearing prey in its midst, to pour out blood, to destroy people, to make dishonest gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Jeremiah 23:25–32"> And for them its prophets plaster whitewash; they are seeing falseness and are practicing divination for them by lying, saying, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh,’ and Yahweh has not spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:7; Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21"> They severely oppress the people of the land, and ⸤they committed robbery⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “and they seized a robbed things ”">aa</a></sup>and they mistreated the needy and the poor, and they oppressed the alien ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “with not”">ab</a></sup>justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:16; Jeremiah 5:1; Psalm 106:23"> And so I sought for them somebody, one repairing the wall and standing in the breach ⸤before me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “to the face of me”">ac</a></sup>on behalf of the land not to destroy it, but I did not find anyone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:43; Ezekiel 7:8–9; Ezekiel 9:10"> and so I poured out my indignation on them. With the fire of my wrath I destroyed them; I returned their way upon their head,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">ad</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
