<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:19; Ezekiel 23:3; Exodus 32:4"> And her whorings from the time of Egypt she did not abandon, for they slept with her in her childhood, and they caressed the bosoms of her virginity, and they poured out their fornication on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 11:5; 2 Kings 15:29; 2 Kings 17:23"> Therefore I gave her into the hand of her lovers, into the hand of the ⸤Assyrians⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons of Assyria”">j</a></sup>⸤after whom she lusted⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “which she lusted for them”">k</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 12:1–2; 2 Kings 17:18–19; Ezekiel 23:31"> And I saw that she was defiled; ⸤they had both taken the same path⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “way one was for the two of them”">s</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:28; Ezekiel 23:22; Genesis 10:10"> And so the ⸤Babylonians⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “sons of Babylon”">aa</a></sup>came to her for the bed of lovemaking; and they defiled her with their fornication, and she was defiled by them, and she turned from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 78:59; Psalm 106:40; Jeremiah 12:8"> And she revealed her whorings, and she revealed her nakedness, and so I turned from her ⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “as that”">ab</a></sup>I turned from her sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:3; Ezekiel 20:7; Ezekiel 16:51"> Yet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “And”">ac</a></sup>she increased her whorings, ⸤recalling⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “remembering”">ad</a></sup>the days of her childhood when she was prostituted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “prostituted herself ”">ae</a></sup>in the land of Egypt.</span>
