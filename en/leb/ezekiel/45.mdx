## Instructions about Divisions of the Renewed Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:29; Zechariah 14:20–21; Numbers 34:13"> And when you allocate the land as an inheritance, you shall provide a contribution for Yahweh as a holy portion from the land, its length being twenty-five thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or 8.33 miles">a</a></sup>and its width ten thousand cubits;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew 3.5 miles; LXX reads 6.67 miles in width, or 20,000 cubits (cf. 45:3, 5; 48:9)">b</a></sup>it is holy in all its territory, all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:16–20; Ezekiel 27:28"> And there shall be from this area five hundred cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, “875 feet by 875 feet”">c</a></sup>by five hundred cubits, squared all around, for the sanctuary; and fifty cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “cubit”">d</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, “87.5 feet”">e</a></sup>of open space shall be for it all around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:10"> And from this measured area you shall measure a length of twenty-five<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="According to the reading tradition (Qere )">f</a></sup>thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, 8.33 miles; cf. 45:5">g</a></sup>and a width of ten thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, 3.5 miles; cf. 45:5">h</a></sup>and in it will be the sanctuary, ⸤the most holy place⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the holiness of holinesses”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:19; Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 40:45"> It<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “this area”">j</a></sup>is a holy portion from the land; it will be for the priests, the servants of the sanctuary ⸤who approach⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the ones who approach”">k</a></sup>to serve Yahweh, and it will be for them a place for houses and a holy place for the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:13; Nehemiah 10:38–39; Ezekiel 48:20"> And an area twenty-five thousand cubits in length and ten thousand cubits in width will be for the Levites, the servants of the temple. It will serve as property for them ⸤as cities to dwell in⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “twenty chambers”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:15–18; Ezekiel 48:30–35"> And as property of the city, you must set apart alongside the contribution of the sanctuary a portion five thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="That is, 1.67 miles">m</a></sup>in width and twenty-five thousand cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="That is, 8.33 miles">n</a></sup>in length, and it shall be for the entire house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:21; Ezekiel 34:24; Ezekiel 46:16–18"> And a portion will be for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “to”">o</a></sup>the prince ⸤on both sides⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “from this and from this”">p</a></sup>of ⸤the holy district⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the contribution of the holiness”">q</a></sup>and both sides of the property of the city, and ⸤alongside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to the front of”">r</a></sup>the property of the city ⸤on the west⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “from/on the side of the sea”">s</a></sup>and alongside the property ⸤on the east⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “from/on the side of east”">t</a></sup>And also its length<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “portion”">u</a></sup>⸤corresponds to one of the tribal portions to the west⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “corresponding to one of the portions from the boundary of the sea”">v</a></sup>running ⸤to the eastern border⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “to the boundary of the east”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–5; Ezekiel 46:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> This shall be to him ⸤with respect to the land⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “to the land”">x</a></sup>as property in Israel; and so my princes shall not again oppress my people, but they shall give the land to the house of Israel according to their tribes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Jeremiah 22:3; Ezekiel 44:6"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh, “Enough of this for you, the princes of Israel; put away violence and destruction and do justice and righteousness; revoke your acts of dispossession from upon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “against”">y</a></sup>my people!” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">z</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:4–6; Proverbs 16:11"> There shall be for you ⸤an honest set of scales⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “a set of scales of justice/honesty”">aa</a></sup>and ⸤an honest ephah⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “an ephah of honesty”">ab</a></sup>and ⸤an honest bath⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “a bath of honesty”; a bath is a liquid measure">ac</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:10"> The ephah and the bath shall be one unit of measurement;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “shall be the same size”">ad</a></sup>the tenth part of the homer is the bath, and the tenth of the homer is the ephah; so the homer shall be its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “the”">ae</a></sup>unit of measurement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> And the shekel shall weigh twenty gerahs, twenty shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Hebrew “shekel”">af</a></sup>and five and twenty shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Hebrew “shekel”">ag</a></sup>and ten and five shekels,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Hebrew “shekel”">ah</a></sup>that shall make the mina for you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Meaning of Hebrew uncertain">ai</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> This is the contribution offering which you shall present: a sixth of the ephah from a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “each”; literally “the”">aj</a></sup>homer of wheat, and a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “the”">ak</a></sup>sixth of the ephah from a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “the”">al</a></sup>homer of barley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the quota of the olive oil, the bath of the olive oil, is the tenth part of a bath from a kor, which is ten baths, or a homer—for ten baths are equal to a homer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 6:30; Colossians 1:21"> And one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “a”">am</a></sup>sheep from the flock from among two hundred from the pastures of Israel will be taken as a grain offering and as a burnt offering and as a fellowship offering to make atonement for them,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">an</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:14–15; Isaiah 16:1"> “All of the people of the land shall join in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “participate/ take part in”">ao</a></sup>to this contribution with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or possibly “for/to”">ap</a></sup>the prince in Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–12; 2 Chronicles 31:3; Isaiah 66:23"> “But on the prince shall be the responsibility for the burnt offerings, and the grain offering, and the libation at the feasts, and at the New Moon festivals, and at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “on”">aq</a></sup>the Sabbaths at all of the assemblies of the house of Israel; he shall provide the sin offering, and the grain offering, and the burnt offering, and the fellowship offering to make atonement for the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 16:16; Exodus 12:2; Ezekiel 43:22"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘On the first month on the first day of the month, you shall take a bull, ⸤a calf⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “son of cattle,” or “son of the herd”">ar</a></sup>without defect, and you shall purify the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:20; Ezekiel 43:14; Leviticus 16:18–20"> And the priest shall take from the blood of the sin offering, and he shall put it on the doorframe of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Or “house”">as</a></sup>and on the four corners of the ledge of the altar and on the doorframe of the gate of the inner courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 19:12; Leviticus 4:27–35; Ezekiel 45:15"> And so you shall do on the seventh day in the month for anyone doing wrong inadvertently or due to ignorance, and so you must make atonement for the temple.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “house”">at</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5–8; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Numbers 9:2–14"> In the first month, on the ⸤fourteenth⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “four ten”">au</a></sup>day of the month, you shall have the Passover, a feast lasting for ⸤seven days⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “a week of days”">av</a></sup>when you shall eat unleavened breads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Matthew 20:28"> And the prince shall provide on that day for himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">aw</a></sup>and for all of the people of the land a bull as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 42:8; Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 23:1–2"> And during the seven days of the religious feast he shall provide as a burnt offering to Yahweh seven bulls and seven rams without defect for each of the seven days, and as a sin offering a he-goat for each day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 28:12–15"> And as a grain offering, an ephah for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Hebrew “to”">ax</a></sup>the bull and an ephah for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Hebrew “to”">ay</a></sup>the ram he must provide, and a hin of olive oil for ⸤each⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Literally “the”">az</a></sup>ephah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:33–43; Numbers 29:12–38; 2 Chronicles 5:3"> In the seventh month, on the ⸤fifteenth⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Literally “five ten”">ba</a></sup>day of the month, during the feast, he shall provide just as he has for these seven days, as he did for the sin offering, as he did for the burnt offering, and as he did for the grain offering, and as he did for the olive oil.’ ”</span>
</div>
