<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 7:20; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 21:5"> “And you, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">a</a></sup>take for yourself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “you”">b</a></sup>a sword, sharp as ⸤a barber’s razor⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the razor of the barbers”">c</a></sup>Take it for yourself, and you must cause it to pass over your head and over your beard, and you must take for yourself a set of scales for weighing, and you must divide them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the hairs">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 4:1–8"> A third you must burn with fire in the midst<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “middle”">e</a></sup>of the city at ⸤the completion⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the completing”">f</a></sup>of the days of the siege, and you must take a third, and you must strike it with the sword around it, and a third you must scatter to the wind, and I will draw a sword behind them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:16; Jeremiah 40:6; Jeremiah 39:10"> And you must take from these a few in number, and you must tuck them in your hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Jeremiah 41:1–18; 2 Kings 25:25"> And from them again you shall take some, and you must throw them in the middle of the fire, and you must burn them with fire; from it a fire will go out to all of the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1; Deuteronomy 4:6; Jeremiah 6:6"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: This is Jerusalem in the midst of the nations where I have put her, and countries are around her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:10; Ezekiel 16:47–48; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> But she has rebelled against my regulations to the point of wickedness more than the nations, and my statutes more than the countries that are around her; for they rejected my regulations, and as for my statutes, they did not walk in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:10–11; 2 Chronicles 33:9; 2 Kings 21:9–11"> Therefore, thus says the Lord Yahweh: Because of your commotion more than the nations that are around you—you did not walk in my statutes, and you did not do my regulations, and according to the regulations of the nations that are around you, you did not do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:7; Jeremiah 24:9; Ezekiel 21:3"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: Look! I, ⸤even I⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “surely I”">g</a></sup>am against you, and I will execute judgment in the midst of you before the eyes of the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:21; Daniel 9:12; Amos 3:2"> and I will do with you that which I have not done, and which I will not do again, because of all of your detestable things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 12:14"> Therefore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to thus”">h</a></sup>parents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “fathers”">i</a></sup>will eat children<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “sons”">j</a></sup>in the midst of you, and children<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “sons”">k</a></sup>will eat their parents,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>and I will execute judgment in you, and I will scatter your entire remnant to every wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:20; Ezekiel 7:9; Ezekiel 11:18"> Therefore ⸤as surely as I live⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “alive I am ”">m</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">n</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, ⸤Surely⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “If not”">o</a></sup>because you have defiled my sanctuary with all of your vile idols and with all of your detestable things, ⸤now indeed I⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and also I”">p</a></sup>will reduce, and my eye will not take pity, and surely I will show no compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:2; Jeremiah 15:2; Jeremiah 43:10–11"> A third of you will die because of the plague, and because of the famine they will perish in the midst of you, and a third will fall through the sword around you, and a third I will scatter to every direction of the wind, and I will draw the sword behind them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:24; Ezekiel 36:5–6; Ezekiel 21:17"> And my anger will come to an end, and I will place my rage on them, and I will relent, and they will know that I, Yahweh, have spoken in my passion ⸤when I fully vent my rage against them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “at my finishing my anger against them”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:4; Nehemiah 2:17; Psalm 79:1–4"> And I will make you into a desolate place and into a disgrace among the nations that surround you before the eyes of ⸤every one who passes by⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “every one of passing by”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:17; Jeremiah 22:8–9; 1 Kings 9:7"> And it will be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “and you will be”">s</a></sup>an object of taunting<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “taunt”">t</a></sup>and an object of mockery, a warning and a horror to the nations that are around you whenever I execute judgments against you in anger and in wrath and in furious punishments! I, Yahweh, have spoken!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:16; Deuteronomy 32:23–24; Ezekiel 14:13"> When I send my arrows of deadly famine against them, which will be as destruction that I will send in order to destroy you, I will increase famine against you, and I will break ⸤the supply of food⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “staff of bread”">u</a></sup>for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 14:21; Leviticus 26:22"> And I will send against you famine and fierce animals,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">v</a></sup>and they will make you childless; and plague and blood will pass through you, and I will bring the sword upon you. I, Yahweh, I have spoken!”</span>
