## Description of Rooms for the Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:20; Ezekiel 40:17; Ezekiel 42:13"> And he brought me out to the outer courtyard ⸤to the north⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to the way of the north”">a</a></sup>and he brought me to the chamber which was opposite the courtyard and which is opposite the building to the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:13"> As to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “along the face of”">b</a></sup>the face of the length of the building with the doorway to the north, it was a hundred cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, 175 feet">c</a></sup>and its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “the”">d</a></sup>width was fifty cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, 87.5 feet">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:15–16; Ezekiel 41:10; Song of Solomon 7:5"> Opposite the twenty cubits ⸤of the inner courtyard⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “which were to the courtyard the inner”">f</a></sup>and opposite the pavement that was to the outer courtyard was a gallery ⸤facing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “toward in the face/front of”">g</a></sup>a gallery in the three stories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Luke 13:24; Matthew 7:14"> And ⸤in front of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">h</a></sup>the chambers was a passageway ten cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, 17.5 feet">i</a></sup>in width toward the inside, a walkway of one cubit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="One cubit of 18–21 inches is problematic; LXX and Syriac read 100 cubits (175 feet) running the length of the building">j</a></sup>and their doorways were to the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:7"> And the upper chambers narrowed, for the galleries took away space from them more than they took from the lower levels and more than they took from the middle level in the building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6; 1 Kings 6:8"> For they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “there”">k</a></sup>were three stories and ⸤they had no pillars⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “there were not for them pillars”">l</a></sup>like<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “as”">m</a></sup>the pillars of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “for”">n</a></sup>the courtyards; therefore they were smaller than the lower stories and than the middle stories from the ground up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:12; Ezekiel 42:10"> And there was a wall that was to the outside ⸤alongside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to the outside to corresponding”">o</a></sup>the chambers on the walkway to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “of”">p</a></sup>the outer courtyard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="That is, “to the north”">q</a></sup>⸤in front of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to in face of”">r</a></sup>the chambers; its length was fifty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="That is, 87.5 feet">s</a></sup>cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “cubit”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:13–14"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “while”">u</a></sup>the length of the chambers which were to the outer courtyard was fifty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “cubit”">v</a></sup>cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="That is, 87.5 feet">w</a></sup>and look!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “yet!”">x</a></sup>The chambers on the front of the temple were a hundred<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “cubit”">y</a></sup>cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="That is, 175 feet">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:19; Ezekiel 44:5"> And from under these chambers was the entrance<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Reading the Ketiv">aa</a></sup>from the east for them ⸤when one enters⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “at/in his/its coming”">ab</a></sup>from the outer courtyard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:1; Ezekiel 42:13; Ezekiel 41:12–15"> All along the width of the wall of the courtyard ⸤eastward⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “on the way of the east”">ac</a></sup>in front of the courtyard to the front of the building were chambers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:4"> And a walkway was ⸤before them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “was to the face of them”">ad</a></sup>like the appearance of the chambers which were on ⸤the north⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “were on the way of the north”">ae</a></sup>just like them in their length, and so was their width and all their exits, and they were built like their arrangements and like their doorways,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and like the doorways of the chambers which were on the way of the south was a doorway at the head of the way<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Dropping one “way”">af</a></sup>⸤before⸥ the stone wall, projecting<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “protecting wall”">ag</a></sup>on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “toward”">ah</a></sup>the way of the east at their coming.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="That is, when people entered the complex">ai</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:17; Leviticus 7:6; Ezekiel 40:46"> And he said to me, “The chambers of the north and the chambers of the south which are ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “to the front of”">aj</a></sup>the courtyard, they ⸤are the holy chambers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “are the chambers of the holiness”">ak</a></sup>in which the priests, who are near to Yahweh, will eat the ⸤most holy objects⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “holy objects of the holy objects”">al</a></sup>There they shall put the ⸤most holy objects⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “holy objects of the holy objects”">am</a></sup>and the grain offering and the sin offering and the guilt offering, for the place is holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:19; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:4–9"> ⸤When the priests enter⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “at coming their”">an</a></sup>then they shall not go out from the sanctuary to the outer courtyard; and there they must put<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “deposit/leave/remove”">ao</a></sup>their garments in which they serve because they are holy. They must put on other garments and then they may approach the area that is for the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:1; Ezekiel 41:15; Ezekiel 40:6–16"> And he completed the measurements of the inner temple, and he brought me to the walkway of the gate ⸤that faces toward the east⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “which its face is to the way of the east”">ap</a></sup>and he measured it ⸤all the way around⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “all around, all around”">aq</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Revelation 11:1–2; Zechariah 2:1"> He measured the east side with the reed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “rod”">ar</a></sup>for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “of measuring”">as</a></sup>measuring, five hundred cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Hebrew has “reeds” here (= 5,250 feet), which is not correct; 500 cubits = 875 feet.">at</a></sup>with respect to reeds with the reed for measurement, he measured it all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He measured the north side as five hundred cubits, with respect to reeds with the reed for measurement all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then he measured the south side as five hundred cubits, with respect to reeds with the reed for measurement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He went around the west side<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Or “side toward/to the sea”">au</a></sup>and he measured five hundred cubits, with respect to reeds with the reed for measurement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:2; Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 40:5"> ⸤Toward the four sides⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “toward the four winds”">av</a></sup>he measured it; there was a wall for it ⸤all the way around⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “all around, all around”">aw</a></sup>Its length was five hundred cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="That is, 875 feet">ax</a></sup>and its width was five hundred cubits, in order to make a separation between what is holy and what is common.</span>
</div>
