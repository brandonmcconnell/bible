## Condemnation of Israel’s Idolatrous Elders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1; 2 Kings 6:32"> And men from the elders of Israel came to me, and they sat ⸤before me⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to the face of me”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 3:7; 1 Kings 14:4"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “was”">b</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to say”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:7; Ezekiel 7:19; Ezekiel 20:3"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">d</a></sup>these men took up their idols into their heart and ⸤they placed before themselves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “they gave before their faces”">e</a></sup>a stumbling block of their guilt.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “iniquity”">f</a></sup>⸤Should I really let myself be consulted by them⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “Surely I will let me be consulted by them?”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:4; Ezekiel 14:7; Isaiah 3:11"> Therefore speak with them, and you must say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Every person from the house of Israel who brings up his idols into his heart and ⸤places before himself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “they gave before their faces”">h</a></sup>a stumbling block of his guilt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “iniquity”">i</a></sup>and yet he comes to the prophet, I Yahweh, I will respond to him through this act<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">j</a></sup>with respect to the multitude<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="According to the Kethib ; the Qere reads “I will respond to him, he who comes with the multitude of his idols”">k</a></sup>of his idols,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Zechariah 11:8; Romans 1:28"> so that I may take hold of the house of Israel by their heart, those who are estranged from me, all of them, through their idols.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Isaiah 30:22; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> Therefore say to the house of Israel, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Return,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “Repent”">l</a></sup>and turn away from your idols and from all of your detestable things! Turn away your faces!” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:48; Isaiah 58:1–2; Leviticus 24:22"> For ⸤each person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a man, a man” or “each one, each one”">m</a></sup>from the house of Israel, and from the alien who dwells as an alien in Israel, who separates himself ⸤from following me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “from behind me”">n</a></sup>and brings up his idols into his heart and ⸤places before himself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “he puts beside his face”">o</a></sup>a stumbling block of his guilt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “iniquity”">p</a></sup>, and yet he comes to the prophet to consult him concerning me, I Yahweh will answer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “am answering”">q</a></sup>him myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:7; Jeremiah 44:11; Ezekiel 5:15"> And I will set my face against that person, and I will make him to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “as”">r</a></sup>be a sign and make him into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “to”">s</a></sup>the subject of proverbs; and I will cut him off from the midst of my people, and you will know I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:25; Jeremiah 4:10; Psalm 81:11–12"> And the prophet, if he is deceived and he speaks a word, I Yahweh, I have deceived that prophet, and I will stretch out my hand against him, and I will destroy him from the midst of my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:49; Ezekiel 14:4; Jeremiah 8:11–12"> And they will bear their guilt, like<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “as”">t</a></sup>the guilt of the inquirer so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “like”">u</a></sup>the guilt of the prophet will be,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 44:10; Revelation 21:7"> so that the house of Israel will not go astray again ⸤from me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “from behind me”">v</a></sup>and they will not make themselves unclean again with all of their transgressions, and they will be for me a people, and I will be for them as God,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">w</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>

## Jerusalem will not be Spared

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “was”">x</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to say”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 4:16; Isaiah 24:20"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">z</a></sup>when a land sins against me by ⸤acting very unfaithfully⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “acting unfaithfully doing infidelity”">aa</a></sup>then I will stretch out my hand against it, and I will break for it the ⸤supply of food⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “staff of bread”">ab</a></sup>and I will send against it famine, and I will cut it off, both human and animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:20; Jeremiah 15:1; Ezekiel 28:3"> And if even the three of these men were in the midst of it—Noah, Daniel, and Job—they, through their righteousness, would save only ⸤themselves⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “their souls”">ac</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">ad</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:17; Leviticus 26:22; Jeremiah 15:3"> “If a fierce animal I should let cross through the land, and it should make it childless and it will be a desolation, so that there will not be one crossing over the land ⸤due to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">ae</a></sup>the presence of the animal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; James 5:16; Ezekiel 33:11"> even if these three men were in the midst of it, ⸤as surely as I live⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “live I”">af</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">ag</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, “surely they will not save sons and daughters; ⸤they themselves alone⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “they to alone them”">ah</a></sup>they will be saved, but the land will be a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Ezekiel 21:3–4; Zephaniah 1:3"> Or, if I bring a sword over that land, and I say, ‘Sword, let it cross over into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “in”">ai</a></sup>the land!’ And I will cut off from it both human and animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And if these three men were in the midst of it, ⸤as surely as I live⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “live I”">aj</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">ak</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, “they will not save sons and daughters, but ⸤they alone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “they to alone them”">al</a></sup>will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 5:12; Jeremiah 14:12"> And if I were to send a plague to that land, and I pour out my rage on it with blood to cut it off, both human and animal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; 1 John 2:29; Isaiah 3:10"> and if Noah, Daniel, and Job were in the midst of her, ⸤as surely as I live⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “live I”">am</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">an</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, “surely not a son, surely not a daughter will they save by their righteousness; they would save ⸤themselves⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “their souls”">ao</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:27; Ezekiel 5:17; Ezekiel 14:13"> For thus says the Lord Yahweh: “How much more when I send my four punishments—the evil sword, and famine, and a fierce animal, and a plague—to Jerusalem to cut it off, both human and animal!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:16; Ezekiel 20:43; Hebrews 12:6–11"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “And”">ap</a></sup>look! A remnant will be left over in it, sons and daughters ⸤who will be brought out⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “the ones being brought out”">aq</a></sup>Look! They are coming out to you, and you will see their way, and with their deeds you will be consoled with respect to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Hebrew “for”">ar</a></sup>the evil that I brought over Jerusalem, all of it that I brought over<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Or “upon”">as</a></sup>it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Revelation 15:4; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And they will console you when you see their way and their deeds, and you will know that not for nothing I did all that I did in it,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">at</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
