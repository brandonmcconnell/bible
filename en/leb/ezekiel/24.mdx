## The Boiling Pot and the Death of Ezekiel’s Wife

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1; Ezekiel 29:17"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was”">a</a></sup>to me in the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to say”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:1; Jeremiah 52:4; 2 Kings 25:1"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">c</a></sup>write for yourself the name of the day, ⸤this very day⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “exactly the day the this”">d</a></sup>The king of Babylon laid siege to Jerusalem on exactly this day!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 17:2; Ezekiel 20:49"> And ⸤deliver a proverb⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “quote a proverb”; “proverb a proverb”">e</a></sup>to ⸤the rebellious house⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the house of rebellion”">f</a></sup>and you must say to them, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Place the pot! Place it and also pour water into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Ezekiel 22:18–22; Matthew 7:2"> Gather its pieces to it, every good piece, thigh and shoulder, fill it with choice bones;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:24–27; Jeremiah 52:10; Jeremiah 39:6"> take the choicest of the flock, and also pile the bones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or possibly “logs”; the difference is one letter in Hebrew">g</a></sup>under it; ⸤boil it vigorously⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “boil its boiling”">h</a></sup>indeed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “also”">i</a></sup>its bones boiled in the midst of it.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Nahum 3:1; Obadiah 1:11"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Woe to the city of bloodguilt! A pot that has its rust in it; ⸤its rust did not go out from it⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “rust her not went forth from her”">j</a></sup>Bring it out ⸤piece by piece⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “according to its pieces”">k</a></sup>⸤one is as good as another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “no lot has fallen on it”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 17:13; Deuteronomy 12:16; Job 16:18"> For her blood was in the midst of her; ⸤she put it on a bare rock⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “on barrenness of a rock she put it”">m</a></sup>she did not pour it on the ground to cover it with dust.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “earth” or “dirt”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21–22; Revelation 18:5–10; Ezekiel 22:30–31"> To stir up rage, to avenge myself through vengeance, I placed its blood on the barrenness of a rock, ⸤so that it may not be covered⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “so as not to be covered”">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6; Habakkuk 2:12; Isaiah 31:9"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Woe to the city of bloodguilt! I, even I, will make the pile of wood great!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Jeremiah 17:3; Lamentations 1:10"> Pile up the logs; kindle the fire; finish cooking the meat, and mix in the spices, and let the bones be burned.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “charred”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:10; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 1:25"> And ⸤make it stand⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “cause it to stand”">q</a></sup>empty upon its burning coals so that it may become hot, and its copper may become molten and be melted in the midst of it,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “her”">r</a></sup>so that its uncleanness and its rust be consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:5; Jeremiah 2:13; Jeremiah 5:3"> Through effort she has caused weariness; ⸤its thick rust⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “the abundance of its rust”">s</a></sup>went not out of it. Its rust! Into the fire with its rust!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:24; Ezekiel 5:13; Zephaniah 3:2"> In your uncleanness is obscene conduct, because I cleansed you, but you were not clean from your uncleanness; you will not be clean again until ⸤I fully vent my rage⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “until causing rest I to my rage”">t</a></sup>on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Ezekiel 18:30; Isaiah 3:11"> I, Yahweh, I have spoken. It is coming, and I did it; I will not disregard, and I will not take pity, and I will not reconsider. According to your ways and according to your deeds they will judge you!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “I will judge”">u</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">v</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “was”">w</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “to say”">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:10; Jeremiah 13:17; Song of Solomon 7:10"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to say”">y</a></sup>look! I am taking from you what is pleasing to your eyes with a plague,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “blow”">z</a></sup>but you shall not mourn, and you shall not weep, and your tears shall not run down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:4; 2 Samuel 15:30; Leviticus 10:6"> Sigh in silence for the dead; you shall not make a mourning ceremony. Bind your turban on you, and you must put your sandals on your feet. You must not cover your upper lip,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “your mustache” (NASB)">aa</a></sup>and the bread of ⸤mourners⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “men”">ab</a></sup>you shall not eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:29–30"> And I spoke to the people in the morning, and my wife died in the evening, and I did in the morning ⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “as that”">ac</a></sup>I was commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:9; Ezekiel 37:18; Malachi 3:7–8"> And the people said to me, “Will you not make known to us what these things that you are doing mean for us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And I said to them that the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “was”">ad</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “to say”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:47; Ezekiel 23:25; Psalm 27:4"> “Say to the house of Israel, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I will be profaning my sanctuary, the pride of your strength and the delight of your eyes and the object of your own<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “souls’,” or “selves’ ”">af</a></sup>affection; and your sons and your daughters whom you left behind will fall by the sword,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 78:64; Jeremiah 16:4–7; Ezekiel 24:16–17"> and you shall do ⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “as that”">ag</a></sup>I did: You shall not cover your upper lip,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “mustache”">ah</a></sup>and you shall not eat the bread of ⸤mourners⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “men”">ai</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:10; Ezekiel 4:17; Leviticus 26:39"> And your turban must be on your heads, and your sandals must be on your feet. You shall not mourn, and you shall not weep, but you shall waste away because of your iniquities, and you shall groan ⸤to one another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “a man to his brother”">aj</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:3; Isaiah 20:3; Luke 11:29–30"> And Ezekiel will be to you as a sign; ⸤everything that he did⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “like all that he did”">ak</a></sup>you shall do; and when it comes you will know that I am the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Psalm 48:2; Jeremiah 11:22"> “And you, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">al</a></sup>will it not be that on the day ⸤when I take from them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “on the day of to take me from them”">am</a></sup>their stronghold, the joy of their glory, the delight of their eyes, and the longing of their inner self,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “soul”">an</a></sup>their sons and daughters—</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; Ezekiel 33:21–22; 1 Samuel 4:12–18"> on that day a survivor will come to you ⸤with the news⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “with a message for ears”">ao</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:22; Ezekiel 29:21; Luke 21:15"> On that day your mouth shall be opened ⸤at the arrival of the fugitive⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “with the fugitive”; or “to the one who has escaped” (NRSV; cf. NASB)">ap</a></sup>and you shall speak, and you shall not be silent any longer, and you shall be to them as a sign, and they will know that I am Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
