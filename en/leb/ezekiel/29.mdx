## Yahweh’s Judgment upon Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the ⸤twelfth⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “two ten”">a</a></sup>day of the month, the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “was”">b</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to say”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 46:2–16"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">d</a></sup>set your face against Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against Egypt, all of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 27:1; Jeremiah 44:30"> Speak, and you must say, ‘thus says the Lord Yahweh:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look! I am against you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Pharaoh, king of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the great sea monster,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the one lying down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the midst of his Nile streams,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who says to me, “It is my Nile, and I made it for myself.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “me”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Ezekiel 38:4; Amos 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And so I will put hooks in your jawbones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will make the fish of your Nile streams stick to your scales,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will bring you up from the midst of your Nile streams,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all of the fish of your Nile streams which cling to your scales.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20; Ezekiel 32:4–6; Jeremiah 8:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will fling you to the desert,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you and all the fish of your Nile streams.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">On the surface of the field you will fall;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will not be gathered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will not be assembled.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To the animals</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of the field</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to the birds</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “bird”">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of the heavens</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will give you as food.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6; Exodus 9:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">And all of the inhabitants of Egypt will know that I am Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of their</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="LXX, Syriac, and Vulgate: “you”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">being a staff of reed for the house of Israel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 17:15–17; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">When they took hold of you with the hand, you snapped,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you split their every shoulder.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And when they leaned on you, you broke,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you caused all of their loins to wobble.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I am bringing on you a sword, and I will cut off from you human and animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Proverbs 16:18; Ezekiel 29:10–12"> And the land of Egypt will become<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “be”">i</a></sup>a desolation and ruins, and they will know that I am Yahweh because he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="That is, Pharaoh">j</a></sup>said, ‘The Nile is ⸤mine⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to me”">k</a></sup>And I, I made it!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 30:6–9; Exodus 14:2"> Therefore look! I am against you and against your Nile streams, and I will make the land of Egypt into ruins, a pile of rubble, a desolation from Migdol to Syene and up to the boundary of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:13; Jeremiah 43:11–12; Ezekiel 30:10–13"> A foot of a human will not pass over it, and a foot of an animal will not pass over it, and so it will not be inhabited for forty years.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “year”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:19; Ezekiel 30:7; Ezekiel 30:23"> And I will make the land of Egypt a desolation in the midst of desolated countries, and its cities to be in the midst of ruined cities. They will be a desolation for forty years, and I will scatter Egypt among the nations, and I will disperse them among the countries.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:26; Isaiah 19:22"> For thus says the Lord Yahweh: “At the end of forty years I will gather Egypt from the peoples among whom they were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 11:11; Jeremiah 44:1"> And I will restore the fortunes of Egypt, and I will bring them back to the land of Pathros, to the land of their origin, and they will be a lowly kingdom there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Ezekiel 30:13; Ezekiel 17:14"> Of all the kingdoms it will be the most lowly, and it will not exalt itself again over the nations, and I will make them small so as not to rule over the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Isaiah 20:5; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And it will not be again for the house of Israel an object of trust, bringing to remembrance their guilt ⸤when they turned back to them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “at their turning”">m</a></sup>and they will know that I am the Lord Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Ezekiel 24:1"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “and it happened”">n</a></sup>in ⸤the twenty-seventh year⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “twenty and seven year”">o</a></sup>in the first month, on the first day of the month, the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “was”">p</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to say”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:6; Jeremiah 25:9; Ezekiel 26:7–12"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">r</a></sup>Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, he made his army labor hard against Tyre. Every head was rubbed bare, and every shoulder was rubbed raw, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “and”">s</a></sup>a wage was not paid for him and for his army from Tyre for the labor that he did against it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:24–25; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Ezekiel 32:11"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I am giving to Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon the land of Egypt, and he will carry its wealth away, and he will plunder its plunder, and he will loot its loot, and it will be a wage for his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Isaiah 10:6–7; Isaiah 45:1–3"> As his wages that he worked for, I will give to him the land of Egypt, because they worked for me,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">t</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; Luke 21:15; Psalm 132:17"> “On that day I will cause ⸤power⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “a horn”">u</a></sup>to grow up for the house of Israel, and for you I will give an opening of your mouth in the midst of them, and they will know that I am Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
