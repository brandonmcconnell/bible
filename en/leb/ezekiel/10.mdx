## God’s Presence Leaves the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 24:10; Revelation 4:2–3; Revelation 1:13"> And I looked, and look! On the expanse that was above the head of the cherubim something like a stone of sapphire,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “lapis lazuli”">a</a></sup>and like the appearance of the shape of a throne it appeared above them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:5; Ezekiel 10:16; Ezekiel 1:13"> And he spoke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “said”">b</a></sup>to the man clothed in linen and said, “Go ⸤in among the wheel area under the cherubim⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to between to the wheel to under the cherub”">c</a></sup>and fill the hollow of your hands with coals of fire from among the cherubim, and toss them on the city.” And he went right before my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:4; Ezekiel 9:3; Ezekiel 8:16"> Now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “And”">d</a></sup>the cherubim were standing on the south of the temple ⸤when the man went⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “at his coming the man”">e</a></sup>and the cloud filled the inner courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Ezekiel 9:3; Exodus 40:35"> And the glory of Yahweh rose up from on the cherub toward<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “onto”">f</a></sup>the threshold of the house,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “temple”">g</a></sup>and the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “temple”">h</a></sup>was filled with the cloud, and the courtyard was filled with the brightness of the glory of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Job 40:9; Exodus 19:16"> And the sound of the wings of the cherubim was heard ⸤as far as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “up to”">i</a></sup>the outer courtyard; it was like the voice of God Shaddai<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Often translated “Almighty”">j</a></sup>⸤when he speaks⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “in his speaking”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalm 99:1; Psalm 80:1"> And then at his command<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “when he commanded”">l</a></sup>to the man clothed in linen, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “to say”">m</a></sup>“Take fire from among the wheel area from among the cherubim,” he went and stood beside the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 13:41–42; Ezekiel 1:13; Ezekiel 41:23–26"> Then the cherub stretched out his hand from among<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “between”">n</a></sup>the cherubim toward the fire that was among<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “between”">o</a></sup>the cherubim, and he lifted up and gave<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “placed”">p</a></sup>it into the hollow of the hand of the man clothed with linen, and he took it, and he went out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:8; Ezekiel 10:21; Isaiah 6:6"> And there appeared for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to”">q</a></sup>the cherubim the form of ⸤a human hand⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “a hand of a man”">r</a></sup>under their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:15–17; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 21:20"> And I saw, and look, four wheels beside the cherubim, ⸤one wheel beside each cherub⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “wheel one beside the cherub one, and wheel one beside the cherub one”">s</a></sup>and the appearance of the wheels was like the outward appearance of ⸤turquoise stone⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “stone of turquoise”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Psalm 36:6; Psalm 104:24"> Their appearance<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="That is, of the wheels">u</a></sup>⸤was the same for each of the four of them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “likeness one was for the four of them”">v</a></sup>as if the wheel was in the midst of a wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:17; Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 1:20"> ⸤When they went⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “In their going”">w</a></sup>to the four of their directions that they went, they did not change direction ⸤when they went⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “At their going”">x</a></sup>for the place to which the head<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “first” or “lead wheel”">y</a></sup>turned, they went behind him; they did not change direction at their going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:18; Revelation 4:8; Revelation 4:6"> And ⸤their whole body⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “the whole of their body”">z</a></sup>and their rims, and their spokes, and their wings, and the wheels were full of eyes all around—⸤the wheels for the four of them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “for the four of them their wheels”">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Concerning the wheels, he was calling them “the wheelwork” ⸤in my hearing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “in my ears”">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Ezekiel 10:21; 1 Kings 7:36"> And ⸤each one had four faces⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “four faces were to each”">ac</a></sup>the face of the one was the face of a cherub, and the face of the second was the face of a human, and the face of the third was the face of a lion, and the fourth was the face of an eagle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:5; Hosea 9:12; Ezekiel 11:22"> And the cherubim rose; that is, the living creatures<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Hebrew “creature”">ad</a></sup>that I saw at the ⸤Kebar River⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “river of Kebar”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:19–21"> And ⸤when the cherubim went⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “at the going of the cherubim”">af</a></sup>the wheels beside them went; and ⸤when the cherubim lifted their wings to rise up from the earth⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “and at the lifting of the cherubim wings their to rise from on the earth”">ag</a></sup>the wheels also did not turn aside from beside them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:20–21; Romans 8:2; Ezekiel 1:12"> ⸤When they stood⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “at their standing”">ah</a></sup>they stood, and at their rising, they rose with them, for the spirit of the living creatures was in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 18:10; Matthew 23:37–39; Psalm 78:60–61"> And the glory of Yahweh went out from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “from on”">ai</a></sup>the threshold of the temple, and it stood above the cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 1:17–21; Ezekiel 1:26–28"> And the cherubim lifted up their wings, and they rose from the earth before my eyes. At their going out, the wheels were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “to”">aj</a></sup>beside them. And he stood at the doorway of the eastern gate of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “house”">ak</a></sup>of Yahweh, and the glory of the God of Israel ⸤was over them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “on them from to above”">al</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Ezekiel 10:15; 1 Kings 7:36"> This was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “these were”">am</a></sup>the living creature that I saw under the God of Israel at the ⸤Kebar River⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “river of Kebar”">an</a></sup>and I knew that they were cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:6; Ezekiel 41:18–19; Ezekiel 10:14"> ⸤Each one had four faces⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “four faces were for one/each”">ao</a></sup>and there were four wings for each, and the likeness of ⸤human hands⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “hands of a man”">ap</a></sup>was under their wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 1:12"> And the likeness of their faces, they were the faces that I saw at the ⸤Kebar River⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Litearlly “river of Kebar”">aq</a></sup>thus was their appearance, and they each went ⸤straight ahead⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “to the opposite of face his”">ar</a></sup></span>
</div>
