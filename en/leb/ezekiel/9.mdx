## Yahweh’s Avenging Messenger Destroys the Wicked in the City

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 6:8; Ezekiel 43:6–7; 2 Kings 10:24"> And he called in my ears with a loud voice, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to say”">a</a></sup>“They have come near,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “Come near” (imperative)">b</a></sup>the punishers of the city, and each with his weapon of destruction in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 16:4; Ezekiel 10:2; Ezekiel 10:6–7"> And look! Six men coming from the way of ⸤the upper gate⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the gate of the upper”">c</a></sup>that faced ⸤northward⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “faced north + directive”">d</a></sup>and each with his weapon for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “of”">e</a></sup>shattering in his hand; and one man was in the midst of them, dressed in linen, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “his”">f</a></sup>writing case of the scribe was at his side. And they came and stood beside the bronze altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:4; Ezekiel 11:22–23; Ezekiel 43:2–4"> And the glory of the God of Israel lifted itself up from upon the cherub that he was on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “which he was on it”">g</a></sup>and went to the threshold of the house,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, the temple">h</a></sup>and he called to the man who was clothed in linen ⸤with a scribal writing case at his side⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “whom a writing case of a scribe was at his side”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 9:4; 2 Corinthians 1:22; Revelation 14:1"> And Yahweh said to him, “Go through in the midst of the city, in the midst of Jerusalem, and you must place a mark on the foreheads of the men ⸤who are groaning and lamenting⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the men the groaning and the lamenting”">j</a></sup>about all of the detestable things ⸤that are being done⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the being done”">k</a></sup>in the midst of her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 8:18; Ezekiel 7:9"> And to the others he said in my hearing, “Go through the city behind him and kill! Your eyes shall not take pity, and you shall not have compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 9:4; Exodus 12:23; Jeremiah 25:29"> You must kill ⸤totally⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to destruction”">l</a></sup>old man, young man and young woman, and little children and women, but ⸤concerning⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “upon”">m</a></sup>every man ⸤with the mark on him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “which is on him the mark”">n</a></sup>you must not approach; and from my sanctuary you must begin.” And they began with the old men who ⸤were before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “were to the face of”">o</a></sup>the house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="That is, the temple">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:17; Ezekiel 7:20–22; Lamentations 2:4–7"> And he said to them, “Defile the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “temple”">q</a></sup>and fill the courtyards with the dead; go out! And they went out, and they killed in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Numbers 16:45; Joshua 7:6"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “and it was”">r</a></sup>⸤as they were striking⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “in their striking”">s</a></sup>and I was left behind, I fell on my face, and I cried out, and I said, “Ah, Lord Yahweh! “Will you be destroying all of the remnant of Israel ⸤while you pour out your rage⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “when pouring out your rage”">t</a></sup>on Jerusalem?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 22:13; Ezekiel 8:12; Ezekiel 7:23"> And he said to me, “The guilt of the house of Israel and Judah is ⸤exceedingly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “with very very”">u</a></sup>great, and the land is filled with bloodguilt, and the city is full of injustice. For they say Yahweh abandoned the land, and ⸤Yahweh does not see⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “not Yahweh is seeing">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:4; Ezekiel 8:18; Ezekiel 11:21"> And I, my eye will not take pity, and I will not have compassion; their way I will bring on their head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 6:7–8; Revelation 16:17; Revelation 16:2"> And look! The man clothed in linen and ⸤with a writing case at his side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “which/whom the writing-case was at his side”">w</a></sup>was bringing back a word, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “to say”">x</a></sup>“I have done all that you commanded me.”</span>
