<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:1; Ezekiel 1:2–3; Ezekiel 14:1"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and it happened”">a</a></sup>in the sixth year, in the sixth month, on the fifth day of the month, I was sitting in my house, and the elders of Judah were sitting ⸤before me⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to the face of me”">b</a></sup>And the hand of the Lord Yahweh fell on me there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Ezekiel 1:26–27; Daniel 7:9–10"> and I saw,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “looked”">c</a></sup>and look! A figure like the appearance of a man;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “fire”; but reading the BHS note 2b, “a man.”">d</a></sup>from the appearance of his waist and below was fire, and from his waist and ⸤upward⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to above”">e</a></sup>was like the appearance of brightness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “shining metal”">f</a></sup>like the ⸤outward appearance of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “eye”">g</a></sup>amber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:16; Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 5:11"> And he sent out the form of a hand, and he took me by a lock of hair of my head, and the Spirit lifted me between earth and heaven, and it brought me to Jerusalem in visions of God to the doorway of the inner gate ⸤that faced north⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the one facing north”">h</a></sup>at which there was the seat of the image of jealousy, which was making jealous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:22–23; Exodus 40:34–35; Ezekiel 11:22–23"> And look! There was the glory of the God of Israel like the vision that I saw in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Jeremiah 3:2; Jeremiah 32:34"> And he said to me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">i</a></sup>lift up now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Particle of entreaty, “please/ please now”">j</a></sup>your eyes ⸤toward the north⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to the way of the north”">k</a></sup>And I lifted up my eyes ⸤toward the north⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to the way of the north”">l</a></sup>and, look, there was to the north of the gate of the altar this image of jealousy at the entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 10:19; Ezekiel 8:9"> And he said to me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">m</a></sup>“⸤Do you see⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “Are you seeing?”">n</a></sup>what they are doing—great detestable things that the house of Israel is committing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “doing”">o</a></sup>here so as to drive me from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “from upon”">p</a></sup>my sanctuary, and yet ⸤you will see again⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “you will return you will see”">q</a></sup>greater<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “worse”">r</a></sup>detestable things.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:5; 1 Kings 7:12"> And he brought me to the doorway of the courtyard, and I saw, and look! A hole in the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:15; Job 34:22; Amos 9:2–3"> And he said to me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">s</a></sup>dig now<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “please”">t</a></sup>through the wall.” And I dug through the wall, and look! There was a doorway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:8"> And he said to me, “Come and see the detestable things, the evil that they are doing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “making”">u</a></sup>here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 1:23; Exodus 20:4; Jeremiah 16:18"> And I came, and I saw, and look, all kinds of creatures<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “creature”">v</a></sup>and detestable beasts;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “beast”">w</a></sup>and all of the idols of the house of Israel were carved on the wall ⸤all around⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “around around”">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 16:17; Numbers 16:35; Numbers 11:16"> And seventy men from the elders of the house of Israel, and Jaazaniah, the son of Shaphan, was standing in the midst of them, and they were standing ⸤before them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to the face of them”">y</a></sup>Each one had his censer in his hand, and the fragrance of the cloud of the incense was going up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 29:15; Ezekiel 9:9; Ezekiel 8:15"> And he said to me, “Have you seen, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">z</a></sup>what the elders of the house of Israel are doing in the dark, each in the inner rooms of his idol, for they are saying, ‘Yahweh is not seeing us; Yahweh has abandoned the land.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:6; Jeremiah 9:3; Ezekiel 8:15"> And he said to me, “Still ⸤you will see again⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “you will return, you will see”">aa</a></sup>greater detestable things that they are doing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:9; Ezekiel 44:4"> And he brought me to the doorway of the gate of the house of Yahweh that is toward the north, and look! There were the women sitting weeping for Tammuz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12–13; Ezekiel 8:9; Ezekiel 8:6"> And he said to me, “Have you seen, son of man?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">ab</a></sup>Still ⸤you will see again⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “you will return, you will see”">ac</a></sup>greater detestable things than these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:19; Deuteronomy 17:3; Jeremiah 32:33"> And he brought me to the inner courtyard of the house of Yahweh, and look, at the doorway of the temple of Yahweh, between the portico and the altar, there were about twenty-five men with their backs to the temple of Yahweh and their faces toward the east, and they were bowing down toward the east before the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:9; Ezekiel 7:23; Jeremiah 6:7"> And he said to me, “Have you seen, son of man?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">ad</a></sup>⸤Was it too small a thing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “was it too little”">ae</a></sup>for the house of Judah ⸤to do⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “from doing”">af</a></sup>the detestable things that they did here? For they filled up the land with violence, and ⸤they provoked me to anger again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “they returned to provoke me to anger”">ag</a></sup>and look! They are putting the branch to their nose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Jeremiah 11:11; Isaiah 1:15"> And so I will act in rage, and my eye will not take pity, and I will not have compassion, and they will cry in my ear with a loud voice, and I will not hear them.”</span>
