## Pharaoh Is Warned Through Assyria’s Destruction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:20; Jeremiah 52:5–6; Ezekiel 1:2"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and it happened/it was”">a</a></sup>⸤in the eleventh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “on one ten”">b</a></sup>year, in the third month, on the first day of the month, the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “was”">c</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to say”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 31:18"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">e</a></sup>say to Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, and to his crowd,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “hordes”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘To whom are you like in your greatness?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:33–34; Daniel 4:10; Daniel 4:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look! Assyria was a cedar in Lebanon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with beautiful branches</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “branch”">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a forest giving shade,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ⸤very high⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “high of height”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its treetop was between the clouds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 17:15; Proverbs 14:28; Ezekiel 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Waters made it great,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the deep made it grow high;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">its rivers were going all around ⸤its planting area⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “base of its tree”; NRSV, “in the place where it was planted”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its channels it sent out</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to all of the trees of the field.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:11; Ezekiel 17:5; Psalm 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore it became tall,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with its height more than all of the trees of the field,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and its branches became numerous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its branches became long</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤from its sending its shoots from abundant water⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “from water much at sending shoots its”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:23; Matthew 13:32; Daniel 4:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">In its branches all the birds</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “bird”">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of the heaven</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “sky”">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">made their nest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and under its branches all the animals</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of the field gave birth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and in its shadow all the many nations lived.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it was beautiful in its greatness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the length of its branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for its root was toward much water.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 51:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cedars in the garden of God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">could not be equal to it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">fir trees</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or possibly, juniper">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">could not resemble its branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and plane trees were not even like its branches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">any tree even in the garden of God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">could not resemble it in its beauty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Daniel 2:37–38; Genesis 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I made it beautiful with the abundance of its branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all of the trees of Eden that were in the garden of God envied it.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:17; Daniel 5:20; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: “⸤Because⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “because that”">o</a></sup>it was tall in height and it set its treetop between<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “to between”">p</a></sup>thick clouds, and ⸤he took pride in his tallness⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “and his heart he raised in his tallness”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11–12; Nahum 3:18; Daniel 5:18–19"> then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “and”">r</a></sup>I gave it into the hand of the leader of nations; he ⸤dealt thoroughly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “doing he did”">s</a></sup>with it according to its wickedness. I drove it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 35:8; Ezekiel 30:11"> And strangers cut it off, the most ruthless of nations, and they abandoned it. On the mountains and in all of the valleys its branches fell, and its branches were broken in all the river channels of the land, and all the peoples of the world went out from its shadow, and they abandoned it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:4; Ezekiel 29:5; Isaiah 18:6"> On its fallen trunk all the birds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “bird”">t</a></sup>of the heaven<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “sky”">u</a></sup>now dwell, and all the animals of the field were on its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 63:9–10; Nehemiah 13:18; 2 Peter 2:6"> This occurred so that all of the trees with abundant water will not become tall, and they will not set their treetop between<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “among”">v</a></sup>their thick foliage,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “clouds”">w</a></sup>and so that⸤all of the trees that are abundantly watered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “all of the drinkers of water”">x</a></sup>⸤will not stand up to them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “will not stand to them”">y</a></sup>in their tallness, for all of them, they have been given over to death, to the world below in the midst of ⸤mortals⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sons of men,” or “children of humankind”">z</a></sup>to the people going down to the grave.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “pit”">aa</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 3:4; Nahum 2:8–10; Revelation 18:9–11"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “On the day of its going down to Sheol, I caused mourning; I covered over it with the deep, and I withheld its rivers, and many waters were restrained, and I brought gloom over it; Lebanon and all of the trees of the field, they had fainted because of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 14:15; Isaiah 14:8; Ezekiel 26:15"> From the sound of its downfall I caused nations to shake when I made it go down to Sheol, with the people going down to the grave,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “the pit”">ab</a></sup>and so in the world below all of the trees of Eden, the choice and the best of Lebanon, ⸤all the well-watered trees⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “all of the drinkers of water”">ac</a></sup>were comforted!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 9:17; Ezekiel 31:6; Ezekiel 31:3"> They also went down with it to Sheol to ⸤those who died by the sword⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “to the slain of the sword”">ad</a></sup>and its army who had lived in its shadow in the midst of nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:19; Ezekiel 32:21; Ezekiel 28:10"> ⸤To whom could you be compared, whether in glory or in majesty⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “To who you are like as in glory and in majesty”">ae</a></sup>among the trees of Eden? And yet you will be brought down with the trees of Eden to the world below; in the midst of the uncircumcised you will lie with ⸤those who died by the sword⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “the slain of the sword”">af</a></sup>That is Pharaoh and ⸤his entire⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “all his hordes/crowd”">ag</a></sup>crowd!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “hordes”">ah</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">ai</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
