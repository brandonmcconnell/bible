## Prophecy Directed at Edom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:1; Ezekiel 21:1; Ezekiel 34:1"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was”">a</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to say”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 32:3; Ezekiel 25:8"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">c</a></sup>set your face against the mountain of Seir and prophesy against it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:12; Ezekiel 35:7; Ezekiel 6:14"> and you must say to it, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I am against you, mountain of Seir, and I will stretch out my hand against you, and I will make you a desolation and a wasteland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:9; Malachi 1:3–4; Joel 3:19"> Your cities I will make ruins, and you will be a desolation, and then you will know that I am Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 137:7; Obadiah 1:10–16; Amos 1:11"> because there has been to you ⸤an ancient hostility⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “enmity of eternity”">d</a></sup>and you handed over the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>to the power of the sword at the time of their disaster, at the time of ⸤their final punishment⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the time of punishment of end”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 18:24; Obadiah 1:15"> Therefore ⸤as I live⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “live I”">g</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">h</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, “Certainly to blood guilt I will prepare you and blood<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “blood vengeance”">i</a></sup>will pursue; ⸤since⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “if not”">j</a></sup>⸤you did not hate blood⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “not blood you hated”">k</a></sup>it will pursue you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 35:3; Judges 5:6–7"> And I will make the mountain of Seir into a desolation and ruin, and I will cut off from it both he who is crossing over and he who is returning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12; Ezekiel 32:4–5; Ezekiel 39:4–5"> And I will fill its mountains with its slain; your hills and your valleys and all of your watercourses, the slain by the sword will fall in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Malachi 1:3–4; Ezekiel 6:7"> ⸤An everlasting desolation⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “of eternity/endless ages”">l</a></sup>I will make you, and your cities will not return, and you will know that I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:5; Psalm 83:4–12; Ezekiel 48:35"> I do this because of your saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “thinking”">m</a></sup>‘The two nations and the two lands, they will be ⸤mine⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to me”">n</a></sup>and we will take possession of it,’ and yet Yahweh was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 9:16; Matthew 7:2; Psalm 73:17–18"> Therefore, ⸤as I live⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “live I”">o</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">p</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, “so I will deal with you according to your anger and according to your jealousy that you did,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “showed” or “acted on”">q</a></sup>because of your hatred against them, and I will make myself known among them when I judge you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:2; Psalm 83:12; Ezekiel 6:7"> And you will know that I, Yahweh, I have heard all of your contemptible words that you said<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “spoke”">r</a></sup>against the mountains of Israel, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “to say”">s</a></sup>‘They will be desolate; they are given to us as food.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; Isaiah 36:20; Jeremiah 29:23"> And you magnified yourself against me with your ⸤insolent speech⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “with your mouth”">t</a></sup>and you spoke voluminously against me with your words—I heard!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:48; Isaiah 14:7–8; Isaiah 65:13–15"> Thus the Lord Yahweh says, ‘⸤As the whole world rejoices⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “as/like the rejoicing of all of the world”">u</a></sup>I will make you a desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:3–4; Lamentations 4:21; Isaiah 34:5–6"> ⸤Like your rejoicing over the inheritance of the house of Israel⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “as/like rejoicing your for the inheritance of the house of Israel”">v</a></sup>⸤because⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “because that”">w</a></sup>it was desolate, I will do to you; you will be a desolation, mountain of Seir and all of Edom, indeed all of it; and they will know that I am Yahweh.’ ” ’</span>
</div>
