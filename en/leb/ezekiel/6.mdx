## Idolatrous Worship and Idolatrous Objects Denounced

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was”">a</a></sup>to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:9; Ezekiel 34:14; Ezekiel 20:46"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">b</a></sup>set your face to the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:30; Ezekiel 36:1–4; Jeremiah 3:23"> and you must say, ‘Mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord Yahweh, thus says the Lord Yahweh to the mountains and to the hills, to the ravines and to the valleys: “Look, I am bringing upon you the sword, and I will destroy your high places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:5; Leviticus 26:30; 2 Kings 23:14"> and your altars will be desolate, and your incense altars will be broken, and I will throw down your slain ones ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">c</a></sup>your idols,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:16; Jeremiah 8:1–2; 2 Kings 23:14"> and I will place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “give”">d</a></sup>the corpses of the children of Israel ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">e</a></sup>their idols, and I will scatter your bones around your altars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:14; Isaiah 6:11; Zechariah 13:2"> In all of your dwellings, the cities will be desolate and the high places will be ruined, so that your altars will be desolate and will suffer punishment. Your idols will be broken and will come to an end, and your incense altars will be cut down, and your works will be destroyed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:33; Ezekiel 13:23; Ezekiel 35:15"> and the slain one will fall in the midst of you, and then you will know that I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:22; Jeremiah 44:28; Ezekiel 12:16"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “And”">f</a></sup>I will spare some, ⸤so there will be⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “at their being”">g</a></sup>for you fugitives from the sword among the nations ⸤when you are scattered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “at you to be scattered”">h</a></sup>in the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:43; Isaiah 7:13; Job 42:6"> And your fugitives will remember me among the nations to which they were taken captive, that I was shattered by ⸤their adulterous heart⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “heart their which was prostituting itself ”">i</a></sup>which departed from me, and by ⸤their adulterous eyes⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “eyes their which were prostituting”">j</a></sup>which went after their idols, and they will feel loathing ⸤for themselves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “for their face”">k</a></sup>for the evil that they did, for all of their detestable things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:12–14; Daniel 9:12; Jeremiah 44:28"> And they will know that I am Yahweh; not ⸤in vain⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to nothing”">l</a></sup>I spoke to bring to them this evil.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:12; Ezekiel 9:4; Ezekiel 21:14–17"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘Clap your hand and stamp with your foot, and say, “Alas, for all of the detestable things of the evil of the house of Israel, ⸤because of which⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “which”">m</a></sup>they will fall with the sword, with the famine, and with the plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Daniel 9:7; Lamentations 4:22"> The one who is far away will die by the plague, and the one who is near will fall by the sword, and the one who is being left behind and being spared will die by the famine, and I will complete my rage on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “toward”">n</a></sup>them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:28; Hosea 4:13; 1 Kings 14:23"> And you will know that I am Yahweh ⸤when their slain ones are in the midst of their idols⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “in the being of their slain ones in the midst of their idols”">o</a></sup>around their altars at every high hill, on the tops of all the mountains and under every green tree and under every leafy oak—the place at which they gave a pleasing scent for all of their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25; Ezekiel 20:33–34; Isaiah 9:12"> And I will stretch out my hand against them, and I will make the land a desolation and a wasteland from the desert to Riblah in all of their dwellings, and they will know that I am Yahweh.” ’ ”</span>
