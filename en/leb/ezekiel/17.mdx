## Parable of Two Eagles and a Vine

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was”">a</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to say”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">c</a></sup>tell a riddle and quote a proverb to the house of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Jeremiah 48:40; Ezekiel 17:7"> and you must say, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “⸤The great eagle with great wings and long feathers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the eagle the great great of the wings long of the feather”">d</a></sup>and ⸤full of variegated plumage⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “full of the plumage which is for him the variegated”">e</a></sup>came to Lebanon and he took the treetop of the cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> He plucked the top of its new plant shoot, and he brought it to the land of Canaan and put it in a city of merchants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 37:1"> and he took from the seed of the land and placed it in ⸤fertile soil⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “a field of seed”">f</a></sup>on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “by”">g</a></sup>many waters; like a willow he planted it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> And it sprouted, and ⸤it became a vine spreading out⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “he was to vine spreading out”">h</a></sup>low of height, turning its branches to him, and its roots were under it, and ⸤it became a vine⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and it was to a vine”">i</a></sup>and it made<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “sprouted”">j</a></sup>branches, and it sent out foliage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:4; Ezekiel 17:15; 2 Kings 24:20"> “ ‘ “And there was another great eagle, great of wings and with abundant plumage, and look! This vine stretched out its roots toward him and extended its branches to him to water it from the garden bed ⸤where it was planted⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “from the garden bed of its planting”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5–6"> It was planted in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “to”">l</a></sup>a good field by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “to”">m</a></sup>many waters<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “water”">n</a></sup>to produce branches<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “branch”">o</a></sup>and to bear fruit to become<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “be”">p</a></sup>⸤a beautiful vine⸥.” ’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to a vine of splendor”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 32:5; Isaiah 30:1–7"> Say, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Will it prosper? Will he not tear out its roots, and will he not make its fruit scaly, and it will wither, and all of the freshness of its vegetation will dry up? And to lift it from its roots ⸤will not require great strength or many people⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “not with strong arm and with many people”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> And look! Though it is planted, will it prosper? ⸤When the east wind strikes it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “at striking her/it the wind of the east”">s</a></sup>will it not dry up completely? On the garden bed of its vegetation it will dry up!” ’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “was”">t</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:3; Ezekiel 1:2"> “Say now to the rebellious house of Israel, ‘Do you not know what these are?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “mean”">u</a></sup>Say, ‘Look! The king of Babylon will come to Jerusalem, and he will take its king and its officials, and he will bring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “take”">v</a></sup>them to himself,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">w</a></sup>to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Kings 24:15–17; Ezekiel 17:5"> And he took from the seed of the kingship, and he made with him a covenant, and ⸤he brought him under oath⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “and he brought him into oath”">x</a></sup>and he took the rulers of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; 1 Samuel 2:30"> so that they would be a humble kingdom and not lift itself up to keep his covenant ⸤in order for it to stand⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to standing her”; or “to let her stand”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Ezekiel 17:7"> But he rebelled against him by sending his messengers to Egypt to give to him horses and a large army. Will he succeed? Will he escape doing these things, and can he break the covenant and escape?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 52:11; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> ⸤As I live⸥,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “live I”">z</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">aa</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, ‘⸤surely⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “if not”">ab</a></sup>in the place of the king ⸤who made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “who he was making”">ac</a></sup>him king, who despised his oath and who broke his covenant with him—in the midst of Babylon he will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7; Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And not with a great army and with a great crowd will Pharaoh work<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “help/aid”">ad</a></sup>with him in the war, at the pouring out of a siege ramp and the building of siege works to destroy many lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:24; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> And he despised the oath to break covenant. And, look, he gave his hand in pledge, and yet he did all of these things. He will not escape.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:2"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘⸤As I live⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “live I”">ae</a></sup>⸤surely⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “if not”">af</a></sup>my oath that he despised and my covenant that he broke I will return upon his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Ezekiel 32:3; Jeremiah 2:35"> And I will spread my net over him, and he will be caught in my hunting net, and I will bring him to Babylon, and I will enter into judgment with him there about his infidelity that he displayed against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:5; 2 Kings 25:11; Ezekiel 12:14"> And all of his choice troops, among all of his troops, they will fall by the sword, and those who are remaining,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “left over”">ag</a></sup>they will be scattered to all the directions of the wind, and you will know that I, Yahweh, I have spoken.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2; Zechariah 6:12–13; Isaiah 11:1–5"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘And I will take, even I, from the treetop of the high cedar, and I will plant it, from the head of its new plant shoot I will pluck a tender one, and I will plant it, even I, on a high and lofty mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Ezekiel 31:6; Isaiah 2:2"> On the height of the mountain of Israel I will plant it, and it will carry<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “bear”">ah</a></sup>branches,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “branch”">ai</a></sup>and it will bear fruit, and ⸤it will become a noble cedar⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “he will be to a cedar noble”">aj</a></sup>and all of the birds of all wings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “kinds”">ak</a></sup>will dwell under it in the shade of its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Ezekiel 24:14"> And all of the trees of the field will know that I, Yahweh, I will bring low a high tree, and I will exalt a low, fresh tree, and I will make a dry tree flourish. I, Yahweh, I have spoken, and I will do it.’ ”</span>
</div>
