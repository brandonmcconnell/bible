## Prophecy Concerning Israel’s Shepherds and Sheep

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was”">a</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:1; Jeremiah 3:15; Isaiah 40:11"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">b</a></sup>prophesy against the shepherds of Israel, prophesy, and you must say to them, to the shepherds, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Woe to the shepherds of Israel who were feeding themselves! Must not the shepherds feed the flock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 11:16; Jeremiah 2:30; Ezekiel 33:25–26"> The fat you eat, and you clothe yourself with the wool; the well-nourished animals you slaughter, but you do not feed the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 18:12–13; Matthew 9:36; Ezekiel 34:16"> The weak you have not strengthened, and the sick you have not healed, and ⸤with respect to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “for”">c</a></sup>the hurt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “wounded”">d</a></sup>you have not bound them up, and you have not brought back the scattered, and you have not sought the lost, but rather you ruled over them with force<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “violence”">e</a></sup>and with ruthlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; 2 Chronicles 18:16; Jeremiah 23:2"> And they were scattered ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from not”">f</a></sup>a shepherd, and they were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “became”">g</a></sup>as food for all the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">h</a></sup>of the field when they were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 142:4; 1 Peter 2:25; John 10:16"> My flock went astray upon all of the mountains and on every high hill, and so upon all the surface of the world<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “earth”">i</a></sup>my flock were scattered, and ⸤there was no one seeking them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “there was not someone seeking them ”">j</a></sup>and ⸤there was no one searching for them⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “there was not someone searching for them ”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:2–3; Micah 3:8–9; Psalm 82:1–7"> Therefore, hear, O shepherds, the word of Yahweh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Acts 20:33; Ezekiel 34:5–6"> “⸤As I live⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “live I”">l</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">m</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, “⸤Surely⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “if not”">n</a></sup>because my flock have become as plunder, and my flock became as food to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “for”">o</a></sup>all the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">p</a></sup>of the field, since there was not a shepherd, since my shepherds have not sought my flock, but the shepherds fed themselves, and they fed not my flock,’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> therefore, O shepherds, hear the word of Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Zechariah 10:3; Ezekiel 13:8"> ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I am against the shepherds, and I will seek my flock from their hand, and I will put an end to them from shepherding flocks, and the shepherds will ⸤no longer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “not any longer”">q</a></sup>feed themselves, and I will deliver my flocks from their mouth, so that they will not be as food for them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:16; Luke 19:10; Jeremiah 23:3"> “ ‘For thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I, even I, will seek my flock, and I will look after them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; John 10:11–12; Luke 19:10"> just like the caring of a shepherd for his herd on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “in”">r</a></sup>the day when he is in the midst of his scattered flock. Thus I will look after my flock, and I will deliver them from all the places ⸤to which they were scattered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “which they were scattered there”">s</a></sup>on the day ⸤of storm and stress⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “on the day of cloud and deep gloom”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25–26; Ezekiel 11:17; Isaiah 11:11–16"> And I will bring them out from the peoples, and I will gather them from the countries, and I will bring them to their soil, and I will feed them on the mountains of Israel, in the valleys, and in all of the settlements of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:9; Jeremiah 31:25; Psalm 31:8–10"> I will feed them in good pasture, and their pasture will be on the mountains of the heights of Israel; there they will lie down in good pasture, and on lush pasture they will feed on the mountains of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; Psalm 23:1–2; John 21:15"> ⸤I myself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “I, I”">u</a></sup>will feed my flock and ⸤I myself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “I, I”">v</a></sup>will allow them to lie down,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">w</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Ezekiel 34:4; Ezekiel 34:11"> “I will seek the lost, and I will bring back the scattered, and I will bind up the one hurt, and I will strengthen the sick; and the fat and the strong I will destroy. I will feed her with justice.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20–22; Matthew 25:32–33; Zechariah 10:3"> “ ‘And you, my flock, thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Look! I am judging between ⸤one sheep and another⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “small sheep and small sheep”">x</a></sup>between the rams and between the he-goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 11:52; Numbers 16:13"> ⸤Is it not enough for you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “is it too little from you”">y</a></sup>that you feed on the good pasture? And still you must trample the remainder of your pasture with your feet, and ⸤clear water⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “and settling of water”">z</a></sup>you drink, and the ⸤leftover water⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “water being left”">aa</a></sup>you must make muddy with your feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And my flock must graze the pasture treaded upon by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “of”">ab</a></sup>your feet, and must drink the mud puddle stirred up by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Hebrew “of”">ac</a></sup>your feet.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:31–46; Ezekiel 34:10; Psalm 22:12–16"> “ ‘Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh to them: “Look! I, even I will judge between fat sheep and between lean sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 11:5; Deuteronomy 33:17; Luke 13:14–16"> because with your flank and with your shoulder you shoved, and with your horns you pushed all of the sick animals ⸤until⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “until that”">ad</a></sup>you scattered it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “them”">ae</a></sup>to the outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:2–3; Psalm 72:12–14; Ezekiel 34:10"> And so I will save my flock, and ⸤they will no longer be⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “not they will be longer for plunder”">af</a></sup>for plunder, and I will judge between ⸤one sheep and another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “sheep and sheep”">ag</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Jeremiah 30:9; Isaiah 40:11"> And I will set up over them one shepherd, and he will feed them; that is, my servant David. He will feed them, and he will be for them as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “like”">ah</a></sup>a shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Ezekiel 36:28; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> And I, Yahweh, I will be for them ⸤as God⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “to a God”">ai</a></sup>and my servant David will be a leader in the midst of them. I, Yahweh, I have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; Ezekiel 37:26; Leviticus 26:6"> And I will make them a covenant of peace, and I will put an end to wild animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">aj</a></sup>from the land, and they will dwell in the desert ⸤safely⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “with confidence/trust”">ak</a></sup>and they will sleep in the forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:12; Isaiah 44:3; Leviticus 26:4"> And I will make them and the area all around my hill a blessing, and I will let the rain go down ⸤at its appointed time⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “in time his”">al</a></sup>they will be rains of blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:8; Leviticus 26:4; Leviticus 26:13"> And the tree<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “trees”">am</a></sup>of the field will give its fruit, and the land will give its produce, and they will be on their land ⸤safely⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “with/in conficence”">an</a></sup>and they will know that I am Yahweh when I break their yoke, and I will deliver them from the hand of the ones enslaving them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27; Jeremiah 30:10; Ezekiel 39:26"> And they will not be any longer plunder to the nations, and the animals of the land will not eat them, and they will dwell ⸤in safety⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “with confidence”">ao</a></sup>and there will not be anyone frightening them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:29; Isaiah 4:2; Ezekiel 34:26–27"> And I will raise for them a garden plot<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “planting”">ap</a></sup>of renown, and they will ⸤no longer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “not any longer”">aq</a></sup>be victims of famine in the land, and they will not bear any more the insult of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; Ezekiel 34:24; Psalm 46:11"> And they will know that I, Yahweh their God, am with them and they are my people, the house of Israel,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">ar</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 100:3; John 10:16; Psalm 78:52"> “and you are my flock, the flock of my pasture. You are ⸤my people⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “humanity, Mankind, human, mankind”">as</a></sup>I am your God,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">at</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.’ ”</span>
</div>
