<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:5; Ezekiel 9:1; Jeremiah 1:10"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “and as”; as is not needed in translation">c</a></sup>the appearance of the vision which I saw was as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “like”">d</a></sup>the vision which I saw at his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “my coming”">e</a></sup>coming to destroy the city, and these visions were also as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “like”">f</a></sup>the vision which I saw by the ⸤Kebar River⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “river of Kebar”">g</a></sup>and I fell on my face.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Isaiah 66:6; Revelation 16:1"> And I heard someone speaking to me from the house,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “from inside”">i</a></sup>and a man was standing beside me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:9; Psalm 47:8; Leviticus 26:30"> And he said to me, “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">j</a></sup>this is the place of my throne and the place for the soles of my feet ⸤where I will dwell⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “which I will dwell there”">k</a></sup>in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup>to eternity, and they, the house of Israel, they and their kings, will not again defile ⸤my holy name⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the name of my holiness”">m</a></sup>with their fornication and with their offerings for the dead<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “corpses”">n</a></sup>of their kings on their high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 8:3–16; 2 Chronicles 33:7"> ⸤When they placed their threshold⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “in/at their placing their threshold”">o</a></sup>with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “beside/next to”">p</a></sup>my threshold, their doorframe beside my doorframe and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “their”">q</a></sup>wall was between me and between them, then they defiled ⸤my holy name⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the name of my holiness”">r</a></sup>with their detestable things that they did, and so I consumed them in my anger.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Ezekiel 11:20; Matthew 28:20"> And if they are ashamed of all that they did, then the plan of the temple and its arrangement and its exits and its entrances and all of its plans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “its entire pattern”">v</a></sup>and all of its statutes. And all of its plans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “purposes”">w</a></sup>and all of its laws<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="According to the reading tradition (Qere ); cf. BHS">x</a></sup>make known to them and write them before their eyes, so that they may remember all of its plans and all of its statutes, so that they do them!</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:33; Exodus 29:35–37; Leviticus 8:35"> For seven days you must provide a goat of sin offering for the day and a bull, ⸤a calf⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Literally “son of cattle,” or “son of the herd”">bh</a></sup>and a ram from the flock without defect; you must provide them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 9:1; Ezekiel 20:40–41; 1 Peter 2:5"> And they will finish the days, ⸤and then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bj" id="fnref-bj" data-fn="Literally “and it will happen”">bj</a></sup>on the eighth day and beyond that the priests will offer on the altar your burnt offerings and your fellowship offerings, and then I will be pleased with you,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bk" id="fnref-bk" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">bk</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
