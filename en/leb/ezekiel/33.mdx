<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Nehemiah 4:20; Nehemiah 4:18"> and he sees the sword coming against the land, and he blows on the horn and he warns the people,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:18–20; Ezekiel 33:8–9; Genesis 42:22"> And as for the watchman, if he sees the sword coming and ⸤he does not blow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “and not he blows on”">g</a></sup>the horn, and the people are not warned, and the sword comes and it takes ⸤their lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “it takes from them a life”">h</a></sup>he will be taken through his guilt, but his blood from the hand of the watchman I will seek.’</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:19; Acts 20:26; Proverbs 15:10"> But, you, if you warn the wicked from his way, to turn from it, and ⸤he does not turn⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “and not he turns”">l</a></sup>from his way, he will die by his guilt, and you will have saved your life.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Ezekiel 18:23; 2 Peter 3:9"> Say to them, ‘⸤As I live⸥,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “live I”">p</a></sup>⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">q</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh, ‘⸤Surely I have no delight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “if”">r</a></sup>in the death of the wicked, ⸤except⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “for it”">s</a></sup>in the wicked returning from his way, and he lives. </div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Ezekiel 18:22; Micah 7:18–19"> All of his ⸤sins that he committed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “sins that he sinned”">ab</a></sup>they will not be remembered against<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “for”">ac</a></sup>him, and he did justice and righteousness; certainly he will live.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “was”">ap</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “to say”">aq</a></sup></span>
