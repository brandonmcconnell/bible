<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 7:2; Ezekiel 6:11–14"> Because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “Because that”">d</a></sup>I will cut off from you both righteous and wicked, therefore my sword will go out from its sheath to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “against”">e</a></sup>all creatures<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “creature”">f</a></sup>from south to north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:30; Nahum 1:9; 1 Samuel 3:12"> And they will know, all creatures, that I, Yahweh, I will bring out my sword from its sheath; it will not return again!” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Ezekiel 6:11; Jeremiah 19:10"> And you, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">g</a></sup>groan ⸤with shaking hips⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “with destruction of loins”">h</a></sup>and you must groan with bitterness before their eyes.</span>

It is coming, and it will happen!” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">j</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “was”">k</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to say”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Ezekiel 21:28; Ezekiel 21:3"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">m</a></sup>prophesy, and you must say, ‘Thus says the Lord,’ say, ‘A sword, a sword is sharpened and is also polished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 110:5–6; Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 21:25–27"> It is sharpened to slaughter a slaughter, ⸤polished to flash like lightning⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “so that there be for her lightning”">n</a></sup>Or will we rejoice?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “be pleased”">o</a></sup>A rod, my son, is despising every tree.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “stick” of correction; Hebrew is uncertain in meaning here">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:19; Jeremiah 25:33; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> And he gives it to be polished, ⸤to be seized by the hand⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to take hold of in the palm of the hand”">q</a></sup>It is sharpened—a sword—and it is polished to give it into the hand of the killer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:6; Joel 1:13; Ezekiel 21:14"> Cry and wail, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">r</a></sup>for it is against my people; it is against all of the princes of Israel. <span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Ezekiel 21:25"> For ⸤examine⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “it was tested”">t</a></sup>And what if also the rod will not be despising?’ ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">u</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 24:1; Numbers 24:10; Leviticus 26:24"> And you, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">v</a></sup>prophesy ⸤and clap your hands⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “strike hand to hand”">w</a></sup>And the sword will ⸤strike twice⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “be doubled”">x</a></sup>let it happen a third time. <span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; Jeremiah 17:27; Ezekiel 21:22"> so that a heart melts, and the fallen ⸤multiply⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “be made many”">y</a></sup>at all of their gates. <span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:25; Ezekiel 22:28; Psalm 37:13"> when seeing a false vision for you in vain, when practicing divination for you falsely, to give<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Or “place”">ax</a></sup>you on the neck of the profane ones of the wicked, ⸤whose day has come⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Literally “who has come their day”">ay</a></sup>⸤the time of final punishment⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Literally “in the time of the punishment of the end”">az</a></sup></span>
