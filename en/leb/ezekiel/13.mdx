## Condemnation of False Prophets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of Yahweh ⸤came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was”">a</a></sup>to me, ⸤saying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “to say”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:17; Jeremiah 37:19; Ezekiel 22:28"> “Son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">c</a></sup>prophesy to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “against”">d</a></sup>the prophets of Israel ⸤who are prophesying⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the ones prophesying”">e</a></sup>and you must say to those who are prophets out of their own ⸤imagination⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “heart”">f</a></sup>‘Hear the word of Yahweh!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 2:14; Jeremiah 23:28–32; Hosea 9:7"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘Alas, for the foolish prophets who are going after their own spirit, ⸤and they did not see anything⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “and not they saw”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Micah 2:11; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10"> Your prophets have been like foxes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “jackals”">h</a></sup>among ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Psalm 106:23; Isaiah 58:12"> You did not go up into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “in”">i</a></sup>the breaches and repair a wall for the house of Israel to stand firm in the battle on the day of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:8; Ezekiel 22:28; Jeremiah 28:15"> They saw falseness and ⸤a lying divination⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a divination of a lie”">j</a></sup>the ones saying, ‘Declaration of Yahweh!’ and Yahweh did not send them, and they wait ⸤for the confirmation of their word⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to confirm a word”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Matthew 24:23–24; Ezekiel 13:2–3"> Have you not seen ⸤a false vision⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “vision of falseness”">l</a></sup>and spoken a ⸤lying divination⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “divination of a lie”">m</a></sup>and you said, ‘Declaration of Yahweh!’ but ⸤I myself did not speak⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “I not I spoke”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 21:3; Ezekiel 28:22"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘Because of your speaking falseness and because you envisioned a lie, therefore, look! I am against you!” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">o</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 87:6; Psalm 69:28; Ezekiel 20:38"> “And it will be my hand against the prophets who are seeing falseness and who are practicing lying divination. They will not be in the council of my people, and they will not be written down in the record book of the house of Israel, and into<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “in”">p</a></sup>the land of Israel they will not come, and then you will know that I am the Lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:28; Ezekiel 13:16; Jeremiah 6:14"> Because, yes, because they led my people astray, ⸤saying⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to say”">q</a></sup>‘Peace!’ And there is not peace. And when anyone<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">r</a></sup>builds a flimsy wall, look, they coat it with whitewash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Isaiah 28:2; Nahum 1:7–8"> Say to those covering it with whitewash that it will fall; there will be ⸤a torrent of rain⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “rain flooding”">s</a></sup>and I will give stones of hail; they will fall!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “and I will give great hailstones. They will fall”">t</a></sup>And a ⸤windstorm⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “wind of a storm”; or “with respect/concerning you stones of hail, they will fall”">u</a></sup>will burst forth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 3:13; Lamentations 2:14–15; Judges 9:38"> And look! When the wall falls, will it not be said to you, ‘Where is the whitewash with which you covered it?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Revelation 16:21; Psalm 148:8"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘And so I will let burst forth a ⸤windstorm⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “a wind of storm”">v</a></sup>in my rage, and ⸤there will be a torrent of rain⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “rain flooding there will be”">w</a></sup>in my anger, and hailstones in my rage ⸤for complete destruction⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “to complete destruction”">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 1:6; Jeremiah 14:15; Jeremiah 6:15"> And I will break down the wall that you covered with whitewash, and I will knock it to the ground, and its foundation will be revealed, and it will fall, and you will come to an end in the midst of it, and you will know that I am Yahweh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:13; Psalm 62:3; Nehemiah 4:3"> And I will fully vent my rage against the wall and against those covering it with whitewash, and I will say to you, “⸤The wall is no more⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “not is the wall”">y</a></sup>and ⸤the people covering it are no more⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “not are the people whitewashing him”">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 8:11"> that is, the prophets of Israel, the ones prophesying concerning Jerusalem and the ones seeing visions of peace, and there is not peace!’ ” ” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “declaration of”">aa</a></sup>the Lord Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:2; 2 Kings 22:14; Judges 4:4"> “And you, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">ab</a></sup>place your face toward the daughters of your people who prophesy from their ⸤imagination⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “heart”">ac</a></sup>and prophesy against them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; Ezekiel 13:20; 2 Timothy 4:3"> And you must say, ‘Thus says the Lord Yahweh: “Woe to ⸤those who sew⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “the sewers of”">ad</a></sup>magic charm bands on all the wrists<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “joints”">ae</a></sup>of the hands of my people and ⸤those who make the veils⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “the makers of the veils”">af</a></sup>that are on the head of people of every height, to ensnare people’s lives!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “souls”">ag</a></sup>Will you ensnare the lives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “souls”">ah</a></sup>of my people ⸤and keep yourselves alive⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “and lives/souls for you, you keep alive”">ai</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 3:5; Proverbs 28:21; Jeremiah 23:17"> And you defiled me among my people for a handful of barley and for morsels of bread to kill persons who should not die and to keep alive persons who should not live by means of your lies to my people who are listening to your lies.” ’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “lie”">aj</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:18; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; Ezekiel 13:8–9"> Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: ‘Look! I am against your magic charm bands ⸤with which you are ensnaring persons⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “which you are ensnaring with them the souls/individuals”">ak</a></sup>as birds; I will tear them from your arms, and I will release the persons that you are ensnaring, treating persons as birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 91:3"> And I will tear off your veils, and I will deliver my people from your hand, and they will not any longer be in your hand as prey, and you will know that I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Jeremiah 14:13–17; 2 Peter 2:18–19"> Because you disheartened the heart of the righteous by deception, and I have not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “did not”">al</a></sup>caused him pain, and strengthened the hands of the wicked so that he did not turn<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “not to turn”">am</a></sup>from his wicked way to save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:21; Ezekiel 12:24; Micah 3:6"> Therefore falseness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “vanity” or “vain/false visions”">an</a></sup>you will not see,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “observe”">ao</a></sup>and divination you will not practice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “you will indeed not practice divination”">ap</a></sup>any longer, and I will rescue my people from your hand, and you will know that I am Yahweh!’ ”</span>
</div>
