## Ruth Meets Boaz

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; Ruth 3:12; Ruth 3:2"> ⸤Now⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and for”">a</a></sup>Naomi ⸤had a relative of her husband⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “was an acquaintance of a man of her husband”">b</a></sup>⸤a prominent rich man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a man mighty of wealth”">c</a></sup>from the clan of Elimelech, whose name was Boaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Leviticus 19:9; Deuteronomy 24:19–21"> And Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, “Please let me go to the field and glean among the ears of grain after someone in whose eyes I may find favor.” And she said to her, “Go, my daughter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:12; Matthew 10:29; Luke 10:31"> So she went and came and gleaned in the field behind the reapers. And she happened by chance upon the tract of field ⸤belonging to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “was for”">d</a></sup>Boaz, who was from the clan of Elimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 129:7–8; Judges 6:12; Luke 1:28"> And look, Boaz came from Bethlehem and said to the reapers, “May Yahweh be with you.” And they said to him, “May Yahweh bless you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 4:21; 1 Chronicles 2:11–12"> And Boaz said to his servant ⸤in charge of the reapers⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the one standing over the reapers”">e</a></sup>“To whom does this young woman belong?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Matthew 24:45; Genesis 24:2"> And ⸤the servant in charge of the reapers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the servant the one standing over the reapers”">f</a></sup>said, “She is a Moabite girl returning with Naomi from the countryside of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 18:23; Proverbs 15:33"> And she said, ‘Please let me glean and let me gather among the sheaves behind the reapers.’ So she came and remained from the morning up to now. ⸤She is sitting for a little while in the house⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “this one she is sitting a little in the house”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; Song of Solomon 1:7–8; 1 Samuel 3:6"> And Boaz said to Ruth, “⸤Listen carefully⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “You have heard”">h</a></sup>my daughter, go no longer to glean in another field. Moreover, do not leave from this one, but ⸤stay close⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “cling”">i</a></sup>with my young women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> Keep your eyes on the field that they reap and go after them. Have I not ordered the servants not to bother you? And if you get thirsty, you shall go to the containers and drink from where the servants have drawn.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23; Ruth 2:13; Luke 1:43"> And she fell on her face and bowed down to the ground and said to him, “Why have I found favor in your eyes by recognizing me—for I am a foreigner?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 14:33; Luke 18:29–30; Luke 5:11"> And Boaz answered and said to her, “All that you have done for your mother-in-law after the death of your husband was fully told to me. How you left your father and mother and the land of your birth, and you went to a people that you did not know ⸤before⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “yesterday three days ago”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 36:7; Psalm 91:4; Psalm 63:7"> May Yahweh reward your work and may a full reward be given to you from Yahweh, the God of Israel, under whose wings you came to take refuge.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:18; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 33:8"> And she said, “May I find favor in your eyes, my lord, for you have comforted me and have spoken ⸤kindly to your servant⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “on the heart of your servant”">k</a></sup>and I am not one of your servants.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ruth 2:18; 2 Kings 4:43–44; Matthew 14:20"> And Boaz said to her ⸤at mealtime⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “at the time of the food”">l</a></sup>“Come here and eat from the bread and dip your morsel in the wine vinegar.” So she sat beside the gleaners, and he offered to her roasted grain. And she ate and was satisfied, and she had some left over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 1:5"> And she got up to glean, and Boaz instructed his servants saying, “Let her also glean between the sheaves and do not reproach her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 1 John 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 24:19–21"> And also pull out for her from your bundles and leave it so that she may glean—and do not rebuke her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–12; Proverbs 31:27; Exodus 16:36"> So she gleaned in the field until the evening and she beat out what she had gleaned, and it was about an ephah of barley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:14; John 6:12–13; 1 Timothy 5:4"> And she picked it up and went to the town. Her mother-in-law saw how much she had gleaned. And she took it out and gave to her what she had left over ⸤after being satisfied⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “from her satiety”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Psalm 41:1; 1 Kings 7:21"> And her mother-in-law said to her, “Where did you glean ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the day”">n</a></sup>and where did you work? May he who took notice of you be blessed.” And she told her mother-in-law ⸤with whom she had worked⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “who she had worked with him”">o</a></sup>and said, “The name of the man who I worked with today is Boaz.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:5; Ruth 3:9–10; Leviticus 25:25"> And Naomi said to her daughter-in-law, “May he be blessed by Yahweh, whose loyal love has not forsaken the living or the dead.” And Naomi said to her, “The man is a close relative for us, he is one of our redeemers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:7–8; Ruth 2:7–8; Ruth 2:22"> And Ruth the Moabite said, “Also, he said to me, ‘You shall stay close with the servants which are mine until they have finished all of the harvest which is mine.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Song of Solomon 1:8; Proverbs 27:10"> And Naomi said to Ruth her daughter-in-law, “It is good, my daughter, that you go out with his maidservants so that you will not ⸤be bothered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “they touch on you”">p</a></sup>in another field.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–8; Deuteronomy 16:9; Ephesians 6:1–3"> So she stayed close with the maidservants of Boaz to glean until the end of the barley harvest and wheat harvest. And she lived with her mother-in-law.</span>
</div>
