## Boaz Redeems Ruth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:7; Ruth 3:12; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And Boaz had gone up to the city gate and sat there. And look, the redeemer of whom Boaz had spoken was passing by. And he said, “Come over here to sit, ⸤friend⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “certain someone”">a</a></sup>And he came over and sat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 31:23; 1 Kings 21:8; Acts 6:12"> And he took ten men from the elders of the city and said, “Sit here.” And they sat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; Psalm 112:5"> And he said to the redeemer, “Naomi, who returned from the countryside of Moab, is selling the tract of land which was for our brother Elimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 25:25–29; Jeremiah 32:7–12; Romans 12:17"> And I thought ⸤I would tell you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “I would open your ear”">b</a></sup>and say, ‘⸤Buy it in the presence of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “Buy before”">c</a></sup>those sitting and before the elders of my people,’ if you want to redeem it, redeem it. But if you do not want to redeem, tell me so that I may know, for there is no one except you to redeem it, and I am after you.” And he said, “I want to redeem it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 38:8; Deuteronomy 25:5–6; Matthew 22:24"> And Boaz said, “On the day of your acquiring the field from the hand of Naomi, you also acquire Ruth the Moabite, the wife of the dead man, in order to raise up for the name of the dead his inheritance.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 25:25; Ruth 3:13"> And the redeemer said, “I am not able to redeem for myself, lest I ruin my inheritance. You redeem for yourself my kinsman-redemption, for I am not able to redeem it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:7–10"> (Now this ⸤was the custom in former times⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “before faces”">d</a></sup>in Israel concerning the kinsman-redemption and transfer of property: to confirm the matter, a man removed his sandal and gave it to his fellow countryman. This was the manner of attesting in Israel.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So the redeemer said to Boaz, “Acquire it for yourself,” and he removed his sandal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:10–12; Genesis 23:16–18"> And Boaz said to the elders and all of the people, “You are witnesses today that I have acquired all that was for Elimelech and that was for Kilion and Mahlon from the hand of Naomi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:6; Ephesians 5:25; Genesis 29:27"> And also Ruth the Moabite, the wife of Mahlon, I have acquired as a wife, to raise up the name of the dead over his inheritance, so that the name of the dead may not be cut off from his relatives and from the gate of his birth place. You are witnesses today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 29:32–24; Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:6"> And all of the people who were at the gate and the elders said, “We are witnesses. May Yahweh make the woman coming into your house as Rachel and as Leah, who ⸤together⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the two of them”">e</a></sup>built the house of Israel. May you have strength in Ephrathah and bestow a name in Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 38:29; 1 Samuel 2:20; 1 Chronicles 2:4"> And may your house be like the house of Perez, whom Tamar bore to Judah from the offspring that Yahweh will give to you from this young woman.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lineage of King David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 33:5; Genesis 29:31; Ruth 3:11"> So Boaz took Ruth and she became his wife. And he went in to her, and Yahweh ⸤enabled her to conceive⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “gave to her conception”">f</a></sup>and she bore a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:5–20; Luke 1:58; Genesis 12:2"> And the women said to Naomi, “Blessed be Yahweh who today ⸤did not leave you without a redeemer⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “did not allow to be missing for you a redeemer”">g</a></sup>And may his name be renowned in Israel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 55:22; Isaiah 46:4; Genesis 45:11"> He shall be for you a restorer of life and a sustainer in your old age, for your daughter-in-law who loves you, who is better for you than seven sons, has borne him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Naomi took the child and she put him on her bosom and became his nurse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 1:58–63"> And the women of the neighborhood gave him a name, saying, “A son has been born to Naomi.” And they called his name Obed. He was the father of Jesse, the father of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 1:3–6; 1 Chronicles 2:4–8; Luke 3:33"> Now these are the descendants of Perez: Perez fathered Hezron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:33; 1 Chronicles 2:9–10; Matthew 1:4"> and Hezron fathered Ram, and Ram fathered Amminadab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:7; Luke 3:32; Matthew 1:4"> and Amminadab fathered Nahshon, and Nahshon fathered Salmon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:32; Matthew 1:5; 1 Chronicles 2:11–12"> and Salmon fathered Boaz, and Boaz fathered Obed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:15; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 1:6"> and Obed fathered Jesse, and Jesse fathered David.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
