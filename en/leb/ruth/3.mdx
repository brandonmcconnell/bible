## Ruth Meets Boaz at the Threshing Floor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ruth 1:9; 1 Timothy 5:8; 1 Timothy 5:14"> Now Naomi her mother-in-law said to her, “My daughter, should I not seek for you security that things may be good for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 2:20–23; Ruth 2:8"> So then, is not Boaz our kinsman whose maidservants you were with? Look, he is winnowing the barley at the threshing floor tonight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:2; 1 Timothy 2:9–10; Esther 5:1"> Wash, anoint yourself, put your clothing on, and go down to the threshing floor. Do not make yourself known to the man until he finishes eating and drinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:22"> And when he lies, take notice of the place where he lies. And you shall go and ⸤uncover⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “open”">a</a></sup>his feet and lie down, and he shall tell you what to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 3:20; Ephesians 6:1"> And she said to her, “I will do all that you say.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 1:8; John 2:5; Exodus 20:12"> And she went down to the threshing floor and did all that her mother-in-law had instructed her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Esther 1:10; 2 Samuel 13:28"> And Boaz ate and drank until his heart was ⸤merry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “good”">b</a></sup>and then he came to lie at the end of the grain heap. And she came in quietly and ⸤uncovered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “opened”">c</a></sup>his feet and lay down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And it happened in the middle of the night the man was startled and he reached out and behold, a woman was lying at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:8; Ruth 2:20; Ruth 3:12"> And he said, “Who are you?” And she said, “I am Ruth, your servant. Spread ⸤your garment⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “your wing”">d</a></sup>over your servant because you are a redeemer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5; Ruth 1:8"> And he said, “You are blessed by Yahweh my daughter. You did better in this last kindness than the first by not going after young men, whether poor or rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:29–31; Proverbs 31:10; Proverbs 12:4"> And so then my daughter, do not be afraid. All that you ask I will do for you, for the entire assembly of my people knows that you are a worthy woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:6; Matthew 7:12"> Now truly I am a redeemer, but there is also a redeemer of a closer relationship than me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ruth 4:5; Jeremiah 4:2; Ruth 2:20"> ⸤Stay tonight,⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “Spend the night tonight”">e</a></sup>and in the morning, if he wants to redeem you, good; but if he is not willing to redeem, ⸤then as Yahweh lives,⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the life of Yahweh”">f</a></sup>I will redeem you. Lie down until the morning.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; Romans 14:16; 1 Corinthians 10:32"> So she lay at his feet until morning and got up before ⸤anyone could recognize each other⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a man could recognize his friend”">g</a></sup>And he said, “It must not be known that ⸤you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the woman”">h</a></sup>came to the threshing floor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 32:8; Galatians 6:10"> And he said, “Bring the cloak that is on you and ⸤hold it out⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “hold on her”">i</a></sup>And she held it out and he measured six measures of barley and put it on her cloak. Then she went into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And she came to her mother-in-law, and she said, “⸤How did it go for you,⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “who are you?”">j</a></sup>my daughter?” And she told her all that the man did for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And she said, “These six measures of barley he gave to me, for he said, ‘You shall not go empty-handed to your mother-in-law.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 37:3–5; Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 30:7"> And she said, “Wait, my daughter, until you know how the matter turns out, for the man will not rest but will settle the matter today.”</span>
