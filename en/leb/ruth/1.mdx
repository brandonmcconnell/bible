## Elimelech Takes His Family to Live in Moab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 26:1; Ezekiel 14:13"> And it happened in the days when ⸤the judges ruled⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the judging of the judges”">a</a></sup>there was a famine in the land, and a man from Bethlehem of Judah went ⸤to reside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to dwell as an alien”">b</a></sup>in the countryside of Moab—he and his wife and his two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:19; Micah 5:2; 1 Samuel 17:21"> And the name of the man was Elimelech, and the name of his wife was Naomi, and the names of his two sons were Mahlon and Kilion. They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem in Judah. And they went to the countryside of Moab and remained there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; Psalm 34:19; Hebrews 12:6"> But Elimelech the husband of Naomi died and she was left behind with ⸤her two sons⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “she and her two sons”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:5; 1 Kings 11:1–2; Deuteronomy 23:3"> And ⸤they took⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “they lifted up”">d</a></sup>for themselves Moabite wives. The name of the one was Orpah and the name of the other was Ruth. And they lived there about ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Psalm 89:30–32; Isaiah 49:21"> But ⸤both⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the two of them”">e</a></sup>Mahlon and Kilion died, and the woman was left without her two sons and without her husband.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Naomi Returns with Ruth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 132:15; Exodus 3:16; Psalm 111:5"> And she got up, she and her daughters-in-law, and returned from the countryside of Moab, because she had heard in the countryside of Moab that Yahweh had ⸤come to the aid of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “had considered”">f</a></sup>his people to give food to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 8:3; Ruth 1:14; Exodus 18:27"> So she set out from the place ⸤where she was⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “which she was there”">g</a></sup>and her two daughters-in-law with her, and they went on the way to return to the land of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Ruth 1:5; Colossians 3:18"> But Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, “Go, each of you return to her mother’s house. May Yahweh ⸤show kindness to you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “do loyal love with you”">h</a></sup>just as you did with the dead and with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 3:1; Acts 20:37; Genesis 29:11"> May Yahweh ⸤grant that you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “give to you”">i</a></sup>find a resting place, each in the house of her husband.” And she kissed them, and they lifted up their voices and cried.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 8:23; Psalm 16:3; Psalm 119:63"> And they said to her, “No, we want to return with you to your people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Genesis 38:11"> And Naomi said, “Return, my daughters. ⸤Why do you still want to go with me⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “To what do you want to go still for me?”">j</a></sup>Are there sons ⸤in my womb⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “in my intestines”">k</a></sup>that may be husbands for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; 1 Timothy 5:9"> Turn back, my daughters! Go, for ⸤I am too old to have a husband⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “I am too old from being to a man”">l</a></sup>If I should think there is hope for me, even if I should have a husband this night, and even if I should bear sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 19:21; Judges 2:15; Psalm 32:4"> would you therefore wait until they were grown? Would you therefore ⸤refrain from marrying⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “Would you therefore let yourself be hindered to not belong to a man?”">m</a></sup>No, my daughters, for it is far more bitter to me than to you. For the hand of Yahweh has gone out against me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; Matthew 16:24; Matthew 10:37"> And they lifted up their voices and cried again. And Orpah kissed her mother-in-law goodbye, but Ruth clung to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 11:24; Joshua 24:15; Zephaniah 1:6"> And she said, “Look, your sister-in-law has returned to her people and to her gods. Return after your sister-in-law too.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:21; Ruth 2:11–12; 2 Kings 2:2–6"> But Ruth said, “⸤Do not urge⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “You shall not press me”">n</a></sup>me to leave you or to return from ⸤following you⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “from behind you”">o</a></sup>For where you go, I will go, and where you lodge, I will lodge. Your people will be my people and your God will be my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:17; 1 Kings 2:23; 2 Kings 6:31"> Where you die I will die, and there I will be buried. So may Yahweh do to me, ⸤and even more, unless⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and so may he do again, that”">p</a></sup>death ⸤separates you and me⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “makes a separation between you and between me”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:14; Ephesians 6:10; Acts 2:42"> When Naomi saw that she was determined to go with her, ⸤she said no more⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “she ceased to speak to her”">r</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:10; Isaiah 23:7; Lamentations 2:15"> So the two of them went until they came to Bethlehem. ⸤And when they came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “And it happened at the moment of coming”">s</a></sup>to Bethlehem, all of the town was stirred because of them. And they said, “Is this Naomi?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 6:4; Hebrews 12:11; Psalm 73:14"> And she said to them, “You should not call me Naomi; call me Mara, for Shaddai<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Often translated “the Almighty”">t</a></sup>has ⸤caused me to be very bitter⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “caused very bitterness to me”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:7–8; Job 1:21; Job 16:8"> I went away full, but Yahweh brought me back empty-handed! Why call me Naomi ⸤when Yahweh has testified against me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “and Yahweh answered against me”">v</a></sup>and Shaddai<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Often translated “the Almighty”">w</a></sup>has brought calamity upon me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ruth 2:23; 2 Samuel 21:9; Exodus 9:31–32"> So Naomi returned, and Ruth the Moabite her daughter-in-law with her, returning from the countryside of Moab. And they came to Bethlehem at the beginning of the harvest of barley.</span>
</div>
</div>
