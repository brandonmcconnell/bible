## No Longer Slaves But Sons and Heirs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Galatians 4:29; Genesis 24:2–3"> Now I say, for as long a time as the heir is a child, he is no different from a slave, although he is master of everything,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> but he is under guardians and managers until the time set by his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:20; Colossians 2:8; Galatians 4:9"> So also we, when we were children, we were enslaved under the elemental spirits of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; John 1:14; Romans 1:3"> But when the fullness of time came, God sent out his Son, born of a woman, born under the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Ephesians 1:5; 1 Peter 1:18–20"> in order that he might redeem those under the law, in order that we might receive the adoption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:15–17; Romans 5:5; Romans 8:9"> And because you are sons, God sent out the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, crying out, “Abba!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Abba” is Aramaic for “father”">a</a></sup>(Father!),”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:16–17; Galatians 3:29–2; Galatians 3:26"> so that you are no longer a slave but a son, and if a son, also an heir through God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Be Enslaved Again

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 2:11–12; 2 Thessalonians 1:8"> But at that time when you did not know God, you were enslaved to the things which by nature are not gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Galatians 3:3; Romans 8:3"> But now, because you have come to know God, or rather have come to be known by God, how can you turn back again to the weak and miserable elemental spirits? Do you want to be enslaved to them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to whom”">b</a></sup>all over again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:5; Leviticus 23:1–44"> You carefully observe days and months and seasons and years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Philippians 2:16; Galatians 2:2"> I am afraid for you, lest perhaps I have labored for you in vain!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:5; 1 Kings 22:4; Genesis 34:15"> I ask you, brothers, become like me, because I also have become like you. You have done me no wrong!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Personal Appeal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:30"> But you know that because of an illness of the flesh I proclaimed the gospel to you the first time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 2:13; Hebrews 13:2"> And you did not despise or disdain what was a trial for you in my flesh, but you welcomed me like an angel of God, like Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:2; 1 John 3:16–18; Colossians 4:13"> So where is your blessing? For I testify to you that, if possible, you would have torn out your eyes and given them to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Proverbs 9:8; John 8:45"> So then, have I become your enemy by being truthful to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Peter 2:3"> They zealously seek you, not commendably, but they want to exclude you, in order that you may seek them zealously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Revelation 3:19; Philippians 1:27"> But it is good to be sought zealously in good at all times, and not only when I am present with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 8:29; Romans 13:14"> My children, for whom I am having birth pains again, until Christ is formed in you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17–18; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Thessalonians 3:9"> But I could wish to be present with you now, and to change my tone because I am perplexed about you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Hagar and Sarah Represent Two Covenants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:23–24; Romans 7:5–6"> Tell me, you who are wanting to be under the law, do you not understand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “hear”">c</a></sup>the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:15; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–2"> For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by the female slave and one by the free woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 4:28–29"> But the one by the female slave was born according to human descent, and the one by the free woman through the promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 21:9–13; Hebrews 10:15–18; Genesis 16:8"> which things are spoken allegorically, for these women are two covenants, one from Mount Sinai, bearing children for slavery, who is Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Acts 1:11; Galatians 1:17"> Now Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia, and corresponds to the present Jerusalem, for she is a slave with her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22; Philippians 3:20; Micah 4:1–2"> But the Jerusalem above is free, which is our mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–5; Psalm 113:9; Ruth 4:14–16"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rejoice, O barren woman, who does not give birth to children;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">burst out and shout, you who do not have birth pains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because many are the children of the desolate woman,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even more than those of the one who has a husband.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 54:1">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Romans 9:8–9; Romans 4:13–18"> But you, brothers, are children of the promise, just as Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:11"> But just as at that time the child born according to human descent persecuted the child born according to the Spirit, so also now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 21:10–12; John 8:35; Romans 8:15–17"> But what does the scripture say? “Drive out the female slave and her son, for the son of the female slave will never inherit with the son”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 21:10, which does not include the words “of the free woman”">e</a></sup>of the free woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:36; John 1:12–13; Galatians 5:1"> Therefore, brothers, we are not children of the female slave but of the free woman.</span>
</div>
</div>
