## Justified by the Law, or Justified by Faith?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:7–8; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Galatians 1:6"> O foolish Galatians, who has bewitched you, before whose eyes Jesus Christ was publicly portrayed as having been crucified?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:16–17; Galatians 3:14; Acts 15:8"> I want only to learn this from you: Did you receive the Spirit by the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:4–8; Galatians 4:7–10; Hebrews 7:16–19"> Are you so foolish? Having begun by the Spirit, are you now trying to be made complete by the flesh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 John 1:8; 1 Corinthians 15:2; Hebrews 10:32–39"> Have you suffered so many things for nothing—if indeed also it was for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Acts 14:9–10; Acts 19:11–12"> Therefore does the one who gives you the Spirit and who works miracles among you do so by the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Romans 4:21–22; James 2:23"> Just as Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him for righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:9; Galatians 3:26–29"> then understand that the ones ⸤who have faith⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “of faith”">a</a></sup>, these are sons of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 28:14"> And the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, proclaimed the good news in advance to Abraham: “In you all the nations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “Gentiles”; the same Greek word can be translated “nations” or “Gentiles” depending on the context">b</a></sup>will be blessed.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 12:3 ; 18:18">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 4:16; Galatians 3:29; Romans 4:24"> So then, the ones who have faith are blessed together with Abraham who believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Romans 8:7; Romans 3:19–20"> For as many as are of the works of the law are under a curse, for it is written, “Cursed is everyone who does not abide by all the things that are written in the book of the law to do them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 27:26">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38"> Now it is clear that no one is justified in the sight of God by the law, because “the one who is righteous will live by faith.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “the one who is righteous by faith will live,” a quotation from Hab 2:4">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Romans 10:5–6; Nehemiah 9:29"> But the law is not from faith, but “the one who does these things will live by them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 18:5">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Deuteronomy 21:23; Hebrews 9:12"> Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us, because it is written, “Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 21:23">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28–29; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Ezekiel 36:26–27"> in order that the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles in Christ Jesus, so that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Promise to Abraham and His Descendant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 9:17; Romans 6:19; Romans 3:5"> Brothers, I am speaking according to a human perspective. Nevertheless, when the covenant of a man has been ratified, no one declares it invalid or adds additional provisions to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 4:13; Luke 1:55"> Now to Abraham and to his descendant the promises were spoken. It does not say, “and to descendants,” as concerning many, but as concerning one, “and to your descendant,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 12:7 ; 13:15 ; 17:7 ; 24:7">h</a></sup>who is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39–40; Genesis 15:13; Romans 4:13–14"> Now I am saying this: the law, that came after four hundred and thirty years, does not revoke a covenant previously ratified by God, in order to nullify the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12–15; Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:13–16"> For if the inheritance is from the law, it is no longer from the promise, but God graciously gave it to Abraham through the promise.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Addition of the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:53; Romans 7:7–13; Hebrews 2:2"> Why then the law? It was added on account of transgressions, until the descendant should come to whom it had been promised, having been ordered through angels by the hand of a mediator.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Deuteronomy 6:4; Job 9:33"> Now the mediator is not for one, but God is one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 9:31; Romans 3:20–22"> Therefore is the law opposed to the promises of God? May it never be! For if a law had been given that was able to give life, certainly righteousness would have been from the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 11:32; Hebrews 9:15; Galatians 3:23"> But the scripture imprisoned all under sin, in order that the promise could be given by faith<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “through the faithfulness of Jesus Christ”">i</a></sup>in Jesus Christ to those who believe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:18; Galatians 3:24–25; Galatians 3:19"> But before faith came, we were detained under the law, imprisoned until the coming faith was revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Acts 13:38–39; Romans 7:24–25"> So then, the law became our guardian until Christ, in order that we could be justified by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:4; Hebrews 10:15–18"> But after faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 6:18; John 1:12–13"> For you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “For in Christ Jesus you are all sons of God through faith”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 13:14; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Romans 6:3–4"> for as many of you as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12–13; Colossians 3:11; Romans 3:29–30"> There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is neither male and female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 3:7"> And if you are Christ’s, then you are descendants of Abraham, heirs according to the promise.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
