## Carry One Another’s Burdens

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 2 Timothy 2:25; 2 Thessalonians 3:15"> Brothers, even if a person is caught in some trespass, you who are spiritual restore such a person in a spirit of humility, looking out for yourself, lest you also be tempted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; John 13:34"> Carry the burdens of one another, and so fulfill the law of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Corinthians 8:2; 1 Corinthians 3:18"> For if anyone thinks he is something although he is nothing, he is deceiving himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:5; Psalm 26:2; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> But let each one examine his own work, and then he will have a reason for boasting in himself alone, and not in someone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:19; Romans 14:10–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10–11"> For each one will carry his own burden.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Good to All People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:17–18; Romans 15:27; Deuteronomy 12:19"> Now the one who is taught the word must share in all good things with the one who teaches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12; Job 4:8"> Do not be deceived: God is not to be mocked, for whatever a person sows, this he will also reap,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:13; James 3:18; 2 Peter 2:19"> because the one who sows to his own flesh will reap corruption from the flesh, but the one who sows to the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; Isaiah 40:30–31"> And let us not grow weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap, if we do not give up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 3:27; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> So then, as we have opportunity, let us do good to all people, and especially to those who belong to the household of faith.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Final Warning

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21–23; Romans 16:22"> See with what large letters I am writing to you with my own hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:11; Colossians 2:23; Acts 15:1"> As many as are wanting to make a good showing in the flesh, these are attempting to compel you to be circumcised, only so that they will not be persecuted for the cross of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Romans 2:17–25"> For not even those who are circumcised observe the law themselves, but they want you to be circumcised in order that they may boast in your flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Jeremiah 9:23–24; Galatians 2:20"> But as for me, may it never be that I boast, except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Colossians 3:10–11; Ephesians 2:10"> For neither is circumcision anything nor uncircumcision, but a new creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 3:29; Romans 9:6–8; Philippians 3:3"> And all those who follow this rule, peace and mercy be on them and on the Israel of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Conclusion and Benediction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:5; Colossians 1:24; 2 Corinthians 11:23–25"> Finally, let no one cause me trouble, for I carry on my body the marks of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 2 Timothy 4:22; Philemon 1:25"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
