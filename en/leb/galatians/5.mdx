## Live in Freedom and Love One Another

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:13; 1 Peter 2:16"> For freedom Christ has set us free. Stand firm, therefore, and do not be subject again to a yoke of slavery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:1; Romans 9:31–32"> Look! I, Paul, tell you that if you become circumcised, Christ will profit you nothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 2:25; James 2:10–11"> And again I testify to every man who becomes circumcised, that he is under obligation to keep the whole law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 9:31–32; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Romans 11:6"> You are estranged from Christ, you who are attempting to be justified by the law; you have fallen from grace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 130:5; 1 Thessalonians 1:10; Psalm 62:5"> For through the Spirit by faith we eagerly await the hope of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 7:19; James 2:14–26"> For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision counts for anything nor uncircumcision, but faith working through love.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:24; Galatians 3:1; Hebrews 12:1"> You were running well. Who hindered you from obeying the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; Romans 8:28"> This persuasion is not from the one who calls you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 5:6–7; Mark 8:15"> A little leaven leavens the whole batch of dough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; 2 Corinthians 2:3; Galatians 1:7"> I have confidence in you in the Lord that you will think nothing different, but the one who is confusing you ⸤will pay the penalty⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “will bear the = his condemnation”">a</a></sup>, whoever he may be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Galatians 6:12; Galatians 4:29"> Now, brothers, if I am still preaching circumcision, why am I still being persecuted? In that case the stumbling block of the cross has been abolished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 5:13; Titus 3:10"> I wish that the ones who are disturbing you would also ⸤castrate themselves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “cut themselves off”">b</a></sup>!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 9:19; 1 Corinthians 8:9"> For you were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not let your freedom become an opportunity for the flesh, but through love serve one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 6:2"> For the whole law is fulfilled in one statement, namely, “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 19:18">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; James 3:14–3; Galatians 5:26"> But if you bite and devour one another, watch out that you are not consumed by one another.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Live by the Spirit and Reject the Deeds of the Flesh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:24–25; Romans 8:12–14; Galatians 6:8"> But I say, live by the Spirit, and you will never carry out the desire of the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:13; John 3:6; Romans 8:5–8"> For the flesh desires against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh, for these are in opposition to one another, so that whatever you want, you may not do these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:14; Romans 6:14–15; Galatians 5:25"> But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Colossians 3:5–8; Matthew 15:18–19"> Now the deeds of the flesh are evident, which are sexual immorality, impurity, licentiousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Revelation 21:8"> idolatry, sorcery, enmity, strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, selfish ambition, dissension, factions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Romans 8:13; Romans 13:13"> envy, drunkenness, carousing, and things like these, things which I am telling you in advance, just as I said before, that the ones who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–17; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; John 15:5"> But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:9; Acts 24:25; Titus 2:2"> gentleness, self control. Against such things there is no law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Romans 13:14; Romans 8:13"> Now those ⸤who belong to Christ⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “of Christ”">d</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Christ Jesus”">e</a></sup>have crucified the flesh together with its feelings and its desires.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:16; Romans 8:4–5; Romans 8:2"> If we live by the Spirit, we must also follow the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 3:14–16; Philippians 2:1–3; James 4:16"> We must not become conceited, provoking one another, envying one another.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
