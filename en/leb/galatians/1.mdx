## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:1; Romans 1:1"> Paul, an apostle not from men nor by men but through Jesus Christ and God the Father who raised him from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:1; Philippians 4:21; Acts 16:5–6"> and all the brothers with me, to the churches of Galatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:3; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 1:7–15"> Grace to you and peace from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 4:25; Galatians 2:20"> who gave himself for our sins in order to rescue us from the present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Jude 1:25; Psalm 72:19"> to whom be the glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Different Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; Galatians 5:7–8; Isaiah 29:13"> I am astonished that you are turning away so quickly from the one who called you by the grace of Christ to a different gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 15:24; Romans 16:17–18; Galatians 5:10"> not that there is a different gospel, except there are some who are disturbing you and wanting to distort the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13–14; Galatians 1:9; Revelation 22:18–19"> But even if we or an angel from heaven should proclaim a gospel to you contrary to what we proclaimed to you, let him be accursed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Proverbs 30:6; Deuteronomy 4:2"> As we said before, and now I say again, if anyone is proclaiming a gospel to you contrary to what you have received, let him be accursed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Acts 5:29; Ephesians 6:6"> For am I now making an appeal to people or to God? Or am I seeking to please people? If I were still trying to please people, I would not be a slave of Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Defense of His Apostleship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 11:23"> For I make known to you, brothers, the gospel that has been proclaimed by me, that it is not ⸤of human origin⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “according to man”">b</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:16; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Galatians 1:1"> For neither did I receive it from man, nor was I taught it, but I received it through a revelation of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Acts 22:3–5"> For you have heard about my former way of life in Judaism, that to an extraordinary degree I was persecuting the church of God, and trying to destroy it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:4–6"> and was progressing in Judaism beyond many contemporaries in my nation, because I was a far more zealous adherent of the traditions handed down by my forefathers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:5; Isaiah 49:1"> But when the one who set me apart<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “when God who set me apart”">c</a></sup>from my mother’s womb and called me by his grace was pleased</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 3:5–10; Ephesians 6:12"> to reveal his Son in me in order that I would proclaim the gospel about him among the Gentiles, immediately I did not consult with flesh and blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:20–25; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33; Galatians 1:18"> nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went away to Arabia and I returned again to Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:22–23; Acts 9:26–29; Acts 22:17–18"> Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to become acquainted with Cephas, and I stayed with him fifteen days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3; Matthew 12:46"> but I did not see any others of the apostles except James, the brother of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 11:10–11"> (Now the things which I am writing to you, behold, I assure you before God that I am not lying.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 15:41; Acts 9:30; Acts 6:9"> Then I came to the regions of Syria and of Cilicia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; Romans 16:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1"> but I was unknown ⸤in person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “by face”">d</a></sup>to the churches of Judea that are in Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:8–10; Acts 9:13; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> and they were only hearing, “The one formerly persecuting us is now proclaiming the faith that formerly he was attempting to destroy,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Acts 11:18; Luke 15:10"> and they were glorifying God because of me.</span>
</div>
</div>
