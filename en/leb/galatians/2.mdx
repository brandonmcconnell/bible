## Paul’s Ministry Recognized by the Jerusalem Apostles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 2:3; Acts 14:12; 2 Corinthians 8:23"> Then after fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking along Titus also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 2:6; Philippians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:5"> Now I went up there because of a revelation and laid out to them the gospel that I preach among the Gentiles, but in private to the influential people, lest somehow I was running, or had run, in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:2–6; Acts 16:3; 1 Corinthians 9:20–21"> But not even Titus who was with me, although he was a Greek, was compelled to be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jude 1:4; Galatians 5:1; Galatians 4:3"> Now this was because of the false brothers secretly brought in, who slipped in to spy out our freedom that we have in Christ Jesus, in order that they might enslave us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 2:14; Galatians 4:16; Colossians 1:5"> to whom not even for an hour did we yield in subjection, in order that the truth of the gospel might remain continually with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:2; Acts 10:34; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> But from those ⸤who were influential⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “who were thought to be something”">a</a></sup>(whatever they were, it makes no difference to me, ⸤God does not show partiality⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “God does not receive the face of man”">b</a></sup>)—for those who were influential added nothing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Galatians 1:16"> But these, when they saw that I had been entrusted with the gospel to the uncircumcision, just as Peter to the circumcision</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Acts 22:21; Acts 2:14–41"> (for the one who was at work through Peter for his apostleship to the circumcision was at work also through me for the Gentiles),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 12:3; Revelation 3:12; Galatians 2:2"> and when James and Cephas and John—those thought to be pillars—acknowledged the grace given to me, they gave to me and Barnabas the right hand of fellowship, in order that we should go to the Gentiles and they to the circumcision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 24:17; 1 John 3:17; Hebrews 13:16"> They asked only that we should remember the poor, the very thing I was also eager to do.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Confronts Peter at Antioch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 5:16; Acts 15:1"> But when Cephas came to Antioch, I opposed him to his face, because he was condemned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Luke 15:2; Acts 11:2–3"> For before certain people came from James, he used to eat with the Gentiles, but when they came, he withdrew and separated himself, because he was afraid of those who were of the circumcision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 4:14; Hebrews 13:9"> and the rest of the Jews also joined in this hypocrisy with him, so that even Barnabas was carried away with them in their hypocrisy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 2:5; Acts 10:28; Acts 15:10–11"> But when I saw that they were not being straightforward with the truth of the gospel, I said to Cephas in the presence of them all, “If you, although you are a Jew, live like a Gentile and not like a Jew, how can you try to compel the Gentiles to live like Jews?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Justified by Faith in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 2:3; Ephesians 2:11–12"> We are Jews by nature and not sinners from among the Gentiles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Psalm 143:2; Romans 9:30"> but knowing that a person is not justified by the works of the law, if not by faith in Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “by the faithfulness of Jesus Christ”">c</a></sup>and we have believed in Christ Jesus so that we may be justified by faith in Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “by the faithfulness of Christ”">d</a></sup>and not by the works of the law, because by the works of the law ⸤no human being will be justified⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “all flesh will not be justified”">e</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:1–2; 1 John 3:8–10; 1 John 3:5"> But if while seeking to be justified by Christ, we ourselves also have been found to be sinners, then is Christ an agent of sin? May it never be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 4:9–12; Galatians 2:4–5; Galatians 2:12–16"> For if I build up again these things which I destroyed, I show myself to be a transgressor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:4; Romans 6:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> For through the law I died to the law, in order that I might live to God. I have been crucified with Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 5:24; 2 Corinthians 5:15; Romans 6:4–6"> and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me, and that life I now live in the flesh, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 11:6; Galatians 3:21; Hebrews 7:11"> I do not declare invalid the grace of God, for if righteousness is through the law, then Christ died to no purpose.</span>
</div>
</div>
