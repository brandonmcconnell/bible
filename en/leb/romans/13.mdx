## Obedience to the Governing Authorities

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 3:1; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Daniel 2:21"> Let every person be subject to the governing authorities, for there is no authority except by God, and those that exist are put in place by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13; Titus 3:1; Romans 13:5"> So then, the one who resists authority resists the ordinance which is from God, and those who resist will receive condemnation on themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4–6; 1 Peter 2:13–14; Proverbs 20:2"> For rulers are not a cause of terror for a good deed, but for bad conduct. So do you want not to be afraid of authority? Do what is good, and you will have praise from it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:19; 2 Chronicles 19:6; Proverbs 16:14"> for it is God’s servant to you for what is good. But if you do what is bad, be afraid, because it does not bear the sword to no purpose. For it is God’s servant, the one who avenges for punishment on the one who does what is bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Acts 24:16; 1 Peter 2:19"> Therefore it is necessary to be in subjection, not only because of wrath but also because of conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:17–21; Mark 12:14–17; Matthew 17:24–27"> For because of this you also pay taxes, for the authorities<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “they”">a</a></sup>are servants of God, busily engaged in this very thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 1 Peter 2:17–18; Luke 20:25"> Pay to everyone what is owed: pay taxes to whom taxes are due; pay customs duties to whom customs duties are due; pay respect to whom respect is due; pay honor to whom honor is due.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Due to the very compressed style in this verse, many words must be supplied to make sense in English">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Love Fulfills the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Matthew 22:39–40; Galatians 5:14"> Owe nothing to anyone, except to love one another, for the one who loves someone else has fulfilled the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Matthew 22:39; Luke 10:27"> For the commandments, “You shall not commit adultery, you shall not commit murder, you shall not steal, you shall not covet,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 20:13–15 , 17 ; Deut 5:17–19 , 21">c</a></sup>and if there is any other commandment, are summed up in this statement: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 19:18">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:39–40; Romans 13:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> Love does not commit evil against a neighbor. Therefore love is the fulfillment of the law.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Peter 4:7; Ephesians 5:14"> And do this because you know the time, that it is already the hour for you to wake up from sleep. For our salvation is nearer now than when we believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31–32; Ephesians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> The night is far gone, and the day has drawn near. Therefore let us throw off<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “let us lay aside”">e</a></sup>the deeds of darkness and put on the weapons of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5"> Let us live decently, as in the day, not in carousing and drunkenness, not in sexual immorality and licentiousness, not in strife and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 3:27"> But put on the Lord Jesus Christ and do not make provision for the desires of the flesh.</span>
</div>
</div>
