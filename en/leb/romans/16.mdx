## Many Personal Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:18; 1 Peter 1:22–23; 1 Timothy 5:9–10"> Now I commend to you Phoebe our sister, who is also a servant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “a deaconess”; some interpreters understand this term to refer to a specific office (deacon/deaconess) which Phoebe held in the local church at Cenchrea">a</a></sup>of the church in Cenchrea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:29; Philippians 1:27; Matthew 10:40–42"> in order that you may welcome her in the Lord in a manner worthy of the saints, and help her in whatever task she may have need from you, for she herself also has been a helper of many, even me myself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19; Acts 18:26"> Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 2:30; 1 John 3:16; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> who risked their own necks for my life, for which not only I am thankful, but also all the churches of the Gentiles;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 4:15; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19"> also greet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The verb is supplied as an understood repetition from v. 3">b</a></sup>the church in their house. Greet Epenetus my dear friend, who is ⸤the first convert⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the first fruits”">c</a></sup>of Asia for Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Matthew 27:55; 1 Timothy 5:10"> Greet Mary, who ⸤has worked hard⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “has labored much”">d</a></sup>for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 16:11; Romans 16:21; Philemon 1:23"> Greet Andronicus and Junia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “Junias,” the masculine form of the same name">e</a></sup>my compatriots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “relatives”">f</a></sup>and my fellow prisoners, who are well known to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “are outstanding among”">g</a></sup>the apostles, who were also in Christ before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 John 3:14; Romans 16:5"> Greet Ampliatus, my dear friend in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:2–3; Romans 16:21"> Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and my dear friend Stachys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Greet Apelles, who is approved in Christ. Greet those of the household of Aristobulus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:21; Romans 16:7"> Greet Herodion my compatriot.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “relative”">h</a></sup>Greet those of the household of Narcissus who are in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 6:10–11; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Greet Tryphena and Tryphosa, the laborers in the Lord. Greet Persis, the dear friend who ⸤has worked hard⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “has labored much”">i</a></sup>in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:21; 1 Timothy 5:2; 2 John 1:1"> Greet Rufus, the chosen one in the Lord, and his mother and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Romans 8:29; Hebrews 3:1"> Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:2; Romans 1:7; Romans 16:14"> Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints who are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:12"> Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the churches of Christ greet you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Concluding Exhortations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> Now I exhort you, brothers, to look out for those who cause dissensions and temptations contrary to the teaching which you learned, and stay away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:4; Matthew 7:15; 1 Timothy 6:5"> For such people do not serve our Lord Christ, but their own stomach, and by smooth speech and flattery they deceive the hearts of the unsuspecting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; 1 Corinthians 14:20; Philippians 2:15"> For the report of your obedience has reached to all; therefore I am rejoicing over you, and I want you to be wise toward what is good, but innocent toward what is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:19; 1 John 3:8; Genesis 3:15"> And in a short time the God of peace will crush Satan under your feet. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “Christ”">j</a></sup>be with you.</span>
</div>
</div>
