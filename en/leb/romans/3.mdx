## Jews Still Have an Advantage

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 2:25–29; Genesis 25:32; Ecclesiastes 6:11"> Therefore, what is the advantage of the Jew, or what is the use of circumcision?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 9:4; Acts 7:38; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> Much in every way. For first, that they were entrusted with the oracles of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 4:2; Romans 9:6"> ⸤What is the result⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “for what”">a</a></sup>if some refused to believe? Their unbelief will not nullify the faithfulness of God, will it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 51:4; Psalm 116:11; Galatians 2:17"> May it never be! But let God be true but every human being a liar, just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“In order that you may be justified in your words,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and may prevail when you are judged.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 51:4">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 6:19; Galatians 3:15; Romans 9:18–20"> But if our unrighteousness demonstrates the righteousness of God, what shall we say? God, who inflicts wrath, is not unjust, is he? (I am speaking according to a human perspective.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Acts 17:31; Job 8:3"> May it never be! For otherwise, how will God judge the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:4; Romans 9:19–20; Acts 13:27–29"> But if by my lying, the truth of God abounded to his glory, why am I also still condemned as a sinner?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 6:1; Romans 6:15; Romans 5:20"> And why not (as we are slandered, and as some affirm that we say), “Let us do evil, in order that good may come of it? Their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “whose”">c</a></sup>condemnation is just!</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Entire World Guilty of Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 6:15; Isaiah 65:5"> What then? Do we have an advantage? Not at all. For we have already charged both Jews and Greeks are all under sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 53:1–3; Psalm 14:1–3; Romans 3:23"> just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There is no one righteous, not even one;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 14:2–4; Psalm 53:2; Titus 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-2">there is no one who understands;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is no one who seeks God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 14:3; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Isaiah 64:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">All have turned aside together; they have become worthless;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There is no one who practices kindness;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is not even one.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Verses 10–12 are a quotation from Ps 14:1–3">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 5:9; Psalm 140:3; Matthew 23:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their throat is an opened grave;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they deceive with their tongues;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the venom of asps is under their lips,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 5:9 and Ps 140:3">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 10:7; Psalm 109:17–18; Psalm 59:12"> <span className="pl pl-2">whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 10:7">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Proverbs 1:16; Proverbs 6:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their feet are swift to shed blood;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-2">destruction and distress are in their paths,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 59:8; Romans 5:1; Luke 1:79"> <span className="pl pl-1">and they have not known the way of peace.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Verses 15–17 are a quotation from Isa 59:7–8">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 36:1; Proverbs 23:17; Proverbs 16:6"> <span className="pl pl-2">The fear of God is not before their eyes.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 36:1">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:22–23; Romans 2:12–18"> Now we know that whatever the law says, it speaks to those under the law, in order that every mouth may be closed and the whole world may become accountable to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Acts 13:39; Romans 3:28"> For by the works of the law ⸤no person will be declared righteous⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “all flesh will not be declared righteous”">i</a></sup>before him, for through the law comes knowledge of sin.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Righteousness through Faith Revealed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Jeremiah 33:16; Philippians 3:9"> But now, apart from the law, the righteousness of God has been revealed, being testified about by the law and the prophets—</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Galatians 3:28; Romans 8:1"> that is, the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “through the faithfulness of Jesus Christ”">j</a></sup>to all who believe. For there is no distinction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; 1 John 1:8–10; Romans 3:9"> for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 3:5–7; Colossians 1:14"> being justified as a gift by his grace, through the redemption which is in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:23–24; 1 Peter 1:18–20; 1 John 4:10"> whom God made publicly available as the mercy seat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “as the place of propitiation”">k</a></sup>through faith in his blood, for a demonstration of his righteousness, because of the passing over of previously committed sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Romans 4:5; Romans 3:30"> in the forbearance of God, for the demonstration of his righteousness in the present time, so that he should be just and the one who justifies the person by faith<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “by Jesus’ faithfulness”">l</a></sup>in Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 4:2; Romans 2:23; 1 Corinthians 1:29–31"> Therefore, where is boasting? It has been excluded. By what kind of law? Of works? No, but by a law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “a principle”">m</a></sup>of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 2:16; Galatians 3:8; Titus 3:7"> For we consider a person to be justified by faith apart from the works of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Zechariah 2:11"> Or is God the God of the Jews only? Is he not also the God of the Gentiles? Yes, also of the Gentiles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:20; Philippians 3:3"> since God is one, who will justify ⸤those who are circumcised⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “circumcision”">n</a></sup>by faith and ⸤those who are uncircumcised⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “uncircumcision”">o</a></sup>through faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Matthew 5:17; Romans 13:8–10"> Therefore, do we nullify the law through faith? May it never be! But we uphold the law.</span>
</div>
</div>
