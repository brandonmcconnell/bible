## Do Not Pass Judgment on One Another

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 8:7–13; 1 Corinthians 9:22"> Now receive the one who is weak in faith, but not for quarrels about opinions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:22–23; Romans 14:14"> One believes he may eat all things, but the one who is weak eats only vegetables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:10; Colossians 2:16–17; Luke 18:9"> The one who eats must not despise the one who does not eat, and the one who does not eat must not judge the one who eats, because God has accepted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:11–12; Romans 9:20; Romans 8:31–39"> Who are you, who passes judgment on the domestic slave belonging to someone else? To his own master he stands or falls, and he will stand, for the Lord is able to make him stand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> One person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “For one person”">a</a></sup>prefers one day over another day, and another person regards every day alike. Each one must be fully convinced in his own mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–5; Matthew 14:19"> The one who is intent on the day is intent on it for the Lord, and the one who eats eats for the Lord, because he is thankful to God, and the one who does not eat does not eat for the Lord, and he is thankful to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> For none of us lives for himself and none dies for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> For if we live, we live for the Lord, and if we die, we die for the Lord. Therefore whether we live or whether we die, we are the Lord’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> For Christ died and became alive again for this reason, in order that he might be Lord of both the dead and the living.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> But why do you judge your brother? Or also, why do you despise your brother? For we will all stand before the judgment seat of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; Psalm 72:11"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“As I live, says the Lord, every knee will bow to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and every tongue will praise God.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 45:23">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:5; Matthew 12:36; Matthew 16:27"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “So then,”">c</a></sup>each one of us will give an account concerning himself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “an account concerning himself to God”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; James 4:11; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> Therefore, let us no longer pass judgment on one another, but rather decide this: not to place a cause for stumbling or a temptation before a brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Romans 14:2; 1 Timothy 4:4"> I know and am convinced in the Lord Jesus that nothing is unclean of itself, except to the one who considers something to be unclean; to that person it is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> For if because of food, your brother is grieved, you are no longer living according to love. Do not destroy by your food that person for whom Christ died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> Therefore do not let your good be slandered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> For the kingdom of God is not eating and drinking, but righteousness and peace and joy in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 5:11; 1 Peter 2:20"> For the one who serves Christ in this way is well-pleasing to God and approved by people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Romans 12:18; Psalm 34:14"> So then, let us pursue ⸤what promotes peace⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the things of peace”">e</a></sup>and ⸤what edifies one another⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the things of edification toward one another”">f</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 14:14–15; Matthew 18:6; Titus 1:15"> Do not destroy the work of God on account of food. All things are clean, but it is wrong for the person ⸤who eats and stumbles in the process⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “who eats with stumbling”">g</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; Romans 14:13; Romans 15:1–2"> It is good not to eat meat or to drink wine or to do anything by which your brother stumbles or is offended or is weakened.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “or is offended or is weakened”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 3:21; Romans 14:5; James 3:13"> The faith that you have, have with respect to yourself before God. Blessed is the one who does not pass judgment on himself by what he approves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 11:29–31"> But the one who doubts is condemned if he eats, because he does not do so from faith, and everything that is not from faith is sin.</span>
</div>
</div>
