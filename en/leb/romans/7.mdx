## Released from the Law through Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:6; Romans 6:3"> Or do you not know, brothers (for I am speaking to those who know the law), that the law is master of a person for as long a time as he lives?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 2:23–24; 1 Corinthians 7:39; 1 Corinthians 7:4"> For the married woman is bound by law to her husband while he lives, but if her husband dies, she is released from the law of the husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; Matthew 5:32; Mark 10:6–12"> Therefore as a result, if she belongs to another man while her husband is living, she will be called an adulteress. But if her husband dies, she is free from the law, so that she is not an adulteress if she belongs to another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 54:5"> So then, my brothers, you also were brought to death with respect to the law through the body of Christ, so that you may belong to another, to the one who was raised from the dead, in order that we may bear fruit for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 5:24"> For when we were in the flesh, sinful desires were working through the law in our members, to bear fruit for death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:23–25; 2 Corinthians 3:6; Romans 7:4"> But now we have been released from the law, because we have died to that by which we were bound, so that we may serve in newness of the Spirit and not in oldness of the letter of the law.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Knowledge of Sin Comes through the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56"> What then shall we say? Is the law sin? May it never be! But I would not have known sin except through the law, for I would not have known covetousness if the law had not said, “Do not covet.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 20:17 ; Deut 5:21">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:11; 1 Corinthians 15:56; Romans 4:15"> But sin, seizing an opportunity through the commandment, produced in me all kinds of covetousness. For apart from the law, sin is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 8:7; James 2:10–11"> And I was alive once, apart from the law, but when the commandment came, sin sprang to life</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:7; Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5"> and I died, and this commandment which was to lead to life was found with respect to me to lead to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 3:13; Romans 7:8; Hebrews 3:13"> For sin, seizing the opportunity through the commandment, deceived me and through it killed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:8; Psalm 119:137; Psalm 19:7–12"> So then, the law is holy, and the commandment is holy and righteous and good.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Internal Conflict with Sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:13–15; Galatians 3:21; Romans 8:3"> Therefore, did that which is good become death to me? May it never be! Rather it was sin, in order that it might be recognized as sin, producing death through what is good for me, in order that sin might become sinful to an extraordinary degree through the commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 21:25; 2 Kings 17:17; Romans 7:18"> For we know that the law is spiritual, but I am fleshly, ⸤sold into slavery to sin⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sold under sin”">b</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:19–20; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> For what I am doing I do not understand, because what I want to do, this I do not practice, but what I hate, this I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 7:12; Romans 7:14; 1 Timothy 1:8"> But if what I do not want to do, this I do, I agree with the law that it is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 7:20; Philippians 3:8–9; Romans 7:18"> But now I am no longer the one doing it, but sin that lives in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:6; Psalm 51:5; Galatians 5:17"> For I know that good does not live in me, that is, in my flesh. For the willing is present in me, but the doing of the good is not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:15"> For the good that I want to do, I do not do, but the evil that I do not want to do, this I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 7:17"> But if what I do not want to do, this I am doing, I am no longer the one doing it, but sin that lives in me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:23; Psalm 119:133"> Consequently, I find the principle with me, the one who wants to do good, that evil is present with me.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “in me”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 1:2; Psalm 40:8; Psalm 119:35"> For I joyfully agree with the law of God in my inner person,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; James 4:1; Romans 8:2"> but I observe another law in my members, at war with the law of my mind and making me captive to the law of sin that exists in my members.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; 2 Timothy 4:18; Titus 2:14"> Wretched man that I am! Who will rescue me from this body of death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:17–24; Colossians 3:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Thanks be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “But thanks be ”">d</a></sup>to God through Jesus Christ our Lord! So then, I myself with my mind am enslaved to the law of God, but with my flesh I am enslaved to the law of sin.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
