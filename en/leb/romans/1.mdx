## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 15:16; 1 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul, a slave of Christ Jesus, called to be an apostle, set apart for the gospel of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:2; Luke 1:70; Romans 3:21"> which he promised previously through his prophets in the holy scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Galatians 4:4; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> concerning his Son, who was born ⸤a descendant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “of the seed”">a</a></sup>of David according to the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Hebrews 5:5–6; Ephesians 1:19–23"> who was declared Son of God in power according to ⸤the Holy Spirit⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the Spirit of holiness”">b</a></sup>by the resurrection from the dead of Jesus Christ our Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Romans 16:26; Acts 6:7"> through whom we have received grace and apostleship for the obedience of faith among all the Gentiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “nations”; the same Greek word can be translated “nations” or “Gentiles” depending on the context">c</a></sup>on behalf of his name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 17:14; 2 Peter 1:10"> among whom you also are the called of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Jude 1:1–2; Ephesians 1:2"> To all those in Rome who are loved by God, called to be saints. Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Wants to Visit Rome

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 16:19; 1 Corinthians 1:4; 1 Thessalonians 1:8–9"> First, I give thanks to my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, because your faith is being proclaimed in the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; 2 Timothy 1:3; Ephesians 1:16–19"> For God, whom I serve with my spirit in the gospel of his Son, is my witness, how constantly I make mention of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 15:30–32; Acts 18:21; Philemon 1:22"> always asking in my prayers if somehow now at last I may succeed to come to you in the will of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Romans 16:25; Romans 15:29"> For I desire to see you, in order that I may impart some spiritual gift to you, in order to strengthen you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:7–10; 3 John 1:3–4; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> that is, to be encouraged together with you through ⸤our mutual faith⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the in one another faith”">d</a></sup>, both yours and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 19:21; Philippians 4:17; 2 Timothy 4:17"> Now I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, that often I intended to come to you, and was prevented until now, in order that I might have some fruit among you also, just as also among the rest of the Gentiles.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “nations”; the same Greek word can be translated “nations” or “Gentiles” depending on the context">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:16–23; Acts 9:15; Colossians 3:11"> I am under obligation both to Greeks and to barbarians, both to the wise and to the foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20; Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 9:17"> Thus ⸤I am eager⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the according to me eagerness”">f</a></sup>to proclaim the gospel also to you who are in Rome.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Gospel’s Power for Salvation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 8:38; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 4:16"> For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes, to the Jew first and also to the Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:4; Galatians 3:11; Hebrews 10:38"> For the righteousness of God is revealed in it from faith to faith, just as it is written, “But the one who is righteous by faith will live.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “But the one who is righteous will live by faith” (differing only in word order)">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Wrath Revealed Against Sinful Humanity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 5:6; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; Romans 1:32"> For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all impiety and unrighteousness of people, who suppress the truth in unrighteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:16–17; Psalm 19:1–6; Romans 1:20"> because what can be known about God is evident among<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “in”; or “within”">h</a></sup>them, for God made it clear to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:19; Isaiah 40:26; Psalm 19:1–6"> For from the creation of the world, his invisible attributes, both his eternal power and deity, are discerned clearly, being understood in the things created, so that they are without excuse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:17–18; 2 Kings 17:15; Jeremiah 2:5"> For although they knew God, they did not honor him as God or give thanks, but they became futile in their reasoning, and their senseless hearts were darkened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:19–21; Romans 11:25; Proverbs 26:12"> Claiming to be wise, they became fools,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 106:20; Jeremiah 2:11; Acts 17:29"> and exchanged the glory of the immortal God with the likeness of an image of mortal human beings and birds and quadrupeds and reptiles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 81:11–12; Acts 7:42; Acts 14:16"> Therefore God gave them over in the desires of their hearts to immorality, that their bodies would be dishonored among themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 13:25; 1 John 2:15–16"> who exchanged the truth of God with a lie, and worshiped and served the creation rather than the Creator, who is blessed for eternity. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God Hands Sinful Humanity over to Depravity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:10; Romans 1:24"> Because of this, God gave them over to degrading passions, for their females exchanged the natural relations for those contrary to nature,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Leviticus 18:22; Romans 1:23–24"> and likewise also the males, abandoning the natural relations with the female, were inflamed in their desire toward one another, males with males committing the shameless deed, and receiving in themselves the penalty that was necessary for their error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Romans 1:21; Titus 1:16"> And just as they did not see fit ⸤to recognize God⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to have God in recognition”">i</a></sup>, God gave them over to a debased mind, to do the things that are not proper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; Proverbs 16:28; Psalm 41:7"> being filled with all unrighteousness, wickedness, greediness, malice, full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, malevolence. They are gossipers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:2; Jude 1:16; 2 Peter 2:18"> slanderers, haters of God, insolent, arrogant, boasters, contrivers of evil, disobedient to parents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:3; Proverbs 18:2; Romans 3:11"> senseless, faithless, unfeeling, unmerciful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 2:1–5; Romans 1:21; Romans 6:21"> who, although they know the requirements of God, that those who do such things are worthy of death, not only do they do the same things, but also they approve of those who do them.</span>
</div>
</div>
