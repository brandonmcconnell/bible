## Accept One Another according to Christ’s Example

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; Romans 14:1; Galatians 6:1–2"> But we who are strong ought to bear the weaknesses of the weak, and not to please ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 10:24; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let each one of us please his neighbor for his good, for the purpose of edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 69:9; Philippians 2:8; John 4:34"> For even Christ did not please himself, but just as it is written, “The insults of those who insult you have fallen on me.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 69:9">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; Romans 4:23–24"> For whatever was written beforehand was written for our instruction, in order that through patient endurance and through the encouragement of the scriptures we may have hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 2:4–5; 1 Peter 3:8; Romans 12:16"> Now may the God of patient endurance and of encouragement grant you ⸤to be in agreement⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to think the same”">b</a></sup>with one another, in accordance with Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:29–30; Revelation 1:6; Ephesians 1:3"> so that with one mind you may glorify with one mouth the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:34; Romans 5:2"> Therefore accept one another, just as Christ also has accepted you, to the glory of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–5; Matthew 15:24; John 1:11"> For I say, Christ has become a servant of the circumcision on behalf of the truth of God, in order to confirm the promises to the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:50; Psalm 18:49; Romans 3:29"> and that the Gentiles may glorify God for his mercy, just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Because of this, I will praise you among the Gentiles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will sing praise to your name.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 18:49">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 67:3–4; Deuteronomy 32:43; Isaiah 42:10–12"> And again it says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rejoice, Gentiles, with his people.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 32:43">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 117:1"> And again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Praise the Lord, all the Gentiles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let all the peoples praise him.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 117:1">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 11:10; Isaiah 11:1; Matthew 12:21"> And again Isaiah says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The root of Jesse will come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even the one who rises to rule over the Gentiles;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in him the Gentiles will put their hope.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 11:10">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 9:8"> Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that you may abound in hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Ministry to the Gentiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 2 Peter 1:12; 1 John 2:21"> Now I myself also am convinced about you, my brothers, that you yourselves also are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, able also to instruct one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Romans 1:5"> But I have written to you more boldly on some points, so as to remind you again because of the grace that has been given to me by God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:13; Romans 1:1; Acts 9:15"> with the result that I am a servant of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, serving the gospel of God as a priest, in order that the offering of the Gentiles may become acceptable, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14–16; 2 Corinthians 12:11–21; Hebrews 5:1"> Therefore I have a reason for boasting in Christ Jesus regarding the things concerning God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Romans 1:5; Acts 21:19"> For I will not dare to speak about anything except that which Christ has accomplished through me, resulting in the obedience of the Gentiles by word and deed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4; Acts 19:11–12"> by the power of signs and wonders, by the power of the Spirit,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “of the Spirit of God”">g</a></sup>so that from Jerusalem and traveling around as far as Illyricum I have fully proclaimed the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 3:9–15; Ephesians 2:20–22"> And so, having as my ambition to proclaim the gospel where Christ has not been named, in order that I will not build on the foundation belonging to someone else,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 52:15; Isaiah 65:1"> but just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Those to whom it was not announced concerning him will see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and those who have not heard will understand.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 52:15">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Travel Plans

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 1:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:17–18"> For this reason also I was hindered many times from coming to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 15:32; Romans 1:10–12; Romans 15:29"> and now, no longer having a place in these regions, but having a desire for many years to come to you</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:28; Romans 1:12; Acts 15:3"> whenever I travel to Spain. For I hope while I am passing through to see you and to be sent on my way by you, whenever I have first enjoyed your company for a while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 24:17; Acts 19:21; Acts 20:22"> But now I am traveling to Jerusalem, serving the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:1–9; Acts 11:27–30; 2 Corinthians 9:2"> For Macedonia and Achaia were pleased to make some contribution for the poor among the saints in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:11; Galatians 6:6; Philemon 1:19"> For they were pleased to do so, and they are obligated to them. For if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual things, they ought also to serve them in material things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 15:24; Philippians 4:17; Colossians 1:6"> Therefore, after I have accomplished this and sealed this fruit for delivery to them, I will depart by way of you for Spain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 1:10–12; Psalm 16:11"> and I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of the blessing of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 1:11; 2 Corinthians 4:11"> Now I exhort you, brothers, through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the love of the Spirit, to contend along with me in your prayers on my behalf to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:2; 2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 9:1"> that I may be rescued from those who are disobedient in Judea, and my ministry in Jerusalem may be acceptable to the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:18; Philemon 1:20; Philemon 1:7"> so that, coming to you with joy by the will of God, I may rest with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; Philippians 4:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23"> Now may the God of peace be with all of you. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
