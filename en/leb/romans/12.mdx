<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Romans 6:13; Hebrews 13:15–16"> Therefore I exhort you, brothers, through the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God, which is your reasonable service.</span>

## A Variety of Gifts in the Body of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:3; Philippians 2:3–8; Proverbs 26:12"> For by the grace given to me I say to everyone who is among you not to think more highly of yourself than what one ought to think, but to think ⸤sensibly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “so as to be sensible”">a</a></sup>, as God has apportioned a measure of faith to each one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; 1 Corinthians 12:12–14; Ephesians 4:15–16"> For just as in one body we have many members, but all the members do not have the same function,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:20; 1 Corinthians 10:17; 1 Corinthians 12:27–28"> in the same way we who are many are one body in Christ, and ⸤individually⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “with respect to one”">b</a></sup>members of one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; 1 Corinthians 7:7; 1 Corinthians 12:28–31"> but having different gifts according to the grace given to us: if it is prophecy, according to the proportion of his faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 13:1"> if it is service, by service; if it is one who teaches, by teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Timothy 5:17; 1 Corinthians 12:28"> if it is one who exhorts, by exhortation; one who gives, with sincerity; one who leads, with diligence; one who shows mercy, with cheerfulness.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Living in Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 34:14; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Love must be without hypocrisy. Abhor what is evil; be attached to what is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; John 13:34–35; Hebrews 13:1"> being devoted to one another in brotherly love, esteeming one another more highly in honor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10–11; Acts 18:25; Revelation 3:15–16"> not lagging in diligence, being enthusiastic in spirit, serving the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Hebrews 10:36; Psalm 62:8"> rejoicing in hope, enduring in affliction, being devoted to prayer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:12; 1 John 3:17"> contributing to the needs of the saints, pursuing hospitality.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Bless those who persecute,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “who persecute you”">c</a></sup>bless and do not curse them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Hebrews 13:3; Psalm 35:13–14"> Rejoice with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:8; Proverbs 3:7; James 3:13–17"> Think the same thing toward one another; ⸤do not think arrogantly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “think not the arrogant”">d</a></sup>, but associate with the lowly. Do not be wise ⸤in your own sight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in the sight of yourselves”">e</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:39"> Pay back no one evil for evil. Take thought for what is good in the sight of all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Matthew 5:9; Romans 14:19"> If it is possible on your part, be at peace with all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35; Hebrews 10:30; Proverbs 24:29"> Do not take revenge yourselves, dear friends, but give place to God’s wrath, for it is written, “Vengeance is mine, I will repay,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 32:35">f</a></sup>says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 25:21–22; Matthew 5:44; Luke 6:27"> But “if your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him something to drink; for by doing this, you will heap up coals of fire upon his head.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Prov 25:21–22">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–30; Proverbs 16:32"> Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.</span>
</div>
