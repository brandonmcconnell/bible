## Israel’s Rejection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:7; Romans 1:9; Galatians 1:20"> I am telling the truth in Christ—I am not lying; my conscience bears witness to me in the Holy Spirit—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:18; Psalm 119:136; Jeremiah 13:17"> that my grief is great and there is constant distress in my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 32:32; Esther 8:6; Romans 11:14"> For I could wish myself to be accursed from Christ for the sake of my brothers, my fellow countrymen according to the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Psalm 147:19; Romans 9:6"> who are Israelites, to whom belong the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the giving of the law, and the temple service, and the promises,</span>

Amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 2:28–29; Galatians 6:16; Romans 11:1–2"> But it is not as if the word of God had failed. For not all those who are descended from Israel are truly Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Hebrews 11:18; John 8:37–39"> nor are they all children because they are descendants of Abraham, but “In Isaac will your descendants be named.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 21:12">b</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Genesis 18:14; Genesis 17:21"> For the statement of the promise is this: “At this time I will return and ⸤Sarah will have⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “there will be to Sarah”">d</a></sup>a son.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 18:10 , 14">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 25:21–23; Luke 16:26; Romans 5:11"> And not only this, but also when Rebecca conceived children by one man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or perhaps “by one act of sexual intercourse ”">f</a></sup>Isaac our father—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> for although they had not yet been born, or done anything good or evil, in order that the purpose of God according to election might remain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:22–23; 2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Kings 22:47"> not by works but by the one who calls—it was said to her, “The older will serve the younger,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 25:23">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Deuteronomy 21:15; Genesis 29:33"> just as it is written, “Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from Mal 1:2–3">h</a></sup></span>

## God’s Sovereign Choice to Show Mercy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:7; Psalm 145:17; Deuteronomy 32:4"> What then shall we say? There is no injustice with God, is there? May it never be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 33:19; Romans 9:16; Micah 7:18"> For to Moses he says, “I will have mercy on whomever I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whomever I have compassion.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 33:19">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; Matthew 11:25–26; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> Consequently therefore, ⸤it does not depend on the⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “not of the”">j</a></sup>one who wills or on the one who runs, but on God who shows mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Exodus 14:17–18"> For the scripture says to Pharaoh, “For this very reason I have raised you up, so that I may demonstrate my power in you, and so that my name might be proclaimed in all the earth.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 9:16">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Exodus 4:21; Romans 9:15–16"> Consequently therefore, he has mercy on whomever he wishes, and he hardens whomever he wishes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:35; 2 Chronicles 20:6; 1 Corinthians 15:12"> Therefore you will say to me, “Why then does he still find fault? For who has resisted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “who resists”">l</a></sup>his will?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 29:16; Isaiah 45:9–11; Isaiah 64:8"> On the contrary, O man, who are you who answers back to God? Will what is molded say to the one who molded it, “Why did you make me like this”?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 29:16 ; 45:9">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; Proverbs 16:4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> Or does the potter not have authority over the clay, to make from the same lump a vessel that is for ⸤honorable use⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “honor”">n</a></sup>and one that is for ⸤ordinary use⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “dishonor”">o</a></sup>?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 16:4; Jude 1:4; Romans 9:21"> And what if God, wanting to demonstrate his wrath and to make known his power, endured with much patience vessels of wrath prepared for destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 1 Peter 1:2–5; Colossians 1:27"> And he did so in order that he could make known the riches of his glory upon vessels of mercy that he prepared beforehand for glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Acts 13:47–48; Ephesians 3:6–8"> us whom he also called, not only from the Jews but also from the Gentiles?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; 1 Peter 2:10; John 16:27"> As he also says in Hosea,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will call those who were not my people, ‘My people,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who were not loved, ‘Loved.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="A quotation from Hos 2:23">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 1:9–10; 1 John 3:1–3; Galatians 3:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it will be in the place where it was said to them, ‘You are not my people,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there they will be called ‘sons of the living God.’ ”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="A quotation from Hos 1:10">q</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 10:20–23; Romans 11:4–6; Isaiah 1:9"> And Isaiah cries out concerning Israel,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Even if the number of the sons of Israel is like the sand of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the remnant will be saved,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 10:23; Revelation 19:11; Acts 17:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤for the Lord will execute his sentence thoroughly and decisively⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “for the Lord will act, closing the account and cutting short”">r</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">upon the earth.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="A paraphrased quotation from Isa 10:22–23">s</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> And just as Isaiah foretold,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“If the Lord of hosts had not left us descendants,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we would have become like Sodom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and would have resembled Gomorrah.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 1:9">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Hebrews 11:7; Galatians 2:16"> What then shall we say? That the Gentiles, who did not pursue righteousness, attained righteousness—even the righteousness that is by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:7; Romans 3:20; James 2:10–11"> But Israel, pursuing the law of righteousness, did not attain to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:8; Luke 7:23"> Why that? Because they did not pursue it by faith, but as if by works. They stumbled over the ⸤stone that causes people to stumble⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “stone of stumbling”">u</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Isaiah 8:14–15; Romans 10:11"> just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I am laying in Zion ⸤a stone that causes people to stumble⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “a stone of stumbling”">v</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ⸤a rock that causes them to fall⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “a rock of offense”">w</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the one who believes in him will not be put to shame.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 28:16 ; 8:14">x</a></sup></span>
</div>
