## A Remnant of Israel Remains

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:22; 1 Samuel 12:22; Philippians 3:5"> Therefore I say, God has not rejected his people, has he? May it never be! For I also am an Israelite, from the descendants of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 94:14; Romans 8:29–30; Exodus 32:1"> God has not rejected his people, whom he foreknew! Or do you not know, in the passage about Elijah, what the scripture says—how he appeals to God against Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10–18; 1 Kings 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> “Lord, they have killed your prophets, they have torn down your altars, and I alone am left, and they are seeking my life!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from 1 Kgs 19:10 , 14">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 19:18; Numbers 25:3; Zephaniah 1:4"> But what does the divine response say to him? “I have left for myself seven thousand people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or perhaps “males,” referring to men only">b</a></sup>who have not bent the knee to Baal.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from 1 Kgs 19:18">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 9:27; Ephesians 1:5–6; Romans 9:11"> So in this way also at the present time, there is a remnant ⸤selected by grace⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “according to selection of grace”">d</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 2:21; Romans 4:4–5; 2 Timothy 1:9"> But if by grace, it is no longer by works, for otherwise grace would no longer be grace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Romans 11:5; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> What then? What Israel was searching for, this it did not obtain. But the elect obtained it, and the rest were hardened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; Ezekiel 12:2"> just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God gave them a spirit of stupor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">eyes that do not see and ears that do not hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until this very day.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 29:4 ; Isa 29:10">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 69:22–23; Luke 12:20; Psalm 28:4"> And David says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let their table become a snare and a trap,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a cause for stumbling and a retribution to them;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 69:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; 2 Peter 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">let their eyes be darkened so that they do not see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cause their backs to bend ⸤continually⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “throughout everything”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 69:22–23">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 13:46–48; Romans 11:31; Romans 10:19"> I say then, they did not stumble so that they fell, did they? May it never be! But by their trespass, salvation has come to the Gentiles, in order to provoke them to jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:25; Colossians 1:27; Romans 11:15"> And if their trespass means riches for the world and their loss means riches for the Gentiles, how much more will their fullness mean?</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gentile Branches Grafted in

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11–12; Ephesians 3:8"> Now I am speaking to you Gentiles. Therefore, inasmuch as I am apostle to the Gentiles, I promote my ministry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:16; 1 Timothy 4:16; Romans 9:3"> if somehow I may provoke my people to jealousy and save some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Romans 5:10–11; Colossians 1:20–21"> For if their rejection means the reconciliation of the world, what will their acceptance mean except life from the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:30; James 1:18; Proverbs 3:9"> Now if the first fruits are holy, so also is the whole batch of dough, and if the root is holy, so also are the branches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 2:11–13; Ephesians 3:6; Matthew 21:43"> Now if some of the branches were broken off, and you, although you were a wild olive tree, were grafted in among them and became a sharer of the root of the olive tree’s richness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:12; Romans 11:20; Ephesians 2:19–20"> do not boast against the branches. But if you boast against them, you do not support the root, but the root supports you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 11:17; Romans 11:11–12; Romans 11:23–24"> Then you will say, “Branches were broken off in order that I could be grafted in.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:16; 2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 10:12"> Well said! They were broken off because of unbelief, but you stand firm because of faith. Do not think arrogant thoughts, but be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4–9; Jeremiah 25:29; Jeremiah 49:12"> For if God did not spare the ⸤natural⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “according to nature”">h</a></sup>branches, neither will he spare you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “perhaps he will not spare you either”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; 1 Corinthians 15:2"> See, then, the kindness and severity of God: severity upon those who have fallen, but upon you the kindness of God—if you continue in his kindness, for otherwise you also will be cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:16; Zechariah 12:10; Matthew 23:39"> And those also, if they do not persist in unbelief, will be grafted in, because God is able to graft them in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 11:17–18; Romans 11:30"> For if you were cut off from what is by nature a wild olive tree, and contrary to nature were grafted into a cultivated olive tree, how much more will these who are ⸤natural branches⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “by nature”">j</a></sup>be grafted into their own olive tree?</span>
</div>
</div>

## All Israel to be Saved

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 21:24; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Romans 16:25"> For I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, of this mystery, so that you will not be wise ⸤in your own sight⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “in yourselves”">k</a></sup>that a partial hardening has happened to Israel, until the full number of the Gentiles has come in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; Jeremiah 31:31–37; Psalm 14:7"> and so all Israel will be saved, just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The deliverer will come out of Zion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will turn away ungodliness from Jacob.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; Ezekiel 36:25–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">And this is the covenant from me with them</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 59:20–21">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I take away their sins.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 27:9 ; Jer 31:33–34">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Matthew 21:43"> With respect to the gospel, they are enemies for your sake, but with respect to election, they are dearly loved for the sake of the fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 7:21; Numbers 23:19; Malachi 3:6"> For the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 3:7; Ephesians 2:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> For just as you formerly were disobedient to God, but now have been shown mercy because of the disobedience of these,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 11:15; Romans 10:16; Romans 11:25"> so also these have now been disobedient for your mercy, in order that they also may now be shown mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Galatians 3:22; Romans 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4–6"> For God confined them all in disobedience, in order that he could have mercy on them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Job 5:9; Job 11:7–9; Psalm 92:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Oh, the depth of the riches</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the wisdom and the knowledge of God!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How unsearchable are his judgments</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and how incomprehensible are his ways!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; 1 Corinthians 2:16; Job 15:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For who has known the mind of the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or who has been his counselor?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 40:13">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 41:11; Job 35:7; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Or who has given in advance to him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it will be paid back to him?”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="A quotation from Job 41:11">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For from him and through him and to him are all things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">To him be glory for eternity! Amen.</span></span>
</div>
