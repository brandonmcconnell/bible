## The Righteousness of God through Faith in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; John 5:34"> Brothers, the desire of my heart and my prayer to God on behalf of them is for their salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> For I testify about them that they have a zeal for God, but not according to knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For ignoring the righteousness of God, and seeking to establish their own,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “their own righteousness”">a</a></sup>they did not subject themselves to the righteousness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> For Christ is the end of the law for righteousness to everyone who believes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> For Moses writes about the righteousness that is from the law: “The person who does this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts explicitly state “these things ”">b</a></sup>will live by it.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 18:5">c</a></sup><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “them”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> But the righteousness from faith speaks like this: “Do not say in your heart,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 9:4">e</a></sup>‘Who will ascend into heaven?’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 30:12">f</a></sup>(that is, to bring Christ down),</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> or “Who will descend into the abyss?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 30:13">g</a></sup>(that is, to bring Christ up from the dead).</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> But what does it say? “The word is near to you, in your mouth and in your heart”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 30:14">h</a></sup>(that is, the word of faith that we proclaim),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “because”">i</a></sup>if you confess with your mouth “Jesus is Lord” and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> For with the heart one believes, resulting in righteousness, and with the mouth one confesses, resulting in salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> For the scripture says, “Everyone who believes in him will not be put to shame.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 28:16">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, for the same Lord is Lord of all, who is rich to all who call upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> For “everyone who calls upon the name of the Lord will be saved.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A quotation from Joel 2:32">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> How then will they call upon him in whom they have not believed? And how will they believe in him about whom they have not heard? And how will they hear about him without one who preaches to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> And how will they preach, unless they are sent? Just as it is written, “How timely are the feet of those who bring good news of good things.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 52:7 ; Nah 1:15">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; John 12:38–40"> But not all have obeyed the good news, for Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our report?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 53:1">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> Consequently, faith comes by hearing, and hearing through the word about Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> But I say, they have not heard, have they? On the contrary,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Their voice has gone out to all the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their words to the ends of the inhabited world.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 19:4">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> But I say, Israel did not know, did they? First, Moses says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will provoke you to jealousy by those who are not a nation;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by a senseless nation I will provoke you to anger.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 32:21">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> And Isaiah is very bold and says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I was found by those who did not seek me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I became known to those who did not ask for me.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 65:1">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> But about Israel he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The whole day long I held out my hands</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to a disobedient and resistant people.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 65:2">q</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
