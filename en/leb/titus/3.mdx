## Conduct Toward All People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 13:1–7; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> Remind them to be subject to the rulers and to the authorities, to obey, to be prepared for every good work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:2"> to speak evil of no one, to be peaceable, gentle, showing all courtesy to all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–3; Romans 6:17; Colossians 1:21"> For we also were once foolish, disobedient, led astray, enslaved to various desires and pleasures, spending our lives in wickedness and envy, despicable, hating one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–10; Titus 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:10"> But when the kindness and love for mankind of God our Savior appeared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8–9; 2 Timothy 1:9; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> he saved us, not by deeds of righteousness that we have done, but because of his mercy, through the washing of regeneration and renewal by the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:28; Romans 5:5; Acts 10:45"> whom he poured out on us abundantly through Jesus Christ our Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17; Titus 2:11; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> so that, having been justified by his grace, we may become heirs according to the hope of eternal life.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Instructions to Titus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Titus 3:14; Titus 1:9; Psalm 16:2–3"> The saying is trustworthy, and I want you to insist concerning these things, so that those who have believed in God may be careful to engage in good deeds. These things are good and beneficial for people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:14; 2 Timothy 2:23; 1 Timothy 4:7"> But avoid foolish controversies and genealogies and contentions and quarrels about the law, for they are useless and fruitless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 John 1:10; Matthew 18:15–17"> Reject a divisive person after a first and second admonition,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26–28"> knowing that such a person is perverted and is sinning, being self-condemned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; 2 Timothy 4:12; 1 Corinthians 16:8–9"> When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, make haste to come to me in Nicopolis, for I have decided to spend the winter there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 18:24; Matthew 22:35; 3 John 1:6–8"> Diligently send on their way Zenas the lawyer and Apollos, so that ⸤they may lack nothing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “nothing may lack to them”">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Titus 3:8; Matthew 7:19"> But also our people must learn to engage in good deeds for necessary needs, so that they will not be unfruitful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Ephesians 6:23–24; 2 John 1:1–2"> All those with me greet you. Greet those who love us in the faith. Grace be with all of you.</span>
</div>
</div>
