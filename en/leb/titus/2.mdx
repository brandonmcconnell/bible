## Sound Instruction and Consistent Behavior

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; 1 Timothy 6:3; Titus 2:11–14"> But you, speak the things which are fitting for sound instruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; 1 Peter 4:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> Older men are to be temperate, worthy of respect, self-controlled, sound in faith, in love, and in endurance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 2:4; 1 Timothy 3:11; 1 Peter 3:3–5"> Older women likewise are to be reverent in their behavior, not slanderous, not enslaved to much wine, teaching what is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:2; 1 Timothy 5:11"> in order that they may encourage the young women to love their husbands and to love their children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10–31; Colossians 3:18; 1 Timothy 5:10"> to be self-controlled, pure, busy at home, good, being subject to their own husbands, in order that the word of God may not be slandered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:1; 1 Peter 5:5; Joel 2:28"> Likewise, exhort the young men to be self-controlled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> concerning everything showing yourself to be an example of good deeds, in your teaching demonstrating soundness, dignity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 2:15; 1 Peter 3:16"> a sound message beyond reproach, in order that ⸤an opponent⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “one from the opposed”">a</a></sup>may be put to shame, because he has nothing bad to say concerning us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–25; 1 Timothy 6:1–2; Colossians 3:22–25"> Slaves must be subject to their own masters in everything, to be well-pleasing, not to talk back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; 1 Peter 2:12; 2 John 1:9"> not stealing, but demonstrating all good faith, in order that they may do credit to the teaching of God our Savior in everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 2:8; 1 Timothy 2:4; Isaiah 45:22"> For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Matthew 16:24; Romans 6:19"> training us in order that, denying impiety and worldly desires, we may live self-controlled and righteously and godly in the present age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Corinthians 1:7; Matthew 16:27"> looking forward to the blessed hope and the glorious appearing of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:14; Ephesians 2:10; 1 Peter 2:9"> who gave himself for us, in order that he might redeem us from all lawlessness and purify for himself a people for his own possession, zealous for good deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; 1 Timothy 4:12–13; Titus 1:13"> Speak these things and exhort and reprove with all authority. Let no one disregard you.</span>
</div>
