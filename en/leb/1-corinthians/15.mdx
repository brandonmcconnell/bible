## Paul’s Gospel and the Resurrection of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–12; 1 Peter 5:12; 1 Corinthians 15:3–11"> Now I make known to you, brothers, the gospel which I proclaimed to you, which you have also received, in which you also stand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 2:1; Hebrews 3:14"> by which you are also being saved, if you hold fast to the message I proclaimed to you, unless you believed to no purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 1:4; Isaiah 53:1–12"> For I passed on to you ⸤as of first importance⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “among the first things ”">a</a></sup>what I also received, that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 6:2; Matthew 12:40; Psalm 16:10–11"> and that he was buried, and that he was raised up on the third day according to the scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:14; 1 Corinthians 1:12; John 1:42"> and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the twelve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 28:10; Mark 16:7; Acts 13:36"> then he appeared to more than five hundred brothers at once, the majority of whom remain until now, but some have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 24:50; Acts 1:2–12; Luke 24:33"> Then he appeared to James, then to all the apostles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1; Acts 26:16; Acts 22:18"> and last of all, as it were to one born at the wrong time, he appeared also to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:7–8; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3"> For I am the least of the apostles, not worthy to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Philippians 2:13"> But by the grace of God I am what I am, and his grace to me has not been in vain, but I labored even more than all of them, and not I, but the grace of God with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; 1 Corinthians 2:2"> Therefore whether I or those, in this way we preached, and in this way you believed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Concerning the Resurrection of the Dead

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:8; Acts 17:32; 2 Timothy 2:18"> Now if Christ is preached as raised up from the dead, how do some among you say that there is no resurrection of the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:25–26; Romans 8:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:14"> But if there is no resurrection of the dead, Christ has not been raised either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:14; Galatians 2:2"> But if Christ has not been raised, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “then both”">b</a></sup>our preaching is in vain, and your faith is in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Job 13:7–10; Acts 2:32"> And also we are found to be false witnesses of God, because we testified against God that he raised Christ, whom he did not raise if after all, then, the dead are not raised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For if the dead are not raised, Christ has not been raised either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 4:25; 1 Peter 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:14"> But if Christ has not been raised, your faith is empty; you are still in your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Revelation 14:13; 1 Thessalonians 4:16"> And as a further result, those who have fallen asleep in Christ have perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 1:12"> If ⸤we have put our hope⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “we are having put our hope”">c</a></sup>in Christ in this life only, we are of all people most pitiable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:23; Acts 26:23; Revelation 1:5"> But now Christ has been raised from the dead, the first fruits of those who have fallen asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:25; Romans 5:12–17; 1 Corinthians 15:22"> For since through a man came death, also through a man came the resurrection of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 5:12–21; 1 Corinthians 15:45–49; John 5:21–29"> For just as in Adam all die, so also in Christ all will be made alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; Galatians 3:29; 1 Corinthians 15:52"> But each in his own group: Christ the first fruits, then those who are Christ’s at his coming,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:14; Matthew 28:18"> then the end, when he hands over the kingdom to the God and Father, when he has abolished all rule and all authority and power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12–13; Psalm 110:1; Matthew 22:44"> For it is necessary for him to reign until he has put all his enemies under his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:10; 1 Corinthians 15:55; Revelation 21:4"> The last enemy to be abolished is death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 8:6; Hebrews 2:8; Matthew 28:18"> For “he subjected all things under his feet.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 8:6">d</a></sup>But when it says “all things” are subjected, it is clear that the one who subjected all things to him is not included.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:23; Ephesians 1:23"> But whenever all things are subjected to him, then the Son himself will be subjected<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “also will be subjected”">e</a></sup>to the one who subjected all things to him, in order that God may be all in all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 6:3–4; 1 Corinthians 15:16; Matthew 20:22"> Otherwise, why do they do it, those who are being baptized on behalf of the dead? If the dead are not raised at all, why indeed are they being baptized on behalf of them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:9; Galatians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 4:7–12"> And why are we in danger every hour?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 1 Corinthians 4:9–13"> I die every day—yes indeed, by my boasting in you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “in you, brothers,”">f</a></sup>which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 22:13; Isaiah 56:12; Luke 12:19"> If according to a human perspective I fought wild beasts at Ephesus, what benefit is it to me? If the dead are not raised, let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="An allusion to Isa 22:13 ; 56:12">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Matthew 24:4; 1 Corinthians 5:6"> Do not be deceived! “Bad company corrupts good morals.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from the Greek poet Menander’s comedy Thais , 218">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:5; Romans 13:11; Hebrews 5:11–12"> Sober up correctly and ⸤stop sinning⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “do not sin”">i</a></sup>, for some have no knowledge of God—I say this to your shame.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Questions Concerning the Resurrection Body

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:3; 1 Corinthians 15:38–53; Romans 9:19"> But someone will say, “How are the dead raised? And with what sort of body do they come?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 12:24; Luke 11:40; Ephesians 5:15"> Foolish person! What you sow does not come to life unless it dies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And what you sow is not the body which it will become, but you sow the bare seed, whether perhaps of wheat or of some of the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; Isaiah 61:11; Mark 4:26–29"> But God gives to it a body just as he wishes, and to each one of the seeds its own body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 1:20–26"> Not all flesh is the same, but there is one flesh of human beings, and another flesh of animals, and another flesh of birds, and another of fish,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and heavenly bodies and earthly bodies. But the glory of the heavenly bodies is of one kind, and the glory of the earthly bodies is of another kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 24:23; Psalm 148:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:19"> There is one glory of the sun, and another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars, for star differs from star in glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Daniel 12:3; Romans 8:21"> Thus also is the resurrection of the dead. It is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruptibility.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; Colossians 3:4; Philippians 3:20–21"> It is sown in dishonor, it is raised in glory. It is sown in weakness, it is raised in power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:50; Luke 24:31; John 20:19"> It is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body. If there is a natural body, there is also a spiritual body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 5:21; Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:2"> Thus also it is written, “The first man, Adam, became a living soul”;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 2:7">j</a></sup>the last Adam became a life-giving spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Colossians 3:9–10; Romans 6:6; Ephesians 4:22–24"> But the spiritual is not first, but the natural; then the spiritual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:31; Genesis 2:7; Genesis 3:19"> The first man is from the earth, made of earth; the second man is from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Genesis 5:3; Romans 5:12–21; John 3:6"> As the one who is made of earth, so also are those who are made of earth, and as the heavenly, so also are those who are heavenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Romans 8:29; 2 Corinthians 3:18; Genesis 5:3"> And just as we have borne the image of the one who is made of earth, we will also bear the image of the heavenly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 3:3–6; Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 4:17"> But I say this, brothers, that flesh and blood is not able to inherit the kingdom of God, nor can corruption inherit incorruptibility.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Philippians 3:21; 1 Corinthians 15:20"> Behold, I tell you a mystery: we will not all fall asleep, but we will all be changed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Matthew 24:31; John 5:28"> in a moment, in the blink of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised imperishable, and we will be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2–4; 1 John 3:2; Romans 2:7"> For it is necessary for this perishable body to put on incorruptibility, and this mortal body to put on immortality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 20:14; Hebrews 2:14–15"> But whenever this perishable body puts on incorruptibility and this mortal body puts on immortality, then the saying that is written will take place:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Death is swallowed up in victory.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Hosea 13:14; Psalm 89:48; Acts 2:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Where, O death, is your victory?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Where, O death, is your sting?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Romans 4:15; Galatians 3:10–13; Romans 7:5–13"> Now the sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 8:37; 1 John 5:4–5"> But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ!</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Chronicles 15:7; Hebrews 6:10"> So then, my dear brothers, be steadfast, immovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, because you know that your labor is not in vain in the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
