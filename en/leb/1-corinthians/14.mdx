## Appropriate Use of Prophecy and Tongues

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> Pursue love, and strive for spiritual gifts, but especially that you may prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 19:6; Acts 10:46"> For the one who speaks in a tongue does not speak to people but to God, because no one understands, but by the Spirit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “in the Spirit”; or “in his spirit”">a</a></sup>he speaks mysteries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:12; 1 Timothy 4:13; Jude 1:20"> But the one who prophesies speaks to people edification and encouragement and consolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:22; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Mark 16:17"> The one who speaks in a tongue edifies himself, but the one who prophesies edifies the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28–30; 1 Corinthians 12:10; Mark 16:17"> Now I want you all to speak with tongues, but even more that you may prophesy. The one who prophesies is greater than the one who speaks with tongues, unless he interprets, in order that the church may receive edification.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; Romans 6:17; 2 Timothy 3:16"> But now, brothers, if I come to you speaking with tongues, how do I benefit you, unless I speak to you either with a revelation or with knowledge or with a prophecy or with a teaching?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:8; Luke 7:32; 1 Corinthians 13:1"> Likewise, the inanimate things which produce a sound, whether flute or lyre, if they do not produce a distinction in the tones, how will it be known what is played on the flute or on the lyre?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 10:9; Ezekiel 33:3–6; Jeremiah 4:19"> For indeed, if the trumpet produces an indistinct sound, who will prepare for battle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:26"> And so you through the tongue, unless you produce a clear message, how will it be known what is spoken? For you will be speaking into the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There are probably so many kinds of languages in the world, and none without meaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 28:2; Romans 1:14; Colossians 3:11"> Therefore, if I do not know the meaning of the language, I will be a barbarian to the one who is speaking, and the one who is speaking will be a barbarian in my judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:3–4; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> In this way also you, since you are zealous of spiritual gifts, seek for the edification of the church, in order that you may abound.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:24; 1 Corinthians 12:30; John 14:13–14"> Therefore the one who speaks in a tongue must pray that he may interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:2; 1 Corinthians 14:15–16; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays but my mind is unproductive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; John 4:23–24; Ephesians 6:18"> ⸤Therefore what should I do⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “what therefore is it”">b</a></sup>? I will pray with my spirit, but I will also pray with my mind. I will sing praise with my spirit, but I will also sing praise with my mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:36; Psalm 106:48; Revelation 5:14"> For otherwise, if you praise in your spirit, how will the one who fills the place of the outsider say the “amen” at your thanksgiving, because he does not know what you are saying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:4; 1 Corinthians 14:6"> For indeed you are giving thanks well, but the other person is not edified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:4–6; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> I give thanks to God that I speak with tongues more than all of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21–22; 1 Corinthians 14:4"> but in the church I prefer to speak five words with my mind, in order that I may instruct other people, than ten thousand words in a tongue.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:2; 1 Corinthians 13:11; Romans 16:19"> Brothers, do not become children in your understanding, but with respect to wickedness be as a child, and in your understanding be mature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 28:11–12; Jeremiah 5:15; Deuteronomy 28:49"> In the law it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“By those who speak a foreign language</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and by the lips of others</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will speak to this people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and not even in this way will they obey me,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 28:11–12">c</a></sup><br />says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:6–12; Mark 16:17; 1 Corinthians 14:1"> So then, tongues are for a sign not to those who believe, but to unbelievers, but prophecy is not for unbelievers, but for those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 2:13; Acts 26:24; 1 Corinthians 11:18"> Therefore, if the whole church comes together at the same time and all speak with tongues, and outsiders or unbelievers enter, will they not say that you are out of your minds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 2:37; 1 Corinthians 2:15; Hebrews 4:12–13"> But if all prophesy, and some unbeliever or outsider enters, he is convicted by all, he is judged by all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:14; Zechariah 8:23; Psalm 72:11"> the secret things of his heart become evident, and so, falling on his face, he will worship God, proclaiming, “God is truly among you!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Specific Instructions for Orderly Worship Services

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 5:19; Romans 14:19; 1 Corinthians 12:7–10"> ⸤Therefore what should you do⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “what therefore is it”">d</a></sup>, brothers? Whenever you come together, each one of you has a psalm, has a teaching, has a revelation, has a tongue, has an interpretation. All things must be done for edification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:26"> If anyone speaks in a tongue, it must be on one occasion two or at most three, and ⸤one after the other⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in turn”">e</a></sup>, and one must interpret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But if there is no interpreter, he must be silent in the church, but let him speak to himself and to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:19–21; 1 John 4:1–3"> Let two or three prophets speak, and the others evaluate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19–20; Job 33:31–33; Job 32:11"> And if something is revealed to another who is seated, the first must be silent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 14:3; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> For you are all able to prophesy ⸤in turn⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “one at a time">f</a></sup>, in order that all may learn and all may be encouraged,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:19–20; 1 John 4:1; 1 Samuel 10:10–13"> and the spirits of prophets are subject to prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 3:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> For God is not a God of disorder but of peace.<br />As in all the churches of the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:11–12; Genesis 3:16; Numbers 30:3–13"> the women must be silent in the churches, for it is not permitted for them to speak, but they must be in submission, just as the law also says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; 1 Peter 3:7; Ephesians 5:25–27"> But if they want to learn something, let them ask their own husbands at home, for it is shameful for a woman to speak in church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 18:1–17; Zechariah 14:8; Acts 17:15"> Or has the word of God gone out from you, or has it come to you only?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:7; 2 Peter 3:2"> If anyone thinks he is a prophet or spiritual,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “or one who possesses the Spirit”">g</a></sup>he should recognize that the things which I am writing to you are of the Lord.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “are a command of the Lord”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Hosea 4:17; Revelation 22:11–12"> But if anyone ignores this, he is ignored.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “if anyone is ignorant, let him be ignorant”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:31; 1 Corinthians 14:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:20"> So then, my brothers, desire to prophesy, and do not prevent speaking with tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; Titus 1:5; 1 Corinthians 14:26–33"> But let all things be done decently and according to proper procedure.</span>
</div>
</div>
