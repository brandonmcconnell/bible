## Concerning Head Coverings in Worship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Philippians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> Become imitators of me, just as I also am of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:17; Proverbs 31:28–31"> Now I praise you that you remember me in all things, and just as I handed over to you the traditions, you hold fast to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Colossians 1:18; Colossians 3:18"> But I want you to know that Christ is the head of every man, and the man is the head of the woman, and the head of Christ is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:30; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 1 Corinthians 12:10"> Every man who prays or prophesies while having something on his head dishonors his head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:12; Luke 2:36; Acts 2:17"> but every woman who prays or prophesies with uncovered head dishonors her head, for she is one and the same with the one whose head is shaved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:5; Numbers 5:18"> For if a woman does not cover herself, let her hair be shorn off. But if it is shameful for a woman to have her head<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to be”">a</a></sup>shorn or shaved, let her cover her head.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “herself”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 5:1; Psalm 8:6"> For indeed a man ought not to cover his head, because he is the image and glory of God, but the woman is the glory of the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:13; Genesis 2:21–23"> For man is not from woman, but woman from man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; Genesis 2:23–24; Genesis 2:20"> For indeed man was not created for the sake of the woman, but woman for the sake of the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:64–65; Hebrews 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> Because of this, the woman ought to have a symbol of authority on her head, on account of the angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; 1 Corinthians 7:10–14; 1 Corinthians 12:12–22"> Nevertheless, neither is woman anything apart from man, nor is man anything apart from woman in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:36; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Hebrews 1:2–3"> For just as the woman is from the man, thus also the man is through the woman. But all things are from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 12:57; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> You judge ⸤for yourselves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “in you yourselves”">c</a></sup>: is it fitting for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:26; 1 Corinthians 14:35"> And does not nature itself teach you that a man, if he wears long hair, it is a dishonor to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But a woman, if she wears long hair, it is her glory, because her hair is given<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “is given to her”">d</a></sup>for a covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Timothy 6:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14"> But if anyone is disposed to be contentious, we have no such custom, nor do the churches of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Improper Conduct at the Lord’s Supper

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; 1 Corinthians 14:23; 1 Corinthians 11:22"> But in giving this instruction I do not praise you, because you come together not for the better but for the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> For in the first place, when you come together as a church, I hear there are divisions among you, and in part I believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Titus 3:10; Acts 20:30"> For indeed it is necessary that there be factions among you, in order that those who are approved may become evident among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Therefore, when you come together in the same place, it is not to eat the Lord’s supper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:13; Jude 1:12; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For when you eat it, each one of you goes ahead to take his own supper, and one is hungry and another is drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:28; Proverbs 17:5; James 2:5–6"> For do you not have houses for eating and drinking? Or do you despise the church of God and humiliate those who do not have anything? What shall I say to you? Shall I praise you? In this I will not praise you!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:19–20; Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–25"> For I received from the Lord what I also passed on to you, that the Lord Jesus, on the night in which he was betrayed, took bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:53–58; 1 Corinthians 11:27–28; 1 Corinthians 10:3–4"> and after he had given thanks, he broke it and said, “This is my body which is for you. Do this in remembrance of me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 22:20; Hebrews 9:15–20; Hebrews 13:20"> Likewise also the cup, after they had eaten, saying, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood. Do this, as often as you drink it, in remembrance of me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 1:7; John 14:3"> For as often as you eat this bread and drink this cup, you proclaim the Lord’s death until he comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 10:21; Hebrews 10:29"> So then, whoever eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an unworthy manner will be guilty of the body and the blood of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 6:4; 2 Corinthians 13:5; Lamentations 3:40"> But let a person examine himself, and in this way let him eat from the bread and let him drink from the cup.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:27; 1 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 11:32–34"> For the one who eats and drinks, if he does not recognize the body, eats and drinks judgment against himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Numbers 20:12; Amos 3:2"> Because of this, many are weak and sick among you, and quite a few ⸤have died⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “have fallen asleep”">e</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 1:9; Psalm 32:3–5; Revelation 2:5"> But if we were evaluating ourselves, we would not be judged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 12:5–11; Psalm 118:18; Proverbs 3:11–12"> But if we are judged by the Lord, we are being disciplined, in order that we will not be condemned with the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> So then, my brothers, when you come together in order to eat the Lord’s supper, wait for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:21–22; 1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Corinthians 4:19"> If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, lest you come together for judgment. And I will give directions about the remaining matters whenever I come.</span>
</div>
</div>
