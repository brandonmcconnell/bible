## Lawsuits between Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:15–17; Acts 19:38; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> Does anyone among you, if he has a matter against someone else, dare to go to court before the unrighteous, and not before the saints?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Daniel 7:22; Revelation 20:4"> Or do you not know that the saints will judge the world? And if by you the world is judged, are you unworthy of the most insignificant courts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:6; Matthew 25:41; 1 Corinthians 6:4"> Do you not know that we will judge angels, not to mention ordinary matters?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12; Acts 6:2–4"> Therefore, if you have courts with regard to ordinary matters, do you seat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “seat” (imperative); the Greek verb can be either indicative mood or imperative mood by its form">a</a></sup>these despised people in the church?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:34; 1 Corinthians 4:14; James 3:13–18"> I say this to your shame. So is there not anyone wise among you who will be able to render a decision between his brothers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 2:14–15; 1 John 2:9–11; 1 John 3:11–15"> But brother goes to court with brother, and this before unbelievers!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 12:17–19; Proverbs 20:22; 1 Peter 2:19–23"> Therefore it is already completely a loss for you that you have lawsuits with one another. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be defrauded?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Colossians 3:25; Mark 10:19"> But you wrong and defraud, and do this to brothers!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:9–10; Galatians 5:19–21; Ephesians 5:4–5"> Or do you not know that the unrighteous will not inherit the kingdom of God? Do not be deceived! Neither sexually immoral people, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor passive homosexual partners, nor dominant homosexual partners,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:11; Galatians 5:21; Ezekiel 22:29"> nor thieves, nor greedy persons, not drunkards, not abusive persons, not swindlers will inherit the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 3:5–7; 1 Corinthians 1:2"> And some of you were these things, but you were washed, but you were sanctified, but you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “of the Lord Jesus Christ”">b</a></sup>and by the Spirit of our God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Avoid Sexual Immorality

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:23–33; 1 Corinthians 9:27; Hebrews 12:15–16"> All things are permitted for me, but not all things are profitable. All things are permitted for me, but I will not be controlled by anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Romans 6:12"> Food is for the stomach, and the stomach for food, but God will abolish ⸤both of them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “both this and these”">c</a></sup>. Now the body is not for sexual immorality, but for the Lord, and the Lord for the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:14; Romans 8:11; Ephesians 1:19–20"> And God both raised up the Lord and will raise us up by his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:30; Romans 12:5; Ephesians 4:15–16"> Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Therefore, shall I take away the members of Christ and make them members of a prostitute? May it never be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Ephesians 5:31; Mark 10:8"> Or do you not know that the one who joins himself to a prostitute is one body with her? For it says, “The two will become one flesh.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 2:24">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 17:21–23; Galatians 2:20; 1 Corinthians 12:13"> But the one who joins himself to the Lord is one spirit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:3; Colossians 3:5"> Flee sexual immorality. Every sin that a person commits is outside his body, but the one who commits sexual immorality sins against his own body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Peter 2:5"> Or do you not know that your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God, and you are not your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:23; Romans 12:1; 1 Peter 2:9"> For you were bought at a price; therefore glorify God with your body.</span>
</div>
</div>
