## Paul’s Approach to Ministry in Corinth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> And I, when I came to you, brothers, did not come with superiority of speech or of wisdom, proclaiming to you the testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “mystery”">a</a></sup>of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 6:14; John 17:3; 1 Corinthians 1:22–25"> For I decided not to know anything among you except Jesus Christ and him crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:4; 1 Corinthians 4:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:5–10"> And I came to you in weakness and in fear and with much trembling,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:20; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> and my speech and my preaching were not with the persuasiveness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “with persuasive words”">b</a></sup>of wisdom, but with a demonstration of the Spirit and power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; 2 Corinthians 12:9; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> in order that your faith would not be in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Wisdom Revealed by the Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 5:14; James 3:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> Now we do speak wisdom among the mature, but wisdom not of this age or of the rulers of this age, who are perishing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–27; 2 Peter 1:3; Matthew 11:25"> but we speak the hidden wisdom of God in a mystery, which God predestined before the ages for our glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:27; James 2:1; Acts 7:2"> which none of the rulers of this age knew. For if they had known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Psalm 31:19; James 1:12"> But just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Things which eye has not seen and ear has not heard,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have not entered into the heart of man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all that</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “which”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God has prepared for those who love him.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 64:4">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:26; Ephesians 3:5; Luke 10:21"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “But to us God has revealed them ”">e</a></sup>to us God has revealed them through the Spirit. For the Spirit searches all things, even the depths of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Romans 11:33–34"> For who among men knows the things of a man, except the spirit of the man that is in him? Thus also no one knows the things of God except the Spirit of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:27; Romans 8:5–6; 1 John 2:20"> Now we have received not the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, in order that we may know the things freely given to us by God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> things which we also speak, not in words taught by human wisdom, but in words taught by the Spirit, explaining spiritual things to spiritual people.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “in spiritual words ”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Romans 8:5–8; Matthew 13:11–17"> But the natural man does not accept the things of the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him, and he is not able to understand them, because they are spiritually discerned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Proverbs 28:5; Hebrews 5:14"> Now the spiritual person discerns all things, but he himself is judged by no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34; John 16:13–16"> “For who has known the mind of the Lord; who has advised him?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 40:13">g</a></sup>But we have the mind of Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>
