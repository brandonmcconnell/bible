## Concerning Christian Marriage

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:8; 1 Corinthians 7:26–27; 1 Corinthians 7:37–38"> Now concerning the things about which you wrote: “It is good for a man not to touch<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="I.e., in a sexual sense">a</a></sup>a woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28; Ephesians 5:33; 1 Corinthians 7:9"> But because of sexual immorality, let each man have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="I.e., in the sense of “have sexual relations with”">b</a></sup>his own wife and let each woman have her own husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Exodus 21:10"> The husband must fulfill his obligation to his wife, and likewise also the wife to her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 3:3; Matthew 19:9; Mark 10:11–12"> The wife does not have authority over her own body, but her husband does. And likewise also the husband does not have authority over his own body, but his wife does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:15; Joel 2:16; Zechariah 12:12–14"> Do not defraud one another, except perhaps by agreement, for a time, in order that you may devote yourselves to prayer, and then you should be ⸤together⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “at the same”">c</a></sup>again, lest Satan tempt you because of your lack of self control.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:8; 1 Corinthians 7:25; 1 Corinthians 7:12"> But I say this as a concession, not as a command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 19:11–12; Romans 12:6; 1 Corinthians 9:5"> I wish all people could be like myself, but each one has his own gift from God, one in this way and another in that way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:32; 1 Corinthians 7:34–35; 1 Corinthians 7:26–27"> Now I say to the unmarried and to the widows: It is good for them if they remain as I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:2; 1 Timothy 5:14; 1 Timothy 5:11"> But if they cannot control themselves, they should marry, for it is better to marry than to burn with sexual desire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 5:32; Luke 16:18; Matthew 19:3–9"> To the married I command—not I, but the Lord—a wife must not separate from her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; 1 Corinthians 7:10; Isaiah 50:1"> But if indeed she does separate, she must remain unmarried or be reconciled to her husband. And a husband must not divorce his wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:17; 1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 7:25"> Now to the rest I say—not the Lord—if any brother has an unbelieving wife and she consents to live with him, he must not divorce her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And if any wife has an unbelieving husband and he consents to live with her, she must not divorce her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15–16; 1 Timothy 4:5; Titus 1:15"> For the unbelieving husband is sanctified by his wife, and the unbelieving wife is sanctified by the brother, since otherwise your children are unclean, but now they are holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:19; 2 Corinthians 13:11; James 3:17–18"> But if the unbeliever leaves, let him leave. The brother or the sister is not bound in such cases. But God has called us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “you” (plural)">d</a></sup>in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Romans 11:14"> For how do you know, wife, whether you will save your husband? Or how do you know, husband, whether you will save your wife?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:17; Romans 12:3–8; 1 Corinthians 7:24"> But to each one as the Lord has apportioned. As God has called each one, thus let him live—and thus I order in all the churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 15:5; Acts 15:28; Acts 15:24"> Was anyone called after being circumcised? He must not undo his circumcision. Was anyone called in uncircumcision? He must not become circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:15; Galatians 5:6; 1 John 2:3–4"> Circumcision is nothing and uncircumcision is nothing, but the keeping of the commandments of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:12"> Each one in the calling in which he was called—in this he should remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–24; Colossians 3:11; Luke 10:40–41"> Were you called while a slave? Do not let it be a concern to you. But if indeed you are able to become free, rather make use of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:16; Philemon 1:16; Colossians 3:22–24"> For the one who is called in the Lord while a slave is the Lord’s freedperson. Likewise the one who is called while free is a slave of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Titus 2:14"> You were bought at a price; do not become slaves of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:20; Colossians 3:23–24; 1 Corinthians 7:17"> Each one in the situation in which he was called, brothers—in this he should remain with God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Concerning the Unmarried

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 4:2; 1 Corinthians 7:40"> Now concerning virgins I do not have a command from the Lord, but I am giving an opinion as one shown mercy by the Lord to be trustworthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:1; 1 Corinthians 7:8; 1 Peter 4:17"> Therefore, I consider this to be good because of the impending distress, that it is good for a man to be thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:20; 1 Corinthians 7:12–14"> Are you bound to a wife? Do not seek release. Are you free from a wife? Do not seek a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Corinthians 7:32–36; 1 Corinthians 7:26"> But if you marry, you have not sinned, and if the virgin marries, she has not sinned. But such people will have affliction in the flesh, and I would spare you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 2:17; 1 Corinthians 7:31; Romans 13:11–12"> But I say this, brothers: the time is shortened, that from now on even those who have wives should be as if they do not have wives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 30:5; Luke 6:25; Luke 6:21"> and those who weep as if they do not weep, and those who rejoice as if they do not rejoice, and those who buy as if they do not possess,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 John 2:17; 1 Peter 4:7; James 4:14"> and those who make use of the world as if they do not make full use of it. For the present form of this world is passing away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 6:25–34; 1 Timothy 5:5; Philippians 4:6"> But I want you to be free from care. The unmarried person cares for the things of the Lord, how he may please the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:25–33"> But the one who is married cares for the things of the world, how he may please his wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 6:20; Luke 2:36–37"> and he is divided. And the unmarried woman or the virgin cares for the things of the Lord, in order that she may be holy both in body and in spirit. But the married woman cares for the things of the world, how she may please her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:33–34; 1 Corinthians 7:36; Luke 8:14"> Now I am saying this for your own benefit, not that I may put a restriction on you, but to promote appropriate and devoted service to the Lord without distraction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:37; 1 Corinthians 7:9; 1 Samuel 2:33"> But if anyone thinks he is behaving dishonorably concerning his virgin, if she is past her prime<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “if his passions are strong” (it is not clear in context whether this term refers to the man or to the woman)">e</a></sup>and it ought to be thus, let him do what he wishes. He does not sin. Let them marry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> But he who stands firm in his heart, not having necessity, but has authority concerning his own will, and has decided this in his own heart, to keep his own virgin, he will do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Corinthians 7:1–2; 1 Corinthians 7:8"> So then, the one who marries<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or perhaps “the one who gives in marriage”">f</a></sup>his own virgin does well, and the one who does not marry her will do better.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Romans 7:2–3; 2 Corinthians 6:14–16; 1 Corinthians 7:10"> A wife is bound for as long a time as her husband lives. But if her husband ⸤dies⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “falls asleep”">g</a></sup>, she is free to marry whomever she wishes, only in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:35; 2 Peter 3:15–16; 1 Corinthians 7:8"> But she is happier if she remains thus, according to my opinion—and I think I have the Spirit of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
