## Concerning the Collection for the Saints

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 2 Corinthians 8:1–9; 2 Corinthians 9:12–15"> Now concerning the collection for the saints: just as I gave directions about it to the churches of Galatia, so you do also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:9–10; Deuteronomy 15:11–14; Deuteronomy 8:18"> On the first day of the week, each one of you ⸤put aside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “put from himself”">a</a></sup>something, saving up ⸤to whatever extent he has prospered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “whatever if anything he has been prospered”">b</a></sup>, in order that whenever I come, at that time collections do not take place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 11:34; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21"> And whenever I arrive, whomever you approve by letters, I will send these to take your gift to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 8:19; Romans 15:25"> And if it is worthwhile for me to go also, they will travel with me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Travel Plans for Paul and Associates

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 19:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> But I will come to you whenever I go through Macedonia (for I am going through Macedonia),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Romans 15:24; Acts 20:38"> and perhaps I will stay with you, or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way wherever I may go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; James 4:15"> For I do not want to see you now in passing, for I hope to remain some time with you, if the Lord allows it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Exodus 23:16; Acts 18:19"> But I will remain in Ephesus until Pentecost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> for a great and effective door has opened for me, and there are many opponents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Romans 16:21"> But if Timothy comes, see that he is with you without cause to fear, for he is carrying out the Lord’s work, as I also am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Acts 15:33; Titus 2:15"> Therefore do not let anyone disdain him, but send him on his way in peace in order that he may come to me, for I am expecting him with the brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:5; Titus 3:4"> Now concerning Apollos our brother, I urged him many times that he should come to you with the brothers, and he was not at all willing that he should come now, but he will come whenever he has an opportunity.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Concluding Exhortations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:13–18; Ephesians 6:10"> Be on the alert, stand firm in the faith, act courageously, be strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> All your actions must be done in love.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Romans 16:5; Romans 16:2"> Now I urge you, brothers—you know about the household of Stephanas, that they are the first fruits of Achaia, and they have devoted themselves to the ministry for the saints—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12; 3 John 1:8"> that you also be subject to such people, and to all those who work together and labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 2:30; Philemon 1:13"> Now I rejoice over the arrival of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus, because these make up for your absence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; 2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:12"> for they have refreshed my spirit and yours. Therefore recognize such people.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Greetings and Benediction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; Philemon 1:2; Romans 16:3–5"> The churches of the province of Asia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Roman province of Asia, known today as Asia Minor">c</a></sup>greet you. Aquila and Prisca greet you in the Lord many times, together with the church in their house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Peter 5:14"> All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; Galatians 6:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:17"> The greeting is by my hand—Paul’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:24; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be accursed. O Lord, come!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Aramaic expression marana tha (“O Lord, come!”) can also be rendered maran atha (“our Lord has come”); it is used here by Paul without explanation">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:16; Revelation 3:19; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
