## Concerning Food Sacrificed to Idols

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:7; Isaiah 5:21; Romans 14:3"> Now concerning food sacrificed to idols, we know that “we all have knowledge.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Considered by many interpreters to be a slogan used by the Corinthians to justify their behavior">a</a></sup>Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:12; 1 Corinthians 3:18; Proverbs 26:12"> If anyone thinks he knows anything, he has not yet known as it is necessary to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:8–9; Exodus 33:17; Nahum 1:7"> But if anyone loves God, this one is known by him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Isaiah 41:24; 1 Corinthians 10:19–20"> Therefore, concerning the eating of food sacrificed to idols, we know that “an idol is nothing in the world” and that “there is no God except one.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Considered by many interpreters to be slogans used by the Corinthians to justify their behavior">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Jeremiah 2:28; Galatians 4:8"> For even if after all there are so-called gods, whether in heaven or on earth, just as there are many gods and many lords,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; John 1:3; Malachi 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">yet to us there is one God, the Father,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from whom are all things, and we are for him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and there is one Lord, Jesus Christ,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">through whom are all things, and we are through him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 14:14; 1 Corinthians 10:28–29; Romans 14:22–23"> But this knowledge is not in everyone. But some, being accustomed until now to the idol, eat this food as food sacrificed to idols, and their conscience, because it is weak, is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:17; Hebrews 13:9; 1 Corinthians 6:13"> But food does not bring us close to God. For neither if we eat do we have more, nor if we do not eat do we lack.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “For” and have “Neither if we do not eat do we lack, nor if we do eat do we have more”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 14:20–21; 1 Peter 2:16"> But watch out lest somehow this right of yours becomes a cause for stumbling to the weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:28–29; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; Romans 14:23"> For if someone should see you who has knowledge reclining for a meal in an idol’s temple, will not his conscience, because it is weak, be strengthened so that he eats the food sacrificed to idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:15; Romans 15:1–3; 1 Corinthians 8:13"> For the one who is weak—the brother for whom Christ died—is destroyed by your knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 18:6; Matthew 25:45; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> Now if you sin in this way against the brothers and wound their conscience, which is weak, you sin against Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 14:21; 2 Corinthians 6:3; 2 Corinthians 11:29"> Therefore, if food causes my brother to sin, I will never eat meat ⸤forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">d</a></sup>, in order that I may not cause my brother to sin.</span>
</div>
</div>
