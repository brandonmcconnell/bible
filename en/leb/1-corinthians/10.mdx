## A History Lesson from Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 14:29; Nehemiah 9:11–12; Psalm 78:13–14"> For I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, that our fathers were all under the cloud and all went through the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–3; 1 Corinthians 1:13–16; Exodus 14:31"> and all were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Nehemiah 9:15; Exodus 16:35"> and all ate the same spiritual food,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:6; John 7:37; Isaiah 48:21"> and all drank the same spiritual drink. For they drank from the spiritual rock that followed them, and the rock was Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 3:17; Jude 1:5; Numbers 14:37"> But God was not pleased with the majority of them, for they were struck down in the desert.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jude 1:7; 1 Corinthians 10:11; Hebrews 4:11"> Now these things happened as examples for us, so that we should not be desirers of evil things, just as those also desired them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 32:6–8; Psalm 106:19–20; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> and not become idolaters, as some of them did, just as it is written, “The people sat down to eat and drink, and stood up to play,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 32:6">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 1 Corinthians 6:18; Psalm 106:29"> nor commit sexual immorality, as some of them committed sexual immorality, and twenty-three thousand fell in one day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 21:5–6; Psalm 78:18; Hebrews 3:8–11"> nor put Christ to the test, as some of them tested him, and were destroyed by snakes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 16:41; Numbers 14:37"> nor grumble, just as some of them grumbled, and were destroyed by the destroyer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:4; Romans 13:11; 1 Corinthians 9:10"> Now these things happened to those people as an example, but are written for our instruction, on whom the ends of the ages have come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 11:20; 2 Peter 3:17; Proverbs 16:18"> Therefore, the one who thinks that he stands must watch out lest he fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:9; 2 Timothy 4:18; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> Temptation has not come upon you except what is common to humanity. But God is faithful, who will not permit you to be tempted beyond what you are able, but will also make a way out together with the temptation, so that you may be able to endure it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Warning Against Idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 1 John 5:21; 1 Peter 2:11"> Therefore, my dear friends, flee from idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 11:13"> I am speaking as to sensible people; you judge what I am saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:26–28; 1 Corinthians 11:23–29; Acts 2:42"> The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not a participation in the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not a participation in the body of Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body, for we all share from the one bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:3–5; Galatians 6:16; Leviticus 7:6"> Consider Israel according to the flesh: are not the ones who eat the sacrifices sharers in the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; 1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 13:2"> Therefore, what am I saying? That food sacrificed to idols is anything, or that an idol is anything?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 9:20; Psalm 106:37–39; Deuteronomy 32:16–17"> No, but that the things which they sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to God, and I do not want you to become sharers with demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; 1 Kings 18:21; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17"> You are not able to drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons. You are not able to share the table of the Lord and the table of demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Deuteronomy 32:16; Hebrews 10:31"> Or are we attempting to provoke the Lord to jealousy? We are not stronger than he is, are we?</span>
</div>
</div>

## Freedom in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 8:9; Ephesians 4:29"> All things are permitted, but not all things are profitable. All things are permitted, but not all things build up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 15:1–2; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Philippians 2:4–5"> Let no one seek ⸤his own good⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the - of himself”; “good” is implied">b</a></sup>but the good of the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Corinthians 8:7; Titus 1:15"> Eat everything that is sold in the meat market, ⸤asking no questions⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “questioning nothing”">c</a></sup>for the sake of the conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 24:1; Exodus 19:5; Psalm 50:12"> for “the earth is the Lord’s, and its fullness.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 24:1, and an allusion to Ps 50:12 ; 89:11">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Luke 10:7–8; 1 Corinthians 5:9–11"> If any of the unbelievers invites you, and you want to go, eat everything that is set before you, ⸤asking no questions⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “questioning nothing”">e</a></sup>for the sake of the conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:10–13; 1 Corinthians 8:7; 1 Corinthians 10:26"> But if someone says to you, “This is offered to idols,” do not eat it, for the sake of that one who informed you and the conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:19; Romans 14:15–21; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> Now I am not speaking about your own conscience, but the conscience of the other person. For why is my freedom judged by another’s conscience?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 14:6; 1 Timothy 4:3–4"> If I partake with thankfulness, why am I slandered concerning that for which I give thanks?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Colossians 3:23; 1 Peter 4:11"> Therefore, whether you eat or you drink or whatever you do, do all things for the glory of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 14:13"> ⸤Give no offense⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “be blameless”">f</a></sup>both to Jews and to Greeks and to the church of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Corinthians 9:19–23"> just as I also please all people in all things, not seeking my own benefit, but the benefit of the many, in order that they may be saved.</span>
</div>
