## Paul Gives Up His Rights as an Apostle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:17; 1 Timothy 2:7; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> Am I not free? Am I not an apostle? Have I not seen Jesus our Lord? Are you not my work in the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:12; John 6:27; 2 Corinthians 3:1–3"> If to others I am not an apostle, yet indeed I am to you, for you are my seal of apostleship in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:16–19; 2 Corinthians 13:3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> My defense to those who examine me is this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; Luke 10:7"> Do we not have the right to eat and drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:42; Hebrews 13:4; Matthew 8:14"> Do we not have the right to take along a sister as wife, like the rest of the apostles and the brothers of the Lord and Cephas?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Acts 13:1–2; Acts 4:36"> Or do only I and Barnabas not have the right ⸤to refrain from working⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “not to work”">a</a></sup>?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 27:18; Deuteronomy 20:6; 2 Timothy 2:3–4"> Who ever serves as a soldier at his own expense? Who plants a vineyard and does not eat the fruit of it? Who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Or who”">b</a></sup>shepherds a flock and does not drink<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “eat”">c</a></sup>from the milk of the flock?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:8; Romans 3:31"> I am not saying these things according to a human perspective. Or does the law not also say these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Timothy 5:18; Psalm 104:27"> For in the law of Moses it is written, “You must not muzzle an ox while it is threshing.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 25:4">d</a></sup>It is not about oxen God is concerned, is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:6; Romans 15:4; Romans 4:23–24"> Or doubtless does he speak ⸤for our sake⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “for the sake of us”">e</a></sup>? For it is written ⸤for our sake⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “for the sake of us”">f</a></sup>, because the one who plows ought to plow in hope and the one who threshes ought to do so in hope of a share.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 15:27; Galatians 6:6; 2 Corinthians 11:15"> If we have sown spiritual things among you, is it too great a thing if we reap material things from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:15; 1 Corinthians 9:18; 2 Corinthians 11:12"> If others share this right over you, do we not do so even more? Yet we have not made use of this right, but we endure all things, in order that we may not cause any hindrance to the gospel of Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 18:8–20; Numbers 5:9–10; Leviticus 6:26"> Do you not know that those performing the holy services eat the things from the temple, and those attending to the altar have a share with the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:10; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; 1 Corinthians 9:4"> In the same way also the Lord ordered those who proclaim the gospel to live from the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:8; 1 Corinthians 9:12"> But I have not made use of any of these rights. And I am not writing these things in order that it may be thus with me. For it would be better to me rather to die than for anyone to deprive me of my reason for boasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Jeremiah 20:9; Colossians 4:17"> For if I proclaim the gospel, it is not to me a reason for boasting, for necessity is imposed on me. For woe is to me if I do not proclaim the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; 1 Corinthians 3:8; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> For if I do this voluntarily, I have a reward, but if I do so unwillingly, I have been entrusted with a stewardship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:7–9; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> What then is my reward? That when I proclaim the gospel, I may offer the gospel free of charge, in order not to make full use of my right in the gospel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Matthew 18:15; James 5:19–20"> For although I am free from all people, I have enslaved myself to all, in order that I may gain more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:3; Galatians 5:18; Acts 21:20–26"> I have become like a Jew to the Jews, in order that I may gain the Jews. To those under the law I became as under the law (although I myself am not under the law) in order that I may gain those under the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Romans 2:14; Galatians 3:2"> To those outside the law I became as outside the law (although I am not outside the law of God, but subject to the law of Christ) in order that I may gain those outside the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:33; Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 9:19"> To the weak I became weak, in order that I may gain the weak. I have become all things to all people, in order that by all means I may save some.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; Mark 8:35; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> I do all this for the sake of the gospel, in order that I may become a participant with it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 12:1; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> Do you not know that those who run in the stadium all run, but one receives the prize? Run in such a way that you may win.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:5; Revelation 2:10; 2 Timothy 4:7–8"> And everyone who competes exercises self-control in all things. Thus those do so in order that they may receive a perishable crown, but we an imperishable one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 1:10; Philippians 1:21; 2 Timothy 2:5"> Therefore I run in this way, not as running aimlessly; I box in this way, not as beating the air.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:25; Colossians 3:5"> But I discipline my body and subjugate it, lest somehow after preaching to others, I myself should become disqualified.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
