## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:1; Ephesians 1:1; 2 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul, called to be an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, and Sosthenes our brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> to the church of God sanctified in Christ Jesus that is in Corinth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “to the church of God that is in Corinth, sanctified in Christ Jesus”">a</a></sup>called to be saints, together with all those who call upon the name of our Lord Jesus Christ in every place, their Lord and ours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Corinthians 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Thanksgiving for the Corinthian Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; John 14:26; 1 Timothy 1:14"> I give thanks to my God always concerning you, because of the grace of God which was given to you in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:11; 2 Corinthians 8:7; Colossians 2:3"> that in everything you were made rich in him, in all speech and all knowledge,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Timothy 2:6"> just as the testimony about Christ has been confirmed in you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 2 Peter 3:12; Hebrews 9:28"> so that you do not lack in any spiritual gift as you eagerly await the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Colossians 1:22"> who will also confirm you until the end, blameless in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Hebrews 10:23; Deuteronomy 7:9"> God is faithful, by whom you were called to fellowship with his Son Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Divisions in the Church at Corinth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:1–4; Ephesians 4:1–7; 1 Corinthians 12:25"> Now I exhort you, brothers, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you all say the same thing and there not be divisions among you, and that you be made complete in the same mind and with the same purpose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:6; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Timothy 2:23–25"> For it has been made clear to me concerning you, my brothers, by ⸤Chloe’s people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “those of Chloe”">b</a></sup>, that there are quarrels among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:21–23; John 1:42; Matthew 23:9–10"> But I say this, that each of you is saying, “I am with Paul,” and “I am with Apollos,” and “I am with Cephas,” and “I am with Christ.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; 2 Corinthians 11:4; Acts 2:38"> Has Christ been divided? Paul was not crucified for you, was he? Or were you baptized in the name of Paul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 18:8; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:4"> I give thanks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “I give thanks to God”">c</a></sup>that I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; John 3:28–29; John 7:18"> lest anyone should say that you were baptized in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:15; 1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 16:33"> (Now I also baptized the household of Stephanas. Beyond that I do not know if I baptized anyone else.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:1; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 2 Peter 1:16"> For Christ did not send me to baptize, but to proclaim the gospel, not with ⸤clever speech⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “wisdom of word”">d</a></sup>, lest the cross of Christ be emptied.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Christ Crucified, the Power and Wisdom of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 2:10"> For the message about the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Jeremiah 8:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will destroy the wisdom of the wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the intelligence of the intelligent I will confound.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 29:14">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Job 12:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> Where is the wise person? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Has not God made foolish the wisdom of the world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 10:21; Romans 1:20–22; Matthew 11:25"> For since, in the wisdom of God, the world through its wisdom did not know God, God was pleased through the foolishness of preaching to save those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:1–4; Matthew 12:38–39; Mark 8:11"> For indeed, Jews ask for sign miracles and Greeks seek wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Peter 2:8"> but we preach Christ crucified, to the Jews a cause for stumbling, but to the Gentiles foolishness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is the power of God and the wisdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 11:33–36; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Zechariah 4:6–7"> For the foolishness of God is wiser than human wisdom,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “man”">f</a></sup>and the weakness of God is stronger than human strength.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “man”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Boast in the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:5; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20; James 1:9–11"> For consider your calling, brothers, that not many were wise according to human standards,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “according to flesh”">h</a></sup>not many were powerful, not many were well born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 11:25; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> But the foolish things of the world God chose in order that he might put to shame the wise, and the weak things of the world God chose in order that he might put to shame the strong,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:24; Romans 4:17; Isaiah 2:11"> and the insignificant of the world, and the despised, God chose, the things that are not, in order that he might abolish the things that are,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 3:27; Jeremiah 9:23; Ephesians 2:9"> so that all flesh may not boast before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Philippians 3:9; Romans 8:1"> But from him you are in Christ Jesus, who became wisdom to us from God, and righteousness and sanctification and redemption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 10:17; Philippians 3:3"> so that, just as it is written, “The one who boasts, let him boast in the Lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A quotation from Jer 9:24">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
