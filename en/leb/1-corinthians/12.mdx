## Varieties of Spiritual Gifts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 14:37; 1 Corinthians 14:1–18"> Now concerning spiritual gifts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “spiritual things ”; possibly “those who possess spiritual gifts”">a</a></sup>brothers, I do not want you to be ignorant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:9; Psalm 115:5"> You know that when you were pagans, you were led astray to the speechless idols, however you were led.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:2–3; Matthew 16:16–17; Romans 10:9"> Therefore I make known to you that no one speaking by the Spirit of God says, “Jesus is accursed,” and no one is able to say “Jesus is Lord” except by the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:4–6"> Now there are varieties of gifts, but the same Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Romans 12:6–8; Romans 14:8–9"> and there are varieties of ministries, and the same Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 12:11; John 5:17"> and there are varieties of activities, but the same God, who works all things in all people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> But to each one is given the manifestation of the Spirit for what is beneficial to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 2:6–10; Psalm 143:10"> For to one is given a word of wisdom through the Spirit, and to another a word of knowledge by the same Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 5:14–15; 2 Corinthians 4:13; Ephesians 2:8"> to another faith by the same Spirit, to another<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and to another”">b</a></sup>gifts of healing by the one Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 John 4:1; Mark 16:17"> to another<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and to another”">c</a></sup>⸤miraculous powers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “activities of power”">d</a></sup>, to another<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and to another”">e</a></sup>prophecy, to another<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and to another”">f</a></sup>distinguishing of spirits, to another kinds of tongues, to another<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and to another”">g</a></sup>interpretation of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4; Hebrews 2:4; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> But in all these things one and the same Spirit is at work, distributing to each one individually just as he wishes.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Unity in the Midst of Diversity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:4–5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> For just as the body is one and has many members, but all the members of the body, although they are many, are one body, thus also Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> For by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “in”">h</a></sup>one Spirit we were all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free persons, and all were made to drink one Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27–28; 1 Corinthians 12:12; Ephesians 4:25"> For the body is not one member, but many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Kings 14:9"> If the foot should say, “Because I am not a hand, I am not a part of the body,” not because of this is it not a part of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:22; Philippians 2:3"> And if the ear should say, “Because I am not an eye, I am not a part of the body, not because of this is it not a part of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:21; Psalm 139:13–16; Proverbs 20:12"> If the whole body were an eye, where would the hearing be? If the whole were hearing, where would the sense of smell be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> But now God has placed the members, each one of them, in the body just as he wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And if they all were one member, where would the body be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:14; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> But now there are many members, but one body.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 10:1–5; Nehemiah 4:16–21; Job 29:11"> Now the eye is not able to say to the hand, “I do not have need of you,” or again, the head to the feet, “I do not have need of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> But by much more the members of the body which are thought to be weaker are necessary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:21; Genesis 3:7"> and the parts of the body which we think to be less honorable, these we clothe with more abundant honor, and our unpresentable parts come to have more abundant presentability,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 3:11; Genesis 2:25"> but our presentable parts do not have need of this. Yet God composed the body by giving more abundant honor to the part which lacked it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; John 17:21–26"> in order that there not be a division in the body, but the members would have the same concern for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Galatians 6:2; Hebrews 13:3"> And if one member suffers, all the members suffer together; if a member<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “one member”">i</a></sup>is honored, all the members rejoice with it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:12; Colossians 1:24; Ephesians 5:30"> Now you are the body of Christ, and members ⸤of it individually⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “by part”">j</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–13; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:18"> and whom God has appointed in the church: first, apostles, second, prophets, third, teachers, then miracles, then gifts of healing, helps, administrations, kinds of tongues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 12:14–20"> Not all are apostles, are they? Not all are prophets, are they? Not all are teachers, are they? Not all are workers of miracles, are they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10"> Not all have gifts of healing, do they? Not all speak with tongues, do they? Not all interpret, do they?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Corinthians 14:39; Matthew 5:6"> But strive<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “you are striving” (some understand the form of this verb to be indicative mood rather than imperative mood)">k</a></sup>for the greater gifts. And I will show you a still more excellent way.</span>
</div>
</div>
