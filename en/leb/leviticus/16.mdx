<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:10; 1 Kings 8:10–12; Hebrews 10:19–20"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Tell<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “Speak to”">c</a></sup>your brother Aaron that he should not enter at any time into the sanctuary ⸤behind⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from the interior/inside to”">d</a></sup>the curtain ⸤in front of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">e</a></sup>the atonement cover that is on the ark, so that he might not die, because I appear in the cloud over the atonement cover.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Hebrews 9:24–25; Hebrews 9:7"> “Aaron must enter the sanctuary with this: ⸤a young bull⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “a bull a son of cattle”">f</a></sup>as a sin offering and a ram as a burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:39–43; Ezekiel 44:17–18; Exodus 30:20"> He must put on ⸤a holy linen tunic⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a tunic of linen of holiness”">g</a></sup>and linen undergarments must be on his body, and he must fasten himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">h</a></sup>with a linen sash, and he must wrap a linen turban around his head<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Supplied from context in the English translation">i</a></sup>—they are holy garments, and he shall wash his body with water, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “and”">j</a></sup>he shall put them on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:21; Leviticus 8:2; Ezra 6:17"> And he must take from the ⸤Israelites⸥’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>community two he-goats as a sin offering and one ram as a burnt offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 7:27"> “And Aaron shall present the sin offering’s bull, which is for himself, and so he shall make atonement for himself and for his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Leviticus 4:4; Matthew 16:21"> And he shall take the two goats, and he shall present them ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">l</a></sup>Yahweh at the tent of assembly’s entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:23–26; Jonah 1:7; Proverbs 16:33"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “And”">m</a></sup>Aaron shall cast lots for the two goats: one lot for Yahweh and one for Azazel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:27–28; Acts 2:23"> And Aaron shall present the goat on which the lot for Yahweh fell, and he shall ⸤sacrifice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “do” or “make”">n</a></sup>it as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:2; Leviticus 16:21–22; Hebrews 9:23–24"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “And”">o</a></sup>he must present alive ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">p</a></sup>Yahweh the goat on which the lot for Azazel fell to make atonement for himself, to send it away into the desert to Azazel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:6; Hebrews 7:27; Hebrews 9:7"> “And Aaron shall present the sin offering’s bull, which is for himself, and so he shall make atonement for himself and for his family; then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “and”">q</a></sup>he shall slaughter the sin offering’s bull, which is for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1; Exodus 30:34–38; Revelation 8:3–4"> And he shall take a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">r</a></sup>censer full of ⸤burning charcoal⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “burning charcoal of fire”">s</a></sup>from upon the altar from ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">t</a></sup>Yahweh and ⸤two handfuls⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “the fullness of the hollow of his hands”">u</a></sup>of incense of powdered fragrant perfumes, and he shall bring it ⸤from behind⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “from the interior/inside of”">v</a></sup>the curtain,</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Luke 1:10; Exodus 34:3"> And ⸤no person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “any man not”">ah</a></sup>shall be in the tent of assembly when he enters to make atonement in the sanctuary until he comes out, and so he shall make atonement for himself and for his family and for all of Israel’s assembly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Ezekiel 43:18–22"> And he shall spatter ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “from”">an</a></sup>the blood on it seven times with his finger, and he shall cleanse it and consecrate<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “he shall consecrate”">ao</a></sup>it from the ⸤Israelites⸥’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “of the sons/children of Israel”">ap</a></sup>impurities.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Isaiah 53:11–12; John 1:29"> Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Or “And”">av</a></sup>the goat shall bear on it to ⸤a barren region⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “a land of separation” or “a land of infertility”">aw</a></sup>all their guilt, and he shall send the goat away into the desert.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:10; Hebrews 7:19; Numbers 19:21"> “And the person who sends out the goat for Azazel shall wash his garments, and he shall wash his body with water, and ⸤afterward⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Literally “after thus”">ay</a></sup>he shall come to the camp.</span>
