## Punishment for Blasphemy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Exodus 12:38"> And an Israelite woman’s son, and he was an Egyptian man’s son, went out in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">t</a></sup>and the Israelite woman’s son and an<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">u</a></sup>Israelite man fought in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 24:15–16; Exodus 3:15"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “And”">v</a></sup>the Israelite woman’s son blasphemed the name, and he cursed, so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “and”">w</a></sup>they brought him to Moses—and the name of his mother was Shelomith the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 18:15–16; Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “And”">x</a></sup>they put him in custody ⸤so that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to”">y</a></sup>the matter might be made clear to them in accordance with the mouth of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “And”">z</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 21:21"> “Bring the curser ⸤outside the camp⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “to from an outside place of the camp”">aa</a></sup>and all the hearers shall place their hands on his head, and the whole community shall stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:13; Exodus 22:28; Leviticus 5:1"> And you shall speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ab</a></sup>saying, ‘⸤Any man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “A man a man”">ac</a></sup>when he curses his God shall bear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “and he shall bear”">ad</a></sup>his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 12:31; James 2:7; John 10:33–36"> And he who blasphemes Yahweh’s name certainly shall be put to death; the whole community certainly shall stone him. As the alien, so the native shall be put to death at blaspheming his name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–12; Deuteronomy 27:24"> “ ‘And when a man kills any human being, he certainly shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 24:21; Exodus 21:34–36"> And ⸤he who kills⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “he who kills the soul of” or “he who kills the life of”">ae</a></sup>a domestic animal must repay for it life in place of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:38; Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 19:21"> And when a man ⸤causes⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “gives”">af</a></sup>a physical defect in his fellow citizen ⸤according to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “as that”">ag</a></sup>what he has done, so it shall be done to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38; Exodus 21:23–25"> fracture in place of fracture, eye in place of eye, tooth in place of tooth—⸤according to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “as that”">ah</a></sup>the physical defect he ⸤causes⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “gives”">ai</a></sup>to the person, likewise it shall be ⸤caused⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “given”">aj</a></sup>to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17–18; Exodus 21:33"> And a killer of a domestic animal must repay for it, and a killer of a human shall be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 9:14"> ⸤You must have⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “it shall be for you”">ak</a></sup>one norm; as for the alien, so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “as”">al</a></sup>it must be for the native, because I am Yahweh your God.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 2:2–3; Numbers 15:35–36"> Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “And”">am</a></sup>Moses spoke to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">an</a></sup>and they brought the curser ⸤outside the camp⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “to from an outside place of the camp”">ao</a></sup>and they stoned him with stones, and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ap</a></sup>did just as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
