## Worship at the Tent of Assembly

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Ezekiel 43:26–27; Leviticus 15:14"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And it happened”">a</a></sup>on the eighth day Moses summoned Aaron and his sons and Israel’s elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Hebrews 10:10–14; Leviticus 8:14"> and he said to Aaron, “Take for yourself ⸤a bull calf⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “a bull-calf a son of cattle” or “a bull-calf a son of the herd”">b</a></sup>as a sin offering and a ram as a burnt offering, without defect,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="An adjective in masculine plural to modify both animals; see NET">c</a></sup>and present them ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">d</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 6:17; Revelation 5:9; Titus 2:14"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “And”">e</a></sup>you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Singular">f</a></sup>must speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>saying, ‘Take ⸤a he-goat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a he-goat of goats”">h</a></sup>as a sin offering and a bull calf and a male sheep, ⸤yearlings⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons of a year”">i</a></sup>without defect,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="An adjective in masculine plural to modify both animals; see NET">j</a></sup>as a burnt offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:43; Ezekiel 43:2; Leviticus 9:23"> and an ox and a ram as fellowship offerings to sacrifice ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">k</a></sup>Yahweh, and a grain offering mixed with oil, because today Yahweh will appear to you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Plural">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 5:2–3; Exodus 19:17; Nehemiah 8:1"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “And”">m</a></sup>they took what Moses had commanded to the ⸤front of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">n</a></sup>the tent of assembly, and the whole community presented themselves, and they stood ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">o</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 9:23; Ezekiel 43:2; Exodus 24:16"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “And”">p</a></sup>Moses said, “This is the word that Yahweh commanded you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Plural">q</a></sup>to do ⸤so that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “and”">r</a></sup>the glory of Yahweh might appear to you.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Plural">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 5:3; Hebrews 5:1; Leviticus 4:3"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “And”">t</a></sup>Moses said to Aaron, “Approach<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “Draw near unto”">u</a></sup>the altar and ⸤sacrifice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “do” or “make”">v</a></sup>your sin offering and your burnt offering, and make atonement for yourself and for the people. And ⸤sacrifice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “do” or “make”">w</a></sup>the people’s offering and make atonement for them, just as Yahweh has commanded.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 4:1–12; Leviticus 1:4–5; Leviticus 4:29"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “And”">x</a></sup>Aaron approached<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “drew near unto”">y</a></sup>the altar, and he slaughtered the bull calf of the sin offering, which was for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 4:6–7; Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 4:25"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “And”">z</a></sup>Aaron’s sons presented the blood to him, and he dipped his finger in the blood, and he put it on the altar’s horns, and he poured out the blood on the altar’s base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 8:16–17; Proverbs 23:26; Leviticus 3:9–11"> And the fat and the kidneys and the lobe ⸤from the sin offering’s liver⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “from the liver from the sin offering”">aa</a></sup>he turned into smoke on the altar, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 8:17; Leviticus 4:11–12; Leviticus 16:27–28"> but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “and”">ab</a></sup>the meat and the skin he burned ⸤with fire⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “in the fire”">ac</a></sup>⸤outside the camp⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “from an outside place to/of the camp”">ad</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Ephesians 5:25–27; Leviticus 1:1–17"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “And”">ae</a></sup>he slaughtered the burnt offering, and Aaron’s sons brought the blood to him, and he sprinkled it on the altar all around;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and they brought the burnt offering to him by its pieces, as well as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “and”">af</a></sup>the head, and he turned them into smoke on the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 8:21"> and he washed the inner parts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “entrails”">ag</a></sup>and the lower leg bones, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “and”">ah</a></sup>he turned them into smoke upon the burnt offering on the altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–31; Hebrews 5:3; Leviticus 9:3"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “And”">ai</a></sup>he presented the people’s offering, and he took the goat of the sin offering, which was for the people, and he slaughtered it and offered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “he offered”">aj</a></sup>it as a sin offering like the first one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 9:12–14; Leviticus 8:18–21; Hebrews 10:1–22"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “And”">ak</a></sup>he presented the burnt offering, and he ⸤sacrificed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “did” or “made”">al</a></sup>it according to the regulation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1–2; John 6:53; Leviticus 9:1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “And”">am</a></sup>he presented the grain offering, and he filled his palm ⸤with some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “from”">an</a></sup>it, and he turned it into smoke on the altar besides the morning’s burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Romans 5:1; Ephesians 2:14–17"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “And”">ao</a></sup>he slaughtered the ox and the ram, the fellowship offerings that are for the people, and Aaron’s sons brought the blood to him, and he sprinkled it on the altar all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16; Leviticus 9:10; Leviticus 3:9"> And as for the fat portions from the ox and from the ram (the fat tail and the layer of fat and the kidneys and the lobe of the liver),</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 3:14–17; Leviticus 7:29–34"> they placed the fat portions on the breast sections, and he turned the fat portions into smoke on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Leviticus 7:24; Exodus 29:24"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “And”">ap</a></sup>Aaron waved the breast sections and the right upper thigh as a wave offering ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">aq</a></sup>Yahweh, just as Moses had commanded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Luke 24:50; Numbers 6:23–27"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “And”">ar</a></sup>Aaron lifted his hand toward<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Or “to”">as</a></sup>the people, and he blessed them, and he came down ⸤after⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “from”">at</a></sup>⸤sacrificing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “doing” or “making”">au</a></sup>the sin offering and the burnt offering and the fellowship offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 9:6; Numbers 16:19; Luke 1:21–22"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Or “And”">av</a></sup>Moses and Aaron entered the tent of assembly. When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Or “And”">aw</a></sup>they came out, they blessed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Or “and they blessed”">ax</a></sup>the people, and Yahweh’s glory appeared to all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 6:21; 1 Kings 18:38–39; 1 Chronicles 21:26"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Or “And”">ay</a></sup>a fire went out ⸤from before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Literally “from to the faces of”">az</a></sup>Yahweh, and it consumed the burnt offering and the fat portions on the altar. And all the people saw it, so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Or “and”">ba</a></sup>they shouted for joy, and they fell on their faces.</span>
