## Unlawful Sexual Relations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 11:44; Ezekiel 20:5"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>and say to them, ‘I am Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Exodus 23:24; 1 Peter 4:2–4"> You must not ⸤carry out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “do”">c</a></sup>the practices of the land of Egypt, in which you lived, and you must not ⸤carry out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “do”">d</a></sup>the practices of the land of Canaan, to which I am bringing you; and you must not follow their statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 18:2; Leviticus 18:26; Ezekiel 20:19"> You must ⸤carry out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “do”">e</a></sup>my regulations, and you must observe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “keep”">f</a></sup>my statutes by following them; I am Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Galatians 3:12"> And you shall observe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “keep”">g</a></sup>my statutes and my regulations ⸤by which the person doing them shall live⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “which the man does them and he shall live in them”">h</a></sup>I am Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17–21; Leviticus 18:7–19; Leviticus 20:11–12"> “ ‘⸤None⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a man a man … not”">i</a></sup>of you shall approach anyone who is ⸤his close relative⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the flesh of his body”">j</a></sup>to expose nakedness; I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; Ezekiel 22:10; Leviticus 18:8–16"> You must not expose your father’s nakedness or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “and”">k</a></sup>your mother’s nakedness—she is your mother; you must not expose her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:11; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> You must not expose the nakedness of your father’s wife—it is your father’s nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> As for your sister’s nakedness, whether your father’s daughter or your mother’s daughter, whether ⸤born at home⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “a relative of house”">l</a></sup>or ⸤born abroad⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a relative of an outside place”">m</a></sup>you must not expose their nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> As for the nakedness of your son’s daughter or your daughter’s daughter, you must not expose their nakedness, because they are your nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> As for the nakedness of the daughter of your father’s wife, she is your sister, a relative of your father; you must not expose her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 20:19; Exodus 6:20"> You must not expose the nakedness of your father’s sister; she is ⸤your father’s close relative⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the flesh of your father”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> You must not expose the nakedness of your mother’s sister, because she is ⸤your mother’s close relative⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the flesh of your mother”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:20"> You must not expose the nakedness of your father’s brother; you must not ⸤have sex with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “approach”">p</a></sup>his wife—she is your aunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 20:12; Genesis 38:26; Ezekiel 22:11"> You must not expose your daughter-in-law’s nakedness; she is your son’s wife; you must not expose her nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Leviticus 20:21; Matthew 22:24"> You must not expose the nakedness of your brother’s wife; she is your brother’s nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Amos 2:7; Deuteronomy 27:23"> You must not expose the nakedness of a woman and her daughter, or her son’s daughter, or her daughter’s daughter; you must not take her as wife<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="The verb לקח is used to speak of marrying (“taking a wife ”)">q</a></sup>to expose her nakedness; they are ⸤close relatives⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “flesh”; see vv. 6 , 12, 13">r</a></sup>—that is wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Genesis 30:15; Genesis 29:28"> And you must not take as wife<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="The verb לקח is used to speak of marrying (“taking a wife ”)">s</a></sup>a woman with her sister, to be a rival-wife, to expose her nakedness before her ⸤during⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “in”">t</a></sup>her life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24; Ezekiel 22:10"> “ ‘And you must not ⸤have sex with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “approach”">u</a></sup>a woman to expose her nakedness ⸤during⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “in”">v</a></sup>her menstrual uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; 1 Corinthians 6:9; Hebrews 13:4"> And ⸤you must not have sex⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “you shall not give your lying down for semen”">w</a></sup>with your fellow citizen’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “neighbor’s”">x</a></sup>wife, becoming unclean with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; Leviticus 20:2–5; Deuteronomy 18:10"> “ ‘And you shall not give ⸤any of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “from”">y</a></sup>your offspring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “descendants”">z</a></sup>in order to sacrifice them to Molech, nor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “and not”">aa</a></sup>shall you profane the name of your God; I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:13; Romans 1:26–27; 1 Corinthians 6:9"> And you shall not lie with a male as lying ⸤with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “of”">ab</a></sup>a woman; that is a detestable thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15–16; Leviticus 20:12"> And you shall not ⸤have sexual relations⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “give your lying down”">ac</a></sup>with any animal, becoming unclean with it; and a woman shall not stand ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">ad</a></sup>an animal to copulate with it—that is a perversion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:12; Leviticus 18:30; Leviticus 20:22–23"> “ ‘You must not make yourself unclean in any of these things, because the nations whom I am driving out from your presence were made unclean by all of these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 18:28; Leviticus 20:22–23; Jeremiah 2:7"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “And”">ae</a></sup>the land became unclean, and ⸤I have brought the punishment of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “I have visited”">af</a></sup>its guilt upon it, and the land has vomited out its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Leviticus 17:10; Leviticus 18:30"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “And”">ag</a></sup>you (neither the native nor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “and”">ah</a></sup>the alien who is dwelling in your midst) shall keep my statutes and my regulations, and you shall not ⸤practice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “do”">ai</a></sup>any of these detestable things</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14; Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 23:18"> (because the people of the land, who were ⸤before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of you”">aj</a></sup>did all these detestable things, so the land became unclean),</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 3:16; Leviticus 20:22; Leviticus 18:25"> so that the land will not vomit you out when you make it unclean just as it vomited out the nation that was ⸤before you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of you”">ak</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Exodus 12:15; Leviticus 17:10"> Indeed, anyone who does any of these detestable things, even<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “and”">al</a></sup>those persons who do so shall be cut off from the midst of their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 20:23; Deuteronomy 18:9–12; Leviticus 22:9"> Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “And”">am</a></sup>you shall keep my obligation to not do any of the statutes ⸤regarding⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “of”">an</a></sup>the detestable things that they did ⸤before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of you”">ao</a></sup>so that you will not make yourselves unclean by them; I am Yahweh your God.’ ”</span>
</div>
