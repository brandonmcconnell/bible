## Additional Laws for Guilt Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 6:17; Leviticus 5:14–7; Leviticus 6:25"> “ ‘And this is the regulation of the guilt offering; it is ⸤a most holy thing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a holiness of holinesses”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Leviticus 6:25; Leviticus 1:11"> In the place where they slaughter the burnt offering,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, taking the verb as an indefinite (thus passive) imperfect 3mp, “the sin offering is slaughtered”">b</a></sup>they must slaughter the guilt offering,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, taking the verb as an indefinite (thus passive) imperfect 3mp, “the guilt offering must be slaughtered”">c</a></sup>and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the priest; understood by context and 3ms verb">d</a></sup>must sprinkle its blood upon the altar all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 3:15–16; Psalm 51:6"> And he must present all of its fat:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “all of its fat from it”">e</a></sup>the fat tail and the fat that covers the inner parts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “entrails”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> and the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is on the loins, and he must remove the lobe on the liver in addition to the kidneys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:13; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> And the priest shall turn it into smoke on the altar as a food offering made by fire for Yahweh; it is a guilt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 6:29; Numbers 18:9–10; Leviticus 2:3"> Every male among the priests may eat it; it must be eaten in a holy place; it is ⸤a most holy thing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a holiness of holinesses”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:25–26; Leviticus 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> The instruction is ⸤the same for the guilt offering as for the sin offering⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “as the sin offering as the guilt offering one for them”">h</a></sup>⸤it belongs to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for him it shall be” or “it will become his”">i</a></sup>the priest, who makes atonement with it.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 18:9; Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:10"> And every grain offering that is baked in the oven and all that is prepared in a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">n</a></sup>cooking pan or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “and”">o</a></sup>on a flat baking pan ⸤belongs to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “for him it shall be” or “it will become his”">p</a></sup>the priest who presented it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14; Exodus 16:18"> And every grain offering, whether mixed with oil or dry, shall be for all of Aaron’s sons ⸤equally⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “each as his brother”">q</a></sup></span>

## Additional Laws for Fellowship Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–21; Ezekiel 45:15; Leviticus 3:1–17"> “ ‘And this is the regulation of the fellowship offerings that he must present to Yahweh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Numbers 6:15; 1 Peter 2:5"> If he presents it for thanksgiving, in addition to the thanksgiving sacrifice he shall present ring-shaped unleavened bread mixed with oil and unleavened bread wafers smeared with oil and well-mixed ring-shaped bread cakes of finely milled flour mixed with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 4:5; Leviticus 23:17; Matthew 13:33"> In addition to ring-shaped cakes of bread with yeast, he must present his grain<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Implied by v. 12">r</a></sup>offering together with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “in addition to”">s</a></sup>his sacrifice of thanksgiving peace offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:19; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 18:24–32"> And he shall present one of each kind of grain<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Implied by v. 12">t</a></sup>offering as a contribution for Yahweh; ⸤it belongs to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “for him it shall be” or “it will become his”">u</a></sup>the priest who sprinkles the fellowship offerings’ blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 22:29–30"> And the meat of the sacrifice of his thanksgiving fellowship offerings must be eaten on the day of his offering; he must not leave it until morning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:5–8; Deuteronomy 12:6; Psalm 116:14"> “ ‘But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “And”">v</a></sup>if his sacrifice is for a vow or as a freewill offering, it must be eaten on the day of his presenting his sacrifice, and on the next day the remainder<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “and the remainder”">w</a></sup>from it may be eaten,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Hosea 6:2; 1 Corinthians 15:4"> but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “and”">x</a></sup>the remainder from the sacrifice’s meat must be burned up in the fire on the third day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:7–8; Leviticus 11:41; Numbers 18:27"> And if indeed some of the meat of his fellowship offerings’ sacrifice is eaten on the third day, it will not be accepted; it will not be considered of benefit for the one who presented it—it shall be unclean meat, and the person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “soul”">y</a></sup>who eats it shall bear his guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 11:41; Titus 1:15; Acts 10:15–16"> And the meat that touches anything unclean must not be eaten; it must be burned with fire, and as for the clean<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Understood by context">z</a></sup>meat, anyone who is clean may eat the meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:2–33; Genesis 17:14; Leviticus 22:3–7"> And the person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “the soul”">aa</a></sup>who eats meat from the fellowship offerings’ sacrifice, which is for Yahweh, and whose uncleanness is upon him—that person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “the soul”">ab</a></sup>shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–59; Leviticus 11:20; Ezekiel 4:14"> And when a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “a soul”">ac</a></sup>touches anything unclean, whether human uncleanness or an unclean animal or any unclean detestable thing, and he eats from the meat of the fellowship offerings’ sacrifice, which is for Yahweh, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “and”">ad</a></sup>that person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “the soul”">ae</a></sup>shall be cut off from his people.’ ”</span>
</div>

## Instructions for the People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “And”">af</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16–17; Leviticus 17:6; 1 Samuel 2:15–17"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ag</a></sup>saying, ‘You<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Plural">ah</a></sup>must not eat any fat of ox, or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “and”">ai</a></sup>sheep, or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “and”">aj</a></sup>goat;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Leviticus 17:15; Leviticus 22:8"> and a dead body’s fat or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “and”">ak</a></sup>mangled carcass’s fat may be used for any ⸤purpose⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “work”">al</a></sup>but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “and”">am</a></sup>you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Plural">an</a></sup>certainly must not eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> When anyone eats fat from the domestic animal from which he presented an offering made by fire for Yahweh, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “and”">ao</a></sup>that person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “the soul”">ap</a></sup>who ate shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Leviticus 3:17; Acts 15:20"> And in any of your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Plural">aq</a></sup>dwellings, you must not eat any blood ⸤belonging to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “to/of”">ar</a></sup>birds<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Hebrew “the bird”; generic article with a collective noun">as</a></sup>or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “and”">at</a></sup>domestic animals.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Hebrew “the domestic animal”; generic article with a collective noun">au</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 10:29; Leviticus 7:20–21; Leviticus 7:25"> Any person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Or “any soul” or “all soul(s)”">av</a></sup>who eats any blood, that person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Or “and that soul”">aw</a></sup>shall be cut off from his people.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1–46; Leviticus 6:1–30; Leviticus 7:3"> As for the one from Aaron’s sons who presents the blood of the fellowship offerings and the fat, the right upper thigh ⸤shall belong to him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Literally “for him it shall be”">bc</a></sup>as his share,</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Leviticus 8:12; Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 40:13–15"> This is what Yahweh commanded to give them from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">bh</a></sup>on the day of his anointing them; it is a lasting statute for their generations.</span>
