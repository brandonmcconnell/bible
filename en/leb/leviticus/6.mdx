## Additional Laws for Grain Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Leviticus 2:9"> and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="That is, the priest; understood by context and 3ms verb">aj</a></sup>in his fist shall take away from it ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “from”">ak</a></sup>the grain offering’s finely milled flour, and ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “from”">al</a></sup>its oil and all of the frankincense that is on the grain offering, and he shall turn into smoke its token portion on the altar as an appeasing fragrance to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 10:12–13"> And Aaron and his sons must eat the remainder of it; they must eat it as unleavened bread in a holy place—in the tent of assembly’s courtyard they must eat it.</span>

## Additional Laws for Sin Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:24; Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 1:3"> “Speak to Aaron and his sons, saying, ‘This is the regulation of the sin offering: In the place where the sin offering is slaughtered, the sin offering must be slaughtered ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">aw</a></sup>Yahweh; it is ⸤a most holy thing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Literally “a holiness of holinesses”">ax</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:17–18; Ezekiel 44:28–29; Hosea 4:8"> The priest ⸤who offers the sin offering⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Literally “one who offers it a sin offering”">ay</a></sup>must eat it in a holy place—in the tent of assembly’s courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 29:37; Haggai 2:12; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> Anything that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Or “Everyone who”">az</a></sup>touches its flesh will become holy, and when ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Literally “from”">ba</a></sup>its blood spatters on a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">bb</a></sup>garment, what was spattered on it you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Singular masculine">bc</a></sup>shall wash in a holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:33; Leviticus 15:12; Hebrews 9:9–10"> And a clay vessel in which it was boiled must be broken, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="Or “and”">bd</a></sup>if it was boiled in a bronze vessel, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-be" id="fnref-be" data-fn="Or “and”">be</a></sup>it shall be thoroughly scoured and rinsed with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 6:25; Leviticus 6:17–18; Numbers 18:10"> Any male among the priests may eat it; it is ⸤a most holy thing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="Literally “a holiness of holinesses”">bf</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 10:18; Leviticus 16:27–28; Hebrews 13:11"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="Or “And”">bg</a></sup>any sin offering from which ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Literally “from”">bh</a></sup>its blood is brought to the tent of assembly to make atonement in the sanctuary must not be eaten; it must be burned in the fire.’ ”</span>
