## Laws for Fellowship Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 6:14; Leviticus 1:3; Romans 5:1–2"> “ ‘Now if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And if”">a</a></sup>his offering is a sacrifice of fellowship offering, if he brings it from the cattle,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “the herd”">b</a></sup>whether male or female, he must bring it without defect before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 29:10; Leviticus 1:4–5; Acts 2:36–38"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “And he”">c</a></sup>must lay his hand on the head of his offering and slaughter it at the entrance of the tent of assembly, and Aaron’s sons shall sprinkle the blood on the altar all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 4:8–9; Exodus 29:22"> He shall present<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “And he shall present”">d</a></sup>from the sacrifice of the fellowship offering an offering made by fire for Yahweh consisting of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Hebrew accusative implies this transition">e</a></sup>the fat covering the inner parts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “entrails”">f</a></sup>and all the fat that is on the inner parts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “entrails”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 3:10; Leviticus 8:25; Exodus 29:22"> the two kidneys,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “and the two kidneys”">h</a></sup>and the fat that is on them, which is on the loins, and he must remove the lobe on the liver in addition to the kidneys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12; Exodus 29:13; 1 Peter 2:5"> Aaron’s sons shall turn it to smoke on the altar in addition to the burnt offering that is on the wood, which is on the fire; it is an offering made by fire as an appeasing fragrance for Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:11–12; Galatians 3:28; Leviticus 1:2"> “ ‘But if his offering for a sacrifice of fellowship offering for Yahweh is from the flock,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">i</a></sup>he must bring a male or a female without defect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 8:62; Ephesians 5:12; Leviticus 17:8–9"> If he brings a sheep as his offering, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “and”">j</a></sup>he shall present it before Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Ephesians 3:12; Leviticus 3:13"> and he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering, and he shall slaughter it before the tent of assembly, and Aaron’s sons shall sprinkle its blood on the altar all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 29:22; Leviticus 9:19; Leviticus 8:25"> He shall present<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “And he shall present”">k</a></sup>from the sacrifice of the fellowship offering an offering made by fire for Yahweh: he must remove its fat, the entire fat tail near the tailbone, and the fat covering the inner parts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “entrails”">l</a></sup>and all the fat that is on the inner parts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “entrails”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> the two kidneys,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “and the two kidneys”">n</a></sup>and the fat that is on them, which is on the loins, and he must remove the lobe on the liver in addition to the kidneys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 21:17; Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 3:16"> The priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “And the priest”">o</a></sup>shall turn it into smoke on the altar as a food offering made by fire for Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 9:15; Isaiah 53:2; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> “ ‘And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “But”">p</a></sup>if his offering is a goat, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “and”">q</a></sup>he shall bring it before Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 3:8; Romans 5:15–21; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> and he shall lay his hand on the head of his offering, and he shall slaughter it before the tent of assembly, and Aaron’s sons shall sprinkle its blood on the altar all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:38; Matthew 22:37; Leviticus 3:9–11"> He shall present<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “And he shall present”">r</a></sup>his offering from it as an offering made by fire for Yahweh: the fat covering the inner parts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “entrails”">s</a></sup>and all the fat that is on the inner parts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “entrails”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> the two kidneys,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “and the two kidneys”">u</a></sup>and the fat that is on them, which is on the loins, and he must remove the lobe on the liver in addition to the kidneys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:23–25; Matthew 22:37; Isaiah 53:10"> The priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “And the priest”">v</a></sup>shall turn them into smoke on the altar as a food offering;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Compare v. 11">w</a></sup>all the fat is an offering made by fire as an appeasing fragrance for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Matthew 26:28; Deuteronomy 15:23"> This is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Understood by context">x</a></sup>a lasting statute for your generations in all your dwellings: you must not eat any fat or any blood.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
