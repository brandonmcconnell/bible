## Laws for Burnt Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 19:3; Exodus 25:22; Exodus 29:42"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh called to Moses and spoke to him from the tent of assembly, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 22:18–19; Romans 12:6; 1 Chronicles 16:29"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>and say to them, ‘When a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “man”—the singular noun is generic, thus the “you” and “your” in the remainder of the verse are all plural">c</a></sup>from you presents an offering to Yahweh, you shall present your offering from domestic animals, from the cattle<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “herd”">d</a></sup>or from the flock.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Hebrews 9:14; Leviticus 22:19–24"> If his offering is a burnt offering from the cattle,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “herd”">f</a></sup>then he must present<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “present it”">g</a></sup>an unblemished male; he must present it at the door of the tent of assembly for his acceptance ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">h</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:10; Exodus 29:15; Leviticus 4:20"> “ ‘He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “And he”">i</a></sup>must lay his hand on the head of the burnt offering and it will be accepted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Same root word as “acceptance” in v. 3">j</a></sup>for him in order to make atonement for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 1:11; Leviticus 3:8; Leviticus 3:2"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “And he”">k</a></sup>must slaughter ⸤the young bull⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the son of the herd” or “the son of the cattle”">l</a></sup>⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">m</a></sup>Yahweh, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “and”">n</a></sup>Aaron’s sons, the priests, will present the blood and sprinkle the blood all around the altar that is at the door of the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:8; Genesis 3:21"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “And”">o</a></sup>he must remove the skin of the burnt offering and cut it into its pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Genesis 22:9; 1 Chronicles 21:26"> The sons of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “And the sons of”">p</a></sup>Aaron the priest will put fire on the altar and arrange the wood on the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 9:13–14; Leviticus 1:12; 1 Kings 18:33"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “And”">q</a></sup>Aaron’s sons, the priests, will arrange the pieces of meat,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Verse 6 in the context uses the same word with reference to the meat">r</a></sup>the head, and the suet on the wood that is on the fire that is on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 8:21; Leviticus 1:13; Ephesians 5:2"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “And”">s</a></sup>he must wash its inner parts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “entrails”">t</a></sup>and its lower leg bones with water, and the priest will turn into smoke the whole animal on the altar as a burnt offering by fire, as an appeasing fragrance for Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6–7; Leviticus 1:2–3; Genesis 8:20"> “ ‘But if his offering is from the flock,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">u</a></sup>from the sheep or from the goats for a burnt offering, he must present<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “present it”">v</a></sup>an unblemished<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Same word as in v. 3">w</a></sup>male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 1:5; Exodus 40:22; Leviticus 7:2"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “And he”">x</a></sup>must slaughter it on the north side of the altar ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">y</a></sup>Yahweh; then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “and”">z</a></sup>Aaron’s sons, the priests, will sprinkle its blood all around the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “And”">aa</a></sup>he must cut it into pieces along with its head and its suet; and the priest will arrange them on the wood that is on the fire that is on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 1:9"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “And”">ab</a></sup>he must wash the inner parts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “entrails”">ac</a></sup>and the lower leg bones with water and the priest shall present the whole animal and will turn it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Understood direct object">ad</a></sup>into smoke on the altar; it is a burnt offering by fire as an appeasing fragrance for Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Luke 2:24; Leviticus 12:8"> “ ‘But if his offering for Yahweh is a burnt offering from the birds, then he must present his offering from the turtledoves or from ⸤the young doves⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the dove”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:8–9; Psalm 22:21; 1 John 2:27"> The priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “And the priest”">af</a></sup>will present it at the altar and must wring off its head and turn it into smoke on the altar, and its blood will be drained out on the wall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “side”">ag</a></sup>of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 1:2; Luke 1:35; Leviticus 16:27"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “And he”">ah</a></sup>must remove its crop with its plumage and throw it to the east beside the altar on the place of the fatty ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:10; John 19:30; Leviticus 1:9–10"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “And”">ai</a></sup>he must tear it apart by its wings but must not sever it; then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “and”">aj</a></sup>the priest will turn it into smoke on the altar. It is a burnt offering by fire as an appeasing fragrance for Yahweh.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
