## Priests and Their Food

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Deuteronomy 15:19; Leviticus 22:32"> “Tell<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “Speak to”">b</a></sup>Aaron and his sons<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to Aaron and to his sons”">c</a></sup>that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “and”">d</a></sup>they must deal respectfully with the ⸤Israelites⸥’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>votive offerings, and they must not profane my holy name, which they are consecrating to me; I am Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 7:20–21; Numbers 19:13; Psalm 16:11"> “Say to them, ‘Throughout your generations, any man from any of your offspring who comes near the votive offerings that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>consecrate to Yahweh with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “and”">g</a></sup>his uncleanness on him, that person shall be cut off<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “and that person shall be cut off”">h</a></sup>from ⸤before me⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of me”">i</a></sup>I am Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 11:39; Leviticus 15:2–3; Leviticus 15:13–16"> “ ‘⸤Anyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “A man a man”">j</a></sup>from Aaron’s offspring, if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “and”">k</a></sup>he is afflicted with a skin disease or a fluid discharge, shall not eat in the sanctuary ⸤until⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “until which”">l</a></sup>he is clean; and the one who touches any unclean person or a man from whom an emission of semen goes out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:43–44; Leviticus 15:7; Leviticus 15:19"> or a man who touches any swarmer that is unclean for him or who touches a person who is unclean for him ⸤due to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “for”">m</a></sup>whatever his uncleanness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Hebrews 10:22; Haggai 2:13"> a person who touches such a thing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “him” or “it”">n</a></sup>shall be unclean until the evening, and he shall not eat from the votive offerings, except<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “but if” or “but rather”">o</a></sup>when he washes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “bathes”">p</a></sup>his body with water</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:22; Deuteronomy 18:3–4; 1 Corinthians 9:13–14"> and ⸤the sun sets⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the sun goes” or “the sun enters”">q</a></sup>and he shall be clean; then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “and”">r</a></sup>afterward he may eat from the votive offerings, because it is his food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 17:15; Exodus 22:31; Ezekiel 44:31"> He shall not eat a naturally dead body or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “and”">s</a></sup>a mangled carcass, ⸤so that he becomes unclean⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “to become unclean”">t</a></sup>by it; I am Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 28:43; Numbers 18:22; Numbers 18:32"> “ ‘And they shall keep my obligation, and they shall not incur guilt because of it, so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “and”">u</a></sup>they die through it, because they have profaned it; I am Yahweh who consecrates them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 12:4; 1 Samuel 21:6; Exodus 29:33"> “ ‘⸤No stranger shall eat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “And any stranger shall not eat”">v</a></sup>the votive offering; nor shall a temporary resident with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “of”">w</a></sup>a priest or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “and”">x</a></sup>a hired worker eat the votive offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:13; Exodus 12:44; Numbers 18:11–13"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “And”">y</a></sup>a priest, if with his money he buys a person as ⸤his possession⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “property of”">z</a></sup>that one may eat it, and the descendants of his house themselves may eat his food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; Leviticus 21:3"> And a priest’s daughter, when ⸤she marries a layman⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “she becomes for a strange man”">aa</a></sup>she herself may not eat ⸤the votive offering⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “the offering/lifting of the votive offering”">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 10:14; Genesis 38:11; Numbers 18:11–19"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “And”">ac</a></sup>a priest’s daughter, when she becomes a widow or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “and”">ad</a></sup>divorced or there is no offspring for her, and she returns to her father’s house as in her childhood, she may eat from her father’s food, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “and”">ae</a></sup>⸤no layman may eat it⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “any stranger shall not eat it”">af</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 27:13; Leviticus 5:15–19"> And if a man eats the votive offering unintentionally, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “and”">ag</a></sup>he shall add to it a fifth of it, and he shall give the votive offering to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 18:32; Leviticus 19:8; Ezekiel 22:26"> And they shall not profane the ⸤Israelites⸥’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ah</a></sup>votive offerings that they present to Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:11–12"> and so cause them, by their eating their votive offerings, to bear guilt requiring a guilt offering, because I am Yahweh, who consecrates them.’ ”</span>
</div>

## Acceptable Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “And”">ai</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2; Deuteronomy 12:6; Numbers 15:3"> “Speak to Aaron and to his sons and to all the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">aj</a></sup>and say to them, ‘Anyone from the house of Israel or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “and”">ak</a></sup>from the alien in Israel who presents his offering for any of their vows or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “and”">al</a></sup>for any of their freewill offerings that they present to Yahweh as a burnt offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 1:10; Leviticus 1:3; Ephesians 5:27"> it must be without defect ⸤to be acceptable for you⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “for your acceptance”">am</a></sup>a male among the cattle, among the sheep, or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “and”">an</a></sup>among the goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:1; Malachi 1:8; Deuteronomy 15:21"> You shall not present any animal in which is a physical defect, because it shall not be ⸤acceptable⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “for acceptance”">ao</a></sup>for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 15:8; Numbers 15:3; Leviticus 3:6"> And if anyone brings a sacrifice of fellowship offerings for Yahweh to fulfill a vow or as a freewill offering from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “among”">ap</a></sup>the cattle or from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “among”">aq</a></sup>the flock,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">ar</a></sup>it must be without defect ⸤to be acceptable⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “for acceptance”">as</a></sup>there must not be any physical defect in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Malachi 1:8; Leviticus 1:9"> The blind or the injured or the maimed or the seeping or one with a skin disorder or one with a skin eruption—these you shall not present to Yahweh, nor shall you give from them an offering made by fire on the altar for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:18"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “And”">at</a></sup>an ox or sheep that is deformed or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Or “and”">au</a></sup>that is stunted, you may present it as a freewill offering, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Or “and”">av</a></sup>for a vow it will not be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20; Deuteronomy 23:1; Leviticus 21:20"> And you shall not present anything for Yahweh with bruised or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Or “and”">aw</a></sup>shattered or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Or “and”">ax</a></sup>torn or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Or “and”">ay</a></sup>cut-off testicles, and you shall not ⸤sacrifice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Literally “do” or “make”">az</a></sup>such in your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 21:8; Ezra 6:8–10; Numbers 15:14–16"> And you shall not present your God’s food from any of these by the hand of ⸤a foreigner⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Literally “a son of a foreign land”">ba</a></sup>because their deformity is in them; a physical defect is in them; they shall not be accepted for you.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Or “And”">bb</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:30; Leviticus 19:23–24; Leviticus 22:25"> “When an ox or a sheep or a goat is born, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Or “and”">bc</a></sup>it shall be under its mother for seven days, and from the eighth day and beyond it is acceptable as an offering made by fire for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:6–7; Deuteronomy 14:21; Exodus 34:26"> And you shall not slaughter an ox or a sheep and ⸤its young⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="Literally “his son” or “his child”">bd</a></sup>on ⸤the same day⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-be" id="fnref-be" data-fn="Literally “on day one” or “in one day”">be</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalm 116:17; Psalm 107:22; Amos 4:5"> And when you sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving to Yahweh, you must sacrifice it to be acceptable for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 7:15–18; Exodus 16:19–20; Leviticus 19:7"> It must be eaten on that day; you must not leave over anything from it until morning; I am Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Leviticus 19:37; Numbers 15:40"> “Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="Or “And”">bf</a></sup>you shall keep my commands, and you shall do them; I am Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Luke 11:2; Matthew 6:9"> “And you shall not profane my holy name, so that I may be consecrated in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">bg</a></sup>I am Yahweh, who consecrates you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 11:45; Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 25:38"> the one who brought you out from the land of Egypt to be as God for you; I am Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
