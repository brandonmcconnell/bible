<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:10"> Six years you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Singular throughout this verse">g</a></sup>shall sow your field, and six years you shall prune your vineyard, and you shall gather its yield.</span>
