## More Laws Regarding Sin Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Proverbs 29:24; Leviticus 19:8"> “ ‘When a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “a soul”">a</a></sup>sins in that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “and”">b</a></sup>he hears the utterance of a curse and he is a witness or he sees or he knows, if he does not make it known, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “and”">c</a></sup>he shall bear his guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Deuteronomy 14:8; Haggai 2:13"> Or if a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “a soul”">d</a></sup>touches anything unclean, ⸤whether⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “or”">e</a></sup>an unclean wild<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Implied by the following phrase specifying domestic animals">f</a></sup>animal’s dead body or an unclean domestic animal’s dead body or an unclean swarmer’s dead body, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “and”">g</a></sup>⸤he is unaware of it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “it is concealed from him”">h</a></sup>he is unclean and he is guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–16; Leviticus 15:1–33; Leviticus 12:1–8"> Or when he touches human uncleanness, ⸤namely⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for” or “to” (see HALOT 510)">i</a></sup>any uncleanness of his by which he might become unclean, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “and”">j</a></sup>⸤he is unaware of it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “it is concealed from him”">k</a></sup>and he himself finds out, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “and”">l</a></sup>he will be guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:2–6; Acts 23:12; Judges 11:31"> Or when a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “a soul”">m</a></sup>swears, speaking thoughtlessly with his lips, to do evil or to do good ⸤with regard to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “for” or “to” (see HALOT 510)">n</a></sup>anything that ⸤the person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the man”">o</a></sup>in a sworn oath speaks thoughtlessly, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “and”">p</a></sup>⸤he is unaware of it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “it is concealed from him”">q</a></sup>he will be guilty ⸤in any of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “for one of”">r</a></sup>these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Numbers 5:7; Leviticus 26:40"> When he becomes guilty ⸤in any of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “for one of”">s</a></sup>these, he shall confess<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “and he shall confess”">t</a></sup>what he has sinned ⸤regarding⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “upon” or “against”">u</a></sup>it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 4:28; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:20"> and he shall bring his guilt offering to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “for”">v</a></sup>Yahweh for his sin that he has ⸤committed⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sinned”">w</a></sup>a female from the flock,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">x</a></sup>a ewe-lamb or ⸤a she-goat⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “a she-goat of goats”">y</a></sup>as a sin offering, and the priest shall make atonement for him for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “from”">z</a></sup>his sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 14:21–22; Matthew 3:16"> “ ‘If<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “And if”">aa</a></sup>⸤he cannot afford a sheep⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “his hand does not touch enough of small livestock”">ab</a></sup>he shall bring as his guilt offering for what he sinned two turtledoves or two ⸤young doves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “sons of dove” or “children of dove”">ac</a></sup>for Yahweh, one for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:15; Leviticus 1:17; Romans 4:25"> He shall bring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “And he shall bring”">ad</a></sup>them to the priest, and he shall present that which is for the sin offering first, and the priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Required by the previous action (see NET)">ae</a></sup>shall wring its head off ⸤at the back of its neck⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “from opposite its neck”">af</a></sup>but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “and”">ag</a></sup>he must not sever it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 4:30; Leviticus 4:34; Leviticus 4:18"> and he shall spatter ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “from”">ah</a></sup>the sin offering’s blood on the altar’s side, and the leftover blood must be drained out on the altar’s base; it is a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:14–17; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> The second<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “And the second”">ai</a></sup>bird he must prepare as a burnt offering according to the regulation, and the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin that he has ⸤committed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “sinned”">aj</a></sup>and he shall be forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Leviticus 2:1–2; Exodus 16:36"> “ ‘But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “And”">ak</a></sup>if ⸤he cannot afford⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “his hand cannot produce for”">al</a></sup>two turtledoves or two ⸤young doves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “sons of dove” or “children of dove”">am</a></sup>then,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “and”">an</a></sup>because he has sinned, he shall bring as his offering a tenth of an<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">ao</a></sup>ephah of finely milled flour as a sin offering. He must not put<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “And he must not put”">ap</a></sup>oil on it, nor should he put frankincense on it, because it is a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35; Leviticus 2:9; Leviticus 2:2"> He shall bring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “And he shall bring”">aq</a></sup>it to the priest, and the priest ⸤shall take a handful of it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “shall scoop up from it the fullness of his handful”">ar</a></sup>for its token portion, and he shall turn it to smoke on the altar ⸤in addition to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “on” or “upon”">as</a></sup>the offerings made by fire to Yahweh; it is a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 4:26; 1 Corinthians 9:13"> Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “And”">at</a></sup>the priest shall make atonement for him because of the sin that he has ⸤committed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “sinned”">au</a></sup>⸤in any of these⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “from one from these”">av</a></sup>and he shall be forgiven. It shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Or “And it shall be”">aw</a></sup>for the priest, like the grain offering.’ ”</span>

## Laws for Guilt Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Or “And”">ax</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 6:6"> “When a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Or “a soul”">ay</a></sup>⸤displays infidelity⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Literally “acts unfaithfully infidelity” or “is unfaithful in unfaithfulness”">az</a></sup>and he sins in an unintentional wrong ⸤in any of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Literally “from”">ba</a></sup>Yahweh’s holy things, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Or “and”">bb</a></sup>he shall bring his guilt offering to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Or “for”">bc</a></sup>Yahweh: a ram without defect from the flock<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">bd</a></sup>as a guilt offering by your valuation in silver shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-be" id="fnref-be" data-fn="Or “convertible into silver shekels” (NET, NRSV, TNK), “of the proper value in silver” (NIV; cp. ESV “valued in silver shekels,” CSB), or “or you may buy one of equal value with silver” (NLT)">be</a></sup>according to the sanctuary shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 22:14; Numbers 5:7; Leviticus 27:27"> And he shall make restitution for what he sinned ⸤because of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="Literally “from” (see HALOT 598)">bf</a></sup>a holy thing and shall add one-fifth of ⸤its value⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="Literally “it”">bg</a></sup>onto it and shall give it to the priest. The priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Or “And the priest”">bh</a></sup>shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and he will be forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 4:13; Leviticus 4:2–4"> “If<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bi" id="fnref-bi" data-fn="Or “And if”">bi</a></sup>a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bj" id="fnref-bj" data-fn="Or “a soul”">bj</a></sup>when he sins ⸤violates⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bk" id="fnref-bk" data-fn="Literally “and she/it does”">bk</a></sup>one from all of Yahweh’s commands that should not ⸤be violated⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bl" id="fnref-bl" data-fn="Literally “they are to be done”">bl</a></sup>but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bm" id="fnref-bm" data-fn="Or “and”">bm</a></sup>he did not know, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bn" id="fnref-bn" data-fn="Or “and”">bn</a></sup>he is guilty and he shall bear his guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 5:15–16; 1 Timothy 2:5–6; Leviticus 1:4"> He shall bring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bo" id="fnref-bo" data-fn="Or “And he shall bring”">bo</a></sup>to the priest a ram without defect from the flock<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bp" id="fnref-bp" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">bp</a></sup>as a guilt offering by your valuation, and the priest shall make atonement for him because of his unintentional wrong (although<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bq" id="fnref-bq" data-fn="Or “and”">bq</a></sup>he himself did not know), and he will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 51:4; Malachi 3:8; 2 Corinthians 5:19–21"> It is a guilt offering; he certainly was guilty before Yahweh.”</span>
