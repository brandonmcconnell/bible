## Laws for Grain Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 6:14–18; Leviticus 9:17; Isaiah 66:20"> “ ‘When a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “a soul”">a</a></sup>brings a grain offering to Yahweh, his offering must be finely milled flour, and he must pour out oil on it and place frankincense on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 5:12; Leviticus 6:15; Acts 10:4"> And he shall bring it to the sons of Aaron, the priests, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the priest—see v. 9">b</a></sup>shall take ⸤his handful from its finely milled flour⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “from there his handful from its finely milled flour”">c</a></sup>and from its oil in addition to all its frankincense. The priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “And the priest”">d</a></sup>shall turn its token portion into smoke on the altar as an offering made by fire, as an appeasing fragrance for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 10:12–13; Numbers 18:9; Leviticus 21:22"> The remainder<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “And the remainder”">e</a></sup>of the grain offering ⸤belongs to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “for”">f</a></sup>Aaron and to his sons—⸤it is a most holy thing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a holiness of holinesses”">g</a></sup>from the offerings made by fire for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “of”">h</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Psalm 22:14"> “ ‘But if you bring a grain offering of something oven-baked, it must be of finely milled flour as ring-shaped unleavened bread mixed with oil or wafers of unleavened bread smeared with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 6:21; Leviticus 7:9"> If your offering is a grain offering baked on a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">i</a></sup>flat baking pan, it must be finely milled flour, unleavened bread mixed with oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:1–19; Psalm 22:1–21; Mark 14:1–15"> break it into pieces and pour out oil on it; it is a grain offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1–2; Leviticus 7:9"> “ ‘If<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “And if”">j</a></sup>your offering is a grain offering prepared in a cooking pan, it must be with finely milled flour in oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And you shall bring the grain offering that is made from these things to Yahweh, and the offerer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Changing from 2ms in the first verb to 3ms in the second verb apparently moves from general to specific; NET takes the second verb as an imperative (“Present it”), and NJPS translates it as an indefinite 3ms, making it passive (“it shall be brought”)">k</a></sup>shall bring it to the priest, and he shall bring it to the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Philippians 4:18; Leviticus 6:15"> And the priest shall take away from the grain offering its token portion, and he shall turn it into smoke on the altar as an offering made by fire, as an appeasing fragrance for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3"> And the remainder of the grain offering ⸤belongs to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “for”">l</a></sup>Aaron and to his sons—⸤it is a most holy thing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a holiness of holinesses”">m</a></sup>from the offerings made by fire for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “of”">n</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; Luke 12:1; Exodus 23:18"> “ ‘Every grain offering you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="The first time a plural 2m verb has been employed since 1:2">o</a></sup>bring to Yahweh must not be made of yeasted food, because you must not turn into smoke any yeast or any honey from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “from it”">p</a></sup>an offering made by fire for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 23:19; Genesis 23:10–11; Exodus 22:29"> As an offering of the choicest portion, you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Another occurrence of the 2mp">q</a></sup>may bring them to Yahweh, but they must not be offered on the altar as an appeasing fragrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:24; Matthew 5:13; Colossians 4:6"> Also all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “And all”">r</a></sup>of your grain offerings you must season with salt; you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “and you”">s</a></sup>must not omit the salt of your God’s covenant from your offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Malachi 1:11; Numbers 28:2"> “ ‘And if you bring to Yahweh a grain offering of firstfruits, you must bring an ear of new grain roasted by fire, coarsely crushed ripe grain, as the grain offering of your firstfruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And you shall put oil on it and place frankincense on it; it is a grain offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Romans 8:26–27; Isaiah 61:1"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “And the”">t</a></sup>priest shall turn into smoke its token portion from its coarsely crushed grain together with all of its frankincense—it is an offering made by fire for Yahweh.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
