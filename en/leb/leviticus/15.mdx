## Instructions About Bodily Discharges

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Leviticus 13:1; Leviticus 11:1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 2 Samuel 3:29; Leviticus 22:4"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>and you shall say to them, ‘⸤Any man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a man a man”">c</a></sup>when a fluid discharge occurs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “becomes” or “shall be”">d</a></sup>from his body, his fluid discharge is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:20; Ezekiel 16:26; Leviticus 12:3"> And this becomes his uncleanness in his fluid discharge: whether his body secretes his fluid discharge or his body blocks his fluid discharge, it is his uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Corinthians 15:33"> Any bed upon which the person who discharges lies down becomes unclean, and any object upon which he sits becomes unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 16:26; Leviticus 17:15"> And anyone who touches his bed must wash his garments and shall wash<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “bathe”">e</a></sup>himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">f</a></sup>with water, and he shall be unclean until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 1:16"> And the person who sits on the object upon which the person who discharges has sat must wash his garments, and he shall wash<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “bathe”">g</a></sup>himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">h</a></sup>with water, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the person who touches the body of the person who discharges must wash his garments, and he shall wash<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “bathe”">i</a></sup>himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">j</a></sup>with water, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:4; 2 Peter 2:1–3; Isaiah 1:16"> And if the person who discharges spits on one who is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The generic article designating a class of persons or things">k</a></sup>clean, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “and”">l</a></sup>that one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">m</a></sup>shall wash his garments, and he shall wash himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">n</a></sup>with water, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:34"> And any saddle upon which the person who discharges rides becomes unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 4:8; Psalm 26:6; Leviticus 15:8"> And any person who touches anything that happened to be under him becomes unclean until the evening, and the person who carries them must wash his garments, and he shall wash himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">o</a></sup>with water, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And anyone whom the person who discharges might touch ⸤without⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and not”">p</a></sup>rinsing off his hands with water shall wash his garments, and he shall wash himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">q</a></sup>with water, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:32–33; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “And”">r</a></sup>a clay vessel that the person who discharges touches must be broken, and any ⸤wood object⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “object of wood”">s</a></sup>must be rinsed with water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 14:8; Leviticus 8:33; Leviticus 15:28"> “ ‘And when the person who discharges becomes clean from his body fluid discharge, he shall count<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “and he shall count”">t</a></sup>for himself seven days for his cleansing; then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “and”">u</a></sup>he shall wash his garments, and he shall wash his body with ⸤fresh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “running”; literally “living”">v</a></sup>water, and he shall be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 12:8; Leviticus 12:6; Hebrews 10:10"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “And”">w</a></sup>on the eighth day he shall take for himself two turtledoves or two ⸤young doves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “sons of dove”">x</a></sup>and he shall come ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">y</a></sup>Yahweh at the tent of assembly’s entrance, and he shall give them to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 4:26; Matthew 3:17"> And the priest shall ⸤sacrifice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “do” or “make”">z</a></sup>one as a sin offering and ⸤the other⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “the one”">aa</a></sup>as a burnt offering, and so the priest shall make atonement for him ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">ab</a></sup>Yahweh from his body fluid discharge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:10–11; Leviticus 22:4; Leviticus 15:5"> “ ‘And if an emission of semen goes out from anyone, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “and”">ac</a></sup>he shall wash all of his body with water, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And any garment and anything leather on which is an emission of semen shall be washed with water, and it shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5; 1 Corinthians 6:12"> If<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “And”">ad</a></sup>there is a woman with whom a man lies down and there is an emission of semen, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “and”">ae</a></sup>they shall wash themselves<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">af</a></sup>with water, and they shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 12:2; Matthew 15:19; Mark 5:25"> “ ‘And when a woman ⸤is menstruating⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “is discharging blood”">ag</a></sup>her body fluid discharge occurs in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “from”">ah</a></sup>her body; for seven days she shall be in her menstruation, and any person who touches her shall become unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 15:4–9; Proverbs 2:16–19; Proverbs 6:35"> And anything upon which she lies down during her menstruation shall become unclean, and anything upon which she sits shall become unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 7:14; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Isaiah 22:14"> And any person who touches her bed must wash his garments, and he shall wash himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">ai</a></sup>with water, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And any person who touches any object on which she sat must wash his garments, and he shall wash himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">aj</a></sup>with water, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And if it is on the bed or on the object on which she sits, at his touching it he becomes unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 20:18; 1 Peter 2:11; Leviticus 18:19"> And if a man indeed lies with her and her menstruation occurs on him, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “and”">ak</a></sup>he shall be unclean for seven days, and any bed on which he lies down becomes unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 5:25; Matthew 9:20; Luke 8:43"> “ ‘And when a woman discharges a body fluid consisting of her blood for many days, but not at the time of her menstruation, or when she discharges in addition to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “beyond”">al</a></sup>her menstruation, all the days of her unclean body fluid discharge she shall become unclean as in the days of her menstruation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Any bed on which she lies during all the days of her body fluid discharge shall become for her as her bed of menstruation, and any object on which she sits becomes unclean as her menstruation’s uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 15:21; Hebrews 10:22; Leviticus 17:15–16"> And any person who touches them becomes unclean, and he shall wash his garments, and he shall wash himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">am</a></sup>with water, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 15:13–15; Galatians 3:13; Ephesians 1:6–7"> “ ‘And if she is clean from her body fluid discharge, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “and”">an</a></sup>she shall count for herself seven days, and afterward she becomes clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And on the eighth day she shall take for herself two turtledoves or two ⸤young doves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “sons of dove”">ao</a></sup>and she shall bring them to the priest at the tent of assembly’s entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the priest shall ⸤sacrifice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “do” or “make”">ap</a></sup>the one as a sin offering and the ⸤other⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “one”">aq</a></sup>as a burnt offering, and so the priest shall make atonement for her ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “the the faces of”">ar</a></sup>Yahweh from her unclean body fluid discharge.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 19:20; Numbers 19:13; Ezekiel 5:11"> “And you shall keep the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">as</a></sup>separate from their uncleanness so that they might not die because of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “by” or “in”">at</a></sup>their uncleanness by their making my tabernacle, which is in their midst, unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 11:46; Leviticus 13:59; Numbers 6:13"> “This is the regulation of the one with the body fluid discharge and the one from whom an emission of semen goes out so that he becomes unclean by it</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 15:19; Leviticus 20:18; Leviticus 15:24–25"> and ⸤concerning⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “of”">au</a></sup>the menstruating woman in her bleeding<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="See similar idiom at 12:2">av</a></sup>and the person who discharges his body fluid, for the male and for the female and for a man who lies with an unclean woman.”</span>
</div>
