## The Place of Sacrifice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> “Speak to Aaron and to his sons and to all the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>and you shall say to them, ‘This is the word that Yahweh has commanded, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 17:8; Leviticus 17:15; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> “⸤Any man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “A man a man”">c</a></sup>from the house of Israel who slaughters an ox or a sheep or a goat in the camp or who slaughters it ⸤outside the camp⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from an outside place of the camp”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:7; Deuteronomy 12:5–21; Isaiah 66:3"> and he does not bring it to the tent of assembly’s entrance to present an offering to Yahweh ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">e</a></sup>Yahweh’s tabernacle, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “and”">f</a></sup>that man shall be accounted bloodguilty—he has poured out blood, and that man shall be cut off from the midst of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:9; 1 Kings 14:23; 2 Chronicles 28:4"> This is so that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>may bring their sacrifices that they are sacrificing ⸤in the open field⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “on the faces of the field”">h</a></sup>and bring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “they shall bring”">i</a></sup>them for Yahweh to the tent of assembly’s entrance to the priest, and they shall sacrifice fellowship offerings for Yahweh with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 18:17; Leviticus 3:2; Exodus 29:18"> And the priest shall sprinkle the blood on Yahweh’s altar at the tent of assembly’s entrance, and he shall ⸤burn⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “turn into smoke”">j</a></sup>the fat as an appeasing fragrance for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:20; Exodus 34:15; Deuteronomy 32:17"> And ⸤they may no longer sacrifice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “not they may sacrifice again”">k</a></sup>their sacrifices to the goat-idols after which they were prostituting. This is ⸤a lasting statute⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “a statute of eternity” or “a statute of long duration”">l</a></sup>for them ⸤throughout⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “to”">m</a></sup>their generations.” ’</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions Against Eating Blood

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2–3; Leviticus 17:4; Leviticus 17:10"> “And you shall say to them, ‘if there is ⸤anyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “A man a man”">n</a></sup>from the house of Israel or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “and”">o</a></sup>from the alien who dwells in their midst who offers a burnt offering or a sacrifice</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 17:4"> and he does not bring it to the tent of assembly’s entrance to ⸤sacrifice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “do” or “make”">p</a></sup>it for Yahweh, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “and”">q</a></sup>that man shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 17:11; Leviticus 3:17; Deuteronomy 12:23"> And if there is ⸤anyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “a man a man”">r</a></sup>from the house of Israel or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “and”">s</a></sup>from the alien who is dwelling in their midst who eats any blood, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “and”">t</a></sup>I will set my face against the person who eats the blood, and I will cut him off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 9:22; 1 John 1:7; Romans 3:25"> Indeed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “Because”">u</a></sup>the flesh’s life is in the blood, and I have given it to you on the altar to make atonement for your lives, because it is the blood with the life that makes atonement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:49"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “Unto thus”">v</a></sup>I said to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">w</a></sup>‘⸤None of you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “all of individual self from you not”">x</a></sup>may eat blood, nor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “and not”">y</a></sup>may the alien who is dwelling in your midst eat blood.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:16; Ezekiel 24:7; Leviticus 7:26"> “And if there is ⸤anyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “a man a man”">z</a></sup>from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">aa</a></sup>or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “and”">ab</a></sup>from the alien who is dwelling in their midst who hunts a wild game animal or a bird that may be eaten, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “and”">ac</a></sup>he shall pour out its blood, and he shall cover it with the soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 9:4; Deuteronomy 12:23; Leviticus 17:11–12"> Indeed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “Because”">ad</a></sup>the life of all flesh, its blood, is in its life, so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “and”">ae</a></sup>I said to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">af</a></sup>‘You may not eat the blood of any flesh, because the life of all flesh is its blood; anyone who eats it must be cut off.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:21; Exodus 22:31; Leviticus 22:8"> “And if there is any person who eats a dead body or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “and”">ag</a></sup>a mangled carcass, whether among the native or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “and”">ah</a></sup>among the alien, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “and”">ai</a></sup>he shall wash his garments, and he shall wash himself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">aj</a></sup>with water, and he shall be unclean until the evening, and he shall be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 19:19–20; Leviticus 7:18"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “And”">ak</a></sup>if he does not wash his garments<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="The direct object is supplied from context in the English translation">al</a></sup>and he does not wash his body, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “and”">am</a></sup>he shall bear his guilt.”</span>
</div>
</div>
