## Instructions About Vows

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 6:2; 1 Samuel 1:28; Judges 11:30–31"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>and say<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “and you shall say”">c</a></sup>to them, ‘When a man makes a vow according to your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Singular when modifying “proper value” throughout the entire chapter">d</a></sup>proper value of persons to Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:25; Numbers 18:16"> if<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “and”">e</a></sup>your proper value is for a male<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “the male”">f</a></sup>from ⸤twenty years of age⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">g</a></sup>up to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “and up to”">h</a></sup>⸤sixty years of age⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a son of sixty years”">i</a></sup>then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “and”">j</a></sup>your proper value shall be fifty shekels of money according to the sanctuary’s shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 27:9–10; Zechariah 11:12–13; Matthew 26:15"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “And”">k</a></sup>if it is for a female, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “and”">l</a></sup>your proper value shall be thirty shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And if from ⸤five years of age⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a son of five years”">m</a></sup>up to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “and up to”">n</a></sup>⸤twenty years of age⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">o</a></sup>then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “and”">p</a></sup>your proper value shall be twenty shekels for the male and ten shekels for the female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:40–43; Numbers 18:14–16"> And if from ⸤a month of age⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a son of a month”">q</a></sup>up to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “and up to”">r</a></sup>⸤five years of age⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “a son of five years”">s</a></sup>then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “and”">t</a></sup>your proper value shall be five shekels of money for the male, and your proper value for the female shall be three shekels of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 90:10"> And if from ⸤sixty years of age⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “a son of sixty years”">u</a></sup>and above: if a male, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “and”">v</a></sup>your proper value shall be fifteen shekels; and for the female, ten shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:7; Leviticus 14:21–22; Luke 21:1–4"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “And”">w</a></sup>if he is poorer than your proper value, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “and”">x</a></sup>he shall present himself ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">y</a></sup>the priest, and the priest shall set a value on him; the priest shall value him ⸤according to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “on a mouth of”">z</a></sup>what the person who made a vow ⸤can afford⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “his hand produces”">aa</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> “ ‘And if it is a domestic animal from which they present an offering for Yahweh, all that he gives from it for Yahweh shall be a holy object.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 27:15–33; James 1:8"> He shall not replace it, nor shall he exchange it, either good with bad or bad with good; and if he indeed exchanges a domestic animal with a domestic animal, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “and”">ab</a></sup>⸤it and its substitution shall be a holy object⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “it shall be and its substitution shall be holy”">ac</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Malachi 1:14"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “And”">ad</a></sup>if it is any unclean animal from which they may not present an offering for Yahweh, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “and”">ae</a></sup>he shall present the animal ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">af</a></sup>the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the priest shall set a value on it, ⸤either good or bad⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “between good and between bad”">ag</a></sup>as the priest sets your proper value, so it shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:19; Leviticus 27:15; Leviticus 22:14"> And if he indeed wants to redeem it, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “and”">ah</a></sup>he shall add a fifth of it onto your proper value.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:29–31; Leviticus 27:21"> “ ‘And if a man consecrates his house as a holy object for Yahweh, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “and”">ai</a></sup>the priest shall set a value on it, ⸤either good or bad⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “between good and between bad”">aj</a></sup>just as the priest sets a value on it, so it shall remain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “And”">ak</a></sup>if the one who consecrates it wants to redeem his house, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “and”">al</a></sup>he shall add a fifth of your proper value’s money onto it, and it shall be his.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11–14; Hosea 3:2; Acts 4:34–37"> “ ‘And if a man consecrates ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “from”">am</a></sup>his property’s fields<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Collective singular; Hebrew “field”">an</a></sup>for Yahweh, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “and”">ao</a></sup>your proper value shall be ⸤in accordance with its seed requirements⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “to the number of its seed”">ap</a></sup>a homer of barley seed for fifty shekels of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If he consecrates his field from the Year of Jubilee, it shall stand as your proper value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 25:15–16; Leviticus 25:51–52; Leviticus 25:27"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “And”">aq</a></sup>if he consecrates his field after the Jubilee, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “and”">ar</a></sup>the priest shall calculate the money for him ⸤according to the number of years⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “on the mouth of the years”">as</a></sup>that are left over until the Year of Jubilee; and it shall be deducted from your proper value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 27:13"> And if he indeed redeems the field that is consecrated, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “and”">at</a></sup>he shall add a fifth of your proper value’s money onto it, and it shall stand for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Or “remain for” or “belong to”">au</a></sup>him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And if he does not redeem the field and if he sells the field to another man, it may not be redeemed again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:29; Numbers 18:14; Leviticus 25:31"> and the field shall be a holy object for Yahweh when it goes out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Or “is released” or “reverts”">av</a></sup>in the Jubilee, like a devoted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Or “permanently set apart”; a different Hebrew word than previously translated “consecrated” in this chapter">aw</a></sup>field; ⸤it shall be the priest’s property⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Literally “to/for the priest it shall be his property”">ax</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Leviticus 25:25"> “ ‘And if he consecrates for Yahweh his acquired<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Or “purchased”">ay</a></sup>field that is not the field of his inherited possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 27:18; Leviticus 27:12"> then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Or “and”">az</a></sup>the priest shall calculate for him the ⸤amount⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Literally “number”">ba</a></sup>of your proper value until the year of the Jubilee, and he shall give your proper value on that day as a holy object for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 25:28; Leviticus 27:20"> In the Year of the Jubilee the field shall return to the one who bought it from him, to the one whose property the land is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 18:16; Numbers 3:47"> And every proper value of yours shall be in the sanctuary’s shekel—the shekel shall be twenty gerahs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13; Numbers 18:17"> “ ‘However, a man shall not consecrate a firstborn among livestock, which belongs as firstborn to Yahweh; whether an ox ⸤or⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Literally “whether” or “if”">bb</a></sup>small livestock, it is for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 27:11"> And if it is among the unclean animals, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Or “and”">bc</a></sup>he shall ransom it according to your proper value, and he shall add a fifth of its value onto it; and if it is not redeemed, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="Or “and”">bd</a></sup>it shall be sold according to your proper value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:17–19; Leviticus 27:21; Exodus 22:20"> However, anything devoted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-be" id="fnref-be" data-fn="Or “permanently set apart”; a different Hebrew word than previously translated “consecrated” in this chapter">be</a></sup>that a man has devoted to Yahweh ⸤from all that he has⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="Literally “from all that is for him”">bf</a></sup>from human or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="Or “and”">bg</a></sup>animal, or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Or “and”">bh</a></sup>from the field of his property, may not be sold, and it may not be redeemed; anything devoted is ⸤a most holy thing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bi" id="fnref-bi" data-fn="Literally “a holy thing of holy things”">bi</a></sup>for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:18–23; Numbers 21:2–3"> Anyone devoted who is devoted from ⸤human beings⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bj" id="fnref-bj" data-fn="Literally “the human”">bj</a></sup>cannot be ransomed—he shall surely be put to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 23:23; Nehemiah 13:12; Genesis 28:22"> “ ‘And any tithe of the land from the land’s seed or from the fruit of the trees is for Yahweh; it is a holy object for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And if a man indeed redeems from his tithe, he shall add a fifth of its value onto it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Ezekiel 20:37; Micah 7:14"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bk" id="fnref-bk" data-fn="Or “And”">bk</a></sup>every tithe of cattle or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bl" id="fnref-bl" data-fn="Or “and”">bl</a></sup>of the flock,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bm" id="fnref-bm" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">bm</a></sup>all which crosses under the rod, the tenth shall be a holy object for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 27:10"> He shall not inspect between the good and the bad, and he shall not exchange it; but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bn" id="fnref-bn" data-fn="Or “and”">bn</a></sup>if he indeed exchanges it, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bo" id="fnref-bo" data-fn="Or “and”">bo</a></sup>⸤it and its substitution shall be⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bp" id="fnref-bp" data-fn="Literally “it shall be and its substitution shall be”">bp</a></sup>a holy object—it shall not be redeemed.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 1:1; Hebrews 12:18–25"> These are the commands that Yahweh commanded Moses for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bq" id="fnref-bq" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">bq</a></sup>on ⸤Mount Sinai⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-br" id="fnref-br" data-fn="Literally “the mountain of Sinai”">br</a></sup></span>
</div>
