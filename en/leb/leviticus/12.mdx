## Purification After Childbirth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 2:22; Leviticus 15:19; Job 25:4"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>saying, ‘When a woman becomes pregnant and she gives birth to a male,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “son”">c</a></sup>then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “and”">d</a></sup>she shall be unclean seven days—as in the time of her menstrual bleeding, she shall become unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:21; Luke 1:59; John 7:22–23"> And on the eighth day his foreskin’s flesh shall be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 2:22–23; Leviticus 15:25–28; Haggai 2:13"> And for thirty-three days she shall stay in the blood of her cleansing; she must not touch any holy object, and she may not come to the sanctuary until the fulfilling of the days of her cleansing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:14–15; Genesis 3:13; Leviticus 12:2"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “And”">e</a></sup>if she gives birth to a female,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “daughter”">f</a></sup>then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “and”">g</a></sup>she shall be unclean for two weeks as in her menstruation, and for sixty-six days she shall stay ⸤through⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “at” or “on”">h</a></sup>the blood of her cleansing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:22; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> And at the fulfilling of the days of her cleansing, whether for a son or for a daughter, she must bring to the priest at the tent of assembly’s entrance a ⸤yearling⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a son of his year”">i</a></sup>male lamb as a burnt offering and ⸤young dove⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “son of a dove”">j</a></sup>or a turtledove as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 15:28–30; Hebrews 9:12–14; Leviticus 4:35"> And the priest shall present it ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">k</a></sup>Yahweh, and he shall make atonement for her, so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “and”">l</a></sup>she shall be clean from the flow of her blood. This is the regulation of childbearing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the childbearing”">m</a></sup>for the male<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “son”">n</a></sup>or for the female.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “daughter”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 5:7; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 1:14"> And if ⸤she cannot afford⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “her hand does not find enough”">p</a></sup>a sheep,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “small livestock”">q</a></sup>then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “and”">r</a></sup>she shall take two turtledoves or two ⸤young doves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “sons of a dove”">s</a></sup>—one as a burnt offering and one as a sin offering—and the priest shall make atonement for her, so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “and”">t</a></sup>she shall be clean.’ ”</span>
</div>
