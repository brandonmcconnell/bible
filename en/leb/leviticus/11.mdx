## Clean and Unclean Animals

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>saying, ‘These are the animals that you may eat from all the animals that are on the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Any among the animals that has a divided hoof and has a split cleft in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “of”">c</a></sup>the hoof, such<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “her/it”">d</a></sup>you may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> However,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “Only”">e</a></sup>these<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="By context; Hebrew “this”">f</a></sup>you may not eat from those that chew the cud and from those that have a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">g</a></sup>divided hoof: the camel, because it is a chewer of cud but it does not have a hoof that is divided—it is unclean for you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> and the coney, because it is a chewer of cud but it does not have a hoof that is divided—it is unclean for you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> and the hare, because it is a chewer of cud but it does not have a hoof that is divided—it is unclean for you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> and the pig, because it has a divided hoof and has a split cleft in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “of”">h</a></sup>the hoof but it does not chew cud—it is unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> You must not eat from their meat, and you must not touch their dead body—they are unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> “ ‘These<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="By context; Hebrew “This”">i</a></sup>you may eat from all that are in the water: any in the water that has a fin and scales, whether in the seas or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “and”">j</a></sup>in the streams—such<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “them”">k</a></sup>you may eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalm 139:21–22"> But any that does not have a fin and scales, whether in the seas or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “and”">l</a></sup>in the streams, ⸤among⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “from”">m</a></sup>all the water’s swarmers among all the living creatures that are in the water—they are a detestable thing to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they shall be detestable to you; you must not eat from their meat, and you must detest their dead body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Any that does not have a fin and scales in the water—it is a detestable thing to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremiah 4:22; Titus 3:3"> “ ‘And these you must detest from the birds; they must not be eaten—they are detestable: the eagle and the vulture and the short-toed eagle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and the red kite and the black kite according to its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; 1 Kings 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> every crow according to its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> and ⸤the ostrich⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the daughter of the ostrich”">n</a></sup>and the short-eared owl and the seagull and the hawk according to its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the little owl and the cormorant and the great owl,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the barn owl and the desert owl and the carrion vulture,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and the stork, the heron according to its kind and the hoopoe and the bat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> “ ‘Any ⸤winged insect⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “swarmer of the wing”">o</a></sup>that walks on all fours is detestable to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Only this may you eat from any of ⸤the winged insects⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the swarmer of the wing”">p</a></sup>that walk on all fours—that which has jointed legs above its feet for leaping upon the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> ⸤From these⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “These from them”">q</a></sup>you may eat the locust according to its kind and the bald locust according to its kind and the cricket according to its kind and the grasshopper according to its kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “And”">r</a></sup>any other<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Implied by context">s</a></sup>⸤winged insect⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “swarmer of wing”">t</a></sup>that has four legs is detestable to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> And by these you shall become unclean—anyone who touches their dead body shall become unclean until the evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> and anyone who carries their dead body must wash his garments, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “ ‘With regard to any animal that has a divided hoof but does not split the hoof, or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “and”">u</a></sup>does not have a cud for chewing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="So HALOT 830 s.v. 4">v</a></sup>—they are unclean for you; anyone who touches them shall become unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> And anything that walks upon its paws among any of the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Collective singular = plural by context">w</a></sup>that walks on all fours—they are unclean for you; anyone who touches their dead body shall become unclean until the evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> and the one who carries their dead body must wash his garments, and he shall be unclean until the evening—they are unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalm 10:3"> “ ‘And these<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="By context; Hebrew “this”">x</a></sup>are the unclean for you among the swarmers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Collective singular = plural">y</a></sup>that swarm on the land: the weasel and the mouse and the thorn-tailed lizard according to its kind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and the gecko and the land crocodile and the lizard and the sand lizard and the chameleon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> These are the unclean for you among all the swarmers; anyone who touches them at their death shall become unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> And anything on which ⸤one of them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “from them”">z</a></sup>falls at their death shall become unclean: any object of wood or garment or skin or sackcloth—any object that has performed work—must be placed in water, and it shall be unclean until the evening, and then it shall be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> And any clay vessel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Context indicates a vessel as distinguished from a tool or utensil">aa</a></sup>into which it falls shall become unclean, and you must break it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> Any of the food that could be eaten on which water from such a vessel comes shall become unclean, and any liquid that could be drunk in any such vessel shall become unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> And anything on which ⸤one of their dead bodies⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “from their dead body”">ab</a></sup>falls shall become unclean: an oven or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “and”">ac</a></sup>a stove must be broken<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “smashed” (NASB, HCSB, NET, NJPS) or “broken in pieces” (ASV, ESV, NRSV)">ad</a></sup>—they are unclean and shall be unclean for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; John 4:14"> Surely<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="So HALOT 45">ae</a></sup>a spring or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “and”">af</a></sup>a cistern collecting water shall be clean, but that which touches their dead body shall become unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> And when ⸤one of their dead bodies⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “from their dead body”">ag</a></sup>falls on any ⸤seed for sowing⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “seed plant that is to be sown”">ah</a></sup>it is clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “And”">ai</a></sup>when water is put on the seed and ⸤one of their dead bodies⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “from their dead body”">aj</a></sup>falls on it, it is unclean for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> “ ‘And when ⸤one of the animals⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “from the animal”">ak</a></sup>dies that is for you to eat, the one who touches its dead body shall become unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Ezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> And the one who eats ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “from”">al</a></sup>its dead body must wash his garments, and he shall be unclean until the evening; and the one who carries its dead body must wash his garments, and he shall be unclean until the evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> “ ‘And any swarmer that swarms on the land is detestable; it must not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Isaiah 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> You must not eat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Hebrew “eat them”">am</a></sup>anything that moves upon its belly or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “and”">an</a></sup>that walks on all fours, even any with numerous feet belonging to any swarmer that swarms on the land, because they are detestable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> You must not defile yourselves with any swarmer that swarms, and you must not make yourselves unclean by them and so be made unclean by them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> because I am Yahweh your God, and you must keep yourselves sanctified, so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “and”">ao</a></sup>you shall be holy, because I am holy. And you must not make yourselves unclean with any swarmer that moves along on the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> because I am Yahweh, who brought you up from the land of Egypt to be for you as God. Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “And”">ap</a></sup>you shall be holy, because I am holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Ezekiel 43:12"> “ ‘This is the regulation of the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Collective singulars in this verse are plural by context">aq</a></sup>and the birds and all living creatures that move along in the water and ⸤concerning⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “of”">ar</a></sup>all the creatures that swarm on the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> to distinguish between the unclean and the clean and between the animal that is to be eaten and the animal that must not be eaten.’ ”</span>
</div>
