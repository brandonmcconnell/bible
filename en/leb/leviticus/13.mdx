## Regulations About Defiling Skin Diseases

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:8; Leviticus 14:56; Leviticus 14:3"> “When a ⸤person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “man”">b</a></sup>has on his body’s skin a swelling or an epidermal eruption or a spot and it becomes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Perfect of הָיָה followed by לְ; see HALOT 244 s.v. 7 .c">c</a></sup>an infectious skin disease on his body’s skin, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “and”">d</a></sup>he shall be brought to Aaron the priest or to one of his sons the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 3:19–20; Acts 20:28; Malachi 2:7"> And the priest shall examine the infection on his body’s skin, and if the hair in the infection turns white and the appearance of the infection is deeper than his body’s skin, it is an infectious skin disease, and the priest shall examine it, and he shall declare him unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 12:15; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Timothy 5:24"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “And”">e</a></sup>if a spot is white on his body’s skin and its appearance is not deeper than the skin and its hair does not turn white, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “and”">f</a></sup>the priest shall confine the afflicted person for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the priest shall examine it on the seventh day, and ⸤if⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “look” or “behold”">g</a></sup>in his eyes, the infection has stayed unchanged, the infection has not spread on the skin, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “and”">h</a></sup>the priest shall confine him for seven days a second time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 14:8; Deuteronomy 32:5"> And the priest shall examine him on the seventh day for a second time, and ⸤if⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “look” or “behold”">i</a></sup>the infection has faded and the infection has not spread on the skin, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “and”">j</a></sup>the priest shall declare him clean—it is an epidermal eruption; and he shall wash his garments, and so he shall be clean.</span>

## Regulations About Contaminated Fabrics

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> and if the infection is yellowish green or reddish on the garment or on the leather or on the woven material or on the fabric or on any leather object,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-cr" id="fnref-cr" data-fn="Literally “an object of leather”">cr</a></sup>it is an infectious skin disease<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-cs" id="fnref-cs" data-fn="Perhaps better translated “mold” rather than “skin disease”">cs</a></sup>and it shall be shown to the priest.</span>
