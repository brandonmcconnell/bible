## Regulations Concerning Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:25; Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 10:6–7"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh said to Moses, “Speak to the priests, Aaron’s sons, and say to them, ‘One must not make himself unclean for a dead person among his own people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13; Leviticus 18:6"> except for his direct relative closest to him: his mother and his father, and his son and his daughter, and his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and for his sister, a virgin, who is closest to him, ⸤who has not had a husband⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “who is not for a man/husband”">b</a></sup>—for her he may defile himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–17"> He must not make himself unclean as ⸤a kinsman by marriage⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a husband among his people”">c</a></sup>defiling himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 19:27–28"> “ ‘And they shall not shave bald patches on their head, and they shall not shave off the corner of their beard, and they shall not make a cut in their body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 18:21; 1 Peter 2:9"> They shall be holy to their God, and they shall not profane the name of their God, because they are bringing near the offerings made by fire to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “of”">d</a></sup>Yahweh—their God’s food—and they shall be holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:13–14; Ezekiel 44:22; Leviticus 21:8"> “ ‘They shall not ⸤marry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “take”">e</a></sup>a woman who is a prostitute and defiled, nor shall they ⸤marry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “take”">f</a></sup>a woman divorced from her husband, because each priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “he”; singular antecedent specified from the context">g</a></sup>is holy for his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:7–8; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> And you shall consecrate him, because he is bringing near your God’s food; he shall be holy to you, since I, Yahweh, who consecrates you, am holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Leviticus 19:29; Ezekiel 9:6"> “ ‘As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “And”">h</a></sup>the daughter of any priest, if she is defiled by prostituting, she is disgracing her father—she shall be burned in the fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:6–7; Leviticus 16:32; Matthew 26:65"> “ ‘As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “And”">i</a></sup>the priest who is higher than his brothers, on whose head the oil of anointment is poured and ⸤he was ordained⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “he has filled up his hand”">j</a></sup>to wear the garments, he shall not dishevel his head, and he shall not tear his garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:14; Leviticus 21:1–2; Numbers 6:7"> And he shall not go near any dead person, nor shall he make himself unclean for his father or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “and”">k</a></sup>for his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:7; Exodus 29:6–7; Leviticus 8:9–12"> And he shall not go out from the sanctuary, and he shall not profane his God’s sanctuary, because the dedication of his God’s oil of anointment is on him; I am Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 14:4"> “ ‘And he himself must take a wife in her virginity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Ezekiel 44:22"> A widow or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “and”">l</a></sup>a divorced woman or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “and”">m</a></sup>a defiled woman, a prostitute—these he must not take; he shall take only<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “instead”">n</a></sup>a virgin from his people as wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 11:16; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> And he shall not profane his offspring among his people, because I am Yahweh, who consecrates him.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “And”">o</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 65:4; Numbers 16:5; Leviticus 10:3"> “Speak to Aaron, saying, ‘A man from your offspring throughout their generations, in whom is a physical defect, shall not come near to present your God’s food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–3; Leviticus 22:19–25; Titus 1:7"> Indeed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “For”">p</a></sup>any man in whom is a physical defect shall not come near: a blind man or lame or disfigured or deformed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> or a man in whom is a broken foot or a broken hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:1; Isaiah 56:3"> or a hunchback or a dwarf, or a spot in his eye or a skin disorder or a skin eruption or a crushed testicle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 21:8; Leviticus 21:17"> Any man from Aaron the priest’s offspring in whom is a physical defect shall not come near to present offerings made by fire to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “of”">q</a></sup>Yahweh; a physical defect is in him; he shall not come near to present his God’s food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 24:8–9"> He may eat his God’s food, from ⸤the most holy things⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the holy things of the holy things”">r</a></sup>and from the holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Ezekiel 44:9–14; Exodus 30:6–8"> But he must not enter the curtain, and he must not come near to the altar, because a physical defect is in him, and he must not profane my sanctuary, because I am Yahweh, who consecrates them.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:18; Malachi 2:1–7; 2 Timothy 2:2"> Thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “And”">s</a></sup>Moses spoke to Aaron and to his sons and to all the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
