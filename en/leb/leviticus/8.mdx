## The Offerings for Consecration

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Hebrews 9:18–23; Ezekiel 43:19–27"> and he slaughtered it, and Moses took the blood and put<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “he put”">ae</a></sup>it with his finger on the altar’s horns all around and purified the altar; then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “and”">af</a></sup>he poured the blood out on the altar’s base—thus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “and”">ag</a></sup>he consecrated it in order to make atonement for it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “to make atonement upon it” (see NET, Tanakh)">ah</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 8:2; Leviticus 1:4–13; Exodus 29:15–18"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “And”">am</a></sup>he brought the ram of the burnt offering near, and Aaron and his sons placed their hands on the ram’s head,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and he slaughtered it. Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “And”">an</a></sup>Moses sprinkled the blood on the altar all around.</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 29:25; Psalm 22:13–14; Hebrews 10:14–22"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="Or “And”">bf</a></sup>Moses took them from upon their palms, and he turned them into smoke upon the burnt offering on the altar; they were a consecration offering as an appeasing fragrance—it was an offering made by fire for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="Or “to”">bg</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:31; 1 Peter 4:11; Leviticus 7:30–34"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Or “And”">bh</a></sup>Moses took the breast section, and he waved it as a wave offering ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bi" id="fnref-bi" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">bi</a></sup>Yahweh from the ram of the consecration offering; it was Moses’ share, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
