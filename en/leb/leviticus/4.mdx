## Laws for Sin Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 19:12; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 4:27"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>saying, ‘If a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “a soul”">c</a></sup>sins by an unintentional wrong from any of Yahweh’s commands that should not be ⸤violated⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “done”">d</a></sup>and he ⸤violates⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “does”">e</a></sup>⸤any of them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from one from them”; see NET">f</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 7:27–28; Leviticus 4:14; Leviticus 9:2"> if the anointed priest sins, ⸤bringing guilt on the people⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to the guilt of the people”">g</a></sup>then,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “and”">h</a></sup>concerning the sin that he has ⸤committed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sinned”">i</a></sup>he shall bring ⸤a young bull⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a bull, a son of cattle”">j</a></sup>without defect for Yahweh as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 1:3–4; Daniel 9:26; Isaiah 53:6"> He shall bring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “And he shall bring”">k</a></sup>the bull to the tent of assembly’s entrance before Yahweh, place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “And place”">l</a></sup>his hand on the bull’s head, and slaughter the bull before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 16:14; Numbers 19:4; Leviticus 4:16–17"> The anointed priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “And the anointed priest”">m</a></sup>shall take ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “from”">n</a></sup>the bull’s blood and shall bring it to the tent of assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 16:14; Leviticus 14:18; Leviticus 26:28"> and the priest shall dip his finger in the blood and shall spatter ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “from”">o</a></sup>the blood seven times before Yahweh in front of the sanctuary’s curtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 9:9; Leviticus 16:18"> The priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “And the priest”">p</a></sup>shall put ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “from”">q</a></sup>the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense before Yahweh, which is in the tent of assembly, and all the rest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Implied by the context">r</a></sup>of the bull’s blood he must pour out on the base of the altar of the burnt offering, which is at the entrance of the tent of assembly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:3–5; Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:31"> “ ‘He must remove<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “And he must remove”">s</a></sup>all the fat from the bull of the sin offering: the fat that covers the inner parts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “entrails”">t</a></sup>and all of the fat that is on the inner parts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “entrails”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 3:4"> the two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “and the two”">v</a></sup>kidneys and the fat that is on them, and the liver’s lobe that he must remove in addition to the kidneys—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> just as it is removed from the ox of the fellowship offerings’ sacrifice—and the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar of the burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:5; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 9:8–11"> But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “And”">w</a></sup>the bull’s skin and its meat, in addition to its head, ⸤its lower leg bones⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “and in addition to its lower leg bones”">x</a></sup>its<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “and its”">y</a></sup>inner parts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “entrails”">z</a></sup>and its offal—</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:11; Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 16:27"> he shall carry<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “and he shall carry out”">aa</a></sup>all of the bull ⸤outside the camp⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “to from an outside place of the camp”">ab</a></sup>to a ceremonially clean place, to the fatty ashes’ dump, and he shall burn it on wood in the fire; it must be burned up on the fatty ashes’ dump.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 5:17; Leviticus 5:2–5; Hosea 5:15"> “ ‘If<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “And if”">ac</a></sup>Israel’s whole assembly did wrong unintentionally and the matter<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “thing”">ad</a></sup>was concealed from the assembly’s eyes, and they acted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “did”">ae</a></sup>against ⸤any of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “one from all of”">af</a></sup>Yahweh’s commands that should not be ⸤violated⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “done”">ag</a></sup>so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “and”">ah</a></sup>they incur guilt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:3; Leviticus 4:28"> when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “and”">ai</a></sup>the sin that they have ⸤committed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “sinned”">aj</a></sup>against that command<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="The 3fs pronominal suffix may refer to “one of Yahweh’s commands”—“one” is also fs">ak</a></sup>becomes known, the assembly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “and the assembly”">al</a></sup>shall present ⸤a young bull⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “a bull, a son of cattle”">am</a></sup>as a sin offering, and they shall bring it before the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 16:21; Leviticus 8:14"> And the elders of the community shall place their hands on the bull’s head before Yahweh, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “someone”; see NET—the 3ms refers to either one of the elders or the priest">an</a></sup>shall slaughter the bull before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 4:5–12; Hebrews 9:12–14"> Then the anointed priest shall bring ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “from”">ao</a></sup>the bull’s blood to the tent of assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 4:6–7"> and the priest shall dip his finger in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “from”">ap</a></sup>the blood and shall spatter it seven times before Yahweh in front of the curtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7"> He must put<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “And he must put”">aq</a></sup>⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “from”">ar</a></sup>the blood on the altar’s horns before Yahweh ⸤in the tent of assembly⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “which is in the tent of assembly”—the reference is to the altar">as</a></sup>and all the rest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Indicated by the context">at</a></sup>of the blood he must pour out on the base of the altar of the burnt offering, which is at the tent of assembly’s entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 5:6; Leviticus 4:26; Numbers 15:25"> He must remove<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Or “And he must remove”">au</a></sup>all its fat from it, and he shall turn it into smoke on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 15:25; Hebrews 10:10–12; Leviticus 12:8"> He shall do<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Or “And he shall do”">av</a></sup>to the bull ⸤just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “as that”">aw</a></sup>he did to the sin offering’s bull, so he must do to it. The priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Or “And the priest”">ax</a></sup>shall make atonement for them, and ⸤they will be forgiven⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Literally “it shall be forgiven to them”">ay</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 3:13; Leviticus 7:2; Leviticus 4:3–35"> He shall place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bk" id="fnref-bk" data-fn="Or “And he shall place”">bk</a></sup>his hand on the he-goat’s head and slaughter it in the place where he slaughtered the burnt offering before Yahweh; it is a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Leviticus 4:18; Leviticus 4:30"> The priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bl" id="fnref-bl" data-fn="Or “And the priest”">bl</a></sup>shall take ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bm" id="fnref-bm" data-fn="Literally “from”">bm</a></sup>the sin offering’s blood with his finger, and he shall put it on the horns of the altar of the burnt offering, and he must pour out the rest of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bn" id="fnref-bn" data-fn="Indicated by context">bn</a></sup>its blood on the base of the altar of the burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Leviticus 4:31; Leviticus 4:35"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bo" id="fnref-bo" data-fn="Or “And he”; the antecedent is the priest (cp. vv. 10 , 31)">bo</a></sup>must turn all of its fat into smoke on the altar like the fat of the fellowship offerings’ sacrifice, and the priest shall make atonement for him ⸤because of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bp" id="fnref-bp" data-fn="Literally “from”">bp</a></sup>his sin, and ⸤he will be forgiven⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bq" id="fnref-bq" data-fn="Literally “it shall be forgiven to him”">bq</a></sup></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Leviticus 4:25; Leviticus 4:34"> The priest<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-cb" id="fnref-cb" data-fn="Or “And the priest”">cb</a></sup>shall take ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-cc" id="fnref-cc" data-fn="Literally “from”">cc</a></sup>its blood with his finger, and he shall put it on the horns of the altar of the burnt offering, and he must pour out all the rest of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-cd" id="fnref-cd" data-fn="Indicated by context">cd</a></sup>its blood on the altar’s base.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 4:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 Peter 2:22"> “ ‘But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-cj" id="fnref-cj" data-fn="Or “And”">cj</a></sup>if he brings a lamb as his offering for a sin offering, he must bring a female without defect.</span>
