## Molech Worship and Spiritism

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:21; Leviticus 18:21; Leviticus 20:27"> “And to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>you shall say, ‘If there is ⸤anyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a man a man”">c</a></sup>from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “and”">e</a></sup>from the alien who is dwelling in Israel, who gives ⸤any of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from”">f</a></sup>his offspring to Molech, he must surely be put to death; the people of the land must stone him with stones.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “the stone”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 18:21; Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 23:38–39"> And I myself will set my face against that man, and I will cut him off from the midst of his people, because he has given ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “from”">h</a></sup>his offspring to Molech, so that ⸤he makes my sanctuary unclean⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to make unclean my sanctuary”">i</a></sup>and profanes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “to profane”">j</a></sup>⸤my holy name⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the name of my holiness”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–5; Acts 17:30; 1 Kings 20:42"> And if the people of the land ever shut their eyes from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “disregard”">l</a></sup>that man at his giving ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “from”">m</a></sup>his offspring to Molech, not putting him to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 106:39; Hosea 2:13; Hosea 2:5"> then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “and”">n</a></sup>I myself will set my face against that man and against his clan, and I will cut him off and all those from the midst of their people who prostitute after Molech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “And”">o</a></sup>the person who turns to the mediums and the soothsayers to prostitute after them, I will set<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “and I will set”">p</a></sup>my face against that person, and I will cut him off from the midst of his people.</span>

## Family and Sexual Offenses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 21:17; Mark 7:10; Deuteronomy 27:16"> “ ‘If there is ⸤anyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a man a man”">q</a></sup>who curses his father or his mother, he shall surely be put to death; he has cursed his father and his mother—his blood is upon him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:4–5; Deuteronomy 22:22–24; Deuteronomy 5:18"> “ ‘As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “And”">r</a></sup>a man who commits adultery with a man’s wife, who commits adultery with his neighbor’s wife, both the man who commits adultery and the woman who commits adultery shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; Deuteronomy 27:23; Amos 2:7"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “And”">s</a></sup>a man who lies with his father’s wife, he has exposed his father’s nakedness; both of them shall be put to death—their blood is on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; Leviticus 18:23; Genesis 38:18"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “And”">t</a></sup>a man who lies with his daughter-in-law, both of them shall be put to death; they have committed a perversion—their blood is on them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9; Leviticus 18:22; Jude 1:7"> “ ‘As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “And”">u</a></sup>the man who lies with a male as lying with a woman, they have committed a detestable thing; they shall surely be put to death—their blood is on them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 18:17; Deuteronomy 27:23; Joshua 7:15"> “ ‘As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “And”">v</a></sup>a man who marries a woman and her mother, that is depravity; they shall burn him and them, so that it shall not become depravity in the midst of you all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Deuteronomy 27:21; Exodus 22:19"> “ ‘As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “And”">w</a></sup>a man who ⸤has sexual relations⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “he gives his lying down”">x</a></sup>with an animal, he shall surely be put to death, and you must kill the animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 21:32; Exodus 19:13; Hebrews 12:20"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “And”">y</a></sup>a woman who approaches any animal to copulate with it, you shall kill<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “and you shall kill”">z</a></sup>the woman and the animal; they shall surely be put to death—their blood is on them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 18:9; Deuteronomy 27:22; Ezekiel 22:11"> “ ‘As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “And”">aa</a></sup>a man who takes his sister, his father’s daughter or his mother’s daughter, and he sees her nakedness and she herself sees his nakedness, it is a disgrace, and they shall be cut off ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “in”">ab</a></sup>the eyes of ⸤their people⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “the sons/children of their people”">ac</a></sup>he has exposed his sister’s nakedness—he must bear his guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 15:24; Leviticus 18:19; Ezekiel 18:6"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “And”">ad</a></sup>a man who lies with a menstruating woman, he exposes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “and he exposes”">ae</a></sup>her nakedness—her source he exposes and she herself reveals her blood’s source—both of them shall be cut off from the midst of their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 18:6; Leviticus 18:12–30; Exodus 6:20"> And you shall not expose the nakedness of your mother’s sister, and you shall not expose your father’s sister, because such a person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">af</a></sup>has dishonored his close relative—they must bear their guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 18:14; Job 18:19; Luke 23:29"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “And”">ag</a></sup>a man who lies with his aunt, he has exposed his uncle’s nakedness—they shall bear their sin; they shall die childless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:16; Matthew 14:3–4"> As for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “And”">ah</a></sup>a man who marries his brother’s wife, it is an abomination; he has exposed his brother’s nakedness—they shall be childless.</span>
</div>

## You Shall Be Holy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 18:25–28; Ezekiel 36:27; Leviticus 18:4–5"> “ ‘And you shall keep all my statutes and all my regulations, and you shall do them, so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “and”">ai</a></sup>the land, to which I am bringing you to inhabit it, shall not vomit you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Leviticus 18:24; Leviticus 18:30"> And you shall not follow the statutes of the nation that I am driving out ⸤from before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “from your faces”">aj</a></sup>because they did all these things, and I detested them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Exodus 6:8; Deuteronomy 7:6"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “And”">ak</a></sup>I said to you, “You yourselves shall take possession of their land, and I myself shall give it to you to possess it—a land flowing with milk and honey”; I am Yahweh your God, who ⸤has set you apart⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “I have set you apart”">al</a></sup>from the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:3–21; Leviticus 11:1–47; Acts 10:11–15"> And you shall distinguish between the clean and the unclean animal and between the unclean and the clean bird; and you shall not defile yourselves with the animal or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “and”">am</a></sup>with the bird or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “and”">an</a></sup>with anything that moves along the ground that I have set apart for you ⸤as unclean⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “to make unclean”">ao</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 20:7; Leviticus 20:24"> And you shall be holy for me, because I, Yahweh, am holy, and I have singled you out from the nations to be mine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 20:6; 1 Samuel 28:7–9"> “ ‘And a man or a woman, if a spirit of the dead or a spirit of divination is in them, they shall surely be put to death; they shall stone them with stones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Hebrew “the stone”">ap</a></sup>—their blood is on them.’ ”</span>
