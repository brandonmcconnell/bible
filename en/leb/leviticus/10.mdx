## The Deaths of Nadab and Abihu

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:61; Deuteronomy 12:32; Leviticus 16:12"> And Aaron’s sons Nadab and Abihu each took his censer, and they put fire in them and placed incense on it;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, the fire">a</a></sup>then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “and”">b</a></sup>they presented ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">c</a></sup>Yahweh illegitimate fire, which he had not commanded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 26:61; Numbers 16:35; Leviticus 9:24"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “And”">d</a></sup>fire went out ⸤from before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from to the faces of”">e</a></sup>Yahweh, and it consumed them so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “and”">f</a></sup>they died ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">g</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 19:22; Leviticus 21:6; Isaiah 49:3"> Therefore<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “And”">h</a></sup>Moses said to Aaron, “This is what Yahweh spoke, saying, ‘Among those who are close to me I will show myself holy, and ⸤in the presence of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “upon the faces of”">i</a></sup>all the people I will display my glory.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “I will be glorified”">j</a></sup>So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “And”">k</a></sup>Aaron was silent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:22; Exodus 6:18; Acts 5:6"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “And”">l</a></sup>Moses summoned Mishael and Elzaphan the sons of Uzziel, Aaron’s uncle, and he said to them, “Come forward.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “Come near” or “Approach”">m</a></sup>Carry your brothers from ⸤the front of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the faces of”">n</a></sup>the sanctuary to ⸤outside the camp⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “from an outside place of the camp”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 8:13"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “And”">p</a></sup>they came forward,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “came near” or “approached”">q</a></sup>and they carried them ⸤outside the camp⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to from an outside place of the camp”">r</a></sup>in their tunics, just as Moses had ordered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 22:18; Joshua 7:1; Numbers 16:22"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “And”">s</a></sup>Moses said to Aaron and to his sons Eleazar and Ithamar, “You must not let your hair hang loosely, and you must not tear your garments, so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “and”">t</a></sup>you will not die and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="That is, God">u</a></sup>will be angry with all the community. But<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “And”">v</a></sup>your brothers, all the house of Israel, may weep because of ⸤the burning that Yahweh caused⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “the burning that Yahweh burned”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Leviticus 8:30; Acts 10:38"> but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “and”">x</a></sup>you must not go out from the entrance to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “of”">y</a></sup>the tent of assembly lest you die, because Yahweh’s anointing oil is on you.” So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “And”">z</a></sup>they did according to Moses’ word.</span>

## Lasting Statutes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “And”">aa</a></sup>Yahweh spoke to Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:15; 1 Timothy 3:3; Ephesians 5:18"> “You and your sons with you may not drink wine or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “and”">ab</a></sup>strong drink when you come to the tent of assembly, so that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “and”">ac</a></sup>you will not die—it is a lasting statute for your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Plural">ad</a></sup>generations—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; 1 Peter 1:14–16; Leviticus 11:47"> and to distinguish between the holy and the unholy,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “the common”">ae</a></sup>as well as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “and”">af</a></sup>between the unclean and the clean,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Malachi 2:7; Nehemiah 8:8"> and to teach the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ag</a></sup>all the rules that Yahweh has spoken to them ⸤through⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “by the hand of”">ah</a></sup>Moses.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 21:22; Numbers 18:9–10; Leviticus 7:9"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “And”">ai</a></sup>Moses spoke to Aaron and to his sons Eleazar and Ithamar, “As for the remaining parts,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Implied by plural form of noun and the immediate context">aj</a></sup>take the remainder of the grain offering from Yahweh’s offerings made by fire and eat it, the unleavened bread, beside the altar, because it is ⸤a most holy thing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “a holiness of holinesses”">ak</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Leviticus 6:16; Numbers 18:10"> And you shall eat it in a holy place, because it is your allotted portion and the allotted portion of your sons from Yahweh’s offerings made by fire, for so I have been commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 18:11; Leviticus 7:29–34; Exodus 29:24–28"> And the wave offering’s breast section and the upper thigh of the contribution offering you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Plural">al</a></sup>must eat in a clean place, you and your sons and your daughters with you, because they are given as your allotted portion and your sons’ allotted portion from the sacrifices of the ⸤Israelites⸥’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">am</a></sup>fellowship offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 7:34; Genesis 17:13; Leviticus 7:29–30"> They must bring the thigh of the contribution offering and the breast section of the wave offering in addition to the offerings made by fire, consisting of the fat portions, to wave as a wave offering ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">an</a></sup>Yahweh; and it will be for you and for your sons with you as a lasting rule, just as Yahweh had commanded.”</span>
</div>

## The Problem of the Uneaten Sin Offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 9:3; Leviticus 9:15; Leviticus 6:26"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “And”">ao</a></sup>Moses sought all over for the goat of the sin offering and behold, it was burned up. So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “And”">ap</a></sup>he was angry with Aaron’s remaining sons Eleazar and Ithamar, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 7:6–7; Numbers 18:1; Leviticus 6:24–30"> “Why did you not eat the sin offering on the sanctuary’s site, because it is ⸤a most holy thing⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “a holiness of holinesses”">aq</a></sup>And he gave it to you to remove the community’s guilt, to make atonement for them ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">ar</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 6:30; Leviticus 6:26"> Look, its blood was not brought ⸤inside the sanctuary⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Or “to the sanctuary inside” or “to the sanctuary within”">as</a></sup>Certainly you should have eaten it in the sanctuary, as I commanded.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 9:12; Leviticus 9:8; Jeremiah 14:12"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “And”">at</a></sup>Aaron said to Moses, “Look, today they presented their sin offering and their burnt offering ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “to the faces of”">au</a></sup>Yahweh, and things such as these have happened to me, and if I were to eat a sin offering today, would it have been good in Yahweh’s eyes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 7:8–9; Matthew 12:20; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Or “And”">av</a></sup>Moses heard, it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Or “and it”">aw</a></sup>was good in his eyes.</span>
