<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5–6; Matthew 18:15; Psalm 141:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who rebukes a person will afterward find more favor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than he who flatters with the tongue.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Job 28:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who trusts in his own heart</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “mind,” or “sense”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is a fool,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he who walks in wisdom will be saved.</span></span>
