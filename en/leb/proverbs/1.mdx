## The Call of Lady Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalm 37:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will also laugh at your calamity;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “downfall”">o</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will mock when panic</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “dread, fear”">p</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">comes upon you.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 25:12–13; Psalm 112:7–8; Isaiah 26:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whoever listens to me will dwell in security</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and rest securely</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “be at ease, peace”">r</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from dread and disaster.”</span></span>
