<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 18:11; Proverbs 19:7; Psalm 52:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wealth of the rich is the city of his strength;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the ruin of the poor is their poverty.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; Psalm 55:21; 2 Samuel 3:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">He who conceals hatred has lips of deceit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he who utters slander—he is a fool.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:2; Proverbs 17:27–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">In many words, transgression is not lacking,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he who restrains his lips is prudent.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 145:19; Isaiah 66:4; Matthew 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The dread of the wicked will come upon him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the desire of the righteous will be granted.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 15:5; Psalm 58:9; Matthew 7:24–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">With the passing of the tempest, there is no wickedness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the righteous have a foundation forever.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 26:6; Matthew 25:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Like vinegar to the tooth and like smoke to the eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">thus is the lazy to one who employs</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “sends”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 9:11; Proverbs 3:2; Psalm 91:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fear of Yahweh adds life,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the years of the wicked are shortened.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:13; Romans 15:13; Psalm 73:24–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">The hope of the righteous is gladness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the expectation of the wicked comes to nothing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 21:15; Romans 2:8–9; Philippians 4:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">A stronghold for the upright is the way of Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but ruin belongs to ⸤evildoers⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “them who do evil”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 37:22; Psalm 125:1; Psalm 37:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The righteous one is forever; he will not be removed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But the wicked will not remain in the land.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “earth”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 37:30; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 10:20–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mouth of the righteous brings forth wisdom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but a tongue of perversity will be cut off.</span></span>
