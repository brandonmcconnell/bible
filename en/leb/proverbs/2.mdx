## The Benefits of Wisdom

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 3:1; Proverbs 1:3; Proverbs 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">My child, if you will receive</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “take, seize”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my sayings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and hide my commands with you,</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:5; Ephesians 1:17–18; Proverbs 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For if you cry out for understanding,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “call to understanding”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if you lift</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “carry, give”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your voice for insight,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Proverbs 16:16; Matthew 19:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">if you seek her like silver</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and search her out ⸤like treasure⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “like the treasures”">d</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; James 1:5; Job 32:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Yahweh will give wisdom;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from his mouth come knowledge and understanding.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 3:15–17; Proverbs 30:5; Psalm 84:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the upright, he stores</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “hides, keeps”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">sound judgment, a shield for those who walk uprightly,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 23:3; 1 Samuel 2:9; John 10:28–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">in order to guard paths of justice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and keep</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “preserve, guard, watch”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the way of his faithful ones.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; Psalm 143:8–10; Matthew 7:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then you will understand righteousness and justice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and uprightness—every good course—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:16; Colossians 3:16; Job 23:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">for wisdom will enter your heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and knowledge will be pleasing to your soul.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “inner self”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 4:6; Ephesians 5:15; Psalm 25:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Discretion will watch over you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">understanding will protect you,</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Proverbs 21:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">those who forsake the paths of uprightness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to walk in ways of darkness,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Proverbs 10:23; Jeremiah 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">those who are happy to do evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for they delight in the deviousness of evil,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 125:5; Proverbs 21:8; Philippians 2:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤who are crooked in their ways⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “that in their ways are crooked”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and devious in their paths;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 23:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">in order to deliver you from a strange woman,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from a foreign woman who flatters</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “makes smooth”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with her sayings,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:59–60; Ezekiel 16:8; Jeremiah 3:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">she who forsakes the partner of her youth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has forgotten the covenant of her God,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 9:18; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Proverbs 5:4–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">for her house sinks to death,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to the dead</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “Rephaim”">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">are her paths.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalm 81:12; Hosea 4:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Of all who go to her, none shall return,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor do they reach paths of life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; 3 John 1:11; Psalm 119:63"> <span className="pl pl-1">So that you will walk on the road of those who are good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the paths of those who are righteous you shall keep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 84:11; Psalm 37:29; Psalm 37:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">For those who are upright will dwell in the land,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “earth”">l</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who are blameless</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “innocent”">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will remain in it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 52:5; Deuteronomy 7:22; Isaiah 3:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">And those who are wicked will be cut off from the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who are treacherous will be uprooted from it.</span></span>
