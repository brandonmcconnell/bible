## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 3:18; John 8:32"> The elder, to the elect lady and her children, whom I love in truth—and not I alone, but also all those who know the truth—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 2:14; 1 John 1:8; 1 John 2:17"> because of the truth that resides in us and will be with us ⸤forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “in perpetuity”; literally “for the age”">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:10; Romans 1:7; 1 Timothy 1:14"> Grace, mercy, peace will be with us from God the Father and from Jesus Christ the Son of the Father in truth and love.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="3 John 1:3–4; Ephesians 5:8; Ephesians 5:2"> ⸤I was very glad⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “I rejoiced exceedingly”">b</a></sup>because I found some of your children walking in the truth, just as ⸤the Father commanded us⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “we received commandment from the Father”">c</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Watch Out for False Teachers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 3:11; John 13:34–35; John 15:12"> And now I ask you, lady (not as if I were writing a new commandment to you, but one that we have had from the beginning), that we should love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:24; John 15:10"> And this is love: that we walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, just as you have heard from the beginning, so that you should walk in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:26; 1 John 4:1–3; 1 John 2:18–22"> For many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not confess Jesus Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “confess Jesus as Christ coming in the flesh” (see 1 John 4:2 )">d</a></sup>coming in the flesh. This person is the deceiver and the antichrist!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Revelation 3:11; 1 Corinthians 3:14"> Watch yourselves that you do not lose what we have worked for, but receive a full reward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:16–17; Colossians 3:16; 1 John 2:22–24"> Everyone who goes too far and does not remain in the teaching of Christ does not have God. The one who remains in the teaching—this person has both the Father and the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Titus 3:10; Romans 16:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into your house and do not speak a greeting to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; Ephesians 5:11; Revelation 18:4"> because the one who speaks a greeting to him shares in his evil deeds.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Conclusion and Final Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="3 John 1:13–14; John 16:12; 1 Corinthians 16:5–7"> Although I have many things to write to you, I do not want to do so by means of paper and ink, but I hope to be with you and to speak ⸤face to face⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “mouth to mouth”">e</a></sup>, so that your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “our”">f</a></sup>joy may be complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:13; 2 John 1:1–13"> The children of your elect sister greet you.</span>
</div>
</div>
