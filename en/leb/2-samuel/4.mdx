## Ish-Bosheth is Assassinated

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 4:4; Isaiah 13:7; Jeremiah 6:24"> When the son of Saul heard that Abner had died in Hebron, ⸤his courage failed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “his hands hung limp”">a</a></sup>and all of Israel was horrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 18:25; Joshua 9:17; 2 Kings 6:23"> Two of the men, commanders of the raiding bands, were for the son of Saul. The name of one was Baanah, and the name of the other was Recab, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite from the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “sons”">b</a></sup>of Benjamin; also, Beeroth was regarded as belonging to Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:33; 1 Samuel 31:7"> The Beerothites fled to Gittaim, and they are resident aliens there until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:3; 2 Samuel 9:6; 1 Chronicles 9:40"> (Now Jonathan the son of Saul had a son who was crippled in the feet. He was five years old when the message of Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel, and his nurse had picked him up and fled. It happened that as she was hurrying away to flee, he fell and became crippled. His name was Mephibosheth.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 24:33–34; 2 Chronicles 25:27; 1 Kings 16:9"> When the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Recab and Baanah, set out, they came at the heat of the day to the house of Ish-Bosheth while ⸤he was taking a noontime rest⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “he was lying with the bed at noon”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:23; 2 Samuel 20:10; 2 Samuel 3:27"> They came as far as the middle of the house as if takers of wheat, and they struck him in the stomach. Then Recab and Baanah his brother escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 14:11; Mark 6:28–29; 1 Samuel 17:54"> When they had come into the house, he was lying on his couch ⸤in his bedchamber⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in the room of his lying”">d</a></sup>and they attacked him and killed him. Then they ⸤beheaded him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “they took away his head”">e</a></sup>and they took his head and went on the way of the Arabah all night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:29; 2 Samuel 22:48; 1 Samuel 19:2–11"> They brought the head of Ish-Bosheth to David at Hebron, and they said to the king, “Here is the head of Ish-Bosheth, the son of Saul your enemy, who sought your life. Yahweh has given to my lord the king vengeance this day on Saul and on his offspring.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 1:29; Genesis 48:16; Psalm 107:2"> Then David answered Recab and Baanah his brother, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, and he said to them, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">f</a></sup>who redeemed my soul from all trouble,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:2–16"> when the one who told me, “Look, Saul is dead,” ⸤thought that he was bringing good news⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “and he was as one bringing good news in his eyes”">g</a></sup>I seized him and killed him at Ziklag, which was as my giving the news back to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 9:12; Numbers 35:31–34; Genesis 9:5–6"> ⸤How much more⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “Also”">h</a></sup>when wicked men kill a righteous man in his house, on his bed! So then, shall I not seek his lifeblood from your hand, so that I may destroy you from the earth?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:15; 2 Samuel 3:32; Matthew 7:2"> Then David commanded the young men, and they killed them, and they cut off their hands and their feet, and they hung them at the pool at Hebron, but the head of Ish-Bosheth they took and buried in the grave of Abner at Hebron.</span>
