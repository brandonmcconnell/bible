## Amnon Assaults His Sister Tamar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:9; 2 Samuel 3:2–3; 2 Samuel 13:15"> It happened afterwards that Absalom the son of David had a beautiful sister whose name was Tamar, and Amnon the son of David fell in love with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; 1 Kings 21:4; Song of Solomon 5:8"> And Amnon ⸤was so frustrated that he felt ill⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was distressed to the point of sickness”">a</a></sup>because of Tamar his sister, because she was a virgin, and it was too difficult in Amnon’s eyes to do anything with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19; James 3:15"> Now Amnon had a friend whose name was Jonadab the son of Shimeah, the brother of David. (Now Jonadab was a very crafty man.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Esther 5:13–14"> And he said to him, “Why are you so sullen ⸤every morning⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in the morning, in the morning”">b</a></sup>O son of the king? Will you not tell me?” And Amnon said to him, “I am in love with Tamar, the sister of my brother Absalom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 6:24–25; Proverbs 19:27; Acts 23:15"> Then Jonadab said to him, “Lie down on your bed and appear ill. If your father comes to see you, you shall say to him, ‘Please let Tamar my sister come and give me food to eat, and let her prepare the food before my eyes, in order that I may see it and eat from her hand.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:6; Matthew 13:33"> So Amnon lay down and pretended to be ill, and the king came to see him. Amnon said to the king, “Please let Tamar my sister come, and let her bake two cakes before my eyes that I may eat from her hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> So David sent to the house for Tamar, saying, “Please go to the house of Amnon your brother and prepare food for him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Tamar went to the house of Amnon her brother. Now he was lying down, and she took the dough and kneaded it and made cakes before his eyes, and she baked the cakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; John 3:20; Judges 3:19"> Then she took the pan and poured it out before him, but he refused to eat. Then Amnon said, “Let all the men go out from me.” So all the men went out from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Amnon said to Tamar, “Bring the food to the private room that I may eat from your hand.” So Tamar took the cakes which she had made and brought them to Amnon her brother in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “to”">c</a></sup>the private room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:11–12"> When she brought them near to him to eat, he took hold of her and said to her, “Come, lie with me, my sister!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:17; Judges 20:6"> Then Tamar said to him, “No, my brother! Do not force me, for such a thing has not been done in Israel. Do not do this disgraceful thing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 20:12; Genesis 19:8"> As for me, where should I take my disgrace? You will be as one of the fools in Israel. So please, speak to the king, for he will not withhold me from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:11; Esther 7:8; Judges 20:5"> But he was not willing to listen to her voice. He was stronger than she, and he forced her and lay with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:17"> Then Amnon hated her ⸤very deeply⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “with a great hatred”">d</a></sup>for the hatred with which he hated her was greater than the love with which he had loved her. So Amnon said to her, “Get up and go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> She said to him, “No, because this evil in sending me away is greater than the other you have done to me.” But he was not willing to listen to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then he called his young man who was serving him and said, “Please send this woman from me to the outside, and bolt the door behind her!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Genesis 37:3; Psalm 45:13–14"> Now there was a long-sleeved robe on her, for so they clothed the daughters of the king who were virgins, in robes. His servant put her outside, and he bolted the door behind her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Jeremiah 2:37; 2 Samuel 1:11"> Tamar put ashes on her head, and she tore the long-sleeved robe which was on her. She put her hand on her head, and ⸤she went away, crying out as she went⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “she went, going and crying aloud”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 46:15; Genesis 34:2"> Absalom her brother said to her, “Was Amnon your brother with you? But now, my sister, be quiet; he is your brother. ⸤Do not take this matter to heart⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “Do not place/set your heart to this matter”">f</a></sup>So Tamar remained a desolate woman in the house of Absalom her brother.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Absalom Kills Amnon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; 2 Samuel 3:28–29; 1 Samuel 2:29"> Now King David heard all these things, and ⸤he became very angry⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “it became very hot for him”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17–18; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 24:50"> Absalom did not speak with Amnon ⸤either bad or good⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “as far as from bad and up to good”">h</a></sup>for Absalom hated Amnon ⸤over the matter when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “because of the thing that”">i</a></sup>he raped Tamar his sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 38:12–13; 1 Samuel 25:4; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> ⸤About two full years later⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “And it happened at two years of days”">j</a></sup>Absalom’s shearers were in Baal Hazor, which is near Ephraim, and Absalom summoned all the sons of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:8–15; Jeremiah 41:6–7; Psalm 55:21"> Then Absalom went to the king and said, “Look, here are your servant’s shearers; please let the king and his servants go with your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 14:23; Judges 19:7–10; 2 Samuel 14:22"> The king said to Absalom, “No my son, not all of us shall go, so that we not be a burden to you.” And he urged him, but he was not willing to go, but he blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:9; 2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 11:13–15"> So Absalom said, “But will you not let Amnon my brother go with us?” And the king said to him, “Why should he go with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26"> But Absalom pressed him, so he sent Amnon with him and all of the sons of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Judges 19:22; Judges 19:9"> Absalom commanded his servants, saying, “Please watch. At the moment the heart of Amnon is ⸤tipsy⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “good”">k</a></sup>with wine, then I shall say to you, ‘Strike Amnon down,’ and you shall kill him! Don’t be afraid. Is it not I myself who has commanded you? Be courageous and be ⸤valiant!⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “as sons of ability”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:9; 1 Kings 1:33; 2 Kings 1:9–12"> So Absalom’s servants did to Amnon just as Absalom commanded, and all the sons of the king got up, and each mounted his mule and fled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> ⸤While they were on the way⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “And it happened they were on the way”">m</a></sup>the message came to David, “Absalom has killed all the sons of the king, and not one of them was left.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:11; 2 Samuel 12:16; Job 1:20"> Then the king rose and tore his garments, and he lay on the ground, and all his servants standing by were tearing their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:9; 2 Samuel 13:3; Genesis 27:41"> Then Jonadab the son of Shimeah, the brother of David, responded and said, “My lord should not think that all the young men, the sons of the king, are dead, because only Amnon is dead. ⸤Absalom was talking about it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “For it was on the mouth of Absalom”">n</a></sup>as it was being determined from the day he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Amnon">o</a></sup>raped Tamar his sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19"> So then, let not my lord the king set his heart on this matter, thinking, ‘All the sons of the king are dead,’ for only Amnon alone is dead.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Absalom Flees

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 4:8–14; Proverbs 28:17; Amos 5:19"> So Absalom fled, and the young man who was keeping watch lifted up his eyes and saw, and there were many people coming from the road behind him from the side of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Jonadab said to the king, “Look, the sons of the king have come. According to the word of your servant, so it has come about.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:33; 2 Samuel 12:21; 2 Samuel 13:15"> When he finished speaking, look, the sons of the king came and they lifted up their voice and wept. Also, the king and all his servants wept a very great weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3; 2 Samuel 14:32; 2 Samuel 13:34"> Absalom fled and went to Talmai the son of Ammihud, the king of Geshur. David mourned over his son ⸤day after day⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “all the days”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:8; 2 Samuel 14:23; 2 Samuel 14:32"> But Absalom had fled and went to Geshur, and he was there three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 38:12; Genesis 37:35"> King David longed to go out to Absalom, for he was consoled that Amnon had died.</span>
</div>
