<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 1 Samuel 30:5; Luke 22:28–29"> So David went up there ⸤along with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and also”">a</a></sup>his two wives, Ahinoam ⸤from Jezreel⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the Jezreelitess”">b</a></sup>and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21; Psalm 115:15; Ruth 2:20"> So David sent messengers to the men of Jabesh-Gilead and said to them, “May you be blessed by Yahweh because you did this loyal love with your lord, with Saul, and you buried him.</span>

## Ish-Bosheth over Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:50; Genesis 32:2; 2 Samuel 3:7–8"> But Abner the son of Ner, the commander of Saul’s army, had taken Ish-Bosheth the son of Saul and brought him over to Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Joshua 13:8–11; Numbers 1:40"> He made him king over Gilead, over the Ashurites, over Jezreel, over Ephraim, over Benjamin, and over Israel, all of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Ish-Bosheth the son of Saul was forty years old when he became king over Israel and he reigned two years; however, the house of Judah ⸤followed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “were after”">e</a></sup>David.</span>

## War between Judah and Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:25; Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:3"> Abner the son of Ner and the servants of Ish-Bosheth the son of Saul went out from Mahanaim to Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; 1 Chronicles 2:16; 2 Samuel 20:23"> Then Joab the son of Zeruiah and the servants of David went out, and they met at the pool of Gibeon. The one group sat on one side of the pool, and the other sat on the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:17; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 17:14"> Abner said to Joab, “Let the young men come up and fight in our presence.” And Joab said, “Let them come up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So they came forward and passed by in number: twelve for Benjamin and for Ish-Bosheth the son of Saul, and twelve from the servants of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then each seized his ⸤opponent⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “neighbor”">f</a></sup>by the head and each thrust his sword in the side of his opponent, so they fell together. So they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">g</a></sup>called the name of that place Helkath Hazzurim,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Helkath Hazzurim possibly means “the slippery slope”">h</a></sup>which is in Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1"> Then the battle ⸤became increasingly fierce⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and it was/became strong/fierce until very”">i</a></sup>on that day, and Abner and the men of Israel were defeated before the servants of David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:8; Habakkuk 3:19; Song of Solomon 8:14"> The three sons of Zeruiah were there, Joab and Abishai and Asahel. Now Asahel was swift with his feet as one of the gazelles which is in the open field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:21; Joshua 1:7; Proverbs 4:27"> So Asahel pursued<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “pursued after”">j</a></sup>Abner, and he did not turn aside to the right or to the left from going after Abner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Abner turned around and said, “Are you this Asahel?” And he said, “Yes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 14:19"> Abner said to him, “Turn aside to your right or to your left; seize for yourself one of the young men, and take his belongings for yourself.” But Asahel was not willing to turn aside from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; Proverbs 29:1; 2 Kings 14:10–12"> So Abner said to Asahel ⸤once again⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “and he again did again”">k</a></sup>“⸤For your own sake⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “for yourself”">l</a></sup>turn aside ⸤from following me⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “from after me”">m</a></sup>Why should I strike you down to the ground? How could I ⸤show my face⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “lift up my face”">n</a></sup>to Joab your brother?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 4:6; 2 Samuel 5:6"> But he refused to turn away, so Abner struck him in the stomach with the butt of the spear, and the spear went out of his back. He fell there and he died ⸤on the spot⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “in his place”">o</a></sup>⸤Then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">p</a></sup>all who came to the place where Asahel fell and died just stood there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So Joab and Abishai pursued<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “pursued after”">q</a></sup>Abner ⸤when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “and”">r</a></sup>the sun went down. And they came to the hill country of Ammah, which is ⸤before Giah⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “lies on the face of Giah”">s</a></sup>on the way to the wilderness of Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “sons”">t</a></sup>of Benjamin rallied after Abner, and they became as one fighting group and stood on the top of a certain hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 46:10; Acts 7:26"> Then Abner called to Joab and said, “Will the sword devour forever? Do you not know that there will be bitterness in the end? ⸤How long⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “And up to when”">u</a></sup>will you not tell the people to turn ⸤away from pursuing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “from after”">v</a></sup>their brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; 2 Samuel 2:14; Proverbs 15:1"> Joab said, “⸤As God lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “The life of God”">w</a></sup>for if you had not spoken, the people would surely have gone up in the morning, each one of them ⸤from following after⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “from after”">x</a></sup>his brother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then Joab blew on the trumpet and all the people stopped, and they no longer pursued after Israel, and they did not fight with them again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Song of Solomon 2:17"> Then Abner and his men went through the Arabah all that night, and they crossed over the Jordan. They went all the forenoon and came to Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> After Joab returned from pursuing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “after”">y</a></sup>Abner, he gathered all the people; nineteen of the servants of David were missing along with Asahel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; 1 Kings 20:11"> The servants of David had killed some of the Benjaminites among the men of Abner; three hundred and sixty men had died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13–16; 1 Samuel 17:58; 2 Chronicles 21:1"> Then they picked up Asahel and buried him in the grave of his father, which was at Bethlehem. Joab and his men went all that night ⸤arriving in Hebron at first light⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “it became light for them in Hebron”">z</a></sup></span>
</div>
