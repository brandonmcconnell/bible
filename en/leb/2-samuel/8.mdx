## David’s Military Victories Continue

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:1–17; 2 Samuel 7:9; 2 Samuel 21:15–22"> It happened afterwards that David attacked the Philistines and subdued them, and he took Metheg Ammah from the hands of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; 1 Samuel 10:27; 2 Samuel 8:6"> When he defeated Moab, he measured them with the cord, making them lie on the ground. He measured two cords to kill and ⸤the third cord⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the filling of a cord”">a</a></sup>to let live.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="This method of selection must have been well known to the reader, but the exact procedure has been obscured over the millennia. It may be that one out of three lived through the process, only to become subservient">b</a></sup>So Moab became servants of David, bringing tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:47; 2 Samuel 10:16; 2 Samuel 10:19"> Then David struck down Hadadezer the son of Rehob, king of Zobah, when he went to restore his monument<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “hand”">c</a></sup>at the Euphrates River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:6; Joshua 11:9; 1 Chronicles 18:4"> David captured from him one thousand and seven hundred horsemen and twenty thousand ⸤foot soldiers⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “men of infantry”">d</a></sup>David hamstrung all the chariot horses, but ⸤from them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from him”">e</a></sup>he spared a hundred chariot horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 11:23–25; Isaiah 7:8; Job 9:13"> When Aram of Damascus came to help Hadadezer, the king of Zobah, David killed twenty-two thousand men of Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 7:9; Psalm 18:34–46"> David placed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, so Aram became servants of David, bringing tribute. Yahweh protected David everywhere he went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 14:26–27; 1 Kings 10:16–17"> David took the small round gold shields which had ⸤belonged⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “been”">f</a></sup>to the servants of Hadadezer, and he brought them to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:8; 1 Chronicles 22:16; Ezekiel 47:16"> From Betah and from Berothai, the towns of Hadadezer, King David took very many bronze items.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Chronicles 8:4; 1 Chronicles 18:9"> When Toi, the king of Hamath, heard that David had defeated all the army of Hadadezer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:10; 1 Chronicles 18:10; Psalm 129:8"> Toi sent Joram his son to King David ⸤to greet him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to ask peace for himself”">g</a></sup>and to congratulate him because he had fought against Hadadezer and defeated him; ⸤for Hadadezer had often been at war with Toi⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “for Hadadezer was a man of wars with Toi”">h</a></sup>⸤He brought with him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “In his hand were”">i</a></sup>objects of silver and objects of gold and objects of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 29:2; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> King David dedicated them also to Yahweh along with the silver and the gold that he had dedicated from all of the nations which he had subdued;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:14; 2 Samuel 8:2; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> from Aram and from Moab and from the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">j</a></sup>and from the Philistines and from Amalek and from the plunder of Hadadezer the son of Rehob, the king of Zobah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 14:7; 2 Samuel 7:9; 1 Chronicles 18:12"> So David made a name for himself when he returned from defeating Aram in the Valley of Salt, eighteen thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Genesis 25:23; 2 Samuel 8:6"> He put garrisons in Edom; all over Edom he put garrisons, and all of Edom became servants of David. Yahweh protected David wherever he went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 5:15; Psalm 89:14; Psalm 72:2"> David reigned over all of Israel, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “David”">k</a></sup>was administering justice and righteousness for all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 4:3; 1 Chronicles 11:6; 2 Samuel 19:13"> Joab the son of Zeruiah was over the army, and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was secretary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 18:16; 1 Chronicles 24:3–4"> Zadok the son of Ahitub and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar were priests and Seraiah was scribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:14; 2 Samuel 20:7; 2 Samuel 20:23"> Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the Kerethites and the Pelethites, and the sons of David were priests.</span>
</div>
</div>
