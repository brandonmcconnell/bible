## David Anointed as King over All of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:1–3; 2 Samuel 19:13; Genesis 29:14"> So all the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron, and they said, “Here we are, ⸤we are your flesh and blood⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “We are your bone and your flesh”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:6; Micah 5:4; Ezekiel 34:23"> ⸤For some time⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “Both yesterday and three days before”">b</a></sup>when Saul was king over us, ⸤you were leading Israel in and out⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “you were going out and coming in, Israel.” (So Kethib ; Qere reads “bringing out and bringing in Israel”)">c</a></sup>Yahweh had said to you, ‘You shall be the shepherd of my people Israel, and you will be the leader over Israel.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:4; 1 Samuel 16:13; 1 Samuel 23:18"> All the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and King David ⸤made a covenant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “cut a covenant”">d</a></sup>with them at Hebron in the presence of Yahweh; then they anointed David as king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:27; Luke 3:23; 1 Kings 2:11"> David was thirty years old when he began to reign; he reigned forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:11; 1 Chronicles 3:4; 1 Kings 2:11"> He reigned over Judah at Hebron for seven years and six months; and he reigned over all Israel and Judah at Jerusalem for thirty-three years.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Capture and Growth of Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 1:8; Jeremiah 37:10; 1 Chronicles 11:4–9"> The king and his men went to Jerusalem, to the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land. They said to David, “You will not come here, for even the blind and the lame can turn you back, saying, ‘David cannot come here.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; 2 Samuel 6:16; 2 Samuel 6:12"> David captured the fortress of Zion, the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 15:16–17; 1 Chronicles 11:6–9; 1 Samuel 17:25"> David had said, “On that day ⸤when we attack the Jebusites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in all of the striking down the Jebusites”">e</a></sup>one must attack the lame and the blind, those who hate the soul of David, by means of the water supply.” For thus the blind and the lame would say, “He cannot come into the house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 1 Kings 9:24; 2 Chronicles 32:5"> David occupied the fortress and called it the city of David. And David built all around it from the Millo and ⸤inward⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to the house”">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Romans 8:31; Psalm 46:11"> David ⸤continued growing stronger and stronger⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “went going and great”">g</a></sup>and Yahweh the God of hosts was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:1; 1 Kings 5:1–2; 1 Kings 5:18"> So Hiram, the king of Tyre, sent messengers to David, along with cedar trees and ⸤craftsmen skilled in wood and in stone masonry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “skilled craftsmen of wood and skilled craftsmen of stone wall”">h</a></sup>and they built a house for David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:16; 1 Kings 10:9; 1 Chronicles 14:2"> David realized that Yahweh had established him as king over Israel and that he had exalted his kingdom because of his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:9; Deuteronomy 17:17; 2 Chronicles 13:21"> David took more concubines and wives from Jerusalem after he came from Hebron, and more sons and daughters were born to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:5–9; Luke 2:31; 1 Chronicles 14:4"> These are the names of the ones born to him in Jerusalem: Shammua and Shobab and Nathan and Solomon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:6; 1 Chronicles 14:5"> Ibhar and Elishua and Nepheg and Japhia;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Chronicles 14:7"> Elishama and Eliada and Eliphelet.</span>
</div>
</div>

## War with the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:14; 1 Chronicles 14:8–9; 1 Chronicles 11:16"> When the Philistines heard that they had anointed David as king over Israel, all the Philistines went up to seek David, but David heard and went down to the stronghold.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Thought by many to be the stronghold of Masada">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 15:8; 2 Samuel 23:13; Genesis 14:5"> Now the Philistines had come, and they spread out in the Valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:1; 1 Samuel 23:2; 2 Samuel 5:23"> And David inquired of Yahweh, saying, “Shall I go up to the Philistines? Will you give them into my hands?” Yahweh said to David, “Go up, for ⸤I will certainly give⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “giving I will give”">j</a></sup>the Philistines into your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 28:21"> So David came to Baal Perazim and defeated them there; and David said, “Yahweh has burst through my enemies before me like the bursting of water.” Therefore he called the name of that place Baal Perazim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Baal Perazim may mean “the master of the bursting through”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:11–12; Deuteronomy 7:5; 1 Samuel 5:2–6"> They had left their idols there, so David and his men carried them away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Samuel 5:18; 1 Kings 20:22"> ⸤Once again⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “And they again did again”">l</a></sup>the Philistines came up and spread out in the Valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:14; Matthew 9:29–30; Matthew 8:23–25"> So David inquired of Yahweh, but he said, “You shall not go up. Rather, go around to their rear and come to them from opposite the balsam trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 7:6; Judges 4:14; Judges 7:15"> ⸤And it shall be⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “And it shall happen”">m</a></sup>that when you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, then pay attention, for then Yahweh has gone out before you to strike down the army of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:16; Joshua 16:10; Isaiah 28:21"> So David did thus, just as Yahweh had commanded him, and he struck down the Philistines from Geba ⸤all the way⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “as you go up”">n</a></sup>to Gezer.</span>
</div>
</div>
