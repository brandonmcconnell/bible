## The Ammonites Refuse David’s Loyal Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–3; Judges 11:12–28; Judges 10:7–9"> ⸤Afterwards the king of the Ammonites died⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “And it happened afterwards that the king of the sons of Ammon died”">a</a></sup>and his son Hanun ruled in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1; Deuteronomy 23:3–6; Nehemiah 13:1–3"> David said, “I will show loyal love with Hanun, the son of Nahash, as his father showed loyal love with me.” So David sent to console him concerning his father, by the hand of his servants. And the servants of David came to the land of the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:16; Genesis 42:9; 1 Corinthians 13:5"> But the commanders of the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">c</a></sup>said to Hanun their master, “⸤In your opinion⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “In your eyes”">d</a></sup>is David honoring your father because he has sent condolences to you? Is it not in order to search the city, to spy it out, and to overthrow it that David sent his servants to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 41:5; 1 Chronicles 19:3–4"> Then Hanun took the servants of David, and he shaved off half of their beards and cut their garments off in the middle up to their buttocks, then sent them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:5; 1 Kings 16:34; Joshua 6:24–26"> When they told David, he sent to meet them, for the men were greatly ashamed. And the king said, “Remain in Jericho until your beards have grown, and then you can return.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel Fights Ammon and Aram

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:3; 2 Samuel 8:5; Genesis 34:30"> When the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">e</a></sup>saw that they had become odious to David, the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">f</a></sup>sent word and hired Aram Beth-Rehob and Aram-Zobah, twenty thousand infantry; and they also hired the king of Maacah, a thousand men, and the men of Tob, twelve thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:8–39; 1 Chronicles 19:8–19"> When David heard, he sent Joab and all the army of mighty warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; Joshua 19:28; Numbers 13:21"> The ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">g</a></sup>came out and ⸤drew up a battle formation⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “drew up battle”">h</a></sup>at the entrance of the gate, but Aram-Zobah and Rehob and the men of Tob and Maacah were alone in the open field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 20:42–43; Joshua 8:21–22"> When Joab saw that ⸤the battle was to be fought on two fronts⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the face of the battle was against him in front and in the rear”">i</a></sup>he chose from all the members of the elite troops of Israel and ⸤lined them up for battle⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “arrayed”">j</a></sup>to meet Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The rest of the army he placed into the hand of his brother Abishai, who ⸤arranged them in battle lines⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “arrayed”">k</a></sup>to meet the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Nehemiah 4:20; Philippians 1:27–28"> Then he said, “If Aram is stronger than I am, you must become my deliverer; but if the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">m</a></sup>are too strong for you, then I will come to rescue you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:13; Deuteronomy 31:6; 1 Samuel 3:18"> Be strong, and let us strengthen ourselves for the sake of the people and for the sake of the cities of our God. May Yahweh do what is good in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 20:13–21; 1 Kings 20:28–30; 1 Chronicles 19:14–15"> Joab and all the people who were with him moved forward into the battle against Aram, and they fled from before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">n</a></sup>saw that Aram had fled, they fled from before Abishai and entered the city. Then Joab returned from fighting against the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">o</a></sup>and came to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Arameans Regroup for Attack

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:2–3; Micah 4:11–12; Isaiah 8:9–10"> When the Arameans saw that they were defeated before Israel, they gathered themselves together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:16; 2 Samuel 8:3–8; 1 Chronicles 18:3"> Then Hadadezer sent and brought out the Arameans who were beyond the Euphrates, and they came to Helam. Now Shobach, the commander of the army of Hadadezer, ⸤was at their head⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “before the face of them”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:17"> David was told, so he gathered all Israel and crossed over the Jordan and came to Helam. Aram ⸤arranged themselves in battle lines⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “arrayed”">q</a></sup>to meet David, and they fought with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:18; 2 Samuel 8:4; Psalm 46:11"> And Aram fled before Israel, and David killed from the Arameans seven hundred chariot teams and forty thousand horsemen. He struck down Shobach, the commander of his army, and he died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:6; Revelation 18:10; Psalm 48:4–5"> When all the kings, the servants of Hadadezer, saw that he had been defeated before Israel, they made peace with Israel and served them, and Aram was afraid to help the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">r</a></sup>any longer.</span>
