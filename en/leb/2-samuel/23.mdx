## David Extols Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12–13; 2 Samuel 7:8–9; Psalm 89:20"> These are the last words of David, the declaration of David the son of Jesse, and the declaration of the man exalted by the Most High, the anointed one of the God of Jacob and the darling of the songs of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:21; Matthew 22:43; Mark 12:36"> “The spirit of Yahweh speaks through me, and his word is upon my tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 22:32; Deuteronomy 32:4; Exodus 18:21"> The God of Israel said to me, the rock of Israel has spoken; ‘He who rules over mankind rules righteously, in the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Psalm 72:6; Hosea 6:5"> Like the light of the morning when the sun rises, shining with no clouds, bringing vegetation from the earth apart from rain.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="In other words, even men who rule rightly pass like the dew of the morning">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; 2 Samuel 7:14–16; Isaiah 9:6–7"> Yet not so is my house with God, for he made an everlasting covenant for me, arranging everything. He has secured all my deliverance, and all my desire he will cause to happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:6; Isaiah 33:12; Deuteronomy 13:13"> But evil persons are like thorns cast aside; all of them, because they cannot be picked up in the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 6:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Isaiah 27:4"> And if a man wants to touch them, he must use an iron instrument or the shaft of a spear; then they are consumed entirely with fire on the spot.”</span>

## David’s Faithful Soldiers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 11:11–47; 1 Chronicles 27:32"> These are the names of the mighty warriors who were David’s: Josheb-Basshebeth a Tahkemonite was chief of three officers; first Adino, whose spear was against eight hundred slain on one occurrence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 1 Chronicles 11:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:26"> Next to him Eleazar, the son of Dodo the son of an Ahohite, was among the three mighty warriors with David when they defied the Philistines and they gathered there for the battle and the men of Israel withdrew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 11:13; Judges 15:14"> He stood up and struck down the Philistines until his hand grew tired and his hand clung to the sword, and Yahweh brought about a great victory on that day. Then the army returned back to him only for stripping the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:13–14; 1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:33"> Next to him was Shamma, the son of Agee the Hararite. When the Philistines assembled at Lehi, a plot of the field was there filled with lentils, and the army fled there from the presence of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; 2 Samuel 23:10; Psalm 3:8"> But he took a stand in the middle of the plot of land and defended it. He killed the Philistines, and Yahweh brought about a great victory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Samuel 5:18; Joshua 12:15"> Then three<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="So Qere ; Kethib reads “thirty”">b</a></sup>of the thirty leaders went down and came to David ⸤at the time of the harvest⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to the harvest”">c</a></sup>to the cave of Adullam, while a group of the Philistines were camping in the valley of the Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:4–5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 10:5"> Now at that time, David was in the stronghold, and a garrison of the Philistines was in Bethlehem at that same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalm 42:1–2; Isaiah 41:17"> David ⸤said longingly⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “desired and said”">d</a></sup>“⸤Oh that someone would bring me a drink⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “Who will let me drink …?”">e</a></sup>of water from the well of Bethlehem that is at the gate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; Philippians 2:17"> So three of the mighty warriors broke into the camp of the Philistines, and they drew water from the well of Bethlehem that was at the gate, and they carried it and brought it to David. But he was not willing to drink it, but poured it out to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> He said, “Far be it from me before Yahweh that I should do this. Is this not the blood of the men who went at the risk of their lives?” So he was not willing to drink it. These things the three mighty warriors did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 10:14; 1 Chronicles 11:20–21"> Now Abishai the brother of Joab the son of Zeruiah was himself the leader of the thirty. He was wielding his spear against three hundred slain and ⸤gained a name⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “and to him a name”">f</a></sup>among the thirty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:8; Matthew 13:23; 1 Corinthians 15:41"> Among the thirty, is it not that he was honored and became a commander for them? But he did not come up to the three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21; 2 Samuel 20:23"> Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the son of Ish-Hai, was a great man of deeds from Kabzeel. He struck down two sons of Ariel of Moab, and he went down and killed a lion in the middle of a pit on a snowy day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:51; 1 Chronicles 11:23; Colossians 2:15"> He also killed a good-looking Egyptian man, in whose hand was a spear. He went down against him with the staff and snatched the spear from the hand of the Egyptian and killed him with his spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> These things Benaiah the son of Jehoiada did and gained a name for himself among the three mighty warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23; 1 Chronicles 27:6; 1 Samuel 22:14"> He was honored more than the thirty, but he did not come up to the three. David appointed him in charge of his bodyguard.</span>
</div>
</div>
