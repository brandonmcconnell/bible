## Absalom Leads a Rebellion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 1:5; 2 Samuel 12:11; 1 Samuel 8:11"> It happened afterward that Absalom made himself a chariot with horses and fifty men running before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 18:14; Ruth 4:1; Job 24:14"> Absalom used to rise early in the morning, and he stood ⸤beside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “on hand of”">a</a></sup>the road at the gate; ⸤anyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “every man”">b</a></sup>who had a legal dispute to bring to the king for judgment Absalom would call to him and say, “⸤Where are you from?⸥”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “Where from this city are you?”">c</a></sup>And he would say, “Your servant is from one of the tribes of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:15; Ezekiel 22:7; 1 Peter 2:17"> Then Absalom would say to him, “Look, your case is good and right, but for you there is no hearing with the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:29; Judges 9:1–5; Proverbs 27:2"> Then Absalom would say, “⸤Oh, that someone would⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “Who will …?”">d</a></sup>appoint me as judge in the land, that ⸤anyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “every man”">e</a></sup>might come to me who had a legal dispute or a case, and I would give him justice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:33; Psalm 55:21; Proverbs 26:25"> It happened whenever anyone drew near to do obeisance to him, that he would stretch his hand and take hold of him and kiss him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Peter 2:3; Proverbs 11:9"> Absalom did like this to all of Israel who came to the king for judgment; so Absalom stole the hearts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “heart”">f</a></sup>of the people of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 58:4; 1 Samuel 16:13; 1 Samuel 16:1–2"> It happened at the end of four<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="So LXX, followed by Syriac and Vulgate; MT has “forty”">g</a></sup>years that Absalom said to the king, “Please let me go and pay my vow which I have made to Yahweh in Hebron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 28:20–21; 2 Samuel 13:37–38; 1 Samuel 1:11"> for your servant made a vow while I was staying in Geshur in Aram, saying, ‘⸤If Yahweh will indeed let me return⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally (following Qere ) “If returning Yahweh will return me”">h</a></sup>to Jerusalem, then I will worship Yahweh.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then the king said to him, “Go in peace.” So he got up and went to Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:38; 2 Samuel 5:5; 2 Samuel 3:2–3"> Then Absalom sent scouts throughout all the tribes of Israel, saying, “When you hear the sound of the trumpet, you shall shout ‘Absalom has become king in Hebron!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:13; 1 Samuel 22:15; Romans 16:18–19"> Two hundred men from Jerusalem went with Absalom as invited guests, going in their innocence as they did not know anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 41:9; 2 Samuel 15:31; Joshua 15:51"> Absalom sent for Ahithophel the Gilonite, the advisor of David, from his city Giloh, while he offered the sacrifices. The conspiracy ⸤grew in strength⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “became strong”">i</a></sup>and the people were going and ⸤increasing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “many”">j</a></sup>with Absalom.</span>
</div>

## David Flees from Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 9:3; Matthew 27:22; 2 Samuel 15:6"> Then the messenger came to David, saying, “The hearts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “heart”">k</a></sup>of the men of Israel have gone after Absalom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:9; Ezekiel 46:18; Psalm 55:3–11"> Then David said to all his servants who were with him in Jerusalem, “Get up and let us flee, for there will be no escape for us from Absalom! Hurry to go, otherwise he will come quickly and overtake us! And he will bring disaster on us and evil! He will attack the city with the edge of the sword!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:66–69; Proverbs 18:24; Luke 22:28–29"> Then the servants of the king said to the king, “According to all that my lord the king chooses, ⸤your servants are ready to act⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “here are your servants”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:21–22; 2 Samuel 20:3; Psalm 3:1"> The king went out with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">m</a></sup>all his household ⸤following him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “at his feet”">n</a></sup>but the king left behind ten concubines to look after the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 3:1–2; Ecclesiastes 10:7; Psalm 66:12"> So the king went out with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">o</a></sup>all the people ⸤following him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “at his feet”">p</a></sup>and they stopped ⸤at the last house⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “at the house of the distance”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Samuel 30:14; 2 Samuel 20:7"> All his servants were passing ⸤by him⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “on his hand”">r</a></sup>all of the Kerethites and all of the Pelethites and all of the Gittites—six hundred men ⸤who had followed him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “who had come at his feet”">s</a></sup>from Gath—passing ⸤before the king⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “on the face of the king”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:2; Ruth 1:11–13"> The king said to Ittai the Gittite, “Why are you also coming with us? Return and stay with the king, for you are a foreigner; moreover, you are an exile. ⸤You are far from your place⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “You as far as your place”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:13; 2 Samuel 2:6; John 1:17"> Yesterday when you came and ⸤today⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the day”">v</a></sup>I have caused you to wander by going with us. Now I am going to where I am going; return and let your brothers return. May loyal love and faithfulness be with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ruth 1:16–17; Proverbs 17:17; 2 Kings 4:30"> But Ittai answered the king and said, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">w</a></sup>and ⸤my lord the king lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “the life of my lord the king”">x</a></sup>surely in the place wherever my lord the king shall be, if for death or if for life, surely there your servant will be.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then David said to Ittai, “Go and pass over.” So Ittai the Gittite passed over and all his men and all of the little children who were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:2; 1 Kings 2:37; John 18:1"> All of the land was weeping with a loud voice as all the people were passing by and the king was crossing through the Wadi Kidron, and all the people were passing ⸤on the road to the wilderness⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “on the face of the road with the wilderness”">y</a></sup></span>

## The Priests Offer Sacrifices for David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 20:25"> Suddenly Zadok was there, and all of the Levites with him, carrying the ark of the covenant of God. They set the ark of God down, and Abiathar offered sacrifices until all the people passed out of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:30; Psalm 26:8; Psalm 43:3–4"> The king said to Zadok, “Let the ark of God return to the city. If I find favor in the eyes of Yahweh, he will let me return and let me see him in his dwelling place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 22:20; 1 Kings 10:9"> But if he says, ‘I take no pleasure in you,’ then ⸤I am ready⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “here I am ”">z</a></sup>Let him do to me that which is good in his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 24:11"> Then the king said to Zadok the priest, “Are you a seer? Return to the city in peace, with Ahimaaz your son and Jonathan the son of Abiathar, your two sons with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:16; 2 Samuel 15:23; 2 Samuel 17:1"> See, I am waiting at the fords of the wilderness until a word comes from you all to inform me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> So Zadok and Abiathar returned the ark of God to Jerusalem and they remained there.</span>
</div>

## Hushai Offers to Serve King David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Esther 6:12; 2 Samuel 19:4; Luke 19:41"> Now David was going up on the Ascent of the Olives, ⸤weeping as he went⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “going up and weeping”">aa</a></sup>with his head covered and going barefoot. All the people who were with him each covered their head ⸤and wept as they went⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “going up and weeping”">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:14; 2 Samuel 17:23; James 3:15"> Now David was told, “Ahithophel was among the conspirators with Absalom.” Then David said, “Please frustrate the counsel of Ahithophel, O Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 16:2; 2 Samuel 1:2; Psalm 3:3–5"> It happened that as David was coming up to the summit where he used to worship God, suddenly Hushai the Arkite was there to meet him. His coat was torn and dirt was on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:35"> David said to him, “If you move on with me, you will be a burden to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 8:2; 2 Samuel 16:16–19; Matthew 10:16"> but if you want to return to the city and say to Absalom, ‘I am your servant, O king. I used to be a servant of your father, but from then and now I will be your servant,’ then you can frustrate the counsel of Ahithophel for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:15–16"> Will not Zadok and Abiathar the priests be with you there? It shall be that all the words you hear from the house of the king you shall tell Zadok and Abiathar the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 15:27; 2 Samuel 18:19–33"> Look, there with them are their two sons, Ahimaaz of Zadok and Jonathan of Abiathar. You shall send to me ⸤by means of them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “by their hand”">ac</a></sup>all the words that you hear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:15–16; 1 Chronicles 27:33"> So Hushai the friend of David came to the city as Absalom was entering Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
