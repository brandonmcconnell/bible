## Yahweh Makes a Covenant with David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Luke 1:74–75; 1 Kings 5:4"> It happened that the king settled in his house. (Now Yahweh had given rest to him from all his enemies all around.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 2 Samuel 5:11; Haggai 1:4"> And the king said to Nathan the prophet, “Look, please, I am living in a house of cedar, but the ark of God is staying in the middle of the tent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 8:17–18; 2 Kings 4:27"> Nathan said to the king, “Go and do all that is in your heart, for Yahweh is with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:3; Numbers 12:6; Amos 3:7"> But it happened ⸤that night⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “in the night the that”">a</a></sup>the word of Yahweh came<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “was”">b</a></sup>to Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:3–32; 1 Kings 5:3–4; 1 Kings 8:16–19"> “Go and tell my servant David, ‘Thus says Yahweh: “Are you the one to build for me a house for my dwelling?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 8:16; Joshua 18:1; Exodus 40:18–19"> For I have not dwelt in a house from the day I brought up the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>from Egypt until this day; rather, I was going about in a tent and in a tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:6; 2 Samuel 5:2; Leviticus 26:11–12"> In all of my going about among all the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>did I speak a word with one of the tribes of Israel whom I commanded to shepherd my people Israel, saying, ‘Why did you not build me a cedar house?’ ” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 6:21; 2 Samuel 12:7"> So then, thus you shall say to my servant David, ‘Thus says Yahweh of hosts, “I took you from the pasture ⸤from following⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from behind”">e</a></sup>the sheep to be a leader over my people, over Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:10; 1 Chronicles 17:8; 1 Samuel 18:14"> and I have been with you ⸤everywhere you went⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “in all that you went”">f</a></sup>I have cut off all of your enemies from in front of you, and I will make a great name for you, as the name of the great ones who are on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 9:15; 1 Chronicles 17:9; Jeremiah 24:6"> I will make a place for my people Israel, and I will plant them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">g</a></sup>so that they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">h</a></sup>can dwell ⸤in their own place⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “under him”">i</a></sup>They will not tremble any longer, and the children of wickedness will not afflict them again, as in the former days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:27; 2 Samuel 7:1; 1 Chronicles 17:10"> ⸤In the manner that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “And as far as” or “And from the day”">j</a></sup>I appointed judges over my people Israel, I will give you rest from all your enemies. And Yahweh declares to you that Yahweh will build a house for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; Acts 2:30; Acts 13:36"> When your days are full and you lie down with your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “fathers”">k</a></sup>I will raise up your offspring after you who will go out from your body, and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:31–33; 1 Kings 8:19; 2 Samuel 7:16"> He will build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Revelation 3:19; 1 Chronicles 17:13"> I will be a father to him, and he will be a son for me, whom I will punish when he does wrong, with a rod of men and with blows of the ⸤human beings⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “children of men”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:23; 1 Samuel 15:28; Psalm 89:28"> But my loyal love shall not depart from him as I took it from Saul, whom I removed from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Psalm 89:36–37; 2 Samuel 7:13"> Your house and your kingdom shall endure forever before you; your throne shall be established forever.” ’ ”</span>

## David Responds to Yahweh’s Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:15; Acts 20:27; 1 Corinthians 15:3"> According to all these words and according to all this vision, this Nathan spoke to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 8:4; 1 Chronicles 17:16; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Then King David went and sat before Yahweh and said, “Who am I, my lord Yahweh, and what is my house that you have brought me up to ⸤this place⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “here”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 55:8–9; 1 Chronicles 17:17; Psalm 36:7"> Still, this was ⸤insignificant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “small”">n</a></sup>in your eyes, my lord Yahweh, and also you have spoken about the house of your servant from afar, and this may be the teaching of humans, my lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 16:7; Psalm 139:1"> ⸤What more can David say to you⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “And what can David still add to say”">o</a></sup>You know your servant, my lord Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 115:1; Psalm 138:2; Ephesians 1:9"> Because of your word and according to your heart, you have done all of this great thing, in order to let your servant know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 86:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalm 86:8"> Therefore you are great, my lord Yahweh, for there is no one like you, and there is no god except you, in all that we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:26; Deuteronomy 10:21; Deuteronomy 15:15"> Who is like your people, like Israel? Israel is the one nation on earth whose God ⸤led them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “went”; LXX reads “led”">p</a></sup>in order to redeem a people for himself, and to make a name for himself, and to do for you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="David switches pronouns from the third person to the second person">q</a></sup>the great and awesome things for your land in the presence of your people whom he redeemed for himself from Egypt, from the nations and their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:18; 1 Peter 2:10; Genesis 17:7"> You have established your people Israel for yourself as a people forever, and you, O Yahweh, have become their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 119:49; Ezekiel 36:37; Genesis 32:12"> So then, Yahweh God, the word that you have spoken concerning your servant and concerning his house, confirm it forever, and do just as you have promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:23–24; Psalm 72:18–19; Matthew 6:9"> Your name shall be great forever, ⸤and they will say⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “saying”">r</a></sup>‘Yahweh of hosts is God over Israel’; and the house of your servant David shall be established before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:25–26; Psalm 10:17; Ruth 4:4"> For you, O Yahweh of hosts, are God of Israel! You have revealed to the ear of your servant, ‘I will build a house for you’; therefore your servant ⸤has found courage⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “has found his heart”">s</a></sup>to pray this prayer to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 17:17; Exodus 34:6; Numbers 23:19"> Now, my Lord Yahweh, you alone are God, and your words are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “will be”">t</a></sup>true. You have promised this good to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 6:24–26; 1 Chronicles 17:27; Psalm 115:12–15"> So then, be content and bless the house of your servant to be forever in your presence, for you, my Lord Yahweh, have spoken, and because of your blessing, may the house of your servant be blessed forever.”</span>
</div>
