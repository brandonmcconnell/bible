## The House of David Grows Stronger

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 14:30; Proverbs 4:18–19; Job 8:7"> The battle was prolonged between the house of Saul and the house of David, but David ⸤was growing stronger and stronger⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “was going and strong”">a</a></sup>while the house of Saul ⸤was becoming weaker and weaker⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “was going and weak”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:1–4; Genesis 49:3–4; 2 Samuel 13:1–29"> And sons were born to David in Hebron; his firstborn was Amnon by Ahinoam ⸤from Jezreel⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the Jezreelitess”">c</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:8; 1 Chronicles 3:1; Deuteronomy 3:14"> His second was Kileab by Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite; the third was Absalom the son of Maacah, who was the daughter of Talmai the king of Geshur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:13–25; 1 Kings 1:5–18"> The fourth was Adonijah the son of Haggith; the fifth was Shephatiah the son of Abital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The sixth was Ithream by Eglah the wife of David. These were born to David in Hebron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:8–11; 1 Kings 2:21–22; 2 Samuel 12:8"> Saul had had a concubine, and her name was Rizpah the daughter of Aiah. Then Ish-Bosheth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Supported by some Hebrew and Greek manuscripts">d</a></sup>said to Abner, “Why ⸤did you have sex with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “have you gone into”">e</a></sup>my father’s concubine?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:8; 2 Samuel 16:9; Mark 6:18–19"> ⸤Abner became very angry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “And it became hot for Abner very”">f</a></sup>at the words of Ish-Bosheth, and he said, “Am I the head of a dog which is for Judah today? Do I not continue to show loyal love with the house of Saul your father, to his brothers, and to his friends? I have not let you fall into the hands of David, yet you have ⸤accused me of sin with this woman⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “you have called me into account with the guilt of the woman”">g</a></sup>today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; 1 Kings 19:2; Ruth 1:17"> Thus ⸤may God punish Abner⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “may God do to Abner, and thus may he add to it”">h</a></sup>⸤if I do not accomplish what Yahweh has sworn to David⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for as that Yahweh has sworn to David, so I will accomplish for him”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 1 Samuel 3:20; 1 Kings 4:25"> specifically, to transfer the kingdom from the house of Saul and to establish the throne of David over Israel and over Judah from Dan to Beersheba!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Ish-Bosheth was no longer able to ⸤answer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “return a word to”">j</a></sup>Abner ⸤because he feared him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “because of his fearing him”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Abner Pledges Support for David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:38–40; 2 Samuel 3:21; Luke 16:5–8"> Abner sent messengers to David ⸤where he was⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “under him”">l</a></sup>saying, “To whom does the land belong? ⸤Make your covenant with me⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “cut your covenant with me”">m</a></sup>Look, my hand is with you to bring all of Israel over to you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 43:3; 1 Samuel 19:11–17; Genesis 44:23"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="David">n</a></sup>said, “Good. ⸤I will make a covenant with you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “I will cut a covenant with you”">o</a></sup>I am asking only one thing from you: You shall not see my face unless you bring Michal the daughter of Saul when you come to see ⸤me⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “my face”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:27; 1 Samuel 18:25; 2 Samuel 2:10"> Then David sent messengers to Ish-Bosheth the son of Saul, saying, “Give me my wife Michal whom I betrothed to myself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “me”">q</a></sup>for a hundred foreskins of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:44"> So Ish-Bosheth sent and took her from her husband, from Paltiel the son of Laish.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="The Hebrew Masoretic text (Kethib ) reads “Lush”; Qere reads “Laish”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:5; 2 Samuel 19:16; 1 Kings 2:8"> But her husband went with her, ⸤weeping all along⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “going and weeping”">s</a></sup>after her as far as Bahurim. Then Abner said to him, “Go and return.” So he returned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The word of Abner ⸤came to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “was with”">t</a></sup>the elders of Israel, saying, “⸤For quite some time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “Both yesterday and the day before”">u</a></sup>you were seeking David as king over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; Psalm 89:19–23; 1 Samuel 9:16"> So then, bring it about, because Yahweh had said to David, “Through the hand of David my servant I am about to save my people Israel from the hand of the Philistines and from the hand of all their enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:29; 1 Samuel 10:20–21; Psalm 68:27"> Abner also spoke ⸤privately to Benjamin⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “in the ears of Benjamin”">v</a></sup>Abner also went ⸤to speak to David⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “to speak in the ears of David”">w</a></sup>in Hebron, all that was good in the eyes of Israel and in the eyes of all the house of Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 26:30; Esther 1:3"> Then Abner came to David in Hebron and with him were twenty men. David had prepared a feast for Abner and for the men who were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:12; 1 Kings 11:37; 2 Samuel 3:10"> And Abner said to David, “Let me get up and go and gather all of Israel to my lord the king, that they may ⸤make a covenant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “cut a covenant”">x</a></sup>with you so you can reign over all which your soul desires.” So David dismissed Abner, and he left in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And look, the servants of David and Joab came from the raid, and they brought much plunder with them. But Abner was not with David at Hebron, for he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="David">y</a></sup>had dismissed him, and he had gone in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> When Joab and all the army that was with him came, they told Joab, “Abner the son of Ner came to the king, and he sent him away, and he left in peace.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joab Assassinates Abner

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:8; 2 Samuel 19:5–7; John 18:35"> Then Joab came to the king and said, “What have you done? Abner came here to you? Why have you dismissed him that he ⸤actually went away⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “and he went going”">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:6; Isaiah 37:28; Numbers 27:17"> You know that Abner the son of Ner came to deceive you. He came to learn about your going out and coming in and to know all which you are doing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 26:23–26; Proverbs 27:4–6"> Then Joab went out from David, and he sent messengers after Abner, and they brought him back from Bor Hasirah, but David did not know it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 2:5; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; 1 Kings 2:32"> When Abner returned to Hebron, Joab took him aside to the middle of the gate to speak with him in private. Then he struck him in the stomach there and he died, ⸤for he had shed the blood of Asahel his brother⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “for the blood of Asahel his brother.” See Num 35:16–25">aa</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Exodus 21:12; Genesis 9:6"> When David heard this afterwards, he said: “I and my kingdom are innocent ⸤before Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “from with Yahweh”">ab</a></sup>forever for the blood of Abner the son of Ner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Psalm 109:8–19; Leviticus 13:44–46"> May the blood come down on the head of Joab and all the house of his father. ⸤May the house of Joab never lack⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “May there not be cut off from the house of Joab”">ac</a></sup>one with a bodily discharge or one with leprosy or one who grasps the distaff or one who falls by the sword or one who is lacking food.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 28:17; Acts 28:4; 2 Samuel 2:19–23"> So Joab and Abishai, his brother, killed Abner because he had killed Asahel, their brother, at Gibeon in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; 2 Samuel 1:11; 2 Samuel 1:2"> David said to Joab and to all the people who were with him, “Tear your clothing and put on sackcloth and mourn before Abner.” Now King David was following after the bier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Job 31:28; 1 Samuel 30:4"> And they buried Abner at Hebron. And the king lifted up his voice and wept at the grave of Abner, and all the people wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:17; 2 Chronicles 35:25; Luke 12:19–20"> The king sang a lament for Abner and said, “Should Abner have died the death of a fool?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 16:21; 2 Samuel 1:12; Job 24:14"> Your hands were not tied and your feet were not in contact with bronze fetters. You have fallen as one who falls before sons of wickedness.” Then ⸤all the people wept over him again⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “and all the people did again to weep over him”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:17; 2 Samuel 1:12; Ruth 1:17"> Then all the people came to give David food. Still on that day, David swore, “⸤May God punish me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “Thus may God do to me and thus may he add”">ae</a></sup>if I taste food or anything before the sun goes down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 7:37; Psalm 62:9; Mark 15:11–13"> All the people noticed, and it was good in their eyes, as everything that the king did was good in the eyes of all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Then all the people and all of Israel realized on that day that ⸤the king had not desired⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “that it was not from the king”">af</a></sup>to kill Abner the son of Ner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Job 32:9; 2 Samuel 2:8; 2 Samuel 3:12"> Then the king said to his servants, “Did you not realize that a prince and a great man has fallen today in Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 2:33–34; Psalm 101:8; Psalm 28:4"> I am weak today even though anointed king, and these men, the sons of Zeruiah, are crueler than I am. May Yahweh pay them back for doing wickedness according to their own wickedness.”</span>
</div>
