## Hushai Frustrates the Counsel of Ahithophel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7–8; Psalm 109:2–4; Psalm 3:3–5"> Then Ahithophel said to Absalom, “Please let me choose twelve thousand men, and I will set out and pursue after David ⸤tonight⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the night”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:14; 1 Kings 22:31; Zechariah 13:7"> I will come upon him while he is weary and ⸤weak⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “slack of hands”">b</a></sup>I will startle him, and all of the people who are with him will flee. Then I will strike down the king while he is alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 48:22; Isaiah 57:21"> Then I will return all the people to you; when all have returned, the man whom you are seeking will be dead,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="According to the MT; the LXX has “and I will return all the people to you the way a bride returns to her husband, except the life of the one man whom you are seeking”">c</a></sup>but all the people will be safe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:20–21; Romans 1:32; 2 Chronicles 30:4"> The word was right in the eyes of Absalom and in the eyes of all the elders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:32–37; 2 Samuel 16:16–19"> Then Absalom said, “Please call also for Hushai the Arkite, and let us hear ⸤what he has to say⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “what is in his mouth”">d</a></sup>also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So Hushai came to Absalom, who said to him, “Shall we do according to this word that Ahithophel has spoken? If not, then you tell us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 31:8"> Then Hushai said to Absalom, “The counsel that Ahithophel gave is not good ⸤at this time⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “at this occurrence”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 13:8; 1 Samuel 16:18; Hebrews 11:32–34"> Hushai continued, “You know your father and his men, that they are mighty warriors and they ⸤are enraged⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “are bitter of soul”">f</a></sup>as a bear robbed of her offspring in the field. Your father is a man of war, so he will not spend the night with the troops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:3; 1 Samuel 14:14–15; Judges 20:32–33"> Now he has hidden himself in one of the caves or in one of the places. At the moment he falls on them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="I.e., Absalom’s troops">g</a></sup>the first time, ⸤whoever hears⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “and the one who hears hears”">h</a></sup>the report will say, ‘There has been a defeat among the people who follow after Absalom.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 2:9–11; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 49:9"> And he is also a ⸤valiant warrior⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “son of ability”">i</a></sup>whose heart is like the heart of the lion. He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="i.e., one of Absalom’s troops">j</a></sup>will ⸤certainly melt⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “melting he will melt”">k</a></sup>with fear, for all Israel knows that your father is a mighty warrior and those who are with him are ⸤valiant warriors⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “son of ability”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Judges 20:1; Psalm 9:16"> I give the advice that all of Israel from Dan to Beersheba should be completely gathered to you, as the sand which is on the seashore for abundance, with ⸤you personally⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “your face”">m</a></sup>going into the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:23; Obadiah 1:3; 1 Kings 20:10"> Then we will come to him in one of the places where he may be found, and we shall come upon him as the dew falls on the ground. He and all the men who are with him will not survive, not even one!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Micah 1:6"> Even if he withdraws to a city, all Israel will bring up ropes to that city, and they will drag him away to the valley until there is not even a pebble to be found.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:34; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; Proverbs 19:21"> Then Absalom and all the men of Israel said, “The advice of Hushai the Arkite is better than the advice of Ahithophel.” (Now Yahweh had ordained to frustrate the good counsel of Ahithophel in order for Yahweh to bring misery upon Absalom).</span>
</div>

## Hushai Sends Word to David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:35–36"> Hushai said to Zadok and to Abiathar the priests, “⸤Thus and so⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “so this and so this”">n</a></sup>Ahithophel advised Absalom and the elders of Israel, but ⸤thus and so⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “so this and so this”">o</a></sup>I have advised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:28; 1 Corinthians 15:54; 2 Corinthians 5:4"> So then, send quickly and tell David, ‘Don’t spend the night at the fords of the wilderness! Moreover, by all means cross over lest the king and all the people who are with him be swallowed up.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; Joshua 18:16; 2 Samuel 15:27"> Now Jonathan and Ahimaaz were staying at En Rogel, so a servant girl used to go and tell them, then they would go and tell King David, for they were not able to be seen coming to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; 2 Samuel 16:5; 2 Samuel 19:16"> But a young man saw them and told Absalom, so both of them went quickly and came to the house of a man at Bahurim. Now he had a well in his courtyard, so they went down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 1:19; Joshua 2:4–24"> Then the woman took and spread a covering over the opening of the well; then she spread out dried grain on it, so nothing was discovered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 1:19; Joshua 2:22–23; 1 Samuel 19:12–17"> When the servants of Absalom came to the woman at the house, they said, “Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?” And she said to them, “They crossed over the brook of water.” So they searched but could not find them, so they returned to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:15–16"> After they left, they came up from the well and went and told King David. They said to David, “Set out and cross over the water quickly, for thus Ahithophel has advised against you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; John 18:9; Proverbs 27:12"> So David and all the people who were with him set out, and they crossed over the Jordan until morning light until ⸤there was no one⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “until one was not missing”">p</a></sup>missing who had not crossed over the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:5; 2 Kings 20:1; 2 Samuel 15:12"> When Ahithophel saw that his advice was not followed, he saddled the donkey, and he set out and went up to his house in his city. ⸤After he set his house in order⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “And he commanded to his household”">q</a></sup>he hanged himself, and he died and was buried in the tomb of his ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “fathers”">r</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 32:2; 2 Samuel 2:8; Joshua 13:26"> Now David had come to Mahanaim, and Absalom had crossed over the Jordan, he and all the men of Israel with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 2 Samuel 19:13; 2 Samuel 20:9–12"> Absalom had appointed Amasa in place of Joab over the army. Now Amasa was the son of a man whose name was Ithra the Israelite, ⸤who had married⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “who went to”">s</a></sup>Abigail the daughter of Nahash the sister of Zeruiah, the mother of Joab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Deuteronomy 3:15; Numbers 32:1–42"> Israel and Absalom camped in the land of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 2:7; 2 Samuel 10:1–2; 2 Samuel 9:4"> Just as David had arrived in Mahanaim, Shobi the son of Nahash from Rabbah of the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">t</a></sup>and Makir the son of Ammiel from Lo Debar and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; Obadiah 1:3; 2 Samuel 16:1–2"> brought beds and basins and objects of pottery, as well as wheat, barley, flour, roasted grain, beans, lentils,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:2; Isaiah 21:14; 2 Samuel 16:14"> honey, curds, sheep, cheese, and cattle for David and for the people who were with him to eat. For they had thought, “The troops are hungry and weary and thirsty in the wilderness.”</span>
</div>
