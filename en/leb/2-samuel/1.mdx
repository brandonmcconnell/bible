## The Report of Saul’s Death by the Amalekite

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:6; 1 Samuel 31:6; 1 Samuel 30:17–26"> ⸤After⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “And it happened after”">a</a></sup>the death of Saul, David returned from defeating the Amalekites and he stayed at Ziklag two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:12; 2 Samuel 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> On the third day, a man came from the camp from being with Saul, with his clothes torn and with dirt on his head. ⸤When he came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “And it happened at his coming”">b</a></sup>to David, he fell to the ground and bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; 2 Kings 5:25"> David said to him, “Where did you come from?” He said to him, “I have escaped from the camp of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 31:1–6; 1 Chronicles 10:1–6"> Then David said to him, “⸤How did things go⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “What was the thing”">c</a></sup>Please tell me.” He answered, “⸤When⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “That”">d</a></sup>the army fled from the battle, and many of the people fell; also, Saul and Jonathan his son died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> Then David asked the young man who was reporting to him, “How do you know that Saul and his son Jonathan died?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21; 1 Chronicles 10:4–6"> The young man who was reporting to him said, “I merely happened to be on Mount Gilboa. Here Saul was leaning on his spear, and look, the chariots and the horsemen were getting close to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:12; 2 Samuel 9:6; Judges 9:54"> When he turned around and saw me, he called to me, and I said, ‘Here I am.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Samuel 30:1"> Then he said to me, ‘Who are you?’ And I said to him, ‘I am an Amalekite.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He said to me, ‘Please stand over me and kill me, for convulsions have seized me, even though my life is still in me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; 2 Kings 11:12"> So I stood over him and killed him, for I knew that he could not live after his falling; I took the crown that was on his head and the bracelet which was on his arm; and here, I have brought them to my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31"> David grabbed at his clothes and tore them, as did all of the men who were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalm 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> Then they mourned and wept and fasted over Saul and Jonathan his son until the evening, as well as over the people of Yahweh and over the house of Israel because they had fallen by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:8"> Then David said to the young man who was reporting to him, “Where are you from?” And he said, “I am the son of an alien man. I am an Amalekite.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Samuel 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> David said to him, “How is it that you were not afraid to stretch out your hand to destroy Yahweh’s anointed one?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 2:34; Judges 8:20; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> Then David called to one of the young men and said to him, “Come near; strike him.” So he struck him down and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> David said to him, “Your blood is on your head, for your mouth has testified against you by saying, ‘I killed Yahweh’s anointed one!’ ”</span>
</div>

## David Laments Jonathan with the “Song of the Bow”

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; Genesis 49:8; Deuteronomy 4:10"> And he ordered “The Bow” to be taught to the children of Judah. Look, it is written on the scroll of Jashar.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The Book of Jashar (“the upright”) is also mentioned in Josh 10:13.">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:6; Judges 11:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not tell it in Gath; do not proclaim it in the streets of Ashkelon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lest the daughters of the Philistines rejoice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lest the daughters of the uncircumcised exult.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Isaiah 21:5; 1 Samuel 10:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">O mountains of Gilboa, let there be no dew or rain upon you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or on the fields of grain for offerings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for there the small shield of the mighty was defiled,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the small shield of Saul was not anointed with oil.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 34:6–7; 1 Samuel 14:6–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the blood of the slain, from the fat of the mighty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the bow of Jonathan did not turn back,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the sword of Saul did not return ⸤without effect⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “empty handedly”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Saul and Jonathan were beloved and pleasant in their lives</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and were not separated in their death.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They were swifter than eagles, stronger than lions.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">O daughters of Israel, weep over Saul,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the one who clothed you with crimson,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the one who ⸤adorned your clothing with golden ornaments⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “put gold ornaments on your clothing”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:19; 2 Samuel 1:27; Judges 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">How the mighty have fallen in the midst of the battle;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Jonathan lies slain on your high places.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; 1 Samuel 23:16; 1 Samuel 20:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">⸤I am distressed⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “Distress is for me”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">over you, my brother Jonathan.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you were very dear to me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your love was more wonderful to me than the love of women.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">How the mighty have fallen and the weapons of warfare perished.”</span></span>
