## Joab Plots to Reconcile David with Absalom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:39; 2 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 18:33"> Joab the son of Zeruiah realized that the mind of the king was on Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:6; Amos 1:1; Ruth 3:3"> So Joab sent to Tekoa and took from there a wise woman, and he said to her, “Please pretend to mourn and put on garments of mourning. You should not anoint yourself with oil, and you must act like this woman who has been mourning over the dead for ⸤a long time⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “many days”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:19; Exodus 4:15; Isaiah 51:16"> Then you must go to the king and speak to him according to this word.” Thus Joab put the words in her mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:2; 2 Kings 6:26–28; 1 Samuel 25:23"> So the Tekoite woman spoke to the king, and she fell on her face to the ground and did obeisance. She said, “Help me, O king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Samuel 12:1–3"> Then the king asked her, “⸤What do you want⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “What is for you”">b</a></sup>And she said, “Truly I am a widow, and my husband is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Deuteronomy 22:26–27; Exodus 2:13"> Your servant had two sons, and they both fought in the open field, and there was no one ⸤to part them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to save between them”">c</a></sup>One struck the other and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Matthew 21:38; 2 Samuel 12:17"> And look, all of the family has risen up against your servant, and they said, ‘Give up the one who struck his brother, that we may kill him in exchange for the life of his brother whom he murdered. We will also wipe out the heir,’ and so they would put out my embers which remain, by not preserving for my husband a name and a remnant on the face of the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Isaiah 11:3–4; Job 29:16"> Then the king said to the woman, “Go to your house, and I myself will give the command concerning you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:24; 1 Kings 2:33; Matthew 27:25"> The Tekoite woman said to the king, “On me, my lord the king, is the guilt, and on the house of my father, but the king on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">d</a></sup>his throne is innocent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The king said, “Whoever has spoken to you, bring him to me, and he will not touch you again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:45; Matthew 10:30; Numbers 35:19"> Then she said, “Please may the king remember Yahweh your God, ⸤to prevent the increase of blood avengers who kill⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from making numerous the avenger of blood to kill”">e</a></sup>so that they not wipe out my son.” He said, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">f</a></sup>surely not one hair shall fall from your son to the ground.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 18:27; 1 Samuel 25:24; Acts 26:1"> The woman said, “Please let your servant speak a word to my lord the king.” And he said, “Speak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:37–38; 2 Samuel 12:7; Judges 20:2"> The woman said, “But why have you plotted like this against the people of God? By speaking this word, he is guilty not to bring back his banished one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 30:23; Numbers 35:28; Hebrews 9:27"> For ⸤we must certainly die⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “dying we must die”">g</a></sup>and we are as the waters spilled to the ground which cannot be gathered. God will not take a life but devises plans for a banished person not to be cast out from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Now I have come to speak this word to my lord the king, because the people made me afraid, and your servant thought, ‘I will speak to the king, perhaps the king will grant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “do/make”">h</a></sup>the request of his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:19"> For the king will listen, to deliver his servant from the hand of the man who seeks to destroy me and my son together from the inheritance of God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:9; 2 Samuel 19:27; 2 Samuel 14:20"> Your servant also thought, ‘May the word of my lord the king ⸤bring rest⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “become a rest”">i</a></sup>for as an angel of God, so is my lord the king, ⸤to sense what is good and what is bad⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to hear the good and the bad”">j</a></sup>May Yahweh your God be with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:25; 1 Samuel 3:17–18; Jeremiah 38:14"> The king answered and said to the woman, “Please do not withhold from me a thing which I am about to ask you.” The woman said, “Please let my lord the king speak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:3; 1 Samuel 1:26; 1 Samuel 20:3"> The king asked, “Was the hand of Joab with you in all of this?” The woman answered and said, “⸤As your soul lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “The life of your soul”">k</a></sup>my lord the king, surely one cannot go to the right or to the left from all that my lord the king has spoken. Yes, your servant Joab himself commanded me, and he put all of these words in the mouth of your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:17; 2 Samuel 19:27; Job 32:21–22"> In order ⸤to change the situation⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to turn the face of the thing”">l</a></sup>your servant Joab did this thing. But my lord has wisdom, as the wisdom of an angel of God, to know all that is on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:11; 1 Samuel 14:39; Mark 6:26"> Then the king said to Joab, “Look, please, I will grant this thing. Go and bring back the young man Absalom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ruth 2:2; Job 31:20; Job 29:11"> Joab fell with his face to the ground and did obeisance. And he blessed the king, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “Joab”">m</a></sup>said, “Today your servant knows that I have found favor in your eyes, my lord the king, in that the king has granted the request of his servant.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Absalom Returns to Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:3; 2 Samuel 13:37–38"> Then Joab got up and went to Geshur and brought Absalom to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 22:4; Genesis 43:3; 2 Samuel 3:13"> The king said, “Let him go over to his house, and he may not see my face.” So Absalom went over to his house, and did not see the face of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:35; Job 2:7"> As far as Absalom, there was not a more handsome man in all of Israel to admire so much; from the sole of his foot up to his crown, there was no physical defect on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:20; 1 Corinthians 11:14; 2 Samuel 18:9"> When he shaved his head, it would happen ⸤every year⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “from the end of days for the days”">n</a></sup>which he did because it was heavy on him, he would shave it off and weigh the hair of his head: two hundred shekels ⸤by the king’s weight⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “by the stone of the king”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:18; 2 Samuel 13:1; Isaiah 14:22"> Three sons were born to Absalom and one daughter, whose name was Tamar. She was a woman beautiful of appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Absalom lived in Jerusalem ⸤two full years⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “two years of days”">p</a></sup>but he did not see the face of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:30–31; Esther 1:12; Matthew 22:3"> So Absalom sent for Joab, in order that he send him to the king, but he was not willing to go to him. He sent again a second time, but he was not willing to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 15:4–5; 2 Kings 10:6–7; 2 Kings 9:33"> So he said to his servants, “Look at the tract of land of Joab ⸤next to mine⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “toward my hand”">q</a></sup>for he has barley plants there. Go, set it ablaze with fire.” So the servants of Absalom set the tract of land ablaze with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then Joab got up and went to Absalom, to the house, and said to him, “Why have your servants set my tract of land ablaze with fire?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:8; Exodus 14:12; Proverbs 28:13"> Absalom said to Joab, “Look, I have sent to you, saying, ‘Come here that I may send you to the king to say, “Why have I come from Geshur? It would be better for me if I were still there.” ’ So then, let me see the face of the king; if there is guilt in me, then let him kill me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Luke 15:20; Genesis 27:26"> So Joab went to the king and he told him. Then he summoned Absalom, and he came to the king, and he bowed down to him with his face to the ground before the king. Then the king kissed Absalom.</span>
</div>
</div>
