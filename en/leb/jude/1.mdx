## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 1:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Peter 2:9"> Jude,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Jude” is the traditional form of the name introducing the letter, but the Greek form is the same as “Judas”">a</a></sup>a slave of Jesus Christ and brother of James, to those who are called, loved in God the Father and kept for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or perhaps “by”">b</a></sup>Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2; Revelation 1:4–6"> May mercy and peace and love be multiplied to you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Contend for the Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; Philippians 1:27; Jude 1:20"> Dear friends, although I was making every effort to write to you concerning our common salvation, I considered it a necessity to write to you to encourage you to contend for the faith delivered once and for all to the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; Galatians 2:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> For certain men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “people,” since the Greek term can be used in a generic sense, but if this statement is related to 2 Peter 2:12, it is more likely men are in view">c</a></sup>have slipped in stealthily, who were designated long ago for this condemnation, ungodly ones, who change the grace of our God into licentiousness and who deny our only Master and Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Condemnation of the Ungodly False Teachers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Hebrews 3:16–2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–12"> Now I want to remind you, although you know everything once and for all,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts place this word in the following clause">d</a></sup>that Jesus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “the Lord”">e</a></sup>having saved the people out of the land of Egypt, the second time destroyed those who did not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 25:41; Ephesians 6:12"> And the angels who did not keep to their own domain but deserted their proper dwelling place, he has kept in eternal bonds under deep gloom for the judgment of the great day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; 2 Peter 2:6; Romans 1:26–27"> as Sodom and Gomorrah and the towns around them indulged in sexual immorality and ⸤pursued unnatural desire⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “went after other flesh”">f</a></sup>in the same way as these, are exhibited as an example by undergoing the punishment of eternal fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Peter 2:10–12"> Despite that, in the same way also these men, because of their dreams, defile the flesh and reject authority and blaspheme majestic beings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 3:2; 2 Peter 2:11; Deuteronomy 34:6"> But Michael the archangel, when he argued with the devil, disputing concerning the body of Moses, did not dare to pronounce a blasphemous judgment, but said, “The Lord rebuke you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:12; Romans 1:21–22"> But these persons blaspheme all that they do not understand, and all that they understand by instinct like the irrational animals, by these things they are being destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:15; 1 John 3:12; Revelation 2:14"> Woe to them! For they have traveled in the way of Cain, and have given themselves up to the error of Balaam for gain, and have perished<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or possibly “will perish”">g</a></sup>in the rebellion of Korah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17–20; Matthew 15:13; Ezekiel 34:8"> These are the ones feasting together without reverence, hidden reefs at your love feasts, caring for themselves, waterless clouds carried away by winds, late autumn trees without fruit, twice dead, uprooted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17; Philippians 3:19; Isaiah 57:20"> wild waves of the sea foaming up their own shameful deeds, wandering stars, for whom the deep gloom of darkness has been reserved for eternity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalm 50:3–5; Hebrews 11:5–6"> And Enoch, the seventh from Adam, also prophesied about<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “against”">h</a></sup>these people, saying, “Behold, the Lord came with tens of thousands of his holy ones</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:16; Ecclesiastes 11:9; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> to execute judgment against all, and to convict all the ungodly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “to convict every soul”">i</a></sup>concerning all their ungodly deeds that they have committed in an ungodly way, and concerning all the harsh things that ungodly sinners have spoken against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18; 2 Peter 2:10; Philippians 2:14"> These people are grumblers, discontented, proceeding according to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “their own”">j</a></sup>desires, and their mouths speaking pompous words, ⸤showing partiality to gain an advantage⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “respecting faces for the sake of advantage”">k</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Persevere in the Love of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:2; 1 John 4:6; Ephesians 4:11"> But you, dear friends, remember the words proclaimed beforehand by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:1–5"> for they said to you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “to you that”">l</a></sup>“In the end time there will be scoffers following according to their own ungodly desires.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Proverbs 18:1; Romans 8:9"> These are the ones who cause divisions, worldly, not having the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Romans 8:26–27; 1 Corinthians 14:15"> But you, dear friends, by building yourselves up in your most holy faith, by praying in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Lamentations 3:25–26; Hebrews 9:28"> keep yourselves in the love of God, looking forward to the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ to eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Galatians 6:1; 1 John 5:16–18"> And have mercy on those who doubt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Revelation 3:4"> and save others by snatching them from the fire, and have mercy on others with fear, hating even the tunic stained by the flesh.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Benediction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 1:22"> Now to the one who is able to protect you from stumbling and make you to stand before his glory blameless<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “without blemish”">m</a></sup>with exultation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Revelation 5:13–14; 1 Peter 5:10–11"> to the only God our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory, majesty, power, and authority before all time and now and for all eternity. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
