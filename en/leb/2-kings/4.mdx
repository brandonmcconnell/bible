## Elisha Answers a Widow’s Request

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 18:25; 2 Kings 2:3; Nehemiah 5:2–5"> A certain woman from the wives of the sons of the prophets cried out to Elisha, saying, “Your servant my husband is dead. Now you know that your servant was a fearer of Yahweh, but the creditor came to take two of my children for himself as slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 17:12; James 2:5; 2 Kings 6:26–27"> Elisha asked her, “What shall I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?” Then she said, “Your servant has nothing in the house except a jar of olive oil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 2:7; Psalm 81:10; John 16:24"> Then he said to her, “Go, ask for yourself some containers from the streets, from all your neighbors. ⸤You must collect as many empty containers as you can⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “You must not collect only a few empty containers”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 8:5–9; Matthew 6:6; Ephesians 3:20"> You must also go and shut the door behind you and your children, and you must pour out oil into all of these containers and set the filled ones aside.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 5:11; Hebrews 11:7–8; 1 Kings 17:15–16"> So she went from him, and she shut the door behind her and her children. They were bringing containers to her, and she kept pouring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 14:20; 2 Kings 13:19; Luke 6:19"> It happened that when the containers were full, she said to her son, “Bring near me another container,” but he said to her, “There is not another container.” Then the olive oil stopped flowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–12; 1 Kings 12:22; Romans 12:17"> So she came and told the man of God, and he said, “Go, sell the olive oil and repay your debt. You and your sons can live on what is left over.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elisha at Shunem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Genesis 19:3; 2 Kings 4:11–12"> It happened one day that Elisha passed through to Shunem where there was a wealthy woman, and she urged him to eat bread; so it happened each time he passed through, he would stop there to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 17:24; Proverbs 31:10–11; 2 Kings 4:7"> She said to her husband, “Please now, I know that he is a holy man of God who is passing ⸤our way⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “upon us”">b</a></sup>regularly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:13; Matthew 10:41–42; Matthew 25:40"> let us make a small enclosed room upstairs and put a bed, table, chair, and lampstand there for him, so that when he comes to us, he can turn and stay there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> One day it happened that he came there and went to the upper room and lay down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 8:4–5; 2 Kings 3:11; 2 Kings 4:29–31"> He said to Gehazi his servant, “Call to this Shunammite,” so he called to her, and she stood before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:16–18; Philippians 4:18–19; Hebrews 6:10"> He said to him, “Please say to her, ‘Look, you took all this trouble, showing care for us; what is there for me to do for you? To speak for you to the king or to the commander of the army?’ ” She said, “I am living among my people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:7; Genesis 15:2–3; 1 Samuel 1:8"> Then he said, “What may be done for her?” Gehazi said, “Well, she has no son, and her husband is old.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he said, “Call for her,” so he called for her and she stood in the doorway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 18:10; Luke 1:13; 2 Kings 4:28"> And he said, “At this time ⸤next spring⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “about that season as the time of life”">c</a></sup>you will be embracing a son.” She said, “No, my lord, O man of God! You must not tell a lie to your servant!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 113:9; Luke 1:36; Genesis 21:1"> But the woman conceived, and she bore a son ⸤in the spring⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “about that season as the time of life”">d</a></sup>which Elisha had promised to her.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elisha Restores the Shunammite’s Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:4"> The child grew older, and it happened one day that he went out to his father and to the reapers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 14:1–2; Jeremiah 4:19"> Then he said to his father, “My head, my head!” So he said to the servant, “Carry him to his mother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:12; John 11:3; John 11:14"> So they carried him and brought him to his mother; he sat on her lap until noon and then died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 4:32; 2 Kings 4:10; 2 Kings 4:7"> She went up, laid him on the bed of the man of God, closed the door, and went out behind it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:38; 2 Kings 4:26; John 11:3"> She called to her husband and said, “Please send one of the servants and one of the female donkeys for me, so that I can go quickly up to the man of God and return.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Numbers 28:11; 1 Chronicles 23:31"> And he said, “Why are you going to him today? It is neither the new moon nor the Sabbath!” And she said, “Peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 4:20; 1 Kings 13:13; 1 Samuel 25:19–20"> She saddled the female donkey, and she said to her servant, “Drive along and go; you must not hold me back from riding, unless I tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 2:25; Isaiah 35:2; 1 Kings 18:19"> So she went and came to the man of God by Mount Carmel. It happened when the man of God saw her ⸤at a distance⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from opposite”">e</a></sup>he said to Gehazi his servant, “There is this Shunammite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 15:36; 2 Kings 4:23; 1 Samuel 17:18"> Now, please run to meet her and ask her, ‘Is it peace for you? Is it peace for your husband? Is it peace for the boy?’ ” She said, “Peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:3; Genesis 18:17; Amos 3:7"> So she came to the man of God at the mountain, and she caught hold of his feet. Then Gehazi came near to push her away, but the man of God said, “Let her alone, for her soul is bitter, and Yahweh has hidden it from me and has not told me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 30:1; 2 Kings 4:16"> Then she said, “Did I ask for a son from my lord? Did I not say that you must not mislead me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 2:14; 1 Kings 18:46; Exodus 14:16"> Then he said to Gehazi, “Gird up your loins and take my staff in your hand and go. If you meet anyone, you must not greet them; if anyone greets you, you must not answer them. You must put my staff on the face of the boy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:16–18; 2 Kings 2:2; Exodus 33:12–16"> Then the mother of the boy said, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">f</a></sup>and ⸤as your soul lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “life of your soul”">g</a></sup>I will surely not leave you.” So he got up and went after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:11; Mark 5:39; Mark 9:19–29"> Gehazi crossed over before them, and he put the staff on the face of the boy; but there was no sound, and there was no sign of life, so he returned to meet him. He told him, saying, “The boy did not wake up.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 8:52–53; John 11:17; 1 Kings 17:17"> When Elisha came to the house, here was the boy dead, lying on his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; 2 Kings 4:4; Acts 9:40"> He went and closed the door behind the two of them and prayed to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 17:21; Acts 20:10"> Then he went up and lay upon the child and put his mouth on his mouth, his eyes on his eyes, and his palms on his palms. As he bent down over him, the flesh of the boy became warm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 8:5; 2 Kings 13:21; 1 Kings 17:22"> He returned and went ⸤to and fro⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “here first and here”">h</a></sup>in the house one time, then he went up and bent over him. Then the boy sneezed seven times and opened his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; 1 Kings 17:23; Luke 7:15"> Elisha called to Gehazi and said, “Call this Shunammite.” So he called her and she came to him; then he said, “Pick up your son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Hebrews 11:35; 2 Kings 2:15; 1 Kings 17:24"> She came and fell at his feet and bowed down to the ground; then she picked up her son and went out.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elisha Secures the Food

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 8:1; 2 Kings 2:3; 2 Kings 2:1"> So Elisha returned to Gilgal. Now the famine was in the land, and the sons of the prophets were sitting before him. He said to his servant, put on the large pot and cook a stew for the sons of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Isaiah 5:4; Matthew 15:13"> One went out to the field to gather herbs, and he found a ⸤wild vine⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a vine of the field”">i</a></sup>and gathered wild gourds from it and filled his cloak. Then he came and cut them into the pot of stew, but they did not know what they were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 16:18; Exodus 15:23; Exodus 10:17"> They served the men to eat, but when they ate from the stew, they cried out and said, “There is death in the pot, O man of God!” They were not able to eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Kings 2:21; Exodus 15:25; 1 Corinthians 1:25"> Then he said, “Bring some flour,” and he threw it into the pot. He then said, “Serve the people and let them eat.” There was nothing harmful in the pot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7; John 6:9; Matthew 14:16–21"> A man came from Baal-Shalishah and brought food to the man of God: firstfruits and twenty loaves of barley bread, with ripe grain in his sack. He said, “Give it to the people and let them eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 9:13; Matthew 14:20; John 6:9"> Then his servant said, “How can I set this before a hundred men?” He said, “Give it to the people and let them eat, for thus Yahweh says, ‘They shall eat and have some left over.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> So he set it before them, and they ate and had some left over, according to the word of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
</div>
