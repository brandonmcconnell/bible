## Elijah Taken Away from Elisha

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 5:24; Hebrews 11:5; 1 Kings 19:16–21"> When Yahweh was about to take Elijah up in the storm to heaven, Elijah and Elisha went from Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 2:6; Ruth 1:15–18; 2 Kings 4:30"> Elijah said to Elisha, “Please stay here, for Yahweh has sent me up to Bethel.” Elisha said, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">a</a></sup>and ⸤as your soul lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “life of your soul”">b</a></sup>I will certainly not leave you!” So they went down to Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; 2 Kings 4:38; 2 Kings 9:1"> Then the sons of the prophets who were in Bethel came out to Elisha, and they said to him, “Do you know that Yahweh is going to take your master ⸤from you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “from over your head”">c</a></sup>today?” He said, “I also know; be quiet!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 2:2; Joshua 6:26; 1 Kings 16:34"> Elijah said to him, “Elisha, please stay here because Yahweh has sent me to Jericho.” And he said, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">d</a></sup>and ⸤as your soul lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “life of your soul”">e</a></sup>I will certainly not leave you!” So they came to Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 2:3; Joshua 1:1–2; Acts 1:11"> Then the sons of the prophets who were in Jericho came near to Elisha, and they said to him, “Do you know that Yahweh is going to take your master ⸤from you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from over your head”">f</a></sup>today?” He said, “I also know; be quiet!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 2:2; Joshua 3:15"> Then Elijah said to him, “Please stay here, because Yahweh has sent me to the Jordan.” He said, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">g</a></sup>and ⸤as your soul lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “life of your soul”">h</a></sup>I will certainly not leave you!” So the two of them went on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 18:13; 2 Kings 2:17; 1 Kings 18:4"> Then fifty men from the sons of the prophets went and stood opposite them at a distance while the two of them stood by the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Exodus 14:21–22; Joshua 3:14–17"> Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up, and struck the water. It divided ⸤in two⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “here and here”">i</a></sup>and the two of them crossed over on dry land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Deuteronomy 21:17; John 16:7"> After they crossed over, Elijah said to Elisha, “Ask what I may do for you before I am taken away from you.” Then Elisha said, “Please let there come to me a double portion of your spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 11:22–24; 2 Kings 2:12; John 16:24"> He said, “What you ask is difficult. If you see me being taken from you, it will be so for you, but if not, it will not happen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Psalm 104:3–4; Hebrews 1:14"> Then they were walking, talking as they went. Suddenly a fiery chariot with horses of fire appeared and separated between the two of them. Elijah went up in the storm to the heavens</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 13:14; Isaiah 37:21; Mark 16:19"> while Elisha was watching and crying out, “My father, my father; the chariot of Israel and its horsemen!” But he could not see him any longer, and he grasped his clothes and tore them in two pieces.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; 2 Kings 2:8; 1 Kings 9:26"> Then he picked up Elijah’s cloak that had fallen off of him, and he returned and stood on the bank of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:12; Psalm 42:10; Psalm 115:2"> He took Elijah’s cloak that had fallen from upon him and struck the water. Then he said, “Where is Yahweh, the God of Elijah?” So he also struck the water, and it divided ⸤in two⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “here and here”">j</a></sup>and Elisha crossed over.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elisha Begins His Ministry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Acts 1:8; Joshua 3:7"> When the sons of the prophets who were at Jericho saw him from the other side, they declared, “The spirit of Elijah rests upon Elisha,” and they came to meet him and bowed down to him to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; Acts 8:39; 2 Corinthians 12:2–3"> Then they said to him, “Look, there are with your servants fifty able men. Please let them go and look for your master, lest the Spirit of Yahweh has lifted him up and thrown him on one of the mountains or into one of the valleys,” but he said, “You must not send them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 8:11; Hebrews 11:5; Romans 10:2"> But they urged him until embarrassing him, so he said, “Send them.” So they sent fifty men, and they looked for three days, but they could not find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then they returned to him while he was staying in Jericho. He said to them, “Did I not tell you not to go?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 23:26; Deuteronomy 28:2–4; Deuteronomy 28:11"> The men of the city said to Elisha, “Please now, the location of the city is good, as my master can see, but the water is bad and the land unproductive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 9:45; Zephaniah 2:9; Ezekiel 47:11"> So he said, “Bring me a new bowl and put salt in it.” So they brought it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 15:25–26; 2 Kings 4:41; Leviticus 2:13"> Then he went out to the spring of waters and threw the salt into it there and said, “Thus says Yahweh, ‘I hereby purify these waters; let there be no longer any death or unproductiveness from it.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then the waters were purified until this very day according to the word of Elisha that he spoke.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:16; Job 19:18; Job 30:1"> Then he went up from there to Bethel; as he was going up along the way, young boys came out from the city and mocked at him and said to him, “Go up, baldhead; go up, baldhead!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 13:9–11; Acts 5:5; Hosea 13:8"> When he turned around and saw them, he cursed them in the name of Yahweh. Then two bears came out of the forest and mauled forty-two boys among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 4:25; 1 Kings 18:19–20; 1 Kings 18:42"> Then he went from there to Mount Carmel and from there he returned to Samaria.</span>
</div>
</div>
