## The Acts of Azariah (Uzziah) in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:3–4; 2 Kings 14:21; 2 Chronicles 26:1"> In the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Azariah the son of Amaziah king of Judah began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:3–4"> He was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Jecoliah of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 14:3–4; 2 Kings 12:2–3; 2 Chronicles 26:4"> He did right in the eyes of Yahweh, according to all that Amaziah his father had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 15:35; 2 Kings 14:4; 2 Kings 12:3"> Only he did not remove the high places; the people were still sacrificing and burning incense on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:14; 2 Kings 7:3"> Yahweh struck the king, and he was infected with a skin disease until the day of his death. He lived in a separate house, while Jotham the son of the king was over the house, governing the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:5–15; 2 Kings 14:18"> The remainder of the acts of Azariah, and all that he did, are they not written on the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:23; Isaiah 6:1"> So Azariah slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fathers”">a</a></sup>and they buried him with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “fathers”">b</a></sup>in the city of David, and Jotham his son became king in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Zechariah in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 14:16–17; 2 Kings 14:29–1; 2 Kings 14:21"> In the thirty-eighth year of Azariah king of Judah, Zechariah the son of Jeroboam reigned over Israel in Samaria for six months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 14:24; 2 Kings 13:2; 2 Kings 13:11"> He did evil in the eyes of Yahweh as his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>had done. He did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat which he caused Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 15:28; 2 Kings 15:14; 2 Kings 9:24"> Shallum the son of Jabesh conspired against him, struck him in front of the people, killed him, and reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 14:28"> Now the remainder of the acts of Zechariah, look, they are written on the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 10:30; Mark 13:31; 2 Kings 13:13"> This is the word of Yahweh which he spoke to Jehu, saying, “Sons of a fourth generation shall sit for you on the throne of Israel,” and it was so.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Shallum Reigns only a Month in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 15:1; Matthew 1:8–9; 2 Kings 15:8"> Shallum the son of Jabesh began to reign in the thirty-ninth year of Uzziah king of Judah, and he reigned ⸤a full month⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “a month of days”">d</a></sup>in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 14:17; 1 Kings 16:17; 1 Kings 16:15"> Then Menahem the son of Gadi came up from Tirzah, and he came to Samaria and struck down Shallum the son of Jabesh in Samaria and killed him. Then he became king in place of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 14:19; 1 Kings 14:29; 2 Kings 15:11"> Now the remainder of the acts of Shallum, and his conspiracy which he conspired; look, they are written on the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 1 Kings 4:24; Hosea 13:16"> At that time Menahem destroyed Tiphsah, all who were in it, and all its territory from Tirzah, because it had not opened to him, so he destroyed it and ripped open all of its pregnant women.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Menahem Reigns in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> In the thirty-ninth year of Azariah king of Judah, Menahem the son of Gadi began to reign over Israel for ten years in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But he did evil in the eyes of Yahweh. He did not depart all of his days from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat which he caused Israel to sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 14:5; 1 Chronicles 5:25–26; 2 Kings 18:16"> Pul the king of Assyria came against the land, so Menahem gave a thousand talents of silver to Pul so that his hand would be with him ⸤to strengthen his hold on the kingdom⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to strengthen the kingship in his hand”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 18:14–17; 2 Kings 23:35; 2 Kings 17:3–4"> Menahem exacted the money from Israel, from all ⸤the very rich⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the mighty of the wealth”">f</a></sup>to give to the king of Assyria fifty shekels of silver for each one. Then the king of Assyria returned and did not stay there in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Now the rest of the acts of Menahem, and all that he did, are they not written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So Menahem slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup>and Pekahiah his son became king in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Pekahiah Reigns in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 15:25; 1 Kings 22:51; Job 20:5"> In the fiftieth year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekahiah the son of Menahem began to reign over Israel in Samaria for two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 15:9; 2 Kings 15:18"> But he did evil in the eyes of Yahweh. He did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat which he caused Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:6; 2 Kings 9:14; 1 Kings 16:9"> Pekah the son of Remaliah, his captain, conspired against him, so he attacked him in Samaria in the citadel fortress of the palace of the king, with Argob and Arieh. With him also were fifty men from the children of the Gileadites, and he killed him and became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Now the remainder of the acts of Pekahiah and all that he did, look, they are written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Pekah Reigns in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:4; Isaiah 7:1; 2 Kings 15:23"> In the fifty-second year of Azariah king of Judah, Pekah the son of Remaliah began to reign over Israel in Samaria for twenty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 15:18; 2 Kings 13:6; 2 Kings 15:9"> He did evil in the eyes of Yahweh. He did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat which he caused Israel to sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 16:7"> In the days of Pekah king of Israel, Tiglath-Pileser the king of Assyria came and took Ijon, Abel-Beth-Maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, the Gilead, the Galilee, and all the land of Naphtali; then he deported them to Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:1; 2 Chronicles 28:4–6; 2 Kings 16:1"> Hoshea the son of Elah conspired against Pekah the son of Remaliah, and he attacked and killed him. He reigned in place of him in the twentieth year of Jotham the son of Uzziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Now the remainder of the acts of Pekah and all that he did, look, they are written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jotham Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:12; 2 Kings 15:7; 2 Chronicles 27:1–9"> In the second year of Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, Jotham the son of Uzziah king of Judah began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:1"> He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem, and the name of his mother was Jerusha the daughter of Zadok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 26:4–5; 2 Kings 15:3–4; 2 Chronicles 27:2"> He did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh, according to all that Uzziah his father had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 12:3; 2 Chronicles 23:20; 2 Chronicles 27:3–9"> Only the high places were not removed; the people still were sacrificing and offering incense on the high places. He built the upper gate of the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Chronicles 27:4–9; 2 Kings 15:6–7"> Now the remainder of the acts of Jotham which he did, are they not written on the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 16:5; Isaiah 7:1; Luke 21:28"> (Now, in those days, Yahweh began to send Rezin the king of Aram and Pekah the son of Remaliah against Judah.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 14:20; 1 Kings 14:31; 2 Kings 16:1"> So Jotham slept with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “fathers”">h</a></sup>and was buried with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “fathers”">i</a></sup>in the city of David his ancestor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “father”">j</a></sup>and his son Ahaz became king in his place.</span>
</div>
