## Joram Meets Moab in Battle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 1 Kings 22:51; 2 Kings 8:16"> Now Joram the son of Ahab had become king over Israel in Samaria in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah. He reigned twelve years</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 10:18; 2 Kings 10:26–28; 1 Kings 21:25"> and did evil in the eyes of Yahweh, yet not as his father or his mother, as he removed the stone pillars of Baal that his father had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 15:34; 2 Kings 15:18"> But he did cling to the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat who caused Israel to sin, and he did not depart from it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; Genesis 26:13–14; Job 1:3"> Now Mesha king of Moab was a sheep breeder, and he used to deliver to the king of Israel a hundred thousand male lambs and a hundred thousand wool rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 1:1; 2 Kings 8:20; 2 Chronicles 21:8–10"> It happened that when Ahab died, Mesha king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:1–25; 1 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 15:4"> So King Joram went out on that day from Samaria, and he mustered all of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 22:4; 2 Chronicles 22:10–12; 2 Chronicles 18:3"> He went and sent a message to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, saying, “The king of Moab has rebelled against me. Will you go with me against Moab for the battle?” And he said, “I will go up. ⸤I am like you⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “Like I am, like you are”">a</a></sup>⸤my people are like your people⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “like my people, like your people”">b</a></sup>⸤my horses are like your horses⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “like my horses, like your horses”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachi 1:2–3; Numbers 21:4"> Then he said, “Which way shall we go up?” And he answered, “By way of the wilderness of Edom.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 22:47; Numbers 20:2; Exodus 17:1"> So the king of Israel and the king of Judah and the king of Edom went around, a way of seven days, but there was no water for the army or for the animals which ⸤were with them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “were at their feet”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 78:34–36; Isaiah 51:20; Isaiah 8:21"> Then the king of Israel said, “Aha, Yahweh has called for these three kings to give them into the hand of Moab.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 22:7; 1 Kings 19:21; 2 Kings 3:3"> Then Jehoshaphat asked, “Is there no prophet of Yahweh here that we might inquire guidance from Yahweh?” One of the servants of the king of Israel answered and said, “Elisha the son of Shaphat is here, who poured water on the hands of Elijah.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, Elisha had served Elijah">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 5:15; 2 Kings 2:21; Isaiah 60:14"> Jehoshaphat said, “The word of Yahweh is with him.” So the king of Israel, Jehoshaphat, and the king of Edom went down to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6; 1 Kings 18:19; 1 Kings 22:10–11"> Then Elisha said to the king of Israel, “⸤What do we have in common⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “What to me and what for you”">f</a></sup>Go to the prophets of your father and to the prophets of your mother.” Then the king of Israel said to him, “No, for Yahweh has called for these three kings to give them into the hand of Moab.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; 1 Kings 17:1; 2 Chronicles 19:3–4"> Then Elisha said, “⸤As Yahweh of hosts lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh of hosts”">g</a></sup>before whom I stand, surely if I was not ⸤regarding the face⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “lifting the face”">h</a></sup>of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I would have not looked at you nor even glanced at you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:23; Ephesians 5:18–19; Ezekiel 1:3"> But now, bring me a musician.” It happened that at the moment the musician played, the hand of Yahweh came upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 2:16–18; 2 Kings 4:3"> He said, “Thus says Yahweh, ‘Make this wadi ⸤full of cisterns⸥,’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “cisterns, cisterns”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 107:35; Isaiah 41:17–18; Isaiah 48:21"> for thus says Yahweh, ‘You will see neither wind nor rain, yet this wadi will be full of water; and you and all of your livestock and your animals shall drink.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:27; Luke 1:37; Jeremiah 32:17"> And since this is too trivial in the eyes of Yahweh, he will also give Moab into your hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Judges 6:16"> and you shall defeat every fortified city, every choice city, and you shall fell every good tree. All of the springs of water you shall stop up, and every tract of good land you shall ruin with the stones.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 29:39–40; Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36"> It happened in the morning about the time of the morning offering, that water was suddenly coming from the direction of Edom and the land was filled with water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; 1 Kings 20:11"> Now all of Moab had heard that the kings had come up to fight against them, and all ⸤who were fighting age and up⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “who girded on a belt and above”">j</a></sup>were called up, and they stood at the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> When they arose early in the morning, the sun shone on the waters, and Moab saw the waters from the opposite side as red as blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 15:9; Isaiah 10:14"> Then they said, “This is blood! Certainly the kings have fought one another, and each has killed his neighbor. Now, to the war booty, O Moab!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3–4; Joshua 8:20–22; Judges 20:40–46"> But when they came to the camp of Israel, Israel stood up and killed Moab, so that they fled from before them. They came at her and defeated Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 16:7; Jeremiah 48:36; Jeremiah 48:31"> The cities they tore down, on every good tract of land they threw stones until it was filled up, every spring of water they stopped up, and every good tree they felled. They let the stone walls at Kir Hareseth remain, but the slingers surrounded and attacked it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; Amos 2:1"> When the king of Moab saw that the battle was too heavy for him, he took with him seven hundred men who drew the sword to break through to the king of Edom, but they were not able.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 6:7; Deuteronomy 12:31; Judges 11:31"> He took his firstborn son who was to become king in his place and offered him as a burnt offering on the wall. Great wrath came upon Israel, and they withdrew from him and returned to the land.</span>
</div>
</div>
