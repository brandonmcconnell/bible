## Jehoahaz and Jehoash in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 11:21; 2 Kings 8:26; 2 Kings 11:4"> In the twenty-third year of Joash the son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz the son of Jehu became king over Israel in Samaria, reigning seventeen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 12:26–33; 1 Kings 14:16; 2 Kings 10:29"> But he did evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and he went after the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat with which he had caused Israel to sin, and he did not depart from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:17; 2 Kings 13:24–25; Judges 2:14"> So the ⸤anger of Yahweh was kindled⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the nose of Yahweh became hot”">a</a></sup>against Israel, and he gave them into the hand of Hazael king of Aram and into the hand of Ben-Hadad the son of Hazael ⸤repeatedly⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “all the days”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 14:26; Exodus 3:7; Psalm 78:34"> Then Jehoahaz entreated Yahweh, and Yahweh listened to him, for he saw the oppression of Israel, because the king of Aram oppressed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:27; 2 Kings 14:25; 2 Kings 14:27"> Yahweh gave Israel a savior, and they went out from under the hand of Aram. So the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>lived in their tents as ⸤formerly⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “as yesterday and the day before”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 16:33; 2 Kings 13:2; Deuteronomy 7:5"> Yet they did not depart from the sins of the house of Jeroboam which he caused Israel to sin, but walked in it; and also, the pole of the Asherah worship was still standing in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 1:3; 2 Kings 10:32; 1 Kings 20:15"> For there was no army left over for Jehoahaz except for fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand infantry, for the king of Aram had destroyed them and made them as the dust at threshing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; 1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 14:31"> Now the remainder of the acts of Jehoahaz and all that he did and his powerful deeds, are they not written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 10:35; 2 Kings 13:13; 2 Kings 14:8"> Then Jehoahaz slept with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>and they buried him in Samaria, and Jehoash his son became king in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Jehoash the son of Jehoahaz became king over Israel in Samaria, reigning sixteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 13:2; 2 Kings 10:29; 2 Kings 3:3"> He did evil in the eyes of Yahweh; he did not depart from all of the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat which he caused Israel to sin, but walked in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 13:14–25; 2 Kings 14:8–16; 2 Chronicles 25:17–24"> The remainder of the acts of Joash<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, the king of Israel rather than the king of Judah; “Jehoash” and “Joash” are variant spellings of one another">f</a></sup>and all that he did, his powerful deeds, and how he fought with Amaziah king of Judah, are they not written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 1:21; 2 Kings 14:28–29; 1 Kings 2:10"> Then Joash slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup>and Jeroboam sat on his throne. Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elisha Passes Away

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 2:12; Philippians 2:26; Genesis 48:1"> Elisha became ill with the illness with which he would die, so Jehoash king of Israel went down to him and wept before him, and said, “My father, my father; the chariot of Israel and its horsemen!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Elisha said to him, “Take a bow and arrows,” so he took him a bow and arrows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:34; Genesis 49:24; Psalm 144:1"> He said to the king of Israel, “⸤Lay your hand on the bow⸥,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “Let your hand climb upon the bow”">h</a></sup>so he lay hold of it; then Elisha put his hand on the hands of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 20:26; 2 Kings 5:10–14; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> Then he said, “Open the window to the east,” so he opened it. Elisha said, “Shoot,” and he shot. Then he said, “An arrow of victory for Yahweh, and an arrow of victory against Aram; you shall fight the Arameans in Aphek until finishing them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 4:6; Exodus 17:11; Isaiah 20:2–4"> Then he said, “Take the arrows,” so he took them. He said to the king of Israel, “Strike the ground,” so he struck three times and stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 13:25; Numbers 16:15; 2 Kings 4:40"> Yet the man of God became angry against him and said, “For striking five or six times, then you would have defeated Aram until finishing them, but now you will defeat Aram only three times.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 24:2; 2 Kings 3:7; 2 Kings 6:23"> Elisha died and they buried him. Now the raiding parties of Moab came ⸤in the spring⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “coming year”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:1–10; Acts 19:12; John 5:28–29"> And it happened that they were burying a man; suddenly they saw the marauding band, so they threw the man in the grave of Elisha. As he went in, the man touched the bones of Elisha, and became alive and got up on his feet!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 13:3–7; Psalm 106:40–42"> Hazael king of Aram oppressed Israel all the days of Jehoahaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 105:8; 2 Kings 14:27; Micah 7:18–20"> But Yahweh had mercy on them and showed compassion to them and turned to them because of his covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He was not willing to destroy them nor cast them from his presence up to now.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 125:3; Luke 18:7"> When Hazael king of Aram died, his son Ben-Hadad became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 13:18–19; 2 Kings 10:32; 2 Kings 14:25"> Jehoash the son of Jehoahaz returned and took the cities from the hand of Ben-Hadad the son of Hazael which he had taken from the hand of Jehoahaz his father in the war. Three times Jehoash defeated him and recovered the towns of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>
