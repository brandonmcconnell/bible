## Hezekiah Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:27–1; Matthew 1:9–10; 1 Chronicles 3:13"> It happened in the third year of Hoshea the son of Elah king of Israel, that Hezekiah the son of Ahaz king of Judah became king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:1–2"> He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Abi, the daughter of Zechariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 20:3; 1 Kings 11:4; 2 Chronicles 31:20–21"> He did right in the eyes of Yahweh according to all that David his ancestor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “father”">a</a></sup>had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; Numbers 21:8–9; 2 Kings 18:22"> He removed the high places, and he smashed the stone pillars; he cut down the poles of Asherah worship and demolished the bronze serpent which Moses had made, for up to those days the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>were offering incense to it and called it Nehushtan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 23:25; 2 Kings 19:10; Matthew 27:43"> He trusted in Yahweh the God of Israel; there was no one like him, before or after, among all the kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 23:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; John 14:15"> He held on to Yahweh; he did not depart from following him, and he kept his commands that Yahweh had commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Genesis 39:2–3; 2 Kings 16:7"> Yahweh was with him; everywhere he went, he succeeded. He rebelled against the king of Assyria and did not serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 14:29; 2 Kings 17:9; 2 Chronicles 28:18"> He attacked the Philistines up to Gaza and its territory from the watchtower up to the fortified city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 10:14; 2 Kings 17:3–23; 2 Kings 18:1"> It happened in the fourth year of King Hezekiah, that is, the seventh year of Hoshea the son of Elah king of Israel, Shalmaneser king of Assyria came against Samaria and laid siege against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 6:7; Amos 9:1–4; Micah 1:6–9"> At the end of three years, he captured it in the sixth year of Hezekiah, that is, the ninth year of Hoshea king of Israel; Samaria was captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; 2 Kings 17:6; Isaiah 37:12"> Then the king of Assyria deported Israel to Assyria and settled them in Halah, in Habor, in the river regions of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; 1 Peter 2:8; Micah 3:4"> because they did not listen to the voice of Yahweh their God, and they transgressed his covenant; all that he had commanded Moses, the servant of Yahweh, they did not listen to nor did they obey.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Sennacherib of Assyria Invades Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 36:1–22; 2 Chronicles 32:1–23; Isaiah 7:17–25"> In the fourteenth year of King Hezekiah, Sennacherib king of Assyria came up against all of the fortified cities of Judah and captured them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 18:7; Luke 14:32; 1 Kings 20:4"> So Hezekiah king of Judah sent word to the king of Assyria at Lachish, saying, “I have done wrong. Withdraw from me. What you impose on me I will bear.” So the king of Assyria imposed on Hezekiah king of Judah three hundred talents of silver and thirty talents of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 16:8; 1 Kings 15:18–19; 2 Kings 12:18"> Then Hezekiah gave all of the silver found in the temple of Yahweh and in the storerooms of the house of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:3; 1 Kings 6:31–35"> At that time, Hezekiah cut off the doors of the temple of Yahweh and the doorposts which Hezekiah king of Judah had overlaid, and he gave them to the king of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 20:1; 2 Kings 20:20; Isaiah 7:3"> So the king of Assyria sent the commander in chief, the chief eunuch, and the ⸤chief advisor⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Traditionally “Rabshekah”">c</a></sup>from Lachish to King Hezekiah at Jerusalem with a heavy army. They went up and came to Jerusalem, then they went up and came and stood at the aqueduct of the upper pool which is on the main road of the ⸤washer’s⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “fuller’s”">d</a></sup>field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 19:2; Isaiah 36:3; 2 Samuel 8:16–17"> Then they called to the king, so Eliakim the son of Hilkiah who was over the palace, Shebna the secretary, and Joah the son of Asaph the recorder, came out to them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Assyrians Advise against Trust in Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:4; 2 Kings 19:10; Isaiah 10:8–14"> Then the chief advisor said to them, “Please say to Hezekiah: ‘Thus says the great king, the king of Assyria: “What is this confidence that you trust?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30–31"> You think only a word of lips, ‘I have advice and power for the war.’ Now, on whom do you trust that you have rebelled against me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 30:7; Ezekiel 29:6–7; Isaiah 30:2–3"> Now, look! You ⸤rely⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “trust for yourself”">e</a></sup>on the staff of this broken reed, on Egypt, which when a man leans on it, it goes into his hand and pierces it! So is Pharaoh the king of Egypt for all who are trusting on him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:1; 2 Kings 18:4–5; Matthew 27:43"> But if you say to me, ‘On Yahweh our God we trust,’ is it not he whose high places and altars Hezekiah removed, and he had said to Judah and to Jerusalem, ‘In the presence of this altar you shall bow down only in Jerusalem?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:42; 1 Kings 20:10; Nehemiah 4:2–5"> So then, please make a wager with my lord, with the king of Assyria, and I will give to you a thousand horses ⸤if you are able on your part to put riders on them.⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “if you are able to give for yourself riders upon them”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8; Deuteronomy 17:16; Isaiah 36:6"> How ⸤can you repulse a single captain among the least of the servants of my master⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “can you return the face of the governor one of the servants of my master”">g</a></sup>? Yet you rely for yourself on Egypt for chariots and horsemen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 19:6; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 Kings 19:22–37"> Have I now come up against this place without Yahweh to destroy it? Yahweh has said to me, ‘Go up against this land and destroy it!’ ” ’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 4:7; Daniel 2:4; Isaiah 36:11–12"> Then Eliakim the son of Hilkiah and Shebna and Joah said to the chief commander, “Please speak to your servants in Aramaic, for we are understanding, but you must not speak Judean with us in the ears of the people who are on the wall.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 73:8; Lamentations 4:5; Ezekiel 4:15"> The chief commander said to them, “Is it solely to your master and to you my master has sent me to speak these words? Is it not for the men who sit on the wall to eat their feces and to drink their urine with you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 36:13–18; Isaiah 10:8–13; Ezekiel 29:3"> Then the chief commander stood and called with a great voice in Judean, and he spoke and said, “Hear the word of the king, the great king of Assyria!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:15; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Chronicles 32:11"> Thus says the king, ‘Do not let Hezekiah deceive you, for he will not be able to rescue you from my<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">h</a></sup>hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 125:1–2; Luke 23:35; 2 Kings 19:32–34"> Do not let Hezekiah make you trust in Yahweh, saying, “Certainly Yahweh will rescue us, and this city shall not be given into the hand of the king of Assyria!” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 4:25; 1 Kings 4:20; Zechariah 3:10"> Do not listen to Hezekiah; for thus says the king of Assyria, ‘Make with me a treaty of peace and come out to me that each may eat from his vine and each from his fig tree, and each may drink water from his cistern!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 11:12"> Until I come and take you to a land like your land, a land of grain and new wine, a land of bread and vineyards, a land of olive trees, olive oil, and honey, that you may live and not die! You must not listen to Hezekiah, for he has misled you by saying, “Yahweh will deliver us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 10:10–11; 2 Kings 19:12–13; 2 Chronicles 32:19"> Did the gods of each of the nations ever rescue the land from the hand of the king of Assyria?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 10:9; 2 Kings 17:23–33; Jeremiah 49:23"> Where are the gods of Hamath and Arpad? Where are the gods of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah? For did they rescue Samaria from my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 3:15; Isaiah 10:15; Exodus 5:2"> Who among all of the gods of the countries have rescued their countries from my hand that Yahweh should rescue Jerusalem from my hand?’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Amos 5:13; Proverbs 9:7; Matthew 7:6"> The people were silent, and they did not answer him a word, for the command of that king was saying, “You shall not answer him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 22:11; Genesis 37:29; 2 Kings 5:7"> Eliakim the son of Hilkiah who was over the palace, and Shebna the secretary, and Joah the son of Asaph the recorder came to Hezekiah with torn clothes, and they told him the words of the chief commander.</span>
</div>
