## Hoshea Reigns in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 15:30"> In the twelfth year of Ahaz the king of Judah, Hoshea the son of Elah began to reign over Israel in Samaria, reigning nine years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 3:2; 2 Kings 13:11; 2 Chronicles 30:5–11"> He did evil in the eyes of Yahweh, only not as the kings of Israel who were before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:14; 2 Kings 15:19; 2 Kings 19:36–37"> Shalmaneser the king of Assyria<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This would have been Shalmaneser V of Assyria (ruled ca. 727–722 bc)">a</a></sup>came up against him, and Hoshea became his vassal and paid tribute to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; 2 Kings 24:20; Ezekiel 17:13–19"> But the king of Assyria found treachery in Hoshea, for he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt, and he did not offer tribute to the king of Assyria as he had year after year; so the king of Assyria arrested him, and confined him in a house of imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; 2 Kings 25:1–3; 2 Kings 18:9"> So the king of Assyria went up in all the land, then he went up to Samaria and besieged it for three years.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel Deported to Assyria and the Reasons It Fell

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Hosea 13:16; Deuteronomy 28:64"> In the ninth year of Hoshea, the king of Assyria captured Samaria and deported Israel to Assyria. He placed them in Halah, in Habor, in the river regions of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 23:16; Psalm 106:35–41; Nehemiah 9:26"> Now this happened because the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>had sinned against Yahweh their God when he brought them up from the land of Egypt from under the hand of Pharaoh the king of Egypt and they feared other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 18:3; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 18:9"> They walked in the statutes of the nations whom Yahweh had driven out from before the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>which the kings of Israel had introduced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:8; Job 31:27; Deuteronomy 13:6"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>secretly did things which were not right, against Yahweh their God; they built high places for themselves in all their towns, from the watchtower up to the fortified city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; Micah 5:14; Exodus 34:13"> They set up for themselves stone pillars and poles of Asherah worship on every high hill and under every green tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:25; 2 Kings 21:6; Jeremiah 44:17"> They burned incense there on all the high places, like the nations which Yahweh deported before them, and they did evil things to provoke Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:4; Exodus 20:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:23–25"> They served idols which Yahweh had said to them, “You shall not do this thing!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:15; 1 Samuel 9:9; Jeremiah 18:11"> Yahweh warned Israel and Judah by the hand of his every prophet, with every seer saying, “Turn from all of your evil ways, and keep my commandments and my ordinances, according to all the law which I commanded your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>which I sent to you by the hand of my servants the prophets.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:27; Acts 7:51; Psalm 106:24"> But they did not listen and they stiffened their necks, like the necks of their ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “fathers">f</a></sup>who did not believe in Yahweh their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 1:21–23"> They rejected his statutes, his covenant which he ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “cut”">g</a></sup>with their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “fathers”">h</a></sup>and his warnings which he gave to them; and they went after the idols, became vain, and went after all the nations which were all around them, which Yahweh had commanded them not to do as they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; 1 Kings 16:31; 1 Kings 14:15"> They abandoned all the commands of Yahweh their God and made for themselves two molten calf-shaped idols; they made a pole of Asherah worship and bowed down to the army of the heavens and served Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:6; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 18:10–12"> They made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire, they practiced divination and read omens, and they sold themselves to do evil in the eyes of Yahweh to provoke him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; 1 Kings 11:13; Hosea 11:12"> So Yahweh was very angry with Israel and he removed them from his presence; none remained except the tribe of Judah alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 16:3; 1 Kings 14:22–23; Ezekiel 16:51–52"> Even Judah did not keep the commands of Yahweh their God, and they walked in the customs of Israel which they introduced,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; Jeremiah 6:30; 2 Kings 13:3"> so Yahweh rejected all the offspring of Israel and punished them, and he gave them into the hand of the plunderers until he banished them from his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:11; 1 Kings 11:31; 1 Kings 14:16"> For he had torn Israel from the house of David, and they made Jeroboam the son of Nebat king, but Jeroboam detached Israel from following Yahweh, and he made them sin a great sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 3:3; 2 Kings 13:6; 2 Kings 13:11"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>walked in all the sins of Jeroboam which he committed, and they did not depart from it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 17:13; 2 Kings 17:20"> until Yahweh removed Israel from his presence as he had foretold by the hand of all his servants, the prophets. And so he deported Israel from upon his land to Assyria until this day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel Repopulated with Foreign Captives

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 18:34; 2 Kings 17:30–31; Matthew 10:5"> The king of Assyria brought from Babylonia, from Cush, from Arva, from Hamath, and Sepharvaim, and he settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>so they took possession of Samaria and lived in her cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 17:32; 2 Kings 17:34; 2 Kings 17:41"> It happened that when they began living there, they did not fear Yahweh, so Yahweh sent lions among them, and they were killing them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Leviticus 26:22 warns that non-belief will result in attacks from wild animals.">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 17:27; 1 Samuel 8:9; Amos 8:14"> So they said to the king of Assyria, “The nations whom you deported and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the customs of the God of the land, so he sent lions among them, and now they are killing them because they do not know the customs of the God of the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:13; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:2"> Then the king of Assyria commanded, saying, “Release one of the priests whom you deported from there, and let him go and settle there. Let him teach them the customs of the God of the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 12:29–32; Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:14"> So one of the priests went, whom they had deported from Samaria, and he settled in Bethel and was teaching them how they should fear Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:31; Micah 4:5; 1 Kings 13:32"> Yet every nation was making their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">l</a></sup>gods, and they put them in the shrine of the high places that the Samaritans had made, every nation in their cities in which they were living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:24"> The men of Babylonia made Succoth Benoth; the men of Cuth made Nergal; the men of Hamath made Ashima.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 17:24; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 19:37"> The Arvites made Nibhaz and Tartak; the Sepharvites were burning their children in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech the gods of Sepharvaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 12:31; 2 Kings 17:29; 1 Kings 13:33"> Those who were fearing Yahweh made priests of the high places ⸤from among themselves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “for them from their ends”">m</a></sup>and they were sacrificing for them in the shrines of the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 17:41; Luke 16:13; Matthew 6:24"> Yahweh they were fearing, but their gods they were serving, according to the customs of the nations from which they were deported.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 32:28; Genesis 35:10; 2 Kings 17:27–28"> Until this day they are doing according to their former customs; none of them are fearing Yahweh, and none of them are doing according to their statutes, to their decisions, to the law, or to the commands that Yahweh commanded the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “sons”">n</a></sup>of Jacob to which he had given the name Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 6:10; Joshua 23:16; Joshua 23:7"> Yahweh had ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “cut”">o</a></sup>a covenant with them and commanded them, “You shall not fear other gods, nor shall you bow down to them, nor shall you serve them, nor shall you sacrifice to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 6:13; Leviticus 19:32"> Rather, Yahweh, who brought you out from the land of Egypt with great strength and with an outstretched arm—him you shall fear, and to him you shall bow down, and to him you shall sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 19:37; Deuteronomy 5:31–2; Deuteronomy 4:44–45"> The statutes, the decisions, the law, and the commands that he wrote to you, you shall observe to do always, and you shall not fear other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:23; Deuteronomy 6:12; Deuteronomy 8:14–18"> The covenant that I have ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “cut”">p</a></sup>with you, you shall not forget, and you shall not fear other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:74–75; 1 Samuel 12:24; Matthew 10:28"> But Yahweh your God you shall fear, and he will deliver you from the hand of all of your enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:28; 2 Kings 17:8; Jeremiah 13:23"> They did not listen but kept on doing according to their former customs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Zephaniah 1:5; 1 Kings 18:21"> So these nations were fearing Yahweh, but they were serving their idols, as were their children and their children’s children; as their ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “fathers”">q</a></sup>did, they are doing until this day.</span>
</div>
</div>
