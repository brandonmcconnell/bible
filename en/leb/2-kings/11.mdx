## Athaliah Usurps the Throne in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:1; 2 Kings 25:25; 2 Chronicles 22:10–12"> Now Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, so she prepared to destroy all of the offspring of the royal family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:26; Jeremiah 35:2; Isaiah 7:6–7"> But Jehosheba the daughter of King Joram and sister of Ahaziah took Jehoash the son of Ahaziah, and she stole him from among the sons of the king who were being put to death, putting him and his nurse in the inner bedroom. So they hid him from the presence of Athaliah, and he was not killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 12:8; 2 Chronicles 22:12; Malachi 3:15"> He remained with her in the temple of Yahweh, hidden for six years while Athaliah was reigning over the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 11:19; 2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 11:17"> But in the seventh year Jehoiada sent and took the commanders of the hundreds of the Carites and the runners, and he brought them to himself to the temple of Yahweh. Then he ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “cut”">a</a></sup>a covenant with them and made them swear in the house of Yahweh and showed them the son of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; Ezekiel 44:2–3; 1 Chronicles 23:32"> He commanded them, saying, “This is the thing which you must do: one third of you ⸤who go off duty on the Sabbath⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “who go on the Sabbath”">b</a></sup>the keepers of the guard in the house of the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:4–5; 1 Chronicles 26:13–19"> and another third at the gate of Sur, and a third at the gate behind the runners, shall guard the post of the palace alternately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:6; 2 Kings 11:5"> Two of the units among you, all ⸤who go on duty on the Sabbath⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the going out of the Sabbath”">c</a></sup>guard the post of the temple of Yahweh for the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 27:17; 2 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Kings 2:28–31"> You must surround the king all about, each with his weapon in his hand; whoever comes to the ranks must be killed. Be with the king ⸤wherever he goes⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “when he goes out and when he comes”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:8; 2 Kings 11:4; 1 Chronicles 26:26"> So the commanders of hundreds did according to all that Jehoiada the priest commanded, and each took his men ⸤who went off duty on the Sabbath⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the coming of the Sabbath”">e</a></sup>and those ⸤who came on duty on the Sabbath⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the going out of the Sabbath”">f</a></sup>and they came to Jehoiada the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:7; 1 Chronicles 18:7; 2 Chronicles 5:1"> Then the priest gave to the commanders of the hundreds spears and small round shields which were King David’s, which were in the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 11:8; Ezekiel 8:16; 2 Kings 11:10"> So the royal runners stood, each with his weapons in his hand, from the side of the temple to the south up to the side of the temple on the north, around the altar and around the temple, about the king all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 25:16; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:39"> Then he brought out the son of the king, put the crown on him with the testimony, and they made him king, anointed him, clapped their hands, and said, “Long live the king!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:12–15"> When Athaliah heard the sound of the runners of the people, she came to the people at the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:31; 2 Kings 23:3; 2 Kings 9:23"> She looked, and there was the king standing by the pillar according to the custom. The commanders and the trumpeters were by the king, and all of the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing on the trumpets. Athaliah tore her clothes and she called, “Treason, treason!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:7; 2 Chronicles 23:14; 2 Chronicles 23:9"> Then Jehoiada the priest commanded the commanders of the hundreds, the appointed of the army, and he said to them, “Bring her out to the house of the ranks! The one coming after her should kill her with the sword,” for the priest had said, “Let her not be killed in the temple of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 9:6; 2 Chronicles 23:15; Matthew 7:2"> So ⸤they grabbed her⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “they put hands to her”">g</a></sup>as she went by the entranceway of the horses to the palace of the king, and she was killed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:3; 2 Chronicles 15:12–14; 2 Chronicles 34:31"> Then Jehoiada ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “cut”">h</a></sup>a covenant between Yahweh and the king and the people, that the people should be as a people for Yahweh, and also a covenant between the king and the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:3; 2 Kings 10:26; 1 Kings 18:40"> Then all the people of the land went to the temple of Baal and tore it down, and its altars and its images they broke completely into pieces. Mattan the priest of Baal they killed in front of the altars. Then the priest put guards over the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:25; Jeremiah 22:4; 2 Chronicles 23:19"> He took the commanders of the hundreds and the Carites and the runners and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the temple of Yahweh. And they marched by the way of the runner’s gate to the palace of the king, and he sat on the throne of the kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 29:2; 2 Kings 11:14"> All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city rested; for Athaliah had been killed with the sword in the palace of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:1–14; 2 Kings 22:1; 2 Kings 11:4"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="2 Kings 11:21–12:21 in the English Bible is 2 Kings 12:1–22 in the Hebrew Bible">i</a></sup>Jehoash was ⸤seven years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a son of seven years”">j</a></sup>when he began to reign.</span>
</div>
