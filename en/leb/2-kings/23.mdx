## Josiah’s Covenantal Reforms

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:29–33; 2 Chronicles 30:2; Deuteronomy 31:28"> So the king sent word, and all of the elders of Judah and Jerusalem gathered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 22:8; Deuteronomy 31:10–13; Revelation 20:12"> Then the king went up to the temple of Yahweh, and all of the men of Judah and all of the inhabitants of Jerusalem were with him, including the priests, the prophets, and all of the people from smallest to greatest; and in their ⸤hearing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “ears”">a</a></sup>he read all of the words of the scroll of the covenant that had been found in the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 11:14; 2 Kings 11:17; Deuteronomy 13:4"> Then the king stood by the pillar, and he ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “cut”">b</a></sup>a covenant before Yahweh, to go after Yahweh and to keep his commands and his warnings and his statutes, with all of his heart and with his all of his soul, to keep the words of this covenant written on this scroll. Then all of the people ⸤joined⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “stood”">c</a></sup>in the covenant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:3; 2 Kings 21:7; 2 Chronicles 33:3"> Then the ⸤king⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, Josiah">d</a></sup>commanded Hilkiah the high priest, the second priests, and the keepers of the threshold, to bring out of the temple of Yahweh all of the objects made for Baal and for the Asherah and for all the host of heaven, and he burned them outside of Jerusalem in the fields of the Kidron, and then he carried their ashes to Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:17–19; 2 Kings 21:3–4; Hosea 10:5"> He removed the priests whom the kings of Judah had ordained to burn incense on the high places at the cities of Judah and around Jerusalem and who offered incense to, to the sun, to the moon, to the constellations, and to all the host of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:4; 2 Kings 23:15; 1 Kings 14:23"> He brought out the Asherah image from the temple of Yahweh outside of Jerusalem to the Wadi of the Kidron and burnt it ⸤there⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “at the wadi of the Kidron”">e</a></sup>then he pulverized it to dust and threw its dust upon the tombs of the children of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 15:12; Ezekiel 16:16; 1 Kings 14:24"> He tore down the shrines of the male shrine prostitutes which were in the temple of Yahweh, where the women were weaving shrines for the Asherah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 15:22; Joshua 21:17; 1 Kings 19:3"> Then he brought all of the priests from the cities of Judah and defiled the high places where the priests from Geba up to Beersheba burned incense. He tore down the high places of the gates which were at the entrance of the gate of Joshua, the governor of the city, which were on the left of each gate of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:10–14; 1 Samuel 2:36; Malachi 2:8–9"> However, the priests of the high places did not come up to the altar of Yahweh in Jerusalem, but they ate unleavened bread in the midst of their relatives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:33; Jeremiah 19:6; Leviticus 18:21"> He defiled the Topheth which is in the Valley of Ben-Hinnom, ⸤to prevent⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “so that not”">f</a></sup>anyone causing his sons or his daughters to pass through the fire for Molech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Deuteronomy 4:19; 2 Chronicles 14:5"> He kept the horses that the kings of Judah had dedicated to the sun from coming to the temple of Yahweh at the side room of Nathan-Melech the eunuch, which was in the court; and the chariots of the sun he burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 21:5; Zephaniah 1:5; Jeremiah 19:13"> The altars which were on the roof of the upper room of Ahaz, which the kings of Judah had made, and the altars which Manasseh had made in the two courtyards of the temple of Yahweh, the king tore down and ran from there and threw their ashes into the Wadi Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 11:7; 1 Kings 11:5; Numbers 21:29"> The high places which were east of Jerusalem, which were on the south of the Mountain of Destruction which Solomon king of Israel had built for Ashtoreth the abomination of the Sidonians and for Chemosh the abomination of Moab and for Milcom the detestable thing of the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">g</a></sup>the king defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:5; Exodus 23:24; 2 Kings 23:16"> He also broke into pieces the stone pillars and cut down the Asherah poles and covered their sites with human bones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 23:6; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 21:22"> Moreover, the altar which was in Bethel, the high place which Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who had caused Israel to sin, had built, even that altar and the high place, Josiah tore down. Then he burned down the high place and crushed the pole of Asherah worship to dust and burned it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 13:1–2; 1 Kings 13:32; John 10:35"> When Josiah turned and saw the tombs which were there on the hill, he sent and took the bones from the tombs and burned them on the altar. Thus he defiled them according to the word of Yahweh that the man of God had proclaimed who had proclaimed these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 13:1; 1 Kings 13:30–31"> Then he said, “What is this gravestone that I am seeing?” The men of the city said to him, “This is the tomb of the man of God who came from Judah and proclaimed these things which you have done against the altar of Bethel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 13:31; 1 Kings 13:1–22"> So Josiah said, “Let him rest and let no man move his bones.” So they left his bones undisturbed with the bones of the prophet who had come from Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:6–7; 2 Kings 17:9; Ezekiel 8:17–18"> Moreover, all of the shrines of the high places which were in the towns of Samaria which the kings of Israel had made to provoke Yahweh, Josiah removed, and he did to them like all of the deeds he had done in Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 11:18; 2 Chronicles 34:5; Exodus 22:20"> Then he slaughtered all of the priests of the high places who were there, on the altars, and he burned the bones of the humans on them. Then he returned to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Passover Renewed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Numbers 9:2–5; 2 Chronicles 35:1–19"> Then the king commanded all of the people, saying, “Keep the Passover to Yahweh your God, as has been written on the scroll of this covenant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:1–3; 2 Chronicles 30:13–20; 2 Chronicles 35:3–19"> For they had not kept this Passover from the days of the judges who had judged over Israel ⸤or⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “and”">h</a></sup>during the days of the kings of Israel and the kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But in the eighteenth year of King Josiah, this Passover was kept for Yahweh in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 2 Kings 21:6; Genesis 31:19"> Moreover, the mediums and the spiritists, the household gods and the idols, and all of the abominations that were seen in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, Josiah removed in order to establish the words of the law written on the scroll that Hilkiah the priest had found in the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 18:5; Jeremiah 29:13; Deuteronomy 6:5"> There was not a king like him before him, who turned to Yahweh with all of his heart and with all of his soul and with all of his might according to the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “Torah”">i</a></sup>of Moses, nor did one arise like him afterwards.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 21:11–13; 2 Kings 24:4; Jeremiah 3:7–10"> However, Yahweh did not turn from the fierceness of his great anger which was kindled against Judah because of all of the provocations with which Manasseh had provoked him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 18:11; 2 Kings 21:13; 2 Kings 21:4"> Yahweh had said, “Even Judah I will remove from my face, as I have removed Israel; I will reject this city that I have chosen, even Jerusalem and the house of which I said, ‘My name shall be there’!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20"> The remainder of the acts of Josiah and all that he did, are they not written on the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:2; 2 Chronicles 35:20–24; Judges 5:19"> In his days, Pharaoh Neco, king of Egypt, went up against the king of Assyria at the Euphrates River. King Josiah went to meet him, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="That is, Neco">j</a></sup>killed him at Megiddo as soon as he saw him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 9:28; 2 Chronicles 36:1–4; 2 Kings 21:24"> So his servants drove him dead in a chariot from Megiddo, and they brought him to Jerusalem and buried him in his tomb. Then the people of the land took Jehoahaz the son of Josiah and anointed him and made him king in place of his father.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehoahaz Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:15; 2 Kings 24:18; Jeremiah 22:11"> Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he became king, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Hamutal the daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Kings 21:2–7; 2 Kings 21:21–22"> He did evil in the eyes of Yahweh according to all his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “fathers”">k</a></sup>had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Kings 25:6; Jeremiah 39:5–6"> Then Pharaoh Neco confined him at Riblah in the land of Hamath, from reigning in Jerusalem, and imposed a levy on the land of a hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehoiakim Replaces Jehoahaz

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:3–4; 2 Kings 24:17; 1 Chronicles 3:15"> Then Pharaoh Neco made Eliakim the son of Josiah king in place of Josiah his father, and he changed his name to Jehoiakim. Then he took Jehoahaz and brought him to Egypt, and he died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; 2 Kings 15:19–20"> The silver and the gold Jehoiakim gave to Pharaoh; however, he taxed the land to give the silver ⸤to meet the demands of Pharaoh⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “on the hunger of Pharaoh”">l</a></sup>Each according to assessment, he exacted payment of the silver and the gold from the people of the land to give to Pharaoh Neco.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5; Jeremiah 26:1; 1 Chronicles 3:15"> Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Zebudah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="According to the reading tradition (Qere) ; Kethib reads “Zebidah”">m</a></sup>the daughter of Pedaiah from Rumah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:22–23; Ezekiel 19:5–9; Jeremiah 36:31"> He did evil in the eyes of Yahweh according to all that his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “fathers”">n</a></sup>had done.</span>
</div>
