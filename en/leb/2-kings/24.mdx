## First Invasion of Nebuchadnezzar; Jehoiakim Submits

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:1; Jeremiah 25:1; Jeremiah 25:9"> In his days, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up because Jehoiakim had become his servant for three years; then he turned and rebelled against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:11; 2 Kings 23:27; 2 Kings 21:12–14"> So Yahweh sent against him raiding bands of Chaldeans, raiding bands of Aram, raiding bands of Moab, and raiding bands of the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">a</a></sup>He had sent them against Judah to destroy it, according to the word of Yahweh that he had spoken by the hand of his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:25; 2 Kings 21:2–11; 2 Kings 23:26–27"> Surely, it was ⸤on the command⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “on the mouth”">b</a></sup>of Yahweh against Judah to remove them from his sight because of the sins of Manasseh, according to all that he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Psalm 106:38; Numbers 35:33"> Also, for the blood of the innocent that he had shed—and he filled Jerusalem with innocent blood—Yahweh was not willing to forgive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:8; Jeremiah 26:1–24; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> The remainder of the acts of Jehoiakim and all that he did, are they not written on the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18–19; 2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 36:30"> So Jehoiakim slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>and Jehoiachin his son became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:5–7; Jeremiah 46:2; Genesis 15:18"> The king of Egypt did not again come out from his land, for the king of Babylon had taken territory from the Wadi of Egypt to the Euphrates River.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehoichin Succeeds Jehoiakim

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:9; 1 Chronicles 3:16; Jeremiah 37:1"> Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Nehushta daughter of Elnathan from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He did evil in the eyes of Yahweh according to all that his father had done.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Second Invasion of Nebuchadnezzar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 25:2; Daniel 1:1–2"> At that time, the servants of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem, and the city came under the siege.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came against the city while his servants were besieging it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:1; Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 25:27"> Jehoiachin king of Judah went out to the king of Babylon, he, his mother, his servants, his commanders, and his court officials. The king of Babylon took him in the eighth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 39:6; 2 Kings 20:17; Jeremiah 20:5"> Then he took from there all of the treasures of the temple of Yahweh and the treasures of the palace of the king. He cut up all of the vessels of gold which Solomon the king of Israel had made in the temple of Yahweh, as Yahweh had foretold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 25:12; 2 Kings 24:16"> He deported all of Jerusalem: all of the commanders, ten thousand of the skilled warriors, and the artisans; no one was left over except the poorest of the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:10; Jeremiah 22:24–28; Esther 2:6"> He deported Jehoiachin to Babylon; the mother of the king, the wives of the king, his court officials, and the citizenry of the land he caused to go into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 24:14; Jeremiah 29:2"> of all of the skilled men, seven thousand, and of the skilled craftsmen and the artisans, one thousand. All of the mighty warriors ⸤fit for war⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “doers of war”">d</a></sup>the king of Babylon brought captive to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; 2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:34"> Then the king of Babylon made Mattaniah his uncle king in his place and changed his name to Zedekiah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Zedekiah Replaces Jehoiachin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:31; Jeremiah 52:1–11; Jeremiah 37:1"> Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah from Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 23:37; Jeremiah 24:8; Ezekiel 21:25"> He did evil in the eyes of Yahweh just like all that Jehoiakim had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Jeremiah 38:17–21"> For it happened because of the anger of Yahweh, in Jerusalem and in Judah, until they were cast out from his presence. Then Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.</span>
</div>
</div>
