## Elijah and the Messengers of Ahaziah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; Numbers 24:7; 2 Kings 3:4–5"> Moab rebelled against Israel after the death of Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 1:16; Matthew 10:25; Mark 3:22"> Ahaziah had fallen through the lattice in his upper room, which was in Samaria, and he was injured. So he sent messengers, and he said to them, “Go, inquire of Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron, if I will survive this injury.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Jonah 2:8; Acts 12:7–11"> Then the angel of Yahweh spoke to Elijah the Tishbite, “Get up, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria and speak to them, ‘Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are going to inquire of Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 3:4; Genesis 2:17; Ezekiel 18:4"> Therefore, thus says Yahweh, ‘The bed upon which you have gone, you will not come down from it, but you shall surely die.’ ” So Elijah went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When the messengers returned to him, he asked them, “Why have you returned?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 16:4; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14; Isaiah 41:22–23"> Then they said to him, “A man came up to meet us, and he said to us, ‘Go, return to the king who sent you and speak to him, “Thus says Yahweh, ‘Is it because there is no God in Israel that you are sending to inquire of Baal-Zebub the god of Ekron? Therefore the bed upon which you have gone, you will not come down from it, for you will surely die.’ ” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:14; Judges 8:18"> Then he spoke to them, “What was the manner of the man who came up to meet you and spoke to you all these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Zechariah 13:4; Mark 1:6"> They answered him, “A ⸤hairy man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “an owner of hair”">a</a></sup>with a leather belt girded around his waist.” And he said, “It is Elijah the Tishbite.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 6:13–14; Luke 6:11–12; Hebrews 11:36"> So ⸤Ahaziah⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “he”">b</a></sup>sent to him the commander of fifty with his fifty men, and he went up to him while he was sitting on the top of the hill. He said to him, “O man of God, the king says, ‘Come down.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:54; Daniel 3:25; 1 Kings 18:36–38"> Then Elijah answered and said to the commander of the fifty, “If I am a man of God, let fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty!” Then fire came down from heaven and consumed him and his fifty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 2:16; Luke 22:63–64; Jeremiah 5:3"> So he ⸤again sent⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “returned and sent”">c</a></sup>another commander of fifty and his fifty men. He answered and said to him, “O man of God, thus says the king, ‘Come down quickly!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 1:9–10"> Then Elijah answered and said to them, “If I am a man of God, then let fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty!” Then the fire of God came down from heaven and consumed him and his fifty.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:5; 1 Samuel 26:21; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> So he ⸤again sent⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “returned and sent”">d</a></sup>a third time a commander of fifty and his fifty, and the commander of the third fifty went up and came and knelt down on his knees before Elijah and entreated him. He said to him, “O man of God, please let my life and the lives of your servants, these fifty, be precious in your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Psalm 49:8; Proverbs 6:26"> Behold, fire from heaven came down and consumed the first two commanders of fifty and their fifties, so then let my life be precious in your eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:12; Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 1:17"> Then the angel of Yahweh spoke to Elijah, “Go down with him. Do not be afraid because of him.” So he got up and went down with him to the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:2–4; 1 Kings 22:28; Exodus 4:22–23"> and he said to him, “Thus says Yahweh, ‘Because you have sent messengers to inquire of Baal-Zebub, the god of Ekron—is it because there is no God in Israel from whom to inquire his word?—therefore the bed upon which you went, you shall not come down from it, for you shall surely die.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Epitaph for Ahaziah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 3:1; 1 Kings 22:51; 2 Kings 8:16–17"> So he died, according to the word of Yahweh which Elijah had spoken, and Joram became king in his place in the second year of Joram the son of Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, because he had no son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:39; 1 Kings 14:19"> The remainder of the acts of Ahaziah which he did, are they not written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
</div>
