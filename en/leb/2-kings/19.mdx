## Isaiah Sends Encouragement to Hezekiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 21:27; 2 Kings 18:37; 2 Chronicles 32:20–22"> It happened that when King Hezekiah heard, he tore his clothes and covered himself with sackcloth and went to the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:1; Isaiah 2:1; Matthew 4:14"> He sent Eliakim who was over the palace, Shebna the secretary, the elders, and the priests, all clothed in sackcloth, to Isaiah the prophet the son of Amoz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:5–7; Isaiah 66:9; Hosea 13:13"> They said to him, “Thus says Hezekiah, ‘A day of distress, rebuke, and disgrace is this day, for the children ⸤are about to be born⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “came up to the outer vagina”">a</a></sup>but there is no strength to bear them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:12; Romans 9:27; Joshua 14:12"> Perhaps Yahweh your God will hear all of the words of the chief commander whom his master the king of Assyria has sent to insult the living God, and he will rebuke the words which Yahweh your God has heard. Therefore lift up a prayer for the remainder who are left.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the servants of King Hezekiah came to Isaiah,</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Obadiah 1:1; 2 Kings 7:6; Psalm 18:14–15"> Look, I am putting in him a spirit. He will hear a rumor and return to his land. Then I will cause him to fall by the sword in his land.” ’ ”</span>

## The Assyrians Defy God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:14; Joshua 10:29; Joshua 12:11"> When the chief commander returned, he found the king of Assyria fighting against Libnah, for he had heard that he had departed from Lachish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:27; Isaiah 37:9; 2 Kings 18:17"> He heard about Tirhakah, the king of Cush, saying, “Look, he has set out to fight with you,” so he again sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 18:5; 2 Kings 18:29–30; 2 Chronicles 32:15–19"> “Thus you shall say to Hezekiah the king of Judah, ‘Let not your God whom you are trusting deceive you, by his saying, “Jerusalem shall not be given into the hand of the king of Assyria!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8–11; 2 Chronicles 32:13–14; 2 Kings 19:17–18"> Look, you have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all the lands, by utterly destroying them, and shall you be delivered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Genesis 11:31; Ezekiel 27:23"> Did the gods of the nations that my ⸤predecessors⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">b</a></sup>destroyed deliver them? Not Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, nor the children of Eden who were in Tel Assar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:34; 2 Kings 17:24; Isaiah 11:11"> Where are the king of Hamath, the king of Arpad, the king of the city of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?’ ”</span>
</div>

## Hezekiah Prays to Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; Psalm 91:1–2; Psalm 123:1–4"> Hezekiah took the letters from the hand of the messengers and read them. Then he went up to the temple of Yahweh, and Hezekiah spread them out before the presence of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; 2 Kings 5:15; Daniel 4:34–35"> Then Hezekiah prayed before the face of Yahweh and said, “O Yahweh, God of Israel who lives above the cherubim. You are God, you alone, of all the kingdoms of the world; you have made the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:40; Psalm 31:2; 2 Kings 19:4"> Incline your ears and hear; open, O Yahweh, your eyes and see and hear the words of Sennacherib which he has sent to insult the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Job 9:2; 1 Corinthians 14:25"> Truly, O Yahweh, the kings of Assyria have utterly destroyed the nations and their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Isaiah 44:9–20; Jeremiah 10:3–9"> He has hurled their gods in the fire because they are not gods, but the work of the hands of a human made of wood and stone, so they destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 83:18; 1 Kings 8:43; Psalm 67:1–2"> So then, O Yahweh our God, rescue us, please, from his hand, that all of the kingdoms of the earth may know that you, O Yahweh, you alone are God!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Isaiah Brings a Prophetic Response

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Lamentations 2:13; Job 16:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> This is the word that Yahweh has spoken concerning him:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">She despises you, she scorns you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the virgin daughter of Zion.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behind you the daughter of Jerusalem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">shakes her head.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; Psalm 71:22; Isaiah 30:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whom have you mocked and reviled?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And against whom have you have raised your voice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and have haughtily lifted your eyes?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Against the Holy One of Israel!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Psalm 20:7; Isaiah 10:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">By the hand of your messengers you have mocked the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you have said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘With my many chariots I have gone up</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the height of the mountains.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">To the remote areas of Lebanon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have felled the tallest of its cedars,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the choicest of its cypresses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have entered the place of overnight lodging.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Even to the edge of forest of its fertile land.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; Isaiah 10:5–6; Isaiah 37:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Have you not heard?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">From long ago I have determined it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the days of old I have planned it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and now I am bringing it to pass.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It shall be turned into a pile of rocks;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">fortified cities are ruined.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 37:28–29; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Psalm 139:1–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your sitting, your going out, and your coming in I know,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your raging against me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:4; Ezekiel 38:4; 2 Kings 19:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because you are raging against me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your arrogance has come up in my ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put my nose ring in your nose</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my bridle in your mouth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will turn you back</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the way that you have come.</span></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 25:20–22; 2 Kings 20:8–9; Exodus 3:12"> “ ‘This will be the sign for you: Eat the volunteer plants for the year, and in the second year, the volunteer plants that spring up from that. But in the third year, sow and reap, plant vineyards and eat their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 19:4; Isaiah 27:6; 2 Chronicles 32:22–23"> The remainder of the house of Judah which survives will again take root below and bear fruit above.</span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–10; 2 Samuel 20:15; Isaiah 37:33–35"> “ ‘Therefore thus says Yahweh to the king of Assyria, “He shall not come to this city, nor shall he shoot an arrow there, nor shall he bring a small shield near her, nor shall he cast a siege ramp against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; 2 Kings 19:36"> By the way that he came to her he shall return; but to this city, he shall not come,” declares Yahweh.</span>

## An Angel Neutralizes the Assyrian Army

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21–22; 2 Samuel 24:16; Exodus 12:29–30"> It happened in that night that an angel of Yahweh went out, and he struck down one hundred and eighty-five thousand in the camp of Assyria. When they got up early in the morning, look! All of them were dead corpses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; 2 Kings 19:7; 2 Kings 19:33"> Then Sennacherib king of Assyria set out and went and returned and lived in Nineveh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ezra 4:2; Genesis 8:4; 2 Kings 19:7"> It happened that he was worshiping in the temple of Nisroch his god, and Adrammelech and Sharezer<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="So Kethib ; Qere adds “his sons”">c</a></sup>struck him with the sword. Then they escaped into the land of Ararat, and Esarhaddon his son became king in his place.</span>
