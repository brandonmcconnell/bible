## Elisha Prophesies Hope for Relief

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 3:5; Exodus 16:12; Ezekiel 37:4"> Elisha said, “Hear the word of Yahweh: ‘Thus says Yahweh, “At this time tomorrow a seah of wheat bread flour will sell for a shekel, and two seahs of barley for a shekel in the gate of Samaria.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:12–14; Genesis 7:11; Malachi 3:10"> Then the officer ⸤on whom the king relied⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “whom the king was leaning on his hand”">a</a></sup>answered the man of God and said, “Look, even if Yahweh is making windows in heaven, could this thing happen?” And he said, “Look, you will be seeing it with your eyes, but you shall not eat from it there.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Four Lepers Report the Departure of the Arameans

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Numbers 5:2–4; 2 Kings 7:4"> Now four men who had a skin disease were at the entrance of the gate, and they said ⸤to each other⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “each to his friend”">b</a></sup>“Why are we sitting here until we die?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Luke 15:17–19; 2 Samuel 14:14"> If we say, ‘Let us go into the city,’ the famine is in the city, and we shall die there; but if we sit here, we shall die. So then, come, let us fall into the camp of the Arameans. If they let us live, we shall live; but if they kill us, then we shall die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:25; 1 Samuel 30:17"> So they got up at dusk to go to the camp of the Arameans. They went up to the edge of the camp of the Arameans, and look, there was no man there!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:24; Isaiah 31:1; 1 Kings 10:29"> Now the Lord had caused the camp of the Arameans to hear the sound of chariots, the sound of horses, and the sound of a great army. So they said ⸤to one another⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “each to his brother”">c</a></sup>“Look, the king of Israel has hired the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Egypt to come against us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalm 48:4–6; Hebrews 6:18"> So they got up and fled at dusk and left their tents, their horses, their donkeys, and the camp as it was, and they fled for their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Joshua 7:21; Matthew 25:18"> When these who had the skin disease came to the edge of the camp, they went into a certain tent and they ate, drank, and took from there silver and gold and clothes. Then they went and hid them, then returned and came to another tent, and they took from there and went and hid them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Philippians 2:4; Nahum 1:15"> Then they said ⸤to one another⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “each to his friend”">d</a></sup>“We are not doing right. This day is a day of good news! If we are silent and wait until the light of morning, they will find us and ⸤we will be punished⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “guiltiness”">e</a></sup>So then, come, let us go and tell the house of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 7:11; Mark 13:34–35; Psalm 127:1"> When they came, they called to the gatekeepers of the city and told them, saying, “We came to the camp of the Arameans, and behold, there was no man or the voice of a man there! Only the horses and the donkeys were tied up, and the tents were left as they were.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the gatekeepers called and told it inside the house of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 6:25–29; Joshua 8:4"> The king got up in the night and said to his servants, “Please let me tell you what the Arameans have done to us. The Arameans know that we are hungry, so they went out from the camp to hide in the field, saying, ‘When they go out from the city, we shall seize them alive and go into the city.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 7:4; Lamentations 4:9; 2 Kings 5:13"> Then one of his servants replied and said, “Please let them take five of the remaining horses which remain in the city; behold, they are like all of the multitude of Israel that remain in it; they are like all the multitude of Israel who have perished. Let us send and see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So he took two charioteer horsemen, and the king sent after the camp of the Arameans, saying, “Go, find out,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 22:24; Matthew 24:16–18; Hebrews 12:1"> and they went after them to the Jordan. Look, all of the way was littered with clothes and equipment which the Arameans had thrown away in their haste. Then the messengers returned and told the king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Prophecy Fulfilled

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 7:1; Isaiah 33:4; Isaiah 33:23"> So the people went out and plundered the camp of the Arameans. A seah of wheat flour went for a shekel and two seahs of barley went for a shekel according to the word of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 7:2; 2 Kings 6:32; Judges 20:43"> Then the king appointed the officer he was depending on over the gate, but the people trampled him and he died, according to that which the man of God had said which he spoke when the king came down to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:14; 2 Kings 7:1–2; 2 Kings 6:32"> It happened as the man of God spoke to the king, saying, “Two seahs of barley shall be sold for a shekel and a seah of wheat flour for a shekel at this time tomorrow in the gate of Samaria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 7:2"> Then the officer had replied to the man of God and said, “Look, even if Yahweh is opening the windows in heaven, could this thing happen?” And he had said, “Look you are about to see it with your eyes, but you will not eat from it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5–6; 2 Chronicles 20:20; Job 20:23"> So it had happened to him; the people trampled him in the gate and he died.</span>
</div>
