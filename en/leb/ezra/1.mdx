## The Decree of Cyrus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; 2 Chronicles 36:22–23; Jeremiah 25:12–14"> In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, in order to accomplish the word of Yahweh by the mouth of Jeremiah, Yahweh stirred the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia and he sent a message to all of his kingdom and also put the message in writing:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 45:12–13; Isaiah 44:26–1; Daniel 5:23"> “Thus says Cyrus king of Persia: Yahweh, the God of the heavens, has given me all the kingdoms of the earth. And he himself has appointed me to build a house for him in Jerusalem, which is in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 6:26; Jeremiah 10:10; Isaiah 45:5"> Whoever among you who is from all of his people, may his God be with him and may he go up to Jerusalem, which is in Judah, and may he build the house of Yahweh, the God of Israel. He is the God who is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:3; Ezra 7:16–18; Ezra 2:68–70"> And let every survivor, from wherever he ⸤resides⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “resides as a foreigner”">a</a></sup>be assisted by the men of that place with silver and gold, with possessions and domestic animals, and with the freewill offering for the house of God which is in Jerusalem.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezra 1:1; 2 Chronicles 36:22; Philippians 2:13"> So the heads of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">b</a></sup>for Judah and Benjamin, and the priests and the Levites—to all whose spirit God had stirred—prepared to go up and build the house of Yahweh which is in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Ezra 1:4; Ezra 8:33"> And all of their neighbors ⸤helped them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “helped with their hands”">c</a></sup>with objects of silver, gold, possessions, domestic animals, and with valuable gifts—besides all of the freewill offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; Ezra 5:14; 2 Kings 24:13"> And Cyrus the king brought out the objects of the house of Yahweh that Nebuchadnezzar had brought from Jerusalem and placed in the house of his gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 5:14; Zechariah 4:6–10; Ezra 1:11"> Cyrus the king of Persia let them go out by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, and he counted them out to Sheshbazzar the prince of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 7:50; Numbers 7:19–89; 2 Chronicles 4:8"> Now these were the inventories: thirty gold metal dishes, one thousand silver metal dishes, twenty-nine vessels,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> thirty bowls of gold, four hundred and ten matching silver metal bowls, and one thousand other objects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19–21; Romans 9:23; Matthew 1:11–12"> All of the objects of gold and silver metal were five thousand four hundred. All this Sheshbazzar brought up along with the exiles from Babylonia to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
</div>
