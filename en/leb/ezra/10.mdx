## The People Respond

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 9:20; Psalm 119:136; Romans 9:2"> While Ezra was praying and confessing, weeping and throwing himself down before the house of God, a very great assembly of men, women, and children from Israel gathered to him. And the people wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:27; Ezra 9:2; Jeremiah 3:12–13"> Shecaniah son of Jehiel, of the sons of Elam, answered and said to Ezra, “We ourselves have broken faith with our God and have married foreign women from the peoples of the land, and even now there is hope for Israel in spite of this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2–3; Ezra 9:4; Joshua 23:12–13"> So now let us make a covenant with our God to send away all of these women and their offspring, according to the advice of my lord and those who tremble at the commandment of our God. Let it be done according to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:10; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Hebrews 12:12–13"> Arise, for it is your task and we are with you. Be strong and do it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:25; Nehemiah 5:12; Proverbs 9:9"> Then Ezra stood up and made the chief priests, Levites, and all of Israel swear to do according to what was said. So they swore this oath.</span>

## Foreign Wives and Children are Sent Away

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 12:22"> Ezra rose from before the house of God and went to the chamber of Jehohanan son of Eliashib. He did not eat food nor drink water because he was mourning over the sin of the exiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 1:1; 2 Chronicles 30:5"> And they sent a proclamation through Judah and Jerusalem to all the ⸤returned exiles⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the exile”">a</a></sup>to assemble in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:26; John 9:22; Judges 21:5"> Anyone who did not come within three days, by decision of the officials and elders, all of his possessions would be devoted to God, and he himself would be excluded from the assembly of exiles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:17–18; Jeremiah 10:13; Ezra 7:8–9"> Then all the men of Judah and Benjamin assembled in Jerusalem within the three days. It was the ninth month on the twentieth day of the month. All the people sat in the public square of the house of God trembling because of this matter and from the rains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; Numbers 32:14; Matthew 23:32"> Then Ezra the priest stood up and said to them, “You have broke faith and married foreign women, increasing the guilt of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Ezra 10:3; Isaiah 1:16–18"> Now make a confession to Yahweh the God of your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “fathers”">b</a></sup>and do his will. Separate yourselves from the peoples of the land and from the foreign women.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:23; Psalm 78:57; Ezra 10:3–4"> Then all the assembly answered with a great voice and said, “It is so. We must do according to your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 10:18–44; Matthew 7:13–14"> But the people are many, and it is the time of rain; we are unable to stand outside. The task is not for one day or two, for we greatly rebelled in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:10; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Numbers 25:4"> Please let our leaders stand for the whole assembly, and let all that are in our cities who have married foreign women come at set times, and with them the elders of and judges of each city, until the fierce wrath of our God because of this matter is averted from us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:16; Nehemiah 10:20; Nehemiah 12:33"> Only Jehonathan son of Asahel and Jahzeiah son of Tikvah stood against this, and the Levites Meshullam and Shabbethai supported them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 29:16; Deuteronomy 13:14; John 7:51"> The ⸤returned exiles⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the exile”">c</a></sup>did so. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They finished investigating all the men who married foreign women by the first day of the first month.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezra 5:2; Ezra 3:2; Zechariah 3:1"> There was found from the sons of the priests those who had married foreign women, from the sons of Jeshua son of Jehozadak and his brothers: Maaseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah.</span>

<div className="bp">
