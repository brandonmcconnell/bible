## Resistance to Rebuilding the House of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9–10; Ezra 1:11; Ezra 10:16"> Now the enemies of Judah and Benjamin heard that the ⸤returned exiles⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the exile”">a</a></sup>were building a temple for Yahweh the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 19:37; 2 Kings 17:24; Galatians 2:4"> And they approached Zerubbabel and the heads of the ⸤families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">b</a></sup>and they said to them, “Let us build with you. Like you, we seek your God and have been sacrificing to him from the days of Esarhaddon the king of Assyria who brought us up here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:20; Matthew 10:16; 2 Chronicles 36:22–3"> But Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and the rest of the heads of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">c</a></sup>of Israel said to them, “It is not for you but for us to build a house for our God. For we ourselves alone will build it for Yahweh the God of Israel, just as Cyrus the king of Persia has commanded us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 3:3; Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremiah 38:4"> Then the people of the land discouraged ⸤the people of Judah⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the hands of the people of Judah”">d</a></sup>and made them afraid to build</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 1:11; Ezra 5:5–18; Psalm 2:1–2"> and bribed officials against them to frustrate their plan for all the days of Cyrus king of Persia until the reign of Darius king of Persia.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Resistance to Rebuilding Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 1:1; Daniel 9:1; Acts 24:5–9"> In the reign of Ahasuerus, at the beginning of his reign, they wrote an accusation against the inhabitants of Judah and Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 18:26; Daniel 2:4; Isaiah 36:11"> And in the days of Artaxerxes, Bishlam, Mithredath, Tabeel, and the rest of their colleagues wrote to Artaxerxes king of Persia. The letter was written in Aramaic and translated from Aramaic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:9; 2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 20:25"> Rehum the royal officer and Shimshai the scribe wrote a letter against Jerusalem to King Artaxerxes as follows</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 5:6; Ezra 6:6; 2 Kings 17:24"> (then Rehum the royal officer, Shimshai the scribe, and the rest of their associates, the judges, the envoys, the officials, the Persians, the Erechs, the Babylonians, the Susians (that is the Elamites)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 4:17; Ezra 4:11; Ezra 7:12"> and the rest of the nations whom the great and noble Osnappar deported and settled in the cities of Samaria and the rest of the province Beyond the River) and now</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> this is the copy of the letter which they sent to him:<br />“To King Artaxerxes from your servants, the men of the province Beyond the River. And now,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 5:9; 2 Chronicles 36:13; Ezra 5:3"> be it known to the king that the Jews who have come up from near you to us have gone to Jerusalem. They are building the rebellious and wicked city. They are finishing the wall and repairing the foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 7:24; Romans 13:6–7; Nehemiah 5:4"> Now be it known to the king that if this city is built and the walls are finished, they will not pay tribute and toll, and the royal revenue will be reduced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 19:12–15; Ezekiel 33:31; John 12:5–6"> Now since we eat the salt of the palace and the dishonor of the king is not proper for us to see, we send and make this known to the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezra 4:12; Esther 3:5–8; 2 Kings 24:20–1"> so that it may be investigated in the book of records of your ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>You will find in the book of records and learn that this city is a rebellious city, hurtful to kings and provinces, and they have rebelled in its midst from ancient times. Because of that this city was destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezra 4:20; 1 Kings 4:24; 2 Samuel 8:3"> We make known to the king that if this city is built and the walls are finished, you will have nothing in the province Beyond the River.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ezra 5:7; Ezra 4:9–11"> The king sent a reply: “To Rehum the royal officer, Shimshai the scribe, and the rest of their associates who live in Samaria and the rest of the province Beyond the River, greetings. And now</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:8"> the letter that you sent to us has been translated and read before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 18:7; Proverbs 25:2; Deuteronomy 13:14"> And ⸤I issued⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “by my means made”">f</a></sup>a decree, and they searched and found that this city from ancient days revolted against kings, and rebellion and sedition has been made in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 4:21; 1 Chronicles 18:3"> Mighty kings have ruled over Jerusalem, governing all the province Beyond the River, to whom tribute, duty, and tax has been given.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So now, issue forth a decree that these men stop and this city not be built, until a decree is issued from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esther 7:3–4; Esther 3:8–9; Ezra 4:13"> And be careful not to be negligent on this matter. Why should damage grow to hurt kings?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Micah 2:1; Ezra 4:17"> Then when a copy of the letter of King Artaxerxes was read before Rehum, Shimshai the scribe, and their associates they returned in a hurry to Jerusalem against the Jews and they stopped them by force and power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Haggai 1:15; Nehemiah 6:9; Ezra 5:5"> Then the work on the house of God in Jerusalem stopped, and was discontinued until the second year of the reign of Darius king of Persia.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
