## Servants for the Temple are Gathered

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezra 8:21; Ezra 8:31; Ezra 7:7"> I gathered them by the river that goes to Ahava and we camped there for three days. And I looked at the people and the priests, I found no one there from the sons of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 2:6; Ezra 8:18; Ezra 10:39"> And I sent for Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam, who were family heads, and for Jehoiarib and Elnathan who were wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 2:43; Exodus 4:15; Numbers 8:22–26"> I sent them to Iddo, the head of the place called Casiphia, and I placed in their mouths what words to say to Iddo and his brothers the temple servants in Casiphia, to send to us ministers for the house of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; Ezra 7:28; Nehemiah 2:8"> With the good hand of our God before us, they brought us a man of understanding from the sons of Mahli, son of Levi, son of Israel—Sherebiah and his sons and brothers, eighteen in total.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16; Nehemiah 10:11"> Also Hashabiah and with him Jeshaiah, the sons of Merari, with his brothers and their sons, twenty in total.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 2:43; Ezra 7:7; 1 Chronicles 9:2"> And from the temple servants, whom David and his officials had set up to serve the Levites, two hundred and twenty in total. All of them were registered by name.</span>

## Ezra Proclaims a Fast

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 1:14; 1 Samuel 7:6"> I proclaimed a fast there at the river Ahava to humble ourselves before our God in order to seek from him a safe journey for us, our children, and our possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Psalm 33:18–19; 2 Chronicles 16:9"> For I was ashamed to ask the king for troops and horses to protect us from enemies on the way because we said to the king, “the hand of our God is favorable to all who seek him, but his strength and anger are against all who forsake them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Matthew 7:7–8; Ezra 8:31"> So we fasted and sought our God for this and he responded to our prayer.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Priests’ Gift for the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 8:18–19"> I set apart twelve of the official priests: Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten of their brothers with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 8:33; Ezra 7:14–16; Ezra 1:8"> Then I weighed out to them silver and gold and the vessels, the offering of the house of our God that the king, his counselors, his commanders, and all of Israel who was present offered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezra 1:9–11"> I weighed out into their hand six hundred and fifty talents of silver, one hundred vessels of silver, one hundred talents of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Lamentations 4:2"> twenty gold bowls worth one thousand darics, and two vessels of good polished bronze as precious as gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 22:2–3; Leviticus 21:6–8; 1 Chronicles 23:28"> And I said to them, “You are holy to Yahweh and the vessels are holy. The silver and gold are a freewill offering to Yahweh, God of your ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “fathers”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezra 8:33–34; Mark 13:34–35; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26"> Guard and look after them until you weigh them before the chief priests, the Levites, and the heads of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">v</a></sup>of Israel in Jerusalem, in the chamber of the house of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezra 8:22; 1 Chronicles 29:2–3; Isaiah 60:13"> So the priests and Levites took over the weighed silver, gold, and the vessels to bring to Jerusalem for the house of our God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Return to Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezra 8:21–22; Ezra 7:9; Ezra 8:15"> On the twelfth day of the first month we set out from the river of Ahava to go to Jerusalem. The hand of our God was on us and he delivered us from the hand of the enemy and from ambushing on the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:11; Ezra 7:8–9"> We came to Jerusalem and remained there three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:4; Nehemiah 3:21; Ezra 8:30"> On the fourth day, the silver, gold, and vessels were weighed in the house of our God into the hand of priest Meremoth, the son of Uriah, and with him Eleazar son of Phinehas. With them were the Levites, Jehozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> The amount and weight of all the weighed items was recorded at that time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezra 6:17; 2 Chronicles 29:31–32; Psalm 66:10–15"> The exiles that returned from captivity offered burnt offerings to the God of Israel; twelve bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, and twelve goats as a sin offering. All was a burnt offering to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezra 7:21–24; Ezra 4:7–23; Ezra 6:13"> They delivered the customs of the king to the king’s satraps and to the governor of the province Beyond the River. And they supported the people of the house of God.</span>
</div>
