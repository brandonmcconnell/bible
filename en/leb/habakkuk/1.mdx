<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 5:4–5; Psalm 34:15–16; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your eyes are too pure to see evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you are not able to look at wrongdoing.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “trouble”">o</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why do you look at the treacherous?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why are you silent when the wicked swallows up</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">someone more righteous than him?</span></span>
