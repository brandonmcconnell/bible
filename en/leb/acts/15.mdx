## The Jerusalem Council

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; Acts 15:5; Leviticus 12:3"> And some men came down from Judea and began teaching the brothers, “Unless you are circumcised according to the custom prescribed by Moses, you cannot be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 15:22–23; Acts 15:6–7; Galatians 2:1–2"> And after there was no little strife and debate by Paul and Barnabas against them, they appointed Paul and Barnabas and some others from among them to go up to the apostles and elders in Jerusalem concerning this issue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 21:5; 1 Corinthians 16:11"> So they were sent on their way by the church, and passed through both Phoenicia and Samaria, telling in detail the conversion of the Gentiles and bringing great joy to all the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:12; Acts 15:3"> And when they arrived in Jerusalem, they were received by the church and the apostles and the elders, and reported all that God had done with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:1–3; Philippians 3:5–8; Acts 15:1"> But some of those who had believed from the party of the Pharisees stood up, saying, “It is necessary to circumcise them and to command them to observe the law of Moses!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 15:22; Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17"> Both the apostles and the elders assembled to deliberate concerning this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Galatians 2:7–9; 1 Chronicles 28:4–5"> And after there was much debate, Peter stood up and said to them, “Men and brothers, you know that in the early days<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “from ancient days”">a</a></sup>God chose among you through my mouth that the Gentiles should hear the message of the gospel and believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:24; Acts 10:47; Acts 14:3"> And God, who knows the heart, testified to them by giving them the Holy Spirit, just as he also did to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; Galatians 5:6; Acts 10:28"> And he made no distinction between us and them, cleansing their hearts by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; Matthew 23:4; Galatians 4:9"> So now why are you putting God to the test by placing on the neck of the disciples a yoke that neither our fathers nor we have been able to bear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Titus 3:4–7; Titus 2:11"> But we believe we will be saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus in ⸤the same⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “which”">b</a></sup>way those also are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 15:4; Acts 21:19"> And the whole group became silent and listened to Barnabas and Paul describing all the signs and wonders God had done among the Gentiles through them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Galatians 2:12; James 1:1"> And after they had stopped speaking, James answered, saying, “Men and brothers, listen to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Peter 1:1; Acts 15:7–9; Luke 2:31–32"> Simeon has described how God first concerned himself to take from among the Gentiles a people for his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:8–12; Acts 13:47"> And with this the words of the prophets agree, just as it is written:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 9:11–12; Psalm 89:35–49; Luke 1:31–33"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘After these things I will return</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and build up again the tent of David that has fallen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the parts of it that had been torn down I will build up again</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will restore it,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 2:11; Zechariah 8:20–23; Isaiah 65:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">so that the rest of humanity may seek the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even all the Gentiles</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The same Greek word can be translated “nations” or “Gentiles” depending on the context">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤who are called by my name⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “on whom my name has been called on them”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">says the Lord,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Amos 9:11–12">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who makes these things</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; Ephesians 3:9; Isaiah 46:9–10"> <br /><span className="pl pl-2">known from of old.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The last phrase of v. 17 and all of v. 18 is an allusion to Isa 45:21">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 15:28; Galatians 1:7–10; Acts 15:24"> Therefore I conclude we should not cause difficulty for those from among the Gentiles who turn to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Revelation 2:20; Leviticus 3:17"> but we should write a letter to them to abstain from the pollution of idols and from sexual immorality and from what has been strangled and from blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:15; Acts 13:27; 2 Corinthians 3:14–15"> For Moses has those who proclaim him in every city from ancient generations, because he is read aloud in the synagogues on every Sabbath.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Letter from the Council

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:27; Acts 15:40; 1 Thessalonians 1:1"> Then it seemed best to the apostles and the elders, together with the whole church, to send men chosen from among them to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas—Judas who was called Barsabbas and Silas, men who were leaders among the brothers—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 23:26; James 1:1; Galatians 1:21"> writing this letter ⸤to be delivered by them⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “by their hand”">g</a></sup><br />The apostles and the elders, brothers.<br />To the brothers who are from among the Gentiles in Antioch and Syria and Cilicia. Greetings!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Galatians 5:12; Galatians 5:10; Acts 15:1"> Because we have heard that some have gone out from among us—to whom we gave no orders—and have thrown you into confusion by words upsetting your ⸤minds⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “souls”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Peter 3:15; Acts 1:14; Acts 15:2"> it seemed best to us, ⸤having reached a unanimous decision⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “having become of one mind”">i</a></sup>and having chosen men, to send them to you together with our dear friends Barnabas and Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 9:23–25; Philippians 2:29–30"> men who have risked their lives on behalf of the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 15:32; 3 John 1:13"> Therefore we have sent Judas and Silas, and they will report the same things by word of mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 16:13; Revelation 2:24; Matthew 11:30"> For it seemed best to the Holy Spirit and to us to place on you no greater burden except these necessary things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 15:20; Acts 21:25; Revelation 2:14"> that you abstain from food sacrificed to idols, and from blood, and from what has been strangled, and from sexual immorality. If you keep yourselves from ⸤these things⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “which things”">j</a></sup>you will do well. Farewell.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Letter Is Delivered to Antioch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 16:4; Acts 6:2; Acts 23:33"> So when they were sent off, they came down to Antioch, and after calling together the community, they delivered the letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 2:4–5; Philippians 3:3; Galatians 5:1"> And when they read it aloud, they rejoiced at the encouragement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:1"> Both Judas and Silas, who were also prophets themselves, encouraged and strengthened the brothers by a long message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 16:36; Hebrews 11:31"> And after spending some time, they were sent away in peace from the brothers to those who had sent them.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A few later manuscripts add v. 34 , “But Silas decided to stay there.”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Colossians 1:28; Acts 13:1"> But Paul and Barnabas remained in Antioch teaching and proclaiming the word of the Lord with many others also.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul and Barnabas Disagree and Part Company

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 14:6; Acts 13:4; Acts 13:51"> And after some days, Paul said to Barnabas, “Come then,let us return and visit the brothers in every town in which we proclaimed the word of the Lord, to see how they are doing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 13:5"> Now Barnabas wanted to take John who was called Mark along also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:13; Proverbs 25:19; Luke 14:27–34"> but Paul held the opinion they should not take this one along, who departed from them in Pamphylia and did not accompany them in the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Colossians 4:10; Ecclesiastes 7:20; Acts 4:36"> And a sharp disagreement took place, so that they separated from one another. And Barnabas took along Mark and sailed away to Cyprus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 14:26; Acts 11:23"> but Paul chose Silas and departed, after being commended to the grace of the Lord by the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Galatians 1:21; Acts 15:32"> And he traveled through Syria and Cilicia, strengthening the churches.</span>
</div>
</div>
