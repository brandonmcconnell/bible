## Paul Tells of His Conversion on the Damascus Road

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:3–8; Acts 26:12–18; Revelation 1:16"> “And it happened that as I was traveling and approaching Damascus around noon, suddenly a very bright light from heaven flashed around me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:9; Matthew 25:45; Genesis 22:11"> and I fell to the ground and heard a voice saying to me, ‘Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting me?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:9; 1 Samuel 8:7; Exodus 16:7–8"> And I answered, ‘Who are you, Lord?’ And he said to me, ‘I am Jesus the Nazarene whom you are persecuting.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Daniel 10:7; Acts 26:13"> (Now those who were with me saw the light but did not hear the voice of the one who was speaking to me.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 22:12–16; Acts 2:37"> So I said, ‘What should I do, Lord?’ And the Lord said to me, ‘Get up and proceed to Damascus, and there it will be told to you about all the things that have been appointed for you to do.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 9:8–9; Acts 13:11; Isaiah 42:16"> And as I could not see as a result of the brightness of that light, I arrived in Damascus led by the hand of those who were with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:22; Acts 6:3; 3 John 1:12"> And a certain Ananias, a devout man according to the law, well spoken of by all the Jews who live there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:17–18; Philemon 1:16"> came to me and stood by me and said to me, ‘Brother Saul, regain your sight!’ And at that same time I looked up at him and saw him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Corinthians 9:1"> And he said, ‘The God of our fathers has appointed you to know his will, and to see the Righteous One and to hear a voice from his mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Acts 4:20; John 15:27"> because you will be a witness for him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “to him”">c</a></sup>to all people of what you have seen and heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 2:21; Titus 3:5"> And now why are you delaying? Get up, be baptized, and wash away your sins, calling on his name!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:1–4; Acts 10:9–10; Acts 9:26–28"> “And it happened that when I returned to Jerusalem and I was praying in the temple courts, I was in a trance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:29; Luke 21:21; Ezekiel 3:6–7"> and saw him saying to me, ‘Hurry and depart ⸤quickly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “with quickness”">d</a></sup>from Jerusalem, because they will not accept your testimony about me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 22:4; Matthew 10:17; Acts 8:3"> And I said, ‘Lord, they themselves know that from synagogue to synagogue I was imprisoning and beating those who believed in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Acts 8:1; Romans 1:32"> And when the blood of your witness Stephen was being shed, I myself also was standing near and was approving, and was guarding the cloaks of those who were killing him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:6–8"> And he said to me, ‘Go, because I will send you far away to the Gentiles!’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Questioned by the Military Tribune

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 25:24; Acts 21:36; John 19:15"> Now they were listening to him until this word, and they raised their voices, saying, “Away with such a man from the earth! For it is not fitting for him to live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:13; Acts 26:11; Ecclesiastes 10:3"> And while they were screaming and throwing off their cloaks and throwing dust into the air,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:31–32; Acts 21:34; John 19:1"> the military tribune ordered him to be brought into the barracks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “headquarters”">e</a></sup>saying he was to be examined with a lash so that he could find out for what reason they were crying out against him in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:37; Acts 25:16; Acts 27:43"> But when they had stretched him out for the lash,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “with straps” (in order to lash him)">f</a></sup>Paul said to the centurion standing there, “Is it permitted for you to flog a man who is a Roman citizen and uncondemned?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Acts 22:29"> And when the centurion heard this, he went to the military tribune and reported it, saying, “What are you about to do? For this man is a Roman citizen!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> So the military tribune came and said to him, “Tell me, are you a Roman citizen?” And he said, “Yes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the military tribune replied, “I acquired this citizenship for a large sum of money.” And Paul said, “But I indeed was born a citizen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Acts 22:24–26; Acts 16:38–39"> Then immediately those who were about to examine him kept away from him, and the military tribune also was afraid when he realized that he was a Roman citizen and that ⸤he had tied him up⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “he was having tied him up”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 23:28; Acts 21:33; Acts 21:11"> But on the next day, because he wanted to know the true reason why he was being accused by the Jews, he released him and ordered the chief priests and the whole Sanhedrin to assemble, and he brought down Paul and had him stand before them.</span>
</div>
