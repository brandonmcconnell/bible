## Attacked by a Mob in Thessalonica

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:1; Philippians 4:16; Acts 20:4"> Now after they traveled through Amphipolis and Apollonia, they came to Thessalonica, where there was a synagogue of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 9:20; Acts 17:17; Acts 18:4"> ⸤And as was his custom⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and in accordance with what he was accustomed to”">a</a></sup>Paul went in to them and on three Sabbath days he discussed with them from the scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:28; Acts 9:22; Luke 24:32"> explaining and demonstrating that it was necessary for the Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “Messiah”">b</a></sup>to suffer and to rise from the dead, and saying, “This Jesus whom I am proclaiming to you is the Christ.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “Messiah”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:43; Acts 14:4"> And some of them were persuaded and joined Paul and Silas, and also a large number of God-fearing Greeks and not a few of the prominent women.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:26; Acts 17:13; Romans 16:21"> But the Jews were filled with jealousy and, taking along some worthless men from the rabble in the marketplace and forming a mob, threw the city into an uproar. And attacking Jason’s house, they were looking for them to bring them out to the popular assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:5; Acts 16:19–21; Acts 28:22"> And when they did not find them, they dragged Jason and some brothers before the city officials, shouting, “These people who have stirred up trouble throughout the world<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “empire”">d</a></sup>have come here also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:2; Acts 16:21; John 19:12"> whom Jason has entertained as guests! And these people are all acting contrary to the decrees of Caesar, saying there is another king, Jesus!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; John 11:48"> And they threw the crowd into confusion, and the city officials who heard these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 17:5"> And after taking money as security from Jason and the rest, they released them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul and Silas in Berea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:2; Acts 17:13–14"> Now the brothers sent away both Paul and Silas at once, during the night, to Berea. ⸤They⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “who” (referring to Paul and Silas)">e</a></sup>went into the synagogue of the Jews when they arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 34:16; Proverbs 2:1–5; John 5:39"> Now these were more open-minded than those in Thessalonica. ⸤They⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “who”">f</a></sup>accepted the message with all eagerness, examining the scriptures every day to see if these things were so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:14; Acts 14:1; Acts 13:50"> Therefore many of them believed, and not a few of the prominent Greek women and men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14–16; Proverbs 28:25; Acts 6:12"> But when the Jews from Thessalonica found out that the message of God had been proclaimed by Paul in Berea also, they came there too, inciting and stirring up the crowds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 17:10; Matthew 10:23; Acts 9:25"> So then the brothers sent Paul away at once to go to the sea, and both Silas and Timothy remained there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 18:5; 1 Thessalonians 3:1; Acts 18:1"> And those who conducted Paul brought him as far as Athens, and after receiving an order for Silas and Timothy that they should come to him as soon as possible, they went away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul in Athens

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 119:158; 2 Peter 2:7; Psalm 119:136"> Now while Paul was waiting for them in Athens, his spirit was provoked within him when he observed the city was full of idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 13:16; Acts 14:1–4; Acts 17:2–4"> So he was discussing in the synagogue with the Jews and the God-fearing Gentiles, and in the marketplace every day with those who happened to be there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; Colossians 2:8; Luke 11:53"> And even some of the Epicurean and Stoic philosophers were conversing with him, and some were saying, “What does this babbler want to say?” But others said, “He appears to be a proclaimer of foreign deities,” because he was proclaiming the good news about Jesus and the resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:27; Acts 17:22; Acts 24:24"> And they took hold of him and brought him to the Areopagus, saying, “May we learn what is this new teaching being proclaimed by you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Hosea 8:12; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> For you are bringing some astonishing things to our ears. Therefore we want to know what ⸤these things mean⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “these things want to be”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16–17; Colossians 4:5; Ephesians 5:16"> (Now all the Athenians and the foreigners who stayed there used to spend their time in nothing else than telling something or listening to something new.)</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Speaks to the Areopagus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 25:19; Acts 19:35; Acts 17:16"> So Paul stood there in the middle of the Areopagus and said, “Men of Athens, I see you are very religious ⸤in every respect⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “with respect to all things ”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 4:22; Galatians 4:8–9; John 17:3"> For as I was passing through and observing carefully your objects of worship, I even found an altar on which was inscribed, ‘To an unknown God.’ Therefore what you worship without knowing it, this I proclaim to you—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Deuteronomy 10:14; Acts 7:48"> the God who made the world and all the things in it. This one, being Lord of heaven and earth, does not live in temples made by human hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 50:8–13; Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 42:5"> nor is he served by human hands as if he needed anything, because he himself gives to everyone life and breath and everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7–8; Genesis 3:20; Romans 5:12–19"> And he made from one man every nation of humanity to live on all the face of the earth, determining their fixed times and the fixed boundaries of their habitation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:7; Acts 14:17"> to search for God, if perhaps indeed they might feel around for him and find him. And indeed he is not far away from each one of us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 12:10; Colossians 1:17; Hebrews 1:3"> for in him we live and move and exist,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some interpreters hold that the phrase “in him we live and move and exist” is a quotation from Epimenides of Crete, but more likely it is a traditional Greek formula">i</a></sup>as even some of ⸤your own⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “with respect to you”">j</a></sup>poets have said: ‘For we also are ⸤his⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “of him ”">k</a></sup>offspring.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A quotation from Aratus, Phaenomena 5">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 40:25; Exodus 20:4; Psalm 115:4–8"> Therefore, because we are offspring of God, we ought not to think the divine being is like gold or silver or stone, an image formed by human skill and thought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Titus 2:11–12; Luke 24:47"> Therefore although God has overlooked the times of ignorance, he now commands all people everywhere to repent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:31–46; 2 Peter 3:7; Romans 2:16"> because he has set a day on which he is going to judge the world in righteousness by the man who he has appointed, having provided proof to everyone by raising him from the dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 17:18; Acts 26:8; Acts 25:19"> Now when they heard about the resurrection of the dead, some scoffed, but others said, “We will hear you about this again also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> So Paul went out from the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 17:19; Romans 11:5–6; Acts 13:48"> But some people joined him and believed, among whom also were Dionysius the Areopagite and a woman ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “by name”">m</a></sup>Damaris and others with them.</span>
</div>
</div>
