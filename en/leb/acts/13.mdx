## Barnabas and Saul Sent Out from Antioch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 15:35; Acts 11:22–27"> Now there were prophets and teachers in Antioch in the church that was there: Barnabas, and Simeon (who was called Niger), and Lucius the Cyrenian, and Manaen (a close friend of Herod the tetrarch), and Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 1:15; Acts 9:15; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> And while they were serving the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, “Set apart now for me Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 2:2; Acts 13:2"> Then, after they had fasted and prayed and placed their hands on them, they sent them away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Confronting a Magician on Cyprus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 13:2; Acts 11:19"> Therefore, sent out by the Holy Spirit, they came down to Seleucia, and from there they sailed away to Cyprus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:14; Acts 13:46; Acts 14:1"> And when they came to Salamis, they began to proclaim the word of God in the synagogues of the Jews. And they also had John as assistant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 1 John 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13"> And when they had crossed over the whole island as far as Paphos, they found a certain man, a magician, a Jewish false prophet whose name was Bar-Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:38; Acts 13:12; Acts 18:12"> who was with the proconsul Sergius Paulus, an intelligent man. This man summoned Barnabas and Saul and wished to hear the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Acts 13:6–7; 2 Timothy 4:14–15"> But Elymas the magician (for his name is translated in this way) opposed them, attempting to turn the proconsul away from the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Micah 3:8; Acts 4:8; Acts 2:4"> But Saul (also called Paul), filled with the Holy Spirit, looked intently at him</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 13:38"> and said, “O you who are full of all deceit and of all unscrupulousness, you son of the devil, you enemy of all righteousness! Will you not stop making crooked the straight paths of the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 9:3; Psalm 32:4; Hebrews 10:31"> And now behold, the hand of the Lord is against you, and you will be blind, not seeing the sun ⸤for a while⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “until the time”">a</a></sup>And immediately mist and darkness fell over him, and he was going around looking for people to lead him by the hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Acts 13:7; Luke 4:22"> Then when the proconsul saw what had happened, he believed, because he was astounded at the teaching about the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Preaching in the Synagogue at Pisidian Antioch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 15:38; Acts 2:10; Acts 14:24–25"> Now ⸤Paul and his companions⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “those around Paul”">b</a></sup>put out to sea from Paphos and came to Perga in Pamphylia, but John departed from them and returned to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:13; Acts 14:19; Acts 13:5"> And they went on from Perga and arrived at Pisidian Antioch. And they entered into the synagogue on the day of the Sabbath and sat down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 15:21; Mark 5:22"> So after the reading from the law and the prophets, the rulers of the synagogue sent word to them, saying, “Men and brothers, if there is any message of exhortation by you for the people, say it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Acts 13:26; Acts 10:2"> So Paul stood up, and motioning with his hand, he said, “Israelite men, and those who fear God, listen!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6–8; Isaiah 63:9–14; Psalm 105:23–24"> The God of this people Israel chose our fathers and exalted the people during their stay in the land of Egypt, and with uplifted arm he led them out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Psalm 95:8–11; Psalm 106:13–29"> And for a period of time of about forty years, he put up with them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Psalm 78:55; Acts 7:45"> And after destroying seven nations in the land of Canaan, he gave their land to his people as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 2:16; 1 Samuel 3:20; 2 Kings 23:22"> This took about four hundred and fifty years. And after these things, he gave them judges until Samuel the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; 1 Samuel 15:1; 1 Samuel 12:12–19"> And then they asked for a king, and God gave them Saul son of Kish, a man from the tribe of Benjamin, for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Kings 15:5; Psalm 78:70–72"> And after removing him, he raised up David for their king, about whom he also said, testifying, ‘I have found David the son of Jesse to be a man in accordance with my heart, who will carry out all my will.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from 1 Sam 13:14">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:1; Psalm 132:11; Revelation 22:16"> From the descendants of this man, according to his promise, God brought to Israel a Savior, Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 1:22; John 1:6–8; Luke 3:2–20"> Before ⸤his coming⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the presence of his coming”">d</a></sup>John had publicly proclaimed a baptism of repentance to all the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Mark 1:7; John 1:26–27"> But while John was completing his mission, he said, ‘What do you suppose me to be? I am not he! But behold, one is coming after me of whom I am not worthy to untie the sandals of his feet!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:77; Romans 1:16; Luke 1:69"> “Men and brothers, sons of the family of Abraham and those among you who fear God—to us the message of this salvation has been sent!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 3:17; Luke 24:24–27; Luke 24:20"> For those who live in Jerusalem and their rulers, because they did not recognize this one, and the voices of the prophets that are read on every Sabbath, fulfilled them by condemning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 23:21–25; Luke 23:14–16; John 19:4"> And although they found no charge worthy of death, they asked Pilate that he be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 23:53; John 19:28; John 19:30"> And when they had carried out all the things that were written about him, they took him down from the tree and placed him in a tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Acts 17:31; Matthew 28:6"> But God raised him from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:3; Acts 1:11"> who appeared for many days to those who had come up with him from Galilee to Jerusalem—who are now his witnesses to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Acts 26:6; Ezekiel 34:23"> And we proclaim the good news to you: that the promise that was made to the fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 2:7; Hebrews 5:5; Hebrews 1:5–6"> this promise<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="It is necessary to repeat the word “promise ” from the previous verse for clarity here">e</a></sup>God has fulfilled to our children<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “to us their children”">f</a></sup>by raising Jesus, as it is also written in the second psalm,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You are my Son;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">today I have fathered you.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 2:7">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; Psalm 89:2–4; Romans 6:9"> But that he has raised him from the dead, no more going to return to decay, he has spoken in this way: ‘I will give you the reliable divine decrees of David.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 55:3">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalm 16:10; Acts 2:27–31; Psalm 49:9"> Therefore he also says in another psalm,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You will not permit your Holy One to experience decay.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 16:10">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 2:29; 2 Samuel 7:12; 1 Kings 2:10"> For David, after serving the purpose of God in his own generation, fell asleep and ⸤was buried with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “was gathered to”">j</a></sup>his fathers, and experienced decay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 13:30; Acts 2:24"> But he whom God raised up did not experience decay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 24:47; 1 John 2:12; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> “Therefore let it be known to you, men and brothers, that through this one forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you, and from all the things from which you were not able to be justified by the law of Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Romans 10:4; Galatians 2:16; Romans 8:3"> by this one everyone who believes is justified!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Hebrews 3:12; Malachi 3:2"> Watch out, therefore, lest what is stated by the prophets come upon you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; 1 Peter 4:17; Romans 11:7–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Look, you scoffers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and be astonished and perish!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For I am doing a work in your days,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a work that you would never believe</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">even if someone were to tell it to you.’ ”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A quotation from Hab 1:5">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Response to the Message in Pisidian Antioch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Acts 10:33; Matthew 19:30"> And as they were going out, they began urging that these things be spoken about to them on the next Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 11:23; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 2:8"> And after the synagogue had broken up, many of the Jews and the devout<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “God-fearing”">l</a></sup>proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas, who were speaking to them and were persuading them to continue in the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalm 110:3; Genesis 49:10; Isaiah 11:10"> And on the coming Sabbath, nearly the whole city came together to hear the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Jude 1:10; Acts 18:6; Acts 5:17"> But when the Jews saw the crowds, they were filled with jealousy, and began contradicting what was being said by Paul by reviling him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 28:28; Matthew 21:43; Acts 18:5–6"> Both Paul and Barnabas spoke boldly and said, “It was necessary that the word of God be spoken first to you, since you reject it and do not consider yourselves worthy of eternal life! Behold, we are turning to the Gentiles!</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Isaiah 42:6; Luke 2:32"> For so the Lord has commanded us:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I have appointed you ⸤to be⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “for”">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a light for the Gentiles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤that you would bring⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “that you would bring”">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">salvation to the end of the earth.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="An allusion to Isa 42:6 ; 49:6">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:4; Romans 11:7"> And when the Gentiles heard this, they began to rejoice and to glorify the word of the Lord. And all those who were designated for eternal life believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 19:10; Acts 19:26"> So the word of the Lord was carried through the whole region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 13:45; Acts 14:2"> But the Jews incited the devout women of high social standing and the most prominent men of the city, and stirred up persecution against Paul and Barnabas and threw them out of their district.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Matthew 10:14; Acts 18:6; Luke 9:5"> So after shaking off the dust from their feet against them, they went to Iconium.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Romans 15:13; 1 Thessalonians 1:6; Acts 4:31"> And the disciples were filled with joy and with the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>
