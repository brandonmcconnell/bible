<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:11; Luke 17:20; Daniel 7:27"> So when they had come together, they began asking him, saying, “Lord, is it at this time you are restoring the kingdom to Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–2; Mark 13:32"> But he said to them, “It is not for you to know the times or seasons that the Father has set by his own authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:15; Romans 15:19"> But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the farthest part of the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Luke 24:50–51; Mark 16:19"> And after he had said these things, while they were watching, he was taken up, and a cloud received him from their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:4; John 20:12; Revelation 7:14"> And as they were staring into the sky while he was departing, behold, two men in white clothing stood by them</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:26"> who also said, “Men ⸤of Galilee⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “Galileans”">d</a></sup>why do you stand there looking<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “gazing”">e</a></sup>into the sky? This Jesus who was taken up from you into heaven like this will come back in the same way you saw him departing into heaven!”</span>

## Matthias Chosen to Replace Judas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:52; Matthew 21:1; Luke 24:50"> Then they returned to Jerusalem from the mountain that is called Olive Grove<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This is a variation of the name “Mount of Olives”">f</a></sup>which is near Jerusalem, ⸤a Sabbath day’s journey away⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “having a journey of a Sabbath”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:8; Luke 22:12; Mark 14:15"> And when they had entered, they went up to the upstairs room where they were staying—Peter and John and James and Andrew, Philip and Thomas, Bartholomew and Matthew, James son of Alphaeus and Simon the Zealot and Judas son of James.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Ephesians 6:18; Colossians 4:2"> All these were busily engaged with one mind in prayer, together with the women and Mary the mother of Jesus and with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “with”">h</a></sup>his brothers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:6; Acts 21:20; Revelation 3:4"> And in those days Peter stood up in the midst of the brothers (and it was a crowd of persons of about one hundred twenty at the same place) and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 41:9; John 13:18; Psalm 55:12–15"> “Men and brothers, it was necessary that the scripture be fulfilled, which the Holy Spirit proclaimed beforehand through the mouth of David concerning Judas, who became a guide to those who arrested Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 1:25; Mark 3:19; John 17:12"> because he was counted among us and received a share in this ministry.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Matthew 26:14–15; Psalm 55:23"> (Now this man acquired a field for the wages of his wickedness, and falling headlong, he burst open in the middle and all his intestines spilled out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:22; Matthew 28:15; Acts 21:40"> And it became known to all who live in Jerusalem, so that that field was called in their own language<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, Aramaic">i</a></sup>“Akeldama,” that is, “Field of Blood.”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 69:25; Psalm 109:8–15; Acts 1:25"> “For it is written in the book of Psalms,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Let his residence become deserted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let there be no one to live in it,’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 69:25">j</a></sup><br />and,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Let another person take his position.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 109:8">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 15:27; Luke 10:1–2; Numbers 27:17"> Therefore it is necessary for one of the men who have accompanied us during all the time in which the Lord Jesus went in and went out among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:2; Acts 4:33; Acts 1:8–9"> beginning from the baptism of John until the day on which he was taken up from us—one of these men must become a witness of his resurrection together with us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 1:26"> And they proposed two men, Joseph called Barsabbas (who was called Justus) and Matthias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Acts 15:8; Hebrews 4:13"> And they prayed and said, “You, Lord, who know the hearts of all, show clearly which one of these two you have chosen</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:2; John 6:70–71; John 17:12"> to take the place in this ministry and apostleship from which Judas turned aside to depart to his own place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:8; Joshua 18:10; 1 Samuel 14:41–42"> And they cast lots for them, and the lot fell on Matthias, and he was added to serve with the eleven apostles.</span>
</div>
</div>
