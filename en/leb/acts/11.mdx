## Peter’s Explanation to the Church in Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 22:27; Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 32:15"> Now the apostles and the brothers who were throughout Judea heard that the Gentiles also had accepted the word<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “message”">a</a></sup>of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 10:9; Acts 15:1"> So when Peter went up to Jerusalem, those of the circumcision took issue with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Galatians 2:12; Luke 15:2"> saying, “You went to men ⸤who were uncircumcised⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “who had uncircumcision">b</a></sup>and ate with them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Acts 14:27; Joshua 22:21–31"> But Peter began and explained it to them in an orderly sequence, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 10:9–32; Acts 9:10; Acts 22:17"> “I was in the city of Joppa praying, and in a trance I saw a vision—an object something like a large sheet coming down, being let down from heaven by its four corners, and it came to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 4:20; Acts 3:4"> As I looked intently into it, I was considering it, and I saw the four-footed animals of the earth and the wild animals and the reptiles and the birds of the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And I also heard a voice saying to me, ‘Get up, Peter, slaughter and eat!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 14:14; Hosea 9:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14"> But I said, ‘Certainly not, Lord! For nothing common or unclean has ever entered into my mouth!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:9; Hebrews 9:13–14; Acts 10:34–35"> But the voice replied from heaven for the second time, ‘The things which God has made clean, you must not consider unclean!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 21:17; Numbers 24:10; 2 Corinthians 12:8"> And this happened three times, and everything was pulled up into heaven again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 4:27; Acts 9:10–12; Exodus 4:14"> And behold, at once three men who had been sent to me from Caesarea approached the house in which we were staying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Romans 3:22; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> And the Spirit told me to accompany them, not hesitating at all. So these six brothers also went with me, and we entered into the man’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:30–32; Acts 10:3–6; Acts 9:43"> And he reported to us how he had seen the angel standing in his house and saying, ‘Send to Joppa and summon Simon, who is also called Peter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 16:15; Acts 10:22; Acts 16:31–34"> who will speak words to you by which you will be saved, you and all your household.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 10:34–46; Acts 2:2–12"> And as I was beginning to speak, the Holy Spirit fell on them, just as also on us at the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Mark 1:8; Proverbs 1:23"> And I remembered the word of the Lord, how he said, ‘John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="An allusion to Acts 1:5">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:47; Romans 9:20–26; Daniel 4:35"> Therefore if God gave them the same gift as also to us when we believed in the Lord Jesus Christ, who was I to be able to hinder God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Ezekiel 36:26"> And when they heard these things, they became silent and praised God, saying, “Then God has granted the repentance leading to life to the Gentiles also!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Developments in the Church in Antioch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 21:2; Acts 15:3"> Now those who had been scattered because of the persecution that took place over Stephen traveled as far as Phoenicia and Cyprus and Antioch, proclaiming the message to no one except Jews alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 1:23–24"> But some of them were men from Cyprus and Cyrene, who, when they came to Antioch, began to speak to the Hellenists also, proclaiming the good news about the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:66; Acts 2:47; Acts 5:14"> And the hand of the Lord was with them, and a large number who believed turned to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:27; Acts 8:14; Acts 13:1–3"> ⸤And the report came to the attention⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “and the report was heard in the ears”">d</a></sup>of the church that was in Jerusalem about them, and they sent out Barnabas as far as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “to go as far as”">e</a></sup>Antioch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Acts 14:22; Acts 13:43"> who, when he arrived and saw the grace of God, rejoiced and encouraged them all to remain true to the Lord with ⸤devoted hearts⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “purpose of heart”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Acts 5:14; Acts 9:31"> because he was a good man and full of the Holy Spirit and of faith. And a large number were added<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “were brought”">g</a></sup>to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 9:11; Acts 9:27; Acts 9:30"> So he departed for Tarsus to look for Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 4:16; Acts 26:28; 1 Peter 4:14"> And when he found him, he brought him to Antioch. And it happened to them also that they met together for a whole year with the church and taught a large number of people. And in Antioch the disciples were first called Christians.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 13:1; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Ephesians 4:11"> Now in those days prophets came down from Jerusalem to Antioch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 21:10; Acts 18:2; Luke 3:1"> And one of them ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “by name”">h</a></sup>Agabus stood up and indicated by the Spirit that a great famine was about to come over the whole inhabited earth (which took place in the time of Claudius).</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12–14; Galatians 2:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2–4"> So from the disciples, ⸤according to their ability to give⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to the degree that anyone was prospering”">i</a></sup>each one of them determined to send financial aid for support to the brothers who lived in Judea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 14:23; Acts 12:25; Acts 15:4"> which they also did, sending the aid to the elders by the hand of Barnabas and Saul.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
