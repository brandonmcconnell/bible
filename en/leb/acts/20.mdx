## Paul Travels Through Macedonia and Greece

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5; Acts 19:21; 1 Timothy 1:3"> Now after the turmoil had ceased, Paul summoned the disciples, and after encouraging them, he said farewell and departed to travel to Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:1; Colossians 1:28"> And after he had gone through those regions and encouraged them ⸤at length⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “with many a word”">a</a></sup>he came to Greece</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:19; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 9:23–24"> and stayed three months. Because a plot was made against him by the Jews as he was about to set sail for Syria, he came to a decision to return through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:29; Ephesians 6:21; 2 Timothy 4:20"> And Sopater son of Pyrrhus from Berea, and Aristarchus and Secundus from Thessalonica, and Gaius from Derbe, and Timothy, and Tychicus and Trophimus from Asia, were accompanying him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 16:10–11; 2 Timothy 4:13"> And these had gone on ahead and were waiting for us in Troas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:3; Acts 16:12; Exodus 23:15"> And we sailed away from Philippi after the days of Unleavened Bread and came to them at Troas within five days, where we stayed seven days.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Eutychus Falls from a Window

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:2; Acts 2:42; John 20:19"> And on the first day of the week, when we had assembled to break bread, Paul began conversing with them, because he was going to leave on the next day, and he extended his message until midnight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 22:12"> Now there were quite a few lamps in the upstairs room where we were gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 13:36; Matthew 26:40–41; Jonah 1:5–6"> And a certain young man ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “by name”">b</a></sup>Eutychus who was sitting in the window was sinking into a deep sleep while Paul was conversing at length. Being overcome by sleep, he fell down from the third story and was picked up dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 5:39; Matthew 9:23–24; 1 Kings 17:21–22"> But Paul went down and threw himself on him, and putting his arms around him, said, “Do not be distressed, for his life is in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 20:7; Acts 20:9"> So he went up and broke bread, and when he had eaten and talked for a long time, until dawn, then he departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 2 Corinthians 1:4"> And they led the youth away alive, and ⸤were greatly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “were not moderately”">c</a></sup>comforted.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Voyage to Miletus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 6:46; Mark 1:35; Mark 6:31–33"> But we went on ahead to the ship and put out to sea for Assos, intending to take Paul on board there. For having made arrangements in this way, he himself was intending to travel by land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And when he met us at Assos, we took him on board and went to Mitylene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 20:17"> And we sailed from there on the next day, and arrived opposite Chios. And on the next day we approached Samos, and on the following day we came to Miletus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:8; Acts 2:1; Acts 19:21"> For Paul had decided to sail past Ephesus so that he would not be having to spend time in Asia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">d</a></sup>For he was hurrying if it could be possible for him to be in Jerusalem on the day of Pentecost.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Farewell to the Ephesian Elders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Titus 1:5; Acts 11:30; 1 Timothy 5:17"> And from Miletus he sent word to Ephesus and summoned the elders of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:5–6; Acts 18:19; Acts 19:1"> And when they came to him, he said to them, “You know from the first day on which I set foot in Asia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">e</a></sup>how I was the whole time with you—</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; 2 Corinthians 2:4; Romans 12:11"> serving the Lord with all humility and with tears, and with the trials that happened to me through the plots of the Jews—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:27; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 5:42"> how I did not shrink from proclaiming to you anything that would be profitable, and from teaching you in public and from house to house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 11:18; 1 John 5:11–13"> testifying both to Jews and to Greeks with respect to repentance toward God and faith in our Lord Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; 2 Peter 1:14"> “And now behold, bound by the Spirit I am traveling to Jerusalem, not knowing the things that will happen to me ⸤there⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “in it”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 9:16; 1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:4"> except that the Holy Spirit testifies to me in town after town, saying that bonds and persecutions await me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:6–8; 2 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 12:10"> But I consider my life as worth ⸤nothing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “not a single word”; literally “not any thing”">g</a></sup>to myself, in order to finish my mission and the ministry that I received from the Lord Jesus, to testify to the gospel of the grace of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 28:31; Acts 20:38; Matthew 4:23"> “And now behold, I know that all of you, among whom I went about proclaiming the kingdom, will see my face no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Ezekiel 3:18–21; Ezekiel 33:2–9"> Therefore I testify to you on this very day that I am guiltless of the blood of all of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 20:20; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:7–10"> for I did not shrink from proclaiming to you the whole purpose of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Peter 5:2–3; 1 Timothy 4:16; Isaiah 40:11"> Be on guard for yourselves and for all the flock among which the Holy Spirit has appointed you as overseers, to shepherd the church of God which he obtained through the blood of his own Son.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “through his own blood”; the Greek construction can be taken either way, with “Son” implied if the meaning is “through the blood of his own”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; 2 Peter 2:1; John 10:12"> I know that after my departure fierce wolves will come in among you, not sparing the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19–20; 2 Timothy 4:3–4; Proverbs 23:33"> And from among you yourselves men will arise, speaking ⸤perversions of the truth⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “crooked things”">i</a></sup>in order to draw away the disciples after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:8; Acts 20:19; Acts 19:10"> Therefore be on the alert, remembering that night and day for three years I did not stop warning each one of you with tears.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 1:12; Ephesians 1:14; Colossians 3:24"> “And now I entrust you to God and to the message of his grace, which is able to build you up and to give you the inheritance among all those who are sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; 1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:18"> I have desired no one’s silver or gold or clothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 18:3; 1 Corinthians 4:12; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9"> You yourselves know that these hands served to meet my needs and the needs of those who were with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Proverbs 19:17; 2 Corinthians 9:6–12"> I have shown you with respect to all things that by working hard in this way it is necessary to help those who are in need, and to remember the words of the Lord Jesus that he himself said, “It is more blessed to give than to receive.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Perhaps an allusion to Matt 10:8; these exact words are not found in the NT gospels">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 21:5; Luke 22:41; Philippians 4:6"> And when he had said these things, he fell to his knees and prayed with them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:26"> And there was considerable weeping by all, and ⸤hugging⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “falling on the neck of”">k</a></sup>Paul, they kissed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 15:3; 1 Corinthians 16:11; Acts 20:25"> especially distressed at the statement that he had said, that they were going to see his face no more. And they accompanied him to the ship.</span>
</div>
</div>
