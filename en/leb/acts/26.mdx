## Paul Makes His Defense Before King Agrippa

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Proverbs 1:24; Acts 26:2"> So Agrippa said to Paul, “It is permitted for you to speak for yourself.” Then Paul extended his hand and began to defend himself:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> “Concerning all the things of which I am accused by the Jews, King Agrippa, I consider myself fortunate that before you I am about to defend myself today,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 6:14; Acts 26:26; Acts 26:7"> because you are especially acquainted with both all the customs and controversial questions with respect to the Jews. Therefore I beg you to listen to me with patience.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:13; 2 Timothy 3:10; Philippians 3:5"> “Now all the Jews know my manner of life from my youth, that had taken place from the beginning among my own people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “nation”">a</a></sup>and in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 22:3; Acts 24:5"> having known me for a long time, if they are willing to testify, that in accordance with the strictest party of our religion I lived as a Pharisee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:13; Acts 24:15; Acts 28:20"> And now I stand here on trial on the basis of hope in the promise made by God to our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:1; Philippians 3:11; 1 Timothy 5:5"> to which our twelve tribes hope to attain as they earnestly serve him night and day. Concerning this hope I am being accused by the Jews, O king!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:12–20; Acts 4:2; Acts 17:31–32"> Why is it thought incredible by you people that God raises the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Acts 22:8; Philippians 3:6"> Indeed, I myself thought it was necessary to do many things opposed to the name of Jesus the Nazarene,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 8:3; Acts 9:13–14; Acts 9:21"> which I also did in Jerusalem, and not only did I lock up many of the saints in prison, having received authority from the chief priests, but also when they were being executed, I cast my vote<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “voting pebble,” but here “vote” rather than “voting pebble” is used in the translation to avoid the idea that this small stone was actually thrown at the accused (it was used as a method of voting)">b</a></sup>against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 22:19; Matthew 10:17; Acts 13:45"> And throughout all the synagogues I punished them often and tried to force them to blaspheme, and because I was enraged at them beyond measure, I was pursuing them even as far as to foreign cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 26:10; Acts 22:5–11; Acts 9:1–8"> In ⸤this activity⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “which”">c</a></sup>I was traveling to Damascus with the authority and full power of the chief priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Revelation 21:23; Revelation 1:16"> In the middle of the day along the road, O king, I saw a light from heaven, more than the brightness of the sun, shining around me and those who were traveling with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 22:2; Acts 9:7"> And when we had all fallen to the ground, I heard a voice saying to me in the Aramaic language, ‘Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting me? It is hard for you to kick against the goads!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 15:20–21; Matthew 25:45; Exodus 16:8"> So I said, ‘Who are you, Lord?’ And the Lord said, ‘I am Jesus whom you are persecuting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 22:14–15; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 10:11"> But get up and stand on your feet, because for this reason I have appeared to you, to appoint you a servant and witness both to the things in which you saw me and to the things in which I will appear to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Jeremiah 1:19"> rescuing you from the people and from the Gentiles to whom I am sending you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Ephesians 1:18; Ephesians 5:8"> to open their eyes so that they may turn from darkness to light and from the power of Satan to God, so that they may receive forgiveness of sins and a share among those who are sanctified by faith in me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 50:5; Galatians 1:16; Ezekiel 2:7–8"> “Therefore, O King Agrippa, I was not disobedient to the heavenly vision,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 3:8; Acts 3:19; Acts 2:38"> but to those in Damascus first, and in Jerusalem and all the region of Judea and to the Gentiles, I proclaimed that they should repent and turn to God, doing deeds worthy of repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 21:30–31; Acts 21:27; Acts 25:3"> On account of these things the Jews seized me in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “while I was in”">d</a></sup>the temple courts and were attempting to kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:14; John 5:46; Romans 3:21"> Therefore I have experienced help from God until this day, and I stand here testifying to both small and great, saying nothing except what both the prophets and Moses have said were going to happen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Luke 24:26; Colossians 1:18"> that the Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “Messiah”">e</a></sup>was to suffer and that as the first of the resurrection from the dead, he was going to proclaim light both to the people and to the Gentiles.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:13–14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Corinthians 4:10"> And as he was saying these things in his defense, Festus said with a loud voice, “You are out of your mind, Paul! Your great learning ⸤is driving⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “is turning”">f</a></sup>you ⸤insane⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to madness”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7–8"> But Paul said, “I am not out of my mind, most excellent Festus, but am speaking words of truth and rationality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:2–3; Acts 2:1–12; Isaiah 30:20"> For the king knows about these things, to whom also I am speaking freely, for I am not convinced that these things in any way have escaped<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “that any of these things in any way has escaped”">h</a></sup>his notice, because this ⸤was⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “is”">i</a></sup>not ⸤done⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “having been done”">j</a></sup>in a corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:22–23"> Do you believe the prophets, King Agrippa? I know that you believe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 11:26; Matthew 10:18; Acts 26:29"> But Agrippa said to Paul, “In a short time are you persuading me to become a Christian?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “In a short time you are persuading me to become a Christian”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Colossians 1:28; Romans 10:1"> And Paul replied, “I pray to God, whether in a short time or in a long time, not only you but also all those who are listening to me today may become such people as I also am, except for these bonds!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 18:15; Acts 28:22; Acts 25:23"> Both the king and the governor got up, and Bernice and those who were sitting with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 23:29; Acts 23:9; Acts 28:18"> And as they were going out, they were talking to one another, saying, “This man is not doing anything deserving death or imprisonment.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 28:18; Acts 25:11–12; Acts 25:25"> And Agrippa said to Festus, “This man could have been released if he had not appealed to Caesar.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
