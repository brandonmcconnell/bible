## Pentecost and the Coming of the Holy Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:6; Philippians 2:2; Acts 1:13–15"> And when the day of Pentecost had come, they were all together in the same place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:31; 1 Kings 19:11; Psalm 18:10"> And suddenly a sound like a violent rushing wind came from heaven and filled the whole house where they were sitting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; John 1:32–33; Jeremiah 23:29"> And divided<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or perhaps “distributed”">a</a></sup>tongues like fire appeared to them and rested on each one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:17; Acts 13:52; Acts 4:31"> And they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other languages<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “tongues”">b</a></sup>as the Spirit gave them ability to speak out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Acts 8:2; Colossians 1:23"> Now there were Jews residing in Jerusalem, devout men from every nation under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9; Acts 2:2"> And when this sound occurred, the crowd gathered and was in confusion, because each one was hearing them speaking in his own language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 2:12; Acts 1:11; John 7:52"> And they were astounded and astonished, saying, “Behold, are not all these who are speaking Galileans?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And how do we hear, each one of us, in ⸤our own native language⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “our own language in which we were born”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:1; Acts 18:2; Revelation 1:4"> Parthians and Medes and Elamites and those residing in Mesopotamia, Judea and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:6; Zechariah 8:23; Zechariah 8:20"> Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt and the parts of Libya toward Cyrene, and the Romans who were in town,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10; Exodus 15:11; Psalm 78:4"> both Jews and proselytes, Cretans and Arabs—we hear them speaking in our own languages the great deeds of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 15:26; Acts 17:20; Luke 18:36"> And all were amazed and greatly perplexed, saying ⸤to one another⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “one to the other”">e</a></sup>“⸤What can this mean?⸥”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “what does this want to be”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:23; Acts 2:15; 1 Samuel 1:14"> But others jeered and said, “They are full of sweet new wine!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter’s Sermon on the Day of Pentecost

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1; Deuteronomy 27:9"> But Peter, standing with the eleven, raised his voice and declared to them, “Judean men, and all those who live in Jerusalem, let this be known to you, and pay attention to my words!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> For these men are not drunk, as you assume, because it is the third hour of the day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> But this is what was spoken through the prophet Joel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘And it will be in the last days,’ God says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and your sons and your daughters will prophesy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and your young men will see visions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and your old men will dream dreams.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">And even on my male slaves and on my female slaves</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will pour out my Spirit in those days, and they will prophesy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will cause wonders in the heaven</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “sky”">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">above</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and signs on the earth below,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">blood and fire and vapor of smoke.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sun will be changed to darkness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the moon to blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">before the great and glorious day of the Lord comes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; Psalm 86:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And it will be that everyone who calls upon the name of the Lord will be saved.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from Joel 2:28–32">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> “Israelite men, listen to these words! Jesus the Nazarene, a man attested to you by God with deeds of power and wonders and signs that God did through him in your midst, just as you yourselves know—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> this man, delivered up by the determined plan and foreknowledge of God, you executed by nailing to a cross through the hand of lawless men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> God raised ⸤him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “whom”; it is necessary to specify “him” in the translation to avoid confusion with the “lawless men” in the previous verse">i</a></sup>up, having brought to an end the pains of death, because it was not possible for him to be held by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; Psalm 62:6"> For David says with reference to him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I saw the Lord before me ⸤continually⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “through everything”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for he is at my right hand so that I will not be shaken.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 16:9; Psalm 63:5; Psalm 71:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this reason my heart was glad</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my tongue rejoiced greatly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">furthermore also my flesh will live in hope,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">because you will not abandon my soul in Hades,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor will you permit your Holy One to experience decay.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 16:11; Psalm 25:4; Psalm 21:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made known to me the paths of life;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will fill me with gladness with your presence.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 16:8–11">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> “Men and brothers, it is possible to speak with confidence to you about the patriarch David, that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is with us until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 132:11–18; Psalm 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> Therefore, because he was a prophet and knew that God had sworn to him with an oath to seat ⸤one of his descendants⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “from the fruit of his loins”">l</a></sup>on his throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; Psalm 16:10; Acts 13:35"> by having foreseen this, he spoke about the resurrection of the Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “Messiah”">m</a></sup>that neither was he abandoned in Hades nor did his flesh experience decay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> This Jesus God raised up, of which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “of whom,” referring to Jesus">n</a></sup>we all are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> Therefore, having been exalted to the right hand of God and having received the promise of the Holy Spirit from the Father, he has poured out this that you see and hear.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “both see and hear”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> For David did not ascend into heaven, but he himself says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at my right hand,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet.” ’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 110:1">p</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Therefore let all the house of Israel know beyond a doubt, that God has made him both Lord and Christ—this Jesus whom you crucified!”</span>
</div>

## The Response to Peter’s Sermon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Now when they heard this, they were pierced to the heart and said to Peter and the other apostles, “What should we do, men and brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> And Peter said<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Some manuscripts explicitly add “said”">q</a></sup>to them, “Repent and be baptized, each one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> For the promise is for you and for your children, and for all those who are far away, as many as the Lord our God calls to himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> And with many other words he solemnly urged and exhorted them, saying, “Be saved from this crooked generation!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 4:4; Acts 2:47; Acts 16:31–34"> So those who accepted his message were baptized, and on that day about three thousand souls were added.</span>

## The Fellowship of the First Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> And they were devoting themselves to the teaching of the apostles and to fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> And fear came on every soul, and many wonders and signs were being performed by the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> And all who believed were in the same place, and had everything in common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> And they began selling their possessions and property, and distributing these things to all, to the degree that anyone had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> And every day, devoting themselves to meeting with one purpose in the temple courts and breaking bread from house to house, they were eating their food with joy and simplicity of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> praising God and having favor with all the people. And the Lord was adding every day to the total of those who were being saved.</span>
