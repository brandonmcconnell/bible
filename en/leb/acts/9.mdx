## Saul’s Conversion on the Damascus Road

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Timothy 1:13; Galatians 1:13"> But Saul, still breathing threats and murder against the disciples of the Lord, went to the high priest</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 19:23; Acts 24:14"> and asked for letters from him to the synagogues in Damascus, so that if he found any who were of the Way, both men and women, he could bring them tied up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or figuratively “bring them under arrest”">a</a></sup>to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:12–13; 1 Corinthians 15:8; Acts 22:6"> Now as he proceeded, it happened that when he approached Damascus, suddenly a light from heaven flashed around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Genesis 22:11; Romans 11:22"> And falling to the ground, he heard a voice saying to him, “Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; 1 Samuel 3:4–10; Job 9:4"> So he said, “Who are you, Lord?” And he said, “I am Jesus, whom you are persecuting!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 22:10; Acts 26:16; 1 Timothy 1:14–16"> But get up and enter into the city, and it will be told to you ⸤what you must do⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “what thing it is necessary that you do”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 22:9; Daniel 10:7; Acts 26:13–14"> (Now the men who were traveling together with him stood speechless, because they heard the voice but saw no one.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 22:11; Acts 9:18; 2 Kings 6:17–20"> So Saul got up from the ground, but although his eyes were open he could see nothing. And leading him by the hand, they brought him into Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jonah 3:6–8; Esther 4:16; 2 Chronicles 33:12–13"> And he was ⸤unable to see⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “not seeing”">c</a></sup>for three days, and he did not eat or drink.</span>

## Ananias Sent to Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Acts 10:3; Acts 22:12"> Now there was a certain disciple in Damascus ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “by name”">d</a></sup>Ananias, and the Lord said to him in a vision, “Ananias!” And he said, “Behold, here I am, Lord!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:39; Acts 22:3; Acts 9:30"> And the Lord said to him, “Get up, go to the street called ‘Straight’ and in the house of Judas look for ⸤a man named Saul from Tarsus⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “Saul by name of Tarsus”">e</a></sup>For behold, he is praying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Acts 9:10; Acts 9:17–18"> and he has seen in a vision a man ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “by name”">f</a></sup>Ananias coming in and placing hands<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “placing his hands”">g</a></sup>on him so that he may regain his sight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 8:3; Acts 22:19–20"> But Ananias replied, “Lord, I have heard from many people about this man, how much harm he has done to your saints in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:21; Acts 7:59; 2 Timothy 2:22"> and here he has authority from the chief priests to tie up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or figuratively “to arrest” or “to imprison”">h</a></sup>all who call upon your name!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:15–16; Ephesians 3:7–8; 2 Timothy 4:16–17"> But the Lord said to him, “Go, because this man is my chosen instrument to carry my name before Gentiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The same Greek word can be translated “nations” or “Gentiles” depending on the context">i</a></sup>and kings and the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:3; Acts 21:11; 2 Corinthians 6:4"> For I will show him how much he must suffer for the sake of my name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 8:17; Acts 6:6; Acts 2:4"> So Ananias departed and entered into the house, and placing his hands on him, he said, “Brother Saul, the Lord Jesus, who appeared to you on the road by which you came, has sent me so that you may regain your sight and be filled with the Holy Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:14; Acts 22:16; Acts 2:38"> And immediately something like scales fell from his eyes and he regained his sight and got up and was baptized,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:20; 1 Samuel 30:12; Galatians 1:17"> and after taking food, he regained his strength. And he was with the disciples in Damascus several days.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Saul Proclaims Christ in Damascus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 20:28; Galatians 2:20; 1 John 4:14–15"> And immediately he began proclaiming Jesus in the synagogues: “This one is the Son of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:13–14; Acts 8:3; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> And all who heard him were amazed, and were saying, “Is this not the one who was wreaking havoc in Jerusalem on those who call upon this name, and had come here for this reason, that he could bring them tied up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or figuratively “bring them under arrest”">j</a></sup>to the chief priests?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 18:5; Isaiah 40:29; Luke 21:15"> But Saul was increasing in strength even more, and was confounding the Jews who lived in Damascus by proving that this one is the Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “Messiah”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 1:17–18; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Acts 14:19"> And when many days had elapsed, the Jews plotted to do away with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:32; Acts 25:3; Acts 20:3"> But their plot became known to Saul, and they were also watching the gates both day and night so that they could do away with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 2:15; 2 Corinthians 11:33; 1 Samuel 19:11–12"> But his disciples took him at night and let him down through the wall by lowering him in a basket.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Saul in Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:20; Galatians 1:17–19; Acts 9:19"> And when he arrived in Jerusalem, he was attempting to associate with the disciples, and they were all afraid of him, because they did not believe that he was a disciple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 4:36; Acts 4:29; Acts 4:13"> But Barnabas took him and brought him to the apostles and related to them how he had seen the Lord on the road and that he had spoken to him, and how in Damascus he had spoken boldly in the name of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 1:21; Numbers 27:16–17; Psalm 121:8"> And he was going in and going out among them in Jerusalem, speaking boldly in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 6:1; Jude 1:9; Acts 11:20"> And he was speaking and debating with the ⸤Greek-speaking Jews⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “Hellenists”">l</a></sup>but they were trying to do away with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 11:25; Acts 8:40; Acts 9:11"> And when the brothers found out, they brought him down to Caesarea and sent him away to Tarsus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 111:10; John 14:16–18; Isaiah 33:6"> Then the church throughout all of Judea and Galilee and Samaria had peace, being strengthened. And living in the fear of the Lord and the encouragement of the Holy Spirit, it was increasing in numbers.</span>

## Aeneas Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:13; Acts 8:25; Proverbs 2:8"> Now it happened that as Peter was traveling through all the places, he also came down to the saints who lived in Lydda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 13:16; John 5:5; Mark 2:3–11"> And he found there a certain man ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “by name”">m</a></sup>Aeneas who was paralyzed, who had been lying on a mat<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “mattress”">n</a></sup>for eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 9:6; Acts 3:12; Acts 3:6"> And Peter said to him, “Aeneas, Jesus Christ heals you! Get up and make your bed yourself!” And immediately he got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Acts 9:42; 1 Chronicles 5:16"> And all those who lived in Lydda and Sharon saw him, who all indeed turned to the Lord.</span>
</div>

## Dorcas Raised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Titus 3:8; Titus 2:7; 2 Chronicles 2:16"> Now in Joppa there was a certain female disciple ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “by name”">o</a></sup>Tabitha (which translated means “Dorcas”).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="“Dorcas” is the Greek translation of the Aramaic name “Tabitha” which means “deer” or “gazelle”">p</a></sup>She was full of good deeds and charitable giving which she was constantly doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Acts 20:8; John 11:36–37"> Now it happened that in those days after becoming sick, she died. And after washing her, they placed her in an upstairs room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 9:36; Acts 11:26; Acts 9:32"> And because Lydda was near Joppa, the disciples, when they heard that Peter was in ⸤Lydda⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “in it”">q</a></sup>sent two men to him, urging, “Do not delay to come to us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 10:7; Proverbs 31:30–31; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> So Peter got up and accompanied them. When he arrived, they brought him up to the upstairs room, and all the widows came to him, weeping and showing him tunics and other clothing that Dorcas used to make while she was with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 9:25; Mark 5:40–42; Luke 8:54"> But Peter sent them all outside, and, falling to his knees, he prayed. And turning toward the body, he said, “Tabitha, get up!” And she opened her eyes, and when she saw Peter, she sat up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Acts 6:1; Genesis 45:26"> And he gave her his hand and raised her up. And he called the saints and the widows and presented her alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:44; John 12:11; John 11:45"> And it became known throughout all Joppa, and many believed in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:6; Acts 10:32"> And it happened that he stayed many days in Joppa with a certain Simon, a tanner.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “with a certain Simon Berseus”; most modern English versions treat the word as Simon’s profession (“Simon the tanner”), but the word may actually be a surname (“Simon Berseus” or “Simon Tanner”)">r</a></sup></span>
</div>
