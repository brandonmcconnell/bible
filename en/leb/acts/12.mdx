## Herod Kills James and Imprisons Peter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:9; John 15:20; Matthew 10:17–18"> Now at that time, Herod the king laid hands on some of those from the church to harm them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:37; Matthew 20:23; Matthew 4:21–22"> So he executed James the brother of John with a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:15; John 12:43; Acts 24:27"> And when he saw that it was pleasing to the Jews, he proceeded to arrest Peter also. (⸤Now this was during the feast⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “now these were the days”">a</a></sup>of Unleavened Bread.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Luke 22:33; Luke 21:12"> After he had arrested ⸤him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “whom”">b</a></sup>he also put him in prison, handing him over to four squads of soldiers to guard him, intending to bring him ⸤out for public trial⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to the people”">c</a></sup>after the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16"> Thus Peter was kept in the prison, but prayer was fervently being made to God by the church for him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter Rescued by an Angel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Hebrews 13:6; Philippians 4:6–7"> Now when Herod was about to bring him out, on that very night Peter was sleeping between two soldiers, bound with two chains, and guards before the door were watching the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Psalm 146:7"> And behold, an angel of the Lord stood near him, and a light shone in the prison cell. And striking Peter’s side, he woke him up, saying, “Get up ⸤quickly⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “with quickness”">d</a></sup>And his chains fell off of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the angel said to him, “Gird yourself and put on your sandals!” And he did so. And he said to him, “Wrap your cloak around you and follow me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 126:1; Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 45:26"> And he went out and was following him. And he did not know that what was being done by the angel was real, but was thinking he was seeing a vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Isaiah 45:1–2"> And after they had passed the first and second guard, they came to the iron gate that leads to the city, which opened for them by itself, and they went out and went forward along one narrow street, and at once the angel departed from him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:28; 2 Peter 2:9"> And when Peter came to himself, he said, “Now I know truly that the Lord has sent out his angel and rescued me from the hand of Herod and all ⸤that the Jewish people expected⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the expectation of the people of the Jews”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 5:14–15; Acts 12:5; Matthew 18:19–20"> And when he realized this, he went to the house of Mary, the mother of John (who is also called Mark), where many people were gathered together and were praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:16; Luke 13:25; John 18:16–17"> And when he knocked at the door of the gateway, a female slave ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “by name”">f</a></sup>Rhoda came up to answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:41; Matthew 28:8"> And recognizing Peter’s voice, because of her joy she did not open the gate, but ran in and announced that Peter was standing at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:10; Luke 24:11; Mark 16:14"> But they said to her, “You are out of your mind!” But she kept insisting it was so. And they kept saying, “It is his angel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But Peter was continuing to knock, and when they opened the door they saw him and were astonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 13:16; Acts 19:33"> But motioning to them with his hand to be silent, he related to them how the Lord had brought him out of the prison. And he said, “Report these things to James and to the brothers,” and he departed and went to another place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:22–25; Acts 16:27; Acts 19:23"> Now when day came, there was not a little commotion among the soldiers as to what then had become of Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:27; Psalm 37:32–33; John 12:10–11"> And when Herod had searched for him and did not find him, he questioned the guards and ordered that they be led away to execution. And he came down from Judea to Caesarea and stayed there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Herod’s Gruesome Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; Matthew 11:21–22; Ezekiel 27:17"> Now he was very angry with the Tyrians and Sidonians. So they came to him with one purpose, and after persuading Blastus, ⸤the king’s chamberlain⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the one over the bedroom of the king”">g</a></sup>they asked for peace, because their country was supported with food from the king’s country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So on an appointed day Herod, after putting on royal clothing and sitting down on the judgment seat, began to deliver a public address to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 14:10–13; Revelation 13:4; Jude 1:16"> But the people began to call out loudly, “The voice of a god and not of a man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 115:1; Acts 10:25–26; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> And immediately an angel of the Lord struck him down ⸤because⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “in return for which”">h</a></sup>he did not give the glory to God. And he was eaten by worms and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 6:7; Acts 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> But the word of God kept on increasing and multiplying.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 12:12; 1 Peter 5:13; Acts 15:37"> So Barnabas and Saul returned to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts read “from”">i</a></sup>Jerusalem when they had completed their service, having taken along with them John (who is also called Mark).</span>
</div>
</div>
