## Peter and John Arrested

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Acts 6:12; Acts 23:6–9"> And while they were speaking to the people, the priests and the captain of the temple and the Sadducees approached them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Acts 24:14–15"> greatly annoyed because they were teaching the people and proclaiming in Jesus the resurrection from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 5:18; Matthew 10:16–17; Acts 16:19–24"> And they laid hands on them and put them in custody until the next day, because it was already evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 2 Corinthians 2:14–17; 2 Timothy 2:9–10"> But many of those who listened to the message believed, and the number of the men was approximately five thousand.</span>

## Peter and John on Trial Before the Sanhedrin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 4:8; Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 20:1"> And it happened that on the next day, their rulers and elders and scribes came together in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 3:2; John 18:24; Matthew 26:3"> and Annas the high priest, and Caiaphas and John and Alexander, and all those who were from the high priest’s family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 11:28; Acts 5:40"> And they made them stand in their midst and began to ask, “By what power or by what name did you do this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20; Acts 2:4"> Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, said to them, “Rulers of the people and elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15–17; Acts 3:7; John 10:32"> if we are being examined today concerning a good deed done to a sick man—by what means<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “through whom”">a</a></sup>this man was healed—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Acts 3:6; Acts 2:36"> let it be known to all of you and to all the people of Israel that by the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, whom you crucified, whom God raised from the dead—by him this man stands before you healthy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Mark 12:10–12"> This one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“This one” refers to Jesus">b</a></sup>is the stone that was rejected by you, the builders, that has become ⸤the cornerstone⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the head of the corner”; this verse is an allusion to Ps 118:22">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Corinthians 3:11; John 3:36"> And there is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven that is given among people by which we must be saved.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; John 7:15; Matthew 11:25"> Now when they saw the boldness of Peter and John, and understood that they were uneducated and untrained men, they were astonished, and recognized them, that they had been with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 19:36; Acts 3:8–12; Acts 4:21"> And because they saw the man who had been healed standing there with them, they had nothing to say in return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 26:30–32; Acts 5:34–42; Matthew 5:22"> But after they had ordered them to go outside the Sanhedrin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “council”">d</a></sup>they began to confer with one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; John 12:18"> saying, “What should we do with these men? For that a remarkable sign has taken place through them is evident to all those who live in Jerusalem, and we are not able to deny it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:28; Romans 15:18–22; Acts 5:24"> But in order that it may not spread much further among the people, let us warn them to speak no more in this name ⸤to anyone at all⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to no man”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:40; Luke 24:46–48; Acts 5:20"> And they called them back and commanded them not to speak or to teach at all in the name of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> But Peter and John answered and said to them, “Whether it is right in the sight of God to listen to you rather than God, you decide!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 22:15; Jeremiah 20:9; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> For we are not able to refrain from speaking about the things that we have seen and heard.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:26; Matthew 21:46; Luke 22:2"> So after threatening them further, they released them, finding no way to punish them on account of the people, because they were all praising God for what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:5; Acts 9:33; Luke 13:11"> For the man on whom this sign of healing had been performed was more than forty years old.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Believers Pray for Continued Bold Witness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Acts 12:11–12; Psalm 119:63"> And when they were released, they went to their own people and reported all that the chief priests and the elders had said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Jeremiah 32:17; Nehemiah 9:6"> And when they heard it, they lifted their voices with one mind to God and said, “Master, you are the one who made the heaven and the earth and the sea and all the things in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 2:1–6; Acts 1:16; Acts 2:30"> the one who said by the Holy Spirit through the mouth of our father David, your servant,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Why do the nations</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “Gentiles”; the same Greek word can be translated “nations” or “Gentiles” depending on the context">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">rage,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the peoples conspire in vain?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 2:2; Revelation 17:12–14; Daniel 9:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The kings of the earth stood opposed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the rulers assembled together at the same place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against the Lord and against his Christ.’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “Messiah”; this is a quotation from Ps 2:1–2">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 4:30; Luke 9:22"> For in truth both Herod and Pontius Pilate, together with the Gentiles and the peoples of Israel, assembled together in this city against your holy servant Jesus whom you anointed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Isaiah 46:10; Genesis 50:20"> to do all that your hand and plan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and your plan”">h</a></sup>had predestined to take place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:13; Acts 14:3; Philippians 1:14"> And now, Lord, concern yourself with their threats and grant your slaves to speak your message with all boldness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Acts 3:6; Acts 5:12"> as you extend your hand to heal and signs and wonders are performed through the name of your holy servant Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Acts 2:2; Acts 2:4"> And when they had prayed, the place in which they were gathered was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak the word<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “message”">i</a></sup>of God with boldness.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Believers Share All Things in Common

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Ephesians 4:2–6; 1 Peter 3:8"> Now the group of those who believed were one heart and soul, and no one said anything of what belonged to him was his own, but all things were theirs in common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:16; Acts 1:22; Acts 1:8"> And with great power the apostles were giving testimony to the resurrection of the Lord Jesus, and great grace was on them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 2:45; Luke 12:33; Luke 16:9"> For there was not even anyone needy among them, because all those who were owners of plots of land or houses were selling them and bringing the proceeds of the things that were sold</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 5:2; Acts 2:45; 2 Corinthians 8:20–21"> and placing them at the feet of the apostles. And it was being distributed to each as anyone had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 2:9; Galatians 2:13; Galatians 2:1"> So Joseph, who was called Barnabas by the apostles (which is translated “son of encouragement”), a Levite of Cyprus by nationality,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Acts 4:34–35; Matthew 19:29"> sold a field<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “a farm”">j</a></sup>that belonged to him and brought the money and placed it at the feet of the apostles.</span>
</div>
