## Ananias and Sapphira Lie to the Holy Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; 2 Timothy 2:20; Matthew 13:47–48"> Now a certain man ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “by name”">a</a></sup>Ananias, together with his wife Sapphira, sold a piece of property,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:37; 2 Peter 2:14–15; Matthew 23:5"> and he kept back for himself some of the proceeds, and his wife was aware of it. And he brought a certain part and placed it at the feet of the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Isaiah 29:15; John 13:2"> But Peter said, “Ananias, for what reason has Satan filled your heart, that you lied to the Holy Spirit and kept back for yourself some of the proceeds of the piece of land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:3; Joshua 7:25–26; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> When it remained to you, did it not remain yours? And when it was sold, was it at your disposal? How is it that you have contrived this deed in your heart? You have not lied to people, but to God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:10–11; Acts 2:43; Acts 5:13"> And when Ananias heard these words, he fell down and died. And great fear came on all those who heard about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:40; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:23"> So the young men stood up, wrapped him up, and carried him out and buried him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And it happened that there was an interval of about three hours, and his wife came in, not knowing what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 5:2"> And Peter said to her, “Tell me whether you both were paid this much for the piece of land.” And she said, “Yes, this much.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Acts 5:3–4; Psalm 95:8–11"> So Peter said to her, “How is it that it was agreed by you two to test the Spirit of the Lord? Behold, the feet of those who buried your husband are at the door, and they will carry you out!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:5"> And immediately she fell down at his feet and died. So when the young men came in, they found her dead, and carried her out and buried her with her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11–12; Acts 19:17"> And great fear came on the whole church and on all who heard about these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Many Signs and Wonders Are Performed by the Apostles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 19:11; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4"> Now many signs and wonders were being performed among the people through the hands of the apostles. And they were all together<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or perhaps “by common consent”">b</a></sup>in Solomon’s Portico.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:21; John 12:42"> And none of the rest dared to join them, but the people spoke highly of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:4; Isaiah 55:11–13"> And even more believers in the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “even more believers were being added to the Lord”">c</a></sup>were being added, large numbers of both men and women,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Acts 19:11–12; John 14:12"> so that they even carried out the sick into the streets and put them on cots and mats<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “mattresses”">d</a></sup>so that when Peter came by, at least his shadow would fall on some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 16:17–18; Mark 2:3–4; Matthew 15:30–31"> And the people of the towns around Jerusalem also came together, bringing the sick and those tormented by unclean spirits, who were all being healed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Apostles Arrested and Imprisoned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 13:45; Acts 7:9; Acts 17:5"> Now the high priest rose up and all those who were with him (that is, the party of the Sadducees), and they were filled with jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 4:3; Luke 21:12; 2 Corinthians 11:23"> And they laid hands on the apostles and put them in the public prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:26; Psalm 34:7; Acts 12:7–11"> But during the night an angel of the Lord opened the doors of the prison and led them out and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 6:63; John 6:68; 1 John 5:11–12"> “Go and stand in the temple courts and proclaim to the people all the words of this life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:2; Acts 22:15; Acts 12:18–19"> And when they heard this, they entered at daybreak into the temple courts and began teaching.<br />Now when the high priest and those with him arrived, they summoned the Sanhedrin—even the whole council of elders of the sons of Israel—and sent to the prison to have them brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But the officers who came<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “when they came”">e</a></sup>did not find them in the prison, and they returned and reported,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 2:4; Psalm 33:10; Daniel 6:22–24"> saying, “We found the prison locked with all security and the guards standing at the doors, but when we opened them, we found no one inside!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:26; John 12:19"> Now when both the captain of the temple and the chief priests heard these words, they were greatly perplexed concerning them, as to what this might be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But someone came and reported to them, “Behold, the men whom you put in prison are standing in the temple courts and teaching the people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:13; Acts 4:21; Matthew 26:5"> Then the captain went with the officers and brought them, not with force (for they were afraid of the people, lest they be stoned by them).</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Apostles on Trial Before the Sanhedrin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:22; Acts 4:7; Luke 22:66"> And when they had brought them, they made them stand in the Sanhedrin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “council”">f</a></sup>and the high priest put a question to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Acts 7:52; Acts 4:10–11"> saying, “⸤We strictly commanded⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “we commanded with a commandment”">g</a></sup>you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Did we not strictly command you”">h</a></sup>not to teach in this name? And behold, you have filled Jerusalem with your teaching! And you are intending to bring upon us the blood of this man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:19; 1 Samuel 15:24; Mark 7:7–9"> But Peter and the apostles answered and said, “It is necessary to obey God rather than men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Acts 10:39"> The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom you killed by hanging him on a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:47; Acts 2:33; Hebrews 2:10"> This one God has exalted to his right hand as Leader and Savior to grant repentance to Israel and forgiveness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:26–27; Hebrews 2:3–4; Acts 1:8"> And we are witnesses of these things, and so is the Holy Spirit whom God has given to those who obey him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 7:54; Acts 2:37; Luke 4:28–29"> Now when they heard this, they were infuriated, and were wanting to execute them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Luke 2:46; Luke 5:17"> But a certain man stood up in the Sanhedrin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “council”">i</a></sup>a Pharisee ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “by name”">j</a></sup>Gamaliel, a teacher of the law respected by all the people, and gave orders to put the men outside for a short time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 22:26; Jeremiah 26:19; Matthew 27:19"> And he said to them, “Men and Israelites, take care for yourselves what you are about to do to these men!</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 8:9; 2 Peter 2:2; Galatians 6:3"> For before these days, Theudas rose up saying he was somebody. A number of men, about four hundred, joined ⸤him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to whom”">k</a></sup>⸤He⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “who”">l</a></sup>was executed, and all who followed him were dispersed and came to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 2:1–2; Psalm 7:14–15; Matthew 26:52"> After this man, Judas the Galilean rose up in the days of the census and ⸤caused people to follow him in revolt⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “caused people to revolt after him”">m</a></sup>And that one perished, and all who followed him were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 8:9–10"> And now I tell you, keep away from these men, and leave them alone, because if this plan or this matter is from people, it will be overthrown.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “it will fail”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Isaiah 46:10; Matthew 16:18"> But if it is from God, you will not be able to overthrow them, lest you even be found fighting against God.” So they were persuaded by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 10:17; Acts 5:28; Acts 4:17–21"> And they summoned the apostles, beat them, commanded them not to speak in the name of Jesus, and released them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13–16; Luke 6:22"> So they went out from the presence of the Sanhedrin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “council”">o</a></sup>rejoicing, because they had been considered worthy to be dishonored for the sake of the name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 2:46; Acts 20:20; Luke 21:37"> Every day, both in the temple courts and from house to house, they did not stop teaching and proclaiming the good news that the Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “Messiah”">p</a></sup>was Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>
