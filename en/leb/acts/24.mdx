## Paul Before Felix at Caesarea Maritima

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 24:11; Acts 23:2; Acts 25:2"> And after five days the high priest Ananias came down with some elders and an attorney, a certain Tertullus, all of whom brought charges against Paul to the governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 29:5; Proverbs 26:28; Acts 24:26–27"> And when he had been summoned, Tertullus began to accuse him, saying, “We have experienced much<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “many years ,” with “years” understood">a</a></sup>peace through you, and reforms are taking place in this nation through your foresight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 23:26; Acts 26:25; Luke 1:3"> Both in every way and everywhere we acknowledge this, most excellent Felix, with all gratitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32"> But so that I may not impose on you for longer, I implore you to hear us briefly with your customary graciousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 28:22; Acts 24:14; Acts 26:5"> For we have found this man to be a public menace and one who causes riots among all the Jews throughout the Roman Empire<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the inhabited earth,” but here this is probably rhetorical hyperbole for the Roman Empire, especially since Felix, the Roman governor, is being addressed">b</a></sup>and a ringleader of the sect of the Nazarenes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:12; Acts 21:27–32; John 18:31"> who even attempted to desecrate the temple, and we arrested ⸤him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “whom”">c</a></sup>⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some later manuscripts include the following additional material between v. 6 and v. 8 : “and we wanted to judge him according to our law, (24:7) but Lysius the military tribune came and took him from our hands with much violence, (24:8) ordering his accusers to come before you.”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 23:35; Acts 25:15–16; Acts 25:5"> When you yourself examine him you will be able to find out from ⸤him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “whom”">e</a></sup>about all these things of which we are accusing him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:16; Micah 7:2–3; Psalm 4:2"> And the Jews also joined in the attack, asserting these things were so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Luke 18:2"> And when the governor gestured for him to speak, Paul replied, “Because I know you have been a judge over this nation for many years, I defend myself cheerfully with respect to the things concerning myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:1; Acts 21:18; Acts 23:11"> You can ascertain that ⸤it has not been more than⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “there are not to me more than”">f</a></sup>twelve days ⸤since⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from which time ”">g</a></sup>I went up to Jerusalem to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 28:17; Acts 24:18"> And neither did they find me arguing with anyone or making a crowd develop in the temple courts nor in the synagogues nor throughout the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 25:7; 1 Peter 3:16"> Nor can they prove the things to you concerning which they are now accusing me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Romans 3:21; Acts 3:13"> But I do confess this to you, that according to the Way (which they call a sect), so I worship the God of our fathers, believing all things that are in accordance with the law and that are written in the prophets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 5:28–29; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–16"> having a hope in God which these men also themselves await: that there is going to be a resurrection of both the righteous and the unrighteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:1; 1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> ⸤For this reason⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “by this”">h</a></sup>also I myself ⸤always⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “through everything”">i</a></sup>do my best to have a clear conscience toward God and people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:10; Romans 15:25–28; 1 Corinthians 16:1–4"> So after many years, I came to practice charitable giving and offerings to my people,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “nation”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 21:26–30; Acts 24:12"> in which they found me purified in the temple courts, not with a crowd or with a disturbance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16"> But there are some Jews from Asia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">k</a></sup>who ought to be present before you and bring charges against me, if they have anything against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> or these men themselves should say what crime they found when I stood before the Sanhedrin,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “council”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 4:2; Acts 26:6–8"> other than concerning this one declaration that I shouted while standing there before them: ‘I am being judged before you today concerning the resurrection of the dead!’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Held Awaiting Trial

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:6; Acts 24:24; Acts 25:26"> But Felix, because he understood the facts concerning the Way more accurately, put them off, saying, “When Lysias the military tribune comes down, I will decide ⸤your case⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the case with respect to you”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 28:16; Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7"> He ordered the centurion for him to be guarded and to have some freedom, and in no way to prevent any of his own people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="This could refer to either friends or relatives">n</a></sup>from serving him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Galatians 2:16; Mark 6:20"> And after some days, when Felix arrived with his wife Drusilla, who was Jewish, he sent for Paul and listened to him concerning faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:8; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:42"> And while he was discussing about righteousness and self control and the judgment that is to come, Felix became afraid and replied, “Go away for the present, and when I have an opportunity, I will summon you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 56:11"> At the same time he was also hoping that money would be given to him by Paul. For this reason also he sent for him as often as possible and talked with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 25:9; Acts 25:14; Acts 12:3"> And when two years had passed, Felix received as successor Porcius Festus. And because he wanted to do a favor for the Jews, Felix left Paul behind ⸤as a prisoner⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “bound”">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
