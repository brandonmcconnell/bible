<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> They were all paying attention to ⸤him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “whom”">d</a></sup>from the least to the greatest, saying, “This man is the power of God that is called ‘Great.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> And they were paying attention to him because for a long time he had astonished them with his magic.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Then they placed their hands on them and they received the Holy Spirit.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> Now Simon, when he saw that the Spirit was given through the laying on of the apostles’ hands, offered them money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> saying, “Give to me also this power, so that whomever I place my hands on may receive the Holy Spirit!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> But Peter said to him, “May your silver ⸤be destroyed along with you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “be for destruction with you”">g</a></sup>because you thought ⸤you could acquire⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to acquire”">h</a></sup>the gift of God by means of money!</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Therefore repent of this wickedness of yours, and ask the Lord if perhaps the intent of your heart may be forgiven you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> For I see you are in ⸤a state of bitter envy⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the gall of bitterness”">j</a></sup>and ⸤bound by unrighteousness⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the fetter of unrighteousness”">k</a></sup></span>

## Philip and the Ethiopian Eunuch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> Now an angel of the Lord spoke to Philip, saying, “Get up and go toward the south<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “go about noon”">l</a></sup>on the road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza.” (This is a desert road.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalm 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> And he got up and went, and behold, there was a man, an Ethiopian eunuch (a court official of Candace,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “the Candace” (the title of the queen of Ethiopia)">m</a></sup>queen of the Ethiopians, who was over all her treasury) who had come to worship in Jerusalem</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> and was returning and sitting in his chariot, and reading aloud the prophet Isaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> And the Spirit said to Philip, “Approach and join this chariot.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> So Philip ran up to it and heard him reading aloud Isaiah the prophet and said, “So then, do you understand what you are reading?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalm 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> And he said, “So how could I, unless someone will guide me?” And he invited Philip to come up and sit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> Now the passage of scripture that he was reading aloud was this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He was led like a sheep to the slaughter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and like a lamb before its shearer is silent,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so he did not open his mouth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> <span className="pl pl-2">In his humiliation justice was taken from him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who can describe his ⸤descendants⸥?</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or perhaps “family history”; literally “generation”">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For his life was taken away from the earth.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 53:7–8">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> And the eunuch answered and said to Philip, “I ask you, about whom does the prophet say this—about himself or about someone else?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> So Philip opened his mouth and beginning from this scripture, proclaimed the good news to him about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> And as they were traveling down the road, they came to some water. And the eunuch said, “Look! Water! What prevents me from being baptized?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="A few later manuscripts add v. 37 , with minor variations: “He said to him, ‘If you believe with your whole heart, you may.’ And he answered and said, ‘I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.’ ” The verse is almost certainly not an original part of the text of Acts.">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> And he ordered the chariot to stop, and they both went down into the water—Philip and the eunuch—and he baptized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> And when they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord carried Philip away, and the eunuch did not see him any longer, for he went on his way rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> But Philip found himself at Azotus, and as he passed through, he proclaimed the good news to all the towns until he came to Caesarea.</span>
