## The First Seven Deacons Appointed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:41; Acts 4:35; Acts 2:47"> Now in these days, as the disciples were increasing in number, a complaint arose by the ⸤Greek-speaking Jews⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “Hellenists”">a</a></sup>against the ⸤Hebraic Jews⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “Hebrews”">b</a></sup>because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “that”">c</a></sup>their widows were being overlooked in the daily distribution of food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:9–14; Exodus 18:17–26; 2 Timothy 2:4"> So the twelve summoned the community of disciples and said, “It is not desirable that we neglect the word of God to serve tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7–15; James 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 1:13"> So, brothers, select from among you seven men ⸤of good reputation⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “well spoken of”">d</a></sup>full of the Spirit and wisdom, whom we will put in charge of this need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:14; 1 Timothy 4:13–16; Philippians 1:9–11"> But we will devote ourselves to prayer and to the ministry of the word.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 11:24; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:5–40"> And the statement pleased the whole group, and they chose Stephen (a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit), and Philip, and Prochorus, and Nicanor, and Timon, and Parmenas, and Nicolaus (a convert from Antioch),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; Numbers 8:10; Acts 1:24"> whom they stood before the apostles. And they prayed and placed their hands on them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Acts 12:24; Colossians 1:6"> And the word of God kept spreading, and the number of disciples in Jerusalem was increasing greatly, and a large number of priests began obeying the faith.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Stephen Arrested

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:29–30; Acts 7:55; Acts 6:5"> Now Stephen, full of grace and power, was performing great wonders and signs among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 22:3; Acts 15:41"> But some of those from the Synagogue of the Freedmen (⸤as it was called⸥),<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “called”">e</a></sup>both Cyrenians and Alexandrians, and those from Cilicia and Asia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">f</a></sup>stood up and disputed with Stephen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:19–20; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> And they were not able to resist the wisdom and the Spirit with which he was speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; 1 Kings 21:10–13; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Then they secretly instigated men who said, “We have heard him speaking blasphemous words against Moses and God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Acts 14:2; Matthew 26:57"> And they incited the people and the elders and the scribes, and they came up and seized him and brought him to the Sanhedrin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “council”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:11; Acts 21:28; Acts 25:8"> And they put forward false witnesses who said, “This man does not stop speaking words against the holy place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “this holy place”">h</a></sup>and the law!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; Hebrews 12:26–28; Acts 21:21"> For we have heard him saying that this Nazarene Jesus will destroy this place and will change the customs that Moses handed down to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Matthew 17:2"> And as they looked intently at him, all those who were sitting in the Sanhedrin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “council”">i</a></sup>saw his face was like the face of an angel.</span>
</div>
</div>
