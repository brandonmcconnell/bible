## Paul Travels on to Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17; Luke 5:4; 1 Samuel 20:41–42"> And it happened that after we tore ourselves away from them, we put out to sea, and running a straight course we came to Cos and on the next day to Rhodes, and from there to Patara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jonah 1:3; Acts 11:19; Acts 15:3"> And finding a ship that was crossing over to Phoenicia, we went aboard and put out to sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 21:16; Acts 4:36; Matthew 4:24"> And after we sighted Cyprus and left it behind ⸤on the port side⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “left”">a</a></sup>we sailed to Syria and arrived at Tyre, because the ship was to unload its cargo there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 20:22–23; Acts 21:10–12; Acts 20:6–7"> And we stayed there seven days after we found the disciples, who kept telling Paul through the Spirit not to set foot in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 20:36; Acts 9:40; Psalm 95:6"> And it happened that when our days were over, we departed and went on our way, while all of them accompanied us, together with their wives and children, as far as outside the city. And after falling to our knees on the beach and praying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:27; John 1:11; 2 Corinthians 2:13"> we said farewell to one another and embarked in the ship, and they returned to their own homes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:24; 1 Samuel 10:4; Acts 28:12"> And when we had completed the voyage from Tyre, we arrived at Ptolemais. And after we greeted the brothers, we stayed one day with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 8:26–40; Ephesians 4:11"> And on the next day we departed and came to Caesarea, and entered into the house of Philip the evangelist, who was one of the seven, and stayed with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 2:17; Revelation 2:20; Joel 2:28"> (⸤Now this man had⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “now to this man were”">b</a></sup>four virgin daughters who prophesied.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 11:28; Acts 21:4; Acts 20:16"> And while we were staying there many days, a certain prophet ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “by name”">c</a></sup>Agabus came down from Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Jeremiah 13:1–11; 1 Samuel 15:27–28"> And he came to us and took Paul’s belt. Tying up his own feet and hands, he said, “This is what the Holy Spirit says: ‘In this way the Jews in Jerusalem will tie up the man whose belt this is, and will deliver him into the hands of the Gentiles.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:21–23; Acts 20:22; Acts 21:4"> And when we heard these things, both we and the local residents urged him not to go up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Philippians 2:17; Philippians 1:20–21"> Then Paul replied, “What are you doing weeping and breaking my heart? For I am ready not only to be tied up, but even to die in Jerusalem for the name of the Lord Jesus!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 22:42; Matthew 26:42; Matthew 26:39"> And because he would not be persuaded, we remained silent, saying, “The will of the Lord be done.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 25:1; Acts 25:6; Acts 25:9"> So after these days we got ready and went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 16:31; Acts 21:8; Psalm 92:14"> And some of the disciples from Caesarea also traveled together with us, bringing us to a certain Mnason of Cyprus, a disciple of long standing,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or perhaps “one of the original disciples”">d</a></sup>with whom we were to be entertained as guests.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Visits the Leaders of the Jerusalem Church

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 15:4; Romans 15:7; Hebrews 13:1–2"> And when we came to Jerusalem, the brothers welcomed us gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 1:19; Galatians 2:9; Acts 15:13"> And on the next day Paul went in with us to James, and all the elders were present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 14:27; Acts 1:17; Romans 15:18–19"> And after greeting them, he began to relate ⸤one after the other⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “with respect to each one”">e</a></sup>the things which God had done among the Gentiles through his ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 10:2; Galatians 1:14; Acts 22:3"> And when they heard this, they began to glorify God. And they said to him, “You see, brother, how many ten thousands there are among the Jews who have believed, and they are all zealous adherents of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 6:12–15; 1 Corinthians 9:19–21; Galatians 5:1–6"> And they have been informed about you that you are teaching all the Jews who are among the Gentiles the abandonment of Moses, telling them not to circumcise their children or to live according to our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “according to the”; the Greek article is used here as a possessive pronoun">f</a></sup>customs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 15:12; Acts 15:22; Acts 19:32"> What then ⸤is to be done⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “is it”">g</a></sup>Doubtless they will all hear that you have come!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 18:18; Numbers 6:2–7"> Therefore do this that we tell you: ⸤we have⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “there are to us”">h</a></sup>four men who have taken a vow upon themselves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “on themselves”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:26; Acts 24:18; Acts 18:18"> Take these men and purify yourself along with them and ⸤pay their expenses⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “spend on them”">j</a></sup>so that they can shave their heads, and everyone will know that the things which they had been informed about you are nothing, but you yourself also agree with observing the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:29; Acts 15:19–20"> But concerning the Gentiles who have believed, we have written a letter after deciding they should avoid food sacrificed to idols and blood and what has been strangled and sexual immorality.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 24:18; Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:13–20"> Then Paul took along the men on the next day, and after he had purified himself together with them, he entered into the temple courts, announcing the completion of the days of purification until ⸤the time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “which time ”">k</a></sup>the offering would be presented on behalf of each one of them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Arrested in the Temple Courts

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 24:18; Acts 13:50"> But when the seven days were about to be completed, the Jews from Asia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">l</a></sup>who had seen him in the temple courts stirred up the whole crowd and laid hands on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 24:5–6; Acts 21:21; Acts 26:20–21"> shouting, “Israelite men, help! This is the man who is teaching everyone everywhere against the people and the law and this place! And furthermore he also brought Greeks into the temple, and has defiled this holy place!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 18:19"> (For they had previously seen Trophimus the Ephesian in the city with him, whom they thought that Paul had brought into the temple.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 26:21; 2 Corinthians 11:26; Acts 16:19–22"> And the whole city was stirred up, and the people came running together, and they seized Paul and dragged him outside of the temple courts, and immediately the doors were shut.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–33; Acts 17:5; Acts 26:9–10"> And as they were seeking to kill him, a report came up to the military tribune of the cohort that all Jerusalem was in confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Isaiah 3:15; Acts 5:40"> ⸤He⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “who”">m</a></sup>immediately took along soldiers and centurions and ran down to them. And when they saw the military tribune and the soldiers, they stopped beating Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Acts 21:11; Acts 20:23"> Then the military tribune came up and arrested him and ordered him to be bound with two chains, and inquired who he was and what it was that he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:32; Acts 23:10; Acts 22:24"> But some in the crowd were shouting one thing and others another, and because he was not able to find out the truth on account of the commotion, he gave orders to bring him into the barracks.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “headquarters”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:2–3; Psalm 55:9; Psalm 58:2"> And when he came to the steps, it happened that he had to be carried by the soldiers on account of the violence of the crowd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 19:15; Acts 22:22; Luke 23:18"> for the crowd of people was following them, shouting, “Away with him!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Permitted to Address the Crowd

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Luke 21:15; Acts 19:30"> And as he was about to be brought into the barracks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “headquarters”">o</a></sup>Paul said to the military tribune, “Is it permitted for me to say something to you?” And he said, “Do you know Greek?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 24:26; Acts 5:36–37; Matthew 5:11"> Then you are not the Egyptian who before these days raised a revolt and led out into the wilderness the four thousand men of the Assassins?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Also known as the “Sicarii” from the Latin word “sicarius” = dagger, after the short dagger used to assassinate political opponents">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Acts 9:11; Acts 23:27"> But Paul said, “I am a Jewish man from Tarsus in Cilicia, a citizen of no unimportant city. Now I ask you, allow me to speak to the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Acts 22:2; John 5:2"> So when he permitted him, Paul, standing there on the steps, motioned with his hand to the people. And when there was a great silence, he addressed them in the Aramaic language, saying,</span>
