## A Lame Beggar Healed at the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 55:17; Acts 10:3; Acts 4:13"> Now Peter and John were going up to the temple at the hour of prayer, the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:8; John 9:8; Luke 16:20"> And a certain man was being carried who was lame ⸤from birth⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “from his mother’s womb”">a</a></sup>⸤He⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “who,” but a new sentence was begun here in the translation in keeping with English style">b</a></sup>was placed every day at the gate of the temple called “Beautiful,” so that he could ask for charitable gifts from those who were going into the temple courts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> When he saw Peter and John about to go into the temple courts, he began asking to receive alms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:9–10; John 11:40; Luke 4:20"> And Peter looked intently at him, together with John, and said, “Look at us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So he fixed his attention on them, expecting to receive something from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 3:16; Acts 10:38; Acts 4:10"> But Peter said, “Silver and gold ⸤I do not possess⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “is not to me”">c</a></sup>but what I have, this I give to you—in the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, walk!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “get up and walk”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Mark 9:27; Acts 9:41"> And taking hold of him by the right hand, he raised him up, and immediately his feet and ankles were made strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 14:10; Isaiah 35:6; Psalm 107:20–22"> And leaping up, he stood and began walking around and entered into the temple courts with them, walking and leaping and praising God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:21; Acts 4:16; Luke 13:17"> And all the people saw him walking and praising God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:2; John 5:20; John 9:3"> And they recognized him, that this one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “that he himself”">e</a></sup>was the one who used to sit asking for alms at the Beautiful Gate of the temple, and they were filled with awe and astonishment at what had happened to him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter’s Sermon in Solomon’s Portico

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:12; John 10:23; Luke 8:38"> And while he was holding fast to Peter and John, all the people ran together to them in the portico called Solomon’s, utterly astonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; John 3:27–28; Daniel 2:28–30"> And when he saw it, Peter replied to the people, “Men and Israelites, why are you astonished at this? Or why are you staring at us, as if by our own power or godliness we have made him walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 22:32; Acts 7:32; Matthew 27:17–25"> The God of Abraham and of Isaac<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “the God of Isaac”">f</a></sup>and of Jacob,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “the God of Jacob”">g</a></sup>the God of our fathers, has glorified his servant Jesus, whom you handed over and denied in the presence of Pilate, after<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “although ”; this genitive absolute construction can be understood as either temporal “after ” or concessive “although ”">h</a></sup>he had decided to release him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Acts 7:52; 1 Peter 3:18"> But you denied the Holy and Righteous One and demanded that a man—a murderer—be granted to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Revelation 21:6; Acts 2:32"> And you killed the originator of life, whom God raised from the dead, of which we are witnesses!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Acts 4:10; Acts 14:9"> And on the basis of faith in his name, his name has made this man strong, whom you see and know, and the faith that is through him has given him this perfect health in the presence of you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Acts 13:27; John 16:3"> And now, brothers, I know that you acted in ignorance, just as your rulers did also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 2:23"> But the things which God foretold through the mouth of all the prophets, that his Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “Messiah”">i</a></sup>would suffer, he has fulfilled in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Isaiah 44:22; Isaiah 43:25"> Therefore repent and turn back, so that your sins may be blotted out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:30–36; Revelation 19:11–16; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> so that times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord, and he may send the Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “Messiah”">j</a></sup>appointed for you—Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:21; Isaiah 1:26; Acts 1:11"> whom heaven must receive until the times of the restoration of all things, about which God spoke through the mouth of his holy prophets from earliest times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 7:37; Galatians 4:4"> Moses said, ‘The Lord God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “The Lord your God”">k</a></sup>will raise up for you a prophet like me from your brothers. You will listen to him in everything that he says to you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 18:15">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:19; John 12:48; Hebrews 10:28–30"> And it will be that every soul who does not listen to that prophet will be destroyed utterly from the people.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 18:19 and Lev 23:29">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 24:27; 1 Samuel 3:20"> And indeed, all the prophets from Samuel and all those who followed him have spoken about and proclaimed these days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:8; Acts 2:39"> You are the sons of the prophets and of the covenant that God ordained with your fathers, saying to Abraham, ‘And in your offspring all the nations of the earth will be blessed.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 22:18">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 1:21; Acts 3:22; Jude 1:24"> God, after he had raised up his servant, sent him to you first, to bless you by turning each of you back from your wickedness!”</span>
</div>
</div>
