## Cornelius Has a Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:1; Matthew 8:5–13; Matthew 27:27"> Now there was a certain man in Caesarea ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “by name”">a</a></sup>Cornelius, a centurion of what was called the Italian Cohort,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–8; Acts 10:4; Acts 10:22"> devout and fearing God together with all his household, doing many charitable deeds for the people and praying to God ⸤continually⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “through everything”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 9:20–21; Acts 3:1; Acts 5:19"> About the ninth hour of the day, he saw clearly in a vision an angel of God coming to him and saying to him, “Cornelius.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 10:31"> And he stared at him and became terrified and said, “What is it, Lord?” And he said to him, “Your prayers and your charitable deeds have gone up for a memorial offering before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 16:9; Acts 9:38; Acts 15:7"> And now, send men to Joppa and summon a certain Simon, who is also called Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:43; Acts 11:13–14; Romans 10:14–17"> This man is staying as a guest with a certain Simon, a tanner,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “with a certain Simon Berseus”; most modern English versions treat the word as Simon’s profession (“Simon the tanner”), but the word may actually be a surname (“Simon Berseus” or “Simon Tanner”)">c</a></sup>whose house is by the sea.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philemon 1:16; 1 Samuel 14:6–7; Matthew 8:9–10"> And when the angel who spoke to him departed, he summoned two of the household slaves and a devout soldier from those who attended him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:36; Psalm 119:59–60; Acts 10:33"> and after he had explained everything to them, he sent them to Joppa.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter Has a Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:5; Psalm 55:17; 1 Samuel 9:25"> And the next day, as they were on their way and approaching the city, Peter went up on the housetop to pray at about the sixth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Revelation 4:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4"> And he became hungry and wanted to eat. But while they were preparing the food, a trance came over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:6; John 1:51; Romans 16:25–26"> And he saw heaven opened and an object something like a large sheet coming down, being let down to the earth by its four corners,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:8–9; John 7:37; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11"> in which were all the four-footed animals and reptiles of the earth and birds of the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 4:31–34; Jeremiah 35:2–5; Acts 10:10"> And a voice came to him, “Get up, Peter, slaughter and eat!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Ezekiel 4:14; Daniel 1:8"> But Peter said, “Certainly not, Lord! For I have never eaten anything common and unclean!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:25; Matthew 15:11; Titus 1:15"> And the voice came again to him for the second time: “The things which God has made clean, you must not consider unclean!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 41:32; John 21:17; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> And this happened three times, and immediately the object was taken up into heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:12; Acts 10:7–19; Acts 5:24"> Now while Peter was greatly perplexed within himself as to what the vision that he had seen might be, behold, the men who had been sent by Cornelius, having found the house of Simon by asking around, stood at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 10:5–6; Acts 11:11"> And they called out and asked if Simon who was also called Peter was staying there as a guest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 8:29; Acts 11:12; Acts 21:4"> And while Peter was reflecting about the vision, the Spirit said to him, “Behold, men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “three men”">d</a></sup>are looking for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 15:7–9; Zechariah 2:9–11; Isaiah 48:16"> But get up, go down, and go with them—not hesitating at all, because I have sent them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Mark 10:51; Acts 10:29"> So Peter went down to the men and said, “Behold, I am he whom you are looking for! What is the reason for which you have come?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 11:14; Acts 10:2; Acts 22:12"> And they said, “Cornelius, a centurion, a righteous and God-fearing man—and well spoken of by the whole nation of the Jews—was directed by a holy angel to summon you to his house and to hear words from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 10:45; Acts 11:12; 1 Peter 4:9"> So he invited them in and entertained them as guests, and on the next day he got up and went away with them. And some of the brothers from Joppa accompanied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 8:40; Zechariah 8:20–23; Zechariah 3:10"> And on the next day he entered into Caesarea. Peter Visits Cornelius<br />Now Cornelius was waiting for them, and had called together his relatives and close friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Daniel 2:46"> So it happened that when Peter entered, Cornelius met him, fell at his feet, and worshiped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 22:8–9; Acts 14:14–15"> But Peter helped him up, saying, “Get up! I myself am also a man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; John 4:35; Acts 10:24"> And as he conversed with him, he went in and found many people gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:9; Ephesians 3:6–7; Galatians 2:12–14"> And he said to them, “You know that it is forbidden for a Jewish man to associate with or to approach a foreigner. And to me God has shown that I should call no man common or unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalm 119:60; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:19–20"> Therefore—and without raising any objection—I came when I was sent for. So I ask for what reason you sent for me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 10:3; Daniel 9:20–21; Nehemiah 9:1–3"> And Cornelius said, “⸤Four days ago at this hour⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from the fourth day until this hour”">e</a></sup>the ninth, I was praying in my house. And behold, a man in shining clothing stood before me</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:4; Daniel 9:23; Luke 1:13"> and said, ‘Cornelius, your prayer has been heard, and your charitable deeds have been remembered before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Therefore send to Joppa and summon Simon who is also called Peter. This man is staying as a guest in the house of Simon, a tanner,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “of Simon Berseus”; most modern English versions treat the word as Simon’s profession (“Simon the tanner”), but the word may actually be a surname (“Simon Berseus” or “Simon Tanner”)">f</a></sup>by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="James 1:19; Galatians 4:14; 2 Chronicles 30:12"> Therefore I sent for you at once, and you ⸤were kind enough to come⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “have done rightly coming”">g</a></sup>So now we all are present before God to hear all the things that have been commanded to you by the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 2:11; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Deuteronomy 10:17"> So Peter opened his mouth and said, “In truth I understand that God is not one who shows partiality,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 2:29; Acts 10:2; Romans 10:12–13"> but in every nation the one who fears him and who does what is right is acceptable to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; Acts 2:36; Revelation 17:14"> As for the message that he sent to the sons of Israel, proclaiming the good news of peace through Jesus Christ—this one is Lord of all—</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 4:14; Acts 2:22; Acts 28:22"> you know the thing that happened throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee, after the baptism that John proclaimed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:18; Acts 2:22; Isaiah 61:1–3"> Jesus of Nazareth—how God anointed him with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed by the devil, because God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:48; Acts 1:8; Acts 10:41"> And we are witnesses of all the things that he did both in the land of the Judeans and in Jerusalem, whom they also executed by hanging him on a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Peter 1:21; Acts 13:30–31; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4"> God raised this one up on the third day and granted that he should become visible,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 14:22; Acts 10:39; John 21:13"> not to all the people but to us who had been chosen beforehand by God as witnesses, who ate and drank with him after he rose from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Peter 4:5"> And he commanded us to preach to the people and to testify solemnly that this one is the one appointed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “one who is designated”">h</a></sup>by God as judge of the living and of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Acts 2:38; Isaiah 53:11"> To this one all the prophets testify, that through his name everyone who believes in him receives forgiveness of sins.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Holy Spirit Given to Gentiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 11:15; Acts 19:6; Acts 15:8"> While Peter was still speaking these words, the Holy Spirit fell on all those who were listening to the message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 10:23; Acts 2:38; Galatians 3:13–14"> And those believers from the circumcision who had accompanied Peter were astonished that the gift of the Holy Spirit had been poured out even on the Gentiles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> for they heard them speaking in tongues and glorifying God. Then Peter said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 8:36; Romans 10:12; Acts 11:15–17"> “Surely no one can withhold the water for these people to be baptized, who have received the Holy Spirit as we also did!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 8:16; Galatians 3:27"> So he ordered that they be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then they asked him to stay for several days.</span>
</div>
