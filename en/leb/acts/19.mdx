## Paul Finds Disciples of John the Baptist in Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:23–28; 1 Corinthians 16:12; 1 Corinthians 1:12"> And it happened that while Apollos was in Corinth, Paul traveled through the inland regions and came<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and came down”">a</a></sup>to Ephesus and found some disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 7:39; Acts 11:15–17; Galatians 3:5"> And he said to them, “Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you believed?” And they said to him, “But we have not even heard that there is a Holy Spirit!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 8:16"> And he said, “Into what then were you baptized?” And they said, “Into the baptism of John.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 1:29–34; Acts 11:16; Acts 1:5"> And Paul said, “John baptized with a baptism of repentance, telling the people that they should believe in the one who was to come after him—that is, in Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:12; Acts 8:16; Romans 6:3–4"> And when they heard this, they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 6:6; Acts 2:4; Mark 16:17"> And when Paul laid hands<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “placed his hands”">b</a></sup>on them, the Holy Spirit came upon them and they began to speak in tongues and to prophesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> (Now the total number of men was about twelve.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 28:23; Acts 1:3; Acts 9:20–22"> So he entered into the synagogue and was speaking boldly for three months, discussing and attempting to convince<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The present tense participle has been translated as a conative present (“attempting to convince”)">c</a></sup>them concerning<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “of the things concerning”">d</a></sup>the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 19:23; Acts 9:2; Acts 18:6–8"> But when some became hardened and were disobedient, reviling the Way before the congregation, he departed from them and took away the disciples, leading discussions every day in the lecture hall of Tyrannus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 20:31; 2 Timothy 1:15; Acts 16:6"> And this took place for two years, so that all who lived in Asia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">e</a></sup>heard the word of the Lord, both Jews and Greeks.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Would-be Exorcists

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 16:17–20; Acts 5:12; Romans 15:18–19"> And God was performing ⸤extraordinary⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “not the ordinary”">f</a></sup>miracles by the hands of Paul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:15; 2 Kings 13:20–21; Mark 16:17"> so that even handkerchiefs or work aprons ⸤that had touched his skin⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from his skin”">g</a></sup>were carried away to those who were sick, and their diseases left them and the evil spirits came out of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 12:27; Mark 9:38; Luke 11:19"> But some itinerant Jewish exorcists also attempted to pronounce the name of the Lord Jesus over those who had evil spirits, saying, “I adjure you by Jesus whom Paul preaches!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> (Now seven sons of a certain Sceva, a Jewish chief priest, were doing this.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 16:17–18; Luke 4:33–35; Mark 1:34"> But the evil spirit answered and said to them, “Jesus I know, and Paul I am acquainted with, but who are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 8:29; Mark 5:3–4; Mark 5:15"> And the man ⸤who had the evil spirit⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “in whom the evil spirit was”">h</a></sup>leaped on them, subdued all of them, and prevailed against them, so that they ran away from that house naked and wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:12; Acts 5:5; Philippians 2:9–11"> And this became known to all who lived in Ephesus, both Jews and Greeks, and fear fell upon them all, and the name of the Lord Jesus was exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:10; Psalm 32:5; Matthew 3:6"> And many of those who had believed came, confessing and disclosing their practices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:8; Exodus 32:20; Genesis 35:4"> and many of those who practiced magic brought together their books and burned them up in the sight of everyone. And they counted up their value and found it was ⸤fifty thousand silver coins⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “five ten thousands of silver coins”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 6:7; Isaiah 55:11"> In this way the word of the Lord was growing in power and was prevailing.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Major Riot in Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:22; Acts 20:16; Acts 23:11"> Now when these things were completed, Paul resolved in the Spirit to go to Jerusalem, passing through Macedonia and Achaia, saying, “After I have been there, it is necessary for me to see Rome also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 16:23; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 16:3"> So after sending two of those who were assisting him, Timothy and Erastus, to Macedonia, he himself stayed some time in Asia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 24:22; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> Now there happened at that time no little disturbance concerning the Way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Acts 16:16; Acts 16:19"> For someone ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “by name”">k</a></sup>Demetrius, a silversmith who made silver replicas of the temple of Artemis, was bringing no little business to the craftsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:19; Hosea 12:7–8; Revelation 18:11–19"> ⸤These⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “whom”">l</a></sup>he gathered together, and the workers occupied with such things, and said, “Men, you know that from this business ⸤we get our prosperity⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “prosperity is to us”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 17:29; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 44:10–20"> and you see and hear that not only in Ephesus but in almost all of Asia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">n</a></sup>this man Paul has persuaded and turned away a large crowd by saying that the gods made by hands are not gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; 1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 23:13"> So not only is there a danger this line of business of ours will come into disrepute, but also the temple of the great goddess Artemis will be regarded as nothing—and she is about to be brought down even from her grandeur, she whom the whole of Asia<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">o</a></sup>and the entire world worship!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Revelation 17:13; Acts 16:19–24"> And when they heard this and became full of rage, they began to shout, saying, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Colossians 4:10; Acts 27:2"> And the city was filled with the tumult, and with one purpose they rushed into the theater, seizing Gaius and Aristarchus, Macedonians who were traveling companions of Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 21:39; 2 Samuel 18:2–3; Acts 17:22–31"> But when Paul wanted to enter into the popular assembly, the disciples would not let him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:10; Acts 21:12; Acts 16:6"> And even some of the Asiarchs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “provincial authorities”">p</a></sup>who were his friends sent word to him and were urging him not to risk himself by going into the theater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Acts 19:40; Matthew 11:7–9"> So some were shouting one thing and some another, for the assembly was in confusion, and the majority did not know ⸤why⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “on account of what”">q</a></sup>they had assembled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 12:17; 2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 1:20"> And some of the crowd advised<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “concluded it was about ”">r</a></sup>Alexander, when the Jews put him forward. But Alexander, motioning with his hand, was wanting to defend himself to the popular assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:28; Matthew 6:7; Revelation 13:4"> But when they recognized that he was a Jew, they were shouting with one voice from all of them for about two hours, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:2; Ephesians 2:12"> And when the city secretary had quieted the crowd, he said, “Ephesian men, for who is there among men who does not know the Ephesian city is honorary temple keeper of the great Artemis and of her image fallen from heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Acts 5:35–39; Proverbs 25:8"> Therefore because these things are undeniable, it is necessary that you be quiet and do nothing rash!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Romans 2:22; Acts 25:8"> For you have brought these men here who are neither temple robbers nor blasphemers of our goddess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:7; 1 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 18:14"> If then Demetrius and the craftsmen who are with him have a complaint against anyone, the court days are observed and there are proconsuls—let them bring charges against one another!</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But if you desire anything further, it will be settled in the lawful assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 17:5–8; Matthew 26:5; Acts 20:1"> For indeed we are in danger of being accused of rioting concerning today, since there is no cause in relation to which we will be able to give an account concerning this disorderly gathering!” And when he had said these things, he dismissed the assembly.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Verse 41 in the English Bible is included as part of v. 40 in the standard editions of the Greek text">s</a></sup></span>
</div>
