## Paul on Malta

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:26; Acts 27:44; Acts 27:39"> And after we were brought safely through, then we found out that the island was called Malta.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Hebrews 13:2; Colossians 3:11"> And the local inhabitants showed ⸤extraordinary⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “not the ordinary”">a</a></sup>kindness to us, for they lit a fire and welcomed us all, because of the rain that had begun and because of the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; Matthew 12:34; Acts 28:4"> And when Paul had gathered a large number of sticks and was placing them on the fire, a viper came out because of the heat and fastened itself on his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:2; Luke 13:4; John 7:24"> And when the local people saw the creature hanging from his hand, they began saying to one another, “Doubtless this man is a murderer whom, although he was rescued from the sea, Justice has not permitted to live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 10:19; Mark 16:18; Psalm 91:13"> He, in turn, shook off the creature into the fire and suffered no harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:22; Acts 14:11–13; Matthew 21:9"> But they were expecting that he was going to swell up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “to burn with fever” (either meaning is possible here)">b</a></sup>or suddenly to fall down dead. So after they had waited for a long time and saw nothing unusual happen to him, they changed their minds and began saying that he was a god.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40–41; Luke 19:6–9; Acts 28:2"> Now in the regions around that place were fields belonging to the chief official of the island, ⸤named⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “by name”">c</a></sup>Publius, who welcomed us and entertained us hospitably for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 6:5; James 5:14–16"> And it happened that the father of Publius was lying down, afflicted with fever and dysentery. Paul went to ⸤him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “whom”">d</a></sup>and after praying, he placed his hands on him and healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Mark 6:54–56; Acts 5:15"> And after this had taken place, the rest of those on the island who had diseases were coming and being healed also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; Philippians 4:11–12; Matthew 6:31–34"> They also honored us with many honors, and when we were putting out to sea, they gave us the things ⸤we needed⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “for the needs”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Arrives in Rome at Last

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 27:6; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 45:20"> Now after three months we put out to sea in a ship that had wintered at the island, an Alexandrian one ⸤with the twin gods Castor and Pollux as its insignia⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “marked with the Dioscuri” (a joint name for the twin gods Castor and Pollux)">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And putting in at Syracuse, we stayed there three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 27:13"> From there we got underway<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “we sailed along” (with “the coast” understood); the exact meaning of the text as it stands is disputed and various additional terms have to be supplied in any case">g</a></sup>and arrived at Rhegium, and after one day a south wind came up and on the second day we came to Puteoli,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:4; Acts 20:6; Acts 19:1"> where we found brothers, and were implored to stay with them seven days. And in this way we came to Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; Romans 15:24; Exodus 4:14"> And from there the brothers, when they heard the news about us, came to meet us as far as the Forum of Appius and Three Taverns. When he saw them, Paul gave thanks to God and took courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Acts 24:23; Acts 28:30–31"> And when we entered into Rome, Paul was allowed to stay by himself with the soldier who was guarding him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul and the Jewish Community in Rome

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 6:14; Acts 25:10"> Now it happened that after three days, he called together those who were the most prominent of the Jews. And when they had assembled, he said to them, “Men and brothers, although I had done nothing against our people or the customs of our fathers, from Jerusalem I was delivered as a prisoner into the hands of the Romans,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:31–32; Acts 23:29; Acts 22:24–25"> who, when they had examined me, were wanting to release me, because there was no basis for an accusation worthy of death with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:32; Acts 25:21; Acts 25:10–12"> But because the Jews objected, I was forced to appeal to Caesar (not as if I had any charge to bring against my own people).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “nation”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:9; Acts 26:6–7"> Therefore for this reason I have requested to see you and to speak with you, for because of the hope of Israel I am wearing this chain!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 50:8"> And they said to him, “We have received no letters about you from Judea, nor has any of the brothers come and reported or spoken anything evil about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Acts 24:14; 1 Peter 3:16"> But we would like to hear from you what you think, for concerning this sect it is known to us that it is spoken against everywhere.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 17:2–3; Acts 19:8; Acts 18:28"> And when they had set a day with him, many more came to him at his lodging place, to whom he was explaining from early in the morning until evening, testifying about the kingdom of God and attempting to convince them about Jesus from both the law of Moses and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 14:4; Acts 17:4–5; Acts 19:8–9"> And some were convinced by what was said, but others refused to believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:7; 2 Peter 1:21; Mark 7:6"> So being in disagreement with one another, they began to leave after Paul made one statement: “The Holy Spirit spoke rightly through the prophet Isaiah to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:19–20"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Go to this people and say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“⸤You will keep on hearing⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “hearing you will hear”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will never understand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ⸤you will keep on seeing⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “seeing you will see”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will never perceive.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-2">For the heart of this people has become dull,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and with their ears they hear with difficulty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they have shut their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lest they see with their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and hear with their ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and understand with their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and turn, and I would heal them.” ’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 6:9–10">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:30–32; Acts 26:17–18; Romans 3:29–30"> Therefore let it be known to you that this salvation of God has been sent to the Gentiles. They also will listen!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Some later manuscripts include v. 29 : “And when he had said these things, the Jews departed, having a great dispute among themselves.”">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> So he stayed two whole years in his own rented house, and welcomed all who came to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 4:29; Ephesians 6:19–20; Matthew 4:23"> proclaiming the kingdom of God and teaching the things concerning the Lord Jesus Christ with all boldness, without hindrance.</span>
</div>
</div>
