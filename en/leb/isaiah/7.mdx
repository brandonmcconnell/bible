## The Sign to Ahaz

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 15:37; 2 Kings 16:1; Isaiah 1:1"> This happened in the days of Ahaz, son of Jotham, son of Uzziah, king of Judah. Rezin, king of Aram, and Pekah, son of Remaliah, king of Israel, went<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew is singular">a</a></sup>up to Jerusalem for warfare against it,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “her”">b</a></sup>but he was not able to fight against it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “her”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 7:13; Isaiah 9:9"> When it was reported to the house of David, saying “Aram stands by Ephraim,” his heart and the heart of his people shook like the shaking of the trees of the forest because of the wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 36:2; Romans 9:27"> Then Yahweh said to Isaiah, “Go out to meet Ahaz, you and Shear-Jashub your son, at the end of the conduit of the upper pool on the highway of the washer’s field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Lamentations 3:26; Deuteronomy 20:3"> And you must say to him, ‘Take heed and be quiet! You must not fear, and your heart must not be faint because of these two stumps of smoldering firebrands, ⸤because of the fierce anger of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “because of the fierceness of the anger of”">d</a></sup>Rezin and Aram and the son of Remaliah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 83:3–4; Zechariah 1:15; Psalm 2:2"> Because Aram has plotted evil against you with Ephraim and the son of Remaliah, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> “Let us go up against Judah and let us tear her apart, ⸤and let us lay it open and so bring it unto ourselves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “and let us break through her to us”">e</a></sup>and let us install the son of Tabeel as king in her midst.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 46:10–11"> Thus says the Lord Yahweh, “It shall not stand, and it shall not come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1–3; Genesis 14:15; 2 Samuel 8:6"> For the head of Aram is Damascus, and the head of Damascus is Rezin, and in sixty-five years from now Ephraim will be too shattered to be a people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Romans 11:20; 2 Chronicles 20:20"> And the head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is the son of Remaliah. If you do not believe then you will not endure.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 8:5; Isaiah 1:5; Isaiah 10:20"> And Yahweh continued to speak to Ahaz, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 38:7–8; Isaiah 38:22"> “Ask for a sign for yourself from Yahweh God; make it deep as Sheol or make it high as above.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 2 Chronicles 28:22; Malachi 3:15"> But Ahaz said, “I will not ask, and I will not put Yahweh to the test.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 34:18"> Then he said, “Hear, house of David! Is it too little for you to make men weary, that you should also make my God weary?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:23; Isaiah 9:6; Luke 1:31"> Therefore the Lord himself will give you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Hebrew is plural">f</a></sup>a sign. Look! the virgin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “young woman”">g</a></sup>is with child and she is about to give birth to a son, and she shall call his name ‘God with us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 7:22; Amos 5:15; Matthew 3:4"> He shall eat curds and honey until he knows to reject the evil and to choose the good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4; Deuteronomy 1:39; Isaiah 9:11"> For before the boy knows to reject the evil and to choose the good, ⸤the land whose two kings you dread will be abandoned.⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the land which you dread will be abandoned because of the face of her two kings”">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## That Day

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 Chronicles 28:19–21"> “Yahweh will bring on you and on your people and on the house of your ancestor<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “father”">i</a></sup>days that have not come since the day Ephraim departed from Judah: the king of Assyria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Joshua 24:12; Deuteronomy 1:44"> And this shall happen: On that day, Yahweh will whistle for the fly that is at the end of the stream of Egypt and the bee that is in the land of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Jeremiah 16:16; Isaiah 2:21"> And all of them will come and settle in the rivers of the cliffs and in the clefts of the rocks and on all of the thornbushes and watering places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Ezekiel 29:18; Isaiah 10:15"> On that day, the Lord will shave the head and the hair of the feet with a razor of the one hired from beyond the river—with the king of Assyria—and it will even take off the beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:17; Jeremiah 39:10; Isaiah 37:30"> And this shall happen: on that day, a young man will keep a young cow of the herd and two sheep alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Matthew 3:4; 2 Samuel 17:29"> And this shall happen: because of the abundance of milk production, he will eat curds, for every one that is left in the midst of the land will eat curds and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Song of Solomon 8:11–12; Matthew 21:33"> And this shall happen on that day: Every place where there are a thousand vines<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “vine”">j</a></sup>for a thousand silver pieces will become briers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “brier”">k</a></sup>and it will be thornbushes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “thornbush”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 27:3"> One will go there with arrows and bow, for all of the land will be briers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “brier”">m</a></sup>and thornbushes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “thornbush”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Isaiah 13:20–22; Isaiah 5:17"> And as for all of the hills that they hoed with the hoe, you will not go there, for fear of briers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “brier”">o</a></sup>and thornbushes.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “thornbush”">p</a></sup>And it will become like pastureland for cattle and overtrodden land for sheep.</span>
</div>
</div>
