## Oracle of Judgment on Moab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 2:1–3; Jeremiah 48:1–47; Isaiah 11:14"> An oracle of Moab:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because Ar is devastated in a night, Moab is destroyed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because Kir of Moab is devastated in a night, it is destroyed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1; Leviticus 21:5; Isaiah 3:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">It has gone up to the house,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “temple”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Dibon to the high places</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for weeping over Nebo,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Moab wails over Medeba.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">⸤Every head is bald⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “On all its heads baldness”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">every beard is shaved.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; 1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">And Heshbon and Elealeh cry</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Hebrew is singular">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their voice is heard as far as Jahaz;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">therefore the armed ones of Moab cry out;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">its soul quivers</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Hebrew words for “cry out” and “quiver” are similar">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:5; Jeremiah 4:20; Luke 19:41–44"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart cries out for Moab;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">its fugitives flee up to Zoar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to Eglath-shelishiyah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For at the ascent of Luhith it goes up it with weeping;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for on the road of Horonaim they raise up a cry of destruction.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 19:5–7; Numbers 32:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the waters of Nimrim are wastelands;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the grass has withered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the vegetation has vanished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is no greenness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:36; Nahum 2:12–13; Isaiah 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore they carry the abundance it has made</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their store of goods over the river of the poplars.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Isaiah 15:2–5; Jeremiah 48:31–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">For a cry for help has encircled the territory of Moab,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">her wailing is heard as far as Eglaim,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and her wailing as far as Beer Elim.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 17:25; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the waters of Dimon</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="One of the Dead Sea Scrolls reads “Dibon” here">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">are full of blood;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but I will place added things upon Dimon:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a lion for the survivors</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “survivor”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of Moab</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and for the remnant of the land.</span></span>
