## Isaiah’s Commission

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:41; Revelation 15:8; Revelation 7:15–17"> In the year of the death of Uzziah the king, I saw the Lord sitting on a high and raised throne, and the hem of his robe was filling the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:8; Ezekiel 1:11; Psalm 104:4"> Seraphs were standing above him. ⸤Each had six wings⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “six wings six wings for one”">a</a></sup>with two he covered his face, and with two he covered his feet, and with two he flew.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:8–9; Psalm 72:19; Numbers 14:21"> And the one called to the other and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Holy, holy, holy is Yahweh of hosts!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The ⸤whole earth is full of his glory⸥.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “fullness of all of the earth glory him”">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Exodus 40:34; 2 Chronicles 5:13–1"> And the pivots of the thresholds shook from the sound of those who called, and the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “temple”">c</a></sup>was filled with smoke.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 5:8–9; James 3:6–10; Jeremiah 9:3–8"> And I said, “Woe to me! For I am destroyed!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “silenced”">d</a></sup>For I am a man ⸤of unclean lips⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “unclean of lips”">e</a></sup>and I am living among<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="With an emphatic sense: “in the very heart and midst of”">f</a></sup>a people ⸤of unclean lips⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “unclean of lips”">g</a></sup>for my eyes have seen the king, Yahweh of hosts!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Hebrews 9:22–26; Ezekiel 10:2"> Then one of the seraphs flew to me, and in his hand was a hot coal he had taken from the altar with tongs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; 1 John 1:7; Isaiah 43:25"> And he touched my mouth, and he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look! This has touched your lips</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has removed your guilt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your sin is annulled.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–13; Acts 26:16–17; Ephesians 3:8"> Then I heard the voice of the Lord saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Whom shall I send?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And who will go for us?”</span><br />And I said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I am here!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Send me!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:14–15; Luke 8:10; Mark 4:12"> And he said, “Go and say to this people,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Keep on listening and do not comprehend!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And keep on looking and do not understand!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; Jeremiah 5:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make the heart of this people insensitive,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and make its ears unresponsive,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and shut its eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that it may not look with its eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and listen with its ears</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and comprehend with its mind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and turn back, and it may be healed for him.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1–12; Psalm 94:3; Leviticus 26:31"> Then I said, “Until when, Lord?”<br />And he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Until the cities lie wasted without inhabitant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and houses without people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the land is ruined and a waste,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:29; Deuteronomy 28:64; 2 Kings 25:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">and Yahweh sends the people far away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the abandonment is great in the midst of the land.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 14:7–9; Romans 11:5–6; Genesis 22:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">And ⸤even if only a tenth part remain⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “still in her a tenth”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">⸤again she will be destroyed⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and she will again and she will be to burn”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a terebinth or like an oak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which although felled, a tree stump remains in them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The seed of holiness will be her tree stump.”</span></span>
</div>
