## Hezekiah’s Illness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–8; 2 Chronicles 32:24; 2 Kings 20:1–11"> In those days, Hezekiah became sick to death, and Isaiah son of Amoz, the prophet, came to him and said to him, “Thus says Yahweh: ‘Order your house, for you are about to die, and you shall not recover.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:6; Psalm 91:15; 1 Kings 8:30"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “And”">a</a></sup>Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to Yahweh,</span>

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:37–39; Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 7:11–14"> And this is the sign to you from Yahweh, that Yahweh will do this thing that he has spoken:</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 116:2; Psalm 150:4; Psalm 145:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, save me, and we will play my music on stringed instruments all the days of our lives at the temple</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “house”">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of Yahweh.”</span></span>
