## An Oracle against Babylon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 47:1–15; Jeremiah 50:1–64; Isaiah 1:1"> The oracle of Babylon that Isaiah son of Amoz saw:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 5:26; Jeremiah 50:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Raise a signal on a bare hill,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lift up your voice to them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">wave the hand and may they enter the gateways of the noblemen.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 3:11; Jeremiah 51:20–24; Revelation 17:12–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I myself I have commanded my consecrated ones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have also summoned my mighty warriors concerning my anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the ⸤ones who exalt over⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “exultant of”">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my majesty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 3:14; Revelation 19:11–21; Joel 2:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">A sound, a noise is on the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the likeness of many people!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A sound of the roar of the kingdoms,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of nations gathering!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh of hosts is mustering an army for battle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–46; Matthew 24:31; Jeremiah 50:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are coming from a distant land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the end of the heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh and the weapons of his indignation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to destroy ⸤the whole earth⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “all of the land/earth”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Isaiah 13:9; Amos 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wail, for the day of Yahweh is near;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it will come like destruction from Shaddai!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Often translated “the Almighty”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Nahum 2:10; Nahum 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore all hands will grow slack,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and every human heart will melt,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 26:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Nahum 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-2">and they will be dismayed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Pangs and labor pains will seize them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will tremble like a woman giving birth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">⸤They will stare at one another⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “A man will stare at his neighbor”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤their faces flushing⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “faces of flames”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Malachi 4:1; Isaiah 13:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look! The day of Yahweh is coming,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">cruel and wrath and the burning of anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to make the earth a desolation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will destroy its sinners from it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:31; Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the stars of the heavens and their constellations will not flash forth their light;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the sun will keep back when it comes out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the moon will not cause its light to shine.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 2:11; Isaiah 5:15; Daniel 5:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will punish the world for its evil</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the wicked for their iniquity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And I will put an end to the pride of the arrogant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will bring the haughtiness of tyrants low.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 14:19–22; Jeremiah 50:27; Isaiah 47:9–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everyone who is found will be pierced through,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and everyone who is carried away will fall by the sword.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:10; Hosea 10:14; Psalm 137:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">And their children will be dashed into pieces before their eyes;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their houses will be plundered, and their wives will be raped.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The reading tradition (Qere ) softens this to “slept with”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 5:28–31; Jeremiah 51:11; Isaiah 41:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look! I am stirring the Medes up against them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who do not value silver</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do not delight in gold.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Isaiah 13:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">And their bows will shatter young men.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And they will not show mercy on the fruit of the womb;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their eyes will not look compassionately on children.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:30; Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 51:41"> And Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the splendor of the Chaldeans’ pride, will be like ⸤when God overthrew⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the overthrow by God of”">h</a></sup>Sodom and Gomorrah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:43; Isaiah 14:23; Jeremiah 50:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will not be inhabited forever,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it will not be dwelled in ⸤forever⸥;</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “until generation and generation”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and no Arab will pitch a tent there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and shepherds will not allow their flocks to lie down there.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Isaiah 34:11–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But wild animals will lie down there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their houses will be full of howling creatures,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the daughters of ostriches</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “ostrich”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will live there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and goats will dance there.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:33; Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 35:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">And hyenas will answer in its palaces,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and jackals in the pleasure palaces;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and its time ⸤is coming soon⸥,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “close to come”">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its days will not be prolonged.</span></span>
</div>
