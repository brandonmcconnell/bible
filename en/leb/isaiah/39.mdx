## The Delegation from Babylon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 20:12–19; Isaiah 39:1–8; 2 Samuel 10:2"> At that time, Merodach-Baladan, son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a present to Hezekiah, for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “and”">a</a></sup>he heard that he had been sick and recovered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:25; 2 Chronicles 32:31; Job 31:25"> And Hezekiah rejoiced over them and showed them his house of aromatic gum, the silver, gold, spices, good oil, all the house of his weapons, and all that was found in his storehouses. There was nothing that Hezekiah had not shown them in his house or in all his dominion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; 2 Chronicles 16:7; 2 Samuel 12:1"> And Isaiah the prophet came to King Hezekiah and said to him, “What did these men say, and from where did they come to you?” And Hezekiah ⸤answered⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “said”">b</a></sup>“They came to me from a distant country, from Babylon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 31:33; Joshua 7:19; 1 John 1:9"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, Isaiah">c</a></sup>said, “What have they seen in your house?” And Hezekiah ⸤answered⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “said”">d</a></sup>“They have seen all that is in my house. There is nothing that I have not shown them in my storehouses.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13–14; 1 Samuel 15:16"> And Isaiah said to Hezekiah, “Hear the word of Yahweh of hosts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 25:13–15; 2 Kings 24:13; Jeremiah 20:5"> ‘Look! days are coming, and all that is in your house and that which your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>have stored up to this day shall be carried off to Babylon. Nothing shall be left,’ says Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 1:1–7; 2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Kings 24:12"> ‘And some of your sons who go out from you, whom you fathered, shall be taken, and they shall be eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:26; 2 Chronicles 34:28; 1 Peter 5:6"> And Hezekiah said to Isaiah, “The word of Yahweh that you have spoken is good,” for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “and”">f</a></sup>he ⸤thought⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “said”">g</a></sup>“Surely there will be peace and security in my days.”</span>
</div>
</div>
