## A Sign regarding Egypt and Cush

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Jeremiah 25:29–30; Amos 1:8"> In the year ⸤the commander-in-chief came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “of the coming of the commander in chief”">a</a></sup>to Ashdod, when Sargon the king of Assyria sent him, and he fought against Ashdod and he took it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Matthew 3:4; 1 Samuel 19:24"> at that time, Yahweh had spoken by the hand of Isaiah son of Amoz, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Go and loosen the sackcloth from your loins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and take off your sandals</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “sandal”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from your feet,”</span><br />and he had done so, walking naked and barefoot.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:5–6"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “And”">c</a></sup>Yahweh said, “Just as my servant Isaiah has walked naked and barefoot three years as a sign and a portent against Egypt and Cush,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:4; Jeremiah 13:26; Jeremiah 13:22"> so shall the king of Assyria lead the captives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “captive”">d</a></sup>of Egypt and the exiles of Cush, young and old, naked and barefoot, ⸤with bared buttocks⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “and bare of buttocks”">e</a></sup>the shame of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 30:5; Isaiah 30:3; 2 Kings 18:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they shall be dismayed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they shall be ashamed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of Cush, their hope,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and because of Egypt, their pride.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Hebrews 2:3"> And the inhabitant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “inhabitant”">f</a></sup>of the coastland will say this on that day:<br />‘Look! This is our hope to whom we fled for help, to be delivered ⸤from⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from the face of”">g</a></sup>the king of Assyria, and how shall we escape?’ ”</span>
</div>
