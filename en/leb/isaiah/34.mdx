## Judgment on the Nations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 68:30; Numbers 23:22; Jeremiah 50:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">And wild oxen shall go down with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and steers with strong bulls.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And their land shall be drenched with</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “from”">i</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">blood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their soil shall be fattened with</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “from”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">fat.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Psalm 11:6; Genesis 19:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">And its streams shall be changed to pitch and its soil to sulfur,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its land shall become like burning pitch.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:6–8; 1 Corinthians 13:2; Jeremiah 39:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Its nobles—but no kingdom is there—shall call,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all its princes shall be nothing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 10:22; Malachi 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And thorns shall go up her citadel fortress,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">weeds</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “weed”">m</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and thistle plants</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “thistle plant”">n</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in her fortresses;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it shall be the settlement of jackals,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">green grass for the daughters of an ostrich.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 13:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">And desert creatures shall meet with hyenas,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a goat-demon shall call to his neighbor;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">surely there Lilith</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew transliteration; possibly a proper name for a Mesopotamian night-demon">o</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shall repose,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and she shall find a resting place for herself.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">There the owl shall nest and lay</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and hatch and care for her chicks in her shadow;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">surely there the birds of prey shall be gathered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">each one with her mate.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 33:9; Joshua 1:8; Matthew 5:18"> Seek from the book of Yahweh and ⸤read⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “call”">p</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">none of these shall be missing;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">none shall miss her mate.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="The Dead Sea Scroll has “his”">q</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">mouth ⸤is the one that⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “he”">r</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">has commanded,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his spirit ⸤is the one that⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “he”">s</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">has gathered them.</span></span>
