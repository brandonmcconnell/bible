## The Reign of Elah in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> In the twenty-sixth year of Asa king of Judah, Elah the son of Baasha became king over Israel for two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 18:3; Genesis 39:4; Daniel 5:1–4"> His servant Zimri the commander of half of the chariots conspired against him. Now he had been in Tirzah drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza who was over the palace in Tirzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 9:31"> Zimri came and struck him down and killed him in the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah and became king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:22; 1 Kings 16:3; 1 Kings 15:29"> It happened that as soon as he became king, at the moment he sat on his throne, he killed all of the house of Baasha. He left ⸤no males⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “no one for him who urinated against a wall”">b</a></sup>among his kindred or any of his friends.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 16:1; 1 Kings 16:7; 2 Chronicles 10:15"> So Zimri destroyed all of the house of Baasha according to the word of Yahweh which he spoke against Baasha by the hand of Jehu the prophet</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; 1 Kings 15:30; Isaiah 41:29"> because of all the sins of Baasha and the sins of Elah his son which they committed and which they caused Israel to sin by provoking Yahweh the God of Israel with their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The remainder of the acts of Elah and all that he did, are they not written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
</div>

## The Very Brief Reign of Zimri in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 15:27; Joshua 19:44; Job 20:5"> In the twenty-seventh year of Asa king of Judah, Zimri reigned seven days in Tirzah. Now the army was encamping against Gibbethon which belonged to the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:2; 2 Kings 8:26; Micah 6:16"> The encamping army heard that Zimri had conspired and moreover had killed the king,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, Elah">c</a></sup>so they made Omri the commander of the army of Israel king over all Israel in the camp on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 9:50; Luke 19:43–44; 2 Kings 18:9–12"> Then Omri went up and all Israel with him from Gibbethon, and they besieged Tirzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:23; 1 Samuel 31:4–5; Job 2:9–10"> It happened that when Zimri saw that the city was taken, he went to the citadel fortress of the house of the king, and he burnt the house of the king over him with fire so that he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 15:26; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 12:28"> This happened because of his sin which he committed by doing evil in the eyes of Yahweh, by going the way of Jeroboam and in his sin which he did by causing Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 16:14; 1 Kings 16:5; 1 Kings 16:27"> The remainder of the acts of Zimri and his conspiracy which he made, are they not written on the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
</div>

## The Reign of Omri in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 12:25; Ephesians 4:3–5; Proverbs 28:2"> At that time, the people of Israel were divided into two parts: half of the people went after Tibni the son of Ginath to make him king, and the other half went after Omri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The people who went after Omri overcame the people who went after Tibni the son of Ginath, so that he died and Omri became king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 16:8; 1 Kings 16:29; 2 Chronicles 22:2"> In the thirty-first year of Asa king of Judah, Omri became king over Israel for twelve years. He reigned in Tirzah six years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 13:32; 1 Kings 16:32; Acts 8:5–8"> then bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer for two talents of silver, fortified the hill, and called the name of the city Samaria that he built after Shemer, the owner of the hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 6:16; 1 Kings 16:30–31; 1 Kings 14:9"> But Omri did evil in the eyes of Yahweh more than all who were before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 16:19; 1 Kings 16:13; 1 Kings 12:26–33"> He went in all the way of Jeroboam the son of Nebat and in his sins that he caused Israel to sin by provoking Yahweh the God of Israel with their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 16:20; 1 Kings 16:14; 1 Kings 16:5"> The remainder of the acts of Omri that he did and his powerful deeds, are they not written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Omri slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “fathers”">d</a></sup>and he was buried in Samaria, and Ahab his son became king in his place.</span>
</div>

## The Reign of Ahab in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Now Ahab son of Omri became king over Israel in the thirty-eighth year of Asa king of Judah. Ahab son of Omri reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty-two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 16:25; 1 Kings 21:25; 1 Kings 14:9"> But Ahab son of Omri did evil in the eyes of Yahweh more than all who were before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 17:16; 2 Kings 10:18; Judges 18:7"> ⸤If it wasn’t enough that he went⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “It happened that it was trivial his going”">e</a></sup>after the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, he also took as wife Jezebel the daughter of Ethbaal the king of the Sidonians. He went and served Baal and bowed down to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Kings 10:21; 2 Kings 10:26–27"> And he built an altar to Baal in the house of Baal which he had built in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 13:6; 1 Kings 21:25; 2 Kings 21:3"> Ahab also made the sacred pole, and ⸤he continued to provoke⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “Ahab added to make to provoke”">f</a></sup>Yahweh the God of Israel more than all the kings of Israel who were before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Joshua 23:14–15; Matthew 24:35"> In his days, Hiel the Bethelite rebuilt Jericho; at the cost of Abiram his firstborn he laid its foundation, and at the cost of Segub his younger sibling, he set up its gates, according to the word of Yahweh which he spoke by the hand of Joshua the son of Nun.</span>
