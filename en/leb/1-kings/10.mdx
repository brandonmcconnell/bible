## The Visit of the Queen of Sheba

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31; 2 Chronicles 9:1–12"> Now the queen of Sheba had heard of the fame of Solomon regarding the name of Yahweh, and she came to test him with hard questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 25:23; Psalm 4:4; Job 4:2"> So she came to Jerusalem with very great wealth; with camels carrying spices, very much gold, and precious stones. She came to Solomon, and she spoke to him all that was on her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 1:5–6; Matthew 13:11; 1 Kings 10:1"> ⸤Solomon answered all of her questions⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “Solomon told her all of her words”">a</a></sup>there was not a thing hidden from the king which he could not explain to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 12:42; 1 Kings 3:28; 2 Chronicles 9:3–4"> When the queen of Sheba observed all the wisdom of Solomon and the house which he had built,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:16; 1 Kings 4:22–23; 2 Kings 16:18"> the food of his table, the seat of his servants, the ⸤manner⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “service”">b</a></sup>of his servants and their clothing, his cupbearers, and his burnt offerings which he offered in the house of Yahweh, ⸤she was breathless⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “and there was not in her spirit/breath any longer”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:5–6"> Then she said to the king, “The report which I heard in my land was true concerning your accomplishments and your wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:9; John 20:25–29; 1 John 3:2"> I had not believed the report to be true until I came and my eyes had seen, and behold! The half had not been told to me. ⸤Your wisdom and prosperity surpass⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “You have added wisdom and prosperity to”">d</a></sup>the report that I had heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 8:34; Proverbs 10:21; Luke 11:31"> Happy are your men and happy are these your servants who stand before you continually hearing your wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 72:2; 1 Kings 5:7; 2 Samuel 8:15"> May Yahweh your God be blessed, who has delighted in you to set you on the throne of Israel, because of the love of Yahweh for Israel forever, and he has made you king to execute justice and righteousness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 10:2; Matthew 2:11; 1 Kings 9:14"> Then she gave the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold, abundant spices, and precious stones. Spices as these did not come again in such abundance as that which the queen of Sheba brought to King Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 9:27–28; 2 Chronicles 8:18; Psalm 45:9"> Moreover, the fleet of ships of Hiram which carried the gold from Ophir also brought from Ophir abundant amounts of almug wood and precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:1–31; Psalm 150:3–5; Revelation 14:2–3"> The king made a raised structure for the house of Yahweh and for the house of the king out of the almug wood, as well as lyres and harps for the singers. This much almug wood has not come nor been seen again up to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 37:4; Matthew 15:28; Psalm 20:4"> King Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all of her desire that she asked, besides that which ⸤King Solomon freely offered her⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “according to the hand of King Solomon”">e</a></sup>Then she turned and went to her land with her servants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:13–28; 1 Kings 9:28"> The weight of the gold that came to Solomon in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 72:10; 1 Chronicles 9:24; Isaiah 21:13"> apart from that of the men of the traders and the profits of the traders, and all the kings of the Arabs and the governors of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 14:26–28; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16"> King Solomon made two hundred shields of hammered gold; six hundred measures of gold went up over each shield.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 1 Kings 14:26"> Also he made three hundred small shields of hammered gold; three minas of gold went up over each of the small shields; and the king put them into the House of the Forest of Lebanon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 45:8; 1 Kings 10:22; Revelation 20:11"> The king also made a large ivory throne, and he overlaid it with fine gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Six steps led up to the throne, and there was a circular top to the throne behind it, and armrests were ⸤on each side of the seat⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from this and from this”">f</a></sup>with two lions standing beside the armrests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 23:24"> Twelve lions were standing there, six on each of the six steps ⸤on either side⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from this and from this”">g</a></sup>nothing like this was made for any of the kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 10:17; 1 Kings 7:2; 2 Chronicles 9:20–22"> All of the drinking vessels of King Solomon were gold, and all the vessels for the House of the Forest of Lebanon were pure gold. There was no silver; it was not considered as something valuable in the days of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 22:48; Genesis 10:4; 2 Chronicles 20:36–37"> For the fleet of Tarshish belonged to the king and was on the sea with the fleet of Hiram; once every three years the fleet of Tarshish used to come carrying gold and silver, ivory, apes, and baboons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12–13; Colossians 2:2–3; 1 Kings 4:29–34"> King Solomon was greater than all the kings of the earth with respect to wealth and wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 1:5; 1 Kings 3:28"> All of the earth was seeking the presence of Solomon, to hear his wisdom which God had put in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 42:11; Psalm 72:10; Psalm 72:15"> They were each bringing his gift; objects of silver and objects of gold, clothing, weapons, spices, horses, and mules. ⸤This used to happen year after year⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “A thing of year to year”">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:25; 1 Kings 4:26; Deuteronomy 17:16"> Solomon gathered chariots and horses; he had fourteen hundred chariots and twelve thousand horses. He stationed them in the cities of the chariots and with the king in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:15–17; Deuteronomy 17:17; 2 Chronicles 9:27"> The king made the silver in Jerusalem as the stones, and the cedars he made as the sycamore fig trees which are in the Shephelah in abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:16; 2 Chronicles 9:28; Isaiah 31:1–3"> The import of the horses which were Solomon’s was from Egypt and from Kue; the traders of the king received horses from Kue at a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; 2 Kings 7:6–7; Malachi 1:1"> A chariot went up and went out from Egypt at six hundred silver shekels and a horse at a hundred and fifty. So it was for all the kings of the Hittites and for the kings of Aram; by their hand they were exported.</span>
</div>
</div>
