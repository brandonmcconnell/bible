## Syrian-Israeli Conflicts: Ben-Hadad vs. Ahab of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 15:18; 1 Kings 15:20; 1 Kings 22:31"> Ben-Hadad king of Aram gathered all of his army, and thirty-two kings were with him, and horses and chariots. He went up and laid siege against Samaria and fought with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 36:2–22; 2 Kings 19:9; Isaiah 37:9–10"> He sent messengers to the city to Ahab king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:9; Isaiah 10:13–14"> He said to him, “Thus says Ben-Hadad: ‘Your silver and your gold are mine, and your women and your best sons are mine.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 15:11–13; Deuteronomy 28:48; 1 Samuel 13:6–7"> Then the king of Israel answered and said, “As your word, my master the king; I am yours, and all that is mine is yours.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The messengers returned and said, “Thus says Ben-Hadad, saying, ‘I sent to you saying, “Your silver and gold are mine, and your women and your best sons you must give to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:34; Joel 3:5; 2 Kings 18:31–32"> So at this time tomorrow, I will send my servants to you that they might search your house and the houses of your servants. All the desire of your eyes ⸤they will lay hands on⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “they will put in their hands”">a</a></sup>and take it away.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 5:7; Proverbs 6:14; 1 Kings 8:1"> Then the king of Israel called all the elders of the land and said, “Please know and realize that this man is seeking trouble, for he sent to me for my women, my sons, my silver, and my gold, and I did not withhold anything from him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> All of the elders and all of the people said to him, “Do not listen and do not consent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So he said to the messengers of Ben-Hadad, “Say to my lord the king, ‘All that you demanded from your servant at the first, I will do, but this thing I am not able to do.’ ” Then the messengers went and ⸤made a report to him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “returned him a word”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 19:2; Exodus 11:8; 2 Kings 19:23–24"> Then Ben-Hadad sent to him and said, “Thus may the gods do to me and thus may they add if the dust of Samaria is sufficient for the hollow of a hand for all of the people who are at my feet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Isaiah 10:15–16; 1 Samuel 17:44–47"> The king of Israel answered and said, “Tell him, ‘Let not him who girds on his armor boast as one who takes off his armor.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 16:9; 1 Kings 20:16; Proverbs 31:4–5"> It happened at the moment he heard this word, he and the kings were drinking in the ⸤tents⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “booths”">c</a></sup>He said to his servants, “Get ready to attack.” So they got ready to attack the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 20:28; Ezekiel 20:22; Isaiah 7:1–9"> Suddenly a certain prophet approached Ahab king of Israel and said, “Thus says Yahweh: ‘Have you seen all this great crowd? Behold, I am giving it into your hand today, that you may know that I am Yahweh.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:50; Genesis 14:14–16; 1 Kings 18:44"> Ahab said, “By whom?” And he said, “Thus says Yahweh: ‘By the servants of the commanders of the provinces.’ ” He asked, “Who will begin the battle?” And he said, “You.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 7:16; 1 Samuel 14:2; Judges 7:7"> So he mustered the servants of the commanders of the provinces, and there were two hundred and thirty-two. After them he mustered all of the army, all the sons of Israel, seven thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16–17; Hosea 4:11; 1 Kings 16:9"> They went out at noon while Ben-Hadad was drinking himself drunk in the tents, he and the thirty-two kings helping him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 20:14–15; 1 Kings 20:19"> Then the servants of the commanders of the provinces went out first, and Ben-Hadad sent, and they reported to him, saying, “Men have come out from Samaria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 14:8–12; 1 Samuel 14:11–12; Proverbs 18:12"> Then he said, “If they have come out for peace, seize them alive; and if they have come out for war, seize them alive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But these had come out from the city, the servants of the commanders of the provinces, and the army that was after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:16–17; 2 Kings 19:36; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> Each man killed his man, and the Arameans fled, so Israel pursued them, but Ben-Hadad king of Aram escaped on a horse with cavalry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 3:18; Judges 3:28; 2 Kings 3:24"> The king of Israel went out and attacked the horses and the chariots and defeated Aram with a great blow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:1; 1 Kings 20:26; 1 Kings 20:13"> Then the prophet came near to the king of Israel, and he said to him, “Go, strengthen yourself; ⸤consider well⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “know and see”">d</a></sup>what you should do, for the king of Aram is coming against you at the turn of the year.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; 1 Samuel 4:8; Psalm 50:21–22"> The servants of the king of Aram said to him, “Their gods are gods of the mountains, therefore they were stronger than we.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">e</a></sup>Let us fight with them in the plain; surely we will be stronger than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 22:31; Proverbs 21:30; 1 Kings 20:16"> Do this thing: remove the kings each from his post, and put a governor in their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 10:3"> You must muster an army for yourself as the army ⸤you have lost⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “as the army which fell from with you”">f</a></sup>and horses and chariots as the horses and chariots you lost, then we will fight them in the plain. Surely we will be stronger than they.” So he listened to their voice and did so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 13:17; 1 Kings 20:22; 1 Samuel 4:1"> It happened at the turning of the year that Ben-Hadad mustered Aram and went up to Aphek for the war with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 6:5; 1 Samuel 13:5–8; 1 Samuel 14:2"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>had been mustered and provisioned, and they went to engage them. The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>encamped opposite them as two flocks of goats, but the Arameans filled the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 20:13; 1 Kings 17:18; Ezekiel 12:16"> Then the man of God approached, and he spoke to the king of Israel, and he said, “Thus says Yahweh: ‘Because Aram has said, “Yahweh is a god of the mountains and not a god of the valleys,” ’ I will give all this great crowd into your hand that you may know that I am Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:17; 2 Chronicles 28:6; Psalm 10:16"> These encamped opposite for seven days, and it happened on the seventh day that ⸤the battle began⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the battle drew near”">i</a></sup>and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>killed the Arameans, one hundred thousand infantry in one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 22:25; 2 Chronicles 18:24; 1 Kings 20:26"> Then those who remained fled to Aphek, to the city, and the wall fell on twenty-seven thousand men who had remained, so Ben-Hadad fled and went to the innermost rooms of the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:31; Genesis 37:34; 1 Kings 20:23"> Then his servants said to him, “Please now, we have heard that the kings of the house of Israel are kings of mercy. Let us now put sackcloth on around our waists and ropes on our heads. Then let us go out to the king of Israel. Perhaps ⸤he will let you live⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “he may let your life live”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 20:3–6; Job 12:17–18; Daniel 5:20–23"> So they tied sackcloth around their waists and ropes on their heads. Then they went to the king of Israel and said, “Your servant Ben-Hadad says, ‘Please let me live.’ ” And he said, “Is my brother still alive?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 16:8; 2 Kings 10:15; Proverbs 25:13"> The men took this as a good omen and they quickly accepted it as true from him, and they said, “Your brother Ben-Hadad lives.” So he said, “Go, get him.” Ben-Hadad came out to him, and Ahab pulled him up on the chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 15:20; Isaiah 26:10; 1 Kings 20:42"> Ben-Hadad said to him, “The cities which my father took from your father I shall return. You may set up streets with stalls for yourself in Damascus just as my father set up in Samaria.” Then Ahab said, “⸤On these terms⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “In covenant”">l</a></sup>I will let you go,” So he made a covenant with him and let him go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 2:15; 1 Kings 13:17–18; 2 Kings 4:1"> A certain man from the sons of the prophets said to his fellow countryman, “By the word of Yahweh, please strike me.” But the man refused to strike him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 13:21–24; 1 Kings 13:26; 1 Samuel 15:22–23"> He said to him, “Because you have not obeyed the voice of Yahweh, look, as you now are going from me, a lion will kill you.” When he went from beside him, the lion found him and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 21:12; 1 Kings 20:35"> Then he found another man and said, “Strike me, please,” so the man struck him sharply and wounded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 14:2; Matthew 6:16; 1 Kings 22:30"> Then the prophet went and ⸤waited⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “stood”">m</a></sup>for the king along the road and disguised himself with a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">n</a></sup>headband over his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Kings 10:24; Job 36:18; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> As the king was passing by, he called to the king and said, “Your servant went out in the thick of the battle, and suddenly a man turned and brought a man to me and said, ‘Guard this man. If by any means he should be missed, it will be your life in his place, or you shall pay a talent of silver.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:5–7; Matthew 25:24–27; Job 15:6"> It happened that your servant ⸤was busy here and there⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “was doing here and here”">o</a></sup>and he ⸤disappeared⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and he was not”">p</a></sup>Then the king said to him, “Your own judgment has been determined.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; 2 Samuel 13:19; Job 2:8"> He quickly removed the headband from his eyes, and the king of Israel recognized him, that he was from the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 22:31–37; 2 Chronicles 18:33–34; 1 Kings 20:39"> He said to him, “Thus says Yahweh: ‘Because you have let the man I devoted for destruction go from your hand, your life shall be in place of his life and your people in place of his people.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Kings 21:4; Esther 5:13; Esther 6:12–13"> Then the king of Israel went to his house, sullen and angry, and he came to Samaria.</span>
</div>
</div>
