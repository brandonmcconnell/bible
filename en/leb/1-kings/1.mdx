## Solomon Is Crowned King

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:38; 1 Kings 1:26"> Then King David said, “Summon Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada,” and they came before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:30; 1 Kings 1:38; 2 Chronicles 33:14"> The king said to them, “Take with you all the servants of your lord, and let them make Solomon my son ride on my mule, and bring him down to Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 2 Samuel 15:10; 1 Samuel 10:1"> Let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him there as king over Israel. Blow on the trumpet and say, ‘Long live King Solomon!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 1:17; Psalm 72:1–2; 1 Kings 1:13"> Then you shall go up after him, and let him come and sit on my throne; he shall be king in my place. I have appointed him to be leader over Israel and Judah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:6; 1 Corinthians 14:16; Psalm 72:19"> Then Benaiah the son of Jehoiada answered the king and said, “Amen! So may Yahweh, the God of my lord the king, confirm it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:13; 1 Kings 1:47; Joshua 1:17"> As Yahweh was with my lord the king, so may he be with Solomon and make his throne greater than the throne of my lord King David.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 1:33"> Then Zadok the priest, Nathan the prophet, Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the Kerethites, and the Pelethites went down, and they let Solomon ride on the mule of King David, and they brought him to Gihon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalm 89:20; 1 Kings 1:34; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> Then Zadok the priest took the horn of oil from the tent, and he anointed Solomon. They blew on the trumpet, and all the people said, “Long live King Solomon!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 11:14; Zechariah 9:9; 2 Kings 11:20"> All the people went up after him, and the people were playing on the flutes and rejoicing with great joy, and the earth shook with their noise.</span>

## Adonijah’s Response to the Coronation of Solomon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:36; 2 Samuel 17:17; 2 Samuel 18:27"> While he was still speaking, suddenly Jonathan the son of Abiathar the priest came. Adonijah said, “Come, for you are a man of valor, and you bring good news.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Jonathan answered and said to Adonijah, “But our lord King David has made Solomon king!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> He sent Zadok the priest with the king, and Nathan the prophet, Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the Kerethites, and the Pelethites; they made him ride on the king’s mule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 1:40; Daniel 5:26–28; 1 Samuel 4:5"> Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anointed him as king at Gihon, and they have gone up from there rejoicing. The city has gone wild; this is the sound which you heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:23; Psalm 132:11; 1 Kings 1:13"> And also, Solomon sits on the throne of the kingdom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Genesis 47:31; 1 Kings 1:37; Ezra 6:10"> The servants of the king also came to congratulate our lord King David, saying, ‘Your God has made the name of Solomon better than your name and his throne greater than your throne!’ So the king worshiped on the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Kings 3:6; Psalm 132:11–12; Psalm 145:2"> What is more, the king said, ‘May Yahweh the God of Israel be blessed, who has given this day one sitting on my throne, and my eyes are seeing it!’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 21:4–5; Proverbs 28:1; Daniel 5:4–6"> Then all the invited guests who were for Adonijah trembled and got up and went, each on his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28; Exodus 21:14; Exodus 27:2"> Adonijah was afraid because of Solomon, and he got up and went and grasped the horns of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Solomon was told, “Look, Adonijah is afraid of King Solomon, and he has grasped the horns of the altar, saying, ‘Let King Solomon swear to me ⸤first⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “as the day”">p</a></sup>that he will surely not kill his servant with the sword!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:45; 2 Samuel 14:11; Proverbs 13:6"> Solomon said, “If he is a son of noble character, not a hair of his head will fall to the ground, but if evil is found in him, then he will die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Kings 1:16; 2 Samuel 14:24; 2 Samuel 1:2"> Then King Solomon sent and brought him down from upon the altar. He came and did obeisance to King Solomon. Solomon said to him, “Go to your house.”</span>
