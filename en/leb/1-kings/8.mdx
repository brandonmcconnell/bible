## Solomon’s Proclamation to the Assembly of Israel

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 97:2; 2 Chronicles 6:1–11; Leviticus 16:2"> Then Solomon said, “Yahweh has said that he would dwell in the very thick cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:13; Psalm 132:13–14; Hebrews 9:24"> I have certainly built a lofty house for you, a place for you to live forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:18; 1 Kings 8:55–56; 2 Chronicles 30:18–20"> Then the king turned his face around, and he blessed all of the assembly of Israel. (Now all the assembly of Israel was standing).</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Chronicles 29:20; 1 Chronicles 29:10"> Then he said, “Blessed be Yahweh the God of Israel who has promised with his mouth to David my father and fulfilled ⸤by his oath⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “by his hand”">j</a></sup>saying,</span>

## Solomon’s Prayer to Yahweh

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:22; Deuteronomy 7:9; Daniel 9:4"> and he said, “O Yahweh, God of Israel, there is no god like you in the heavens above or on the earth beneath, keeping the covenant and the loyal love for your servants who are walking before you with all their heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:16; 1 Kings 8:15; 2 Samuel 7:12"> You have kept for your servant David my father what you promised to him, and you have spoken with your mouth, and with your hand you have fulfilled it this very day.</span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 8:39; 1 Kings 8:34; 1 Kings 8:36"> You must listen to the plea of your servant and your people Israel which they pray toward this place; and you must hear from the place where you live, from heaven you must hear and you must forgive.</span>

<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalm 73:21–22; 1 Kings 8:22; Philippians 4:6"> any prayer or any plea which is offered by any person for all of your people Israel, who each knows the infestation of his own heart and spreads out his palms to this house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Jeremiah 17:10; Acts 1:24"> then you shall hear in heaven the place of your dwelling, and you shall forgive and act and give to the man whose heart you know, according to all his ways, for you alone know the heart of all the sons of man.</span>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1–2; Acts 8:27–40; Exodus 18:8–12"> Also for the foreigner who is not from your people Israel, and he comes from a distant land because of your name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:24; 2 Kings 17:36; Exodus 3:13–16"> (for they shall hear of your great name and your powerful hand and your outstretched arm), and he shall come and pray toward this house,</span>

<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:34; Psalm 78:67–69; 1 Kings 8:16"> If your people go out to battle against his enemy in the way that you shall send them and they pray to Yahweh, toward the city which you have chosen and the house which I have built for your name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Psalm 9:4; Jeremiah 5:28"> then you shall hear in heaven their prayer and their plea, and you shall ⸤vindicate⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “their judgment”">t</a></sup>them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; Proverbs 20:9; 1 John 1:8–10"> “If they sin against you (for there is not a person who does not sin) and you are angry with them and you give them to an enemy and they take them captive to the land of the enemy far or near,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalm 106:6; Nehemiah 1:6–11; Nehemiah 9:26–30"> and then they return their heart in the land where they have been taken captive and they return and plead to you in the land of their captivity, saying, ‘We have sinned and we did wrong. <span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Daniel 6:10; Jeremiah 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29"> if they return to you with all of their heart and with all of their soul in the land of their enemies who took them captive and they pray to you toward their land which you gave to their ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “fathers”">u</a></sup>the city that you have chosen and the house that you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="The Hebrew Masoretic text (Kethib ) reads “you have built”;Qere reads “I have built”">v</a></sup>built for your name,</span>

<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalm 106:46; 2 Chronicles 30:9; Acts 7:10"> You shall forgive your people who sinned against you, even for all their transgressions which they committed against you. <span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:10; Jeremiah 11:4; Deuteronomy 4:20"> for they are your people and your inheritance whom you brought from Egypt from the middle of the smelter of iron.</span>
