## The Prophet Elijah Arises

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25–26; James 5:17; Luke 1:17"> Elijah the Tishbite from Tishbe of Gilead said to Ahab, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">a</a></sup>the God of Israel before whom I stand, there shall surely not be dew nor rain these years ⸤except by my command⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “except at the word of my mouth”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:1; Jeremiah 7:1; Hosea 1:1–2"> Then the word of Yahweh came to him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Jeremiah 36:26; Psalm 83:3"> “Go from this place and turn to the east; you must hide yourself in the Wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">c</a></sup>Kerith ⸤which faces the Jordan⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “on the face of the Jordan”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9; Psalm 147:9; Job 38:41"> It shall be that you shall drink from the wadi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">e</a></sup>and I have commanded the crows to sustain you there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; Matthew 16:24; John 15:14"> So he went and did according to the word of Yahweh. He went and stayed in the Wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">f</a></sup>Kerith ⸤which faces the Jordan⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “on the face of the Jordan”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 78:23–24; Matthew 6:31–33; Jeremiah 37:21"> The crows were bringing bread and meat in the morning for him and bread and meat in the evening, and he drank from the wadi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 54:10; Isaiah 40:30–31"> It happened ⸤after a while⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “from the end of days”">i</a></sup>that the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A seasonal stream that is often dry">j</a></sup>dried up, because there was no rain in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; 1 Kings 17:2; Hebrews 13:6"> Then the word of Yahweh came to him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:26; Obadiah 1:20; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> “Get up and go to Zarephath which belongs to Sidon and stay there. Look, I have commanded a woman there, a widow, to sustain you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:7; Genesis 24:17; Genesis 21:15"> So he arose and went to Zarephath and came to the gate of the city. There was a widow woman gathering wood, so he called to her, and he said, “Please bring a little water for me in a vessel so that I can drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 25:35–40; 1 Kings 18:4; Hebrews 13:2"> She went to fetch it, and he called to her and said, “Please bring me a morsel of bread in your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; 1 Kings 17:1; Genesis 21:16"> She said, “⸤As Yahweh your God lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh your God”">k</a></sup>surely I do not have a cake, ⸤but only a handful of flour⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “except that which fills a hand with flour”">l</a></sup>in the jar and a little olive oil in the jug. Here I am gathering a few pieces of wood, and I will go and prepare it for me and my son, that we might eat it and die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Malachi 3:10; Genesis 22:1–2"> Elijah said to her, “Don’t be afraid. Go and do according to your word; only make for me a small bread cake from it first, and bring it out to me. Make it for yourself and for your son afterward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:36–38; Matthew 14:17–20; 2 Kings 4:42–44"> For thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: ‘The jar of flour will not be emptied and the jug of olive oil will not run out until the day Yahweh gives rain on the surface of the earth.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; Mark 12:43; Matthew 15:28"> So she went and did according to the word of Elijah; then both she and he ate with her household for many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:28–30; John 4:50–51; Luke 1:37"> The jar of flour was not emptied and the jug of olive oil did not run out, according to the word of Yahweh which he spoke by the hand of Elijah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; Daniel 5:23; John 11:14"> It happened after these things that the son of the woman, the mistress of the house, became ill; and his illness was very severe until there was no breath left in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 5:8; John 2:4; 2 Kings 3:13"> She said to Elijah, “⸤What do you have against me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “What for me and for you”">m</a></sup>O man of God, that you have come to me to make known my guilt and to cause my son to die?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 4:32; 2 Kings 4:10; Acts 9:37"> Then he said to her, “Give me your son.” He took him from her lap and carried him up to the upper room where he was staying, and he laid him on his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 5:15–18; Matthew 21:22; Joshua 7:8–9"> Then he called to Yahweh and said, “O Yahweh my God, are you also causing evil to come upon the widow with whom I am dwelling as an alien by causing her son to die?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:10; Acts 9:40; 2 Kings 4:33–35"> He stretched himself out on the child three times and called to Yahweh and said, “O Yahweh my God, please let the life of this child return within him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 11:11; Hebrews 11:35"> Yahweh listened to the voice of Elijah, and the life of the child returned within him, and he lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 4:36–37; Acts 9:41; Luke 7:15"> Elijah then took the child and brought him down from the upper room to the house and gave him to his mother. Elijah said, “Look, your son is alive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 16:30; John 2:11; John 3:2"> Then the woman said to Elijah, “Now this I know, that you are a man of God and the word of Yahweh in your mouth is truth.”</span>
</div>
