## War between Elijah and the Prophets of Baal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25; James 5:17; Deuteronomy 28:12"> It happened many days later that the word of Yahweh came to Elijah in the third year, saying, “Go, present yourself to Ahab so that I may give rain on the surface of the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 6:25; Proverbs 28:1; Leviticus 26:26"> So Elijah went to present himself to Ahab. Now the famine was severe in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; 1 Kings 16:9; Matthew 10:28"> Ahab summoned Obadiah who was over the house. (Now Obadiah was fearing Yahweh greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 18:13; Matthew 10:40–42; Revelation 17:4–6"> It had happened that when Jezebel killed the prophets of Yahweh, Obadiah took a hundred prophets and hid them ⸤by fifties⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “fifty men”">a</a></sup>in the cave and sustained them with food and water.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:5–6; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:22"> Ahab said to Obadiah, “Go through the land to all the springs of water and to all the wadis.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">b</a></sup>Perhaps we may find green grass that we may keep horses and mules alive and that we might not lose any of the animals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3"> So they divided the land for themselves in order to pass through it; Ahab went one way by himself, and Obadiah went another way by himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 1:6–8; Genesis 44:20; Isaiah 60:14"> It happened that Obadiah was on the way, and suddenly Elijah was there to meet him. When he recognized him, he fell on his face and said, “Is this you, my lord Elijah?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Kings 18:3; 1 Peter 2:17–18"> He said to him, “I am. Go, say to your lord, ‘Elijah is here.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:21; 1 Kings 18:12; 1 Kings 17:18"> He said, “How have I sinned that you are giving your servant into the hand of Ahab to kill me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 1:29; 1 Kings 18:15"> ⸤As Yahweh your God lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh your God”">c</a></sup>surely there is not a nation or a kingdom to which my lord has not sent me to seek you. If they would say, ‘He is not here,’ then he would make the kingdom or the nation swear that it could not find you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 18:8; 1 Kings 18:14"> Now you are saying, ‘Go, say to your lord: “Elijah is here.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 2:16; Acts 8:39; Ezekiel 8:3"> And it will happen that I will go from you and the Spirit of Yahweh will carry you up to where I do not know. Then I will come to tell Ahab, but he will not find you, and then he will kill me, even though your servant has feared Yahweh from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; Matthew 25:35; Psalm 18:21–24"> Has it not been told to my lord what I did when Jezebel killed the prophets of Yahweh? I hid a hundred men of the prophets of Yahweh ⸤by fifties⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “fifty fifty men”">d</a></sup>in the cave, and I sustained them with food and water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28"> Now you are saying, ‘Go, say to your lord: “Elijah is here,” ’ and he will kill me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 18:10; Luke 2:13–14"> Elijah said, “⸤As Yahweh of hosts lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh of hosts”">e</a></sup>before whom I stand, I will certainly show myself to him today.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So Obadiah went to meet Ahab, and he told him, so Ahab went to meet Elijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 7:25; 1 Kings 21:20; Acts 16:20"> When Ahab saw Elijah, Ahab said to him, “Is this you who throws Israel into confusion?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 1 Kings 9:9; Jeremiah 2:19"> He said, “I did not throw Israel into confusion; rather you and the house of your father have by forsaking the commands of Yahweh when you went after the Baals!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 19:26; 1 Kings 16:33; 2 Peter 2:1"> So then, send word and assemble all of Israel to me on Mount Carmel, with the four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal and the four hundred prophets of Asherah, who eat at the table of Jezebel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:9"> So Ahab sent word among the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>and he assembled the prophets to Mount Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Joshua 24:15; 1 Corinthians 10:21–22"> Elijah approached to all the people and said, “How long will you go limping over two opinions? If Yahweh is God, go after him; but if Baal, go after him.” But the people did not answer him a word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10; 1 Kings 19:14; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> Then Elijah said to the people, “I alone am left a prophet of Yahweh, but the prophets of Baal are four hundred and fifty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Let them give us two bulls, and let them choose for themselves one bull, cut him in pieces, and put it on the wood, but don’t let them start a fire on it. I will prepare the other bull and set it on the wood, but I will put no fire on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 18:38; 1 Chronicles 21:26; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> Then you call on the name of your god, and I will call on the name of Yahweh, and it shall be that the god who answers by fire, he is God.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Baal is known in Canaanite texts as being the god in control of lightning and fire">g</a></sup>Then all the people answered and said, “The word is good!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, “Choose for yourselves one bull and prepare it first, for you are the majority, and call on the name of your god, but don’t set fire under it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Matthew 6:7"> So they took the bull that he allowed to them, prepared it, and called upon the name of Baal from morning until noon, saying, “O Baal, answer us!” But there was no voice and there was no answer, so they limped<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, moved about the altar in a cultic dance step">h</a></sup>about the altar which they had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 78:65–66; Isaiah 41:23; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> It happened at noon that Elijah mocked them and said, “Call out with a loud voice, for he is a god! Perhaps he is meditating, or ⸤is using the bathroom⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “has backed off for himself”">i</a></sup>or is on a journey. Perhaps he is asleep and must wake up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 19:28; Deuteronomy 14:1; Mark 5:5"> So they called out with a loud voice, and they cut themselves with swords and with spears as was their custom, until the blood poured out over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 18:26; 1 Samuel 18:10; Exodus 29:39"> It happened as noon passed, they raged until the time of the evening offering, but there was no voice, there was no answer, and no one paid attention.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:16; 1 Kings 19:14; 1 Kings 19:10"> Then Elijah said to all the people, “Come near to me,” so all the people came closer to him. He repaired the altar of Yahweh that had been destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 35:10; Genesis 32:28"> Elijah took twelve stones according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, to whom the word of God came, saying, “Israel shall be your name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Exodus 20:24–25; Judges 21:4"> With them, he built an altar in the name of Yahweh, and he made a trench ⸤which would have held⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “as the house of”">j</a></sup>about two seahs of seed, all around the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 22:9; Judges 6:20; Leviticus 1:6–8"> And he arranged the wood, cut the bull into pieces, and placed it on the wood.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="in the Hebrew Bible, 1 Kings 18:34 begins here">k</a></sup>Then he said, “Fill four jars with water, and pour it on the burnt offering and on the wood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> He said, “Do it again!” They did it again. He said, “Do it a third time!” So they did it a third time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 18:32; 1 Kings 18:38"> The water went all around the altar, and the trench also was filled with water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 18:29; 2 Kings 19:19; Exodus 3:6"> It happened at the offering of the evening oblation, Elijah the prophet went near, and he said, “O Yahweh, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel; let it be known today that you are God in Israel and that I am your servant and that I have done all of these things by your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Luke 1:16–17; Ezekiel 36:25–27"> Answer me, O Yahweh, answer me; that this people may know that you, O Yahweh, are God and that you have turned their hearts back again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:26; Leviticus 9:24; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> Then the fire of Yahweh fell, and it consumed the burnt offering, and the wood, and the stones, and the dust; and the water which was in the trench it licked up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:24; 2 Chronicles 7:3; 1 Kings 18:21"> When all the people saw, they fell on their faces and said, “Yahweh, he is God! Yahweh, he is God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Judges 4:7; Deuteronomy 13:5"> Then Elijah said to them, “Seize the prophets of Baal; don’t let any man of them escape!” So they seized them, and Elijah brought them down to the wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="A valley that is dry most of the year, but contains a stream during the rainy season">l</a></sup>of Kishon and killed them there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 18:1; Ecclesiastes 9:7; 1 Kings 17:1"> Then Elijah said to Ahab, “Go up, eat and drink, for there is the sound of the noise of rain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Daniel 9:7; Joshua 7:6"> So Ahab went up to eat and to drink while Elijah went to the top of Carmel, bent down to the earth, and put his face between his knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 18:1"> Then he said to his servant, “Please go and look in the direction of the sea.” So he went up and looked; then he said, “There is nothing.” Then he said, “Go back,” seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 12:54; Job 8:7; Zechariah 4:10"> It happened that at the seventh time, he said, “Look, there is a small cloud, as the hand of a man, coming up from the sea.” Then Elijah said, “Go up, say to Ahab, ‘Harness your horses and go down, lest the rain stop you.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 18:39–40; Joshua 17:16; 1 Kings 21:1"> ⸤In no time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “Until here and until here”">m</a></sup>the heavens grew black with clouds and wind, and there was heavy rain. Ahab rode and he went to Jezreel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 1:3; 2 Kings 9:1"> but the hand of Yahweh was on Elijah; he girded up his loins and ran before Ahab as one comes to Jezreel.</span>
</div>
