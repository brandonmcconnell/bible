## Solomon’s Foreign Wives

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Proverbs 6:24; Nehemiah 13:23–27"> King Solomon loved many foreign women: the daughter of Pharaoh, Moabite, Ammonite, Edomite, Sidonian, Hittite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:16; Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Joshua 23:12–13"> from the nations which Yahweh had said to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>“You shall not ⸤marry them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “go into them”">b</a></sup>and they shall not ⸤marry you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “go into you”">c</a></sup>They will certainly turn your heart after other gods.” But Solomon clung to them to love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:28; 2 Samuel 5:13–16; 2 Chronicles 11:21"> He had seven hundred princesses and three hundred concubines, and his wives turned his heart.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon’s Apostasy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:4; 1 Kings 8:61; 1 Kings 11:6"> It happened at the time of Solomon’s old age that his wives guided his heart after other gods, and his heart was not fully with Yahweh his God as the heart of David his father had been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 2:13; 1 Kings 11:7; 2 Kings 23:13"> Solomon went after Ashtoreth the god of the Sidonians and after Milcom the abhorrence of the Ammonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:8; Joshua 14:14"> So Solomon did evil in the eyes of Yahweh and did not fully follow after Yahweh as David his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:29; Judges 11:24; Acts 7:43"> At that time, Solomon built a high place for Chemosh, the abomination of Moab, on the mountain which ⸤faces⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “was on the face of”">d</a></sup>Jerusalem and for Molech, the abomination of the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:11–12; 1 Kings 11:1; Ezekiel 16:22–29"> Thus he did for all of his foreign wives, offering incense and sacrificing to their gods.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh’s Judgment on Solomon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 9:2; 1 Kings 3:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> Yahweh was angry with Solomon, for he had turned his heart from Yahweh, the God of Israel who had appeared to him twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:12–13; 2 Chronicles 7:17–22; 1 Kings 9:4–7"> And he had commanded him concerning this matter not to go after other gods, but he did not keep that which Yahweh commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15–16; 1 Kings 11:31; 2 Kings 17:21"> So Yahweh said to Solomon, “Because this was with you, and you did not keep my covenant and my ordinances which I have commanded you, I will certainly tear the kingdom from you, and I will give it to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 20:19; 1 Samuel 9:4–5; 2 Kings 22:19–20"> However, I will not do it in your days, for the sake of David your father; from the hand of your son I will tear it away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; Deuteronomy 12:11; 1 Kings 11:32"> Yet all of the kingdom I will not tear away. I will give one tribe to your son for the sake of my servant David and for the sake of Jerusalem which I have chosen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Psalm 89:30–34; Isaiah 13:17"> Then Yahweh raised an adversary against Solomon, Hadad the Edomite, from the descendants of that king in Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 1 Chronicles 18:12–13; Deuteronomy 20:13"> It had happened that when David was at Edom, Joab the commander of the army went up to bury the slain, and he killed every male in Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> For Joab and all Israel had stayed there six months until he exterminated every male in Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 2:13–14; Exodus 2:1–10; 2 Kings 11:2"> But Hadad himself had fled, and some Edomite men from the servants of his father with him, to go to Egypt, when Hadad was a young boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 10:12; Deuteronomy 33:2; Deuteronomy 1:1"> They had set out from Midian until they came to Paran where they took men from Paran with them and came to Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt. He gave to him a house and assigned food for him and gave him land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:4; Jeremiah 43:7–9; Genesis 39:21"> Hadad found great favor in the eyes of Pharaoh, and he gave him the sister of his wife, the sister of Tahpenes the queen, as wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:7; 1 Samuel 1:24"> The sister of Tahpenes bore Genubath his son for him, and Tahpenes weaned him in the middle of the house of Pharaoh. Genubath was in the house of Pharaoh in the midst of the children<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “sons”">f</a></sup>of Pharaoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 2:10; Exodus 4:19; Matthew 2:20"> Now Hadad heard in Egypt that David had slept with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup>and that Joab the commander of the army was dead. Then Hadad said to Pharaoh, “Send me away that I may go to my land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:22–23; Luke 22:35; Mark 14:31"> Pharaoh said to him, “What do you lack with me that you now are seeking to go to your land?” He said, “No, but you must surely send me away.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 11:14; 2 Samuel 8:3; 2 Samuel 10:15–18"> God had also raised Rezon the son of Eliada as an adversary against him, who had fled from Hadadezer the king of Zobah, his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:8; 2 Samuel 10:18; 1 Kings 20:34"> He gathered men around him and he became the commander of bandits. When David killed some of them, they went to Damascus and settled ⸤there⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “in it”">h</a></sup>and they reigned in Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; Psalm 106:40; 1 Kings 5:4"> He was an adversary for Israel all the days of Solomon, and along with the evil that Hadad did, he detested Israel while he reigned over Aram.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh Raises Up Jeroboam

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:6; 1 Kings 12:2; 1 Kings 11:28"> Now Jeroboam the son of Nebat, an Ephraimite from Zeredah (now the name of his mother was Zeruah, a widow woman), a servant of Solomon ⸤rebelled against the king⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “raised a hand against the king”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 9:15; Proverbs 30:32"> This is the reason that he rebelled against the king: when Solomon built the Millo, he closed the gap of the city of David his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; 2 Samuel 19:20; Judges 1:22–23"> Now the man Jeroboam was a man of ability, and Solomon saw that the young man ⸤was a diligent worker⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “was a doer of work he”">j</a></sup>so he appointed him over all of the forced labor for the house of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15; 1 Kings 14:2; 2 Chronicles 9:29"> It happened at that time that Jeroboam went out from Jerusalem, and he accidentally met Ahijah the Shilonite the prophet on the way. Now he had clothed himself with new clothing. While the two of them were alone in the field,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:27–28; 1 Samuel 24:4–5"> Ahijah took hold of the new cloak which was on him and tore it into twelve pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 11:11–12"> Then he said to Jeroboam, “Take for yourself ten pieces, for thus says Yahweh, the God of Israel: ‘Behold, I am about to tear the kingdom from the hand of Solomon, and I will give to you ten tribes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 12:20–21; 1 Kings 14:21"> but one tribe shall be for him, for the sake of my servant David and for the sake of Jerusalem, the city which I have chosen from all the tribes of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 11:9; 1 Kings 11:5–7; 1 Kings 9:5–7"> because he has forsaken me, and they bowed down to Ashtoreth, the god of the Sidonians, to Chemosh, the god of Moab, and to Milcom, the god of the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">k</a></sup>They did not walk in my ways to do right in my eyes, my ordinances, or my judgments, as did David his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 11:12–13; 1 Kings 11:31; Job 11:6"> But I will not take all of the kingdom from his hand, but I will make him a leader all the days of his life for the sake of David my servant whom I chose, who kept my commandments and my ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; 1 Kings 11:12; Exodus 20:5–6"> But I will take the kingship from the hand of his son, and I will give ten tribes to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 8:19; 1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 15:4"> To his son I will give one tribe in order to be a lamp for my servant David, always before my face, in Jerusalem the city in which I have chosen to place my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:26; 1 Kings 11:26; 2 Samuel 3:21"> You I will take, and you shall reign over all your soul desires, and you shall be king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:11; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:8"> It shall be that if you listen to all that I command you and you walk in my ways and you do right in my eyes by keeping my statutes and my commandments, as David my servant did, then I will be with you, and I will build an enduring house for you as I built for David, and I will give Israel to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Luke 2:11; Isaiah 11:1–10"> I will punish the offspring of David on account of this; however, not always.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:2–9; Isaiah 14:24–27; Lamentations 3:37"> Then Solomon sought to kill Jeroboam, but Jeroboam got up and fled to Egypt, to Shishak the king of Egypt, and he remained in Egypt until the death of Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:29–31"> Now the rest of the acts of Solomon and all that he did and his wisdom; are they not written on the scroll of the acts of Solomon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11"> All the days that Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all of Israel were forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 21:18; 1 Kings 2:10; Matthew 1:7"> Then Solomon slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>and they buried him in the city of David his father, and Rehoboam his son became king in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>
