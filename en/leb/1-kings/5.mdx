## Solomon’s Wisdom: Architecture

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:1; 2 Chronicles 2:3; 1 Kings 5:10"> Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to Solomon when he heard that they had anointed him as king in place of his father, for Hiram had always been a friend for David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:3"> Then Solomon sent to Hiram, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:3; 1 Chronicles 22:8; Psalm 110:1"> “You knew David my father, that he was not able to build a house for the name of Yahweh his God, ⸤in view of the warfare⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “because of the face of the warfare”">a</a></sup>which surrounded him, until Yahweh placed them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, David’s enemies">b</a></sup>under the soles of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Kings 4:24; Acts 9:31"> But now Yahweh my God has given me rest all around me. There is no adversary, and there is no bad occurrence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:12; 1 Chronicles 22:10; 2 Samuel 7:12–13"> Here I am, intending to build a house for the name of Yahweh my God, as Yahweh promised to my father David, saying, ‘Your son, whom I will set in your place on your throne, shall build the house for my name.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:8; 1 Kings 6:9–10; Romans 12:17"> So then, command that they may cut cedars for me from Lebanon, and let my servants be with your servants. The wage of your servants I will give to you according to all that you say, for you know that there is no one among us who knows how to cut timber like the Sidonians.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24; Isaiah 9:6; 2 Chronicles 2:11–12"> When Hiram heard the words of Solomon, he rejoiced greatly, and he said, “Blessed be Yahweh this day, who has given to David a wise son over this great people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:34; 2 Chronicles 3:5; 2 Samuel 6:5"> Hiram sent to Solomon, saying, “I have heard what you have sent to me; I will do all of your desire concerning the timber of cedars and concerning the timber of cypresses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; Ezekiel 27:17; Acts 12:20"> My servants will bring them down from Lebanon to the sea, and I will make them into rafts in the sea to float to the place which you indicated to me. Then I shall break them up there, and you may carry them further, and ⸤you shall meet my needs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “you shall do my desire”">c</a></sup>by giving food for my house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So Hiram was giving to Solomon the cedar timbers and the cypress timbers, ⸤everything he needed⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “all of his desire”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:10; 1 Kings 4:22"> Then Solomon gave to Hiram twenty thousand dry measures of wheat as food for his household, and twenty dry measures of ⸤specially prepared olive oil⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “beaten olive oil”">e</a></sup>thus Solomon gave to Hiram year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; Amos 1:9; James 1:5"> Yahweh gave wisdom to Solomon as he promised to him, and there was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and the two of them ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “cut”">f</a></sup>a covenant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 1 Kings 4:6"> Then King Solomon conscripted a forced labor from all Israel, and the forced labor numbered thirty thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 4:6–19; 1 Chronicles 27:1–15"> He sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand ⸤every month⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the month”">g</a></sup>the work groups were a month in Lebanon and two months at home; now Adoniram was over the forced labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 9:20–22; 2 Chronicles 2:17–18; 2 Chronicles 8:7–9"> Solomon had seventy thousand ⸤common laborers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “carrying burden”">h</a></sup>and eighty thousand stone craftsmen in the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 9:23; 2 Chronicles 2:2"> Besides the chiefs of the officers Solomon had, there were three thousand three hundred having charge over the people who were doing the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 6:7; 1 Chronicles 22:2; Isaiah 28:16"> When the king commanded, they quarried great stones and precious stones to lay the foundation of the house with hewn stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:9; Psalm 83:7"> So Solomon’s builders and Hiram’s builders and the Gebalites hewed stones, and they prepared the timber and the stone to build the house.</span>
