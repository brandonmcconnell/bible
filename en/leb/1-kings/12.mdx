## Israel’s Appeal to Rehoboam

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 9:6; 2 Chronicles 10:1–19; Judges 9:1"> Then Rehoboam went to Shechem, for all of Israel had come to Shechem to make him king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 10:2–3; 1 Kings 11:26–31"> It happened that Jeroboam the son of Nebat heard of it while he was still in Egypt where he had fled from the face of King Solomon, and Jeroboam had lived in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So they sent and summoned him, and Jeroboam and all the assembly of Israel came. Then they spoke to Rehoboam, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–18; 1 Kings 4:7; Matthew 11:29–30"> “Your father made our yoke heavy; now lighten the hard labor of your father and the heavy yoke which he placed on us, and we will serve you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 12:12"> He said, “Go up for three days and then return to me”; so the people went away.</span>
</div>

## Rehoboam Seeks Counsel to Respond

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 12:12; Job 32:7; 2 Samuel 16:20"> Then King Rehoboam consulted with the old men who had been ⸤serving⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “standing”">a</a></sup>before Solomon his father when he was alive, saying, “How are you advising me ⸤to answer this people⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to return a word to this people”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; 2 Chronicles 10:6–7; 1 Kings 12:13"> They said to him, “If you will be a servant today to this people, then you will serve them; and if you answer them and speak good words to them, they will always be your servants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20; Ecclesiastes 10:2–3; 2 Chronicles 10:8"> But he rejected the advice of the old men, which they gave him, and he consulted with the youngsters who had grown up with him, who were ⸤serving⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “standing”">c</a></sup>before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 22:6–8; 2 Samuel 17:5–6; 2 Chronicles 10:9"> He said to them, “What are you advising that we should reply to this people who spoke to me by saying, ‘Lighten the yoke your father put on us.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 10:10–11; Proverbs 10:14; Isaiah 47:6"> Then the youngsters who had grown up with him spoke to him, saying, “Thus you shall say to this people who spoke to you: ‘Your father made our yoke heavy, but you lighten it for us,’ you shall say to them, ‘My little finger is thicker than my father’s loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:11; Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 28:13–14"> So then, my father loaded a heavy yoke on all of you, but I will add to your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, but I will discipline you with scorpions!’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rehoboam Responds Unwisely with Disastrous Results

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 12:5; 2 Chronicles 10:12–14"> Jeroboam and all of the people came to Rehoboam on the third day, as the king had spoken: “Return to me on the third day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 3:17; Proverbs 13:20; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> Then the king answered all the people harshly, as he had rejected the advice of the old men that they had offered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 5:16–18; 1 Kings 12:10–11; Exodus 5:5–9"> He spoke to them according to the advice of the youngsters, saying, “My father made your yoke heavy, but I will add onto your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, but I will discipline you with scorpions.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; Deuteronomy 2:30; Judges 14:4"> So the king did not listen to the people, for it was a turning of events from Yahweh in order to fulfill his word which Yahweh had spoken through the hand of Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam the son of Nebat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; 2 Chronicles 10:16"> When all of Israel saw that the king would not listen to them, the people answered the king, saying, “⸤What share do we have in David⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “What is for us a share in David”">d</a></sup>There is no inheritance in the son of Jesse. To your tents, Israel! Now look to your house, David!” Then Israel went to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">e</a></sup>tents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 1 Kings 11:13; 2 Chronicles 11:13–17"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>were living in the cities of Judah, and Rehoboam was reigning over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 4:6; 2 Samuel 20:24; 1 Kings 5:14"> King Rehoboam sent Adoram who was over the forced labor, and all of Israel cast stones at him and he died, but King Rehoboam managed to get up on the chariot to flee to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 17:21; 2 Chronicles 10:19; Joshua 4:9"> So Israel rebelled against the house of David until this day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Civil War Averted

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 11:13; 1 Kings 11:32; Hosea 8:4"> It happened that just when all of Israel heard that Jeroboam had returned, they sent and called him to the assembly and made him king over all of Israel. Not one followed after the house of David except the tribe of Judah alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:1–3; 1 Chronicles 21:5; 2 Chronicles 14:8"> When Rehoboam came to Jerusalem, he assembled all of the house of Judah and the tribe of Benjamin, a hundred and eighty thousand ⸤choice troops⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “chosen makers of war”">g</a></sup>to fight with the house of Israel, to restore the kingship to Rehoboam the son of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:2; 2 Chronicles 12:5–7; 1 Kings 13:1"> Then the word of God came to Shemaiah the man of God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 12:17"> “Say to Rehoboam the son of Solomon the king of Judah and to all the house of Judah and Benjamin and the remainder of the people, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:7–8; Hosea 8:4"> ‘Thus says Yahweh: “You shall not go up and you shall not fight with your brothers the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>Return each of you to his house, for this thing was from me.” ’ ” So they heeded the word of Yahweh, and they returned to go home according to the word of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeroboam Becomes King over Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 8:17; Judges 8:8; Judges 9:45–49"> Then Jeroboam built Shechem in the hill country of Ephraim, and he resided in it. Then he went out from there and built Penuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:18–21; Isaiah 7:9; Mark 2:6–8"> Then Jeroboam ⸤said to himself⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “said to his heart”">i</a></sup>“Now the kingdom will return to the house of David</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:5–7; Proverbs 29:25; Deuteronomy 12:14"> if this people go up to offer sacrifices in the house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “temple”">j</a></sup>of Yahweh in Jerusalem. The heart of this people will return to their master Rehoboam the king of Judah, and they shall kill me and ⸤return to him⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “return to Rehoboam the king of Judah”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Kings 10:29; Exodus 32:8; 2 Kings 17:16"> And the king had decided, so he made two golden calves and he said to them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="That is, the people">l</a></sup>“⸤You have been going up to Jerusalem long enough⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “enough for you from going up to Jerusalem”">m</a></sup>here are your gods, O Israel, who brought you up from the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Judges 18:27–31; Hosea 4:15"> He put one in Bethel, and the other he put in Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:21; 1 Kings 13:34; 2 Kings 10:31"> This thing became a sin, and the people walked before the one as far as Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:9; 2 Kings 17:32; 1 Kings 13:32–33"> Then he built the houses on the high places, and he appointed priests ⸤from all walks of life⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “from the fringes of the people”">n</a></sup>who were not from the sons of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 8:5; 1 Kings 8:2; Amos 7:10–13"> Jeroboam also inaugurated a religious feast in the eighth month on the fifteenth day of the month, like the religious feast which was in Judah, and he offered sacrifices on the altar. Thus he did in Bethel, by sacrificing to the calves that he had made; and he placed in Bethel the priests of the high places which he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 13:1; Numbers 15:39; Matthew 15:6"> He offered sacrifices on the altar which he had made in Bethel on the fifteenth day of the eighth month which his heart had devised. He inaugurated a religious feast for the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>and he went up to the altar to offer incense.</span>
</div>
