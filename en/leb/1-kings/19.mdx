## Elijah Flees to Horeb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 18:40; 1 Kings 21:25; 1 Kings 21:5–7"> Then Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done and how he had killed all the prophets with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; 2 Kings 6:31; 1 Kings 20:10"> Then Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah, saying, “Thus may the gods do to me, and may they add to it, surely at this time tomorrow I will make your life as the life of one of them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Matthew 26:70–74; Matthew 26:56"> Then he became afraid,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="According to Greek, Syriac, and Latin manuscripts. Hebrew reads “he saw”">a</a></sup>got up, and ⸤fled for his life⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “he went to his life”">b</a></sup>He came to Beersheba which belongs to Judah, and he left his servant there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jonah 4:3; Numbers 11:15; Jonah 4:8"> Then he went into the wilderness one day’s journey, and he went and sat under a certain broom tree. Then ⸤he asked Yahweh that he might die⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “he asked his life to die”">c</a></sup>and he said, “It is enough now, Yahweh; take my life, for I am no better than my ancestors.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “fathers”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 34:7; Acts 12:7; Hebrews 13:5"> He lay down and fell asleep under a certain broom tree, and suddenly this angel was touching him and said to him, “Get up, eat!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Matthew 4:11; Matthew 6:32"> He looked, and behold, a bread cake on hot coals was near his head and a jar of water, so he ate and drank. Then he did it again and lay down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 103:13–14; Deuteronomy 33:25"> The angel of Yahweh appeared a second time and touched him and said, “Get up, eat, for the journey is greater than you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Exodus 3:1"> So he got up, ate, drank, and went in the strength of that food forty days and forty nights up to Horeb, the mountain of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elijah Encounters Yahweh at Horeb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 19:13; Exodus 33:21–22; Jeremiah 2:18"> He came to the cave there and spent the night there. Suddenly the word of Yahweh came to him and asked him, “⸤Elijah, what are you doing here⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “What is for you here, Elijah”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; 1 Kings 18:22; Exodus 34:14"> Then he said, “I have been very zealous for Yahweh the God of hosts, for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>have forsaken your covenant. They have demolished your altars, and they have killed your prophets with the sword. I alone am left over, and they seek to take my life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:4; Psalm 50:3; Job 38:1"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, Yahweh">g</a></sup>said, “Go out and stand on the mountain before Yahweh.” Suddenly Yahweh was passing by, with a great and strong wind ripping the mountains and crushing rocks before Yahweh; but Yahweh was not in the wind. After the wind, there was an earthquake; but Yahweh was not in the earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 4:16; Zechariah 4:6; Deuteronomy 4:33"> After the earthquake was a fire, but Yahweh was not in the fire. After the fire there was the sound of a gentle whisper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9; Isaiah 6:2; Genesis 16:8"> It happened at the moment Elijah heard, he covered his face with his cloak and went out and stood at the entrance of the cave. Suddenly a voice came to him and said, “⸤Elijah, why are you here⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “What is for you here, Elijah”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 19:9–10; Jeremiah 22:9; Isaiah 62:1"> He said, “I have been very zealous for Yahweh the God of Hosts, for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>have forsaken your covenant, demolished your altars, and killed your prophets with the sword; I alone am left, and they seek to take my life!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; Jeremiah 1:10; 2 Kings 8:7–15"> Then Yahweh said to him, “Go, return on your way to the wilderness of Damascus. Go and anoint Hazael as king over Aram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1–14; 1 Kings 19:19–21; 2 Kings 2:9"> and Jehu son of Nimshi you shall anoint as king over Israel. You shall also anoint Elisha son of Shaphat from Abel-Meholah as prophet in your place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 13:22; 2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 13:3"> It shall be that the one who escapes from the sword of Hazael, Jehu will kill; the one who escapes from the sword of Jehu, Elisha shall kill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 11:4–5; Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 10:20–22"> I will leave in Israel seven thousand, all of the knees that have not bowed down to Baal and all of the mouths that have not kissed him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 2:8; 2 Kings 2:13–14; 1 Samuel 28:14"> So he went from there and found Elisha son of Shaphat while he was plowing with twelve pairs of oxen before him. When he and the twelve passed Elijah, he threw his cloak on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 8:21–22; Luke 9:61–62; Acts 20:37"> Then he left the oxen and ran after Elijah and said, “Please let me kiss my father and my mother, and then I will go after you.” Then he said, “Go, return, for what I have done to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:22; 2 Timothy 4:11; Philemon 1:13"> So he returned from after him, and he took a pair of oxen and slaughtered them, and with the yoke of the oxen he boiled the flesh and gave it to the people and they ate. Then he arose and went after Elijah and served him.</span>
</div>
</div>
