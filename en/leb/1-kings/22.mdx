## Jehoshaphat of Judah Allies with Ahab of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 20:34"> They lived three years, and there was no war between Aram and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 15:24; 1 Kings 22:1; 1 Kings 22:41"> It happened in the third year, Jehoshaphat king of Judah went down to the king of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:43; 1 Kings 4:13; Joshua 21:38"> and the king of Israel said to his servants, “Do you know Ramoth-Gilead belongs to us, and we are doing nothing about taking it from the hand of the king of Aram?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 3:7; Ephesians 5:11; 2 John 1:11"> Then he said to Jehoshaphat, “Will you go with me to the battle for Ramoth-Gilead?” Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, “⸤I am like you⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “Like I am, like you are”">a</a></sup>⸤my people are like your people⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “like my people, like your people”">b</a></sup>⸤my horses are like your horses⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “like my horses, like your horses”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; 2 Chronicles 18:4–5; Jeremiah 42:2–6"> Jehoshaphat also said to the king of Israel, “Please inquire for the word of Yahweh today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 18:19; Jeremiah 23:14–17; Ezekiel 13:7–16"> Then the king of Israel assembled the prophets, about four hundred men, and he said to them, “Shall I go against Ramoth-Gilead for the battle, or should I refrain?” Then they said, “Go up, for the Lord will give it into the hand of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 3:11–13; 2 Chronicles 18:6–7"> So Jehoshaphat said, “Is there no prophet of Yahweh here that we might still inquire from him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 5:10; John 3:19–21; John 7:7"> Then the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “There is still one man to inquire from Yahweh, but I despise him, for he never prophesies anything good concerning me, but only bad: Micaiah the son of Imlah.” Then Jehoshaphat said, “The king should not say so.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 9:32; Isaiah 39:7; 1 Kings 22:26–27"> The king of Israel summoned a certain court official, and he said, “Quickly fetch Micaiah son of Imlah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 5:1; Esther 6:8–9; 1 Kings 18:29"> The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah were each sitting on his throne, dressed in their robes, at the threshing floor at the entrance of the gate of Samaria, with all the prophets prophesying before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:18–21; Deuteronomy 33:17; Ezekiel 22:27–28"> Zedekiah son of Kenaanah made horns of iron for himself and said, “Thus says Yahweh: ‘With these you shall gore the Arameans until finishing them.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:22; 1 Kings 22:6–15; 1 Kings 22:32–36"> All of the prophets were likewise prophesying, saying, “Go up to Ramoth-Gilead and triumph, and Yahweh will give it into the hand of the king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 2:6–7; Psalm 11:1; Isaiah 30:10–11"> Then the messenger who had gone to summon Micaiah said to him, “Please now, the words of the prophet are ⸤unanimously⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “one mouth”">d</a></sup>favorable to the king. Please let your words be as one word with them, and speak favorably.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; Numbers 22:18; 2 Chronicles 18:12–13"> Then Micaiah said, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">e</a></sup>surely only as Yahweh speaks to me, that will I speak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:27; Judges 10:14; Matthew 26:45"> When he came to the king, the king asked him, “Micaiah, shall we go to Ramoth-Gilead to the battle, or shall we refrain?” He said to him, “Go up and triumph, and Yahweh will give it into the hand of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 19:13; Matthew 26:63; Joshua 6:26"> Then the king said to him, “How many times must I make you swear that you shall not tell me anything but truth in the name of Yahweh?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 27:17; Matthew 9:36; 1 Kings 22:34–36"> So he said, “I saw all of Israel scattering to the mountains, like the sheep without a shepherd. Yahweh also said, ‘There are no masters for these, let them return in peace, each to his house.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:8; Proverbs 10:24; Luke 11:45"> Then the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “Did I not say to you that he would not prophesy good concerning me, but disaster?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:1; Job 1:6; Daniel 7:9–10"> And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, Micaiah">f</a></sup>said, “Therefore, hear the word of Yahweh. I saw Yahweh sitting on his throne with all the hosts of heaven standing beside him from his right hand and from his left hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; Job 12:16; Jeremiah 4:10"> And Yahweh said, ‘Who will entice Ahab so that he will go up and fall at Ramoth-Gilead?’ Then ⸤this one was saying one thing and the other one was saying another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “this one said in thus and this one was saying in thus”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 2:1; 1 Kings 22:23; Job 1:6–7"> Then a spirit came out and stood before Yahweh and said, ‘I will entice him,’ and Yahweh said to him, ‘How?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 9:23; 1 Samuel 19:9; 1 Samuel 16:14"> He said, ‘I will go out and I will be a false spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.’ And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, Yahweh">h</a></sup>said, ‘You shall entice and succeed, go out and do so.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:9; Matthew 24:24–25; Isaiah 44:20"> So then, see that Yahweh has placed a false spirit in the mouth of all of these your prophets, and Yahweh has spoken disaster concerning you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 23:2; Lamentations 3:30; Micah 5:1"> Then Zedekiah son of Kenaanah came near and slapped Micaiah on the cheek and said, “When did the Spirit of Yahweh pass from me to speak with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 20:30; 2 Peter 2:1; Revelation 19:20"> Then Micaiah said, “Behold, you are about to see on that day when you go ⸤from room to room⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “private room in private room”">i</a></sup>to hide.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The king of Israel said, “Take Micaiah and return him to Amon the commander of the city and to Jehoash the son of the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:10; 2 Chronicles 18:25–27; Revelation 2:10"> and say, ‘Thus says the king: “Put this fellow in the house of imprisonment and feed him reduced rations of food and water until I come in peace.” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 16:29; Micah 1:2; Acts 13:10–11"> Then Micaiah said, “If you indeed return in peace, then Yahweh has not spoken with me.” Then he said, “Let all the peoples hear!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 18:28; 1 Kings 22:2–9"> Then the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went up to Ramoth-Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:22; Jeremiah 23:24; 1 Samuel 28:8"> Then the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, “I will disguise myself and go into the battle, but you wear your robes.” So the king of Israel disguised himself, and he went into the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 20:24; 2 Chronicles 18:30; Jeremiah 16:6"> The king of Aram commanded his thirty-two chariot commanders, saying, “You shall not fight with small or great, but only against the king of Israel, him alone!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 13:20; Jonah 2:1–2; Psalm 130:1–4"> When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat, they said, “Surely he is the king of Israel,” and they turned to fight against him, so Jehoshaphat called out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 76:10; 1 Kings 22:31"> When the chariot commanders saw that it was not the king of Israel, they turned away from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:23; Micah 6:13; 1 Samuel 17:49"> But another man drew his bow fully and struck the king of Israel between the armor scales and the breastplate; so he said to his chariot driver, “Turn ⸤the chariot⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “your hand”">j</a></sup>and bring me out from the camp, for I am wounded.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 22:28; 1 Kings 20:42"> The battle intensified on that day, and the king was being propped up in the chariot opposite Aram, but he died in the evening, and the blood of the wound ran out to the floor of the chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 14:12; 1 Kings 22:31; Judges 21:24"> Then the shout passed through the camp about sunset, saying, “Each man to his city and each to his land!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So the king died, and ⸤they brought him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “and he went”">k</a></sup>to Samaria, and they buried the king in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Matthew 24:35; Jeremiah 44:21–23"> They washed the chariot by the pool of Samaria, and the dogs licked his blood (now, the prostitutes washed themselves there) according to the word of Yahweh which he had spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Amos 3:15; Ezekiel 27:6; Psalm 45:8"> The remainder of the acts of Ahab and all that he did, and the ivory palace and all the cities that he built, are they not written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:12; 2 Kings 1:17; 2 Kings 1:2"> So Ahab slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>and Ahaziah his son became king in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Jehoshaphat of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:31; 1 Kings 22:2; 2 Chronicles 17:1"> Jehoshaphat the son of Asa became king over Judah in the fourth year of Ahab king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 1 Kings 15:2; 1 Kings 15:10"> Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-five years in Jerusalem. The name of his mother was Azubah daughter of Shilhi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Kings 15:14; 2 Kings 12:3; Proverbs 4:27"> He walked in all of the way of Asa his father, and he did not turn aside from it, doing right in the eyes of Yahweh.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="1 Kings 22:44 begins here in the Hebrew Bible">m</a></sup>Only he did not remove the high places; the people were still sacrificing and burning incense on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Kings 22:2; 2 Chronicles 19:2; 2 Kings 8:18"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="1 Kings 22:44– 53 in the English Bible is 22:45–54 in the Hebrew Bible">n</a></sup>But, Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 11:41; 2 Chronicles 20:34"> The remainder of the acts of Jehoshaphat, his powerful deeds he did, and how he fought, are they not written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 Kings 15:12; 1 Kings 14:24; Deuteronomy 23:17"> The remainder of the male shrine prostitutes who were left over in the days of Asa his father he exterminated from the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Kings 3:9; 2 Kings 8:20"> There was not a king in Edom; a governor served as king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Kings 9:26; 1 Kings 10:22; 1 Kings 9:28"> Jehoshaphat built<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="According to the reading tradition(Qere ); what is written (Kethib ) is “ten”">o</a></sup>ships of the Tarshish type to go to Ophir for the gold; but he did not go because the ships were destroyed at Ezion-Geber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Then Ahaziah son of Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, “Let my servants go with your servants in the ships,” but Jehoshaphat was not willing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:1; 1 Kings 22:40; 1 Kings 15:24"> And Jehoshaphat slept with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “fathers”">p</a></sup>and was buried with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “fathers”">q</a></sup>in the city of David his ancestor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “father”">r</a></sup>and Joram his son became king in his place.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Ahaziah of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 1 Kings 22:40; 1 Kings 15:25"> Ahaziah son of Ahab became king over Israel in Samaria in the seventeenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned over Israel two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 Kings 15:26; 1 Kings 21:25; Revelation 3:20"> He did evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and he went in the way of his father and his mother and in the way of Jeroboam son of Nebat who caused Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 Kings 16:30–32; Isaiah 65:3; 2 Kings 3:2"> He served Baal and bowed down to him; and he provoked Yahweh the God of Israel according to all that his father did.</span>
