## Solomon’s Wisdom: Political Administration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:12; 2 Chronicles 9:30; 1 Kings 12:19–20"> King Solomon was king over all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Corinthians 12:28; 2 Samuel 8:15–18"> Now these are the officials who were his: Azariah the son of Zadok was the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; Isaiah 62:6; 1 Chronicles 18:15"> Elihoreph and Ahijah, the sons of Shisha, were the secretaries; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was the recorder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the army, and Zadok and Abiathar were priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:33; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 16:16"> Azariah the son of Nathan was over the governors, and Zabud the son of Nathan was a priest, an advisor to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 2 Samuel 20:24; 1 Kings 12:18"> Ahishar was over the palace, and Adoniram the son of Abda was over the forced labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:1–15"> Solomon had twelve governors over all Israel, and they sustained the king and his palace, ⸤each one was to sustain for each month of the year⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a month in the year he was over one to sustain”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Judges 19:1; Judges 17:1"> These are their names: Ben-Hur was in the hill country of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 21:16; 1 Samuel 6:20; 1 Samuel 6:12"> Ben-Deker was in Makaz and in Shaalbim and in Beth-Shemesh and Elon of Beth-Hanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; Joshua 12:17; Joshua 17:2"> Ben-Hesed was in the Arubbot; Socoh and all the land of Hepher were his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 11:2; Joshua 12:23; Joshua 17:11"> Ben-Abinadab was in all of Naphat of Dor; Taphath the daughter of Solomon was his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19; Joshua 3:16"> Baanah the son of Ahilud was in Taanach and Megiddo and all Beth-Shean which is beside Zarethan below Jezreel, of Beth-Shean up to Abel-Meholah up to the other side of Jokmeam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:4; Numbers 32:41; 1 Kings 22:3"> Ben-Geber was in Ramoth-Gilead; the villages of Jair, the son of Manasseh which are in the Gilead were his, and the region of Argob which is in the Bashan, sixty great cities, with walls having crossbars of bronze, were his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; 2 Samuel 17:24; 2 Samuel 2:8"> Ahinadab the son of Iddo was in Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–39; 2 Samuel 15:27; 1 Samuel 18:18"> Ahimaaz was in Naphtali; he moreover also had taken Basemath the daughter of Solomon as wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–31; 2 Samuel 15:32"> Baanah the son of Hushai was in Asher and Bealoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 19:17–23"> Jehoshaphat the son of Paruah was in Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 1:8; Joshua 18:20–28; Zechariah 12:13"> Shimei the son of Ela was in Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–17; Joshua 13:9–12; Numbers 21:21–35"> Geber the son of Uri was in the land of Gilead, the land of Sihon, the king of the Amorites, and of Og, the king of Bashan; one governor which was over the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; 1 Kings 3:8; Genesis 32:12"> Judah and Israel were as many as the sand which is on the seashore in abundance, eating and drinking and rejoicing!</span>

## Solomon’s Wisdom: Prosperity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Joshua 1:4; Psalm 68:29"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1 Kings 4:21–5:18 in the English Bible is 5:1–32 in the Hebrew Bible">b</a></sup>Now Solomon was ruling over all the kingdoms from the River<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, the Euphrates">c</a></sup>to the land of the Philistines, and up to the border of Egypt, who were bringing tribute and were serving Solomon all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The food of Solomon for one day was thirty dry measures of choice meal and sixty dry measures of flour;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:17–18"> ten stall-fed oxen and twenty pasture-fed oxen and a hundred sheep, besides deer and buck gazelles and roebucks and well-fed fowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; Psalm 72:11; Psalm 72:3"> For he was ruling over all the west of the River<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the Euphrates">d</a></sup>from Tiphsah up to Gaza, over all the kings west of the River;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, the Euphrates">e</a></sup>and he had peace from every side all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Micah 4:4; Zechariah 3:10; Judges 20:1"> Judah and Israel lived in security, each man under his vine and under his fig tree, from Dan as far as Beersheba, all the days of Solomon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:14; 2 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Samuel 8:4"> Now Solomon had forty thousand stalls of horses for his war chariots and twelve thousand horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> These governors sustained King Solomon and all who came near to the table of King Solomon, each in his month; they did not omit anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esther 8:10; Esther 8:14; Micah 1:13"> The barley and the straw for the horses and for packhorses they brought to the place where they were, each according to his share.</span>
</div>

## Solomon’s Wisdom: Literature, Zoology, Biology, Dendrology

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 3:17; 1 Kings 4:20"> God gave wisdom to Solomon and very great discernment, as well as ⸤breadth of understanding⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “width of heart”">f</a></sup>as the sand which is on the edge of the seashore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12; Genesis 25:6; Daniel 1:20"> The wisdom of Solomon was greater than the wisdom of all the people<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “sons/children”">g</a></sup>of the east and more than all the wisdom of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:6; 1 Chronicles 15:19; 1 Kings 3:12"> He was wiser than all the men: Ethan the Ezrahite; Heman, Calcol, and Darda the children of Mahol; and ⸤he was very well known⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “his name was among all the peoples around”">h</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Psalm 51:7; Psalm 92:12"> He spoke concerning the trees, from the cedar which is in Lebanon up to the hyssop which grows on the wall; he also spoke concerning the animals, concerning the birds, concerning the creeping things, and concerning the fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:23; 1 Kings 10:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> They came from all the nations to hear the wisdom of Solomon; from all the kings of the earth who had heard of his wisdom.</span>
