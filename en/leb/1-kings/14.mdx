## Judgment for Jeroboam Continues

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; 1 Kings 13:33–34; 1 Samuel 31:2"> At that time, Abijah son of Jeroboam became ill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:8; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Samuel 14:2"> Jeroboam said to his wife, “Please get up and disguise yourself so that they will not know that you are the wife of Jeroboam, and go to Shiloh. Look, Ahijah the prophet is there, and he spoke concerning me before I became king over this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; 1 Kings 13:7; 2 Kings 4:42"> You must take ten loaves of bread in your hand and cakes and a jar of honey, and you must go to him. He shall tell you what will happen to the boy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 11:29; 1 Samuel 3:2; 1 Samuel 4:15"> The wife of Jeroboam did so. She got up, went to Shiloh, and came to the house of Ahijah. Now Ahijah was not able to see, for ⸤his eyes were fixed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “his eyes stood”">a</a></sup>because of his old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 6:8–12; Proverbs 21:30; 2 Kings 4:27"> Then Yahweh said to Ahijah, “Look, the wife of Jeroboam is coming to seek a word from you about her son, for he is ill. ⸤Thus and so⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “As this and as this”">b</a></sup>you shall say to her. When she comes, she will be disguising herself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 5:17–28; Acts 5:9–10; Luke 20:20–23"> It happened at the moment Ahijah heard the sound of her footsteps coming through the doorway, he said, “Come in, wife of Jeroboam. Why are you making yourself unrecognizable? I have been sent a hard message for you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 16:2; 2 Samuel 12:7–8; 1 Samuel 15:16"> Go, tell Jeroboam, ‘Thus says Yahweh the God of Israel: “Because I raised you from the midst of the people and I made you leader over my people Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Kings 11:33–38; 2 Chronicles 28:1"> I tore the kingdom from the house of David and I gave it to you. But you were not like my servant David who kept my commandments, and who went after me with all his heart, to do only what is right in my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:35; Nehemiah 9:26; Psalm 50:17"> But you did more evil than all who were before you. You have gone and made for yourself other gods and molten idols to provoke me, but me you have ⸤completely disregarded⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “hurled behind your back”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Kings 21:21; 2 Kings 14:26"> Therefore, look, I am about to bring disaster upon the house of Jeroboam, and I will cut off ⸤males⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “those who urinate against a wall”">d</a></sup>from Jeroboam, bond or free, in Israel. I will burn after the house of Jeroboam as one burns after the manure, until it is finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 16:4; Jeremiah 15:3; 1 Kings 21:23–24"> He who dies for Jeroboam in the city, the dogs will eat. He who dies in the open field, the birds from the heavens will eat, for Yahweh has spoken it.” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 14:3; 1 Kings 14:16–17; 2 Kings 1:6"> As for you, get up. Go to your house. When your feet enter the city, the child will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:12; Job 19:28"> All of Israel will mourn for him, and they will bury him. This one alone will come to a tomb for Jeroboam, because a good thing has been found in him by Yahweh the God of Israel in the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 15:27–29; Ezekiel 7:2–7; Ezekiel 12:22–28"> Yahweh will raise up a king for himself over Israel who will even now cut off the house of Jeroboam this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:15–16; Psalm 52:5; 2 Kings 15:29"> Yahweh will strike Israel as one shakes the reed plant in the water, and he will root Israel up from this good land that he gave to their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>He will scatter them beyond the River<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, the Euphrates">f</a></sup>because they have made their sacred poles which are provoking Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:34; 1 Kings 16:2; 1 Kings 13:34"> He will give up Israel because of the sin of Jeroboam which he sinned and which he caused Israel to sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 16:15; 1 Kings 16:23"> Then the wife of Jeroboam got up, went, and came to Tirzah. As she was coming to the threshold of the house, the boy died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 14:13"> They buried him and all of Israel mourned for him, according to the word of Yahweh which he had spoken by the hand of his servant Ahijah the prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2–20; 1 Chronicles 27:24; 1 Kings 16:20"> The remainder of the acts of Jeroboam, how he fought and how he reigned, behold, they are written in the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:12; Psalm 3:5; 1 Kings 11:43"> The days which Jeroboam reigned were twenty-two years, and he slept with his ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup>Then Nadab his son became king in place of him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rehoboam Reigns in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:13; 1 Kings 11:36; 1 Kings 14:31"> Now Rehoboam the son of Solomon reigned in Judah. He was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city which, from all of the tribes of Israel, Yahweh chose to place his name. And the name of his mother was Naamah the Ammonitess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Psalm 78:58"> But Judah did evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and they annoyed him more than their fathers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “ancestors”">h</a></sup>did with their sins that they had committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Isaiah 57:5; 2 Kings 17:9–10"> They also built for themselves high places and stone pillars and sacred poles on every high hill and under every green tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17; 1 Corinthians 6:9; 1 Kings 15:12"> There were also male shrine prostitutes in the land, and they did according to all the abominations of the nations which Yahweh had driven out from before the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Pharaoh Shishak of Egypt Raids Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 12:2–4; 2 Chronicles 12:9"> It happened in the fifth year of King Rehoboam that Shishak the king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 15:18; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 7:51"> and he took the treasures of the house of Yahweh, and he took all the treasures of the king’s house. He took the small gold shields that Solomon had made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11; 1 Samuel 22:17; 1 Kings 18:46"> so King Rehoboam made small copper shields in place of them and ⸤entrusted them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “he assigned on the hand”">j</a></sup>to the commanders of the royal guard who keep the doorway of the king’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:11"> Whenever the king came to the house of Yahweh, the royal guard carried them and brought them back to the alcove room of the royal guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 22:45; 1 Kings 15:23; 1 Kings 14:19"> The remainder of the acts of Rehoboam and all that he did, are they not written on the scroll of the events of the days of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; 1 Kings 12:21; 1 Kings 15:6–7"> There was always war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:16; Matthew 1:7; 1 Kings 15:3"> Then Rehoboam slept with his ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “fathers”">k</a></sup>and he was buried with his ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>in the city of David. The name of his mother was Naamah the Ammonitess. Abijam his son became king in his place.</span>
</div>
