## Solomon Builds the Temple for Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:1–2; Acts 7:47; 1 Chronicles 29:19"> It happened in the four hundred and eightieth year after the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>went out from the land of Egypt, in the fourth year ⸤of Solomon’s rule⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “Solomon to rule”">b</a></sup>over Israel, the month of Ziv (that is the second month), that he began to build the house for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:1–41; Ezekiel 41:1–15; Revelation 21:16–17"> Now the house that King Solomon built for Yahweh was sixty cubits in its length and twenty cubits in its width and thirty cubits in its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11; 2 Chronicles 3:3–4; Ezekiel 41:15"> The vestibule on the face of the main hall of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “house”">c</a></sup>was twenty cubits in its length, and the width of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “house”">d</a></sup>was ten cubits wide on the face of the temple.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “house”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:16; Ezekiel 41:26; Ezekiel 41:16"> And he made for the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “house”">f</a></sup>specially designed framed windows,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:16; 1 Kings 6:19–21; 1 Kings 6:31"> and he built a structure against the wall of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “house”">g</a></sup>running all along the walls of the house, for the outer sanctuary and for the inner sanctuary, and made side rooms all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The lower structure was five cubits in its width and the middle was six cubits in its width and the third was seven cubits in its width, for he made niches for the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “house”">h</a></sup>all around to the outside, so that beams would not attach to the walls of the temple.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “house”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:5–6; 1 Peter 2:5; Exodus 20:25"> Now while the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “house”">j</a></sup>was being built, it was built with stones finished at the quarry, so that no hammer or stone shaping tool or any instrument of iron was heard in the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “house”">k</a></sup>as it was being built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:6–7"> The doorway of the side room in the middle of the side of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “house”">l</a></sup>was on the south; they went up with a stairway to the middle and from the middle to the third floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 6:14; 1 Kings 6:38"> So he built the house and finished it. He covered the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “house”">m</a></sup>with rafters and wood planks and with the cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He also built the structure against all of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “house”">n</a></sup>five cubits in height and fastened it to the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “house”">o</a></sup>with beams of cedar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the word of Yahweh came to Solomon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:10; Colossians 1:23; 1 Kings 3:14"> “Regarding this temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “house”">p</a></sup>that you are building: if you walk in my ordinances and if you do my judgments and you keep all my commandments to walk in them, then I will establish my promise with you which I made to David your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:6; Exodus 25:8; Leviticus 26:11"> And I will dwell ⸤among⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “in the middle of”">q</a></sup>the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">r</a></sup>and I will not forsake my people Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 6:9; 1 Kings 6:38; Acts 7:47–48"> So Solomon built the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “house”">s</a></sup>and finished it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:7"> He lined the walls ⸤of the inside of the house⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “the house from house”">t</a></sup>with boards of cedar; from the floor of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “house”">u</a></sup>up to the rafters of the ceiling he covered them with wood ⸤on the inside⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “from house”">v</a></sup>He also covered the floor of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “house”">w</a></sup>with cypress boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Kings 8:6; Hebrews 9:3"> He built twenty cubits from the rear of the house with boards of cedar from the floor up to the ceiling, and he built for it an inner sanctuary on the inside, as the ⸤most holy place⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “as the holy place of the holy places.” Often referred to as the Holy of Holies">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The main hall of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “house”">y</a></sup>was forty cubits ⸤in front of the inner sanctuary⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="So LXX supported by the Vulgate. MT has “before me”">z</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:24"> with the cedar within the inner house having carvings of gourds and buds of flowers. It was entirely of cedar; there was not a stone visible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 8:6–10; Psalm 28:2; 1 Kings 6:5"> Now in the inner sanctuary in the middle of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “house”">aa</a></sup>he prepared the inside to place the ark of the covenant of Yahweh there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Kings 6:22; 1 Kings 7:48"> In front, the inner sanctuary was twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide and twenty cubits high, and he overlaid it with pure gold and covered the altar with cedar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 3:7–9; 2 Chronicles 3:14–16; 1 Kings 6:5"> Solomon overlaid the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “house”">ab</a></sup>on the inside with pure gold, and he drew across it with golden chains in front of the inner sanctuary, which he overlaid with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:7–14"> All of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “house”">ac</a></sup>he overlaid with gold until all of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “house”">ad</a></sup>was finished; all of the altar which belonged to the inner sanctuary he overlaid with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 37:7–9; Genesis 3:24; 1 Peter 1:12"> He made two cherubim of olive wood for the inner sanctuary, ten cubits high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Five cubits was the first wing of the cherub, and five cubits the second wing of the cherub, from the tip of his one wing up to the tip of his other wing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The second cherub was ten cubits according to ⸤the same⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “one”">ae</a></sup>measurement, and there was one shape for the two cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The height of the first cherub was ten cubits and so was the second cherub.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 37:9; Exodus 25:20; 2 Chronicles 5:8"> He placed the cherubim in the middle of the inner house, and they spread out the wings of the cherubim; the wing of the first cherub touched against the wall and the wing of the second cherub was touching against the second wall; their wings spread to the middle of the house and were touching wing to wing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He also overlaid the cherubim with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 6:32; Psalm 92:12–15; 2 Chronicles 4:2–5"> On all of the walls around the house, he carved engravings of cherubim and palm tree images and budding flowers both inside and out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 21:18–21; Isaiah 60:17; Isaiah 54:11–12"> He overlaid the floor of the house with gold both inside and out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–20; Ephesians 3:18; John 14:6"> He made doors of olive wood for the doorway of the inner sanctuary, as well as for the doorpost of the fifth doorframe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 6:18; 1 Kings 6:29"> On the two doors of olive wood he made carvings of cherubim and palm tree images and budding flowers, and he overlaid them with gold ⸤by beating⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “and he beat out”">af</a></sup>out the gold on the cherubim and the palm tree images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Thus he made doorframes of olive wood on four sides for the doorway of the main hall</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 41:23–25; 1 Kings 5:8"> and two doors of cypress wood; one door with two folding panels and the second door with two folding panels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> He carved cherubim and palm tree images and budding flowers and overlaid them with gold evenly applied on the carved work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Chronicles 7:7; Exodus 27:9–19"> Then he built the inner courtyard with three rows of dressed stone and a row of cedar beams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:2"> In the fourth year,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="That is, the fourth year of Solomon’s reign">ag</a></sup>the house of Yahweh was founded in the month of Ziv.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; 1 Kings 7:1; Zechariah 4:9"> In the eleventh year in the month of Bul, that is, the eighth month, the house was finished according to all his specifications and according to all his plans. He had built it in seven years.</span>
</div>
