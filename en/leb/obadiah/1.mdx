## God’s Judgment on Edom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:11–12; Joel 3:19; Ezekiel 35:3–15"> The vision of Obadiah. Thus says my Lord Yahweh concerning Edom: Edom’s Approaching Destruction<br />We have heard a report from Yahweh, and a messenger has been sent among the nations: “Rise up and let us rise against it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Edom">a</a></sup>for battle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:18; Isaiah 23:9; Micah 7:10"> “Look, I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The NET Bible note for this verse points out: “The Hebrew perfect verb form used here usually describes past events. However, here and several times in the following verses it is best understood as portraying certain fulfillment of events that at the time of writing were still future. It is the perfect of certitude”">b</a></sup>make you insignificant among the nations. You will be utterly despised!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Proverbs 29:23; Jeremiah 49:16"> The pride of your heart has deceived you, you who live in the clefts of a rock, the heights of its dwelling, you who say in your heart: ‘Who can bring me down to the ground?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 9:2; Job 20:6–7; Habakkuk 2:9"> Even if you soar like the eagle, even if your nest is set among the stars, from there I will bring you down!” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">c</a></sup>Yahweh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:9; Deuteronomy 24:21; Isaiah 17:6"> “If thieves came to you, if plunderers of the night—How you have been destroyed!—would they not steal ⸤what they wanted⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “their sufficiency”">d</a></sup>If grape gatherers came, would they not leave gleanings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:10; Psalm 139:1; Isaiah 45:3"> How Esau has been pillaged; his treasures have been ransacked!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 41:9; Jeremiah 49:7; Jeremiah 30:14"> All of your ⸤allies⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “men of your covenant”">e</a></sup>have driven you up to the boundary; your ⸤confederates⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “men of your peace”">f</a></sup>have deceived you and have prevailed against you. Those who eat your bread have set an ambush for you, there is no<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The preposition בּ plus אֵין indicates the absence of a thing within a location">g</a></sup>understanding of it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “in him”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Job 5:12–14; Psalm 33:10"> On that day,” ⸤declares⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a declaration of”">i</a></sup>Yahweh, “will I not destroy the wise men from Edom, and understanding from the mountain of Esau?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Amos 1:12; Isaiah 63:1–3"> And your warriors will be shattered, O Teman, so that ⸤everyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “each”">j</a></sup>from the mountain of Esau will be cut off because of the slaughter!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Edom’s Treachery against Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Malachi 1:3–4; Genesis 27:41"> “Because of the violence done to your brother Jacob, shame will cover you and you will be cut off forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 137:7; Nahum 3:10; Joel 3:3"> On ⸤the day you stood nearby⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the day of your standing nearby”">k</a></sup>⸤on the day strangers took⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the day of the taking captive by strangers”">l</a></sup>his wealth, and foreigners entered his gates and cast lots over Jerusalem, you were also like one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Micah 4:11; Proverbs 17:5"> But you should not have gloated<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="The Hebrew expression “to look upon” often has the sense of “to feast the eyes upon” or “to gloat over”">m</a></sup>over your brother’s day, on the day of his misfortune, and you should not have rejoiced over the ⸤people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “son”">n</a></sup>of Judah on the day of their perishing, and you should not have opened your mouth wide on the day of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 35:10"> You should not have entered the gate of my people on the day of their disaster. You also should not have gloated over his misery on the day of his disaster, and you should not have stretched out your hands on the day of his disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:6; Psalm 31:8; Obadiah 1:12"> And you should not have stood at the crossroads to cut off his fugitives and you should not have handed over his survivors on the day of distress.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Future Judgment of the Nations

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 2:13; Habakkuk 2:8; Ezekiel 30:3"> “For the day of Yahweh is near against all the nations! Just as you have done, it will be done to you. Your deeds will return on your own head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Jeremiah 49:12; Jeremiah 25:15–16"> For just as you have drunk on ⸤my holy mountain⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the mountain of my holiness”">o</a></sup>all the nations will drink continually. They will drink and they will slurp, and they will be as if they had never been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> But on Mount Zion there will be an escape, and it will be holy, and the house of Jacob will take possession of their dispossessors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Isaiah 10:17; Obadiah 1:16"> And the house of Jacob will be a fire and the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau stubble; and they will set them on fire and will consume them. And there will not be a survivor for the house of Esau,” for Yahweh has spoken.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Future Blessing for Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:44; Zephaniah 2:4–7; Amos 9:12"> Those of the Negev will take possession of the mountain of Esau, and those of the Shephelah will possess the land of the Philistines, and they shall take possession of the territory of Ephraim and the territory of Samaria, and Benjamin will take possession of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–10; Luke 4:26; Jeremiah 32:44"> And the exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">p</a></sup>of this army of the people of Israel will possess Canaan up to Zarephath, and the exiles<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “exile”">q</a></sup>of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad will take possession of the cities of the Negev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; James 5:20; Revelation 11:15"> And those who have been saved will go up on Mount Zion to rule the mountain of Esau. And the kingdom ⸤will belong to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “will be for”">r</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
</div>
