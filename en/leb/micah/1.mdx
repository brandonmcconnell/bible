<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:18; Isaiah 1:1; Hosea 1:1"> The word of Yahweh that came to Micah the Morashtite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, the kings of Judah, that he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Psalm 11:4; Revelation 3:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear, all you peoples;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">give heed, O earth and its fullness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And let the Lord Yahweh be against you as a witness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the Lord from ⸤his holy temple⸥.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the temple of his holiness”">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:11–15; Isaiah 8:7–8; 2 Chronicles 32:1–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">For her wounds are incurable,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because it has come to Judah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It has reached to the gate of my people, to Jerusalem.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; 2 Kings 16:8; 2 Samuel 8:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore you will give parting gifts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to Moresheth-Gath;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the houses of Achzib will be a deception</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the kings of Israel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will again bring the conqueror upon you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">O inhabitants of Mareshah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the glory of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will come to Adullam.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Job 1:20; Jeremiah 7:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make yourselves bald</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cut off your hair</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the children of your pleasure.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Expand your baldness as the eagle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for they will go into exile away from you.</span></span>
