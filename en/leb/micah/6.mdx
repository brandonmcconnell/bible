<div className="bp poetry">

## What Does Yahweh Require?

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 51:16–17; Romans 10:2–3; Psalm 40:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">With what shall I approach Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and bow down to God on high?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shall I approach him with burnt offerings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with bull calves a year old?</span></span>

## The Destruction of the Wicked

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:51; 1 Kings 16:25–33; Jeremiah 7:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you have observed the regulations of Omri</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the works of the house of Ahab.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And you have walked in their counsels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that I am making you a desolation</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your inhabitants an object of scorn.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So you will bear the scorn of my people.</span></span>
