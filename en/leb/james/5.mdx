## Woes on the Rich Who Oppress Others

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 11:28; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 11:4"> Come now, you rich people, weep and cry aloud over the miseries that are coming upon you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Job 13:28; Luke 12:33"> Your wealth has rotted, and your clothing has become moth-eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; James 5:7–8; Revelation 21:8"> Your gold and silver have become corroded, and their corrosion will be a witness against you, and it will consume your flesh like fire. You have stored up treasure in the last days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Jeremiah 22:13; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> Behold, the wages that were held back by you from the workers who reap your fields cry out, and the cries of the reapers have come to the ears of the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:6; Luke 16:25; Luke 16:19"> You have lived self-indulgently on the earth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “land”">a</a></sup>and have lived luxuriously. You have fattened your hearts in the day of slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:22–23; John 19:9–11; Isaiah 53:7"> You have condemned, you have murdered the righteous person; he does not resist you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Exhortation to Patience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1–3; James 5:8–9; Joel 2:23"> Therefore be patient, brothers, until the coming of the Lord. Behold, the farmer waits for the precious fruit of the soil, being patient concerning it until it receives the early and late rains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 5:9; Galatians 5:22; Psalm 37:7"> You also be patient. Strengthen your hearts, because the coming of the Lord is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; 1 Corinthians 4:5; James 4:11"> Brothers, do not complain against one another, in order that you may not be judged. Behold, the judge stands before the doors!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 11:32–38; Matthew 5:11–12; Hebrews 13:7"> Brothers, take as an example of perseverance and endurance the prophets who spoke in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 1:12; Exodus 34:6; Job 1:21–22"> Behold, we consider blessed those who have endured. You have heard about the patient endurance of Job, and you saw the outcome from the Lord, that the Lord is compassionate and merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 5:33–37; Matthew 23:16–22; 2 Corinthians 1:17–20"> Now above all, my brothers, do not swear either by heaven or by earth or by any other oath, but let your yes be yes and your no, no, in order that you may not fall under judgment.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Effective Prayer of Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 50:15; Jonah 2:2; Acts 16:24–25"> Is anyone among you suffering misfortune? He should pray. Is anyone cheerful? He should sing praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 6:13; Acts 28:8; Mark 16:18"> Is anyone among you sick? He should summon the elders of the church and they should pray over him, anointing him with olive oil in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 5:14–16; James 5:13; James 5:16"> And the prayer of faith will save the one who is sick, and the Lord will raise him up, and if he has committed sins ⸤he will be forgiven⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “it will be forgiven him”">b</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12–13; Proverbs 15:29; Matthew 21:22"> Therefore confess your sins to one another, and pray for one another, so that you may be healed. The effective prayer of a righteous person accomplishes much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Revelation 11:6"> Elijah was a human being<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “man,” but in a generic sense; the emphasis is not on Elijah’s masculine gender but his shared humanity">c</a></sup>with the same nature as us, and ⸤he prayed fervently⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “he prayed with prayer”">d</a></sup>for it not to rain, and it did not rain on the land for three years and six months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 18:42–45; Jeremiah 14:22; 1 Kings 18:18"> And he prayed again, and the sky gave rain and the earth produced its fruit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:1; Matthew 18:15; James 5:20"> My brothers, if anyone among you should wander away from the truth and someone turns him back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; Proverbs 10:12; 1 Timothy 4:16"> he should know that the one who turns a sinner back from the error of his way will save that person’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “his,” but in context this refers to the sinner, not the person who turns the sinner back">e</a></sup>soul from death, and will cover over a great number of sins.</span>
</div>
</div>
