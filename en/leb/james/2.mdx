## Partiality Forbidden

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 24:23; James 2:9; Leviticus 19:15"> My brothers, do not hold your faith in our glorious Lord Jesus Christ with partiality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 15:22; Esther 3:10; Esther 8:2"> For if someone<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a man,” but clearly in a generic sense here meaning “someone, a person”">a</a></sup>enters into your assembly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “synagogue,” but here probably referring to a Christian assembly">b</a></sup>in fine clothing with a gold ring on his finger, and a poor person in filthy clothing also enters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:9; James 2:6; Luke 7:44–46"> and you look favorably on the one wearing the fine clothing and you say, “Be seated here in a good place,” and to the poor person you say, “You stand or be seated there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “you stand there or be seated”">c</a></sup>by my footstool,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:24; Job 34:19; Matthew 7:1–5"> have you not made distinctions among yourselves and become judges with evil thoughts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Luke 6:20; Revelation 2:9"> Listen, my dear brothers! Did not God choose the poor of the world to be rich in faith, and heirs of the kingdom that he has promised to those who love him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 17:5; 1 Corinthians 11:22; Proverbs 14:31"> But you have dishonored the poor! Are not the rich exploiting you and they themselves dragging you into the courts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Revelation 13:5–6; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Do they themselves not blaspheme the good name ⸤of the one to whom you belong⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “that was called over you”">d</a></sup>?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:18; Galatians 5:14; Matthew 22:39"> However, if you carry out the royal law according to the scripture, “You shall love your neighbor as yourself,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 19:18">e</a></sup>you are doing well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; James 2:1–4; 1 John 3:4"> But if you show partiality, you commit sin, and thus are convicted by the law as transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Matthew 5:18–19; Deuteronomy 27:26"> For whoever keeps the whole law but stumbles in one point only has become guilty of all of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:13–14; Matthew 5:21–28; Deuteronomy 5:17–18"> For the one who said “Do not commit adultery”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 20:14 ; Deut 5:18">f</a></sup>also said “Do not murder.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 20:13 ; Deut 5:17">g</a></sup>Now if you do not commit adultery but you do murder, you have become a transgressor of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 1:25; James 2:8; 2 Peter 1:4–8"> Thus speak and thus act as those who are going to be judged by the law of liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Matthew 6:15; Proverbs 21:13"> For judgment is merciless to the one who has not practiced mercy. Mercy triumphs over judgment.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Faith and Works

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Matthew 7:21–23; James 1:22–25"> What is the benefit, my brothers, if someone says that he has faith but does not have works? That faith is not able to save him, is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 3:11; Matthew 25:35–40; Isaiah 58:7"> If a brother or a sister is poorly clothed and lacking food for the day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 3:27–28; 1 John 3:16–18; Matthew 25:42–45"> and one of you should say to them, “Go in peace, keep warm and ⸤eat well⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “be satisfied”">h</a></sup>,” but does not give them what is necessary for the body, what is the benefit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 2:26; 2 Peter 1:5–9; James 2:19–20"> Thus also faith, if it does not have works, is dead by itself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 3:13; James 2:14; Galatians 5:6"> But someone will say, “You have faith and I have works.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some see the quotation by “someone” extending to the end of v. 18 , while others see it extending through v. 19">i</a></sup>Show me your faith apart from your works, and I will show you my faith by my works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 4:34; Mark 1:24; Matthew 8:29"> You believe that God is one; you do well. Even the demons believe, and shudder!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 2:17; James 2:26; Proverbs 12:11"> But do you want to know, O foolish person, that faith apart from works is useless?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Genesis 22:9–12; John 8:39"> Was not Abraham our father justified by works when he offered up his son Isaac on the altar?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:3; Hebrews 11:17–19; Galatians 5:6"> You see that faith was working together with his works, and by the works the faith was perfected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Isaiah 41:8; 2 Chronicles 20:7"> And the scripture was fulfilled that says, “And Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him for righteousness,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 15:6">j</a></sup>and he was called God’s friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:15–18; Psalm 60:12"> You see that a person is justified by works and not by faith alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; Joshua 6:22–25; Joshua 2:19–21"> And likewise was not Rahab the prostitute also justified by works when she welcomed the messengers and sent them out by a different route?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:20; James 2:17; James 2:14"> For just as the body without the spirit is dead, so also faith without works is dead.</span>
</div>
</div>
