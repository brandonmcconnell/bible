## Subject Yourselves to God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:23"> From where are conflicts and from where are quarrels among you? Is it not from this, from your pleasures that wage war among your members?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:24; Matthew 7:7–8; James 1:5"> You desire and do not have; you murder and are filled with envy, and are not able to obtain; you fight and quarrel. You do not have because you do not ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:14; 1 John 3:22; James 1:6–7"> You ask and do not receive, because you ask with wrong motives, in order that you may spend it on your pleasures.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; Romans 8:7; John 15:19"> Adulterous people! Do you not know that friendship with the world is enmity with God? Therefore whoever wants to be a friend of the world makes himself an enemy of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 6:19; Numbers 11:29"> Or do you think that in vain the scripture says, “The spirit which he caused to dwell in us desires jealously”?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This translation takes “spirit” to be the capacity within people that produces a divided mind and conflicts regarding God; other translations take this to refer to the Holy Spirit: “He (God) jealously desires the Spirit which he caused to dwell in us”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Proverbs 3:34; 1 Peter 5:5"> But he gives greater grace. Therefore it says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God opposes the proud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but gives grace to the humble.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Prov 3:34">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; Ephesians 4:27; Ephesians 6:11–12"> Therefore subject yourselves to God. But resist the devil, and he will flee from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 145:18; Hebrews 10:22; Malachi 3:7"> Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:25"> Lament and mourn and weep! Let your laughter be turned to mourning, and your joy to gloominess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 147:6; Matthew 23:12; 1 Peter 5:6"> Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will exalt you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1; Romans 2:1; James 5:9"> Do not speak evil of one another, brothers. The one who speaks evil of a brother or judges his brother speaks evil of the law and judges the law. But if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law but a judge of the law.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The words “of the law” are not in the Greek text, but are an understood repetition from the previous clause">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:4; Romans 2:1; Matthew 10:28"> There is one lawgiver and judge who is able to save and to destroy. But who are you to judge your neighbor?</span>
</div>
</div>

## Arrogant Boasting About the Future

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Luke 12:17–20; Genesis 11:3–4"> Come now, you who say, “Today or tomorrow we will travel to such and such a city and spend a year there, and carry on business and make a profit,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; Psalm 39:5; 1 John 2:17"> you who do not know ⸤what will happen tomorrow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the circumstance of tomorrow”">d</a></sup>, ⸤what your life will be like⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “of what sort your life”">e</a></sup>. For you are a smoky vapor that appears for a short time and then disappears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Hebrews 6:3; Lamentations 3:37"> Instead you should say, “If the Lord wills, we will live and do this or that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; 1 Corinthians 5:6; Psalm 52:7"> But now you boast in your arrogance. All such boasting is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:41; John 13:17; John 15:22"> Therefore, to the one who knows to do good and does not do it, to him it is sin.</span>
</div>
