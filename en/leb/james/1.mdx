## Trials, Testing, and Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:6–8; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Consider it all joy, my brothers, whenever you encounter various trials,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 5:3–4; Hebrews 10:36; Colossians 1:11"> because you know that the testing of your faith produces endurance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:19; 1 Peter 5:10; Matthew 5:48"> And let endurance have its perfect effect, so that you may be mature and complete, lacking in nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:3–6; John 15:7; Proverbs 3:5–7"> Now if any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask for it from God, who gives to all without reservation and not reproaching, and it will be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:22–24"> But let him ask for it in faith, without any doubting, for the one who doubts is like the surf of the sea, driven by the wind and tossed about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:3; Isaiah 58:3–4; Proverbs 21:27"> For that person must not suppose that he will receive anything from the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; Matthew 6:24; James 4:8"> he is a double-minded man, unstable in all his ways.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; James 2:5–6; Proverbs 19:1"> Now let the brother of humble circumstances boast in his high position,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; 1 John 2:17; Job 14:2"> but the rich person in his humiliation, because he will pass away like a flower of the grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7–8; Psalm 37:35–36; Psalm 49:6–14"> For the sun rises with its burning heat and dries up the grass, and its flower falls off, and the beauty of its appearance is lost. So also the rich person in his pursuits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “on his (business) journeys”; or “in his ways”">a</a></sup>will wither away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Peter 1:6–8; Revelation 2:10"> Blessed is the person<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the man,” but clearly in a generic sense here meaning “someone, a person”">b</a></sup>who endures testing, because when he is approved he will receive the crown of life that he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Most manuscripts read “the Lord” here, while others read “God”">c</a></sup>has promised to those who love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:12–13; James 1:12; Romans 9:19–20"> No one who is being tempted should say, “I am being tempted by God,” for God ⸤cannot be tempted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “is without temptation”">d</a></sup>by evil, and he himself tempts no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; Ephesians 4:22; Matthew 15:18"> But each one is tempted when he is dragged away and enticed by his own desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 7:14; Job 15:35; Romans 5:12–21"> Then desire, after it has conceived, gives birth to sin, and sin, when it is brought to completion, gives birth to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; James 1:19; Philippians 2:12"> Do not be deceived, my dear brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 3:27; Matthew 7:11; Hebrews 13:8"> Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom there is no variation or shadow of change.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:29–31; 1 Peter 1:23; John 3:3–5"> By his will he gave birth to us through the message of truth, so that we should be a kind of first fruits of his creatures.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Doers of the Message, Not Just Hearers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 17:27; Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 14:29"> Understand this, my dear brothers: every person must be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:26; James 3:17–18"> for human<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “man’s”">e</a></sup>anger does not accomplish the righteousness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:11"> Therefore, putting aside all moral uncleanness and wicked excess, welcome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “receive”">f</a></sup>with humility the implanted message which is able to save your souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46–48; John 13:17"> But be doers of the message and not hearers only,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “not only hearers”">g</a></sup>deceiving yourselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Ezekiel 33:31–32; James 2:14–26"> because if anyone is a hearer of the message and not a doer, this one is like someone<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a man,” but clearly in a generic sense here meaning “someone, a person”">h</a></sup>staring at ⸤his own face⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the face of his existence”">i</a></sup>in a mirror,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Peter 3:11; Luke 1:66; Luke 7:39"> for he looks at himself and goes away and immediately forgets what sort of person he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:28; John 13:17; 2 Corinthians 3:17–18"> But the one who looks into the perfect law of liberty and continues to do it, not being a forgetful hearer but a doer who acts, this one will be blessed ⸤in what he does⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “in his doing”">j</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Psalm 141:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> If anyone thinks he is religious, although he does not bridle his tongue but deceives his heart, this person’s religion is worthless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; 1 John 3:17–19; Galatians 6:9–10"> Pure and undefiled religion in the sight of our God and Father is this: to look after orphans and widows in their affliction, and to keep oneself unstained by the world.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
