## Restraining the Tongue

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 12:47–48; 1 Timothy 1:7; 1 Peter 5:3"> Not many should become teachers, my brothers, because you know that we will receive a greater judgment.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “greater condemnation”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 13:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> For we all stumble in many ways. If anyone does not stumble in what he says, he is a perfect individual,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “man,” but clearly in a generic sense here meaning “someone, a person”">b</a></sup>able to hold in check his whole body also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 32:9; Psalm 39:1; James 1:26"> And if we put bits in the mouths of horses so that they obey us, we also guide their whole bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 107:25–27; Matthew 8:24; Acts 27:14–38"> Behold also ships: although they are so large and are driven by strong winds, they are guided by a very small rudder wherever the inclination of the pilot wishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 18:21; Jeremiah 9:3–8; Proverbs 12:18"> So also the tongue is a small member of the body and boasts great things. Behold how small a fire sets ablaze how great a forest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 120:2–3; Proverbs 16:27; Mark 7:15"> And the tongue is a fire! The world of unrighteousness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “a fire, the world of unrighteousness! The tongue is set among our members”">c</a></sup>the tongue, is set among our members, defiling the whole body and setting on fire ⸤the course of human existence⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the wheel of origin”">d</a></sup>, being set on fire by hell.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 5:4"> For every species of animals and birds, of reptiles and sea creatures, is being tamed and has been tamed by the human species,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalm 140:3; Psalm 55:21"> but no human being is able to tame the tongue. It is a restless evil, full of deadly poison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 10:7; Genesis 1:26–27; Psalm 62:4"> With it we bless the Lord and Father, and with it we curse people who are made in the likeness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:14; 1 Peter 3:9; 1 Corinthians 3:3"> From the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brothers, these things ought not to be so!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> A spring does not pour forth from the same opening fresh and bitter water, does it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> A fig tree is not able, my brothers, to produce olives, or a grapevine figs. Neither can a saltwater spring produce fresh water.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Wisdom That Comes Down from Above

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:23; James 3:17; Matthew 5:5"> Who is wise and understanding among you? Let him show by his good behavior his works, with the humility of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4; Philippians 2:3"> But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast and tell lies against the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> This is not the wisdom that comes down from above, but is earthly, unspiritual, demonic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 3:14; 1 Corinthians 3:3; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> For where there is jealousy and selfish ambition, there is disorder and every evil practice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 5:22–23; Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceful, gentle, obedient, full of mercy and good fruits, nonjudgmental, without hypocrisy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Hosea 10:12; Philippians 1:11"> And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace among<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “for”; or possibly “by”">e</a></sup>those who make peace.</span>
</div>
