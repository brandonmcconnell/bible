## David Returns to Achish

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 40:27–31; 1 Samuel 21:10–15; Isaiah 51:12"> Then David ⸤thought to himself⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “said to his heart”">a</a></sup>“Now I will perish one day by the hand of Saul! There is nothing better for me but that I must certainly escape to the land of the Philistines. Then Saul will desist from searching for me further in all of the territories of Israel, and so I will escape from his hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:10; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Kings 2:39–40"> So David got up and crossed over, he and the six hundred men who were with him, to Achish the son of Maoch, the king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 1 Samuel 30:5; 1 Samuel 25:18–35"> David settled with Achish in Gath, he and his men, each with his household.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “family”">b</a></sup>David took along his two wives Ahinoam ⸤from Jezreel⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the Jezreelitess”">c</a></sup>and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:21"> And it was reported to Saul that David had fled to Gath, so ⸤he no longer searched for him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “he does Qere did not again to seek him longer”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 46:34; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> Then David said to Achish, “Please, if I have found favor in your eyes, then let them give me a place in one of the ⸤country towns⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “towns of the open field”">e</a></sup>that I can live there. Why should your servant live in ⸤the royal city⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the city of the king”">f</a></sup>with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:28; Joshua 15:31; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> So Achish gave him Ziklag on that day. (Therefore, Ziklag has belonged to the kings of Judah until this day.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:3"> The number of days that David lived in the countryside of the Philistines was one year and four months.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:7–8; Joshua 13:2; Joshua 13:13"> Now David and his men went up and raided the Geshurites and the Girzites and the Amalekites, for they had been living in the land for a long time ⸤in the direction of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “as you come”">g</a></sup>Shur and ⸤as far as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “up to”">h</a></sup>the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 25:17–19"> So David struck the land and did not leave a man or a woman alive; he took the sheep,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “flock,” referring to either sheep or goats or both">i</a></sup>the cattle, the donkeys, the camels, and the clothing. Then he returned and came to Achish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Chronicles 2:9; 1 Chronicles 2:25"> And Achish said, “Against whom have you raided today?” David said, “Against the Negev of Judah and against the Negev of the Jerahmeelites and against the Negev of the Kenites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Proverbs 12:19; 1 Samuel 22:22"> And David did not leave alive a man or a woman to bring them back to Gath, thinking,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “saying”">j</a></sup>“So that they will not report about us, saying, ‘David did thus and so.’ ” Thus was his practice all the days that he lived in the countryside of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; 1 Samuel 13:4"> And Achish trusted David, saying, “⸤He has made himself utterly hated⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “Stinking he stink” = “he really stinks”">k</a></sup>among his people in Israel, and he will be my servant forever.”</span>
</div>
