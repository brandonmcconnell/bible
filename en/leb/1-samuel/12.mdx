## Samuel’s Charge to Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Samuel 11:14–15; 1 Samuel 10:1"> Then Samuel said to all Israel, “Look, I have listened to your voice regarding all that you have said to me, so I have set a king over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; 1 Samuel 8:1; 1 Samuel 8:20"> And so then here is the king walking about before you. Now I am old and gray, but my sons (look at them!) are with you; and I have walked about before you from my youth until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Numbers 16:15; 1 Peter 5:2"> Here I am! Testify against me before Yahweh and before his anointed one! Whose ox have I taken? Or whose donkey have I taken? Or whom have I exploited? Whom have I oppressed? Or from whose hand have I taken a bribe, that I may shut my eyes ⸤regarding⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “with”">a</a></sup>him?–then I will restore it to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Psalm 37:5–6; 3 John 1:12"> Then they said, “You have not exploited us or oppressed us, and you have not taken anything from the hand of anyone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 22:4; Acts 24:20; Acts 23:9"> So he said to them, “Yahweh is witness against you, and his anointed one is witness this day, that you have not found anything in my hand.” Then they said, “He is witness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Micah 6:4; Exodus 6:26; Psalm 99:6"> Then Samuel said to the people, “Yahweh is witness, who appointed Moses and Aaron, and who brought your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “fathers”">b</a></sup>up from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; Micah 6:1–5; Acts 17:3"> So then take your stand, so that I may judge you before Yahweh with regard to all the deeds of justice<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “righteous deeds ”">c</a></sup>of Yahweh that he performed with both you and your ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “fathers”">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:14–16; Exodus 2:23–24; Psalm 78:54–55"> “When Jacob came to Egypt, your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>cried out to Yahweh, so he sent Moses and Aaron, and they brought your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “fathers”">f</a></sup>out from Egypt and settled them in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 13:1; Judges 10:7; Judges 3:12"> But they forgot Yahweh their God, so he sold them into the hand of Sisera, commander of the hosts of Hazor, and into the hand of the Philistines, and into the hand of the king of Moab, and they fought against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 10:10; Judges 2:13; Judges 3:7"> So they cried out to Yahweh and said, ‘We have sinned, because we have forsaken Yahweh and have served the Baals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew plural “Be’alim” (Ba’al was a major Canaanite deity)">g</a></sup>and the Ashtoreths.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew plural “Ashtoroth” (Ashtoreth was the female consort of Ba’al)">h</a></sup>But now deliver us from the hand of our enemies and we will serve you!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 4:6; Judges 8:35"> So Yahweh sent Jerub-Baal and Bedan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Many English translations follow the LXX here and read “Barak”">i</a></sup>and Jephthah and Samuel. Then he delivered you from the hand of your enemies all around, and you lived in security.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 8:23; 1 Samuel 8:5–7; Genesis 17:7"> “And when you saw that Nahash, the king of the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">j</a></sup>was coming against you, you said to me, ‘No! A king shall reign over us,’ although Yahweh your God is your king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hosea 13:11; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Samuel 8:5"> So then look! Here is the king you have chosen, for whom you have asked! Look, Yahweh has placed a king over you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 24:14; Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Psalm 81:12–15"> If you will fear Yahweh, and serve him, and listen to his voice, and not be rebellious against ⸤what Yahweh says⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of Yahweh”">k</a></sup>and both you and the king who rules over you will follow after Yahweh your God, all will be well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 24:20; Isaiah 1:20; Isaiah 3:11"> But, if you do not listen to the voice of Yahweh, and you rebel against ⸤what Yahweh says⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of Yahweh”">l</a></sup>then the hand of Yahweh will be against you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Here LXX reads “against you and against your king”">m</a></sup>as it was against your ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “fathers”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:13; Exodus 14:31; 1 Samuel 12:7"> So then take your stand again and see this great thing that Yahweh is going to do before your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Proverbs 26:1; 1 Samuel 7:9–10"> Is the wheat harvest not today? I will call out to Yahweh so that he still sends thunder and rain, so that you will know and will see that your wickedness is great that you have done in the eyes of Yahweh by asking for a king for yourselves.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:31; Ezra 10:9; Psalm 106:12–13"> So Samuel called out to Yahweh, and Yahweh brought thunder and rain that same day, so all the people feared Yahweh and Samuel greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 9:28; 1 John 5:16; 1 Samuel 12:23"> Then all the people said to Samuel, “Pray for your servants to Yahweh your God so that we will not die, because we have added to all our sins by requesting a king for ourselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:16; Joshua 23:6; 1 Peter 3:16"> And Samuel said to the people, “Do not fear! You have done all this evil; only do not turn aside from ⸤following⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “after”">o</a></sup>Yahweh. But you must serve Yahweh with all your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:18; Jeremiah 10:15; Jeremiah 16:19"> And do not turn aside after the triviality,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="That is, the triviality of lifeless idols. The same Hebrew word, tohu , is used in Genesis 1:2 to describe the pre-created state of the earth.">p</a></sup>which have no value and cannot deliver, for they are triviality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Deuteronomy 14:2; Psalm 106:8"> For Yahweh will not forsake his people for the sake of his great name, because Yahweh has decided to make you his own people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:9; Romans 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:3"> Also, as for me, far be it from me ⸤that I should sin⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “from sinning”">q</a></sup>against Yahweh by ceasing to pray for you! I will instruct you in the good and righteous way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:21; Proverbs 1:7; Isaiah 5:12"> Only fear Yahweh and serve him faithfully with all of your heart. For consider ⸤what great things he has done for you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “what he made great with you”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 24:20; Hosea 10:3; 1 Samuel 31:1–5"> But if you continue to do wickedness, both you and your king will be swept away.”</span>
</div>
