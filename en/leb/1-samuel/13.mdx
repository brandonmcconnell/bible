## Saul Rules over Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:5; Micah 6:6"> Saul was thirty<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A few LXX manuscripts have “thirty,” but the number of years is missing from the Masoretic Hebrew text">a</a></sup>⸤years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “a son of years”">b</a></sup>at the beginning of his reign, and he reigned forty-two years<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Masoretic Hebrew text reads “two years” here, but this number seems far too small; either it is not correct or part of the number is missing (the present translation supplies “forty”); other English versions differ as to Saul’s age at coronation and the total years of his reign">c</a></sup>over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 13:5; 1 Samuel 14:31"> He chose for himself three thousand from Israel. Two thousand of these were with Saul at Micmash in the hill country of Bethel, and a thousand were with Jonathan at Gibeah in Benjamin. He sent away the rest of the people, each to his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; Judges 3:27; Judges 6:34"> Jonathan defeated the garrison of the Philistines that was at Geba, and the Philistines heard about it. Then Saul blew the trumpet throughout all the land, saying, “Let the Hebrews hear!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Exodus 5:21; Zechariah 11:8"> And all Israel did hear, saying, “Saul has defeated the garrison of the Philistines; and also, Israel has become a stench among the Philistines!” So the people were called out after Saul at Gilgal.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Saul’s Blunder

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:23; Joshua 11:4; Joshua 18:12"> And the Philistines assembled to fight with Israel, thirty thousand chariots and six thousand horsemen and an army as numerous as sand which is on the seashore. And they came up and encamped at Micmash, east of Beth Aven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 6:2; Hebrews 11:38; Judges 10:9"> When the men of Israel saw that it was ⸤too difficult⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “too narrow”">d</a></sup>for them, because the army was hard pressed, the people hid themselves in the caves, in the thorn bushes, in the cliffs, in the vaults<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “cellars” or “tombs” ‘ or “strongholds”">e</a></sup>and in the wells.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:8; Leviticus 26:17; Numbers 32:1–5"> Some of the Hebrews crossed over the Jordan to the land of Gad and Gilead. But Saul was still at Gilgal, and all the army ⸤followed him trembling⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “trembled after him”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8"> He waited seven days according to the appointed time Samuel determined, but Samuel did not come to Gilgal, and ⸤the army started to slip away from him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the people scattered away from him”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 3:4; Proverbs 21:27; Proverbs 15:8"> So Saul said, “Bring here to me the burnt offering and the fellowship offerings.” Then he offered up the burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Ruth 2:4; Psalm 129:8"> ⸤Just as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “And it happened as”">h</a></sup>he finished offering the burnt sacrifice, Samuel was coming. So Saul went out to meet him and to bless him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 13:5; 1 Samuel 13:16"> But Samuel said, “What have you done?” Saul said, “Because I saw that the army ⸤was scattering⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “dispersed themselves”">i</a></sup>from me and you did not come ⸤at the appointed time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “according to the appointed time of the days”">j</a></sup>and that the Philistines had gathered at Micmash,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 66:3; Amos 8:5; 2 Corinthians 9:7"> therefore I said, ‘Now the Philistines will come down against me at Gilgal, and I have not yet implored the face of Yahweh.’ So I forced myself and offered the burnt offering.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 2 Chronicles 16:9; 1 Samuel 15:28"> Then Samuel said to Saul, “You have behaved foolishly! You have not kept the command of Yahweh your God which he commanded you. For then, Yahweh would have established your kingdom over Israel forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 13:22; 1 Samuel 15:28; Psalm 89:19–37"> But now, your kingdom will not endure. Yahweh has sought for himself a man according to his own heart, and Yahweh has appointed him as leader over his people, because you have not kept what Yahweh commanded you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; 1 Samuel 13:6–7"> Then Samuel got up and went up from Gilgal to Gibeah of Benjamin. And Saul mustered the people who were found with him, about six hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:3"> Saul and Jonathan his son and the army that remained with them were staying in Geba of Benjamin, and the Philistines encamped at Micmash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 18:23; 1 Samuel 14:15; Joshua 19:3"> The ⸤raiders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “destroyers”">k</a></sup>went out from the camp of the Philistines in three divisions. One division turned on the road<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “way”">l</a></sup>to Ophrah toward the land of Shual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:34; Joshua 18:13–14; Joshua 16:3"> One division turned on the road<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “way”">m</a></sup>to Beth Horon, and one turned on the road<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “way”">n</a></sup>toward the border overlooking the valley of Zeboim toward the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 24:14; Judges 5:8; Jeremiah 24:1"> Now no skilled craftsman could be found in all the land of Israel, for the Philistines had said, “So that the Hebrews cannot make swords or spears for themselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So all Israel went down to the Philistines, each to have his plowshare, his mattock, his axe, and his iron plowshare<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="So the Masoretic Hebrew text; LXX reads “sickle”">o</a></sup>sharpened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The charge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “fee”">p</a></sup>was ⸤two-thirds of a shekel⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a pim” (possibly a stone weight used as a measure)">q</a></sup>for the plowshare and for the mattock, and ⸤a third of a shekel for the pick⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “and for three, a pick” (uncertain; perhaps meaning “a third of a shekel,” or a three-pronged pick”)">r</a></sup>and for the axe, and to set the goading sticks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Judges 5:8; Zechariah 4:6; 1 Samuel 17:50"> ⸤So⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally, “And it happened”">s</a></sup>on the day of battle, there was not a sword or a spear found in the hands of all the army that was with Saul and Jonathan, but ⸤Saul and his son Jonathan had them⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally, “it could be found for Saul and for Jonathan his son”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 10:28; 1 Samuel 14:1; 1 Samuel 14:4–5"> Now the garrison of the Philistines went out to the pass of Micmash.</span>
</div>
</div>
