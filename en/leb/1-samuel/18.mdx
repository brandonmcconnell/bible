<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; Proverbs 18:24; 1 Samuel 18:3"> ⸤When⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “And it happened as”">a</a></sup>he finished speaking to Saul, the soul of Jonathan became attached to the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:15; 1 Samuel 16:21–23"> Saul took him on that very day and did not allow him to return to his father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:8–17; 1 Samuel 20:42; 2 Samuel 21:7"> Then Jonathan made a covenant with David, because he loved him as his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Luke 15:22; Esther 6:8–9"> Jonathan stripped off the robe ⸤that he was wearing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “which was on him”">b</a></sup>and gave it to David, along with his fighting attire,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “gown,” in this context “fighting tunic,” perhaps “armor”">c</a></sup>and even his sword, his bow, and his belt.</span>

## David’s Successes Arouse Saul’s Suspicions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:30; 1 Samuel 18:14–15; Colossians 4:5"> David went out ⸤whenever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in all that”">d</a></sup>Saul sent him, and he succeeded. So Saul appointed him over the men of the war, and it ⸤pleased⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “was good in the eyes of”">e</a></sup>all the people and even ⸤pleased⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “was good in the eyes of”">f</a></sup>the servants of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Judges 11:34; Psalm 68:25"> ⸤When they were coming back⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “And it happened at their coming”">g</a></sup>after David had returned from striking down the Philistine, the women went out from all the cities of Israel singing and dancing to meet King Saul with tambourines, with joy, and with three-stringed instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; 1 Samuel 29:5; Exodus 15:21"> And the women sang as they danced, and they said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Saul has struck down his thousands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but David his ten thousands!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; Proverbs 13:10; James 4:5"> ⸤Saul became very angry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “And it was very hot for Saul”">h</a></sup>and ⸤this saying displeased him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “this thing was bad in his eyes”">i</a></sup>and he thought, “They have attributed to David ten thousands, but to me they have attributed thousands! ⸤What more can he have but the kingdom⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “And still for him is only the kingdom”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 7:22; Genesis 31:2; Matthew 20:15"> So Saul was watching<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The Masoretic Hebrew text (Kethib ) reads “sin”; the translation follows the reading tradition (Qere) which has “eyeing” or “watching”">k</a></sup>David with suspicion from that day onward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 18:29"> ⸤On⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “And it happened from”">l</a></sup>the next day, the evil spirit from God rushed upon Saul, and he prophesied in the middle of the house. Now David was playing the lyre with his hand on that day ⸤as usual⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “as day on day”">m</a></sup>and the spear was in Saul’s hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:33; Isaiah 54:17; Luke 4:30"> Then Saul hurled the spear and thought, “⸤I will pin David to the wall⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “I will strike David and to the wall”">n</a></sup>But David eluded him twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:15; 1 Samuel 18:29; 1 Samuel 16:13–14"> ⸤Now Saul was threatened by the presence of David⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “And Saul was afraid from the presence of David”">o</a></sup>because Yahweh was with him, but had departed from Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:2; Psalm 121:8; 1 Samuel 18:16–17"> So Saul removed him ⸤from his presence⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “from with him”">p</a></sup>and made him commander of a thousand, ⸤so he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="That is, David">q</a></sup>marched in and out at the front of the army⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “for he was going out and was coming in before the army”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 39:23; Matthew 28:20; 1 Samuel 16:18"> And David was achieving success in all his ways and Yahweh was with him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 3:17; Colossians 4:5; James 1:5"> but when Saul saw that he was very successful, ⸤he was severely threatened by him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “he was terrified from his face”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:5; Numbers 27:17; 1 Kings 3:7"> However, all of Israel and Judah were loving David, for he was going forth and marching ahead of them.</span>
</div>

## David Marries Saul’s Daughter Michal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25; 1 Samuel 25:28; 1 Samuel 18:25"> Then Saul said to David, “Here is my older daughter Merab. I will give her to you as your wife. Only be ⸤a brave warrior⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “a son of valor”">t</a></sup>for me and fight the battles of Yahweh.” For Saul thought, “My hand will not be against him, but let the hand of the Philistines be against him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 18:23"> But David said to Saul, “Who am I, and who are my relatives, the clan of my father in Israel, that I should be a son-in-law to the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 2 Samuel 21:8; Judges 14:20"> ⸤But⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">u</a></sup>at the time Saul’s daughter Merab was to be given to David, she was given instead to Adriel the Meholathite as wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:28"> Now Saul’s daughter Michal loved David, so they told Saul, and the matter ⸤pleased him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “was right in his eyes”">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:17; Exodus 10:7; Jeremiah 9:8"> And Saul thought, “I will give her to him, so that she may be a snare for him and the hand of the Philistines may be against him.” So Saul said to David, “For a second time you can become my son-in-law today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 36:1–3; Psalm 55:21; 2 Samuel 13:28–29"> Then Saul commanded his servants, “Speak to David in secret, saying, ‘Look, the king is pleased with you, and all his servants love you. So then, become a son-in-law of the king.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 119:141; Proverbs 14:20; Ecclesiastes 9:15–16"> And Saul’s servants spoke these words ⸤to David privately⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “in the ears of David”">w</a></sup>But David said, “Is it insignificant ⸤in your sight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “in your eyes”">x</a></sup>to become the son-in-law of the king, as I am a poor and lightly esteemed man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So the servants of Saul informed him, saying, “⸤This is what David said⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “According to these words David spoke”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 34:12; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 14:24"> Then Saul said, “This is what you must say to David: ‘⸤The king desires no bride price⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “There is not a desire for the king for a bride price”">z</a></sup>except for a hundred foreskins of the Philistines, to avenge himself on the enemies of the king.’ ” (Now Saul had planned to allow David to fall by the hand of the Philistines.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:21"> So his servants told David these words, and the matter ⸤pleased David⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “was right in the eyes of David”">aa</a></sup>to become the son-in-law of the king as ⸤the specified time had not expired⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “the days were not full”">ab</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:14; Judges 14:19; 1 Samuel 18:13"> And David got up, and he and his men went and struck down two hundred men of the Philistines. Then David brought their foreskins, and ⸤they presented the full number⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “they filled them up”">ac</a></sup>to become the king’s son-in-law. Then Saul gave him Michal his daughter as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:20; 1 Samuel 26:25; Genesis 37:8–11"> When Saul ⸤realized⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “saw and knew”">ad</a></sup>that Yahweh was with David and ⸤his own daughter Michal⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “Michal the daughter of Saul”">ae</a></sup>loved him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:4; John 11:53; James 2:19"> Saul ⸤was threatened by David still more⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “added to be afraid from the presence of David”">af</a></sup>so Saul ⸤became a perpetual enemy of David⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “was an enemy with David all the days”">ag</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:5; 2 Samuel 11:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> Then the commanders of the Philistines went out for battle, and as often as they went out, David succeeded more than all the servants of Saul, and his name became very esteemed.</span>
</div>
