## Saul Takes Revenge on the Priests Who Helped David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalm 57:1–11; Psalm 142:1–7; 1 Chronicles 11:15"> David went from there and escaped to the cave of Adullam. When his brothers and all his father’s household<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “family”">a</a></sup>heard, they came down to him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; 1 Samuel 30:22–24; Psalm 72:12–14"> Every man in distress and every man who had a creditor and every embittered man gathered to him, and he became ⸤their commander⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “over them as commander”">b</a></sup>Now there were about four hundred men with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:18; 2 Samuel 15:25–26; Judges 11:29"> And David went up from there to Mizpah of Moab. He said to the king of Moab, “Please let my father and my mother ⸤stay⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “come/go out”">c</a></sup>with you until I know what God will do for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 11:1; 1 Chronicles 12:16; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> So ⸤he brought them before the king of Moab⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “he led them into the presence of the king of Moab”">d</a></sup>and they stayed with him all the days David was in the stronghold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25; 1 Chronicles 21:9; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> Then Gad the prophet said to David, “You should not stay in the stronghold; leave and go into the land of Judah.” So David left and came to the forest of Hereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 1 Samuel 18:10; 1 Samuel 20:33"> Now Saul heard that David and the men who were with him had been located. Saul was sitting at Gibeah under the tamarisk tree at Ramah. Now his spear was in his hand and all his servants were stationed around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 12:16; 1 Samuel 8:14–15; 1 Samuel 22:13"> Then Saul said to the servants who were standing around him, “Please listen, ⸤Benjaminites⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “children of Benjamin”">e</a></sup>Will the son of Jesse give you all fields and vineyards? Will he make all of you commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:21; 1 Samuel 20:13–17"> For all of you have conspired against me, and ⸤no one discloses to me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “there is no one revealing to my ear”">f</a></sup>the ⸤making⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “cutting”">g</a></sup>of an agreement between my son and the son of Jesse! None of you have had sympathy for me or ⸤disclosed to me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “revealing to my ear”">h</a></sup>that ⸤my son commissioned my servant against me to ambush me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “my son has raised my servant against me to an ambush”">i</a></sup>as has been done this day!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 52:1–9; 1 Samuel 14:3; Ezekiel 22:9"> But Doeg the Edomite, who was stationed among the servants of Saul, answered and said, “I saw the son of Jesse going to Nob, to Ahimelech the son of Ahitub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; 1 Samuel 30:8; 1 Samuel 23:4"> And he inquired of Yahweh for him and gave him provisions and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:15"> So the king sent to summon Ahimelech the son of Ahitub the priest, and all his father’s household, the priests who were at Nob. So all of them came to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:6; 1 Samuel 22:7; Isaiah 65:1"> Saul said, “Listen please, son of Ahitub.” He said, “Here I am, my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Luke 23:2–5; Amos 7:10"> Then Saul said to him, “Why did you conspire against me, you and the son of Jesse, when you gave to him bread and a sword, and by inquiring of God for him so that he might arise against me to ambush me as has been done this day?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:32; 1 Samuel 19:4–5; 1 Samuel 22:13"> But Ahimelech answered the king and said, “And who among all your servants is as faithful as David? He is the son-in-law of the king who moves quickly to safeguard you and is honored in your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:36; 1 Peter 3:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Only today I began to inquire of God for him. Far be it from me that ⸤the king should impute anything against his servant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the king will not put against his servant a matter”">j</a></sup>or against my father’s household, for your servant has not known any of this matter, little or much.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 28:15; Daniel 3:19–20; 1 Kings 19:2"> Then the king said, “You must certainly die, Ahimelech, you and all your father’s household!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:17; 1 Samuel 14:45; 2 Kings 1:13–14"> So the king said to the runners who were stationed around him, “Turn and kill the priests of Yahweh, because ⸤they also support David⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “also their hand is with David”">k</a></sup>and because they knew that he was fleeing and ⸤did not disclose it to me⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “did not reveal it to his ear”; the Masoretic Hebrew text (Kethib ) reads “his,” but the reading tradition (Qere) has “my”">l</a></sup>But the servants of the king were not willing to raise their hand to attack the priests of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:10–11; Hosea 7:3; 1 Samuel 2:18"> Then the king said to Doeg, “You turn and attack the priests!” So Doeg the Edomite turned and attacked the priests himself, and on that day he killed eighty-five men who wore the linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Hosea 10:14; 1 Samuel 21:1"> And ⸤he put to the sword⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “he killed with the mouth of the sword”">m</a></sup>Nob, the city of the priests, from man to woman, from child to infant, and ox and donkey and sheep;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “goats”; literally, “small livestock animals”">n</a></sup>all ⸤to the sword⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “with the mouth of the sword”">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:6; 1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> But, one of the sons of Ahimelech the son of Ahitub, whose name was Abiathar, escaped and fled after David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:1–9; Psalm 44:22"> Then David said to Abiathar, “I knew on that day that Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would certainly tell Saul. ⸤I am responsible for the deaths of all your father’s household⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “I have turned against all the life of your father’s house”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26; Hebrews 12:1–3; John 10:28–30"> Stay with me! Do not fear, because he who seeks my life seeks your life. You are in good care with me.”</span>
</div>
