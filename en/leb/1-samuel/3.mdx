## Samuel’s First Encounter with Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 8:11–12; Psalm 74:9; 1 Samuel 3:21"> Now the boy Samuel was serving Yahweh ⸤in the presence⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “in the face of”">a</a></sup>of Eli. The word of Yahweh was rare in those days; visions were not widespread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:15; Psalm 90:10; Genesis 27:1"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">b</a></sup>one day when Eli was lying in his place (now his eyes had begun to grow weak so that he was not able to see)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 27:4; Leviticus 24:2–4; Exodus 27:20–21"> and the lamp of God had not yet gone out, Samuel was lying in the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Although the Hebrew text reads “temple,” this is a reference to the tabernacle (see 1:3, 9)">c</a></sup>of Yahweh where the ark of God was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 6:8; 1 Corinthians 12:6–11; Exodus 3:4"> Then Yahweh called out to Samuel and he said, “Here I am!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he ran to Eli and said, “Here I am, because you called me.” But he said, “I did not call you. Go back and lie down.” So he went and lay down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; 1 Samuel 4:16; 2 Samuel 18:22"> And Yahweh called Samuel again, so Samuel got up and went to Eli and said, “Here I am, because you called me.” And he said, “I did not call you, my son. Go back and lie down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:2; Jeremiah 9:24"> Now Samuel did not yet know Yahweh, and the word of Yahweh had not yet been revealed to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 33:14–15; 1 Corinthians 13:11–12"> Again Yahweh called Samuel a third time, so he got up and went to Eli and said, “Here I am, because you called me.” Then Eli realized that Yahweh was calling the boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 6:8; Psalm 85:8"> So Eli said to Samuel “Go lie down. ⸤If he calls⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen that if he calls”">d</a></sup>to you, then you must say, ‘Speak Yahweh, because your servant is listening.’ ” So Samuel went and lay down in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 3:4–6"> Then Yahweh came and stood there and called out ⸤as before⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “as an occurrence, in an occurrence”">e</a></sup>“Samuel! Samuel!” And Samuel said, “Speak, because your servant is listening.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; Jeremiah 19:3; 2 Kings 21:12"> So Yahweh said to Samuel, “Look, I am doing something in Israel ⸤which will cause⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “about which”">f</a></sup>the two ears of everyone who hears it to tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:27–36; Zechariah 1:6; Joshua 23:15"> On that day I will fulfill against Eli all that I have spoken against his household, ⸤from beginning to end⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “begin and finish”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 19:18; Matthew 10:37; 1 Samuel 2:12"> I will make him know that I am about to judge his household forever because of the iniquity that he knew, for his sons were bringing curses on themselves,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="LXX reads “were cursing God”">h</a></sup>but he did not rebuke them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 22:14; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 15:1"> Therefore I swore to the house of Eli that the iniquity of the house of Eli would not be atoned for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “be expiated”">i</a></sup>by sacrifice or by offering forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:6–8; Malachi 1:10; 1 Samuel 1:9"> So Samuel lay down until morning; then he opened the doors of the house of Yahweh, but Samuel was afraid of telling the vision to Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then Eli called Samuel and said, “Samuel, my son!” And he said, “Here I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:35; Ruth 1:17; Psalm 141:5"> And he said, “What is the message<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “word”">j</a></sup>that he spoke to you? Please do not conceal it from me. May God ⸤punish you severely⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “do to you and so may he add”">k</a></sup>if you conceal anything from me of all the words that he told you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 2:10; Isaiah 39:8; Judges 10:15"> So Samuel told him all the words and did not conceal anything from him. And he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="That is, Eli">l</a></sup>said, “He is Yahweh, he will do ⸤what is⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the”">m</a></sup>good in his ⸤sight⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “eyes”">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:2; 1 Samuel 9:6; 1 Kings 8:56"> And Samuel grew up, and Yahweh was with him. ⸤He did not allow any of his prophecies to go unfulfilled⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “He let none from all of his words fall to the ground.”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 2 Samuel 3:10; 1 Timothy 1:12"> All Israel from Dan to Beersheba realized that Samuel was faithful<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “reliable” or “trustworthy”">p</a></sup>as a prophet to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:1; Genesis 12:7; 1 Samuel 3:4"> And Yahweh appeared again in Shiloh, for Yahweh revealed himself to Samuel in Shiloh through the word of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
</div>
