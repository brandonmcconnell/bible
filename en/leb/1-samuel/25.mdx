## David and Abigail

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 38:13; 1 Samuel 23:24; Joshua 15:55"> Now there was a man in Maon, whose business was in Carmel. <span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 31:10; Isaiah 32:5–7"> The name of the man was Nabal,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The name “Nabal” means “foolish”">c</a></sup>and the name of his wife was Abigail.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The name “Abigail” means “my father delights”">d</a></sup>Now the woman was wise and beautiful, but the man was stubborn and ⸤mean⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “bad of deeds”">e</a></sup>and he was as his heart.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Masoretic Hebrew text (Kethib ) reads “as his heart,” whereas the reading tradition (Qere) reads “Calebite”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 38:13; 2 Samuel 13:23"> David heard in the wilderness that Nabal was shearing his sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; 1 Samuel 17:22"> So David sent ten young men, and David said to the young men, “Go up to Carmel and go to Nabal; ⸤you will greet him in my name⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “you will ask for him in my name as far as peace”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 10:5; 1 Chronicles 12:18; Psalm 122:7"> Then you must say to him, ‘Long life to you, and ⸤may it go well with you, with your house, and with all that is yours⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “you are peace and your house is peace and all that is yours is peace”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:21; 1 Samuel 25:15–16; 1 Samuel 22:2"> Now I have heard that ⸤you have shearers⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “shearing is for you”">i</a></sup>Now while your shepherds were with us, we did not mistreat them, and nothing of theirs was missing, all the days they were in Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 9:19; Nehemiah 8:10–12; Luke 14:12–14"> Ask your servants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “young men”">j</a></sup>and they will tell you! Let the young men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “the servants”; “young men” is used here to connect with v. 5">k</a></sup>find favor in your eyes because we have come on a feast day. Please give ⸤whatever you have on hand⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “what your hand finds”">l</a></sup>for your servants and for your son David.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 2:15; 2 Chronicles 14:7; Genesis 8:4"> So David’s young men came and they spoke all these words to Nabal in the name of David. Then they waited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 9:28; Isaiah 32:7; 1 Samuel 22:7–8"> But Nabal answered David’s servants and said, “Who is David? And who is the son of Jesse? Today, there are many servants breaking away from the presence of their masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 8:6; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> Should I take my bread and my water and my meat which I have slaughtered for my shearers and give it to men whom I do not know where they are from?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:21–22; 2 Samuel 24:13; Hebrews 13:17"> So David’s young men turned on their way and returned and came and told him according to all these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:13; Proverbs 25:8; 1 Samuel 24:5–6"> Then David said to his men, “Each man strap on his sword!” So each one strapped on his sword, and David also strapped on his sword. About four hundred men went up after David, while two hundred remained with the baggage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:10; Mark 15:29"> But a young man of the servants told Abigail, Nabal’s wife, saying, “Look, David sent messengers from the desert to greet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “bless”">m</a></sup>our master, but he addressed them angrily,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:7; 1 Samuel 25:21; Philippians 2:15"> even though the men were very good to us; we were not mistreated and did not miss anything all the days we went about with them ⸤while we were⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “at our being”">n</a></sup>in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:10; Exodus 14:22; Zechariah 2:5"> They were a wall to us both night and day, all ⸤the days we were⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the days of our being”">o</a></sup>with them keeping the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:7; Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Samuel 20:9"> And so then, know and ⸤consider⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “see”">p</a></sup>what you should do, for evil has been decided against our master and against all his household, and he is such ⸤a wicked man⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a son of uselessness”">q</a></sup>⸤nobody can reason with him⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “from speaking to him”">r</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1; Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 6:4–5"> Then Abigail ⸤quickly took⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “hastened and took”">s</a></sup>two hundred loaves of bread, two skins of wine, five prepared sheep, five seahs of roasted grain, a hundred raisin cakes, and two hundred fig cakes, and she put them on the donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 32:20; Genesis 32:16; Proverbs 31:11–12"> Then she said to her servants, “Go ahead before me; look, I am coming after you,” but she did not tell her husband Nabal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 4:24"> ⸤And then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">t</a></sup>as she was riding on the donkey and was going down the ravine of the mountain, David and his men were coming down to meet her, and she met them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Ephesians 4:31; Romans 12:21"> Now David had said, “Surely ⸤in vain⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “for the vanity”">u</a></sup>I guarded all that this fellow had in the desert. <span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 14:10; 1 Samuel 20:13; 1 Samuel 3:17"> ⸤May God severely punish the enemies of David⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “May God do so to the enemies of David”; following the LXX some English versions omit the words “the enemies of”">v</a></sup>and again do thus if I leave behind ⸤anything that is his⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “from all that is for him”">w</a></sup>until the morning, not even ⸤one male⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “from one urinating against a wall”">x</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esther 8:3; 1 Samuel 25:28; 2 Samuel 14:9"> She fell at his feet and said, “On me, my lord, be the guilt! <span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:17; Malachi 2:2; 2 Samuel 13:33"> Please do not let my lord set his heart against ⸤this worthless man⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “this man of wickedness”">aa</a></sup>Nabal; for as his name, so is he. Nabal is his name, and stupidity is with him! But I, your female servant, did not see the young men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “the servants”">ab</a></sup>of my lord whom you sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:32; Romans 12:19–20; Genesis 20:6"> So then, my lord, ⸤as Yahweh lives and as your soul lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “the life of Yahweh and the life of your soul”">ac</a></sup>since Yahweh restrained you from bloodguilt ⸤by taking matters into your own hand⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “saving your hand for you”">ad</a></sup>so then, may your enemies be like Nabal, even ⸤those who seek to do my lord harm⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “the seeking to my lord evil”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 30:26; 2 Kings 5:15"> So then, this gift which your female servant has brought to my lord, may it be given to the young men ⸤who follow my lord⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “the ones going about at the feet of my lord”">af</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:17; 2 Samuel 7:11; 1 Samuel 24:11"> Please forgive the transgression of your female servant, because Yahweh will certainly make a lasting house<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="That is, a lasting dynasty">ag</a></sup>for my lord, because my lord is fighting the battles of Yahweh, and evil will not be found in you ⸤as long as you live⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “from your days”">ah</a></sup></span>

But as for the life of your enemy, he will sling it from within<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “in the midst of”">aj</a></sup>the pocket of the sling!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:14; Psalm 89:20; 1 Samuel 23:17"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen”">ak</a></sup>when Yahweh has done for my lord according to all the good that he has spoken concerning you, and has appointed you as leader over Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 40:14; Romans 12:19; Luke 23:42"> then this will not be an obstacle for you or a stumbling block of conscience for my lord either by the shedding of blood without cause or by ⸤my lord taking matters into his own hands⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “the saving of my lord for himself”">al</a></sup>And when Yahweh does good to my lord, then remember your female servant.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; Proverbs 28:23; Proverbs 9:9"> And blessed be your discretion, and blessed be you who have prevented me this day from bloodguilt and from delivering myself by my own hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:11; 1 Samuel 25:26; Joshua 10:6"> But ⸤as Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “the life of Yahweh”">am</a></sup>the God of Israel who has prevented me from harming you, if you had not hurried and come to meet me, surely there would not have been ⸤one male⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “one urinating against a wall”">an</a></sup>left alive for Nabal by the light of morning!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 19:21; 1 Samuel 20:42; 2 Kings 5:19"> Then David took from her hand what she had brought for him, and he said to her, “Go up to your house in peace. <span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:28; 1 Samuel 25:34; 1 Samuel 25:22"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">as</a></sup>in the morning when the wine had gone out of Nabal, his wife told him these words. Then his heart died ⸤within him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “in his midst”">at</a></sup>and he became like a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:10; 2 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 6:9"> ⸤And then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">au</a></sup>about ten days later, Yahweh struck Nabal and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; 1 Samuel 25:34; Proverbs 22:23"> When David heard that Nabal had died, he said, “Blessed be Yahweh who has vindicated the case of my reproach from the hand of Nabal, and he has kept back his servant from evil; but Yahweh has returned the evil of Nabal on his own head.” Then David sent and spoke with Abigail to take her for his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 24:51; Genesis 24:37–38"> So the servants of David came to Abigail at Carmel, and they spoke to her, saying, “David has sent us to you to take you for his wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; John 13:3–5; Ruth 2:10"> She got up and bowed down with her face to the ground and said, “Here is your female servant, as a slave to wash the feet of my lord’s servants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 24:61–67; Psalm 45:10–11"> Then Abigail ⸤quickly got up⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “hastened and got up”">av</a></sup>and rode on the donkey, along with five of her maidservants who ⸤attended her⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “the ones going about at her feet”">aw</a></sup>and she went after the messengers of David and became his wife.</span>
</div>
