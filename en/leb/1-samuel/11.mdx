## Saul Defeats the Ammonites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Kings 20:34; Ezekiel 17:13"> Now Nahash the Ammonite went up and encamped against Jabesh Gilead. All the men of Jabesh said to Nahash, “⸤Make a treaty with us⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “Cut with us a covenant”">a</a></sup>and we will serve you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:26; Genesis 34:14; Numbers 16:14"> But Nahash the Ammonite said to them, “On this condition ⸤I will make a treaty⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “I will cut”">b</a></sup>with you, by gouging out the right eye of each of you, so that I can make it a disgrace for all Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So the elders of Jabesh said to him, “Leave us alone for seven days so that we may send messengers in all the territory of Israel, and if there is no deliverer for us, then we will come out to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 2:4; 1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 15:34"> When the messengers came to Gibeah of Saul, ⸤they reported these things to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “they spoke the words in the ears of”">c</a></sup>the people. Then all the people lifted up their voices and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Judges 18:23; Genesis 21:17"> ⸤Just then⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “And look/behold”">d</a></sup>Saul was coming from the field behind the cattle. Saul said, “What is the matter with the people, that they are weeping?” So they recounted to him the words of the men of Jabesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 16:13; Judges 6:34"> Then the Spirit of God rushed upon Saul when he heard these words, and ⸤he became very angry⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “his nose became very hot”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:29; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:5–11"> So he took a yoke of oxen and cut them into pieces and sent them throughout all the territory of Israel by the hand of the messengers, saying, “Whoever is not going out after Saul and after Samuel, so will it be done to his oxen.” Then the fear of Yahweh fell on the people and they went out as one man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 1:4–5; 2 Samuel 24:9; 2 Chronicles 17:12–19"> He mustered them at Bezek; the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>were three hundred thousand, and the men of Judah were thirty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 18:17"> They said to the messengers who had come, “Thus you will say to the men of Jabesh Gilead: ‘Tomorrow deliverance for you will come ⸤when the sun is hot⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “at the heating of the sun”">g</a></sup>When the messengers went and told the men of Jabesh, they rejoiced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:2–3"> The men of Jabesh said, “Tomorrow we will come out to you and you may do to us ⸤whatever seems good to you⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “as all of the good that is in your eyes”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 7:16; James 2:13; Exodus 14:24"> ⸤And the⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and it happened the”">i</a></sup>next day Saul placed the people in three divisions. Then they came into the middle of the camp ⸤at the early morning watch⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “at the night watch of the morning”">j</a></sup>and struck down ⸤the Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “Ammon”">k</a></sup>until the heat of the day. It happened that the remainder were scattered ⸤so that no two among them remained together⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “and they did not remain among them two together”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Luke 19:27; Psalm 21:8"> Then the people said to Samuel, “Who is the one who asked, ‘Will Saul reign over us?’ Give the men to us that we may kill them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; 1 Samuel 19:5; Exodus 14:13"> But Saul said, “No one will be put to death on this day, because ⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the day”">m</a></sup>Yahweh has provided deliverance in Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 7:16; 1 Chronicles 12:38–39"> Then Samuel said to the people, “Come, let us go to Gilgal, and let us renew the kingship there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:8; 1 Samuel 10:17; 1 Chronicles 29:21–24"> So all the people went to Gilgal and they made Saul king there before Yahweh in Gilgal. They sacrificed fellowship offerings there before Yahweh. Then Saul rejoiced there greatly along with all the men of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
