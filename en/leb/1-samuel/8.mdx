## Israel’s Demand for a King

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–19; 1 Timothy 5:21; Nehemiah 7:2"> When Samuel grew old he appointed his sons as judges over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:38; Genesis 22:19; 1 Chronicles 6:28"> The name of his firstborn son was Joel, and the name of his second son was Abijah. They were judges in Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Psalm 15:5; 1 Timothy 6:10"> But his sons did not walk in his ways; they turned aside after gain, they took bribes, and they perverted justice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 2 Samuel 5:3; Exodus 3:16"> So all the elders of Israel gathered together and came to Samuel at Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:14–15; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:6–8"> They said to him, “Look, you are old and your sons do not follow in your ways. So then appoint a king for us to judge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “to rule”">a</a></sup>us, like all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; James 1:5; 1 Samuel 12:17"> But the matter ⸤was displeasing to Samuel⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “was evil in the eyes of Samuel”">b</a></sup>when they said, “Give us a king to judge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “to rule”">c</a></sup>us,” so Samuel prayed to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; 1 Samuel 10:19; Exodus 16:8"> Then Yahweh said to Samuel, “Listen to the voice of the people concerning all that they say to you; for they have not rejected you, but they have rejected me from being king over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> Like all the deeds they have done from the day I brought them up from Egypt until this day, they have forsaken me and have served other gods—so they are doing to you also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:25; 1 Samuel 8:11–18; Ezekiel 46:18"> And so then, listen to their voice. However, you must earnestly warn them; you must explain to them the custom of the king who will rule over them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So Samuel spoke all the words of Yahweh to the people who were requesting a king from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; 1 Samuel 10:25"> He said, “This will be the custom of the king who will reign over you: he will take your sons and appoint them to his chariots and his horsemen, and they will run before his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:7; 1 Kings 4:7; 1 Chronicles 27:1–22"> He will appoint for himself commanders of thousands and commanders of fifties, and those ⸤to do his plowing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to plow his plowing”">d</a></sup>and to reap his harvest, and those to make weapons of war and the equipment for his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He will take your daughters as his perfume makers and as cooks and as bakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:18; 1 Kings 21:7; 1 Kings 21:19"> He will take the best of your fields and your vineyards and your olive trees and will give them to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> He will take a tenth of your seed and your vineyards and give it to his high officials and to his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He will take your male slaves and your female slaves and the best of your young men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="LXX reads “cattle”">e</a></sup>and your donkeys and ⸤will use them for his projects⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “will put them to his work”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He will take a tenth of your flocks,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Hebrew term refers collectively to both sheep and goats (small livestock animals)">g</a></sup>and you yourselves will become his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> So you will cry out on that day because of your king whom you have chosen for yourselves, but Yahweh will not answer you on that day!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Jeremiah 7:13; Isaiah 66:4"> However, the people refused to listen to the voice of Samuel and they said, “No, but there must be a king over us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; Psalm 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> so that we ⸤also⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “even we”">h</a></sup>may be like all the nations, and our king may rule us and go out before us and fight our battles.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:11"> Now when Samuel heard all the words of the people, he repeated them in the ears of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Hosea 13:11"> Then Yahweh said to Samuel, “Listen to their voice, and appoint a king for them.” So Samuel spoke to the men of Israel, “Each of you go to his own town.”</span>
</div>
</div>
