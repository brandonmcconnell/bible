## Revival and Victory at Mizpah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:5–7; 1 Samuel 6:21; Isaiah 52:11"> The men of Kiriath Jearim came and brought up the ark of Yahweh, and they brought it to the house of Abinadab in Gibeah. They consecrated Eleazer his son to guard the ark of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10–11; Jeremiah 3:22–25; Matthew 5:4"> ⸤From⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “And it happened from”">a</a></sup>the day the ark stayed in Kiriath Jearim, ⸤days multiplied and became twenty years⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “and the days were numerous, and they were twenty years”">b</a></sup>while all the house of Israel mourned after Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 13:4; Matthew 4:10"> And Samuel spoke to all the house of Israel, saying, “If with all your heart you are turning to Yahweh, remove the foreign gods and Ashtoreths<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew plural “Ashtaroth” (Ashtoreth was the female consort of Ba’al)">c</a></sup>from your midst. Commit your hearts to Yahweh and serve him alone. Then he will deliver you from the hand of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 14:8; Hosea 14:3; Judges 2:11"> So the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>removed the Baals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew plural “Be’alim” (Ba’al was a major Canaanite deity)">e</a></sup>and the Ashtoreths, and they served Yahweh alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 1 Samuel 12:23; Joshua 15:38"> Then Samuel said, “Gather all Israel to Mizpah, and I will pray to Yahweh for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:14; 1 Samuel 1:15; Judges 10:10"> So they gathered to Mizpah and drew water and poured it out before Yahweh. They fasted on that day and said there, “We have sinned against Yahweh!” So Samuel judged the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>at Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:11; 1 Samuel 13:6; Exodus 14:10"> Now when the Philistines heard that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>had gathered at Mizpah, the rulers of the Philistines went up against Israel. And when the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>heard of it, ⸤they were afraid of the Philistines⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and they were afraid from (= because of) the presence of the Philistines”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 37:4; James 5:16; 1 Samuel 12:19–24"> Then the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>said to Samuel, “⸤You must not cease⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “You must not be silent from us”">k</a></sup>from crying out to Yahweh our God, so that he will deliver us from the hand of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1; Psalm 99:6; 1 Samuel 7:17"> So Samuel took a single ⸤nursing lamb⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “lamb of milk”">l</a></sup>and sacrificed it as a whole burnt offering to Yahweh. Then Samuel cried out to Yahweh on behalf of Israel, and Yahweh answered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; 2 Samuel 22:14–15; Joshua 10:10"> ⸤While⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">m</a></sup>Samuel was sacrificing the burnt offering, the Philistines drew near for the battle against Israel. But Yahweh thundered against the Philistines with a great noise on that day and threw them into confusion so that they were defeated before Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the men of Israel went out from Mizpah and pursued the Philistines and they struck them down as far as below Beth Car.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Joshua 4:9; Acts 26:22"> So Samuel took a single stone and put it between Mizpah and Shen, and he named it Ebenezer and said, “Up to here Yahweh has helped us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 13:1; 1 Samuel 17:49–53; 1 Samuel 14:6–16"> So the Philistines were subdued and they did not come into the territory of Israel again, and the hand of Yahweh was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 106:34; Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 7:16"> The towns<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “cities”">n</a></sup>which the Philistines had taken from Israel were returned to Israel from Ekron to Gath, and Israel delivered their territories from the hand of the Philistines. Then there was peace between Israel and the Amorites.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Circuit Ministry of Samuel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:6; Acts 13:20–21; Judges 2:16"> Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:4; Judges 12:14; Judges 5:10"> He used to go on the circuit ⸤from year to year⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “from what is required year in year”">o</a></sup>He went around Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah, and he judged Israel in all these places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:19; 1 Samuel 8:4; 1 Samuel 1:1"> Then ⸤he returned⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and his return was ”">p</a></sup>to Ramah, because his house was there, and there he judged Israel, and he built an altar to Yahweh there.</span>
</div>
