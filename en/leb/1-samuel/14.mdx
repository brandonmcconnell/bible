<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:15–16; Isaiah 10:28–29"> Now Saul was staying at the outskirts of Gibeah under the pomegranate tree that was in Migron, and the troops that were with him were about six hundred men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:20; 1 Samuel 2:28; 1 Samuel 4:21"> Now Ahijah, the son of Ahitub (the brother of Ichabod), the son of Phinehas, the son of Eli the priest of Yahweh at Shiloh, was carrying an ephod. The troops did not know that Jonathan had gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23"> Now between the passes where Jonathan sought to go over to the garrison of the Philistines there was a crag of rock ⸤on one side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “from the beyond from this”">c</a></sup>and a crag of rock ⸤on the other⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from the beyond from this”">d</a></sup>The name of the one was Bozez and the name of the other was Seneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The one crag on the north was opposite Micmash and the other on the south was opposite Geba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Romans 8:31; Zechariah 4:6"> So Jonathan said to ⸤his armor bearer⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the young man carrying his weapons”">e</a></sup>“Come, let us go over to the garrisons of these uncircumcised; perhaps Yahweh will act for us, for there is no hindrance for Yahweh to save by many or by few.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:3; Zechariah 8:23; 1 Samuel 10:7"> And ⸤his armor bearer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the one carrying his weapons”">f</a></sup>said, “Do all that is in your heart ⸤that you are inclined to do⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “turn/incline for yourself”">g</a></sup>⸤I am with you all of the way⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “Behold, I am with you according to your heart”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 7:9–14"> Then Jonathan said, “Look, we are about to go over to the men; and we will show ourselves to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 6:36–40; Genesis 24:13–14"> If they say to us: ‘Wait until we reach you,’ ⸤then we will stand as we are⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and we will stand under us”">i</a></sup>and not go up to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Samuel 10:7; Judges 7:11"> But if they say, ‘Come up to us,’ then we will go up, for Yahweh has given them into our hand, and this will be the sign for us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; 1 Samuel 14:22; Judges 6:2"> So the two of them showed themselves to the garrison of the Philistines, and the Philistines said, “The Hebrews are coming out from the holes in which they have hidden themselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:43–44; 2 Samuel 5:24; Judges 7:15"> Then the men of the garrison answered Jonathan and ⸤his armor bearer⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the one carrying his weapons”">j</a></sup>“Come up to us and we will show you something!” Then Jonathan said to ⸤his armor bearer⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the one carrying his weapons”">k</a></sup>“Come up after me, for Yahweh has given them into the hand of Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10; Leviticus 26:7–8"> So Jonathan went up on his hands and his feet, with ⸤his armor bearer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the one carrying his weapons”">l</a></sup>after him. They fell before Jonathan and then ⸤his armor bearer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the one carrying his weapons”">m</a></sup>would kill them after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So was the first attack in which Jonathan and ⸤his armor bearer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the one carrying his weapons”">n</a></sup>killed about twenty men within about half of a furrow in an acre of an open field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:17; Genesis 35:5; 2 Samuel 5:24"> Then there was terror<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “panic”">o</a></sup>in the camp, in the open field, and among all the army of the garrison. Even the ⸤raiders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “destroyers”">p</a></sup>trembled. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:22–25; Isaiah 19:2; Judges 7:22"> And the lookouts of Saul in Gibeah of Benjamin saw ⸤that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “and look”">r</a></sup>the multitude ⸤surged back and forth⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “waved, going here and there”">s</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:7–8; 1 Samuel 5:2; Judges 20:23"> Then Saul said to Ahijah, “Bring near the ark of God”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="LXX reads “bring near the ephod”">v</a></sup>(for the ark of God was ⸤at that time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “on that day”">w</a></sup>with the ⸤Israelites⸥).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">x</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 2 Chronicles 20:23; 1 Samuel 14:16"> Then Saul and all the troops who were with him were assembled on command and came up to the battle, and look! Each Philistine’s sword was against his friend; and there was a very great confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:4; Judges 7:23"> The Hebrews who had been for the Philistines ⸤previously⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “as yesterday three days ago”">aa</a></sup>who had gone up with them into the camp all around, even they ⸤joined the Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “were with Israel”">ab</a></sup>who were with Saul and Jonathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:6; 1 Samuel 31:7"> All the men of Israel who had hidden themselves in the hill country of Ephraim heard that the Philistines had fled, so even they pursued them closely in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 14:30; 1 Samuel 13:5; Psalm 44:6–8"> So on that day Yahweh delivered Israel, and the battle shifted to Beth Aven.</span>

## Saul’s Oath Leads to Trouble

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Romans 10:2; Judges 1:28"> Now the men of Israel were hard pressed on that day, because Saul had made the army take an oath, saying, “Cursed be the man who eats any food until evening, when I will have avenged myself on my enemies!” So none of the army tasted any food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Numbers 13:27; Deuteronomy 9:28"> (Now all the people of the land used to go into the forest, for there was honey on the surface of the ground.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:2"> When the army came to the forest, look! There was honey flowing, but no one put his hand to his mouth, for the army was afraid of the solemn oath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:29; 1 Samuel 14:43; 1 Samuel 30:12"> However, Jonathan had not heard about the oath of his father with the army, so he extended the end of the staff which was in his hand, and he dipped it into the honeycomb.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Hebrew “the honeycomb of the honey”">ac</a></sup>Then he put his hand to his mouth and his eyes gleamed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="The Masoretic Hebrew text (Kethib ) reads “saw”; the reading tradition (Qere) reads “gleamed”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:43; 1 Samuel 14:24"> Then a man from the army informed him and said, “Your father made the army swear a solemn oath, saying, ‘Cursed be the man who eats food today,’ ” so the army is exhausted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 18:18; Joshua 7:25"> Then Jonathan said, “My father has brought trouble on the land! See now that my eyes have brightened because I have tasted a little of this honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:18"> ⸤How much more could have been done⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “even that if”">ae</a></sup>if the troops had eaten freely today from the plunder of their enemies that they had found! For now the loss among the Philistines is not great.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 10:12; Joshua 19:42"> They defeated the Philistines that day from Micmash to Aijalon, and the troops were very weary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:19; Leviticus 3:17; Leviticus 17:10–14"> Then the troops took the plunder: they took sheep and cattle and ⸤calves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “the children of cattle”">af</a></sup>and slaughtered them on the ground and the troops ate them all with the blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Romans 2:1; Matthew 7:5"> So they reported it to Saul, saying, “Look! The troops are sinning against Yahweh by eating the animals with the blood!” And he said, “You have dealt treacherously! Roll to me a large stone ⸤today⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “the day”">ag</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Then Saul said, “Disperse yourselves among the troops and say to them, ‘Bring to me each one his ox and each his sheep and slaughter them in this place and eat, but do not sin against Yahweh by eating the animals with the blood.’ ” So all the troops brought them, each leading his ox in his hand that night, and slaughtered it there.</span>
</div>

## Jonathan Rescued from His Father Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; 1 Samuel 7:12; 2 Timothy 3:5"> Then Saul built an altar to Yahweh; ⸤it was the first altar he built⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “with it he began to build an altar”">ah</a></sup>to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1–2; Psalm 73:28; Malachi 2:7"> Saul said, “Let us go down after the Philistines by night, and let us plunder them until the morning light, and let us not leave alive a man among them.” So they said, “Do all that is good in your eyes.” But the priest said, “Let us draw near to God here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:7–8; 1 Samuel 23:9–12"> So Saul inquired of God, “Should I go down after the Philistines? Will you give them into the hand of Israel?” But he did not answer him on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Judges 20:2; Joshua 7:14–26; Numbers 24:17"> Then Saul said, “Come here, all you leaders of the people, ⸤so that we find out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “and know and see”">ai</a></sup>what the sin was this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:44; 2 Samuel 12:5; 1 Samuel 14:24"> For as Yahweh lives, who delivers Israel, I swear that even if it is in Jonathan my son, ⸤he will certainly die⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “surely dying he will die”">aj</a></sup>But nobody from all the army answered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:15; 1 Samuel 14:7; 1 Samuel 14:36"> Then he said to all Israel, “You will be ⸤on one side⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “opposite one”">ak</a></sup>and I and my son Jonathan will be ⸤on the other⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “opposite one”">al</a></sup>And the army said to Saul, “Do what is good in your eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Acts 1:24; Joshua 7:16–18; 1 Samuel 10:20–21"> Then Saul said to Yahweh the God of Israel, “⸤Render a decision perfectly⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “give what is perfect”; the LXX includes additional words not found in the Masoretic Hebrew text but included in a number of modern English versions: “If this guilt is on me or in my son Jonathan, give Urim, but if this guilt is in your people Israel, give Thummim”">am</a></sup>Jonathan and Saul were chosen by lot and the people went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then Saul said, “Let them cast the lot between me and my son Jonathan,” and Jonathan was chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; 1 Samuel 14:27; Jonah 1:7–10"> So Saul said, “Tell me what you have done.” So Jonathan told him and said, “I ⸤merely tasted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “tasted I tasted”">an</a></sup>a little honey with the end of the staff that was in my hand. Here I am, I must die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:39; Ruth 1:17; 1 Samuel 25:22"> Then Saul said, “So may God do to me and ⸤more⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “so may he add”">ao</a></sup>you will certainly die today, Jonathan!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 21:18; Acts 27:34; 2 Samuel 14:11"> But the army said to Saul, “Must Jonathan die, who accomplished this great victory in Israel? Far from it! As Yahweh lives, not a hair from his head will fall to the ground, for he has worked with God this day.” So the army ransomed Jonathan and he did not die.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Saul went up from pursuing the Philistines, and the Philistines went to their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:6; 1 Samuel 14:52; 2 Samuel 8:3"> So Saul took the kingship over Israel, and he fought all around against his enemies, against Moab, against the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Ammon”">ap</a></sup>against Edom, against the kings of Zobah, and against the Philistines. <span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; 1 Samuel 15:3–7; Deuteronomy 25:19"> He acted bravely and defeated the Amalekites and rescued Israel from the hand of those who plundered it.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:33; 1 Samuel 31:2; 2 Samuel 6:20–23"> Now the sons of Saul were Jonathan, Ishvi, and Malki-Shua; the names of his two daughters were as follows: the name of the firstborn was Merab and the younger was Michal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 3:27"> The name of Saul’s wife was Ahinoam the daughter of Ahimaaz, and the name of the commander of his army was Abner, the son of Ner, Saul’s uncle.</span>
