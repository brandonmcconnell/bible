## Jonathan Intercedes for David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:1; 1 Samuel 18:9"> Now Saul spoke to Jonathan his son and to all his servants about killing David, but Saul’s son Jonathan ⸤liked David very much⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “delighted in David very”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; Acts 9:24; 1 Samuel 18:1–3"> So Jonathan informed David, saying, “My father Saul is trying to kill you; now please take care! In the morning you should stay in the hiding place and conceal yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:9; 1 Samuel 20:13"> I will go out and stand ⸤at my father’s side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “at the hand of my father”">b</a></sup>in the field where you are, and I will speak about you to my father; ⸤if I find out anything⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “if I see and what?”">c</a></sup>I will tell it to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:20; Proverbs 31:8–9; Genesis 42:22"> So Jonathan spoke well about David to his father Saul and said to him, “The king should not sin against his servant David, because he has not sinned against you, and because his service for you has been very good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:13; 1 Chronicles 11:14; Judges 9:17"> He put his life in his hand and attacked the Philistine, and Yahweh brought about a great victory for all of Israel, and you saw it and rejoiced! Now why should you sin against innocent blood by killing David without cause?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–25; Jeremiah 5:2; Psalm 15:4"> And Saul listened to the voice of Jonathan and swore, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the life of Yahweh”">d</a></sup>he will not be put to death!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:2; 1 Samuel 16:21; 1 Samuel 18:13"> Jonathan called to David and told him all of these words. Then Jonathan brought David to Saul and he was before him as ⸤formerly⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “yesterday three days ago”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## David Has to Flee Again

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 27:3; Psalm 18:32–50"> War came again, so David went out and fought against the Philistines and ⸤defeated them thoroughly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “he struck them down with a great blow”">f</a></sup>so that they fled before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:14; 1 Samuel 18:10–11; 1 Samuel 16:16"> Then the evil spirit from Yahweh came upon Saul while he was sitting in his house with his spear in his hand. And David was playing a stringed instrument in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:33; 1 Samuel 18:11; Proverbs 21:30"> So Saul tried ⸤to pin David to the wall with the spear⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to strike with the spear on David and to the wall”">g</a></sup>but ⸤he eluded Saul⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “he escaped from the presence of Saul”">h</a></sup>so that he struck the spear into the wall, and David fled and escaped that same night.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 59:1–17; Judges 16:2"> Then Saul sent messengers to David’s house to guard him and to kill him in the morning, but Michal his wife told David, saying, “If ⸤you do not save your life⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “you are not saving your life”">i</a></sup>tonight, then tomorrow you will be killed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 2:15; Acts 9:24–25; Psalm 34:19"> So Michal lowered David through the window, and he went and fled and escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 31:19; Judges 18:17; Judges 18:14"> Then Michal took the household god<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew teraphim">j</a></sup>and put it on the bed and put a quilt of goat’s hair at its head and covered it with the clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:5; 2 Samuel 16:17–19; 2 Samuel 17:20"> And Saul sent messengers to arrest David, but she said, “He is ill.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3–4; Psalm 37:12; Job 31:31"> So Saul sent the messengers to see David, saying, “Bring him up to me in the bed, so that I can kill him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When the messengers came, ⸤to their surprise⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “and look”">k</a></sup>the idol was on the bed with the quilt of goat’s hair at the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:22; 1 Samuel 22:17; Exodus 1:17–19"> Then Saul said to Michal, “Why have you deceived me like this and sent away my enemy, so that he escaped?” Michal said to Saul, “He said to me, ‘Let me go, why should I kill you?’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:17; Psalm 116:11; 1 Samuel 15:34"> So David fled and escaped, and he came to Samuel at Ramah and told him all that Saul had done to him. Then he and Samuel went and stayed in Naioth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; 1 Samuel 22:9–10; 1 Samuel 26:1"> And it was told to Saul, “David is in Naioth in Ramah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joel 2:28; John 7:32; 1 Samuel 10:5–6"> So Saul sent messengers to capture David. When they saw the company of the prophets prophesying and Samuel standing as chief over them, then the Spirit of God came upon Saul’s messengers, and they also prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 27:22; Jeremiah 13:23; Joel 2:28"> So they told Saul, and he sent other messengers, and they also prophesied. Again Saul sent messengers a third time, and they also prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then he also went to Ramah. When he came to the great cistern which was in Secu, he asked and said, “Where are Samuel and David?” Someone said, “Look they are in Naioth in Ramah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 16:9; 1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:20"> So he went there to Naioth in Ramah and the Spirit of God came upon him also, and ⸤he walked along prophesying⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “he went going and he prophesied”">l</a></sup>until he came to Naioth in Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20; Isaiah 20:2; Micah 1:8"> He also stripped off his clothes and prophesied before Samuel. He lay naked all that day and all night. Therefore they say, “Is Saul also among the prophets?”</span>
</div>
