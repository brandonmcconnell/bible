## Saul Inquires of the Medium at Endor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 25:1; Leviticus 20:27"> (Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had mourned for him, and they had buried him in Ramah, his own city. And Saul had expelled the mediums<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “necromancers”">c</a></sup>and the soothsayers from the land.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Joshua 19:18; 2 Kings 4:8"> Then the Philistines assembled and came and encamped at Shunem, so Saul assembled all Israel, and they encamped at Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 57:20–21; Daniel 5:6; Isaiah 21:3–4"> When Saul saw the army of the Philistines, he was afraid and his heart trembled greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 12:6; 1 Samuel 14:37; Lamentations 2:9"> And Saul inquired of Yahweh, but Yahweh did not answer him, not by dreams or by the Urim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A device for revealing God’s decisions; see Exod 28:30; Num 27:21; Neh 7:65; Prov 16:33">d</a></sup>or by the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 16:16; Joshua 17:11; 1 Chronicles 10:13"> So Saul said to his servants, “Search for me ⸤a woman who is a medium⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a woman of an owner of a spirit of the dead” (= “a woman who owns a ritual pit for summoning spirits of the dead”)">e</a></sup>so that I may go to her and inquire of her.” His servants said to him, “Look there is a woman who is a medium in Endor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; 1 Chronicles 10:13; 1 Kings 22:30"> So Saul disguised himself and put on other clothes, and he went ⸤with two of his men⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “and two of his men with him”">f</a></sup>And they came to the woman by night and he said, “Please consult a spirit for me through ⸤the ritual pit⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the spirit of the dead”">g</a></sup>and bring up for me the one whom I tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:3; 2 Kings 5:7; 2 Samuel 18:13"> But the woman said to him, “Look, you know what Saul did, how he exterminated the mediums<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “necromancers”">h</a></sup>and the soothsayers from the land! Why are you setting a trap for my life to kill me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:11; 1 Samuel 19:6; Deuteronomy 18:10–12"> Then Saul swore to her by Yahweh, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">i</a></sup>⸤you will not be punished⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “guilt will not meet you”">j</a></sup>for this thing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So the woman asked, “Whom shall I bring up for you?” He said, “Bring up Samuel for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:3; 1 Kings 14:5"> When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out with a loud voice, and the woman said to Saul, “Why did you deceive me? You are Saul!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 82:6–7; John 10:34–35; Exodus 22:28"> The king said to her, “Do not be afraid! What do you see?” And the woman said to Saul, “I see a god<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “a divine being”">k</a></sup>coming up from the ground!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:27; 2 Kings 2:8; 1 Samuel 24:8"> Then he said to her, “What is his appearance?” She said, “An old man is coming up, and ⸤he is wrapped in a robe⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “he is wrapping himself with a robe”">l</a></sup>Then Saul realized that it was Samuel, and he knelt with his face to the ground and bowed down.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 16:23–26; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 18:12"> Then Samuel said to Saul, “Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?” And Saul said, “⸤I am in distress⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “Great distress is for me”">m</a></sup>For the Philistines are about to make war against me, but God has turned away from me, and he does not answer me any more, not ⸤by the prophets⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “by the hand of the prophets”">n</a></sup>or by the dreams. So I called to you to let me know what I should do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 6:27; Lamentations 2:5; Psalm 68:1–3"> Then Samuel said, “Why do you ask me, since Yahweh has turned away from you and has become your enemy?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:27–29; 1 Samuel 16:13; 1 Samuel 13:13–14"> Yahweh has done to you just as he spoke by my hand! Yahweh has torn the kingdom from your hand and has given it to your neighbor, to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:9; 1 Kings 20:42; Psalm 50:21–22"> Because you ⸤did not obey⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “did not listen to”">o</a></sup>Yahweh and did not carry out the fierce anger of his ⸤wrath⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “face”">p</a></sup>against Amalek, therefore Yahweh has done this thing to you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:5; Jeremiah 28:16–17; 1 Kings 22:20"> And Yahweh will also give Israel with you into the hands of the Philistines, and tomorrow you and your sons will be with me, and Yahweh will also give the army of Israel into the hand of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:5; Job 26:2; Job 15:20–24"> ⸤Then Saul immediately fell prostrate⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “Saul did at once and fell with the fullness of his height”">q</a></sup>to the ground, and he was very afraid because of the words of Samuel; there was no more strength in him, for he had not eaten food all day and all night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 12:3; Job 13:14; 1 Samuel 19:5"> Then the woman came to Saul and realized that he was absolutely<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “very”">r</a></sup>terrified, so she said to him, “Look, your female servant ⸤has obeyed you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “has listened to your voice”">s</a></sup>and I have ⸤risked my life⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “I have put my life in my palm”">t</a></sup>I have listened to your words that you have spoken to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So then, you also please listen to the voice of your female servant, and let me set before you a morsel of bread, and you eat so that ⸤you will have strength⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “it will be your strength”">u</a></sup>in you when you go on your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">v</a></sup>way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 21:4; Luke 24:29; Luke 14:23"> But he refused and said, “I will not eat!” However, his servants urged him, and the woman also. So he listened ⸤to what they said⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “their voice”">w</a></sup>and he got up from the ground and sat on the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 15:23; Genesis 18:7–8"> Now the woman had a fattened bull calf in the house, ⸤so she quickly slaughtered it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “and she did quickly and she slaughtered it”">x</a></sup>and took flour, kneaded dough, and baked him some unleavened bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> She brought it before Saul and his servants, and they ate. Then they got up and went away that very night.</span>
</div>
