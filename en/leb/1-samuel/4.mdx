## The Battle of Aphek

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:1; 1 Samuel 7:12; 1 Samuel 5:1"> And the word of Samuel came to all Israel. Now Israel went out to meet the Philistines for battle, and they encamped at Ebenezer, and the Philistines encamped at Aphek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 Samuel 17:8; Joshua 7:5–8"> The Philistines lined up for the battle to meet Israel, and the battle was prolonged<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “drawn out”">a</a></sup>until Israel was defeated before the Philistines, ⸤who⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “and they”">b</a></sup>killed about four thousand men ⸤on the battlefield⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “in the battle line in the field”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:5; Numbers 10:35; 1 Corinthians 10:1–5"> When the army<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “people”">d</a></sup>came back to the camp, the elders of Israel asked, “Why has Yahweh defeated us today before the Philistines? Let us bring the ark of the covenant of Yahweh to us from Shiloh so that it may come into our midst and deliver<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “save”">e</a></sup>us from the hand of our enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:2; Numbers 7:89; Psalm 80:1"> So the army sent to Shiloh and brought from there the ark of the covenant of Yahweh of hosts who sits between the cherubim. And the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were there with the ark of the covenant of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:5; Job 20:5; Amos 6:3"> Now when the ark of the covenant of Yahweh arrived at the camp, all Israel ⸤let out a loud shout⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “shouted a great shout”">f</a></sup>so that the earth shook.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 32:17–18"> When the Philistines heard the noise of the shouting, they said, “What is the noise of this loud shouting in the camp of the Hebrews?” Then they learned that the ark of Yahweh had come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Exodus 14:25; Exodus 15:14–16"> So the Philistines were afraid, for they said, “God has come<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “gods have come”">g</a></sup>to the camp.” And they said, “Woe to us, ⸤for this has never happened before!⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “it has not happened like this since yesterday and since the day before that”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 9:14; Exodus 7:5; Psalm 78:43–51"> Woe to us! Who can deliver us from the hand of these mighty gods? These are the same gods who struck the Egyptians with all sorts of plagues in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:13; Judges 13:1; 2 Samuel 10:12"> Take courage and be men, you Philistines, lest you end up serving the Hebrews just like they have served you. Be men and fight!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; 2 Kings 14:12; Deuteronomy 28:25"> So the Philistines fought and Israel was defeated and each man fled to his tent, for the slaughter was very great. Thirty thousand foot soldiers from Israel fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:34; Psalm 78:64; 1 Samuel 2:32"> Furthermore, the ark of God was captured,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “taken”">i</a></sup>and the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Nehemiah 9:1; 2 Samuel 15:32"> A man of Benjamin ran from the battle line and came to Shiloh that same day, and his clothes were torn and earth was on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:9; 1 Samuel 4:18; Joshua 7:9"> When he came, ⸤there was⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “look”">j</a></sup>Eli sitting on his chair ⸤by the side of the road⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “by the hand of the road”">k</a></sup>watching, because his heart was anxious about the ark of God. Now the man had come ⸤to give his report⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to tell”">l</a></sup>in the city, and all the city cried out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When Eli heard the noise of the outcry, he said, “What is the noise of this commotion?” Then the man ⸤came quickly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “hastened and came”">m</a></sup>and told Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:2; Psalm 90:10; 1 Kings 14:4"> Now Eli was ⸤ninety-eight years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “a son of ninety and eight years”">n</a></sup>and his eyes ⸤stayed fixed ahead⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “were standing”">o</a></sup>and he was not able to see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:4; 1 Samuel 3:6; Joshua 7:19"> And the man said to Eli, “I am the one who has come from the battle line! I have fled today from the battle line!” And he said, “⸤What exactly happened⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “what is the thing/matter”">p</a></sup>my son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:11; 1 Samuel 4:10–11"> Then the messenger answered and said, “Israel has fled before the Philistines. There has been a great defeat among the troops. Also, your two sons have died, Hophni and Phinehas, and the ark of God has been captured.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:21–22; 1 Peter 4:17–18; Psalm 69:9"> Just as he mentioned the ark of God, he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="That is, Eli">q</a></sup>fell from his chair backwards against the side of the gate. He broke his neck and died, because the man was old and heavy. He had judged Israel forty years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Now his daughter-in-law, the wife of Phinehas, was pregnant and about to give birth. When she heard the news concerning the capture of the ark of God and that her father-in-law and her husband had died, she ⸤went into labor⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “bowed down”">r</a></sup>and gave birth, because her labor pains came upon her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:17–18; Psalm 77:2; John 16:21"> Just before the time of her death, those ⸤attending her⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “standing over her”">s</a></sup>said, “Do not fear, for you have given birth to a son.” But she did not answer, or ⸤pay any attention⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “set her heart”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 26:8; Jeremiah 2:11; Psalm 78:61"> She called the boy Ichabod,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="The name “Ichabod” means “no glory”">u</a></sup>saying, “The glory has departed from Israel,” referring to the capture of the ark of God and concerning her father-in-law and husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 137:5–6; John 2:17"> And she said, “The glory has departed from Israel, because the ark of God was captured.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Or “taken”">v</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
