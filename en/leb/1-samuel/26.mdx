## David Again Spares Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; Psalm 54:1–7; Joshua 15:24"> The Ziphites came to Saul at Gibeah, saying, “Is not David hiding on the hill of Hakilah opposite Jeshimon?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; Psalm 140:4–9"> Then Saul got up and went down to the wilderness of Ziph, and three thousand chosen men of Israel with him, to seek David in the wilderness of Ziph.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:19; 1 Samuel 26:1"> Now Saul was on the hill of Hakilah, which is opposite Jeshimon by the road, but David was staying in the wilderness. When he realized that Saul had come to the wilderness after him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; Joshua 2:1"> David sent spies, and he learned that Saul had come ⸤for certain⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to be established”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:55; 1 Samuel 17:20; 1 Samuel 14:50–51"> Then David got up and came to the place where Saul had encamped, and David saw the place where Saul was lying down, ⸤as well as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “and”">b</a></sup>Abner the son of Ner, the commander of his army. (Now Saul was lying in the encampment, and the army was encamping around him.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 7:10–11; 2 Samuel 2:18; Genesis 15:20"> David answered and said to Ahimelech the Hittite and to Abishai the son of Zeruiah the brother of Joab, saying, “Who will go down with me to Saul, in the camp?” And Abishai said, “I will go down with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3"> So David and Abishai came to the army by night, and ⸤there was⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “look” or “behold”">c</a></sup>Saul lying asleep in the encampment with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">d</a></sup>his spear thrust into the ground near his head, and Abner and the army were lying all around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:23; Deuteronomy 32:30"> Then Abishai said to David, “God has handed over your enemy into your hand today! So then, ⸤please let me pin him to the ground with the spear⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “let me strike him please with the spear and in the ground”">e</a></sup>⸤one time⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “one occurrence”">f</a></sup>and ⸤I will not strike him twice⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “I will not repeat for him”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:14; Psalm 105:15; 2 Samuel 1:16"> But David said to Abishai, “Do not destroy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “kill”">h</a></sup>him! For who has stretched out his hand against Yahweh’s anointed one and remained blameless?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Deuteronomy 31:14; Psalm 37:13"> And David said, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">i</a></sup>⸤certainly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “if surely”">j</a></sup>Yahweh will strike him, or his day will come and he will die, or he will go down in the battle and ⸤perish⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “will be carried away”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:6; 1 Samuel 24:12; 2 Samuel 1:14"> ⸤Yahweh forbid me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “Far be it from me from Yahweh”">l</a></sup>from stretching out my hand against Yahweh’s anointed one! So then, please take the spear that is near his head and the jar of water, and let us go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Genesis 15:12; Isaiah 29:10"> So David took the spear and the jar of water from near Saul’s head, and they went away. ⸤No one saw, no one knew, and no one awakened⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “there was not anyone seeing and there was not anyone knowing and there was not anyone awakening”">m</a></sup>for all of them were sleeping because a deep sleep of Yahweh had fallen upon them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:8; Judges 9:7"> Then David went to the other side and stood on the top of the hill at a distance; the distance was great between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> David called out to the army and to Abner the son of Ner, “Will you not answer, Abner?” And Abner answered and said, “Who are you that you call to the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So David said to Abner, “Are you not a man? And who is like you in Israel? Why did you not keep watch over your lord the king? For one of the people came to destroy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “to kill”">n</a></sup>your lord the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 79:11; 2 Samuel 12:5; 1 Samuel 26:9"> This thing that you have done is not good. ⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the life of Yahweh”">o</a></sup>⸤surely you people deserve to die⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “you are sons of death”">p</a></sup>since you have not kept watch over your lord, over Yahweh’s anointed one! So then, see where the king’s spear is and the jar of water that was near his head!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:16; 1 Samuel 24:8"> Then Saul recognized David’s voice and said, “Is this your voice, my son David?” And David said, “It is my voice, my lord the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:9; 1 Samuel 24:11–14; John 10:32"> Then he said, “Why is my lord pursuing after his servant? For what have I done? And what evil is in my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:16; 2 Samuel 16:11; 2 Samuel 20:19"> And so then, please let my lord the king listen to the words of his servant: If Yahweh has incited you against me, may he delight in an offering; but if ⸤it is mortals⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “it is the sons of humankind”">q</a></sup>may they be accursed ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">r</a></sup>Yahweh, for they have driven me away today from sharing in the inheritance of Yahweh, saying, ‘Go, serve other gods!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:14; Matthew 26:55; 1 Samuel 25:29"> And so then, do not let my blood fall to the ground ⸤away from⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “from before”">s</a></sup>the presence of Yahweh, for the king of Israel has gone out to seek a single flea, as one hunts a partridge in the mountains.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:24; 1 Samuel 24:17; Exodus 9:27"> Then Saul said, “I have sinned! Come back, David my son, for I will not harm you again, because my life was precious in your eyes this day. Look, I have acted like a fool and ⸤have made a terrible mistake⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “have gone greatly astray”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> David answered and said, “Here is the king’s spear; let one of the young men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “servants”">u</a></sup>come over and take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 62:12; 1 Samuel 24:6–7; Psalm 18:20–26"> Yahweh repays to each one his righteousness and his faithfulness, for Yahweh gave you into my hand today, but I was not willing to stretch out my hand against Yahweh’s anointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; Psalm 54:7"> Look, as your life was precious in my eyes this day, may my life be great in the eyes of Yahweh, and may he rescue me from all trouble.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 24:9–10; Proverbs 26:25; 1 Samuel 24:19"> Then Saul said to David, “Blessed are you, my son David; ⸤you will not only do many things, but also will always succeed⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “you will do many things and also always you will prevail”">v</a></sup>Then David went on his way and Saul returned to his place.</span>
</div>
</div>
