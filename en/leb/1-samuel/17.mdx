## The Philistine Champion Taunts Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:19"> Then Saul and the men of Israel were gathered and encamped in the valley of Elah, and they formed ranks for the battle to meet the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The Philistines were standing on the hill on one side and the army of Israel was standing on the hill on the other side with the valley between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:22; 2 Samuel 21:16–22; 1 Chronicles 20:4–8"> Then ⸤a champion⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “a man of the space between”">b</a></sup>went out from the camps of the Philistines, whose name was Goliath from Gath. <span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 17:41; 1 Chronicles 11:23"> The shaft of his spear was like a weaver’s beam and the point of his spear weighed six hundred iron shekels. ⸤His shield bearer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “The one who carried his shield”">f</a></sup>was walking in front of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 21:3; 1 Samuel 17:26"> He stood and called to the battle lines of Israel and said to them, “Why have you come out to form ranks for battle? Am I not the Philistine, and you the servants of Saul? Commission for yourselves a man and let him come down to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1"> If he is able to fight with me and he defeats me, then we will be your servants; but if I prevail over him and defeat him, then you will be our servants and you will serve us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:25–26; 1 Samuel 17:45; 1 Samuel 17:36"> Then the Philistine said, “I hereby defy the battle lines of Israel today! Give me a man so that we may fight each other!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Deuteronomy 31:8; Proverbs 28:1"> When Saul and all Israel heard these words of the Philistine, they were dismayed and very afraid.</span>
</div>

<div className="bt">

## David Appears before Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29"> Now the words which David had spoken were heard and they reported them ⸤to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “before the face/in the presence of”">ac</a></sup>Saul, and he summoned him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:18; Deuteronomy 20:1–3; Psalm 3:6"> David said to Saul, “Do not let anyone’s heart fail concerning him! Your servant will go and fight with this Philistine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 13:31; Deuteronomy 9:2; Revelation 13:4"> But Saul said to David, “You will not be able to go against this Philistine to fight with him, because you are only a boy, whereas he has been a man of war since his childhood!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And David said to Saul, “Your servant has been a shepherd of the flock for his father. If the lion or the bear would come and carry off a sheep from the group,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Amos 3:12; 2 Timothy 4:17–18; Daniel 6:22"> I would go out after it and strike it down and rescue the sheep from its mouth. If it rose against me, I would grab it by its beard and strike it down and kill it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; Acts 5:38–39; Romans 2:28–29"> Your servant has struck down both the lion and the bear, and this uncircumcised Philistine will be like one of them, because he defied the battle lines of the living God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:17–18; Psalm 138:7–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And David said, “Yahweh, who rescued me from the hand of the lion and from the hand of the bear, will rescue me from the hand of this Philistine!” Then Saul said to David, “Go and may Yahweh be with you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Then Saul clothed David with his own fighting attire and put a helmet of bronze on his head and clothed him with body armor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 1:7"> Then David strapped on his sword over his fighting attire, but he tried in vain to walk around, for he was not trained to use them. So David said to Saul, “I am not able to walk with these, because I am not trained to use them.” So David removed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Matthew 10:10; Judges 15:15–16"> Then he took his staff in his hand, picked out for himself five smooth stones from the wadi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="A valley that is usually dry but contains a stream during the rainy season">ad</a></sup>and he put them in his shepherd’s bag, in the pouch. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Then the Philistine ⸤came on, getting nearer and nearer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “went going and near”">ae</a></sup>to David, with ⸤his shield bearer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “the man carrying the shield”">af</a></sup>in front of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:12; 1 Samuel 17:33; Psalm 123:3–4"> When the Philistine looked and saw David, he despised him, for he was only a boy and ruddy with a handsome appearance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:8; 2 Samuel 3:8; 1 Samuel 24:14"> So the Philistine said to David, “Am I a dog, that you are coming to me with sticks?” Then the Philistine cursed David by his gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:9–10; Proverbs 18:12; 1 Kings 20:10–11"> The Philistine said to David, “Come to me so that I can give your flesh to the birds of heaven and to the wild animals of the field!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; 2 Chronicles 32:8; 2 Corinthians 3:5"> Then David said to the Philistine, “You are coming to me with a sword and with a spear and with a javelin, but I am coming to you in the name of Yahweh of hosts, the God of the battle lines of Israel, whom you have defied!</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Joshua 4:24; 2 Kings 19:19"> This day Yahweh will deliver you into my hand, and I will strike you down ⸤and cut off your head⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “and I will remove your head from upon you”">ag</a></sup>Then I will give the corpses of the army of the Philistines this day to the birds of heaven and to the animals of the earth, so that all the earth may know that there is a God who is for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalm 44:6–7; Zechariah 4:6; Hosea 1:7"> And all of this assembly will know that Yahweh does not rescue with sword or with spear, for the battle belongs to Yahweh, and he will give you into our hands!”</span>
</div>

## David Defeats Goliath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 28:1; Psalm 27:1"> ⸤When⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “And it happened that when”">ah</a></sup>the Philistine got up and came and drew near to meet David, David ⸤ran quickly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “made quickly and he ran”">ai</a></sup>to the battle line to meet the Philistine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–28; 2 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 22:34"> Then David put his hand into the bag and took a stone from it and slung it. He struck the Philistine on his forehead, and the stone sank into his forehead, and he fell on his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Judges 15:15; 1 Samuel 17:39; 1 Samuel 23:21"> So David prevailed over the Philistine with the sling and with the stone, and he struck down the Philistine and killed him, but there was no sword in David’s hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Hebrews 11:34; 1 Samuel 21:9; 2 Samuel 23:21"> Then David ran and stood over the Philistine and took his sword and drew it from its sheath and killed him and cut off his head with it. When the Philistines saw that their champion was dead, they fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Joshua 15:11; Joshua 15:33–36; Joshua 15:45–46"> The men of Israel and Judah got up, raised the war cry, and pursued the Philistines ⸤as far as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “up to your coming to ”">aj</a></sup>the valley<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="So the Masoretic Hebrew text; LXX reads “Gath”">ak</a></sup>and up to the gates of Ekron. So the slain of the Philistines fell on the way<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “road”">al</a></sup>to Shaaraim up to Gath and as far as Ekron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; 2 Kings 7:7–16; Jeremiah 30:16"> Then the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">am</a></sup>returned from pursuing the Philistines and plundered their camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 16:33; Joshua 4:7–8; 1 Samuel 21:9"> And David took the head of the Philistine and brought it to Jerusalem and placed his weapons in his tent.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Then the king said, “You inquire whose son this young man is.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:54"> So when David returned from striking down the Philistine, Abner took him and brought him before Saul. The head of the Philistine was in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:12; 1 Samuel 16:18–19"> Then Saul said to him, “Whose son are you, young man?” And David said, “I am the son of your servant Jesse the Bethlehemite.”</span>
