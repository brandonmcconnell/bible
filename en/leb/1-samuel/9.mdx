## Saul, the Requested King, Introduced

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:51; Acts 13:21; 1 Chronicles 9:36–39"> Now there was a man from Benjamin whose name was Kish, the son of Abiel, the son of Zeror, the son of Becorath, the son of Aphiah, the son of a Benjaminite, ⸤a very wealthy man⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a man mighty of wealth” or “a man mighty of valor”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 6:2; Numbers 13:33; 1 Samuel 16:7"> He had a son whose name was Saul,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The name “Saul” means “the requested one”">b</a></sup>a young and handsome man. There was not a man from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>more handsome than he was; from his shoulders up, he was taller than all the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 5:10; 1 Samuel 10:2; Judges 10:4"> Now the female donkeys of Kish the father of Saul got lost, and Kish said to Saul his son, “Please take one of the servants with you and get up; go and seek the female donkeys.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42; Joshua 24:33; John 3:23"> So he passed through the hill country of Ephraim; he passed through the land of Shalisha but they did not find them. They passed through the land of Shaalim but they were not there. He passed through the land of Benjamin but did not find them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:2; 1 Samuel 1:1; Luke 12:11"> When they entered the land of Zuph, Saul said to his servant who was with him, “Come, let us return, lest my father cease caring about the female donkeys and worry about us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 13:1; Deuteronomy 33:1; 1 Timothy 6:11"> But he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, Saul’s servant">d</a></sup>said to him, “Look, a man of God is in this town, and the man is honored. All that he says certainly comes true. So then let us go there; perhaps he will tell us about our journey on which we have gone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 14:3; 2 Kings 8:8; 2 Kings 4:42"> So Saul said to his servant, “Look, we may go, but what should we bring to the man? For the bread is gone from our bags, and there is no present to bring to the man of God. What do we have with us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The servant again answered Saul and said, “Look, ⸤I have⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “what was found”">e</a></sup>in my hand a quarter shekel of silver! I will give it to the man of God so that he will tell us our way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 30:10; 2 Kings 17:13; Amos 7:12"> (Formerly in Israel, when a man went to inquire of God, he would say: “Come, let us go up to the seer.” For the prophet of today was formerly called a seer.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13–14"> So Saul said to his servant, “⸤Your suggestion is a good one⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “Your word is good”">f</a></sup>Come, let us go.” And they went to the town where the man of God was.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 24:11; Exodus 2:16; Genesis 24:18–20"> They were going up the ascent of the town when they found young women going out to draw water. They said to them, “Is there the seer here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; 1 Samuel 16:2; 1 Samuel 10:5"> They answered them and said, “There is. Look, he is just in front of you! Hurry now, because he has come to the town today, because there is a sacrifice for the people today at the high place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:11; Matthew 26:26; Luke 24:30"> As soon as you enter the town, you will find him, before he goes up to the high place to eat. For the people will not eat until he comes, because he must bless the sacrifice. Then afterward the invited guests will eat. So then, go up, because you will find him today!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So they went up to the town. As they were entering into the middle of the town, Samuel was coming forth to meet them, to go up to the high place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 13:21; 1 Samuel 15:1; Job 33:16"> Now Yahweh ⸤had revealed this to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “had opened the ear”">g</a></sup>Samuel the day before Saul arrived, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; Psalm 106:44; Exodus 3:7–9"> “This time tomorrow I will send to you a man from the land of Benjamin, and you must anoint him as leader over my people Israel. He will deliver my people from the hand of the Philistines. For I have seen the suffering of my people, because their cry of distress has come to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 13:11; 1 Samuel 16:6–12; Nehemiah 13:25"> When Samuel saw Saul, Yahweh answered him, “Here is the man about whom I told you! This is the one who will govern my people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then Saul approached Samuel in the middle of the gate and said, “Please tell me, where is the house of the seer?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:25; John 4:29"> Samuel answered Saul and said, “I am the seer. Go up ⸤ahead of me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “before my face”">h</a></sup>to the high place, and you will eat with me today; then I will send you away in the morning. I will tell you all that is ⸤on your mind⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “in your heart”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; 1 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 9:3"> And as for your female donkeys that were lost ⸤three days ago⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the day three of the days”">j</a></sup>⸤do not be concerned about them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “you shall not put your heart to them”">k</a></sup>because they have been found. For whom is all the desire of Israel? Is it not for you and for all the house of your father?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:17; Judges 20:46–48; Psalm 68:27"> Saul answered and said, “Am I not a Benjaminite, from the smallest of the tribes of Israel, and my family the humblest of all the families of the tribes of Benjamin? Why do you speak to me ⸤in this way⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “according to this word”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 43:32; Luke 14:10"> So Samuel took Saul and his servant and brought them to a room in the building and gave them a place at the head of ⸤the invited guests⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the ones being invited”">m</a></sup>There were about thirty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:5; Genesis 43:34"> Then Samuel said to the cook, “Bring the portion that I gave to you, about which I said to you, ‘Keep it with you.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:4; Leviticus 7:32–33; Exodus 29:22"> So the cook took up the shank and what was on it and put it before Saul, and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="That is, Samuel">n</a></sup>said, “Look, ⸤the saved portion⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “what was being kept”">o</a></sup>is placed ⸤before you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to your face”">p</a></sup>—eat, because it has been kept for you for the appointed time,” ⸤and he said⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “saying”">q</a></sup>“I have invited the people.” So Saul ate with Samuel that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 10:9; Deuteronomy 22:8; 2 Samuel 11:2"> When they came down from the high place to the town, he spoke with Saul on the roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 44:4; Joshua 7:13; Judges 19:28"> They got up early, ⸤and as dawn was breaking⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “as the dawn was going up”">r</a></sup>Samuel called to Saul on the roof, saying, “Get up, so that I can send you away.” So Saul got up and the two of them, he and Samuel, went outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 15:14–15; 1 Samuel 20:38–39; 2 Kings 9:5–6"> As they were going down to the outskirts of the town, Samuel said to Saul, “Tell the servant to pass on before us. When he has passed, you stand here ⸤a while⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “as the day”">s</a></sup>so that I can make known to you the word of God.”</span>
</div>
</div>
