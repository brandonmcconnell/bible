## Saul and Jonathan Die on Mount Gilboa

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:1–12; 1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21"> Now the Philistines were fighting against Israel, and the men of Israel fled before the Philistines, and they fell slain on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 8:33; 1 Samuel 14:49; 1 Samuel 23:17"> And the Philistines overtook Saul and his sons, and the Philistines killed Jonathan and Abinadab and Malki-Shua, the sons of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:6; Genesis 49:23; 1 Kings 22:34"> ⸤Saul was in the thick of the battle⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “The battle weighed heavily on Saul”">a</a></sup>and ⸤the archers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the shooters, the men with the bow”">b</a></sup>spotted<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “found”">c</a></sup>him, and he was badly wounded by the archers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:54; 2 Samuel 1:6; 2 Samuel 1:9–10"> Then Saul said to ⸤his armor bearer⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the carrier of his weapons”">d</a></sup>“Draw your sword and thrust me through with it, so that these uncircumcised do not come and thrust me through and make a fool of me!” But ⸤his armor bearer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the carrier of his weapons”">e</a></sup>was not willing to do so because he was very afraid. So Saul took the sword and fell on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 10:5"> And when ⸤his armor bearer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the carrier of his weapons”">f</a></sup>saw that Saul was dead, he also fell on his sword and died with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:17; Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Chronicles 10:6"> So Saul died, and his three sons, ⸤his armor bearer⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the carrier of his weapons”">g</a></sup>and all his men together that same day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Leviticus 26:32; Leviticus 26:36"> And when the men of Israel who were on the other side of the valley and those who were beyond the Jordan saw that the men of Israel had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned the towns and fled. Thus the Philistines came and lived in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:25; 1 Chronicles 10:8"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally, “And it happened”">h</a></sup>the next day, the Philistines came to strip the dead and they found Saul and his three sons lying dead on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:20; Judges 16:23–24; 1 Samuel 17:54"> So they cut off his head and stripped off his armor. Then they sent messengers around in the land of the Philistines to proclaim victory in the temples<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “houses”">i</a></sup>of their idols and to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 2:13; Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 21:9"> And they put his armor in the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “house”">j</a></sup>of the Ashtoreth,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew plural “Ashtaroth” (Ashtoreth was the female consort of Ba’al)">k</a></sup>and they fastened his corpse to the wall of Beth Shan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–11; 2 Samuel 2:4"> When the inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead heard about it, what the Philistines had done to Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:14; Jeremiah 34:5; 2 Samuel 2:4–7"> all of ⸤the valiant men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the men of ability”">l</a></sup>set out and went all night and took the corpse of Saul and the corpses of his sons from the wall of Beth Shan, and they came to Jabesh and burned them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:12–14; Genesis 50:10; 1 Samuel 22:6"> Then they took their bones and buried them under the tamarisk in Jabesh, and they fasted for seven days.</span>
</div>
