## Israel Defeats the Amalekites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 12:14; Psalm 2:10–11"> Then Samuel said to Saul, “Yahweh sent me to anoint you as king over his people Israel. So then, ⸤listen to the words⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “listen to the voice of the words”">a</a></sup>of Yahweh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:17–19; Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 24:20"> Thus says Yahweh of hosts: ‘I have observed what Amalek did to Israel, ⸤how he opposed him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “what he placed against him in the way”">b</a></sup>when he went up from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Numbers 24:20; Numbers 31:17"> So then, go and attack Amalek and utterly destroy all that is his! You must not spare him, but kill both man and woman, both child and nursing infant, both ox and sheep, both camel and donkey.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 15:24; 1 Samuel 11:8; 1 Samuel 13:15"> Saul summoned the army and mustered them at Telaim; two hundred thousand foot soldiers and ten thousand men of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Saul came up to the city of Amalek and set an ambush in the wadi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A valley that is usually dry but contains a stream during the rainy season">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 10:29–32; Exodus 18:19"> Saul said to the Kenites, “Go, leave! Withdraw from among the Amalekites so that I do not destroy you with them. You have shown loyal love to all the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>when they came up from Egypt.” So the Kenites left from among the Amalekites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 16:7; Genesis 25:18; 1 Samuel 14:48"> Then Saul defeated the Amalekites from Havilah as one goes to Shur which is ⸤east of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “upon the face of”">e</a></sup>Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1; Esther 3:1; Numbers 24:7"> He captured Agag the king of Amalek alive, but all the people he utterly destroyed with the ⸤edge⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “mouth”">f</a></sup>of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; 1 Samuel 15:19; 1 Samuel 15:3"> However, Saul and the army spared Agag and the best of the sheep and the cattle and the second best of the young fatlings and ⸤all that was valuable⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “all the good things ”">g</a></sup>they were not willing to utterly destroy them. But all the possessions that were despised or worthless, they utterly destroyed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Samuel Announces the Downfall of Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then the word of Yahweh came to Samuel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 6:6; 2 Samuel 24:16"> “I regret that I made Saul king, for he has turned back from following me and has not kept my word.” ⸤Samuel became angry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “it became hot for Samuel”">h</a></sup>and he cried out to Yahweh all night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 15:55; 2 Samuel 18:18; 1 Samuel 25:2"> Then Samuel got up early in the morning to meet Saul. Samuel was told, “Saul has gone to Carmel, and look, he is setting up a monument for himself.” Then he turned around and crossed over and went down to Gilgal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 17:2; Ruth 3:10; Proverbs 31:31"> When Samuel came to Saul, Saul said to him, “May you be blessed by Yahweh! I have kept the word of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:13–15; Jeremiah 2:18–19; Jeremiah 2:34–37"> But Samuel said, “Then what is this bleating of the sheep that I hear in my ears and the lowing of the cattle that I am hearing?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:21; 1 Samuel 15:9; Exodus 32:22–23"> Saul said, “They have brought them from the Amalekites; the troops spared the best of the sheep and the cattle in order to sacrifice them to Yahweh your God. But the rest we have utterly destroyed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:27; 1 Kings 22:16; 1 Samuel 12:7"> Then Samuel said to Saul, “Stop and let me tell you what Yahweh said to me last night.” So he said to him, “Speak.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:22; 1 Samuel 9:21; 1 Samuel 15:1–3"> Samuel said, “Even though you are small in your own eyes, are you not the head of the tribes of Israel? Yahweh has anointed you as king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 10:29; Genesis 13:13; Genesis 15:16"> When Yahweh sent you on your way, he said to you: ‘Go! You must utterly destroy the sinners, the Amalekites, and you must fight against them until you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The Masoretic Hebrew text reads “they”; LXX, Peshitta, and Targum read “you”">i</a></sup>have destroyed them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:32; 2 Chronicles 33:6; 2 Chronicles 36:12"> Why did you not listen to the voice of Yahweh and fall with shouting on the plunder? You have done evil in the sight of Yahweh!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:13; Matthew 19:20; Romans 10:3"> Then Saul said to Samuel, “I have listened to the voice of Yahweh, and I have gone on the way that Yahweh sent me! I brought Agag the king of Amalek, and the Amalekites I have utterly destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:15; Exodus 32:22–23; Genesis 3:13"> The troops took from the plunder, sheep and cattle, the best of the things devoted to destruction, to sacrifice to Yahweh your God at Gilgal.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Psalm 51:16–17; Proverbs 21:3"> Then Samuel said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Is there as much delight for Yahweh in burnt offerings and sacrifices</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as there is in ⸤obeying⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “listening to the voice of”">j</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Look! ⸤To obey⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “Listening”">k</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is better than sacrifice;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to give heed than the fat of rams.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:16; 1 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For rebellion is like the sin of divination;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">arrogance is like iniquity and idolatry.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because you have rejected the word of Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he has rejected you from being king!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; Isaiah 51:12–13; Numbers 22:34"> Then Saul said to Samuel, “I have sinned because I have transgressed ⸤the commandment of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of Yahweh”">l</a></sup>and your words, for I feared the troops and I listened to their voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 10:17"> So then, please pardon my sin and return with me ⸤so that I can worship⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “so that I can bow down to”">m</a></sup>Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 16:1"> But Samuel said to Saul, “I will not return with you, for you have rejected the word of Yahweh, and he has rejected you from being king over Israel!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 11:30–31"> As Samuel turned around to go, he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="That is, Saul">n</a></sup>caught hold of the hem of his robe, and it tore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:17–18; Acts 13:22; Daniel 4:32"> Then Samuel said to him, “Yahweh has torn the kingdom of Israel from you today and has given it to your neighbor ⸤who is better than you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the better than you”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:14; Numbers 23:19; Titus 1:2"> Moreover, the Glory of Israel will not break faith and will not regret, for he is not a human that he should regret.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 5:44; John 12:43; Isaiah 29:13"> Then he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="That is, Saul">p</a></sup>said, “I have sinned! Now please honor me before the elders of my people and before Israel, and return with me ⸤so that I can worship⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “so that I can bow down to”">q</a></sup>Yahweh your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Samuel returned after Saul, and Saul ⸤worshiped⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “bowed down to”">r</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 18:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Jeremiah 48:44"> Then Samuel said, “Bring Agag the king of Amalek out to me!” Agag came to him confidently,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “trembling”; literally “in chains/bonds” (HALOT 609 s.v. 2); the meaning here is uncertain (LXX has “trembling”)">s</a></sup>for Agag thought, “Surely the bitterness of death ⸤is over⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “has turned aside”; other English versions follow the LXX and Peshitta and omit “has turned aside,” reading “Surely the bitterness of death!”">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 1:7; Genesis 9:6; Matthew 7:2"> Samuel said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Just as your sword bereaved women,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so will your mother be bereaved among women!”</span><br />Then Samuel hacked Agag to pieces in the presence of Yahweh at Gilgal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; 1 Samuel 7:17"> Then Samuel went to Ramah and Saul went up to his house in Gibeah of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; 1 Samuel 19:24; 1 Samuel 16:1"> Samuel ⸤did not see Saul again⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “did not add to see Saul”">u</a></sup>until the day of his death, but Samuel mourned over Saul, and Yahweh regretted that he made Saul king over Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>
