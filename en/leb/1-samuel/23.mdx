<div className="bp">

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:4–6; Job 20:5; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> When it was told to Saul that David had gone to Keilah, Saul said, “God ⸤has given him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “has made him a stranger”">f</a></sup>into my hand, because he has shut himself in by going into a city with ⸤two barred gates⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “two gates and a bar”">g</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Samuel 23:6; Numbers 27:21"> When David learned that Saul was plotting evil against him, he said to Abiathar the priest, “Bring the ephod here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; 1 Samuel 23:8; Genesis 18:24"> And David said, “O Yahweh, God of Israel, your servant has clearly heard that Saul is seeking to come to Keilah to destroy the city because of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Psalm 50:15; Matthew 7:7–8"> Will the rulers of Keilah deliver me into his hand? Will Saul come down as your servant has heard? O Yahweh, God of Israel, please tell your servant!” And Yahweh said, “He will come down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:20; Psalm 31:8; Psalm 62:1"> Then David said, “Will the rulers of Keilah deliver me and my men into the hand of Saul?” And Yahweh said, “They will deliver you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:2; 1 Samuel 25:13; 1 Samuel 30:9–10"> So David and his men got up, about six hundred men, and went out from Keilah and wandered wherever they could go. ## David Eludes Saul’s Pursuit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 54:3–4; Psalm 32:7; Joshua 15:24"> David remained in the wilderness, in the strongholds, and in the hill country in the wilderness of Ziph. And Saul sought him ⸤continually⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “all the days”">i</a></sup>but God did not give him into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> When David realized that Saul had gone out to seek his life, David was in the wilderness of Ziph at Horesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; Hebrews 12:12–13; Job 4:3–4"> So Jonathan the son of Saul got up and went to David at Horesh, and ⸤encouraged him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “he strengthened his hand”">j</a></sup>through God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:31; 1 Samuel 24:20; Acts 28:16"> He said to him, “Do not be afraid, for the hand of my father Saul will not find you. You will be king over Israel, and ⸤I will be second to you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “I shall be for you as second”">k</a></sup>My father Saul knows this also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:7; 1 Samuel 20:42; 1 Samuel 18:3"> Then the two of them ⸤made⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “cut”">l</a></sup>a covenant before Yahweh. David remained at Horesh, and Jonathan went to his house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:1; 1 Samuel 26:3; Psalm 54:1–7"> Then the Ziphites went up to Saul at Gibeah, saying, “Is not David hiding among us in the strongholds at Horesh on the hill of Hakilah, which is south of Jeshimon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:12; Proverbs 29:26; Proverbs 11:23"> So then, O king, ⸤whenever you want⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “for all the wanting of your soul”">m</a></sup>to come down, come down, and it will be for us to deliver him into the hand of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Isaiah 66:5; Micah 3:11"> And Saul said to them, “May you be blessed by Yahweh, for you have shown me compassion!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:13"> Go, please, make certain again! Find out and see ⸤exactly where he is⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “his place where his foot is ”">n</a></sup>and who has seen him there! For they have said to me, ‘He is very cunning.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 3:15–16; Mark 14:1; Numbers 10:36"> Look, find out all of the hiding places where he hides. Then return to me ⸤with dependable information⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “to being established”">o</a></sup>and I will go with you. And then if he is there in the land, then I will seek him among all the thousands of Judah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:2; Joshua 15:55"> Then they got up and went to Ziph before Saul.<br />Now David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon in the Arabah, to the south of Jeshimon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 15:8; 1 Samuel 23:28"> And Saul and his men went to seek him, and they told David, so he went down to the rock and stayed in the wilderness of Maon. When Saul heard this, he pursued David into the wilderness of Maon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 17:9; 2 Corinthians 1:8; Psalm 140:1–9"> Saul went ⸤on one side of the mountain⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “from the side of the mountain from this”">p</a></sup>and David and his men ⸤went on the other side of the mountain⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “from the side of the mountain from this”">q</a></sup>David was hurrying to get away from Saul, while Saul and his men were closing in on David and his men to capture them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 19:9; Psalm 116:3; Deuteronomy 32:36"> But a messenger came to Saul, saying, “Hurry and come, because the Philistines have made a raid on the land!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Saul returned from pursuing David, and he went to confront<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “to meet”">r</a></sup>the Philistines. Therefore, they called that place the Rock of Division.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “Sela’ Hammahlekot”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:2; Joshua 15:62; Ezekiel 47:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="1 Sam 23:29–24:22 in the English Bible is 24:1–23 in the Hebrew Bible">t</a></sup>David went up from there and stayed in the strongholds of En Gedi.</span>
</div>
