## David Spares Saul in the Cave at En Gedi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:28–29; Ezekiel 22:9; 1 Samuel 23:19"> When Saul returned from pursuing the Philistines, they told him, “Look, David is in the wilderness of En Gedi.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:2; Psalm 104:18; Psalm 37:32"> So Saul took three thousand chosen men from all Israel, and he and his men went to search for David ⸤in the direction of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “on the face of”">a</a></sup>the Rocks of the Mountain Goats.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “of the Jael”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 3:24; Psalm 57:1–11; Psalm 142:1–7"> He came to the sheep pens beside the road, and a cave was there. Then Saul went in ⸤to relieve himself⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “to cover his feet”">c</a></sup>Now David and his men were sitting in the innermost part of the cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:8–11; 1 Samuel 24:10; 1 Samuel 26:23"> And David’s men said to him, “Look, today is the day about which Yahweh said to you, ‘See, I am giving your enemy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="The Masoretic Hebrew text (Kethib ) reads “enemies,” but the reading tradition (Qere) has the singular “enemy”">d</a></sup>into your hand, and you can do to him ⸤whatever seems good to you⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “as that which is good in your eyes”">e</a></sup>So David got up and secretly cut the hem of Saul’s robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:10; 2 Kings 22:19; 1 John 3:20–21"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">f</a></sup>afterward David ⸤felt guilty⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the heart of David struck him”">g</a></sup>because he had cut ⸤the hem of Saul’s robe⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the hem which was for Saul”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:14; Job 31:29–30; 1 Samuel 26:9–11"> He said to his men, “Far be it from me in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “from”">i</a></sup>Yahweh, that I do this thing to my lord, to Yahweh’s anointed one, by stretching out my hand against him! For he is the anointed one of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Psalm 7:4; Romans 12:17–21"> So David rebuked his men with the words and did not allow them to rise against Saul. And Saul got up from the cave, and he went on his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">j</a></sup>way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:23–24; Romans 13:7; Exodus 20:12"> Then David got up afterward and went out of the cave and called after Saul, “My lord the king!” When Saul looked after him, David knelt down with his face to the ground and bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:21–22; Proverbs 29:12; Proverbs 16:28"> Then David said to Saul, “Why do you listen to the words of people who say: ‘Look, David is ⸤seeking to do you harm⸥’?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “seeking your evil”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 105:15; 1 Samuel 24:4; 1 Samuel 26:8–9"> Look, this day your eyes have seen that Yahweh gave you today into my hand in the cave, and some said to kill you. But ⸤I took pity⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “she took pity,” with “she” = “my eye,” a common Hebrew expression">l</a></sup>on you and said, ‘I will not stretch out my hand against my lord, because he is Yahweh’s anointed one.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:20; 1 Samuel 23:14; 1 Samuel 23:23"> Now, my father, see, yes, see, the hem of your robe in my hand! For ⸤when I cut⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “at my cutting”">m</a></sup>the hem of your robe I did not kill you. Know and ⸤realize⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “see”">n</a></sup>that there is no evil or rebellion in my hand. I did not sin against you, but you are hunting down my life to take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 43:1; Judges 11:27; Psalm 35:1"> May Yahweh judge between me and you, and may Yahweh avenge me on you, but my hand will not be against you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 7:20; Matthew 7:16–18; Matthew 12:33–34"> Just as the ancient proverb says, ‘From the wicked, wickedness goes out,’ but my hand will not be against you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:20; 1 Samuel 17:43; 2 Samuel 9:8"> After whom did the king of Israel go out? After whom are you pursuing? After a dead dog? After one flea?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 43:1; Psalm 35:1; Psalm 119:154"> May Yahweh be the judge, and let him judge between me and you, and may he see and plead my case. ⸤May he vindicate me against you⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “May he judge me from your hand”">o</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:17; Genesis 33:4; Luke 21:15"> When David finished speaking these words to Saul, Saul said, “Is this your voice, my son David?” And Saul lifted up his voice and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; 1 Samuel 26:21; Genesis 38:26"> Then he said to David, “You are more righteous than I, for you have repaid me the good, but I have repaid you the evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:23; Psalm 31:8; 1 Samuel 23:12"> You have explained to me today that you have dealt well with me, how<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “that”">p</a></sup>Yahweh delivered me into your hand but you did not kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 18:20; 1 Samuel 23:21; Proverbs 25:21–22"> For a man has found his enemy but sent him on his way safely. Now may Yahweh reward you with good in return for this day, for what you have done for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:17; Matthew 2:3–6; 2 Samuel 3:17–18"> So now then, look, I know that you will certainly be king and the kingdom of Israel will be established in your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:23; 2 Samuel 21:6–8; 1 Samuel 20:14–17"> So then, swear to me by Yahweh that you will not cut off my ⸤descendants⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “seed”">q</a></sup>after me and that you will not wipe out my name from my father’s house.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:29; John 2:24; Matthew 10:16–17"> So David swore this on oath to Saul, and Saul went to his house, but David and his men went up to the stronghold.</span>
</div>
</div>
