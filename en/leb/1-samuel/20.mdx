<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then Jonathan said to David, “⸤Whatever you wish⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “What your soul says”">g</a></sup>I will do for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:2; Numbers 28:11"> David said to Jonathan, “Look, tomorrow is the new moon, and I should certainly sit with the king to eat. You must send me away so that I can hide myself in the field until the third evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:58; 1 Samuel 9:12; Deuteronomy 12:5"> If your father misses me at all, then you must say, ‘David earnestly asked from me to run to Bethlehem his city, for ⸤the yearly sacrifice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “the sacrifice of the days”">h</a></sup>is there for all the clan.’</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:32; 1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:18"> So you must show loyal love to your servant, for you have brought your servant into a covenant of Yahweh with you. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:42; 1 Samuel 19:2; 1 Samuel 20:38"> Then Jonathan said, “Far be it from you! <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jonathan said to David, “Come, let us go out to the field.” So the two of them went out to the field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 22:22; Job 31:4; Proverbs 25:2–3"> Then Jonathan said to David, “Yahweh the God of Israel is my witness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The phrase “is my witness” is supplied based on the Syriac version">k</a></sup>that I will question my father ⸤by this time the day after tomorrow⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “as the time tomorrow the third”">l</a></sup>And look, ⸤if he is well disposed toward you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “if he is good to David”">m</a></sup>will I not send word to you and ⸤disclose it to you⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “I will reveal it to your ear”">n</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:3; Ephesians 5:1–2"> And not while I am still alive, will you not show the loyal love of Yahweh with me, that I may not die?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or perhaps “and if I die, may you not …”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:7; 1 Samuel 24:21; 2 Samuel 9:1–7"> And do not cut off your loyal love from ⸤my family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “my house”">s</a></sup>forever, not even when Yahweh ⸤exterminates⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “cuts off”">t</a></sup>each of the enemies of David from the face of the earth.”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:2; 1 Samuel 20:5"> On the third day you must go down quickly and go to the place where you hid yourself ⸤on the day all this started⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “on the day of the deed”">w</a></sup>and remain beside the stone Ezel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> I will shoot three arrows to the side as if I were shooting at a target.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:2; Amos 8:14; Jeremiah 12:16"> ⸤Then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “And look”">x</a></sup>I will send word to my servant, ‘Go, find the arrows!’ If I clearly say to the boy, ‘Look, the arrows are ⸤on this side of you⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “from you and here”">y</a></sup>⸤bring⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “take”">z</a></sup>it,’ then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “and”">aa</a></sup>come, for it means peace for you. </div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:18; Judges 16:20"> The king sat at his seat ⸤as before⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “as occurrence at occurrence”">af</a></sup>the seat by the wall, and Jonathan got up, and Abner sat beside Saul, but David’s place was empty.</span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; 1 Samuel 19:5; Luke 23:22"> But Jonathan answered his father Saul and said to him, “Why should he be put to death? What has he done?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:7; 1 Samuel 18:11; 1 Samuel 19:10"> Then Saul hurled his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Hebrew “the”">an</a></sup>spear at him to kill him. <span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:20–22"> He said to his servant,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Or “boy”">as</a></sup>“Run, please find the arrows that I am shooting!” The boy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “servant”">at</a></sup>ran, and he shot the arrow to pass over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:22"> When the boy came up to the place of the arrow that Jonathan had shot, Jonathan called out after the boy and said, “Is not the arrow ⸤beyond you⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “from you and beyond”">au</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalm 55:6–9; Proverbs 6:4–5; Mark 13:14–16"> Then Jonathan called out after the boy, “Quick, hurry, do not linger!” And Jonathan’s servant collected the arrows and came to his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But the boy did not know anything about this; only Jonathan and David knew the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Jonathan gave his weapons to his servant and said to him, “Go, bring them to the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 18:3; 2 Samuel 9:6"> The boy left, and then David got up from the south side,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="So Masoretic Hebrew text; LXX reads “from the mound” (of stones)">av</a></sup>and he fell on his face to the ground and bowed three times. <span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:17; 1 Samuel 23:18; 1 Samuel 20:22–23"> Jonathan said to David, “Go in peace, because we have sworn, the two of us, an oath in the name of Yahweh, saying, “Yahweh will be between me and you, and between my offspring and your offspring forever.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="In the Hebrew Bible, 1 Sam 21 begins here. 1 Samuel 20:42b–21:15 in the English Bible is 21:1–16 in the Hebrew Bible">ay</a></sup>Then he got up and left, and Jonathan went into the city.</span>
