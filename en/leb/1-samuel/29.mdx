## David Leaves the Service of Achish

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:1; Joshua 19:30; 1 Kings 20:30"> Now the Philistines assembled all their forces at Aphek, and Israel was encamped at the spring that is in Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:1–2; Joshua 13:3; 1 Samuel 29:6–7"> The rulers of the Philistines were passing on according to hundreds and thousands, David and his men passing on at the rear with Achish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 6:5; 1 Samuel 27:7; John 19:6"> Then the commanders of the Philistines said, “What are these Hebrews doing here?” And Achish said to the commanders of the Philistines, “Is this not David, the servant of Saul, the king of Israel, who has been with me ⸤for days and years⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “these days or these years”">a</a></sup>I have not found anything threatening in him from the day of his desertion until this day!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:21; 1 Chronicles 12:19; Luke 16:8"> But the commanders of the Philistines were angry with him and they said to him, “Send the man back so that he might return to his place where you have assigned him! But he will not go down with us into the battle, so that he does not become an adversary to us in the battle. By what could this fellow make himself favorable to his lord? Is it not with the heads of these men?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; 1 Samuel 18:6–7; Proverbs 27:14"> Is this not David about whom they sing in the dances, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Saul has killed his thousands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but David his ten thousands’?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:25; 2 Kings 19:27; 1 Samuel 20:3"> So Achish called David and said to him, “⸤As Yahweh lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “The life of Yahweh”">b</a></sup>certainly you were honest, and ⸤I am pleased to have you marching with me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “good in my eyes your going out and your coming in with me”">c</a></sup>in the campaign.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “army”">d</a></sup>For I have not found any wrong in you from the day you came to me until this day, but in the eyes of the rulers, you are not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 22:34"> So then, return and go in peace, so that you do not do ⸤something that displeases⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “bad/evil in the eyes of”">e</a></sup>the rulers of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Psalm 34:13–14; 1 Samuel 28:2"> Then David said to Achish, “But what have I done? And what have you found in your servant from the day that I ⸤entered your service⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “that I was to your face” = “was before you”">f</a></sup>until this day, that I should not go and fight against the enemies of my lord the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:27; 2 Samuel 14:17; 2 Samuel 14:20"> And Achish answered and said to David, “I know that you are good in my eyes, like an angel of God! However, the commanders of the Philistines have said, ‘He must not go up with us into the battle.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:19; 1 Samuel 30:1–2; 1 Chronicles 12:22"> So then, rise early in the morning, you and the servants of your lord who came with you. When you rise early in the morning and it is light enough for you, leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:1; 2 Samuel 4:4; Joshua 19:18"> So David set out early, he and his men, to leave in the morning to return to the land of the Philistines, but the Philistines went up to Jezreel.</span>
</div>
