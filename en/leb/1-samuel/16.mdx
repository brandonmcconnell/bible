## David Is Anointed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1; 1 Samuel 15:23; Psalm 78:68–71"> Then Yahweh said to Samuel, “⸤How long⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “Until when”">a</a></sup>will you mourn about Saul? I have rejected him from being king over Israel! Fill up your horn with oil and go. I will send you to Jesse the Bethlehemite, for I have chosen a king for myself among his sons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:12; 1 Samuel 20:29; Matthew 10:16"> But Samuel said, “How can I go? If Saul hears, he will kill me.” Yahweh said, “You must take a heifer from the herd ⸤with you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in/with your hand”">b</a></sup>and you must say, ‘I have come to sacrifice to Yahweh.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 16:12–13; 2 Samuel 15:11"> You will invite Jesse to the sacrifice and I will make known to you what you must do. You will anoint for me the one whom I tell you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:13; 1 Chronicles 12:17–18; 1 Samuel 21:1"> So Samuel did what Yahweh said. He came to Bethlehem, and the elders of the city came trembling to meet him. They said, “⸤Have you come in peace⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “Is your coming peace?”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 3:5; Leviticus 20:7–8; Exodus 19:10"> He said, “I come in peace. I have come to sacrifice to Yahweh. Sanctify yourselves and come with me to the sacrifice.” So he sanctified Jesse and his sons and invited them to the sacrifice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Samuel 17:22"> ⸤When they came⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “And it happened at their coming”">d</a></sup>he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, Samuel">e</a></sup>saw Eliab and said, “Surely his anointed one is before Yahweh!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:24; Jeremiah 17:10; Luke 16:15"> But Yahweh said to Samuel, “Do not look at his appearance or at the height of his stature, because I have rejected him. For God does not see what man sees, for a man ⸤looks on the outward appearance⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sees as far as the eyes”">f</a></sup>but Yahweh ⸤looks on the heart⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sees as far as the heart”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13"> Then Jesse called Abinadab and made him pass before Samuel, and he said, “This one also Yahweh has not chosen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13; 2 Samuel 13:3"> So Jesse made Shammah pass before Samuel, but he said, “Yahweh also has not chosen this one.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13–15"> And Jesse made seven of his sons pass before Samuel, but Samuel said to Jesse, “Yahweh has not chosen any of these.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 7:8; 2 Samuel 13:3"> Then Samuel said to Jesse, “⸤Are all the young men here⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “Are the boys finished?”">h</a></sup>And he said, “The youngest still remains, but look, he is shepherding the flock.” And Samuel said to Jesse, “Send and bring him, for we cannot ⸤sit down⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “turn”; for “sit down” see HALOT 739 s.v. 2.c">i</a></sup>until he comes here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:7; Psalm 89:19–20; Song of Solomon 5:10"> So he sent and brought him. Now he was ruddy with beautiful eyes and of ⸤handsome⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “good”">j</a></sup>appearance. And Yahweh said, “Arise, anoint him, for this is he.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:6; Hebrews 1:9; John 3:34"> So Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the midst of his brothers. Then the Spirit of Yahweh rushed upon David from that day ⸤on⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “and forward”">k</a></sup>Then Samuel got up and went to Ramah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Saul in Torment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 51:11; 1 Samuel 18:12; Judges 9:23"> Now the Spirit of Yahweh departed from Saul and an evil spirit from Yahweh tormented him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So the servants of Saul said to him, “Look please, an evil spirit from God is tormenting you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Kings 10:8; 1 Samuel 19:9"> Please, let our lord command your servants who are before you! Let them seek a man skilled in playing on the lyre. ⸤When⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “And it will happen that ”">l</a></sup>the evil spirit from God is upon you, he can play ⸤on it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “with his hand”">m</a></sup>and ⸤you will feel better⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “and it will be good for you”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Saul said to his servants, “Please select a man ⸤who plays a stringed instrument well⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “doing well to play a stringed instrument”">o</a></sup>and bring him to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:19; Genesis 39:23; Genesis 39:2"> One of the servants answered and said, “Look, I have seen a son of Jesse the Bethlehemite who is skillful in playing a stringed instrument, a ⸤brave man, a warrior, prudent in speech, and handsome⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “a hero of ability, a man of war, discerning of word, and a man of appearance”">p</a></sup>And Yahweh is with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; 1 Samuel 17:15; Psalm 78:70–72"> So Saul sent messengers to Jesse and said, “Send me David your son who is with the sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Proverbs 18:16; 1 Samuel 17:18"> And Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread and a skin of wine and one ⸤young goat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “kid of a goat”">q</a></sup>and sent them to Saul by the hand of David his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:46; Proverbs 22:29; Psalm 146:3"> So David came to Saul and ⸤entered his service⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “stood before him”">r</a></sup>He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="That is, Saul">s</a></sup>loved him greatly and ⸤he became Saul’s armor bearer⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “he became for him a carrier of weapons”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then Saul sent word to Jesse, saying, “Please let David stand before me, because he has found favor ⸤in my sight⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “in my eyes”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:14–16; 1 Samuel 18:10–11; Luke 11:24–26"> So whenever the evil spirit from God came to Saul, David would take the stringed instrument and play it with his hand. Then ⸤it would bring relief⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “it would be relieved”">v</a></sup>for Saul; ⸤he would feel better⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “it would be good for him”">w</a></sup>and the evil spirit would depart from him.</span>
</div>
</div>
