## The Family of Elkanah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:34; 1 Chronicles 6:25–27; 1 Samuel 1:19"> There was a certain man from Ramathaim Zophim, from the hill country of Ephraim, whose name was Elkanah the son of Jeroham, the son of Elihu, the son of Tohu, the son of Zuph, an Ephraimite.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “Ephrathite”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:1–2; Judges 13:2; Genesis 29:23–29"> He had two wives; the name of the first was Hannah, and the name of the second was Peninnah. Now Peninnah had children, but Hannah had no children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 2:41; Joshua 18:1; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> Now this man used to go up from his town ⸤year by year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “from days to days”">b</a></sup>to worship and to sacrifice to Yahweh of hosts in Shiloh, ⸤where⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “and there”">c</a></sup>the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were priests to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:17–18; Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:5–7"> ⸤On⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “And it happened on”">d</a></sup>the day Elkanah sacrificed, he would give portions to his wife Peninnah and to all her sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:2; Genesis 29:30–31; Genesis 45:22"> But to Hannah he would give ⸤a double portion⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a portion of two faces”">e</a></sup>because he loved Hannah, though Yahweh had closed her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 6:14; Job 24:21; Leviticus 18:18"> (Now her rival wife would provoke her severely in order to upset her because Yahweh had closed her womb.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:19"> And so he used to do<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="So Hebrew; because of the abrupt change of subject some revocalize the verb to read “it used to be”">f</a></sup>year after year; ⸤whenever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “as often as”; literally “from enough”">g</a></sup>she went up to the house of Yahweh, she<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, Penninah">h</a></sup>would provoke her so that she<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="That is, Hannah">i</a></sup>would weep and would not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:15; Job 6:14; Isaiah 54:6"> So Elkanah her husband would say to her: “Hannah, why do you weep and why do you not eat? And ⸤why are you heartsick⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “why is your heart evil”">j</a></sup>Am I not better to you than ten sons?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:3; Psalm 27:4; Psalm 5:7"> Then Hannah got up after eating and drinking at Shiloh. (Now Eli the priest was sitting on his chair by the doorpost of the temple<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="This is not the temple of Solomon (ca. 966 bc), but rather the tabernacle that first resided at Shiloh after the conquest of Joshua.">k</a></sup>of Yahweh.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 7:11; Luke 22:44; Job 10:1"> She was ⸤deeply troubled⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “bitter of soul”">l</a></sup>so she prayed to Yahweh and wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Numbers 6:5; Genesis 29:32"> She ⸤made a vow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “vowed a vow”">m</a></sup>and said: “O Yahweh of hosts, if you will look with compassion on the misery of your female servant, and will remember me, and not forget your female servant, and will give to your female servant ⸤a male child⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “an offspring of men”">n</a></sup>then I will give him to Yahweh all the days of his life, and a razor will never pass over his head.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="“A razor will never pass over his head” indicates that Hannah promised to dedicate her son to Yahweh as a Nazirite, according to the terms of Num 6:1–21">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16; Luke 18:1"> ⸤While⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “And it happened that when”">p</a></sup>she continued to pray before Yahweh, Eli was observing her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 8:26; Acts 2:13; Genesis 24:42–45"> Now Hannah had been speaking in her heart; her lips were moving, but her voice could not be heard, so Eli considered her to be drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:25; Job 8:2; Proverbs 6:9"> Then Eli said to her, “How long will you behave like someone who is drunk? Put away your wine!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 62:8; Lamentations 2:19; Psalm 42:4"> But Hannah answered and said, “No, my lord. I am a woman ⸤deeply distressed⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “difficult of spirit”">q</a></sup>but I have not drunk wine or strong drink. Rather, I have poured out my soul before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:12; Job 6:2–3; Matthew 12:34–35"> Do not regard your female servant as ⸤worthless⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “as a daughter of Belial/daughter of worthlessness”">r</a></sup>but because of the extent of my worries and my provocation I have spoken all of this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Judges 18:6; Psalm 20:3–5"> Then Eli answered and said, “Go in peace, and may the God of Israel grant your request that you have asked of him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Romans 15:13; Ecclesiastes 9:7"> And she said, “May your female slave find favor in your sight.” Then the woman went on her way and ate something, and ⸤her face did not look sad any longer⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “her faces were not for her any longer”">s</a></sup></span>
</div>

## The Birth of Samuel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:1; Genesis 30:22; 1 Samuel 1:11"> Then they rose early in the morning and worshiped before Yahweh ⸤and returned⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “and they returned and went”">t</a></sup>to their house at Ramah. Then Elkanah ⸤had sexual relations with⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “knew”">u</a></sup>Hannah his wife, and Yahweh remembered her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 41:51–52; Matthew 1:21; Exodus 2:10"> ⸤In due time⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “At the turning of the days”">v</a></sup>Hannah conceived and gave birth to a son. She called his name Samuel, for she said, “I requested him from Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:3; Deuteronomy 12:11; Joshua 24:15"> So the man Elkanah went up with all his household ⸤to make the annual sacrifice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “to sacrifice the sacrifice of days”">w</a></sup>to Yahweh and to pay his vow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:28; 1 Samuel 1:11; Luke 2:22"> But Hannah did not go up, for she said to her husband, “Once the boy is weaned, I will bring him, so that he may appear before Yahweh; and he will remain there forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:25; Luke 11:27; Genesis 21:7–8"> So her husband Elkanah said to her, “⸤Do what seems right to you⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “do what is good in your eyes”">x</a></sup>stay until you wean him. Only may Yahweh fulfill his word.” So the woman remained and nursed her son until she weaned him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 15:9–10; Joshua 18:1; 1 Samuel 4:3–4"> Then she brought him up with her when she had weaned him, along with three bulls, one ephah of flour, and a skin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “a clay jar”">y</a></sup>of wine. She brought him to the house of Yahweh at Shiloh while the boy was still young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 18:15–16; Luke 2:22"> They slaughtered the bull, and they brought the boy to Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 4:30; 2 Kings 2:4; 2 Kings 2:6"> She said, “Excuse me, my lord. As ⸤you live⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “your soul lives”">z</a></sup>my lord, I am the woman who stood with you in this place to pray to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 5:15; Psalm 118:5; Matthew 7:7"> I prayed for this boy, and Yahweh has given me my request that I asked from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:52; 2 Timothy 3:15"> I in turn have lent him to Yahweh. ⸤As long as he lives⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “All the days that he lives”">aa</a></sup>he is lent to Yahweh.” Then they worshiped Yahweh there.</span>
</div>
