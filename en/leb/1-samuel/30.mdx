## David Recovers His Loved Ones

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:7; 1 Samuel 29:11; 1 Samuel 27:8–10"> ⸤Now⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">a</a></sup>when David and his men came to Ziklag on the third day, the Amalekites had raided the Negev and Ziklag. When they attacked Ziklag, they burned it with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:11; Job 38:11; Psalm 76:10"> They took captive the women<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="LXX adds “and all”">b</a></sup>who were in it, ⸤from the youngest to the oldest⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “from small and up to great”">c</a></sup>They did not kill anyone, but carried them off and went on their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 34:19; 1 Peter 1:6–7; Revelation 3:9"> When David and his men came to the city, ⸤they saw⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “and look”">d</a></sup>and it was burned with fire, and their wives, their sons, and their daughters had been taken captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; Numbers 14:1; Judges 21:2"> Then David and the people who were with him raised their voices and wept until there was not enough strength in them to weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 2 Samuel 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:2"> Two of David’s wives had been taken captive. Ahinoam ⸤from Jezreel⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the Jezreelitess”">e</a></sup>and Abigail, the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 56:3–4; Psalm 56:11; Psalm 62:5"> And ⸤David was in a very precarious situation⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “it was very pressed for David”">f</a></sup>for the people spoke of stoning him, for the souls of all the people were bitter, each one over his sons and his daughters. But David strengthened himself in Yahweh his God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26; 1 Samuel 22:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:2–9"> Then David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, “Please bring the ephod here for me.” So Abiathar brought the ephod to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:18; Proverbs 3:5–6; 1 Samuel 23:2"> And David inquired of Yahweh, saying, “Should I pursue after this band of raiders? Will I overtake them?” He said to him, “Pursue them, for you will certainly overtake them, and you will certainly rescue them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:2"> So David went, he and the six hundred men who were with him, and they came to the Wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A valley that is usually dry but contains a stream during the rainy season">g</a></sup>Besor, but the rest remained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:9; 1 Samuel 30:21; 1 Samuel 14:31"> David pursued, he and four hundred men; but two hundred men stayed because they were too exhausted to pass over the Wadi Besor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Deuteronomy 23:7; Matthew 25:35"> Then they found an Egyptian man in the open country and brought him to David, and they gave him food and he ate; they also gave him water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 15:19; 1 Samuel 14:27; 1 Samuel 30:13"> They gave him a slice of fig cake and two raisin cakes; he ate and ⸤this revived him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “his spirit returned to him”">h</a></sup>because he had not eaten food or drunk water for three days and three nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 2:13; Proverbs 12:10; Job 31:13–15"> Then David said to him, “⸤To whom do you belong⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “To whom are you”">i</a></sup>and from where are you? The young man said, “I am an Egyptian young man, a servant of an Amalekite man, but my master abandoned me because I became ill three days ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 1:38; 2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:44"> We raided the Negev of the Kerethites and that which belongs to Judah and then the Negev of Caleb, and we burned Ziklag with fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Joshua 9:19–20; Ezekiel 17:16"> So David asked him, “Will you take me down to this band of raiders?” He said, “Swear to me by God that you will not kill me and that you will not deliver me into my master’s hand! Then I will take you down to this band.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; 2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 16:23–30"> So he took him down, and ⸤there they were⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “look”">j</a></sup>spread out over the surface of all the land, eating and drinking and dancing because of all of the abundant plunder which they had taken from the land of the Philistines and from the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:11; Psalm 18:42; 1 Samuel 15:3"> Then David attacked them from twilight until the evening of the next day. Not a man of them escaped ⸤except⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “but if”">k</a></sup>four hundred young men who rode off on camels and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> So David recovered all that the Amalekites had taken; David also rescued his two wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 91:9–10; 1 Samuel 30:8; Job 1:10"> None of theirs was missing ⸤from the smallest to the greatest⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “from the small and up to the great”">l</a></sup>even sons and daughters, from the plunder up to everything they had taken for themselves; David brought back everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:26; Numbers 31:9–12; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> And David took all of the sheep,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “flock,” referring to either sheep or goats or both">m</a></sup>and the cattle they drove along in front of that livestock, and they said, “This is David’s plunder.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; 1 Samuel 30:10; Judges 8:15"> Then David came to the two hundred men ⸤who had been too exhausted to follow⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “who were exhausted from going after”">n</a></sup>David; they had left them behind at the Wadi<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="A valley that is usually dry but contains a stream during the rainy season">o</a></sup>Besor. They went out to meet David and to meet the people who were with him. David came near with the people and ⸤asked them how they were doing⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “asked for them as far as peace”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> Then all the corrupt and useless men among the men who went with David reacted and said, “Because they did not go with us, we will not give them anything from the plunder which we recovered, ⸤except⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “but if”">q</a></sup>each man may take his own wife and children. They must take them along and go!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Psalm 44:2–7; Acts 7:2"> But David said to them, “You should not do so, my brothers, with what Yahweh has given to us! He has preserved us and has given the raiding band that came against us into our hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Numbers 31:27; Psalm 68:12"> And who would listen to you regarding this matter? For as the share of the one who went down into the battle, so the share of the one who remained with the baggage will be. They will share alike.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13"> So<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “And”">r</a></sup>from that day and beyond, he made it a rule and a regulation for Israel until this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Genesis 33:11; Isaiah 32:8"> Then David came to Ziklag, and he sent some of the plunder to the elders of Judah, to his friends, saying, “⸤Here is⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “Look”">s</a></sup>a gift for you from the plunder of the enemies of Yahweh!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 15:48; Joshua 19:8; Judges 1:22–23"> It was for those in Bethel, for those in Ramoth of the Negev, for those in Jattir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Joshua 15:50; Joshua 21:14"> for those in Aroer, for those in Siphmoth, for those in Eshtemoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Samuel 27:10; 1 Samuel 15:6"> for those in Racal, for those in the towns of the Jerahmeelites, for those in the towns of the Kenites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4; Joshua 15:42"> for those in Hormah, for those in Bor Ashan, for those in Athach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:1; 2 Samuel 15:10; 2 Samuel 4:1"> for those in Hebron, and for all the places ⸤where David and his men had roamed⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “where David had gone about there, he and his men”">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
