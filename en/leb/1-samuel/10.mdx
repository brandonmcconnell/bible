## Saul Is Anointed as King

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13; Psalm 78:71; 1 Samuel 9:16"> Then Samuel took a flask of oil and poured it over his head and kissed him and said, “⸤Has not⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “Is it not that”">a</a></sup>Yahweh anointed you as leader over his inheritance?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:3–5; Genesis 35:19–20; Joshua 18:28"> As you go from with me ⸤today⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the day”">b</a></sup>you will find two men near the burial site of Rachel in the territory of Benjamin at Zelzah. They will say to you, ‘The female donkeys that you went to search for have been found.’ Now look, your father ⸤is no longer concerned about⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “has given up the matter of”">c</a></sup>the female donkeys and has begun worrying about you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Here the pronoun is plural, referring to Saul and his servant">d</a></sup>saying, ‘What should I do about my son?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 28:22"> Then you will go on from there and further you will come to the oak of Tabor. There three men will meet you, who are going up to God at Bethel. One will be carrying three male kid goats, one will be carrying three loaves of bread, and one will be carrying a skin of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 18:15"> ⸤They will ask how you are doing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “they will ask peace for you”">e</a></sup>and will give you two loaves, which you will take from their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Samuel 13:3; 1 Samuel 19:20"> After this, you will come to the Gibeah of God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “to Gibeath Elohim,” which means “the hill of God”">f</a></sup>where there are sentries<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “garrisons” or “overseers”">g</a></sup>of the Philistines. ⸤Just as you enter⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “and will it happen that as you enter”">h</a></sup>the town there, you will meet a procession of prophets coming down from the high place, with harp, tambourine, flute, and zither before them, and they will be prophesying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:10; 1 Samuel 19:23–24; Numbers 11:25"> Then the Spirit of Yahweh will rush upon you,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “will come upon you in power”">i</a></sup>and you will prophesy with them; and you will turn into ⸤a different person⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “another man”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Joshua 1:5; Isaiah 7:14"> When these signs come to you, do for yourself what your hand finds to do, for God will be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:14–15; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 13:8–15"> Then you will go down before me to Gilgal. Look, I am coming down to you to offer burnt offerings ⸤and to make⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to sacrifice sacrifices of”">k</a></sup>fellowship offerings. You must wait seven days until I come to you. Then I will let you know what you should do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 38:7–8; 1 Samuel 10:2–6; Judges 7:11"> ⸤Just as he turned⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “And it happened as he turned”">l</a></sup>his shoulder to depart from Samuel, God ⸤changed his⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “gave for him another”">m</a></sup>heart. And all these signs were fulfilled on that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5–6; 1 Samuel 19:20–24"> When they went from there to Gibeah, a procession of prophets met him, and the Spirit of God rushed upon him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “came upon him in power”">n</a></sup>and he prophesied among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:24; John 7:15; Matthew 13:54–55"> ⸤And when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “and it happened”">o</a></sup>all who knew him ⸤formerly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “three days from yesterday”">p</a></sup>saw that he prophesied with prophets, the people said to one another, “What is this that has happened to the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45; James 1:17"> And a man from there responded and said, “And who is their father?” Therefore it became a proverb: “Is Saul also among the prophets?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When he finished prophesying, he went to the high place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:50; 2 Kings 5:25; 1 Samuel 9:3–10"> Then Saul’s uncle said to him and to his servant, “Where did you go?” And he said, “To search for the female donkeys; and when we saw none, we went to Samuel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So Saul’s uncle said to him, “Please tell me, what did Samuel say to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; Judges 14:6; 1 Samuel 9:20"> Then Saul said to his uncle, “He told us for certain that the female donkeys had been found.” But he did not tell him about the matter of the kingship of which Samuel had spoken.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 1 Samuel 7:5–6"> Then Samuel summoned the people to Yahweh at Mizpah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:8–9; Nehemiah 9:27–28; Judges 2:1"> and he said to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>“Thus says Yahweh the God of Israel: ‘I brought Israel up from Egypt, and I delivered you from the hand of the Egyptians and from the hand of all the kingdoms that were oppressing you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 8:19; Joshua 24:1"> But you today have rejected your God who always delivers you from all of your calamities and your distresses. You have said to him, ‘No, but you must appoint a king over us!’ So then present yourselves before Yahweh by your tribes and by your clans.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 7:16–18; Acts 1:24–26; 1 Samuel 14:41"> So Samuel brought near all the tribes of Israel, and the tribe of Benjamin was selected by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then he brought near the tribe of Benjamin according to its families, and the family of Matri was selected by lot. Then Saul the son of Kish was chosen, and they sought him, but he could not be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 23:2–4"> So they inquired again of Yahweh, “⸤Did the man come here⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “Did he come still here a man”">r</a></sup>And Yahweh said, “Look, he is hiding himself among the baggage.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Samuel 17:4"> So they ran and took him from there, and when he took his stand among the people, he was taller than all the people from his shoulders and up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 1 Kings 1:39; 2 Samuel 21:6"> Then Samuel said to all the people, “Do you see him whom Yahweh has chosen? For there is no one like him among all the people!” And all the people shouted and said, “Long live the king!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–18; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; Romans 13:1–7"> Then Samuel told the people the custom of the kingship, and he wrote the rules down on a scroll and laid it before Yahweh. Then Samuel sent all the people away, each to his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; Joshua 18:28; 1 Samuel 15:34"> And Saul also went to his house at Gibeah, and the troops whose hearts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “heart”">s</a></sup>God had touched went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:13; 2 Chronicles 17:5; 1 Kings 10:25"> However, some ⸤worthless men⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons of wickedness”">t</a></sup>said, “How can this man deliver us?” So they despised him and brought no gift to him, but he kept silent.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="The Dead Sea Scrolls contained a nearly complete scroll of 1 and 2 Samuel, the oldest Hebrew manuscript extant. There is a story therein that provides a setting for the acts of Nahash in 1 Samuel 11, which otherwise seems to occur obtrusively. This story may be translated: “Now Nahash, king of the Ammonites, harshly tormented the Gadites and the Reubenites, and he gouged out all their right eyes, and struck terror and dread in Israel. No Israelite beyond the Jordan remained whose right eye was not gouged out by Nahash king of the Ammonites, except for seven thousand men who had fled from the Ammonites and entered Jabesh Gilead. About a month later …” This early text leaves off with 11:1 at this point">u</a></sup></span>
