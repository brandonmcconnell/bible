## The Ark of the Covenant among the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:12; 1 Samuel 4:1; Joshua 13:3"> Now the Philistines had captured<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “taken”">a</a></sup>the ark of God and brought it from Ebenezer to Ashdod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 16:23; Daniel 5:2; 1 Chronicles 10:10"> Then the Philistines took the ark of God and brought it to the temple of Dagon and placed it beside Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 19:1; Isaiah 46:1–2; Isaiah 46:7"> When the Ashdodites got up early the next morning, ⸤there was⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “look”">b</a></sup>Dagon fallen with his face to the ground before the ark of Yahweh! So they took Dagon and returned him to his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 1:7; Isaiah 2:18–19; Jeremiah 10:11"> When they got up early in the morning the next day, ⸤there was⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “look”">c</a></sup>Dagon fallen again with his face to the ground before the ark of Yahweh! The head of Dagon and the palms of his two hands were cut off, lying at<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “to”">d</a></sup>the threshold; only the body<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The phrase may suggest that only the “fish” part of Dagon was left">e</a></sup>of Dagon was left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:9; Psalm 115:4–7; Joshua 5:15"> (Therefore the priests of Dagon and all who come into the house of Dagon do not tread on the threshold of Dagon in Ashdod until this very day.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; Acts 13:11; Exodus 9:3"> Now the hand of Yahweh was heavy against the Ashdodites and he destroyed them and struck them with tumors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Masoretic Hebrew text (Kethib ) reads “boils”; the reading tradition (Qere) has “tumors”">f</a></sup>both in Ashdod and its territories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Jeremiah 48:7; 1 Chronicles 15:13"> The men of Ashdod saw that it was so, and they said, “The ark of the God of Israel should not remain with us, because his hand is harsh on us and on Dagon our god!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:11; 1 Samuel 17:4; Zechariah 12:3"> So they sent and gathered all the rulers of the Philistines to them, and they asked, “What shall we do with the ark of the God of Israel?” And they said, “Let the ark of the God of Israel be brought around toward Gath.” So they moved the ark of the God of Israel to Gath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; 1 Samuel 7:13; Deuteronomy 2:15"> After they moved it, the hand of Yahweh was against the city, causing a very great confusion, and he struck the men of the city ⸤from the youngest to the oldest⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from small up to great”">g</a></sup>causing tumors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The Masoretic Hebrew text (Kethib ) reads “boils”; the reading tradition (Qere) has “tumors”">h</a></sup>to break out on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Judges 1:18; Joshua 15:45"> So they sent the ark of God to Ekron. But when the ark of God came to Ekron, the Ekronites cried out, saying, “They have brought around the ark of the God of Israel to kill us and our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “me and my”">i</a></sup>people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:6; Jeremiah 48:42–44; Isaiah 13:7–9"> So they sent and gathered all the rulers of the Philistines, and they said, “Send away the ark of the God of Israel and let it return to its place, so that it will not kill us and our people.” For a ⸤deadly confusion⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a confusion of death”; indicates a genitive of attribute">j</a></sup>was throughout all the city; the hand of God was very heavy there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 12:30; Amos 5:19; 1 Kings 19:17"> The men who did not die were struck with the tumors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="The Masoretic Hebrew text (Kethib ) reads “boils”; the reading tradition (Qere) has “tumors”">k</a></sup>so that the cry of the city for help went up to heaven.</span>
</div>
</div>
