## David Encounters the Priests of Nob

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 2:26; Nehemiah 11:32; Isaiah 10:32"> Now David came to Nob, to Ahimelech the priest. And Ahimelech came trembling to meet David, and he said to him, “Why are you alone and there are no men with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 2:12; Colossians 3:9; 1 Samuel 22:22"> So David said to Ahimelech the priest, “The king charged me with a matter and said to me, ‘No one must know anything about this matter on which I am sending you, with which I have charged you and the servants.’ ” So ⸤I have arranged to meet with my servants at a certain place⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “I have made an appointment with my servants/young men to a place, a certain one ”">a</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:9; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> David answered the priest and said to him, “Indeed, women were held back from us ⸤as it has been when I’ve gone out before⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “as yesterday three days ago my going out”">e</a></sup>And the things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or possibly “equipment” or “weapons”">f</a></sup>of the young men are holy when<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “and”">g</a></sup>it is an ordinary journey. ⸤How much more⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “And even that”">h</a></sup>⸤today⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the day”">i</a></sup>will the things<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or possibly “equipment” or “weapons”">j</a></sup>be holy?”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:9; Psalm 52:1–9; 1 Chronicles 27:29"> Now there was a man from the servants of Saul on that day, detained before Yahweh, whose name was Doeg the Edomite, the chief of Saul’s shepherds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> David asked Ahimelech, “Is there not ⸤at your disposal⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “under your hand”">k</a></sup>a spear or a sword? <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:2; 1 Samuel 17:50–54; Exodus 28:6–14"> So the priest said, “The sword of Goliath the Philistine whom you killed in the valley of Elah is here, wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod. If you want to take it for yourself, then take it, for there is no other except it here.” And David said, “There is none like it; give it to me.”</span>

## David Flees to Achish in Gath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:21; 1 Kings 19:3; 1 Samuel 27:1–2"> So David got up and fled on that day from the presence of Saul, and he came to Achish the king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:5; 1 Samuel 18:7–8; 1 Samuel 16:1"> The servants of Achish said to him, “Is not this David the king of the land? Is it not for this one that they sang in the dances, saying, ‘Saul killed his thousands, but David his ten thousands?’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Psalm 56:3; Psalm 34:4"> ⸤David took these words seriously⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “David put these words in his heart”">l</a></sup>and ⸤felt severely threatened by⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “he was very afraid from the face of”">m</a></sup>Achish the king of Gath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Ecclesiastes 7:7; Psalm 34:1–22"> So he changed his behavior ⸤before them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “in their eyes”">n</a></sup>and pretended to be mad ⸤in their presence⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “in their hands”">o</a></sup>He made scratches on the doors of the gate and let his saliva run down into his beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:7"> Then Achish said to his servants, “Look, you see a madman! Why did you bring him to me?</span>
