## The Faithfulness of Three Young Israelites and God’s Deliverance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:19; Jeremiah 16:20; Isaiah 46:6"> Nebuchadnezzar the king made a statue of gold. Its height was sixty cubits and its width was six cubits; he set it up in the valley of Dura in the province of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 3:27; Proverbs 29:12; 1 Kings 12:32"> Then Nebuchadnezzar the king sent directions to assemble the satraps, the prefects, and the governors, the counselors, the treasurers, the judges, the magistrates and all of the officials of the provinces to come to the dedication of the statue that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Revelation 17:13; Revelation 13:13–16"> Then the satraps, the prefects, the governors, the counselors, the treasurers, the judges, the magistrates, and all the officials of the province were assembled for the dedication of the statue that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up, and were standing ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to the front”">a</a></sup>the statue that Nebuchadnezzar had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 4:1; Daniel 6:25; Micah 6:16"> Then the herald proclaimed ⸤aloud⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “with power”">b</a></sup>“To you ⸤it is commanded⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “they are saying”">c</a></sup>O peoples, ⸤nations and people of all languages⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the nations and the languages”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 3:15; Daniel 3:10; Daniel 3:7"> that at the time that you hear the sound of the horn, the flute, the lyre, the trigon, the harp, the drum and all kinds of music, you must fall down and you must worship the statue of gold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Aramaic “the gold”">e</a></sup>that Nebuchadnezzar the king has set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Matthew 13:42; Jeremiah 29:22"> And ⸤whoever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “who that”">f</a></sup>does not fall down and worship will be thrown ⸤immediately⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the time”">g</a></sup>into the midst of the furnace of blazing fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 17:8; Revelation 13:3; Revelation 13:8"> ⸤Therefore⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “All of because this”">h</a></sup>⸤at that time⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “at the time”">i</a></sup>⸤as soon as⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “as that”">j</a></sup>all the peoples heard the sound of the horn, the flute, the lyre, the trigon, the harp and all kinds of music, all the peoples, ⸤the nations and people of all languages⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the nations and the languages”">k</a></sup>were falling down and were worshiping the statue of gold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Aramaic “the gold”">l</a></sup>that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:12–16; Daniel 6:12–13; Esther 3:8–9"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “All because of this”">m</a></sup>⸤at this time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “at it the time”">n</a></sup>some ⸤astrologers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “Chaldean men”">o</a></sup>came forward and ⸤they denounced the Jews⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “they ate their pieces of the Jews”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:10; Daniel 6:6; Daniel 6:21"> ⸤They responded and said⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “they answered and were saying”">q</a></sup>to Nebuchadnezzar the king, “O king, may you live ⸤forever⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to eternity”">r</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 3:4–7; Daniel 6:12; Esther 3:12–14"> You, O king, have made a decree that ⸤everyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “all of humanity”">s</a></sup>who hears the sound of the horn, the flute, the lyre, the trigon, the harp and the drum and all kinds of music, he shall fall down and shall worship the statue of gold.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Aramaic “the gold”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> ⸤And whoever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “And who that”">u</a></sup>⸤does not fall down⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “not he falls down”">v</a></sup>and worship shall be thrown into the midst of the furnace of blazing fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 6:13; Daniel 2:49; Acts 17:7"> However there are Judean men whom you have appointed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Aramaic “whom you have appointed them”">w</a></sup>over the affairs of the province of Babylon—Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego—these men, O king, ⸤pay you no heed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “not they pay heed to you”">x</a></sup>and were not serving your god, and the statue of gold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Aramaic “the gold”">y</a></sup>that you set up they are not worshiping.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; Daniel 2:12; Esther 3:5–6"> Then Nebuchadnezzar said in rage and anger to bring in Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego; then they brought in these men before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:2; Daniel 3:1; Isaiah 46:1"> Nebuchadnezzar answered and said to them, “Is it true, Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, that you are not serving my god, and you are not worshiping the statue of gold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Aramaic “the gold”">z</a></sup>that I have set up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 5:29; 2 Chronicles 32:15–17; Exodus 5:2"> Now if you are ready so that ⸤when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “at that time”">aa</a></sup>you hear the sound of the horn, the flute, the lyre, the trigon, the harp and the drum and all kinds of music, you fall down and you worship the statue that I have made, that will be good. But if you do not worship it, ⸤immediately⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “at that very time”">ab</a></sup>you will be thrown into the midst of the furnace of blazing fire, and ⸤who is the god⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “who he god”">ac</a></sup>who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “that”">ad</a></sup>will rescue you from my hands?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 1:7; Daniel 3:12"> Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego answered and said to the king, Nebuchadnezzar, “We have no need on this matter ⸤to present a defense to you⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “to return to you”">ae</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Luke 1:37; Hebrews 7:25"> ⸤If it is so⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “If there is”">af</a></sup>our God, whom we serve, is able to rescue us from the furnace of blazing fire. And from your hand, O king, let him rescue us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Matthew 10:39; Revelation 2:10–11"> And if not, let it be known to you, O king, that ⸤we will not serve your gods⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “your gods not there is us serving”">ag</a></sup>and the statue of gold<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Aramaic “the gold”">ah</a></sup>that you have set up we will not worship.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Wrath of the King and His Fiery Furnace of Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 3:13; Leviticus 26:24; Proverbs 21:24"> Then Nebuchadnezzar was filled with anger and the image of his face was changed ⸤toward⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “against”">ai</a></sup>Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, so he ⸤ordered and said⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “answering and saying”">aj</a></sup>to heat up the one furnace seven times what was usual to heat it up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:23; Acts 16:25; Daniel 3:15"> And he commanded ⸤the strongest men of the guards⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “to the strong men, men of strength”">ak</a></sup>who were in his army to bind Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego and to throw them into the furnace of blazing fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then these men were bound with their garments, their trousers and their turbans and their other clothing, and they were thrown into the midst of the furnace of blazing fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Proverbs 21:18; Proverbs 11:8"> ⸤Therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “because of this”">al</a></sup>⸤because⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “from that”">am</a></sup>the word of the king was severe and the furnace was exceedingly hot, the flame of the fire killed these men who lifted up Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 6:16–17; Jeremiah 38:6; Psalm 34:19"> But these men, the three of them, Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, fell down into the midst of the furnace of blazing fire, and they were bound.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:7; Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:9–10"> Then Nebuchadnezzar the king was astonished, and he rose up in haste and he asked, saying to his advisers, “Did we not throw three men, bound, into the midst of the fire? They answered, saying to the king, “Certainly, O king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Acts 28:5; Mark 16:18"> ⸤He answered⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “was answering”">an</a></sup>saying, “Look, I see four men unbound walking in the midst of the fire and there is no damage to them, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Aramaic “its”">ao</a></sup>appearance of the fourth man resembles the son of a god.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 3:17; Isaiah 52:12; Revelation 19:5"> Then Nebuchadnezzar approached the door of the furnace of blazing fire, and he called out, saying, “Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, ⸤servants⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “his servant”">ap</a></sup>of the Most High God, come out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “Go out”">aq</a></sup>and come here!” So then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego came out from the middle of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Hebrews 11:34; Isaiah 26:11"> And the satraps, the prefects, the governors and the advisors of the king were assembling, and they saw these men, that the fire had no power over their bodies, and the hair of their heads was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Aramaic plural">ar</a></sup>not singed, and their garments were not harmed, and the smell of fire did not come from them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The King’s Praise for the Great Deliverance of the Three Young Israelites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 14:7–8; Psalm 34:7–8; Psalm 84:11–12"> Nebuchadnezzar ⸤responded⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “answering”">as</a></sup>saying, “Blessed be their God, the God of Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, who sent his angel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “messenger”">at</a></sup>and rescued his servants who trusted in him, and the command of the king they disobeyed, and they gave their bodies so that they did not serve and did not worship any god except their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:5; Psalm 76:10; Deuteronomy 32:31"> And from me ⸤is set forth⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “setting out”">au</a></sup>a decree that any people, nation, or language that may utter criticism against their God—the God of Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego—will be broken into pieces and their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Aramaic “his”">av</a></sup>house will be made like ruins. ⸤For⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “All of because”">aw</a></sup>there is not another God who is able to rescue like this God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 2:49; 1 Samuel 2:30; Daniel 3:12"> Then the king promoted Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego in the province of Babylon.</span>
</div>
