## The Vision of a Small Horn that Oppresses Israel and Threatens the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 11:4; Daniel 9:2; Daniel 7:28"> In the third year of the kingdom of Belshazzar the king, a vision appeared ⸤to me, Daniel⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “I Daniel”">a</a></sup>after the one that appeared to me ⸤previously⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in the beginning”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 1:2; Nehemiah 1:1; Genesis 10:22"> And I saw in the vision, ⸤and when I saw⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “and it was at my seeing”">c</a></sup>I was in Susa, the citadel that was in the province of Elam. And I saw in the vision, and ⸤I myself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “I, I was”">d</a></sup>was at the stream of Ulai.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “Ulai Canal” (NASB); “stream of the Ulai” (NEB)">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 8:20; Daniel 10:5; Zechariah 6:1"> And I lifted up my eyes and I saw, and look! A ram<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “One ram”">f</a></sup>standing ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">g</a></sup>the stream, and ⸤it had⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “for it”">h</a></sup>two horns, and the horns were long, but the one was longer than the second, and the longer one came up ⸤after the other one⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “in the behind of the other”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 11:16; Daniel 11:36; Daniel 8:7"> I saw the ram charging westward and northward and southward, and ⸤none of the beasts stood before it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “all the beasts not they withstood to the face of him”">j</a></sup>and there was no rescuing from ⸤its power⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “its hand”">k</a></sup>and it did ⸤what it wanted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “according to its will”">l</a></sup>and it became strong.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “it/he magnified itself/himself”">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 8:21; Daniel 11:3; Daniel 8:8"> And I was considering this, and look, a he-goat coming from the west across the face of the whole earth, and it was not touching the ground;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “earth,” or “land”">n</a></sup>and the he-goat had ⸤a conspicuous horn⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “a horn of vision”">o</a></sup>between its eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then it came toward the ram ⸤that had the two horns⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the owner of the two horns”">p</a></sup>that I saw standing ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">q</a></sup>the stream, and it ran at it with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “in”">r</a></sup>the rage of its power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:7; Daniel 11:11; Joshua 8:20"> And I saw it approaching the ram and it was furious at it, and it struck the ram, and it broke its two horns, and ⸤there was not strength in the ram to stand before him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “and not was strength in the ram to withstand to the face of him”">s</a></sup>and he threw it down to the ground and trampled it, ⸤and there was no one who could rescue the ram⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “and not he/it was one rescuing”">t</a></sup>from its power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 7:2; Revelation 7:1; Daniel 5:20"> And the he-goat grew ⸤exceedingly great⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “until much”">u</a></sup>and ⸤at the height of its power⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “and at his being powerful”">v</a></sup>the great horn was broken, and four conspicuous horns<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="LXX reads “others” here for “conspicuous.” The words in Hebrew can be confused for each other">w</a></sup>came up in place of it toward the four winds of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 11:16; Ezekiel 20:6; Ezekiel 20:15"> ⸤And from one of them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “And from the one from them”">x</a></sup>came forth a horn, ⸤a little one⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “one from little”">y</a></sup>and it grew exceedingly<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “pre-eminently”">z</a></sup>toward the south, and toward the east, and toward the beautiful land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13; Revelation 12:4; Daniel 8:7"> And it grew up to the host of heaven, and it threw down to the ground some of the host and some of the stars and trampled them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:11; Revelation 13:5–7; Daniel 8:25"> Even against the prince of the hosts it acted arrogantly and took away from him the regular burnt offering, and the place of his sanctuary was overthrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:1; Isaiah 59:14; Daniel 11:31–36"> And on account of transgression, the host was given over to the horn in addition to the regular burnt offering. And it cast down truth to the ground, and it ⸤acted⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “did”">aa</a></sup>and it had success.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 11:2; Revelation 6:10; Luke 21:24"> And I heard a certain holy one speaking, and a certain other holy one said to the specific one ⸤who was speaking⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “the one speaking”">ab</a></sup>“⸤For how long⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “Until when”">ac</a></sup>is the vision concerning the regular burnt offering, and the transgression that makes desolate, and the giving over of the sanctuary and the host to trampling?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Daniel 7:25; Revelation 11:2–3"> And he said to me, “For two thousand three hundred evenings and mornings, then the sanctuary will be restored.”</span>
</div>

## Gabriel Gives Angelic Help and Interprets the Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 10:16; Revelation 1:13; 1 Peter 1:10–11"> ⸤And then⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">ad</a></sup>when I, Daniel, saw the vision, and I was seeking understanding, there was one standing before me with the appearance of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Luke 1:26; Daniel 8:2"> And I heard the voice of a human at the Ulai, and he called and said, “Gabriel, make this man understand the vision.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 8:19; Daniel 12:4; Habakkuk 2:3"> And he came beside ⸤where I was standing⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “my standing”">ae</a></sup>and ⸤when he came⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “at his coming”">af</a></sup>I became terrified and I fell prostrate on my face. And he said to me, “Understand, son of man,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">ag</a></sup>that the vision is for the time of the end.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 10:18; Ezekiel 2:2; Daniel 10:16"> And ⸤when he spoke⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “at his speaking”">ah</a></sup>with me I fell into a trance with my face to the ground, and he touched me and made me stand on by feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Daniel 11:27; Revelation 17:17"> And he said, “Look, I am making known to you what will happen in the period of wrath,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “the wrath”">ai</a></sup>for it refers to the appointed time of the end.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 8:3; Daniel 11:1–2"> “The ram that you saw ⸤who had two horns⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “the owner of the two horns”">aj</a></sup>represents the kings of Media and Persia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 10:20; Daniel 11:3; Daniel 8:5–8"> “And the hairy he-goat is the ⸤kingdom⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “king”">ak</a></sup>of Greece, and the great horn that is between his eyes—he is the first king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 11:4; Daniel 8:3; Daniel 8:8"> And the horn that was broken, and then there arose four horns in place of it—these are four kingdoms that will arise from his nation, but not with his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 13:11–14; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Revelation 19:20"> And at the end of their kingdom, when the transgressions are completed, a king will arise, fierce in countenance and skilled in riddles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 8:12; Daniel 11:31–36; Daniel 7:25"> And his power will grow, but not by his own power, and he will cause fearful destruction, and he will succeed and ⸤he will act⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “he will do”">al</a></sup>and he will destroy the mighty and the people of the holy ones.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “saints”">am</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 8:11; Revelation 17:14; Job 34:20"> And by his planning<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “cunning”">an</a></sup>he will make a success of deceit by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “in”">ao</a></sup>his hand, and in his ⸤mind⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “heart”">ap</a></sup>he will ⸤boast⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “magnify himself”">aq</a></sup>and in their ease he will destroy many, and even against the prince of princes he will rise up, and he will be broken, but not by human hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 10:1; Daniel 12:9; Daniel 12:4"> And the vision of the evening and the morning that has been ⸤described⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Literally “told”">ar</a></sup>it is true; and you, seal up the vision, for ⸤it refers to many days to come⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “for to days many”">as</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Habakkuk 3:16; Daniel 8:7"> And I, Daniel, was overcome, and I became ill for some days, and I ⸤performed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “did”">at</a></sup>the business of the king, and I was dismayed over the vision and ⸤I did not understand it⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “there was not understanding”">au</a></sup></span>
