## Daniel’s Vision of a Dazzling Heavenly Messenger

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:28; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 8:26"> In the third year of Cyrus the king of the Persians, a word<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “matter,” or “message”">a</a></sup>was revealed to Daniel, who was called by his name Belteshazzar, and the word<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “matter,” or “message”">b</a></sup>was reliable and it concerned a great tribulation,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “conflict”">c</a></sup>and he understood the word<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “matter,” or “message”">d</a></sup>and ⸤he received understanding⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “and understanding to him”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:4; Daniel 9:24–27; Ezra 9:4–5"> In those days, I, Daniel, I myself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="This is an emphatic use of the first person personal pronoun">f</a></sup>was in mourning ⸤for three whole weeks⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “three weeks of days”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 6:17; Daniel 11:8; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> I had not eaten any choice food, and meat and wine did not enter my mouth, and ⸤I did not use any ointment⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “I have not anointed at all”">h</a></sup>⸤until the end of three whole weeks⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “until being full/complete three weeks of days”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:14; Daniel 8:2; Ezekiel 1:3"> And then on the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I myself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="This is an emphatic use of the first person personal pronoun">j</a></sup>was on the bank of the great river; that is, the Tigris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13–15; Daniel 12:6–7; Ezekiel 9:2"> And I lifted up my eyes and I saw,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “looked”">k</a></sup>and there was a man, and he was dressed in linen, and his waist was girded with the gold of Uphaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 19:12; Revelation 1:13–17; Matthew 17:2"> Now his body was like turquoise,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “yellow jasper” or other gold-colored stones; the exact identity of ancient precious stones is difficult">l</a></sup>and his face was like the appearance of lightning, and his eyes were like torches of fire, and his arms and his legs were like the gleam of polished bronze, and the sound of his words was like the sound of a multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Acts 22:9; Genesis 3:10"> And I saw, I, Daniel ⸤alone⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “to alone me”">m</a></sup>the vision; and the people who were with me ⸤did not see⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “not they saw”">n</a></sup>the vision; nevertheless, a great trembling fell upon them and they fled in order to hide themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Habakkuk 3:16; Daniel 8:27"> And I myself, Daniel, alone saw this great vision, and as a result ⸤no strength was left in me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “not was left in me strength”">o</a></sup>and ⸤my complexion grew deathly pale⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “and complexion my changed over me to destruction”">p</a></sup>and I did not retain any strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; Job 4:13; Genesis 15:12"> And I heard the sound of his words, and ⸤when I heard⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “at my hearing”">q</a></sup>the sound of his words I myself began falling into a trance on my face, ⸤with my face to the ground⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “and my face toward the ground”">r</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Jeremiah 1:9; Daniel 8:18"> And look, a hand touched me and it roused me to my knees<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “on my knees”">s</a></sup>and the palms<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “palm”">t</a></sup>of my hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 26:16; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 8:16–17"> And he said to me, “Daniel, a man beloved, pay attention to the words that I am speaking to you and ⸤stand upright where you are⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “stand on your position”">u</a></sup>for I have now been sent to you.” And ⸤while he was speaking⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “at his speaking”">v</a></sup>with me this word,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Or “matter,” or “message”">w</a></sup>I stood up trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:30–31; Daniel 9:20–23; Daniel 10:19"> And he said to me, “You must not fear, Daniel, for from the first day that you set your heart to understand and to humble yourself ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">x</a></sup>your God, your words were heard, and I myself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="This is an emphatic use of the first person personal pronoun">y</a></sup>have come because of your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Daniel 10:20–21"> But the prince of the kingdom of Persia stood ⸤before me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “to front of me”">z</a></sup>for twenty-one days. And look,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Or “finally”">aa</a></sup>Michael, one of the chief princes, came to assist me, and I left him there beside the king of the Persians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Daniel 2:28; Daniel 12:4"> And I have come<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “I came”">ab</a></sup>to instruct you about what<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Hebrew “that”">ac</a></sup>will happen to your people ⸤in the future⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “at the end of the days”">ad</a></sup>for there is a further vision here ⸤for the future⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “for the days”">ae</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Ezekiel 24:27; Daniel 10:9"> And while he was speaking with me according to these words, I turned my face toward the ground and I was speechless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 6:7; Daniel 8:15; Jeremiah 1:9"> Then look, there was ⸤one in the form of a human⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “as the likeness of a son of man”">af</a></sup>he touched my lips and I opened my mouth and I spoke and I said to the one standing ⸤before me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “to opposite me”">ag</a></sup>“My lord, because of the vision my anxieties fell upon me and I have not retained my strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Exodus 24:10–11; Mark 12:36"> So ⸤how am I, a servant of my lord⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “how is he able a servant of my lord this”">ah</a></sup>to speak with ⸤you, my lord⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “with my lord this”">ai</a></sup>and I ⸤just now⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “from now”">aj</a></sup>⸤have no strength in me⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “not it remains in me strength”">ak</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 10:16; Luke 22:43; Daniel 8:18"> And ⸤he again touched⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “he added to and touched”">al</a></sup>me, ⸤the one in the form of a human⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “like vision of a man”">am</a></sup>and he strengthened me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Joshua 1:9; Judges 6:23"> And he said, “You must not fear, O beloved man. ⸤Peace be to you⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “Peace to/for you”">an</a></sup>be strong and be courageous!” And ⸤when he spoke⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “at his speaking”">ao</a></sup>with me, I was strengthened and I said, “Let my lord speak, for you have strengthened me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 8:21; Daniel 11:2–4; Daniel 8:5–8"> Then he asked, “Do you know why I have come to you? And now I return to fight against the prince of Persia and I myself<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="This is an emphatic use of the first person personal pronoun">ap</a></sup>am going, and look,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “listen”">aq</a></sup>the prince of Javan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="That is, Greece">ar</a></sup>will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 10:13; Daniel 12:1; Revelation 12:7"> However, I will tell you ⸤what is inscribed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “the being inscribed”">as</a></sup>in the book of truth, and there is not one who contends with me against these beings ⸤except⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “but if”">at</a></sup>Michael, your prince.”</span>
</div>
