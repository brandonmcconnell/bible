<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 11:16; Daniel 11:36; Daniel 8:21"> And a mighty king will arise, and he will rule with great dominion, and he will do ⸤as he pleases⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “as his pleasure”">e</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 11:38–39; Daniel 11:19–20; Isaiah 9:14"> And a branch from her roots<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="That is, a member of her family">n</a></sup>will rise up in his place, and he will come against the army and he will enter the stronghold of the king of the north and he will take action against them and he will prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 37:19; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 43:12–13"> And also their gods with their idols and with the ⸤precious vessels⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “vessels of their desire”">o</a></sup>silver and gold he will take to Egypt into captivity, and for years ⸤he will leave the king of the north alone⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “he will stand back from the king of the north”">p</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:42; Daniel 11:7; Daniel 11:26"> “But his sons will wage war and they will gather a multitude of great forces and ⸤he will advance with great force⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “he will come, to come”">r</a></sup>and he will overflow like a flood and he will pass through and he will return, and they will wage war up to his fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 8:7; Daniel 11:5; Psalm 33:16–17"> And the king of the south will become furious, and he will go and he will battle against him, against the king of the north; and he will muster a great multitude, and the multitude will be given into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 12:22–23; 2 Kings 14:10; Isaiah 10:7–12"> When the multitude is carried off, his heart will be exalted and he will overthrow ⸤tens of thousands⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “myriads”">s</a></sup>but he will not prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:16; Daniel 12:7; Daniel 11:6–7"> And the king of the north will again raise a multitude, greater than the former, and ⸤at the end of some years⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “to the end of the times years”">t</a></sup>he will surely come with a great army and with great supplies.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “resources”">u</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Acts 4:25–28"> “And in these times many will rise up against the king of the south, and ⸤the violent ones of your people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the sons of the lawless ones of your people”">v</a></sup>will lift themselves ⸤to fulfill⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “to cause to stand”">w</a></sup>the vision, but they will fall.</span>
