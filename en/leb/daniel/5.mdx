<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 1:2; 2 Kings 24:13; Jeremiah 27:16–22"> ⸤When he tasted the wine⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “At the taste of the wine”">d</a></sup>Belshazzar commanded that they bring the vessels of gold and silver that Nebuchadnezzar his ⸤predecessor⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “father”">e</a></sup>had taken from the temple that was in Jerusalem, so that the king and his lords,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “nobles”">f</a></sup>his wives and his concubines may drink from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then they brought in the vessels of gold that they took from the temple, the house of God that was in Jerusalem, and the king and his lords,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “nobles”">g</a></sup>his wives and his concubines drank from them.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:31; Daniel 5:24–28; Job 20:5"> ⸤Immediately⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “In its the hour”">i</a></sup>⸤human fingers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “fingers of the hand of a man”">j</a></sup>appeared and they wrote ⸤opposite⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “to over against”">k</a></sup>the lampstand on the plaster of the wall of the palace of the king, and the king was watching<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “gazing at”">l</a></sup>the palm of the hand that was writing.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Daniel 5:16; Daniel 2:48"> The king cried ⸤aloud⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “with strength”">o</a></sup>to bring in the conjurers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “enchanters”">p</a></sup>the ⸤astrologers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “Chaldeans”">q</a></sup>and the diviners; the king ⸤spoke⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “answered”">r</a></sup>and said to the wise men of Babylon, “Any man that can read this writing and can tell me its explanation will be clothed in purple and will have a necklace of gold hung around his neck and he will rule as third in authority in the kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:27; Daniel 4:7; Genesis 41:8"> Then all the wise men of the king came in, but they were not able to read the writing or to make known its explanation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Aramaic “the explanation/ interpretation”">s</a></sup></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:8–9; Daniel 2:47–48; Daniel 4:18"> There is a man in your kingdom ⸤who has the spirit of the holy gods in him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “who the spirit of holy gods is in him”">aa</a></sup>And in the days of your ⸤predecessor⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “father”">ab</a></sup>enlightenment and insight and wisdom like the wisdom of the gods was found in him; and, O king, Nebuchadnezzar your ⸤predecessor⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “father”">ac</a></sup>appointed him as chief of the magicians,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “soothsayer-priests”">ad</a></sup>the conjurers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “enchanters”">ae</a></sup>the ⸤astrologers⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “Chaldeans”">af</a></sup>and the diviners. Your ⸤predecessor⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “father”">ag</a></sup>the king did this</span>

## Daniel Deciphers and Interprets the Writing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 6:13; Daniel 5:11; Daniel 2:25"> Then Daniel was brought in before the king, and the king ⸤spoke⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “answered”">ak</a></sup>and said to Daniel, “You are Daniel ⸤who are one of the exiles⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “who from sons of the exile”">al</a></sup>of Judah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Aramaic “Jehud”">am</a></sup>whom my ⸤predecessor⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “father”">an</a></sup>the king, brought from Judah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Aramaic “Jehud”">ao</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12"> And I have heard that a spirit of the gods is in you and enlightenment and insight and excellent wisdom was found in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:7–8; Isaiah 47:12; Isaiah 29:10–12"> And now the wise men and the conjurers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “enchanters”">ap</a></sup>were brought in before me so that they could read this writing in order to make its explanation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “interpretation”">aq</a></sup>known to me, but they were not able to disclose the explanation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “interpretation”">ar</a></sup>of the matter.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 5:16; Genesis 14:23; 2 Kings 5:26"> Then Daniel answered and said before the king, “Let your gifts be for yourself or your rewards give to another; nevertheless, I will read the writing to the king and I will make known to him the explanation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Or “interpretation”">au</a></sup></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:23; Exodus 10:3; James 4:17"> “But you his ⸤successor⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="Literally “son”">bf</a></sup>Belshazzar, have not humbled your heart ⸤even though⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="Literally “all of because that”">bg</a></sup>you knew all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 12:10; Daniel 4:37; Jeremiah 10:23"> And now you have exalted yourself against the Lord of heaven, and the vessels of his temple you have brought in before you, and you and your lords,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Or “nobles”">bh</a></sup>your wives and your concubines, have been drinking wine from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bi" id="fnref-bi" data-fn="Aramaic “with”">bi</a></sup>them, and you have praised the gods of silver, gold, bronze, iron, wood, and stone<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bj" id="fnref-bj" data-fn="All the materials in this list have the definite article that does not have to be translated into English">bj</a></sup>that do not see and do not hear and do not know, but the God ⸤who holds your life in his hand⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bk" id="fnref-bk" data-fn="Literally “who your breath is in his hand”">bk</a></sup>and all of your ways ⸤come from him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bl" id="fnref-bl" data-fn="Literally “belong to him”">bl</a></sup>you have not honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 5:5"> So then the palm of the hand was sent out from his presence and this writing was inscribed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> “Now this was the writing that was inscribed: ‘Mene, Mene, Tekel and Parsin.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:11–12; Isaiah 47:1–15; Jeremiah 27:7"> “This is the explanation of the matter:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bm" id="fnref-bm" data-fn="Or “word, message”">bm</a></sup>‘Mene’—God has numbered your kingdom and brought an end to it.</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 13:17; Daniel 6:28; Daniel 5:31"> “ ‘Peres’—your kingdom has been divided and given to the Medes and Persians.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 5:16; Daniel 5:7"> Then Belshazzar commanded, and they clothed Daniel with purple and placed a necklace of gold around his neck, and they made a proclamation concerning him that he would be the third ruler in authority in the kingdom.</span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 9:1; Daniel 6:1"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bp" id="fnref-bp" data-fn="Daniel 5:31–6:28 in the English Bible is 6:1–29 in the Hebrew Bible">bp</a></sup>And Darius the Mede received the kingdom ⸤when he was about sixty-two years old⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bq" id="fnref-bq" data-fn="Literally “when a son of sixty and two years”">bq</a></sup></span>
