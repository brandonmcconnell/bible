## Daniel’s Vision of Four Beasts and the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:17; Amos 3:7; Daniel 5:1"> In the first year of Belshazzar the king of Babylon, Daniel saw a dream and visions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “the visions”">a</a></sup>of his head as he lay on his bed; then he wrote down the dream and the ⸤summary⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “head”">b</a></sup>of the words ⸤as follows⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “saying”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 7:1; Revelation 17:15"> Daniel ⸤explained⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “answered”">d</a></sup>and said, “I was looking in my vision in the night, and look, the four winds of heaven were stirring up the great sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Daniel 7:17; Daniel 2:32–33"> And four great beasts were coming up from the sea, differing ⸤from one another⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “this from this”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Isaiah 14:13–17; Ezekiel 17:3"> The first was like a lion and ⸤had⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “for him/it”">f</a></sup>the wings of an eagle. I was watching until its wings were plucked off, and it was lifted up from the earth and it was raised on its feet like a human, and a human heart was given to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 2:39; Isaiah 13:17–18; Proverbs 17:12"> And look, another beast, a second one, looking like a bear. And it was raised up on one side and three tusks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “ribs”">g</a></sup>were in its mouth between its teeth, and so ⸤it was told⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “they were saying”">h</a></sup>“Arise, eat much flesh!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:2; Daniel 8:20–22; Daniel 8:5–8"> After this I was watching and look, another beast like a leopard; ⸤it had four wings⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and there were to it four wings”">i</a></sup>of a bird on its back, and ⸤the beast had⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to the beast”">j</a></sup>four heads, and dominion was given to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Revelation 12:3; Revelation 17:12"> After this in the visions of the night I was looking and there was a fourth beast, terrifying and frightful and exceedingly strong, and ⸤it had⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “were to it”">k</a></sup>⸤great iron teeth⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “teeth of iron for it great”">l</a></sup>and it was devouring and crushing, and it stamped the remainder<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “residue”">m</a></sup>with its feet; and it was different from all the other beasts ⸤that preceded it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “that preceding her/it”">n</a></sup>⸤and it had ten horns⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “and ten horns for her/it”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 13:5–6; Revelation 13:1; Psalm 12:3"> I was considering the horns, and look, another little horn came up ⸤among⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “between”">p</a></sup>them, and three of the earlier horns ⸤were rooted out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “dehorned”">q</a></sup>from before it, and there were eyes like the eyes of a human<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “humankind”">r</a></sup>in this horn and also a mouth that was speaking boastfully.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:14; Mark 9:3; Daniel 7:13"> “⸤I continued watching⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “I was watching”">s</a></sup>until thrones were placed and an Ancient of Days sat; his clothing was like white snow and the hair of his head was like pure wool and his throne was a flame<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “ablaze with”">t</a></sup>of fire and its wheels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Aramaic “wheel”">u</a></sup>were burning fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:27; Deuteronomy 33:2; Revelation 20:11–15"> A stream of fire issued forth and flowed ⸤from his presence⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “from before him”">v</a></sup>thousands upon thousands served him and ten thousand upon ten thousand stood before him. The judge sat, and the books were opened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Revelation 20:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> “⸤I continued watching⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “I was watching”">w</a></sup>then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Aramaic “in then”">x</a></sup>because of the noise of the boastful words of the horn who was speaking; ⸤I continued watching⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “I was watching”">y</a></sup>until the beast was slain and its body was destroyed, and it was given over to burning with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 8:7; Daniel 7:4–6"> And as for the remainder of the beasts, ⸤their dominion was taken away⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “they took away their dominion”">z</a></sup>but a prolongation ⸤of their life⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “in life”">aa</a></sup>was given to them for a season and a time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Revelation 1:7; Luke 21:27"> “⸤I continued watching⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “I was watching”">ab</a></sup>in the visions of the night, and look, with the clouds of heaven one like a son of man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “mortal,” or “son of humankind”">ac</a></sup>was coming, and he came to the Ancient of Days, and ⸤was presented⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “they presented him”">ad</a></sup>before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27; John 3:35"> And to him was given dominion and glory and kingship that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Aramaic “and”">ae</a></sup>all the peoples, the nations, and languages would serve him; his dominion is a dominion ⸤without end⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “of eternity”">af</a></sup>that will not cease, and his kingdom is one that will not be destroyed.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Daniel Explains and Interprets the Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Jeremiah 17:16; Genesis 40:7–8"> “⸤As for me, Daniel⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “I Daniel”">ag</a></sup>my spirit was troubled ⸤within me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “in the midst of sheath”">ah</a></sup>and the visions of my head terrified me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:13–14; Zechariah 2:3; Revelation 5:5"> So I approached one of the attendants and I asked him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Aramaic “from him”">ai</a></sup>about the truth concerning all this; and he told me<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Aramaic “to me”">aj</a></sup>that he would make known to me the explanation of the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 8:19–22; Daniel 2:37–40; John 18:36"> ‘These great beasts which are four in number are four kings who will arise from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Isaiah 60:12–14; Revelation 5:10"> But the holy ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “saints”">ak</a></sup>of the Most High will receive the kingdom, and they will take possession of the kingdom forever, ⸤forever and ever⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “forever and to forever”">al</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:7; Daniel 2:40–43"> “Then I desired to make certain concerning the fourth beast that was different from ⸤all the others⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “all of them”">am</a></sup>—exceedingly terrifying, with its iron teeth and its claws of bronze; it devoured and crushed and stamped the remainder with its feet—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 7:23; Daniel 7:8; Daniel 7:11"> and concerning the horns that were on its head, and concerning the other horn that came up and ⸤before which three horns fell⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “and fell from before it three”">an</a></sup>and this horn ⸤had eyes⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “and eyes to it”">ao</a></sup>and a mouth speaking ⸤boastfully⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “abundantly”">ap</a></sup>and its appearance was larger than its companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 17:14; Daniel 8:24"> ⸤I continued watching⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “I was watching”">aq</a></sup>and this horn made war with the holy ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “saints”">ar</a></sup>and it prevailed over them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4; Revelation 5:10"> until the Ancient of Days came and gave judgment to the holy ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Or “saints”">as</a></sup>of the Most High; and the time arrived and the holy ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Or “saints”">at</a></sup>took possession of the kingdom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 2:40; Daniel 7:7; Luke 2:1"> “And he said, ‘The fourth beast is the fourth kingdom that will be on the earth that will be different from all the other kingdoms, and it will devour the whole earth and it will trample it and it will crush it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:20; Daniel 11:36; Revelation 13:1"> And as for the ten horns coming from it, from this kingdom<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Or “the kingdom”">au</a></sup>ten kings will arise, and another will arise after them. And he will be different from the earlier ones, and he will subdue three kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 12:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> And he will speak words against the Most High, and he will wear out the holy ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Or “saints”">av</a></sup>of the Most High, and he will attempt to change times and law, and they will be given into his hand for a time and two times and half a time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 20:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 11:13"> Then the court will sit, and his dominion will be removed,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Or “taken away”">aw</a></sup>to be eradicated and to be destroyed ⸤totally⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Literally “until the end”">ax</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalm 72:11; Luke 1:33; Revelation 11:15"> And the kingdom and the dominion and the greatness of the kingdoms under the whole heaven will be given to the nation of the holy ones<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Or “saints”">ay</a></sup>of the Most High; his kingdom ⸤is an everlasting kingdom⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Literally “is a kingdom of eternity”">az</a></sup>and all the dominions will serve and obey him.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51; Daniel 10:8"> ⸤This is the end of the account⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Literally “Up to here is the end of the account”">ba</a></sup>⸤As for me, Daniel⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Literally “I Daniel”">bb</a></sup>—my thoughts terrified me greatly and my face changed over me, but I kept the matter in my heart.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Or “to myself”">bc</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
