## The Time of the Judgment and the Resurrection of Two Groups

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:7; Matthew 24:21; Mark 13:19"> “Now at that time, Michael, the great prince, will arise, ⸤the protector⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the one who stands with”">a</a></sup>over the sons of your people, and it will be a time of distress that has not been since your people ⸤have been⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to be” or “being”">b</a></sup>a nation until that time. And at that time your people will escape, ⸤everyone who is found⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “all of the being found”">c</a></sup>written in the scroll.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “book”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Isaiah 26:19; Matthew 25:46"> And many from those sleeping ⸤in the dusty ground⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “of the ground of dust”">e</a></sup>will awake, some to ⸤everlasting life⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “life of eternity”">f</a></sup>and some to disgrace and ⸤everlasting contempt⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “contempt of eternity”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Proverbs 4:18; James 5:19–20"> But the ones having insight will shine like the brightness of the expanse, and the ones providing justice for the many<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “leading the many in righteousness”">h</a></sup>will be like the stars ⸤forever and ever⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “to eternity and ever”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:9; Revelation 22:10; Daniel 8:26"> But you, Daniel, keep the words secret and seal the scroll<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “book”">j</a></sup>until the time of the end; many will run back and forth and knowledge will increase.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 10:4–6; Daniel 10:16; Daniel 10:10"> Then I looked, I myself, Daniel, and look, there were two others standing: ⸤one on this bank of the stream and one on the other⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “one here on the bank of the stream and one there on the bank of the stream”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 8:13; Ezekiel 9:2; Zechariah 1:12–13"> Then he said to the man who was clothed in linen who was above<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “from above”">l</a></sup>the water of the stream,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “river”">m</a></sup>“⸤How long until⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “Until when”">n</a></sup>the end of the wonders?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:25; Revelation 12:14; Daniel 8:24"> And I heard the man who was clothed in linen who was above<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “from above”">o</a></sup>the water of the stream,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “river”">p</a></sup>and he raised his right hand and his left hand to heaven and he swore ⸤by the one who lives forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “by the life of the eternity”">q</a></sup>that an appointed time, appointed times, and half an appointed time would pass when the shattering of ⸤the power of the holy people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the power of the people of holiness”">r</a></sup>⸤would be completed⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “to come to an end”">s</a></sup>then all these things will be accomplished.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Daniel Seeks Additional Insights

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:7; 1 Peter 1:11; Daniel 10:14"> Now I myself heard, but I did not understand, and I said, “My lord, what will be the outcome of these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Daniel 8:26; Revelation 10:4"> And he said, “Go, Daniel, for the words are secret and are sealed up until the time of the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:11; Daniel 11:35; Hosea 14:9"> Many will be purified and will be cleansed and will be refined, but the wicked will act wickedly ⸤and none of the wicked will understand⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “and not will understand all the wicked”">t</a></sup>but ⸤those who have insight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “the ones having insight”">u</a></sup>will understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 11:31; Matthew 24:15; Mark 13:14"> And from the time the regular burnt offering is removed and the abomination that causes desolation ⸤is set up⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “to be set up” or “to put in place”">v</a></sup>there will be one thousand two hundred and ninety days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Daniel 8:14; Romans 11:15"> Happy is the one who is persevering, and attains to the one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; Zechariah 3:7"> But you, go on to the end and rest, and you will arise for your allotted inheritance at the end of the days.”</span>
</div>
