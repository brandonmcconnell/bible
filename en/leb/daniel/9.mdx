## Daniel’s Prayer for His People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 11:1; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 5:31–1"> In the first year of Darius, the son of Ahasuerus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, Xerxes">a</a></sup>from the offspring of the Medes, who became king over the kingdom of the Chaldeans—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Jeremiah 25:11–12; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> in the first year of his kingship I, Daniel, observed in the scrolls the number of the years that it was that were to be fulfilled according to the word of Yahweh to Jeremiah the prophet for the devastation of Jerusalem—seventy years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:10–13; James 5:16–18"> Then I turned my face to the Lord God to seek him by prayer and pleas for mercy,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “supplications”">b</a></sup>in fasting and in sackcloth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Exodus 20:6; Deuteronomy 5:10"> And I prayed to Yahweh my God, and I made confession and I said, “O Lord, the great and awesome God, keeping the covenant and loyal love with those who love him and with those who keep his commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Malachi 3:7; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:15"> we have sinned and we have done wrong and we acted wickedly and we rebelled and have been turning aside from your commandments and from your ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:10; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Luke 20:10–12"> We have not listened to your servants the prophets, who spoke in your name to our kings, our princes and our ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers”">c</a></sup>and to all the people of the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 44:15; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> “⸤Righteousness belongs to you, O Lord⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “for you Lord the righteousness”">d</a></sup>and on us is open shame, just as it is this day to the people of Judah and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem and to all Israel, ⸤those who are near and those who are far off⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “those near and those far”">e</a></sup>in all the lands to which you have driven them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “to which you have driven them there”">f</a></sup>because of their infidelity which they displayed against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 9:6–7; Lamentations 1:7–8; Lamentations 1:18"> Yahweh, on us is open shame, on our kings, on our princes, and on our ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “fathers”">g</a></sup>because we have sinned against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–8; Daniel 9:7; Daniel 9:5"> ⸤Compassion and forgiveness belong to the Lord, our God⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to the Lord our God the compassion and the forgiveness”">h</a></sup>for we have rebelled against him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 9:6; 2 Kings 18:12; Nehemiah 9:13–17"> and we have not listened to the voice of Yahweh our God, by following his law ⸤which he placed before us⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “which he gave to the face of us”">i</a></sup>by the hand of his servants the prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5–10; Isaiah 1:4–6; Deuteronomy 28:15–68"> “And all Israel transgressed your law and turned aside so as not to listen to your voice, and so the curse and the oath which was written in the law of Moses, the servant of God, has been poured out upon us, because we have sinned against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:9; Lamentations 1:12; Job 12:17"> And so he has carried out his words which he spoke against us and against our rulers who ruled us, to bring upon us a great calamity which was not done under all of heaven as it was done in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Isaiah 9:13; Daniel 9:11"> Just as it is written in the law of Moses, all of this calamity has come upon us, and ⸤we have not implored⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “we not implored”">j</a></sup>the face of Yahweh our God so as to turn from our iniquities and to attend closely to your faithfulness.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “truth”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:7; Nehemiah 9:33; Psalm 51:14"> So Yahweh has kept watch over the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “his”">l</a></sup>calamity, and now he has brought it upon us. Indeed, Yahweh our God is righteous concerning all his works<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “acts”">m</a></sup>that he has done, but we have not listened to his voice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 32:11; Nehemiah 1:10"> “And now, Lord our God, who have brought your people out from the land of Egypt with a strong hand, and you have made for yourself a name until this day—we have sinned, we have acted wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Joel 3:17; Psalm 87:1–3"> Lord, according to all your righteousness, please let your anger and your rage turn away from your city Jerusalem, ⸤your holy mountain⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the mountain of your holiness”">n</a></sup>because through our sins and through the iniquities of our ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “fathers”">o</a></sup>Jerusalem and your people have become an object of mockery among all of our neighbors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 80:19; Lamentations 5:18; 2 Corinthians 1:20"> “And now, listen to the prayer of your servant and to his pleas for mercy. Shine<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “let shine” or “cause to shine”">p</a></sup>your face upon your desolate sanctuary ⸤for your sake, O Lord⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “for the sake of the Lord”">q</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 37:17; Jeremiah 36:7; 2 Kings 19:16"> Incline your ear, my God, and listen; open your eyes and look at our desolation and the city that is called by your name, for we are not presenting our pleas for mercy ⸤before you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “to the face of you”">r</a></sup>because of our righteousness, but rather because of your great compassion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 9:18; Ephesians 1:12; Amos 7:2"> Lord, listen! Lord, forgive! Lord, pay heed and act! You must not delay ⸤for your sake⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “for the sake of you”">s</a></sup>my God; because ⸤your city and your people are called by your name⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “your name is called over your city and over your people”">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Gabriel’s Announcement and Presentation of the Seventy Sets of Seven

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 145:18; Daniel 9:3–4; Romans 3:23"> Now I was still speaking and praying, and confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel, and presenting my plea ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">u</a></sup>Yahweh my God, on behalf ⸤of the holy mountain of my God⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the mountain of the holiness of my God”">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Daniel 8:16; 1 Kings 18:36"> And I was still speaking in prayer, and the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision ⸤previously⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “in the beginning”">w</a></sup>⸤in my weariness touched me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “my being wearied, touching me”">x</a></sup>at the time of the evening offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 9:24–27; Daniel 10:21; Daniel 8:16"> And he instructed me and he spoke with me and he said, “Daniel I have now come out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “forth”">y</a></sup>to teach you understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 10:11–12"> At the beginning of your pleas for mercy, a word went out, and now I have come to declare<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “deliver”">z</a></sup>it, for you are highly esteemed, and so consider the word and understand the vision.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Seventy Sets of Seven Detailed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:11–14; Romans 5:10; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> “Seventy weeks is decreed for your people and ⸤for your holy city⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “for the city of your holiness”">aa</a></sup>to put an end to the transgression and to seal up sin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Or “to make an end to sin”">ab</a></sup>and to make atonement for guilt and to bring in everlasting righteousness and to seal vision and prophet and to anoint ⸤the most holy place⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “the holy place of holy places”">ac</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 4:24; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 55:4"> And you must know and you must understand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “so you are to know and discern” (NASB), or “know then and understand” (e.g. NEB)">ad</a></sup>that from the time of the going out of the word to restore and build Jerusalem until an anointed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “messiah”">ae</a></sup>one—a leader—will be ⸤seven weeks and sixty-two weeks⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “weeks seven and weeks sixty and two”">af</a></sup>it will be restored and will be built with streets and a moat, but ⸤in a time of oppression⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “in distress/oppression of the times/time”">ag</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Nahum 1:8; Mark 13:2"> “And after the sixty and two weeks an anointed one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Or “messiah”">ah</a></sup>shall be cut off, and ⸤he shall have nothing⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “there is not for him”">ai</a></sup>and the people of the coming leader will destroy the city and the sanctuary,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “holy place”">aj</a></sup>and its end will be with the flood and on to the end there shall be war; these desolations are determined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:15; Luke 21:20; Daniel 12:11"> And he will make a strong covenant with the many for one week, but in half of the week he will let cease sacrifice and offering ⸤and in its place⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “and on a wing of”">ak</a></sup>a desolating abomination comes even until ⸤the determined complete destruction⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “a complete destruction and being determined”">al</a></sup>is poured out on the desolator.”</span>
</div>
