## Daniel’s Development in the King’s Court

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5–7; 2 Kings 24:1–2; 2 Kings 24:13"> In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; Zechariah 5:11; Jeremiah 27:19–20"> And the Lord gave Jehoiakim the king of Judah into his hand and ⸤some of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “from the end/extremity/limits”">a</a></sup>of the utensils of the temple of God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “the God”">b</a></sup>and he brought them to the land of Shinar to the temple of his gods, and he brought the utensils to ⸤the treasury⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the house of the treasury”">c</a></sup>of his gods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 39:7; 2 Kings 20:17–18; 2 Kings 24:15"> And the king ⸤ordered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “said to”">d</a></sup>Ashpenaz, the commander of his court officials, to bring ⸤some of the Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “from the sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>from ⸤the royal family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “from the seed of the kingship”">f</a></sup>and from the lords,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “nobles”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 5:11; Acts 7:22; Proverbs 22:29"> youths who ⸤have no physical defect⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “there is not in them any physical defect”">h</a></sup>and who are ⸤handsome⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “good/pleasing of appearance”">i</a></sup>and who are prudent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “insightful”">j</a></sup>in all wisdom and ⸤endowed with knowledge⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “who have knowledge of knowledge”">k</a></sup>and who understand insight, and who have the ⸤ability⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “strength”">l</a></sup>in them to serve in the palace of the king. And the king ordered him to teach them the literature and the language of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 1:19; 1 Kings 10:8; Daniel 1:8"> And the king assigned to them ⸤his daily portion⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the portion of the day on its day”">m</a></sup>from the fine food of the king, and from ⸤the wine that he drank⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the wine of his drink”">n</a></sup>and instructed that ⸤they were to be educated for three years⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “and to be educated them years three”">o</a></sup>And at the end of their training, ⸤they were to be stationed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “they would stand”">p</a></sup>⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">q</a></sup>the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 2:17"> Now there was among them ⸤from the Judeans⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “from the children of Judah”">r</a></sup>Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael and Azariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 5:12; Daniel 4:8; Daniel 2:49"> And the commander of the court officials gave them names, and he called Daniel, Belteshazzar; and Hananiah, Shadrach; and Mishael, Meshach; and Azariah, Abednego.</span>

## Daniel’s Resolve

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 141:4; Ezekiel 4:13–14; Leviticus 11:45–47"> Now ⸤Daniel resolved⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “Daniel placed upon his heart”">s</a></sup>that he would not defile himself with the fine food of the king, and with ⸤the wine that he drank⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “the wine of his drink”">t</a></sup>and so he requested from the commander of the court officials permission so that he would not defile himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:21; Proverbs 16:7; Psalm 106:46"> And God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Hebrew “the God”">u</a></sup>gave Daniel favor and compassion ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">v</a></sup>the commander of the court officials,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 6:16–18; John 12:42–43; Proverbs 29:25"> and the commander of the court officials said to Daniel, “I am afraid of my lord, the king, who has determined your food and your drink, for ⸤why should⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “to what would”">w</a></sup>he see your face having a worse appearance than the young men who ⸤are your age⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “like your age”; “your” is plural">x</a></sup>Then you will endanger my head with the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">y</a></sup>Daniel asked the guard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “overseer”">z</a></sup>whom the commander of the court officials had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:2; Daniel 1:16; Genesis 1:29–30"> “Please test your servants for ten days, and let them give us ⸤some of the vegetables⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “from the vegetables”">aa</a></sup>and let us eat and let us drink water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “And”">ab</a></sup>let our appearances and the appearance of the young men who are eating the fine food of the king be compared ⸤before you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “in your presence”">ac</a></sup>and then deal with your servants ⸤according to what you see⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “and according to/that you see”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So he agreed to this proposal with them, and he tested them for ten days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:25; Proverbs 10:22; 2 Kings 4:42–44"> And at the end of ten days their appearances appeared better and they were healthier of body than all the young men who were eating the fine food of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 1:12"> ⸤So⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “And it happened”">ae</a></sup>the guard ⸤continued to withhold⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “he was … withdrawing”">af</a></sup>their fine food and the wine of their drink, and he gave them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Hebrew “to them”">ag</a></sup>vegetables.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 1:5; Daniel 2:21"> ⸤And as for these four young men⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “And these young men, four of them”">ah</a></sup>God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “the God”">ai</a></sup>gave to them knowledge and insight into all literature and wisdom, and Daniel had insight into all visions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “vision”">aj</a></sup>and dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 1:5"> And at the end of the time the king had set to bring them, the commander of the court officials brought them in ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">ak</a></sup>Nebuchadnezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 41:46; Daniel 1:5"> And the king spoke with them, ⸤and among all of them no one was found like⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Literally “and he was not found from all of them like”">al</a></sup>Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah; then they stood ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “to the face of”">am</a></sup>the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 8:19; Isaiah 47:12–14; 1 Kings 4:29–34"> And in every matter of wisdom and understanding about which the king inquired from them, he found them ten times better than all of the magicians<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “soothsayer-priests”">an</a></sup>and conjurers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Or “enchanters”">ao</a></sup>that ⸤were in his entire kingdom⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “in the whole of his kingdom”">ap</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 6:28; Daniel 10:1"> And Daniel was there until the first year of Cyrus the king.</span>
</div>
