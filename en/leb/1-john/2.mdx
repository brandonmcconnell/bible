## Keeping the New Commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:5; Romans 8:34; John 14:6"> My little children, I am writing these things to you in order that you may not sin. And if anyone sins, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:14; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 4:10"> and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “he himself” (emphatic)">a</a></sup>is the propitiation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “expiation”; or “atoning sacrifice”">b</a></sup>for our sins, and not for ours only, but also for the sins of the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 14:15; John 15:10; Luke 6:46"> And by this we know that we have come to know him, if we keep his commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Titus 1:16; James 2:14–16"> The one who says “I have come to know him,” and does not keep his commandments is a liar, and the truth is not in this person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 14:23; John 14:21; 1 John 4:12–13"> But whoever keeps his word, truly in this person the love of God has been perfected. By this we know that we are in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21; John 13:15; 1 Corinthians 11:1"> The one who says that he resides in him ought also to walk<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “to walk in this way”">c</a></sup>just as that one walked.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 3:11; Leviticus 19:18; Mark 12:29–34"> Dear friends, I am not writing a new commandment to you, but an old commandment which you have had from the beginning. The old commandment is the message which you have heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; John 13:34"> Again, I am writing a new commandment to you, which is true in him and in you, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or perhaps “that”">d</a></sup>the darkness is passing away and the true light already is shining.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:11; 1 John 3:13–17"> The one who says he is in the light and hates his brother is in the darkness until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 3:14; Romans 9:32–33; Romans 14:13"> The one who loves his brother resides in the light, and there is no cause for stumbling in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 12:35; Titus 3:3; 1 John 1:6"> But the one who hates his brother is in the darkness, and walks in the darkness, and does not know where he is going, because the darkness has blinded his eyes.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Encouragement and Assurance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:43; Acts 13:38; Ephesians 1:7"> I am writing to you, little children, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “that”">e</a></sup>your sins have been forgiven you on account of his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 14:7; 1 John 2:14; Luke 10:22"> I am writing to you, fathers, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “that”">f</a></sup>you have known the One who is from the beginning. I am writing to you, young men, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “that”">g</a></sup>you have conquered the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 119:11; 1 John 2:13; Colossians 3:16"> I have written to you, children, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “that”">h</a></sup>you have known the Father. I have written to you, fathers, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “that”">i</a></sup>you have known the One who is from the beginning. I have written to you, young men, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “that”">j</a></sup>you are strong, and the word of God resides in you, and you have conquered the evil one.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Love the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 4:4; Romans 12:2; Matthew 6:24"> Do not love the world or the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 13:14; Galatians 5:17; Psalm 119:36–37"> because everything that is in the world—the desire of the flesh and the desire of the eyes and the arrogance of material possessions—is not from the Father, but is from the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 12:2; 1 Corinthians 7:31"> And the world is passing away, and its desire,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “and the desire for it”">k</a></sup>but the one who does the will of God remains ⸤forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">l</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Departure of the False Teachers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 4:3; 2 John 1:7; Matthew 24:24"> Children, it is the last hour, and just as you have heard that antichrist is coming, even now many antichrists have arisen, by which we know that it is the last hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 20:30; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Luke 8:13"> They went out from us, but they were not of us; for if they had been of us, they would have remained with us. But they went out, in order that it might be shown that all of them are not of us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:27; 2 Corinthians 1:21–22"> And you have an anointing from the Holy One, and you all know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 1:12; Jude 1:5; Proverbs 1:5"> I have not written to you because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “that”">m</a></sup>you do not know the truth, but because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “that”">n</a></sup>you do know it, and because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “that”">o</a></sup>every lie is not of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 John 1:7; 1 John 4:3; 1 John 2:18"> Who is the liar except the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ? This person is the antichrist, the one who denies the Father and the Son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 4:15; John 8:19; John 5:23"> Everyone who denies the Son does not have the Father either; the one who confesses the Son has the Father also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 14:23; John 15:9–10; 1 John 4:13"> As for you, what you have heard from the beginning must remain in you. If what you have heard from the beginning remains in you, you also will remain in the Son and in the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:28; Titus 3:7; Jude 1:21"> And this is the promise which he himself promised us: eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 John 1:7; Acts 20:29–30"> These things I have written to you concerning the ones who are trying to deceive you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:26; 1 John 2:20–21; 1 Corinthians 2:13"> And as for you, the anointing which you received from him remains in you, and you do not have need that anyone teach you. But as his anointing teaches you about all things, and is true and is not a lie, and just as it has taught you, you reside in him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Children

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 John 4:17; Mark 8:38; 1 John 3:2"> And now, little children, remain in him, so that whenever he is revealed we may have confidence and not be put to shame before him at his coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 3:3–5; 1 John 3:7; 1 John 5:1"> If you know that he is righteous, you know that everyone<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “everyone also”">p</a></sup>who practices righteousness has been fathered by him.</span>
</div>
