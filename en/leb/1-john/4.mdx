## How to Recognize the Spirit of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; 2 Peter 2:1; 1 Timothy 4:1"> Dear friends, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits to determine if they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:3; 1 Timothy 3:16"> By this you know the Spirit of God: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “confesses Jesus as Christ who has come in the flesh” (see also 2 John 7 )">a</a></sup>has come in the flesh is from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 2:22; 2 John 1:7; 1 John 2:18"> and every spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God, and this is the spirit of the antichrist, of which you have heard that it is coming, and now it is already in the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:31; 1 John 5:4; Romans 8:37"> You are from God, little children, and have conquered them, because the one who is in you is greater than the one who is in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:23; 2 Peter 2:2–3; John 17:14"> They are from the world; ⸤therefore⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “because of this”">b</a></sup>they speak from the world and the world listens to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:17; John 10:27; 1 Corinthians 2:12–14"> We are from God. The one who knows God listens to us; whoever is not from God does not listen to us. By this we know the Spirit of truth and the spirit of deceit.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Love Revealed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:20–1; 1 John 4:8; 1 Peter 1:22"> Dear friends, let us love one another, because love is from God, and everyone who loves has been fathered by God and knows God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Psalm 86:15; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> The one who does not love does not know God, because God is love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:16; 1 John 5:11; Romans 8:32"> By this the love of God is revealed in us: that God sent his one and only Son into the world in order that we may live through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; Romans 5:8–10; 1 John 4:19"> In this is love: not that we have loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son to be the propitiation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “expiation”; or “atoning sacrifice”">c</a></sup>for our sins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12–13; Colossians 3:13; John 13:34"> Dear friends, if God loved us in this way, we also ought to love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 1:18; 1 John 2:5; 1 John 4:17–18"> No one has seen God at any time. If we love one another, God resides in us and his love is perfected in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 John 3:24; 1 Corinthians 3:16–17"> By this we know that we reside in him and he in us: that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “because”">d</a></sup>he has given us of his Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16–17; John 4:42"> And we have seen and testify that the Father has sent the Son to be the Savior of the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 10:9; 1 John 5:5; Matthew 10:32"> Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God resides in him and he in God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 3:24; Psalm 36:7–9; 1 John 3:1"> And we have come to know and have believed the love that God has in us. God is love, and the one who resides in love resides in God, and God resides in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:12; 1 John 2:28; Romans 8:29"> By this love is perfected with us, so that we may have confidence in the day of judgment, because just as that one is, so also are we in this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Romans 8:15; 1 John 4:12"> There is no fear in love, but perfect love drives out fear, because fear includes punishment, and the one who is afraid has not been perfected in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:10; John 3:16; John 15:16"> We love, because he first loved us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 John 2:9; 1 John 4:12"> If anyone says, “I love God,” and hates his brother, he is a liar, for the one who does not love his brother whom he has seen is not able to love God whom he has not seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 22:37–39; Leviticus 19:18; 1 John 3:11"> And this is the commandment we have from him: that the one who loves God should love his brother also.</span>
</div>
</div>
