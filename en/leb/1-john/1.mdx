## Prologue

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:20; John 20:27; 1 John 4:14"> What was from the beginning, what we have heard, what we have seen with our eyes, what we have looked at and our hands have touched, concerning the word of life—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:4; 1 John 5:20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> and the life was revealed, and we have seen and testify and announce to you the eternal life which was with the Father and was revealed to us—</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:9; John 17:3; John 17:21"> what we have seen and heard, we announce to you also, in order that you also may have fellowship with us, and indeed our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:11; John 16:24; 2 John 1:12"> And these things we write, in order that our joy may be complete.</span>
</div>

## God is Light, so Walk in the Light

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; James 1:17; Isaiah 60:19"> And this is the message which we have heard from him and announce to you, that God is light and there is no darkness in him at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:4; John 3:19–21"> If we say that we have fellowship with him and walk in the darkness, we lie and do not practice the truth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “we are lying and are not practicing the truth”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; Revelation 1:5"> But if we walk in the light as he is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us from all sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2"> If we say that we do not have sin, we deceive ourselves and the truth is not in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 33:8"> If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just, so that he will forgive us our sins and will cleanse us from all unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:8; 1 John 5:10; 1 John 2:4"> If we say that we have not sinned, we make him a liar, and his word is not in us.</span>
</div>
