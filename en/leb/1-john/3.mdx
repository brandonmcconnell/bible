<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:11; 1 John 2:4; 1 John 3:9"> Everyone who resides in him does not sin. Everyone who sins has neither seen him nor known him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; John 8:44; Colossians 2:15"> The one who practices sin is of the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. For this reason the Son of God was revealed: in order to destroy the works of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; 1 Peter 1:23; John 1:13"> Everyone who is fathered by God does not practice sin, because his seed resides in him, and he is not able to sin, because he has been fathered by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 3 John 1:11; Luke 6:35"> By this the children of God and the children of the devil are evident: everyone who does not practice righteousness is not of God, namely, the one who does not love his brother.</span>

## God is Love, so Love One Another

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12; 2 John 1:5; 1 John 4:7"> For this is the message that you have heard from the beginning: that we should love one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Genesis 4:4–15; Proverbs 29:27"> not as Cain, who was of the evil one and violently murdered his brother. And ⸤for what reason⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “on account of what”">a</a></sup>did he violently murder him? Because his deeds were evil and the deeds of his brother were righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 17:14; Luke 21:17"> Do not marvel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “And do not marvel”">b</a></sup>brothers, if the world hates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:35; 1 John 5:2; 1 John 4:20–21"> We know that we have passed over from death to life because we love the brothers. The one who does not love remains in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:21–22; Proverbs 26:24–26; Revelation 21:8"> Everyone who hates his brother is a murderer, and you know that every murderer does not have eternal life residing in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:9–11; Ephesians 5:2; John 13:34"> We have come to know love by this: that ⸤he⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “that one” (referring to Jesus Christ)">c</a></sup>laid down his life on behalf of us, and we ought to lay down our lives on behalf of the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:7–10; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> But whoever has the world’s material possessions and observes his brother ⸤in need⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “having need”">d</a></sup>and shuts his heart against him, how does the love of God reside in him?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 2:15–16; Romans 12:9; 1 Peter 1:22"> Little children, let us not love with word or with tongue, but in deed and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 John 3:21; Hebrews 6:10–11"> By this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “And by this”">e</a></sup>we know that we are of the truth and will convince our heart before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 139:1–4; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 4:13"> that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “because”">f</a></sup>if our heart condemns us, that God is greater than our heart and knows all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Hebrews 4:16; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Dear friends, if our heart does not condemn us, we have confidence before God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 15:7; John 9:31; 1 John 5:14"> and whatever we ask we receive from him, because we keep his commandments and do what is pleasing ⸤in his sight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the sight of him”">g</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:29; John 13:34; 1 Peter 1:22"> And this is his commandment: that we believe in the name of his Son Jesus Christ and love one another, just as ⸤he commanded us⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “he gave us commandment”">h</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:15–16; 1 John 4:12–13"> And the one who keeps his commandments resides in him, and he in him. And by this we know that he resides in us: by the Spirit whom he has given to us.</span>
</div>
