## Our Assurance of Eternal Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:31; John 1:12; 2 Peter 1:10–11"> These things I have written to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, in order that you may know that you have eternal life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:13; Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> And this is the confidence that we have before him: that if we ask anything according to his will, he hears us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:9–10; Mark 11:24; Proverbs 15:29"> And if we know that he hears us in whatever we ask, we know that we have the requests that we have asked from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4–6; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 11:14"> If anyone should see his brother sinning a sin not leading to death, he should ask, and he will grant life to him, to those who sin not leading to death. (There is a sin leading to death; I do not say that he should ask about that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 3:4; 1 John 5:16; James 4:7–10"> All unrighteousness is sin, and there is a sin not leading to death.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:9; 1 John 5:4; 1 John 5:1"> We know that everyone who is fathered by God does not sin, but the one fathered by God, he protects him, and the evil one does not touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 4:4–6; Ephesians 2:2"> We know that we are from God, and the whole world lies in the power of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:3; Luke 24:45; John 14:9"> And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us understanding, in order that we may know the one who is true, and we are in the one who is true, in his Son Jesus Christ. This one is the true God and eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:14; 1 Corinthians 10:7; Exodus 20:3–4"> Little children, guard yourselves from idols.</span>
</div>
