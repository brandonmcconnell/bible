## Miriam Dies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; Exodus 15:20; Numbers 33:36"> Then the entire community of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>came to the desert of Zin on the first month, and the people stayed in Kadesh; Miriam died and was buried there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 15:23–24"> There was no water for the community, and they were gathered before Moses and Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 17:2; Numbers 14:1–2; Numbers 16:31–35"> And the people quarreled with Moses and spoke, saying, “If only we died when our brothers were dying ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">b</a></sup>Yahweh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 17:3; Psalm 106:21; Numbers 16:13–14"> Why have you brought the assembly of Yahweh, us and our livestock, into this desert to die here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:14; Jeremiah 2:2; Jeremiah 2:6"> Why have you brought us from Egypt to bring us to this bad place? It is not a place of seed or figs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “fig”">c</a></sup>or vines<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “vine”">d</a></sup>or pomegranate trees,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “tree”">e</a></sup>and there is not water to drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:19"> And Moses and Aaron went from the presence of the assembly to the doorway of the tent of assembly. They fell on their faces, and the glory of Yahweh appeared to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Acts 2:1–4; Revelation 22:17"> “Take the staff and summon the community, you and Aaron your brother, and speak to the rock before their eyes, and it will give water. Bring out for them water from the rock, and let the community and their livestock drink.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 17:10"> So Moses took the staff from ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">f</a></sup>Yahweh just as he command him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 106:32–33; Acts 14:9–15; Luke 9:54–55"> and Moses and Aaron summoned the assembly to the presence of the rock, and he said to them, “Please listen, you rebels; can we bring out water for you from this rock?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6; Deuteronomy 8:15"> Then Moses lifted up his hand and struck the rock with his staff twice. And abundant water went out, and the community and their livestock drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 27:14; Ezekiel 36:23"> But Yahweh said to Moses and Aaron, “Because you have not trusted in me, to regard me as holy ⸤in the sight of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “before the eyes of”">g</a></sup>the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>you will not bring this assembly into the land that I have given to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Psalm 95:8; Deuteronomy 33:8"> Those were the waters of Meribah, where the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>quarreled with Yahweh, and he showed himself holy among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 23:7; Obadiah 1:10–12"> From Kadesh Moses sent messengers to the king of Edom: “Thus your brother Israel has said, ‘You know all the hardship that has found us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:40; Deuteronomy 26:6; Genesis 46:6"> our ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “fathers”">j</a></sup>went down to Egypt, and we lived in Egypt ⸤a long time⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “many days”">k</a></sup>and the Egyptians mistreated us and our ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:19; Exodus 23:20; Exodus 33:2"> Then we cried to Yahweh, and he heard our voice; he sent an angel and brought us out from Egypt. And look, we are in Kadesh, a city on the edge of your territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:27; Deuteronomy 2:29; Numbers 21:22–24"> Please let us go through your land. We will not go through a field or vineyard, and we will not drink water from a well. We will go along the road of the king; we will not turn aside right or left until we have gone through your territory.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then Edom said to him, “You will not pass through us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “me”">m</a></sup>lest ⸤we will go out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “I will go out”">n</a></sup>to meet you with the sword.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:28; Deuteronomy 2:6; Exodus 12:38"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>said to him, “We will go up on the main road, and if we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “I”">p</a></sup>and our livestock<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “my livestock”">q</a></sup>drink your water, we will pay for it.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “I will give their worth”">r</a></sup>It is only a small matter; let us pass through on our feet.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “I will go through on my feet”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 11:17; Amos 1:11; Genesis 32:6"> But he said, “You will not go through.” And Edom went out to meet ⸤them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">t</a></sup>with a large army and a strong hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Deuteronomy 2:29; Deuteronomy 2:4–8"> So Edom refused to give Israel passage through his territory, and Israel turned aside from him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Aaron Dies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:14; Numbers 21:4"> And they set out from Kadesh. The ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">u</a></sup>the whole community, came to Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 33:37"> Yahweh said to Moses and to Aaron on Mount Hor, on the boundary of the land of Edom, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 31:2"> “Let Aaron be gathered to his people; he will not come into the land that I have given to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup>because you rebelled against ⸤my word⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “my mouth”">w</a></sup>at the waters of Meribah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 33:38–39"> Take Aaron and Eleazar his son, and take them up Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 20:24; Isaiah 22:21–22; Hebrews 7:11"> Strip off Aaron’s garments, and put them on Eleazar his son; Aaron will be gathered to his people, and he will die there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> So Moses did just as Yahweh commanded, and they went up to Mount Hor before the eyes of all the community.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Exodus 29:29–30; Deuteronomy 34:9"> And Moses stripped off Aaron’s garments and put them on Eleazar his son. Aaron died there on the top of the mountain; and Moses and Eleazar went down from the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:8; 2 Chronicles 35:24–25; Genesis 1:10"> All the community saw that Aaron died; so all the house of Israel wept for Aaron thirty days.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “day”">x</a></sup></span>
</div>
