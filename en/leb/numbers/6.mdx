## Rules Concerning Nazirites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Amos 2:11–12; Romans 1:1"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>and say to them, ‘When a man or a woman takes a special vow, a vow of a ⸤Nazirite⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “one separated”">b</a></sup>to keep separate for Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:15; 1 Timothy 5:23; Amos 2:12"> he will abstain from wine and fermented drink; he will not drink wine vinegar or vinegar of fermented drink; he will not drink the fruit juice of grapes or eat fresh or dry grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 6:5; Numbers 6:21; Numbers 6:8–9"> All the days of his separation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="That is, “the Nazirite”">c</a></sup>you will not eat from anything that is made from the grapevine, from sour grapes to the skin of grapes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 1:11; Judges 16:17; Judges 13:5"> “ ‘All the days of the vow of his separation a razor will not pass over his head. Until fulfilling the days that he separated himself to Yahweh he will be holy and grow long the locks of the hair of his head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 19:28; Leviticus 21:1–3"> “ ‘All the days of keeping himself separated for Yahweh he will not go to a person who is dead;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 9:6; Leviticus 21:1–2; Leviticus 21:10–12"> for even his father, mother, brother, or sister he will not make himself unclean ⸤by their death⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “with their dying”">d</a></sup>because the separation to his God is on his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:17–18"> He will be holy for Yahweh all the days of his separation.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, “the Nazirite”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 6:18; Acts 18:18; Leviticus 14:9"> “ ‘If someone dies suddenly and makes the head of his separation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, “the Nazirite”">f</a></sup>unclean, he will shave off the hair of his head on the day of his cleansing; on the seventh day he will shave it off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:29; Leviticus 1:14; Leviticus 15:14"> On the eighth day he will bring two turtledoves or two ⸤young pigeons⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the sons of doves”">g</a></sup>to the priest by the doorway of the tent of assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 14:30–31; Leviticus 5:8–10"> and the priest will offer one for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering, and he will make atonement for him because he sinned concerning the corpse. He will consecrate his head on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 5:6; Matthew 24:13; Leviticus 14:24"> He will rededicate to Yahweh the days of his separation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, “the Nazirite”">h</a></sup>and bring a ram-lamb ⸤in its first year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “a son of his year”">i</a></sup>as a guilt offering. The former days of his vow will fall away because his separation was defiled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 21:26"> “ ‘This is the regulation of the Nazirite for the day of the fulfilling of the days of his separation: one will bring him to the doorway of the tent of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 3:6; Leviticus 4:32; Leviticus 4:2–3"> He will present his offering to Yahweh, one ram-lamb ⸤in its first year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “a son of his year”">j</a></sup>without defect as a burnt offering, and one ewe-lamb ⸤in its first year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “a daughter of his year”">k</a></sup>without defect as a sin offering, and one ram without defect as a fellowship offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 2:4; Exodus 29:2; Numbers 15:10"> and a basket of unleavened bread, finely milled flour of ring-shaped bread cakes mixed with oil, and wafers of unleavened bread smeared with oil, and their grain offering and their libations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The priest will present ⸤before Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “before the face of Yahweh”">l</a></sup>and offer his sin offering, his burnt offering;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> he will offer a ram as a sacrifice of a fellowship offering to Yahweh, in addition to the basket of the unleavened bread; the priest will offer his grain offering and his libation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:24; Numbers 6:9; Acts 18:18"> The Nazirite will shave off the hair of ⸤his consecrated head⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the head of his separation”">m</a></sup>at the doorway of the tent of assembly, and he will take the hair of ⸤his consecrated head⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the head of his separation”">n</a></sup>and he will put it on the fire that is beneath the sacrifice of the fellowship offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:15; Leviticus 7:30; Exodus 29:23–28"> The priest will take the shoulder from the ram that is boiled, and one ring-shaped bread cake of unleavened bread from the basket, and one wafer of unleavened bread, and he will put them on the hands of the Nazirite after he has shaved ⸤his consecrated head⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “his separation”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:7; Exodus 29:27–28; Numbers 5:25"> The priest will wave them as a wave offering ⸤before the presence of⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “before the face of”">p</a></sup>Yahweh; they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “it”">q</a></sup>are a holy object to the priest, in addition to the breast section of the wave offering, and in addition to the upper thigh of the contribution; and afterward the Nazirite may drink wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Ezra 2:69; Numbers 5:29"> “ ‘This is the regulation of the Nazirite who has made a vow of his offering to Yahweh according to his separation, ⸤in addition to what he can afford⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “except from that which overtakes his hand”">r</a></sup>In accordance to the word of his vow that he vowed, he will do, concerning the instruction of his separation.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Priestly Blessing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; 1 Chronicles 23:13; Genesis 48:20"> “Speak to Aaron and his sons, saying, ‘You will bless the ⸤Israelites⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">s</a></sup>You will say to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; Psalm 121:4–7; Philippians 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will bless you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and keep you;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 80:19; Psalm 119:135; Psalm 31:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will make shine his face on you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and be gracious to you;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalm 4:6; Psalm 29:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will lift up his face upon you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will give you peace.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:14; Daniel 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 28:10"> And they will put my name on the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">t</a></sup>and I will bless them.”</span>
</div>
