## The Seven Lamps

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:25; Leviticus 24:1–2"> “Speak to Aaron, and say to him: ‘When you are setting up the lamps, the seven lamps will give light in front of the face of the lampstand.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Aaron did so; he set up the lampstand in front of the face of its lamps, just as Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–40; Exodus 25:9; Exodus 25:18"> And this is ⸤how the lampstand was made⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the work of the lampstand”">a</a></sup>a hammered-work of gold; from its base up to its blossom,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “flower”">b</a></sup>it was hammered-work according to the pattern that Yahweh showed Moses; so he made the lampstand.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses Consecrates the Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:1; James 4:8; Exodus 19:15"> “Take the Levites from the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>and purify them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 19:13; Numbers 19:7–10; Leviticus 8:6"> So you will do to them, to purify them: sprinkle on them waters of purification, and ⸤they will shave their whole body⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “they will send a razor on all their body”">d</a></sup>and wash their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 29:1; Leviticus 16:3"> And they will take a ⸤young bull⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a bull, a son of cattle”">e</a></sup>and its grain offering of finely milled flour mixed with oil, and you will take a second ⸤young bull⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “a bull, a son of cattle”">f</a></sup>as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 40:12; Leviticus 8:3; Exodus 29:4–37"> You will bring the Levites ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">g</a></sup>the tent of assembly, and you will summon the entire community of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Acts 6:6; 1 Timothy 4:14"> And you will bring the Levites ⸤before Yahweh⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of Yahweh”">i</a></sup>and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>will lay their hands on the Levites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30; Leviticus 8:29; Numbers 1:49–53"> and ⸤Aaron will offer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “Aaron will wave”">k</a></sup>the Levites as a wave offering ⸤before Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of Yahweh”">l</a></sup>from the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>and they will do the work of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 10:4–10; Exodus 29:10; Leviticus 1:4"> And the Levites will lay their hands on the head of the one bull and offer it as a sin offering and the other one as a burnt offering to Yahweh, to make atonement for the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 8:21; Numbers 8:11; Romans 15:16"> And you will present the Levites ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">n</a></sup>Aaron and ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">o</a></sup>his sons, and he ⸤will offer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “Aaron will wave”">p</a></sup>them as a wave offering to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 3:45; Malachi 3:17; Numbers 18:6"> “And you will separate the Levites from the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">q</a></sup>and the Levites will be for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 8:11; Numbers 8:13; Numbers 3:23–37"> And after this the Levites will come to serve at the tent of assembly, and you will purify them, and you ⸤will offer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “Aaron will wave”">r</a></sup>them as a wave offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 3:9"> For they are given to me exclusively from the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">s</a></sup>I have taken them for myself in place of the firstborn of every womb, every firstborn from the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12–15"> For every firstborn among the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">u</a></sup>is mine, both humankind and animal. On the day I destroyed every firstborn in the land of Egypt I consecrated them to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and I have taken the Levites in the place of every firstborn among the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 16:46; 2 Chronicles 26:16–20"> And I have given the Levites; they are given to Aaron and his sons from the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">w</a></sup>to do the work of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">x</a></sup>in the tent of the assembly and to make atonement for the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">y</a></sup>so a plague will not be among the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">z</a></sup>when the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">aa</a></sup>come near the sanctuary.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Moses and Aaron and the entire community of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ab</a></sup>did to the Levites; everything that Yahweh commanded Moses concerning the Levites, the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ac</a></sup>did to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 8:7; Numbers 19:19; Numbers 8:15"> And the Levites purified themselves, and they washed their garments, and ⸤Aaron offered them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “Aaron waved them”">ad</a></sup>as a wave offering ⸤before Yahweh⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of Yahweh”">ae</a></sup>and Aaron made atonement for them to purify them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:2; 2 Chronicles 35:8–15; Numbers 8:15"> After this the Levites came to do their work in the tent of assembly before Aaron and his sons. Just as Yahweh commanded Moses concerning the Levities, so they did to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; 1 Chronicles 23:3; Numbers 4:23"> “This is what is for the Levites: ⸤those twenty-five years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “from a son of twenty-five years”">af</a></sup>and above will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Hebrew “and he will”">ag</a></sup>come to help with the service in the work of the tent of assembly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 4:23; 2 Timothy 4:7"> and ⸤those fifty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “from a son of fifty years”">ah</a></sup>will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “he will”">ai</a></sup>return from the service of the work and will serve no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:32; 1 Timothy 4:15"> They<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">aj</a></sup>can attend<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “assist”">ak</a></sup>their brothers in the tent of assembly to keep their responsibilities, but they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">al</a></sup>will not do work. This is what you will do concerning the Levities and their responsibilities.”</span>
</div>
