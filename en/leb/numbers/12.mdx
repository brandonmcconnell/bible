## Aaron and Miriam Murmur Against Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 2:21; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:37"> And Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses because of the Cushite woman whom he took (because he took a Cushite wife);</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:12–13; Numbers 16:3; Micah 6:4"> and they said, “Has Yahweh spoken only through Moses? Has not Yahweh also spoken through us?” And Yahweh heard it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; James 3:13; 2 Corinthians 11:5"> Now the man, Moses, was more humble than any other person on the face of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 76:9; Numbers 16:16–21"> and Yahweh said suddenly to Moses, Aaron, and Miriam, “Go out, you three, to the tent of assembly.” So the three of them went out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Exodus 34:5; Psalm 99:7"> And Yahweh went down in a column of cloud and stood at the doorway of the tent, and he called Aaron and Miriam, and the two of them went,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5; Genesis 31:10–11; Genesis 46:2"> and he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Please hear my words:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If there is a prophet among you, I, Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will make myself known to him in a vision.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will speak to him in a dream.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew “the dream”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–6; Joshua 1:1; Psalm 105:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Not so with my servant Moses;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in all my house he is faithful.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10; 1 Corinthians 13:12; Exodus 33:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will speak to him mouth to mouth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in clearness, not in riddles;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will look at the form of Yahweh.</span><br />Why were you not afraid to speak against my servant, against Moses?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Hosea 5:15; Numbers 11:1"> ⸤And Yahweh became very angry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “And the nose of Yahweh became hot”">b</a></sup>with them, and he went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:9; 2 Kings 5:27; Exodus 4:6"> And the cloud departed from on the tent, and behold, Miriam was infected with ⸤a skin disease⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The precise meaning is uncertain; many modern translations suggest “leprosy”">c</a></sup>white like snow; when Aaron turned toward Miriam, behold, she was afflicted with a skin disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19; 2 Samuel 24:10; Proverbs 30:32"> So Aaron said to Moses, “Please, my lord, please do not put on us this sin in which we were foolish and in which we have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 88:4–5; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Timothy 5:6"> Please do not let her be like the dead, whose flesh is half consumed when coming out from the womb of its mother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:21; Luke 6:28; James 5:15"> And Moses cried to Yahweh, saying, “God, ⸤please heal her⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “Please heal please her”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:9; Job 30:10; Isaiah 50:6"> But Yahweh said to Moses, “If her father had surely spit in her face, would she not bear her shame for seven days? Let her be confined for seven days to an outside place of the camp, and afterward she may be gathered.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:8–9; Micah 7:8–9; Micah 6:4"> So Miriam was confined to the outside place of the camp seven days, and the people did not set out until Miriam was gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 11:35; Numbers 33:18; Numbers 10:12"> And afterward the people set out from Hazeroth, and they encamped in the desert of Paran.</span>
