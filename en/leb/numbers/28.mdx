## Daily Sacrifice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Genesis 8:21; Numbers 9:2–3"> “Command the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>and say to them, ‘⸤You will be careful to present⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “you will observe to present”">b</a></sup>my offering, my food of my offerings made by fire, of a fragrance of appeasement to me, at its appointed time.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 6:9; Daniel 11:31; John 1:29"> You will say to them, ‘This is the offering made by fire that you will offer to Yahweh: two male lambs without defect ⸤in their first year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons of a year”">c</a></sup>as a continual burnt offering each day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36; Exodus 12:6"> You will offer one male lamb in the morning, and the second male lamb you will offer ⸤at twilight⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “between the two evenings”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 16:36; Exodus 29:38–42"> and a tenth of an ephah of finely milled flour as a grain offering, mixed with a fourth of a measure of beaten oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Leviticus 6:9; Psalm 50:8"> It is a continual burnt offering that was ⸤ordained⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “done”">e</a></sup>on Mount Sinai as a fragrance of appeasement, an offering made by fire for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 29:42; Numbers 28:14; Isaiah 57:6"> The libation with it will be a fourth of a liquid measure for each male lamb; in the sanctuary you will pour out the libation of fermented drink for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the second male lamb you will offer ⸤at twilight⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “between the two evenings”">f</a></sup>as the grain offering of the morning and as its libation you will offer it, an offering made by fire, a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Psalm 92:1–4; Revelation 1:10"> “ ‘On the day of the Sabbath, two male lambs without defect ⸤in their first year⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons of a year”">g</a></sup>and two-tenths of finely milled flour mixed with oil for a grain offering and its libation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–5; Numbers 29:22; Numbers 29:31"> This is the burnt offering every Sabbath in addition to the continual burnt offering and its libation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Ezekiel 46:6; Ezra 3:5"> “ ‘And at the beginning of each of your months, you will present a burnt offering for Yahweh: two bulls and one ram, seven male lambs without defect ⸤in their first year⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons of a year”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:4–12; Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 29:10"> and three-tenths of finely milled flour mixed with oil for a grain offering, for each bull; and two-tenths of finely milled flour mixed with oil for a grain offering for the one ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and a tenth of finely milled flour mixed with oil as a grain offering for each male lamb, for a burnt offering of a fragrance of appeasement, an offering of fire for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Their libations will be half a liquid measure of wine for the bull and a third of a liquid measure of wine for the ram and a fourth of a liquid measure of wine for the male lamb; this is the burnt offering for every month for the months of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:22; Numbers 15:24"> And one male goat as a sin offering for Yahweh; it will be offered in addition to the continual burnt offering and its libation.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Exodus 12:18; Leviticus 23:5–8"> “ ‘On the fourteenth day of the first month is the Passover for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 13:6"> On the fifteenth day of this month is a religious feast, unleavened bread must be eaten for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7–8"> On the first day there will be a holy convocation; ⸤you will not do any regular work⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “you will not do work of labor”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Ezekiel 45:21–25; Numbers 29:8"> You will present an offering by fire, a burnt offering for Yahweh: two bulls and one ram and seven male lambs ⸤in their first year⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons of a year”">j</a></sup>they will be for you without defect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For their grain offering, you will offer finely milled flour mixed with oil: three-tenths for the bull and two-tenths for the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> You will offer a tenth for each of the seven male lambs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:3"> and a goat for one sin offering to make atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:10"> You will offer these besides the burnt offering of the morning, which is for the continual burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Like this you will offer daily, for seven days, the food of the offering made by fire, a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh; it will be offered in addition to the continual burnt offering and its libation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 13:6"> On the seventh day you will have a holy convocation; ⸤you will not do any regular work⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “you will not do work of labor”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Offerings for the Festival of Weeks

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 34:22; Leviticus 23:10"> “ ‘And on the day of firstfruits, when you are presenting a new offering for Yahweh during your Festival of Weeks, you will have a holy convocation; ⸤you will not do any regular work⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “you will not do work of labor”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 23:18–19; Numbers 28:11; Numbers 28:19"> You will present a burnt offering for a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh: two bulls, one ram, seven male lambs ⸤in their first year⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons of a year”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and their grain offering will be finely milled flour mixed with oil: three-tenths for each bull, two-tenths for one ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> a tenth for each of the male lambs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22; 1 Peter 2:24"> and one male goat to make atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 28:3; Malachi 1:13–14"> In addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering, you will offer them without defect with their libation.</span>
</div>
