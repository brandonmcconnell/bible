## Laws for Vows

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Deuteronomy 1:13–17; Exodus 18:25"> Then Moses spoke to ⸤the leaders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the heads”">a</a></sup>of the tribes concerning the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>saying, “This is the word that Yahweh commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 50:14; Psalm 116:14; Ecclesiastes 5:4–5"> if a man makes a vow for Yahweh or swears an oath with a binding pledge on himself, he must not render his word invalid; he must do all that went out from his mouth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> “If a woman makes a vow to Yahweh, and she binds a pledge on herself in her father’s house in your childhood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> but if her father hears her vow or her pledge that she bound on herself and says nothing to her, then all her vows will stand, and every pledge that she binds on her life will stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 7:10–13; Matthew 15:4–6; Ephesians 6:1"> If her father forbids her on the day he hears of it, all her vows or her pledges that she bound on herself will not stand, and Yahweh will forgive her because her father has forgiven her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 56:12"> “If ⸤she has a husband⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “she is to a man”">c</a></sup>while bound by her vows or a rash promise of her lips,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and her husband hears of it and is silent on the day he hears it, her vows will stand, and her pledge that she bound upon herself will stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:16; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 14:34"> But if on the day her husband hears of it, he forbids her, then he will nullify her vow that she is under, and the rash promise of her lips that she bound on herself; and Yahweh will forgive her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:37; Romans 7:2; Leviticus 21:7"> “But the vow of a widow or a woman who is divorced, all that she binds on herself will stand on her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But if she made a vow in her husband’s house, or bound herself on a pledge with a sworn oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and her husband heard it but was silent to her, and he did not forbid her, all her vows will stand and every pledge that she bound on herself will stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 30:8; 1 Corinthians 11:3; Numbers 15:25"> But if her husband nullified them on the day he hears them, all her vows going out of her lips concerning her vows or the pledge on herself will not stand; her husband has nullified them, and Yahweh will forgive her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Isaiah 58:5"> “Any vow and any sworn oath of a pledge to inflict on herself, her husband can confirm it or her husband can nullify it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But if her husband is completely silent from day to day, then he confirms all her vows or all her pledges that are on her; he confirms them because he was silent to her on the day he heard them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> But if he indeed nullifies them after he hears them, then he will bear her guilt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54–57; Leviticus 11:46–47; Numbers 5:29–30"> These are the decrees that Yahweh commanded Moses, as between a husband and his wife, and between a father and his daughter, while her childhood is in her father’s house.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
