## Korah, Dathan, and Abiram Rebel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:11; Deuteronomy 11:6; Exodus 6:21"> Now Korah son of Izhar, son of Kohath, son of Levi, and Dathan and Abiram sons of Eliab, and On son of Peleth, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “sons”">a</a></sup>of Reuben,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 26:9; Ezekiel 23:10; Genesis 6:4"> took two hundred and fifty men from the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>leaders of the community summoned from the assembly, ⸤renowned men⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “men of name”">c</a></sup>and ⸤they confronted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “they rose up before”">d</a></sup>Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 106:16; Numbers 14:14; Exodus 19:6"> They were assembled in front of Moses and Aaron, and they said to them, “⸤You take too much upon yourselves⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “It is much for you”">e</a></sup>All of the community is holy, every one of them, and Yahweh is in their midst, so why do you raise yourselves over the assembly of Yahweh?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 20:6; Joshua 7:6"> When Moses heard this, he fell on his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 65:4; Numbers 17:5; 2 Timothy 2:19"> And he said to Korah and to his entire company, saying, “Tomorrow morning Yahweh will make known who is his and who is holy, and he will bring him near to him, whomever he chooses he will bring near to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1; Numbers 16:46–48; 1 Kings 18:21–23"> Do this: take for yourselves censers, Korah and all of your company;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “his company”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 16:3; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Kings 18:17–18"> tomorrow put fire in them and place incense on them ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">g</a></sup>Yahweh; the man whom Yahweh chooses will be the holy one. You take too much upon yourselves, sons of Levi!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Moses said to Korah, “Please listen, sons of Levi!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 7:13; Deuteronomy 10:8; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> Is it too little for you that the God of Israel set you apart from the community of Israel ⸤to allow you to approach him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “to bring you near to him”">h</a></sup>to do the work of the tabernacle of Yahweh, to stand ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">i</a></sup>the community to serve them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; 3 John 1:9; Numbers 18:7"> ⸤He has allowed you to approach him⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “He has brought you near”">j</a></sup>you with all your brothers, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “sons”">k</a></sup>of Levi, but yet you also seek the priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 16:7–8; Exodus 17:2; Numbers 16:3"> Therefore you and your company have banded together against Yahweh. What is Aaron that you grumble against him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13–14; Jude 1:8; Proverbs 29:9"> Moses sent to call for Dathan and Abiram son of Eliab, but they said, “We will not come!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “We will not come up!”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Exodus 2:14; Numbers 11:5"> Is it too little that you have brought us from a land that flows with milk and honey to kill us in the desert, and that you also appoint yourself as a ruler over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:24; Numbers 36:8–10; Numbers 20:5"> Surely, you have not brought us to a land that flows with milk and honey, and you have not given us the inheritance of fields and a vineyard. Will you gouge out the eyes of these men? We will not come!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “We will not come up!”">m</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:4–5; 2 Corinthians 7:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:10"> Then Moses became angry, and he said to Yahweh, “Do not notice their grain offering. I have not offered one donkey from them, and I have not mistreated one of them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:3; 1 Samuel 12:7; 2 Timothy 2:14"> And Moses said to Korah, “You and your entire company will be ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">n</a></sup>Yahweh tomorrow, you and they and Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:7"> Each one take his censer, and put incense on it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “on them”">o</a></sup>and you will present it ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">p</a></sup>Yahweh, and each of you bring his censer, two hundred and fifty censers, you and Aaron, each his censer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> So each of them took his censer, and they put fire on them, and they placed incense on them; they stood at the doorway of the tent of the assembly of Moses and Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:10; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 16:7"> And Korah summoned them, the entire community, by the doorway of the tent of assembly, and the glory of Yahweh appeared to all the community.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:45; Exodus 32:10; Psalm 73:19"> “Separate yourselves from the midst of this community, that I can destroy them in a moment.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:16; Zechariah 12:1; Job 12:10"> And they fell on their faces, and they said, “God, God of the spirits of all flesh, will one man sin and you become angry toward the entire community?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 16:21"> “Speak to the community, saying, ‘Move away from the dwelling of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 11:16–17; Numbers 11:25; Numbers 11:30"> So Moses stood up and went to Dathan and Abiram; the elders of Israel followed after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Revelation 18:4"> He said to the community, saying, “Please turn away from the tents of these wicked men, and do not touch anything ⸤that belongs to them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “that is to them”">q</a></sup>or you will be destroyed with all their sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 9:4; Isaiah 28:14; Job 40:10–11"> And so they moved away from around the dwellings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “dwelling”">r</a></sup>of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram; and Dathan and Abiram came out standing at the doorway of their tents, with their wives, sons, and little children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:38; Exodus 3:12; John 14:11"> And Moses said, “In this you will know that Yahweh has sent me to do all these works; it is not from my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 4:22; 1 Kings 22:28; Exodus 32:34"> ⸤If they die a natural death⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “If they die like the death of every human”">s</a></sup>or ⸤if a natural fate is visited upon them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “If the fate of every human is visited upon them”">t</a></sup>Yahweh has not sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 55:15; Numbers 16:33; Job 31:3"> But if Yahweh creates something new, and the ground opens its mouth and swallows them up and ⸤all that belongs to them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “All that is for them”">u</a></sup>and they go down alive to Sheol, and you will know that these men have despised Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalm 106:17–18; Numbers 27:3; Deuteronomy 11:6"> And it happened, as soon as he finished ⸤speaking⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “to speak”">v</a></sup>all these words, the ground that was under them split open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalm 106:17; Numbers 26:11; Revelation 12:16"> The land opened its mouth and swallowed them up with their houses and every person ⸤that belonged to Korah⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “that was to Korah”">w</a></sup>and all the property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:18; Isaiah 14:9; Psalm 9:15"> They went down alive to Sheol, they and all that belonged to them, and the land covered over them, and they perished from the midst of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 14:5; Isaiah 33:3; Revelation 6:15–17"> All Israel who were around them fled at their cry, because they said, “Lest the land swallow us up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Leviticus 10:2; Psalm 106:18"> And fire went out from Yahweh, and it consumed the two hundred and fifty men presenting the incense.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Numbers 16:36–17:13 in the English Bible is 17:1–28 in the Hebrew Bible">x</a></sup>And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Numbers 16:18; Numbers 16:7"> “Say to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, ‘⸤Take out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “raise up”">y</a></sup>the censers from among the place of burning because they are sacred, and scatter the fire outside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Ezekiel 14:8; 2 Peter 2:6"> The censers of these ⸤who have sinned⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sinners”">z</a></sup>at the cost of their lives, let them be made into gilded leafing plating for the altar; because they presented them ⸤before Yahweh⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of Yahweh”">aa</a></sup>they are holy; and they will be a sign for the ⸤Israelites⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ab</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Eleazar the priest took the bronze censers that the ones who were burned presented, and they hammered them out thinly as plating for the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 18:4–7; 2 Chronicles 26:16–21"> it was a memorial for the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ac</a></sup>so that ⸤no strange man⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="NASB translates “no layman”">ad</a></sup>who is not from ⸤the offspring⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “the seed”">ae</a></sup>of Aaron should approach the presence of Yahweh to burn a smoke offering;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="That is, an incense offering">af</a></sup>he will not be like Korah and his company, just as Yahweh had spoken to him by the hand of Moses.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “through Moses”">ag</a></sup></span>
</div>

## The Israelites Grumble

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–7; Psalm 106:23; Numbers 14:2"> The next day all the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ah</a></sup>grumbled against Moses and Aaron, saying, “You have killed the people of Yahweh!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Leviticus 9:23; Numbers 20:6"> Then, when the community had gathered against Moses and Aaron, they turned to the tent of assembly, and behold, the cloud covered it, and the glory of Yahweh appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Moses and Aaron came to the front of the tent of assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 16:21–22; Numbers 16:24; Numbers 20:6"> “Get away from the midst of this community, and I will finish them in an instant,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “in a moment”">ai</a></sup>but they fell on their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Leviticus 10:6"> And Moses and Aaron said, “Take the censer, and put fire on it from the altar. Place incense on it, and bring it quickly to the community, and make atonement for them, because wrath went out from the presence of Yahweh, and a plague has begun.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Deuteronomy 33:10–11; Psalm 106:29"> And so Aaron took it just as Moses had spoken, and he ran into the midst of the assembly, for behold, the plague had begun among the people; so he gave the incense and made atonement for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:25; Hebrews 7:24–25; Numbers 16:18"> He stood between the dead and between the living, and the plague was stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 25:9; Numbers 16:32–35; Hebrews 2:1–3"> Those who died by the plague were fourteen thousand seven hundred, besides those who died on account of Korah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:26–30; Numbers 16:43"> Then Aaron returned to Moses at the doorway of the tent of assembly, and the plague was stopped.</span>
</div>
