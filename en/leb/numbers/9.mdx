## The Passover

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 1:1; Exodus 40:2"> Yahweh spoke to Moses in the desert of Sinai, in the second year after they came out from the land of Egypt, in the first month, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; 2 Chronicles 35:1"> “Let the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>observe the Passover at its appointed time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 12:6–11; Leviticus 23:5; 2 Chronicles 30:2"> On the fourteenth day of this month ⸤at twilight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “between the two evenings”">b</a></sup>you will perform it at its appointed time according to all its decrees; and according to all its stipulations you will observe it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So Moses spoke to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>to observe the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:10; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 12:1–13"> And they observed the Passover on the fourteenth day of the month ⸤at twilight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “between the two evenings”">d</a></sup>in the desert of Sinai. According to all that Yahweh commanded Moses, thus the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 27:2; Exodus 18:15; Numbers 5:2"> And it happened, men who were unclean ⸤by a dead person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “by a life of a person”">f</a></sup>were not able to perform the Passover on that day. And they came ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">g</a></sup>Moses and Aaron on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Exodus 12:27"> And those men said to him, “Although we are unclean ⸤by a dead person⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “by a life of a person”">h</a></sup>why are we hindered from presenting the offering of Yahweh at its appointed time in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 85:8; Numbers 27:5; 1 Corinthians 4:4"> Moses said to them, “Stay. I will hear what Yahweh commands to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–2; Ephesians 2:12–13; 1 Corinthians 11:28"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>saying, ‘Each man that is unclean ⸤by a dead person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “by a life of a person”">k</a></sup>or is on a far journey, you or your ⸤descendants⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “generations”">l</a></sup>he will observe the Passover of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:2–15; Exodus 12:43–49; John 19:36"> On the second month on the fourteenth day ⸤at twilight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “between the two evenings”">m</a></sup>they will observe it; they will eat it with unleavened bread and bitter plants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 19:36; Exodus 12:46; Exodus 12:10"> They will leave none of it until morning, and they will not break a bone in it; they will observe it according to every decree of the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Exodus 12:15; Numbers 9:7"> But the man who is clean and not on a journey, and he fails to observe the Passover, that person will be cut off from the people because he did not present the offering of Yahweh on its appointed time. That man will bear his guilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 12:48–49; Leviticus 24:22; Isaiah 56:3–7"> If an alien dwells with you he will observe the Passover of Yahweh according to the decree of the Passover and according to its stipulation; thus you will have one decree for you, for the alien and for the native of the land.’ ”</span>
</div>

## The Cloud and the Fire

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Exodus 40:34; Nehemiah 9:19"> And on a day setting up the tabernacle, the cloud covered the tent of the tabernacle, the tent of the testimony; in the evening it was on the tabernacle as an appearance of fire until morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Exodus 40:38; 1 Corinthians 10:1"> So it was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “it will be”">n</a></sup>continually; the cloud would cover it and the appearance of fire by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 40:36–38; Psalm 32:8; John 10:9"> Whenever the cloud lifted up from on the tent, after that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>would set out, and in the place where the cloud dwelled, there the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">p</a></sup>camped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1; 2 John 1:6; Exodus 17:1"> On the ⸤command of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “mouth of Yahweh”">q</a></sup>the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">r</a></sup>would set out, and on the ⸤command of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “mouth of Yahweh”">s</a></sup>they encamped; all the days that the cloud dwelled on the tabernacle they encamped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 3:7; Numbers 1:52–53; Numbers 3:8"> And when the cloud prolonged on the tabernacle many days the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">t</a></sup>kept the kept requirement of Yahweh and did not set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> When the cloud ⸤remained⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “it was there”">u</a></sup>a number of days on the tabernacle, on the ⸤command of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “mouth of Yahweh”">v</a></sup>they encamped; and on the ⸤command of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “mouth of Yahweh”">w</a></sup>they set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Nehemiah 9:12"> When the cloud ⸤remained⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “it was there”">x</a></sup>from evening until morning, and the cloud lifted up in the morning, they would set out, or if it remained in the daytime and at night, when the cloud lifted up they set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 32:8; Exodus 40:36–37; Exodus 40:16"> When it was two days, a month, ⸤or a year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “or days”">y</a></sup>that the cloud prolonged to dwell on the tabernacle, the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">z</a></sup>encamped, and they did not set out; when it lifted up they set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 22:3; Numbers 9:19; Genesis 26:5"> On the ⸤command of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “mouth of Yahweh”">aa</a></sup>they encamped, and on the ⸤command of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “mouth of Yahweh”">ab</a></sup>they set out. They kept the requirement of Yahweh, on the ⸤command of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “mouth of Yahweh”">ac</a></sup>in the hand of Moses.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “through Moses”">ad</a></sup></span>
</div>
