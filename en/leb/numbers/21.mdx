## Arad Captured

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Numbers 33:40; Joshua 12:14"> The Canaanite king of Arad, who was dwelling in the Negev,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="An arid region south of the Judean hills">a</a></sup>heard that Israel came along the way of Atharim; he fought against Israel and took some of them captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:30; Genesis 28:20; 1 Samuel 1:11"> Israel made a vow to Yahweh, and they said, “If you will surely give this people into our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “my”">b</a></sup>hand, then we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “I”">c</a></sup>⸤will destroy⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “devote to God”">d</a></sup>their cities.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 14:45; Deuteronomy 1:44; Psalm 10:17"> Yahweh heard the voice of Israel; he gave to them the Canaanites, and ⸤they destroyed them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “they devoted to God”">e</a></sup>and their cities. They called the name of the place Hormah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:9; Judges 11:18; Numbers 32:7"> They set out from Mount Hor by the way of the ⸤Red Sea⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sea of reed”">f</a></sup>to go around the land of Edom; but ⸤the people became impatient⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the life of the people became short”">g</a></sup>along the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 78:19; Numbers 16:13–14; Exodus 17:2–3"> The people spoke against God and against Moses, “Why have you brought us from Egypt to die in the desert? There is no food and no water, and our hearts detest this miserable food.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Bronze Serpent

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Jeremiah 8:17; Deuteronomy 8:15"> And Yahweh sent among the people poisonous snakes; they bit the people, and many people from Israel died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 8:24; Numbers 11:2; Psalm 78:34"> The people came to Moses and said, “We have sinned because we have spoken against Yahweh and against you. Pray to Yahweh and let him remove the snakes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “snake”">h</a></sup>from among us.” So Moses prayed for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 3:14; Psalm 106:43–45; Psalm 145:8"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Make for yourself a snake and place it on a pole. When<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “And it will happen”">i</a></sup>anyone is bitten and looks at it, that person will live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:14–15; Hebrews 12:2; John 6:40"> So Moses made a snake of bronze, and he placed it on the pole; whenever<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “And it will happen”">j</a></sup>a snake bit someone, and that person looked at the snake of bronze, he lived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 33:43–45"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>set out and encamped at Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They set out from Oboth and encamped at Iye Abarim in the desert, which was in front of Moab ⸤toward the sunrise⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “from the east of the sun”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:13–14"> From there they set out and encamped at the valley of Zered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Numbers 22:36; Isaiah 16:2"> From there they set out and encamped beyond Arnon, which is in the desert that goes out from the boundary of the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">m</a></sup>because Arnon is the boundary of Moab, between Moab and the Amorites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; 2 Samuel 1:18"> Therefore thus it is said in the scroll of the Wars of Yahweh,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Waheb in Suphah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the wadis of Arnon,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 21:28; Deuteronomy 2:18; Deuteronomy 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">and the slope of the wadis</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that spreads out to the dwelling of Ar</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and lies at the boundary of Moab.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 20:8; Isaiah 12:3; Isaiah 41:17–18"> From there they went to Beer, which is the water well where Yahweh spoke to Moses, “Gather the people, that I may give them water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:13; Exodus 15:1–2; Psalm 106:12"> Then Israel sang this song, “Arise, well water! Sing to it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 Timothy 6:17–18; James 4:12"> Well water that the princes dug, that the leaders of the people dug, with a staff and with their rods.” And from the desert they continued to Mattanah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and from Mattanah to Nahaliel, and from Nahaliel to Bamoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:28; Numbers 23:14; Deuteronomy 1:5"> and from Bamoth to the valley that is in the territory of Moab, by the top of Pisgah, which overlooks the surface of the wasteland.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Sihon and Og Defeated

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–28; Numbers 20:14–19; Judges 11:19–21"> Israel sent messengers to Sihon, the king of the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">o</a></sup>saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:17"> “Let us go through your land; we will not turn aside into a field or vineyard; we will not drink well water along the way of the king until we have gone through your territory.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 11:20; Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Isaiah 15:4"> But Sihon did not allow Israel to go through his territory. Sihon gathered all his people and went out to meet Israel; he came to the desert, to Jahaz, and he fought against Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Joshua 24:8; Genesis 32:22"> But Israel struck him with the edge of the sword, and they took possession of his land from Arnon to Jabbok, until the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">p</a></sup>because the boundary of the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">q</a></sup>was strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 32:33–42; Isaiah 16:8–9; Jeremiah 48:2"> Israel took all these cities, and Israel inhabited all the cities of the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">r</a></sup>in Heshbon, and in all its environs.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “her daughters;” other modern versions translate “its villages”">s</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Because Heshbon was the city of Sihon king of the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">t</a></sup>who had fought against the former king of Moab and taken all his land from his hand until Arnon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; Numbers 21:14; Habakkuk 2:6"> Thus the ones who quote proverbs say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Come to Heshbon! Let it be built!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And let the city of Sihon be established.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:15; Jeremiah 48:45–46; Isaiah 15:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because fire went out from Heshbon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a flame from the city of Sihon;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it consumed Ar of Moab,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the lords of the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “the dominant”">u</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">high places of Arnon.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 11:24; 2 Kings 23:13; Jeremiah 48:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">Woe to you, Moab!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have perished, people of Chemosh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He has given his sons as fugitives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his daughters into captivity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the king of the Amorites,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">v</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Sihon.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Isaiah 15:2; Jeremiah 48:22"> <span className="pl pl-2">We destroyed them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Heshbon has perished up to Dibon;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we laid waste up to Nophah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which ⸤reaches⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “is up to”">w</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Medeba.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 13:8–12; Numbers 32:33–42; Deuteronomy 3:16–17"> Thus Israel lived in the land of the Amorites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">x</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:32; Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:35"> Moses sent to explore Jaazer; they captured ⸤its environs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Hebrew “her daughters;” other modern versions translate “its villages”">y</a></sup>and dispossessed the Amorites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">z</a></sup>who were there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 1:4; Deuteronomy 29:7"> Then they turned and went up by the way of the Bashan, and Og king of the Bashan and all his people went out to meet them for battle at Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 135:10–11; Joshua 8:7; Deuteronomy 3:2–3"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Do not fear him because I will give him and all his people and all his land into your hand. You will do to him just as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">aa</a></sup>who was living in Heshbon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:7–8; Joshua 13:12; Deuteronomy 3:3–17"> And so they destroyed him and his sons, and all his people until they had not spared a survivor; and they took possession of his land.</span>
</div>
</div>
