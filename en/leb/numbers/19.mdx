## Ashes of the Red Heifer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 22:20–25; Deuteronomy 21:3; 1 Samuel 6:7"> “This is the decree of the law that Yahweh has commanded, saying, ‘Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>and let them take to you a red heifer without a physical defect, on which a yoke ⸤has not been placed⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “has not gone up”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 4:12; Leviticus 4:21; Leviticus 16:27"> And you will give it to Eleazar the priest, and it will be brought<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “he will bring it out”">c</a></sup>out to a place outside the camp, and it will be slaughtered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “he will slaughter it”">d</a></sup>in his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 4:17; Leviticus 4:6; Leviticus 16:14"> Then Eleazar the priest will take some of its blood on his finger and spatter it toward the mouth of the tent of assembly seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 29:14; Leviticus 4:11–12; Psalm 22:14"> The heifer will be burned<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “The heifer will burn”">e</a></sup>in his sight; its skin, its meat, and its blood, in addition to its offal, will burn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 14:4; Leviticus 14:49; Psalm 51:7"> The priest will take cedar wood, hyssop, and crimson thread, and he will throw them in the midst of ⸤the burning heifer⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the burning of the heifer”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 11:40; Numbers 19:8"> The priest will wash his garments and his body in the water, and afterward he will come to the camp; the priest will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The one who burns it will wash his garments and his body in water; he will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13; Numbers 19:13; Numbers 19:20–21"> A clean man will gather the ashes of the heifer, and he will put them in a clean ⸤place outside the camp⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “an outside place of the camp”">g</a></sup>it will be for the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>as a requirement for waters of impurity; it is a purification offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 19:7–8; Colossians 3:11"> The one who gathers the ashes of the heifer will wash his garments; he will be unclean until evening. It will be an eternal decree for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>and for one who dwells as an alien in their midst.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; Numbers 31:19; Leviticus 21:1"> “ ‘The one who touches a corpse of ⸤any person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “any human person”">j</a></sup>will be unclean for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 31:19; Revelation 7:14; Leviticus 7:17"> He will purify himself on the third day, and on the seventh day he will be clean. If he does not purify himself on the third day, he will not be clean on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 15:31; Leviticus 22:3; Leviticus 7:20"> Anyone who touches a corpse, the person of a human being who died, and does not purify himself, defiles the tabernacle of Yahweh, and that person will be cut off from Israel because the waters of impurity were not sprinkled on him. He will still be unclean, and uncleanness is on him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> “ ‘This is the law of a person who dies in a tent: everyone who comes into the tent and all who are in the tent will be unclean seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 14:36; Leviticus 11:32; Numbers 31:20"> Every container that is opened that does not have a lid cord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="That is, “that does not have a lid tied shut”">k</a></sup>on it is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 31:19; Matthew 23:27; Luke 11:44"> Anyone ⸤in the open field⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “upon the face of a field”">l</a></sup>who touches ⸤one who has been slain⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the dead of sword”">m</a></sup>or a corpse, or a bone of a person, or a burial site, he will be unclean for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 7:38; Genesis 26:19; Revelation 7:17"> For the unclean person they will take<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “he will take”">n</a></sup>from the powder of the ⸤burnt purification offering⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “burning of the sin offering”">o</a></sup>and they will put<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “he will put”">p</a></sup>running water into a container.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 9:14; Numbers 19:9"> A clean person will take hyssop and dip it into the water and sprinkle it on the tent and on all the objects and persons who were there, and on one who touched the bone, or the one slain, or the dead, or the burial site.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; Hebrews 10:22; Ezekiel 36:25"> The clean person will spatter the unclean on the third day and on the seventh day; and on the seventh day he will purify him, and he will wash his garments; he will bathe in the waters, and in the evening he will be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 16:16; Genesis 17:14; Numbers 19:13"> But the man who is unclean and does not purify himself, that person will be cut off from the midst of the assembly because he defiled the sanctuary of Yahweh; the water of impurity was not sprinkled on him; he is unclean.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 9:13–14; Hebrews 10:4; Hebrews 9:10"> “ ‘It will be an eternal decree for them. The one who spatters the waters of impurity will wash his garments, and the one who touches the waters of impurity will be unclean until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Haggai 2:13; Leviticus 7:19; Matthew 15:19–20"> Anything that the unclean person touches will be unclean, and the person who touches it will be unclean until the evening.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
