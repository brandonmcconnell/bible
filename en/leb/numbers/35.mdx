## Cities for the Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 22:1; Numbers 31:12; Numbers 33:50"> Yahweh spoke to Moses on the desert plains of Moab beyond the Jordan across Jericho, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 14:3–4; Ezekiel 48:8; Joshua 21:2–42"> “Command the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>that they give to the Levites from the inheritance of their property cities to live in; and you will give to the Levites pastureland all around the cities.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “them”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:14; Joshua 21:11; Ezekiel 45:2"> The cities will be theirs to live in, and their pasturelands will be for their domestic animals, for their possessions, and their animals.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> “The pasturelands of the cities that you will give to the Levites will extend from the wall of the city to a distance of a thousand cubits all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> You will measure outside the city the eastern edge two thousand cubits, for the southern edge two thousand cubits, for the western edge two thousand cubits, and for the northern edge two thousand cubits, with the city in the middle; this will be for them the pasturelands of the cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 21:3; Psalm 9:9"> “All the cities that you will give the Levites will be six cities of refuge, to which the killer can flee; in addition to them you will give forty-two cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:3–42; 1 Chronicles 6:54–81"> All the cities that you will give to the Levites will be forty-eight cities, them with their pasturelands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:54; Numbers 33:54; Joshua 21:1–42"> And the cities that you will give from the property of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>you will take more from the larger group and less from the smaller group; each of them will give according to the portion of their inheritance according to the portion that he inherits.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Cities of Refuge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:9; Deuteronomy 19:1–2; Leviticus 14:34"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>and say to them, ‘When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:2; Numbers 35:6"> you will select for yourselves cities for your cities of refuge, that a killer who has killed a person unintentionally can flee there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 20:3–6; Deuteronomy 19:6; Joshua 20:9"> The cities will be to you a refuge from a redeemer, so that the killer will not die until he stands before the community for judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The cities that you are to give will be your six cities of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:8–10; Deuteronomy 4:41–43; Joshua 20:7–9"> You will give three cities across the Jordan and three cities in the land of Canaan; they will be cities of refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 15:16; Galatians 3:28; Romans 3:29"> To the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>to the alien, and to the temporary resident in their midst there will be these six cities as a refuge to which anyone who unintentionally kills a person may flee.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17; Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–13"> “ ‘But if he hit him with an object of iron, so that he dies, the killer must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 21:18"> And if he hit him with a stone in the hand, by which he will die, and he does die, he is a killer; the killer must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Or if he hit him with a wooden object, by which he will die, and he does die, he is a killer; the killer must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 20:3; Deuteronomy 19:6; Deuteronomy 19:12"> The blood avenger himself will put the killer to death; he must put him to death when meeting him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 21:14; Deuteronomy 19:11; 2 Samuel 3:27"> If he shoves him in hatred, or he throws something at him with intention, and he dies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> or if he hits him in hostility with his hand, and he dies, the one that struck him will put to death the killer when meeting him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 35:11; Exodus 21:13; Joshua 20:3"> “ ‘Or if in an instant he shoved him, not in hostility, or threw something at him without intention,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> or with any stone, without seeing it dropped on him so that he dies, while he was not seeking his injury,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 20:6; Numbers 35:12"> then the community will judge between the striker and between the blood avenger according to these ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 4:3; Romans 3:24–26"> The community will deliver the killer from the hand of the blood avenger, and the community will restore him to the city of his refuge to which he fled; and he will live there in it until the death of the high priest who was anointed with holy oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But if the killer surely goes out of the territory of the city of his refuge to which he fled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 22:2; Deuteronomy 19:10; Deuteronomy 19:6"> and the blood avenger finds him outside the territory of the city of his refuge, and the blood avenger kills the killer, ⸤he will not be guilty of blood⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “there will not be blood for him”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 10:26–30; Hebrews 6:4–8"> because he must live in the city of his refuge until the death of the high priest. But after the death of the high priest the killer will return to the land of his property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 27:11; Numbers 27:1"> These things will be as a decree of justice for you for your generations in all your dwellings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28; Deuteronomy 19:15; Matthew 18:16"> “ ‘If anyone kills a person, the killer will be put to death ⸤according to the testimony⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “according to the mouth”">h</a></sup>of witnesses, but someone cannot die on testimony of one person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:11–13; Genesis 9:5–6; Psalm 51:14"> Also, you will not take a ransom payment for the life of a killer who is guilty of death; indeed, he must surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 4:12; Galatians 3:10–13; Galatians 2:21"> You will not take a ransom payment for the one that flees to the city of his refuge, so that he may return to live in the land before the death of the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 11:50–51; Psalm 106:38; Hosea 4:2–3"> So you will not pollute the land in which you are; because blood pollutes the land, and no atonement can be made for the land for the blood that is poured out on it except with the blood of the one who poured it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 5:3; Psalm 135:21; Revelation 21:27"> You will not defile the land on which you are living because I am living in the midst of it; I am Yahweh; I am living in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
