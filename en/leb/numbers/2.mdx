## The Arrangement of the Camps

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; Numbers 10:22; Numbers 1:50"> “The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>will encamp each with his standard, with a banner according to ⸤their families⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">b</a></sup>they will encamp around the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:10; Ruth 4:20; Luke 3:32–33"> The ones who encamp on the eastern side, toward the sunrise, will be of the standard of the camp of Judah according to their divisions; and the leader of the descendants of Judah will be Nahshon son of Amminadab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:22; Numbers 1:27"> and his division and ⸤the ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">c</a></sup>are seventy-four thousand six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 1:8; Numbers 7:18; Numbers 7:23"> And the ones who encamp next to him will be the tribe of Issachar. And the leader of the descendants of Issachar will be Nethanel son of Zuar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 26:25; Numbers 1:29"> and his division are fifty-four thousand four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:9; Numbers 7:24; Numbers 10:16"> For the tribe of Zebulun: the leader of the descendants of Zebulun will be Eliab son of Helon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 26:26–27; Numbers 1:31"> and his division and the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of him,” or “the ones mustered of him”">d</a></sup>are fifty-seven thousand four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 10:14"> All those counted from the camp of Judah are one hundred and eighty-six thousand four hundred. They will set out first according to their divisions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:5; Genesis 49:3–4; Numbers 7:35"> “The standard of the camp of Reuben will be to the south according to their divisions. The leader of the descendants will be Elizur son of Shedeur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 26:7; Numbers 1:21"> And his division and the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of him,” or “the ones mustered of him”">e</a></sup>are forty-six thousand five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 1:6; Numbers 10:19; Numbers 7:41"> Those encamped next to him will be the tribe of Simeon. The leader of the descendants of Simeon will be Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 26:14; Numbers 1:23"> And his division and the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">f</a></sup>are fifty-nine thousand three hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:14; Numbers 7:42; Numbers 7:47"> For the tribe of Gad: the leader of the descendants of Gad will be Eliasaph son of Reuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:18; Numbers 1:25"> And his division and the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">g</a></sup>are forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 10:18; Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:24"> All ⸤those counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “those mustered”">h</a></sup>from the camp of Reuben are one hundred and fifty-one thousand four hundred and fifty. They will set out second according to their divisions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 10:21; Numbers 10:17; Numbers 3:38"> “The tent of assembly the camp of the Levites will set out in the midst of the camps; they will set out just as they encamped, ⸤each according to their standards⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “each man on his hand according to their standards”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 10:22; Genesis 48:14–20"> “The standard of the camp of Ephraim according to their divisions will be to the west. The leader of the descendants of Ephraim will be Elishama son of Ammihud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:33; Numbers 26:37"> And his division and the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">j</a></sup>are forty thousand five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 7:54; Numbers 10:23"> The tribe of Manasseh will be next to him. The leader of the descendants of the tribe of Manasseh will be Camaliel son of Pedahzur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 26:34; Numbers 1:35"> And his division and the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">k</a></sup>are thirty-two thousand two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 1:11; Numbers 7:60; Numbers 10:24"> For the tribe of Benjamin: the leader of the descendants of Benjamin will be Abidan son of Gideoni.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:37; Numbers 26:41"> And his division and the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">l</a></sup>are thirty-five thousand four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:22; Numbers 2:31; Psalm 80:2"> All ⸤those counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “those mustered”">m</a></sup>from the camp of Ephraim are one hundred and eight thousand one hundred. They will set out third according to their divisions.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 1:12; Numbers 7:66; Numbers 10:25"> “The standard of the camp of Dan according to their divisions will be to the north. The leader of the descendants of Dan will be Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 26:43; Numbers 1:39"> And his division and the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">n</a></sup>are sixty-two thousand seven hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:13; Numbers 7:77; Numbers 7:72"> Those encamped next to him will be the tribe of Asher. The leader of the descendants of Asher will be Pagiel son of Ocran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 1:41; Numbers 26:47"> And his division and the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">o</a></sup>are forty-one thousand five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:15; Numbers 10:27; Numbers 26:48–50"> For the tribe of Naphtali: the leader of the descendants of Naphtali will be Ahira son of Enan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 1:42–43; Numbers 26:50"> And his division and the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">p</a></sup>are fifty-three thousand four hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 10:25; Numbers 2:16; Numbers 2:9"> All the ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">q</a></sup>from the camp of Dan are one hundred and fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They will set out ⸤last⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “from behind”">r</a></sup>according to their divisions.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 38:26; Numbers 2:9"> These were the ones counted of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">s</a></sup>according to ⸤their families⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">t</a></sup>all those counted from the camps according to their divisions were six hundred and three thousand five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 26:57–62; Numbers 1:47–49"> The Levites were not counted in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">u</a></sup>just as Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 119:6; Numbers 24:2; Numbers 24:5–6"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup>did everything that Yahweh commanded Moses. They encamped according to their standards, and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Hebrew “each one,” or “each man”">w</a></sup>set out each one according to their clans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “his clans”">x</a></sup>among ⸤their families⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “the house of his fathers”">y</a></sup></span>
</div>
