## War Against the Midianites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> “Seek vengeance for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>on the Midianites; afterward you will be gathered to your people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> Moses spoke to the people, saying, “Arm yourself from among your men for the battle, so that they will ⸤go⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “be”">b</a></sup>against Midian to mete out the vengeance of Yahweh on Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> A thousand from each tribe of every tribe of Israel you will send to battle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So theywere assigned from the thousands of Israel, a thousand from each tribe, twelve thousand equipped for battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> Moses sent them, a thousand from each tribe, to the battle, and Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest to the battle with them, and the vessels of the sanctuary and the trumpets of the blast were in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> And they fought against Midian just as Yahweh commanded Moses, and they killed every male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> They killed the kings of Midian in addition to the ones they had slain: Evi and Rekem and Zur and Hur and Reba, the five kings of Midian; they also killed Balaam son of Beor by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>took captive the women of Midian and their children, and they plundered all their domestic animals and all their livestock and all their wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> They burned all their cities where they dwelled and all their camps with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> They took all the plunder and all the war-booty with the humans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “human”">d</a></sup>and domestic animals.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> They brought the captives, the war-booty, and the plunder to Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and to the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>to the camp to the desert-plateau of Moab, which was on the Jordan across Jericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> And Moses and Eleazar the priest and all the leaders of the community went out to meet them outside the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> But Moses was angry toward the leaders of the troops, the commanders of the thousands and the commanders of the hundreds, who came from the battle of the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> And Moses said to them, “You have kept alive every female?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> Behold, these women ⸤caused⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “were to”">g</a></sup>the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>by the word of Balaam, to be in apostasy against Yahweh in the matter of Peor, so that the plague was among the community of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Now kill every male among the little children, and kill every woman who has ⸤had sexual intercourse with a man⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “who has known the bed of a male”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> But all the females who have not ⸤had sexual intercourse with a man⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “who has known the bed of a male”">j</a></sup>keep alive for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> And you, camp outside the camp seven days; all who killed a person and all who touched the slain purify yourselves on the third day and on the seventh day, you and your captives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> You will purify yourselves and every garment and every object of hide and all the work of goats’ hair, and every object of wood.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> Then Eleazar the priest said to the men of the battle who came from the war, “This is the decree of the law that Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Only the gold and the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin, and the lead—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> everything that will go through the fire—you will pass through the fire, and it will be clean, and only in waters of impurity will it be purified. Whatever does not go into the fire you will pass through the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> And you will wash your garments on the seventh day and be clean, and afterward you will come into the camp.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Division of the War-Booty

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Yahweh said to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “You and Eleazar the priest and the leaders of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">k</a></sup>of the community, ⸤take count⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “lift up the head”">l</a></sup>of the war-booty that was captured, both humans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “human”">m</a></sup>and the domestic animals;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">n</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalm 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> divide the war-booty between those who engaged in the war, who went out to the battle, and all the community.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> Exact a tribute for Yahweh from the men of the war, those who went out to the battle, one from five hundred persons, and from the cattle and from the male donkeys and from the flock;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> take from their half and give it to Eleazar the priest as a contribution to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> From half of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>take one share drawn by lot from the fifty from the humans,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Hebrew “human”">p</a></sup>from the cattle, from the male donkeys, from the flock, from all the domestic animals,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">q</a></sup>and give them to the Levities who keep the responsibilities of the tabernacle of Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Moses and Eleazar the priest did just as Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Thus the war-booty that remained of the plunder that the people of the battle plundered was six hundred and seventy-five thousand flocks of sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> seventy-two thousand cattle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> sixty-one thousand male donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and the life of humankind, from the women who did not ⸤have sexual intercourse with a man⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “who has known the bed of a male”">r</a></sup>all the persons<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “person”">s</a></sup>were thirty-two thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The half of the share that was going out to the battle: the number of the flock of sheep was three hundred and thirty-seven thousand five hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> the tribute to Yahweh from the flock was six hundred and seventy-five;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> and the cattle were thirty-six thousand; and the tribute to Yahweh was seventy-two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Of the male donkeys there were thirty thousand five hundred, and the tribute to Yahweh was sixty-one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> the humans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “human”">t</a></sup>were sixteen thousand, and the tribute to Yahweh was thirty-two persons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> And Moses gave away the tribute of the contribution of Yahweh to Eleazar the priest, just as Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> From the half of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">u</a></sup>which Moses divided from the men who were fighting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> the half that belonged to the community was three hundred and thirty-seven thousand five hundred from the flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and thirty-six thousand cattle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and thirty thousand five hundred male donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and sixteen thousand humans.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “human”">v</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> From the half that belonged to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">w</a></sup>Moses took one share drawn by lot out of every fifty humans<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “human”">x</a></sup>and domestic animals, and he gave them to the Levites, who keep the responsibility of the tabernacle of Yahweh, just as Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Then the leaders of the thousands of the army, the commanders of the thousands and the commanders of the hundreds, approached Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalm 72:14"> and they said to Moses, “Your servants have ⸤taken count⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “lifted up the head”">y</a></sup>of the men of war who were ⸤in our charge⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “in our hand”">z</a></sup>and no man is missing from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalm 116:12"> So we brought the offering of Yahweh, what each man found, objects of gold, bangles,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “bangle”">aa</a></sup>bracelets,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “bracelet”">ab</a></sup>rings,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Hebrew “ring”">ac</a></sup>earrings,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Hebrew “earring”">ad</a></sup>and female ornaments,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Hebrew “ornament”">ae</a></sup>to make atonement for ourselves ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">af</a></sup>Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from them, all objects of work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> All the gold of the contribution that they raised up to Yahweh, from the commanders of the thousands and the commanders of the hundreds, was sixteen thousand seven hundred and fifty shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> The men of battle plundered each for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> So Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from the commanders of the thousands and hundreds, and they brought it to the tent of the assembly as a memorial for the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ag</a></sup>before Yahweh.</span>
</div>
