## God Commands Moses to Take a Census

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

You and Aaron must muster them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “count them,” or “summon them,” or “enroll them”">f</a></sup>for their wars.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 7:30; Genesis 30:5–20; Genesis 49:1–33"> And these are the names of the men who will ⸤assist you⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “stand with you”">h</a></sup>from Reuben, Elizur son of Shedeur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 7:36; Numbers 2:12"> from Simeon, Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:32; Numbers 10:14; Matthew 1:2–5"> from Judah, Nahshon son of Amminadab;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 7:18; Numbers 10:15; Numbers 2:5"> from Issachar, Nethanel son of Zuar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 7:24; Numbers 10:16; Numbers 2:7"> from Zebulun, Eliab son of Helon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 7:54; Numbers 7:48; 1 Chronicles 7:26–27"> From the descendants of Joseph: from Ephraim, Elishama son of Ammihud; from Manasseh, Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 7:60; Numbers 10:24; Numbers 2:22"> From Benjamin, Abidan son of Gideoni;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 7:66; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 10:25"> from Dan, Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 7:72; Numbers 10:26; Numbers 2:27"> from Asher, Pagiel son of Ocran;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 2:14; Numbers 7:42; Numbers 10:20"> from Gad, Eliasaph son of Deuel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 7:78; Numbers 10:27; Numbers 2:29"> and from Naphtali, Ahira son of Enan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Exodus 18:21; Exodus 18:25"> These are the ones summoned from the community, the leaders of their ancestors’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “fathers’ ”">i</a></sup>tribes; they are the heads of Israel’s clans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:3; Revelation 7:4–17; Numbers 1:5–15"> So Moses and Aaron took these men who had been designated by name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezra 2:59; Nehemiah 7:61; Hebrews 7:3"> and they summoned the entire community on the first day of the second month. <span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:2; Numbers 26:1–2; 2 Samuel 24:1–10"> just as Yahweh commanded Moses. And he counted them in the desert of Sinai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 26:5–7; Numbers 2:10–11; Genesis 46:9"> The descendants of Reuben, the firstborn of Israel, their genealogies according to their clans, according to ⸤their families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">l</a></sup>according to the number of names, every male individually from ⸤twenty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">m</a></sup>and above, everyone who is able to go to war:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 26:7; Numbers 2:10–11"> those who were counted from the tribe of Reuben were forty-six thousand five hundred.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 26:12–14; Genesis 34:25–30; Numbers 2:12–13"> From the descendants of Simeon, their genealogies according to their clans, according to ⸤their families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">n</a></sup>those who were counted according to the number of their names, every individual male from ⸤twenty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">o</a></sup>and above, everyone who is able to go to war:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 26:14; Numbers 2:13; Numbers 25:8–9"> those who were counted from the tribe of Simeon were fifty-nine thousand three hundred.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 26:15–18; Genesis 46:16; Numbers 2:14–15"> From the descendants of Gad, their genealogies according to their clans, according to ⸤their families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">p</a></sup>according to the number of names, from ⸤those twenty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">q</a></sup>and above, everyone who is able to go to war:</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:35; Numbers 26:19–22; 2 Samuel 24:9"> From the descendants of Judah, their genealogies according to their clans, according to ⸤their families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">r</a></sup>according to the number of names, from ⸤those twenty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">s</a></sup>and above, everyone who is able to go to war:</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 26:35–37; Deuteronomy 33:17; Genesis 46:20"> From the descendants of Joseph: from the descendants of Ephraim, their genealogies according to their clans, according to ⸤their families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">x</a></sup>according to the number of names, from ⸤those twenty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">y</a></sup>and above, everyone who is able to go to war:</span>

<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Numbers 26:48–50; Genesis 46:24"> From the descendants of Naphtali, their genealogies according to their clans, according to ⸤their families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “their fathers”">ah</a></sup>according to the number of names, from ⸤those twenty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">ai</a></sup>and above, everyone who is able to go to war:</span>

<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 26:64; Numbers 1:2–16"> These are the ones counted whom Moses and Aaron mustered,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Or “counted,” or “summoned,” or “enrolled”">aj</a></sup>with the twelve leaders of Israel, each one from ⸤his family⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Literally “the house of his fathers”">ak</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Numbers 2:2; Numbers 2:34; Numbers 10:1–36"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ax</a></sup>will encamp, each in their own camp, and each by their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Hebrew “his” or “its”">ay</a></sup>own banner according to their divisions.</span>
