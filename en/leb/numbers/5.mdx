## Rules Concerning Those Unclean

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 21:1; Leviticus 13:46; Numbers 12:14"> “Command the ⸤Israelites⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>they must send everyone from the camp who is afflicted with a rash,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The precise meaning is uncertain; many modern translations suggest “leprosy”">b</a></sup>everyone with a fluid discharge, and everyone unclean through contact with a corpse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:14; Leviticus 26:11–12; Psalm 68:18"> You will send away ⸤both male and female⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “from male until female”">c</a></sup>you will send them ⸤outside the camp⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to an outside place of the camp”">d</a></sup>They must not make unclean their camps where I am dwelling in their midst.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>did so. They sent them away ⸤outside the camp⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to an outside place of the camp”">f</a></sup>just as Yahweh spoke to Moses, so did the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Rules of Restitution

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1–4; Leviticus 5:14–7"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>‘When a man or woman ⸤commits⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “does”">i</a></sup>any of the sins of humankind by acting unfaithfully, it is a sin against Yahweh, and that person will be guilty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 5:5; Leviticus 26:40; Joshua 7:19"> they will confess their sin that they did and will make restitution for their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">j</a></sup>guilt by adding a fifth ⸤to it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “on top of it”">k</a></sup>and giving it ⸤to whomever was wronged⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “to whomever he was guilty”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 25:25–26"> But if the man does not have a redeemer to make restitution to him for the reparation, the reparation is to be given to Yahweh for the priest, in addition to the ram of atonement by which atonement is made for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:3–4; Leviticus 7:6–14; Leviticus 6:17–18"> And every contribution of all the holy objects of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>that they bring to the priest for him will be his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:13"> The holy objects of a man will be for him;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="That is, the priest">n</a></sup>whatever he gives to the priest will be for him.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rules Concerning an Unfaithful Wife

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 5:19–20; Proverbs 2:16–17"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>and say to them, ‘If any man’s wife goes astray and acts unfaithfully to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 18:20; Leviticus 20:10; Proverbs 7:18–19"> and a man sleeps with her and ejaculates and it is hidden from the eyes of her husband and she is concealed, although she is defiled, and there is no witness against her and she was not caught,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:6; Proverbs 6:34; Zephaniah 3:8"> if a spirit of jealousy comes over him, and he is jealous of his wife and she is defiled; or if a spirit of jealousy comes over him and he is jealous of his wife but she is not defiled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:16; 1 Kings 17:18; Leviticus 5:11"> he will bring his wife to the priest. And he will bring her offering for her, one-tenth of an ephah of flour. He will not pour oil on it, and he will not put frankincense on it because it is a grain offering of jealousy, a grain offering of remembering,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “memorial”">p</a></sup>a reminding of guilt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 2:22–23; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 13:4"> “ ‘Then the priest will bring her near and present her ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “before the face of”">q</a></sup>Yahweh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 30:18; John 8:6; Numbers 19:2–9"> the priest will take holy waters in a clay vessel, and from the dust that is on the floor of the tabernacle, and the priest will put it into the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 5:4; Numbers 5:22; Revelation 10:9–10"> And the priest will present the woman ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “before the face of”">r</a></sup>Yahweh, and he will uncover the head of the woman; he will then put in her hands the grain offering of the remembering—which is the grain offering of jealousy—and in the hand of the priest will be the waters of bitterness that brings a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:2; Numbers 5:12; Matthew 26:63"> Then the priest will make her swear an oath, and he will say to the woman, “If a man has not slept with you, and if you have not had an impurity affair under your husband, go unpunished from the waters of bitterness that brings this curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 5:12"> But if you have had an affair under your husband, and if you are defiled and a man other than your husband had intercourse with you,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; Nehemiah 10:29; 1 Samuel 14:24"> the priest will make the woman swear an oath of the sworn oath of the curse, the priest will say to the woman, “May Yahweh give you a curse and a sworn oath in the midst of your people with Yahweh making<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “giving”">s</a></sup>your hip fall away<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Or “waste away”">t</a></sup>and your stomach swollen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 109:18; Psalm 89:52; Job 31:39–40"> and these waters that bring a curse will go into your intestines to cause your womb to swell and to make your hip fall away.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “waste away”">u</a></sup>And the women will say, “Amen. Amen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Psalm 51:1; Psalm 51:9"> “ ‘And the priests will write these curses on the scroll, and he will wipe them off into the waters of the bitterness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 5:3–4; Malachi 3:5"> He will make the woman drink the waters of the bitterness that brings<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Hebrew “bring”">v</a></sup>a curse, and the waters of bitterness that bring a curse will go into her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 8:27; Numbers 5:18; Numbers 5:15"> The priest will take the grain offering of jealousy from the hand of the woman, and he will wave the grain offering ⸤before Yahweh⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “before the face of Yahweh”">w</a></sup>and he will present it to the altar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Leviticus 2:9; Leviticus 5:12"> the priest will grasp her memorial offering from the grain offering, and he will turn it into smoke on the altar, and afterward he will make the woman drink the waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:18; Zechariah 8:13; Jeremiah 29:18"> When he has made her drink the waters, it will come about, if she has defiled herself and acted unfaithfully to her husband and the waters of bitterness that bring a curse go into her and her stomach swells and her hip falls away,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “wastes away”">x</a></sup>the woman will be as a curse in the midst of her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 7:7–10; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalm 113:9"> And if the woman is not defiled, and she is pure, she will go unpunished and be able to conceive children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 5:19; Numbers 5:12; Numbers 5:15"> “ ‘This is the regulation of jealousy, when a woman has an affair under her husband and she is defiled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> or when a spirit of jealousy comes over a man and he is jealous of his wife, he will present the woman ⸤before Yahweh⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “before the face of”">y</a></sup>and the priest will do to her all of this law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Romans 2:8–9; Ezekiel 18:4"> The man will go unpunished from guilt, and the woman, she will bear her guilt.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
