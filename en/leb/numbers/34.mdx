## The Land of Canaan Is Divided

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalm 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> “Command the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>and say to them, ‘When you come into the land of Canaan, this is the land that was allotted to you as an inheritance, the land of Canaan according to its boundaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> Your southern edge will be from the desert of Zin toward the side of Edom, and your southern border will be from the end of the Salt Sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, the Dead Sea">b</a></sup>to the east;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> your boundary will turn from the south to the ascent of Akrabbim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “Scorpions”">c</a></sup>and will pass over to Zin, and its limits will be from the south of Kadesh Barnea; it will continue to Hazar Addar and pass over to Azmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> The boundary will turn from Azmon to the valley of Egypt, and its limits will be to the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> “ ‘Your western boundary will be the Great Sea;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean">d</a></sup>this will be your western boundary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> Your northern border will be from the Great Sea;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean">e</a></sup>you will make a boundary from the Great Sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="That is, the Mediterranean">f</a></sup>to Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> From Mount Hor you will make a boundary to reach Hamath; the limits of the territory will be at Zedad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> The boundary will go out to Ziphron, and its limits will be at Hazar Enan. This will be your boundary to the north.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “ ‘You will mark out your eastern boundary from Hazar Enan to Shepham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> the boundary will go down from Shepham to Riblah from the east side of Ain, and the boundary will go down and border on the eastern side of the Sea of Kinnereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> The boundary will go down to the Jordan, and its limits will be at the Salt Sea.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="That is, the Dead Sea">g</a></sup>This will be your land according to its boundaries all around.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> So Moses commanded the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>saying, “This is the land that you will obtain as an inheritance for yourself by lot, which Yahweh commanded to give to the nine and a half tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> For the tribe of the children of the Reubenites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “Reubenite”">i</a></sup>the children of the Gadites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “Gadite”">j</a></sup>and the half-tribe of Manasseh took their inheritance according to the house of their families.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “their fathers”">k</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> The two and a half tribes have taken their inheritance from beyond the Jordan across Jericho, east toward the sunrise.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> “These are the names of the men who will divide up the land for you for inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> You will take one leader from every tribe to divide up the land for inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> These are the names of the men: of the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> of the tribe of the descendants of Simeon, Samuel son of Ammihud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalm 68:27"> of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Chislon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “sons”">l</a></sup>of Dan, the leader Bukki son of Jogli;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “sons”">m</a></sup>of Joseph, the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “sons”">n</a></sup>of Manasseh, the leader Hanniel son of Ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “sons”">o</a></sup>of Ephraim, the leader Kemuel son of Shiphtan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “sons”">p</a></sup>of Zebulun, the leader Elizaphan son of Parnach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “sons”">q</a></sup>of Issachar, the leader Paltiel son of Azzan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “sons”">r</a></sup>of Asher, the leader Ahihud son of Shelomi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “sons”">s</a></sup>of Naphtali, the leader Pedahel son of Ammihud.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These are those whom Yahweh commanded to allot to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">t</a></sup>the land of Canaan.</span>
</div>
