## Laws of Inheritance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:33; Galatians 3:28; 1 Chronicles 7:15"> Then the daughters of Zelophehad, the son of Hepher, the son of Gilead, the son of Makir, the son of Manasseh, of the clan of Manasseh the son of Joseph, came near; and these were the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 15:33–34; Deuteronomy 17:8–10; Exodus 18:19–26"> They stood before Moses and before Eleazar the priest and before the leaders of the entire community at the doorway of the tent of assembly,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “meeting”">a</a></sup>saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Ezekiel 18:4; Numbers 14:35"> “Our father died in the desert; he was not in the midst of the company of those who banded together against Yahweh in the company of Korah, but he died in his own sin, and he had no sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 17:4; Psalm 109:13; Exodus 32:11"> Why should the name of our father disappear from the midst of his clan because he does not have a son? Give us property in the midst of the brothers of our father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12–13; Job 23:4; Proverbs 3:5–6"> So Moses brought their case before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Psalm 68:5–6"> And Yahweh said to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Jeremiah 49:11; Joshua 17:4"> “⸤The statements of the daughters of Zelophehad are right⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “Such as the daughters of Zelophehad are speaking”">b</a></sup>You must surely give them the property of an inheritance in the midst of their father’s brothers, and you must transfer the inheritance of their father to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And you must speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">c</a></sup>saying, ‘If a man dies and has no son, you must transfer his inheritance to his daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And if he has no daughter, you must give his inheritance to his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> If he has no brothers, then you must give his inheritance to his father’s brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 35:29; Ruth 4:3–6; Jeremiah 32:8"> If his father has no brothers, then you must give his inheritance to his nearest relative from his own clan, and he will take possession of it. It will be as a decree of stipulation for the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>just as Yahweh commanded Moses.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 33:47–48; Deuteronomy 34:1–4; Deuteronomy 3:23–27"> Yahweh said to Moses, “Go up to this mountain of Abarim, and see the land that I have given to the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Numbers 31:2; Numbers 20:24–28"> When you see it, you will be gathered to your people, just as Aaron your brother was gathered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 20:8–13; Exodus 17:7"> because you rebelled against my word in the desert of Zin when the community quarreled regarding my holiness at the waters.” (These are the waters of Meribah-Kadesh in the desert of Zin.)</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joshua Succeeds Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Moses spoke to Yahweh, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Jeremiah 3:15; Hebrews 12:9"> “Let Yahweh, the God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the community</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:5; Mark 6:34; 1 Kings 22:17"> who will go out before them and will come in before them, and who will lead them out and bring them in, so the community of Yahweh will not be like a flock that does not have a shepherd.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:9; Numbers 27:23; Genesis 41:38"> Then Yahweh said to Moses, “Take Joshua son of Nun, a man in whom is the spirit, and place your hand on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:23"> Have him stand before Eleazar the priest and before the entire community, and ⸤commission him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “command him”">f</a></sup>⸤in their sight⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “before their eyes”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 1:16–18; 1 Samuel 10:9; 2 Kings 2:9–10"> You will give to him from your authority so that the entire community of Israel will ⸤obey him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “hear him ”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 9:14; 1 Samuel 28:6; Exodus 28:30"> He will stand before Eleazar the priest, who will ask for him by the decision<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “judgment”">i</a></sup>of the Urim before Yahweh. On ⸤his command⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “his word”">j</a></sup>they will go out, and at ⸤his command⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “his word”">k</a></sup>they will come in, both he and all of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup>with him, the entire community.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Moses did just as Yahweh commanded him, and he took Joshua and set him before Eleazar the priest and before the entire community.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 27:19; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> And he placed his hands on him and ⸤commissioned him⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “commanded him”">m</a></sup>just as Yahweh spoke by the hand of Moses.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “through Moses”">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
