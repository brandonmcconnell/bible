## The Plague of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Numbers 33:49; Joshua 2:1"> When Israel dwelled in Shittim, the people began to prostitute<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “have sexual relations”">a</a></sup>themselves with the daughters of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:20; Exodus 20:5; Exodus 34:15–16"> And they invited the people to the sacrifices of their gods, and the people ate and worshiped their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 22:17; Psalm 106:28–29; Hosea 9:10"> So Israel was joined together to Baal Peor, and ⸤Yahweh became angry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the nose of Yahweh became hot”">b</a></sup>with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Deuteronomy 4:3; Deuteronomy 21:23"> Yahweh said to Moses, “Take all ⸤the leaders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the heads”">c</a></sup>of the people and hang them before Yahweh in the sun, so the fierce anger of Yahweh will turn from Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; 1 Kings 18:40; Deuteronomy 13:9"> So Moses said to the judges of Israel, “Each of you kill his men who are joined together with Baal Peor.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 2:17; Numbers 22:4; Jeremiah 36:23"> And behold, a man from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>came and brought to his brothers a Midianite woman before the eyes of Moses and before the eyes of all of the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>and they were weeping at the doorway of the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 6:25; Psalm 106:30; Joshua 22:30–31"> When Phinehas son of Eleazar son of Aaron the priest saw, he got up from the midst of the community and took a spear in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 16:46–48; Psalm 106:29–31; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> He went after the man of Israel into the woman’s section of the tent, and he drove the two of them, the man of Israel and the woman, into her belly. And the plague among the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:8; Deuteronomy 4:3–4; Numbers 25:4–5"> The ones who died in the plague were twenty-four thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Psalm 78:58; Deuteronomy 32:16"> “Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, turned away my anger from among the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>when he was jealous with my jealousy in their midst, and I did not destroy the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>with my jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Malachi 2:4–5; Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 54:10"> Therefore say, ‘Behold, I am giving to him my covenant of peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Romans 10:2–4; Jeremiah 33:22"> and it will be for him and his ⸤offspring⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “seed”">i</a></sup>after him a covenant of an eternal priesthood because he was jealous for his God and made atonement for the ⸤Israelites⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 1:23; Numbers 26:14; 2 Chronicles 19:7"> The name of the man of Israel who was struck with the Midianite woman was Zimri son of Salu, a leader of ⸤the family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “the father’s house” or “the ancestor’s house”">k</a></sup>of the Simeonites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “Simeonite”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 31:8; Joshua 13:21; Numbers 25:18"> The name of the Midianite woman who was struck was Cozbi daughter of Zur, ⸤a leader⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “a head”">m</a></sup>of a tribe of ⸤the family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “the father’s house” or “the ancestor’s house”">n</a></sup>in Midian.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 31:1–3; Revelation 18:6"> “Attack the Midianites and strike them</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 2:14; Exodus 32:35; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> because they were attacking you with their deception, with which they have deceived you on the matter of Peor and on the matter of Cozbi the daughter of the leader of Midian, their sister who was struck on the day of the plague because of the matter of Peor.”</span>
</div>
</div>
