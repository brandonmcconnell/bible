## Offers for the Seventh Month

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24–25; 1 Chronicles 15:28; Isaiah 27:13"> “ ‘On the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you will have a holy convocation; ⸤you will not do any regular work⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “you will not do work of labor”">a</a></sup>It will be a day for you of blowing trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 28:27; Numbers 28:19; Numbers 29:36"> You will offer a burnt offering as a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh: one bull, one ram, and seven male lambs ⸤in their first year⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons of a year”">b</a></sup>they will be without defect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Their grain offering will be finely milled flour mixed with oil: three-tenths for the bull, two-tenths for the ram;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and one-tenth for each of the seven male lambs;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:30; Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22"> with one male goat for a sin offering, to make atonement for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:3–8; Numbers 28:11–15; Numbers 29:18"> in addition to the burnt offering of the new moon and its grain offering, the continual burnt offering and its grain offering, and their libations, according to their stipulations, as a fragrance of appeasement by fire for Yahweh.</span>
</div>
