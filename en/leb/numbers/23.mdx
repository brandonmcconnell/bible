## Balaam’s Oracles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; Jude 1:11; Exodus 27:1–8"> Balaam said to Balak, “Build for me this: seven altars. And prepare for me this: seven bulls and seven rams.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Numbers 23:30"> And Balak did just as Balaam spoke, and Balak offered Balaam a bull and a ram on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 23:15; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 22:2"> And Balaam said to Balak, “Station yourself at your burnt offering, and I will go; perhaps Yahweh will come to meet me, and whatever he shows me I will tell to you.” So he went to a barren height.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 23:16; Ephesians 2:9; Luke 18:12"> And God met with Balaam, and he said to him, “I have arranged seven altars, and I have offered a bull and a ram on the altar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Jeremiah 1:9; Isaiah 59:21"> Yahweh put a word in the mouth of Balaam and said, “Return to Balak, and you must speak thus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So he returned to him, and behold, he was standing beside his burnt offering, he and all the leaders of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Numbers 24:3; Numbers 22:5–6"> And he lifted up his oracle and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“From Aram Balak lead me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the mountains of the east the king of Moab,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Go for me, curse Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and go, denounce Israel.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:23; Isaiah 44:25; Numbers 23:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">How can I curse whom God has not cursed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and how can I denounce whom Yahweh has not denounced?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 32:8; Exodus 33:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because from the top of the rocks I see him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from hilltops I watch him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold, a people who dwell alone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they do not consider themselves among the nations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 37:37; Genesis 13:16; Psalm 116:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who can count the dust of Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or as a number the fourth part of Israel?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let my life die the death of an upright person,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and let my end be like his!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; Numbers 24:10; Numbers 22:17"> And Balak said to Balaam, “What have you done to me? I took you to curse my enemies, and look, you have surely blessed them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:38; Numbers 23:20; Numbers 23:26"> He answered and said, “⸤Should I not speak⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “Should I not observe to speak”">a</a></sup>what Yahweh puts in my mouth?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 109:17; James 3:9–10; Numbers 22:41"> Then Balak said, “Please walk with me to another place where you will see them, but you will only see part of them and will not see all of them; and curse them for me from there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; Numbers 23:29; Isaiah 1:10–11"> So he took him to the field of Zophim to the top of Pisgah, and he built seven altars, and he offered a bull and a ram on each altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 23:3; Numbers 22:8"> Balaam<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “He”">b</a></sup>said to Balak, “Station yourself here at the burnt offering while I myself meet with Yahweh there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 23:5; Numbers 24:1; Numbers 22:35"> Then Yahweh met with Balaam, and he put a word in his mouth, and he said, “Return to Balak, and you must speak thus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 23:26; 1 Samuel 3:17; Jeremiah 37:17"> He came to him, and behold, he was standing at his burnt offering, and the princes of Moab with him. And Balak said to him, “What has Yahweh spoken?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 3:20"> Then ⸤he uttered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “he lifted up”">c</a></sup>his oracle, and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Stand up, Balak, and hear;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">listen to me, son of Zippor!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:29; James 1:17; Malachi 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is not a man, that he should lie,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor a son of humankind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that he should change his mind.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Has he said, and will he not do it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And has he spoken, and will he not fulfill it?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:27–29; Numbers 22:12; Genesis 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-2">Behold, I have received a command to bless;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when he has blessed, I cannot cause it to return.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalm 23:4; Matthew 1:23; Jeremiah 50:20"> <span className="pl pl-2">He has no regard for evil in Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he does not see trouble in Israel;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh his God is with him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a shout</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “a blast”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of a king is among them.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 24:8; Deuteronomy 33:17; Job 39:9–11"> <span className="pl pl-2">God, who brings them out from Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is like the strength</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “like the horns”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of a wild ox for them.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 10:18–19; Joshua 13:22; Galatians 1:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-2">Because there is no sorcery against Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and there is no divination against Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Now</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “At the right time”">h</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it will be said to Jacob and Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">what God has done!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 49:9; Numbers 24:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look! the people will rise like the lion;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he raises himself and will not lie down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until he eats the prey</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and drinks the blood of the slain.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 2:1–3"> Then Balak said to Balaam, “Do not curse them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">i</a></sup>at all, nor bless them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">j</a></sup>at all!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Numbers 22:18; Acts 4:19–20"> But Balaam answered and said to Balak, “Did I not speak to you, saying, ‘Whatever Yahweh speaks I will do’?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 11:29; Proverbs 21:30; Numbers 23:19–20"> Then Balak said to Balaam, “Please, come, I will take you to another place; perhaps ⸤it will be acceptable to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “it will be right in the eyes of”">k</a></sup>God, and you will curse for me from there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 21:20; Psalm 106:28"> So Balak took Balaam to the top of Peor, which looks down on the face of the Jeshimon.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “the wasteland”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 23:1–2"> And Balaam said to Balak, “Build for me these seven altars, and prepare for me these seven bulls and seven rams.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Balak did just as Balaam said, and he offered a bull and a ram on each altar.</span>
</div>
