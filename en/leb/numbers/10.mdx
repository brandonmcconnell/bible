## The Silver Trumpets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13; Joel 1:14; Psalm 81:3"> “Make yourself two silver trumpets; make them of hammered-work. ⸤You will use them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “They will be for you”">a</a></sup>for calling the community and for breaking the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Joel 2:15–16"> You will blow them, and all the community will assemble to the doorway of the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Exodus 18:21; Numbers 1:4–16"> But if they blow only one, the leaders, the heads of the thousands of Israel, will assemble to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 10:14; Isaiah 58:1"> When you will blow a blast, the camps that are camping on the east will set out;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 10:18; Numbers 2:10–16"> when you blow a second blast, the camps that are camping on the south will set out; they will blow a blast for their journeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 2:1; Numbers 10:3–4"> But when summoning the assembly, you will blow, but you will not signal with a loud noise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 6:4–16; Numbers 31:6; 1 Chronicles 15:24"> The sons of Aaron, the priests, will blow on the trumpets; this will be an eternal decree for your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:18; Judges 2:18; Genesis 8:1"> If you go to war in your land against the enemy who attacks you, you will signal with a loud noise on the trumpets. You will be remembered ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">b</a></sup>Yahweh your God, and you will be rescued from your enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 81:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 11:24–26"> “And on the day of your joy and in your appointed times, at the beginning of your months, you will blow on the trumpets in addition to your burnt offerings and in addition to the sacrifices of your fellowship offerings. And they will be as a memorial for you ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">c</a></sup>your God; I am Yahweh your God.”</span>
</div>

## The Israelites Depart from Sinai

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:17; Numbers 1:1; Numbers 9:11"> And it happened, in the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth of the month the cloud was lifted from upon the tabernacle of the testimony.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some modern translations (e.g., the NRSV) have “tabernacle of the covenant”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 21:21; Numbers 12:16; Numbers 9:5"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>set out for their journey<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew “journeys”">f</a></sup>from the desert of Sinai, and the cloud dwelled in the desert of Paran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Numbers 9:23"> They set out ⸤for the first time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the beginning”">g</a></sup>on the command of Yahweh in the hand of Moses.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “through Moses”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 1:7; Numbers 7:12"> The standard of the camp of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “sons”">i</a></sup>of Judah set out for the first time according to their divisions, with Nahshon son of Amminadab over its division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 7:18; Numbers 1:8"> And Nathanel son of Zuar was over the division of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “sons”">j</a></sup>of Issachar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 7:24; Numbers 1:9"> Eliab son of Helon was over the division of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “sons”">k</a></sup>of Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:6–8; Numbers 1:51; Hebrews 12:28"> The tabernacle was taken down, and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari, the bearers of the tabernacle, set out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 2:10–16; Numbers 1:5; Numbers 26:5–18"> And the standard of the camp of Reuben according to their divisions; Elizur son of Shedeur was over their division.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:14; Numbers 2:14; Numbers 7:42"> Eliasaph son of Deuel was over the division of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “sons”">l</a></sup>of Gad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:4–16"> The Kohathites, the bearers of the sanctuary, set out, and they set up the tabernacle before they arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 2:18–24; Numbers 1:10; Numbers 26:23–41"> And the stand of the camp of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “sons”">m</a></sup>of Ephraim set out according to their divisions; Elishama son of Ammihud was over its division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 7:54"> Gamaliel son of Pedahzur was over the division of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or “sons”">n</a></sup>of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 1:11; Numbers 7:60"> Abidan son of Gideoni was over the division of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “sons”">o</a></sup>of Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:9; Numbers 1:12; Genesis 49:16–17"> Then the standard of the camp of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “sons”">p</a></sup>of Dan, who formed a rear guard for all the camps, set out according to their divisions; Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai was over its division.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:13; Numbers 7:72"> Pagiel son of Ocran was over the division of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Or “sons”">q</a></sup>of Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:15; Numbers 7:78"> Ahira son of Enan was over the division of the tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “sons”">r</a></sup>of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 2:34; Numbers 24:4–5; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> These were the departures of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">s</a></sup>according to their divisions; and so they set out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Judges 4:11; Exodus 3:1"> Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, the father-in-law of Moses, “We are setting out to the place that Yahweh said, ‘I will give it to you’; go with us, and we will ⸤treat you well⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “do good to you”">t</a></sup>because ⸤Yahweh promised⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “Yahweh spoke”">u</a></sup>good concerning Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:15–17; Psalm 45:10; Matthew 21:29"> But he said to him, “I will not go. I will only go to my land and to my family.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 29:15; Galatians 6:2; 1 Corinthians 12:14–21"> He<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="That is, Moses">v</a></sup>said, “Please, do not abandon us because you know our encampment in the desert, and ⸤you should be our guide⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “you should be our eyes”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 10:18; 1 John 1:3"> Moreover, if you go with us, the good that Yahweh will do to us we will do to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:33; Exodus 3:1; Psalm 95:11"> And so they set out from the mountain of Yahweh a journey of three days, with the ark of the covenant of Yahweh setting out ahead of them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Or “before them”">x</a></sup>three days’ journey to search out a resting place for them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Nehemiah 9:19; Psalm 105:39"> and the cloud of Yahweh was over them by day when they set out from the camp.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalm 68:1–2; Isaiah 17:12–14; Psalm 132:8"> And whenever the ark was setting out Moses would say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rise up, Yahweh!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May your enemies be scattered;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">may the ones that hate you flee from your presence.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:10; Psalm 90:13–17; Isaiah 63:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">And when it rested he would say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Return, Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to the countless thousands of Israel.”</span></span>
