## The Israelites Complain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; Numbers 16:35; 2 Kings 1:12"> And it happened, the people were like those who ⸤complain of hardship⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “complain of bad”">a</a></sup>⸤in the hearing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in the ears”">b</a></sup>of Yahweh, and Yahweh ⸤became angry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “his nose became hot”">c</a></sup>and the fire of Yahweh burned among them, and it consumed the edge of the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 21:7; James 5:16; Numbers 16:45–48"> Then the people cried out to Moses, and Moses prayed to Yahweh, and the fire died down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:22"> And he called the name of that place Taberah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This word is difficult, but some modern translations suggest the word in Hebrew means “burning” (see NRSV, NASB)">d</a></sup>because the fire of Yahweh burned among them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Exodus 12:38; Psalm 106:14"> The riff-raff that were in their midst ⸤had a strong desire⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “desired a desire”">e</a></sup>and the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>turned back and also wept, and they said, “Who will feed us meat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:3; Psalm 17:14; Philippians 3:19"> We remember the fish that we ate in Egypt for nothing, the cucumber, melon, leek, the onions, and the garlic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 21:5; 2 Samuel 13:4"> But now ⸤our strength is dried up⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “our life is dry”">g</a></sup>there is nothing whatsoever except ⸤for the manna before us⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “for the manna of our eyes”">h</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 16:31; Genesis 2:12; Exodus 16:14–15"> Now the manna was like coriander seed, and its outward appearance was like that of bdellium-gum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 16:31; Exodus 16:16–18; Exodus 16:23"> The people went about and gathered it, and they ground it with mills or crushed it with mortar. Then they boiled it in a pot and made it into bread-cakes; and it tasted like olive oil cakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 16:13–14; Psalm 78:23–25; Deuteronomy 32:2"> When the dew came down on the camp at night, the manna came down with it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 78:21; Numbers 16:27; Numbers 14:1–2"> Moses heard the people weeping according to their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “its”">i</a></sup>clans, each at the doorway of their tents. Then ⸤Yahweh became very angry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the nose of Yahweh became very hot”">j</a></sup>and in the eyes of Moses it was bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:12; Exodus 5:22; Jeremiah 15:10"> And Moses said to Yahweh, “Why have you brought trouble to your servant? Why have I not found favor in your eyes, that the burdens of all these people have been placed on me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:23; Isaiah 40:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:7"> Did I conceive all these people? If I have fathered them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “him/it”">k</a></sup>that you could say to me, ‘Carry them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “him/it”">l</a></sup>in your lap, just as a foster-father carries the suckling on the land that you swore an oath to their ancestors?’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “fathers”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; Mark 8:4; John 6:5–9"> From where do I have meat to give all these people? They weep before me, saying, ‘Give us meat and let us eat!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 18:18; Deuteronomy 1:9–12; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> I am not able to carry all these people along alone; they are too heavy for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 1:20–24; 1 Kings 19:4; Jonah 4:3"> If this is how you are going to treat me, please kill me immediately if I find favor in your eyes, and do not let me see my misery.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 24:1; Exodus 24:9; Deuteronomy 16:18"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Gather for me seventy men from the elders of Israel whom you know are elders of the people and their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “his/its”">n</a></sup>officials; take them to the tent of assembly, and they will stand there with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; 1 Samuel 10:6; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> I will come down and speak with you there; I will take away from the spirit that is on you, and I will place it on them; and they will bear the burdens of the people with you; you will not bear it alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 19:10; Acts 7:39; Exodus 16:3–7"> And you will say to the people, ‘Sanctify yourselves tomorrow, for you will eat meat because you have wept in the ears of Yahweh, saying, “Who will feed us good meat? It was good for us in Egypt.” Yahweh will give to you meat, and you will eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> You will eat, not one day, or two days, or five days, or ten days, or twenty days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 106:15; Numbers 21:5; Psalm 78:27–30"> ⸤but for a whole month⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “until a period of one month”">o</a></sup>until it comes out from your nose and becomes as nausea to you; because you have rejected Yahweh, who is in your midst, and you wept before ⸤his presence⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “before his face”">p</a></sup>saying, “Why did we ever leave Egypt?” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:37; Numbers 1:46; Genesis 12:2"> But Moses said, “There are six hundred thousand on foot, among whom I am in the midst, and you yourself said, ‘I will give meat to them, and they will eat for a whole month.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; 2 Kings 7:2; John 6:9"> Should flocks and cattle be slaughtered for them? Should all the fish of the sea be gathered together for them, to be enough for them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:1; Isaiah 50:2; Genesis 18:14"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “⸤Is Yahweh’s power limited⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “Is Yahweh’s hand short?”">q</a></sup>Now you will see if my word will happen or not.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 11:16; Numbers 11:26"> So Moses went out, and he spoke the words of Yahweh to the people, and he gathered together seventy men from the elders of the people, and he ⸤made them stand⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “caused them to stand”">r</a></sup>all around the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Joel 2:28–29; 1 Samuel 10:10"> Then Yahweh went down in the cloud and spoke to him, and he took away the spirit that was on him, and he ⸤put it⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “gave it”">s</a></sup>on the seventy elders. And as soon as the spirit was resting on them they prophesied, but they did not do it again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:5; 1 Samuel 20:26; 1 Samuel 10:22"> But two men were left in the camp; the name of one was Eldad, and the name of the second was Medad, and the spirit rested on them; they were among those who were written down, but they did not go out to the tent, so they prophesied in the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> So a boy<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “the boy”">t</a></sup>ran and told Moses and said, “Eldad and Medad are prophesying in the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:38–40; Luke 9:49–50; John 3:26"> And Joshua son of Nun, the assistant of Moses from time of his youth, answered, “Moses, my lord, stop them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:5; Matthew 9:37–38; Luke 10:2"> But Moses said to him, “Are you jealous for my sake? Would that he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="That is, Yahweh">u</a></sup>give all Yahweh’s people prophets, that Yahweh put his spirit on them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Then Moses and the elders of Israel were gathered to the camp.</span>
</div>

## The Quail

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 16:13; Psalm 105:40; Psalm 78:26–29"> Then a wind set out from Yahweh, and it drove quails from the west, and he spread them out on the camp about a day’s journey on one side and about a day’s journey on the other, all around the camp, about two cubits on the surface of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:11; Exodus 16:36"> And so the people ⸤worked⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “arose”">v</a></sup>all day and all night and all the next day, and they gathered the quail (the least of the ones collecting gathered ten homers).<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="HALOT 330, “a dry measure”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 78:30–31; Psalm 106:14–15; Numbers 25:9"> While the meat was still between their teeth, before it was consumed, Yahweh was angry with the people, and Yahweh struck a very great plague among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:22; 1 Corinthians 10:6; Numbers 33:16"> And he called the name of that place Kibroth Hattaavah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Hebrew “the graves of greediness”">x</a></sup>because they buried the people that ⸤were greedy⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “craved”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 33:17; Numbers 12:16; Deuteronomy 1:1"> From Kibroth Hattaavah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “the graves of greediness”">z</a></sup>the people set out to Hazeroth; and they stayed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “they were”">aa</a></sup>in Hazeroth.</span>
