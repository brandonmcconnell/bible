<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

And they saw the land of Jazer and the land of Gilead, and behold it was a place for livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “sons”">c</a></sup>of Gad and the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “sons”">d</a></sup>of Reuben came, and they said to Moses and to Eleazar the priest and to the leaders of the community, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:34; Isaiah 16:8–9; Numbers 32:34–38"> “Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:34; Deuteronomy 2:24–35; Numbers 21:24"> the land that Yahweh struck before the community of Israel, is a land of livestock, and your servants have livestock.”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; 2 Samuel 11:11; Philippians 2:4"> But Moses said to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “sons”">f</a></sup>of Gad and to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “sons”">g</a></sup>of Reuben, “Will your brothers go to war while you yourselves live here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27–4; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:28"> Why are you discouraging the hearts of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>from crossing into the land that Yahweh gave to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:19–25; Numbers 14:2; Numbers 13:2–26"> This is what your fathers did when I sent them from Kadesh Barnea to see the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 13:23–10; Deuteronomy 1:24–28"> When they went up to the valley<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “the wadi”">i</a></sup>of Eshcol and saw the land, they discouraged the heart of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>so that they did not come to the land that Yahweh gave to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Psalm 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> So ⸤Yahweh’s anger burned⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “Yahweh’s nose became hot”">k</a></sup>on that day, and he swore an oath, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 14:28–30; Numbers 14:24; Numbers 26:2"> ‘The men who went up from Egypt, from ⸤those twenty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">l</a></sup>and above, will not see the land that I swore with an oath to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob because they have not wholly followed me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 14:24"> except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite and Joshua son of Nun, because they followed Yahweh wholly.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:33–35; Hebrews 3:16–19"> And ⸤Yahweh became angry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “Yahweh’s nose became hot”">m</a></sup>and he made them wander in the desert forty years until the entire generation who did evil in the ⸤sight of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “eyes of Yahweh”">n</a></sup>⸤had died⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “had completed”">o</a></sup></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 7:19–22"> If you turn from following him, he will again abandon them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">q</a></sup>in the wilderness, and you would have destroyed all these people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Numbers 34:22"> They came near to him and said, “We will build sheep pens here for the flock of our livestock and cities for our little children;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Numbers 32:29–32; Deuteronomy 3:18–20"> but we ourselves will become armed and ready before the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">r</a></sup>until we have brought them to their place, and our little children will live in the fortified cities because of the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 22:1–5"> We will not return to our houses until the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">s</a></sup>each obtain their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Hebrew “his”">t</a></sup>inheritance for themselves.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 1:13–15"> So Moses said to them, “If you do this thing, if you arm yourselves ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">u</a></sup>Yahweh for the war,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and everyone of you armed cross the Jordan ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">v</a></sup>Yahweh until he has driven out his enemies from before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 22:9; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 3:20"> and the land is subdued ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">w</a></sup>Yahweh, then afterward you will return and be free of obligation from Yahweh and from Israel, and this land will be your property ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">x</a></sup>Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:12; Proverbs 13:21; Genesis 44:16"> But if you do not do so, behold, you have sinned against Yahweh, and know that your sin will find you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:16; Numbers 30:2; Numbers 32:34–42"> Build for yourselves cities for your little children and sheep pens for your flocks; what has gone out from your mouth you will do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 1:13–14"> So the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “sons”">y</a></sup>of Gad and the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Or “sons”">z</a></sup>of Reuben said to Moses, saying, “Your servants will do just as my lord commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 1:14"> Our little children, our wives, our livestock, and all of our animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">aa</a></sup>⸤will remain⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “will be there”">ab</a></sup>in the cities of Gilead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:12; Numbers 32:17; Numbers 12:11"> but your servants, everyone who is armed for battle, will cross over ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">ac</a></sup>Yahweh to the war, just as my lord says.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Moses said to them, “If the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “sons”">af</a></sup>of Gad and the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Or “sons”">ag</a></sup>of Reuben, everyone who is armed for the war, cross over the Jordan ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">ah</a></sup>Yahweh, and the land is subdued before you, you will give them the land of Gilead as property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:19"> But if they will not cross over with you armed, they will acquire land in your midst in Canaan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Or “sons”">ai</a></sup>of Gad and the descendants of Reuben answered and said, “What Yahweh has commanded your servants, we will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We ourselves will cross over armed ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">aj</a></sup>Yahweh to the land of Canaan, and the property of our inheritance will remain with us beyond the Jordan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 29:8; Deuteronomy 3:12–17"> So Moses gave to them, to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Or “sons”">ak</a></sup>of Gad and the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Or “sons”">al</a></sup>of Reuben, and to half of the tribe of Joseph’s son Manasseh, the kingdom of Sihon the king of Amorites and the kingdom of Og the king of the Bashan, the land with its cities and their territories, the cities of the surrounding land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 17:2"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Or “sons”">am</a></sup>of Gad rebuilt Dibon, Ataroth, and Aroer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:3"> and Atroth Shophan, Jazer, and Jogbehah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 32:3"> and Beth Nimrah and Beth Haran, the cities of Mibzar, and the sheep pens for flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:27; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 15:4"> The descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Or “sons”">an</a></sup>of Reuben rebuilt Heshbon, Elealeh, and Kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Numbers 32:3; Joshua 23:7"> and Nebo, Baal Meon (their names<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Hebrew “name”">ao</a></sup>were changed), and Sibmah, and ⸤they renamed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “they called to names the names”">ap</a></sup>the cities that they rebuilt.</span>

<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Joshua 13:29–31; Deuteronomy 3:13–15"> So Moses gave Gilead to Makir son of Manasseh, and he lived in it.</span>
