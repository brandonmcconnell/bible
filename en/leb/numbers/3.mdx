## Aaron’s Sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Genesis 10:1; Exodus 6:16"> These are the genealogies of Aaron and Moses ⸤at the time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “on a day”">a</a></sup>when Yahweh spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:23; Numbers 26:60; Exodus 28:1"> These are the names of the descendants of Aaron: Nadab the firstborn, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; Leviticus 8:30; Leviticus 8:12"> These are the names of the descendants of Aaron, the priests, the anointed ones whom ⸤he consecrated as priests⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “he filled their hands to serve as a priest”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2; Numbers 26:61"> Nadab and Abihu died ⸤before Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “before the face of Yahweh”">c</a></sup>when they presented a strange fire ⸤before Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “before the face of Yahweh”">d</a></sup>in the desert of Sinai, and ⸤they had no children⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons were not for them”">e</a></sup>Eleazar and Ithamar served as priest during the presence of Aaron their father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 2:33; Numbers 1:49–53"> “Bring near the tribe of Levi, and ⸤set the tribe⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “cause it to stand”">f</a></sup>⸤before Aaron⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of Aaron”">g</a></sup>the priest, and they will minister to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:50; Numbers 8:15; Numbers 8:11"> They shall observe his duties and the duties of the entire community before the tent of assembly, to do the work of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; Numbers 10:17; Isaiah 52:11"> And they will keep all the vessels of the tent of assembly and the responsibilities of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>to do the work<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or “service”">i</a></sup>of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; Numbers 18:6–7; Ephesians 4:8"> You will give the Levites to Aaron and to his descendants; they are surely assigned to him from among the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:38; Romans 12:7"> But you will count Aaron and his descendants; they will keep their priesthood, and the stranger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “outsider”">k</a></sup>who approaches will be put to death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Yahweh spoke to Moses saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:41; Numbers 8:16; Numbers 18:6"> “I myself receive the Levites from the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup>in the place of all the firstborn of the offspring of the womb from the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>The Levites will be mine</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12; Exodus 13:2"> because all the firstborn are mine; on the day of my killing all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated for myself all the firstborn in Israel, ⸤both humankind and animal⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “from humankind to animal”">n</a></sup>they will be mine. I am Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Yahweh spoke to Moses in the desert of Sinai, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Mark 10:14; 2 Timothy 3:15"> “Muster<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “count,” or “summon,” or “enroll”">o</a></sup>the descendants of Levi according to ⸤their families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">p</a></sup>according to their clans. You will count every male from ⸤one month⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the son of a month”">q</a></sup>and above.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Numbers 4:37; Numbers 4:49"> So Moses mustered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or “counted,” or “summoned,” or “enrolled”">r</a></sup>them according to the ⸤command of Yahweh⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “mouth of Yahweh”">s</a></sup>just as he commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; Exodus 6:16–19; Numbers 15:5–23"> These were the sons of Levi according to their names: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 26:1–32; Exodus 6:17–19"> And these are the names of the sons of Gershon according to their clans: Libni and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 15:17–21"> And the sons of Kohath according to their clans: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:19; Numbers 3:33"> The sons of Merari according to their tribes: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of the Levites according to ⸤their families⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> ⸤To Gershon belonged⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “To Gershon was”">u</a></sup>the clan of the Libnites and the clan of the Shimeites; these are the clans of the Gershonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 4:38–40"> The ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">v</a></sup>according to the number of every male from ⸤one month⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “the son of a month and above”">w</a></sup>and above were seven thousand five hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:17"> The clans of the Gershonites will camp behind the tabernacle to the west,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and the leader of ⸤the family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “the house of his father”">x</a></sup>of the Gershonites is Eliasaph son of Lael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 25:9; 1 Chronicles 23:32; 1 Chronicles 9:14–33"> And the responsibility of the descendants of Gershon in the tent of assembly is the tabernacle, and the tent covering it and the curtain of the doorway of the tent of the assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 38:9–16; Exodus 27:9–16"> and the hangings of the courtyard and the curtain of the doorway of the courtyard that is around the tabernacle and the altar, and its tent cords, all of its use.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:23; Numbers 3:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> ⸤To Kohath belonged⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “For Kohath was ”">y</a></sup>the clan of Amramites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “Amramite”">z</a></sup>the clan of the Izharites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “Izharite”">aa</a></sup>the clan of the Hebronites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “Hebronite”">ab</a></sup>and the clan of the Uzzielites;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Hebrew “Uzzielite”">ac</a></sup>these were the clans of the Kohathites.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Hebrew “Kohathite”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 4:35–36; Numbers 3:7"> According to the number of every male from ⸤one month⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “the son of a month”">ae</a></sup>and above there were eight thousand six hundred keeping the responsibility of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:23; Numbers 2:10"> The clan of the descendants of Kohath will encamp on the side of the tabernacle to the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The leader of ⸤his family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “the house of his father”">af</a></sup>according to the clans of the Kohathites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Hebrew “Kohathite”">ag</a></sup>is Elizaphan the son of Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 36:35–36; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 26:31–33"> Their responsibility was the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, and the vessels of the sanctuary, with which they ministered, and the curtain, and all of its use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 4:27; Numbers 4:16; Numbers 20:25–28"> The ⸤chief of the leaders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “the leader of leaders”">ah</a></sup>of the Levites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ai" id="fnref-ai" data-fn="Hebrew “Levite”">ai</a></sup>was Eleazar son of Aaron the priest who had oversight of those keeping the responsibility of the sanctuary.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 6:19; 1 Chronicles 23:21"> ⸤To Merari belonged⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aj" id="fnref-aj" data-fn="Literally “for Merari was ”">aj</a></sup>the clan of Mahlites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ak" id="fnref-ak" data-fn="Hebrew “Mahlite”">ak</a></sup>and the clan of the Mushites:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-al" id="fnref-al" data-fn="Hebrew “Mushite”">al</a></sup>these are the clans of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:11; Numbers 1:21"> The ⸤ones counted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-am" id="fnref-am" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">am</a></sup>according to the number of every male from ⸤one month⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-an" id="fnref-an" data-fn="Literally “the son of a month”">an</a></sup>and above were six thousand two hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 3:28–29"> The leader of ⸤the family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “the house of his father”">ao</a></sup>according to the clans of Merari is Zuriel son of Abihail; they will encamp of the side of the tabernacle to the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 26:32; Exodus 26:37; Exodus 26:15–29"> The responsibility of the sons of Merari was the supervision of the frames of the tabernacle, its bars, pillars, bases, and all its vessels and all its service,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and the pillars around the courtyard, and their bases, pegs, and cords.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:3"> Those encamped before the tabernacle to the east—before the tent of assembly to the east—were Moses and Aaron and his sons; they will keep the responsibility of the sanctuary ⸤for the Israelites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Literally “for the responsibility of the children of Israel”">ap</a></sup>and the stranger<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Or “outsider”">aq</a></sup>who approaches will be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Matthew 7:14; Numbers 4:47–48"> All those counted from the Levites whom Moses and Aaron mustered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ar" id="fnref-ar" data-fn="Or “counted,” or “summoned,” or “enrolled”">ar</a></sup>according to ⸤the word of Yahweh⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of Yahweh”">as</a></sup>according to their clans, every male from ⸤one month⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “the son of a month”">at</a></sup>and above were twenty-two thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:15; Luke 10:20; Psalm 87:6"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Muster every firstborn male from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-au" id="fnref-au" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">au</a></sup>from ⸤one month⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-av" id="fnref-av" data-fn="Literally “the son of a month”">av</a></sup>and above and ⸤count⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aw" id="fnref-aw" data-fn="Literally “and lift up the number of”">aw</a></sup>their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 8:16"> And you will receive the Levites for me—I am Yahweh—in the place of all the firstborn among the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ax" id="fnref-ax" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ax</a></sup>and the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ay" id="fnref-ay" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">ay</a></sup>of the Levites in the place of all the firstborn among the animals<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-az" id="fnref-az" data-fn="Hebrew “animal”">az</a></sup>among the ⸤Israelites⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ba" id="fnref-ba" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ba</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> So Moses mustered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bb" id="fnref-bb" data-fn="Or “counted” or “summoned” or “enrolled”">bb</a></sup>all the firstborn among the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bc" id="fnref-bc" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">bc</a></sup>just as Yahweh commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 3:39"> And all the firstborn males<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bd" id="fnref-bd" data-fn="Hebrew “male”">bd</a></sup>among the number of names from ⸤one month⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-be" id="fnref-be" data-fn="Literally “the son of a month”">be</a></sup>and above, the ⸤ones counted⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bf" id="fnref-bf" data-fn="Literally “the ones counted of them,” or “the ones mustered of them”">bf</a></sup>were twenty-two thousand two hundred and seventy-three.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:40–41"> “Receive the Levites in the place of all the firstborn among the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bg" id="fnref-bg" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">bg</a></sup>and the animals of the Levites in the place of their animals; the Levites will be mine. I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16"> And the ransom of the two hundred and seventy-three of the firstborn of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bh" id="fnref-bh" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">bh</a></sup>who are excessive over the Levites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:6; Numbers 18:16"> you will receive five shekels a person, in the sanctuary shekel; you will collect twenty gerahs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bi" id="fnref-bi" data-fn="Hebrew “gerah”">bi</a></sup>per shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> You will give the money to Aaron, and to his sons the ransom of the ones who are excessive among them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And Moses received the money of the redemption from the ones who were excessive from those redeemed of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 3:46–47; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> From the firstborn of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bj" id="fnref-bj" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">bj</a></sup>he took the money, one thousand three hundred and sixty-five shekels, in the sanctuary shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:48; Numbers 16:15; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> And Moses gave the money of the ransom to Aaron and to his sons according to the ⸤word⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-bk" id="fnref-bk" data-fn="Literally “mouth”">bk</a></sup>of Yahweh just as Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
