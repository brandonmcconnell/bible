## The Census of the Kohathites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; Numbers 3:19"> “⸤Take a census⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and lift up the number of”">a</a></sup>of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “sons”">b</a></sup>of Kohath from the midst of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “sons”">c</a></sup>of Levi, according to their clans and ⸤their families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 4:23; 1 Chronicles 23:3–5; 2 Kings 11:4–12"> from ⸤thirty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a son of thirty years”">e</a></sup>and above, up to ⸤fifty years old⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “a son of fifty years”">f</a></sup>everyone who comes to the service to do the work in the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 4:19; Mark 13:34; Numbers 4:30"> This is the work of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “sons”">g</a></sup>of Kohath in the tent of assembly, concerning the holiness of the sanctuary:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3; Exodus 40:3"> When setting out the camp, Aaron and his sons will go and lower the curtain of the covering and cover with it the ark of the testimony.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “the ark of the covenant”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 25:13–15; Exodus 35:19; Numbers 4:7–8"> They will put on it a covering of ⸤fine leather⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the hide of a sea cow”">i</a></sup>and they will spread a cloth of perfect blue over it, and they will place its poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–8; Exodus 37:10–16; Exodus 25:23–30"> And over the table of the presence they will spread out a blue cloth and put on it the plates, dishes, and libation bowls, and the pitchers of the libation; and the bread of continuity will be on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:6–7; Numbers 4:11–13; Numbers 4:9"> They will spread over it a scarlet cloth, and they will cover it with a covering of ⸤fine leather⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the hide of a sea cow”">j</a></sup>and they will place its poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Exodus 37:17–24; Revelation 1:20–1"> They will take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand for the light source, its lamps, a pair of its tongs, its small pans, and all the vessels of its oil with which they attend to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:12; Numbers 4:6"> They will put it and all its vessels inside a covering of ⸤fine leather⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the hide of a sea cow”">k</a></sup>and put it on the carrying frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:5; Exodus 30:1–19; Exodus 40:26–27"> Over the altar of gold they will spread a blue cloth, and they will cover it with a covering of ⸤fine leather⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “the hide of a sea cow”">l</a></sup>and place its poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:29; Exodus 25:9; 2 Chronicles 4:22"> They will take all the vessels of the cultic service with which they serve in the sanctuary and put them on a blue cloth, and they will cover them with a covering of ⸤fine leather⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “the hide of a sea cow”">m</a></sup>and they will put them on the carrying frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 6:12–13; Exodus 39:1; Exodus 39:41"> They will remove the fat-soaked ashes from the altar and spread a purple cloth over it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:19; Exodus 38:1–7"> they will put on it all the vessels with which they serve, the fire pans, forks, shovels, and bowls—all the vessels of the altar. They will spread on it a covering of ⸤fine leather⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “the hide of a sea cow”">n</a></sup>and they will place its poles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6–7; Numbers 7:9; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And when Aaron and his sons have finished covering the sanctuary and all the vessels of the sanctuary when the camp sets out, the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Or “sons”">o</a></sup>of Kohath will come after to carry these, but they must not touch the sanctuary, or they will die. These are the load of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “sons”">p</a></sup>of Kohath in the tent of assembly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:6; Leviticus 24:2; Exodus 30:23–38"> “Eleazar son of Aaron the priest is to supervise the oil of the light source, the incense, ⸤the regular grain offering⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “the grain offering of continuity”">q</a></sup>the oil of anointment, the supervision of all the tabernacle and all that is in it, in the sanctuary and in its vessels.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:6–7; Numbers 17:10; Numbers 18:5"> “You must not cut off the tribe of the clan of the Kohathites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Hebrew “Kohathite”">r</a></sup>from the midst of the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 4:15"> Do this to them and they will live and not die when they come near the most holy things. Aaron and his sons will go and appoint them, each one to his task and burden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Exodus 19:21; Numbers 4:15"> But they must not go and look ⸤for a moment⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “as devouring”">s</a></sup>at the holy objects.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 3:18; Numbers 3:21; Numbers 3:24"> “⸤Take a census⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “And lift up the number of”">t</a></sup>of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Or “sons”">u</a></sup>of Gershon also, according to ⸤their families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">v</a></sup>and clans,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 4:3; 1 Timothy 1:18; 1 Corinthians 9:7"> from ⸤those thirty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “a son of twenty years”">w</a></sup>and above until ⸤fifty years old⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “a son of fifty years”">x</a></sup>you will muster<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “count,” or “summon,” or “enroll”">y</a></sup>them, all who come to help to do the work of the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 4:19; Numbers 4:31–32; Numbers 4:47"> This is the work of the clans of the Gershonites:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Hebrew “Gershonite”">z</a></sup>to serve and to carry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 3:25–26; Exodus 26:14; Numbers 7:5–7"> They will carry the curtains of the tabernacle and the tent of assembly and its covering and the covering of ⸤fine leather⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “the hide of a sea cow”">aa</a></sup>which ⸤is on top of it⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “is above upon it”">ab</a></sup>and the curtain of the doorway of the tent of assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 27:9"> and the curtains of the courtyard, and the covering for the doorway of the gate of the courtyard, which is all around on the tabernacle and altar, and their cords and all the vessels of their work; and all that is done to them they will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 1:70; 1 Corinthians 11:2"> And all the work of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Or “sons”">ac</a></sup>of the Gershonites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Hebrew “Gershonite”">ad</a></sup>will be at ⸤the command⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Literally “the mouth”">ae</a></sup>of Aaron and his sons, for all they are to carry and for all their work, and you will appoint to them responsibility for all they are to carry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; 1 Corinthians 12:5–6"> This is the work of the clan of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Or “sons”">af</a></sup>of the Gershonites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ag" id="fnref-ag" data-fn="Hebrew “Gershonite”">ag</a></sup>in the tent of assembly, and their responsibility lies ⸤under the direction⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ah" id="fnref-ah" data-fn="Literally “in the hand”">ah</a></sup>of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.</span>

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 3:36–37; Exodus 26:15; Numbers 7:8–9"> And this is the responsibility of ⸤those who are to carry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ao" id="fnref-ao" data-fn="Literally “them carrying”">ao</a></sup>all their work in the tent of assembly: the frames of the tabernacle and its bars, pillars, and bases,</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 4:28; Joshua 3:6; Isaiah 3:6"> This is the work of the clan of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ap" id="fnref-ap" data-fn="Or “sons”">ap</a></sup>of Merari, for all their work in the tent of assembly ⸤under the direction⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aq" id="fnref-aq" data-fn="Literally “in the hand”">aq</a></sup>of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:23; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> from ⸤those thirty years old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-as" id="fnref-as" data-fn="Literally “a son of thirty years”">as</a></sup>and above until ⸤fifty years old⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-at" id="fnref-at" data-fn="Literally “a son of fifty years”">at</a></sup>everyone who comes to the service to work in the tent of assembly,</span>
