## Aaron’s Staff Is Chosen

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Ezekiel 19:14; Genesis 49:10"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>and take from among them twelve staffs, ⸤a staff from each family⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “a staff from a house of a father”">b</a></sup>from among all their leaders according to their families’ households. Write the name of each man on his staff,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Numbers 18:1; Numbers 3:2–3"> and the name of Aaron on the staff of Levi, because one staff is for the head of each of ⸤their families⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the house of their fathers”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 30:36; Exodus 29:42–43; Exodus 25:16–22"> You must then put them in the tent of assembly ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">d</a></sup>the testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “the statute”">e</a></sup>where I meet with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Numbers 17:8; Numbers 16:11"> And it will happen, the man whom I will choose, his staff will blossom, and so I will rid from upon myself the grumblings of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>who are grumbling against you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Moses spoke to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">g</a></sup>and all their leaders gave him a staff for each leader, one from ⸤each of their families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “each from the house of their fathers”">h</a></sup>twelve staffs, and the staff of Aaron was in the midst of their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Exodus 38:21; Numbers 18:2"> And Moses put the staffs before Yahweh in the tent of testimony.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; John 15:1–6; Psalm 132:17–18"> Then the next day, Moses went into the tent of the testimony, and behold the staff of Aaron for the house of Levi blossomed and put forth a flower and produced blossoms, and it produced almonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then Moses brought out to all the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>all the staffs before the presence of Yahweh, and they saw, and each man took his staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Ephesians 5:6; Isaiah 1:2"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Bring back the staff of Aaron before the testimony<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “the statute”">j</a></sup>as a guard and sign for the children of rebellion, and let them finish their grumblings before me and not die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So Moses did; just as Yahweh commanded him, so he did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 26:11; Isaiah 57:16; Isaiah 6:5"> And the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>said to Moses, saying, “Look! We will die! We will be destroyed! All of us will perish!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; 2 Samuel 6:6–12; Genesis 3:3"> Anyone who approaches the tabernacle of Yahweh will die. ⸤Will we all die⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “Will we all die to perish?”">l</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
