## Inheritance Through Marriage

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; 1 Chronicles 7:14–16; Joshua 17:2–3"> ⸤The leaders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “The heads”">a</a></sup>of the families<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “fathers”">b</a></sup>of the clans of descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “sons”">c</a></sup>of Gilead the son of Makir, the son of Manasseh, of the clans of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “sons”">d</a></sup>of Joseph came near and spoke ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “before the face of”">e</a></sup>Moses and ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “before the face of”">f</a></sup>⸤the leaders⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the heads”">g</a></sup>of the families<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “of the fathers”">h</a></sup>of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 17:3–6; Numbers 27:1–7; Numbers 33:54"> And they said, “Yahweh commanded my lord to give the land by lot as an inheritance to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>and my lord was commanded by Yahweh to give the inheritance of Zelophehad our brother to his daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But if they become wives to one of the sons from another tribe of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>their inheritance will disappear from the inheritance of our ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “fathers”">l</a></sup>and it will be added to the inheritance of the tribe to which they belong; the lot of our inheritance would disappear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10–18; Isaiah 61:2; Leviticus 25:23"> When the Jubilee of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>⸤comes⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “will be”">n</a></sup>it will be added to the inheritance of the tribe to which they belong; and their inheritance will disappear from the tribe of our father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Deuteronomy 5:28"> Then Moses commanded the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>by the command of Yahweh, saying, “The tribe of the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Or “sons”">p</a></sup>of Joseph is right regarding what they are speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 36:12; 2 Corinthians 6:14; Genesis 24:3"> This is the word that Yahweh commanded the daughters of Zelophehad, saying, ‘⸤Let them marry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “Let them be as wives”">q</a></sup>⸤whomever they like⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “they will be as the good in their eyes”">r</a></sup>only ⸤they must marry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “they must be as wives”">s</a></sup>from within the clan of the tribe of their father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:3; Numbers 36:9"> Thus an inheritance of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">t</a></sup>will not go around from tribe to tribe. Rather, the inheritance of each tribe of his father will remain with the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">u</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:22"> Every daughter who possesses an inheritance from the tribes of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup>will ⸤marry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “be a wife to”">w</a></sup>one of the clan of the tribe of her father, so that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">x</a></sup>will possess the inheritance of his ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Or “fathers”">y</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore an inheritance will not go around from one tribe to another tribe because the tribes of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">z</a></sup>will each hold to their own inheritance.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 39:42–43"> Just as Yahweh commanded to Moses, so the daughters of Zelophehad did:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 26:33"> Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, ⸤married⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “were as wives to”">aa</a></sup>the sons of their uncles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> ⸤They married⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “They were as wives to”">ab</a></sup>those from the sons of Manasseh son of Joseph, and their inheritance ⸤remained⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “was”">ac</a></sup>among the tribe of the clan of their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Or “fathers”">ad</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:34; Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 22:1"> These were the commands and the stipulations that Yahweh commanded by the hand of Moses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ae" id="fnref-ae" data-fn="Or “through Moses”">ae</a></sup>to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-af" id="fnref-af" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">af</a></sup>on the desert-plateaus of Moab by the Jordan across Jericho.</span>
</div>
