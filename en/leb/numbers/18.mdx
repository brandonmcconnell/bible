## The Duties of the Priests and Levities

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 28:38; Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 2:24"> Yahweh said to Aaron, “You, your sons, and your family with you will bear the guilt of the sanctuary, and you and your sons with you will bear the guilt of your priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:34; Numbers 18:4; Ezekiel 44:15"> Moreover, bring your brothers with you, the tribe of Levi the tribe of your father, that they may be joined to you and minister to you, you and your sons with you before the tent of testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 4:19–20"> They will keep your responsibility and the responsibility of all the tent, only they may not come near the vessels of the sanctuary and the altar, so both you and they will not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:10"> They will be joined to you, and they will keep the responsibility of the tent of assembly for the entire service of the tent; a stranger may not come near you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Leviticus 24:3; Numbers 16:46"> You will keep the responsibility of the sanctuary and the responsibility of the altar, and there will no longer be wrath on the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 3:9; Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45"> Look, I myself have chosen your brothers the Levites from the midst of the children. They are a gift to you given from Yahweh to perform the work of the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 15:15–16; John 3:27; Numbers 3:10"> But you with your sons will keep your priesthood to perform your priestly duties for everything at the altar<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “for all the things of the altar”">b</a></sup>and for ⸤the area behind the curtain⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “the house of the curtain”">c</a></sup>I give you the priesthood as a gift, but the stranger who approaches will be put to death.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Portions for the Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 6:16; Leviticus 7:6; Exodus 29:29"> Yahweh spoke to Aaron, “Behold, I myself have given to you the responsibility of my contributions for all the holy objects of the ⸤Israelites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup>I have given them as a portion to you and your sons as an eternal decree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 7:7; Leviticus 14:13; Leviticus 6:25–26"> This will be for you from the sanctuary of the holy things from the fire; all of their offerings, from every grain offering, from every sin offering, and from every guilt offering which they will bring to me is ⸤a most holy thing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “a holy object of holiness”">e</a></sup>for you and your sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 7:6; Leviticus 6:29; Leviticus 6:16"> You will eat it in the most holy place;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Alternatively “as a holy object of holiness”">f</a></sup>every male will eat it. It will be a holy object to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:3; Exodus 29:27–28; Leviticus 10:14"> This is also for you: the contribution of their gift of the wave offerings of the children Israel. I have given them to you and your sons and your daughters with you as an eternal decree; whoever is clean in your house may eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:4; Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26"> All the finest olive oil and all the finest new wine and their best grain that they have given to Yahweh, I have given them to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:29; Exodus 23:19; Micah 7:1"> The firstfruits of all that is in their land that they present to Yahweh will be for you; whoever is clean in your house may eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Ezekiel 44:29"> All consecrated possessions<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “possession”">g</a></sup>in Israel will be for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:20; Exodus 13:2; Exodus 13:12–13"> All the first offspring of a womb of any creature that they offer to Yahweh, whether human or animal, will be yours; you will surely redeem the firstborn of the human and the unclean firstborn of the animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:2–7"> As to their price of redemption, from ⸤a one-month-old⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “a son of a month”">h</a></sup>you will redeem them according to your proper value, five shekels of silver according to the shekel of the sanctuary, which is twenty gerah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 29:16; Deuteronomy 15:19–22; Leviticus 3:2–5"> Only the firstborn of an ox or the firstborn of a sheep or the firstborn of a goat you will not redeem; they are holy. Their blood you will sprinkle over the altar, and their fat you will turn into smoke as an offering made by fire, a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 7:31–34; Exodus 29:26–28"> But their flesh will be for you like the breast section of the wave offering, and it will be for you like the right upper thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:5; Leviticus 2:13; Numbers 18:11"> All the contributions of holiness that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>offer to Yahweh I have given to you and your sons and your daughters with you as an eternal decree; it is an eternal covenant of salt ⸤before⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of”">j</a></sup>Yahweh to you and your offspring with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:28; Deuteronomy 10:9; Joshua 13:33"> Then Yahweh said to Aaron, “You will not receive an inheritance in their land, and there will not be a plot of ground for you in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Deuteronomy 14:22–29; Nehemiah 10:37"> “Behold, I have given to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “sons”">l</a></sup>of Levi every tithe in Israel as an inheritance in return for their service, which they are doing, the work of the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 22:9; Numbers 1:51–52; Numbers 3:38"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">m</a></sup>will not come near again to the tent of assembly, or ⸤they will bear sin⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="The NRSV translates “they will incur guilt”">n</a></sup>and die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:7; Numbers 18:20; Numbers 18:1"> The Levites<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Hebrew “Levite”">o</a></sup>will perform the service of the tent of assembly, and they will bear their guilt, an eternal decree for all your generations. But they will not receive an inheritance in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">p</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Malachi 3:8–10"> because the tithes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Hebrew “tithe”">q</a></sup>of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">r</a></sup>that are ⸤offered⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Hebrew “raised up”">s</a></sup>to Yahweh as a contribution, I have given to the Levites as an inheritance; therefore I said to them, ‘They will not receive an inheritance in the midst of the ⸤Israelites⸥.’ ”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">t</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:38; Numbers 18:21"> “You will speak to the Levites and say to them, ‘When you receive the tithe from the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">u</a></sup>that I have given to you from them for your inheritance, ⸤you will present⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “you will raise up”">v</a></sup>a contribution from it to Yahweh, a tithe from a tithe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 18:30; Deuteronomy 15:14; 2 Kings 6:27"> Your contribution will be credited to you like the grain from the threshing floor and like the produce from the press.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20–10; Genesis 14:18"> So ⸤you will present⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “you will raise up”">w</a></sup>your own contribution to Yahweh from all your tithes that you receive from the ⸤Israelites⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">x</a></sup>from it you will give the contribution of Yahweh to Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> From all your gifts ⸤you will present⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “you will raise up”">y</a></sup>every contribution of Yahweh, from all its fat, the part that is sacred.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9–10; Genesis 43:11; Matthew 6:33"> You will say to them, ‘When ⸤you are presenting⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “you are raising up”">z</a></sup>its fat, the rest will be credited to the Levites like a yield of the threshing floor and like a yield from the press.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:10–14; 1 Timothy 5:17–18; Luke 10:7"> You may eat it anywhere, you and your household, because it is a wage in return for your service in the tent of assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 19:8; Leviticus 22:2; Malachi 1:7"> You will not bear any sin because ⸤you have presented⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Literally “you have raised up”">aa</a></sup>its fat; you will not defile the holy objects of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">ab</a></sup>or you will die.’ ”</span>
</div>
