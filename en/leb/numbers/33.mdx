<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 9:17–23; Deuteronomy 10:11; Numbers 10:6"> Moses wrote down their movements according to their journeys on the command of Yahweh, and these are their journeys according to their movements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 14:8; Exodus 12:37; Exodus 13:4"> They set out from Rameses on the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the next day after the Passover the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">b</a></sup>went out ⸤boldly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “with a hand that was raised”">c</a></sup>⸤in the sight⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “for the eyes”">d</a></sup>of all the Egyptians</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Isaiah 19:1; Exodus 18:11"> while the Egyptians were burying all the firstborn among them whom Yahweh struck. Yahweh also executed punishments among their gods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 12:37"> Then the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup>set out from Rameses, and they camped in Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 13:20"> They journeyed from Succoth and camped in Etham, which is on the edge of the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 14:2; Exodus 14:9; Numbers 33:8"> Then they set out from Etham and returned to Pi-Hahiroth, which faces Baal Zephon, and they camped before Migdol.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 15:22–26; Exodus 14:21–31"> They set out from Pi-Hahiroth and went through the midst of the sea into the desert; and they went a journey of three days into the desert of Etham and camped at Marah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 15:27"> They set out from Marah and came to Elim, and in Elim there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees, and they camped there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 16:1; Exodus 17:1"> They set out from Elim, and they camped at the ⸤Red Sea⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sea of reed”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 16:1"> They set out from the ⸤Red Sea⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sea of reed”">g</a></sup>and camped at the desert of Sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> They set out from the desert of Sin and camped at Dophkah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They set out from Dophkah and camped at Alush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 17:1–8; Exodus 19:2"> They set out from Alush and encamped at Rephidim; and it was there that the people had no water to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:1–2; Exodus 16:1"> They set out from Rephidim and camped in the desert of Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 11:34; Numbers 10:11–13; Numbers 10:33"> They set out from the desert of Sinai and camped at Kibroth Hattaavah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 11:35"> They set out from Kibroth Hattaavah and camped at Hazeroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 12:16"> They set out from Hazeroth and camped at Rithmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They set out from Rithmah and camped at Rimmon Perez.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> They set out from Rimmon Perez and camped at Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:1"> They set out from Libnah and camped at Rissah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> They set out from Rissah and camped at Kehelathah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> They set out from Kehelathah and camped at Mount Shapher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> They set out from Mount Shapher and camped at Haradah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They set out from Haradah and camped at Makheloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> They set out from Makheloth and camped at Tahath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> They set out from Tahath and camped at Terah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> They set out from Terah and camped at Mithcah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They set out from Mithcah and camped at Hashmonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:5–6"> They set out from Hashmonah and camped at Moserah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Genesis 36:27; 1 Chronicles 1:43"> They set out from Moserah and camped at Bene-Jaakan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:7"> They set out from Bene-Jaakan and camped at Hor Haggidgad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:7"> They set out from Hor Haggidgad and camped at Jotbathah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> They set out from Jotbathah and camped at Abronah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:8; 1 Kings 9:26; 1 Kings 22:48"> They set out from Abronah and camped at Ezion Geber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 27:14; Deuteronomy 32:51"> They set out from Ezion Geber and camped in the desert of Zin, that is, Kadesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:4; Numbers 20:22–23; Numbers 20:16"> They set out from Kadesh and camped at Mount Hor, at the edge of the land of Edom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 20:24–28"> Aaron the priest went up to Mount Hor at the ⸤command⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “mouth”">h</a></sup>of Yahweh, and he died there in the fortieth year after the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">i</a></sup>had gone out from the land of Egypt, in the fifth month on the first day of the month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Aaron was one hundred and twenty-three years old when he died on Mount Hor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 21:1–9"> Now the Canaanite, the king of Arad, who was living in the Negev<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="An arid region south of the Judean hills">j</a></sup>in the land of Canaan, heard of the coming of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 21:4"> Then they set out from Mount Hor and camped at Zalmonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> They set out from Zalmonah and camped at Punon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 21:10"> They set out from Punon and camped at Oboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 21:11"> They set out from Oboth and camped at Iye Abarim, the boundary of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> They set out from Iyim and camped at Dibon Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 6:14; Isaiah 15:2"> They set out from Dibon Gad and camped at Almon-Diblatayim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:49; Deuteronomy 34:1; Numbers 27:12"> They set out from Almon-Diblatayim and camped in the mountains of Abarim, before Nebo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> They set out from the mountains of Abarim and camped on the desert-plateau of Moab by the Jordan across Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Joshua 2:1; Joshua 13:20; Numbers 25:1–9"> They camped by the Jordan, from Beth-Jeshimoth up to Abel Shittim, on the desert-plateau of Moab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 33:48–49"> Then Yahweh spoke to Moses on the desert-plateau of Moab by the Jordan across Jericho, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Joshua 3:17; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">l</a></sup>and say to them, ‘When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Exodus 23:24; Leviticus 26:1; Deuteronomy 20:16–18"> you will drive out the inhabitants of the land from your presence, and you will destroy all their idols and all the images of their molten idols, and you will demolish all their high places;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:31; Psalm 115:16; Deuteronomy 32:8"> you will dispossess the land and live in it because I have given the land to you to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Numbers 26:53–56; Joshua 18:11–48; Joshua 15:1–12"> You will distribute the land by lot according to your clans; to the larger group you will give a larger inheritance, and to the smaller group you will give less inheritance. However the lot falls for him, there the lot will be. You will distribute it according to the tribes of your ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “fathers”">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Judges 2:3; Psalm 106:34–36; Deuteronomy 7:16"> But if you do not drive out the inhabitants of the land from your presence, then it will happen that whomever you let remain of them will be like irritants in your eyes and like thorns in your sides; they will be your enemies in the land in which you live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 21:23–24; Deuteronomy 28:63; Leviticus 20:23"> And just as I planned to do to them, I will do to you.’ ”</span>
</div>
