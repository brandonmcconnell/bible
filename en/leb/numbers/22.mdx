## Balak and Balaam

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:1; Deuteronomy 3:8; Numbers 21:20"> The ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>set out, and they encamped on the desert-plateau of Moab, across from Jericho beyond the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Numbers 21:20–35; Numbers 21:3"> Balak son of Zippor saw all that Israel did to the Amorites,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “Amorite”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 15:15; Isaiah 23:5; Joshua 9:24"> and Moab was very terrified in the presence of the people because ⸤they⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “he” or “it”">c</a></sup>were numerous; and Moab dreaded the presence of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 25:15–18; Numbers 31:8; Numbers 24:17"> And Moab said to the elders of Midian, “Now the crowd will lick up all around us, like a bull devours the grass of the field.” And Balak son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:4; Joshua 24:9; Micah 6:5"> He sent messengers to Balaam son of Beor at Pethor, which is by the river,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, the Euphrates">e</a></sup>in the land of the children of his people, to summon him, saying, “Look! A people went out from Egypt. Look! They cover ⸤the surface of the land⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the eye of the land”">f</a></sup>they are about to dwell opposite me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:7–8; Acts 16:16"> Now, please go, curse this people for me because they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">g</a></sup>are stronger than me; perhaps I will be able to strike them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">h</a></sup>and drive them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">i</a></sup>out from the land because I know whoever you bless is blessed, and whoever you cursed is cursed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; Micah 3:11; Romans 16:18"> So the elders of Moab and the elders of Midian went with a fee for divination in their hand; they came to Balaam and spoke the words of Balak to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 22:19–20; Jeremiah 12:2"> He said to them, “Spend the night here, and I will return, and I will return word to you, just as Yahweh speaks to me.” So the princes of Moab stayed with Balaam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:3; Numbers 22:20; Genesis 16:8"> And God came to Balaam and said, “Who are these men with you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Balaam said to God, “Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, sent word to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> ‘Look! A people went out from Egypt. Look! They cover ⸤the surface of the land⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “the eye of the land”">j</a></sup>Now, go, curse them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">k</a></sup>for me. Perhaps I will be able to attack them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">l</a></sup>and drive them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Hebrew “him”">m</a></sup>out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Ephesians 1:3; Genesis 12:2"> God said to Balaam, “You will not go with them; you will not curse the people, because they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Hebrew “he”">n</a></sup>are blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 22:14"> Balaam got up in the morning, and he said to the princes of Balak, “Go to your land, because Yahweh refused to allow me to go with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 22:37; Numbers 22:13"> The princes of Moab got up and went to Balak, and they said, “Balaam refused to come with us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 10:7–8; Numbers 22:7–8"> Balak again sent many princes, who were more honored ⸤than the former⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “than these”">o</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They came to Balaam and said to him, “Thus says Balak son of Zippor, ‘Please, let nothing keep you from coming to me</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; Esther 5:11; Matthew 4:8–9"> because I will surely honor you greatly, and all that you say to me I will do. Please, come; curse this people for me.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 24:13; 1 Kings 22:14; 2 Chronicles 18:13"> Balaam answered and said to the servants of Balak, “Even though Balak gives to me his house full of silver and gold, I am not able to go beyond ⸤the command of Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of Yahweh”">p</a></sup>my God to do a little or a lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:3; 1 Timothy 6:9–10"> And now please, ⸤you also stay here⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “please stay in this”">q</a></sup>the night, and ⸤let me find out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “let me know”">r</a></sup>again what Yahweh will say with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 23:12; Numbers 24:13; Numbers 23:26"> And God came to Balaam at night, and he said to him, “If the men have come to call you, get up and go with them; but only the word that I will speak to you, you will do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 1:15–16"> So Balaam got up in the morning and saddled his donkey, and he went with the princes of Moab.</span>
</div>

## Balaam and the Angel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 4:24; Lamentations 2:4; Numbers 22:32"> But ⸤God became angry⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “God’s nose became hot”">s</a></sup>because he was going, and the angel of Yahweh stood in the road as an adversary to him; he was riding on his donkey, and two servants were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jude 1:11; 2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–29"> The donkey saw the angel of Yahweh standing in the road with his sword drawn in his hand, and the donkey turned aside from the road and went into the field. And Balaam struck the donkey to turn her back to the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The angel of Yahweh stood in the narrow path of the vineyards, with ⸤a wall on either side⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “a wall from this and a wall from this”">t</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 47:12; Job 5:13–15"> When the donkey saw the angel of Yahweh, she pressed herself into the wall, and she pressed the foot of Balaam into the wall, so he struck her again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hosea 2:6; Isaiah 26:11"> Then the angel of Yahweh went further ahead and stood in a narrow place where there was not a way to turn aside to the right or left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 14:16; James 1:19; Proverbs 27:3–4"> When the donkey saw the angel of Yahweh, she lay down under Balaam, so ⸤Balaam became angry⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “Balaam’s nose became hot”">u</a></sup>and he struck the donkey with his staff.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; Luke 1:37; Exodus 4:11"> Yahweh opened the mouth of the donkey, and she said to Balaam, “What did I do to you that you struck me these three times?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 12:10; Matthew 15:19; Proverbs 12:16"> Balaam said to the donkey, “Because you made a mockery of me! If only I had a sword in my hand, I would kill you right now!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Peter 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28"> The donkey said to Balaam, “Am I not your donkey on which you have ridden all your life until this day? Have I been in the habit of doing this to you?” He said, “No.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 21:19; Luke 24:31; Acts 26:18"> Then Yahweh exposed the eyes of Balaam, and he saw the angel of Yahweh standing in the road with his sword drawn in his hand, and he bowed down and worshiped to his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Acts 13:10; Numbers 22:22"> The angel of Yahweh said to him, “Why have you struck this donkey three times? Look, I have come out as an adversary because your conduct is perverse before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 16:33–35; Numbers 14:37; 1 Kings 13:24–28"> The donkey saw me and turned aside from me these three times. If she had not turned aside from my face, then I would have killed you and kept her alive.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Job 34:31–32; 2 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 15:24"> Balaam said to the angel of Yahweh, “I have sinned because I did not know that you were standing to meet me in the road. Now, ⸤if it is displeasing to you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “if it is evil in your eyes”">v</a></sup>I will turn back.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:9–12; Isaiah 37:26–29; Numbers 22:20–21"> The angel of Yahweh said to Balaam, “Go with the men, but speak only the word that I will speak to you.” So Balaam went with the princes of Balak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 16:2; Acts 28:15; Jeremiah 48:20"> When Balak heard that Balaam was coming, he went out to meet him by the city of Moab, which was on the boundary of Arnon at the end of the territory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 24:11; John 5:44; Matthew 4:8–9"> And Balak said to Balaam, “Did I not urgently send to meet with you? Why did you not come to me? Am I really not able to honor you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 22:18; Proverbs 19:21; Numbers 23:16"> Balaam said to Balak, “Look, I came to you now. Am I really able to speak anything at all? I speak the word that God puts in my mouth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Balaam went with Balak, and they came to Kiriath-Huzoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 23:14; Proverbs 1:16; Numbers 23:2"> And Balak sacrificed cattle and sheep, and he sent them to Balaam and to the princes who were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 23:13; Numbers 25:2–3; 2 Chronicles 11:15"> And it happened, in the morning Balak took Balaam and took him up to Bamoth-Baal, and he saw from there the end of the nation.</span>
</div>
