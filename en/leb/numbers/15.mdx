## Various Sacrifices and Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Numbers 15:18; Leviticus 25:2"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>and say to them, ‘When you come into the land of your dwellings that I am about to give to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:18; Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18"> you will make an offering by fire for Yahweh from the cattle or from the flock, a burnt offering or a sacrifice to fulfill a vow, or as a freewill offering or at your feasts, to make a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:40; Leviticus 23:13; Leviticus 6:14"> And the one who presents an offering<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “his offering”">b</a></sup>for Yahweh, he will present a grain offering of finely milled flour; a tenth will be mixed with a fourth of the liquid measure of oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 28:14; Judges 9:13"> and you will add a fourth of wine for the libation upon the burnt offering, or to the sacrifice for each ram-lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:12–14; Numbers 15:4"> Or for the ram you will make a grain offering of two-tenths of finely milled flour mixed into a third of a liquid measure of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> You will present a third of the liquid measure of wine for the libation, a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1; Leviticus 7:11–18; Leviticus 1:3"> When you prepare ⸤a bull⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “a son of cattle”">c</a></sup>as a burnt offering or a sacrifice to fulfill a vow or a fellowship offering for Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 28:14; Numbers 28:12; Leviticus 6:14"> you will present with ⸤the bull⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the son of the cattle”">d</a></sup>a grain offering of three-tenths of finely milled flour mixed with half a liquid measure of oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 15:5; Numbers 6:15"> and you will present half a liquid measure of wine as a libation, as an offering made by fire, a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> “ ‘This is how it should be done for each bull, or for the each ram, or for the small four-footed mammal, or ram-lambs, or goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> According to the number that you prepare, so should you do to each according to their number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Every native must do these things to present an offering made by fire, a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If an alien dwells among you, or whoever is in your midst throughout your generations,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew “for your generations”">e</a></sup>and prepares an offering made by fire, a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh, he should do as you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Numbers 15:29; Exodus 12:49"> For the assembly, there will be one decree for you and for the alien who dwells among you; it is an eternal decree for all your generations. ⸤You as well as the alien⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “like you like the alien”">f</a></sup>will be ⸤before Yahweh⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of Yahweh”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> There will be one law and one stipulation for you and for the alien dwelling among you.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 15:2; Deuteronomy 26:1–15"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">h</a></sup>and say to them, ‘When you come into the land to which I am about to bring you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:11–12"> whenever you eat from the food of the land, you will lift up a contribution to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14; Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 26:2–10"> You must lift up a contribution of the first batch of your ring-shaped dough bread; you must lift it up as a contribution of the threshing floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:28; Numbers 18:26"> You will give to Yahweh a contribution from the first of your dough throughout your generations.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “for your generations”">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 5:15–17"> “ ‘But if you go astray and you do not follow<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “do”">j</a></sup>all these commandments that Yahweh commanded to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> all that Yahweh commanded you by the hand of Moses<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or “through Moses”">k</a></sup>from the day that Yahweh commanded and beyond, throughout your generations,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “for your generations”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:13–21; Leviticus 5:15"> and if it was done unintentionally ⸤without the knowledge⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “from the eyes”">m</a></sup>of the community, then the entire community must prepare one ⸤young bull⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “a bull a son of cattle”">n</a></sup>as a burnt offering, as a fragrance of appeasement for Yahweh, and its grain offering and its libation, according to the stipulation, and one male goat as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 2:17"> The priest will make atonement for all of the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">o</a></sup>and ⸤they will be forgiven⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “it will be forgiven to them”">p</a></sup>because it was unintentional; they will bring their offering, an offering made by fire for Yahweh, their sin offering ⸤before Yahweh⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of Yahweh”">q</a></sup>for their unintentional sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 15:24"> All of the community of the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">r</a></sup>will be forgiven, as well as the alien that dwells in their midst, because the whole community was involved in the unintentional wrong.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–28; Acts 17:30; Acts 3:17"> “ ‘If one person sins unintentionally, that person will present a female goat ⸤in its first year⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Literally “a daughter of a year”">s</a></sup>as a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35"> And the priest will make atonement for the person who ⸤sinned unintentionally⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “sinned unintentionally when sinning an unintentional wrong”">t</a></sup>⸤before Yahweh⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “in the presence of Yahweh”">u</a></sup>to make atonement for him, and he will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 15:15; Leviticus 16:29; Leviticus 17:15"> For the native among the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">v</a></sup>and the alien that dwells in their midst, there will be one law for anyone who commits an unintentional wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Psalm 19:13; Deuteronomy 1:43"> But the one ⸤who acts presumptuously⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “who acts with a high hand”">w</a></sup>from among the native or alien blasphemes against Yahweh, and that person must be cut off from the midst of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9; Proverbs 13:13; Ezekiel 18:20"> Because he despised the word of Yahweh and broke his command, that person will be surely cut off and bear the guilt.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Violation of the Sabbath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Exodus 31:14–15; Exodus 20:8–10"> When the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">x</a></sup>were in the desert, they found a man who was gathering wood on the day of the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:3–20"> The ones who found him gathering wood brought him to Moses, Aaron, and to all the community.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12; Numbers 9:8"> And they put him under watch because it was not made clear what should be done to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Exodus 31:14–15; 1 Kings 21:13"> And Yahweh said to Moses, “Surely the man must be put to death by stoning him; all the community must stone him with stones from outside the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 7:25"> So the entire community brought him out to a place outside the camp, and ⸤they stoned him to death⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-y" id="fnref-y" data-fn="Literally “they stoned him with stones and he died”">y</a></sup>just as Yahweh commanded Moses.</span>
</div>

## Garment Fringes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:12; Matthew 9:20"> “Speak to the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-z" id="fnref-z" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">z</a></sup>and tell them to make for themselves tassels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-aa" id="fnref-aa" data-fn="Hebrew “tassel”">aa</a></sup>on the hems of their garments throughout their generations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ab" id="fnref-ab" data-fn="Hebrew “for their generations”">ab</a></sup>and to put a blue cord on the tassel of the hem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalm 106:39; Proverbs 28:26; Psalm 73:27"> You will have a tassel ⸤for you to look at⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ac" id="fnref-ac" data-fn="Literally “and you will look at it”">ac</a></sup>and remember all the commands of Yahweh and do them, and not follow ⸤after the unfaithfulness of your own heart and eyes⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-ad" id="fnref-ad" data-fn="Literally “after your heart and after your eyes, which you are unfaithful after them”">ad</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 12:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:7; Leviticus 11:44–45"> so that you will remember and do all my commandments, and you will be holy for your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–10; Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 11:16"> I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God; I am Yahweh your God.”</span>
