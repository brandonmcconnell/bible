## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:14"> The elder, to Gaius the beloved, whom I love in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:3–9; 2 Peter 3:18; Colossians 1:4–6"> Dear friend, I pray you may prosper concerning everything and be healthy, just as your soul prospers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:4; 2 John 1:2; Psalm 119:11"> For I rejoiced exceedingly when the brothers came and testified to your truth, just as you are walking in the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 4:19; 2 Timothy 1:2; Proverbs 23:24"> I have no greater joy than this: that I hear my children are walking in the truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions to Gaius

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; Luke 12:42; Luke 16:10–12"> Dear friend, you act faithfully<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “you act loyally”">a</a></sup>in whatever you do for the brothers, ⸤even though they are strangers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “and this strangers”">b</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Titus 3:13; Acts 15:3"> They have testified to your love before the church; you will do well to send them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “whom”">c</a></sup>on their way in a manner worthy of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:5; Revelation 2:3; 2 Kings 5:15–16"> For they have gone out on behalf of the name, accepting nothing from the pagans.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, Gentile unbelievers (as opposed to Gentile Christians)">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:5–9; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Corinthians 7:2–3"> Therefore we ought to support such people, so that we become fellow workers with the truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Diotrephes Causes Trouble

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 1:7–16; Luke 22:24–27; Matthew 23:4–8"> I wrote something to the church, but Diotrephes, who wants to be first among them, does not acknowledge us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 66:5; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Luke 6:22"> Therefore, if I come, I will call attention to ⸤the deeds he is doing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “his deeds which he is doing”">e</a></sup>, disparaging us with evil words. And not being content with these, he does not receive the brothers himself, and he hinders those wanting to do so and throws them out of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; Psalm 37:27; Ephesians 5:1"> Dear friend, do not imitate what is evil, but what is good. The one who does good is of God; the one who does evil has not seen God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Demetrius Commended

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7; John 21:24; Acts 22:12"> Demetrius has been testified to by all, even by the truth itself. And we also testify to him, and you know that our testimony is true.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Conclusion and Final Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 John 1:12"> I have many things to write to you, but I do not want to write to you by means of ink and pen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; Ephesians 6:23; Romans 16:1–16"> But I hope to see you right away, and to speak ⸤face to face⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “mouth to mouth”">f</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 10:3"> Peace be to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
