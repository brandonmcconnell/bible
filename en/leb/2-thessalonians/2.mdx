## The Man of Lawlessness Will Be Revealed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Mark 13:27; Matthew 24:31"> Now we ask you, brothers, concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our assembling to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 John 4:1–2; Mark 13:7; Matthew 24:4–6"> that you not be easily shaken from your composure, nor be troubled either by a spirit or by a message or by a letter ⸤alleged to be from us⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “as if by us”">a</a></sup>, to the effect that the day of the Lord has arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:4–6; 1 John 2:18; Ephesians 5:6"> Do not let anyone deceive you in any way, for that day will not come unless the rebellion comes first, and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Daniel 7:25; Revelation 13:6–7"> who opposes and who exalts himself over every so-called god or object of worship, so that he sits down in the temple of God, proclaiming that he himself is God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 1:15; Galatians 5:21; Matthew 16:9"> Do you not remember that while we were still with you, we were saying these things to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> And you know that which restrains him now, so that he will be revealed in his own time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 4:3; Revelation 17:7; 2 Timothy 2:17–18"> For the mystery of lawlessness is at work already; only the one who now restrains<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “restrains it” (referring to the “mystery of lawlessness”; or “restrains him” (referring to the “man of lawlessness” in v. 3 )">b</a></sup>will do so until he is out of the way,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “out of the midst”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; Revelation 19:20–21; Isaiah 11:4"> and then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus will slay with the breath of his mouth, and wipe out by the appearance of his coming,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Revelation 19:20; Ephesians 2:2"> whose coming is in accordance with the working of Satan, with all power and signs and lying wonders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Proverbs 1:7; 2 Corinthians 4:2–3"> and with every unrighteous deception against those who are perishing, in place of which they did not accept the love of the truth, so that they would be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:28; John 12:39–43; 1 Timothy 4:1"> And because of this, God sends them ⸤a powerful delusion⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “a working of deceit”">d</a></sup>so that they will believe the lie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Romans 8:7–8; Mark 16:16"> in order that all may be condemned who did not believe the truth, but delighted in unrighteousness.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Stand Firm and Hold Fast

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 1 Thessalonians 1:4"> But we ought to give thanks to God always concerning you, brothers dearly loved by the Lord, because God has chosen you as first fruits for salvation by the sanctification of the Spirit and faith in the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 1:5"> for which purpose he called<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “he also called”">e</a></sup>you through our gospel for the obtaining of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> So then, brothers, stand firm and hold fast to the traditions which you were taught, whether by spoken word or by letter from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:2–5; John 3:16; 1 Peter 1:3–8"> Now may our Lord Jesus Christ himself and God our Father, who has loved us and given us eternal encouragement and good hope by grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> encourage your hearts and strengthen you in every good work and word.</span>
</div>
</div>
