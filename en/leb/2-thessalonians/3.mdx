## Paul’s Prayer Request

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:25; Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 16:9"> Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may progress and be honored, just as also it was with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Romans 15:31; Deuteronomy 32:20"> and that we may be delivered from evil and wicked people, ⸤for not all have the faith⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “for not of all is the faith”">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 10:13"> But the Lord is faithful, who will strengthen you and guard you from the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Philemon 1:21; 1 Thessalonians 4:10"> And we have confidence in the Lord about you, that you are both doing and you will do the things that we are commanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 119:36; 1 Chronicles 29:18; Deuteronomy 30:6"> Now may the Lord direct your hearts toward the love of God and toward the patient endurance of Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Warning Against Lazy, Irresponsible Behavior

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 3:7; Romans 16:17"> But we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you keep away from every brother who lives irresponsibly and not according to the tradition that they received from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:9; 1 Corinthians 4:16"> For you yourselves know how it is necessary to imitate us, that we did not behave irresponsibly among you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; Ephesians 4:28"> nor did we eat bread from anyone without paying, but with toil and labor, we were working night and day in order not to be a burden to any of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7; 1 Thessalonians 2:6; 1 Corinthians 9:4–14"> not that we do not have the right, but so that we may give ourselves as an example to you, so that you may imitate us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 20:4"> For even when we were with you, we used to command this to you: that if anyone does not want to work, neither should he eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Thessalonians 4:11"> For we hear that some among you are living irresponsibly, working at nothing, but being busybodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 4:28; 1 Thessalonians 4:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:8"> Now we command and we exhort such people in the Lord Jesus Christ that, working with quietness, they eat their own bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 6:9–10; Hebrews 12:3; 2 Corinthians 4:1"> But as for you, brothers, do not be discouraged while doing what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Corinthians 5:11"> But if anyone does not obey our message through this letter, take note not to associate with him, in order that he may be put to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Corinthians 5:5"> And do not consider him as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalm 29:11; John 16:33; Matthew 1:23"> Now may the Lord of peace himself grant you peace through everything in every way. May the Lord be with all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:21; 2 Thessalonians 1:5; Colossians 4:18"> The greeting is by my hand, Paul’s, which is ⸤a sign of genuineness⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “a sign”">b</a></sup>in every letter: ⸤this is how I write⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “in this way I write”">c</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.</span>
