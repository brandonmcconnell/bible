## Mordecai is Promoted

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 2:7; Esther 2:15; Proverbs 13:22"> On that day King Ahasuerus gave Queen Esther the house of Haman, the enemy of the Jews; and Mordecai came before the king, for Esther had told what he was to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 3:10; Genesis 41:42; Daniel 2:48"> And the king removed his signet ring that he had taken away from Haman, and he gave it to Mordecai. So Esther set Mordecai over the house of Haman.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 12:4; Isaiah 38:2; 2 Kings 4:27"> And Esther again spoke before the king, and she fell before his feet and wept, pleading for his grace to avert Haman the Agagite’s evil plan and the plot that he devised against the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:2; Esther 4:11"> And the king held out to Esther the scepter of gold, and Esther rose and stood before the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esther 7:3; Esther 5:8; Esther 3:12–13"> and she said, “If it is good to the king, and if I have found favor before him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “if I have won his favor”">a</a></sup>and if the king is pleased with this matter, and ⸤I have his approval⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “I am good in his eyes”">b</a></sup>let an edict be written to revoke the letters of the plans of Haman son of Hammedatha the Agagite, which he wrote to destroy the Jews that are in all the provinces of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 7:4; Romans 10:1; Romans 9:2–3"> For ⸤how can I bear⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “how am I able”">c</a></sup>to look on the disaster that will find my people, and ⸤how can I bear⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “how am I able”">d</a></sup>to look on the destruction of my family?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 7:10–1; Galatians 3:13; Proverbs 13:22"> And King Ahasuerus said to Queen Esther and to Mordecai the Jew, “Look, I have given Haman’s house to Esther, and they have hanged him on the gallows because he ⸤plotted against⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sent his hand against”">e</a></sup>the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 1:19; Esther 3:12; Esther 8:5"> Write ⸤as you see fit⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “as it is good in your eyes”">f</a></sup>concerning the Jews in the name of the king, and seal it with the king’s signet ring; for a decree that is written in the name of the king and sealed with the king’s signet ring cannot be revoked.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esther 1:22; Esther 1:1; 2 Kings 18:26"> And the secretaries of the king were summoned<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “called”">g</a></sup>at that time, in the third month, which is in the month of Sivan on the twenty-third day, and an edict was written according to all that Mordecai commanded, to the Jews and to the governors and satraps and officials of the provinces from India to Cush<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="That is, “Ethiopia”">h</a></sup>—one hundred and twenty-seven provinces<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew “province”">i</a></sup>—each province according to its own script and to every people in their own ⸤language⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “tongue”">j</a></sup>and to the Jews in their own script and language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Jeremiah 2:23; Isaiah 66:20"> And he wrote in the name of King Ahasuerus, and he sealed the letters with the king’s signet ring and sent them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Hebrew “the letters”">k</a></sup>by couriers on horses, riding on royal horses ⸤bred by⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons of”">l</a></sup>racing mares.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="See HALOT 1244 s.v.">m</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esther 3:13; Psalm 37:14–15; Psalm 146:6–9"> In them the king allowed the Jews who were in every city to assemble and ⸤defend their lives⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “to stand for their lives ”">n</a></sup>to destroy and kill and annihilate any army of any people or province attacking them, including women and children, and to plunder their spoil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esther 9:1; Esther 3:13–15; Exodus 15:9–10"> in one day in all the provinces of King Ahasuerus, on the thirteenth day of the twelfth month, which is the month of Adar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 6:10; Esther 3:14; Judges 16:28"> A copy of the ⸤edict⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “writing”">o</a></sup>was to be given as law in each province to inform all the people, so that the Jews would be ready<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="See HALOT 902 s.v.">p</a></sup>on that day to avenge themselves from their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 3:15; Daniel 8:2; Esther 2:3"> The mounted couriers on the royal horses went out without delay, urged by the king’s word. The law was given in the citadel of Susa.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 16:19; Matthew 6:29; Esther 3:15"> Mordecai went out from the presence of the king in ⸤royal clothing⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “clothing of the kingdom”">q</a></sup>of blue cloth and white linen, and a great crown of gold and a robe of fine white linen and purple, and the city of Susa was shouting and rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:10; Psalm 97:11; Psalm 18:28"> For the Jews, there was light and gladness, joy and honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esther 9:19; Zechariah 8:20–23; 1 Samuel 25:8"> In every province and city, wherever the king’s edict and his law came, there was gladness and joy for the Jews, a banquet and a ⸤holiday⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “day of good”">r</a></sup>and many of the people from the country were posing as Jews because the fear of the Jews had fallen on them.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
