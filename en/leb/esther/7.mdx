## Esther’s Banquet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Esther 3:15"> So the king and Haman went ⸤to dine⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to drink”">a</a></sup>with Queen Esther.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 5:3; John 16:24; Esther 5:6"> And the king again said to Esther, on the second day ⸤while they were drinking⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “at the meal of wine”">b</a></sup>“What is your petition, Queen Esther? It will be given to you. What is your request? It will be given to you—even half the kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:26; Psalm 122:6–9; Esther 7:7"> Then Queen Esther answered, and she said, “If I have found favor in your eyes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “If I have won your favor”">c</a></sup>O king, and if it is good to the king, let my life be given to me at my petition and my people at my request;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 3:9; Deuteronomy 28:68; Esther 3:13"> I and my people have been sold to be destroyed and killed, to be annihilated. If we had been sold as male and female slaves I would have kept quiet, because this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “there”">d</a></sup>is not a need sufficient to trouble the king.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See HALOT 1437, s.v. NRSV translates, “but no enemy can compensate for this damage to the king”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:3; Genesis 27:33; Job 9:24"> And King Ahasuerus said to Queen Esther, “Who is he, and where is he, who ⸤gave himself the right to do this⸥?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “has filled his heart to do so”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:5–12; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Psalm 139:19–22"> And Esther said, “The adversary and enemy is this evil Haman!” And Haman was terrified before the king and queen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Haman is Hanged

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 1:12; Proverbs 19:12; Revelation 3:9"> The king rose in his anger ⸤from the banquet⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “from the banquet of wine”">g</a></sup>and went to the palace garden, and Haman stood to beg for his life from Queen Esther, for ⸤he realized that the king was determined to make an end to his life⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “that evil had been determined for him from the king”">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 1:6; Esther 6:12; Job 9:24"> And the king returned from the palace garden to the ⸤banquet hall⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “house of the meal of wine”">i</a></sup>where Haman was lying prostrate on the couch that Esther was on, and the king said, “Will he also molest the queen with me in the house?” As the words<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Hebrew “word”">j</a></sup>went from the king’s mouth they covered Haman’s face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 7:15–16; Proverbs 11:5–6; Esther 1:10"> And Habrona, one of the eunuchs in the presence of the king, said, “Look, the same gallows that Haman had prepared for Mordecai who spoke good for the sake of the king stands at Haman’s house, fifty cubits high.” And the king said, “Hang him on it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 7:16; Daniel 6:24; Ezekiel 5:13"> And they hanged Haman on the gallows that he had prepared for Mordecai, and the anger of the king was abated.</span>
</div>
