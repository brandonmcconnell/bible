## Mordecai Tells Esther of Haman’s Plot

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; Ezekiel 27:30–31; 2 Samuel 13:19"> Mordecai learned all that had been done and he tore his clothes and put on sackcloth and ashes. And he went through the middle of the city and cried out a loud and bitter cry;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> he went up to the entrance of the gate of the king, for he could not go to the gate of the king in sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Daniel 9:3; Isaiah 58:5"> In every province each place where the king’s edict and his law came, there was great mourning for the Jews with fasting, crying, wailing, and sackcloth; and ashes were spread out as a bed for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 77:2; Jeremiah 31:15; Esther 1:12"> And Esther’s maids and her eunuchs came and they told her, and the queen was deeply distressed; she sent garments to clothe Mordecai so that he might remove his sackcloth—but he did not accept them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Romans 12:15; Esther 1:10"> Then Esther called Hathach from the king’s eunuchs ⸤who regularly attended to her⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “who stood before her presence”">a</a></sup>and she ordered him to go to Mordecai to learn what was happening and why.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 4:3; Esther 7:2; Esther 9:12"> So Hathach went out to Mordecai, to the public square of the city, which was in front of the gate of the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 3:2–15"> and Mordecai told him all that had happened to him, and the exact amount of money that Haman has promised to pay to the treasury of the king for the destruction of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4; Job 9:15; 1 Timothy 6:13"> And he gave him a copy of the edict of the law that had been issued in Susa for their destruction to show Esther, and to inform her, and to charge her to go to the king and make supplication to him and entreat before him for her people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Hathach went back and told Esther the words of Mordecai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Esther spoke to Hathach and ⸤she gave him a message for Mordecai⸥:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “she charged him to Mordecai”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esther 5:1–2; Esther 8:4; Daniel 2:9"> “All the king’s servants and the people of the king’s provinces know that if any man or woman who goes to the king to the inner courtyard, who is not called, he has one law, to be killed, except if the king extends to him the gold scepter so that he may live. I have not been called to come to the king ⸤for thirty days⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “these thirty days”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And they told Mordecai the words of Esther.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 24:10–12; Philippians 2:30; John 12:25"> Then Mordecai told them to reply to Esther: “Do not think that your life will be saved in the palace of the king more than all the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 45:4–8; 1 Samuel 12:22; Deuteronomy 32:36"> For if indeed you keep silent at this time, relief and deliverance will arise for the Jews from another place, and you and the family of your father will perish. Who knows? Perhaps you have come to a royal position for a time such as this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Esther replied to Mordecai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:24; 2 Chronicles 20:3; Joel 2:12–17"> “Go, gather all the Jews that are found in Susa and fast for me; do not eat or drink for three days, both night and day. I and my young girls will fast likewise, and then I will go to the king, which is not according to the law; if I perish, I perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Mordecai went away and he did everything that Esther commanded him.</span>
</div>
