## Esther’s Banquet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 6:4; Esther 4:16; Revelation 3:21"> And it happened, on the third day, and Esther put on royal clothes, and she stood in the inner courtyard of the ⸤king’s palace⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “house of the king”">a</a></sup>opposite the ⸤king’s palace⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “house of the king”">b</a></sup>the king was sitting on his royal throne in the ⸤throne room⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “house of the reign” or “house of the kingdom”">c</a></sup>opposite the doorway of the palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 8:4; Esther 4:11; Proverbs 21:1"> When the king saw Queen Esther standing in the courtyard she found favor in his eyes, and the king held out the gold scepter that was in his hand to Esther, and Esther approached and touched the top of the scepter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:23; Esther 7:2; Esther 5:6"> And the king said to her, “What is it, Queen Esther? What is your request? It will be given to you—even half the kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Proverbs 29:11; Esther 3:15"> And Esther said, “If it is good to the king, let the king and Haman come today to the banquet that I have prepared for him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esther 6:14"> And the king said, “Bring Haman quickly ⸤to fulfill⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to do”">d</a></sup>the request of Esther.” So the king and Haman came to the banquet that Esther had prepared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 7:2; Esther 5:3; Esther 9:12"> And the king said to Esther ⸤while they were drinking wine⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “at the meal of wine”">e</a></sup>“What is your petition? It will be given to you. What is your request? Even half the kingdom, it will done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Esther answered and said, “This is my petition and my request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 7:3; Proverbs 16:9; Esther 8:5"> If I have found favor in the eyes of the king,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “I have won the favor of the king">f</a></sup>and if it is good to the king to grant my petition and fulfill my request, let the king and Haman come to the banquet that I will prepare for them tomorrow, and I will do according to the word of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esther 3:5; Matthew 2:16; Psalm 27:3"> And Haman went out on that day rejoicing and ⸤feeling good⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “good of heart”">g</a></sup>But when Haman saw Mordecai at the gate of the king, and he did not rise or tremble before him, Haman was filled ⸤with rage toward⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “rage to”">h</a></sup>Mordecai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 6:13; Ecclesiastes 7:9; 2 Samuel 13:22–23"> But Haman controlled himself and went to his house, and he sent for and brought his friends and Zeresh his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esther 9:7–10; Esther 3:1; Luke 12:19–20"> And Haman recounted to them the splendor of his wealth and the number of his sons and all the ways that the king had honored him and promoted him above the officials and king’s servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 27:1; Psalm 37:35–36; Job 20:5–8"> ⸤And Haman added⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “And Haman said also”">i</a></sup>“Esther the Queen did not let just anyone come to the banquet that she prepared with the king except me, and I am also invited tomorrow to her banquet with the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 15:20; Job 18:4; Philippians 4:11–12"> But all this ⸤fails to satisfy me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “it is not sufficient for me”">j</a></sup>⸤when⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “at every time”">k</a></sup>I see Mordecai the Jew setting at the gate of the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 6:4; Psalm 37:14; 1 Kings 21:7"> And Zeresh his wife and all of his friends said to him, “Let them make a gallows fifty cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew “cubit”">l</a></sup>high, and in the morning tell the king, “Let them hang Mordecai on it; then go with the king to the banquet happily.” The advice pleased Haman, so he had the gallows made.</span>
</div>
