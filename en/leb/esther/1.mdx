## The King’s Banquets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 9:1; Esther 8:9; Daniel 6:1"> And it happened in the days of Ahasuerus, the Ahasuerus who reigned from India to Cush<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, “Ethiopia”">a</a></sup>—over one hundred and twenty-seven provinces.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “province”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:1; 2 Samuel 7:1; Daniel 8:2"> In those days as King Ahasuerus was sitting on the throne of his kingdom, which was in the citadel of Susa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 6:21; Esther 2:18; Daniel 5:1"> he gave a banquet in the third year of his reign for all his officials and servants. The army of Persia and Media and the nobles and officials of the provinces were in his presence</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 40:10; Ephesians 1:18; Psalm 76:1–4"> as he displayed the wealth of the glory of his kingdom and the glorious splendor of his greatness for many days, one hundred and eighty days<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew “day”">c</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esther 7:7–8; 2 Chronicles 30:21–25; 2 Chronicles 7:8–9"> And when those days were completed, the king gave for all the people that were present at the citadel of Susa, both great and small, a banquet in the courtyard of the king’s palace garden that lasted seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 6:4; Ezekiel 23:41; Amos 2:8"> There were curtains of finely woven linen and blue cloth tied with cords of fine white linen and purple cloth to silver curtain rings and pillars of alabaster, and couches of gold and silver on a paved floor of alabaster, precious stone, mother-of-pearl, and costly stones.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew “stone”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 5:2–4; 2 Chronicles 9:20; Esther 2:18"> Drinks were served in goblets of gold and ⸤goblets of different kinds⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “goblets from changing goblets”">e</a></sup>and there was ⸤plentiful royal wine according to the bounty of the king⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “much wine of the kingdom according to the hand of the king”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:15–16; Jeremiah 51:7; Jeremiah 35:8"> There were no restrictions on the drinking, for the king had instructed every official of his palace to do as each one pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Esther 5:4"> Furthermore, Queen Vashti gave a banquet for the women in ⸤the palace⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “in the house of the reign” or “in the house of the kingdom”">g</a></sup>that belonged to King Ahasuerus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Queen Vashti Refuses the King’s Request

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 16:25; Esther 7:9; Proverbs 20:1"> On the seventh day, when the heart of the king was merry with wine, he said to Mehuman, Biztha, Harbona, Bigtha, Abagtha, Zethar, and Carcas, seven of the eunuchs attending King Ahasuerus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:30; Mark 6:21–22; Proverbs 16:9"> to bring Queen Vashti before the king ⸤with her royal crown⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “with her headdress of the kingdom”">h</a></sup>to show the people and the officials her beauty, ⸤for she was very attractive⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for she was of good appearance”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 19:12; Proverbs 20:2; 1 Peter 3:1"> But Queen Vashti refused to come at the word of the king that was ⸤conveyed by⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “by the hand of”">j</a></sup>the eunuchs. And the king became very angry, and his anger burned in him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:32; Daniel 2:2; Jeremiah 10:7"> And the king said to the wise men, ⸤the ones who know the times⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “the knowers of the times”">k</a></sup>—for it was the procedure of the king before all those who knew ⸤law and rights⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or “custom and legalities”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 25:19; Ezra 7:14; Matthew 18:10"> and those next to him were Carshena, Shethar, Admatha, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena, Memucan, the seven officials of Persia and Media who ⸤had access to the king⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “saw the face of the king”">m</a></sup>and sat first in the kingdom—</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esther 6:6"> “According to the law, what is to be done with Queen Vashti, because she has not done the command of King Ahasuerus ⸤conveyed by⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “by the hand of”">n</a></sup>the eunuchs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 18:14; 1 Corinthians 6:7–8; Acts 25:10"> And Memucan said before the king and the officials, “Not only has Queen Vashti done wrong to the king, but to all the officials and all of the people who are in all the provinces of King Ahasuerus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:16; Ephesians 5:33"> For this deed of the queen ⸤will be known⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “will go out”">o</a></sup>to all the women, causing them ⸤to look with contempt on their husbands⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “to despise their husbands in their eyes”">p</a></sup>as they will say, ‘King Ahasuerus commanded Queen Vashti to be brought before him but she did not come!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 11:3; Judges 5:29"> This day the women of nobility from Persia and Media will respond to all the officials of the king and there will be no end to contempt and anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; Esther 8:8; Esther 3:9"> ⸤If it pleases⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Literally “If it is good to”">q</a></sup>the king, let ⸤a royal edict⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Literally “a word of the kingdom”">r</a></sup>go out from him, and let it be written among the laws of Persia and Media so that it will not be altered, that Vashti cannot come before King Ahasuerus; and let the king give her royal position to her neighbor who is better than she.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 3:18; Ephesians 5:22; Ephesians 5:33"> And let the king’s decree that he will make be proclaimed in all his kingdom, because it is vast and all the women will honor their husbands, great and small.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or “high and low”">s</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:37; Esther 2:4; Esther 1:19"> ⸤This advice pleased the king⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-t" id="fnref-t" data-fn="Literally “The word was good in the eyes of the king">t</a></sup>and the officials, and the king acted according to the word of Memucan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esther 3:12; Esther 8:9; Ephesians 5:22–24"> And he sent letters to all the provinces of the king, to each province according to its own script, and to every people in their own ⸤language⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “tongue”">u</a></sup>that every man should be the master of his house and who speaks in the ⸤language⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-v" id="fnref-v" data-fn="Literally “tongue”">v</a></sup>of his people.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
