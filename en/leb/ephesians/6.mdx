## Children and Parents

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:20–25; Proverbs 23:22; Proverbs 6:20"> Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 27:16; Matthew 15:4–6"> “Honor your father and mother” (which is the first<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “is a very important”">a</a></sup>commandment with a promise),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:16; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:28"> “in order that it may be well with you, and you may ⸤live a long time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “be long-lived”">b</a></sup>on the earth.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 20:12 and/or Deut 5:16">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:21; Proverbs 22:6; Proverbs 29:15"> And fathers, do not make your children angry, but bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Slaves and Masters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 2:18–21; Titus 2:9–10; Colossians 3:17–24"> Slaves, obey your earthly masters with fear and trembling, in the sincerity of your heart, as to Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:22–23; 1 Thessalonians 2:4; Galatians 1:10"> not ⸤while being watched⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “with eye service”">d</a></sup>, as people pleasers, but as slaves of Christ doing the will of God from the heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 6:5–6; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> serving with goodwill as to the Lord and not to people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Luke 14:14; Proverbs 11:18"> because you know that each one of you, whatever good he should do,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “if he should do anything good”">e</a></sup>this he will receive back from the Lord, whether slave or free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 3:25–1; Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11"> And masters, do the same things to them, giving up threats, knowing that both their Lord and yours is in heaven, and there is no partiality with him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Spiritual Warfare

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:31; Joshua 1:9"> Finally, become strong in the Lord and in the might of his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Romans 13:12; Ephesians 6:13"> Put on the full armor of God, so that you may be able to stand against the stratagems of the devil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Ephesians 2:2"> because our struggle is not against blood and flesh, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the world rulers of this darkness, against the spiritual forces of wickedness in the heavenly places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Luke 21:36; Ephesians 6:11–17"> Because of this, take up the full armor of God, in order that you may be able to resist in the evil day, and having done everything, to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:8; Isaiah 11:5"> Stand therefore, girding your waist with truth, and putting on the breastplate of righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and binding shoes under your feet with the preparation of the good news of peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; 1 John 5:4–5; Proverbs 18:10"> in everything taking up the shield of faith, with which you are able to quench all the flaming arrows of the evil one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 59:17; Revelation 19:15"> and receive the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; Colossians 4:2; Philippians 4:6"> with all prayer and supplication praying at all times in the Spirit, and to this end being alert with all perseverance and supplication for all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; Acts 4:29; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> and for me, that a word may be given to me at the opening of my mouth, to make known with boldness the mystery of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Colossians 4:4; 2 Timothy 2:9"> for the sake of which I am an ambassador in chains, that ⸤in them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “in it” since “chain” is singular in the Greek text">f</a></sup>I may speak freely, as it is necessary for me to speak.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Conclusion and Benediction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; 2 Timothy 4:12; Titus 3:12"> Now, so that you also may know ⸤my circumstances⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the things with me”">g</a></sup>, what I am doing, Tychicus, my dear brother and faithful servant in the Lord, will make known to you all things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:7–8; 2 Thessalonians 2:17"> whom I have sent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “whom I am sending”">h</a></sup>to you for this very reason, that you may know ⸤our circumstances⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the things concerning us”">i</a></sup>, and he may encourage your hearts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 6:16; Galatians 5:6; 1 Peter 5:14"> Peace to the brothers and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 22:37; 2 Corinthians 8:12; Matthew 6:13"> Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ in incorruptibility.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “with undying love”">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
