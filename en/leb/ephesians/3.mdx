## The Mystery of Christ Revealed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Timothy 1:8; Philippians 1:13–16"> On account of this I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for the sake of you Gentiles</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 1:25–27; 1 Timothy 1:11; 2 Timothy 1:11"> —if indeed you have heard about the stewardship of God’s grace given to me for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; Romans 16:25; Ephesians 1:9–11"> According to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “given to me for you, that according to”">a</a></sup>revelation the mystery was made known to me, just as I wrote beforehand in brief,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:6"> so that you may be able when you read<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “by reading”">b</a></sup>to understand my insight into the mystery of Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:10–12; 2 Peter 3:2; Titus 1:1–3"> (which in other generations was not made known to the sons of men as it has now been revealed to his holy apostles and prophets by the Spirit):</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:26–29; 1 John 1:3; 1 John 2:25"> that the Gentiles are fellow heirs, and fellow members of the body, and fellow sharers of the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 1:19; Ephesians 3:20; Ephesians 3:2"> of which I became a servant, according to the gift of God’s grace given to me, according to the working of his power.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Ephesians 2:7"> To me, the least of all the saints, was given this grace: to proclaim the good news of the fathomless riches of Christ to the Gentiles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 13:35; 2 Timothy 1:9"> and to enlighten everyone as to what is the administration of the mystery hidden from the ages by God, who created all things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:12; Ephesians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 2:7"> in order that the many-sided wisdom of God might be made known now to the rulers and the authorities in the heavenly places through the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:4"> according to the purpose of the ages which he carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 5:2; Hebrews 4:14–16"> in whom we have boldness and access in confidence through faith in him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “through his i.e., Christ’s faithfulness”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; 2 Corinthians 1:6"> Therefore I ask you not to be discouraged at my afflictions on behalf of you, which are your glory.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Prayer for Spiritual Strength

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 95:6; Ephesians 1:16–19; Philippians 2:10"> On account of this, I bend my knees before the Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Revelation 5:8–14; Ephesians 1:21"> from whom every<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “the whole”">d</a></sup>family in heaven and on earth is named,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> that he may grant you according to the riches of his glory to be strengthened with power through his Spirit in the inner person,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:7; 1 John 4:16; John 14:23"> that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith (you having been firmly rooted and established in love),</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 103:11–12; Job 11:7–9; Ephesians 1:18–23"> in order that you may be strong enough to grasp together with all the saints what is the breadth, and length, and height, and depth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 1:23; Colossians 2:9–10; Colossians 1:10"> and to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, in order that you may be filled up to all the fullness of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; Romans 4:21; Jeremiah 32:27"> Now to the one who is able to do beyond all measure more than all that we ask or think, according to the power that is at work in us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:25; Romans 11:36; 2 Peter 3:18"> to him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus to all generations forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
