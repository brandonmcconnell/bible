## Made Alive in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 2:13; Ephesians 2:5–6; Colossians 3:1–4"> And you, ⸤although you were dead⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “and you being dead”">a</a></sup>in your trespasses and sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 6:12; 1 John 5:19; Ephesians 2:3"> in which you formerly walked according to the course of this world, according to the ruler of the authority of the air, the spirit now working in the sons of disobedience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 3:3; Ephesians 4:22; Galatians 5:16–24"> among whom also we all formerly lived in the desires of our flesh, doing the will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “desires”">b</a></sup>of the flesh and of the mind, and we were children of wrath<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="This phrase is a Semitic idiom which can mean either (1) “children characterized by wrath” or (2) “children destined for wrath”">c</a></sup>by nature, as also the rest of them were.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Ephesians 1:7; Psalm 86:15"> But God, being rich in mercy, because of his great love with which he loved us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 3:24; Ephesians 2:8; Romans 5:10"> and we being dead in trespasses, he made us alive together with Christ (by grace you are saved),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:1–3; Ephesians 1:3; Romans 6:4–5"> and raised us together and seated us together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:16–17; Titus 3:4; Ephesians 2:4"> in order that he might show in the coming ages the surpassing riches of his grace in kindness upon us in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 2:5; Romans 4:16"> For by grace you are saved through faith, and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 11:6; Titus 3:3–5"> it is not from works, so that no one can boast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:10; Hebrews 13:21"> For we are his creation, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, so that we may walk in them.</span>
</div>

## Jewish and Gentile Believers United in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Colossians 2:11; Colossians 2:13; Colossians 3:11"> Therefore remember that formerly you, the Gentiles in the flesh, the so-called uncircumcision by the so-called circumcision in the flesh, made by hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 4:8; Colossians 1:21; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> that you were at that time apart from Christ, alienated from the citizenship of Israel, and strangers to the covenants of promise, not having hope, and without God in the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:39; Colossians 1:20–22; Colossians 1:13–14"> But now in Christ Jesus you, the ones who once were far away, have become near by the blood of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; Colossians 3:15"> For he himself is our peace, who made both one and broke down the dividing wall of the partition, the enmity, in his flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 2:14; Galatians 3:10; Colossians 1:21–22"> invalidating the law of commandments in ordinances, in order that he might create the two in himself into one new man, thus making peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–22; Romans 5:10; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> and might reconcile both in one body to God through the cross, killing the enmity in himself.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “by it” (referring to the cross)">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 57:19–21; Acts 10:36; Romans 5:1"> And coming, he proclaimed the good news of peace to you who were far away and peace to the ones who were near,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:13; Ephesians 3:12; Romans 5:2"> because through him we both have access in one Spirit to the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 1 John 3:1; Galatians 6:10"> Consequently, therefore, you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but you are fellow citizens of the saints and members of the household of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; Isaiah 28:16; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus himself being the cornerstone,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or perhaps “capstone”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; 1 Corinthians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> in whom the whole building, joined together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–5; 1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:16"> in whom you also are built up together into a dwelling place of God in the Spirit.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “are built up together in spirit = spiritually into a dwelling place of God” (cf. nrsv)">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
