## Imitators of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 4:11; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Ephesians 4:32"> Therefore become imitators of God, as beloved children,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 13:34; Galatians 2:20; Colossians 3:14"> and live in love, just as also Christ loved us, and gave himself for us an offering and sacrifice to God for a fragrant smell.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “a fragrant offering”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Ephesians 5:5; Galatians 5:19–21"> But sexual immorality, and all uncleanness, or greediness, must not even be named among you (as is fitting for saints),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ephesians 4:29; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> and obscenity, and foolish talk, or coarse jesting (which are not proper), but rather thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; 1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Ephesians 5:3"> For this you know ⸤for certain⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “for this you know, knowing”">b</a></sup>, that every sexually immoral person, or unclean person, or greedy person (who is an idolater), does not have an inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 1:18; Colossians 2:8; Matthew 24:4"> Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God is coming on the sons of disobedience.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Walk Like Children of Light

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 5:22"> Therefore do not be sharers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “partners”">c</a></sup>with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; 1 John 1:7; Acts 26:18"> for you were formerly darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Live like children of light</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:9–10; Galatians 5:22–23; 3 John 1:11"> (for the fruit of the light is in all goodness and righteousness and truth),</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 12:28; Psalm 19:14"> trying to learn what is well-pleasing to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:12; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> And do not participate in the unfruitful deeds of darkness, but rather even expose them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> For it is shameful even to speak about the things being done by them in secret,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:20–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Hosea 2:10"> but all things exposed by the light are made visible,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Romans 13:11–12; Isaiah 26:19"> for everything made visible is light. Therefore it says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Wake up, sleeper,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and rise from the dead,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Christ will shine on you.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation of unknown origin with possible allusions to Isa 26:19 , 51:17 , 52:1 , 60:1">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; James 3:13; Philippians 1:27"> Therefore, consider carefully how you live, not as unwise but as wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Amos 5:13; John 12:35"> making the most of the time because the days are evil.</span>
</div>

## Being Filled by the Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 1:9"> Because of this do not become foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 1:15; Romans 13:13"> And do not be drunk with wine (in which is dissipation), but be filled by the Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Psalm 95:2"> speaking to one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and singing praise in your heart to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Hebrews 13:15"> giving thanks always for all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to the God and Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Ephesians 5:24; Ephesians 5:22"> being subject to one another ⸤out of reverence for⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in the fear of”">e</a></sup>Christ</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:18–1; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Titus 2:5"> —wives to their own husbands as to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3–10; Colossians 1:18; Ephesians 1:22–23"> because the husband is the head of the wife, as also Christ is the head of the church (he himself being the Savior of the body).</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 5:33; Colossians 3:20; Titus 2:7"> But as the church is subject to Christ, thus also wives should be subject to their husbands in everything.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Husbands and Wives

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:28"> Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for her;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 15:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> in order that he might sanctify her by cleansing her with the washing of water by the word;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:19"> in order that he might present to himself the church glorious, not having a spot or wrinkle or any such thing, but that she may be holy and blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Matthew 19:5; Genesis 2:21–24"> Thus also husbands ought to love their own wives as their own bodies. The one who loves his own wife loves himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14–15; Ephesians 5:31; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> For no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, just as also Christ does the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; Romans 12:5"> because we are members of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5; Mark 10:7–8"> “For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Gen 2:24">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> (This mystery is great, but I am speaking with reference to Christ and the church.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:2–7; Esther 1:20; Ephesians 5:25"> Only you also, each one of you, must thus love his own wife as himself, and the wife ⸤must respect⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “that she fear”">g</a></sup>her husband.</span>
</div>
