## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 1:1–2"> Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, to the saints who are in Ephesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A number of the earliest and most important manuscripts omit “in Ephesus,” though it is hard to make sense of the line without this phrase (cf. rsv, “to the saints who are also faithful”)">a</a></sup>and faithful in Christ Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Galatians 1:3; Titus 1:4"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Praise to God for Spiritual Blessings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 1:3; Ephesians 2:6"> Blessed is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “blessed be ”">b</a></sup>the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Romans 8:28–30; Ephesians 2:10"> just as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and blameless before him in love,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “before him, having predestined us in love” (the phrase “in love” could go either with v. 4 or v. 5 )">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 8:29–30; Ephesians 1:11; Galatians 4:5–6"> having predestined us to adoption through Jesus Christ to himself according to the good pleasure of his will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 3:22–26; Ephesians 1:12; Colossians 1:13"> to the praise of the glory of his grace that he bestowed on us in the beloved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Romans 3:24; 1 John 1:7–9"> in whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 5:12; Ephesians 3:10; Romans 5:15"> that he caused to abound to us in all wisdom and insight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–28; Romans 16:25–27; Ephesians 3:3–9"> making known to us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure that he purposed in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Philippians 2:9–10; Galatians 4:4"> for the administration of the fullness of times, to bring together all things in Christ, the things in the heavens and the things on the earth, in him</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 8:28; 1 Peter 1:4; Isaiah 46:10–11"> in whom also we were chosen, having been predestined according to the purpose of the One who works all things according to the counsel of his will,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6; Jeremiah 17:5–7; Ephesians 1:13–14"> that we who hoped beforehand in Christ should be for the praise of his glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 4:30; 2 Timothy 2:19"> in whom also you, when you heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation, in whom also when you believed you were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 4:30"> who is the down payment of our inheritance, until the redemption of the possession, to the praise of his glory.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Prayer for Spiritual Wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philemon 1:5; Colossians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> Because of this I also, hearing of ⸤your faith⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “the according to you faith”">d</a></sup>in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Colossians 1:9; Colossians 1:3"> do not cease giving thanks for you, making mention in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; James 3:17–18; Colossians 1:9–10"> that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, ⸤the glorious Father⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “the Father of glory”">e</a></sup>, may give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Luke 24:45"> (the eyes of your hearts having been enlightened), so that you may know what is the hope of his calling, what are the riches of the glory of his inheritance among the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 2:12"> and what is the surpassing greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the working of his mighty strength</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:12; Mark 16:19; Psalm 110:1"> which he has worked<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “he worked”">f</a></sup>in Christ, raising him from the dead and seating him at his right hand in the heavenly places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:9–11; Colossians 2:10; Colossians 1:15–16"> above all rule and authority and power and lordship and every name named, not only in this age but also in the coming one,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Colossians 2:10; 1 Corinthians 15:25–27"> and he subjected all things under his feet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="An allusion to Ps 8:6">g</a></sup>and gave him as head over all things to the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:18–19; John 1:16; Colossians 2:9–10"> which is his body, the fullness of the one who fills all things in every way.</span>
</div>
</div>
