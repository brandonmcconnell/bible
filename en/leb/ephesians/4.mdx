## Christians Should Live in Unity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Philippians 1:27; 1 Thessalonians 2:12"> Therefore I, the prisoner in the Lord, exhort you to live in a manner worthy of the calling with which you were called:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; 1 Corinthians 13:7; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> with all humility and gentleness, with patience, putting up with one another in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Colossians 3:13–15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13"> being eager to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 12:4–5; 1 Corinthians 12:4–13"> one body and one Spirit (just as also you were called with one hope of your calling),</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Peter 3:21"> one Lord, one faith, one baptism,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Malachi 2:10; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> one God and Father of all, who is over all, and through all, and in all.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gifted Leaders Bring the Church to Maturity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> Now to each one of us was given this grace, according to the measure of Christ’s gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 68:18; Colossians 2:15; 1 Samuel 30:26"> Therefore it says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Ascending on high he led captivity captive;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he gave gifts to men.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 68:18">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 3:13; Matthew 12:40; Proverbs 30:4"> Now “he ascended,” what is it, except that he also descended<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some secondary manuscripts add “first”">b</a></sup>to the lower regions of the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 7:26; Hebrews 4:14; Ephesians 1:20–23"> The one who descended himself is also the one who ascended above all the heavens, in order that he might fill all things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:28–29; Ephesians 2:20"> And he himself gave some as apostles and some as prophets and some as evangelists and some as pastors and teachers</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11–14; Ephesians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> for the equipping of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for building up the body of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:28; John 17:21; Ephesians 4:5"> until we all reach the unity of the faith and the knowledge of the Son of God, to a mature man, to a measure of the maturity of the fullness of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 13:9; Romans 16:17–18; 1 Corinthians 14:20"> so that we may no longer be infants, tossed about by waves and carried about by every wind of teaching, by the trickery of people, by craftiness with reference to the scheming of deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 3:18; Ephesians 4:25; Zechariah 8:16"> But speaking the truth in love, we are to grow into him with reference to all things, who is the head, Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 2:19; John 15:5; Colossians 2:2"> from whom the whole body, joined together and held together by every supporting ligament, according to the working by measure of each single part, the growth of the body makes for the building up of itself in love.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Appeal for New Behavior

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; 1 Peter 4:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> This therefore I say and testify in the Lord, that you no longer walk as the Gentiles walk: in the futility of their mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Ephesians 2:12; Romans 1:21–23"> being darkened in understanding, alienated from the life of God, because of the ignorance that is in them, because of the hardness of their heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Romans 1:24–26; 1 Timothy 4:2"> who, becoming callous, gave themselves over to licentiousness, for the pursuit of all uncleanness in greediness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Titus 2:11–14; John 6:45; Matthew 11:29"> But you did not learn Christ in this way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 5:20; Ephesians 1:13; Hebrews 3:7–8"> if indeed you have heard about him, and you were taught by him (just as truth is in Jesus),</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 6:6; Hebrews 12:1; James 1:21"> that you take off, according to your former way of life, the old man, who is being destroyed according to deceitful desires,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Romans 8:6; Psalm 51:10"> be renewed in the spirit of your mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:10–14; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ephesians 2:10"> and put on the new man (in accordance with God), who is created in righteousness and holiness from the truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Old Behavior, New Behavior, and Motivation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Zechariah 8:16; Proverbs 12:22"> Therefore, putting aside the lie, speak truth each one of you with his neighbor,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="An allusion to Zech 8:16">c</a></sup>because we are members of one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 1:19; Psalm 4:4; Psalm 37:8"> Be angry and do not sin;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="An allusion to Ps 4:4">d</a></sup>do not let the sun set on your anger,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:16"> nor give place to the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Galatians 6:10"> The one who steals must steal no longer, but instead must labor, working with his own hands what is good, so that he may have something to share with the one who has need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Ecclesiastes 10:12"> ⸤No rotten word must proceed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “Every rotten word must not proceed”">e</a></sup>from your mouth, but only something good for the building up of the need, in order that it may give grace to those who hear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:19; Isaiah 63:10; Ephesians 1:13–14"> and do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Colossians 3:8; Ecclesiastes 7:9; Titus 3:2–3"> All bitterness, and rage, and wrath, and clamor, and abusive speech, must be removed from you, together with all wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:12–13; Matthew 6:14–15; Mark 11:25"> Become<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “But become”">f</a></sup>kind toward one another, compassionate, forgiving one another, just as also God in Christ has forgiven you.</span>
</div>
</div>
