## Absent from the Body and at Home with the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 19:25–26; 1 John 3:2; 2 Peter 1:13–14"> For we know that if our earthly house, the tent, is destroyed, we have a building from God, a house not made by hands, eternal in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 8:23; 1 Corinthians 15:53–54; 2 Corinthians 5:3–4"> For indeed, in this house we groan, because we desire to put on our dwelling from heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 3:7–11; Revelation 16:15; Revelation 3:18"> if indeed, even after we have taken it off,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A number of important early manuscripts read “have put it on” here">a</a></sup>we will not be found naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:53–54; 2 Corinthians 5:2; Isaiah 25:8"> For indeed we who are in this tent groan, being burdened ⸤for this reason, that⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in that”">b</a></sup>we do not want to be unclothed, but to be clothed, in order that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 1:13–14; Romans 8:23"> Now the one who has prepared us for this very thing is God, who has given us the down payment, the Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:1; Philippians 3:20–21; Hebrews 11:13"> Therefore, although we are always confident and know that while we are at home in the body we are absent from the Lord—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:1–27; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> for we live by faith, not by sight—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20–24; 2 Corinthians 5:6; John 14:3"> so we are confident and prefer rather to be absent from the body and to be at home with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; 2 Peter 3:14; Romans 14:18"> Therefore indeed we have as our ambition, whether at home in the body or absent from the body, to be acceptable to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Revelation 22:12; Ephesians 6:8"> For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, in order that each one may receive back the things through the body according to what he has done, whether good or bad.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Controlled by the Love of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jude 1:23; Hebrews 10:31; 2 Timothy 2:24–26"> Therefore, because we know the fear of the Lord, we are attempting to persuade people, but we are revealed to God, and I hope to be revealed in your consciences.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:1; 2 Corinthians 1:14; Galatians 6:12–14"> We are not commending ourselves to you again, but are giving you an opportunity to boast about us, in order that you may have an answer for those who boast in appearance and not in heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:16–17; 2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6"> For if we are out of our senses, it is for God; if we are of sound mind, it is for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; Romans 14:7–9; Matthew 10:37–38"> For the love of Christ controls us, because we have concluded this: that one died for all; as a result all died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 14:7–9; 1 Peter 4:2–4; 1 Peter 1:14–15"> And he died for all, in order that those who live should no longer live for themselves, but for the one who died for them and was raised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Philippians 3:7–8; John 6:63"> So then, from now on we know no one ⸤from a human point of view⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “according to the flesh”">c</a></sup>, if indeed we have known Christ ⸤from a human point of view⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “according to the flesh”">d</a></sup>, but now we know him this way no longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Ephesians 4:22–24; Isaiah 43:18–19"> Therefore if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; the old things have passed away; behold, new things have come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:20–21; 2 Corinthians 5:19–20; Romans 5:10–11"> And all these things are from God, who has reconciled us to himself through Christ, and who has given us the ministry of reconciliation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; 1 John 2:1–2"> namely, that God was in Christ reconciling the world to himself, not counting their trespasses against them, and entrusting to us the message of reconciliation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; Luke 14:23; Malachi 2:7"> Therefore we are ambassadors on behalf of Christ, as if God were imploring you through us. We beg you on behalf of Christ, be reconciled to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:22–24; 1 Peter 3:18; Romans 5:19"> He made the one who did not know sin to be sin on our behalf, in order that we could become the righteousness of God in him.</span>
</div>
</div>
