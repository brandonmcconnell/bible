## New Covenant Ministry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11; 2 Corinthians 10:12; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> Are we beginning to commend ourselves again? Or do we, like some, need letters of recommendation to you or from you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:1–2; 1 Thessalonians 1:8; Romans 1:8"> You are our letter, inscribed on our hearts, known and read by all people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19; Jeremiah 31:33; Hebrews 8:10"> revealing that you are a letter of Christ, delivered by us, inscribed not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, not on stone tablets but on tablets of human hearts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 3:12; 2 Corinthians 2:14"> Now we possess such confidence through Christ toward God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Philippians 4:13; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> Not that we are adequate in ourselves to consider anything as from ourselves, but our adequacy is from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 6:63; Romans 7:6; Jeremiah 31:31"> who also makes us adequate as servants of a new covenant, not of the letter, but of the Spirit, for the letter kills, but the Spirit gives life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:9–11; Romans 10:4; Exodus 31:18"> But if the ministry of death in letters carved on stone came with glory, so that the sons of Israel were not able to look intently into the face of Moses because of the glory of his face, which was transitory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:18; Galatians 3:14"> how will the ministry of the Spirit not be even more with glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 3:21–22; Hebrews 12:18–21; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For if there was glory in the ministry of condemnation, by much more will the ministry of righteousness overflow with glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 1:17; Haggai 2:7–9; Acts 26:13"> For indeed what had been glorified has not been glorified in this case, on account of the glory that surpasses it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 5:20–21; Hebrews 8:13; Hebrews 7:21–25"> For if what was transitory came with glory, by much more what remains is with glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; Acts 4:13; Philippians 1:20"> Therefore, because we have such a hope, we use much boldness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:33–35; Hebrews 10:1–9; Ephesians 2:14–15"> and not as Moses used to place a veil over his face, in order that the sons of Israel would not stare at the end of what was transitory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Acts 26:18; Romans 11:25"> But their minds were hardened. For until this very day, the same veil remains upon the reading of the old covenant, not being uncovered, because it is done away with in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 13:27–29"> But until today, whenever Moses is read aloud, a veil lies upon their heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 34:34; Isaiah 29:18; Isaiah 25:7"> but whenever one turns to the Lord, the veil is removed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:2; Galatians 5:13; John 8:32"> Now the Lord is the Spirit, and where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:2"> And we all, with unveiled face, reflecting<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “contemplating”">a</a></sup>the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from glory into glory, just as from the Lord, the Spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>
