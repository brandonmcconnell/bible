## Paul’s Ministry Defended

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; Matthew 11:29; Galatians 5:2"> Now I, Paul, appeal to you myself by the humility and gentleness of Christ, who ⸤when I am present in person⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to the face”">a</a></sup>am humble among you, but when I am absent am bold toward you—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 13:2; Galatians 5:16–25"> now I ask when I am present that I will not need to be bold with the confidence with which I propose to show boldness toward some who consider us as behaving according to the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Galatians 2:20; 1 Peter 4:1–2"> For although we are living in the flesh, we do not wage war according to the flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:7; Ephesians 6:13–18; Jeremiah 1:10"> for the weapons of our warfare are not merely human, but powerful to God for the tearing down of fortresses, tearing down arguments</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 55:7; 1 Peter 1:14–15"> and all pride that is raised up against the knowledge of God, and taking every thought captive to the obedience of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:9; Numbers 16:26–30; 2 Corinthians 7:15"> And ⸤we are ready⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “having with ready”">b</a></sup>to punish all disobedience, whenever your obedience is completed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:12; John 7:24; 1 Corinthians 14:37"> You are looking at things according to appearance. If anyone is convinced he himself is Christ’s, he should consider this concerning himself again: that just as Christ himself is, so also are we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:10; 2 Corinthians 12:6; Galatians 1:1"> For even if I boast somewhat more about our authority that the Lord gave us ⸤for building you up and not for tearing you down⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “for building up and not for tearing down of you”">c</a></sup>, I will not be put to shame,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> so that I do not want to appear as if I were terrifying you by my letters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; Exodus 4:10; Galatians 4:13–14"> because it is said, “His letters are severe and powerful, but his bodily presence is weak and his speech is of no account.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:20; 2 Corinthians 13:2–3; 2 Corinthians 13:10"> Let such a person consider this: that what we are in word by letters when we are absent, we will also be in ⸤actions⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “deed”">d</a></sup>when we are present.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; Proverbs 26:12; Luke 18:11"> For we do not dare to classify or to compare ourselves with some who commend themselves, but they themselves, when they measure themselves by themselves and compare themselves with themselves, do not understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:6; Romans 15:20; 1 Peter 4:10"> But we will not boast ⸤beyond limits⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “to the things immeasurable”">e</a></sup>, but according to the measure of the assignment that God has assigned to us as a measure to reach even as far as you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:11; Romans 1:16"> For we are not overextending ourselves, as if we had not reached you, because we have reached even as far as you with the gospel of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 15:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> not boasting ⸤beyond limits⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to the things immeasurable”">f</a></sup>in the labors of others, but having hope that as your faith is growing to be enlarged greatly by you according to our assignment,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 15:24–28; Acts 19:21"> so that we may proclaim the gospel in the regions that lie beyond you, and not boast in the things accomplished in the area assigned to someone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 1 Corinthians 1:31; James 1:9–10"> But “the one who boasts, let him boast in the Lord.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Jer 9:24">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 21:2; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> For it is not the one commending himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord commends.</span>
</div>
</div>
