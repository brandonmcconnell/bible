## Proclaiming Jesus Christ as Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:13; Galatians 6:9; Ephesians 3:13"> Because of this, since we have this ministry, just as we have been shown mercy, we do not lose heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Thessalonians 2:3–5; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> but we have renounced shameful hidden things, not behaving with craftiness or adulterating the word of God, but with the open proclamation of the truth commending ourselves to every person’s conscience before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> But if indeed our gospel is veiled, it is veiled among those who are perishing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:40; 2 Corinthians 3:14; Acts 26:18"> among whom the god of this age has blinded the minds of the unbelievers, so that they would not see the light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:24; 1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Thessalonians 2:5–6"> For we do not proclaim ourselves, but Christ Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Jesus Christ”">a</a></sup>as Lord, and ourselves as your slaves for the sake of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 60:2; Genesis 1:3; 1 Peter 2:9"> For God who said, “Light will shine out of darkness,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="An allusion to Gen 1:3">b</a></sup>is the one who has shined in our hearts for the enlightenment of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “in the face of Jesus Christ”">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Treasure in Earthenware Jars

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; 2 Corinthians 5:1; Ephesians 2:8–9"> But we have this treasure in earthenware jars, in order that the extraordinary degree of the power may be from God and not from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="James 1:2–4; Romans 8:35–37; 1 Peter 4:12–14"> We are afflicted in every way, but not crushed; perplexed, but not despairing;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Micah 7:8; Psalm 37:24–25"> persecuted, but not abandoned; struck down, but not destroyed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 6:5; 2 Timothy 2:11; 1 Peter 4:13"> always carrying around the death of Jesus in our body, in order that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:49; Romans 8:36; Psalm 44:22"> For we who are alive are continually being handed over to death because of Jesus, in order that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our mortal flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Corinthians 4:10; Acts 20:24"> So then, death is at work in us, but life in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 116:10; Hebrews 11:1–40; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> But because we have the same spirit of faith in accordance with what is written, “I believed, therefore I spoke,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 116:10">d</a></sup>we also believe, therefore we also speak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; Romans 8:11; Jude 1:24"> because we know that the one who raised Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “the Lord Jesus”">e</a></sup>will also raise us together with Jesus and present us together with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Ephesians 3:20–21; 2 Corinthians 9:11–12"> For all these things ⸤are for your sake⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “for the sake of you”">f</a></sup>, in order that the grace that is increasing through the many may cause thanksgiving to abound to the glory of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; Colossians 3:10; Isaiah 40:29"> Therefore we do not lose heart, but even if our outer person is being destroyed, yet our inner person is being renewed day after day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:18; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 5:3–5"> For our momentary light affliction is producing in us an eternal weight of glory ⸤beyond all measure and proportion⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “according to an extraordinary degree to an extraordinary degree”">g</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 8:24–25; Hebrews 11:1"> because we are not looking at what is seen, but what is not seen. For what is seen is temporary, but what is not seen is eternal.</span>
</div>
</div>
