## Forgive and Comfort the Offender

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12–13; Galatians 4:12; Proverbs 17:25"> But if anyone has caused sorrow, he has not caused me sorrow, but ⸤to some degree⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “in part”">b</a></sup>—⸤in order not to say too much⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “in order not to burden”">c</a></sup>—to all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 7:11"> This punishment by the majority is sufficient for such a person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 6:1–2; Colossians 3:13"> So then, you should rather forgive and comfort him, lest somehow this person should be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:22–23; Galatians 6:1–2; Galatians 6:10"> Therefore I urge you to confirm your love for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:6; Philemon 1:21; Philippians 2:22"> Because for this reason also I wrote, in order that I could know your proven character, whether you are obedient in everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:23; Matthew 18:18; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> Now to whomever you forgive anything, I also do; for indeed, whatever I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, it is ⸤for your sake⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “for the sake of you”">d</a></sup>in the presence of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> in order that we may not be exploited by Satan (for we are not ignorant of his schemes).</span>
</div>
</div>

## Triumphant Ministry in Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> Now when I arrived in Troas for the gospel of Christ and a door was opened for me by the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5–6; 2 Corinthians 12:18; Titus 1:4"> I did not experience rest in my spirit, because<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This infinitive (“find”) is understood as causal">e</a></sup>I did not find Titus my brother, but saying farewell to them, I departed for Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Romans 8:37; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16"> But thanks be to God, who always leads us in triumphal procession in Christ, and who reveals the fragrance of the knowledge of him through us in every place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Ezekiel 20:41"> For we are the aroma of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; John 9:39; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> to those on the one hand an odor from death to death, and to those on the other hand a fragrance from life to life. And who is qualified for these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 2 Peter 2:1–3"> For we are not like the majority who peddle the word of God, but as from pure motives—but as from God—we speak before God in Christ.</span>
</div>
