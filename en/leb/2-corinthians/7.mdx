## Paul’s Joy at the Corinthians’ Repentance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:7; 1 Peter 2:11; Proverbs 8:13"> Therefore since we have these promises, dear friends, let us cleanse ourselves from all defilement of body and spirit, accomplishing holiness in the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 20:33; 2 Corinthians 6:12–13; 2 Corinthians 11:9"> ⸤Make room for us in your hearts⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “make room for us”">a</a></sup>. We have wronged no one, we have ruined no one, we have defrauded no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 1 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:11"> I do not say this ⸤to condemn you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “for condemnation”">b</a></sup>, because I have already said that you are in our hearts, so that we die together and we live together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 3:12; James 1:2"> Great is my confidence toward you; great is my boasting on your behalf; I am filled with encouragement; I am overflowing with joy in all our affliction.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Acts 20:1"> For even when we arrived in Macedonia, our body had no rest, but we were afflicted in every way—quarrels outside, fears within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 7:13"> But God, who comforts the humble, comforted us by the coming of Titus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:2; Jude 1:3; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> and not only by his coming, but also by the comfort with which he was comforted among you, because he reported to us your longing, your mourning, your zeal for me, so that I rejoiced even more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 20:7–9; Matthew 26:21–22"> For if indeed I grieved you by my letter, I do not regret it. Even if I did regret it (I see<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “for I see”">c</a></sup>that that letter grieved you, even though for ⸤a short time⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “an hour”">d</a></sup>),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 15:7; Jeremiah 31:18–20; 2 Corinthians 7:10–11"> now I rejoice, not that you were grieved, but that you were grieved to repentance. For you were grieved according to the will of God, so that you suffered loss in no way through us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:19; Acts 11:18; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> For grief according to the will of God brings about a repentance leading to salvation, not to be regretted, but worldly grief brings about death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Timothy 5:21–22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> For behold how much diligence this very thing, being grieved according to the will of God, has brought about in you: what defense of yourselves, what indignation, what fear, what longing, what zeal, what punishment! In everything you have demonstrated yourselves to be innocent in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 Timothy 3:5; 2 Corinthians 2:17"> Consequently, even if I wrote to you, it was not because of the one who did wrong or because of the one who had been wronged, but in order that your diligence ⸤on our behalf⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “on behalf of us”">e</a></sup>might be revealed to you before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:6; 1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Corinthians 16:13"> Because of this we have been encouraged, and in addition to our encouragement, we rejoiced much more over the joy of Titus, because his spirit had been refreshed by all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:4; 2 Thessalonians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 1:18–20"> For if I have boasted anything to him about you, I have not been put to shame, but as I have spoken everything to you in truth, thus also our boasting to Titus ⸤has proven to be true⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “has become truth”">f</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; 2 Corinthians 2:9; 1 John 3:17"> And his affection for you is all the more when he remembers the obedience of all of you as you welcomed him with fear and trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:3; Philemon 1:8; Philemon 1:21"> I rejoice, because in everything I am completely confident in you.</span>
</div>
</div>
