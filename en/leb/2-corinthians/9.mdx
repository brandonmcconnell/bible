## God Loves a Cheerful Giver

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 8:4–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:9–10"> For it is unnecessary for me to write to you concerning the ministry to the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:10; 2 Corinthians 8:8; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> because I know your readiness to help, concerning which I keep on boasting to the Macedonians about you, that Achaia has been ready to help since last year, and your zeal has stirred up the majority of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 2 Corinthians 7:14; Titus 3:1"> But I am sending the brothers in order that our boasting about you would not prove to be empty in this case, so that you may be prepared just as I was saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 15:26; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> lest somehow if Macedonians should come with me and find you unprepared, we—⸤not to speak of you⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “so that we do not speak about you”; some manuscripts have “so that I do not speak about you”">a</a></sup>—would be humiliated in connection with this project.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:17"> Therefore I considered it necessary to urge the brothers that they should go on ahead to you and make arrangements in advance for your generous gift that was promised previously, so this would be prepared as a generous gift and not as grudgingly granted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Luke 6:38; Proverbs 22:9"> Now the point is this: the one who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and the one who sows bountifully will also reap bountifully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 20:35; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> Each one should give as he has decided in his heart, not ⸤reluctantly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “out of sorrow”">b</a></sup>or from compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Philippians 4:18–19; Proverbs 10:22"> And God is able to cause all grace to abound to you, so that in everything at all times, because you have enough of everything, you may overflow in every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 112:9; Psalm 112:3; Proverbs 21:21"> Just as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He scattered widely, he gave to the poor;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">his righteousness remains ⸤forever⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 112:9">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10; 2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12"> Now the one who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will provide and multiply your seed, and will cause the harvest of your righteousness to grow,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Chronicles 29:12–14; 1 Timothy 6:17–18"> being made rich in every way for all generosity, which is producing through us thanksgiving to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15; Philemon 1:4–7; Philippians 2:25"> because the service of this ministry is not only supplying the needs of the saints, but also is overflowing through many expressions of thanksgiving to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Matthew 5:16; 2 Corinthians 2:12"> Through the proven character of this service they will glorify God because of the submission of your confession to the gospel of Christ and the generosity of your participation toward them and toward everyone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:14; Philippians 4:1; Philippians 4:18–19"> and they are longing for you in their prayers for you, because of the surpassing grace of God to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Ephesians 5:20; John 3:16"> Thanks be to God for his indescribable gift!</span>
</div>
</div>
