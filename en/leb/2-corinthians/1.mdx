## Thanksgiving for God’s Compassion and Comfort

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 1:3; Romans 15:5–6"> Blessed is the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Isaiah 51:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:16–17"> who comforts us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to comfort those who are in all affliction with the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; Philippians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 4:10–11"> For just as the sufferings of Christ overflow to us, thus through Christ our comfort overflows also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; 2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 4:15–18"> But if we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation; if we are comforted, it is for your comfort that is at work in the patient endurance of the same sufferings that we also suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 1:4–7; James 1:12"> And our hope for you is firm, because we know that as you are sharers in the sufferings, so also you will be sharers in the comfort.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7–12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; 1 Corinthians 4:8"> For we do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, concerning our affliction that happened in the province of Asia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">a</a></sup>that we were burdened to an extraordinary degree, beyond our strength, so that we were in despair even of living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; Hebrews 11:19; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> But we ourselves had the sentence of death in ourselves, so that we would not be putting confidence in ourselves, but in God who raises the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:10; Psalm 34:19; 2 Peter 2:9"> who delivered us from so great a risk of death, and will deliver us, in whom we have put our hope that he will also deliver us again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 1:19; 2 Corinthians 4:15; Acts 12:5"> while you also join in helping ⸤on our behalf⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “on behalf of us”">b</a></sup>by prayer, so that thanks may be given ⸤on our behalf⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “on behalf of us”">c</a></sup>by many persons for this gracious gift given to us through the help of many.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Clear Conscience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; James 3:13–18"> For our reason for boasting is this: the testimony of our conscience that we conducted ourselves in the world, and especially toward you, in holiness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “sincerity”">d</a></sup>and purity of motive from God, not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and not”">e</a></sup>in merely human wisdom, but by the grace of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; Philemon 1:6; 2 Corinthians 13:6"> For we are not writing anything else to you except what you can read or also understand. But I hope that you will understand ⸤completely⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to the end”">f</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:8; 1 Thessalonians 2:19–20"> just as you have also understood us in part, that we are your reason for boasting, just as you are also ours in the day of our Lord Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Delays His Visit to Corinth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:19; Romans 1:11; Romans 15:29"> And with this confidence, I was wanting to come to you previously, in order that you may have a second proof of my goodwill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:5–7; Acts 21:5; Acts 19:21–22"> and through you to go to Macedonia, and to come to you again from Macedonia, and to be sent on my way by you to Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:37; James 5:12; 2 Corinthians 10:2–3"> Therefore, when I was wanting to do this, perhaps then was I making use of vacillation? Or was I deciding what I was deciding according to the flesh, in order that with me my “yes” may be “yes” and my “no” may be “no” at the same time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:9; Revelation 3:7; John 7:28"> But God is faithful, so that our word to you is not “yes” and “no.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:17; 2 John 1:9"> For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, the one who was proclaimed among you by us, by me and Silvanus and Timothy, did not become “yes” and “no,” but has become “yes” in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 2:24–25; Galatians 3:22; Revelation 7:12"> For as many as are the promises of God, in him they are “yes”; therefore also through him is the “amen” to the glory of God through us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:27; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 John 2:20"> Now the one who establishes us together with you in Christ and who anoints us is God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 1:13–14"> who also sealed us and gave the down payment of the Spirit in our hearts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 4:21; Romans 1:9"> But I call upon God as witness against my life, that in order to spare you, I did not come again to Corinth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Corinthians 15:1; Philippians 1:25–26"> Not that we lord it over your faith, but we are fellow workers for your joy, because by faith you stand firm.</span>
</div>
</div>
