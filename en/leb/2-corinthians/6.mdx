## Afflictions of God’s Servants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; Hebrews 12:15; 1 Peter 4:10"> Now because we are fellow workers, we also urge you not to receive the grace of God in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:8; Hebrews 4:7; Hebrews 3:13"> For he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“At the acceptable time I heard you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and in the day of salvation I helped you.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 49:8">a</a></sup><br />Behold, now is the acceptable time; behold, now is the day of salvation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:13; 1 Corinthians 9:12; Matthew 17:27"> We are giving no one an occasion for taking offense in anything, in order that our ministry will not have fault found with it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:10; 2 Corinthians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> but commending ourselves as servants of God in every way, in much endurance, in afflictions, in distresses, in difficulties,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:23–25; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 19:23–34"> in beatings, in prisons, in disturbances, in troubles, in sleepless nights, in going hungry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 1:27; Galatians 5:22"> in purity, in knowledge, in patience, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in love without hypocrisy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Timothy 2:15; Ephesians 6:11–20"> in the word of truth, in the power of God, with the weapons of righteousness for the right hand and left hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 27:63; 1 Peter 4:14; 1 Timothy 4:10"> through glory and dishonor, through slander and good repute, regarded as deceivers and yet truthful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:10–11; Romans 8:36; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> as unknown and yet known completely, as dying, and behold, we go on living, as disciplined, and yet not put to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 16:22; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Revelation 2:9"> as grieving, but always rejoicing, as poor, but making many rich, as having nothing, and possessing everything.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3–4; Philippians 1:8; Psalm 119:32"> ⸤We have spoken freely and openly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the mouth of us has opened”">b</a></sup>to you, Corinthians; our heart is open wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:2; Micah 2:7; Philippians 1:8"> You are not restricted by us, but you are restricted in your affections.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="3 John 1:4; 1 John 3:18; 1 Thessalonians 2:11"> Now the same way in exchange (I am speaking as to children), you open wide your hearts also.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do Not Become Unevenly Yoked with Unbelievers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; 1 Corinthians 10:21; James 4:4"> Do not become unevenly yoked with unbelievers, for what participation is there between righteousness and lawlessness? Or what fellowship does light have with darkness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; 1 Corinthians 10:20–21; 1 John 5:11–13"> And what agreement does Christ have with Beliar? Or what share does a believer have with an unbeliever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45; Hebrews 8:10"> And what agreement does the temple of God have with idols? For we are the temple of the living God, just as God said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will live in</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “with”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">them and will walk about among them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will be their God and they will be my people.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Lev 26:12 (also similar to Jer 32:38 , Ezek 37:27 )">d</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; Revelation 18:4; 2 Corinthians 7:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore “come out from their midst</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and be separate,” says the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“and do not touch what is unclean,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 52:11">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will welcome you,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A paraphrased quotation from Ezek 20:41">f</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:7; John 1:12; 2 Samuel 7:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">and I will be a father to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you will be sons and daughters to me,”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A paraphrased quotation from 2 Sam 7:14 and Isa 43:6">g</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">says the all-powerful Lord.</span></span>
</div>
