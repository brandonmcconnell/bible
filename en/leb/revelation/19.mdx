## The Warrior on the White Horse

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 19:16; Luke 10:22; Revelation 2:17–18"> Now his eyes were a flame of fire, and on his head were many royal headbands having a name written that no one except he himself knows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 63:1–6; John 1:14"> And he was dressed in an outer garment dipped in blood, and his name is called the Word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jude 1:14; Revelation 7:9; Revelation 19:8"> And the armies that are in heaven, dressed in clean, white fine linen, were following him on white horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; Revelation 1:16; Revelation 2:27"> And out of his mouth came a sharp sword, so that with it he could strike the nations. And he will shepherd them with an iron rod,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="An allusion to Ps 2:9">f</a></sup>and he stomps the winepress of the wine of the furious wrath of God, the All-Powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 6:15; Daniel 2:47"> And he has a name written on his outer garment and on his thigh: “King of kings and Lord of lords.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 19:21; Revelation 8:13; Ezekiel 39:17–20"> And I saw one angel standing in the sun, and he cried out with a loud voice, saying to all the birds that fly ⸤directly overhead⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally, “in midheaven”">g</a></sup>,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Come! <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:18–20; Revelation 6:15; 1 Samuel 17:44"> <span className="pl pl-1">in order that you may eat the flesh of kings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the flesh of military tribunes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the flesh of the powerful,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the flesh of horses and those seated on them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the flesh of all people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">both free and slave,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and small and great!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:16; Revelation 16:14; Revelation 17:12–14"> And I saw the beast and the kings of the earth and their armies assembled to make war with the one who is seated on the horse and with his army.</span>

The two were thrown alive into the lake of fire that burns with sulphur.</span>
