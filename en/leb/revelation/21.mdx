## The New Heaven, New Earth, and New Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17–19; 2 Peter 3:13; Isaiah 66:22"> And I saw a new heaven and a new earth, for the first heaven and the first earth had passed away, and the sea did not exist any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 21:10"> And I saw the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared like a bride adorned for her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16; John 14:23"> And I heard a loud voice from the throne saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, the dwelling of God is with humanity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will take up residence with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will be his people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and God himself will be with them.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “with them as their God”">a</a></sup><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he will wipe away every tear from their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and death will not exist any longer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “or”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">mourning or wailing or pain will not exist any longer.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The former things</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “any longer, because the former things ”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have passed away.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 43:19"> And the one seated on the throne said, “Behold, I am making all things new!” And he said, “Write, because these words are faithful and true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 4:10; Revelation 7:17"> And he said to me, “It is done! I am<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts explicitly state “am”">d</a></sup>the Alpha and the Omega, the beginning and the end. To the one who is thirsty I will give water from the spring of the water of life freely.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “without charge”">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Hebrews 8:10; Revelation 2:11"> The one who conquers will inherit these things, and I will be ⸤his God⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to him God”">f</a></sup>and he will be ⸤my son⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to me a son”">g</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Revelation 2:11; Ephesians 5:5–6"> But as for the cowards and unbelievers and detestable persons and murderers and sexually immoral people and sorcerers and idolaters and all liars, their share is in the lake that burns with fire and sulphur, which is the second death.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Description of the New Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:7; Revelation 16:1–17"> And one of the seven angels who had the seven bowls full of the seven last plagues came and spoke with me, saying, “Come, I will show you the bride, the wife of the Lamb.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 40:1–49"> And he carried me away in the Spirit to a great and lofty mountain, and showed me the holy city, Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 22:1"> that has the glory of God. Its radiance is like a precious stone, something like a jasper stone, shining like crystal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34; Revelation 7:4–8; Revelation 21:17–21"> It has<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “having,” referring back to the city mentioned in v. 11">h</a></sup>a great and high wall that has twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and names written on the gates which are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “which are the names”">i</a></sup>of the twelve tribes of the sons of Israel—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34"> on the east, three gates, and on the north, three gates, and on the south, three gates, and on the west, three gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 54:11"> And the wall of the city has twelve foundations, and on them are twelve names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1; Revelation 11:1–2; Ezekiel 41:1–5"> And the one who spoke with me was holding a golden measuring rod in order that he could measure the city and its gates and its wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:35; Ezekiel 48:8–20"> And the city is laid out as a square, and its length is the same as its width. And he measured the city with the measuring rod at twelve thousand stadia;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or “two thousand two hundred kilometers”; or “fourteen hundred miles” (a stade was a unit of length about 185 meters or 607 feet)">j</a></sup>the length and the width and the height of it are equal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Deuteronomy 3:11; Revelation 13:18"> And he measured its wall, one hundred forty-four cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="This number in cubits amounts to 12 times 12; it would be about 65 meters (216 feet)">k</a></sup>⸤according to human measure⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “according to the measure of a man”">l</a></sup>, which is the angel’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:11; Revelation 21:21; Revelation 21:19"> And the material of its wall is jasper, and the city is pure gold, similar in appearance to pure glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; Exodus 28:17–21; Job 28:16–19"> The foundations of the wall of the city are adorned with every kind of precious stone: the first foundation jasper, the second sapphire, the third chalcedony, the fourth emerald,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 4:3"> the fifth sardonyx, the sixth carnelian, the seventh chrysolite, the eighth beryl, the ninth topaz, the tenth chrysoprase, the eleventh jacinth, the twelfth amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:18; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 22:2"> And the twelve gates are twelve pearls, each one of the gates was from a single pearl. And the street of the city is pure gold, like transparent glass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:23; 1 Kings 8:27; 2 Chronicles 2:6"> And I did not see a temple in it, for the Lord God All-Powerful is its temple, and the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:11"> And the city has no need of the sun or of the moon, that they shine on it, for the glory of God illuminates it, and its lamp is the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 21:26; Revelation 22:2; Zechariah 8:22–23"> And the nations will walk by its light, and the kings of the earth will bring their glory into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 60:11; Zechariah 14:7; Revelation 22:5"> And its gates will never be shut by day (for there will be no night there),</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 21:24"> and they will bring the glory and the honor of the nations into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:14–15; Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 20:15"> And every unclean thing and one who practices<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and the one who practices”">m</a></sup>detestable things and falsehood will never enter into it, except those who are written in the book of life of the Lamb.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
