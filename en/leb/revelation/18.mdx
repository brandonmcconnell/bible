<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Revelation 18:8; Revelation 18:15–17"> standing ⸤far off⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “from afar”">c</a></sup>because of the fear of her torment, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Woe, woe, the great city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Babylon the powerful city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because in one hour your judgment has come!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Amos 2:6; 2 Peter 2:3"> and cinnamon and amomum<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “spice”; the term can refer to “spice” in general, or specifically to amomum , a spice from India">d</a></sup>and incense and ointment and frankincense and wine and olive oil and fine wheat flour and wheat and domesticated animals and sheep and horses and carriages and ⸤slaves⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “bodies”">e</a></sup>and human lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Psalm 106:14; Luke 16:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">“And ⸤the fruit your soul desires⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “your fruit of desire”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">has departed from you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all the luxury and the splendor has perished from you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will never find them any more.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 17:4; Luke 16:19–31; Revelation 18:10–11"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Woe, woe, the great city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">dressed in fine linen and purple cloth and scarlet cloth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and adorned with gold and precious stones and pearls,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:27–36; Revelation 17:16; Isaiah 23:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">because in one hour such great wealth has been laid waste!”</span><br />And every shipmaster and ⸤every seafarer⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “everyone who sails to a place”">h</a></sup>and sailors and all those who labor on the sea stood ⸤far off⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “from afar”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Ezekiel 27:30–32; Revelation 18:9–10"> and began to cry out when they saw the smoke of her burning, saying, “Who is like the great city?”</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:10; Revelation 19:1–3; Luke 18:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rejoice over her, heaven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the saints and the apostles and the prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because God has pronounced your judgment on her!”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:13; Jeremiah 25:10; Jeremiah 7:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the sound of harpists and musicians and flute players and trumpeters</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will never be heard in you again!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And every craftsman of every trade</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will never be found in you again!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the sound of a mill</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will never be heard in you again!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 18:3; Isaiah 23:8; Nahum 3:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And the light of a lamp</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will never shine in you again!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the sound of a bridegroom and bride</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will never be heard in you again!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For your merchants were the most important people of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because with your sorcery they deceived all the nations.</span></span>
