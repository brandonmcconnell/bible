## The Opening of the Seventh Seal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Habakkuk 2:20; Revelation 6:1"> And when he opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven for about half an hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Revelation 11:15; Revelation 8:6–1"> And I saw the seven angels who stand before God, and seven trumpets were given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Revelation 8:4; Psalm 141:2"> And another angel who had a golden censer came and stood at the altar, and a large amount of incense was given to him, in order that he could offer the prayers of all the saints on the golden altar that is before the throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 141:2; Luke 1:10; Revelation 8:3"> And the smoke of the incense went up before God with the prayers of the saints from the hand of the angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19; Ezekiel 10:2–7"> And the angel took the censer and filled it with the fire from the altar and threw it to the earth, and there were thunders and sounds and lightnings and an earthquake.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Blowing of the Trumpets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:2"> And the seven angels who had the seven trumpets prepared themselves in order to blow them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Revelation 9:4; Revelation 9:18"> And the first blew the trumpet, and there was hail and fire mixed with blood, and it was thrown to the earth, and a third of the earth was burned up, and a third of the trees were burned up, and all the green grass was burned up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:25; Exodus 7:17–21; Mark 11:23"> And the second angel blew the trumpet, and something like a great mountain burning with fire was thrown into the sea, and a third of the sea became blood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 16:3; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:12"> and a third of the creatures in the sea—the ones which had life—died, and a third of the ships were destroyed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 16:4; Revelation 9:1; Isaiah 14:12"> And the third angel blew the trumpet, and a great star burning like a torch fell from heaven, and it fell on a third of the rivers and on the springs of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:15; Jeremiah 9:15; Deuteronomy 29:18"> And the name of the star was called Wormwood, and a third of the waters became wormwood, and many people died from the waters because they were made bitter.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Joel 2:31; Joel 2:10"> And the fourth angel blew the trumpet, and a third of the sun was struck, and a third of the moon, and a third of the stars, so that a third of them were darkened, and the day did not shine with respect to a third of it, and the night likewise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 9:12; Revelation 11:14; Revelation 14:6"> And I looked, and I heard one eagle flying ⸤directly overhead⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “in midheaven,” i.e., “at the zenith”">a</a></sup>, saying with a loud voice, “Woe, woe, woe to those who live on the earth, from the remaining sounds of the trumpets of the three angels who are about to blow the trumpet!”</span>
</div>
</div>
