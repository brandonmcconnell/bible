## Satan Bound for a Thousand Years

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> And I saw an angel descending from heaven, holding the key of the abyss and a great chain in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Revelation 12:9; Isaiah 27:1"> And he seized the dragon—the ancient serpent, who is the devil and Satan—and bound him for a thousand years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 20:1; Revelation 17:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> and threw him into the abyss, and shut it and sealed it above him, in order that he could not deceive the nations again until the thousand years are completed. After these things it is necessary for him to be released for a short time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:18; Matthew 19:28"> And I saw thrones, and they sat down on them, and authority to judge was granted to them. And I saw the souls of those who had been beheaded because of the testimony about Jesus and because of the word of God, and who had not worshiped the beast or his image, and did not receive the mark on their forehead and on their hand, and they came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:11; Revelation 11:15; Luke 14:14"> (The rest of the dead did not come to life until the thousand years were completed.) This is the first resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Revelation 1:6; 2 Timothy 2:12"> Blessed and holy is the one who has a part in the first resurrection. Over this person the second death has no authority, but they will be priests of God and of Christ, and they will reign with him a thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “for the thousand”">a</a></sup>years.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Satan’s Release and Defeat

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 20:2"> And when the thousand years are completed, Satan will be released from his prison</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Ezekiel 38:1; Revelation 20:10"> and he will go out to deceive the nations that are at the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, to assemble them for battle, ⸤whose number⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “of whom the number of them”">b</a></sup>is like the sand of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 39:6; Ezekiel 38:9"> And they went up on the broad plain of the earth and surrounded the fortified camp of the saints and the beloved city, and fire came down from heaven and consumed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Revelation 19:20; Matthew 25:46"> And the devil who deceived them was thrown into the lake of fire and sulphur, where the beast and the false prophet also are, and they will be tormented day and night ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “for the ages of the ages”">c</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Judgment at the Great White Throne

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; 2 Peter 3:7; Matthew 25:31"> And I saw a great white throne and the one who was seated on it, ⸤from whose presence⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from the presence of whom”">d</a></sup>earth and heaven fled, and a place was not found for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Matthew 16:27; Daniel 7:10"> And I saw the dead—the great and the small—standing before the throne, and books were opened. And another book was opened, which is the book of life, and the dead were judged by what was written in the books, according to their deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Revelation 6:8; Hosea 13:14"> And the sea gave up the dead who were in it, and Death and Hades gave up the dead who were in them, and each one was judged according to their deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:26; Revelation 20:6; Revelation 21:8"> And Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. This is the second death—the lake of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:36; Matthew 13:50; Matthew 25:41"> And if anyone was not found written in the book of life, he was thrown into the lake of fire.</span>
</div>
</div>
