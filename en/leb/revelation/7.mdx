## The Sealing of the 144,000

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:31; Daniel 7:2; Jeremiah 49:36"> After this I saw four angels standing at the four corners of the earth, holding back the four winds of the earth, so that no wind could blow on the earth or on the sea or on any tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Corinthians 1:22; Revelation 7:3–8"> And I saw another angel ascending ⸤from the east⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “from the rising of the sun”">a</a></sup>, holding the seal of the living God, and he cried out with a loud voice to the four angels ⸤who were given permission⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “to whom was granted to them”">b</a></sup>to damage the earth and the sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:4; Revelation 22:4; Revelation 9:4"> saying, “Do not damage the earth or the sea or the trees until we have sealed the slaves of our God on their foreheads.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 14:1; Revelation 14:3; Matthew 19:28"> And I heard the number of the ones who were sealed, one hundred forty-four thousand sealed from every tribe of the sons of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 1:2–4; Numbers 10:14–27; Numbers 13:4–16"> from the tribe of Judah, twelve thousand sealed, from the tribe of Reuben, twelve thousand, from the tribe of Gad, twelve thousand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:36"> from the tribe of Asher, twelve thousand, from the tribe of Naphtali, twelve thousand, from the tribe of Manasseh, twelve thousand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Numbers 26:14; Numbers 25:14"> from the tribe of Simeon,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “Symeon,” an alternate spelling of the Greek form of the name">c</a></sup>twelve thousand, from the tribe of Levi, twelve thousand, from the tribe of Issachar, twelve thousand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> from the tribe of Zebulun, twelve thousand, from the tribe of Joseph, twelve thousand, from the tribe of Benjamin, twelve thousand were sealed.</span>

## The Great Crowd before the Throne

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; John 12:13; Psalm 117:1–2"> After these things I looked, and behold, a great crowd that no one was able to number, from every nation and tribe and people and language, standing before the throne and before the Lamb, dressed in white robes and with palm branches in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:1; Psalm 3:8; Isaiah 45:21"> And they were crying out with a loud voice, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Salvation to our God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who is seated on the throne,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to the Lamb!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 5:11–13; Revelation 19:4–6; Revelation 4:10"> And all the angels stood around the throne and the elders and the four living creatures, and they fell down on their faces before the throne and worshiped God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:12–14; Jude 1:25; Psalm 100:4"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Amen! Praise and glory,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and wisdom and thanksgiving,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and honor and power and strength</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">be to our God ⸤forever and ever⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “for the ages of the ages”">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">. Amen!”</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 7:9; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 5:5"> And one of the elders answered, saying to me, “These who are dressed in the white robes—who are they, and from where have they come?”</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:3–4; Revelation 22:3; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of this, they are before the throne of God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they serve</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “worship”">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">him day and night in his temple,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the one who is seated on the throne ⸤will shelter⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “will take up residence over”">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:10; Psalm 121:6; Matthew 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will not be hungry any longer or be thirsty any longer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor will the sun ever beat down on them, nor any heat,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="An allusion to Isa 49:10">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 7:37–38; Psalm 36:8–9; John 4:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">because the Lamb who is in the midst of the throne will shepherd them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will lead them to springs of living waters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and God will wipe away every tear from their eyes.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="An allusion to Isa 25:8">h</a></sup></span>
