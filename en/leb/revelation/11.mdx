## The Two Witnesses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 21:15; Ezekiel 42:15–20; Zechariah 2:1–2"> And a measuring rod similar in appearance to a staff was given to me, saying, “Get up and measure the temple of God and the altar and those who worship in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Daniel 7:25; Luke 21:24"> And leave out the courtyard outside of the temple, and do not measure it, because it has been given to the Gentiles, and they will trample the holy city for forty two months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 19:15"> And I will grant authority to my two witnesses, and they will prophesy for one thousand two hundred sixty days, dressed in sackcloth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 4:11–14; Zechariah 4:2–3; Jeremiah 11:16"> These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands that stand before the Lord of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:14; 2 Kings 1:10–12; Hosea 6:5"> And if anyone wants to harm them, fire comes out of their mouth and consumes their enemies. And if anyone wants to harm them, in this way he must be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Psalm 105:26–36"> These have the authority to shut the sky, so that ⸤it does not rain⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “it does not rain rain”; the redundancy is removed in the translation">a</a></sup>during the days of their prophecy. And they have authority over the waters, to turn them to blood, and to strike the earth with every plague as often as they wish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:7; Daniel 8:23–24; Daniel 7:25"> And when they have completed their testimony, the beast that comes up from the abyss will make war with them and will conquer them and will kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Revelation 18:10; Revelation 18:21"> And their dead bodies will lie in the street of the great city which is called symbolically Sodom and Egypt, where also their Lord was crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 79:2–3; Revelation 17:15; Revelation 10:11"> And those from peoples and tribes and languages and nations will see their dead bodies three and a half days, and they will not allow their dead bodies to be placed in a tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:17; Revelation 3:10; Acts 5:33"> And those who live on the earth will rejoice over them, and will celebrate and will send gifts to one another, because these two prophets tormented those who live on the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:11; Romans 8:2"> And after the three and a half days, the breath of life from God entered into them, and they stood on their feet, and great fear fell on those who saw them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; 2 Kings 2:11; Acts 1:9"> And they heard a loud voice from heaven saying to them, “Come up here,” and they went up into heaven in a cloud, and their enemies watched them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:7; Revelation 6:12; Joshua 7:19"> And at that hour a great earthquake took place, and a tenth of the city collapsed, and seven thousand ⸤people⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “names of men”">b</a></sup>were killed by the earthquake, and the rest became terrified and gave glory to the God of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Revelation 9:12; Revelation 15:1"> The second woe has passed. Behold, the third woe is coming quickly!</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 4:4; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 7:11"> And the twenty-four elders who are seated on their thrones before God fell down on their faces and worshiped God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 16:5; Revelation 4:8–9"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We give thanks to you, Lord God All-Powerful,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the one who is and the one who was,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you have taken your great power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have begun to reign.</span></span>
