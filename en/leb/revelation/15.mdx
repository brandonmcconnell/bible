## Seven Angels with Seven Last Plagues

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Revelation 15:6–7; Revelation 21:9"> And I saw another great and marvelous sign in heaven: seven angels having seven plagues that are the last ones, because with them the wrath of God is completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 12:11; Revelation 19:1–7"> And I saw something like a sea of glass mixed with fire, and those who had conquered the beast and his image and the number of his name were standing by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “on”; or “at”; or “near”">a</a></sup>the glassy sea, holding harps from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 111:2; Exodus 15:1–18; Psalm 139:14"> And they were singing the song of Moses, the slave of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Great and marvelous are your works,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Lord God All-Powerful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">righteous and true are your ways,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">King of the ages!</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “nations”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalm 86:9; Malachi 1:11; Jeremiah 10:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Who would never fear, Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and glorify your name?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For only you are holy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because all the nations</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “Gentiles”; the same Greek word can be translated “nations” or “Gentiles” depending on the context">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will come</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and worship before you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because your righteous deeds have been revealed.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Numbers 1:50; Matthew 27:51"> And after these things I looked, and the temple, the tent of the testimony in heaven, was opened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Revelation 15:1; Ezekiel 44:17–18"> and the seven angels who had the seven plagues came out from the temple, dressed in clean, bright linen garments, and girded with golden belts around their chests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 10:6; Revelation 5:8; Revelation 16:2–1"> And one of the four living creatures gave to the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the wrath of God, who lives ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “for the ages of the ages”">d</a></sup>,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:4; 1 Kings 8:10–11; Exodus 40:34–35"> and the temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God and from his power, and no one was able to enter into the temple until the seven plagues of the seven angels were completed.</span>
</div>
