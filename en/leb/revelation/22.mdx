## Paradise Restored

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; John 4:14; Psalm 36:8–9"> And he showed me the river of the water of life, clear as crystal, coming out from the throne of God and of the Lamb</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; 1 Peter 2:24; Revelation 2:7"> in the middle of its street, and ⸤on both sides of the river⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “of the river from here and from there”">a</a></sup>is the tree of life, producing twelve fruits—yielding its fruit according to every month—and the leaves of the tree are for the healing of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Revelation 21:3–4; John 12:26"> And there will not be any curse any longer, and the throne of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and his slaves will serve<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “will worship”">b</a></sup>him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 3:12"> and they will see his face, and his name will be on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4"> And night will not exist any longer, and they will not have need of the light of a lamp and the light<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “of the light”">c</a></sup>of the sun, because the Lord God will give light to them, and they will reign ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “for the ages of the ages”">d</a></sup>.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Time of Fulfillment is Near

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:1; Revelation 21:5; Revelation 19:9"> And he said to me, “These words are faithful and true, and the Lord, the God of the spirits of the prophets, has sent his angel to show his slaves the things which must take place in a short time.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:3; Revelation 3:11; Revelation 22:20"> “And behold, I am coming quickly! Blessed is the one who keeps the words of the prophecy of this book.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 1:1; Revelation 19:19"> And I, John, am the one who heard and who saw these things. And when I heard and saw them, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel who showed these things to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Colossians 2:18–19; Revelation 14:7"> And he said to me, “⸤Do not do that!⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “do not see to it”">e</a></sup>I am your fellow slave, and of your brothers the prophets, and of those who keep the words of this book. Worship God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Revelation 1:3; 1 Peter 4:7"> And he said to me, “Do not seal up the words of the prophecy of this book, for the time is near!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:10; Proverbs 4:18; Ezekiel 3:27"> The one who does evil, let him do evil still, and the defiled, let him be defiled still, and the righteous, let him practice righteousness still, and the holy, let him be holy still.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 40:10"> “Behold, I am coming quickly, and my reward is with me, to repay each one according to what his deeds are!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 44:6"> I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; 1 John 5:3; Revelation 22:7"> Blessed are the ones who wash their robes, so that their authority will be over the tree of life and they may enter into the city through the gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Galatians 5:19–21; Philippians 3:2"> Outside are the dogs and the sorcerers and the sexually immoral people and the murderers and the idolaters and everyone who loves and who practices falsehood.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; 2 Peter 1:19; Isaiah 11:1"> “I, Jesus, sent my angel to testify to you about these things for the churches. I am the root and the descendant of David, the bright morning star.”</span>
</div>
</div>
