## The Angel and the Little Scroll

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Matthew 17:2; Revelation 1:15–16"> And I saw another powerful angel descending from heaven, wrapped in a cloud, and a rainbow over his head, and his face was like the sun, and his feet were like pillars of fire,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “like fiery pillars”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:9–10; Revelation 10:5; Revelation 5:1–5"> and holding in his hand a little scroll that was opened. And he put his right foot on the sea and his left on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 31:4; Revelation 4:5; Joel 3:16"> And he cried out with a loud voice like a lion roars, and when he cried out, the seven thunders sounded<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “spoke”">b</a></sup>their own voices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:4; Daniel 8:26; Daniel 12:9"> And when the seven thunders spoke,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “sounded”">c</a></sup>I was about to write, and I heard a voice from heaven saying, “Seal up the things which the seven thunders spoke, and do not write them!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Deuteronomy 32:40; Revelation 16:17"> And the angel whom I saw standing on the sea and on the land raised his right hand to heaven</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 4:11; Revelation 4:9; Revelation 16:17"> and swore by the one who lives ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “for the ages of the ages”">d</a></sup>, who created heaven and the things in it, and the earth and the things in it, and the sea and the things in it, “There will be no more delay!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 3:3–9; Romans 16:25; Luke 24:44–47"> But in the days of the sound of the seventh angel, whenever he is about to blow the trumpet, then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “and”">e</a></sup>the mystery of God is completed, as he announced to his own slaves the prophets.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 10:2; Revelation 10:4–5; Isaiah 30:21"> And the voice that I had heard from heaven was speaking with me again and saying, “Go, take the opened scroll in the hand of the angel who is standing on the sea and on the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:1–3; Jeremiah 15:16; Ezekiel 2:8"> And I went to the angel and told him to give me the little scroll, and he said to me, “Take and eat it up, and it will make your stomach bitter, but in your mouth it will be sweet as honey.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:3; Proverbs 16:24; Psalm 119:103"> And I took the little scroll from the hand of the angel and ate it up, and it was sweet as honey in my mouth, and when I had eaten it, my stomach was made bitter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4; Jeremiah 1:9–10; Revelation 17:15"> And they said to me, “It is necessary for you to prophesy again about many peoples and nations and languages and kings.”</span>
</div>
