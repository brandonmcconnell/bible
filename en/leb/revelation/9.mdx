## Tormenting Locusts from the Abyss

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 8:10; Romans 10:7; Revelation 9:2"> And the fifth angel blew the trumpet, and I saw a star that had fallen from heaven to the earth, and the key of the shaft of the abyss was given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joel 2:10; Genesis 19:28; Joel 2:2"> And he opened the shaft of the abyss, and smoke went up from the shaft like smoke from a great furnace, and the sun and the air were darkened by the smoke from the shaft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Revelation 9:5; Joel 2:25"> And out of the smoke locusts came to the earth, and power was given to them like the scorpions of the earth have power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 7:2–4; Ezekiel 9:4; Revelation 14:1"> And it was told to them that they should not damage the grass of the earth or any green plant or any tree, except those people who do not have the seal of God on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 9:10; Job 2:6; Revelation 9:3"> And it was granted to them that they should not kill them, but that they would be tormented five months, and their torment is like the torment of a scorpion when it stings a person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 23:30; Jeremiah 8:3; Revelation 6:16"> And in those days people will seek death and will never find it, and they will long to die, and death will flee from them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; Nahum 3:17; Joel 2:4–5"> And the appearance of the locusts was like horses prepared for battle, and on their heads were something like crowns similar in appearance to gold, and their faces were like men’s faces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 1:6; 1 Corinthians 11:14–15; 1 Peter 3:3"> and they had hair like the hair of women, and their teeth were like the teeth of lions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joel 2:5–8; Nahum 2:4–5; Job 39:25"> and they had breastplates like iron breastplates, and the sound of their wings was like the sound of many ⸤horse-drawn chariots⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “chariots of horses”">a</a></sup>running into battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:5; Revelation 9:3; Revelation 9:19"> And they have tails similar in appearance to scorpions, and stings, and their power to injure people for five months is in their tails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Revelation 9:1–2; Ephesians 2:2"> They have as king over them the angel of the abyss, ⸤whose name⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the name to him”">b</a></sup>in Hebrew is Abaddon, and in Greek he has the name Apollyon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 11:14; Revelation 8:13–2; Revelation 9:13–21"> The first woe has passed. Behold, two woes are still coming after these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Third of Humanity Killed by Plagues

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Exodus 30:2–3; Revelation 9:1"> And the sixth angel blew the trumpet, and I heard one voice from the horns<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “four horns”">c</a></sup>of the golden altar that is before God</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 16:12; Revelation 9:15; 2 Samuel 8:3"> saying to the sixth angel, the one who had the trumpet, “Release the four angels who have been bound at the great river Euphrates!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 8:7; Revelation 8:9"> And the four angels who had been prepared for the hour and day and month and year were released, in order that they should kill a third of humanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Psalm 68:17; Daniel 7:10"> And the number of the troops of the cavalry was ⸤two hundred million⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “twenty thousands of ten thousands”">d</a></sup>; I heard the number of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 9:18; Revelation 14:10; 1 Chronicles 12:8"> And in this way I saw the horses in the vision, and those seated on them, who had fiery red and hyacinth-colored and sulphurous yellow breastplates, and the heads of the horses were like the heads of lions, and out of their mouths came fire and smoke and sulphur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 9:17; Revelation 9:15"> By these three plagues a third of humanity was killed—by the fire and the smoke and the sulphur that came out of their mouths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; Revelation 9:10; Ephesians 4:14"> For the power of the horses is in their mouths and in their tails, for their tails are similar in appearance to snakes, having heads, and with them they cause injury.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “they injure”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Deuteronomy 31:29; 1 Timothy 4:1"> And the rest of humanity who were not killed by these plagues did not repent of the works of their hands, ⸤in order not to worship⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “so that they did not worship”">f</a></sup>the demons and the gold and silver and bronze and stone and wooden idols, which are able neither to see nor to hear nor to walk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; 2 Corinthians 12:21; Revelation 17:5"> and they did not repent of their murders or of their magic spells or of their sexual immorality or of their thefts.</span>
</div>
</div>
