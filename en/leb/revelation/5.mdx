## The Scroll with Seven Seals and the Lamb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:9–10; Isaiah 29:11; Revelation 5:7"> And I saw in the right hand of the one who is seated on the throne a scroll, written inside and on the back, sealed up with seven seals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 29:11–12; Revelation 5:5; Revelation 10:1"> And I saw a powerful angel proclaiming with a loud voice, “Who is worthy to open the scroll and to break its seals?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:13; Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34"> And no one in heaven or on earth or under the earth was able to open the scroll or to look into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 12:8–9; Revelation 4:1"> And I began to weep loudly because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or to look into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Revelation 22:16"> And one of the elders said to me, “Do not weep! Behold, the lion of the tribe of Judah, the root of David, has conquered, so that he can open the scroll and its seven seals.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 4:10; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> And I saw in the midst of the throne and of the four living creatures and in the midst of the elders a Lamb standing as though slaughtered, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of God sent into all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 5:1; Revelation 4:2–3"> And he came and took the scroll<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A very few less important manuscripts supply the words “the scroll” here; in Greek the direct object (“the scroll”) is frequently understood anyway and must be supplied in the English translation">a</a></sup>from the right hand of the one who was seated on the throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 141:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Philippians 2:9–11"> And when he took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb, each one of whom had a harp and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; 1 John 1:7; Matthew 26:28"> And they were singing a new song, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are worthy to take the scroll</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to open its seals,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you were slaughtered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and bought people for God by your blood</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from every tribe and language and people and nation,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:6; Daniel 7:27; Revelation 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">and made them a kingdom and priests to our God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will reign on the earth.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 7:10; Hebrews 12:22; Revelation 4:4"> And I looked, and I heard the voice of many angels around the throne and of the living creatures and of the elders, and their number was ten thousand times ten thousand and thousands times thousands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 19:1; Revelation 4:11"> saying with a loud voice,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Worthy is the Lamb who was slaughtered</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to receive power and riches</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and wisdom and strength</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and honor and glory and praise!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:10; 1 Chronicles 29:11; Psalm 148:2–13"> And I heard every creature that is in heaven and on the earth and under the earth and in the sea and everything in them saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“To the one who is seated on the throne and to the Lamb</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be praise and honor and glory and power</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “sovereignty”">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">⸤forever and ever⸥</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “for the ages of the ages”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 19:4; Revelation 4:9–11; Revelation 5:8"> And the four living creatures were saying, “Amen!” and the elders fell down and worshiped.</span>
</div>
