## The Lamb and the 144,000 on Mount Zion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:32; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 7:3–17"> And I looked, and behold, the Lamb standing on Mount Zion, and with him one hundred forty-four thousand who had his name and the name of his Father written on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Ezekiel 43:2; Revelation 1:15"> And I heard a sound from heaven like the sound of many waters and like the sound of loud thunder, and the sound that I heard was like harpists playing on their harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 15:3"> And they were singing something like a new song before the throne and before the four living creatures and the elders, and no one was able to learn the song except the one hundred forty-four thousand who had been bought from the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; 2 Corinthians 11:2; James 1:18"> These are those who have not been defiled with women, for they are virgins. These are the ones who follow the Lamb wherever he goes. These were bought from humanity as first fruits to God and to the Lamb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Psalm 32:2; Zephaniah 3:13"> and in their mouth a lie was not found; they are blameless.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Three Angels and Their Messages

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:15; Revelation 8:13; Ephesians 3:9–11"> And I saw another angel flying ⸤directly overhead⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally, “in midheaven”">a</a></sup>, having an eternal gospel to proclaim to those who reside on the earth, and to every nation and tribe and language and people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Revelation 15:4; Nehemiah 9:6"> saying with a loud voice, “Fear God and give him glory, because the hour of his judgment has come, and worship the one who made the heaven and the earth and the sea and the springs of water!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 51:7–8; Revelation 18:2–3"> And another second angel followed, saying, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great, who caused all the nations to drink from the wine of the passion of her sexual immorality.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 14:11; Revelation 14:6–8; Revelation 13:3–6"> And another third angel followed them, saying with a loud voice, “If anyone worships the beast and his image and receives a mark on his forehead or on his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 16:19; Jeremiah 25:15–17"> he himself also will drink of the wine of the anger of God that has been mixed full strength in the cup of his wrath, and will be tortured with fire and sulphur in the presence of the holy angels and in the presence of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Isaiah 34:10; Matthew 25:41"> And the smoke of their torture went up ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “for the ages of the ages”">b</a></sup>, and those who worshiped the beast and his image did not have rest day and night, ⸤along with anyone who received⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “and if anyone received”">c</a></sup>the mark of his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Revelation 12:17; Revelation 3:10"> Here is the patient endurance of the saints, those who keep the commandments of God and the faith in Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 20:6; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> And I heard a voice from heaven saying, “Write:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on!’ ”</span><br />“Yes,” says the Spirit, “in order that they may rest from their labors, for their deeds follow after them.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Earth is Harvested

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Isaiah 19:1; Matthew 17:5"> And I looked, and behold, a white cloud, and on the cloud was seated one like a son of man, having on his head a golden crown, and in his hand a sharp sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:39; Mark 4:29"> And another angel came out of the temple, crying out with a loud voice to the one seated on the cloud, “Send out your sickle and reap, because the hour to reap has come, because the harvest of the earth is ripe!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Revelation 14:19; Revelation 14:14"> And the one seated on the cloud swung his sickle on the earth, and the earth was reaped.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:18; Revelation 14:14–15; Revelation 15:5–6"> And another angel came out of the temple that is in heaven; he also had a sharp sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Revelation 16:8; Revelation 14:15–16"> And another angel who had authority over the fire went out from the altar, and he called out with a loud voice to the one who had the sharp sickle, saying, “Send out your sharp sickle and harvest the clusters of grapes from the vine of the earth, because its grapes are at their prime!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Revelation 19:15–21"> And the angel swung his sickle into the earth and harvested the vine of the earth, and threw the grapes into the great winepress of the wrath of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 1:15; Revelation 19:14–21; Isaiah 63:1–3"> And the winepress was stomped outside the city, and blood went out from the winepress up to the bridles of the horses, about one thousand six hundred stadia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, about 300 km (200 mi); a stade (plural stadia ) was a measure of distance about 192 m (607 ft)">d</a></sup></span>
</div>
