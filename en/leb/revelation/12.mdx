## The Woman and Her Son Versus the Dragon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 21:25; Ephesians 5:32; Luke 21:11"> And a great sign appeared in heaven: a woman clothed with the sun and with the moon under her feet, and on her head a crown of twelve stars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 16:21; Galatians 4:19; Revelation 12:4"> and ⸤who was pregnant⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “who was having in the belly”">a</a></sup>and was crying out because she was having birth pains, and was in torment to give birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:12; Revelation 12:9; Daniel 7:24"> And another sign appeared in heaven, and behold, a great fiery red dragon, having seven heads and ten horns, and on his heads were seven royal headbands.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “diadem crowns”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 8:9–12; Revelation 12:9; John 8:44"> And his tail swept away a third of the stars from heaven and threw them to the earth. And the dragon stood before the woman who was about to give birth, in order that whenever she gave birth to her child he could devour it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:26–27; Revelation 12:2"> And she gave birth to a son, a male child, who is going to shepherd all the nations with an iron rod,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="An allusion to Ps 2:9">c</a></sup>and her child was snatched away to God and to his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:5; Revelation 11:2–3; Revelation 12:14"> And the woman fled into the wilderness, where she had a place prepared there by God, so that they could feed her there for one thousand two hundred sixty days.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Satan Thrown Down from Heaven to Earth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Ephesians 6:12"> And there was war in heaven; Michael and his angels ⸤fought against⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “made war with”">d</a></sup>the dragon, and the dragon and his angels ⸤fought back⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “made war”">e</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 37:10; Revelation 12:11; Jude 1:6"> And they did not prevail, nor was a place found for them any longer in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 10:18; John 12:31; Revelation 20:10"> And the great dragon was thrown down, the ancient serpent, who is called the devil and Satan, who deceives the whole world. He was thrown down to the earth, and his angels were thrown down with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Zechariah 3:1–2; Revelation 11:15"> And I heard a loud voice in heaven saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Now the salvation and the power</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the kingdom of our God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the authority of his Christ have come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the accuser of our brothers has been thrown down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the one who accuses them before our God day and night.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:3–5; John 16:33; 1 John 5:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they conquered him by the blood of the Lamb</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and by the word of their testimony,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they did not love their lives until death.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Revelation 8:13; Psalm 96:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of this, rejoice, you heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who live in them!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Woe to the earth and to the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because the devil has come down to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">having great anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because he knows that he has little time!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 3:15; Revelation 12:3–5; John 16:33"> And when the dragon saw that he had been thrown down to the earth, he pursued the woman who had given birth to the male child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 40:31; Revelation 12:6"> And the two wings of a great eagle were given to the woman, in order that she could fly into the wilderness, to her place where she is fed there for a time, and times, and half a time, from the presence of the serpent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Isaiah 59:19; Psalm 18:4"> And from his mouth the serpent spouted water like a river after the woman, in order that he could make her swept away by a river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 17:6; Exodus 12:35–36; 2 Kings 8:9"> And the earth came to the aid of the woman, and the earth opened its mouth and swallowed up the river that the dragon had spouted out of his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:12; Genesis 3:15; Revelation 11:7"> And the dragon was angry at the woman, and went away to ⸤fight against⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “to make war with”">f</a></sup>the rest of her children, those who keep the commandments of God and who hold to the testimony about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And he stood on the sand of the sea.</span>
</div>
