## The Seven Bowls of the Wrath of God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Jeremiah 10:25; Revelation 15:1"> And I heard a loud voice from the temple saying to the seven angels, “Go and pour out on the earth the seven bowls of the wrath of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 8:7; Revelation 16:11; Revelation 13:15–18"> And the first went and poured out his bowl on the earth, and there was an evil and painful sore on the people who had the mark of the beast and who worshiped his image.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–21; Revelation 8:8–9; Revelation 11:6"> And the second poured out his bowl on the sea, and it became blood, like that of a dead person, and every living thing that was in the sea died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 7:17–20; Revelation 8:10–11; Hosea 13:15"> And the third poured out his bowl on the rivers and the springs of water, and they became blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 11:17; Revelation 1:4"> And I heard the angel of the waters saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are righteous, the one who is and the one who was, the Holy One,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you have judged these things,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Revelation 11:18; Revelation 6:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">because they have poured out the blood of the saints and prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you have given them blood to drink;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are deserving!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Revelation 6:9; Revelation 15:3–4"> And I heard the altar saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yes, Lord God All-Powerful,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">true and righteous are your judgments.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:20; Revelation 14:18; Jonah 4:8"> And the fourth poured out his bowl on the sun, and it was granted to it to burn up people with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:21; Revelation 16:21; Revelation 11:13"> And people were burned up by the great heat, and they blasphemed the name of God who has the authority over these plagues, and they did not repent to give him glory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 9:2; Matthew 8:12; Isaiah 8:21–22"> And the fifth poured out his bowl on the throne of the beast, and his kingdom became darkened, and they began chewing their tongues because of the pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 16:9; Revelation 16:2; Revelation 16:21"> And they blasphemed the God of heaven because of their pain and because of their sores, and they did not repent of their deeds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 9:14; Jeremiah 51:36; Isaiah 44:27"> And the sixth poured out his bowl on the great river Euphrates, and its water was dried up, in order that the way would be prepared for the kings from ⸤the east⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the rising of the sun”">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Revelation 20:10"> And I saw coming out of the mouth of the dragon and out of the mouth of the beast and out of the mouth of the false prophet three unclean spirits like frogs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; 1 Timothy 4:1; Revelation 20:8"> For they are the spirits of demons performing signs that go out to the kings of the whole inhabited world, to gather them for the battle of the great day of God the All-Powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Matthew 24:42–43; 2 Peter 3:10"> (Behold, I am coming like a thief. Blessed is the one who is on the alert and who keeps his clothing, so that he does not walk around naked and they see his shamefulness!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 12:11; Judges 5:19; Joel 3:9–14"> And he gathered them to the place called in Hebrew Armageddon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 19:30; Revelation 21:6; Ephesians 6:12"> And the seventh poured out his bowl on the air, and a loud voice came out of the temple, from the throne, saying, “It is done!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Revelation 4:5; Revelation 11:19"> And there were lightnings and sounds and thunders, and there was a great earthquake, as has not happened from the time humanity has been on the earth—so great in this way was the earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 18:5; Revelation 17:18; Revelation 18:21"> And the great city was split into three parts, and the cities of the nations fell. And Babylon the great was remembered before God, to give her the cup of the wine of his furious wrath.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “of the anger of the wrath of him”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 6:14; Revelation 20:11; Jeremiah 4:23–25"> And every island fled, and mountains were not found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 11:19; Joshua 10:11; Revelation 16:9"> And large hailstones, weighing about a hundred pounds,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “weighing a talent”; a talent was 125 Roman pounds (of 12 ounces each), so this is just under 100 lbs or 43 kg">c</a></sup>came down from the sky upon people, and the people blasphemed God because of the plague of hail, because the plague of it was very great.</span>
</div>
</div>
