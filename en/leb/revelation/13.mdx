## The First Beast from the Sea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 12:3; Revelation 17:3; Daniel 7:23–25"> And I saw coming up out of the sea a beast that had ten horns and seven heads, and on its horns ten royal headbands,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “ten diadem crowns”">a</a></sup>and on its heads a blasphemous name.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts read “blasphemous names”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 7:4–7; 1 Peter 5:8; Revelation 17:12"> And the beast that I saw was similar to a leopard, and its feet were like a bear’s, and its mouth was like the mouth of a lion, and the dragon gave it his power and his throne and great authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:8; Revelation 13:14; Revelation 17:17"> And one of its heads appeared as though slaughtered to death, and its ⸤fatal wound⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “wound of death”">c</a></sup>had been healed. And the whole earth was astonished and followed after the beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 13:2; Revelation 18:18; Revelation 13:15"> And they worshiped the dragon because he had given authority to the beast, and they worshiped the beast, saying, “Who is like the beast, and who is able to make war with him?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; Daniel 7:25; Daniel 11:36"> And a mouth was given to him speaking great things and blasphemies, and authority to act was given to him for forty-two months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 12:34; Hebrews 9:24; Revelation 12:12"> And he opened his mouth for blasphemies toward God, to blaspheme his name and his dwelling, those who live in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 7:21; Revelation 11:7; Daniel 7:25"> And it was given to him to make war with the saints and to conquer them. And authority was given to him over every tribe and people and language and nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; Ephesians 1:4; Matthew 25:34"> And all those who live on the earth will worship him, ⸤everyone whose name is not written⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “of whom his name is not written”">d</a></sup>from the foundation of the world in the book of life of the Lamb who was slaughtered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 2:17; Revelation 2:11"> If anyone has an ear, let him hear!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:2; Isaiah 33:1; Revelation 14:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">If anyone is going into captivity, into captivity he goes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If anyone is to be killed by the sword, by the sword he is to be killed.</span><br />Here is the patient endurance and the faith of the saints.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Second Beast from the Earth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 7:8; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; Matthew 7:15"> And I saw another beast coming up from the earth, and he had two horns like a lamb, and he was speaking like a dragon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 17:10–11; Revelation 14:11; Revelation 14:9"> And he exercises all the authority of the first beast on behalf of him, and he causes the earth and those who live in it to worship the first beast ⸤whose fatal wound had been healed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “of whom the wound of his death had been healed”">e</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Revelation 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10"> And he performs great signs, so that he even causes fire from heaven to come down to the earth before people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 12:9; Revelation 19:20; Revelation 13:8"> And he deceives those who live on the earth because of the signs that it has been granted to him to perform on behalf of the beast, telling those who live on the earth to make an image to the beast who has the wound of the sword and yet lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 20:4; Revelation 19:20; Daniel 3:3–6"> And it was given to him to put breath into the image of the beast, in order that the image of the beast both spoke and caused that all those, unless they worshiped the image of the beast, should be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 20:4; Revelation 19:20; Ezekiel 9:4"> And he causes all people, the small and the great, and the rich and the poor, and the free and the slave, that they give them a mark on their right hand or on their forehead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 15:2; Revelation 13:16; Revelation 14:11"> and that no one was able to buy or to sell except the one who had the mark—the name of the beast or the number of his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 15:2; Revelation 17:9; Revelation 21:17"> Here is wisdom: the one who has understanding, let him calculate the number of the beast, for it is man’s number,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “it is the number of a man”">f</a></sup>and his number is six hundred sixty-six.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A few significant manuscripts read “six hundred sixteen”">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
