## Prologue

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Revelation 22:6; Galatians 1:12"> The revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave him to show to his slaves the things which must take place in a short time, and communicated it by sending it through his angel to his slave John,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:6; John 19:35; 1 John 1:1"> who testified about the word of God and the testimony of Jesus Christ, all that he saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Revelation 22:10; Romans 13:11"> Blessed is the one who reads aloud and blessed are those who hear the words of the prophecy and observe the things written in it, because the time is near!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Greetings to the Churches

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; John 1:1; Hebrews 13:8"> John, to the seven churches in Asia:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A reference to the Roman province of Asia (modern Asia Minor)">a</a></sup>grace to you and peace from the one who is and the one who was and the one who is coming, and from the seven spirits who are before his throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 89:27; Hebrews 9:14; 1 John 1:7"> and from Jesus Christ the faithful witness, the firstborn from the dead and the ruler of the kings of the earth.<br />To the one who loves us and released us from our sins by his blood</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Revelation 20:6; Exodus 19:6"> and made us a kingdom, priests to his God and Father—to him be the glory and the power ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “for the ages of the ages”">b</a></sup>. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Zechariah 12:10; Daniel 7:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, he is coming with the clouds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and every eye will see him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even every one who pierced him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the tribes of the earth will mourn over him.</span><br />Yes, amen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Isaiah 43:10"> I am the Alpha and the Omega, says the Lord God, the one who is and the one who was and the one who is coming, the All-Powerful.</span>
</div>
</div>

## John’s Vision of the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Revelation 3:10; John 16:33"> I, John, your brother and co-sharer in the affliction and kingdom and steadfastness in Jesus, was on the island called Patmos because of the word of God and the testimony about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:10; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4; Revelation 4:1–2"> I was in the Spirit on the Lord’s day, and I heard behind me a great sound like a trumpet</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:1; Jeremiah 30:2; Habakkuk 2:2"> saying, “What you see, write in a book and send it to the seven churches: to Ephesus and to Smyrna and to Pergamum and to Thyatira and to Sardis and to Philadelphia and to Laodicea.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> And I turned to see the voice which was speaking with me, and when I turned, I saw seven gold lampstands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Daniel 10:5–6; Daniel 10:16"> and in the midst of the lampstands one like a son of man, dressed in a robe reaching to the feet and girded around his chest with a golden belt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 19:12"> and his head and hair were white like wool, white as snow, and his eyes were like a fiery flame,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 14:2"> and his feet were like fine bronze when it has been fired in a furnace, and his voice was like the sound of many waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:12"> and he had in his right hand seven stars, and a sharp double-edged sword coming out of his mouth, and his face was like the sun shining in its strength.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Daniel 10:8–10"> And when I saw him, I fell at his feet like a dead person, and he placed his right hand on me, saying, “Do not be afraid! I am the first and the last,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 6:9; Hebrews 7:25; Romans 14:8–9"> and the one who lives, and I was dead, and behold, I am living ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “for the ages of the ages”">c</a></sup>, and I hold the keys of death and of Hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 4:1–11; Revelation 1:11–3"> Therefore, write the things which you saw, and the things which are, and the things which are about to take place after these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:1; Revelation 3:1; Revelation 1:16"> As for the mystery of the seven stars which you saw in my right hand and the seven gold lampstands—the seven stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches.</span>
</div>
</div>
