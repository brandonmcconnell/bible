## Instructions About Widows

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; 1 Peter 5:5–6; Titus 2:2"> Do not rebuke an older man, but appeal to him as a father, younger men as brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Matthew 12:50; Philippians 4:8"> older women as mothers, younger women as sisters, with all purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Acts 6:1; Deuteronomy 10:18"> Honor widows who are truly widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; John 19:26–27; Matthew 15:4–6"> But if any widow has children or grandchildren, they must learn to show profound respect for their own household first, and to pay back recompense to their parents, for this is pleasing in the sight of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3; Luke 2:37; 1 Corinthians 7:32"> But the widow who is one truly, and is left alone, has put her hope in God and continues in her petitions and prayers night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 5:5; Revelation 18:7; Luke 15:24"> But the one who lives for sensual pleasure is dead even though she lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:11; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:1"> And command these things, in order that they may be irreproachable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Luke 11:11–13"> But if someone does not provide for his own relatives, and especially the members of his household, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 5:11; Luke 2:36–37"> Let a widow be put on the list if she is not less than sixty years old, the wife of one husband,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; Titus 3:8; Acts 9:36"> being well-attested by good works, if she has brought up children, if she has shown hospitality, if she has washed the feet of the saints, if she has helped those who are oppressed, if she has devoted herself to every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; Hosea 13:6; James 5:5"> But refuse younger widows, for whenever their physical desires lead them away from Christ, they want to marry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 11:34"> thus incurring condemnation because they have broken their former pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6–11; Acts 20:30"> And at the same time also, going around from house to house, they learn to be idle, and not only idle, but also gossipy and busybodies, saying the things that are not necessary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 6:1; Titus 2:8"> Therefore I want younger widows to marry, to bear children, to manage a household, to give the adversary no opportunity for reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:2; Jude 1:4–5; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> For already some have turned away and followed after Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3–5; 1 Timothy 5:8"> If any believing woman has widows, she must help them, and the church must not be burdened, in order that it may help those who are truly widows.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Honoring Worthy Elders and Dealing With Sinners

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Galatians 6:6"> The elders who lead well must be considered worthy of double honor, especially those who labor by speaking and teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Corinthians 9:14; 1 Corinthians 9:7–10"> For the scripture says, “You must not muzzle an ox while it is threshing,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A quotation from Deut 25:4">a</a></sup>and “The worker is worthy of his wages.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quotation from Luke 10:7">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> Do not accept an accusation against an elder except on the evidence of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 1:13; 1 Timothy 1:20"> Reprove those who sin in the presence of all, in order that the rest also may experience fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Timothy 6:13"> I testify solemnly before God and Christ Jesus and the elect angels that you observe these things without prejudice, doing nothing according to partiality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 3:10; 1 Timothy 3:6"> Lay hands on no one hastily, and do not participate in the sins of others. Keep yourself pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 104:15; Proverbs 31:4–7; 1 Timothy 4:4"> (No longer drink only water, but use a little wine for your stomach and your frequent illnesses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Acts 8:18; Galatians 5:19–21"> The sins of some people are evident, preceding them to judgment, but for some also they follow after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Luke 11:33; Psalm 37:5–6"> Likewise also good works are evident, and those considered otherwise are not able to be hidden.</span>
</div>
</div>
