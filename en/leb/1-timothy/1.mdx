## Greeting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:3; Colossians 1:27; Acts 9:15"> Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus according to the command of God our Savior and of Christ Jesus our hope,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:2; 1 Corinthians 4:14–17"> to Timothy, my true child in the faith. Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for Timothy in Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:3; Galatians 1:6–7; 2 John 1:9–10"> Just as I urged you when I traveled to Macedonia, remain in Ephesus, so that you may instruct certain people not to teach other doctrine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:7; Titus 1:14; Titus 3:9"> and not to pay attention to myths and endless genealogies, which cause useless speculations rather than God’s plan that is by faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; Galatians 5:22; 2 Timothy 2:22"> But the goal of our instruction is love from a pure heart and a good conscience and a faith without hypocrisy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 1:10; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Titus 3:9"> from which some have deviated, and have turned away into fruitless discussion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 1:22; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Peter 2:12"> wanting to be teachers of the law, although they do not understand either the things which they are saying or the things concerning which they are speaking confidently.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:12–13; Psalm 19:7–10; Romans 7:16"> But we know that the law is good, if anyone makes use of it lawfully,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; 1 Peter 4:18; Romans 5:20"> knowing this, that the law is not given for a righteous person but for the lawless and rebellious, for the ungodly and sinners, for the unholy and totally worldly, for the one who kills his father and the one who kills his mother, for murderers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Leviticus 18:22; 2 Timothy 4:3"> sexually immoral people, homosexuals, kidnappers, liars, perjurers, and ⸤whatever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “if anything”">a</a></sup>else is opposed to sound teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 2:7; Titus 1:3; 1 Timothy 6:15"> according to the glorious gospel of the blessed God that I was entrusted with.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Thankfulness for the Mercy Shown to Him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Timothy 4:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> ⸤I give thanks⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “I have thankfulness”">b</a></sup>to the one who strengthens me, Christ Jesus our Lord, because he considered me faithful, placing me into ministry,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “service”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Peter 2:10; Hebrews 6:4–8"> although I was formerly a blasphemer and a persecutor and a violent man, but I was shown mercy because I acted ignorantly in unbelief,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 1:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> and the grace of our Lord abounded with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; John 3:16–17"> The saying is trustworthy and worthy of all acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the foremost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13; Ephesians 2:7; Luke 7:47"> But because of this I was shown mercy, in order that in me foremost, Christ Jesus might demonstrate his total patience, for an example for those who are going to believe in him for eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Jude 1:25; 1 Timothy 6:15–16"> Now to the King of the ages, immortal, invisible, to the only God, be honor and glory ⸤forever and ever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “to the ages of the ages”">d</a></sup>. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Charge to Timothy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 6:20; 2 Timothy 4:1–3"> I am setting before you this instruction, Timothy my child, in accordance with the prophecies spoken long ago about you, in order that by them you may fight the good fight,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:9; 1 Peter 3:15–16; Revelation 3:10"> having faith and a good conscience, which some, because they have rejected these, have suffered shipwreck concerning their faith,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 2 Timothy 2:17; 1 Corinthians 11:32"> among whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan, in order that they may be taught not to blaspheme.</span>
</div>
</div>
