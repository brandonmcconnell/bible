## The Coming Apostasy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:1–9; 2 Peter 2:1; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> Now the Spirit explicitly says that in the last times some will depart from the faith, paying attention to deceitful spirits and teachings of demons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:19; Acts 20:30; Romans 16:18"> by the hypocrisy of liars, who are seared in their own conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:6"> who forbid marrying and insist on abstaining from foods that God created for sharing in with thankfulness by those who believe and who know the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:25; Romans 14:14"> because everything created by God is good and nothing is to be rejected if it is received with thankfulness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:3; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Luke 4:4"> for it is made holy by the word of God and prayer.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Good Servant of Christ

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:7–10; Colossians 3:16; Matthew 13:52"> By teaching these things to the brothers, you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus, trained in the words of the faith and of the good teaching that you have followed faithfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16; Titus 2:12; 1 Timothy 1:4"> But reject those ⸤worthless myths told by elderly women⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “worthless and characteristic of an elderly woman myths”">a</a></sup>, and train yourself for godliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; 1 Timothy 6:6; Psalm 37:3–4"> For the training of the body is ⸤somewhat⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “for a little”">b</a></sup>profitable, but godliness is profitable for everything, because it holds promise for the present life and for the life to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15"> The statement is trustworthy and deserving of complete acceptance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 2:2; 1 Timothy 2:6"> For to this end we labor and suffer reproach,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and strive”">c</a></sup>because we have put our hope in the living God, who is the Savior of all people, especially of believers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:2; 2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 2:15"> Command these things and teach them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> Let no one look down on your youth, but be an example for the believers in word, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:2"> Until I come, pay attention to the public reading,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Many English translations supply “of scripture” here to clarify what is to be read aloud">d</a></sup>to exhortation, to teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 1 Peter 4:9–11; 1 Timothy 1:18"> Do not neglect the gift that is in you, that was granted to you through prophecy with the laying on of hands by the council of elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; Philippians 2:15–16; Psalm 143:5"> Practice these things. Be diligent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “be in these things ,” though most English versions supply a predicate here">e</a></sup>in these things, in order that your progress may be evident to everyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7; 2 John 1:8–9"> Fix your attention on yourself and on your teaching. Continue in them, for by doing this you will save both yourself and those who hear you.</span>
</div>
</div>
