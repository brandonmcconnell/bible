## Qualifications for Overseers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2"> The saying is trustworthy: if anyone aspires to supervision, he desires a good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:6–9; 2 Timothy 2:24; Romans 12:13"> Therefore the overseer must be irreproachable, the husband of one wife, temperate, self-controlled, respectable, hospitable, skillful in teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Ephesians 5:18; Titus 3:2"> not addicted to wine, not a violent person, but gentle, peaceable, not loving money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:6; Genesis 18:19; 1 Timothy 3:12"> managing his own household well, having children in submission with all dignity</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:13; Ephesians 5:24; Acts 20:28"> (but if someone does not know how to manage his own household, how will he take care of the church of God?),</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:4; Hebrews 5:12–13; 1 Peter 2:2"> not newly converted, lest he become conceited and fall into the condemnation of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Timothy 2:26; Colossians 4:5"> But he must also have a good testimony from those outside, in order that he may not fall into disgrace and the trap of the devil.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Qualifications for Deacons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:1; 1 Timothy 5:23; Acts 6:3–6"> Deacons likewise must be dignified, not insincere, not devoted to much wine, not fond of dishonest gain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; 2 John 1:9–10; 1 Timothy 1:5"> holding the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; 1 Timothy 3:13; Titus 1:6–7"> and these also must be tested first; then let them serve if they are above reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 2:3; Titus 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:2"> The wives<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “The women”">a</a></sup>likewise must be dignified, not slanderous, temperate, faithful in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:4–5; Philippians 1:1"> Deacons must be husbands of one wife, managing their children and their own households well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 21:35"> For those who have served well acquire a good standing for themselves, and great boldness in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Mystery of Godliness Described

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:34; Philemon 1:22; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> I am writing these things to you, hoping to come to you in a short time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 2:5; Ephesians 2:21–22; 1 Thessalonians 1:9"> But if I am delayed, I am writing in order that you may know how one must conduct oneself in the household of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and mainstay<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “basis”; or “support”">b</a></sup>of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 1:14; Hebrews 1:3; Mark 16:19"> And most certainly, great is the mystery of godliness:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who was revealed in the flesh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">was vindicated by</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or perhaps “in”">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the Spirit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was seen by angels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">was proclaimed among the Gentiles</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “nations”; the same Greek word can be translated “nations” or “Gentiles” depending on the context">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">was believed on in the world,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">was taken up in glory.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
