## Instructions to Pray for All People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:3; Matthew 6:9–10; James 5:16"> Therefore, I urge first of all that petitions, prayers, requests, and thanksgiving be made on behalf of all people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–13; Jeremiah 29:7; Ezra 6:10"> on behalf of kings and all those who are in authority, in order that we may live a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and dignity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 1 Timothy 1:1; 1 Peter 2:20"> This is good and acceptable before God our Savior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:32; 2 Peter 3:9; Ezekiel 18:23"> who wants all people to be saved and to come to a knowledge of the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:6; Galatians 3:20; Hebrews 9:15"> For there is one God and one mediator between God and human beings, the man Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; 1 Peter 3:18; 1 John 2:1–2"> who gave himself a ransom for all, the testimony at the proper time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 2 Timothy 1:11; Romans 9:1"> for which I was appointed a herald and an apostle—I am speaking the truth, I am not lying—a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalm 134:2; Psalm 63:4; Hebrews 10:22"> Therefore I want the men in every place to pray, lifting up holy hands without anger and dispute.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for Women

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Titus 2:3–5; Psalm 149:4"> Likewise also the women should adorn themselves in appropriate clothing, with modesty and self-control, not with braided hair and gold jewelry or pearls or expensive clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 3:3–5; Revelation 2:19"> but with good deeds which are fitting for women who profess godliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34–35; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> A woman must learn in quietness with all submission.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34"> But I do not permit a woman to teach or to exercise authority over a man, but ⸤to remain quiet⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to be in quietness”">a</a></sup>.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; Genesis 1:27"> For Adam was formed first, then Eve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 3:6; Genesis 3:12–13"> and Adam was not deceived, but the woman, because she was deceived, came into transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:12; Genesis 3:15–16; 1 Peter 4:7"> But she will be saved through the bearing of children, if she continues in faith and love and holiness with self-control.</span>
</div>
</div>
