<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:3; 2 Samuel 5:11–16; Ezra 3:7"> And Hiram, king of Tyre, sent messengers to David and cedar trees, ⸤masons⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “craftsmen of the wall”">a</a></sup>and ⸤carpenters⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “craftsmen of wood”">b</a></sup>to build a house for him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; 2 Samuel 5:13; Ecclesiastes 7:26–29"> And David took more wives in Jerusalem, and David fathered more sons and daughters.</span>

## David Defeats the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; Revelation 11:15–18; Psalm 2:1–6"> When the Philistines heard that David had been anointed as king over all Israel, all the Philistines went up to search for David. But David heard and went out from before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:15; 1 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Samuel 23:13"> Now the Philistines came and carried out a raid in the Valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:6; 1 Chronicles 14:14; 1 Chronicles 13:3"> Then David inquired of God, saying, “Shall I go up against the Philistines? Will you put them into my hand?” And Yahweh said to him, “Go up. I will put them into your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:21; Exodus 14:28; 2 Samuel 5:20"> And he went up to Baal-Perazim, and David struck them there. And David said, “God has burst out against my enemies by my hand like waters burst out.” Therefore they will call the name of this place Baal-Perazim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; 1 Samuel 5:2–6; 2 Kings 19:18"> And they abandoned their gods there. And David commanded, and they were burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 1 Kings 20:22; 2 Samuel 5:22–25"> Then the Philistines ⸤carried out another raid⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “they added again and made a raid”">c</a></sup>in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 14:10; Psalm 27:4"> Then David inquired again of God. And God said to him, “You shall not go up after them. Go around from before them and come against them the opposite way of the balsam trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:4; Acts 2:2; Philippians 2:12–13"> And it shall be that when you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, then go out into battle, for God goes out before you to strike the camp of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:14; John 13:17; Genesis 6:22"> And David did as God commanded him, and they struck the camp of the Philistines from Gibeon to Gezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 6:27; Exodus 15:14–16"> And the fame of David went out through all the lands. And Yahweh put the fear of him upon all the nations.</span>
