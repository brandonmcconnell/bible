## David’s Wives and Children

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 1 Chronicles 17:1–5; Psalm 132:5"> And David built houses for himself in the city of David, and he established a place for the ark of God. And he pitched a tent for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Deuteronomy 31:9; Numbers 18:1–8"> Then David ordered that none except the Levites should carry the ark of God, for Yahweh had chosen them to carry the ark of Yahweh and to serve him forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:5; 1 Chronicles 15:1"> And David gathered all Israel to Jerusalem to bring up the ark of Yahweh to its place that he had established for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:16–22; 1 Chronicles 12:26–28; 1 Chronicles 6:16–30"> And David gathered the sons of Aaron and the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–24"> Of the sons of Kohath: Uriel the chief and one hundred and twenty of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:29–30"> Of the sons of Merari: Asaiah the chief and two hundred and twenty of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:11; 1 Chronicles 23:8"> Of the sons of Gershom: Joel the chief and one hundred and thirty of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 6:22"> Of the sons of Elizaphan: Shemaiah the chief and two hundred of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:30–31"> Of the sons of Hebron: Eliel the chief and eighty of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> Of the sons of Uzziel: Amminadab the chief and one hundred and twelve of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Kings 2:35; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> Then David summoned Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:6; Exodus 19:14–15; 1 Chronicles 15:14"> And he said to them, “You are the heads of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">a</a></sup>for the Levites. Sanctify yourselves and your brothers and bring up the ark of Yahweh, the God of Israel, to the place I have established for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:3; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 John 1:8–10"> Because the first time you did not, and Yahweh our God burst out against us, for we did not care for it according to the law.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:15; Joel 2:16–17; 2 Chronicles 29:34"> Then the priests and the Levites sanctified themselves to bring up the ark of Yahweh, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 7:9; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> And the sons of the Levites carried the ark of God according to what Moses commanded, according to the word of Yahweh, on their shoulders with the carrying poles upon them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 16:42"> Then David ordered the chiefs of the Levites to appoint their brothers as singers with instruments of song, musical instruments, stringed instruments, and cymbals, ⸤raising their voices for joy⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “causing to hear to the mountains with a sound of rejoicing”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 6:44; 1 Chronicles 6:33"> So the Levites appointed Heman son of Joel, and from his brothers, Asaph the son of Berekiah, and from the sons of Merari, their brothers, Ethan the son of Kushaiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 26:8; 1 Chronicles 13:14"> and with them their brothers of the second rank, Zechariah the son, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, and the gatekeepers Obed-Edom and Jeiel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:6; 1 Chronicles 16:5; 1 Chronicles 13:8"> Now the singers, Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, were to sound bronze cymbals;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 46:1–11"> Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah were to play on harps according to Alamoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; Psalm 81:1–2"> and Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah were to play lyres, directing according to the Sheminith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 1 Chronicles 25:7–8"> And Kenaniah, chief of the Levites in singing, was to be an instructor in singing because he understood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 84:10; 2 Kings 22:4; 1 Chronicles 9:21–23"> And Berekiah and Elkanah were gatekeepers for the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:6; Numbers 10:8; 1 Chronicles 15:28"> And Shebaniah, Jehoshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Elizezer the priests ⸤sounded the trumpets⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “trumpet blowers with trumpets”">c</a></sup>before the ark of God. And Obed-Edom and Jehiah were gatekeepers for the ark.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12–23; Micah 5:2; Philippians 3:3"> And it happened that David and the elders of Israel and the commanders of thousands went to bring up the ark of the covenant of Yahweh from the house of Obed-Edom with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; 1 Chronicles 29:14; Psalm 66:13–15"> And it happened that when God helped the Levites carrying the ark of the covenant of Yahweh, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 6:14"> Now David was clothed with a robe of fine linen; so also all the Levites who were carrying the ark, the singers, and Kenaniah the chief of the singing. And David wore a linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> And all Israel brought up the ark of the covenant of Yahweh with shouting, with the sound of a shofar,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “horn, trumpet”">d</a></sup>and with trumpets and cymbals, making loud music with musical instruments and stringed instruments.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20–23; 2 Samuel 6:16; Deuteronomy 31:26"> And when the ark of the covenant of Yahweh came into the city of David, then Michal the daughter of Saul looked down from the window and saw the king, David, leaping and dancing. And she despised him in her heart.</span>
</div>
