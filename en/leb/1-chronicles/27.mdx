## Military Divisions

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 4:27; 2 Chronicles 26:11–13; Deuteronomy 1:15"> And as for the number of the ⸤Israelites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>the heads of ⸤families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">b</a></sup>the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and their officials who serve the king in all matters of the working groups that came and went, month by month, throughout all the months of the year, each working group: twenty-four thousand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:11; 2 Samuel 23:8"> Over the first working group for the first month: Jashobeam the son of Zabdiel. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 26:20; Numbers 10:14; Genesis 49:8–10"> From the sons of Perez who was the chief of the commanders of the armies for the first month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:9; 1 Chronicles 11:12"> And over the working groups of the second month: Dodai the Ahohite and his working group, and Mikloth the commander. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:17; 1 Kings 4:4–5"> The third commander of the army for the third month was Benaiah son of Jehoiada the chief priest. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:20–23; 1 Chronicles 11:22–25; 2 Samuel 22:20–23"> This was Benaiah who was a mighty warrior of thirty, and over the thirty and his working group was Ammizabad his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:26; 2 Samuel 23:24; 2 Samuel 2:18–23"> The fourth, for the fourth month, was Asahel the brother of Joab and his son Zebadiah after him. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:25; 1 Chronicles 26:29"> The fifth, for the fifth month, the commander was Shamhuth the Izrahite. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 23:26"> The sixth, for the sixth month, was Ira the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:26"> The seventh, for the seventh month, was Helez the Pelonite, from the sons of Ephraim. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:29; Numbers 26:20"> The eighth, for the eighth month, was Sibbecai the Hushathite of the Zerahites. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 23:27"> The ninth, for the ninth month, was Abiezer the Anathothite of the Benjamites. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:30; 2 Samuel 23:28"> The tenth, for the tenth month, was Maharai the Netophathite of the Zerahites. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:31; 2 Samuel 23:30"> The eleventh, for the eleventh month, was Benaiah the Pirathonite from the sons of Ephraim. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 23:29; 1 Chronicles 4:13; Judges 3:9"> The twelfth, for the twelfth month, was Heldai the Netophathite, of Othniel. And he was over his working group of twenty-four thousand.</span>
</div>

## Leaders of the Tribes and Other High Ranking Officials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And over the tribes of Israel, for the Reubenites: the commander was Eliezer the son of Zikri. For the Simeonites: Shephatiah the son of Maacah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:30; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 12:27–28"> For Levi: Hashabiah the son of Kemuel. For Aaron, Zadok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 17:29; 1 Samuel 17:13"> For Judah: Elihu, from the brothers of David. For Issachar: Omri the son of Michael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For Zebulun: Ishmaiah the son of Obadiah. For Naphtali: Jerimoth the son of Azriel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For the Ephraimites: Hoshea the son of Azaziah. For the half-tribe of Manasseh: Joel the son of Pedaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 1 Samuel 14:50–51; 2 Samuel 3:37"> For the half-tribe of Manasseh in Gilead: Iddo the son of Zechariah. For Benjamin: Jaasiel son of Abner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:1"> For Dan: Azarel the son of Jeroham. These were the commanders of the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; 1 Chronicles 21:2–5; Hebrews 11:12"> And David did not ⸤take a census of those twenty years old or under⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “take up their count from a son of twenty years and to less”">c</a></sup>because Yahweh promised<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “said”">d</a></sup>to make Israel more numerous than the stars of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:1–17; 2 Samuel 24:1–15"> Joab the son of Zeruiah began to count, but he did not finish. But wrath came upon Israel concerning this, and the number was not included in ⸤the chronicles⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in the numbering of the words of the days”">e</a></sup>of King David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:2; Exodus 1:11; 2 Chronicles 26:10"> And over the treasury rooms of the king was Azmaveth the son of Adiel. And over the treasuries in the country, in the cities, in the unwalled villages, and in the towers was Jonathan the son of Uzziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And over those who did the work in the field to till the soil was Ezri the son of Kelub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And over the vineyards was Shemei the Ramathite. And over the produce in the vineyards for the storehouses of the wine was Zabdi the Shiphmite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 10:27; 2 Chronicles 1:15; 1 Kings 4:7"> And over the olive trees and the sycamore trees that were in the Shephelah was Baal-Hanan the Gederite. And over the treasury houses of olive oil was Joash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:16; Isaiah 65:10"> And over the cattle pastured in the Sharon was Shitrai the Sharonite. And over the cattle in the valley was Shaphat the son of Adlai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 47:6; Job 1:3"> And over the camels was Obil the Ishmaelite. And over the female donkeys was Jehdeiah the Meronothite.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="1 Chronicles 27:31 begins here in the Hebrew Bible">f</a></sup>And over the sheep was Jaziz the Hagrite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:10"> All of these were officials of the property of King David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:21; 2 Samuel 13:3; 1 Chronicles 11:11"> And Jehonathan the uncle of David was an adviser, a man of insight, and he was a scribe. And Jehiel the son of Hachmoni was with the sons of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 15:32"> And Ahithophel was an adviser to the king, and Hushai the Arkite was a friend of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 1:7; 1 Chronicles 27:5"> And after Ahithophel came Jehoiada, the son of Benaiah, and Abiathar. And Joab was the commander of the king’s army.</span>
</div>
