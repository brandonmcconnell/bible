## David’s Charge to Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:2; 1 Chronicles 11:10–47; 1 Chronicles 27:1–34"> Then David assembled all the commanders of Israel, the commanders of the tribes and the commanders of working groups who served the king, and the commanders of thousands, the commanders of hundreds, and the commanders over all the property of the king and his sons, with the court officials and mighty warriors, and all the mighty warriors of ability, at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalm 99:5; Isaiah 66:1; 1 Chronicles 17:1–2"> And King David rose to his feet and said, “Listen to me, my brothers and my people. ⸤I myself wanted⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “I myself with my heart”">a</a></sup>to build a house of rest for the ark of the covenant of Yahweh and the footstool of our God, and I prepared to build.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:8; 1 Chronicles 17:4; 2 Chronicles 6:8–9"> But God said to me, ‘You may not build a house for my name because you are a man of war, and you have shed blood.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Chronicles 5:2; 1 Samuel 16:6–13"> But Yahweh the God of Israel chose me from all of the house of my father to be king over Israel forever, for he chose Judah as leader, and in the house of Judah, the house of my father, and among the sons of my father he took pleasure in me to reign over all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:1–9; 1 Chronicles 23:1; 1 Chronicles 22:9–10"> And from all my sons—for Yahweh has given many sons to me—he has chosen Solomon my son to sit on the throne of the kingdom of Yahweh over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:9–10; Hebrews 3:6"> And he said to me, ‘Solomon your son, he himself shall build my house and my courts, for I have chosen him as a son to myself, and I myself will be as a father to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:13; 1 Kings 8:61; 1 Kings 6:12–13"> and I will establish his kingdom forever if ⸤he firmly performs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “if he is strong to do”">b</a></sup>my commandments and my judgments as he has to this day.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 5:32–3; Proverbs 2:1–5"> So now in the sight of all Israel, the assembly of Yahweh, and in the hearing of our God, observe and seek all the commandments of Yahweh your God so that you may take possession of this good land and bestow it as an inheritance to your children after you forever.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## David’s Charge to Solomon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:13; James 4:8–11; Matthew 7:7–8"> “And you, Solomon my son, know the God of your father and serve him with a whole heart and a willing mind, for Yahweh searches all hearts and understands every plan and all thoughts. If you seek him, he will be found by you, but if you abandon him, he will reject you forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:6; 1 Timothy 4:16; 1 Chronicles 22:16–19"> Look now, for Yahweh has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary; be strong and do it!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:19; 1 Kings 6:3; Exodus 25:40"> Then David gave to Solomon his son the plan of the vestibule and of its houses, its treasuries, its upper rooms, its inner chambers, and of the house of the lid of the ark,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:20; 1 Kings 14:26; Exodus 25:40"> and the plan of all ⸤that he had in mind⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “that was in the spirit with him”">c</a></sup>for the courtyards of the house of Yahweh and for all the surrounding storage rooms, the treasuries of the house of God, and the treasuries for sanctified objects,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:29; 1 Chronicles 25:1–31; 1 Kings 7:1–51"> and for the working groups of the priests and the Levites, and for all the work of the service of the house of Yahweh, and for all the vessels for the service of the house of Yahweh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and for the gold, according to the weight of the gold for all the vessels of the service, and the service of all the vessels of silver according to the weight of all the vessels ⸤for every kind of service⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “service and service”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Zechariah 4:11–14; Revelation 1:12–13"> And the weight of the golden lampstands and the golden lamps, ⸤the weight of each lampstand and each lamp⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “by weight of lampstand and lampstand, with its lamp and to the lamps”">e</a></sup>and the silver according to the weight of a lampstand and its lamp, according to the use of ⸤each lampstand⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “lampstand and lampstand”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:48; 2 Chronicles 4:8; Exodus 25:23–30"> and the weight of gold for the tables of the arranged bread, ⸤each table⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “to table and table”">g</a></sup>and silver for the tables of silver,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 10:21; 2 Chronicles 4:20–22; Numbers 7:13–14"> and pure gold for the three-pronged meat forks, and the bowls and the pitchers, and for the golden bowls, by weight for ⸤each bowl⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “for bowl and bowl”">h</a></sup>and for the silver bowls, by weight for ⸤each bowl⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for bowl and bowl”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 25:18–22; Exodus 30:1–10; Psalm 80:1"> and for the altar of incense made of refined gold, by weight, and gold for the plan for the chariot—the cherubim with outspread and covering wings over the ark of the covenant of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11–12; Exodus 25:40; Ezekiel 3:14"> “All this I give you in writing; from the hand of Yahweh he instructed me about all the workings of this plan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:13; Joshua 1:5–9; Isaiah 41:10"> Then David said to Solomon his son, “Be strong and courageous, and do this! Do not be afraid and do not be dismayed, for Yahweh God, my God, is with you. He will not fail you and will not forsake you until all the work of the service of the house of Yahweh is finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:1–26; 1 Chronicles 22:17–18; Exodus 31:3"> And behold, the working groups of the priests and the Levites for all the service of the house of God; and with you in all the work for all those willing with the skill for every service, and the commanders and all the people for all your commands.”</span>
</div>
</div>
