## The Divisions of the Gatekeepers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 23:19; Numbers 26:9–11; Psalm 44:1"> As for the working groups of the gatekeepers: of the Korahites, Meshelemiah the son of Kore, from the sons of Asaph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:21"> And Meshelemiah had sons: Zechariah the firstborn, Jediael the second, Zebadiah the third, Jathniel the fourth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Elam the fifth, Jehohana the sixth, Eliehoenai the seventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 15:24; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> And Obed-Edom had sons: Shemaiah the firstborn, Jehozabad the second, Joah the third, Sachar the fourth, Nethanel the fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalm 128:1"> Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Peullethai the eighth; for God blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Timothy 6:12; Judges 6:12"> And to Shemaiah his son were sons born who were rulers in the house of their father, for they were mighty warriors of ability.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The sons of Shemaiah: Othni, Rephael, Obed, and Elzabad, whose brothers were sons of ability, Elihu and Semakiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:6; 1 Peter 4:11; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11"> All these from the sons of Obed-Edom, they and their sons, and their brothers were men of ability with strength for service; sixty-two of Obed-Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:14; 1 Chronicles 26:1"> And Meshelemiah had sons and brothers, sons of ability, eighteen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:38; 1 Chronicles 5:1–2"> And Hosah, from the sons of Merari, had sons: Shimri the chief (though he was not firstborn, his father appointed him as chief),</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Hilkiah the second, Tabaliah the third, Zechariah the fourth: all the sons and brothers of Hosah were thirteen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8"> These working groups of gatekeepers, corresponding to the chief men, had responsibilities like their brothers to serve in the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:8; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Chronicles 24:5"> And they cast lots, small and great alike, by their fathers’ houses, ⸤for their gates⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “for gate and gate”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the lot fell to the east to Shelemiah. As for Zechariah his son, ⸤a wise counselor⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “giving counsel with understanding”">b</a></sup>they cast lots. And his lot came out for the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 25:24; 1 Chronicles 26:17"> To Obed-Edom went out the south, and to his sons, the storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 1 Chronicles 26:10–12; 1 Kings 10:5"> For Shuppim and Hosah it went out for the west, at the gate of Shalleketh, on the road that goes up, guard for guard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 9:24"> To the east, six Levites; to the north, four daily; to the south, four daily; and at the storerooms, two by two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:11"> For the court to the west, four at the road, two at the court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 16:11"> These were the working groups of the gatekeepers among the sons of the Korahites and among the sons of Merari.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Treasurers and Other Officials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:22; 1 Chronicles 28:12–19; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> And of the Levites: Ahijah was over the treasuries of the house of God and over the treasuries of the sanctified objects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:7; 1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 29:8"> The sons of Ladan: the sons of the Gershonites belonging to Ladan, the heads of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">c</a></sup>belonging to Ladan the Gershonite: Jehieli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; Nehemiah 10:38; 1 Chronicles 26:20"> The sons of Jehieli, Zetham and Joel his brother, were over the treasuries of the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 3:27; 1 Chronicles 23:12; Numbers 3:19"> Of the Amramites, the Izharites, the Hebronites, and the Ozzielites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:20; 1 Chronicles 23:15–16"> Shebuel the son of Gershom, the son of Moses, was commander over the treasuries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17–18; 1 Chronicles 23:15; Exodus 18:4"> And his brothers: from Eliezer were Rehabiah his son, and Jeshaiah his son, and Joram his son, and Zikri his son, and Shelomoth his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:11; 1 Chronicles 29:2–9; 1 Chronicles 22:14"> That Shelomoth and his brothers were over all the treasuries of the sanctified objects that King David and the heads of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">d</a></sup>to the commanders of thousands and hundreds and the commanders of the army had dedicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 12:14; Joshua 6:19; Nehemiah 10:32–34"> From the battles and from the spoil they had dedicated these things to strengthen the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 17:55; 2 Samuel 10:9–14"> And all that Samuel the seer, and Saul the son of Kish, and Abner the son of Ner, and Joab the son of Zeruiah had dedicated—all the dedicated gifts were under the hand of Shelomith and his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:4; Nehemiah 11:16; 2 Chronicles 34:13"> For the Izharites: Kenaniah and his sons were appointed to the duties outside of Israel as officials and as judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:17; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> For the Hebronites: Hashabiah and his brothers, one thousand seven hundred men of ability, were over the administration of Israel beyond the Jordan westward, for all the work of Yahweh and for the service of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; Joshua 21:39; 1 Chronicles 29:27"> For the Hebronites: Jeriah the chief of the Hebronites for the genealogy for the ⸤families⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">e</a></sup>(In the fortieth year of the reign of David they were examined, and there was found among them mighty warriors of strength at Jazer in Gilead.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:11; 1 Chronicles 12:37; 1 Chronicles 23:24"> And his brothers, sons of ability, were two thousand seven hundred heads of the ⸤families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">f</a></sup>and King David appointed them over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, for every matter of God and matter of the king.</span>
</div>
