## The Death of Saul and His Sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1–13; 1 Chronicles 10:8; 2 Samuel 1:6"> Now the Philistines fought against Israel, and every Israelite fled away from before the Philistines. And they fell slain upon Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:39; Exodus 20:5; 1 Samuel 14:6"> And the Philistines ⸤overtook⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “caught up behind”">a</a></sup>Saul and his sons. And the Philistines killed Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malki-Shua, the sons of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 49:23–24; 1 Samuel 31:3–6; 2 Samuel 1:4–10"> And the battle was heavy against Saul, and the ⸤archers⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “shooters with the bow”">b</a></sup>found him, and he was wounded by the ⸤archers⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “shooters”">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:4; Judges 9:54; 1 Samuel 17:26"> And Saul said to his armor-bearer, “Draw your sword and thrust me through with it, lest these uncircumcised come and deal harshly with me.” But he was very afraid. Then Saul took the sword and fell upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he also fell upon the sword, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 4:10–11; 1 Samuel 4:18"> So Saul died. And his three sons and all his house died together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:2"> And when all the men of Israel who were in the valley saw that they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="That is, the army">d</a></sup>had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned their cities and fled. Then the Philistines came and dwelled in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:8; 2 Kings 3:23; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> And it happened that on the next day when the Philistines came to strip the dead, they found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Judges 16:23–24; Matthew 14:11"> And they stripped him and took his head and his weapons, and they sent them throughout the land of the Philistines to bring news to their idols and the nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–7; 1 Samuel 31:10"> And they put his weapons in the temple of their gods, and they fastened his skull to the temple of Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–11; 2 Samuel 2:4–7"> When all Jabesh-Gilead heard all that the Philistines had done to Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; 2 Samuel 3:35; Genesis 50:10"> every strong man arose and took the dead body of Saul and the dead bodies of his sons and brought them to Jabesh. And they buried their bones under the oak in Jabesh and fasted seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 15:23"> So Saul died on account of his sin which he had sinned against Yahweh concerning the command of Yahweh that he did not keep. He also consulted a medium to seek guidance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 28:17"> But he did not seek Yahweh. So Yahweh put him to death and turned over the kingship to David, the son of Jesse.</span>
