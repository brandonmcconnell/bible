## David Organizes the Musicians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Chronicles 6:33; 1 Chronicles 6:39"> And David and the commanders of the army set apart for the service the sons of Asaph, of Heman, and of Jeduthun, who prophesied with stringed instruments, with harps, and with cymbals. And their inventory of the men of the work and for their duty was:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:6; 1 Chronicles 16:5; 1 Chronicles 25:1"> for the sons of Asaph: Zakkur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asarelah, sons of Asaph, under the hand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="That is, under the direction">a</a></sup>of Asaph, who prophesied under the hands<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="That is, under the direction">b</a></sup>of the king.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:9; Psalm 127:3; 1 Samuel 9:9"> All these were sons to Heman, the seer of the king, according to the words of God to raise a horn.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="That is, to exalt Heman">e</a></sup>And God had given to Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.</span>

Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman were under the ⸤direction⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “hands”">g</a></sup>of the king.</span>
