## Offerings for the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:5; 1 Kings 3:7; 2 Chronicles 13:7"> Then David the king said to all the assembly, “God has chosen Solomon my son alone, a young man and inexperienced, and the work is great, for the citadel is not for humankind, but for Yahweh God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:3–5; 2 Corinthians 8:3; Isaiah 54:11–12"> So I have provided for the house of my God according to all my strength, the gold for the things of gold, the silver for the things of silver, the bronze for the things of bronze, the iron for the things of iron, and the wood for the things of wood, along with an abundance of onyx stones, stones for settings, stones of turquoise, and variegated stones, and all kinds of precious stones and alabaster stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:24; Psalm 27:4; Psalm 26:8"> And moreover, with respect to my provisions for the house of my God I have a treasure of gold and silver that I have given to the house of my God above all that I have provided for the holy house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:28; 1 Chronicles 22:14; Job 28:16"> three thousand talents of gold from the gold of Ophir and seven thousand talents of refined silver to overlay the walls of the houses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–3; Exodus 35:5–9; Ezra 7:15–16"> gold for the golden things and silver for the silver things, for all the work by the hand of skilled craftsmen. Now who will offer willingly, ⸤consecrating himself⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to consecrate his hand”">a</a></sup>today to Yahweh?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 27:25–1; Isaiah 60:3–10"> Then the commanders of the ⸤families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">b</a></sup>the commanders of the tribes of Israel, and the commanders of thousands and hundreds, and the commanders of the work of the king offered themselves willingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–71; Ezra 2:69"> And they gave for the service of the house of God five thousand talents and ten thousand darics of gold, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and one hundred thousand talents of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:8; 1 Chronicles 26:21–22"> And whoever had precious stones gave them to the treasury of the house of Yahweh under the authority<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “hand”">c</a></sup>of Jehiel the Gershonite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7–8; 2 Corinthians 8:3; 1 Kings 8:61"> And the people rejoiced over their freewill offering, for with a whole heart they had willingly offered to Yahweh, and King David also rejoiced with great joy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 1:3; Romans 1:7"> Then David blessed Yahweh in the sight of all the assembly, and David said, “Blessed are you, O Yahweh, God of Israel, our father forever and ever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Daniel 4:34–35; 1 Timothy 1:17"> To you, O Yahweh, is the greatness and the power and the splendor and the glory and the strength, for everything in the heavens and in the earth. Yours, O Yahweh, is the kingdom and exaltation over all as head!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 3:16; 2 Chronicles 20:6; Deuteronomy 8:18"> And wealth and glory are from you, and you rule over all. And in your hand is power and might. And in your hand is power to make great and to give strength to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 106:1; 2 Corinthians 9:15; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> And now, our God, we give thanks to you and offer praise to the name of your splendor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:17; 2 Corinthians 3:5; Romans 11:36"> And indeed, who am I, and who are my people that we retain power to offer according to these offerings willingly? For everything is from you, and from your hand we have given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 144:4; Leviticus 25:23; Psalm 102:11"> For we are strangers before you and sojourners like all our ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “fathers”">d</a></sup>Our days are like the shadow upon earth, and there is no hope of abiding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:14; Psalm 24:1; 2 Chronicles 31:10"> O Yahweh our God, all this abundance that we have provided to build for you a house for your holy name is from your hand, and all of it is yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Proverbs 11:20; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> And I know, my God, that you yourself test the heart and are pleased with uprightness. I, in the uprightness of my heart, have willingly offered all these things, and now I have seen your people who are found here willingly offer to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 10:23; Hebrews 13:21"> O Yahweh, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, our ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>keep this forever as a plan and thoughts of the heart of your people, and establish their heart toward you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalm 72:1; 1 Chronicles 28:9; James 1:17"> Grant to Solomon my son a heart of peace to keep your commands, your statutes, and your regulations to do everything, and to build the citadel for which I have provided.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalm 95:6; Exodus 4:31; Psalm 135:19–21"> And David said to all the assembly, “Bless Yahweh your God!” And all the assembly blessed Yahweh, the God of their ancestors.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “fathers”">f</a></sup>And they bowed down and prostrated themselves before Yahweh and the king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon’s Coronation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:4–9; Numbers 15:7; Ezra 6:17"> And they offered sacrifices to Yahweh, and on that next day they offered burnt offerings to Yahweh: one thousand bulls, one thousand rams, one thousand lambs, with their libations and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 2:35; 1 Chronicles 23:1; Exodus 24:11"> And they ate and drank before Yahweh on that day with great joy. And they made Solomon the son of David king the second time, and they anointed him commander for Yahweh, and Zadok the priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 2:12; 1 Chronicles 28:5; Romans 13:1"> Then Solomon sat upon the throne of Yahweh as king in place of David his father. And he prospered, and all Israel obeyed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:3–9; Ezekiel 17:18; Genesis 24:2"> And all the officials and mighty warriors, and also all the sons of King David, ⸤pledged allegiance to⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “placed a hand under”">g</a></sup>King Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 1:12; 2 Chronicles 1:1; Ecclesiastes 2:9"> And Yahweh made Solomon exceedingly great in the sight of all Israel, and he bestowed upon him royal splendor that was not bestowed upon any king before him in Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of King David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:14; Psalm 78:71–72"> And David the son of Jesse reigned over all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11; 2 Samuel 5:4–5; 1 Chronicles 3:4"> And the days that he reigned over Israel were forty years. In Hebron he reigned seven years, and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty-three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Acts 13:36; 1 Chronicles 23:1"> And he died in a good old age, full of days, wealth, and honor. And Solomon his son reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 2 Samuel 12:1–7; 1 Chronicles 21:9–11"> Now the words of King David, from the first to the last, see, they are written among the words of Samuel the seer, and among the words of Nathan the prophet, and among the words of Gad the seer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 4:23; Daniel 2:21"> with all the accounts of his kingdom, his power, and the times that came upon him, and upon Israel, and upon all the kingdoms of the earth.</span>
</div>
