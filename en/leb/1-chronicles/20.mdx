## The Capture of Rabbah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:1; 2 Samuel 12:26–31; Amos 1:14"> And it happened that ⸤in the spring time of year⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “at the time of the returning of the year”">a</a></sup>the time when kings go out to battle, Joab led the troops of the army and destroyed the land of the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">b</a></sup>And he came and besieged Rabbah, but David remained in Jerusalem. And Joab struck Rabbah and destroyed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:30–31; 2 Samuel 8:11–12; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> And David took the crown of their king from his head and found it to weigh a talent of gold. And in it was a precious stone. Then it was placed upon the head of David. And he brought out the booty of the city, a large amount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:2–5; 1 Kings 9:21; Judges 8:6–7"> And the people who were in it he brought out, and ⸤he set them to work with saws and iron implements and axes⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The translation comes from 2 Samuel 12:31 since the Hebrew text here is uncertain">c</a></sup>Thus David did to all the cities of the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">d</a></sup>Then David returned, and all the nation went with him.</span>

## The Philistine Giants Slain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18–22; 2 Samuel 21:15; Joshua 12:12"> And after this there arose a war in Gezer with the Philistines. Then Sibbecai the Hushathite struck down Sippai, one of the descendants of the Rephaim. And they were subdued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Samuel 21:9; 1 Samuel 17:7"> And again there was war with the Philistines. And Elhanan son of Jair struck down Lahmi, the brother of Goliath the Gittite, the shaft of whose spear was like a weaver’s beam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:20"> And again there was war in Gath. And there was a ⸤very tall⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “measured”">e</a></sup>man there, and ⸤he had six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot, twenty-four in all⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “and his fingers, six and six, twenty-four”">f</a></sup>He himself was also a descendant of the Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Samuel 16:9; Isaiah 37:23"> And he taunted Israel, but Jehonathan son of Shimea, brother of David, struck him down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Joshua 14:12; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> These were born to the giants in Gath, and they fell by the hand of David and by the hand of his servants.</span>
