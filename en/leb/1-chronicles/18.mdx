## David’s Victory over His Enemies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–18; 1 Samuel 5:8; 2 Samuel 1:20"> And it happened that after David struck the Philistines and subdued them, he captured Gath and its villages out of the hand of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 3:4–5; 1 Kings 10:25; Isaiah 16:1"> And he struck Moab, and the Moabites became servants to David, bearing tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 60:1; 2 Samuel 8:3; Genesis 15:18"> And David struck Hadadezer, king of Zobah-Hamath, as he set up his monument at the Euphrates River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:4; 1 Kings 10:26; 1 Kings 4:2"> And David captured from him one thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand foot soldiers. And David hamstrung all the chariot horses but left one hundred of them to remain for chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:6; 2 Samuel 8:5–6; 1 Samuel 14:47"> And when the Arameans of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah-Hamath, David struck down twenty-two thousand men of Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:8; Psalm 121:8; Proverbs 21:31"> Then David set up garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became servants to David, bearing tribute. And Yahweh delivered David wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> And David took small round shields of gold that were upon the servants of Hadadezer and brought them to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:8; 2 Chronicles 4:12–18; 1 Kings 7:15–47"> And from Tibhath and from Cun, cities of Hadadezer, David captured very much bronze. Solomon made the bronze sea with it along with the columns and objects of bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:9"> And when Tou the king of Hamath heard that David had defeated the whole army of Hadadezer the king of Zobah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:10; Isaiah 39:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> he sent Hadoram his son to King David to ask him for peace and to bless him because he had fought against Hadadezer and defeated him, for Tou ⸤had been at war⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “a man of battles”">a</a></sup>with Hadadezer. And he brought with him all the objects of gold, silver, and bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Chronicles 26:26–27; 1 Kings 7:51"> King David dedicated these also to Yahweh, along with the silver and gold that he had taken from all the nations, from Edom, Moab, the sons of Ammon, the Philistines, and Amalek.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:11; 2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 8:13–14"> And Abishai son of Zeruiah defeated eighteen thousand of Edom in the Valley of Salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:6; Genesis 27:37; Psalm 144:10"> And he put garrisons in Edom, and all Edom became servants to David. And Yahweh delivered David wherever he went.</span>
</div>
</div>

## David’s Administration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:15; Psalm 89:14; Psalm 78:71–72"> So David reigned over all Israel, and ⸤he administered justice⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “he was a doer of justice”">b</a></sup>and righteousness to all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 4:3; 2 Samuel 8:16"> And Joab son of Zeruiah was over the army, and Jehoshaphat son of Ahilud was recorder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Kings 4:3"> And Zadok son of Ahitub and Abimelech son of Abiathar were priests, and Shavsha was secretary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 2 Samuel 15:18; 1 Kings 2:34–35"> And Benaiah son of Jehoiada was over the Kerethites and the Pelethites. And the sons of David were chief officials under the hand of the king.</span>
</div>
