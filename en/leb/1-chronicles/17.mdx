## Yahweh’s Covenant with David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:7–9; 1 Chronicles 15:1; 1 Chronicles 17:5"> And as David lived in his house, David said to Nathan the prophet, “Look, I live in a house of cedar, but the ark of the covenant of Yahweh is under tent curtains.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2; 1 Corinthians 13:9; Zechariah 8:23"> And Nathan said to David, “Whatever is in your heart, do, for God is with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Numbers 12:6; Isaiah 30:21"> And it happened that same night the word of God came to Nathan, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:2–3; Isaiah 55:8–9; 2 Chronicles 6:8–9"> “Go and say to David my servant, ‘Thus says Yahweh, “You are not to build a house for me to live in,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:6; Exodus 40:2–3; 1 Kings 8:16"> for I have not lived in a house from the day that I brought out Israel unto this day. I have been from tent to tent and from tabernacle to tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:7; Numbers 10:33–36; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> In every place I have moved about, did I say one word with the judges of Israel I commanded to shepherd my people, saying, ‘Why have you not built for me a house of cedar?’ ” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:21; Exodus 3:1–10; 1 Samuel 17:15"> So now, thus you shall say to my servant David, ‘Thus says Yahweh of hosts, “I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, to be leader over my people Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:14; 1 Chronicles 17:2; Psalm 113:7–8"> and I have been with you wherever you went and have cut down your enemies from before you and will make a name for you, like the name of the great ones on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:23; Ezekiel 28:4; Ephesians 5:6"> And I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and I will plant them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “him” (i.e., the nation)">a</a></sup>and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or “he”">b</a></sup>shall dwell in their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “his”">c</a></sup>place and be in anguish no longer. And wicked men will no longer wear them down as before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:11; Judges 2:14–18; Exodus 1:21"> from the day that I commanded judges over my people Israel. And I will subdue your enemies, and I declare to you that Yahweh will build for you a house.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “household” (that is, a dynasty)">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalm 132:11; 1 Kings 1:21; 1 Chronicles 28:5"> And it shall be that when your days are fulfilled to go and sleep with your ancestors,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “fathers”">e</a></sup>then I will raise up your seed after you, who will be one of your sons, and I will establish his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 5:5; Daniel 2:44; Isaiah 9:7"> He himself will build for me a house, and I will establish his throne forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:5; Isaiah 55:3; 1 Chronicles 17:12"> I myself will be a father to him, and he himself will be a son to me. And I will not remove my steadfast love from him as I took it away from whoever was before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 89:36; Luke 1:32–33; Psalm 72:17"> And I will establish him in my house and in my kingdom forever, and his throne will be established forever.” ’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:28; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 7:17"> According to all these words and according to all this revelation, thus did Nathan speak to David.</span>
</div>
</div>

## David’s Prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:18; Genesis 32:10; Ephesians 3:8"> Then King David went and sat before Yahweh and said, “Who am I, O Yahweh God, and what is my house that you have brought me thus far?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:11–15; Philippians 2:8–11; Isaiah 49:6"> And this was a small matter in your eyes, O God. And you have spoken concerning the house of your servant for a long time and have shown me ⸤future generations to come⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “with a turn of humankind ascending”">f</a></sup>O Yahweh God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 21:17; 1 Samuel 2:30; 2 Samuel 7:20–24"> ⸤What more⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “What to add again”">g</a></sup>can David do to you for honoring your servant? Now you know your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; 2 Samuel 7:21; Daniel 9:17"> O Yahweh, for the sake of your servant and according to your heart you have done all this greatness, to make known all these great things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Jeremiah 10:6–7; Ephesians 3:20"> O Yahweh, there is none like you, and there is no God except you, according to all that we have heard with our ears.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9–10; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 48:9"> And who is like your people Israel, the one nation upon earth whom God went to redeem for himself, to establish for you a reputation<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “name”">h</a></sup>for great and awesome things, to drive out nations from before your people whom you redeemed from Egypt?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; 1 Peter 2:9; Romans 11:1–12"> And you made your people Israel an everlasting nation for yourself. And you, O Yahweh, became their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 119:49; Jeremiah 11:5; Luke 1:38"> So now, O Yahweh, let the word that you spoke to your servant and to his house be established forever, that you might do what you have spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Jeremiah 31:1; Revelation 21:3"> And let your name be established and be magnified forever, saying, ‘Yahweh of hosts, the God of Israel, is Israel’s God, and may the house of David your servant be established before you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:15; Ezekiel 36:37; Psalm 10:17"> For you, my God, ⸤have informed⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “have opened the ear of”">i</a></sup>your servant that you will build a house for him. Therefore, your servant has found courage to pray before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 34:6–7; Hebrews 6:18; Titus 1:2"> So now, O Yahweh, you yourself are God, and you have spoken to your servant this good thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; Romans 11:29; Psalm 72:17"> And now may you begin to bless the house of your servant ⸤to continue forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “to be forever”">j</a></sup>before you, for you, O Yahweh, have blessed. And it will be blessed forever.”</span>
</div>
