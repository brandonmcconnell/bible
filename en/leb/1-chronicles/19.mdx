## The Ammonites Humiliate David’s Messengers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:1–2; 2 Samuel 10:1–19; 1 Samuel 12:12"> And it happened that after this Nahash the king of the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">a</a></sup>died, and his son reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:3–6; 1 Samuel 30:26; Genesis 19:37–38"> And David said, “I will deal kindly with Hanun the son of Nahash because his father showed kindness to me.” So David sent messengers to comfort him concerning his father. And the servants of David came to the land of the ⸤Ammonites⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">b</a></sup>to Hanun, to comfort him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 1:23–24; 1 Corinthians 13:5–7; 1 Kings 12:8–11"> But the princes of the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">c</a></sup>said to Hanun, “⸤Do you think⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “In your eyes”">d</a></sup>because David sent comforters to you that David honors your father? Is it not for the purpose of exploring so as to overthrow and spy out the land that his servants have come to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:4–5; Isaiah 15:2; Mark 12:4"> So Hanun took the servants of David and shaved them and cut off their garments at the middle, up to the buttocks, and sent them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 6:24–26; Matthew 18:31; 1 Kings 16:34"> And they went and told David concerning the men, and he inquired of them, for the men were very disgraced. And the king said, “Dwell in Jericho until your beards grow out; then return.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:5; Genesis 34:30; 1 Chronicles 18:9"> And when the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">e</a></sup>saw that they made themselves odious to David, Hanun and the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">f</a></sup>sent one thousand talents of silver to hire for themselves horses and chariots from Aram-Naharaim, Aram-Maacah, and Zobah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 21:30; Joshua 13:9; Joshua 13:16"> And they hired for themselves thirty-two thousand chariots and the king of Maacah and his people, and they came and encamped before Medeba. And the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">g</a></sup>were gathered from their cities and came to the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Chronicles 11:10–47; 2 Samuel 23:8–39"> And when David heard, he sent Joab and the entire army of mighty warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 20:1; 1 Kings 20:24; Isaiah 28:6"> And the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">h</a></sup>went out and took up positions for battle at the entrance of the city. And the kings who had come were alone in the field.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:9–14; Judges 20:42–43; Joshua 8:22"> When Joab saw that there was ⸤a battle line⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “the face of the battle”">i</a></sup>against him at the front and the back, he chose from among the chosen men in Israel and arranged them to meet Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:12; 1 Chronicles 11:20; 1 Chronicles 19:9"> And the remainder of the people he put in the hand of Abishai his brother, and they were arranged to meet the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">j</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 1:27–28; Nehemiah 4:20; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> And he said, “If Aram is too strong for me, then you must be a help to me, but if the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">k</a></sup>are too strong for you, then I will help you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:14; Psalm 27:14; 2 Samuel 10:12"> Be strong! Let us strengthen ourselves on behalf of our people and on behalf of the cities of our God. And may Yahweh do what is good in his eyes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 20:19–21; 2 Chronicles 13:5–16; Jeremiah 46:15–16"> And Joab and the people who were with him drew near before Aram for battle, and they fled before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Leviticus 26:7"> And when the ⸤Ammonites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">l</a></sup>saw that Aram had fled, they also fled before Abishai his brother, and they came to the city. Then Joab came to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:16; Zechariah 14:1–3; Micah 4:11–12"> And when Aram saw that they were defeated before Israel, they sent messengers and brought out Aram, who was from beyond the Euphrates,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Or “the river”">m</a></sup>with Shophach the commander of the army of Hadadezer before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 22:6–7; 1 Chronicles 19:9"> And when it was told to David, he gathered all Israel and crossed the Jordan. And he came to them and was arrayed against them. Then David was arrayed to meet Aram in battle, and they fought with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:18; Psalm 33:16; Psalm 18:32"> And Aram fled before Israel. And David killed from Aram the men of seven thousand chariots and forty thousand foot soldiers, and he put to death Shophach the commander of the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:19; Joshua 9:9–11; 1 Kings 20:1"> And when the servants of Hadadezer saw that they were defeated before Israel, they made peace with David and became servants to him. So Aram was no longer willing to help the ⸤Ammonites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Literally “sons of Ammon”">n</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
