## The Descendants of David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 46:12; Genesis 38:29; Numbers 26:20–21"> The sons of Judah: Perez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Shobal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:25; 1 Chronicles 2:52–54; Joshua 15:33"> And Reaiah the son of Shobal fathered Jahath, and Jahath fathered Ahumai and Lahad. These were the clans of the Zorathites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 15:11; 2 Chronicles 11:6"> And these were the sons of Etam: Jezreel, Ishma, and Idbash. And the name of their sister was Hazzelelponi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:18; 1 Chronicles 2:50; 1 Chronicles 4:39"> and Penuel was the father of Gedor, and Ezer was the father of Hushah. These were the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephrathah, the father of Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:24"> And Ashhur, the father of Tekoa, had two wives: Helah and Naarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Naarah bore him Ahuzzam, Hepher, Temeni, and Haahashtari. These were the sons of Naarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The sons of Helah: Zereth, Izhar, and Ethnan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Koz fathered Anub, Zobebah, and the clans of Aharhel, the son of Harum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:21; 1 Chronicles 7:23; Genesis 35:18"> And Jabez was more honorable than his brothers. And his mother called his name Jabez, saying, “Because I bore him in pain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–11; Psalm 116:1–4; Proverbs 10:22"> And Jabez called upon the God of Israel, saying, “Oh that you would surely bless me and enlarge my border. And may your hand be with me, that you would keep me from evil so that it might not harm me!” And God granted what he asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Chelub, the brother of Shuhah, fathered Mehir. He was the father of Eshton.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And Eshton fathered Beth-Rapha, Paseah, and Tehinnah, the father of the city of Nahash. These are the men of Recah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 15:17; Judges 1:13; Judges 3:9–11"> The sons of Kenaz: Othniel and Seraiah. And the sons of Othniel: Hathath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:35; 2 Kings 24:14"> And Meonothai fathered Ophrah; and Seraiah fathered Joab, the father of Ge-Harashim, because they were craftsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 13:6; Numbers 14:6–10; Joshua 15:13–20"> The sons of Caleb, the son of Jephunneh: Iru, Elah, and Naam. And the sons of Elah: Kenaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The sons of Jehallelel: Ziph, Ziphah, Tiria, and Asarel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:19; 1 Chronicles 6:57; Joshua 15:50"> The sons of Ezrah: Jether, Mered, Epher, and Jalon. And she became pregnant with Miriam, Shammai, and Ishbah, the father of Eshtemoa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:42; 1 Chronicles 4:39; Joshua 15:58"> And his Judahite wife bore Jered the father of Gedor, Heber the father of Soco, and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. And these were the sons of Bithiah daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mered married.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; 1 Samuel 23:1–13"> The sons of the wife of Hodiah, the sister of Naham, were the fathers of Keilah the Garmite and Eshtemoa the Maacathite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The sons of Shimon: Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-Hanan, and Tolon. The sons of Ishi: Zoheth and Ben-Zoheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 38:5; 1 Chronicles 2:3; Numbers 26:20"> The sons of Shelah, the son of Judah: Er the father of Lecah, Laadah the father of Mareshah, and the families of the house of linen workers at Beth Ashbea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and Jokim, and the men of Cozeba, and Joash, and Saraph, who ruled in Moab and returned to Lehem. (Now the records are ancient).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalm 81:6; 1 Chronicles 4:14"> These were the potters and inhabitants of Netaim and Gederah. They lived there with the king in his service.</span>

## Descendants of Simeon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 46:10; Exodus 6:15; Numbers 26:12–14"> The sons of Simeon: Nemuel, Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, Shaul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Shallum his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The sons of Mishma: Hammuel his son, Zaccur his son, Shimei his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 26:14; Numbers 26:22; Numbers 2:13"> Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brothers did not have many children. But all their families did not multiply like the men of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 19:2–3; Joshua 15:28–29; Joshua 19:9"> And they lived in Beersheba, Moladah, Hazar Shual,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:3–4"> Bilhah, Ezem, Tolad,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:28; Joshua 19:4–5; 1 Samuel 30:1"> Bethuel, Hormah, Ziklag,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 19:5–6"> Beth-Marcaboth, Hazar-Susim, Beth-Biri, and Shaaraim. These were their cities until David reigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 19:7"> And their villages were Etam, Ain, Rimmon, Token, and Ashan, five cities</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 19:8"> and all their villages that surrounding these cities as far as Baal. These were their settlements, and they kept a genealogical record for them.</span>

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Elioenai, Jaakobah, Jeshohaiah, Asaiah, Adiel, Jesimiel, Benaiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and Ziza the son of Shiphi, son of Allon, son of Jedaiah, son of Shimri, son of Shemaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:24; Genesis 6:4"> These ⸤mentioned by name⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “coming in the names”">a</a></sup>were leaders in their families, and their fathers’ households increased greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:4; 1 Chronicles 4:18; Joshua 15:58"> And they went to the entrance of Gedor, up to the east side of the valley, to seek pasture for their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Judges 18:7–10; Psalm 105:23; Psalm 78:51"> And they found fertile and good pasture, and the land was very broad, quiet, and at ease, for the former inhabitants there were from Ham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 14:28–32; 2 Kings 18:8–12; 1 Chronicles 4:34–38"> These, mentioned by name, came in the days of Hezekiah, king of Judah, and attacked their tents and the Meunites who were found there. And they devoted them to destruction to this day, and they settled among them because there was pasture there for their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 36:8–9; Deuteronomy 1:2"> And some of them from the Simeonites went to Mount Seir, five hundred men. And Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi, were under their leadership.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 15:7–8; 2 Samuel 8:12"> And they destroyed the remainder of the Amalekites who had escaped, and they have lived there to this day.</span>
