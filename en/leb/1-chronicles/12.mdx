## David and His Mighty Men at Ziklag

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:2–6; 1 Chronicles 11:24–25; 1 Chronicles 8:33"> Now these are the ones who came to David at Ziklag while he was still shut up because ⸤of Saul⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “of the face of Saul”">a</a></sup>the son of Kish. And they were the mighty warriors helping with the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Judges 20:16; 1 Samuel 17:49"> They were armed with the bow, able to shoot right-handed or left-handed, slinging stones or shooting arrows with the bow; they were kinsmen of Saul from Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:28; 2 Samuel 21:6; 1 Chronicles 11:33"> The leader was Ahiezer, then Jehoash, sons of Shemaah the Gibeonite; Jeiel and Pelet, sons of Azmaveth; Beracah; Jehu the Anathothite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 9:3; Joshua 15:36; 1 Chronicles 11:15"> Ishmaiah the Gibeonite, a mighty warrior among the thirty who was over the thirty;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1 Chronicles 12:5 begins here in the Hebrew Bible">b</a></sup>Jeremiah; Jahaziel; Johanan; Jozabad the Gederathite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1 Chronicles 12:5–40 in the English Bible is 12:6–41 in the Hebrew Bible">c</a></sup>Eluzai; Jerimoth; Bealiah; Shemariah; Shephatiah the Hariphite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Elkanah, Isshiah, Azarel, Joezer, and Jashobeam the Korahites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 15:58; 1 Chronicles 4:18; 1 Chronicles 4:39"> Joelah and Zebadiah, sons of Jeroham from the Gedor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 17:10; 2 Samuel 23:20"> And from the Gadites, valiant mighty warriors, ⸤soldiers fit for war⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “men of battle for the war”">d</a></sup>expert with shield and spear, defected to David at the fortress toward the wilderness. And ⸤they had faces like lions⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “faces of a lion were their faces”">e</a></sup>and were swift as gazelles upon the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Ezer the chief, Obadiah second, Eliab third,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Mishmannah fourth, Jeremiah fifth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Attai sixth, Eliel seventh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Jehonan eighth, Elzabad ninth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Jeremiah tenth, Macbannai eleventh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Leviticus 26:8"> These were the sons of Gad, leaders of the army. The smallest one was as a hundred, and the greatest as a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 3:15; Joshua 4:18; Jeremiah 49:19"> These were they who crossed the Jordan in the first month, when it was filled over its banks. And they put to flight all who were in the valley to the east and to the west.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And some of the sons of Benjamin and from Judah came to the fortress, to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Jude 1:9; 2 Corinthians 13:11"> And David went out ⸤to meet them⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “before them”">f</a></sup>and answered and said to them, “If you come in peace to me, to help me, ⸤my heart will be joined with you⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the heart will be to me to you together”">g</a></sup>but if you come to betray me to my adversaries, although there is no wrong on my hands, may the God of our fathers see and pass judgment.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:34; Judges 3:10; 2 Samuel 17:25"> Then the Spirit came upon Amasai, chief of the thirty who said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We are yours, O David!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And we are with you, O son of Jesse!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Peace! Peace to you, and peace to those who help you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for your God helps you.”</span><br />So David appointed them as heads of the troops.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 29:2–9"> And some Manassites deserted to David when he came with the Philistines to the battle against Saul, but he did not help them, for the rulers of the Philistines sent him away upon counsel, saying, “He will desert to his master Saul at the cost of our heads.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:17; Deuteronomy 1:15; 1 Samuel 29:11"> When he went to Ziklag some Manassites deserted to him: Adnah, Jehozabad, Jediael, Michael, Jozabad, Elihu, and Zillethai, heads of the thousands that were for Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1–17; 1 Chronicles 12:20; 1 Chronicles 5:24"> And they themselves helped David against the troops, for all of them were mighty men of strength and were commanders in the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; Job 17:9; Genesis 32:2"> ⸤Day in and day out⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “for to the time of day to the day”">h</a></sup>they came to David to help him until there was a great army, like the army of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:3–4; 1 Chronicles 11:10; 1 Samuel 16:1"> And these are the numbers of the chiefs of those prepared for the army who came to David at Hebron to turn the kingdom of Saul over to him according to the word of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The sons of Judah bearing shield and spear: six thousand eight hundred armed troops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> From the men of Simeon, mighty warriors of strength for war: seven thousand one hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> From the Levites: four thousand six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Kings 11:9; 2 Kings 25:18; 1 Chronicles 6:49–57"> Jehoiada the chief officer of the house of Aaron, and with him, three thousand seven hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 6:8; 1 Chronicles 6:53"> And Zadok, a young man, a mighty warrior of strength, and the house of his father: twenty-two commanders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 1 Chronicles 12:2; Genesis 31:23"> From the men of Benjamin, kinsmen of Saul, three thousand, and ⸤even still⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “and even they”">i</a></sup>the majority of them ⸤remained loyal⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “keeping the responsibility”">j</a></sup>to the house of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 6:4"> And from the men of Ephraim: twenty thousand eight hundred mighty warriors of strength, famous men in the house of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 17:1–18"> And from the half-tribe of Manasseh: eighteen thousand who were designated by name to come to make David king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 5:17; Luke 12:56–57; Esther 1:13"> And from the men of Issachar: ⸤men who were skilled in understanding the times⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “men who knew the understanding of the times”">k</a></sup>to know what Israel should do. Their chiefs were two hundred, and all their kinsmen were under their command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalm 12:2; John 1:47"> From Zebulun, those who went out armed, equipped for battle with all the weapons of war: fifty thousand to help David ⸤with singleness of purpose⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “with not heart and heart”">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> From Naphtali: one thousand commanders; and those with them with shield and spear: thirty-seven thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> From the Danites, those equipped for battle: twenty-eight thousand six hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:33; Joel 2:7"> And from Asher, those who went out armed and prepared for battle: forty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 14:3; Deuteronomy 3:12–16; Joshua 13:7–32"> And from beyond the Jordan, from the Reubenites and the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh, with all their weapons of war for battle: one hundred and twenty thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 49:8–10; 1 Kings 8:61; Psalm 101:2"> All these were men of war arrayed in battle line ⸤with a whole heart⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “with heart well”">m</a></sup>They came to Hebron to make David king over all Israel. Likewise, all the rest of Israel had one heart to make David king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; 2 Samuel 19:42; Genesis 26:30"> And they were there with David three days, eating and drinking, for their kinsmen were prepared for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:18; 2 Samuel 16:1; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> And also their relatives, as far as Issachar and Zebulun and Naphtali, brought food on donkeys, on camels, on mules, and on oxen—provisions of flour, cakes of figs, raisin cakes, wine and oil, cattle and sheep in abundance, for there was great joy in Israel.</span>
</div>
