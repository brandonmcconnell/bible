## David Organizes the Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:28; 1 Chronicles 28:5; 1 Kings 1:33–39"> Now David was old and full of days. And he made Solomon his son king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; 2 Chronicles 34:29–30; 1 Chronicles 13:1"> And he assembled all the commanders of Israel and the priests and the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 4:2–49"> And the Levites thirty years old and above were counted, and their number according to their head count for the men was thirty-eight thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:8; Deuteronomy 16:18; 1 Chronicles 26:29–31"> Of these, David said, “Twenty-four thousand are to direct the work of the house of Yahweh, along with six thousand judges and officials,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:25–26; 2 Chronicles 8:14; Psalm 87:7"> four thousand gatekeepers, and four thousand offering praise with the instruments that I have made for praise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 6:1; 1 Chronicles 6:16"> And David organized them in divisions according to the sons of Levi: to Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:21; Exodus 6:17; 1 Chronicles 6:17–20"> For the Gershonites were Ladan and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:33–34; 1 Chronicles 15:20–21; 1 Chronicles 15:7"> The sons of Ladan: Jehiel the chief and Zetham and Joel, three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The sons of Shimei: Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran, three. These were the heads of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">a</a></sup>of Ladan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the sons of Shimei: Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah; these four were the sons of Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jahath was the chief, and Zizah the second, but Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons. They were enrolled as one for the house of a father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 26:58"> The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Exodus 6:20; 1 Samuel 2:28"> The sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. And Aaron was set apart to consecrate the most holy things. He and his sons were to burn offerings forever before Yahweh to serve him and to bless in his name forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Psalm 90:1; 1 Chronicles 26:23–25"> But Moses the man of God and his sons were reckoned among the tribe of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 18:3–4; Exodus 2:22; Exodus 4:20"> The sons of Moses: Gershom and Eliezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 25:20; 1 Chronicles 24:20"> The sons of Gershom: Shebuel the chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:25"> And the sons of Eliezer were Rehabiah, the head. And Eliezer had no other sons, but the sons of Rehabiah were very many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:26; 1 Chronicles 24:22"> The sons of Izhar: Shelomith the chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:23; 1 Chronicles 15:9; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> The sons of Hebron: Jeriah the chief, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The sons of Uzziel: Micah the chief and Isshiah the second.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:20; 1 Chronicles 24:26–30"> The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli: Eleazar and Kish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:28; Numbers 36:6–8"> And Eleazar died, and he did not have sons, but only daughters. And the sons of Kish, their relatives, married them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:30"> The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth, three.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 10:21; 1 Chronicles 23:3"> These were the sons of Levi according to the house of their fathers, the heads of the ⸤families⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">b</a></sup>according to their enrollment, by the number of the names, according to their head count, who were to do the work of the service of the house of Yahweh, from twenty years old and above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 132:13–14; 1 Chronicles 22:18; 2 Samuel 7:11"> For David said, “Yahweh, the God of Israel, has given rest to his people, and he dwells in Jerusalem forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 4:5; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:15"> And also, the Levites do not need to carry the tabernacle and all its vessels for its service.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Psalm 72:20; 2 Samuel 23:1"> For by the last words of David, they are the number of the sons of Levi, from twenty years old and above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:3–6; 2 Chronicles 31:11; 2 Chronicles 35:11–14"> For their station was ⸤to assist⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “for the hand of”">c</a></sup>the sons of Aaron with the service of the house of Yahweh, over the courtyards, over the chambers, and over the cleansing of all the sanctified objects, and with the work of the service of the house of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 19:35–36; Exodus 25:30; Leviticus 6:20–23"> and with the rows of bread, the flour, the grain offering, the wafers of unleavened bread, the baked offering, the offering mixed with oil, and for all the amounts and measurements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalm 92:1–3; Ezra 3:10–11; 1 Chronicles 9:33"> And they were to stand ⸤every morning⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “in the morning, in the morning”">d</a></sup>thanking and praising Yahweh, and likewise in the evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; Numbers 28:1–29; Numbers 10:10"> and for every burnt offering offered to Yahweh, on Sabbaths, on new moon festivals, and at the appointed feasts, according to the customary number ⸤required of them⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “over them”">e</a></sup>regularly before Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:6–9"> And they shall keep the responsibility of the tent of assembly,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “meeting”">f</a></sup>the responsibility of the sanctified objects, and the responsibility of the sons of Aaron, their brothers, to serve the house of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
</div>
