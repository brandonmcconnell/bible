## A Summary of the Covenant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:2; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Jeremiah 4:2"> “The rest of the people, the priests, the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, the temple servants, and all who separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to observe the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew torah">a</a></sup>of God—their wives, their sons, their daughters, and all who know and understand—</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Psalm 119:106; Titus 2:11–14"> are helping their brothers, their nobles, and entering into a solemn oath to walk in the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew torah">b</a></sup>of God which was given by the hand of Moses the servant of God, and to observe and do all of the commandments of Yahweh our Lord and his judgments and regulations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Exodus 34:16; Ezra 9:1–3"> We will not give our daughters to the peoples of the land nor take their daughters for our sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–22; Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 23:10–11"> And the peoples of the land who bring merchandise and any grain on the Sabbath day to sell, we will not accept it from them on the Sabbath or on a holy day. We will forego the crops of the seventh year and cancel every debt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 17:24–27; Exodus 30:11–16; Proverbs 3:9"> “We put on ourselves the commandment upon us to yearly give a third of a shekel for the service of the house of our God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:4; Leviticus 24:5–6; Numbers 28:1–29"> for the rows of bread, the offering of the daily sacrifice, the continual burnt sacrifice, the Sabbaths, the new moon festivals, the appointed festival times, the holy objects, the sin offerings that make atonement for Israel, and for all the work of the house of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:31; Isaiah 40:16; Leviticus 6:12–13"> And we have cast lots for the contributions of the wood offering of the priests, the Levites, and the people to bring it to the house of our God, by our fathers’ houses, at designated times, year by year, to burn on the altar of Yahweh our God—as it is written in the law.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew torah">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:2; Exodus 23:19; Exodus 34:26"> We also bring the first fruits of our soil and the first fruits of all the fruit trees, year by year, for the house of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Leviticus 27:26–27; Exodus 13:12–15"> And the firstborn of our sons and beasts—as it is written in the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew torah">d</a></sup>—and the firstborn of our cattle and sheep, to bring to the house of our God and to the priests serving in the house of our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 23:17; Numbers 18:21; Nehemiah 13:9"> And we bring the best of our dough, our offerings, the fruit of every tree, new wine, and olive oil to the priests, to the chambers of the house of our God; and to bring the tithe of our soil to the Levites, for the Levites receive the tithes in all of our rural towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:12–13; Numbers 18:26–28; 2 Chronicles 31:11–12"> And the priest, the descendant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “son”">e</a></sup>of Aaron, will be with the Levites during the tithe the Levites receive. The Levites will bring up a tithe of the tithes for the house of our God to the chambers of the storehouse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 Chronicles 31:12; Hebrews 10:25; Deuteronomy 14:23–27"> For the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">f</a></sup>and the ⸤Levites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Levi”">g</a></sup>will bring to the storerooms the offering of grain, new wine, and olive oil. These are the objects of the sanctuary and the priests who minister and the gatekeepers and singers. We will not neglect the house of our God.”</span>
</div>
