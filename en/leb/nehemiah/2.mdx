## Nehemiah Sent

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 7:1; Nehemiah 1:11; Nehemiah 1:1"> It happened in the month of Nisan, in the twentieth year of King Artaxerxes, when wine was before him, I carried the wine and gave it to the king. And I had never been sad before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 15:13; Genesis 40:7"> So the king said to me, “Why is your face sad since you are not sick? This is nothing but sadness of the heart.” And I was very much afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Daniel 2:4; 1 Kings 1:31"> I said to the king, “May the king live forever! Why should my face not be sad when the city of my ancestors’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “fathers’ ”">a</a></sup>burial site<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew “sites”">b</a></sup>is ruined and her gates are consumed by fire?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 4:6; Proverbs 3:6; Nehemiah 1:4"> Then the king said to me, “What is your request?” So I prayed to the God of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 14:22; Ruth 2:13; Esther 7:3"> Then I said to the king, “If it pleases the king, and if your servant has found favor in your presence, I ask that you send me to Judah, to the city of my ancestors’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “fathers’ ”">c</a></sup>burial sites, so that I may rebuild it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:6; Isaiah 65:24; Nehemiah 5:14"> With the queen sitting beside him, the king said to me, “How long will your journey be and when will you return?” So it pleased the king and he sent me, and I set for him an appointed time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 7:21; Ezra 8:36; Nehemiah 2:9"> Then I said to the king, “If it is good for the king, let letters be given to me for the governors in the province Beyond the River, that they may let me pass until I come to Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:6; Isaiah 66:14; Nehemiah 7:2"> Also, a letter to Asaph, keeper of the king’s land reserve, that he should give me timber for laying the beams for the gates of the citadel of the house and for the walls of the city, and for the house which I will enter.” And the king gave permission to me, according to the good hand of God on me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 8:22"> I came to the governors of the province Beyond the River, and I gave them the letters of the king. Then the king sent troop commanders and horses with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; Nehemiah 4:7; Nehemiah 13:4"> But when Sanballat the Horonite and the Ammonite servant Tobiah heard this, ⸤they were greatly displeased⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “it was a great disaster for them”">d</a></sup>that a person had come to seek the welfare of the ⸤Israelites⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">e</a></sup></span>
</div>

## Nehemiah Inspects the Walls and Decides to Restore Them

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 8:32"> I came to Jerusalem and was there for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Ezra 7:27; Jeremiah 31:33"> I got up during the night, I and a few men with me. I did not tell anybody what my God put in my heart to do for Jerusalem. No animal was with me except the animal that I was riding on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Nehemiah 2:3; Nehemiah 3:13–14"> I went out during the night at the gate of the valley by the Dragon spring and to the Dung Gate. And I examined the walls in Jerusalem and its gates that had been destroyed by the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:15; 2 Kings 20:20; 2 Chronicles 32:30"> I crossed over to the Fountain Gate and to the King’s Pool, but there was no place for ⸤my mount⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “the animal under me”">f</a></sup>to cross over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:23; John 18:1; Jeremiah 31:38–40"> So I went up by the valley during the night and was examining the wall. Then I returned and came to the Valley Gate and returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The prefects did not know where I had gone and what I was doing. I had not yet told the Jews, the priests, the nobles, the prefects, and the rest of the workers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:14; Nehemiah 1:3; Isaiah 35:3–4"> Then I said to them, “You see the misery that we are in, that Jerusalem is ruined and its gates burned by the fire. Come, build the walls of Jerusalem and we shall no longer be a disgrace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; 2 Chronicles 32:5; 1 Chronicles 19:13"> I told them of the good hand of my God that was upon me and surely the words of the king that were spoken to me. And they said, “Let us arise and build!” And they strengthened their hands for this good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:6; Nehemiah 6:1–2; Psalm 44:13–14"> But Sanballat the Horonite, the Ammonite servant Tobiah, and Geshem the Arab heard it, and they mocked and despised us, saying, “What is this thing that you are doing? Are you rebelling against the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 8:21; Ezra 4:3; Psalm 35:27"> Then I answered and said to them, “The God of the heavens himself will let us succeed, and we his servants shall arise and build. But for you there is no share, right, or memorial in Jerusalem.”</span>
</div>
</div>
