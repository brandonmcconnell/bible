## Ezra Reads the Law to the People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 7:6; Nehemiah 3:26; 2 Chronicles 34:15"> All of the people gathered as one to the public square before the Water Gate. They asked Ezra the scribe to bring the book of the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Hebrew torah">a</a></sup>of Moses that Yahweh had commanded Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:24; Malachi 2:7; Numbers 29:1"> So Ezra the priest brought the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew torah">b</a></sup>before the assembly for each man and woman to hear with understanding, on the first day of the seventh month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 3:22; Revelation 2:29; Hebrews 2:1–3"> He read from it facing the public square before the Water Gate from dawn until noon that day, opposite the men, women, and those with understanding. The ears of all the people were attentive to the book of the law.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Hebrew torah">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 10:29; Nehemiah 12:13; Nehemiah 10:20"> Then Ezra the scribe stood on a wooden podium that had been made for the occasion. And beside him stood Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah on his right. On his left was Pedaiah, Mishael, Malkijah, Hashum, Hashbaddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:20; 1 Kings 8:14; Luke 4:16–17"> Ezra opened the book in the sight of all the people, because he was above all of the people. When he opened it all the people stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Lamentations 3:41; Exodus 4:31"> Then Ezra blessed Yahweh the great God, and all of the people answered, “Amen! Amen!” while lifting their hands. Then they bowed down and worshiped Yahweh with their noses to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:4; Leviticus 10:11; Nehemiah 3:23"> And Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, Pelaiah, and the Levites were teaching the people from the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Hebrew torah">d</a></sup>while the people were in their places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Acts 28:23; Luke 24:32"> So they read the book from the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Hebrew torah">e</a></sup>of God, making it clear and giving the meaning so that they could understand the reading.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:65; Nehemiah 7:70; Deuteronomy 12:12"> Nehemiah the governor, Ezra the priest and scribe, and the Levites who taught the people said to all of the people, “This day is holy to Yahweh your God. Do not mourn nor weep.” For all of the people wept when they heard the words of the law.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Hebrew torah">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalm 28:7–8; Proverbs 17:22; 2 Corinthians 12:8–9"> Then he said to them, “Go, eat festive food and drink sweet drinks, and send a share to those for whom nothing is prepared; for this day is holy to our lord. Do not be grieved because the joy of Yahweh is your refuge.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:30"> So the Levites silenced all of the people, saying, “Silence, for this day is holy. Do not be grieved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 119:174; Psalm 119:111; Psalm 119:130"> All of the people went to eat, to drink, to send a share, and to have great joy because they understood the words that they had made known to them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Festival of Booths

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 2:1–6; Proverbs 8:33–34; Proverbs 12:1"> On the second day the heads of the ⸤families⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “fathers”">g</a></sup>of all the people, the priests, and the Levites gathered together to Ezra the scribe to study the words of the law.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Hebrew torah">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34; Deuteronomy 16:13–15; Leviticus 23:40–43"> They found written in the law,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Hebrew torah">i</a></sup>which Yahweh had commanded by the hand of Moses, that the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">j</a></sup>should live in booths during the festival of the seventh month,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40; Deuteronomy 16:16; Leviticus 23:4"> and that they should proclaim and give voice in all of their cities in Jerusalem, saying, “Go out to the hill and bring olive tree branches, olive oil wood branches, myrtle shrub branches, palm tree branches, and other leafy tree branches to make booths, as it is written.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; 2 Kings 14:13; Nehemiah 12:37"> So the people went out and brought them and made booths for themselves, each on his roof, in their courtyards, in the courtyards of the house of God, in the public square of the Water Gate, and in the public square of the Gate of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:13; Ezra 3:4; 2 Chronicles 7:8–10"> And all of the assembly of those who returned from captivity made booths and lived in the booths because the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">k</a></sup>had not done it from the days of Jeshua son of Nun until that day. And there was very great joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 29:35; Leviticus 23:36; Deuteronomy 31:10–13"> And he read from the scroll of the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Hebrew torah">l</a></sup>of God day by day from the first day up to the last day. They celebrated the festival for seven days, and on the eighth day there was an assembly according to the rule.</span>
</div>
