## Israel Separates Itself

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:3–5; Nehemiah 9:3; Nehemiah 13:23"> On that day the book of Moses was read in the hearing of the people and it was found written in it that no Ammonite or Moabite should ever come into the assembly of God</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Joshua 24:9–10; Numbers 23:8–11"> because they did not come to meet the ⸤Israelites⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “sons/children of Israel”">a</a></sup>with bread and water, but hired Balaam against them in order to curse them—but our God changed the curse into a blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:2; Exodus 12:38; Numbers 11:4"> So it happened when they heard the law<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Hebrew torah">b</a></sup>that they separated all of the foreign people from Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Nehemiah Brings Reform

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Nehemiah 6:17–18; Nehemiah 13:28"> Before this, Eliashib the priest who was appointed over the chambers of the house of our God—the one related to Tobiah—</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; 2 Chronicles 34:11; Numbers 18:21–24"> prepared for Tobiah a large chamber where they had formerly put the grain offering, the frankincense, the temple objects, tithes of grain, wine, and oil commanded for the Levites, the singers, the gatekeepers, and the offerings of the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:14; Nehemiah 2:5–6; Matthew 13:25"> During all of this, I was not in Jerusalem because in the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes king of Babylon I went to the king. At the end of some days I asked permission from the king to leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:5; Matthew 21:12–13; Lamentations 1:10"> So I came to Jerusalem. And I came to learn of the wrong that Eliashib had done for Tobiah by making him a room in the courtyard of the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 11:15–17; Ezra 10:1; Psalm 69:9"> It was very displeasing for me, and I threw all of the objects from the house of Tobiah outside of the chamber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:5; 2 Chronicles 29:15–19; Nehemiah 12:45"> And I spoke in order, and they cleansed the chambers. Then I returned the objects of the house of God—the grain offering and the frankincense.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37; Malachi 3:8; Malachi 1:6–14"> And then I came to learn that the food of the Levites had not been given to them, so that the Levites and the singers, the doers of the work, had gone back each to his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:39; Nehemiah 13:17; Nehemiah 13:25"> So I quarreled with the prefects, and I said, “Why is the house of God forsaken?” And I gathered them and set them at their station.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:37–39; Malachi 3:10; Nehemiah 12:44"> So all of Judah brought the tithe of grain, new wine, and olive oil into the storehouses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 22:7; Nehemiah 7:2; Nehemiah 12:44"> I appointed as treasurer over the storehouses Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, Pedaiah from the Levites, and as their hand Hanan son of Zaccur, son of Mattaniah, for they were considered faithful. The responsibility given to them was to distribute to their brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalm 122:6–9; Nehemiah 13:22; Nehemiah 13:31"> Remember me, my God, concerning this, and do not wipe out my loyal acts which I have done in the house of my God and in his service.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Nehemiah Begins Sabbath Reforms

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:31; Nehemiah 13:21; Exodus 34:21"> In those days I saw in Judah people treading the wine press on the Sabbath, bringing in heaps of grain and loading them on donkeys along with wine, grapes and figs, and every kind of burden and bringing it all to Jerusalem on the day of the Sabbath. And I warned them at that time against selling food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:12; Deuteronomy 5:14"> Tyrian men who lived in Jerusalem brought fish and every kind of merchandise and sold it on the Sabbath to the descendants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “sons”">c</a></sup>of Judah and in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:11; Nehemiah 13:25; Isaiah 1:10"> So I quarreled with the nobles of Judah and said to them, “What is this evil thing that you are doing, profaning the day of the Sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21–23; Jeremiah 44:9; Numbers 32:14"> Did not your ancestors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or “fathers”">d</a></sup>do this also, and our God brought on us all of this disaster and on this city too? Now you are adding fierce wrath on Israel by profaning the Sabbath!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 23:32; Exodus 31:14–17; Jeremiah 17:19–22"> So when it became dark at the gates of Jerusalem before the Sabbath, I commanded that the doors be shut and said that they should not be opened until after the Sabbath. And I appointed some of my young men over the gates to prevent any ⸤goods⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “burden”">e</a></sup>being brought in on the day of the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So the merchants and the sellers of merchandise spent the night outside of Jerusalem once or twice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4; 1 Peter 2:14; Ezra 7:26"> But I warned them and said to them, “Why are you spending the night opposite the wall? If you do it again, I will lay hands against you.” From that time on they did not come on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:30; Nehemiah 13:14; Nehemiah 13:31"> And then I told two Levites that they must purify themselves and come to guard the gates in order to consecrate the day of the Sabbath. Remember this also, my God, and take pity on me according to the greatness of your loyal love.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Mixed Marriages are Condemned

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:1–2; Ezra 10:10; Nehemiah 10:30"> Also in those days I saw Jews who had married women of Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:9"> Half of their children spoke Ashdodite and could not speak Judean, but only the tongues of other nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Nehemiah 13:17; Nehemiah 10:29–30"> So I quarreled with them and cursed them and beat some of their men and pulled out their hair. I made them take an oath by God: “Do not give your daughters to their sons or take their daughters for your sons or for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 11:1–8; 1 Kings 3:13; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> Did not King Solomon of Israel sin in this way? And among the many nations there was no king like him, and he was beloved by his God, and God made him king over all Israel. Yet the foreign women made even him sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; 1 Samuel 30:24"> Should we listen to you all and do this great evil, acting unfaithfully against our God by marrying foreign women?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 12:22"> One from the sons of Jehoiada, son of the high priest Eliashib, who was the son-in-law of Sanballat the Horonite was there. I chased him away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:14; Numbers 25:12–13; Malachi 2:4–8"> Remember them, my God, because of their defilements of the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood and the Levites.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:30; Nehemiah 12:1–26; 1 Chronicles 23:1–26"> So I cleansed them from everything foreign, and I established responsibilities for the priests and Levites, each in his own work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:14; Nehemiah 13:22; Psalm 106:4"> a contribution of the wood offering at appointed times, and for the first fruits. Remember me, my God, for good.</span>
</div>
</div>
