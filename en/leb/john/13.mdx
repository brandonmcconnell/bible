## Jesus Washes His Disciples’ Feet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 12:23; 1 John 4:19; John 13:34"> Now before the feast of Passover, Jesus, knowing that his hour had come that he would depart from this world to the Father, and having loved his own in the world, loved them to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 1:13–17; Acts 5:3; John 13:26–27"> And as a dinner was taking place, when the devil had already put into the heart of Judas son of Simon Iscariot that he should betray him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 11:27; Hebrews 1:2"> because he knew that the Father had given him all things into his hands, and that he had come forth from God and was going away to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 22:27; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Luke 12:37"> he got up from the dinner and took off his outer clothing, and taking a towel, tied it around himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 7:44; Psalm 51:2; Genesis 18:4"> Then he poured water into the washbasin and began to wash the feet of the disciples, and to wipe them dry with the towel ⸤which he had tied around himself⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “with which he was girded”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 3:11–14; John 1:27; Luke 5:8"> Then he came to Simon Peter. He said to him, “Lord, are you going to wash my feet?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:8; John 12:16; John 14:26"> Jesus answered and said to him, “What I am doing you do not understand now, but you will understand after these things.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; Acts 22:16; Titus 3:5"> Peter said to him, “You will never wash my feet ⸤forever⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">b</a></sup>Jesus replied to him, “Unless I wash you, you do not have a share with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalm 51:7; Psalm 51:2; 1 Peter 3:21"> Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:3; 2 Corinthians 7:1; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> Jesus said to him, “The one who has bathed ⸤only needs⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “does not have need except”">c</a></sup>to wash his feet, but is completely clean. And you are clean, but not all of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 13:26; John 13:2; John 13:21"> (For he knew the one who would betray him; because of this he said, “Not all of you are clean.”)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 13:7; John 13:4; Ezekiel 24:24"> So when he had washed their feet and taken his outer clothing and reclined at table again, he said to them, “Do you understand what I have done for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 6:46; 1 Corinthians 12:3; John 11:28"> You call me ‘Teacher’ and ‘Lord,’ and you speak correctly, for I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Acts 20:35; Romans 12:16"> If then I—your Lord and Teacher—wash your feet, you also ought to wash one another’s feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 11:29; 1 Peter 3:17–18"> For I have given you an example, that just as I have done for you, you also do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:40; John 15:20; Matthew 10:24–25"> Truly, truly I say to you, a slave is not greater than his master, nor a messenger greater than the one who sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 11:28; James 4:17; James 1:25"> If you understand these things, you are blessed if you do them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 41:9; Matthew 26:23; John 17:12"> “I am not speaking about all of you. I know whom I have chosen, but in order that the scripture would be fulfilled, ‘The one who eats my bread has lifted up his heel against me.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 41:9">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; John 14:29; John 16:4"> From now on I am telling you before it happens, in order that when it happens you may believe that I am he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Luke 9:48; Matthew 25:40"> Truly, truly I say to you, the one who receives anyone I send receives me, and the one who receives me receives the one who sent me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Predicts Judas’ Betrayal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 13:18; Matthew 26:21; Mark 14:18"> When he had said these things, Jesus was troubled in spirit and testified and said, “Truly, truly I say to you that one of you will betray me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 22:23; Matthew 26:22; Mark 14:19"> The disciples began looking at one another, uncertain about whom he was speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 19:26; John 21:7; John 21:20"> One of his disciples—the one whom Jesus loved—was reclining ⸤close beside⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “in the bosom of” (a position dictated by ancient banqueting practice)">e</a></sup>Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 12:17; Luke 1:22; Acts 13:16"> So Simon Peter gestured for this one to inquire who it was about whom he was speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 21:20; Genesis 44:4–12; Esther 7:5"> He leaned back<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Then he leaned back”">f</a></sup>accordingly against Jesus’ chest and said to him, “Lord, who is it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 26:23; John 13:30; Mark 14:19–20"> Jesus replied, “It is he to whom I dip the piece of bread and give it to him.” Then after dipping the piece of bread, he gave it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “after dipping the piece of bread, he took it and gave it ”">g</a></sup>to Judas son of Simon Iscariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:3; John 13:2; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> And after the piece of bread, then Satan entered into him. Then Jesus said to him, “What you are doing, do quickly!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> (Now no one of those reclining at table knew for what reason he said this to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 12:5–6; John 13:1; Ephesians 4:28"> For some were thinking because Judas had the money box, Jesus was telling him, “Purchase ⸤what we need⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “of which we have need”">h</a></sup>for the feast,” or that he should give something to the poor.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Job 24:13–15; Proverbs 4:16"> So after he had taken the piece of bread, he went out immediately. And it was night.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Predicts Peter’s Denial

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 14:13; 1 Peter 4:11; John 7:39"> Then, when he had gone out, Jesus said, “Now the Son of Man is glorified, and God is glorified in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Isaiah 53:10–12; John 17:4–6"> If God is glorified in him, God will also glorify him in himself, and will glorify him immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 7:33–34; John 8:21–24; John 16:16–22"> Children, yet a little time I am with you. You will seek me and just as I said to the Jews, “Where I am going you cannot come,” now I say also to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Leviticus 19:18; John 15:17"> “A new commandment I give to you: that you love one another—just as I have loved you, that you also love one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 John 4:20–21; 1 John 3:10–14; 1 John 2:10"> By this everyone will know that you are my disciples—if you have love for one another.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 13:33; John 21:21–22"> Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, where are you going?” Jesus replied,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “replied to him”">i</a></sup>“Where I am going you cannot follow me now, but you will follow later.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 21:13; Acts 20:24; Mark 14:27–31"> Peter said to him, “Lord, why am I not able to follow you now? I will lay down my life for you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Jesus replied, “Will you lay down your life for me? Truly, truly I say to you, the rooster will not crow until you have denied me three times!</span>
</div>
</div>
