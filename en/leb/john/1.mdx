## The Prologue to John’s Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> This one was in the beginning with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:16–17; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Revelation 4:11"> All things came into being through him, and apart from him not one thing came into being that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or “came into being. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 3:19–20; John 12:36–40; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> And the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not overcome<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or “comprehend” (if primarily referring to people in the world)">c</a></sup>it.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:4; John 1:36; John 5:33–35"> This one came for a witness, in order that he could testify about the light, so that all would believe through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:20; John 3:28; Acts 19:4"> That one was not the light, but came<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="The verb is implied from the previous verse, and must be supplied in the English translation">e</a></sup>in order that he could testify about the light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 12:46; John 1:4; 1 John 2:8"> The true light, who gives light to every person, was coming into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 17:25; 1 John 3:1; 1 Corinthians 2:8"> He was in the world, and the world came into being through him, and the world did not recognize<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “acknowledge”">f</a></sup>him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 3:26; Romans 8:14; 1 John 3:1"> But as many as received him—to those who believe in his name—he gave to them authority to become children of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 3:9; James 1:18; 1 Peter 1:23"> who were born not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of a husband, but of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 John 4:9; 1 Timothy 3:16; Isaiah 40:5"> And the Word became flesh and took up residence among us, and we saw his glory, glory as of the one and only from the Father, full of grace and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Colossians 1:17; Luke 3:16"> John testified about him and cried out, saying, “This one was he about whom I said, ‘The one who comes after me is ahead of me, because he existed before me.’ ”</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 6:46; Matthew 11:27"> No one has seen God at any time; the one and only, God, the one who is in the bosom of the Father—that one has made him known.</span>
</div>

## John the Baptist Testifies to Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 3:15–18; John 10:24; Acts 19:4"> And this is the testimony of John, when the Jews sent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “sent to him”">g</a></sup>priests and Levites from Jerusalem so that they could ask him, “Who are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 1:7–8; Matthew 3:11–12; John 3:28–36"> And he confessed—and he did not deny, and confessed—“I am not the Christ!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 11:14"> And they asked him, “Then who are you? Are you Elijah?” And he said, “I am not!” “Are you the Prophet?”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A reference to the “Prophet like Moses” of Deut 18:15 (see Acts 3:22 )">h</a></sup>And he answered, “No!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:13"> Then they said to him, “Who are you, so that we can give an answer to those who sent us? What do you say about yourself?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:3; Matthew 3:3"> He said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I am ‘the voice of one crying out in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Make straight the way of the Lord,” ’</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 40:3">i</a></sup><br />just as Isaiah the prophet said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Matthew 23:13–15; Luke 11:39–44"> (And they had been sent from the Pharisees.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18; Deuteronomy 18:15; John 1:20–22"> And they asked him and said to him, “Why then are you baptizing, if you are not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:10–11; Luke 3:16; Acts 1:5"> John answered them, saying, “I baptize with water. In your midst stands one whom you do not know—</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 1:30; Mark 1:7; Matthew 3:11"> the one who comes after me, of whom I am not worthy ⸤to untie⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “that I might untie”">j</a></sup>the strap of his sandal!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:40; John 3:23; Judges 7:24"> These things took place in Bethany on the other side of the Jordan, where John was baptizing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 John 2:2; 1 John 3:5"> On the next day he saw Jesus coming to him and said, “Look! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:27; John 1:15; Luke 3:16"> This one is the one about whom I said, ‘After me is coming a man who is ahead of me, because he existed before me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:17; Malachi 4:2–5"> And I did not know him, but in order that he could be revealed to Israel, because of this I came baptizing with water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 1:10; Luke 3:22; Matthew 3:16"> And John testified, saying, “I have seen the Spirit descending like a dove from heaven and remaining upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:5; Matthew 3:11; Luke 3:16"> And I did not know him, but the one who sent me to baptize with water, that one said to me, ‘The one upon whom you see the Spirit descending and remaining upon him—this one is the one who baptizes with the Holy Spirit.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 2:7; John 1:49; Psalm 89:26–27"> And I have seen and testify that this one is the Chosen One<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “the Son of God”">k</a></sup>of God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Two of John’s Disciples Follow Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 3:25–26; Malachi 3:16"> On the next day again John was standing there, and two of his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 1:29; 1 Peter 1:19–20; Isaiah 65:1–2"> and looking at Jesus as he was walking by, he said, “Look! The Lamb of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 10:17; John 1:43; Zechariah 8:21"> And the two disciples heard him speaking, and they followed Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 18:7; John 3:2; John 1:49"> And Jesus, turning around and seeing them following him, said to them, “What do you seek?” And they said to him, “Rabbi” (which means when translated “Teacher”), “where are you staying?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:29; Matthew 11:28–30; John 6:37"> He said to them, “Come and you will see!” So they came and saw where he was staying, and they stayed with him that day (it was about the tenth hour).</span>
</div>
</div>

## Andrew Declares Jesus to be the Messiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42"> Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of the two who heard John and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 4:25; John 1:45; Psalm 89:20"> This one first found his own brother Simon and said to him, “We have found the Messiah!” (which is translated “Christ”).</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 21:15–17; 1 Corinthians 3:22; 1 Corinthians 15:5"> He brought him to Jesus. Looking at him, Jesus said, “You are Simon the son of John. You will be called Cephas” (which is interpreted “Peter”).</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Calls Philip and Nathanael

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; John 14:8; Isaiah 65:1"> On the next day he wanted to depart for Galilee, and he found Philip. And Jesus said to him, “Follow me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:21; Matthew 11:21; Mark 8:22"> (Now Philip was from Bethsaida, the town of Andrew and Peter.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:18–22; Luke 24:27; Micah 5:2"> Philip found Nathanael and said to him, “We have found the one whom Moses wrote about in the law, and the prophets wrote about—Jesus son of Joseph from Nazareth!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 7:52; John 7:41–42; John 4:29"> And Nathanael said to him, “Can anything good come out of Nazareth?” Philip said to him, “Come and see!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Psalm 32:2; Psalm 73:1"> Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward him and said about him, “Look! ⸤A true Israelite⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Literally “truly an Israelite”">l</a></sup>in whom is no deceit!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; John 2:25; Psalm 139:1–2"> Nathanael said to him, “From where do you know me?” Jesus answered and said to him, “Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:15; Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 27:42"> Nathanael answered him, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are the king of Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 11:40; John 20:29; Luke 1:45"> Jesus answered and said to him, “Because I said to you that I saw you under the fig tree, do you believe? You will see greater things than these!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 28:12; Acts 7:56; Luke 22:43"> And he said to him, “Truly, truly I say to all of you, you will see heaven opened and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man.”</span>
</div>
