## A Meeting with Nicodemus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 19:39; John 7:47–50; John 3:10"> Now there was a man of the Pharisees ⸤whose name was⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “the name to him”">a</a></sup>Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 5:36; Acts 10:38; Acts 2:22"> This man came to him at night and said to him, “Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher who has come from God, for no one is able to perform these signs that you are performing unless God were with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; John 3:5–6; 1 Peter 1:23–25"> Jesus answered and said to him, “Truly, truly I say to you, unless someone is born from above,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The same Greek word can mean either “from above” or “again,” which allows for the misunderstanding by Nicodemus here; Jesus was speaking of new birth “from above,” while Nicodemus misunderstood him to mean a second physical birth">b</a></sup>he is not able to see the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:18; John 3:3"> Nicodemus said to him, “How can a man be born when he is an old man? He is not able to enter into his mother’s womb for the second time and be born, can he?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25–27; Ephesians 5:26; Acts 2:38"> Jesus answered, “Truly, truly I say to you, unless someone is born of water and spirit, he is not able to enter into the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:16–21; Ezekiel 36:26–27; Romans 8:4–9"> What is born of the flesh is flesh, and what is born of the Spirit is spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 3:3; Ephesians 4:22–24; Romans 12:1–2"> Do not be astonished that I said to you, ‘It is necessary for you to be born from above.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The same Greek word can mean either “from above” or “again” (see also v. 3 )">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:4–5; 1 Corinthians 2:11; Acts 2:2"> The wind blows wherever it wishes, and you hear the sound of it, but you do not know where it comes from and where it is going. So is everyone who is born of the Spirit.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 6:60; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 42:16"> Nicodemus answered and said to him, “How can these things be?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:29; Jeremiah 8:8–9; Ezekiel 11:19"> Jesus answered and said to him, “Are you the teacher of Israel, and you do not understand these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:38; 1 John 5:6–12; John 12:49"> Truly, truly I say to you, we speak what we know, and we testify about what we have seen, and you do not accept our testimony!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; 1 Corinthians 2:7–9; Hebrews 5:11"> If I tell you earthly things and you do not believe, how will you believe if I tell you heavenly things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 30:4; John 6:38; Acts 2:34"> And no one has ascended into heaven except the one who descended from heaven—the Son of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 21:7–9; John 8:28; John 12:32–34"> And just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="An allusion to Num 21:5–9">d</a></sup>thus it is necessary that the Son of Man be lifted up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:16; John 6:40; John 12:44–46"> so that everyone who believes in him will have eternal life.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some interpreters and Bible translations extend the quotation of Jesus’ words through v. 21">e</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Love for the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:8; 1 John 4:9–10; John 3:15"> For in this way God loved the world, so that he gave his one and only Son, in order that everyone who believes in him will not perish, but will have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 4:14; Luke 19:10; John 12:47–48"> For God did not send his Son into the world in order that he should judge<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or “he should condemn”">f</a></sup>the world, but in order that the world should be saved through him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 16:16; 1 John 5:10; Romans 8:1"> The one who believes in him is not judged,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “condemned”">g</a></sup>but the one who does not believe has already been judged,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “been condemned”">h</a></sup>because he has not believed in the name of the one and only Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 8:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:12; John 9:39–41"> And this is the judgment: that the light has come into the world, and people loved the darkness rather than the light, because their deeds were evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11–13; John 7:7; Job 24:13–17"> For everyone who practices evil hates the light and does not come to the light, lest his deeds be exposed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 1:6; Psalm 139:23–24; 3 John 1:11"> But the one who practices the truth comes to the light, in order that his deeds may be revealed, that they are done in God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Additional Testimony by John the Baptist About Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:1–3; John 3:26; John 2:13"> After these things Jesus and his disciples came into Judean territory, and there he spent time with them and was baptizing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 1:4–5; Ezekiel 43:2; Matthew 3:5–6"> Now John was also baptizing at Aenon near Salim, because water was plentiful there, and they were coming and were being baptized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 4:12; Matthew 14:3; Mark 6:17"> (For John had not yet been thrown into prison.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 2:6; Matthew 3:11; Hebrews 6:2"> So a dispute occurred on the part of John’s disciples with a Jew<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some significant early manuscripts read “the Jews”">i</a></sup>concerning purification.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 1:7; John 1:15; John 12:19"> And they came to John and said to him, “Rabbi, he who was with you on the other side of the Jordan, about whom you testified—look, this one is baptizing, and all are coming to him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 1:17; 1 Corinthians 4:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> John answered and said, “A man can receive not one thing unless it is granted to him from heaven!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:20; John 1:23; Matthew 3:11–12"> You yourselves testify about me that I said, ‘I am not the Christ, but I am sent before that one.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; Hosea 2:19; Isaiah 54:5"> The one who has the bride is the bridegroom. But the friend of the bridegroom, who stands and hears him, rejoices ⸤greatly⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “with joy”">j</a></sup>because of the bridegroom’s voice. So this joy of mine is complete.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Colossians 1:18; Psalm 72:17–19; Hebrews 3:2–6"> It is necessary for that one to increase, but for me to decrease.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:23; Matthew 28:18; Romans 9:5"> The one who comes from above is over all. The one who is from the earth is from the earth and speaks from the earth; the one who comes from heaven is over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:11; John 15:15; John 1:11"> What he has seen and heard, this he testifies, and no one accepts his testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 6:17; Ephesians 1:13; Titus 1:1–2"> The one who accepts his testimony has attested that God is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Matthew 12:18; John 15:26"> For the one whom God sent speaks the words of God, for he does not give the Spirit by measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 28:18; John 5:22; John 5:20"> The Father loves the Son and has given all things into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:24; John 1:12; 1 Thessalonians 5:9"> The one who believes in the Son has eternal life, but the one who disobeys the Son will not see life—but the wrath of God remains on him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Some interpreters and Bible translations extend the quotation of John the Baptist’s words through v. 36">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>
