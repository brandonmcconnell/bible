## Jesus Promises the Holy Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:3–5; 1 John 5:2–3; John 14:21–24"> “If you love me, you will keep my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:26; John 15:26; John 16:7–15"> And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Advocate, in order that he may be with you ⸤forever⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">g</a></sup>—</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; John 16:13; 1 John 2:27"> the Spirit of truth, whom the world is not able to receive, because it does not see him or know him. You know him, because he resides with you and will be in you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 14:3; Psalm 23:4; Matthew 18:20"> “I will not leave you as orphans; I am coming to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:16; John 7:33; Hebrews 7:25"> Yet a little time and the world will see me no longer, but you will see me; because I live, you also will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 15:5–7; Galatians 2:20; John 17:21–23"> On that day you will know that I am in my Father, and you are in me, and I am in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:5"> The one who has my commandments and keeps them—that one is the one who loves me. And the one who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him and will reveal myself to him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 6:16; Matthew 10:3"> Judas (not Iscariot) said to him, “Lord, why<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “and why”">h</a></sup>is it that you are going to reveal yourself to us and not to the world?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:15; Revelation 3:20–21"> Jesus answered and said to him, “If anyone loves me he will keep my word, and my Father will love him, and we will come to him and ⸤will take up residence with him⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “will make abode with him”">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 7:16; John 14:10; John 8:42"> The one who does not love me does not keep my words, and the word that you hear is not mine, but the Father’s who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:6–8; John 13:19; John 16:12"> These things I have spoken to you while residing with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:26; John 14:16; 1 John 2:27"> But the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name—that one will teach you all things, and will remind you of everything that I said to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; Isaiah 41:10"> “Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you—not as the world gives, I give to you. Do not let your hearts be troubled, and do not let them be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:29–30; 1 Corinthians 15:24–28; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> You have heard that I said to you, ‘I am going away, and I am coming to you.’ If you loved me, you would have rejoiced that I am going to the Father, because the Father is greater than I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 13:19; John 16:4–31; Matthew 24:24–25"> And now I have told you before it happens, so that when it happens, you may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:19; 1 John 4:4; Revelation 12:9"> I will no longer speak much with you, for the ruler of the world is coming, and he has ⸤no power⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “nothing”">j</a></sup>⸤over⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “in”">k</a></sup>me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 10:18; Philippians 2:8; John 12:49"> But so that the world may know that I love the Father, and just as the Father has commanded me, thus I am doing. Get up, let us go from here!</span>
</div>
</div>
