## Jesus Is Betrayed and Arrested

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:32; Matthew 26:36; 2 Samuel 15:23"> When Jesus had said these things, he went out with his disciples to the other side of the ravine of the Kidron, where there was a garden into which he and his disciples entered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Luke 22:39; Mark 11:11–12"> (Now Judas, the one who betrayed him, also knew about the place, because Jesus often gathered there with his disciples.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 18:12; Luke 22:47–53; Psalm 22:12"> So Judas, taking the cohort and officers from the chief priests and from the Pharisees, came there with lanterns and torches and weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 13:1; John 18:7; Psalm 27:3"> Then Jesus, because he knew all the things that were coming upon him, went out and said to them, “Who are you looking for?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; John 19:19; Matthew 2:23"> They replied to him, “Jesus the Nazarene.” He said to them, “I am he.” (Now Judas, the one who betrayed him, was also standing with them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalm 27:2; Psalm 70:2–3; 2 Kings 1:9–15"> So when he said to them, “I am he,” ⸤they drew back⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “they went to the back”">a</a></sup>and fell to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 18:4"> Then he asked them again, “Who are you looking for?” And they said, “Jesus the Nazarene.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:28; Matthew 26:56; 2 Corinthians 12:9"> Jesus replied, “I said to you that I am he! So if you are looking for me, let these men go,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 17:12"> in order that the word that he had spoken would be fulfilled: “Those whom you have given to me—I have not lost anyone of them.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A paraphrase of the statement in John 17:12">b</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 14:47; Matthew 26:51–54; Luke 22:49–51"> Then Simon Peter, who had a sword, drew it and struck the slave of the high priest and cut off his right ear. (Now the name of the slave was Malchus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 20:22; Matthew 26:39; John 20:17"> So Jesus said to Peter, “Put the sword into its sheath! The cup that the Father has given me—shall I not drink it?”</span>

## Peter Denies Jesus the First Time

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 22:54; Mark 14:54; Matthew 26:58–68"> So Simon Peter and another disciple followed Jesus. (Now that disciple was known to the high priest, and entered with Jesus into the courtyard of the high priest.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 26:69; Mark 14:66–68; Luke 22:55–57"> But Peter was standing by the door outside. So the other disciple who was known to the high priest went out and spoke to the doorkeeper and brought Peter in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 26:69–70; John 21:15; Luke 22:56–57"> Then the female slave who was the doorkeeper said to Peter, “You are not also one of the disciples of this man, are you?” He said, “I am not!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 14:54; John 18:25; Mark 14:67"> (Now the slaves and the officers were standing there, having made a charcoal fire because it was cold, and they were warming themselves. And Peter was also standing there with them and warming himself.)</span>

## Jesus Before Annas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:55–65; Luke 20:20; Luke 22:63–71"> So the high priest questioned Jesus about his disciples and about his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:26; Isaiah 48:16; Matthew 4:23"> Jesus replied to him, “I have spoken openly to the world. I always taught in the synagogue and in the temple courts where all the Jews assemble, and I have said nothing in secret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; Acts 24:12–13; Luke 22:67"> Why are you asking me? Ask those who heard what I have said to them! Behold, these people know what I said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:3; Mark 14:65; Luke 22:63–64"> Now when he had said these things, one of the officers who was standing by gave a slap in the face to Jesus, saying, “Do you reply to the high priest in this way?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 2:20–23; 2 Corinthians 10:1; Matthew 5:39"> Jesus replied to him, “If I have spoken wrongly, testify about what is wrong! But if I have spoken correctly, why do you strike me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:13; Matthew 26:57"> Then Annas sent him, tied up, to Caiaphas the high priest.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 18:33; John 19:9; Matthew 27:27"> Then they brought Jesus from Caiaphas to the governor’s residence. Now it was early, and they did not enter into the governor’s residence so that they would not be defiled, but could eat the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 27:23; Mark 15:2–5; Matthew 27:11–14"> So Pilate came outside to them and said, “What accusation do you bring against this man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 3:13; Luke 20:19–26; Mark 10:33"> They answered and said to him, “If this man were not doing evil, we would not have handed him over to you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:6–7; John 19:15; Acts 25:18–20"> So Pilate said to them, “You take him and judge him according to your law!” The Jews said to him, “It is not permitted for us to kill anyone,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; Matthew 20:19; Matthew 26:2"> in order that the word of Jesus would be fulfilled that he had spoken, indicating by what sort of death he was going to die.</span>

## Pilate Questions Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 23:3–4; John 18:37; John 19:3"> Then Pilate entered again into the governor’s residence and summoned Jesus and said to him, “Are you the king of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Jesus replied, “Do you say this from yourself, or have others said this to you about me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 18:14–16; Nehemiah 4:2; Romans 3:1–2"> Pilate replied, “I am not a Jew, am I? Your people and the chief priests handed you over to me! What have you done?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 17:20–21; Colossians 1:12–14; Romans 14:17"> Jesus replied, “My kingdom is not of this world. If my kingdom were of this world, my servants would be fighting so that I would not be handed over to the Jews. But now my kingdom is not from here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 4:6; John 8:47; John 8:14"> Then Pilate said to him, “So then you are a king!” Jesus replied, “You say that I am a king. For this reason I was born, and for this reason I have come into the world: in order that I can testify to the truth. Everyone who is of the truth hears my voice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 19:4; Luke 23:4; John 19:6"> Pilate said to him, “What is truth?”<br />And when he had said this, he went out again to the Jews and said to them, “I find no basis for an accusation against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:15–18; Luke 23:16–23; Mark 15:6–14"> But it is your custom that I release for you one prisoner at the Passover. So do you want me to release for you the king of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:25; Mark 15:15; Matthew 27:16"> Then they shouted again, saying, “Not this man, but Barabbas!” (Now Barabbas was a revolutionary.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or perhaps “robber”">c</a></sup></span>
