## Jesus as the Good Shepherd

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; John 10:8–10; Romans 16:18"> “Truly, truly I say to you, the one who does not enter through the door into the fold of the sheep, but climbs up at some other place—that one is a thief and a robber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:14; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:4"> But the one who enters through the door is the shepherd of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:11–16; 2 Timothy 2:19; John 10:4"> For this one the doorkeeper opens, and the sheep hear his voice, and he calls his own sheep by name and leads them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; John 10:16; John 12:26"> Whenever he sends out all his own, he goes before them, and the sheep follow him because they know his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 John 4:5–6; Colossians 2:6–10; Mark 4:24"> And they will never follow a stranger, but will flee from him, because they do not know the voice of strangers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Psalm 82:5; Proverbs 28:5"> Jesus told them this parable, but they did not understand what it was that he was saying to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:1"> Then Jesus said to them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “to them”">a</a></sup>again, “Truly, truly, I say to you, I am the door of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Jeremiah 23:1; John 10:1"> All those who came before me are thieves and robbers, but the sheep do not listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; John 10:7"> I am the door. If anyone enters through me, he will be saved, and will come in and will go out and will find pasture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 19:10; John 6:51; John 12:47"> The thief ⸤comes only⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “does not come except”">b</a></sup>so that he can steal and kill and destroy; I have come so that they may have life, and have it abundantly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:14–15; Psalm 23:1; John 15:13"> “I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:29; Zechariah 11:16–17; Ezekiel 34:2–6"> The hired hand, who is not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf approaching and abandons the sheep and runs away—and the wolf seizes them and scatters them—</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:20; John 12:6; Acts 18:17"> because he is a hired hand and ⸤he is not concerned⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “it is not a concern to him”">c</a></sup>about the sheep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 10:27; John 10:11; 2 Timothy 2:19"> “I am the good shepherd, and I know my own, and my own know me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:4; John 10:11; Isaiah 53:10"> just as the Father knows me and I know the Father, and I lay down my life for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; John 11:52; 1 Peter 2:25"> And I have other sheep which are not from this fold. I must bring these also, and they will hear my voice, and they will become one flock—one shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:18; John 10:11; Hebrews 2:9"> Because of this the Father loves me, because I lay down my life so that I may take possession of it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:9; Titus 2:14; John 2:19–21"> No one takes it from me, but I lay it down ⸤voluntarily⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “from myself”">d</a></sup>I have authority to lay it down, and I have authority to take possession of it again. This commandment I received from my Father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:16; 1 Corinthians 3:3; John 7:40–43"> Again there was a division among the Jews because of these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:20; Mark 3:21; John 8:47–48"> And many of them were saying, “He has a demon and is out of his mind! Why do you listen to him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalm 94:9"> Others were saying, “These are not the words of one who is possessed by a demon! A demon is not able to open the eyes of the blind, is it?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus at the Feast of the Dedication

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then the feast of the Dedication took place in Jerusalem. It was winter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> and Jesus was walking in the temple in the Portico of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 22:67–70; John 1:19; John 8:25"> So the Jews surrounded him and began to say to him, “⸤How long will you keep us in suspense⸥?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “until when will you take away our life”">e</a></sup>If you are the Christ, tell us plainly!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 10:38; John 8:58; John 14:11"> Jesus answered them, “I told you and you do not believe! The deeds that I do in the name of my Father, these testify about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; John 12:37–40"> But you do not believe, because you are not of my sheep!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 10:16; John 10:14; Revelation 3:20"> My sheep listen to my voice, and I know them, and they follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:37; John 3:15–16"> And I give them eternal life, and they will never perish ⸤forever⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">f</a></sup>and no one will seize them out of my hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:37; John 17:2; John 17:9"> My Father, who has given them to me, is greater than all, and no one can seize them from the Father’s hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 1:1–2; John 14:9; John 17:21–23"> The Father and I are one.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 5:18; John 8:59; 1 Samuel 30:6"> Then<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “Then”">g</a></sup>the Jews picked up stones again so that they could stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 John 3:12; John 5:36; Psalm 35:12"> Jesus answered them, “I have shown you many good deeds from the Father. For which one of them are you going to stone me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 5:18; Leviticus 24:16; John 10:30"> The Jews answered him, “We are not going to stone you concerning a good deed, but concerning blasphemy, and because you, although you are a man, make yourself to be God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalm 82:6–7; Exodus 7:1; Psalm 82:1"> Jesus answered them, “Is it not written in your law, ‘I said, “You are gods” ’?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 82:6 (in common usage “law” could refer to the entire Old Testament)">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 13:1; Luke 16:17"> If he called them ‘gods’ to whom the word of God came—and the scripture cannot be broken—</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 5:17–18; John 3:17; Jeremiah 1:5"> do you say about he whom the Father set apart and sent into the world, ‘You are blaspheming,’ because I said, ‘I am the Son of God’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 15:24; John 10:25; John 12:37–40"> If I do not do the deeds of my Father, do not believe me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 14:20; John 10:25; John 10:30"> But if I am doing them, even if you do not believe me, believe the deeds, so that you may know and understand that the Father is in me and I am in the Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:30; John 8:59; John 7:44"> So they were seeking again to seize him, and he departed out of their hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 1:28; John 11:54; John 7:1"> And he went away again on the other side of the Jordan, to the place where John was baptizing at an earlier time, and he stayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 1:33–34; John 1:29–30; Mark 1:37"> And many came to him and began to say, “John performed no sign, but everything John said about this man was true!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 4:41; John 2:23; John 7:31"> And many believed in him there.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
