## Jesus Is Anointed at Bethany

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Then, six days before the Passover, Jesus came to Bethany, where Lazarus was, whom Jesus had raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> So they made him a dinner there, and Martha was serving, but Lazarus was one of the ones reclining at table with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Then Mary took a pound<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Greek term refers to a Roman pound, 327.45 grams (approximately 12 ounces)">a</a></sup>of ointment of very valuable genuine nard and anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped his feet with her hair. And the house was filled with the fragrance of the ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> But Judas Iscariot, one of his disciples (the one who was going to betray him) said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> “⸤Why⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “because of what”">b</a></sup>was this ointment not sold for three hundred denarii and given to the poor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; Psalm 50:16–20; James 2:6"> (Now he said this not because ⸤he was concerned⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “it was a concern to him”">c</a></sup>about the poor, but because he was a thief, and having the money box, he used to steal what was put into it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> So Jesus said, “Leave her alone, so that she may keep it for the day of my preparation for burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> For you have the poor with you always, but you do not always have me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Decision to Kill Lazarus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> Now a large crowd<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “the large crowd”">d</a></sup>of Jews found out that he was there, and they came, not only because of Jesus, but so that they could see Lazarus also, whom he raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> So the chief priests decided that they would kill Lazarus also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> because on account of him many of the Jews were going and believing in Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Triumphal Entry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> On the next day the large crowd who had come to the feast, when they heard that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> took the branches of palm trees and went out to meet him, and began crying out,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Hosanna!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the one who comes in the name of the Lord,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 118:25–26">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even the king of Israel!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> So Jesus found a young donkey and sat on it, just as it is written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Do not be afraid, daughter of Zion!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold, your king is coming,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">seated on the foal of a donkey!”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Zech 9:9">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> (His disciples did not understand these things at first, but when Jesus was glorified, then they remembered that these things had been written about him and they did these things to him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> So the crowd who was with him when he called Lazarus out of the tomb and raised him from the dead were continuing to testify.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> Because of this also the crowd went to meet him, for they had heard that he had performed this sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Psalm 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> So the Pharisees said to one another, “You see that you are accomplishing nothing! Behold, the world has gone after him.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Greeks Seeking Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> Now some Greeks were among those who had gone up in order to worship at the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> So these approached Philip, who was from Bethsaida in Galilee, and began asking him saying, “Sir, we want to see Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philip went and told Andrew. Andrew and Philip went and told Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> And Jesus answered them, saying, “The hour has come that the Son of Man will be glorified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> Truly, truly I say to you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains by itself alone. But if it dies, it bears much fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> The one who loves his life loses it, and the one who hates his life in this world preserves it for eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> If anyone serves me, he must follow me, and where I am, there my servant will be also. If anyone serves me, the Father will honor him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Predicts His Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> “Now my soul is troubled, and what shall I say? ‘Father, deliver me from this hour’? But for this reason I have come to this hour!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> Father, glorify your name!” Then a voice came from heaven, “I have both glorified it, and I will glorify it again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> Now the crowd that stood there and heard it said ⸤it had thundered⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “thunder had happened”">g</a></sup>Others were saying, “An angel has spoken to him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> Jesus answered and said, “This voice has not happened for my sake, but for your sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> Now is the judgment of this world! Now the ruler of this world will be thrown out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> And I, when I am lifted up from the earth, will draw all people to myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> (Now he said this to indicate by what sort of death he was going to die.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> Then the crowd replied to him, “We have heard from the law that the Christ remains ⸤forever⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Literally “for the age”; probably an allusion to Ps 89:35–37 (in common usage “law” could refer to the entire Old Testament)">h</a></sup>And how do you say that the Son of Man must be lifted up? Who is this Son of Man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> So Jesus said to them, “Yet a little time the light is with you! Walk while you have the light, so that the darkness does not overtake you! And the one who walks in the darkness does not know where he is going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> While you have the light, believe in the light, in order that you may become sons of light.” Jesus said these things, and then he went away and was hidden from them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The People Still Refuse to Believe

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> But as many signs as he had performed before them, they did not believe in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> in order that the word of the prophet Isaiah would be fulfilled, who said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Lord, who has believed our message?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 53:1">i</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> For this reason they were not able to believe, because again Isaiah said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“He has blinded their eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and hardened their hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lest they see with their eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and understand with their hearts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and turn, and I heal them.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 6:10">j</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> Isaiah said these things because he saw his glory, and he spoke about him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> Yet despite that, even many of the rulers believed in him, but because of the Pharisees they did not confess it, so that they would not be expelled from the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> For they loved the praise of men more than praise from God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Final Public Appeal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> But Jesus cried out and said, “The one who believes in me does not believe in me, but in the one who sent me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> and the one who sees me sees the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; Psalm 36:9; John 9:5"> I have come as a light into the world, in order that everyone who believes in me will not remain in the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> And if anyone hears my words and does not observe them, I will not judge him. For I have not come to judge the world, but to save the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> The one who rejects me and does not accept my words has one who judges him; the word that I have spoken will judge him on the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> For I have not spoken from myself, but the Father himself who sent me ⸤has commanded me⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “has given me commandment”">k</a></sup>what I should say and what I should speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> And I know that his commandment is eternal life. So the things that I say, just as the Father said to me, thus I say.”</span>
</div>
