## The Feeding of Five Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 6:31–44; John 6:23; Luke 5:1"> After these things Jesus went away to the other side of the sea of Galilee (that is, Tiberias).</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:24–25; Matthew 12:15; Matthew 15:30–31"> And a large crowd was following him because they were observing the signs that he was doing on those who were sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 6:15; Luke 9:28; Luke 6:12–13"> So Jesus went up on the mountain and sat down there with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 2:13; John 11:55; John 5:1"> (Now the Passover, the feast of the Jews, was near.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 15:33; Luke 9:12–13; Matthew 14:14–15"> ⸤Then Jesus, when he looked up⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “then Jesus lifting up the eyes”">a</a></sup>and saw that a large crowd was coming to him, said to Philip, “Where can we buy bread so that these people can eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 22:1; Deuteronomy 13:3; 2 Chronicles 32:31"> (Now he said this to test him, because he knew what he was going to do.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 11:21–22; Mark 6:37; John 12:5"> Philip replied to him, “Two hundred denarii worth of bread would not be enough for them, in order that each one could receive a little.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:40–44; Matthew 4:18"> One of his disciples, Andrew the brother of Simon Peter, said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 14:17; Mark 6:38"> “Here is a boy who has five barley loaves and two fish, but what are these for so many people?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:14–16; Matthew 15:35–36; Mark 6:39–41"> Jesus said, “Make the people recline.” (Now there was a lot of grass in the place.) So the men reclined, approximately five thousand in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 6:23; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Matthew 15:36"> Then Jesus took the bread, and after he had given thanks, he distributed it to those who were reclining—likewise also of the fish, as much as they wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:53; Nehemiah 9:25; Nehemiah 8:10"> And when they were satisfied, he said to his disciples, “Gather the remaining fragments so that nothing is lost.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; 2 Kings 4:2–7; 2 Corinthians 9:8–9"> So they gathered them, and filled twelve baskets with fragments from the five barley loaves which were left over by those who had eaten.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–18; Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:37"> Now when the people saw the sign that he performed, they began to say, “This one is truly the Prophet who is to come into the world!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:36; John 6:15–21; John 7:3–4"> Then Jesus, because he knew that they were about to come and seize him in order to make him king, withdrew again up the mountain by himself alone.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Walks on the Water

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now when evening came, his disciples went down to the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:45; John 6:24–25; John 2:12"> And getting into a boat, they began to go to the other side of the sea, to Capernaum. And it had already become dark, and Jesus had not yet come to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalm 107:25; Psalm 135:7; Matthew 14:24"> And the sea began to be stirred up because a strong wind was blowing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 6:47–49; Matthew 14:25–26; Job 9:8"> Then when they had rowed about twenty-five or thirty stadia,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A “stade” or “stadium” (plur. “stadia”) is about 607 ft (187 m), so this was around 3 miles (5 km)">b</a></sup>they saw Jesus walking on the sea and coming near the boat, and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 14:27–31; Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 44:8"> But he said to them, “It is I! Do not be afraid!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:32–33; Revelation 3:20"> So they were wanting to take him into the boat, and immediately the boat came to the land to which they were going.</span>
</div>

## Discourse About the Bread of Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45; Matthew 14:22; John 6:2"> On the next day, the crowd that was on the other side of the sea saw that other boats were not there (except one), and that Jesus had not entered with his disciples into the boat, but his disciples had departed alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:11–12"> Other boats from Tiberias came near the place where they had eaten the bread after the Lord had given thanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:17; Luke 8:40; Mark 6:53"> So when the crowd saw that Jesus was not there, nor his disciples, they themselves got into the boats and came to Capernaum seeking Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Matthew 23:7"> And when they found him on the other side of the sea, they said to him, “Rabbi, when did you get here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 4:3–4; Philippians 3:19; Philippians 2:21"> Jesus replied to them and said, “Truly, truly I say to you, you seek me not because you saw signs, but because you ate of the loaves and were satisfied!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 3:2; Isaiah 55:2; John 6:54"> Do not work for the food that perishes, but the food that remains to eternal life, which the Son of Man will give to you. For God the Father has set his seal on this one.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 9:6; Matthew 19:16"> So they said to him, “What shall we do that we can accomplish the works of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 3:23; Acts 16:31; John 3:36"> Jesus answered and said to them, “This is the work of God: that you believe in the one whom that one sent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; John 10:38; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> So they said to him, “Then what sign will you perform, so that we can see it and believe you? What will you do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:15; Psalm 105:40; Psalm 78:24–25"> Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, just as it is written, ‘He gave them bread from heaven to eat.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 78:24 which refers to the events of Exod 16:4–36">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:1; John 6:58; Exodus 16:4"> Then Jesus said to them, “Truly, truly I say to you, Moses did not give you bread from heaven, but my Father is giving you the true bread from heaven!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:42; John 17:8; John 3:13"> For the bread of God is the one who comes down from heaven and gives life to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 4:15; John 6:26; Psalm 4:6"> So they said to him, “Sir, always give us this bread!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Revelation 22:17; John 6:41"> Jesus said to them, “I am the bread of life. The one who comes to me will never be hungry, and the one who believes in me will never be thirsty again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8–9; Luke 16:31; John 12:37"> But I said to you that you have seen me and do not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; John 6:39; John 17:24"> Everyone whom the Father gives to me will come to me, and the one who comes to me I will never throw out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:30; John 4:34; John 3:31"> because I have come down from heaven not that I should do my will, but the will of the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:40; John 18:9"> Now this is the will of the one who sent me: that everyone whom he has given me, I would not lose any of them,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="This pronoun is neuter singular in Greek, but is collective">d</a></sup>but raise them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="This pronoun is neuter singular in Greek, but is collective">e</a></sup>up on the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:54; John 5:24; Romans 6:23"> For this is the will of my Father, that everyone who looks at the Son and believes in him would have eternal life, and I will raise him up on the last day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 6:51–52; John 6:48; John 6:33"> Now the Jews began to grumble about him because he said, “I am the bread that came down from heaven,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> and they were saying, “Is this one not Jesus the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How does he now say, ‘I have come down from heaven’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; John 6:64; Mark 9:33"> Jesus answered and said to them, “Do not grumble ⸤among yourselves⸥!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “with one another”">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 6:65; John 6:39–40; Matthew 11:25–27"> No one is able to come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him, and I will raise him up on the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 10:16"> It is written in the prophets, ‘And they will all be taught by God.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Isa 54:13">g</a></sup>Everyone who hears from the Father and learns comes to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 1:18; John 7:29; Matthew 11:27"> (Not that anyone has seen the Father except the one who is from God—this one has seen the Father.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="The switch from first person in vv. 44–45 to third person here and back to first person in vv. 47–51 suggests that this verse is a parenthetical comment by the author rather than the words of Jesus">h</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:36; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 5:9–10"> Truly, truly I say to you, the one who believes has eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 11:24–25; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> I am the bread of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–5; Jude 1:5; Zechariah 1:5"> Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness and they died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 6:33; John 6:51; Romans 8:10"> This is the bread that comes down from heaven so that someone may eat from it and not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 22:19; John 6:33; Hebrews 10:5–12"> I am the living bread that came down from heaven. If anyone eats from this bread, he will live ⸤forever⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">i</a></sup>And the bread that I will give for the life of the world is my flesh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 6:41"> So the Jews began to quarrel ⸤among themselves⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “with one another”">j</a></sup>saying, “How can this man give us his flesh to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 John 5:12; Matthew 26:26–28; John 6:55"> Then Jesus said to them, “Truly, truly I say to you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you do not have life in yourselves!</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:27; Philippians 3:7–10"> The one who eats my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up on the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 15:1; John 8:31; John 6:32"> For my flesh is true food, and my blood is true drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="1 John 3:24; John 15:4–5; John 14:20"> The one who eats my flesh and drinks my blood resides in me and I in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; John 5:26; John 17:21"> Just as the living Father sent me, and I live because of the Father, so also the one who eats me—that one will live because of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:47–51; John 6:41"> This is the bread that came down from heaven, not as the fathers ate and died. The one who eats this bread will live ⸤forever⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">k</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Many of Jesus’ Disciples Offended by His Teaching

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 6:24; Proverbs 8:1–3; Luke 4:31"> He said these things while teaching in the synagogue in Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 6:66; John 8:43; John 8:31"> Thus many of his disciples, when they heard it, said, “This saying is hard! Who can understand it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:24–25; John 6:64; John 21:17"> But Jesus, because he knew within himself that his disciples were grumbling about this, said to them, “Does this cause you to be offended?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 16:19; John 3:13; John 17:4–5"> Then what if you see the Son of Man ascending where he was before?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Galatians 5:25; Hebrews 4:12; Psalm 119:93"> The Spirit is the one who gives life; the flesh profits nothing. The words that I have spoken to you are spirit and are life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="John 10:26; 2 Timothy 2:19; Hebrews 4:13"> But there are some of you who do not believe.” (For Jesus knew from the beginning who they were who did not believe, and who it was who would betray him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="John 6:44–45; John 6:37; John 3:27"> And he said, “Because of this I said to you that no one can come to me unless it has been granted to him by the Father.”</span>
</div>
