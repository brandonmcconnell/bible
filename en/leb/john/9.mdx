## A Man Born Blind Is Given Sight

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And as he went away, he saw a man blind from birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 13:2; John 9:34; Acts 28:4"> And his disciples asked him, saying, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he should be born blind?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:40; Ecclesiastes 9:1–2"> Jesus replied, “Neither this man sinned nor his parents, but it happened so that the works of God could be revealed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:35; Ephesians 5:16; John 4:34"> It is necessary for us to do the deeds of the one who sent me while it is day; night is coming, when no one can work!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; John 12:46; Acts 13:47"> While I am in the world, I am the light of the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 8:23; Mark 7:33; Revelation 3:18"> When he had said these things, he spat on the ground and made clay with the saliva, and smeared the clay on his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; John 9:11; John 11:37"> And he said to him, “Go, wash in the pool of Siloam” (which is translated “sent”). So he went and washed and came back seeing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:8; 1 Samuel 21:11; Luke 18:35"> Then the neighbors and those who saw him previously (because he was a beggar) began to say, “Is this man not the one who used to sit and beg?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Others were saying, “It is this man”; others were saying, “No, but he is like him.” That one was saying, “I am he!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 9:26; John 9:21; Ecclesiastes 11:5"> So they began to say to him, “How<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Then how”">a</a></sup>were your eyes opened?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 9:27; Jeremiah 36:17"> He replied, “The man who is called Jesus made clay and smeared it on my eyes and said to me, ‘Go to Siloam and wash!’ So I went, and I washed, and I received sight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 5:11–13; Exodus 2:18–20; John 7:11"> And they said to him, “Where is that man?” He said, “I do not know.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Reaction of the Pharisees to the Healing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 11:57; John 11:46–47; John 12:42"> They brought him—the one formerly blind—to the Pharisees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:9; Luke 13:10–17; Matthew 12:1–14"> (Now the day on which Jesus made the clay and opened his eyes was the Sabbath.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:26–27; John 9:10–11"> So the Pharisees also were asking him again how he received sight. And he said to them, “He put clay on my eyes, and I washed, and I see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:43; John 10:19; Matthew 12:2"> So some of the Pharisees were saying, “This man is not from God, because he does not observe the Sabbath!” Others<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “But others”">b</a></sup>were saying, “How can a man who is a sinner perform such signs?” And there was a division among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:19; John 6:14; Acts 3:22–26"> So they said to the blind man again, “What do you say about him, because he opened your eyes?” And he said, “He is a prophet.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 3:15–19; John 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> So the Jews did not believe concerning him that he had been blind and received sight, until they summoned the parents of the one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “of him”">c</a></sup>who received sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 9:8–9; Acts 4:14; Acts 3:10"> And they asked them, saying, “Is this man your son, whom you say was born blind? Then how does he now see?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So his parents answered and said, “We know that this man is our son, and that he was born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But how he now sees we do not know, or who opened his eyes we do not know. Ask him! ⸤He is a mature adult⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “he has maturity”">d</a></sup>he will speak for himself!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 7:13; John 16:2; Luke 6:22"> (His parents said these things because they were afraid of the Jews, for the Jews had already decided that if anyone should confess him to be Christ, he would be expelled from the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 9:21"> Because of this his parents said, “⸤He is a mature adult⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Literally “he has maturity”">e</a></sup>ask him.”)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 7:19; John 9:16; John 8:49"> So they summoned the man who had been blind for the second time and said to him, “Give glory to God! We know that this man is a sinner!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 John 5:10; John 9:30; John 5:11"> Then that man replied, “Whether he is a sinner I do not know. One thing I know—that although I was blind, now I see!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So they said to him, “What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 22:67; John 9:10–15"> He replied to them, “I told you already and you did not listen! Why do you want to hear it again? You do not want to become his disciples also, do you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 2:17; John 7:19; Matthew 27:39"> They reviled<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “And they reviled”">f</a></sup>him and said, “You are his disciple! But we are disciples of Moses!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:14; Numbers 12:2–7; Acts 7:35"> We know that God has spoken to Moses, but we do not know where this man is from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; Psalm 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man answered and said to them, “For the remarkable thing is this, that you do not know where he is from, and he opened my eyes!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 28:9"> We know that God does not listen to sinners, but if someone is devout and does his will, he listens to this one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Revelation 16:18; Job 20:4"> From ⸤time immemorial⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “the age”">g</a></sup>it has not been heard that someone opened the eyes of one born blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 9:16; John 3:2; Acts 5:38–39"> If this man were not from God, he would not be able to do anything!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 9:22; John 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They answered and said to him, “You were born completely in sin, and are you attempting to teach us?” And they threw him out.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus as the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 20:31; 1 John 5:13; John 10:36"> Jesus heard that they had thrown him out, and finding him, he said, “Do you believe in the Son of Man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Song of Solomon 5:9; John 1:38"> He answered and said, “And who is he, sir, that I may believe in him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 4:26; Psalm 25:8–9; John 14:21–23"> Jesus said to him, “You have both seen him, and he is the one who is speaking with you.” 〚</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 14:33; Matthew 28:9; Luke 24:52"> And he said, “I believe, Lord!” and he worshiped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 3:19; John 12:46; Acts 26:18"> And Jesus said,〛<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="A number of important manuscripts lack v. 38 and the first part of v. 39 (“and Jesus said”)">h</a></sup>“For judgment I have come into this world, so that those who do not see may see, and those who see may become blind!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Matthew 15:12–14; John 7:47–52"> Some of the Pharisees who were with him heard these things and said to him, “We are not also blind, are we?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; John 15:22–24; 1 John 1:8–10"> Jesus said to them, “If you were blind, you would not have sin. But now you say, ‘We see,’ your sin remains.</span>
</div>
