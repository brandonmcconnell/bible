## Jesus Appears to the Disciples in Galilee

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:23; Matthew 28:16"> After these things Jesus revealed himself again to the disciples by the Sea of Tiberias. Now he revealed himself in this way:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21–22; John 1:45–1; John 4:46"> Simon Peter and Thomas (who was called Didymus)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Greek term means “the Twin”">a</a></sup>and Nathanael from Cana in Galilee and the sons of Zebedee and two others of his disciples were together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:5; Matthew 4:18–20; 2 Thessalonians 3:7–9"> Simon Peter said to them, “I am going fishing!” They said to him, “We also are coming with you.” They went out and got into the boat, and during that night they caught nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:14; Luke 24:15–16; Luke 24:31"> Now when it was already early morning, Jesus stood on the beach. However, the disciples did not know that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Philippians 4:11–13; 1 John 2:13"> So Jesus said to them, “Children, you do not have any fish to eat, do you? They answered him, “No.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:4–7; Acts 2:41; John 2:5"> And he said to them, “Throw the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some.” So they threw it, and were no longer able to haul it in from the large number of the fish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:2; Matthew 14:28–29"> Then that disciple whom Jesus loved said to Peter, “It is the Lord!” So Simon Peter, when he heard that it was the Lord, tied around himself his outer garment (for he was naked)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="I.e., “he was naked underneath the outer garment,” which he tucked into his belt; alternatively, this could mean “for he was stripped for work”">b</a></sup>and threw himself into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11"> But the other disciples came in the boat, dragging the net of fish, because they were not far from the land, but about two hundred cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Approximately 100 yards or 92 meters, based on a cubit of 18 inches.">c</a></sup>away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 21:13; 1 Kings 19:5–6; John 21:10"> So when they got out on the land, they saw a charcoal fire laid there, and a fish lying on it, and bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus said to them, “Bring some of the fish that you have just now caught.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 5:6–8; Acts 2:41"> So Simon Peter got into the boat and hauled the net to the land, full of large fish—one hundred fifty-three—and although there were so many, the net was not torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:41; Luke 9:45; John 16:19"> Jesus said to them, “Come, eat breakfast!” But none of the disciples dared to ask him, “Who are you?” because they knew that it was the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 21:9; Acts 10:41; Luke 24:42–43"> Jesus came and took the bread and gave it to them, and the fish likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 20:26; John 20:19"> This was now the third time Jesus was revealed to the disciples after he had been raised from the dead.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter Is Restored Three Times

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; 1 John 4:19; Romans 15:1"> Now when they had eaten breakfast, Jesus said to Simon Peter, “Simon son of John, do you love me more than these?” He said to him, “Yes, Lord, you know that I love you.” He said to him, “Feed my lambs!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2; 1 Peter 2:25"> He said to him again a second time, “Simon son of John, do you love me?” He said to him, “Yes, Lord, you know that I love you.” He said to him, “Shepherd my sheep!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:38; John 16:30; Mark 14:72"> He said to him a third time, “Simon son of John, do you love me?” Peter was distressed because he said to him a third time, “Do you love me?” and he said to him, “Lord, you know everything! You know that I love you!” Jesus said to him, “Feed my sheep!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:36; Acts 12:3–4; Acts 21:11"> Truly, truly I say to you, when you were young, you tied your clothes around yourself and walked wherever you wanted. But when you grow old, you will stretch out your hands, and someone else will tie you up and carry you where you do not want to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 21:22; Matthew 10:38"> (Now he said this to indicate by what kind of death he would glorify God.) And after he had said this, he said to him, “Follow me!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter and the Other Disciple Jesus Loved

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:7; John 20:2; John 21:24"> Peter turned and saw the disciple whom Jesus loved following them (who also leaned back on his chest at the dinner and said, “Lord, who is the one betraying you?”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 13:23–24; Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3–4"> So when he saw this one, Peter said to Jesus, “Lord, but what about this one?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:25; Revelation 3:11; Mark 9:1"> Jesus said to him, “If I want him to remain until I come, what is that to you? You follow me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Job 33:13; Daniel 4:35"> So this saying went out to the brothers that that disciple would not die. But Jesus did not say to him that he would not die, but “If I want him to remain until I come, what is that to you?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Concluding Word of Testimony

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 19:35; John 15:27; 1 John 5:6"> This is the disciple who is testifying about these things, and who has written down these things. And we know that his testimony is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalm 40:5; John 20:30–31; Psalm 71:15"> Now there are also many other things that Jesus did, which—if they were written down one after the other—I suppose not even the world itself could contain the books that would be written.</span>
</div>
</div>
