## Pilate Attempts to Release Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 20:19"> So then Pilate took Jesus and had him flogged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Luke 23:11; Matthew 27:27–31"> And the soldiers wove a crown of thorns and placed it on his head, and put a purple robe on him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 27:29; Matthew 26:49; Luke 1:28"> and were coming up to him and saying, “Hail, king of the Jews!” and were giving him slaps in the face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; John 19:6; Luke 23:4"> And Pilate came outside again and said to them, “Behold, I am bringing him outside to you, so that you will know that I find no basis for an accusation against him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:2; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> Then Jesus came outside wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe, and he said to them, “Behold the man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:31; John 19:4; Acts 2:23"> So when they saw him, the chief priests and the officers shouted, saying, “Crucify! Crucify!” Pilate said to them, “You take him and crucify him! For I do not find a basis for an accusation against him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 5:18; Matthew 26:63–66"> The Jews replied to him, “We have a law, and according to the law he ought to die, because he made himself out to be the Son of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 19:13; Acts 14:11–19"> So when Pilate heard this statement, he was even more afraid,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 18:33; Psalm 38:13–15; Philippians 1:28"> and he entered into the governor’s residence again and said to Jesus, “Where are you from?” But Jesus did not give him an answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 5:19; John 18:39; Daniel 3:14–15"> So Pilate said to him, “Will you not speak to me? Do you not know that I have authority to release you, and I have authority to crucify you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:1; John 3:27; Acts 2:23"> Jesus replied to him, “You would not have any authority over me unless it was given to you from above. For this reason the one who handed me over to you has greater sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 18:33–36; Mark 6:16–26"> From this point on Pilate was seeking to release him, but the Jews shouted, saying, “If you release this man, you are not a friend of Caesar! Everyone who makes himself out to be a king opposes Caesar!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalm 82:5–7; Matthew 27:19; Amos 4:7"> So Pilate, when he heard these words, brought Jesus outside and sat down on the judgment seat, in the place called The Stone Pavement (but Gabbatha in Aramaic).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:5; Mark 15:25"> (Now it was the day of preparation of the Passover; it was about the sixth hour.) And he said to the Jews, “Behold your king!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 23:18; Acts 21:36; John 19:6"> Then those shouted, “Away with him! Away with him! Crucify him!” Pilate said to them, “Shall I crucify your king?” The chief priests replied, “We do not have a king except Caesar!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:24–25; Matthew 27:26–31; Mark 15:15–20"> So then he handed him over to them in order that he could be crucified. Jesus Is Crucified<br />So they took Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:33; Luke 14:27; Luke 23:26"> and carrying for himself the cross, he went out to the place called The Place of a Skull (which is called Golgotha in Aramaic),</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Mark 15:24–27; Galatians 3:13"> where they crucified him, and with him two others, ⸤one on each side⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “from here and from here”">a</a></sup>and Jesus in the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Luke 23:38; Mark 15:26"> And Pilate also wrote a notice and placed it on the cross, and it was written: “Jesus the Nazarene, the king of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 26:14; Revelation 16:16; Acts 22:2"> So many of the Jews read this notice, because the place where Jesus was crucified was near the city. And it was written in Aramaic, in Latin, and in Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:19"> Then the chief priests of the Jews said to Pilate, “Do not write ‘The king of the Jews,’ but, ‘He said, I am king of the Jews.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalm 76:10; Psalm 65:7; Proverbs 8:29"> Pilate replied, “What I have written, I have written.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24; Matthew 27:35"> Then the soldiers, when they had crucified Jesus, took his clothing and made four shares—for each soldier a share—and the tunic. (Now the tunic was seamless, woven from the top ⸤in a single piece⸥.)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “through the whole”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalm 22:18; John 19:28; John 19:36–37"> So they said to one another, “Let us not tear it apart, but cast lots for it, to see whose it will be,” so that the scripture would be fulfilled that says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“They divided my garments among themselves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and for my clothing they cast lots.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 22:18">c</a></sup><br />Thus the soldiers did these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:55–56; John 20:1"> Now his mother and the sister of his mother, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene were standing near the cross of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 21:24; John 20:2; John 2:4"> So Jesus, seeing his mother and the disciple whom he loved standing there, said to his mother, “Woman, behold your son!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:48–50; John 16:32; 1 Timothy 5:2–4"> Then he said to the disciple, “Behold your mother!” And from that hour the disciple took her into his own home.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Dies on the Cross

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalm 69:21; Psalm 22:15; John 19:30"> After this, Jesus, knowing that now at last everything was completed, in order that the scripture would be fulfilled, said, “I am thirsty.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 19:18; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:36"> A jar full of sour wine was standing there, so they put a sponge full of the sour wine on a branch of hyssop and brought it to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 17:4; Philippians 2:8; Colossians 2:14–17"> Then when he had received the sour wine, Jesus said, “It is completed,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or (traditionally) “it is finished”">d</a></sup>and bowing his head, he gave up his spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:42; Mark 15:42"> Then the Jews, because it was the day of preparation, so that the bodies would not remain on the cross on the Sabbath (for that Sabbath was an important day), asked Pilate that their legs could be broken and they could be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43"> So the soldiers came and broke the legs of the first and of the other who had been crucified with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But when they came to Jesus, after they saw he was already dead, they did not break his legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 John 5:6; Zechariah 12:10; Zechariah 13:1"> But one of the soldiers pierced his side with a spear, and blood and water came out immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 21:24; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 John 1:1–3"> And the one who has seen it has testified, and his testimony is true, and that person knows that he is telling the truth, so that you also may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalm 34:20; Numbers 9:12; Exodus 12:46"> For these things happened in order that the scripture would be fulfilled: “Not a bone of his will be broken.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A quotation from Exod 12:46 , Num 9:12 , and Ps 34:20">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7; Psalm 22:16–17"> And again another scripture says, “They will look on the one whom they have pierced.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="A quotation from Zech 12:10">f</a></sup></span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Is Buried

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:42–47; Matthew 27:57–61; Proverbs 29:25"> And after these things, Joseph who was from Arimathea, who was a disciple of Jesus (but a secret one for fear of the Jews), asked Pilate that he might take away the body of Jesus. And Pilate allowed it, so he came and took away his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:50–52; Song of Solomon 4:14; John 3:1–21"> And Nicodemus—the one who had come to him formerly at night—also came, bringing a mixture of myrrh and aloes weighing about a hundred pounds.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="The Greek term refers to a Roman pound, 327.45 grams (approximately 12 ounces)">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:44; John 20:5–7; Acts 5:6"> So they took the body of Jesus and wrapped it in strips of linen cloth with the fragrant spices, as is the Jews’ custom to prepare for burial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:53; Matthew 27:60; John 20:15"> Now there was a garden at the place where he was crucified, and in the garden a new tomb in which no one was yet buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 19:31; John 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> So there, on account of the day of preparation of the Jews, because the tomb was close by, they buried Jesus.</span>
</div>
