## A Woman Caught in Adultery

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 11:1; Luke 19:37"> But Jesus went to the Mount of Olives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:55; Luke 4:20; Luke 21:37"> Now early in the morning he came again to the temple courts. And all the people were coming,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “were coming to him”">a</a></sup>and he sat down and began to teach them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Now the scribes and the Pharisees brought to him a woman<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “brought a woman”">b</a></sup>caught in adultery. And standing her in their midst,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> they said to him, testing him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some manuscripts omit “testing him ”">c</a></sup>“Teacher, this woman was caught in the very act of committing adultery!</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> (Now they were saying this to test him, so that they would have an occasion to bring charges against him.) ## Jesus, the Light of the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Then Jesus spoke to them again, saying, “I am the light of the world! The one who follows me will never walk in darkness, but will have the light of life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> So the Pharisees said to him, “You testify concerning yourself! Your testimony is not true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> Jesus answered and said to them, “Even if I testify concerning myself, my testimony is true, because I know where I have come from and where I am going. But you do not know where I have come from or where I am going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> You judge according to externals; I do not judge anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> But even if I judge, my judgment is true, because I am not alone, but I and the Father who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> And even in your law it is written that the testimony of two men is true.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="An allusion to Deut 17:6">l</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> I am the one who testifies concerning myself, and the Father who sent me testifies concerning me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> So they were saying to him, “Where is your father?” Jesus replied, “You know neither me nor my Father! If you had known me, you would have known my Father also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> He spoke these words by the treasury while teaching in the temple courts, and no one seized him, because his hour had not yet come.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Predicts His Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> So he said to them again, “I am going away, and you will seek me and will die in your sin. Where I am going you cannot come!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; Psalm 22:6; John 8:48"> Then the Jews began to say, “Perhaps he will kill himself, because he is saying, ‘Where I am going you cannot come.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> And he said to them, “You are from below; I am from above. You are from this world; I am not from this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> Thus I said to you that you will die in your sins. For if you do not believe that I am he, you will die in your sins.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> So they began to say to him, “Who are you?” Jesus said to them, “⸤What⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Literally “that which”">m</a></sup>I have been saying to you from the beginning.</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> Then Jesus said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “said to them”">n</a></sup>“When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will recognize that I am he, and I do nothing from myself, but just as the Father taught me, I say these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> And the one who sent me is with me. He has not left me alone, because I always do the things that are pleasing to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> While he was saying these things, many believed in him.</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> Then Jesus said to those Jews who had believed him, “If you continue in my word you are truly my disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> and you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> They replied to him, “We are descendants of Abraham and have not been enslaved to anyone at any time. How do you say, ‘You will become free’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jesus replied to them, “Truly, truly I say to you, that everyone who commits sin is a slave of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> And the slave does not remain in the household ⸤forever⸥;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">o</a></sup>the son remains ⸤forever⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">p</a></sup></span>

## The Priority of Jesus Over Abraham

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> Truly, truly I say to you, if anyone keeps my word, he will never experience death ⸤forever⸥.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-u" id="fnref-u" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">u</a></sup></span>

Abraham and the prophets died, and you say, ‘If anyone keeps my word, he will never taste death ⸤forever⸥.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-w" id="fnref-w" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">w</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> You are not greater than our father Abraham who died, are you? And the prophets died! Who do you make yourself to be?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jesus replied, “If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing. The one who glorifies me is my Father, about whom you say, ‘He is our God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> And you have not known him, but I know him. And if I were to say that I do not know him, I would be a liar like you! But I know him and I keep his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> Abraham your father rejoiced that he would see my day, and he saw it and was glad.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> So the Jews said to him, “⸤You are⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-x" id="fnref-x" data-fn="Literally “you have”">x</a></sup>not yet fifty years old, and have you seen Abraham?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> Jesus said to them, “Truly, truly I say to you, before Abraham was, I am!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> Then they picked up stones in order to throw them at him. But Jesus was hidden and went out of the temple courts.</span>
