## Jesus Prays to be Glorified

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:4–5; Philippians 2:9–11"> Jesus said these things, and lifting up his eyes to heaven he said, “Father, the hour has come! Glorify your Son, in order that your Son may glorify you—</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 10:28–29; John 6:39; Daniel 7:14"> just as you have given him authority over all flesh, in order that he would give eternal life to them—everyone whom you have given him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 5:20; 1 John 5:11–12; Jeremiah 9:23–24"> Now this is eternal life: that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom you have sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 4:34; Acts 20:24; 2 Timothy 4:7"> I have glorified you on earth by completing the work that you have given me ⸤to do⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “that I may do it”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Colossians 1:15–17; John 10:30"> And now, Father, you glorify me ⸤at your side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “by the side of yourself”">b</a></sup>with the glory that I had ⸤at your side⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “by the side of you”">c</a></sup>before the world existed.</span>
</div>

## Jesus Prays for His Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 17:26; John 14:21–24; 1 John 5:20"> “I have revealed your name to the men whom you gave me out of the world. They were yours, and you have given them to me, and they have kept your word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:16–17; John 16:15; John 14:7–10"> Now they understand that all the things that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Literally “whatever”">d</a></sup>you have given me are from you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 17:14; John 15:15; John 16:30"> because the words that you gave to me I have given to them, and they received them and know truly that I have come from you, and they have believed that you have sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; John 17:2; John 17:6"> I am asking on behalf of them. I am not asking on behalf of the world, but on behalf of those whom you have given me, because they are yours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 10:30; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 5:8–14"> and all my things are yours, and your things are mine, and I have been glorified in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or “by”; or “through”">e</a></sup>them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:9; Proverbs 18:10; John 17:12–18"> And I am no longer in the world, and they are in the world, and I am coming to you. Holy Father, keep them in your name, which you have given to me, so that they may be one, just as we are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalm 109:6–19; John 10:27–28; John 18:9"> When I was with them, I kept them in your name, which you have given to me, and guarded them, and none of them has perished except the son of destruction, in order that the scripture would be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Galatians 5:22; John 15:11"> And now I am coming to you, and I am saying these things in the world so that they may have my joy completed in themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:16; John 15:18–21; 1 John 5:19–20"> I have given them your word, and the world has hated them, because they are not of the world just as I am not of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalm 121:7; 1 John 5:18; Matthew 6:13"> I do not ask that you take them out of the world, but that you protect them from the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 17:14"> They are not of the world, just as I am not of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:3; Psalm 119:160; Ephesians 5:26"> Sanctify them in the truth—your word is truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 20:21; John 17:3"> Just as you sent me into the world, I also have sent them into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Titus 2:14; Hebrews 2:11; John 17:17"> And for them I sanctify myself, so that they themselves also may be sanctified in the truth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Prays for the Unity of Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:6–11; Acts 2:41; Acts 4:4"> “And I do not ask on behalf of these only, but also on behalf of those who believe in me through their word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12; Colossians 3:11–14; John 17:11"> that they all may be one, just as you, Father, are in me and I am in you, that they also may be in us, in order that the world may believe that you sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; John 14:20; Mark 16:17–20"> And the glory that you have given to me, I have given to them, in order that they may be one, just as we are one—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:21; John 16:27; 1 John 3:1"> I in them, and you in me, in order that they may be completed in one, so that the world may know that you sent me, and you have loved them just as you have loved me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 17:5; John 12:26; John 14:3"> “Father, those whom you have given to me—I want that those also may be with me where I am, in order that they may see my glory that you have given me because you loved me before the foundation of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:11; John 7:29; John 17:8"> Righteous Father, although the world does not know you, yet I have known you, and these men have come to know that you sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 17:23; John 17:6; John 15:9"> And I made known to them your name, and will make it known, in order that the love with which you loved me may be in them, and I may be in them.”</span>
</div>
