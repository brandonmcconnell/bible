## Jesus at the Feast of Tabernacles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Has not Moses given you the law, and none of you carries out the law? Why do you seek to kill me?”</span>

## Is Jesus the Christ?

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 7:10–11"> Then some of the inhabitants of Jerusalem began to say, “Is this not the one whom they are seeking to kill?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> And behold, he is speaking openly and they are saying nothing to him! Can it be that the rulers truly know that this man is the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> Yet we know where this man is from, but the Christ, whenever he comes—no one knows where he is from!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Then Jesus cried out in the temple courts, teaching and saying, “You both know me and you know where I am from! And I have not come from myself, but the one who sent me is true, whom you do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> I know him, because I am from him and he sent me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> So they were seeking to seize him, and no one laid a hand on him, because his hour had not yet come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:42; Matthew 12:23; John 10:41–42"> But from the crowd many believed in him and were saying, “Whenever the Christ comes, he will not perform more signs than this man has done, will he?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 18:3; Luke 22:52–53; John 11:47–48"> The Pharisees heard the crowd murmuring these things about him, and the chief priests and the Pharisees sent officers in order ⸤to take him into custody⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “that they could seize him”">g</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 16:5; John 13:33; Mark 16:19"> Then Jesus said, “Yet a little time I am with you, and I am going to the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Proverbs 1:24–31; John 13:33–36; John 14:6"> You will seek me and will not find me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Some manuscripts explicitly state “me”">h</a></sup>and where I am, you cannot come.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="James 1:1; Isaiah 11:12; Zephaniah 3:10"> So the Jews said to one another, “Where is this one going to go, that we will not find him? He is not going to go to the Dispersion among the Greeks and teach the Greeks, is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 3:9; John 7:34; John 12:34"> What is this saying that he said, ‘You will seek me and will not find me,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Some manuscripts explicitly state “me”">i</a></sup>and where I am, you cannot come’?”</span>
