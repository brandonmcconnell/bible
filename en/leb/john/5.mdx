## A Paralytic Is Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; Exodus 34:23"> After these things there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32"> Now there is in Jerusalem near the Sheep Gate a pool called in Aramaic Bethzatha,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The majority of later manuscripts read “Bethesda,” while other early manuscripts read “Bethsaida”">a</a></sup>which has five porticoes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 3:1–4; Luke 7:22; Matthew 15:30"> In these were lying a large number of those who were sick, blind, lame, paralyzed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The majority of later manuscripts add the following words: “waiting for the moving of the water. 4 For an angel of the Lord from time to time went down in the pool and stirred up the water. So the one who went in first after the stirring of the water was healed of whatever disease he suffered.”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 3:2; Luke 8:43; Mark 9:21"> And a certain man was there who had been thirty-eight years in his sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 18:41; Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 4:13"> Jesus, when he saw this one lying there and knew that he had been sick a long time already, said to him, “Do you want to become well?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalm 72:12; Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Corinthians 9:24"> The one who was sick answered him, “Sir, I do not have anyone that, whenever the water is stirred up, could put me into the pool. But ⸤while⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “during which time ”">c</a></sup>I am coming, another goes down before me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 5:24; Matthew 9:6; Mark 2:11"> Jesus said to him, “Get up! Pick up your mat and walk!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 3:7–8; John 9:14; Mark 3:2–4"> And immediately the man became well and picked up his mat and began to walk. (Now it was the Sabbath on that day.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:21; Luke 6:2; John 9:16"> So the Jews were saying to the one who had been healed, “It is the Sabbath, and it is not permitted for you to pick up the mat!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “your mat”">d</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 2:9–11; John 9:16"> But he answered them, “The one who made me well—that one said to me, ‘Pick up your mat and walk!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Judges 6:29; Romans 10:2"> So they asked him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “They asked him”">e</a></sup>“Who is the man who said to you, ‘Pick up your mat and walk?’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:30; Luke 24:31; John 8:59"> But the one who was healed did not know who it was, for Jesus had withdrawn while a crowd was in the place.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Equal with God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 8:11; Leviticus 26:23–24; Psalm 118:18"> After these things Jesus found him at the temple and said to him, “Look, you have become well! Sin no longer, lest something worse happen to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 9:11–12; John 4:29; John 9:25"> The man went and reported to the Jews that Jesus was the one who made him well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:39; John 7:19–20; Mark 3:6"> And on account of this the Jews began to persecute Jesus, because he was doing these things on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 9:4; John 14:10; Colossians 1:16"> But he answered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “Jesus answered”">f</a></sup>them, “My Father is working until now, and I am working.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 10:30; John 10:33; John 19:7"> So on account of this the Jews were seeking even more to kill him, because he not only was breaking the Sabbath, but also was calling God his own Father, thus making himself equal with God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Authority of the Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:49; John 8:28; John 14:10"> So Jesus answered and said to them, “Truly, truly I say to you, the Son can do nothing from himself except what he sees the Father doing. For whatever that one does, these things also the Son does likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 3:35; Matthew 3:17; John 17:26"> For the Father loves the Son and shows him everything that he himself is doing. And greater works than these he will show him, so that you will be astonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:11; Deuteronomy 32:39; John 11:25"> For just as the Father raises the dead and makes them alive, thus also the Son makes alive whomever he wishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:27; Acts 17:31; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> For the Father does not judge anyone, but he has given all judgment to the Son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 John 2:23; Luke 10:16; Matthew 11:27"> in order that all people will honor the Son, just as they honor the Father. The one who does not honor the Son does not honor the Father who sent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:40; 1 John 5:11–13"> Truly, truly I say to you that the one who hears my word and who believes the one who sent me has eternal life, and does not come into judgment, but has passed from death into life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:28; Ephesians 5:14; Ephesians 2:5"> “Truly, truly I say to you, that an hour is coming—and now is here—when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God, and the ones who hear will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 11:26; John 1:4; Exodus 3:14"> For just as the Father has life in himself, thus also he has granted to the Son to have life in himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 10:42; John 5:22; Acts 17:31"> And he has granted him authority to carry out judgment, because he is the Son of Man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 20:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Isaiah 26:19"> “Do not be astonished at this, because an hour is coming in which all those in the tombs will hear his voice</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Romans 2:6–10; Acts 24:15"> and they will come out—those who have done good things to a resurrection of life, but those who have practiced evil things to a resurrection of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:19; John 4:34"> I am able to do nothing from myself. Just as I hear, I judge, and my judgment is just, because I do not seek my own will, but the will of the one who sent me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Further Testimony About the Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 27:2; John 8:54; Revelation 3:14"> “If I testify about myself, my testimony is not true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 1:33; John 12:28–30; John 5:36–37"> There is another who testifies about me, and I know that the testimony which he testifies about me is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:29–34; John 3:26–36; John 1:15–27"> You have sent to John and he has testified to the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 19:41–42; John 20:31; 1 John 5:9"> (And I do not receive testimony from people, but I say these things in order that you may be saved.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; John 1:7–8; Matthew 11:11"> That one was the lamp which was burning and shining, and you wanted to rejoice for an hour in his light.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 10:25; John 10:37–38; John 15:24"> “But I have a testimony greater than John’s, for the works which the Father has given to me that I should complete them—the very works which I am doing—these testify about me, that the Father has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 8:18; John 1:18; Matthew 3:17"> And the Father who sent me, that one has testified about me. You have neither heard his voice at any time nor seen his form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 John 2:14; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; John 12:44–48"> And you do not have his word residing in yourselves, because the one whom that one sent, in this one you do not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Luke 24:44; 2 Peter 1:19–21"> You search<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or “Search” (an imperative)">g</a></sup>the scriptures because you think that you have eternal life in them, and it is these that testify about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 3:19; John 1:11; Psalm 81:11"> And you are not willing to come to me so that you may have life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 7:18; John 5:34; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> “I do not accept glory<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or “honor”">h</a></sup>from people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 3:17; John 2:25"> but I know you, that you do not have the love of God in yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 10:25; Matthew 24:5; John 17:4–6"> I have come in my Father’s name, and you do not accept me. If another should come in his own name, you would accept that one!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 12:43; Romans 2:29; Philippians 2:3"> How are you able to believe, if you accept glory from one another, and do not seek the glory which is from the only God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Romans 3:19–20; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–11"> Do not think that I will accuse you before the Father! The one who accuses you is Moses, in whom you have put your hope!</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; Deuteronomy 18:18–19; John 1:45"> For if you had believed Moses, you would believe me, for that one wrote about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Luke 16:29"> But if you do not believe that one’s writings, how will you believe my words?”</span>
</div>
</div>
