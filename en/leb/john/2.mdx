## The Wedding at Cana: Water Turned into Wine

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; John 21:2"> And on the third day, there was a wedding at Cana in Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> And both Jesus and his disciples were invited to the wedding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalm 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> And when the wine ran out, the mother of Jesus said to him, “They have no wine!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> And Jesus said to her, “What ⸤does your concern have to do with me⸥,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “to me and to you”">a</a></sup>woman? My hour has not yet come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother said to the servants, “Whatever he says to you, do it!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> Now six stone water jars were set there, in accordance with the ceremonial cleansing of the Jews, each holding two or three measures.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A “measure” was about 9 gallons (40 liters)">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> Jesus said to them, “Fill the water jars with water.” And they filled them to the brim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> And he said to them, “Now draw some out and take it to the head steward. So they took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalm 119:100"> Now when the head steward tasted the water which had become wine and did not know where it was from—but the servants who had drawn the water knew—the head steward summoned the bridegroom</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> and said to him, “⸤Everyone⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “every man”">c</a></sup>serves the good wine first, and whenever they are drunk, the inferior. You have kept the good wine until now!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> This beginning of signs Jesus performed at Cana in Galilee, and revealed his glory, and his disciples believed in him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Cleansing of the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> And he found in the temple courts those who were selling oxen and sheep and doves, and the money changers seated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> And he made a whip of cords and drove them all out of the temple courts, both the sheep and the oxen, and he poured out the coins of the money changers and overturned their tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> And to the ones selling the doves he said, “Take these things away from here! Do not make my Father’s house ⸤a marketplace⸥!”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Literally “a market house”; or “a house of merchants” (an allusion to Zech 14:21 )">f</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalm 69:9; Psalm 119:139"> His disciples remembered that it is written, “Zeal for your house will consume me.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="A quotation from Ps 69:9">g</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> So the Jews answered and said to him, “What sign do you show to us, because you are doing these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus answered and said to them, “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then the Jews said, “This temple has been under construction<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="This translation of the aorist verb is based on a very close parallel in Ezra 5:16 (LXX), where it is clear from the following verb that the construction had not yet been completed">h</a></sup>forty-six years, and will you raise it up in three days?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But he was speaking about the temple of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> So when he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered that he had said this, and they believed the scripture and the saying that Jesus had spoken.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus at the Passover

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> Now while he was in Jerusalem at the Passover, during the feast, many believed in his name because they saw his signs which he was doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> But Jesus himself did not entrust himself to them, because he knew all people,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="The Greek term is masculine and thus refers to “all people ” rather than “all things ” (which would be neuter)">i</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> and because he did not ⸤need⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Literally “have need that”">j</a></sup>anyone ⸤to testify⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Literally “should testify”">k</a></sup>about man, for he himself knew what was in man.</span>
</div>
