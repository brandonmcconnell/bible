## The Samaritan Woman at Jacob’s Well

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 3:26; John 3:22; 2 Corinthians 4:5"> Now when Jesus knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus was making and baptizing more disciples than John</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 10:48; 1 Corinthians 1:13–17"> (although Jesus himself was not baptizing, but his disciples),</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; John 10:40; John 2:11"> he left Judea and departed again for Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 17:11; Luke 2:49; Luke 9:51–52"> And it was necessary for him to go through Samaria.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 24:32; Genesis 33:19; Genesis 48:22"> Now he came to a town of Samaria called Sychar, near the piece of land that Jacob had given to his son Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:17; Matthew 8:24"> And Jacob’s well was there, so Jesus, because he had become tired from the journey, simply sat down at the well. It was about the sixth hour.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:42; 1 Kings 17:10; Genesis 24:43"> A woman of Samaria came to draw water. Jesus said to her, “Give me water to drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:13; John 4:39; John 4:5"> (For his disciples had gone away into the town so that they could buy food.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 10:28; Matthew 10:5; 2 Kings 17:24–41"> So the Samaritan woman said to him, “How do you, being a Jew, ask from me water to drink, since I am a Samaritan woman?” (For Jews have no dealings with Samaritans.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 7:17; John 7:37–39"> Jesus answered and said to her, “If you had known the gift of God and who it is who says to you, ‘Give me water to drink,’ you would have asked him, and he would have given you living water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 3:4"> The woman said to him, “Sir, you have no bucket and the well is deep! From where then do you get this living water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 4:6; John 8:53; Matthew 12:42"> You are not greater than our father Jacob, are you, who gave us the well and drank from it himself, and his sons and his livestock?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:27; Isaiah 65:13–14; Luke 16:24"> Jesus answered and said to her, “Everyone who drinks of this water will be thirsty again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:38–39; John 6:35; Isaiah 49:10"> But whoever drinks of this water which I will give to him will never be thirsty for eternity, but the water which I will give to him will become in him a well of water springing up to eternal life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 6:34; Romans 8:5; Psalm 4:6"> The woman said to him, “Sir, give me this water, so that I will not be thirsty or come here to draw water!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Revelation 2:23; John 4:18"> He said to her, “Go, call your husband and come here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The woman answered and said to him, “I do not have a husband.” Jesus said to her, “You have said rightly, ‘I do not have a husband,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:20; 1 Corinthians 7:10–11; Romans 7:3"> for you have had five husbands, and the one whom you have now is not your husband; this you have said truthfully!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 6:14; John 9:17"> The woman said to him, “Sir, I see that you are a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 12:6–7; Deuteronomy 27:12; Deuteronomy 11:29"> Our fathers worshiped on this mountain, and you people say that in Jerusalem is the place where it is necessary to worship.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:8; Malachi 1:11; John 4:23"> Jesus said to her, “Believe me, woman, that an hour is coming when neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem will you worship the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 17:23; Isaiah 2:3; Romans 9:4–5"> You worship what you do not know. We worship what we know, because salvation is from the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Philippians 3:3; John 5:25; Matthew 15:7–9"> But an hour is coming—and now is here—when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth, for indeed the Father seeks such people to be his worshipers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Matthew 15:8–9; Philippians 3:3"> God is spirit, and the ones who worship him must worship in spirit and truth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:49; Daniel 9:24–26; John 1:41–42"> The woman said to him, “I know that Messiah is coming” (the one called Christ); “whenever that one comes, he will proclaim all things to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:24; Mark 14:61–62; Romans 10:20–21"> Jesus said to her, “I, the one speaking to you, am he.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Disciples and the Harvest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 7:39; John 4:8–9"> And at this point his disciples came, and they were astonished that he was speaking with a woman. However, no one said, “What do you seek?” or “Why are you speaking with her?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 4:7; Luke 24:33; Mark 16:8–10"> So the woman left her water jar and went away into the town and said to the people,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Assuming the term is used here in a generic sense to refer to persons of either gender, it should be translated “people”; if instead the term here refers only to the town leaders or elders who met at the town gate, then “men” would be appropriate">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 22:17; 1 Corinthians 14:24–25; John 4:25"> “Come, see a man who told me everything I have ever done! Perhaps this one is the Christ?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 5:20; Acts 8:5–8; Luke 17:16–18"> They went out from the town and were coming to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:38; Genesis 24:33; Acts 16:30–34"> In the meanwhile the disciples were asking him, saying, “Rabbi, eat something!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 23:12; Psalm 119:103; John 4:34"> But he said to them, “I have food to eat that you do not know about.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 16:6–11; Luke 9:45"> So the disciples began to say to one another, “No one brought him anything to eat, did they?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 6:38; John 5:30; Job 23:12"> Jesus said to them, “My food is that I do the will of the one who sent me and complete his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 10:2–3; Matthew 9:37–38; John 4:30"> Do you not say, ‘There are yet four months and the harvest comes’? Behold, I say to you, lift up your eyes and look at the fields, that they are white for harvest already.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some interpreters and Bible translations place the word “already” at the beginning of the next verse: “Already the one who reaps receives wages …”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Romans 1:13; 1 Corinthians 3:5–9"> The one who reaps receives wages and gathers fruit for eternal life, in order that the one who sows and the one who reaps can rejoice together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Micah 6:15; Job 31:8; Luke 19:21"> For in this instance the saying is true, ‘It is one who sows and another who reaps.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 1:7; 2 Chronicles 36:15; Acts 10:42–43"> I sent you to reap what you did not work for; others have worked, and you have entered into their work.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Samaritans and the Savior of the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 4:29; John 4:5; John 10:41–42"> Now from that town many of the Samaritans believed in him because of the word of the woman who testified, “He told me everything that I have done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Acts 16:15; Luke 24:29"> So when the Samaritans came to him, they began asking him to stay with them. And he stayed there two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4–5; Acts 1:8; Acts 8:12"> And many more believed because of his word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 1:29; 1 John 5:20"> And they were saying to the woman, “No longer because of ⸤what you said⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “your speaking”">c</a></sup>do we believe, for we ourselves have heard, and we know that this one is truly the Savior of the world!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Return to Galilee

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Romans 15:8; Mark 7:27–28"> And after the two days he departed from there into Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 13:57; Luke 4:24; Mark 6:4"> For Jesus himself testified that a prophet has no honor in his own homeland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 2:13–16; Deuteronomy 16:16"> So when he came to Galilee, the Galileans welcomed him, because they had seen all the things he had done in Jerusalem at the feast (for they themselves had also come to the feast).</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Royal Official’s Son Is Healed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 2:1–11; Luke 7:2; Hosea 5:15"> Now he came again to Cana in Galilee, where he had made the water wine. And there was at Capernaum a certain royal official whose son was sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 4:3; John 11:21; John 11:32"> This man, when he heard that Jesus had come from Judea into Galilee, went to him and asked that he come down and heal his son, for he was about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 20:29; Hebrews 2:4; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> So Jesus said to him, “Unless you people see signs and wonders, you will never believe!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Mark 5:23; Psalm 88:10–12; Psalm 40:17"> The royal official said to him, “Sir, come down before my child dies!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 8:13; Mark 7:29–30; Romans 4:20–21"> Jesus said to him, “Go, your son will live.” The man believed the word that Jesus spoke to him, and he departed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="1 Kings 17:23; John 4:50; John 4:53"> Now as he was going down, his slaves met him, saying that his child was alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> So he inquired from them the hour at which he had gotten better. Then they said to him, “Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalm 107:20; Luke 19:9; Acts 16:34"> So the father knew that it was that<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “that it was at that same hour”">d</a></sup>same hour at which Jesus said to him, “Your son will live,” and he himself believed, and his whole household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 4:45; John 2:1–11"> Now this is again a second sign Jesus performed when he came from Judea into Galilee.</span>
</div>
</div>
