## Lazarus Dies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> Now a certain man was sick, Lazarus from Bethany, the village of Mary and her sister Martha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> (Now it was Mary who anointed the Lord with perfumed oil and wiped his feet with her hair, whose brother Lazarus was sick.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> So the sisters sent word to him, saying, “Lord, behold, the one whom you love is sick.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> And when he heard it, Jesus said, “This sickness is not to death, but for the glory of God, in order that the Son of God may be glorified through it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> (Now Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> So when he heard that he was sick, then he remained in the place ⸤where⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Literally “in which”">a</a></sup>he was two days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> The disciples said to him, “Rabbi, the Jews were seeking just now to stone you, and are you going there again?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus replied, Are there not twelve hours in the day? If anyone walks around in the daylight, he does not stumble, because he sees the light of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> But if anyone walks around in the night, he stumbles, because the light is not in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> He said these things, and after this he said to them, “Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep, but I am going so that I can awaken him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So the disciples said to him, “Lord, if he has fallen asleep, he will get well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> (Now Jesus had been speaking about his death, but they thought that he was speaking about ⸤real sleep⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Literally “the sleep of slumber”">b</a></sup>)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> So Jesus then said to them plainly, “Lazarus has died,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> and I am glad ⸤for your sake⸥<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Literally “for the sake of you”">c</a></sup>that I was not there, so that you may believe. But let us go to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> Then Thomas (the one who is called Didymus)<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Didymus” means “the twin” in Greek">d</a></sup>said to his fellow disciples, “Let us go also, so that we may die with him.”</span>

## Jesus the Resurrection and the Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> So when he arrived, Jesus found he had already been four days in the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> (Now Bethany was near Jerusalem, about fifteen stadia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A “stade” or “stadium” (plur. “stadia”) is about 607 ft (187 m), so this was just under two miles (3 km)">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> So many of the Jews came to Martha and Mary in order to console them concerning their brother.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> Now Martha, when she heard that Jesus was coming, went to meet him, but Mary was sitting in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> So Martha said to Jesus, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> Even<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Some manuscripts have “But even”">f</a></sup>now I know that whatever you ask God, God will grant you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus said to her, “Your brother will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha said to him, “I know that he will rise again in the resurrection at the last day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said to her, “I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me, even if he dies, will live,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> and everyone who lives and believes in me will never die ⸤forever⸥.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Literally “for the age”">g</a></sup>Do you believe this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> She said to him, “Yes, Lord, I have believed that you are the Christ, the Son of God, who comes into the world.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Weeps

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> And when she had said this, she went and called her sister Mary privately, saying, “The Teacher is here and is calling for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalm 27:8; Song of Solomon 3:1–4"> So that one, when she heard it, got up quickly and went to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> (Now Jesus has not yet come into the village, but was still in the place where Martha went to meet him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> So the Jews who were with her in the house and were consoling her, when they saw Mary—that she stood up quickly and went out—followed her, because they thought that she was going to the tomb in order to weep there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> Then Mary, when she came where Jesus was and saw him, fell at his feet, saying to him, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> Then Jesus, when he saw her weeping and the Jews who came with her weeping, was deeply moved in spirit and was troubled within himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> And he said, “Where have you laid him?” They said to him, “Lord, come and see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Jesus wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> So the Jews were saying, “See how he loved him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> But some of them said, “Was not this man who opened the eyes of the blind able to do something so that this man also would not have died?”</span>
</div>

## Lazarus Is Raised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Then Jesus, deeply moved within himself again, came to the tomb. Now it was a cave, and a stone was lying on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalm 49:7"> Jesus said, “Take away the stone.” Martha, the sister of the one who had died, said to him, “Lord, he is stinking already, because it has been four days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus said to her, “Did I not say to you that if you believed, you would see the glory of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalm 123:1"> So they took away the stone. And Jesus lifted up his eyes above and said, “Father, I give thanks to you that you hear me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> And I know that you always hear me, but for the sake of the crowd standing around I said it, so that they may believe that you sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> And when he had said these things, he cried out with a loud voice, “Lazarus, come out!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> The one who had died came out, his feet and his hands bound with strips of cloth, and his face wrapped with a facecloth. Jesus said to them, “Untie him and let him go.”</span>

## The Jewish Leaders Plot to Kill Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Then many of the Jews who had come with Mary and saw the things which he did believed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But some of them went to the Pharisees and told them the things which Jesus had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> So the chief priests and the Pharisees called together the Sanhedrin and said, “What are we doing? For this man is performing many signs!</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we allow him to go on in this way, everyone will believe in him, and the Romans will come and take away both our place<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Generally understood to be a reference to the Jerusalem temple">h</a></sup>and our<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Literally “both the place and the nation of us”; the possessive pronoun is repeated in the translation (rather than the article) in keeping with English style">i</a></sup>nation.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> But a certain one of them, Caiaphas (who was high priest in that year), said to them, “You do not know anything at all!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> Nor do you consider that it is profitable for you that one man should die for the people, and the whole nation not perish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> (Now he did not say this from himself, but being high priest in that year, he prophesied that Jesus was going to die for the nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> and not for the nation only, but also that the children of God who are scattered would be gathered into one.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> So from that day they resolved that they should kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> So Jesus was no longer walking openly among the Jews, but went away from there to the region near the wilderness, to a city called Ephraim, and there he stayed with the disciples.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> Now the Passover of the Jews was near, and many went up to Jerusalem from the surrounding country before the Passover, so that they could purify themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> So they were looking for Jesus, and were speaking with one another while standing in the temple courts, “What do you think? That he will not come to the feast?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> (Now the chief priests and the Pharisees had given orders that if anyone knew where he was, they should report it, in order that they could arrest him.)</span>
</div>
