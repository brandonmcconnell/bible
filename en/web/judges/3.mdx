<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 2:21–22; Deuteronomy 7:22; Proverbs 17:3"> Now these are the nations which the LORD left, to test Israel by them, even as many as had not known all the wars of Canaan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 3:5; Genesis 3:7; 1 Corinthians 9:26–27"> only that the generations of the children of Israel might know, to teach them war, at least those who knew nothing of it before:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joshua 13:3; Joshua 13:5; Judges 10:7"> the five lords of the Philistines, all the Canaanites, the Sidonians, and the Hivites who lived on Mount Lebanon, from Mount Baal Hermon to the entrance of Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 3:1; Judges 2:22; Exodus 15:25"> They were left to test Israel by them, to know whether they would listen to the LORD’s commandments, which he commanded their fathers by Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–38; Genesis 10:15–18; Exodus 3:8"> The children of Israel lived among the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Exodus 34:16; 1 Kings 11:1–5"> They took their daughters to be their wives, and gave their own daughters to their sons and served their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; 2 Kings 23:6; 1 Kings 18:19"> The children of Israel did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, and forgot the LORD their God, and served the Baals and the Asheroth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 2:14; Habakkuk 3:7; Judges 4:9"> Therefore the LORD’s anger burned against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of Cushan Rishathaim king of Mesopotamia; and the children of Israel served Cushan Rishathaim eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Judges 1:13; Psalms 22:5"> When the children of Israel cried to the LORD, the LORD raised up a savior to the children of Israel, who saved them, even Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 14:19"> The LORD’s Spirit came on him, and he judged Israel; and he went out to war, and the LORD delivered Cushan Rishathaim king of Mesopotamia into his hand. His hand prevailed against Cushan Rishathaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 8:28; Joshua 11:23"> The land had rest forty years, then Othniel the son of Kenaz died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:9; Judges 2:19; Judges 2:14"> The children of Israel again did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, and the LORD strengthened Eglon the king of Moab against Israel, because they had done that which was evil in the LORD’s sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 34:3; Judges 5:14"> He gathered the children of Ammon and Amalek to himself; and he went and struck Israel, and they possessed the city of palm trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47–48; Deuteronomy 28:40; Leviticus 26:23–25"> The children of Israel served Eglon the king of Moab eighteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Psalms 78:34; Isaiah 36:16"> But when the children of Israel cried to the LORD, the LORD raised up a savior for them: Ehud the son of Gera, the Benjamite, a left-handed man. The children of Israel sent tribute by him to Eglon the king of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Song of Solomon 3:8; Revelation 2:12; Psalms 149:6"> Ehud made himself a sword which had two edges, a cubit<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimeters.">a</a></sup>in length; and he wore it under his clothing on his right thigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:20; Job 15:27; Jeremiah 50:11"> He offered the tribute to Eglon king of Moab. Now Eglon was a very fat man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When Ehud had finished offering the tribute, he sent away the people who carried the tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 4:20; Judges 3:20; Acts 23:18–19"> But he himself turned back from the stone idols that were by Gilgal, and said, “I have a secret message for you, O king.” The king said, “Keep silence!” All who stood by him left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:7; Amos 3:15; Psalms 29:1"> Ehud came to him; and he was sitting by himself alone in the cool upper room. Ehud said, “I have a message from God to you.” He arose out of his seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Job 20:25; 1 Samuel 15:33"> Ehud put out his left hand, and took the sword from his right thigh, and thrust it into his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The handle also went in after the blade; and the fat closed on the blade, for he didn’t draw the sword out of his body; and it came out behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then Ehud went out onto the porch, and shut the doors of the upper room on him, and locked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:3"> After he had gone, his servants came and saw that the doors of the upper room were locked. They said, “Surely he is covering his feet<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, “relieving himself”.">b</a></sup>in the upper room.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They waited until they were ashamed; and behold, he didn’t open the doors of the upper room. Therefore they took the key and opened them, and behold, their lord had fallen down dead on the floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Ehud escaped while they waited, passed beyond the stone idols, and escaped to Seirah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 6:34; 1 Samuel 13:3; Judges 5:14"> When he had come, he blew a trumpet in the hill country of Ephraim; and the children of Israel went down with him from the hill country, and he led them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 12:5; Judges 7:9; Judges 7:15"> He said to them, “Follow me; for the LORD has delivered your enemies the Moabites into your hand.” They followed him, and took the fords of the Jordan against the Moabites, and didn’t allow any man to pass over.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Judges 3:17; Psalms 17:10"> They struck at that time about ten thousand men of Moab, every strong man and every man of valor. No man escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 3:11; Judges 5:31"> So Moab was subdued that day under the hand of Israel. Then the land had rest eighty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 5:6; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Samuel 17:50"> After him was Shamgar the son of Anath, who struck six hundred men of the Philistines with an ox goad. He also saved Israel.</span>
