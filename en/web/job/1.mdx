<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 5:11; Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20"> There was a man in the land of Uz, whose name was Job. That man was blameless and upright, and one who feared God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).">a</a></sup>and turned away from evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 127:3–5; Job 42:13; Psalms 128:3"> There were born to him seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 29:25; Job 42:12; Judges 6:3"> His possessions also were seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred yoke of oxen, five hundred female donkeys, and a very great household; so that this man was the greatest of all the children of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; Psalms 133:1"> His sons went and held a feast in the house of each one on his birthday; and they sent and called for their three sisters to eat and to drink with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 42:8; Genesis 8:20; 1 Samuel 16:5"> It was so, when the days of their feasting had run their course, that Job sent and sanctified them, and rose up early in the morning, and offered burnt offerings according to the number of them all. For Job said, “It may be that my sons have sinned, and renounced God in their hearts.” Job did so continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 2:1; Job 38:7; Zechariah 3:1"> Now on the day when God’s sons came to present themselves before the LORD,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name (Hebrew “יהוה”, usually pronounced Yahweh).">b</a></sup>Satan also came among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Revelation 12:12–17; Matthew 12:43"> The LORD said to Satan, “Where have you come from?” Then Satan answered the LORD, and said, “From going back and forth in the earth, and from walking up and down in it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:3; Job 1:1; Numbers 12:7–8"> The LORD said to Satan, “Have you considered my servant, Job? For there is no one like him in the earth, a blameless and an upright man, one who fears God, and turns away from evil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 16:26; 1 Timothy 4:8; Job 2:10"> Then Satan answered the LORD, and said, “Does Job fear God for nothing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:7; Zechariah 2:5; 1 Peter 1:5"> Haven’t you made a hedge around him, and around his house, and around all that he has, on every side? You have blessed the work of his hands, and his substance is increased in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 2:5; Job 19:21; Revelation 16:11"> But stretch out your hand now, and touch all that he has, and he will renounce you to your face.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 22:31–32; John 3:35–36; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> The LORD said to Satan, “Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">c</a></sup>all that he has is in your power. Only on himself don’t stretch out your hand.” So Satan went out from the presence of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 21:34; Proverbs 27:1; Job 1:4"> It fell on a day when his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:13; Jeremiah 51:31; 1 Samuel 4:17"> that a messenger came to Job, and said, “The oxen were plowing, and the donkeys feeding beside them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Job 6:19; Isaiah 45:14"> and the Sabeans attacked, and took them away. Yes, they have killed the servants with the edge of the sword, and I alone have escaped to tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:12; Genesis 19:24; 1 Kings 18:38"> While he was still speaking, another also came and said, “The fire of God has fallen from the sky, and has burned up the sheep and the servants, and consumed them, and I alone have escaped to tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 11:28; Habakkuk 1:6; Genesis 11:31"> While he was still speaking, another also came and said, “The Chaldeans made three bands, and swept down on the camels, and have taken them away, yes, and killed the servants with the edge of the sword; and I alone have escaped to tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 1:4; Job 1:13; Job 27:14"> While he was still speaking, there came also another, and said, “Your sons and your daughters were eating and drinking wine in their oldest brother’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 42:36; Luke 13:1–5; Judges 16:30"> and behold, there came a great wind from the wilderness, and struck the four corners of the house, and it fell on the young men, and they are dead. I alone have escaped to tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 5:6; 2 Samuel 12:16–20; Genesis 37:34"> Then Job arose, and tore his robe, and shaved his head, and fell down on the ground, and worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:15; 1 Timothy 6:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:18"> He said, “Naked I came out of my mother’s womb, and naked will I return there. The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away. Blessed be the LORD’s name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 1:12; Job 2:10; 1 Peter 1:7"> In all this, Job didn’t sin, nor charge God with wrongdoing.</span>
