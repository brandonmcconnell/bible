<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Haggai 1:11; 2 Kings 4:31–35; Ruth 1:1"> Now Elisha had spoken to the woman whose son he had restored to life, saying, “Arise, and go, you and your household, and stay for a while wherever you can; for the LORD has called for a famine. It will also come on the land for seven years.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; Judges 3:3; 1 Samuel 27:1–3"> The woman arose, and did according to the man of God’s word. She went with her household, and lived in the land of the Philistines for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:16; Luke 18:3–5; 2 Kings 4:13"> At the end of seven years, the woman returned from the land of the Philistines. Then she went out to beg the king for her house and for her land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 5:20–27; 2 Kings 2:14; 2 Kings 6:32"> Now the king was talking with Gehazi the servant of the man of God, saying, “Please tell me all the great things that Elisha has done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 4:35; Matthew 10:29–30; 2 Kings 6:26"> As he was telling the king how he had restored to life him who was dead, behold, the woman whose son he had restored to life begged the king for her house and for her land. Gehazi said, “My lord, O king, this is the woman, and this is her son, whom Elisha restored to life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:7; Proverbs 21:1; 2 Samuel 9:7"> When the king asked the woman, she told him. So the king appointed to her a certain officer, saying, “Restore all that was hers, and all the fruits of the field since the day that she left the land, even until now.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 11:24; 2 Kings 6:24; 1 Kings 20:1"> Elisha came to Damascus; and Benhadad the king of Syria was sick. He was told, “The man of God has come here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 19:15; 2 Kings 1:2; 1 Samuel 9:7"> The king said to Hazael, “Take a present in your hand, and go meet the man of God, and inquire of the LORD by him, saying, ‘Will I recover from this sickness?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; 2 Kings 13:14; 1 Kings 19:15"> So Hazael went to meet him and took a present with him, even of every good thing of Damascus, forty camels’ burden, and came and stood before him and said, “Your son Benhadad king of Syria has sent me to you, saying, ‘Will I recover from this sickness?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 7:7; Genesis 2:17; Amos 7:1"> Elisha said to him, “Go, tell him, ‘You will surely recover;’ however the LORD has shown me that he will surely die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Romans 9:2; Jeremiah 9:18"> He settled his gaze steadfastly on him, until he was ashamed. Then the man of God wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Nahum 3:10; 2 Kings 15:16"> Hazael said, “Why do you weep, my lord?” He answered, “Because I know the evil that you will do to the children of Israel. You will set their strongholds on fire, and you will kill their young men with the sword, and will dash their little ones in pieces, and rip up their pregnant women.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:43; 1 Kings 19:15; 2 Samuel 9:8"> Hazael said, “But what is your servant, who is but a dog, that he could do this great thing?” Elisha answered, “The LORD has shown me that you will be king over Syria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 8:10; Matthew 26:16; 2 Kings 5:25"> Then he departed from Elisha, and came to his master, who said to him, “What did Elisha say to you?” He answered, “He told me that you would surely recover.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 8:13; 1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 11:26–37"> On the next day, he took a thick cloth, dipped it in water, and spread it on the king’s face, so that he died. Then Hazael reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 2 Kings 3:1; 2 Chronicles 21:1–20"> In the fifth year of Joram the son of Ahab king of Israel, Jehoshaphat being king of Judah then, Jehoram the son of Jehoshaphat king of Judah began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:5–10"> He was thirty-two years old when he began to reign. He reigned eight years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:26; 1 Kings 11:1–5; 2 Chronicles 21:13"> He walked in the way of the kings of Israel, as did Ahab’s house, for he married Ahab’s daughter. He did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 2 Samuel 7:12–13; Luke 1:32–33"> However, the LORD would not destroy Judah, for David his servant’s sake, as he promised him to give to him a lamp for his children always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:47; 2 Kings 3:9; 2 Kings 8:22"> In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah, and made a king over themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:17"> Then Joram crossed over to Zair, and all his chariots with him; and he rose up by night and struck the Edomites who surrounded him with the captains of the chariots; and the people fled to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:8; Joshua 21:13; Genesis 27:40"> So Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah to this day. Then Libnah revolted at the same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 15:36; 1 Kings 11:41; 2 Chronicles 21:11–20"> The rest of the acts of Joram, and all that he did, aren’t they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:6; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Chronicles 21:1"> Joram slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in David’s city; and Ahaziah his son reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 9:29; 2 Kings 8:16–17; 2 Chronicles 21:20–6"> In the twelfth year of Joram the son of Ahab king of Israel, Ahaziah the son of Jehoram king of Judah began to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:2; 2 Kings 9:21–27; 2 Kings 11:1"> Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he began to reign; and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Athaliah the daughter of Omri king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:3–4; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> He walked in the way of Ahab’s house and did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, as did Ahab’s house, for he was the son-in-law of Ahab’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:5; 1 Kings 4:13; 2 Chronicles 19:2"> He went with Joram the son of Ahab to war against Hazael king of Syria at Ramoth Gilead, and the Syrians wounded Joram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 9:15–16; 2 Kings 8:28; 2 Chronicles 22:5–7"> King Joram returned to be healed in Jezreel from the wounds which the Syrians had given him at Ramah, when he fought against Hazael king of Syria. Ahaziah the son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to see Joram the son of Ahab in Jezreel, because he was sick.</span>
