<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:1; 2 Kings 4:29; 1 Kings 1:39"> Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets, and said to him, “Put your belt on your waist, take this vial of oil in your hand, and go to Ramoth Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 9:5; 1 Kings 19:16–17; 2 Kings 9:11"> When you come there, find Jehu the son of Jehoshaphat the son of Nimshi, and go in and make him rise up from among his brothers, and take him to an inner room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:16; Exodus 29:7; 1 Samuel 16:2"> Then take the vial of oil, and pour it on his head, and say, ‘The LORD says, “I have anointed you king over Israel.”’ Then open the door, flee, and don’t wait.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So the young man, the young prophet, went to Ramoth Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 3:19"> When he came, behold, the captains of the army were sitting. Then he said, “I have a message for you, captain.” Jehu said, “To which one of us?” He said, “To you, O captain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:7; 1 Kings 19:16; 2 Kings 9:3"> He arose, and went into the house. Then he poured the oil on his head, and said to him, “The LORD, the God of Israel, says, ‘I have anointed you king over the people of the LORD, even over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:15; 1 Kings 18:4; Deuteronomy 32:43"> You must strike your master Ahab’s house, that I may avenge the blood of my servants the prophets, and the blood of all the servants of the LORD, at the hand of Jezebel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:22; Deuteronomy 32:36; 2 Kings 14:26"> For the whole house of Ahab will perish. I will cut off from Ahab everyone who urinates against a wall,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, male">a</a></sup>both him who is shut up and him who is left at large in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 15:29; 1 Kings 21:22; 1 Kings 14:10–11"> I will make Ahab’s house like the house of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha the son of Ahijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 21:23; 2 Kings 9:35–36; 2 Kings 9:3"> The dogs will eat Jezebel on the plot of ground of Jezreel, and there shall be no one to bury her.’” Then he opened the door and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:26; Acts 26:24; Hosea 9:7"> When Jehu came out to the servants of his lord and one said to him, “Is all well? Why did this madman come to you?” He said to them, “You know the man and how he talks.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> They said, “That is a lie. Tell us now.” He said, “He said to me, ‘The LORD says, I have anointed you king over Israel.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; 1 Kings 1:39; 1 Kings 1:34"> Then they hurried, and each man took his cloak, and put it under him on the top of the stairs, and blew the trumpet, saying, “Jehu is king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 8:28; 1 Kings 22:3; 1 Kings 16:16"> So Jehu the son of Jehoshaphat the son of Nimshi conspired against Joram. (Now Joram was defending Ramoth Gilead, he and all Israel, because of Hazael king of Syria;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 8:29; 1 Samuel 27:9–11; 2 Chronicles 22:6"> but King Joram had returned to be healed in Jezreel of the wounds which the Syrians had given him when he fought with Hazael king of Syria.) Jehu said, “If this is your thinking, then let no one escape and go out of the city to go to tell it in Jezreel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:6–7; 2 Kings 8:28–29"> So Jehu rode in a chariot and went to Jezreel, for Joram lay there. Ahaziah king of Judah had come down to see Joram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:4; Ezekiel 33:2–9; 2 Kings 7:14"> Now the watchman was standing on the tower in Jezreel, and he spied the company of Jehu as he came, and said, “I see a company.” Joram said, “Take a horseman, and send to meet them, and let him say, ‘Is it peace?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 9:19; 2 Kings 9:22; Jeremiah 16:5"> So one went on horseback to meet him, and said, “the king says, ‘Is it peace?’” Jehu said, “What do you have to do with peace? Fall in behind me!” The watchman said, “The messenger came to them, but he isn’t coming back.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then he sent out a second on horseback, who came to them and said, “The king says, ‘Is it peace?’” Jehu answered, “What do you have to do with peace? Fall in behind me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:27; 1 Kings 19:17; Ecclesiastes 9:10"> The watchman said, “He came to them, and isn’t coming back. The driving is like the driving of Jehu the son of Nimshi, for he drives furiously.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 21:1–7; 2 Chronicles 22:7; Micah 1:13"> Joram said, “Get ready!” They got his chariot ready. Then Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah went out, each in his chariot; and they went out to meet Jehu, and found him on Naboth the Jezreelite’s land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:13; Nahum 3:4; 1 Kings 16:30–33"> When Joram saw Jehu, he said, “Is it peace, Jehu?” He answered, “What peace, so long as the prostitution of your mother Jezebel and her witchcraft abound?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 11:14; 2 Chronicles 23:13"> Joram turned his hands and fled, and said to Ahaziah, “This is treason, Ahaziah!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 22:34; Proverbs 21:30; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> Jehu drew his bow with his full strength, and struck Joram between his arms; and the arrow went out at his heart, and he sunk down in his chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; 1 Kings 21:24–29; Isaiah 13:1"> Then Jehu said to Bidkar his captain, “Pick him up, and throw him in the plot of the field of Naboth the Jezreelite; for remember how, when you and I rode together after Ahab his father, the LORD laid this burden on him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 21:19; Deuteronomy 5:9; 2 Chronicles 25:4"> ‘Surely I have seen yesterday the blood of Naboth, and the blood of his sons,’ says the LORD; ‘and I will repay you in this plot of ground,’ says the LORD. Now therefore take and cast him onto the plot of ground, according to the LORD’s word.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 1:27; Joshua 17:11; 2 Kings 8:29"> But when Ahaziah the king of Judah saw this, he fled by the way of the garden house. Jehu followed after him, and said, “Strike him also in the chariot!” They struck him at the ascent of Gur, which is by Ibleam. He fled to Megiddo, and died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:24; 2 Kings 23:30; 2 Kings 12:21"> His servants carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem, and buried him in his tomb with his fathers in David’s city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:18–19; 2 Chronicles 22:1–2; 2 Kings 8:24–25"> In the eleventh year of Joram the son of Ahab, Ahaziah began to reign over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:30; Ezekiel 23:40; Ezekiel 24:17"> When Jehu had come to Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it; and she painted her eyes, and adorned her head, and looked out at the window.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 16:9–20"> As Jehu entered in at the gate, she said, “Do you come in peace, Zimri, you murderer of your master?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Esther 2:15; Exodus 32:26; 2 Chronicles 11:12"> He lifted up his face to the window, and said, “Who is on my side? Who?” Two or three eunuchs looked out at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Micah 7:10; Lamentations 1:15; Isaiah 25:10"> He said, “Throw her down!” So they threw her down; and some of her blood was sprinkled on the wall, and on the horses. Then he trampled her under foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 16:31; 1 Kings 21:25; Isaiah 65:15"> When he had come in, he ate and drank. Then he said, “See now to this cursed woman, and bury her; for she is a king’s daughter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 12:23; Isaiah 14:18–20; Job 31:3"> They went to bury her, but they found no more of her than the skull, the feet, and the palms of her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 21:23; Leviticus 8:36; 2 Kings 14:25"> Therefore they came back, and told him. He said, “This is the LORD’s word, which he spoke by his servant Elijah the Tishbite, saying, ‘The dogs will eat the flesh of Jezebel on the plot of Jezreel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:2; Psalms 83:10; Jeremiah 16:4"> and the body of Jezebel will be as dung on the surface of the field on Jezreel’s land, so that they won’t say, “This is Jezebel.”’”</span>
