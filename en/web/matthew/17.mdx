<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:44; Mark 9:4; Malachi 4:5"> Behold, Moses and Elijah appeared to them talking with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 9:5–6; Luke 9:33; John 14:8–9"> Peter answered and said to Jesus, “Lord, it is good for us to be here. If you want, let’s make three tents here: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:17; Matthew 12:18; Deuteronomy 18:15"> While he was still speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them. Behold, a voice came out of the cloud, saying, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. Listen to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 26:14; 2 Peter 1:18; Daniel 8:17"> When the disciples heard it, they fell on their faces, and were very afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 10:18; Matthew 14:27; Daniel 8:18"> Jesus came and touched them and said,<span className="woj">“Get up, and don’t be afraid.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:36; Mark 9:8; Acts 12:10–11"> Lifting up their eyes, they saw no one, except Jesus alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 8:30; Mark 9:9–13; Matthew 17:23"> As they were coming down from the mountain, Jesus commanded them, saying,<span className="woj">“Don’t tell anyone what you saw, until the Son of Man has risen from the dead.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; John 1:21"> His disciples asked him, saying, “Then why do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 1:16–17; Malachi 4:6"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Elijah indeed comes first, and will restore all things;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3–12; Matthew 16:21; Acts 13:24–28"> <span className="woj">but I tell you that Elijah has come already, and they didn’t recognize him, but did to him whatever they wanted to. Even so the Son of Man will also suffer by them.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 11:14"> Then the disciples understood that he spoke to them of John the Baptizer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 9:14–29; Mark 1:40; Acts 10:25–26"> When they came to the multitude, a man came to him, kneeling down to him and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Job 1:10–19; Mark 9:20–22"> “Lord, have mercy on my son, for he is epileptic and suffers grievously; for he often falls into the fire, and often into the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 17:19–20; Luke 9:40; Acts 19:15–16"> So I brought him to your disciples, and they could not cure him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Matthew 6:30; Mark 16:14"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“Faithless and perverse generation! How long will I be with you? How long will I bear with you? Bring him here to me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Mark 1:34; Mark 5:8"> Jesus rebuked the demon, and it went out of him, and the boy was cured from that hour.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:6; Mark 11:23; Luke 1:37"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Because of your unbelief. <span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="woj">But this kind doesn’t go out except by prayer and fasting.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU omits verse 21.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 18:31–34; Matthew 17:23"> While they were staying in Galilee, Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“The Son of Man is about to be delivered up into the hands of men,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Isaiah 53:10–12; Mark 8:31"> <span className="woj">and they will kill him, and the third day he will be raised up.”</span>They were exceedingly sorry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 38:26; Exodus 30:13; Mark 9:33"> When they had come to Capernaum, those who collected the didrachma coins<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A didrachma is a Greek silver coin worth 2 drachmas, about as much as 2 Roman denarii, or about 2 days’ wages. A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">c</a></sup>came to Peter, and said, “Doesn’t your teacher pay the didrachma?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 22:21; Romans 13:6–7; Matthew 22:17"> He said, “Yes.” When he came into the house, Jesus anticipated him, saying,<span className="woj">“What do you think, Simon? From whom do the kings of the earth receive toll or tribute? From their children, or from strangers?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Peter said to him, “From strangers.” Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Therefore the children are exempt.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; 1 Corinthians 8:9; 1 Corinthians 9:19–22"> <span className="woj">But, lest we cause them to stumble, go to the sea, cast a hook, and take up the first fish that comes up. When you have opened its mouth, you will find a stater coin.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A stater is a silver coin equivalent to four Attic or two Alexandrian drachmas, or a Jewish shekel: just exactly enough to cover the half-shekel temple tax for two people.
