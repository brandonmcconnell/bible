<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 61:1–3; Acts 10:38; Luke 8:1"> When Jesus had finished directing his twelve disciples, he departed from there to teach and preach in their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 6:17; Matthew 14:3; Matthew 9:14"> Now when John heard in the prison the works of Christ, he sent two of his disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:27; Malachi 3:1; Deuteronomy 18:15–18"> and said to him, “Are you he who comes, or should we look for another?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Go and tell John the things which you hear and see:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> <span className="woj">the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear,</span><span className="woj">the dead are raised up, and the poor have good news preached to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14–15; 1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 26:31"> <span className="woj">Blessed is he who finds no occasion for stumbling in me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 1:6; John 5:35; Mark 1:3–5"> As these went their way, Jesus began to say to the multitudes concerning John,<span className="woj">“What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken by the wind?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; 2 Kings 1:8; Zechariah 13:4"> <span className="woj">But what did you go out to see? A man in soft clothing? Behold, those who wear soft clothing are in kings’ houses.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 1:76; Matthew 14:5; Matthew 21:24–26"> <span className="woj">But why did you go out? To see a prophet? Yes, I tell you, and much more than a prophet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3; Malachi 4:5"> <span className="woj">For this is he, of whom it is written, ‘Behold, I send my messenger before your face, who will prepare your way before you.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 7:28; Ephesians 3:8; Matthew 3:11"> <span className="woj">Most certainly I tell you, among those who are born of women there has not arisen anyone greater than John the Baptizer; yet he who is least in the Kingdom of Heaven is greater than he.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 16:16; Luke 13:24; Ephesians 6:11–13"> <span className="woj">From the days of John the Baptizer until now, the Kingdom of Heaven suffers violence, and the violent take it by force.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, plunder it.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:17–18; Romans 3:21; Acts 13:27"> <span className="woj">For all the prophets and the law prophesied until John.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Matthew 17:10–13; Mark 9:11–13"> <span className="woj">If you are willing to receive it, this is Elijah, who is to come.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:8; Matthew 13:9; Mark 4:23"> <span className="woj">He who has ears to hear, let him hear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 7:31–35; Matthew 12:34; Matthew 23:36"> <span className="woj">“But to what shall I compare this generation? It is like children sitting in the marketplaces, who call to their companions</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 28:9–13; Isaiah 30:29; Jeremiah 9:17–20"> <span className="woj">and say, ‘We played the flute for you, and you didn’t dance. We mourned for you, and you didn’t lament.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Matthew 3:4; John 10:20"> <span className="woj">For John came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, ‘He has a demon.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 9:10–11; Luke 15:1–2; Luke 19:7"> <span className="woj">The Son of Man came eating and drinking, and they say, ‘Behold, a gluttonous man and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’ But wisdom is justified by her children.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU reads “actions” instead of “children”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:13–15; Jeremiah 8:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26"> Then he began to denounce the cities in which most of his mighty works had been done, because they didn’t repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:6–7; Acts 12:20; Luke 9:10"> <span className="woj">“Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the mighty works had been done in Tyre and Sidon which were done in you, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 11:24; Matthew 10:15; Hebrews 10:26–31"> <span className="woj">But I tell you, it will be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon on the day of judgment than for you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; John 4:46–54; Genesis 13:13"> <span className="woj">You, Capernaum, who are exalted to heaven, you will go down to Hades.</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, Hell">c</a></sup><span className="woj">For if the mighty works had been done in Sodom which were done in you, it would have remained until today.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:22; Mark 6:11"> <span className="woj">But I tell you that it will be more tolerable for the land of Sodom on the day of judgment, than for you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 21:16; Psalms 8:2; Matthew 16:17"> At that time, Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“I thank you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that you hid these things from the wise and understanding, and revealed them to infants.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Job 33:13"> <span className="woj">Yes, Father, for so it was well-pleasing in your sight.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 14:6–9; Matthew 28:18; Luke 10:22"> <span className="woj">All things have been delivered to me by my Father. No one knows the Son, except the Father; neither does anyone know the Father, except the Son and he to whom the Son desires to reveal him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 11:29; John 7:37; John 6:37"> <span className="woj">“Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:16; 1 John 2:6; Philippians 2:7–8"> <span className="woj">Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:3; John 16:33; Philippians 4:13"> <span className="woj">For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.”</span></span>
