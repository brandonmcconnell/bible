<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:19; Acts 16:1; 1 Thessalonians 1:1–10"> Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the assembly of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 1:8"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Peter 3:18; 2 Peter 1:5–10; Proverbs 4:18"> We are bound to always give thanks to God for you, brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">a</a></sup>even as it is appropriate, because your faith grows exceedingly, and the love of each and every one of you toward one another abounds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:36; James 5:7–8; Hebrews 12:1–3"> so that we ourselves boast about you in the assemblies of God for your perseverance and faith in all your persecutions and in the afflictions which you endure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:28; Acts 14:22; Luke 20:35"> This is an obvious sign of the righteous judgment of God, to the end that you may be counted worthy of God’s Kingdom, for which you also suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 2:8; Colossians 3:25; Psalms 94:20–23"> For it is a righteous thing with God to repay affliction to those who afflict you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Matthew 16:27; Jude 1:14–15"> and to give relief to you who are afflicted with us when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with his mighty angels in flaming fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17; 2 Peter 3:7; Hebrews 10:27"> punishing those who don’t know God, and to those who don’t obey the Good News of our Lord Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Revelation 14:10–11; Matthew 25:46"> who will pay the penalty: eternal destruction from the face of the Lord and from the glory of his might,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 1:18; 1 Corinthians 3:13; John 17:10"> when he comes in that day to be glorified in his saints and to be admired among all those who have believed, because our testimony to you was believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 2:13; Colossians 1:9–13; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> To this end we also pray always for you that our God may count you worthy of your calling, and fulfill every desire of goodness and work of faith with power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:10; 1 Peter 4:14; John 17:10"> that the name of our Lord Jesus<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR adds “Christ”">b</a></sup>may be glorified in you, and you in him, according to the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
