<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; 2 Corinthians 11:14; Revelation 12:9"> Now the serpent was more subtle than any animal of the field which the LORD God had made. He said to the woman, “Has God really said, ‘You shall not eat of any tree of the garden’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 58:4"> The woman said to the serpent, “We may eat fruit from the trees of the garden,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 2:16–17; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Exodus 19:12–13"> but not the fruit of the tree which is in the middle of the garden. God has said, ‘You shall not eat of it. You shall not touch it, lest you die.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14"> The serpent said to the woman, “You won’t really die,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> for God knows that in the day you eat it, your eyes will be opened, and you will be like God, knowing good and evil.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 2:16; James 1:14–15; 1 Timothy 2:14"> When the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was a delight to the eyes, and that the tree was to be desired to make one wise, she took some of its fruit, and ate. Then she gave some to her husband with her, and he ate it, too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:6; Genesis 2:25; Genesis 3:5"> Their eyes were opened, and they both knew that they were naked. They sewed fig leaves together, and made coverings for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Proverbs 15:3; Hebrews 4:13"> They heard the LORD God’s voice walking in the garden in the cool of the day, and the man and his wife hid themselves from the presence of the LORD God among the trees of the garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 4:9; Genesis 16:8; Genesis 18:20–21"> The LORD God called to the man, and said to him, “Where are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 2:25; Psalms 119:120; Isaiah 47:3"> The man said, “I heard your voice in the garden, and I was afraid, because I was naked; so I hid myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Genesis 4:10; Psalms 50:21"> God said, “Who told you that you were naked? Have you eaten from the tree that I commanded you not to eat from?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; James 1:13–15; Job 31:33"> The man said, “The woman whom you gave to be with me, she gave me fruit from the tree, and I ate it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14; Genesis 4:10–12"> The LORD God said to the woman, “What have you done?” The woman said, “The serpent deceived me, and I ate.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 7:17; Isaiah 65:25; Psalms 72:9"> The LORD God said to the serpent,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Because you have done this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you are cursed above all livestock,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and above every animal of the field.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You shall go on your belly</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you shall eat dust all the days of your life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 1 John 3:8; Revelation 12:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will put hostility between you and the woman,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and between your offspring and her offspring.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will bruise your head,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will bruise his heel.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Colossians 3:18; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> To the woman he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will greatly multiply your pain in childbirth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You will bear children in pain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your desire will be for your husband,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will rule over you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:20–22; Genesis 5:29; Isaiah 24:5–6"> To Adam he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Because you have listened to your wife’s voice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have eaten from the tree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">about which I commanded you, saying, ‘You shall not eat of it,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the ground is cursed for your sake.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will eat from it with much labor all the days of your life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:7; Job 5:5; Isaiah 7:23"> <span className="pl pl-2">It will yield thorns and thistles to you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will eat the herb of the field.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 34:15; Psalms 104:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will eat bread by the sweat of your face until you return to the ground,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for you were taken out of it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For you are dust,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you shall return to dust.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:23; Matthew 1:23; Genesis 5:29"> The man called his wife Eve because she would be the mother of all the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–3"> The LORD God made garments of animal skins for Adam and for his wife, and clothed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14; Proverbs 3:18"> The LORD God said, “Behold, the man has become like one of us, knowing good and evil. Now, lest he reach out his hand, and also take of the tree of life, and eat, and live forever—”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Genesis 3:19; Genesis 4:12"> Therefore the LORD God sent him out from the garden of Eden, to till the ground from which he was taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Psalms 104:4; Exodus 25:18–22"> So he drove out the man; and he placed cherubim<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="cherubim are powerful angelic creatures, messengers of God with wings.
