<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:12; Romans 4:18; Deuteronomy 1:10"> The LORD brought him outside, and said, “Look now toward the sky, and count the stars, if you are able to count them.” He said to Abram, “So your offspring will be.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:23; Galatians 3:6–14; Romans 4:9"> He believed in the LORD, who credited it to him for righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Acts 7:2–4; Romans 4:13"> He said to Abram, “I am the LORD who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldees, to give you this land to inherit it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:18; Luke 1:34; Isaiah 7:11"> He said, “Lord GOD, how will I know that I will inherit it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 2:24; Leviticus 14:30; Leviticus 14:22"> He said to him, “Bring me a heifer three years old, a female goat three years old, a ram three years old, a turtledove, and a young pigeon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Leviticus 1:17; Genesis 15:17"> He brought him all these, and divided them in the middle, and laid each half opposite the other; but he didn’t divide the birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:4; Psalms 119:13; Ezekiel 17:3"> The birds of prey came down on the carcasses, and Abram drove them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 2:21; Job 33:15; Daniel 10:8–9"> When the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell on Abram. Now terror and great darkness fell on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:6–7; Exodus 1:11; Exodus 12:40–41"> He said to Abram, “Know for sure that your offspring will live as foreigners in a land that is not theirs, and will serve them. They will afflict them four hundred years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:9–11; Exodus 6:5–6; Exodus 12:32–38"> I will also judge that nation, whom they will serve. Afterward they will come out with great wealth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 5:26; Genesis 25:7–9; 2 Chronicles 34:28"> but you will go to your fathers in peace. You will be buried at a good old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 21:26; Daniel 8:23; Exodus 12:40"> In the fourth generation they will come here again, for the iniquity of the Amorite is not yet full.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:18–19; Judges 6:21; Jeremiah 11:4"> It came to pass that, when the sun went down, and it was dark, behold, a smoking furnace and a flaming torch passed between these pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 1:3–4; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 24:7"> In that day the LORD made a covenant with Abram, saying, “I have given this land to your offspring, from the river of Egypt to the great river, the river Euphrates:</span>
