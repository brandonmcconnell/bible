<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 4:25; Job 25:4; Psalms 51:5"> Adam lived one hundred thirty years, and became the father of a son in his own likeness, after his image, and named him Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:1–3; Luke 3:36–38; Genesis 5:26"> The days of Adam after he became the father of Seth were eight hundred years, and he became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:8; Genesis 5:11; Genesis 3:19"> All the days that Adam lived were nine hundred thirty years, then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:26"> Seth lived one hundred five years, then became the father of Enosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Seth lived after he became the father of Enosh eight hundred seven years, and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> All of the days of Seth were nine hundred twelve years, then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 3:37; 1 Chronicles 1:2"> Enosh lived ninety years, and became the father of Kenan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Enosh lived after he became the father of Kenan eight hundred fifteen years, and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> All of the days of Enosh were nine hundred five years, then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> Kenan lived seventy years, then became the father of Mahalalel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Kenan lived after he became the father of Mahalalel eight hundred forty years, and became the father of other sons and daughters</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and all of the days of Kenan were nine hundred ten years, then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:2"> Mahalalel lived sixty-five years, then became the father of Jared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Mahalalel lived after he became the father of Jared eight hundred thirty years, and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All of the days of Mahalalel were eight hundred ninety-five years, then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 4:17; 1 Chronicles 1:3; Jude 1:14–15"> Jared lived one hundred sixty-two years, then became the father of Enoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Jared lived after he became the father of Enoch eight hundred years, and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> All of the days of Jared were nine hundred sixty-two years, then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 3:37"> Enoch lived sixty-five years, then became the father of Methuselah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> After Methuselah’s birth, Enoch walked with God for three hundred years, and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> All the days of Enoch were three hundred sixty-five years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 11:5–6; 1 John 1:7; Genesis 5:22"> Enoch walked with God, and he was not found, for God took him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 4:18"> Methuselah lived one hundred eighty-seven years, then became the father of Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Methuselah lived after he became the father of Lamech seven hundred eighty-two years, and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> All the days of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty-nine years, then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Lamech lived one hundred eighty-two years, then became the father of a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:5; Hebrews 11:7; Matthew 24:37"> He named him Noah, saying, “This one will comfort us in our work and in the toil of our hands, caused by the ground which the LORD has cursed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Lamech lived after he became the father of Noah five hundred ninety-five years, and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> All the days of Lamech were seven hundred seventy-seven years, then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 6:10; Genesis 10:21; Genesis 9:18–19"> Noah was five hundred years old, then Noah became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth.</span>
