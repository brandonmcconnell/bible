<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 3:1; 1 Peter 2:13–17; Daniel 2:21"> Let every soul be in subjection to the higher authorities, for there is no authority except from God, and those who exist are ordained by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13; Titus 3:1; Romans 13:5"> Therefore he who resists the authority withstands the ordinance of God; and those who withstand will receive to themselves judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:4–6; 1 Peter 2:13–14; Proverbs 20:2"> For rulers are not a terror to the good work, but to the evil. Do you desire to have no fear of the authority? Do that which is good, and you will have praise from the authority,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 12:19; 2 Chronicles 19:6; Proverbs 16:14"> for he is a servant of God to you for good. But if you do that which is evil, be afraid, for he doesn’t bear the sword in vain; for he is a servant of God, an avenger for wrath to him who does evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:2; Acts 24:16; 1 Peter 2:19"> Therefore you need to be in subjection, not only because of the wrath, but also for conscience’ sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 22:17–21; Mark 12:14–17; Matthew 17:24–27"> For this reason you also pay taxes, for they are servants of God’s service, continually doing this very thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; 1 Peter 2:17–18; Luke 20:25"> Therefore give everyone what you owe: if you owe taxes, pay taxes; if customs, then customs; if respect, then respect; if honor, then honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:10; Matthew 22:39–40; Galatians 5:14"> Owe no one anything, except to love one another; for he who loves his neighbor has fulfilled the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Matthew 22:39; Luke 10:27"> For the commandments, “You shall not commit adultery,” “You shall not murder,” “You shall not steal,”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds “You shall not give false testimony,”">a</a></sup>“You shall not covet,” and whatever other commandments there are, are all summed up in this saying, namely, “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 22:39–40; Romans 13:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7"> Love doesn’t harm a neighbor. Love therefore is the fulfillment of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Peter 4:7; Ephesians 5:14"> Do this, knowing the time, that it is already time for you to awaken out of sleep, for salvation is now nearer to us than when we first believed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:31–32; Ephesians 5:11; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> The night is far gone, and the day is near. Let’s therefore throw off the deeds of darkness, and let’s put on the armor of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 5:21; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–5"> Let’s walk properly, as in the day; not in reveling and drunkenness, not in sexual promiscuity and lustful acts, and not in strife and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 3:27"> But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, for its lusts.</span>
