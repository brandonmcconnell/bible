<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25; James 5:17; Deuteronomy 28:12"> After many days, the LORD’s word came to Elijah, in the third year, saying, “Go, show yourself to Ahab; and I will send rain on the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 6:25; Proverbs 28:1; Leviticus 26:26"> Elijah went to show himself to Ahab. The famine was severe in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; 1 Kings 16:9; Matthew 10:28"> Ahab called Obadiah, who was over the household. (Now Obadiah feared the LORD greatly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 18:13; Matthew 10:40–42; Revelation 17:4–6"> for when Jezebel cut off the LORD’s prophets, Obadiah took one hundred prophets, and hid them fifty to a cave, and fed them with bread and water.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:5–6; Joel 1:18; Joel 2:22"> Ahab said to Obadiah, “Go through the land, to all the springs of water, and to all the brooks. Perhaps we may find grass and save the horses and mules alive, that we not lose all the animals.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:3"> So they divided the land between them to pass throughout it. Ahab went one way by himself, and Obadiah went another way by himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 1:6–8; Genesis 44:20; Isaiah 60:14"> As Obadiah was on the way, behold, Elijah met him. He recognized him, and fell on his face, and said, “Is it you, my lord Elijah?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Kings 18:3; 1 Peter 2:17–18"> He answered him, “It is I. Go, tell your lord, ‘Behold, Elijah is here!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:21; 1 Kings 18:12; 1 Kings 17:18"> He said, “How have I sinned, that you would deliver your servant into the hand of Ahab, to kill me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 1:29; 1 Kings 18:15"> As the LORD your God lives, there is no nation or kingdom where my lord has not sent to seek you. When they said, ‘He is not here,’ he took an oath of the kingdom and nation that they didn’t find you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 18:8; 1 Kings 18:14"> Now you say, ‘Go, tell your lord, “Behold, Elijah is here.”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 2:16; Acts 8:39; Ezekiel 8:3"> It will happen, as soon as I leave you, that the LORD’s Spirit will carry you I don’t know where; and so when I come and tell Ahab, and he can’t find you, he will kill me. But I, your servant, have feared the LORD from my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; Matthew 25:35; Psalms 18:21–24"> Wasn’t it told my lord what I did when Jezebel killed the LORD’s prophets, how I hid one hundred men of the LORD’s prophets with fifty to a cave, and fed them with bread and water?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28"> Now you say, ‘Go, tell your lord, “Behold, Elijah is here”.’ He will kill me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 18:10; Luke 2:13–14"> Elijah said, “As the LORD of Armies lives, before whom I stand, I will surely show myself to him today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So Obadiah went to meet Ahab, and told him; and Ahab went to meet Elijah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 7:25; 1 Kings 21:20; Acts 16:20"> When Ahab saw Elijah, Ahab said to him, “Is that you, you troubler of Israel?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:2; 1 Kings 9:9; Jeremiah 2:19"> He answered, “I have not troubled Israel, but you and your father’s house, in that you have forsaken the LORD’s commandments and you have followed the Baals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 19:26; 1 Kings 16:33; 2 Peter 2:1"> Now therefore send, and gather to me all Israel to Mount Carmel, and four hundred fifty of the prophets of Baal, and four hundred of the prophets of the Asherah, who eat at Jezebel’s table.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:9"> So Ahab sent to all the children of Israel, and gathered the prophets together to Mount Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Joshua 24:15; 1 Corinthians 10:21–22"> Elijah came near to all the people, and said, “How long will you waver between the two sides? If the LORD is God, follow him; but if Baal, then follow him.”<br />The people didn’t say a word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 19:10; 1 Kings 19:14; 2 Timothy 4:3–4"> Then Elijah said to the people, “I, even I only, am left as a prophet of the LORD; but Baal’s prophets are four hundred fifty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Let them therefore give us two bulls; and let them choose one bull for themselves, and cut it in pieces, and lay it on the wood, and put no fire under; and I will dress the other bull, and lay it on the wood, and put no fire under it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 18:38; 1 Chronicles 21:26; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> You call on the name of your god, and I will call on the LORD’s name. The God who answers by fire, let him be God.”<br />All the people answered, “What you say is good.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, “Choose one bull for yourselves, and dress it first, for you are many; and call on the name of your god, but put no fire under it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:5; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Matthew 6:7"> They took the bull which was given them, and they dressed it, and called on the name of Baal from morning even until noon, saying, “Baal, hear us!” But there was no voice, and nobody answered. They leaped about the altar which was made.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 78:65–66; Isaiah 41:23; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> At noon, Elijah mocked them, and said, “Cry aloud, for he is a god. Either he is deep in thought, or he has gone somewhere, or he is on a journey, or perhaps he sleeps and must be awakened.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 19:28; Deuteronomy 14:1; Mark 5:5"> They cried aloud, and cut themselves in their way with knives and lances until the blood gushed out on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 18:26; 1 Samuel 18:10; Exodus 29:39"> When midday was past, they prophesied until the time of the evening offering; but there was no voice, no answer, and nobody paid attention.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:16; 1 Kings 19:14; 1 Kings 19:10"> Elijah said to all the people, “Come near to me!”; and all the people came near to him. He repaired the LORD’s altar that had been thrown down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 17:34; Genesis 35:10; Genesis 32:28"> Elijah took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, to whom the LORD’s word came, saying, “Israel shall be your name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; Exodus 20:24–25; Judges 21:4"> With the stones he built an altar in the LORD’s name. He made a trench around the altar large enough to contain two seahs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1 seah is about 7 liters or 1.9 gallons or 0.8 pecks">a</a></sup>of seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 22:9; Judges 6:20; Leviticus 1:6–8"> He put the wood in order, and cut the bull in pieces and laid it on the wood. He said, “Fill four jars with water, and pour it on the burnt offering and on the wood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> He said, “Do it a second time;” and they did it the second time. He said, “Do it a third time;” and they did it the third time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Kings 18:32; 1 Kings 18:38"> The water ran around the altar; and he also filled the trench with water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Kings 18:29; 2 Kings 19:19; Exodus 3:6"> At the time of the evening offering, Elijah the prophet came near and said, “LORD, the God of Abraham, of Isaac, and of Israel, let it be known today that you are God in Israel and that I am your servant, and that I have done all these things at your word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Luke 1:16–17; Ezekiel 36:25–27"> Hear me, LORD, hear me, that this people may know that you, LORD, are God, and that you have turned their heart back again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:26; Leviticus 9:24; 2 Chronicles 7:1"> Then the LORD’s fire fell and consumed the burnt offering, the wood, the stones, and the dust; and it licked up the water that was in the trench.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:24; 2 Chronicles 7:3; 1 Kings 18:21"> When all the people saw it, they fell on their faces. They said, “The LORD, he is God! The LORD, he is God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Judges 4:7; Deuteronomy 13:5"> Elijah said to them, “Seize the prophets of Baal! Don’t let one of them escape!”<br />They seized them; and Elijah brought them down to the brook Kishon, and killed them there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 18:1; Ecclesiastes 9:7; 1 Kings 17:1"> Elijah said to Ahab, “Get up, eat and drink; for there is the sound of abundance of rain.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Daniel 9:7; Joshua 7:6"> So Ahab went up to eat and to drink. Elijah went up to the top of Carmel; and he bowed himself down on the earth, and put his face between his knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:3; Luke 18:7; Luke 18:1"> He said to his servant, “Go up now and look toward the sea.”<br />He went up and looked, then said, “There is nothing.”<br />He said, “Go again” seven times.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 12:54; Job 8:7; Zechariah 4:10"> On the seventh time, he said, “Behold, a small cloud, like a man’s hand, is rising out of the sea.”<br />He said, “Go up, tell Ahab, ‘Get ready and go down, so that the rain doesn’t stop you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Kings 18:39–40; Joshua 17:16; 1 Kings 21:1"> In a little while, the sky grew black with clouds and wind, and there was a great rain. Ahab rode, and went to Jezreel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 1:3; 2 Kings 9:1"> The LORD’s hand was on Elijah; and he tucked his cloak into his belt and ran before Ahab to the entrance of Jezreel.</span>
</div>
</div>
