<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> “Speak to the children of Israel, that they take an offering for me. From everyone whose heart makes him willing you shall take my offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> This is the offering which you shall take from them: gold, silver, bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Ezekiel 16:10"> blue, purple, scarlet, fine linen, goats’ hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> rams’ skins dyed red, sea cow hides,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, fine leather">a</a></sup>acacia wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil and for the sweet incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> onyx stones, and stones to be set for the ephod and for the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Kings 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> Let them make me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> According to all that I show you, the pattern of the tabernacle, and the pattern of all of its furniture, even so you shall make it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> “They shall make an ark of acacia wood. Its length shall be two and a half cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimeters.">b</a></sup>its width a cubit and a half, and a cubit and a half its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; 2 Chronicles 3:4"> You shall overlay it with pure gold. You shall overlay it inside and outside, and you shall make a gold molding around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> You shall cast four rings of gold for it, and put them in its four feet. Two rings shall be on the one side of it, and two rings on the other side of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> You shall make poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You shall put the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark to carry the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 8:8; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> The poles shall be in the rings of the ark. They shall not be taken from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; 1 Kings 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> You shall put the covenant which I shall give you into the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> You shall make a mercy seat of pure gold. Two and a half cubits shall be its length, and a cubit and a half its width.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; 1 Kings 8:6–7"> You shall make two cherubim of hammered gold. You shall make them at the two ends of the mercy seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Make one cherub at the one end, and one cherub at the other end. You shall make the cherubim on its two ends of one piece with the mercy seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:18; 1 Kings 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> The cherubim shall spread out their wings upward, covering the mercy seat with their wings, with their faces toward one another. The faces of the cherubim shall be toward the mercy seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> You shall put the mercy seat on top of the ark, and in the ark you shall put the covenant that I will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Isaiah 37:16"> There I will meet with you, and I will tell you from above the mercy seat, from between the two cherubim which are on the ark of the covenant, all that I command you for the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; 1 Kings 7:48"> “You shall make a table of acacia wood. Its length shall be two cubits, and its width a cubit, and its height one and a half cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; 1 Kings 6:20–22"> You shall overlay it with pure gold, and make a gold molding around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> You shall make a rim of a hand width around it. You shall make a golden molding on its rim around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> You shall make four rings of gold for it, and put the rings in the four corners that are on its four feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> The rings shall be close to the rim, for places for the poles to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> You shall make the poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with gold, that the table may be carried with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> You shall make its dishes, its spoons, its ladles, and its bowls with which to pour out offerings. You shall make them of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> You shall set bread of the presence on the table before me always.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 7:49; Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> “You shall make a lamp stand of pure gold. The lamp stand shall be made of hammered work. Its base, its shaft, its cups, its buds, and its flowers shall be of one piece with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> There shall be six branches going out of its sides: three branches of the lamp stand out of its one side, and three branches of the lamp stand out of its other side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremiah 1:11–12; Zechariah 4:3"> three cups made like almond blossoms in one branch, a bud and a flower; and three cups made like almond blossoms in the other branch, a bud and a flower, so for the six branches going out of the lamp stand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and in the lamp stand four cups made like almond blossoms, its buds and its flowers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and a bud under two branches of one piece with it, and a bud under two branches of one piece with it, and a bud under two branches of one piece with it, for the six branches going out of the lamp stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 9:15"> Their buds and their branches shall be of one piece with it, all of it one beaten work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> You shall make its lamps seven, and they shall light its lamps to give light to the space in front of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Kings 12:13; Jeremiah 52:18; 2 Kings 25:14"> Its snuffers and its snuff dishes shall be of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> It shall be made of a talent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds or 965 Troy ounces">c</a></sup>of pure gold, with all these accessories.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> See that you make them after their pattern, which has been shown to you on the mountain.</span>
</div>
