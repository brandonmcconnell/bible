<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood. Its length was two and a half cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimeters.">a</a></sup>and its width a cubit and a half, and a cubit and a half its height.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> He overlaid it with pure gold inside and outside, and made a molding of gold for it around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He cast four rings of gold for it in its four feet—two rings on its one side, and two rings on its other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Numbers 4:14–15"> He made poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; 2 Samuel 6:3–7; Numbers 1:50"> He put the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to bear the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> He made a mercy seat of pure gold. Its length was two and a half cubits, and a cubit and a half its width.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> He made two cherubim of gold. He made them of beaten work, at the two ends of the mercy seat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> one cherub at the one end, and one cherub at the other end. He made the cherubim of one piece with the mercy seat at its two ends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> The cherubim spread out their wings above, covering the mercy seat with their wings, with their faces toward one another. The faces of the cherubim were toward the mercy seat.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Ezekiel 40:39–42; Malachi 1:12"> He made the table of acacia wood. Its length was two cubits, and its width was a cubit, and its height was a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He overlaid it with pure gold, and made a gold molding around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He made a border of a hand’s width around it, and made a golden molding on its border around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He cast four rings of gold for it, and put the rings in the four corners that were on its four feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The rings were close by the border, the places for the poles to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He made the poles of acacia wood, and overlaid them with gold, to carry the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; 1 Kings 7:50; 2 Kings 12:13"> He made the vessels which were on the table, its dishes, its spoons, its bowls, and its pitchers with which to pour out, of pure gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; 2 Chronicles 13:11"> He made the lamp stand of pure gold. He made the lamp stand of beaten work. Its base, its shaft, its cups, its buds, and its flowers were of one piece with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> There were six branches going out of its sides: three branches of the lamp stand out of its one side, and three branches of the lamp stand out of its other side:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> three cups made like almond blossoms in one branch, a bud and a flower, and three cups made like almond blossoms in the other branch, a bud and a flower; so for the six branches going out of the lamp stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremiah 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> In the lamp stand were four cups made like almond blossoms, its buds and its flowers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> and a bud under two branches of one piece with it, and a bud under two branches of one piece with it, and a bud under two branches of one piece with it, for the six branches going out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:5; Exodus 25:31; Psalms 51:17"> Their buds and their branches were of one piece with it. The whole thing was one beaten work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 1:12; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> He made its seven lamps, and its snuffers, and its snuff dishes, of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He made it of a talent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds or 965 Troy ounces">b</a></sup>of pure gold, with all its vessels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 30:1–5; Hebrews 7:25; Revelation 8:3–4"> He made the altar of incense of acacia wood. It was square: its length was a cubit, and its width a cubit. Its height was two cubits. Its horns were of one piece with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He overlaid it with pure gold: its top, its sides around it, and its horns. He made a gold molding around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He made two golden rings for it under its molding crown, on its two ribs, on its two sides, for places for poles with which to carry it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He made the poles of acacia wood, and overlaid them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 141:2; Hebrews 7:25; 1 John 2:20"> He made the holy anointing oil and the pure incense of sweet spices, after the art of the perfumer.</span>
</div>
