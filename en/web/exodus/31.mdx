<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 36:1; Exodus 35:30; John 3:27"> “Behold, I have called by name Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 35:31; Isaiah 28:26; 1 Kings 7:14"> I have filled him with the Spirit of God, in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all kinds of workmanship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 26:1; Exodus 25:32–35; 2 Chronicles 2:13–14"> to devise skillful works, to work in gold, and in silver, and in bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 28:9–21"> and in cutting of stones for setting, and in carving of wood, to work in all kinds of workmanship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:10; Exodus 35:34–1; Exodus 28:3"> Behold, I myself have appointed with him Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan; and in the heart of all who are wise-hearted I have put wisdom, that they may make all that I have commanded you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–9; Exodus 25:10–22; Exodus 26:1–37"> the Tent of Meeting, the ark of the covenant, the mercy seat that is on it, all the furniture of the Tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 37:10–28; Exodus 30:1–10; Exodus 25:23–40"> the table and its vessels, the pure lamp stand with all its vessels, the altar of incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–8; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 40:11"> the altar of burnt offering with all its vessels, the basin and its base,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 4:5–14; Exodus 28:1–43; Exodus 39:1–43"> the finely worked garments—the holy garments for Aaron the priest, the garments of his sons to minister in the priest’s office—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 37:29; Exodus 30:23–38"> the anointing oil, and the incense of sweet spices for the holy place: according to all that I have commanded you they shall do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Ezekiel 20:20; Leviticus 19:30"> “Speak also to the children of Israel, saying, ‘Most certainly you shall keep my Sabbaths; for it is a sign between me and you throughout your generations, that you may know that I am the LORD who sanctifies you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Numbers 15:32–36; Ezekiel 20:21"> You shall keep the Sabbath therefore, for it is holy to you. Everyone who profanes it shall surely be put to death; for whoever does any work therein, that soul shall be cut off from among his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 2:2; Exodus 16:23; Leviticus 23:3"> Six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day is a Sabbath of solemn rest, holy to the LORD. Whoever does any work on the Sabbath day shall surely be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Genesis 9:13; Genesis 17:11"> Therefore the children of Israel shall keep the Sabbath, to observe the Sabbath throughout their generations, for a perpetual covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 1:31; Exodus 31:13; Genesis 2:2–3"> It is a sign between me and the children of Israel forever; for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, and on the seventh day he rested, and was refreshed.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:13; Exodus 32:15–16; Deuteronomy 5:22"> When he finished speaking with him on Mount Sinai, he gave Moses the two tablets of the covenant, stone tablets, written with God’s finger.</span>
