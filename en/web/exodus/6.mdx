<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Exodus 12:31; Exodus 3:19–20"> The LORD said to Moses, “Now you shall see what I will do to Pharaoh, for by a strong hand he shall let them go, and by a strong hand he shall drive them out of his land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:24; Malachi 3:6; Acts 17:24–25"> God spoke to Moses, and said to him, “I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 83:18; Psalms 68:4; Genesis 17:1"> I appeared to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, as God Almighty; but by my name the LORD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LORD or GOD in all caps is from the Hebrew יהוה Yahweh except when otherwise noted as being from the short form יה Yah.">a</a></sup>I was not known to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:4; Genesis 15:18; Genesis 17:7–8"> I have also established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land of their travels, in which they lived as aliens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 2:24; Psalms 106:44–45; Isaiah 63:9"> Moreover I have heard the groaning of the children of Israel, whom the Egyptians keep in bondage, and I have remembered my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:8; 1 Chronicles 17:21; Exodus 7:4"> Therefore tell the children of Israel, ‘I am the LORD, and I will bring you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians, and I will rid you out of their bondage, and I will redeem you with an outstretched arm, and with great judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Deuteronomy 29:13; Deuteronomy 4:20"> I will take you to myself for a people. I will be your God; and you shall know that I am the LORD your God, who brings you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 26:3; Genesis 15:18; Ezekiel 20:42"> I will bring you into the land which I swore to give to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob; and I will give it to you for a heritage: I am the LORD.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 5:21; Job 21:4; Exodus 14:12"> Moses spoke so to the children of Israel, but they didn’t listen to Moses for anguish of spirit, and for cruel bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 6:29; Exodus 5:23; Exodus 3:10"> “Go in, speak to Pharaoh king of Egypt, that he let the children of Israel go out of his land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 4:10; Jeremiah 1:6; Exodus 6:30"> Moses spoke before the LORD, saying, “Behold, the children of Israel haven’t listened to me. How then shall Pharaoh listen to me, when I have uncircumcised lips?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 27:23; 1 Timothy 6:13; Psalms 91:11"> The LORD spoke to Moses and to Aaron, and gave them a command to the children of Israel, and to Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 46:9; 1 Chronicles 5:3; Numbers 26:5–7"> These are the heads of their fathers’ houses. The sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch, and Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi; these are the families of Reuben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:24; Genesis 46:10; Numbers 26:12–13"> The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, and Jamin, and Ohad, and Jachin, and Zohar, and Shaul the son of a Canaanite woman; these are the families of Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:17; Genesis 46:11; 1 Chronicles 6:1"> These are the names of the sons of Levi according to their generations: Gershon, and Kohath, and Merari; and the years of the life of Levi were one hundred thirty-seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; Numbers 3:18; 1 Chronicles 23:7"> The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei, according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:2; Numbers 3:19"> The sons of Kohath: Amram, and Izhar, and Hebron, and Uzziel; and the years of the life of Kohath were one hundred thirty-three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 23:21; 1 Chronicles 6:19"> The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites according to their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 26:59; Exodus 2:1–2; Exodus 6:18"> Amram took Jochebed his father’s sister to himself as wife; and she bore him Aaron and Moses. The years of the life of Amram were one hundred thirty-seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:1; 1 Chronicles 6:37–38; Numbers 16:32"> The sons of Izhar: Korah, and Nepheg, and Zichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 10:4; Numbers 3:30; Nehemiah 3:20"> The sons of Uzziel: Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 4:19–20; Numbers 2:3; Numbers 1:7"> Aaron took Elisheba, the daughter of Amminadab, the sister of Nahshon, as his wife; and she bore him Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–23; Numbers 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:1"> The sons of Korah: Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph; these are the families of the Korahites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Numbers 25:7–13; Joshua 22:31–32"> Eleazar Aaron’s son took one of the daughters of Putiel as his wife; and she bore him Phinehas. These are the heads of the fathers’ houses of the Levites according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 12:51; Exodus 12:17; Exodus 7:4"> These are that Aaron and Moses to whom the LORD said, “Bring out the children of Israel from the land of Egypt according to their armies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 6:26; Exodus 7:10; Psalms 77:20"> These are those who spoke to Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring out the children of Israel from Egypt. These are that Moses and Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> On the day when the LORD spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 6:11; Exodus 7:2; Exodus 6:2"> The LORD said to Moses, “I am the LORD. Tell Pharaoh king of Egypt all that I tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> Moses said before the LORD, “Behold, I am of uncircumcised lips, and how shall Pharaoh listen to me?”</span>
