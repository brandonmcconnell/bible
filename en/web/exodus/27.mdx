<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 38:1–7; Exodus 20:24–26; Exodus 40:10"> “You shall make the altar of acacia wood, five cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimeters.">a</a></sup>long, and five cubits wide. The altar shall be square. Its height shall be three cubits.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The altar was to be about 2.3×2.3×1.4 meters or about 7½×7½×4½ feet.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 118:27; Exodus 29:12; Leviticus 4:7"> You shall make its horns on its four corners. Its horns shall be of one piece with it. You shall overlay it with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 7:45; Numbers 4:14; 1 Chronicles 28:17"> You shall make its pots to take away its ashes; and its shovels, its basins, its meat hooks, and its fire pans. You shall make all its vessels of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 35:16; Exodus 25:12; Exodus 38:4–5"> You shall make a grating for it of network of bronze. On the net you shall make four bronze rings in its four corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 38:4"> You shall put it under the ledge around the altar beneath, that the net may reach halfway up the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 35:13–15; Numbers 4:44; Exodus 30:4"> You shall make poles for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 25:28; Exodus 30:4; Numbers 4:13–14"> Its poles shall be put into the rings, and the poles shall be on the two sides of the altar when carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 25:9; Hebrews 8:5"> You shall make it hollow with planks. They shall make it as it has been shown you on the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 38:9–20; Ezekiel 40:32; Ezekiel 42:3"> “You shall make the court of the tabernacle: for the south side southward there shall be hangings for the court of fine twined linen one hundred cubits long for one side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 26:19–21; Jeremiah 52:21; Exodus 36:38"> Its pillars shall be twenty, and their sockets twenty, of bronze. The hooks of the pillars and their fillets shall be of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Likewise for the length of the north side, there shall be hangings one hundred cubits long, and its pillars twenty, and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars, and their fillets, of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For the width of the court on the west side shall be hangings of fifty cubits; their pillars ten, and their sockets ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The width of the court on the east side eastward shall be fifty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:9; Exodus 26:36"> The hangings for the one side of the gate shall be fifteen cubits; their pillars three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For the other side shall be hangings of fifteen cubits; their pillars three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 26:36; Judges 5:30; Exodus 28:39"> For the gate of the court shall be a screen of twenty cubits, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, the work of the embroiderer; their pillars four, and their sockets four.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the pillars of the court around shall be filleted with silver; their hooks of silver, and their sockets of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The length of the court shall be one hundred cubits, and the width fifty throughout, and the height five cubits, of fine twined linen, and their sockets of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; Numbers 4:32"> All the instruments of the tabernacle in all its service, and all its pins, and all the pins of the court, shall be of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 24:1–4; Zechariah 4:11–14; Psalms 23:5"> “You shall command the children of Israel, that they bring to you pure olive oil beaten for the light, to cause a lamp to burn continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 19:21; Numbers 18:23; 1 Samuel 3:3"> In the Tent of Meeting, outside the veil which is before the covenant, Aaron and his sons shall keep it in order from evening to morning before the LORD: it shall be a statute forever throughout their generations on the behalf of the children of Israel.</span>
