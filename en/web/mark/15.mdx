<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 23:1–5; Luke 22:66"> Immediately in the morning the chief priests, with the elders, scribes, and the whole council, held a consultation, bound Jesus, carried him away, and delivered him up to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Matthew 27:11–14; 1 Timothy 6:13"> Pilate asked him, “Are you the King of the Jews?” He answered,<span className="woj">“So you say.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Mark 15:5; John 18:29–31"> The chief priests accused him of many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 19:10; Matthew 27:13; Matthew 26:62"> Pilate again asked him, “Have you no answer? See how many things they testify against you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; Zechariah 3:8; Psalms 71:7"> But Jesus made no further answer, so that Pilate marveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:39–40; Acts 25:9; Acts 24:27"> Now at the feast he used to release to them one prisoner, whomever they asked of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:18–19; Matthew 27:16; Luke 23:25"> There was one called Barabbas, bound with his fellow insurgents, men who in the insurrection had committed murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The multitude, crying aloud, began to ask him to do as he always did for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:14–16; John 19:4–5; John 18:39"> Pilate answered them, saying, “Do you want me to release to you the King of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Titus 3:3; Genesis 4:4–6"> For he perceived that for envy the chief priests had delivered him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:14; John 18:40; Hosea 5:1"> But the chief priests stirred up the multitude, that he should release Barabbas to them instead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 9:6–7; Zechariah 9:9"> Pilate again asked them, “What then should I do to him whom you call the King of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They cried out again, “Crucify him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:41; Luke 23:23–24; Isaiah 53:3"> Pilate said to them, “Why, what evil has he done?” But they cried out exceedingly, “Crucify him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:1; Matthew 27:26; Isaiah 50:6"> Pilate, wishing to please the multitude, released Barabbas to them, and handed over Jesus, when he had flogged him, to be crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:9; Matthew 27:27–31; John 18:28"> The soldiers led him away within the court, which is the Praetorium; and they called together the whole cohort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 27:28–30; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> They clothed him with purple; and weaving a crown of thorns, they put it on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:2; Genesis 37:10"> They began to salute him, “Hail, King of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Isaiah 53:3–5; Luke 23:11"> They struck his head with a reed and spat on him, and bowing their knees, did homage to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:16; Matthew 27:31"> When they had mocked him, they took the purple cloak off him, and put his own garments on him. They led him out to crucify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Luke 23:26; Romans 16:13"> They compelled one passing by, coming from the country, Simon of Cyrene, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to go with them that he might bear his cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 27:33–44; John 19:17–27; Luke 23:27–38"> They brought him to the place called Golgotha, which is, being interpreted, “The place of a skull.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; John 19:28–30; Luke 23:36"> They offered him wine mixed with myrrh to drink, but he didn’t take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 23:34; John 19:23–24; Matthew 27:35–36"> Crucifying him, they parted his garments among them, casting lots on them, what each should take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 19:14; Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45"> It was the third hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="9:00 a.m.">a</a></sup>when they crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Matthew 2:2; John 19:18–22"> The superscription of his accusation was written over him: “THE KING OF THE JEWS.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 23:32–33; Matthew 27:38; John 19:18"> With him they crucified two robbers, one on his right hand, and one on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The Scripture was fulfilled which says, “He was counted with transgressors.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU omits verse 28.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12"> Those who passed by blasphemed him, wagging their heads and saying, “Ha! You who destroy the temple and build it in three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> save yourself, and come down from the cross!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:23–24; Matthew 27:41–43; Luke 23:35–37"> Likewise, also the chief priests mocking among themselves with the scribes said, “He saved others. He can’t save himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Luke 23:39–43; Zechariah 9:9"> Let the Christ, the King of Israel, now come down from the cross, that we may see and believe him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="TR omits “him”">c</a></sup>Those who were crucified with him also insulted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Luke 23:44–45; Amos 8:9–10"> When the sixth hour<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="or, noon">d</a></sup>had come, there was darkness over the whole land until the ninth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="3:00 p.m.">e</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> At the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying,<span className="woj">“Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani?”</span>which is, being interpreted,<span className="woj">“My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:47–49; Mark 9:11–13; Matthew 17:11–13"> Some of those who stood by, when they heard it, said, “Behold, he is calling Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; John 19:28–30"> One ran, and filling a sponge full of vinegar, put it on a reed and gave it to him to drink, saying, “Let him be. Let’s see whether Elijah comes to take him down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 19:30; Luke 23:46; Matthew 27:50"> Jesus cried out with a loud voice, and gave up the spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–23; Luke 23:45; Hebrews 9:3–12"> The veil of the temple was torn in two from the top to the bottom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Luke 23:47–48; Matthew 27:43"> When the centurion, who stood by opposite him, saw that he cried out like this and breathed his last, he said, “Truly this man was the Son of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> There were also women watching from afar, among whom were both Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of James the less and of Joses, and Salome;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> who, when he was in Galilee, followed him and served him; and many other women who came up with him to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42; John 19:31"> When evening had now come, because it was the Preparation Day, that is, the day before the Sabbath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 2:38; Luke 2:25; Luke 23:51"> Joseph of Arimathaea, a prominent council member who also himself was looking for God’s Kingdom, came. He boldly went in to Pilate, and asked for Jesus’ body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 19:31–37"> Pilate was surprised to hear that he was already dead; and summoning the centurion, he asked him whether he had been dead long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 27:58; John 19:38; Mark 15:39"> When he found out from the centurion, he granted the body to Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 22:16"> He bought a linen cloth, and taking him down, wound him in the linen cloth and laid him in a tomb which had been cut out of a rock. He rolled a stone against the door of the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 15:40; Mark 16:1; Luke 23:55–2"> Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses, saw where he was laid.</span>
