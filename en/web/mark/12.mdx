<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 5:1–4; Mark 13:34; Song of Solomon 8:11–12"> He began to speak to them in parables.<span className="woj">“A man planted a vineyard, put a hedge around it, dug a pit for the wine press, built a tower, rented it out to a farmer, and went into another country.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 15:1–8; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 44:4"> <span className="woj">When it was time, he sent a servant to the farmer to get from the farmer his share of the fruit of the vineyard.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15; Nehemiah 9:26; Jeremiah 29:26"> <span className="woj">They took him, beat him, and sent him away empty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="woj">Again, he sent another servant to them; and they threw stones at him, wounded him in the head, and sent him away shamefully treated.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:6; Matthew 23:37; Nehemiah 9:30"> <span className="woj">Again he sent another, and they killed him, and many others, beating some, and killing some.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 1:1–2; Matthew 3:17; John 1:34"> <span className="woj">Therefore still having one, his beloved son, he sent him last to them, saying, ‘They will respect my son.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 37:20; Matthew 2:16; Acts 2:23"> <span className="woj">But those farmers said among themselves, ‘This is the heir. Come, let’s kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 21:39; Luke 20:15; Matthew 21:33"> <span className="woj">They took him, killed him, and cast him out of the vineyard.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 10:20–12; Matthew 21:43; Luke 19:27"> <span className="woj">What therefore will the lord of the vineyard do? He will come and destroy the farmers, and will give the vineyard to others.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 28:16"> <span className="woj">Haven’t you even read this Scripture:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘The stone which the builders rejected</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">was made the head of the corner.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Acts 3:12–16; Habakkuk 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1 woj">This was from the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">It is marvelous in our eyes’?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 22:22; John 7:44"> They tried to seize him, but they feared the multitude; for they perceived that he spoke the parable against them. They left him and went away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> They sent some of the Pharisees and the Herodians to him, that they might trap him with words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> When they had come, they asked him, “Teacher, we know that you are honest, and don’t defer to anyone; for you aren’t partial to anyone, but truly teach the way of God. Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> Shall we give, or shall we not give?” But he, knowing their hypocrisy, said to them,<span className="woj">“Why do you test me? Bring me a denarius, that I may see it.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> They brought it. He said to them,<span className="woj">“Whose is this image and inscription?”</span>They said to him, “Caesar’s.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Give to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s, and to God the things that are God’s.”</span>They marveled greatly at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> Some Sadducees, who say that there is no resurrection, came to him. They asked him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> “Teacher, Moses wrote to us, ‘If a man’s brother dies and leaves a wife behind him, and leaves no children, that his brother should take his wife and raise up offspring for his brother.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> There were seven brothers. The first took a wife, and dying left no offspring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The second took her, and died, leaving no children behind him. The third likewise;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and the seven took her and left no children. Last of all the woman also died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurrection, when they rise, whose wife will she be of them? For the seven had her as a wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Isn’t this because you are mistaken, not knowing the Scriptures nor the power of God?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> <span className="woj">For when they will rise from the dead, they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> <span className="woj">But about the dead, that they are raised, haven’t you read in the book of Moses about the Bush, how God spoke to him, saying, ‘I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> <span className="woj">He is not the God of the dead, but of the living. You are therefore badly mistaken.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> One of the scribes came and heard them questioning together, and knowing that he had answered them well, asked him, “Which commandment is the greatest of all?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“The greatest is: ‘Hear, Israel, the Lord our God, the Lord is one.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> <span className="woj">You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, with all your mind, and with all your strength.’</span><span className="woj">This is the first commandment.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> <span className="woj">The second is like this: ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’</span><span className="woj">There is no other commandment greater than these.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> The scribe said to him, “Truly, teacher, you have said well that he is one, and there is none other but he;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> and to love him with all the heart, with all the understanding, all the soul, and with all the strength, and to love his neighbor as himself, is more important than all whole burnt offerings and sacrifices.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> When Jesus saw that he answered wisely, he said to him,<span className="woj">“You are not far from God’s Kingdom.”</span>No one dared ask him any question after that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> Jesus responded, as he taught in the temple,<span className="woj">“How is it that the scribes say that the Christ is the son of David?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> <span className="woj">For David himself said in the Holy Spirit,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘The Lord said to my Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">“Sit at my right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">until I make your enemies the footstool of your feet.”’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> <span className="woj">Therefore David himself calls him Lord, so how can he be his son?”</span>The common people heard him gladly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> In his teaching he said to them,<span className="woj">“Beware of the scribes, who like to walk in long robes, and to get greetings in the marketplaces,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> <span className="woj">and to get the best seats in the synagogues and the best places at feasts,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> <span className="woj">those who devour widows’ houses, and for a pretense make long prayers. These will receive greater condemnation.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> Jesus sat down opposite the treasury and saw how the multitude cast money into the treasury. Many who were rich cast in much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> A poor widow came and she cast in two small brass coins,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="literally, lepta (or widow’s mites). Lepta are very small brass coins worth half a quadrans each, which is a quarter of the copper assarion. Lepta are worth less than 1% of an agricultural worker’s daily wages.">a</a></sup>which equal a quadrans coin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A quadrans is a coin worth about 1/64 of a denarius. A denarius is about one day’s wages for an agricultural laborer.">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> He called his disciples to himself and said to them,<span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you, this poor widow gave more than all those who are giving into the treasury,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> <span className="woj">for they all gave out of their abundance, but she, out of her poverty, gave all that she had to live on.”</span></span>
