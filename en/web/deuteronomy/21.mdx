<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; Psalms 5:6; Proverbs 28:17"> If someone is found slain in the land which the LORD your God gives you to possess, lying in the field, and it isn’t known who has struck him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4; Deuteronomy 16:18–19"> then your elders and your judges shall come out, and they shall measure to the cities which are around him who is slain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 19:2; Matthew 11:28–30; Jeremiah 31:18"> It shall be that the elders of the city which is nearest to the slain man shall take a heifer of the herd, which hasn’t been worked with and which has not drawn in the yoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:21–24; 1 Peter 3:18"> The elders of that city shall bring the heifer down to a valley with running water, which is neither plowed nor sown, and shall break the heifer’s neck there in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:13; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 10:8"> The priests the sons of Levi shall come near, for them the LORD your God has chosen to minister to him, and to bless in the LORD’s name; and according to their word shall every controversy and every assault be decided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 73:13; Psalms 26:6; Psalms 51:7"> All the elders of that city which is nearest to the slain man shall wash their hands over the heifer whose neck was broken in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 21:21–23; Job 21:31–34; 2 Samuel 16:8"> They shall answer and say, “Our hands have not shed this blood, neither have our eyes seen it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 1:14; 2 Kings 24:4; Ezekiel 23:24–25"> Forgive, LORD, your people Israel, whom you have redeemed, and don’t allow innocent blood among your people Israel.” The blood shall be forgiven them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:12–13; 2 Kings 10:30–31; Deuteronomy 13:18"> So you shall put away the innocent blood from among you, when you shall do that which is right in the LORD’s eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:10–16; Joshua 21:44"> When you go out to battle against your enemies, and the LORD your God delivers them into your hands and you carry them away captive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 31:10; Genesis 34:3; Genesis 12:14–15"> and see among the captives a beautiful woman, and you are attracted to her, and desire to take her as your wife,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 14:9; Numbers 6:9; Ephesians 4:22"> then you shall bring her home to your house. She shall shave her head and trim her nails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 45:10–11; Luke 14:26–27"> She shall take off the clothing of her captivity, and shall remain in your house, and bewail her father and her mother a full month. After that you shall go in to her and be her husband, and she shall be your wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Genesis 34:2; Deuteronomy 22:19"> It shall be, if you have no delight in her, then you shall let her go where she desires; but you shall not sell her at all for money. You shall not deal with her as a slave, because you have humbled her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:33; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 29:18"> If a man has two wives, the one beloved and the other hated, and they have borne him children, both the beloved and the hated, and if the firstborn son is hers who was hated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:10; 1 Chronicles 5:2; 2 Chronicles 21:3"> then it shall be, in the day that he causes his sons to inherit that which he has, that he may not give the son of the beloved the rights of the firstborn before the son of the hated, who is the firstborn;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:3; Genesis 25:31–34; Psalms 105:36"> but he shall acknowledge the firstborn, the son of the hated, by giving him a double portion of all that he has; for he is the beginning of his strength. The right of the firstborn is his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 20:12; Amos 4:11–12"> If a man has a stubborn and rebellious son who will not obey the voice of his father or the voice of his mother, and though they chasten him, will not listen to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Deuteronomy 21:2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> then his father and his mother shall take hold of him and bring him out to the elders of his city and to the gate of his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1; Proverbs 23:19–21"> They shall tell the elders of his city, “This our son is stubborn and rebellious. He will not obey our voice. He is a glutton and a drunkard.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:19–20; Deuteronomy 13:10–11; Leviticus 24:16"> All the men of his city shall stone him to death with stones. So you shall remove the evil from among you. All Israel shall hear, and fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 19:31–38; Acts 26:31; Luke 23:33"> If a man has committed a sin worthy of death, and he is put to death, and you hang him on a tree,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; John 19:31; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> his body shall not remain all night on the tree, but you shall surely bury him the same day; for he who is hanged is accursed of God. Don’t defile your land which the LORD your God gives you for an inheritance.</span>
