<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 10:11; 1 Corinthians 7:15; Ezekiel 44:22"> When she has departed out of his house, she may go and be another man’s wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> If the latter husband hates her, and writes her a certificate of divorce, puts it in her hand, and sends her out of his house; or if the latter husband dies, who took her to be his wife;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:1; Joshua 22:17–18; Leviticus 18:24–28"> her former husband, who sent her away, may not take her again to be his wife after she is defiled; for that would be an abomination to the LORD. You shall not cause the land to sin, which the LORD your God gives you for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18; Deuteronomy 20:7; Genesis 2:24"> When a man takes a new wife, he shall not go out in the army, neither shall he be assigned any business. He shall be free at home one year, and shall cheer his wife whom he has taken.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19; Luke 12:15; Revelation 18:22"> No man shall take the mill or the upper millstone as a pledge, for he takes a life in pledge.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 21:16; 1 Timothy 1:10; Exodus 22:1–4"> If a man is found stealing any of his brothers of the children of Israel, and he deals with him as a slave, or sells him, then that thief shall die. So you shall remove the evil from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2; Mark 1:44; Leviticus 13:1–46"> Be careful in the plague of leprosy, that you observe diligently and do according to all that the Levitical priests teach you. As I commanded them, so you shall observe to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 12:10–15; 1 Corinthians 10:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Remember what the LORD your God did to Miriam, by the way as you came out of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:8; Exodus 22:27"> When you lend your neighbor any kind of loan, you shall not go into his house to get his pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> You shall stand outside, and the man to whom you lend shall bring the pledge outside to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Job 22:6; Job 24:3"> If he is a poor man, you shall not sleep with his pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:25; Daniel 4:27; Exodus 22:26–27"> You shall surely restore to him the pledge when the sun goes down, that he may sleep in his garment and bless you. It shall be righteousness to you before the LORD your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 14:31; Proverbs 22:16"> You shall not oppress a hired servant who is poor and needy, whether he is one of your brothers or one of the foreigners who are in your land within your gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Proverbs 3:27–28; James 5:4"> In his day you shall give him his wages, neither shall the sun go down on it, for he is poor and sets his heart on it, lest he cry against you to the LORD, and it be sin to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; 2 Chronicles 25:4; Jeremiah 31:29–30"> The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, neither shall the children be put to death for the fathers. Every man shall be put to death for his own sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 23:6"> You shall not deprive the foreigner or the fatherless of justice, nor take a widow’s clothing in pledge;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:15; Deuteronomy 15:15; Deuteronomy 16:12"> but you shall remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and the LORD your God redeemed you there. Therefore I command you to do this thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Leviticus 19:9–10; Deuteronomy 14:29"> When you reap your harvest in your field, and have forgotten a sheaf in the field, you shall not go again to get it. It shall be for the foreigner, for the fatherless, and for the widow, that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 19:10"> When you beat your olive tree, you shall not go over the boughs again. It shall be for the foreigner, for the fatherless, and for the widow.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:9–10; Deuteronomy 24:19"> When you harvest your vineyard, you shall not glean it after yourselves. It shall be for the foreigner, for the fatherless, and for the widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:18; 1 John 4:10–11; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> You shall remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt. Therefore I command you to do this thing.</span>
