<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 1:14; 2 Chronicles 20:4; Esther 4:16"> Gather yourselves together, yes, gather together, you nation that has no shame,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; Hosea 13:3; Nahum 1:6"> before the appointed time when the day passes as the chaff, before the fierce anger of the LORD comes on you, before the day of the LORD’s anger comes on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 5:14–15; Isaiah 26:20–21; Psalms 76:9"> Seek the LORD, all you humble of the land, who have kept his ordinances. Seek righteousness. Seek humility. It may be that you will be hidden in the day of the LORD’s anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 9:5–7; Amos 1:6–8; Jeremiah 47:1–7"> For Gaza will be forsaken, and Ashkelon a desolation. They will drive out Ashdod at noonday, and Ekron will be rooted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:16; Jeremiah 47:7; Joshua 13:3"> Woe to the inhabitants of the sea coast, the nation of the Cherethites! The LORD’s word is against you, Canaan, the land of the Philistines. I will destroy you until there is no inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 17:2; Ezekiel 25:5; Isaiah 5:17"> The sea coast will be pastures, with cottages for shepherds and folds for flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Ezekiel 39:25; Zephaniah 3:20"> The coast will be for the remnant of the house of Judah. They will find pasture. In the houses of Ashkelon, they will lie down in the evening, for the LORD, their God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).">a</a></sup>will visit them and restore them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 1:13; Psalms 83:4–7; Jeremiah 48:27–29"> I have heard the reproach of Moab and the insults of the children of Ammon, with which they have reproached my people and magnified themselves against their border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 15:1–9; Isaiah 11:14; Deuteronomy 29:23"> Therefore, as I live, says the LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, surely Moab will be as Sodom, and the children of Ammon as Gomorrah, a possession of nettles and salt pits, and a perpetual desolation. The remnant of my people will plunder them, and the survivors of my nation will inherit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 16:6; Jeremiah 48:29; Zephaniah 2:8"> This they will have for their pride, because they have reproached and magnified themselves against the people of the LORD of Armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Micah 4:1–3; Zechariah 13:2; Psalms 138:4"> The LORD will be awesome to them, for he will famish all the gods of the land. Men will worship him, everyone from his place, even all the shores of the nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:4–9; Isaiah 43:3; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> You Cushites also, you will be killed by my sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nahum 3:7; Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 10:12"> He will stretch out his hand against the north, destroy Assyria, and will make Nineveh a desolation, as dry as the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19–22; Isaiah 34:11–17; Amos 9:1"> Herds will lie down in the middle of her, all kinds of animals. Both the pelican and the porcupine will lodge in its capitals. Their calls will echo through the windows. Desolation will be in the thresholds, for he has laid bare the cedar beams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Isaiah 22:2; Isaiah 47:7–8"> This is the joyous city that lived carelessly, that said in her heart, “I am, and there is no one besides me.” How she has become a desolation, a place for animals to lie down in! Everyone who passes by her will hiss and shake their fists.</span>
</div>
