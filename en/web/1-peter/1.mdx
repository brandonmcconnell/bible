<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="web" data-translation-id="ENGWEBP" data-translation-label="WEB" data-book="1PE" data-book-slug="1-peter" data-book-name="1 Peter" data-chapter="1" data-verses="25">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:1; Acts 2:5–11; Hebrews 11:13"> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Christ” means “Anointed One”.">a</a></sup>to the chosen ones who are living as foreigners in the Dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Ephesians 1:4–5; Hebrews 12:24"> according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, in sanctification of the Spirit, that you may obey Jesus Christ and be sprinkled with his blood: Grace to you and peace be multiplied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:4; Romans 15:13"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who according to his great mercy caused us to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Hebrews 9:15; 1 Peter 5:4"> to an incorruptible and undefiled inheritance that doesn’t fade away, reserved in Heaven for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 1:6; Ephesians 2:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:13–14"> who by the power of God are guarded through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 5:10; Romans 12:12"> In this you greatly rejoice, though now for a little while, if need be, you have been grieved in various trials,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 23:10; Isaiah 48:10; 1 Peter 4:12"> that the proof of your faith, which is more precious than gold that perishes, even though it is tested by fire, may be found to result in praise, glory, and honor at the revelation of Jesus Christ—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 20:29; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Romans 15:13"> whom, not having known, you love. In him, though now you don’t see him, yet believing, you rejoice greatly with joy that is unspeakable and full of glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 6:22; James 1:21; Hebrews 11:13"> receiving the result of your faith, the salvation of your souls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:17; Luke 10:24; Luke 24:25–27"> Concerning this salvation, the prophets sought and searched diligently. They prophesied of the grace that would come to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 52:13–14; 2 Peter 1:21; Luke 24:25–27"> searching for who or what kind of time the Spirit of Christ which was in them pointed to when he predicted the sufferings of Christ and the glories that would follow them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 15:26; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Ephesians 3:10"> To them it was revealed that they served not themselves, but you, in these things, which now have been announced to you through those who preached the Good News to you by the Holy Spirit sent out from heaven; which things angels desire to look into.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Luke 12:35; 1 Peter 4:7"> Therefore prepare your minds for action.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="literally, “gird up the waist of your mind” or “put on the belt of the waist of your mind”">b</a></sup>Be sober, and set your hope fully on the grace that will be brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ—</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 12:2; Titus 3:3–5; 1 Peter 4:2–3"> as children of obedience, not conforming yourselves according to your former lusts as in your ignorance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Hebrews 12:14"> but just as he who called you is holy, you yourselves also be holy in all of your behavior,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 20:7"> because it is written, “You shall be holy, for I am holy.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34–35; 2 Chronicles 19:7; Matthew 6:9"> If you call on him as Father, who without respect of persons judges according to each man’s work, pass the time of your living as foreigners here in reverent fear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:20; Titus 2:14; Psalms 49:7–8"> knowing that you were redeemed, not with corruptible things like silver or gold, from the useless way of life handed down from your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 1:7; 1 Peter 2:22–24; John 1:29"> but with precious blood, as of a lamb without blemish or spot, the blood of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:9–10; Hebrews 9:26"> who was foreknown indeed before the foundation of the world, but was revealed in this last age for your sake,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Hebrews 2:9; John 12:44"> who through him are believers in God, who raised him from the dead and gave him glory, so that your faith and hope might be in God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:5; Hebrews 13:1; Romans 12:9–10"> Seeing you have purified your souls in your obedience to the truth through the Spirit in sincere brotherly affection, love one another from the heart fervently,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3; Hebrews 4:12; John 1:13"> having been born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, through the word of God, which lives and remains forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 40:6–8; Psalms 103:15; James 4:14"> For,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“All flesh is like grass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all of man’s glory like the flower in the grass.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The grass withers, and its flower falls;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Matthew 5:18; Psalms 119:89"> <span className="pl pl-2">but the Lord’s word endures forever.”</span><br />This is the word of Good News which was preached to you.</span>
</div>
</div>
