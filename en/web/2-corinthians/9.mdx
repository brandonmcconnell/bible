<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 8:4–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:9–10"> It is indeed unnecessary for me to write to you concerning the service to the saints,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:10; 2 Corinthians 8:8; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> for I know your readiness, of which I boast on your behalf to those of Macedonia, that Achaia has been prepared for the past year. Your zeal has stirred up very many of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 2 Corinthians 7:14; Titus 3:1"> But I have sent the brothers so that our boasting on your behalf may not be in vain in this respect, that, just as I said, you may be prepared,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 15:26; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 8:24"> lest by any means, if anyone from Macedonia comes there with me and finds you unprepared, we (to say nothing of you) would be disappointed in this confident boasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:17"> I thought it necessary therefore to entreat the brothers that they would go before to you and arrange ahead of time the generous gift that you promised before, that the same might be ready as a matter of generosity, and not of greediness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Luke 6:38; Proverbs 22:9"> Remember this: he who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly. He who sows bountifully will also reap bountifully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Acts 20:35; 1 Chronicles 29:17"> Let each man give according as he has determined in his heart, not grudgingly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20; Philippians 4:18–19; Proverbs 10:22"> And God is able to make all grace abound to you, that you, always having all sufficiency in everything, may abound to every good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 112:9; Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 21:21"> As it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He has scattered abroad. He has given to the poor.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His righteousness remains forever.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 55:10; 2 Corinthians 9:6; Hosea 10:12"> Now may he who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food, supply and multiply your seed for sowing, and increase the fruits of your righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Chronicles 29:12–14; 1 Timothy 6:17–18"> you being enriched in everything for all generosity, which produces thanksgiving to God through us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15; Philemon 1:4–7; Philippians 2:25"> For this service of giving that you perform not only makes up for lack among the saints, but abounds also through much giving of thanks to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Matthew 5:16; 2 Corinthians 2:12"> seeing that through the proof given by this service, they glorify God for the obedience of your confession to the Good News of Christ and for the generosity of your contribution to them and to all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:14; Philippians 4:1; Philippians 4:18–19"> while they themselves also, with supplication on your behalf, yearn for you by reason of the exceeding grace of God in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:14; Ephesians 5:20; John 3:16"> Now thanks be to God for his unspeakable gift!</span>
