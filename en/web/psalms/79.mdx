<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:33; Jeremiah 34:20; Jeremiah 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have given the dead bodies of your servants to be food for the birds of the sky,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the flesh of your saints to the animals of the earth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:4; Jeremiah 14:16; Revelation 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have shed their blood like water around Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There was no one to bury them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 80:6; Lamentations 5:1; Psalms 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">We have become a reproach to our neighbors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a scoffing and derision to those who are around us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:46; Psalms 74:1; Psalms 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, LORD?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will you be angry forever?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will your jealousy burn like fire?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:25; 2 Thessalonians 1:8; Psalms 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pour out your wrath on the nations that don’t know you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on the kingdoms that don’t call on your name,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:34–35; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">for they have devoured Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and destroyed his homeland.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; Psalms 142:6; Psalms 116:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don’t hold the iniquities of our forefathers against us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let your tender mercies speedily meet us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for we are in desperate need.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:7; Psalms 25:11; 2 Chronicles 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help us, God of our salvation, for the glory of your name.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Deliver us, and forgive our sins, for your name’s sake.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:10; Exodus 7:5; Psalms 115:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let it be known among the nations, before our eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that vengeance for your servants’ blood is being poured out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Psalms 146:6–7; Isaiah 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the sighing of the prisoner come before you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">According to the greatness of your power, preserve those who are sentenced to death.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:18; Genesis 4:15; Leviticus 26:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pay back to our neighbors seven times into their bosom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their reproach with which they have reproached you, Lord.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 95:7; Isaiah 43:21; Psalms 74:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">So we, your people and sheep of your pasture,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will give you thanks forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We will praise you forever, to all generations.</span></span>
