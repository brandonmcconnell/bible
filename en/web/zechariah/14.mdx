<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 13:9; Isaiah 2:12; Isaiah 13:6"> Behold, a day of the LORD comes, when your plunder will be divided within you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 13:16; Romans 9:27–29"> For I will gather all nations against Jerusalem to battle; and the city will be taken, the houses rifled, and the women ravished. Half of the city will go out into captivity, and the rest of the people will not be cut off from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 12:9; Zephaniah 3:19; Zechariah 2:8–9"> Then the LORD will go out and fight against those nations, as when he fought in the day of battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 1:3–4; Ezekiel 11:23; Acts 1:11–12"> His feet will stand in that day on the Mount of Olives, which is before Jerusalem on the east; and the Mount of Olives will be split in two from east to west, making a very great valley. Half of the mountain will move toward the north, and half of it toward the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jude 1:14–15; Amos 1:1; Matthew 25:31"> You shall flee by the valley of my mountains, for the valley of the mountains shall reach to Azel. Yes, you shall flee, just like you fled from before the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. The LORD my God will come, and all the holy ones with you.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Septuagint reads “him” instead of “you”.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:10–11; Psalms 112:4; John 12:46"> It will happen in that day that there will not be light, cold, or frost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 22:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:2; Daniel 12:4"> It will be a unique day which is known to the LORD—not day, and not night; but it will come to pass that at evening time there will be light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 22:1–2; John 7:38; John 4:14"> It will happen in that day that living waters will go out from Jerusalem, half of them toward the eastern sea, and half of them toward the western sea. It will be so in summer and in winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5–6; Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27"> The LORD will be King over all the earth. In that day the LORD will be one, and his name one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Joshua 15:32; 1 Kings 15:22"> All the land will be made like the Arabah, from Geba to Rimmon south of Jerusalem; and she will be lifted up and will dwell in her place, from Benjamin’s gate to the place of the first gate, to the corner gate, and from the tower of Hananel to the king’s wine presses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 22:3; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Amos 9:15"> Men will dwell therein, and there will be no more curse; but Jerusalem will dwell safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:17–20; Isaiah 66:15–16; Micah 4:11–13"> This will be the plague with which the LORD will strike all the peoples who have fought against Jerusalem: their flesh will consume away while they stand on their feet, and their eyes will consume away in their sockets, and their tongue will consume away in their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 12:4; Revelation 17:12–17; Judges 7:22"> It will happen in that day that a great panic from the LORD will be among them; and they will each seize the hand of his neighbor, and his hand will rise up against the hand of his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:18; Zechariah 12:2; 2 Chronicles 20:25–27"> Judah also will fight at Jerusalem; and the wealth of all the surrounding nations will be gathered together: gold, silver, and clothing, in great abundance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:12"> A plague like this will fall on the horse, on the mule, on the camel, on the donkey, and on all the animals that will be in those camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 14:17–19; Isaiah 66:23; Isaiah 66:18–21"> It will happen that everyone who is left of all the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up from year to year to worship the King, the LORD of Armies, and to keep the feast of booths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 4:7–8; Isaiah 60:12; Jeremiah 14:4"> It will be that whoever of all the families of the earth doesn’t go up to Jerusalem to worship the King, the LORD of Armies, on them there will be no rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 14:15; Deuteronomy 11:10–11; Zechariah 14:12"> If the family of Egypt doesn’t go up and doesn’t come, neither will it rain on them. This will be the plague with which the LORD will strike the nations that don’t go up to keep the feast of booths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 3:19"> This will be the punishment of Egypt and the punishment of all the nations that don’t go up to keep the feast of booths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16–17; Exodus 39:30; Leviticus 8:9"> In that day there will be inscribed on the bells of the horses, “HOLY TO THE LORD”; and the pots in the LORD’s house will be like the bowls before the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:9; Romans 14:6–7; 1 Corinthians 10:31"> Yes, every pot in Jerusalem and in Judah will be holy to the LORD of Armies; and all those who sacrifice will come and take of them, and cook in them. In that day there will no longer be a Canaanite<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, merchant">b</a></sup>in the house of the LORD of Armies.</span>
