<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 8:5–13; Luke 7:1–10; Matthew 7:28–29"> After he had finished speaking in the hearing of the people, he entered into Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 5:2–3; Genesis 24:27; John 4:46–47"> A certain centurion’s servant, who was dear to him, was sick and at the point of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 8:5; John 4:47; Luke 9:38"> When he heard about Jesus, he sent to him elders of the Jews, asking him to come and save his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; Matthew 10:11; Luke 7:6–7"> When they came to Jesus, they begged him earnestly, saying, “He is worthy for you to do this for him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:3–9; 1 Kings 5:1"> for he loves our nation, and he built our synagogue for us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 5:24; Genesis 32:10; James 4:6"> Jesus went with them. When he was now not far from the house, the centurion sent friends to him, saying to him, “Lord, don’t trouble yourself, for I am not worthy for you to come under my roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 107:20; Psalms 33:9; Luke 4:36"> Therefore I didn’t even think myself worthy to come to you; but say the word, and my servant will be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Acts 23:17; Acts 22:25–26"> For I also am a man placed under authority, having under myself soldiers. I tell this one, ‘Go!’ and he goes; and to another, ‘Come!’ and he comes; and to my servant, ‘Do this,’ and he does it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Romans 3:1–3; Matthew 8:10"> When Jesus heard these things, he marveled at him, and turned and said to the multitude who followed him,<span className="woj">“I tell you, I have not found such great faith, no, not in Israel.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:50–53; Matthew 8:13; Matthew 15:28"> Those who were sent, returning to the house, found that the servant who had been sick was well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 10:38"> Soon afterwards, he went to a city called Nain. Many of his disciples, along with a great multitude, went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 8:42; Luke 8:52; 1 Kings 17:9"> Now when he came near to the gate of the city, behold, one who was dead was carried out, the only born<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The phrase “only born” is from the Greek word “μονογενη”, which is sometimes translated “only begotten” or “one and only”.">a</a></sup>son of his mother, and she was a widow. Many people of the city were with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 86:15"> When the Lord saw her, he had compassion on her and said to her,<span className="woj">“Don’t cry.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:25; John 11:43–44; Acts 9:40–41"> He came near and touched the coffin, and the bearers stood still. He said,<span className="woj">“Young man, I tell you, arise!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; 2 Kings 4:32–37; 1 Kings 17:23–24"> He who was dead sat up and began to speak. Then he gave him to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:39; Luke 1:68"> Fear took hold of all, and they glorified God, saying, “A great prophet has arisen among us!” and, “God has visited his people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 6:14; Matthew 9:26"> This report went out concerning him in the whole of Judea and in all the surrounding region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:26; Matthew 11:2–19"> The disciples of John told him about all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; John 4:25; Genesis 22:18"> John, calling to himself two of his disciples, sent them to Jesus, saying, “Are you the one who is coming, or should we look for another?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:19"> When the men had come to him, they said, “John the Baptizer has sent us to you, saying, ‘Are you he who comes, or should we look for another?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:10; Psalms 90:7–9; Hebrews 12:6"> In that hour he cured many of diseases and plagues and evil spirits; and to many who were blind he gave sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Go and tell John the things which you have seen and heard: that the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have good news preached to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Romans 9:32–33"> <span className="woj">Blessed is he who finds no occasion for stumbling in me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; John 1:23; Luke 1:80"> When John’s messengers had departed, he began to tell the multitudes about John,<span className="woj">“What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken by the wind?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; Esther 8:15; Esther 1:3"> <span className="woj">But what did you go out to see? A man clothed in soft clothing? Behold, those who are gorgeously dressed and live delicately are in kings’ courts.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:76; John 5:35; Luke 20:6"> <span className="woj">But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I tell you, and much more than a prophet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2"> <span className="woj">This is he of whom it is written,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">‘Behold, I send my messenger before your face,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">who will prepare your way before you.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17; Matthew 11:11; Colossians 1:25–27"> <span className="woj">“For I tell you, among those who are born of women there is not a greater prophet than John the Baptizer; yet he who is least in God’s Kingdom is greater than he.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 3:12; Luke 7:35; Acts 18:25"> When all the people and the tax collectors heard this, they declared God to be just, having been baptized with John’s baptism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 13:34; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Matthew 22:35"> But the Pharisees and the lawyers rejected the counsel of God, not being baptized by him themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Lamentations 2:13; Mark 4:30"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR adds “But the Lord said,”">b</a></sup><span className="woj">“To what then should I compare the people of this generation? What are they like?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Jeremiah 5:3–5; Proverbs 17:16"> <span className="woj">They are like children who sit in the marketplace and call to one another, saying, ‘We piped to you, and you didn’t dance. We mourned, and you didn’t weep.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Mark 1:6; John 8:52"> <span className="woj">For John the Baptizer came neither eating bread nor drinking wine, and you say, ‘He has a demon.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:36; John 12:2"> <span className="woj">The Son of Man has come eating and drinking, and you say, ‘Behold, a glutton and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–36; Matthew 11:19; Hosea 14:9"> <span className="woj">Wisdom is justified by all her children.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:5–6; Luke 14:1"> One of the Pharisees invited him to eat with him. He entered into the Pharisee’s house and sat at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:6–13; Matthew 21:31"> Behold, a woman in the city who was a sinner, when she knew that he was reclining in the Pharisee’s house, brought an alabaster jar of ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 51:17; Genesis 18:4"> Standing behind at his feet weeping, she began to wet his feet with her tears, and she wiped them with the hair of her head, kissed his feet, and anointed them with the ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Luke 15:2; Mark 7:21"> Now when the Pharisee who had invited him saw it, he said to himself, “This man, if he were a prophet, would have perceived who and what kind of woman this is who touches him, that she is a sinner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 16:19; Luke 5:31; Ezekiel 33:31"> Jesus answered him,<span className="woj">“Simon, I have something to tell you.”</span>He said, “Teacher, say on.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 7:47; Romans 5:20; Matthew 18:28"> <span className="woj">“A certain lender had two debtors. The one owed five hundred denarii, and the other fifty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 9:18–19; Colossians 3:13; Romans 3:24"> <span className="woj">When they couldn’t pay, he forgave them both. Which of them therefore will love him most?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 116:16–18; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 7:47"> Simon answered, “He, I suppose, to whom he forgave the most.” He said to him,<span className="woj">“You have judged correctly.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Judges 19:21; Genesis 18:4"> Turning to the woman, he said to Simon,<span className="woj">“Do you see this woman? I entered into your house, and you gave me no water for my feet, but she has wet my feet with her tears, and wiped them with the hair of her head.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:5; 2 Samuel 19:39; Romans 16:16"> <span className="woj">You gave me no kiss, but she, since the time I came in, has not ceased to kiss my feet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 9:8; Matthew 6:17"> <span className="woj">You didn’t anoint my head with oil, but she has anointed my feet with ointment.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 John 4:19; 1 John 3:18; Isaiah 1:18"> <span className="woj">Therefore I tell you, her sins, which are many, are forgiven, for she loved much. But one to whom little is forgiven, loves little.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5; Luke 5:20"> He said to her,<span className="woj">“Your sins are forgiven.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 5:20–21; Matthew 9:3; Mark 2:7"> Those who sat at the table with him began to say to themselves, “Who is this who even forgives sins?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Luke 8:48; Luke 18:42"> He said to the woman,<span className="woj">“Your faith has saved you. Go in peace.”</span></span>
