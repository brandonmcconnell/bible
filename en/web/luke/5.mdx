<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 1:16–20; Matthew 4:18–22; Mark 3:9"> Now while the multitude pressed on him and heard the word of God, he was standing by the lake of Gennesaret.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 1:19; Matthew 4:21"> He saw two boats standing by the lake, but the fishermen had gone out of them and were washing their nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 4:18; Matthew 13:1–2; John 1:41–42"> He entered into one of the boats, which was Simon’s, and asked him to put out a little from the land. He sat down and taught the multitudes from the boat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 21:6; Matthew 17:27"> When he had finished speaking, he said to Simon,<span className="woj">“Put out into the deep and let down your nets for a catch.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:5; John 15:14; Luke 6:46–48"> Simon answered him, “Master, we worked all night and caught nothing; but at your word I will let down the net.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; John 21:6–11; Ecclesiastes 11:6"> When they had done this, they caught a great multitude of fish, and their net was breaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Philippians 4:3; Acts 11:25"> They beckoned to their partners in the other boat, that they should come and help them. They came and filled both boats, so that they began to sink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:5; Job 42:5–6; Revelation 1:17"> But Simon Peter, when he saw it, fell down at Jesus’ knees, saying, “Depart from me, for I am a sinful man, Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:32; Psalms 8:8; Mark 9:6"> For he was amazed, and all who were with him, at the catch of fish which they had caught;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Matthew 13:47; Ezekiel 47:9–10"> and so also were James and John, sons of Zebedee, who were partners with Simon. Jesus said to Simon,<span className="woj">“Don’t be afraid. From now on you will be catching people alive.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 4:20; Luke 5:28; Matthew 19:27"> When they had brought their boats to land, they left everything, and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Luke 17:16; Matthew 8:2–4"> While he was in one of the cities, behold, there was a man full of leprosy. When he saw Jesus, he fell on his face and begged him, saying, “Lord, if you want to, you can make me clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; John 4:50–53; Hosea 14:4"> He stretched out his hand and touched him, saying,<span className="woj">“I want to. Be made clean.”</span>Immediately the leprosy left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 17:14; Mark 1:44"> He commanded him to tell no one,<span className="woj">“But go your way and show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing according to what Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:30–31; Luke 12:1; Mark 1:45–2"> But the report concerning him spread much more, and great multitudes came together to hear and to be healed by him of their infirmities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–36; Matthew 14:23"> But he withdrew himself into the desert and prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Matthew 11:5"> On one of those days, he was teaching; and there were Pharisees and teachers of the law sitting by who had come out of every village of Galilee, Judea, and Jerusalem. The power of the Lord was with him to heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 2:3–12; Matthew 9:2–8; John 5:5–6"> Behold, men brought a paralyzed man on a cot, and they sought to bring him in to lay before Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 2:4; Matthew 10:27; Matthew 24:17"> Not finding a way to bring him in because of the multitude, they went up to the housetop and let him down through the tiles with his cot into the middle before Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 14:9; Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5"> Seeing their faith, he said to him,<span className="woj">“Man, your sins are forgiven you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Mark 2:6–7; Luke 7:49"> The scribes and the Pharisees began to reason, saying, “Who is this who speaks blasphemies? Who can forgive sins, but God alone?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:38; Isaiah 66:18; Psalms 139:2"> But Jesus, perceiving their thoughts, answered them,<span className="woj">“Why are you reasoning so in your hearts?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 2:9; Matthew 9:5"> <span className="woj">Which is easier to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven you,’ or to say, ‘Arise and walk’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Isaiah 53:11; Luke 8:54"> <span className="woj">But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins,”</span>he said to the paralyzed man,<span className="woj">“I tell you, arise, take up your cot, and go to your house.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 9:24; Luke 5:13; Psalms 107:20–22"> Immediately he rose up before them, and took up that which he was laying on, and departed to his house, glorifying God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Matthew 12:23; Luke 7:16"> Amazement took hold on all, and they glorified God. They were filled with fear, saying, “We have seen strange things today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 2:13–22; Matthew 9:9–17; John 1:43"> After these things he went out and saw a tax collector named Levi sitting at the tax office, and said to him,<span className="woj">“Follow me!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 5:11; Matthew 19:22–27; 1 Kings 19:19–21"> He left everything, and rose up and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 2:15; Luke 15:1; 1 Corinthians 10:27"> Levi made a great feast for him in his house. There was a great crowd of tax collectors and others who were reclining with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–32; Luke 5:17; Mark 7:3"> Their scribes and the Pharisees murmured against his disciples, saying, “Why do you eat and drink with the tax collectors and sinners?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Matthew 9:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Those who are healthy have no need for a physician, but those who are sick do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 15:10; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> <span className="woj">I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners, to repentance.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 9:14–17; Mark 2:18–22; John 3:25"> They said to him, “Why do John’s disciples often fast and pray, likewise also the disciples of the Pharisees, but yours eat and drink?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 3:29; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Can you make the friends of the bridegroom fast while the bridegroom is with them?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Acts 14:23; Acts 3:21"> <span className="woj">But the days will come when the bridegroom will be taken away from them. Then they will fast in those days.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 9:16–17; Mark 2:21–22; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> He also told a parable to them.<span className="woj">“No one puts a piece from a new garment on an old garment, or else he will tear the new, and also the piece from the new will not match the old.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 9:13; Psalms 119:83; Joshua 9:4"> <span className="woj">No one puts new wine into old wineskins, or else the new wine will burst the skins, and it will be spilled and the skins will be destroyed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 8:8–13; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ezekiel 36:26"> <span className="woj">But new wine must be put into fresh wineskins, and both are preserved.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Mark 7:7–13; Jeremiah 6:16"> <span className="woj">No man having drunk old wine immediately desires new, for he says, ‘The old is better.’”</span></span>
