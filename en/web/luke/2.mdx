<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 24:14; Acts 25:21"> Now in those days, a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that all the world should be enrolled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Matthew 4:24; Acts 26:30"> This was the first enrollment made when Quirinius was governor of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> All went to enroll themselves, everyone to his own city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:42; 1 Samuel 16:1"> Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judea, to David’s city, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:22–27; Matthew 1:18–19"> to enroll himself with Mary, who was pledged to be married to him as wife, being pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 33:11; Revelation 12:1–5; Micah 5:2"> While they were there, the day had come for her to give birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Galatians 4:4; 2 Corinthians 8:9; Isaiah 53:2–3"> She gave birth to her firstborn son. She wrapped him in bands of cloth and laid him in a feeding trough, because there was no room for them in the inn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 31:39–40; Psalms 78:70–71; 1 Samuel 17:34–35"> There were shepherds in the same country staying in the field, and keeping watch by night over their flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 1 Kings 8:11"> Behold, an angel of the Lord stood by them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> The angel said to them, “Don’t be afraid, for behold, I bring you good news of great joy which will be to all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; Matthew 1:21; Acts 2:36"> For there is born to you today, in David’s city, a Savior, who is Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Christ” means “Anointed One”.">a</a></sup>the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> This is the sign to you: you will find a baby wrapped in strips of cloth, lying in a feeding trough.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:20–21; Revelation 5:11; Psalms 148:2"> Suddenly, there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army praising God and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 5:1; Luke 19:38; Colossians 1:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Glory to God in the highest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on earth peace, good will toward men.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:22; Matthew 2:1–2; Matthew 2:9–11"> When the angels went away from them into the sky, the shepherds said to one another, “Let’s go to Bethlehem, now, and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has made known to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:32; Luke 2:12; Luke 2:7"> They came with haste and found both Mary and Joseph, and the baby was lying in the feeding trough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; Luke 8:39; Psalms 71:17–18"> When they saw it, they publicized widely the saying which was spoken to them about this child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 2:47; Luke 4:36; Luke 1:65–66"> All who heard it wondered at the things which were spoken to them by the shepherds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 2:51; Luke 1:66; Proverbs 4:4"> But Mary kept all these sayings, pondering them in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; Acts 11:18"> The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things that they had heard and seen, just as it was told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:31; Genesis 17:12; Galatians 4:4–5"> When eight days were fulfilled for the circumcision of the child, his name was called Jesus, which was given by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:1–8"> When the days of their purification according to the law of Moses were fulfilled, they brought him up to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 13:2; Exodus 22:29; Numbers 3:13"> (as it is written in the law of the Lord, “Every male who opens the womb shall be called holy to the Lord”),</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 12:6–8; Leviticus 12:2; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> and to offer a sacrifice according to that which is said in the law of the Lord, “A pair of turtledoves, or two young pigeons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; Luke 2:38; Luke 1:6"> Behold, there was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon. This man was righteous and devout, looking for the consolation of Israel, and the Holy Spirit was on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 8:51; Hebrews 11:5; Luke 9:27"> It had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he should not see death before he had seen the Lord’s Christ.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Christ” (Greek) and “Messiah” (Hebrew) both mean “Anointed One”">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 4:1; Luke 2:41; Luke 2:51"> He came in the Spirit into the temple. When the parents brought in the child, Jesus, that they might do concerning him according to the custom of the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 1:64; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:13–14"> then he received him into his arms and blessed God, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Genesis 15:15; Philippians 1:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Now you are releasing your servant, Master,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">according to your word, in peace;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 49:6; Luke 2:10–11; Luke 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">for my eyes have seen your salvation,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 62:1–2; Isaiah 42:1–4; Psalms 98:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-2">which you have prepared before the face of all peoples;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1–3; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 26:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">a light for revelation to the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the glory of your people Israel.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; Luke 2:48"> Joseph and his mother were marveling at the things which were spoken concerning him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> Simeon blessed them, and said to Mary, his mother, “Behold, this child is appointed for the falling and the rising of many in Israel, and for a sign which is spoken against.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 12:24–35"> Yes, a sword will pierce through your own soul, that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Genesis 30:13; Acts 21:9"> There was one Anna, a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher (she was of a great age, having lived with a husband seven years from her virginity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:5; Psalms 23:6; Acts 14:23"> and she had been a widow for about eighty-four years), who didn’t depart from the temple, worshiping with fastings and petitions night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 2:25; 2 Corinthians 9:15; Mark 15:43"> Coming up at that very hour, she gave thanks to the Lord, and spoke of him to all those who were looking for redemption in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 2:4; Galatians 4:4–5; Deuteronomy 12:32"> When they had accomplished all things that were according to the law of the Lord, they returned into Galilee, to their own city, Nazareth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 1:80; Isaiah 11:1–5"> The child was growing, and was becoming strong in spirit, being filled with wisdom, and the grace of God was upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Numbers 28:16; Deuteronomy 12:18"> His parents went every year to Jerusalem at the feast of the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> When he was twelve years old, they went up to Jerusalem according to the custom of the feast;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:21–23; Exodus 12:15; 2 Chronicles 25:17"> and when they had fulfilled the days, as they were returning, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem. Joseph and his mother didn’t know it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; Psalms 42:4"> but supposing him to be in the company, they went a day’s journey; and they looked for him among their relatives and acquaintances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> When they didn’t find him, they returned to Jerusalem, looking for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> After three days they found him in the temple, sitting in the middle of the teachers, both listening to them and asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Luke 4:32; Psalms 119:99"> All who heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 2:49; Matthew 12:46; Luke 3:23"> When they saw him, they were astonished; and his mother said to him, “Son, why have you treated us this way? Behold, your father and I were anxiously looking for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 6:38; John 8:29; Malachi 3:1"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Why were you looking for me? Didn’t you know that I must be in my Father’s house?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 18:34; Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32"> They didn’t understand the saying which he spoke to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Daniel 7:28; Genesis 37:11"> And he went down with them and came to Nazareth. He was subject to them, and his mother kept all these sayings in her heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Proverbs 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 2:26"> And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men.</span>
