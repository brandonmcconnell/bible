<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 3:4; Matthew 12:12–13; Luke 14:3"> Then Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“I will ask you something: Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good, or to do harm? To save a life, or to kill?”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–2; Acts 5:33; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> But they were filled with rage, and talked with one another about what they might do to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Mark 6:46; Mark 1:35"> In these days, he went out to the mountain to pray, and he continued all night in prayer to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 3:13–19; Matthew 9:36–4; Revelation 21:14"> When it was day, he called his disciples, and from them he chose twelve, whom he also named apostles:</span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13"> Matthew; Thomas; James the son of Alphaeus; Simon who was called the Zealot;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:22; John 6:70–71; Mark 3:18"> Judas the son of James; and Judas Iscariot, who also became a traitor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Matthew 4:23–25; Psalms 107:17–20"> He came down with them and stood on a level place, with a crowd of his disciples and a great number of the people from all Judea and Jerusalem and the sea coast of Tyre and Sidon, who came to hear him and to be healed of their diseases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:15; Matthew 15:22; Acts 5:16"> as well as those who were troubled by unclean spirits; and they were being healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 3:10; Acts 5:15–16"> All the multitude sought to touch him, for power came out of him and healed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 2:5; Luke 6:20–24; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> He lifted up his eyes to his disciples, and said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1 woj">“Blessed are you who are poor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2 woj">for God’s Kingdom is yours.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; Matthew 10:22; 1 Peter 4:12–16"> <span className="pl pl-1 woj">Blessed are you when men hate you, and when they exclude and mock you, and throw out your name as evil, for the Son of Man’s sake.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:41; Revelation 21:7; Revelation 3:12"> <span className="pl pl-2 woj">Rejoice in that day and leap for joy, for behold, your reward is great in heaven, for their fathers did the same thing to the prophets.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 8:7; James 1:24; Romans 2:1"> <span className="woj">Why do you see the speck of chaff that is in your brother’s eye, but don’t consider the beam that is in your own eye?</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> <span className="woj">“For there is no good tree that produces rotten fruit, nor again a rotten tree that produces good fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 12:33; James 3:12; Galatians 5:19–23"> <span className="woj">For each tree is known by its own fruit. For people don’t gather figs from thorns, nor do they gather grapes from a bramble bush.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Matthew 12:34–37; Ephesians 4:29"> <span className="woj">The good man out of the good treasure of his heart brings out that which is good, and the evil man out of the evil treasure of his heart brings out that which is evil, for out of the abundance of the heart, his mouth speaks.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; Malachi 1:6; Matthew 7:21–23"> <span className="woj">“Why do you call me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ and don’t do the things which I say?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 1:22–25; Matthew 7:24–27; Luke 11:28"> <span className="woj">Everyone who comes to me, and hears my words and does them, I will show you who he is like.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10–15; Psalms 62:2; Matthew 7:25–26"> <span className="woj">He is like a man building a house, who dug and went deep and laid a foundation on the rock. When a flood arose, the stream broke against that house, and could not shake it, because it was founded on the rock.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; James 1:22–26; Matthew 23:3"> <span className="woj">But he who hears and doesn’t do, is like a man who built a house on the earth without a foundation, against which the stream broke, and immediately it fell; and the ruin of that house was great.”</span></span>
