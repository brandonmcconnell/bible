<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joel 2:32; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 7:3–17"> I saw, and behold, the Lamb standing on Mount Zion, and with him a number, one hundred forty-four thousand, having his name and the name of his Father written on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 5:8; Ezekiel 43:2; Revelation 1:15"> I heard a sound from heaven like the sound of many waters and like the sound of a great thunder. The sound which I heard was like that of harpists playing on their harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 5:9; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 15:3"> They sing a new song before the throne and before the four living creatures and the elders. No one could learn the song except the one hundred forty-four thousand, those who had been redeemed out of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 3:4; 2 Corinthians 11:2; James 1:18"> These are those who were not defiled with women, for they are virgins. These are those who follow the Lamb wherever he goes. These were redeemed by Jesus from among men, the first fruits to God and to the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Psalms 32:2; Zephaniah 3:13"> In their mouth was found no lie, for they are blameless.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds “before the throne of God”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 16:15; Revelation 8:13; Ephesians 3:9–11"> I saw an angel flying in mid heaven, having an eternal Good News to proclaim to those who dwell on the earth—to every nation, tribe, language, and people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Revelation 15:4; Nehemiah 9:6"> He said with a loud voice, “Fear the Lord, and give him glory, for the hour of his judgment has come. Worship him who made the heaven, the earth, the sea, and the springs of waters!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 51:7–8; Revelation 18:2–3"> Another, a second angel, followed, saying, “Babylon the great has fallen, which has made all the nations to drink of the wine of the wrath of her sexual immorality.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 14:11; Revelation 14:6–8; Revelation 13:3–6"> Another angel, a third, followed them, saying with a great voice, “If anyone worships the beast and his image, and receives a mark on his forehead or on his hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 16:19; Jeremiah 25:15–17"> he also will drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is prepared unmixed in the cup of his anger. He will be tormented with fire and sulfur in the presence of the holy angels and in the presence of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Isaiah 34:10; Matthew 25:41"> The smoke of their torment goes up forever and ever. They have no rest day and night, those who worship the beast and his image, and whoever receives the mark of his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 13:10; Revelation 12:17; Revelation 3:10"> Here is the perseverance of the saints, those who keep the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 20:6; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Thessalonians 5:10"> I heard a voice from heaven saying, “Write, ‘Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on.’” “Yes,” says the Spirit, “that they may rest from their labors, for their works follow with them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Isaiah 19:1; Matthew 17:5"> I looked, and saw a white cloud, and on the cloud one sitting like a son of man, having on his head a golden crown, and in his hand a sharp sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:39; Mark 4:29"> Another angel came out of the temple, crying with a loud voice to him who sat on the cloud, “Send your sickle and reap, for the hour to reap has come; for the harvest of the earth is ripe!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Revelation 14:19; Revelation 14:14"> He who sat on the cloud thrust his sickle on the earth, and the earth was reaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:18; Revelation 14:14–15; Revelation 15:5–6"> Another angel came out of the temple which is in heaven. He also had a sharp sickle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Revelation 16:8; Revelation 14:15–16"> Another angel came out from the altar, he who has power over fire, and he called with a great voice to him who had the sharp sickle, saying, “Send your sharp sickle and gather the clusters of the vine of the earth, for the earth’s grapes are fully ripe!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:32–33; Revelation 19:15–21"> The angel thrust his sickle into the earth, and gathered the vintage of the earth and threw it into the great wine press of the wrath of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 1:15; Revelation 19:14–21; Isaiah 63:1–3"> The wine press was trodden outside of the city, and blood came out of the wine press, up to the bridles of the horses, as far as one thousand six hundred stadia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1600 stadia = 296 kilometers or 184 miles">b</a></sup></span>
