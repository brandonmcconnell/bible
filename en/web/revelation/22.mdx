<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 7:17; John 4:14; Psalms 36:8–9"> He showed me a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds “pure”">a</a></sup>river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; 1 Peter 2:24; Revelation 2:7"> in the middle of its street. On this side of the river and on that was the tree of life, bearing twelve kinds of fruits, yielding its fruit every month. The leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Revelation 21:3–4; John 12:26"> There will be no curse any more. The throne of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and his servants will serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 5:8; Revelation 14:1; Revelation 3:12"> They will see his face, and his name will be on their foreheads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4"> There will be no night, and they need no lamp light or sun light; for the Lord God will illuminate them. They will reign forever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:1; Revelation 21:5; Revelation 19:9"> He said to me, “These words are faithful and true. The Lord God of the spirits of the prophets sent his angel to show to his bondservants the things which must happen soon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 1:3; Revelation 3:11; Revelation 22:20"> <span className="woj">“Behold, I am coming soon! Blessed is he who keeps the words of the prophecy of this book.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Revelation 1:1; Revelation 19:19"> Now I, John, am the one who heard and saw these things. When I heard and saw, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel who had shown me these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 19:10; Colossians 2:18–19; Revelation 14:7"> He said to me, “You must not do that! I am a fellow bondservant with you and with your brothers, the prophets, and with those who keep the words of this book. Worship God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Revelation 1:3; 1 Peter 4:7"> He said to me, “Don’t seal up the words of the prophecy of this book, for the time is at hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:10; Proverbs 4:18; Ezekiel 3:27"> He who acts unjustly, let him act unjustly still. He who is filthy, let him be filthy still. He who is righteous, let him do righteousness still. He who is holy, let him be holy still.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Isaiah 62:11; Isaiah 40:10"> <span className="woj">“Behold, I am coming soon! My reward is with me, to repay to each man according to his work.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 44:6"> <span className="woj">I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; 1 John 5:3; Revelation 22:7"> <span className="woj">Blessed are those who do his commandments,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU reads “wash their robes” instead of “do his commandments”.">b</a></sup><span className="woj">that they may have the right to the tree of life, and may enter in by the gates into the city.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 21:8; Galatians 5:19–21; Philippians 3:2"> <span className="woj">Outside are the dogs, the sorcerers, the sexually immoral, the murderers, the idolaters, and everyone who loves and practices falsehood.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 5:5; 2 Peter 1:19; Isaiah 11:1"> <span className="woj">I, Jesus, have sent my angel to testify these things to you for the assemblies. I am the root and the offspring of David, the Bright and Morning Star.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:14; Revelation 21:6; Isaiah 55:1–3"> The Spirit and the bride say, “Come!” He who hears, let him say, “Come!” He who is thirsty, let him come. He who desires, let him take the water of life freely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:2; Proverbs 30:6"> I testify to everyone who hears the words of the prophecy of this book: if anyone adds to them, God will add to him the plagues which are written in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Luke 11:52; Revelation 22:2"> If anyone takes away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God will take away his part from the tree<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="TR reads “Book” instead of “tree”">c</a></sup>of life, and out of the holy city, which are written in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 22:7; Hebrews 9:28; Revelation 22:12"> He who testifies these things says,<span className="woj">“Yes, I am coming soon.”</span>Amen! Yes, come, Lord Jesus!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:18; Romans 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with all the saints. Amen.</span>
