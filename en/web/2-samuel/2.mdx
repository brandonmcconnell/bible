<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:31; 1 Samuel 23:4; Genesis 32:2"> After this, David inquired of the LORD, saying, “Shall I go up into any of the cities of Judah?” The LORD said to him, “Go up.” David said, “Where shall I go up?” He said, “To Hebron.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 1 Samuel 30:5; Luke 22:28–29"> So David went up there with his two wives, Ahinoam the Jezreelitess, and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:1; 1 Chronicles 12:1–7; 1 Samuel 27:2–3"> David brought up his men who were with him, every man with his household. They lived in the cities of Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:5; 1 Samuel 31:11–13; 2 Samuel 5:3"> The men of Judah came, and there they anointed David king over the house of Judah. They told David, “The men of Jabesh Gilead were those who buried Saul.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:21; Psalms 115:15; Ruth 2:20"> David sent messengers to the men of Jabesh Gilead, and said to them, “Blessed are you by the LORD, that you have shown this kindness to your lord, even to Saul, and have buried him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 14:22; Exodus 34:6; Matthew 5:44"> Now may the LORD show loving kindness and truth to you. I also will reward you for this kindness, because you have done this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; 2 Samuel 10:12; Genesis 15:1"> Now therefore let your hands be strong, and be valiant; for Saul your lord is dead, and also the house of Judah have anointed me king over them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:50; Genesis 32:2; 2 Samuel 3:7–8"> Now Abner the son of Ner, captain of Saul’s army, had taken Ishbosheth the son of Saul and brought him over to Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Joshua 13:8–11; Numbers 1:40"> He made him king over Gilead, over the Ashurites, over Jezreel, over Ephraim, over Benjamin, and over all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Ishbosheth, Saul’s son, was forty years old when he began to reign over Israel, and he reigned two years. But the house of Judah followed David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11; 2 Samuel 5:4–5; 1 Chronicles 3:4"> The time that David was king in Hebron over the house of Judah was seven years and six months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:25; Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:3"> Abner the son of Ner, and the servants of Ishbosheth the son of Saul, went out from Mahanaim to Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; 1 Chronicles 2:16; 2 Samuel 20:23"> Joab the son of Zeruiah and David’s servants went out, and met them by the pool of Gibeon; and they sat down, the one on the one side of the pool and the other on the other side of the pool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:17; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 17:14"> Abner said to Joab, “Please let the young men arise and compete before us!” Joab said, “Let them arise!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then they arose and went over by number: twelve for Benjamin and for Ishbosheth the son of Saul, and twelve of David’s servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They each caught his opponent by the head and thrust his sword in his fellow’s side; so they fell down together. Therefore that place in Gibeon was called Helkath Hazzurim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Helkath Hazzurim” means “field of daggers”.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1"> The battle was very severe that day; and Abner was beaten, and the men of Israel, before David’s servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:8; Habakkuk 3:19; Song of Solomon 8:14"> The three sons of Zeruiah were there: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. Asahel was as light of foot as a wild gazelle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:21; Joshua 1:7; Proverbs 4:27"> Asahel pursued Abner. He didn’t turn to the right hand or to the left from following Abner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then Abner looked behind him and said, “Is that you, Asahel?” He answered, “It is.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 14:19"> Abner said to him, “Turn away to your right hand or to your left, and grab one of the young men, and take his armor.” But Asahel would not turn away from following him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; Proverbs 29:1; 2 Kings 14:10–12"> Abner said again to Asahel, “Turn away from following me. Why should I strike you to the ground? How then could I look Joab your brother in the face?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 4:6; 2 Samuel 5:6"> However, he refused to turn away. Therefore Abner with the back end of the spear struck him in the body, so that the spear came out behind him; and he fell down there and died in the same place. As many as came to the place where Asahel fell down and died stood still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But Joab and Abishai pursued Abner. The sun went down when they had come to the hill of Ammah, that lies before Giah by the way of the wilderness of Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The children of Benjamin gathered themselves together after Abner and became one band, and stood on the top of a hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 46:10; Acts 7:26"> Then Abner called to Joab, and said, “Shall the sword devour forever? Don’t you know that it will be bitterness in the latter end? How long will it be then, before you ask the people to return from following their brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; 2 Samuel 2:14; Proverbs 15:1"> Joab said, “As God<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).">b</a></sup>lives, if you had not spoken, surely then in the morning the people would have gone away, and not each followed his brother.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Joab blew the trumpet; and all the people stood still and pursued Israel no more, and they fought no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Song of Solomon 2:17"> Abner and his men went all that night through the Arabah; and they passed over the Jordan, and went through all Bithron, and came to Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Joab returned from following Abner; and when he had gathered all the people together, nineteen men of David’s and Asahel were missing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; 1 Kings 20:11"> But David’s servants had struck Benjamin Abner’s men so that three hundred sixty men died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13–16; 1 Samuel 17:58; 2 Chronicles 21:1"> They took up Asahel and buried him in the tomb of his father, which was in Bethlehem. Joab and his men went all night, and the day broke on them at Hebron.</span>
