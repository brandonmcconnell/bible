<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 4:4; Titus 3:3–4; 1 Samuel 20:14–17"> The king said, “Is there not yet any of Saul’s house, that I may show the kindness of God to him?” Ziba said to the king, “Jonathan still has a son, who is lame in his feet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:27–29"> The king said to him, “Where is he?” Ziba said to the king, “Behold, he is in the house of Machir the son of Ammiel, in Lo Debar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then King David sent and brought him out of the house of Machir the son of Ammiel, from Lo Debar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:4; 1 Chronicles 9:40; 1 Chronicles 8:34"> Mephibosheth, the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, came to David, fell on his face, and showed respect. David said, “Mephibosheth?” He answered, “Behold, your servant!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:1; 1 Kings 2:7; 2 Samuel 19:28"> David said to him, “Don’t be afraid, for I will surely show you kindness for Jonathan your father’s sake, and will restore to you all the land of Saul your father. You will eat bread at my table continually.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:9; Matthew 15:26–27; 1 Samuel 24:14–15"> He bowed down, and said, “What is your servant, that you should look at such a dead dog as I am?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:29; 2 Samuel 16:4; Isaiah 32:8"> Then the king called to Ziba, Saul’s servant, and said to him, “All that belonged to Saul and to all his house I have given to your master’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; 2 Samuel 19:28; 2 Kings 25:29"> Till the land for him—you, your sons, and your servants. Bring in the harvest, that your master’s son may have bread to eat; but Mephibosheth your master’s son will always eat bread at my table.” Now Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1–4; 2 Samuel 19:26; 2 Samuel 19:17"> Then Ziba said to the king, “According to all that my lord the king commands his servant, so your servant will do.” So Mephibosheth ate at the king’s table like one of the king’s sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:40–44; 1 Chronicles 8:8; 1 Chronicles 8:34–40"> Mephibosheth had a young son, whose name was Mica. All who lived in Ziba’s house were servants to Mephibosheth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:7; 2 Samuel 9:3; 2 Samuel 9:10–11"> So Mephibosheth lived in Jerusalem, for he ate continually at the king’s table. He was lame in both his feet.</span>
