<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 55:17; Acts 10:3; Acts 4:13"> Peter and John were going up into the temple at the hour of prayer, the ninth hour.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="3:00 p.m.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:8; John 9:8; Luke 16:20"> A certain man who was lame from his mother’s womb was being carried, whom they laid daily at the door of the temple which is called Beautiful, to ask gifts for the needy of those who entered into the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Seeing Peter and John about to go into the temple, he asked to receive gifts for the needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:9–10; John 11:40; Luke 4:20"> Peter, fastening his eyes on him, with John, said, “Look at us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He listened to them, expecting to receive something from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 3:16; Acts 10:38; Acts 4:10"> But Peter said, “I have no silver or gold, but what I have, that I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, get up and walk!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Mark 9:27; Acts 9:41"> He took him by the right hand and raised him up. Immediately his feet and his ankle bones received strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 14:10; Isaiah 35:6; Psalms 107:20–22"> Leaping up, he stood and began to walk. He entered with them into the temple, walking, leaping, and praising God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:21; Acts 4:16; Luke 13:17"> All the people saw him walking and praising God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:2; John 5:20; John 9:3"> They recognized him, that it was he who used to sit begging for gifts for the needy at the Beautiful Gate of the temple. They were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:12; John 10:23; Luke 8:38"> As the lame man who was healed held on to Peter and John, all the people ran together to them in the porch that is called Solomon’s, greatly wondering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; John 3:27–28; Daniel 2:28–30"> When Peter saw it, he responded to the people, “You men of Israel, why do you marvel at this man? Why do you fasten your eyes on us, as though by our own power or godliness we had made him walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 22:32; Acts 7:32; Matthew 27:17–25"> The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the God of our fathers, has glorified his Servant Jesus, whom you delivered up and denied in the presence of Pilate, when he had determined to release him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Acts 7:52; 1 Peter 3:18"> But you denied the Holy and Righteous One and asked for a murderer to be granted to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Revelation 21:6; Acts 2:32"> and killed the Prince of life, whom God raised from the dead, to which we are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Acts 4:10; Acts 14:9"> By faith in his name, his name has made this man strong, whom you see and know. Yes, the faith which is through him has given him this perfect soundness in the presence of you all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Acts 13:27; John 16:3"> “Now, brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here may be also correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">b</a></sup>I know that you did this in ignorance, as did also your rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 2:23"> But the things which God announced by the mouth of all his prophets, that Christ should suffer, he thus fulfilled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Isaiah 44:22; Isaiah 43:25"> “Repent therefore, and turn again, that your sins may be blotted out, so that there may come times of refreshing from the presence of the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:30–36; Revelation 19:11–16; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> and that he may send Christ Jesus, who was ordained for you before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:21; Isaiah 1:26; Acts 1:11"> whom heaven must receive until the times of restoration of all things, which God spoke long ago by the mouth of his holy prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 7:37; Galatians 4:4"> For Moses indeed said to the fathers, ‘The Lord God will raise up a prophet for you from among your brothers, like me. You shall listen to him in all things whatever he says to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:19; John 12:48; Hebrews 10:28–30"> It will be that every soul that will not listen to that prophet will be utterly destroyed from among the people.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 24:27; 1 Samuel 3:20"> Yes, and all the prophets from Samuel and those who followed after, as many as have spoken, also told of these days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:8; Acts 2:39"> You are the children of the prophets, and of the covenant which God made with our fathers, saying to Abraham, ‘All the families of the earth will be blessed through your offspring.’<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, seed">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 1:21; Acts 3:22; Jude 1:24"> God, having raised up his servant Jesus, sent him to you first to bless you, in turning away every one of you from your wickedness.”</span>
</div>
