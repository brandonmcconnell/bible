<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Luke 24:19; Matthew 4:23–24"> The first book I wrote, Theophilus, concerned all that Jesus began both to do and to teach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 1:9; John 20:21; Isaiah 59:20–21"> until the day in which he was received up, after he had given commandment through the Holy Spirit to the apostles whom he had chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5–7; Acts 13:31; John 20:26"> To these he also showed himself alive after he suffered, by many proofs, appearing to them over a period of forty days and speaking about God’s Kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 24:49; John 14:16; John 15:26"> Being assembled together with them, he commanded them,<span className="woj">“Don’t depart from Jerusalem, but wait for the promise of the Father, which you heard from me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Acts 11:15–16; Luke 3:16"> <span className="woj">For John indeed baptized in water, but you will be baptized in the Holy Spirit not many days from now.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:11; Luke 17:20; Daniel 7:27"> Therefore, when they had come together, they asked him, “Lord, are you now restoring the kingdom to Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–2; Mark 13:32"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“It isn’t for you to know times or seasons which the Father has set within his own authority.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:15; Romans 15:19"> <span className="woj">But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you. You will be witnesses to me in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the uttermost parts of the earth.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Luke 24:50–51; Mark 16:19"> When he had said these things, as they were looking, he was taken up, and a cloud received him out of their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:4; John 20:12; Revelation 7:14"> While they were looking steadfastly into the sky as he went, behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Behold”, from “ἰδοὺ”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">a</a></sup>two men stood by them in white clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:26"> who also said, “You men of Galilee, why do you stand looking into the sky? This Jesus, who was received up from you into the sky, will come back in the same way as you saw him going into the sky.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:52; Matthew 21:1; Luke 24:50"> Then they returned to Jerusalem from the mountain called Olivet, which is near Jerusalem, a Sabbath day’s journey away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:8; Luke 22:12; Mark 14:15"> When they had come in, they went up into the upper room where they were staying, that is Peter, John, James, Andrew, Philip, Thomas, Bartholomew, Matthew, James the son of Alphaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas the son of James.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Ephesians 6:18; Colossians 4:2"> All these with one accord continued steadfastly in prayer and supplication, along with the women and Mary the mother of Jesus, and with his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:6; Acts 21:20; Revelation 3:4"> In these days, Peter stood up in the middle of the disciples (and the number of names was about one hundred twenty), and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Psalms 55:12–15"> “Brothers, it was necessary that this Scripture should be fulfilled, which the Holy Spirit spoke before by the mouth of David concerning Judas, who was guide to those who took Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 1:25; Mark 3:19; John 17:12"> For he was counted with us, and received his portion in this ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Matthew 26:14–15; Psalms 55:23"> Now this man obtained a field with the reward for his wickedness; and falling headlong, his body burst open and all his intestines gushed out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:22; Matthew 28:15; Acts 21:40"> It became known to everyone who lived in Jerusalem that in their language that field was called ‘Akeldama,’ that is, ‘The field of blood.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 69:25; Psalms 109:8–15; Acts 1:25"> For it is written in the book of Psalms,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Let his habitation be made desolate.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let no one dwell in it;’</span>and,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Let another take his office.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 15:27; Luke 10:1–2; Numbers 27:17"> “Of the men therefore who have accompanied us all the time that the Lord Jesus went in and out among us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:2; Acts 4:33; Acts 1:8–9"> beginning from the baptism of John to the day that he was received up from us, of these one must become a witness with us of his resurrection.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 1:26"> They put forward two: Joseph called Barsabbas, who was also called Justus, and Matthias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Acts 15:8; Hebrews 4:13"> They prayed and said, “You, Lord, who know the hearts of all men, show which one of these two you have chosen</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:2; John 6:70–71; John 17:12"> to take part in this ministry and apostleship from which Judas fell away, that he might go to his own place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:8; Joshua 18:10; 1 Samuel 14:41–42"> They drew lots for them, and the lot fell on Matthias; and he was counted with the eleven apostles.</span>
