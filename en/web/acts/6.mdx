<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:41; Acts 4:35; Acts 2:47"> Now in those days, when the number of the disciples was multiplying, a complaint arose from the Hellenists<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The Hellenists used Greek language and culture, even though they were also of Hebrew descent.">a</a></sup>against the Hebrews, because their widows were neglected in the daily service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:9–14; Exodus 18:17–26; 2 Timothy 2:4"> The twelve summoned the multitude of the disciples and said, “It is not appropriate for us to forsake the word of God and serve tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:7–15; James 3:17–18; Deuteronomy 1:13"> Therefore, select from among you, brothers, seven men of good report, full of the Holy Spirit and of wisdom, whom we may appoint over this business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:14; 1 Timothy 4:13–16; Philippians 1:9–11"> But we will continue steadfastly in prayer and in the ministry of the word.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 11:24; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:5–40"> These words pleased the whole multitude. They chose Stephen, a man full of faith and of the Holy Spirit, Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, and Nicolaus, a proselyte of Antioch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; Numbers 8:10; Acts 1:24"> whom they set before the apostles. When they had prayed, they laid their hands on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Acts 12:24; Colossians 1:6"> The word of God increased and the number of the disciples greatly multiplied in Jerusalem. A great company of the priests were obedient to the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:29–30; Acts 7:55; Acts 6:5"> Stephen, full of faith and power, performed great wonders and signs among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 22:3; Acts 15:41"> But some of those who were of the synagogue called “The Libertines”, and of the Cyrenians, of the Alexandrians, and of those of Cilicia and Asia arose, disputing with Stephen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:19–20; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> They weren’t able to withstand the wisdom and the Spirit by which he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; 1 Kings 21:10–13; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Then they secretly induced men to say, “We have heard him speak blasphemous words against Moses and God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Acts 14:2; Matthew 26:57"> They stirred up the people, the elders, and the scribes, and came against him and seized him, then brought him in to the council,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:11; Acts 21:28; Acts 25:8"> and set up false witnesses who said, “This man never stops speaking blasphemous words against this holy place and the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; Hebrews 12:26–28; Acts 21:21"> For we have heard him say that this Jesus of Nazareth will destroy this place, and will change the customs which Moses delivered to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 13:43; Ecclesiastes 8:1; Matthew 17:2"> All who sat in the council, fastening their eyes on him, saw his face like it was the face of an angel.</span>
