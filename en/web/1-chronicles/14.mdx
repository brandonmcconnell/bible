<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:3; 2 Samuel 5:11–16; Ezra 3:7"> Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David with cedar trees, masons, and carpenters, to build him a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:7; Psalms 89:20–37; 2 Samuel 7:16"> David perceived that the LORD had established him king over Israel, for his kingdom was highly exalted, for his people Israel’s sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; 2 Samuel 5:13; Ecclesiastes 7:26–29"> David took more wives in Jerusalem, and David became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:5–9; 2 Samuel 5:14; Luke 3:31"> These are the names of the children whom he had in Jerusalem: Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 5:15; 1 Chronicles 3:6"> Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:8; 2 Samuel 5:16"> Elishama, Beeliada, and Eliphelet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; Revelation 11:15–18; Psalms 2:1–6"> When the Philistines heard that David was anointed king over all Israel, all the Philistines went up to seek David; and David heard of it, and went out against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:15; 1 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Samuel 23:13"> Now the Philistines had come and made a raid in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:6; 1 Chronicles 14:14; 1 Chronicles 13:3"> David inquired of God, saying, “Shall I go up against the Philistines? Will you deliver them into my hand?” The LORD said to him, “Go up; for I will deliver them into your hand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:21; Exodus 14:28; 2 Samuel 5:20"> So they came up to Baal Perazim, and David defeated them there. David said, God has broken my enemies by my hand, like waters breaking out. Therefore they called the name of that place Baal Perazim.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Baal Perazim” means “The Lord who breaks out”.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; 1 Samuel 5:2–6; 2 Kings 19:18"> They left their gods there; and David gave a command, and they were burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 1 Kings 20:22; 2 Samuel 5:22–25"> The Philistines made another raid in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 14:10; Psalms 27:4"> David inquired again of God; and God said to him, “You shall not go up after them. Turn away from them, and come on them opposite the mulberry trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:4; Acts 2:2; Philippians 2:12–13"> When you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the mulberry trees, then go out to battle; for God has gone out before you to strike the army of the Philistines.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:14; John 13:17; Genesis 6:22"> David did as God commanded him; and they attacked the army of the Philistines from Gibeon even to Gezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 6:27; Exodus 15:14–16"> The fame of David went out into all lands; and the LORD brought the fear of him on all nations.</span>
