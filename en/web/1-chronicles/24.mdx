<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:6; Exodus 6:23; Numbers 26:60"> These were the divisions of the sons of Aaron. The sons of Aaron: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 10:2; Numbers 3:4; Numbers 26:61"> But Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and had no children; therefore Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 1 Chronicles 24:31; 1 Samuel 21:1"> David, with Zadok of the sons of Eleazar and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, divided them according to their ordering in their service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:24; Numbers 25:11–13; 1 Chronicles 15:6–12"> There were more chief men found of the sons of Eleazar than of the sons of Ithamar; and they were divided like this: of the sons of Eleazar there were sixteen, heads of fathers’ houses; and of the sons of Ithamar, according to their fathers’ houses, eight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:31; Joshua 18:10; Acts 4:1"> Thus they were divided impartially by drawing lots; for there were princes of the sanctuary and princes of God, both of the sons of Eleazar, and of the sons of Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:16; Matthew 8:19; Nehemiah 8:1"> Shemaiah the son of Nethanel the scribe, who was of the Levites, wrote them in the presence of the king, the princes, Zadok the priest, Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and the heads of the fathers’ households of the priests and of the Levites; one fathers’ house being taken for Eleazar, and one taken for Ithamar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 2:36; Nehemiah 11:10; Nehemiah 12:19"> Now the first lot came out to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 2:39; Nehemiah 7:35; Nehemiah 12:15"> the third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:17"> the fifth to Malchijah, the sixth to Mijamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:5; Nehemiah 12:4; Nehemiah 12:17"> the seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to Abijah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 2:36; Nehemiah 12:10; Nehemiah 7:39"> the ninth to Jeshua, the tenth to Shecaniah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10"> the eleventh to Eliashib, the twelfth to Jakim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> the thirteenth to Huppah, the fourteenth to Jeshebeab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:40; Ezra 2:37; Ezra 10:20"> the fifteenth to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> the seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Happizzez,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> the nineteenth to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Jehezkel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> the twenty-first to Jachin, the twenty-second to Gamul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the twenty-third to Delaiah, and the twenty-fourth to Maaziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 1 Corinthians 14:40; Hebrews 7:11"> This was their ordering in their service, to come into the LORD’s house according to the ordinance given to them by Aaron their father, as the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:24; 1 Chronicles 23:16; 1 Chronicles 6:18"> Of the rest of the sons of Levi: of the sons of Amram, Shubael; of the sons of Shubael, Jehdeiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:17; 1 Chronicles 26:25"> Of Rehabiah: of the sons of Rehabiah, Isshiah the chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:18; Numbers 3:27; Numbers 3:19"> Of the Izharites, Shelomoth; of the sons of Shelomoth, Jahath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:31"> The sons of Hebron: Jeriah, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:20"> The sons of Uzziel: Micah; of the sons of Micah, Shamir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:20; 1 Chronicles 15:18; 1 Chronicles 23:20"> The brother of Micah: Isshiah; of the sons of Isshiah, Zechariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:21"> The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. The son of Jaaziah: Beno.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 23:21; 1 Chronicles 6:19"> The sons of Merari by Jaaziah: Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:22"> Of Mahli: Eleazar, who had no sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Of Kish, the son of Kish: Jerahmeel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:23; 1 Chronicles 6:47"> The sons of Mushi: Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the sons of the Levites after their fathers’ houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:5–6; 1 Chronicles 26:13; 1 Chronicles 25:8"> These likewise cast lots even as their brothers the sons of Aaron in the presence of David the king, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the heads of the fathers’ households of the priests and of the Levites, the fathers’ households of the chief even as those of his younger brother.</span>
