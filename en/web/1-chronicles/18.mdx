<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:1–18; 1 Samuel 5:8; 2 Samuel 1:20"> After this, David defeated the Philistines and subdued them, and took Gath and its towns out of the hand of the Philistines.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 60:1; 2 Samuel 8:3; Genesis 15:18"> David defeated Hadadezer king of Zobah, toward Hamath, as he went to establish his dominion by the river Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:4; 1 Kings 10:26; 1 Kings 4:2"> David took from him one thousand chariots, seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand footmen; and David hamstrung all the chariot horses, but reserved of them enough for one hundred chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:6; 2 Samuel 8:5–6; 1 Samuel 14:47"> When the Syrians of Damascus came to help Hadadezer king of Zobah, David struck twenty-two thousand men of the Syrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:8; Psalms 121:8; Proverbs 21:31"> Then David put garrisons in Syria of Damascus; and the Syrians became servants to David and brought tribute. The LORD gave victory to David wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 10:16–17; 2 Chronicles 12:9–10"> David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer, and brought them to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:8; 2 Chronicles 4:12–18; 1 Kings 7:15–47"> From Tibhath and from Cun, cities of Hadadezer, David took very much bronze, with which Solomon made the bronze sea, the pillars, and the vessels of bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:9"> When Tou king of Hamath heard that David had struck all the army of Hadadezer king of Zobah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:10; Isaiah 39:1; 2 Chronicles 9:1"> he sent Hadoram his son to King David to greet him and to bless him, because he had fought against Hadadezer and struck him (for Hadadezer had wars with Tou); and he had with him all kinds of vessels of gold and silver and bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Chronicles 26:26–27; 1 Kings 7:51"> King David also dedicated these to the LORD, with the silver and the gold that he carried away from all the nations: from Edom, from Moab, from the children of Ammon, from the Philistines, and from Amalek.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:11; 2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 8:13–14"> Moreover Abishai the son of Zeruiah struck eighteen thousand of the Edomites in the Valley of Salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:6; Genesis 27:37; Psalms 144:10"> He put garrisons in Edom; and all the Edomites became servants to David. The LORD gave victory to David wherever he went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 78:71–72"> David reigned over all Israel; and he executed justice and righteousness for all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:6; 1 Kings 4:3; 2 Samuel 8:16"> Joab the son of Zeruiah was over the army; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was recorder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Kings 4:3"> Zadok the son of Ahitub and Abimelech the son of Abiathar were priests; Shavsha was scribe;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; 2 Samuel 15:18; 1 Kings 2:34–35"> and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites; and the sons of David were chief officials serving the king.</span>
</div>
