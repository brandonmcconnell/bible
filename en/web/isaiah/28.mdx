<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 7:5; Isaiah 28:7; 2 Kings 14:25–27"> Woe to the crown of pride of the drunkards of Ephraim, and to the fading flower of his glorious beauty, which is on the head of the fertile valley of those who are overcome with wine!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Nahum 1:8; Ezekiel 13:11"> Behold, the Lord has one who is mighty and strong. Like a storm of hail, a destroying storm, and like a storm of mighty waters overflowing, he will cast them down to the earth with his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 26:6; Hebrews 10:29; Revelation 11:2"> The crown of pride of the drunkards of Ephraim will be trodden under foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nahum 3:12; Micah 7:1; Isaiah 28:1"> The fading flower of his glorious beauty, which is on the head of the fertile valley, shall be like the first-ripe fig before the summer, which someone picks and eats as soon as he sees it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 5:4; Isaiah 62:3; Isaiah 60:19"> In that day, the LORD of Armies will become a crown of glory and a diadem of beauty to the residue of his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 18:32–34; John 5:30; Joshua 1:9"> and a spirit of justice to him who sits in judgment, and strength to those who turn back the battle at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:10–12; Luke 21:34; Isaiah 19:14"> They also reel with wine, and stagger with strong drink. The priest and the prophet reel with strong drink. They are swallowed up by wine. They stagger with strong drink. They err in vision. They stumble in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:26; Proverbs 26:11; Habakkuk 2:15–16"> For all tables are completely full of filthy vomit and filthiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 131:2; Jeremiah 6:10; John 12:38"> Whom will he teach knowledge? To whom will he explain the message? Those who are weaned from the milk, and drawn from the breasts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 28:13; 2 Timothy 3:7; Jeremiah 11:7"> For it is precept on precept, precept on precept; line on line, line on line; here a little, there a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:21; Deuteronomy 28:49; Isaiah 33:19"> But he will speak to this nation with stammering lips and in another language,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 11:28–29; Isaiah 30:15; Jeremiah 6:16"> to whom he said, “This is the resting place. Give rest to the weary,” and “This is the refreshing;” yet they would not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 13:14; Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Peter 3:16"> Therefore the LORD’s word will be to them precept on precept, precept on precept; line on line, line on line; here a little, there a little; that they may go, fall backward, be broken, be snared, and be taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 29:20; Isaiah 1:10"> Therefore hear the LORD’s word, you scoffers, that rule this people in Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Ezekiel 13:22; Amos 2:4"> “Because you have said, ‘We have made a covenant with death, and we are in agreement with Sheol.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Sheol is the place of the dead.">a</a></sup>When the overflowing scourge passes through, it won’t come to us; for we have made lies our refuge, and we have hidden ourselves under falsehood.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 118:22; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:6–8"> Therefore the Lord GOD says, “Behold, I lay in Zion for a foundation a stone, a tried stone, a precious cornerstone of a sure foundation. He who believes shall not act hastily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:13; Amos 7:7–9; Revelation 16:21"> I will make justice the measuring line, and righteousness the plumb line. The hail will sweep away the refuge of lies, and the waters will overflow the hiding place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:7; Isaiah 8:10; Isaiah 28:15"> Your covenant with death shall be annulled, and your agreement with Sheol<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Sheol is the place of the dead.">b</a></sup>shall not stand. When the overflowing scourge passes through, then you will be trampled down by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Isaiah 36:22; 2 Kings 24:2"> As often as it passes through, it will seize you; for morning by morning it will pass through, by day and by night; and it will be nothing but terror to understand the message.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; 1 Corinthians 1:18–31; Romans 9:30–32"> For the bed is too short to stretch out on, and the blanket is too narrow to wrap oneself in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:11; Joshua 10:12; 2 Samuel 5:20"> For the LORD will rise up as on Mount Perazim. He will be angry as in the valley of Gibeon; that he may do his work, his unusual work, and bring to pass his act, his extraordinary act.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22–23; Isaiah 24:1–23; Matthew 27:44"> Now therefore don’t be scoffers, lest your bonds be made strong; for I have heard a decree of destruction from the Lord, GOD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name (Hebrew “יהוה”, usually pronounced Yahweh).">c</a></sup>of Armies, on the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Jeremiah 22:29; Revelation 2:11"> Give ear, and hear my voice! Listen, and hear my speech!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Hosea 10:11–12"> Does he who plows to sow plow continually? Does he keep turning the soil and breaking the clods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:9; Matthew 23:23; Exodus 9:31–32"> When he has leveled its surface, doesn’t he plant the dill, and scatter the cumin seed, and put in the wheat in rows, the barley in the appointed place, and the spelt in its place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 31:3–6; Exodus 36:2; Job 39:17"> For his God instructs him in right judgment and teaches him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15; Isaiah 27:7–8; Jeremiah 46:28"> For the dill isn’t threshed with a sharp instrument, neither is a cart wheel turned over the cumin; but the dill is beaten out with a stick, and the cumin with a rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:9–10; Matthew 3:12; John 12:24"> Bread flour must be ground; so he will not always be threshing it. Although he drives the wheel of his threshing cart over it, his horses don’t grind it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Jeremiah 32:19; Job 5:9"> This also comes out from the LORD of Armies, who is wonderful in counsel, and excellent in wisdom.</span>
