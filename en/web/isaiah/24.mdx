<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Isaiah 2:19; Isaiah 42:15"> Behold, the LORD makes the earth empty, makes it waste, turns it upside down, and scatters its inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 4:9; Lamentations 5:12–14; Ezekiel 7:12–13"> It will be as with the people, so with the priest; as with the servant, so with his master; as with the maid, so with her mistress; as with the buyer, so with the seller; as with the creditor, so with the debtor; as with the taker of interest, so with the giver of interest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1; Isaiah 6:11; Deuteronomy 29:23"> The earth will be utterly emptied and utterly laid waste; for the LORD has spoken this word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Isaiah 33:9; Isaiah 3:26"> The earth mourns and fades away. The world languishes and fades away. The lofty people of the earth languish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Romans 8:20–21; Daniel 7:25"> The earth also is polluted under its inhabitants, because they have transgressed the laws, violated the statutes, and broken the everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 4:1; Matthew 7:14; Zechariah 5:3–4"> Therefore the curse has devoured the earth, and those who dwell therein are found guilty. Therefore the inhabitants of the earth are burned, and few men are left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 16:10; Joel 1:10–12; Isaiah 16:8"> The new wine mourns. The vine languishes. All the merry-hearted sigh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hosea 2:11; Revelation 18:22; Ezekiel 26:13"> The mirth of tambourines ceases. The sound of those who rejoice ends. The joy of the harp ceases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 5:22; Ecclesiastes 9:7; Isaiah 5:20"> They will not drink wine with a song. Strong drink will be bitter to those who drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:5–6; Isaiah 24:12"> The confused city is broken down. Every house is shut up, that no man may come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 7:14; Isaiah 24:7–9; Jeremiah 48:33"> There is a crying in the streets because of the wine. All joy is darkened. The mirth of the land is gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Micah 1:12; Lamentations 5:18"> The city is left in desolation, and the gate is struck with destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Ezekiel 6:8–11; Ezekiel 11:16–20"> For it will be so within the earth among the peoples, as the shaking of an olive tree, as the gleanings when the vintage is done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Jeremiah 31:12; Isaiah 54:1"> These shall lift up their voice. They will shout for the majesty of the LORD. They cry aloud from the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 1:11; Isaiah 42:4; Isaiah 60:9"> Therefore glorify the LORD in the east, even the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, in the islands of the sea!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 5:11; Isaiah 33:1"> From the uttermost part of the earth have we heard songs. Glory to the righteous! But I said, “I pine away! I pine away! woe is me!” The treacherous have dealt treacherously. Yes, the treacherous have dealt very treacherously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:43–44; 1 Kings 19:17; Leviticus 26:21–22"> Fear, the pit, and the snare are on you who inhabit the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 7:11; Psalms 18:7; Joshua 10:10–11"> It will happen that he who flees from the noise of the fear will fall into the pit; and he who comes up out of the middle of the pit will be taken in the snare; for the windows on high are opened, and the foundations of the earth tremble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4–10; Deuteronomy 11:6; Revelation 20:11"> The earth is utterly broken. The earth is torn apart. The earth is shaken violently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; Hosea 4:1–5; Daniel 11:19"> The earth will stagger like a drunken man, and will sway back and forth like a hammock. Its disobedience will be heavy on it, and it will fall and not rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 76:12; Isaiah 34:2–17; Zechariah 14:12–19"> It will happen in that day that the LORD will punish the army of the high ones on high, and the kings of the earth on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Zechariah 9:11; Isaiah 42:22; Jeremiah 38:6–13"> They will be gathered together, as prisoners are gathered in the pit, and will be shut up in the prison; and after many days they will be visited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19; Revelation 21:23; Isaiah 13:10"> Then the moon will be confounded, and the sun ashamed; for the LORD of Armies will reign on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem; and glory will be before his elders.</span>
