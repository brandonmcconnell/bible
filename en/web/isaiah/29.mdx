<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 4:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:9; Micah 6:6–7"> Woe to Ariel! Ariel, the city where David encamped! Add year to year; let the feasts come around;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:25–30; Isaiah 3:26; Isaiah 10:32"> then I will distress Ariel, and there will be mourning and lamentation. She shall be to me as an altar hearth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, Ariel">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43–44; Ezekiel 21:22; 2 Kings 25:1–4"> I will encamp against you all around you, and will lay siege against you with posted troops. I will raise siege works against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Psalms 44:25; Isaiah 3:8"> You will be brought down, and will speak out of the ground. Your speech will mumble out of the dust. Your voice will be as of one who has a familiar spirit, out of the ground, and your speech will whisper out of the dust.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Isaiah 17:13–14; Isaiah 37:36"> But the multitude of your foes will be like fine dust, and the multitude of the ruthless ones like chaff that blows away. Yes, it will be in an instant, suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:8; 1 Samuel 2:10; Matthew 24:7"> She will be visited by the LORD of Armies with thunder, with earthquake, with great noise, with whirlwind and storm, and with the flame of a devouring fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 20:8; Micah 4:11–12; Zechariah 12:9"> The multitude of all the nations that fight against Ariel, even all who fight against her and her stronghold, and who distress her, will be like a dream, a vision of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:12; Psalms 73:20; Isaiah 10:7–16"> It will be like when a hungry man dreams, and behold, he eats; but he awakes, and his hunger isn’t satisfied; or like when a thirsty man dreams, and behold, he drinks; but he awakes, and behold, he is faint, and he is still thirsty. The multitude of all the nations that fight against Mount Zion will be like that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 51:21–22; Isaiah 19:14; Revelation 17:6"> Pause and wonder! Blind yourselves and be blind! They are drunken, but not with wine; they stagger, but not with strong drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 11:8; Isaiah 44:18; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–12"> For the LORD has poured out on you a spirit of deep sleep, and has closed your eyes, the prophets; and he has covered your heads, the seers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:11; Daniel 12:4; Isaiah 8:16"> All vision has become to you like the words of a book that is sealed, which men deliver to one who is educated, saying, “Read this, please;” and he says, “I can’t, for it is sealed;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 7:15–16; Isaiah 29:18; Jeremiah 5:4"> and the book is delivered to one who is not educated, saying, “Read this, please;” and he says, “I can’t read.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:2; Ezekiel 33:31–33; Mark 7:2–13"> The Lord said, “Because this people draws near with their mouth and honors me with their lips, but they have removed their heart far from me, and their fear of me is a commandment of men which has been taught;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:19–24; Isaiah 28:21"> therefore, behold, I will proceed to do a marvelous work among this people, even a marvelous work and a wonder; and the wisdom of their wise men will perish, and the understanding of their prudent men will be hidden.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:12; Isaiah 47:10; Isaiah 30:1"> Woe to those who deeply hide their counsel from the LORD, and whose deeds are in the dark, and who say, “Who sees us?” and “Who knows us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:19–21; Isaiah 64:8; Isaiah 45:9–11"> You turn things upside down! Should the potter be thought to be like clay, that the thing made should say about him who made it, “He didn’t make me;” or the thing formed say of him who formed it, “He has no understanding”?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 32:15; Matthew 19:30; Hebrews 10:37"> Isn’t it yet a very little while, and Lebanon will be turned into a fruitful field, and the fruitful field will be regarded as a forest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; Matthew 11:5; Mark 7:37"> In that day, the deaf will hear the words of the book, and the eyes of the blind will see out of obscurity and out of darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 2:5; Isaiah 61:1; Matthew 11:29"> The humble also will increase their joy in the LORD, and the poor among men will rejoice in the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 2:1; Isaiah 28:14–22; Luke 16:14"> For the ruthless is brought to nothing, and the scoffer ceases, and all those who are alert to do evil are cut off—</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 5:10–12; James 5:6; Acts 3:14"> who cause a person to be indicted by a word, and lay a snare for one who reproves in the gate, and who deprive the innocent of justice with false testimony.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 45:17; Isaiah 51:2; Jeremiah 30:10"> Therefore the LORD, who redeemed Abraham, says concerning the house of Jacob: “Jacob shall no longer be ashamed, neither shall his face grow pale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 2:10; Isaiah 19:25; Isaiah 45:11"> But when he sees his children, the work of my hands, in the middle of him, they will sanctify my name. Yes, they will sanctify the Holy One of Jacob, and will stand in awe of the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 15:17–19; Hebrews 5:2; Isaiah 28:7"> They also who err in spirit will come to understanding, and those who grumble will receive instruction.”</span>
</div>
</div>
