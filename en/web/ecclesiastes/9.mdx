<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:3; John 10:27–30; 1 Peter 1:5"> For all this I laid to my heart, even to explore all this: that the righteous, and the wise, and their works, are in the hand of God; whether it is love or hatred, man doesn’t know it; all is before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:18; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Malachi 3:5"> All things come alike to all. There is one event to the righteous and to the wicked; to the good, to the clean, to the unclean, to him who sacrifices, and to him who doesn’t sacrifice. As is the good, so is the sinner; he who takes an oath, as he who fears an oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11; Ecclesiastes 1:17"> This is an evil in all that is done under the sun, that there is one event to all. Yes also, the heart of the sons of men is full of evil, and madness is in their heart while they live, and after that they go to the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:7–12; Isaiah 38:18; Luke 16:26–29"> For to him who is joined with all the living there is hope; for a living dog is better than a dead lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 26:14; Ecclesiastes 1:11; Psalms 6:5"> For the living know that they will die, but the dead don’t know anything, neither do they have any more a reward; for their memory is forgotten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 146:3–4; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Job 3:17–18"> Also their love, their hatred, and their envy has perished long ago; neither do they any longer have a portion forever in anything that is done under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Deuteronomy 12:7; Ecclesiastes 10:19"> Go your way—eat your bread with joy, and drink your wine with a merry heart; for God has already accepted your works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:14; Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8"> Let your garments be always white, and don’t let your head lack oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; Ecclesiastes 2:10; Malachi 2:15"> Live joyfully with the wife whom you love all the days of your life of vanity, which he has given you under the sun, all your days of vanity, for that is your portion in life, and in your labor in which you labor under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 5:16; Romans 12:11"> Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with your might; for there is no work, nor plan, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in Sheol,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Sheol is the place of the dead.">a</a></sup>where you are going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23; Proverbs 21:30–31; Lamentations 3:37–38"> I returned and saw under the sun that the race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong, neither yet bread to the wise, nor yet riches to men of understanding, nor yet favor to men of skill; but time and chance happen to them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Luke 21:34–36; Ecclesiastes 8:5–7"> For man also doesn’t know his time. As the fish that are taken in an evil net, and as the birds that are caught in the snare, even so are the sons of men snared in an evil time, when it falls suddenly on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:16; Ecclesiastes 7:15; Ecclesiastes 6:1"> I have also seen wisdom under the sun in this way, and it seemed great to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:15–22; 2 Kings 6:24–20"> There was a little city, and few men within it; and a great king came against it, besieged it, and built great bulwarks against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:13; Genesis 40:23; Esther 6:2–3"> Now a poor wise man was found in it, and he by his wisdom delivered the city; yet no man remembered that same poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Proverbs 24:5; Proverbs 21:22"> Then I said, “Wisdom is better than strength.” Nevertheless the poor man’s wisdom is despised, and his words are not heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 3:17–18; Proverbs 28:23; James 1:20"> The words of the wise heard in quiet are better than the cry of him who rules among fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 7:11–12; Ecclesiastes 9:16; Joshua 7:5"> Wisdom is better than weapons of war; but one sinner destroys much good.</span>
