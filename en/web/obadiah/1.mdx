<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:11–12; Joel 3:19; Ezekiel 35:3–15"> The vision of Obadiah. This is what the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word translated “Lord” is “Adonai.”">a</a></sup>GOD<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name (Hebrew “יהוה”, usually pronounced Yahweh).">b</a></sup>says about Edom. We have heard news from the LORD, and an ambassador is sent among the nations, saying, “Arise, and let’s rise up against her in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:18; Isaiah 23:9; Micah 7:10"> Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">c</a></sup>I have made you small among the nations. You are greatly despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Proverbs 29:23; Jeremiah 49:16"> The pride of your heart has deceived you, you who dwell in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is high, who says in his heart, ‘Who will bring me down to the ground?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 9:2; Job 20:6–7; Habakkuk 2:9"> Though you mount on high as the eagle, and though your nest is set among the stars, I will bring you down from there,” says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:9; Deuteronomy 24:21; Isaiah 17:6"> “If thieves came to you, if robbers by night—oh, what disaster awaits you—wouldn’t they only steal until they had enough? If grape pickers came to you, wouldn’t they leave some gleaning grapes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:10; Psalms 139:1; Isaiah 45:3"> How Esau will be ransacked! How his hidden treasures are sought out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Jeremiah 49:7; Jeremiah 30:14"> All the men of your alliance have brought you on your way, even to the border. The men who were at peace with you have deceived you, and prevailed against you. Friends who eat your bread lay a snare under you. There is no understanding in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Job 5:12–14; Psalms 33:10"> “Won’t I in that day”, says the LORD, “destroy the wise men out of Edom, and understanding out of the mountain of Esau?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Amos 1:12; Isaiah 63:1–3"> Your mighty men, Teman, will be dismayed, to the end that everyone may be cut off from the mountain of Esau by slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Malachi 1:3–4; Genesis 27:41"> For the violence done to your brother Jacob, shame will cover you, and you will be cut off forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 137:7; Nahum 3:10; Joel 3:3"> In the day that you stood on the other side, in the day that strangers carried away his substance and foreigners entered into his gates and cast lots for Jerusalem, even you were like one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Micah 4:11; Proverbs 17:5"> But don’t look down on your brother in the day of his disaster, and don’t rejoice over the children of Judah in the day of their destruction. Don’t speak proudly in the day of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 35:10"> Don’t enter into the gate of my people in the day of their calamity. Don’t look down on their affliction in the day of their calamity, neither seize their wealth on the day of their calamity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:6; Psalms 31:8; Obadiah 1:12"> Don’t stand in the crossroads to cut off those of his who escape. Don’t deliver up those of his who remain in the day of distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 2:13; Habakkuk 2:8; Ezekiel 30:3"> For the day of the LORD is near all the nations! As you have done, it will be done to you. Your deeds will return upon your own head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Jeremiah 49:12; Jeremiah 25:15–16"> For as you have drunk on my holy mountain, so all the nations will drink continually. Yes, they will drink, swallow down, and will be as though they had not been.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> But in Mount Zion, there will be those who escape, and it will be holy. The house of Jacob will possess their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Isaiah 10:17; Obadiah 1:16"> The house of Jacob will be a fire, the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau for stubble. They will burn among them and devour them. There will not be any remaining to the house of Esau.” Indeed, the LORD has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:44; Zephaniah 2:4–7; Amos 9:12"> Those of the South will possess the mountain of Esau, and those of the lowland, the Philistines. They will possess the field of Ephraim, and the field of Samaria. Benjamin will possess Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–10; Luke 4:26; Jeremiah 32:44"> The captives of this army of the children of Israel, who are among the Canaanites, will possess even to Zarephath; and the captives of Jerusalem, who are in Sepharad, will possess the cities of the Negev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; James 5:20; Revelation 11:15"> Saviors will go up on Mount Zion to judge the mountains of Esau, and the kingdom will be the LORD’s.</span>
