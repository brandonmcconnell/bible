<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:21; Hebrews 7:1; Joshua 8:2"> Now when Adoni-Zedek king of Jerusalem heard how Joshua had taken Ai, and had utterly destroyed it; as he had done to Jericho and her king, so he had done to Ai and her king; and how the inhabitants of Gibeon had made peace with Israel, and were among them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Joshua 2:24; Exodus 15:14–16"> they were very afraid, because Gibeon was a great city, as one of the royal cities, and because it was greater than Ai, and all its men were mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:9; Micah 1:13; Joshua 10:1"> Therefore Adoni-Zedek king of Jerusalem sent to Hoham king of Hebron, Piram king of Jarmuth, Japhia king of Lachish, and Debir king of Eglon, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 9:15; Joshua 10:1; Isaiah 8:9–10"> “Come up to me and help me. Let’s strike Gibeon; for they have made peace with Joshua and with the children of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 8:9–10; Genesis 15:16; Joshua 10:6"> Therefore the five kings of the Amorites, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon, gathered themselves together and went up, they and all their armies, and encamped against Gibeon, and made war against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 5:10; Deuteronomy 1:15"> The men of Gibeon sent to Joshua at the camp at Gilgal, saying, “Don’t abandon your servants! Come up to us quickly and save us! Help us; for all the kings of the Amorites that dwell in the hill country have gathered together against us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 8:1; Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 8:14"> So Joshua went up from Gilgal, he and the whole army with him, including all the mighty men of valor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 3:2; Psalms 27:1–2"> The LORD said to Joshua, “Don’t fear them, for I have delivered them into your hands. Not a man of them will stand before you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; 2 Timothy 2:3; Proverbs 24:11–12"> Joshua therefore came to them suddenly. He marched from Gilgal all night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Joshua 16:3; Joshua 16:5"> The LORD confused them before Israel. He killed them with a great slaughter at Gibeon, and chased them by the way of the ascent of Beth Horon, and struck them to Azekah and to Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:2; Judges 5:20; Psalms 18:12–14"> As they fled from before Israel, while they were at the descent of Beth Horon, the LORD hurled down great stones from the sky on them to Azekah, and they died. There were more who died from the hailstones than those whom the children of Israel killed with the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Isaiah 38:8; Isaiah 60:20"> Then Joshua spoke to the LORD in the day when the LORD delivered up the Amorites before the children of Israel. He said in the sight of Israel, “Sun, stand still on Gibeon! You, moon, stop in the valley of Aijalon!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Isaiah 38:8; Joshua 10:14"> The sun stood still, and the moon stayed, until the nation had avenged themselves of their enemies. Isn’t this written in the book of Jashar? The sun stayed in the middle of the sky, and didn’t hurry to go down about a whole day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; Exodus 14:14; Joshua 10:42"> There was no day like that before it or after it, that the LORD listened to the voice of a man; for the LORD fought for Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 10:43; Joshua 10:6"> Joshua returned, and all Israel with him, to the camp to Gilgal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 24:8; Amos 9:2; Psalms 139:7–10"> These five kings fled, and hid themselves in the cave at Makkedah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Joshua was told, saying, “The five kings have been found, hidden in the cave at Makkedah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Job 21:30; Amos 9:1"> Joshua said, “Roll large stones to cover the cave’s entrance, and set men by it to guard them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:37–41; Jeremiah 8:14; Isaiah 9:14–15"> but don’t stay there. Pursue your enemies, and attack them from the rear. Don’t allow them to enter into their cities; for the LORD your God has delivered them into your hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 8:24; 2 Samuel 20:6; Joshua 10:10"> When Joshua and the children of Israel had finished killing them with a very great slaughter until they were consumed, and the remnant which remained of them had entered into the fortified cities,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 11:7; Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 57:4"> all the people returned to the camp to Joshua at Makkedah in peace. None moved his tongue against any of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:32"> Then Joshua said, “Open the cave entrance, and bring those five kings out of the cave to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:1; Joshua 10:5; Joshua 10:3"> They did so, and brought those five kings out of the cave to him: the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachi 4:3; Romans 16:20; Revelation 2:26–27"> When they brought those kings out to Joshua, Joshua called for all the men of Israel, and said to the chiefs of the men of war who went with him, “Come near. Put your feet on the necks of these kings.”<br />They came near, and put their feet on their necks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10"> Joshua said to them, “Don’t be afraid, nor be dismayed. Be strong and courageous, for the LORD will do this to all your enemies against whom you fight.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 8:29; 2 Samuel 21:9; Matthew 27:25"> Afterward Joshua struck them, put them to death, and hanged them on five trees. They were hanging on the trees until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:29; Deuteronomy 21:22–23; Joshua 7:26"> At the time of the going down of the sun, Joshua commanded, and they took them down off the trees, and threw them into the cave in which they had hidden themselves, and laid great stones on the mouth of the cave, which remain to this very day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 7:16; Deuteronomy 20:16–17"> Joshua took Makkedah on that day, and struck it with the edge of the sword, with its king. He utterly destroyed it and all the souls who were in it. He left no one remaining. He did to the king of Makkedah as he had done to the king of Jericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 15:42; Jeremiah 52:1"> Joshua passed from Makkedah, and all Israel with him, to Libnah, and fought against Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The LORD delivered it also, with its king, into the hand of Israel. He struck it with the edge of the sword, and all the souls who were in it. He left no one remaining in it. He did to its king as he had done to the king of Jericho.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:5"> Joshua passed from Libnah, and all Israel with him, to Lachish, and encamped against it, and fought against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> The LORD delivered Lachish into the hand of Israel. He took it on the second day, and struck it with the edge of the sword, with all the souls who were in it, according to all that he had done to Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Judges 1:29; Joshua 16:3"> Then Horam king of Gezer came up to help Lachish; and Joshua struck him and his people, until he had left him no one remaining.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 12:12; Joshua 15:39"> Joshua passed from Lachish, and all Israel with him, to Eglon; and they encamped against it and fought against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 26:44; Joshua 10:37; Job 19:10"> They took it on that day, and struck it with the edge of the sword. He utterly destroyed all the souls who were in it that day, according to all that he had done to Lachish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13; Joshua 14:13–14"> Joshua went up from Eglon, and all Israel with him, to Hebron; and they fought against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> They took it, and struck it with the edge of the sword, with its king and all its cities, and all the souls who were in it. He left no one remaining, according to all that he had done to Eglon; but he utterly destroyed it, and all the souls who were in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 12:13; Joshua 15:49"> Joshua returned, and all Israel with him, to Debir, and fought against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Obadiah 1:18; Joshua 11:8; Deuteronomy 3:3"> He took it, with its king and all its cities. They struck them with the edge of the sword, and utterly destroyed all the souls who were in it. He left no one remaining. As he had done to Hebron, so he did to Debir, and to its king; as he had done also to Libnah, and to its king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2–16; Exodus 23:31–33; Joshua 8:27"> So Joshua struck all the land, the hill country, the South, the lowland, the slopes, and all their kings. He left no one remaining, but he utterly destroyed all that breathed, as the LORD, the God of Israel, commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Joshua 11:16; Joshua 15:51; Deuteronomy 9:23"> Joshua struck them from Kadesh Barnea even to Gaza, and all the country of Goshen, even to Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Joshua 10:14; Psalms 46:7; Psalms 80:3"> Joshua took all these kings and their land at one time because the LORD, the God of Israel, fought for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 10:15; Joshua 4:19; 1 Samuel 11:14"> Joshua returned, and all Israel with him, to the camp to Gilgal.</span>
</div>
