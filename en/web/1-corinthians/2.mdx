<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> When I came to you, brothers, I didn’t come with excellence of speech or of wisdom, proclaiming to you the testimony of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 6:14; John 17:3; 1 Corinthians 1:22–25"> For I determined not to know anything among you except Jesus Christ and him crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:4; 1 Corinthians 4:10–13; 2 Corinthians 12:5–10"> I was with you in weakness, in fear, and in much trembling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:20; 1 Thessalonians 1:5; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> My speech and my preaching were not in persuasive words of human wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7; 2 Corinthians 12:9; 2 Corinthians 6:7"> that your faith wouldn’t stand in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 5:14; James 3:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4"> We speak wisdom, however, among those who are full grown, yet a wisdom not of this world nor of the rulers of this world who are coming to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:26–27; 2 Peter 1:3; Matthew 11:25"> But we speak God’s wisdom in a mystery, the wisdom that has been hidden, which God foreordained before the worlds for our glory,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:27; James 2:1; Acts 7:2"> which none of the rulers of this world has known. For had they known it, they wouldn’t have crucified the Lord of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; Psalms 31:19; James 1:12"> But as it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Things which an eye didn’t see, and an ear didn’t hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which didn’t enter into the heart of man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">these God has prepared for those who love him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 14:26; Ephesians 3:5; Luke 10:21"> But to us, God revealed them through the Spirit. For the Spirit searches all things, yes, the deep things of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 20:27; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Romans 11:33–34"> For who among men knows the things of a man except the spirit of the man which is in him? Even so, no one knows the things of God except God’s Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 2:27; Romans 8:5–6; 1 John 2:20"> But we received not the spirit of the world, but the Spirit which is from God, that we might know the things that were freely given to us by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:4; 1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 1:17"> We also speak these things, not in words which man’s wisdom teaches but which the Holy Spirit teaches, comparing spiritual things with spiritual things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Romans 8:5–8; Matthew 13:11–17"> Now the natural man doesn’t receive the things of God’s Spirit, for they are foolishness to him; and he can’t know them, because they are spiritually discerned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:21; Proverbs 28:5; Hebrews 5:14"> But he who is spiritual discerns all things, and he himself is to be judged by no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13–14; Romans 11:34; John 16:13–16"> “For who has known the mind of the Lord that he should instruct him?” But we have Christ’s mind.</span>
