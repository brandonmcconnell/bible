<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 12:13; 2 Corinthians 8:1–9; 2 Corinthians 9:12–15"> Now concerning the collection for the saints: as I commanded the assemblies of Galatia, you do likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:9–10; Deuteronomy 15:11–14; Deuteronomy 8:18"> On the first day of every week, let each one of you save as he may prosper, that no collections are made when I come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; 1 Corinthians 11:34; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21"> When I arrive, I will send whoever you approve with letters to carry your gracious gift to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:4; 2 Corinthians 8:19; Romans 15:25"> If it is appropriate for me to go also, they will go with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 19:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; 2 Corinthians 1:15–17"> I will come to you when I have passed through Macedonia, for I am passing through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Romans 15:24; Acts 20:38"> But with you it may be that I will stay with you, or even winter with you, that you may send me on my journey wherever I go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 18:21; 1 Corinthians 4:19; James 4:15"> For I do not wish to see you now in passing, but I hope to stay a while with you, if the Lord permits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 2:1; Exodus 23:16; Acts 18:19"> But I will stay at Ephesus until Pentecost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:12; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> for a great and effective door has opened to me, and there are many adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Romans 16:21"> Now if Timothy comes, see that he is with you without fear, for he does the work of the Lord, as I also do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:12; Acts 15:33; Titus 2:15"> Therefore let no one despise him. But set him forward on his journey in peace, that he may come to me; for I expect him with the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:5; Titus 3:4"> Now concerning Apollos the brother, I strongly urged him to come to you with the brothers, but it was not at all his desire to come now; but he will come when he has an opportunity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:13–18; Ephesians 6:10"> Watch! Stand firm in the faith! Be courageous! Be strong!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:34–35; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Let all that you do be done in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:16; Romans 16:5; Romans 16:2"> Now I beg you, brothers—you know the house of Stephanas, that it is the first fruits of Achaia, and that they have set themselves to serve the saints—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12; 3 John 1:8"> that you also be in subjection to such, and to everyone who helps in the work and labors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 2:30; Philemon 1:13"> I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus; for that which was lacking on your part, they supplied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; 2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:12"> For they refreshed my spirit and yours. Therefore acknowledge those who are like that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; Philemon 1:2; Romans 16:3–5"> The assemblies of Asia greet you. Aquila and Priscilla greet you warmly in the Lord, together with the assembly that is in their house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 16:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Peter 5:14"> All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Colossians 4:18; Galatians 6:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:17"> This greeting is by me, Paul, with my own hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 6:24; John 14:21; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> If any man doesn’t love the Lord Jesus Christ, let him be cursed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Greek: anathema.">a</a></sup>Come, Lord!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Aramaic: Maranatha!">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 16:20; Romans 16:23"> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:16; Revelation 3:19; 2 Corinthians 12:15"> My love to all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen.</span>
