<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:25; Leviticus 21:11; Leviticus 10:6–7"> The LORD said to Moses, “Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and say to them, ‘A priest shall not defile himself for the dead among his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:13; Leviticus 18:6"> except for his relatives that are near to him: for his mother, for his father, for his son, for his daughter, for his brother,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and for his virgin sister who is near to him, who has had no husband; for her he may defile himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:16–17"> He shall not defile himself, being a chief man among his people, to profane himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20; Leviticus 19:27–28"> “‘They shall not shave their heads or shave off the corners of their beards or make any cuttings in their flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Leviticus 18:21; 1 Peter 2:9"> They shall be holy to their God, and not profane the name of their God, for they offer the offerings of the LORD made by fire, the bread of their God. Therefore they shall be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 21:13–14; Ezekiel 44:22; Leviticus 21:8"> “‘They shall not marry a woman who is a prostitute, or profane. A priest shall not marry a woman divorced from her husband; for he is holy to his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 20:7–8; Leviticus 19:2; Leviticus 11:44–45"> Therefore you shall sanctify him, for he offers the bread of your God. He shall be holy to you, for I the LORD, who sanctify you, am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 38:24; Leviticus 19:29; Ezekiel 9:6"> “‘The daughter of any priest, if she profanes herself by playing the prostitute, she profanes her father. She shall be burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 10:6–7; Leviticus 16:32; Matthew 26:65"> “‘He who is the high priest among his brothers, upon whose head the anointing oil is poured, and who is consecrated to put on the garments, shall not let the hair of his head hang loose, or tear his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 19:14; Leviticus 21:1–2; Numbers 6:7"> He must not go in to any dead body, or defile himself for his father or for his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:7; Exodus 29:6–7; Leviticus 8:9–12"> He shall not go out of the sanctuary, nor profane the sanctuary of his God; for the crown of the anointing oil of his God is upon him. I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 14:4"> “‘He shall take a wife in her virginity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 21:7; Ezekiel 44:22"> He shall not marry a widow, or one divorced, or a woman who has been defiled, or a prostitute. He shall take a virgin of his own people as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 11:16; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> He shall not profane his offspring among his people, for I am the LORD who sanctifies him.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 16:5; Leviticus 10:3"> “Say to Aaron, ‘None of your offspring throughout their generations who has a defect may approach to offer the bread of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2–3; Leviticus 22:19–25; Titus 1:7"> For whatever man he is that has a defect, he shall not draw near: a blind man, or a lame, or he who has a flat nose, or any deformity,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> or a man who has an injured foot, or an injured hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:1; Isaiah 56:3"> or hunchbacked, or a dwarf, or one who has a defect in his eye, or an itching disease, or scabs, or who has damaged testicles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 21:6; Leviticus 21:8; Leviticus 21:17"> No man of the offspring of Aaron the priest who has a defect shall come near to offer the offerings of the LORD made by fire. Since he has a defect, he shall not come near to offer the bread of his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 2:3; Numbers 18:19; Leviticus 24:8–9"> He shall eat the bread of his God, both of the most holy, and of the holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 21:12; Ezekiel 44:9–14; Exodus 30:6–8"> He shall not come near to the veil, nor come near to the altar, because he has a defect; that he may not profane my sanctuaries, for I am the LORD who sanctifies them.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:18; Malachi 2:1–7; 2 Timothy 2:2"> So Moses spoke to Aaron, and to his sons, and to all the children of Israel.</span>
