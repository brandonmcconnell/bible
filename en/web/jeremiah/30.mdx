<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:22; Isaiah 51:11; Isaiah 60:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thanksgiving will proceed out of them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the voice of those who make merry.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will multiply them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will not be few;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will also glorify them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will not be small.</span></span>
