<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Hebrews 9:11; Colossians 2:17"> For the law, having a shadow of the good to come, not the very image of the things, can never with the same sacrifices year by year, which they offer continually, make perfect those who draw near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Micah 7:19; Psalms 103:12"> Or else wouldn’t they have ceased to be offered, because the worshipers, having been once cleansed, would have had no more consciousness of sins?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Exodus 30:10; Leviticus 23:27–28"> But in those sacrifices there is a yearly reminder of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 10:11; Isaiah 1:11–15; Micah 6:6–8"> For it is impossible that the blood of bulls and goats should take away sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:6–8; Isaiah 1:11; Isaiah 7:14"> Therefore when he comes into the world, he says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You didn’t desire sacrifice and offering,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you prepared a body for me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 1:10; Matthew 3:17; Psalms 147:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You had no pleasure in whole burnt offerings and sacrifices for sin.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 40:7–8; John 4:34; John 6:38"> <span className="pl pl-2">Then I said, ‘Behold, I have come (in the scroll of the book it is written of me)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to do your will, O God.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 10:5; Mark 12:33"> Previously saying, “Sacrifices and offerings and whole burnt offerings and sacrifices for sin you didn’t desire, neither had pleasure in them” (those which are offered according to the law),</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 7:18–19; Hebrews 12:27–28; Hebrews 10:7"> then he has said, “Behold, I have come to do your will.” He takes away the first, that he may establish the second,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 10:14; Hebrews 10:12; 1 Corinthians 6:11"> by which will we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 5:1; Numbers 28:3; Hebrews 10:4"> Every priest indeed stands day by day serving and offering often the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Colossians 3:1; Romans 8:34"> but he, when he had offered one sacrifice for sins forever, sat down on the right hand of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 1:13; Psalms 110:1; Luke 20:43"> from that time waiting until his enemies are made the footstool of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 10:1; Hebrews 9:14"> For by one offering he has perfected forever those who are being sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7; Revelation 2:11; Revelation 3:6"> The Holy Spirit also testifies to us, for after saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 8:8–12; Romans 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">“This is the covenant that I will make with them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after those days,” says the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will put my laws on their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will also write them on their mind;”</span><br />then he says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Hebrews 8:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will remember their sins and their iniquities no more.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 10:2; Hebrews 10:14"> Now where remission of these is, there is no more offering for sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 4:16"> Having therefore, brothers, boldness to enter into the holy place by the blood of Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 14:6; John 10:9; Hebrews 9:3"> by the way which he dedicated for us, a new and living way, through the veil, that is to say, his flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:14–16; Hebrews 8:1"> and having a great priest over God’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:25; John 3:5; Ephesians 5:26"> let’s draw near with a true heart in fullness of faith, having our hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience and having our body washed with pure water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:24; 1 Corinthians 1:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> let’s hold fast the confession of our hope without wavering; for he who promised is faithful.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 John 3:18; Hebrews 13:1"> Let’s consider how to provoke one another to love and good works,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 2:42; 1 Thessalonians 5:11; Matthew 18:20"> not forsaking our own assembling together, as the custom of some is, but exhorting one another, and so much the more as you see the Day approaching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 6:4–6; John 9:41"> For if we sin willfully after we have received the knowledge of the truth, there remains no more a sacrifice for sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; Hebrews 2:3; Zephaniah 1:18"> but a certain fearful expectation of judgment, and a fierceness of fire which will devour the adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:2; Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:2–13"> A man who disregards Moses’ law dies without compassion on the word of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 6:6; Ephesians 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> How much worse punishment do you think he will be judged worthy of who has trodden under foot the Son of God, and has counted the blood of the covenant with which he was sanctified an unholy thing, and has insulted the Spirit of grace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35–36; Romans 12:19; Nahum 1:2"> For we know him who said, “Vengeance belongs to me. I will repay,” says the Lord. Again, “The Lord will judge his people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; Luke 12:5; Hebrews 12:29"> It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4; Hebrews 12:4; 2 Timothy 2:3–13"> But remember the former days, in which, after you were enlightened, you endured a great struggle with sufferings:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:9; Philippians 4:14; Hebrews 11:36"> partly, being exposed to both reproaches and oppressions, and partly, becoming partakers with those who were treated so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Peter 1:4; 2 Timothy 1:16; Matthew 5:11–12"> For you both had compassion on me in my chains and joyfully accepted the plundering of your possessions, knowing that you have for yourselves a better possession and an enduring one in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Galatians 6:8–10; Hebrews 3:6"> Therefore don’t throw away your boldness, which has a great reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1; Psalms 37:7; James 5:7–11"> For you need endurance so that, having done the will of God, you may receive the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 5:7–9; Habakkuk 2:3–4; Luke 18:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">“In a very little while,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he who comes will come and will not wait.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Galatians 3:11; Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the righteous one will live by faith.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If he shrinks back, my soul has no pleasure in him.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 John 5:5; 1 Peter 1:5; Mark 16:16"> But we are not of those who shrink back to destruction, but of those who have faith to the saving of the soul.</span>
</div>
</div>
