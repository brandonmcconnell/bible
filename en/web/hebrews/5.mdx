<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 8:3; Hebrews 10:11; Hebrews 7:27"> For every high priest, being taken from among men, is appointed for men in things pertaining to God, that he may offer both gifts and sacrifices for sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; Hebrews 2:18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> The high priest can deal gently with those who are ignorant and going astray, because he himself is also surrounded with weakness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Leviticus 16:6"> Because of this, he must offer sacrifices for sins for the people, as well as for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Numbers 16:40; Numbers 16:5"> Nobody takes this honor on himself, but he is called by God, just like Aaron was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; John 8:54; Acts 13:33"> So also Christ didn’t glorify himself to be made a high priest, but it was he who said to him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are my Son.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Today I have become your father.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:21"> As he says also in another place,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are a priest forever,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after the order of Melchizedek.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 14:32–39; Luke 22:41–44; John 17:1"> He, in the days of his flesh, having offered up prayers and petitions with strong crying and tears to him who was able to save him from death, and having been heard for his godly fear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 2:8; Hebrews 10:5–9; John 4:34"> though he was a Son, yet learned obedience by the things which he suffered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:15"> Having been made perfect, he became to all of those who obey him the author of eternal salvation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 5:5–6; Hebrews 6:20"> named by God a high priest after the order of Melchizedek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; 2 Peter 3:16"> About him we have many words to say, and hard to interpret, seeing you have become dull of hearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 6:1; 1 Corinthians 3:1–3; 1 Peter 2:2"> For although by this time you should be teachers, you again need to have someone teach you the rudiments of the first principles of the revelations of God. You have come to need milk, and not solid food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; 1 Peter 2:2; 2 Timothy 3:16"> For everyone who lives on milk is not experienced in the word of righteousness, for he is a baby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 5:21; Ephesians 1:18"> But solid food is for those who are full grown, who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern good and evil.</span>
