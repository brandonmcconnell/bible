<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:18–20; Psalms 57:2; Hebrews 6:20"> For this Melchizedek, king of Salem, priest of God Most High, who met Abraham returning from the slaughter of the kings and blessed him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 18:21; Psalms 85:10–11; 1 Samuel 8:17"> to whom also Abraham divided a tenth part of all (being first, by interpretation, “king of righteousness”, and then also “king of Salem”, which means “king of peace”,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 7:23–28; Exodus 6:20–27"> without father, without mother, without genealogy, having neither beginning of days nor end of life, but made like the Son of God), remains a priest continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 14:20; Acts 2:29; Acts 7:8–9"> Now consider how great this man was, to whom even Abraham the patriarch gave a tenth out of the best plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 27:30–33; 2 Chronicles 31:4–6; Numbers 18:21–32"> They indeed of the sons of Levi who receive the priest’s office have a commandment to take tithes from the people according to the law, that is, of their brothers, though these have come out of the body of Abraham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 4:13; Hebrews 7:3–4; Genesis 13:14–17"> but he whose genealogy is not counted from them has accepted tithes from Abraham, and has blessed him who has the promises.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:27; Genesis 48:15–20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> But without any dispute the lesser is blessed by the greater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 6:20; Hebrews 5:6; Revelation 1:18"> Here people who die receive tithes, but there one receives tithes of whom it is testified that he lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 5:12; Genesis 14:20; Hebrews 7:4"> We can say that through Abraham even Levi, who receives tithes, has paid tithes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Hebrews 7:5; Genesis 46:26"> for he was yet in the body of his father when Melchizedek met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 8:7; Galatians 2:21; Galatians 4:9"> Now if perfection was through the Levitical priesthood (for under it the people have received the law), what further need was there for another priest to arise after the order of Melchizedek, and not be called after the order of Aaron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Isaiah 66:21; Ezekiel 16:61"> For the priesthood being changed, there is of necessity a change made also in the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 7:11; Hebrews 7:14; Numbers 17:5"> For he of whom these things are said belongs to another tribe, from which no one has officiated at the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Isaiah 11:1; Luke 3:33"> For it is evident that our Lord has sprung out of Judah, about which tribe Moses spoke nothing concerning priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17–21; Hebrews 7:3"> This is yet more abundantly evident, if after the likeness of Melchizedek there arises another priest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Colossians 2:20; Hebrews 7:28"> who has been made, not after the law of a fleshly commandment, but after the power of an endless life;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 5:6; Hebrews 7:21"> for it is testified,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You are a priest forever,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">according to the order of Melchizedek.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:3; Acts 13:39; Galatians 4:9"> For there is an annulling of a foregoing commandment because of its weakness and uselessness</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:39; Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 9:9"> (for the law made nothing perfect), and a bringing in of a better hope, through which we draw near to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:6; Psalms 110:4"> Inasmuch as he was not made priest without the taking of an oath</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Hebrews 7:17; Hebrews 6:16–18"> (for they indeed have been made priests without an oath), but he with an oath by him that says of him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Lord swore and will not change his mind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘You are a priest forever,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">according to the order of Melchizedek.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:25; Genesis 44:32; Hebrews 8:6–12"> By so much, Jesus has become the guarantee of a better covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10–11; 1 Chronicles 6:3–14; Hebrews 7:8"> Many, indeed, have been made priests, because they are hindered from continuing by death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 12:34; Hebrews 7:28; Isaiah 9:6–7"> But he, because he lives forever, has his priesthood unchangeable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 8:34; 1 John 2:1–2; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Therefore he is also able to save to the uttermost those who draw near to God through him, seeing that he lives forever to make intercession for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:14–15; 1 Peter 2:22"> For such a high priest was fitting for us: holy, guiltless, undefiled, separated from sinners, and made higher than the heavens;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:12; Hebrews 9:14"> who doesn’t need, like those high priests, to offer up sacrifices daily, first for his own sins, and then for the sins of the people. For he did this once for all, when he offered up himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:10; Hebrews 7:21; Hebrews 1:2"> For the law appoints men as high priests who have weakness, but the word of the oath, which came after the law, appoints a Son forever who has been perfected.</span>
