<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:7; 2 Corinthians 4:18; Romans 8:24–25"> Now faith is assurance of things hoped for, proof of things not seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Hebrews 11:4"> For by this, the elders obtained approval.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 33:6; 2 Peter 3:5; John 1:3"> By faith we understand that the universe has been framed by the word of God, so that what is seen has not been made out of things which are visible.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 12:24; Genesis 4:3–5; 1 John 3:11–12"> By faith Abel offered to God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain, through which he had testimony given to him that he was righteous, God testifying with respect to his gifts; and through it he, being dead, still speaks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 5:21–24; Jude 1:14; Hebrews 11:6"> By faith Enoch was taken away, so that he wouldn’t see death, and he was not found, because God translated him. For he has had testimony given to him that before his translation he had been well pleasing to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 8:17; Jeremiah 29:13–14; Matthew 6:33"> Without faith it is impossible to be well pleasing to him, for he who comes to God must believe that he exists, and that he is a rewarder of those who seek him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 6:13–1; 1 Peter 3:20; Ezekiel 14:14"> By faith Noah, being warned about things not yet seen, moved with godly fear,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, reverence">a</a></sup>prepared a ship for the saving of his house, through which he condemned the world and became heir of the righteousness which is according to faith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Acts 7:2–4; Matthew 7:24–25"> By faith Abraham, when he was called, obeyed to go out to the place which he was to receive for an inheritance. He went out, not knowing where he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 13:18; Genesis 13:3"> By faith he lived as an alien in the land of promise, as in a land not his own, dwelling in tents with Isaac and Jacob, the heirs with him of the same promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Philippians 3:20; Hebrews 12:22"> For he was looking for the city which has foundations, whose builder and maker is God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:20–21; Hebrews 10:23; Genesis 18:11–14"> By faith even Sarah herself received power to conceive, and she bore a child when she was past age, since she counted him faithful who had promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 32:12; Genesis 15:5"> Therefore as many as the stars of the sky in multitude, and as innumerable as the sand which is by the sea shore, were fathered by one man, and him as good as dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 23:4; Psalms 39:12; Hebrews 11:27"> These all died in faith, not having received the promises, but having seen<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR adds “and being convinced of”">b</a></sup>them and embraced them from afar, and having confessed that they were strangers and pilgrims on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18–7; Hebrews 11:16; Hebrews 13:14"> For those who say such things make it clear that they are seeking a country of their own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 24:6–8; Genesis 12:10; Genesis 32:9–11"> If indeed they had been thinking of that country from which they went out, they would have had enough time to return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 13:14; Hebrews 11:10; Philippians 3:20"> But now they desire a better country, that is, a heavenly one. Therefore God is not ashamed of them, to be called their God, for he has prepared a city for them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 22:16; Genesis 22:1–12; James 2:21–24"> By faith, Abraham, being tested, offered up Isaac. Yes, he who had gladly received the promises was offering up his only born<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The phrase “only born” is from the Greek word “μονογενη”, which is sometimes translated “only begotten” or “one and only”.">c</a></sup>son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:12; Romans 9:7; Genesis 17:19"> to whom it was said, “Your offspring will be accounted as from Isaac,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 4:17–21; Matthew 9:28; Ephesians 3:20"> concluding that God is able to raise up even from the dead. Figuratively speaking, he also did receive him back from the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 27:27–40; Genesis 28:2–3"> By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau, even concerning things to come.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 47:31–1; Genesis 48:5–22"> By faith Jacob, when he was dying, blessed each of the sons of Joseph, and worshiped, leaning on the top of his staff.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 13:19; Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32"> By faith Joseph, when his end was near, made mention of the departure of the children of Israel, and gave instructions concerning his bones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 1:22; Exodus 1:16; Hebrews 13:6"> By faith Moses, when he was born, was hidden for three months by his parents, because they saw that he was a beautiful child; and they were not afraid of the king’s commandment.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 2:10–11; Acts 7:21–24"> By faith Moses, when he had grown up, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Timothy 2:3–10; Job 36:21"> choosing rather to share ill treatment with God’s people than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:35; Hebrews 11:6; 1 Peter 4:14"> considering the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures of Egypt; for he looked to the reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:18; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:1"> By faith he left Egypt, not fearing the wrath of the king; for he endured, as seeing him who is invisible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 12:21–30; Hebrews 9:19; Hebrews 12:24"> By faith he kept the Passover and the sprinkling of the blood, that the destroyer of the firstborn should not touch them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 14:13–21; Psalms 106:9–11; Psalms 78:13"> By faith they passed through the Red Sea as on dry land. When the Egyptians tried to do so, they were swallowed up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 6:3–20; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5"> By faith the walls of Jericho fell down after they had been encircled for seven days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 2:25; Joshua 6:22–25; Matthew 1:5"> By faith Rahab the prostitute didn’t perish with those who were disobedient, having received the spies in peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Samuel 1:20; 1 Samuel 16:13"> What more shall I say? For the time would fail me if I told of Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel, and the prophets—</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 144:1–2; Hebrews 6:12–15; Hebrews 10:36"> who through faith subdued kingdoms, worked out righteousness, obtained promises, stopped the mouths of lions,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Kings 6:16–18; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10; 1 Samuel 17:51–52"> quenched the power of fire, escaped the edge of the sword, from weakness were made strong, grew mighty in war, and caused foreign armies to flee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 4:27–37; 1 Kings 17:22–24; Philippians 3:11"> Women received their dead by resurrection. Others were tortured, not accepting their deliverance, that they might obtain a better resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:2; Genesis 39:20; Jeremiah 37:15–21"> Others were tried by mocking and scourging, yes, moreover by bonds and imprisonment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:21; Jeremiah 26:23; 1 Kings 19:10"> They were stoned. They were sawn apart. They were tempted. They were slain with the sword. They went around in sheep skins and in goat skins; being destitute, afflicted, ill-treated—</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 18:4; 1 Samuel 22:1; 1 Kings 19:9"> of whom the world was not worthy—wandering in deserts, mountains, caves, and the holes of the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 10:23–24; Hebrews 11:13; Hebrews 11:2"> These all, having been commended for their faith, didn’t receive the promise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Revelation 6:11; Hebrews 8:6; Hebrews 10:11–14"> God having provided some better thing concerning us, so that apart from us they should not be made perfect.</span>
</div>
</div>
