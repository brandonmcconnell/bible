<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 14:3–8; John 11:1; Luke 24:50"> Then, six days before the Passover, Jesus came to Bethany, where Lazarus was, who had been dead, whom he raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 22:27; Luke 10:38–42; Revelation 3:20"> So they made him a supper there. Martha served, but Lazarus was one of those who sat at the table with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; John 11:2; Luke 10:38–39"> Therefore Mary took a pound<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="a Roman pound of 12 ounces, or about 340 grams">a</a></sup>of ointment of pure nard, very precious, and anointed Jesus’ feet and wiped his feet with her hair. The house was filled with the fragrance of the ointment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:4; 1 Samuel 17:28–29; John 13:2"> Then Judas Iscariot, Simon’s son, one of his disciples, who would betray him, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:22; Luke 12:33; Mark 14:4–5"> “Why wasn’t this ointment sold for three hundred denarii<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="300 denarii was about a year’s wages for an agricultural laborer.">b</a></sup>and given to the poor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 13:29; Psalms 50:16–20; James 2:6"> Now he said this, not because he cared for the poor, but because he was a thief, and having the money box, used to steal what was put into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 26:12; Mark 14:6; Matthew 27:57–60"> But Jesus said,<span className="woj">“Leave her alone. She has kept this for the day of my burial.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:11; Mark 14:7; Matthew 26:11"> <span className="woj">For you always have the poor with you, but you don’t always have me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:14; John 11:43–45; Acts 3:10–11"> A large crowd therefore of the Jews learned that he was there; and they came, not for Jesus’ sake only, but that they might see Lazarus also, whom he had raised from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 16:31; Matthew 2:16; John 11:47–53"> But the chief priests conspired to put Lazarus to death also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 11:45; John 12:18; Acts 13:45"> because on account of him many of the Jews went away and believed in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 11:7–10; Matthew 21:4–9; Luke 19:35–38"> On the next day a great multitude had come to the feast. When they heard that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Revelation 15:3; Matthew 21:9–11"> they took the branches of the palm trees and went out to meet him, and cried out, “Hosanna!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Hosanna” means “save us” or “help us, we pray”.">c</a></sup>Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord, the King of Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Mark 11:1–7; Matthew 21:1–7"> Jesus, having found a young donkey, sat on it. As it is written,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Isaiah 62:11; Zephaniah 3:16–17"> “Don’t be afraid, daughter of Zion. Behold, your King comes, sitting on a donkey’s colt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 2:22; John 14:26; John 7:39"> His disciples didn’t understand these things at first, but when Jesus was glorified, then they remembered that these things were written about him, and that they had done these things to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 1 John 5:9–12; John 19:35"> The multitude therefore that was with him when he called Lazarus out of the tomb and raised him from the dead was testifying about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 12:11"> For this cause also the multitude went and met him, because they heard that he had done this sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:6; Psalms 22:27; 1 John 2:2"> The Pharisees therefore said among themselves, “See how you accomplish nothing. Behold, the world has gone after him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:35; Isaiah 11:10; Acts 17:4"> Now there were certain Greeks among those who went up to worship at the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Luke 19:2–4; John 6:40"> Therefore, these came to Philip, who was from Bethsaida of Galilee, and asked him, saying, “Sir, we want to see Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 10:13–14; Luke 9:49–50; John 1:40–41"> Philip came and told Andrew, and in turn, Andrew came with Philip, and they told Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:31–32; John 17:1–5; Isaiah 60:9"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“The time has come for the Son of Man to be glorified.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:36–38; Isaiah 53:10–12; Hebrews 2:9–10"> <span className="woj">Most certainly I tell you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains by itself alone. But if it dies, it bears much fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 10:39; Luke 9:23–24; Mark 8:35"> <span className="woj">He who loves his life will lose it. He who hates his life in this world will keep it to eternal life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:24; Galatians 1:10; 1 Samuel 2:30"> <span className="woj">If anyone serves me, let him follow me. Where I am, there my servant will also be. If anyone serves me, the Father will honor him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 12:23; Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:42"> <span className="woj">“Now my soul is troubled. What shall I say? ‘Father, save me from this time’? But I came to this time for this cause.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 13:31–32; Matthew 26:42; Revelation 5:9–14"> <span className="woj">Father, glorify your name!”</span>Then a voice came out of the sky, saying, “I have both glorified it and will glorify it again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 37:2–5; Revelation 6:1; Revelation 14:2"> Therefore the multitude who stood by and heard it said that it had thundered. Others said, “An angel has spoken to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:42; John 11:15; John 5:34"> Jesus answered,<span className="woj">“This voice hasn’t come for my sake, but for your sakes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 5:19; 1 John 3:8"> <span className="woj">Now is the judgment of this world. Now the prince of this world will be cast out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 3:14; John 8:28; 1 Peter 2:24"> <span className="woj">And I, if I am lifted up from the earth, will draw all people to myself.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 18:32; John 21:19"> But he said this, signifying by what kind of death he should die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Isaiah 9:7; Daniel 7:14"> The multitude answered him, “We have heard out of the law that the Christ remains forever. How do you say,<span className="woj">‘The Son of Man must be lifted up’</span>? Who is this Son of Man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 12:46; John 16:16; Ephesians 5:8"> Jesus therefore said to them,<span className="woj">“Yet a little while the light is with you. Walk while you have the light, that darkness doesn’t overtake you. He who walks in the darkness doesn’t know where he is going.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; John 8:59; Acts 13:47–48"> <span className="woj">While you have the light, believe in the light, that you may become children of light.”</span>Jesus said these things, and he departed and hid himself from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 16:31; John 1:11; John 15:24"> But though he had done so many signs before them, yet they didn’t believe in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 10:16; Romans 10:20"> that the word of Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, which he spoke:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Lord, who has believed our report?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">To whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 10:38; John 5:44; 2 Peter 2:14"> For this cause they couldn’t believe, for Isaiah said again:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; Matthew 13:13–15; Luke 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">“He has blinded their eyes and he hardened their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lest they should see with their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and perceive with their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and would turn,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I would heal them.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Isaiah 6:9–10; John 1:14"> Isaiah said these things when he saw his glory, and spoke of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 9:22; John 7:13; Isaiah 51:7"> Nevertheless, even many of the rulers believed in him, but because of the Pharisees they didn’t confess it, so that they wouldn’t be put out of the synagogue,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 5:44; Matthew 23:5–7; John 5:41"> for they loved men’s praise more than God’s praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; John 13:20; John 5:24"> Jesus cried out and said,<span className="woj">“Whoever believes in me, believes not in me, but in him who sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 14:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Colossians 1:15"> <span className="woj">He who sees me sees him who sent me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 8:12; Psalms 36:9; John 9:5"> <span className="woj">I have come as a light into the world, that whoever believes in me may not remain in the darkness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:17; John 8:15–16; John 12:48"> <span className="woj">If anyone listens to my sayings and doesn’t believe, I don’t judge him. For I came not to judge the world, but to save the world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 10:16; Hebrews 9:27–28; Romans 2:16"> <span className="woj">He who rejects me, and doesn’t receive my sayings, has one who judges him. The word that I spoke will judge him in the last day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 14:10; John 8:26; John 5:30"> <span className="woj">For I spoke not from myself, but the Father who sent me gave me a commandment, what I should say and what I should speak.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 17:3; John 6:68; 1 John 5:20"> <span className="woj">I know that his commandment is eternal life. The things therefore which I speak, even as the Father has said to me, so I speak.”</span></span>
