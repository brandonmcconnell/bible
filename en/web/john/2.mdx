<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:46; Proverbs 31:10–12; John 21:2"> The third day, there was a wedding in Cana of Galilee. Jesus’ mother was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:40–49; Matthew 10:40–42; 1 Corinthians 7:39"> Jesus also was invited, with his disciples, to the wedding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Philippians 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> When the wine ran out, Jesus’ mother said to him, “They have no wine.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 7:6; John 13:1; John 7:30"> Jesus said to her,<span className="woj">“Woman, what does that have to do with you and me? My hour has not yet come.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:14; Genesis 6:22; Hebrews 11:8"> His mother said to the servants, “Whatever he says to you, do it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 3:25; Hebrews 9:10; Ephesians 5:26"> Now there were six water pots of stone set there after the Jews’ way of purifying, containing two or three metretes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="2 to 3 metretes is about 20 to 30 U. S. Gallons, or 75 to 115 liters.">a</a></sup>apiece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 4:2–6; John 2:5; John 2:3"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Fill the water pots with water.”</span>So they filled them up to the brim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 2:9; Proverbs 3:5–6; Romans 13:7"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Now draw some out, and take it to the ruler of the feast.”</span>So they took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 4:46; John 7:17; Psalms 119:100"> When the ruler of the feast tasted the water now become wine, and didn’t know where it came from (but the servants who had drawn the water knew), the ruler of the feast called the bridegroom</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 7:16–17; Proverbs 9:1–6; Luke 16:25"> and said to him, “Everyone serves the good wine first, and when the guests have drunk freely, then that which is worse. You have kept the good wine until now!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 20:30–31; John 1:14; John 3:2"> This beginning of his signs Jesus did in Cana of Galilee, and revealed his glory; and his disciples believed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> After this, he went down to Capernaum, he, and his mother, his brothers, and his disciples; and they stayed there a few days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> The Passover of the Jews was at hand, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> He found in the temple those who sold oxen, sheep, and doves, and the changers of money sitting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> He made a whip of cords and drove all out of the temple, both the sheep and the oxen; and he poured out the changers’ money and overthrew their tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> To those who sold the doves, he said,<span className="woj">“Take these things out of here! Don’t make my Father’s house a marketplace!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> His disciples remembered that it was written, “Zeal for your house will eat me up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> The Jews therefore answered him, “What sign do you show us, seeing that you do these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus answered them,<span className="woj">“Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The Jews therefore said, “It took forty-six years to build this temple! Will you raise it up in three days?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But he spoke of the temple of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> When therefore he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered that he said this, and they believed the Scripture and the word which Jesus had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 7:31; Luke 8:13; John 6:14"> Now when he was in Jerusalem at the Passover, during the feast, many believed in his name, observing his signs which he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:64; Hebrews 4:13; Acts 1:24"> But Jesus didn’t entrust himself to them, because he knew everyone,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 13:11; Matthew 9:4; John 6:64"> and because he didn’t need for anyone to testify concerning man; for he himself knew what was in man.</span>
