<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:23; Matthew 28:16"> After these things, Jesus revealed himself again to the disciples at the sea of Tiberias. He revealed himself this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 4:21–22; John 1:45–1; John 4:46"> Simon Peter, Thomas called Didymus,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, Twin">a</a></sup>Nathanael of Cana in Galilee, and the sons of Zebedee, and two others of his disciples were together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:5; Matthew 4:18–20; 2 Thessalonians 3:7–9"> Simon Peter said to them, “I’m going fishing.” They told him, “We are also coming with you.” They immediately went out and entered into the boat. That night, they caught nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 20:14; Luke 24:15–16; Luke 24:31"> But when day had already come, Jesus stood on the beach; yet the disciples didn’t know that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Philippians 4:11–13; 1 John 2:13"> Jesus therefore said to them,<span className="woj">“Children, have you anything to eat?”</span>They answered him, “No.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:4–7; Acts 2:41; John 2:5"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Cast the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some.”</span>They cast it therefore, and now they weren’t able to draw it in for the multitude of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 13:23; John 20:2; Matthew 14:28–29"> That disciple therefore whom Jesus loved said to Peter, “It’s the Lord!” So when Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he wrapped his coat around himself (for he was naked), and threw himself into the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:11"> But the other disciples came in the little boat (for they were not far from the land, but about two hundred cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="200 cubits is about 100 yards or about 91 meters">b</a></sup>away), dragging the net full of fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 21:13; 1 Kings 19:5–6; John 21:10"> So when they got out on the land, they saw a fire of coals there, with fish and bread laid on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Bring some of the fish which you have just caught.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 5:6–8; Acts 2:41"> Simon Peter went up, and drew the net to land, full of one hundred fifty-three great fish. Even though there were so many, the net wasn’t torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:41; Luke 9:45; John 16:19"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Come and eat breakfast!”</span>None of the disciples dared inquire of him, “Who are you?” knowing that it was the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 21:9; Acts 10:41; Luke 24:42–43"> Then Jesus came and took the bread, gave it to them, and the fish likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 20:26; John 20:19"> This is now the third time that Jesus was revealed to his disciples after he had risen from the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; 1 John 4:19; Romans 15:1"> So when they had eaten their breakfast, Jesus said to Simon Peter,<span className="woj">“Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me more than these?”</span>He said to him, “Yes, Lord; you know that I have affection for you.” He said to him,<span className="woj">“Feed my lambs.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2; 1 Peter 2:25"> He said to him again a second time,<span className="woj">“Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me?”</span>He said to him, “Yes, Lord; you know that I have affection for you.” He said to him,<span className="woj">“Tend my sheep.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 13:38; John 16:30; Mark 14:72"> He said to him the third time,<span className="woj">“Simon, son of Jonah, do you have affection for me?”</span>Peter was grieved because he asked him the third time,<span className="woj">“Do you have affection for me?”</span>He said to him, “Lord, you know everything. You know that I have affection for you.” Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“Feed my sheep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:36; Acts 12:3–4; Acts 21:11"> <span className="woj">Most certainly I tell you, when you were young, you dressed yourself and walked where you wanted to. But when you are old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will dress you and carry you where you don’t want to go.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Peter 1:14; John 21:22; Matthew 10:38"> Now he said this, signifying by what kind of death he would glorify God. When he had said this, he said to him,<span className="woj">“Follow me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 21:7; John 20:2; John 21:24"> Then Peter, turning around, saw a disciple following. This was the disciple whom Jesus loved, the one who had also leaned on Jesus’ chest at the supper and asked, “Lord, who is going to betray you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 13:23–24; Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3–4"> Peter, seeing him, said to Jesus, “Lord, what about this man?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:25; Revelation 3:11; Mark 9:1"> Jesus said to him,<span className="woj">“If I desire that he stay until I come, what is that to you? You follow me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:29; Job 33:13; Daniel 4:35"> This saying therefore went out among the brothers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here may be also correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">c</a></sup>that this disciple wouldn’t die. Yet Jesus didn’t say to him that he wouldn’t die, but,<span className="woj">“If I desire that he stay until I come, what is that to you?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 19:35; John 15:27; 1 John 5:6"> This is the disciple who testifies about these things, and wrote these things. We know that his witness is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 40:5; John 20:30–31; Psalms 71:15"> There are also many other things which Jesus did, which if they would all be written, I suppose that even the world itself wouldn’t have room for the books that would be written.</span>
