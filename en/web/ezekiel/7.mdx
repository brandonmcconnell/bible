<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover the LORD’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 8:2; 1 Peter 4:7; Ezekiel 7:3"> “You, son of man, the Lord GOD says to the land of Israel, ‘An end! The end has come on the four corners of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 33:20"> Now the end is on you, and I will send my anger on you, and will judge you according to your ways. I will bring on you all your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Ezekiel 11:21; Ezekiel 5:11"> My eye will not spare you, neither will I have pity; but I will bring your ways on you, and your abominations will be among you. Then you will know that I am the LORD.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nahum 1:9; 2 Kings 21:12–13; Ezekiel 5:9"> “The Lord GOD says: ‘A disaster! A unique disaster! Behold, it comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Zechariah 13:7; Ezekiel 7:3; 2 Peter 2:5"> An end has come. The end has come! It awakes against you. Behold, it comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 7:12; Isaiah 22:5"> Your doom has come to you, inhabitant of the land! The time has come! The day is near, a day of tumult, and not of joyful shouting, on the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:8; Ezekiel 14:19; Ezekiel 20:8"> Now I will shortly pour out my wrath on you, and accomplish my anger against you, and will judge you according to your ways. I will bring on you all your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 20:13; Micah 6:9; Isaiah 9:13"> My eye won’t spare, neither will I have pity. I will punish you according to your ways. Your abominations will be among you. Then you will know that I, the LORD, strike.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:5; James 4:6; 1 Thessalonians 5:3"> “‘Behold, the day! Behold, it comes! Your doom has gone out. The rod has blossomed. Pride has budded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:18; Ezekiel 7:23; Isaiah 59:6–8"> Violence has risen up into a rod of wickedness. None of them will remain, nor of their multitude, nor of their wealth. There will be nothing of value among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13–14; 1 Corinthians 7:29–31; Ezekiel 7:5–7"> The time has come! The day draws near. Don’t let the buyer rejoice, nor the seller mourn; for wrath is on all its multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 25:24–28; Leviticus 25:31; Job 15:25"> For the seller won’t return to that which is sold, although they are still alive; for the vision concerns the whole multitude of it. None will return. None will strengthen himself in the iniquity of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Jeremiah 7:20; Jeremiah 51:27"> They have blown the trumpet, and have made all ready; but no one goes to the battle, for my wrath is on all its multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:18; Ezekiel 5:12; Lamentations 1:20"> “‘The sword is outside, and the pestilence and the famine within. He who is in the field will die by the sword. He who is in the city will be devoured by famine and pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Isaiah 38:14; Ezra 9:15"> But of those who escape, they will escape and will be on the mountains like doves of the valleys, all of them moaning, everyone in his iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Hebrews 12:12; Jeremiah 6:24"> All hands will be feeble, and all knees will be weak as water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Amos 8:10; Isaiah 15:2–3; Isaiah 3:24"> They will also clothe themselves with sackcloth, and horror will cover them. Shame will be on all faces, and baldness on all their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Zephaniah 1:18; Luke 12:19–20"> They will cast their silver in the streets, and their gold will be as an unclean thing. Their silver and their gold won’t be able to deliver them in the day of the LORD’s wrath. They won’t satisfy their souls or fill their bellies; because it has been the stumbling block of their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:21; Jeremiah 7:30; Ezekiel 8:15–16"> As for the beauty of his ornament, he set it in majesty; but they made the images of their abominations and their detestable things therein. Therefore I have made it to them as an unclean thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; Psalms 74:2–8; 2 Chronicles 36:18–19"> I will give it into the hands of the strangers for a prey, and to the wicked of the earth for a plunder; and they will profane it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:17; Psalms 74:18–23; Psalms 74:10–11"> I will also turn my face from them, and they will profane my secret place. Robbers will enter into it, and profane it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:9; Jeremiah 27:2; Ezekiel 11:6"> “‘Make chains, for the land is full of bloody crimes, and the city is full of violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Psalms 106:41; Ezekiel 21:31"> Therefore I will bring the worst of the nations, and they will possess their houses. I will also make the pride of the strong to cease. Their holy places will be profaned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:15–16; Isaiah 57:21; Lamentations 4:17–18"> Destruction comes! They will seek peace, and there will be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:20; Ezekiel 14:1; Micah 3:6"> Mischief will come on mischief, and rumor will be on rumor. They will seek a vision of the prophet; but the law will perish from the priest, and counsel from the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Ezekiel 7:4–8; Ezekiel 26:16"> The king will mourn, and the prince will be clothed with desolation. The hands of the people of the land will be troubled. I will do to them after their way, and according to their own judgments I will judge them. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’”</span>
