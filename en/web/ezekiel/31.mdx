<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:20; Jeremiah 52:5–6; Ezekiel 1:2"> In the eleventh year, in the third month, in the first day of the month, the LORD’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 31:18"> “Son of man, tell Pharaoh king of Egypt and his multitude:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Whom are you like in your greatness?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:33–34; Daniel 4:10; Daniel 4:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, the Assyrian was a cedar in Lebanon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with beautiful branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and with a forest-like shade,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">of high stature;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its top was among the thick boughs.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 17:15; Proverbs 14:28; Ezekiel 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The waters nourished it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The deep made it to grow.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Its rivers ran all around its plantation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It sent out its channels to all the trees of the field.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:11; Ezekiel 17:5; Psalms 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore its stature was exalted above all the trees of the field;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its boughs were multiplied.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Its branches became long by reason of many waters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when it spread them out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:23; Matthew 13:32; Daniel 4:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the birds of the sky made their nests in its boughs.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Under its branches, all the animals of the field gave birth to their young.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All great nations lived under its shadow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thus it was beautiful in its greatness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the length of its branches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for its root was by many waters.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 51:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The cedars in the garden of God could not hide it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The cypress trees were not like its branches.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The pine trees were not like its branches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nor was any tree in the garden of God like it in its beauty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Daniel 2:37–38; Genesis 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I made it beautiful by the multitude of its branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so that all the trees of Eden,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that were in the garden of God, envied it.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:17; Daniel 5:20; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> “Therefore thus said the Lord GOD: ‘Because he is exalted in stature, and he has set his top among the thick branches, and his heart is lifted up in his height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11–12; Nahum 3:18; Daniel 5:18–19"> I will deliver him into the hand of the mighty one of the nations. He will surely deal with him. I have driven him out for his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 35:8; Ezekiel 30:11"> Foreigners, the tyrants of the nations, have cut him off and have left him. His branches have fallen on the mountains and in all the valleys, and his boughs are broken by all the watercourses of the land. All the peoples of the earth have gone down from his shadow and have left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:4; Ezekiel 29:5; Isaiah 18:6"> All the birds of the sky will dwell on his ruin, and all the animals of the field will be on his branches,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 63:9–10; Nehemiah 13:18; 2 Peter 2:6"> to the end that none of all the trees by the waters exalt themselves in their stature, and don’t set their top among the thick boughs. Their mighty ones don’t stand up on their height, even all who drink water; for they are all delivered to death, to the lower parts of the earth, among the children of men, with those who go down to the pit.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 3:4; Nahum 2:8–10; Revelation 18:9–11"> “The Lord GOD says: ‘In the day when he went down to Sheol,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Sheol is the place of the dead.">a</a></sup>I caused a mourning. I covered the deep for him, and I restrained its rivers. The great waters were stopped. I caused Lebanon to mourn for him, and all the trees of the field fainted for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 14:15; Isaiah 14:8; Ezekiel 26:15"> I made the nations to shake at the sound of his fall, when I cast him down to Sheol<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Sheol is the place of the dead.">b</a></sup>with those who descend into the pit. All the trees of Eden, the choice and best of Lebanon, all that drink water, were comforted in the lower parts of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:17; Ezekiel 31:6; Ezekiel 31:3"> They also went down into Sheol with him to those who are slain by the sword; yes, those who were his arm, who lived under his shadow in the middle of the nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:19; Ezekiel 32:21; Ezekiel 28:10"> “‘To whom are you thus like in glory and in greatness among the trees of Eden? Yet you will be brought down with the trees of Eden to the lower parts of the earth. You will lie in the middle of the uncircumcised, with those who are slain by the sword.<br />“‘This is Pharaoh and all his multitude,’ says the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>
