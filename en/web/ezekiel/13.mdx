<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="web" data-translation-id="ENGWEBP" data-translation-label="WEB" data-book="EZK" data-book-slug="ezekiel" data-book-name="Ezekiel" data-chapter="13" data-verses="23">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:17; Jeremiah 37:19; Ezekiel 22:28"> “Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel who prophesy, and say to those who prophesy out of their own heart, ‘Hear the LORD’s word:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Lamentations 2:14; Jeremiah 23:28–32; Hosea 9:7"> The Lord GOD says, “Woe to the foolish prophets, who follow their own spirit, and have seen nothing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Micah 2:11; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–10"> Israel, your prophets have been like foxes in the waste places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:30; Psalms 106:23; Isaiah 58:12"> You have not gone up into the gaps or built up the wall for the house of Israel, to stand in the battle in the LORD’s day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:8; Ezekiel 22:28; Jeremiah 28:15"> They have seen falsehood and lying divination, who say, ‘The LORD says;’ but the LORD has not sent them. They have made men to hope that the word would be confirmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6; Matthew 24:23–24; Ezekiel 13:2–3"> Haven’t you seen a false vision, and haven’t you spoken a lying divination, in that you say, ‘The LORD says;’ but I have not spoken?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Ezekiel 21:3; Ezekiel 28:22"> “‘Therefore the Lord GOD says: “Because you have spoken falsehood and seen lies, therefore, behold, I am against you,” says the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 87:6; Psalms 69:28; Ezekiel 20:38"> “My hand will be against the prophets who see false visions and who utter lying divinations. They will not be in the council of my people, neither will they be written in the writing of the house of Israel, neither will they enter into the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the Lord GOD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:28; Ezekiel 13:16; Jeremiah 6:14"> “‘Because, even because they have seduced my people, saying, “Peace;” and there is no peace. When one builds up a wall, behold, they plaster it with whitewash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Isaiah 28:2; Nahum 1:7–8"> Tell those who plaster it with whitewash that it will fall. There will be an overflowing shower; and you, great hailstones, will fall. A stormy wind will tear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 3:13; Lamentations 2:14–15; Judges 9:38"> Behold, when the wall has fallen, won’t it be said to you, “Where is the plaster with which you have plastered it?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; Revelation 16:21; Psalms 148:8"> “‘Therefore the Lord GOD says: “I will even tear it with a stormy wind in my wrath. There will be an overflowing shower in my anger, and great hailstones in wrath to consume it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Micah 1:6; Jeremiah 14:15; Jeremiah 6:15"> So I will break down the wall that you have plastered with whitewash, and bring it down to the ground, so that its foundation will be uncovered. It will fall, and you will be consumed in the middle of it. Then you will know that I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:13; Psalms 62:3; Nehemiah 4:3"> Thus I will accomplish my wrath on the wall, and on those who have plastered it with whitewash. I will tell you, ‘The wall is no more, nor those who plastered it—</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:10; Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 8:11"> to wit, the prophets of Israel who prophesy concerning Jerusalem, and who see visions of peace for her, and there is no peace,’” says the Lord GOD.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:2; 2 Kings 22:14; Judges 4:4"> You, son of man, set your face against the daughters of your people, who prophesy out of their own heart; and prophesy against them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; Ezekiel 13:20; 2 Timothy 4:3"> and say, “The Lord GOD says: ‘Woe to the women who sew magic bands on all elbows and make veils for the head of persons of every stature to hunt souls! Will you hunt the souls of my people and save souls alive for yourselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 3:5; Proverbs 28:21; Jeremiah 23:17"> You have profaned me among my people for handfuls of barley and for pieces of bread, to kill the souls who should not die and to save the souls alive who should not live, by your lying to my people who listen to lies.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:18; 2 Timothy 3:8–9; Ezekiel 13:8–9"> “Therefore the Lord GOD says: ‘Behold, I am against your magic bands, with which you hunt the souls to make them fly, and I will tear them from your arms. I will let the souls fly free, even the souls whom you ensnare like birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 91:3"> I will also tear your veils and deliver my people out of your hand; and they will no longer be in your hand to be ensnared. Then you will know that I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Jeremiah 14:13–17; 2 Peter 2:18–19"> Because with lies you have grieved the heart of the righteous, whom I have not made sad; and strengthened the hands of the wicked, that he should not return from his wicked way, and be saved alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:21; Ezekiel 12:24; Micah 3:6"> Therefore you shall no more see false visions nor practice divination. I will deliver my people out of your hand. Then you will know that I am the LORD.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
