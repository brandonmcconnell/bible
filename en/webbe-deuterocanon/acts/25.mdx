<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 23:34; Acts 25:5; Acts 8:40"> Festus therefore, having come into the province, after three days went up to Jerusalem from Caesarea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 25:15; Proverbs 4:16; Acts 24:1"> Then the high priest and the principal men of the Jews informed him against Paul, and they begged him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 9:24; Acts 23:12–15; 1 Samuel 23:19–21"> asking a favour against him, that he would summon him to Jerusalem, plotting to kill him on the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 24:23"> However Festus answered that Paul should be kept in custody at Caesarea, and that he himself was about to depart shortly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 25:25; John 18:29–30; Acts 25:18–19"> “Let them therefore”, he said, “that are in power amongst you go down with me, and if there is anything wrong in the man, let them accuse him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 25:17; Acts 25:10; Matthew 27:19"> When he had stayed amongst them more than ten days, he went down to Caesarea, and on the next day he sat on the judgement seat, and commanded Paul to be brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 24:13; Luke 23:10; Acts 25:24"> When he had come, the Jews who had come down from Jerusalem stood around him, bringing against him many and grievous charges which they could not prove,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 24:12; Acts 28:17; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> while he said in his defence, “Neither against the law of the Jews, nor against the temple, nor against Caesar, have I sinned at all.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 24:27; Acts 12:3; Acts 25:20"> But Festus, desiring to gain favour with the Jews, answered Paul and said, “Are you willing to go up to Jerusalem and be judged by me there concerning these things?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 27:18; 2 Corinthians 4:2; Acts 28:18"> But Paul said, “I am standing before Caesar’s judgement seat, where I ought to be tried. I have done no wrong to the Jews, as you also know very well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 28:19; Acts 26:32; Acts 25:25"> For if I have done wrong and have committed anything worthy of death, I don’t refuse to die; but if none of those things is true that they accuse me of, no one can give me up to them. I appeal to Caesar!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 19:21; Philippians 1:12–14; Philippians 1:20"> Then Festus, when he had conferred with the council, answered, “You have appealed to Caesar. To Caesar you shall go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:40; Acts 26:1; Acts 26:27–28"> Now when some days had passed, King Agrippa and Bernice arrived at Caesarea and greeted Festus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 24:27"> As he stayed there many days, Festus laid Paul’s case before the king, saying, “There is a certain man left a prisoner by Felix;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 25:1–3; Esther 3:9; Luke 18:3–5"> about whom, when I was at Jerusalem, the chief priests and the elders of the Jews informed me, asking for a sentence against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:51; Deuteronomy 19:17–18; Acts 23:30"> I answered them that it is not the custom of the Romans to give up any man to destruction before the accused has met the accusers face to face and has had opportunity to make his defence concerning the matter laid against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 25:6; Acts 25:10"> When therefore they had come together here, I didn’t delay, but on the next day sat on the judgement seat and commanded the man to be brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> When the accusers stood up, they brought no charges against him of such things as I supposed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 23:29; Acts 18:15; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4"> but had certain questions against him about their own religion and about one Jesus, who was dead, whom Paul affirmed to be alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 25:9"> Being perplexed how to enquire concerning these things, I asked whether he was willing to go to Jerusalem and there be judged concerning these matters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:16; Luke 2:1; Acts 25:10–11"> But when Paul had appealed to be kept for the decision of the emperor, I commanded him to be kept until I could send him to Caesar.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:15; Matthew 10:18; Isaiah 52:15"> Agrippa said to Festus, “I also would like to hear the man myself.”<br />“Tomorrow,” he said, “you shall hear him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 26:30; Acts 25:13; 1 John 2:16"> So on the next day, when Agrippa and Bernice had come with great pomp, and they had entered into the place of hearing with the commanding officers and the principal men of the city, at the command of Festus, Paul was brought in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 22:22; Acts 25:7; Acts 25:2–3"> Festus said, “King Agrippa, and all men who are here present with us, you see this man about whom all the multitude of the Jews petitioned me, both at Jerusalem and here, crying that he ought not to live any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 23:29; Acts 25:11–12; Luke 23:4"> But when I found that he had committed nothing worthy of death, and as he himself appealed to the emperor, I determined to send him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 26:2–3"> of whom I have no certain thing to write to my lord. Therefore I have brought him out before you, and especially before you, King Agrippa, that, after examination I may have something to write.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 7:51; Proverbs 18:13"> For it seems to me unreasonable, in sending a prisoner, not to also specify the charges against him.”</span>
</div>
</div>
