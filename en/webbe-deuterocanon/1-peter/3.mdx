<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Titus 2:3–4; 1 Timothy 5:5; Luke 2:37"> For this is how in the past the holy women who hoped in God also adorned themselves, being in subjection to their own husbands.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 34:12–16; James 1:26; 1 Peter 2:1"> For,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He who would love life</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and see good days,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">let him keep his tongue from evil</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his lips from speaking deceit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 5:9; Psalms 34:14; Hebrews 12:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let him turn away from evil and do good.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Let him seek peace and pursue it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 34:15–16; John 9:31; Proverbs 15:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the eyes of the Lord are on the righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his ears open to their prayer;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the face of the Lord is against those who do evil.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:28; 1 Peter 2:19–20; Matthew 5:10–12"> But even if you should suffer for righteousness’ sake, you are blessed. “Don’t fear what they fear, neither be troubled.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Luke 21:14–15"> But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts. Always be ready to give an answer to everyone who asks you a reason concerning the hope that is in you, with humility and fear,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Hebrews 13:18; 1 Peter 2:15"> having a good conscience. Thus, while you are spoken against as evildoers, they may be disappointed who curse your good way of life in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 3:14; 1 Peter 4:19; Matthew 26:39"> For it is better, if it is God’s will, that you suffer for doing what is right than for doing evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Hebrews 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> Because Christ also suffered for sins once, the righteous for the unrighteous, that he might bring you to God, being put to death in the flesh, but made alive in the Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Peter 4:6; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 42:7"> in whom he also went and preached to the spirits in prison,</span>
