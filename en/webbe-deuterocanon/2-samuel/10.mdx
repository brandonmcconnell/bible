<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 20:42–43; Joshua 8:21–22"> Now when Joab saw that the battle was set against him before and behind, he chose of all the choice men of Israel and put them in array against the Syrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The rest of the people he committed into the hand of Abishai his brother; and he put them in array against the children of Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 6:2; Nehemiah 4:20; Philippians 1:27–28"> He said, “If the Syrians are too strong for me, then you shall help me; but if the children of Ammon are too strong for you, then I will come and help you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:13; Deuteronomy 31:6; 1 Samuel 3:18"> Be courageous, and let’s be strong for our people and for the cities of our God; and may the LORD do what seems good to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 20:13–21; 1 Kings 20:28–30; 1 Chronicles 19:14–15"> So Joab and the people who were with him came near to the battle against the Syrians, and they fled before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When the children of Ammon saw that the Syrians had fled, they likewise fled before Abishai, and entered into the city. Then Joab returned from the children of Ammon and came to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 14:2–3; Micah 4:11–12; Isaiah 8:9–10"> When the Syrians saw that they were defeated by Israel, they gathered themselves together.</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:17"> David was told that; and he gathered all Israel together, passed over the Jordan, and came to Helam. The Syrians set themselves in array against David and fought with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 19:18; 2 Samuel 8:4; Psalms 46:11"> The Syrians fled before Israel; and David killed seven hundred charioteers of the Syrians and forty thousand horsemen, and struck Shobach the captain of their army, so that he died there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:6; Revelation 18:10; Psalms 48:4–5"> When all the kings who were servants to Hadadezer saw that they were defeated before Israel, they made peace with Israel and served them.
