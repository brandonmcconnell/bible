<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 5:1–3; Mark 2:13; Luke 8:4–10"> Again he began to teach by the seaside. A great multitude was gathered to him, so that he entered into a boat in the sea and sat down. All the multitude were on the land by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; Mark 3:23; Mark 4:11"> He taught them many things in parables, and told them in his teaching,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 13:26; Mark 4:26–29"> <span className="woj">“Listen! Behold, the farmer went out to sow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:19; Mark 4:15; Matthew 13:4"> <span className="woj">As he sowed, some seed fell by the road, and the birds</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds “of the air”">a</a></sup><span className="woj">came and devoured it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26; Luke 8:6; Luke 8:13"> <span className="woj">Others fell on the rocky ground, where it had little soil, and immediately it sprang up, because it had no depth of soil.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:11; Jeremiah 17:5–8; Jonah 4:8"> <span className="woj">When the sun had risen, it was scorched; and because it had no root, it withered away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:3; Luke 8:14; Genesis 3:17–18"> <span className="woj">Others fell amongst the thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked it, and it yielded no fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 15:5; Colossians 1:6; Mark 4:20"> <span className="woj">Others fell into the good ground and yielded fruit, growing up and increasing. Some produced thirty times, some sixty times, and some one hundred times as much.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 11:15; Mark 4:23–24; Revelation 3:22"> He said,<span className="woj">“Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:10–17; Mark 4:34; Proverbs 13:20"> When he was alone, those who were around him with the twelve asked him about the parables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Luke 8:10; 1 John 5:20"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“To you is given the mystery of God’s Kingdom, but to those who are outside, all things are done in parables,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Isaiah 44:18; Matthew 13:14–15"> <span className="woj">that ‘seeing they may see and not perceive, and hearing they may hear and not understand, lest perhaps they should turn again, and their sins should be forgiven them.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Matthew 13:51–52; Mark 7:17–18"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Don’t you understand this parable? How will you understand all of the parables?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 8:4; Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:37"> <span className="woj">The farmer sows the word.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 13:19; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> <span className="woj">The ones by the road are the ones where the word is sown; and when they have heard, immediately Satan comes and takes away the word which has been sown in them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> <span className="woj">These in the same way are those who are sown on the rocky places, who, when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with joy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Thessalonians 3:3–5; Matthew 12:31"> <span className="woj">They have no root in themselves, but are short-lived. When oppression or persecution arises because of the word, immediately they stumble.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:14; Jeremiah 4:3; Matthew 13:22"> <span className="woj">Others are those who are sown amongst the thorns. These are those who have heard the word,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> <span className="woj">and the cares of this age, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts of other things entering in choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Luke 8:15; John 15:4–5"> <span className="woj">Those which were sown on the good ground are those who hear the word, accept it, and bear fruit, some thirty times, some sixty times, and some one hundred times.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:15; Luke 11:33; Luke 8:16"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Is a lamp brought to be put under a basket</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="literally, a modion, a dry measuring basket containing about a peck (about 9 litres)">b</a></sup><span className="woj">or under a bed? Isn’t it put on a stand?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 10:26–27"> <span className="woj">For there is nothing hidden except that it should be made known, neither was anything made secret but that it should come to light.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 4:9; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:29"> <span className="woj">If any man has ears to hear, let him hear.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Luke 8:18; Matthew 7:2"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Take heed what you hear. With whatever measure you measure, it will be measured to you; and more will be given to you who hear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 8:18; Matthew 25:28–29"> <span className="woj">For whoever has, to him more will be given; and he who doesn’t have, even that which he has will be taken away from him.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9; Luke 8:11"> He said,<span className="woj">“God’s Kingdom is as if a man should cast seed on the earth,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:37–38"> <span className="woj">and should sleep and rise night and day, and the seed should spring up and grow, though he doesn’t know how.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 4:31–32; Hosea 6:3; Philippians 1:9–11"> <span className="woj">For the earth bears fruit by itself: first the blade, then the ear, then the full grain in the ear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43"> <span className="woj">But when the fruit is ripe, immediately he puts in the sickle, because the harvest has come.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:31–32; Luke 13:18–21; Lamentations 2:13"> He said,<span className="woj">“How will we liken God’s Kingdom? Or with what parable will we illustrate it?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Luke 13:18–19; Daniel 2:44–45"> <span className="woj">It’s like a grain of mustard seed, which, when it is sown in the earth, though it is less than all the seeds that are on the earth,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 4:20–22; Daniel 4:10–14; Ezekiel 31:3–10"> <span className="woj">yet when it is sown, grows up and becomes greater than all the herbs, and puts out great branches, so that the birds of the sky can lodge under its shadow.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 13:34–35; John 16:12; Hebrews 5:11–14"> With many such parables he spoke the word to them, as they were able to hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 16:25; Mark 4:10; Matthew 15:15–20"> Without a parable he didn’t speak to them; but privately to his own disciples he explained everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Luke 8:22; Luke 8:25"> On that day, when evening had come, he said to them,<span className="woj">“Let’s go over to the other side.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 3:9; Mark 4:1; Mark 5:2"> Leaving the multitude, they took him with them, even as he was, in the boat. Other small boats were also with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–24; Job 1:19; Luke 8:22–23"> A big wind storm arose, and the waves beat into the boat, so much that the boat was already filled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Hebrews 4:15; Isaiah 54:6–8"> He himself was in the stern, asleep on the cushion; and they woke him up and asked him, “Teacher, don’t you care that we are dying?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 107:29; Psalms 89:9; Psalms 65:7"> He awoke and rebuked the wind, and said to the sea,<span className="woj">“Peace! Be still!”</span>The wind ceased and there was a great calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Matthew 8:26; Matthew 14:31"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Why are you so afraid? How is it that you have no faith?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Job 38:11; Psalms 89:7"> They were greatly afraid and said to one another, “Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?”</span>
