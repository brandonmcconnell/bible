<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 4:47; Luke 5:18; Luke 18:35–38"> When he entered again into Capernaum after some days, it was heard that he was at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Mark 1:45; Psalms 40:9"> Immediately many were gathered together, so that there was no more room, not even around the door; and he spoke the word to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 5:18–26; Matthew 9:1–8; Matthew 4:24"> Four people came, carrying a paralytic to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 5:19; Deuteronomy 22:8"> When they could not come near to him for the crowd, they removed the roof where he was. When they had broken it up, they let down the mat that the paralytic was lying on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; James 5:15; Mark 2:9–10"> Jesus, seeing their faith, said to the paralytic,<span className="woj">“Son, your sins are forgiven you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:21–22; Mark 8:17; 2 Corinthians 10:5"> But there were some of the scribes sitting there and reasoning in their hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Luke 5:21; Micah 7:18"> “Why does this man speak blasphemies like that? Who can forgive sins but God alone?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 139:2; Acts 8:22; Luke 5:22"> Immediately Jesus, perceiving in his spirit that they so reasoned within themselves, said to them,<span className="woj">“Why do you reason these things in your hearts?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 5:22–25; Matthew 9:5"> <span className="woj">Which is easier, to tell the paralytic, ‘Your sins are forgiven;’ or to say, ‘Arise, and take up your bed, and walk’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Matthew 9:6–8; John 5:20–27"> <span className="woj">But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins”</span>—he said to the paralytic—</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 1:41; John 5:8–10; John 6:63"> <span className="woj">“I tell you, arise, take up your mat, and go to your house.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 9:33; Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:16"> He arose, and immediately took up the mat and went out in front of them all, so that they were all amazed and glorified God, saying, “We never saw anything like this!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 4:1; Proverbs 1:20–22; Luke 19:48"> He went out again by the seaside. All the multitude came to him, and he taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:27–32; Luke 6:15; Matthew 9:9–13"> As he passed by, he saw Levi the son of Alphaeus sitting at the tax office. He said to him,<span className="woj">“Follow me.”</span>And he arose and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 15:1; Luke 5:29–30; Matthew 9:10–11"> He was reclining at the table in his house, and many tax collectors and sinners sat down with Jesus and his disciples, for there were many, and they followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 19:7; Luke 18:11"> The scribes and the Pharisees, when they saw that he was eating with the sinners and tax collectors, said to his disciples, “Why is it that he eats and drinks with tax collectors and sinners?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 9:12–13; Luke 15:7; Luke 5:31–32"> When Jesus heard it, he said to them,<span className="woj">“Those who are healthy have no need for a physician, but those who are sick. I came not to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:12; Matthew 6:18; Matthew 9:14–17"> John’s disciples and the Pharisees were fasting, and they came and asked him, “Why do John’s disciples and the disciples of the Pharisees fast, but your disciples don’t fast?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 25:1–10; Genesis 29:22; Judges 14:10–11"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Can the groomsmen fast while the bridegroom is with them? As long as they have the bridegroom with them, they can’t fast.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Revelation 21:9; Isaiah 54:5"> <span className="woj">But the days will come when the bridegroom will be taken away from them, and then they will fast in that day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:16; 1 Corinthians 10:13; Isaiah 57:16"> <span className="woj">No one sews a piece of unshrunk cloth on an old garment, or else the patch shrinks and the new tears away from the old, and a worse hole is made.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 9:17; Psalms 119:83; Luke 5:37–38"> <span className="woj">No one puts new wine into old wineskins; or else the new wine will burst the skins, and the wine pours out, and the skins will be destroyed; but they put new wine into fresh wineskins.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:1–8; Deuteronomy 23:24–25; Luke 6:1–5"> He was going on the Sabbath day through the grain fields; and his disciples began, as they went, to pluck the ears of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:10; Hebrews 12:3; Matthew 12:2"> The Pharisees said to him, “Behold, why do they do that which is not lawful on the Sabbath day?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:3–6; Luke 10:26; Matthew 22:31"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Did you never read what David did when he had need and was hungry—he, and those who were with him?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:17; Leviticus 24:5–9; 1 Samuel 21:1"> <span className="woj">How he entered into God’s house at the time of Abiathar the high priest, and ate the show bread, which is not lawful to eat except for the priests, and gave also to those who were with him?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 2:16; Luke 6:9; Deuteronomy 5:14"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 12:8; Revelation 1:10; John 5:17"> <span className="woj">Therefore the Son of Man is lord even of the Sabbath.”</span></span>
