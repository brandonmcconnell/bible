<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 26:19; Luke 19:32–34; Matthew 21:6–7"> They went away, and found a young donkey tied at the door outside in the open street, and they untied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Some of those who stood there asked them, “What are you doing, untying the young donkey?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> They said to them just as Jesus had said, and they let them go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 19:35–36; Matthew 21:7–8"> They brought the young donkey to Jesus and threw their garments on it, and Jesus sat on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 23:40"> Many spread their garments on the way, and others were cutting down branches from the trees and spreading them on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 118:25–26; Matthew 21:9; Matthew 23:39"> Those who went in front and those who followed cried out, “Hosanna!<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Hosanna” means “save us” or “help us, we pray”.">b</a></sup>Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:24–25; Luke 2:14; Luke 19:38–40"> Blessed is the kingdom of our father David that is coming in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 8:1–2; Luke 19:41–45; Luke 21:37–38"> Jesus entered into the temple in Jerusalem. When he had looked around at everything, it being now evening, he went out to Bethany with the twelve.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:18–22; Matthew 4:2; John 19:28"> The next day, when they had come out from Bethany, he was hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Luke 13:6–9; Isaiah 5:7"> Seeing a fig tree afar off having leaves, he came to see if perhaps he might find anything on it. When he came to it, he found nothing but leaves, for it was not the season for figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Matthew 7:19; Matthew 3:10"> Jesus told it,<span className="woj">“May no one ever eat fruit from you again!”</span>and his disciples heard it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 2:13–17; Matthew 21:12–16; Luke 19:45–47"> They came to Jerusalem, and Jesus entered into the temple and began to throw out those who sold and those who bought in the temple, and overthrew the money changers’ tables and the seats of those who sold the doves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He would not allow anyone to carry a container through the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Luke 19:46"> He taught, saying to them,<span className="woj">“Isn’t it written, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer for all the nations’?</span><span className="woj">But you have made it a den of robbers!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 7:28; Mark 12:12; Mark 14:1–2"> The chief priests and the scribes heard it, and sought how they might destroy him. For they feared him, because all the multitude was astonished at his teaching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 21:37; Mark 11:11; John 12:36"> When evening came, he went out of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Mark 11:14; John 15:6"> As they passed by in the morning, they saw the fig tree withered away from the roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:33; Matthew 23:7; Matthew 25:41"> Peter, remembering, said to him, “Rabbi, look! The fig tree which you cursed has withered away.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 17:20; John 15:7; Matthew 21:21"> <span className="woj">For most certainly I tell you, whoever may tell this mountain, ‘Be taken up and cast into the sea,’ and doesn’t doubt in his heart, but believes that what he says is happening, he shall have whatever he says.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; 1 John 5:14–15; 1 John 3:22"> <span className="woj">Therefore I tell you, all things whatever you pray and ask for, believe that you have received them, and you shall have them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Colossians 3:13; Ephesians 4:32"> <span className="woj">Whenever you stand praying, forgive, if you have anything against anyone; so that your Father, who is in heaven, may also forgive you your transgressions.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="woj">But if you do not forgive, neither will your Father in heaven forgive your transgressions.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="NU omits verse 26.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 20:1–8; Matthew 21:23–27; Psalms 2:1–5"> They came again to Jerusalem, and as he was walking in the temple, the chief priests, the scribes, and the elders came to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; Acts 7:38–39; Numbers 16:3"> and they began saying to him, “By what authority do you do these things? Or who gave you this authority to do these things?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 20:3–8; Matthew 21:24; Isaiah 52:13"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“I will ask you one question. Answer me, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 3:1–20; John 3:25–36; Mark 1:1–11"> <span className="woj">The baptism of John—was it from heaven, or from men? Answer me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 1:29; John 1:34; Matthew 21:31–32"> They reasoned with themselves, saying, “If we should say, ‘From heaven;’ he will say, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 21:46; Mark 12:12; Matthew 14:5"> If we should say, ‘From men’”—they feared the people, for all held John to really be a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12"> They answered Jesus, “We don’t know.”<br />Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.”</span></span>
</div>
