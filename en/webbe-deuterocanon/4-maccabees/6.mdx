<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> When Eleazar had in this manner answered the exhortations of the tyrant, the spearbearers came up, and rudely dragged Eleazar to the instruments of torture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> First, they stripped the old man, adorned as he was with the beauty of piety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then tying back his arms and hands, they disdainfully flogged him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> A herald opposite cried out, “Obey the commands of the king!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But the high-minded and truly noble Eleazar, as one tortured in a dream, ignored it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But raising his eyes on high to heaven, the old man’s flesh was stripped off by the scourges, and his blood streamed down, and his sides were pierced through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Falling on the ground from his body having no power to endure the pains, he still kept his reasoning upright and unbending.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then one of the harsh spearbearers rushed at him and began to kick him in the side to force him to get up again after he fell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> But he endured the pains, despised the cruelty, and persevered through the indignities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Like a noble athlete, the old man, when struck, vanquished his torturers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> His face sweating, and he panting for breath, he was admired even by the torturers for his courage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore, partly in pity for his old age,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> partly from the sympathy of acquaintance, and partly in admiration of his endurance, some of the attendants of the king said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> “Why do you unreasonably destroy yourself, O Eleazar, with these miseries?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> We will bring you some meat cooked by yourself, and you can save yourself by pretending that you have eaten swine’s flesh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Eleazar, as though the advice more painfully tortured him, cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> “Let us who are children of Abraham not be so evil advised as to give way and make use of an unbecoming pretence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For it would be irrational, if having lived up to old age in all truth, and having scrupulously guarded our character for it, we would now turn back</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and ourselves become a pattern of impiety to the young, as being an example of eating pollution.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> It would be disgraceful if we would live on some short time, and that scorned by all men for cowardice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and be condemned by the tyrant for cowardice by not contending to the death for our divine law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Therefore you, O children of Abraham, die nobly for your religion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> You spearbearers of the tyrant, why do you linger?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Beholding him so high-minded against misery, and not changing at their pity, they led him to the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then with their wickedly contrived instruments they burnt him on the fire, and poured stinking fluids down into his nostrils.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He being at length burnt down to the bones, and about to expire, raised his eyes Godward, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> “You know, O God, that when I might have been saved, I am slain for the sake of the law by tortures of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Be merciful to your people, and be satisfied with the punishment of me on their account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Let my blood be a purification for them, and take my life in exchange for theirs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Thus speaking, the holy man departed, noble in his torments, and even to the agonies of death resisted in his reasoning for the sake of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Confessedly, therefore, religious reasoning is master of the emotions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> For had the emotions been superior to reasoning, I would have given them the witness of this mastery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But now, since reasoning conquered the emotions, we befittingly award it the authority of first place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> It is only fair that we should allow that the power belongs to reasoning, since it masters external miseries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> It would be ridiculous if it weren’t so. I prove that reasoning has not only mastered pains, but that it is also superior to the pleasures, and withstands them.</span>
</div>
</div>
