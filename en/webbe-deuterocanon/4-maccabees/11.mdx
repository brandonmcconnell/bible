<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> When he had died, disfigured in his torments, the fifth leapt forward, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> “I don’t intend, O tyrant, to get excused from the torment which is on behalf of virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But I have come of my own accord, that by my death you may owe heavenly vengeance and punishment for more crimes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> O you hater of virtue and of men, what have we done that you thus revel in our blood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Does it seem evil to you that we worship the Founder of all things, and live according to his surpassing law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But this is worthy of honours, not torments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> if you had been capable of the higher feelings of men, and possessed the hope of salvation from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Behold now, being alien from God, you make war against those who are religious towards God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> As he said this, the spearbearers bound him and drew him to the rack,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> to which binding him at his knees, and fastening them with iron fetters, they bent down his loins upon the wedge of the wheel; and his body was then dismembered, scorpion-fashion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> With his breath thus confined, and his body strangled, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> “A great favour you bestow upon us, O tyrant, by enabling us to manifest our adherence to the law by means of nobler sufferings.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He also being dead, the sixth, quite a youth, was brought out. On the tyrant asking him whether he would eat and be delivered, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> “I am indeed younger than my brothers, but in understanding I am as old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I was born and reared to the same end. We are bound to die also on behalf of the same cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So if you think it is proper to torment us for not eating the unclean, then torment!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> As he said this, they brought him to the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Extended upon this, with limbs racked and dislocated, he was gradually roasted from beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Having heated sharp spits, they approached them to his back; and having transfixed his sides, they burnt away his entrails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He, while tormented, said, “O good and holy contest, in which for the sake of religion, we kindred have been called to the arena of pain, and have not been conquered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For religious understanding, O tyrant, is unconquered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Armed with upright virtue, I also will depart with my kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> I, too, bearing with me a great avenger, O inventor of tortures, and enemy of the truly pious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> We six youths have destroyed your tyranny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For isn’t your inability to overrule our reasoning, and to compel us to eat the unclean, your destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Your fire is cold to us. Your racks are painless, and your violence harmless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For the guards not of a tyrant but of a divine law are our defenders. Through this we keep our reasoning unconquered.”</span>
