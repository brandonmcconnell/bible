<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> “Why do you delay, O tyrant? For we are more ready to die than to transgress the injunctions of our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> We would be disgracing our fathers if we didn’t obey the law, and take knowledge for our guide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> O tyrant, counsellor of law-breaking, do not, hating us as you do, pity us more than we pity ourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For we consider your escape to be worse than death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> You try to scare us by threatening us with death by tortures, as though you had learnt nothing by the death of Eleazar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But if aged men of the Hebrews have died in the cause of religion after enduring torture, more rightly should we younger men die, scorning your cruel tortures, which our aged instructor overcame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Make the attempt, then, O tyrant. If you put us to death for our religion, don’t think that you harm us by torturing us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For we through this ill-treatment and endurance will gain the rewards of virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> But you, for the wicked and despotic slaughter of us, will, from the Divine vengeance, endure eternal torture by fire.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> When they had said this, the tyrant was not only exasperated against them for being disobedient, but enraged with them for being ungrateful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So, at his bidding, the torturers brought the oldest of them, and tearing through his tunic, bound his hands and arms on each side with straps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> When they had laboured hard in scourging him without effect, they hurled him on the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The noble youth, extended upon this, became dislocated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> With every member disjointed, he denounced the tyrant, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> “O most accursed tyrant, and enemy of heavenly justice, and cruel-hearted, I am no murderer, nor sacrilegious man, whom you torture, but a defender of the Divine law.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And when the spearmen said, “Consent to eat, that you may be released from your tortures,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> he answered, “Not so powerful, O accursed lackeys, is your wheel, as to stifle my reasoning. Cut my limbs, and burn my flesh, and twist my joints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For through all my torments I will convince you that the children of the Hebrews are alone unconquered on behalf of virtue.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> While he was saying this, they heaped up fuel, and setting fire to it, strained him on the wheel still more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The wheel was defiled all over with blood. The hot ashes were quenched by the droppings of gore, and pieces of flesh were scattered about the axles of the machine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Although the framework of his bones was now destroyed, the high-minded and Abrahamic youth didn’t groan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But, as though transformed by fire into immortality, he nobly endured the rackings, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> “Imitate me, O kindred. Never desert your station, nor renounce my brotherhood in courage. Fight the holy and honourable fight of religion,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> by which means our just and paternal Providence, becoming merciful to the nation, will punish the pestilent tyrant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Saying this, the revered youth abruptly closed his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> When all admired his courageous soul, the spearmen brought forward him who was second oldest, and having put on iron gauntlets with sharp hooks, bound him to the rack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> When, on enquiring whether he would eat before he was tortured, they heard his noble sentiment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> After they with the iron gauntlets had violently dragged all the flesh from the neck to the chin, the panther-like animals tore off the very skin of his head, but he, bearing with firmness this misery, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> “How sweet is every form of death for the religion of our fathers!” Then he said to the tyrant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> “Don’t you think, most cruel of all tyrants, that you are now tortured more than I, finding your arrogant conception of tyranny conquered by our perseverance in behalf of our religion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For I lighten my suffering by the pleasures which are connected with virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But you are tortured with threatenings for impiety. You won’t escape, most corrupt tyrant, the vengeance of Divine wrath.”</span>
</div>
