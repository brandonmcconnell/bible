<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:6; Isaiah 30:12; Isaiah 59:13"> Woe to her who is rebellious and polluted, the oppressing city!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 22:21; Jeremiah 7:23–28"> She didn’t obey the voice. She didn’t receive correction. She didn’t trust in the LORD. She didn’t draw near to her God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:8; Ezekiel 22:25–27; Jeremiah 5:6"> Her princes within her are roaring lions. Her judges are evening wolves. They leave nothing until the next day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:26; Hosea 9:7; Malachi 2:8"> Her prophets are arrogant and treacherous people. Her priests have profaned the sanctuary. They have done violence to the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalms 145:17; Lamentations 3:23"> The LORD, within her, is righteous. He will do no wrong. Every morning he brings his justice to light. He doesn’t fail, but the unjust know no shame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:6; Zephaniah 2:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11"> I have cut off nations. Their battlements are desolate. I have made their streets waste, so that no one passes by. Their cities are destroyed, so that there is no man, so that there is no inhabitant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 9:9; Jeremiah 36:3; Micah 2:1–2"> I said, “Just fear me. Receive correction,” so that her dwelling won’t be cut off, according to all that I have appointed concerning her. But they rose early and corrupted all their doings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joel 3:2; Zephaniah 1:18; Micah 7:7"> “Therefore wait for me”, says the LORD, “until the day that I rise up to the prey, for my determination is to gather the nations, that I may assemble the kingdoms to pour on them my indignation, even all my fierce anger, for all the earth will be devoured with the fire of my jealousy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 11:1; Psalms 22:27; Isaiah 19:18"> For then I will purify the lips of the peoples, that they may all call on the LORD’s name, to serve him shoulder to shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 18:1"> From beyond the rivers of Cush, my worshippers, even the daughter of my dispersed people, will bring my offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:4; Joel 2:26–27; Isaiah 11:9"> In that day you will not be disappointed for all your doings in which you have transgressed against me; for then I will take away out from amongst you your proudly exulting ones, and you will no more be arrogant in my holy mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 14:32; Nahum 1:7; Zechariah 13:8–9"> But I will leave amongst you an afflicted and poor people, and they will take refuge in the LORD’s name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:5; Zephaniah 2:7; Micah 4:7"> The remnant of Israel will not do iniquity nor speak lies, neither will a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth, for they will feed and lie down, and no one will make them afraid.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 12:6; Zechariah 2:10–11; Micah 4:8"> Sing, daughter of Zion! Shout, Israel! Be glad and rejoice with all your heart, daughter of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:22; Zephaniah 3:5; Isaiah 33:22"> The LORD has taken away your judgements. He has thrown out your enemy. The King of Israel, the LORD, is amongst you. You will not be afraid of evil any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 12:12; Isaiah 35:3–4; Isaiah 41:10"> In that day, it will be said to Jerusalem, “Don’t be afraid, Zion. Don’t let your hands be weak.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 149:4; Psalms 147:11; Jeremiah 32:41"> The LORD, your God, is amongst you, a mighty one who will save. He will rejoice over you with joy. He will calm you in his love. He will rejoice over you with singing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 42:2–4; Ezekiel 36:24; Lamentations 2:6–7"> I will remove those who grieve about the appointed feasts from you. They are a burden and a reproach to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 49:25–26; Isaiah 60:14; Jeremiah 30:16"> Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">a</a></sup>at that time I will deal with all those who afflict you; and I will save those who are lame and gather those who were driven away. I will give them praise and honour, whose shame has been in all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:14; Malachi 3:12; Isaiah 56:5"> At that time I will bring you in, and at that time I will gather you; for I will give you honour and praise amongst all the peoples of the earth when I restore your fortunes before your eyes, says the LORD.</span>
</div>
