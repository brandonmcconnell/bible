<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Zilpah, Leah’s servant, bore Jacob a second son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Solomon 6:9"> Leah said, “Happy am I, for the daughters will call me happy.” She named him Asher.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> Leah said, “God has given me my hire, because I gave my servant to my husband.” She named him Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Leah conceived again, and bore a sixth son to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> Leah said, “God has endowed me with a good dowry. Now my husband will live with me, because I have borne him six sons.” She named him Zebulun.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> She conceived, bore a son, and said, “God has taken away my reproach.”</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> Jacob said to him, “You know how I have served you, and how your livestock have fared with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> For it was little which you had before I came, and it has increased to a multitude. The LORD has blessed you wherever I turned. Now when will I provide for my own house also?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> Laban said, “What shall I give you?”<br />Jacob said, “You shall not give me anything. If you will do this thing for me, I will again feed your flock and keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> I will pass through all your flock today, removing from there every speckled and spotted one, and every black one amongst the sheep, and the spotted and speckled amongst the goats. This will be my hire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> So my righteousness will answer for me hereafter, when you come concerning my hire that is before you. Every one that is not speckled and spotted amongst the goats, and black amongst the sheep, that might be with me, will be considered stolen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> Laban said, “Behold, let it be according to your word.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> He set three days’ journey between himself and Jacob, and Jacob fed the rest of Laban’s flocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> Jacob took to himself rods of fresh poplar, almond, and plane tree, peeled white streaks in them, and made the white appear which was in the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> He set the rods which he had peeled opposite the flocks in the watering troughs where the flocks came to drink. They conceived when they came to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> The flocks conceived before the rods, and the flocks produced streaked, speckled, and spotted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Jacob separated the lambs, and set the faces of the flocks towards the streaked and all the black in Laban’s flock. He put his own droves apart, and didn’t put them into Laban’s flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Whenever the stronger of the flock conceived, Jacob laid the rods in front of the eyes of the flock in the watering troughs, that they might conceive amongst the rods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> but when the flock were feeble, he didn’t put them in. So the feebler were Laban’s, and the stronger Jacob’s.</span>
