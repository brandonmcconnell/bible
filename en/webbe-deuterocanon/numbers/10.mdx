<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13; Joel 1:14; Psalms 81:3"> “Make two trumpets of silver. You shall make them of beaten work. You shall use them for the calling of the congregation and for the journeying of the camps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:5; Joel 2:15–16"> When they blow them, all the congregation shall gather themselves to you at the door of the Tent of Meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Exodus 18:21; Numbers 1:4–16"> If they blow just one, then the princes, the heads of the thousands of Israel, shall gather themselves to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 10:14; Isaiah 58:1"> When you blow an alarm, the camps that lie on the east side shall go forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 10:18; Numbers 2:10–16"> When you blow an alarm the second time, the camps that lie on the south side shall go forward. They shall blow an alarm for their journeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joel 2:1; Numbers 10:3–4"> But when the assembly is to be gathered together, you shall blow, but you shall not sound an alarm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 6:4–16; Numbers 31:6; 1 Chronicles 15:24"> “The sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow the trumpets. This shall be to you for a statute forever throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:18; Judges 2:18; Genesis 8:1"> When you go to war in your land against the adversary who oppresses you, then you shall sound an alarm with the trumpets. Then you will be remembered before the LORD your God, and you will be saved from your enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 81:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 11:24–26"> “Also in the day of your gladness, and in your set feasts, and in the beginnings of your months, you shall blow the trumpets over your burnt offerings, and over the sacrifices of your peace offerings; and they shall be to you for a memorial before your God. I am the LORD your God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:17; Numbers 1:1; Numbers 9:11"> In the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth day of the month, the cloud was taken up from over the tabernacle of the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 21:21; Numbers 12:16; Numbers 9:5"> The children of Israel went forward on their journeys out of the wilderness of Sinai; and the cloud stayed in the wilderness of Paran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:6; Numbers 9:23"> They first went forward according to the commandment of the LORD by Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 2:3–9; Numbers 1:7; Numbers 7:12"> First, the standard of the camp of the children of Judah went forward according to their armies. Nahshon the son of Amminadab was over his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 7:18; Numbers 1:8"> Nethanel the son of Zuar was over the army of the tribe of the children of Issachar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 7:24; Numbers 1:9"> Eliab the son of Helon was over the army of the tribe of the children of Zebulun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 7:6–8; Numbers 1:51; Hebrews 12:28"> The tabernacle was taken down; and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari, who bore the tabernacle, went forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 2:10–16; Numbers 1:5; Numbers 26:5–18"> The standard of the camp of Reuben went forward according to their armies. Elizur the son of Shedeur was over his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:6; Numbers 7:36"> Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai was over the army of the tribe of the children of Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 1:14; Numbers 2:14; Numbers 7:42"> Eliasaph the son of Deuel was over the army of the tribe of the children of Gad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 10:17; Numbers 7:9; Numbers 4:4–16"> The Kohathites set forward, bearing the sanctuary. The others set up the tabernacle before they arrived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 2:18–24; Numbers 1:10; Numbers 26:23–41"> The standard of the camp of the children of Ephraim set forward according to their armies. Elishama the son of Ammihud was over his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:10; Numbers 7:54"> Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur was over the army of the tribe of the children of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 1:11; Numbers 7:60"> Abidan the son of Gideoni was over the army of the tribe of the children of Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 6:9; Numbers 1:12; Genesis 49:16–17"> The standard of the camp of the children of Dan, which was the rear guard of all the camps, set forward according to their armies. Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai was over his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 1:13; Numbers 7:72"> Pagiel the son of Ochran was over the army of the tribe of the children of Asher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Numbers 1:15; Numbers 7:78"> Ahira the son of Enan was over the army of the tribe of the children of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 2:34; Numbers 24:4–5; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> Thus were the travels of the children of Israel according to their armies; and they went forward.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Judges 4:11; Exodus 3:1"> Moses said to Hobab, the son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses’ father-in-law, “We are journeying to the place of which the LORD said, ‘I will give it to you.’ Come with us, and we will treat you well; for the LORD has spoken good concerning Israel.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ruth 1:15–17; Psalms 45:10; Matthew 21:29"> He said to him, “I will not go; but I will depart to my own land, and to my relatives.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Job 29:15; Galatians 6:2; 1 Corinthians 12:14–21"> Moses said, “Don’t leave us, please; because you know how we are to encamp in the wilderness, and you can be our eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Deuteronomy 10:18; 1 John 1:3"> It shall be, if you go with us—yes, it shall be—that whatever good the LORD does to us, we will do the same to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:33; Exodus 3:1; Psalms 95:11"> They set forward from the Mount of the LORD three days’ journey. The ark of the LORD’s covenant went before them three days’ journey, to seek out a resting place for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Nehemiah 9:19; Psalms 105:39"> The cloud of the LORD was over them by day, when they set forward from the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 68:1–2; Isaiah 17:12–14; Psalms 132:8"> When the ark went forward, Moses said, “Rise up, LORD, and let your enemies be scattered! Let those who hate you flee before you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:10; Psalms 90:13–17; Isaiah 63:17"> When it rested, he said, “Return, LORD, to the ten thousands of the thousands of Israel.”</span>
</div>
