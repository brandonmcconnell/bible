<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 23:10; Numbers 15:18; Leviticus 25:2"> “Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them, ‘When you have come into the land of your habitations, which I give to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 29:18; Genesis 8:21; Philippians 4:18"> and will make an offering by fire to the LORD—a burnt offering, or a sacrifice, to accomplish a vow, or as a free will offering, or in your set feasts, to make a pleasant aroma to the LORD, of the herd, or of the flock—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:40; Leviticus 23:13; Leviticus 6:14"> then he who offers his offering shall offer to the LORD a meal offering of one tenth of an ephah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="1 ephah is about 22 litres or about 2/3 of a bushel">a</a></sup>of fine flour mixed with one fourth of a hin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A hin is about 6.5 litres or 1.7 gallons.">b</a></sup>of oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 28:7; Numbers 28:14; Judges 9:13"> You shall prepare wine for the drink offering, one fourth of a hin, with the burnt offering or for the sacrifice, for each lamb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 28:12–14; Numbers 15:4"> “‘For a ram, you shall prepare for a meal offering two tenths of an ephah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1 ephah is about 22 litres or about 2/3 of a bushel">c</a></sup>of fine flour mixed with the third part of a hin of oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> and for the drink offering you shall offer the third part of a hin of wine, of a pleasant aroma to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 3:1; Leviticus 7:11–18; Leviticus 1:3"> When you prepare a bull for a burnt offering or for a sacrifice, to accomplish a vow, or for peace offerings to the LORD,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 28:14; Numbers 28:12; Leviticus 6:14"> then he shall offer with the bull a meal offering of three tenths of an ephah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1 ephah is about 22 litres or about 2/3 of a bushel">d</a></sup>of fine flour mixed with half a hin of oil;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 15:5; Numbers 6:15"> and you shall offer for the drink offering half a hin of wine, for an offering made by fire, of a pleasant aroma to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Thus it shall be done for each bull, for each ram, for each of the male lambs, or of the young goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> According to the number that you shall prepare, so you shall do to everyone according to their number.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “‘All who are native-born shall do these things in this way, in offering an offering made by fire, of a pleasant aroma to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If a stranger lives as a foreigner with you, or whoever may be amongst you throughout your generations, and will offer an offering made by fire, of a pleasant aroma to the LORD, as you do, so he shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Numbers 15:29; Exodus 12:49"> For the assembly, there shall be one statute for you and for the stranger who lives as a foreigner, a statute forever throughout your generations. As you are, so the foreigner shall be before the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> One law and one ordinance shall be for you and for the stranger who lives as a foreigner with you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 15:2; Deuteronomy 26:1–15"> “Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them, ‘When you come into the land where I bring you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 5:11–12"> then it shall be that when you eat of the bread of the land, you shall offer up a wave offering to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 2:14; Exodus 34:26; Deuteronomy 26:2–10"> Of the first of your dough you shall offer up a cake for a wave offering. As the wave offering of the threshing floor, so you shall heave it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 29:28; Numbers 18:26"> Of the first of your dough, you shall give to the LORD a wave offering throughout your generations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:2; Leviticus 4:27; Leviticus 5:15–17"> “‘When you err, and don’t observe all these commandments which the LORD has spoken to Moses—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> even all that the LORD has commanded you by Moses, from the day that the LORD gave commandment and onward throughout your generations—</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 4:23; Leviticus 4:13–21; Leviticus 5:15"> then it shall be, if it was done unwittingly, without the knowledge of the congregation, that all the congregation shall offer one young bull for a burnt offering, for a pleasant aroma to the LORD, with its meal offering and its drink offering, according to the ordinance, and one male goat for a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 4:20; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 2:17"> The priest shall make atonement for all the congregation of the children of Israel, and they shall be forgiven; for it was an error, and they have brought their offering, an offering made by fire to the LORD, and their sin offering before the LORD, for their error.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 15:24"> All the congregation of the children of Israel shall be forgiven, as well as the stranger who lives as a foreigner amongst them; for with regard to all the people, it was done unwittingly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–28; Acts 17:30; Acts 3:17"> “‘If a person sins unwittingly, then he shall offer a female goat a year old for a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 4:35"> The priest shall make atonement for the soul who errs when he sins unwittingly before the LORD. He shall make atonement for him; and he shall be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 15:15; Leviticus 16:29; Leviticus 17:15"> You shall have one law for him who does anything unwittingly, for him who is native-born amongst the children of Israel, and for the stranger who lives as a foreigner amongst them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26; Psalms 19:13; Deuteronomy 1:43"> “‘But the soul who does anything with a high hand, whether he is native-born or a foreigner, blasphemes the LORD. That soul shall be cut off from amongst his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:9; Proverbs 13:13; Ezekiel 18:20"> Because he has despised the LORD’s word, and has broken his commandment, that soul shall be utterly cut off. His iniquity shall be on him.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 35:2–3; Exodus 31:14–15; Exodus 20:8–10"> While the children of Israel were in the wilderness, they found a man gathering sticks on the Sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:3–20"> Those who found him gathering sticks brought him to Moses and Aaron, and to all the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Leviticus 24:12; Numbers 9:8"> They put him in custody, because it had not been declared what should be done to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Exodus 31:14–15; 1 Kings 21:13"> The LORD said to Moses, “The man shall surely be put to death. All the congregation shall stone him with stones outside of the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 7:25"> All the congregation brought him outside of the camp, and stoned him to death with stones, as the LORD commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:12; Matthew 9:20"> “Speak to the children of Israel, and tell them that they should make themselves fringes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="or, tassels (Hebrew צִיצִ֛ת)">e</a></sup>on the borders of their garments throughout their generations, and that they put on the fringe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="or, tassel">f</a></sup>of each border a cord of blue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 106:39; Proverbs 28:26; Psalms 73:27"> It shall be to you for a fringe,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="or, tassel">g</a></sup>that you may see it, and remember all the LORD’s commandments, and do them; and that you don’t follow your own heart and your own eyes, after which you used to play the prostitute;</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Romans 12:1; 1 Thessalonians 4:7; Leviticus 11:44–45"> so that you may remember and do all my commandments, and be holy to your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9–10; Ezekiel 36:25–27; Hebrews 11:16"> I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: I am the LORD your God.”</span>
</div>
