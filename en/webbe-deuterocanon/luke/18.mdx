<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> <span className="woj">I tell you, this man went down to his house justified rather than the other; for everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, but he who humbles himself will be exalted.”</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> A certain ruler asked him, saying, “Good Teacher, what shall I do to inherit eternal life?”</span>

</div>

Sell all that you have and distribute it to the poor. Then you will have treasure in heaven; then come, follow me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> But when he heard these things, he became very sad, for he was very rich.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you, there is no one who has left house, or wife, or brothers, or parents, or children, for God’s Kingdom’s sake,</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; Psalms 22:1–31"> He took the twelve aside and said to them,<span className="woj">“Behold, we are going up to Jerusalem, and all the things that are written through the prophets concerning the Son of Man will be completed.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> He cried out, “Jesus, you son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; Psalms 141:1; Luke 18:15"> Those who led the way rebuked him, that he should be quiet; but he cried out all the more, “You son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>

<div className="bp">
