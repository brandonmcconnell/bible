<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 13:2–3; Ezra 8:23; Nehemiah 8:2"> Now in the twenty-fourth day of this month the children of Israel were assembled with fasting, with sackcloth, and dirt on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 9:3–10; Nehemiah 13:3; Ezra 10:11"> The offspring of Israel separated themselves from all foreigners and stood and confessed their sins and the iniquities of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:7–8; Nehemiah 8:3–4"> They stood up in their place, and read in the book of the law of the LORD their God a fourth part of the day; and a fourth part they confessed and worshipped the LORD their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:7; Nehemiah 12:8; Nehemiah 9:5"> Then Jeshua, Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani of the Levites stood up on the stairs, and cried with a loud voice to the LORD their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 103:1–2; 1 Peter 1:3; 2 Corinthians 4:6"> Then the Levites, Jeshua, and Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabneiah, Sherebiah, Hodiah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah, said, “Stand up and bless the LORD your God from everlasting to everlasting! Blessed be your glorious name, which is exalted above all blessing and praise!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:16; Genesis 1:1; Revelation 4:11"> You are the LORD, even you alone. You have made heaven, the heaven of heavens, with all their army, the earth and all things that are on it, the seas and all that is in them, and you preserve them all. The army of heaven worships you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Genesis 17:5; Isaiah 51:2"> You are the LORD, the God who chose Abram, brought him out of Ur of the Chaldees, gave him the name of Abraham,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:6; Genesis 15:18–21; Hebrews 6:18"> found his heart faithful before you, and made a covenant with him to give the land of the Canaanite, the Hittite, the Amorite, the Perizzite, the Jebusite, and the Girgashite, to give it to his offspring, and have performed your words, for you are righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–9; Acts 7:34; Exodus 3:16"> “You saw the affliction of our fathers in Egypt, and heard their cry by the Red Sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:20; Daniel 9:15; Exodus 18:11"> and showed signs and wonders against Pharaoh, against all his servants, and against all the people of his land, for you knew that they dealt proudly against them, and made a name for yourself, as it is today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:13; Exodus 14:21–22; Exodus 14:27–28"> You divided the sea before them, so that they went through the middle of the sea on the dry land; and you cast their pursuers into the depths, as a stone into the mighty waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 13:21–22; Nehemiah 9:19; Psalms 107:7"> Moreover, in a pillar of cloud you led them by day; and in a pillar of fire by night, to give them light in the way in which they should go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 119:160; Exodus 20:1; Psalms 119:127–128"> “You also came down on Mount Sinai, and spoke with them from heaven, and gave them right ordinances and true laws, good statutes and commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 20:8–11; Genesis 2:3; Ezekiel 20:12"> and made known to them your holy Sabbath, and commanded them commandments, statutes, and a law, by Moses your servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–4; Deuteronomy 1:8; Exodus 17:6"> and gave them bread from the sky for their hunger, and brought water out of the rock for them for their thirst, and commanded them that they should go in to possess the land which you had sworn to give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; Psalms 81:11–14; Hebrews 3:15"> “But they and our fathers behaved proudly, hardened their neck, didn’t listen to your commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 86:5; Joel 2:13; Psalms 145:8–9"> and refused to obey. They weren’t mindful of your wonders that you did amongst them, but hardened their neck, and in their rebellion appointed a captain to return to their bondage. But you are a God ready to pardon, gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and abundant in loving kindness, and didn’t forsake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–23; Exodus 32:4–8; Ezekiel 20:7–44"> Yes, when they had made themselves a moulded calf, and said, ‘This is your God who brought you up out of Egypt,’ and had committed awful blasphemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Nehemiah 9:27; Malachi 3:6"> yet you in your manifold mercies didn’t forsake them in the wilderness. The pillar of cloud didn’t depart from over them by day, to lead them in the way; neither did the pillar of fire by night, to show them light, and the way in which they should go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 143:10; Numbers 11:17; Isaiah 63:11–14"> You gave also your good Spirit to instruct them, and didn’t withhold your manna from their mouth, and gave them water for their thirst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Deuteronomy 8:4; Psalms 34:10"> “Yes, forty years you sustained them in the wilderness. They lacked nothing. Their clothes didn’t grow old, and their feet didn’t swell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Deuteronomy 3:1–17; Deuteronomy 2:26–36"> Moreover you gave them kingdoms and peoples, which you allotted according to their portions. So they possessed the land of Sihon, even the land of the king of Heshbon, and the land of Og king of Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 15:5; 1 Chronicles 27:23"> You also multiplied their children as the stars of the sky, and brought them into the land concerning which you said to their fathers that they should go in to possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 21:43; Joshua 11:23; Psalms 44:2–3"> “So the children went in and possessed the land; and you subdued before them the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, and gave them into their hands, with their kings and the peoples of the land, that they might do with them as they pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:15; Nehemiah 9:35; Deuteronomy 3:5"> They took fortified cities and a rich land, and possessed houses full of all good things, cisterns dug out, vineyards, olive groves, and fruit trees in abundance. So they ate, were filled, became fat, and delighted themselves in your great goodness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 14:9; Judges 2:11–12; Nehemiah 9:18"> “Nevertheless they were disobedient and rebelled against you, cast your law behind their back, killed your prophets that testified against them to turn them again to you, and they committed awful blasphemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 106:41–45; 2 Kings 14:27; 2 Kings 13:5"> Therefore you delivered them into the hand of their adversaries, who distressed them. In the time of their trouble, when they cried to you, you heard from heaven; and according to your manifold mercies you gave them saviours who saved them out of the hands of their adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31–1; Judges 4:1; 1 Kings 8:39"> But after they had rest, they did evil again before you; therefore you left them in the hands of their enemies, so that they had the dominion over them; yet when they returned and cried to you, you heard from heaven; and many times you delivered them according to your mercies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:16; Leviticus 18:5; Nehemiah 9:26"> and testified against them, that you might bring them again to your law. Yet they were arrogant, and didn’t listen to your commandments, but sinned against your ordinances (which if a man does, he shall live in them), turned their backs, stiffened their neck, and would not hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Isaiah 42:24"> Yet many years you put up with them, and testified against them by your Spirit through your prophets. Yet they would not listen. Therefore you gave them into the hand of the peoples of the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Lamentations 3:22; Jeremiah 4:27; Nehemiah 9:17"> “Nevertheless in your manifold mercies you didn’t make a full end of them, nor forsake them; for you are a gracious and merciful God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5; Daniel 9:4"> Now therefore, our God, the great, the mighty, and the awesome God, who keeps covenant and loving kindness, don’t let all the travail seem little before you that has come on us, on our kings, on our princes, on our priests, on our prophets, on our fathers, and on all your people, since the time of the kings of Assyria to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:25; Psalms 106:6; Job 34:23"> However you are just in all that has come on us; for you have dealt truly, but we have done wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:19; 2 Kings 17:13; Deuteronomy 31:21"> Also our kings, our princes, our priests, and our fathers have not kept your law, nor listened to your commandments and your testimonies with which you testified against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:47; Deuteronomy 32:12–15; Jeremiah 5:19"> For they have not served you in their kingdom, and in your great goodness that you gave them, and in the large and rich land which you gave before them. They didn’t turn from their wicked works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezra 9:9; Deuteronomy 28:48; 2 Chronicles 12:8"> “Behold, we are servants today, and as for the land that you gave to our fathers to eat its fruit and its good, behold, we are servants in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Deuteronomy 28:48; Ezra 7:24"> It yields much increase to the kings whom you have set over us because of our sins. Also they have power over our bodies and over our livestock, at their pleasure, and we are in great distress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; 2 Chronicles 34:31; Ezra 10:3"> Yet for all this, we make a sure covenant, and write it; and our princes, our Levites, and our priests, seal it.”</span>
</div>
