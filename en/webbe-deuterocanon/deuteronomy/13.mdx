<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:25–28; 1 John 4:1; Jeremiah 29:8"> If a prophet or a dreamer of dreams arises amongst you, and he gives you a sign or a wonder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:6; Matthew 7:22–23; Revelation 13:13–14"> and the sign or the wonder comes to pass, of which he spoke to you, saying, “Let’s go after other gods” (which you have not known) “and let’s serve them,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:2; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Psalms 66:10"> you shall not listen to the words of that prophet, or to that dreamer of dreams; for the LORD your God is testing you, to know whether you love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Deuteronomy 10:20; 1 Corinthians 6:17"> You shall walk after the LORD your God, fear him, keep his commandments, and obey his voice. You shall serve him, and cling to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:13; Deuteronomy 18:20; Jeremiah 14:15"> That prophet, or that dreamer of dreams, shall be put to death, because he has spoken rebellion against the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you out of the house of bondage, to draw you aside out of the way which the LORD your God commanded you to walk in. So you shall remove the evil from amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:2–7; 2 Peter 2:1; Ephesians 4:14"> If your brother, the son of your mother, or your son, or your daughter, or the wife of your bosom, or your friend who is as your own soul, entices you secretly, saying, “Let’s go and serve other gods”—which you have not known, you, nor your fathers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> of the gods of the peoples who are around you, near to you, or far off from you, from the one end of the earth even to the other end of the earth—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Proverbs 1:10; Deuteronomy 7:16"> you shall not consent to him nor listen to him; neither shall your eye pity him, neither shall you spare, neither shall you conceal him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Luke 14:26; Matthew 10:37"> but you shall surely kill him. Your hand shall be first on him to put him to death, and afterwards the hands of all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 7:25; Leviticus 24:14–16; Exodus 20:2"> You shall stone him to death with stones, because he has sought to draw you away from the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:20; Deuteronomy 17:13; 1 Timothy 5:20"> All Israel shall hear, and fear, and shall not do any more wickedness like this amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 20:1–17; Joshua 22:11–34"> If you hear about one of your cities, which the LORD your God gives you to dwell there, that</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 John 2:19; Deuteronomy 13:2; 2 Corinthians 6:15"> certain wicked fellows have gone out from amongst you and have drawn away the inhabitants of their city, saying, “Let’s go and serve other gods,” which you have not known,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:4; Isaiah 11:3–4; John 7:24"> then you shall enquire, investigate, and ask diligently. Behold, if it is true, and the thing certain, that such abomination was done amongst you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Leviticus 27:28; Deuteronomy 7:2"> you shall surely strike the inhabitants of that city with the edge of the sword, destroying it utterly, with all that is therein and its livestock, with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:2; Joshua 8:28; Isaiah 25:2"> You shall gather all its plunder into the middle of its street, and shall burn with fire the city, with all of its plunder, to the LORD your God. It shall be a heap forever. It shall not be built again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 26:4; Deuteronomy 7:26; Joshua 7:26"> Nothing of the devoted thing shall cling to your hand, that the LORD may turn from the fierceness of his anger and show you mercy, and have compassion on you and multiply you, as he has sworn to your fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:28; Matthew 7:21; Deuteronomy 12:25"> when you listen to the LORD your God’s voice, to keep all his commandments which I command you today, to do that which is right in the LORD your God’s eyes.</span>
