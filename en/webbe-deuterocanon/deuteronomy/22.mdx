<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 23:4–5; Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 58:7"> You shall not see your brother’s ox or his sheep go astray and hide yourself from them. You shall surely bring them again to your brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; 1 Thessalonians 4:6"> If your brother isn’t near to you, or if you don’t know him, then you shall bring it home to your house, and it shall be with you until your brother comes looking for it, and you shall restore it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So you shall do with his donkey. So you shall do with his garment. So you shall do with every lost thing of your brother’s, which he has lost and you have found. You may not hide yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:1–2; Hebrews 12:12–13; Matthew 5:44"> You shall not see your brother’s donkey or his ox fallen down by the way, and hide yourself from them. You shall surely help him to lift them up again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:4–15; Deuteronomy 18:12"> A woman shall not wear men’s clothing, neither shall a man put on women’s clothing; for whoever does these things is an abomination to the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 22:28; Proverbs 12:10; Genesis 8:17"> If you come across a bird’s nest on the way, in any tree or on the ground, with young ones or eggs, and the hen sitting on the young, or on the eggs, you shall not take the hen with the young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Proverbs 22:4"> You shall surely let the hen go, but the young you may take for yourself, that it may be well with you, and that you may prolong your days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:2; Isaiah 22:1; Exodus 21:28–36"> When you build a new house, then you shall make a railing around your roof, so that you don’t bring blood on your house if anyone falls from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19; 2 Corinthians 11:3; Matthew 9:16"> You shall not sow your vineyard with two kinds of seed, lest all the fruit be defiled, the seed which you have sown, and the increase of the vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–16"> You shall not plough with an ox and a donkey together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:19"> You shall not wear clothes of wool and linen woven together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 23:5; Numbers 15:37–41"> You shall make yourselves fringes<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, tassels">a</a></sup>on the four corners of your cloak with which you cover yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 29:21; Genesis 29:31; Ephesians 5:28–29"> If any man takes a wife, and goes in to her, hates her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:8"> accuses her of shameful things, gives her a bad name, and says, “I took this woman, and when I came near to her, I didn’t find in her the tokens of virginity;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> then the young lady’s father and mother shall take and bring the tokens of the young lady’s virginity to the elders of the city in the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The young lady’s father shall tell the elders, “I gave my daughter to this man as his wife, and he hates her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Behold, he has accused her of shameful things, saying, ‘I didn’t find in your daughter the tokens of virginity;’ and yet these are the tokens of my daughter’s virginity.” They shall spread the cloth before the elders of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 18:21"> The elders of that city shall take the man and chastise him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Matthew 19:8–9; Deuteronomy 24:1–4"> They shall fine him one hundred shekels of silver,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces, so 100 shekels is about a kilogram or 2.2 pounds.">b</a></sup>and give them to the father of the young lady, because he has given a bad name to a virgin of Israel. She shall be his wife. He may not put her away all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But if this thing is true, that the tokens of virginity were not found in the young lady,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:5; Genesis 34:7; Judges 20:6"> then they shall bring out the young lady to the door of her father’s house, and the men of her city shall stone her to death with stones, because she has done folly in Israel, to play the prostitute in her father’s house. So you shall remove the evil from amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:45–47; John 8:4–5"> If a man is found lying with a woman married to a husband, then they shall both die, the man who lay with the woman and the woman. So you shall remove the evil from Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:18–19; Deuteronomy 20:7"> If there is a young lady who is a virgin pledged to be married to a husband, and a man finds her in the city, and lies with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–22; Deuteronomy 21:14; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> then you shall bring them both out to the gate of that city, and you shall stone them to death with stones; the lady, because she didn’t cry, being in the city; and the man, because he has humbled his neighbour’s wife. So you shall remove the evil from amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:14"> But if the man finds the lady who is pledged to be married in the field, and the man forces her and lies with her, then only the man who lay with her shall die;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:22"> but to the lady you shall do nothing. There is in the lady no sin worthy of death; for as when a man rises against his neighbour and kills him, even so is this matter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7"> for he found her in the field, the pledged to be married lady cried, and there was no one to save her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17"> If a man finds a lady who is a virgin, who is not pledged to be married, grabs her and lies with her, and they are found,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Deuteronomy 22:24; Deuteronomy 21:14"> then the man who lay with her shall give to the lady’s father fifty shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">c</a></sup>of silver. She shall be his wife, because he has humbled her. He may not put her away all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Leviticus 18:8"> A man shall not take his father’s wife, and shall not uncover his father’s skirt.</span>
