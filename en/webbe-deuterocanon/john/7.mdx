<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> <span className="woj">If a boy receives circumcision on the Sabbath, that the law of Moses may not be broken, are you angry with me because I made a man completely healthy on the Sabbath?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> <span className="woj">Don’t judge according to appearance, but judge righteous judgement.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Jesus therefore cried out in the temple, teaching and saying,<span className="woj">“You both know me, and know where I am from. <span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 55:1; John 4:14; John 6:35"> Now on the last and greatest day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried out,<span className="woj">“If anyone is thirsty, let him come to me and drink!</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 7:12"> So a division arose in the multitude because of him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 7:32; Acts 5:21–27"> The officers therefore came to the chief priests and Pharisees; and they said to them, “Why didn’t you bring him?”</span>
