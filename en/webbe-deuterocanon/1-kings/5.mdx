<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:1; 2 Chronicles 2:3; 1 Kings 5:10"> Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to Solomon, for he had heard that they had anointed him king in the place of his father, and Hiram had always loved David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:3"> Solomon sent to Hiram, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:3; 1 Chronicles 22:8; Psalms 110:1"> “You know that David my father could not build a house for the name of the LORD his God because of the wars which were around him on every side, until the LORD put his enemies under the soles of his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 22:9; 1 Kings 4:24; Acts 9:31"> But now the LORD my God has given me rest on every side. There is no enemy and no evil occurrence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 17:12; 1 Chronicles 22:10; 2 Samuel 7:12–13"> Behold, I intend to build a house for the name of the LORD my God, as the LORD spoke to David my father, saying, ‘Your son, whom I will set on your throne in your place shall build the house for my name.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:8; 1 Kings 6:9–10; Romans 12:17"> Now therefore command that cedar trees be cut for me out of Lebanon. My servants will be with your servants; and I will give you wages for your servants according to all that you say. For you know that there is nobody amongst us who knows how to cut timber like the Sidonians.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 23:24; Isaiah 9:6; 2 Chronicles 2:11–12"> When Hiram heard the words of Solomon, he rejoiced greatly, and said, “Blessed is the LORD today, who has given to David a wise son to rule over this great people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 6:34; 2 Chronicles 3:5; 2 Samuel 6:5"> Hiram sent to Solomon, saying, “I have heard the message which you have sent to me. I will do all your desire concerning timber of cedar, and concerning cypress timber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; Ezekiel 27:17; Acts 12:20"> My servants will bring them down from Lebanon to the sea. I will make them into rafts to go by sea to the place that you specify to me, and will cause them to be broken up there, and you will receive them. You will accomplish my desire, in giving food for my household.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So Hiram gave Solomon cedar timber and cypress timber according to all his desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 2:10; 1 Kings 4:22"> Solomon gave Hiram twenty thousand cors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="20,000 cors would be about 120,000 bushels or about 4.2 megalitres of wheat, which would weigh about 3,270 metric tonnes.">a</a></sup>of wheat for food to his household, and twenty cors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="20 cors is about 1,100 gallons or about 4220 litres.">b</a></sup>of pure oil. Solomon gave this to Hiram year by year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; Amos 1:9; James 1:5"> The LORD gave Solomon wisdom, as he promised him. There was peace between Hiram and Solomon, and the two of them made a treaty together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 9:15; 1 Kings 4:6"> King Solomon raised a levy out of all Israel; and the levy was thirty thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 4:6–19; 1 Chronicles 27:1–15"> He sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand a month by courses: for a month they were in Lebanon, and two months at home; and Adoniram was over the men subject to forced labour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 9:20–22; 2 Chronicles 2:17–18; 2 Chronicles 8:7–9"> Solomon had seventy thousand who bore burdens, and eighty thousand who were stone cutters in the mountains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 9:23; 2 Chronicles 2:2"> besides Solomon’s chief officers who were over the work: three thousand and three hundred who ruled over the people who laboured in the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 6:7; 1 Chronicles 22:2; Isaiah 28:16"> The king commanded, and they cut out large stones, costly stones, to lay the foundation of the house with worked stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 13:5; Ezekiel 27:9; Psalms 83:7"> Solomon’s builders and Hiram’s builders and the Gebalites cut them, and prepared the timber and the stones to build the house.</span>
