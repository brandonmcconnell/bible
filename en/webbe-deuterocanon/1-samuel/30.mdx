<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:7; 1 Samuel 29:11; 1 Samuel 27:8–10"> When David and his men had come to Ziklag on the third day, the Amalekites had made a raid on the South and on Ziklag, and had struck Ziklag and burnt it with fire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:11; Job 38:11; Psalms 76:10"> and had taken captive the women and all who were in it, both small and great. They didn’t kill any, but carried them off and went their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:19; 1 Peter 1:6–7; Revelation 3:9"> When David and his men came to the city, behold, it was burnt with fire; and their wives, their sons, and their daughters were taken captive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; Numbers 14:1; Judges 21:2"> Then David and the people who were with him lifted up their voice and wept until they had no more power to weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:42–43; 2 Samuel 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:2"> David’s two wives were taken captive, Ahinoam the Jezreelitess, and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 56:3–4; Psalms 56:11; Psalms 62:5"> David was greatly distressed, for the people spoke of stoning him, because the souls of all the people were grieved, every man for his sons and for his daughters; but David strengthened himself in the LORD his God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26; 1 Samuel 22:20–21; 1 Samuel 23:2–9"> David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelech, “Please bring the ephod here to me.” Abiathar brought the ephod to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:18; Proverbs 3:5–6; 1 Samuel 23:2"> David enquired of the LORD, saying, “If I pursue after this troop, will I overtake them?” He answered him, “Pursue, for you will surely overtake them, and will without fail recover all.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:2"> So David went, he and the six hundred men who were with him, and came to the brook Besor, where those who were left behind stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:9; 1 Samuel 30:21; 1 Samuel 14:31"> But David pursued, he and four hundred men; for two hundred stayed behind, who were so faint that they couldn’t go over the brook Besor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 12:20–21; Deuteronomy 23:7; Matthew 25:35"> They found an Egyptian in the field, and brought him to David, and gave him bread, and he ate; and they gave him water to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 15:19; 1 Samuel 14:27; 1 Samuel 30:13"> They gave him a piece of a cake of figs and two clusters of raisins. When he had eaten, his spirit came again to him; for he had eaten no bread, and drank no water for three days and three nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 2:13; Proverbs 12:10; Job 31:13–15"> David asked him, “To whom do you belong? Where are you from?” He said, “I am a young man of Egypt, servant to an Amalekite; and my master left me, because three days ago I got sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 1:38; 2 Samuel 8:18; 1 Kings 1:44"> We made a raid on the South of the Cherethites, and on that which belongs to Judah, and on the South of Caleb; and we burnt Ziklag with fire.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Joshua 9:19–20; Ezekiel 17:16"> David said to him, “Will you bring me down to this troop?” He said, “Swear to me by God that you will not kill me and not deliver me up into the hands of my master, and I will bring you down to this troop.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; 2 Samuel 13:28; Judges 16:23–30"> When he had brought him down, behold, they were spread around over all the ground, eating, drinking, and dancing, because of all the great plunder that they had taken out of the land of the Philistines, and out of the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:11; Psalms 18:42; 1 Samuel 15:3"> David struck them from the twilight even to the evening of the next day. Not a man of them escaped from there, except four hundred young men who rode on camels and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 14:16"> David recovered all that the Amalekites had taken, and David rescued his two wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 91:9–10; 1 Samuel 30:8; Job 1:10"> There was nothing lacking to them, neither small nor great, neither sons nor daughters, neither plunder, nor anything that they had taken. David brought them all back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:26; Numbers 31:9–12; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> David took all the flocks and the herds, which they drove before those other livestock, and said, “This is David’s plunder.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 13:1; 1 Samuel 30:10; Judges 8:15"> David came to the two hundred men, who were so faint that they could not follow David, whom also they had made to stay at the brook Besor; and they went out to meet David, and to meet the people who were with him. When David came near to the people, he greeted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:12; Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Kings 21:10"> Then all the wicked men and worthless fellows of those who went with David answered and said, “Because they didn’t go with us, we will not give them anything of the plunder that we have recovered, except to every man his wife and his children, that he may lead them away and depart.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Psalms 44:2–7; Acts 7:2"> Then David said, “Do not do so, my brothers, with that which the LORD has given to us, who has preserved us, and delivered the troop that came against us into our hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Numbers 31:27; Psalms 68:12"> Who will listen to you in this matter? For as his share is who goes down to the battle, so shall his share be who stays with the baggage. They shall share alike.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13"> It was so from that day forward that he made it a statute and an ordinance for Israel to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Genesis 33:11; Isaiah 32:8"> When David came to Ziklag, he sent some of the plunder to the elders of Judah, even to his friends, saying, “Behold, a present for you from the plunder of the LORD’s enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 15:48; Joshua 19:8; Judges 1:22–23"> He sent it to those who were in Bethel, to those who were in Ramoth of the South, to those who were in Jattir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 13:16; Joshua 15:50; Joshua 21:14"> to those who were in Aroer, to those who were in Siphmoth, to those who were in Eshtemoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 1:16; 1 Samuel 27:10; 1 Samuel 15:6"> to those who were in Racal, to those who were in the cities of the Jerahmeelites, to those who were in the cities of the Kenites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 1:17; Joshua 19:4; Joshua 15:42"> to those who were in Hormah, to those who were in Borashan, to those who were in Athach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:1; 2 Samuel 15:10; 2 Samuel 4:1"> to those who were in Hebron, and to all the places where David himself and his men used to stay.</span>
