<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 27:1; Jeremiah 44:30"> Speak and say, ‘The Lord GOD says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Behold, I am against you, Pharaoh king of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the great monster that lies in the middle of his rivers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that has said, ‘My river is my own,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I have made it for myself.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Ezekiel 38:4; Amos 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will put hooks in your jaws,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will make the fish of your rivers stick to your scales.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will bring you up out of the middle of your rivers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with all the fish of your rivers which stick to your scales.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20; Ezekiel 32:4–6; Jeremiah 8:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I’ll cast you out into the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you and all the fish of your rivers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You’ll fall on the open field.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You won’t be brought together or gathered.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have given you for food to the animals of the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to the birds of the sky.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6; Exodus 9:14"> “‘“All the inhabitants of Egypt will know that I am the LORD, because they have been a staff of reed to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 17:15–17; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> When they took hold of you by your hand, you broke and tore all their shoulders. When they leaned on you, you broke and paralysed all of their thighs.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> “‘Therefore the Lord GOD says: “Behold, I will bring a sword on you, and will cut off man and animal from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Proverbs 16:18; Ezekiel 29:10–12"> The land of Egypt will be a desolation and a waste. Then they will know that I am the LORD.<br />“‘“Because he has said, ‘The river is mine, and I have made it,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 30:6–9; Exodus 14:2"> therefore, behold, I am against you and against your rivers. I will make the land of Egypt an utter waste and desolation, from the tower of Seveneh even to the border of Ethiopia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:13; Jeremiah 43:11–12; Ezekiel 30:10–13"> No foot of man will pass through it, nor will any animal foot pass through it. It won’t be inhabited for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:19; Ezekiel 30:7; Ezekiel 30:23"> I will make the land of Egypt a desolation in the middle of the countries that are desolate. Her cities amongst the cities that are laid waste will be a desolation forty years. I will scatter the Egyptians amongst the nations, and will disperse them through the countries.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:26; Isaiah 19:22"> “‘For the Lord GOD says: “At the end of forty years I will gather the Egyptians from the peoples where they were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 11:11; Jeremiah 44:1"> I will reverse the captivity of Egypt, and will cause them to return into the land of Pathros, into the land of their birth. There they will be a lowly kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Ezekiel 30:13; Ezekiel 17:14"> It will be the lowest of the kingdoms. It won’t lift itself up above the nations any more. I will diminish them so that they will no longer rule over the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Isaiah 20:5; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> It will no longer be the confidence of the house of Israel, bringing iniquity to memory, when they turn to look after them. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.”’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Ezekiel 24:1"> It came to pass in the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, in the first day of the month, the LORD’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:6; Jeremiah 25:9; Ezekiel 26:7–12"> “Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon caused his army to serve a great service against Tyre. Every head was made bald, and every shoulder was worn; yet he had no wages, nor did his army, from Tyre, for the service that he had served against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:24–25; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Ezekiel 32:11"> Therefore the Lord GOD says: ‘Behold, I will give the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. He will carry off her multitude, take her plunder, and take her prey. That will be the wages for his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Isaiah 10:6–7; Isaiah 45:1–3"> I have given him the land of Egypt as his payment for which he served, because they worked for me,’ says the Lord GOD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; Luke 21:15; Psalms 132:17"> “In that day I will cause a horn to sprout for the house of Israel, and I will open your mouth amongst them. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”</span>
</div>
