<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your borders are in the heart of the seas.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your builders have perfected your beauty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Isaiah 14:8; Song of Solomon 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have made all your planks of cypress trees from Senir.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They have taken a cedar from Lebanon to make a mast for you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 2:13; Zechariah 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have made your oars of the oaks of Bashan.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They have made your benches of ivory inlaid in cypress wood from the islands of Kittim.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Exodus 25:4; Genesis 10:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your sail was of fine linen with embroidered work from Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that it might be to you for a banner.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Blue and purple from the islands of Elishah was your awning.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 10:18; 1 Kings 9:27; Ezekiel 27:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The inhabitants of Sidon and Arvad were your rowers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your wise men, Tyre, were in you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They were your pilots.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; Joshua 13:5; Psalms 83:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The old men of Gebal</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its wise men were your repairers of ship seams in you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All the ships of the sea with their mariners were in you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to deal in your merchandise.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:5; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 66:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">“‘“Persia, Lud, and Put were in your army,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your men of war.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They hung the shield and helmet in you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They showed your beauty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The men of Arvad with your army were on your walls all around,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and valiant men were in your towers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They hung their shields on your walls all around.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They have perfected your beauty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 23:10; Ezekiel 38:13"> “‘“Tarshish was your merchant by reason of the multitude of all kinds of riches. They traded for your wares with silver, iron, tin, and lead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:2; Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 39:1"> “‘“Javan, Tubal, and Meshech were your traders. They traded the persons of men and vessels of bronze for your merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Ezekiel 38:6; 1 Chronicles 1:6"> “‘“They of the house of Togarmah traded for your wares with horses, war horses, and mules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Revelation 18:12; 1 Kings 10:22"> “‘“The men of Dedan traded with you. Many islands were the market of your hand. They brought you horns of ivory and ebony in exchange.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Samuel 10:6"> “‘“Syria was your merchant by reason of the multitude of your handiworks. They traded for your wares with emeralds, purple, embroidered work, fine linen, coral, and rubies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:33; 1 Kings 5:9; Jeremiah 8:22"> “‘“Judah and the land of Israel were your traders. They traded wheat of Minnith, confections, honey, oil, and balm for your merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:8; Genesis 15:2; Ezekiel 47:16–18"> “‘“Damascus was your merchant for the multitude of your handiworks by reason of the multitude of all kinds of riches, with the wine of Helbon, and white wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–24; Psalms 45:8; Song of Solomon 4:13–14"> “‘“Vedan and Javan traded with yarn for your wares; wrought iron, cassia, and calamus were amongst your merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 25:3; Ezekiel 27:15"> “‘“Dedan was your merchant in precious saddle blankets for riding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 60:7; Genesis 25:13; 1 Kings 10:15"> “‘“Arabia and all the princes of Kedar were your favourite dealers in lambs, rams, and goats. In these, they were your merchants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 72:10"> “‘“The traders of Sheba and Raamah were your traders. They traded for your wares with the best of all spices, all precious stones, and gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:12; Amos 1:5"> “‘“Haran, Canneh, Eden, the traders of Sheba, Asshur and Chilmad, were your traders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 2:8"> These were your traders in choice wares, in wrappings of blue and embroidered work, and in cedar chests of rich clothing bound with cords, amongst your merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 2:16; Psalms 48:7; Isaiah 23:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“‘“The ships of Tarshish were your caravans for your merchandise.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You were replenished</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and made very glorious in the heart of the seas.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 48:7; Ezekiel 26:19; Acts 27:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your rowers have brought you into great waters.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The east wind has broken you in the heart of the seas.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Ezekiel 27:24; Ezekiel 27:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your riches, your wares, your merchandise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your mariners, your pilots, your repairers of ship seams,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the dealers in your merchandise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all your men of war who are in you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with all your company which is amongst you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will fall into the heart of the seas in the day of your ruin.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:10; Exodus 15:14; Ezekiel 31:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">At the sound of the cry of your pilots,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the pasture lands will shake.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:16; Ezekiel 32:10; Revelation 18:17–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">All who handle the oars,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the mariners and all the pilots of the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will come down from their ships.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will stand on the land,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; 2 Samuel 1:2; Lamentations 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-2">and will cause their voice to be heard over you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will cry bitterly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will cast up dust on their heads.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will wallow in the ashes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:18; Isaiah 22:12; Isaiah 15:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will make themselves bald for you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and clothe themselves with sackcloth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will weep for you in bitterness of soul,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with bitter mourning.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:17; Revelation 18:18; Ezekiel 27:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">In their wailing they will take up a lamentation for you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and lament over you, saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Who is there like Tyre,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like her who is brought to silence in the middle of the sea?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 28:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">When your wares came from the seas,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you filled many peoples.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You enriched the kings of the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the multitude of your riches and of your merchandise.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Zechariah 9:3–4; Ezekiel 26:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the time that you were broken by the seas,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the depths of the waters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your merchandise and all your company fell within you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 23:6; Ezekiel 26:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the inhabitants of the islands are astonished at you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their kings are horribly afraid.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They are troubled in their face.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Psalms 37:10; Zephaniah 2:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The merchants amongst the peoples hiss at you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have come to a terrible end,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will be no more.”’”</span></span>
