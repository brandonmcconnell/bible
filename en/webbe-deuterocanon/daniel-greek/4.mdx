<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Nebuchadnezzar the king, to all the peoples, nations, and languages, who dwell in all the earth: Peace be multiplied to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> It has seemed good to me to show the signs and wonders that the Most High God has worked towards me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> <span className="pl pl-2">How great are his signs!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">How mighty are his wonders!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">His dominion is from generation to generation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> I, Nebuchadnezzar, was at rest in my house, and flourishing in my palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> I saw a dream which made me afraid; and the thoughts on my bed and the visions of my head troubled me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Therefore I made a decree to bring in all the wise men of Babylon before me, that they might make known to me the interpretation of the dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then the magicians, the enchanters, the Chaldeans, and the soothsayers came in; and I told the dream before them; but they didn’t make known to me its interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But at the last Daniel came in before me, whose name was Belteshazzar, according to the name of my god, and in whom is the spirit of the holy gods. I told the dream before him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> “Belteshazzar, master of the magicians, because I know that the spirit of the holy gods is in you, and no secret troubles you, tell me the visions of my dream that I have seen, and its interpretation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> These were the visions of my head on my bed: I saw, and behold, a tree in the middle of the earth; and its height was great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The tree grew, and was strong, and its height reached to the sky, and its sight to the end of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Its leaves were beautiful, and it had much fruit, and in it was food for all. The animals of the field had shade under it, and the birds of the sky lived in its branches, and all flesh was fed from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “I saw in the visions of my head on my bed, and behold, a watcher and a holy one came down from the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He cried aloud, and said this, ‘Cut down the tree and cut off its branches! Shake off its leaves and scatter its fruit! Let the animals get away from under it, and the fowls from its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Nevertheless leave the stump of its roots in the earth, even with a band of iron and bronze, in the tender grass of the field; and let it be wet with the dew of the sky. Let his portion be with the animals in the grass of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Let his heart be changed from man’s, and let an animal’s heart be given to him. Then let seven times pass over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> “‘The sentence is by the decree of the watchers, and the demand by the word of the holy ones, to the intent that the living may know that the Most High rules in the kingdom of men, and gives it to whomever he will, and sets up over it the lowest of men.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> “This dream I, King Nebuchadnezzar, have seen; and you, Belteshazzar, declare the interpretation, because all the wise men of my kingdom are not able to make known to me the interpretation; but you are able, for the spirit of the holy gods is in you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then Daniel, whose name was Belteshazzar, was stricken mute for a while, and his thoughts troubled him. The king answered, “Belteshazzar, don’t let the dream, or the interpretation, trouble you.” Belteshazzar answered, “My lord, may the dream be for those who hate you, and its interpretation to your adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The tree that you saw, which grew and was strong, whose height reached to the sky, and its sight to all the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> whose leaves were beautiful, and its fruit plentiful, and in it was food for all; under which the animals of the field lived, and on whose branches the birds of the sky had their habitation—</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> it is you, O king, who have grown and become strong; for your greatness has grown, and reaches to the sky, and your dominion to the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> “Whereas the king saw a watcher and a holy one coming down from the sky, and saying, ‘Cut down the tree, and destroy it; nevertheless leave the stump of its roots in the earth, even with a band of iron and bronze, in the tender grass of the field, and let it be wet with the dew of the sky. Let his portion be with the animals of the field, until seven times pass over him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> “This is the interpretation, O king, and it is the decree of the Most High, which has come on my lord the king:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> that you shall be driven from men, and your dwelling shall be with the animals of the field. You shall be made to eat grass as oxen, and shall be wet with the dew of the sky, and seven times shall pass over you; until you know that the Most High rules in the kingdom of men, and gives it to whomever he will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Their command to leave the stump of the roots of the tree means your kingdom will be sure to you, after you will have known that the heavens do rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Therefore, O king, let my counsel be acceptable to you, and break off your sins by righteousness, and your iniquities by showing mercy to the poor. Perhaps there may be a lengthening of your tranquillity.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> All this came on the King Nebuchadnezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> At the end of twelve months he was walking in the royal palace of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The king spoke and said, “Is not this great Babylon, which I have built for the royal dwelling place, by the might of my power and for the glory of my majesty?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> While the word was in the king’s mouth, a voice came from the sky, saying, “O King Nebuchadnezzar, to you it is spoken: ‘The kingdom has departed from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> You shall be driven from men, and your dwelling shall be with the animals of the field. You shall be made to eat grass as oxen. Seven times shall pass over you, until you know that the Most High rules in the kingdom of men, and gives it to whomever he will.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> This was fulfilled the same hour on Nebuchadnezzar. He was driven from men, and ate grass as oxen, and his body was wet with the dew of the sky, until his hair had grown like eagles’ feathers, and his nails like birds’ claws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> At the end of the days I, Nebuchadnezzar, lifted up my eyes to heaven, and my understanding returned to me, and I blessed the Most High, and I praised and honoured him who lives forever.<br /><span className="pl pl-2">For his dominion is an everlasting dominion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and his kingdom from generation to generation.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> <span className="pl pl-2">All the inhabitants of the earth are reputed as nothing;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and he does according to his will in the army of heaven,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">and amongst the inhabitants of the earth;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no one can stop his hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-3">or ask him, “What are you doing?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> At the same time my understanding returned to me; and for the glory of my kingdom, my majesty and brightness returned to me. My counsellors and my lords sought me; and I was established in my kingdom, and excellent greatness was added to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and extol and honour the King of heaven; for all his works are right and his ways just; and those who walk in pride he is able to humble.</span>
