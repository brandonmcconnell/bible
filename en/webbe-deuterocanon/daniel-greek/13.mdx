## THE HISTORY OF SUSANNA

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The History of Susanna is translated from chapter 13 of Daniel in the Greek Septuagint. It is not found in the traditional Hebrew text of Daniel. The History of Susanna is recognised as Deuterocanonical Scripture by the Roman Catholic, Greek Orthodox, and Russian Orthodox Churches.">a</a></sup>A man lived in Babylon, and his name was Joakim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He took a wife, whose name was Susanna, the daughter of Helkias, a very fair woman, and one who feared the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Her parents were also righteous, and taught their daughter according to the law of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Now Joakim was a great rich man, and had a beautiful garden next to his house. The Jews used to come to him, because he was more honourable than all others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The same year, two of the elders of the people were appointed to be judges, such as the Lord spoke of, that wickedness came from Babylon from elders who were judges, who were supposed to govern the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> These were often at Joakim’s house. All that had any lawsuits came to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> When the people departed away at noon, Susanna went into her husband’s garden to walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The two elders saw her going in every day and walking; and they were inflamed with lust for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> They perverted their own mind and turned away their eyes, that they might not look to heaven, nor remember just judgements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And although they both were wounded with lust for her, yet dared not show the other his grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For they were ashamed to declare their lust, what they desired to do with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Yet they watched eagerly from day to day to see her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The one said to the other, “Let’s go home, now; for it is dinner time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So when they had gone out, they parted company, and turning back again, they came to the same place. After they had asked one another the cause, they acknowledged their lust. Then they appointed a time both together, when they might find her alone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> It happened, as they watched on an opportune day, she went in as before with only two maids, and she desired to wash herself in the garden; for it was hot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> There was nobody there except the two elders who had hid themselves and watched her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then she said to her maids, “Bring me olive oil and ointment, and shut the garden doors, that I may wash myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> They did as she asked them and shut the garden doors, and went out themselves at the side doors to fetch the things that she had commanded them. They didn’t see the elders, because they were hidden.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Now when the maids had gone out, the two elders rose up and ran to her, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> “Behold, the garden doors are shut, that no man can see us, and we are in love with you. Therefore consent to us, and lie with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If you will not, we will testify against you, that a young man was with you; therefore you sent your maids away from you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then Susanna sighed, and said, “I am trapped; for if I do this thing, it is death to me. If I don’t do it, I can’t escape your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> It is better for me to fall into your hands, and not do it, than to sin in the sight of the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> With that Susanna cried with a loud voice; and the two elders cried out against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then one of them ran and opened the garden doors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So when the servants of the house heard the cry in the garden, they rushed in at the side door to see what had happened to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> But when the elders had told their tale, the servants were greatly ashamed; for there was never such a report made of Susanna.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> It came to pass on the next day, when the people assembled to her husband Joakim, the two elders came full of their wicked intent against Susanna to put her to death,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and said before the people, “Send for Susanna, the daughter of Helkias, Joakim’s wife.” So they sent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and she came with her father and mother, her children, and all her kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Now Susanna was a very delicate woman, and beautiful to behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> These wicked men commanded her to be unveiled, for she was veiled, that they might be filled with her beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Therefore her friends and all who saw her wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Then the two elders stood up in the midst of the people and laid their hands upon her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> She, weeping, looked up towards heaven; for her heart trusted in the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The elders said, “As we walked in the garden alone, this woman came in with two maids, shut the garden doors, and sent the maids away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Then a young man who was hidden there came to her and lay with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And we, being in a corner of the garden, saw this wickedness and ran to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And when we saw them together, we couldn’t hold the man; for he was stronger than we, and opened the doors, and leapt out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> But having taken this woman, we asked who the young man was, but she would not tell us. We testify these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Then the assembly believed them, as those who were elders of the people and judges; so they condemned her to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then Susanna cried out with a loud voice, and said, “O everlasting God, you know the secrets, and know all things before they happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> You know that they have testified falsely against me. Behold, I must die, even though I never did such things as these men have maliciously invented against me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> The Lord heard her voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Therefore when she was led away to be put to death, God raised up the holy spirit of a young youth, whose name was Daniel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> He cried with a loud voice, “I am clear from the blood of this woman!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Then all the people turned them towards him, and said, “What do these words that you have spoken mean?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> So he, standing in the midst of them, said, “Are you all such fools, you sons of Israel, that without examination or knowledge of the truth you have condemned a daughter of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Return again to the place of judgement; for these have testified falsely against her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Therefore all the people turned again in haste, and the elders said to him, “Come, sit down amongst us, and show it to us, seeing God has given you the honour of an elder.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Then Daniel said to them, “Put them far apart from each another, and I will examine them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> So when they were put apart one from another, he called one of them, and said to him, “O you who have become old in wickedness, now your sins have returned which you have committed before,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> in pronouncing unjust judgement, condemning the innocent, and letting the guilty go free; although the Lord says, ‘You shall not kill the innocent and righteous.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Now then, if you saw her, tell me, under which tree did you see them companying together?”<br />He answered, “Under a mastick tree.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> And Daniel said, “You have certainly lied against your own head; for even now the angel of God has received the sentence of God and will cut you in two.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> So he put him aside, and commanded to bring the other, and said to him, “O you seed of Canaan, and not of Judah, beauty has deceived you, and lust has perverted your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Thus you have dealt with the daughters of Israel, and they for fear were intimate with you; but the daughter of Judah would not tolerate your wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> Now therefore tell me, under which tree did you take them being intimate together?”<br />He answered, “Under an evergreen oak tree.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Then Daniel said to him, “You have also certainly lied against your own head; for the angel of God waits with the sword to cut you in two, that he may destroy you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> With that, all the assembly cried out with a loud voice, and blessed God, who saves those who hope in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> Then they arose against the two elders, for Daniel had convicted them of false testimony out of their own mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> According to the law of Moses they did to them what they maliciously intended to do to their neighbour. They put them to death, and the innocent blood was saved the same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> Therefore Helkias and his wife praised God for their daughter Susanna, with Joakim her husband, and all the kindred, because there was no dishonesty found in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> And from that day forth, Daniel had a great reputation in the sight of the people.</span>
</div>
</div>
