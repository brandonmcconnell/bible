<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word translated “Lord” is “Adonai.”">a</a></sup>gave Jehoiakim king of Judah into his hand, with part of the vessels of the house of God;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).">b</a></sup>and he carried them into the land of Shinar to the house of his god. He brought the vessels into the treasure house of his god.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The king spoke to Ashpenaz the master of his eunuchs, that he should bring in some of the children of Israel, even of the royal offspring<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, seed">c</a></sup>and of the nobles—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> youths in whom was no defect, but well-favoured, and skilful in all wisdom, and endowed with knowledge, and understanding science, and who had the ability to serve in the king’s palace; and that he should teach them the learning and the language of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The king appointed for them a daily portion of the king’s delicacies, and of the wine which he drank, and that they should be nourished three years; that at its end they should serve the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Now amongst these were of the children of Judah: Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The prince of the eunuchs gave names to them: to Daniel he gave the name Belteshazzar; to Hananiah, Shadrach; to Mishael, Meshach; and to Azariah, Abednego.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> But Daniel purposed in his heart that he would not defile himself with the king’s delicacies, nor with the wine which he drank. Therefore he requested of the prince of the eunuchs that he might not defile himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Now God made Daniel find kindness and compassion in the sight of the prince of the eunuchs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The prince of the eunuchs said to Daniel, “I fear my lord the king, who has appointed your food and your drink. For why should he see your faces worse looking than the youths who are of your own age? Then you would endanger my head with the king.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then Daniel said to the steward whom the prince of the eunuchs had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> “Test your servants, I beg you, ten days; and let them give us vegetables to eat and water to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then let our faces be examined before you, and the face of the youths who eat of the king’s delicacies; and as you see, deal with your servants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So he listened to them in this matter, and tested them for ten days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> At the end of ten days, their faces appeared fairer, and they were fatter in flesh, than all the youths who ate of the king’s delicacies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So the steward took away their delicacies, and the wine that they would drink, and gave them vegetables.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Now as for these four youths, God gave them knowledge and skill in all learning and wisdom; and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> At the end of the days which the king had appointed for bringing them in, the prince of the eunuchs brought them in before Nebuchadnezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The king talked with them; and amongst them all was found no one like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah. Therefore they served the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> In every matter of wisdom and understanding concerning which the king enquired of them, he found them ten times better than all the magicians and enchanters who were in all his realm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Daniel continued serving even to the first year of King Cyrus.</span>
</div>
</div>
