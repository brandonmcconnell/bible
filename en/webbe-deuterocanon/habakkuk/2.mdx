</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; Revelation 14:13; Revelation 1:18–19"> The LORD answered me, “Write the vision, and make it plain on tablets, that he who runs may read it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Hebrews 10:36–37; Ezekiel 12:25"> For the vision is yet for the appointed time, and it hurries towards the end, and won’t prove false. Though it takes time, wait for it, because it will surely come. It won’t delay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38; Galatians 3:11–12"> Behold, his soul is puffed up. It is not upright in him, but the righteous will live by his faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:11–12; Proverbs 27:20; Proverbs 20:1"> Yes, moreover, wine is treacherous: an arrogant man who doesn’t stay at home, who enlarges his desire as Sheol;<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Sheol is the place of the dead.">a</a></sup>he is like death and can’t be satisfied, but gathers to himself all nations and heaps to himself all peoples.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:7; Micah 2:4; Jeremiah 50:13"> Won’t all these take up a parable against him, and a taunting proverb against him, and say, ‘Woe to him who increases that which is not his, and who enriches himself by extortion! How long?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 47:11; Jeremiah 8:17"> Won’t your debtors rise up suddenly, and wake up those who make you tremble, and you will be their victim?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Habakkuk 2:17; Jeremiah 27:7"> Because you have plundered many nations, all the remnant of the peoples will plunder you because of men’s blood, and for the violence done to the land, to the city and to all who dwell in it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:16; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Isaiah 28:15"> Woe to him who gets an evil gain for his house, that he may set his nest on high, that he may be delivered from the hand of evil!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nahum 1:14; 2 Kings 9:26; Jeremiah 36:31"> You have devised shame to your house by cutting off many peoples, and have sinned against your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 24:27; Job 31:38–40"> For the stone will cry out of the wall, and the beam out of the woodwork will answer it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 3:10; Daniel 4:27–31; Nahum 3:1"> Woe to him who builds a town with blood, and establishes a city by iniquity!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 21:30"> Behold, isn’t it from the LORD of Armies that the peoples labour for the fire, and the nations weary themselves for vanity?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Psalms 22:27; Psalms 72:19"> For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the LORD’s glory, as the waters cover the sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:7; Genesis 9:22; Revelation 18:3"> “Woe to him who gives his neighbour drink, pouring your inflaming wine until they are drunk, so that you may gaze at their naked bodies!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Nahum 3:5–6; Isaiah 51:21–23"> You are filled with shame, and not glory. You will also drink and be exposed! The cup of the LORD’s right hand will come around to you, and disgrace will cover your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:8; Psalms 55:23; Zechariah 11:1"> For the violence done to Lebanon will overwhelm you, and the destruction of the animals will terrify you, because of men’s blood and for the violence done to the land, to every city and to those who dwell in them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 44:9–10"> “What value does the engraved image have, that its maker has engraved it; the molten image, even the teacher of lies, that he who fashions its form trusts in it, to make mute idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:17; Jeremiah 10:4; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> Woe to him who says to the wood, ‘Awake!’ or to the mute stone, ‘Arise!’ Shall this teach? Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver, and there is no breath at all within it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:7; Psalms 46:10"> But the LORD is in his holy temple. Let all the earth be silent before him!”</span>
</div>
