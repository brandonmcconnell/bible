<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 18:18; 1 Peter 1:22–23; 1 Timothy 5:9–10"> I commend to you Phoebe, our sister, who is a servant<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, deacon">a</a></sup>of the assembly that is at Cenchreae,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:29; Philippians 1:27; Matthew 10:40–42"> that you receive her in the Lord in a way worthy of the saints, and that you assist her in whatever matter she may need from you, for she herself also has been a helper of many, and of my own self.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19; Acts 18:26"> Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Philippians 2:30; 1 John 3:16; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> who risked their own necks for my life, to whom not only I give thanks, but also all the assemblies of the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 4:15; Philemon 1:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19"> Greet the assembly that is in their house. Greet Epaenetus, my beloved, who is the first fruits of Achaia to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 16:12; Matthew 27:55; 1 Timothy 5:10"> Greet Mary, who laboured much for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 16:11; Romans 16:21; Philemon 1:23"> Greet Andronicus and Junia, my relatives and my fellow prisoners, who are notable amongst the apostles, who were also in Christ before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:1; 1 John 3:14; Romans 16:5"> Greet Amplias, my beloved in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:2–3; Romans 16:21"> Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and Stachys, my beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 14:18; 1 Peter 1:7; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Greet Apelles, the approved in Christ. Greet those who are of the household of Aristobulus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:21; Romans 16:7"> Greet Herodion, my kinsman. Greet them of the household of Narcissus, who are in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Hebrews 6:10–11; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Greet Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who labour in the Lord. Greet Persis, the beloved, who laboured much in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:21; 1 Timothy 5:2; 2 John 1:1"> Greet Rufus, the chosen in the Lord, and his mother and mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 1:2; Romans 8:29; Hebrews 3:1"> Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">b</a></sup>who are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:2; Romans 1:7; Romans 16:14"> Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints who are with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:26; 1 Corinthians 16:20; 2 Corinthians 13:12"> Greet one another with a holy kiss. The assemblies of Christ greet you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jude 1:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> Now I beg you, brothers, look out for those who are causing the divisions and occasions of stumbling, contrary to the doctrine which you learnt, and turn away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 2:4; Matthew 7:15; 1 Timothy 6:5"> For those who are such don’t serve our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly; and by their smooth and flattering speech they deceive the hearts of the innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; 1 Corinthians 14:20; Philippians 2:15"> For your obedience has become known to all. I rejoice therefore over you. But I desire to have you wise in that which is good, but innocent in that which is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:19; 1 John 3:8; Genesis 3:15"> And the God of peace will quickly crush Satan under your feet.<br />The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:1"> Timothy, my fellow worker, greets you, as do Lucius, Jason, and Sosipater, my relatives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; 1 Corinthians 16:21; Romans 16:8"> I, Tertius, who write the letter, greet you in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:14; Acts 19:22; 2 Timothy 4:20"> Gaius, my host and host of the whole assembly, greets you. Erastus, the treasurer of the city, greets you, as does Quartus, the brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all! Amen.</span>
