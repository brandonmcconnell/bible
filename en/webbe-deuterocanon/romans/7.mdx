<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:6; Romans 6:3"> Or don’t you know, brothers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">a</a></sup>(for I speak to men who know the law), that the law has dominion over a man for as long as he lives?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 2:23–24; 1 Corinthians 7:39; 1 Corinthians 7:4"> For the woman that has a husband is bound by law to the husband while he lives, but if the husband dies, she is discharged from the law of the husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; Matthew 5:32; Mark 10:6–12"> So then if, while the husband lives, she is joined to another man, she would be called an adulteress. But if the husband dies, she is free from the law, so that she is no adulteress, though she is joined to another man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Galatians 5:22–23; Isaiah 54:5"> Therefore, my brothers, you also were made dead to the law through the body of Christ, that you would be joined to another, to him who was raised from the dead, that we might produce fruit to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:3; Galatians 5:16–17; Galatians 5:24"> For when we were in the flesh, the sinful passions which were through the law worked in our members to bring out fruit to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:23–25; 2 Corinthians 3:6; Romans 7:4"> But now we have been discharged from the law, having died to that in which we were held; so that we serve in newness of the spirit, and not in oldness of the letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Romans 4:15; 1 Corinthians 15:56"> What shall we say then? Is the law sin? May it never be! However, I wouldn’t have known sin except through the law. For I wouldn’t have known coveting unless the law had said, “You shall not covet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 7:11; 1 Corinthians 15:56; Romans 4:15"> But sin, finding occasion through the commandment, produced in me all kinds of coveting. For apart from the law, sin is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Romans 8:7; James 2:10–11"> I was alive apart from the law once, but when the commandment came, sin revived and I died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:7; Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5"> The commandment which was for life, this I found to be for death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 3:13; Romans 7:8; Hebrews 3:13"> for sin, finding occasion through the commandment, deceived me, and through it killed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:8; Psalms 119:137; Psalms 19:7–12"> Therefore the law indeed is holy, and the commandment holy, righteous, and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 1:13–15; Galatians 3:21; Romans 8:3"> Did then that which is good become death to me? May it never be! But sin, that it might be shown to be sin, was producing death in me through that which is good; that through the commandment sin might become exceedingly sinful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 21:25; 2 Kings 17:17; Romans 7:18"> For we know that the law is spiritual, but I am fleshly, sold under sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; Romans 7:19–20; Ecclesiastes 7:20"> For I don’t understand what I am doing. For I don’t practise what I desire to do; but what I hate, that I do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 7:12; Romans 7:14; 1 Timothy 1:8"> But if what I don’t desire, that I do, I consent to the law that it is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 7:20; Philippians 3:8–9; Romans 7:18"> So now it is no more I that do it, but sin which dwells in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:6; Psalms 51:5; Galatians 5:17"> For I know that in me, that is, in my flesh, dwells no good thing. For desire is present with me, but I don’t find it doing that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 7:15"> For the good which I desire, I don’t do; but the evil which I don’t desire, that I practise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 7:17"> But if what I don’t desire, that I do, it is no more I that do it, but sin which dwells in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:14; Romans 7:23; Psalms 119:133"> I find then the law that, while I desire to do good, evil is present.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 1:2; Psalms 40:8; Psalms 119:35"> For I delight in God’s law after the inward person,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 5:17; James 4:1; Romans 8:2"> but I see a different law in my members, warring against the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity under the law of sin which is in my members.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 6:6; 2 Timothy 4:18; Titus 2:14"> What a wretched man I am! Who will deliver me out of the body of this death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:17–24; Colossians 3:17; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> I thank God through Jesus Christ, our Lord! So then with the mind, I myself serve God’s law, but with the flesh, sin’s law.</span>
