<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:6; Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 22:31"> “The LORD was very displeased with your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Malachi 3:7; Jeremiah 3:22; Jeremiah 4:1"> Therefore tell them, the LORD of Armies says: ‘Return to me,’ says the LORD of Armies, ‘and I will return to you,’ says the LORD of Armies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 78:8; Psalms 106:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> Don’t you be like your fathers, to whom the former prophets proclaimed, saying: The LORD of Armies says, ‘Return now from your evil ways and from your evil doings;’ but they didn’t hear nor listen to me, says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Job 14:10–12; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> Your fathers, where are they? And the prophets, do they live forever?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 2:17; Deuteronomy 28:15; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> But my words and my decrees, which I commanded my servants the prophets, didn’t they overtake your fathers?<br />“Then they repented and said, ‘Just as the LORD of Armies determined to do to us, according to our ways and according to our practices, so he has dealt with us.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 1:1"> On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, which is the month Shebat, in the second year of Darius, the LORD’s word came to the prophet Zechariah the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 6:4; Isaiah 55:13; Zechariah 13:7"> “I had a vision in the night, and behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 4:4–5; Zechariah 5:5; Zechariah 2:3"> Then I asked, ‘My lord, what are these?’”<br />The angel who talked with me said to me, “I will show you what these are.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 1:11; Zechariah 6:5–8; Hebrews 1:14"> The man who stood amongst the myrtle trees answered, “They are the ones the LORD has sent to go back and forth through the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 1:10; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 103:20–21"> They reported to the LORD’s angel who stood amongst the myrtle trees, and said, “We have walked back and forth through the earth, and behold, all the earth is at rest and in peace.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 7:5; Jeremiah 29:10; Psalms 102:13"> Then the LORD’s angel replied, “O LORD of Armies, how long will you not have mercy on Jerusalem and on the cities of Judah, against which you have had indignation these seventy years?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Isaiah 40:1–2; Jeremiah 30:10–22"> The LORD answered the angel who talked with me with kind and comforting words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joel 2:18; Isaiah 40:1; Zechariah 1:17"> So the angel who talked with me said to me, “Proclaim, saying, ‘The LORD of Armies says: “I am jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with a great jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:12–17; Jeremiah 51:24; Isaiah 54:8"> I am very angry with the nations that are at ease; for I was but a little displeased, but they added to the calamity.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Ezra 6:14–15; Zechariah 2:10–11"> Therefore the LORD says: “I have returned to Jerusalem with mercy. My house shall be built in it,” says the LORD of Armies, “and a line shall be stretched out over Jerusalem.”’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Zechariah 2:12; Zephaniah 3:15–17"> “Proclaim further, saying, ‘The LORD of Armies says: “My cities will again overflow with prosperity, and the LORD will again comfort Zion, and will again choose Jerusalem.”’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 5:13; 2 Kings 24:1–20; Zechariah 5:9"> I lifted up my eyes and saw, and behold, four horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 1:21; Zechariah 8:14; Zechariah 2:2"> I asked the angel who talked with me, “What are these?”<br />He answered me, “These are the horns which have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 10:3–5; Zechariah 12:2–6; Zechariah 9:12–16"> The LORD showed me four craftsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Zechariah 1:19; Psalms 75:4–5"> Then I asked, “What are these coming to do?”<br />He said, “These are the horns which scattered Judah, so that no man lifted up his head; but these have come to terrify them, to cast down the horns of the nations that lifted up their horn against the land of Judah to scatter it.”</span>
</div>
