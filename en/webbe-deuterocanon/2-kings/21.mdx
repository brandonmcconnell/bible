<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; 2 Chronicles 32:33–9; Matthew 1:10"> Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Hephzibah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:31; 2 Chronicles 36:14; 2 Chronicles 33:2–4"> He did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, after the abominations of the nations whom the LORD cast out before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:4; 2 Kings 17:16; Deuteronomy 17:3"> For he built again the high places which Hezekiah his father had destroyed; and he raised up altars for Baal, and made an Asherah, as Ahab king of Israel did, and worshipped all the army of the sky, and served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 8:29; 2 Samuel 7:13; Jeremiah 32:34"> He built altars in the LORD’s house, of which the LORD said, “I will put my name in Jerusalem.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 7:12; 2 Kings 23:4; 2 Kings 23:12"> He built altars for all the army of the sky in the two courts of the LORD’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 17:17; Leviticus 19:31; Leviticus 18:21"> He made his son to pass through the fire, practised sorcery, used enchantments, and dealt with those who had familiar spirits and with wizards. He did much evil in the LORD’s sight, to provoke him to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:16; 1 Kings 9:3; 2 Samuel 7:13"> He set the engraved image of Asherah that he had made in the house of which the LORD said to David and to Solomon his son, “In this house, and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, I will put my name forever;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:10; Deuteronomy 5:28–29; Psalms 81:11–16"> I will not cause the feet of Israel to wander any more out of the land which I gave their fathers, if only they will observe to do according to all that I have commanded them, and according to all the law that my servant Moses commanded them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 29:12; Nehemiah 9:26; 1 Kings 14:16"> But they didn’t listen, and Manasseh seduced them to do that which is evil more than the nations did whom the LORD destroyed before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 23:34–37; 2 Chronicles 33:10; 2 Chronicles 36:15"> The LORD spoke by his servants the prophets, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 24:3–4; 1 Kings 21:26; 2 Kings 21:9"> “Because Manasseh king of Judah has done these abominations, and has done wickedly above all that the Amorites did, who were before him, and has also made Judah to sin with his idols;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:3; 1 Samuel 3:11; Daniel 9:12"> therefore the LORD the God of Israel says, ‘Behold, I will bring such evil on Jerusalem and Judah that whoever hears of it, both his ears will tingle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 7:7–8; Lamentations 2:8; Isaiah 34:11"> I will stretch over Jerusalem the line of Samaria, and the plumb line of Ahab’s house; and I will wipe Jerusalem as a man wipes a dish, wiping it and turning it upside down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:33; 2 Kings 19:4; Psalms 89:38–45"> I will cast off the remnant of my inheritance and deliver them into the hands of their enemies. They will become a prey and a plunder to all their enemies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:30; Deuteronomy 31:27; Ezekiel 20:21"> because they have done that which is evil in my sight, and have provoked me to anger since the day their fathers came out of Egypt, even to this day.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; 2 Chronicles 33:9; 2 Kings 21:11"> Moreover Manasseh shed innocent blood very much, until he had filled Jerusalem from one end to another; in addition to his sin with which he made Judah to sin, in doing that which was evil in the LORD’s sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20–21; 2 Chronicles 33:1–20"> Now the rest of the acts of Manasseh, and all that he did, and his sin that he sinned, aren’t they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:20; 2 Kings 21:26; 2 Chronicles 32:33"> Manasseh slept with his fathers, and was buried in the garden of his own house, in the garden of Uzza; and Amon his son reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:21–23; 1 Kings 22:51; 1 Chronicles 3:14"> Amon was twenty-two years old when he began to reign; and he reigned two years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Meshullemeth the daughter of Haruz of Jotbah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 23:32; 2 Kings 21:11; Acts 7:51"> He did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, as Manasseh his father did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He walked in all the ways that his father walked in, and served the idols that his father served, and worshipped them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 22:17; 1 Kings 11:33; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> and he abandoned the LORD, the God of his fathers, and didn’t walk in the way of the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 12:20; 2 Kings 14:19; 2 Chronicles 33:24–25"> The servants of Amon conspired against him, and put the king to death in his own house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 14:5; 2 Kings 11:17; 2 Kings 14:21"> But the people of the land killed all those who had conspired against King Amon; and the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 20:20; 2 Kings 21:17"> Now the rest of the acts of Amon which he did, aren’t they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 21:18; Matthew 1:10; 1 Kings 13:5"> He was buried in his tomb in the garden of Uzza, and Josiah his son reigned in his place.</span>
