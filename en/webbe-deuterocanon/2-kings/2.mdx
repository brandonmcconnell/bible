<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 18:13; 2 Kings 2:17; 1 Kings 18:4"> Fifty men of the sons of the prophets went and stood opposite them at a distance; and they both stood by the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; Exodus 14:21–22; Joshua 3:14–17"> Elijah took his mantle, and rolled it up, and struck the waters; and they were divided here and there, so that they both went over on dry ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 11:17; Deuteronomy 21:17; John 16:7"> When they had gone over, Elijah said to Elisha, “Ask what I shall do for you, before I am taken from you.”<br />Elisha said, “Please let a double portion of your spirit be on me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 6:17; Psalms 104:3–4; Hebrews 1:14"> As they continued on and talked, behold, a chariot of fire and horses of fire separated them; and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven.</span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 19:19; 2 Kings 2:8; 1 Kings 9:26"> He also took up Elijah’s mantle that fell from him, and went back and stood by the bank of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:12; Psalms 42:10; Psalms 115:2"> He took Elijah’s mantle that fell from him, and struck the waters, and said, “Where is the LORD, the God of Elijah?” When he also had struck the waters, they were divided apart, and Elisha went over.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; Acts 8:39; 2 Corinthians 12:2–3"> They said to him, “See now, there are with your servants fifty strong men. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 8:11; Hebrews 11:5; Romans 10:2"> When they urged him until he was ashamed, he said, “Send them.”<br />Therefore they sent fifty men; and they searched for three days, but didn’t find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> They came back to him while he stayed at Jericho; and he said to them, “Didn’t I tell you, ‘Don’t go’?”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 9:45; Zephaniah 2:9; Ezekiel 47:11"> He said, “Bring me a new jar, and put salt in it.” Then they brought it to him.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So the waters were healed to this day, according to Elisha’s word which he spoke.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 13:9–11; Acts 5:5; Hosea 13:8"> He looked behind him and saw them, and cursed them in the LORD’s name. Then two female bears came out of the woods and mauled forty-two of those youths.</span>
