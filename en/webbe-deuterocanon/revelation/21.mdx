<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17–19; 2 Peter 3:13; Isaiah 66:22"> I saw a new heaven and a new earth, for the first heaven and the first earth have passed away, and the sea is no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Revelation 3:12; Revelation 21:10"> I saw the holy city, New Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared like a bride adorned for her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:27; 2 Corinthians 6:16; John 14:23"> I heard a loud voice out of heaven saying, “Behold, God’s dwelling is with people; and he will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and God himself will be with them as their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 25:8; Revelation 7:17; 1 Corinthians 15:26"> He will wipe away every tear from their eyes. Death will be no more; neither will there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain any more. The first things have passed away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:17; Isaiah 42:9; Isaiah 43:19"> He who sits on the throne said,<span className="woj">“Behold, I am making all things new.”</span>He said,<span className="woj">“Write, for these words of God are faithful and true.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 4:10; Revelation 7:17"> He said to me,<span className="woj">“I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End. I will give freely to him who is thirsty from the spring of the water of life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Hebrews 8:10; Revelation 2:11"> <span className="woj">He who overcomes, I will give him these things. I will be his God, and he will be my son.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–10; Revelation 2:11; Ephesians 5:5–6"> <span className="woj">But for the cowardly, unbelieving, sinners,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR and NU omit “sinners”">a</a></sup><span className="woj">abominable, murderers, sexually immoral, sorcerers,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “sorcerers” here also includes users of potions and drugs.">b</a></sup><span className="woj">idolaters, and all liars, their part is in the lake that burns with fire and sulphur, which is the second death.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Revelation 19:7; Revelation 16:1–17"> One of the seven angels who had the seven bowls which were loaded with the seven last plagues came, and he spoke with me, saying, “Come here. I will show you the bride, the Lamb’s wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 40:1–49"> He carried me away in the Spirit to a great and high mountain, and showed me the holy city, Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 4:6; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 22:1"> having the glory of God. Her light was like a most precious stone, like a jasper stone, clear as crystal;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34; Revelation 7:4–8; Revelation 21:17–21"> having a great and high wall with twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and names written on them, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:31–34"> On the east were three gates, and on the north three gates, and on the south three gates, and on the west three gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 11:10; Ephesians 2:20; Isaiah 54:11"> The wall of the city had twelve foundations, and on them twelve names of the twelve Apostles of the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1; Revelation 11:1–2; Ezekiel 41:1–5"> He who spoke with me had for a measure a golden reed to measure the city, its gates, and its walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:35; Ezekiel 48:8–20"> The city is square. Its length is as great as its width. He measured the city with the reed: twelve thousand and twelve stadia.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="12,012 stadia = 2,221 kilometres or 1,380 miles. TR reads 12,000 stadia instead of 12,012 stadia.">c</a></sup>Its length, width, and height are equal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Deuteronomy 3:11; Revelation 13:18"> Its wall is one hundred and forty-four cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="144 cubits is about 65.8 metres or 216 feet">d</a></sup>by the measure of a man, that is, of an angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 21:11; Revelation 21:21; Revelation 21:19"> The construction of its wall was jasper. The city was pure gold, like pure glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; Exodus 28:17–21; Job 28:16–19"> The foundations of the city’s wall were adorned with all kinds of precious stones. The first foundation was jasper, the second sapphire,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="or, lapis lazuli">e</a></sup>the third chalcedony, the fourth emerald,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 4:3"> the fifth sardonyx, the sixth sardius, the seventh chrysolite, the eighth beryl, the ninth topaz, the tenth chrysoprase, the eleventh jacinth, and the twelfth amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:18; Revelation 17:4; Revelation 22:2"> The twelve gates were twelve pearls. Each one of the gates was made of one pearl. The street of the city was pure gold, like transparent glass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 4:23; 1 Kings 8:27; 2 Chronicles 2:6"> I saw no temple in it, for the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb are its temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; Revelation 22:5; Revelation 21:11"> The city has no need for the sun or moon to shine, for the very glory of God illuminated it and its lamp is the Lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 21:26; Revelation 22:2; Zechariah 8:22–23"> The nations will walk in its light. The kings of the earth bring the glory and honour of the nations into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 60:11; Zechariah 14:7; Revelation 22:5"> Its gates will in no way be shut by day (for there will be no night there),</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 21:24"> and they shall bring the glory and the honour of the nations into it so that they may enter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Revelation 22:14–15; Isaiah 52:1; Revelation 20:15"> There will in no way enter into it anything profane, or one who causes an abomination or a lie, but only those who are written in the Lamb’s book of life.</span>
