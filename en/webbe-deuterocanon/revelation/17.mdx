<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 19:2; Revelation 17:1–17; Jeremiah 51:13"> One of the seven angels who had the seven bowls came and spoke with me, saying, “Come here. I will show you the judgement of the great prostitute who sits on many waters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Jeremiah 51:7; Revelation 18:3"> with whom the kings of the earth committed sexual immorality. Those who dwell on the earth were made drunken with the wine of her sexual immorality.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:3; Revelation 13:1–6; Revelation 12:6"> He carried me away in the Spirit into a wilderness. I saw a woman sitting on a scarlet-coloured beast, full of blasphemous names, having seven heads and ten horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 18:16; Jeremiah 51:7; Revelation 19:2"> The woman was dressed in purple and scarlet, and decked with gold and precious stones and pearls, having in her hand a golden cup full of abominations and the impurities of the sexual immorality of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 14:8; Revelation 18:2; Revelation 17:7"> And on her forehead a name was written, “MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF THE PROSTITUTES AND OF THE ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 16:6; Revelation 13:15; Revelation 2:13"> I saw the woman drunken with the blood of the saints and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus. When I saw her, I wondered with great amazement.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 17:1–6; Revelation 17:8"> The angel said to me, “Why do you wonder? I will tell you the mystery of the woman and of the beast that carries her, which has the seven heads and the ten horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 17:11; Revelation 11:7; Revelation 3:10"> The beast that you saw was, and is not; and is about to come up out of the abyss and to go into destruction. Those who dwell on the earth and whose names have not been written in the book of life from the foundation of the world will marvel when they see that the beast was, and is not, and shall be present.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR reads “yet is” instead of “shall be present”">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 13:18; Revelation 17:7; Matthew 13:11"> Here is the mind that has wisdom. The seven heads are seven mountains on which the woman sits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> They are seven kings. Five have fallen, the one is, and the other has not yet come. When he comes, he must continue a little while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 17:8"> The beast that was, and is not, is himself also an eighth, and is of the seven; and he goes to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 12:3; Revelation 13:1; Daniel 7:24"> The ten horns that you saw are ten kings who have received no kingdom as yet, but they receive authority as kings with the beast for one hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Ezekiel 38:10; Isaiah 10:5–7"> These have one mind, and they give their power and authority to the beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 8:30; 1 Timothy 6:15; Matthew 22:14"> These will war against the Lamb, and the Lamb will overcome them, for he is Lord of lords and King of kings; and those who are with him are called, chosen, and faithful.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 17:1; Jeremiah 51:13; Jeremiah 51:55"> He said to me, “The waters which you saw, where the prostitute sits, are peoples, multitudes, nations, and languages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 18:8; Ezekiel 16:37–44; Leviticus 21:9"> The ten horns which you saw, they and the beast will hate the prostitute, will make her desolate, will strip her naked, will eat her flesh, and will burn her utterly with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Proverbs 21:1; Revelation 10:7"> For God has put in their hearts to do what he has in mind, to be of one mind, and to give their kingdom to the beast, until the words of God should be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 16:19; Revelation 18:2; Daniel 7:23"> The woman whom you saw is the great city which reigns over the kings of the earth.”</span>
</div>
