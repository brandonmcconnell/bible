<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Revelation 11:2; Deuteronomy 19:15"> I will give power to my two witnesses, and they will prophesy one thousand and two hundred and sixty days, clothed in sackcloth.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 4:11–14; Zechariah 4:2–3; Jeremiah 11:16"> These are the two olive trees and the two lamp stands, standing before the Lord of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:14; 2 Kings 1:10–12; Hosea 6:5"> If anyone desires to harm them, fire proceeds out of their mouth and devours their enemies. If anyone desires to harm them, he must be killed in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Luke 4:25; Psalms 105:26–36"> These have the power to shut up the sky, that it may not rain during the days of their prophecy. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:7; Daniel 8:23–24; Daniel 7:25"> When they have finished their testimony, the beast that comes up out of the abyss will make war with them, and overcome them, and kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:14; Revelation 18:10; Revelation 18:21"> Their dead bodies will be in the street of the great city, which spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt, where also their Lord was crucified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 79:2–3; Revelation 17:15; Revelation 10:11"> From amongst the peoples, tribes, languages, and nations, people will look at their dead bodies for three and a half days, and will not allow their dead bodies to be laid in a tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 18:17; Revelation 3:10; Acts 5:33"> Those who dwell on the earth will rejoice over them, and they will be glad. They will give gifts to one another, because these two prophets tormented those who dwell on the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 2:7; Romans 8:11; Romans 8:2"> After the three and a half days, the breath of life from God entered into them, and they stood on their feet. Great fear fell on those who saw them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 4:1; 2 Kings 2:11; Acts 1:9"> I heard a loud voice from heaven saying to them, “Come up here!” They went up into heaven in a cloud, and their enemies saw them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 14:7; Revelation 6:12; Joshua 7:19"> In that day there was a great earthquake, and a tenth of the city fell. Seven thousand people were killed in the earthquake, and the rest were terrified and gave glory to the God of heaven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 8:13; Revelation 9:12; Revelation 15:1"> The second woe is past. Behold, the third woe comes quickly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:14; Revelation 12:10; Daniel 7:27"> The seventh angel sounded, and great voices in heaven followed, saying, “The kingdom of the world has become the Kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ. He will reign forever and ever!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 4:4; Revelation 4:10; Revelation 7:11"> The twenty-four elders, who sit on their thrones before God’s throne, fell on their faces and worshipped God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 1:8; Revelation 16:5; Revelation 4:8–9"> saying: “We give you thanks, Lord God, the Almighty, the one who is and who was,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR adds “and who is coming”">a</a></sup>because you have taken your great power and reigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 19:5; Revelation 20:12; Revelation 6:10–11"> The nations were angry, and your wrath came, as did the time for the dead to be judged, and to give your bondservants the prophets, their reward, as well as to the saints and those who fear your name, to the small and the great, and to destroy those who destroy the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 16:21; Revelation 15:5–8; Ezekiel 38:22"> God’s temple that is in heaven was opened, and the ark of the Lord’s covenant was seen in his temple. Lightnings, sounds, thunders, an earthquake, and great hail followed.</span>
</div>
