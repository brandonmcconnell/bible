<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 4:13; Luke 8:37; Matthew 8:18"> He entered into a boat and crossed over, and came into his own city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 2:1–12; Luke 5:18–26; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> Behold, they brought to him a man who was paralysed, lying on a bed. Jesus, seeing their faith, said to the paralytic,<span className="woj">“Son, cheer up! Your sins are forgiven you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 26:65; Luke 5:21; Leviticus 24:16"> Behold, some of the scribes said to themselves, “This man blasphemes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 6:8; Matthew 12:25; Revelation 2:23"> Jesus, knowing their thoughts, said,<span className="woj">“Why do you think evil in your hearts?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 5:8–14; Mark 2:9–12; Acts 3:16"> <span className="woj">For which is easier, to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven;’ or to say, ‘Get up, and walk’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Matthew 9:5; John 20:21–23"> <span className="woj">But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins—”</span>(then he said to the paralytic),<span className="woj">“Get up, and take up your mat, and go to your house.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He arose and departed to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Matthew 15:31; Acts 4:21"> But when the multitudes saw it, they marvelled and glorified God, who had given such authority to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 5:27–38; Mark 2:14–22; Luke 19:2–10"> As Jesus passed by from there, he saw a man called Matthew sitting at the tax collection office. He said to him,<span className="woj">“Follow me.”</span>He got up and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 5:29–32; Mark 2:15–17"> As he sat in the house, behold, many tax collectors and sinners came and sat down with Jesus and his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:19; Luke 5:30; Mark 2:16"> When the Pharisees saw it, they said to his disciples, “Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 5:31; Revelation 3:17–18; Mark 2:17"> When Jesus heard it, he said to them,<span className="woj">“Those who are healthy have no need for a physician, but those who are sick do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Luke 19:10; Hosea 6:6"> <span className="woj">But you go and learn what this means: ‘I desire mercy, and not sacrifice,’</span><span className="woj">for I came not to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.”</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NU omits “to repentance”.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:33–39; Mark 2:18–22; Matthew 6:16"> Then John’s disciples came to him, saying, “Why do we and the Pharisees fast often, but your disciples don’t fast?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 19:9; John 3:29; Revelation 21:2"> Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Can the friends of the bridegroom mourn as long as the bridegroom is with them? But the days will come when the bridegroom will be taken away from them, and then they will fast.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:1–2; John 16:12; Psalms 125:3"> <span className="woj">No one puts a piece of unshrunk cloth on an old garment; for the patch would tear away from the garment, and a worse hole is made.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 9:4; Job 32:19; Psalms 119:83"> <span className="woj">Neither do people put new wine into old wineskins, or else the skins would burst, and the wine be spilled, and the skins ruined. No, they put new wine into fresh wineskins, and both are preserved.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:41–56; Mark 5:22–43; Matthew 8:2"> While he told these things to them, behold, a ruler came and worshipped him, saying, “My daughter has just died, but come and lay your hand on her, and she will live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 10:38; Galatians 6:9–10; Matthew 8:7"> Jesus got up and followed him, as did his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 8:43–56; Matthew 14:36; Mark 6:56"> Behold, a woman who had a discharge of blood for twelve years came behind him, and touched the fringe<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, tassel">b</a></sup>of his garment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 5:26–33; Luke 8:45–47; Acts 19:12"> for she said within herself, “If I just touch his garment, I will be made well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 7:50; Matthew 15:28; Matthew 9:2"> But Jesus, turning around and seeing her, said,<span className="woj">“Daughter, cheer up! Your faith has made you well.”</span>And the woman was made well from that hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; Mark 5:35–40; Luke 8:49–51"> When Jesus came into the ruler’s house and saw the flute players and the crowd in noisy disorder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:10; John 11:11–13; Matthew 27:39–43"> he said to them,<span className="woj">“Make room, because the girl isn’t dead, but sleeping.”</span>They were ridiculing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 9:27; Mark 5:41; Luke 8:54"> But when the crowd was sent out, he entered in, took her by the hand, and the girl arose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 14:1–2; Mark 1:45"> The report of this went out into all that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Mark 10:46–48"> As Jesus passed by from there, two blind men followed him, calling out and saying, “Have mercy on us, son of David!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 9:23–24; Matthew 13:58; Matthew 8:2"> When he had come into the house, the blind men came to him. Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Do you believe that I am able to do this?”</span>They told him, “Yes, Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Mark 10:52; Matthew 8:13"> Then he touched their eyes, saying,<span className="woj">“According to your faith be it done to you.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Luke 5:14; Matthew 17:9"> Then their eyes were opened. Jesus strictly commanded them, saying,<span className="woj">“See that no one knows about this.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:36; Matthew 9:26; Mark 1:44–45"> But they went out and spread abroad his fame in all that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Matthew 12:22–24; Luke 11:14"> As they went out, behold, a mute man who was demon possessed was brought to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 35:6; Mark 7:32–37; Exodus 4:11–12"> When the demon was cast out, the mute man spoke. The multitudes marvelled, saying, “Nothing like this has ever been seen in Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 11:15; Mark 3:22; Matthew 12:23–24"> But the Pharisees said, “By the prince of the demons, he casts out demons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 4:23–24; Acts 10:38; Mark 6:56"> Jesus went about all the cities and the villages, teaching in their synagogues and preaching the Good News of the Kingdom, and healing every disease and every sickness amongst the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 6:34; Numbers 27:17; Jeremiah 50:6"> But when he saw the multitudes, he was moved with compassion for them because they were harassed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="TR reads “weary” instead of “harassed”">c</a></sup>and scattered, like sheep without a shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 10:2; Mark 16:15; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> Then he said to his disciples,<span className="woj">“The harvest indeed is plentiful, but the labourers are few.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 10:1–2; Jeremiah 3:15; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> <span className="woj">Pray therefore that the Lord of the harvest will send out labourers into his harvest.”</span></span>
