<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1–4; Isaiah 42:9; Matthew 13:35"> that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying,</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Galatians 5:15; Jeremiah 17:10; Matthew 9:4"> Knowing their thoughts, Jesus said to them,<span className="woj">“Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation, and every city or house divided against itself will not stand.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Mark 9:40; Luke 11:23; Joshua 24:15"> <span className="woj">“He who is not with me is against me, and he who doesn’t gather with me, scatters.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 3:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Hebrews 6:4–6"> <span className="woj">Whoever speaks a word against the Son of Man, it will be forgiven him; but whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit, it will not be forgiven him, either in this age, or in that which is to come.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 6:43–44; Matthew 7:16–20; James 4:8"> <span className="woj">“Either make the tree good and its fruit good, or make the tree corrupt and its fruit corrupt; for the tree is known by its fruit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Matthew 15:18; Ephesians 4:29"> <span className="woj">You offspring of vipers, how can you, being evil, speak good things? For out of the abundance of the heart, the mouth speaks.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:22; Mark 8:11–12; Luke 11:16"> Then certain of the scribes and Pharisees answered, “Teacher, we want to see a sign from you.”</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 11:24–26; Luke 8:28–32; Matthew 8:29"> <span className="woj">“When an unclean spirit has gone out of a man, he passes through waterless places seeking rest, and doesn’t find it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 John 4:4; Revelation 13:8–9; Jude 1:4–5"> <span className="woj">Then he says, ‘I will return into my house from which I came;’ and when he has come back, he finds it empty, swept, and put in order.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Luke 11:26; Mark 5:9; Hebrews 6:4–8"> <span className="woj">Then he goes and takes with himself seven other spirits more evil than he is, and they enter in and dwell there. The last state of that man becomes worse than the first. Even so will it be also to this evil generation.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; John 2:12; John 7:3"> While he was yet speaking to the multitudes, behold, his mother and his brothers stood outside, seeking to speak to him.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 3:32–33; Matthew 10:37; Luke 2:52"> But he answered him who spoke to him,<span className="woj">“Who is my mother? Who are my brothers?”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Luke 8:21; Mark 3:35; John 15:14"> <span className="woj">For whoever does the will of my Father who is in heaven, he is my brother, and sister, and mother.”</span></span>
