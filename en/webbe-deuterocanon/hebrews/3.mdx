<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Philippians 3:14; Hebrews 4:14–15"> Therefore, holy brothers, partakers of a heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and High Priest of our confession: Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; 1 Timothy 3:15; Ephesians 2:22"> who was faithful to him who appointed him, as also Moses was in all his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:9; 1 Peter 2:5–7; Zechariah 6:12–13"> For he has been counted worthy of more glory than Moses, because he who built the house has more honour than the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 1:2; Esther 2:10"> For every house is built by someone; but he who built all things is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 12:7; Hebrews 3:2; Exodus 14:31"> Moses indeed was faithful in all his house as a servant, for a testimony of those things which were afterward to be spoken,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Hebrews 10:35"> but Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Christ” means “Anointed One”.">a</a></sup>is faithful as a Son over his house. We are his house, if we hold fast our confidence and the glorying of our hope firm to the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 95:7–11; Hebrews 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> Therefore, even as the Holy Spirit says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today if you will hear his voice,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 28:14; Exodus 17:7; Zechariah 7:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-2">don’t harden your hearts as in the rebellion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the day of the trial in the wilderness,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Deuteronomy 4:9; Exodus 19:4"> <span className="pl pl-2">where your fathers tested me and tried me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and saw my deeds for forty years.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 95:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 67:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore I was displeased with that generation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and said, ‘They always err in their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they didn’t know my ways.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:34–35; Hebrews 4:3; Numbers 32:10–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">As I swore in my wrath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘They will not enter into my rest.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:5; Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 17:9"> Beware, brothers, lest perhaps there might be in any one of you an evil heart of unbelief, in falling away from the living God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Hebrews 10:24–25; 2 Timothy 4:2"> but exhort one another day by day, so long as it is called “today”, lest any one of you be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 3:6; Hebrews 6:11; Hebrews 12:10"> For we have become partakers of Christ, if we hold the beginning of our confidence firm to the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 3:7–8; Psalms 95:7; Hebrews 10:38"> while it is said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Today if you will hear his voice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">don’t harden your hearts, as in the rebellion.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:2; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 26:65"> For who, when they heard, rebelled? Wasn’t it all those who came out of Egypt led by Moses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:29; Numbers 26:64–65; Numbers 14:32–33"> With whom was he displeased forty years? Wasn’t it with those who sinned, whose bodies fell in the wilderness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 14:30; Hebrews 4:6; Deuteronomy 1:34–35"> To whom did he swear that they wouldn’t enter into his rest, but to those who were disobedient?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jude 1:5; John 3:36; Mark 16:16"> We see that they weren’t able to enter in because of unbelief.</span>
