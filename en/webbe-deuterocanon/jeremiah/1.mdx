<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 11:21; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> The words of Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, one of the priests who were in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 13:20; Jonah 1:1; Jeremiah 1:11"> The LORD’s<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name (Hebrew “יהוה”, usually pronounced Yahweh).">a</a></sup>word came to him in the days of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2; 2 Chronicles 36:5–8; 2 Kings 23:34"> It came also in the days of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, to the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah, the son of Josiah, king of Judah, to the carrying away of Jerusalem captive in the fifth month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:16; Ezekiel 1:3; Jeremiah 1:2"> Now the LORD’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:16; Isaiah 49:1; Galatians 1:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Before I formed you in the womb, I knew you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Before you were born, I sanctified you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have appointed you a prophet to the nations.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10–16; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> Then I said, “Ah, Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word translated “Lord” is “Adonai.”">b</a></sup>GOD! Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">c</a></sup>I don’t know how to speak; for I am a child.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Ezekiel 3:27; Numbers 22:20"> But the LORD said to me, “Don’t say, ‘I am a child;’ for you must go to whomever I send you, and you must say whatever I command you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 51:7"> Don’t be afraid because of them, for I am with you to rescue you,” says the LORD.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Exodus 4:11–12; Luke 12:12"> Then the LORD stretched out his hand and touched my mouth. Then the LORD said to me, “Behold, I have put my words in your mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Isaiah 44:26–28"> Behold, I have today set you over the nations and over the kingdoms, to uproot and to tear down, to destroy and to overthrow, to build and to plant.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:2"> Moreover the LORD’s word came to me, saying, “Jeremiah, what do you see?”<br />I said, “I see a branch of an almond tree.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 12:25; Ezekiel 12:22–23"> Then the LORD said to me, “You have seen well; for I watch over my word to perform it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7; Zechariah 4:2"> The LORD’s word came to me the second time, saying, “What do you see?”<br />I said, “I see a boiling cauldron; and it is tipping away from the north.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 41:25; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 10:22"> Then the LORD said to me, “Out of the north, evil will break out on all the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 9:11"> For behold, I will call all the families of the kingdoms of the north,” says the LORD.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“They will come, and they will each set his throne at the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and against all its walls all around, and against all the cities of Judah.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:9; Jeremiah 19:4; Acts 7:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will utter my judgements against them concerning all their wickedness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in that they have forsaken me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have burnt incense to other gods,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and worshipped the works of their own hands.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:7–8; 1 Kings 18:46; 1 Peter 1:13"> “You therefore put your belt on your waist, arise, and say to them all that I command you. Don’t be dismayed at them, lest I dismay you before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7; Ezekiel 3:8–9; Jeremiah 15:20"> For behold, I have made you today a fortified city, an iron pillar, and bronze walls against the whole land—against the kings of Judah, against its princes, against its priests, and against the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 20:11"> They will fight against you, but they will not prevail against you; for I am with you”, says the LORD, “to rescue you.”</span>
</div>
