<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 23:34; Jeremiah 22:18"> But he will die in the place where they have led him captive. He will see this land no more.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; 1 Kings 21:23–24; 2 Chronicles 36:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will be buried with the burial of a donkey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">drawn and cast out beyond the gates of Jerusalem.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:30; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 7:22–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I spoke to you in your prosperity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you said, ‘I will not listen.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This has been your way from your youth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that you didn’t obey my voice.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20–21; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 24:15–16"> I would give you into the hand of those who seek your life, and into the hand of them of whom you are afraid, even into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of the Chaldeans.</span>
