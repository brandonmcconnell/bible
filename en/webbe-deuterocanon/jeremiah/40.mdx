<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:24–28; Jeremiah 22:8–9; Lamentations 2:15–17"> The captain of the guard took Jeremiah and said to him, “The LORD your God pronounced this evil on this place;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 2:5; Jeremiah 50:7; Daniel 9:11–12"> and the LORD has brought it, and done according as he spoke. Because you have sinned against the LORD, and have not obeyed his voice, therefore this thing has come on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:11–12; Genesis 20:15; Genesis 13:9"> Now, behold, I release you today from the chains which are on your hand. If it seems good to you to come with me into Babylon, come, and I will take care of you; but if it seems bad to you to come with me into Babylon, don’t. Behold, all the land is before you. Where it seems good and right to you to go, go there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:14; 2 Kings 8:7–9; Jeremiah 41:2"> Now while he had not yet gone back, “Go back then,” he said, “to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan, whom the king of Babylon has made governor over the cities of Judah, and dwell with him amongst the people; or go wherever it seems right to you to go.”<br />So the captain of the guard gave him food and a present, and let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:14; Judges 20:1; Judges 21:1"> Then Jeremiah went to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam to Mizpah, and lived with him amongst the people who were left in the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:10; 2 Kings 25:4; Jeremiah 39:4"> Now when all the captains of the forces who were in the fields, even they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had made Gedaliah the son of Ahikam governor in the land, and had committed to him men, women, children, and of the poorest of the land, of those who were not carried away captive to Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Deuteronomy 3:14; Jeremiah 42:8"> then Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and Johanan and Jonathan the sons of Kareah, and Seraiah the son of Tanhumeth, and the sons of Ephai the Netophathite, and Jezaniah the son of the Maacathite, they and their men came to Gedaliah to Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:11; 2 Kings 25:24; 1 Samuel 20:16–17"> Gedaliah the son of Ahikam the son of Shaphan swore to them and to their men, saying, “Don’t be afraid to serve the Chaldeans. Dwell in the land, and serve the king of Babylon, and it will be well with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:12; Deuteronomy 1:38; Isaiah 16:9"> As for me, behold, I will dwell at Mizpah, to stand before the Chaldeans who will come to us; but you, gather wine and summer fruits and oil, and put them in your vessels, and dwell in your cities that you have taken.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 16:4; Genesis 36:8; Jeremiah 24:9"> Likewise when all the Jews who were in Moab, and amongst the children of Ammon, and in Edom, and who were in all the countries, heard that the king of Babylon had left a remnant of Judah, and that he had set over them Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:5"> then all the Jews returned out of all places where they were driven, and came to the land of Judah, to Gedaliah, to Mizpah, and gathered very much wine and summer fruits.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:1; Jeremiah 40:8"> Moreover Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces who were in the fields, came to Gedaliah to Mizpah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 41:10; 2 Samuel 10:1–6; Jeremiah 25:21"> and said to him, “Do you know that Baalis the king of the children of Ammon has sent Ishmael the son of Nethaniah to take your life?”<br />But Gedaliah the son of Ahikam didn’t believe them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:8; 2 Samuel 21:17; Jeremiah 42:2"> Then Johanan the son of Kareah spoke to Gedaliah in Mizpah secretly, saying, “Please let me go, and I will kill Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and no man will know it. Why should he take your life, that all the Jews who are gathered to you should be scattered, and the remnant of Judah perish?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 10:16–17; Romans 3:8; Jeremiah 41:2"> But Gedaliah the son of Ahikam said to Johanan the son of Kareah, “You shall not do this thing, for you speak falsely of Ishmael.”</span>
</div>
