<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 1:1; Ephesians 1:1; 2 Corinthians 1:1"> Paul, called to be an apostle of Jesus Christ<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Christ” means “Anointed One”.">a</a></sup>through the will of God, and our brother Sosthenes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; 1 Corinthians 1:30"> to the assembly of God which is at Corinth—those who are sanctified in Christ Jesus, called saints, with all who call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ in every place, both theirs and ours:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; 2 Corinthians 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; John 14:26; 1 Timothy 1:14"> I always thank my God concerning you for the grace of God which was given you in Christ Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:11; 2 Corinthians 8:7; Colossians 2:3"> that in everything you were enriched in him, in all speech and all knowledge—</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 1:2; 2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Timothy 2:6"> even as the testimony of Christ was confirmed in you—</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Philippians 3:20; 2 Peter 3:12; Hebrews 9:28"> so that you come behind in no gift, waiting for the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23–24; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Colossians 1:22"> who will also confirm you until the end, blameless in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; Hebrews 10:23; Deuteronomy 7:9"> God is faithful, through whom you were called into the fellowship of his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:1–4; Ephesians 4:1–7; 1 Corinthians 12:25"> Now I beg you, brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">b</a></sup>through the name of our Lord, Jesus Christ, that you all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions amongst you, but that you be perfected together in the same mind and in the same judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 18:6; 1 Timothy 6:4; 2 Timothy 2:23–25"> For it has been reported to me concerning you, my brothers, by those who are from Chloe’s household, that there are contentions amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:21–23; John 1:42; Matthew 23:9–10"> Now I mean this, that each one of you says, “I follow Paul,” “I follow Apollos,” “I follow Cephas,” and, “I follow Christ.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; 2 Corinthians 11:4; Acts 2:38"> Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you baptised into the name of Paul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 18:8; Romans 16:23; 1 Corinthians 1:4"> I thank God that I baptised none of you except Crispus and Gaius,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:2; John 3:28–29; John 7:18"> so that no one should say that I had baptised you into my own name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:15; 1 Corinthians 16:17; Acts 16:33"> (I also baptised the household of Stephanas; besides them, I don’t know whether I baptised any other.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:1; 1 Corinthians 2:13; 2 Peter 1:16"> For Christ sent me not to baptise, but to preach the Good News—not in wisdom of words, so that the cross of Christ wouldn’t be made void.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Romans 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 2:10"> For the word of the cross is foolishness to those who are dying, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 29:14; Jeremiah 8:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will destroy the wisdom of the wise.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will bring the discernment of the discerning to nothing.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 1:22; Job 12:17; 1 Corinthians 3:19"> Where is the wise? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Hasn’t God made foolish the wisdom of this world?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 10:21; Romans 1:20–22; Matthew 11:25"> For seeing that in the wisdom of God, the world through its wisdom didn’t know God, it was God’s good pleasure through the foolishness of the preaching to save those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 16:1–4; Matthew 12:38–39; Mark 8:11"> For Jews ask for signs, Greeks seek after wisdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Corinthians 2:2; 1 Peter 2:8"> but we preach Christ crucified, a stumbling block to Jews and foolishness to Greeks,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:30; Colossians 2:3; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is the power of God and the wisdom of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 11:33–36; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Zechariah 4:6–7"> because the foolishness of God is wiser than men, and the weakness of God is stronger than men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 2:5; 1 Corinthians 3:18–20; James 1:9–11"> For you see your calling, brothers, that not many are wise according to the flesh, not many mighty, and not many noble;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 2:5; Matthew 11:25; 1 Corinthians 1:20"> but God chose the foolish things of the world that he might put to shame those who are wise. God chose the weak things of the world that he might put to shame the things that are strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:24; Romans 4:17; Isaiah 2:11"> God chose the lowly things of the world, and the things that are despised, and the things that don’t exist, that he might bring to nothing the things that exist,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Romans 3:27; Jeremiah 9:23; Ephesians 2:9"> that no flesh should boast before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Philippians 3:9; Romans 8:1"> Because of him, you are in Christ Jesus, who was made to us wisdom from God, and righteousness and sanctification, and redemption,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; 2 Corinthians 10:17; Philippians 3:3"> that, as it is written, “He who boasts, let him boast in the Lord.”</span>
</div>
</div>
