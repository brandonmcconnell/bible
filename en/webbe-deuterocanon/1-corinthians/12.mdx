<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 14:37; 1 Corinthians 14:1–18"> Now concerning spiritual things, brothers, I don’t want you to be ignorant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 4:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:9; Psalms 115:5"> You know that when you were heathen,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or Gentiles">a</a></sup>you were led away to those mute idols, however you might be led.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 John 4:2–3; Matthew 16:16–17; Romans 10:9"> Therefore I make known to you that no man speaking by God’s Spirit says, “Jesus is accursed.” No one can say, “Jesus is Lord,” but by the Holy Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:4–6"> Now there are various kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–12; Romans 12:6–8; Romans 14:8–9"> There are various kinds of service, and the same Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:7; 1 Corinthians 12:11; John 5:17"> There are various kinds of workings, but the same God who works all things in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> But to each one is given the manifestation of the Spirit for the profit of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 2:6; 1 Corinthians 2:6–10; Psalms 143:10"> For to one is given through the Spirit the word of wisdom, and to another the word of knowledge according to the same Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 5:14–15; 2 Corinthians 4:13; Ephesians 2:8"> to another faith by the same Spirit, and to another gifts of healings by the same Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:39; 1 John 4:1; Mark 16:17"> and to another workings of miracles, and to another prophecy, and to another discerning of spirits, to another different kinds of languages, and to another the interpretation of languages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4; Hebrews 2:4; 2 Corinthians 10:13"> But the one and the same Spirit produces all of these, distributing to each one separately as he desires.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 12:4–5; Colossians 3:15; 1 Corinthians 12:27"> For as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, being many, are one body; so also is Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 4:5; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> For in one Spirit we were all baptised into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether bond or free; and were all given to drink into one Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27–28; 1 Corinthians 12:12; Ephesians 4:25"> For the body is not one member, but many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; 2 Kings 14:9"> If the foot would say, “Because I’m not the hand, I’m not part of the body,” it is not therefore not part of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:22; Philippians 2:3"> If the ear would say, “Because I’m not the eye, I’m not part of the body,” it’s not therefore not part of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:21; Psalms 139:13–16; Proverbs 20:12"> If the whole body were an eye, where would the hearing be? If the whole were hearing, where would the smelling be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:28; Romans 12:3; 1 Corinthians 12:11"> But now God has set the members, each one of them, in the body, just as he desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> If they were all one member, where would the body be?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:14; 1 Corinthians 12:12"> But now they are many members, but one body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 10:1–5; Nehemiah 4:16–21; Job 29:11"> The eye can’t tell the hand, “I have no need for you,” or again the head to the feet, “I have no need for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 14:28; Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Ecclesiastes 4:9–12"> No, much rather, those members of the body which seem to be weaker are necessary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 3:21; Genesis 3:7"> Those parts of the body which we think to be less honourable, on those we bestow more abundant honour; and our unpresentable parts have more abundant modesty,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 3:11; Genesis 2:25"> while our presentable parts have no such need. But God composed the body together, giving more abundant honour to the inferior part,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:11; 1 Corinthians 1:10–12; John 17:21–26"> that there should be no division in the body, but that the members should have the same care for one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Galatians 6:2; Hebrews 13:3"> When one member suffers, all the members suffer with it. When one member is honoured, all the members rejoice with it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ephesians 4:12; Colossians 1:24; Ephesians 5:30"> Now you are the body of Christ, and members individually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11–13; Romans 12:6–8; 1 Corinthians 12:18"> God has set some in the assembly: first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then miracle workers, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, and various kinds of languages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:4–11; 1 Corinthians 12:14–20"> Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Are all miracle workers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:10"> Do all have gifts of healings? Do all speak with various languages? Do all interpret?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:1; 1 Corinthians 14:39; Matthew 5:6"> But earnestly desire the best gifts. Moreover, I show a most excellent way to you.</span>
</div>
</div>
