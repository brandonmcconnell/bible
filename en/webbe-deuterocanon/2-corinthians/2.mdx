<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:8; 2 Corinthians 1:14; 1 Corinthians 12:26"> For if I make you grieve, then who will make me glad but he who is made to grieve by me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 5:10; 2 Corinthians 8:22; Philemon 1:21"> And I wrote this very thing to you, so that when I came, I wouldn’t have sorrow from them of whom I ought to rejoice; having confidence in you all that my joy would be shared by all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:12; Proverbs 27:5–6; Leviticus 19:17–18"> For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote to you with many tears, not that you should be made to grieve, but that you might know the love that I have so abundantly for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:12–13; Galatians 4:12; Proverbs 17:25"> But if any has caused sorrow, he has caused sorrow not to me, but in part (that I not press too heavily) to you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4–5; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Corinthians 7:11"> This punishment which was inflicted by the many is sufficient for such a one;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Galatians 6:1–2; Colossians 3:13"> so that, on the contrary, you should rather forgive him and comfort him, lest by any means such a one should be swallowed up with his excessive sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:22–23; Galatians 6:1–2; Galatians 6:10"> Therefore I beg you to confirm your love towards him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:6; Philemon 1:21; Philippians 2:22"> For to this end I also wrote, that I might know the proof of you, whether you are obedient in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:23; Matthew 18:18; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> Now I also forgive whomever you forgive anything. For if indeed I have forgiven anything, I have forgiven that one for your sakes in the presence of Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> that no advantage may be gained over us by Satan, for we are not ignorant of his schemes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 16:8; Acts 14:27; Colossians 4:3"> Now when I came to Troas for the Good News of Christ, and when a door was opened to me in the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:5–6; 2 Corinthians 12:18; Titus 1:4"> I had no relief for my spirit, because I didn’t find Titus my brother, but taking my leave of them, I went out into Macedonia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Romans 8:37; 2 Corinthians 2:15–16"> Now thanks be to God who always leads us in triumph in Christ, and reveals through us the sweet aroma of his knowledge in every place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Ezekiel 20:41"> For we are a sweet aroma of Christ to God in those who are being saved and in those who perish:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5–6; John 9:39; 1 Peter 2:7–8"> to the one a stench from death to death, to the other a sweet aroma from life to life. Who is sufficient for these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; 2 Peter 2:1–3"> For we are not as so many, peddling the word of God. But as of sincerity, but as of God, in the sight of God, we speak in Christ.</span>
