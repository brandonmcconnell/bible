<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6; Isaiah 12:2; 2 Kings 19:35"> But I will have mercy on the house of Judah, and will save them by the LORD their God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The Hebrew word rendered “God” is “אֱלֹהִ֑ים” (Elohim).">c</a></sup>and will not save them by bow, sword, battle, horses, or horsemen.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Now when she had weaned Lo-Ruhamah, she conceived, and bore a son.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:1"> He said, “Call his name Lo-Ammi,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lo-Ammi means “not my people”.">d</a></sup>for you are not my people, and I will not be yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:18; Galatians 4:6–7; John 1:12"> Yet the number of the children of Israel will be as the sand of the sea, which can’t be measured or counted; and it will come to pass that, in the place where it was said to them, ‘You are not my people,’ they will be called ‘sons of the living God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 11:12–13; Jeremiah 50:4–5"> The children of Judah and the children of Israel will be gathered together, and they will appoint themselves one head, and will go up from the land; for great will be the day of Jezreel.</span>
