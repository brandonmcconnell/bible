<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 11:1; Judges 4:16; Judges 4:13"> The LORD sold them into the hand of Jabin king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor; the captain of whose army was Sisera, who lived in Harosheth of the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 1:19; Psalms 106:42; Judges 3:9"> The children of Israel cried to the LORD, for he had nine hundred chariots of iron; and he mightily oppressed the children of Israel for twenty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Luke 2:36; 2 Kings 22:14"> Now Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lappidoth, judged Israel at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 18:19; Jeremiah 31:15; Exodus 18:26"> She lived under Deborah’s palm tree between Ramah and Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim; and the children of Israel came up to her for judgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 13:47; Judges 8:18; Hebrews 11:32"> She sent and called Barak the son of Abinoam out of Kedesh Naphtali, and said to him, “Hasn’t the LORD, the God of Israel, commanded, ‘Go and lead the way to Mount Tabor, and take with you ten thousand men of the children of Naphtali and of the children of Zebulun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 18:40; Ezekiel 38:10–16; Psalms 83:9–10"> I will draw to you, to the river Kishon, Sisera, the captain of Jabin’s army, with his chariots and his multitude; and I will deliver him into your hand.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–14; Matthew 14:30–31"> Barak said to her, “If you will go with me, then I will go; but if you will not go with me, I will not go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 2:14; 1 Samuel 2:30; Judges 4:17–22"> She said, “I will surely go with you. Nevertheless, the journey that you take won’t be for your honour; for the LORD will sell Sisera into a woman’s hand.” Deborah arose, and went with Barak to Kedesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 5:18; Judges 5:15; Judges 4:14"> Barak called Zebulun and Naphtali together to Kedesh. Ten thousand men followed him; and Deborah went up with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 1:16; Joshua 19:33; Numbers 10:29"> Now Heber the Kenite had separated himself from the Kenites, even from the children of Hobab, Moses’ brother-in-law, and had pitched his tent as far as the oak in Zaanannim, which is by Kedesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 89:12; Jeremiah 46:18; Joshua 19:12"> They told Sisera that Barak the son of Abinoam had gone up to Mount Tabor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 4:2–3; Judges 4:7"> Sisera gathered together all his chariots, even nine hundred chariots of iron, and all the people who were with him, from Harosheth of the Gentiles, to the river Kishon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; 2 Samuel 5:24; Isaiah 52:12"> Deborah said to Barak, “Go; for this is the day in which the LORD has delivered Sisera into your hand. Hasn’t the LORD gone out before you?” So Barak went down from Mount Tabor, and ten thousand men after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 10:10; Psalms 83:9–10; Judges 5:20–21"> The LORD confused Sisera, all his chariots, and all his army, with the edge of the sword before Barak. Sisera abandoned his chariot and fled away on his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 2:12; Leviticus 26:7–8; Jacob 2:13"> But Barak pursued the chariots and the army to Harosheth of the Gentiles; and all the army of Sisera fell by the edge of the sword. There was not a man left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:22; Isaiah 57:21; Amos 5:19–20"> However Sisera fled away on his feet to the tent of Jael the wife of Heber the Kenite; for there was peace between Jabin the king of Hazor and the house of Heber the Kenite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 6:19"> Jael went out to meet Sisera, and said to him, “Turn in, my lord, turn in to me; don’t be afraid.” He came in to her into the tent, and she covered him with a rug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Judges 5:25–26; Genesis 24:43; Isaiah 41:17"> He said to her, “Please give me a little water to drink; for I am thirsty.” She opened a container of milk, and gave him a drink, and covered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 2:3–5; 2 Samuel 17:20"> He said to her, “Stand in the door of the tent, and if any man comes and enquires of you, and says, ‘Is there any man here?’ you shall say, ‘No.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 5:26–27; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Judges 3:21"> Then Jael, Heber’s wife, took a tent peg, and took a hammer in her hand, and went softly to him, and struck the pin into his temples, and it pierced through into the ground, for he was in a deep sleep; so he fainted and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:10–15; 2 Samuel 17:3"> Behold, as Barak pursued Sisera, Jael came out to meet him, and said to him, “Come, and I will show you the man whom you seek.” He came to her; and behold, Sisera lay dead, and the tent peg was in his temples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:24; Psalms 18:47; Psalms 18:39"> So God subdued Jabin the king of Canaan before the children of Israel on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:12"> The hand of the children of Israel prevailed more and more against Jabin the king of Canaan, until they had destroyed Jabin king of Canaan.</span>
