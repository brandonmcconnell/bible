<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 2 Peter 1:12–15; 1 Peter 1:22"> This is now, beloved, the second letter that I have written to you; and in both of them I stir up your sincere mind by reminding you</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judah 1:17; 2 Peter 2:21; Luke 1:70"> that you should remember the words which were spoken before by the holy prophets and the commandment of us, the emissaries of the Lord and Saviour,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judah 1:18; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; 2 Peter 2:10"> knowing this first, that in the last days mockers will come, walking after their own lusts</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:15; Malachi 2:17; Jeremiah 5:12–13"> and saying, “Where is the promise of his coming? For, from the day that the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:3; Genesis 1:9; Psalms 24:2"> For they wilfully forget that there were heavens from of old, and an earth formed out of water and amid water by the word of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:38–39; 2 Peter 2:5; Luke 17:27"> by which means the world that existed then, being overflowed with water, perished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 51:6; 2 Peter 3:12"> But the heavens that exist now and the earth, by the same word have been stored up for fire, being reserved against the day of judgement and destruction of ungodly men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:4; Romans 11:25; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> But don’t forget this one thing, beloved, that one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Romans 2:4; Habakkuk 2:3"> The Lord is not slow concerning his promise, as some count slowness; but he is patient with us, not wishing that anyone should perish, but that all should come to repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:7"> But the day of the Lord will come as a thief in the night, in which the heavens will pass away with a great noise, and the elements will be dissolved with fervent heat; and the earth and the works that are in it will be burnt up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27; 1 Peter 2:12"> Therefore, since all these things will be destroyed like this, what kind of people ought you to be in holy living and godliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 34:4; Micah 1:4"> looking for and earnestly desiring the coming of the day of God, which will cause the burning heavens to be dissolved, and the elements will melt with fervent heat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Revelation 21:1; Isaiah 66:22"> But, according to his promise, we look for new heavens and a new earth, in which righteousness dwells.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Philippians 1:10"> Therefore, beloved, seeing that you look for these things, be diligent to be found in peace, without defect and blameless in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9; 1 Timothy 1:16"> Regard the patience of our Lord as salvation; even as our beloved brother Paul also, according to the wisdom given to him, wrote to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:6; Hebrews 5:11; Jacob 1:8"> as also in all of his letters, speaking in them of these things. In those, there are some things that are hard to understand, which the ignorant and unsettled twist, as they also do to the other Scriptures, to their own destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Colossians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> You therefore, beloved, knowing these things beforehand, beware, lest being carried away with the error of the wicked, you fall from your own steadfastness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Ephesians 4:15; 1 Peter 2:2"> But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Yeshua the Messiah. To him be the glory both now and forever. Amen.</span>
