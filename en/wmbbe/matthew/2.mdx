<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Yochanan 7:42; Luke 2:4–7"> Now when Yeshua was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of King Herod, behold, wise men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The word for “wise men” (magoi) can also mean teachers, scientists, physicians, astrologers, seers, interpreters of dreams, or sorcerers.">a</a></sup>from the east came to Jerusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremiah 23:5; Revelation 22:16"> “Where is he who is born King of the Jews? For we saw his star in the east, and have come to worship him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; Yochanan 11:47–48; Matthew 23:37"> When King Herod heard it, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Matthew 7:29; Acts 6:12"> Gathering together all the chief priests and scribes of the people, he asked them where the Messiah would be born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Yochanan 7:42; Ruth 1:1; 1 Samuel 16:1"> They said to him, “In Bethlehem of Judea, for this is written through the prophet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:23–25; Micah 5:2; Isaiah 9:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘You Bethlehem, land of Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">are in no way least amongst the princes of Judah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for out of you shall come a governor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who shall shepherd my people, Israel.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:21; Psalms 55:21"> Then Herod secretly called the wise men, and learnt from them exactly what time the star appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:19–20; Jeremiah 41:5–7; Luke 20:20–21"> He sent them to Bethlehem, and said, “Go and search diligently for the young child. When you have found him, bring me word, so that I also may come and worship him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 2:2; Proverbs 8:17; Psalms 25:12"> They, having heard the king, went their way; and behold, the star, which they saw in the east, went before them until it came and stood over where the young child was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 105:3; Luke 2:20; Luke 2:10"> When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 72:10; Luke 2:16"> They came into the house and saw the young child with Miriam, his mother, and they fell down and worshipped him. Opening their treasures, they offered to him gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 2:22; Matthew 2:19; Matthew 2:13"> Being warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they went back to their own country another way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 10:7; Acts 5:19; Matthew 1:20"> Now when they had departed, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream, saying, “Arise and take the young child and his mother, and flee into Egypt, and stay there until I tell you, for Herod will seek the young child to destroy him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 2:20–21; Acts 26:21; Matthew 1:24"> He arose and took the young child and his mother by night and departed into Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hosea 11:1; Exodus 4:22; Numbers 24:8"> and was there until the death of Herod, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the Lord through the prophet, saying, “Out of Egypt I called my son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 28:15; Judges 16:10"> Then Herod, when he saw that he was mocked by the wise men, was exceedingly angry, and sent out and killed all the male children who were in Bethlehem and in all the surrounding countryside, from two years old and under, according to the exact time which he had learnt from the wise men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then that which was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:15; Jeremiah 9:17–21; Jeremiah 4:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">“A voice was heard in Ramah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lamentation, weeping and great mourning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Rachel weeping for her children;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she wouldn’t be comforted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they are no more.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Daniel 8:25; Matthew 2:12–13"> But when Herod was dead, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 4:19; 1 Kings 12:1–3; Matthew 2:13"> “Arise and take the young child and his mother, and go into Eretz-Israel, for those who sought the young child’s life are dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 6:22"> He arose and took the young child and his mother, and came into Eretz-Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 48:17–18; Luke 2:39; Yochanan 7:52"> But when he heard that Archelaus was reigning over Judea in the place of his father, Herod, he was afraid to go there. Being warned in a dream, he withdrew into the region of Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 13:5; Yochanan 1:45–46; Mark 1:24"> and came and lived in a city called Nazareth; that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through the prophets that he will be called a Nazarene.</span>
