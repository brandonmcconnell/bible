</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Luke 7:9; Mark 6:6"> When Yeshua heard it, he marvelled and said to those who followed,<span className="woj">“Most certainly I tell you, I haven’t found so great a faith, not even in Israel.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:30; Luke 13:28; Matthew 21:43"> <span className="woj">but the children of the Kingdom will be thrown out into the outer darkness. There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.”</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; Luke 4:38–39; Luke 8:54"> He touched her hand, and the fever left her. So she got up and served him.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR reads “them” instead of “him”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Matthew 14:14; Mark 1:32–34"> When evening came, they brought to him many possessed with demons. He cast out the spirits with a word, and healed all who were sick,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; 1 Peter 2:24; Matthew 1:22"> that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying, “He took our infirmities and bore our diseases.”</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 9:59–62; Leviticus 21:11–12; Numbers 6:6–7"> Another of his disciples said to him, “Lord, allow me first to go and bury my father.”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 8:22–25; Mark 4:36–41; Matthew 9:1"> When he got into a boat, his disciples followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 8:23; Psalms 107:23–27; Mark 4:37–38"> Behold, a violent storm came up on the sea, so much that the boat was covered with the waves; but he was asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jonah 1:6; 2 Chronicles 14:11; Mark 4:38–39"> The disciples came to him and woke him up, saying, “Save us, Lord! We are dying!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; Psalms 65:7; Psalms 107:28–30"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Why are you fearful, O you of little faith?”</span>Then he got up, rebuked the wind and the sea, and there was a great calm.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 7:37; Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:33"> The men marvelled, saying, “What kind of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 8:26–39; Mark 5:1–20; Acts 10:38"> When he came to the other side, into the country of the Gergesenes,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="NU reads “Gadarenes”">b</a></sup>two people possessed by demons met him there, coming out of the tombs, exceedingly fierce, so that nobody could pass that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Luke 4:34; Jacob 2:19"> Behold, they cried out, saying, “What do we have to do with you, Yeshua, Son of God? Have you come here to torment us before the time?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:8; Leviticus 11:7; Mark 5:11"> Now there was a herd of many pigs feeding far away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Revelation 20:1–2; Luke 8:30–33; Mark 5:12"> The demons begged him, saying, “If you cast us out, permit us to go away into the herd of pigs.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 5:13; Acts 2:23; Revelation 20:7"> He said to them,<span className="woj">“Go!”</span><br />They came out and went into the herd of pigs; and behold, the whole herd of pigs rushed down the cliff into the sea and died in the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:34–36; Mark 5:14–16; Acts 19:15–17"> Those who fed them fled and went away into the city and told everything, including what happened to those who were possessed with demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 16:39; Luke 8:37–39; Luke 5:8"> Behold, all the city came out to meet Yeshua. When they saw him, they begged that he would depart from their borders.</span>
