<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:11–16; 2 Timothy 2:19; Yochanan 10:4"> <span className="woj">The gatekeeper opens the gate for him, and the sheep listen to his voice. He calls his own sheep by name and leads them out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; Yochanan 10:16; Yochanan 12:26"> <span className="woj">Whenever he brings out his own sheep, he goes before them; and the sheep follow him, for they know his voice.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:5–6; Colossians 2:6–10; Mark 4:24"> <span className="woj">They will by no means follow a stranger, but will flee from him; for they don’t know the voice of strangers.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 56:11; Psalms 82:5; Proverbs 28:5"> Yeshua spoke this parable to them, but they didn’t understand what he was telling them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Yochanan 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; Yochanan 10:1"> Yeshua therefore said to them again,<span className="woj">“Most certainly, I tell you, I am the sheep’s door.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:2; Jeremiah 23:1; Yochanan 10:1"> <span className="woj">All who came before me are thieves and robbers, but the sheep didn’t listen to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Yochanan 14:6; Ephesians 2:18; Yochanan 10:7"> <span className="woj">I am the door. If anyone enters in by me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out and will find pasture.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Yochanan 6:51; Yochanan 12:47"> <span className="woj">The thief only comes to steal, kill, and destroy. I came that they may have life, and may have it abundantly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Yochanan 10:14–15; Psalms 23:1; Yochanan 15:13"> <span className="woj">“I am the good shepherd.</span><span className="woj">The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:29; Zechariah 11:16–17; Ezekiel 34:2–6"> <span className="woj">He who is a hired hand, and not a shepherd, who doesn’t own the sheep, sees the wolf coming, leaves the sheep, and flees. The wolf snatches the sheep and scatters them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 2:20; Yochanan 12:6; Acts 18:17"> <span className="woj">The hired hand flees because he is a hired hand and doesn’t care for the sheep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Yochanan 10:27; Yochanan 10:11; 2 Timothy 2:19"> <span className="woj">I am the good shepherd. I know my own, and I’m known by my own;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 1:4; Yochanan 10:11; Isaiah 53:10"> <span className="woj">even as the Father knows me, and I know the Father. I lay down my life for the sheep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 56:8; Yochanan 11:52; 1 Peter 2:25"> <span className="woj">I have other sheep which are not of this fold.</span><span className="woj">I must bring them also, and they will hear my voice. They will become one flock with one shepherd.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Yochanan 10:18; Yochanan 10:11; Hebrews 2:9"> <span className="woj">Therefore the Father loves me, because I lay down my life,</span><span className="woj">that I may take it again.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 2:9; Titus 2:14; Yochanan 2:19–21"> <span className="woj">No one takes it away from me, but I lay it down by myself. I have power to lay it down, and I have power to take it again. I received this commandment from my Father.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Yochanan 9:16; 1 Corinthians 3:3; Yochanan 7:40–43"> Therefore a division arose again amongst the Judeans because of these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Yochanan 7:20; Mark 3:21; Yochanan 8:47–48"> Many of them said, “He has a demon and is insane! Why do you listen to him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 4:11; Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 94:9"> Others said, “These are not the sayings of one possessed by a demon. It isn’t possible for a demon to open the eyes of the blind, is it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> It was the Feast of Hanukkah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="The “Feast of the Dedication” is the Greek name for “Hanukkah”, a celebration of the rededication of the Temple.">a</a></sup>at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> It was winter, and Yeshua was walking in the temple, in Solomon’s porch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 22:67–70; Yochanan 1:19; Yochanan 8:25"> The Judeans therefore came around him and said to him, “How long will you hold us in suspense? If you are the Messiah, tell us plainly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Yochanan 10:38; Yochanan 8:58; Yochanan 14:11"> Yeshua answered them,<span className="woj">“I told you, and you don’t believe. The works that I do in my Father’s name, these testify about me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Yochanan 8:47; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4; Yochanan 12:37–40"> <span className="woj">But you don’t believe, because you are not of my sheep, as I told you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Yochanan 10:16; Yochanan 10:14; Revelation 3:20"> <span className="woj">My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Yochanan 6:39–40; Yochanan 6:37; Yochanan 3:15–16"> <span className="woj">I give eternal life to them. They will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Yochanan 6:37; Yochanan 17:2; Yochanan 17:9"> <span className="woj">My Father who has given them to me is greater than all. No one is able to snatch them out of my Father’s hand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Yochanan 1:1–2; Yochanan 14:9; Yochanan 17:21–23"> <span className="woj">I and the Father are one.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Yochanan 5:18; Yochanan 8:59; 1 Samuel 30:6"> Therefore the Judeans took up stones again to stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Yochanan 3:12; Yochanan 5:36; Psalms 35:12"> Yeshua answered them,<span className="woj">“I have shown you many good works from my Father. For which of those works do you stone me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Yochanan 5:18; Leviticus 24:16; Yochanan 10:30"> The Judeans answered him, “We don’t stone you for a good work, but for blasphemy, because you, being a man, make yourself God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 82:6–7; Exodus 7:1; Psalms 82:1"> Yeshua answered them,<span className="woj">“Isn’t it written in your law, ‘I said, you are gods’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Romans 13:1; Luke 16:17"> <span className="woj">If he called them gods, to whom the word of God came (and the Scripture can’t be broken),</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Yochanan 5:17–18; Yochanan 3:17; Jeremiah 1:5"> <span className="woj">do you say of him whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world, ‘You blaspheme,’ because I said, ‘I am the Son of God’?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Yochanan 15:24; Yochanan 10:25; Yochanan 12:37–40"> <span className="woj">If I don’t do the works of my Father, don’t believe me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Yochanan 14:20; Yochanan 10:25; Yochanan 10:30"> <span className="woj">But if I do them, though you don’t believe me, believe the works, that you may know and believe that the Father is in me, and I in the Father.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Yochanan 7:30; Yochanan 8:59; Yochanan 7:44"> They sought again to seize him, and he went out of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Yochanan 1:28; Yochanan 11:54; Yochanan 7:1"> He went away again beyond the Jordan into the place where Yochanan was immersing at first, and he stayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Yochanan 1:33–34; Yochanan 1:29–30; Mark 1:37"> Many came to him. They said, “Yochanan indeed did no sign, but everything that Yochanan said about this man is true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Yochanan 4:41; Yochanan 2:23; Yochanan 7:31"> Many believed in him there.</span>
