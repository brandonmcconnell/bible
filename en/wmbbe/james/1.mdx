<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:15; 1 Peter 1:1; Yochanan 7:35"> Jacob, a servant of God and of the Lord Yeshua the Messiah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Messiah” means “Anointed One”.">a</a></sup>to the twelve tribes which are in the Diaspora: Greetings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:6–8; Jacob 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Count it all joy, my brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">b</a></sup>when you fall into various temptations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 5:3–4; Hebrews 10:36; Colossians 1:11"> knowing that the testing of your faith produces endurance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:19; 1 Peter 5:10; Matthew 5:48"> Let endurance have its perfect work, that you may be perfect and complete, lacking in nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:3–6; Yochanan 15:7; Proverbs 3:5–7"> But if any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God, who gives to all liberally and without reproach, and it will be given to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:22–24"> But let him ask in faith, without any doubting, for he who doubts is like a wave of the sea, driven by the wind and tossed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jacob 4:3; Isaiah 58:3–4; Proverbs 21:27"> For that man shouldn’t think that he will receive anything from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; Matthew 6:24; Jacob 4:8"> He is a double-minded man, unstable in all his ways.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; Jacob 2:5–6; Proverbs 19:1"> Let the brother in humble circumstances glory in his high position;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; 1 Yochanan 2:17; Job 14:2"> and the rich, in that he is made humble, because like the flower in the grass, he will pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7–8; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 49:6–14"> For the sun arises with the scorching wind and withers the grass; and the flower in it falls, and the beauty of its appearance perishes. So the rich man will also fade away in his pursuits.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Peter 1:6–8; Revelation 2:10"> Blessed is a person who endures temptation, for when he has been approved, he will receive the crown of life which the Lord promised to those who love him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:12–13; Jacob 1:12; Romans 9:19–20"> Let no man say when he is tempted, “I am tempted by God,” for God can’t be tempted by evil, and he himself tempts no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; Ephesians 4:22; Matthew 15:18"> But each one is tempted when he is drawn away by his own lust and enticed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Job 15:35; Romans 5:12–21"> Then the lust, when it has conceived, bears sin. The sin, when it is full grown, produces death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; Jacob 1:19; Philippians 2:12"> Don’t be deceived, my beloved brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Yochanan 3:27; Matthew 7:11; Hebrews 13:8"> Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom can be no variation nor turning shadow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:29–31; 1 Peter 1:23; Yochanan 3:3–5"> Of his own will he gave birth to us by the word of truth, that we should be a kind of first fruits of his creatures.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 17:27; Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 14:29"> So, then, my beloved brothers, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, and slow to anger;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:26; Jacob 3:17–18"> for the anger of man doesn’t produce the righteousness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:11"> Therefore, putting away all filthiness and overflowing of wickedness, receive with humility the implanted word, which is able to save your souls.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, preserve your life.">c</a></sup></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46–48; Yochanan 13:17"> But be doers of the word, and not only hearers, deluding your own selves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Ezekiel 33:31–32; Jacob 2:14–26"> For if anyone is a hearer of the word and not a doer, he is like a man looking at his natural face in a mirror;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Peter 3:11; Luke 1:66; Luke 7:39"> for he sees himself, and goes away, and immediately forgets what kind of man he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Yochanan 13:17; 2 Corinthians 3:17–18"> But he who looks into the perfect Torah of freedom and continues, not being a hearer who forgets but a doer of the work, this man will be blessed in what he does.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Psalms 141:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> If anyone amongst you thinks himself to be religious while he doesn’t bridle his tongue, but deceives his heart, this man’s religion is worthless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; 1 Yochanan 3:17–19; Galatians 6:9–10"> Pure religion and undefiled before our God and Father is this: to visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep oneself unstained by the world.</span>
</div>
</div>
