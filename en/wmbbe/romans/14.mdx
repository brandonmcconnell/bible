<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 15:1; 1 Corinthians 8:7–13; 1 Corinthians 9:22"> Now accept one who is weak in faith, but not for disputes over opinions.</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 14:10; Colossians 2:16–17; Luke 18:9"> Don’t let him who eats despise him who doesn’t eat. Don’t let him who doesn’t eat judge him who eats, for God has accepted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jacob 4:11–12; Romans 9:20; Romans 8:31–39"> Who are you who judge another’s servant? To his own lord he stands or falls. Yes, he will be made to stand, for God has power to make him stand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:23; Galatians 4:9–10"> One man esteems one day as more important. Another esteems every day alike. Let each man be fully assured in his own mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:30–31; 1 Timothy 4:3–5; Matthew 14:19"> He who observes the day, observes it to the Lord; and he who does not observe the day, to the Lord he does not observe it. He who eats, eats to the Lord, for he gives God thanks. He who doesn’t eat, to the Lord he doesn’t eat, and gives God thanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 4:2; 2 Corinthians 5:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20"> For none of us lives to himself, and none dies to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:20; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 15:23"> For if we live, we live to the Lord. Or if we die, we die to the Lord. If therefore we live or die, we are the Lord’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; Luke 24:26; Hebrews 12:2"> For to this end Messiah died, rose, and lived again, that he might be Lord of both the dead and the living.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> But you, why do you judge your brother? Or you again, why do you despise your brother? For we will all stand before the judgement seat of Messiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:22–25; Philippians 2:10–11; Psalms 72:11"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘As I live,’ says the Lord, ‘to me every knee will bow.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Every tongue will confess to God.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:5; Matthew 12:36; Matthew 16:27"> So then each one of us will give account of himself to God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Jacob 4:11; 1 Corinthians 8:9–13"> Therefore let’s not judge one another any more, but judge this rather, that no man put a stumbling block in his brother’s way, or an occasion for falling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Romans 14:2; 1 Timothy 4:4"> I know and am persuaded in the Lord Yeshua that nothing is unclean of itself; except that to him who considers anything to be unclean, to him it is unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; Philippians 2:2–4; Galatians 5:13"> Yet if because of food your brother is grieved, you walk no longer in love. Don’t destroy with your food him for whom Messiah died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 12:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:22; 1 Corinthians 10:29–30"> Then don’t let your good be slandered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Romans 15:13; 1 Corinthians 4:20"> for God’s Kingdom is not eating and drinking, but righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Corinthians 5:11; 1 Peter 2:20"> For he who serves Messiah in these things is acceptable to God and approved by men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Romans 12:18; Psalms 34:14"> So then, let’s follow after things which make for peace, and things by which we may build one another up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 14:14–15; Matthew 18:6; Titus 1:15"> Don’t overthrow God’s work for food’s sake. All things indeed are clean, however it is evil for that man who creates a stumbling block by eating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; Romans 14:13; Romans 15:1–2"> It is good to not eat meat, drink wine, nor do anything by which your brother stumbles, is offended, or is made weak.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Yochanan 3:21; Romans 14:5; Jacob 3:13"> Do you have faith? Have it to yourself before God. Happy is he who doesn’t judge himself in that which he approves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Titus 1:15; 1 Corinthians 11:29–31"> But he who doubts is condemned if he eats, because it isn’t of faith; and whatever is not of faith is sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Now to him who is able to establish you according to my Good News and the proclaiming of Yeshua the Messiah, according to the revelation of the mystery which has been kept secret through long ages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> but now is revealed, and by the Scriptures of the prophets, according to the commandment of the eternal God, is made known for obedience of faith to all the nations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> to the only wise God, through Yeshua the Messiah, to whom be the glory forever! Amen.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR places verses 24-26 after Romans 16:24 as verses 25-27.">a</a></sup></span>
</div>
