<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:20–22; Romans 9:1–3; Yochanan 5:34"> Brothers, my heart’s desire and my prayer to God is for Israel, that they may be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:20; Romans 9:31–32; Proverbs 19:2"> For I testify about them that they have a zeal for God, but not according to knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Philippians 3:9; Luke 16:15; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For being ignorant of God’s righteousness, and seeking to establish their own righteousness, they didn’t subject themselves to the righteousness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 3:24; Romans 8:3–4; Matthew 5:17–18"> For Messiah is the fulfilment<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, completion, or end">a</a></sup>of the law for righteousness to everyone who believes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 18:5; Ezekiel 20:11; Nehemiah 9:29"> For Moses writes about the righteousness of the law, “The one who does them will live by them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:11–14; Proverbs 30:4; Ephesians 4:8–10"> But the righteousness which is of faith says this, “Don’t say in your heart, ‘Who will ascend into heaven?’ (that is, to bring Messiah down);</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 13:20; Revelation 1:18; Romans 4:25"> or, ‘Who will descend into the abyss?’ (that is, to bring Messiah up from the dead.)”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:14; Romans 1:16–17; 1 Timothy 4:6"> But what does it say? “The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart;” that is, the word of faith which we proclaim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 12:8; Acts 16:31; Philippians 2:11"> that if you will confess with your mouth that Yeshua is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:15; Romans 10:9; Galatians 2:16"> For with the heart one believes resulting in righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made resulting in salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:33; 1 Peter 2:6"> For the Scripture says, “Whoever believes in him will not be disappointed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Philippians 4:19; Revelation 17:14; Galatians 3:28"> For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek; for the same Lord is Lord of all, and is rich to all who call on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:21; Joel 2:32"> For, “Whoever will call on the name of the Lord will be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Acts 26:17–18; Titus 1:3"> How then will they call on him in whom they have not believed? How will they believe in him whom they have not heard? How will they hear without a proclaimer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Nahum 1:15; Ephesians 2:17"> And how will they proclaim unless they are sent? As it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“How beautiful are the feet of those who proclaim the Good News of peace,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who bring glad tidings of good things!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1; Romans 3:3; Yochanan 12:38–40"> But they didn’t all listen to the glad news. For Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our report?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 3:2; Galatians 3:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:13"> So faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 19:4; Romans 1:8; Matthew 24:14"> But I say, didn’t they hear? Yes, most certainly,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Their sound went out into all the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their words to the ends of the world.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 11:11; Romans 11:14"> But I ask, didn’t Israel know? First Moses says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will provoke you to jealousy with that which is no nation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will make you angry with a nation void of understanding.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 65:1–2; Romans 9:30; Isaiah 55:4–5"> Isaiah is very bold and says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I was found by those who didn’t seek me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I was revealed to those who didn’t ask for me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 65:2–5; 1 Peter 2:8; Jeremiah 35:15"> But about Israel he says, “All day long I stretched out my hands to a disobedient and contrary people.”</span>
