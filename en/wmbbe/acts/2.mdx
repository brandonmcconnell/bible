<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> him, being delivered up by the determined counsel and foreknowledge of God, you have taken by the hand of lawless men, crucified and killed;</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 63:5; Psalms 71:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore my heart was glad, and my tongue rejoiced.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Moreover my flesh also will dwell in hope,</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> Being therefore exalted by the right hand of God, and having received from the Father the promise of the Holy Spirit, he has poured out this which you now see and hear.</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> <span className="pl pl-2">until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet.”’</span></span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Now when they heard this, they were cut to the heart, and said to Peter and the rest of the emissaries, “Brothers, what shall we do?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> Peter said to them, “Repent and be immersed, every one of you, in the name of Yeshua the Messiah for the forgiveness of sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> For the promise is to you and to your children, and to all who are far off, even as many as the Lord our God will call to himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> With many other words he testified and exhorted them, saying, “Save yourselves from this crooked generation!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 Yochanan 1:3"> They continued steadfastly in the emissaries’ teaching and fellowship, in the breaking of bread, and prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Yochanan 14:12; Luke 8:37"> Fear came on every soul, and many wonders and signs were done through the emissaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 Yochanan 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> All who believed were together, and had all things in common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 Yochanan 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Jacob 2:14–16"> They sold their possessions and goods, and distributed them to all, according as anyone had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> Day by day, continuing steadfastly with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread at home, they took their food with gladness and singleness of heart,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> praising God and having favour with all the people. The Lord added to the assembly day by day those who were being saved.</span>
