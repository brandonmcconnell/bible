<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:1; Philippians 4:16; Acts 20:4"> Now when they had passed through Amphipolis and Apollonia, they came to Thessalonica, where there was a Jewish synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 9:20; Acts 17:17; Acts 18:4"> Paul, as was his custom, went in to them; and for three Sabbath days reasoned with them from the Scriptures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 18:28; Acts 9:22; Luke 24:32"> explaining and demonstrating that the Messiah had to suffer and rise again from the dead, and saying, “This Yeshua, whom I proclaim to you, is the Messiah.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:43; Acts 14:4"> Some of them were persuaded and joined Paul and Silas: of the devout Greeks a great multitude, and not a few of the chief women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Galatians 5:26; Acts 17:13; Romans 16:21"> But the unpersuaded Jews took along<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR reads “And the Jews who were unpersuaded, becoming envious and taking along” instead of “But the unpersuaded Jews took along”">a</a></sup>some wicked men from the marketplace and gathering a crowd, set the city in an uproar. Assaulting the house of Jason, they sought to bring them out to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 24:5; Acts 16:19–21; Acts 28:22"> When they didn’t find them, they dragged Jason and certain brothers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where the context allows may be also correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">b</a></sup>before the rulers of the city, crying, “These who have turned the world upside down have come here also,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:2; Acts 16:21; Yochanan 19:12"> whom Jason has received. These all act contrary to the decrees of Caesar, saying that there is another king, Yeshua!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 2:3; Yochanan 11:48"> The multitude and the rulers of the city were troubled when they heard these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 17:5"> When they had taken security from Jason and the rest, they let them go.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:2; Acts 17:13–14"> The brothers immediately sent Paul and Silas away by night to Beroea. When they arrived, they went into the Jewish synagogue.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 34:16; Proverbs 2:1–5; Yochanan 5:39"> Now these were more noble than those in Thessalonica, in that they received the word with all readiness of mind, examining the Scriptures daily to see whether these things were so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:14; Acts 14:1; Acts 13:50"> Many of them therefore believed; also of the prominent Greek women, and not a few men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14–16; Proverbs 28:25; Acts 6:12"> But when the Jews of Thessalonica had knowledge that the word of God was proclaimed by Paul at Beroea also, they came there likewise, agitating the multitudes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 17:10; Matthew 10:23; Acts 9:25"> Then the brothers immediately sent out Paul to go as far as to the sea, and Silas and Timothy still stayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 18:5; 1 Thessalonians 3:1; Acts 18:1"> But those who escorted Paul brought him as far as Athens. Receiving a commandment to Silas and Timothy that they should come to him very quickly, they departed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 119:158; 2 Peter 2:7; Psalms 119:136"> Now while Paul waited for them at Athens, his spirit was provoked within him as he saw the city full of idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 13:16; Acts 14:1–4; Acts 17:2–4"> So he reasoned in the synagogue with the Jews and the devout persons, and in the marketplace every day with those who met him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20–21; Colossians 2:8; Luke 11:53"> Some of the Epicurean and Stoic philosophers also<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="TR omits “also”">c</a></sup>were conversing with him. Some said, “What does this babbler want to say?”<br />Others said, “He seems to be advocating foreign deities,” because he preached Yeshua and the resurrection.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 1:27; Acts 17:22; Acts 24:24"> They took hold of him and brought him to the Areopagus, saying, “May we know what this new teaching is, which you are speaking about?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:23; Hosea 8:12; 1 Corinthians 2:14"> For you bring certain strange things to our ears. We want to know therefore what these things mean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16–17; Colossians 4:5; Ephesians 5:16"> Now all the Athenians and the strangers living there spent their time in nothing else, but either to tell or to hear some new thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 25:19; Acts 19:35; Acts 17:16"> Paul stood in the middle of the Areopagus and said, “You men of Athens, I perceive that you are very religious in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Yochanan 4:22; Galatians 4:8–9; Yochanan 17:3"> For as I passed along and observed the objects of your worship, I also found an altar with this inscription: ‘TO AN UNKNOWN GOD.’ What therefore you worship in ignorance, I announce to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 8:27; Deuteronomy 10:14; Acts 7:48"> The God who made the world and all things in it, he, being Lord of heaven and earth, doesn’t dwell in temples made with hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 50:8–13; Genesis 2:7; Isaiah 42:5"> He isn’t served by men’s hands, as though he needed anything, seeing he himself gives to all life and breath and all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7–8; Genesis 3:20; Romans 5:12–19"> He made from one blood every nation of men to dwell on all the surface of the earth, having determined appointed seasons and the boundaries of their dwellings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:23–24; Deuteronomy 4:7; Acts 14:17"> that they should seek the Lord, if perhaps they might reach out for him and find him, though he is not far from each one of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 12:10; Colossians 1:17; Hebrews 1:3"> ‘For in him we live, move, and have our being.’ As some of your own poets have said, ‘For we are also his offspring.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 40:25; Exodus 20:4; Psalms 115:4–8"> Being then the offspring of God, we ought not to think that the Divine Nature is like gold, or silver, or stone, engraved by art and design of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Titus 2:11–12; Luke 24:47"> The times of ignorance therefore God overlooked. But now he commands that all people everywhere should repent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 25:31–46; 2 Peter 3:7; Romans 2:16"> because he has appointed a day in which he will judge the world in righteousness by the man whom he has ordained; of which he has given assurance to all men, in that he has raised him from the dead.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 17:18; Acts 26:8; Acts 25:19"> Now when they heard of the resurrection of the dead, some mocked; but others said, “We want to hear you again concerning this.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Thus Paul went out from amongst them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 17:19; Romans 11:5–6; Acts 13:48"> But certain men joined with him and believed, including Dionysius the Areopagite, and a woman named Damaris, and others with them.</span>
</div>
