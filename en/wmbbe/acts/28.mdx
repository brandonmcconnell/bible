<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 27:26; Acts 27:44; Acts 27:39"> When we had escaped, then they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="NU reads “we”">a</a></sup>learnt that the island was called Malta.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:14; Hebrews 13:2; Colossians 3:11"> The natives showed us uncommon kindness; for they kindled a fire and received us all, because of the present rain and because of the cold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; Matthew 12:34; Acts 28:4"> But when Paul had gathered a bundle of sticks and laid them on the fire, a viper came out because of the heat and fastened on his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:2; Luke 13:4; Yochanan 7:24"> When the natives saw the creature hanging from his hand, they said to one another, “No doubt this man is a murderer, whom, though he has escaped from the sea, yet Justice has not allowed to live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 10:19; Mark 16:18; Psalms 91:13"> However he shook off the creature into the fire, and wasn’t harmed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:22; Acts 14:11–13; Matthew 21:9"> But they expected that he would have swollen or fallen down dead suddenly, but when they watched for a long time and saw nothing bad happen to him, they changed their minds and said that he was a god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40–41; Luke 19:6–9; Acts 28:2"> Now in the neighbourhood of that place were lands belonging to the chief man of the island, named Publius, who received us and courteously entertained us for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 6:5; Jacob 5:14–16"> The father of Publius lay sick of fever and dysentery. Paul entered in to him, prayed, and laying his hands on him, healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Mark 6:54–56; Acts 5:15"> Then when this was done, the rest also who had diseases in the island came and were cured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; Philippians 4:11–12; Matthew 6:31–34"> They also honoured us with many honours; and when we sailed, they put on board the things that we needed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 27:6; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 45:20"> After three months, we set sail in a ship of Alexandria which had wintered in the island, whose figurehead was “The Twin Brothers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Touching at Syracuse, we stayed there three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 27:13"> From there we circled around and arrived at Rhegium. After one day, a south wind sprang up, and on the second day we came to Puteoli,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:4; Acts 20:6; Acts 19:1"> where we found brothers,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="The word for “brothers” here and where context allows may also be correctly translated “brothers and sisters” or “siblings.”">b</a></sup>and were entreated to stay with them for seven days. So we came to Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; Romans 15:24; Exodus 4:14"> From there the brothers, when they heard of us, came to meet us as far as The Market of Appius and The Three Taverns. When Paul saw them, he thanked God and took courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Acts 24:23; Acts 28:30–31"> When we entered into Rome, the centurion delivered the prisoners to the captain of the guard, but Paul was allowed to stay by himself with the soldier who guarded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 6:14; Acts 25:10"> After three days Paul called together those who were the Jewish leaders. When they had come together, he said to them, “I, brothers, though I had done nothing against the people or the customs of our fathers, still was delivered prisoner from Jerusalem into the hands of the Romans,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:31–32; Acts 23:29; Acts 22:24–25"> who, when they had examined me, desired to set me free, because there was no cause of death in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:32; Acts 25:21; Acts 25:10–12"> But when the Judeans spoke against it, I was constrained to appeal to Caesar, not that I had anything about which to accuse my nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:9; Acts 26:6–7"> For this cause therefore I asked to see you and to speak with you. For because of the hope of Israel I am bound with this chain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 50:8"> They said to him, “We neither received letters from Judea concerning you, nor did any of the brothers come here and report or speak any evil of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Acts 24:14; 1 Peter 3:16"> But we desire to hear from you what you think. For, as concerning this sect, it is known to us that everywhere it is spoken against.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 17:2–3; Acts 19:8; Acts 18:28"> When they had appointed him a day, many people came to him at his lodging. He explained to them, testifying about God’s Kingdom, and persuading them concerning Yeshua, both from the Torah of Moses and from the Prophets, from morning until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 14:4; Acts 17:4–5; Acts 19:8–9"> Some believed the things which were spoken, and some disbelieved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:7; 2 Peter 1:21; Mark 7:6"> When they didn’t agree amongst themselves, they departed after Paul had spoken one message: “The Holy Spirit spoke rightly through Isaiah the prophet to our fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:19–20"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Go to this people and say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in hearing, you will hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but will in no way understand.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In seeing, you will see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but will in no way perceive.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this people’s heart has grown callous.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their ears are dull of hearing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their eyes they have closed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lest they should see with their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hear with their ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">understand with their heart,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and would turn again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then I would heal them.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:30–32; Acts 26:17–18; Romans 3:29–30"> “Be it known therefore to you that the salvation of God is sent to the nations, and they will listen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> When he had said these words, the Jews departed, having a great dispute amongst themselves.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="NU omits verse 29.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Paul stayed two whole years in his own rented house and received all who were coming to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 4:29; Ephesians 6:19–20; Matthew 4:23"> proclaiming God’s Kingdom and teaching the things concerning the Lord Yeshua the Messiah with all boldness, without hindrance.</span>
