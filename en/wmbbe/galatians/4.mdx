<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Galatians 4:29; Genesis 24:2–3"> But I say that so long as the heir is a child, he is no different from a bondservant, though he is lord of all,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> but is under guardians and stewards until the day appointed by the father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:20; Colossians 2:8; Galatians 4:9"> So we also, when we were children, were held in bondage under the elemental principles of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Yochanan 1:14; Romans 1:3"> But when the fullness of the time came, God sent out his Son, born to a woman, born under the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Ephesians 1:5; 1 Peter 1:18–20"> that he might redeem those who were under the law, that we might receive the adoption as children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:15–17; Romans 5:5; Romans 8:9"> And because you are children, God sent out the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, “Abba,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Abba is a Greek spelling for the Aramaic word for “Father” or “Daddy” used in a familiar, respectful, and loving way.">a</a></sup>Father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:16–17; Galatians 3:29–2; Galatians 3:26"> So you are no longer a bondservant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Messiah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 2:11–12; 2 Thessalonians 1:8"> However at that time, not knowing God, you were in bondage to those who by nature are not gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Galatians 3:3; Romans 8:3"> But now that you have come to know God, or rather to be known by God, why do you turn back again to the weak and miserable elemental principles, to which you desire to be in bondage all over again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:5; Leviticus 23:1–44"> You observe days, months, seasons, and years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Philippians 2:16; Galatians 2:2"> I am afraid for you, that I might have wasted my labour for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:5; 1 Kings 22:4; Genesis 34:15"> I beg you, brothers, become as I am, for I also have become as you are. You did me no wrong,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:30"> but you know that because of weakness in the flesh I preached the Good News to you the first time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 2:13; Hebrews 13:2"> That which was a temptation to you in my flesh, you didn’t despise nor reject; but you received me as an angel of God, even as Messiah Yeshua.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:2; 1 Yochanan 3:16–18; Colossians 4:13"> What was the blessing you enjoyed? For I testify to you that, if possible, you would have plucked out your eyes and given them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Proverbs 9:8; Yochanan 8:45"> So then, have I become your enemy by telling you the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Peter 2:3"> They zealously seek you in no good way. No, they desire to alienate you, that you may seek them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Revelation 3:19; Philippians 1:27"> But it is always good to be zealous in a good cause, and not only when I am present with you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 8:29; Romans 13:14"> My little children, of whom I am again in travail until Messiah is formed in you—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17–18; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Thessalonians 3:9"> but I could wish to be present with you now, and to change my tone, for I am perplexed about you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:23–24; Romans 7:5–6"> Tell me, you that desire to be under the law, don’t you listen to the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:15; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–2"> For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by the servant, and one by the free woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 4:28–29"> However, the son by the servant was born according to the flesh, but the son by the free woman was born through promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 21:9–13; Hebrews 10:15–18; Genesis 16:8"> These things contain an allegory, for these are two covenants. One is from Mount Sinai, bearing children to bondage, which is Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Acts 1:11; Galatians 1:17"> For this Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia, and answers to the Jerusalem that exists now, for she is in bondage with her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22; Philippians 3:20; Micah 4:1–2"> But the Jerusalem that is above is free, which is the mother of us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–5; Psalms 113:9; Ruth 4:14–16"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rejoice, you barren who don’t bear.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Break out and shout, you who don’t travail.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">For the desolate women have more children than her who has a husband.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Romans 9:8–9; Romans 4:13–18"> Now we, brothers, as Isaac was, are children of promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:11"> But as then, he who was born according to the flesh persecuted him who was born according to the Spirit, so also it is now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 21:10–12; Yochanan 8:35; Romans 8:15–17"> However, what does the Scripture say? “Throw out the servant and her son, for the son of the servant will not inherit with the son of the free woman.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Yochanan 8:36; Yochanan 1:12–13; Galatians 5:1"> So then, brothers, we are not children of a servant, but of the free woman.</span>
</div>
