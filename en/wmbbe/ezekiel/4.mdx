<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 1:2–9; Amos 3:2; Ezekiel 12:3–16"> “You also, son of man, take a tile, and lay it before yourself, and portray on it a city, even Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:22; Jeremiah 6:6; Luke 19:42–44"> Lay siege against it, build forts against it, and cast up a mound against it. Also set camps against it and plant battering rams against it all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:6; Isaiah 8:18; Isaiah 20:3"> Take for yourself an iron pan and set it for a wall of iron between you and the city. Then set your face towards it. It will be besieged, and you shall lay siege against it. This shall be a sign to the house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 16:22; Leviticus 10:17; Numbers 18:1"> “Moreover lie on your left side, and lay the iniquity of the house of Israel on it. According to the number of the days that you shall lie on it, you shall bear their iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Isaiah 53:6"> For I have appointed the years of their iniquity to be to you a number of days, even three hundred and ninety days. So you shall bear the iniquity of the house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:34; Daniel 12:11–12; Daniel 9:24–26"> “Again, when you have accomplished these, you shall lie on your right side, and shall bear the iniquity of the house of Judah. I have appointed forty days, each day for a year, to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 6:2"> You shall set your face towards the siege of Jerusalem, with your arm uncovered; and you shall prophesy against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:25"> Behold, I put ropes on you, and you shall not turn yourself from one side to the other, until you have accomplished the days of your siege.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:13; Isaiah 28:25; Ezekiel 4:16"> “Take for yourself also wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt, and put them in one vessel. Make bread of it. According to the number of the days that you will lie on your side, even three hundred and ninety days, you shall eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 45:12; Deuteronomy 28:51–68"> Your food which you shall eat shall be by weight, twenty shekels<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A shekel is about 10 grams or about 0.35 ounces.">a</a></sup>a day. From time to time you shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13; Yochanan 3:34; Ezekiel 4:16"> You shall drink water by measure, the sixth part of a hin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A hin is about 6.5 litres or 1.7 gallons.">b</a></sup>From time to time you shall drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:12; Genesis 18:6"> You shall eat it as barley cakes, and you shall bake it in their sight with dung that comes out of man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 1:8; Hosea 9:3–4"> The LORD said, “Even thus will the children of Israel eat their bread unclean, amongst the nations where I will drive them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 10:14; Ezekiel 20:49; Ezekiel 9:8"> Then I said, “Ah Lord GOD! Behold, my soul has not been polluted; for from my youth up even until now I have not eaten of that which dies of itself, or is torn of animals. No abominable meat has come into my mouth!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then he said to me, “Behold, I have given you cow’s dung for man’s dung, and you shall prepare your bread on it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:16; Isaiah 3:1; Leviticus 26:26"> Moreover he said to me, “Son of man, behold, I will break the staff of bread in Jerusalem. They will eat bread by weight, and with fearfulness. They will drink water by measure, and in dismay;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:23; Leviticus 26:39; Ezekiel 33:10"> that they may lack bread and water, be dismayed one with another, and pine away in their iniquity.</span>
</div>
</div>
