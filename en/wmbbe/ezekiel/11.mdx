<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 11:13"> Moreover the Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the LORD’s house, which looks eastward. Behold, twenty-five men were at the door of the gate; and I saw amongst them Jaazaniah the son of Azzur, and Pelatiah the son of Benaiah, princes of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Jeremiah 5:5; Psalms 2:1–2"> He said to me, “Son of man, these are the men who devise iniquity, and who give wicked counsel in this city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:27; Ezekiel 12:22; Ezekiel 11:7–11"> who say, ‘The time is not near to build houses. This is the cauldron, and we are the meat.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46–47; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 3:2–15"> Therefore prophesy against them. Prophesy, son of man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 16:17; Revelation 2:23"> The LORD’s Spirit fell on me, and he said to me, “Speak, ‘The LORD says: “Thus you have said, house of Israel; for I know the things that come into your mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 22:27; Ezekiel 22:12"> You have multiplied your slain in this city, and you have filled its streets with the slain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:3–13; Micah 3:2–3; 2 Kings 25:18–22"> “‘Therefore the Lord GOD says: “Your slain whom you have laid in the middle of it, they are the meat, and this is the cauldron; but you will be brought out of the middle of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:24; Isaiah 24:17–18; Job 3:25"> You have feared the sword; and I will bring the sword on you,” says the Lord GOD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Psalms 106:41; Deuteronomy 28:36"> “I will bring you out of the middle of it, and deliver you into the hands of strangers, and will execute judgements amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 52:9–10; 2 Kings 25:19–21"> You will fall by the sword. I will judge you in the border of Israel. Then you will know that I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7–10"> This will not be your cauldron, neither will you be the meat in the middle of it. I will judge you in the border of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 8:14"> You will know that I am the LORD, for you have not walked in my statutes. You have not executed my ordinances, but have done after the ordinances of the nations that are around you.”’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 9:8; Acts 5:5"> When I prophesied, Pelatiah the son of Benaiah died. Then I fell down on my face, and cried with a loud voice, and said, “Ah Lord GOD! Will you make a full end of the remnant of Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The LORD’s word came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Isaiah 65:5; Yochanan 16:2"> “Son of man, your brothers, even your brothers, the men of your relatives, and all the house of Israel, all of them, are the ones to whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, ‘Go far away from the LORD. This land has been given to us for a possession.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Psalms 90:1"> “Therefore say, ‘The Lord GOD says: “Whereas I have removed them far off amongst the nations, and whereas I have scattered them amongst the countries, yet I will be to them a sanctuary for a little while in the countries where they have come.”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Jeremiah 3:18; Ezekiel 34:13"> “Therefore say, ‘The Lord GOD says: “I will gather you from the peoples, and assemble you out of the countries where you have been scattered, and I will give you Eretz-Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 42:7–8"> “‘They will come there, and they will take away all its detestable things and all its abominations from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 31:33; Psalms 51:10"> I will give them one heart, and I will put a new spirit within them. I will take the stony heart out of their flesh, and will give them a heart of flesh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:11; Psalms 105:45; Jeremiah 30:22"> that they may walk in my statutes, and keep my ordinances, and do them. They will be my people, and I will be their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Jeremiah 1:16"> But as for them whose heart walks after the heart of their detestable things and their abominations, I will bring their way on their own heads,’ says the Lord GOD.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:19; Ezekiel 1:19–20"> Then the cherubim lifted up their wings, and the wheels were beside them. The glory of the God of Israel was over them above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 14:4; Ezekiel 8:4; Ezekiel 10:4"> The LORD’s glory went up from the middle of the city, and stood on the mountain which is on the east side of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:12; Ezekiel 8:3; Ezekiel 11:1"> The Spirit lifted me up, and brought me in the vision by the Spirit of God into Kasdimah, to the captives. So the vision that I had seen went up from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:4; Ezekiel 3:27; Ezekiel 3:17"> Then I spoke to the captives all the things that the LORD had shown me.</span>
