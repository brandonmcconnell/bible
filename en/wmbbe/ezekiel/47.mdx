<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Zechariah 14:8; Joel 3:18"> He brought me back to the door of the temple; and behold, waters flowed out from under the threshold of the temple eastward, for the front of the temple faced towards the east. The waters came down from underneath, from the right side of the temple, on the south of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:4; Ezekiel 44:2"> Then he brought me out by the way of the gate northward, and led me around by the way outside to the outer gate, by the way of the gate that looks towards the east. Behold, waters ran out on the right side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Acts 10:45–46; Acts 11:16–18"> When the man went out eastward with the line in his hand, he measured one thousand cubits,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimetres.">a</a></sup>and he caused me to pass through the waters, waters that were to the ankles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 1:6; Romans 15:19; Acts 19:10–20"> Again he measured one thousand, and caused me to pass through the waters, waters that were to the knees. Again he measured one thousand, and caused me to pass through waters that were to the waist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Habakkuk 2:14; Matthew 13:31–32"> Afterward he measured one thousand; and it was a river that I could not pass through, for the waters had risen, waters to swim in, a river that could not be walked through.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:5; Ezekiel 40:4; Zechariah 4:2"> He said to me, “Son of man, have you seen this?” Then he brought me and caused me to return to the bank of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:12; Revelation 22:2; 2 Kings 2:13"> Now when I had returned, behold, on the bank of the river were very many trees on the one side and on the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 3:16; Deuteronomy 3:17; 2 Kings 2:19–22"> Then he said to me, “These waters flow out towards the eastern region and will go down into the Arabah. Then they will go towards the sea and flow into the sea which will be made to flow out; and the waters will be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Yochanan 7:37–38; Yochanan 6:63; Yochanan 4:14"> It will happen that every living creature which swarms, in every place where the rivers come, will live. Then there will be a very great multitude of fish; for these waters have come there, and the waters of the sea will be healed, and everything will live wherever the river comes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Psalms 104:25; Numbers 34:6"> It will happen that fishermen will stand by it. From En Gedi even to En Eglaim will be a place for the spreading of nets. Their fish will be after their kinds, as the fish of the great sea, exceedingly many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Revelation 22:11; Jeremiah 17:6"> But its swamps and marshes will not be healed. They will be given up to salt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 22:2; Psalms 1:3; Jeremiah 17:8"> By the river banks, on both sides, will grow every tree for food, whose leaf won’t wither, neither will its fruit fail. It will produce new fruit every month, because its waters issue out of the sanctuary. Its fruit will be for food, and its leaf for healing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:4–6; Jeremiah 3:18; Numbers 34:2–12"> The Lord GOD says: “This shall be the border by which you shall divide the land for inheritance according to the twelve tribes of Israel. Joseph shall have two portions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5–6; Genesis 12:7; Ezekiel 48:29"> You shall inherit it, one as well as another; for I swore to give it to your fathers. This land will fall to you for inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 34:8; Ezekiel 48:1"> “This shall be the border of the land: “On the north side, from the great sea, by the way of Hethlon, to the entrance of Zedad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:1; Numbers 13:21; 2 Samuel 8:8"> Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim (which is between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath), to Hazer Hatticon, which is by the border of Hauran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:1; Numbers 34:9"> The border from the sea shall be Hazar Enon at the border of Damascus; and on the north northward is the border of Hamath. This is the north side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Genesis 31:23; Genesis 31:47"> “The east side, between Hauran, Damascus, Gilead, and Eretz-Israel, shall be the Jordan; from the north border to the east sea you shall measure. This is the east side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:28; Deuteronomy 32:51; Numbers 34:5"> “The south side southward shall be from Tamar as far as the waters of Meriboth Kadesh, to the brook, to the great sea. This is the south side southward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Ezekiel 48:1"> “The west side shall be the great sea, from the south border as far as opposite the entrance of Hamath. This is the west side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “So you shall divide this land to yourselves according to the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Acts 11:18"> You shall divide it by lot for an inheritance to you and to the aliens who live amongst you, who will father children amongst you. Then they shall be to you as the native-born amongst the children of Israel. They shall have inheritance with you amongst the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In whatever tribe the stranger lives, there you shall give him his inheritance,” says the Lord GOD.</span>
