<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 66:23; Ezekiel 44:1–2; Exodus 20:9"> “‘The Lord GOD says: “The gate of the inner court that looks towards the east shall be shut the six working days; but on the Sabbath day it shall be opened, and on the day of the new moon it shall be opened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:3; Ezekiel 46:8; 1 Chronicles 29:10–12"> The prince shall enter by the way of the porch of the gate outside, and shall stand by the post of the gate; and the priests shall prepare his burnt offering and his peace offerings, and he shall worship at the threshold of the gate. Then he shall go out, but the gate shall not be shut until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 1:10; Yochanan 10:9; Hebrews 10:19–22"> The people of the land shall worship at the door of that gate before the LORD on the Sabbaths and on the new moons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:17; Numbers 28:9–10"> The burnt offering that the prince shall offer to the LORD shall be on the Sabbath day, six lambs without defect and a ram without defect;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:24; Ezekiel 46:7; Deuteronomy 16:17"> and the meal offering shall be an efah for the ram, and the meal offering for the lambs as he is able to give, and a hin<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="A hin is about 6.5 litres or 1.7 gallons.">a</a></sup>of oil to an efah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="1 efah is about 22 litres or about 2/3 of a bushel">b</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1"> On the day of the new moon it shall be a young bull without defect, six lambs, and a ram. They shall be without defect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5"> He shall prepare a meal offering: an efah for the bull, and an efah for the ram, and for the lambs according as he is able, and a hin of oil to an efah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:2; Ezekiel 44:1–3; Colossians 1:18"> When the prince enters, he shall go in by the way of the porch of the gate, and he shall go out by its way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 23:14–17; 2 Peter 2:20–21"> “‘“But when the people of the land come before the LORD in the appointed feasts, he who enters by the way of the north gate to worship shall go out by the way of the south gate; and he who enters by the way of the south gate shall go out by the way of the north gate. He shall not return by the way of the gate by which he came in, but shall go out straight before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 42:4; 1 Chronicles 29:20; 1 Chronicles 29:22"> The prince shall go in with them when they go in. When they go out, he shall go out.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5; Ezekiel 46:7; Numbers 15:1–41"> “‘“In the feasts and in the appointed holidays, the meal offering shall be an efah<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1 efah is about 22 litres or about 2/3 of a bushel">c</a></sup>for a bull, and an efah for a ram, and for the lambs as he is able to give, and a hin of oil to an efah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:1–2; Ezekiel 45:17; Ezekiel 44:3"> When the prince prepares a free will offering, a burnt offering or peace offerings as a free will offering to the LORD, one shall open for him the gate that looks towards the east; and he shall prepare his burnt offering and his peace offerings, as he does on the Sabbath day. Then he shall go out; and after his going out one shall shut the gate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 50:4; Yochanan 1:29; Exodus 12:5"> “‘“You shall prepare a lamb a year old without defect for a burnt offering to the LORD daily. Morning by morning you shall prepare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 28:5"> You shall prepare a meal offering with it morning by morning, the sixth part of an efah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="1 efah is about 22 litres or about 2/3 of a bushel">d</a></sup>and the third part of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour; a meal offering to the LORD continually by a perpetual ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 10:1–10; Numbers 28:6; Exodus 29:42"> Thus they shall prepare the lamb, the meal offering, and the oil, morning by morning, for a continual burnt offering.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:3; Galatians 4:7; Genesis 25:5–6"> “‘The Lord GOD says: “If the prince gives a gift to any of his sons, it is his inheritance. It shall belong to his sons. It is their possession by inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10; Luke 19:25–26; Galatians 4:30–31"> But if he gives of his inheritance a gift to one of his servants, it shall be his to the year of liberty; then it shall return to the prince; but as for his inheritance, it shall be for his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 45:8; 1 Kings 21:19; Ezekiel 22:27"> Moreover the prince shall not take of the people’s inheritance, to thrust them out of their possession. He shall give inheritance to his sons out of his own possession, that my people not each be scattered from his possession.”’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:9; Ezekiel 44:4–5; Ezekiel 40:44–46"> Then he brought me through the entry, which was at the side of the gate, into the holy rooms for the priests, which looked towards the north. Behold, there was a place on the back part westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:13; Ezekiel 44:29; Ezekiel 44:19"> He said to me, “This is the place where the priests shall boil the trespass offering and the sin offering, and where they shall bake the meal offering, that they not bring them out into the outer court, to sanctify the people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then he brought me out into the outer court and caused me to pass by the four corners of the court; and behold, in every corner of the court there was a court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In the four corners of the court there were courts enclosed, forty cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="A cubit is the length from the tip of the middle finger to the elbow on a man’s arm, or about 18 inches or 46 centimetres.">e</a></sup>long and thirty wide. These four in the corners were the same size.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> There was a wall around in them, around the four, and boiling places were made under the walls all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:20; Yochanan 21:15–17; 1 Peter 5:2"> Then he said to me, “These are the boiling houses, where the ministers of the house shall boil the sacrifice of the people.”</span>
</div>
