<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 9:1; 1 Samuel 15:23; Psalms 78:68–71"> The LORD said to Samuel, “How long will you mourn for Saul, since I have rejected him from being king over Israel? Fill your horn with oil, and go. I will send you to Jesse the Bethlehemite, for I have provided a king for myself amongst his sons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:12; 1 Samuel 20:29; Matthew 10:16"> Samuel said, “How can I go? If Saul hears it, he will kill me.” The LORD said, “Take a heifer with you, and say, ‘I have come to sacrifice to the LORD.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 16:12–13; 2 Samuel 15:11"> Call Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will show you what you shall do. You shall anoint to me him whom I name to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:13; 1 Chronicles 12:17–18; 1 Samuel 21:1"> Samuel did that which the LORD spoke, and came to Bethlehem. The elders of the city came to meet him trembling, and said, “Do you come peaceably?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 3:5; Leviticus 20:7–8; Exodus 19:10"> He said, “Peaceably; I have come to sacrifice to the LORD. Sanctify yourselves, and come with me to the sacrifice.” He sanctified Jesse and his sons, and called them to the sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13; 1 Samuel 17:22"> When they had come, he looked at Eliab, and said, “Surely the LORD’s anointed is before him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Yochanan 7:24; Jeremiah 17:10; Luke 16:15"> But the LORD said to Samuel, “Don’t look on his face, or on the height of his stature, because I have rejected him; for I don’t see as man sees. For man looks at the outward appearance, but the LORD looks at the heart.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13"> Then Jesse called Abinadab, and made him pass before Samuel. He said, “The LORD has not chosen this one, either.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13; 2 Samuel 13:3"> Then Jesse made Shammah to pass by. He said, “The LORD has not chosen this one, either.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13–15"> Jesse made seven of his sons to pass before Samuel. Samuel said to Jesse, “The LORD has not chosen these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 78:70–71; 2 Samuel 7:8; 2 Samuel 13:3"> Samuel said to Jesse, “Are all your children here?” He said, “There remains yet the youngest. Behold, he is keeping the sheep.” Samuel said to Jesse, “Send and get him, for we will not sit down until he comes here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Lamentations 4:7; Psalms 89:19–20; Song of Solomon 5:10"> He sent, and brought him in. Now he was ruddy, with a handsome face and good appearance. The LORD said, “Arise! Anoint him, for this is he.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:6; Hebrews 1:9; Yochanan 3:34"> Then Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the middle of his brothers. Then the LORD’s Spirit came mightily on David from that day forward. So Samuel rose up and went to Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 51:11; 1 Samuel 18:12; Judges 9:23"> Now the LORD’s Spirit departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from the LORD troubled him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Saul’s servants said to him, “See now, an evil spirit from God troubles you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Kings 10:8; 1 Samuel 19:9"> Let our lord now command your servants who are in front of you to seek out a man who is a skilful player on the harp. Then when the evil spirit from God is on you, he will play with his hand, and you will be well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Saul said to his servants, “Provide me now a man who can play well, and bring him to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:19; Genesis 39:23; Genesis 39:2"> Then one of the young men answered and said, “Behold, I have seen a son of Jesse the Bethlehemite who is skilful in playing, a mighty man of valour, a man of war, prudent in speech, and a handsome person; and the LORD is with him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; 1 Samuel 17:15; Psalms 78:70–72"> Therefore Saul sent messengers to Jesse, and said, “Send me David your son, who is with the sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Proverbs 18:16; 1 Samuel 17:18"> Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread, a container of wine, and a young goat, and sent them by David his son to Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:46; Proverbs 22:29; Psalms 146:3"> David came to Saul and stood before him. He loved him greatly; and he became his armour bearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Saul sent to Jesse, saying, “Please let David stand before me, for he has found favour in my sight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:14–16; 1 Samuel 18:10–11; Luke 11:24–26"> When the spirit from God was on Saul, David took the harp and played with his hand; so Saul was refreshed and was well, and the evil spirit departed from him.</span>
