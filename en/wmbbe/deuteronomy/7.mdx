<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:3; Acts 13:19; Deuteronomy 4:38"> When the LORD your God brings you into the land where you go to possess it, and casts out many nations before you—the Hittite, the Girgashite, the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite—seven nations greater and mightier than you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 2:2; Exodus 23:32–33; Deuteronomy 20:16–17"> and when the LORD your God delivers them up before you, and you strike them, then you shall utterly destroy them. You shall make no covenant with them, nor show mercy to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Joshua 23:12–13; Exodus 34:15–16"> You shall not make marriages with them. You shall not give your daughter to his son, nor shall you take his daughter for your son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:15; Judges 2:11; Deuteronomy 4:26"> For that would turn away your sons from following me, that they may serve other gods. So the LORD’s anger would be kindled against you, and he would destroy you quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 23:24; Deuteronomy 12:2–3; Exodus 34:13"> But you shall deal with them like this: you shall break down their altars, dash their pillars in pieces, cut down their Asherah poles, and burn their engraved images with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:2; Exodus 19:5–6; 1 Peter 2:9"> For you are a holy people to the LORD your God. The LORD your God has chosen you to be a people for his own possession, above all peoples who are on the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Romans 9:11–15; Romans 11:6"> The LORD didn’t set his love on you nor choose you, because you were more in number than any people; for you were the fewest of all peoples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; Exodus 32:13; Ephesians 2:4–5"> but because the LORD loves you, and because he desires to keep the oath which he swore to your fathers, the LORD has brought you out with a mighty hand and redeemed you out of the house of bondage, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 10:23; 2 Timothy 2:13; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> Know therefore that the LORD your God himself is God, the faithful God, who keeps covenant and loving kindness to a thousand generations with those who love him and keep his commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 59:18; Nahum 1:2; Romans 12:19"> and repays those who hate him to their face, to destroy them. He will not be slack to him who hates him. He will repay him to his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Yochanan 14:15; Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 5:32"> You shall therefore keep the commandments, the statutes, and the ordinances which I command you today, to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Deuteronomy 7:9; Leviticus 26:3–13"> It shall happen, because you listen to these ordinances and keep and do them, that the LORD your God will keep with you the covenant and the loving kindness which he swore to your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Yochanan 14:21; Yochanan 15:10; Deuteronomy 28:3–5"> He will love you, bless you, and multiply you. He will also bless the fruit of your body and the fruit of your ground, your grain and your new wine and your oil, the increase of your livestock and the young of your flock, in the land which he swore to your fathers to give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 23:26–33; Deuteronomy 28:4; Psalms 127:3"> You will be blessed above all peoples. There won’t be male or female barren amongst you, or amongst your livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Psalms 105:36–37; Leviticus 26:3–4"> The LORD will take away from you all sickness; and he will put none of the evil diseases of Egypt, which you know, on you, but will lay them on all those who hate you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:33; Judges 8:27; Deuteronomy 13:8"> You shall consume all the peoples whom the LORD your God shall deliver to you. Your eye shall not pity them. You shall not serve their gods; for that would be a snare to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 13:32; Numbers 33:53; Deuteronomy 18:21"> If you shall say in your heart, “These nations are more than I; how can I dispossess them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 105:5; Deuteronomy 31:6; Deuteronomy 1:29"> you shall not be afraid of them. You shall remember well what the LORD your God did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Nehemiah 9:10–11; Jeremiah 32:20–21"> the great trials which your eyes saw, the signs, the wonders, the mighty hand, and the outstretched arm, by which the LORD your God brought you out. So shall the LORD your God do to all the peoples of whom you are afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 24:12; Exodus 23:28–30"> Moreover the LORD your God will send the hornet amongst them, until those who are left, and hide themselves, perish from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:5; Deuteronomy 10:17; Nehemiah 9:32"> You shall not be scared of them; for the LORD your God is amongst you, a great and awesome God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 23:29–30; Joshua 15:63"> The LORD your God will cast out those nations before you little by little. You may not consume them at once, lest the animals of the field increase on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:20; Deuteronomy 2:15; Jeremiah 17:18"> But the LORD your God will deliver them up before you, and will confuse them with a great confusion, until they are destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 10:8; Joshua 23:9; Joshua 1:5"> He will deliver their kings into your hand, and you shall make their name perish from under the sky. No one will be able to stand before you until you have destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; 1 Chronicles 14:12; Deuteronomy 17:1"> You shall burn the engraved images of their gods with fire. You shall not covet the silver or the gold that is on them, nor take it for yourself, lest you be snared in it; for it is an abomination to the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:17; Hosea 14:8; Ezekiel 11:18"> You shall not bring an abomination into your house and become a devoted thing like it. You shall utterly detest it. You shall utterly abhor it; for it is a devoted thing.</span>
