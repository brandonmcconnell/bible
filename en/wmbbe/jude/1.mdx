<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 1:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Peter 2:9"> Judah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, Judah">a</a></sup>a servant of Yeshua the Messiah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Messiah” means “Anointed One”.">b</a></sup>and brother of Jacob, to those who are called, sanctified by God the Father, and kept for Yeshua the Messiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2; Revelation 1:4–6"> May mercy, peace, and love be multiplied to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; Philippians 1:27; Judah 1:20"> Beloved, while I was very eager to write to you about our common salvation, I was constrained to write to you exhorting you to contend earnestly for the faith which was once for all delivered to the holy ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; Galatians 2:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> For there are certain men who crept in secretly, even those who were long ago written about for this condemnation: ungodly men, turning the grace of our God into indecency, and denying our only Master, God, and Lord, Yeshua the Messiah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Hebrews 3:16–2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–12"> Now I desire to remind you, though you already know this, that the Lord, having saved a people out of the land of Egypt, afterward destroyed those who didn’t believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 25:41; Ephesians 6:12"> Angels who didn’t keep their first domain, but deserted their own dwelling place, he has kept in everlasting bonds under darkness for the judgement of the great day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; 2 Peter 2:6; Romans 1:26–27"> Even as Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities around them, having in the same way as these given themselves over to sexual immorality and gone after strange flesh, are shown as an example, suffering the punishment of eternal fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Peter 2:10–12"> Yet in the same way, these also in their dreaming defile the flesh, despise authority, and slander celestial beings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 3:2; 2 Peter 2:11; Deuteronomy 34:6"> But Michael, the archangel, when contending with the devil and arguing about the body of Moses, dared not bring against him an abusive condemnation, but said, “May the Lord rebuke you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:12; Romans 1:21–22"> But these speak evil of whatever things they don’t know. They are destroyed in these things that they understand naturally, like the creatures without reason.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:15; 1 Yochanan 3:12; Revelation 2:14"> Woe to them! For they went in the way of Cain, and ran riotously in the error of Balaam for hire, and perished in Korah’s rebellion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17–20; Matthew 15:13; Ezekiel 34:8"> These are hidden rocky reefs in your love feasts when they feast with you, shepherds who without fear feed themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; autumn trees without fruit, twice dead, plucked up by the roots;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17; Philippians 3:19; Isaiah 57:20"> wild waves of the sea, foaming out their own shame; wandering stars, for whom the blackness of darkness has been reserved forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 50:3–5; Hebrews 11:5–6"> About these also Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied, saying, “Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Behold”, from “ἰδοὺ”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. It is often used as an interjection.">c</a></sup>the Lord came with ten thousands of his holy ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:16; Ecclesiastes 11:9; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> to execute judgement on all, and to convict all the ungodly of all their works of ungodliness which they have done in an ungodly way, and of all the hard things which ungodly sinners have spoken against him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18; 2 Peter 2:10; Philippians 2:14"> These are murmurers and complainers, walking after their lusts—and their mouth speaks proud things—showing respect of persons to gain advantage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:2; 1 Yochanan 4:6; Ephesians 4:11"> But you, beloved, remember the words which have been spoken before by the emissaries of our Lord Yeshua the Messiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:1–5"> They said to you, “In the last time there will be mockers, walking after their own ungodly lusts.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Proverbs 18:1; Romans 8:9"> These are those who cause divisions and are sensual, not having the Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Romans 8:26–27; 1 Corinthians 14:15"> But you, beloved, keep building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:16; Lamentations 3:25–26; Hebrews 9:28"> Keep yourselves in God’s love, looking for the mercy of our Lord Yeshua the Messiah to eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jacob 5:19–20; Galatians 6:1; 1 Yochanan 5:16–18"> On some have compassion, making a distinction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Revelation 3:4"> and some save, snatching them out of the fire with fear, hating even the clothing stained by the flesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 1:22"> Now to him who is able to keep them<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="TR and NU read “you”">d</a></sup>from stumbling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory in great joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Revelation 5:13–14; 1 Peter 5:10–11"> to God our Saviour, who alone is wise, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and forever. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
