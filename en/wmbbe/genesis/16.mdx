<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 16:5; Genesis 12:4–5; 2 Samuel 5:13"> Sarai, Abram’s wife, took Hagar the Egyptian, her servant, after Abram had lived ten years in the land of Canaan, and gave her to Abram her husband to be his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 30:23; 2 Samuel 6:16; 1 Corinthians 13:4–5"> He went in to Hagar, and she conceived. When she saw that she had conceived, her mistress was despised in her eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Exodus 5:21; Psalms 7:8"> Sarai said to Abram, “This wrong is your fault. I gave my servant into your bosom, and when she saw that she had conceived, she despised me. May the LORD judge between me and you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:5; Proverbs 14:29; 1 Peter 3:7"> But Abram said to Sarai, “Behold, your maid is in your hand. Do to her whatever is good in your eyes.” Sarai dealt harshly with her, and she fled from her face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Exodus 15:22; Genesis 21:17"> The LORD’s angel found her by a fountain of water in the wilderness, by the fountain on the way to Shur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 3:9; Genesis 4:10; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> He said, “Hagar, Sarai’s servant, where did you come from? Where are you going?”<br />She said, “I am fleeing from the face of my mistress Sarai.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5–6; Ecclesiastes 10:4; Titus 2:9"> The LORD’s angel said to her, “Return to your mistress, and submit yourself under her hands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Psalms 83:6–7; Genesis 25:12–18"> The LORD’s angel said to her, “I will greatly multiply your offspring, that they will not be counted for multitude.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 2:23–24; Exodus 3:7; Isaiah 7:14"> The LORD’s angel said to her, “Behold, you are with child, and will bear a son. You shall call his name Ishmael, because the LORD has heard your affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 21:20; Job 39:5–8"> He will be like a wild donkey amongst men. His hand will be against every man, and every man’s hand against him. He will live opposed to all of his brothers.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–12; Genesis 32:30; Proverbs 5:21"> She called the name of the LORD who spoke to her, “You are a God who sees,” for she said, “Have I even stayed alive after seeing him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 24:62; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:31"> Therefore the well was called Beer Lahai Roi.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Beer Lahai Roi means “well of the one who lives and sees me”.">a</a></sup>Behold, it is between Kadesh and Bered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 25:12; Genesis 21:9–21; Genesis 17:25–26"> Hagar bore a son for Abram. Abram called the name of his son, whom Hagar bore, Ishmael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.</span>
