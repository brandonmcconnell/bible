<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 25:11; Genesis 21:22–32"> There was a famine in the land, in addition to the first famine that was in the days of Abraham. Isaac went to Abimelech king of the Philistines, to Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Psalms 37:3; Genesis 17:1"> The LORD appeared to him, and said, “Don’t go down into Egypt. Live in the land I will tell you about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Psalms 105:9; Genesis 17:8"> Live in this land, and I will be with you, and will bless you. For I will give to you, and to your offspring, all these lands, and I will establish the oath which I swore to Abraham your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17–18"> I will multiply your offspring as the stars of the sky, and will give all these lands to your offspring. In your offspring all the nations of the earth will be blessed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 7:24; Genesis 22:18; Genesis 22:16"> because Abraham obeyed my voice, and kept my requirements, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 20:1"> Isaac lived in Gerar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 12:13; Genesis 20:2"> The men of the place asked him about his wife. He said, “She is my sister,” for he was afraid to say, “My wife”, lest, he thought, “the men of the place might kill me for Rebekah, because she is beautiful to look at.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; Proverbs 5:18–19; Proverbs 7:6"> When he had been there a long time, Abimelech king of the Philistines looked out at a window, and saw, and, behold, Isaac was caressing Rebekah, his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Abimelech called Isaac, and said, “Behold, surely she is your wife. Why did you say, ‘She is my sister’?”<br />Isaac said to him, “Because I said, ‘Lest I die because of her.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:9–10; Genesis 12:18–19"> Abimelech said, “What is this you have done to us? One of the people might easily have lain with your wife, and you would have brought guilt on us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 105:15; Genesis 20:6; Proverbs 6:29"> Abimelech commanded all the people, saying, “He who touches this man or his wife will surely be put to death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:8; Genesis 24:1; Genesis 26:3"> Isaac sowed in that land, and reaped in the same year one hundred times what he planted. The LORD blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 112:3; Proverbs 10:22; Genesis 24:35"> The man grew great, and grew more and more until he became very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 37:11; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 27:4"> He had possessions of flocks, possessions of herds, and a great household. The Philistines envied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 21:30; Genesis 21:25"> Now all the wells which his father’s servants had dug in the days of Abraham his father, the Philistines had stopped, and filled with earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 1:9"> Abimelech said to Isaac, “Go away from us, for you are much mightier than we.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Isaac departed from there, encamped in the valley of Gerar, and lived there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 13:2; Hosea 2:17; Numbers 32:38"> Isaac dug again the wells of water, which they had dug in the days of Abraham his father, for the Philistines had stopped them after the death of Abraham. He called their names after the names by which his father had called them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Yochanan 4:10–11; Yochanan 7:38; Song of Solomon 4:15"> Isaac’s servants dug in the valley, and found there a well of flowing<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, living. Or, fresh.">a</a></sup>water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:25"> The herdsmen of Gerar argued with Isaac’s herdsmen, saying, “The water is ours.” So he called the name of the well Esek,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="“Esek” means “contention”.">b</a></sup>because they contended with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> They dug another well, and they argued over that, also. So he called its name Sitnah.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="“Sitnah” means “hostility”.">c</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 17:6; Exodus 1:7; Psalms 118:5"> He left that place, and dug another well. They didn’t argue over that one. So he called it Rehoboth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="“Rehoboth” means “broad places”.">d</a></sup>He said, “For now the LORD has made room for us, and we will be fruitful in the land.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:1; Genesis 21:31; Genesis 46:1"> He went up from there to Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 15:1; Exodus 3:6; Isaiah 43:1–2"> The LORD appeared to him the same night, and said, “I am the God of Abraham your father. Don’t be afraid, for I am with you, and will bless you, and multiply your offspring for my servant Abraham’s sake.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Psalms 116:17; Genesis 12:7–8"> He built an altar there, and called on the LORD’s name, and pitched his tent there. There Isaac’s servants dug a well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–32; Genesis 20:3"> Then Abimelech went to him from Gerar with Ahuzzath his friend, and Phicol the captain of his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 26:16; Judges 11:7; Acts 7:14"> Isaac said to them, “Why have you come to me, since you hate me, and have sent me away from you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 21:22–23; Isaiah 45:14; Isaiah 61:9"> They said, “We saw plainly that the LORD was with you. We said, ‘Let there now be an oath between us, even between us and you, and let’s make a covenant with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 24:31; Genesis 21:22; Genesis 22:17"> that you will do us no harm, as we have not touched you, and as we have done to you nothing but good, and have sent you away in peace.’ You are now the blessed of the LORD.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Romans 12:18; Hebrews 12:14"> He made them a feast, and they ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:16–17; Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 25:33"> They rose up some time in the morning, and swore an oath to one another. Isaac sent them away, and they departed from him in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 2:4–5"> The same day, Isaac’s servants came, and told him concerning the well which they had dug, and said to him, “We have found water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Genesis 26:28"> He called it “Shibah”.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Shibah means “oath” or “seven”.">e</a></sup>Therefore the name of the city is “Beersheba”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Beersheba means “well of the oath” or “well of the seven”">f</a></sup>to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 36:13; Exodus 34:16"> When Esau was forty years old, he took as wife Judith, the daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Basemath, the daughter of Elon the Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 27:46–2; Genesis 28:8; Genesis 6:2"> They grieved Isaac’s and Rebekah’s spirits.</span>
</div>
