<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:6; Zephaniah 3:9; Isaiah 19:18"> The whole earth was of one language and of one speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2; Genesis 14:1"> As they travelled east,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="LXX reads “from the east”.">a</a></sup>they found a plain in the land of Shinar, and they lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:10; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 2:3"> They said to one another, “Come, let’s make bricks, and burn them thoroughly.” They had brick for stone, and they used tar for mortar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Yochanan 5:44; Luke 1:51"> They said, “Come, let’s build ourselves a city, and a tower whose top reaches to the sky, and let’s make a name for ourselves, lest we be scattered abroad on the surface of the whole earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:21; Psalms 33:13–14; Yochanan 3:13"> The LORD came down to see the city and the tower, which the children of men built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–4; Genesis 3:22; Genesis 11:1"> The LORD said, “Behold, they are one people, and they all have one language, and this is what they begin to do. Now nothing will be withheld from them, which they intend to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Psalms 55:9; Job 5:12–13"> Come, let’s go down, and there confuse their language, that they may not understand one another’s speech.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Deuteronomy 32:8; Genesis 10:25"> So the LORD scattered them abroad from there on the surface of all the earth. They stopped building the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Genesis 10:5; Genesis 10:20"> Therefore its name was called Babel, because there the LORD confused the language of all the earth. From there, the LORD scattered them abroad on the surface of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:17–27; Genesis 10:21–22; Luke 3:34–36"> This is the history of the generations of Shem: Shem was one hundred years old when he became the father of Arpachshad two years after the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Psalms 144:12; Genesis 1:28"> Shem lived five hundred years after he became the father of Arpachshad, and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:36"> Arpachshad lived thirty-five years and became the father of Shelah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Arpachshad lived four hundred and three years after he became the father of Shelah, and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Shelah lived thirty years, and became the father of Eber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Shelah lived four hundred and three years after he became the father of Eber, and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Chronicles 1:19; Numbers 24:24"> Eber lived thirty-four years, and became the father of Peleg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Eber lived four hundred and thirty years after he became the father of Peleg, and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> Peleg lived thirty years, and became the father of Reu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Peleg lived two hundred and nine years after he became the father of Reu, and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> Reu lived thirty-two years, and became the father of Serug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Reu lived two hundred and seven years after he became the father of Serug, and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 24:2"> Serug lived thirty years, and became the father of Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Serug lived two hundred years after he became the father of Nahor, and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 3:34; Joshua 24:2"> Nahor lived twenty-nine years, and became the father of Terah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Nahor lived one hundred and nineteen years after he became the father of Terah, and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 24:2; 1 Chronicles 1:26–27; Genesis 22:20–24"> Terah lived seventy years, and became the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 12:4; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 11:31"> Now this is the history of the generations of Terah. Terah became the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran. Haran became the father of Lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:7; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4"> Haran died in the land of his birth, in Ur-Kasdim, while his father Terah was still alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:15; Genesis 22:20; Genesis 24:15"> Abram and Nahor married wives. The name of Abram’s wife was Sarai, and the name of Nahor’s wife was Milcah, the daughter of Haran, who was also the father of Iscah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Genesis 21:1–2; Luke 1:7"> Sarai was barren. She had no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4; Genesis 24:10"> Terah took Abram his son, Lot the son of Haran, his son’s son, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, his son Abram’s wife. They went from Ur-Kasdim, to go into the land of Canaan. They came to Haran and lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> The days of Terah were two hundred and five years. Terah died in Haran.</span>
