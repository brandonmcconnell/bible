<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 2:17"> When Jacob saw Rachel the daughter of Laban, his mother’s brother, and the sheep of Laban, his mother’s brother, Jacob went near, and rolled the stone from the well’s mouth, and watered the flock of Laban his mother’s brother.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:28; Genesis 13:8; Genesis 14:14–16"> Jacob told Rachel that he was her father’s relative, and that he was Rebekah’s son. She ran and told her father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Acts 20:37; Romans 16:16"> When Laban heard the news of Jacob, his sister’s son, he ran to meet Jacob, and embraced him, and kissed him, and brought him to his house. Jacob told Laban all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:12–13; Genesis 2:23; Judges 9:2"> Laban said to him, “Surely you are my bone and my flesh.” Jacob stayed with him for a month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 30:28; Genesis 31:7"> Laban said to Jacob, “Because you are my relative, should you therefore serve me for nothing? Tell me, what will your wages be?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 12:11; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 24:16"> Leah’s eyes were weak, but Rachel was beautiful in form and attractive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 12:12; Exodus 22:16–17; Genesis 34:12"> Jacob loved Rachel. He said, “I will serve you seven years for Rachel, your younger daughter.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 6:5; Psalms 12:2"> Laban said, “It is better that I give her to you, than that I should give her to another man. Stay with me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7; Ephesians 5:2; Hosea 12:12"> Jacob served seven years for Rachel. They seemed to him but a few days, for the love he had for her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 15:1; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 4:1"> Jacob said to Laban, “Give me my wife, for my days are fulfilled, that I may go in to her.”</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Laban said, “It is not done so in our place, to give the younger before the firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 18:18; Judges 14:12; Genesis 2:2–3"> Fulfil the week of this one, and we will give you the other also for the service which you will serve with me for seven more years.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Jacob did so, and fulfilled her week. He gave him Rachel his daughter as wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 29:24; Genesis 30:3–8"> Laban gave Bilhah, his servant, to his daughter Rachel to be her servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:20; Genesis 31:15"> He went in also to Rachel, and he loved also Rachel more than Leah, and served with him seven more years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 21:15"> The LORD saw that Leah was hated, and he opened her womb, but Rachel was barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:7; Exodus 4:31; Exodus 3:7"> Leah conceived, and bore a son, and she named him Reuben. For she said, “Because the LORD has looked at my affliction; for now my husband will love me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 34:25; Genesis 49:5–6; Genesis 34:30"> She conceived again, and bore a son, and said, “Because the LORD has heard that I am hated, he has therefore given me this son also.” She named him Simeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 32:26–29; Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 46:11"> She conceived again, and bore a son. She said, “Now this time my husband will be joined to me, because I have borne him three sons.” Therefore his name was called Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; 1 Chronicles 5:2; Genesis 49:8–12"> She conceived again, and bore a son. She said, “This time I will praise the LORD.” Therefore she named him Judah. Then she stopped bearing.</span>
