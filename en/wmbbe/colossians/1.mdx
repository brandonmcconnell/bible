<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 1:2; Colossians 1:9; 2 Timothy 1:3"> We give thanks to God the Father of our Lord Yeshua the Messiah, praying always for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:15; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Thessalonians 1:3"> having heard of your faith in Messiah Yeshua and of the love which you have towards all the holy ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:3–4; Ephesians 1:13; 2 Timothy 4:8"> because of the hope which is laid up for you in the heavens, of which you heard before in the word of the truth of the Good News</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Yochanan 15:16; Philippians 1:11; Colossians 1:23"> which has come to you, even as it is in all the world and is bearing fruit and growing, as it does in you also, since the day you heard and knew the grace of God in truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 4:12; Philemon 1:23; Colossians 4:7"> even as you learnt from Epaphras our beloved fellow servant, who is a faithful servant of Messiah on your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="NU reads our">c</a></sup>behalf,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 15:30; 2 Timothy 1:7; Galatians 5:22"> who also declared to us your love in the Spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jacob 3:17; Philippians 1:9–11; Jacob 1:5"> For this cause, we also, since the day we heard this, don’t cease praying and making requests for you, that you may be filled with the knowledge of his will in all spiritual wisdom and understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ephesians 4:1; Hebrews 13:21; 2 Peter 1:2–3"> that you may walk worthily of the Lord, to please him in all respects, bearing fruit in every good work and increasing in the knowledge of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 3:16; Philippians 4:13; Ephesians 4:2"> strengthened with all power, according to the might of his glory, for all endurance and perseverance with joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 20:32; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:18"> giving thanks to the Father, who made us fit to be partakers of the inheritance of the holy ones in light,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Acts 26:18; 1 Peter 2:9"> who delivered us out of the power of darkness, and translated us into the Kingdom of the Son of his love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ephesians 1:7; Romans 3:24–25; 1 Peter 1:19–20"> in whom we have our redemption,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="TR adds “through his blood,”">d</a></sup>the forgiveness of our sins.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; Yochanan 14:9; Yochanan 1:1"> He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Yochanan 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> For by him all things were created in the heavens and on the earth, visible things and invisible things, whether thrones or dominions or principalities or powers. All things have been created through him and for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 1:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; Acts 17:28"> He is before all things, and in him all things are held together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ephesians 1:22–23; Revelation 1:5; Ephesians 4:15–16"> He is the head of the body, the assembly, who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, that in all things he might have the preeminence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 2:9; Yochanan 1:16; Ephesians 1:23"> For all the fullness was pleased to dwell in him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:18–21; Ephesians 1:10; 1 Yochanan 4:9–10"> and through him to reconcile all things to himself by him, whether things on the earth or things in the heavens, having made peace through the blood of his cross.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 2:12; Titus 3:3–7; Ephesians 2:1–3"> You, being in past times alienated and enemies in your mind in your evil deeds,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Judah 1:24; Ephesians 1:4"> yet now he has reconciled in the body of his flesh through death, to present you holy and without defect and blameless before him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Hebrews 3:6; Matthew 24:13–14"> if it is so that you continue in the faith, grounded and steadfast, and not moved away from the hope of the Good News which you heard, which was proclaimed in all creation under heaven, of which I, Paul, was made a servant.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 5:3; 2 Timothy 1:8; 2 Corinthians 1:5–8"> Now I rejoice in my sufferings for your sake, and fill up on my part that which is lacking of the afflictions of Messiah in my flesh for his body’s sake, which is the assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ephesians 3:2; Colossians 1:23; Galatians 2:7–8"> of which I was made a servant according to the stewardship of God which was given me towards you to fulfil the word of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 8:10; Romans 16:25–26; Ephesians 3:3–10"> the mystery which has been hidden for ages and generations. But now it has been revealed to his holy ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Yochanan 14:20; Romans 8:10; Ephesians 3:16–17"> to whom God was pleased to make known what are the riches of the glory of this mystery amongst the Gentiles, which is Messiah in you, the hope of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:27; Jeremiah 3:15; Ephesians 4:11–13"> We proclaim him, admonishing every man and teaching every man in all wisdom, that we may present every man perfect in Messiah Yeshua;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:10; Ephesians 1:19; 1 Corinthians 9:25–27"> for which I also labour, striving according to his working, which works in me mightily.</span>
</div>
