<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 3:3–4; Luke 24:48; Romans 8:17–18"> Therefore I exhort the elders amongst you, as a fellow elder and a witness of the sufferings of Messiah, and who will also share in the glory that will be revealed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:7; 1 Timothy 3:8; Titus 3:1"> shepherd the flock of God which is amongst you, exercising the oversight, not under compulsion, but voluntarily; not for dishonest gain, but willingly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:9; Philippians 3:17; Ezekiel 34:4"> not as lording it over those entrusted to you, but making yourselves examples to the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jacob 1:12; 1 Corinthians 9:25; 1 Yochanan 3:2"> When the chief Shepherd is revealed, you will receive the crown of glory that doesn’t fade away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jacob 4:6; Proverbs 3:34; Ephesians 5:21"> Likewise, you younger ones, be subject to the elder. Yes, all of you clothe yourselves with humility and subject yourselves to one another; for “God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jacob 4:10; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 29:23"> Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in due time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 55:22; Philippians 4:6; Psalms 37:5"> casting all your worries on him, because he cares for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:11; Jacob 4:7; Ephesians 4:27"> Be sober and self-controlled. Be watchful. Your adversary, the devil, walks around like a roaring lion, seeking whom he may devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jacob 4:7; Acts 14:22; Ephesians 6:11–13"> Withstand him steadfast in your faith, knowing that your brothers who are in the world are undergoing the same sufferings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Peter 1:6–7"> But may the God of all grace, who called you to his eternal glory by Messiah Yeshua, after you have suffered a little while, perfect, establish, strengthen, and settle you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 4:11; Romans 11:36; Revelation 1:6"> To him be the glory and the power forever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 13:22; Acts 11:23; 1 Corinthians 15:1"> Through Silvanus, our faithful brother, as I consider him, I have written to you briefly, exhorting and testifying that this is the true grace of God in which you stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Acts 12:25; Psalms 87:4"> She who is in Babylon, chosen together with you, greets you. So does Mark, my son.</span>
