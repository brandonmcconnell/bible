<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 10:42; 2 Timothy 4:8; 1 Timothy 5:21"> I command you therefore before God and the Lord Yeshua the Messiah, who will judge the living and the dead at his appearing and his Kingdom:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:14; 1 Timothy 4:15–16; Titus 2:15"> proclaim the word; be urgent in season and out of season; reprove, rebuke, and exhort with all patience and teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; 2 Timothy 3:1–6; 1 Timothy 4:1–3"> For the time will come when they will not listen to the sound doctrine, but having itching ears, will heap up for themselves teachers after their own lusts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 7:11; 1 Timothy 1:4; Acts 7:57"> and will turn away their ears from the truth, and turn away to fables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:8; 1 Peter 1:13; 2 Timothy 2:3"> But you be sober in all things, suffer hardship, do the work of an evangelist, and fulfil your ministry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:23; Philippians 2:17; 2 Peter 1:14–15"> For I am already being offered, and the time of my departure has come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; 1 Corinthians 9:24–27; Hebrews 12:1–2"> I have fought the good fight. I have finished the course. I have kept the faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jacob 1:12; Revelation 2:10; 1 Peter 5:4"> From now on, the crown of righteousness is stored up for me, which the Lord, the righteous judge, will give to me on that day; and not to me only, but also to all those who have loved his appearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:4; 2 Timothy 4:21"> Be diligent to come to me soon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Yochanan 2:15–16; 2 Timothy 1:15; Colossians 4:14–15"> for Demas left me, having loved this present world, and went to Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia; and Titus to Dalmatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 12:12; Philemon 1:24; Acts 12:25"> Only Luke is with me. Take Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 6:21; Acts 20:4; Titus 3:12"> But I sent Tychicus to Ephesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:11; 2 Corinthians 11:27; Acts 16:11"> Bring the cloak that I left at Troas with Carpus when you come—and the books, especially the parchments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 1:6; Psalms 28:4"> Alexander the coppersmith did much evil to me. The Lord will repay him according to his deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:8; Philippians 3:2; Matthew 10:16–17"> Beware of him, for he greatly opposed our words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 7:60; Yochanan 16:32; Mark 14:50"> At my first defence, no one came to help me, but all left me. May it not be held against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Philippians 1:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:37"> But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, that through me the message might be fully proclaimed, and that all the Gentiles might hear. So I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 2 Thessalonians 3:3; Judah 1:24–25"> And the Lord will deliver me from every evil work and will preserve me for his heavenly Kingdom. To him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 18:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19; Romans 16:3–4"> Greet Prisca and Aquila, and the house of Onesiphorus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Acts 19:22; Philippians 2:26–27"> Erastus remained at Corinth, but I left Trophimus at Miletus sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:9; Philippians 4:22; 3 Yochanan 1:14"> Be diligent to come before winter. Eubulus salutes you, as do Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philemon 1:25; Galatians 6:18; Colossians 4:18"> The Lord Yeshua the Messiah be with your spirit. Grace be with you. Amen.</span>
