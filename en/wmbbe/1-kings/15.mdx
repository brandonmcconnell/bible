<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 14:31; 2 Chronicles 13:1–22"> Now in the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam the son of Nebat, Abijam began to reign over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2; 2 Chronicles 11:20–22; 1 Kings 15:13"> He reigned three years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Maacah the daughter of Abishalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; Psalms 119:80; 1 Kings 3:14"> He walked in all the sins of his father, which he had done before him; and his heart was not perfect with the LORD his God, as the heart of David his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 2 Chronicles 21:7; Isaiah 37:35"> Nevertheless for David’s sake, the LORD his God gave him a lamp in Jerusalem, to set up his son after him and to establish Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 14:8; 2 Samuel 11:15–17; 1 Kings 15:3"> because David did that which was right in the LORD’s eyes, and didn’t turn away from anything that he commanded him all the days of his life, except only in the matter of Uriah the Hittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 14:30"> Now there was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2–22; 1 Kings 14:29"> The rest of the acts of Abijam, and all that he did, aren’t they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? There was war between Abijam and Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:1; 1 Kings 14:31; Matthew 1:7–8"> Abijam slept with his fathers, and they buried him in David’s city; and Asa his son reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> In the twentieth year of Jeroboam king of Israel, Asa began to reign over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 15:2; 1 Kings 15:13; 2 Chronicles 13:2"> He reigned forty-one years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Maacah the daughter of Abishalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:2; 2 Chronicles 15:17; 1 Kings 15:3"> Asa did that which was right in the LORD’s eyes, as David his father did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 22:46; 1 Kings 14:23–24; 2 Chronicles 14:2–5"> He put away the sodomites out of the land, and removed all the idols that his fathers had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; Deuteronomy 7:5; Zechariah 13:3"> He also removed Maacah his mother from being queen, because she had made an abominable image for an Asherah. Asa cut down her image and burnt it at the brook Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 22:43; 1 Kings 8:61; 1 Kings 15:3"> But the high places were not taken away. Nevertheless the heart of Asa was perfect with the LORD all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 2 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Chronicles 15:18"> He brought into the LORD’s house the things that his father had dedicated, and the things that he himself had dedicated: silver, gold, and utensils.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:32; 1 Kings 14:30; 2 Chronicles 16:1–6"> There was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all their days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 12:27; 2 Chronicles 16:1–6; Joshua 18:25"> Baasha king of Israel went up against Judah, and built Ramah, that he might not allow anyone to go out or come in to Asa king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 14:26; 2 Kings 12:18; 1 Kings 11:23–24"> Then Asa took all the silver and the gold that was left in the treasures of the LORD’s house, and the treasures of the king’s house, and delivered it into the hand of his servants. Then King Asa sent them to Ben Hadad, the son of Tabrimmon, the son of Hezion, king of Syria, who lived at Damascus, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:31; 2 Chronicles 16:7; Romans 3:8"> “Let there be a treaty between me and you, like that between my father and your father. Behold, I have sent to you a present of silver and gold. Go, break your treaty with Baasha king of Israel, that he may depart from me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; Judges 18:29; Joshua 11:2"> Ben Hadad listened to King Asa, and sent the captains of his armies against the cities of Israel, and struck Ijon, and Dan, and Abel Beth Maacah, and all Chinneroth, with all the land of Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 14:17; 1 Kings 16:15–18; 2 Chronicles 16:5"> When Baasha heard of it, he stopped building Ramah, and lived in Tirzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 21:17; Joshua 18:24; Joshua 18:26"> Then King Asa made a proclamation to all Judah. No one was exempted. They carried away the stones of Ramah, and its timber, with which Baasha had built; and King Asa used it to build Geba of Benjamin, and Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Chronicles 16:11–14; 1 Kings 14:29–31; 1 Kings 15:7–8"> Now the rest of all the acts of Asa, and all his might, and all that he did, and the cities which he built, aren’t they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah? But in the time of his old age he was diseased in his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 1:8; 1 Kings 22:41–43; 2 Chronicles 17:1–9"> Asa slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in his father David’s city; and Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 14:20; 1 Kings 14:12"> Nadab the son of Jeroboam began to reign over Israel in the second year of Asa king of Judah; and he reigned over Israel two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 15:34; 1 Kings 15:30"> He did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, and walked in the way of his father, and in his sin with which he made Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:44; Joshua 21:23; 1 Kings 14:14"> Baasha the son of Ahijah, of the house of Issachar, conspired against him; and Baasha struck him at Gibbethon, which belonged to the Philistines; for Nadab and all Israel were besieging Gibbethon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:35"> Even in the third year of Asa king of Judah, Baasha killed him and reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 14:9–16; 2 Kings 19:25; 2 Kings 10:10–11"> As soon as he was king, he struck all the house of Jeroboam. He didn’t leave to Jeroboam any who breathed, until he had destroyed him, according to the saying of the LORD, which he spoke by his servant Ahijah the Shilonite;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 15:26; 1 Kings 14:22; 1 Kings 14:9–16"> for the sins of Jeroboam which he sinned, and with which he made Israel to sin, because of his provocation with which he provoked the LORD, the God of Israel, to anger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Kings 14:19; 1 Kings 16:20; 1 Kings 16:14"> Now the rest of the acts of Nadab, and all that he did, aren’t they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 15:16"> There was war between Asa and Baasha king of Israel all their days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Kings 16:8"> In the third year of Asa king of Judah, Baasha the son of Ahijah began to reign over all Israel in Tirzah for twenty-four years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Kings 15:26; 1 Kings 14:16; 1 Kings 12:28–29"> He did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, and walked in the way of Jeroboam, and in his sin with which he made Israel to sin.</span>
</div>
