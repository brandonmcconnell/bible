<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 2:20–22; 2 Corinthians 7:7–11; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> You are arrogant, and didn’t mourn instead, that he who had done this deed might be removed from amongst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:17; 2 Corinthians 13:2"> For I most certainly, as being absent in body but present in spirit, have already, as though I were present, judged him who has done this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Yochanan 20:23; Colossians 3:17; Matthew 16:19"> In the name of our Lord Yeshua the Messiah, when you are gathered together with my spirit with the power of our Lord Yeshua the Messiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:14–15; Galatians 6:1–2"> you are to deliver such a one to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Yeshua.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:9; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Jacob 4:16"> Your boasting is not good. Don’t you know that a little yeast leavens the whole lump?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Peter 1:19–20; Ephesians 4:22; Colossians 3:5–9"> Purge out the old yeast, that you may be a new lump, even as you are unleavened. For indeed Messiah, our Passover, has been sacrificed in our place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Peter 2:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:3; Exodus 12:15"> Therefore let’s keep the feast, not with old yeast, neither with the yeast of malice and wickedness, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Corinthians 6:14"> I wrote to you in my letter to have no company with sexual sinners;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:27; Yochanan 17:15–16; Matthew 5:14–16"> yet not at all meaning with the sexual sinners of this world, or with the covetous and extortionists, or with idolaters, for then you would have to leave the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 16:17; 2 Thessalonians 3:14; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> But as it is, I wrote to you not to associate with anyone who is called a brother who is a sexual sinner, or covetous, or an idolater, or a slanderer, or a drunkard, or an extortionist. Don’t even eat with such a person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:11; 1 Timothy 3:7; Luke 12:14"> For what do I have to do with also judging those who are outside? Don’t you judge those who are within?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Deuteronomy 13:5; Deuteronomy 21:21"> But those who are outside, God judges. “Put away the wicked man from amongst yourselves.”</span>
