<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:1; 1 Chronicles 17:1–5; Psalms 132:5"> David made himself houses in David’s city; and he prepared a place for God’s ark, and pitched a tent for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Deuteronomy 31:9; Numbers 18:1–8"> Then David said, “No one ought to carry God’s ark but the Levites. For the LORD has chosen them to carry God’s ark, and to minister to him forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:5; 1 Chronicles 15:1"> David assembled all Israel at Jerusalem, to bring up the LORD’s ark to its place, which he had prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 6:16–22; 1 Chronicles 12:26–28; 1 Chronicles 6:16–30"> David gathered together the sons of Aaron and the Levites:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:22–24"> of the sons of Kohath, Uriel the chief, and his brothers one hundred and twenty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:29–30"> of the sons of Merari, Asaiah the chief, and his brothers two hundred and twenty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:11; 1 Chronicles 23:8"> of the sons of Gershom, Joel the chief, and his brothers one hundred and thirty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 6:22"> of the sons of Elizaphan, Shemaiah the chief, and his brothers two hundred;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:19; 1 Chronicles 26:30–31"> of the sons of Hebron, Eliel the chief, and his brothers eighty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> of the sons of Uzziel, Amminadab the chief, and his brothers one hundred and twelve.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:28; 1 Kings 2:35; 1 Samuel 22:20–23"> David called for Zadok and Abiathar the priests, and for the Levites: for Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:6; Exodus 19:14–15; 1 Chronicles 15:14"> and said to them, “You are the heads of the fathers’ households of the Levites. Sanctify yourselves, both you and your brothers, that you may bring the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel, up to the place that I have prepared for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:3; 1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 Yochanan 1:8–10"> For because you didn’t carry it at first, the LORD our God broke out in anger against us, because we didn’t seek him according to the ordinance.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:15; Joel 2:16–17; 2 Chronicles 29:34"> So the priests and the Levites sanctified themselves to bring up the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Numbers 7:9; 2 Chronicles 5:9"> The children of the Levites bore God’s ark on their shoulders with its poles, as Moses commanded according to the LORD’s word.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 2 Chronicles 5:13; 1 Chronicles 16:42"> David spoke to the chief of the Levites to appoint their brothers as singers with instruments of music, stringed instruments, harps, and cymbals, sounding aloud and lifting up their voices with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 6:44; 1 Chronicles 6:33"> So the Levites appointed Heman the son of Joel; and of his brothers, Asaph the son of Berechiah; and of the sons of Merari their brothers, Ethan the son of Kushaiah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4; 1 Chronicles 26:8; 1 Chronicles 13:14"> and with them their brothers of the second rank: Zechariah, Ben, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, and Jeiel, the doorkeepers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 25:6; 1 Chronicles 16:5; 1 Chronicles 13:8"> So the singers, Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, were given cymbals of bronze to sound aloud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:1–11"> and Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah, with stringed instruments set to Alamoth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5; 1 Chronicles 25:6–7; Psalms 81:1–2"> and Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah, with harps tuned to the eight-stringed lyre, to lead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:27; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 1 Chronicles 25:7–8"> Chenaniah, chief of the Levites, was over the singing. He taught the singers, because he was skilful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 84:10; 2 Kings 22:4; 1 Chronicles 9:21–23"> Berechiah and Elkanah were doorkeepers for the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:6; Numbers 10:8; 1 Chronicles 15:28"> Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer, the priests, blew the shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, trumpets">a</a></sup>before God’s ark; and Obed-Edom and Jehiah were doorkeepers for the ark.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:12–23; Micah 5:2; Philippians 3:3"> So David, the elders of Israel, and the captains over thousands went to bring the ark of the LORD’s covenant up out of the house of Obed-Edom with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 23:29; 1 Chronicles 29:14; Psalms 66:13–15"> When God helped the Levites who bore the ark of the LORD’s covenant, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 6:14"> David was clothed with a robe of fine linen, as were all the Levites who bore the ark, the singers, and Chenaniah the choir master with the singers; and David had an ephod of linen on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; 1 Chronicles 15:16; 2 Chronicles 5:12–13"> Thus all Israel brought the ark of the LORD’s covenant up with shouting, with sound of the cornet, with shofars<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="or, trumpets">b</a></sup>, and with cymbals, sounding aloud with stringed instruments and harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:20–23; 2 Samuel 6:16; Deuteronomy 31:26"> As the ark of the LORD’s covenant came to David’s city, Michal the daughter of Saul looked out at the window, and saw king David dancing and playing; and she despised him in her heart.</span>
</div>
