<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:39; Exodus 20:5; 1 Samuel 14:6"> The Philistines followed hard after Saul and after his sons; and the Philistines killed Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 49:23–24; 1 Samuel 31:3–6; 2 Samuel 1:4–10"> The battle went hard against Saul, and the archers overtook him; and he was distressed by reason of the archers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:4; Judges 9:54; 1 Samuel 17:26"> Then Saul said to his armour bearer, “Draw your sword, and thrust me through with it, lest these uncircumcised come and abuse me.” But his armour bearer would not, for he was terrified. Therefore Saul took his sword and fell on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When his armour bearer saw that Saul was dead, he likewise fell on his sword and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 13:10–11; 1 Samuel 4:10–11; 1 Samuel 4:18"> So Saul died with his three sons; and all his house died together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36; Deuteronomy 28:33; Judges 6:2"> When all the men of Israel who were in the valley saw that they fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned their cities, and fled; and the Philistines came and lived in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:8; 2 Kings 3:23; 2 Chronicles 20:25"> On the next day, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, they found Saul and his sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:23; Judges 16:23–24; Matthew 14:11"> They stripped him and took his head and his armour, then sent into the land of the Philistines all around to carry the news to their idols and to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:2–7; 1 Samuel 31:10"> They put his armour in the house of their gods, and fastened his head in the house of Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:11–13; 1 Samuel 11:1–11; 2 Samuel 2:4–7"> When all Jabesh Gilead heard all that the Philistines had done to Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; 2 Samuel 3:35; Genesis 50:10"> all the valiant men arose and took away the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons, and brought them to Jabesh, and buried their bones under the oak in Jabesh, and fasted seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 20:6; Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 15:23"> So Saul died for his trespass which he committed against the LORD, because of the LORD’s word, which he didn’t keep, and also because he asked counsel of one who had a familiar spirit, to enquire,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; 1 Samuel 13:14; 1 Samuel 28:17"> and didn’t enquire of the LORD. Therefore he killed him, and turned the kingdom over to David the son of Jesse.</span>
