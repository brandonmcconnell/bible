<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; 2 Kings 15:29; 2 Chronicles 28:20"> and Beerah his son, whom Tilgath Pilneser king of Assyria carried away captive. He was prince of the Reubenites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:17"> His brothers by their families, when the genealogy of their generations was listed: the chief, Jeiel, and Zechariah,</span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 22:8–9"> and he lived eastward even to the entrance of the wilderness from the river Euphrates, because their livestock were multiplied in the land of Gilead.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Their brothers of their fathers’ houses: Michael, Meshullam, Sheba, Jorai, Jacan, Zia, and Eber, seven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> These were the sons of Abihail, the son of Huri, the son of Jaroah, the son of Gilead, the son of Michael, the son of Jeshishai, the son of Jahdo, the son of Buz;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Ahi the son of Abdiel, the son of Guni, chief of their fathers’ houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:29; Song of Solomon 2:1; Isaiah 35:2"> They lived in Gilead in Bashan and in its towns, and in all the pasture lands of Sharon as far as their borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:16; 2 Kings 14:28; 2 Kings 15:32"> All these were listed by genealogies in the days of Jotham king of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam king of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:3; Joshua 4:12–13"> The sons of Reuben, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, of valiant men, men able to bear buckler and sword, able to shoot with bow, and skilful in war, were forty-four thousand and seven hundred and sixty that were able to go out to war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 25:15; 1 Chronicles 1:31; 1 Chronicles 5:10"> They made war with the Hagrites, with Jetur, and Naphish, and Nodab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 22:4–5; Psalms 9:10; 2 Chronicles 18:31"> They were helped against them, and the Hagrites were delivered into their hand, and all who were with them; for they cried to God in the battle, and he answered them because they put their trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:13; Numbers 31:35"> They took away their livestock: of their camels fifty thousand, and of sheep two hundred and fifty thousand, and of donkeys two thousand, and of men one hundred thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:8; Romans 8:31; Joshua 23:10"> For many fell slain, because the war was of God. They lived in their place until the captivity.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:8–9; Song of Solomon 4:8; Psalms 133:3"> The children of the half-tribe of Manasseh lived in the land. They increased from Bashan to Baal Hermon, Senir, and Mount Hermon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 4:38"> These were the heads of their fathers’ houses: Epher, Ishi, Eliel, Azriel, Jeremiah, Hodaviah, and Jahdiel—mighty men of valour, famous men, heads of their fathers’ houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Revelation 17:5; Psalms 106:34–39; Exodus 34:15"> They trespassed against the God of their fathers, and played the prostitute after the gods of the peoples of the land whom God destroyed before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 15:19; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 15:29"> So the God of Israel stirred up the spirit of Pul king of Assyria, and the spirit of Tilgath Pilneser king of Assyria, and he carried away the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, and brought them to Halah, Habor, Hara, and to the river of Gozan, to this day.</span>
