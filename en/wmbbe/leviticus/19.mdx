<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44–45; Leviticus 20:7; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> “Speak to all the congregation of the children of Israel, and tell them, ‘You shall be holy; for I, the LORD your God, am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Exodus 20:8; Hebrews 12:9"> “‘Each one of you shall respect his mother and his father. You shall keep my Sabbaths. I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:23; Psalms 115:4–7"> “‘Don’t turn to idols, nor make molten gods for yourselves. I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:19; Leviticus 22:29; Leviticus 7:16"> “‘When you offer a sacrifice of peace offerings to the LORD, you shall offer it so that you may be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:11–17"> It shall be eaten the same day you offer it, and on the next day. If anything remains until the third day, it shall be burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 7:18–21; Leviticus 22:25; Jeremiah 16:18"> If it is eaten at all on the third day, it is an abomination. It will not be accepted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 22:15; Leviticus 5:1"> but everyone who eats it shall bear his iniquity, because he has profaned the holy thing of the LORD, and that soul shall be cut off from his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Deuteronomy 24:19–22; Ruth 2:2"> “‘When you reap the harvest of your land, you shall not wholly reap the corners of your field, neither shall you gather the gleanings of your harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:6; Leviticus 25:6; Isaiah 24:13"> You shall not glean your vineyard, neither shall you gather the fallen grapes of your vineyard. You shall leave them for the poor and for the foreigner. I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 101:7; Ephesians 4:25; Colossians 3:9"> “‘You shall not steal. “‘You shall not lie. “‘You shall not deceive one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 18:21"> “‘You shall not swear by my name falsely, and profane the name of your God. I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jacob 5:4; Malachi 3:5; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> “‘You shall not oppress your neighbour, nor rob him. “‘The wages of a hired servant shall not remain with you all night until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:17"> “‘You shall not curse the deaf, nor put a stumbling block before the blind; but you shall fear your God. I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Proverbs 24:23; Deuteronomy 27:19"> “‘You shall do no injustice in judgement. You shall not be partial to the poor, nor show favouritism to the great; but you shall judge your neighbour in righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 20:19"> “‘You shall not go around as a slanderer amongst your people. “‘You shall not endanger the life<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="literally, “blood”">a</a></sup>of your neighbour. I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Yochanan 2:9; 1 Yochanan 2:11; Luke 17:3"> “‘You shall not hate your brother in your heart. You shall surely rebuke your neighbour, and not bear sin because of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 22:39–40; Mark 12:31–34"> “‘You shall not take vengeance, nor bear any grudge against the children of your people; but you shall love your neighbour as yourself. I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Deuteronomy 22:9–11; Matthew 9:16–17"> “‘You shall keep my statutes. “‘You shall not cross-breed different kinds of animals. “‘You shall not sow your field with two kinds of seed; “‘Don’t wear a garment made of two kinds of material.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:23–24; Exodus 21:20–21"> “‘If a man lies carnally with a woman who is a slave girl, pledged to be married to another man, and not ransomed or given her freedom; they shall be punished. They shall not be put to death, because she was not free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 5:1–6; Leviticus 5:15"> He shall bring his trespass offering to the LORD, to the door of the Tent of Meeting, even a ram for a trespass offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> The priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the trespass offering before the LORD for his sin which he has committed; and the sin which he has committed shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Acts 7:51; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> “‘When you come into the land, and have planted all kinds of trees for food, then you shall count their fruit as forbidden.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="literally, “uncircumcised”">b</a></sup>For three years it shall be forbidden to you. It shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9; Deuteronomy 18:4; Numbers 18:12–13"> But in the fourth year all its fruit shall be holy, for giving praise to the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Haggai 1:9–11; Malachi 3:8–10; Leviticus 26:3–4"> In the fifth year you shall eat its fruit, that it may yield its increase to you. I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–14; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Chronicles 33:6"> “‘You shall not eat any meat with the blood still in it. You shall not use enchantments, nor practise sorcery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Deuteronomy 14:1; Ezekiel 44:20"> “‘You shall not cut the hair on the sides of your head or clip off the edge of your beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Leviticus 21:5; 1 Kings 18:28"> “‘You shall not make any cuttings in your flesh for the dead, nor tattoo any marks on you. I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17–18; Leviticus 21:7; Hosea 4:12–14"> “‘Don’t profane your daughter, to make her a prostitute; lest the land fall to prostitution, and the land become full of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> “‘You shall keep my Sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary; I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Leviticus 20:27; Leviticus 20:6–7"> “‘Don’t turn to those who are mediums, nor to the wizards. Don’t seek them out, to be defiled by them. I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:1; Lamentations 5:12; Proverbs 16:31"> “‘You shall rise up before the grey head and honour the face of the elderly; and you shall fear your God. I am the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21; Malachi 3:5"> “‘If a stranger lives as a foreigner with you in your land, you shall not do him wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 12:48–49"> The stranger who lives as a foreigner with you shall be to you as the native-born amongst you, and you shall love him as yourself; for you lived as foreigners in the land of Egypt. I am the LORD your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:15; Deuteronomy 25:13; Proverbs 16:11"> “‘You shall do no unrighteousness in judgement, in measures of length, of weight, or of quantity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Exodus 20:2; Deuteronomy 25:13–15"> You shall have just balances, just weights, a just efah,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="1 efah is about 22 litres or about 2/3 of a bushel">c</a></sup>and a just hin.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="A hin is about 6.5 litres or 1.7 gallons.">d</a></sup>I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:1; Leviticus 18:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:1"> “‘You shall observe all my statutes and all my ordinances, and do them. I am the LORD.’”</span>
