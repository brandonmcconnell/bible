<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:1; Jeremiah 25:1; Jeremiah 25:9"> In his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up, and Jehoiakim became his servant three years. Then he turned and rebelled against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:11; 2 Kings 23:27; 2 Kings 21:12–14"> The LORD sent against him bands of the Kasdim, bands of the Syrians, bands of the Moabites, and bands of the children of Ammon, and sent them against Judah to destroy it, according to the LORD’s word which he spoke by his servants the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:25; 2 Kings 21:2–11; 2 Kings 23:26–27"> Surely at the commandment of the LORD this came on Judah, to remove them out of his sight for the sins of Manasseh, according to all that he did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Psalms 106:38; Numbers 35:33"> and also for the innocent blood that he shed; for he filled Jerusalem with innocent blood, and the LORD would not pardon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:8; Jeremiah 26:1–24; Jeremiah 22:13–17"> Now the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and all that he did, aren’t they written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:18–19; 2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 36:30"> So Jehoiakim slept with his fathers, and Jehoiachin his son reigned in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:5–7; Jeremiah 46:2; Genesis 15:18"> The king of Egypt didn’t come out of his land any more; for the king of Babylon had taken, from the brook of Egypt to the river Euphrates, all that belonged to the king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:9; 1 Chronicles 3:16; Jeremiah 37:1"> Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem three months. His mother’s name was Nehushta the daughter of Elnathan of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, according to all that his father had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 25:2; Daniel 1:1–2"> At that time the servants of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up to Jerusalem, and the city was besieged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to the city while his servants were besieging it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:1; Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 25:27"> and Jehoiachin the king of Judah went out to the king of Babylon—he, his mother, his servants, his princes, and his officers; and the king of Babylon captured him in the eighth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 39:6; 2 Kings 20:17; Jeremiah 20:5"> He carried out from there all the treasures of the LORD’s house and the treasures of the king’s house, and cut in pieces all the vessels of gold which Solomon king of Israel had made in the LORD’s temple, as the LORD had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 25:12; 2 Kings 24:16"> He carried away all Jerusalem, and all the princes, and all the mighty men of valour, even ten thousand captives, and all the craftsmen and the smiths. No one remained except the poorest people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:10; Jeremiah 22:24–28; Esther 2:6"> He carried away Jehoiachin to Babylon, with the king’s mother, the king’s wives, his officers, and the chief men of the land. He carried them into captivity from Jerusalem to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:28; 2 Kings 24:14; Jeremiah 29:2"> All the men of might, even seven thousand, and the craftsmen and the smiths one thousand, all of them strong and fit for war, even them the king of Babylon brought captive to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; 2 Chronicles 36:4; 2 Kings 23:34"> The king of Babylon made Mattaniah, Jehoiachin’s father’s brother, king in his place, and changed his name to Zedekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 23:31; Jeremiah 52:1–11; Jeremiah 37:1"> Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Hamutal the daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 23:37; Jeremiah 24:8; Ezekiel 21:25"> He did that which was evil in the LORD’s sight, according to all that Jehoiakim had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11; Jeremiah 38:17–21"> For through the anger of the LORD, this happened in Jerusalem and Judah, until he had cast them out from his presence. Then Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.</span>
