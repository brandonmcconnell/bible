<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:29; Proverbs 4:9; Genesis 41:42"> <span className="pl pl-1">for they will be a garland to grace your head,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and chains around your neck.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">You shall cast your lot amongst us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We’ll all have one purse”—</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 119:101; Psalms 1:1; Proverbs 4:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">my son, don’t walk on the path with them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Keep your foot from their path,</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:11; Luke 7:30; Proverbs 1:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">but you have ignored all my counsel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and wanted none of my reproof;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Proverbs 10:24; Psalms 37:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I also will laugh at your disaster.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will mock when calamity overtakes you,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Luke 21:34–35; Psalms 58:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">when calamity overtakes you like a storm,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when your disaster comes on like a whirlwind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when distress and anguish come on you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Jacob 4:3; Jeremiah 11:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then they will call on me, but I will not answer.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will seek me diligently, but they will not find me,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 1:22; Job 21:14–15; Proverbs 6:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">because they hated knowledge,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and didn’t choose the fear of the LORD.</span></span>
