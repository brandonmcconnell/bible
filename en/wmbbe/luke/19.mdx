<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:1–27; Luke 18:35; 2 Kings 2:18–22"> He entered and was passing through Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 18:24–27; 2 Chronicles 17:5–6"> There was a man named Zacchaeus. He was a chief tax collector, and he was rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:8; Luke 9:7–9; Yochanan 12:21"> He was trying to see who Yeshua was, and couldn’t because of the crowd, because he was short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; Luke 5:19; 1 Kings 10:27"> He ran on ahead and climbed up into a sycamore tree to see him, for he was going to pass that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Yochanan 14:23; Hebrews 13:2"> When Yeshua came to the place, he looked up and saw him, and said to him,<span className="woj">“Zacchaeus, hurry and come down, for today I must stay at your house.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 5:29; Acts 16:34; Acts 16:15"> He hurried, came down, and received him joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:39; Luke 7:34"> When they saw it, they all murmured, saying, “He has gone in to lodge with a man who is a sinner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:6; Numbers 5:7; Luke 18:22–23"> Zacchaeus stood and said to the Lord, “Behold, Lord, half of my goods I give to the poor. If I have wrongfully exacted anything of anyone, I restore four times as much.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; Luke 13:16; Romans 4:16"> Yeshua said to him,<span className="woj">“Today, salvation has come to this house, because he also is a son of Abraham.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Matthew 1:21; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> <span className="woj">For the Son of Man came to seek and to save that which was lost.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> As they heard these things, he went on and told a parable, because he was near Jerusalem, and they supposed that God’s Kingdom would be revealed immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> He said therefore,<span className="woj">“A certain nobleman went into a far country to receive for himself a kingdom and to return.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; Yochanan 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> <span className="woj">He called ten servants of his and gave them ten mina coins,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="10 minas was more than 3 years’ wages for an agricultural labourer.">a</a></sup><span className="woj">and told them, ‘Conduct business until I come.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> <span className="woj">But his citizens hated him, and sent an envoy after him, saying, ‘We don’t want this man to reign over us.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 2:4–6"> <span className="woj">“When he had come back again, having received the kingdom, he commanded these servants, to whom he had given the money, to be called to him, that he might know what they had gained by conducting business.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jacob 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> <span className="woj">The first came before him, saying, ‘Lord, your mina has made ten more minas.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> <span className="woj">“He said to him, ‘Well done, you good servant! Because you were found faithful with very little, you shall have authority over ten cities.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> <span className="woj">“The second came, saying, ‘Your mina, Lord, has made five minas.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> <span className="woj">“So he said to him, ‘And you are to be over five cities.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; Jacob 4:17"> <span className="woj">Another came, saying, ‘Lord, behold, your mina, which I kept laid away in a handkerchief,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 Yochanan 4:18"> <span className="woj">for I feared you, because you are an exacting man. You take up that which you didn’t lay down, and reap that which you didn’t sow.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> <span className="woj">“He said to him, ‘Out of your own mouth I will judge you, you wicked servant! You knew that I am an exacting man, taking up that which I didn’t lay down and reaping that which I didn’t sow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> <span className="woj">Then why didn’t you deposit my money in the bank, and at my coming, I might have earned interest on it?’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> <span className="woj">He said to those who stood by, ‘Take the mina away from him and give it to him who has the ten minas.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> <span className="woj">“They said to him, ‘Lord, he has ten minas!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Yochanan 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> <span className="woj">‘For I tell you that to everyone who has, will more be given; but from him who doesn’t have, even that which he has will be taken away from him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Psalms 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> <span className="woj">But bring those enemies of mine who didn’t want me to reign over them here, and kill them before me.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> Having said these things, he went on ahead, going up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> When he came near to Bethsphage<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="TR, NU read “Bethpage” instead of “Bethsphage”">b</a></sup>and Bethany, at the mountain that is called Olivet, he sent two of his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> saying,<span className="woj">“Go your way into the village on the other side, in which, as you enter, you will find a colt tied, which no man has ever sat upon. Untie it and bring it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Psalms 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> <span className="woj">If anyone asks you, ‘Why are you untying it?’ say to him: ‘The Lord needs it.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 22:13"> Those who were sent went away and found things just as he had told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> As they were untying the colt, its owners said to them, “Why are you untying the colt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Yochanan 10:35; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> They said, “The Lord needs it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; Matthew 21:7; Yochanan 12:14"> Then they brought it to Yeshua. They threw their cloaks on the colt and sat Yeshua on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Matthew 21:8"> As he went, they spread their cloaks on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Yochanan 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; Matthew 21:1"> As he was now getting near, at the descent of the Mount of Olives, the whole multitude of the disciples began to rejoice and praise God with a loud voice for all the mighty works which they had seen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 13:35; Psalms 118:22–26"> saying, “Blessed is the King who comes in the name of the Lord! Peace in heaven, and glory in the highest!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; Matthew 23:13; Acts 4:16–18"> Some of the Pharisees from the multitude said to him, “Rabbi, rebuke your disciples!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; Matthew 3:9"> He answered them,<span className="woj">“I tell you that if these were silent, the stones would cry out.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; Yochanan 11:35; Psalms 119:136"> When he came near, he saw the city and wept over it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> saying,<span className="woj">“If you, even you, had known today the things which belong to your peace! But now, they are hidden from your eyes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; Ezekiel 4:2"> <span className="woj">For the days will come on you when your enemies will throw up a barricade against you, surround you, hem you in on every side,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Luke 13:34–35; Yochanan 3:18–21"> <span className="woj">and will dash you and your children within you to the ground. They will not leave in you one stone on another, because you didn’t know the time of your visitation.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 21:12–13; Mark 11:15–17; Yochanan 2:13–17"> He entered into the temple and began to drive out those who bought and sold in it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Psalms 93:5"> saying to them,<span className="woj">“It is written, ‘My house is a house of prayer,’</span><span className="woj">but you have made it a ‘den of robbers’!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 26:55; Mark 11:27–33"> He was teaching daily in the temple, but the chief priests, the scribes, and the leading men amongst the people sought to destroy him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:3; Acts 16:14; Yochanan 7:46–49"> They couldn’t find what they might do, for all the people hung on to every word that he said.</span>
