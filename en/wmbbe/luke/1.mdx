<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Yochanan 20:31; Acts 18:25; 1 Corinthians 14:19"> that you might know the certainty concerning the things in which you were instructed.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 24:10; Matthew 2:1; Nehemiah 12:4"> There was in the days of Herod, the king of Judea, a certain priest named Zechariah, of the priestly division of Abijah. He had a wife of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elisheva.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; Genesis 17:1; Philippians 2:15"> They were both righteous before God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Genesis 18:11; 1 Samuel 1:2"> But they had no child, because Elisheva was barren, and they both were well advanced in years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:14; 1 Chronicles 24:19; 2 Chronicles 31:2"> Now while he executed the priest’s office before God in the order of his division</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:11; Exodus 30:7–8; 1 Chronicles 23:13"> according to the custom of the priest’s office, his lot was to enter into the temple of the Lord and burn incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 16:17; Revelation 8:3; Hebrews 4:14"> The whole multitude of the people were praying outside at the hour of incense.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 40:26–27; Acts 10:3–4; Judges 13:3"> An angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing on the right side of the altar of incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:22; Judges 6:22; Luke 2:9–10"> Zechariah was troubled when he saw him, and fear fell upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:21; Acts 10:31; Genesis 25:21"> But the angel said to him, “Don’t be afraid, Zechariah, because your request has been heard. Your wife, Elisheva, will bear you a son, and you shall call his name Yochanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:58; Proverbs 23:24; Genesis 21:6"> You will have joy and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Numbers 6:2–4; Luke 7:33"> For he will be great in the sight of the Lord, and he will drink no wine nor strong drink. He will be filled with the Holy Spirit, even from his mother’s womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 21:32; Malachi 3:1"> He will turn many of the children of Israel to the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 4:5–6; Matthew 11:14; Luke 1:76"> He will go before him in the spirit and power of Elijah, ‘to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children,’ and the disobedient to the wisdom of the just; to prepare a people prepared for the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 38:22; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:7"> Zechariah said to the angel, “How can I be sure of this? For I am an old man, and my wife is well advanced in years.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 1:26; Matthew 18:10; Daniel 9:21–23"> The angel answered him, “I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God. I was sent to speak to you and to bring you this good news.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:26; Ezekiel 24:27; Luke 1:22"> Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Behold”, from “ἰδοὺ”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 6:23–27"> The people were waiting for Zechariah, and they marvelled that he delayed in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 1:62; Yochanan 13:24; Acts 21:40"> When he came out, he could not speak to them. They perceived that he had seen a vision in the temple. He continued making signs to them, and remained mute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:25; 2 Kings 11:5–7"> When the days of his service were fulfilled, he departed to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> After these days Elisheva his wife conceived, and she hid herself five months, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–23; Isaiah 54:1–4; Genesis 21:1–2"> “Thus has the Lord done to me in the days in which he looked at me, to take away my reproach amongst men.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; Luke 1:19; Yochanan 7:41"> Now in the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city of Galilee named Nazareth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:16"> to a virgin pledged to be married to a man whose name was Joseph, of David’s house. The virgin’s name was Miriam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 6:12; Daniel 10:19; Luke 1:30"> Having come in, the angel said to her, “Rejoice, you highly favoured one! The Lord is with you. Blessed are you amongst women!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:17; Luke 1:12; Acts 10:4"> But when she saw him, she was greatly troubled at the saying, and considered what kind of salutation this might be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Romans 8:31; Luke 12:32"> The angel said to her, “Don’t be afraid, Miriam, for you have found favour with God.</span>

<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 2:44; Hebrews 1:8; Daniel 7:13–14"> and he will reign over the house of Jacob forever. There will be no end to his Kingdom.”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 13:8–12; Acts 9:6"> Miriam said to the angel, “How can this be, seeing I am a virgin?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 1:18; Yochanan 1:49; Matthew 1:20"> The angel answered her, “The Holy Spirit will come on you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore also the holy one who is born from you will be called the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Behold, Elisheva your relative also has conceived a son in her old age; and this is the sixth month with her who was called barren.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Yochanan 20:29; Yochanan 11:40; Luke 11:27–28"> Blessed is she who believed, for there will be a fulfilment of the things which have been spoken to her from the Lord!”</span>

<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Psalms 111:9; Ephesians 3:20"> <span className="pl pl-2">For he who is mighty has done great things for me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Holy is his name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 103:17–18; Exodus 20:6; Genesis 17:7"> <span className="pl pl-2">His mercy is for generations and generations on those who fear him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 98:1; Isaiah 52:10; 1 Samuel 2:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has shown strength with his arm.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Jacob 4:10; Psalms 107:40–41"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has put down princes from their thrones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has exalted the lowly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Psalms 107:8–9; Luke 6:21; Psalms 34:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has filled the hungry with good things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has sent the rich away empty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 98:3; Micah 7:20; Isaiah 44:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has given help to Israel, his servant, that he might remember mercy,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 105:6–10; Genesis 17:19; Psalms 132:11–17"> <span className="pl pl-2">as he spoke to our fathers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to Abraham and his offspring</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="or, seed">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-2">forever.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Miriam stayed with her about three months, and then returned to her house.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Genesis 21:2–3; Luke 2:6–7"> Now the time that Elisheva should give birth was fulfilled, and she gave birth to a son.</span>

<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Luke 1:13; 2 Samuel 12:25; Luke 1:63"> His mother answered, “Not so; but he will be called Yochanan.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> They said to her, “There is no one amongst your relatives who is called by this name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Luke 1:22"> They made signs to his father, what he would have him called.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Luke 1:60; Isaiah 30:8; Luke 1:13"> He asked for a writing tablet, and wrote, “His name is Yochanan.”<br />They all marvelled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Exodus 4:15–16; Ezekiel 33:22"> His mouth was opened immediately and his tongue freed, and he spoke, blessing God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 1:39; Acts 5:11; Luke 7:16"> Fear came on all who lived around them, and all these sayings were talked about throughout all the hill country of Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Acts 11:21; Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51"> All who heard them laid them up in their heart, saying, “What then will this child be?” The hand of the Lord was with him.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Acts 3:21–24; Romans 1:2; 2 Peter 1:21"> <span className="pl pl-2">(as he spoke by the mouth of his holy prophets who have been from of old),</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Psalms 106:10; Jeremiah 23:6; Zephaniah 3:15–20"> <span className="pl pl-2">salvation from our enemies and from the hand of all who hate us;</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73" data-xref="Genesis 22:16–18; Psalms 105:9; Deuteronomy 7:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">the oath which he swore to Abraham our father,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7; Zephaniah 3:15–17; Isaiah 54:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-2">to grant to us that we, being delivered out of the hand of our enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">should serve him without fear,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="Ephesians 4:24; Ephesians 2:10; Titus 2:11–14"> <span className="pl pl-2">in holiness and righteousness before him all the days of our life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v76" data-v="76" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 40:3–5; Mark 1:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And you, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for you will go before the face of the Lord to prepare his ways,</span><br /></span>
