<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 40:3; Zechariah 1:16; Ezekiel 47:4"> I lifted up my eyes, and saw, and behold, a man with a measuring line in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:39; Ezekiel 45:6; Revelation 21:15–17"> Then I asked, “Where are you going?” He said to me, “To measure Jerusalem, to see what is its width and what is its length.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Zechariah 1:19; Zechariah 1:8–11; Zechariah 4:5"> Behold, the angel who talked with me went out, and another angel went out to meet him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Jeremiah 33:22; Ezekiel 38:11"> and said to him, “Run, speak to this young man, saying, ‘Jerusalem will be inhabited as villages without walls, because of the multitude of men and livestock in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 4:5; Zechariah 9:8; Psalms 3:3"> For I,’ says the LORD, ‘will be to her a wall of fire around it, and I will be the glory in the middle of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Ezekiel 17:21; Isaiah 52:11–12"> Come! Come! Flee from the land of the north,’ says the LORD; ‘for I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the sky,’ says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Genesis 19:17; Isaiah 52:11"> ‘Come, Zion! Escape, you who dwell with the daughter of Babylon.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 17:8; Deuteronomy 32:10; Yochanan 15:21–23"> For the LORD of Hosts says: ‘For honour he has sent me to the nations which plundered you; for he who touches you touches the apple of his eye.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 14:2; Zechariah 4:9"> For, behold, I will shake my hand over them, and they will be a plunder to those who served them; and you will know that the LORD of Hosts has sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Yochanan 1:14; Zephaniah 3:14–15"> Sing and rejoice, daughter of Zion! For behold, I come and I will dwell within you,’ says the LORD.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; 1 Peter 2:9–10; Acts 28:28"> Many nations shall join themselves to the LORD in that day, and shall be my people; and I will dwell amongst you, and you shall know that the LORD of Hosts has sent me to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 1:17; Deuteronomy 32:9; Jeremiah 10:16"> The LORD will inherit Judah as his portion in the holy land, and will again choose Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Psalms 68:5; Psalms 78:65"> Be silent, all flesh, before the LORD; for he has roused himself from his holy habitation!”</span>
