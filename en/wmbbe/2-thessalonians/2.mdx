<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14–17; Mark 13:27; Matthew 24:31"> Now, brothers, concerning the coming of our Lord Yeshua the Messiah and our gathering together to him, we ask you</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:1–2; Mark 13:7; Matthew 24:4–6"> not to be quickly shaken in your mind or troubled, either by spirit or by word or by letter as if from us, saying that the day of Messiah has already come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 24:4–6; 1 Yochanan 2:18; Ephesians 5:6"> Let no one deceive you in any way. For it will not be unless the rebellion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, falling away, or, defection">a</a></sup>comes first, and the man of sin is revealed, the son of destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:2; Daniel 7:25; Revelation 13:6–7"> He opposes and exalts himself against all that is called God or that is worshipped, so that he sits as God in the temple of God, setting himself up as God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Peter 1:15; Galatians 5:21; Matthew 16:9"> Don’t you remember that when I was still with you, I told you these things?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> Now you know what is restraining him, to the end that he may be revealed in his own season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Yochanan 4:3; Revelation 17:7; 2 Timothy 2:17–18"> For the mystery of lawlessness already works. Only there is one who restrains now, until he is taken out of the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:3; Revelation 19:20–21; Isaiah 11:4"> Then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord will kill with the breath of his mouth and destroy by the manifestation of his coming;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; Revelation 19:20; Ephesians 2:2"> even he whose coming is according to the working of Satan with all power and signs and lying wonders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:18; Proverbs 1:7; 2 Corinthians 4:2–3"> and with all deception of wickedness for those who are being lost, because they didn’t receive the love of the truth, that they might be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 1:28; Yochanan 12:39–43; 1 Timothy 4:1"> Because of this, God sends them a powerful delusion, that they should believe a lie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Romans 8:7–8; Mark 16:16"> that they all might be judged who didn’t believe the truth, but had pleasure in unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:4–5; 1 Thessalonians 1:4"> But we are bound to always give thanks to God for you, brothers loved by the Lord, because God chose you from the beginning for salvation through sanctification of the Spirit and belief in the truth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:12; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 1:5"> to which he called you through our Good News, for the obtaining of the glory of our Lord Yeshua the Messiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:2; 2 Thessalonians 3:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> So then, brothers, stand firm and hold the traditions which you were taught by us, whether by word or by letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 5:2–5; Yochanan 3:16; 1 Peter 1:3–8"> Now our Lord Yeshua the Messiah himself, and God our Father, who loved us and gave us eternal comfort and good hope through grace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:3; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2"> comfort your hearts and establish you in every good work and word.</span>
