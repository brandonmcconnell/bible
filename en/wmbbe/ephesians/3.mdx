<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; Romans 16:25; Ephesians 1:9–11"> how that by revelation the mystery was made known to me, as I wrote before in few words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; 1 Corinthians 4:1; 2 Corinthians 11:6"> by which, when you read, you can perceive my understanding in the mystery of Messiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:10–12; 2 Peter 3:2; Titus 1:1–3"> which in other generations was not made known to the children of men, as it has now been revealed to his holy emissaries and prophets in the Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 3:26–29; 1 Yochanan 1:3; 1 Yochanan 2:25"> that the Gentiles are fellow heirs and fellow members of the body, and fellow partakers of his promise in Messiah Yeshua through the Good News,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 1:19; Ephesians 3:20; Ephesians 3:2"> of which I was made a servant according to the gift of that grace of God which was given me according to the working of his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Colossians 1:27; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Ephesians 2:7"> To me, the very least of all holy ones, was this grace given, to proclaim to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Messiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:26; Matthew 13:35; 2 Timothy 1:9"> and to make all men see what is the administration<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="TR reads “fellowship” instead of “administration”">a</a></sup>of the mystery which for ages has been hidden in God, who created all things through Yeshua the Messiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:12; Ephesians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 2:7"> to the intent that now through the assembly the manifold wisdom of God might be made known to the principalities and the powers in the heavenly places,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:9; Ephesians 1:11; Ephesians 1:4"> according to the eternal purpose which he accomplished in Messiah Yeshua our Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 2:18; Romans 5:2; Hebrews 4:14–16"> In him we have boldness and access in confidence through our faith in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; 2 Thessalonians 3:13; 2 Corinthians 1:6"> Therefore I ask that you may not lose heart at my troubles for you, which are your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Ephesians 1:16–19; Philippians 2:10"> For this cause, I bow my knees to the Father of our Lord Yeshua the Messiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Revelation 5:8–14; Ephesians 1:21"> from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> that he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, that you may be strengthened with power through his Spirit in the inner person,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:7; 1 Yochanan 4:16; Yochanan 14:23"> that Messiah may dwell in your hearts through faith, to the end that you, being rooted and grounded in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 103:11–12; Job 11:7–9; Ephesians 1:18–23"> may be strengthened to comprehend with all the holy ones what is the width and length and height and depth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 1:23; Colossians 2:9–10; Colossians 1:10"> and to know Messiah’s love which surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; Romans 4:21; Jeremiah 32:27"> Now to him who is able to do exceedingly abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that works in us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judah 1:25; Romans 11:36; 2 Peter 3:18"> to him be the glory in the assembly and in Messiah Yeshua to all generations, forever and ever. Amen.</span>
