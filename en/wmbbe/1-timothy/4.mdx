<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ephesians 4:19; Acts 20:30; Romans 16:18"> through the hypocrisy of men who speak lies, branded in their own conscience as with a hot iron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 13:4; 1 Timothy 4:4; Romans 14:6"> forbidding marriage and commanding to abstain from foods which God created to be received with thanksgiving by those who believe and know the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 14:20; 1 Corinthians 10:25; Romans 14:14"> For every creature of God is good, and nothing is to be rejected if it is received with thanksgiving.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Titus 2:7–10; Colossians 3:16; Matthew 13:52"> If you instruct the brothers of these things, you will be a good servant of Messiah Yeshua, nourished in the words of the faith and of the good doctrine which you have followed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:16; Titus 2:12; 1 Timothy 1:4"> But refuse profane and old wives’ fables. Exercise yourself towards godliness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; 1 Timothy 6:6; Psalms 37:3–4"> For bodily exercise has some value, but godliness has value in all things, having the promise of the life which is now and of that which is to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:15"> This saying is faithful and worthy of all acceptance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; 1 Yochanan 2:2; 1 Timothy 2:6"> For to this end we both labour and suffer reproach, because we have set our trust in the living God, who is the Saviour of all men, especially of those who believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:2; 2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 2:15"> Command and teach these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 2 Timothy 2:22; 2 Timothy 2:15"> Let no man despise your youth; but be an example to those who believe, in word, in your way of life, in love, in spirit, in faith, and in purity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:2"> Until I come, pay attention to reading, to exhortation, and to teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 1 Peter 4:9–11; 1 Timothy 1:18"> Don’t neglect the gift that is in you, which was given to you by prophecy with the laying on of the hands of the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:16; Philippians 2:15–16; Psalms 143:5"> Be diligent in these things. Give yourself wholly to them, that your progress may be revealed to all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Titus 1:9; Titus 2:7; 2 Yochanan 1:8–9"> Pay attention to yourself and to your teaching. Continue in these things, for in doing this you will save both yourself and those who hear you.</span>
