<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:10; 2 Corinthians 4:8; 2 Corinthians 11:27"> but in everything commending ourselves as servants of God: in great endurance, in afflictions, in hardships, in distresses,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 1:27; Galatians 5:22"> in pureness, in knowledge, in perseverance, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in sincere love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; 2 Timothy 2:15; Ephesians 6:11–20"> in the word of truth, in the power of God, by the armour of righteousness on the right hand and on the left,</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:3–4; Philippians 1:8; Psalms 119:32"> Our mouth is open to you, Corinthians. Our heart is enlarged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:2; Micah 2:7; Philippians 1:8"> You are not restricted by us, but you are restricted by your own affections.</span>
