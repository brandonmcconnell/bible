<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 9:10–28"> At the end of twenty years, in which Solomon had built the LORD’s house and his own house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 9:11–18"> Solomon built the cities which Huram had given to Solomon, and caused the children of Israel to dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 1 Chronicles 18:3; 2 Samuel 8:3"> Solomon went to Hamath Zobah, and prevailed against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:17–19"> He built Tadmor in the wilderness, and all the storage cities, which he built in Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:24; Joshua 16:5; 2 Chronicles 14:7"> Also he built Beth Horon the upper and Beth Horon the lower, fortified cities with walls, gates, and bars;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 7:2; 2 Chronicles 8:4; 2 Chronicles 1:14"> and Baalath, and all the storage cities that Solomon had, and all the cities for his chariots, the cities for his horsemen, and all that Solomon desired to build for his pleasure in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and in all the land of his dominion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 15:18–21; Deuteronomy 7:1; 1 Kings 9:20–22"> As for all the people who were left of the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, who were not of Israel—</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:21; 1 Kings 4:6"> of their children who were left after them in the land, whom the children of Israel didn’t consume—of them Solomon conscripted forced labour to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–12; Exodus 19:5–6; Galatians 4:31"> But of the children of Israel, Solomon made no servants for his work, but they were men of war, chief of his captains, and rulers of his chariots and of his horsemen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 9:23; 1 Kings 5:16; 2 Chronicles 2:18"> These were the chief officers of King Solomon, even two-hundred and fifty, who ruled over the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 3:1; 1 Kings 7:8; 1 Kings 9:24"> Solomon brought up Pharaoh’s daughter out of David’s city to the house that he had built for her; for he said, “My wife shall not dwell in the house of David king of Israel, because the places where the LORD’s ark has come are holy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 4:1; 2 Chronicles 15:8; Yochanan 10:23"> Then Solomon offered burnt offerings to the LORD on the LORD’s altar which he had built before the porch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16; Exodus 29:38–42; Ezekiel 46:3–15"> even as the duty of every day required, offering according to the commandment of Moses on the Sabbaths, on the new moons, and on the set feasts, three times per year, during the feast of unleavened bread, during the feast of weeks, and during the feast of booths.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="or, feast of booths (Sukkot)">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Chronicles 9:17; Ezra 6:18; Nehemiah 12:36"> He appointed, according to the ordinance of David his father, the divisions of the priests to their service, and the Levites to their offices, to praise and to minister before the priests, as the duty of every day required, the doorkeepers also by their divisions at every gate, for David the man of God had so commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:12; 1 Chronicles 9:29; 1 Kings 7:51"> They didn’t depart from the commandment of the king to the priests and Levites concerning any matter or concerning the treasures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; 1 Kings 6:7"> Now all the work of Solomon was accomplished from the day of the foundation of the LORD’s house until it was finished. So the LORD’s house was completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:22; Deuteronomy 2:8; Numbers 33:35"> Then Solomon went to Ezion Geber and to Eloth, on the seashore in the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:10; 2 Chronicles 9:13; Ecclesiastes 2:8"> Huram sent him ships and servants who had knowledge of the sea by the hands of his servants; and they came with the servants of Solomon to Ophir, and brought from there four hundred and fifty talents<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="A talent is about 30 kilograms or 66 pounds or 965 Troy ounces, so 450 talents is about 13.5 metric tonnes">b</a></sup>of gold, and brought them to King Solomon.</span>
