<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:12; 2 Samuel 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> on the third day, behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="“Behold”, from “הִנֵּה”, means look at, take notice, observe, see, or gaze at. When he came to David, he fell to the earth and showed respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; 2 Kings 5:25"> David said to him, “Where do you come from?” He said to him, “I have escaped out of the camp of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 31:1–6; 1 Chronicles 10:1–6"> David said to him, “How did it go? <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> David said to the young man who told him, “How do you know that Saul and Jonathan his son are dead?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21; 1 Chronicles 10:4–6"> The young man who told him said, “As I happened by chance on Mount Gilboa, behold, Saul was leaning on his spear; and behold, the chariots and the horsemen followed close behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:12; 2 Samuel 9:6; Judges 9:54"> When he looked behind him, he saw me and called to me. I answered, ‘Here I am.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Samuel 30:1"> He said to me, ‘Who are you?’ I answered him, ‘I am an Amalekite.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He said to me, ‘Please stand beside me, and kill me, for anguish has taken hold of me because my life lingers in me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; 2 Kings 11:12"> So I stood beside him and killed him, because I was sure that he could not live after he had fallen. I took the crown that was on his head and the bracelet that was on his arm, and have brought them here to my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31"> Then David took hold on his clothes and tore them; and all the men who were with him did likewise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> They mourned, wept, and fasted until evening for Saul and for Jonathan his son, and for the people of the LORD,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="When rendered in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS, “LORD” or “GOD” is the translation of God’s Proper Name.">b</a></sup>and for the house of Israel, because they had fallen by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:8"> David said to the young man who told him, “Where are you from?” He answered, “I am the son of a foreigner, an Amalekite.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Samuel 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> David said to him, “Why were you not afraid to stretch out your hand to destroy the LORD’s anointed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 2:34; Judges 8:20; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> David called one of the young men and said, “Go near, and cut him down!” He struck him so that he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> David said to him, “Your blood be on your head, for your mouth has testified against you, saying, ‘I have slain the LORD’s anointed.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Genesis 50:11"> David lamented with this lamentation over Saul and over Jonathan his son</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; Genesis 49:8; Deuteronomy 4:10"> (and he commanded them to teach the children of Judah the song of the bow; behold, it is written in the book of Jashar):</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:27; 2 Samuel 1:25; Deuteronomy 4:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Your glory, Israel, was slain on your high places!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">How the mighty have fallen!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:6; Judges 11:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don’t tell it in Gath.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Don’t publish it in the streets of Ashkelon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lest the daughters of the Philistines rejoice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">lest the daughters of the uncircumcised triumph.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Isaiah 21:5; 1 Samuel 10:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">You mountains of Gilboa,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">let there be no dew or rain on you, and no fields of offerings;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for there the shield of the mighty was defiled and cast away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the shield of Saul was not anointed with oil.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 34:6–7; 1 Samuel 14:6–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the blood of the slain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the fat of the mighty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Jonathan’s bow didn’t turn back.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Saul’s sword didn’t return empty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Saul and Jonathan were lovely and pleasant in their lives.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">In their death, they were not divided.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They were swifter than eagles.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They were stronger than lions.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">You daughters of Israel, weep over Saul,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who clothed you delicately in scarlet,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who put ornaments of gold on your clothing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:19; 2 Samuel 1:27; Judges 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">How the mighty have fallen in the middle of the battle!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Jonathan was slain on your high places.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; 1 Samuel 23:16; 1 Samuel 20:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am distressed for you, my brother Jonathan.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have been very pleasant to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your love to me was wonderful,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">surpassing the love of women.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">How the mighty have fallen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the weapons of war have perished!”</span></span>
