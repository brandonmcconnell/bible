<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 111:1; Psalms 95:3; 1 Corinthians 14:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord, you have proved me, and known me.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I should spread my wings to fly</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alex. κατ᾿ ὄρθον, toward the dawn.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">straight forward, and sojourn at the extremity of the sea, it would be vain,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">for even there your hand would guide me, and your right hand would hold me.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For darkness will not be darkness with you; but night will be light as day: as its darkness, so shall its light be to you.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will give you thanks; for you are fearfully wondrous; wondrous are your works; and my soul knows it well.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But your friends, O God, have been greatly honored by me; their rule has been greatly strengthened.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will number them, and they shall be multiplied beyond the sand; I awake, and am still with you.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you will say concerning their thought, that they shall take your cities in vain.</span><br /></span>
