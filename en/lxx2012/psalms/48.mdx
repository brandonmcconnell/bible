<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 87:5; Psalms 78:3–6; Psalms 46:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">or the price of the redemption of his soul, though he labor for ever,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 26:3; Psalms 40:10; Psalms 77:10–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">and live to the end, so that he should not see corruption.</span><br /></span>
