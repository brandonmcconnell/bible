<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:2; Psalms 51:8; Psalms 31:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heart grew hot within me, and a fire would kindle in my meditation: I spoke with my tongue,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Ezra 9:6; Matthew 11:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord, make me to know my end, and the number of my days, what it is; that I may know what I lack.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 21:18–19; Acts 12:23; Psalms 102:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And now what is my expectation? is it not the Lord? and my ground of hope is with you. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 3:24; Psalms 32:3; Job 30:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me from all my transgressions: you have made me a reproach to the foolish.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 10:17; Psalms 6:6; 2 Corinthians 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. made dumb.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">dumb, and opened not my mouth; for you are he that made me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:7; Psalms 88:9; Psalms 69:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remove your scourges from me: I have fainted by reason of the strength of your hand.</span><br /></span>
