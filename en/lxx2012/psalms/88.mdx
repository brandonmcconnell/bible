<div className="hs">(89) A Psalm of instruction for Aetham the Israelite.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 12:2; Psalms 22:2; Luke 18:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will sing of your mercies, O Lord, for ever: I will declare your truth with my mouth to all generations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 31:2; Psalms 141:1–2; Lamentations 3:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you have said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: your truth shall be established in the heavens.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 107:18; Lamentations 3:15–19; Mark 14:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">I made a covenant with my chosen ones, I swore to David my servant.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 28:1; 2 Corinthians 1:9; Psalms 143:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. prepare.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">establish your seed for ever, and build up your throne to all generations. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 53:8; Psalms 31:22; Esias 14:9–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The heavens shall declare your wonders, O Lord; and your truth in the assembly of the saints.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 69:15; Psalms 86:13; Psalms 143:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For who in the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. clouds.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">heavens shall be compared to the Lord? and who shall be likened to the Lord among the sons of God?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 42:7; Job 6:4; Romans 2:5–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is glorified in the council of the saints; great and terrible toward all that are round about him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 31:11; Esias 49:7; Jeremias 32:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord God of hosts, who is like to you? you are mighty, O Lord, and your truth is round about you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 11:13; Psalms 38:10; Psalms 86:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You rule the power of the sea; and you calm the tumult of its waves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Esias 26:19; Psalms 30:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have brought down the proud as one that is slain; and with the arm of your power you have scattered your enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 26:6; Proverbs 15:11; Psalms 73:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The heavens are your, and the earth is your: you have founded the world, and the fullness of it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:5; Ecclesiastes 2:16; Esias 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have created the north and the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. sea.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">west: Thabor and Hermon shall rejoice in your name.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:3; Psalms 30:2; Mark 1:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your is the mighty arm: let your hand be strengthened, let your right hand be exalted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 13:24; Psalms 13:1; Psalms 44:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Justice and judgment are the establishment of your throne: mercy and truth shall go before your face.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 6:4; Luke 22:44; Job 7:11–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed is the people that knows the joyful sound: they shall walk, O Lord, in the light of your countenance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 90:11; Esias 53:4–6; Romans 8:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">And in your name shall they rejoice all the day: and in your righteousness shall they be exalted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 22:16; Psalms 116:3; Psalms 69:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you are the boast of their strength; and in your good pleasure shall our horn be exalted,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 88:8; Psalms 38:11; Job 19:12–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">for our help is of the Lord; and of the Holy One of Israel, our king.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then you spoke in vision to your children, and said, I have laid help on a mighty one; I have exalted one chosen out of my people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have found David my servant; I have anointed him by my holy</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. oil, see Ps. 91. (92) 10.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">mercy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my hand shall support him; and my arm shall strengthen him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">The enemy shall have no advantage against him; and the son of transgression shall not hurt him again.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will hew down his foes before him, and put to flight those that hate him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">But my truth and my mercy shall be with him; and in my name shall his horn be exalted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will set his hand in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">He shall call upon me, saying, You are my Father, my God, and the helper of my salvation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will keep my mercy for him for ever, and my covenant shall be firm with him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will establish his seed for ever and ever, and his throne as the days of heaven.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> <span className="pl pl-1">If his children should forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> <span className="pl pl-1">if they should profane my ordinances, and not keep my commandments;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will visit their transgressions with a rod, and their sins with scourges.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> <span className="pl pl-1">But my mercy I will not utterly remove from him, nor wrong my truth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Neither will I by any means profane my covenant; and I will not make void the things that proceed out of my lips.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Once have I sworn by my holiness, that I will not lie to David.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> <span className="pl pl-1">His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> <span className="pl pl-1">and as the moon that is established for ever, and as the faithful witness in heaven. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you have cast off and set at nothing, you have rejected your anointed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have overthrown the covenant of your servant; you have profaned his sanctuary, casting it to the ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have broken down all his hedges; you have made his strong holds a terror.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> <span className="pl pl-1">All that go by the way have spoiled him: he is become a reproach to his neighbors.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have exalted the right hand of his enemies; you have made all his enemies to rejoice.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have turned back the help of his sword, and have not helped him in the battle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have deprived him of</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. purification.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">glory: you have broken down his throne to the ground.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have shortened the days of his</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Alex. time.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">throne: you have poured shame upon him. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, O Lord, will you turn away, for ever? shall your anger flame out as fire?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember what my being is: for have you created all the sons of men in vain?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> <span className="pl pl-1">What man is there who shall live, and not see death? shall any one deliver his soul from the hand of Hades? Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> <span className="pl pl-1">Where are your ancient mercies, O Lord, which you swore to David in your truth?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> <span className="pl pl-1">Remember, O Lord, the reproach of your servants, which I have borne in my bosom, even the reproach of many nations;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> <span className="pl pl-1">wherewith your enemies have reviled, O Lord: wherewith they have reviled the recompense of your anointed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed be the Lord for ever. So be it, so be it.</span></span>
