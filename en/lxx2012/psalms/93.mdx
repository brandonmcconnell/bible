<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He that chastises the heathen, shall not he punish, even he that teaches man knowledge?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord knows the thoughts of men, that they are vain.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">If the Lord had not helped me, my soul had almost sojourned in Hades.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I said, My foot has been moved;</span><br /></span>
