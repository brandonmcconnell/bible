<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 12:30; Ephesians 1:3; Psalms 59:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, the praise of Israel, dwell in a sanctuary.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Malachias 4:1; Psalms 50:3; Psalms 18:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you are he that drew me out of the womb; my hope from my mother's breasts.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; Kings III 13:34; Job 18:16–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was cast on you from the womb: you are my God from my mother's belly.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 7:12–13; Psalms 18:40; Psalms 64:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. calves">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">bullocks have compassed me: fat bulls have beset me round.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am poured out like water, and all my bones are loosened: my heart in the midst of my belly is become like melting wax.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">My strength is dried up, like a potsherd; and my tongue is glued to my throat; and you have brought me down to the dust of death.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For many dogs have compassed me: the assembly of the wicked doers has beset me round: they pierced my hands and my feet.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, O Lord, remove not my help afar off: be ready for my aid.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver my soul from the sword; my only-begotten one from the power of the dog.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You⌃ that fear the Lord, praise him; all you⌃ seed of Jacob, glorify him: let all the seed of Israel fear him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he has not despised nor been angry at the supplication of the poor; nor turned away his face from me; but when I cried to him, he heard me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">My praise is of you in the great congregation: I will pay my vows before them that fear him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">The poor shall eat and be satisfied; and they shall praise the Lord that seek him: their heart shall live for ever.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> <span className="pl pl-1">And my seed shall serve him: the generation that is coming shall be reported to the Lord.</span><br /></span>
