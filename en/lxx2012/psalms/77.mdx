<div className="bt">
<div className="hs">(78) A Psalm of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. understanding.">a</a></sup>instruction for Asaph.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 3:4; Psalms 142:1–3; Psalms 50:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give heed, O my people, to my law: incline your ear to the words of my mouth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Genesis 37:35; Esias 26:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will open my mouth in parables: I will utter dark sayings which have been from the beginning.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 61:2; Psalms 142:2–3; Job 31:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">All which we have heard and known, and our fathers have declared to us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 7:13–15; Psalms 6:6; Job 2:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">They were not hid from their children to a second generations; the fathers declaring the praises of the Lord, and his mighty acts, and his wonders which he wrought.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:7; Esias 51:9; Psalms 143:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he raised up a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, to make it known to their children:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 42:8; Psalms 4:4; Psalms 139:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">that another generation might know, even the sons which should be born; and they should arise and declare them to their children.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 85:1; Lamentations 3:31–32; Psalms 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">That they might set their hope on God, and not forget the works of God, but diligently seek his commandments.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Romans 9:6; 2 Peter 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">That they should not be as their fathers, a perverse and provoking generation; a generation which set not its heart aright, and its spirit was not</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, faithfully secured.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">steadfast with God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 40:11; Esias 49:14–15; Esias 63:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The children of Ephraim, bending and shooting with the bow, turned back in the day of battle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 42:3; Mark 9:24; Exodus 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They kept not the covenant of God, and would not walk in his law.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 143:5; Psalms 105:5; Chronicles I 16:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they forgot his benefits, and his miracles which he had showed them;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 71:24; Psalms 145:11; Deuteronomy 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">the miracles which he wrought before their fathers, in the land of Egypt, in the plain of Tanes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 73:17; Exodus 15:11; Psalms 89:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He clave the sea, and led them through: he made the waters to stand as in a bottle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:27; Psalms 136:4; Exodus 15:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he guided them with a cloud by day, and all the night with a light of fire.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 9:29; Deuteronomy 9:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">he clave a rock in the wilderness, and made them drink as in a great deep.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:21; Ambacum 3:15; Joshua 3:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he brought water out of the rock, and caused waters to flow down as rivers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Kings II 22:15; Ambacum 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they sinned yet more against him; they provoked the Most High in the wilderness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 97:4; Kings II 22:14; Revelation 11:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they tempted God in their hearts, in asking meat for the desire of their souls.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 14:28; Psalms 29:10; Naum 1:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They spoke also against God, and said, Will God be able to prepare a table in the wilderness?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:21; Psalms 78:52; Exodus 14:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Forasmuch as he struck the rock, and the waters flowed, and the torrents ran abundantly; will he be able also to give bread, or prepare a table for his people?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the Lord heard, and was provoked: and fire was kindled in Jacob, and wrath went up against Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because they believed not in God, and trusted not in his salvation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet he commanded the clouds from above, and opened the doors of heaven,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">and rained upon them manna to eat, and gave them the bread of heaven.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Man ate angels' bread; he sent them provision to the full.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">He removed the south wind from heaven; and by his might he brought in the southwest wind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he rained upon them flesh like dust, and feathered birds like the sand of the seas.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they fell into the midst of their camp, round about their tents.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <span className="pl pl-1">So they ate, and were completely filled; and he gave them their desire.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> <span className="pl pl-1">They were not disappointed of their desire: but when their food was yet in their mouth,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> <span className="pl pl-1">then the indignation of God rose up against them, and killed the fattest of them, and overthrew the choice men of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the midst of all this they sinned yet more, and believed not his miracles.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> <span className="pl pl-1">And their days were consumed in vanity, and their years with anxiety.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> <span className="pl pl-1">When he killed them, they sought him: and they returned and called betimes upon God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they remembered that God was their helper, and the most high God was their redeemer.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet they loved him only with their mouth, and lied to him with their tongue.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> <span className="pl pl-1">For their heart was not right with him, neither were they steadfast in his covenant.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> <span className="pl pl-1">But he is compassionate, and will</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. be propitious, or, merciful to.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">forgive their sins, and will not destroy them: yes, he will frequently turn away his wrath, and will not kindle all his anger.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he remembered that they are flesh; a wind that passes away, and returns not.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> <span className="pl pl-1">How often did they provoke him in the wilderness, and anger him in a dry land!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yes, they turned back, and tempted God, and provoked the Holy One of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> <span className="pl pl-1">They remembered not his hand, the day in which he delivered them from the hand of the oppressor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> <span className="pl pl-1">How he had wrought his signs in Egypt, and his wonders in the field of Tanes:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> <span className="pl pl-1">and had changed their rivers into blood; and their</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. rains, or, showers.">d</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">streams, that they should not drink.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> <span className="pl pl-1">He sent against them the dog-fly, and it devoured them; and the frog, and it spoiled them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he gave their fruit to the canker worm, and their labors to the locust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> <span className="pl pl-1">He killed their vines with hail, and their sycamores with frost.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he gave up their cattle to hail, and their substance to the fire.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> <span className="pl pl-1">He sent out against them the fury of his anger, wrath, and indignation, and affliction, a message by evil angels.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> <span className="pl pl-1">He made a way for his wrath; he spared not their souls from death, but consigned their cattle to death;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> <span className="pl pl-1">and struck every firstborn in the land of Egypt; the first fruits of their labors in the tents of Cham.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he removed his people like sheep; he led them as a flock in the wilderness.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he guided them with hope, and they feared not: but the sea covered their enemies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he brought them in to the mountain of his sanctuary, this mountain which his right hand had purchased.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he cast out the nations from before them, and made them to inherit by a line of inheritance, and made the tribes of Israel to dwell in their tents.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet they tempted and provoked the most high God, and kept not his testimonies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they turned back, and broke covenant, even as also their fathers: they became like a crooked bow.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> <span className="pl pl-1">And they provoked him with their high places, and moved him to jealousy with their graven images.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> <span className="pl pl-1">God heard and</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Lit. overlooked them. See Acts 17:30.">e</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">lightly regarded them, and greatly despised Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he rejected the tabernacle of Selom, his tent where he lived among men.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he gave their strength into captivity, and their beauty into the enemy's hand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. shut up.">f</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">gave his people to the sword; and disdained his inheritance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> <span className="pl pl-1">Fire devoured their young men; and their virgins mourned not.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their priests fell by the sword; and their widows shall not be wept for.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> <span className="pl pl-1">So the Lord awaked as one out of sleep, and as a mighty man who has been heated with wine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he struck his enemies in the hinder parts: he brought on them a perpetual reproach.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he rejected the tabernacle of Joseph, and chose not the tribe of Ephraim;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68"> <span className="pl pl-1">but chose the tribe of Juda, the mount Sion which he loved.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69"> <span className="pl pl-1">And he built his sanctuary as the place of unicorns; he founded it for ever on the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70"> <span className="pl pl-1">He chose David also his servant, and took him up from the flocks of sheep.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71"> <span className="pl pl-1">He took him from following the ewes great with young, to be the shepherd of Jacob his servant, and Israel his inheritance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72"> <span className="pl pl-1">So he tended them in the innocency of his heart; and guided them by the skillfulness of his hands.</span></span>
