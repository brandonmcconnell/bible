<div className="hs">(31) For the end, a Psalm of David, an utterance of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. ecstasy. See title.">a</a></sup>extreme fear.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 25:2; Daniel 4:37; Psalms 35:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord, I have hoped in you; let me never be ashamed: deliver me in your righteousness and rescue me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 103:3–4; Kings IV 20:5; Psalms 147:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Incline your ear to me; make haste to rescue me: be you to me for a protecting God, and for a house of refuge to save me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 86:13; Psalms 16:10; Jonas 2:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you are my strength and my refuge; and you shall guide me for your name's sake, and maintain me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 97:12; Revelation 4:8; Chronicles I 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You shall bring me out of the snare which they have hidden for me; for you, O Lord, are my defender.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 54:7–8; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Psalms 126:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Into your hands I will commit my spirit: you have redeemed me, O Lord God of truth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 12:19; 2 Corinthians 12:7; Psalms 119:117"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have hated them that idly persist in vanities: but I have hoped in the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:29; Psalms 143:7; Psalms 30:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will exult and be glad in your mercy: for you have looked upon my affliction; you have saved my soul from distresses.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:6–7; Psalms 34:6; Psalms 130:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And you have not shut me up into the hands of the enemy: you have set my feet in a wide place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 6:5; Psalms 115:17–18; Esias 38:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pity me, O Lord, for I am afflicted: my eye is troubled with indignation, my soul and by belly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 54:4; Psalms 143:1; Psalms 143:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my life is spent with grief, and my years with groanings: my strength has been weakened through poverty, and my bones are troubled.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 31:4; Jeremias 31:13–14; Ecclesiastes 3:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I became a reproach among all my enemies, but exceedingly so to my neighbors, and a fear to my acquaintance: they that saw me without fled from me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 145:2; Psalms 146:1–2; Psalms 44:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have been forgotten as a dead man out of mind: I am become as a broken vessel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I heard the slander of many that lived round about: when they were gathered together against me, they took counsel to take my life.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I hoped in you, O Lord: I said, You are my God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">My lots are in your hands: deliver me from the hand of my enemies,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">and from them that persecute me. Make your face to shine upon your servant: save me in your mercy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord, let me not be ashamed, for I have called upon you: let the ungodly be ashamed, and brought down to Hades.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let the deceitful lips become dumb, which speak iniquity against the righteous with pride and scorn.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">How abundant is the multitude of your goodness, O Lord, which you have laid up for them that fear you! you have wrought it out for them that hope on you, in the presence of the sons of men.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will hide them in the secret of your presence from the vexation of man: you will screen them in a tabernacle from the contradiction of tongues.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Blessed be the Lord: for he has magnified his mercy in a fortified city.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I said in my extreme fear, I am cast out from the sight of your eyes: therefore you did listen, O Lord, to the voice of my supplication when I cried to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Love the Lord, all you⌃ his saints: for the Lord seeks for truth, and renders a reward to them that deal very proudly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be of good courage, and let your heart be strengthened, all you⌃ that hope in the Lord.</span></span>
