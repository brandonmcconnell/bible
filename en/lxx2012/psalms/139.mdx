<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 23:24; Job 31:4; Genesis 28:10–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have sharpened their tongue as the tongue of a serpent; the poison of asps is under their lips. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 19:14; Hebrews 4:12–13; Psalms 50:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Keep me, O Lord, from the hand of the sinner; rescue me from unjust men; who have purposed to overthrow my goings.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:27; Psalms 34:7; Revelation 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The proud have hid a snare for me, and have stretched out ropes for snares for my feet; they set a stumbling block for me near the path. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 11:33; Job 42:3; Job 26:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said to the Lord, You are my God; listen, O Lord, to the voice of my supplication.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 23:23–24; Jonas 1:3; Jonas 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord God, the strength of my salvation; you have screened my head in the day of battle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 9:2–4; Jonas 2:2; Job 34:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me not, O Lord, to the sinner,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">according to my desire: they have devised mischief against me; forsake me not, lest they should be exalted. Pause.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 19:6; Psalms 74:16–17; Malachias 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for the head of them that compass me, the mischief of their lips shall cover them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; Esias 41:13; Psalms 73:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Coals of fire shall fall upon them on the earth; and you shall cast them down in afflictions: they shall not bear up under them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 12:22; Jeremias 23:24; Psalms 94:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">A talkative man shall not prosper on the earth: evils shall hunt the unrighteous man to destruction.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Job 34:22; Hebrews 4:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know that the Lord will maintain the cause of the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. singular.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">poor, and the right of the needy ones.</span><br /></span>
