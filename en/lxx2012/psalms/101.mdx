<div className="hs">(102) A Prayer for the Poor; when he is deeply afflicted, and pours out his supplication before the Lord.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 89:1; Psalms 71:22–23; Psalms 145:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Hear my prayer, O Lord, and let my cry come to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 119:106; Kings III 9:4; 1 Timothy 3:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Turn not away your face from me: in the day when I am afflicted, incline your ear to me: in the day when I shall call upon you, speedily hear me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 119:37; Job 31:1; Kings II 11:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my days have vanished like smoke, and my bones have been parched like a stick.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 11:20; 2 Corinthians 6:14–16; Matthew 7:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am blighted like grass, and my heart is dried up; for I have forgotten to eat my bread.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 15:3; Psalms 18:27; Proverbs 30:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">By reason of the voice of my groaning, my bone has cleaved to my flesh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 119:63; Luke 12:43–44; Revelation 21:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have become like a pelican of the wilderness;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 4:10–12; Acts 5:1–10; Psalms 52:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have become like an owl in a ruined house. I have watched, and am become as a sparrow dwelling alone on a roof.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 75:10; Jeremias 21:12; Proverbs 16:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the day long my enemies have reproached me; and they that praised me have sworn against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">For I have eaten ashes as it were bread, and mingled my drink with weeping;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">because of your anger and your wrath: for you have lifted me up, and dashed me down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">My days have declined like a shadow; and I am withered like grass.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, Lord, endure for ever, and your memorial to generation and generation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You shall arise, and have mercy upon Sion: for it is time to have mercy upon her, for the set time is come.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">For your servants have taken pleasure in her stones, and they shall pity her dust.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">So the nations shall fear your name, O Lord, and all kings your glory.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord shall build up Sion,</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, then shall be.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and shall appear in his glory.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has had regard to the prayer of the lowly, and has not despised their petition.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let this be written for another generation; and the people that shall be created shall praise the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">For he has looked out from the height of his sanctuary; the Lord looked upon the earth from heaven;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">to hear the groaning of the fettered ones, to loosen the sons of the slain;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">to proclaim the name of the Lord in Sion, and his praise in Jerusalem;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">when the people are gathered together, and the kings, to serve the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">He answered him in the way of his strength: tell me the fewness of my days.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Take me not away in the midst of my days: your years are through all generations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. plural.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">beginning you, O Lord, did lay the foundation of the earth; and the heavens are the works of your hands.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall perish, but you remain: and they all shall wax old as a garment; and as a vesture shall you fold them, and they shall be changed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you are the same, and your years shall not fail.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <span className="pl pl-1">The children of your servants shall dwell securely, and their seed shall</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, be directed aright.">c</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">prosper for ever.</span></span>
