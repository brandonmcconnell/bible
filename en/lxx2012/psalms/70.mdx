<div className="hs">(71) By David, a Psalm sung by the sons of Jonadab, and the first that were taken captive.</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 40:13–17; Psalms 71:12; Psalms 143:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord, I have hoped in you: let me never be put to shame.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 35:26; John 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">In your righteousness deliver me and rescue me: incline your ear to me, and save me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 40:15; Acts 1:18; Jezekiel 26:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be to me a protecting God, and a strong hold to save me: for you are my fortress and my refuge.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Lamentations 3:25; Psalms 40:16; Psalms 35:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me, O my God, from the hand of the sinner, from the hand of the transgressor and unjust man.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:17; Psalms 141:1; Psalms 69:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you are my support, O Lord; O Lord, you are my hope from my youth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">On you have I been stayed from the womb: from the belly of my mother you are my protector: of you is my</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. hymn singing.">a</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">praise continually.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am become as it were a wonder to many: but you are my strong helper.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let my mouth be filled with praise, that I may hymn your glory, and your majesty all the day.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cast me not off at the time of old age; forsake me not when my strength fails.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For my enemies have spoken against me; and they that lay wait for my soul have taken counsel together,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">saying, God has forsaken him: persecute you⌃ and take him; for there is none to deliver him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, go not far from me, O my God, draw near to my help.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let those that plot against my soul be ashamed and utterly fail: let those that seek my hurt be clothed with shame and dishonor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I will hope continually, and will praise you more and more.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">My mouth shall declare your righteousness openly, and your salvation all the day; for I am not acquainted with the affairs of men.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will go on in the might of the Lord: O Lord, I will make mention of your righteousness only.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">O God, you have taught me from my youth, and until now will I declare your wonders;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> <span className="pl pl-1">even until I am old and advanced in years. O God, forsake me not; until I shall have declared your arm to all the generation that is to come:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> <span className="pl pl-1">even your power and your righteousness, O God, up to the highest heavens, even the mighty works which you have done: O God, who is like to you?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> <span className="pl pl-1">What afflictions many and sore have you showed me! yet you did turn and quicken me, and brought me again from the depths of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">You did multiply your righteousness, and did turn and comfort me, and brought me again out of the depths of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will also therefore give thanks to you, O God, because of your truth, on an instrument of psalmody: I will sing psalms to you on the harp, O Holy One of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> <span className="pl pl-1">My lips shall rejoice when I sing to you; and my soul, which you have redeemed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Moreover also my tongue shall</span><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. meditate.">b</a></sup><br /><span className="pl pl-1">dwell all the day upon your righteousness; when they shall be ashamed and confounded that seek my hurt.</span></span>
