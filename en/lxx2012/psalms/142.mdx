<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cause me to hear your mercy in the morning; for I have hoped in you; make known to me, O Lord, the way wherein I should walk; for I have lifted up my soul to you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deliver me from my enemies, O Lord; for I have fled to you for refuge.</span><br /></span>
