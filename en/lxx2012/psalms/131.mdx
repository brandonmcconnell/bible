<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:20; Mark 10:15; Psalms 43:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">how he swore to the Lord, and vowed to the God of Jacob, saying,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; Esias 26:4; Psalms 146:5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. if I shall, etc. Hebraism.">a</a></sup><span className="pl pl-1">I will not go into the tabernacle of my house; I will not go up to the couch of my bed;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will not give sleep to my eyes, nor slumber to my eyelids, nor rest to my temples,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> <span className="pl pl-1">until I find a place for the Lord, a tabernacle for the God of Jacob.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Behold, we heard of it in Ephratha; we found it in the fields of the wood.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let us enter into his tabernacles: let us worship at the place where his feet stood.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your priests shall clothe themselves with righteousness; and your saints shall exult.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the sake of your servant David turn not away the face of your anointed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord swore in truth to David, and he will not annul it, saying, Of the fruit of your body will I set a king upon your throne.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> <span className="pl pl-1">If your children will keep my covenant, and these my testimonies which I shall teach them, their children also shall sit upon your throne for ever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord has elected Sion, he has chosen her for a habitation for himself, saying,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is my rest for ever: here will I dwell; for I have chosen it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will surely bless her provision: I will satisfy her poor with bread.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will clothe her priests with salvation; and her saints shall greatly exult.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">There will I cause to spring up a horn to David: I have prepared a lamp for my anointed.</span><br /></span>
