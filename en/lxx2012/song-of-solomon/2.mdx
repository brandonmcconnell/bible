<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:13; Song of Songs 4:7–8; Revelation 22:17"> My kinsman answers, and says to me, Rise up, come, my companion, my fair one, my dove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 60:1–2; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Esias 54:6–8"> For, behold, the winter is past, the rain is gone, it has departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 55:12; Colossians 3:16; Psalms 89:15"> The flowers are seen in the land; the time of pruning has arrived; the voice of the turtle-dove has been heard in our land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:10; Song of Songs 7:11–13; Esias 55:10–11"> The fig tree has put forth its young figs, the vines put forth the tender grape, they yield a smell: arise, come, my companion, my fair one, my dove; yes, come.</span>
