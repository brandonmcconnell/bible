<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Song of Songs 6:2; Song of Songs 4:16; Revelation 22:17"> Let my kinsman come down into his garden, and eat the fruit of his choice berries. I am come into my garden, my sister, my spouse: I have gathered my myrrh with my spices; I have eaten my bread with my honey; I have drunk my wine with my milk. Eat, O friends, and drink; yes, brethren, drink abundantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 3:20; Song of Songs 2:14; Song of Songs 6:9"> I sleep, but my heart is awake: the voice of my kinsman knocks at the door, saying, Open, open to me, my companion, my sister, my dove, my perfect one: for my head is filled with dew, and my locks with the drops of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:7; Romans 7:22–23; Proverbs 13:4"> I have put off my coat; how shall I put it on? I have washed my feet, how shall I defile them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:4; Genesis 43:30; 2 Corinthians 8:16"> My kinsman put forth his hand by the hole of the door, and my belly moved for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Song of Songs 5:13; Ephesians 3:17; Song of Songs 5:2"> I rose up to open to my kinsman; my hands dropped myrrh, my fingers choice myrrh, on the handles of the lock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Song of Songs 5:2; Revelation 3:19; Osee 5:15"> I opened to my kinsman; my kinsman was gone: my soul failed at his speech: I sought him, but found him not; I called him, but he answered me not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Song of Songs 3:3; Matthew 21:33–41; Acts 5:40–41"> The watchman that go their rounds in the city found me, they struck me, they wounded me; the keepers of the walls took away my veil from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:7; Song of Songs 2:5; Song of Songs 3:5"> I have charged you, O daughters of Jerusalem, by the powers and the virtues of the field: if you⌃ should find my kinsman, what are you⌃ to say to him? That I am wounded with love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:8; Song of Songs 6:1; Song of Songs 6:9–10"> What is your kinsman more than another kinsman, O you beautiful among women? what is your kinsman more than another kinsman, that you have so charged us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 45:2; Song of Songs 2:1; Philippians 2:9–11"> My kinsman is white and ruddy, chosen out from myriads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Song of Songs 7:5; Song of Songs 5:2; Daniel 7:9"> His head is as very fine gold, his locks are flowing, black as a raven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Song of Songs 4:1; Song of Songs 1:15; Hebrews 4:13"> His eyes are as doves, by the pools of waters, washed with milk, sitting by the pools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:10; Song of Songs 2:1; Song of Songs 6:2"> His cheeks are as bowls of spices pouring forth perfumes: his lips are lilies, dropping choice myrrh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 24:10; Esias 54:11; Jezekiel 1:26–28"> His hands are as turned gold set with beryl: his belly is an ivory tablet on a sapphire stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Revelation 1:15–16; Kings III 4:33; Matthew 17:2"> His legs are marble pillars set on golden sockets: his form is as Libanus, choice as the cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 45:2; Song of Songs 7:9; Esias 9:6–7"> His throat is most sweet, and altogether desirable. This is my kinsman, and this is my companion, O daughters of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Whither is your kinsman gone, you beautiful among women? whither has your kinsman turned aside? tell us, and we will seek him with you.</span>
