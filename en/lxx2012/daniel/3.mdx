<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ambacum 2:19; Jeremias 16:20; Esias 46:6"> In his eighteenth year Nabuchodonosor the king made a golden image, its height was sixty cubits, its breadth six cubits: and he set it up in the plain of Deira, in the province of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 3:27; Proverbs 29:12; Kings III 12:32"> And he sent forth to gather the governors, and the captains, and the heads of provinces, chiefs, and princes, and those who were in authority, and all the rulers of districts, to come to the dedication of the image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Revelation 17:13; Revelation 13:13–16"> So the heads of provinces, the governors, the captains, the chiefs, the great princes, those who were in authority, and all the rulers of districts, were gathered to the dedication of the image which king Nabuchodonosor had set up; and they stood before the image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 4:1; Daniel 6:25; Michaeas 6:16"> Then a herald cried aloud, To you it is commanded, you⌃ peoples, tribes, and languages,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 3:15; Daniel 3:10; Daniel 3:7"> at what hour you⌃ shall hear the sound of the trumpet, and pipe, and harp, and sackbut, and lute, and every kind of music, you⌃ shall fall down and worship the golden image which king Nabuchodonosor has set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:50; Matthew 13:42; Jeremias 29:22"> And whoever shall not fall down and worship, in the same hour he shall be cast into the burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 17:8; Revelation 13:3; Revelation 13:8"> And it came to pass when the nations heard the sound of the trumpet, and pipe, and harp, and sackbut, and lute, and all kinds of music, all the nations, tribes, and languages, fell down and worshipped the golden image which king Nabuchodonosor had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:12–16; Daniel 6:12–13; Esther 3:8–9"> Then came near certain Chaldeans, and accused the Jews to the king, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 5:10; Daniel 6:6; Daniel 6:21"> O king, live for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 3:4–7; Daniel 6:12; Esther 3:12–14"> You, O king, have made a decree that every man who shall hear the sound of the trumpet, and pipe, and harp, sackbut, and lute, and all kinds of music,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and shall not fall down and worship the golden image, shall be cast into the burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 6:13; Daniel 2:49; Acts 17:7"> There are certain Jews whom you have appointed over the affairs of the province of Babylon, Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, who have not obeyed your decree, O king: they serve not your gods, and worship not the golden image which you have set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; Daniel 2:12; Esther 3:5–6"> Then Nabuchodonosor in wrath and anger commanded to bring Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago: and they were brought before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 50:2; Daniel 3:1; Esias 46:1"> And Nabuchodonosor answered and said to them, Is it true, Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, that you⌃ serve not my gods, and worship not the golden image which I have set up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Chronicles II 32:15–17; Exodus 5:2"> Now then if you⌃ be ready, whenever you⌃ shall hear the sound of the trumpet, and pipe, and harp, and sackbut, and lute, and harmony, and every kind of music, to fall down and worship the golden image which I have made; well: but if you⌃ worship not, in the same hour you⌃ shall be cast into the burning fiery furnace; and who is the God that shall deliver you out of my hand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 1:7; Daniel 3:12"> Then answered Sedrach, Misach and Abdenago and said to king Nabuchodonosor, We have no need to answer you concerning this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Luke 1:37; Hebrews 7:25"> For our God whom we serve is in the heavens, able to deliver us from the burning fiery furnace, and he will rescue us from your hands, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Matthew 10:39; Revelation 2:10–11"> But if not, be it known to you, O king, that we will not serve your gods, nor worship the image which you have set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 3:13; Leviticus 26:24; Proverbs 21:24"> Then Nabuchodonosor was filled with wrath, and the form of his countenance was changed toward Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago: and he gave orders to heat the furnace seven times more than usual, until it should burn to the uttermost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 16:23; Acts 16:25; Daniel 3:15"> And he commanded mighty men to bind Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, and to cast them into the burning fiery furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then those men were bound with their coats, and caps, and hose, and were cast into the midst of the burning fiery furnace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:33; Proverbs 21:18; Proverbs 11:8"> forasmuch as the king's word prevailed; and the furnace was made exceeding hot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 6:16–17; Jeremias 38:6; Psalms 34:19"> Then these three men, Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, fell bound into the midst of the burning furnace, and walked in the midst of the flame, singing praise to God, and blessing the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 6:7; Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:9–10"> And Nabuchodonosor heard them singing praises; and he wondered, and rose up in haste, and said to his nobles, Did we not cast three men bound into the midst of the fire? and they said to the king, Yes, O king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 43:2; Acts 28:5; Mark 16:18"> And the king said, But I see four men loose, and walking in the midst of the fire, and there has no harm happened to them; and the appearance of the fourth is like the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 3:17; Esias 52:12; Revelation 19:5"> Then Nabuchodonosor drew near to the door of the burning fiery furnace, and said, Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, you⌃ servants of the most high God, proceed forth, and come here. So Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, came forth out of the midst of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esias 43:2; Hebrews 11:34; Esias 26:11"> Then were assembled the satraps, and captains, and heads of provinces, and the royal princes; and they saw the men, and perceived that the fire had not had power against their bodies, and the hair of their head was not burnt, and their coats were not scorched, nor was the smell of fire upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 14:7–8; Psalms 34:7–8; Psalms 84:11–12"> And king Nabuchodonosor answered and said, Blessed be the God of Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, who has sent his angel, and delivered his servants, because they trusted in him; and they have changed the king's word, and delivered their bodies to be burnt, that they might not serve nor worship any god, except their own God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:5; Psalms 76:10; Deuteronomy 32:31"> Therefore I publish a decree: Every people, tribe, or language, that shall speak reproachfully against the God of Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago shall be destroyed, and their houses shall be plundered: because there is no other God who shall be able to deliver thus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 2:49; Kings I 2:30; Daniel 3:12"> Then the king promoted Sedrach, Misach, and Abdenago, in the province of Babylon, and advanced them, and gave them authority to rule over all the Jews who were in his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> King Nabuchodonosor to all nations, tribes, and tongues, who dwell in all the earth; Peace be multiplied to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> It seemed good to me to declare to you the signs and wonders which the most high God has wrought with me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> how great and mighty they are: his kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and his power to all generations.</span>
