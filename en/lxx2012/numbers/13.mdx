<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And afterwards the people set forth from Aseroth, and encamped in the wilderness of Pharan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:22–25; Numbers 32:8; Numbers 34:18"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 12:16; Numbers 32:8; Deuteronomy 1:19"> Send for you men, and let them spy the land of the Chananites, which I give to the sons of Israel for a possession; one man for a tribe, you shall send them away according to their families, every one of them a prince.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Moses sent them out of the wilderness of Pharan by the word of the Lord; all these were the princes of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And these are their names: of the tribe of Ruben, Samuel the son of Zachur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 34:19; Numbers 14:6; Numbers 13:30"> Of the tribe of Symeon, Saphat the son of Suri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Of the tribe of Judah, Chaleb the son of Jephonne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 24:13; Numbers 11:28; Numbers 13:16"> Of the tribe of Issachar, Ilaal the son of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Of the tribe of Ephraim, Ause the son of Naue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Of the tribe of Benjamin, Phalti the son of Raphu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Of the tribe of Zabulon, Gudiel the son of Sudi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Of the tribe of Joseph of the sons of Manasse, Gaddi the son of Susi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Of the tribe of Dan, Amiel the son of Gamali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Of the tribe of Aser, Sathur the son of Michael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Of the tribe of Nephthali, Nabi the son of Sabi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 13:8; Exodus 17:9; Numbers 14:6"> Of the tribe of Gad, Gudiel the son of Macchi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 1:9; Genesis 12:9; Judges 1:19"> These are the names of the men whom Moses sent to spy out the land; and Moses called Ause the son of Naue, Joshua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:14; Exodus 3:8"> And Moses sent them to spy out the land of Chanaan, and said to them, Go up by this wilderness; and you⌃ shall go up to the mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and you⌃ shall see the land, what it is, and the people that dwells on it, whether it is strong or weak, or whether they are few or many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 31:6–8; Jezekiel 34:14"> And what the land is on which they dwell, whether it is good or bad; and what the cities are wherein these dwell, whether they dwell in walled cities or unwalled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 33:36; Numbers 27:14"> And what the land is, whether rich or<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. q. d. neglected">a</a></sup>poor; whether there are trees in it or no: and you⌃ shall persevere and take of the fruits of the land: and the days were the days of spring, the forerunners of the grape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:12; Psalms 78:43; Esias 19:11"> And they went up and surveyed the land from the wilderness of Sin to Rhoob, as men go in to Aemath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:24; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:24–25"> And they went up by the wilderness, and departed as far as Chebron; and there was Achiman, and Sessi, and Thelami, the progeny of Enach. Now Chebron was built seven years before Tanin of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And they came to the valley of the cluster and surveyed it; and they cut down thence a bough and one cluster of grapes upon it, and bore it on staves, and they took of the pomegranates and the figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Numbers 14:33–34"> And they called that place, The valley of the cluster, because of the cluster which the children of Israel cut down from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 20:1; Deuteronomy 1:19"> And they returned from thence, having surveyed the land, after forty days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Exodus 3:17; Exodus 13:5"> And they proceeded and came to Moses and Aaron and all the congregation of the children of Israel, to the wilderness of Pharan Cades; and they brought word to them and to all the congregation, and they showed the fruit of the land:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Deuteronomy 1:28; Numbers 13:33"> and they reported to him, and said, We came into the land into which you sent us, a land flowing with milk and honey; and this is the fruit of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 14:43; Judges 6:3; Genesis 15:19–21"> Only the nation that dwells upon it is bold, and they have very great and strong walled towns, and we saw there the children of Enach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Romans 8:31; Philippians 4:13"> And Amalec dwells in the land toward the south: and the Chettite and the Evite, and the Jebusite, and the Amorite dwells in the hill country: and the Chananite dwells by the sea, and by the river Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Joshua 14:8; Hebrews 3:19"> And Chaleb stayed the people from speaking<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, to.">b</a></sup>before Moses, and said to him, Nay, but we will go up by all means, and will inherit it, for we shall surely prevail against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Numbers 14:36–37; Jezekiel 36:13–14"> But the men that went up together with him said, We<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. do not.">c</a></sup>will not go up, for we shall not by any means be able to go up against the nation, for it is much stronger than we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; Esias 40:22; Genesis 6:4"> And they brought a horror of that land which they surveyed upon the children of Israel, saying, The land which we passed by to survey it, is a land that eats up its inhabitants; and all the people whom we saw in it are men of extraordinary stature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And there we saw the giants; and we were before them as locusts, yes even so were we before them.</span>
