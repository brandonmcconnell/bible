<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Jezekiel 19:14; Genesis 49:10"> Speak to the children of Israel, and take<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. a rod">a</a></sup>rods of them, according to the houses of their families, a rod from all their princes, according to the houses of their families, twelve rods, and write the name of each on his rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 6:20; Numbers 18:1; Numbers 3:2–3"> And write the name of Aaron on the rod of Levi; for it is one rod for each: they shall give them according to the tribe of the house of their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 30:36; Exodus 29:42–43; Exodus 25:16–22"> And you shall put them in the tabernacle of witness, before the testimony, where I will be made known to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 16:5; Numbers 17:8; Numbers 16:11"> And it shall be, the man whom I shall choose, his rod shall blossom; and I will remove from me the murmuring of the children of Israel, which they murmur against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Moses spoke to the children of Israel, and all their chiefs gave him a rod each, for one chief a rod, according to the house of their families, twelve rods; and the rod of Aaron was in the midst of the rods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Exodus 38:21; Numbers 18:2"> And Moses laid up the rods before the Lord in the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 17:24; John 15:1–6; Psalms 132:17–18"> And it came to pass on the morrow, that Moses and Aaron went into the tabernacle of witness; and, behold, the rod of Aaron for the house of Levi blossomed, and put forth a bud, and bloomed blossoms and produced almonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Moses brought forth all the rods from before the Lord to all the sons of Israel; and they looked, and each one took his rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Ephesians 5:6; Esias 1:2"> And the Lord said to Moses, Lay up the rod of Aaron before the testimonies to be kept as a sign for the children of the disobedient; and let their murmuring cease from me, and they shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Moses and Aaron did as the Lord commanded Moses, so did they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 26:11; Esias 57:16; Esias 6:5"> And the children of Israel spoke to Moses, saying, Behold, we are cut off, we are destroyed, we are consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Kings II 6:6–12; Genesis 3:3"> Every one that touches the tabernacle of the Lord, dies: shall we die utterly?</span>
