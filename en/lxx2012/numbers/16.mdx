<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jude 1:11; Deuteronomy 11:6; Exodus 6:21"> And Core the son of Isaar the son of Caath the son of Levi, and Dathan and Abiron, sons of Eliab, and Aun the son of Phaleth the son of Ruben, spoke;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 26:9; Jezekiel 23:10; Genesis 6:4"> and rose up before Moses, and two hundred and fifty men of the sons of Israel, chiefs of the assembly, chosen councillors, and men of renown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 106:16; Numbers 14:14; Exodus 19:6"> They rose up against Moses and Aaron, and said, Let it be enough for you that all the congregation are holy, and the Lord is among them; and why do you⌃ set up yourselves against the congregation of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 20:6; Joshua 7:6"> And when Moses heard it, he fell on his face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 65:4; Numbers 17:5; 2 Timothy 2:19"> And he spoke to Core and all his assembly, saying, God has visited and known those that are his and who are holy, and has brought them to himself; and whom he has chosen for himself, he has brought to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1; Numbers 16:46–48; Kings III 18:21–23"> This do you⌃: take to yourselves censers, Core and all his company;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 16:3; Ephesians 1:4; Kings III 18:17–18"> and put fire on them, and put incense on them before the Lord to-morrow; and it shall come to pass that the man whom the Lord has chosen, he shall be holy: let it be enough for you, you⌃ sons of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Moses said to Core, Listen to me, you⌃ sons of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 7:13; Deuteronomy 10:8; Jezekiel 44:10–11"> Is it a little thing for you, that the God of Israel has separated you from the congregation of Israel, and brought you near to himself to minister in the services of the tabernacle of the Lord, and to stand before the tabernacle to minister for them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 13:10; 3 John 1:9; Numbers 18:7"> And he has brought you near and all your brethren the sons of Levi with you, and do you⌃ seek to be priests also?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 16:7–8; Exodus 17:2; Numbers 16:3"> Thus it is with you and all your congregation which is gathered together against God: and who is Aaron, that you⌃ murmur against him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:13–14; Jude 1:8; Proverbs 29:9"> And Moses sent to call Dathan and Abiron sons of Eliab; and they said, We<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. do not.">a</a></sup>will not go up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Exodus 2:14; Numbers 11:5"> Is it a little thing that you have brought us up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Some read, out of.">b</a></sup>to a land flowing with milk and honey, to kill us in the wilderness, and that you altogether rule over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 20:24; Numbers 36:8–10; Numbers 20:5"> You are a prince, and have you brought us into a land flowing with milk and honey, and have you given us an inheritance of land and vineyards? would you have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. cut out.">c</a></sup>put out the eyes of those men? we<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. do not.">d</a></sup>will not go up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 4:4–5; 2 Corinthians 7:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:10"> And Moses was exceeding indignant, and said to the Lord, Do you take no heed to their sacrifice: I have not taken away<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="חפד for חפוד">e</a></sup>the desire of any one of them, neither have I hurt any one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 12:3; Kings I 12:7; 2 Timothy 2:14"> And Moses said to Core, Sanctify your company, and be ready before the Lord, you and Aaron and they, to-morrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings I 12:7"> And take each man his censer, and you⌃ shall put incense upon them, and shall bring each one his censer before the Lord, two hundred and fifty censers, and you and Aaron shall bring each his censer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And each man took his censer, and they put on them fire, and laid incense on them; and Moses and Aaron stood by the doors of the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 14:10; Numbers 16:42; Exodus 16:7"> And Core raised up against them all his company by the door of the tabernacle of witness; and the glory of the Lord appeared to all the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 16:45; Exodus 32:10; Psalms 73:19"> Separate your selves from the midst of this congregation, and I will consume them at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:16; Zacharias 12:1; Job 12:10"> And they fell on their faces, and said, O God, the God of spirits and of all flesh, if one man has sinned, shall the wrath of the Lord be upon the whole congregation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 16:21"> Speak to the congregation, saying, Depart from the company of Core round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 11:16–17; Numbers 11:25; Numbers 11:30"> And Moses rose up and went to Dathan and Abiron, and all the elders of Israel went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Esias 52:11; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Revelation 18:4"> And he spoke to the congregation, saying, Separate yourselves from the tents of these stubborn men, and touch nothing that belongs to them, lest you⌃ be consumed with them in all their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 9:4; Esias 28:14; Job 40:10–11"> And they stood aloof from the tent of Core round about; and Dathan and Abiron went forth and stood by the doors of their tents, and their wives and their children and their store.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 6:38; Exodus 3:12; John 14:11"> And Moses said, Hereby shall you⌃ know that the Lord has sent me to perform all these works, that I have not done them of myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 4:22; Kings III 22:28; Exodus 32:34"> If these men shall die according to the death of all men, if also their visitation shall be according to the visitation of all men, then the Lord has not sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 55:15; Numbers 16:33; Job 31:3"> But if the Lord shall show by a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, vision. Some copies read χάσματι">f</a></sup>wonder, and the earth shall open her mouth and swallow them up, and their houses, and their tents, and all that belongs to them, and they shall go down alive into Hades, then you⌃ shall know that these men have provoked the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 106:17–18; Numbers 27:3; Deuteronomy 11:6"> And when he ceased speaking all these words, the ground clave asunder beneath them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:17; Numbers 26:11; Revelation 12:16"> And the ground opened, and swallowed them up, and their houses, and all the men that were with Core, and their cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jezekiel 32:18; Esias 14:9; Psalms 9:15"> And they went down and all that they had, alive into Hades; and the ground covered them, and they perished from the midst of the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zacharias 14:5; Esias 33:3; Revelation 6:15–17"> And all Israel round about them fled from the sound of them, for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. saying.">g</a></sup>they said, Lest the earth swallow us up also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Leviticus 10:2; Psalms 106:18"> And fire went forth from the Lord, and devoured the two hundred and fifty men that offered incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the Lord said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 27:28; Numbers 16:18; Numbers 16:7"> and to Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, Take up the brazen censers out of the midst of the men that have been burnt, and scatter the strange fire yonder, for they have sanctified the censers</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 26:10; Jezekiel 14:8; 2 Peter 2:6"> of these sinners against their own souls, and do you make them beaten plates a covering to the altar, because they were brought before the Lord and hallowed; and they became a sign to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest took the brazen censers, which the men who had been burnt brought near, and they put them as a covering on the altar:</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 18:4–7; Chronicles II 26:16–21"> a memorial to the children of Israel that no stranger might draw near, who is not of the seed of Aaron, to offer incense before the Lord; so he shall not be as Core and as they that conspired with him, as the Lord spoke to him by the hand of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 16:1–7; Psalms 106:23; Numbers 14:2"> And the children of Israel murmured the next day against Moses and Aaron, saying, You⌃ have killed the people of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 16:19; Leviticus 9:23; Numbers 20:6"> And it came to pass when the congregation combined against Moses and Aaron, that they ran impetuously to the tabernacle of witness; and the cloud covered it, and the glory of the Lord appeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Moses and Aaron went in, in front of the tabernacle of witness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 16:21–22; Numbers 16:24; Numbers 20:6"> Depart out of the midst of this congregation, and I will consume them at once: and they fell upon their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; Chronicles I 27:24; Leviticus 10:6"> And Moses said to Aaron, Take a censer, and put on it fire from the altar, and put incense on it, and carry it away quickly into the camp, and make atonement for them; for wrath is gone forth from the presence of the Lord, it has begun to destroy the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Deuteronomy 33:10–11; Psalms 106:29"> And Aaron took as Moses spoke to him, and ran among the congregation, for already the plague had begun among the people; and he put on incense, and made an atonement for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Kings II 24:25; Hebrews 7:24–25; Numbers 16:18"> And he stood between the dead and the living, and the plague ceased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 25:9; Numbers 16:32–35; Hebrews 2:1–3"> And they that died in the plague were fourteen thousand and seven hundred, besides those that died on account of Core.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Chronicles I 21:26–30; Numbers 16:43"> And Aaron returned to Moses to the door of the tabernacle of witness, and the plague ceased.</span>
