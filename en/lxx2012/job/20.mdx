<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 42:9; Job 11:1"> Then Sophar the Minaean answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Psalms 31:22; Job 20:3"> I did not suppose that you would answer thus: neither do you⌃ understand more than I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 33:3; Job 20:2; Psalms 78:2–5"> I will hear my shameful reproach; and the spirit of my understanding answers me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 9:1–3; Job 15:10; Job 32:7"> Have you not known these things of old, from the time that man was set upon the earth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 37:35–36; Job 18:5–6; Acts 12:22–23"> But the mirth of the ungodly is a signal downfall, and the joy of transgressors is destruction:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 14:13–14; Obdias 1:3–4; Genesis 11:4"> although his gifts should go up to heaven, and his sacrifice reach the clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 7:10; Kings III 14:10; Job 4:20"> For when he shall seem to be now established, then he shall utterly perish: and they that knew him shall say, Where is he?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:5; Psalms 73:20; Esias 29:7–8"> Like a dream that has fled away, he shall not be found; and he has fled like a vision of the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 7:8; Job 7:10; Job 8:18"> The eye has looked upon him, but shall not see him again; and his place shall no longer perceive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 20:18; Luke 19:8; Job 27:16–17"> Let his inferiors destroy his children, and let his hands kindle the fire of sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 13:26; Psalms 25:7; Job 21:26"> His bones have been filled with vigour of his youth, and it shall lie down with him in the dust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 15:16; Psalms 10:7; Ecclesiastes 11:9"> Though evil be sweet in his mouth, though he will hide it under his tongue;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 9:43–49; Romans 8:13; Matthew 5:29–30"> though he will not spare it, and will not leave it, but will keep it in the midst of his throat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 3:13; Psalms 51:8–9; Psalms 32:3–4"> yet he shall not at all be able to help himself; the gall of an asp is in his belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 23:8; Matthew 27:3–4"> His wealth unjustly collected shall be vomited up; a messenger of wrath shall drag him out of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Romans 3:13; Deuteronomy 32:24"> And let him suck the poison of serpents, and let the serpent's tongue kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Job 29:6; Luke 16:24"> Let him not see the milk of the pastures, nor the supplies of honey and butter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:10; Job 20:15; Jeremias 51:44"> He has laboured unprofitably and in vain, for wealth of which he shall not taste: it is as a lean thing, unfit for food, which he can’t swallow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 35:9; Proverbs 14:31; James 5:4"> For he has broken down the houses of many mighty men: and he has plundered an habitation, though he built it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13–14; Esias 57:20–21"> There is no security to his possessions; he shall not be saved by his desire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 17:11; Job 15:29; Luke 16:24–25"> There is nothing remaining of his provisions; therefore his goods shall not flourish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 15:29; Revelation 18:7; Kings IV 24:2"> But when he shall seem to be just satisfied, he shall be straitened; and all distress shall come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 78:30–31; Numbers 11:33; Psalms 11:6"> If by any means he would fill his belly, let God send upon him the fury of wrath; let him bring a torrent of pains upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esias 24:18; Amos 5:19; Kings II 22:35"> And he shall by no means escape from the power of the sword; let the brazen bow wound him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 16:13; Job 18:11; Deuteronomy 32:41"> And let the arrow pierce through his body; and let the stars be against his dwelling-place: let terrors come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 21:9; Psalms 120:4; Esias 30:33"> And let all darkness wait for him: a fire that burns not out shall consume him; and let a stranger plague his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esias 26:21; Luke 12:2–3; Job 16:18"> And let the heaven reveal his iniquities, and the earth rise up against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 27:14–19; Revelation 18:17; James 5:1–3"> Let destruction bring his house to an end; let a day of wrath come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 27:13; Job 31:2–3; Psalms 11:5–6"> This is the portion of an ungodly man from the Lord, and the possession of his goods appointed him by the all-seeing God.</span>
