<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 13:2–5; Proverbs 26:4; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23"> But I also have a heart as well as you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 30:1; Job 17:6; Psalms 91:15"> For a righteous and blameless man has become a subject for mockery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:8; Proverbs 13:9; Psalms 17:5"> For it had been ordained that he should fall under others at the appointed time, and that his houses should be spoiled by transgressors: let not however any one trust that, being evil, he shall be held guiltless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:24; Psalms 73:11–12; Psalms 17:14"> even as many as provoke the Lord, as if there were indeed to be no inquisition made of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 1:3; Proverbs 6:6; Jeremias 8:7"> But ask now the beasts, if they may speak to you; and the birds of the air, if they may declare to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Tell the earth, if it may speak to you: and the fishes of the sea shall explain to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 41:20; Jeremias 27:5–6; Romans 11:36"> Who then has not known in all these things, that the hand of the Lord has made them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 17:28; Job 33:4; Acts 17:25"> Whereas the life of all living things is in his hand, and the breath of every man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 34:3; Hebrews 5:14; Job 6:30"> For the ear tries words, and the palate tastes meats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 32:7; Job 15:10; Job 8:8"> In length of time is wisdom, and in long life knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 9:4; Job 36:5; Proverbs 2:6–7"> With him are wisdom and power, with him counsel and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 3:7; Esias 22:22; Malachias 1:4"> If he should cast down, who will build up? if he should shut up against man, who shall open?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 17:1; Amos 5:8; Genesis 6:13"> If he should withhold the water, he will dry the earth: and if he should let it loose, he overthrows and destroys it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 13:9; Jezekiel 14:9; Kings III 22:22–23"> With him are strength and power: he has knowledge and understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 29:14; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; Kings II 17:23"> He leads counselors away captive, and maddens the judges of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 2:21; Psalms 116:16; Chronicles II 33:11–14"> He seats kings upon thrones, and girds their loins with a girdle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 45:1; Esias 37:36–38; Revelation 17:14"> He sends away priests into captivity, and overthrows the mighty ones of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 32:9; Job 17:4; Job 12:24"> He changes the lips of the trusty, and he knows the understanding of the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Esias 23:9; Kings IV 9:34–37"> He pours dishonor upon princes, and heals the lowly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; 1 Corinthians 4:5; 1 Corinthians 2:10"> Revealing deep things out of darkness: and he has brought into light the shadow of death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esias 9:3; Esias 26:15; Esias 60:22"> Causing the nations to wander, and destroying them: overthrowing the nations, and leading them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Job 12:20; Osee 7:11"> Perplexing the minds of the princes of the earth: and he causes them to wander in a way, they have not known, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 5:14; Psalms 107:27; Esias 19:14"> Let them grope in darkness, and let there be no light, and let them wander as a drunken man.</span>
