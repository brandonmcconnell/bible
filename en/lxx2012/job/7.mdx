<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 39:4; Job 14:5–6; Job 5:7"> Is not the life of man upon earth a state of trial? and his existence as that of a hireling by the day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Deuteronomy 24:15; James 5:4"> Or as a servant that fears his master, and one who has grasped a shadow? or as a hireling waiting for his pay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Psalms 39:5"> So have I also endured months of vanity, and nights of pain have been appointed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:67; Job 7:13–14; Job 17:12"> Whenever I lie down, I say, When will it be day? and whenever I rise up, again I say when will it be evening? and I am full of pains from evening to morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 17:14; Esias 14:11; Job 2:7–8"> And my body is covered with loathsome worms; and I waste away, scraping off clods of dust from my eruption.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 9:25; Job 17:15; Job 17:11"> And my life is lighter than a word, and has perished in vain hope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:14; Psalms 78:39; Jeremias 15:15"> Remember then that my life is breath, and my eye shall not yet again see good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 20:9; Job 8:18; Psalms 37:36"> The eye of him that sees me shall not see me again: your eyes are upon me, and I am no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 30:15; Job 10:21; Kings II 14:14"> I am as a cloud that is cleared away from the sky: for if a man go down to the grave, he shall not come up again:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 8:18; Job 20:9; Psalms 103:16"> and he shall surely not return to his own house, neither shall his place know him any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 40:9; Kings I 1:10; Job 10:1"> Then neither will I refrain my mouth: I will speak being in distress; being in anguish I will disclose the bitterness of my soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 32:2–3; Lamentations 3:7; Job 38:6–11"> Am I a sea, or a serpent, that you have set a watch over me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 6:6; Psalms 77:4; Job 9:27–28"> I said that my bed should comfort me, and I would privately counsel with myself on my couch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 2:1; Genesis 41:8; Genesis 40:5–7"> You scare me with dreams, and do terrify me with visions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 27:5; Kings II 17:23"> You will separate life from my spirit; and yet keep my bones from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 19:4; Job 10:1; Job 9:21"> For I shall not live for ever, that I should patiently endure: depart from me, for my life is vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 2:6; Psalms 8:4; Psalms 144:3"> For what is man, that you have magnified him? or that you give heed to him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 38:12–13; Jeremias 9:7; 1 Peter 1:7"> Will you visit him till the morning, and judge him till the time of rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 9:18; Job 14:6; Psalms 13:1–3"> How long do you not let me alone, nor let me go, until I shall swallow down my spittle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 3:12; Job 7:11–12; Job 3:24"> If I have sinned, what shall I be able to do, O you that understand the mind of men? why have you made me as your accuser, and why am I a burden to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; Job 10:14; 1 John 1:9"> Why have you not forgotten my iniquity, and purged my sin? but now I shall depart to the earth; and in the morning I am no more.</span>
