<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 27:1; Job 13:12; Numbers 23:7"> And Job continued and said in his parable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 1:10; Job 1:1–5; Jeremias 31:28"> Oh that I were as in months past, wherein God preserved me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 18:28; John 8:12; Job 18:6"> As when his lamp shone over my head; when by his light I walked through darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 25:14; Proverbs 3:32; Psalms 27:5"> As when I steadfastly pursued my ways, when God took care of my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 128:3; Psalms 127:3–5; Deuteronomy 33:27–29"> When I was very fruitful, and my children were about me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 81:16; Job 20:17; Deuteronomy 32:13–14"> when my ways were moistened with butter, and the mountains flowed for me with milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zacharias 8:16; Ruth 4:1–2; Deuteronomy 16:18"> When I went forth early in the city, and the seat was placed for me in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 16:31; Romans 13:7"> The young men saw me, and hid themselves: and all the old men stood up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 21:5; Job 40:4; Proverbs 10:19"> And the great men ceased speaking, and laid their finger on their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 137:6; Jezekiel 3:26"> And they that heard me blessed me, and their tongue clave to their throat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Job 31:20; Proverbs 29:2"> For the ear heard, and blessed me; and the eye saw me, and turned aside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 72:12; Proverbs 21:13; James 1:27"> For I saved the poor out of the hand of the oppressor, and helped the fatherless who had no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philemon 1:7; Job 22:9; Job 31:19–20"> Let the blessing of the perishing one come upon me; yes, the mouth of the widow has blessed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 61:10; Esias 59:17; Psalms 132:9"> Also I put on righteousness, and clothed myself with judgment like a mantle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 10:31; Matthew 11:5; 1 Corinthians 12:12–31"> I was the eye of the blind, and the foot of the lame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:7; Psalms 68:5; Job 31:18"> I was the father of the helpless; and I searched out the cause which I knew not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 3:7; Proverbs 30:14; Kings I 17:35"> And I broke the jaw teeth of the unrighteous; I plucked the spoil out of the midst of their teeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 30:6–7; Genesis 32:12; Job 42:16–17"> And I said, My age shall continue as the stem of a palm tree; I shall live a long while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Jeremias 17:8; Osee 14:5–7"> My root was spread out by the water, and the dew would lodge on my crop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:24; Esias 40:31; Psalms 18:34"> My glory was fresh in me, and by bow prospered in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 32:11–12; Job 29:9–10"> Men heard me, and gave heed, and they were silent at my counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:2; Song of Songs 4:11; Matthew 22:46"> At my word they spoke not again, and they were very gland whenever I spoke to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Osee 6:3; Psalms 72:6; Zacharias 10:1"> As the thirsty earth expecting the rain, so they waited for my speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 89:15; Psalms 4:6; Psalms 126:1"> Were I to laugh on them, they would not believe it; and the light of my face has not failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:3–4; 1 Thessalonians 3:2–3; Genesis 14:14–17"> I chose out their way, and sat chief, and lived as a king in the midst of warriors, as one comforting mourners.</span>
