<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 4:1"> But Job answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 31:6; Job 23:2; Job 4:5"> Oh that one would indeed weigh the wrath that is upon me, and take up my griefs in a balance together!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Job 23:2; Psalms 40:5"> And verily they would be heavier than the sand by the seashore: but, as it seems, my words are vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 38:2; Job 30:15; Psalms 88:15–16"> For the arrows of the Lord are in my body, whose violence drinks up my blood: whenever I am going to speak, they pierce me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joel 1:18–20; Jeremias 14:6; Psalms 42:1"> What then? will the wild ass bray for nothing, if he is not seeking food? or again, will the ox low at the manger, when he has a fodder?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 34:3; Luke 14:34; Colossians 4:6"> Shall bread be eaten without salt? or again, is there taste in empty words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 3:24; Jezekiel 4:16; Daniel 10:3"> For my wrath can’t cease; for I perceive my food as the smell of a lion to be loathsome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 119:81; Job 17:14–16; Job 6:11–13"> For oh that he would grant my desire, and my petition might come, and the Lord would grant my hope!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 19:4; Jonas 4:3; Jonas 4:8"> Let the Lord begin and wound me, but let him not utterly destroy me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 23:12; Leviticus 19:2; Esias 57:15"> Let the grave be my city, upon the walls of which I have leaped: I will not shrink from it; for I have not denied the holy words of my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 103:14–16; Psalms 102:23; Psalms 39:5"> For what is my strength, that I continue? what is my time, that my soul endures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 41:24; Job 40:18"> Is my strength the strength of stones? or is my flesh of brass?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 26:2; 2 Corinthians 1:12; Job 19:28"> Or have I not trusted in him? but help is far from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 17:17; 1 Corinthians 12:26; Galatians 6:2"> Mercy has rejected me; and the visitation of the Lord has disregarded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; Jeremias 15:18; Psalms 41:9"> My nearest relations have not regarded me; they have passed me by like a failing brook, or like a wave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They who used to reverence me, now have come against me like snow or congealed ice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 17:1; Job 24:19"> When it has melted at the approach of heat, it is not known what it was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Thus I also have been deserted of all; and I am ruined, and become an outcast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 21:14; Genesis 25:15; Jeremias 25:23"> Behold the ways of the Thaemanites, you⌃ that mark the paths of the Sabaeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 14:3–4; Romans 5:5; Romans 9:33"> They too that trust in cities and riches shall come to shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 38:11; 2 Timothy 4:16; Jeremias 17:5–6"> But you⌃ also have come to me without pity; so that beholding my wound you⌃ are afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 20:33; Job 42:11; Kings I 12:3"> What? have I made any demand of you? or do I ask for strength from you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 49:15; Psalms 49:7–8; Psalms 107:2"> to deliver me from enemies, or to rescue me from the hand of the mighty ones?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 3:2; Proverbs 9:9; Psalms 39:1–2"> Teach you⌃ me, and I will be silent: if in anything I have erred, tell me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 32:3; Job 13:5; Proverbs 25:11"> But as it seems, the words of a true man are vain, because I do not ask strength of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 8:2; Ephesians 4:14; Job 42:3"> Neither will your reproof cause me to cease my words, for neither will I endure the sound of your speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Job 24:9; Job 24:3; 2 Peter 2:3"> Even because you⌃ attack the fatherless, and insult your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 13:4; Job 33:3; Job 27:4"> But now, having looked upon your countenances, I will not lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 17:10; Job 34:5; Job 27:4–6"> Sit down now, and let there not be unrighteousness; and unite again with the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 12:11; Job 6:6; Job 34:3"> For there is no injustice in my tongue; and does not my throat meditate understanding?</span>
