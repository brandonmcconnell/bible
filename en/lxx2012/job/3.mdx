<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 20:14–15; Psalms 106:33; Psalms 39:2–3"> After this Job opened his mouth, and cursed his day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 18:14"> saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 20:14–18; Jeremias 15:10; Job 10:18–19"> Let the day perish in which I was born, and that night in which they said, Behold a boy!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 27:20; Exodus 10:22–23; Joel 2:2"> Let that night be darkness, and let not the Lord regard it from above, neither let light come upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 10:21–22; Psalms 23:4; Job 28:3"> But let darkness and the shadow of death seize it; let blackness come upon it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> let that day and night be cursed, let darkness carry them away; let it not come into the days of the year, neither let it be numbered with the days of the months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 24:8; Jeremias 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23"> But let that night be pain, and let not mirth come upon it, nor joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 41:1; Job 41:10; Mark 5:38"> But let him that curses that day curse it, even he that is ready to attack the great whale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 41:18; Jeremias 13:16; Job 30:26"> Let the stars of that night be darkened; let it remain dark, and not come into light; and let it not see the morning star arise:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 20:17; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Genesis 20:18"> because it shut not up the gates of my mother's womb, for so it would have removed sorrow from my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 10:18–19; Esias 46:3; Psalms 71:6"> For why died I not in the belly? and why did I not come forth from the womb and die immediately?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 66:12; Genesis 30:3; Genesis 50:23"> and why did the knees support me? and why did I suck the breasts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Job 21:13; Job 14:10–12"> Now I should have lain down and been quiet, I should have slept and been at rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 15:28; Psalms 89:48; Psalms 49:14"> with kings and councillors of the earth, who gloried in their swords;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 10:27; Job 22:25; Sophonias 1:18"> or with rulers, whose gold was abundant, who filled their houses with silver:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 58:8; Ecclesiastes 6:3; 1 Corinthians 15:8"> or I should have been as an untimely birth proceeding from his mother's womb, or as infants who never saw light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 14:13; Job 17:16; Hebrews 4:11"> There the ungodly have burnt out the fury of rage; there the wearied in body rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 39:7; Esias 14:3–4; Exodus 5:6–8"> And the men of old time have together ceased to hear the exactor's voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:8; Job 30:23; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> The small and great are there, and the servant that feared his lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 20:18; Job 7:15–16; Kings I 1:10"> For why is light given to those who are in bitterness, and life to those souls which are in griefs?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Proverbs 2:4; Kings III 19:4"> who desire death, and obtain it not, digging for it as for treasures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and would be very joyful if they should gain it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 19:8; Lamentations 3:7; Esias 40:27"> Death is rest to such a man, for God has hedged him in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 102:9; Psalms 42:3–4; Psalms 80:5"> For my groaning comes before my food, and I weep being beset with terror.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 1:5; Job 30:15; Job 31:23"> For the terror of which I meditated has come upon me, and that which I had feared has befallen me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 7:14; Job 27:9; Psalms 143:11"> I was not at peace, nor quiet, nor had I rest; yet wrath came upon me.</span>
