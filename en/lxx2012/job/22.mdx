<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Eliphaz the Thaemanite answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:10; Galatians 6:7–8; Job 35:6–8"> Is it not the Lord that teaches understanding and knowledge?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; 2 Corinthians 7:1; Psalms 147:10–11"> For what matters it to the Lord, if you were blameless in your works? or is it profitable that you should perfect your way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:3; Psalms 143:2; Revelation 3:19"> Will you maintain and plead your own cause? and will he enter into judgment with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 40:12; Job 15:5–6; Job 15:31–34"> Is not your wickedness abundant, and your sins innumerable?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:26; Jezekiel 18:12; Jezekiel 18:16"> And you have taken security of your brethren for nothing, and have taken away the clothing of the naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 31:17; Matthew 25:42; Esias 58:7"> Neither have you given water to the thirsty to drink, but have taken away the morsel of the hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 9:15; Job 29:7–17; Job 13:8"> And you have accepted the persons of some; and you have established those that were already settled on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:21; Job 24:3; Jezekiel 22:7"> But you have sent widows away empty, and has afflicted orphans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 18:8–10; Job 13:21; Job 6:4"> Therefore snares have compassed you, and disastrous war has troubled you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 69:1–2; Lamentations 3:54; Jonas 2:3"> The light has proved darkness to you, and water has covered you on your lying down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 11:8; Esias 66:1; Psalms 8:3–4"> Does not he that dwells in the high places observe? and has he not brought down the proud?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 8:12; Psalms 10:11; Jezekiel 9:9"> And you have said, What does the Mighty One know? does he judge in the dark?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 97:2; Psalms 139:11–12; Psalms 33:14"> A cloud is his hiding-place, and he shall not be seen; and he passes through the circle of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:26–27; Genesis 6:5; Genesis 6:11–13"> Will you not mark the old way, which righteous men have trodden?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 15:32; Genesis 7:17–24; Psalms 55:23"> who were seized before their time: their foundations are as an overflowing stream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Job 21:14–15; Romans 1:28; Psalms 4:6"> Who say, What will the Lord do to us? or what will the Almighty bring upon us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 21:16; Job 12:6; Acts 14:17"> Yet he filled their houses with good things: but the counsel for the wicked is far from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 58:10; Psalms 107:42; Psalms 52:6"> The righteous have seen it, and laughed, and the blameless one has derided them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6–7; Job 1:16; Job 20:26"> Verily their substance has been utterly destroyed, and the fire shall devour what is left of their property.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:9; Esias 27:5; Philippians 4:7"> Be firm, I pray you, if you can endure; then your fruit shall prosper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:11; Job 23:12; Deuteronomy 4:1–2"> And receive a declaration from his mouth, and lay up his words in your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 11:13–14; Zacharias 1:3; Job 8:5–6"> And if you shall turn and humble yourself before the Lord, you have thus removed unrighteousness far from your habitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esias 13:12; Kings III 9:28; Genesis 10:29"> You shall lay up for yourself treasure in a heap on the rock; and Sophir shall be as the rock of the torrent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 33:6; Psalms 16:5–6; Genesis 15:1"> So the Almighty shall be your helper from enemies, and he shall bring you forth pure as silver that has been tried by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 37:4; Esias 58:14; Job 27:10"> Then shall you have boldness before the Lord, looking up cheerfully to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esias 58:9; Psalms 116:1; 1 John 5:14–15"> And he shall hear you when you pray to him, and he shall grant you power to pay your vows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Psalms 90:17; Psalms 20:4"> And he shall establish to you again a habitation of righteousness and there shall be light upon your paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="James 4:6; Job 5:19–27; Luke 1:52"> Because you have humbled yourself; and you shall say, Man has behaved proudly, but he shall save him that is of lowly eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Job 42:7–8; James 5:15–16; Genesis 18:26–32"> He shall deliver the innocent, and do you save yourself by your pure hands.</span>
