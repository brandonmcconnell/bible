<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 2:11; Job 8:1; Job 25:1"> Then Baldad the Sauchite answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 13:5–6; Proverbs 18:13; Job 3:17"> How long will you continue? forbear, that we also may speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 73:22; Job 17:10; Job 17:4"> For therefore have we been silent before you like brutes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 16:9; Job 14:18; Job 13:14"> Anger has possessed you: for what if you should die; would the earth under heaven be desolate? or shall the mountains be overthrown from their foundations?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 13:9; Proverbs 24:20; Proverbs 20:20"> But the light of the ungodly shall be quenched, and their flame shall not go up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Job 21:17; Psalms 18:28"> His light shall be darkness in his habitation, and his lamp shall be put out with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 4:12; Psalms 33:10; Psalms 18:36"> Let the meanest of men spoil his goods, and let his counsel deceive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 9:15; Job 22:10; Psalms 35:8"> His foot also has been caught in a snare, and let it be entangled in a net.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 8:14–15; Job 1:15; Job 1:17"> And let snares come upon him: he shall strengthen those that thirst for his destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:13; Romans 11:9; Psalms 11:6"> His snare is hid in the earth, and that which shall take him is by the path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 15:21; Jeremias 6:25; Leviticus 26:36"> Let pains destroy him round about, and let many enemies come about him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings I 2:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Psalms 34:10"> vex him with distressing hunger: and a signal destruction has been prepared for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonas 2:6; Zacharias 14:12; Revelation 6:8"> Let the soles of his feet be devoured: and death shall consume his beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 10:28; Psalms 55:4; Job 11:20"> And let health be utterly banished from his tabernacle, and let distress seize upon him with a charge from the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 11:6; Deuteronomy 29:23; Esias 34:9–10"> It shall dwell in his tabernacle in his night: his excellency shall be sown with brimstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Osee 9:16; Esias 5:24; Job 15:30"> His roots shall be dried up from beneath, and his crop shall fall away from above.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 34:16; Proverbs 10:7; Psalms 109:13"> Let his memorial perish out of the earth, and his name shall be publicly cast out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Job 20:8; Job 10:22; Job 3:20"> Let one drive him from light into darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 22:30; Esias 14:21–22; Job 20:26–28"> He shall not be known among his people, nor his house preserved on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 37:13; Jeremias 50:27; Jezekiel 21:25"> But strangers shall dwell in his possessions: the last groaned for him, and wonder seized the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Jeremias 9:3; Jeremias 10:25"> These are the houses of the unrighteous, and this is the place of them that know not the Lord.</span>
