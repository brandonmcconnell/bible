<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Sophonias 2:8–9; Jezekiel 25:8–9; Amos 1:3"> Thus says the Lord; For three sins of Moab, and for four, I will not turn away from it; because they burnt the bones of the king of Idumea to lime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 48:24; Jeremias 48:41; Amos 1:14"> But I will send forth a fire on Moab, and it shall devour the foundations of its cities: and Moab shall perish in weakness, with a shout, and with the sound of a trumpet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 24:17; Jeremias 48:7; Jeremias 48:25"> And I will destroy the judge out of her, and kill all her princes with him, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:24; Jezekiel 20:16; Jezekiel 20:13"> Thus says the Lord; For three sins of the children of Judah, and for four, I will not turn away from him; because they have rejected the law of the Lord, and have not kept his ordinances, and their vain idols which they made, which their fathers followed, caused them to err.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Osee 8:14; Jeremias 17:27; Jeremias 21:10"> And I will send a fire on Juda, and it shall devour the foundations of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:3; Michaeas 3:2–3; Amos 5:11–12"> Thus says the Lord; for three sins of Israel, and for four, I will not turn away from him; because they sold the righteous for silver, and the poor for sandals,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 5:12; Esias 10:2; Jezekiel 22:11"> wherewith to tread on the dust of the earth, and they have struck upon the heads of the poor, and have perverted the way of the lowly: and a son and his father have gone into the same maid, that they might profane the name of their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:12–17; Exodus 22:26–27; Amos 6:6"> And binding their clothes with cords they have made them curtains near the altar, and they have drunk wine gained by extortion in the house of their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 18:16; Malachias 4:1; Deuteronomy 3:11"> Nevertheless I cut off the Amorite from before them, whose height was as the height of a cedar, and he was strong as an oak; and I dried up his fruit from above, and his roots from beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:7; Exodus 12:51; Amos 3:1"> And I brought you up out of the land of Egypt, and led you about in the desert forty years, that you⌃ should inherit the land of the Amorites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 7:25; Numbers 6:2–3; Kings III 20:41"> And I took of your sons for prophets, and of your young men for consecration. Are not these things so, you⌃ sons of Israel? says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 7:13; Esias 30:10; Jeremias 11:21"> But you⌃ gave the consecrated ones wine to drink; and you⌃ commanded the prophets, saying, Prophesy not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 1:14; Esias 43:24; Jezekiel 16:43"> Therefore, behold, I roll under you, as a wagon full of straw is rolled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 9:23; Psalms 33:16; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> And flight shall perish from the runner, and the strong shall not hold fast his strength, and the warrior shall not save his life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 33:16–17; Jezekiel 39:3"> and the archer shall not withstand, and he that is swift of foot shall in no wise escape; and the horseman shall not save his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 14:52; Jeremias 48:41; Kings IV 7:8–20"> And the strong shall find no confidence in power: the naked shall flee away in that day, says the Lord.</span>
