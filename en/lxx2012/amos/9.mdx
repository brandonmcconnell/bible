<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 51:53; Psalms 139:7–10; Obdias 1:4"> Though they hid themselves in hell, thence shall my hand drag them forth; and though they go up to heaven, thence will I bring them down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 16:16; Jeremias 23:23–24; Esias 27:1"> If they hide themselves in the top of Carmel, thence will I search them out and take them; and if they should go down from my presence into the depths of the sea, there will I command the serpent, and he shall bite them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Jeremias 21:10; Jeremias 44:11"> And if they should go into captivity before the face of their enemies, there will I command the sword, and it shall kill them: and I will set my eyes against them for evil, and not for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 8:8; Psalms 46:6; Esias 64:1"> And the Lord, the Lord God Almighty, is he that takes hold of the land, and causes it to shake, and all that inhabit it shall mourn; and its destruction shall go up as a river, and shall descend as the river of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:8; Amos 4:13; Psalms 104:3"> It is he that builds his ascent up to the sky, and establishes his promise on the earth; who calls the water of the sea, and pours it out on the face of the earth; the Lord Almighty is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 47:4; Deuteronomy 2:23; Kings IV 16:9"> Are not you⌃ to me as the sons of the Ethiopians, O children of Israel? says the Lord. Did I not bring Israel up out of the land of Egypt, and the Philistines from Cappadocia, and the Syrians out of the deep?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 30:11; Amos 9:4; Jeremias 44:27"> Behold, the eyes of the Lord God are upon the kingdom of sinners, and I will cut it off from the face of the earth; only I will not utterly cut off the house of Jacob, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 22:31; Deuteronomy 28:64; Esias 30:28"> For I will give commandment, and sift the house of Israel among all the Gentiles, as corn is sifted in a sieve, and yet a fragment shall not in any wise fall upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 6:3; Esias 33:14; Amos 6:1"> All the sinners of my people shall die by the sword, who say, Calamities shall certainly not draw near, nor come upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 15:15–17; Jeremias 33:14–16; Esias 16:5"> In that day I will raise up the tabernacle of David that is fallen, and will rebuild the ruins of it, and will set up the parts thereof that have been broken down, and will build it up as in the ancient days:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 43:7; Esias 65:1; Acts 15:17"> that the remnant of men, and all the Gentiles upon whom my name is called, may earnestly seek me, says the Lord who does all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joel 3:18; John 4:35; Leviticus 26:5"> Behold, the days come, says the Lord, when the harvest shall overtake the vintage, and the grapes shall ripen at seed time; and the mountains shall drop sweet wine, and all the hills shall be planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 61:4; Jeremias 30:3; Jeremias 30:18"> And I will turn the captivity of my people Israel, and they shall rebuild the ruined cities, and shall inhabit them; and they shall plant vineyards, and shall drink the wine from them; and they shall form gardens, and eat the fruit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 24:6; Jezekiel 37:25; Esias 60:21"> And I will plant them on their land, and they shall no more be plucked up from the land which I have given them, says the Lord God Almighty.</span>
