<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 1:17; Kings III 22:51; Kings IV 8:16"> And Joram the son of Achaab<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. reigned.">a</a></sup>began to reign in Israel in the eighteenth year of Josaphat king of Juda, and he reigned twelve years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 10:18; Kings IV 10:26–28; Kings III 21:25"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, only not as his father, nor as his mother: and he removed the pillars of Baal which his father had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 14:16; Kings III 15:34; Kings IV 15:18"> Only he adhered to the sin of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, who made Israel to sin; he departed not from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 8:2; Genesis 26:13–14; Job 1:3"> And Mosa king of Moab was a sheep-master, and he rendered to the king of Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. in the rising up.">b</a></sup>in the beginning of the year, one hundred thousand lambs, and one hundred thousand rams, with the wool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 1:1; Kings IV 8:20; Chronicles II 21:8–10"> And it came to pass, after the death of Achaab, that the king of Moab<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. despised.">c</a></sup>rebelled against the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings II 24:1–25; Kings I 11:8; Kings I 15:4"> And king Joram went forth in that day out of Samaria, and numbered Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 22:4; Chronicles II 22:10–12; Chronicles II 18:3"> And he went and sent to Josaphat king of Juda, saying, The king of Moab has rebelled against me: will you go with me against Moab to war? And he said, I will go up: you are as I, I am as you; as my people, so is your people, as my horses, so are your horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Malachias 1:2–3; Numbers 21:4"> And he said, What way shall I go up? and he said, The way of the wilderness of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 22:47; Numbers 20:2; Exodus 17:1"> And the king of Israel went, and the king of Juda, and the king of Edom: and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. compassed a journey of seven days.">d</a></sup>they fetched a compass of seven days' journey; and there was no water for the army, and for the cattle<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. at their feet.">e</a></sup>that went with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 78:34–36; Esias 51:20; Esias 8:21"> And the king of Israel said, Alas! that the Lord should have called the three kings on their way, to give them into the hand of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 22:7; Kings III 19:21; Kings IV 3:3"> And Josaphat said, Is there not here a prophet of the Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. and we will.">f</a></sup>that we may enquire of the Lord by him? And one of the servants of the king of Israel answered and said, There is here Elisaie son of Saphat, who poured water on the hands of Eliu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 5:15; Kings IV 2:21; Esias 60:14"> And Josaphat said, He has the word of the Lord. And the king of Israel, and Josaphat king of Juda, and the king of Edom, went down to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 22:6; Kings III 18:19; Kings III 22:10–11"> And Elisaie said to the king of Israel, What have I to do with you? go to the prophets of your father, and the prophets of your mother. And the king of Israel said to him,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. is it that.">g</a></sup>Has the Lord called the three kings to deliver them into the hands of Moab?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 5:16; Kings III 17:1; Chronicles II 19:3–4"> And Elisaie said, As the Lord of hosts before whom I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. stood.">h</a></sup>stand lives, unless I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. regard.">i</a></sup>regarded the presence of Josaphat the king of Juda, I would not have looked on you, nor seen you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 16:23; Ephesians 5:18–19; Jezekiel 1:3"> And now fetch me a harper. And it came to pass, as the harper harped, that the hand of the Lord came upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 2:16–18; Kings IV 4:3"> And he said, Thus says the Lord, Make this<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Lit. brook.">j</a></sup>valley full of trenches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 107:35; Esias 41:17–18; Esias 48:21"> For thus says the Lord, You⌃ shall not see wind, neither shall you⌃ see rain, yet this valley shall be filled with water, and you⌃, and your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Lit. posessions.">k</a></sup>flocks, and your cattle shall drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 32:27; Luke 1:37; Jeremias 32:17"> And this is a light thing in the eyes of the Lord: I will also deliver Moab<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. in.">l</a></sup>into your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 13:17; Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Judges 6:16"> And you⌃ shall strike every strong city, and you⌃ shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. throw down.">m</a></sup>cut down every good tree, and you⌃ shall stop all wells of water, and spoil every good piece of land with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 29:39–40; Daniel 9:21; Kings III 18:36"> And it came to pass in the morning, when the sacrifice was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. going up.">n</a></sup>offered, that, behold! waters came from the way of Edom, and the land was filled with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; Kings III 20:11"> And all Moab heard that the three kings were come up to fight against them; and they cried out<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. from.">o</a></sup>on every side, even all that were girded with a girdle,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="Alex. καὶ ἐπάνα, 'and above.'">p</a></sup>and they said, Ho! and stood upon the border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And they rose early in the morning, and the sun rose upon the waters, and Moab saw the waters on the opposite side red as blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Exodus 15:9; Esias 10:14"> And they said, This is the blood of the sword; and the kings have fought, and each man has struck his neighbor; now then to the spoils, Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3–4; Joshua 8:20–22; Judges 20:40–46"> And they entered into the camp of Israel; and Israel arose and struck Moab, and they fled from before them; and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gr. went in, going in and striking.">q</a></sup>went on and struck Moab as they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 16:7; Jeremias 48:36; Jeremias 48:31"> And they razed the cities, and cast every man his stone on every good piece of land and filled it; and they stopped every well, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Gr. threw down.">r</a></sup>cut down every good tree, until they left only the stones of the wall cast down; and the slingers compassed the land, and struck it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 3:9; Amos 2:1"> And the king of Moab saw that the battle prevailed against him; and he took with him seven hundred men that drew sword, to cut through to the king of Edom: and they could not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Michaeas 6:7; Deuteronomy 12:31; Judges 11:31"> And he took his oldest son whom he had designed to reign in his stead, and offered him up for a whole burnt offering on the walls. And there was a great<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Gr. repentance.">s</a></sup>indignation against Israel; and they departed from him, and returned to their land.</span>
