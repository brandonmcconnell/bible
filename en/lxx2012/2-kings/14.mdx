<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 13:10; Chronicles II 25:1–4; Kings IV 14:15"> In the second year of Joas the son of Joachaz king of Israel, did Amessias also the son of Joas the king of Juda begin to reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. a son of 25 years in his reigning.">a</a></sup>Twenty and five years old was he when he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. reigned.">b</a></sup>began to reign, and he reigned twenty and nine years in Jerusalem: and his mother's name was Joadim of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:2–3; Zacharias 1:4–6; Kings III 11:4"> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, but not as David his father: he did according to all things that his father Joas did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 12:3; Kings IV 16:4; Kings IV 15:35"> Only he removed not the high places: as yet the people sacrificed and burnt incense on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:3–4; Kings IV 12:20–21; Numbers 35:33"> And it came to pass when the kingdom was established in his hand, that he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. struck.">c</a></sup>killed his servants that had<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. struck.">d</a></sup>slain the king his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:16; Jezekiel 18:20; Jezekiel 18:4"> But he killed not the sons of those that had slain him; according as it is written in the book of the laws of Moses, as the Lord gave commandment, saying, The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, and the children shall not be put to death for the fathers; but every one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or. be put to death, etc.">e</a></sup>shall die for his own sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 8:13; Chronicles I 18:12; Joshua 15:38"> He struck of Edom ten thousand in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="The Gr. is from בוי מלח Keri.">f</a></sup>valley of salt, and took<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Heb. Selah">g</a></sup>the Rock in the war, and called its name Jethoel until this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:17–24; Kings II 2:14–17; Kings IV 14:11"> Then Amessias sent messengers to Joas son of Joachaz son of Ju king of Israel, saying, Come, let us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. appear to faces.">h</a></sup>look one another in the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; Kings III 4:33; Chronicles II 33:11"> And Joas the king of Israel sent to Amessias king of Juda, saying, The thistle that was in Libanus sent to the cedar that was in Libanus, saying, Give my daughter to your son to wife: and the wild beasts of the field that were in Libanus passed by and trod down the thistle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Chronicles II 26:16; Deuteronomy 8:14; Chronicles II 32:25"> You have struck and wounded Edom, and your heart has lifted you up:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. glorify yourself sitting in your house.">i</a></sup>stay at home and glorify yourself; for therefore are you quarrelsome to your hurt? So both you will fall and Juda with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 19:38; Chronicles II 25:16; Joshua 15:10"> Nevertheless Amessias listened not: so Joas king of Israel went up, and he and Amessias king of Juda looked one another in the face in Baethsamys of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings II 18:17; Kings I 4:10; Kings III 22:36"> And Juda<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. fell.">j</a></sup>was overthrown before Israel, and every man fled to his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:16; Nehemiah 12:39; Zacharias 14:10"> And Joas king of Israel took Amessias the son of Joas the son of Ochozias, in Baethsamys; and he came to Jerusalem, and broke down the wall of Jerusalem, beginning at the gate of Ephraim as far as the gate of the corner, four hundred cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 14:26; Kings III 7:51; Kings III 15:18"> And he took the gold, and the silver, and all the vessels that were found in the house of the Lord, and in the treasures of the king's house, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. and Heb. lit. sons of exchanges.">k</a></sup>hostages, and returned to Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 13:12–13; Kings III 14:19–20; Kings IV 10:34–35"> And the rest of the acts of Joas, even all that he did in his might, how he warred with Amessias king of Juda, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 13:9; Kings IV 13:13; Kings III 1:21"> And Joas slept with his fathers, and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel; and Jeroboam his son reigned in his stead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:25–28; Kings IV 13:10; Kings IV 14:23"> And Amessias the son of Joas king of Juda lived after the death of Joas son of Joachaz king of Israel fifteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 11:41; Kings III 14:29; Kings IV 13:8"> And the rest of the acts of Amessias, and all that he did, are not these written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Juda?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 10:31; Kings IV 15:25; Kings IV 12:20–21"> And they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. conspired with a conspiracy.">l</a></sup>formed a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem, and he fled to Lachis: and they sent after him to Lachis, and killed him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 9:28; Kings IV 12:21; Chronicles II 33:20"> And they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. lifted him.">m</a></sup>brought him upon horses; and he was buried in Jerusalem with his fathers in the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 21:24; Matthew 1:8–9; Kings IV 15:13"> And all the people of Juda took Azarias, and he was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. a son of 16 years.">n</a></sup>sixteen years old, and made him king in the room of his father Amessias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings IV 16:6; Kings III 9:26; Deuteronomy 2:8"> He built Aeloth, and restored it to Juda, after the king slept with his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Osee 1:1; Amos 7:9–11; Amos 1:1"> In the fifteenth year of Amessias son of Joas king of Juda<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-o" id="fnref-o" data-fn="Gr. reigned.">o</a></sup>began Jeroboam son of Joas to reign over Israel in Samaria forty and one years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings IV 13:6; Kings IV 13:11; Genesis 38:7"> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord: he departed not from all the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nabat, who led Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:17; Matthew 12:39–40; Joshua 19:13"> He recovered the coast of Israel from the entering in of Aemath to the sea of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-p" id="fnref-p" data-fn="i.e. the plain.">p</a></sup>Araba, according to the word of the Lord God of Israel, which he spoke by<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-q" id="fnref-q" data-fn="Gr. the hand of his servant.">q</a></sup>his servant Jonas the son of Amathi, the prophet of Gethchopher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; Exodus 3:7; Kings IV 13:4"> For the Lord saw that the affliction of Israel was very bitter, and that they were few in number, straitened and in lack, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-r" id="fnref-r" data-fn="Or, left alone.">r</a></sup>destitute, and Israel had no helper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings IV 13:23; Kings IV 13:5; Romans 11:2–12"> And the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-s" id="fnref-s" data-fn="Or, said not that he would.">s</a></sup>said that he would not blot out the seed of Israel from under heaven; so he delivered them by the hand of Jeroboam the son of Joas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings III 11:24; Chronicles I 18:5–6; Kings II 8:6"> And the rest of the acts of Jeroboam and all that he did, and his mighty deeds, which he achieved in war, and how he recovered Damascus and Aemath to Juda in Israel, are not these things written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings IV 15:8"> And Jeroboam slept with his fathers, even with the kings of Israel; and Zacharias his son reigned in his stead.</span>
