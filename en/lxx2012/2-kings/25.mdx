<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 52:4–11; Chronicles I 6:15; Jeremias 34:1–6"> And it came to pass in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, that Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon came, and all his host, against Jerusalem; and he encamped against it, and built a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, circumvallation.">a</a></sup>mound against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And the city was besieged until the eleventh year of king Sedekias on the ninth day of the month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 52:6; Jezekiel 5:12; Jeremias 38:2"> And the famine prevailed in the city, and there was no bread for the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:21; Kings IV 25:5; Jeremias 39:2–7"> And the city was broken up, and all the men of war went forth by night, by the way of the gate between the walls, this is the gate of the king's garden: and the Chaldeans were set against the city round about: and the king went by the way of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. from the Heb.">b</a></sup>the plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 30:16; Jeremias 39:5; Amos 2:14–16"> And the force of the Chaldeans pursued the king, and overtook him in the plains of Jericho: and all his army was dispersed from about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings IV 23:33; Jeremias 34:21–22; Jeremias 21:7"> And they took the king, and brought him to the king of Babylon to Reblatha; and he gave judgment upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 32:4–5; Judges 16:21; Jeremias 39:6–7"> And he killed the sons of Sedekias before his eyes, and put out the eyes of Sedekias, and bound him in fetters, and brought him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 52:12–16; Jeremias 40:1–4; Lamentations 4:12"> And in the fifth month, on the seventh day of the month (this is the nineteenth year of Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon), came Nabuzardan,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. chief cook.">c</a></sup>captain of the guard, who stood before the king of Babylon, to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 2:5; Psalms 74:3–7; Kings III 9:8"> And he burnt the house of the Lord, and the king's house, and all the houses of Jerusalem, even every house did the captain of the guard burn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Jeremias 39:8; Jeremias 52:14–23"> And the force of the Chaldeans pulled down the wall of Jerusalem round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:2; Jeremias 15:1–2; Jeremias 39:9"> And Nabuzardan the captain of the guard removed the rest of the people that were left in the city, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. deserters.">d</a></sup>men who had deserted to the king of Babylon, and the rest of the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 40:7; Kings IV 24:14; Jezekiel 33:24"> But the captain of the guard left of the poor of the land to be vine-dressers and husbandmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:18; Lamentations 1:10; Kings IV 20:17"> And the Chaldeans broke to pieces the brazen pillars that were in the house of the Lord, and the bases, and the brazen sea that was in the house of the Lord, and carried their brass to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; Kings III 7:47–50; Exodus 38:3"> And the caldrons, and the shovels, and the bowls, and the censers, and all the brazen vessels with which they minister, he took.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 5:2–3; Numbers 7:13–14; Kings III 7:48–51"> And the captain of the guard took the fire-pans, and the gold and silver bowls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 7:47"> Two pillars, and one sea, and the bases which Solomon made for the house of the Lord: there was no weight of the brass of all the vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 7:15–22; Jeremias 52:21–23"> The height of one pillar was eighteen cubits, and the chapiter upon it was of brass: and the height of the chapiter was three cubits: the border, and the pomegranates on the chapiter round about were all of brass: and so it was with the second pillar with its border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 29:25; Ezra 7:1; Jeremias 21:1"> And the captain of the guard took Saraias the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. first priest.">e</a></sup>high-priest, and Sophonias<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. the son of the second rank.">f</a></sup>the second in order, and the three doorkeepers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esther 1:14"> And they took out of the city one eunuch who was commander of the men of war, and five men that saw the face of the king, that were found in the city, and the secretary of the commander-in-chief, who took account of the people of the land, and sixty men of the people of the land that were found in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 4:16; Jeremias 52:26–27; Kings IV 23:33"> And Nabuzardan the captain of the guard took them, and brought them to the king of Babylon to Reblatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Kings IV 23:27; Deuteronomy 4:26"> And the king of Babylon struck them and killed them at Reblatha in the land of Aemath. So Juda was carried away from his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 39:14; Kings IV 22:12; Jeremias 41:2"> And as for the people that were left in the land of Juda, whom Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon left, even over them he set Godolias son of Achicam son of Saphan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremias 40:7–9; Jeremias 40:11–12; Joshua 18:26"> And all the captains of the host, they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had thus appointed Godolias, and they came to Godolias to Massephath, both Ismael the son of Nathanias, and Jona son of Careth, and Saraias, son of Thanamath the Netophathite, and Jezonias son of a Machathite, they and their men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 40:9–10; Jeremias 43:6; Kings II 19:23"> And Godolias swore to them and their men, and said to them, Fear not the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. passage.">g</a></sup>incursion of the Chaldeans; dwell in the land, and serve the king of Babylon, and it shall be well with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zacharias 7:5; Zacharias 8:19; Jeremias 40:15"> And it came to pass in the seventh month that Ismael son of Nathanias son of Helisama, of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. seed of the kings.">h</a></sup>seed royal, came, and ten men with him, and he struck Godolias, that he died, him and the Jews and the Chaldeans that were with him in Massepha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 43:4–7; Jeremias 41:16–18; Jeremias 42:14–22"> And all the people, great and small rose up, they and the captains of the forces, and went into Egypt; because they were afraid of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremias 52:31–34; Genesis 40:20; Genesis 40:13"> And it came to pass in the thirty-seventh year of the carrying away of Joachim king of Juda, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, that Evialmarodec king of Babylon in the first year of his reign lifted up the head of Joachim king of Juda, and brought him out of his prison-house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:37; Jeremias 27:6–11; Daniel 5:18–19"> And he spoke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. good things with him.">i</a></sup>kindly to him, and set his throne above the thrones of the kings that were with him in Babylon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings II 9:7; Esias 61:3; Genesis 41:42"> And changed his prison garments: and he ate bread continually before him all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 11:3; Matthew 6:11; Nehemiah 11:23"> And his portion, a continual portion, was given him out of the house of the king,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. a rate of a day in his day.">j</a></sup>a daily rate for every day all the days of his life.</span>
