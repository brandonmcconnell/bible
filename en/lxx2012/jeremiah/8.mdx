<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:5; Kings IV 23:16; Jeremias 7:32–34"> At that time, says the Lord, they shall bring out the bones of the kings of Juda, and the bones of his princes, and the bones of the priests, and the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, out of their graves;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings IV 23:5; Jeremias 9:22; Deuteronomy 4:19"> and they shall spread them out to the sun, and the moon, and to all the stars, and to all the host of heaven, which they have loved, and which they have served, and after which they have walked, and to which they have held, and which they have worshipped; they shall not be mourned for, neither shall they be buried; but they shall be for an example on the face of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 9:6; Jeremias 29:14; Jeremias 23:3"> because they chose death rather than life, even to all the remnant that are left of that family, in every place whither I shall drive them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Michaeas 7:8; Osee 6:1"> For thus says the Lord, Shall not he that falls arise? or he that turns away, shall he not turn back again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 9:6; Jeremias 5:3; Zacharias 7:11"> Therefore has this my people turned away with a shameless revolting, and strengthened themselves in their willfulness, and refused to return?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 14:2; Jezekiel 18:28; Luke 15:17–19"> Listen, I pray you, and hear: will they not speak thus, There is no man that repents of his wickedness, saying, What have I done? the runner has failed from his course, as a tired horse in his neighing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 1:3; Jeremias 5:4–5; Proverbs 6:6–8"> Yes, the stork in the heaven knows her time, also the turtle-dove and wild swallow; the sparrows observe the times of their coming in; but this my people knows not the judgments of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 9:41; Job 5:12–13; Esias 10:1–2"> How will you⌃ say, We are wise, and the law of the Lord is with us? In vain have the scribes used a false pen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 6:15; Psalms 119:98–100; Job 5:12"> The wise men are ashamed, and alarmed, and taken; because they have rejected the word of the Lord; what wisdom is there in them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 6:12–13; Jezekiel 33:31; Esias 56:10–12"> Therefore will I give their wives to others, and their fields to new inheritors; and they shall gather their fruits, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 21:19; Joel 1:7; Jeremias 17:8"> There are no grapes on the vines, and there are no figs on the fig-trees, and the leaves have fallen off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 23:15; Jeremias 9:15; Lamentations 3:19"> Why do we sit still? assemble yourselves, and let us enter into the strong cities, and let us be cast out there: for God has cast us out, and made us drink water of gall, because we have sinned before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 14:19; Jeremias 4:10; Jeremias 8:11"> We assembled for peace, but there was no prosperity; for a time of healing, but behold anxiety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:29; Judges 5:22; Jeremias 4:15–16"> We shall hear the neighing of his swift horses out of Dan: the whole land quaked at the sound of the neighing of his horses; and he shall come, and devour the land and the fullness of it; the city, and them that dwell in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:11; Psalms 58:4–5; Deuteronomy 32:24"> For, behold, I send forth against you deadly serpents, which can’t be charmed, and they shall bite you</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 1:16–17; Jeremias 6:24; Esias 22:4"> mortally with the pain of your distressed heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 39:3; Esias 13:5; Psalms 135:21"> Behold, there is a sound of the cry of the daughter of my people from a land afar off: Is not the Lord in Sion? is there not a king there? because they have provoked me with their graven images, and with strange vanities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 10:5; Matthew 25:1–12; Luke 13:25"> The summer is gone, the harvest is past, and we are not saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 14:17; Joel 2:6; Naum 2:10"> For the breach of the daughter of my people I have been saddened: in my perplexity pangs have seized upon me as of a woman in travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 46:11; Genesis 37:25; Jeremias 51:8"> And is there no balm in Galaad, or is there no physician there? why has not the healing of the daughter of my people taken place?</span>
