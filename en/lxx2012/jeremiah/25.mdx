<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 36:1; Kings IV 24:1–2; Daniel 1:1"> THE WORD THAT CAME TO JEREMIAS concerning all the people of Juda in the fourth year of Joakim, son of Josias, king of Juda;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 18:11; Jeremias 19:14–15; Mark 7:14–15"> which he spoke to all the people of Juda, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 11:7; Jeremias 26:5; Jeremias 1:2"> In the thirteenth year of Josias, son of Amos, king of Juda, even until this day for three and twenty years, I have both spoken to you, rising early and speaking,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 25:3; Jeremias 26:5; Jeremias 13:10–11"> and I sent to you my servants the prophets, sending them early; (but you⌃ listened not, and listened not with your ears;) saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 7:7; Genesis 17:8; Esias 55:6–7"> Turn you⌃ every one from his evil way, and from your evil practices, and you⌃ shall dwell in the land which I gave to you and your fathers, of old and for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19; Jeremias 7:6; Jeremias 35:15"> Go you⌃ not after strange gods, to serve them, and to worship them, that you⌃ provoke me not by the works of your hands, to do you hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 21:15; Proverbs 8:36; Deuteronomy 32:21"> But you⌃ listened not to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore thus says the Lord; Since you⌃ believed not my words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 13:3; Jeremias 18:16; Jeremias 1:15"> behold I will send and take a family from the north, and will bring them against this land, and against the inhabitants of it, and against all the nations round about it, and I will make them utterly waste, and make them a desolation, and a hissing, and an everlasting reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23; Jezekiel 26:13"> And I will destroy from among them the voice of joy, and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride, the scent of ointment, and the light of a candle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zacharias 1:12; Chronicles II 36:21–22"> And all the land shall be a desolation; and they shall serve among the Gentiles seventy years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Esias 13:19; Jeremias 29:10"> And when the seventy years are fulfilled, I will take vengeance on that nation, and will make them a perpetual desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 1:10; Jeremias 1:5; Daniel 5:31"> And I will bring upon that land all my words which I have spoken against it, even all things that are written in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremias 6:26; Jeremias 50:27; Psalms 2:9"> THE PROPHECIES OF JEREMIAS AGAINST THE NATIONS OF AeLAM.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 11:20; Amos 2:14; Jeremias 52:8–11"> Thus says the Lord, The bow of Aelam is broken, even the chief of their power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremias 4:8; Jeremias 25:34"> And I will bring upon Aelam the four winds from the four corners of heaven, and I will disperse them toward all these winds; and there shall be no nation to which they shall not come—even the outcasts of Aelam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Esias 27:10–11; Esias 32:14"> And I will put them in fear before their enemies that seek their life; and I will bring evils upon them according to my great anger; and I will send forth my sword after them, until I have utterly destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremias 4:7; Jeremias 5:6; Osee 13:7–8"> And I will set my throne in Aelam, and will send forth thence king and rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But it shall come to pass at the end of days, that I will turn the captivity of Aelam, says the Lord.</span>
