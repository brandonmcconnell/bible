<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 13:11; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremias 27:2"> Thus says the Lord, Go and procure for yourself a linen girdle, and put it about your loins, and let it not be put in water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 2:8; John 15:14; Proverbs 3:5"> So I procured the girdle according to the word of the Lord, and put it about my loins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 13:8"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Michaeas 4:10; Psalms 137:1; Jeremias 51:63–64"> Take the girdle that is upon your loins, and arise, and go to the Euphrates, and hide it there in a hole of the rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> So I went, and hid it by the Euphrates, as the Lord commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And it came to pass after many days, that the Lord said to me, Arise, go to the Euphrates, and take thence the girdle, which I commanded you to hide there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Esias 64:6; Jeremias 13:10"> So I went to the river Euphrates, and dug, and took the girdle out of the place where I had buried it: and, behold, it was rotten, utterly good for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying, Thus says the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremias 13:15–17; Sophonias 3:11"> Thus will I mar the pride of Juda, and the pride of Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 16:12; Jeremias 9:14; Chronicles II 36:15–16"> even this great pride of the men that will not listen to my words, and have gone after strange gods, to serve them, and to worship them: and they shall be as this girdle, which can be used for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremias 33:9; Psalms 81:11"> For as a girdle cleaves about the loins of a man, so have I caused to cleave to myself the house of Israel, and the whole house of Juda; that they might be to me a famous people, and a praise, and a glory: but they did not listen to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:19"> And you shall say to this people, Every bottle shall be filled with wine: and it shall come to pass, if they shall say to you, Shall we not certainly know that every bottle shall be filled with wine? that you shall say to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 63:6; Psalms 75:8; Psalms 60:3"> Thus says the Lord, Behold, I will fill the inhabitants of this land, and their kings the sons of David that sit upon their throne, and the priests, and the prophets, and Juda and all the dwellers in Jerusalem, with strong drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 2:9; Jeremias 19:9–11"> And I will scatter them a man and his brother, and their fathers and their sons together: I will not have compassion, says the Lord, and I will not spare, neither will I pity to save them from destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 28:14–22; Revelation 2:29; Acts 4:19–20"> Hear you⌃, and give ear, and be not proud: for the Lord has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 59:9; Esias 8:22; Esias 5:30"> Give glory to the Lord your God, before he cause darkness, and before your feet stumble on the dark mountains, and you⌃ shall wait for light, and behold the shadow of death, and they shall be brought into darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachias 2:2; Jeremias 9:1; Jeremias 14:17"> But if you⌃ will not listen, your soul shall weep in secret because of pride, and your eyes shall pour down tears, because the Lord's flock is sorely bruised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 22:26; Kings IV 24:12; Kings IV 24:15"> Say you⌃ to the king and the princes, Humble yourselves, and sit down; for your crown of glory is removed from your head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 52:27; Jeremias 39:9; Deuteronomy 28:52"> The cities toward the south were shut, and there was none to open them: Juda is removed into captivity, they have suffered a complete removal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 6:22; Jeremias 1:14; Ambacum 1:6"> Lift up your eyes, O Jerusalem, and behold them that come from the north; where is the flock that was given you, the sheep of your glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 13:8; Jeremias 4:31; Jeremias 5:31"> What will you say when they shall visit you, for you did teach them lessons for rule against yourself; shall not pangs seize you as a woman in travail?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Naum 3:5; Jeremias 16:10–11; Jeremias 5:19"> And if you should say in your heart, Therefore have these things happened to me? Because of the abundance of your iniquity have your skirts been revealed, that your heels might be exposed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremias 2:22; Jeremias 9:5; Matthew 19:24–28"> If the Ethiopian shall change his skin, or the leopardess her spots, then shall you⌃ be able to do good, having learned evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Leviticus 26:33; Jezekiel 5:12"> So I scattered them as sticks carried by the wind into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 20:29; Jeremias 2:32; Psalms 9:17"> Thus is your lot, and the reward of your disobedience to me, says the Lord; as you did forget me, and trust in lies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Osee 2:10; Jezekiel 23:29"> I also will expose your skirts upon your face, and your shame shall be seen;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Osee 8:5; Jezekiel 6:13; Jezekiel 24:13"> your adultery also, and your neighing, and the looseness of your fornication: on the hills and in the fields I have seen your abominations. Woe to you, O Jerusalem, for you have not been purified so as to follow me; how long yet shall it be?</span>
