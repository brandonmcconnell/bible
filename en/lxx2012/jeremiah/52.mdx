<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 24:18; Joshua 10:29; Chronicles II 36:11"> It was the twenty-first year of Sedekias, when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. And his mother's name was Amitaal, the daughter of Jeremias, of Lobena.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 39:1; Zacharias 8:19; Jezekiel 24:1–2"> And it came to pass in the ninth year of his reign, in the ninth month, on the tenth day of the month, that Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon came, and all his host, against Jerusalem, and they made a rampart round it, and built a wall round about it with large stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the city was besieged, until the eleventh year of king Sedekias,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 39:2; Esias 3:1; Kings IV 25:3"> on the ninth day of the month, and then the famine was severe in the city, and there was no bread for the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 39:4–7; Jeremias 51:32; Kings IV 25:4"> And the city was broken up, and all the men of war went out by night by the way of the gate, between the wall and the outworks, which were by the king's garden; and the Chaldeans were by the city round about; and they went by the way leading to the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 34:21; Jeremias 32:4; Jeremias 38:23"> But the host of the Chaldeans pursued after the king, and overtook him in the country beyond Jericho; and all his servants were dispersed from about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 39:5; Numbers 13:21; Kings IV 25:6"> And they took the king, and brought him to the king of Babylon to Deblatha, and he judged him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 22:30; Kings IV 25:7; Genesis 21:16"> And the king of Babylon killed the sons of Sedekias before his eyes; and he killed all the princes of Juda in Deblatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:13; Jeremias 34:3–5"> And he put out the eyes of Sedekias, and bound him in fetters; and the king of Babylon brought him to Babylon, and put him into the grinding-house, until the day when he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 25:8; Jeremias 39:9; Jeremias 52:29"> And in the fifth month, on the tenth day of the month, Nabuzardan the captain of the guard, who waited on the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 79:1; Michaeas 3:12; Chronicles II 36:19"> and he burnt the house of the Lord, and the king's house; and all the houses of the city, and every great house he burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:3; Kings IV 25:10"> And the host of the Chaldeans that was with the captain of the guard pulled down all the wall of Jerusalem round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 25:12; Jeremias 39:9–10; Jeremias 40:5–7"> But the captain of the guard left the remnant of the people to be vinedressers and husbandmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 27:19–22; Jeremias 52:21–23; Chronicles II 4:12–15"> And the Chaldeans broke in pieces the brazen pillars that were in the house of the Lord, and the bases, and the brazen sea that was in the house of the Lord, and they took the brass thereof, and carried it away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 27:3; Numbers 4:14; Kings III 7:45"> Also the rim, and the bowls, and the flesh hooks, and all the brazen vessels, wherewith they ministered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 7:49–50; Chronicles II 4:6–22; Leviticus 26:12"> and the basins, and the snuffers, and the oil-funnels, and the candlesticks, and the censers, and the cups, the golden, of gold, and the silver, of silver, the captain of the guard took away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings III 7:47; Kings IV 25:16; Chronicles II 4:18"> And the two pillars, and the one sea, and the twelve brazen oxen under the sea, which things king Solomon made for the house of the Lord; the brass of which articles was without weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 25:17; Kings III 7:15–21; Chronicles II 3:15–17"> And as for the pillars, the height of one pillar was thirty-five cubits; and a line of twelve cubits compassed it round; and the thickness of it all round was four fingers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 28:25; Kings IV 25:17; Chronicles II 3:15"> And there was a brazen chapiter upon them, and the length was five cubits, even the height of one chapiter; and there were on the chapiter round about network and pomegranates, all of brass: and correspondingly the second pillar had eight pomegranates to a cubit for the twelve cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 7:20"> And the pomegranates were ninety-six on a side; and all the pomegranates on the network round about were one hundred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings IV 25:18; Jeremias 29:25; Jeremias 35:4"> And the captain of the guard took the chief priest, and the second priest, and those that kept the way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings IV 25:19; Esther 1:14; Matthew 18:10"> and one eunuch, who was over the men of war, and seven men of renown, who were in the king's presence that were found in the city; and the scribe of the forces, who did the part of a scribe to the people of the land; and sixty men of the people of the land, who were found in the midst of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And Nabuzardan the captain of the king's guard took them, and brought them to the king of Babylon to Deblatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:28; Esias 32:13–14; Esias 6:11–12"> And the king of Babylon struck them in Deblatha, in the land of Aemath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings IV 25:27–30; Psalms 3:3; Genesis 40:13"> And it came to pass in the thirty-seventh year after that Joakim king of Juda had been carried away captive, in the twelfth month, on the four and twentieth day of the month, that Ulaemadachar king of Babylon, in the year in which he began to reign, raised the head of Joakim king of Juda, and shaved him, and brought him out of the house where he was kept,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 12:25; Daniel 5:18–19; Daniel 2:37"> and spoke kindly to him, and set his throne above the kings that were with him in Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings II 9:7; Kings III 2:7; Genesis 41:14"> and changed his prison garments: and he ate bread continually before him all the days that he lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings II 9:10; Luke 11:3; Matthew 6:11"> And his appointed portion was given him continually by the king of Babylon from day to day, until the day when he died.</span>
