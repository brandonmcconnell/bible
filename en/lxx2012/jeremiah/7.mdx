<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 26:2; Revelation 3:22; Amos 7:16"> Hear you⌃ the word of the Lord, all Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 18:11; Jeremias 26:13; Esias 1:16–19"> Thus says the Lord God of Israel, Correct your ways and your devices, and I will cause you to dwell in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Michaeas 3:11; Matthew 3:9–10; Sophonias 3:11"> Trust not in yourselves with lying words, for they shall not profit you at all, saying, It is the temple of the Lord, the temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 6:12–13; Esias 1:19; Jeremias 7:3"> For if you⌃ thoroughly correct your ways and your practices, and do indeed execute judgment between a man and his neighbor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 22:21–24; Zacharias 7:9–12; Jeremias 13:10"> and oppress not the stranger, and the orphan, and the widow, and shed not innocent blood in this place, and go not after strange gods to your hurt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Jeremias 25:5; Jeremias 3:18"> then will I cause you to dwell in this place, in the land which I gave to your fathers of old and for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 28:15; Jezekiel 13:6–16; Jeremias 23:14–16"> But whereas you⌃ have trusted in lying words, whereby you⌃ shall not be profited;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 7:6; Exodus 20:3; James 4:1–4"> and you⌃ murder, and commit adultery, and steal, and swear falsely, and burn incense to Baal, and are gone after strange gods whom you⌃ know not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 32:34; Jezekiel 23:37; Jezekiel 23:39"> so that it is evil with you; yet have you⌃ come, and stood before me in the house, whereon my name is called, and you⌃ have said, We have refrained from doing all these abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Esias 56:7; Matthew 21:13"> Is my house, whereon my name is called, a den of robbers in your eyes? And, behold, I have seen it, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Jeremias 26:6; Kings I 4:10–11"> For go you⌃ to my place which is in Selo, where I caused my name to dwell before, and see what I did to it because of the wickedness of my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 65:12; Chronicles II 36:15–16; Jeremias 7:25"> And now, because you⌃ have done all these deeds, and I spoke to you, but you⌃ listened not to me; and I called you, but you⌃ answered not;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 78:60; Kings IV 25:9; Jeremias 7:4"> therefore I also will do to the house whereon my name is called, wherein you⌃ trust, and to the place which I gave to you and to your fathers, as I did to Selo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 15:1; Kings IV 17:23; Jeremias 52:3"> And I will cast you out of my sight, as I cast away your brethren, all the seed of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 11:14; Jeremias 14:11–12; Jeremias 15:1"> Therefore pray not you for this people, and intercede not for them to be pitied, yes, pray not, and approach me not for them: for I will not listen to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 8:6–18; Jezekiel 14:23; Jeremias 6:27"> Seest you not what they do in the cities of Juda, and in the streets of Jerusalem?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 19:13; Jeremias 44:17–19; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> Their children gather wood, and their fathers kindle a fire, and their women knead dough, to make cakes to the host of heaven; and they have poured out drink-offerings to strange gods, that they might provoke me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 20:11; Jeremias 2:19; Daniel 9:7–8"> Do they provoke me to anger? says the Lord: do they not provoke themselves, that their faces may be ashamed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 42:18; Lamentations 2:3–5; Esias 42:25"> Therefore thus says the Lord; Behold, my anger and wrath shall be poured out upon this place, and upon the men, and upon the cattle, and upon every tree of their field, and upon the fruits of the land; and it shall burn, and not be quenched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Osee 8:13; Jeremias 6:20; Amos 5:21–23"> Thus says the Lord, Gather your whole burnt offerings with your meat-offerings, and eat flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Osee 6:6; Kings I 15:22; Psalms 51:16–17"> For I spoke not to your fathers, and commanded them not in the day wherein I brought them up out of the land of Egypt, concerning whole burnt offerings and sacrifice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 15:26; Deuteronomy 5:33; Exodus 19:5–6"> but I commanded them this thing, saying, Hear you⌃ my voice, and I will be to you a God, and you⌃ shall be to me a people: and walk you⌃ in all my ways which I shall command you, that it may be well with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 81:11–12; Jeremias 8:5; Jeremias 7:26"> But they listened not to me, and their ear gave no heed, but they walked in the imaginations of their evil heart, and went backward, and not forward;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:15; Jeremias 25:4; Nehemiah 9:26"> from the day that their fathers went forth out of the land of Egypt, even until this day. And I sent to you all my servants, the prophets, by day and early in the morning: yes, I sent them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 16:12; Chronicles II 30:8; Jeremias 7:24"> but they listened not to me, and their ear gave no heed; and they made their neck harder than their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremias 26:2; Jeremias 1:7; Esias 65:12"> Therefore you shall speak this word to them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esias 59:14–15; Proverbs 1:7; Esias 1:4–5"> This is the nation which has not listened to the voice of the Lord, nor received correction: truth has failed from their mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Job 1:20; Michaeas 1:16; Jeremias 6:30"> Cut off your hair, and cast it away, and take up a lamentation on your lips; for the Lord has reprobated and rejected the generation that does these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremias 32:34; Jezekiel 7:20; Daniel 9:27"> For the children of Juda have wrought evil before me, says the Lord; they have set their abominations in the house on which my name is called, to defile it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings IV 23:10; Jeremias 32:35; Leviticus 18:21"> And they have built the altar of Taphes, which is in the valley of the son of Ennom, to burn their sons and their daughters with fire; which I did not command them to do, neither did I design it in my heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremias 19:11; Jeremias 19:6; Kings IV 23:10"> Therefore, behold, the days come, says the Lord, when they shall no more say, The altar of Taphes, and the valley of the son of Ennom, but, The valley of the slain; and they shall bury in Taphes, for lack of room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremias 12:9; Deuteronomy 28:26; Revelation 19:17–18"> And the dead bodies of this people shall be for food to the birds of the sky, and to the wild beasts of the earth; and there shall be none to drive them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 18:23; Osee 2:11; Jezekiel 26:13"> And I will destroy out of the cities of Juda, and the streets of Jerusalem, the voice of them that make merry, and the voice of them that rejoice, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride; for the whole land shall become a desolation.</span>
