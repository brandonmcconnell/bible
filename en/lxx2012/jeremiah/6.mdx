<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:14; Judges 1:21; Joshua 18:21–28"> Strengthen yourselves, you⌃ children of Benjamin, to flee out of the midst of Jerusalem, and sound an alarm with the trumpet in Thecue, and set up a signal over Baethacharma: for evil threatens from the north, and a great destruction is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 4:31; Esias 1:8; Esias 3:16–17"> And your pride, O daughter of Sion, shall be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 19:43; Jeremias 39:1–3; Kings IV 24:10–12"> The shepherds and their flocks shall come to her; and they shall pitch their tents against her round about, and shall feed their flocks each with his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 15:8; Sophonias 2:4; Joel 3:9"> Prepare yourselves for war against her; rise up, and let us go up against her at noon. Woe to us! for the day has gone down, for the shadows of the day fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 52:13; Esias 32:14; Chronicles II 36:19"> Rise, and let us go up against her by night, and destroy her foundations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:19–20; Jeremias 32:24"> For thus says the Lord, Hew down her trees, array a numerous force against Jerusalem. O false city; there is all oppression in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 20:8; Jezekiel 7:23; Jezekiel 7:11"> As a cistern cools water, so her wickedness cools her, ungodliness and misery shall be heard in her, as continually before her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:18; Osee 9:12; Jeremias 17:23"> You shall be chastened, O Jerusalem, with pain and the scourge, lest my soul depart from you; lest I make you a desert land, which shall not be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 49:9; Jeremias 16:16; Obdias 1:5–6"> For thus says the Lord, Glean, glean thoroughly as a vine the remnant of Israel: turn back your hands as a grape-gatherer to his basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 7:51; Jeremias 7:26; John 7:7"> To whom shall I speak, and testify, that he may listen? behold, your ears are uncircumcised, and they shall not be able to hear: behold, the word of the Lord is become to them a reproach, they will not at all desire it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 9:21; Jeremias 20:9; Michaeas 3:8"> And I allowed my wrath to come to full, yet I kept it in, and did not utterly destroy them: I will pour it out on the children without, and on the assembly of young men together: for man and woman shall be taken together, the old man with him that is full of days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 8:10; Esias 5:25; Deuteronomy 28:39–43"> And their houses shall be turned to others, with their fields and their wives together: for I will stretch out my hand upon the inhabitants of this land, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 23:11; Jeremias 8:10; Michaeas 3:11"> For from the least of them even to the greatest they have all committed iniquity; from the priest even to the false prophet they have all wrought falsely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 13:10; Jeremias 23:17; Jeremias 4:10"> And they healed the breach of my people imperfectly, making light of it, and saying, Peace, peace, and where is peace?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 8:12; Matthew 15:14; Jeremias 3:3"> They were ashamed because they failed; yet they were not ashamed as those who are truly ashamed, and they knew not their own disgrace: therefore shall they utterly fall when they do fall, and in the time of visitation shall they perish, said the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 30:21; Deuteronomy 32:7; Jeremias 18:15"> Thus says the Lord, Stand you⌃ in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths of the Lord; and see what is the good way, and walk in it, and you⌃ shall find purification for your souls. But they said, We will not walk in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 25:4; Esias 58:1; Zacharias 7:11"> I have set watchmen over you, saying, Hear you⌃ the sound of the trumpet. But they said, We will not hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 4:10; Jeremias 31:10; Michaeas 6:5"> Therefore have the nations heard, and they that feed their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 1:2; Jeremias 8:9; Jeremias 17:10"> Hear, O earth: behold, I will bring evils upon this people, even the fruit of their rebellions; for they have not heeded my words, and they have rejected my law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esias 1:11; Amos 5:21–22; Esias 60:6"> Therefore do you⌃ bring me frankincense from Saba, and cinnamon from a land afar off? your whole burnt offerings are not acceptable, and your sacrifices have not been pleasant to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 8:14; Jezekiel 3:20; Jeremias 13:16"> Therefore thus says the Lord, Behold, I will bring weakness upon this people, and the fathers and sons shall be weak together; the neighbor and his friend shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 50:41–43; Jeremias 10:22; Jeremias 6:1"> Thus says the Lord, Behold, a people comes from the north, and nations shall be stirred up from the end of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esias 13:18; Jeremias 50:42; Luke 21:25–26"> They shall lay hold on bow and spear; the people is fierce, and will have no mercy; their voice is as the roaring sea; they shall array themselves for war against you as fire on horses and chariots, O daughter of Sion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 4:31; Jeremias 50:43; Jeremias 49:24"> We have heard the report of them: our hands are weakened: anguish has seized us, the pangs as of a woman in travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 49:29; Jeremias 20:10; Psalms 31:13"> Go not forth into the field, and walk not in the ways; for the sword of the enemy lingers round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 4:8; Zacharias 12:10; Esias 32:11"> O daughter of my people, gird yourself with sackcloth: sprinkle yourself with ashes; make for yourself pitiable lamentation, as the mourning for a beloved son: for misery will come suddenly upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremias 1:18; Jeremias 15:20; Jezekiel 3:8–10"> I have caused you to be tried among tried nations, and you shall know me when I have tried their way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremias 9:4; Jeremias 5:23; Jeremias 18:18"> They are all disobedient, walking perversely: they are brass and iron; they are all corrupted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:13; Jeremias 9:7; 1 Peter 4:12"> The bellows have failed from the fire, the lead has failed: the silversmith works at his trade in vain; their wickedness is not consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Osee 9:17; Psalms 119:119; Esias 1:22"> Call you⌃ them reprobate silver, because the Lord has rejected them.</span>
