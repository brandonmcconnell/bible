<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 34:2; Luke 3:19–20; Kings II 24:11–12"> Thus says the Lord; Go you, and go down to the house of the king of Juda, and you shall speak there this word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 1:32; Esias 9:7; Jeremias 22:4"> and you shall say, Hear the word of the Lord, O king of Juda, that sit on the throne of David, you, and your house, and your people, and they that go in at these gates:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 21:12; Michaeas 6:8; Jeremias 22:17"> thus says the Lord; Execute you⌃ judgment and justice, and rescue the spoiled out of the hand of him that wrongs him: and oppress not the stranger, and orphan, and widow, and sin not, and shed no innocent blood in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 17:25"> For if you⌃ will indeed perform this word, then shall there enter in by the gates of this house kings sitting upon the throne of David, and riding on chariots and horses, they, and their servants, and their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 6:8; Hebrews 6:13; Genesis 22:16"> But if you⌃ will not perform these words, by myself have I sworn, says the Lord, that this house shall be brought to desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Esias 6:11; Esias 27:10"> For thus says the Lord concerning the house of the king of Juda; You are Galaad to me, and the head of Libanus: yet surely I will make you a desert, even cities that shall not be inhabited:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 37:24; Esias 10:33–34; Jeremias 4:6–7"> and I will bring upon you a destroying man, and his axe: and they shall cut down your choice cedars, and cast them into the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings III 9:8–9; Chronicles II 7:20–22; Deuteronomy 29:23–26"> And nations shall pass through this city, and each shall say to his neighbor, Why has the Lord done thus to this great city?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings IV 22:17; Chronicles II 34:25; Jeremias 50:7"> And they shall say, Because they forsook the covenant of the Lord their God, and worshipped strange gods, and served them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:2; Jeremias 22:11; Esias 57:1"> Weep not for the dead, nor lament for him: weep bitterly for him that goes away: for he shall return no more, nor see his native land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:15; Chronicles II 36:1–4; Kings IV 23:34"> For thus says the Lord concerning Sellem the son of Josias, who reigns in the place of Josias his father, who has gone forth out of this place; He shall not return there any more:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 23:34; Jeremias 22:18"> but in that place whither I have carried him captive, there shall he die, and shall see this land no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Michaeas 3:10; Leviticus 19:13"> He that builds his house not with justice, and his upper chambers not with judgment, who works by means of his neighbor for nothing, and will by no means give him his reward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 7:2; Esias 5:8–9; Aggaeus 1:4"> You have built for yourself a well-proportioned house, airy chambers, fitted with windows, and wainscoted with cedar, and painted with vermilion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings IV 23:25; Esias 3:10; Ecclesiastes 10:17"> Shall you reign, because you are provoked with your father Achaz? they shall not eat, and they shall not drink: it is better for you to execute judgment and justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 9:24; Esias 1:17; 1 John 2:3–4"> They understood not, they judged not the cause of the afflicted, nor the cause of the poor: is not this your not knowing me? says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 2:14; Kings IV 24:4; Jeremias 22:3"> Behold, your eyes are not good, nor your heart, but they go after your covetousness, and after the innocent blood to shed it, and after acts of injustice and slaughter, to commit them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 13:30; Jeremias 16:6; Jeremias 16:4"> Therefore thus says the Lord concerning Joakim son of Josias, king of Juda, even concerning this man; they shall not bewail him, saying, Ah brother! neither shall they at all weep for him, saying, Alas Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 36:30; Kings III 21:23–24; Chronicles II 36:6"> He shall be buried with the burial of an ass; he shall be dragged roughly along and cast outside the gate of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 27:12; Jezekiel 23:22; Lamentations 1:2"> Go up to Libanus, and cry; and utter your voice to Basan, and cry aloud to the extremity of the sea: for all your lovers are destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 32:30; Jeremias 3:25; Jeremias 7:22–28"> I spoke to you on occasion of your trespass, but you said, I will not listen. This has been your way from your youth, you have not listened to my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 5:30–31; Jeremias 12:10; Jeremias 23:1–2"> The wind shall tend all your shepherds, and your lovers shall go into captivity; for then shall you be ashamed and disgraced because of all your lovers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremias 6:24; Jeremias 4:30–31; Jeremias 22:6"> O you that dwell in Libanus, making your nest in the cedars, you shall groan heavily, when pangs as of a travailing woman are come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 37:1; Song of Songs 8:6; Chronicles I 3:16"> As I live, says the Lord, though Jechonias son of Joakim king of Juda were indeed the seal upon my right hand, thence would I pluck you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 34:20–21; Jeremias 21:7; Kings IV 24:15–16"> and I will deliver you into the hands of them that seek your life, before whom you are afraid, into the hands of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings IV 24:15; Jeremias 15:2–4; Esias 22:17"> And I will cast forth you, and your mother that bore you, into a land where you were not born; and there you⌃ shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremias 44:14; Kings IV 25:27–30; Psalms 86:4"> But they shall by no means return to the land which they long for in their souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Osee 8:8; Jeremias 48:38"> Jechonias is dishonored as a good-for-nothing vessel; for he is thrown out and cast forth into a land which he knew not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Michaeas 1:2; Jeremias 6:19"> Land, land, hear the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremias 36:30; Psalms 94:20; Matthew 1:11–16"> Write you⌃ this man an outcast: for there shall none of his seed at all grow up to sit on the throne of David, or as a prince yet in Juda.</span>
