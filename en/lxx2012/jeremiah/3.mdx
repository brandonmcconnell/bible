<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1–4; Jezekiel 16:26; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> If a man put away his wife, and she depart from him, and become another man's, shall she return to him any more at all? shall not that woman be utterly defiled? Yet you⌃ have gone a-whoring with many shepherds, and have returned to me, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 2:7; Genesis 38:14; Jezekiel 8:4–6"> Lift up your eyes to look straight forward, and see where you have not been utterly defiled. You have sat for them by the wayside as a deserted crow, and have defiled the land with your fornications and your wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremias 14:4; Deuteronomy 28:23"> And you did retain many shepherds for a stumbling block to yourself: you had a whore's face, you did become shameless toward all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 2:2; Jeremias 3:19; Psalms 71:17"> Have you not called me as it were a home, and the father and guide of your virgin-time?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Michaeas 7:3; Esias 57:16; Jezekiel 22:6"> Will God's anger continue for ever, or be preserved to the end? Behold, you have spoken and done these bad things, and had power to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings IV 17:7–17; Jezekiel 16:31; Jeremias 7:24"> And the Lord said to me in the days of Josias the king, Have you seen what things the house of Israel has done to me? they have gone on every high mountain, and under every shady tree, and have committed fornication there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:46; Osee 6:1–4; Osee 14:1"> And I said after she had committed all these acts of fornication, Turn again to me. Yet she returned not. And faithless Juda saw her faithlessness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:1; Osee 4:15–17; Esias 50:1"> And I saw that (for all the sins of which she was convicted, wherein the house of Israel committed adultery, and I put her away, and gave into her hands a bill of divorcement,) yet faithless Juda feared not, but went and herself also committed fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 2:27; Esias 57:6; Jeremias 3:2"> And her fornication was nothing accounted of; and she committed adultery with wood and stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 7:14; Jeremias 12:2; Chronicles II 34:33–18"> And for all these things faithless Juda turned not to me with all her heart, but falsely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:11; Jezekiel 16:51–52; Osee 11:7"> And the Lord said to me, Israel has justified himself more than faithless Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Jezekiel 33:11; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> Go and read these words toward the north, and you shall say, Return to me, O house of Israel, says the Lord; and I will not set my face against you: for I am merciful, says the Lord, and I will not be angry with you for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 2:25; Jeremias 3:6; Deuteronomy 12:2"> Nevertheless, know your iniquity, that you have sinned against the Lord your God, and have scattered your ways to strangers under every shady tree, but you did not listen to my voice, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Osee 2:19–20; Jeremias 2:19; Jeremias 31:32"> Turn, you⌃ children that have revolted, says the Lord; for I will rule over you: and I will take you one of a city, and two of a family, and I will bring you in to Sion:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 23:4; Acts 20:28; Ephesians 4:11–12"> and I will give you shepherds after my heart, and they shall certainly tend you with knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 65:17; Amos 9:14–15; Jezekiel 37:26"> And it shall come to pass that when you⌃ are multiplied and increased upon the land, says the Lord, in those days they shall say no more, The ark of the covenant of the Holy One of Israel: it shall not come to mind; it shall not be named; neither shall it be visited; nor shall this be done any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 43:7; Michaeas 4:1–5; Jeremias 17:12"> In those days and at that time they shall call Jerusalem the throne of the Lord; and all the nations shall be gathered to it: and they shall not walk any more after the imaginations of their evil heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 31:8; Osee 1:11; Amos 9:15"> In those days the house of Juda, shall come together to the house of Israel, and they shall come, together, from the land of the north, and from all the countries, to the land, which I caused their fathers to inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremias 3:4; Jeremias 31:9; Esias 63:16"> And I said, So be it, Lord, for you said I will set you among children, and will give you a choice land, the inheritance of the Almighty God of the Gentiles: and I said, You⌃ shall call me Father; and you⌃ shall not turn away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esias 48:8; Jeremias 5:11; Osee 5:7"> But as a wife acts treacherously against her husband, so has the house of Israel dealt treacherously against me, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Jeremias 31:9; Jeremias 2:32"> A voice from the lips was heard, even of weeping and supplication of the children of Israel: for they have dealt unrighteously in their ways, they have forgotten God their Holy One.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Osee 14:1; Osee 14:4; Zacharias 13:9"> Turn, you⌃ children that are given to turning, and I will heal your bruises. Behold, we will be your servants; for you are the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 121:1–2; Psalms 3:8; Esias 12:2"> Truly the hills and the strength of the mountains were a lying refuge: but by the Lord our God is the salvation of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Osee 9:10; Jezekiel 16:63; Jezekiel 16:61"> But shame has consumed the labors of our fathers from our youth; their sheep and their calves, and their sons and their daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 22:21; Proverbs 5:13; Esias 50:11"> We have lain down in our shame, and our disgrace has covered us: because we and our fathers have sinned before our God, from our youth until this day; and we have not listened to the voice of the Lord our God.</span>
