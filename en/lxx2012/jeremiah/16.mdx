<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 1:2; Jeremias 2:1; Jeremias 1:4"> And you shall not take a wife, says the Lord God of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:26–27; Luke 23:29; Genesis 19:14"> and there shall be no son born to you, nor daughter in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 16:9; Jeremias 16:5; Jeremias 6:21"> For thus says the Lord concerning the sons and concerning the daughters that are born in this place, and concerning their mothers that have born them, and concerning their fathers that have begotten them in this land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 25:33; Psalms 83:10; Jeremias 9:22"> They shall die of grievous death; they shall not be lamented, nor buried; they shall be for an example on the face of the earth; and they shall be for the wild beasts of the land, and for the birds of the sky: they shall fall by the sword, and shall be consumed with famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:16–23; Esias 27:11; Jeremias 15:1–4"> Thus says the Lord, Enter not into their mourning feast, and go not to lament, and mourn not for them: for I have removed my peace from this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Jeremias 41:5; Jeremias 47:5"> They shall not bewail them, nor make cuttings for them, and they shall not shave themselves for them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Osee 9:4; Jezekiel 24:17; Deuteronomy 26:14"> and there shall be no bread broken in mourning for them for consolation over the dead: they shall not give one to drink a cup for consolation over his father or his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 15:17; Ecclesiastes 7:2–4; Esias 22:12–14"> You shall not enter into the banquet-house, to sit with them to eat and to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 25:10; Jeremias 7:34; Jezekiel 26:13"> For thus says the Lord God of Israel; Behold, I will make to cease out of this place before your eyes, and in your days, the voice of joy, and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 5:19; Kings III 9:8–9; Deuteronomy 29:24–25"> And it shall come to pass, when you shall report to this people all these words, and they shall say to you, Therefore has the Lord pronounced against us all these evils? what is our unrighteousness? and what is our sin which we have sinned before the Lord our God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 106:35–41; Deuteronomy 29:25–26; Jeremias 22:9"> Then you shall say to them, Because your fathers forsook me, says the Lord, and went after strange gods and served them, and worshipped them, and forsook me, and kept not my law;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 13:10; Jeremias 7:26; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> (and you⌃ sinned worse than your fathers; for, behold, you⌃ walk every one after the lusts of your own evil heart, so as not to listen to me);</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36; Deuteronomy 4:26–28; Jeremias 15:14"> therefore I will cast you off from this good land into a land which neither you⌃ nor your fathers have known; and you⌃ shall serve their other gods, who shall have no mercy upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremias 23:7–8; Deuteronomy 15:15; Exodus 20:2"> Therefore, behold, the days come, says the Lord, when they shall no more say, The Lord lives, that brought up the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 3:18; Jeremias 24:6; Jeremias 32:37"> but, The Lord lives, who brought up the house of Israel from the land of the north, and from all countries whither they were thrust out: and I will restore them to their own land, which I gave to their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ambacum 1:14–15; Amos 4:2; Michaeas 7:2"> Behold, I will send many fishers, says the Lord, and they shall fish them; and afterward I will send many hunters, and they shall hunt them upon every mountain, and upon every hill, and out of the holes of the rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Jeremias 23:24; Proverbs 5:21"> For my eyes are upon all their ways; and their iniquities have not been hidden from my eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 35:33–34; Esias 40:2; Revelation 18:6"> And I will recompense their mischiefs doubly, and their sins, whereby they have profaned my land with the carcasses of their abominations, and with their iniquities, whereby they have trespassed against my inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 25:4; Jeremias 17:17; Naum 1:7"> O Lord, you are my strength, and my help, and my refuge in days of evil: to you the Gentiles shall come from the end of the earth, and shall say, How vain were the idols which our fathers procured to themselves, and there is no help in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esias 37:19; Psalms 115:4–8; Galatians 4:8"> Will a man make gods for himself, whereas these are no gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 9:16; Psalms 83:18; Jeremias 33:2"> Therefore, behold, I will at this time manifest my hand to them, and will make known to them my power; and they shall know that my name is the Lord.</span>
