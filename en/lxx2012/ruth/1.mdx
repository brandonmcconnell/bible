<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 12:10; Genesis 26:1; Jezekiel 14:13"> And it came to pass when the judges ruled, that there was a famine in the land: and a man went from Bethleem Juda to sojourn in the land of Moab, he, and his wife, and his two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 35:19; Michaeas 5:2; Kings I 17:21"> And the man's name was Elimelech, and his wife's name Noemin, and the names of his two sons Maalon and Chelaion, Ephrathites of Bethleem of Juda: and they came to the land of Moab, and remained there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hebrews 12:10–11; Psalms 34:19; Hebrews 12:6"> And Elimelech the husband of Noemin died; and she was left, and her two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 1:5; Kings III 11:1–2; Deuteronomy 23:3"> And they took to themselves wives, women of Moab; the name of the one was Orpha, and the name of the second Ruth; and they lived there about ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Psalms 89:30–32; Esias 49:21"> And both Maalon and Chelaion died also; and the woman was left of her husband and her two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 132:15; Exodus 3:16; Psalms 111:5"> And she rose up and her two daughters-in-law, and they returned out of the country of Moab, for she heard in the country of Moab that the Lord had visited his people to give them bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 8:3; Ruth 1:14; Exodus 18:27"> And she went forth out of the place where she was, and her two daughters-in-law with her: and they went by the way to return to the land of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 2:20; Ruth 1:5; Colossians 3:18"> And Noemin said to her daughter-in-law, Go now, return each to the house of her mother: the Lord deal mercifully with you, as you⌃ have dealt with the dead, and with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 3:1; Acts 20:37; Genesis 29:11"> The Lord grant you that you⌃ may find rest each of you in the house of her husband: and she kissed them; and they lifted up their voice, and wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zacharias 8:23; Psalms 16:3; Psalms 119:63"> And they said to her, We will return with you to your people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Genesis 38:11"> And Noemin said, Return now, my daughters; and why do you⌃ go with me? have I yet sons in my womb to be your husbands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; 1 Timothy 5:9"> Turn now, my daughters, for I am too old to be married: for I said, Suppose I were married, and should bear sons;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 19:21; Judges 2:15; Psalms 32:4"> would you⌃ wait for them till they should be grown? or would you⌃ refrain from being married for their sakes? Not so, my daughters; for I am grieved for you, that the hand of the Lord has gone forth against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:24; Matthew 16:24; Matthew 10:37"> And they lifted up their voice, and wept again; and Orpha kissed her mother-in-law and returned to her people; but Ruth followed her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 11:24; Joshua 24:15; Sophonias 1:6"> And Noemin said to Ruth, Behold, your sister-in-law has returned to her people and to her gods; turn now you also after your sister-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 15:21; Ruth 2:11–12; Kings IV 2:2–6"> And Ruth said, Intreat me not to leave you, or to return from following you; for wherever you go, I will go, and wherever you lodge, I will lodge; your people shall be my people, and your God my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings I 3:17; Kings III 2:23; Kings IV 6:31"> And wherever you die, I will die, and there will I be buried: the Lord do so to me, and more also, if I leave you, for death only shall divide between me and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:14; Ephesians 6:10; Acts 2:42"> And Noemin seeing that she was determined to go with her, ceased to speak to her any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:10; Esias 23:7; Lamentations 2:15"> And they went both of them until they came to Bethleem: and it came to pass, when they arrived at Bethleem, that all the city rang with them, and they said, Is this Noemin?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 6:4; Hebrews 12:11; Psalms 73:14"> And she said to them, Nay, do not call me Noemin; call me 'Bitter,' for the Mighty One has dealt very bitterly with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 2:7–8; Job 1:21; Job 16:8"> I went out full, and the Lord has brought me back empty: and why call you⌃ me Noemin, whereas the Lord has humbled me and the Mighty One has afflicted me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ruth 2:23; Kings II 21:9; Exodus 9:31–32"> So Noemin and Ruth the Moabitess, her daughter-in-law, returned from the country of Moab; and they came to Bethleem in the beginning of barley harvest.</span>
