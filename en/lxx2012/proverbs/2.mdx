<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 3:1; Proverbs 1:3; Proverbs 4:1"> My son, if you will receive the utterance of my commandment, and hide it with you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 23:12; Proverbs 18:1; Psalms 119:111–112"> your ear shall listen to wisdom; you shall also apply your heart to understanding, and shall apply it to the instruction of your son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="James 1:5; Ephesians 1:17–18; Proverbs 3:6"> For if you shall call to wisdom, and utter your voice for understanding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 13:44; Proverbs 16:16; Matthew 19:29"> and if you shall seek it as silver, and search diligently for it as for treasures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Jeremias 24:7; Jeremias 32:40–41"> then shall you understand the fear of the Lord, and find the knowledge of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17–18; James 1:5; Job 32:8"> For the Lord gives wisdom; and from his presence come knowledge and understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 3:15–17; Proverbs 30:5; Psalms 84:11"> and he treasures up salvation for them that walk uprightly: he will protect their way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 23:3; Kings I 2:9; John 10:28–29"> that he may guard the righteous ways: and he will preserve the way of them that fear him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 6:16; Psalms 143:8–10; Matthew 7:13–14"> Then shall you understand righteousness, and judgment; and shall direct<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. all good a les.">a</a></sup>all your course aright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 15:16; Colossians 3:16; Job 23:12"> For if wisdom shall come into your understanding, and discernment shall seem pleasing to your soul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 4:6; Ephesians 5:15; Psalms 25:21"> good counsel shall guard you, and holy understanding shall keep you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 8:13; Proverbs 13:20"> to deliver you from the evil way, and from the man that speaks nothing faithfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:19–20; Proverbs 4:19; Proverbs 21:16"> Alas for those who forsake right paths, to walk in ways of darkness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 1:32; Proverbs 10:23; Jeremias 11:15"> who rejoice in evils, and delight in wicked perverseness;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 125:5; Proverbs 21:8; Philippians 2:15"> whose paths are crooked, and their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. wheel tracks. Compare Heb 12:13.">b</a></sup>courses winding;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 23:27"> to remove you far from the straight way, and to estrange you from a righteous purpose. My son, let not evil counsel overtake you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:59–60; Jezekiel 16:8; Jeremias 3:4"> of her who has forsaken the instruction of her youth, and forgotten the covenant of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 9:18; 1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Proverbs 5:4–14"> For she has fixed her house near death, and guided her wheels near Hades with the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Heb.">c</a></sup>giants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:26; Psalms 81:12; Osee 4:14"> None that go by her shall return, neither shall they take hold of right paths, for they are not apprehended of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Singular variation from Heb.">d</a></sup>years of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; 3 John 1:11; Psalms 119:63"> For had they gone in good paths, they would have found the paths of righteousness<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. smooth.">e</a></sup>easy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 84:11; Psalms 37:29; Psalms 37:9"> For the upright shall dwell in the earth, and the holy shall be left behind in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 52:5; Deuteronomy 7:22; Esias 3:10–11"> The paths of the ungodly shall perish out of the earth, and transgressors shall be driven away from it.</span>
