<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 17:25"> A wise son makes his father glad: but a foolish son is a grief to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Proverbs 12:28; Psalms 49:6–10"> Treasures shall not profit the lawless: but righteousness shall deliver from death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 37:3; Matthew 6:30–33; Psalms 37:25"> The Lord will not famish a righteous soul: but he will overthrow the life of the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 21:5; Proverbs 20:4; Proverbs 19:15"> Poverty brings a man low: but the hands of the vigorous make rich. A son who is instructed shall be wise, and shall use the fool for a servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:8; Proverbs 30:25; Proverbs 17:2"> A wise son is saved from heat: but a lawless son is blighted of the winds in harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:2; Proverbs 28:20; Job 29:13"> The blessing of the Lord is upon the head of the just: but untimely grief shall cover the mouth of the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 112:6; Luke 1:48; Mark 14:9"> The memory of the just is praised; but the name of the ungodly man is extinguished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Proverbs 12:1; James 3:13"> A wise man in heart will receive commandments; but he that is unguarded in his lips shall be overthrown in his perverseness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 28:18; Matthew 10:26; Psalms 26:11–12"> He that walks simply, walks confidently; but he that perverts his ways shall be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 35:19; Proverbs 6:13; 3 John 1:10"> He that winks with his eyes deceitfully, procures griefs for men; but he that reproves boldly is a peacemaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; Ephesians 4:29; Proverbs 10:6"> There is a fountain of life in the hand of a righteous man; but destruction shall cover the mouth of the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:8; 1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Proverbs 17:9"> Hatred stirs up strife; but affection covers all that do not love strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Proverbs 6:32; Esias 50:4"> He that brings forth wisdom from his lips smites the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. heartless man, Hebraism.">a</a></sup>fool with a rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 18:7; Proverbs 10:8; Proverbs 13:3"> The wise will hide discretion; but the mouth of the hasty draws near to ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 18:11; Proverbs 19:7; Psalms 52:7"> The wealth of rich men is a strong city; but poverty is the ruin of the ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:23; Esias 3:10–11; Hebrews 6:10"> The works of the righteous produce life; but the fruits of the ungodly produce sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 1:5–11; Luke 11:28; Proverbs 6:23"> Instruction keeps the right ways of life; but instruction unchastened goes astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26; Psalms 55:21; Kings II 3:27"> Righteous lips cover enmity; but they that utter railings are most foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="James 1:19; James 3:2; Proverbs 17:27–28"> By a multitude of words you shall not escape sin; but if you refrain your lips you will be prudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 17:9; Proverbs 12:18; Genesis 6:5"> The tongue of the just is tried silver; but the heart of the ungodly shall fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Osee 4:6; Proverbs 17:16; Proverbs 1:29"> The lips of the righteous know sublime truths: but the foolish die in lack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 107:38; Genesis 26:12; Kings I 2:7–8"> The blessing of the Lord is upon the head of the righteous; it enriches him, and grief of heart shall not be added to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:21; Proverbs 2:14; Proverbs 14:9"> A fool does mischief in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. laughter.">b</a></sup>sport; but wisdom brings forth prudence for a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Esias 66:4; Matthew 5:6"> The ungodly is engulfed in destruction; but the desire of the righteous is acceptable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 15:5; Psalms 58:9; Matthew 7:24–27"> When the storm passes by, the ungodly vanishes away; but the righteous turns aside and escapes for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 26:6; Matthew 25:26"> As a sour grape is hurtful to the teeth, and smoke to the eyes, so iniquity hurts those that practise it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 9:11; Proverbs 3:2; Psalms 91:16"> The fear of the Lord adds length of days: but the years of the ungodly shall be shortened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 8:13; Romans 15:13; Psalms 73:24–26"> Joy rests long with the righteous: but the hope of the ungodly shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 21:15; Romans 2:8–9; Philippians 4:13"> The fear of the Lord is a strong hold of the saints: but ruin comes to them that work wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 37:22; Psalms 125:1; Psalms 37:9–10"> The righteous shall never fail: but the ungodly shall not dwell in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 37:30; Proverbs 10:13; Proverbs 10:20–21"> The mouth of the righteous drops wisdom: but the tongue of the unjust shall perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Proverbs 12:6; Proverbs 11:11"> The lips of just men drop grace: but the mouth of the ungodly is perverse.</span>
