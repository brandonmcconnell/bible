<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 21:2; Kings I 16:7; Luke 16:15"> All the works of the humble man are manifest with God; but the ungodly shall perish in an evil day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–17; Proverbs 11:21; Proverbs 8:13"> Every one that is proud in heart is unclean before God, and he that unjustly strikes hands with hand shall not be held guiltless. The beginning of a good way is to do justly; and it is more acceptable with God than to offer sacrifices. He that seeks the Lord shall find knowledge with righteousness: and they that rightly seek him shall find peace. All of the works of the Lord are done with righteousness; and the ungodly man is kept for the evil day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 32:1–2; Genesis 44:5; Genesis 44:15"> There is an oracle upon the lips of a king; and his mouth shall not err in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 20:10; Proverbs 20:23"> The poise of the balance is righteousness with the Lord; and his works are righteous measures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 25:5; Proverbs 29:14; Luke 12:48"> An evil-doer is an abomination to a king; for the throne of rule is established by righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 14:35; Psalms 101:5–7; Proverbs 22:11"> Righteous lips are acceptable to a king; and he loves right words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 20:2; Proverbs 19:12; Ecclesiastes 10:4"> The anger of a king is a messenger of death; but a wise man will pacify him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 72:6; Job 29:23–24; Osee 6:3"> The son of a king is in the light of life; and they that are in favor with him are as a cloud of latter rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 8:10–11; Matthew 16:26; Proverbs 8:19"> The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, Abodes. Comp. Heb. See Lu 13:35.">a</a></sup>brood of wisdom is more to be chosen than gold, and the brood of prudence more to be chosen than silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 35:8; Proverbs 19:16; Proverbs 10:9"> The paths of life turn aside from evil; and the ways of righteousness are length of life. He that receives instruction shall be in prosperity; and he that regards reproofs shall be made wise. He that keeps his ways, preserves his own soul; and he that loves his life will spare his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 11:2; Esias 2:11–12; Proverbs 18:12"> Pride goes before destruction, and folly before a fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:3; Esias 57:15; Psalms 138:6"> Better is a meek-spirited man with<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, affliction.">b</a></sup>lowliness, than one who divides spoils with the proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 17:7–8; Psalms 2:12; Esias 30:18"> He who is skillful in business finds good: but he that trusts in God is most blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 16:23–24; Esias 50:4; Proverbs 15:7"> Men call the wise and understanding evil: but they that are pleasing in speech shall hear more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 13:14; John 6:63; John 5:24"> Understanding is a fountain of life to its possessors; but the instruction of fools is evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 15:28; Matthew 12:34–35; Colossians 3:16"> The heart of the wise will discern the things which proceed from his own mouth; and on his lips he will wear knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Proverbs 24:13–14; Proverbs 25:11–12"> Good words are honeycombs, and the sweetness thereof is a healing of the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 14:12; Proverbs 12:15; 2 Corinthians 13:5"> There are ways that seem to be right to a man, but the end of them looks to the depth of hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:7; Proverbs 14:23; Proverbs 9:12"> A man who labors, labors for himself, and drives from him his own ruin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="James 3:6; Proverbs 6:14; Esias 5:18"> But the perverse bears destruction upon his own mouth: a foolish man digs up evil for himself, and treasures fire on his own lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 17:9; Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 15:18"> A perverse man spreads mischief, and will kindle a torch of deceit with mischiefs; and he separates friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 2:12–15; Proverbs 12:26; Proverbs 1:10–14"> A transgressor tries to ensnare friends, and leads them in ways that are not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Michaeas 7:3; Matthew 14:7–8"> And the man that fixes his eyes devises perverse things, and marks out with his lips all evil: he is a furnace of wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 20:29; Leviticus 19:32; Proverbs 3:1–2"> Old age is a crown of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. boasting.">c</a></sup>honor, but it is found in the ways of righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 25:28"> A man slow to anger is better than a strong man; and he that governs his temper better than he that takes a city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Proverbs 18:18; Jonas 1:7; Acts 1:26"> All evils come upon the ungodly into their bosoms; but all righteous things come of the Lord.</span>
