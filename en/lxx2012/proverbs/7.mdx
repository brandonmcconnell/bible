<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 1:8; Revelation 1:3; Luke 11:28"> My son, keep my words, and hide with you my commandments. My son, honor the Lord, and you shall be strong; and fear none but him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 17:8; Proverbs 4:4; Leviticus 18:5"> keep my commandments, and you shall live; and keep my words as the pupils of your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:18–20; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 6:8–9"> And bind them on your fingers, and write them on the table of your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 4:6–8; Proverbs 2:2–4; Song of Songs 8:1"> Say that wisdom is your sister, and gain prudence as an acquaintance for yourself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:16; Proverbs 6:24; Proverbs 5:3"> that she may keep you from the strange and wicked woman, if she should assail you with flattering words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 26:8; Judges 5:28; Kings II 6:16"> For she looks from a window out of her house into the streets, at one whom she may see of the senseless ones, a young man void of understanding,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 6:32; Proverbs 1:22; Proverbs 1:32"> passing by the corner in the passages<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. of.">a</a></sup>near her house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 5:8; Proverbs 7:12; Judges 16:1"> and speaking, in the dark of the evening,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Job 24:13–15; Romans 13:12–14; Ephesians 5:11"> when there happens to be the stillness of night and of darkness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Esias 3:16–24; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> and the woman meets him having the appearance of a harlot, that causes the hearts of young men to flutter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 9:13; Titus 2:5; Proverbs 31:10–31"> And she is fickle, and debauched, and her feet abide not at home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:28; Proverbs 9:14; Revelation 18:3"> For at one time she wanders without, and at another time she lies in wait in the streets, at every corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 39:12; Numbers 25:6–8; Genesis 39:7"> Then she caught him, and kissed him, and with an impudent face said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 21:27; Leviticus 7:11; Proverbs 17:1"> I have a peace-offering; today I pay my vows:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> therefore I came forth to meet you, desiring your face; and I have found you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 19:9; Jezekiel 27:7; Kings III 10:28"> I have spread my bed with sheets, and I have covered it with double tapestry from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 45:8; Esias 57:7–9; Song of Songs 4:13–14"> I have sprinkled my couch with saffron, and my house with cinnamon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Come, and let us enjoy love until the morning; come, and let us embrace in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 20:11; Luke 12:45–46; Luke 12:39"> For my husband is not at home, but is gone on a long journey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:4"> having taken in his hand a bundle of money: after many days he will return to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 5:3; Proverbs 7:5; Kings IV 4:8"> So with much converse she prevailed on him to go astray, and with the snares of her lips forced him from the right path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 13:27; Acts 16:24; Acts 14:13"> And he followed her, being gently led on, and that as an ox is led to the slaughter, and as a dog to bonds, or as a hart shot in the liver with an arrow:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Proverbs 9:18; Proverbs 1:17"> and he hastens as a bird into a snare, not knowing that he is running for his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 5:7; Proverbs 4:1; 1 John 2:1"> Now then, my son, listen to me, and attend to the words of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 5:8; Esias 53:6; Proverbs 6:25"> Let not your heart turn aside to her ways<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. + 'and stray not in her paths.'">b</a></sup>:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Nehemiah 13:26; Kings III 11:1–2"> for she has wounded and cast down many, and those whom she has slain are innumerable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 5:5; Proverbs 9:18; Proverbs 2:18–19"> Her house is the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. ways.">c</a></sup>way of hell, leading down to the chambers of death.</span>
