<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 6:15; Kings I 2:25; Proverbs 1:24–31"> A reprover is better than a stiff-necked man: for when the latter is suddenly set on fire, there shall be no remedy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 11:10; Proverbs 28:28"> When the righteous are praised, the people will rejoice: but when the ungodly rule, men mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 27:11; Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 15:20"> When a man loves wisdom, his father rejoices: but he that keeps harlots will waste wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 29:14; Proverbs 20:8; Daniel 11:20"> A righteous king establishes a country: but a transgressor destroys it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Osee 5:1; Job 17:5; Psalms 5:9"> He that prepares a net in the way of his own friend, entangles his own feet in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:12; Exodus 15:1; 2 Timothy 2:26"> A great snare is spread for a sinner: but the righteous shall be in joy and gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 29:16; Psalms 41:1; Proverbs 21:13"> A righteous man knows how to judge for the poor: but the ungodly understands not knowledge; and the poor man has not an understanding mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 11:11; Jeremias 15:1; James 5:15–18"> Lawless men burn down a city: but wise men turn away wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 26:4; Ecclesiastes 10:13; Matthew 7:6"> A wise man shall judge nations: but a worthless man being angry laughs and fears not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 15:18–19; 1 John 3:12–13; Genesis 4:5–8"> Bloody men hate a holy person, but the upright will seek his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:11; Proverbs 12:16; Michaeas 7:5"> A fool utters all his mind: but the wise reserves his in part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 20:8; Kings IV 10:6–7; Psalms 52:2–4"> When a king hearkens to unjust language, all his subjects are transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:45; Proverbs 22:2; Psalms 13:3"> When the creditor and debtor meet together, the Lord oversees them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 16:12; Esias 11:4; Psalms 72:2–4"> When a king judges the poor in truth, his throne shall be established<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. 'for ever.' See Amos 1. 11; Mich 7. 18; in the Greek.">a</a></sup>for a testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 17:25; Proverbs 29:17"> Stripes and reproofs give wisdom: but an erring child disgraces his parents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:36; Psalms 91:8; Psalms 58:10"> When the ungodly abound, sins abound: but when they fall, the righteous are warned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 10:1; Proverbs 29:15; Proverbs 23:13–14"> Chasten your son, and he shall give you rest; and he shall give honor to your soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 1:25; Luke 11:28; John 13:17"> There shall be no interpreter to a sinful nation: but he that observes the law is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, 'most blessed.'">b</a></sup>blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 26:3; Job 19:16; Proverbs 30:22"> A stubborn servant will not be reproved by words: for even if he understands, still he will not obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 1:19; Proverbs 26:12; Proverbs 14:29"> If you see a man hasty in his words, know that the fool has hope rather than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He that lives wantonly from a child, shall be a servant, and in the end shall grieve over himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 17:19; Proverbs 26:21"> A furious man stirs up strife, and a passionate man digs up sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 23:12; Luke 14:11; Proverbs 11:2"> Pride brings a man low, but the Lord upholds the humble-minded with honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 5:1; Proverbs 1:11–19; Proverbs 8:36"> He that shares with a thief, hates his own soul: and if any having heard an oath uttered tell not of it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 118:8; Luke 12:4; Proverbs 18:10"> they fearing and reverencing men unreasonably have been overthrown, but he that trusts in the Lord shall rejoice. Ungodliness causes a man to stumble: but he that trusts in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Possibly, 'in the Lord.' See 2 Pet 2:1.">c</a></sup>his master shall be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 19:6; Proverbs 19:21; Psalms 20:9"> Many wait on the favor of rulers; but justice comes to a man from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 15:17–19; Psalms 139:21"> A righteous man is an abomination to an unrighteous man, and the direct way is an abomination to the sinner.</span>
