<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Proverbs 28:16; Esther 4:6"> As dew in harvest, and as rain in summer, so honor is not seemly for a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 23:8; Nehemiah 13:2; Psalms 109:28"> As birds and sparrows fly, so a curse shall not come upon any one without a cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 10:13; Psalms 32:9; 1 Corinthians 4:21"> As a whip for a horse, and a goad for an ass, so is a rod for a simple nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 26:5; 1 Peter 3:9; Jude 1:9"> Answer not a fool according to his folly, lest you become like him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 12:16; Matthew 16:1–4; Proverbs 28:11"> Yet answer a fool according to his folly, lest he seem wise in his own conceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 10:26; Proverbs 25:13; Proverbs 13:17"> He that sends a message by a foolish messenger procures for himself a reproach from his own ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 26:9; Psalms 64:8; Proverbs 17:7"> As well take away the motion of the legs, as transgression from the mouth of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:1; Proverbs 30:22; Proverbs 19:10"> He that binds up a stone in a sling, is like one that gives glory to a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 23:35"> Thorns<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Compare Heb.">a</a></sup>grow in the hand of a drunkard, and servitude in the hand of fools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 2:6; Proverbs 11:31"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Great variation from Hebrew here.">b</a></sup>All the flesh of fools endures much hardship; for their fury is brought to nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:22; Matthew 12:45; Exodus 8:15"> As when a dog goes to his own vomit, and becomes abominable, so is fool who returns in his wickedness to his own sin. &#91;There is a shame that brings sin: and there is a shame that is glory and grace.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 29:20; 1 Corinthians 3:18–19; Proverbs 26:5"> I have seen a man who seemed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. by.">c</a></sup>to himself to be wise; but a fool had more hope than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 22:13; Proverbs 19:15; Proverbs 15:19"> A sluggard when sent on a journey says, There is a lion in the ways, and there are murderers in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 6:12; Proverbs 12:27; Proverbs 12:24"> As a door turns on the hinge, so does a sluggard on his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 19:24"> A sluggard having hid his hand in his bosom, will not be able to bring it up to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 26:12; 1 Peter 3:15; Proverbs 12:15"> A sluggard seems to himself wiser than one who<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Compare Heb.">d</a></sup>most satisfactorily brings back a message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:3; 2 Timothy 2:23–24; Luke 12:14"> As he that lays hold of a dog's tail, so is he that makes himself the champion of another's cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 25:18; Esias 50:11; Proverbs 7:23"> As those who need correction put forth fair words to men, and he that first falls in with the proposal will be overthrown;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 5:4; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 15:21"> so are all that lay wait for their own friends, and when they are revealed, say, I did it in jest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 22:10; James 3:6; Proverbs 16:28"> With much wood fire increases; but where there is not a double-minded man, strife ceases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Proverbs 29:22; Kings II 20:1"> A hearth for coals, and wood for fire; and railing man for the tumult of strife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 18:8; Proverbs 20:19; Jezekiel 22:9"> The words of cunning knaves are soft; but they strike even to the inmost parts of the bowels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:47–48; Luke 11:39; Proverbs 10:18"> Silver dishonestly given is to be considered as a potsherd: smooth lips cover a grievous heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 12:20; Proverbs 10:18; Psalms 41:6"> A weeping enemy promises all things with his lips, but in his heart he contrives deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 6:16–19; Jeremias 12:6; Psalms 28:3"> Though your enemy entreat you with a loud voice, consent not: for there are seven abominations in his heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 55:21–23; Kings I 18:17; Kings I 18:21"> He that hides enmity frames deceit: but being easily discerned, exposes his own sins in the public assemblies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 28:10; Psalms 9:15; Psalms 7:15–16"> He that digs a pit for his neighbor shall fall into it: and he that rolls a stone, rolls it upon himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:44–49; Luke 20:20–21; John 10:32–33"> A lying tongue hates the truth; and an unguarded mouth causes tumults.</span>
