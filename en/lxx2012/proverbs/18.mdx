<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 5:15–17; Proverbs 2:1–6; Proverbs 20:3"> A man who wishes to separate from friends seeks excuses; but at all times he will be liable to reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 1:7; Proverbs 12:23; Psalms 1:1–2"> A senseless man feels no need of wisdom, for he is rather led by folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 22:10; 1 Peter 4:14; Proverbs 11:2"> When an ungodly man comes into a depth of evils, he despises them; but dishonor and reproach come upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 20:5; Proverbs 10:11; Colossians 4:6"> A word in the heart of a man is a deep water, and a river and fountain of life spring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 19:15; Deuteronomy 16:19; Proverbs 28:21"> It is not good to accept the person of the ungodly, nor is it holy to pervert justice in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 27:3; Proverbs 16:27–28; Proverbs 22:24–25"> The lips of a fool bring him into troubles, and his bold mouth calls for death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 13:3; Proverbs 12:13; Proverbs 10:14"> A fool's mouth is ruin to him, and his lips are a snare to his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 12:18; Leviticus 19:16; Proverbs 16:28"> Fear casts down the slothful; and the souls of the effeminate shall hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 10:4; Hebrews 6:12; Proverbs 24:30–34"> A man who helps not himself by his labor is brother of him that ruins himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings II 22:3; Psalms 91:2; Esias 26:4"> The name of the Lord is of great strength; and the righteous<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. having run.">a</a></sup>running to it are exalted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 10:15; Psalms 52:5–7; Ecclesiastes 7:12"> The wealth of a rich man is a strong city; and its glory casts a broad shadow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 15:33; 1 Peter 5:5; Jezekiel 28:2"> Before ruin a man's heart is exalted, and before honor it is humble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 7:51; Deuteronomy 13:14; Proverbs 20:25"> Whoso answers a word before he hears a cause, it is folly and reproach to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 15:13; Proverbs 17:22; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> A wise servant calms a man's anger; but who can endure a faint-hearted man?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 1:5; Proverbs 15:14; Proverbs 1:5"> The heart of the sensible man purchases discretion; and the ears of the wise seek understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 19:6; Genesis 43:11; Proverbs 17:8"> A man's gift<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See Ps 188. (119) 32.">b</a></sup>enlarges him, and seats him among princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Acts 24:12–13; Kings II 19:24–27"> A righteous man accuses himself at the beginning of his speech, but<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Comp. Mark 14. ult. and margin, with 2 Tim 4. 14-17.">c</a></sup>when he has entered upon the attack, the adversary is reproved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Nehemiah 11:1; Chronicles I 24:31"> A silent man quells strifes, and determines between great powers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 16:32; Acts 15:39; Proverbs 6:19"> A brother helped by a brother is as a strong and high city; and is as strong as a well-founded palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 12:13–14; Proverbs 25:11–12; Proverbs 13:2"> A man fills his belly with the fruits of his mouth; and he shall be satisfied with the fruits of his lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Matthew 12:35–37; Colossians 4:6"> Life and death are in the power of the tongue; and they that rule it shall eat the fruits thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Proverbs 31:10–31; Proverbs 12:4"> He that has found a good wife has found favours, and has received gladness from God.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Heb. omits this verse.">d</a></sup>He that puts away a good wife, puts away a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. plural.">e</a></sup>good thing, and he that keeps an adulteress is foolish and ungodly.</span>
