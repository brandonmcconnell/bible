<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Psalms 34:11; Proverbs 1:8"> Hear, you⌃ children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:6; Deuteronomy 32:2; Titus 1:9"> For I give you a good gift; forsake you⌃ not my law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:5; Chronicles I 29:1; Romans 12:16"> For I also was a son obedient to my father, and loved in the sight of my mother:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 3:1; Ephesians 6:4; John 12:50"> who spoke and instructed me, saying, Let our speech be fixed in your heart, keep our commandments, forget them not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="James 1:5; Proverbs 3:13–18; Proverbs 1:22–23"> and do not neglect the speech of my mouth.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alexandrian codex has: “Get wisdom, get understanding, forget not, nor decline from the words of my mouth.”">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10; Proverbs 4:21–22; Ephesians 3:17"> And forsake it not, and it shall cleave to you: love it, and it shall keep you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; Kings I 2:30; Proverbs 3:35"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. dig a trench about her, see Heb.">b</a></sup>Secure it, and it shall exalt you: honor it, that it may embrace you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 1:9; 1 Peter 5:4; Hebrews 2:7–9"> that it may give to your head a crown of graces, and may cover you with a crown of delight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; Proverbs 3:2; Jeremias 9:20"> Hear, my son, and receive my words; and the years of your life shall be increased, that the resources of your life may be many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 25:4–5; Ecclesiastes 12:9; Proverbs 8:9"> For I teach you the ways of wisdom; and I cause you to go in right<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. wheel-tracks, see chap 2:18; Heb 12:13.">c</a></sup>paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 3:23; Psalms 18:36; 1 Peter 2:8"> For when you go, your steps shall not be straitened; and when you run, you shall not be distressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Proverbs 3:18; Proverbs 23:23"> Take hold of my instruction; let it not go, —but keep it for yourself for your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 1:1; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Proverbs 1:10"> Go not in the ways of the ungodly, neither covet the ways of transgressors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Job 22:23; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> In whatever place they shall pitch their camp, go not there; but turn from them, and pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 36:4; Michaeas 2:1; Esias 57:20"> For they can’t sleep, unless they have done evil: their sleep is taken away, and they rest not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:4–5; Psalms 14:4; Michaeas 6:12"> For these live upon the bread of ungodliness, and are drunken with wine of transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:12; Job 11:17; Daniel 12:3"> But the ways of the righteous shine like light; they go on and shine, until the day<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. order itself aright.">d</a></sup>be fully come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 12:35; Esias 59:9–10; Job 18:5–6"> But the ways of the ungodly are dark; they know not how they stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 5:1; Esias 55:3; Proverbs 7:1"> My son, attend to my speech; and apply your ear to my words:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 3:21; Proverbs 3:3; Proverbs 7:1–2"> that your fountains may not fail you; keep them in your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 4:10; Proverbs 12:18; Jeremias 33:6"> For they are life to those that find them, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, healing.">e</a></sup>health to all their flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Matthew 12:34–35; Mark 7:21–23"> Keep your heart with the utmost care; for out of these are the issues of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 1:26; Proverbs 8:8; Ephesians 4:25–31"> Remove from you a froward mouth, and put far away from you unjust lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 119:37; Matthew 6:22; Job 31:1"> Let your eyes look right on, and let your eyelids assent to just things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:13; Psalms 37:23; 2 Thessalonians 3:3"> Make straight paths for your feet, and order your ways aright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Joshua 1:7; Deuteronomy 28:14"> Turn not aside to the right hand nor to the left, but turn away your foot from an evil way:<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Heb. omits.">f</a></sup>for God knows the ways on the right hand, but those on the left are crooked: and he will make your ways straight, and will guide your steps in peace.</span>
