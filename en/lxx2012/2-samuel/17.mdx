<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 59:7–8; Psalms 109:2–4; Psalms 3:3–5"> And Achitophel said to Abessalom, Let me now choose out for myself twelve thousand men, and I will arise and follow after David this night:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings II 16:14; Kings III 22:31; Zacharias 13:7"> and I will come upon him when he is weary and weak-handed, and I will strike him with terror; and all the people with him shall flee, and I will strike the king only of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Esias 48:22; Esias 57:21"> And I will bring back all the people to you, as a bride returns to her husband: only you seek the life of one man, and all the people shall have peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 18:20–21; Romans 1:32; Chronicles II 30:4"> And the saying was right in the eyes of Abessalom, and in the eyes of all the elders of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 15:32–37; Kings II 16:16–19"> And Abessalom said, Call now also Chusi the Arachite, and let us hear what is in his mouth, even in his also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And Chusi went in to Abessalom, and Abessalom spoke to him, saying, After this manner spoke Achitophel: shall we do according to his word? but if not, do you speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 31:8"> And Chusi said to Abessalom, This counsel which Achitophel has counselled this one time is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Osee 13:8; Kings I 16:18; Hebrews 11:32–34"> And Chusi said, You know your father and his men, that they are very mighty, and bitter in their spirit, as a bereaved bear in the field,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. omit the wods in italics.">a</a></sup>and as a wild boar in the plain: and your father is a man of war, and will not give the people rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 24:3; Kings I 14:14–15; Judges 20:32–33"> For, behold, he is now hidden in one of the hills or in some other place: and it shall come to pass when he falls upon them at the beginning, that some one will certainly hear, and say, There has been a slaughter among the people that follow after Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 2:9–11; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 49:9"> Then even he that is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. a son of strength.">b</a></sup>strong, whose heart is as the heart of a lion, —it shall utterly melt: for all Israel knows that your father is mighty, and they that are with him are mighty men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Judges 20:1; Psalms 9:16"> For thus I have surely given counsel, that all Israel be generally gathered to you from Dan even to Bersabee, as the sand that is upon the sea-shore for multitude: and that your presence<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. going.">c</a></sup>go in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings I 23:23; Obdias 1:3; Kings III 20:10"> And we will come upon him in one of the places where we shall find him, and we will encamp against him, as the dew falls upon the earth; and we will not leave of him and of his men so much as one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Michaeas 1:6"> And if he shall have taken refuge with his army in a city, then shall all Israel take ropes to that city, and we will draw it even into the river, that there may not be left there even a stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 15:34; 1 Corinthians 1:19–20; Proverbs 19:21"> And Abessalom, and all the men of Israel said, The counsel of Chusi the Arachite is better than the counsel of Achitophel. For the Lord ordained to disconcert the good counsel of Achitophel, that the Lord might bring all evil upon Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings II 15:35–36"> And Chusi the Arachite said to Sadoc and Abiathar the priests, Thus and thus Achitophel counselled Abessalom and the elders of Israel; and thus and thus have I counselled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings II 15:28; 1 Corinthians 15:54; 2 Corinthians 5:4"> And now send quickly and report to David, saying, Lodge not this night in Araboth of the wilderness: even go and make haste, lest one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Alex. καταπίῃ.">d</a></sup>swallow up the king, and all the people with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 15:36; Joshua 18:16; Kings II 15:27"> And Jonathan and Achimaas stood by the well of Rogel, and a maidservant went and reported to them, and they go and tell king David; for they might not be seen to enter into the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings II 3:16; Kings II 16:5; Kings II 19:16"> But a young man saw them and told Abessalom: and the two went quickly, and entered into the house of a man in Baurim; and he had a well in his court, and they went down into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 1:19; Joshua 2:4–24"> And a woman took a covering, and spread it over the mouth of the well, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="See Num 11:32; Jerem 8:2.">e</a></sup>spread out ground corn upon it to dry, and the thing was not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 1:19; Joshua 2:22–23; Kings I 19:12–17"> And the servants of Abessalom came to the woman into the house, and said, Where are Achimaas and Jonathan? and the woman said to them, They are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, lately gone over.">f</a></sup>gone a little way beyond<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, the small streams.">g</a></sup>the water. And they sought and found them not, and returned to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings II 17:15–16"> And it came to pass after they were gone, that they came up out of the pit, and went on their way; and reported to king David, and said to David, Arise you⌃ and go quickly over the water, for thus has Achitophel counselled concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 10:16; John 18:9; Proverbs 27:12"> And David rose up and all the people with him, and they passed over Jordan till the morning light; there was not one missing who did not pass over Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:5; Kings IV 20:1; Kings II 15:12"> And Achitophel saw that his counsel was not followed, and he saddled his ass, and rose and departed to his house into his city; and he gave orders to his household, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Comp. the Greek with Matt 27:5.">h</a></sup>hanged himself, and died, and was buried in the sepulchre of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 32:2; Kings II 2:8; Joshua 13:26"> And David passed over to Manaim: and Abessalom crossed over Jordan, he and all the men of Israel with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Chronicles I 2:13; Kings II 19:13; Kings II 20:9–12"> And Abessalom appointed Amessai in the room of Joab over the host. And Amessai was the son of a man<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. and his name, etc.">i</a></sup>whose name was Jether of Jezrael: he went in to Abigaia the daughter of Naas, the sister of Saruia the mother of Joab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Deuteronomy 3:15; Numbers 32:1–42"> And all Israel and Abessalom encamped in the land of Galaad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings III 2:7; Kings II 10:1–2; Kings II 9:4"> And it came to pass when David came to Manaim, that Uesbi the son of Naas of Rabbath of the sons of Ammon, and Machir son of Amiel of Lodabar, and Berzelli the Galaadite of Rogellim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings I 25:18; Obdias 1:3; Kings II 16:1–2"> brought ten embroidered beds, (with double coverings,) and ten caldrons, and earthenware, and wheat, and barley, and flour, and meal, and beans, and pulse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings II 16:2; Esias 21:14; Kings II 16:14"> and honey, and butter, and sheep, and cheeses of kine: and they brought them to David and to his people with him to eat; for one said, The people is faint and hungry and thirsty in the wilderness.</span>
