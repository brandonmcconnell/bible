<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings II 13:39; Kings II 2:18; Kings II 18:33"> And Joab the son of Saruia knew that the heart of the king was toward Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:6; Amos 1:1; Ruth 3:3"> And Joab sent to Thecoe, and took thence a cunning woman, and said to her, Mourn, I pray you, and put on mourning apparel, and anoint you not with oil, and you shall be as a woman mourning for one that is dead thus for many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 14:19; Exodus 4:15; Esias 51:16"> And you shall go to the king, and speak to him according to this word. And Joab put the words in her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings II 1:2; Kings IV 6:26–28; Kings I 25:23"> So the woman of Thecoe went in to the king and fell upon her face to the earth, and did him obeisance, and said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, save.">a</a></sup>Help, O king, help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 9:8–15; Kings II 12:1–3"> And the king said to her, What is the matter with you? And she said, I am indeed a widow woman, and my husband is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 4:8; Deuteronomy 22:26–27; Exodus 2:13"> And moreover your handmaid had two sons, and they fought<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. both.">b</a></sup>together in the field, and there was no one to part them; and the one struck the other his brother, and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 35:19; Matthew 21:38; Kings II 12:17"> And behold the whole family rose up against your handmaid, and they said, Give up the one that struck his brother, and we will put him to death for the life of his brother, whom he killed, and we will take away even your heir: so they will quench my coal that is left, so as not to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. place.">c</a></sup>leave my husband remnant or name on the face of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 18:13; Esias 11:3–4; Job 29:16"> And the king said to the woman, Go<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. in health.">d</a></sup>in peace to your house, and I will give commandment concerning you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 25:24; Kings III 2:33; Matthew 27:25"> And the woman of Thecoe said to the king, On me, my lord, O king, and on my father's house be the iniquity, and the king and his throne be guiltless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And the king said, Who was it that spoke to you? you shall even bring him to me, and one shall not touch<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="q.d. your son.">e</a></sup>him any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 14:45; Matthew 10:30; Numbers 35:19"> And she said, Let now the king remember concerning his Lord God in that the avenger of blood is multiplied to destroy, and let them not take away my son. And he said, As the lord lives, not a hair of your son shall fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 18:27; Kings I 25:24; Acts 26:1"> And the woman said, Let now your servant speak a word to my lord the king. And he said, Say on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings II 13:37–38; Kings II 12:7; Judges 20:2"> And the woman said, Why have you devised this thing against the people of God? or is this word out of the king's mouth as a transgression, so that the king should not bring back his banished?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 30:23; Numbers 35:28; Hebrews 9:27"> For we shall surely die, and be as water poured upon the earth, which shall not be gathered up, and God shall take the life, even as he devises to thrust forth from him his outcast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And now whereas I came to speak this word to my lord the king, the reason is that the people will see me, and your handmaid will say, Let one now speak to my lord the king, if perhaps the king will perform the request of his handmaid;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 26:19"> for the king will hear. Let him rescue his handmaid out of the hand of the man that seeks to cast out me and my son from the inheritance of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings I 29:9; Kings II 19:27; Kings II 14:20"> And the woman said, If now the word of my lord the king be gracious, —well: for as an angel of God, so is my lord the king, to hear good and evil: and the Lord your God shall be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 38:25; Kings I 3:17–18; Jeremias 38:14"> And the king answered, and said to the woman, Hide not from me, I pray you, the matter which I ask you. And the woman said, Let my lord the king by all means speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings II 14:3; Kings I 1:26; Kings I 20:3"> And the king said, Is not the hand of Joab in all this matter with you? and the woman said to the king, As your soul lives, my lord, O king,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. If there is, etc.">f</a></sup>there is no turning to the right hand or to the left from all that my lord the king has spoken; for your servant Joab himself charged me, and he put all these words in the mouth of your handmaid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings II 14:17; Kings II 19:27; Job 32:21–22"> In order that this form of speech might come about it was that your servant Joab has framed this matter: and my lord is wise as is the wisdom of an angel of God, to know all things that are in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings II 14:11; Kings I 14:39; Mark 6:26"> And the king said to Joab, Behold now, I have done to you according to this your word: go, bring back the young man Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ruth 2:2; Job 31:20; Job 29:11"> And Joab fell on his face to the ground, and did obeisance, and blessed the king: and Joab said, To-day your servant knows that I have found grace in your sight, my lord, O king, for my lord the king has performed the request of his servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings II 3:3; Kings II 13:37–38"> And Joab arose, and went to Gedsur, and brought Abessalom to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 22:4; Genesis 43:3; Kings II 3:13"> And the king said, Let him return to his house, and not see my face. And Abessalom returned to his house, and saw not the king's face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 1:6; Deuteronomy 28:35; Job 2:7"> And there was not a man in Israel so<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. praised.">g</a></sup>very comely as Abessalom: from the sole of his foot even to the crown of his head there was no blemish in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 44:20; 1 Corinthians 11:14; Kings II 18:9"> And when he polled his head, (and it was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. from the beginning of days to days. Hebraism.">h</a></sup>at the beginning of every year that he polled it, because it grew, heavy upon him,) even when he polled it, he weighed the hair of his head, two hundred shekels according to the royal shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings II 18:18; Kings II 13:1; Esias 14:22"> And there were born to Abessalom three sons and one daughter, and her name was Themar: she was a very beautiful woman, and she becomes the wife of Roboam son of Solomon, and she bears to him Abia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And Abessalom remained in Jerusalem two<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. years of days.">i</a></sup>full years, and he saw not the king's face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings II 14:30–31; Esther 1:12; Matthew 22:3"> And Abessalom sent to Joab to bring him in to the king, and he would not come to him: and he sent to him the second time, and he would not come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 15:4–5; Kings IV 10:6–7; Kings IV 9:33"> And Abessalom said to his servants, Behold, Joab's portion in the field is next to mine, and he has in it barley; go and set it on fire. And the servants of Abessalom set the field on fire: and the servants of Joab come to him with their clothes torn, and they said to him, The servants of Abessalom have set the field on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Joab arose, and came to Abessalom into the house, and said to him, Why have your servants set my field on fire?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings I 20:8; Exodus 14:12; Proverbs 28:13"> And Abessalom said to Joab, Behold, I sent to you, saying, Come hither, and I will send you to the king, saying, Why did I come out of Gedsur? it would have been better for me to have remained there: and now, behold, I have not seen the face of the king; but if there is iniquity in me, then put me to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 33:4; Luke 15:20; Genesis 27:26"> And Joab went in to the king, and brought him word: and he called Abessalom, and he went in to the king, and did him obeisance, and fell upon his face to the ground, even in the presence of the king; and the king kissed Abessalom.</span>
