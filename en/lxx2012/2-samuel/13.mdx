<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:9; Kings II 3:2–3; Kings II 13:15"> And it happened after this that Abessalom the son of David had a very beautiful sister, and her name was Themar; and Amnon the son of David loved her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Kings III 21:4; Song of Songs 5:8"> And Amnon was distressed even to sickness, because of Themar his sister; for she was a virgin, and it seemed very difficult for Amnon to do anything to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings I 16:9; 1 Corinthians 3:19; James 3:15"> And Amnon had a friend, and his name was Jonadab, the son of Samaa the brother of David: and Jonadab was a very cunning man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Esther 5:13–14"> And he said to him, What ails you that you are thus weak? O son of the king, morning by morning?<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. do.">a</a></sup>will you not tell me? and Ammon said, I love Themar the sister of my brother Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 6:24–25; Proverbs 19:27; Acts 23:15"> And Jonadab said to him, Lie upon your bed, and make yourself sick, and your father shall come in to see you; and you shall say to him, Let, I pray you, Themar my sister come, and feed me with morsels, and let her prepare food before my eyes, that I may see and eat at her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 18:6; Matthew 13:33"> So Ammon lay down, and made himself sick; and the king came in to see him: and Amnon said to the king, Let, I pray you, my sister Themar come to me, and make a couple of cakes in my sight, and I will eat them at her hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And David sent to Themar to the house, saying, Go now to your brother's house, and dress him food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Themar went to the house of her brother Amnon, and he was lying down: and she took the dough and kneaded it, and made cakes in his sight, and baked the cakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 45:1; John 3:20; Judges 3:19"> And she took the frying pan and poured them out before him, but he would not eat. And Amnon said, Send out every man from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. above.">b</a></sup>about me. And they removed every man from about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Amnon said to Themar, Bring in the food into the closet, and I will eat of your hand. And Themar took the cakes which she had made, and brought them to her brother Amnon into the chamber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:11–12"> And she brought them to him to eat, and he caught hold of her, and said to her, Come, lie with me, my sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:17; Judges 20:6"> And she said to him, Nay, my brother, do not humble me, for it<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. will not. q.d. non est fasciendum.">c</a></sup>ought not to be so done in Israel; do not this folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 20:12; Genesis 19:8"> And I, whither shall I remove my reproach? and you shall be as one of the fools in Israel. And now, speak, I pray you, to the king, for surely he will not keep me from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 12:11; Esther 7:8; Judges 20:5"> But Amnon would not listen to her voice; and he prevailed against her, and humbled her, and lay with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:17"> Then Amnon hated her with very great hatred; for the hatred with which he hated her was greater than the love with which he had loved her, for the last wickedness was greater than the first: and Amnon said to her, Rise, and be gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Themar spoke to him concerning this great mischief, greater, said she, than the other that you did me, to send me away: but Amnon would not listen to her voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he called his servant who had charge of the house, and said to him, Put now this woman out from me, and shut the door after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 5:30; Genesis 37:3; Psalms 45:13–14"> And she had on her a variegated robe, for so were the king's daughters that were virgins attired in their apparel: and his servant led her forth, and shut the door after her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:6; Jeremias 2:37; Kings II 1:11"> And Themar took ashes, and put them on her head; and she tore the variegated garment that was upon her: and she laid her hands on her head, and went crying continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 12:19; Genesis 46:15; Genesis 34:2"> And Abessalom her brother said to her, Has your brother Amnon been with you? now then, my sister, be silent, for he is your brother: be not careful to mention this matter. So Themar lived as a widow in the house of her brother Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:7; Kings II 3:28–29; Kings I 2:29"> And king David heard of all these things, and was very angry; but he did not grieve the spirit of his son Amnon, because be loved him, for he was his firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17–18; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 24:50"> And Abessalom spoke not to Amnon, good or bad, because Abessalom hated Amnon, on account of his humbling his sister Themar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 38:12–13; Kings I 25:4; Chronicles II 26:10"> And it came to pass at the end of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="q.d. a two-year of days.">d</a></sup>two whole years, that they were shearing sheep for Abessalom in Belasor near Ephraim: and Abessalom invited all the king's sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings II 11:8–15; Jeremias 41:6–7; Psalms 55:21"> And Abessalom came to the king, and said, Behold,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. they are shearing for your servant.">e</a></sup>your servant has a sheep-shearing; let now the king and his servants go with your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 14:23; Judges 19:7–10; Kings II 14:22"> And the king said to Abessalom, Nay, my son, let us not all go, and let us not be burdensome to you. And he pressed him; but he would not go, but blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Kings II 20:9; Kings II 3:27; Kings II 11:13–15"> And Abessalom said to him, And if not, let I pray you, my brother Amnon go with us. And the king said to him, Why should he go with you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 26:24–26"> And Abessalom pressed him, and he sent with him Amnon and all the king's sons; and Abessalom made a banquet like the banquet of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 19:6; Judges 19:22; Judges 19:9"> And Abessalom charged his servants, saying, Mark when the heart of Amnon shall be merry with wine, and I shall say to you, Strike Amnon, and kill him: fear not; for is it not I that command you? Be courageous,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. and become sons of strength.">f</a></sup>and be valiant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings II 18:9; Kings III 1:33; Kings IV 1:9–12"> And the servants of Abessalom did to Amnon as Abessalom commanded them: and all the sons of the king rose up, and they mounted every man his mule, and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And it came to pass, when they were in the way, that a report came to David, saying, Abessalom has slain all the king's sons, and there is not one of them left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings II 1:11; Kings II 12:16; Job 1:20"> Then the king arose, and tore his garments, and lay upon the ground: and all his servants that were standing round him tore their garments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings I 16:9; Kings II 13:3; Genesis 27:41"> And Jonadab the son of Samaa brother of David, answered and said, Let not my Lord the king say that he has slain all the young men the sons of the king, for Amnon only of them all is dead; for he was appointed to death by the mouth of Abessalom from the day that he humbled his sister Themar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings II 19:19"> And now let not my lord the king take the matter to heart, saying, All the king's sons are dead: for Amnon only of them is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 4:8–14; Proverbs 28:17; Amos 5:19"> And Abessalom escaped: and the young man the watchman, lifted up his eyes, and looked; and, behold, much people went in the way behind him from the side of the mountain in the descent: and the watchman came and told the king, and said, I have seen men by the way of Oronen, by the side of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And Jonadab said to the king, Behold, the king's sons are present: according to the word of your servant, so has it happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings II 18:33; Kings II 12:21; Kings II 13:15"> And it came to pass when he had finished speaking, that, behold, the king's sons came, and lifted up their voices and wept: and the king also and all his servants wept with a very great weeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Kings II 3:3; Kings II 14:32; Kings II 13:34"> But Abessalom fled, and went to Tholmi son of Emiud the king of Gedsur to the land of Chamaachad: and king David mourned for his son continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Kings II 15:8; Kings II 14:23; Kings II 14:32"> So Abessalom fled, and departed to Gedsur, and was there three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 38:12; Genesis 37:35"> And king David ceased to go out after Abessalom, for he was comforted concerning Amnon, touching his death.</span>
