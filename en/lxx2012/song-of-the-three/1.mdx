<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Azarias stood up, and prayed on this manner; and opening his mouth in the midst of the fire said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Blessed are you, O Lord God of our fathers: your name is worthy to be praised and glorified for evermore:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For you are righteous in all the things that you have done to us: yes, true are all your works, your ways are right, and all your judgments truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> In all the things that you have brought upon us, and upon the holy city of our fathers, even Jerusalem, you have executed true judgment: for according to truth and judgment did you bring all these things upon us because of our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For we have sinned and committed iniquity, departing from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> In all things have we trespassed, and not obeyed your commandments, nor kept them, neither done as you have commanded us, that it might go well with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Therefore all that you have brought upon us, and every thing that you have done to us, you have done in true judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And you did deliver us into the hands of lawless enemies, most hateful forsakers of God, and to an unjust king, and the most wicked in all the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And now we can’t open our mouths, we are become a shame and reproach to your servants; and to them that worship you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Yet deliver us not up wholly, for your name's sake, neither disannul you your covenant:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And cause not your mercy to depart from us, for your beloved Abraham's sake, for your servant Isaac's sake, and for your holy Israel's sake;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> To whom you have spoken and promised, that you would multiply their seed as the stars of heaven, and as the sand that lies upon the seashore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For we, O Lord, are become less than any nation, and be kept under this day in all the world because of our sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Neither is there at this time prince, or prophet, or leader, or burnt offering, or sacrifice, or oblation, or incense, or place to sacrifice before you, and to find mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Nevertheless in a contrite heart and an humble spirit let us be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Like as in the burnt offerings of rams and bullocks, and like as in ten thousands of fat lambs: so let our sacrifice be in your sight this day, and grant that we may wholly go after you: for they shall not be confounded that put their trust in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And now we follow you with all our heart, we fear you, and seek your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Put us not to shame: but deal with us after your lovingkindness, and according to the multitude of your mercies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Deliver us also according to your marvelous works, and give glory to your name, O Lord: and let all them that do your servants hurt be ashamed;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And let them be confounded in all their power and might, and let their strength be broken;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And let them know that you are God, the only God, and glorious over the whole world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the king's servants, that put them in, ceased not to make the oven hot with rosin, pitch, tow, and small wood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> So that the flame streamed forth above the furnace forty and nine cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And it passed through, and burned those Chaldeans it found about the furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But the angel of the Lord came down into the oven together with Azarias and his fellows, and struck the flame of the fire out of the oven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And made the midst of the furnace as it had been a moist whistling wind, so that the fire touched them not at all, neither hurt nor troubled them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then the three, as out of one mouth, praised, glorified, and blessed, God in the furnace, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Blessed are you, O Lord God of our fathers: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And blessed is your glorious and holy name: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Blessed are you in the temple of your holy glory: and to be praised and glorified above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Blessed are you that behold the depths, and sit upon the cherubims: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Blessed are you on the glorious throne of your kingdom: and to be praised and glorified above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Blessed are you in the firmament of heaven: and above all to be praised and glorified for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> O all you⌃ works of the Lord, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> O you⌃ heavens, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> O you⌃ angels of the Lord, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> O all you⌃ waters that be above the heaven, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> O all you⌃ powers of the Lord, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> O you⌃ sun and moon, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> O you⌃ stars of heaven, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> O every shower and dew, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> O all you⌃ winds, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> O you⌃ fire and heat, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> O you⌃ winter and summer, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> O you⌃ dews and storms of snow, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> O you⌃ nights and days, bless you⌃ the Lord: bless and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> O you⌃ light and darkness, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> O you⌃ ice and cold, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> O you⌃ frost and snow, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> O you⌃ lightnings and clouds, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> O let the earth bless the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> O you⌃ mountains and little hills, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> O all you⌃ things that grow in the earth, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> O you⌃ mountains, bless you⌃ the Lord: Praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> O you⌃ seas and rivers, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> O you⌃ whales, and all that move in the waters, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> O all you⌃ fowls of the air, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> O all you⌃ beasts and cattle, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> O you⌃ children of men, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> O Israel, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> O you⌃ priests of the Lord, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> O you⌃ servants of the Lord, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> O you⌃ spirits and souls of the righteous, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> O you⌃ holy and humble men of heart, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65"> O Ananias, Azarias, and Misael, bless you⌃ the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever: for he has delivered us from hell, and saved us from the hand of death, and delivered us out of the midst of the furnace and burning flame: even out of the midst of the fire has he delivered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66"> O give thanks to the Lord, because he is gracious: for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67"> O all you⌃ that worship the Lord, bless the God of gods, praise him, and give him thanks: for his mercy endures for ever.</span>
