<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Esther 3:15"> So the king and Aman went in to drink with the queen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 5:3; John 16:24; Esther 5:6"> And the king said to Esther at the banquet on the second day, What is it, queen Esther? and what is your request, and what is your petition? and it shall be done for you, to the half of my kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 38:26; Psalms 122:6–9; Esther 7:7"> And she answered and said, If I have found favor in the sight of the king, let my life be granted to my petition, and my people to my request.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 3:9; Deuteronomy 28:68; Esther 3:13"> For both I and my people are sold for destruction, and pillage, and slavery; both we and our children for bondmen and bondwomen: and I consented not to it, for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="see Heb. slanderer">a</a></sup>is not worthy of the king's palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:3; Genesis 27:33; Job 9:24"> And the king said, Who is this that has dared to do this thing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 18:5–12; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Psalms 139:19–22"> And Esther said, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. hostile man.">b</a></sup>adversary is Aman, this wicked man. Then Aman was troubled before the king and the queen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 1:12; Proverbs 19:12; Revelation 3:9"> And the king rose up from the banquet to go into the garden: and Aman began to entreat the queen; for he saw that he was in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. evils.">c</a></sup>an evil case.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 1:6; Esther 6:12; Job 9:24"> And the king returned from the garden; and Aman had fallen upon the bed, intreating the queen. And the king said, Will you even force my wife in my house? And when Aman heard it, he changed countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 7:15–16; Proverbs 11:5–6; Esther 1:10"> And Bugathan, one of the chamberlains, said to the king, Behold, Aman has also prepared a gallows for Mardochaeus, who spoke concerning the king, and a gallows of fifty cubits high has been set up in the premises of Aman. And the king said, Let him be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, impaled.">d</a></sup>hanged thereon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 7:16; Daniel 6:24; Jezekiel 5:13"> So Aman was hanged on the gallows that had been prepared for Mardochaeus: and then the king's wrath was appeased.</span>
