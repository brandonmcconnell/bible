<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="James 1:19; Hebrews 12:28–29; Proverbs 15:8"> Be not hasty with your mouth, and let not your heart be swift to utter anything before God; for God is in heaven above, and you upon earth: therefore let your words be few.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 6:7; Proverbs 10:19; Esias 55:9"> For through the multitude of trial a dream comes; and a fool's voice is with a multitude of words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Proverbs 10:19; Ecclesiastes 10:12–14"> Whenever you shall vow a vow to God, defer not to pay it; for he has no pleasure in fools: pay you therefore whatever you shall have vowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 30:2; Deuteronomy 23:21–23; Psalms 76:11"> It is better that you should not vow, than that you should vow and not pay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:4; Deuteronomy 23:22; Proverbs 20:25"> Suffer not your mouth to lead your flesh to sin; and say not in the presence of God, It was an error: lest God be angry at your voice, and destroy the works of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:26; 2 John 1:8; James 3:2"> For there is evil in a multitude of dreams and vanities and many words: but fear you God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:13; Matthew 12:36; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> If you should see the oppression of the poor, and the wresting of judgment and of justice in the land, wonder not at the matter: for there is a high one to watch over him that is high, and high ones over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 12:5; Ecclesiastes 4:1; Ecclesiastes 3:16"> Also the abundance of the earth is for every one: the king is dependent on the tilled field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 4:7–23; Psalms 115:16; Genesis 1:29–30"> He that loves silver shall not be satisfied with silver: and who has loved gain, in the abundance thereof? this is also vanity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:10; Matthew 6:24; Luke 12:15"> In the multitude of good they are increased that eat it: and what virtue has the owner, but the right of beholding it with his eyes?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Ambacum 2:13; Psalms 119:36–37"> The sleep of a servant is sweet, whether he eat little or much: but to one who is satiated with wealth, there is none that suffers him to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 3:24; Psalms 4:8; Jeremias 31:26"> There is an infirmity which I have seen under the sun, namely, wealth kept for its owner to his hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:1–2; Luke 18:22–23; Genesis 19:14"> And that wealth shall perish in an evil trouble: and the man begets a son, and there is nothing in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–20; Aggaeus 2:16–17; Proverbs 23:5"> As he came forth naked from his mother's womb, he shall return back as he came, and he shall receive nothing for his labor, that it should go with him in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 49:17; Job 1:21; 1 Timothy 6:7"> And this is also an evil infirmity: for as he came, so also shall he return: and what is his gain, for which he vainly labors?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:29; Ecclesiastes 1:3; John 6:27"> Yes, all his days are in darkness, and in mourning, and much sorrow, and infirmity, and wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Jezekiel 4:16–17; Psalms 90:7–11"> Behold, I have seen good, that it is a fine thing for a man to eat and to drink, and to see good in all his labor in which he may labor under the sun, all the number of the days of his life which God has given to him: for it is his portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:24; Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Ecclesiastes 3:22"> Yes, and as for every man to whom God has given wealth and possessions, and has given him power to eat thereof, and to receive his portion, and to rejoice in his labor; this is the gift of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:18; Ecclesiastes 3:13; Ecclesiastes 6:2"> For he shall not much remember the days of his life; for God troubles him in the mirth of his heart.</span>
