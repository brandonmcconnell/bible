<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:16; Ecclesiastes 5:8; Esias 5:7"> So I returned, and saw all the oppressions that were done under the sun: and behold the tear of the oppressed, and they had no comforter; and on the side of them that oppressed them was power; but they had no comforter:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Job 3:11–26; Ecclesiastes 2:17; Ecclesiastes 9:4–6"> and I praised all the dead that had already died more than the living, as many as are alive until now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 23:29; Ecclesiastes 6:3–5; Matthew 24:19"> Better also than both these is he who has not yet been, who has not seen all the evil work that is done under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 John 3:12; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 2:26"> And I saw all labor, and all the diligent work, that this is a man's envy from his neighbor. This is also vanity and waywardness of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 9:20; Proverbs 13:4; Proverbs 6:10–11"> The fool folds his hands together, and eats his own flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 16:8; Proverbs 15:16–17; Psalms 37:16"> Better is a handful of rest than two handfuls of trouble and waywardness of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:1; Psalms 78:33; Zacharias 1:6"> So I returned, and saw vanity under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 2:16; Proverbs 27:20; Luke 12:20"> There is one alone, and there is not a second; yes, he has neither son nor brother: yet there is no end to all his labor; neither is his eye satisfied with wealth; and for whom do I labor, and deprive my soul of good? this is also vanity, and an evil trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 27:17; Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 12:18–21"> Two are better than one, seeing they have a good reward for their labor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:18; Esias 35:3–4"> For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falls, and there is not a second to lift him up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 1:1–4"> Also if two should lie together, they also get heat: but how shall one be warmed alone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 3:16–17; Ephesians 4:3; Kings II 23:9"> And if one should prevail against him, the two shall withstand him; and a threefold cord shall not be quickly broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 25:16; Chronicles II 16:9–10; Proverbs 19:1"> Better is a poor and wise child than an old and foolish king, who knows not how to take heed any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Genesis 41:14; Job 5:11"> For he shall come forth out of the house of the prisoners to reign, because he also that was in his kingdom has become poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings II 15:6"> I saw all the living who were walking under the sun, with the second youth who shall stand up in each one's place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:14; Kings III 1:40; Ecclesiastes 2:17"> There is no end to all the people, to all who were before them: and the last shall not rejoice in him: for this also is vanity and waywardness of spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Keep your foot, whenever you go to the house of God; and when you are near to hear, let your sacrifice be better than the gift of fools: for they know not that they are doing evil.</span>
