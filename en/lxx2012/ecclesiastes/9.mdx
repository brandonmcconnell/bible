<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:3; John 10:27–30; 1 Peter 1:5"> I saw that the righteous, and the wise, and their works, are in the hand of God: yes, there is no man that knows either love or hatred, though all are before their face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachias 3:18; Ecclesiastes 2:14–16; Malachias 3:5"> Vanity is in all: there is one event to the righteous, and to the wicked; to the good, and to the bad; both to the pure, and to the impure; both to him that sacrifices, and to him that sacrifice not: as is the good, so is the sinner: as is the swearer, even so is he that fears an oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 17:9; Ecclesiastes 8:11; Ecclesiastes 1:17"> There is this evil in all that is done under the sun, that there is one event to all: yes, the heart of the sons of men is filled with evil, and madness is in their heart during their life, and after that they go to the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 14:7–12; Esias 38:18; Luke 16:26–29"> for who is he that has fellowship with all the living? there is hope of him: for a living dog is better than a dead lion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 26:14; Ecclesiastes 1:11; Psalms 6:5"> For the living will know that they shall die: but the dead know nothing, and there is no longer any reward to them; for their memory is lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 146:3–4; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Job 3:17–18"> also their love, and their hatred, and their envy, have now perished; yes, there is no portion for them any more for ever in all that is done under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:12–13; Deuteronomy 12:7; Ecclesiastes 10:19"> Go, eat your bread with mirth, and drink your wine with a joyful heart; for now God has favourably accepted your works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 19:14; Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 19:8"> Let your garments be always white; and let not oil be lacking on your head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; Ecclesiastes 2:10; Malachias 2:15"> And see life with the wife whom you love all the days of the life of your vanity, which are given you under the sun: for that is your portion in your life, and in your labor wherein you labor under the sun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 3:23; Ephesians 5:16; Romans 12:11"> Whatsoever your hand shall find to do, do with all your might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in Hades wither you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 9:23; Proverbs 21:30–31; Lamentations 3:37–38"> I returned, and saw under the sun, that the race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong, nor yet bread to the wise, nor yet wealth to men of understanding, nor yet favor to men of knowledge; for time and chance will happen to them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Luke 21:34–36; Ecclesiastes 8:5–7"> For surely man also knows not his time: as fishes that are taken in an evil net, and as birds that are caught in a snare; even thus the sons of men are snared at an evil time, when it falls suddenly upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:16; Ecclesiastes 7:15; Ecclesiastes 6:1"> This I also saw to be wisdom under the sun, and it is great before me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 20:15–22; Kings IV 6:24–20"> suppose there were a little city, and few men in it; and there should come against it a great king, and surround it, and build great mounds against it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:13; Genesis 40:23; Esther 6:2–3"> and should find in it a poor wise man, and he should save the city through his wisdom: yet no man would remember that poor man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:19; Proverbs 24:5; Proverbs 21:22"> And I said Wisdom is better than power: yet the wisdom of the poor man is set at nothing, and his words not listened to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 3:17–18; Proverbs 28:23; James 1:20"> The words of the wise are heard in quiet more than the cry of them that rule in folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 7:11–12; Ecclesiastes 9:16; Joshua 7:5"> Wisdom is better than weapons of war: and one sinner will destroy much good.</span>
