<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 18:8–11; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 20:21"> Then said Solomon, The Lord said that he would dwell in thick darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:4–6; Psalms 135:21; Chronicles I 28:6"> But I have built a house to your name, holy to you, and prepared for you to dwell in for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings III 8:14; Chronicles II 29:29; Kings III 8:55–61"> And the king turned his face, and blessed all the congregation of Israel: and all the congregation of Israel stood by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 72:18–19; Matthew 24:35; Psalms 68:32–35"> And he said, Blessed be the Lord God of Israel: he has even fulfilled with his hands as he spoke with his mouth to my father David, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:24; Kings III 8:16; Kings I 13:13–14"> From the day when I brought up my people out of the land of Egypt, I chose no city of all the tribes of Israel, to build a house that my name should be there; neither did I choose a man to be a leader over my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:4; Psalms 132:13; Chronicles II 12:13"> But I chose Jerusalem that my name should be there; and I chose David to be over my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 17:1; Kings III 5:3; Chronicles I 22:7"> And it came into the heart of David my father, to build a house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:12; Mark 14:8; Kings III 8:18–21"> But the Lord said to my father David, Whereas it came into your heart to build a house for my name, you did well that it came into your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings II 7:12–13; Chronicles I 17:4; Chronicles I 17:11–12"> Nevertheless you shall not build the house; for your son who shall come forth out of your loins, he shall build the house for my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:4; Chronicles I 28:5; Chronicles I 17:11"> And the Lord has confirmed this word, which he spoke; and I am raised up in the room of my father David, and I sit upon the throne of Israel as the Lord said, and I have built the house for the name of the Lord God of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 5:10; Chronicles II 5:7; Kings III 8:21"> and I have set there the ark in which is the covenant of the Lord, which he made with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 63:4; Esias 50:10; Job 11:13"> And he stood before the altar of the Lord in the presence of all the congregation of Israel, and spread out his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 8:54; Nehemiah 8:4; Psalms 95:6"> For Solomon had made a brazen scaffold, and set it in the midst of the court of the sanctuary; the length of it was five cubits, and the breadth of it five cubits, and the height of it three cubits: and he stood upon it, and fell upon his knees before the whole congregation of Israel, and spread abroad his hands to heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 15:11; Deuteronomy 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5"> and said, Lord God of Israel, there is no God like you in heaven, or on the earth; keeping covenant and mercy with your servants that walk before you with their whole heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:9–10; Kings II 7:12; Kings III 8:24"> Even as you have kept them with your servant David my father, as you have spoken to him in words:—you have both spoken with your mouth, and have fulfilled it with your hands, as it is this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings III 2:4; John 15:14–15; Chronicles II 7:18"> and now, Lord God of Israel, keep with your servant David my father the things which you spoke to him, saying, There shall not fail you a man before me sitting on the throne of Israel, if only your sons will take heed to their way to walk in my law, as you did walk before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 11:5; Esias 41:17; Chronicles II 6:4"> And now, Lord God of Israel, let, I pray you, your word be confirmed, which you have spoken to your servant David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:6; Esias 66:1; Psalms 139:7–10"> For will God indeed dwell with men upon the earth? if the heaven and the heaven of heavens will not suffice you, what then is this house which I have built?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings III 8:28; Luke 18:1–7; John 17:20"> Yet you shall have respect to the prayer of your servant, and to my petition, O Lord God, so as to listen to the petition and the prayer which your servant prays before you this day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 34:15; Daniel 6:10; Psalms 121:5"> so that your eyes should be open over this house by day and by night, towards this place, whereon you said your name should be called, so as to hear the prayer which your servant prays towards this house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Michaeas 7:18; Daniel 9:19; Esias 43:25"> And you shall hear the supplication of your servant, and of your people Israel, whatever prayers they shall make towards this place: and you shall listen in your dwelling-place out of heaven, yes you shall hear, and be merciful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 22:11; Leviticus 5:1; Numbers 5:19–22"> If a man sin against his neighbor, and he bring an oath upon him so as to make him swear, and he come and swear before the altar in this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esias 3:10–11; Proverbs 17:15; Jeremias 51:56"> then shall you listen out of heaven, and do, and judge your servants, to recompense the transgressor, and to return his ways upon his head: and to justify the righteous, to recompense him according to his righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 26:17; Kings IV 17:7–18; Joshua 7:8"> And if your people Israel should be put to the worse before the enemy, if they should sin against you, and then turn and confess to your name, and pray and make supplication before you in this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremias 33:6–13; Ezra 1:1–6; Genesis 13:15"> then shall you listen out of heaven and shall be merciful to the sins of your people Israel, and you shall restore them to the land which you gave to them and to their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; Deuteronomy 11:17; Leviticus 26:19"> When heaven is restrained, and there is no rain, because they shall have sinned against you, and when they shall pray towards this place, and praise your name, and shall turn from their sins, because you shall afflict them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings III 8:35–36; Jeremias 5:24; Zacharias 10:1"> then shall you listen from heaven, and you shall be merciful to the sins of your servants, and of your people Israel; for you shall show them the good way in which they shall walk; and you shall send rain upon your land, which you gave to your people for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 9:3–11; Joel 2:25; Ruth 1:1"> If there should be famine upon the land, if there should be death, a pestilent wind an blight; if there should be locust and caterpiller, and if the enemy should harass them before their cities: in whatever plague and whatever distress they may be;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Psalms 32:2–6; Chronicles II 6:12–13"> Then whatever prayer and whatever supplication shall be made by any man and all your people Israel, if a man should know his own plague and his own sickness, and should spread forth his hands toward this house;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:9; Kings I 16:7; Revelation 2:23"> then shall you hear from heaven, out of your prepared dwelling-place, and shall be merciful, and shall recompense to the man according to his ways, as you shall know his heart to be; for you alone know the heart of the children of men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:31; Job 28:28; Psalms 128:1"> that they may reverence all your ways all the days which they live upon the face of the land, which you gave to our fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Esias 56:3–7; Kings III 8:41–43; Matthew 8:10–11"> And every stranger who is not himself of your people Israel, and who shall have come from a distant land because of your great name, and your mighty hand, and your high arm; when they shall come and worship toward this place; —</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:14; Kings III 8:16; Revelation 11:15"> then shall you listen out of heaven, out of your prepared dwelling-place, and shall do according to all that the stranger shall call upon you for; that all the nations of the earth may know your name, and that they may fear you, as your people Israel do, and that they may know that your name is called upon this house which I have built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:6–13; Chronicles II 18:31; Chronicles II 20:4"> And if your people shall go forth to war against their enemies by the way by which you shall send them, and shall pray to you toward this city which you have chosen, and toward the house which I have built to your name;</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Daniel 9:17–19; Psalms 9:3–4; Jeremias 5:28"> then shall you hear out of heaven their prayer and their supplication, and maintain their cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2; 1 John 1:8–10"> Whereas if they shall sin against you, (for there is no man who will not sin,) and you shall strike them, and deliver them up before their enemies, and they that take them captive shall carry them away into a land of enemies, to a land far off or near;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremias 31:18–20; Daniel 9:5–11; Esias 64:6–12"> and if they shall repent in their land whither they were carried captive, and shall also turn and make supplication to you in their captivity, saying, We have sinned, we have transgressed, we have wrought unrighteously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Chronicles II 33:11–13; Daniel 6:10; Daniel 9:3–4"> and if they shall turn to you with all their heart and all their soul in the land of them that carried them captives, whither they carried them captives, and shall pray toward their land which you gave to their fathers, and the city which you did choose, and the house which I built to your name:—</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Michaeas 7:18–20; Chronicles II 6:35; Psalms 25:18"> then shall you hear out of heaven, out of your prepared dwelling-place, their prayer and their supplication, and you shall execute justice, and shall be merciful to your people that sin against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:15; Psalms 116:2; Nehemiah 1:6"> And now, Lord, let, I pray you, your eyes be opened, and your ears be attentive to the petition made in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Esias 61:10; Chronicles I 28:2; Psalms 132:8–10"> And now, O Lord God, arise into your resting-place, you, and the ark of your strength: let your priests, O Lord God, clothe themselves with salvation, and your sons rejoice in prosperity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Esias 55:3; Psalms 132:1; Esias 61:1"> O Lord God, turn not away the face of your anointed: remember the mercies of your servant David.</span>
