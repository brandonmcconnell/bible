<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 18:31–32"> And Josaphat king of Juda returned to his house at Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 139:21–22; 2 John 1:10–11; James 4:4"> And there went out to meet him Jeu the prophet the son of Anani, and said to him, King Josaphat, do you help a sinner, or act friendly towards one hated of the Lord? Therefore has wrath come upon you from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 12:12; Ezra 7:10; Kings III 14:13"> Nevertheless some good things have been found in you, forasmuch as you did remove the groves from the land of Juda, and did direct your heart to seek after the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings I 7:15–17; Kings I 7:3–4; Chronicles II 29:10–11"> And Josaphat lived in Jerusalem: and he again went out among the people from Bersabee to the mount of Ephraim, and turned them back to the Lord God of their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18–20; Chronicles II 19:8; 1 Peter 2:13–14"> And he appointed judges in all the strong cities of Juda, city by city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Leviticus 19:15; Ecclesiastes 5:8"> And he said to the judges, Take good heed what you⌃ do: for you⌃ judge not for man, but for the Lord, and with you are matters of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Genesis 18:25; Romans 2:11"> And now let the fear of the Lord be upon you, and be wary, and do your duty: for there is no unrighteousness with the Lord our God, neither is it for him to respect persons, nor take bribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 17:8–9; Exodus 18:19–26; Deuteronomy 17:8–13"> Moreover Josaphat appointed in Jerusalem some of the priests, and Levites, and heads of houses of Israel, for the judgment of the Lord, and to judge the dwellers in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles II 19:7; Kings II 23:3; Deuteronomy 1:16–17"> And he charged them, saying, Thus shall you⌃ do in the fear of the Lord, in truth and with a perfect heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:8–13; Jezekiel 33:6; Jezekiel 3:18–21"> Whatsoever man of your brethren that dwell in their cities shall bring the cause that comes before you, between blood and blood, and between precept and commandment, and ordinances and judgments, you⌃ shall even decide for them; so they shall not sin against the Lord, and there shall not be wrath upon you, and upon your brethren: thus you⌃ shall do, and you⌃ shall not sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Chronicles II 19:8; Psalms 18:25–26; 2 Timothy 2:1"> And, behold, Amarias the priest is head over you in every matter of the Lord; and Zabdias the son of Ismael is head over the house of Juda in every matter of the king; and the scribes and Levites are before you: be strong and active, and the Lord shall be with the good.</span>
