<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings III 18:38; Chronicles I 21:26; Leviticus 9:23–24"> And when Solomon had finished praying, then the fire came down from heaven, and devoured the whole burnt offerings and the sacrifices; and the glory of the Lord filled the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 15:8; Chronicles II 5:14; Exodus 24:17"> And the priests could not enter into the house of the Lord at that time, for the glory of the Lord filled the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 5:13; Chronicles II 20:21; Luke 1:50"> And all the children of Israel saw the fire descending, and the glory of the Lord was upon the house: and they fell upon their face to the ground on the pavement, and worshipped, and praised the Lord; for it is good to do so, because his mercy endures for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 8:62–63"> And the king and all the people were offering sacrifices before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 10:22; Jezekiel 45:17; Chronicles II 30:24"> And king Solomon offered a sacrifice of calves twenty and two thousand, of sheep one hundred and twenty thousand: so the king and all the people dedicated the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 5:12; Chronicles I 15:16–21; Chronicles II 7:3"> And the priests were standing at their watches, and the Levites with instruments of music of the Lord, belonging to king David, to give thanks before the Lord, for his mercy endures for ever, with the hymns of David, by their ministry: and the priests were blowing the trumpets before them, and all Israel standing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 8:64–66; Hebrews 13:10–12; Chronicles II 36:14"> And Solomon consecrated the middle of the court that was in the house of the Lord: for he offered there the whole burnt offerings and the fat of the peace-offerings, for the brazen altar which Solomon had made was not sufficient to receive the whole burnt offerings, and the meat-offerings, and the fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Kings III 8:65; Nehemiah 8:13–18"> And Solomon kept the feast at that time seven days, and all Israel with him, a very great assembly, from the entering in of Aemath, and as far as the river of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:36; Kings III 8:65; Joel 1:14"> And on the eighth day he kept a solemn assembly: for he kept a feast of seven days as the dedication of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 8:66; Psalms 100:2; Deuteronomy 12:18"> And on the twenty-third day of the seventh month he dismissed the people to their tents, rejoicing, and with a glad heart because of the good deeds which the Lord had done to David, and to Solomon, and to Israel his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 9:1–9; Ecclesiastes 2:10–11; Chronicles II 2:1"> So Solomon finished the house of the Lord, and the king's house: and in whatever Solomon wished in his heart to do in the house of the Lord and in his own house, he prospered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Kings III 9:2; Chronicles II 7:16"> And the Lord appeared to Solomon by night, and said to him, I have heard your prayer, and I have chosen this place to myself for a house of sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles II 6:26–28; Deuteronomy 11:17; Revelation 11:6"> If I should restrain the heaven and there should be no rain, and if I should command the locust to devour the trees, and if I should send pestilence upon my people;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40–41; James 4:9–10; Esias 55:6–7"> then if my people, on whom my name is called, should repent, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their evil ways, I also will hear from heaven, and I will be merciful to their sins, and I will heal their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:12; Chronicles II 6:40; Nehemiah 1:6"> And now my eyes shall be open, and my ears attentive to the prayer of this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Chronicles II 7:15; Chronicles II 7:12; Chronicles II 6:5–6"> And now I have chosen and sanctified this house, that my name should be there for ever: and my eyes and my heart shall be there always.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zacharias 3:7; Jezekiel 36:27; Kings III 11:38"> And if you will walk before me as David your father did, and will do according to all that I have commanded you, and will keep my ordinances and my judgments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 6:16; Jeremias 33:20–21; Kings II 7:13–16"> then will I establish the throne of your kingdom, as I covenanted with David your father, saying, There shall not fail you a man ruling in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 26:14; Deuteronomy 28:15; Leviticus 26:33–46"> But if you⌃ should turn away, and forsake my ordinances and my commandments, which I have set before you, and go and serve other gods, and worship them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings IV 17:20; Deuteronomy 28:37; Jeremias 24:9"> then will I remove you from the land which I gave them; and this house which I have consecrated to my name I will remove out of my sight, and I will make it a proverb and a byword among all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 22:8–9; Jeremias 16:10–12; Jeremias 19:8"> And as for this lofty house, every one that passes by it shall be amazed, and shall say, Therefore has the Lord done thus to this land, and to this house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 9:12; Lamentations 2:16–17; Jezekiel 14:23"> And men shall say, Because they forsook the Lord God of their fathers, who brought them out of the land of Egypt, and they attached themselves to other gods, and worshipped them, and served them: and therefore he has brought upon them all this evil.</span>
