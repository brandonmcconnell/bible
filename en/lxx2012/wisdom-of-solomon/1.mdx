<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Love righteousness, you⌃ that be judges of the earth: think of the Lord with a good (heart,) and in simplicity of heart seek him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> For he will be found of them that tempt him not; and shows himself to such as do not distrust him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For froward thoughts separate from God: and his power, when it is tried, reproves the unwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For into a malicious soul wisdom shall not enter; nor dwell in the body that is subject to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and remove from thoughts that are without understanding, and will not abide when unrighteousness comes in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For wisdom is a loving spirit; and will not acquit a blasphemer of his words: for God is witness of his reins, and a true beholder of his heart, and a hearer of his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For the Spirit of the Lord fills the world: and that which contains all things has knowledge of the voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Therefore he that speaks unrighteous things can’t be hid: neither shall vengeance, when it punishes, pass by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For inquisition shall be made into the counsels of the ungodly: and the sound of his words shall come to the Lord for the manifestation of his wicked deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For the ear of jealousy hears all things: and the noise of murmurings is not hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Therefore beware of murmuring, which is unprofitable; and refrain your tongue from backbiting: for there is no word so secret, that shall go for nothing: and the mouth that belies slays the soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Seek not death in the error of your life: and pull not upon yourselves destruction with the works of your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For God made not death: neither has he pleasure in the destruction of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For he created all things, that they might have their being: and the generations of the world were healthful; and there is no poison of destruction in them, nor the kingdom of death upon the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> (For righteousness is immortal:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But ungodly men with their works and words called it to them: for when they thought to have it their friend, they consumed to nothing, and made a covenant with it, because they are worthy to take part with it.</span>
