<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Nevertheless your saints had a very great light, whose voice they hearing, and not seeing their shape, because they also had not suffered the same things, they counted them happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But for that they did not hurt them now, of whom they had been wronged before, they thanked them, and implored them pardon for that they had been enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Instead whereof you gave them a burning pillar of fire, both to be a guide of the unknown journey, and an harmless sun to entertain them honorably.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For they were worthy to be deprived of light and imprisoned in darkness, who had kept your sons shut up, by whom the uncorrupt light of the law was to be given to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when they had determined to kill the babes of the saints, one child being cast forth, and saved, to reprove them, you took away the multitude of their children, and destroyed them altogether in a mighty water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Of that night were our fathers certified before, that assuredly knowing to what oaths they had given credence, they might afterwards be of good cheer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> So of your people was accepted both the salvation of the righteous, and destruction of the enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For wherewith you did punish our adversaries, by the same you did glorify us, whom you had called.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For the righteous children of good men did sacrifice secretly, and with one consent made a holy law, that the saints should be like partakers of the same good and evil, the fathers now singing out the songs of praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But on the other side there sounded an ill according cry of the enemies, and a lamentable noise was carried abroad for children that were bewailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The master and the servant were punished after one manner; and like as the king, so suffered the common person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So they all together had innumerable dead with one kind of death; neither were the living sufficient to bury them: for in one moment the noblest offspring of them was destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For whereas they would not believe any thing by reason of the enchantments; upon the destruction of the firstborn, they acknowledged this people to be the sons of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For while all things were in quiet silence, and that night was in the midst of her swift course,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Your Almighty word leaped down from heaven out of your royal throne, as a fierce man of war into the midst of a land of destruction,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And brought your unfeigned commandment as a sharp sword, and standing up filled all things with death; and it touched the heaven, but it stood upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then suddenly visions of horrible dreams troubled them sore, and terrors came upon them unlooked for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And one thrown here, and another there, half dead, showed the cause of his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For the dreams that troubled them did foreshew this, lest they should perish, and not know why they were afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Yes, the tasting of death touched the righteous also, and there was a destruction of the multitude in the wilderness: but the wrath endured not long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For then the blameless man made haste, and stood forth to defend them; and bringing the shield of his proper ministry, even prayer, and the propitiation of incense, set himself against the wrath, and so brought the calamity to an end, declaring that he was your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So he overcame the destroyer, not with strength of body, nor force of arms, but with a word subdued him that punished, alleging the oaths and covenants made with the fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For when the dead were now fallen down by heaps one upon another, standing between, he stayed the wrath, and parted the way to the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For in the long garment was the whole world, and in the four rows of the stones was the glory of the fathers graven, and your Majesty upon the diadem of his head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> To these the destroyer gave place, and was afraid of them: for it was enough that they only tasted of the wrath.</span>
