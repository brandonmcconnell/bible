<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> O God of my fathers, and Lord of mercy, who have made all things with your word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And ordained man through your wisdom, that he should have dominion over the creatures which you have made,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And order the world according to equity and righteousness, and execute judgment with an upright heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Give me wisdom, that sits by your throne; and reject me not from among your children:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For I your servant and son of your handmaid am a feeble person, and of a short time, and too young for the understanding of judgment and laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For though a man be never so perfect among the children of men, yet if your wisdom be not with him, he shall be nothing regarded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> You have chosen me to be a king of your people, and a judge of your sons and daughters:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> You have commanded me to build a temple upon your holy mount, and an altar in the city wherein you dwell, a resemblance of the holy tabernacle, which you have prepared from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And wisdom was with you: which knows your works, and was present when you made the world, and knew what was acceptable in your sight, and right in your commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> O send her out of your holy heavens, and from the throne of your glory, that being present she may labor with me, that I may know what is pleasing to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For she knows and understands all things, and she shall lead me soberly in my doings, and preserve me in her power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So shall my works be acceptable, and then shall I judge your people righteously, and be worthy to sit in my father's seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For what man is he that can know the counsel of God? or who can think what the will of the Lord is?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For the thoughts of mortal men are miserable, and our devices are but uncertain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For the corruptible body presses down the soul, and the earthy tabernacle weighs down the mind that muses upon many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And hardly do we guess aright at things that are upon earth, and with labor do we find the things that are before us: but the things that are in heaven who has searched out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And your counsel who has known, except you give wisdom, and send your Holy Spirit from above?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For so the ways of them which lived on the earth were reformed, and men were taught the things that are pleasing to you, and were saved through wisdom.</span>
