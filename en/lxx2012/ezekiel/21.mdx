<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:46; Jezekiel 4:7; Jezekiel 25:2"> Therefore prophesy, son of man, set your face steadfastly toward Jerusalem, and look toward their holy places, and you shall prophesy against the land of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 9:22; Jeremias 21:13; Jezekiel 5:8"> and you shall say to the land of Israel, Thus says the Lord; Behold, I am against you, and I will draw forth my sword out of its sheath, and I will destroy out of you the transgressor and unrighteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:47; Jezekiel 7:2; Jezekiel 6:11–14"> Because I will destroy out of you the unrighteous and the transgressor, therefore so shall my sword come forth out of its sheath against all flesh from the south to the north:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:30; Naum 1:9; Kings I 3:12"> and all flesh shall know that I the Lord have drawn forth my sword out of its sheath: it shall not return any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 22:4; Jezekiel 6:11; Jeremias 19:10"> And you, son of man, groan with the breaking of your loins; you shall even groan heavily in their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 7:17; Jezekiel 7:26; Esias 13:7"> And it shall come to pass, if they shall say to you, Therefore do you groan? that you shall say, For the report; because it comes: and every heart shall break, and all hands shall become feeble, and all flesh and every spirit shall faint, and all thighs shall be defiled with moisture: behold, it comes, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 34:5–6; Jezekiel 21:28; Jezekiel 21:3"> Son of man, prophesy, and you shall say, Thus says the Lord; Say, Sword, sword, be sharpened and rage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 110:5–6; Jezekiel 20:47; Jezekiel 21:25–27"> that you may kill victims; be sharpened that you may be bright, ready for slaughter, kill, set at nothing, despise every tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:19; Jeremias 25:33; Jeremias 51:20–23"> And he made it ready for his hand to hold: the sword is sharpened, it is ready to put into the hand of the slayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:6; Joel 1:13; Jezekiel 21:14"> Cry out and howl, son of man: for this sword is come upon my people, this sword is come upon all the princes of Israel: they shall be as strangers: judgment with the sword is come upon my people: therefore clap your hands, for sentence has been passed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Jezekiel 21:25"> and what if even the tribe be rejected? it shall not be, says the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 24:1; Numbers 24:10; Leviticus 26:24"> And you, son of man, prophesy, and clap your hands, and take a second sword: the third sword is the sword of the slain, the great sword of the slain: and you shall strike them with amazement, lest the heart should faint</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:10; Jeremias 17:27; Jezekiel 21:22"> and the weak ones be multiplied at every gate—they are given up to the slaughter of the sword: it is well fitted for slaughter, it is well fitted for glittering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:20; Jezekiel 21:4; Jezekiel 14:17"> And do you go on, sharpen yourself on the right and on the left wherever your face may set itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:13; Jezekiel 21:14; Zacharias 6:8"> And I also will clap my hands, and let loose my fury: I the Lord have spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:1–3; Jeremias 1:10; Jezekiel 5:1–17"> and you, son of man, appoint you two ways, that the sword of the king of Babylon may enter in: the two shall go forth of one country; and there shall be a force at the top of the way of the city, you shall set it at the top of the way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Amos 1:14; Jeremias 49:2; Jezekiel 25:5"> that the sword may enter in upon Rabbath of the children of Ammon, and upon Judea, and upon Jerusalem in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 18:20; Proverbs 16:33; Judges 17:5"> For the king of Babylon shall stand on the old way, at the head of the two ways, to use divination, to make bright the arrow, and to enquire of the graven images, and to examine the victims.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:2; Jezekiel 26:9; Jeremias 51:14"> On his right was the divination against Jerusalem, to cast a mound, to open the mouth in shouting, to lift up the voice with crying, to cast a mound against her gates, to cast up a heap, and to build forts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Jezekiel 21:24; Jezekiel 29:16"> And he was to them as one using divination before them, and he himself recounting his iniquities, that they might be borne in mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Osee 4:2; Michaeas 3:10–12; Jeremias 3:2"> Therefore thus says the Lord, Because you⌃ have caused your iniquities to be remembered, in the discovery of your wickedness, so that your sins should be seen, in all your wickedness and in your evil practices; because you⌃ have caused remembrance of them, in these shall you⌃ be taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 35:5; Jezekiel 21:29; Jezekiel 17:19"> And you profane wicked prince of Israel, whose day, even an end, is come in a sea of iniquity, thus says the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:7; Jeremias 13:18"> You have taken off the mitre and put on the crown, it shall not have such another after it: you have abased that which was high, and exalted that which was low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Michaeas 5:2; Jeremias 23:5–6; Zacharias 9:9"> Injustice, injustice, injustice, will I make it: woe to it: such shall it be until he comes to whom it belongs; and I will deliver it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:20; Jezekiel 21:9–10; Sophonias 2:8–10"> And you, son of man, prophesy, and you shall say, Thus says the Lord, concerning the children of Ammon, and concerning their reproach; and you shall say, O sword, sword, drawn for slaughter, and drawn for destruction, awake, that you may gleam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:25; Jezekiel 22:28; Psalms 37:13"> While you are seeing vain visions, and while you are prophesying falsehoods, to bring yourself upon the necks of ungodly transgressors, the day is come, even an end, in a season of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:38; Genesis 15:14; Jeremias 47:6–7"> Turn, rest not in this place wherein you were born: in your own land will I judge you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Naum 1:6; Jeremias 6:22–23; Jezekiel 14:19"> And I will pour out my wrath upon you, I will blow upon you with the fire of my wrath, and I will deliver you into the hands of barbarians skilled in working destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jezekiel 25:10; Malachias 4:1; Jezekiel 20:47–48"> You shall be fuel for fire; your blood shall be in the midst of your land; there shall be no remembrance at all of you: for I the Lord have spoken it.</span>
