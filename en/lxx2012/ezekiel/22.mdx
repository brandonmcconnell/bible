<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 58:1; Jezekiel 24:6; Jezekiel 24:9"> And you, son of man, will you judge the bloody city? yes, declare you to her all her iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:27; Romans 2:5; 2 Peter 2:3"> And you shall say, Thus says the Lord God: O city that sheds blood in the midst of her, so that her time should come, and that forms devices against herself, to defile herself;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 21:16; Psalms 44:13–14; Jezekiel 22:2"> in their blood which you have shed, you have transgressed; and in your devices which you have formed, you have polluted yourself; and you have brought near your days, and have brought on the time of your years: therefore have I made you a reproach to the Gentiles, and a mockery to all the countries,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 22:2; Jeremias 15:2–3"> to those near you, and to those far distant from you; and they shall mock you, you that are notoriously unclean, and abundant in iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:27; Esias 1:23; Zacharias 3:3"> Behold, the princes of the house of Israel have conspired in you each one with his kindred, that they might shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Exodus 22:21–22; Leviticus 20:9"> In you they have reviled father and mother; and in you they have behaved unjustly toward the stranger: they have oppressed the orphan and widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:38–39; Jezekiel 22:26; Jezekiel 20:13"> And they have set at nothing my holy things, and in you they have profaned my sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Osee 4:14; Leviticus 19:16; Osee 4:10"> There are robbers in you, to shed blood in you; and in you they have eaten upon the mountains: they have wrought ungodliness in the midst of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 18:19; Leviticus 18:7–8; Jezekiel 18:6"> In you they have uncovered the father's shame; and in you they have humbled her that was set apart for uncleanness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; Kings II 13:14; Leviticus 20:17"> They have dealt unlawfully each one with his neighbor's wife; and each one in ungodliness has defiled his daughter-in-law: and in you they have humbled each one his sister, the daughter of his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:25; Jezekiel 23:35; Psalms 106:21"> In you they have received gifts to shed blood; they have received in you interest and usurious increase; and by oppression you have brought your wickedness to the full, and have forgotten me, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:17; Esias 33:15; Amos 2:6–8"> And if I shall strike my hand at your iniquities which you have accomplished, which you have wrought, and at your blood that has been shed in the midst of you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:7; Jezekiel 17:24; Jezekiel 24:14"> shall your heart endure? shall your hands be strong in the days which I bring upon you? I the Lord have spoken, and will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:8; Jezekiel 22:22; Deuteronomy 4:27"> And I will scatter you among the nations, and disperse you in the countries, and your uncleanness shall be removed out of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 6:7; Psalms 83:18; Jezekiel 7:24"> And I will give heritages in you in the sight of the nations, and you⌃ shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:119; Esias 1:22; Jeremias 6:28–30"> Son of man, behold, the house of Israel are all become to me as it were mixed with brass, and iron, and tin, and lead; they are mixed up in the midst of the silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Jezekiel 24:3–6; Michaeas 4:12"> Therefore say, Thus says the Lord God; Because you⌃ have become one mixture, therefore I will gather you into the midst of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 4:11–12; Esias 54:16; Jezekiel 24:13"> As silver, and brass, and iron, and tin, and lead, are gathered into the midst of the furnace, to blow fire into it, that they may be melted: so will I take you in my wrath, and I will gather and melt you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 64:7; Naum 1:6; Psalms 50:3"> And I will blow upon you in the fire of my wrath, and you⌃ shall be melted in the midst thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:8; Jezekiel 20:33; Osee 5:10"> As silver is melted in the midst of a furnace, so shall you⌃ be melted in the midst thereof; and you⌃ shall know that I the Lord have poured out my wrath upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles II 36:14–16; Jeremias 2:30; Chronicles II 28:22"> Son of man, say to her, You are the land that is not rained upon, neither has rain come upon you in the day of wrath;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Osee 6:9; Jeremias 2:34; Jezekiel 13:19"> whose princes in the midst of her are as roaring lions seizing prey, devouring souls by oppression, and taking bribes; and your widows are multiplied in the midst of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Jezekiel 44:23; Jezekiel 22:8"> Her priests also have set at nothing my law, and profaned my holy things: they have not distinguished between the holy and profane, nor have they distinguished between the unclean and the clean, and have hid their eyes from my sabbaths, and I was profaned in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:13; Esias 1:23; Jezekiel 22:6"> Her princes in the midst of her are as wolves ravening to shed blood, that they may get dishonest gain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jezekiel 13:6–7; Jezekiel 13:10–16; Jeremias 23:25–32"> And her prophets that daub them shall fall, that see vanities, that prophesy falsehoods, saying, Thus says the Lord, when the Lord has not spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:7; Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21"> That sorely oppress the people of the land with injustice, and commit robbery; oppressing the poor and needy, and not dealing justly with the stranger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Esias 59:16; Jeremias 5:1; Psalms 106:23"> And I sought from among them a man behaving uprightly, and standing before me perfectly in the time of wrath, so that I should not utterly destroy her: but I found him not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:43; Jezekiel 7:8–9; Jezekiel 9:10"> So I have poured out my wrath upon her in the fury of my anger, to accomplish it. I have recompensed their ways on their own heads, says the Lord God.</span>
