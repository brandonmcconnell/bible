<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Moreover the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:2; Jezekiel 20:4; Esias 58:1"> Son of man, testify to Jerusalem of her iniquities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:45; Esias 51:1–2; Deuteronomy 20:17"> and you shall say, Thus says the Lord to Jerusalem; Your root and your birth are of the land of Chanaan: your father was an Amorite, and your mother a Chettite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Osee 2:3; Genesis 15:13; Exodus 5:16–21"> And as for your birth in the day wherein you were born, you did not bind your breasts, and you were not washed in water, neither was you salted with salt, neither was you swathed in swaddling-bands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:10; Genesis 21:10; Exodus 1:22"> Nor did my eye pity you, to do for you one of all these things, to feel at all for you; but you were cast out on the face of the field, because of the deformity of your person, in the day wherein you were born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 2:4–5; John 5:25; Titus 3:3–7"> And I passed by to you, and saw you polluted in your blood; and I said to you, Let there be life out of your blood:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:10; Exodus 1:7; Esias 62:3"> increase; I have made you as the springing grass of the field. So you did increase and grow, and did enter into great cities: your breasts were set, and your hair grew, whereas you were naked and bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 3:9; Jeremias 2:2–3; Romans 5:8"> And I passed by you and saw you, and, behold, it was your time and a time of resting; and I spread my wings over you, and covered your shame, and swear to you: and I entered into covenant with you, says the Lord, and you became mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ruth 3:3; 1 John 2:27"> And I washed you in water, and washed your blood from you, and anointed you with oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:18; Jezekiel 16:13; Esias 61:10"> And I clothed you with embroidered garments, and clothed you beneath with purple, and girded you with fine linen, and clothed you with silk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 24:22; Esias 3:19; Genesis 41:42"> and decked you also with ornaments, and put bracelets on your hands, and a necklace on your neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 3:21; Esias 28:5; Jeremias 13:18"> And I put a pendant on your nostril, and rings in your ears, and a crown of glory on your head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13–14; Psalms 50:2; Kings III 4:21"> So you were adorned with gold and silver; and your raiment was of fine linen, and silk, and variegated work: you did eat fine flour, and oil, and honey, and did become extremely beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 10:24; Lamentations 2:15; 1 Corinthians 4:7"> And your name went forth among the nations for your beauty: because it was perfected with elegance, and in the comeliness which I put upon you, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 57:8; Jeremias 2:20; Jezekiel 16:25"> You did trust in your beauty, and did go a-whoring because of your renown, and did pour out your fornication on every passer by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings IV 23:7; Jezekiel 7:20; Chronicles II 28:24"> And you did take of your garments, and made to yourself idols of needlework, and did go a-whoring after them; therefore you shall never come in, nor shall the like take place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:14–21; Jeremias 3:9; Jezekiel 16:11"> And you took your fair ornaments of my gold and of my silver, of what I gave you, and you made to yourself male images, and you did commit whoredom with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And you did take your variegated apparel and did clothe them, and you did set before them my oil and my incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:13; Osee 2:8–13; Genesis 8:21"> And you took my bread which I gave you, (yes I fed you with fine flour and oil and honey) and did set them before them for a sweet smelling savor: yes, it was so, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:37; Jezekiel 20:31; Jeremias 7:31"> And you took your sons and your daughters, whom you bore, and did sacrifice these to them to be destroyed. You did go a-whoring as if that were little,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings IV 17:17; Leviticus 18:21; Deuteronomy 18:10"> and did kill your children, and gave them up in offering them to them for an expiation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremias 2:2; Osee 11:1; Jezekiel 16:43"> This is beyond all your fornication, and you did not remember your infancy, when you were naked and bare, and did live though defiled in your blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 12:12; Jezekiel 2:10; Jezekiel 24:6"> And it came to pass after all your wickedness, says the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 78:58; Jezekiel 16:39; Jezekiel 16:31"> that you did build yourself a house of fornication, and did make yourself a public place in every street;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:31; Jeremias 3:2; Genesis 38:21"> and on the head of every way you did set up your fornications, and did defile your beauty, and did open your feet to every passer by, and did multiply your fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:19–21; Jezekiel 20:7–8; Jezekiel 8:10"> And you did go a-whoring after the children of Egypt your neighbors, great of flesh; and did go a-whoring, often to provoke me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Esias 9:12; Jezekiel 16:57; Jezekiel 16:37"> And if I stretch out my hand against you, then will I abolish your statutes, and deliver you up to the wills of them that hate you, even to the daughters of the Philistines that turned you aside from the way wherein you sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings IV 16:7; Osee 10:6; Kings IV 16:10–18"> And you did go a-whoring to the daughters of Assur, and not even thus was you satisfied; yes, you did go a-whoring, and was not satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings IV 21:9; Judges 2:12–19; Jezekiel 13:14–23"> And you did multiply your covenants with the land of the Chaldeans; and not even with these was you satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremias 3:3; Esias 1:3; Esias 3:9"> Why should I make a covenant with your daughter, says the Lord, while you do all these things, the works of a harlot? and you have gone a-whoring in a threefold degree with your daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:33–34; Esias 52:3; Jezekiel 16:39"> You have built a house of harlotry in every top of a way, and have set up your high place in every street; and you did become as a harlot gathering hires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremias 2:28; Jezekiel 23:37; Jeremias 2:25"> An adulteress resembles you, taking rewards of her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Esias 57:9; Osee 8:9–10; Jezekiel 16:41"> She has even given rewards to all that went a-whoring after her, and you have given rewards to all your lovers, yes, you did load them with rewards, that they should come to you from every side for your fornication.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And there has happened in you perverseness in your fornication beyond other women, and they have committed fornication with you, in that you give hires over and above, and hires were not given to you; and thus perverseness happened in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 4:18; Kings III 22:19; John 4:10"> Therefore, harlot, hear the word of the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:10; Jezekiel 24:13; Sophonias 3:1"> Thus says the Lord, Because you have poured forth your money, therefore your shame shall be revealed in your harlotry with your lovers, and with regard to all the imaginations of your iniquities, and for the blood of your children which you have given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Osee 2:10; Jeremias 13:22; Osee 2:3"> Therefore, behold, I will gather all your lovers with whom you have consorted, and all whom you have loved, with all whom you did hate; and I will gather them against you round about, and will expose your wickedness to them, and they shall see all your shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Genesis 9:6; Sophonias 1:17"> And I will be avenged on you with the vengeance of an adulteress, and I will bring upon you blood of fury and jealousy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:26; Osee 2:3; Jezekiel 16:24–25"> And I will deliver you into their hands, and they shall break down your house of harlotry, and destroy your high place; and they shall strip you of your garments, and shall take your proud ornaments, and leave you naked and bare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:47; Ambacum 1:6–10; John 8:5–7"> And they shall bring multitudes upon you, and they shall stone you with stones, and pierce you with their swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:48; Jeremias 52:13; Jeremias 39:8"> And they shall burn your houses with fire, and shall execute vengeance on you in the sight of many women: and I will turn you back from harlotry, and I will no more give you rewards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:13; Jezekiel 21:17; Esias 40:1–2"> So will I slacken my fury against you, and my jealousy shall be removed from you, and I will rest, and be no more careful for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:22; Jezekiel 11:21; Psalms 78:42"> Because you did not remember your infancy, and you did grieve me in all these things; therefore, behold, I have recompensed your ways upon your head, says the Lord: for thus have you wrought ungodliness above all your other iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Jezekiel 18:2–3; Psalms 106:35–38; Kings I 24:13"> These are all the things they have spoken against you in a proverb, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Zacharias 11:8; Esias 1:4; Jezekiel 23:37–39"> As is the mother, so is your mother's daughter: you are she that has rejected her husband and her children; and the sisters of your sisters have rejected their husbands and their children: your mother was a Chettite, and your father an Amorite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:4; Jezekiel 16:48–49; Jezekiel 16:53–56"> Your elder sister who dwells on your left hand is Samaria, she and her daughters: and your younger sister, that dwells on the right hand, is Sodom and her daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Kings IV 21:9; Jezekiel 16:51; Jezekiel 5:6–7"> Yet notwithstanding you have not walked in their ways, neither have you done according to their iniquities within a little, but you have exceeded them in all your ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 10:15; Luke 10:12; Acts 7:52"> As I live, says the Lord, this Sodom and her daughters have not done as you and your daughters have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Psalms 138:6; Jezekiel 28:2; Genesis 13:10"> Moreover this was the sin of your sister Sodom, pride: she and her daughters lived in pleasure, in fullness of bread and in abundance: this belonged to her and her daughters, and they helped not the hand of the poor and needy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Genesis 19:24; Genesis 18:20; Genesis 19:5"> And they boasted, and wrought iniquities before me: so I cut them off as I saw fit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Jeremias 3:8–11; Luke 12:47–48; Romans 3:9–20"> Also Samaria has not sinned according to half of your sins; but you have multiplied your iniquities beyond them, and you have justified your sisters in all your iniquities which you have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Jezekiel 36:15; Jezekiel 16:56"> You therefore bear your punishment, for that you have corrupted your sisters by your sins which you have committed beyond them; and you have made them appear more righteous than yourself: you therefore be ashamed, and bear your dishonor, in that you have justified your sisters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Jezekiel 29:14; Jeremias 12:16; Jezekiel 39:25"> And I will turn their captivity, even the captivity of Sodom and her daughters; and I will turn the captivity of Samaria and her daughters; and I will turn your captivity in the midst of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Jezekiel 14:22–23; Jeremias 2:26; Jezekiel 36:31–32"> that you may bear your punishment, and be dishonored for all that you have done in provoking me to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Jezekiel 36:11; Malachias 3:4; Jezekiel 16:53"> And your sister Sodom and her daughters shall be restored as they were at the beginning, and you and your daughters shall be restored as you⌃ were at the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Esias 65:5; Luke 15:28–30; Sophonias 3:11"> And surely your sister Sodom was not mentioned by your mouth in the days of your pride:</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Kings IV 16:5–7; Jezekiel 16:36–37; Chronicles II 28:5–6"> before your wickedness was revealed, even now you are the reproach of the daughters of Syria, and of all that are round about her, even of the daughters of the Philistines that compass you round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:49; Lamentations 5:7; Genesis 4:13"> As for your ungodliness and your iniquities, you have borne them, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Esias 24:5; Jezekiel 17:19; Deuteronomy 29:25"> Thus says the Lord; I will even do to you as you have done, as you have dealt shamefully in these things to transgress my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Osee 2:19–20; Hebrews 12:24; Jezekiel 16:8"> And I will remember my covenant made with you in the days of your infancy, and I will establish to you an everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Hebrews 8:13; Galatians 4:26–31; Esias 66:7–12"> Then you shall remember your way, and shall be utterly dishonored when you receive your elder sisters with your younger ones: and I will give them to you for building up, but not by your covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Jeremias 24:7; Jezekiel 6:7; Jezekiel 39:22"> And I will establish my covenant with you; and you shall know that I am the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 39:9; Romans 3:19; Jezekiel 36:31–32"> that you may remember, and be ashamed, and may no more be able to open your mouth for your shame, when I am reconciled to you for all that you have done, says the Lord.</span>
