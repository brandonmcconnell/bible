<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the word of the Lord came to me saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 19:1; Jeremias 9:10; Jezekiel 28:12"> And you, son of man, take up a lamentation against Sor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:4; Esias 23:11; Esias 23:2–3"> and you shall say to Sor that dwells at the entrance of the sea, to the mart of the nations coming from many islands, Thus says the Lord to Sor; You have said, I have clothed myself with my beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:5"> In the heart of the sea your sons have put beauty upon you for Beelim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Esias 14:8; Song of Songs 4:8"> Cedar in Senir was employed for you in building: boards of cypress timber were taken out of Libanus, and wood to make you masts of fir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Esias 2:13; Zacharias 11:2"> They made your oars of wood out of the land of Basan; your sacred utensils they made of ivory, your shady houses of wood from the isles of Chetiim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 10:9; Exodus 25:4; Genesis 10:4"> Fine linen with embroidery from Egypt supplied the couch, to put honor upon you, and to clothe you with blue and purple from the isles of Elisai; and they became your coverings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 10:18; Kings III 9:27; Jezekiel 27:11"> And your princes were the dwellers in Sidon, and the Aradians were your rowers: your wise men, O Sor, who were in you, these were your pilots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 5:18; Joshua 13:5; Psalms 83:7"> The elders of the Biblians, and their wise men, who were in you, these helped your counsel: and all the ships of the sea and their rowers traded for you to the utmost west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jezekiel 38:5; Jezekiel 30:5; Esias 66:19"> Persians and Lydians and Libyans were in your army: your warriors hung in you shields and helmets; these gave you your glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:3–4"> The sons of the Aradians and your army were upon the walls; there were guards in your towers: they hung their quivers on your battlements round about; these completed your beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Esias 23:10; Jezekiel 38:13"> The Carthaginians were your merchants because of the abundance of all your strength; they furnished your market with silver, and gold, and iron, and tin, and lead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:2; Esias 66:19; Jezekiel 39:1"> Greece, both the whole world, and the adjacent coasts, these traded with you in the persons of men, and they gave as your merchandise vessels of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Jezekiel 38:6; Chronicles I 1:6"> Out of the house of Thogarma horses and horsemen furnished the market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Revelation 18:12; Kings III 10:22"> The sons of the Rhodians were your merchants; from the islands they multiplied your merchandise, even elephants' teeth: and to them that came in you did return your prices,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Jezekiel 28:13; Kings II 10:6"> even men as your merchandise, from the multitude of your trading population, myrrh and embroidered works from Tharsis: Ramoth also and Chorchor furnished your market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:33; Kings III 5:9; Jeremias 8:22"> Juda and the children of Israel, these were your merchants; in the sale of corn and ointments and cassia: and they gave the best honey, and oil, and resin, to your trading population.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 7:8; Genesis 15:2; Jezekiel 47:16–18"> The people of Damascus were your merchants by reason of the abundance of all your power; wine out of Chelbon, and wool from Miletus; and they brought wine into your market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–24; Psalms 45:8; Song of Songs 4:13–14"> Out of Asel came wrought iron, and there is the sound of wheels among your trading population.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 25:3; Jezekiel 27:15"> The people of Daedan were your merchants, with choice cattle for chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 60:7; Genesis 25:13; Kings III 10:15"> Arabia and all the princes of Kedar, these were your traders with you, bringing camels, and lambs, and rams, in which they trade with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Esias 60:6; Psalms 72:10"> The merchants of Sabba and Ramma, these were your merchants, with choice spices, and precious stones: and they brought gold to your market.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 19:12; Esias 37:12; Amos 1:5"> Charra, and Chanaa, these were your merchants: Assur, and Charman, were your merchants:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Kings IV 2:8"> bringing for merchandise blue, and choice stores bound with cords, and cypress wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 2:16; Psalms 48:7; Esias 23:14"> Ships were your merchants, in abundance, with your trading population: and you were filled and very heavily loaded in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 48:7; Jezekiel 26:19; Acts 27:14"> Your rowers have brought you into great waters: the south wind has broken you in the heart of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Jezekiel 27:24; Jezekiel 27:12"> Your forces, and your gain, and that of your traders, and your rowers, and your pilots, and your counselors, and they that traffic with you, and all your warriors that are in you: and all your company in the midst of you shall perish in the heart of the sea, in the day of your fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:10; Exodus 15:14; Jezekiel 31:16"> At the cry of your voice your pilots shall be greatly terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:16; Jezekiel 32:10; Revelation 18:17–24"> And all the rowers and the mariners shall come down from the ships, and the pilots of the sea shall stand on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremias 6:26; Kings II 1:2; Lamentations 2:10"> And they shall wail over you with their voice, and cry bitterly, and put earth on their heads, and spread ashes under them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:17; Revelation 18:18; Jezekiel 27:2"> And their sons shall take up a lament for you, even a lamentation for Sor, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Jezekiel 27:3; Jezekiel 28:16"> How large a reward have you gained from the sea? you have filled nations out of your abundance; and out of your mixed merchandise you have enriched all the kings of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jezekiel 27:26–27; Zacharias 9:3–4; Jezekiel 26:19–21"> Now are you broken in the sea, your traders are in the deep water, and all your company in the midst of you: all your rowers have fallen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jezekiel 32:10; Esias 23:6; Jezekiel 26:15–18"> All the dwellers in the islands have mourned over you, and their kings have been utterly amazed, and their countenance has wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremias 18:16; Psalms 37:10; Sophonias 2:15"> Merchants from the nations have hissed at you; you are utterly destroyed, and shall not be any more for ever.</span>
