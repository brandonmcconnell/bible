<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jezekiel 8:1; Jezekiel 20:1; Jezekiel 29:17"> And the word of the Lord came to me, in the ninth year, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 39:1; Jeremias 52:4; Kings IV 25:1"> Son of man, write for yourself daily from this day, on which the king of Babylon set himself against Jerusalem, even from this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 11:3; Jezekiel 17:2; Jezekiel 20:49"> And speak a parable to the provoking house, and you shall say to them, Thus says the Lord; Set on the caldron, and pour water into it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Michaeas 3:2–3; Jezekiel 22:18–22; Matthew 7:2"> and put the pieces into it, every prime piece, the leg and shoulder taken off from the bones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 52:24–27; Jeremias 52:10; Jeremias 39:6"> which are taken from choice cattle, and burn the bones under them: her bones are boiled and cooked in the midst of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:2; Naum 3:1; Obdias 1:11"> Therefore thus says the Lord; O bloody city, the caldron in which there is scum, and the scum has not gone out of, she has brought it forth piece by piece, no lot has fallen upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 17:13; Deuteronomy 12:16; Job 16:18"> For her blood is in the midst of her; I have set it upon a smooth rock: I have not poured it out upon the earth, so that the earth should cover it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:21–22; Revelation 18:5–10; Jezekiel 22:30–31"> that my wrath should come up for complete vengeance to be taken: I set her blood upon a smooth rock, so as not to cover it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:6; Ambacum 2:12; Esias 31:9"> Therefore thus says the Lord, I will also make the firebrand great,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Lamentations 2:16; Jeremias 17:3; Lamentations 1:10"> and I will multiply the wood, and kindle the fire, that the flesh may be consumed, and the liquor boiled away;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremias 21:10; Malachias 4:1; Esias 1:25"> and that it may stand upon the coals, that her brass may be thoroughly heated, and be melted in the midst of her filthiness, and her scum may be consumed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 9:5; Jeremias 2:13; Jeremias 5:3"> and her abundant scum may not come forth of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:24; Jezekiel 5:13; Sophonias 3:2"> Her scum shall become shameful, because you did defile yourself: and what if you shall be purged no more until I have accomplished my wrath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 16:27; Jezekiel 18:30; Esias 3:11"> I the Lord have spoken; and it shall come, and I will do it; I will not delay, neither will I have any mercy: I will judge you, says the Lord, according to your ways, and according to your devices: therefore will I judge you according to your bloodshed, and according to your devices will I judge you, you unclean, notorious, and abundantly provoking one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 22:10; Jeremias 13:17; Song of Songs 7:10"> Son of man, behold I take from you the desire of your eyes by violence: you shall not lament, neither shall you weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Osee 9:4; Kings II 15:30; Leviticus 10:6"> You shall groan for blood, and have mourning upon your loins; your hair shall not be braided upon you, and your sandals shall be on your feet; you shall in no wise be comforted by their lips, and you shall not eat the bread of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:29–30"> And I spoke to the people in the morning, as he commanded me in the evening, and I did in the morning as it was commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:9; Jezekiel 37:18; Malachias 3:7–8"> And the people said to me, Will you not tell us what these things are that you do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then I said to them, The word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jezekiel 23:47; Jezekiel 23:25; Psalms 27:4"> Say to the house of Israel, Thus says the Lord; Behold, I will profane my sanctuary, the boast of your strength, the desire of your eyes, and for which your souls are concerned; and your sons and your daughters, whom you⌃ have left, shall fall by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 78:64; Jeremias 16:4–7; Jezekiel 24:16–17"> And you⌃ shall do as I have done: you⌃ shall not be comforted at their mouth, and you⌃ shall not eat the bread of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:10; Jezekiel 4:17; Leviticus 26:39"> And your hair shall be upon your head, and your shoes on your feet: neither shall you⌃ at all lament or weep; but you⌃ shall pine away in your iniquities, and shall comfort every one his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:3; Esias 20:3; Luke 11:29–30"> And Jezekiel shall be for a sign to you: according to all that I have done shall you⌃ do, when these things shall come; and you⌃ shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:21; Psalms 48:2; Jeremias 11:22"> And you, son of man, shall it not be in the day when I take their strength from them, the pride of their boasting, the desires of their eyes, and the pride of their soul, their sons and their daughters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; Jezekiel 33:21–22; Kings I 4:12–18"> that in that day he that escapes shall come to you, to tell it you in your ears?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:22; Jezekiel 29:21; Luke 21:15"> In that day your mouth shall be opened to him that escapes; you shall speak, and shall be no longer dumb: and you shall be for a sign to them, and they shall know that I am the Lord.</span>
