<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And after him rose up Nathan to prophesy in the time of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> As is the fat taken away from the peace offering, so was David chosen out of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He played with lions as with kids, and with bears as with lambs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Slew he not a giant, when he was yet but young? and did he not take away reproach from the people, when he lifted up his hand with the stone in the sling, and beat down the boasting of Goliath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For he called upon the most high Lord; and he gave him strength in his right hand to kill that mighty warrior, and set up the horn of his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So the people honored him with ten thousands, and praised him in the blessings of the Lord, in that he gave him a crown of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For he destroyed the enemies on every side, and brought to nothing the Philistines his adversaries, and brake their horn in sunder to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> In all his works he praised the Holy One most high with words of glory; with his whole heart he sung songs, and loved him that made him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He set singers also before the altar, that by their voices they might make sweet melody, and daily sing praises in their songs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> He beautified their feasts, and set in order the solemn times until the end, that they might praise his holy name, and that the temple might sound from morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Lord took away his sins, and exalted his horn for ever: he gave him a covenant of kings, and a throne of glory in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> After him rose up a wise son, and for his sake he lived at large.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Solomon reigned in a peaceful time, and was honored; for God made all quiet round about him, that he might build an house in his name, and prepare his sanctuary for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> How wise was you in your youth and, as a flood, filled with understanding!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Your soul covered the whole earth, and you filled it with dark parables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Your name went far to the islands; and for your peace you were beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The countries marveled at you for your songs, and proverbs, and parables, and interpretations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> By the name of the Lord God, which is called the Lord God of Israel, you did gather gold as tin and did multiply silver as lead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> You did bow your loins to women, and by your body you were brought into subjection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> You did stain your honor, and pollute your seed: so that you brought wrath upon your children, and was grieved for your folly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So the kingdom was divided, and out of Ephraim ruled a rebellious kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But the Lord will never leave off his mercy, neither shall any of his works perish, neither will he abolish the posterity of his elect, and the seed of him that loves him he will not take away: therefore he gave a remnant to Jacob, and out of him a root to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Thus rested Solomon with his fathers, and of his seed he left behind him Roboam, even the foolishness of the people, and one that had no understanding, who turned away the people through his counsel. There was also Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who caused Israel to sin, and showed Ephraim the way of sin:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And their sins were multiplied exceedingly, that they were driven out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For they sought out all wickedness, till the vengeance came upon them.</span>
