<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, neither delight in ungodly sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Though they multiply, rejoice not in them, except the fear of the Lord be with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Trust not you in their life, neither respect their multitude: for one that is just is better than a thousand; and better it is to die without children, than to have them that are ungodly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> For by one that has understanding shall the city be replenished: but the kindred of the wicked shall speedily become desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Many such things have I seen with my eyes, and my ear has heard greater things than these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> In the congregation of the ungodly shall a fire be kindled; and in a rebellious nation wrath is set on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He was not pacified toward the old giants, who fell away in the strength of their foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Neither spared he the place where Lot sojourned, but abhorred them for their pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He pitied not the people of perdition, who were taken away in their sins:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Nor the six hundred thousand footmen, who were gathered together in the hardness of their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And if there be one stiff-necked among the people, it is marvel if he escape unpunished: for mercy and wrath are with him; he is mighty to forgive, and to pour out displeasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> As his mercy is great, so is his correction also: he judges a man according to his works</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The sinner shall not escape with his spoils: and the patience of the godly shall not be frustrate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Make way for every work of mercy: for every man shall find according to his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The Lord hardened Pharaoh, that he should not know him, that his powerful works might be known to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> His mercy is manifest to every creature; and he has separated his light from the darkness with an adamant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Say not you, I will hide myself from the Lord: shall any remember me from above? I shall not be remembered among so many people: for what is my soul among such an infinite number of creatures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Behold, the heaven, and the heaven of heavens, the deep, and the earth, and all that therein is, shall be moved when he shall visit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The mountains also and foundations of the earth be shaken with trembling, when the Lord looks upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> No heart can think upon these things worthily: and who is able to conceive his ways?</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> It is a tempest which no man can see: for the most part of his works are hid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Who can declare the works of his justice? or who can endure them? for his covenant is afar off, and the trial of all things is in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He that lacks understanding will think upon vain things: and a foolish man erring imagines follies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> My son, listen to me, and learn knowledge, and mark my words with your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> I will show forth doctrine in weight, and declare his knowledge exactly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The works of the Lord are done in judgment from the beginning: and from the time he made them he disposed the parts thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> He garnished his works for ever, and in his hand are the chief of them to all generations: they neither labor, nor are weary, nor cease from their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> None of them hinders another, and they shall never disobey his word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> After this the Lord looked upon the earth, and filled it with his blessings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> With all manner of living things has he covered the face thereof; and they shall return into it again.</span>
