<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Instead of a friend become not an enemy; for thereby you shall inherit an ill name, shame, and reproach: even so shall a sinner that has a double tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Extol not yourself in the counsel of your own heart; that your soul be not torn in pieces as a bull straying alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> You shall eat up your leaves, and lose your fruit, and leave yourself as a dry tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> A wicked soul shall destroy him that has it, and shall make him to be laughed to scorn of his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Sweet language will multiply friends: and a fairspeaking tongue will increase kind greetings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Be in peace with many: nevertheless have but one counsellor of a thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> If you would get a friend, prove him first and be not hasty to credit him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For some man is a friend for his own occasion, and will not abide in the day of your trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And there is a friend, who being turned to enmity, and strife will discover your reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Again, some friend is a companion at the table, and will not continue in the day of your affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But in your prosperity he will be as yourself, and will be bold over your servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> If you be brought low, he will be against you, and will hide himself from your face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Separate yourself from your enemies, and take heed of your friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> A faithful friend is a strong defence: and he that has found such an one has found a treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Nothing does countervail a faithful friend, and his excellency is invaluable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> A faithful friend is the medicine of life; and they that fear the Lord shall find him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Whoso fears the Lord shall direct his friendship aright: for as he is, so shall his neighbor be also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> My son, gather instruction from your youth up: so shall you find wisdom till your old age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Come to her as one that plows and sows, and wait for her good fruits: for you shall not toil much in laboring about her, but you shall eat of her fruits right soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> She is very unpleasant to the unlearned: he that is without understanding will not remain with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> She will lie upon him as a mighty stone of trial; and he will cast her from him ere it be long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For wisdom is according to her name, and she is not manifest to many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Give ear, my son, receive my advice, and refuse not my counsel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And put your feet into her fetters, and your neck into her chain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Bow down your shoulder, and bear her, and be not grieved with her bonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Come to her with your whole heart, and keep her ways with all your power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Search, and seek, and she shall be made known to you: and when you have got hold of her, let her not go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For at the last you shall find her rest, and that shall be turned to your joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Then shall her fetters be a strong defence for you, and her chains a robe of glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For there is a golden ornament upon her, and her bands are purple lace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> You shall put her on as a robe of honor, and shall put her about you as a crown of joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> My son, if you will, you shall be taught: and if you will apply your mind, you shall be prudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> If you love to hear, you shall receive understanding: and if you bow your ear, you shall be wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Stand in the multitude of the elders; and cleave to him that is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Be willing to hear every godly discourse; and let not the parables of understanding escape you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And if you see a man of understanding, get you betimes to him, and let your foot wear the steps of his door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Let your mind be upon the ordinances of the Lord and meditate continually in his commandments: he shall establish your heart, and give you wisdom at your owns desire.</span>
