<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Have mercy upon us, O Lord God of all, and behold us:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And send your fear upon all the nations that seek not after you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Lift up your hand against the strange nations, and let them see your power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> As you were sanctified in us before them: so be you magnified among them before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And let them know you, as we have known you, that there is no God but only you, O God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Show new signs, and make other strange wonders: glorify your hand and your right arm, that they may set forth your wondrous works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Raise up indignation, and pour out wrath: take away the adversary, and destroy the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Sake the time short, remember the covenant, and let them declare your wonderful works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Let him that escapes be consumed by the rage of the fire; and let them perish that oppress the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Strike in sunder the heads of the rulers of the heathen, that say, There is none other but we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Gather all the tribes of Jacob together, and inherit you them, as from the beginning. Though I was the last to wake up, yet I received their inheritance as from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> O Lord, have mercy upon the people that is called by your name, and upon Israel, whom you have named your firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> O be merciful to Jerusalem, your holy city, the place of your rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Fill Sion with your unspeakable oracles, and your people with your glory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Give testimony to those that you have possessed from the beginning, and raise up prophets that have been in your name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Reward them that wait for you, and let your prophets be found faithful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> O Lord, hear the prayer of your servants, according to the blessing of Aaron over your people, that all they which dwell upon the earth may know that you are the Lord, the eternal God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The belly devours all meats, yet is one meat better than another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> As the palate tastes various kinds of venison: so does an heart of understanding false speeches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> A froward heart causes heaviness: but a man of experience will recompense him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> A woman will receive every man, yet is one daughter better than another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The beauty of a woman cheers the countenance, and a man loves nothing better.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> If there be kindness, meekness, and comfort, in her tongue, then is not her husband like other men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He that gets a wife begins a possession, a help like to himself, and a pillar of rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Where no hedge is, there the possession is spoiled: and he that has no wife will wander up and down mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Who will trust a thief well appointed, that skips from city to city? so who will believe a man that has no house, and lodges wherever the night takes him?</span>
