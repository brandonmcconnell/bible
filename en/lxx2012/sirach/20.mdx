<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> There is a reproof that is not comely: again, some man holds his tongue, and he is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> It is much better to reprove, than to be angry secretly: and he that confesses his fault shall be preserved from hurt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> How good is it, when you are reproved, to show repentance! for so shall you escape wilful sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> As is the lust of an eunuch to deflower a virgin; so is he that executes judgment with violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> There is one that keeps silence, and is found wise: and another by much babbling becomes hateful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Some man holds his tongue, because he has not to answer: and some keeps silence, knowing his time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> A wise man will hold his tongue till he see opportunity: but a babbler and a fool will regard no time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He that uses many words shall be abhorred; and he that takes to himself authority therein shall be hated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> There is a sinner that has good success in evil things; and there is a gain that turns to loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There is a gift that shall not profit you; and there is a gift whose recompence is double.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> There is an abasement because of glory; and there is that lifts up his head from a low estate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> There is that buys much for a little, and repays it sevenfold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> A wise man by his words makes him beloved: but the graces of fools shall be poured out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The gift of a fool shall do you no good when you have it; neither yet of the envious for his necessity: for he looks to receive many things for one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He gives little, and upbraids much; he opens his mouth like a crier; to day he lends, and to morrow will he ask it again: such an one is to be hated of God and man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The fool says, I have no friends, I have no thank for all my good deeds, and they that eat my bread speak evil of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> How often, and of how many shall he be laughed to scorn! for he knows not aright what it is to have; and it is all one to him as if he had it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> To slip upon a pavement is better than to slip with the tongue: so the fall of the wicked shall come speedily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> An unseasonable tale will always be in the mouth of the unwise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> A wise sentence shall be rejected when it comes out of a fool's mouth; for he will not speak it in due season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> There is that is hindered from sinning through lack: and when he takes rest, he shall not be troubled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> There is that destroys his own soul through bashfulness, and by accepting of persons overthrows himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> There is that for bashfulness promises to his friend, and makes him his enemy for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> A lie is a foul blot in a man, yet it is continually in the mouth of the untaught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> A thief is better than a man that is accustomed to lie: but they both shall have destruction to heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> The disposition of a liar is dishonourable, and his shame is ever with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> A wise man shall promote himself to honor with his words: and he that has understanding will please great men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He that tils his land shall increase his heap: and he that pleases great men shall get pardon for iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Presents and gifts blind the eyes of the wise, and stop up his mouth that he can’t reprove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Wisdom that is hid, and treasure that is hoarded up, what profit is in them both?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Better is he that hides his folly than a man that hides his wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Necessary patience in seeking the Lord is better than he that leads his life without a guide.</span>
