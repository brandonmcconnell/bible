<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that loves his son causes him often to feel the rod, that he may have joy of him in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He that chastises his son shall have joy in him, and shall rejoice of him among his acquaintance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> He that teaches his son grieves the enemy: and before his friends he shall rejoice of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Though his father die, yet he is as though he were not dead: for he has left one behind him that is like himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> While he lived, he saw and rejoiced in him: and when he died, he was not sorrowful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He left behind him an avenger against his enemies, and one that shall requite kindness to his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> He that makes too much of his son shall bind up his wounds; and his bowels will be troubled at every cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> An horse not broken becomes headstrong: and a child left to himself will be wilful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Cocker your child, and he shall make you afraid: play with him, and he will bring you to heaviness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Laugh not with him, lest you have sorrow with him, and lest you gnash your teeth in the end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Give him no liberty in his youth, and wink not at his follies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Bow down his neck while he is young, and beat him on the sides while he is a child, lest he wax stubborn, and be disobedient to you, and so bring sorrow to your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Chastise your son, and hold him to labor, lest his lewd behavior be an offense to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Better is the poor, being sound and strong of constitution, than a rich man that is afflicted in his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Health and good estate of body are above all gold, and a strong body above infinite wealth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> There is no riches above a sound body, and no joy above the joy of the heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Death is better than a bitter life or continual sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Delicates poured upon a mouth shut up are as messes of meat set upon a grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> What good does the offering to an idol? for neither can it eat nor smell: so is he that is persecuted of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He sees with his eyes and groanes, as an eunuch that embraces a virgin and sighs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Give not over your mind to heaviness, and afflict not yourself in your own counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The gladness of the heart is the life of man, and the joyfulness of a man prolongs his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Love your own soul, and comfort your heart, remove sorrow far from you: for sorrow has killed many, and there is no profit therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Envy and wrath shorten the life, and carefulness brings age before the time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> A cheerful and good heart will have a care of his meat and diet.</span>
