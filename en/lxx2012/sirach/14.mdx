<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Blessed is the man that has not slipped with his mouth, and is not pricked with the multitude of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Blessed is he whose conscience has not condemned him, and who is not fallen from his hope in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Riches are not comely for a niggard: and what should an envious man do with money?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He that gathers by defrauding his own soul gathers for others, that shall spend his goods riotously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He that is evil to himself, to whom will he be good? he shall not take pleasure in his goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> There is none worse than he that envies himself; and this is a recompence of his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And if he does good, he does it unwillingly; and at the last he will declare his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The envious man has a wicked eye; he turns away his face, and despises men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> A covetous man's eye is not satisfied with his portion; and the iniquity of the wicked dries up his soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> A wicked eye envies his bread, and he is a niggard at his table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> My son, according to your ability do good to yourself, and give the Lord his due offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Remember that death will not be long in coming, and that the covenant of the grave is not showed to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Do good to your friend before you die, and according to your ability stretch out your hand and give to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Defraud not yourself of the good day, and let not the part of a good desire overpass you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Shall you not leave your travails to another? and your labors to be divided by lot?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Give, and take, and sanctify your soul; for there is no seeking of dainties in the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All flesh waxes old as a garment: for the covenant from the beginning is, You shall die the death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> As of the green leaves on a thick tree, some fall, and some grow; so is the generation of flesh and blood, one comes to an end, and another is born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Every work rots and consumes away, and the worker thereof shall go withal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Blessed is the man that does meditate good things in wisdom, and that reasons of holy things by his understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He that considers her ways in his heart shall also have understanding in her secrets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Go after her as one that traces, and lie in wait in her ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He that pries in at her windows shall also listen at her doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He that does lodge near her house shall also fasten a pin in her walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> He shall pitch his tent near to her, and shall lodge in a lodging where good things are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He shall set his children under her shelter, and shall lodge under her branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> By her he shall be covered from heat, and in her glory shall he dwell.</span>
