<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> There shall no evil happen to him that fears the Lord; but in temptation even again he will deliver him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> A wise man hates not the law; but he that is an hypocrite therein is as a ship in a storm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> A man of understanding trusts in the law; and the law is faithful to him, as an oracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Prepare what to say, and so you shall be heard: and bind up instruction, and then make answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The heart of the foolish is like a cartwheel; and his thoughts are like a rolling axle tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> A stallion horse is as a mocking friend, he neighs under every one that sits upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Why does one day excel another, when as all the light of every day in the year is of the sun?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> By the knowledge of the Lord they were distinguished: and he altered seasons and feasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some of them has he made high days, and hallowed them, and some of them has he made ordinary days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And all men are from the ground, and Adam was created of earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> In much knowledge the Lord has divided them, and made their ways diverse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Some of them has he blessed and exalted and some of them he sanctified, and set near himself: but some of them has he cursed and brought low, and turned out of their places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> As the clay is in the potter's hand, to fashion it at his pleasure: so man is in the hand of him that made him, to render to them as likes him best.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Good is set against evil, and life against death: so is the godly against the sinner, and the sinner against the godly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So look upon all the works of the most High; and there are two and two, one against another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> I awaked up last of all, as one that gathers after the grape gatherers: by the blessing of the Lord I profited, and filled my winepress like a gatherer of grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Hear me, O you⌃ great men of the people, and listen with your ears, you⌃ rulers of the congregation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Give not your son and wife, your brother and friend, power over you while you live, and give not your goods to another: lest it repent you, and you entreat for the same again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> As long as you live and have breath in you, give not yourself over to any.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For better it is that your children should seek to you, than that you should stand to their courtesy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> In all your works keep to yourself the preeminence; leave not a stain in your honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> At the time when you shall end your days, and finish your life, distribute your inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Fodder, a wand, and burdens, are for the ass; and bread, correction, and work, for a servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> If you set your servant to labor, you shall find rest: but if you let him go idle, he shall seek liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> A yoke and a collar do bow the neck: so are tortures and torments for an evil servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Send him to labor, that he be not idle; for idleness teaches much evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Set him to work, as is fit for him: if he be not obedient, put on more heavy fetters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> But be not excessive toward any; and without discretion do nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> If you have a servant, let him be to you as yourself, because you have bought him with a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If you have a servant, entreat him as a brother: for you have need of him, as of your own soul: if you entreat him evil, and he run from you, which way will you go to seek him?</span>
