<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Or of iterating and speaking again that which you have heard; and of revealing of secrets. So shall you be truly shamefaced and find favor before all men. Of these things be not you ashamed, and accept no person to sin thereby:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Of the law of the most High, and his covenant; and of judgment to justify the ungodly;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Of reckoning with your partners and travellers; or of the gift of the heritage of friends;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Of exactness of balance and weights; or of getting much or little;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And of merchants' indifferent selling; of much correction of children; and to make the side of an evil servant to bleed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Sure keeping is good, where an evil wife is; and shut up, where many hands are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Deliver all things in number and weight; and put all in writing that you give out, or receive in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Be not ashamed to inform the unwise and foolish, and the extreme aged that contends with those that are young: thus shall you be truly learned, and approved of all men living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> A daughter is a wakeful care to a father; and the care for her takes away sleep: when she is young, lest she pass away the flower of her age; and being married, lest she should be hated:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In her virginity, lest she should be defiled and gotten with child in her father's house; and having an husband, lest she should misbehave herself; and when she is married, lest she should be barren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Keep a sure watch over a shameless daughter, lest she make you a laughing stock to your enemies, and a byword in the city, and a reproach among the people, and make you ashamed before the multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Behold not every body's beauty, and sit not in the midst of women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For from garments comes a moth, and from women wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Better is the churlishness of a man than a courteous woman, a woman, I say, which brings shame and reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I will now remember the works of the Lord, and declare the things that I have seen: In the words of the Lord are his works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The sun that gives light looks upon all things, and the work thereof is full of the glory of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The Lord has not given power to the saints to declare all his marvelous works, which the Almighty Lord firmly settled, that whatever is might be established for his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He seeks out the deep, and the heart, and considers their crafty devices: for the Lord knows all that may be known, and he sees the signs of the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He declares the things that are past, and for to come, and reveals the steps of hidden things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> No thought escapes him, neither any word is hidden from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He has garnished the excellent works of his wisdom, and he is from everlasting to everlasting: to him may nothing be added, neither can he be diminished, and he has no need of any counsellor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Oh how desirable are all his works! and that a man may see even to a spark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> All these things live and remain for ever for all uses, and they are all obedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> All things are double one against another: and he has made nothing imperfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> One thing establishes the good or another: and who shall be filled with beholding his glory?</span>
