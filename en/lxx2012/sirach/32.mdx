<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> If you be made the master of a feast, lift not yourself up, but be among them as one of the rest; take diligent care for them, and so sit down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And when you have done all your office, take your place, that you may be merry with them, and receive a crown for your well ordering of the feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Speak, you that are the elder, for it becomes you, but with sound judgment; and hinder not musick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Pour not out words where there is a musician, and show not forth wisdom out of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> A concert of musick in a banquet of wine is as a signet of carbuncle set in gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> As a signet of an emerald set in a work of gold, so is the melody of musick with pleasant wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Speak, young man, if there be need of you: and yet scarcely when you are twice asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Let your speech be short, comprehending much in few words; be as one that knows and yet holds his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If you be among great men, make not yourself equal with them; and when ancient men are in place, use not many words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Before the thunder goes lightning; and before a shamefaced man shall go favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Rise up betimes, and be not the last; but get you home without delay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> There take your pastime, and do what you will: but sin not by proud speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And for these things bless him that made you, and has replenished you with his good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Whoso fears the Lord will receive his discipline; and they that seek him early shall find favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He that seeks the law shall be filled therewith: but the hypocrite will be offended thereat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They that fear the Lord shall find judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> A sinful man will not be reproved, but finds an excuse according to his will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> A man of counsel will be considerate; but a strange and proud man is not daunted with fear, even when of himself he has done without counsel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Do nothing without advice; and when you have once done, repent not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Go not in a way wherein you may fall, and stumble not among the stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Be not confident in a plain way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And beware of your own children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In every good work trust your own soul; for this is the keeping of the commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He that believes in the Lord takes heed to the commandment; and he that trusts in him shall fare never the worse.</span>
