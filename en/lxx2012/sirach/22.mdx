<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A slothful man is compared to a filthy stone, and every one will hiss him out to his disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> A slothful man is compared to the filth of a dunghill: every man that takes it up will shake his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> An evil-nurtured man is the dishonor of his father that became the father of him: and a foolish daughter is born to his loss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> A wise daughter shall bring an inheritance to her husband: but she that lives dishonestly is her father's heaviness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> She that is bold dishonors both her father and her husband, but they both shall despise her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> A tale out of season is as musick in mourning: but stripes and correction of wisdom are never out of time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Whoso teaches a fool is as one that glues a potsherd together, and as he that wakes one from a sound sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> He that tells a tale to a fool speaks to one in a slumber: when he has told his tale, he will say, What is the matter?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> If children live honestly, and have wherewithal, they shall cover the baseness of their parents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But children, being haughty, through disdain and lack of nurture do stain the nobility of their kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Weep for the dead, for he has lost the light: and weep for the fool, for he lacks understanding: make little weeping for the dead, for he is at rest: but the life of the fool is worse than death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Seven days do men mourn for him that is dead; but for a fool and an ungodly man all the days of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Talk not much with a fool, and go not to him that has no understanding: beware of him, lest you have trouble, and you shall never be defiled with his fooleries: depart from him, and you shall find rest, and never be disquieted with madness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> What is heavier than lead? and what is the name thereof, but a fool?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Sand, and salt, and a mass of iron, is easier to bear, than a man without understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> As timber girded and bound together in a building can’t be loosed with shaking: so the heart that is established by advised counsel shall fear at no time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> A heart settled upon a thought of understanding is as a fair plaistering on the wall of a gallery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Pales set on an high place will never stand against the wind: so a fearful heart in the imagination of a fool can’t stand against any fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> He that pricks the eye will make tears to fall: and he that pricks the heart makes it to show her knowledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Whoso casts a stone at the birds frays them away: and he that upbraids his friend breaks friendship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Though you drew a sword at your friend, yet despair not: for there may be a returning to favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> If you have opened your mouth against your friend, fear not; for there may be a reconciliation: except for upbraiding, or pride, or disclosing of secrets, or a treacherous wound: for for these things every friend will depart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Be faithful to your neighbor in his poverty, that you may rejoice in his prosperity: abide stedfast to him in the time of his trouble, that you may be heir with him in his heritage: for a mean estate is not always to be contemned: nor the rich that is foolish to be had in admiration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> As the vapor and smoke of a furnace goes before the fire; so reviling before blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> I will not be ashamed to defend a friend; neither will I hide myself from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And if any evil happen to me by him, every one that hears it will beware of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Who shall set a watch before my mouth, and a seal of wisdom upon my lips, that I fall not suddenly by them, and that my tongue destroy me not?</span>
