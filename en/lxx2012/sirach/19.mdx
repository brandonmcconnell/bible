<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> A laboring man that A is given to drunkenness shall not be rich: and he that contemns small things shall fall by little and little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Wine and women will make men of understanding to fall away: and he that cleaves to harlots will become impudent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Moths and worms shall have him to heritage, and a bold man shall be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He that is hasty to give credit is lightminded; and he that sins shall offend against his own soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Whoso takes pleasure in wickedness shall be condemned: but he that resists pleasures crowns his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He that can rule his tongue shall live without strife; and he that hates babbling shall have less evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Rehearse not to another that which is told to you, and you shall fare never the worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Whether it be to friend or foe, talk not of other men's lives; and if you can without offense, reveal them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For he heard and observed you, and when time comes he will hate you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> If you have heard a word, let it die with you; and be bold, it will not burst you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> A fool travails with a word, as a woman in labor of a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> As an arrow that sticks in a man's thigh, so is a word within a fool's belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Admonish a friend, it may be he has not done it: and if he have done it, that he do it no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Admonish your friend, it may be he has not said it: and if he have, that he speak it not again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Admonish a friend: for many times it is a slander, and believe not every tale.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> There is one that slips in his speech, but not from his heart; and who is he that has not offended with his tongue?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Admonish your neighbor before you threaten him; and not being angry, give place to the law of the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The fear of the Lord is the first step to be accepted of him, and wisdom obtains his love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The knowledge of the commandments of the Lord is the doctrine of life: and they that do things that please him shall receive the fruit of the tree of immortality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The fear of the Lord is all wisdom; and in all wisdom is the performance of the law, and the knowledge of his omnipotency.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If a servant say to his master, I will not do as it pleases you; though afterward he do it, he angers him that nourishes him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The knowledge of wickedness is not wisdom, neither at any time the counsel of sinners prudence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> There is a wickedness, and the same an abomination; and there is a fool lacking in wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> He that has small understanding, and fears God, is better than one that has much wisdom, and transgresses the law of the most High.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> There is an exquisite subtilty, and the same is unjust; and there is one that turns aside to make judgment appear; and there is a wise man that justifies in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> There is a wicked man that hangs down his head sadly; but inwardly he is full of deceit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Casting down his countenance, and making as if he heard not: where he is not known, he will do you a mischief before you be aware.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And if for lack of power he be hindered from sinning, yet when he finds opportunity he will do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> A man may be known by his look, and one that has understanding by his countenance, when you meet him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> A man's attire, and excessive laughter, and gait, show what he is.</span>
