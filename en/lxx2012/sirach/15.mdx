<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> He that fears the Lord will do good, and he that has the knowledge of the law shall obtain her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And as a mother shall she meet him, and receive him as a wife married of a virgin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> With the bread of understanding shall she feed him, and give him the water of wisdom to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> He shall be stayed upon her, and shall not be moved; and shall rely upon her, and shall not be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> She shall exalt him above his neighbors, and in the midst of the congregation shall she open his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He shall find joy and a crown of gladness, and she shall cause him to inherit an everlasting name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But foolish men shall not attain to her, and sinners shall not see her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> For she is far from pride, and men that are liars can’t remember her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Praise is not seemly in the mouth of a sinner, for it was not sent him of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For praise shall be uttered in wisdom, and the Lord will prosper it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Say not you, It is through the Lord that I fell away: for you ought not to do the things that he hates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Say not you, He has caused me to err: for he has no need of the sinful man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The Lord hates all abomination; and they that fear God love it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> He himself made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his counsel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> If you will, to keep the commandments, and to perform acceptable faithfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He has set fire and water before you: stretch forth your hand to whether you will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Before man is life and death; and whether him likes shall be given him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For the wisdom of the Lord is great, and he is mighty in power, and sees all things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And his eyes are upon them that fear him, and he knows every work of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> He has commanded no man to do wickedly, neither has he given any man licence to sin.</span>
