<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Do no evil, so shall no harm come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Depart from the unjust, and iniquity shall turn away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> My son, sow not upon the furrows of unrighteousness, and you shall not reap them sevenfold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Seek not of the Lord preeminence, neither of the king the seat of honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> justify not yourself before the Lord; and boast not of your wisdom before the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Seek not to be judge, being not able to take away iniquity; lest at any time you fear the person of the mighty, an stumbling block in the way of your uprightness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Offend not against the multitude of a city, and then you shall not cast yourself down among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Bind not one sin upon another; for in one you shall not be unpunished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Say not, God will look upon the multitude of my oblations, and when I offer to the most high God, he will accept it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Be not faint-hearted when you make your prayer, and neglect not to give alms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Laugh no man to scorn in the bitterness of his soul: for there is one which humbles and exalts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Devise not a lie against your brother; neither do the like to your friend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Use not to make any manner of lie: for the custom thereof is not good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Use not many words in a multitude of elders, and make not much babbling when you pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Hate not laborious work, neither husbandry, which the most High has ordained.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Number not yourself among the multitude of sinners, but remember that wrath will not wait long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Humble yourself greatly: for the vengeance of the ungodly is fire and worms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Change not a friend for any good by no means; neither a faithful brother for the gold of Ophir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Forego not a wise and good woman: for her grace is above gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Whereas your servant works truly, entreat him not evil. nor the hireling that gives himself wholly for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Let your soul love a good servant, and defraud him not of liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Have you cattle? have an eye to them: and if they be for your profit, keep them with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Have you children? instruct them, and bow down their neck from their youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Have you daughters? have a care of their body, and show not yourself cheerful toward them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Marry your daughter, and so shall you have performed a weighty matter: but give her to a man of understanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Have you a wife after your mind? forsake her not: but give not yourself over to a light woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Honor your father with your whole heart, and forget not the sorrows of your mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Remember that you were begotten of them; and how can you recompense them the things that they have done for you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Fear the Lord with all your soul, and reverence his priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Love him that made you with all your strength, and forsake not his ministers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Fear the Lord, and honor the priest; and give him his portion, as it is commanded you; the first fruits, and the trespass offering, and the gift of the shoulders, and the sacrifice of sanctification, and the first fruits of the holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And stretch your hand to the poor, that your blessing may be perfected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> A gift has grace in the sight of every man living; and for the dead detain it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Fail not to be with them that weep, and mourn with them that mourn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Be not slow to visit the sick: fir that shall make you to be beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Whatsoever you take in hand, remember the end, and you shall never do amiss.</span>
