<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Deuteronomy 31:14; 1 Timothy 1:18"> And the days of David drew near that he should die: and he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. answered.">a</a></sup>addressed his son Solomon, saying, I go the way of all the earth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 1:6–7; Joshua 23:14; Ephesians 6:10"> but be you strong, and show yourself a man;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:9; Deuteronomy 17:18–20; Proverbs 3:1–4"> and keep the charge of the Lord your God, to walk in his ways, to keep the commandments and the ordinances and the judgments which are written in the law of Moses; that you may understand what you shall do in all things that I command you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings IV 20:3; Kings II 7:25; Kings III 8:25"> that the Lord may confirm his word which he spoke, saying, If your children shall take heed to their way to walk before me in truth with all their heart, I promise you, saying, there shall not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. be destroyed to you.">b</a></sup>fail you a man on the throne of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 3:27; Kings II 20:10; Kings II 18:5"> Moreover you know all that Joab the son of Saruia did to me, what he did to the two captains of the forces of Israel, to Abenner the son of Ner, and to Amessai the son of Jether, that he killed them, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. ordered.">c</a></sup>shed the blood of war in peace, and put innocent blood on his girdle that was about his loins, and on his sandal that was on his foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 2:9; Esias 57:21; Psalms 37:37"> Therefore you shall deal with him according to your wisdom, and you shall not bring down his grey hairs in peace to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. Hades.">d</a></sup>the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 17:27–29; Kings II 9:7; Kings II 19:31–40"> But you shall deal kindly with the sons of Berzelli the Galaadite, and they shall be among those that eat at your table; for thus they drew near to me when I fled from the face of your brother Abessalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 16:5–8; Kings III 2:36–46; Kings II 19:16–23"> And, behold, there is with you Semei the son of Gera, a Benjamite of Baurim: and he cursed me with a grievous curse in the day when I went into the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. camps.">e</a></sup>camp; and he came down to Jordan to meet me, and I swore to him by the Lord, saying, I will not put you to death with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 2:6; Genesis 42:38; Genesis 44:31"> But you shall by no means hold him guiltless, for you are a wise man, and will know what you shall do to him, and shall bring down his grey hairs with blood to the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:29; Acts 13:36; Kings III 1:21"> And David slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 5:4–5; Chronicles I 29:26–27"> And the days which David reigned over Israel were forty years; he reigned seven years in Chebron, and thirty-three years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 1:1; Chronicles I 29:23–25; Psalms 132:12"> And Solomon sat on the throne of his father David, and his kingdom was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. prepared.">f</a></sup>established greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings I 16:4–5; Kings III 1:5–10; Luke 10:5–6"> And Adonias the son of Aggith came in to Bersabee the mother of Solomon, and did obeisance to her: and she said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. Is your entrance peace?">g</a></sup>Do you enter peaceably? and he said, Peaceably:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings II 14:12; Luke 7:40"> I have business with you. And she said to him, Say on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:9–10; Chronicles I 28:5–7; Kings III 1:25"> And he said to her, You know that the kingdom was mine, and all Israel turned their face toward me for a king; but the kingdom was turned from me and became my brother's: for it was appointed to him from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 30:7; Psalms 132:10"> And now I make one request of you, do not turn away your face. And Bersabee said to him, Speak on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings II 12:8; Kings III 1:2–4; Kings II 3:7"> And he said to her, Speak, I pray you, to king Solomon, for he will not turn away his face from you, and let him give me Abisag the Somanite for a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 14:15"> And Bersabee said, Well; I will speak for you to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 45:9; Kings III 15:13; Leviticus 19:32"> And Bersabee went in to king Solomon to speak to him concerning Adonias; and the king rose up to meet her, and kissed her, and sat on the throne, and a throne was set for the mother of the king, and she sat on his right hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 7:7–11; Matthew 11:24; John 2:3–4"> And she said to him, I ask of you one little request; turn not away my face from you. And the king said to her, Ask, my mother, and I will not reject you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 1:3; Kings II 16:21–22"> And she said, Let, I pray you, Abisag the Somanite be given to Adonias your brother to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:2; Chronicles I 3:5; Matthew 20:22"> And king Solomon answered and said to his mother, And why have you asked Abisag for Adonias? ask for him the kingdom also; for he is my elder brother, and he has for his companion Abiathar the priest, and Joab the son of Saruia the commander-in-chief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ruth 1:17; Psalms 140:9; Psalms 64:8"> And king Solomon swore by the Lord, saying, God do so to me, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. add these things.">h</a></sup>more also, if it be not that Adonias has spoken this word against his own life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:10; Chronicles I 17:10; Chronicles I 17:17"> And now as the Lord lives who has established me, and set me on the throne of my father David, and he has made me a house, as the Lord spoke, this day shall Adonias be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings II 8:18; Kings III 2:46; Judges 8:20–21"> So king Solomon sent by the hand of Banaeas the son of Jodae, and he killed him, and Adonias died in that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 21:18; Kings I 22:20–23; Jeremias 1:1"> And the king said to Abiathar the priest, Depart you quickly to Anathoth to your farm, for you are<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. a man of death.">i</a></sup>worthy of death this day; but I will not kill you, because you have borne the ark of the covenant of the Lord before my father, and because you were afflicted in all things wherein my father was afflicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings I 2:27–36; Kings I 3:12–14; Jeremias 7:12–14"> And Solomon removed Abiathar from being a priest of the Lord, that the word of the Lord might be fulfilled, which he spoke<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, against.">j</a></sup>concerning the house of Heli in Selom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings III 1:50; Kings III 1:7; Kings II 18:2"> And the report came to Joab son of Saruia; for Joab had turned after Adonias, and he went not after Solomon: and Joab fled to the tabernacle of the Lord, and caught hold of the horns of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings III 2:25; Exodus 21:14; Kings III 2:46"> And it was told Solomon, saying, Joab has fled to the tabernacle of the Lord, and behold! he has hold of the horns of the altar. And king Solomon sent to Joab, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Gr. What has happened to you?">k</a></sup>What ails you, that you have fled to the altar? and Joab said, Because I was afraid of you, and fled for refuge to the Lord. And Solomon sent Banaeas son of Jodae, saying, Go and kill him, and bury him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And Banaeas son of Jodae came to Joab to the tabernacle of the Lord, and said to him, Thus says the king, Come forth. And Joab said, I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. do not.">l</a></sup>will not come forth, for I will die here. And Banaeas son of Jodae returned and spoke to the king, saying, Thus has Joab spoken, and thus has he answered me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:8–9; Numbers 35:33; Exodus 21:14"> And the king said to him, Go, and do to him as he has spoken, and kill him: and you shall bury him, and you shall remove this day the blood which he shed without cause, from me and from the house of my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 9:24; Judges 9:57; Psalms 7:16"> And the Lord has returned upon his own head the blood of his unrighteousness, inasmuch as he attacked two men more righteous and better than himself, and killed them with the sword, and my father David knew not of their blood, even Abenner the son of Ner the commander-in-chief of Israel, and Amessa the son of Jether the commander-in-chief of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Kings III 2:32; Luke 2:14"> And their blood is returned upon his head, and upon the head of his seed for ever: but to David, and his seed, and his house, and his throne, may there be peace for ever from the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 3:1; Joshua 15:61; Kings III 2:25"> So Banaeas son of Jodae went up, and attacked him, and killed him, and buried him in his house in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Chronicles I 24:3; Chronicles I 29:22"> And the king<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. gave.">m</a></sup>appointed Banaeas son of Jodae in his place over the host; and the kingdom was established in Jerusalem; and as for Sadoc the priest, the king appointed him to be high priest in the room of Abiathar. And Solomon son of David reigned over Israel and Juda in Jerusalem: and the Lord gave understanding to Solomon, and very much wisdom, and largeness of heart, as the sand by the sea-shore.</span>
