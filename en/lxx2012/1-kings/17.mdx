<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 4:25–26; James 5:17; Luke 1:17"> And Eliu the prophet, the Thesbite of Thesbae of Galaad, said to Achaab, As the Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, lives, before whom I stand,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. if there shall be, etc.">a</a></sup>there shall not be these years dew nor rain, except by the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. mouth of my word.">b</a></sup>word of my mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 18:1; Jeremias 7:1; Osee 1:1–2"> And the word of the Lord came to Eliu, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 12:6; Jeremias 36:26; Psalms 83:3"> Depart hence eastward, and hide you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. in.">c</a></sup>by the brook of Chorrath, that is before Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 17:9; Psalms 147:9; Job 38:41"> And it shall be that you shall drink water of the brook, and I will charge the ravens to feed you there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings III 19:9; Matthew 16:24; John 15:14"> And Eliu did according to the word of the Lord, and he sat by the brook of Chorrath before Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 78:23–24; Matthew 6:31–33; Jeremias 37:21"> And the ravens brought him loaves in the morning, and flesh in the evening and he drank water of the brook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 54:10; Esias 40:30–31"> And it came to pass after<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. days.">d</a></sup>some time, that the brook was dried up, because there had been no rain upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; Kings III 17:2; Hebrews 13:6"> And the word of the Lord came to Eliu, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 4:26; Obdias 1:20; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> Arise, and go to Sarepta of the Sidonian land: behold, I have there commanded a widow-woman to maintain you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:7; Genesis 24:17; Genesis 21:15"> And he arose and went to Sarepta, and came to the gate of the city: and, behold, a widow-woman was there gathering sticks; and Eliu cried after her, and said to her,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. take etc. into.">e</a></sup>Fetch me, I pray you, a little water in a vessel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. and I will etc.">f</a></sup>that I may drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 25:35–40; Kings III 18:4; Hebrews 13:2"> And she went to fetch it; and Eliu cried after her, and said, Bring me, I pray you, a morsel of the bread that is in your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 15:33–34; Kings III 17:1; Genesis 21:16"> And the woman said, As the Lord your God lives, I have not a cake, but only a handful of meal in the pitcher, and a little oil in a cruse, and, behold, I am going to gather two sticks, and I shall go in and dress it for myself and my children, and we shall eat it and die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 6:33; Malachias 3:10; Genesis 22:1–2"> And Eliu said to her, Be of good courage, go in and do according to your word: but make me thereof a little cake, and you shall bring it out to me first, and you shall make some for yourself and your children last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 15:36–38; Matthew 14:17–20; Kings IV 4:42–44"> For thus says the Lord, The pitcher of meal shall not fail, and the cruse of oil shall not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, be diminished, or fail of, etc.">g</a></sup>diminish, until the day that the Lord gives rain upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; Mark 12:43; Matthew 15:28"> And the woman went and did so, and did eat, she, and he, and her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:28–30; John 4:50–51; Luke 1:37"> And the pitcher of meal failed not, and the cruse of oil was not diminished, according to the word of the Lord which he spoke by the hand of Eliu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Peter 1:7; Daniel 5:23; John 11:14"> And it came to pass afterward, that the son of the woman the mistress of the house was sick; and his sickness was very severe, until there was no breath left in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 5:8; John 2:4; Kings IV 3:13"> And she said to Eliu, What have I to do with you, O man of God? have you come in to me to bring my sins to remembrance, and to kill my son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings IV 4:32; Kings IV 4:10; Acts 9:37"> And Eliu said to the woman, Give me your son. And he took him out of her bosom, and took him up to the chamber in which he himself lodged, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. caused him to sleep.">h</a></sup>laid him on the bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="James 5:15–18; Matthew 21:22; Joshua 7:8–9"> And Eliu cried aloud, and said, Alas, O Lord, the witness of the widow with whom I sojourn, you have wrought evil for her in slaying her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:10; Acts 9:40; Kings IV 4:33–35"> And he breathed on the child thrice, and called on the Lord, and said, O Lord my God, let, I pray you, the soul of this child return to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Revelation 11:11; Hebrews 11:35"> And it was so, and the child cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings IV 4:36–37; Acts 9:41; Luke 7:15"> and he brought him down from the upper chamber into the house, and gave him to his mother; and Eliu said, See, your son lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 16:30; John 2:11; John 3:2"> And the woman said to Eliu, Behold, I know that you are a man of God, and the word of the Lord in your mouth is true.</span>
