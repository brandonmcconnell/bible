<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 2:35; Kings III 2:26–27"> And Banaeas son of Jodae over the host; and Sadoc and Abiathar were priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 27:33; Kings II 15:37; Kings II 16:16"> And Ornia the son of Nathan was over the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. appointed ones.">a</a></sup>officers; and Zabuth son of Nathan was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. and Alex. insert 'priest.'">b</a></sup>the king's<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, companion.">c</a></sup>friend.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 27:1–15"> And Solomon had twelve officers over all Israel, to provide for the king and his household; each one's turn came to supply for a month in the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 24:33; Judges 19:1; Judges 17:1"> And these were their names: Been the son of Or in the mount of Ephraim, one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 21:16; Kings I 6:20; Kings I 6:12"> The son of Dacar, in Makes, and in Salabin, and Baethsamys, and Elon as far as Bethanan, one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 15:35; Joshua 12:17; Joshua 17:2"> The son of Esdi in Araboth; his was Socho, and all the land of Opher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 11:2; Joshua 12:23; Joshua 17:11"> All Nephthador belonged to the son of Aminadab, Tephath daughter of Solomon was his wife, one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19; Joshua 3:16"> Bana son of Achiluth had Ithaanach, and Mageddo, and his was the whole house of San which was by Sesathan below Esrae, and from Bethsan as far as Sabelmaula, as far as Maeber Lucam, one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:4; Numbers 32:41; Kings III 22:3"> The son of Naber in Raboth Galaad, to him fell the lot of Ergab in Basan, sixty great cities with walls, and brazen bars, one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 13:26; Kings II 17:24; Kings II 2:8"> Achinadab son of Saddo, had Maanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 19:32–39; Kings II 15:27; Kings I 18:18"> Achimaas was in Nephthalim, and he took Basemmath daughter of Solomon to wife, one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 19:24–31; Kings II 15:32"> Baana son of Chusi, in Aser and in Baaloth, one,</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 1:8; Joshua 18:20–28; Zacharias 12:13"> Semei son of Ela, in Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:26–17; Joshua 13:9–12; Numbers 21:21–35"> Gaber son of Adai in the land of Gad, the land of Seon king of Esebon, and of Og king of Basan, and one officer in the land of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Kings III 3:8; Genesis 32:12"> 27 And thus the officers provided king Solomon: and they execute every one in his month all the orders for the table of the king, they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. change.">d</a></sup>omit nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Joshua 1:4; Psalms 68:29"> 28 And they carried the barley and the straw for the horses and the chariots to the place where the king might be, each according to his charge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And these were the requisite supplies for Solomon: in one day thirty measures of fine flour, and sixty measures of fine pounded meal,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:17–18"> and ten choice calves, and twenty pastured oxen, and one hundred sheep, besides stags, and choice fatted does.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Chronicles I 22:9; Psalms 72:11; Psalms 72:3"> For he had dominion on this side the river, and he was at peace on all sides round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings III 3:12; James 3:17; Kings III 4:20"> And the Lord gave understanding to Solomon, and very much wisdom, and enlargement of heart, as the sand on the seashore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Daniel 5:11–12; Genesis 25:6; Daniel 1:20"> And Solomon abounded greatly beyond the wisdom of all the ancients, and beyond all the wise men of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Chronicles I 2:6; Chronicles I 15:19; Kings III 3:12"> And he was wiser than all other men: and he was wiser than Gaethan the Zarite, and than Aenan, and than Chalcad and Darala the son of Mal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Song of Songs 1:1–17; Matthew 13:35; Proverbs 1:1–31"> And Solomon spoke three thousand proverbs, and his songs were five thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings IV 19:23; Psalms 51:7; Psalms 92:12"> And he spoke of trees, from the cedar in Libanus even to the hyssop which comes out through the wall: he spoke also of cattle, and of birds, and of reptiles, and of fishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Chronicles II 9:23; Kings III 10:1; Chronicles II 9:1"> And all the nations came to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and ambassadors from all the kings of the earth, as many as heard of his wisdom.<br /><sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Here the chapter ends, according to Heb. and Alex.">e</a></sup>And Solomon took to himself the daughter of Pharao to wife, and brought her into the city of David until he had finished the house of the Lord, and his own house, and the wall of Jerusalem. Then went up Pharao the king of Egypt, and took Gazer, and burnt it and the Chananite dwelling in Mergab; and Pharao gave them as a dowry to his daughter the wife of Solomon: and Solomon rebuilt Gazer.</span>
