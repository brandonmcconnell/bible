<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:17; Proverbs 6:24; Nehemiah 13:23–27"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This verse, or part verse is not numbered in Brenton's text. Verse 1 is in the position below, following verse 3.">a</a></sup>And king Solomon was a lover of women.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Note that the verses between this point and the beginning of verse 9 are in the order chosen by Brenton, and are marked thus: 1) etc. as they are numbered by Brenton.">b</a></sup>3) And he had seven hundred wives, princesses, and three hundred concubines. 1) And he took strange women, as well as the daughter of Pharao, Moabitish, Ammanitish women, Syrians and Idumeans, Chettites, and Amorites;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 34:16; Deuteronomy 7:3–4; Joshua 23:12–13"> of the nations concerning whom the Lord forbade the children of Israel, saying, You⌃ shall not go in to them, and they shall not come in to you, lest they turn away your hearts after their idols: Solomon clave to these in love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 9:4; Kings III 8:61; Kings III 11:6"> And it came to pass in the time of the old age of Solomon, that his heart was not perfect with the Lord his God, as was the heart of David his father. 3 And the strange women turned away his heart after their gods. 7 Then Solomon built a high place to Chamos the idol of Moab, and to their king the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Heb.">c</a></sup>idol of the children of Ammon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 2:13; Kings III 11:7; Kings IV 23:13"> and to Astarte the abomination of the Sidonians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:8; Joshua 14:14"> And thus he acted towards all his strange wives, who burnt incense and sacrificed to their idols. 6 And Solomon did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord: he went not after the Lord, as David his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings III 9:2; Kings III 3:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> And the Lord was angry with Solomon, because he turned away his heart from the Lord God of Israel, who had appeared twice to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 6:12–13; Chronicles II 7:17–22; Kings III 9:4–7"> and charged him concerning this matter, by no means to go after other gods, but to take heed to do what the Lord God commanded him; neither was his heart perfect with the Lord, according to the heart of David his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings III 12:15–16; Kings III 11:31; Kings IV 17:21"> And the Lord said to Solomon, Because it has been thus with you, and you have not kept my commandments and my ordinances which I commanded you, I will surely rend your kingdom out of your hand, and give it to your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings IV 20:19; Kings I 9:4–5; Kings IV 22:19–20"> Only in your days I will not do<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. them.">d</a></sup>it for David your father's sake: but I will take it out of the hand of your son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings III 12:20; Deuteronomy 12:11; Kings III 11:32"> Only I will not take away the whole kingdom: I will give one tribe to your son for David my servant's sake, and for the sake of Jerusalem, the city which I have chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:26; Psalms 89:30–34; Esias 13:17"> And the Lord raised up an enemy to Solomon, Ader the Idumaean, and Esrom son of Eliadae who lived in Raama, and Adadezer king of Suba his master; (and men gathered to him, and he was head of the conspiracy, and he seized on Damasec,) and they were adversaries to Israel all the days of Solomon: and Ader the Idumaean was of the seed royal in Idumaea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings II 8:14; Chronicles I 18:12–13; Deuteronomy 20:13"> And it happened, that while David was utterly destroying Edom, while Joab captain of the host was going to bury the dead, when they killed every male in Idumaea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> (for Joab and all Israel abode there six months in Idumaea, until he utterly destroyed every male in Idumaea;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 2:13–14; Exodus 2:1–10; Kings IV 11:2"> that Ader ran away, he and all the Idumaeans of the servants of his father with him; and they went into Egypt; and Ader was then a little child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 10:12; Deuteronomy 33:2; Deuteronomy 1:1"> And there rise up men out of the city of Madiam, and they come to Pharan, and take men with them, and come to Pharao king of Egypt: and Ader went in to Pharao, and he gave him a house, and appointed him provision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 39:4; Jeremias 43:7–9; Genesis 39:21"> And Ader found great favor in the sight of Pharao, and he gave him his wife's sister in marriage, the elder sister of Thekemina.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:7; Kings I 1:24"> And the sister of Thekemina bore to him, even to Ader, Ganebath her son; and Thekemina brought him up in the midst of the sons of Pharao, and Ganebath was in the midst of the sons of Pharao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings III 2:10; Exodus 4:19; Matthew 2:20"> And Ader heard in Egypt that David slept with his fathers, and that Joab the captain of the host was dead; and Ader said to Pharao, Let me go, and I will return to my country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 18:22–23; Luke 22:35; Mark 14:31"> And Pharao said to Ader,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. wherein are you lacking?.">e</a></sup>What lack you with me? that behold! you seek to depart to your country? and Ader said to him, By all means let me go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings III 11:14; Kings II 8:3; Kings II 10:15–18"> So Ader returned to his country; this is the mischief which Ader did, and he was a bitter enemy of Israel, and he reigned in the land of Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Chronicles II 13:6; Kings III 12:2; Kings III 11:28"> And Jeroboam the son of Nabat, the Ephrathite of Sarira, the son of a widow, was servant of Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Kings III 9:24; Kings III 9:15; Proverbs 30:32"> And this was the occasion<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. of his lifting.">f</a></sup>of his lifting up his hands against king Solomon: now king Solomon built the citadel, he completed the fortification of the city of David his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Kings II 19:20; Judges 1:22–23"> And the man Jeroboam was very strong; and Solomon saw the young man that he was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. a man of works.">g</a></sup>active, and he set him over the levies of the house of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Kings III 12:15; Kings III 14:2; Chronicles II 9:29"> And it came to pass at that time, that Jeroboam went forth from Jerusalem, and Achia the Selonite the prophet found him in the way, and caused him to turn aside out of the way: and Achia was clad with a new garment, and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. both.">h</a></sup>two were alone in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings I 15:27–28; Kings I 24:4–5"> And Achia laid hold of his new garment that was upon him, and tore it into twelve pieces:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings III 11:11–12"> and he said to Jeroboam, Take to yourself ten pieces, for thus says the Lord God of Israel, Behold, I rend the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon, and will give you ten<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. sceptres. See Heb.">i</a></sup>tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings III 11:13; Kings III 12:20–21; Kings III 14:21"> Yet he shall have two tribes, for my servant David's sake, and for the sake of Jerusalem, the city which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings III 11:9; Kings III 11:5–7; Kings III 9:5–7"> Because he forsook me, and sacrificed to Astarte the abomination of the Sidonians, and to Chamos, and to the idols of Moab, and to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, Moloch, or Milcom.">j</a></sup>their king the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or, provocation.">k</a></sup>abomination of the children of Ammon, and he walked not in my ways, to do that which was right before me, as David his father did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings III 11:12–13; Kings III 11:31; Job 11:6"> Howbeit I will not take the whole kingdom out of his hand, (for I will certainly resist him all the days of his life,) for David my servant's sake, whom I have chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings III 12:20; Kings III 11:12; Exodus 20:5–6"> But I will take the kingdom out of the hand of his son, and give you ten tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings IV 8:19; Kings III 11:13; Kings III 15:4"> But to his son I will give the two remaining tribes, that my servant David may have an establishment continually before me in Jerusalem, the city which I have chosen for myself to put my name there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:26; Kings III 11:26; Kings II 3:21"> And I will take you, and you shall reign<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Gr. in all, or among all which, etc.">l</a></sup>as your soul desires, and you shall be king over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Kings II 7:11; Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 31:8"> And it shall come to pass, if you will keep all the commandments that I shall give you, and will walk in my ways, and do that which is right before me, to keep my ordinances and my commandments, as David my servant did, that I will be with you, and will build you a sure house, as I built to David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Chronicles II 12:2–9; Esias 14:24–27; Lamentations 3:37"> And Solomon sought to kill Jeroboam: but he arose and fled into Egypt, to Susakim king of Egypt, and he was in Egypt until Solomon died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Chronicles II 9:29–31"> And the rest of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. words.">m</a></sup>history of Solomon, and all that he did, and all his wisdom, behold are not these things written in the book of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Gr. words or things.">n</a></sup>life of Solomon?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Kings III 2:11"> And the days during which Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel were forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Kings IV 21:18; Kings III 2:10; Matthew 1:7"> And Solomon slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David his father. And it came to pass when Jeroboam son of Nabat heard of it, even while he was yet in Egypt as he fled from the face of Solomon and lived in Egypt, he straightway comes into his own city, into the land of Sarira in the mount of Ephraim. And king Solomon slept with his fathers, and Roboam his son reigned in his stead.</span>
