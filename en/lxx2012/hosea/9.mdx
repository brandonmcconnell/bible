<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Osee 10:5; Osee 4:12; Jeremias 44:17"> Rejoice not, O Israel, neither make merry, as other nations: for you have gone a-whoring from your God; you have loved gifts upon every threshing floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Osee 2:9; Michaeas 6:13–16; Joel 1:3–7"> The threshing floor and wine-press knew them not, and the wine disappointed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 4:13; Osee 8:13; Leviticus 25:23"> They lived not in the Lord's land: Ephraim lived in Egypt, and they shall eat unclean things among the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Osee 8:13; Deuteronomy 26:14; Jeremias 6:20"> They have not offered wine to the Lord, neither have their sacrifices been sweet to him, but as the bread of mourning to them; all that eat them shall be defiled; for their bread for their soul shall not enter into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 10:3; Jeremias 5:31; Osee 2:11"> What will you⌃ do in the day of the general assembly, and in the day of the feast of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Osee 10:8; Esias 5:6; Esias 7:23"> Therefore, behold, they go forth from the trouble of Egypt, and Memphis shall receive them, and Machmas shall bury them: as for their silver, destruction shall inherit it; thorns shall be in their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 26:24–25; Michaeas 7:4; Kings IV 9:11"> The days of vengeance are come, the days of your recompense are come; and Israel shall be afflicted as the prophet that is mad, as a man deranged: by reason of the multitude of your iniquities your madness has abounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Osee 5:1; Jezekiel 3:17; Esias 62:6"> The watchman of Ephraim was with God: the prophet is a crooked snare in all his ways: they have established madness in the house of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Osee 10:9; Judges 19:16–21; Osee 8:13"> They have corrupted themselves according to the days of the hill: he will remember their iniquities, he will take vengeance on their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 11:13; Osee 4:14; Numbers 25:1–18"> I found Israel as grapes in the wilderness, and I saw their fathers as an early watchman in a fig tree: they went in to Beel-phegor, and were shamefully estranged, and the abominable became as the beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:18; Osee 9:14; Osee 4:7"> Ephraim has flown away as a bird; their glories from the birth, and the travail, and the conception.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Osee 7:13; Deuteronomy 32:25; Deuteronomy 31:17"> For even if they should rear their children, yet shall they be utterly bereaved: therefore also there is woe to them, though my flesh is of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 26:1–21; Jezekiel 27:3; Osee 13:8"> Ephraim, even as I saw, gave their children for a prey; yes, Ephraim was ready to bring out his children to slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:29; 1 Corinthians 7:26; Mark 13:17"> Give them, O Lord: what will you give them? a miscarrying womb, and dry breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Osee 4:15; Esias 1:23; Amos 5:5"> All their wickedness is in Galgal: for there I hated them: because of the wickedness of their practices, I will cast them out of my house, I will not love them any more: all their princes are disobedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Osee 9:11–13; Jezekiel 24:21; Esias 5:24"> Ephraim is sick, he is dried up at his roots, he shall in no wise any more bear fruit: therefore even if they should beget children, I will kill the desired fruit of their womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Osee 7:13; Deuteronomy 28:64–65; Acts 3:23"> God shall reject them, because they have not listened to him: and they shall be wanderers among the nations.</span>
