<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Osee 7:13; Michaeas 7:3–7; Osee 6:4"> When I have healed Israel, then shall the iniquity of Ephraim be revealed, and the wickedness of Samaria; for they have wrought falsehood: and a thief shall come in to him, even a robber spoiling in his way;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:2; Amos 8:7; Jeremias 14:10"> that they may concert together as men singing in their heart: I remember all their wickedness: now have their own counsels compassed them about; they came before my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Michaeas 7:3; Romans 1:32; Jeremias 28:1–4"> They gladdened kings with their wickedness, and princes with their lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremias 9:2; James 4:4; Jeremias 5:7–8"> They are all adulterers, as an oven glowing with flame for hot-baking, on account of the kneading of the dough, until it is leavened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 28:1; Matthew 14:6; Genesis 40:20"> In the days of our kings, the princes began to be inflamed with wine: he stretched out his hand with pestilent fellows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Osee 7:7; Osee 7:4; Kings I 19:11–15"> Therefore their hearts are inflamed as an oven, while they rage all the night: Ephraim is satisfied with sleep; the morning is come; he is burnt up as a flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 15:25; Kings IV 15:30; Kings IV 15:10"> They are all heated like an oven, and have devoured their judges: all their kings are fallen; there was not among them one that called on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:35; Ezra 9:12; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> Ephraim is mixed among his people; Ephraim became a cake not turned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Osee 8:7; Esias 42:22–25; Kings IV 13:3–7"> Strangers devoured his strength, and he knew it not; and grey hairs came upon him, and he knew it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Osee 5:5; Esias 9:13; Amos 4:6–13"> And the pride of Israel shall be brought down before his face: yet they have not returned to the Lord their God, neither have they diligently sought him for all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Osee 12:1; Osee 5:13; Osee 11:11"> And Ephraim was as a silly dove, not having a heart: he called to Egypt, and they went to the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jezekiel 12:13; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 31:16–29"> Whenever they shall go, I will cast my net upon them; I will bring them down as the birds of the sky, I will chasten them with the rumor of their coming affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Osee 9:12; Osee 11:12; Esias 63:8"> Woe to them! for they have started aside from me: they are cowards; for they have sinned against me: yet I redeemed them, but they spoke falsehoods against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 4:3; Job 35:9–10; Jeremias 3:10"> And their hearts did not cry to me, but they howled on their beds: they pined for oil and wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Naum 1:9; Psalms 62:3; Jeremias 17:9"> They were instructed by me, and I strengthened their arms; and they devised evils against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 78:57; Osee 9:3; Jezekiel 23:32"> They turned aside to that which is not, they became as a bent bow: their princes shall fall by the sword, by reason of the unbridled state of their tongue: this is their setting at nothing in the land of Egypt.</span>
