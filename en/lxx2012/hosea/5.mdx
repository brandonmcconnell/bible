<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Osee 9:15; Esias 29:15; Osee 6:9"> which they that hunt the prey have fixed: but I will correct you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 3:2; Hebrews 4:13; Osee 13:1"> I know Ephraim, and Israel is not far from me: for now Ephraim has gone grievously a-whoring, Israel is defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Osee 4:12; 1 John 2:3–4; Osee 4:1"> They have not framed their counsels to return to their God, for the spirit of fornication is in them, and they have not known the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Osee 7:10; Amos 5:2; Jezekiel 23:31–35"> And the pride of Israel shall be brought low before his face; and Israel and Ephraim shall fall in their iniquities; and Judas also shall fall with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Michaeas 6:6–7; John 7:34; Proverbs 1:28"> They shall go with sheep and calves diligently to seek the Lord; but they shall not find him, for he has withdrawn himself from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Osee 6:7; Esias 48:8; Jeremias 3:20"> For they have forsaken the Lord; for strange children have been born to them: now shall the cankerworm devour them and their heritages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Osee 4:15; Osee 9:9; Kings I 15:34"> Blow you⌃ the trumpet on the hills, sound aloud on the heights: proclaim in the house of On, Benjamin is amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zacharias 1:6; Esias 46:10; Osee 9:11–17"> Ephraim has come to nothing in the days of reproof: in the tribes of Israel I have shown faithful dealings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Deuteronomy 27:17; Psalms 93:3–4"> The princes of Juda became as they that removed the bounds: I will pour out upon them my fury as water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Michaeas 6:16; Kings III 12:26–33"> Ephraim altogether prevailed against his adversary, he trod judgment under foot, for he began to go after vanities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 51:8; Job 13:28; Esias 50:9"> Therefore I will be as consternation to Ephraim, and as a goad to the house of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Osee 7:11; Osee 12:1; Osee 10:6"> And Ephraim saw his disease, and Judas his pain; then Ephraim went to the Assyrians, and sent ambassadors to king Jarim: but he could not heal you, and your pain shall in nowise cease from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 7:2; Michaeas 5:8; Osee 13:7–8"> Therefore I am as a panther to Ephraim, and as a lion to the house of Juda: and I will tear, and go away; and I will take, and there shall be none to deliver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 26:16; Jeremias 29:12–14; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> I will go and return to my place, until they are brought to nothing, and then shall they seek my face.</span>
