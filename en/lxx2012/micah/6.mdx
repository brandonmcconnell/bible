<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 22:29; Deuteronomy 32:1; Michaeas 1:2"> Hear now a word: the Lord God has said; Arise, plead with the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Osee 4:1; Osee 12:2; Esias 1:18"> Hear you⌃, O mountains, the controversy of the Lord, and you⌃ valleys even the foundations of the earth: for the Lord has a controversy with his people, and will plead with Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 2:5; Esias 43:22–23; Psalms 81:13"> O my people, what have I done to you? or wherein have I grieved you? or wherein have I troubled you? answer me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 12:51; Deuteronomy 7:8; Amos 2:10"> For I brought tee up out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed you out of the house of bondage, and sent before you Moses, and Aaron, and Mariam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 5:9–10; Numbers 25:1; Judges 5:11"> O my people, remember now, what counsel Balac king of Moab took against you, and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him, from the reeds to Galgal; that the righteousness of the Lord might be known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 51:16–17; Romans 10:2–3; Psalms 40:6–8"> Wherewithal shall I reach the Lord, and lay hold of my God most high? shall I reach him by whole burnt offerings, by calves of a year old?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 16:3; Kings I 15:22; Psalms 51:16"> Will the Lord accept thousands of rams, or ten thousands of fat goats? should I give my firstborn for ungodliness, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12–13; Proverbs 21:3; Colossians 3:12"> Has it not been told you, O man, what is good? or what does the Lord require of you, but to do justice, and love mercy, and be ready to walk with the Lord your God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Osee 14:9; Esias 30:27"> The Lord's voice shall be proclaimed in the city, and he shall save those that fear his name: hear, O tribe; and who shall order the city?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 3:10; Amos 8:5–6; Jeremias 5:26–27"> Is there not fire, and the house of the wicked heaping up wicked treasures, and that with the pride of unrighteousness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Osee 12:7; Leviticus 19:36; Proverbs 16:11"> Shall the wicked be justified by the balanced, or deceitful weights in the bag,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 1:23; Jeremias 9:8; Osee 7:13"> whereby they have accumulated their ungodly wealth, and they that dwell in the city have uttered falsehoods, and their tongue has been exalted in their mouth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:23; Deuteronomy 28:21–22; Osee 13:16"> Therefore will I begin to strike you; I will destroy you in your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Osee 4:10; Leviticus 26:26; Esias 65:13"> You shall eat, and shall not be satisfied; and there shall be darkness upon you; and he shall depart from you, and you shall not escape; and all that shall escape shall be delivered over to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Sophonias 1:13; Amos 5:11; Jeremias 12:13"> You shall sow, but you shall not reap; you shall press the olive, but you shall not anoint yourself with oil; and shall make wine, but you⌃ shall drink no wine: and the ordinances of my people shall be utterly abolished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremias 51:51; Kings III 16:25–33; Jeremias 7:24"> For you have kept the statues of Zambri, and done all the works of the house of Achaab; and you⌃ have walked in their ways, that I might deliver you to utter destruction, and those that inhabit the city to hissing: and you⌃ shall bear the reproach of nations.</span>
