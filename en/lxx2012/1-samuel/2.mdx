<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 1:46–56; Psalms 13:5; Esias 12:2–3"> My heart is established in the Lord, my horn is exalted in my God; my mouth is enlarged over my enemies, I have rejoiced in your salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings II 22:32; Esias 44:6; Psalms 86:8"> For there is none holy as the Lord, and there is none righteous as our God; there is none holy besides you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 8:13; Kings III 8:39; Proverbs 24:12"> Boast not, and utter not high things; let not high-sounding words come out of your mouth, for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and God prepares his own designs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 76:3; Psalms 37:15; Psalms 46:9"> The bow of the mighty has waxed feeble, and the weak have girded themselves with strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 15:9; Luke 1:53; Psalms 113:9"> They that were full of bread are brought low; and the hungry have forsaken the land; for the barren has born seven, and she that abounded in children has waxed feeble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Esias 26:19; Revelation 1:18"> The Lord kills and makes alive; he brings down to the grave, and brings up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 75:7; Job 5:11; Deuteronomy 8:17–18"> The Lord makes poor, and makes rich; he brings low, and lifts up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 113:7–8; Daniel 2:48; James 2:5"> He lifts up the poor from the earth, and raises the needy from the dunghill; to seat him with the princes of the people, and causing them to inherit the throne of glory:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 121:3; Psalms 91:11–12; Psalms 33:16–17"> granting his petition to him that prays; and he blesses the years of the righteous, for by strength can’t man prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 2:9; Psalms 89:24; Psalms 18:13–14"> The Lord will weaken his adversary; the Lord is holy. Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom, nor let the mighty man boast in his strength, and let not the rich man boast in his wealth; but let him that boasts boast in this, to understand and know the Lord,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Perhaps 'and the Lord executes,' etc. Comp. Jer. 9:24">a</a></sup>and to execute judgment and justice in the midst of the earth. The Lord has gone up to the heavens, and has thundered: he will judge the extremities of the earth, and he gives strength to our kings, and will exalt the horn of his Christ. And she left him there before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 3:1; Kings I 2:18; Kings I 1:28"> and departed to Armathaim: and the child ministered in the presence of the Lord before Heli the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 1:28–30; Jeremias 2:8; Judges 2:10"> And the sons of Heli the priest were evil sons, not knowing the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 7:29–34"> And the priest's claim from every one of the people that sacrificed was this: the servant of the priest came when the flesh was in seething, and a flesh-hook of three teeth was in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 29:27–28; Esias 56:11; Kings I 2:29"> And he struck it into the great caldron, or into the brazen vessel, or into the pot, and whatever came up with the flesh-hook, the priest took for himself: so they did to all Israel that came to sacrifice to the Lord in Selom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16; Leviticus 3:3–5; Romans 16:18"> And before the fat was burnt for a sweet savor, the servant of the priest would come, and say to the man that sacrificed, Give flesh to roast for the priest, and I will by no means take of you sodden flesh out of the caldron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 7:23–25; Leviticus 3:16; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> And if the man that sacrificed said, First let the fat be burned, as it is fit, and take for yourself of all things which your soul desires: then he would say, Nay, for you shall give it me now; and if not I will take it by force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachias 2:7–9; Genesis 13:13; Malachias 2:13"> So the sin of the young men was very great before the Lord, for they set at nothing the offering of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings I 3:1; Exodus 28:4; Kings I 2:11"> And Samuel ministered before the Lord, a child girded with a linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Kings I 1:3; Kings I 1:21; Exodus 23:14"> And his mother made him a little doublet, and brought it to him from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. days to days.">b</a></sup>year to year, in her going up in company with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings I 1:27–28; Luke 2:34; Ruth 4:11"> And Heli blessed Helcana and his wife, saying The Lord recompense to you seed of this woman, in return for the loan which you have lent to the Lord: and the man returned to his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 2:40; Kings I 3:19; Genesis 21:1"> And the Lord visited Anna, and she bore yet three sons, and two daughters. And the child Samuel grew before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; Kings I 2:13–17; Jeremias 7:9–10"> And Heli was very old, and he heard what his sons did to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremias 8:12; Philippians 3:19; Acts 9:4"> And he said to them, Why do you⌃ according to this thing, which I hear from the mouth of all the people of the Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachias 2:8; 1 Timothy 3:7; Acts 6:3"> Nay my sons, for the report which I hear is not good; do not so, for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, the reports which I hear of the people not serving, etc. Comp. 1 Ch. 13. .4">c</a></sup>the reports which I hear are not good, so that the people do not serve God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings I 3:14; Joshua 11:20; Hebrews 10:26"> If a man should at all sin against another, then shall they pray for him to the Lord; but if a man sin against the Lord, who shall entreat for him? But they listened not to the voice of their father, because the Lord would by all means destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 2:52; Luke 2:40; Acts 2:47"> And the child Samuel advanced, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. was good.">d</a></sup>was in favor with God and with men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:1; Exodus 4:27; Exodus 4:14–16"> And a man of God came to Heli, and said, Thus says the Lord, I plainly revealed myself to the house of your father, when they were servants in Egypt to the house of Pharao.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 28:1; Leviticus 8:7–8; Numbers 18:19"> And I chose the house of your father out of all the tribes of Israel to minister to me in the priest's office, to go up to my altar, and to burn incense, and to wear an ephod. And I gave to the house of your father all the offerings by fire of the children of Israel for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Deuteronomy 32:15; Kings I 2:13–17"> And therefore have you looked upon my incense-offering and my meat-offering with a shameless eye, and have honored your sons above me, so that they should bless themselves with the first fruits of every sacrifice of Israel before me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:26; Jeremias 18:9–10; Proverbs 3:9–10"> Therefore thus says the Lord God of Israel, I said, Your house and the house of your father shall pass before me for ever: but now the Lord says, That be far from me; for I will only honor them that honor me, and he that sets me at nothing shall be despised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings I 22:17–20; Kings III 2:26–27; Psalms 37:17"> Behold, the days come when I will destroy your seed and the seed of your father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Zacharias 8:4; Kings I 4:11; Psalms 78:59–64"> And you shall not have an old man in my house for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Kings III 2:26–27; Kings III 1:7; Kings I 22:21–23"> And if I do not destroy a man of your from my altar, it shall be that his eyes may fail and his soul may perish; and every one that remains in your house shall fall by the sword of men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Kings I 4:11; Kings III 13:3; Kings I 4:17"> And this which shall come upon your two sons Ophni and Phinees shall be a sign to you; in one day they shall both die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings II 7:27; Kings II 7:11; Kings III 11:38"> And I will raise up to myself a faithful priest, who shall do all that is in my heart and in my soul; and I will build him a sure house, and he shall walk before my Christ for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Kings III 2:27; Malachias 1:13; Jezekiel 44:10–12"> And it shall come to pass that he that survives in your house, shall come to do obeisance before him for a little piece of silver,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Alex.+ 'and for a piece of bread.'">e</a></sup>saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. cast.">f</a></sup>Put me into one of your priest's offices to eat bread.</span>
