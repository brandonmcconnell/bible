<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles I 10:1–12; Kings I 28:4; Kings II 1:21"> And the Philistines fought with Israel: and the men of Israel fled from before the Philistines, and they fall down wounded in the mountain in Gelbue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 8:33; Kings I 14:49; Kings I 23:17"> And the Philistines press closely on Saul and his sons, and the Philistines strike Jonathan, and Aminadab, and Melchisa son of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Kings II 1:6; Genesis 49:23; Kings III 22:34"> And the battle prevails against Saul, and the shooters with arrows, even the archers find him, and he was wounded<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. in the hypochondria.">a</a></sup>under the ribs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:54; Kings II 1:6; Kings II 1:9–10"> And Saul said to his armor-bearer, Draw your sword and pierce me through with it; lest these uncircumcised come and pierce me through, and mock me. But his armor-bearer would not, for he feared greatly: so Saul took his sword and fell upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles I 10:5"> And his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, and he fell also himself upon his sword, and died with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 12:17; Osee 13:10–11; Chronicles I 10:6"> So Saul died, and his three sons, and his armor-bearer, in that day together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:33; Leviticus 26:32; Leviticus 26:36"> And the men of Israel who were on the other side of the valley, and those beyond Jordan, saw that the men of Israel fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead; and they leave their cities and flee: and the Philistines come and dwell in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 20:25; Chronicles I 10:8"> And it came to pass on the morrow that the Philistines come to strip the dead, and they find Saul and his three sons fallen on the mountains of Gelbue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings II 1:20; Judges 16:23–24; Kings I 17:54"> And they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Comp. reads, ἀποκεφαλίζουσιν. so in Heb.">b</a></sup>turned him, and stripped off his armor, and sent it into the land of the Philistines, sending round glad tidings to their idols and to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 2:13; Joshua 17:11; Kings I 21:9"> And they set up his armor at the temple of Astarte, and they fastened his body on the wall of Baethsam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 11:1–11; Kings II 2:4"> And the inhabitants of Jabis Galaad hear what the Philistines did to Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Chronicles II 16:14; Jeremias 34:5; Kings II 2:4–7"> And they rose up, even every man of might, and marched all night, and took the body of Saul and the body of Jonathan his son from the wall of Baethsam; and they bring them to Jabis, and burn them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings II 21:12–14; Genesis 50:10; Kings I 22:6"> And they took their bones, and buried them in the field that is in Jabis, and fasted seven days.</span>
