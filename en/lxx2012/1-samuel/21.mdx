<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Galatians 2:12; Colossians 3:9; Kings I 22:22"> And David said to the priest, The king gave me a command today, and said to me, Let no one know the matter on which I send you, and concerning which I have charged you: and I have charged my servants to be in the place that is called,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="This is another instance of double translation, מקוצ suggesting probably both the idea of place and faithfulness.">a</a></sup>The faithfulness of God,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Phellani maemoni, a corruption of פלכי אלמבי.">b</a></sup>Phellani Maemoni.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 19:15; Matthew 12:3–4; Exodus 25:30"> And the priest answered David, and said, There are no common loaves under my hand, for I have none but holy loaves: if the young men have been kept at least from women, then they shall eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 24:9; 1 Thessalonians 4:3–4; 2 Timothy 2:20–21"> And David answered the priest, and said to him, Yes, we have been kept from women for three days: when I came forth for the journey all the young men were purified; but this expedition is unclean, therefore it shall be sanctified this day because of my weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 6:3–4; Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:3–4"> So Abimelech the priest gave him the show bread; for there were no loaves there, but only the presence loaves which had been removed from the presence of the Lord, in order that hot bread should be set on, on the day on which he took them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings I 22:9; Psalms 52:1–9; Chronicles I 27:29"> And there was there on that day one of Saul's servants<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The word Νεεσσαρὰν is another repitition. Heb. בעצר.">c</a></sup>detained before the Lord, and his name was Doec the Syrian, tending the mules of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And David said to Abimelech, See if there is here under your hand spear or sword, for I have not<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. taken.">d</a></sup>brought in my hand my sword or my weapons, for the word of the king was urgent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 17:2; Kings I 17:50–54; Exodus 28:6–14"> And the priest said, Behold the sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you struck in the valley of Ela; and it is wrapped in a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Alex.+ behind the ephod (or shoulder-piece) —so the Heb.">e</a></sup>cloth: if you will take it, take it for yourself, for there is no other except it here. And David said, Behold, there is none like it; give it me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 26:21; Kings III 19:3; Kings I 27:1–2"> And he gave it him; and David arose, and fled in that day from he presence of Saul: and David came to Anchus king of Geth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings I 29:5; Kings I 18:7–8; Kings I 16:1"> And the servants of Anchus said to him, Is not this David the king of the land? Did not the dancing women begin the song to him, saying, Saul has struck his thousand, and David his ten thousands?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Psalms 56:3; Psalms 34:4"> And David<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. put.">f</a></sup>laid up the words in his heart, and was greatly afraid of Anchus king of Geth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Ecclesiastes 7:7; Psalms 34:1–22"> And he changed his appearance before him, and feigned himself a false character in that day; and drummed upon the doors of the city, and used extravagant gestures with his hands, and fell against the doors of the gate, and his spittle ran down upon his beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:7"> And Anchus said to his servants, Behold! you⌃ see the man is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. or man epileptic.">g</a></sup>mad: why have you⌃ brought him in to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Am I in lack of madmen, that you⌃ have brought him in to me to play the madman? He shall not come into the house.</span>
