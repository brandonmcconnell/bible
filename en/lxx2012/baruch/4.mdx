<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> This is the book of the commandments of God, and the law that endures for ever: all they that keep it shall come to life; but such as leave it shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Turn you, O Jacob, and take hold of it: walk in the presence of the light thereof, that you may be illuminated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Give not your honor to another, nor the things that are profitable to you to a strange nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> O Israel, happy are we: for things that are pleasing to God are made known to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Be of good cheer, my people, the memorial of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> You⌃ were sold to the nations, not for your destruction: but because you⌃ moved God to wrath, you⌃ were delivered to the enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For you⌃ provoked him that made you by sacrificing to devils, and not to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> You⌃ have forgotten the everlasting God, that brought you up; and you⌃ have grieved Jerusalem, that nursed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For when she saw the wrath of God coming upon you, she said, Listen, O you⌃ that dwell about Sion: God has brought upon me great mourning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For I saw the captivity of my sons and daughters, which the Everlasting brought upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> With joy did I nourish them; but sent them away with weeping and mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Let no man rejoice over me, a widow, and forsaken of many, who for the sins of my children am left desolate; because they departed from the law of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They knew not his statutes, nor walked in the ways of his commandments, nor trod in the paths of discipline in his righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Let them that dwell about Sion come, and remember you⌃ the captivity of my sons and daughters, which the Everlasting has brought upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For he has brought a nation upon them from far, a shameless nation, and of a strange language, who neither reverenced old man, nor pitied child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> These have carried away the dear beloved children of the widow, and left her that was alone desolate without daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But what can I help you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For he that brought these plagues upon you will deliver you from the hands of your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Go your way, O my children, go your way: for I am left desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> I have put off the clothing of peace, and put upon me the sackcloth of my prayer: I will cry to the Everlasting in my days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Be of good cheer, O my children, cry to the Lord, and he will deliver you from the power and hand of the enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For my hope is in the Everlasting, that he will save you; and joy is come to me from the Holy One, because of the mercy which shall soon come to you from the Everlasting our Saviour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For I sent you out with mourning and weeping: but God will give you to me again with joy and gladness for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Like as now the neighbors of Sion have seen your captivity: so shall they see shortly your salvation from our God which shall come upon you with great glory, and brightness of the Everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> My children, suffer patiently the wrath that is come upon you from God: for your enemy has persecuted you; but shortly you shall see his destruction, and shall tread upon his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> My delicate ones have gone rough ways, and were taken away as a flock caught of the enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Be of good comfort, O my children, and cry to God: for you⌃ shall be remembered of him that brought these things upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For as it was your mind to go astray from God: so, being returned, seek him ten times more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For he that has brought these plagues upon you shall bring you everlasting joy with your salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Take a good heart, O Jerusalem: for he that gave you that name will comfort you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Miserable are they that afflicted you, and rejoiced at your fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Miserable are the cities which your children served: miserable is she that received your sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> For as she rejoiced at your ruin, and was glad of your fall: so shall she be grieved for her own desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> For I will take away the rejoicing of her great multitude, and her pride shall be turned into mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> For fire shall come upon her from the Everlasting, long to endure; and she shall be inhabited of devils for a great time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> O Jerusalem, look about you toward the east, and behold the joy that comes to you from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Behold, your sons come, whom you sent away, they come gathered together from the east to the west by the word of the Holy One, rejoicing in the glory of God.</span>
