<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Therefore the Lord has made good his word, which he pronounced against us, and against our judges that judged Israel, and against our kings, and against our princes, and against the men of Israel and Juda,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> To bring upon us great plagues, such as never happened under the whole heaven, as it came to pass in Jerusalem, according to the things that were written in the law of Moses;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> That a man should eat the flesh of his own son, and the flesh of his own daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Moreover he has delivered them to be in subjection to all the kingdoms that are round about us, to be as a reproach and desolation among all the people round about, where the Lord has scattered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Thus we were cast down, and not exalted, because we have sinned against the Lord our God, and have not been obedient to his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> To the Lord our God appertains righteousness: but to us and to our fathers open shame, as appears this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For all these plagues are come upon us, which the Lord has pronounced against us</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Yet have we not prayed before the Lord, that we might turn every one from the imaginations of his wicked heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore the Lord watched over us for evil, and the Lord has brought it upon us: for the Lord is righteous in all his works which he has commanded us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Yet we have not listened to his voice, to walk in the commandments of the Lord, that he has set before us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And now, O Lord God of Israel, that have brought your people out of the land of Egypt with a mighty hand, and high arm, and with signs, and with wonders, and with great power, and have gotten yourself a name, as appears this day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> O Lord our God, we have sinned, we have done ungodly, we have dealt unrighteously in all your ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Let your wrath turn from us: for we are but a few left among the heathen, where you have scattered us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Hear our prayers, O Lord, and our petitions, and deliver us for your own sake, and give us favor in the sight of them which have led us away:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> That all the earth may know that you are the Lord our God, because Israel and his posterity is called by your name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> O Lord, look down from your holy house, and consider us: bow down your ear, O Lord, to hear us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Open your eyes, and behold; for the dead that are in the graves, whose souls are taken from their bodies, will give to the Lord neither praise nor righteousness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But the soul that is greatly vexed, which goes stooping and feeble, and the eyes that fail, and the hungry soul, will give you praise and righteousness, O Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Therefore we do not make our humble supplication before you, O Lord our God, for the righteousness of our fathers, and of our kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For you have sent out your wrath and indignation upon us, as you have spoken by your servants the prophets, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Thus says the Lord, Bow down your shoulders to serve the king of Babylon: so shall you⌃ remain in the land that I gave to your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if you⌃ will not hear the voice of the Lord, to serve the king of Babylon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> I will cause to cease out of the cites of Judah, and from without Jerusalem, the voice of mirth, and the voice of joy, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride: and the whole land shall be desolate of inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But we would not listen to your voice, to serve the king of Babylon: therefore have you made good the words that you spoke by your servants the prophets, namely, that the bones of our kings, and the bones of our fathers, should be taken out of their place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And, behold, they are cast out to the heat of the day, and to the frost of the night, and they died in great miseries by famine, by sword, and by pestilence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the house which is called by your name have you laid waste, as it is to be seen this day, for the wickedness of the house of Israel and the house of Juda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> O Lord our God, you have dealt with us after all your goodness, and according to all that great mercy of yours,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> As you spoke by your servant Moses in the day when you did command him to write the law before the children of Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> If you⌃ will not hear my voice, surely this very great multitude shall be turned into a small number among the nations, where I will scatter them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For I knew that they would not hear me, because it is a stiff-necked people: but in the land of their captivities they shall remember themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And shall know that I am the Lord their God: for I will give them an heart, and ears to hear:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And they shall praise me in the land of their captivity, and think upon my name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And return from their stiff neck, and from their wicked deeds: for they shall remember the way of their fathers, which sinned before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And I will bring them again into the land which I promised with an oath to their fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and they shall be lords of it: and I will increase them, and they shall not be diminished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And I will make an everlasting covenant with them to be their God, and they shall be my people: and I will no more drive my people of Israel out of the land that I have given them.</span>
