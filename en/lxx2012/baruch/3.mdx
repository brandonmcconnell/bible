<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> O Lord Almighty, God of Israel, the soul in anguish the troubled spirit, cries to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Hear, O Lord, and have mercy; for you are merciful: and have pity upon us, because we have sinned before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For you endure for ever, and we perish utterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> O Lord Almighty, you God of Israel, hear now the prayers of the dead Israelites, and of their children, which have sinned before you, and not listened to the voice of you their God: for the which cause these plagues cleave to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Remember not the iniquities of our forefathers: but think upon your power and your name now at this time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For you are the Lord our God, and you, O Lord, will we praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And for this cause you have put your fear in our hearts, to the intent that we should call upon your name, and praise you in our captivity: for we have called to mind all the iniquity of our forefathers, that sinned before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Behold, we are yet this day in our captivity, where you have scattered us, for a reproach and a curse, and to be subject to payments, according to all the iniquities of our fathers, which departed from the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Hear, Israel, the commandments of life: give ear to understand wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> How is it Israel, that you are in your enemies' land, that you have become old in a strange country, that you are defiled with the dead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> That you are counted with them that go down into the grave?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> You have forsaken the fountain of wisdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For if you had walked in the way of God, you should have dwelled in peace for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Learn where is wisdom, where is strength, where is understanding; that you may know also where is length of days, and life, where is the light of the eyes, and peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Who has found out her place? or who has come into her treasures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Where are the princes of the heathen become, and such as ruled the beasts upon the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They that had their pastime with the fowls of the air, and they that hoarded up silver and gold, wherein men trust, and made no end of their getting?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For they that wrought in silver, and were so careful, and whose works are unsearchable,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They are vanished and gone down to the grave, and others are come up in their steads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Young men have seen light, and lived upon the earth: but the way of knowledge have they not known,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Nor understood the paths thereof, nor laid hold of it: their children were far off from that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> It has not been heard of in Chanaan, neither has it been seen in Theman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The Agarenes that seek wisdom upon earth, the merchants of Meran and of Theman, the authors of fables, and searchers out of understanding; none of these have known the way of wisdom, or remember her paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> O Israel, how great is the house of God! and how large is the place of his possession!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Great, and has none end; high, and unmeasurable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> There were the giants famous from the beginning, that were of so great stature, and so expert in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Those did not the Lord choose, neither gave he the way of knowledge to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> But they were destroyed, because they had no wisdom, and perished through their own foolishness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Who has gone up into heaven, and taken her, and brought her down from the clouds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Who has gone over the sea, and found her, and will bring her for pure gold?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> No man knows her way, nor thinks of her path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But he that knows all things knows her, and has found her out with his understanding: he that prepared the earth for evermore has filled it with four-footed beasts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> He that sends forth light, and it goes, calls it again, and it obeys him with fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> The stars shined in their watches, and rejoiced: when he calls them, they say, Here we be; and so with cheerfulness they showed light to him that made them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> This is our God, and there shall none other be accounted of in comparison of him</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> He has found out all the way of knowledge, and has given it to Jacob his servant, and to Israel his beloved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Afterward did he show himself upon earth, and conversed with men.</span>
