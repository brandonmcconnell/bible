<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:17; Psalms 78:51; Psalms 105:36"> Ruben, you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, you my firstborn, etc., nom. and voc. not being always regularly distinguished in the LXX. See Heb 1:8 ὁ θεὸς.">a</a></sup>are my firstborn, you my strength, and the first of my children, hard to be endured, hard and self-willed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Chronicles I 5:1; Deuteronomy 27:20"> You were insolent like water, burst not forth with violence, for you went up to the bed of your father; then you defiled the couch, whereupon you went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 34:25–31; Genesis 46:10–11; Genesis 29:33–34"> Symeon and Levi, brethren, accomplished the injustice of their cutting off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 34:30; Psalms 26:9; Psalms 16:9"> Let not my soul come into their counsel, and let not my inward parts contend in their conspiracy, for in their wrath they killed men, and in their passion they houghed a bull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:1–9; Chronicles I 4:24–31; Chronicles I 6:65"> Cursed be their wrath, for it was willful, and their anger, for it was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="more lit. hardened or aggravated.">b</a></sup>cruel: I will divide them in Jacob, and scatter them in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 7:14; Genesis 27:29; Chronicles I 5:2"> Juda, your brethren have praised you, and your hands shall be on the back of your enemies; your father's sons shall do you reverence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 24:9; Numbers 23:24; Revelation 5:5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="The terminations of proper names are occasionally varied.">c</a></sup>Juda is a lion's whelp: from the tender plant, my son, you are gone up, having couched you lie as a lion, and as a whelp; who shall stir him up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Numbers 24:17; Esias 9:6"> A ruler shall not fail from Juda, nor a prince from his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. thighs.">d</a></sup>loins, until there come the things stored up for him; and he is the expectation of nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 63:1–3; Joel 3:18; Revelation 7:14"> Binding his foal to the vine, and the foal of his ass to the branch of it, he shall wash his robe in wine, and his garment in the blood of the grape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 23:29"> His eyes shall be more cheering than wine, and his teeth whiter than milk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:18–19; Genesis 30:20; Joshua 19:10–16"> Zabulon shall dwell on the coast, and he shall be by a haven of ships, and shall extend to Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles I 12:32; Genesis 30:18; Deuteronomy 33:18"> Issachar has desired that which is good; resting between the inheritances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 81:6; Matthew 23:4; Joshua 14:15"> And having seen the resting place that it was good, and the land that it was fertile, he subjected his shoulder to labor, and became a husbandman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Deuteronomy 33:22; Judges 18:26–27"> Dan shall judge his people, as one tribe too in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles I 12:35; Judges 18:22–31; Judges 14:1–15"> And let Dan be a serpent in the way, besetting the path, biting the heel of the horse (and the rider shall fall backward),</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:174; Psalms 119:166; Michaeas 7:7"> waiting for the salvation of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 30:11; Chronicles I 5:11–22; Deuteronomy 33:20–21"> Gad, a plundering troop shall plunder him; but he shall plunder him, pursuing him closely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:24–25; Genesis 30:13; Genesis 46:17"> Aser, his bread shall be fat; and he shall yield dainties to princes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:23; Genesis 30:8; Joshua 19:32–39"> Nephthalim is a spreading stem, bestowing beauty on its fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:52; Psalms 128:3; Psalms 1:1–3"> Joseph is a son increased; my dearly loved son is increased; my youngest son, turn to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 37:24; Psalms 64:3; Psalms 118:13"> Against whom men taking evil counsel reproached him, and the archers pressed hard upon him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 132:2; Esias 28:16; Psalms 132:5"> But their bow and arrows were mightily consumed, and the sinews of their arms were slackened by the hand of the mighty one of Jacob; thence is he that strengthened Israel from the God of your father;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 28:13; Genesis 35:11; Philippians 4:19"> and my God helped you, and he blessed you with the blessing of heaven from above, and the blessing of the earth possessing all things, because of the blessing of the breasts and of the womb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:15–16; Ambacum 3:6; Esias 66:5"> the blessings of your father and your mother—it has prevailed above the blessing of the lasting mountains, and beyond the blessings of the everlasting hills; they shall be upon the head of Joseph, and upon the head of the brothers of whom he took the lead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Genesis 35:18; Jezekiel 22:27"> Benjamin, as a ravening wolf, shall eat still in the morning, and at evening he gives food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 7:4; Esther 8:11; Kings III 18:31"> All these are the twelve sons of Jacob; and their father spoke these words to them, and he blessed them; he blessed each of them according to his blessing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 47:30; Genesis 50:13; Genesis 15:15"> And he said to them, I am added to my people; you⌃ shall bury me with my fathers in the cave, which is in the field of Ephron the Chettite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 23:20; Genesis 23:16–18; Genesis 23:8–9"> in the double cave which is opposite Mambre, in the land of Chanaan, the cave which Abraam bought of Ephron the Chettite, for a possession of a sepulchre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 25:9; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 50:13"> There they buried Abraam and Sarrha his wife; there they buried Isaac, and Rebecca his wife; there they buried Lea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 23:17–20"> in the portion of the field, and of the cave that was in it, purchased of the sons of Chet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 49:29; Genesis 25:8; Genesis 35:29"> And Jacob ceased giving charges to his sons; and having lifted up his feet on the bed, he died, and was gathered to his people.</span>
