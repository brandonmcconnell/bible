<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 37:28; Psalms 105:17; Genesis 37:36"> And Joseph was brought down to Egypt; and Petephres the eunuch of Pharao, the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. chief cook. The same Hebrew word signifies a cook, who was also a butcher; and a guardsman, who was also an executioner.">a</a></sup>captain of the guard, an Egyptian, bought him of the hands of the Ismaelites, who brought him down there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Kings I 18:14; Psalms 91:15; Jeremias 15:20"> And the Lord was with Joseph, and he was a prosperous man; and he was in the house with his lord the Egyptian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 1:3; Genesis 30:27; Matthew 5:16"> And his master knew that the Lord was with him, and the Lord prospers in his hands whatever he happens to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:21–22; Proverbs 27:18; Genesis 19:19"> And Joseph found grace in the presence of his lord, and was well-pleasing to him; and he set him over his house, and all that he had he gave into the hand of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Kings II 6:11–12; Acts 27:24"> And it came to pass after that he was set over his house, and over all that he had, that the Lord blessed the house of the Egyptian for Joseph's sake; and the blessing of the Lord was on all his possessions in the house, and in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 16:12; Luke 19:17; Proverbs 31:11"> And he committed all that he had into the hands of Joseph; and he knew not of anything that belonged to him, save the bread which he himself ate. And Joseph was handsome in form, and exceedingly beautiful in countenance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings II 13:11; Jezekiel 16:34; 2 Peter 2:14"> And it came to pass after these things, that his master's wife cast her eyes upon Joseph, and said, Lie with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 23:26–28; Proverbs 22:14; Proverbs 9:13–18"> But he would not; but said to his master's wife, If because of me my master knows nothing in his house, and has given into my hands all things that belong to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings II 12:13; Genesis 20:6; Genesis 42:18"> and in this house there is nothing above me, nor has anything been kept back from me, but you, because you are his wife—how then shall I do this wicked thing, and sin against God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:11; Genesis 39:8; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> And when she talked with Joseph day by day, and he listened not to her to sleep with her, so as to be with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ephesians 5:12; Ephesians 5:3; Malachias 3:5"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. there happened such a day, and, etc.">b</a></sup>it came to pass on a certain day, that Joseph went into the house to do his business, and there was no one of the household within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:22; Proverbs 7:13–27; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> And she caught hold of him by his clothes, and said, Lie with me; and having left his clothes in her hands, he fled, and went forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And it came to pass, when she saw that he had left his clothes in her hands, and fled, and gone forth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Peter 2:20; Psalms 35:11"> that she called those that were in the house, and spoke to them, saying, See, he has brought in to us a Hebrew servant to mock us—he came in to me, saying, Lie with me, and I cried with a loud voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And when he heard that I lifted up my voice and cried, having left his clothes with me, he fled, and went forth out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 37:32; Jeremias 4:22; Jeremias 9:3–5"> So she leaves the clothes by her, until the master came to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; Kings III 18:17; Psalms 37:14"> And she spoke to him according to these words, saying, The Hebrew servant, whom you brought in to us, came in to me to mock me, and said to me, I will lie with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And when he heard that I lifted up my voice and cried, having left his clothes with me, he fled and departed forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 4:5–6; Proverbs 18:17; Proverbs 6:34–35"> And it came to pass, when his master heard all the words of his wife, that she spoke to him, saying, Thus did your servant to me, that he was very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:15; 2 Timothy 2:9; Psalms 105:18–19"> And his master took Joseph, and cast him into the prison, into the place where the king's prisoners are kept, there in the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:21; Esias 41:10; 1 Peter 3:17"> And the Lord was with Joseph, and poured down mercy upon him; and he gave him favor in the sight of the chief keeper of the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:3–4; Genesis 39:4; Kings I 2:30"> And the chief keeper of the prison gave the prison into the hand of Joseph, and all the prisoners<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lit. men led away to prison or punishment.">c</a></sup>as many as were in the prison; and all things whatever they do there, he did them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esias 43:2; Genesis 39:2–3; Psalms 1:3"> Because of him the chief keeper of the prison knew nothing, for all things were in the hand of Joseph, because the Lord was with him; and whatever things he did, the Lord made them to prosper in his hands.</span>
