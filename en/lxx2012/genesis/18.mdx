<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 14:13; Genesis 48:3; Genesis 13:18"> And God appeared to him by the oak of Mambre, as he sat by the door of his tent at noon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 13:2; Genesis 18:22; Genesis 19:1"> And he lifted up his eyes and saw, and behold! three men stood before him; and having seen them he ran to meet them from the door of his tent, and did obeisance to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 32:5"> And he said, Lord, if indeed I have found grace in your sight, pass not by your servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 24:32; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 43:24"> Let water now be brought, and let them wash your feet, and do you⌃ refresh yourselves under the tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Judges 13:15; Judges 6:18"> And I will bring bread, and you⌃ shall eat, and after this you⌃ shall depart on your journey, on account of which refreshment you⌃ have turned aside to your servant. And he said, So do, as you have said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 13:33; Hebrews 13:2; Acts 16:15"> And Abraam hasted to the tent to Sarrha, and said to her, Hasten, and knead three measures of fine flour, and make cakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachias 1:14; Luke 15:27; Luke 15:30"> And Abraam ran to the kine, and took a young calf, tender and good, and gave it to his servant, and he hasted to dress it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 19:3; Judges 13:15; Revelation 3:20"> And he took butter and milk, and the calf which he had dressed; and he set them before them, and they did eat, and he stood by them under the tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Titus 2:5; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 4:9"> And he said to him, Where is Sarrha your wife? And he answered and said, Behold! in the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 21:2; Genesis 17:21; Romans 9:8–9"> And he said, I will return and come to you according to this period seasonably, and Sarrha your wife shall have a son; and Sarrha heard at the door of the tent, being behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:17; Hebrews 11:11–12; Luke 1:36"> And Abraam and Sarrha were old, advanced in days, and the custom of women ceased with Sarrha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:6; Genesis 17:17; Luke 1:18–20"> And Sarrha laughed in herself, saying,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. The difference turns on the word Heb. pleasure. Gr until now.">a</a></sup>The thing has not as yet happened to me, even until now, and my lord is old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 2:25"> And the Lord said to Abraam, Why is it that Sarrha has laughed in herself, saying, Shall I then indeed bear? but I am grown old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 1:37; Matthew 19:26; Mark 10:27"> Shall anything be impossible with the Lord? At this time I will return to you seasonably, and Sarrha shall have a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 1:8; Proverbs 28:13; Ephesians 4:23"> But Sarrha denied, saying, I did not laugh, for she was afraid. And he said to her, Nay, but you did laugh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 15:3; Acts 21:5; Acts 20:38"> And the men having risen up from thence looked towards Sodom and Gomorrha. And Abraam went with them, attending them on their journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 3:7; Psalms 25:14; John 15:15"> And the Lord said, Shall I hide from Abraam my servant what things I intend to do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:8; Genesis 26:4; Galatians 3:14"> But Abraam shall become a great and populous nation, and in him shall all the nations of the earth be blest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:6–7; Deuteronomy 4:9–10; 1 Timothy 3:4–5"> For I know that he will order his sons, and his house after him, and they will keep the ways of the Lord, to do justice and judgment, that the Lord may bring upon Abraam all things whatever he has spoken to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 19:13; Jezekiel 16:49–50; Esias 3:9"> And the Lord said, The cry of Sodom and Gomorrha has been increased towards me, and their sins are very great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Genesis 11:5; Sophonias 1:12"> I will therefore go down and see, if they completely<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. συντελόῦνται. Heb. A.V. have done altogether.">b</a></sup>correspond with the cry which comes to me, and if not, that I may know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 19:1; Acts 7:55; Jezekiel 22:30"> And the men having departed thence, came to Sodom; and Abraam was still standing before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 16:22; Kings II 24:17; Genesis 20:4"> And Abraam drew near and said, Wouldest you destroy the righteous with the wicked, and shall the righteous be as the wicked?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremias 5:1; Esias 1:9; Matthew 7:13–14"> Should there be fifty righteous in the city, will you destroy them? will you not spare the whole place for the sake of the fifty righteous, if they be in it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 8:3; Esias 3:10–11; Job 8:20"> By no means shall you do as this thing is so as to destroy the righteous with the wicked, so the righteous shall be as the wicked: by no means. You that judge the whole earth, shall you not do right?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremias 5:1; Matthew 24:22; Esias 65:8"> And the Lord said, If there should be in Sodom fifty righteous in the city, I will spare the whole city, and the whole place for their sakes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 3:19; Esias 6:5; 1 Corinthians 15:47–48"> And Abraam answered and said, Now I have begun to speak to my Lord, and I am earth and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 18:29; Kings III 20:32–33; Genesis 18:26"> But if the fifty righteous should be diminished to forty-five, will you destroy the whole city because of the five lacking? And he said, I will not destroy it, if I should find there forty-five.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Hebrews 4:16"> And he continued to speak to him still, and said, But if there should be found there forty? And he said, I will not destroy it for the forty's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Psalms 9:12; Esther 4:11–16"> And he said, Will there be anything against me, Lord, if I shall speak? but if there be found there thirty? And he said, I will not destroy it for the thirty's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 11:8; Genesis 18:27; Luke 18:1"> And he said, Since I am able to speak to the Lord, what if there should be found there twenty? And he said, I will not destroy it, if I should find there twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="James 5:15–17; Judges 6:39; Matthew 7:7"> And he said, Will there be anything against me, Lord, if I speak yet once? but if there should be found there ten? And he said, I will not destroy it for the ten's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 18:22; Genesis 32:26; Genesis 31:55"> And the Lord departed, when he left off speaking to Abraam, and Abraam returned to his place.</span>
