<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 8:44; 2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14"> And the serpent said to the woman,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. you⌃ shall not die by death.">a</a></sup>You⌃ shall not surely die.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 59:6; Genesis 2:25; Genesis 3:5"> And the eyes of both were opened, and they perceived that they were naked, and they sewed fig leaves together, and made themselves aprons to go round them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 23:24; Proverbs 15:3; Hebrews 4:13"> And they heard the voice of the Lord God walking in the garden in the afternoon; and both Adam and his wife hid themselves from the face of the Lord God in the midst of the trees of the garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 4:9; Genesis 16:8; Genesis 18:20–21"> And the Lord God called Adam and said to him, Adam, where are you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 2:25; Psalms 119:120; Esias 47:3"> And he said to him, I heard<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, the sound of you walking.">b</a></sup>your voice as you walked in the garden, and I feared because I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. am.">c</a></sup>was naked and I hid myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:20; Genesis 4:10; Psalms 50:21"> And God said to him, Who told you that you were naked, unless you have eaten of the tree concerning which I charged you of it alone not to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; James 1:13–15; Job 31:33"> And Adam said, The woman whom you gave to be with me—she gave me of the tree and I ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; 1 Timothy 2:14; Genesis 4:10–12"> And the Lord God said to the woman, Why have you done this? And the woman said, The serpent deceived me and I ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Michaeas 7:17; Esias 65:25; Psalms 72:9"> And the Lord God said to the serpent, Because you have done this you are cursed above all cattle and all the brutes of the earth, on your breast and belly you shall go, and you shall eat earth all the days of your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 16:20; 1 John 3:8; Revelation 12:17"> And I will put enmity between you and the woman and between your seed and her seed, he shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. keep. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:20–22; Genesis 5:29; Esias 24:5–6"> And to Adam he said, Because you have listened to the voice of your wife, and eaten of the tree concerning which I charged you of it only not to eat—of that you have eaten, cursed is the ground in your labors, in pain shall you eat of it all the days of your life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 13:7; Job 5:5; Esias 7:23"> Thorns and thistles shall it bring forth to you, and you shall eat the herb of the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 34:15; Psalms 104:29"> In the sweat of your face shall you eat your bread until you return to the earth out of which you were taken, for earth you are and to earth you shall return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:23; Matthew 1:23; Genesis 5:29"> And Adam called the name of his wife<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. Zoe.">g</a></sup>Life, because she was the mother of all living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 61:10; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 2 Corinthians 5:2–3"> And the Lord God made for Adam and his wife garments of skin, and clothed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14; Proverbs 3:18"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Alex. + the Lord.">h</a></sup>God said, Behold, Adam is become as one of us, to know good and evil, and now lest at any time he stretch forth his hand, and take of the tree of life and eat, and so he shall live forever—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 4:2; Genesis 3:19; Genesis 4:12"> So the Lord God sent him forth out of the garden of Delight to cultivate the ground out of which he was taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 1:7; Psalms 104:4; Exodus 25:18–22"> And he cast out Adam and caused him to dwell over against the garden of Delight, and stationed the cherubs and the fiery sword that turns about to keep the way of the tree of life.</span>
