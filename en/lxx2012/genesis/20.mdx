<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 26:1; Genesis 26:6; Genesis 18:1"> And Abraam removed thence to the southern country, and lived between Cades and Sur, and sojourned in Gerara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 12:15; Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:11–13"> And Abraam said concerning Sarrha his wife, She is my sister, for he feared to say, She is my wife, lest at any time the men of the city should kill him for her sake. So Abimelech king of Gerara sent and took Sarrha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 20:7; Matthew 27:19; Psalms 105:14"> And God came to Abimelech by night in sleep, and said, Behold, you die for the woman, whom you have taken, whereas she has lived with a husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 18:23–25; Chronicles I 21:17; Kings II 4:11"> But Abimelech had not touched her, and he said, Lord, will you destroy an ignorantly sinning and just nation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 7:8; Kings III 9:4; Daniel 6:22"> Said he not to me, She is my sister, and said she not to me, He is my brother? with a pure heart and in the righteousness of my hands have I done this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings I 25:34; Proverbs 21:1; Kings I 25:26"> And God said to him in sleep, Yes, I knew that you did this with a pure heart, and I spared you, so that you should not sin against me, therefore I suffered you not to touch her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Job 42:8; Kings I 7:5; Kings II 24:17"> But now return the man his wife; for he is a prophet, and shall pray for you, and you shall live; but if you restore her not, know that you shall die and all your.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Abimelech rose early in the morning, and called all his servants, and he spoke all these words in their ears, and all the men feared exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 12:18; Genesis 26:10; Genesis 39:9"> And Abimelech called Abraam and said to him, What is this that you have done to us? Have we sinned against you, that you have brought upon me and upon my kingdom a great sin? You have done to me a deed, which no one ought to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Abimelech said to Abraam, What have you seen in me that you have done this?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Genesis 12:12; Job 28:28"> And Abraam said, Why I said, Surely there is not the worship of God in this place, and they will kill me because of my wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 5:22"> For truly she is my sister by my father, but not by my mother, and she became my wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 12:1; Genesis 12:9; Acts 7:3–5"> And it came to pass when God brought me forth out of the house of my father, that I said to her, This righteousness you shall perform to me, in every place into which we may enter, say of me, He is my brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 12:16; Genesis 20:2; Genesis 20:7"> And Abimelech took a thousand<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. didrachma.">a</a></sup>pieces of silver, and sheep, and calves, and servants, and maidservants, and gave them to Abraam, and he returned him Sarrha his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 34:10; Genesis 13:9; Genesis 47:6"> And Abimelech said to Abraam, Behold, my land is before you, dwell wherever it may please you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 27:5; Genesis 20:5; Genesis 24:65"> And to Sarrha he said, Behold, I have given your brother a thousand pieces of silver, those shall be to you for the price of your countenance, and to all the women with you, and speak the truth in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="James 5:16; Matthew 21:22; Job 42:9–10"> And Abraam prayed to God, and God healed Abimelech, and his wife, and his women servants, and they bore children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 12:17; Genesis 30:2; Genesis 16:2"> Because the Lord had fast closed from without every womb in the house of Abimelech, because of Sarrha Abraam's wife.</span>
