<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 1:32–33; Numbers 25:17–18; Job 2:11"> And she bore to him Zombran, and Jezan, and Madal, and Madiam, and Jesboc, and Soie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 25:13; Jeremias 49:8; Jezekiel 27:20"> And Jezan begot Saba and Dedan. And the sons of Dedan were the Assurians and the Latusians, and Laomim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 60:6"> And the sons of Madiam were Gephar and Aphir, and Enoch, and Abeida, and Eldaga; all these were sons of Chettura.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 24:36; Hebrews 1:2; Galatians 3:29"> But Abraam gave all his possessions to Isaac his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 21:14; Judges 6:3; Acts 14:17"> But to the sons of his concubines Abraam gave gifts, and he sent them away from his son Isaac, while he was yet living, to the east into the country of the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 12:4"> And these were the years of the days of the life of Abraam as many as he lived, one hundred and seventy-five years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 35:28–29; Genesis 15:15; Chronicles I 29:28"> And Abraam failing died in a good old age, an old man and full of days, and was added to his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 50:13; Genesis 35:29; Genesis 49:29–30"> And Isaac and Ismael his sons buried him in the double cave, in the field of Ephron the son of Saar the Chettite, which is over against Mambre:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 23:16; Genesis 49:31"> even the field and the cave which Abraam bought of the sons of Chet; there they buried Abraam and Sarrha his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 24:62"> And it came to pass after Abraam was dead, that God blessed Isaac his son, and Isaac lived by the well of the vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:6; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:13"> And these are the generations of Ismael the son of Abraam, whom Agar the Egyptian the handmaid of Sarrha bore to Abraam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 1:29–31; Esias 60:7; Song of Songs 1:5"> And these are the names of the sons of Ismael, according to the names of their generations. The firstborn of Ismael, Nabaioth, and Kedar, and Nabdeel, and Massam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 21:11; Esias 21:16"> and Masma, and Duma, and Masse,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles I 5:19; Job 2:11"> and Choddan, and Thaeman, and Jetur, and Naphes, and Kedma.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Genesis 17:23"> These are the sons of Ismael, and these are their names in their tents and in their dwellings, twelve princes according to their nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 25:7–8"> And these are the years of the life of Ismael, one hundred and thirty-seven years; and he failed and died, and was added to his<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. family.">a</a></sup>fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 16:12; Genesis 20:1; Kings IV 23:29"> And he lived from Evilat to Sur, which is opposite Egypt, until one comes to the Assyrians; he lived in the presence of all his brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; Luke 3:34; Acts 7:8"> And these are the generations of Isaac the son of Abraam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 22:23"> Abraam begot Isaac. And Isaac was forty years old when he took as wife Rebecca, daughter of Bathuel the Syrian, out of Syrian Mesopotamia, sister of Laban the Syrian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Esias 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> And Isaac prayed the Lord concerning Rebecca his wife, because she was barren; and the Lord heard him, and his wife Rebecca conceived in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 9:9; Kings I 28:6; Kings I 30:8"> And the babes leaped within her; and she said, If it will be so with me, why is this to me? And she went to enquire of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:10–13; Genesis 27:40; Genesis 27:29"> And the Lord said to her, There are two nations in your womb, and two peoples shall be separated from your belly, and one people shall excel the other, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. the greater shall serve the less. Rom 9:12.">b</a></sup>elder shall serve the younger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the days were fulfilled that she should be delivered, and she had twins in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:11; Genesis 27:16; Genesis 27:23"> And the first came out red, hairy all over like a skin; and she called his name Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:36; Osee 12:3; Genesis 38:28–30"> And after this came forth his brother, and his hand took hold of the heel of Esau; and she called his name Jacob. And Isaac was sixty years old when Rebecca bore them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 21:20; Genesis 10:9"> And the lads grew, and Esau was a man skilled in hunting, dwelling in the country, and Jacob a simple man, dwelling in a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 27:19; Genesis 27:31; Genesis 27:4"> And Isaac loved Esau, because his venison was his food, but Rebecca loved Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 13:25; Esias 40:30–31; Kings I 14:28"> And Jacob cooked pottage, and Esau came from the plain, fainting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 36:1; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:9"> And Esau said to Jacob, Let me taste of that red pottage, because I am fainting; therefore his name was called Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Jacob said to Esau, Sell me this day your birthright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachias 3:14; Job 21:15; Job 22:17"> And Esau said, Behold, I am going to die, and for what good does this birthright belong to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 27:36; Hebrews 6:16"> And Jacob said to him, Swear to me this day; and he swore to him; and Esau sold his birthright to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; Hebrews 12:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> And Jacob gave bread to Esau, and pottage of lentiles; and he ate and drank, and he arose and departed; so Esau slighted his birthright.</span>
