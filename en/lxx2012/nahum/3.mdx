<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Sophonias 3:1–3; Ambacum 2:12; Osee 4:2"> O city of blood, wholly false, full of unrighteousness; the prey shall not be handled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 47:3; Naum 2:3–4; Job 39:22–25"> The noise of whips, and the noise of the rumbling of wheels, and of the pursuing horse, and of the bounding chariot,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ambacum 3:11; Jezekiel 39:4; Esias 34:3"> and of the mounting rider, and of the glittering sword, and of the gleaming arms, and of a multitude of slain, and of heavy falling: and there was no end to her nations, but they shall be weak in their bodies</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 47:9; Esias 47:12–13; Revelation 17:1–5"> because of the abundance of fornication: she is a fair harlot, and well-favoured, skilled in sorcery, that sells the nations by her fornication, and peoples by her sorceries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 13:26; Jezekiel 23:29; Jeremias 13:22"> Behold, I am against you, says the Lord God Almighty, and I will uncover your skirts in your presence, and I will show the nations your shame, and the kingdoms your disgrace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachias 2:9; Jeremias 51:37; Job 9:31"> And I will cast abominable filth upon you according to your unclean ways, and will make you a public example.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremias 15:5; Esias 51:19; Revelation 18:10"> And it shall be that every one that sees you shall go down from you, and shall say, Wretched Nineve! who shall lament for her? whence shall I seek comfort for her?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:2; Jezekiel 30:14–16; Jeremias 46:25–26"> Prepare you a portion, tune the chord, prepare a portion for Ammon: she that dwells among the rivers, water is round about her, whose dominion is the sea, and whose walls are water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jezekiel 30:5; Jezekiel 27:10; Esias 20:5"> And Ethiopia is her strength, and Egypt; and there was no limit of the flight of her enemies; and the Libyans became her helpers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 20:4; Joel 3:3; Osee 13:16"> Yet she shall go as a prisoner into captivity, and they shall dash her infants against the ground at the top of all her ways: and they shall cast lots upon all her glorious possessions, and all her nobles shall be bound in chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 49:26; Esias 2:10; Psalms 75:8"> And you shall be drunken, and shall be overlooked; and you shall seek for yourself strength because of your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 6:13; Esias 28:4; Ambacum 1:10"> All your strongholds are as fig-trees having watchers: if they be shaken, they shall fall into the mouth of the eater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 51:30; Esias 19:16; Jeremias 50:37"> Behold, your people within you are as women: the gates of your land shall surely be opened to your enemies: the fire shall devour your bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Chronicles II 32:3–4; Naum 2:1; Chronicles II 32:11"> Draw you water for a siege, and well secure your strongholds: enter into the clay, and be you trodden in the chaff, make the fortifications stronger than brick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Naum 2:13; Naum 3:13"> There the fire shall devour you; the sword shall utterly destroy you, it shall devour you as the locust, and you shall be pressed down as a palmerworm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Nehemiah 9:23; Jeremias 33:22"> You have multiplied your merchandise beyond the stars of heaven: the palmerworm has attacked it, and has flown away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 51:27; Revelation 9:7"> Your mixed multitude has suddenly departed as the grasshopper, as the locust perched on a hedge in a frosty day; the sun arises, and it flies off, and knows not its place: woe to them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 22:17; Psalms 76:5–6; Jeremias 50:18"> Your shepherds have slumbered, the Assyrian king has laid low your mighty men: your people departed to the mountains, and there was none to receive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Michaeas 1:9; Lamentations 2:15; Job 27:23"> There is no healing for your bruise; your wound has rankled: all that hear the report of you shall clap their hands against you; for upon whom has not your wickedness passed continually?</span>
