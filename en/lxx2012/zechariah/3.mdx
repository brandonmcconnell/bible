<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zacharias 4:10; Jeremias 50:20; Esias 28:16"> Hear now, Jesus the high priest, you, and your neighbors that are sitting before you: for they are diviners, for, behold, I bring forth my servant The<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Luke 1:78.">d</a></sup>Branch.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> In that day, says the Lord Almighty, you⌃ shall call together every man his neighbor under the vine and under the fig tree.</span>
