<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 22:6–7; Jeremias 22:23; Luke 21:23–24"> Open your doors, O Libanus, and let the fire devour your cedars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 2:12–17; Jezekiel 31:17; Esias 10:33–34"> Let the pine howl, because the cedar has fallen; for the mighty men have been greatly afflicted: howl, you⌃ oaks of the land of Basan; for the thickly planted forest has been torn down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremias 50:44; Jeremias 25:34–36; Zacharias 11:8"> There is a voice of the shepherds mourning; for their greatness is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. distressed.">a</a></sup>brought low: a voice of roaring lions; for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, roaring.">b</a></sup>pride of Jordan is brought down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zacharias 11:7; Matthew 23:37; Romans 15:8"> Thus says the Lord Almighty, Feed the sheep of the slaughter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 50:7; Osee 12:8; 1 Timothy 6:5–10"> which their possessors have slain, and have not repented: and they that sold them said, Blessed be the Lord; for we have become rich: and their shepherds have suffered no sorrow for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 13:14; Matthew 22:7; Esias 9:19–21"> Therefore I will no longer have mercy upon the inhabitants of the land, says the Lord: but, behold, I will deliver up the men every one into the hand of his neighbor, and into the hand of his king; and they shall destroy the land, and I will not rescue out of their hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Zacharias 11:14; Zacharias 11:4; Esias 11:4"> And I will tend the flock of slaughter in the land of Chanaan: and I will take for myself two rods; the one I called Beauty, and the other I called Line; and I will tend the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 49:7; Matthew 23:34–36; John 15:18"> And I will cut off three shepherds in one month; and my soul shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, be sorely displeased with them. Gr. be weighed down upon them.">c</a></sup>grieve over them, for their souls cried out against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 43:11; Matthew 13:10–11; Matthew 21:19"> And I said, I will not tend you: that which dies, let it die; and that which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, fails.">d</a></sup>falls off, let it fall off; and let the rest eat every one the flesh of his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zacharias 11:7; Psalms 89:39; Hebrews 8:8–13"> And I will take my beautiful staff, and cast it away, that I may break my covenant which I made with all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Sophonias 3:12; Luke 2:38; Deuteronomy 31:21"> And it shall be broken in that day; and the Chananites, the sheep that are kept for me, shall know that it is the word of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 26:15; Exodus 21:32; Luke 22:3–6"> And I will say to them, If it be good in your eyes, give me my price, or refuse it. And they weighed for my price thirty pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Acts 1:18–19; Esias 53:2–3"> And the Lord said to me, Drop them into the furnace, and I will see if it is good metal, as I was proved for their sakes. And I took the thirty pieces of silver, and cast them into the furnace in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 9:21; Esias 11:13; Acts 23:7–10"> And I cast away my second rod, even Line, that I might break the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Compare Heb. Alex. διαθήκην covenant.">e</a></sup>possession between Juda and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 6:10–12; Luke 11:40; Jeremias 2:26–27"> And the Lord said to me, Take yet to you shepherd's implements belonging to an unskillful shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:2–6; Jeremias 23:2; Matthew 23:2–4"> For, behold, I will raise up a shepherd against the land: he shall not visit that which is perishing, and he shall not seek that which is scattered, and he shall not heal that which is bruised, nor guide that which is whole: but he shall devour the flesh of the choice ones, and shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, wring their necks.">f</a></sup>dislocate the joints of their necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 23:1; Jezekiel 13:3; 1 Corinthians 8:4"> Alas for the vain shepherds that have forsaken the sheep! the sword shall be upon<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. his arms.">g</a></sup>the arms of such a one, and upon his right eye: his arm shall be completely withered, and his right eye shall be utterly darkened.</span>
