<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 6:2; Revelation 6:8; Zacharias 1:8"> and in the third chariot white horses; and in the fourth chariot piebald and ash-colored horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zacharias 1:9; Zacharias 5:10; Zacharias 1:19–21"> And I answered and said to the angel that talked with me, What are these, my Lord?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 1:14; Hebrews 1:7; Psalms 104:3–4"> And the angel that talked with me answered and said, These are the four winds of heaven, and they are going forth to stand before the Lord of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 51:48; Jeremias 4:6; Jeremias 6:1"> As for the chariot in which were the black horses, they went out to the land of the north; and the white went out after them; and the piebald went out to the land of the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles II 16:9; Zacharias 1:10; Job 1:6–7"> And the ash-colored went out, and looked to go and compass the earth: and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, one said.">a</a></sup>he said, Go, and compass the earth. And they compassed the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 5:13; Zacharias 1:15; Esias 18:3–4"> And he cried out and spoke to me, saying, Behold, these go out to the land of the north, and they have quieted my anger in the land of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zacharias 8:1; Zacharias 7:1; Zacharias 1:1"> And the word of the Lord came to me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 7:14–16; Ezra 8:26–30; Esias 66:20"> Take the things of the captivity from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Heb. proper names. See A. V.">b</a></sup>the chief men, and from the useful men of it, and from them that have understood it; and you shall enter in that day into the house of Josias the son of Sophonias that came out of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zacharias 3:1; Psalms 21:3; Song of Songs 3:11"> And you shall take silver and gold, and make crowns, and you shall put them upon the head of Jesus the son of Josedec the high priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zacharias 3:8; Esias 4:2; Jeremias 33:15"> and you shall say to him, Thus says the Lord Almighty; Behold the man whose name is The Branch; and he shall spring up<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. from beneath him.">c</a></sup>from his stem, and build the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Esias 49:5–6; Hebrews 3:1"> And he shall receive<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lit. virtue.">d</a></sup>power, and shall sit and rule upon his throne; and there shall be a priest on his right hand, and a peaceful counsel shall be between them both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:13; Exodus 12:14; Mark 14:9"> And the crown shall be to them that wait patiently, and to the useful men<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. of it.">e</a></sup>of the captivity, and to them that have known it, and for the favor of the son of Sophonias, and for a psalm in the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 60:10; Zacharias 3:7; Esias 58:10–14"> And they that are far from them shall come and build in the house of the Lord, and you⌃ shall know that the Lord Almighty has sent me to you: and this shall come to pass, if you⌃ will diligently listen to the voice of the Lord your God.</span>
