<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> When he, too, had undergone blessed martyrdom, and died in the caldron into which he had been thrown, the seventh, the youngest of all, came forward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> whom the tyrant pitying, though he had been dreadfully reproached by his brethren,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> seeing him already encompassed with chains, had him brought nearer, and endeavoured to counsel him, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> You see the end of the madness of your brethren: for they have died in torture through disobedience; and you, if disobedient, having been miserably tormented, will yourself perish prematurely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But if you obey, you shall be my friend, and have a charge over the affairs of the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And having thus exhorted him, he sent for the mother of the boy; that, by condoling with her for the loss of so many sons, he might incline her, through the hope of safety, to render the survivor obedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he, after his mother had urged him on in the Hebrew tongue, (as we shall soon relate) says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Release me that I may speak to the king and all his friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And they, rejoicing exceedingly at the promise of the youth, quickly let him go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he, running up to the pans, said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Impious tyrant, and most blasphemous man, were you not ashamed, having received prosperity and a kingdom from God, to kill His servants, and to rack the doers of godliness?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Therefore the divine vengeance is reserving you for eternal fire and torments, which shall cling to you for all time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Were you not ashamed, man as you are, yet most savage, to cut out the tongues of men of like feeling and origin, and having thus abused to torture them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But they, bravely dying, fulfilled their religion towards God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But you shall groan according to your deserts for having slain without cause the champions of virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Therefore, he continued, I myself, being about to die,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> will not forsake my brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And I call upon the God of my fathers to be merciful to my race.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But you, both living and dead, he will punish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Thus having prayed, he hurled himself into the pans; and so expired.</span>
