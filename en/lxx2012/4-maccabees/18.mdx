<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> O Israelitish children, descendants of the seed of Abraham, obey this law, and in every way be religious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Knowing that religious reasoning is lord of the passions, and those not only inward but outward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> When those persons giving up their bodies to pains for the sake of religion, were not only admired by men, but were deemed worthy of a divine portion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the nation through them obtained peace, and having renewed the observance of the law in their country, drove the enemy out of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the tyrant Antiochus was both punished upon earth, and is punished now he is dead; for when he was quite unable to compel the Israelites to adopt foreign customs, and to desert the manner of life of their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> then, departing from Jerusalem, he made war against the Persians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the righteous mother of the seven children spoke also as follows to her offspring: I was a pure virgin, and went not beyond my father's house; but I took care of the built-up rib.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> No destroyer of the desert, or ravisher of the plain, injured me; nor did the destructive, deceitful snake, make spoil of my chaste virginity; and I remained with my husband during the period of my prime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And these my children, having arrived at maturity, their father died: blessed was he! for having sought out a life of fertility in children, he was not grieved with a period of loss of children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And he used to teach you, when yet with you, the law and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> He used to read to you the slaying of Abel by Cain, and the offering up of Isaac, and the imprisonment of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And he used to tell you of the zealous Phinehas; and informed you of Ananias and Azarias, and Misael in the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And he used to glorify Daniel, who was in the den of lions, and pronounce him blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he used to put you in mind of the scripture of Esaias, which says, Even if you pass through the fire, it shall not burn you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> He chanted to you David, the hymn-writer, who says, Many are the afflictions of the just.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He declared the proverbs of Solomon, who says, He is a tree of life to all those who do His will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He used to verify Ezekiel, who said, Shall these dry bones live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For he did not forget the song which Moses taught, proclaiming, I will kill, and I will make to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> This is our life, and the length of our days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> O that bitter, and yet not bitter, day when the bitter tyrant of the Greeks, quenching fire with fire in his cruel caldrons, brought with boiling rage the seven sons of the daughter of Abraham to the catapelt, and to all his torments!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He pierced the balls of their eyes, and cut out their tongues, and put them to death with varied tortures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Therefore divine retribution pursued and will pursue the pestilent wretch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But the children of Abraham, with their victorious mother, are assembled together to the choir of their father; having received pure and immortal souls from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> To whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen.</span>
