<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Tobias then answered and said, Father, I will do all things which you have commanded me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> But how can I receive the money, seeing I know him not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then he gave him the handwriting, and said to him, Seek you a man which may go with you, while I yet live, and I will give him wages: and go and receive the money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Therefore when he went to seek a man, he found Raphael that was an angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> But he knew not; and he said to him, Canst you go with me to Rages? and know you those places well?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> To whom the angel said, I will go with you, and I know the way well: for I have lodged with our brother Gabael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Tobias said to him, Tarry for me, till I tell my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then he said to him, Go and wait not. So he went in and said to his father, Behold, I have found one which will go with me. Then he said, Call him to me, that I may know of what tribe he is, and whether he be a trusty man to go with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So he called him, and he came in, and they saluted one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Tobit said to him, Brother, show me of what tribe and family you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> To whom he said, Do you seek for a tribe or family, or an hired man to go with your son? Then Tobit said to him, I would know, brother, your kindred and name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then he said, I am Azarias, the son of Ananias the great, and of your brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then Tobit said, You are welcome, brother; be not now angry with me, because I have enquired to know your tribe and your family; for you are my brother, of an honest and good stock: for I know Ananias and Jonathas, sons of that great Samaias, as we went together to Jerusalem to worship, and offered the firstborn, and the tenths of the fruits; and they were not seduced with the error of our brethren: my brother, you are of a good stock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But tell me, what wages shall I give you? will you a drachm a day, and things necessary, as to my own son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Yes, moreover, if you⌃ return safe, I will add something to your wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So they were well pleased. Then said he to Tobias, Prepare yourself for the journey, and God send you a good journey. And when his son had prepared all things far the journey, his father said, Go you with this man, and God, which dwells in heaven, prosper your journey, and the angel of God keep you company. So they went forth both, and the young man's dog with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But Anna his mother wept, and said to Tobit, Why have you sent away our son? is he not the staff of our hand, in going in and out before us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Be not greedy to add money to money: but let it be as refuse in respect of our child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For that which the Lord has given us to live with does suffice us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then said Tobit to her, Take no care, my sister; he shall return in safety, and your eyes shall see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For the good angel will keep him company, and his journey shall be prosperous, and he shall return safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then she made an end of weeping.</span>
