<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And when they had supped, they brought Tobias in to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And as he went, he remembered the words of Raphael, and took the ashes of the perfumes, and put the heart and the liver of the fish thereupon, and made a smoke therewith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The which smell when the evil spirit had smelled, he fled into the utmost parts of Egypt, and the angel bound him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And after that they were both shut in together, Tobias rose out of the bed, and said, Sister, arise, and let us pray that God would have pity on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then began Tobias to say, Blessed are you, O God of our fathers, and blessed is your holy and glorious name for ever; let the heavens bless you, and all your creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> You made Adam, and gave him Eve his wife for an helper and stay: of them came mankind: you have said, It is not good that man should be alone; let us make to him an aid like to himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And now, O Lord, I take not this my sister for lush but uprightly: therefore mercifully ordain that we may become aged together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And she said with him, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So they slept both that night. And Raguel arose, and went and made a grave,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Saying, I fear lest he also be dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> But when Raguel was come into his house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He said to his wife Edna. Send one of the maids, and let her see whether he be alive: if he be not, that we may bury him, and no man know it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> So the maid opened the door, and went in, and found them both asleep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And came forth, and told them that he was alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then Raguel praised God, and said, O God, you are worthy to be praised with all pure and holy praise; therefore let your saints praise you with all your creatures; and let all your angels and your elect praise you for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> You are to be praised, for you have made me joyful; and that is not come to me which I suspected; but you have dealt with us according to your great mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> You are to be praised because you have had mercy of two that were the only begotten children of their fathers: grant them mercy, O Lord, and finish their life in health with joy and mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then Raguel bade his servants to fill the grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And he kept the wedding feast fourteen days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> For before the days of the marriage were finished, Raguel had said to him by an oath, that he should not depart till the fourteen days of the marriage were expired;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And then he should take the half of his goods, and go in safety to his father; and should have the rest when I and my wife be dead.</span>
