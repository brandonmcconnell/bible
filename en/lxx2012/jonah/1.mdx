<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 11:32; Luke 11:29–30; Kings IV 14:25"> Now the word of the Lord came to Jonas the son of Amathi, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 18:20–21; Genesis 10:11; Esias 58:1"> Rise, and go to Nineve, the great city, and preach in it; for the cry of its wickedness is come up to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 139:7–12; Genesis 4:16; Joshua 19:46"> But Jonas rose up to flee to Tharsis from the presence of the Lord. And he went down to Joppa, and found a ship going to Tharsis: and he paid his fare, and went up into it, to sail with them to Tharsis from the presence of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 107:23–31; Matthew 8:24–27; Exodus 10:19"> And the Lord raised up a wind on the sea; and there was a great storm on the sea, and the ship was in danger of being broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 2:28; Acts 27:18–19; Kings III 18:26"> And the sailors were alarmed, and cried every one to his god, and cast out the wares that were in the ship into the sea, that it might be lightened of them. But Jonas was gone down into the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. hollow.">a</a></sup>hold of the ship, and was asleep, and snored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jonas 3:9; Psalms 107:28–29; Ephesians 5:14"> And the shipmaster came to him, and said to him, Why snore you? arise, and call upon your God, that God may save us, and we perish not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 16:33; Joshua 7:13–18; Kings I 10:20–21"> And each man said to his neighbor, Come, let us cast lots, and find out for whose sake this mischief is upon us. So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 47:3; Kings I 30:13; Joshua 7:19"> And they said to him, Tell us<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. + 'for those whose cause this evil is upon us'">b</a></sup>what is your occupation, and whence come you, and of what country and what people are you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 146:5–6; Psalms 95:5–6; Nehemiah 9:6"> And he said to them, I am a servant of the Lord; and I worship the Lord God of heaven, who made the sea, and the dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jonas 1:3; Job 27:22; Joshua 7:25"> Then the men feared exceedingly, and said to him, What is this that you have done? for the men knew that he was fleeing from the face of the Lord, because he had told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings II 21:1–6; Michaeas 6:6–7; Kings I 6:2–3"> And they said to him, What shall we do to you, that the sea may be calm to us? for the sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. went.">c</a></sup>rose, and lifted its wave exceedingly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Kings II 24:17; Chronicles I 21:17; Joshua 7:12"> And Jonas said to them, Take me up, and cast me into the sea, and the sea shall be calm to you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Job 34:29"> And the men tried hard to return to the land, and were not able: for the sea<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Lit. went and rose up more.">d</a></sup>rose and grew more and more tempestuous against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 115:3; Psalms 135:6; Ephesians 1:11"> And they cried to the Lord, and said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. by no means. See Acts 10. 14">e</a></sup>Forbid it, Lord: let us not perish for the sake of this man's life, and bring not righteous blood upon us: for you, Lord, have done as you would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 8:24; Psalms 93:3–4; Psalms 107:29"> So they took Jonas, and cast him out into the sea: and the sea ceased from its raging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 50:14; Genesis 8:20; Daniel 6:26"> And the men feared the Lord very greatly, and offered a sacrifice to the Lord, and vowed vows.</span>
