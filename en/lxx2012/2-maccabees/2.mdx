<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> It is also found in the records, that Jeremy the prophet commanded them that were carried away to take of the fire, as it has been signified:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And how that the prophet, having given them the law, charged them not to forget the commandments of the Lord, and that they should not err in their minds, when they see images of silver and gold, with their ornaments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And with other such speeches exhorted he them, that the law should not depart from their hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> It was also contained in the same writing, that the prophet, being warned of God, commanded the tabernacle and the ark to go with him, as he went forth into the mountain, where Moses climbed up, and saw the heritage of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And when Jeremy came there, he found an hollow cave, wherein he laid the tabernacle, and the ark, and the altar of incense, and so stopped the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And some of those that followed him came to mark the way, but they could not find it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Which when Jeremy perceived, he blamed them, saying, As for that place, it shall be unknown until the time that God gather his people again together, and receive them to mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then shall the Lord show them these things, and the glory of the Lord shall appear, and the cloud also, as it was showed under Moses, and as when Solomon desired that the place might be honorably sanctified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It was also declared, that he being wise offered the sacrifice of dedication, and of the finishing of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And as when Moses prayed to the Lord, the fire came down from heaven, and consumed the sacrifices: even so prayed Solomon also, and the fire came down from heaven, and consumed the burnt offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Moses said, Because the sin offering was not to be eaten, it was consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So Solomon kept those eight days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The same things also were reported in the writings and commentaries of Neemias; and how he founding a library gathered together the acts of the kings, and the prophets, and of David, and the epistles of the kings concerning the holy gifts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> In like manner also Judas gathered together all those things that were lost by reason of the war we had, and they remain with us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Therefore if you⌃ have need thereof, send some to fetch them to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Whereas we then are about to celebrate the purification, we have written to you, and you⌃ shall do well, if you⌃ keep the same days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> We hope also, that the God, that delivered all his people, and gave them all an heritage, and the kingdom, and the priesthood, and the sanctuary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> As he promised in the law, will shortly have mercy upon us, and gather us together out of every land under heaven into the holy place: for he has delivered us out of great troubles, and has purified the place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Now as concerning Judas Maccabeus, and his brethren, and the purification of the great temple, and the dedication of the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And the wars against Antiochus Epiphanes, and Eupator his son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And the manifest signs that came from heaven to those that behaved themselves manfully to their honor for Judaism: so that, being but a few, they overcame the whole country, and chased barbarous multitudes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And recovered again the temple renowned all the world over, and freed the city, and upheld the laws which were going down, the Lord being gracious to them with all favor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> All these things, I say, being declared by Jason of Cyrene in five books, we will assay to abridge in one volume.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For considering the infinite number, and the difficulty which they find that desire to look into the narrations of the story, for the variety of the matter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> We have been careful, that they that will read may have delight, and that they that are desirous to commit to memory might have ease, and that all into whose hands it comes might have profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Therefore to us, that have taken upon us this painful labor of abridging, it was not easy, but a matter of sweat and watching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Even as it is no ease to him that prepares a banquet, and seeks the benefit of others: yet for the pleasuring of many we will undertake gladly this great pains;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Leaving to the author the exact handling of every particular, and laboring to follow the rules of an abridgement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For as the master builder of a new house must care for the whole building; but he that undertakes to set it out, and paint it, must seek out fit things for the adorning thereof: even so I think it is with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> To stand upon every point, and go over things at large, and to be curious in particulars, belongs to the first author of the story:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> But to use brevity, and avoid much laboring of the work, is to be granted to him that will make an abridgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Here then will we begin the story: only adding thus much to that which has been said, that it is a foolish thing to make a long prologue, and to be short in the story itself.</span>
