<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And it happened, after that Alexander son of Philip, the Macedonian, who came out of the land of Chettiim, had struck Darius king of the Persians and Medes, that he reigned in his stead, the first over Greece,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And made many wars, and won many strong holds, and killed the kings of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And went through to the ends of the earth, and took spoils of many nations, insomuch that the earth was quiet before him; whereupon he was exalted and his heart was lifted up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And he gathered a mighty strong host and ruled over countries, and nations, and kings, who became tributaries to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And after these things he fell sick, and perceived that he should die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Therefore he called his servants, such as were honorable, and had been brought up with him from his youth, and parted his kingdom among them, while he was yet alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> So Alexander reigned twelve years, and then died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And his servants bare rule every one in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And after his death they all put crowns upon themselves; so did their sons after them many years: and evils were multiplied in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And there came out of them a wicked root Antiochus surnamed Epiphanes, son of Antiochus the king, who had been an hostage at Rome, and he reigned in the hundred and thirty and seventh year of the kingdom of the Greeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> In those days went there out of Israel wicked men, who persuaded many, saying, Let us go and make a covenant with the heathen that are round about us: for since we departed from them we have had much sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So this device pleased them well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then certain of the people were so forward herein, that they went to the king, who gave them licence to do after the ordinances of the heathen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Whereupon they built a place of exercise at Jerusalem according to the customs of the heathen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And made themselves uncircumcised, and forsook the holy covenant, and joined themselves to the heathen, and were sold to do mischief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now when the kingdom was established before Antiochus, he thought to reign over Egypt that he might have the dominion of two realms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Therefore he entered into Egypt with a great multitude, with chariots, and elephants, and horsemen, and a great navy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And made war against Ptolemee king of Egypt: but Ptolemee was afraid of him, and fled; and many were wounded to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Thus they got the strong cities in the land of Egypt and he took the spoils thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And after that Antiochus had struck Egypt, he returned again in the hundred forty and third year, and went up against Israel and Jerusalem with a great multitude,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And entered proudly into the sanctuary, and took away the golden altar, and the candlestick of light, and all the vessels thereof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And the table of the show bread, and the pouring vessels, and the vials. and the censers of gold, and the veil, and the crown, and the golden ornaments that were before the temple, all which he pulled off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> He took also the silver and the gold, and the precious vessels: also he took the hidden treasures which he found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And when he had taken all away, he went into his own land, having made a great massacre, and spoken very proudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Therefore there was a great mourning in Israel, in every place where they were;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So that the princes and elders mourned, the virgins and young men were made feeble, and the beauty of women was changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Every bridegroom took up lamentation, and she that sat in the marriage chamber was in heaviness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The land also was moved for the inhabitants thereof, and all the house of Jacob was covered with confusion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And after two years fully expired the king sent his chief collector of tribute to the cities of Juda, who came to Jerusalem with a great multitude,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And spoke peaceful words to them, but all was deceit: for when they had given him credence, he fell suddenly upon the city, and struck it very sore, and destroyed much people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And when he had taken the spoils of the city, he set it on fire, and pulled down the houses and walls thereof on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But the women and children took they captive, and possessed the cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Then builded they the city of David with a great and strong wall, and with mighty towers, and made it a strong hold for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And they put therein a sinful nation, wicked men, and fortified themselves therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> They stored it also with armor and food, and when they had gathered together the spoils of Jerusalem, they laid them up there, and so they became a sore snare:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> For it was a place to lie in wait against the sanctuary, and an evil adversary to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thus they shed innocent blood on every side of the sanctuary, and defiled it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Insomuch that the inhabitants of Jerusalem fled because of them: whereupon the city was made an habitation of strangers, and became strange to those that were born in her; and her own children left her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Her sanctuary was laid waste like a wilderness, her feasts were turned into mourning, her sabbaths into reproach her honor into contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> As had been her glory, so was her dishonor increased, and her excellency was turned into mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Moreover king Antiochus wrote to his whole kingdom, that all should be one people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And every one should leave his laws: so all the heathen agreed according to the commandment of the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Yes, many also of the Israelites consented to his religion, and sacrificed to idols, and profaned the sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> For the king had sent letters by messengers to Jerusalem and the cities of Juda that they should follow the strange laws of the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And forbid burnt offerings, and sacrifice, and drink offerings, in the temple; and that they should profane the sabbaths and festival days:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And pollute the sanctuary and holy people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Set up altars, and groves, and chapels of idols, and sacrifice swine's flesh, and unclean beasts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> That they should also leave their children uncircumcised, and make their souls abominable with all manner of uncleanness and profanation:</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> To the end they might forget the law, and change all the ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And whoever would not do according to the commandment of the king, he said, he should die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> In the selfsame manner wrote he to his whole kingdom, and appointed overseers over all the people, commanding the cities of Juda to sacrifice, city by city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Then many of the people were gathered to them, to wit every one that forsook the law; and so they committed evils in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> And drove the Israelites into secret places, even wherever they could flee for succour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Now the fifteenth day of the month Casleu, in the hundred forty and fifth year, they set up the abomination of desolation upon the altar, and builded idol altars throughout the cities of Juda on every side;</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> And burnt incense at the doors of their houses, and in the streets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And when they had torn in pieces the books of the law which they found, they burnt them with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> And whoever was found with any the book of the testament, or if any committed to the law, the king's commandment was, that they should put him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> Thus did they by their authority to the Israelites every month, to as many as were found in the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Now the five and twentieth day of the month they did sacrifice upon the idol altar, which was upon the altar of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> At which time according to the commandment they put to death certain women, that had caused their children to be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And they hanged the infants about their necks, and rifled their houses, and killed them that had circumcised them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> Howbeit many in Israel were fully resolved and confirmed in themselves not to eat any unclean thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> Therefore the rather to die, that they might not be defiled with meats, and that they might not profane the holy covenant: so then they died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> And there was very great wrath upon Israel.</span>
