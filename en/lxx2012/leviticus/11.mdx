<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 15:11; Leviticus 11:11; Hebrews 9:10"> Speak you⌃ to the sons of Israel, saying, These are the beasts which you⌃ shall eat of all beasts that are upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 2:10; 2 Corinthians 6:17; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> Every beast parting the hoof and making divisions of two claws, and chewing the cud among beasts, these you⌃ shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But of these you⌃ shall not eat, of those that chew the cud, and of those that part the hoofs, and divide claws; the camel, because it chews the cud, but does not divide the hoof, this is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 104:18; Proverbs 30:26; Matthew 7:26"> And the rabbit, because it chews the cud, but does not divide the hoof, this is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:7"> And the hare, because it does not chew the cud, and does not divide the hoof, this is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 66:17; Esias 65:4; Esias 66:3"> And the swine, because this animal divides the hoof, and makes claws of the hoof, and it does not chew the cud, is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 52:11; Colossians 2:16; Hebrews 9:10"> You⌃ shall not eat of their flesh, and you⌃ shall not touch their carcasses; these are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 5:6; James 2:18; Acts 20:21"> And these are what you⌃ shall eat of all that are in the waters: all things that have fins and scales in the waters, and in the seas, and in the brooks, these you⌃ shall eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 29:27; Leviticus 7:18; Psalms 139:21–22"> And all things which have not fins or scales in the water, or in the seas, and in the brooks, of all which the waters produce, and of every soul living in the water, are an abomination; and they shall be abominations to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> You⌃ shall not eat of their flesh, and you⌃ shall abhor their carcasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And all things that have not fins or scales of those that are in the waters, these are an abomination to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Job 39:27–30; Jeremias 4:22; Titus 3:3"> And these are the things which you⌃ shall abhor of birds, and they shall not be eaten, they are an abomination: the eagle and the ossifrage, and the sea-eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And the vulture, and the kite, and the like to it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 12:24; Kings III 17:6; Proverbs 30:17"> and the sparrow, and the owl, and the cormorant, and the like to it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 34:11–15; Ephesians 5:7–11; Ephesians 2:2–3"> and every raven, and the birds like it, and the hawk and his like,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and the night-raven and the cormorant and the stork,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and the red-bill, and the pelican, and swan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and the heron, and the lapwing, and the like to it, and the hoopoe and the bat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Philippians 3:18–19; Leviticus 11:23"> And all winged creatures that creep, which go upon four feet, are abominations to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But these you⌃ shall eat of the creeping winged animals, which go upon four feet, which have legs above their feet, to leap with on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Mark 1:6; Exodus 10:4–5"> And these of them you⌃ shall eat: the caterpillar and his like, and the attacus and his like, and the cantharus and his like, and the locust and his like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Every creeping thing from among the birds, which has four feet, is an abomination to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:33; Ephesians 2:1–3; 2 Corinthians 6:17"> And by these you⌃ shall be defiled; every one that touches their carcasses shall be unclean till the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:5; Leviticus 14:8; Numbers 31:24"> And every one that takes of their dead bodies shall wash his garments, and shall be unclean till the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And whichever among the beasts divides the hoof and makes claws, and does not chew the cud, shall be unclean to you; every one that touches their dead bodies shall be unclean till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 11:23; Leviticus 11:20"> And every one among all the wild beasts that moves upon its fore feet, which goes on all four, is unclean to you; every one that touches their dead bodies shall be unclean till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25"> And he that takes of their dead bodies shall wash his garments, and shall be unclean till evening: these are unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Leviticus 11:41–42; 2 Timothy 3:2–5; Psalms 10:3"> And these are unclean to you of reptiles upon the earth, the weasel, and the mouse, and the lizard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> the ferret, and the chameleon, and the evet, and the newt, and the mole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 11:24–25; Leviticus 11:8"> These are unclean to you of all the reptiles which are on the earth; every one who touches their carcasses shall be unclean till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Titus 3:5; Titus 2:14"> And on whatever one of their dead bodies shall fall it shall be unclean; whatever wooden vessel, or garment, or skin, or sack it may be, every vessel in which work should be done, shall be dipped in water, and shall be unclean till evening; and then it shall be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 14:45"> And every earthen vessel into which one of these things shall fall, whatever is inside it, shall be unclean, and it shall be broken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Titus 1:15; Proverbs 21:4; Proverbs 21:27"> And all food that is eaten, on which water shall come from such a vessel, shall be unclean; and every beverage which is drunk in any such vessel, shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 15:12; Leviticus 6:28; Leviticus 11:33"> And every thing on which there shall fall of their dead bodies shall be unclean; ovens and stands for jars shall be broken down: these are unclean, and they shall be unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zacharias 13:1; John 4:14"> Only if the water be of fountains of water, or a pool, or confluence of water, it shall be clean; but he that touches their carcasses shall be unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:37; 1 Peter 1:23; 1 John 5:18"> And if one of their carcasses should fall upon any sowing seed which shall be sown, it shall be clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But if water be poured on any seed, and one of their dead bodies fall upon it, it is unclean to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Leviticus 11:40; Leviticus 11:24; Leviticus 15:7"> And if one of the cattle die, which it is lawful for you to eat, he that touches their carcasses shall be unclean till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Leviticus 22:8; Jezekiel 44:31; Deuteronomy 14:21"> And he that eats of their carcasses shall wash his garments, and be unclean till evening; and he that carries any of their carcasses shall wash his garments, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 11:29; Leviticus 11:20; Leviticus 11:23"> And every reptile that creeps on the earth, this shall be an abomination to you; it shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Titus 1:12; Esias 65:25; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> And every animal that creeps on its belly, and every one that goes on four feet continually, which abounds with feet among all the reptiles creeping upon the earth—you⌃ shall not eat it, for it is an abomination to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Leviticus 20:25; Leviticus 11:41–42"> And you⌃ shall not defile your souls with any of the reptiles that creep upon the earth, and you⌃ shall not be polluted with them, and you⌃ shall not be unclean by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 20:2; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Leviticus 19:2"> For I am the Lord your God; and you⌃ shall be sanctified, and you⌃ shall be holy, because I the Lord your God am holy; and you⌃ shall not defile your souls with any of the reptiles creeping upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44; Exodus 20:2; Exodus 6:7"> For I am the Lord who brought you up out of the land of Egypt to be your God; and you⌃ shall be holy, for I the Lord am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 14:54; Leviticus 15:32; Jezekiel 43:12"> This is the law concerning beasts and birds and every living creature moving in the water, and every living creature creeping on the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Jezekiel 44:23; Romans 14:13–23"> to distinguish between the unclean and the clean; and between those that bring forth alive, such as should be eaten, and those that bring forth alive, such as should not be eaten.</span>
