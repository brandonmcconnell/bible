<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 28:1; Jezekiel 43:26–27; Leviticus 15:14"> And it came to pass on the eighth day, that Moses called Aaron and his sons, and the elders of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Hebrews 10:10–14; Leviticus 8:14"> and Moses said to Aaron, Take to yourself a young calf of the herd for a sin-offering, and a ram for a whole burnt offering, unblemished, and offer them before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 6:17; Revelation 5:9; Titus 2:14"> And speak to the elders of Israel, saying, Take one kid of the goats for a sin-offering, and a young calf, and a lamb of a year old for a whole burnt offering, spotless,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 29:43; Jezekiel 43:2; Leviticus 9:23"> and a calf and a ram for a peace offering before the Lord, and fine flour mingled with oil, for today the Lord will appear among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Chronicles II 5:2–3; Exodus 19:17; Nehemiah 8:1"> And they took as Moses commanded them before the tabernacle of witness, and all the congregation drew near, and they stood before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 9:23; Jezekiel 43:2; Exodus 24:16"> And Moses said, This is the thing which the Lord has spoken; do it, and the glory of the Lord shall appear among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 5:3; Hebrews 5:1; Leviticus 4:3"> And Moses said to Aaron, Draw near to the altar, and offer your sin-offering, and your whole burnt offering, and make atonement for yourself, and for your house; and offer the gifts of the people, and make atonement for them, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 4:1–12; Leviticus 1:4–5; Leviticus 4:29"> And Aaron drew near to the altar, and killed the calf of his sin-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 4:6–7; Leviticus 8:15; Leviticus 4:25"> And the sons of Aaron brought the blood to him, and he dipped his finger into the blood, and put it on the horns of the altar, and he poured out the blood at the bottom of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 8:16–17; Proverbs 23:26; Leviticus 3:9–11"> And he offered up on the altar the fat and the kidneys and the lobe of the liver of the sin-offering, according as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 8:17; Leviticus 4:11–12; Leviticus 16:27–28"> And the flesh and the hide he burnt with fire outside of the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; Ephesians 5:25–27; Leviticus 1:1–17"> And he killed the whole burnt offering; and the sons of Aaron brought the blood to him, and he poured it on the altar round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And they brought the whole burnt offering, according to its pieces; them and the head he put upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 8:21"> And he washed the belly and the feet with water, and he put them on the whole burnt offering on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 4:27–31; Hebrews 5:3; Leviticus 9:3"> And he brought the gift of the people, and took the goat of the sin-offering of the people, and killed it, and purified it as also the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 9:12–14; Leviticus 8:18–21; Hebrews 10:1–22"> And he brought the whole burnt offering, and offered it in due form.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1–2; John 6:53; Leviticus 9:1"> And he brought the sacrifice and filled his hands with it, and laid it on the altar, besides the morning whole burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:20; Romans 5:1; Ephesians 2:14–17"> And he killed the calf, and the ram of the sacrifice of peace-offering of the people; and the sons of Aaron brought the blood to him, and he poured it out on the altar round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 3:16; Leviticus 9:10; Leviticus 3:9"> And he took the fat of the calf, and the hind quarters of the ram, and the fat covering the belly, and the two kidneys, and the fat upon them, and the caul on the liver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 3:14–17; Leviticus 7:29–34"> And he put the fat on the breasts, and offered the fat on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 7:30–34; Leviticus 7:24; Exodus 29:24"> And Aaron separated the breast and the right shoulder as a choice-offering before the Lord, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Luke 24:50; Numbers 6:23–27"> And Aaron lifted up his hands on the people and blessed them; and after he had offered the sin-offering, and the whole burnt offerings, and the peace-offerings, he came down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 9:6; Numbers 16:19; Luke 1:21–22"> And Moses and Aaron entered into the tabernacle of witness. And they came out and blessed all the people, and the glory of the Lord appeared to all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Judges 6:21; Kings III 18:38–39; Chronicles I 21:26"> And fire came forth from the Lord, and devoured the offerings on the altar, both the whole burnt offerings and the fat; and all the people saw, and were amazed, and fell upon their faces.</span>
