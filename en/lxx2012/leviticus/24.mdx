<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; Ephesians 5:8–14; Exodus 27:20–21"> Charge the children of Israel, and let them take for you pure olive oil beaten for the light, to burn a lamp continually,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> outside the veil in the tabernacle of witness; and Aaron and his sons shall burn it from evening until morning before the Lord continually, a perpetual statute throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 31:8; Exodus 37:17–24; Exodus 39:37"> You⌃ shall burn the lamps on the pure lamp-stand before the Lord till the morrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:30; Kings III 18:31; Exodus 40:23"> And you⌃ shall take fine flour, and make of it twelve loaves; each loaf shall be of two tenth parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 7:48; Exodus 25:23–24; Chronicles II 4:19"> And you⌃ shall put them in two rows, each row containing six loaves, on the pure table before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 2:2; Revelation 8:3–4; Genesis 9:16"> And you⌃ shall put on each row pure frankincense and salt; and these things shall be for loaves for a memorial, set forth before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:4; Numbers 4:7; Chronicles I 9:32"> On the sabbath-day they shall be set forth before the Lord continually before the children of Israel, for an everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 8:31; Luke 6:4; Mark 2:26"> And they shall be for Aaron and his sons, and they shall eat them in the holy place: for this is their most holy portion of the offerings made to the Lord, a perpetual statute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Exodus 12:38"> And there went forth a son of an Israelitish woman, and he was son of an Egyptian man among the sons of Israel; and they fought in the camp, the son of the Israelitish woman, and a man who was an Israelite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 24:15–16; Exodus 3:15"> And the son of the Israelitish woman named THE NAME and curse; and they brought him to Moses: and his mother's name was Salomith, daughter of Dabri of the tribe of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 18:15–16; Numbers 15:34; Numbers 27:5"> And they put him in ward, to judge him by the command of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:7; Leviticus 20:27; Deuteronomy 21:21"> Bring forth him that cursed outside the camp, and all who heard shall lay their hands upon his head, and all the congregation shall stone him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 9:13; Exodus 22:28; Leviticus 5:1"> And speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, Whosoever shall curse God shall bear his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 12:31; James 2:7; John 10:33–36"> And he that names the name of the Lord, let him die the death: let all the congregation of Israel stone him with stones; whether he be a stranger or a native, let him die for naming the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 9:5–6; Deuteronomy 19:11–12; Deuteronomy 27:24"> And whoever shall strike a man and he die, let him die the death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 24:21; Exodus 21:34–36"> And whoever shall strike a beast, and it shall die, let him render life for life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:38; Matthew 7:2; Deuteronomy 19:21"> And whoever shall inflict a blemish on his neighbor, as he has done to him, so shall it be done to himself in return;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:21; Matthew 5:38; Exodus 21:23–25"> bruise for bruise, eye for eye, tooth for tooth: as any one may inflict a blemish on a man, so shall it be rendered to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 24:17–18; Exodus 21:33"> Whosoever shall strike a man, and he shall die, let him die the death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 12:49; Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 9:14"> There shall be one judgment for the stranger and the native, for I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 10:28–29; Hebrews 2:2–3; Numbers 15:35–36"> And Moses spoke to the children of Israel, and they brought him that had cursed out of the camp, and stoned him with stones: and the children of Israel did as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
