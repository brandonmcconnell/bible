<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 11:44–45; Leviticus 20:7; 1 Peter 1:15–16"> Speak to the congregation of the children of Israel, and you shall say to them, You⌃ shall be holy; for I the Lord your God am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Exodus 20:8; Hebrews 12:9"> Let every one of you reverence his father and his mother; and you⌃ shall keep my sabbaths: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:1; Exodus 20:23; Psalms 115:4–7"> You⌃ shall not follow idols, and you⌃ shall not make to yourselves molten gods: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 22:19; Leviticus 22:29; Leviticus 7:16"> And if you⌃ will sacrifice a peace-offering to the Lord, you⌃ shall offer it acceptable from yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 7:11–17"> In what day soever you⌃ shall sacrifice it, it shall be eaten; and on the following day, and if any of it should be left till the third day, it shall be thoroughly burnt with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 7:18–21; Leviticus 22:25; Jeremias 16:18"> And if it should be at all eaten on the third day, it is unfit for sacrifice: it shall not be accepted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 22:15; Leviticus 5:1"> And he that eats it shall bear his iniquity, because he has profaned the holy things of the Lord; and the souls that eat it shall be destroyed from among their people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Deuteronomy 24:19–22; Ruth 2:2"> And when you⌃ reap the harvest of your land, you⌃ shall not complete the reaping of your field with exactness, and you shall not gather that which falls from your reaping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 17:6; Leviticus 25:6; Esias 24:13"> And you shall not go over the gathering of your vineyard, neither shall you gather the remaining grapes of your vineyard: you shall leave them for the poor and the stranger: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 101:7; Ephesians 4:25; Colossians 3:9"> You⌃ shall not steal, you⌃ shall not lie, neither shall one bear false witness as an informer against his neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Deuteronomy 5:11; Leviticus 18:21"> And you⌃ shall not swear unjustly by my name, and you⌃ shall not profane the holy name of your God: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:4; Malachias 3:5; Deuteronomy 24:14–15"> You shall not injure your neighbor, neither do you rob him, neither shall the wages of your hireling remain with you until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Leviticus 19:32; Leviticus 25:17"> You shall not revile the deaf, neither shall you put a stumbling block in the way of the blind; and you shall fear the Lord your God: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:17; Proverbs 24:23; Deuteronomy 27:19"> You shall not act unjustly in judgment: you shall not accept the person of the poor, nor admire the person of the mighty; with justice shall you judge your neighbor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 23:1; Exodus 23:7; Proverbs 20:19"> You shall not walk deceitfully among your people; you shall not rise up against the blood of your neighbor: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 2:9; 1 John 2:11; Luke 17:3"> You shall not hate your brother in your heart: you shall in any wise rebuke your neighbor, so you shall not bear sin on his account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 13:9; Matthew 22:39–40; Mark 12:31–34"> And your hand shall not avenge you; and you shall not be angry with the children of your people; and you shall love your neighbor as yourself; I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Deuteronomy 22:9–11; Matthew 9:16–17"> You⌃ shall observe my law: you shall not let your cattle gender with one of a different kind, and you shall not sow your vineyard with diverse seed; and you shall not put upon yourself a mingled garment woven of two materials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:23–24; Exodus 21:20–21"> And if any one lie carnally with a woman, and she should be a home-servant kept for a man, and she has not been ransomed, and her freedom has not been given to her, they shall be visited with punishment; but they shall not die, because she was not set at liberty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 6:6–7; Leviticus 5:1–6; Leviticus 5:15"> And he shall bring for his trespass to the Lord to the door of the tabernacle of witness, a ram for a trespass-offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:26; Leviticus 4:20"> And the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the trespass-offering, before the Lord, for the sin which he sinned; and the sin which he sinned shall be forgiven him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 22:27; Acts 7:51; Jeremias 9:25–26"> And whenever you⌃ shall enter into the land which the Lord your God gives you, and shall plant any fruit tree, then shall you⌃ purge away its uncleanness; its fruit shall be three years uncleansed to you, it shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 3:9; Deuteronomy 18:4; Numbers 18:12–13"> And in the fourth year all its fruit shall be holy, a subject of praise to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Aggaeus 1:9–11; Malachias 3:8–10; Leviticus 26:3–4"> And in the fifth year you⌃ shall eat the fruit, its produce is an increase to you. I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:10–14; Kings IV 17:17; Chronicles II 33:6"> Eat not on the mountains, nor shall you⌃ employ auguries, nor divine by inspection of birds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 21:5; Deuteronomy 14:1; Jezekiel 44:20"> You⌃ shall not make a round cutting of the hair of your head, nor disfigure your beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:1; Leviticus 21:5; Kings III 18:28"> And you⌃ shall not make cuttings in your body for a dead body, and you⌃ shall not inscribe on yourselves any marks. I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17–18; Leviticus 21:7; Osee 4:12–14"> You shall not profane your daughter to prostitute her; so the land shall not go a whoring, and the land be filled with iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 26:2; Leviticus 19:3; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> You⌃ shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuaries: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Esias 8:19; Leviticus 20:27; Leviticus 20:6–7"> You⌃ shall not attend to those who have in them divining spirits, nor attach yourselves to enchanters, to pollute yourselves with them: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:1; Lamentations 5:12; Proverbs 16:31"> You shall rise up before the hoary head, and honor the face of the old man, and shall fear your God: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21; Malachias 3:5"> And if there should come to you a stranger in your land, you⌃ shall not afflict him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Leviticus 19:18; Exodus 12:48–49"> The stranger that comes to you shall be among you as the native, and you shall love him as yourself; for you⌃ were strangers in the land of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:15; Deuteronomy 25:13; Proverbs 16:11"> You⌃ shall not act unrighteously in judgment, in measures and weights and scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Exodus 20:2; Deuteronomy 25:13–15"> There shall be among you just balances and just weights and just liquid measure. I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:1; Leviticus 18:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:1"> And you⌃ shall keep all my law and all my ordinances, and you⌃ shall do them: I am the Lord your God.</span>
