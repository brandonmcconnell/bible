<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings II 19:41; Judges 12:1–6; Job 5:2"> And the men of Ephraim said to Gedeon, What is this that you have done to us, in that you did not call us when you went to fight with Madiam? and they chode with him sharply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:4–7; Philippians 2:2–3; James 3:13–18"> And he said to them, What have I now done in comparison of you? is not the gleaning of Ephraim better than the vintage of Abiezer?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:1; Judges 7:24–25; John 4:37"> The Lord has delivered into your hand the princes of Madiam, Oreb and Zeb; and what could I do in comparison of you? Then was their spirit calmed toward him, when he spoke this word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16; Kings I 14:28–29"> And Gedeon came to Jordan, and went over, himself and the three hundred with him, hungry, yet pursuing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Psalms 60:6; Kings I 25:18"> And he said to the men of Socchoth, Give, I pray you, bread to feed this people that follow me; because they are faint, and behold, I am following after Zebee and Salmana, kings of Madiam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 20:11; Philippians 2:21; Proverbs 18:23"> And the princes of Socchoth said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. is the hand.">a</a></sup>Are the hands of Zebee and Salmana now in your hand, that we should give bread to your host?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 7:15"> And Gedeon said, Therefore when the Lord gives Zebee and Salmana into my hand, then will I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. dig down.">b</a></sup>tear your flesh with the thorns of the wilderness, and the Barkenim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:30–31; Kings III 12:25"> And he went up thence to Phanuel, and spoke to them likewise: and the men of Phanuel answered him as the men of Socchoth had answered him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 8:17; Kings III 22:27–28"> And Gedeon said to the men of Phanuel, When I return in peace, I will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. thresh.">c</a></sup>break down this tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 20:25; Judges 20:2"> And Zebee and Salmana were in Carcar, and their host was with them, about fifteen thousand, all that were left of all the host of the aliens; and they that fell were one hundred and twenty thousand men that drew the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 32:35; Numbers 32:42; Judges 18:27"> And Gedeon went up by the way of them that lived in tents, eastward of Nabai and Jegebal; and he struck the host, and the host was secure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Amos 2:14; Job 12:16–21"> And Zebee and Salmana fled; and he pursued after them, and took the two kings of Madiam, Zebee and Salmana, and discomfited all the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And Gedeon the son of Joas returned from the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. array.">d</a></sup>battle, down from the battle of Ares.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 1:24–25; Kings I 30:11–15"> And he took prisoner a young lad of the men of Socchoth, and questioned him; and he wrote to him the names of the princes of Socchoth and of their elders, seventy-seven men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 8:6–7"> And Gedeon came to the princes of Socchoth, and said, Behold Zebee and Salmana, about whom you⌃ reproached me, saying, Are the hands of Zebee and Salmana now in your hand, that we should give bread to your men that are faint?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 8:7; Ezra 2:6; Proverbs 10:13"> And he took the elders of the city with the thorns of the wilderness and the Barkenim, and with them he tore the men of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 8:9; Kings III 12:25"> And he overthrew the tower of Phanuel, and killed the men of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 4:6; Jude 1:16; Psalms 89:12"> And he said to Zebee and Salmana, Where are the men whom you⌃ killed in Thabor? and they said, As you, so were they, according to the likeness of the son of a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Gedeon said, They were my brethren and the sons of my mother: as the Lord lives, if you⌃ had preserved them alive, I would not have slain you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 149:9; Kings I 15:33; Joshua 10:24"> And he said to Jether his firstborn, Rise and kill them; but the lad drew not his sword, for he was afraid, for he was yet very young.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 83:11; Judges 8:26; Esias 3:18"> And Zebee and Salmana said, Rise you and fall upon us, for your power is as that of a man; and Gedeon arose, and killed Zebee and Salmana: and he took the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="i.e. round like the moon; perhaps circular, or in the form of a crescent.">e</a></sup>round ornaments that were on the necks of their camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 6:15; Judges 9:8–15; Kings I 12:12"> And the men of Israel said to Gedeon, Rule, my lord, over us, both you, and your son, and your son's son; for you have saved us out of the hand of Madiam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esias 33:22; Kings I 12:12; Kings I 10:19"> And Gedeon said to them, I will not rule, and my son shall not rule among you; the Lord shall rule over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:13; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 37:28"> And Gedeon said to them, I will make a request of you, and do you⌃ give me every man an earring out of his spoils: for they had golden earrings, for they were Ismaelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And they said,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. giving we will give.">f</a></sup>We will certainly give them: and he opened his garment, and each man cast therein an earring of his spoils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 18:16; John 19:2; Revelation 18:12"> And the weight of the golden earrings which he asked, was a thousand and seven hundred pieces of gold, besides the crescents, and the chains, and the garments, and the purple cloths that were on the kings of Madiam, and besides the chains that were on the necks of their camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:14; Psalms 106:39"> And Gedeon made an ephod of it, an set it in his city in Ephratha; and all Israel went there a whoring after it, and it became a stumbling block to Gedeon and his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 5:31; Judges 3:11; Judges 3:30"> And Madiam, was straitened before the children of Israel, and they did not lift up their head any more; and the land had rest forty years in the days of Gedeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 6:32; Judges 7:1; Kings I 12:11"> And Jerobaal the son of Joas went and sat in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 9:5; Judges 9:2; Kings III 11:3"> And Gedeon had seventy sons begotten of his body, for he had many wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 9:18; Genesis 22:24; Genesis 16:15"> And his concubine was in Sychem, and she also bore him a son, and gave him the name Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Judges 6:24; Job 5:26"> And Gedeon son of Joas died in his city, and he was buried in the sepulchre of Joas his father in Ephratha of Abi-Esdri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 9:46; Judges 2:19; Judges 9:4"> And it came to pass when Gedeon was dead, that the children of Israel turned, and went a whoring after Baalim, and made for themselves a covenant with Baal that he should be their god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 78:42; Psalms 106:21; Psalms 78:11"> And the children of Israel remembered not the Lord their God who had delivered them out of the hand of all that afflicted them round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:14–15; Judges 9:5; Judges 9:16–19"> And they did not deal mercifully with the house of Jerobaal, (the same is Gedeon) according to all the good which he did<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. with.">g</a></sup>to Israel.</span>
