<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26–29; Judges 2:19–20; Judges 2:13–14"> And the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the Lord, and the Lord gave them into the hand of Madiam seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:38; Kings I 13:6; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> And the hand of Madiam prevailed against Israel: and the children of Israel made for themselves because of Madiam the caves in the mountains, and the dens, and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="some read κρεμαστὰ ὀχυρώμασι, q.d. loca pensilia.">a</a></sup>holes in the rocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 6:33; Judges 3:13; Judges 7:12"> And it came to pass when the children of Israel sowed, that Madiam and Amalec went up, and the children of the east went up together with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:16; Deuteronomy 28:51; Michaeas 6:15"> And they encamped against them, and destroyed their fruits until they came to Gaza; and they left not the support of life in the land of Israel, not even ox or ass among the herds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 8:10; Jeremias 46:23"> For they and their stock came up, and their tents were with them, as the locust in multitude, and there was no number to them and their camels; and they came to the land of Israel, and laid it waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:9; Jeremias 5:17; Psalms 50:15"> And Israel was greatly impoverished<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. from before the face of.">b</a></sup>because of Madiam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And the children of Israel cried to the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. from before the face of.">c</a></sup>because of Madiam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:5–32; Esias 63:9–14; Psalms 136:10–16"> And the Lord sent<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. a man a prophet.">d</a></sup>a prophet to the children of Israel; and he said to them, Thus says the Lord God of Israel, I am he that brought you up out of the land of Egypt, and I brought you up out of the house of your bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 44:2–3"> And I delivered you out of the hand of Egypt, and out of the hand of all that afflicted you, and I cast them out before you; and I gave you their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 10:2; Kings IV 17:35–39; Jeremias 9:13"> And I said to you, I am the Lord your God: you⌃ shall not fear the gods of the Amorites, in whose land you⌃ dwell; but you⌃ listened not to my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 17:2; Hebrews 11:32; Judges 13:3"> And an angel of the Lord came, and sat down under the fir tree, which was in Ephratha in the land of Joas father of Esdri; and Gedeon his son was threshing wheat in a wine-press in order to escape from the face of Madiam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Luke 1:28; Judges 2:18"> And the angel of the Lord appeared to him and said to him, The Lord is with you, you mighty in strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 77:7–9; Chronicles II 15:2; Esias 59:1–2"> And Gedeon said to him, Be gracious<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Heb. כי a particle of entreaty, here rendered literally.">e</a></sup>with me, my Lord: but if the Lord is with us, why have these evils found us? and where are all his miracles, which our fathers have related to us, saying, Did not the Lord bring us up out of Egypt? and now he has cast us out, and given us into the hand of Madiam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 1:5–9; Hebrews 11:34; Kings I 12:11"> And the angel of the Lord turned to him, and said, Go in this your strength, and you shall save Israel out of the hand of Madiam: behold, I have sent you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 9:21; Exodus 3:11; Exodus 4:10"> And Gedeon said to him, Be gracious with me, my Lord: whereby shall I save Israel? behold, my thousand is weakened in Manasse, and I am the least in my father's house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Exodus 3:12; Acts 11:21"> And the angel of the Lord said to him, The Lord shall be with you, and you shall strike Madiam as one man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 86:17; Exodus 33:13; Esias 7:11"> And Gedeon said to him, If now I have found mercy in your eyes, and you will do this day for me all that you have spoken of with me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 13:15; Genesis 18:3; Genesis 18:5"> depart not hence until I come to you, and I will bring forth an offering and offer it before you: and he said, I will remain until you return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 18:6–8; Judges 13:15–19; Leviticus 2:4"> And Gedeon went in, and prepared a kid of the goats, and an ephah of fine flour unleavened; and he put the flesh in the basket, and poured the broth into the pot, and brought them forth to him under the turpentine tree, and drew near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 13:19; Kings III 18:33–34"> And the angel of God said to him, Take the flesh and the unleavened cakes, and put them on that rock, and pour out the broth close by: and he did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; Kings III 18:38; Chronicles II 7:1"> And the angel of the Lord stretched out the end of the rod that was in his hand, and touched the flesh and the unleavened bread; and fire came up out of the rock, and consumed the flesh and the unleavened bread, and the angel of the Lord vanished from his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 32:30; Exodus 33:20; Deuteronomy 5:26"> And Gedeon saw that he was an angel of the Lord; and Gedeon said, Ah, ah, Lord my God! for I have seen the angel of the Lord face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; John 20:26; Genesis 43:23"> And the Lord said to him, Peace be to you, fear not, you shall not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 22:14; Exodus 17:15; Judges 8:32"> And Gedeon built there an altar to the Lord, and called it The peace of the Lord, until this day, as it is still in Ephratha of the father of Esdri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 34:13; Judges 3:7; Acts 5:29"> And it came to pass in that night, that the Lord said to him, Take the young bullock which your father has, even the second bullock of seven years old, and you shall destroy the altar of Baal which your father has, and the grove which is by it you shall destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; Kings II 24:18; 1 Corinthians 14:33"> And you shall build an altar to the Lord your God on the top of this Maozi in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Or, the ordered place.">f</a></sup>the orderingit, and you shall take the second bullock, and shall offer up whole burnt offerings with the wood of the grove, which you shall destroy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; John 2:5; Deuteronomy 4:1"> And Gedeon took ten men of his servants, and did as the Lord spoke to him: and it came to pass, as he feared the house of his father and the men of the city<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, because of the doing it.">g</a></sup>if he should do it by day, that he did it by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the men of the city rose up early in the morning; and behold, the altar of Baal had been demolished, and the grove by it had been destroyed; and they saw the second bullock, which Gedeon offered on the altar that had been built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And a man said to his neighbor, Who has done this thing? and they enquired and searched, and learned that Gedeon the son of Joas had done this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 26:9; Philippians 3:6; Jeremias 50:38"> And the men of the city said to Joas, Bring out your son, and let him die, because he has destroyed the altar of Baal, and because he has destroyed the grove that is by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremias 10:11; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 5:11"> And Gedeon the son of Joas said to all the men who rose up against him, Do you⌃ now plead for Baal, or will you⌃ save him? whoever will plead for him, let him be slain this morning: if he be a god let him plead for himself, because one has thrown down his altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Kings I 12:11; Judges 7:1; Kings II 11:21"> And he called it in that day Jerobaal, saying, Let Baal plead thereby, because his altar has been thrown down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 17:16; Judges 6:3; Romans 8:35–39"> And all Madiam, and Amalek, and the sons of the east gathered themselves together, and encamped in the valley of Jezrael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 3:27; Chronicles II 24:20; Judges 3:10"> And the Spirit of the Lord came upon Gedeon, and he blew with the horn, and Abiezer came to help after him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Chronicles II 30:6–12; Judges 4:6"> And Gedeon sent messengers into all Manasse, and into Aser, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. in.">h</a></sup>into Zabulon, and into Nephthali; and he went up to meet them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Judges 6:14; Matthew 16:1; Judges 6:17–20"> And Gedeon said to God, If you will save Israel by my hand, as you have said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Osee 14:5; Matthew 10:5–6; Matthew 15:24"> behold, I put the fleece of wool in the threshing floor: if there be dew on the fleece only, and drought on all the ground, I shall know that you will save Israel by my hand, as you have said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Esias 35:7"> And it was so: and he rose up early in the morning, and wrung the fleece, and dew dropped from the fleece, a bowl full of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 18:32; Esias 50:2; Psalms 107:33–35"> And Gedeon said to God, Let not, I pray you, your anger be kindled with me, and I will speak yet once; I will even yet make one trial more with the fleece: let now the drought be upon the fleece only, and let there be dew on all the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And God did so in that night; and there was drought on the fleece only, and on all the ground there was dew.</span>
