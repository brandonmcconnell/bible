<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 8:10; Judges 20:17; Judges 20:15"> And all the tribes of Israel stood before the Lord in the assembly of the people of God, four hundred thousand footmen that drew sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 19:22–27; Luke 14:31–32; Luke 12:58–59"> And the children of Benjamin heard that the children of Israel were gone up to Massepha: and the children of Israel came and said, Tell us, where did this wickedness take place?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 19:15–28"> And the Levite, the husband of the woman that was slain, answered and said, I and my concubine went to Gabaa of Benjamin to lodge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:22; Judges 19:25–26; Genesis 19:4–8"> And the men of Gabaa rose up against me, and compassed the house by night against me; they wished to kill me, and they have humbled my concubine, and she is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 19:29; Joshua 7:15; Judges 19:23"> And I laid hold of my concubine, and divided her in pieces, and sent the parts into every coast of the inheritance of the children of Israel; for they have wrought lewdness and abomination in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 19:30; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 1 Corinthians 5:10–12"> Behold, all you⌃ are children of Israel; and consider and take counsel here among yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Judges 20:1; Proverbs 21:3"> And all the people rose up as one man, saying, No one of us shall return to his tent, and no one of us shall return to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jonas 1:7; Nehemiah 11:1; Joshua 14:2"> And now this is the thing which shall be done in Gabaa; we will go up against it by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Moreover we will take ten men for one hundred for all the tribes of Israel, and one hundred for a thousand, and a thousand for ten thousand, to take provision, to cause them to come to Gabaa of Benjamin, to do to it according to all the abomination, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. sons of Belial.">a</a></sup>they wrought in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And all the men of Israel were gathered to the city as one man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Deuteronomy 20:10; Romans 12:18"> And the tribes of Israel sent men through the whole tribe of Benjamin, saying, What is this wickedness that has been wrought among you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:12; Deuteronomy 13:13; Judges 19:22"> Now then give up the men the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. it, sc. Gabas.">b</a></sup>sons of transgressors that are in Gabaa, and we will put them to death, and purge out wickedness from Israel: but the children of Benjamin consented not to listen to the voice of their brethren the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 20:20; Job 15:25–26; Numbers 21:23"> And the children of Benjamin were gathered from their cities to Gabaa, to go forth to fight with the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:41; Judges 20:25; Numbers 1:37"> And the children of Benjamin from their cities were numbered in that day, twenty-three thousand, every man drawing a sword, besides the inhabitants of Gabaa, who were numbered seven hundred chosen men of all the people,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See chap 3:15.">c</a></sup>able to use both hands alike;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 3:15; Chronicles I 12:2; Kings I 17:49–50"> All these could sling with stones at a hair, and not miss.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Chronicles II 17:14–18; Numbers 26:51"> And the men of Israel, exclusive of Benjamin, were numbered four hundred thousand men that drew sword; all these were men of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 20:23; Judges 20:26–27; Numbers 27:21"> And they arose and went up to Baethel, and enquired of God: and the children of Israel said, Who shall go up for us first to fight with the children of Benjamin? And the Lord said, Juda shall go up first as leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 7:16; Joshua 3:1; Joshua 6:12"> And the children of Israel rose up in the morning, and encamped against Gabaa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And they went out, all the men of Israel, to fight with Benjamin, and engaged with them at Gabaa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 20:25; Psalms 73:18–19; Chronicles II 28:10"> And the sons of Benjamin went forth from Gabaa, and they destroyed in Israel on that day two and twenty thousand men down to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 64:5; Judges 20:17; Kings I 30:6"> And the men of Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Or, grew strong.">d</a></sup>strengthened themselves, and again engaged in battle in the place where they had engaged on the first day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 20:26–27; Psalms 78:34–36; Joshua 7:6"> And the children of Israel went up, and wept before the Lord till evening, and enquired of the Lord, saying, Shall we again draw near to battle with our brethren the children of Benjamin? and the Lord said, Go up against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the children of Israel advanced against the children of Benjamin on the second day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Judges 20:21; Genesis 18:25; Romans 2:5"> And the children of Benjamin went forth to meet them from Gabaa on the second day, and destroyed of the children of Israel yet further eighteen thousand men down to the ground: all these drew sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 20:23; Joel 2:12–18; Judges 20:18"> And the children of Israel and all the people went up, and came to Baethel; and they wept, and sat there before the Lord; and they fasted on that day until evening, and offered whole burnt offerings and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Or, unblemished, according to the Heb. peace offering.">e</a></sup>perfect sacrifices, before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Judges 20:18; Kings I 4:3–4"> for the ark of the Lord God was there in those days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 7:9; Joshua 24:33; Deuteronomy 18:5"> and Phinees the son of Eleazar the son of Aaron stood before it in those days; and the children of Israel enquired of the Lord, saying, Shall we yet again go forth to fight with our brethren the sons of Benjamin? and the Lord said, Go up, to-morrow I will give them into your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 8:4; Kings II 5:23; Judges 20:34"> And the children of Israel set an ambush against Gabaa round about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the children of Israel went up against the children of Benjamin on the third day, and arrayed themselves against Gabaa as before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 19:13–14; Joshua 7:5; Esias 10:29"> And the children of Benjamin went out to meet the people, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. were emptied out of the city.">f</a></sup>were all drawn out of the city, and began to strike and kill the people as before in the roads,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. which is one going up.">g</a></sup>whereof one goes up to Baethel, and one to Gabaa in the field, about thirty men of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 8:15–16"> And the children of Benjamin said, They fall before us as at the first: but the children of Israel said, Let us flee, and draw them out from the city into the roads; and they did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 8:18–22"> And all the men rose up out of their places, and engaged in Baal Thamar; and the liers in wait of Israel advanced from their place from<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Heb. plain of the south.">h</a></sup>Maraagabe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 8:14; Esias 47:11; Job 21:13"> And there came over against Gabaa ten thousand chosen men out of all Israel; and the fight was severe; and they knew not that evil<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. is coming upon them.">i</a></sup>was coming upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 20:5; Judges 20:15; Judges 20:44–46"> And the Lord struck Benjamin before the children of Israel; and the children of Israel destroyed of Benjamin in that day one hundred and twenty-five thousand men: all these drew sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Joshua 8:15–29"> And the children of Benjamin saw that they were struck; and the men of Israel gave place to Benjamin, because they trusted in the ambuscade which they had prepared against Gabaa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 8:19; Joshua 6:5; Exodus 19:13"> And when they retreated, then the liers in wait rose up, and they<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. extended themselves.">j</a></sup>moved toward Gabaa, and the whole ambush came forth, and they struck the city with the edge of the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 8:20; Genesis 17:21; Kings IV 4:16"> And the children of Israel had a signal of battle with the liers in wait, that they should send up a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or, a concerted signal.">k</a></sup>signal of smoke from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Judges 20:32"> And the children of Israel saw that the liers in wait had seized Gabaa, and they stood in line of battle; and Benjamin began to strike down<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="Or, slain ones, i.e. to strike and cause to fall.">l</a></sup>wounded ones among the men of Israel about thirty men; for they said, Surely they fall again before us,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Gr. as the first battle was.">m</a></sup>as in the first battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 8:20; Revelation 19:3; Genesis 19:28"> And the signal went up increasingly over the city as a pillar of smoke; and Benjamin looked behind him, and behold the destruction of the city went up to heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 Peter 2:12; Luke 21:26; Revelation 6:15–17"> And the men of Israel turned back, and the men of Benjamin hasted, because they saw that evil had come upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Joshua 8:15; Osee 9:9; Lamentations 1:3"> And they turned to the way of the wilderness from before the children of Israel, and fled: but the battle overtook them, and they from the cities destroyed them in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 8:20–22"> And they cut down Benjamin, and pursued him from Nua closely till they came opposite Gabaa on the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And there fell of Benjamin eighteen thousand men: all these were men of might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Joshua 15:32; Judges 21:13; Zacharias 14:10"> And the rest turned, and fled to the wilderness to the rock of Remmon; and the children of Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or, cut off as stragglers. Gr. gleaned, or picked straws.">n</a></sup>picked off of them five thousand men; and the children of Israel went down after them as far as Gedan, and they struck of them two thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Judges 20:35; Judges 20:15"> And all that fell of Benjamin were twenty-five thousand men that drew sword in that day: all these were men of might.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Esias 1:9; Psalms 103:9–10; Ambacum 3:2"> And the rest turned, and fled to the wilderness to the rock of Remmon, even six hundred men; and they sojourned four months in the rock of Remmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:15–17; Chronicles II 25:13; Chronicles II 28:6–9"> And the children of Israel returned to the children of Benjamin, and struck them with the edge of the sword from the city of Methla, even to the cattle, and every thing that was found in all the cities: and they burnt with fire the cities they found.</span>
