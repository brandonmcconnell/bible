<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Judges 17:6; Judges 19:1; Judges 1:34"> In those days there was no king in Israel; and in those days the tribe of Dan sought for itself an inheritance to inhabit, because no inheritance had fallen to it until that day in the midst of the tribes of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 2:1; Judges 18:11"> And the sons of Dan sent from their families five men of valour, from Saraa and from Esthaol, to spy out the land and to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, survey or examine it.">a</a></sup>search it; and they said to them, Go and search out the land. And they came as far as the mount of Ephraim to the house of Michaias and they lodged there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 12:6; Genesis 27:22; Matthew 26:73"> in the house of Michaias, and they recognized the voice of the young man the Levite, and turned in there; and said to him, Who brought you in hither? and what do you in this place? and what have you here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 17:10; Jezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 28:21"> And he said to them, Thus and thus did Michaias to me, and he hired me, and I became his priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:10; Esias 30:1; Kings III 22:5"> And they said to him, Enquire now of God, and we shall know whether our way will prosper, on which we are going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings III 22:6; Kings III 22:15; Psalms 33:18"> And the priest said to them, Go in peace; your way in which you⌃ go, is before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:27–28; Kings I 3:13"> And the five men went on, and came to Laisa; and they saw the people in the midst of it dwelling securely, at ease as is the manner of the Sidonians, and there is no one<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Here probably διατρέπων and ἐκπιέζων both come under the Heb. מבליס and ἐκπιέζων and θησανροὺς both under עצר.">b</a></sup>perverting or shaming a matter in the land, no heir extorting treasures; and they are far from the Sidonians, and they have no intercourse with any one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 18:11; Judges 18:2; Judges 13:2"> And the five men came to their brethren to Saraa and Esthaol, and said to their brethren, Why sit you⌃ here idle?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 2:23–24; Numbers 13:30; Joshua 18:3"> And they said, Arise, and let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and, behold, it is very good, yet you⌃ are still: delay not to go, and to enter in to possess the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 18:27; Judges 18:7; Deuteronomy 2:29"> And whenever you⌃ shall go, you⌃ shall come in upon a people secure, and the land is extensive, for God has given it into your hand; a place where there is no lack of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. of the things in the land or earth.">c</a></sup>anything that the earth affords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 18:16"> And there departed thence of the families of Dan, from Saraa and from Esthaol, six hundred men, girded with weapons of war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 13:25; Joshua 15:60; Chronicles II 1:4"> And they went up, and encamped in Cariathiarim in Juda; therefore it was called in that place the camp of Dan, until this day: behold, it is behind Cariathiarim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 24:30; Judges 19:1; Judges 18:2–3"> And they went on thence to the mount of Ephraim, and came to the house of Michaias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 17:5; Judges 18:3–4; Kings I 14:28"> And the five men who went to spy out the land of Laisa answered, and said to their brethren, You⌃ know that there is in this place an ephod, and theraphin, and a graven and a molten image; and now consider what you⌃ shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 43:27; Kings I 17:22; John 14:27"> And they turned aside there, and went into the house of the young man, the Levite, even into the house of Michaias, and asked him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. as to or concerning peace.">d</a></sup>how he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 18:11"> And the six hundred men of the sons of Dan who were girded with their weapons of war<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Lit. standing.">e</a></sup>stood by the door of the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 18:14; Judges 17:4–5; Judges 18:2"> And the five men who went to spy out the land went up, and entered into the house of Michaias, and the priest stood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And they took the graven image, and the ephod, and the theraphin, and the molten image; and the priest said to them, What are you⌃ doing?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 29:9; Job 21:5; Judges 17:10"> And they said to him, Be silent, lay your hand upon your mouth, and come with us, and be to us a father and a priest: is it better for you to be the priest of the house of one man, or to be the priest of a tribe and house for a family of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esias 56:11; Jezekiel 13:19; Proverbs 30:15"> And the heart of the priest was glad, and he took the ephod, and the theraphin, and the graven image, and the molten image, and went in the midst of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So they turned and departed, and put their children and their property and their baggage before them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> They went some distance from the house of Michaias, and, behold, Michaias and the men in the houses near Michaias' house, cried out, and overtook the children of Dan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 114:5; Kings II 14:5; Kings I 11:5"> And the children of Dan turned their face, and said to Michaias, What is the matter with you that you have cried out?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ambacum 2:18–19; Esias 44:18–20; Psalms 115:8"> And Michaias said, Because you⌃ have taken my graven image which I made, and my priest, and are gone; and what have I remaining? and what is this that you⌃ say to me, Why cry you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 27:2; Job 3:5; Kings I 30:6"> And the children of Dan said to him, Let not your voice be heard with us, lest angry men run upon you, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Lit. add. q.d. to the deeds already done.">f</a></sup>take away your life, and the lives of your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the children of Dan went their way; and Michaias saw that they were stronger than himself, and he returned to his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Judges 18:7; Judges 18:10"> And the children of Dan took what Michaias had made, and the priest that he had, and they came to Laisa, to a people quiet and secure; and they struck them with the edge of the sword, and burnt the city with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Kings II 10:6; Judges 18:7; Numbers 13:21"> And there was no deliverer, because the city is far from the Sidonians, and they have no intercourse with men, and it is in the valley of the house of Raab; and they built the city, and lived in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:47; Genesis 14:14; Kings III 15:20"> And they called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel; and the name of the city was<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Heb. Ulamaish, or Laish of old.">g</a></sup>Ulamais before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings I 4:10–11; Judges 13:1; Judges 17:5"> And the children of Dan set up the graven image for themselves; and Jonathan son of Gerson son of Manasse, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of Dan till the time of the carrying away of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. land.">h</a></sup>nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 18:1; Kings I 1:3; Judges 19:18"> And they set up for themselves the graven image which Michaias made, all the days that the house of God was in Selom; and it was so in those days that there was no king in Israel.</span>
