<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 25:10–20; Exodus 26:33; Numbers 10:33–36"> And they made ten curtains for the tabernacle;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:3"> of eight and twenty cubits the length of one curtain: the same measure was to all, and the breadth of one curtain was of four cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And they made the veil of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and fine linen twined, the woven work with cherubs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Peter 1:7; Numbers 4:14–15"> And they put it on four posts of incorruptible wood overlaid with gold; and their capitals were gold, and their four sockets were silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Kings II 6:3–7; Numbers 1:50"> And they made the veil of the door of the tabernacle of witness of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and fine linen twined, woven work with cherubs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Titus 2:14; Romans 3:25"> and their posts five, and the rings; and they gilded their capitals and their clasps with gold, and they had five sockets of brass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 10:2; Psalms 104:4; Psalms 80:1"> And they made the court toward the south; the curtains of the court of fine linen twined, one hundred cubits<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, on each side.">a</a></sup>every way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and their posts twenty, and their sockets twenty;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 1:51; Hebrews 1:14; Genesis 28:12"> and on the north side one hundred every way, and on the south side one hundred every way, and their posts twenty and their sockets twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:14; Jezekiel 40:39–42; Malachias 1:12"> And on the west side curtains of fifty cubits, their posts ten and their sockets ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And on the east side curtains of fifty cubits of fifteen cubits behind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and their pillars three, and their sockets three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And at the second back on this side and on that by the gate of the court, curtains of fifteen cubits, their pillars three and their sockets three;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> all the curtains of the tabernacle of fine linen twined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the sockets of their pillars of brass, and their hooks of silver, and their capitals overlaid with silver, and all the posts of the court overlaid with silver:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:29; Kings III 7:50; Kings IV 12:13"> and the veil of the gate of the court, the work of an embroiderer of blue, and purple, and spun scarlet, and fine linen twined; the length of twenty cubits, and the height and the breadth of five cubits, made equal to the curtains of the court;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 25:31–39; Hebrews 9:2; Chronicles II 13:11"> and their pillars four, and their sockets four of brass, and their hooks of silver, and their capitals overlaid with silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And all the pins of the court round about of brass, and they were overlaid with silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And this was the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, appointment.">b</a></sup>construction of the tabernacle of witness, accordingly as it was appointed to Moses; so that the public service should belong to the Levites, through Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:5; Jeremias 1:11; Exodus 25:33"> And Beseleel the son of Urias of the tribe of Juda, did as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 25:35"> And Eliab the son of Achisamach of the tribe of Dan was there, who was chief artificer in the woven works and needle-works and embroideries,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. to weave.">c</a></sup>in weaving with the scarlet and fine linen.</span>
