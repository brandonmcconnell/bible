<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 2:14; Jeremias 1:6"> And Moses answered and said, If they believe me not, and do not listen to my voice (for they will say, God has not appeared to you), what shall I say to them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Exodus 4:20; Esias 11:4"> And the Lord said to him, What is this thing that is in your hand? and he said, A rod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 7:10–15; Amos 5:19; Exodus 4:17"> And he said, Cast it on the ground: and he cast it on the ground, and it became a serpent, and Moses fled from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 28:3–6; Genesis 22:1–2; Psalms 91:13"> And the Lord said to Moses, Stretch forth your hand, and take hold of its tail: so he stretched forth his hand and took hold of the tail,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Genesis 48:3; Exodus 3:15"> and it became a rod in his hand, —that they may believe you, that the God of your fathers has appeared to you, the God of Abraam, and God of Isaac, and God of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Kings IV 5:27; Numbers 12:10"> And the Lord said again to him, Put your hand into your bosom; and he put his hand into his bosom, and brought his hand out of his bosom, and his hand became as snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings IV 5:14; Deuteronomy 32:39; Matthew 8:3"> And he said again, Put your hand into your bosom; and he put his hand into his bosom, and brought his hand out of his bosom, and it was again restored to the complexion of his other flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 28:10; Deuteronomy 32:39; Exodus 4:30–31"> And if they will not believe you, nor listen to the voice of the first sign, they will believe you because of the voice of the second sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 7:19–25; Exodus 1:22; Matthew 7:2"> And it shall come to pass if they will not believe you for these two signs, and will not listen to your voice, that you shall take of the water of the river and pour it upon the dry land, and the water which you shall take from the river shall be blood upon the dry land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremias 1:6; Exodus 6:12; Acts 7:22"> And Moses said to the Lord, I pray, Lord, I have not been sufficient in<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. before yesterday, neither before the third day.">a</a></sup>former times, neither from the time that you have begun to speak to your servant: I am weak in speech, and slow-tongued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 3:6; Psalms 146:8; Jezekiel 3:26–27"> And the Lord said to Moses, Who has given a mouth to man, and who has made the very hard of hearing, and the deaf, the seeing and the blind? have not I, God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 1:9; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20"> And now go and I will open your mouth, and will<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="See 1 Cor 2:16. Gr.">b</a></sup>instruct you in what you shall say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonas 1:3; Exodus 4:1; Matthew 13:41"> And Moses said, I pray you, Lord, appoint another able person whom you shall send.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 14:13–15; Exodus 4:27; Chronicles I 21:7"> And the Lord was greatly angered against Moses, and said, Behold! is not Aaron the Levite your brother? I know that he will surely speak to you; and, behold, he will come forth to meet you, and beholding you he will rejoice within himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 51:16; Deuteronomy 18:18; Luke 21:15"> And you shall speak to him; and you shall put my words into his mouth, and I will open your mouth and his mouth, and I will instruct you in what you⌃ shall do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 7:1–2; Psalms 82:6; John 10:34–35"> And he shall speak for you to the people, and he shall be your mouth, and you shall be for him in things pertaining to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 4:2; 1 Corinthians 1:27; Exodus 7:9–20"> And this rod that was turned into a serpent you shall take in your hand, wherewith you shall work miracles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Kings I 1:17; 1 Timothy 6:1"> And Moses went and returned to Jothor his father-in-law, and says, I will go and return to my brethren in Egypt, and will see if they are yet living. And Jothor said to Moses, Go in health. And in those days after some time, the king of Egypt died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 2:20; Exodus 2:23; Exodus 2:15"> And the Lord said to Moses in Madiam, Go, depart into Egypt, for all that sought your life are dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 17:9; Numbers 20:8–9; Exodus 18:3–4"> And Moses took his wife and his children, and mounted them on the beasts, and returned to Egypt; and Moses took the rod which he had from God in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 9:18; Esias 63:17; Exodus 14:8"> And the Lord said to Moses, When you go and return to Egypt, see—all the miracles I have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lit. put into your hands.">c</a></sup>charged you with, you shall work before Pharao: and I will harden his heart, and he shall certainly not send away the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Osee 11:1; Romans 9:4; Jeremias 31:9"> And you shall say to Pharao, These things says the Lord, Israel is my firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 11:5; Exodus 12:29; Psalms 135:8"> And I said to you, Send away my people, that they may serve me: now if you will not send them away, see, I will kill your firstborn son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Numbers 22:22–23; Osee 13:8"> And it came to pass that the angel of the Lord met him by the way in the inn, and sought to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 5:2–3; Genesis 17:14; Kings II 16:7"> and Sepphora having taken a stone cut off the foreskin of her son, and fell at his feet and said, The blood of the circumcision of my son is staunched:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and he departed from him, because she said, The blood of the circumcision of my son is staunched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Exodus 4:14–16; Kings III 19:8"> And the Lord said to Aaron, Go into the wilderness to meet Moses; and he went and met him in the mount of God, and they kissed each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 4:8–9; Exodus 4:15–16; Matthew 21:29"> And Moses reported to Aaron all the words of the Lord, which he sent, and all the things which he charged him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 3:16; Exodus 24:11; Exodus 24:1"> And Moses and Aaron went and gathered the elders of the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 4:16"> And Aaron spoke all these words, which God spoke to Moses, and wrought the miracles before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 3:18; Exodus 2:25; Psalms 106:12–13"> and the people believed and rejoiced, because God visited the children of Israel, and because he saw their affliction: and the people bowed and worshipped.</span>
