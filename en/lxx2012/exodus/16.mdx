<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 33:10–12; Exodus 15:27; Exodus 17:1"> And they departed from Aelim, and all the congregation of the children of Israel came to the wilderness of Sin, which is between Aelim and Sina; and on the fifteenth day, in the second month after their departure from the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 15:24; Psalms 106:25"> all the congregation of the children of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 11:4–5; Numbers 20:3–5; Exodus 17:3"> And the children of Israel said to them, Would we had died struck by the Lord in the land of Egypt, when we sat by the flesh-pots, and ate bread to satiety! for you⌃ have brought us out into this wilderness, to kill all this congregation with hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3; Psalms 78:24–25; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And the Lord said to Moses, Behold, I will rain bread upon you out of heaven: and the people shall go forth, and they shall gather their daily portion for the day, that I may try them whether they will walk in my law or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:22–23; Exodus 35:2–3; Leviticus 25:21–22"> And it shall come to pass on the sixth day that they shall prepare whatever they have brought in, and it shall be double of what they shall have gathered for the day, daily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Numbers 16:28; Exodus 16:12–13"> And Moses and Aaron said to all the congregation of the children of Israel, At even you⌃ shall know that the Lord has brought you out of the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 40:5; Numbers 16:11; John 11:40"> and in the morning you⌃ shall see the glory of the Lord, inasmuch as he hears your murmuring against God; and who are we, that you⌃ continue to murmur against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:2; Kings I 8:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> And Moses said, This shall be when the Lord gives you in the evening flesh to eat, and bread in the morning to satiety, because the Lord has heard your murmuring, which you⌃ murmur against us: and what are we? for your murmuring is not against us, but against God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:16; Exodus 16:2; Exodus 16:8"> And Moses said to Aaron, Say to all the congregation of the children of Israel, Come near before God; for he has heard your murmuring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 16:7; Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42"> And when Aaron spoke to all the congregation of the children of Israel, and they turned toward the wilderness, then the glory of the Lord appeared in a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Jezekiel 39:22; Exodus 6:7"> I have heard the murmuring of the children of Israel: speak to them, saying, Towards evening you⌃ shall eat flesh, and in the morning you⌃ shall be satisfied with bread; and you⌃ shall know that I am the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 11:9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:27–28"> And it was evening, and quails came up and covered the camp:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 11:7–9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24"> in the morning it came to pass as the dew ceased round about the camp, that, behold, on the face of the wilderness was a small thing like white coriander seed, as frost upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; John 6:58; Exodus 16:31"> And when the children of Israel saw it, they said one to another, What is this? for they knew not what it was; and Moses said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:36; Exodus 16:18; Exodus 16:32–33"> This is the bread which the Lord has given you to eat. This is that which the Lord has appointed: gather of it each man for his family, a homer for each person,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. by the head.">a</a></sup>according to the number of your souls, gather each of you with his fellow-lodgers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the children of Israel did so, and gathered some much and some less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> And having measured the homer full, he that gathered much had nothing over, and he that had gathered less had no lack; each gathered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="i.e. just sufficient for.">b</a></sup>according to the need of those who belonged to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Exodus 23:18; Matthew 6:34"> And Moses said to them, Let no man leave of it till the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:14; Numbers 16:15; Ephesians 4:26"> But they did not listen to Moses, but some left of it till the morning; and it bred worms and stank: and Moses was irritated with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–11; Ecclesiastes 12:1; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> And they gathered it every morning, each man what he needed, and when the sun waxed hot it melted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 16:5; Leviticus 25:22; Exodus 16:16"> And it came to pass on the sixth day, they gathered double what was needed, two homers for one man; and all the chiefs of the synagogue went in and reported it to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 31:15; Exodus 20:8–11"> And Moses said to them, Is not this the word which the Lord spoke? To-morrow is the sabbath, a holy rest to the Lord: bake that you⌃ will bake, and seethe that you⌃ will seethe, and all that is over leave to be laid by for the morrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:20; Exodus 16:33"> And they left of it till the morning, as Moses commanded them; and it stank not, neither was there a worm in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Exodus 16:29; Nehemiah 9:14"> And Moses said, Eat that today, for today is a sabbath to the Lord: it shall not be found in the plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:13; Exodus 20:9–11; Jezekiel 46:1"> Six days you⌃ shall gather it, and on the seventh day is a sabbath, for there shall be none on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4"> And it came to pass on the seventh day that some of the people went forth to gather, and found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:10; Psalms 106:13; Numbers 14:11"> And the Lord said to Moses, How long are you⌃ unwilling to listen to my commands and my law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; Esias 58:13–14; Jezekiel 20:12"> See, for the Lord has given you this day as the sabbath, therefore he has given you on the sixth day the bread of two days: you⌃ shall sit each of you in your houses; let no one go forth from his place on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:12–14; Leviticus 23:3; Hebrews 4:9"> And the people kept sabbath on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Song of Songs 2:3; Numbers 11:6–9; Exodus 16:15"> And the children of Israel called the name of it Man; and it was as white coriander seed, and the taste of it as a wafer with honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 105:5; Luke 22:19; Psalms 111:4–5"> And Moses said, This is the thing which the Lord has commanded, Fill an homer with manna, to be laid up for your generations; that they may see the bread which you⌃ ate in the wilderness, when the Lord led you forth out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4"> And Moses said to Aaron, Take a golden pot, and cast into it one full homer of manna; and you shall lay it up before God, to be kept for your generations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 25:21; Exodus 25:16"> as the Lord commanded Moses: and Aaron laid it up before the testimony to be kept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:20–21; Joshua 5:12; Deuteronomy 8:2–3"> And the children of Israel ate manna forty years, until they came to the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. οἰκοuμένη.">c</a></sup>land they ate the manna, until they came to the region of Phoenicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 16:16; Exodus 16:32–33"> Now the homer was the tenth part of three measures.</span>
