<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 13:4; Deuteronomy 16:1; Exodus 34:18"> This month shall be to you the beginning of months: it is the first to you among the months of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 1:2; 1 Corinthians 5:7; John 1:29"> Speak to all the congregation of the children of Israel, saying, On the tenth of this month let them take each man a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="πρόβατον, sheep generally but ver. 5 seems to show that the word lamb. is admissable in this passage.">a</a></sup>lamb according to the houses of their families, every man a lamb for his household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And if they be few in a household, so that there are not enough for the lamb, he shall take with himself his neighbor that lives near to him, —as to the number of souls, every one according to that which suffices him shall make a reckoning for the lamb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Peter 1:18–19; Hebrews 9:13–14; Malachias 1:14"> It shall be to you a lamb unblemished, a male of a year old: you⌃ shall take it of the lambs and the kids.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5; Chronicles II 30:15–18; Numbers 28:16"> And it shall be kept by you till the fourteenth of this month, and all the multitude of the congregation of the children of Israel shall kill it toward evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Exodus 12:22–23; Hebrews 9:22"> And they shall take of the blood, and shall put it on the two door-posts, and on the lintel, in the houses in which soever they shall eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 34:25; Deuteronomy 16:7; Exodus 13:3"> And they shall eat the flesh in this night roast with fire, and they shall eat unleavened bread with bitter herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 12:8; Deuteronomy 16:7; Lamentations 1:13"> You⌃ shall not eat of it raw nor sodden in water, but only roast with fire, the head with the feet and the appurtenances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 23:18; Exodus 34:25; Exodus 29:34"> Nothing shall be left of it till the morning, and a bone of it you⌃ shall not break; but that which is left of it till the morning you⌃ shall burn with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 12:27; Ephesians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> And thus shall you⌃ eat it: your loins girded, and your sandals on your feet, and your staves in your hands, and you⌃ shall eat it in haste. It is a passover to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 33:4; Exodus 12:23; Psalms 82:6"> and I will go throughout the land of Egypt in that night, and will strike every firstborn in the land of Egypt both man and beast, and on all the gods of Egypt will I execute vengeance: I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Exodus 12:23; 1 John 1:7"> And the blood shall be for a sign to you on the houses in which you⌃ are, and I will see the blood, and will protect you, and there shall not be on you the plague of destruction, when I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, strike the land Hebraism.">b</a></sup>strike in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 23:21; Exodus 12:24; Exodus 13:9–10"> And this day shall be to you a memorial, and you⌃ shall keep it a feast to the Lord through all your generations; you⌃ shall keep it a feast for a perpetual ordinance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 17:14; Deuteronomy 16:3; Exodus 23:15"> Seven days you⌃ shall eat unleavened bread, and from the first day you⌃ shall utterly remove leaven from your houses: whoever shall eat leaven, that soul shall be utterly destroyed from Israel, from the first day until the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 23:7–8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 16:23"> And the first day shall be called holy, and the seventh day shall be a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. called holy.">c</a></sup>holy convocation to you: you⌃ shall do no servile work on them, only as many things as will necessarily be done by every soul, this only shall be done by you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 7:5; Exodus 13:3; Exodus 12:41"> And you⌃ shall keep this commandment, for on this day will I bring out your force out of the land of Egypt; and you⌃ shall make this day a perpetual ordinance for you throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5–8; Exodus 12:15; Exodus 12:1–2"> Beginning the fourteenth day of the first month, you⌃ shall eat unleavened bread from evening, till the twenty-first day of the month, till evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:15; Exodus 23:15; Deuteronomy 16:3"> Seven days leaven shall not be found in your houses; whoever shall eat anything leavened, that soul shall be cut off from the congregation of Israel, both among the occupiers of the land and the original inhabitants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> You⌃ shall eat nothing leavened, but in every habitation of yours you⌃ shall eat unleavened bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 12:3; Luke 22:7–13; Chronicles II 35:5–6"> And Moses called all the elders of the children of Israel, and said to them, Go away and take to yourselves a lamb according to your kindred, and kill the passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28; Numbers 19:18; Psalms 51:7"> And you⌃ shall take a bunch of hyssop, and having dipped it into some of the blood that is by the door, you⌃ shall touch the lintel, and shall put it upon both door-posts, even of the blood which is by the door; but you⌃ shall not go out every one from the door of his house till the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Revelation 7:3; Revelation 9:4; Hebrews 11:28"> And the Lord shall pass by to strike the Egyptians, and shall see the blood upon the lintel, and upon both the door-posts; and the Lord shall pass by the door, and shall not suffer the destroyer to enter into your houses to strike you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Genesis 17:8–10"> And keep you⌃ this thing as an ordinance for yourself and for your children for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 3:17; Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 12:8–9"> And if you⌃ should enter into the land, which the Lord shall give you, as he has spoken, keep this service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 13:14–15; Deuteronomy 32:7; Psalms 145:4"> And it shall come to pass, if your sons say to you, What is this service?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 4:31; Exodus 12:11; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> that you⌃ shall say to them, This passover is a sacrifice to the Lord, as he defended the houses of the children of Israel in Egypt, when he struck the Egyptians, but delivered our houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 11:28"> And the people bowed and worshipped. And the children of Israel departed and did as the Lord commanded Moses and Aaron, so did they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 78:51; Exodus 4:23; Psalms 105:36"> And it came to pass at midnight that the Lord struck all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharao that sat on the throne, to the firstborn of the captive-maid in the dungeon, and the firstborn of all cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 11:6; Amos 5:17; Proverbs 21:13"> And Pharao rose up by night, and his servants, and all the Egyptians; and there was a great cry in all the land of Egypt, for there was not a house in which there was not one dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 6:1; Exodus 10:9; Exodus 3:19–20"> And Pharao called Moses and Aaron by night, and said to them, Rise and depart from my people, both you⌃ and the children of Israel. Go and serve the Lord your God, even as you⌃ say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 10:26; Genesis 27:34; Genesis 27:38"> And take with you your sheep, and your oxen: bless me also, I pray you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 105:38; Exodus 11:1; Genesis 20:3"> And the Egyptians constrained the people, so that they cast them out of the land with haste, for they said, We all shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 8:3"> And the people took their dough before their<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. lumps of meal.">d</a></sup>meal was leavened, bound up as it was in their garments, on their shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 3:21–22; Exodus 11:2–3; Genesis 15:14"> And the children of Israel did as Moses commanded them, and they asked of the Egyptians articles of silver and gold and apparel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 15:14; Acts 7:10; Exodus 3:21–22"> And the Lord gave his people favor in the sight of the Egyptians, and they lent to them; and they spoiled the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 1:46; Exodus 38:26; Numbers 33:3"> And the children Israel<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. having departed.">e</a></sup>departed from Ramesses to Socchoth, to the full number of six hundred thousand footmen, even men, besides the baggage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 11:4; Zacharias 8:23"> And a great mixed company went up with them, and sheep and oxen and very much cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 11:1; Exodus 6:1; Exodus 12:31–33"> And they baked the dough which they brought out of Egypt, unleavened cakes, for it had not been leavened; for the Egyptians cast them out, and they could not remain, neither did they prepare provision for themselves for the journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Acts 7:6; Acts 13:17"> And the sojourning of the children of Israel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. which.">f</a></sup>while they sojourned in the land of Egypt and the land of Chanaan, was four hundred and thirty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ambacum 2:3; John 7:8; Acts 1:7"> And it came to pass after the four hundred and thirty years, all the forces of the Lord came forth out of the land of Egypt by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–6; Exodus 12:14; Exodus 13:10"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="ὥστε seems to be given for ל, instead of 'when he brought,' etc.">g</a></sup>It is a watch kept to the Lord, so that he should bring them out of the land of Egypt; that very night is a watch kept to the Lord, so that it should be to all the children of Israel to their generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Exodus 12:48; Numbers 9:14; Exodus 12:11"> And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron, This is the law of the passover: no stranger shall eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 17:12–13; Genesis 17:23"> And every slave or servant bought with money—him you shall circumcise, and then shall he eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 22:10; Ephesians 2:12"> A sojourner or hireling shall not eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 9:12; John 19:36; John 19:33"> In one house shall it be eaten, and you⌃ shall not carry of the flesh out from the house; and a bone of it you⌃ shall not break.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:13; Exodus 12:3"> All the congregation of the children of Israel shall keep it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Numbers 9:14; Colossians 3:11; Galatians 3:28"> And if any proselyte shall come to you to keep the passover to the Lord, you shall circumcise every male of him, and then shall he approach to sacrifice it, and he shall be even as the original inhabitant of the land; no uncircumcised person shall eat of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 15:15–16; Numbers 15:29; Leviticus 24:22"> There shall be one law to the native, and to the proselyte coming among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 7:24–25; Exodus 6:26; Exodus 7:4"> And the children of Israel did as the Lord commanded Moses and Aaron for them, so they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 6:26"> And it came to pass in that day that the Lord brought out the children of Israel from the land of Egypt with their forces.</span>
