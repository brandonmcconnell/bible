<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; Nehemiah 11:2; Ezra 2:68"> Speak to the children of Israel, and take first fruits of all, who may be disposed in their heart to give; and you⌃ shall take my first fruits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:9; Job 28:2"> And this is the offering which you⌃ shall take of them; gold and silver and brass,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 19:8; Genesis 41:42; Jezekiel 16:10"> and blue, and purple, and double scarlet, and fine spun linen, and goats' hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 26:37–1; Exodus 26:14–15; Exodus 36:20"> and rams' skins dyed red, and blue skins, and incorruptible wood,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 27:20; Exodus 25:37; Exodus 40:24–25"> and oil for the light, incense for anointing oil, and for the composition of incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:6–30"> and sardius stones, and stones for the carved work of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. shoulder-piece.">a</a></sup>breast-plate, and the full-length robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; Kings III 6:13; Revelation 21:3"> And you shall make me a sanctuary, and I will appear among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Acts 7:44"> And you shall make for me according to all things which I show you in the mountain; even the pattern of the tabernacle, and the pattern of all its furniture: so shall you make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4; Exodus 37:1–3; Deuteronomy 10:1–3"> And you shall make the ark of testimony of incorruptible wood; the length of two cubits and a half, and the breadth of a cubit and a half, and the height of a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:24; Exodus 30:3; Chronicles II 3:4"> And you shall gild it with pure gold, you shall gild it within and without; and you shall make for it golden wreaths twisted round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 27:7; Exodus 25:26; Exodus 25:15"> And you shall cast for it four golden rings, and shall put them on the four sides; two rings on the one side, and two rings on the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 15:15; Numbers 4:11; Exodus 27:6"> And you shall make staves of incorruptible wood, and shall gild them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And you shall put the staves into the rings on the sides of the ark, to bear the ark with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings III 8:8; Chronicles II 5:9"> The staves shall remain fixed in the rings of the ark.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:26; Kings III 8:9; Hebrews 9:4"> And you shall put into the ark the testimonies which I shall give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 9:5; Exodus 37:6; Hebrews 4:16"> And you shall make a propitiatory, a lid of pure gold; the length of two cubits and a half, and the breadth of a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jezekiel 10:20; Hebrews 9:5; Kings III 8:6–7"> And you shall make two cherubs graven in gold, and you shall put them on both sides of the propitiatory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They shall be made, one cherub on this side, and another cherub on the other side of the propitiatory; and you shall make the two cherubs on the two sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Chronicles I 28:18; Kings III 8:7; Hebrews 9:5"> The cherubs shall stretch forth their wings above, overshadowing the propitiatory with their wings; and their faces shall be toward each other, the faces of the cherubs shall be toward the propitiatory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 26:34; Romans 10:4; Exodus 25:16–17"> And you shall set the propitiatory on the ark above, and you shall put into the ark the testimonies which I shall give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 29:42–43; Numbers 7:89; Esias 37:16"> And I will make myself known to you from thence, and I will speak to you above the propitiatory between the two cherubs, which are upon the ark of testimony, even in all things which I shall charge you concerning the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2; Exodus 37:10–16; Kings III 7:48"> And you shall make a golden table of pure gold, in length two cubits, and in breadth a cubit, and in height a cubit and a half.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 25:11; Kings III 6:20–22"> And you shall make for it golden wreaths twisted round about, and you shall make for it a crown of an hand-breadth round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 37:2; Exodus 30:3"> And you shall make a twisted wreath for the crown round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And you shall make four golden rings; and you shall put the four rings upon the four parts of its feet under the crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Exodus 25:28"> And the rings shall be for bearings for the staves, that they may bear the table with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 25:14; Acts 9:15; Numbers 10:17"> And you shall make the staves of incorruptible wood, and you shall gild them with pure gold; and the table shall be borne with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 4:7; Exodus 37:16; Revelation 3:20"> And you shall make its dishes and its censers, and its bowls, and its cups, with which you shall offer drink-offerings: of pure gold shall you make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Leviticus 24:5–9; Matthew 12:4; Exodus 39:36"> And you shall set upon the table show bread before me continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Kings III 7:49; Zacharias 4:2; Exodus 37:17–24"> And you shall make a candlestick of pure gold; you shall make the candlestick of graven work: its stem and its branches, and its bowls and its knops and its lilies shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. of it.">b</a></sup>of one piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And six branches proceeding sideways, three branches of the candlestick from one side of it, and three branches of the candlestick from the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 17:4–8; Jeremias 1:11–12; Zacharias 4:3"> And three bowls fashioned like almonds, on each branch a knop and a lily; so to the six branches proceeding from the candlestick,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> and in the candlestick four bowls fashioned like almonds, in each branch knops and the flowers<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. of it.">c</a></sup>of the same.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> A knop under two branches out of it, and a knop under four branches out of it; so to the six branches proceeding from the candlestick; and in the candlestick four bowls fashioned like almonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 25:18; Kings III 10:16–17; Chronicles II 9:15"> Let the knops and the branches be of one piece, altogether graven of one piece of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 8:2; Revelation 1:20–1; Leviticus 24:2–4"> And you shall make its seven lamps: and you shall set on it the lamps, and they shall shine from one front.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Kings IV 12:13; Jeremias 52:18; Kings IV 25:14"> And you shall make its funnel and its snuff-dishes of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> All these articles shall be a talent of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 26:30; Acts 7:44"> See, you shall make them according to the pattern showed you in the mount.</span>
