<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Numbers 3:13; Deuteronomy 15:19"> Sanctify to me every firstborn, first produced, opening every womb among the children of Israel both of man and beast: it is mine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:18; Deuteronomy 5:15; Exodus 20:2"> And Moses said to the people, Remember this day, in which you⌃ came forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, for with a strong hand the Lord brought you forth thence; and leaven shall not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 34:18; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 12:2"> For on this day you⌃ go forth in the month of new corn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Joshua 24:11; Exodus 12:25–26"> And it shall come to pass when the Lord your God shall have brought you into the land of the Chananites, and the Chettites, and Amorites, and Evites, and Jebusites, and Gergesites, and Pherezites, which he swore to your fathers to give you, a land flowing with milk and honey, that you shall perform this service in this month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 12:15–20; Exodus 34:18; Leviticus 23:8"> Six days you⌃ shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day is a feast to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:19; Matthew 16:6"> Seven days shall you⌃ eat unleavened bread; nothing leavened shall be seen with you, neither shall you have leaven in all your borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26–27; Psalms 44:1"> And you shall tell your son in that day, saying, Therefore the Lord<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. did thus to me.">a</a></sup>dealt thus with me, as I was going out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:8; Deuteronomy 11:18–19; Numbers 15:39"> And it shall be to you a sign upon your hand and a memorial before your eyes, that the law of the Lord may be in your mouth, for with a strong hand the Lord God brought you out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:14; Exodus 12:24–25; Exodus 23:15"> And preserve you⌃ this law according to the times of the seasons,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. from days to days. Hebraism.">b</a></sup>from year to year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 13:5"> And it shall come to pass when the Lord your God shall bring you into the land of the Chananites, as he swore to your fathers, and shall give it you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 18:15; Leviticus 27:26; Exodus 34:19"> that you shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, separate.">c</a></sup>set apart every offspring opening the womb, the males to the Lord, every one that opens the womb out of the herds or among your cattle, as many as you shall have: you shall sanctify the males to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:20; Numbers 18:15–17; Revelation 14:4"> Every offspring opening the womb of the ass you shall change for a sheep; and if you will not change it, you shall redeem it: every firstborn of man of your sons shall you redeem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 13:3; Joshua 4:6; Psalms 145:4"> And if your son should ask you hereafter, saying, What is this? then you shall say to him, With a strong hand the Lord brought us out of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:29"> And when Pharao hardened his heart so as not to send us away, he killed every firstborn in the land of Egypt, both the firstborn of man and the firstborn of beast; therefore do I sacrifice every offspring that opens the womb, the males to the Lord, and every firstborn of my sons I will redeem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 13:9; Deuteronomy 11:18; Deuteronomy 6:7–9"> And it shall be for a sign upon your hand, and immovable before your eyes, for with a strong hand the Lord brought you out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:11–12; Nehemiah 9:17; Numbers 14:1–4"> And when Pharao sent forth the people, God led them not by the way of the land of the Phylistines, because it was near; for God said, Lest at any time the people repent when they see war, and return to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 14:2; Deuteronomy 32:10; Numbers 33:6–8"> And God led the people round by the way to the wilderness, to the Red Sea: and in the fifth generation the children of Israel went up out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 50:24–25; Joshua 24:32; Acts 7:16"> And Moses took the bones of Joseph with him, for he had solemnly adjured the children of Israel, saying, God will surely visit you, and you⌃ shall carry up my bones hence with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 33:5–6; Exodus 12:37"> And the children of Israel departed from Socchoth, and encamped in Othom by the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:1–2; Nehemiah 9:12; Deuteronomy 1:33"> And God led them, in the day by a pillar of cloud, to show them the way, and in the night by a pillar of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 121:5–8; Revelation 10:1"> And the pillar of cloud failed not by day, nor the pillar of fire by night, before all the people.</span>
