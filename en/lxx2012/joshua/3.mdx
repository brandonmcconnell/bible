<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; Kings II 6:13; Deuteronomy 31:25"> and they charged the people, saying, When you⌃ shall see the ark of the covenant of the Lord our God, and our priests and the Levites bearing it, you⌃ shall depart from your places, and you⌃ shall go after it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 89:7; Hebrews 12:28–29; Exodus 3:5"> But let there be a distance between you and it; you⌃ shall stand as much as two thousand cubits from it. Do not draw near to it, that you⌃ may know the way which you⌃ are to go; for you⌃ have not gone the way<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. from yesterday and the third day. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Leviticus 20:7; Joel 2:16"> And Joshua said to the people, Sanctify yourselves against to-morrow, for to-morrow the Lord will do wonders among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 14:2–3; Numbers 14:15; Michaeas 2:13"> And Joshua said to the priests, Take up the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and go before the people: and the priests took up the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and went before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 4:14; Joshua 1:5; Chronicles I 29:25"> And the Lord said to Joshua, This day do I begin to exalt you before all the children of Israel, that they may know that as I was with Moses, so will I also be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 3:3; Joshua 3:17; Chronicles II 29:15"> And now charge the priests that bear the ark of the covenant, saying, As soon as you⌃ shall enter on a part of the water of Jordan, then you⌃ shall stand in Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:1; Deuteronomy 12:8"> And Joshua said to the children of Israel,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. bring hither. sc. yourselves.">b</a></sup>Come hither, and listen to the word of the Lord our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Exodus 33:2; Psalms 44:2–3"> Hereby you⌃ shall know that the living God is among you, and will utterly destroy from before our face the Chananite, and the Chettite and Pherezite, and the Evite, and the Amorite, and the Gergesite, and the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zacharias 6:5; Zacharias 4:14; Psalms 24:1"> Behold, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth passes over Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 4:2; Joshua 4:9; Joshua 4:4"> Choose for yourselves twelve men of the sons of Israel, one of each tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 15:8; Psalms 78:13; Joshua 3:15–16"> And it shall come to pass, when the feet of the priests that bear the ark of the covenant of the Lord of the whole earth rest in the water of Jordan, the water of Jordan below shall fail, and the water coming down from above shall stop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 7:44–45; Joshua 6:6; 1 Corinthians 1:24–25"> And the people removed from their tents to cross over Jordan, and the priests bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord before the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Chronicles I 12:15; Joshua 4:18; Joshua 3:13"> And when the priests that bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord entered upon Jordan, and the feet of the priests that bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord were dipped in part of the water of Jordan; (now Jordan<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. its whole channel.">c</a></sup>overflowed all its banks<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. as on the days.">d</a></sup>about the time of wheat harvest:)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joshua 3:13; Kings III 4:12; Kings III 7:46"> then the waters that came down from above stopped; there stood one solid heap very far off, as far as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. a part.">e</a></sup>the region of Kariathiarim, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. that which came down.">f</a></sup>the lower part came down to the sea of Araba, the salt sea, till it completely failed; and the people stood opposite Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 14:22; Exodus 14:29; Psalms 66:6"> And the priests that bore the ark of the covenant of the Lord stood on dry land in the midst of Jordan; and all the children of Israel went through on dry land, until all the people had completely gone over Jordan.</span>
