<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 6:21; Hebrews 7:1; Joshua 8:2"> And when Adoni-bezec king of Jerusalem heard that Joshua had taken Gai, and had destroyed it, as he did to Jericho and its king, even so they did to Gai and its king, and that the inhabitants of Gabaon had gone over to Joshua and Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:25; Joshua 2:24; Exodus 15:14–16"> then they were greatly terrified by them, for the king knew that Gabaon was a great city, as one of the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. mother-cities.">a</a></sup>chief cities, and all its men were mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Chronicles II 11:9; Michaeas 1:13; Joshua 10:1"> So Adoni-bezec king of Jerusalem sent to Elam king of Hebron, and to Phidon king of Jerimuth, and to Jephtha king of Lachis, and to Dabin king of Odollam, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 9:15; Joshua 10:1; Esias 8:9–10"> Come up hither to me, and help me, and let us take Gabaon; for the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. they.">b</a></sup>Gabaonites have gone over to Joshua and to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 8:9–10; Genesis 15:16; Joshua 10:6"> And the five kings of the Jebusites went up, the king of Jerusalem, and the king of Chebron, and the king of Jerimuth, and the king of Lachis, and the king of Odollam, they and all their people; and encamped around Gabaon, and besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 9:6; Joshua 5:10; Deuteronomy 1:15"> And the inhabitants of Gabaon sent to Joshua into the camp to Galgala, saying, Slack not your hands from your servants: come up quickly to us, and help us, and rescue us; for all the kings of the Amorites who dwell in the hill country are gathered together against us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 8:1; Esias 8:12; Esias 8:14"> And Joshua went up from Galgala, he and all the people of war with him, every one mighty in strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 8:31; Deuteronomy 3:2; Psalms 27:1–2"> And the Lord said to Joshua, Fear them not, for I have delivered them into your hands; there shall not one of them be left before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; 2 Timothy 2:3; Proverbs 24:11–12"> And when Joshua came suddenly upon them, he had advanced all the night out of Galgala.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Joshua 16:3; Joshua 16:5"> And the Lord struck them with terror before the children of Israel; and the Lord destroyed them with a great slaughter at Gabaon; and they pursued them by the way of the going up of Oronin, and they struck them to Azeca and to Makeda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 28:2; Judges 5:20; Psalms 18:12–14"> And when they fled from the face of the children of Israel at the descent of Oronin, then the Lord cast upon them hailstones from heaven to Azeca; and they were more that died by the hailstones, than those whom the children of Israel killed with the sword in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ambacum 3:11; Esias 38:8; Esias 60:20"> Then Joshua spoke to the Lord, in the day in which the Lord delivered the Amorite into the power of Israel, when he destroyed them in Gabaon, and they were destroyed from before the children of Israel: and Joshua said, Let the sun stand over against Gabaon, and the moon over against the valley of Aelon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ambacum 3:11; Esias 38:8; Joshua 10:14"> And the sun and the moon stood still, until God executed vengeance on their enemies; and the sun stood still in the midst of heaven; it did not proceed to set till the end of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e. additional day without a night between.">c</a></sup>one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:30; Exodus 14:14; Joshua 10:42"> And there was not such a day either before or after, so that God should listen to a man, because the Lord fought on the side of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Kings I 24:8; Amos 9:2; Psalms 139:7–10"> And these five kings fled, and hid themselves in a cave that is in Makeda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And it was told Joshua, saying, The five kings have been found hid in the cave that is in Makeda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:66; Job 21:30; Amos 9:1"> And Joshua said, Roll stones to the mouth of the cave, and set men to watch over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 18:37–41; Jeremias 8:14; Esias 9:14–15"> But do not you⌃ stand, but pursue after your enemies, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. fasten upon or seize.">d</a></sup>attack the rear of them, and do not suffer them to enter into their cities; for the Lord our God has delivered them into our hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 8:24; Kings II 20:6; Joshua 10:10"> And it came to pass when Joshua and all Israel ceased destroying them utterly with a very great slaughter, that they that escaped took refuge in the strong cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 11:7; Esias 54:17; Esias 57:4"> And all the people returned<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. sound, or healthy.">e</a></sup>safe to Joshua to Makeda; and no one of the children of Israel murmured with his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings I 15:32"> And Joshua said, Open the cave, and bring out these five kings out of the cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:1; Joshua 10:5; Joshua 10:3"> And they brought out the five kings out of the cave, the king of Jerusalem, and the king of Chebron, and the king of Jerimuth, and the king of Lachis, and the king of Odollam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Malachias 4:3; Romans 16:20; Revelation 2:26–27"> And when they brought them out to Joshua, then Joshua called together all Israel, and the chiefs<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. of the war.">f</a></sup>of the army that went with him, saying to them, Come forward and set your feet on their necks; and they came and set their feet on their necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:10; Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10"> And Joshua said to them, Do not fear them, neither be cowardly; be courageous and strong, for thus the Lord will do to all your enemies, against whom you⌃ fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 8:29; Kings II 21:9; Matthew 27:25"> And Joshua killed them, and hanged them on five trees; and they hung upon the trees until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 8:29; Deuteronomy 21:22–23; Joshua 7:26"> And it came to pass toward the setting of the sun, Joshua commanded, and they took them down from the trees, and cast them into the cave into which they had fled for refuge, and rolled stones to the cave, which remain till this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 6:21; Deuteronomy 7:16; Deuteronomy 20:16–17"> And they took Makeda on that day, and killed<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. it.">g</a></sup>the inhabitants with the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. mouth.">h</a></sup>edge of the sword, and they utterly destroyed every living thing that was in it; and there was none left in it that was preserved and had escaped; and they did to the king of Makeda, as they did to the king of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 21:13; Joshua 15:42; Jeremias 52:1"> And Joshua and all Israel with him departed out of Makeda to Lebna, and besieged Lebna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the Lord delivered it into the hands of Israel: and they took it, and its king, and killed the inhabitants with the edge of the sword, and every thing breathing in it; and there was not left in it any that survived and escaped; and they did to its king, as they did to the king of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 12:11; Joshua 10:5"> And Joshua and all Israel with him departed from Lebna to Lachis, and he encamped about it, and besieged it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the Lord delivered Lachis into the hands of Israel; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Gr. he, i.e. Israel.">i</a></sup>they took it on the second day, and they put the inhabitants to death with the edge of the sword, and utterly destroyed it, as they had done to Lebna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Judges 1:29; Joshua 16:3"> Then Elam the king of Gazer went up to help Lachis; and Joshua struck him and his people with the edge of the sword, until there was not left to him one that was preserved and escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Joshua 10:3; Joshua 12:12; Joshua 15:39"> And Joshua and all Israel with him departed from Lachis to Odollam, and he besieged it and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Or, vigourously attacked it.">j</a></sup>took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 26:44; Joshua 10:37; Job 19:10"> And the Lord delivered it into the hand of Israel; and he took it on that day, and killed the inhabitants with the edge of the sword, and killed every thing breathing in it, as they did to Lachis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Judges 1:10; Joshua 15:13; Joshua 14:13–14"> And Joshua and all Israel with him departed to Chebron, and encamped about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And he struck it with the edge of the sword, and all the living creatures that were in it; there was no one preserved: they destroyed it and all things in it, as they did to Odollam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Joshua 15:15; Joshua 12:13; Joshua 15:49"> And Joshua and all Israel returned to Dabir; and they encamped about it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Obdias 1:18; Joshua 11:8; Deuteronomy 3:3"> and they took it, and its king, and its villages: and he struck it with the edge of the sword, and they destroyed it, and every thing breathing in it; and they did not leave in it any one that was preserved: as they did to Chebron and her king, so they did to Dabir and her king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2–16; Exodus 23:31–33; Joshua 8:27"> And Joshua struck all the land of the hill country, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Heb. south.">k</a></sup>Nageb and the plain country, and Asedoth, and her kings, they did not leave of them one that was saved: and they utterly destroyed every thing that had the breath of life, as the Lord God of Israel commanded,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Joshua 11:16; Joshua 15:51; Deuteronomy 9:23"> from Cades Barne to Gaza, all Gosom, as far as Gabaon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Joshua 10:14; Psalms 46:7; Psalms 80:3"> And Joshua struck, once for all, all their kings, and their land, because the Lord God of Israel fought on the side of Israel.</span>
