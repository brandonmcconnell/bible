<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 2:15; Osee 8:1; Acts 20:26–27"> Cry aloud, and spare not; lift up your voice as with a trumpet, and declare to my people their sins, and to the house of Jacob their iniquities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:16; Esias 29:13; Matthew 15:7–9"> They seek me day by day, and desire to know my ways, as a people that had done righteousness, and had not forsaken the judgment of their God: they now ask of me righteous judgment, and desire to draw near to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Malachias 3:14; Leviticus 16:29"> saying, Why have we fasted, and you regard not? why have we afflicted our souls, and you did not know it? Nay, in the days of your fasts you⌃ find your pleasures, and all them that are under your power you⌃ wound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Kings III 21:9–13; Esias 59:2; Proverbs 21:27"> If you⌃ fast for quarrels and strifes, and strike the lowly with your fists, therefore do you⌃ fast to me as you⌃ do this day, so that your voice may be heard in crying?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 9:3–19; Zacharias 7:5; Esias 49:8"> I have not chosen this fast, nor such a day for a man to afflict his soul; neither though you should bend down your neck as a ring, and spread under you sackcloth and ashes, neither thus shall you⌃ call a fast acceptable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:10–12; Esias 58:9; Jeremias 34:8–11"> I have not chosen such a fast, says the Lord; but do you loose every burden of iniquity, do you untie the knots of hard bargains, set the bruised free, and cancel every unjust account.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 25:35–45; Luke 3:11; 1 John 3:17–18"> Break your bread to the hungry, and lead the unsheltered poor to your house: if you see one naked, clothe him, and you shall not disregard the relations of your own seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 52:12; Jeremias 33:6; Psalms 37:6"> Then shall your light break forth as the morning, and your health shall speedily spring forth: and your righteousness shall go before you, and the glory of God shall compass you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremias 29:12–13; Psalms 50:15; Esias 65:24"> Then shall you cry, and God shall listen to you; while you are yet speaking he will say, Behold, I am here. If you remove from you the band, and the stretching forth of the hands, and murmuring speech;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 11:24–25; Esias 58:7–8; Esias 42:16"> and if you give bread to the hungry from your heart, and satisfy the afflicted soul; then shall your light spring up in darkness, and your darkness shall be as noon-day:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 4:14; Jeremias 31:12; Psalms 32:8"> and your God shall be with you continually, and you shall be satisfied according as your soul desires; and your bones shall be made fat, and shall be as a well-watered garden, and as a fountain from which the water has not failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 61:4; Jezekiel 36:33; Amos 9:14"> And your old waste desert places shall be built up, and your foundations shall last through all generations; and you shall be called a repairer of breaches, and you shall cause your paths between to be in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 17:21–27; Psalms 92:1–2; Nehemiah 13:15–22"> If you turn away your foot from the sabbath, so as not to do your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. pleasures.">a</a></sup>pleasure on the holy days, and shall call the sabbaths delightful, holy to God; if you shall not lift up your foot to work, nor speak a word in anger out of your mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Job 22:26; Esias 33:16"> then shall you trust on the Lord; and he shall bring you up to the good places of the land, and feed you with the heritage of Jacob your father: for the mouth of the Lord has spoken this.</span>
