<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:26; Esias 41:1; Esias 1:2"> Draw near, you⌃ nations; and listen, you⌃ princes; let the earth hear, and they that are in it; the world, and the people that are therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 25:15–29; Sophonias 3:8; Zacharias 14:12–16"> For the wrath of the Lord is upon all nations, and his anger upon the number of them, to destroy them, and give them up to slaughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 2:20; Jezekiel 39:4; Esias 34:7"> And their slain shall be cast forth, and their corpses; and their ill savor shall come up, and the mountains shall be made wet with their blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 6:13–14; Matthew 24:29; Jezekiel 32:7–8"> And all the powers of the heavens shall melt, and the sky shall be rolled up like a scroll: and all the stars shall fall like leaves from a vine, and as leaves fall from a fig tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 46:10; Malachias 1:4; Esias 63:1"> My sword has been made drunk in heaven: behold, it shall come down upon Idumea, and with judgment upon the people doomed to destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 63:1; Jeremias 49:13; Esias 34:5"> The sword of the Lord is filled with blood, it is glutted with fat, with the blood of goats and lambs, and with the fat of goats and rams: for the Lord has a sacrifice in Bosor, and a great slaughter in Idumea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 68:30; Numbers 23:22; Jeremias 50:27"> And the mighty ones shall fall with them, and the rams and the bulls; and the land shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. made drunken">a</a></sup>soaked with blood, and shall be filled with their fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 63:4; Esias 61:2; Jeremias 46:10"> For it is the day of judgment of the Lord, and the year of the recompence of Sion in judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Psalms 11:6; Genesis 19:28"> And her valleys shall be turned into pitch, and her land into sulphur; and her land shall be as pitch burning night and day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 19:3; Esias 13:20; Malachias 1:3–4"> and it shall never be quenched, and her smoke shall go up: it shall be made desolate throughout her generations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Lamentations 2:8; Kings IV 21:13; Sophonias 2:14"> and for a long time birds and hedgehogs, and ibises and ravens shall dwell in it: and the measuring line of desolation shall be cast over it, and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Vide supra, 13:22.">b</a></sup>satyrs shall dwell in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 3:6–8; 1 Corinthians 13:2; Jeremias 39:6"> Her princes shall be no more; for her kings and her great men shall be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremias 9:11; Jeremias 10:22; Malachias 1:3"> And thorns shall spring up in their cities, and in her strong holds: and they shall be habitations of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Vide supra, Job 30. 29; Isa. 13. 21, etc.">c</a></sup>monsters, and a court of ostriches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 13:21–22"> And devils shall meet with satyrs, and they shall cry one to the other: there shall satyrs rest, having found for themselves a place of rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 14:13"> There has the hedgehog made its nest, and the earth has safely preserved its young: there have the deer met, and seen one another's faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 33:9; Joshua 1:8; Matthew 5:18"> They passed by in full number, and not one of them perished: they sought not one another; for the Lord commanded them, and his Spirit gathered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 34:10; Acts 17:26; Esias 17:14"> And he shall cast lots for them, and his hand has portioned out their pasture, saying, You⌃ shall inherit the land for ever: they shall rest on it through all generations.</span>
