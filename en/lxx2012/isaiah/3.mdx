<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Jezekiel 14:13; Esias 1:24"> Behold now, the Lord, the Lord of hosts, will take away from Jerusalem and from Judea the mighty man and mighty woman, the strength of bread, and the strength of water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 9:14–15; Kings IV 24:14–16; Lamentations 5:12–14"> the great and mighty man, the warrior and the judge, and the prophet, and the counsellor, and the elder,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 8:18; Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21"> the captain of fifty also, and the honorable counsellor, and the wise artificer, and the intelligent hearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16; Chronicles II 33:1; Kings III 3:7–9"> And I will make youths their princes, and mockers shall have dominion over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 9:3–8; Malachias 3:5; Matthew 27:28–30"> And the people shall fall, man upon man, and every man upon his neighbor: the child shall insult the elder man, and the base the honorable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 4:1; John 6:15; Judges 11:6–8"> For a man shall lay hold of his brother, as one of his father's household, saying, You have raiment, be you our ruler, and let my meat be under you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Osee 5:13; Lamentations 2:13; Jezekiel 34:4"> And he shall answer in that day, and say, I will not be your ruler; for I have no bread in my house, nor raiment: I will not be the ruler of this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:22; Jezekiel 9:9; Malachias 3:13–15"> For Jerusalem is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Or, forsaken, or, let go.">a</a></sup>ruined, and Judea has fallen, and their tongues have spoken with iniquity, disobedient as they are towards the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Genesis 18:20–21; Kings IV 9:30"> Therefore now their glory has been brought low, and the shame of their countenance has withstood them, and they have proclaimed their sin as Sodom, and made it manifest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 128:1–2; Hebrews 6:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> Woe to their soul, for they have devised an evil counsel against themselves, saying against themselves, Let us bind the just, for he is burdensome to us: therefore shall they eat the fruits of their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:13; James 2:13; Psalms 62:12"> Woe to the transgressor! evils shall happen to him according to the works of his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 3:4; Naum 3:13; Kings IV 11:1"> O my people, your exactors<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. glean you.">b</a></sup>strip you, and extortioners rule over you: O my people, they that pronounce you blessed lead you astray, and pervert the path of your feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Michaeas 6:2; Osee 4:1–2; Proverbs 22:22–23"> But now the Lord will stand up for judgment, and will enter into judgment with his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:4; Psalms 143:2; Esias 3:2–3"> The Lord himself shall enter into judgment with the elders of the people, and with their rulers: but why have you⌃ set my vineyard on fire, and why is the spoil of the poor in your houses?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Michaeas 3:2–3; Psalms 94:5; Amos 2:6–7"> Why do you⌃ wrong my people, and shame the face of the poor?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 32:9–11; Jezekiel 16:49–50; Esias 4:4"> Thus says the Lord, Because the daughters of Sion are haughty, and have walked with an outstretched neck, and with winking of the eyes, and motion of the feet, at the same time drawing their garments in trains, and at the same time sporting with their feet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremias 13:22; Michaeas 1:11; Revelation 16:2"> therefore the Lord will humble the chief daughters of Sion, and the Lord will expose their form in that day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Judges 8:26"> and the Lord will take away the glory of their raiment, the curls and the fringes, and the crescents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:11; Numbers 31:50; Exodus 35:22"> and the chains, and the ornaments of their faces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; Genesis 35:4; Jezekiel 16:12"> and the array of glorious ornaments, and the armlets, and the bracelets, and the wreathed work, and the finger-rings, and the ornaments for the right hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 24:47; 1 Peter 3:3–4; Luke 15:22"> and the ear-rings, and the garments with scarlet borders, and the garments with purple grounds, and the shawls to be worn in the house, and the Spartan transparent dresses, and those made of fine linen, and the purple ones, and the scarlet ones, and the fine linen, interwoven with gold and purple, and the light coverings for couches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And there shall be instead of a sweet smell, dust; and instead of a girdle, you shall gird yourself with a rope; and instead of a golden ornament for the head, you shall have baldness on account of your works; and instead of a tunic with a scarlet ground, you shall gird yourself with sackcloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; Ruth 3:15; Jezekiel 16:10"> And your most beautiful son whom you love shall fall by the sword; and your mighty men shall fall by the sword, and shall be brought low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Jezekiel 27:31; Esias 22:12"> And the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. cases, or, repositories.">c</a></sup>stores of your ornaments shall mourn, and you shall be left alone, and shall be levelled with the ground.</span>
