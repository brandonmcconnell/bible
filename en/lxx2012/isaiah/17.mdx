<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 25:2; Zacharias 9:1; Amos 1:3–5"> THE WORD AGAINST DAMASCUS. Behold, Damascus shall be taken away from among cities, and shall become a ruin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 25:5; Jeremias 7:33; Numbers 32:34"> abandoned for ever, to be a fold and resting-place for flocks, and there shall be none to go after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Osee 9:11; Esias 8:4; Esias 7:16"> And she shall no longer be a strong place for Ephraim to flee to, and there shall no longer be a kingdom in Damascus, or a remnant of Syrians; for you are no better than the children of Israel, even than their glory; thus says the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 10:16; Deuteronomy 32:15–27; Esias 24:13"> There shall be in that day a failure of the glory of Jacob, and the riches of his glory shall be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Joel 3:13; Jeremias 51:33"> And it shall be as if one should gather standing corn, and reap the grain of the ears; and it shall be as if one should gather ears in a rich valley;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 24:13; Deuteronomy 4:27; Romans 9:27"> and as if there should be left stubble therein, or as it were the berries of an olive tree, two or three on the topmost bough, or as if four or five should be left on their branches; thus says the Lord, the God of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Michaeas 7:7; Osee 6:1; Osee 3:5"> In that day a man shall trust in him that made him, and his eyes shall have respect to the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 30:22; Esias 27:9; Esias 2:8"> And they shall not at all trust in their altars, nor in the works of their hands, which their fingers made; and they shall not look to the trees, nor to their abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 3:11–15; Esias 27:10; Esias 24:1–12"> In that day your cities shall be deserted, as the Amorites and the Evaeans deserted theirs, because of the children of Israel; and they shall be desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:21; Deuteronomy 32:18; Esias 26:4"> Because you have forsaken God your Saviour, and have not been mindful of the Lord your helper; therefore shall you plant a<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. faithless">a</a></sup>false plant, and a false seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 4:8; Osee 8:7; Galatians 6:7–8"> In the day wherein you shall plant you shall be deceived; but if you sow in the morning, the seed shall spring up for a crop in the day wherein you shall obtain an inheritance, and as a man's father, you shall obtain an inheritance for your sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 6:23; Luke 21:25; Psalms 18:4"> Woe to the multitude of many nations, as the swelling sea, so shall you⌃ be confounded; and the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. back; Complut. reads ἠχος, ‘noise’">b</a></sup>force of many nations shall sound like water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 9:5; Psalms 1:4; Esias 41:15–16"> many nations like much water, as when much water rushes violently: and they shall drive him away, and pursue him afar, as the dust of chaff when men winnow before the wind, and as a storm whirling the dust of the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 19:35; Proverbs 22:23; Jeremias 13:25"> Toward evening, and there shall be grief; before the morning, and he shall not be. This is the portion of them that spoiled you, and the inheritance to them that robbed you of your inheritance.</span>
