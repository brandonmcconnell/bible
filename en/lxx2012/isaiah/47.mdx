<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 51:33; Psalms 137:8; Zacharias 2:7"> Come down, sit on the ground, O virgin daughter of Babylon: sit on the ground, O daughter of the Chaldeans: for you shall no more be called tender and luxurious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 20:4; Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5"> Take a millstone, grind meal: remove your veil, uncover your white hairs, make bare the leg, pass through the rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Naum 3:5; Jeremias 51:20–24; Jeremias 50:27–28"> Your shame shall be uncovered, your reproaches shall be brought to light: I will exact of you due vengeance, I will no longer deliver you to men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 44:6; Esias 43:14; Esias 49:26"> Your deliverer is the Lord of hosts, the Holy One of Israel is his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 18:16–19; Ambacum 2:20; Esias 47:7"> Sit you down pierced with woe, go into darkness, O daughter of the Chaldeans: you shall no more be called the strength of a kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles II 28:9; Zacharias 1:15; Deuteronomy 28:50"> I have been provoked with my people; you have defiled my inheritance: I gave them into your hand, but you did not extend mercy to them: you made the yoke of the aged man very heavy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Esias 47:5; Jeremias 5:31"> and said, I shall be a princess for ever: you did not perceive these things in your heart, nor did you remember the latter end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Sophonias 2:15; Esias 32:9; Esias 47:10"> But now hear these words, you luxurious one, who are the one that sits at ease, that is secure, that says in her heart, I am, and there is not another; I shall not sit a widow, neither shall I know bereavement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 73:19; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Esias 47:12–13"> But now these two things shall come upon you suddenly in one day, the loss of children and widowhood shall come suddenly upon you, for your sorcery, for the strength of your enchantments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 5:21; Esias 29:15; Romans 1:22"> for your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. the hope of your wickedness.">a</a></sup>trusting in wickedness: for you said, I am, and there is not another: know you, the understanding of these things and your harlotry shall be your shame; for you said in your heart, I am, and there is not another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:3; Revelation 18:9–10; Daniel 5:25–30"> And destruction shall come upon you, and you shall not be aware; there shall be a pit, and you shall fall into it: and grief shall come upon you, and you shall not be able to be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, pure.">b</a></sup>clear; and destruction shall come suddenly upon you, and you shall not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 13:8–12; Naum 3:4; Jeremias 2:28"> Stand now with your enchantments, and with the abundance of your sorcery, which you have learned from your youth; if you can be profited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 44:25; Daniel 2:2–10; Jezekiel 24:12"> You are wearied in your counsels. Let now the astrologers of the heaven stand and deliver you, let them that see the stars tell you what is about to come upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachias 4:1; Naum 1:10; Jezekiel 15:7"> Behold, they all shall be burnt up as sticks in the fire; neither shall they at all deliver their life from the flame. Because you have coals of fire, sit you upon them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 56:11; Jezekiel 27:12–25; Jeremias 51:6–9"> these shall be your help. You have wearied yourself with traffic from your youth: every man has wandered to his own home, but you shall have no deliverance.</span>
