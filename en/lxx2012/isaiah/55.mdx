<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 60:9; Esias 60:5; Zacharias 2:11"> Nations which know you not, shall call upon you, and peoples which are not acquainted with you, shall flee to you for refuge, for the sake of the Lord your God, the Holy One of Israel; for he has glorified you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremias 29:12–14; Psalms 145:18; Esias 45:19"> Seek you⌃ the Lord, and when you⌃ find him, call upon him; and when he shall draw near to you,</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 92:5; Psalms 40:5; Osee 14:9"> For my counsels are not as your counsels, nor are my ways as your ways, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 11:31–36; Psalms 103:11; Matthew 11:25"> But as the heaven is distant from the earth, so is my way distant from your ways, and your thoughts from my mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:9–11; Deuteronomy 32:2; Esias 30:23"> For as rain shall come down, or snow, from heaven, and shall not return until it have saturated the earth, and it bring forth, and bud, and give seed to the sower, and bread for food:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Esias 46:10; Romans 10:17"> so shall my word be, whatever shall proceed out of my mouth, it shall by no means turn back, until all the things which I willed shall have been accomplished; and I will make your ways prosperous, and will effect my commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 5:22; Esias 44:23; Psalms 105:43"> For you⌃ shall go forth with joy, and shall be taught with gladness: for the mountains and the hills shall exult to welcome you with joy, and all the trees of the field shall applaud with their branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 3:20–21; Esias 41:19; 1 Peter 2:9–10"> And instead of the bramble shall come up the cypress, and instead of the nettle shall come up the myrtle: and the Lord shall be for a name, and for an everlasting sign, and shall not fail.</span>
