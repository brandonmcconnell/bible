<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:48–50; Acts 17:24; Matthew 5:34–35"> Thus says the Lord, Heaven is my throne, and the earth is my footstool: what kind of a house will you⌃ build me? and of what kind is to be the place of my rest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 57:15; Psalms 34:18; Psalms 51:17"> For all these things are mine, says the Lord: and to whom will I have respect, but to the humble and meek, and the man that trembles at my words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 15:8; Amos 5:21–22; Esias 1:11–15"> But the transgressor that sacrifices a calf to me, is as he that kills a dog; and he that offers fine flour, as one that offers swine's blood; he that gives frankincense for a memorial, is as a blasphemer. Yet they have chosen their own ways, and their soul has delighted in their abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 65:12; Jeremias 7:13; Proverbs 10:24"> I also will choose their mockeries, and will recompense their sins upon them; because I called them, and they did not listen to me; I spoke, and they heard not: and they did evil before me, and chose the things wherein I delighted not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:18–20; Esias 66:2; Matthew 5:10–12"> Hear the words of the Lord, you⌃ that tremble at his word; speak you⌃,<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Alex. ἡμῶν, but Heb. and Vat. 'your.'">a</a></sup>our brethren, to them that hate you and abominate you, that the name of the Lord may be glorified, and may appear<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, your.">b</a></sup>their joy; but they shall be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 3:7–16; Amos 1:2; Esias 34:8"> A voice of a cry from the city, a voice from the temple, a voice of the Lord rendering recompence to his adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 12:1–5; Esias 54:1; Galatians 4:26"> Before she that travailed brought forth, before the travail-pain came on, she escaped it and brought forth a male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 64:4; Esias 49:20–22; Romans 15:18–21"> Who has heard such a thing? and who has seen after this manner? Has the earth travailed in one day? or has even a nation been born at once, that Sion has travailed, and brought forth her children?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 37:3; Genesis 18:14"> But I have raised this expectation, yet you have not remembered me, says the Lord: behold, have not I made the bearing and barren woman? says your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 26:8; Deuteronomy 32:43; Psalms 137:6"> Rejoice, O Jerusalem, and all you⌃ that love her hold in her a general assembly: rejoice greatly with her, all that now mourn over her:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 60:16; Joel 3:18; Esias 60:5"> that you⌃ may suck, and be satisfied with the breast of her consolation; that you⌃ may milk out, and delight yourselves with the influx of her glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 48:18; Esias 60:16; Esias 45:14"> For thus says the Lord, Behold, I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="i.e. turn myself.">c</a></sup>turn toward them as a river of peace, and as a torrent bringing upon them in a flood the glory of the Gentiles: their children shall be borne upon the shoulders, and comforted on the knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:4; Esias 51:3; Esias 40:1"> As if his mother should comfort one, so will I also comfort you; and you⌃ shall be comforted in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Zacharias 10:7; Ezra 8:31; Ezra 7:9"> And you⌃ shall see, and your heart shall rejoice, and your bones shall<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. spring up.">d</a></sup>thrive like grass: and the hand of the Lord shall be known to them that fear him, and he shall threaten the disobedient.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 30:33; 2 Thessalonians 1:6–9; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> For, behold, the Lord will come as fire, and his chariots as a storm, to render his vengeance with wrath, and his rebuke with a flame of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 38:21–22; Esias 30:30; Jezekiel 39:2–10"> For with the fire of the Lord all the earth shall be judged, and all flesh with his sword: many shall be slain by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 65:3–4; Deuteronomy 14:3–8; Esias 1:28–29"> They that sanctify themselves and purify themselves in the gardens, and eat swine's flesh in the porches, and the abominations, and the mouse, shall be consumed together, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 86:9; Revelation 11:15; 2 Corinthians 4:4–6"> And I know their works and their imagination. I am going to gather all nations and tongues; and they shall come, and see my glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 52:15; Romans 15:21; Esias 55:5"> And I will leave a sign upon them, and I will send forth them that have escaped of them to the nations, to Tharsis, and Phud, and Lud, and Mosoch, and to Thobel, and to Greece, and to the isles afar off, to those who have not heard my name, nor seen my glory; and they shall declare my glory among the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Esias 60:3–14; Esias 65:11; Esias 11:9"> And they shall bring your brethren out of all nations for a gift to the Lord with horses, and chariots, in litters drawn by mules with awnings, to the holy city Jerusalem, said the Lord, as though the children of Israel should bring their sacrifices to me with psalms into the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Exodus 19:6; Revelation 1:6"> And I will take of them priests and Levites, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esias 65:17; 2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1"> For as the new heaven and the new earth, which I make, remain before me, says the Lord, so shall your seed and your name continue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 46:1; Psalms 86:9; Colossians 2:16–17"> And it shall come to pass from month to month, and from sabbath to sabbath, that all flesh shall come to worship before me in Jerusalem, says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 14:10–11; Esias 14:11; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16"> And they shall go forth, and see the carcasses of the men that have transgressed against me: for their worm shall not die, and their fire shall not be quenched; and they shall be a spectacle to all flesh.</span>
