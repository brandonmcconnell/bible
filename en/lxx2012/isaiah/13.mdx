<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 47:1–15; Jeremias 50:1–64; Esias 1:1"> THE VISION WHICH ESAIAS SON OF AMOS SAW AGAINST BABYLON.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 51:58; Esias 5:26; Jeremias 50:2"> Lift up a standard on the mountain of the plain, exalt the voice to them, beckon with the hand, open the gates, you⌃ rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Joel 3:11; Jeremias 51:20–24; Revelation 17:12–18"> I give command, and I bring them: giants are coming to fulfil my wrath, rejoicing at the same time and insulting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joel 3:14; Revelation 19:11–21; Joel 2:25"> A voice of many nations on the mountains, even like to that of many nations; a voice of kings and nations gathered together: the Lord of hosts has given command to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Lit. fighting with armor or weapons">a</a></sup>a warlike nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremias 51:20–46; Matthew 24:31; Jeremias 50:9"> to come from a land afar off, from the utmost foundation of heaven; the Lord and his warriors are coming to destroy all the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joel 1:15; Esias 13:9; Amos 5:18"> Howl you⌃, for the day of the Lord is near, and destruction from God shall arrive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 21:7; Naum 2:10; Naum 1:6"> Therefore every hand shall become powerless, and every soul of man shall be dismayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 26:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Naum 2:10"> The elders shall be troubled, and pangs shall seize them, as of a woman in travail: and they shall mourn one to another, and shall be amazed, and shall change their countenance as a flame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Naum 1:6; Malachias 4:1; Esias 13:15–18"> For behold! the day of the Lord is coming which can’t be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. healed">b</a></sup>escaped, a day of wrath and anger, to make the world desolate, and to destroy sinners out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:31; Matthew 24:29; Mark 13:24"> For the stars of heaven, and Orion, and all the host of heaven, shall not give their light; and it shall be dark at sunrise, and the moon shall not give her light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 2:11; Esias 5:15; Daniel 5:22–23"> And I will command evils for the whole world, and will visit their sins on the ungodly: and I will destroy the pride of transgressors, and will bring low the pride of the haughty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Esias 4:1; Esias 24:6; Job 28:16"> And they that are left shall be more precious than gold tried in the fire; and a man shall be more precious than the stone that is in Suphir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–27; Joel 3:16; Matthew 24:29"> For the heaven shall be enraged, and the earth shall be shaken from her foundation, because of the fierce anger of the Lord of hosts, in the day in which his wrath shall come on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings III 22:17; Jeremias 50:16; Kings III 22:36"> And they that are left shall be as a fleeing fawn, and as a stray sheep, and there shall be none to gather them: so that a man shall turn back to his people, and a man shall flee to his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 14:19–22; Jeremias 50:27; Esias 47:9–14"> For whoever shall be taken shall be overcome; and they that are gathered together shall fall by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Naum 3:10; Osee 10:14; Psalms 137:8–9"> And they shall dash their children before their eyes; and they shall spoil their houses, and shall take their wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 5:28–31; Jeremias 51:11; Esias 41:25"> Behold, I will stir up against you the Medes, who do not regard silver, neither have they need of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings IV 8:12; Chronicles II 36:17; Esias 13:16"> They shall break the bows of the young men; and they shall have no mercy on your children; nor shall their eyes spare your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 4:30; Sophonias 2:9; Jeremias 51:41"> And Babylon, which is called glorious by the king of the Chaldeans, shall be as when God overthrew Sodoma, and Gomorrha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremias 51:43; Esias 14:23; Jeremias 50:3"> It shall never be inhabited, neither shall any enter into it for many generations: neither shall the Arabians pass through it; nor shall shepherds at all rest in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 18:2; Esias 34:11–15"> But wild beasts shall rest there; and the houses shall be filled with howling; and<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="See Job 30:29; Isa 34:13, etc.">c</a></sup>monsters shall rest there, and devils shall dance there, and satyrs shall dwell there; and hedgehogs shall make their nests in their houses.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="See Heb 10.37; Hab 2.3">d</a></sup>It will come soon, and will not wait.</span>
