<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:27; Esias 62:4; Kings I 2:5"> Rejoice, you barren that bear not; break forth and cry, you that do not travail: for more are the children of the desolate than of her that has a husband: for the Lord has said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 33:20; Jeremias 10:20; Esias 49:19–20"> Enlarge the place of your tent, and of your curtains: fix the pins, spare not, lengthen your cords, and strengthen your pins;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 36:35–36; Genesis 28:14; Esias 43:5–6"> spread forth your tent yet to the right and the left: for your seed shall inherit the Gentiles, and you shall make the desolate cities to be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 41:10; 1 Peter 2:6; Jeremias 31:19"> Fear not, because you have been put to shame, neither be confounded, because you were reproached: for you shall forget your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. ancient Or. everlasting">a</a></sup>former shame, and shall no more at all remember the reproach of your widowhood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25–27; Osee 2:19–20; 2 Corinthians 11:2–3"> For it is the Lord that made you; the Lord of hosts is his name: and he that delivered you, he is the God of Israel, and shall be called so by the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 62:4; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Osee 2:14–15"> The Lord has not called you as a deserted and faint-hearted woman, nor as a woman hated from her youth, says your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 30:5; 2 Peter 3:8; Michaeas 4:6"> For a little while I left you: but with great mercy will I have compassion upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremias 31:3; Esias 60:10; Jezekiel 39:23–24"> In a little wrath I turned away my face from you; but with everlasting mercy will I have compassion upon you, says the Lord that delivers you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 12:1; Genesis 8:21; Hebrews 6:16–18"> From the time of the water of Noe this is my purpose: as I swore to him at that time, saying of the earth, I will no more be angry with you, neither when you are threatened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 89:33–34; Psalms 46:2; Ephesians 2:4–5"> shall the mountains depart, nor shall your hills be removed: so neither shall my mercy fail you, nor shall the covenant of your peace be at all removed: for<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Alex. κύριος for κύριε adopted here. Compare Mat. 16. 22, with this passage.">b</a></sup>the Lord who is gracious to you has spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 2:4–6; Acts 14:22; Jeremias 30:17"> Afflicted and outcast you have not been comforted: behold, I will prepare carbuncle for your stones, and sapphire for your foundations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and I will make your buttresses jasper, and your gates crystal, and your border precious stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 6:45; Philippians 4:7; Psalms 119:165"> And I will cause all your sons to be taught of God, and your children to be in great peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 3:25–26; Jeremias 30:10; Zacharias 9:8"> And you shall be built in righteousness: abstain from injustice, and you shall not fear; and trembling shall not come near you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 41:11–16; Psalms 37:12–13; Zacharias 12:9"> Behold, strangers shall come to you by me, and shall sojourn with you, and shall run to you for refuge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 9:16; Proverbs 16:4; Esias 46:11"> Behold, I have created you, not as the coppersmith blowing coals, and bringing out a vessel fit for work; but I have created you, not for ruin, that I should destroy you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 8:28–39; Esias 54:15; John 10:28–30"> I will not suffer any<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. instrument.">c</a></sup>weapon formed against you to prosper; and every voice that shall rise up against you for judgment, you shall vanquish them all; and your adversaries shall be condemned thereby. There is an inheritance to them that serve the Lord, and you⌃ shall be righteous before me, says the Lord.</span>
