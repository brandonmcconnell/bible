<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:20; Romans 9:24–26; 1 Peter 2:10"> I became manifest to them that asked not for me; I was found of them that sought me not: I said, Behold, I am here, to a nation, who called not on my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 10:21; Proverbs 1:24; Jeremias 4:14"> I have stretched forth my hands all day to a disobedient and gainsaying people, to them that walked in a way that was not good, but after their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 1:29; Deuteronomy 32:21; Esias 66:17"> This is the people that provokes me continually in my presence; they offer sacrifices in gardens, and burn incense on bricks to devils, which exist not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 66:17; Leviticus 11:7; Esias 66:3"> They lie down to sleep in the tombs and in the caves for the sake of dreams, even they that eat swine's flesh, and the broth of their sacrifices: all their vessels are defiled:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 18:9–12; Luke 7:39; Proverbs 16:5"> who say, Depart from me, draw not near to me, for I am pure. This is the smoke of my wrath, a fire burns with it continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 50:3; Jeremias 16:18; Psalms 79:12"> Behold, it is written before me: I will not be silent until I have recompensed into their bosom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jezekiel 20:27–28; Esias 57:7; Exodus 20:5"> their sins and the sins of their fathers, says the Lord, who have burnt incense on the mountains, and reproached me on the hills: I will recompense their works into their bosom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 9:27–29; Amos 9:8–9; Romans 11:24–26"> Thus says the Lord, As a grape-stone shall be found in the cluster, and they shall say, Destroy it not; for a blessing is in it: so will I do for the sake of him that serves me, for his sake I will not destroy them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Amos 9:11–15; Esias 27:6; Romans 11:5–7"> And I will lead forth the seed that came of Jacob and of Juda, and they shall inherit my holy mountain: and my elect and my servants shall inherit it, and shall dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 35:2; Esias 33:9; Osee 2:15"> And there shall be in the forest folds of flocks, and the valley of Achor shall be for a resting-place of herds for my people, who have sought me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 2:2; Esias 1:28; Deuteronomy 32:17"> But you⌃ are they that have left me, and forget my holy mountain, and prepare a table for the devil, and fill up the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. mixture.">a</a></sup>drink-offering to Fortune.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremias 7:13; Chronicles II 36:15–16; Zacharias 7:11–13"> I will deliver you up to the sword, you⌃ shall all fall by slaughter: for I called you, and you⌃ listened not; I spoke, and you⌃ refused to hear; and you⌃ did evil in my sight, and chose the things wherein I delighted not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Malachias 3:18; Esias 66:14; Esias 61:7"> Therefore thus says the Lord, Behold, my servants shall eat, but you⌃ shall hunger: behold, my servants shall drink, but you⌃ shall thirst: behold, my servants shall rejoice, but you⌃ shall be ashamed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 8:12; Psalms 66:4; Matthew 22:13"> behold, my servants shall exult with joy, but you⌃ shall cry for the sorrow of your heart, and shall howl for the vexation of your spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 62:2; Romans 9:26; Acts 11:26"> For you⌃ shall leave your name for a loathing to my chosen, and the Lord shall destroy you: but my servants shall be called by a new name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 19:18; Jeremias 4:2; Psalms 31:5"> which shall be blessed on the earth; for they shall bless the true God: and they that swear upon the earth shall swear by the true God; for they shall forget the former affliction, it shall not come into their mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:13; Revelation 21:1–5; Esias 66:22"> For there shall be a new heaven and a new earth: and they shall not at all remember the former, neither shall they at all come into their mind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 98:1–9; Revelation 19:1–6; Esias 66:10–14"> But they shall find in her joy and exultation; for, behold, I make Jerusalem a rejoicing, and my people a joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17; Esias 25:8"> And I will rejoice in Jerusalem, and will be glad in my people: and there shall no more be heard in her the voice of weeping, or the voice of crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 5:26; Ecclesiastes 8:12–13; Deuteronomy 4:40"> Neither shall there be there any more a child that dies untimely, or an old man who shall not complete his time: for the youth shall be one hundred years old, and the sinner who dies at one hundred years shall also be accursed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Esias 62:8–9; Esias 32:18"> and they shall build houses, and themselves shall dwell in them; and they shall plant vineyards, and themselves shall eat the fruit thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 92:12–14; Psalms 91:16; Psalms 21:4"> They shall by no means build, and others inhabit; and they shall by no means plant, and others eat: for as the days of the tree of life shall be the days of my people, they shall long enjoy the fruits of their labors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esias 61:9; Jeremias 32:38–39; Deuteronomy 28:3–12"> My chosen shall not toil in vain, neither shall they beget children to be cursed; for they are a seed blessed of God, and their offspring with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 10:12; 1 John 5:14–15; Esias 58:9"> And it shall come to pass, that before they call, I will listen to them; while they are yet speaking, I will say, What is it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Esias 11:6–9; Michaeas 4:3; Acts 9:19–21"> Then wolves and lambs shall feed together, and the lion shall eat chaff like the ox, and the serpent earth as bread. They shall not injure nor destroy in my holy mountain, says the Lord.</span>
