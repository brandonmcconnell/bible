<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 51:7; Psalms 94:15; Hebrews 12:14"> Listen to me, you⌃ that follow after righteousness, and seek the Lord: look to the solid rock, which you⌃ have hewn, and to the hole of the pit which you⌃ have dug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jezekiel 33:24; Galatians 3:9–14; Romans 4:1–5"> Look to Abraam your father, and to Sarrha that bore you: for he was alone when I called him, and blessed him, and loved him, and multiplied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 49:13; Joel 2:3; Esias 52:9"> And now I will comfort you, O Sion: and I have comforted all her desert places; and I will make her desert places as a garden, and her<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Heb. שוב ambiguous.">a</a></sup>western places as the garden of the Lord; they shall find in her gladness and exultation, thanksgiving and the voice of praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Esias 2:3; 1 Corinthians 9:21"> Hear me, hear me, my people; and you⌃ kings, listen to me: for a law shall proceed from me, and my judgment shall be for a light of<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, the Gentiles.">b</a></sup>the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 10:17–18; Esias 46:13; Esias 42:4"> My righteousness speedily draws near, and my salvation shall go forth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Not in Heb. or Alex.">c</a></sup>as light, and on my arm shall the Gentiles trust: the isles shall wait for me, and on my arm shall they trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Psalms 102:25–26; Esias 34:4"> Lift up your eyes to the sky, and look on the earth beneath: for the sky was darkened like smoke, and the earth shall wax old like a garment, and the inhabitants shall die in like manner: but my righteousness shall not fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 5:11; Psalms 37:31; Jezekiel 2:6"> Hear me, you⌃ that know judgment, the people in whose heart is my law: fear not the reproach of men, and be not overcome by their contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 51:6; Esias 50:9; Osee 5:12"> For as a garment will be devoured by time, and as wool will be devoured by a moth, so shall they be consumed; but my righteousness shall be for ever, and my salvation for all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 74:13–14; Esias 52:1; Psalms 89:10"> Awake, awake, O Jerusalem, and put on the strength of your arm; awake as<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. the beginning of day.">d</a></sup>in the early time, as the ancient generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 43:16; Esias 63:11–12; Esias 50:2"> Are you not it that dried the sea, the water, even the abundance of the deep; that made the depths of the sea a way of passage for the delivered and redeemed?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 25:8; Revelation 21:4; Revelation 7:17"> for by the help of the Lord they shall return, and come to Sion with joy and everlasting exultation, for praise and joy shall come upon their head: pain, and grief, and groaning, have fled away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 118:6; 1 Peter 1:24; Esias 2:22"> I, even I, am he that comforts you: consider who you are, that you were afraid of mortal man, and of the son of man, who are withered as grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Esias 45:12; Esias 40:22; Psalms 104:2"> And you have forgotten God who made you, who made the sky and founded the earth; and you were continually afraid because of the wrath of him that afflicted you: for whereas he counselled to take you away, yet now where is the wrath of him that afflicted you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esias 48:20; Zacharias 9:11; Esias 52:2"> For in your deliverance he shall not halt, nor wait;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremias 31:35; Esias 54:5; Amos 9:5–6"> for I am your God, that troubles the sea, and causes the waves thereof to roar: the Lord of hosts is my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 59:21; Esias 49:2; John 17:8"> I will put my words into your mouth, and I will shelter you under the shadow of my hand, with which I fixed the sky, and founded the earth: and the Lord shall say to Sion, You are my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Esias 52:1; Revelation 14:10; Psalms 60:3"> Awake, awake, stand up, O Jerusalem, that have drunk at the hand of the Lord the cup of his fury: for you have drunk out and drained the cup of calamity, the cup of wrath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 49:21; Psalms 88:18; Psalms 142:4"> and there was none to comfort you of all the children whom you bore; and there was none to take hold of your hand, not even of all the children whom you have reared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Amos 7:2; Esias 47:9; Jezekiel 14:21"> Therefore these things are against you; who shall sympathize with you in your grief? downfall, and destruction, famine, and sword: who shall comfort you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Lamentations 2:11–12; Revelation 16:9–11; Jezekiel 39:19"> Your sons are the perplexed ones, that sleep at the top of every street as a half-boiled beet; they that are full of the anger of the Lord, caused to faint by the Lord God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 29:9; Esias 54:11; Esias 51:17"> Therefore hear, you afflicted one, and drunken, but not with wine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Proverbs 22:23; Esias 51:17; Jeremias 50:34"> thus says the Lord God that judges his people, Behold, I have taken out of your hand the cup of calamity, the cup of my wrath; and you shall not drink it any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 10:24; Zacharias 12:2; Esias 49:25–26"> And I will give it into the hands of them that injured you, and them that afflicted you; who said to your soul, Bow down, that we may pass over: and you did level your body with the ground to them passing by without.</span>
