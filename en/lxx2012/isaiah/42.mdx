<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 12:18–21; Luke 3:22; Esias 61:1"> Jacob is<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="See Mat 12. 18, etc.">a</a></sup>my servant, I will help him: Israel is my chosen, my soul has accepted him; I have put my Spirit upon him; he shall bring forth judgment to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 12:16–20; 2 Timothy 2:24; Matthew 11:29"> He shall not cry, nor lift up his voice, nor shall his voice be heard without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jezekiel 34:16; Jeremias 31:25; Psalms 147:3"> A bruised reed shall he not break, and smoking flax shall he not quench; but he shall bring forth judgment to truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 53:2–12; Genesis 49:10; Esias 52:13–15"> He shall shine out, and shall not be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Lit. broken">b</a></sup>discouraged, until he have set judgment on the earth: and in his name shall the Gentiles trust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Esias 40:22; Esias 45:12; Jeremias 10:12"> Thus says the Lord God, who made the heaven, and established it; who settled the earth, and the things in it, and gives breath to the people on it, and spirit to them that tread on it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 2:32; John 8:12; Acts 13:47"> I the Lord God have called you in righteousness, and will hold your hand, and will strengthen you: and I have given you for the covenant of a race, for a light of the Gentiles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 61:1; Ephesians 1:17–18; Esias 35:5"> to open the eyes of the blind, to bring the bound and them that sit in darkness out of bonds and the prison-house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 48:11; Esias 43:11; Exodus 34:14"> I am the Lord God: that is my name: I will not give my glory to another, nor my praises to graven images.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 43:19; Esias 46:9–10; John 13:19"> Behold, the ancient things have come to pass, and so will the new things which I tell you: yes, before I tell them they are made known to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 33:3; Psalms 40:3; Romans 15:9–11"> Sing a new hymn to the Lord: you⌃ who are his dominion, glorify his name from the end of the earth: you⌃ that go down to the sea, and sail upon it; the islands, and they that dwell in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 32:16; Esias 60:7; Esias 41:18–19"> Rejoice, you wilderness, and the villages thereof, the hamlets, and the dwellers in Kedar: the inhabitants of the rock shall rejoice, they shall shout from the top of the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:9–11; Psalms 117:1–2; Revelation 7:9–12"> They shall give glory to God, and shall proclaim his praises in the islands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 15:1–3; Psalms 78:65; Esias 59:16–19"> The Lord God of hosts shall go forth, and crush the war: he shall stir up jealousy, and shall shout mightily against his enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 83:1–2; Ecclesiastes 8:11–12; 2 Peter 3:15"> I have been silent: shall I also always be silent and forbear: I have endured like a travailing woman: I will now amaze and wither at once.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 50:2; Naum 1:4–6; Esias 44:27"> I will make desolate mountains and hills, and will dry up all their grass; and I will make the rivers islands, and dry up the pools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Esias 30:21; Luke 3:5; Esias 60:19–20"> And I will bring the blind by a way that they knew not, and I will cause them to tread paths which they have not known: I will turn darkness into light for them, and crooked things into straight. These things will I do, and will not forsake them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Esias 1:29; Esias 44:11"> But they are turned back: be you⌃ utterly ashamed that trust in graven images, who say to the molten images, You⌃ are our gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esias 43:8; Proverbs 20:12; Mark 7:34–37"> Hear, you⌃ deaf, and look up, you⌃ blind, to see.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; Jezekiel 12:2; Jeremias 5:21"> And who is blind, but my servants? and deaf, but they that rule over them? yes, the servants of God have been made blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:2–4; Romans 2:21; Acts 28:22–27"> You⌃ have often seen, and have not taken heed; your ears have been opened, and you⌃ have not heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10; Romans 3:31; Esias 46:12–13"> The Lord God has taken counsel that he might be justified, and might magnify his praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 102:20; Jeremias 50:17; Esias 51:23"> And I saw, and the people were spoiled and plundered: for there is a snare in the secret chambers everywhere, and in the houses also, where they have hidden them: they became a spoil, and there was no one that delivered the prey, and there was none who said, Restore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Esias 48:18; Deuteronomy 32:29"> Who is there among you that will give ear to these things? listen you⌃ to the things which are coming to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esias 47:6; Esias 10:5–6; Matthew 22:7"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Some read τίς, who">c</a></sup>For what did he give to Jacob up to spoil, and Israel to them that plundered him? Did not God do it against whom they sinned? and they would not walk in his ways, nor listen to his law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Osee 7:9; Esias 57:11; Malachias 2:2"> So he brought upon them the fury of his wrath; and the war, and those that burnt round about them, prevailed against them; yet no one of them knew it, neither did they lay it to heart.</span>
