<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings IV 19:1–37; Ezra 9:5; Esias 36:22–38"> And it came to pass, when king Ezekias heard it, that he tore his clothes, and put on sackcloth, and went up to the house of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esias 1:1; Kings IV 18:18; Esias 36:3"> And he sent Heliakim the steward, and Somnas the scribe, and the elders of the priests clothed with sackcloth, to Esaias the son of Amos, the prophet. And they said to him, Thus says Ezekias,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 66:9; Osee 13:13; Esias 26:17–18"> To-day is a day of affliction, and reproach, and rebuke, and anger: for the pangs are come upon the travailing woman, but she has not strength to bring forth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esias 1:9; Esias 36:20; Esias 10:22"> May the Lord your God hear the words of Rabsaces, which the king of the Assyrians has sent, to reproach the living God, even to reproach with the words which the Lord your God has heard: therefore you shall pray to your Lord for these that are left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So the servants of king Ezekias came to Esaias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 7:4; Esias 35:4; Mark 5:36"> And Esaias said to them, Thus shall you⌃ say to your master, Thus says the Lord, Be not you afraid at the words which you have heard, wherewith the ambassadors of the king of the Assyrians have reproached me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esias 31:8–9; Chronicles II 32:21; Esias 33:10–12"> Behold, I will send a blast upon him, and he shall hear a report, and return to his own country, and he shall fall by the sword in his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 10:29; Numbers 33:20–21; Kings IV 19:8–9"> So Rabsaces returned, and found the king of the Assyrians besieging Lobna: for he had heard that he had departed from Lachis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 18:1; Esias 20:5; Esias 37:7"> And Tharaca king of the Ethiopians went forth to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. besiege">a</a></sup>attack him. And when he heard it, he turned aside, and sent messengers to Ezekias, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esias 36:15; Kings IV 18:5; Matthew 27:43"> Thus shall you⌃ say to Ezekias king of Judea, Let not your God, in whom you trust, deceive you, saying, Jerusalem shall not be delivered into the hand of the king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 36:18–20; Kings IV 18:33–35; Esias 14:17"> Have you not heard what the kings of the Assyrians have done, how they have destroyed the whole earth? and shall you be delivered?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Kings IV 17:6; Kings IV 18:11"> Have the gods of the nations which my fathers destroyed delivered them, both Gozan, and Charrhan, and Rapheth, which are in the land of Theemath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Kings IV 19:13; Kings IV 17:24; Esias 36:19"> Where are the kings of Emath? and where is the king of Arphath? and where is the king of the city of Eppharuaim, and of Anagugana?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Kings IV 19:14; Psalms 123:1–4; Joel 2:17–20"> And Ezekias received the letter from the messengers, and read it, and went up to the house of the Lord, and opened it before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 7:8–9; Daniel 9:3–4; James 5:13"> And Ezekias prayed to the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Psalms 99:1; Psalms 86:10"> O Lord of hosts, God of Israel, who sit upon the cherubs, you alone are the God of every kingdom of the world: you have made heaven and earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Chronicles II 6:40; Psalms 17:6; Esias 37:4"> Incline your ear, O Lord, listen, O Lord; open your eyes, O Lord, look, O Lord: and behold the words of Sennacherim, which he has sent to reproach the living God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Naum 2:11–12; Kings IV 15:29; Kings IV 17:6"> For of a truth, Lord, the kings of the Assyrians have laid waste the whole world, and the countries thereof,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Esias 44:9–10; Jeremias 10:11; Osee 8:6"> and have cast their idols into the fire: for, they were no gods, but the work of men's hands, wood and stone; and they have cast them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:10; Kings III 18:36–37; Psalms 83:17–18"> But now, O Lord our God, deliver us from his hands, that every kingdom of the earth may know that you are God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esias 58:9; Esias 38:3–6; Acts 4:31"> And Esaias the son of Amos was sent to Ezekias, and said to him, Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel, I have heard your prayer to me concerning Sennacherim king of the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 16:4; Lamentations 2:13; Esias 1:8"> This is the word which God has spoken concerning him; The virgin daughter of Sion has despised you, and mocked you; the daughter of Jerusalem has shaken her head at you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jezekiel 39:7; Daniel 5:20–23; Esias 17:7"> Whom have you reproached and provoked? and against whom have you lifted up your voice? and have you not lifted up your eyes on high against the Holy One of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esias 14:8; Esias 10:18; Exodus 15:9"> For you have reproached the Lord by messengers; for you have said, With the multitude of chariots have I ascended to the height of mountains, and to the sides of Libanus; and I have<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. cut">b</a></sup>cropped the height of his cedars and the beauty of his cypresses; and I entered into the height of the forest region:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings III 20:10; Esias 36:12; Kings IV 19:23–24"> and I have made a bridge, and dried up the waters, and every pool of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:27–28; Acts 2:23; 1 Peter 2:8"> Have you not heard of these things which I did of old? I appointed them from ancient times; but now have I manifested my purpose of desolating nations in their strong holds, and them that dwell in strong cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 129:6; Psalms 37:2; 1 Peter 1:24"> I weakened their hands, and they withered; and they became as dry grass on the housetops, and as grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:13; Psalms 139:1–11; Jeremias 23:23–24"> But now I know your rest, and your going out, and your coming in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jezekiel 38:4; Esias 30:28; Esias 37:34"> And your wrath wherewith you have been enraged, and your rancour has come up to me; therefore I will put a hook in your nose, and a bit in your lips, and will turn you back by the way by which you came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Kings IV 19:29; Leviticus 25:4–5; Kings III 13:3–5"> And this shall be a sign to you, Eat this year what you have sown; and the second year that which is left: and the third year sow, and reap, and plant vineyards, and eat the fruit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Esias 27:6; Kings IV 19:30–31; Psalms 80:9"> And they that are left in Judea shall take root downward, and bear fruit upward:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Esias 9:7; Kings IV 19:31; Esias 59:17"> for out of Jerusalem there shall be<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Lit. the left men">c</a></sup>a remnant, and the saved ones out of mount Sion: the zeal of the Lord of hosts shall perform this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Esias 17:12; Esias 10:32–34; Esias 17:14"> Therefore thus says the Lord concerning the king of the Assyrians, He shall not enter into this city, nor cast a weapon against it, nor bring a shield against it, nor make a rampart round it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Esias 37:29; Proverbs 21:30"> But by the way by which he came, by it shall he return, and shall not enter into this city: thus says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Kings IV 20:6; Esias 38:6; Esias 31:5"> I will protect this city to save it for my own sake, and for my servant David's sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Esias 10:12; Kings IV 19:35; Esias 10:33–34"> And the angel of the Lord went forth, and killed out of the camp of the Assyrians one hundred and eighty-five thousand: and they arose in the morning and found all these bodies dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jonas 1:2; Jonas 3:3; Genesis 10:11–12"> And Sennacherim king of the Assyrians turned and departed, and lived in Nineve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 8:4; Jeremias 51:27; Ezra 4:2"> And while he was worshipping Nasarach his country's god in the house, Adramelech and Sarasar his sons struck him with swords; and they escaped into Armenia: and Asordan his son reigned in his stead.</span>
