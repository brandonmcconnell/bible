<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremias 32:17; Esias 50:2; Numbers 11:23"> Has the hand of the Lord no power to save? or has he made his ear heavy, so that he should not hear?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremias 5:25; Proverbs 15:29; Michaeas 3:4"> Nay, your iniquities separate between you and God, and because of your sins has he turned away his face from you, so as not to have mercy upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Esias 1:15; Jezekiel 13:8; Jeremias 2:34"> For your hands are defiled with blood, and your fingers with sins; your lips also have spoken iniquity, and your tongue meditates unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Job 15:35; James 1:15; Michaeas 7:2–5"> None speaks justly, neither is there true judgment: they trust in vanities, and speak empty words; for they conceive trouble, and bring forth iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:7; Esias 14:29; Proverbs 23:32"> They have hatched asps' eggs, and weave a spider's web: and he that is going to eat of their eggs, having crushed an addled egg, has found also in it a basilisk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esias 57:12; Revelation 3:17–18; Jeremias 6:7"> Their web shall not become a garment, nor shall they at all clothe themselves with their works; for their works are works of iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:15–17; Proverbs 6:17; Mark 7:21–22"> And their feet run to wickedness, swift to shed blood; their thoughts also are thoughts<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. from murders, but Alex. reads ὐφρόνων.">a</a></sup>of murder; destruction and misery are in their ways;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esias 57:20–21; Romans 3:17; Luke 1:79"> and the way of peace they know not, neither is there judgment in their ways; for their paths by which they go are crooked, and they know not peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Esias 5:30; Job 30:26; Lamentations 5:16–17"> Therefore has judgment departed from them, and righteousness shall not overtake them: while they waited for light, darkness came upon them; while they waited for brightness, they walked in perplexity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:29; Job 5:14; Lamentations 3:6"> They shall feel for the wall as blind men, and shall feel for it as if they had no eyes: and they shall feel at noon-day as at midnight; they shall groan as dying men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 38:14; Jezekiel 7:16; Jeremias 9:1"> They shall proceed together as a bear and as a dove: we have waited for judgment, and there is no salvation, it is gone far from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; Osee 5:5; Jeremias 14:7"> For our iniquity is great before you, and our sins have risen up against us: for our iniquities are in us, and we know our unrighteous deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; James 3:6; Hebrews 3:12"> We have sinned, and dealt falsely, and revolted from our God: we have spoken unrighteous words, and have been disobedient; we have conceived and uttered from our heart unrighteous words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ambacum 1:4; Ecclesiastes 3:16; Jeremias 5:31"> And we have turned judgment back, and righteousness has departed afar off: for truth is consumed in their ways, and they could not pass by a straight path.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Michaeas 7:2; Jeremias 7:28; 1 John 3:11–12"> And truth has been taken away, and they have turned aside their mind from understanding. And the Lord saw it, and it pleased him not that there was no judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jezekiel 22:30; Psalms 106:23; Esias 64:7"> And he looked, and there was no man, and he observed, and there was none to help: so he defended them with his arm, and established them with his mercy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ephesians 6:17; Ephesians 6:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:8"> And he put on righteousness as a breast-plate, and placed the helmet of salvation on his head; and he clothed himself with the garment of vengeance, and with his cloak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremias 17:10; Esias 63:6; Romans 2:6"> as one about to render a recompence, even reproach to his adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Psalms 113:3; Zacharias 4:6"> So shall they of the west fear the name of the Lord, and they that come from the rising of the sun his glorious name: for the wrath of the Lord shall come as a mighty river, it shall come with fury.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 11:26–27; Titus 2:11–14; Acts 3:19"> And the deliverer shall come for Sion's sake, and shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremias 31:31–34; Hebrews 10:16; Jezekiel 36:25–27"> And this shall be my covenant with them, said the Lord; My Spirit which is upon you, and the words which I have put in your mouth, shall never fail from your mouth, nor from the mouth of your seed, for the Lord has spoken it, henceforth and for ever.</span>
