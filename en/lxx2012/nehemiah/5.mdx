<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Esias 5:7; Exodus 22:25–27; 1 Corinthians 6:6–8"> And the cry of the people and their wives was great against their brethren the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachias 2:2; Psalms 128:2–4; Psalms 127:3–5"> And some said, We are numerous with our sons and our daughters; so we will take corn, and eat, and live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 25:35–39; Malachias 3:8–11; Genesis 47:15–25"> And some said, As to our fields and vineyards and houses, let us pledge them, and we will take corn, and eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezra 4:13; Ezra 4:20; Deuteronomy 28:47–48"> And some said, We have borrowed money for the king's tributes:—our fields, and our vineyards, and houses are pledged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings IV 4:1; Leviticus 25:39–43; Esias 58:7"> And now our flesh is as the flesh of our brethren, our children are as their children: yet, behold, we are reducing our sons and our daughters to slavery, and some of our daughters are enslaved: and there is no power of our hands, for our fields and our vineyards belong to the nobles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 11:8; Nehemiah 13:25; Ephesians 4:26"> And I was much grieved as I heard their cry and these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:19–20; Leviticus 25:36; Exodus 22:25"> And my heart took counsel within me, and I contended against the nobles, and the princes, and I said to them, Should every man demand of his brother what you⌃ demand? And I appointed against them a great assembly,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 25:47–49; 1 Corinthians 8:11; Matthew 25:29"> and I said to them, We of our free-will have redeemed our brethren the Jews that were sold to the Gentiles; and do you⌃ sell your brethren? and shall they be delivered to us? And they were silent, and found no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 25:36; Kings II 12:14; Romans 2:24"> And I said, The thing which you⌃ do is not good; you⌃ will not so walk in the fear of our God because of the reproach of the Gentiles our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:1; Exodus 22:25–27; Jezekiel 18:13"> Both my brethren, and my acquaintances, and I, have lent them money and corn: let us now leave off this exaction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 58:6; Leviticus 6:4–5; Luke 3:8"> Restore to them, I pray, as at this day, their fields, and their vineyards, and their olive yards, and their houses, and bring forth to them corn and wine and oil of the money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezra 10:5; Jeremias 34:8–10; Matthew 19:21–22"> And they said, We will restore, and we will not exact of them; we will do thus as you say. Then I called the priests, and bound them by oath to do according to this word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 18:6; Chronicles I 16:36; Matthew 10:14"> And I shook out my garment, and said, So may God shake out every man who shall not keep to this word, from his house, and from his labors, he shall be even thus shaken out, as an outcast and empty. And all the congregation said, Amen, and they praised the Lord: and the people did this thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:6; 2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Corinthians 9:4–15"> From the day that he charged me to be their ruler in the land of Juda, from the twentieth year even to the thirty-second year of Arthasastha, twelve years, I and my brethren ate not provision extorted from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:9; Esias 50:10; Proverbs 16:6"> But as for the former acts of extortion wherein those who were before me oppressed them, they even took of them their last money, forty didrachmas for bread and wine; and the very outcasts of them exercised authority over the people: but I did not so, because of the fear of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:5–6; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Also in the work of the wall I treated them not with rigor, I bought not land: and all that were gathered together came there to the work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings III 18:19; 1 Peter 4:9–10; Kings II 9:7"> And the Jews, to the number of one hundred and fifty men, besides those coming to us from the nations round about, were at my table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Kings III 4:22–23; Psalms 37:21; Psalms 37:26"> And there came to me for one day one calf, and I had six choice sheep and a goat; and every ten days wine in abundance of all sorts: yet with these I required not the bread of extortion, because the bondage was heavy upon this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:31; Psalms 106:4; Nehemiah 13:14"> Remember me, O God, for good, in all that I have done to this people.</span>
