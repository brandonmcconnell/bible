<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Jezekiel 20:11; Leviticus 18:5"> And now, Israel, hear the ordinances and judgments, all that I teach you this day to do: that you⌃ may live, and be multiplied, and that you⌃ may go in and inherit the land, which the Lord God of your fathers gives you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Proverbs 30:6; Revelation 22:18–19"> You⌃ shall not add to the word which I command you, and you⌃ shall not take from it: keep the commandments of the Lord our God, all that I command you this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; Psalms 106:28–29; Joshua 22:17"> Your eyes have seen all that the Lord our God did in the case of Beel-phegor; for every man that went after Beel-phegor, the Lord your God has utterly destroyed him from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 23:8; John 6:67–69"> But you⌃ that kept close to the Lord your God are all alive today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2; Hebrews 3:5"> Behold, I have shown you ordinances and judgments as the Lord commanded me, that you⌃ should do so in the land into which you⌃ go to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Job 28:28; Psalms 111:10; 2 Timothy 3:15"> And you⌃ shall keep and do them: for this is your wisdom and understanding before all nations, as many as shall hear all these ordinances; and they shall say, Behold, this great nation is a wise and understanding people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Kings II 7:23; Psalms 34:18"> For what manner of nation is so great, which has God so near to them as the Lord our God is in all things in whatever we may call upon him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 147:19–20; Romans 7:12–14; Psalms 119:127–128"> And what manner of nation is so great, which has righteous ordinances and judgments according to all this law, which I set before you this day?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 11:19; Psalms 78:3–8"> Take heed to yourself, and keep your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. soul.">a</a></sup>heart diligently: forget not any of the things, which your eyes have seen, and let them not depart from your heart all the days of your life; and you shall teach your sons and your sons' sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Hebrews 12:18–19; Exodus 19:16"> even the things that happened in the day in which you⌃ stood before the Lord our God in Choreb in the day of the assembly; for the Lord said to me, Gather the people to me, and let them hear my words, that they may learn to fear me all the days which they live upon the earth, and they shall teach their sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:23; Exodus 19:16–18; Hebrews 12:18–19"> And you⌃ drew near and stood under the mountain; and the mountain burned with fire up to heaven: there was darkness, blackness, and tempest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:15; Deuteronomy 5:4; Deuteronomy 5:22"> And the Lord spoke to you out of the midst of the fire a voice of words, which you⌃ heard: and you⌃ saw no likeness, only you⌃ heard a voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 31:18; Deuteronomy 9:9–11"> And he announced to you his covenant, which he commanded you to keep, even the ten<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. words or sayings.">b</a></sup>commandments; and he wrote them on two tables of stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 105:44–45; Jezekiel 21:1–23"> And the Lord commanded me at that time, to teach you ordinances and judgments, that you⌃ should do them on the land, into which you⌃ go to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:12; Esias 40:18; 2 Corinthians 4:4–6"> And take good heed to your hearts, for you⌃ saw no similitude in the day in which the Lord spoke to you in Choreb in the mountain out of the midst of the fire:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:29; Deuteronomy 5:8; Exodus 20:4–5"> lest you⌃ transgress, and make to yourselves a carved image, any kind of figure, the likeness of male or female,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:23"> the likeness of any beast of those that are on the earth, the likeness of any winged bird which flies under heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> the likeness of any reptile which creeps on the earth, the likeness of any fish of those which are in the waters under the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 1:25; Deuteronomy 17:3; Kings IV 21:3"> and lest having looked up to the sky, and having seen the sun and the moon and the stars, and all the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. order of heaven.">c</a></sup>heavenly bodies, you should go astray and worship them, and serve them, which the Lord your God has distributed to all the nations under heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Kings III 8:51; Jeremias 11:4; Exodus 19:5–6"> But God took you, and led you forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be to him a people of inheritance, as at this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 20:12; Psalms 106:32–33"> And the Lord God was angry with me for the things said by you, and swore that I should not go over this Jordan, and that I should not enter into the land, which the Lord your God gives you for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:25; Deuteronomy 3:27; 2 Peter 1:13–15"> For<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Gr. I die.">d</a></sup>I am to die in this land, and shall not pass over this Jordan; but you⌃ are to pass over, and shall inherit this good land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:9; Hebrews 3:12; Joshua 23:16"> Take heed to yourselves, lest you⌃ forget the covenant of the Lord our God, which he made with you, and you⌃ transgress, and make to yourselves a graven image of any of the things concerning which the Lord your God commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:29; Naum 1:6; Deuteronomy 9:3"> For the Lord your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:16; Osee 9:9; 1 Corinthians 10:22"> And when you shall have begotten sons, and shall have sons' sons, and you⌃ shall have lived a long time on the land, and shall have transgressed, and made a graven image of any thing, and shall have done wickedly before the Lord your God to provoke him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Michaeas 6:2; Jeremias 22:29"> I call heaven and earth this day to witness against you, that you⌃ shall surely perish from off the land, into which you⌃ go across Jordan to inherit it there; you⌃ shall not prolong your days upon it, but shall be utterly cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:62–64; Leviticus 26:33; Jezekiel 12:15"> And the Lord shall scatter you among all nations, and you⌃ shall be left few in number among all the nations, among which the Lord shall bring you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Jeremias 16:13; Deuteronomy 28:36"> And you⌃ shall there serve other gods, the works of the hands of men, wood and stones, which<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Gr. shall not see, etc.">e</a></sup>can’t see, nor can they hear, nor eat, nor smell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Psalms 119:2; Jeremias 29:12–14; Chronicles II 15:4"> And there you⌃ shall seek the Lord your God, and you⌃ shall find him whenever you⌃ shall seek him with all your heart, and with all<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Gr. your.">f</a></sup>your soul in your affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joel 2:12–13; Jeremias 23:20; Osee 3:5"> And<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Gr. all these words shall find you. Hebraism.">g</a></sup>all these things shall come upon you in the last days, and you shall turn to the Lord your God, and shall listen to his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:8; Psalms 116:5; Nehemiah 9:31"> Because the Lord your God is a God of pity: he will not forsake you, nor destroy you; he will not forget the covenant of your fathers, which the Lord swore to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Job 8:8; Matthew 24:31; Psalms 44:1"> Ask of the former days which were before you, from the day when God created man upon the earth, and beginning at the one end of heaven to the other end of heaven, if there has happened any thing like to this great event, if such a thing has been heard:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Judges 6:22; Deuteronomy 5:24; Exodus 33:20"> if a nation have heard the voice of the living God speaking out of the midst of the fire, as you have heard and have lived;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 26:8; Deuteronomy 5:15; Exodus 6:6"> if God has assayed to go and take to himself a nation out of the midst of another nation with trial, and with signs, and with wonders, and with war, and with a mighty hand, and with a high arm, and with great sights, according to all the things which the Lord our God did in Egypt<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Gr. before you seeing.">h</a></sup>in your sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Esias 45:18; Kings I 2:2; Esias 45:5"> So that you should know that the Lord your God he is God, and there is none beside him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 19:9; Exodus 19:19; Deuteronomy 4:33"> His voice was made audible from heaven to instruct you, and he showed you upon the earth his great fire, and you heard his words out of the midst of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:15; Esias 63:9; Exodus 13:9"> Because he loved your fathers, he also chose you their seed after them, and he brought you himself with his great strength out of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:1; Deuteronomy 9:1–5; Exodus 23:27–28"> to destroy nations<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-i" id="fnref-i" data-fn="Or, greater.">i</a></sup>great and stronger than you before your face, to bring you in, to give you their land to inherit, as you have it this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Joshua 2:11; Deuteronomy 4:35; Chronicles I 29:11"> And you shall know this day, and shall consider in your heart, that the Lord your God he is God in heaven above, and on the earth beneath, and there is none else but he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:3; Ephesians 6:3; Deuteronomy 12:28"> And keep you⌃ his commandments, and his ordinances, all that I command you this day; that it may be well with you, and with your sons after you, that you⌃ may be long-lived upon the earth, which the Lord your God gives you for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 35:6; Numbers 35:14–15; Joshua 20:2–9"> Then Moses separated three cities beyond Jordan on the east,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 6:18; Deuteronomy 19:1–10; Numbers 35:15–28"> that the slayer might flee there, who should have slain his neighbor unintentionally, and should not have hated him<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-j" id="fnref-j" data-fn="Gr. before yesterday and the third day. Hebraism.">j</a></sup>in times past, and he shall flee to one of these cities and live:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Joshua 20:8; Kings III 4:13; Joshua 21:27"> Bosor in the wilderness, in the plain country of Ruben, and Ramoth in Galaad belonging to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-k" id="fnref-k" data-fn="Or, the Gaddite">k</a></sup>Gad, and Gaulon in Basan belonging to Manasse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:3; Leviticus 27:34; Malachias 4:4"> This is the law which Moses set before the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Kings III 2:3; Deuteronomy 4:1; Psalms 119:7"> These are the testimonies, and the ordinances, and the judgments, which Moses spoke to the sons of Israel, when they came out of the land of Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:29; Deuteronomy 4:47; Deuteronomy 1:4–5"> on<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-l" id="fnref-l" data-fn="i. e. the east side.">l</a></sup>the other side of Jordan, in the valley near the house of Phogor, in the land of Seon king of the Amorites, who lived in Esebon, whom Moses and the sons of Israel struck when they came out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:1–14; Numbers 21:33–35; Deuteronomy 29:7–8"> And they inherited his land, and the land of Og king of Basan, two kings of the Amorites, who were beyond Jordan eastward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Deuteronomy 2:36; Deuteronomy 3:12"> From Aroer, which is on the border of the brook Arnon, even to the mount of Seon, which is Aermon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 13:20; Deuteronomy 34:1"> All<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-m" id="fnref-m" data-fn="Heb. the plain.">m</a></sup>Araba beyond Jordan eastward under Asedoth<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-n" id="fnref-n" data-fn="Or, the quarried rock. Heb. Ashdoth Pisgah.">n</a></sup>hewn in the rock.</span>
