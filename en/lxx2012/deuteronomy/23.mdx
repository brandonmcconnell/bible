<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Esias 56:3–4; Leviticus 21:17–21"> He that is fractured or mutilated in his private parts shall not enter into the assembly of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Zacharias 9:6; Esias 57:3; Hebrews 12:8"> One born of a harlot shall not enter into the assembly of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23; Ruth 4:6"> The Ammanite and Moabite shall not enter into the assembly of the Lord, even until the tenth generation he shall not enter into the assembly of the Lord, even for ever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; 2 Peter 2:15; Kings III 18:4"> because they met you not with bread and water by the way, when you⌃ went out of Egypt; and because they hired against you Balaam the son of Beor of Mesopotamia to curse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 8:31; Ephesians 2:4–5"> But the Lord your God would not listen to Balaam; and the Lord your God changed the curses into blessings, because the Lord your God loved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:12; Kings II 12:31; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> You shall not speak peaceably or profitably to them all your days for ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:19; Exodus 22:21; Exodus 23:9"> You shall not abhor an Edomite, because he is your brother; you shall not abhor an Egyptian, because you were a stranger in his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:5–6; Ephesians 2:12–13; Deuteronomy 23:1–3"> If sons be born to them, in the third generation they shall enter into the assembly of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; Judges 20:26; Revelation 19:11–14"> And if you should go forth to engage with your enemies, then you shall keep you from every wicked thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:16; Numbers 5:2–3; 1 Corinthians 5:11–13"> If there should be in you a man who is not clean by reason of his issue by night, then he shall go forth out of the camp, and he shall not enter into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Ephesians 5:26–27; Leviticus 14:9"> And it shall come to pass toward evening he shall wash his body with water, and when the sun has gone down, he shall go into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And you shall have a place outside of the camp, and you shall go out there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jezekiel 24:6–8"> and you shall have a trowel on your girdle; and it shall come to pass when you would relieve yourself abroad, that you shall dig with it, and shall bring back the earth and cover your nuisance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> Because the Lord your God walks in your camp to deliver you, and to give up your enemy before your face; and your camp shall be holy, and there shall not appear in you a disgraceful thing, and so he<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e. would if you wort disobedient.">a</a></sup>shall turn away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Kings I 30:15; Obdias 1:14"> You shall not deliver a servant to his master, who coming from his master attaches himself to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Zacharias 7:10; Jeremias 7:6"> He shall dwell with you, he shall dwell among you where he shall please; you shall not afflict him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Kings IV 23:7; Kings III 14:24; Leviticus 19:29"> There shall not be a harlot of the daughters of Israel, and there shall not be a fornicator of the sons of Israel; there shall not be an<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Or, sodomitess or harlot.">b</a></sup>idolatress of the daughters of Israel, and there shall not be an<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Or, sodomite.">c</a></sup>initiated person of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 3:2; Matthew 7:6; Revelation 22:15"> You shall not bring the hire of a harlot, nor the price of a dog into the house of the Lord your God, for any vow; because even both are an abomination to the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:35–37; Psalms 15:5"> You shall not lend to your brother on usury of silver, or usury of meat, or usury of any thing which you may lend out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 15:3; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> You may lend on usury to a stranger, but to your brother you shall not lend on usury; that the Lord your God may bless you in all your works upon the land, into which you are entering to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–5; Psalms 66:13–14; Psalms 116:18"> And if you will vow a vow to the Lord your God, you shall not delay to pay it; for the Lord your God will surely require it of you, and otherwise it shall be sin in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if you should be unwilling to vow, it is not sin in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:25; Judges 11:30–31; Kings I 1:11"> You shall observe the words that proceed from between your lips; and as you have vowed a gift to the Lord God, so shall you do that which you have spoken with your mouth.</span>
