<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Moses finished speaking all these words to all the children of Israel;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:7; Numbers 27:17; Kings III 3:7"> and said to them, I am this day one hundred and twenty years old; I shall not be able any longer to come in or go out; and the Lord said to me, You shall not go over this Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:3; Deuteronomy 31:23; Deuteronomy 3:28"> The Lord your God who goes before you, he shall destroy these nations before you, and you shall inherit them: and it shall be Joshua that goes before your face, as the Lord has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 23:28–31; Deuteronomy 2:33; Deuteronomy 3:3–11"> And the Lord your God shall do to them as he did to Seon and Og the two kings of the Amorites, who were beyond Jordan, and to their land, as he destroyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:2; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Exodus 23:32–33"> And the Lord has delivered<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="i.e. the nations.">a</a></sup>them to you; and you⌃ shall do to them, as I charged you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Ephesians 6:10; Psalms 27:14"> Be courageous and strong, fear not, neither be cowardly neither be afraid before them; for it is the Lord your God that advances with you in the midst of you, neither will he by any means forsake you, nor desert you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Deuteronomy 31:6; Deuteronomy 1:38"> And Moses called Joshua, and said to him before all Israel, Be courageous and strong; for you shall go in before this people into the land which the Lord swore to your fathers to give to them, and you shall give it to them for an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:14; Deuteronomy 31:6; Joshua 1:9"> And the Lord that goes with you shall not forsake you nor abandon you; fear not, neither be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 4:15; Joshua 3:3; Deuteronomy 17:18"> And Moses wrote the words of this law in a book, and gave it to the priests the sons of Levi who bear the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and to the elders of the sons of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 23:34–43; Deuteronomy 15:1–2"> And Moses charged them in that day, saying, After seven years, in the time of the year of release, in the feast of tabernacles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Kings IV 23:2; Joshua 8:34–35; Deuteronomy 16:16–17"> when all Israel come together to appear before the Lord your God, in the place which the Lord shall choose, you⌃ shall read this law before all Israel in their ears,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:10; Psalms 34:11–14; Deuteronomy 6:6–7"> having assembled the people, the men, and the women, and the children, and the stranger that is in your cities, that they may hear, and that they may learn to fear the Lord your God; and they shall listen to do all the words of this law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:2; Deuteronomy 6:7; Proverbs 22:6"> And their sons who have not known shall hear, and shall learn to fear the Lord your God all the days that they live upon the land, into which you⌃ go over Jordan to inherit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Deuteronomy 31:23; Job 2:1"> And the Lord said to Moses, Behold, the days of your death are at hand; call Joshua, and stand you⌃ by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony, and I will give him a charge. And Moses and Joshua went to the tabernacle of testimony, and stood by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 40:38; Psalms 99:7; Exodus 33:9–10"> And the Lord descended in a cloud, and stood by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony; and the pillar of the cloud stood by the doors of the tabernacle of testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Judges 10:13; Judges 2:12"> And the Lord said to Moses, Behold, you shall sleep with your fathers, and this people will arise and go a whoring after the strange gods of the land, into which they are entering: and they will forsake me, and break my covenant, which I made with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 14:42; Esias 8:17; Psalms 104:29"> And I will be very angry with them in that day, and I will leave them and turn my face away from them, and they shall be devoured; and many evils and afflictions shall come upon them; and they shall say in that day, Because the Lord my God is not with me, these evils have come upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16–17"> And I will surely turn away my face from them in that day, because of all their evil doings which they have done, because they turned aside after strange gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Deuteronomy 31:21–22; Deuteronomy 31:26"> And now write the words of this song, and teach it to the children of Israel, and you⌃ shall put it into their mouth, that this song may witness for me among the children of Israel to their face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Osee 13:6; Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Nehemiah 9:25–26"> For I will bring them into the good land, which I swore to their fathers, to give to them a land flowing with milk and honey: and they shall eat and be filled and satisfy themselves; then will they turn aside after other gods, and serve them, and they will provoke me, and break my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Osee 5:3; Psalms 139:2; John 2:24–25"> And this song shall stand up to witness against them; for they shall not forget it out of their mouth, or out of the mouth of their seed; for I know their wickedness, what they are doing here this day, before I have brought them into the good land, which I swore to their fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; Deuteronomy 31:9"> And Moses wrote this song in that day, and taught it to the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:14; Acts 7:45"> And he charged Joshua, and said, Be courageous and strong, for you shall bring the sons of Israel into the land, which the Lord swore to them, and he shall be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9; Deuteronomy 17:18"> And when Moses finished writing all the words of this law in a book, even to the end,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:9"> then he charged the Levites who bear the ark of the covenant of the Lord, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:19; Kings IV 22:8–11; Galatians 2:19"> Take the book of this law, and you⌃ shall put it in the side of the ark of the covenant of the Lord your God; and it shall be there<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. within you.">b</a></sup>among you for a testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:20; Deuteronomy 9:24; Acts 7:51"> For I know your provocation, and your stiff neck; for yet during my life with you at this day, you⌃ have been provoking in your conduct toward God: how shall you⌃ not also be so after my death?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Deuteronomy 4:26; Deuteronomy 30:19"> Gather together to me the heads of your tribes, and your elders, and your judges, and your officers, that I may speak in their ears all these words; and I call both heaven and earth to witness against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:19; Deuteronomy 32:5; Luke 21:24"> For I know that after my death you⌃ will utterly transgress, and turn aside out of the way which I have commanded you; and evils shall come upon you<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. at the end of the days.">c</a></sup>in the latter days, because you⌃ will do evil before the Lord, to provoke him to anger by the works of your hands. And Moses spoke all the words of this song even to the end, in the ears of the whole assembly.</span>
