<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:31; Deuteronomy 4:38; Deuteronomy 1:28"> Hear, O Israel: You go this day across Jordan to inherit nations greater and stronger than yourselves, cities great and walled up to heaven;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Naum 1:6; Deuteronomy 7:24"> a people great and many and tall, the sons of Enac, whom you know, and concerning whom you have heard say, Who can stand before the children of Enac?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Hebrews 12:29; Romans 8:31"> And you shall know today, that the Lord your God he shall go before your face: he is a consuming fire; he shall destroy them, and he shall turn them back before you, and shall destroy them quickly, as the Lord said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; Deuteronomy 9:5; Deuteronomy 18:9–14"> Speak not in your heart, when the Lord your God has destroyed these nations before your face, saying, For my righteousness the Lord brought me in to inherit this good land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Titus 3:5; Genesis 17:8; Genesis 26:4"> Not for your righteousness, nor for the holiness of your heart, do you go in to inherit their land, but because of the wickedness of these nations the Lord will destroy them from before you, and that he may establish the covenant, which the Lord swore to our fathers, to Abraam, and to Isaac, and to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:16; Acts 7:51; Deuteronomy 31:27"> And you shall know today, that it is not for your righteousnesses the Lord your God gives you this good land to inherit, for you are a stiff-necked people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 16:2; Exodus 14:11; Numbers 21:5"> Remember, forget not, how much you provoked the Lord your God in the wilderness: from the day that you⌃ came forth out of Egypt, even till you⌃ came into this place, you⌃ continued to be disobedient toward the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:19–22; Exodus 32:16; Exodus 32:7–10"> Also in Choreb you⌃ provoked the Lord, and the Lord was angry with you to destroy you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Exodus 24:18; Kings III 19:8"> when I went up into the mountain to receive the tables of stone, the tables of the covenant, which the Lord made with you, and I was in the mountain forty days and forty nights, I ate no bread and drank no water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 31:18; Hebrews 8:10; Deuteronomy 10:4"> And the Lord gave me the two tables of stone written with the finger of God, and on them there had been written all the words which the Lord spoke to you in the mountain in the day of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:9; Numbers 10:33; Hebrews 8:6–10"> And it came to pass after forty days and forty nights, the Lord gave me the two tables of stone, the tables of the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:7–8; Deuteronomy 31:29; Judges 2:17"> And the Lord said to me, Arise, go down quickly from hence, for your people whom you brought out of the land of Egypt have transgressed; they have gone aside quickly out of the way which I commanded them, and have made themselves a molten image.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:6; Kings IV 17:14; Deuteronomy 10:16"> And the Lord spoke to me, saying, I have spoken to you once and again, saying, I have seen this people, and, behold, it is a stiff-necked people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Exodus 32:10–13; Psalms 9:5"> And now suffer me utterly to destroy them, and I will blot out their name from under heaven, and will make of you a nation great and strong, and more numerous than this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 19:18; Deuteronomy 5:23; Deuteronomy 4:11"> And I turned and went down from the mountain; and the mountain burned with fire to heaven; and the two tables of the testimonies were<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="Gr. on.">a</a></sup>in my two hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 32:19; Acts 7:40–41"> And when I saw that you⌃ had sinned against the Lord your God, and had made to yourselves a molten image, and had gone astray out of the way, which the Lord commanded you to<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-b" id="fnref-b" data-fn="Gr. to do.">b</a></sup>keep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> then I took hold of the two tables, and cast them out of my two hands, and broke them before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 34:28; Deuteronomy 9:9; Psalms 106:23"> And I made my petition before the Lord as also at the first forty days and forty nights: I ate no bread and drank no water, on account of all your sins which you⌃ sinned in doing evil before the Lord God to provoke him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:10; Exodus 32:14; James 5:16–17"> And I<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-c" id="fnref-c" data-fn="Gr. am.">c</a></sup>was greatly terrified because of the wrath and anger, because the Lord was provoked with you utterly to destroy you; yet the Lord listened to me at this time also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 32:35; Exodus 32:21; Hebrews 7:26–28"> And he was angry with Aaron to destroy him utterly, and I prayed for Aaron also at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; Esias 31:7; Esias 2:18–21"> And your sin which you⌃ had made, even the calf, I took, and burnt it with fire, and pounded it and ground it down till it became fine; and it became like dust, and I cast the dust into the brook that descended from the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 11:34; Exodus 17:7; Numbers 11:1–5"> Also in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-d" id="fnref-d" data-fn="Heb. Taberah, Massah, and Kibroth Hattavah.">d</a></sup>burning, and in the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-e" id="fnref-e" data-fn="Heb. Taberah, Massah, and Kibroth Hattavah.">e</a></sup>temptation, and at the<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-f" id="fnref-f" data-fn="Heb. Taberah, Massah, and Kibroth Hattavah.">f</a></sup>graves of lust, you⌃ provoked the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 106:24–25; Hebrews 4:2; Psalms 78:22"> And when the Lord sent you forth from Cades Barne, saying, Go up and inherit the land which I give to you, then you⌃ disobeyed the word of the Lord your God, and believed him not, and listened not to his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:27; Acts 7:51; Deuteronomy 9:6–7"> You⌃ were disobedient<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-g" id="fnref-g" data-fn="Or, towards the Lord.">g</a></sup>in the things relating to the Lord from the day in which he became known to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18; Deuteronomy 9:16"> And I prayed before the Lord forty days and forty nights, the number that I prayed before, for the Lord said that he would utterly destroy you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 32:11–13; Titus 2:14; Psalms 107:2"> And I prayed to God, and said, O Lord, King of gods, destroy not your people and your<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-h" id="fnref-h" data-fn="Or, portion or part.">h</a></sup>inheritance, whom you did redeem, whom you brought out of the land of Egypt with your great power, and with your strong hand, and with your high arm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremias 14:21; Michaeas 7:18–19; Kings I 25:25"> Remember Abraam, and Isaac, and Jacob your servants, to whom you swore by yourself: look not upon the hardness of heart of this people, and their impieties, and their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 14:16; Exodus 32:12"> Lest the inhabitants of the land whence you brought us out speak, saying, Because the Lord could not bring them into the land of which he spoke to them, and because he hated them, has he brought them forth to kill them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:10; Kings III 8:51; Deuteronomy 9:26"> And these are your people and your portion, whom you brought out of the land of Egypt with your great strength, and with your mighty hand, and with your high arm.</span>
