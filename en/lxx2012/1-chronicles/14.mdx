<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Chronicles II 2:3; Kings II 5:11–16; Ezra 3:7"> And Chiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, and cedar timbers, and masons, and carpenters, to build a house for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:7; Psalms 89:20–37; Kings II 7:16"> And David knew that the Lord had designed him to be king over Israel; because his kingdom was highly exalted, on account of his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; Kings II 5:13; Ecclesiastes 7:26–29"> And David took more wives in Jerusalem: and there were born to David more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:5–9; Kings II 5:14; Luke 3:31"> And these are the names of those that were born, who were born to him in Jerusalem; Samaa, Sobab, Nathan, and Solomon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 5:15; Chronicles I 3:6"> and Baar, and Elisa, and Eliphaleth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and Nageth, and Naphath, and Japhie,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Chronicles I 3:8; Kings II 5:16"> and Elisamae, and Eliade, and Eliphala.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings I 21:11; Revelation 11:15–18; Psalms 2:1–6"> And the Philistines heard that David was anointed king over all Israel: and all the Philistines went up to seek David; and David heard it, and went out to meet them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Chronicles I 11:15; Chronicles I 14:13; Kings II 23:13"> And the Philistines came and assembled together in the giants' valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:6; Chronicles I 14:14; Chronicles I 13:3"> And David enquired of God, saying, Shall I go up against the Philistines? and will you deliver them into my hand? And the Lord said to him, Go up, and I will deliver them into your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Esias 28:21; Exodus 14:28; Kings II 5:20"> And he went up to Baal Pharasin, and David struck them there; and David said, God has broken through enemies by my hand like a breach of water: therefore he called the name of that place, the Breach of Pharasin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; Kings I 5:2–6; Kings IV 19:18"> And the Philistines left their gods there; and David gave orders to burn them with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Chronicles I 14:9; Kings III 20:22; Kings II 5:22–25"> And the Philistines once more assembled themselves in the giants' valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 9:6–7; Chronicles I 14:10; Psalms 27:4"> And David enquired of God again; and God said to him, You shall not go after them; turn away from them, and you shall come upon them near the pear trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Esias 13:4; Acts 2:2; Philippians 2:12–13"> And it shall be, when you shall hear the sound of their tumult in the tops of the pear trees, then you shall go into the battle: for God has gone out before you to strike the army of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:14; John 13:17; Genesis 6:22"> And he did as God commanded him: and he struck the army of the Philistines from Gabaon to Gazera.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 6:27; Exodus 15:14–16"> And the name of David was famous in all the land; and the Lord put the terror of him on all the nations.</span>
