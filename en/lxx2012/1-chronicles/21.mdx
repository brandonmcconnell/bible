<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Kings II 24:1–25; Zacharias 3:1; Job 2:4–6"> And the devil stood up against Israel, and moved David to number Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Chronicles I 27:23–24; Kings II 3:10; Kings II 24:15"> And king David said to Joab and to the captains of the forces, Go, number Israel from Bersabee even to Dan, and bring me the account, and I shall know their number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:11; Esias 26:15; Chronicles I 19:13"> And Joab said, May the Lord add to his people, one hundred-fold as many as they are, and let the eyes of my lord the king see it: all are the servants of my lord. Why does my lord seek this thing? do it not, lest it become a sin to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; Daniel 3:18"> Nevertheless the king's word prevailed against Joab; and Joab went out and passed through all Israel, and came to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Kings II 24:9; Chronicles I 27:23"> And Joab gave the number of the mustering of the people to David: and all Israel was a million and one hundred thousand men that drew sword: and the sons of Juda were four hundred and seventy thousand men that drew sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Chronicles I 27:24; Numbers 1:47–49; Kings II 20:9–10"> But he numbered not Levi and Benjamin among them; for the word of the king was painful to Joab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Kings III 15:5; Joshua 7:5; Kings II 21:14"> And there was evil in the sight of the Lord respecting this thing; and he struck Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Kings II 12:13; 1 John 1:9; Genesis 34:7"> And David said to God, I have sinned exceedingly, in that I have done this thing: and now, I pray you, remove the sin of your servant; for I have been exceedingly foolish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Kings I 9:9; Kings II 24:11; Chronicles I 29:29"> And the Lord spoke to Gad the seer, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Kings III 13:21–22; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 1:29–31"> Go and speak to David, saying, Thus says the Lord, I bring three things upon you: choose one of them for yourself, and I will do it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 19:20"> And Gad came to David, and said to him, Thus says the Lord, Choose for yourself,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:27; Acts 12:23; Jezekiel 14:19–21"> either three years of famine, or that you should flee three months from the face of your enemies, and the sword of your enemies shall be employed to destroy you, or that the sword of the Lord and pestilence should be three days in the land, and the angel of the Lord shall be destroying in all the inheritance of Israel. And now consider what I shall answer to him that sent the message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 130:7; Esias 55:7; Philippians 1:23"> And David said to Gad, They are very hard for me, even all the three: let me fall now into the hands of the Lord, for his mercies are very abundant, and let me not fall by any means into the hands of man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 12:30; Chronicles I 27:24; Kings IV 19:35"> So the Lord brought pestilence upon Israel: and there fell of Israel seventy thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 6:6; Exodus 32:14; Jeremias 26:18"> And God sent an angel to Jerusalem to destroy it: and as he was destroying, the Lord saw, and repented for the evil, and said to the angel that was destroying, Let it suffice you; withhold your hand. And the angel of the Lord stood by the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Kings III 21:27; Kings IV 6:17"> And David lifted up his eyes, and saw the angel of the Lord, standing between the earth and the heaven, and his sword drawn in his hand, stretched out over Jerusalem: and David and the elders clothed in sackcloth, fell upon their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jezekiel 16:63; Kings II 24:17; John 10:11–12"> And David said to God, Was it not I that gave orders to number the people? and I am the guilty one; I have greatly sinned: but these sheep, what have they done? O Lord God, let your hand be upon me, and upon my father's house, and not on your people for destruction, O Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Chronicles II 3:1; Kings II 24:18; Chronicles I 21:11"> And the angel of the Lord told Gad to tell David, that he should go up to erect an altar to the Lord, in the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 9:6; John 2:5; Kings IV 5:10–14"> And David went up according to the word of Gad, which he spoke in the name of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 6:11"> And Orna turned and saw the king; and he hid himself and his four sons with him. Now Orna was threshing wheat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Kings I 25:23; Kings II 24:18–20"> And David came to Orna; and Orna came forth from the threshing floor, and did obeisance to David with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Kings II 24:21; Kings III 21:2; Numbers 25:8"> And David said to Orna, Give me your place of the threshing floor, and I will build upon it an altar to the Lord: give it me for its worth in money, and the plague shall cease from among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Kings II 24:22–23; Kings I 6:14; Genesis 23:4–6"> And Orna said to David, Take it to yourself, and let my lord the king do what is right in his eyes: see, I have given the calves for a whole burnt offering, and the plow for wood, and the corn for a meat-offering; I have given all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:16–17; Genesis 14:23; Genesis 23:13"> And king David said to Orna, Nay; for I will surely buy it for its worth in money: for I will not take your property for the Lord, to offer a whole burnt offering to the Lord without cost to myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Kings II 24:24–25"> And David gave to Orna for his place six hundred shekels of gold by weight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 9:24; Judges 6:21; Proverbs 15:8"> And David built there an altar to the Lord, and offered up whole burnt offerings and peace-offerings: and he cried to the Lord, and he answered him by fire out of heaven on the altar of whole burnt offerings, and it consumed the whole burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 18:11; Psalms 103:20; Chronicles I 21:15–16"> And the Lord spoke to the angel; and he put up the sword into its sheath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> At that time when David saw that the Lord answered him in the threshing floor of Orna the Jebusite, he also sacrificed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Chronicles I 16:39; Chronicles II 1:3; Chronicles II 1:13"> And the tabernacle of the Lord which Moses made in the wilderness, and the altar of whole burnt offerings, were at that time in the high place at Gabaon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Hebrews 12:28–29; Revelation 15:4"> And David could not go before it to enquire of God; for he hasted not because of the sword of the angel of the Lord.</span>
