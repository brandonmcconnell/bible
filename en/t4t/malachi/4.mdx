## The coming time of punishment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 1:8; 2 PETER 3:7; MALACHI 3:2"> This is also what the Commander of the armies of angels says: “There will be a time that I will judge and punish people. When that happens, it will be like &#91;SIM&#93; a very hot furnace. At that time, all the proud and wicked people will be burned up like &#91;MET&#93; stubble burns. They will be burned up completely, like roots and branches and everything else on a tree burns completely in a very hot fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:6; PSALMS 84:11; Isaiah 30:26"> But as for you who revere me &#91;MTY&#93;, the one who will save you will come to you like &#91;MET&#93; the sun shines in the morning, and enable you to become righteous (OR, do what is righteous); he will restore you and protect you like a bird protects its chicks &#91;MET&#93; under its wings. When he comes, you will be very joyful, like &#91;SIM&#93; calves that go out from a barn into the pasture leaping joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 40:12; ZECHARIAH 10:5; ROMANS 16:20"> At the time when I judge people, you will tread on wicked people as though &#91;SIM&#93; they were the dirt under your feet. That is what I, the Commander of the armies of angels, promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:10; JAMES 2:9–13; LUKE 16:29–31"> Be sure to obey the laws that I gave to Moses, who served me well. Obey all the commandments and regulations that I gave him on Sinai Mountain, for all you people of Israel to obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 1:17; MATTHEW 17:10–13; MARK 9:11–13"> Listen to this: Some day I will send to you the prophet Elijah. He will arrive before the great and dreadful/terrible day when I, Yahweh, will judge and punish people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 1:16–17; Isaiah 11:4; HEBREWS 10:26–31"> Because of what he preaches, parents and their children will love each other again &#91;IDM&#93;. If that does not happen, I will come and curse your country and destroy it.”</span>
</div>
