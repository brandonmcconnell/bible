## Yahweh’s love for the people of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:8–9; JEREMIAH 31:3; DEUTERONOMY 10:15"> Yahweh says, “I have always loved you Israeli people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:10; EZEKIEL 35:3–4; JEREMIAH 49:16–18"> But you reply, “In what way have you loved us?”<br />Yahweh replies, “This is how I loved you: It is true that your ancestor Jacob was &#91;RHQ&#93; Esau’s younger brother, and I loved Jacob, but I rejected Esau. I caused his hilly region to become ◄desolate/a place where crops did not grow►. I caused the land which he possessed to become a desert where jackals/wolves live.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:14; EZEKIEL 35:9; EZEKIEL 11:10"> The descendants of Esau who live in Edom may say, “Yes, we have been crushed/ruined, but we will rebuild houses in the ruins.”<br />But the Commander of the armies of angels replies, “They may build houses again, but I will demolish them again. Their country will be called ‘The land where wicked people live’ and their people will be called ‘The people with whom Yahweh is angry forever’.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:7; PSALMS 58:10–11; EZEKIEL 38:16"> When you Israeli people yourselves see what I will do to them, you will say, “It is evident far beyond the borders of Israel that Yahweh is very great!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Unacceptable sacrifices

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 6:46; Isaiah 1:2; JOHN 13:13–17"> But the Commander of the armies of angels says to the priests, “Boys honor their fathers, and servants respect their masters. So, if I am like your father and your master, why &#91;RHQ&#93; do you not honor and respect me? You have despised me &#91;MTY&#93;!”<br />But you ask, “◄How/In what way► have we despised you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MALACHI 1:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:27–32; MALACHI 1:8"> Yahweh replies, “Instead of honoring me, you have despised me by offering sacrifices on my altar that are unacceptable to me.”<br />Then you ask, “What sacrifices have we given that have been unacceptable to you?”<br />Yahweh replies, “You think that ◄it does not matter/it is okay► if you do not respect my altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:21; LEVITICUS 22:19–25; HOSEA 8:13"> You offer for sacrifices animals that are blind. ◄Is that not wrong?/That is disgusting!► &#91;RHQ&#93; And you offer for sacrifices animals that are crippled or that have diseases. ◄Is that also not wrong?/That also is disgusting!► &#91;RHQ&#93; If you tried to give presents like that to your governor, ◄do you think &#91;RHQ&#93; that he would be pleased?/he would certainly not be pleased!► ◄Would he &#91;RHQ&#93; accept such gifts?He would certainly not accept such gifts!► That is what the Commander of the armies of angels says!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 32:11; JEREMIAH 27:18; 1 PETER 1:17"> And he also says, “You plead with me to be merciful to you. But you bring to me sacrifices that are not acceptable to me, so why should I be kind to you &#91;RHQ&#93;?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MICAH 3:11; AMOS 5:21–24; JEREMIAH 6:20"> The Commander of the armies of angels also says, “I wish/desire that one of you would shut the gates of the temple courtyard, in order that no one could offer those worthless sacrifices. I am not pleased with you, and I will not accept the offerings that you bring to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 45:6; Isaiah 54:5; Isaiah 66:19–20"> People of other nations honor me &#91;MTY&#93; every day, from sunrise/morning until sunset/evening. All over the world people offer incense and other pure/acceptable offerings to me &#91;MTY&#93;. They do that because people among all the nations know that I &#91;MTY&#93; am very great.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MALACHI 1:6–8; MALACHI 2:8; AMOS 2:7"> But you priests show that you despise me by what you do. You say, ‘It is all right if people pollute the altar by bringing sacrifices that are unacceptable.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MICAH 6:3; LEVITICUS 22:19–23; MARK 14:37–38"> You say ‘We are tired of burning all these sacrifices on the altar.’ You ◄turn up your noses at/act very disrespectfully toward► me. When you bring to me animals that have been stolen or ones that are crippled or that have diseases, to offer them for sacrifices, do you think that &#91;RHQ&#93; I should accept them?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 8:12; ACTS 5:1–10; ECCLESIASTES 5:4–5"> I will curse anyone who tries to cheat me by solemnly promising to bring to me a perfect ram from his flock of sheep and then brings to me one that has defects. If anyone does that, I will punish him, because I am a great king; I am the Commander of the armies of angels and people of the other nations revere me &#91;MTY&#93;, but you do not!”</span>
</div>
