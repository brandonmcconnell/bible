## A warning to the priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HOSEA 5:1; MALACHI 1:6; JEREMIAH 13:13"> You priests, I will say something to warn you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 17:18; REVELATION 14:7; PSALMS 69:22"> The Commander of the armies of angels says this: “Pay attention to what I am saying, and then decide &#91;IDM&#93; to honor me &#91;MTY&#93;. If you do not do that, I will curse you, and I will curse the things that I have given to you to bless you. And I have already cursed them, because you have not honored &#91;IDM&#93; me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NAHUM 3:6; MALACHI 2:9; JOB 20:7"> I will punish your descendants &#91;MET&#93;, and it will be as though I will splatter on your faces some of the material inside the stomachs of the animals that are brought to be sacrificed, and you will be thrown away with the rest of that material.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:12; EZEKIEL 38:23; MATTHEW 3:12"> When that happens, you will know that I warned you like this, in order that my agreement with you priests who are descendants of Levi will continue to be obeyed. That is what I, the Commander of the armies of angels, am saying to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 25:12–13; EZEKIEL 34:25; EZEKIEL 37:26"> My agreement with your ancestor Levi was because I wanted the priests to live prosperously and peacefully. And that is what I have done for them. I required that they greatly respect me and revere &#91;DOU&#93; me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 12:3; 2 TIMOTHY 2:15–16; REVELATION 14:5"> They told the people that what I instructed them to do was right. And they did not tell &#91;MTY&#93; lies. They worked for me peacefully and loyally, and they helped many people to stop sinning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 2:24–25; LEVITICUS 10:11; DEUTERONOMY 21:5"> What priests say &#91;MTY&#93; should enable more people to know about me, and people should go to them &#91;MTY&#93; to be taught what I want them to know, because priests should be messengers from me, the Commander of the armies of angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; JEREMIAH 18:15; Isaiah 30:11"> But, you priests have stopped doing what I wanted you to do. What you have taught people has caused many of them to sin. You have rejected the agreement that I made with the descendants of Levi long ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:30; DEUTERONOMY 1:17; MATTHEW 19:17–18"> Therefore I have caused all the people to despise you, and I have caused you to be humiliated, because you have not obeyed me. When you teach people my commands, you do not treat all people equally.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Wrong things that they have done regarding marriage

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; MALACHI 2:11; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:6"> Now I will warn you about something else. We all certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; have the same heavenly Father. We are certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; all created by the same God. So why are &#91;RHQ&#93; some of you disobeying/despising the agreement that Yahweh made with our ancestors, by not doing for each other what you said that you would do?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:14–18; Ezra 9:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> You people of Judah have been unfaithful to Yahweh. You have done detestable things in Jerusalem and in other places in Israel. You Israeli men have defiled the temple that Yahweh loves. You have done that by marrying women who worship idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:29; LEVITICUS 20:3; EZEKIEL 24:21"> I wish that Yahweh would expel from Israel every man who has done that, even though they say that they are obeying the Commander of the armies of angels by bringing offerings to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–15; JEREMIAH 14:12; PSALMS 78:34–37"> This is another thing that you do: You cover Yahweh’s altar with your tears. You wail because he no longer pays attention to your offerings; but he is not pleased with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MALACHI 2:15; ECCLESIASTES 9:9; Isaiah 54:6"> You cry out, saying, “Why does Yahweh not like our offerings?” The answer is that Yahweh heard what each of you men solemnly promised to your wives when you were young. But you men have not done what you promised your wives; you sent them away, the ones to whom you made that agreement.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:4–6; MALACHI 2:14; MARK 10:6–8"> It is certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; Yahweh who joined you together. Your spirits and your bodies belong to him. So what he wants &#91;RHQ&#93; from you are godly children. So make sure that each of you men remain ◄with/loyal to► the woman that you married when you were young.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:3–9; MATTHEW 5:31–32; MARK 10:2–12"> Yahweh, the God to whom we Israelis belong, says, “I hate divorce!” So if you men divorce your wives, you are overwhelming them by being cruel to them. So be sure that you are ◄not disloyal/remain united► to your wives. That is what the Commander of the armies of angels says.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; 2 PETER 3:3–4; ECCLESIASTES 8:11"> Yahweh also says, “What you have said has caused me to become disgusted.”<br />You reply, “What have we said that caused him to become disgusted?”<br />The answer is that you have caused him to become disgusted by saying that Yahweh is pleased with all those who do evil things. You have caused him to become disgusted by constantly asking, “Why does God not act fairly?”</span>
</div>
</div>
