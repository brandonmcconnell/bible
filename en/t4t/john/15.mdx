## Jesus taught them that they needed to remain closely united to him in order to live in a way that would please God.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:21; Isaiah 27:2–3; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:9"> Jesus talked to us as we were walking along. Speaking figuratively of the need for us to live in a way that God wants us to, he said, “I am like &#91;MET&#93; a genuine vine, not like those Jewish leaders who do not teach the truth. My father is like &#91;MET&#93; a gardener who works to take care of a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:10; HEBREWS 12:10–11; MATTHEW 15:13"> Just like a gardener cuts off the branches that bear no grapes &#91;MET&#93;, God gets rid of those who do not please him even though they say that they belong to him. Those branches that bear fruit, the gardener trims so that they may bear more grapes. Similarly, my Father disciplines/corrects those who live as he wants them to live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 17:17; EPHESIANS 5:26; 1 PETER 1:22"> You are already like the branches that a gardener trims because you have believed the message that I have told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 JOHN 2:6; GALATIANS 2:20; JOHN 8:31"> Remain having a close relationship with me. If you do that, I will remain having a close relationship with you. A branch of a vine cannot bear fruit if it is cut off and left by itself. To bear fruit, it must remain attached to the vine. Similarly, you cannot live the way that God wants you to if you do not remain united to me &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:13; COLOSSIANS 1:10; JOHN 15:16"> I am like &#91;MET&#93; a vine. You are like &#91;MET&#93; the branches. All those who have a close relationship with me and with whom I have a close relationship will do much that pleases God, like &#91;MET&#93; a vine that bears much fruit. Remember that you can do nothing &#91;HYP&#93; that truly pleases God without my help.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:19; HEBREWS 6:7–8; MATTHEW 13:41"> A gardener cuts off and throws away useless branches. Then, after they dry up, he picks them up and throws them into a fire and burns them &#91;SIM&#93;. Similarly, everyone who does not remain having a close relationship with me, God will get rid of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 37:4; 1 JOHN 5:14; JOHN 14:13"> If you remain having a close relationship with me and you keep living in accordance with my message, you can ask God to do anything for you, and he will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 8:31; MATTHEW 5:16; PHILIPPIANS 1:11"> The way my Father is honored is by your &#123;The way you honor my Father is by&#125; doing much that pleases him &#91;MET&#93;, and by doing that, you will show that you are my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 17:23–24; JOHN 17:26; 1 JOHN 2:28"> I have loved you just as my Father has loved me. Now keep living in a way that is appropriate for those whom I love.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 14:15; 1 JOHN 5:3; 1 JOHN 3:21–24"> If you obey what I have commanded you, you will be acting in a way that is appropriate for those whom I love, just like I have obeyed what my Father has commanded me and I act in a way that is appropriate for someone whom he loves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 17:13; ROMANS 15:13; JOHN 16:24"> I have told you these things so that you may be joyful as I am joyful, and that you may be completely joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 JOHN 4:21; 1 PETER 4:8; JOHN 13:34"> What I am commanding you is this: Love each other just like I have loved you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 10:11; EPHESIANS 5:2; ROMANS 5:6–8"> The best way that people can show that they love someone is to die for that person. There is no way that you can love someone in a greater way than that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:50; 1 JOHN 5:3; JAMES 2:23"> You show that you are my friends if you keep doing what I have commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 25:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:9–12; MATTHEW 13:11"> I will no longer call you my servants, because servants do not know why their masters want them to do things. Instead, I have said that you are my friends, because I, acting like a friend, have revealed to you everything that my Father told me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 15:19; JEREMIAH 1:5–7; EPHESIANS 2:10"> You did not decide to become my disciples. Instead, I chose you, so that you would do many things that please him &#91;MET&#93;. The results of what you do will last forever. I also chose you so that my Father will do for you whatever you, using my authority, ask him to do &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 15:12; 1 JOHN 3:14–17; 1 PETER 2:17"> I repeat what I have commanded you: Love each other.”</span>
</div>

## Jesus taught them that those who were opposed to God would hate them, too.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 JOHN 3:13; JOHN 7:7; MARK 13:13"> “The people who are opposed to God will hate you. When that happens, remember that they hated me first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 JOHN 4:4–5; JOHN 17:14–16; EPHESIANS 1:4–11"> If you belonged to those who are opposed to God &#91;MTY&#93;, they would love you like they love those who belong to them. But you do not belong to those who are opposed to God &#91;MTY&#93;. Instead, I chose you so that you would separate yourselves from &#91;MTY&#93; them. That is why those who are opposed to God &#91;MTY&#93; hate you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 3:12; JOHN 13:16; MATTHEW 10:24"> Remember these words that I told you: ‘No servant is greater than his master.’ That means that you, who are like my servants, cannot expect people to treat you better than they treat me. So, since they have ◄persecuted me/caused me to suffer►, they will ◄persecute you/cause you to suffer► also. If they had paid attention to the things I taught them, they would pay attention to what you teach them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:22; JOHN 16:3; MATTHEW 10:18"> They will treat you like that because you belong to me &#91;MTY&#93;, and because they do not know the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 9:41; JAMES 4:17; ACTS 17:30"> If I had not come and spoken God’s message to them, they would not be guilty of rejecting me and my message. But now I have come and told them God’s message, so they will have no excuse when God judges them for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 8:40–42; 2 JOHN 1:9; 1 JOHN 2:23"> All those who hate me, it is as though they hate my Father as well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 5:36; JOHN 14:9; JOHN 7:31"> If I had not done among them the miracles that no one else ever did, they would not be guilty of the sin of rejecting me. But now, although they have seen those miracles, they have hated both me and my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 109:3; PSALMS 69:4; PSALMS 35:19"> But this has happened in order that these words that have been written in their Scriptures might be fulfilled &#123;to fulfill this that ◄someone/the Psalmist► wrote in their Scriptures&#125;: ‘They hated me for no reason.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 14:26; JOHN 14:16–17; JOHN 16:13–15"> Later I will send to you from my Father the one who will ◄encourage/be like a legal counsel for► you. He is the Spirit who will teach you God’s truth. He will come from my Father. He will tell people about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 JOHN 4:14; JOHN 21:24; ACTS 10:39–42"> But you disciples must also tell people about me, because you have been with me from the time when I started my ministry &#91;MTY&#93;.”</span>
