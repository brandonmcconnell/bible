## Jesus healed a man who was born blind.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> As Jesus walked along with us, he saw a man who had been blind from the time he was born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 13:2; JOHN 9:34; ACTS 28:4"> We disciples asked him, “Teacher, was this man blind from when he was born because his parents sinned or because he himself sinned?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 11:4; JOHN 11:40; ECCLESIASTES 9:1–2"> Jesus replied, “His being blind was not because he or his parents sinned. Instead, he has been blind in order that people can see the power of God &#123;the power of God can be seen&#125; as a result of what will now happen to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 12:35; EPHESIANS 5:16; JOHN 4:34"> While there is still time, I must do the work that the one who sent me wants me to do. Just like daytime is followed by nighttime when people do not work, at the end of our lives &#91;MET&#93; it is too late for us to do what God wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 8:12; JOHN 12:46; ACTS 13:47"> While I am still living in this world, I am the one who enables people to know about God, like &#91;MET&#93; a light enables the people in &#91;MTY&#93; this world to see what is in the darkness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARK 8:23; MARK 7:33; REVELATION 3:18"> After he said that, he spat on the ground. He made a little bit of mud with the saliva, and put it on the man’s eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5; JOHN 9:11; JOHN 11:37"> Then he said to him, “Go and wash in Siloam pool!” (That name means ‘sent;’ just like they sent the water by a channel into the pool, God sent Jesus). So the man went and washed in the pool, and when he went home he was able to see!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:8; 1 SAMUEL 21:11; LUKE 18:35"> His neighbors and others who previously had seen him when he was begging said, “He is the man who used to sit here and beg, isn’t he?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Some said, “Yes, he is.” Others said, “No, he is not. It is just a man who looks like him!” But the man himself said, “Yes, I am that man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 9:26; JOHN 9:21; ECCLESIASTES 11:5"> So they said to him, “How is it that now you can see?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 9:6–7; JOHN 9:27; JEREMIAH 36:17"> He replied, “The man whose name is Jesus made some mud and put it on my eyes. Then he told me to go to Siloam pool and wash. So I went there and washed, and then I could see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 5:11–13; EXODUS 2:18–20; JOHN 7:11"> They said to him, “Where is that man now?” He said, “I do not know.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Pharisees investigated the healing of the blind man.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 11:57; JOHN 11:46–47; JOHN 12:42"> They took to the Pharisees the man who was previously blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 5:9; LUKE 13:10–17; MATTHEW 12:1–14"> The day on which Jesus made the mud and enabled the man to see again was a ◄Sabbath/Jewish day of rest►. The Pharisees considered that healing someone was work, and their rules did not permit people to do any work ◄on the Sabbath/on the Jewish rest day►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 9:26–27; JOHN 9:10–11"> So the Pharisees also asked that man, “How did you become able to see?” He said to them, “The man put mud on my eyes. Then I washed, and now I can see!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 7:43; JOHN 10:19; MATTHEW 12:2"> So some of the Pharisees said, “Since this man Jesus disobeys our rules about working ◄on the Sabbath/on the Jewish rest day►, he is not from God.” But others said, “If he was a sinner, ◄he could certainly not do such miracles!/how could he do such miracles?► &#91;RHQ&#93;” So they were divided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 4:19; JOHN 6:14; ACTS 3:22–26"> So one of them said to the blind man again, “You are the man whom he enabled to see. What do you yourself say about him?” The man said, “I think he is a prophet!” So they told him to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:15–19; JOHN 5:44; Isaiah 26:11"> The Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; still did not believe that the man was blind when he was born, and that he was now able to see. So they sent someone to bring the man’s parents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 9:8–9; ACTS 4:14; ACTS 3:10"> When they got there, one of the Jewish leaders asked them, “Is that man your son? Do you say that he was blind when he was born? If that is true, how is he now able to see?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> His parents replied, “We know that he is our son. We know that he was blind when he was born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But we do not know how he is able to see now. We also do not know who enabled him to see. Ask our son! He is old enough to answer questions from authorities like you He can tell you himself!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 7:13; JOHN 16:2; LUKE 6:22"> The Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; had previously declared that they would prevent anyone who declared that Jesus was the Messiah from entering their synagogues. His parents knew that, so they were afraid of the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 9:21"> That is the reason that they said, “He is old enough to answer questions, so ask him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:19; JOHN 9:16; JOHN 8:49"> So they sent someone to bring back to them the man who had been blind. When he got there, the Jewish leaders said to him, “Knowing that God is listening &#91;IDM&#93;, tell the truth! We know that the man who healed you is a sinner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 JOHN 5:10; JOHN 9:30; JOHN 5:11"> He replied, “I do not know if he is a sinner or not. But one thing I do know is that I was blind, but now I can see!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So they said to him, “What did he do to you? How did he enable you to see?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 22:67; JOHN 9:10–15"> He replied, “I told you that already, but you did not &#91;RHQ&#93; pay attention! Why do you want to hear me tell you again? ◄Do you also want to become his disciples?/You talk as though &#91;IRO&#93; you also want to become his disciples &#91;RHQ&#93;►.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ROMANS 2:17; JOHN 7:19; MATTHEW 27:39"> Then they insulted him angrily. They said, “You are that man’s disciple, but we are Moses’ disciples!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 8:14; NUMBERS 12:2–7; ACTS 7:35"> We know that God spoke to Moses; but this man, we do not know where he is from or where he gets any authority from!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 12:37; PSALMS 119:18; Isaiah 29:18"> The man replied, “That is very surprising! You say that you do not know where he gets any authority. But he enabled me to see!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:29; Isaiah 1:15; PROVERBS 28:9"> We know that God does not help sinners who ask God to help them. Instead, he listens to and helps godly people who pray. He listens to people who do what God wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 64:4; REVELATION 16:18; JOB 20:4"> No one has ever enabled a man to see who was blind when he was born like I was. That has never happened since the world began!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 9:16; JOHN 3:2; ACTS 5:38–39"> So if this man had not come from God, he would not be able to do anything like that!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JOHN 9:22; JOHN 9:2; Isaiah 66:5"> They replied to him, “You ◄were born as a result of your parents’ sin &#91;EUP&#93;/bastard►! ◄Do you think you are qualified to teach us?/You are not qualified to teach us!► &#91;RHQ&#93;” Then they threw him out of the synagogue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOHN 20:31; 1 JOHN 5:13; JOHN 10:36"> Jesus heard people say that they had thrown that man out. He found the man and said to him, “Do you believe that the one who came down from heaven is the Messiah?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ROMANS 10:14; SONG OF SONGS 5:9; JOHN 1:38"> The man answered, “Sir, who is he? Tell me, in order that I may believe in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JOHN 4:26; PSALMS 25:8–9; JOHN 14:21–23"> Jesus said to him, “You have seen him. In fact, it is I, the one who am speaking to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:33; MATTHEW 28:9; LUKE 24:52"> The man said, “Lord, I believe that you are the Messiah!” Then he knelt down before Jesus and worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOHN 3:19; JOHN 12:46; ACTS 26:18"> Jesus said, “I have come into this world to judge the people in the world. The result will be that those who realize that they do not know God’s truth will perceive it. That is like &#91;MET&#93; enabling those who are blind to see. But the result will also be that people who falsely think &#91;IRO&#93; that they understand God’s truth will never understand it. That is like &#91;MET&#93; people who are blind remaining blind permanently.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="REVELATION 3:17; MATTHEW 15:12–14; JOHN 7:47–52"> Some of the Pharisees who were with him heard him say that, and said to him, “You are not suggesting that we are like blind people, are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 5:21; JOHN 15:22–24; 1 JOHN 1:8–10"> Jesus said to them, “If you realized that you did not yet know God’s truth, but you wanted to, then you would be like blind people who wanted to see. God would be able to forgive your sins. But you are now falsely claiming that you know God’s truth, so you are like people who are blind who claim that they can see. Because of that, God is not able to forgive your sins.”</span>
