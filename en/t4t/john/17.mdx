## Jesus prayed that God would honor him.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 13:31–32; JOHN 17:4–5; PHILIPPIANS 2:9–11"> After Jesus said those things, he looked up toward heaven. Then he prayed, “My Father, it is now the time &#91;MTY&#93; for me to suffer and die. Honor me as I do that, in order that I may honor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 10:28–29; JOHN 6:39; DANIEL 7:14"> You gave me authority over all people, in order that I might enable all those whom you chose to come to me to live eternally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 JOHN 5:20; 1 JOHN 5:11–12; JEREMIAH 9:23–24"> The way for people to live eternally is for them to know that you are the only true God, and to know that I, Jesus, am the Messiah, the one you have sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 4:34; ACTS 20:24; 2 TIMOTHY 4:7"> I have honored you here on this earth by completing all the work that you gave me to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 1:1–3; COLOSSIANS 1:15–17; JOHN 10:30"> My Father, now honor me when I am with you again, by causing me to have the greatness I had when I was with you before the world began.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus prayed that God would protect his disciples.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 17:26; JOHN 14:21–24; 1 JOHN 5:20"> “I have revealed what you are like to the people whom you brought to me from among those who do not belong to you &#91;MTY&#93;. Those who came to me belonged to you, and you brought them to me. Now they have obeyed your message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 7:16–17; JOHN 16:15; JOHN 14:7–10"> Now they know that everything you have given me, your message and your work, comes from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 17:14; JOHN 15:15; JOHN 16:30"> I gave them the message that you gave me, and they have accepted it. They now know for certain that I came from you. They now believe that you sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:24; JOHN 17:2; JOHN 17:6"> I am praying for them. I am not praying for those who do not belong to you &#91;MTY&#93;. Instead, I am praying for those whom you have brought to me, because they belong to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 10:30; 1 PETER 2:9; REVELATION 5:8–14"> All the disciples that I have belong to you, and all those who belong to you also belong to me. They have shown how great I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:9; PROVERBS 18:10; JOHN 17:12–18"> I will not be staying in the world any longer. I will be coming back to you. They, however, will be here in the world among those who are opposed to you. My Holy Father, protect them from spiritual harm by your power &#91;MTY&#93;, the power that you gave me, in order that they may be united as we are united.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 109:6–19; JOHN 10:27–28; JOHN 18:9"> While I have been with them, I have completely protected them by the power &#91;MTY&#93; that you gave me. As a result, only one of them will be eternally separated from you. He is the one who was doomed to be eternally separated from you. That has happened to fulfill what a prophet wrote in the Scriptures would happen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:2; GALATIANS 5:22; JOHN 15:11"> Father, now I am about to return to you. I have said these things while I am still here in the world in order that my disciples may fully experience being joyful, as I have been joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 17:16; JOHN 15:18–21; 1 JOHN 5:19–20"> I have given them your message. As a result, those who are opposed to you &#91;MTY&#93; have hated them, because my disciples do not belong to those who oppose you &#91;MTY&#93;, just like I do not belong to those who oppose you &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 121:7; 1 JOHN 5:18; MATTHEW 6:13"> I am asking you, not that you take them out of this world, but instead that you protect them from Satan, the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 17:14"> They do not belong to those who are opposed to you &#91;MTY&#93;, just like I also do not belong to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 15:3; PSALMS 119:160; EPHESIANS 5:26"> Set my disciples apart so that they may ◄completely belong to/serve► you, by enabling them to live in accordance with what is true. Your message is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:20; JOHN 20:21; JOHN 17:3"> Just like you sent me here into this world, now I surely will be sending them to other places in &#91;MTY&#93; the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="TITUS 2:14; HEBREWS 2:11; JOHN 17:17"> I dedicate myself to completely belong to you, in order that they also may truly be dedicated &#123;dedicate themselves&#125; completely to you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus prayed for future believers.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 17:6–11; ACTS 2:41; ACTS 4:4"> “I am praying not only for these eleven disciples. I am praying also for those who will believe in me as a result of hearing their message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:12; COLOSSIANS 3:11–14; JOHN 17:11"> My Father, I want all of them to be united, just like I am united with you because of my relationship with you, and as you are united with me because of your relationship with me. I also want them to be united with us. I want that to happen so that those who do not know you &#91;MTY&#93; may know that you sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 3:18; JOHN 14:20; MARK 16:17–20"> I have honored my disciples just like you honored me, in order that they may be united, as we are united.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 17:21; JOHN 16:27; 1 JOHN 3:1"> I want them to be united just like they are united with me and as you are united with me. May they be completely united, in order that those who do not belong to you &#91;MTY&#93; may know that you sent me and that you have loved them just like you have loved me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 17:5; JOHN 12:26; JOHN 14:3"> My Father, I want the disciples you have brought to me to some day be with me in heaven, where I will be. I want them to see my greatness. I want them to see the greatness you gave me because you loved me. You gave me that greatness before you created the world.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 17:11; JOHN 7:29; JOHN 17:8"> My righteous Father, although the people who do not belong to you &#91;MTY&#93; do not know what you are like, I know what you are like, and my disciples know that you sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 17:23; JOHN 17:6; JOHN 15:9"> I have revealed to them what you are like, and I will continue to reveal to them what you are like. I will do that in order that they may love others just like you love me, and in order that I may be in them by my Spirit.”</span>
</div>
</div>
