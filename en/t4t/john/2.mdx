## In Cana Jesus did his first miracle, turning water into delicious wine.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 4:46; PROVERBS 31:10–12; JOHN 21:2"> Two days later there was a wedding celebration in Cana town, in Galilee district. Jesus’ mother was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 1:40–49; MATTHEW 10:40–42; 1 CORINTHIANS 7:39"> Jesus and we his disciples were also there, because we had been invited &#123;someone had invited us&#125; also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 104:15; PHILIPPIANS 4:6; Isaiah 24:11"> When the guests had drunk all the wine that was there, Jesus’ mother said to him, “The wine is all gone; can you do something about that?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 7:6; JOHN 13:1; JOHN 7:30"> Jesus said to her, “Ma’am/Woman, ◄do not tell me what to do!/why do you tell me what to do?► &#91;RHQ&#93; It is not yet time &#91;MTY&#93; to show that I am the Messiah by working miracles.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 15:14; GENESIS 6:22; HEBREWS 11:8"> Then Jesus’ mother said to the servants, “Do whatever he tells you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 3:25; HEBREWS 9:10; EPHESIANS 5:26"> There were six empty stone jars there. The Jews habitually put water in them to use for washing things to make them acceptable to God. Each jar held ◄20 to 30 gallons/80 to 120 liters►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 KINGS 4:2–6; JOHN 2:5; JOHN 2:3"> Jesus said to the servants, “Fill the jars with water!” So they filled the jars to the brim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 2:9; PROVERBS 3:5–6; ROMANS 13:7"> Then he told them, “Now, ladle out some of it and take it ◄to the head waiter/to the master of ceremonies►.” So the servants did that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 4:46; JOHN 7:17; PSALMS 119:100"> ◄The head waiter/The master of ceremonies► tasted the water, which had now become wine. He did not know where the wine had come from, but the servants who had ladled out the wine knew. The wine was delicious! So he called the bridegroom over,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="REVELATION 7:16–17; PROVERBS 9:1–6; LUKE 16:25"> and said to him, “Everyone else serves the best wine first. Then when the guests have drunk so much that they cannot tell the difference, they serve the cheap wine. But you have not done what others do. You have kept the best wine until now!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 20:30–31; JOHN 1:14; JOHN 3:2"> That was the first miracle that Jesus did. He did it in Cana town, in Galilee district. By doing it he showed how awesome he is, and as a result, we his disciples believed that he truly was the Messiah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## After Jesus expelled the merchants from the temple, the Jewish leaders wanted him to do a miracle to show by what authority he did that. He told them metaphorically that he would become alive again three days after he died.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 2:41; JOHN 11:55; JOHN 2:23"> Later, when it was almost time for the Jewish Passover celebration, Jesus and we his disciples went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 19:45–46; MARK 11:15; MARK 11:17"> There, in the Temple courtyard, he saw some men who were selling cattle, sheep, and pigeons for sacrifices. He also saw men who were sitting at tables, making a big profit as they gave people Temple tax coins in exchange for their Roman coins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 18:6; ZECHARIAH 4:6; 2 CORINTHIANS 10:4"> Then Jesus made a whip from some cords and used it to chase out the sheep and cattle from the Temple courtyard. By overturning their tables he scattered the coins of the men who were exchanging them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:13; JEREMIAH 7:11; MARK 11:17"> He said to those who were selling doves, “Take these doves away from here! Stop defiling my Father’s Temple by making it a market!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 69:9; PSALMS 119:139"> Then we disciples remembered that these words had been written &#123;that someone/David had written these words&#125; in the Scriptures prophesying what the Messiah would say: “Others &#91;PRS&#93; will strongly oppose me because I greatly desire that people respect your Sacred Tent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:23; MARK 8:11; MARK 11:27–28"> So one of the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; replied to him, “What miracle will you perform to show us that you have authority from God to do these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARK 14:58; MARK 15:29; MATTHEW 27:40"> Jesus replied to them, “If/When you destroy this temple, I will build it again within three days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; said, “We have been building this Temple for 46 years and it is not finished yet! So ◄there is no way you will build it within three days!/how will you build it within three days?► &#91;RHQ&#93;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 6:19; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:16; EPHESIANS 2:20–22"> But when Jesus said that about the temple, he was really talking about his own body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 12:16; JOHN 16:4; LUKE 24:7–8"> Later, after Jesus had died and had become alive again, we his disciples remembered the words he had said. As a result, we believed what had been prophesied in the Scriptures about the Messiah becoming alive again, and we believed that what Jesus said was true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 7:31; LUKE 8:13; JOHN 6:14"> While Jesus was in Jerusalem at the Passover celebration, many people came to believe that he was the Messiah because they saw the miracles he was performing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 6:64; HEBREWS 4:13; ACTS 1:24"> But he did not let them tell him what he should do as the Messiah, because he knew within himself what they were all thinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 13:11; MATTHEW 9:4; JOHN 6:64"> He did not need anyone to tell him what others were thinking, because he already knew what they were thinking and wanting.</span>
</div>
