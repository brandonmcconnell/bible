## Jesus appeared to seven disciples and enabled them miraculously to catch a lot of fish.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 6:1; JOHN 6:23; MATTHEW 28:16"> After that, Jesus showed himself to us disciples when we were at Tiberias Lake, which is another name for Galilee Lake. This is what happened:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:21–22; JOHN 1:45–1; JOHN 4:46"> Simon Peter, Thomas who was called &#123;whom we called&#125; The Twin, Nathaniel from Cana town in Galilee, my older brother and I, and two other disciples were together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 5:5; MATTHEW 4:18–20; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:7–9"> Simon Peter said to us, “I am going to try to catch some fish.” We said, “We will go with you.” So we went down to the lake and got into the boat. But that night we caught nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 20:14; LUKE 24:15–16; LUKE 24:31"> Early the next morning, Jesus stood on the shore, but we did not know that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:19; PHILIPPIANS 4:11–13; 1 JOHN 2:13"> He called out to us, “My friends, you have not caught any fish, have you?” We answered, “You are correct, we have not caught any.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 5:4–7; ACTS 2:41; JOHN 2:5"> He said to us, “Throw your net out from the right-hand side of the boat! Then you will find some!” We did that, and we caught so many fish in the net that we were unable to pull the net into the boat!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 13:23; JOHN 20:2; MATTHEW 14:28–29"> But I knew that it was Jesus, so I said to Peter, “It is the Lord!” Peter had taken off his cloak while he was working, but as soon as he heard me say “It is the Lord!” he wrapped his cloak around himself and jumped into the water and swam to shore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:11"> The rest of us came to the shore in the boat, pulling the net full of fish. We were not far from shore, only about 100 yards/meters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 21:13; 1 KINGS 19:5–6; JOHN 21:10"> When we got to the shore, we saw that there was a fire of burning coals there, with a large fish on the fire, and some buns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Jesus said to us, “Bring some of the fish that you have just caught!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 5:6–8; ACTS 2:41"> Simon Peter got in the boat and dragged the net to the shore. It was full of large fish. There were 153 of them! But in spite of there being so many fish, the net was not torn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ACTS 10:41; LUKE 9:45; JOHN 16:19"> Jesus said to us, “Come and eat some breakfast!” None of us dared to ask him, “Who are you?” because we knew that it was the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 21:9; ACTS 10:41; LUKE 24:42–43"> Jesus took the buns and gave them to us. He did the same with the fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 20:26; JOHN 20:19"> That was the third time that Jesus appeared to us disciples after God caused him to become alive again after he died.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus asked Peter three times if he really loved Jesus. Then Jesus told Peter how Peter would die.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; 1 JOHN 4:19; ROMANS 15:1"> When we had finished eating, Jesus said to Simon Peter, “John’s son Simon, do you love me more than these other disciples do?” Peter said to him, “Yes, Lord, you know that you are dear to me (OR, that I love you).” Jesus said, “Give to those who belong to me what they need spiritually, like &#91;MET&#93; a shepherd provides food for his lambs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ACTS 20:28; 1 PETER 5:2; 1 PETER 2:25"> Jesus said to him again, “John’s son Simon, do you love me?” He replied, “Yes, Lord, you know that you are dear to me (OR, that I love you).” Jesus said to him, “Take care of those who belong to me, like &#91;MET&#93; a shepherd takes care of his sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 13:38; JOHN 16:30; MARK 14:72"> Jesus said to him a third time, “John’s son Simon, am I really dear to you (OR, do you really love me)?” Peter was grieved because Jesus asked him this three times, and because the third time he changed the question. He said, “Lord, you know everything. You know that you are dear to me (OR, that I love you).” Jesus said, “Give to those who belong to me what they need spiritually, as &#91;MET&#93; a shepherd provides food for his sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 13:36; ACTS 12:3–4; ACTS 21:11"> Now listen to this carefully: When you were young, you put your clothes on by yourself, and you went wherever you wanted to go. But when you are old, you will stretch out your arms, and someone will fasten them with a rope and will lead you to a place where you do not want to go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 PETER 1:14; JOHN 21:22; MATTHEW 10:38"> Jesus said this to indicate how Peter would die ◄violently/on a cross► in order to honor God. Then Jesus said to him, “Keep being my faithful disciple until you die.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Peter asked Jesus what would happen to John.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 21:7; JOHN 20:2; JOHN 21:24"> Peter turned around and saw that I was following them. I was the one who leaned close to Jesus during the Passover meal and said, “Lord, who is going to enable your enemies to seize you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 13:23–24; ACTS 1:6–7; MATTHEW 24:3–4"> When Peter saw me, he asked, “Lord, what is going to happen to him?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="REVELATION 2:25; REVELATION 3:11; MARK 9:1"> Jesus said to him, “If I want him to remain alive until I return, ◄that is not your concern!/what is that to you?► &#91;RHQ&#93; You be my faithful disciple!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:29; JOB 33:13; DANIEL 4:35"> Some of the other believers heard a report of what Jesus had said about me, and they thought that Jesus meant that I would not die. But Jesus did not say that I would not die. He said only, “If I want him to remain alive until I return, ◄that is not your concern!/what is that to you?► &#91;RHQ&#93;”</span>
</div>
