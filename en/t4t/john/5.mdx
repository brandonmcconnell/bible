## The Jewish leaders did not like the fact that Jesus healed a paralyzed man on the Sabbath day.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:16; EXODUS 23:14–17; EXODUS 34:23"> Some time later, Jesus went up to Jerusalem when the Jews were having another celebration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:39; Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32"> At one of the gates into the city called the Sheep Gate, there is a pool. In our language we call it Bethzatha. Around the pool were five open areas with roofs over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARK 3:1–4; LUKE 7:22; MATTHEW 15:30"> Many people were lying there. They were people who were blind, lame, or paralyzed.<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="-4 send down an angel, and the angel would stir the water. Then the first person who got down into the water after the water was stirred would be healed of whatever disease that person had.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 3:2; LUKE 8:43; MARK 9:21"> One of those who was there had been paralyzed for 38 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 18:41; HEBREWS 4:15; HEBREWS 4:13"> Jesus saw him lying there and found out that the man had been like that for a long time. He said to the man, “Do you want to become well?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 72:12; DEUTERONOMY 32:36; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:24"> The paralyzed man replied to him, “Yes, sir, I want to get well, but there is no one to help me get down into the pool when the water is stirred &#123;stirs&#125;. While I am trying to get to the pool, someone else always gets there before me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 5:24; MATTHEW 9:6; MARK 2:11"> Jesus said to him, “Get up! Then pick up your mat and walk!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ACTS 3:7–8; JOHN 9:14; MARK 3:2–4"> The man immediately was healed. He picked up his mat and started walking! The day on which this happened was a Jewish day of rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:21; LUKE 6:2; JOHN 9:16"> So the Jewish leaders said to the man who had been healed, “Today is ◄the Sabbath/our rest day►, and in our Jewish laws it is written that people should not work on our ◄Sabbath/rest day►, so you should not be carrying your mat!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MARK 2:9–11; JOHN 9:16"> The man replied to them, “The man who healed me, he himself said to me, ‘Pick up your mat and walk!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:23; JUDGES 6:29; ROMANS 10:2"> They asked him, “Who is the man who said to you, ‘Pick it up and walk!’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 4:30; LUKE 24:31; JOHN 8:59"> But since Jesus had disappeared in the crowd without telling the man his name, the man did not know who it was who had healed him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Jewish leaders became very angry with Jesus for saying that he was the man who was also God.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 8:11; LEVITICUS 26:23–24; PSALMS 118:18"> Later, Jesus found the man in the Temple courtyard. He told the man his name, and said to him, “Listen! You are healed! So stop sinning! If you do not stop sinning, something will happen to you that will be worse than the illness you had before!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 9:11–12; JOHN 4:29; JOHN 9:25"> The man went away and told the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; that it was Jesus who had healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 10:39; JOHN 7:19–20; MARK 3:6"> So the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; started to harass Jesus, because Jesus was doing these things ◄on the Sabbath/on the Jewish rest day►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 9:4; JOHN 14:10; COLOSSIANS 1:16"> Then Jesus replied to them, “My Father has always been working every day, including ◄the Sabbath/the day of rest►, up until now. I am doing the same thing!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 10:30; JOHN 10:33; JOHN 19:7"> The Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; considered that he was disobeying their rules about ◄the Sabbath/the day of rest►, and that by saying that God was his Father, he was making himself equal with God. And they considered that both these things were grounds for killing him. So they tried even harder to find a way to kill him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus replied to their objections by stating five reasons why they should believe that he is the ◄Son of/man who was also► God.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 12:49; JOHN 8:28; JOHN 14:10"> Jesus replied to them by saying, “You need to know this: I can do nothing by my own authority. I do only the kind of things that I see my Father doing. Whatever kinds of things my Father is doing, those are the things I am doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 3:35; MATTHEW 3:17; JOHN 17:26"> My Father loves me, and he shows me everything that he is doing. He will show me the miracles that he wants me to do that will be greater than the ones that you have already seen me do, so that you may be amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 8:11; DEUTERONOMY 32:39; JOHN 11:25"> For example, just like my Father causes people who have died to become alive again, I will give eternal life to everyone that I want to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 5:27; ACTS 17:31; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:10"> Furthermore, my Father is not the one who judges people concerning their sins. Instead, he has given to me the work of judging people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 JOHN 2:23; LUKE 10:16; MATTHEW 11:27"> in order that all people may honor me, just like they honor my Father. My Father considers that anyone who does not honor me is not honoring him, the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 3:36; JOHN 6:40; 1 JOHN 5:11–13"> Listen to this carefully: Those who hear my message and believe that God is the one who sent me have eternal life. God will not ◄condemn them/say that he will punish them►. They are no longer separated from God. Instead, they have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 5:28; EPHESIANS 5:14; EPHESIANS 2:5"> Listen to this carefully: There will be a time when those who are ◄spiritually dead/separated from God► will hear the voice of me, ◄the Son of God/the man who is also God►. In fact, it is that time already. Those who hear and pay attention to my message will have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 11:26; JOHN 1:4; EXODUS 3:14"> My Father has power to make things live. Similarly, he has given me the power to enable people to live eternally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ACTS 10:42; JOHN 5:22; ACTS 17:31"> Because I am the one who came from heaven, he has given me the authority to judge people concerning their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="REVELATION 20:12; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:14–17; Isaiah 26:19"> Do not be surprised about that, because there will be a time when all people who have died will hear my voice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2–3; ROMANS 2:6–10; ACTS 24:15"> and they will become alive again. Those who have lived good lives will rise from their graves and live forever. But those who have lived evil lives will rise, and I will ◄condemn them/declare that I will punish them for their sins►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 6:38; JOHN 5:19; JOHN 4:34"> I do not do anything like that by my own authority. I judge people only according to what I hear my Father tell me. I will judge people fairly, because I do not want to please only myself. Instead, I want to please my Father, who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:2; JOHN 8:54; REVELATION 3:14"> If I were the only one to tell people about myself, people could rightly say that what I say is not true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOHN 1:33; JOHN 12:28–30; JOHN 5:36–37"> But there is someone else who tells people about me. And I know that what he tells people about me is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 1:29–34; JOHN 3:26–36; JOHN 1:15–27"> As for you, when you sent messengers to John the Baptizer to ask about me, he told the truth about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKE 19:41–42; JOHN 20:31; 1 JOHN 5:9"> I do not need that people tell others about me. But instead, I am reminding you about what John told people about me, in order that you will believe it and be saved &#123;God will save you&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 PETER 1:19; JOHN 1:7–8; MATTHEW 11:11"> John’s message about me (OR, John) &#91;MET&#93; was like a lamp that shines brightly. For a short while you were willing to be made happy by that message &#123;to let that message make you happy&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOHN 10:25; JOHN 10:37–38; JOHN 15:24"> But there is something else that tells you about me. It should prove who I am more than what John said about me. The miracles &#91;PRS&#93; that my Father told me to do, the miracles that I am performing, show (OR, prove) to people that my Father sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JOHN 8:18; JOHN 1:18; MATTHEW 3:17"> Furthermore, my Father, who sent me, tells people about me. You have never heard his voice or seen him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 JOHN 2:14; DEUTERONOMY 6:6–9; JOHN 12:44–48"> Furthermore, you have not believed in me, the one he sent. So you have not believed his message in your inner beings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKE 24:27; LUKE 24:44; 2 PETER 1:19–21"> You carefully study (OR, Study) the Scriptures, because you think that by studying them you will find the way to have eternal life. And those Scriptures tell people about me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JOHN 3:19; JOHN 1:11; PSALMS 81:11"> But you refuse ◄to come to me/to believe my message► in order that you may have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JOHN 7:18; JOHN 5:34; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:6"> It does not matter to me whether people praise me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 JOHN 4:20; 1 JOHN 3:17; JOHN 2:25"> But you want people to praise you. I know that within yourselves you do not love God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="JOHN 10:25; MATTHEW 24:5; JOHN 17:4–6"> Although I have come to earth with my Father’s authority &#91;MTY&#93;, you do not accept me. But if someone else comes with his own authority &#91;MTY&#93;, you accept him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="JOHN 12:43; ROMANS 2:29; PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> You accept your praising each other, but you do not try to do things that will result in God himself praising you. So ◄there is no way you can believe in me!/how can you believe my message?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="ROMANS 3:19–20; GALATIANS 3:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:7–11"> But do not think that I am the one who will accuse you while my Father is listening! No, it is Moses who will accuse you! You thought that he would defend you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:15; DEUTERONOMY 18:18–19; JOHN 1:45"> Moses wrote about me, so if you had believed what he wrote, you would have believed what I said</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="LUKE 16:31; LUKE 16:29"> But because you did not believe what he wrote about me, ◄you will certainly not believe what I say!/how will you believe what I say?► &#91;RHQ&#93;”</span>
</div>
