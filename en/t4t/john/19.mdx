## Pilate finally permitted them to crucify Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; MATTHEW 20:19"> Then Pilate took Jesus inside and had soldiers ◄scourge Jesus/strike Jesus with a whip that had pieces of metal or bone fastened to it►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARK 15:16–20; LUKE 23:11; MATTHEW 27:27–31"> The soldiers also took some branches with thorns and wove them to make something like a crown. Then they put it on his head. They also put a purple robe on him. They did these things to ridicule him by pretending that he was a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:29; MATTHEW 26:49; LUKE 1:28"> Then they kept coming to him and saying, “Hooray for the King of the Jews &#91;IRO&#93;!” and slapping him on his face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 18:38; JOHN 19:6; LUKE 23:4"> Once more Pilate came outside and said to the crowd, “Look! I am bringing him out to you so that you may know that I do not find that he has done anything for which we should punish him any more.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 19:2; HEBREWS 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> When Jesus came out, wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe, Pilate said to them, “Look at this wretched man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 18:31; JOHN 19:4; ACTS 2:23"> When the chief priests and Temple guards saw him, they shouted, “Command your soldiers to kill him by nailing him to a cross! Crucify him!” Pilate, knowing that they could not legally do it themselves, said to them, “You yourselves take him and nail him to a cross! As for me, I do not find that he has done anything for which we should punish him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:16; JOHN 5:18; MATTHEW 26:63–66"> The Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; replied, “Our ancestor Moses gave us the law that says we must kill anyone who claims to be God. This man claims that he is ◄the Son of/the man who is also► God, so you must have him killed &#123;command your soldiers to kill him&#125;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 19:13; ACTS 14:11–19"> When Pilate heard that, he was more afraid of what would happen to himself if he commanded the soldiers to kill Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 18:33; PSALMS 38:13–15; PHILIPPIANS 1:28"> So he took Jesus back inside the headquarters. He said to Jesus, “Where do you really come from?” But Jesus did not answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 5:19; JOHN 18:39; DANIEL 3:14–15"> So Pilate said to him, “Are you refusing to answer me? Do you not know that I have authority to release you, and I also have authority to have you crucified &#123;command my soldiers to crucify you&#125;?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 13:1; JOHN 3:27; ACTS 2:23"> Jesus replied, “The only authority you have is what has been given to you by God &#91;MTY&#93; &#123;what God &#91;MTY&#93; has given you&#125;. The high priest put me into your hands. He has done to me what he wanted to do, and you do not really want to do it. So he is guilty of committing a greater sin than you are.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ACTS 17:6–7; JOHN 18:33–36; MARK 6:16–26"> Because of that, Pilate kept trying to release Jesus. But the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93;, threatening to report to the Emperor that Pilate was not going to punish a man who claimed he was a king, continued to shout, “Anyone who claims that he is a king is opposing the Emperor! So if you release this man, we will make sure that the Emperor learns about it, and then he will not consider you as his friend!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 82:5–7; MATTHEW 27:19; AMOS 4:7"> When Pilate heard that, he brought Jesus out again. He sat down at the place where he made decisions about punishing people. The place was called &#123;People called it&#125; The Stone Pavement. In the Aramaic language its name was Gabbatha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:62; JOHN 19:5; MARK 15:25"> It was almost noontime, on the day that they prepared things for the Passover celebration (OR, the day before the Sabbath during the Passover celebration).<br />Pilate said to the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93;, ridiculing them, “Look at your king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 23:18; ACTS 21:36; JOHN 19:6"> They shouted, “Take him away! Take him away! Have him crucified &#123;Command your soldiers to nail him to a cross&#125;!” Pilate said to them, “He is your king! Do you really want me to tell my soldiers to nail him to a cross?” The chief priests replied, “The Emperor is our king! We do not have any other king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 23:24–25; MATTHEW 27:26–31; MARK 15:15–20"> Then at last Pilate agreed to do what they wanted, and he told the soldiers to crucify Jesus. They nailed Jesus to a cross. John 19:16b-24<br />Then the soldiers took Jesus away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 23:33; LUKE 14:27; LUKE 23:26"> As they left, he himself was carrying the cross on which they were going to nail him. They went to a place called The Place of a Skull. In the Aramaic language it is called &#123;they call it&#125; Golgotha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; MARK 15:24–27; GALATIANS 3:13"> There, after removing most of his clothes, the soldiers nailed him to the cross. They also nailed two other criminals to crosses. There was one on each side, and Jesus was in the middle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:37; LUKE 23:38; MARK 15:26"> Pilate also had them write on a board a notice that stated why they were executing him, and fasten it to the cross. But all they wrote was ‘Jesus from Nazareth, the King of the Jews’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ACTS 26:14; REVELATION 16:16; ACTS 22:2"> Many Jews were able to read this sign, because the place where Jesus was nailed &#123;where they nailed Jesus&#125; to the cross was very close to Jerusalem, where many people had come for the celebration, and because it was written &#123;they wrote it&#125; in three languages: Hebrew, Latin, and Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOHN 19:14; JOHN 19:19"> So the Jewish priests went back to Pilate and protested, saying to him, “Change what they have written from ‘The King of the Jews’ to ‘This man said that he is the King of the Jews’!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 76:10; PSALMS 65:7; PROVERBS 8:29"> Pilate replied, “What I told them to write is what they have written, and I will not change it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 23:34; MARK 15:24; MATTHEW 27:35"> After the soldiers nailed Jesus to the cross, they took his clothes and divided them into four parts, one part for each soldier. But they kept his cloak separate. This cloak was without seam, woven from top to bottom, one piece of cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 22:18; JOHN 19:28; JOHN 19:36–37"> So they said to each other, “Let’s not tear it. Instead, let’s decide ◄by throwing lots/by gambling► who will get it.” So that is what the soldiers did. As a result, these words were fulfilled &#123;they fulfilled these words&#125; that the Psalmist had written in Scripture,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">They divided most of my clothes among themselves.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They cast lots for one piece of my clothing.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus entrusted his mother to John’s care.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 23:49; MATTHEW 27:55–56; JOHN 20:1"> Near the cross where they had nailed Jesus stood his mother, his mother’s sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and another Mary, the woman from Magdala village.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 21:24; JOHN 20:2; JOHN 2:4"> Jesus saw his mother standing there. He also saw me standing nearby. Then he said to his mother, “This man will now be like your son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:48–50; JOHN 16:32; 1 TIMOTHY 5:2–4"> And he said to me, “Treat this woman as &#91;MET&#93; your mother.” So from that time I took her to my home and took care of her.</span>
</div>

## Jesus died.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 69:21; PSALMS 22:15; JOHN 19:30"> Later, Jesus knew that everything that God sent him to do had now been completed &#123;that he had now completed everything that God sent him to do&#125;, but he knew that something else that was written in the Scriptures had to be fulfilled &#123;that he had to fulfill something else that they had written in the Scriptures&#125;. So he said, “I am thirsty!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:18; LUKE 23:36; MARK 15:36"> There was a jar of sour wine there. So someone took a stalk of a plant called hyssop and fastened a sponge to it. Then he dipped the sponge into the wine and lifted it up to Jesus’ lips.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 17:4; PHILIPPIANS 2:8; COLOSSIANS 2:14–17"> When Jesus tasted the sour wine, he shouted, “I have finished all that I came to do!” Then he bowed his head and ◄died/handed over his spirit to God►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOHN 19:14; JOHN 19:42; MARK 15:42"> That was the day that they prepared everything for their ◄Sabbath/day of rest►. The next day was a special day of rest, because it was the day of rest during the Passover celebration. The Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; did not want the bodies of the three men to remain on the cross during their ◄Sabbath/day of rest► because leaving bodies hanging overnight would be contrary to their Jewish laws. So they went to Pilate and asked him to command that the legs of the three men on the crosses be broken &#123;the soldiers to break the legs of the three men on the crosses&#125;, so that they would die quickly. Then their bodies could be taken down and buried &#123;someone could take down their bodies and bury them&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOHN 19:18; LUKE 23:39–43"> So, after Pilate agreed, the soldiers went and broke the legs of the first man whom they had nailed on a cross near Jesus. Then they broke the legs of the second man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But when they came to Jesus, they saw that he was dead already. So they did not break his legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 JOHN 5:6; ZECHARIAH 12:10; ZECHARIAH 13:1"> Instead, one of the soldiers pierced Jesus’ side with a spear to make sure that Jesus was dead. Immediately blood clots and other liquid flowed out, which showed that Jesus was really dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOHN 21:24; HEBREWS 2:3–4; 1 JOHN 1:1–3"> I, John, saw this myself, and what I am writing is true. I know that I am telling the truth, and I am saying this in order that you may believe in (OR, my testimony about) Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 34:20; NUMBERS 9:12; EXODUS 12:46"> These things happened in order that these words would be fulfilled &#123;to fulfill these words&#125; that are written in Scripture: “Not one of his bones will be broken &#123;No one will break any of his bones&#125;.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 12:10; REVELATION 1:7; PSALMS 22:16–17"> And they fulfilled another Scripture passage that has these words: ‘They will look on the one whom they have pierced’.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph and others put Jesus’ body in a cave.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MARK 15:42–47; MATTHEW 27:57–61; PROVERBS 29:25"> Later, Joseph, from Arimathea town, went to Pilate and asked Pilate to allow him to take Jesus’ body down from the cross. Joseph was a disciple of Jesus, but he did not tell anyone that, because he was afraid of the other Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93;. Pilate permitted him to take Jesus’ body, so he went, along with others, and they took Jesus’ body down from the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOHN 7:50–52; SONG OF SONGS 4:14; JOHN 3:1–21"> Nicodemus was one of them. He was the man who previously went to visit Jesus at night. Nicodemus bought an expensive mixture of myrrh and aloe spices to put on the body. It weighed about ◄75 pounds/35 kilograms►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JOHN 11:44; JOHN 20:5–7; ACTS 5:6"> They took the body of Jesus and wrapped strips of linen cloth around it, putting the spices in with the strips of cloth. They did this according to the Jewish customs about burying bodies in tombs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKE 23:53; MATTHEW 27:60; JOHN 20:15"> Close to the place where Jesus was crucified &#123;where they nailed Jesus to the cross&#125; there was a grove of trees, and at the edge of that grove was a new burial cave. Nobody had ever been put in that cave previously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JOHN 19:31; JOHN 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> The Jewish day of rest would start at sunset, and they had to finish burying his body before then. So, since that cave was nearby, they laid Jesus’ body there and rolled a huge stone in front of the entrance.</span>
