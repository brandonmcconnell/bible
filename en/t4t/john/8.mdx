## Jesus evaded a trap about condemning a woman who had been caught committing adultery.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:1; MARK 11:1; LUKE 19:37"> But Jesus went with us disciples to Olive Tree Hill and we stayed near there that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:55; LUKE 4:20; LUKE 21:37"> Early the next morning, we returned to the Temple courtyard. Many people gathered around Jesus, so he sat down to teach them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then men who taught the Jewish laws and some of &#91;SYN&#93; the Pharisee religious group brought a woman to him. She was seized &#123;They had arranged to seize her&#125; while she was having sex with a man who was not her husband. They made her stand up in front of the group that was listening to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then they said to Jesus, “Teacher, this woman was seized &#123;we seized this woman&#125; while she was having sex with a man who is not her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:10; DEUTERONOMY 22:21–24; EZEKIEL 16:38–40"> Moses commanded us in the laws that he gave us that we should throw stones at such women and kill them. So what do you say we should do?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:13; LUKE 10:25; ECCLESIASTES 3:7"> They asked this question as a trap so that they could accuse him. If he said that they should not kill her, they would shame him for disobeying the law of Moses. If he said that they should kill her, they could accuse him to the Roman governor.<br />But Jesus bent down and wrote something on the ground with his finger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:1–5; ROMANS 2:21–25; ROMANS 2:1–3"> While they continued to question him, he stood up and said to them, “Whichever one of you has never sinned can be the first one to start throwing stones at her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then he stooped down and wrote some more on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:22; ROMANS 2:15; 1 JOHN 3:20"> After they heard what he said, those who were questioning him went away, one by one, the older ones first and then the younger ones, knowing they were all sinners. Finally only Jesus was there, along with the woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> Jesus stood up and asked her, “Woman, where are they? Has no one said you must die for your sin?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 3:17; JOHN 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> She said, “No, sir, no one.” Then Jesus said, “I do not condemn you either. Go home now, and do not continue your sinful life any longer!”&#93;</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus replied to the Pharisees’ objection to his claim to be like a light for the people of the world.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 12:46; JOHN 9:5; JOHN 12:35"> Jesus spoke to the people again. He said, “I am like a light &#91;MET&#93; for all the people in &#91;MTY&#93; the world. Just like a light reveals what has been in the darkness &#91;MET&#93;, I reveal God’s truth to them. Those who walk in the darkness are unaware of what is around them. But those who become my disciples will always be aware of God’s truth &#91;MET&#93;. They will have my light which shows them how to have eternal life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 5:31–47"> So the Pharisees said to him, “You are just telling about yourself! Since there is no one else to verify these things that you say about yourself, we do not need to accept that what you say is true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 16:28; JOHN 13:3; JOHN 8:42"> Jesus replied, “Even if I were the only one to say these things about myself, what I say is true, because I know that I came from heaven, and I know that I am going back to heaven. But you do not know where I came from or where I am going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:7; JOHN 7:24; JOHN 12:47"> You judge people according to human standards. The reason that I am here is not ◄to condemn people/to say that I will punish people► for their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ACTS 17:31; JOHN 8:29; JOHN 16:32"> But if I did judge people, what I decided would be correct, because I am not the only one who will decide those things. I and the one who sent me will both decide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:15; DEUTERONOMY 17:6; HEBREWS 10:28"> It is written &#123;Moses wrote&#125; in your law that if at least two people testify that some event has happened, people should consider that what they say is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 8:38; JOHN 8:58; JOHN 8:25"> I am telling you about myself, and the other one who is telling you about me is my Father who sent me. So you should believe that what we declare is true.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 16:3; JOHN 8:54–55; JOHN 14:6–9"> Then they asked him, “If you have a father whom we can question about you, where is he?” Jesus replied, “You do not know who I really am. If you knew who I really am, you would know who my Father is also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 7:30; JOHN 7:8; MARK 12:41"> He said these things when he was in the Temple courtyard, near the place where the people put their offerings. This was also very close to the place where the Jewish council met. But no one seized him to arrest him, because it was not yet time &#91;MTY&#93; for him to suffer and die.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus replied to the Pharisees’ continued rejection of Jesus’ claims about himself.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOHN 7:34; JOHN 8:24; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:17–18"> Jesus also said to them, “I will soon be going away. Then at the end of your life you will seek me, but you will die without God forgiving your sins. Where I will go, you will not be able to come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 8:52; PSALMS 22:6; JOHN 8:48"> So the Jewish leaders said among themselves, “Is he going to kill himself? Is that the reason that he said, ‘Where I go, you will not be able to come’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 3:31; JOHN 17:14; JOHN 17:16"> But Jesus continued by saying to them, “You were born here in this world, but I came from heaven. You belong to those who are opposed to God &#91;MTY&#93;. I do not belong to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 3:36; MARK 16:16; JOHN 3:18"> I told you that you will die without God forgiving your sins. If you do not believe that I am who I say that I am, you will die without God forgiving your sins.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 5:17–29; LUKE 22:67; JOHN 1:22"> So they said to him, “You! Who do you think you are?” Jesus said to them, “Ever since I began teaching, I have been telling you who I am!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 15:15; JOHN 12:47–50; JOHN 8:40"> I could judge you and say that you are guilty of many things. But instead, I will say only what the one who sent me tells me to say. What he says is true, and I tell the people in the world &#91;MTY&#93; what I have heard from him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; JOHN 8:43; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:3–4"> They did not understand that he was talking about his Father in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 5:19; JOHN 5:30; JOHN 6:38"> So Jesus said, “I am the one who came down from heaven, but when you lift me up on a cross to kill me, you will know who I am. You will also know that I do not do anything with my own authority. Instead, I say just what my Father has taught me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 6:38; JOHN 16:32; JOHN 4:34"> He is the one who sent me, and he helps me. Because I always do the things that please him, he has never abandoned me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 7:31; JOHN 2:23; JOHN 11:45"> As Jesus was saying these things, many people believed that he was ◄from God/the Messiah►.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus told his critics which people were true believers like Abraham and which ones really were serving Satan.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 JOHN 1:9; JOHN 15:4–9; 2 TIMOTHY 3:14"> Then Jesus said to the Jews who now said they believed in him, “If you continue to live in accordance with my message, you will truly be my disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ROMANS 8:2; JOHN 8:36; JOHN 17:17"> Then you will know God’s truth, and as a result of your believing his truth, he &#91;PRS&#93; will free you from being controlled by the one who has made you his slaves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:9; JOHN 8:39; GENESIS 15:13"> They replied to him, “We are descendants of Abraham. We have never been anyone’s slaves. So why do you say we will be freed from being someone’s slaves?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ROMANS 6:16; 2 PETER 2:19; PROVERBS 5:22"> Jesus replied, “Listen carefully to what I am going to tell you. All those who continue to sin are forced to obey their sinful desires &#91;MET&#93;, just like a slave is forced to obey his master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:4–7; LUKE 15:31; ROMANS 8:15–17"> A slave is not a permanent member of a family. But a son is a member of a family forever. Similarly, you say you are members of God’s family because you are descendants of Abraham, but really, because you are like slaves of your sinful desires, you are no longer permanent members of God’s family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 3:17; GALATIANS 5:1; ROMANS 8:2"> So if you allow me to free you, you will truly be free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 2:14; JOHN 8:45–47; JOHN 7:1"> I know that you are Abraham’s descendants. But you are trying to kill me because you are not allowing &#91;PRS&#93; my message to continue to change your inner being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 5:19; JOHN 8:44; JOHN 8:41"> I am telling you what I saw when I was with my Father. But you do the things that you have heard from your father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:7; JOHN 8:37; ROMANS 9:7"> They replied to him, “Abraham is our ancestor.” Jesus said to them, “If you were Abraham’s descendants, your character would be like Abraham’s character, and you would do good things like Abraham did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JOHN 8:26; GALATIANS 4:29; GALATIANS 4:16"> I have been telling you the truth that I heard from God, but you are trying to kill me. Abraham did not do things like that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; JOHN 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> No! You are doing the things that your real father does.”<br />They said to him, “We do not know about you, but we are not illegitimate children. And spiritually, we have only one Father. That is God, and you do not know who your father is!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JOHN 12:49; JOHN 14:10; JOHN 17:8"> Jesus said to them, “If God were your father, you would love me, because I came from God, and now I have come here to this world. My coming was not ◄because I appointed myself/with my own authority►. He sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="JOHN 12:39–40; ROMANS 8:7–8; JOHN 7:17"> ◄And I will tell you why you do not understand what I say./Do you know why you do not understand what I say?► &#91;RHQ&#93; It is because you do not want to accept my message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="ACTS 13:10; 1 JOHN 3:12; 1 JOHN 3:8–10"> You belong to your father, ◄the devil/Satan►, and you desire to do what he wants. He has caused people to become murderers from the time when people first sinned. He has abandoned God’s truth because he is a liar by his nature. Whenever he lies, he is speaking according to his nature, because he is a liar and is the one who originates &#91;MET&#93; all lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:3–4; GALATIANS 4:16; JOHN 18:37"> But because I tell you the truth, you do not believe me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:15; JOHN 16:8; 1 PETER 2:22"> Since I have never sinned, ◄none of you can show that I have sinned./can anyone among you show that I have sinned?► &#91;RHQ&#93; So, since I tell you the truth, ◄there is no good reason for your not believing me!/why is it that you do not believe me?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JOHN 10:26–27; 2 JOHN 1:9; JOHN 18:37"> Those who belong to God habitually obey God’s message. You do not belong to God, so you do not obey his message.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="JOHN 7:20; JOHN 8:52; MATTHEW 10:5"> The Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; replied to him, “◄We are certainly right by saying that you believe what is false as the Samaritans do!/Are we not right by saying that you believe what is false as the Samaritans do?► &#91;RHQ&#93; And that ◄a demon/an evil spirit► controls you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 PETER 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; JOHN 13:31–32"> Jesus replied, “A demon does not control me! I honor my Father, and you do not honor me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="JOHN 5:41; JOHN 8:54; JOHN 7:18"> I am not trying to honor myself. There is someone else who desires to honor me, and he is the one who will judge whether it is I who am telling the truth or whether it is you who are telling the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="JOHN 11:25–26; JOHN 5:24; JOHN 3:15–16"> But the truth is that anyone who obeys what I say will never die!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="JOHN 9:24; JOHN 8:48; HEBREWS 2:9"> Then the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93;, thinking that he was talking about ordinary death and not about spiritual death, said to him, “Now we are sure that a demon controls you! Abraham and the prophets died long ago! But you say that anyone who obeys what you teach will never die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="JOHN 4:12; ROMANS 9:5; HEBREWS 7:1–7"> ◄You are certainly not greater than our ancestor Abraham!/Do you think you are greater than our ancestor Abraham?► &#91;RHQ&#93; He died, and all the prophets died, so who do you think you are by saying something like that?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="JOHN 8:50; PROVERBS 25:27; 2 PETER 1:17"> Jesus replied, “If I were honoring myself, that would be worthless. My Father is the one who you say is your God. He is the one who honors me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="JOHN 8:19; JOHN 8:44; JOHN 7:28–29"> Although you do not know him, I know him and have a close relationship with him. If I said that I did not know him, I would be a liar like you are. But I know him, and I obey what he says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:13; GENESIS 22:18; GALATIANS 3:7–9"> Your ancestor Abraham was happy when he thought about what I would do during my life &#91;MTY&#93;. It was as though he saw that, and was happy.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Then the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; said to him, “You are not 50 years old yet! So ◄you certainly did not see him!/how could you have seen him?► &#91;RHQ&#93; Because he died long ago!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="EXODUS 3:14; COLOSSIANS 1:17; JOHN 17:5"> Jesus said to them, “The truth is that I existed before Abraham was born!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="JOHN 11:8; JOHN 10:30–33; JOHN 10:39–40"> So, because they were very angry about Jesus thus saying that he ◄had eternally existed/was equal with God►, they picked up stones to throw at him to kill him. But Jesus caused them not to be able to see him, and he left the Temple courtyard.</span>
</div>
