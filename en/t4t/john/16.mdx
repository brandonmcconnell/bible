## Jesus told them some things that the Holy Spirit would do.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 13:36; JOHN 7:33; JOHN 16:28"> “Now I am about to return to the one who sent me. But I am disappointed that none of you is asking me, ‘Where are you going?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 14:1; JOHN 16:20–22; LUKE 24:17"> Instead, because I have told you these things, you are very sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 14:26; JOHN 14:16–17; JOHN 15:26"> But the truth is that it is good for you that I am going away, because if I do not go away, the Holy Spirit, who will ◄encourage/be like a legal counsel for► you will not come to you. But when I go, I will send him to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ACTS 2:37; JUDE 1:15; ZECHARIAH 12:10"> When he comes, he will prove that those who do not belong to God &#91;MTY&#93; are wrong about what is sinful and about who is really righteous and about whom God will judge and condemn for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 15:22–25; JOHN 8:23–24; JOHN 12:47–48"> He will tell people that their greatest sin is that they do not believe in me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:21; 1 PETER 3:18; ROMANS 10:3–4"> He will tell people that because I am going back to my Father, and you will no longer see me, you will know that I am the one who was truly righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 12:31; 1 JOHN 3:8; HEBREWS 2:14"> He will tell people that the fact that God has already determined that he will punish Satan, the one who rules this world, shows that some day God will also punish those who do not belong to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARK 4:33; JOHN 15:15; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:1–2"> I have many more things that I would like to tell you, but you are not able to accept them now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 14:26; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:10–13; JOHN 14:17"> But God’s Spirit is the one who will teach you God’s truth. When he comes, he will guide you so that you understand all spiritual truth. He will not speak from his own authority. Instead, it is the things that he hears my Father say that he will tell you. He will also tell you about things that will happen later.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 JOHN 4:13–14; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:14–18; JOHN 15:26"> He will honor me by revealing my truth to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 17:10; MATTHEW 11:27; JOHN 3:35"> Everything that my Father has is mine. That is why I said that the Spirit is able to reveal my truth to you.”</span>
</div>

## Jesus told them that after he left they would be sad, but that they would later be joyful when they saw him again.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 7:33; ACTS 1:3; JOHN 14:18–24"> “After a short time I will leave you, and you will not see me. Then a short time after that you will see me again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 16:16; MARK 9:32; JOHN 16:5"> So some of us said to each other, “What does he mean by saying ‘After a short time you will not see me,’ and ‘A short time after that you will see me again’? And what does he mean by ‘Because I am going back to my Father’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 24:25; MATTHEW 16:9–11; HEBREWS 5:12"> We kept asking each other, “What does he mean by saying ‘After a little while’? We do not understand what he is saying.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 16:30; JOHN 16:16; JOHN 14:19"> Jesus realized that we wanted to ask him about that. So he said to us, “You are asking &#91;RHQ&#93; each other what I meant when I said, ‘After a short time you will not see me, and then a short time after that you will see me again.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JAMES 1:2; MATTHEW 5:4; PSALMS 126:5–6"> Listen to this carefully: After I ◄leave you/die►, those who oppose God &#91;MTY&#93; will be happy, but you will be sad. But later you will stop being sad and you will become joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 113:9; Isaiah 13:8; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:3"> A woman who is about to bear a child feels pain, because that is what happens &#91;MTY&#93; at that time. But after her baby is born, she forgets that pain, because she is very joyful that her child has been born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 PETER 1:8; ACTS 13:52; JOHN 16:6"> It will be the same with you. I will soon die and you will be sad. But after that, I will see you again. Then you will be joyful, and no one will be able to stop you from being joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 15:7; JOHN 14:13–14; MATTHEW 21:22"> When that happens, you will not ask me any questions about anything. Listen to this carefully: After that happens, my Father will do for you anything you ask, because of his relationship with me &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:7–8; JAMES 4:2–3; JOHN 15:11"> Up to the present time, using my authority you have not asked God to do anything for you &#91;MTY&#93;. Now keep asking him for things that you need. If you do that, you will receive them, and then you will be completely joyful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 16:12; MARK 4:13; MATTHEW 13:34–35"> Although I have been speaking these things using figurative language, there will soon be a time when I will no longer use that kind of language. Instead, I will tell you plainly ◄about my Father/what my Father wants►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ROMANS 8:34; JOHN 17:24; JOHN 17:19"> At that time, you will ask him for things ◄because you belong to me/with my authority► &#91;MTY&#93;. I will not need to ask my Father to do what you ask.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOHN 14:21; JOHN 14:23; JOHN 17:23"> My Father himself loves you because you have loved me and because you have believed that I came from God my Father, so he wants you to ask him (OR, so he does not need anyone to persuade him to help you)</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 13:1; JOHN 13:3; JOHN 8:14"> I came from my Father into this world. Very soon I will be leaving this world and going back to my Father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 16:25"> Then we, his disciples, said, “Now you are speaking plainly, without using figurative language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 21:17; JOHN 17:8; JOHN 16:17"> Now we understand that you know everything. You do not need anyone to ask you questions about anything, because you know what we want to ask before we ask you. That also leads us to believe that you came from God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKE 9:44–45; JOHN 13:38"> Jesus replied, “Now you &#91;RHQ&#93; say that you believe that I came from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:7; MATTHEW 26:31; JOHN 8:29"> But listen! There will soon be a time, and that time is already here, when you will all run away! Each of you will run away to your own home. You will leave me, and I will be alone. But I will not really be alone at that time, because my Father is always with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 14:27; 1 JOHN 5:4; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:16"> I have told you these things in order that you may have inner peace because of your relationship with me. In this world you will have trouble. But be courageous! I have defeated those who are opposed to me &#91;MTY&#93;, and you can defeat them, too!”</span>
</div>
