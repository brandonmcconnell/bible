## Moses urged the people to obey God’s laws

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ROMANS 10:5; EZEKIEL 20:11; LEVITICUS 18:5"> “Now, you Israeli people, obey all the rules and regulations &#91;DOU&#93; that I will teach you. If you do that, you will remain alive and you will enter and occupy/capture the land that Yahweh, the God whom your ancestors worshiped, is giving to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:32; PROVERBS 30:6; REVELATION 22:18–19"> Do not add anything to what I command you, and do not take anything away from what I tell you. Obey all the commands of Yahweh our God that I am giving to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 25:1–9; PSALMS 106:28–29; JOSHUA 22:17"> “You have seen what Yahweh did at Baal-Peor Mountain. He got rid of all the people who worshiped the god Baal there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:20; JOSHUA 23:8; JOHN 6:67–69"> but you who faithfully continued to worship Yahweh our God are still alive today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:20; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:1–2; HEBREWS 3:5"> “Note that I have taught you all the rules and regulations, just like Yahweh our God told me to do. He wants you to obey them when you are living in the land that you are about to enter and occupy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 28:28; PSALMS 111:10; 2 TIMOTHY 3:15"> Obey them faithfully because, if you do that, you will show the people of other nations that you are very wise. When they hear about all these laws, they will say, ‘The people of this great nation of Israel are certainly very wise &#91;DOU&#93;!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 145:18; 2 SAMUEL 7:23; PSALMS 34:18"> Even if other nations are great, there is none &#91;RHQ&#93; of them that has a god who is as near to them as Yahweh our God is to us! Whenever we call out to him to help us, he answers/helps us</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 147:19–20; ROMANS 7:12–14; PSALMS 119:127–128"> And there is no &#91;RHQ&#93; other nation, even if it is a great nation, that has laws that are as just/fair as the laws that I am telling to you today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:7; DEUTERONOMY 11:19; PSALMS 78:3–8"> “But be very careful! &#91;DOU, IDM&#93; Do not forget what you &#91;SYN&#93; have seen God do. Remember those things as long as you are alive. Tell them to your children and your grandchildren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 19:9; HEBREWS 12:18–19; EXODUS 19:16"> Tell them about the day that your ancestors stood in the presence of Yahweh our God at Sinai Mountain, when he said to me, ‘Gather the people together, in order that they can hear what I say. I want them to learn to have an awesome respect for me as long as they are alive, and I want them to teach their children to do that also.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:23; EXODUS 19:16–18; HEBREWS 12:18–19"> Tell them that your ancestors came near the bottom of the mountain, while the mountain burned with a fire that went up to the sky, and the mountain was covered with dark clouds and black smoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:15; DEUTERONOMY 5:4; DEUTERONOMY 5:22"> Then Yahweh spoke to your ancestors out of the middle of the fire. Your ancestors heard him speak, but they did not see him. They only heard his voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 34:28; EXODUS 31:18; DEUTERONOMY 9:9–11"> And he declared to them his agreement. That agreement contained the Ten Commandments that he wants you to obey. He wrote those on two stone tablets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 105:44–45; EZEKIEL 21:1–23"> Yahweh commanded me to teach all the rules and regulations to you, in order that you would obey them in the land that you are about to enter and occupy.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses warned them about worshiping idols

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:12; Isaiah 40:18; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:4–6"> “On the day that Yahweh spoke to your ancestors at Sinai Mountain, he was invisible. So, be careful</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ACTS 17:29; DEUTERONOMY 5:8; EXODUS 20:4–5"> that you do not sin by making for yourselves any idol of something that can be seen! Do not make an idol that resembles any person, either a man or a woman,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 1:23"> or that resembles any animal or any bird</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> or any ◄reptile/creature that scurries across the ground► or any fish in the deep ocean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ROMANS 1:25; DEUTERONOMY 17:3; 2 KINGS 21:3"> And be careful to not look up toward the sky and be tempted to worship anything that you see there—the sun or the moon or the stars. Yahweh our God has given those to be a blessing to all people everywhere, but you must not worship them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:51; JEREMIAH 11:4; EXODUS 19:5–6"> Yahweh has brought your ancestors out of Egypt, where they were suffering as though they were in a blazing furnace, in order that they would be people who belong to him, which is what you are today.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:37; NUMBERS 20:12; PSALMS 106:32–33"> “But Yahweh was angry with me because of what your ancestors made me do. He vowed that I would not cross the Jordan River to enter the good/fertile land that he is giving to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:25; DEUTERONOMY 3:27; 2 PETER 1:13–15"> He said that I must die here in this land and never cross the Jordan River. But you will go across it, and you will occupy that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:9; HEBREWS 3:12; JOSHUA 23:16"> Be sure that you do not forget the agreement that Yahweh our God made with you. He commanded you to not make any kind of idol, because he has forbidden that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:29; NAHUM 1:6; DEUTERONOMY 9:3"> You must not do that because Yahweh your God will destroy anyone who worships idols, like &#91;MET&#93; a fire completely destroys things. He wants people to worship only him, he ◄cannot accept/will punish► people who worship anyone or anything else.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:16; HOSEA 9:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:22"> “When you have been in the land of Canaan for a long time and you have children and grandchildren, do not sin by making an idol that represents anything at all, because Yahweh says that is evil, and if you do that, you will cause him to become angry with you and punish you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:1; MICAH 6:2; JEREMIAH 22:29"> Today I am requesting everyone who is in heaven and everyone who is on the earth &#91;MTY&#93; to watch what you are doing. If you disobey what I am telling you, you will soon all die in the land that you will be crossing the Jordan River to occupy. You will not live very long there; Yahweh will completely get rid of many &#91;HYP&#93; of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:62–64; LEVITICUS 26:33; EZEKIEL 12:15"> And the rest of you, Yahweh will force you to go and live among the people of many other nations. Only a few of you will ◄survive/remain alive► there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:64; JEREMIAH 16:13; DEUTERONOMY 28:36"> When you are in those nations, you will worship gods that are made of wood and stone, gods made by humans, gods that cannot see anything or hear anything or eat anything or smell anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 119:2; JEREMIAH 29:12–14; 2 CHRONICLES 15:4"> But while you are there, if you will try to know Yahweh your God, and if you try with your entire inner being to know him, he will ◄answer/reveal himself to► you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOEL 2:12–13; JEREMIAH 23:20; HOSEA 3:5"> In the future, after you have been mistreated there and all those bad things happen to you, you will again worship only Yahweh and obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:8; PSALMS 116:5; Nehemiah 9:31"> Yahweh is a God who acts mercifully. If you continue to obey him, he will not abandon you or destroy you or forget the agreement that he solemnly made with your ancestors.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Only Yahweh is God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOB 8:8; MATTHEW 24:31; PSALMS 44:1"> “Now think about the past, about the time before you were born, about all the time since God first created people here on the earth. You could search everywhere, in heaven and on the earth. ◄Has anything like this ever happened that is as great as what Yahweh did for the Israeli people?/Certainly nothing has ever happened that is as great as what Yahweh did for the Israeli people.► &#91;RHQ&#93; ◄Has anyone ever heard of anything like this?/Certainly no one has ever heard of anything like this.► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JUDGES 6:22; DEUTERONOMY 5:24; EXODUS 33:20"> ◄Has any group ever remained alive after they heard a god speak to them from the middle of a fire, like we did?/Certainly no group has ever remained alive after they heard a god speak to them from the middle of a fire, like we did.► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 26:8; DEUTERONOMY 5:15; EXODUS 6:6"> Certainly no other god has ever tried to take a huge group of people from one nation to another location, like he did for us when he brought us out of Egypt. We saw Yahweh our God use great power &#91;MTY, DOU&#93; when he performed many kinds of &#91;DOU&#93; miracles, and sent plagues, and did many other very terrifying things in Egypt for us, and rescued us when the army of Egypt tried to fight against us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 45:18; 1 SAMUEL 2:2; Isaiah 45:5"> “Yahweh showed all these things to you, in order that you would know that only he is truly God, and that there is no other God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EXODUS 19:9; EXODUS 19:19; DEUTERONOMY 4:33"> He allowed your ancestors to hear him speak from heaven in order that he could discipline them. Here on the earth he allowed them to see his great fire on Sinai Mountain, and he spoke to them from the middle of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:15; Isaiah 63:9; EXODUS 13:9"> Because he loved our ancestors, he chose you Israelis who are their descendants, and by his great power he brought your ancestors out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:1; DEUTERONOMY 9:1–5; EXODUS 23:27–28"> As they advanced, he expelled the people of nations that were greater and more powerful than they were, in order that he could allow them to capture their land and cause it to become yours, which is what is happening now.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:11; DEUTERONOMY 4:35; 1 CHRONICLES 29:11"> “So today you should ◄meditate on/think about► the fact that Yahweh is God, that he is/rules in heaven and also on the earth, and that there is no other god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:3; EPHESIANS 6:3; DEUTERONOMY 12:28"> Obey all the rules and regulations that I am giving to you today, in order that things will go well for you and for your descendants, and that you will live a long time in the land that Yahweh our God is giving to you, to belong to you forever.”</span>
</div>
