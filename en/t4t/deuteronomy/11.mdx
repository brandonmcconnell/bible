## Moses reminded them of the great things that God did

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:5; DEUTERONOMY 10:12; LUKE 1:74–75"> “Because of all that Yahweh your God has done for you, you must love him and continually obey all his rules and regulations and commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:2–5; DEUTERONOMY 29:10; ACTS 26:22"> It was you and your ancestors, not your children, whom he disciplined by causing all of you to have/experience difficulties. So, starting today, continue to think about how very great and very powerful he is &#91;DOU, MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:19; DEUTERONOMY 4:34; PSALMS 105:27–45"> Think about the various miracles &#91;DOU&#93; that he performed in Egypt. Think about what he did to the king of Egypt and to his country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 106:11; EXODUS 14:23–31; EXODUS 15:19"> Think about what he did to the army of Egypt, to their horses and their chariots. Think about how he caused the Red Sea to flood/cover over them while they were pursuing your ancestors, and how the army of Egypt was completely ◄wiped out/destroyed►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 77:20; PSALMS 106:12–48; PSALMS 78:14–72"> Think about what Yahweh did for your ancestors in the desert before you arrived at this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:9–10; PSALMS 106:17; NUMBERS 16:1–35"> Think about what he did to Dathan and Abiram, the two sons of Eliab from the tribe of Reuben. While all of your ancestors were watching, the earth split open, and they fell into the opening and disappeared, along with their families and their tents, their servants, and their animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 145:4–6; PSALMS 145:12; PSALMS 150:2"> You &#91;SYN&#93; and your ancestors have seen all these miracles that Yahweh performed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:10; JOSHUA 1:6–7; EPHESIANS 3:16"> So, obey all the commandments that I am giving you today, in order that you will be strong and able to cross the river and occupy the land that you are about to enter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 3:8; DEUTERONOMY 4:40; DEUTERONOMY 6:2"> and in order that you will live for a long time in that land, the land that Yahweh solemnly promised your ancestors that he would give to them and to their descendants, a land that is very fertile &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 14:18"> The land that you are about to enter and occupy is not like the land of Egypt, where your ancestors lived. In Egypt, after they planted seeds, it was necessary for them to work hard &#91;MTY&#93; to water the plants that grew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:7; DEUTERONOMY 8:7–9; PSALMS 65:12–13"> But the land that you are about to enter is a land where there are many hills and valleys, where there is plenty of rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:6; PSALMS 33:18; 1 KINGS 9:3"> Yahweh takes care of that land. He &#91;SYN&#93; watches over it every day, from the beginning of each year to the end of each year.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:12; DEUTERONOMY 4:29; DEUTERONOMY 6:17"> Today I am commanding you to love Yahweh our God and to serve him with your entire inner beings. If you do that,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JAMES 5:7; LEVITICUS 26:4; EZEKIEL 34:26"> each year he will send rain on your land at the ◄right times/times when it is needed►, ◄in the autumn/at the end of the dry season► and ◄in the spring/before the next dry season starts►. As a result, you will have grain and grapes to make wine and olives to make olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 104:14; DEUTERONOMY 6:11; JOEL 2:19"> And he will cause grass to grow in your fields for your livestock to eat. You will have all the food that you want.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 31:27; HEBREWS 3:12; HEBREWS 2:1"> “But I warn you, do not turn away from Yahweh our God and start to worship other gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:35; DEUTERONOMY 6:15; 2 CHRONICLES 6:26"> because if you do that, Yahweh will become very angry with you. He will prevent any rain from falling. As a result, the crops will not grow, and you will soon die from hunger in the good land that Yahweh is about to give to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:16; DEUTERONOMY 6:6–9; PROVERBS 3:1"> So, keep thinking &#91;IDM&#93; about these commands. Write them on tiny scrolls and fasten them to your arms/wrists, and write them on bands that you fasten to your foreheads to help you to remember them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:7; Isaiah 38:19; DEUTERONOMY 4:9–10"> Teach them to your children again and again. Talk about them all the time: When you are in your houses and when you are walking outside; talk about them when you are lying down at night and when you are getting up in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:9"> Write them on the doorposts and on the gates of your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 4:10; PROVERBS 3:2; PROVERBS 9:11"> Do that in order that you and your children will live for a long time in the land that Yahweh promised to our ancestors that he would give to them. That land will belong to you and your descendants as long as there is a sky above the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:17; DEUTERONOMY 11:13; DEUTERONOMY 10:20"> “Faithfully continue to obey what I am commanding you to do—to love Yahweh our God, and to conduct your lives as he wants you to do, and to ◄be faithful to/have a close relationship with► him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:38; DEUTERONOMY 9:1; DEUTERONOMY 7:1–2"> If you do that, Yahweh will expel all the people-groups in that land as you advance, people-groups that are more numerous and more powerful than you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 14:9; EXODUS 23:31; GENESIS 15:18–21"> All the ground in that land on which you walk will be yours. Your territory will extend from the desert in the south to the Lebanon Mountains in the north, and from the Euphrates River in the east to the Mediterranean Sea in the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:24; EXODUS 23:27; JOSHUA 1:5"> Yahweh our God will cause all the people in that land to be afraid of you, which is what he promised, with the result that no people-group will be able to stop you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:1; DEUTERONOMY 30:15–20; GALATIANS 3:10"> “Listen carefully: Today I am telling you that Yahweh will either bless you or he will curse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; DEUTERONOMY 28:1–14; Isaiah 3:10"> If you obey the commandments of Yahweh our God that I am giving to you today, he will bless you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:15–68; ROMANS 2:8–9; GALATIANS 3:10"> If you do not obey them, and if you turn away from him to worship other gods that you have never known about before, he will curse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:12–26; JOSHUA 8:30–35"> And when Yahweh brings you into the land that you are about to enter and occupy, some of you must stand on top of Gerizim Mountain and proclaim what will cause Yahweh to bless you, and the others must stand on top of Ebal Mountain and proclaim what will cause Yahweh to curse you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 12:6; JUDGES 7:1; JOSHUA 4:19"> (Those two mountains are &#91;RHQ&#93; west of the Jordan River, west of the Jordan Valley near the huge oak tree at Moreh village in the land where the Canaan people-group lives. They live close to the sacred trees near Gilgal.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:11; DEUTERONOMY 9:1; JOSHUA 3:13–17"> “You will soon cross the Jordan River to occupy the land that Yahweh our God is giving to you. When you enter that land and start to live there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:20; DEUTERONOMY 12:32; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:1–2"> be sure to obey all the rules and regulations that I am giving to you today.”</span>
</div>
