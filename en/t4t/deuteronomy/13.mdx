## Moses warned them against idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:25–28; 1 JOHN 4:1; JEREMIAH 29:8"> “Possibly there will be people among you who say that they are prophets. They may say that they are able to interpret the meaning of dreams or perform various kinds of miracles &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:6; MATTHEW 7:22–23; REVELATION 13:13–14"> They will say those things in order to induce/persuade you to worship gods that you have never known about before. But even if what they predict happens,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:19; PSALMS 66:10"> do not pay attention to what they say. Yahweh our God will be testing you to find out if you love him with all your inner being.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:10; DEUTERONOMY 10:20; 1 CORINTHIANS 6:17"> You must conduct your lives as Yahweh our God wants you to, and you must revere him, and do what he tells you to do &#91;MTY&#93;, and worship only him, and ◄be faithful to/have a close relationship with► him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 5:13; DEUTERONOMY 18:20; JEREMIAH 14:15"> “But you must execute anyone who falsely says that he is a prophet, or someone who falsely says that he can interpret dreams, or who tells you to rebel against Yahweh our God, who rescued your ancestors from being slaves in Egypt. People like that are only wanting to cause you to stop conducting your lives as Yahweh has commanded you to do. Execute them, to get rid of this evil among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:2–7; 2 PETER 2:1; EPHESIANS 4:14"> It does not matter if even your brother or your daughter or your wife or some close/dear friend secretly urges you, saying ‘Let’s worship other gods. They are gods which you or your ancestors have never known about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Some of them may encourage you to worship gods that people-groups that live near you worship, or gods that groups who live far away worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:13; PROVERBS 1:10; DEUTERONOMY 7:16"> Do not &#91;SYN&#93; do what they suggest. Do not even listen to them. Do not even be merciful to them, and do not keep secret what they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ACTS 7:58; LUKE 14:26; MATTHEW 10:37"> Execute them! You who are their relative must be the first one to throw stones at them to kill them, and then let everyone else &#91;MTY&#93; throw stones at them, too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:25; LEVITICUS 24:14–16; EXODUS 20:2"> Kill such people by throwing stones at them, because they are trying to cause you to stop worshiping Yahweh our God, who rescued your ancestors from being slaves in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:20; DEUTERONOMY 17:13; 1 TIMOTHY 5:20"> When they are executed, all the Israeli people will hear what happened, and they will become afraid, and none of them will do such an evil thing again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JUDGES 20:1–17; JOSHUA 22:11–34"> “When you are living in one of the towns in the land that Yahweh our God is giving to you, you may hear</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 JOHN 2:19; DEUTERONOMY 13:2; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:15"> that some worthless people there among you &#91;IDM&#93; are deceiving the people of their town, saying, ‘Let’s go and worship other gods,’ but they are gods that you have never heard about before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:4; Isaiah 11:3–4; JOHN 7:24"> Investigate it thoroughly/carefully. If you find out that it is true that such a disgraceful thing has happened,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 22:20; LEVITICUS 27:28; DEUTERONOMY 7:2"> kill all the people in that town. And kill all their livestock, too. Destroy the town completely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:2; JOSHUA 8:28; Isaiah 25:2"> Gather all the possessions that belonged to the people who lived there and pile them up in the city plaza. Then burn the town and everything in it, as though it were an offering to Yahweh that would be completely burned on the altar. The ruins/ashes must stay there forever; the town must never be rebuilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 26:4; DEUTERONOMY 7:26; JOSHUA 7:26"> None of you Israelis must take for yourselves &#91;IDM&#93; anything that Yahweh has said must be destroyed. But if you do what I say, Yahweh will stop being angry with you, and he will act mercifully toward you. And he will cause you to have many children/descendants, which is what he promised our ancestors that he would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:28; MATTHEW 7:21; DEUTERONOMY 12:25"> Yahweh our God will do all those things if you do what he is telling you to do, and if you obey all the commandments that I am giving to you today and do what Yahweh says is right for you to do.”</span>
