## The reason that God will enable them to be victorious

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:31; DEUTERONOMY 4:38; DEUTERONOMY 1:28"> “You people of Israel, listen to me! You will soon &#91;HYP&#93; cross the Jordan River. In the land that you will enter, there are large cities that have very high walls around them that seem to &#91;HYP&#93; extend up to the sky. There are people-groups in that land that are more numerous and more powerful than you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:22; NAHUM 1:6; DEUTERONOMY 7:24"> Those people are very tall and strong. Some of them are giants who are descendants of Anak. You know about them, and you have heard people say that no one &#91;RHQ&#93; can defeat the descendants of Anak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:24; HEBREWS 12:29; ROMANS 8:31"> But I want you to know that Yahweh our God will go ahead of you. He will be like a raging/roaring fire. While you advance, he will defeat and destroy them. As a result, you will quickly be able to expel some of them and kill the others, which is what Yahweh promised that you would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:17; DEUTERONOMY 9:5; DEUTERONOMY 18:9–14"> “After Yahweh our God has expelled them for you, do not say to yourselves, ‘It is because we are righteous that Yahweh has enabled us to capture this land.’ The truth is that it is because the people in that land are wicked that Yahweh will expel them as you advance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="TITUS 3:5; GENESIS 17:8; GENESIS 26:4"> I say again that it is not because you are righteous or because you do things that are righteous that you will enter and capture that land. It is because those people-groups are very wicked that Yahweh our God will expel them as you advance, and because he intends to do what he vowed to your ancestors Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob that he would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:16; ACTS 7:51; DEUTERONOMY 31:27"> I want you to know that it is not because you are righteous that Yahweh our God is giving you this good/fertile land. I say that because you are not righteous; you are a very stubborn &#91;IDM&#93; people.”</span>
</div>

## Moses reminded them of the golden calf

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 16:2; EXODUS 14:11; NUMBERS 21:5"> “Never forget what your ancestors did in the desert that caused Yahweh our God to become angry. From the day that we left Egypt until the day that we arrived here, you and your ancestors have continually rebelled against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 106:19–22; EXODUS 32:16; EXODUS 32:7–10"> Even at Sinai Mountain your ancestors caused Yahweh to become angry. Because he was very angry, he was ready to get rid of all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 34:28; EXODUS 24:18; 1 KINGS 19:8"> When I climbed the mountain to receive from him the stone tablets on which he had written the Ten Commandments that he was giving to them, I stayed there 40 days and nights, and during that time I did not eat or drink anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 31:18; HEBREWS 8:10; DEUTERONOMY 10:4"> Yahweh gave me the two stone tablets on which he had written the commandments with his own fingers. They were the words/commandments that he had spoken to your ancestors from the fire on that mountain, when they were gathered together at the bottom of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:9; NUMBERS 10:33; HEBREWS 8:6–10"> “At the end of those 40 days and nights, Yahweh gave me those two stone tablets on which he had written those commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 32:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 31:29; JUDGES 2:17"> But then he said to me, ‘Go down the mountain immediately, because the people whom you are leading, the people whom you led out of Egypt, have committed a terrible sin! They have very quickly done what I commanded them not to do. They have made for themselves a metal statue of a calf to worship.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:6; 2 KINGS 17:14; DEUTERONOMY 10:16"> Then Yahweh said to me, ‘I have been watching these people, and I see that they are very stubborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:20; EXODUS 32:10–13; PSALMS 9:5"> So do not try to stop me. I am going to destroy all of them, with the result that no one anywhere will remember ◄their names &#91;MTY&#93;/that they ever existed►. Then I will enable you to become the ancestor of a nation that will be more numerous and more powerful than they are.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 19:18; DEUTERONOMY 5:23; DEUTERONOMY 4:11"> So I turned and went down the mountain, carrying in my hands the two stone tablets on which the Ten Commandments were written. Fire was burning all over the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 32:19; ACTS 7:40–41"> I looked, and I was shocked to see that your ancestors had committed a great sin against Yahweh. They very quickly had begun to do what Yahweh our God had commanded them not to do. They had requested Aaron to make for them a metal image of a calf to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So while they were watching, I lifted up those two stone tablets and threw them on the ground, and they broke into pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 34:28; DEUTERONOMY 9:9; PSALMS 106:23"> Then I ◄prostrated myself/lay► on the ground in Yahweh’s presence as I had done before, and I did not eat or drink anything for 40 days and nights. I did that because your ancestors had sinned against Yahweh and caused him to become very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:10; EXODUS 32:14; JAMES 5:16–17"> I was afraid/worried that because Yahweh was very angry with them, he would get rid of all of them. But again I prayed that he would not do that, and again he listened to me and answered my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 32:35; EXODUS 32:21; HEBREWS 7:26–28"> Yahweh was also very angry with Aaron for making that golden calf and was ready to kill Aaron. But at the same time I prayed for Aaron also, and Yahweh answered my prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 32:20; Isaiah 31:7; Isaiah 2:18–21"> Your ancestors had sinned by requesting Aaron to make a metal statue of a calf. So I took that statue and I melted it in a fire and crushed it and ground it into very tiny pieces &#91;HYP&#93;. Then I threw those tiny pieces into the stream that flowed down the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:34; EXODUS 17:7; NUMBERS 11:1–5"> Your ancestors also caused Yahweh to become very angry by what they did at places which they named Taberah, Massah, and Kibroth-Hattaavah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 106:24–25; HEBREWS 4:2; PSALMS 78:22"> And when we were at Kadesh-Barnea, Yahweh said to your ancestors, ‘Go up and capture the land that I am about to give to you!’ But they rebelled against him. They did not trust him, and they did not obey what he told &#91;MTY&#93; them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:27; ACTS 7:51; DEUTERONOMY 9:6–7"> Your ancestors rebelled against Yahweh from the first day that I knew them, in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:18; DEUTERONOMY 9:16"> So, as I said, I lay on the ground in Yahweh’s presence for 40 days and nights, because Yahweh had said that he would get rid of your ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 32:11–13; TITUS 2:14; PSALMS 107:2"> And I prayed to Yahweh, saying, ‘Lord God, these people belong to you; do not destroy them. They are people whom you rescued and brought out of Egypt by your very great power. &#91;DOU, MTY&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:21; MICAH 7:18–19; 1 SAMUEL 25:25"> Do not forget what you promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Please ignore these stubborn and wicked people and the sin that they have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:16; EXODUS 32:12"> If you do not do that, and if you get rid of them, the people of Egypt will hear about it and say that you were not able to bring them into the land that you promised to give to them. They will say that you took them into the desert only to kill them there because you hated them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:10; 1 KINGS 8:51; DEUTERONOMY 9:26"> Do not forget that they are your people. You chose them to belong to you. You brought them out from Egypt by your very great power &#91;DOU, MTY&#93;.’ ”</span>
</div>
