## Exclusion from Yahweh’s people

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:28; Isaiah 56:3–4; LEVITICUS 21:17–21"> “Any man whose testicles are crushed or whose penis is cut off shall not be ◄included as one of/allowed to worship with► Yahweh’s people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:6; Isaiah 57:3; HEBREWS 12:8"> “No ◄illegitimate person/person whose mother and father were not married►, or descendant of that person, extending to the tenth generation, shall be included as one of Yahweh’s people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23; RUTH 4:6"> “No one from the Ammon or Moab people-groups shall be ◄included as one of/allowed to worship with► Yahweh’s people, extending to the tenth generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:2; 2 PETER 2:15; 1 KINGS 18:4"> One reason is that their leaders refused to give your ancestors food and water when they were traveling from Egypt to Canaan. Another reason is that they wanted to pay Balaam, the son of Beor from Pethor town in Mesopotamia, to curse you Israelis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:7–8; ROMANS 8:31; EPHESIANS 2:4–5"> But Yahweh our God did not pay attention to Balaam; instead, he caused Balaam to bless your ancestors, because Yahweh loved them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezra 9:12; 2 SAMUEL 12:31; Nehemiah 13:23–25"> As long as Israel is a nation, you must not do anything to cause things to go well for those two people-groups or enable them to prosper.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:19; EXODUS 22:21; EXODUS 23:9"> “But do not despise anyone from the Edom people-group, because they are descendants of your ancestor Jacob, just like you are. And do not despise people from Egypt, because they treated your ancestors well when they first lived in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 20:5–6; EPHESIANS 2:12–13; DEUTERONOMY 23:1–3"> The children and grandchildren of people from Edom and Egypt who live among you may be ◄included among/allowed to worship with► Yahweh’s people.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Keeping the camp clean

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:18; JUDGES 20:26; REVELATION 19:11–14"> “When your soldiers are living in tents at a time of fighting your enemies, they must avoid doing things that would make them unacceptable to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:16; NUMBERS 5:2–3; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:11–13"> If any soldier becomes unacceptable to God because semen comes out of his body during the night, the next morning he must go outside the camp and stay there during that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:22; EPHESIANS 5:26–27; LEVITICUS 14:9"> But in the evening of that day, he must bathe himself, and at sunset he is allowed to come back into the camp.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> “Your soldiers must have a toilet area outside the camp where you can go when you need to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:6–8"> When you go to fight against your enemies, carry a stick along with your weapons, in order that when you need to defecate &#91;EUP&#93;, you can dig a hole with the stick, and then cover up the hole when you have finished defecating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 17:1; LEVITICUS 26:12; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:16"> You must keep the camp acceptable to Yahweh our God, because he is with you in your camp to protect you and to enable you to defeat your enemies. Do not do anything disgraceful/indecent that would cause Yahweh to abandon you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Various other laws

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:15; OBADIAH 1:14"> “If slaves who escape from their masters come to you and request you to protect them, do not send them back to their masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 22:21; ZECHARIAH 7:10; JEREMIAH 7:6"> Allow them to stay/live among you, in whatever town they choose, and do not mistreat them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 KINGS 23:7; 1 KINGS 14:24; LEVITICUS 19:29"> “Do not allow any Israeli man or woman to become a prostitute at the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:2; MATTHEW 7:6; REVELATION 22:15"> Also, do not allow any people who earned money from being a prostitute to bring any of that money into the temple of Yahweh our God, even if they solemnly promised to pay that money to Yahweh. Yahweh hates those who are prostitutes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 22:25; LEVITICUS 25:35–37; PSALMS 15:5"> “When you lend money or food or anything else to a fellow Israeli, do not charge them interest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:10; DEUTERONOMY 15:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:58"> You are allowed to charge interest when you lend money to foreigners who live in your land, but not when you lend money to Israelis. Do this in order that Yahweh our God will bless you in everything that you do in the land that you are about to enter and occupy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:4–5; PSALMS 66:13–14; PSALMS 116:18"> “When you vow to give something to Yahweh your God or to do something for him, do it as soon as you can. Yahweh expects you to do what you promised, and if you do not do it, you will be committing a sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if you &#91;SYN&#93; do not vow to do something, that is not sinful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:25; JUDGES 11:30–31; 1 SAMUEL 1:11"> But if you voluntarily promise to do something, you must do it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:26; ROMANS 12:13; HEBREWS 13:5"> “When you walk through someone else’s vineyard, you are allowed to pick and eat as many grapes as you want, but you must not cut some from a vine and carry them away in a container.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:1–2; MARK 2:23; LUKE 6:1–2"> When you walk along a path in someone else’s field of grain, you are allowed to pluck/pick some of the grain and eat it, but you must not cut any grain with a sickle and take it with you.”</span>
</div>
</div>
