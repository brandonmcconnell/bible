## The commandment to love God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:1; DEUTERONOMY 12:1; DEUTERONOMY 4:45"> “These are the commandments and rules and regulations that Yahweh our God commanded me to teach to you. He wants you to obey them in the land that you are about to enter and occupy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:40; DEUTERONOMY 13:4; EXODUS 20:20"> He wants you to revere him, and he wants you and your descendants to always obey all these rules and regulations that I am giving to you, in order that you may live for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 22:17; EXODUS 3:8; GENESIS 26:4"> So, you Israeli people, listen to them carefully and obey them. If you do that, things will go well with you, and you will become a nation with a very large population when you are living in that very fertile &#91;IDM&#93; land. That is what Yahweh, the God whom our ancestors worshiped, promised would happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MARK 12:29–32; Isaiah 44:6; 1 TIMOTHY 2:5"> “You Israeli people, listen! Only Yahweh is our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARK 12:30; MATTHEW 22:37; LUKE 10:27"> You must love him with all your inner being and with all that you feel and with all that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:16; PROVERBS 7:3; JEREMIAH 31:33"> Never forget &#91;IDM&#93; these commands that I am giving to you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:19; EPHESIANS 6:4; PSALMS 78:4–6"> Teach them to your children again and again. Talk about them all the time: When you are in your houses and when you are walking outside; talk about them when you are lying down and when you are doing things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:21; PROVERBS 3:3; DEUTERONOMY 11:18"> Write them on tiny scrolls and fasten them to your arms/wrists, and write them on bands that you fasten to your foreheads to help you to remember them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:20; Isaiah 30:8; Isaiah 57:8"> Write them on the doorposts and on the gates of your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:13; PSALMS 105:44; Nehemiah 9:25"> “Yahweh our God vowed to your ancestors Abraham and Isaac and Jacob that he will give you a land that already has in it large and prosperous cities that you did not build.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:12–18; DEUTERONOMY 8:10–20; EZEKIEL 16:10–20"> He said that the houses in those cities will already be full of many good things that others put there; you did not put them there. There will be wells that others have dug. There will be vineyards and olive trees that others planted. So when Yahweh brings you into that land, and you have all that you want to eat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> be sure that you do not forget Yahweh who rescued you from being slaves in Egypt and gave all these things to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 4:8; DEUTERONOMY 10:20; MATTHEW 4:10"> “You must revere Yahweh our God, and you must serve only him and say that he should &#91;MTY&#93; punish you if you do not do what you have promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:6; 1 JOHN 5:21; DEUTERONOMY 13:7"> You must not worship any other gods, the gods that the people-groups who will live near you worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:24; EXODUS 20:5; DEUTERONOMY 7:4"> Yahweh our God, who lives among you, ◄will not accept/will punish► people who worship anyone or anything else. So if you worship any other god, Yahweh will be very angry with you, and he will get rid of you completely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 17:7; LUKE 4:12; MATTHEW 4:7"> Do not do sinful things to find out if you can do them without Yahweh our God punishing you, like your ancestors did at Massah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:22; PSALMS 119:4; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:58"> Be sure that you always obey all the laws, the commandments, the rules, and the regulations that he has given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:40; DEUTERONOMY 12:25; ROMANS 12:2"> Do what Yahweh says is right and good. If you do that, things will go well with you. You will be able to enter and occupy the good/fertile land that Yahweh vowed to give to our ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:52–53; EXODUS 23:28–30; JUDGES 3:1–4"> He will do that by expelling your enemies from that land, just like he promised to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 13:14; EXODUS 12:26; JOSHUA 4:6–7"> “In the future, when your children ask you, ‘Why did Yahweh our God command us to obey all these rules and regulations?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 6:17–18; EPHESIANS 2:11–12; DEUTERONOMY 15:15"> tell them, ‘Our ancestors were slaves of the king in Egypt, but Yahweh brought them out of Egypt by his great power &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:34; PSALMS 58:10–11; DEUTERONOMY 7:19"> They saw him do many kinds of miracles &#91;DOU&#93; and do terrifying things to the people of Egypt and to the king and his officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:18; DEUTERONOMY 6:10; EXODUS 13:5"> He rescued our ancestors from Egypt and brought them here to give them this land, just like he vowed that he would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:39; PSALMS 41:2; DEUTERONOMY 10:12–13"> And he commanded us to obey all these laws and to revere him, for our own good and in order that he would protect our nation and enable us to prosper, as he is doing now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:13; ROMANS 10:3; LEVITICUS 18:5"> Yahweh our God will ◄approve of us/consider us to be righteous► if we carefully obey everything that he has commanded us to do.’ ”</span>
</div>
