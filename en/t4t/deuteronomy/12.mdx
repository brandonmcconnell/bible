## The one place for worship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:40; JOB 7:1; DEUTERONOMY 4:19"> “◄I will now tell you again/These, again, are► the rules and regulations that you must faithfully obey in the land that Yahweh, the God whom our ancestors worshiped, is giving to you to occupy. You must obey these laws all the time that you are alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:5; 2 KINGS 16:4; NUMBERS 33:51–52"> You must destroy all the places where the people-groups that you will expel worship their gods, on the tops of mountains and hills and under big green trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDGES 2:2; ZECHARIAH 13:2; NUMBERS 33:52"> You must tear down their altars and smash their sacred pillars. Completely burn the statues of their goddess Asherah in fires, and chop down their wooden idols, in order that no one will ever worship them &#91;MTY&#93; in those places again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:23; DEUTERONOMY 20:18; DEUTERONOMY 16:21–22"> “Do not worship Yahweh like the people in Canaan worship their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:11; DEUTERONOMY 26:2; 2 CHRONICLES 7:12"> Instead, you must go to the place that Yahweh will choose. It will be in the area where one of your tribes will live. That is where you must go to worship Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:19–20; MALACHI 3:10; LEVITICUS 27:32–33"> That is the place where you must bring your sacrifices that will be completely burned on the altar, and your other sacrifices, ◄your tithes/ten percent of all of your crops►, other offerings that you decide to give, and the firstborn animals from your cattle and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:18; LEVITICUS 23:40; DEUTERONOMY 14:26"> There, in the presence of Yahweh our God, who has greatly blessed you, you and your families will eat the good things that you have worked to produce, and you will be happy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 21:25; JUDGES 17:6; PROVERBS 21:2"> “When you are in that land, you must not do some of the things that we have been doing. Until now, you have all been worshiping Yahweh the way you wanted to,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:56; PSALMS 95:11; DEUTERONOMY 25:19"> because you have not yet arrived in the land which Yahweh is giving to you, where you will be able to live peacefully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:11; EZEKIEL 34:28; DEUTERONOMY 3:27"> But when you cross the Jordan River, you will start to live in the land that Yahweh our God is giving to you. He will protect you from all your enemies that will surround you, and you will live peacefully/safely.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:5; DEUTERONOMY 15:20; DEUTERONOMY 17:8"> “Yahweh will choose one place where he wants you to worship him. That is the place to which you must bring all the offerings that I command you to bring: The sacrifices that must be completely burned on the altar, your other sacrifices, other offerings that you yourselves decide to give, ◄your tithes/ten percent of all your crops►, and all the special offerings that you will solemnly promise to give to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:7; DEUTERONOMY 10:9; DEUTERONOMY 26:12"> Rejoice there in the presence of Yahweh, along with your children, your male and female servants, and the descendants of Levi who live in your towns. Do not forget that the descendants of Levi will not have their own land, like you will have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:28–32; 1 KINGS 15:34; 2 CHRONICLES 15:17"> Be sure that you do not offer sacrifices anywhere you want to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:11; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:19; DEUTERONOMY 12:5"> You must offer them only in the place that Yahweh will choose for you, in an area that belongs to one of your tribes. That is the only place where he wants you to offer sacrifices that will be completely burned on the altar, and to do the other things that I am commanding you to do when you worship him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:5; DEUTERONOMY 15:22–23; DEUTERONOMY 14:26"> “However, you will be permitted to kill and eat the meat of your animals in the places where you live. As often as you want to, you may eat the meat of the animals that Yahweh our God will bless you by giving them to you. Those who have done things that cause them to become unacceptable to God and those who have not done things like that may all eat that meat, just like you would eat the meat of a deer or an antelope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 9:4; DEUTERONOMY 15:23; DEUTERONOMY 12:23–24"> But you must not eat the blood of any animals; you must let the blood drain onto the ground before you cook the meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 26:14; DEUTERONOMY 12:6; DEUTERONOMY 14:22–29"> In the places where you live, you must not eat the things that you are offering to Yahweh: You must not eat the ◄tithes/ten percent► of your grain or of your wine, or of your olive oil, or the firstborn of your cattle and sheep, or the offerings that you yourselves decide to give to Yahweh, or any other offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:7; DEUTERONOMY 14:23; DEUTERONOMY 12:5"> Instead, you and your children and your male and female servants and the descendants of Levi who live in your towns must go to the place that he has chosen and eat those things there in the presence of Yahweh. And you should rejoice in the presence of Yahweh about everything that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 31:4–21; Nehemiah 10:34–39; DEUTERONOMY 14:27–29"> Be sure that you take care of the descendants of Levi all during the time that you live in your land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:8; DEUTERONOMY 11:24; EXODUS 34:24"> “When Yahweh our God gives you much more land than you will have when you first occupy it, which is what he has promised to do, and you say ‘I would like some meat to eat’ because you are craving/wanting some meat, you will be permitted to eat meat whenever you want to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:5; 1 KINGS 14:21; DEUTERONOMY 16:11"> If the place that Yahweh our God chooses to be the place for you to worship him is far from where you live, you are permitted to kill some of your cattle or sheep that Yahweh has given to you, and you may eat that meat at the places where you live, just like I have told you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:15–16"> Those who have done things that cause them to become unacceptable to God, and those who have not done things like that, are all permitted to all eat that meat, just like you would eat the meat of a deer or an antelope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 17:11; GENESIS 9:4; LEVITICUS 17:13–14"> But be sure that you do not eat the blood of any animal, because it is the blood that sustains life in living beings, and so you must not eat the life along with the meat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:16; DEUTERONOMY 15:23"> Do not eat the blood; instead, let it drain onto the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:38; DEUTERONOMY 4:40; Isaiah 3:10"> If you obey this command and do what Yahweh says is right for you to do, things will go well for you and for your descendants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:19; NUMBERS 5:9–10; DEUTERONOMY 12:11"> “But the sacred offerings that Yahweh has told you to give and the other offerings that you yourselves decide to give, you must take to the place that Yahweh will choose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:13; LEVITICUS 17:11; LEVITICUS 1:9"> You must offer there the sacrifices that will be completely burned on Yahweh’s altar. You must also offer there the sacrifices from which you may eat some of meat after you kill the animals and drain out the blood and some of it is thrown on the sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:40; JOHN 15:14; JOHN 15:10"> Faithfully obey all these things that I have commanded you. If you do that, things will go well forever for you and your descendants, because you will be doing what Yahweh our God says is right for you to do and what pleases him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:4; DEUTERONOMY 19:1; DEUTERONOMY 9:3"> “When you enter the land that you will occupy, as you advance, Yahweh our God will get rid of the people-groups that live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:2; EZEKIEL 20:32; DEUTERONOMY 7:16"> After he does that, be sure that you do not worship the gods that they have been worshiping, because if you do that, you will be caught in the same trap that they were caught in &#91;MET&#93;. Do not ask anyone about those gods, saying, ‘Tell me how they worshiped their gods, in order that I can worship them also.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:35; DEUTERONOMY 18:9–10; 2 KINGS 17:15–17"> Do not try to worship Yahweh our God like they have worshiped their gods, because when they worship them, they do disgusting things, things that Yahweh hates. The worst thing that they do is that they sacrifice their own children and burn them on their altars.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:2; REVELATION 22:18–19; JOSHUA 1:7"> “Be sure to do everything that I have commanded you to do. Do not add anything to these commands, and do not take anything away from them.”</span>
</div>
</div>
