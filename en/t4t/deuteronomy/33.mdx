<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:6; GENESIS 49:26; JAMES 5:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I desire/hope that very nice fruit will grow on trees in their ancient mountains/hills &#91;DOU&#93;,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ACTS 7:35; PSALMS 24:1; EXODUS 3:2–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">and that their land will be have many good crops,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">blessed by Yahweh, the one who appeared to me in a burning bush.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I desire/hope that Yahweh will bless the tribes of Joseph in all those ways,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because he was the leaders of his older brothers when they were in Egypt.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
