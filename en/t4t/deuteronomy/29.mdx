## The agreement made in Moab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:2–3; HEBREWS 8:9; EXODUS 24:2–8"> These are the things that the Israelis were required to do to keep the agreement that Yahweh was making with them. Moses/I commanded them to keep this agreement when they/we were in the Moab region on the east side of the Jordan River. This was in addition to the agreement that Yahweh had made with them/us at Sinai Mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 19:4; EXODUS 8:12; JOSHUA 24:5–6"> Moses/I summoned all the Israeli people and said to them, “You saw &#91;SYN&#93; for yourselves what Yahweh did to the king of Egypt and to his officials and to his entire country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:32–35; Nehemiah 9:9–11; DEUTERONOMY 7:18–19"> You &#91;SYN&#93; saw all the plagues that Yahweh caused them to experience, and all the various miracles &#91;DOU&#93; that Yahweh performed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:18; Isaiah 6:9–10; PROVERBS 20:12"> But until now, Yahweh has not enabled you to understand the meaning of all that you have seen and heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:4; Nehemiah 9:21; DEUTERONOMY 8:2"> For 40 years Yahweh has led you while you traveled/walked through the desert. During that time, your clothes and your sandals have not worn out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:3; EPHESIANS 5:18; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:25"> You did not have bread to eat or wine or other fermented/strong drinks to drink, but Yahweh took care of you, in order that you would know that he is your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:24–17; NUMBERS 32:33–42; PSALMS 135:10–12"> “And when we came to this place, Sihon, the king who ruled in Heshbon city, and Og, the king who ruled the Bashan region, came with their armies to attack us, but we defeated them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:12–13; NUMBERS 32:33"> We took/conquered their land and divided it among the tribes of Reuben and Gad, and half of the tribe of Manasseh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:3; DEUTERONOMY 4:6; LUKE 11:28"> “So faithfully keep all of this agreement, in order that you will be successful in everything that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOEL 2:16–17; REVELATION 20:12; DEUTERONOMY 31:12–13"> Today all of us are standing in the presence of Yahweh our God. The leaders of all our tribes, our elders, our officials, all you Israeli men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 12:38; JOSHUA 9:21–27; EXODUS 12:48–49"> your wives, your children, and the foreigners who live among you and cut wood for you and carry water for you, are here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 15:12–15; 2 KINGS 11:17; DEUTERONOMY 5:2–3"> We are here today to promise to keep this solemn agreement with Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 6:7; GENESIS 17:7; DEUTERONOMY 28:9"> He is making this agreement with you in order to confirm that you are his people, and that he is your God. This agreement is what he promised you, and which is what he vowed to give to your ancestors Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:31–34; HEBREWS 8:7–12"> This agreement is not only with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 2:39; 1 CORINTHIANS 7:14; JEREMIAH 50:5"> He is making this agreement with us who are here today and also with our descendants who are not yet born.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:19; DEUTERONOMY 2:9; DEUTERONOMY 2:4"> “You remember the things that our ancestors suffered in Egypt, and how they traveled through the land that belonged to other nations after they came out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:36"> In those countries they saw those disgusting idols made of wood and stone and decorated with silver and gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:15; DEUTERONOMY 11:16–17; DEUTERONOMY 13:1–15"> So be sure that no man or woman or family or tribe that is here today turns away from Yahweh our God, to worship/serve the gods of those nations. Doing that would be like a root of a plant that would grow among you and bear poisonous and bitter fruit &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:24; NUMBERS 15:30; NUMBERS 15:39"> “Be sure that no one here today who hears this agreement thinks, ‘Everything will go well with me, even if I stubbornly do what I want to.’ If you are stubborn like that, the result will be that Yahweh will eventually get rid of all of you, both good people and evil people &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 74:1; PSALMS 79:5; DEUTERONOMY 9:14"> Yahweh will not forgive anyone who is stubborn like that. Instead, he will be extremely angry &#91;DOU&#93; with that person, and that person will experience all the curses that I have told you about, until Yahweh gets rid of that person and his family &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:41; JOSHUA 7:1–26; EZEKIEL 13:9"> From all the tribes of Israel, Yahweh will separate that individual. Yahweh will cause him to experience all the disasters that I have listed in the agreement that states the things that Yahweh will do to curse those who disobey the laws that I have written in this scroll/book.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 19:8"> “In the future, your descendants and people from other countries will see the disasters and the illnesses that Yahweh has caused to happen to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 2:9; JEREMIAH 17:6; Isaiah 34:9"> They will see that all your land has been ruined by burning sulfur and salt. Nothing will have been planted. Not even weeds will grow there. Your land will resemble Sodom and Gomorrah cities, and Admah and Zeboiim cities, which Yahweh destroyed when he was very angry &#91;DOU&#93; with the people who lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:8–9; 1 KINGS 9:8–9; ROMANS 2:5"> And the people from those other nations will ask, ‘Why did Yahweh do this to this land? Why was he very angry with the people who lived here?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:9; Isaiah 47:6; JEREMIAH 50:7"> “Then other people will reply, ‘It is because they refused to keep the agreement that Yahweh, the God whom their ancestors worshiped, had made with them when he brought them out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:64; JUDGES 2:12–13; JUDGES 5:8"> Instead, they served/worshiped other gods that they had never worshiped before, gods that Yahweh had told them not to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DANIEL 9:11–14; DEUTERONOMY 28:15–68; DEUTERONOMY 27:15–26"> So, Yahweh became very angry with the Israeli people in this land, and he caused them to experience all the disasters that their leader warned them about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:20; 1 KINGS 14:15; PSALMS 52:5"> Yahweh became extremely angry &#91;DOU&#93; with them and took/yanked them &#91;MET&#93; out of their land and banished them into another land, and they are still there.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DANIEL 2:22; ACTS 1:7; ROMANS 11:33–34"> “There are some things that Yahweh our God has ◄kept secret/not revealed►, but he has revealed his laws to us, and he expects us and our descendants to obey them forever.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
