## Rules concerning fighting wars

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 32:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 31:8; PSALMS 20:7"> “When your soldiers go to fight your enemies, and you see that they have many horses and chariots and that their army is much bigger than yours, do not be afraid of them, because Yahweh our God, who brought your ancestors safely out of Egypt, will be with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:6; 2 CHRONICLES 13:12; NUMBERS 10:8–9"> When you are ready to start the battle, the Supreme Priest must stand in front of the troops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:6; PSALMS 3:6; REVELATION 2:10"> He must say to them, ‘You Israeli men, listen to me! Today you are going to fight against your enemies. Do not be timid or afraid &#91;DOU&#93; or tremble or panic &#91;DOU&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 14:14; DEUTERONOMY 3:22; DEUTERONOMY 1:30"> because Yahweh our God will go with you. He will fight your enemies for you, and he will enable you to defeat them.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:27; NUMBERS 31:14; DEUTERONOMY 16:18"> Then the army officers must say to the troops, ‘If anyone among you has just built a new house &#91;RHQ&#93; and has not dedicated it to God, he should go home and dedicate the house. If he does not do that, if he dies in the battle, someone else will dedicate the house and live in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:23–25; DEUTERONOMY 28:1–30; JEREMIAH 31:5"> If anyone among you has planted a vineyard and has not yet harvested any grapes from it &#91;RHQ&#93;, he should go home. If he stays here and dies in the battle, someone else will harvest the grapes and enjoy the wine made from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:5; DEUTERONOMY 28:30; LUKE 14:18–20"> If anyone among you has become engaged to marry a woman but has not married her yet &#91;RHQ&#93;, he should go home. If he stays here and dies in the battle, someone else will marry her.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 7:3; DEUTERONOMY 1:28; REVELATION 21:8"> “Then the officers must also say, ‘If anyone among you is afraid or timid &#91;RHQ&#93;, he should go home, in order that he does not cause his fellow soldiers to also stop being courageous.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And when the officers have finished speaking to the troops, they must appoint commanders to lead the groups of soldiers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 10:5–6; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:18–1; EPHESIANS 2:17"> “When you come near a city that is far away to attack it, first tell the people there that if they surrender, you will not attack them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 19:14; JOSHUA 11:19–20; JOSHUA 16:10"> If they open the gates of the city and surrender, they all will become your slaves to work for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But if they refuse to surrender peacefully, and decide instead to fight against you, your troops must surround the city and break through the walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 2:6–12; 1 KINGS 11:15–16; PSALMS 110:1"> Then, when Yahweh our God enables you to capture &#91;IDM&#93; the city, you must kill all the men in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:2; JOSHUA 22:8; NUMBERS 31:9"> But you are allowed to take for yourselves the women, the children, the livestock, and everything else that you want to take from the city. You will be allowed to enjoy all the things that belonged to your enemies; it is Yahweh our God who has given those things to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> You should do that in all the cities that are far from the land in which you will settle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:14; NUMBERS 21:2–3; NUMBERS 33:52"> “But in the cities that are in the land that Yahweh our God is giving to you, you must kill all the people and all the animals &#91;LIT&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; JEREMIAH 50:35–40; DEUTERONOMY 7:1–2"> You must get rid of them completely. Get rid of the Heth people-group, the Amor people-group, the Canaan people-group, the Periz people-group, the Hiv people-group, and the Jebus people-group; that is what Yahweh our God commanded you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:30–31; EXODUS 23:33; EPHESIANS 5:11"> If you do not do that, they will teach you to sin against Yahweh our God and do the disgusting things that they do when they worship their gods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:10; JOHN 15:2–8; DEUTERONOMY 26:6"> “When you surround a city for a long time, trying to capture it, do not cut down the fruit trees outside the city. You are allowed to eat the fruit from the trees, but do not get rid of the trees, because they certainly are not &#91;RHQ&#93; your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:15; JEREMIAH 33:4; ECCLESIASTES 9:14"> You are permitted to cut down the other trees and use the wood to make ladders and towers to enable you to go over the walls and capture the city.”</span>
</div>
</div>
