## Moses repeated for them the Ten Commandments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:1; MATTHEW 23:3; DEUTERONOMY 29:10"> Moses/I summoned all the people of Israel and said to them,<br />“You Israeli people, listen to all the rules and regulations that I am giving to you today. Learn them and be sure to obey them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:23; EXODUS 19:5–8; EXODUS 24:8"> When we were at Sinai Mountain, Yahweh our God made an agreement with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:10–15; GENESIS 17:21; GENESIS 17:7"> But this agreement was not only for our ancestors. He made it also for us who are alive now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 19:18–19; DEUTERONOMY 34:10; EXODUS 19:9"> Yahweh spoke with us face-to-face on that mountain, from the middle of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:19; EXODUS 19:16; EXODUS 20:18–21"> On that day, I stood between your ancestors and Yahweh to tell them what he said, because they were afraid of the fire, and they did not climb up the mountain. This is what Yahweh said:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 20:2–17; PSALMS 81:5–10; LEVITICUS 26:1–2"> ‘I am Yahweh your God, the one you worship. I am the one who brought you out of the land of Egypt. I am the one who freed you from being slaves there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 20:3; MATTHEW 4:10; 1 JOHN 5:21"> You must worship only me; you must not worship any other god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 20:4; DEUTERONOMY 4:15–19"> You must not carve/make for yourselves any idol that represents anything in the sky or anything that is on the ground or anything that swims in the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 20:4–6; EXODUS 34:7; DANIEL 9:4–9"> You must not bow down to any idol and worship it, because I am Yahweh God, and I ◄am very jealous/want you to worship me only►. I punish those who sin and hate me. I punish not only them, but I will punish ◄their descendants, down to the third and fourth generation/their children and grandchildren and great-grandchildren►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 8:28; 1 JOHN 1:7; JEREMIAH 32:18"> But I will steadfastly love ◄thousands of generations/all the descendants► of those who love me and obey my commandments (OR, I will love for thousands of generations).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 20:7; LEVITICUS 19:12; DEUTERONOMY 6:13"> Do not use/speak my name carelessly (OR, for wrong/evil purposes), because I am Yahweh God, the one whom you should worship, and I will certainly punish those who use/speak my name for wrong/evil purposes &#91;LIT&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 20:8–11; Isaiah 58:13; Isaiah 56:6"> Do not forget that the seventh day of every week is mine. Dedicate those days to me just like I, Yahweh your God, am commanding you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:12; LUKE 13:14–16; EXODUS 23:12"> There are six days each week for you to do all your work,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:4; GENESIS 2:2; EXODUS 23:12"> but the seventh day is a rest day, a day dedicated to me, Yahweh your God. On that day you must not do any work. You and your sons and daughters and your male and female slaves must not work. You must not even force your livestock to work, and you must not tell foreigners to work, those who are living in your country. You must allow your slaves to rest on that day just like you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:6; DEUTERONOMY 15:15; DEUTERONOMY 16:12"> Do not forget that you were slaves in Egypt, and that I, Yahweh your God, brought you out from there by my very great power. &#91;DOU, MTY&#93; That is the reason that I am commanding that all of you must rest on the seventh/Sabbath day each week.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12; EPHESIANS 6:1–3; COLOSSIANS 3:20"> Honor/Respect your fathers and your mothers, just like I, Yahweh your God, am commanding you, in order that you may live a long time in the land that I, Yahweh your God, will give you, and in order that things will go well for you there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 20:13; ROMANS 13:9; MATTHEW 5:21–22"> Do not murder anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 20:14; LUKE 18:20; LEVITICUS 20:10"> Do not ◄commit adultery/have sex with anyone other than your spouse►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 20:15; ROMANS 13:9; EPHESIANS 4:28"> Do not steal anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 20:16; EXODUS 23:1; PROVERBS 19:9"> Do not tell lies about anyone when you are speaking in a court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 20:17; ROMANS 13:9; LUKE 12:15"> Do not ◄covet/desire to have► someone else’s wife, someone else’s house, someone else’s fields, someone else’s male slave or female slave, someone else’s livestock, someone else’s donkeys, or anything else that another person owns.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 24:12; EXODUS 31:18; DEUTERONOMY 4:12–15"> Those are the commandments that Yahweh spoke to your ancestors. When they were gathered there at the bottom of the mountain, he spoke with a very loud voice from the middle of the fire, and there were dark clouds surrounding the mountain. He spoke only those ten commandments, no more. Then he wrote them on two stone tablets and gave them to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 20:18–19; HEBREWS 12:18–21"> After your ancestors heard Yahweh’s voice when he spoke to them out of the darkness, while there was a big fire burning on the mountain, their leaders and elders came to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:33; EXODUS 19:19; EXODUS 33:20"> and one of them said, ‘Listen to us! Yahweh our God showed us that he is very great and glorious when we heard him speak &#91;MTY&#93; from the fire. Today we have realized that it is possible for us humans to continue to live even though God has spoken to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:16; GALATIANS 3:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:7–9"> But we are afraid that we will die &#91;RHQ&#93; if we hear Yahweh our God speak &#91;MTY&#93; to us again; his terrible fire will destroy/kill us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:33; ROMANS 3:20; MATTHEW 26:63"> ◄Who has/Certainly no human has► remained alive after hearing the all-powerful God speak &#91;MTY&#93; to them from a fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 20:19; HEBREWS 12:19"> So Moses, you go up the mountain and listen to everything that Yahweh our God will say. Then come back and tell us everything that he has said, and we will listen to what he has said and obey it.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:17; NUMBERS 36:5; NUMBERS 27:7"> Yahweh heard your leaders say that, so when I went back up the mountain, Yahweh said to me, ‘I have heard what your leaders have said, and what they have said is right/correct.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; EZEKIEL 33:31–32; DEUTERONOMY 11:1"> I surely wish that they would always think like that and revere me and obey all my commandments, in order that things may go well for them and for their descendants forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> So go down and tell them to return to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:19; EZEKIEL 20:11; DEUTERONOMY 5:1"> But then you come back up here and stand near me, and I will give to you all the rules and regulations that I want them to obey. Then you can teach them to the people, in order that they will obey them when they are in the land that I am giving to them.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:6; PROVERBS 4:27; JOSHUA 1:7"> So I went back down to the people, and I said to them, ‘Be sure that you do everything that Yahweh our God has commanded us to do. Do not disobey any of his laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:23; DEUTERONOMY 4:40; LUKE 1:6"> Conduct your lives as Yahweh our God has commanded us to do, in order that you may live a long time, and in order that things will go well for you when you are living in the land that you will occupy.’ ”</span>
</div>
