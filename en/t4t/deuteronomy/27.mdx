## The stone tablets set up on Ebal Mountain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 15:14; LUKE 11:28; JAMES 2:10"> Moses/I, along with the other Israeli leaders, said this to the people: “Obey all the commandments that I am giving to you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:31; EZEKIEL 36:26; EZEKIEL 11:19"> Soon you will cross the Jordan River and enter the land that Yahweh, the God whom our ancestors worshiped, promised to give to you. There, in that very fertile &#91;IDM&#93; land on the west side of the Jordan River, set up some large stones on Ebal Mountain, and cover them with plaster. Then write on those stones all these laws and teachings/instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 20:25; EXODUS 24:4; JOSHUA 8:30–31"> And build a stone altar there to offer sacrifices to Yahweh, but do not cut those stones with iron tools.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:2; LEVITICUS 1:1–17"> The altar that you make to burn sacrifices to Yahweh our God must be made with uncut stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:1–17; Isaiah 12:3; PSALMS 100:1–2"> And there you must sacrifice offerings to maintain fellowship with Yahweh, and you must eat your share of those offerings and rejoice/celebrate in the presence of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:2; JOHN 16:25; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:12"> And, when you write these laws on those stones, you must write them very clearly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 PETER 2:10–11; DEUTERONOMY 26:16–18; EPHESIANS 5:8–9"> Then Moses/I, along with the priests, said to all the Israeli people, “You Israeli people, be quiet and listen to what I am saying. Today you have become the people who belong to Yahweh, our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:1; 1 PETER 1:14–16; MATTHEW 5:48"> So, you must do what he tells you, and obey all the rules and regulations that I am giving to you today.”</span>
</div>

## The curses from disobeying God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> On that same day Moses/I said to the Israeli people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JUDGES 9:7; GENESIS 30:24; GENESIS 35:18"> “After you have crossed over the Jordan River, the tribes of Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph, and Benjamin must stand on Gerizim Mountain and request Yahweh to bless the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:4; GENESIS 29:32; GENESIS 30:20"> And the tribes of Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali must stand on Ebal Mountain and listen to the things that Yahweh will do when he curses the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 9:11; DEUTERONOMY 33:9–10; JOSHUA 8:33"> The descendants of Levi must shout these words loudly:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 34:17; EXODUS 20:4; DEUTERONOMY 5:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who makes an idol of stone, or of wood, or of metal, and secretly sets it up and worships it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh considers that those things, made by humans &#91;SYN&#93;, are detestable/disgusting.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:3; EXODUS 21:17; DEUTERONOMY 21:18–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who dishonors his father or his mother.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:14; PROVERBS 22:28; PROVERBS 23:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who removes someone else’s markers of property boundaries.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:14; MATTHEW 15:14; PROVERBS 28:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who leads a blind person to go ◄in the wrong direction/where that person does not want to go►.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:18; DEUTERONOMY 24:17; PROVERBS 17:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who deprives foreigners or orphans or widows of the things that the laws state must be done for them.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:30; LEVITICUS 18:8; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who ◄shows no respect for/disgraces his father►</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by having sex with &#91;EUP&#93; any of his father’s wives.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:23; EXODUS 22:19; LEVITICUS 20:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who has sex &#91;EUP&#93; with any animal.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:17; LEVITICUS 18:9; EZEKIEL 22:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who has sex with his sister or with his half-sister.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:14; LEVITICUS 18:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who has sex with &#91;EUP&#93; his mother-in-law.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:31; LEVITICUS 24:17; 2 SAMUEL 11:15–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who secretly murders someone else.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:17; EXODUS 23:7–8; PSALMS 15:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who accepts a bribe to murder someone who ◄is innocent/has not done something that is wrong.►’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:10; DEUTERONOMY 28:15–68; ROMANS 10:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Yahweh will curse anyone who disobeys these laws and refuses to declare that these laws are good.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And all the people must reply, ‘◄Amen/We want that to happen►.’”</span></span>
