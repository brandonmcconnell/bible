## Moses reminded them of the defeat of King Og

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:33–35; DEUTERONOMY 1:4; DEUTERONOMY 29:7"> “Then we turned north and went toward the Bashan region. Og, the king of that area, and all his soldiers marched south to fight against us at Edrei town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="REVELATION 2:10; 2 CHRONICLES 20:17; NUMBERS 21:23–25"> Yahweh said to me, ‘Do not be afraid of him, because I will enable your army to defeat &#91;MTY&#93; him and all his army and to capture all their land. Do to him what you did to Sihon, the king of the Amor people-group, who ruled in Heshbon city.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:35; JOSHUA 13:12; JOSHUA 13:30"> “So Yahweh enabled us to defeat &#91;IDM&#93; King Og and all his army. We killed them all; we did not allow any of them to remain alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 4:13; JOSHUA 12:4; JOSHUA 13:30–31"> There were 60 cities in that region near Argob, in King Og’s kingdom of Bashan. But we captured all of them</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:28; NUMBERS 13:28; HEBREWS 11:30"> All those cities had high walls around them with gates and bars. We also captured many villages that did not have walls around them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:34; PSALMS 135:10–12; DEUTERONOMY 2:24"> We completely destroyed everything, just as we had done in the area that King Sihon ruled. We killed all the men, women, and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:35; JOSHUA 8:27; JOSHUA 11:11–14"> But from those cities we took for ourselves all the livestock and other valuable things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:9–12; JOSHUA 12:2–6; NUMBERS 32:33–42"> “So at that time we took from those two kings of the Amor people-group all the land east of the Jordan River, from the Arnon River gorge in the south to Hermon Mountain in the north.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 5:23; PSALMS 29:6; EZEKIEL 27:5"> (That mountain is called Sirion by the people of Sidon city and is called Senir by the Amor people-group.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:31; JOSHUA 13:11–12; DEUTERONOMY 4:49"> “We captured all the towns on the ◄plateau/high level land►, and all the Gilead region, and all the Bashan region as far east as Edrei and Salecah towns, which also belonged to Og’s kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 14:5; JEREMIAH 49:2; 2 SAMUEL 12:26"> (Og was the last king who was a descendant of the Repha giants. His bed was made of iron. It was almost ◄14 feet/4 meters► long and ◄6 feet/2 meters► wide. It is still &#91;RHQ&#93; in Rabbah city in the Ammon area.)</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses reminded them of dividing the land east of the Jordan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:36; NUMBERS 32:32–38; JOSHUA 13:8–28"> “From the land that we captured at that time, I allotted to the tribes of Reuben and Gad the land north of Aroer town near the Arnon River, and some of the hilly area of the Gilead region, along with the nearby towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:39–42; JOSHUA 13:29–32; 1 CHRONICLES 5:23–26"> The other part of the Gilead region and all of the Bashan region, which was the Argob region that King Og had ruled, I allotted to half of the tribe of Manasseh.” (The entire Bashan region is called the land of the Repha giants.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:41; JOSHUA 13:13; 2 SAMUEL 3:3"> “Jair, from the tribe of Manasseh, conquered all the Bashan area as far north as the border of the Geshur and Maacah territories. He gave his own name to the villages there, and they are still called the villages of Jair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:39–40; JOSHUA 17:1–3; JOSHUA 22:7"> The northern part of the Gilead region I allotted to the Machir clan, who are descendants of the tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:24; DEUTERONOMY 2:37; 2 SAMUEL 24:5"> I allotted to the tribes of Reuben and Gad the southern part of the Gilead region, extending south to the Arnon River. The middle of the river is the southern boundary. The northern boundary is the Jabbok River, which is part of the border of the Ammon area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOSHUA 12:3; GENESIS 14:3; JOSHUA 3:16"> To the west their territory extended to the Jordan River Valley, from Galilee Lake in the north to the Dead Sea in the south and to the slopes of Pisgah Mountain to the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:20–24; JOSHUA 4:12–13; JOSHUA 1:12–15"> At that time, I told you three tribes, ‘Yahweh our God is giving you this land east of the Jordan River, for you to occupy/capture. So now, give your soldiers weapons, and send them across the Jordan River ahead of the men from the other Israeli tribes to help them to conquer/capture the land that has been allotted to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But your wives and children and your very numerous cattle must stay in the towns that I have allotted to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOSHUA 22:4; JOSHUA 22:8"> Your men must help your fellow Israelis until Yahweh enables them to live there peacefully after they capture all the land that Yahweh our God is giving to them on the west side of the Jordan River, just like he did for you here on the east side of the river. After that, you all may return to this land that I have allotted to you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:18–23; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:10; PSALMS 9:10"> And I told Joshua, ‘You &#91;SYN&#93; have seen everything that Yahweh our God did to those two kings, Sihon and Og. He will do the same thing to the people who are now in the land that you will be entering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 14:14; DEUTERONOMY 20:4; DEUTERONOMY 1:30"> Do not be afraid of those people, because Yahweh our God is the one who will fight for you all.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses reminded them that he was not allowed to enter Canaan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 12:8–9"> “At that time, I earnestly prayed, saying</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 15:11; DEUTERONOMY 11:2; PSALMS 71:19"> ‘Yahweh our Lord, you have merely begun to show me that you are very great and to show me the powerful things that you can do &#91;MTY&#93;. There is certainly no &#91;RHQ&#93; god in heaven or on earth who can do the powerful things/deeds that you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:21–22; EZEKIEL 20:6; EXODUS 3:8"> So please allow me to cross the Jordan River and to see the good/fertile land on the west side, the beautiful hilly area and the mountains in Lebanon.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:37; DEUTERONOMY 31:2; 1 CHRONICLES 22:7–9"> But Yahweh was angry with me because of what your ancestors had made me do, so he would not pay attention to me. Instead, he said, ‘That is enough talk from you! Do not talk to me about that again!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:12; DEUTERONOMY 34:1–4; GENESIS 13:14–15"> Climb up to the top of Pisgah Mountain and look toward the west and to the east, toward the north and to the south. Look at &#91;MTY&#93; it all carefully, because you will not cross the Jordan River to see the land from there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:3; DEUTERONOMY 31:7; DEUTERONOMY 1:38"> But tell Joshua what he must do and encourage him to be strong, because he is the one who will lead the people across the river in order that they can occupy/capture the land that you will see from the top of the mountain.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 34:6; DEUTERONOMY 4:46; NUMBERS 25:3"> So we remained in the Jordan River Valley close to Beth-Peor town.”</span>
</div>
