## A new copy of the Ten Commandments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 34:1–2; EXODUS 25:10–15; DEUTERONOMY 10:3–4"> “Then Yahweh said to me, ‘Cut two stone tablets like the first ones. And make a wooden chest to put them in. Then bring the tablets up to me on this mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:9; DEUTERONOMY 10:5; DEUTERONOMY 4:13"> I will write on those tablets the same words that I wrote on the first tablets, the ones that you broke. Then you can put them in the chest.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 34:4; EXODUS 37:1–9; EXODUS 25:10"> “So I made a chest. I used wood from an acacia tree to make it. Then I cut two tablets of stone like the first ones, and I went up the mountain carrying the tablets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:10; EXODUS 34:28; HEBREWS 12:18–19"> There Yahweh wrote on the tablets the same Ten Commandments that he had written on the first tablet. They are the commandments that he told to your ancestors from the middle of the fire on the mountain, when they were gathered at the bottom of the mountain. Then Yahweh gave the tablets to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 34:29; EXODUS 40:20; DEUTERONOMY 10:2"> and I turned and went down the mountain. Then, just as he had commanded, I put them in the chest that I had made. And they are still there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:38; NUMBERS 20:23–28; NUMBERS 33:30–33"> (Then, from the wells that belonged to the people of Jaakan, the Israeli people traveled to Moserah. There Aaron died and was buried, and his son Eleazar took his place and became the Supreme Priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:32–34"> From there, the Israelis traveled to Gudgodah, and from there to Jotbathah, which was a place that had many streams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:5; DEUTERONOMY 18:5; NUMBERS 6:23–26"> At that time, Yahweh ◄set apart/appointed► the tribe of Levi to carry the chest that contained the tablets on which were written the Ten Commandments, and to stand in Yahweh’s presence in the sacred tent, to offer sacrifices, and to pray for Yahweh to bless the people. They are still doing those things at the present time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:28; NUMBERS 18:20–24; DEUTERONOMY 18:1–2"> That is the reason that the tribe of Levi did not receive any land like the other tribes did. What they received was the work/privilege of being Yahweh’s priests, which is what he said that they should do.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:18–19; DEUTERONOMY 9:25; EXODUS 34:28"> “I stayed on the mountain 40 days and nights, just like I did the first time. I prayed to Yahweh, and he ◄answered my prayers/did what I requested► again and said that he would not destroy your ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 33:1; EXODUS 32:34"> Then Yahweh said to me, ‘Continue your journey, going in front of the people, to occupy the land that I vowed your ancestors that I would give to you.’”</span>

## What Yahweh required

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MICAH 6:8; DEUTERONOMY 6:5; LUKE 10:27"> “Now, you Israeli people, I will tell you &#91;RHQ&#93; what Yahweh our God requires/demands that you do. He requires you to revere him, to conduct your lives as he wants you to, to love him, and to serve him wholeheartedly &#91;DOU&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:39; DEUTERONOMY 6:24; JAMES 1:25"> and to obey all of his commandments &#91;DOU&#93; which I am giving to you today for your benefit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 19:5; PSALMS 24:1; PSALMS 115:16"> “Do not forget that Yahweh our God owns even the sky and everything that is in it. He also owns the earth and everything that is on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:37; DEUTERONOMY 7:7–8; ROMANS 9:13–23"> But in spite of owning all those things, Yahweh loved your ancestors, and from all the people-groups on the earth he chose us, their descendants, and we are still his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:4; DEUTERONOMY 30:6; LEVITICUS 26:41"> So you must stop sinning &#91;MET&#93; and stop being stubborn &#91;IDM&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ACTS 10:34; ROMANS 2:11; DANIEL 2:47"> Yahweh our God is greater than all gods, and he is greater than all rulers. He is very powerful &#91;DOU&#93; and awesome. He ◄is impartial/does not do things that favor one ◄group/person► more than others►, and he does not accept bribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 146:9; PSALMS 68:5; JEREMIAH 49:11"> He makes sure that orphans and widows are treated fairly. He loves even ◄foreigners/people from other countries► who live among us Israeli people, and he gives them food and clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 22:21; LEVITICUS 19:33–34; LUKE 17:18"> So you must love those foreigners too, because you were once foreigners when you were living in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:13; DEUTERONOMY 13:4; LUKE 4:8"> Revere Yahweh our God and worship only him. ◄Be faithful to/Have a close relationship with► him, and say that he should punish you if you do not do what you promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:14; PSALMS 106:21–22; EXODUS 15:2"> He is the one whom you must praise. He is our God, and we have seen the great and amazing things that he has done for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 46:27; EXODUS 1:5; GENESIS 15:5"> When our ancestors, Jacob and his family, went down to Egypt, there were only seventy of them. But now Yahweh our God has caused us to be as numerous as the stars in the sky.”</span>
</div>
