## Various other laws

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 23:4–5; EZEKIEL 34:16; Isaiah 58:7"> “If you see some Israeli’s ox or sheep that has strayed away, do not act as though you did not see it. Take it back to its owner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:12; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:6"> But if the owner does not live near you, or if you do not know who the owner is, take the animal to your house. It can stay with you until the owner comes, searching for it. Then you must give the animal to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> You must do the same thing if you see a donkey, or a piece of clothing, or anything else that someone has lost. Do not refuse to do what you should do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:1–2; HEBREWS 12:12–13; MATTHEW 5:44"> “And if you see a fellow Israeli’s donkey or cow that has fallen down on the road, do not act as though you did not see it. Help the owner to lift the animal up so that it can stand on its feet again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 11:4–15; DEUTERONOMY 18:12"> “Women must not wear men’s clothes, and men must not wear women’s clothes. Yahweh our God hates people who do things like that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:28; PROVERBS 12:10; GENESIS 8:17"> “If you happen to find a bird’s nest in a tree or on the ground, and the mother bird is sitting in the nest on its eggs or with the baby birds, do not take the mother bird and kill it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:40; PROVERBS 22:4"> You are permitted to take the baby birds, but you must allow the mother bird to fly away. Do this in order that things will go well for you and that you will live for a long time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 11:2; Isaiah 22:1; EXODUS 21:28–36"> “If you build a new house, you must put a ◄railing/low wall► around the edge of the roof. If you do that, and if someone falls off the roof and dies, ◄you will not be guilty of causing that person to die/his death &#91;MTY&#93; will not be your fault►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:19; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:3; MATTHEW 9:16"> “Do not plant any crop in the area where your grapevines are growing. If you do, you are not allowed to harvest the crops that you have planted or to harvest the grapes to make wine from them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:14–16"> “Do not hitch together an ox and a donkey for plowing your fields.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:19"> “Do not wear clothing that is made by weaving together ◄wool and linen/two different kinds of thread►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:5; NUMBERS 15:37–41"> “Twist threads together to make tassels and sew/fasten them on the four bottom corners of your cloak.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rules concerning sex

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 29:21; GENESIS 29:31; EPHESIANS 5:28–29"> “Suppose a man marries a young woman and has sex with her and later decides that he does not want her any more,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:19; PROVERBS 18:21; PROVERBS 18:8"> and he says false things about her, and claims that she was not a virgin when he married her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> If that happens, the young woman’s parents must take the sheet that was on the bed when that man and their daughter were married, which still has bloodstains on it, and show it to the elders of the city at the ◄gate of/central meeting place in► the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then the father of the young woman must say to the elders, ‘I gave my daughter to this man to be his wife. But now he says that he doesn’t want her any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he has falsely said that she was not a virgin when he married her. But look! Here is what proves that my daughter was a virgin! Look at the bloodstains on the sheet where they slept the night that they were married!’ And he will show the sheet to the elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 18:21"> Then the elders of that city must take that man and whip him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:29; MATTHEW 19:8–9; DEUTERONOMY 24:1–4"> They shall require that he pay a fine of 100 pieces of silver and give the money to the father of the young woman, because the man caused an Israeli young woman to be disgraced. Also, that woman must continue to live with him; she is his wife. He is not allowed to divorce her during the rest of his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> “But if what the man said is true, and there is nothing to prove that she was a virgin when he married her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:5; GENESIS 34:7; JUDGES 20:6"> they must take that young woman to the door of her father’s house. Then the men of that city must execute her by throwing stones at her. They must do that because she has done something in Israel that is very disgraceful, by having sex with some man while she was still living in her father’s house. By executing her like that, you will get rid of this evil practice among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:10; EZEKIEL 23:45–47; JOHN 8:4–5"> “If a man is caught while he is having sex with another man’s wife, both of them must be executed. By doing that, you will get rid of this evil practice in Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:18–19; DEUTERONOMY 20:7"> “Suppose in some town a man sees a young woman who is engaged/promised to be married to another man, and he is caught having sex with &#91;EUP&#93; her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:21–22; DEUTERONOMY 21:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:13"> You must take both of them to the ◄gate of/central meeting place in► that town. There you must execute them both by throwing stones at them. You must execute the young woman because she did not shout for help even though she was in the town. And the man must be executed because he had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with someone who was already engaged/promised to be married. By doing that, you will get rid of this evil practice among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 13:14"> “But suppose out in the open countryside a man meets a young woman who is engaged/promised to be married, and he rapes her. If that happens, only that man must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:22"> You must not punish the young woman, because she did not do anything for which she deserves to be executed. This case is like when one man attacks another man in the countryside and murders him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 13:7"> because the man who raped her saw her when she was in the open countryside, and even though she called out for help, there was no one there who could rescue her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 22:16–17"> “If a man rapes a young woman who is not engaged/promised to be married, if someone sees him while he is doing that,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:19; DEUTERONOMY 22:24; DEUTERONOMY 21:14"> that man must pay 50 pieces of silver to the young woman’s father, and he must marry her, because he forced her to have sex with him. He is not allowed to divorce her for the rest of his life.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:20; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:1; LEVITICUS 18:8"> “ man must not disgrace his father by having sex with any of his father’s wives.”</span>
</div>
</div>
