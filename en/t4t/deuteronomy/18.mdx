## What the priests were to receive

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:8–9; DEUTERONOMY 10:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:13–14"> “The priests, who are all from the tribe of Levi, will not receive any land in Israel. Instead, they will receive some of the food that other people offer to be burned on the altar to be sacrificed to Yahweh and some of the other sacrifices that will be offered to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="REVELATION 1:5–6; PSALMS 84:11; 1 PETER 2:5"> They will not be allotted any land like the other tribes will be. What they will receive is the work/privilege of being Yahweh’s priests, which is what he said that they should have.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:30–34; DEUTERONOMY 12:27"> “When the people bring an ox or a sheep to be sacrificed, they must give to the priests the shoulder, the cheeks/jaws, and the stomach of those animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44–47; NUMBERS 18:12–24; 2 CHRONICLES 31:4–10"> You must also give to them the first part of the grain that you harvest, and the first part of the wine that you make, and the first part of the olive oil that you make, and the first part of the wool that you shear from your sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:8; NUMBERS 3:10; EXODUS 28:1–14"> You must do this because Yahweh our God has chosen the tribe of Levi from all of your tribes, in order that men from that tribe would always be the priests who would serve him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:2–3; DEUTERONOMY 12:5; DEUTERONOMY 16:2"> “If any man from the tribe of Levi who has been living in one of the towns in Israel wants to come from there to the place of worship that Yahweh has chosen, and start living there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 31:2–4"> he is permitted to serve Yahweh there as a priest, just like the other men from the tribe of Levi who have been serving there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; Nehemiah 12:47; 2 CHRONICLES 31:4"> He must be given the same amount of food that the other priests receive. He is permitted to keep the money that his relatives give him for selling some of their possessions.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A warning about pagan practices

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:29–31; LEVITICUS 18:26–27; LEVITICUS 18:30"> When you enter the land that Yahweh our God is giving you, you must not imitate the disgusting things that the people-groups who are there now do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:31; EXODUS 22:18; LEVITICUS 19:26"> You must not sacrifice any of your children by burning them on your altars. Do not practice ◄divination/using supernatural power to reveal what will happen in the future►. Do not do/practice ◄soothsaying/magic to find out what will happen in the future►. Do not ◄interpret omens/say that because of something that you have seen you know what will happen►. Do not practice sorcery/witchcraft. Do not practice ◄putting spells on people/saying things to cause bad things to happen to others►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:31; 1 SAMUEL 28:11–14"> Do not try to talk with spirits of dead people. Do not do/practice magic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:24; DEUTERONOMY 9:4; LEVITICUS 18:27"> Yahweh hates people who do any of those disgusting things. And as you advance through that land, he is going to expel the people-groups because they do/practice those disgusting things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:48; GENESIS 17:1; GENESIS 6:9"> But you must always avoid doing any of those things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The promise to send a prophet like Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ACTS 14:16; DEUTERONOMY 18:10; GENESIS 20:6"> The people-groups that you are about to expel from the land that you will occupy ◄consult/seek advice from► soothsayers and those who practice divination. But as for you, Yahweh our God does not allow you to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 3:22–23; ACTS 7:37; JOHN 1:45"> Some day he will send from among you a prophet like me. He is the one who will tell you what will happen in the future, and he is the one whom you must heed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 20:19; DEUTERONOMY 5:23–28; DEUTERONOMY 9:10"> On the day that your ancestors were gathered at the bottom of Sinai Mountain, they pleaded with me saying, ‘We do not want Yahweh to speak &#91;MTY&#93; to us again, and we do not want to see this huge fire that is burning on the mountain!’ Your ancestors said that because they were afraid that they would die if Yahweh spoke to them again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:28"> Then Yahweh said to me, ‘What they have said is true/wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:15; JOHN 8:28; JOHN 12:49–50"> So I will send from among them a prophet who will be like you. I will tell him what to say, and then he will tell people everything that I tell him to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ACTS 3:22–23; HEBREWS 2:3; HEBREWS 10:26"> He will speak for me. And I will punish anyone who does not heed what he says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:1–5; JEREMIAH 27:15; JEREMIAH 23:13–15"> But if any other person says that he is a prophet and dares to speak a message which he falsely says comes from me but which I did not tell him to speak, or if anyone who speaks a message that he says other gods &#91;MTY&#93; have revealed to him, he must be executed for doing that.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 JOHN 4:1–3; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:24; REVELATION 2:2"> But if you say to yourself, ‘How can we know if a message that someone tells us does not come from Yahweh?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:20; 2 KINGS 20:1; JEREMIAH 28:1–17"> The answer is that when someone speaks a message about what will happen in the future, a message that he says was revealed by Yahweh, if what he says does not happen, you will know that the message did not come from Yahweh. That person has wrongly claimed that it was revealed to him by Yahweh. So you do not need to be afraid of anything that he says.”</span>
</div>
</div>
