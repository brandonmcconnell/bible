## The blessings from obeying God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; EXODUS 23:22; LEVITICUS 26:3–13"> “If you do what Yahweh our God tells you to do and faithfully obey everything that I am today commanding you to do, he will cause you to become greater than any other nation on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:15; 1 TIMOTHY 4:8"> If you obey Yahweh, this is what he will do to bless you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 144:12–15; GENESIS 39:5; Haggai 2:19"> He will bless everything that you do when you are in the cities and he will bless everything that you do when you are working in the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:22; GENESIS 49:25; DEUTERONOMY 7:13"> He will bless you by giving you many children &#91;IDM&#93; and by giving you abundant crops, and plenty of cattle and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He will bless you by causing you to have good harvests with plenty of grain &#91;MTY&#93; to make bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 121:8; NUMBERS 27:17; 2 CHRONICLES 1:10"> He will bless you everywhere—when you go out of your houses and when you come into your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 22:38–41; LEVITICUS 26:7–8; PSALMS 89:23"> Yahweh will enable you to defeat the armies of your enemies; they will attack you from one direction, but they will run away from you in seven/many directions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:10; LEVITICUS 26:10; PROVERBS 3:9–10"> Yahweh will bless you by filling your barns with grain, and he will bless all the work that you do; he will bless you in the land that he is giving to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 19:5–6; DEUTERONOMY 7:6; DEUTERONOMY 26:18–19"> “If you obey all the commandments that Yahweh our God has given to you and if you conduct your lives as he wants you to do, you will continue to be his people, which is what he promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:14; DEUTERONOMY 11:25; JEREMIAH 33:9"> “When all the people-groups on the earth will realize that you belong to &#91;MTY&#93; Yahweh, and they will be afraid of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:9; PROVERBS 10:22; LEVITICUS 26:9"> “And Yahweh will cause you to prosper very much. He will give you many children &#91;IDM&#93;, many cattle, and abundant crops in the land that he vowed to our ancestors that he would give to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:6; JOEL 2:23–24; DEUTERONOMY 15:10"> “When rain is needed, Yahweh will send it from where he stores it in the sky, and he will bless all your work, with the result that you will be able to lend money to many other nations, but you will not need to borrow money from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:1; DEUTERONOMY 4:6–9; PHILIPPIANS 1:27"> If you faithfully obey all of the commands of Yahweh our God that I am giving you today, Yahweh will cause your nation to be a leader among the nations, not inferior to them; you will always be prosperous and never be needy/poor &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; DEUTERONOMY 5:32; DEUTERONOMY 11:16"> Yahweh will do all these things for you if you do not turn away from obeying what I am commanding you today, and if you never worship or serve other gods.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The curses from disobeying God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MALACHI 2:2; DEUTERONOMY 28:2; DEUTERONOMY 29:20"> But if you do not do what Yahweh our God is telling you to do, and if you do not faithfully obey all his rules and regulations that I am giving to you today, this is what he will do to curse you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MALACHI 3:9–12; PROVERBS 3:33; JOEL 1:4"> He will curse you when you are in the cities and he will curse you when you are working in the fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:5; PSALMS 69:22"> He will curse you by not allowing you to have good harvests with plenty of grain &#91;MTY&#93; to make bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:26; LEVITICUS 26:19–20; JOB 18:16–19"> He will curse you by giving you only a few children &#91;IDM&#93;, by causing you to have poor crops, and by not enabling you to have many cattle and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:6; JUDGES 5:6–7; 2 CHRONICLES 15:5"> He will curse you everywhere—when you go out of your houses and when you come into your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 66:15; MALACHI 2:2; DEUTERONOMY 4:26"> If you do evil things and reject Yahweh, he will curse you by causing you to ◄be confused/not know what to do►, and to be frustrated in everything that you do, until your enemies quickly and completely destroy you &#123;you will be quickly and completely destroyed&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:25; JEREMIAH 24:10; JEREMIAH 16:4"> Yahweh will cause you to experience terrible diseases, until not one of you remains alive in the land that you are about to enter and occupy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="AMOS 4:9; LEVITICUS 26:16; Haggai 2:17"> Yahweh will strike/afflict you with diseases that shrivel your bodies, with fever, with inflammation/swelling. It will be extremely hot, and it will not rain. There will be scorching winds, and Yahweh will cause your crops to rot. All these things will strike you until you die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:19; JEREMIAH 14:1–6; 1 KINGS 18:2"> There will be no rain &#91;MET&#93;, with the result that the ground will be as hard as iron &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:12; Isaiah 5:24; AMOS 4:11"> Instead of sending rain, Yahweh will send strong winds to blow sand and dust over your land, until your land is ruined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 30:17; EZEKIEL 23:46; JEREMIAH 24:9"> Yahweh will enable your enemies to defeat you; your soldiers will attack your enemies from one direction, but they will run away from your enemies in seven/many directions, and when the people of other nations see what is happening to you, they will say that it is horrible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 34:20; JEREMIAH 16:4; JEREMIAH 7:33"> You will die, and birds and wild animals will come and eat your corpses, and there will not be anyone to scare/shoo them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 5:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:35; LEVITICUS 21:20"> Yahweh will cause you to have boils on your skin, like he caused the people of Egypt to have many years ago. He will cause you to have tumors, open sores, and your skin will itch, and there will be nothing that will cure those diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 2:9–11; Isaiah 19:11–17; Isaiah 6:9–10"> Yahweh will cause some of you to become insane; he will cause some of you to become blind, and he will cause some of your minds to become confused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 59:10; JOB 5:14; JUDGES 6:1–6"> Because you will not be able to see where you are going, at midday you will ◄grope/feel your way► around with your hands, like people do in the darkness. You will continually be oppressed and robbed, and no one will help you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="AMOS 5:11; JEREMIAH 8:10; JOB 31:10"> Some of you men will be engaged/promised to marry a young woman, but someone else will rape her. You will build houses, but you will never live in them. You will plant grapevines, but you will not eat the grapes; someone else will eat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOB 1:14–15; JUDGES 6:1"> Your enemies will butcher your cattle while you watch them, and you will not get any of the meat to eat. They will drag away your donkeys while you watch them do it, and they will not give them back to you. They will take away your sheep; and no one will help you rescue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JOEL 3:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:41; JOB 17:5"> While you watch, your sons and daughters will be given to foreigners to become their slaves. Every day you will very sadly watch for your children to return, but ◄you will watch in vain/they will never return►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:17; DEUTERONOMY 28:29–30; Isaiah 1:7"> People from a foreign nation will take all the crops that you worked hard to produce, and they will constantly treat you harshly and cruelly &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="REVELATION 16:10–11; DEUTERONOMY 28:68; Isaiah 33:14"> The result will be that all these terrible things that you see/experience will cause you to become insane.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:27; Isaiah 3:24; Isaiah 3:17"> Yahweh will cause your legs to be covered with painful boils that cannot be healed, and you will have boils from the bottoms of your feet to the tops of your heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JEREMIAH 16:13; DEUTERONOMY 4:28; 2 KINGS 25:11"> Yahweh will cause your king and the rest of you to be taken to another country, to a place in which you and your ancestors have never lived before, and there you will worship and serve gods that are made of wood or stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:9; 1 KINGS 9:7–8; JEREMIAH 24:9"> When the people-groups in nearby countries see what has happened to you, they will be shocked/horrified; they will make fun &#91;DOU&#93; of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; MICAH 6:15; JOEL 1:4"> You will plant plenty of seeds in your fields, but you will reap only a small harvest, because locusts will eat the crops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; JOEL 2:2–4; JONAH 4:7"> You will plant grapevines and take care of them, but you will not pick any grapes to make wine, because worms will eat the vines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MICAH 6:15; PSALMS 23:5; PSALMS 104:15"> Olive trees will grow everywhere in your land, but you will not get any olive oil to rub on your skin because the olives will drop on the ground before they are ripe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:32; LAMENTATIONS 1:5; 2 KINGS 24:14"> You will have sons and daughters, but they will not stay with you, because they will be captured and taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="AMOS 7:1–2; DEUTERONOMY 28:38–39"> Swarms of locusts/insects will eat your crops and the leaves of all your trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:13; 1 SAMUEL 13:19–23; 2 KINGS 17:20"> Foreigners who live in your land will become more and more powerful, and you will become less and less powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:12–13; LAMENTATIONS 1:5"> They will have money to lend to you, but you will not have any money to lend to them. They will be superior &#91;IDM&#93; to you, and you will be inferior to them &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:15; Isaiah 1:20; JEREMIAH 24:9–10"> “You will experience all these disasters, and you will continue to experience them you until you are destroyed, if you do not do what Yahweh your God told you to do, and do not obey all the rules and regulations that he gave to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; DEUTERONOMY 28:37; EZEKIEL 14:8"> “These disasters will warn &#91;DOU&#93; you and your descendants forever about what happens to groups who disobey Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:11; DEUTERONOMY 12:7–12; Nehemiah 9:35–37"> “Because Yahweh blessed you abundantly in many ways, you should have served him very joyfully &#91;DOU&#93;, but you did not do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="JEREMIAH 28:13–14; LAMENTATIONS 5:2–6; EZEKIEL 4:16–17"> “Therefore, you will work for the enemies whom Yahweh will send to attack you. You will be hungry and thirsty; you will not have clothes to wear; and you will lack everything else that you need. And Yahweh will cause you to become slaves &#91;MET&#93; and work hard until he gets rid of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:19; JEREMIAH 49:22; JEREMIAH 48:40"> “Yahweh will cause an army from very far away &#91;HYP&#93; to attack you, whose soldiers speak a language that you do not know/understand. They will swoop down on you quickly like an eagle &#91;SIM&#93; attacks its prey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Isaiah 47:6; 2 CHRONICLES 36:17; LUKE 21:23–24"> They will be fierce looking. They will not act mercifully toward anyone, not even young children and old people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:33; Isaiah 62:8; LEVITICUS 26:26"> They will kill and eat your livestock, and they will eat your crops, and you will ◄starve/die because you do not have anything to eat►. They will not leave for you any grain or wine or olive oil or cattle or sheep; and you will all die from hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:4–7; EZEKIEL 4:1–8; MATTHEW 24:15–16"> Your enemies will surround your towns throughout the land that Yahweh your God is about to give to you, and they will break down the high and strong walls around your towns, walls which you trusted/thought would protect you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:10; JEREMIAH 19:9; LEVITICUS 26:29"> “When your enemies are surrounding your towns, you will be extremely hungry, with the result that you will eat the flesh of your own sons and daughters that Yahweh your God has given to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:9; MATTHEW 20:15; DEUTERONOMY 13:6"> When your enemies have surrounded your towns, even the most gentle and sensitive men among you will want food very desperately, with the result that they will kill their own children and eat their flesh, because they will not have anything else to eat. They will not even share any of it with their brothers or wives whom they love very much or with any of their children who are still alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:54; Isaiah 3:16; LAMENTATIONS 4:3–6"> Even the most gentle and sensitive/kind women among you, who are very refined/rich with the result that they have never had to walk anywhere &#91;HYP&#93;, will do the same thing. When your enemies have surrounded your towns, those women will be extremely hungry, with the result that after they give birth to a baby, they will secretly kill it and eat its flesh and also eat its afterbirth. And they will not share any of it with their husbands whom they love very much or with any of their other children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Isaiah 42:8; JEREMIAH 7:9–10; MALACHI 1:14"> “If you do not faithfully obey all of the laws that I am writing, and if you do not revere Yahweh our glorious God &#91;MTY&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="MARK 13:19; DEUTERONOMY 28:46; DANIEL 9:12"> he will punish you by causing you and your descendants to experience great afflictions and plagues which will last for many years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:15; EXODUS 15:26; DEUTERONOMY 28:27"> He will cause you to experience the plagues that he sent on the people of Egypt, and you will never be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:25–26"> He will also cause you to experience many other sicknesses and diseases that I have not talked about in these laws, until you all will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:22; Nehemiah 9:23; DEUTERONOMY 4:27"> You became as numerous as the stars in the sky, but only a few of you will remain alive, if you do not obey Yahweh your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:26; JEREMIAH 32:41; DEUTERONOMY 30:9"> Yahweh was very happy to do good things for you and to cause you to become very numerous, but now he will be happy to ruin you and get rid of you. Those of you who do not die from these plagues will be snatched away from the land that you will soon be entering to possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:27–28; LEVITICUS 26:33; JEREMIAH 16:13"> “Yahweh will scatter you among many people-groups, all over the earth, and in those areas/places you will serve other gods that are made of wood or stone, gods which you and your ancestors have never known/worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:36; ROMANS 11:10; AMOS 9:4"> In those places you will be restless. Yahweh will cause you to be constantly worried/anxious, feel hopeless, and discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:13; HEBREWS 10:27; REVELATION 6:15–17"> You will always be afraid that your enemies will kill you. You will be very fearful, all day and all night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:34; JOB 7:3–4; REVELATION 9:6"> Because you will be very fearful/afraid because of the terrible things that you see, each morning you will say ‘I wish it were evening already!’ and each evening you will say ‘I wish it were morning already!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:12; JEREMIAH 43:7; HOSEA 8:13"> Yahweh will send some of you back to Egypt in ships, even though he promised that you would never be forced to go there again. There in Egypt you will try to sell yourselves to become slaves of your enemies in order to have food to eat, but no one will buy you. All those things will happen to you if you do not obey the laws that Yahweh is giving to you.”</span>
