## The Passover Festival

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 34:18; EXODUS 12:2–20; EXODUS 13:4"> “Each year honor Yahweh our God by celebrating the Passover Festival in the month of Abib in early spring. It was on a night in that month that Yahweh rescued your ancestors from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:5; DEUTERONOMY 12:26; LUKE 22:15"> In order to celebrate that festival, go to the place that Yahweh will choose for you to worship him, and offer there one young animal from your cattle or your sheep to be the Passover sacrifice, to honor Yahweh &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 12:39; EXODUS 34:18; EXODUS 12:19–20"> When you eat the Passover meal, the bread that you eat must not have yeast in it. You must eat this kind of bread, which will be called the bread of suffering/misery, for seven days. This is to help you to remember all during the time that you are alive that when your ancestors left Egypt, where they were suffering because they were slaves, they left very quickly. They did not put in yeast and wait for the dough to swell up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 34:25; EXODUS 12:10; EXODUS 13:7"> During that festival, you must not have any yeast in any house in your land for seven days. Furthermore, the meat of the animals that you sacrifice on the evening of the first day of the Passover Festival must be eaten during that night; do not allow any of it to remain until the next day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:2; DEUTERONOMY 12:5–6"> “To honor Yahweh our God, you must offer the Passover sacrifice only at the place that he chooses for you to worship him; do not offer that sacrifice in any other town in the land that Yahweh is giving to you. Offer that sacrifice when the sun is setting, at the same time of day that your ancestors started to leave Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:13; EXODUS 12:8–9; JOHN 2:23"> Boil the meat and eat it at the place of worship that Yahweh our God chooses. The next morning, you may return to your tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 13:6–8; LEVITICUS 23:6–8; LEVITICUS 23:36"> Each day for six days the bread that you eat must have no yeast in it. On the seventh day, you must all gather to worship Yahweh our God. It will be a day of rest; you must not do any work on that day.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The festival for harvesting grain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 34:22; EXODUS 23:16; ACTS 2:1"> “Each year, from the day that you start to harvest your grain, count seven weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 16:2; LEVITICUS 12:8; MALACHI 3:10–11"> Then, to honor Yahweh our God, celebrate the Harvest Festival. Do that by bringing to him an offering of grain. Yahweh has blessed you by causing it to grow in your fields during that year. If you had a big harvest, bring a big offering. If you had a small harvest, bring a small offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:7; DEUTERONOMY 12:12; DEUTERONOMY 12:18"> Each married couple should be joyful in the presence of Yahweh. Their children, their servants, the descendants of Levi who are in that town, and the foreigners, orphans, and widows who are living among you, should also be joyful. Bring those offerings to the place of worship that Yahweh will choose.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:15; LAMENTATIONS 3:19–20; ROMANS 6:17–18"> “When you celebrate these festivals by obeying these commands, remember that your ancestors were previously slaves in Egypt.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Festival of Living in Shelters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 23:16; JOHN 7:2; 2 CHRONICLES 5:3"> “Each year, after you have threshed all your grain and pressed the juice from all your grapes, you must celebrate the Festival of Living in Temporary Shelters for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9–12; DEUTERONOMY 16:11; Isaiah 30:29"> Each married couple along with their children, their servants, the descendants of Levi who are in that town, and the foreigners, orphans, and widows who are living among you, should be joyful in the presence of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:36–42; DEUTERONOMY 30:16; DEUTERONOMY 16:10"> Honor Yahweh our God by celebrating this festival for seven days in the place that Yahweh chooses for you to worship him. You should all be joyful, because Yahweh will have blessed your harvest and all the other work that you have done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:9–10; PSALMS 96:8; Haggai 1:9"> “So, each year all of you Israeli men must gather with your families to worship Yahweh our God at the place that he will choose, to celebrate three festivals: The Passover Festival, the Harvest Festival, and the Festival of Living in Temporary Shelters. Each of you men must bring &#91;LIT&#93; an offering for Yahweh to these festivals</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 9:6–7; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:12; DEUTERONOMY 16:10"> in proportion to the blessings that Yahweh has given you during that year.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judging people fairly

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:4; EXODUS 18:25–26; ROMANS 13:1–6"> “Appoint judges and other officials throughout your tribes, in all the towns in the land that Yahweh our God is giving to you. They must judge people fairly/justly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:15; EXODUS 23:2; PROVERBS 17:23"> They must not judge unjustly. They must not favor one person more than another. The judges must not accept bribes, because if a judge accepts a bribe, even if he is wise and honest, it will be very difficult for him to judge fairly &#91;IDM&#93;; he will do what the person who gave him the bribe wants him to do and declare that the righteous/innocent people must be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:8; EZEKIEL 18:9; EZEKIEL 18:5"> You must be completely fair and just &#91;DOU&#93;, in order that you will enter and occupy the land that Yahweh our God is giving to you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Warning against worshiping other gods

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 33:3; 2 KINGS 17:16; EXODUS 34:13"> “When you make an altar to worship Yahweh our God, do not put next to it any wooden statue that represents the goddess Asherah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:1; EXODUS 20:4; ZECHARIAH 8:17"> And do not set up any stone pillar to worship any idol, because Yahweh hates them.”</span>
</div>
</div>
