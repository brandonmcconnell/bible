## A prophecy about Yahweh bringing his people back to Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:4; EZEKIEL 34:14; EZEKIEL 37:22"> You human, prophesy about the mountains in Israel. Say to them, “You mountains in Israel, listen to what Yahweh says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:13; EZEKIEL 25:3; EZEKIEL 35:10"> This is what Yahweh the Lord says: Your enemies happily said about you, ‘Aha! The mountains in Israel that have existed for a long time have become ours!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 44:13–14; LAMENTATIONS 2:15–16; JEREMIAH 18:16"> Therefore prophesy about those mountains and say, “This is what Yahweh the Lord declares: Armies of other nations attacked you &#91;DOU&#93; from every direction, with the result that your land became abandoned, and people of other nations occupied you, and they spoke maliciously about you and slandered you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:28; DEUTERONOMY 11:11; EZEKIEL 36:6"> Therefore, you mountains in Israel, listen to this message from me. I, Yahweh the Lord, have something to say to the hills and mountains, to the ravines and valleys, to the deserted ruins and towns whose valuable possessions have been taken and their people ridiculed by the people of other nations around them. This is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare: I am very angry &#91;DOU&#93; with the people of Edom and other nations; they have forced you Israeli people to endure them insulting you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:5; AMOS 1:1–15; EZEKIEL 20:15"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: Lifting up my arm, I solemnly declare that the people of the nations that are around you will also endure being insulted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; HEBREWS 10:37; EZEKIEL 34:26–29"> But I say to you mountains in Israel that huge crops of fruit will grow on your trees for my Israeli people, because they will soon return home from Babylonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 46:11; HOSEA 2:21–23; EZEKIEL 36:34"> I ◄am concerned about/will take care of► you, and I will bless you. I will enable farmers to plow the ground and plant seed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:33; JEREMIAH 33:12; EZEKIEL 36:37"> I will cause the number of people who live on you mountains and everywhere else in Israel to greatly increase. People will live in the towns and rebuild houses where there are now only ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:12; JEREMIAH 23:5–8; JEREMIAH 31:27"> I will cause the number of people and domestic animals to increase. People will have many children &#91;IDM, DOU&#93;. I will enable people to live there like they did previously, and I will enable them to prosper like they did before. Then you will know that it is I, Yahweh, who have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:7; EZEKIEL 36:13; JEREMIAH 32:44"> I will enable my Israeli people to walk through your mountains. They will own you; you will be their permanent possession. Never again will their children die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:32"> This is also what I, Yahweh the Lord, say to you: People say that only a small amount of food is produced on your mountains, and as a result the people of Israel die from hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:25–28; AMOS 9:15; Isaiah 60:21"> Therefore, I, Yahweh the Lord, declare that no longer will that happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:29; PSALMS 89:50; EZEKIEL 36:6"> No longer will the people of other nations ridicule you; no longer will they laugh at you; no longer will the armies of other nations conquer you. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have declared that it will happen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:7; NUMBERS 35:33–34; MICAH 2:10"> “You human, when the Israeli people were living in their own land, they ◄defiled it/caused it to become unacceptable to me► by the things that they did. I considered that their behavior was disgusting, like &#91;SIM&#93; the rags/cloths that women use during their monthly menstrual periods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 34:21; EZEKIEL 7:8; LAMENTATIONS 2:4"> So I severely punished &#91;MTY&#93; them, because they had murdered &#91;MTY&#93; many people and because they had defiled the land by worshiping idols there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:24; DEUTERONOMY 28:64; EZEKIEL 5:12"> I caused them to be scattered among many nations &#91;DOU&#93;. I punished &#91;MTY&#93; them like they deserved to be punished because of their very evil behavior &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 2:24; Isaiah 52:5; JEREMIAH 33:24"> And wherever they went among those nations, they caused my reputation &#91;MTY&#93; to be disgraced. What has happened is that the people in those countries said about the Israeli people, ‘They belong to Yahweh, but they were forced to leave the land that he gave to them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; PSALMS 74:18; EZEKIEL 20:9"> But I was concerned about my reputation, which the people of Israel had disgraced among the nations to which they were forced to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 106:8; EZEKIEL 36:32; PSALMS 115:1–2"> Therefore, say to the Israeli people, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: You Israeli people, it is not for your sake that I am going to do these things. Instead, it is for the sake of my sacred reputation, which you have disgraced among the nations to which you were forced to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:41; Isaiah 5:16; EZEKIEL 39:7"> I will show that my reputation is sacred, a reputation which you have disgraced among the nations to which you were forced to go. And when I show the nations that I am holy, the people of those nations will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:21; EZEKIEL 34:13; AMOS 9:14–15"> What I will do is to bring you back from those nations. I will gather you from all the countries to which you were forced to go, and bring you back to your own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:22; JOHN 3:5; JEREMIAH 33:8"> It will be as though &#91;MET&#93; I will sprinkle clean water on you, and then you will be clean. I will cause you to be cleansed from everything that has defiled you, and I will cause you to stop worshiping idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:19–20; PSALMS 51:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:17"> I will give you a completely new way of thinking &#91;IDM&#93;. I will enable you to stop being stubborn &#91;IDM&#93;, and I will enable you to obey me from your inner beings &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="TITUS 3:3–6; HEBREWS 13:21; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:16"> I will put my Spirit within you and enable you to carefully obey all my laws &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:33; JEREMIAH 32:38; EZEKIEL 37:27"> You will again live in the land that I gave to your ancestors. You will be my people, and I will be your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 1:7–9; MATTHEW 1:21; HOSEA 2:21–23"> I will free you from all your disgraceful behavior. I will cause your grain to be plentiful, and I will not send a famine to you again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:27; JOEL 2:26; DEUTERONOMY 29:23–28"> I will cause your fruit trees to produce plenty of fruit and your ground to produce plenty of good crops, with the result that you will no longer be ridiculed by the people of other nations because of your not having enough food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:9; EZEKIEL 20:43; Nehemiah 9:26–35"> When that happens, you will think about your previous evil behavior and wicked deeds, and you will ◄be very displeased with/hate► yourselves for your sins and the detestable things that you did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:5; EZEKIEL 36:22; 2 TIMOTHY 1:9"> But I, Yahweh the Lord, declare that I want you to know that it is not for your sake that I will do those things. You Israeli people ought to be ashamed of your behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; EZEKIEL 36:10; JEREMIAH 33:10"> This is also what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: At the time that I forgive you for all the sins that you have committed, I will enable you to live in your towns again and to build houses where there are now only ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:14; JEREMIAH 25:9–11; 2 CHRONICLES 36:21"> People who walk through your country will see that your land is again cultivated instead of ◄being desolate/having nothing growing in it►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; JOEL 2:3; JEREMIAH 33:9"> They will say, “This land that was ruined has become very fertile/productive like &#91;SIM&#93; the garden of Eden! The cities that were piles of ruins, empty and destroyed, now have walls around them, and people live in those cities.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:35; NUMBERS 23:19; EZEKIEL 17:24"> When that happens, the people in the nations that are around you that still exist will know that it is I, Yahweh, who have enabled you to rebuild what was destroyed, and to again plant crops in the fields that had nothing growing in them. I, Yahweh, have said that it will happen, and I will cause it to happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 JOHN 5:14; HEBREWS 10:21–22; EZEKIEL 14:3"> This is also what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: Again I will heed what you Israeli people are pleading for me to do for you. I will cause your people to be as numerous as your sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:63; ACTS 2:5–11; JOHN 10:16"> as numerous as the flocks of sheep that will be needed for offerings in Jerusalem during your regular festivals. The cities that are now ruined will be filled with people, and then you will know that I, Yahweh the Lord, have the power to do what I say that I will do.’”</span>
