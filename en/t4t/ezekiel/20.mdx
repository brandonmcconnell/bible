## The people of Israel continued to rebel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:1; EZEKIEL 30:20; EZEKIEL 24:1"> Almost seven years afterKing Jehoiachin and the rest of us had been captured and taken to Babylonia, on the tenth day of the fifth monthof that year, some Israeli elders came to me to askif I had a message for them from Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Then Yahweh gave me a message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:9; MATTHEW 15:8–9; PSALMS 50:15–21"> “You human, speak to the Israeli elders and say to them, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: Yousay that you have come to ask if there is any message from me &#91;RHQ&#93;, but as surely as I am alive, I will notanswer you if you ask meto tell you anything.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:2; EZEKIEL 23:36; EZEKIEL 14:20"> If you are &#91;RHQ&#93; willing to warn them, remind them of the detestable things that their ancestors did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:6; EZEKIEL 20:15; EXODUS 3:8"> Then say to them, ‘On the day that I chose you Israeli peopleto belong to me, with my hand lifted up, I made a solemn promise to your ancestorswhile they were still in Egypt. I said to them, “I am Yahweh, your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 3:8; JEREMIAH 32:22; EZEKIEL 20:15"> I will bring you out of Egypt and lead you to a land that I have chosen for you. It is a very fertile &#91;IDM&#93; and ◄very beautiful/more beautiful than any other► land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:3; EZEKIEL 20:8; LEVITICUS 11:44"> Each of you must get rid of the disgusting idols that you loved &#91;IDM&#93;, and do not defile yourselves byworshiping those idolsthat you saw in Egypt. I, Yahweh your God, am saying this to you.” ’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:7; Isaiah 63:10; EZEKIEL 20:21"> But they rebelled against me. They would not pay attention to me. They did not get rid of the disgusting idols they loved, and they did not reject those idolsthat they saw in Egypt. So because I was angry with them, I said that I would punish &#91;MTY&#93; them in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:7; EZEKIEL 20:22; EXODUS 32:12"> But for the sake of my own reputation, I decided to do something in order that I would not be dishonored by the people of the nations who would see it when I brought my people out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 15:22; EXODUS 13:17–18; EXODUS 14:17–22"> Therefore I led them out of Egypt and brought them into the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 10:5; LEVITICUS 18:5; DEUTERONOMY 4:8"> I gave them all my laws and decrees &#91;DOU&#93;, in order that they would obey them, and as a result they would live for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:20; EZEKIEL 37:28; LEVITICUS 21:23"> Also, I established the Sabbath/rest days to be a symbol between us to show that I, Yahweh, had caused them to be set apartfrom the other nations.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:21; EZEKIEL 20:8; Isaiah 56:6"> But the Israeli people rebelled against me in the desert. They did not obey my commands &#91;DOU&#93;; they rejected them, even though they tell people who obey them that they will live for a long time, and they profaned the Sabbath days. So I said that I would get rid of them in the desert, and that would show that I was very angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:9; EZEKIEL 36:22–23; EZEKIEL 20:22"> But again, for the sake of my own reputation, I decided to do something else in order that I would not be dishonored/despised by the people of the nations who had seen me bring my people out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 95:11; PSALMS 106:26; HEBREWS 3:18"> I lifted up my hand and solemnly declared to them in the desert that I would not take them into the land that Ihad promised to give them, a land that was very fertile &#91;IDM&#93; and ◄very beautiful/more beautiful than any other► land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:39; EZEKIEL 20:8; EZEKIEL 14:3–4"> The reason that I promised that was because they rejected and disobeyed all my laws &#91;DOU&#93; and ◄desecrated/refused to rest on► the Sabbath days. They did that because they &#91;SYN&#93; were devoted toworshiping their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:13; JEREMIAH 4:27; JEREMIAH 5:18"> But, I still pitied them and did not get rid of all of them in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:2–4; PSALMS 78:6–8; 1 PETER 1:18"> I said to their children, ‘Do not do the things that your parents continually did, or defile yourselves byworshiping their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:27; DEUTERONOMY 5:32–8; EZEKIEL 37:24"> I am Yahweh your God. Carefully obey &#91;DOU&#93; my laws and commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; JEREMIAH 17:22; EXODUS 31:13–17"> Respect my Sabbath days, in order that by doing that &#91;PRS&#93;, it will remind you that you belong to me, Yahweh your God.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:13; NUMBERS 21:5; EZEKIEL 20:8"> But their children also rebelled against me. They did not carefully obey my laws &#91;DOU&#93;, even though the people who obey them will live for a long time; and they profaned the Sabbath days. So again I said that by getting rid of them in the desert, I would show that I was very angry with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:14; EZEKIEL 20:9; JEREMIAH 14:21"> But I did not do that &#91;IDM&#93;. Again, for the sake of my own reputation, I decided to do something in order that I would not be dishonored/despised by the people of the nations who had seen it when I brought my people out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:33; DEUTERONOMY 28:64–68; DEUTERONOMY 32:26–27"> I lifted up my hand and solemnly declared to them in the desert that I would cause them to be scattered among many nations &#91;DOU&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:16; EZEKIEL 6:9; AMOS 2:4"> because they had rejected and disobeyed all my laws &#91;DOU&#93; and desecrated the Sabbath days, and they had eagerly desiredto worship the idols that their parentshad worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 81:12; Isaiah 66:4; EZEKIEL 20:39"> I also allowed them to obey laws that were not good, and laws that would not enable them to live a long time if they obeyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:7; LEVITICUS 18:21; EZEKIEL 20:31"> I allowed them to become unacceptable to me by their sacrificing their firstborn children in the fire. I did that in order that they would be horrified, and in order that they would know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ROMANS 2:24; EZEKIEL 2:7; EZEKIEL 3:11"> Therefore, you human, speak to the Israeli people. Say this to them, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says to you: This is one way that your ancestors dishonored me by turning away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:13; Nehemiah 9:22–26; JEREMIAH 3:6"> After I brought them into the land that I had solemnly promised to give to them, every time that they saw a high hill or a big green tree, they offered sacrificesto idols there. They made offerings to them, and that caused me to become angry. They presented to those idols their fragrant burning incense, and poured out wine offerings to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:24–25; EZEKIEL 16:31"> Then I asked them, “Who &#91;RHQ&#93; told you to worship at a shrine like this, here on this hilltop?” So they are still called “hilltop shrines”.’</span>
</div>
</div>

## Punishment and restoration

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 16:12; JEREMIAH 7:26; JUDGES 2:19"> Therefore, say this to the Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93;: 'This is what Yahweh the Lord says: “You are going to &#91;RHQ&#93; defile yourselves like your ancestors did by strongly desiringto worship their disgusting statues of idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:26; PSALMS 106:37–39; JEREMIAH 7:31"> When you sacrifice your children in the fire, you are still continuing to make yourselves unacceptable to me byworshiping your many idols. You Israeli people, should I allow you to inquirewhat I want you to do? I, Yahweh the Lord, say that as surely as I am alive, I will not answer if you inquire.'</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:5; JEREMIAH 44:17; PSALMS 139:2"> You say, ‘We want to be like the other nations, like the other people-groups in the world. We want to worshipidols made of wood and stonelike they do.’ But what you want will never happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:5; EZEKIEL 8:18; JEREMIAH 44:6"> I, Yahweh your Lord, say that as surely as I am alive, I will use my great power &#91;DOU, MTY&#93; to rule over you, and show that I am angry with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:38; AMOS 9:9–10; EZEKIEL 34:16"> With my great power I will gather you from the places to which you have been scattered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="HOSEA 2:14; JEREMIAH 2:35; EZEKIEL 17:20"> and I will bring you to a desertthat is surrounded by other nations. There, while I am looking at you, I will judge you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:5–10; NUMBERS 11:1–35; EZEKIEL 20:13"> I will punish you, like I punished your ancestors in the desert near Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:13; LEVITICUS 27:32; MATTHEW 25:32–33"> I will count you &#91;MTY&#93; as you walk by, and I will force you to obey the agreement that I made with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:9; AMOS 9:9–10; PSALMS 95:11"> I will get rid of those people among you who rebel &#91;DOU&#93; against me. Although I will bring them out ofBabylonia, where they are now living, they will not enter Israel. Then you will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="HOSEA 4:17; Isaiah 1:13–15; EZEKIEL 43:7"> As for you Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93;, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: 'Go and worship your idols now &#91;IRO&#93;, each of you. But afterwards, you will surely heed me and no longer dishonor me &#91;MTY&#93; by taking gifts to your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; MALACHI 3:4; Isaiah 60:7"> I, Yahweh your Lord, declare that there on my sacred hill, Zion, that high hill in Israel, you will bring gifts to me, and I will accept them. I will require you to bring to me gifts and offerings there, and your sacrifices that are dedicated to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:25; EZEKIEL 11:17; EZEKIEL 38:23"> When I bring you out from the other nations &#91;DOU&#93; to which you have been scattered, I will accept youas though you were fragrant incense. By doing that, I will show the people of other nations that I have set you apart to belong to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:13; EZEKIEL 38:23; EZEKIEL 36:23–24"> Then, when I bring you into the land of Israel, the land that I solemnly promised with my hand lifted up that I would give to your ancestors, you will know that I, Yahweh, have done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:9; HOSEA 5:15; JEREMIAH 31:18"> And therein Israel you will remember how you conducted your lives previously, you will remember the actions that caused you to become unacceptable to me, and you will hate/despise yourselves for all the evil things that you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:24; PSALMS 115:1; EZEKIEL 20:9"> When I act toward you Israeli people to protect my reputation, and not just because of your evil deeds and corrupt behavior, you Israeli people will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.' That is what Yahweh the Lord declares.”</span>
</div>

## Prophecy against the southern part of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="AMOS 7:16; EZEKIEL 21:2; JEREMIAH 13:19"> “You human, turn toward the south. Preach about what will happen to that dry land, to the forest there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:14; Isaiah 24:1–6; EZEKIEL 17:24"> Say to the forest in the southpart of Israel: 'Listen to this message that Yahweh the Lord is saying about you: “I am about to start a fire in your midst, and it will burn up all your trees, both the green trees and the withered/dry trees. Nothing will extinguish the blazing flames. And the fire will scorch the faces of everyone who lives in that area, from the south to the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="JEREMIAH 40:2–3; JEREMIAH 7:20; DEUTERONOMY 29:24–28"> Everyone will see that it is I, Yahweh, who have lit this fire, and no one will be able to put it out.” ' ”</span>
