## A prophecy about Gog

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:1; EZEKIEL 27:13; EZEKIEL 38:3"> “You human, turn and face Magog, the country where Gog is the king. He is also the ruler of the nations of Meshech and Tubal. Prophesy about the terrible things that will happen to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:8; EZEKIEL 39:1–10; EZEKIEL 29:3"> and say, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: Gog, you who rule Meshech and Tubal, I am opposed to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:4; DANIEL 11:40; EZEKIEL 38:15"> It will be as though I will turn you around and put hooks in your jaws and bring you to Israel—you and all of your army, including your horses and men carrying weapons who ride the horses, and many other soldiers carrying large shields and small shields, all of them carrying swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:10; GENESIS 10:6; EZEKIEL 30:5"> Armies from Persia, Ethiopia, and Put/Libya will also come, all of them with shields and helmets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:14; GENESIS 10:2–3; 1 CHRONICLES 1:5–6"> An army from Gomer north of Israel will come, and an army from Togarmah far north of Israel will come. Armies of many nations will accompany you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 25:8; JOEL 3:9–12; Isaiah 37:22"> Tell Gog, ‘Get ready and be prepared to be the commander of all those groups of soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:16; Isaiah 24:22; EZEKIEL 38:11–12"> At some future time, I will command you to lead those armies to attack Israel, a country whose buildings have been rebuilt after they were destroyed in wars. Their people will have been brought back from many nations to live again on the hills of Israel, which had been deserted for a long time. They had been brought back from other nations and will be living peacefully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 28:2; JEREMIAH 4:13; JOEL 2:2"> You and all those armies from many nations will go up to Israel, advancing like &#91;SIM&#93; a big storm. Your army will be like a huge cloud that covers the land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 36:4; MICAH 2:1; PSALMS 83:3–4"> But this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: On that day, you will have an idea about &#91;DOU&#93; doing an evil thing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:4–5; PROVERBS 3:29–30; JEREMIAH 49:31–32"> You will say to yourself, “My army will invade a country where the villages do not have walls around them. We will attack people who are peaceful and do not suspect/think that they will be attacked. Their towns and villages do not have walls with gates and bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 10:6; EZEKIEL 38:8; EZEKIEL 29:19"> So it will be easy for us to attack the people who are living again in those towns that were previously destroyed. They are people who have been gathered from many countries where they had lived for many years, people who now live safely in their land with all their livestock and other possessions. They are living in the country that is in the middle of the most important countries (OR, that they think is &#91;IRO&#93; the most important country) in the world. Our soldiers will take away &#91;DOU&#93; all their valuable possessions.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:15; EZEKIEL 27:22–23; EZEKIEL 32:2"> Then people of Sheba and Dedan and the merchants of Tarshish and nearby villages will come and say to you, “Are you gathering all your soldiers in order to attack Israel and take away all their silver and gold? Do you plan to take away their livestock and all their other valuable possessions &#91;DOU&#93;?”’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:8; ZECHARIAH 2:5; JEREMIAH 23:6"> Therefore, you human, prophesy about Gog and say to him, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: At that time, when my people of Israel are living safely, you will certainly think about that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:2; EZEKIEL 38:6; REVELATION 20:8"> So you will come from your place far north of Israel, with the armies of many other nations, all riding horses, a huge army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:21; EZEKIEL 38:23; HOSEA 3:5"> You will march toward my Israeli people, and your soldiers will cover the land like a huge cloud. Gog, I will bring your army to attack the country that belongs to me, but what I do for you will show the people of other nations that I am holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DANIEL 11:40–45; Isaiah 63:1–6; JOEL 3:9–14"> This is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say to Gog: In past years, when I gave messages to my servants, the prophets in Israel, there were messages &#91;RHQ&#93; about you. At that time, they prophesied for many years that I would bring your armies to attack my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:5–6; HEBREWS 12:29; PSALMS 89:46"> So this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say will happen: When your army attacks Israel, I will be very angry with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–7; REVELATION 11:13; REVELATION 16:20"> I will be very furious, and to show that I am angry, there will be a great earthquake in Israel, where your armies will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HOSEA 4:3; NAHUM 1:4–6; REVELATION 6:12–13"> The fish in the sea, the birds, the wild animals, and the animals/creatures that crawl on the ground, and all the people on the earth will tremble because of what I am doing. Mountains will fall down, cliffs will crumble, and walls everywhere will fall to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Haggai 2:22; JUDGES 7:22; EZEKIEL 14:17"> Gog, on all the mountains in the country that belongs to me I will cause your soldiers to fight against each other with their swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; PSALMS 11:6; REVELATION 16:21"> I will punish &#91;MTY&#93; you and your soldiers with plagues and murders &#91;MTY&#93;. And I will send down from the sky, on you and your troops that have come from many nations, huge amounts of rain and hail and burning sulfur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 9:16; REVELATION 19:1–6; EZEKIEL 38:16"> By doing that, I will cause the people of many nations to know that I am very great and holy, and they will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do the things that I say that I will do.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
