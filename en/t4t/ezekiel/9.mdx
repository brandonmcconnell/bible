## The command to punish Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 6:8; EZEKIEL 43:6–7; 2 KINGS 10:24"> Then I heard Yahweh call out loudly, “You men who are going to punish this city, bring here the guards of the city, each carrying a weapon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:4; EZEKIEL 10:2; EZEKIEL 10:6–7"> Then I saw six men coming from the north gate of the temple area. Each one was carrying a weapon. With them was a man wearing a white linen robe. Hecarried at his side a case containing things to write with. They all came into the temple and stood beside the bronze altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:4; EZEKIEL 11:22–23; EZEKIEL 43:2–4"> Then the ◄glory/dazzling light► that symbolized the presence of the God of Israel rose up from above the four winged creatures and moved to the entrance of the temple. Then Yahweh called to the man wearing the linen robe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="REVELATION 9:4; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:22; REVELATION 14:1"> and said to him, “Go throughout Jerusalem and put a mark on the foreheads of those who are very sad &#91;DOU&#93; because of the detestable things that are being done inside the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:11; EZEKIEL 8:18; EZEKIEL 7:9"> While I was listening, he said to the other six men, “Follow the man wearing the white robe, and kill people. Do not pity them or act mercifully toward them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="REVELATION 9:4; EXODUS 12:23; JEREMIAH 25:29"> Slaughter old men, young men and women, older women and children, but do not harm any of those who have that mark on their forehead. Start at my temple.” So they began by killing the elders who were worshiping idols in front of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:17; EZEKIEL 7:20–22; LAMENTATIONS 2:4–7"> Then Yahweh said to those men, “Pollute the temple by filling the courtyardwith the corpses of those whom you kill! Start now!” So they went out and starting killing people throughout the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:13; NUMBERS 16:45; JOSHUA 7:6"> While they were doing that, I was left alone. I prostrated myself on the ground and cried out, “Yahweh my Lord, are you going to get rid of all the people of Israel and Judah who are still alive, while you are severely punishing &#91;MTY&#93; the people of Jerusalem?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 22:13; EZEKIEL 8:12; EZEKIEL 7:23"> He replied, “The sins of the people &#91;MTY&#93; of Israel and Judah are extremely great. People are murdered &#91;MTY&#93; everywhere in this country, and this city is full of people who act unjustly. They say, ‘Yahweh has abandoned this country, and he does not seewhat we are doing.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 7:4; EZEKIEL 8:18; EZEKIEL 11:21"> So I will not pity them or act mercifully toward them. I will do to them the evil things that they have done to other people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 6:7–8; REVELATION 16:17; REVELATION 16:2"> Then the man wearing the linen robe returned, saying, “I have done what you commanded me to do.”</span>
