<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:12; LEVITICUS 26:33; EZEKIEL 4:1–8"> When the time during whichyou will symbolize that the city will be surroundedby enemy troops ends, put a third of the hair inside thedrawing of the city and burn it. That will symbolize that I will strike the people of Jerusalem withtheir enemies’ swords. Then allow the wind to scatter the other third of the hair. That will symbolize thateven if they flee from the city, I will stillcause their enemies to pursue them and attack them with swords.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:10; EZEKIEL 16:47–48; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> But the wicked people of Jerusalem rebelled against obeying my commands, and they show that they are more wicked than the people of the surrounding countries. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:10–11; 2 CHRONICLES 33:9; 2 KINGS 21:9–11"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: ‘You people of Jerusalem have been more rebellious than the people of the nations around you; you have not obeyed any of my laws &#91;DOU&#93;. You have not even obeyed the laws of the nations around you!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 15:7; JEREMIAH 24:9; EZEKIEL 21:3"> Therefore, this is whatI, Yahweh the Lord, say: ‘I am opposed to you people of Jerusalem. I will punish you, and the people of other nations will see it happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:21; DANIEL 9:12; AMOS 3:2"> Because of all your detestable idols, I will punish youpeople of Jerusalem like I have never done before and will never do again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:64; EZEKIEL 12:14"> As a result, parents among you will eat their children, and children will eat their parentsbecause there will be nothing else to eat. I will punish you severely, and I will cause those who will still be alive to be scattered in all directions &#91;IDM&#93;.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 7:20; EZEKIEL 7:9; EZEKIEL 11:18"> Therefore, I, Yahweh the Lord, declare that as surely as I am alive, because you have polluted my temple with all your disgusting statues of idols and by the other detestable things that you do, I will no longer bless you. I will not pity you or act mercifully toward you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:2; JEREMIAH 15:2; JEREMIAH 43:10–11"> One-third of your people will die inside the city because of the plagues they will experience or by ◄famine/lack of food►. One-third of your people will be killed byyour enemies’ swords outside the city. And one-third I will cause to be scattered in all directions; butyour enemies will still pursue you and kill you with their swords.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:24; EZEKIEL 36:5–6; EZEKIEL 21:17"> Then I will no longer be angry with you; I will stop punishing &#91;MTY&#93; you after I have gotten revenge on you. And when I stop punishing you, you will know that I, Yahweh, have spoken to you because I have been very strongly concerned aboutwhat you have done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:4; Nehemiah 2:17; PSALMS 79:1–4"> I will cause your city to be a ruins, with the result that people of other nations that are around you will pass by and see it and sneer at you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:17; JEREMIAH 22:8–9; 1 KINGS 9:7"> They will make fun of you and taunt you. When I severely punish you because of my being very angry &#91;DOU&#93; with you, they will be horrified and they will be warned. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:16; DEUTERONOMY 32:23–24; EZEKIEL 14:13"> When I cut off your food supply and cause there to be more famine, it will be as though &#91;MET&#93; I am shooting at you with my arrows that will destroy you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:22; EZEKIEL 14:21; LEVITICUS 26:22"> I will cause you to experience a famine, and I will send wild beasts to attack you and your children, and all your children will be killed.
