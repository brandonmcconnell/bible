## The vision about the valley of dry bones

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:22; EZEKIEL 1:3; ACTS 8:39"> One day Yahweh gave me another vision. In the vision I felt the power &#91;MTY&#93; of God on me, and by his Spirit he took me to the middle of a valley. It was full of bones of people who had been killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 141:7; EZEKIEL 37:11; DEUTERONOMY 11:30"> He led me to walk back and forth among those bones. I saw that there were very many bones there, bones that were very dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 11:25–26; JOHN 5:21; DEUTERONOMY 32:39"> He asked me, “You human, do you think that these bones can become living people again?”<br />I replied, “Yahweh my Lord, only you know if that can happen.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 5:25; JOHN 5:28–29; MATTHEW 21:21"> Then he said to me, “Prophesy to these bones. Say to them, ‘You dry bones, listen to what Yahweh says.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:14; PSALMS 104:29–30; GENESIS 2:7"> This is what Yahweh the Lord says to you bones: I will put my breath into each of you, and you will become alive again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOEL 2:27; Isaiah 49:23; JOEL 3:17"> I will fasten tendons to your bones, and cause your bones to be covered with flesh. I will cover the flesh with skin. Then I will breathe into you, and you will become alive. When that happens, you will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ACTS 2:2; 1 KINGS 19:11–13; JEREMIAH 13:5–7"> So I spoke to the bones what Yahweh commanded me to speak. As I was speaking, there was a noise, a rattling sound, and the bones came together, bones joining to each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> While I was looking, I saw tendons fastening to them and flesh covering them, and then skin covered the flesh, but they did not breathe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 3:8; SONG OF SONGS 4:16; PSALMS 104:30"> Then he said to me, “You human, prophesy to the wind. Say to the wind, ‘Wind &#91;APO&#93;, Yahweh says to you, blow from all four directions. Breathe into these people who have been killed, in order that they can become alive again!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="REVELATION 11:11; PSALMS 104:30; REVELATION 20:4–5"> So I said what he commanded me to say, and then breath entered them. They became alive and stood up, like a huge army.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:1; PSALMS 77:7–9; EZEKIEL 39:25"> Then he said to me, “You human, these bones represent all the Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93;. The people say, ‘It is as though our bones are dried up; there are no more good things that we can ◄hope for/confidently expect to happen to us►; our nation is destroyed/finished.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 26:19; HOSEA 13:14; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:16"> So prophesy and say to them, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: My people, it will be as though I will open your graves and cause your corpses to become alive again. I will bring you back to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:6; EZEKIEL 16:62; PSALMS 126:2–3"> Then, what that happens, you my people will know that I, Yahweh the Lord, have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:27; JOEL 2:28–29; EZEKIEL 36:36"> I will put my Spirit in you, and it will be as though you will become alive again, and I will enable you to live in your own land again. Then you will know that it is I, Yahweh, who said that it would happen and who has caused it to happen. That is what I, Yahweh, declare.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A prophecy about being one nation with one king

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 15:9; 2 CHRONICLES 10:19; 2 CHRONICLES 10:17"> “You human, take a piece of wood and write on it, ‘This represents Judah and all the tribes of Judah.’ Then take another piece of wood and write on it, ‘This represents Israel and all the tribes of Israel.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HOSEA 1:11; JEREMIAH 50:4; Isaiah 11:13"> Then join them together to become as though they were one piece of wood in your hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:19; EZEKIEL 12:9; EZEKIEL 17:12"> When your fellow Israelis ask you, ‘What does ◄this action/what you just did► mean?’,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:6; EZEKIEL 37:16–17; EPHESIANS 2:13–14"> tell them, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: One of the pieces of wood in my hand represents Israel and all the tribes of Israel. I am joining it to the piece of wood that represents Judah, to form one piece of wood in his hand.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:3; NUMBERS 17:6–9; HOSEA 12:10"> Then, you human, hold up the pieces of wood that you have written on, in order that the people can see them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:24; JEREMIAH 29:14; JEREMIAH 23:8"> Say to the people, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: I will take you Israeli people out of the countries to which you have been forced to go. I will gather you from all those nations and bring you back to your own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:24; EZEKIEL 34:23–24; JEREMIAH 3:18"> And I will cause you to again be one nation in that land, on the mountains in Israel. And there will be one king to rule over all of you. Never again will you be two nations or divided into two kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="REVELATION 21:3–4; EZEKIEL 36:28–29; 1 JOHN 1:9"> No longer will you ◄defile yourselves/make yourselves unacceptable to me► by worshiping idols and disgusting statues of your gods, because I will enable you to quit your sinful behavior and to stop rejecting me. You will be my people, and I will be your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:23–24; HOSEA 3:5; EZEKIEL 37:25"> Someone who will be like &#91;MET&#93; King David who served me well will be the king of your descendants, and it will be as though &#91;MET&#93; he will be their shepherd. They will carefully obey all my laws &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="AMOS 9:15; EZEKIEL 37:24; EZEKIEL 28:25"> They will live in the land that I gave to Jacob, who also served me well; they will live in the land where your ancestors lived. They and their children and their grandchildren will live there forever, and the one who will be like &#91;MET&#93; King David will be their king forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; JEREMIAH 30:19; EZEKIEL 34:25"> I will make a peace agreement with them; it will be an agreement that will endure forever. I will again give them that land and cause their population to increase. And I will put my temple among them forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="REVELATION 21:3; JOHN 1:14; EZEKIEL 37:23"> The place where I will live will be among them; I will be their God and they will be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 17:17–19; EZEKIEL 20:12; EXODUS 31:13"> Then, when my temple is forever there among them, the people of other nations will know that I, Yahweh, have caused the Israeli people to be set apart to belong to me.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
