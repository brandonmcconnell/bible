## It is those who sin who will be punished

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:29–30; LAMENTATIONS 5:7; EZEKIEL 37:11"> “You people quote &#91;RHQ&#93; this proverb and say that it is about Israel:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Parents/People eat sour grapes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but it is their children who have a very sour taste in their mouths',</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which means that you think that it is not fair for you to be punished for your ancestors’ sins.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:19–20; EZEKIEL 33:11–20; EZEKIEL 18:30"> But I, Yahweh the Lord, declare that as surely as I am alive, you Israeli people will no longer quote that proverb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:20; ROMANS 6:23; NUMBERS 16:22"> Everyone who is alive belongs to me. That includes children and their parents; they all belong to me. And it is those who sin who will diebecause of their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:14; 1 JOHN 3:7; PROVERBS 21:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, suppose there is a righteous person</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whoalways does what is fair and right &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:15; DEUTERONOMY 4:19; EZEKIEL 6:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He does not eatmeat sacrificed to idols on the hilltops,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he does not request help from idols.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He does not have sex with someone else’s wife</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or have sex with a woman during her monthly menstrual period.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:16; EXODUS 22:26; EZEKIEL 18:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">He does not mistreat/oppress people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if someoneborrows money from him and gives him his cloak to guarantee that he will pay the money back, he always gives the cloak back to that personbefore the sun goes down.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He does not rob people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He gives food to hungry people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He gives clothing to people who need clothes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 22:25; ZECHARIAH 8:16; DEUTERONOMY 23:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">When he lends money to people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he does not do it just to charge big interest.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He does not do &#91;SYN&#93; things that are evil.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He always decides things fairly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:11; HABAKKUK 2:4; AMOS 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He faithfully obeys all &#91;DOU&#93; my laws.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">That man is truly righteous;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he will remain alive.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, promise.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 21:12; 1 JOHN 3:12; JOHN 18:40"> But suppose that man has a son who acts violently, who murders people and does any of these other things, even though his father has not done any of them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="REVELATION 22:14; MATTHEW 7:21–27; 1 KINGS 13:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He eatsmeat sacrificed to idols on the hilltops.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has sex with other people’s wives.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:17; EZEKIEL 8:6; 2 KINGS 21:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">He mistreats/oppresses poor and needy people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He robs people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If someone gives him his cloak to guaranteethat he will pay the money back,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he never gives the cloak back to himbefore the sun goes down.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He seeks help from idols.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He does other detestable things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 22:25; LEVITICUS 20:9; EZEKIEL 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">When he lends money, he chargesvery big interest.</span><br />If you think that such a person willbe allowed to remain alive &#91;RHQ&#93;, you are certainly wrong. Because he has done those detestable things, he will surely be executed, and it will be his own fault &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 34:21; PROVERBS 23:24; JEREMIAH 8:6"> But suppose that man has a son who sees all the sins that his father commits, but he does not do those things:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:6–7; EZEKIEL 18:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He does not eatmeat sacrificed to idols on the hilltops.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He does not request help from idols.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He does not have sex with other people’s wives.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:7; Isaiah 58:7–10; JOB 22:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He does not mistreat/oppress people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If he lends money to someone, he does not require that person to give him his cloak to guarantee that he will pay the money back.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He does not rob anyone.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He gives food to those who need it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He gives clothes to those who need clothes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:8–9; MATTHEW 18:27–35; EZEKIEL 18:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He does not commit sins, like his father did,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and does not charge big interest &#91;DOU&#93; when he lends money.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He faithfully obeys all &#91;DOU&#93; my laws.</span><br />That person will not be executed for his father’s sins; he will surely remain alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:18; EZEKIEL 18:26; EZEKIEL 18:24"> But his father will be executed for the sins that he committed, because he cheated people and robbed people and did other things that are evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 20:5; ZECHARIAH 1:3–6; DEUTERONOMY 5:9"> If you ask, ‘Why should the man’s son not ◄suffer/be punished► for the evil things that his father did?’, I will answer that the son has done what is fair and right &#91;DOU&#93; and has obeyed all my laws, so he will surely remain alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:16; 2 KINGS 14:6; EZEKIEL 18:4"> It is those who sin who will diebecause of their sins. People will not be punished for the sins of their parents, or for the sins of their children. Good/Righteous people will be rewarded for their being good/righteous, and evil people will be punished for theirdoing evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:27–28; TITUS 2:11–14; EZEKIEL 33:19"> But if a wicked person ◄turns away from/quits► doing all the evil things that he did previously, and if he starts to obey faithfully all my laws, and does what is fair and right &#91;DOU&#93;, he will surely remain alive; he will not be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MICAH 7:19; EZEKIEL 33:16; Isaiah 43:25"> He will not be punished for the sins he committed previously. Because of the good/righteous things that he has done since that time, he willbe allowed to remain alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:11; 1 TIMOTHY 2:4; 2 PETER 3:9"> I, Yahweh the Lord, declare that I certainly am not &#91;RHQ&#93; happy about wicked people dying. Instead, I am happy when they turn away from their wicked behavior, andbecause of that, they remain alive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:18; PROVERBS 21:16; 2 JOHN 1:8"> But if a righteous person stops doing righteous things and starts to commit sins and does the same detestable things that wicked people do, he will certainly not &#91;RHQ&#93; be allowed to remain alive. I will not think about the righteous things that he did previously. Because he did not faithfullydo what pleases me, and because of all the sins that he has committed, he will be executed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:17; MALACHI 3:13–15; MALACHI 2:17"> But you say, ‘Yahweh does not act fairly!’ You Israeli people, listen to what I say: It is certainly not &#91;RHQ&#93; what I do that is unfair, it is what you continually do that is not fair/right!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> If a righteous person ◄turns away from/quits► doing what is righteous and commits sins, he will diefor committing those sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:21; Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 55:7"> But if a wicked person turns away from doing wicked things and does what is just and right &#91;DOU&#93;, he will save himselffrom being executed/punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:29; LUKE 15:17–18; JAMES 2:10–12"> Because he has thought about all the evil things that he did and has turned away from doing them, he will surelybe allowed to live. He will not be executed for having done evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:25; PROVERBS 19:3; EZEKIEL 18:2"> But you Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93; say, ‘Yahweh does not act fairly.’ You people of Israel, my behavior is certainly not &#91;RHQ&#93; unfair! It is your behavior that is not right.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:21; MATTHEW 16:27; MATTHEW 3:2"> Therefore, you Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93;, I, Yahweh the Lord, will judge each of you according to what you have done. Repent! Turn away from all your wicked behavior! Then you will not be destroyed &#91;PRS&#93; because of your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 51:10; EZEKIEL 36:26; EZEKIEL 11:19"> Get rid of all your wicked behavior, and get a new way of thinking &#91;IDM, DOU&#93;. You Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93;, ◄do you really want to be executed for your sins?/you act as though you want to be executed for your sins► &#91;RHQ&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 PETER 3:9; EZEKIEL 18:23; EZEKIEL 33:11"> I, Yahweh the Lord, declare that I am not pleased about your dying. So repent, and remain alive!”</span>
</div>
