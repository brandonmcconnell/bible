## Egypt is compared to defeated Assyria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:20; JEREMIAH 52:5–6; EZEKIEL 1:2"> Almost eleven years after we had been taken to Babylonia, on the first day of the third month of that year, Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:19; EZEKIEL 30:10; EZEKIEL 31:18"> “You human, say to the King of Egypt and all of his people,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You think that there is &#91;RHQ, IRO&#93; no country whose power is as great as the power of your country.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:33–34; DANIEL 4:10; DANIEL 4:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">You think that your country is as great as Assyria was:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Assyria was previously like &#91;MET&#93; a tall cedar tree in Lebanon;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it was as though it had big beautiful branches</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that provided shade for other trees in the forest.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It was very tall;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">its top was above the leaves of the other trees.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="REVELATION 17:15; PROVERBS 14:28; EZEKIEL 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Water came from deep springs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and as a result that cedar tree grew tall and very green.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then water flowed around the base of the tree</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">into channels that took water to other nearby trees.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 4:11; EZEKIEL 17:5; PSALMS 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">That huge tree grew very tall,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">higher than all the other trees around it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Its branches grew very thick and long &#91;DOU&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the abundant water at the base of the tree.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:23; MATTHEW 13:32; DANIEL 4:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Birds built their nests in the branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and wild animals gave birth to their babies under those branches.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And it was as though people of all the great nations lived in the shade of that tree.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">It was majestic and beautiful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">its branches spread out widely</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because the roots of the tree grew down into the ground where there was a plentiful supply of water.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:13; GENESIS 13:10; Isaiah 51:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The cedar trees in my garden in Eden were not as great as that tree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the branches of the pine/cypress trees were not as long and thick as the branches of that cedar tree.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the branches of the plane trees were not as long and thick, either.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">No tree in my garden was as beautiful as that cedar tree.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:13; DANIEL 2:37–38; GENESIS 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because I caused that tree to become very beautiful</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with its magnificent green branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all the leaders of other countries represented by &#91;MET&#93; those other trees in Eden envied the country represented by that tree.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:17; DANIEL 5:20; 2 CHRONICLES 32:25"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: ‘That tree, which represents Assyria, grew very tall, and its top was higher than the other trees, and it became very proud because of being very tall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:11–12; NAHUM 3:18; DANIEL 5:18–19"> Therefore, I enabled another mighty nation to conquer it and to destroy it as it deserved to be destroyed. I have already discarded it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:7; EZEKIEL 35:8; EZEKIEL 30:11"> A foreign army, one that has caused people of other nations to be terrified, cut it down and left it. Its branches fell on the mountains and in the valleys. Some of its branches lay broken in all the ravines in the land. All the people of other nations came out from being under its shade and left it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:4; EZEKIEL 29:5; Isaiah 18:6"> Birds ◄settled/went to live► on the fallen tree, and wild animals lived among its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 63:9–10; Nehemiah 13:18; 2 PETER 2:6"> The result of what I have done is that no other trees, even if they have plenty of water, will ever grow very high and become proud, and lift their tops above the branches of other trees. I do not want any other tree to grow high like that cedar tree did; trees will all certainly die and decay in the ground, like people die and go down to their graves.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MALACHI 3:4; NAHUM 2:8–10; REVELATION 18:9–11"> This is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: ‘When that great tree was cut down, it was as though the springs that watered it mourned for it, because I caused the plentiful water from the springs to dry up. I caused the mountains in Lebanon to become black, and all the trees there to wither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 14:15; Isaiah 14:8; EZEKIEL 26:15"> I caused the people of other nations to tremble when they heard that tree fall to the ground. They realized that it would decay, like all people who die and are buried decay. And all the leaders of other countries represented by other beautiful trees in my garden in Eden and in Lebanon, were like beautiful trees that were very proud. They were ones which had roots that grew down deep into the ground where there was plenty of water. They were comforted when the king represented by &#91;MET&#93; that cedar tree was there with them in the place where the dead people are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 9:17; EZEKIEL 31:6; EZEKIEL 31:3"> The leaders of other countries represented by &#91;MET&#93; trees that grew in the shade of the huge tree, the allies of the great nation that the cedar tree represents, had also joined those who had been killed by the sword and gone down to where the dead people are.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:19; EZEKIEL 32:21; EZEKIEL 28:10"> This parable is about you people of Egypt. You think that &#91;RHQ, IRO&#93; there is no other nation that is as great and glorious as yours is. But your nation will also be destroyed, as Assyria was, along with those other nations. Your people will be there among the other people who are not fit to worship me, people who have been killed by their enemies’ swords. That is what will happen to the king of Egypt and all his people. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have predicted it.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
