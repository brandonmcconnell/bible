## The trade with Tyre

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh said this to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 19:1; JEREMIAH 9:10; EZEKIEL 28:12"> “You human, sing a funeral song about Tyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:4; Isaiah 23:11; Isaiah 23:2–3"> The city of Tyre is located on an island at the edge of the sea, and their merchants ◄traded/bought and sold goods► with people-groups who live along many seacoasts. This is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say that you should tell them: ‘You people of Tyre said that your city was very beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:5"> You controlled what was bought and sold by people who lived along the sea.<br /><span className="pl pl-2">Those who built your city caused it to become very beautiful.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:9; Isaiah 14:8; SONG OF SONGS 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">You were like &#91;SIM&#93; a huge ship</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that was built from the from pine/cypress trees on Hermon Mountain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then they took cedar wood from Lebanon to make a mast for the ship.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 10:4; Isaiah 2:13; ZECHARIAH 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They carved your oars from oak trees from the Bashan region.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They made the deck from cypress/pine wood from Cyprus island,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they covered the decks with ivory.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:9; EXODUS 25:4; GENESIS 10:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sails for the ship were made from fine embroidered linen from Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those sails were like flags that people could see far away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For shade they hung pieces of blue and purple cloth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">brought from people living on Cyprus island.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 10:18; 1 KINGS 9:27; EZEKIEL 27:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Men from Sidon and Arvad cities pulled the oars;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the men who steered the boat were skilled men from Tyre.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 KINGS 5:18; JOSHUA 13:5; PSALMS 83:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Experienced craftsmen from Byblos city on the coast ◄did the caulking/filled the spaces between the planks in the sides► of the ship.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Sailors from many countries came in their ships to buy and sell goods there.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:5; EZEKIEL 30:5; Isaiah 66:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Men from as far away as Persia, Lydia, and Libya were soldiers in your army.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They hung their shields and helmets on the walls of your city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that caused people to admire your city.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Men from Arvad and Helech cities were watchmen on the walls of your city;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">men from Gammad town were in your towers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They also hung their shields on your walls;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they also caused your city to be very beautiful.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 10:4; Isaiah 23:10; EZEKIEL 38:13"> Because of the many things that you had to trade, men from Tarshish in Spain sent merchants who brought silver, iron, tin, and lead to trade for things that you had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 10:2; Isaiah 66:19; EZEKIEL 39:1"> Merchants from Greece, Tubal, and Meshech brought slaves and things made from bronze to trade for things that you had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 10:3; EZEKIEL 38:6; 1 CHRONICLES 1:6"> Men from Beth-Togarmah in Armenia brought work horses, war horses, and mules to trade for things that you had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 10:7; REVELATION 18:12; 1 KINGS 10:22"> Merchants came to you from Rhodes island. People from nations along the coast traded with you; they brought ◄ivory/elephant tusks► and valuable black ebony wood to trade for things that you had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 10:6; EZEKIEL 28:13; 2 SAMUEL 10:6"> Because you had very many things to trade, people from Syria brought to you valuable turquoise stones, purple cloth, embroidered cloth, fine linen cloth, and jewelry made from coral and rubies to trade for your things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JUDGES 11:33; 1 KINGS 5:9; JEREMIAH 8:22"> Men from Judah and Israel brought wheat from Minnith city in Ammon, and figs, honey, olive oil, and ointment to trade for your things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:8; GENESIS 15:2; EZEKIEL 47:16–18"> Because you had very many things to trade, men from Damascus brought wine from Helbon town and white wool from the Zahar area to trade for many things that you had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 30:23–24; PSALMS 45:8; SONG OF SONGS 4:13–14"> Greek men from the Uzal area brought things made of iron, and cassia spice, and fragrant calamus seeds to trade for things that you had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 25:3; EZEKIEL 27:15"> Merchants came from Dedan in southern Edom bringing saddle blankets to trade for things that you had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 60:7; GENESIS 25:13; 1 KINGS 10:15"> Men from Arabia and all the rulers of the Kedar region sent merchants to trade lambs and rams and male goats for things that you had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 10:7; Isaiah 60:6; PSALMS 72:10"> Merchants from Sheba and Raamah in Arabia brought many kinds of very good spices and jewels and gold to exchange for things that you had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 KINGS 19:12; Isaiah 37:12; AMOS 1:5"> Men came from Haran, Canneh, Eden, Sheba, Asshur and Kilmad in Mesopotamia with their goods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 KINGS 2:8"> They brought pretty things to trade: Blue cloth, embroidered cloth, and rugs of many colors that were rolled up and tied with ropes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 2:16; PSALMS 48:7; Isaiah 23:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cargo ships from Tarshish carried all those things that you traded;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the warehouses on your island were full of all those things.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The destruction of Tyre

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 48:7; EZEKIEL 26:19; ACTS 27:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“ ‘The men who row your boats took the ships full of cargo out on the stormy seas.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But the strong east wind wrecked those cargo boats.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:4; EZEKIEL 27:24; EZEKIEL 27:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everything in the boats ◄was lost/sank to the bottom of the sea►—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all the valuable cargo &#91;DOU&#93; and many of &#91;HYP&#93; the sailors and ship pilots,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the ship workers and merchants and soldiers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">On the day that the ships were wrecked,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">many of those on the ships sank to the bottom of the sea.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:10; EXODUS 15:14; EZEKIEL 31:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people in cities along the coast trembled</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when they heard your ship pilots cry out.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:16; EZEKIEL 32:10; REVELATION 18:17–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the men who remained who pulled the oars left the ships;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the sailors and pilots go to the shore and stand on the beach.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:26; 2 SAMUEL 1:2; LAMENTATIONS 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They cry aloud because of what has happened to your wealth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they weep bitterly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They throw dirt on their heads</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and roll around in ashes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EZEKIEL 7:18; Isaiah 22:12; Isaiah 15:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shave their heads to show that they are very sad because of what has happened to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they put on rough sackcloth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They weep for you very bitterly</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and mourn for you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:17; REVELATION 18:18; EZEKIEL 27:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">While they wail and mourn because of what has happened to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they sing this sad funeral song:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“There was certainly never &#91;RHQ&#93; a city like Tyre</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that now is silent,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">covered by the waves of the sea.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="REVELATION 18:19; EZEKIEL 27:3; EZEKIEL 28:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The goods that your merchants traded</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">were things that pleased the people of many countries.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Kings in very distant places became rich</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the wealth that they obtained from trading with you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:26–27; ZECHARIAH 9:3–4; EZEKIEL 26:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now your city is like a ship &#91;MET&#93; that is wrecked in the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and everything that was in it is broken, at the bottom of the sea.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All of your cargo and your sailors have sunk to the bottom of the sea along with the ships.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:10; Isaiah 23:6; EZEKIEL 26:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the people who live along the seacoast are appalled/horrified</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of what has happened to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their kings are very horrified,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">causing their faces to be twisted as they watch.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:16; PSALMS 37:10; ZEPHANIAH 2:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The merchants of the other nations shake their heads</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because it is difficult for them to believe what has happened;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your city has disappeared,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it will not exist any more.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
