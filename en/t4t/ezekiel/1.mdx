## Ezekiel saw Yahweh’s glory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:16; EZEKIEL 11:24; ACTS 7:56"> I am Ezekiel, a priest, the son of Buzi. When I was 30 years old, I wasliving among theIsraeli people who had been ◄exiled from/forced to leave► Judahand had come to Babylon. I was living along the Kebar River/Canalsouth of Babylon. Almost five years after King Jehoiachin had been ◄exiled/forced to leave Judah►, on the fifth day of the fourth monthof that year, it was as though the sky was opened and I saw visions from God.<br />On that day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:14; EZEKIEL 3:22; 2 KINGS 3:15"> God gave me messagesin visions here in Babylonia, and I felt the power &#91;MTY&#93; of Yahweh on me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:27; EZEKIEL 8:2; JEREMIAH 23:19"> In one of the visions, I saw a windstorm coming from the north. There was a huge cloud, and lightning was flashing continually, and a brilliant light surrounded the cloud. In the center of where the lightning was flashing there was something that resembled glowing bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="REVELATION 4:6–8; EZEKIEL 1:26; REVELATION 6:6"> In the center of the storm I saw what resembled four living creatures. They resembled humans,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:14; REVELATION 4:7–8; EZEKIEL 1:15"> but each of them had four faces and four wings &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="REVELATION 1:15; DANIEL 10:6; PSALMS 104:4"> Their legs were straight. Their feet resembled the hooves of calves, and the creatures shone like polished bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:21; EZEKIEL 10:11; Isaiah 6:6"> On their four sides under their wings there were hands like humans have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:22; EZEKIEL 10:11; EZEKIEL 1:11–12"> As the four creaturesstood there, they formed a circle/square, with their wings touching each other. They did not turn when they were moving; they went straight ahead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:14; REVELATION 4:7; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:9–10"> Eachof the creatures had four faces. The face that was in front of each one was a face that resembled a human face. The face on the right side resembled a lion’s face. The face on the left side resembled an ox’s face. The face in back resembled an eagle’s face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 6:2; EZEKIEL 1:23; EZEKIEL 10:19"> Two of each creature’s wings were lifted up and touched the wings of the creatures that were on each side. The other two wings were folded against the creature’s body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:9; EZEKIEL 10:22; HEBREWS 1:14"> The creatures went straight ahead in whatever direction the spiritthat controlled/guided them wanted them to go, without changing directions while they were moving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="REVELATION 4:5; PSALMS 104:4; MATTHEW 28:3"> Thefour creatures resembled burning coals or torches. A blazing fire moved back and forth among the creatures, and lighting flashed from among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:27; ZECHARIAH 4:10; MARK 13:27"> The creatures moved back and forthextremely rapidly, like &#91;SIM&#93; flashes of lightning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 7:9; EZEKIEL 10:9; EZEKIEL 1:19–21"> While I looked at the four living creatures, I saw a wheel on the ground beside each of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 10:6; EZEKIEL 10:9–11; EXODUS 39:13"> Each of the wheels was the same, and they all shone like &#91;SIM&#93; ◄chrysolite/a valuable green stone►. Each seemed to have one wheel inside another wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:9; EZEKIEL 1:12; Isaiah 55:11"> Whenever they moved, they would go straight in one of the four directions that they faced; they did not turnin another direction while they moved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:12; REVELATION 4:6; REVELATION 4:8"> The rims of the wheels were covered with eyes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:16; EZEKIEL 10:19; PSALMS 103:20"> Whenever the living creatures moved, the wheels moved with them. So whenever the creatures rose from the ground, the wheelsalso rose up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:12; EZEKIEL 10:17; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:32"> Wherever the spiritthat controlled the creatures wanted the creatures to go, they went, and the wheels went with them, because the spiritthat controlled/guided them was in the wheels &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:17; EZEKIEL 1:19–20; ROMANS 8:2"> Whenever the creatures moved, the wheels moved. Whenever the creatures stoodstill, the wheels stopped. Whenever the creatures rose up from the ground, the wheels rose up with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:1; EZEKIEL 1:26; REVELATION 4:6"> Above the heads of the creatures there was something that resembled a dome. It shone like ice (OR, crystal) shines, and it was awesome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 4:18; EZEKIEL 1:11–12; PSALMS 89:7"> Under the dome, the creatures stretched out their wings. Each one had two wings; each wing stretched towards the creature thatwas next to it, and two wings that were against the creature’s body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:2; DANIEL 10:6; REVELATION 1:15"> Whenever the creatures moved, their wings made a sound that resembled the roar made by a rushing stream. It also sounded like the voice of Almighty God, and like &#91;SIM&#93; the noise of a huge army marching. Whenever the creatures stood stillon the ground, they lowered their wings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:22"> While they stoodon the ground with their wings lowered, there was a voice from the dome that was over their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:1; EXODUS 24:10; REVELATION 1:13"> Above the dome was something that resembled ahuge thronethat was made of a huge ◄sapphire/valuable blue stone►. Sitting on the throne was someone that resembled a human.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:4; EZEKIEL 8:2; HEBREWS 12:29"> I saw that above his waist his body resembled metal that was glowing as though it hada very hot fire inside it. And I saw that below his waist there was a very brilliant light that surrounded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="REVELATION 10:1; REVELATION 4:3; GENESIS 17:3"> Itshone like &#91;SIM&#93; a rainbow shines in the clouds on a rainy day.<br />That was the brilliant light that represented the presence of Yahweh. When I saw it, I prostrated myself on the ground, and I heard him speak.</span>
</div>
