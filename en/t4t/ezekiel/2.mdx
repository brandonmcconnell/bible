## Ezekiel was called to be a prophet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 10:11; EZEKIEL 3:17; EZEKIEL 4:1"> He said to me, “You human, stand up while I speak to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:24; NUMBERS 11:25–26; EZEKIEL 3:12"> While he spoke to me, God’s Spirit entered me and enabled me to stand up. Then I heard him speak to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:25; 1 SAMUEL 8:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 9:24"> He said, “You human, I will send you to the Israeli people. They are people who have turned away from me and rebelled against me. Their ancestors rebelled against me, and they themselves are still rebelling against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:7; Isaiah 48:4; JEREMIAH 5:3"> The people to whom I will send you are very stubborn &#91;DOU&#93;. But say to them, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says to you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:33; EZEKIEL 3:27; MATTHEW 10:12–15"> Andwhen you tell them my messages, perhaps those rebellious people will heed those messages and perhaps they will not heed them, but they will know thatyou are a prophet who has been among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:8; Isaiah 51:12; 1 PETER 3:14"> And you human, you must not be afraid of them or afraid of what they say. Living among them will be like living in the midst of briers or scorpions, but do not be afraid of them. They are rebellious people &#91;MTY&#93;, but do not allow them to cause you to become afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:28; EZEKIEL 3:17; JEREMIAH 1:7"> Tell them my message, but do not expect them to pay attention to it, because they arevery rebellious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="REVELATION 10:9; JEREMIAH 15:16; Isaiah 50:5"> But human, you must pay attention to what I say. Do not be rebellious like they are. Now open your mouth and eat what I give to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:3; REVELATION 5:1–5; DANIEL 10:10"> Then, as I watched, I saw his hand that was stretched toward me. In his hand was a scroll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="REVELATION 8:13; Isaiah 3:11; REVELATION 9:12"> He unrolled the scroll. On both sides of it were written words that expressed sorrow and mourning and words about trouble/disaster.</span>
</div>
