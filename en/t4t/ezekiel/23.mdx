## A parable about Jerusalem and Samaria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh said to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:44–46; JEREMIAH 3:7–10"> “You human, listen to this parable about Jerusalem and Samaria. Once there were two women, daughters of the same mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 17:7; EZEKIEL 16:22; JOSHUA 24:14"> They lived in Egypt. And from the time that they were young women, they became prostitutes. In that land, men fondled their breasts and caressed their bosoms &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:8; PSALMS 76:2; JEREMIAH 2:2–3"> The older sister was Oholah, and her younger sister was Oholibah. It was as though they became my wives. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HOSEA 5:13; 2 KINGS 17:3; 2 KINGS 16:7"> Oholah acted like a prostitute while she was still my wife. She desired to have sex with those she loved—soldiers from Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:23"> Some of them were army officers and commanders. They wore beautiful blue uniforms. They were all handsome young men. Some of them rode horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HOSEA 5:3; HOSEA 6:10; EZEKIEL 22:3–4"> She acted like a prostitute toward all the important Assyrian officials, and became unacceptable to me by worshiping all the idols of the men she wanted to have sex with.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:19; EZEKIEL 23:3; EXODUS 32:4"> When she was a young woman in Egypt, she started being a prostitute, and young men caressed her breasts and had sex with her; and when she became older, she did not quit acting like a prostitute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HOSEA 11:5; 2 KINGS 15:29; 2 KINGS 17:23"> So I allowed the Assyrian soldiers, whom she wanted to have sex with, to capture her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:29; HOSEA 2:10; JEREMIAH 22:8–9"> They stripped all her clothes off her. They took away her sons and daughters. <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:8–11; EZEKIEL 16:47–51; EZEKIEL 23:4"> Heryounger sister Oholibah saw those thingsthat happened to Oholah, but she was a prostitute, and she desired to have sex with men more than herolder sister had desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 KINGS 16:7–15; EZEKIEL 23:23; 2 CHRONICLES 28:16–23"> Oholibah also wanted to have sex with the Assyrian soldiers. Some of them were army officers and commanders. They all wore beautiful blue uniforms. They were all handsome young men. Some of them rode horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HOSEA 12:1–2; 2 KINGS 17:18–19; EZEKIEL 23:31"> I saw what she did that caused her to became unacceptable to me, just like herolder sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:10; JEREMIAH 22:14; JEREMIAH 50:2"> But she did even worse things. She saw drawings of men from Babylonia on the walls, painted in red.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 8:18; 1 SAMUEL 18:4; Isaiah 22:21"> The menin the drawings had belts around their waists and long turbans on their heads. They all resembled officers from Babylonia &#91;DOU&#93; who rode in chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:29; MATTHEW 5:28; JOB 31:1"> As soon as she saw those drawings, she wanted to have sex with those men, and she sent messages to them in Babylonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:28; EZEKIEL 23:22; GENESIS 10:10"> Then the soldiers from Babylonia came to her, they lay in bed with her, and they had sex with her. Then she became disgusted with them and turned away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 78:59; PSALMS 106:40; JEREMIAH 12:8"> But when she continued to openly/boldly act like a prostitute &#91;DOU&#93;, I became disgusted with her, and I rejected her like I had rejected herolder sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:3; EZEKIEL 20:7; EZEKIEL 16:51"> But she became even more immoral, as she remembered when she was a young woman who was a prostitute in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:15; EZEKIEL 16:26; JEREMIAH 5:8"> There she wanted to have sex with those who loved her, whose genitals were very long, like &#91;SIM&#93; those of donkeys, and whose sexual emissions were huge, like those of horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So she desired to be immoral like she was when she was young, when men in Egypt caressed her bosom and fondled her breasts.”</span>

## Yahweh will punish Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:37; REVELATION 17:16; JEREMIAH 6:22–23"> “Oholibahrepresents you people of Jerusalem. Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord say: ‘I will cause those soldiers who loved you but whom you turned away from because you became disgusted with them, to be angry with you. I will cause them to come and attack you from every side—</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:21; 2 KINGS 24:2; 2 KINGS 20:14–17"> soldiers from Babylon and all the other places in Babylonia, andtheir allies from Pekod and Shoa and Koaregions, and all the army of Assyria. Yes, all of them are handsome young men, army officers and commanders, officers who ride in chariots, all riding on horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 24:14; EZEKIEL 26:10; EZEKIEL 23:45"> Their huge army will attack you with weapons, riding in chariots and pulling wagons that will carry their army supplies. They will surround you, carrying large and small shields, and wearing helmets. I will allow them to capture you and punish you in the way that they always punishtheir enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:47; EZEKIEL 8:1–18; EZEKIEL 15:6–7"> Because I am very angry with you, I will cause them to act very furiously toward you. They will cut off your noses and your ears. Then, those who are still alive, they will kill with their swords. They will take away your sons and daughters, and a fire will burn up those who remain alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:39; JEREMIAH 13:22; EZEKIEL 23:29"> They will strip off your clothes and your fine jewelry and take them away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:41; EZEKIEL 23:3; EZEKIEL 23:19"> In that way, I will stop all the immoral behavior that began when you became a prostitute in Egypt. You will no longer desire to do those things, or think aboutwhat you did in Egypt.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:22; EZEKIEL 23:17; JEREMIAH 34:20"> This is what I, Yahweh the Lord say: ‘I am about to allow those whom you hate, those you became disgusted with and turned away from, to capture you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:39; EZEKIEL 23:25–26; EZEKIEL 23:45–47"> They will be cruel and take away everything that you have worked hard to acquire. They will leave you completely naked &#91;DOU&#93;, and everyone will see that you truly are a prostitute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:9; PSALMS 106:35–38; JEREMIAH 2:18–20"> It is because of what you have done that you will be punished like that; you have been an immoral prostitute; you have had sex with men of other nations, and you have caused yourselves to become unacceptable to me byworshiping their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 KINGS 21:13; JEREMIAH 7:14–15; EZEKIEL 16:47–51"> You have behaved likethe people of Samaria, who are like your older sister. So I will cause you to be punished &#91;MET&#93; like they were punished.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; PSALMS 60:3; EZEKIEL 22:4–5"> This is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: ‘You will suffer like people suffer when they drink from a cup a lot of strong alcoholic drink:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will be as though you will drink from the cup that the people of Samaria drank from;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a big and tall cup.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because of your drinking what is in that cup,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">many people will scorn you and make fun of you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because there is a lot of liquid in that cup.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 51:17; JEREMIAH 25:27; JEREMIAH 25:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you become very drunk,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will become very sad,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">becausedrinking what is in that cup &#91;MTY&#93; will cause you to become ruined and deserted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like what happened &#91;MET&#93; to the people of Samaria, who are like &#91;MET&#93; your sister.</span></span>

## God will punish Samaria and Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:2; Isaiah 58:1; EZEKIEL 16:2"> Yahweh said to me, “You human, you must &#91;RHQ&#93; judgethe people of those two cities represented by Oholah and Oholibah. You must remind them of their detestable behavior:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:36; JEREMIAH 32:35; JEREMIAH 7:31"> It is as though the people of those cities have committed adultery and have murdered &#91;MTY&#93; people. They have been unfaithful to me by worshiping idols. They have even sacrificed their own children, who belonged to me, to be food for their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:13; 2 KINGS 21:7; EZEKIEL 7:20"> They have done otherdisgraceful things: They have caused my temple to be an unacceptable place for worship, and have not respected the Sabbath days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 KINGS 21:4; EZEKIEL 44:7; JEREMIAH 23:11"> On the same day that they sacrificed their children to their idols, they entered my temple, which caused it to be an unacceptable place for worshiping me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:30; JEREMIAH 4:30; EZEKIEL 16:13–16"> They sent messages to men in countries far away. And when those men arrived, the women of those two cities bathed themselves for them, painted their eyebrows, and put on jewelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Esther 1:6; JEREMIAH 44:17; Isaiah 65:11"> They sat on a beautiful couch, with a table in front of it on which they had put incense and olive oil that belonged to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:11–12; REVELATION 12:3; EXODUS 32:18–19"> Soon there was a noisy crowd around them. Among the crowd there were men from Sheba who had come from the desertof Arabia. They put bracelets on the arms of the two sisters and put beautiful crowns on their heads.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:3; JEREMIAH 13:23; Ezra 9:7"> Then I said about the woman who had become exhausted by her having sex with many men, ‘Now allow those men to act toward her as though she is a prostitute, because that is all that she is.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:9–13; EZEKIEL 23:3"> So they had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with those two women, Oholah and Oholibah, like men have sex with prostitutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="HOSEA 6:5; LEVITICUS 20:10; EZEKIEL 23:36–39"> But righteous men will condemn them to be punished, like women who commit adultery and who murder others are punished, because those women commit adultery and they murder &#91;MTY&#93; others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:40; JEREMIAH 15:4; JEREMIAH 24:9"> So this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: Bring a mob to attack Samaria and Jerusalem, and allow that mob to cause the people of those cities to be terrified, and to rob them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:40–41; JEREMIAH 39:8; EZEKIEL 24:21"> The mob will throw stones at themto kill them; they will cut them into pieces with their swords, they will kill their sons and daughters, and burn down their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:6; EZEKIEL 23:27; EZEKIEL 16:41"> In that way I will cause them to stop their immoral behavior. It will warn other women to not imitate/do what you people of Jerusalem are doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="EZEKIEL 9:10; EZEKIEL 7:4; EZEKIEL 6:7"> Youpeople of Jerusalem will be punished for your immoral behavior and for your worshiping idols. Then you will know that I, Yahweh the Lord, have the power to do what I say that I will do.”</span>
