## Ezekiel symbolizes what will happen when they are exiled

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MARK 4:12; JEREMIAH 5:21; PSALMS 78:40"> “You human, you are living among people who are very rebellious. They have eyes, but they do not seewhat I want them to see; they have ears, but they do not hearwhat I want them to hear, because they are rebellious/stubborn people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 36:3; 2 TIMOTHY 2:25; JEREMIAH 26:3"> Therefore, you human, pack your belongings as though you were ◄going into exile/being forced to go to another country►. Then, during the daytime, while people are watching, prepare to go to another place. Even though the people are rebellious, perhaps they will understandwhat you are symbolizing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:4; EZEKIEL 12:12; 2 KINGS 25:4"> During the daytime, while they are watching, bring out the things that you want to take and pack them. Then in the evening, while they are watching, do what those who are preparing to go into exile do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:4; JEREMIAH 39:2–4"> Dig through thecity wall and take your things through the hole.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:3; Isaiah 8:18; EZEKIEL 24:24"> Then put themin a sack on your shoulder while they are watching and leave at dusk. Cover your face in order that you cannot see the path. I want you to do this because I want you to warn the Israeli people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:18; EZEKIEL 37:10; EZEKIEL 37:7"> So I did what Yahweh told me to do. During the day I brought things out of my houseas though I was packing to go into exile. Then in the evening I dug through thecity wall. Then while people watched, I put my sack of belongings on my shoulder and left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The next morning, Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:19; EZEKIEL 17:12; EZEKIEL 20:49"> “You human, the rebellious people &#91;MTY&#93; of Israel asked you, ‘What are you doing?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:25; Isaiah 13:1; JEREMIAH 24:8"> So go back and say to them, ‘What I did is about the king in Jerusalem and all the other people of Israel who are there.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:15; JEREMIAH 15:2; JEREMIAH 52:28–30"> Tell them, ‘What I did is a warning to you.’ What has happened to you will happen to them. They will be captured and forced to go to another country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:4; JEREMIAH 39:4; EZEKIEL 12:6"> Their king will put his belongings on his shoulder at dusk and leave. His servants will dig a hole through the city wall, and he will take his belongings through it. He will cover his face in order thatothers will not recognize him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:7; HOSEA 7:12; EZEKIEL 17:20"> Butit will be as though I will spread a net for himto be captured; enemy soldiers will capture him and take him to Babyloncity. But he will notbe able to see it; and there he will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:2; 2 KINGS 25:4–5; EZEKIEL 17:21"> I will scatter in all directions &#91;IDM&#93; all those who have been around him—his advisors and his soldiers—and I will cause his enemies to pursue them, with their swords ready to strike them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:7; EZEKIEL 12:16; EZEKIEL 12:20"> Then, when I scatter them among many nations &#91;DOU&#93;, they will know that I, Yahweh,have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:8–9; EZEKIEL 14:22–23; EZEKIEL 6:8–10"> But I will spare/save a few of them from being killed by swords and from dying from famines or plagues, with the result that in the nations to which they are forced to go, they will realize/admit that they have been doing disgusting things, and they will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 5:9; PSALMS 80:5; JOB 3:24"> “You human, tremble while you eat your food, and shudder fearfully while you drink your water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 7:14; EZEKIEL 6:6–7; EZEKIEL 6:14"> Tell this to the Israeli people: 'This is what Yahweh the Lord says about those who are still living in Jerusalem and inother places in Israel: They also will be very anxious/worried &#91;DOU&#93; while they eat their food and drink their water, because their country will soon have everything taken away. That will happen because the people who live there continually act very violently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:7; Isaiah 7:23–24; JEREMIAH 25:9"> The towns where people live will be devastated, and the land will become barren/empty. Then you people will know that it is I, Yahweh, who have the power to do what I say that I will do.' ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:2–3; EZEKIEL 11:3; EZEKIEL 12:27"> “You human, the people in Israel have this proverb/saying: ‘The days pass, and whatthe prophets predict never happens.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOEL 2:1; EZEKIEL 7:5–7; ZEPHANIAH 1:14"> So say to them, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: I am going to prove that ◄what they say/that proverb► is not correct, and then they will never say that again in Israel.’ Tell them, ‘It will soon be the time when everything that the prophets have predicted will happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:13–16; EZEKIEL 13:23; ZECHARIAH 13:2–4"> No longer willfalse prophets tell to the Israeli people false visions, or prophecies that try to please people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:5; EZEKIEL 12:28; Isaiah 55:11"> Instead, I, Yahweh, will say what I want to tell the people, and what I predict will quickly happen. You rebellious people, I will cause to happen everything that I say will happen. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Yahweh also gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DANIEL 10:14; EZEKIEL 12:22; 2 PETER 3:4"> “You human, the Israeli people are constantly saying about you, ‘The things that he sees in visionswill not happen soon. They are aboutthings that will happen many years from now, far in the future.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:23–25; MARK 13:32–37; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:2–3"> Therefore say to them, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord &#91;DOU&#93; says: I will no longer delaycausing to happen any of the things that I have prophesied. Whatever I have predicted willvery soon happen.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
