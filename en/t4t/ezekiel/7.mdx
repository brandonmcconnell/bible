## Israel will soon be destroyed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="AMOS 8:2; 1 PETER 4:7; EZEKIEL 7:3"> “You human, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say to the people &#91;MTY&#93; of Israel: All of Israel will soon be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:30; EZEKIEL 5:13; EZEKIEL 33:20"> Youpeople of Israel, the end has come. I will punish you severely. I will judge you for all the wicked things that you have done, and pay you back for your disgusting behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:7; EZEKIEL 11:21; EZEKIEL 5:11"> I will not pity you or act mercifully toward you. I will surely punish you for your wicked behavior &#91;DOU&#93;. Then you will know that it has happened because I, Yahweh, have done it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NAHUM 1:9; 2 KINGS 21:12–13; EZEKIEL 5:9"> This is also what Yahweh the Lord says: “You will soon experience many terrible disasters!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:7; EZEKIEL 7:3; 2 PETER 2:5"> This will be the endof Israel; your country will be finished! And your lives will be ended &#91;PRS&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:28; EZEKIEL 7:12; Isaiah 22:5"> It will be the end of you people who live in the landof Israel. The time has come; the daywhen you will be destroyed is near. At that time the people who worship idols on the mountains will not be happy; they will panic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 9:8; EZEKIEL 14:19; EZEKIEL 20:8"> I am very angry with you and am about to pour out my punishment &#91;MTY&#93; on you. I will judge you for all the wicked things that you have done and pay you back for your disgusting behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="REVELATION 20:13; MICAH 6:9; Isaiah 9:13"> I will not pity you or act mercifully toward you. I will surely punish you for your wicked behavior &#91;DOU&#93;. Then you will know that it is I, Yahweh, who have punished you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:5; JAMES 4:6; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:3"> The dayof your punishment is here! It has arrived! Disasters have come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:18; EZEKIEL 7:23; Isaiah 59:6–8"> People are acting violently and doing more wicked things. And nothing that belongs to those people will be left, none of their money, nothing that is valuable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 5:13–14; 1 CORINTHIANS 7:29–31; EZEKIEL 7:5–7"> Now is the time; that day has arrived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:24–28; LEVITICUS 25:31; JOB 15:25"> People who buy things should not rejoicebecause of buying things very cheaply, and those who sell things should not be sadbecause they had to sell things cheaply, becauseYahweh will be punishing &#91;MTY&#93; everyone. During the rest of their lives, people who sell some of their property will never be able to buy it back, because I, Yahweh, will never change my mind about what I have declared; because of your sins, I will destroy all of you people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:5; JEREMIAH 7:20; JEREMIAH 51:27"> Even though yoursoldiers blow trumpetsto prepare for a battle, no soldier will go into a battle, because I will have killed all of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:18; EZEKIEL 5:12; LAMENTATIONS 1:20"> When those things happen, your enemies will be outsidethe city with their swords, and there will be plagues and famines insidethe city. Thosewho flee from the city into the countryside will be killed bytheir enemies’ swords, and those who stay in the city will die from famines and plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; Isaiah 38:14; Ezra 9:15"> Those who survive and escape will flee to the mountains, and they all will moan like doves because of their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 21:7; HEBREWS 12:12; JEREMIAH 6:24"> Because everyone will be very afraid, all the people’s hands will become limp and their knees will become very weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="AMOS 8:10; Isaiah 15:2–3; Isaiah 3:24"> They will put on clothes made from rough cloth, and they will be terrified. Their faces will show that they are ashamed, and they will shave their headsto show that they are very sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:4; ZEPHANIAH 1:18; LUKE 12:19–20"> They will throw their silver into the streets, and consider that their gold is only like &#91;SIM&#93; garbage, because they will realize that their silver and gold will not be able to rescue them when I, Yahweh, punish them. They will not be able to use their gold and silver to buy things to fill their stomachs, because having a lot of gold and silver has led them to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:21; JEREMIAH 7:30; EZEKIEL 8:15–16"> They were proud of their beautiful jewelry, and they used it to make their detestable idols and disgusting statuesof their gods. So I will cause them to be disgusted with those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 KINGS 24:13; PSALMS 74:2–8; 2 CHRONICLES 36:18–19"> I will give their silver and gold to foreigners who invade your country and take away your valuable treasures. I will give those things to wicked people, and they will do disgraceful things to those treasures that I give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:17; PSALMS 74:18–23; PSALMS 74:10–11"> I will not ◄interfere with/stop► &#91;IDM&#93; robbers when they enter my sacred temple and desecrate it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 9:9; JEREMIAH 27:2; EZEKIEL 11:6"> Prepare chains to be fastened on you. People are being murdered &#91;MTY&#93; throughout the country, and people are acting violently throughout the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:7; PSALMS 106:41; EZEKIEL 21:31"> So I will bring armies of the nations whose people are extremely wicked to occupy the houses of the Israeli people. I will cause Israeli peoplewho think that they are &#91;IRO&#93; strong/mighty to no longer be proud. Your enemies will cause your places of worship to no longer be acceptable to be used.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:15–16; Isaiah 57:21; LAMENTATIONS 4:17–18"> When your enemies cause you to be terrified, you will plead for them to make peace, but there will be no peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:20; EZEKIEL 14:1; MICAH 3:6"> You will experience many disasters, and you will continually hear rumorsabout disasters that are happening in other places. People will plead with prophets to tell them what visions they have received, but the prophets will not have received any visions. Priests will no longer teach people the lawsthat I gave to Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:30; EZEKIEL 7:4–8; EZEKIEL 26:16"> Your king will mourn, and his son will no longer expectthat good things will happen. The hands of people throughout the country will tremble. And I will do to them what they deserve for their wicked behavior. I will judgeand condemn them the same way they have judgedand condemned others. Then they will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.”</span>
</div>
