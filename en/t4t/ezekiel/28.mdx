<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:2; GENESIS 10:15; JEREMIAH 25:22"> “You human, turn toward Sidon city, and declare the terrible things that will happen to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:13; EXODUS 14:17; EZEKIEL 20:41"> Give the people of Sidon this message from me, Yahweh the Lord:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I am your enemy, you people of Sidon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and by what I do to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will reveal that I am very great/glorious.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When I punish you and reveal that I am holy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">everyone who is watching that will know that it is I, Yahweh, who have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span></span>

<div className="bp">

I will gather them from distant countries where I have scattered them. And I will reveal to the nations that I am holy among my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:24; EZEKIEL 28:22; JEREMIAH 32:15"> My people will live safely in Israel; they will build houses and plant vineyards.
