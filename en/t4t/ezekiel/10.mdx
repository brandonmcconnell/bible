## Yahweh’s glory leaves the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 24:10; REVELATION 4:2–3; REVELATION 1:13"> Then in the vision I saw what resembled a throne made of ◄sapphire/a very valuable blue stone►. It was above the thing that resembled a dome that was above the heads of the four winged creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="REVELATION 8:5; EZEKIEL 10:16; EZEKIEL 1:13"> Yahweh said to the man wearing the linen robe, “Go between the wheels that are under the winged creatures. Pick up as many hot coals as you can, and scatter them over the city.” And while I watched, the man wearing the linen robe left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:4; EZEKIEL 9:3; EZEKIEL 8:16"> The four winged creatures were standing on the south side of the temple when the man wearing the linen robe entered. Then a cloud filled the inner courtyard of the temple area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:28; EZEKIEL 9:3; EXODUS 40:35"> And the ◄glory/dazzling light► of Yahweh rose up from above the winged creatures and went to the entrance of the temple. The cloud filled the temple, and the courtyard was full of the ◄glory/dazzling light► of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:24; JOB 40:9; EXODUS 19:16"> The sound made by the wings of the winged creatures could be heard as far away as the courtyard outside the temple. It wasvery loud, like &#91;SIM&#93; the voice of Almighty God when he speaks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:2; PSALMS 99:1; PSALMS 80:1"> When Yahweh commanded the man wearing the linen robe to take burning coals from among those winged creatures, the man went intothe courtyard and stood beside one of the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:41–42; EZEKIEL 1:13; EZEKIEL 41:23–26"> Then one of those winged creatures reached out his hand to the fire that was there among them. He picked up some of the coals and put them in the hands of the man wearing the linen robe, and that man took them and left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:8; EZEKIEL 10:21; Isaiah 6:6"> Under the wings of the winged creatures was something that resembled a human’s hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:15–17; DANIEL 10:6; REVELATION 21:20"> Then in the vision I saw four wheels alongside the winged creatures. There was one wheel beside each of the winged creatures. The wheels shone like &#91;SIM&#93; very valuable stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:16; PSALMS 36:6; PSALMS 104:24"> The wheels were all alike: Each had one wheel inside another wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:17; EZEKIEL 10:22; EZEKIEL 1:20"> Whenever they moved, they went straight in whatever direction one of the winged creatures faced. The wheels did not turn in another direction while the winged creatures flew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:18; REVELATION 4:8; REVELATION 4:6"> Their bodies, including their backs and hands and wings, were covered with eyes. The wheels were also covered with eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> I heard someone call them ‘the whirling wheels’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="REVELATION 4:7; EZEKIEL 10:21; 1 KINGS 7:36"> Each of the winged creatures had four faces. One face was like the face of a bull, one face was like the face of a human, one face was like the face of a lion, and one face was like the face of an eagle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:5; HOSEA 9:12; EZEKIEL 11:22"> Then the winged creatures rose up. They were the same living creatures that I had seen alongside the Kebar River/Canal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:19–21"> When the winged creatures moved, the wheels moved with them. When the winged creatures spread their wings to fly over the ground, the wheels did not leave them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:20–21; ROMANS 8:2; EZEKIEL 1:12"> When the winged creatures stopped, the wheels stopped. When the winged creatures started to fly, the wheels flew with them, because the spirit of the living creatures was in the wheels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 18:10; MATTHEW 23:37–39; PSALMS 78:60–61"> Then the ◄glory/dazzling light► left the entrance of the temple and stopped above the winged creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:16; EZEKIEL 1:17–21; EZEKIEL 1:26–28"> While I watched, the winged creatures spread their wings and started to fly, and the wheels went with them. They stopped at the gate on the east side of the temple area, and the ◄glory/dazzling light► of God, the one whom the Israelishad worshiped, was above them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:1; EZEKIEL 10:15; 1 KINGS 7:36"> Those were the same four living creatures that I had seen alongside the Kebar River/Canal, and I realized that they were the winged creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:6; EZEKIEL 41:18–19; EZEKIEL 10:14"> Each of them had four faces and four wings, and under their wings was what resembled a human’s hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 10:11; EZEKIEL 1:10; EZEKIEL 1:12"> Their faces were the same as the faces that I had seen at the Kebar River/Canal. Each of them flew straight ahead.</span>
