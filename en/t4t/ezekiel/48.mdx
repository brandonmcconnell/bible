## The division of the land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 47:15–17; JOSHUA 19:40–47; REVELATION 7:4–8"> “Here is a list of the tribes of Israel and the territory that each tribe is to receive: The tribe of Dan will be allotted land at the northern boundary of Israel: It will extend east from the Mediterranean Sea east to Hethlon city, then from there to Hamath Pass, and on to the town of Hazar-Enan south of Damascus, between Damascus and Hamath.<br />The tribe of Dan will receive land on the northern border of Israel from the eastern end of the country west to the Mediterranean Sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:24–31; GENESIS 30:12–13"> South of their land will be the territory for the tribe of Asher.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:32–39; GENESIS 30:7–8"> South of their land will be the territory for the tribe of Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:1–11; JOSHUA 13:29–31; GENESIS 48:14–20"> South of their land will be the territory for the tribe of Manasseh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:8–10; JOSHUA 17:14–18; JOSHUA 16:1–10"> South of their land will be the territory for the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:15–21; GENESIS 29:32; GENESIS 49:3–4"> South of their land will be the territory for the tribe of Reuben.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:1–63; JOSHUA 19:9; GENESIS 29:35"> South of their land will be the territory for the tribe of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:1–6; Isaiah 12:6; Isaiah 33:20–22"> South of their land will be an area set apart for special use. The temple will be in the center of that area.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It must be ◄8.3 miles/13.3 km.► long and ◄6.7 miles/10.6 km.► wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:4; EZEKIEL 44:28; MATTHEW 10:10"> For the priests, there must be a strip of land ◄8.3 miles/13.3 km.► long and ◄3.3 miles/5.3 km.► wide. The temple will be in the center of that area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 40:46; EZEKIEL 44:10; MATTHEW 24:45–46"> That area is for the priests who are descendants of Zadok. They are ones who served me faithfully and did not turn away from me, like most of the descendants of Levi and the other Israeli people did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:21; EZEKIEL 45:4"> When the land is distributed, that area must be given to the priests; it is land that is very sacred. It will be next to the land that will be given to the other descendants of Levi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:3; DEUTERONOMY 12:19; EZEKIEL 45:5"> The land that will be allotted to the other descendants of Levi will be the same size as the land allotted to the priests. So together, those two portions of land will be ◄8.3 miles/13.3 km.► long and ◄6.6 miles/10.6 km.► wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:28; LEVITICUS 27:9–10; EXODUS 22:29"> None of this special land will ever be permitted to be sold or traded or used by other people, because it belongs to me, Yahweh. It is set apart for me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 42:20; EZEKIEL 45:6; EZEKIEL 44:23"> The rest of that sacred area, a strip of land ◄8.3 miles/13.3 km.► long and ◄1.7 miles/2.65 km.► wide will be allotted for other people to use. It will be land where they will be permitted to build homes and have pasturelands. There will be a city at the center.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="REVELATION 21:16"> The city will be square, ◄1.5 miles/2.4 km.► on each side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Around the city there will be an area for farming, that will be ◄150 yards/135 meters► in each direction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 9:27; Ezra 2:43–58; EZEKIEL 45:6"> Outside the city, to the east and to the west, there will be a farming area. It will extend ◄3 miles/4.8 km.► to the east and ◄3 miles/4.8 km.► to the west. Men who work there will produce food for the people who work in the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:6; 1 KINGS 4:7–23; Nehemiah 11:1–36"> Some of those who come from the various Israeli tribes to work in the city may also work on the farmland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="REVELATION 21:16; HEBREWS 12:17"> That entire special area, including the sacred lands and the city, will be a square that is ◄8.3 miles/13.3 km.► on each side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 48:22; EZEKIEL 37:24; EZEKIEL 48:8"> The areas to the east and to the west of the sacred area and the city will belong to the king. One area will extend east to the eastern boundary of Israel and the other will extend west to the Mediterranean Sea. The sacred area in which the temple is located will be in the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> The area that belongs to the king will be between the land of the tribe of Judahto the north and the land of the tribe of Benjamin to the south.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:21–28; EZEKIEL 48:1–7; GENESIS 35:16–19"> South of that special area, each of the other tribes must be allotted one portion of land that extends from the eastern boundary of Israel west to the Mediterranean Sea.<br />Beginning at the north will be land for the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:1–9; GENESIS 29:33; GENESIS 49:5–7"> South of their land will be the territory for the tribe of Simeon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:17–23; GENESIS 30:14–18"> South of their land will be the territory for the tribe of Issachar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:10–16; GENESIS 30:19–20"> South of their land will be the territory for the tribe of Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:24–28; GENESIS 30:10–11"> South of their land will be the territory for the tribe of Gad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 47:15; 2 CHRONICLES 20:2; EZEKIEL 47:19–20"> The southern boundary of the tribe of Gad will extend south from Tamar to the springs at Meribah-Kadesh, and from there to the west along the dry riverbed at the border of Egypt to the Mediterranean Sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:1–21; NUMBERS 34:2; NUMBERS 34:13"> That is the land that you must allot to the tribes of Israel, to belong to them permanently. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The gates of the city

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EZEKIEL 48:16; REVELATION 21:16; EZEKIEL 48:32–35"> These will be the exits from the city: On the north side, which will be ◄1.5 miles/2.4 km.► long,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="REVELATION 21:12–13; Isaiah 26:1–2; REVELATION 21:21"> there must be three gates; each one will have the name of one of the tribes of Israel. The first one will be named for Reuben, the next for Judah, the next for Levi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> On the east side, also ◄1.5 miles/2.4 km.► long, will be gates named for Joseph, Benjamin, and Dan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> On the south side, also ◄1.5 miles/2.4 km.► long, will be gates named for Simeon, Issachar, and Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> On the west side, also ◄1.5 miles/2.4 km.► long, will be gates named for Gad, Asher, and Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="REVELATION 21:3; ZECHARIAH 2:10; REVELATION 22:3"> So the distance around the city will thus be ◄6 miles/9.6 km.►.<br />And from that time on, the name of the city will be ‘Yahweh Is There’.’</span>
</div>
</div>
