## Ezekiel condemned the idol worshipers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:1; EZEKIEL 20:1; 2 KINGS 6:32"> One day some of the elders of Israel came to me and sat down in front of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="AMOS 3:7; 1 KINGS 14:4"> Then Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:7; EZEKIEL 7:19; EZEKIEL 20:3"> “You human, these men want to &#91;IDM&#93; worship idols, and they are allowing idols to induce them to sin &#91;IDM&#93;. So I will certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; answer them if they ask me for advice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:4; EZEKIEL 14:7; Isaiah 3:11"> So say this to them: ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: When any Israeli person wants to worship idols which will induce him to sin, and he goes to a prophetto get advice, I, Yahweh, will give him the answer that he deserves to receive because he worship idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; ZECHARIAH 11:8; ROMANS 1:28"> I will do that in order that the Israeli people, who have abandoned me in order to worship their idols, will sincerely &#91;IDM&#93; worship me again.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:30; Isaiah 30:22; EZEKIEL 36:31–32"> Therefore, say to the Israeli people, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: ◄Repent/Stop sinning►! Stop worshiping your idols, and stop all your other detestable behavior!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 12:48; Isaiah 58:1–2; LEVITICUS 24:22"> When any of you Israeli people or any foreigner living among you turns away from me and starts worshiping idols &#91;IDM&#93; that induce him to sin, and then goes to a prophet to find out what I want him to do, I myself will answer him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 15:7; JEREMIAH 44:11; EZEKIEL 5:15"> I will show that I detest him, and causewhat happens to him to be a warning to others, and cause him to be someone whom people despise. I will cause him to no longer be allowed to associate with my people. Then you will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:25; JEREMIAH 4:10; PSALMS 81:11–12"> And if a prophet is deceived and gives a false prophecy, even though I haveallowed him to give that message, I will get rid of him and remove him from among my Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:49; EZEKIEL 14:4; JEREMIAH 8:11–12"> Both the prophet and the one who asks him for advice will be guilty, and I will punish both of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:23; EZEKIEL 44:10; REVELATION 21:7"> Then you Israeli people will no longer abandon me, and you will no longer become unacceptable to me because of your sins. You will be my people, and I will be your God. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say.”</span>

## Yahweh will punish Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:26; EZEKIEL 4:16; Isaiah 24:20"> “You human, ifthe people of some country sin against me by abandoning me, and I punish &#91;IDM&#93; them by cutting off their food supply and by sending a famine, with the result that people and animals die,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:20; JEREMIAH 15:1; EZEKIEL 28:3"> even if Noah, Daniel, and Job were there, they would save only themselves because of their being righteous. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:17; LEVITICUS 26:22; JEREMIAH 15:3"> Or, if I send wild animals throughout the country, and they attack and killmany of &#91;HYP&#93; the people, with the result that travel in that country becomes very dangerous and no one travels through that country,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 19:29; JAMES 5:16; EZEKIEL 33:11"> as surely as I am alive, even if those three men were in that country, they could not save even their own sons or daughtersfrom being killed. Only those three would ◄be saved/remain alive►, and the country would become desolate. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:25; EZEKIEL 21:3–4; ZEPHANIAH 1:3"> Or, if I bring enemy soldiers &#91;MTY&#93; to attack that country, and they kill &#91;MTY&#93; people and animals throughout that country with their swords,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> as surely as I am alive, even if those three men were in that country, they would not be able to save even their own sons or daughtersfrom being killed. They would save only themselves. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:22; EZEKIEL 5:12; JEREMIAH 14:12"> Or, if I send a plague into that land and cause the people and the animals to diefrom the plague because I am very angry with those people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:14; 1 JOHN 2:29; Isaiah 3:10"> as surely as I am alive, even if Noah, Daniel and Job were in that country, they would not be able to save even their sons and daughters. They would save only themselves because of their being righteous. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:27; EZEKIEL 5:17; EZEKIEL 14:13"> So now this is what I,, Yahweh the Lord, say: I will cause four things to happen to the people of Jerusalem to punish them very severely. Some people and animals will be killed by swords, some will die from famines, others will be attacked and killed by wild animals, and others will die in plagues.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:16; EZEKIEL 20:43; HEBREWS 12:6–11"> But some of your people will remain alive, both adults and children. They will come to you, Ezekiel, in Babylon. And when you see theirdisgusting behavior and actions, you will realize that I had good reasons for causing the people of Jerusalem to experience those many great disasters &#91;DOU&#93; that I have sent to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:8–9; REVELATION 15:4; DEUTERONOMY 8:2"> When you see those things that they do, you will know that there were very good reasons &#91;LIT&#93; for me to do everything that I caused to happen to them. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare.”</span>
