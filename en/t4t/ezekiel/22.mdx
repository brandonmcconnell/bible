## The sins of the people of Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; EZEKIEL 24:6; EZEKIEL 24:9"> “You human, are you ready to condemn the people of Jerusalem? It is &#91;RHQ&#93; a city full of murderers &#91;MTY&#93;. Remind them of all the detestable things that they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:27; ROMANS 2:5; 2 PETER 2:3"> Then say, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: By your murdering people and by ◄defiling yourselves/making yourselves unacceptable to God► by making idols, you people of this city &#91;APO&#93; have brought to yourselves the time when you will be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 KINGS 21:16; PSALMS 44:13–14; EZEKIEL 22:2"> You have become guilty by doing both of those things &#91;DOU&#93;. You have brought to an end your time to remain alive &#91;DOU&#93;. Therefore I will cause you to become a group whom the people of other nations will scorn; they will all laugh at you &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; JEREMIAH 15:2–3"> People in countries that are near you and people who live far from you will make fun of you, because your city is full of lawless people and full of confusion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:27; Isaiah 1:23; ZECHARIAH 3:3"> Think about how each of your Israeli kings have used their power to cause people to be murdered &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:16; EXODUS 22:21–22; LEVITICUS 20:9"> Your people do not respect/honor their parents; they have oppressed foreigners; they mistreat orphans and widows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:38–39; EZEKIEL 22:26; EZEKIEL 20:13"> You despise my sacred places and ◄dishonor/do not respect► the Sabbath days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HOSEA 4:14; LEVITICUS 19:16; HOSEA 4:10"> Among you are men who tell lies in order to cause others to be executed &#91;MTY&#93;. There are those who eatfood offered to idols at the hilltop shrines, and they perform disgusting sexual acts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:19; LEVITICUS 18:7–8; EZEKIEL 18:6"> There are men who have sex with their father’s wives &#91;EUP&#93;, and men who have sex with women during their monthly menstrual periods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:15; 2 SAMUEL 13:14; LEVITICUS 20:17"> There are men who have sex with someone else’s wife. Some men have sex with their daughters-in-law or with their sisters or half-sisters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:25; EZEKIEL 23:35; PSALMS 106:21"> There are among you men who accept bribes in order to cause someone to be executed &#91;MTY&#93;. You charge high interest when you lend people money. You become rich by forcing people to give you money. Andthe worst thing is that you have forgotten me, Yahweh the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 21:17; Isaiah 33:15; AMOS 2:6–8"> So I will shake my fists at you to show that I am angry with you because of your stealing money from people and murdering &#91;MTY&#93; people who live among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 21:7; EZEKIEL 17:24; EZEKIEL 24:14"> When I finishpunishing you, you will no longer &#91;RHQ&#93; be courageous/brave &#91;DOU&#93;. I, Yahweh, have said what I will do to you, and I will do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:8; EZEKIEL 22:22; DEUTERONOMY 4:27"> I will cause you to be scattered among many nations &#91;DOU&#93;, and I will cause you to stop your sinful behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:7; PSALMS 83:18; EZEKIEL 7:24"> When the people of other nations see that you have been humiliated, you will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The people of Jerusalem must be purified

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Yahweh also said to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 119:119; Isaiah 1:22; JEREMIAH 6:28–30"> “You human, your Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93; have becomeuseless to me. They are like &#91;MET&#93; dross/slag to me. They are like &#91;SIM&#93; theworthless copper, tin, iron, and lead that remains after silver is melted in avery hot furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:30; EZEKIEL 24:3–6; MICAH 4:12"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: Because you have all become like dross/slag, I will gather you in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:11–12; Isaiah 54:16; EZEKIEL 24:13"> People putore containing silver, copper, iron, lead, and tin in a very hot furnace and melt them in a blazing fireto burn up the impurities. Similarly &#91;SIM&#93;, I will gather you togetherinside Jerusalem, and because I am very angry with you, what I will do will be as though I am melting you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:7; NAHUM 1:6; PSALMS 50:3"> It will be as though I will blow on you with a hot breath that shows that I am very angry, andit will be as though you will be melted,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:8; EZEKIEL 20:33; HOSEA 5:10"> as though you will be melted like &#91;SIM&#93; silver is melted in a furnace, and then you will know that I, Yahweh, have poured out my punishment &#91;MTY&#93; on you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The sins of the leaders in Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:14–16; JEREMIAH 2:30; 2 CHRONICLES 28:22"> “You human, say to the Israeli people, ‘WhenYahweh is angry with you, there has been no rain or showers in your country.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="HOSEA 6:9; JEREMIAH 2:34; EZEKIEL 13:19"> Your leaders are like &#91;SIM&#93; lions that tear apart the animals that they have killed. Your leaders destroy their people. They steal treasures and other valuable things from people, and they cause many women to become widowsby murdering their husbands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:10; EZEKIEL 44:23; EZEKIEL 22:8"> Their priests disobey my laws and disrespect my sacred things by saying that there is no difference between things that are sacred and those that are not sacred, and by ignoring my laws about honoring the Sabbath days. As a result, they no longer honor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:13; Isaiah 1:23; EZEKIEL 22:6"> Their officials are like &#91;SIM&#93; wolves that tear apart the animals that they have killed: They murder people &#91;DOU&#93; in order to get their money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:6–7; EZEKIEL 13:10–16; JEREMIAH 23:25–32"> Their prophets, by falsely saying they have received visionsfrom God, and by giving false messages, try to ◄cover up &#91;MET&#93;/say that it is all right to commit► those sins. They say, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says,’ when I have said nothing to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:7; EXODUS 23:9; EXODUS 22:21"> The Israeli people force others to give them money, and they rob people. They oppress poor &#91;DOU&#93; people, and they mistreat foreigners among them by not treating them fairlyin the courts.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:16; JEREMIAH 5:1; PSALMS 106:23"> I looked among them to find a man who would cause the people of the city to repent &#91;MET&#93; so that I would not need to get rid of them. But I did not find anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:43; EZEKIEL 7:8–9; EZEKIEL 9:10"> So because I am very angry with them, I will severely punish them &#91;MTY&#93; for all the wicked things that they have done. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh the Lord, have said it.”</span>
</div>
</div>
