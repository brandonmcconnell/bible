## A parable about the leaders of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:1; JEREMIAH 3:15; Isaiah 40:11"> “You human, prophesy about theleaders of Israel. They should be taking care of my people like &#91;MET&#93; shepherds take care of their sheep. Say to them, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says: Terrible things will happen to you leaders &#91;MET&#93; of Israel who only take care of yourselves! You should certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; take care of your people like &#91;MET&#93; a shepherd takes care of his flock of sheep!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:16; JEREMIAH 2:30; EZEKIEL 33:25–26"> It is as though you eat the curds, you slaughter the very good animals and wear clothes made from their wool. But you do not take care of the flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:12–13; MATTHEW 9:36; EZEKIEL 34:16"> You have not enabled those who are weak to become strong. You have not healed those who were sick. You have not bandaged those who were wounded/injured. You have not brought back those who went astray. You have not searched for those who became lost. You have ruled your people harshly and cruelly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:36; 2 CHRONICLES 18:16; JEREMIAH 23:2"> So they were scattered, because you did not take care of them like &#91;MET&#93; a shepherd takes care of his sheep. And when they were scattered, it was as though wild animals attacked and killed them and ate their flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 142:4; 1 PETER 2:25; JOHN 10:16"> My people wandered like sheep all over the high hills and mountains. They were scattered all over the earth, and no one searched for them &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:2–3; MICAH 3:8–9; PSALMS 82:1–7"> Therefore, you who should be acting like shepherds, listen to what I, Yahweh the Lord say:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 PETER 2:13; ACTS 20:33; EZEKIEL 34:5–6"> As surely as I am alive, my people are like a flock of sheep that has no shepherd, and as a result it is as though wild animals have attacked my people and eaten them. You did not search for them; instead, you only wanted to provide food for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Therefore, you who should be like &#91;MET&#93; shepherds, listen to what I, Yahweh say:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:2; ZECHARIAH 10:3; EZEKIEL 13:8"> I am opposed to you leaders who should be like &#91;MET&#93; shepherds for my people. I will remove you from taking care of my people, with the result that you will no longer feed yourselves instead of feeding them. I will rescue my people from you, with the result that you will longer be able to butcher them and eat them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOHN 10:16; LUKE 19:10; JEREMIAH 23:3"> This is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say to you leaders: I myself will search for my sheep and take care of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; JOHN 10:11–12; LUKE 19:10"> Like a shepherd finds his scattered sheep, I will rescue my people from all the places to which they have been scattered, at a time when things were very gloomy and they experienced disasters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:25–26; EZEKIEL 11:17; Isaiah 11:11–16"> I will bring them back from many countries and gather them together again in their own land. Because my people are like sheep &#91;MET&#93;, it is as though I will enable them to find good pastureland on the hills of Israel, and in the ravines, and in the villages of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 10:9; JEREMIAH 31:25; PSALMS 31:8–10"> It is as though I will allow them to graze in good pastures on the mountaintops. They will lie down in good ◄grazing areas/grassy fields► there &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:15; PSALMS 23:1–2; JOHN 21:15"> I myself will take care of my people and enable them to lie down and rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 19:10; EZEKIEL 34:4; EZEKIEL 34:11"> I will search for those who are lost and bring back the ones who have strayed away. I will bandage those who have been injured and strengthen those who are weak. But I will get rid of those who are fat and powerful. I will act fairly toward the people who are like &#91;MET&#93; my sheep. That is what I, Yahweh, promise.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:20–22; MATTHEW 25:32–33; ZECHARIAH 10:3"> And as for you who are like &#91;MET&#93; my sheep, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: I will judge between each of you; and I will separate those that are peaceful like sheep from those that are always trying to dominate others like goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:13; LUKE 11:52; NUMBERS 16:13"> It is bad that &#91;RHQ&#93; some of you keep the best pastures for yourselves. It is even worse that &#91;RHQ&#93; you trample the good grass with your feet. You yourselves drink the clear water &#91;RHQ&#93;. But with your feet you cause the other water to become muddy &#91;RHQ&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> It is as though you &#91;RHQ&#93; are forcing my flock to eat the grass that you have trampled and to drink the water that you have caused to become muddy!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:31–46; EZEKIEL 34:10; PSALMS 22:12–16"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say to you: I myself will judge between those of you who are like &#91;MET&#93; the fat sheep and those of you who are like the thin sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:5; DEUTERONOMY 33:17; LUKE 13:14–16"> Youwho are like strong sheep, it is as though with your shoulders and buttocks you have shoved away those who are like thin sheep, and you have butted them with your horns, until you have chased them all away from the good pastureland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:2–3; PSALMS 72:12–14; EZEKIEL 34:10"> But I will rescue my people, and they no longer will be ◄harassed/treated cruelly►. I will judge between one person and another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:24–25; JEREMIAH 30:9; Isaiah 40:11"> And I will appoint one leader &#91;MET&#93; for them, someone who will be like King David, who served me very well. That leader will take care of them and be like their shepherd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:33; EZEKIEL 36:28; JEREMIAH 23:5–6"> I, Yahweh, will be their God, and the one who is like &#91;MET&#93; King David will be their king. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:6–9; EZEKIEL 37:26; LEVITICUS 26:6"> I will make a peace agreement with the people of Israel. In this agreement I will promise to get rid of all the wild animals in Israel, in order that my people may live safely, even in the desert and in the forests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:12; Isaiah 44:3; LEVITICUS 26:4"> I will bless them, and I will bless the places close to my sacred hill. I will bless them by sending them rain showers at the right season; they will be showers to bless them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:8; LEVITICUS 26:4; LEVITICUS 26:13"> The fruit trees will produce fruit and the ground will produce crops. And my people will live safely in their land. When I rescue them from people who caused them to be slaves &#91;MET, DOU&#93;, they will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:27; JEREMIAH 30:10; EZEKIEL 39:26"> Soldiers from other nations will no longer take away their valuable possessions, and wild animals will no longer attack them. They will live safely, and no one will cause them to be afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:29; Isaiah 4:2; EZEKIEL 34:26–27"> I will cause their land to be famous/well-known for its good crops. There will no longer be famines in the land, and people in other nations will no longer ridicule them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:27; EZEKIEL 34:24; PSALMS 46:11"> Then they will know that I, Yahweh their God, am helping them, and they will know that they, the Israeli people, are my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; JOHN 10:16; PSALMS 78:52"> It will be as though &#91;MET&#93; my people are my sheep whom I will take care of, and I will be their God. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
