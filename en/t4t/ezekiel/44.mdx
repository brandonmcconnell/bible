## The king, the Levites, and the priests.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:1; EZEKIEL 40:17; EZEKIEL 42:14"> Then in the vision the man brought me back to the outer entryway to the temple area, the one on the east side, but the outer entrance was shut.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:2–4; EXODUS 24:10; Isaiah 6:1–5"> Yahweh said to me, “This entrance must remain shut. It must not be opened by anyone; no one will be permitted to enter it. It must remain shut because I, Yahweh, the God of Israel, have caused it to be sacred by entering through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:2; EZEKIEL 34:24; EZEKIEL 37:25"> Only the king of Israel will be permitted to sit inside this entryway to eat food in the presence of Yahweh. But he will must enter and leave the temple area through the entry room of this entryway.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:28; REVELATION 1:17; EZEKIEL 3:23"> Then the man brought me through the north entryway to the front of the temple. I looked and saw that the glory of Yahweh filled his temple, and I prostrated myself on the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 40:4; EZEKIEL 43:10–11; DEUTERONOMY 12:32"> Yahweh said to me, “You human, look carefully, and listen carefully to everything that I tell you about all the regulations concerning the temple. Note carefully the temple entrance and all the exits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:9; EZEKIEL 45:9; 1 PETER 4:3"> Say this to the rebellious Israeli people: ‘This is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: You Israeli people, I will no longer endure the detestable things that you do!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:25; JEREMIAH 9:26; EZEKIEL 44:9"> In addition to all the other detestable things that you do, you brought into my temple foreign men who had not been circumcised and who were godless &#91;IDM&#93;. By doing that, you caused my temple to be an unacceptable place to worship me, while you offered food and fat and blood, and you disobeyed my agreement with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:14; NUMBERS 18:3–5; EZEKIEL 40:45–46"> Instead of doing what I commanded you to do in regard to my holy things, you appointed foreigners to be in charge of my temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOEL 3:17; EZEKIEL 44:7; ZECHARIAH 14:21"> But this is what I, Yahweh, have said: No foreigners, no men who are not circumcised, no godless people are permitted to enter my temple, not even foreigners who live among you Israelis permanently.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:26; EZEKIEL 48:11; 2 KINGS 23:8–9"> Most of the descendants of Levi abandoned me along with most of the Israeli people, and started to worship idols. They must be punished for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:9; 2 CHRONICLES 29:34; 2 CHRONICLES 30:17"> They are still permitted to work in my temple and to be in charge of the entrances of the temple, and they are permitted to slaughter the animals that will be completely burned on the altar, and to burn other sacrifices for the people, and they will be able to help the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 KINGS 16:10–16; PSALMS 106:26; EZEKIEL 14:3–4"> But because they helped the people to worship idols and caused many of the Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93; to sin by worshiping idols, I have lifted up my arm and solemnly declared that they must be punished for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:3; 2 KINGS 23:9; EZEKIEL 16:61"> They must not come near me to serve as priests. They must not come near any sacred things or the holy offerings. They must be ashamed of the shameful things that they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:28–32; NUMBERS 18:4"> But I will still put them in charge of the work in the temple and allow them to do all the work that needs to be done there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:8; EZEKIEL 48:11; EZEKIEL 44:7"> But most of the priests who are descendants of Levi and from the clan of Zadok worked in my temple when the other Israelis abandoned me. So they are permitted to come near to me to work for me. They will stand in my presence to offer sacrifices of animals’ fat and blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 41:22; MALACHI 1:12; NUMBERS 18:7–8"> They are the only ones who are permitted to enter my temple. They are the only ones who are permitted to come near my altar to serve me and do what I tell them to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="REVELATION 19:8; EXODUS 28:43; EXODUS 39:27–29"> When they enter one of the entrances into the inner courtyard, they must wear linen clothes. They must not wear any clothes made of wool while they do their work at the entrances of the inner courtyard or inside the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 39:28; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:4–10; Isaiah 3:20"> They must have linen turbans wrapped around their heads and wrap linen undergarments around their waists. They must not wear anything that would cause them to perspire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:20; LEVITICUS 6:27; LEVITICUS 6:10–11"> Before they go out into the outer courtyard where other people are, they must take off the clothes that they have been wearing and leave them in the sacred rooms, and they must put on other clothes, in order that the other people are not punished because of their touching the sacred clothes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 11:14; NUMBERS 6:5; LEVITICUS 21:5–24"> The priests must not shave their heads or allow their hair to become long; but they must regularly trim the edges of their hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:9; TITUS 1:7–8; 1 TIMOTHY 5:23"> Priests must not drink wine before they enter the inner courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:7; LEVITICUS 21:13–14; 1 TIMOTHY 3:2"> Also, priests must not marry widows of men who were not priests or women who have been divorced. Priests are permitted to marry only Israeli &#91;MTY&#93; virgins or widows of other priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:26; LEVITICUS 10:10–11; 2 TIMOTHY 2:24–25"> Priests must teach the people the difference between things that are holy/sacred and things that are not, and they must teach them the difference between things that are acceptable to me and things that are not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:4; EZEKIEL 22:26; 2 CHRONICLES 19:8–10"> When there are disputes between people, the priests are the ones who must be the judges and decide according to my laws what must be done. They must obey all my laws and decrees concerning the sacred festivals, and they must keep my Sabbath days ◄holy/set apart►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:16; LEVITICUS 22:4; MATTHEW 8:21–22"> A priest is permitted to go near the corpse of his father or his mother or his son or his daughter or his brother or his unmarried sister; but he is still defiled by doing that. But a priest must not defile himself by going near a corpse of anyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:13–14; NUMBERS 19:11–14; NUMBERS 6:10–21"> But even if a priest touches the corpse of a close relative, he must perform the rituals to become acceptable to serve me again. After he performs those rituals, he must wait seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:9–11; EZEKIEL 44:17; LEVITICUS 4:3–35"> Then, on the day that he enters the inner courtyard again to serve me in the temple, he must give an offering to remove his guilt for his sin. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:33; NUMBERS 18:20; DEUTERONOMY 18:1–2"> The priests are not permitted to own any property. They will have only what I provide for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:14; LEVITICUS 27:21; LEVITICUS 27:28"> They will eat the offerings other people make from grain, offerings to remove people’s guilt for having sinned, and offerings made when people fail to give to Yahweh the things that they are required to give. Everything else in Israel that is completely dedicated to me, Yahweh, will belong to the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:35–37; 2 CHRONICLES 31:10; DEUTERONOMY 18:4"> The best fruits of the first part of each harvest and all the other special gifts that are given to me will belong to the priests. You must give them the first part of your finely-ground flour, in order that I will bless the people who live in &#91;MTY&#93; your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:8; EXODUS 22:31; DEUTERONOMY 14:21"> Priests must not eat the flesh of any bird or animal that was found dead or that has been killed by wild animals.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
