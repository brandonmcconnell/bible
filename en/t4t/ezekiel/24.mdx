## A parable about meat cooking in a pot

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:1; EZEKIEL 20:1; EZEKIEL 29:17"> Almost nine yearsafter we had been taken to Babylonia, on the tenth day of the tenth month of that year, Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:1; JEREMIAH 52:4; 2 KINGS 25:1"> “You human, write down what day of the month this is. On this daythe army ofthe King of Babylon has surrounded Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:3; EZEKIEL 17:2; EZEKIEL 20:49"> Tell those rebellious Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93; in Babylon a parable. Say this to them: This is what Yahweh the Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Pour water into the cooking pot</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and put the pot onthe fire.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICAH 3:2–3; EZEKIEL 22:18–22; MATTHEW 7:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Put into the pot some pieces of meatfrom one of your best sheep:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Put in the leg and shoulder, which are the best pieces.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then fill the rest of the pot with the best bones.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:24–27; JEREMIAH 52:10; JEREMIAH 39:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pile wood on the fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cook the bones and the meat in the boiling water.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:2; NAHUM 3:1; OBADIAH 1:11"> Do that because this is what Yahweh the Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Terrible things will happen toJerusalem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is city that is full of murderers &#91;MTY&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a city that is like &#91;MET&#93; a scorched pot</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose black residue inside cannot be removed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Take the pieces of meat out of the pot,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but do not choose which pieces to take out.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 17:13; DEUTERONOMY 12:16; JOB 16:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The blood of the people who were murdered in Jerusalem is still there;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they were murdered on the bare rocks,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not on the soil, where their blood could be covered.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:21–22; REVELATION 18:5–10; EZEKIEL 22:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I am the one who caused the blood of those who were murdered &#91;MTY&#93; to be smeared on the bare rock,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">where their blood could not be covered;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I did that in order thatI could see it and</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then be angry and get revenge.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:6; HABAKKUK 2:12; Isaiah 31:9"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Terrible things will happen to that city that is full of murderers &#91;MTY&#93;!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It will be as though I also will pile high the wood in the fire.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:16; JEREMIAH 17:3; LAMENTATIONS 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, heap on the wood</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and light the fire!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cook the meat well,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and mix some spices with it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">cook it until the bones are charred.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:10; MALACHI 4:1; Isaiah 1:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then set the empty pot on the coalsof the fire</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until the pot becomes very hot and the copper glows,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the result that the impurities and the rust will be burned up.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:5; JEREMIAH 2:13; JEREMIAH 5:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though I tried to get rid of that rust,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but I was not able to do it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not even by putting that pot in a fire.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:24; EZEKIEL 5:13; ZEPHANIAH 3:2"> The rustin the pot represents your immoral behavior. I tried to cleanse you from your wicked behavior, but you did not allow me to do that. So you will not be cleansedfrom the guilt of your sin untilI have punished you and I am no longer angry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:27; EZEKIEL 18:30; Isaiah 3:11"> I, Yahweh, have said that I will surely punish you. And it is time for me to do that. I will not change my mind; I will not refrain from punishing you, and I will not pity you. I will judge you and punish you as you deserve to be punished for your sinful behavior. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh the Lord, have said it.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ezekiel’s wife died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> One day Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:10; JEREMIAH 13:17; SONG OF SONGS 7:10"> “You human, I am going to suddenly take from youyour wife, whom you love very much. Butwhen she dies, do notshow that you are sad or lament or cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HOSEA 9:4; 2 SAMUEL 15:30; LEVITICUS 10:6"> Groan quietly; do not cry openly for her. Keep your turban wrapped around your head, andinstead of being barefoot, keep your sandals on your feet. Do not cover the lower part of your faceto show that you are sad. And do not eat the kind of food that people who are mourning usually eat.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 7:29–30"> Soone morning I talked to the peopleas usual, and that evening my wife suddenly died. The next morning I did what Yahweh had told me to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:9; EZEKIEL 37:18; MALACHI 3:7–8"> Then the people asked me, “What do the things that you are doing signify to us?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So I said to them, “This is what Yahweh told me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:47; EZEKIEL 23:25; PSALMS 27:4"> ‘Tell the Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93; that I am about to destroy the temple, the building that you are very proud of, the building that you delight to look at. Your children whom you leftin Jerusalem when you were forced to come to Babylon will be killed bytheir enemies’ swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 78:64; JEREMIAH 16:4–7; EZEKIEL 24:16–17"> When that happens, you will do like I have done: You will not cover the lower part of your faces, or eat the kinds of food that people who are mourning usually eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:10; EZEKIEL 4:17; LEVITICUS 26:39"> You will keep your turbans wrapped around your heads and keep your sandals on your feet. You will not mourn or cry, but your bodies will become very thin and slowly die because of your sins. And you will groan to each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:3; Isaiah 20:3; LUKE 11:29–30"> Ezekiel will be a warning to you, and you must do what he has done. When that happens, you will know that I, Yahweh the Lordhave the power to do what I say that I will do.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:21; PSALMS 48:2; JEREMIAH 11:22"> Then Yahweh said to me, “You human, soon I will destroy their sacred temple, which they rejoice about and which they respect and delight to look at, and I will get rid of their sons and daughters also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOB 1:15–19; EZEKIEL 33:21–22; 1 SAMUEL 4:12–18"> On that day, someone will escape from Jerusalem and come and tell you what has happened there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:22; EZEKIEL 29:21; LUKE 21:15"> When that happens, you will be able to speak again &#91;MTY&#93; without constraint. You two will talk together &#91;DOU&#93;. You will be a warning to the people; and they will know that I, Yahweh the Lord, have the power to do what I say that I will do.”</span>
</div>
</div>
