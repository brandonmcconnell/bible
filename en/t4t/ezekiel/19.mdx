## A funeral song for Israel’s leaders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:2; EZEKIEL 26:17; 2 KINGS 24:6"> Yahweh said to me, “Ezekiel, sing a sad funeral songa which will be a parable abouttwo of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:3; Isaiah 5:29; JOB 4:11"> Say to the Israeli people,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘It is as though &#91;MET&#93; your mother was a brave female lion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who raised her cubs among other lions.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:25; 2 KINGS 23:31–32; 2 CHRONICLES 36:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">She taught one of them to huntfor other animals to kill,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he even learned tokill and eat people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:4; 2 KINGS 23:33–34; 2 CHRONICLES 36:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">When people from other nations heard about him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they trapped him in a pit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then they used hooks</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to drag him to Egypt.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 19:3; 2 KINGS 23:34–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">His mother waited for him to return,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but soon she stopped hoping/expecting that he would return.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So she raised another cub</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who also became very fierce.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:5; 2 CHRONICLES 36:9; JEREMIAH 26:1–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">He hunted along with other lionsfor animals to kill,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he even learned tokill and eat people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:12; EZEKIEL 12:19; MICAH 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He destroyed forts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he ruined cities.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When he roared loudly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">everyone was terrified.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:20; EZEKIEL 19:4; EZEKIEL 12:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">So people of other nations planned to kill him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and men came from many places</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to spread out a net for him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they caught him in a trap.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:6; EZEKIEL 6:2; JEREMIAH 36:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">They tied him with chains</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and took him to Babylonia.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And there he was locked in a prison,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that no one on the hills of Israel ever heard him roar again.’</span><br />Also, say to the Israeli people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 80:8–11; DEUTERONOMY 8:7; NUMBERS 24:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘It is as though &#91;SIM&#93; your mother was a grapevine</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that was planted along a stream.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There was plenty of water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so it had lots of branches and produced a lot of grapes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:3; PSALMS 80:15; DANIEL 4:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">That grapevine grew and became taller than all the nearby trees;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">everyone could see that it was very strong and healthy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And those branches were good for making scepters</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that symbolize the power/authorityof a king.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:10; HOSEA 13:15; Isaiah 27:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">ButYahweh became very angry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so he pulled up the vine by its roots</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and threw it on the ground,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">where the very hot winds from the desert dried up all its fruit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The strong branches wilted</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and were burned in a fire.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HOSEA 2:3; EZEKIEL 19:10; PSALMS 63:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now that vine has been planted in a hot, dry desert.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 80:15–16; EZEKIEL 19:11; 2 KINGS 24:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">A fire started to burn its stem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then started to burn the branches</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and burned all the grapes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now not even one strong branch remains;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will never become scepters for a king.’</span><br />That funeral song must be sung very sadly.”</span>
