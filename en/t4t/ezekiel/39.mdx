<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:2–3; EZEKIEL 35:3; NAHUM 3:5"> Yahweh said to me, “You human, prophesy about more terrible things that will happen to Gog, and say this to him: ‘Gog, I am opposed to you who rule Meshech and Tubal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 40:14; EZEKIEL 38:15; Isaiah 37:29"> I will turn you around and drag you and your armies from far north of Israel and send you to fight on the mountains in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 76:3; HOSEA 1:5; JEREMIAH 21:4–5"> When you are there, I will snatch your bows from your left hands and cause your arrows to fall from your right hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:17–20; EZEKIEL 33:27; EZEKIEL 38:21"> You and all the soldiers that are with you will die on the mountains in Israel. I will give your corpses to be food for the birds that eat dead flesh, and to the wild animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:19; EZEKIEL 32:4; JEREMIAH 8:2"> You will die in the open fields. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh the Lord, have said that it will happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:16; AMOS 1:4; EZEKIEL 30:8"> I will cause many fires to burn in Magog and among all those who live safely in the areas along their coasts, and they will know that it is I, Yahweh, who have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:16; EZEKIEL 38:23; EXODUS 20:7"> I will enable my Israeli people to know that I am holy. I will no longer allow people to damage my reputation, and people in other nations will know that I, Yahweh the Lord, am the Holy One in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:17; REVELATION 16:17; EZEKIEL 7:2–10"> I, Yahweh the Lord, declare that it will soon be the day that those things will happen. It will be the day that I have spoken to you about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 46:9; Isaiah 66:24; MALACHI 1:5"> At that time, those Israelis who live in the towns will go out and gather the weapons from your dead soldiers, and use them to make fires to cook their food. They will burn the small and large shields, the bows and arrows, the war clubs, and spears. There will be enough weapons to use as firewood for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 14:2; MICAH 5:8"> They will not need to gather firewood in the fields or cut wood from trees in the forests, because those weapons will be all the firewood that they will need. And they will take valuable things from those who took valuable things from them, and steal things from people who stole things from them. That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare will happen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 47:18; NUMBERS 34:11; EZEKIEL 38:2"> At that time I will create a graveyard for you, Gog, and your soldiers, in the valley east of the Dead Sea. That graveyard will block the road that travelers usually walk on, because you, Gog and all the soldiers of your huge army will be buried there. So it will be named ‘the Valley of Gog’s Huge Army’.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:23; EZEKIEL 39:14; EZEKIEL 39:16"> For seven months the people of Israel will be burying those corpses. It will be necessary to bury all of them, in order that the land will not be ◄defiled/considered unacceptable to me► because of any unburied corpses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:22; ZEPHANIAH 3:19–20; JEREMIAH 33:9"> All the people of Israel will do the work of burying them. The day when I win that victory they will honor me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:12; NUMBERS 19:11–19"> After those seven months are ended, the Israeli people will appoint men to go throughout the land to bury corpses, in order that the land will not remain defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 11:44"> When they go through the land, when one of them sees a human bone, he will set up a marker beside it. When the gravediggers see the markers, they will pick up the bones and bury them in the Valley of Gog’s Huge Army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> There will be a city there named Hamonah, which means ‘huge army’. And by doing this work of burying the corpses, they will ◄cleanse the land/cause the land to be acceptable to me again►.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:7; REVELATION 19:17–18; Isaiah 34:6"> Yahweh said to me, “You human, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: Summon every kind of bird and wild animal. Say to them, ‘Gather together from everywhere and come to the feast that Yahweh is preparing for you. It will be a great feast on the mountains in Israel. There you will eat men’s flesh and drink their blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 22:12; JEREMIAH 51:40; REVELATION 19:17–18"> You will eat the flesh of strong soldiers and drink the blood of kings as if they were fat animals—rams and lambs, goats and bulls—from the Bashan region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> At that feast that Yahweh is preparing for you, you will eat fat until your stomachs are full, and you will drink blood until it is as though you are drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="REVELATION 19:18; Haggai 2:22; PSALMS 76:5–6"> It will be as though you are eating at a table that I have set up for you. You will eat all you want of the flesh of horses and their riders, strong soldiers &#91;DOU&#93; of every kind.’ That is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:16; EZEKIEL 38:23; Isaiah 37:20"> I will show people of many nations that I am glorious, and all those nations will see how I punish them &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:7; EZEKIEL 28:26; EZEKIEL 39:28"> After that time, the Israeli people will know that I, Yahweh their God, have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:8–9; Isaiah 1:15; Isaiah 59:2"> And the people of other nations will know that the Israeli people had been forced to go to other countries because they sinned by not being faithful to me. I ◄turned away from/abandoned► them, and allowed their enemies to capture them &#91;IDM&#93;, and many &#91;HYP&#93; of them were killed by their enemies’ swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:19; JEREMIAH 2:17; JEREMIAH 4:18"> I punished them like they deserved to be punished because of their disgusting behavior and sins, and I turned away from them &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:13; Isaiah 27:12–13; HOSEA 1:11"> Therefore, this is now what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: I will now bring back from exile/Babylonia the descendants of Jacob; I will pity all the Israeli people, and I will also jealously protect my reputation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MICAH 4:4; 1 KINGS 4:25; EZEKIEL 16:63"> When the Israeli people are back in their own country, they will live safely in their land, with no one to cause them to be afraid, but they will be ashamed when they think about the disgraceful and unfaithful things that they did previously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:16; EZEKIEL 38:23; EZEKIEL 36:23–24"> When I have brought them back from their enemies’ countries and gathered them together in Israel, the people of many nations will know that I am holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EZEKIEL 39:22; AMOS 9:9; Isaiah 27:12"> And the Israeli people will know that I, Yahweh their Lord, have the power to do what I say that I will do. They will know that because even though I forced them to go to other countries, I will gather them together in their own country. I will not leave any of them in those countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOEL 2:28; 1 JOHN 3:24; Isaiah 32:15"> I will no longer turn away from them; I will pour out my Spirit on the Israeli people. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.”</span>
</div>
