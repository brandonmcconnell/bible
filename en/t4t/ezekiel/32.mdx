## Egypt will be destroyed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:1; EZEKIEL 33:21; EZEKIEL 1:2"> On the fifteenth day of that same month, Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:14; EZEKIEL 32:2; JEREMIAH 1:10"> “You human, wail about the many people of Egypt, because I will send them to the place under the earth, where they and people of other mighty nations will be. I will send them there, along with others, down to the place where the dead are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:18; EZEKIEL 32:29–30; JEREMIAH 9:25–26"> Say to them, ‘You people of &#91;APO&#93; Egypt, you think that &#91;IRO, RHQ&#93; you are more beautiful than the people of other nations. But you also will descend to the place where the godless dead people are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 28:3; EZEKIEL 32:23–26; EZEKIEL 32:29–30"> Your enemies will pull out their swords and kill many of you. A huge number of the people of Egypt who remain will be dragged away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9–10; EZEKIEL 32:27; EZEKIEL 32:19"> In the place where the dead people are, mighty leaders of other countries will make fun of the people of Egypt and say, “They have come here to lie with us godless people who were killed by our enemies’ swords!” ’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:26; EZEKIEL 32:29–30; EZEKIEL 32:24"> The corpses of the people of Assyria will be there. They will be surrounded by corpses of soldiers who were killed by their enemies’ swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:20; EZEKIEL 32:24–27; PSALMS 27:13"> The people’s graves are there in the deep pit, and corpses of soldiers of their army lie around their graves. Corpses of all those who had caused many others in other places to be terrified also will be there, having been killed by swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:18; EZEKIEL 26:20; JEREMIAH 49:34–39"> A great number of the people of Elam will be there. They also were killed by their enemies’ swords. They had caused people in many places &#91;HYP&#93; to be terrified. At that time they will lie there in that deep pit below the earth, and they, along with the others who have gone there, will be disgraced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 139:8; 1 CHRONICLES 10:4; 2 SAMUEL 1:20"> They will lie there among others who were slaughtered, surrounded by the graves of a huge crowd of other people. While they were alive, they caused people of other nations to be terrified; but they were godless, and now, having been killed by their enemies swords, they will lie with others in that deep pit, disgraced, all of them avoiding each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:13; GENESIS 10:2; EZEKIEL 38:2–3"> Corpses of soldiers of Meshech and Tubal will be there, surrounded by the graves of a huge crowd of their people. They were all godless people who were killed by swords because while they were alive, they also caused people in many places &#91;HYP&#93; to be terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:18–19; JOB 3:13–15; EZEKIEL 32:21"> They will surely &#91;RHQ&#93; not be buried honorably like the soldiers who have died, whose shields were buried with their corpses, whose swords were placed on their skulls in the graves. While those godless people were alive, they had caused many people in the land to be terrified, so they were punished for their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DANIEL 2:34–35"> You king of Egypt, you also be killed and will lie there with other godless people who have been killed by their enemies’ swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:7–22; MALACHI 1:3–4; OBADIAH 1:1–9"> People of the Edom people-group will be there, along with their kings and leaders. They were powerful, but they will be killed and lie there in the place where the other dead people are. They will lie there in that deep pit, with the other godless people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:6; EZEKIEL 39:2; EZEKIEL 28:21"> All the rulers of countries north of Israel, including people from Sidon, will be there. Because of their power, they caused people to be terrified, but they will lie there. They were godless, and they will lie there along with others who were killed by their enemies’ swords. They, along with everyone else who descends into that deep pit, will be disgraced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:16; EZEKIEL 14:22; LAMENTATIONS 2:13"> The king of Egypt and all his army will see them, and they will be comforted to know that there were other huge groups of people who were killed by their enemies’ swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:27; GENESIS 35:5; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:11"> While he was living, I allowed him to cause others in many countries to be terrified, but he and his huge army will be there among other godless people who had been killed by their enemies’ swords. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh the Lord, am the one who has said that it would happen.”</span>
