## A message about Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:17; EZEKIEL 26:1; EZEKIEL 20:1"> Almost ten yearsafter we had been taken to Babylonia, on the twelfth day of the tenth monthof that year, Yahweh gave me another message. He said to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:2; JEREMIAH 44:30; JEREMIAH 46:2–16"> “You human, turn toward Egypt and proclaim the terrible things that will happen to the king of Egypt and all his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:2; Isaiah 27:1; JEREMIAH 44:30"> Give the king this message from me, Yahweh the Lord:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘You king of Egypt, I am your enemy;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you are like &#91;MET&#93; a great monster/crocodile that lies in the streamsalong the Nile River.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You say, “The Nile River is mine;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I made it for myself.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 KINGS 19:28; EZEKIEL 38:4; AMOS 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">But it will be as though I will put hooks in your jaws</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and drag you out from the river onto the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with fish sticking to your scales.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 34:20; EZEKIEL 32:4–6; JEREMIAH 8:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will leave you and all those fish to die in the desert;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will fall onto the ground,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your corpse will not be picked up and buried,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because I have declared that your flesh will be food for the wild animals and birds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:21; Isaiah 36:6; EXODUS 9:14"> When that happens, all the people of Egypt will know that it is I, Yahweh, who have the power to do what I say that I will do. The Israeli people trusted that you would help them. But you have been like &#91;MET&#93; a reed pole in their hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; EZEKIEL 17:15–17; JEREMIAH 17:5–6"> And when they leaned on that pole, it splintered and tore open their shoulders. When they leaned on you, it was as though you were a pole that broke, and as a result you wrenched their backs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:17; EZEKIEL 30:10; EZEKIEL 29:19–20"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: “I will bring your enemies to attack you with their swords, and they will kill your people and your animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:3; PROVERBS 16:18; EZEKIEL 29:10–12"> Egypt will become an empty desert. Then the people of Egypt will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.” You proudly said, “The Nile River is mine; I made it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:12; EZEKIEL 30:6–9; EXODUS 14:2"> Therefore I am opposed to you and your streams, and I will cause Egypt to be ruined, and cause it to be an empty desert, from Migdol in the north to Aswan in the south, as far south as the border of Ethiopia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 32:13; JEREMIAH 43:11–12; EZEKIEL 30:10–13"> For 40 years no one will walk through that area, and no one will live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:19; EZEKIEL 30:7; EZEKIEL 30:23"> Egypt will be barren/empty, and it will be surrounded by other desolate nations. The cities in Egypt will be empty and deserted for 40 years, surrounded by ruined cities in nearby nations. I will cause the people of Egypt to be scattered to countries far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:26; Isaiah 19:22"> But this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, also say: “At the end of 40 years, I will enable the people of Egypt to return home again from the nations to which they were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:14; Isaiah 11:11; JEREMIAH 44:1"> I will bring back the people of Egypt who had been captured, and I will enable them to live again in the Pathros area in the south, where they lived previously. But Egypt will be a very unimportant &#91;DOU&#93; kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:11; EZEKIEL 30:13; EZEKIEL 17:14"> It will be the least important of all the nations; it will never again be greater than the nearby countries. I will cause Egypt to be very weak, with the result that it will never again rule over other nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HOSEA 8:13; Isaiah 20:5; EZEKIEL 29:6–7"> When that happens, the leaders of Israel will no longer be tempted to ask Egypt to help them. Egypt will be punished, and that will cause the Israeli people to not forget that they previously sinned by trusting that Egypt could help them. And the people of Israel will know that I, Yahweh the Lord, have the power to do what I say that I will do.” ' ”</span>
</div>

## Yahweh promises that the Babylonians will conquer Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:1; EZEKIEL 1:2; EZEKIEL 24:1"> Almost twenty-seven years after we had been taken to Babylonia, on the first day of the new year, Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:6; JEREMIAH 25:9; EZEKIEL 26:7–12"> “You human, the army of king Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon fought very hard against Tyre, with the result that their heads were rubbed bare and their shoulders became ◄raw/full of blisters►. But Nebuchadnezzar and his army did not get any valuable things from Tyre to reward them for their hard work to destroy Tyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:24–25; JEREMIAH 43:10–13; EZEKIEL 32:11"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: 'I will enable King Nebuchadnezzar’s army to conquer Egypt. They will carry away from there all the valuable things, in order that the king can give them to his soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:9; Isaiah 10:6–7; Isaiah 45:1–3"> I will enable them to conquer Egypt as a reward for what they did to Tyre, because I, Yahweh say that it was as though his army was working for me, doing what I wanted them to do, when they destroyed Tyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:10; LUKE 21:15; PSALMS 132:17"> And some day Israel will become a glorious nation &#91;MET&#93; again. When that happens, I will cause the Israeli people to respect what you say &#91;MTY&#93;. And then they will know that it has happened because I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.' ”</span>
</div>
