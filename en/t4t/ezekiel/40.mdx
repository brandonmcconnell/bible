## In a vision, Ezekiel was taken to Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:21; EZEKIEL 1:2–3; EZEKIEL 3:14"> Almost 25 years after we had been takento Babylonia, on the tenth day of the first month of that year, almost 14years after Jerusalem had been destroyed, in a vision I felt the power &#91;MTY&#93; of Yahweh on me, and he took me to Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:1; DANIEL 7:1; DANIEL 7:7"> He set me on a very high mountain. On the south side of that mountain there were some buildings that appeared to be part of a city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 47:3; REVELATION 11:1; DANIEL 10:5–6"> When he took there, I saw a man whose face was like &#91;SIM&#93; bronze. He was standing in the entrance of a building. He had a linen cord and a measuring stick in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:5; MATTHEW 10:27; EZEKIEL 2:7–8"> He said to me, “You human, look carefully at everything that I am going to show you, and pay attention to everything that I say and everything that I will show you, because that is why you have been brought here. And then later you must tell the Israeli people everything that you have seen here.”</span>
</div>

## The east entryway to the outer courtyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 42:20; Isaiah 26:1; REVELATION 21:12"> In the vision I saw that there was a wall that completely surrounded the temple area. The measuring stick in the man’s hand was ◄10-1/2 feet/3.3 meters► long. He measured the wall: It was ◄10-1/2 feet/3.3 meters► thick and ◄10-1/2 feet/3.3 meters► high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:1; EZEKIEL 8:16; EZEKIEL 11:1"> Then he went to the entryway on the east side of the temple. He climbed the steps and measured the outer threshold/opening for the entryway: It was ◄10-1/2 feet/3.3 meters► deep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 40:36; EZEKIEL 40:33; 1 CHRONICLES 23:28"> Next, there was an entry hallway with three ◄alcoves/very small rooms► along each side where the guards stood. Each of the alcoves was ◄10-1/2 feet/3.3 meters► on each side. There was a dividing wall between each alcove that was ◄8-3/4 feet/2.7 meters► wide. At the end of the entry hallway was the inner threshold/opening for the entryway, ◄10-1/2 feet/3.3 meters► deep. It led to the entry room to the inner courtyard and faced the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then he measured the entry room at the end of the entry hallway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:19"> It was about ◄14 feet/4.2 meters► long, and its supporting columns were ◄3-1/2 feet/1.1 meters► thick. The entry room of the entryway was at the end of the entry hallway that faced the temple and extended into the courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Inside the east entryway there were three alcoves on each side of the entry hallway. They each had the same length and width. And the measurements of the walls that divided the alcoves were all identical.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then he measured the threshold/opening for the entryway: It was ◄17-1/2 feet/5.3 meters► wide, and the entry hallway between the alcoves was ◄22-3/4 feet/6.9 meters► wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> In front of each alcove was a low wall about ◄21 inches/53 cm.► high, and the alcoves were ◄10-1/2 feet/3.3 meters► on each side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then he measured the width of the roof of the entrance hallway, the distance between the rear wall of one alcove to the rear wall of the opposite alcove, including the supporting walls along each side. It was ◄43-3/4 feet/13.3 meters►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 27:9; Isaiah 62:9; EZEKIEL 42:1"> Then he measured all along the dividing walls between the alcoves all along the inside of the entry hallway: It was ◄105 feet/31.8 meters►. He measured as far as the entry room of the entryway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The length from theouter opening of the entryway to the far end of the entry room was ◄87-1/2 feet/26.5 meters►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 3:5; 1 KINGS 6:4; EZEKIEL 41:26"> There were small windows in the outside walls of all the alcoves, and also in the inner dividing walls between the alcoves. There were also small windows in the entry room. The dividing walls were decorated with carvings of palm trees.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
