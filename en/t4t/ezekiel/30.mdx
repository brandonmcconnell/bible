## Yahweh will punish Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 13:6; EZEKIEL 21:12; JOEL 1:11"> “You human, prophesy about what will happen to Egypt. Say, ‘This is what Yahweh the Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Weep and wail &#91;DOU&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because terrible things will happen some day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:15; JOEL 2:1–2; JOEL 1:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">That day is near,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the day when I, Yahweh, will punish people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it will be a day of dark clouds and disaster for many nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:19; EZEKIEL 29:8; Isaiah 16:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">An enemy army &#91;PRS&#93; will come to attack Egypt with their swords,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there will be great distress among the people in Ethiopia.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Many people will be killed in Egypt;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">everything valuable will be taken away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and even the foundations of the buildings will be torn down.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:20; EZEKIEL 27:10; JEREMIAH 25:24"> Soldiers of Ethiopia, Libya, Lydia, and from all of Arabia, and all the other groups who are their allies, will be killed in the battles.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:10; Isaiah 20:3–6; Isaiah 31:3"> This is what I, Yahweh say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The allies of Egypt will be defeated,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the power that the people of Egypt are proud of will end.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">From Migdol city in the north to Aswan city in the south, the soldiers of Egypt will be killed by their enemies’ swords”;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that is what I, Yahweh the Lord, declare.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:12; JEREMIAH 25:18–26; EZEKIEL 32:18–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt will become the most deserted country in the world,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and its cities will be ruined,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">surrounded by ruined cities in nearby nations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:6; EZEKIEL 29:16; AMOS 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then, when I cause everything in Egypt to be burned in fires,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all their allies are defeated,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 18:1–2; Isaiah 23:5; EZEKIEL 32:9–10"> At that time, I will send messengers to go swiftly up the Nile River in boats to cause the people of Ethiopia who ◄are complacent/confident/think that nothing terrible will happen to them► to become afraid. They will be terrified &#91;PRS&#93; when Egypt is destroyed; and it will soon be that time!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:19; EZEKIEL 32:11–16; EZEKIEL 29:4–5"> This is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“By the power of &#91;MTY&#93; of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will get rid of very many people in Egypt.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:7; EZEKIEL 32:12; EZEKIEL 39:11–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nebuchadnezzar and his army, whose soldiers are extremely ruthless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will come to destroy Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will pull out their swords</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and fill Egypt with the corpses of those whom they have killed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:3; NAHUM 1:4; REVELATION 16:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause the Nile River to become dry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will sell the nation to evil men.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">By the power &#91;MTY&#93; of foreigners</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will ruin the land and everything that is in it.</span>That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have predicted it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:11; JEREMIAH 46:14; JEREMIAH 2:16"> This is also what I, Yahweh the Lord, say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will cause the idols and statues of idols &#91;DOU&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in Memphis city to be destroyed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No longer will there be a king in Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all over the land, people will be terrified.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cause the people of Egypt to be very afraid.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:14; JEREMIAH 46:25; PSALMS 78:43"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause the Pathros area in southern Egypt to be abandoned.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will start fires in Zoan city in northeast Egypt</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and punish the people in Thebes, the capital of southern Egypt.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="REVELATION 16:1; NAHUM 1:6; PSALMS 11:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will pour out my punishment &#91;MTY&#93; on the fortress in Pelusium at the eastern end of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will get rid of very many people in Thebes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:8–9; EZEKIEL 28:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will burn Egypt by fires;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the people in Pelusium will suffer severe pain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Enemies will conquer Thebes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the people in Memphis will constantly be terrified.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 41:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many young men in Heliopolis and Bubastis cities in northern Egypt will be killed by their enemies swords,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the others from those cities will be captured and taken to Babylon.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:3; EZEKIEL 29:15; Isaiah 10:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will be a dark day of destruction at Tahpenes city in northeast Egypt</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I cause the power &#91;MET&#93; of Egypt to end;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that country will no longer be proud of being strong.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will be as though a dark cloud will cover Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because the people of its villages will be captured and forced to go to Babylon.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:14; REVELATION 17:1; EZEKIEL 5:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">That is how I will punish Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and people will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The king of Egypt will be defeated by Nebuchadnezzar’s army

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:17; EZEKIEL 26:1; EZEKIEL 29:1"> Almost eleven years after we had been taken to Babylon, on the seventh day of the first month of that year, Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:13; JEREMIAH 46:11; PSALMS 37:17"> “You human, I have enabled the army of Nebuchadnezzar to defeat the army of the King of Egypt. It is as though I have broken one of the arms of the King of Egypt, and it has not been bandaged in order that it could be healed, and it has not been put in splints in order that after it heals the arm will be strong enough to hold a sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 37:17; JEREMIAH 37:7; 2 KINGS 24:7"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: ‘I am the enemy of the King of Egypt. I will completely destroy the power of Egypt; it is as though now I will break both of the king’s arms, the good/strong one and the broken one, and cause the sword to fall from his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:26; EZEKIEL 30:17–18; EZEKIEL 29:12–13"> I will cause the people of Egypt to be scattered among the nations &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:25; ZEPHANIAH 2:12; Isaiah 45:5"> It is as though I will strengthen the arms of the King of Babylon and put a sword in his hand, and as though I will break the arms of the king of Egypt, and he will groan in front of the King of Babylon like &#91;SIM&#93; a soldier who is wounded and about to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:21; EZEKIEL 32:15; EZEKIEL 29:16"> I will cause the power &#91;MTY&#93; of the King of Babylon to become greater, and the power of the king of Egypt will be useless &#91;MET&#93;. When that happens, when I give power to the army of the King of Babylon, they will use that power to attack Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DANIEL 11:42; EZEKIEL 29:12; EZEKIEL 30:23"> I will cause the people of Egypt to be scattered among the nations &#91;DOU&#93;. And when that happens, the people of Egypt will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do &#91;DOU&#93;.’ ”</span>
</div>
