## A prophecy about the destruction of Edom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:1; EZEKIEL 21:1; EZEKIEL 34:1"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 6:2; GENESIS 32:3; EZEKIEL 25:8"> “You human, turn toward Edom and prophesy what will happen to its people. Say that this is what Yahweh says to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:12; EZEKIEL 35:7; EZEKIEL 6:14"> ‘You who live near Seir Mountain in Edom, I am opposed to you, and I will use my power &#91;MTY&#93; to strike you and cause your country to become a wasteland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 35:9; MALACHI 1:3–4; JOEL 3:19"> I will cause your country to be ruined and desolate. When that happens, people will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 137:7; OBADIAH 1:10–16; AMOS 1:11"> You have always been enemies of the Israeli people. You rejoiced when they experienced a great disaster: their enemies attacked them with swords, when I punished them for the sins that they had committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 63:2–6; REVELATION 18:24; OBADIAH 1:15"> Therefore, I, Yahweh the Lord, declare that as surely as I am alive, I will allow your enemies to slaughter &#91;MTY&#93; you and continue to slaughter you. You were happy when &#91;LIT&#93; others were slaughtered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:11; EZEKIEL 35:3; JUDGES 5:6–7"> so I will cause Seir Mountain to be abandoned/deserted, and I will get rid of anyone who enters it or leaves it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:12; EZEKIEL 32:4–5; EZEKIEL 39:4–5"> I will cause your mountains to be filled with the corpses of those who have been killed. The corpses of those who have been killed by your enemies’ swords will lie on your hills and in your valleys and in all your ravines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:13; MALACHI 1:3–4; EZEKIEL 6:7"> I will cause your land to be deserted forever. No one will live in your towns again. When that happens, you will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:5; PSALMS 83:4–12; EZEKIEL 48:35"> You people said, “Israel and Judah will become ours. We will take over their territory!” You said that even though I, Yahweh, was still there and protecting them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 9:16; MATTHEW 7:2; PSALMS 73:17–18"> Therefore, I, Yahweh the Lord, declare that as surely as I am alive, I will punish &#91;MTY&#93; you for being angry with my people, envying them, and hating them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:2; PSALMS 83:12; EZEKIEL 6:7"> Then you will know that I, Yahweh, have heard all the disgusting things that you have said about the mountains in Israel. You said that they have been ruined/devastated, and that they have been given to you to occupy &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 11:36; Isaiah 36:20; JEREMIAH 29:23"> When you insulted me, I heard all that you said about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:48; Isaiah 14:7–8; Isaiah 65:13–15"> So this is what I, Yahweh the Lord, say: You people who live on Seir Mountain and in all the other places in Edom, when I cause your land to become desolate, everyone in the entire world will rejoice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 35:3–4; LAMENTATIONS 4:21; Isaiah 34:5–6"> You were happy when the land &#91;MTY&#93; of the Israeli people became desolate, so I will cause your land to become desolate. When that happens, people will know that I, Yahweh, have the power to do what I say that I will do.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
