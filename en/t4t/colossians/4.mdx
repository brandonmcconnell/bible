## Pray to God persistently. And pray that God will give me opportunities to declare the message about Christ.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:17–18; PHILIPPIANS 4:6; EPHESIANS 6:18"> Pray to God persistently, and as you pray, be alert and be thanking God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:19–20; ACTS 14:27; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:1–2"> Since I am in prison &#91;MTY&#93; because I declared the message about Christ that God has now revealed, pray also for me that God will give me opportunities &#91;MTY&#93; to speak, in order that I might declare it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 4:1–4; EPHESIANS 6:20; COLOSSIANS 4:6"> That is, pray for me that God will enable me to make his message known publicly. I need to do this because I know that I should declare it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Always speak wisely and graciously to those who do not believe in the Lord Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:15–17; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:12; JAMES 3:13"> Act wisely towards those who do not believe in the Lord Jesus &#91;MTY&#93;; use every opportunity to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:29; 1 PETER 3:15; ECCLESIASTES 10:12"> In particular, always speak graciously and in a pleasant way to those who do not believe in the Lord Jesus. As a result, you will come to know in what manner you should answer each one of them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Tychicus will tell you all that has been happening to me.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:21–23; ACTS 20:4; 2 TIMOTHY 4:12"> Tychicus will tell you all that has been happening to me. He is a fellow believer whom I/we (exc) love and who helps me faithfully and who serves the Lord Jesus together with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:22; COLOSSIANS 2:2; PHILIPPIANS 2:28"> The reason that I am sending Tychicus to you with this letter is that he might know how you are and in order that he might strengthen you spiritually. (OR, in order that you might know about us (exc) and in order that he might comfort you.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PHILEMON 1:10–19; COLOSSIANS 4:7"> I am sending him to you with Onesimus, who is a faithful fellow believer whom I/we (inc) love and who is your fellow townsman. They will tell you all about what has been happening here.</span>
</div>
</div>

## My fellow workers here greet you.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PHILEMON 1:24; 2 TIMOTHY 4:11; ACTS 19:29"> Aristarchus, who is in prison with me, and Mark, who is Barnabas’ cousin, ◄say that they are thinking fondly about you/send you their greetings►. I have instructed you about Mark, so if he comes to you, welcome him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ACTS 11:2; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:1; ACTS 10:45"> Jesus, who is also called Justus, also ◄says that he is thinking fondly about you/greets you/sends you his greetings►. These three men are the only Jewish believers who are working with me in order that people would submit to God as their king, and they have all comforted me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 15:30; COLOSSIANS 1:9; COLOSSIANS 1:7"> Epaphras, who is your fellow townsman and who serves Christ Jesus, ◄says that he is thinking fondly about you/greets you/sends you his greetings►. Epaphras prays earnestly for you very often, that you might be spiritually mature and that you might know fully all that God wills for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:1; ROMANS 10:2; REVELATION 1:11"> I can assure you that he prays this because he is deeply concerned for you, for those who live in Laodicea city, and for those who live in Hierapolis city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PHILEMON 1:24; 2 TIMOTHY 4:10–11"> Luke, the doctor, whom I/we (exc) love, and Demas ◄say that they are thinking fondly about you/send you their greetings►.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Obey these instructions.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 16:5; PHILEMON 1:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:9"> Tell the fellow believers who live in Laodicea city and Nympha<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="meetsin his house. [Nympha is a name for a woman.]">a</a></sup>and the congregation that meets in her house that we ◄are thinking fondly about them/send them our greetings►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:27; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:14"> After someone reads this letter publicly to you, cause someone to also read it to the congregation that is in Laodicea city. And as for the letter that I wrote to the congregation that is in Laodicea city, make sure that it is also read among you &#123;you should also read that letter&#125; publicly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PHILEMON 1:2; ACTS 1:17; EZEKIEL 44:23–24"> Say to Archippus, “Make sure that you complete the task that God appointed you to do, because you have a close relationship with the Lord Jesus.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## I, Paul, am penning this myself in order that you may know that this letter was sent by me. Remember to pray about the fact that I am in prison.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:3; 2 TIMOTHY 4:22; 1 TIMOTHY 6:21"> I, Paul, ◄am thinking fondly about you/send you my greetings►. Having taken the pen from my scribe, I am now writing this myself in order that you may know that I have truly sent this letter. Remember to pray about the fact that I am in prison &#91;MTY&#93;. I pray that our Lord Jesus Christ would continue to act graciously towards you all.</span>
</div>
</div>
