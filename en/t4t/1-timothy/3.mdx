<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="TITUS 1:7; ACTS 20:28; 1 PETER 5:2"> Anyone who aspires to be an elder in the congregation (OR, a bishop) desires a noble/honorable task.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TITUS 1:6–9; 2 TIMOTHY 2:24; ROMANS 12:13"> Since that is a task that others should respect, an overseer must live in such a way that no one can truly ◄say that he has done anything that is wrong/find fault with him►. Specifically, he must be faithful to his wife. He must think clearly about what he does. He must be able to control his behavior. He must be sensible. He must be dignified/respectable. He must welcome and care for guests. He must be able to teach God’s truth well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TITUS 1:7; EPHESIANS 5:18; TITUS 3:2"> He must not be a drunkard. He must not be ready/quick to fight those who make him angry. On the contrary, he must be gentle and he must not be quarrelsome. He must not be greedy for a lot of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TITUS 1:6; GENESIS 18:19; 1 TIMOTHY 3:12"> He must lead and care for his own household/family well. For example, he must be a man whom his children obey and completely respect,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 3:13; EPHESIANS 5:24; ACTS 20:28"> because if anyone does not know how he should lead well and care for well the people who live in his own house, ◄he certainly cannot care for God’s congregation!/how can he care for God’s congregation?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 6:4; HEBREWS 5:12–13; 1 PETER 2:2"> He must not be one who has just recently trusted in Jesus, because if you choose a man like that, he might become conceited/proud because you chose him so soon. As a result of his being conceited/proud, God will condemn him like he condemned the devil because he was conceited/proud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 8:21; 2 TIMOTHY 2:26; COLOSSIANS 4:5"> Moreover, an elder must live in such a way that non-Christians speak well of him, because if he conducts himself like that, people will not say evil things about him, and the devil will not capture/trap him &#91;MET&#93; like people capture animals in a trap.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:1; 1 TIMOTHY 5:23; ACTS 6:3–6"> Those whom you choose to serve as deacons likewise must be ones who conduct themselves suitably/appropriately. Specifically, they must be ◄serious/worthy that people respect them►. They must ◄be sincere/mean what they say►. They must not like to drink a lot of alcohol. They must not ◄be greedy/have a strong desire to get money►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 1:19; 2 JOHN 1:9–10; 1 TIMOTHY 1:5"> They must sincerely believe the message that God has now revealed to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:22; 1 TIMOTHY 3:13; TITUS 1:6–7"> Like you do for the elders, you must examine ◄their character/how they conduct their lives► before you appoint them to serve. Then if they are without fault, let them serve as deacons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TITUS 2:3; TITUS 3:2; 1 TIMOTHY 3:2"> Their wives (OR, The women who are deacons) likewise must conduct themselves suitably/appropriately. Specifically, they must be ◄serious/worthy that people respect them►. They must not speak evil about people. They must not drink a lot of alcohol. They must be faithful in everything that they do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 3:2; 1 TIMOTHY 3:4–5; PHILIPPIANS 1:1"> Deacons must be faithful to their wives, and they must lead well and care well for their children and other people in their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:21; HEBREWS 6:10; ACTS 21:35"> Because if deacons serve well, people will respect them, and they will be able to speak very boldly/confidently about what they believe concerning Christ Jesus.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 11:34; PHILEMON 1:22; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:15–17"> Although I hope that I will come and visit you soon, I am writing these things to you now</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 PETER 2:5; EPHESIANS 2:21–22; 1 THESSALONIANS 1:9"> in order that if I ◄am delayed/things prevent me from visiting to you soon►, you will know how believers should conduct their lives and you will be able to teach that to them. I am talking about all those who are members of God’s family, all the congregations that belong to the all-powerful God, all those who uphold/support &#91;MET&#93; the true message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 1:14; HEBREWS 1:3; MARK 16:19"> It is certainly true that the message that God has now revealed is very wonderful! From it, we learn this spiritual truth that we say/sing about Christ (OR, We affirm this teaching to be true and very important.) It is what shows us how to live ◄in a godly manner/in a manner that pleases God►. It is what God has now revealed, even though it was not known before. We ◄affirm/say that it is true► concerning Jesus Christ that,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is the one who appeared on the earth in a human body.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God’s Spirit showed/demonstrated that he is/was truly the Messiah (OR, that he always acted righteously);</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Angels saw him; people preached about him in many nations;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">People in many parts of the world believed the message about him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">God took him up into heaven.</span></span>
