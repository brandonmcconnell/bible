<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 12:7; LEVITICUS 19:36; PROVERBS 16:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do you think that &#91;RHQ&#93; I should say nothing about people who use scales that do not weigh correctly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and who use weights that are not accurate?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; JEREMIAH 9:8; HOSEA 7:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The rich people among you always act violently to get money from poor people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All of the people in Jerusalem are liars,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they &#91;SYN&#93; always deceive people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ACTS 12:23; DEUTERONOMY 28:21–22; HOSEA 13:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, I have already begun to get rid of you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to ruin you because of the sins that you have committed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HOSEA 4:10; LEVITICUS 26:26; Isaiah 65:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Soon you will eat food, but you will not have enough to satisfy you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your stomachs will still feel as though they are empty.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will try to save up money,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you will not be able to save anything,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because I will send your enemies to take it from you in wars &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:13; AMOS 5:11; JEREMIAH 12:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will plant seeds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you will not harvest anything.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will press olives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but others, not you, will use the olive oil.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will trample on grapes and make wine from the juice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but others, not you, will drink the wine.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:51; 1 KINGS 16:25–33; JEREMIAH 7:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those things will happen to you because you obey only the wicked laws of King Omri,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you do the terrible things that wicked King Ahab and his descendants commanded.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So, I will destroy your country,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will cause your people to be despised;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people of other nations will insult you.’”</span></span>
