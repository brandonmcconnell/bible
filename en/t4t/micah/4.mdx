<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

## The Israeli people will return from exile

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 7:14; Isaiah 24:23; MICAH 2:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">My people who did not die while they were exiled will become a strong nation again.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then I, Yahweh, will be their king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will rule from Jerusalem forever.</span></span>
