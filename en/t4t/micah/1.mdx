## Yahweh will punish Samaria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; PSALMS 11:4; REVELATION 3:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">You people everywhere on the earth, pay attention to this &#91;DOU&#93;!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh our God is accusing you from his holy temple in heaven.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 4:13; Isaiah 26:21; DEUTERONOMY 32:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will come down from heaven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and walk on the tops of the highest mountains.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NAHUM 1:5; PSALMS 97:5; JUDGES 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will be as though the mountains will melt under his feet</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; wax melts in front of a fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and like &#91;SIM&#93; water disappears</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when it rushes/flows down into a valley.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="AMOS 8:14; JEREMIAH 2:19; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those things will happen because of the terrible sins &#91;DOU&#93; that the people &#91;MTY&#93; of Israel, the descendants of Jacob, have committed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But it was &#91;RHQ&#93; the people of Samaria city who persuaded all the people of Israel to sin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it was &#91;RHQ&#93; because the people of Jerusalem set up altars to worship their gods</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that the other people of Judah were persuaded to worship idols on their hilltops.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:14; MICAH 3:12; LAMENTATIONS 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">So Yahweh will cause Samaria to become a heap of rubble/ruins;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it will be only a field for planting vineyards.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will cause the stones of its buildings to roll down into the valley,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the foundations of the buildings will be uncovered.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:18; HOSEA 2:12; HOSEA 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the idols in Samaria will be smashed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the gifts given to prostitutes at the temples of their idols will be destroyed in a fire.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And because people paid prostitutes there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their enemies will take away those idols and sell them to get money to pay to prostitutes in other countries.</span></span>

## Micah mourned for Judah also

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Isaiah 20:2–4; JEREMIAH 4:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because Samaria will be destroyed, I will weep and wail.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will walk around ◄barefoot/without any sandals on my feet► and naked.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will howl like a jackal/wolf</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and screech like an owl,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:11–15; Isaiah 8:7–8; 2 CHRONICLES 32:1–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">because Samaria will be completely destroyed &#91;MET&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">nothing can save that city.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the same thing will happen to Judah!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It is as though the enemy army has already reached the city gates of Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the main city where my people live.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 1:20; JEREMIAH 6:26; JOSHUA 18:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not tell that to our enemies in Gath city in Philistia!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do not cry, lest the people there find out what is happening!!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Instead, just roll in the dirt in Beth-Leaphrah because the name of that town means ‘house of dust.’</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:17; 2 CHRONICLES 32:9; JOSHUA 10:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You people of Lachish city, whose name sounds like ‘team,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">hitch your horses to pull the chariots in which you can ride to flee from your enemies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Israeli people rebelled against Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you ◄imitated them/did the same evil things that they did►,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and that caused the people of Jerusalem &#91;IDM&#93; to start sinning, too.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:44; 2 KINGS 16:8; 2 SAMUEL 8:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You people of Judah, send a farewell gift to the people of Moresheth town,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because their enemies will soon destroy it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The name of the town Aczib means ‘deception,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the kings of Israel will soon find out that the people of that town will deceive them.</span></span>
