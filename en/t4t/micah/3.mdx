## Micah rebuked the Israeli leaders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 1:10; JEREMIAH 5:4–5; PSALMS 82:1–5"> Then I said, “You Israeli &#91;MTY&#93; leaders, listen to what I say!<br /><span className="pl pl-1">You should certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; know what things are right to do</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and what things are wrong,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:27; PROVERBS 28:4; PSALMS 53:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">but you hate what is good</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you love what is evil.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You act like butchers:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is as though you strip the skin off my people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and tear the flesh from their bones.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 14:4; EZEKIEL 11:6–7; EZEKIEL 11:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though you chop them into pieces like &#91;SIM&#93; meat to be cooked in a pot.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:28; Isaiah 1:15; EZEKIEL 8:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then, when you have troubles, you plead to Yahweh to help you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he will not answer you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">At that time, he will turn away from you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the evil things that you have done.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 8:20–22; AMOS 8:9–10; Isaiah 29:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">So now it is as though &#91;MET&#93; night will descend on you prophets;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will not receive any more visions.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is as though &#91;MET&#93; the sun will set for you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the time when you are greatly honored will end.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:4; Isaiah 44:25; 1 SAMUEL 28:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then you ◄seers/people who predict what will happen in the future► &#91;DOU&#93; will be disgraced;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will cover your faces because you will be ashamed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because when you ask me what will happen, there will be no answer from me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:12–13; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me, I am full of God’s power,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">power from the Spirit of Yahweh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am courageous and strong</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to declare to the Israeli people &#91;MTY, DOU&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that they have sinned and rebelled against Yahweh.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:23; PSALMS 58:1–2; PROVERBS 17:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">You leaders of the people &#91;MTY&#93; of Israel, listen to this!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You hate it when people do what is just;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and when people say what is true,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you distort it, saying it is false.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:9–12; JEREMIAH 22:13–17; MATTHEW 27:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though you are building houses in Jerusalem on foundations</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that consist of murdering people and doing what is corrupt.</span></span>
