## As Christ suffered, he learned to be a compassionate Supreme Priest by obeying God.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:3; HEBREWS 10:11; HEBREWS 7:27"> Every Jewish Supreme Priest was chosen by God &#123;God chose every Jewish Supreme Priest&#125; from among ordinary men. They were appointed &#123;He appointed them&#125; in order that they would come before him on behalf of the people. Specifically, God appointed them in order that they would bring gifts to him on behalf of the people, and in order to sacrifice animals to him for people who sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:15; HEBREWS 2:18; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:9–10"> The Supreme Priests could deal gently with those who ignorantly sinned, since the Supreme Priests themselves tended to sin easily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:7; HEBREWS 9:7; LEVITICUS 16:6"> As a result, they had to offer something to God for their own sins, just like they had to offer something to God for other people who sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 28:1; NUMBERS 16:40; NUMBERS 16:5"> Furthermore, it is an honor to be a Supreme Priest so no one honors himself by appointing himself to become a Supreme Priest. Instead, God chose each man to become a Supreme Priest, as he chose Aaron to be the first Supreme Priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 2:7; JOHN 8:54; ACTS 13:33"> Similarly, Christ also did not honor himself by appointing himself to become a Supreme Priest. Instead, God appointed him by saying to him what he never said to any other priest, what the Psalmist wrote in the Scriptures,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">You (sg) are my Son! Today I have declared that I am your Father!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 110:4; HEBREWS 7:17; HEBREWS 7:21"> And he also said to Christ what the Psalmist wrote in another Scripture passage,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">You are a priest eternally just like Melchizedek was a priest.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 14:32–39; LUKE 22:41–44; JOHN 17:1"> When Christ lived on the earth &#91;MTY&#93;, he prayed &#91;DOU&#93; to God and tearfully cried out loudly to him. Specifically, he asked God, who was able to help him, that he would not fear the sufferings just before he died. As a result, God listened to him, because Christ reverently submitted to what God wanted him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:8; HEBREWS 10:5–9; JOHN 4:34"> Although Christ is God’s own Son, he learned to obey God by suffering before he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:10; HEBREWS 9:12; HEBREWS 9:15"> By becoming ◄all that God intended him to be/perfect►, he has now become fully qualified to be our Supreme Priest. As a result, he is the one who saves eternally all who obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:5–6; HEBREWS 6:20"> Furthermore, God has designated him to be our Supreme Priest in the way that Melchizedek was a Supreme Priest.</span>
</div>
</div>

## You are not ready for more advanced spiritual truth.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:15; ACTS 28:27; 2 PETER 3:16"> Although there is much to say to you(pl) about how Christ resembles Melchizedek, this is hard for me to explain to you because you now understand things so slowly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:1; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:1–3; 1 PETER 2:2"> You became Christians long ago. So by now you should be teaching spiritual truths to others. But you still need someone to teach you again the truths that God has revealed. I am talking about the truths that we teach people when they first believe in Christ. You need those elementary truths like babies need milk &#91;MET&#93;. You are not ready for advanced teaching, which is like the solid food which mature people need &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 14:20; 1 PETER 2:2; 2 TIMOTHY 3:16"> Remember that those who are still learning these elementary truths &#91;MET&#93; have not become familiar with what God says concerning becoming/being righteous. They are just like &#91;MET&#93; babies who need milk!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 2:14–15; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:21; EPHESIANS 1:18"> But the more advanced spiritual truth is for people who are spiritually mature, just like &#91;MET&#93; solid food is for people who are physically mature. They can tell the difference between what is good and what is evil, because they have trained themselves to keep doing that.</span>
</div>
</div>
