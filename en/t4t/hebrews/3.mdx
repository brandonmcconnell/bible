## Our Supreme Priest, Jesus, deserves more honor than Moses does.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 1:9; PHILIPPIANS 3:14; HEBREWS 4:14–15"> My fellow believers, God has set you apart and has chosen you, just like he chose me. So consider Jesus. He is God’s messenger to us. He is also the Supreme Priest whom we say we believe in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 12:7; 1 TIMOTHY 3:15; EPHESIANS 2:22"> He faithfully served God, who appointed him, just like Moses faithfully served God’s people &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 3:9; 1 PETER 2:5–7; ZECHARIAH 6:12–13"> Just like every house is made by someone &#123;as someone makes every house&#125;, Jesus made everything, and he is God/Divine. So God has considered that Jesus is worthy that people honor him more than they honor Moses, just like the one who builds a house deserves that people honor him more than they should honor the house he built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 12:7; HEBREWS 3:2; EXODUS 14:31"> Moses very faithfully served God as he ◄helped/cared for► &#91;MET&#93; God’s people, just like a servant faithfully serves his master. The result was that Moses testified about what Jesus would say later.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:16; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:16; HEBREWS 10:35"> But Christ faithfully serves God as he ◄helps/cares for► his own people &#91;MTY, MET&#93;, just like a son helps/cares for his own family. And we are God’s people &#91;MTY&#93; if we continue to confidently believe in Christ and if we continue to confidently wait for what God will do for us.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do not stubbornly rebel against God as their Jewish ancestors did.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 95:7–11; HEBREWS 3:15; Isaiah 55:3"> The Holy Spirit caused the Psalmist to write these words in the Scriptures to the Israelites:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now, when you (pl) hear God speaking to you &#91;MTY&#93;,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:14; EXODUS 17:7; ZECHARIAH 7:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">do not stubbornly ◄disobey/refuse to obey him► &#91;IDM&#93;, as your Jewish ancestors stubbornly disobeyed him when they rebelled against him in the desert.</span><br />At that time, God said to your ancestors, “They tried to determine how many things that displeased me they could do in the desert without me punishing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ACTS 7:36; DEUTERONOMY 4:9; EXODUS 19:4"> Your ancestors repeatedly tested whether I would be patient with them, even though for 40 years they saw all the amazing things I did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 95:10; 2 THESSALONIANS 2:10–12; PSALMS 67:2"> So, I became disgusted with those people who saw those things, and I said about them, ‘They are constantly disloyal to me, and they do not understand how I wanted them to conduct their lives.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:34–35; HEBREWS 4:3; NUMBERS 32:10–13"> As a result, because I was angry with them, I solemnly declared, ‘They will not enter the land of Canaan where I would let them rest &#91;MTY&#93;!’ ”</span>

## Beware that you do not stop trusting in Christ.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:5; JEREMIAH 7:24; JEREMIAH 17:9"> So, my fellow believers, be careful that none of you is so evil that you stop trusting in Christ. That would cause you to reject God who is all-powerful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:11; HEBREWS 10:24–25; 2 TIMOTHY 4:2"> Instead, each of you must encourage each other every day, while you still have the opportunity &#91;IDM&#93;, in order that none of you may stubbornly reject God by letting others deceive you (OR, as you deceive yourselves), with the result that you (sg) sin &#91;PRS&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:6; HEBREWS 6:11; HEBREWS 12:10"> We must encourage one another, because we (inc) benefit from all Christ has done only if we firmly keep trusting in him from the time when we first confidently trusted in him until the time when we die &#91;EUP&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:7–8; PSALMS 95:7; HEBREWS 10:38"> We can do this by paying attention to what the Psalmist wrote in that Scripture passage in which God said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now, when you hear me speaking to you(pl) &#91;MTY&#93;, do not stubbornly disobey me as your ancestors stubbornly disobeyed me when they rebelled against me.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## It was your ancestors, ones for whom God did great things, who rebelled against God.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:2; NUMBERS 14:30; NUMBERS 26:65"> ◄You must keep trusting in God because you must remember who it was who rebelled against God although they heard him speaking to them./Do you remember who it was who rebelled against God although they heard him speaking to them?► &#91;RHQ&#93; It was people who had certainly experienced &#91;LIT&#93; God’s power. It was all those people whom Moses led miraculously out of Egypt. &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:29; NUMBERS 26:64–65; NUMBERS 14:32–33"> And ◄you must remember who it was that God was disgusted with for 40 years./do you remember who it was that God was disgusted with for 40years?► &#91;RHQ&#93; It was those same people who had sinned like that, and who as a result died in the desert! &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:30; HEBREWS 4:6; DEUTERONOMY 1:34–35"> And ◄you must remember about whom God solemnly declared, “They will not enter the land where I would let them rest.”/do you remember about whom God solemnly declared, “They will not enter the land where I would let them rest”?► &#91;RHQ&#93; It was those Israelites who disobeyed God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JUDE 1:5; JOHN 3:36; MARK 16:16"> So, from that example we (inc) realize that it was because they did not keep trusting in God that they were unable to enter the land where they would rest.</span>
</div>
