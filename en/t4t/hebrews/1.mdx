## Hebrews 1:1-3


## God formerly communicated with us through the prophets, but now he has communicated with us through his son, who is just like God.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 PETER 1:20–21; JOEL 2:28; NUMBERS 12:6–8"> Long ago God communicated frequently to our ancestors in various ways by what the prophets said and wrote.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 1:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:6; COLOSSIANS 1:16–18"> But now when this final age is beginning, God has communicated to us just once by means of what ◄his Son/the man who was also God► said and did. God appointed him in order that he would possess everything that truly belongs to God. God also appointed him in order that he would create the universe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:15–17; REVELATION 4:11; JOHN 14:9–10"> He manifests God’s glory. He exactly represents what God is like. He ◄sustains everything by means of his powerful words. When he had enabled people to be freed from the guilt of their sins &#91;MET&#93;, he sat down in heaven &#91;EUP&#93; to rule at the place of greatest honor &#91;MTY&#93; with God &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s son is greater than the angels.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:21; PHILIPPIANS 2:9–11; 1 PETER 3:22"> By doing that, he showed that he was very much greater than the angels, to the extent that his relationship &#91;MTY&#93; to God, as his Son, is more excellent than the relationship the angels have to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 2:7; ACTS 13:33; HEBREWS 5:5"> We know that because in the Scriptures no one &#91;RHQ&#93; ever reported that God said to any angel what he said to his Son,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">You (sg) are my Son!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Today I have declared to all that I am your Father &#91;DOU&#93;!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And he said in another Scripture passage,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will be his Father,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he will be my Son &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 97:7; COLOSSIANS 1:18; DEUTERONOMY 32:43"> And we know his Son is greater than the angels because in another Scripture passage someone wrote this about God’s esteemed Son, when God was about to send him into the world:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">All God’s angels must worship him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 104:4; HEBREWS 1:14; 2 KINGS 6:17"> And in the Scriptures it is written that someone said this about the angels:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">God makes the angels who serve him to be changeable like &#91;MET&#93; winds and flames of fire.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; JEREMIAH 23:5–6"> But on the other hand, in the Scriptures it is written that God said this to his Son:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">You (sg) who are also God will rule forever &#91;MTY&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you will reign righteously over your kingdom &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 45:7; PSALMS 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have loved people’s righteous deeds and you have hated people’s lawless deeds.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So I, your God, have caused you to be more joyful &#91;MTY&#93; than anyone else.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 102:25–27; GENESIS 1:1; JOHN 1:1–3"> And we also know that his Son is superior to angels because in the Scriptures the Psalmist wrote that someone said to God’s Son,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lord, it was you who created the earth in the beginning.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You also made the rest of the universe (OR, the things in the sky) &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; MARK 13:31; LUKE 21:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everything in the universe will disappear, but you will keep on living forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will wear out as clothing wears out.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 102:26–27; HEBREWS 13:8; JOHN 8:58"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will dispose of them as one rolls up an old coat before getting rid of it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then, you will exchange everything that is in the universe for what is new,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as someone puts on a new garment in exchange for an old garment &#91;SIM&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you are not like what you created;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You stay the same, and you live forever &#91;LIT&#93;!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 110:1; MARK 12:36; MATTHEW 22:44"> We also know that his Son is superior to angels because no one ever stated &#91;RHQ&#93; in the Scriptures that God said to any angel what he said to his Son,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Sit in the place of honor next to me and rule with me &#91;MTY&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">while I put all of your enemies completely under your control &#91;MET&#93;!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 34:7; PSALMS 103:20–21; PSALMS 91:11–12"> The angels are only spirits who serve God &#91;RHQ&#93;. God sends them to earth in order to help those he has saved (OR, those whom he will save).</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
