## The rituals that were performed by the Jewish high priests were only a model of what Jesus our high priest does in heaven.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:3; COLOSSIANS 3:1; REVELATION 3:21"> The main point of all that I have written is that we have a Supreme Priest like that. He has sat down to rule &#91;MTY&#93; with God &#91;EUP&#93; at the place of greatest honor in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 33:7; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:1; HEBREWS 9:23–24"> He ministers in the Most Holy Place, that is, in the true place of worship in heaven. That is a place that Moses’ tent represented. The Lord set up the true place of worship. No human set it up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:2; HEBREWS 5:1; HEBREWS 9:14"> Every Supreme Priest was appointed &#123;God appointed every Supreme Priest&#125; to offer to God gifts and sacrifices for people who sinned. So, since Christ became a Supreme Priest, he also had to offer something.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:11–15; HEBREWS 5:1; NUMBERS 17:12–13"> Since there are already Jewish priests who offer gifts as God’s laws require, if Christ were now living on the earth, he would not be a Supreme Priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 25:40; COLOSSIANS 2:17; HEBREWS 10:1"> The Jewish priests perform rituals which are only a model &#91;DOU&#93; of what Christ would do in heaven &#91;MTY&#93;. What God told Moses when Moses was about to set up the tent for worshipping him supports the idea that those rituals were only a model. God said, “Be sure that you (sg) make everything according to the model that I showed you on Sinai Mountain!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:22; GALATIANS 3:16–21; HEBREWS 12:24"> But now as it is, Christ ministers in a more excellent way than the Jewish priests do. Likewise, the new covenant that he established between God and people is better than the old one. When the new covenant was established &#123;God established the new covenant&#125;, he promised us better things than the laws that God gave Moses did.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The first covenant was not adequate, so God needed a new one.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:11; GALATIANS 3:21; HEBREWS 7:18"> If that first covenant had been perfectly adequate &#91;LIT&#93;, God would not have thought that he needed another covenant/agreement to replace it. But it was not adequate, so he needed a new one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:31–34; HEBREWS 10:16–17; HEBREWS 12:24"> Because God declared that the Israelites were guilty of not obeying the first covenant, he wanted a new covenant. This is what ◄a prophet/Jeremiah► wrote about that:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Lord says, “Listen! There will soon be a time</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I will make a new covenant with the people &#91;MTY&#93; of Israel and the people &#91;MTY&#93; of Judah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:32; GALATIANS 4:24; MARK 8:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">That covenant will not be like the covenant that I made with their ancestors</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I led them out of Egypt &#91;MET&#93; like a father leads a child by the hand.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They did not continue to obey my covenant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so I rejected them,” says the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:26–28; JEREMIAH 31:33; EZEKIEL 11:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“This is the covenant that I will make with the Israelites &#91;MTY&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">after the first covenant has ended &#91;MTY&#93;,” says the Lord:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will enable them to understand my laws &#91;MTY&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will enable them to obey them (OR, truly know them) &#91;MTY&#93; sincerely.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will be their God, and they will be my people &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:34; Isaiah 54:13; JOHN 6:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a result, no one will need to teach a fellow citizen</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or tell his fellow kinsmen, ‘You need to know the Lord,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because all my people will know me:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My people of every status will know me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:16–17; Isaiah 43:25; ROMANS 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will mercifully forgive them for the wicked things they have done.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will no longer consider that they are guilty for &#91;DOU&#93; their sins.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:17; HEBREWS 7:18–19; HEBREWS 7:11–12"> Since God spoke about a new covenant, he considered that the first covenant was no longer in use, and that it would soon disappear, just like anything that gets old will disappear &#91;MET, DOU&#93;.</span>
</div>
</div>
