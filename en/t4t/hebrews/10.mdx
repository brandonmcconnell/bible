## The blood of animals can never take away the guilt for sins.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:5; HEBREWS 9:11; COLOSSIANS 2:17"> Just like a shadow vaguely represents the thing that it is a shadow of, the laws that God gave Moses only poorly represent &#91;MET&#93; the good things that were to come later. Those laws were not all the good things themselves that God has promised. So, by offering the same kinds of sacrifices every year, people &#91;PRS&#93; who approach God can never become ◄perfect/all that God intends them to be►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; MICAH 7:19; PSALMS 103:12"> If God had removed the guilt for having sinned of those who brought the sacrifices, they would not feel that they were still guilty. So they would certainly have stopped offering those sacrifices! &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:7; EXODUS 30:10; LEVITICUS 23:27–28"> But rather, the fact that they offer those sacrifices each year reminds them that they are still guilty for their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:11; Isaiah 1:11–15; MICAH 6:6–8"> The principle is that blood of animals such as bulls or goats can never remove the guilt of those who have sinned.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Christ set us apart for God by offering his own body once as a sacrifice.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 40:6–8; Isaiah 1:11; Isaiah 7:14"> So, as Christ was coming into the world, he spoke to his Father about offering himself as a sacrifice for people’s sin. The Psalmist wrote this that Christ said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is not sacrifices and offerings that you (sg) have wanted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you have prepared for me a body to serve you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MALACHI 1:10; MATTHEW 3:17; PSALMS 147:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Animals that are completely burned up as sacrifices have not pleased you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and other sacrifices that atone for those who have sinned have not pleased you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 40:7–8; JOHN 4:34; JOHN 6:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then because of this, I said, “My God, ◄listen!/here I am!►</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have come here in order to do what you want me to do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">just like has been written &#123;as they have written&#125; about me in the Scriptures.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:5; MARK 12:33"> First, Christ said, “It is not sacrifices and offerings and animals that the priests have completely burned up and other offerings to atone for those who have sinned that you have really wanted. They have not pleased you.” Christ said that even though those things were offered &#123;they offered all those things&#125; according to the laws God gave Moses!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:18–19; HEBREWS 12:27–28; HEBREWS 10:7"> Then, concerning his offering himself as a sacrifice to atone for people’s sin, he said, “Listen! I have come here to do what you want me to do!” In that way Christ got rid of the first way of atoning for sin, in order to establish the second way of atoning for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:14; HEBREWS 10:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 6:11"> Because of Jesus Christ doing what God wanted him to do, we have been dedicated to &#123;he has set us apart for&#125; God by his offering his own body only once as a sacrifice, a sacrifice that will not need to be repeated.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Scripture supports the claim that Christ made one sacrifice that will be adequate forever.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:1; NUMBERS 28:3; HEBREWS 10:4"> As every Jewish priest stands daily in front of the altar, he performs rituals and offers the same kind of sacrifices that could never remove the guilt from anyone who sinned &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:3; COLOSSIANS 3:1; ROMANS 8:34"> But Christ offered a sacrifice that will be adequate forever, and he offered it only one time! Then he sat down to rule with God at the place of highest honor &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:13; PSALMS 110:1; LUKE 20:43"> From now on, he is waiting for God to completely subdue all Christ’s enemies &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:25; HEBREWS 10:1; HEBREWS 9:14"> By offering himself once, he has provided that those whom God has set apart will be eternally made ◄perfect/all that God intends them to be►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:7; REVELATION 2:11; REVELATION 3:6"> The Holy Spirit also confirms to us that this is true. First the Lord says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:33–34; HEBREWS 8:8–12; ROMANS 11:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the time &#91;MTY&#93; when the first covenant that God made with my people has ended,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will make a new covenant with them &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will do like this for them:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cause them to understand my laws</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will cause them to obey them (OR, enable them to know them sincerely).</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:34; HEBREWS 8:12"> Then the Lord said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will forgive them for their &#91;DOU&#93; sins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will consider that they are no longer guilty for having sinned.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:2; HEBREWS 10:14"> When God has forgiven someone’s sins, that person does not need to make any more offerings to atone for his sin!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Let us come to God and let him make us pure and help us to keep believing his truth and do things that please him.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:18; HEBREWS 9:12; HEBREWS 4:16"> So, my fellow believers, because we trust in what Jesus accomplished when his own blood flowed for us, we can confidently go into God’s very presence that was symbolized by the very holy place in the tent &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 14:6; JOHN 10:9; HEBREWS 9:3"> Jesus enabled us to go into God’s presence by making a new and effective way. Specifically, he offered his body as a sacrifice for us in order that nothing would stop us from entering God’s presence, just like &#91;MET&#93; the curtain of the very holy place prevented people from entering God’s presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:17; HEBREWS 4:14–16; HEBREWS 8:1"> Christ is a great priest who rules over us, who are God’s people &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:25; JOHN 3:5; EPHESIANS 5:26"> Just like the priests were sprinkled &#123;as Moses sprinkled the priests&#125; with blood &#91;MET&#93; to symbolize that they were no longer guilty for having sinned, we also no longer are guilty for having done evil. Just like the priests ceremonially washed their bodies with pure water to prepare themselves to serve God, we are allowing God to continually make us pure. So, we (inc) must approach God sincerely by confidently trusting in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:24; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:9; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:3"> We must unwaveringly keep holding tight to what we believe. Since God faithfully does all he promised to do, we must confidently expect him to keep doing that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:11; 1 JOHN 3:18; HEBREWS 13:1"> Since God faithfully does all that he promised to do, let us consider how each of us can motivate other believers, in order that believers will love each other, and in order that each one will do good things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ACTS 2:42; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:11; MATTHEW 18:20"> We must not stop assembling ourselves to worship the Lord, as some people have done. Instead, each one of us must encourage/exhort other believers. Let us do that even more since we know that the day that the Lord will return &#91;MTY&#93; is near.</span>
</div>
</div>

## If we have known and then rejected the message about Christ, God will surely punish us.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 PETER 2:20–22; HEBREWS 6:4–6; JOHN 9:41"> We(inc) must do those things, because if we deliberately and habitually sin after we have known the true message about Christ, no other sacrifice will remove our guilt for having sinned in that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 26:11; HEBREWS 2:3; ZEPHANIAH 1:18"> Instead, we must fearfully expect that God will judge and angrily punish his enemies in a furious fire &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:2; DEUTERONOMY 19:15; DEUTERONOMY 17:2–13"> Everyone who rejected the laws that God gave Moses was mercilessly killed when at least two or three people testified that they had done that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:6; EPHESIANS 4:30; Isaiah 63:10"> That was severe punishment. But Christ is ◄God’s Son/the man who is also God►. His blood, by means of which he put into effect the new covenant, is sacred. Because of Christ’s &#91;MTY&#93; blood flowing for us when he died, God freed us from our guilt. So, you can be sure &#91;RHQ&#93; that anyone who shows contempt for those truths and who insults the Spirit of God, the one who acts with kindness toward us in a way we do not deserve, deserves to be punished even worse than those Israelis were punished &#91;MET&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:35–36; ROMANS 12:19; NAHUM 1:2"> We can be sure of that (OR, need to think about that carefully) since we know that God said, “I myself will get revenge on those who sinned, and I will punish them as they deserve &#91;DOU&#93;.” Moses wrote, “The Lord will judge his people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:28; LUKE 12:5; HEBREWS 12:29"> It will be a terrible thing if God who is all-powerful &#91;MTY&#93; seizes and punishes you &#91;IDM&#93;!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Remember how you accepted persecution because of your faith in Christ, and do not be discouraged if you are persecuted now.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:4; HEBREWS 12:4; 2 TIMOTHY 2:3–13"> Recall previous times when you first understood &#91;MET&#93; the message about Christ. You endured a hard struggle, but you continued to trust him when you suffered because you believed in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 4:9; PHILIPPIANS 4:14; HEBREWS 11:36"> At times you were publicly insulted &#123;people publicly insulted you&#125; and you suffered &#123;people persecuted you&#125;. But you showed great concern for those who were treated like that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 PETER 1:4; 2 TIMOTHY 1:16; MATTHEW 5:11–12"> You not only were kind to those who were in prison because they believed in Christ, but you also accepted it joyfully when unbelievers took away your possessions. You accepted it because you yourselves knew very well that you have eternal possessions in heaven that are much better than those that they took from you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:58; GALATIANS 6:8–10; HEBREWS 3:6"> So, do not become discouraged when they cause you to suffer &#123;you are persecuted&#125;, because if you continue to trust in God, he will greatly reward you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:1; PSALMS 37:7; JAMES 5:7–11"> You must patiently continue to trust in him in order that, because of your doing what God wants you to do, he will give you what he has promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JAMES 5:7–9; HABAKKUK 2:3–4; LUKE 18:8"> You must do that since a prophet wrote in the Scriptures that God said about the Messiah,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">In just a short time the one I promised would come will surely come;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he will not delay coming.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:11; HABAKKUK 2:4; ROMANS 1:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But those whom I have summoned, who act righteously, must continually live trusting in me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because if they, in a cowardly manner, cease to trust in me, I</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">◄will not be pleased/will be angry► with them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 JOHN 5:5; 1 PETER 1:5; MARK 16:16"> But we are not ones who in a cowardly manner stop trusting in God, with the result that God will severely punish us. Instead, we are ones who trust in him, with the result that God will save us &#91;SYN&#93; eternally.</span>
</div>
