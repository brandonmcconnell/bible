## Endure with me what we suffer for Christ Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:2–3; JAMES 1:12; 2 TIMOTHY 4:5"> Endure as I do what we suffer for Christ Jesus, like a good soldier endures what he suffers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:4; LUKE 8:14; 2 PETER 2:20"> You know that soldiers, in order to please their captain, do not ◄become involved in civilian affairs/spend time doing other kinds of work► &#91;MET&#93;. So, like soldiers, do not let other matters ◄distract you as you serve/hinder you from serving► Christ Jesus!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 9:24–27; REVELATION 3:11; 2 TIMOTHY 4:7–8"> Similarly, you know that athletes who do not obey the rules of the contest will not be given &#123;not win&#125; the prize &#91;MET&#93;. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:36; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:7–11; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:6–9"> You also know that a hard-working farmer should be the first to receive some of the harvest &#91;MET&#93;. So, like farmers, work hard for Christ Jesus and expect that God will reward you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JAMES 1:5; 1 JOHN 5:20; LUKE 24:45"> Think about what I have just written, because, if you do, the Lord will enable you to understand everything that you need to understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ACTS 2:24; ROMANS 2:16; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:11–20"> Endure everything by remembering how Jesus Christ endured it when he suffered. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:7; ACTS 28:31; 2 TIMOTHY 1:8"> I am suffering here in prison because some people oppose the good message that I tell. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 PETER 5:10; COLOSSIANS 1:24; 2 TIMOTHY 2:3"> Therefore I willingly endure all that I am suffering for the sake of those whom God has chosen. <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 6:8; COLOSSIANS 3:3–4; ROMANS 6:5"> Endure everything as you remember that these words that we all say/sing (OR, that we teach people) are trustworthy:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Since we have stopped behaving as we did in the past, as though we died when he died,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we shall also live with him.</span><br /></span>

## Remind teachers about the things that I have told you and command them not to quarrel; and be sure that you yourself are the kind of person that God will approve of.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TITUS 3:9–11; 2 TIMOTHY 2:23; 2 TIMOTHY 4:1"> Those whom you appointed to teach others God’s truth, keep reminding them about these things that I have told you. Tell them strongly that, since God knows what they are doing (OR, that God will judge them for everything that is wrong that they do, and therefore) they must not quarrel ◄about words/about matters that are not important►, because, when teachers quarrel, it helps no one, and because, when they quarrel, they spiritually destroy those who hear them (OR, they cause those who hear them to quit ◄being committed to Christ/believing in Christ►.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:4; 2 PETER 1:10; 1 TIMOTHY 4:6"> ◄Do your best/Try hard► to be the kind of person that God will approve of. Be like a good worker as you teach the true message accurately. If a worker works well, he will not need to be ashamed of (OR, will be pleased about) what he does.</span>
</div>

## Have nothing to do with godless and foolish talk.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TITUS 3:9; 1 TIMOTHY 6:20; 2 TIMOTHY 2:14"> ◄Stay away from/Do not talk with► those who talk foolishly and ◄godlessly/say things that displease God►, because those who talk foolishly will become even more ◄ungodly/displeasing to God►,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 1:20; NAHUM 3:15; JAMES 5:3"> and because their message will harm people &#91;SIM&#93; like gangrene/cancer does. You know Hymenaeus and Philetus. They are two such people who talk in this manner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:12; 1 TIMOTHY 1:19; HEBREWS 3:10"> Specifically, they teach a wrong message; that is, they say wrongly that God will not cause dead people to live again because he has already given us spiritual life. They claim that our spiritual life is the only new life that we will receive. By saying that, they cause some people</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 10:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:3; NAHUM 1:7"> not to continue to believe in Christ. However, the congregations of God are strong &#91;MET&#93;. They are like a strong foundation on which is written: “The Lord knows the people who really belong to him,” and “Every person who calls Jesus &#91;MTY&#93; ‘Lord’ must stop doing wicked things”.</span>
</div>

## Avoid the bad things that some young people desire to do. Do good instead.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 9:21–23; 1 PETER 2:5; 1 TIMOTHY 3:15"> In a wealthy person’s house there are not only utensils made of gold and of silver, but also utensils made of wood and of clay. The gold and silver utensils are used at special occasions/events, and the others are used at ordinary occasions. Similarly, in a congregation there are those who ◄are ready/desire► to do great things for the Lord Jesus, and there are those like the teachers of false doctrines/teachings who are not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 3:17; EPHESIANS 2:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:1"> Therefore, those who rid themselves of what is evil in their lives will be able to work well for the Lord. They will be like utensils &#91;MET&#93; used at special occasions, set apart for and especially useful to the owner of the big house. They will be like fine utensils that have been prepared &#123;are ready&#125; to do any kind of good work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 PETER 2:11; 1 PETER 3:11; 1 CORINTHIANS 6:18"> Because of that, you (sg) must avoid doing the wrong actions that many young people desire to do. Instead, you must earnestly do right actions, believe the true teaching, love God and others, and you must continually be at peace with those who ask the Lord to help them and who are pure in every way (OR, who serve Jesus faithfully).</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do not discuss foolish questions but gently instruct those who oppose the true message.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 2:14; TITUS 3:9; 1 TIMOTHY 4:7"> Do not talk with anyone who foolishly wants to argue about matters that are not important. Do not talk with them, because you know that when people talk about foolish things, they begin to quarrel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="TITUS 3:2; JAMES 1:19–20; 1 TIMOTHY 3:2–3"> But those who serve the Lord must not quarrel. Instead, they should be kind to all people, they should be able to teach God’s truth well, and they should be patient with people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ACTS 11:18; GALATIANS 6:1; 1 TIMOTHY 2:4"> That is, they should gently instruct/teach people who oppose the true message. They should do that in order that perhaps God will cause others to completely change what they think, so that they may acknowledge/believe the true message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 3:7; Isaiah 49:25–26; PSALMS 124:7"> In that way they may get free from what is like &#91;MET&#93; a trap set by the devil. The devil has deceived them in order that they might do what he wants them to do.</span>
</div>
