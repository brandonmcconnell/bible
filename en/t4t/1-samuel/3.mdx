## Yahweh Called Samuel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="AMOS 8:11–12; PSALMS 74:9; 1 SAMUEL 3:21"> While he was still a boy, Samuel was working for Yahweh while Eli supervised him. At that time there were very few messages that anyone received from Yahweh, and very few people saw visions that Yahweh gave them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 4:15; PSALMS 90:10; GENESIS 27:1"> By that time Eli’s eyes were very weak; he was almost blind. One night he was sleeping in his room,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 27:4; LEVITICUS 24:2–4; EXODUS 27:20–21"> and Samuel was sleeping in the sacred tent, where the chest that contained the Ten Commandments was kept. While the sacred lamp was still burning,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 6:8; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:6–11; EXODUS 3:4"> Yahweh called, “Samuel! Samuel!” Samuel replied, “I’m here!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then he got up and ran to Eli. He said to him, “I am here, because you called me!” But Eli replied, “No, I did not call you. Go back to your bed.” So Samuel went and lay down again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:2; 1 SAMUEL 4:16; 2 SAMUEL 18:22"> Then Yahweh called again, “Samuel!” So Samuel got up again and went to Eli and said, “I am here, because you called me!” But Eli said, “No, my son, I did not call you. Go back and lie down.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ACTS 19:2; JEREMIAH 9:24"> At that time Samuel did not yet know that Yahweh could speak to him, because Yahweh had not previously revealed anything to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 33:14–15; 1 CORINTHIANS 13:11–12"> After Samuel lay down again, Yahweh called him a third time. So again Samuel got up and went to Eli and said, “I am here, because you called me!”<br />Then Eli realized that it was Yahweh who was calling the boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 20:19; Isaiah 6:8; PSALMS 85:8"> So he said to Samuel, “Go and lie down again. If someone calls you again, say ‘Speak to me, Yahweh, because I am listening!’ ” So Samuel went and lay down again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 3:8; 1 SAMUEL 3:4–6"> Then Yahweh came and stood there and called as he had done the other times, “Samuel! Samuel!” Then Samuel said, “Speak to me, because I am listening!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:5; JEREMIAH 19:3; 2 KINGS 21:12"> Then Yahweh said to Samuel, “Listen carefully. I am about to do something here in Israel that will shock/horrify &#91;IDM&#93; everyone who hears about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:27–36; ZECHARIAH 1:6; JOSHUA 23:15"> When that happens, I will punish Eli and his family. I will do to them everything that I said that I would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:18; MATTHEW 10:37; 1 SAMUEL 2:12"> His sons have shown great disrespect for me by the disgraceful things they have done, and Eli did not restrain them. So I told him that I would punish his family forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 22:14; 1 SAMUEL 2:25; JEREMIAH 15:1"> I solemnly promised to Eli’s family, ‘You will never be able to avoid the consequences for your sin, even if you give me sacrifices or offerings.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:6–8; MALACHI 1:10; 1 SAMUEL 1:9"> Samuel lay down again until morning. Then he got up and opened the doors of the Sacred Tent as usual. He was afraid to tell Eli about the vision that Yahweh had given him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But Eli called him and said, “Samuel, my son!” Samuel answered, “I am here!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:35; RUTH 1:17; PSALMS 141:5"> Eli asked him, “What was it that Yahweh told you? Do not conceal it! I want God to punish you severely if you do not tell me everything that he said to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 2:10; Isaiah 39:8; JUDGES 10:15"> So Samuel told him everything. He did not refuse to tell him anything. Then Eli said, “He is Yahweh. I am willing for him to do what he thinks is best.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 39:2; 1 SAMUEL 9:6; 1 KINGS 8:56"> As Samuel grew up, Yahweh helped him, and caused everything to ◄come true/happen► that Samuel predicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JUDGES 20:1; 2 SAMUEL 3:10; 1 TIMOTHY 1:12"> So all the people of Israel, from the northern end of the country to the southern end, realized that Samuel was truly a prophet of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 3:1; GENESIS 12:7; 1 SAMUEL 3:4"> Yahweh continued to appear to Samuel in Shiloh and give messages to him.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
