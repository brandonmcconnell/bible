## The Israelis asked for a king

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:18–19; 1 TIMOTHY 5:21; Nehemiah 7:2"> When Samuel became old, he appointed his two sons, Joel and Abijah, to lead/rule the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:38; GENESIS 22:19; 1 CHRONICLES 6:28"> They judged people’s disputes/cases in Beersheba town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:19; PSALMS 15:5; 1 TIMOTHY 6:10"> But they were not like their father. They wanted only to get a lot of money. They accepted bribes, and they did not make honest decisions about people’s disputes/cases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 7:17; 2 SAMUEL 5:3; EXODUS 3:16"> Finally, the Israeli leaders met at Ramah town to discuss the matter with Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:14–15; HOSEA 13:10–11; 1 SAMUEL 8:6–8"> They said to him, “Listen! You are now old, and your sons are not like you. Appoint a king to rule over us, like the kings that other countries have!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:11; JAMES 1:5; 1 SAMUEL 12:17"> Samuel was very unhappy with them for requesting that, so he prayed to Yahweh about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 10:16; 1 SAMUEL 10:19; EXODUS 16:8"> Yahweh replied, “Do what they have requested you to do. But ◄do not think/the truth is not► that you are the one whom they are really rejecting. I have been their king, and I am the one they are really rejecting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 106:34–40; EXODUS 32:1; JUDGES 13:1"> Ever since I brought them out of Egypt, they have rejected me, and they have worshiped other gods. And now they are also rejecting you in the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 10:25; 1 SAMUEL 8:11–18; EZEKIEL 46:18"> Do what they are asking you to do. But warn/tell them about how their kings will act toward them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So Samuel told those people what Yahweh had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 14:52; DEUTERONOMY 17:14–20; 1 SAMUEL 10:25"> He said, “If a king rules over you, this is what he will do to you: He will force many of your sons to join the army. He will make some of them run in front of his chariots to clear people out of his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:7; 1 KINGS 4:7; 1 CHRONICLES 27:1–22"> Some of them will be commanders of his soldiers, but others will work for him like slaves. He will force some of them to plow his fields and then later harvest his crops. He will force others to make his weapons and equipment for his chariots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The king will take some of your daughters from you and force them to make perfumes for him and cook food for him and bake bread for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 46:18; 1 KINGS 21:7; 1 KINGS 21:19"> He will take your best fields and vineyards and olive tree groves/orchards, and give them to his own officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; GENESIS 37:36"> He will take a tenth of your harvests and distribute it among the officers and servants who work in his palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He will take from you your male and female servants, your best cattle and donkeys, and force them to work for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He will take one tenth of your sheep and goats. And you will become his slaves!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MICAH 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; PROVERBS 1:25–28"> When that time comes, you will complain loudly to the king, the king that you yourselves have chosen, but Yahweh will not ◄pay attention to/help► you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:16; JEREMIAH 7:13; Isaiah 66:4"> But the people refused to pay attention to what Samuel said. They said, “We do not care what you say! We want a king!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 8:5; PSALMS 106:35; DEUTERONOMY 7:6"> We want to be like the other nations. We want a king to rule us and to lead our soldiers when they go to fight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 11:11"> When Samuel told Yahweh what the people had said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 8:7; HOSEA 13:11"> Yahweh replied, “Do what they are telling you to do. Give them a king!” So Samuel agreed, and then he sent the people home.</span>
