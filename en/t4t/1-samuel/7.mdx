## Yahweh rescued the Israelis

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 4:8; DEUTERONOMY 13:4; MATTHEW 4:10"> Then Samuel said to all the Israeli people, “If you truly &#91;IDM&#93; want to act like Yahweh’s people should, you must get rid of your statues of the goddess Astarte and the idols/statues of all the other foreign gods. You must decide to serve/worship only Yahweh. If you do that, he will rescue you from the power of &#91;MTY&#93; the Philistia people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSEA 14:8; HOSEA 14:3; JUDGES 2:11"> So the Israelis got rid of all their statues of the gods Baal and Astarte, and they worshiped only Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JUDGES 20:1; 1 SAMUEL 12:23; JOSHUA 15:38"> Then Samuel told them, “All you Israeli people must gather with me at Mizpah. Then I will pray to Yahweh for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 14:14; 1 SAMUEL 1:15; JUDGES 10:10"> So they gathered at Mizpah, which was the town where Samuel previously was the leader of the Israeli people. They had a big ceremony there. They drew water from a well, and poured the water on the ground while Yahweh watched. To show that they were sorry for having worshiped idols, they did not eat any food on that day, and they confessed that they had sinned against Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:11; 1 SAMUEL 13:6; EXODUS 14:10"> When the kings of the Philistia area heard that the Israeli people had gathered at Mizpah, they led their armies there to attack the Israelis. When the Israelis found out that the Philistia army was approaching them, they became very afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 37:4; JAMES 5:16; 1 SAMUEL 12:19–24"> They told Samuel, “Pray to Yahweh to rescue us from the Philistia army &#91;MTY&#93;, and do not stop pleading!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:1; PSALMS 99:6; 1 SAMUEL 7:17"> So Samuel took a very young lamb and killed it and offered it to Yahweh to be a sacrifice that was completely burned on the altar. Then he prayed and pleaded that Yahweh would help the Israelis, and Yahweh did help them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:10; 2 SAMUEL 22:14–15; JOSHUA 10:10"> While Samuel was burning the offering, the Philistia army came near to attack the Israelis. But Yahweh caused it to thunder very loudly. The soldiers of the Philistia army became very frightened, and then they ◄became confused/did not know what to do►. So the Israelis were able to defeat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> The Israeli men ran out of Mizpah and pursued the Philistia soldiers almost to Beth-Car town, and killed many Philistia soldiers while they were trying to run away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 1:10; JOSHUA 4:9; ACTS 26:22"> After that happened, Samuel took a large stone and set it up between Mizpah and Jeshanah towns. He named the stone ‘Ebenezer’, which means ‘stone of help’, because he said “Yahweh has helped us until the present time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 13:1; 1 SAMUEL 17:49–53; 1 SAMUEL 14:6–16"> So the Philistia people were defeated, and for a long time they did not enter the Israeli land to attack them again. During the time that Samuel was alive, Yahweh powerfully protected &#91;MTY&#93; the Israeli people from being attacked by the Philistia army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 106:34; DEUTERONOMY 7:2; DEUTERONOMY 7:16"> The Israeli army was able to capture again the Israeli villages between Ekron and Gath that the Philistia army had captured before. The Israelis were also able to take again the other areas around those cities that the Philistia army had taken from the Israelis previously. And there was peace between the Israelis and the Amor people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 7:6; ACTS 13:20–21; JUDGES 2:16"> Samuel continued to be the leader of the Israeli people until he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 10:4; JUDGES 12:14; JUDGES 5:10"> As long as he was alive, every year he traveled back and forth between Bethel and Gilgal and Mizpah cities. In those cities he listened to disputes between people and made decisions about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 1:19; 1 SAMUEL 8:4; 1 SAMUEL 1:1"> After he listened to disputes and made decisions in each of those towns, he returned to his home at Ramah, and he would listen to people’s disputes there, also, and make decisions about them. And he built an altar at Ramah to offer sacrifices to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
