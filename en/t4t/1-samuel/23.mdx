## David saved the people of Keilah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:44; MICAH 6:15; Nehemiah 3:17–18"> One day someone told David, “You need to know that the Philistia army is attacking Keilah town and they are stealing grain from where men are threshing it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:4; 2 SAMUEL 5:19; 2 SAMUEL 5:23"> David asked Yahweh, “Should my men and I go to fight against those men from the Philistia people-group?”<br />Yahweh answered, “Yes, go. Attack them, and rescue the people of Keilah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 12:5; PSALMS 11:1; 1 SAMUEL 23:15"> But David’s men said to him, “We are afraid that Saul will attack us here in Judah. We will be more afraid if we go to Keilah where the Philistia army is!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 7:7; JOSHUA 8:7; 2 KINGS 3:18"> So David asked Yahweh again if they should go to Keilah. Yahweh answered, “Yes, go down to Keilah. I will help you to defeat the men of the Philistia people-group.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So David and his men went to Keilah. They fought against the men of the Philistia people-group and captured many of their cattle. David and his men killed many of the Philistia men and rescued the people of Keilah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:20; 1 SAMUEL 14:3; 1 SAMUEL 14:18"> Abiathar, the son of Ahimelech, fled to be with David at Keilah, and he brought the sacred vest with him to use it to determine what God wanted to be done.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Saul pursued David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 24:4–6; JOB 20:5; 1 SAMUEL 26:8–9"> Soon Saul found out that David was at Keilah. So he said, “That is good! God is enabling me to capture him! He has trapped himself in that town, because it has high walls with gates around it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> So Saul summoned his army, and they prepared to go down to Keilah to attack David and his men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:7; 1 SAMUEL 23:6; NUMBERS 27:21"> But David found out that Saul was planning for his army to attack him. So he said to Abiathar the priest, “Bring here the sacred vest.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 3:15–16; 1 SAMUEL 23:8; GENESIS 18:24"> So when Abiathar brought it, David prayed, “Yahweh, God of us Israelis, I have heard that Saul is planning to come here with his army and destroy Keilah because I am here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:3; PSALMS 50:15; MATTHEW 7:7–8"> Will Saul come down here to Keilah, as people reported to me? Will the leaders of Keilah enable Saul to capture me? Yahweh, God of us Israelis, please tell me!”<br />Yahweh answered, “Yes, Saul will come down.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:20; PSALMS 31:8; PSALMS 62:1"> Then David asked, “Will the leaders of Keilah enable Saul’s army to capture me and my men if we stay here?”<br />By means of the stones in the sacred vest, Yahweh replied, “Yes, they will.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:2; 1 SAMUEL 25:13; 1 SAMUEL 30:9–10"> So David and his 600 men left Keilah. They continued to move from one place to another, wherever they thought that Saul would not find them. And when Saul found out that David had escaped from Keilah, he did not go there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 54:3–4; PSALMS 32:7; JOSHUA 15:24"> David and his men stayed in hiding places in the desert and in the hills of Ziph Desert. Every day Saul sent men to search for David, but Yahweh did not allow them to capture David.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> While David and his men were at a place named Horesh in Ziph desert, he found out that Saul was coming there to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3–4; HEBREWS 12:12–13; JOB 4:3–4"> But Saul’s son Jonathan went to David at Horesh, and encouraged him to continue to trust God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 20:31; 1 SAMUEL 24:20; ACTS 28:16"> Jonathan told him, “Do not be afraid, because my father will not be able to harm &#91;IDM&#93; you. Some day you will be the king of Israel, and I will be the second most important man in Israel. My father Saul also knows that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 21:7; 1 SAMUEL 20:42; 1 SAMUEL 18:3"> Then the two of them repeated their solemn promise that Yahweh had heard them make previously, that they would always be loyal to each other. Then Jonathan went home, but David stayed at Horesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:1; 1 SAMUEL 26:3; PSALMS 54:1–7"> Some people from Ziph went to Saul when he was at Gibeah, and they told him, “David and his men are hiding in our land &#91;RHQ&#93;! They are hiding in places at Horesh on Hakilah Hill, south of a place named Jeshimon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:12; PROVERBS 29:26; PROVERBS 11:23"> So, O king, come down there any time that you want to. It is our duty to capture him and turn him over to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:8; Isaiah 66:5; MICAH 3:11"> Saul replied, “I hope/desire that Yahweh will bless you for telling that to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 5:13"> Go back and find out more about him. Find out exactly where he is staying, and find out who has seen him there. People tell me that he is very clever, so we need to be clever also to be able to capture him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ROMANS 3:15–16; MARK 14:1; NUMBERS 10:36"> Find out all the places where he and his men hide. Then come back and tell me everything that you have found out. Then I will take my army and go there with you. If David is in any of the clans/caves of Judah, we will search for him and find him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:2; JOSHUA 15:55"> So those people went back to Ziph before Saul went there. At that time David and his men were in Maon Desert, south of Jeshimon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JUDGES 15:8; 1 SAMUEL 23:28"> Saul and his soldiers went to search for David, but David heard about that. So he and his men went further south to a rocky hill/peak in the Maon Desert. When Saul heard about that, he and his men followed David and his men to the Maon Desert.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 17:9; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:8; PSALMS 140:1–9"> Saul and his soldiers were walking along one side of the hill/peak, and David and his men were on the other side. David and his men were hurrying to escape from Saul’s soldiers, because Saul and his soldiers were coming much closer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 KINGS 19:9; PSALMS 116:3; DEUTERONOMY 32:36"> But then a messenger came to Saul and said to him, “Come quickly! The Philistia army is attacking the people in our land!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Saul stopped pursuing David, and he and his soldiers went to fight against the Philistia army. That is the reason that people call that place ‘Escape Rock’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 20:2; JOSHUA 15:62; EZEKIEL 47:10"> David and his men also left the Maon Desert and went to places to hide safely at En-Gedi.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
