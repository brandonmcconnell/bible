## David spared Saul’s life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:28–29; EZEKIEL 22:9; 1 SAMUEL 23:19"> After Saul and his soldiers returned home after fighting against the Philistia army, someone reported to Saul that David and his men had gone into the desert near En-Gedi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:2; PSALMS 104:18; PSALMS 37:32"> When Saul heard that, he chose 3,000 men from various areas in Israel, and they went to search for David and his men at a place named Rocks of Wild Goats.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDGES 3:24; PSALMS 57:1–11; PSALMS 142:1–7"> At a place where the road was alongside some sheep pens, Saul left the road and entered a cave to defecate &#91;EUP&#93;. He did not know that David and his men were hiding further inside that same cave!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:8–11; 1 SAMUEL 24:10; 1 SAMUEL 26:23"> David’s men saw Saul and whispered to David, “Today is the day that Yahweh spoke about when he said, ‘I will enable you to defeat your enemy.’ You can do to him whatever you want to!” So David crept toward the entrance of the cave and with his knife he cut off a piece of Saul’s robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 24:10; 2 KINGS 22:19; 1 JOHN 3:20–21"> And then he returned to his men.<br />But then David felt guilty for having cut off a piece of Saul’s robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 1:14; JOB 31:29–30; 1 SAMUEL 26:9–11"> He said to his men, “I should not have done that to the king! I hope/desire that Yahweh will never allow me to attack the one whom God has appointed, because Yahweh is the one who chose him to be the king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:44; PSALMS 7:4; ROMANS 12:17–21"> By saying that, David restrained his men, and did not allow them to kill Saul.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:23–24; ROMANS 13:7; EXODUS 20:12"> After Saul left the cave and started to walk on the road again, David came out of the cave and shouted to Saul, “King Saul!” Saul turned around and looked, and David bowed down with his face touching the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:21–22; PROVERBS 29:12; PROVERBS 16:28"> Then he said to Saul, “◄Why do you pay attention to people when they say ‘David wants to harm you’?/You should not pay attention to people when they say ‘David wants to harm you’.► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 105:15; 1 SAMUEL 24:4; 1 SAMUEL 26:8–9"> Today you can see with your own eyes that what they say is not true. Yahweh put you in a place where my men and I could have killed you when you were in this cave. Some of my men told me that I should do that, but I did not do that. I said to them, ‘I will not harm my master, because he is the king whom Yahweh appointed.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:20; 1 SAMUEL 23:14; 1 SAMUEL 23:23"> Your majesty, look at this piece of your robe that is in my hand! I cut it from your robe, but I did not kill you. So now you should be able to understand that I am not planning to do anything evil to you. I have not done anything wrong to you, but you are searching for me to kill me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 43:1; JUDGES 11:27; PSALMS 35:1"> I hope/desire that Yahweh will judge and decide which of us ◄is doing what is right/pleases him►. And I hope/desire that he will punish you for the wrong things that you have done to me. But I will not try to harm you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:20; MATTHEW 7:16–18; MATTHEW 12:33–34"> There is a proverb that has the words, ‘Evil things are done by evil people.’ But I am not evil, so I will not do evil things to you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:20; 1 SAMUEL 17:43; 2 SAMUEL 9:8"> “You are the king of Israel. So ◄why are you pursuing me?/you should not be pursuing me.► &#91;RHQ&#93; I am as harmless as &#91;MET&#93; a dead dog or a flea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 43:1; PSALMS 35:1; PSALMS 119:154"> I hope/desire that Yahweh will judge which of us is doing what ◄he wants/is right►. I trust that he will act like a lawyer and judge ◄my case/what I have done►, and that he will rescue me from your power &#91;MTY&#93;.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:17; GENESIS 33:4; LUKE 21:15"> When David finished speaking, Saul called out to him and asked, “My son David, is that your voice that I am hearing?” Then he began to cry loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:44; 1 SAMUEL 26:21; GENESIS 38:26"> He said, “You are a better man than I am. You have done something very good to me when I tried to do something very bad to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:23; PSALMS 31:8; 1 SAMUEL 23:12"> When Yahweh put me in a place in that cave where you could have easily killed me, you did not do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 18:20; 1 SAMUEL 23:21; PROVERBS 25:21–22"> ◄Who else would/No one else would► find his enemy and allow his enemy to escape when he could kill him instead &#91;RHQ&#93;. I hope/desire that Yahweh will reward you for being kindly to me today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:17; MATTHEW 2:3–6; 2 SAMUEL 3:17–18"> I know that some day you will surely become the king, and that your kingdom will prosper as you rule the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 21:23; 2 SAMUEL 21:6–8; 1 SAMUEL 20:14–17"> Now while Yahweh is listening, solemnly promise to me that you will not kill my family and get rid of all my descendants.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:29; JOHN 2:24; MATTHEW 10:16–17"> David solemnly promised Saul that he would not harm Saul’s family. Then Saul went back home, and David and his men went back up into the place where they had been hiding.</span>
</div>
