## David and his men hid in a cave at Adullam

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 57:1–11; PSALMS 142:1–7; 1 CHRONICLES 11:15"> David and his men left Gath and went east to hide in a cave on a hill at Adullam city. Soon his older brothers and all his other relatives came and stayed with him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:28; 1 SAMUEL 30:22–24; PSALMS 72:12–14"> Then other men came there. Some were men who had caused trouble, some were men who owed money, and some were men who were not content &#91;IDM&#93; with the government. They continued to come until there were 400 men there, and David was their leader.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 3:18; 2 SAMUEL 15:25–26; JUDGES 11:29"> Later they left there and went east to Mizpah city in the Moab region. There David requested the king of Moab, “Please allow my father and mother to live here with you until I know what God is going to do for me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 11:1; 1 CHRONICLES 12:16; 1 CHRONICLES 29:29"> The king gave him permission, so David’s parents stayed with the king of Moab all the time that David and the men who were with him were hiding in that area.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 29:25; 1 CHRONICLES 21:9; 1 CHRONICLES 29:29"> One day the prophet Gad told David, “Leave ◄your fortress/this area► and return to Judah.” So David and his men went to Hereth forest in Judah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Saul killed Ahimelech and many other priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 19:9; 1 SAMUEL 18:10; 1 SAMUEL 20:33"> One day, someone told Saul that David and his men had arrived in Judah. On that day, Saul was sitting underneath the tamarisk tree on a hill at Gibeah town. He was holding his spear, and was surrounded by his army officers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:16; 1 SAMUEL 8:14–15; 1 SAMUEL 22:13"> He shouted to them, “You men of the tribe of Benjamin, listen to me! Do you think that the son of Jesse will give all of you fields and vineyards if he becomes your king? Will he appoint all of you to become generals and captains in his army &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 18:3; 1 SAMUEL 23:21; 1 SAMUEL 20:13–17"> Is that why you have all conspired against me &#91;RHQ&#93;? None of you told me when my son Jonathan made a solemn agreement with that son of Jesse. None of you feels sorry for me. That son of Jesse was my servant, but now my son is encouraging him to hide in order to ◄ambush me/attack me suddenly►, as he is doing today!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 52:1–9; 1 SAMUEL 14:3; EZEKIEL 22:9"> Doeg, a man from the Edom people-group, was standing there with Saul’s officers. He said to Saul, “When I was at Nob, I saw that son of Jesse talking to Ahimelech, the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:21; 1 SAMUEL 30:8; 1 SAMUEL 23:4"> Ahimelech asked Yahweh what David should do. Then Ahimelech gave to David some food and the sword of Goliath, the giant from the Philistia people-group.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 3:15"> Then Saul summoned Ahimelech and all Ahimelech’s relatives who were priests at Nob. So they all came to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 9:6; 1 SAMUEL 22:7; Isaiah 65:1"> Saul said to Ahimelech, “You son of Ahitub, listen to me!”<br />Ahimelech answered, “Yes, sir!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:8; LUKE 23:2–5; AMOS 7:10"> Saul said, “Why are you and Jesse’s son conspiring to get rid of me? You gave him some bread and a sword. You requested God to tell David what he should do. David has rebelled against me, and right now he is hiding somewhere, waiting to attack me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 20:32; 1 SAMUEL 19:4–5; 1 SAMUEL 22:13"> Ahimelech replied, “I do not understand why you are saying that, because David, your son-in-law, the captain of your bodyguards, is very loyal to you. No one is more loyal to you than David! Everyone in your household respects him very much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:36; 1 PETER 3:16–17; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:12"> Furthermore, this was certainly not &#91;RHQ&#93; the first time that I requested God to say what he wanted David to do. And it is not right for you to accuse me or any of my relatives of trying to get rid of you, because I do not know anything about anyone wanting to do that.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:15; DANIEL 3:19–20; 1 KINGS 19:2"> The king then shouted, “Ahimelech, you and your all your relatives are going to be executed right now!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 1:17; 1 SAMUEL 14:45; 2 KINGS 1:13–14"> Then he commanded his bodyguards, “Kill these priests of Yahweh, because they are allies of David, and they are conspiring with David against me! They knew that David was trying to run away from me, but they did not tell me!”<br />But Saul’s bodyguards refused to kill Yahweh’s priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 26:10–11; HOSEA 7:3; 1 SAMUEL 2:18"> Then the king said to Doeg, “You kill them!” So Doeg, the man from the Edom people-group, went out and struck them with his sword. On that day he killed eighty-five men who were wearing the sacred vests because they were God’s priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:3; HOSEA 10:14; 1 SAMUEL 21:1"> He also went and killed many people in Nob, the city where the priests lived. He killed men, women, children, babies, cattle, donkeys, and sheep there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:6; 1 SAMUEL 30:7; 1 KINGS 2:26–27"> But Abiathar, Ahimelech’s son, escaped. He ran away and joined David and the men who were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He told David that Saul had commanded Doeg to kill Yahweh’s priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 21:1–9; PSALMS 44:22"> Then David said to him, “That man from the Edom people-group, Doeg, was there at Nob on the day that I was there. I knew that he would surely tell Saul what happened. So it is my fault that your father and all of his family have been killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:26; HEBREWS 12:1–3; JOHN 10:28–30"> You stay with me, and do not be afraid. The man who wants to kill you wants to kill me also, but you will be safe if you stay with me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
