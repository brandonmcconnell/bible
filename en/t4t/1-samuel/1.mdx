## Samuel was born

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:34; 1 CHRONICLES 6:25–27; 1 SAMUEL 1:19"> There was a man named Elkanah who lived in Ramah town in the hilly area where the tribe of Ephraim lived. His father was Jeroham, his grandfather was Elihu, and his great-grandfather was Tohu. He belonged to the Zuph clan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 16:1–2; JUDGES 13:2; GENESIS 29:23–29"> He had two wives: Hannah and Peninnah. Peninnah had several children, but Hannah had no children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 2:41; JOSHUA 18:1; DEUTERONOMY 12:5–7"> Once every year Elkanah went up with his family from Ramah to Shiloh city. He went there to worship Yahweh, the commander of the armies of angels, and to offer sacrifices to him. The two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, helped their father to do the work as priests of Yahweh there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:17–18; DEUTERONOMY 16:11; DEUTERONOMY 12:5–7"> Each time Elkanah offered sacrifices there, he gave some of the meat to Peninnah and some to each of her sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 30:2; GENESIS 29:30–31; GENESIS 45:22"> But even though he loved Hannah very much, he gave her only one special amount of meat because Yahweh had not permitted her to give birth to any children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 6:14; JOB 24:21; LEVITICUS 18:18"> And his other wife, Peninnah, would ridicule Hannah to make her feel miserable, because Yahweh had not allowed her to bear children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:19"> This happened every year. When they went up to the temple of Yahweh at Shiloh, Peninnah ridiculed Hannah so much that Hannah cried and would not eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="RUTH 4:15; JOB 6:14; Isaiah 54:6"> Then Elkanah would say to her, “Hannah, ◄why are you crying?/please quit crying!► &#91;RHQ&#93; ◄Why are you not eating?/Please eat something!► &#91;RHQ&#93; Why are you so upset/sad? You have me; is that not more important to you than if you had ten sons? (OR, I love you more than your sons would love you, even if you had ten sons.)”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 3:3; PSALMS 27:4; PSALMS 5:7"> One year, after they had finished eating and drinking at Shiloh, Hannah stood up to pray. Eli the priest was nearby, sitting on a chair by the doorway of the sacred tent of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 7:11; LUKE 22:44; JOB 10:1"> Hannah was very distressed, and she cried very sorrowfully as she prayed to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDGES 13:5; NUMBERS 6:5; GENESIS 29:32"> She made a vow saying, “O Almighty Commander of the armies of angels, if you will look at me and see how miserable I am, and think about me and allow me to give birth to a son, then I will dedicate him to you to serve you for the rest of his life. And to show that he is dedicated to you, ◄no one will ever be allowed to cut his hair/his hair will never be cut►.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:17; JAMES 5:16; LUKE 18:1"> As she was praying like that to Yahweh, Eli the priest saw Hannah’s lips moving as she was praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 8:26; ACTS 2:13; GENESIS 24:42–45"> But Hannah was only praying silently; she was not making any sound. So Eli thought that she was drunk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:25; JOB 8:2; PROVERBS 6:9"> He said to her, “How long will you continue to get drunk &#91;RHQ&#93;? ◄Throw away your wine/Quit drinking wine►!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 62:8; LAMENTATIONS 2:19; PSALMS 42:4"> Hannah replied, “Sir, I am not drunk! I have not been drinking wine or any other alcoholic drink. I feel very distressed and I have been telling Yahweh about how I feel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:12; JOB 6:2–3; MATTHEW 12:34–35"> Do not think that I am a worthless/wicked woman! I have been praying like this because I am very distressed &#91;DOU&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARK 5:34; JUDGES 18:6; PSALMS 20:3–5"> Eli replied, “I wish/desire that things will go well for you. I desire that God, the one we Israeli people worship, will give you what you requested from him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="RUTH 2:13; ROMANS 15:13; ECCLESIASTES 9:7"> She replied, “I want you to think highly/well about me.” Then she returned to her family and after she ate something, her face no longer looked sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 21:1; GENESIS 30:22; 1 SAMUEL 1:11"> Early the next morning, Elkanah and his family got up and worshiped Yahweh again, and then they returned to their home at Ramah. Elkanah ◄slept with/had sex with► Hannah. Then Yahweh answered her prayer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 41:51–52; MATTHEW 1:21; EXODUS 2:10"> and she became pregnant and gave birth to a son. She named him Samuel, which sounds like the words in the Hebrew language that mean ‘heard by God’, because she said “Yahweh heard me when I requested from him a son.”</span>
</div>

## Hannah dedicated Samuel to Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 1:3; DEUTERONOMY 12:11; JOSHUA 24:15"> The following year, Elkanah again went up to Shiloh with his family to make the kind of sacrifice he made each year, and also to give a special offering to God that he had promised to give him previously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 1:28; 1 SAMUEL 1:11; LUKE 2:22"> But Hannah did not go with them. She said to her husband, “After I have weaned the baby, I will take him to Shiloh and present him to Yahweh, and he will stay there for the rest of his life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:25; LUKE 11:27; GENESIS 21:7–8"> Elkanah said, “Do what seems the best to you. Stay here until you have weaned him. What I desire is that Yahweh will enable you to do what you have promised.” So Hannah stayed at home and nursed her son until she weaned him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:9–10; JOSHUA 18:1; 1 SAMUEL 4:3–4"> After she weaned him, even though Samuel was very young, she took him to the temple/house of Yahweh at Shiloh. To offer as a sacrifice, she took along a three-year-old bull, a sack containing ◄20 pounds/9 kg.► of flour, and a container of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 18:15–16; LUKE 2:22"> After the priest had slaughtered the bull and offered it to Yahweh on the altar, Hannah and Elkanah brought the boy to Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 KINGS 4:30; 2 KINGS 2:4; 2 KINGS 2:6"> Then Hannah said to him, “Sir, ◄Do you remember me?/I solemnly affirm►, I am the woman who prayed as I stood here beside you several years ago.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 JOHN 5:15; PSALMS 118:5; MATTHEW 7:7"> I prayed that Yahweh would enable me to give birth to a child, and this is the child whom I requested Yahweh to give me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 24:26; GENESIS 24:52; 2 TIMOTHY 3:15"> So now I am presenting him to Yahweh. He will belong to Yahweh as long as he lives.” Then Elkanah and his family worshiped Yahweh there.</span>
