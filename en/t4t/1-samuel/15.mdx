## Saul’s army defeated the Amalekites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 9:16; 1 SAMUEL 12:14; PSALMS 2:10–11"> One day Samuel said to Saul, “Yahweh sent me to appoint you to be the king of the Israeli people. So now listen to this message from Yahweh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:17–19; EXODUS 17:8–16; NUMBERS 24:20"> Yahweh, the commander of the armies of angels has declared this: ‘I am going to punish the descendants of Amalek for attacking the Israeli people after the Israelis left Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:16–18; NUMBERS 24:20; NUMBERS 31:17"> So now go with your army and attack the Amalek people-group. Destroy them completely—destroy them and everything that belongs to them—the men and women, their children and infants, their cattle and sheep and camels and donkeys. Do not ◄spare any of them/allow any of them to remain alive►!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:24; 1 SAMUEL 11:8; 1 SAMUEL 13:15"> So Saul summoned the army, and they gathered at Telaim town. There were 200,000 soldiers. 10,000 of them were from Judah, and the others were from the other Israeli tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Saul went with his army to a town where some of the Amalek people-group lived. His army prepared to attack them suddenly by hiding in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDGES 1:16; NUMBERS 10:29–32; EXODUS 18:19"> Then Saul sent this message to the Ken people-group who lived in that area: “You acted kindly toward all our Israeli ancestors when they left Egypt. But we are going to kill all of the Amalek people-group, because they opposed/attacked our ancestors. So move away from where the Amalek people-group live. If you do not move away, you will be killed when they are killed.” So when the Ken people-group heard that, they immediately left that area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 16:7; GENESIS 25:18; 1 SAMUEL 14:48"> Then Saul’s army slaughtered the Amalek people-group, from Havilah town in the east to Shur town in the west. Shur was at the border between Israel and Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:1; Esther 3:1; NUMBERS 24:7"> Saul’s army captured Agag, the king of the Amalek people-group, but they killed everyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:15; 1 SAMUEL 15:19; 1 SAMUEL 15:3"> They not only ◄spared/did not kill► Agag, but they also took the best sheep and goats and cattle. They took everything that was good. They destroyed only the animals that they considered to be worthless.</span>

## Yahweh rejected Saul as king

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Yahweh said to Samuel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 13:13; GENESIS 6:6; 2 SAMUEL 24:16"> “I am sorry that I appointed Saul to be your king, because he has turned away from me and has not obeyed what I commanded him to do.” Samuel was very disturbed/upset when he heard that, and he cried out to Yahweh all that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:55; 2 SAMUEL 18:18; 1 SAMUEL 25:2"> Early the next morning, Samuel got up and went to talk with Saul. But someone told Samuel, “Saul went to Camel city, where he has set up a monument to honor himself. Now he has left there and gone down to Gilgal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 17:2; RUTH 3:10; PROVERBS 31:31"> When Samuel arrived at Gilgal he approached Saul, and Saul said to him,, “I wish/desire that Yahweh will bless you! I have obeyed what Yahweh told me to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MALACHI 3:13–15; JEREMIAH 2:18–19; JEREMIAH 2:34–37"> But Samuel replied, “If that is true, why is it that I hear cattle mooing and I hear sheep bleating?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:21; 1 SAMUEL 15:9; EXODUS 32:22–23"> Saul replied, “The soldiers took them from the Amalek people-group. They saved the best sheep and cattle, in order to offer them as sacrifices to Yahweh, your God. But we have completely destroyed all the others.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 9:27; 1 KINGS 22:16; 1 SAMUEL 12:7"> Samuel said to Saul, “Stop talking! Allow me to tell you what Yahweh said to me last night.” Saul replied, “Tell me what he said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 10:22; 1 SAMUEL 9:21; 1 SAMUEL 15:1–3"> Samuel said, “Previously you did not think that you were important. But now you have become &#91;RHQ&#93; the leader of the tribes of Israel. Yahweh appointed you to be their king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:29; GENESIS 13:13; GENESIS 15:16"> And Yahweh sent you to do something for him. He said to you, ‘Go and get rid of all those sinful people, the Amalek people-group. Attack them and kill all of them.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 14:32; 2 CHRONICLES 33:6; 2 CHRONICLES 36:12"> So why did you not obey Yahweh &#91;RHQ&#93;? Why did your men take the best animals &#91;RHQ&#93;? Why did you do what Yahweh said was evil?” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:13; MATTHEW 19:20; ROMANS 10:3"> Saul replied to Samuel, “Hey, I did what Yahweh sent me to do! I brought back King Agag, but we killed everyone else!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:15; EXODUS 32:22–23; GENESIS 3:13"> My men brought back only the best sheep and cattle and other things, in order to sacrifice them to Yahweh your God here at Gilgal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HOSEA 6:6; PSALMS 51:16–17; PROVERBS 21:3"> But Samuel replied,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Which do you think pleases Yahweh more, animals that are completely burned on the altar and other sacrifices,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or people obeying him &#91;SYN&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is better to obey Yahweh than to offer sacrifices to him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is better to pay attention to what he says than to burn the fat of rams, even though God said they should be sacrificed to him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JEREMIAH 28:16; 1 SAMUEL 13:14; LEVITICUS 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">To rebel against God is as sinful as doing sorcery/black magic,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and being stubborn is as sinful as worshiping idols.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So, because you disobeyed what Yahweh told you to do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he has declared that you will no longer be king.”</span></span>

## Saul asks to be forgiven

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:13; Isaiah 51:12–13; NUMBERS 22:34"> Then Saul said to Samuel, “Yes, I have sinned. I disobeyed what you told me to do, which is what Yahweh commanded. I did that because I was ◄afraid of/worried about► what my men would say if I did not do what they wanted. So I did what they demanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 10:17"> But now, please forgive me for having sinned. And come back with me to where the people are in order that I may worship Yahweh.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="HOSEA 4:6; 1 SAMUEL 13:14; 1 SAMUEL 16:1"> But Samuel replied, “No, I will not go back with you. You have rejected/disobeyed what Yahweh commanded you to do. So he has rejected you, and declared that you will no longer be the king of Israel. So I do not want to talk any more with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:30–31"> As Samuel turned to leave, Saul tried to stop him by grabbing the edge of Samuel’s robe, and it tore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 28:17–18; ACTS 13:22; DANIEL 4:32"> Samuel said to him, “You tore my robe! And today Yahweh has torn away from you the kingdom of Israel! He will appoint someone else to be king, someone who is a better man than you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:14; NUMBERS 23:19; TITUS 1:2"> And since the one who is the glorious God of the Israeli people does not lie, he will not change ◄his mind/what he has said►. Humans sometimes change their minds, but God does not do that, because he is not a human.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOHN 5:44; JOHN 12:43; Isaiah 29:13"> Then Saul pleaded again. He said, “I know that I have sinned. But please honor me in front of the leaders of the Israeli people and in front of all the other Israeli people by coming back to them with me in order that I may worship Yahweh your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Samuel finally agreed to do that, and they went together back to where the people were, and Saul worshiped Yahweh there.</span>

## Samuel executed King Agag

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="REVELATION 18:7; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:3; JEREMIAH 48:44"> Then Samuel said, “Bring King Agag to me.” So they brought Agag to him. Agag was confidently expecting that they would spare him/not kill him. He was thinking, “Surely I will not have to endure an agonizing death!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JUDGES 1:7; GENESIS 9:6; MATTHEW 7:2"> But Samuel said to him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You have killed the sons of many women with your sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so now your mother will no longer have a son.”</span>And Samuel cut Agag into pieces with his sword, there at Gilgal, in the presence of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 11:4; 1 SAMUEL 7:17"> Then Samuel left there and returned to his home in Ramah, and Saul went to his home in Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:11; 1 SAMUEL 19:24; 1 SAMUEL 16:1"> Samuel never saw Saul again, but he was very sad about what Saul had done. And Yahweh was very sorry that he had appointed Saul to be the king of Israel.</span>
