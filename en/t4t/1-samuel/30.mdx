## David’s army defeated the Amalekites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:7; 1 SAMUEL 29:11; 1 SAMUEL 27:8–10"> Three days later, when David and his men arrived at Ziklag, they discovered that men of the Amalek people-group had raided Ziklag and towns in the southern part of Judah. They had destroyed Ziklag and burned down all the buildings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 27:11; JOB 38:11; PSALMS 76:10"> They had captured the women and the children and everyone else, and had taken them away. But they had not killed anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 34:19; 1 PETER 1:6–7; REVELATION 3:9"> When David and his men came to Ziklag, they saw that the town had been burned, and that their wives and sons and daughters had been captured and taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 11:4; NUMBERS 14:1; JUDGES 21:2"> David and his men cried loudly, until they were so weak that they could not cry any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:42–43; 2 SAMUEL 2:2; 1 SAMUEL 1:2"> David’s two wives, Ahinoam and Abigail, had also been taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 56:3–4; PSALMS 56:11; PSALMS 62:5"> David’s men were threatening to kill him by throwing stones at him, because they were very angry because their sons and daughters had been taken away. David was very distressed, but Yahweh his God gave him strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:26; 1 SAMUEL 22:20–21; 1 SAMUEL 23:2–9"> David did not know what to do, so he said to Abiathar the priest, “Bring to me the sacred vest.” So Abiathar brought it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:18; PROVERBS 3:5–6; 1 SAMUEL 23:2"> and David asked Yahweh, “Should I and my men pursue the men who took our families? Will we be able to catch up to them?” Yahweh answered by means of the stones in Abiathar’s sacred pouch, “Yes, pursue them. You will catch up to them, and you will be able to rescue your families.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 27:2"> So David and the 600 men who were with him left, and they came to the Besor Ravine. Some of his men stayed there with some of their supplies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:9; 1 SAMUEL 30:21; 1 SAMUEL 14:31"> David and 400 men continued to pursue the men who captured their families. The other 200 men stayed there at the ravine, because they were so exhausted that they could not cross the ravine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 12:20–21; DEUTERONOMY 23:7; MATTHEW 25:35"> As David and the 400 men were going, they saw a man from Egypt in a field; so they took him to David. They gave the man some water to drink and some food to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JUDGES 15:19; 1 SAMUEL 14:27; 1 SAMUEL 30:13"> They also gave him a piece of fig cake and two clusters/packs of raisins. The man had not had anything to eat or drink for three days and nights, but after he ate and drank he felt refreshed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JAMES 2:13; PROVERBS 12:10; JOB 31:13–15"> David asked him, “Who is your master? And where do you come from?” He replied, “I am from Egypt. I am a slave of a man from the Amalek people-group. Three days ago my master left me here, because I was sick and I was not able to go with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 KINGS 1:38; 2 SAMUEL 8:18; 1 KINGS 1:44"> We had raided the southern part of Judah where the Kereth people-group live, and some other towns in Judah, and the area south of Hebron city where the descendants of Caleb live. We also burned Ziklag town.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:12; JOSHUA 9:19–20; EZEKIEL 17:16"> David asked him, “Can you lead us to this group of raiders?” He replied, “Yes, I will do that if you ask God to listen while you promise that you will not kill me or give me back to my master. If you promise that, I will take you to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="REVELATION 11:10–13; 2 SAMUEL 13:28; JUDGES 16:23–30"> David agreed to do that, so the man from Egypt led David and his men to where the men from the Amalek people-group were. Those men were lying on the ground, eating and drinking and celebrating because of having captured many things from the Philistia and Judah areas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 11:11; PSALMS 18:42; 1 SAMUEL 15:3"> David and his men fought against them from sunset that day until the evening of the following day. Four hundred of them escaped and rode away on camels, but none of the others escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 14:16"> David rescued his two wives, and he and his men got back everything else that the men of the Amalek people-group had taken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 91:9–10; 1 SAMUEL 30:8; JOB 1:10"> Nothing was missing. They took all their people back to Ziklag—young people and old people, their wives, their sons and their daughters. They also recovered all the other things that the men of the Amalek people-group had taken from Ziklag.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:26; NUMBERS 31:9–12; 2 CHRONICLES 20:25"> They took with them the sheep and cattle that had been captured, and his men caused those animals to go in front of them, saying, “These are animals that we captured in the battle; they belong to David!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:1; 1 SAMUEL 30:10; JUDGES 8:15"> David and his men got back to where the other 200 men were waiting, the men who did not go with David because they were very exhausted. They had stayed at Besor Ravine. When they saw David and his men coming, they went out to greet them. And David greeted them and said to them, “I hope that things are going well with you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:12; DEUTERONOMY 13:13; 1 KINGS 21:10"> But some of the men who had gone with David, men who were evil and troublemakers, said, “These 200 men did not go with us. So we should not give to them any of the things that we recovered/captured. Each of them should take only his wife and children and go back to their homes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:18; PSALMS 44:2–7; ACTS 7:2"> David replied, “No, my fellow Israelis, it would not be right to divide up like that the things that Yahweh has allowed us to capture. Yahweh has protected us and enabled us to defeat the enemies who attacked our town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 22:8; NUMBERS 31:27; PSALMS 68:12"> ◄Who will pay attention to you if you say things like that?/No one will pay attention to you if you say things like that.► &#91;RHQ&#93; The men who stayed here with our supplies will get the same amount that the men who went into the battle will get. They will all receive the same amount.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:13"> David made that to be a law &#91;DOU&#93; for the Israeli people, and that is still a law in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:27; GENESIS 33:11; Isaiah 32:8"> When David and all the others arrived in Ziklag, David sent to his friends who were leaders in Judah some of the things that they had captured from the Amalek people-group. He said to them, “Here is a present for you. These are things that we took from Yahweh’s enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:48; JOSHUA 19:8; JUDGES 1:22–23"> Here is a list of the cities and towns to whose leaders David sent gifts: Bethel, Ramoth in the southern part of Judah, Jattir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:16; JOSHUA 15:50; JOSHUA 21:14"> Aroer, Siphmoth, Eshtemoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JUDGES 1:16; 1 SAMUEL 27:10; 1 SAMUEL 15:6"> Racal, the cities where the descendants of Jehrameel lived and the cities where the Ken people-group lived,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JUDGES 1:17; JOSHUA 19:4; JOSHUA 15:42"> Hormah, Bor-Ashan, Athach,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 2:1; 2 SAMUEL 15:10; 2 SAMUEL 4:1"> Hebron, and all the other places where David and his men had gone when they were hiding from Saul.</span>
