## David and his men hid in a cave

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARK 2:26; Nehemiah 11:32; Isaiah 10:32"> David fled from there and went to Nob city to see Ahimelech the priest. Ahimelech trembled when he saw David because he was afraid that something bad had happened. He said to David, “Why are you alone? Why have no men come with you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:12; COLOSSIANS 3:9; 1 SAMUEL 22:22"> David deceived Ahimelech by replying, “The king sent me. But he does not want anyone to know anything about what he sent me to do. I have told my men where they should meet me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 3:6; JUDGES 9:29; 1 SAMUEL 21:4"> Now I want to know, do you have any food here for me to eat &#91;IDM&#93;? Could you give me five loaves of bread, or whatever other food that you can find?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 19:15; MATTHEW 12:3–4; EXODUS 25:30"> The priest answered David, “There is no ordinary bread here, but I have some of the sacred bread that was placed before Yahweh. Your men may eat it if they have not slept with &#91;EUP&#93; women recently.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:9; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:3–4; 2 TIMOTHY 2:20–21"> David replied, “They have not been near women for many days. I do not allow my men to defile themselves by sleeping with women while they are preparing to fight in battles. They must continue to keep themselves acceptable to God when they are on ordinary trips, and today they have kept themselves acceptable to God because now we are doing something very special/important.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 6:3–4; LEVITICUS 24:5–9; MATTHEW 12:3–4"> The only bread that the priest had was the sacred bread that had been placed in Yahweh’s presence in the Sacred tent. So the priest gave David some of that bread. On that day the priest had taken those loaves from the table and replaced them with ◄fresh/newly baked► loaves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:9; PSALMS 52:1–9; 1 CHRONICLES 27:29"> It happened that Doeg, from the Edom people-group, was there on that day to make himself acceptable to Yahweh, and he saw what Ahimelech did. He was one of Saul’s officials and the leader of Saul’s shepherds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> David asked Ahimelech, “Do you have a spear or a sword that I could use? The king appointed us to do this task and told us to leave immediately, so I did not have time to bring any weapons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:2; 1 SAMUEL 17:50–54; EXODUS 28:6–14"> Ahimelech replied, “I have only the sword that belonged to Goliath the giant from the Philistia people-group whom you killed in Elah Valley. It is wrapped in a cloth, and is behind the sacred vest in the Sacred Tent. If you want it, take it, because I have no other weapon here.”<br />David replied, “Truly, there is no other sword that is as good as that one! Give it to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 26:21; 1 KINGS 19:3; 1 SAMUEL 27:1–2"> So Ahimelech gave it to him, and David left there. He and his men and went to Gath city in the Philistia region, to stay with King Achish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 29:5; 1 SAMUEL 18:7–8; 1 SAMUEL 16:1"> But the officers of King Achish objected to David being there. They said to King Achish, “This man is &#91;RHQ&#93; David, the king of Israel! He is the one &#91;RHQ&#93; whom our enemies, the Israeli people, honored by dancing and singing,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Saul has killed thousands of his enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but David has killed tens of thousands of them!’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 2:19; PSALMS 56:3; PSALMS 34:4"> David heard what those men were saying, so he was afraid of what King Achish might do to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:25; ECCLESIASTES 7:7; PSALMS 34:1–22"> So he pretended that he was insane. He started scratching on the gates of the city and allowing his saliva to run down his beard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:7"> Then King Achish said to his men, “Look at this man! He is acting like an insane man! Why have you brought him to me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Have you brought him because I do not have enough insane men here already &#91;RHQ&#93;? I do not want &#91;RHQ&#93; him to come into my house!”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
