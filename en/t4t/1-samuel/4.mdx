<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 29:1; 1 SAMUEL 7:12; 1 SAMUEL 5:1"> And Samuel told to all the people of Israel the messages that God gave him. The Philistines captured the sacred chest<br />At that time the Israeli army went to fight against the army of the Philistine people. The Israeli army set up their tents at Ebenezer, and the Philistine army set up their tents at Aphek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:40; 1 SAMUEL 17:8; JOSHUA 7:5–8"> The Philistine army attacked the Israeli army, and as the battle continued, the Philistines defeated the Israelis and killed about 4,000 of their soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 3:5; NUMBERS 10:35; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:1–5"> When the remaining Israeli soldiers returned to their camp, the Israeli elders/leaders said, “Why did Yahweh allow the Philistine army to defeat us today? We should bring the chest that contains the Ten Commandments here from Shiloh, in order that Yahweh will go with us when we go to the battle again, and in order that our enemies will not defeat us again!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:2; NUMBERS 7:89; PSALMS 80:1"> So the soldiers did that. They sent some men to Shiloh, and those men brought back the chest that contained the Ten Commandments. They thought that if they did that, Yahweh would help them. They believed that Yahweh sat on a throne between the statues of winged creatures that were on top of the chest that Yahweh Almighty had given them. The two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, went with them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:5; JOB 20:5; AMOS 6:3"> When the Israeli people saw the men bringing the box into their camp, they were so happy that they shouted loudly. They shouted so loudly that they made the ground shake!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 32:17–18"> The Philistines asked, “What are the people in the Hebrew camp shouting about?” Someone told them that they were shouting because the chest that contained the Ten Commandments of Yahweh had been brought to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:30; EXODUS 14:25; EXODUS 15:14–16"> Then the Philistines became very afraid. They said, “One of the Israelis’ gods has come into their camp to help them We are in big trouble now! Nothing like this has happened to us before!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 9:14; EXODUS 7:5; PSALMS 78:43–51"> ◄Who can/Can anyone► save us from their powerful gods &#91;RHQ&#93;? They are the gods who struck the people of Egypt with many plagues before the Israelis left Egypt and traveled through the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 16:13; JUDGES 13:1; 2 SAMUEL 10:12"> You Philistine men, be courageous! Fight very hard! If you do not do that, they will defeat us, and then you will become their slaves, just like they have been our slaves previously!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:17; 2 KINGS 14:12; DEUTERONOMY 28:25"> So the Philistine men fought very hard, and they defeated the Israelis. They killed 30,000 Israeli soldiers, and the other Israeli soldiers fled and ran away to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:34; PSALMS 78:64; 1 SAMUEL 2:32"> The Philistines captured the sacred chest, and they killed Eli’s two sons, Hophni and Phinehas.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Eli died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:6; Nehemiah 9:1; 2 SAMUEL 15:32"> On that same day, one man of the tribe descended from Benjamin tore his clothes and threw dirt on his head to show that he was very sad. He ran from the place where the armies were fighting, and he arrived at Shiloh late that afternoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 1:9; 1 SAMUEL 4:18; JOSHUA 7:9"> Eli was waiting beside the road. He wanted to hear news about the battle, and he was also very anxious to know if anything bad had happened to God’s sacred chest. When the messenger arrived and told people what had happened, everyone in the town started to cry loudly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Eli asked, “Why are they making all that noise?” The messenger ran over to Eli and told him the news.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 3:2; PSALMS 90:10; 1 KINGS 14:4"> At that time, Eli was 98 years old, and he was blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 1:4; 1 SAMUEL 3:6; JOSHUA 7:19"> The messenger said to Eli, “I have just come from where the armies were fighting. I left there earlier today.” Eli asked, “What happened?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 3:11; 1 SAMUEL 4:10–11"> The man replied, “The Philistines defeated our Israeli army. They killed thousands of our soldiers, and the others ran away. The Philistines killed your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas. They also captured God’s sacred chest.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 4:21–22; 1 PETER 4:17–18; PSALMS 69:9"> Eli was very old, and he was very fat; and when he heard what had happened to the sacred chest, he fell backward from his chair beside the town gate. His neck was broken and he died. He had led the Israeli people for 40 years before he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The wife/widow of Eli’s son Phinehas was pregnant, and it was almost time for her to give birth to her baby. When she heard that God’s sacred chest had been captured and that her husband and her father-in-law were dead, her labor pains suddenly began. She quickly gave birth to a boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 35:17–18; PSALMS 77:2; JOHN 16:21"> The women who were helping her tried to encourage her by saying to her, “You have given birth to a son!” But she did not pay any attention &#91;DOU&#93; to what they said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 26:8; JEREMIAH 2:11; PSALMS 78:61"> She named the boy Ichabod, which means ‘no glory’, because she said, “God’s glory has departed from Israel.” She said that because God’s sacred chest had been captured and because her husband and her father-in-law had died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 137:5–6; JOHN 2:17"> She said, “God’s glory has left Israel, because God’s sacred chest has been captured!” And then she died.</span>
</div>
</div>
