## Jonathan and his armor-bearer killed many Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 6:27; MICAH 7:5; 2 SAMUEL 1:25–26"> One day, Jonathan said to the young man who carried his weapons, “Come with me; we will go over to where the Philistia soldiers have put up their tents.” So they went, but Jonathan did not tell his father what they were going to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 13:15–16; Isaiah 10:28–29"> On that day, Saul and the 600 soldiers who were with him were sitting around a pomegranate tree at a place where the people threshed grain, near Gibeah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:20; 1 SAMUEL 2:28; 1 SAMUEL 4:21"> Ahijah the priest was also there. He was wearing the sacred vest. Ahijah was the son of Ahitub, who was a brother of Ichabod. Ichabod and Ahitub were the sons of Phinehas, who was the son of Eli, who had been Yahweh’s priest at Shiloh.<br />No one knew that Jonathan had left the Israeli camp.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 13:23"> Jonathan planned that he and the young man would go through a narrow ◄pass/place between two cliffs► to get to where the Philistia army was. The cliff on one side of the pass was named Bozez, and the other cliff was named Seneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> One cliff faced north toward Micmash, and the cliff on the other side faced south toward Geba town.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:26; ROMANS 8:31; ZECHARIAH 4:6"> Jonathan said to the young man who carried his weapons, “Come with me. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:3; ZECHARIAH 8:23; 1 SAMUEL 10:7"> The young man who was carrying Jonathan’s weapons said, “Do what you think is the best thing for us to do. I will help you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 7:9–14"> Then Jonathan said, “Okay, come with me. We will cross the valley to where the Philistia army is, and allow them to see us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 6:36–40; GENESIS 24:13–14"> If they then say to us, ‘You two stay there until we come down to you,’ we will stay there and not go up to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 24:14; 1 SAMUEL 10:7; JUDGES 7:11"> But if they say to us, ‘Come up here and fight against us,’ that will show us that Yahweh will enable us to defeat them. Then we will go up and fight them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 13:6; 1 SAMUEL 14:22; JUDGES 6:2"> When the two of them crossed the valley, the Philistia soldiers saw them coming. They said, “Look! The Hebrews are crawling out of the holes in which they have been hiding!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:43–44; 2 SAMUEL 5:24; JUDGES 7:15"> Then the Philistia soldiers who were closest to Jonathan and the young man who carried his weapons said, “Come up here, and we will teach you something about how to fight &#91;IDM&#93;!”<br />Jonathan said to the young man who was with him, “Come behind me and climb up, because Yahweh is going to help us to defeat them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 8:31; JOSHUA 23:10; LEVITICUS 26:7–8"> So Jonathan climbed up, using his hands and his feet because it was very steep. The young man climbed up following him. As Jonathan climbed, he struck and killed many Philistia soldiers, and the young man who was with him killed many more as he followed behind Jonathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> In that first battle the two of them killed about 20 Philistia soldiers in an area that was about half an acre.</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 13:17; GENESIS 35:5; 2 SAMUEL 5:24"> Then all the other Philistia soldiers, the ones in the camp and the ones who had been attacking the Israeli towns, ones who were out in the field much closer, panicked. Then God caused the ground to shake, and they all became terrified/very afraid.</span>

## The Israelis defeated the Philistia army

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 20:22–25; Isaiah 19:2; JUDGES 7:22"> Saul’s ◄lookouts/men who were watching to see if enemies were coming► were in Gibeah town in the land of the tribe of Benjamin. They saw that the soldiers of the Philistia army were running away in all directions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Saul realized that some of his soldiers must have attacked the Philistia army. So he said to the soldiers who were with him, “Check to see if any of our men are not here.” So they checked, and they found out that Jonathan and the man who carried his weapons were gone.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:7–8; 1 SAMUEL 5:2; JUDGES 20:23"> So Saul said to Ahijah the Supreme Priest, “Bring the sacred vest here and find out what we should do.” On that day Ahijah was wearing the sacred vest, going in front of the Israelis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 14:24; NUMBERS 27:21; JOSHUA 9:14"> But while Saul was talking to the priest, he saw that the Philistia soldiers were becoming more panicked/confused. So Saul said to Ahijah, “Take your hand down from the sacred vest. Do not waste any more time using the marked stones in the vest to find out what Yahweh wants us to do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JUDGES 7:22; 2 CHRONICLES 20:23; 1 SAMUEL 14:16"> Then Saul gathered his men and they went toward the battle. They found that the Philistia soldiers were so confused that they were striking each other with their swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 29:4; JUDGES 7:23"> Before that, some of the Hebrew men had deserted their army and gone to join with the Philistia army. But now those men revolted and joined with Saul and Jonathan and the other Israeli soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 13:6; 1 SAMUEL 31:7"> Some of the Israeli soldiers had previously run away and hidden in the mountains where the tribe of Ephraim lived. <span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 14:30; 1 SAMUEL 13:5; PSALMS 44:6–8"> So Yahweh rescued the Israelis on that day. The Israeli soldiers continued to pursue their enemies beyond Beth-Aven town.</span>
</div>

## The dispute about Jonathan eating honey

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:26; ROMANS 10:2; JUDGES 1:28"> Before Saul’s soldiers went to the battle, Saul declared to them solemnly, “I do not want any of you to eat any food before this evening, before we have defeated all our enemies. If anyone eats anything, Yahweh will curse/punish him.” So none of the Israeli soldiers ate any food, and they became faint/weak because they were very hungry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 3:8; NUMBERS 13:27; DEUTERONOMY 9:28"> The Israeli army went into the forest, and they found honeycombs on the ground, but they did not eat any honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:2"> They were afraid to eat any, because they had solemnly promised that they would not eat any food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 14:29; 1 SAMUEL 14:43; 1 SAMUEL 30:12"> But Jonathan did not hear what his father commanded because he had left the camp very early in the morning. So when he saw a honeycomb, he dipped the end of his walking stick into it and ate some honey. After he ate the honey, he felt stronger.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 14:43; 1 SAMUEL 14:24"> But one of the Israeli soldiers saw him and said to him, “Your father solemnly declared to us that Yahweh would curse/punish anyone who ate any food today. So now we are very tired and weak from being hungry because we obeyed him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:18; JOSHUA 7:25"> Jonathan exclaimed, “My father has caused trouble for all of us! See how refreshed/strong I am after eating a little honey!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:18"> If he had permitted all of us to eat from the food we took from our enemies while we were pursuing them, we would have been able to kill many more of their soldiers!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:12; JOSHUA 19:42"> The Israelis pursued and killed Philistia soldiers all that day, from Micmash town west to Aijalon. But they continued to become weaker from being hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:19; LEVITICUS 3:17; LEVITICUS 17:10–14"> They had taken many sheep and cattle that the Philistia soldiers had abandoned. Now, because they were extremely hungry, they butchered some of those animals and ate the meat without draining the blood from the animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ROMANS 2:1; MATTHEW 7:5"> One of the soldiers told Saul, “Look! The men are sinning against Yahweh by eating meat that still has blood in it!”<br />Saul replied to the men who were near him, “They have disobeyed Yahweh! Roll a large stone over here!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> After they did that, he said to those men, “Go and tell all the soldiers that each of them must bring an ox or a sheep to me, and kill it here on this stone, and drain the blood before he eats any of the meat. They should not sin against Yahweh by eating meat from some animal without draining its blood.” So that night all the soldiers brought animals and slaughtered them there. Then Saul built an altar to worship Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 7:17; 1 SAMUEL 7:12; 2 TIMOTHY 3:5"> That was the first time that he built an altar for Yahweh.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The soldiers rescued Jonathan from Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 48:1–2; PSALMS 73:28; MALACHI 2:7"> Then Saul said to the Israeli soldiers, “Let’s pursue the Philistia soldiers tonight. We can attack them all night. We will not allow any of them to escape alive.”<br />The Israeli soldiers answered, “We will do whatever you think is the best thing for us to do.”<br />But the priest said, “We should ask Yahweh what he thinks we should do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 28:6; 1 SAMUEL 30:7–8; 1 SAMUEL 23:9–12"> So Saul asked God, “Should we pursue the Philistine soldiers? Will you enable us to defeat them?” But God did not answer Saul that day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JUDGES 20:2; JOSHUA 7:14–26; NUMBERS 24:17"> Then Saul summoned all the leaders of his army. He said to them, “I am sure that God has not answered me because someone has sinned. We must find out what sin someone has committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 14:44; 2 SAMUEL 12:5; 1 SAMUEL 14:24"> Yahweh has rescued us from the Philistia army. Just as certain as Yahweh lives, whoever has sinned must be executed. Even if it is my son Jonathan who has sinned, he must be executed.”<br />His men knew who was guilty, but none of them said anything to Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 15:15; 1 SAMUEL 14:7; 1 SAMUEL 14:36"> Then Saul said to all the Israeli soldiers, “You stand on one side. My son Jonathan and I will stand on the other side.”<br />His men replied, “Do whatever you think is best.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="ACTS 1:24; JOSHUA 7:16–18; 1 SAMUEL 10:20–21"> Then Saul prayed to Yahweh, the Israelis’ God, “Tell me who is guilty and who is not guilty.” Then the priest ◄cast lots/threw the stones that were marked►, and they indicated that it was either Jonathan or Saul who was the guilty one, and that the other men were not guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then Saul said to the priest, “Throw the stones again to indicate which of us two is guilty.” So he did, and the stones indicated that Jonathan was the guilty one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:19; 1 SAMUEL 14:27; JONAH 1:7–10"> Then Saul said to Jonathan, “Tell me what you have done that was wrong.”<br />Jonathan replied, “I ate a little bit of honey. It was only a little bit that was on the end of my stick. Do I deserve to be executed because of doing that?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 14:39; RUTH 1:17; 1 SAMUEL 25:22"> Saul replied, “Yes, you must be executed! I hope/wish that God will strike me and kill me if you are not executed for having done that!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LUKE 21:18; ACTS 27:34; 2 SAMUEL 14:11"> But the Israeli soldiers said to Saul, “Jonathan has won a great victory for all us Israelis. Should he be executed for eating some honey &#91;RHQ&#93;? Certainly not! Just as surely as Yahweh lives, we will not allow you to injure him in any manner &#91;IDM&#93;, because today God helped Jonathan to kill many soldiers of the Philistia army!”<br />So by saying that the Israeli soldiers rescued Jonathan, and he was not executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Then Saul ordered his soldiers to stop pursuing the Philistia army, so the Philistia soldiers returned to their homes.</span>
</div>

## Saul’s army defeated many of their enemies

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 10:6; 1 SAMUEL 14:52; 2 SAMUEL 8:3"> After Saul became the ruler/king of the Israeli people, his army fought against their enemies in many areas. Wherever the Israeli army fought, they defeated their enemies.</span>

<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 2:8; 1 SAMUEL 17:55; 2 SAMUEL 3:27"> Saul’s wife was Ahinoam, the daughter of Ahimaaz. The commander of Saul’s army was Abner, who was the son of Saul’s uncle Ner.</span>
