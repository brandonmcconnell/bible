## Goliath challenged the Israeli army

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:35; 2 CHRONICLES 28:18; 1 CHRONICLES 11:13"> The Philistines gathered their army to fight the Israeli army. They gathered together near Socoh, in the area where the descendants of Judah lived. They set up their tents at Ephes-Dammim, which is between Socoh and Azekah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 21:9; 1 SAMUEL 17:19"> Saul gathered the Israeli army near Elah Valley, and they set up their tents. Then they all took their places, ready to fight the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So the Philistine and Israeli armies faced each other. They were on two hills, with a valley between them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:22; 2 SAMUEL 21:16–22; 1 CHRONICLES 20:4–8"> Then Goliath, from Gath city, came out from the Philistine camp. He was a champion warrior/soldier. He was ◄over 9 feet/3 meters► tall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> He wore a helmet made of bronze to protect his head, and he wore a coat made of metal plates to protect his body. The coat weighed ◄125 pounds/56 kg.►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:45; 2 CHRONICLES 9:15; 1 KINGS 10:16"> He wore bronze guards/protectors on his legs. He had a long bronze dagger fastened on his back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 21:19; 1 SAMUEL 17:41; 1 CHRONICLES 11:23"> He also had a big spear. It had a cord on it to enable him to throw it better (OR, which was as thick as a weaver’s beam). The tip of the spear was made of iron and weighed ◄15 pounds/almost 7 kg.►. A soldier carrying a huge shield walked in front of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 8:17; 1 CHRONICLES 21:3; 1 SAMUEL 17:26"> Goliath stood there and shouted to the Israeli army, “Why are you all lined up for battle? <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 11:1"> If he fights with me and kills me, then my fellow Philistines will all be your slaves. But if I defeat him and kill him, then you Israelis will all be our slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:25–26; 1 SAMUEL 17:45; 1 SAMUEL 17:36"> I defy/challenge you men of the Israeli army. Send me a man who will fight with me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:9; DEUTERONOMY 31:8; PROVERBS 28:1"> When Saul and all the Israeli soldiers heard that, they were extremely terrified &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>

## David went to Saul’s camp

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:10–11; GENESIS 35:19; 1 SAMUEL 16:18"> David was the son of Jesse. Jesse was from the clan of Ephrath. He lived in Bethlehem, in the area where the descendants of Judah lived. Jesse had eight sons. When Saul was king, Jesse had already become a very old man &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:13; 2 SAMUEL 21:21; 1 SAMUEL 17:28"> Jesse’s three oldest sons, Eliab and Abinadab and Shammah, had gone with Saul to fight the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:11; GENESIS 25:23"> David was Jesse’s youngest son. While his three oldest brothers were with Saul,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:11; 1 SAMUEL 16:19–23"> David went back and forth: Sometimes he went to Saul’s camp, and sometimes he stayed in Bethlehem to take care of his father’s sheep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:2; LUKE 4:2"> For 40 days Goliath came out from the Philistine camp and stood there taunting the Israeli army/telling the Israelis to choose one man to fight with him. He did it twice each day, once in the morning and once in the evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:18; 2 SAMUEL 17:28; RUTH 2:14"> One day, Jesse said to David, “Here is a sack of roasted grain and ten loaves of bread. Take these quickly to your older brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 37:14; 1 SAMUEL 16:20; 2 SAMUEL 17:29"> And here are ten large chunks of cheese. Take them to their commander. And see how things are going with your older brothers. Then if they are okay, bring back something to show they are all right.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> His brothers were with Saul and all the other Israeli soldiers, camped alongside Elah Valley, preparing to fight the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:5; LUKE 19:43; EPHESIANS 6:1–2"> So David arranged for another shepherd to take care of the sheep. Early the next morning he took the food and went to the Israeli camp, as Jesse told him to do. He arrived there just as the Israeli soldiers were forming their ranks and going out to the battlefield. As they went, they were shouting a war cry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The Philistine army and the Israeli army stood on the hillsides, facing each other, ready for the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKE 10:5–6; JUDGES 18:15; Isaiah 10:28"> David gave the food to the man who was taking care of the war equipment. He told him to take care of the food that he brought, and then he went and greeted his older brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:8–10"> While he was talking with them, he saw Goliath coming out from among the Philistine soldiers, shouting to the Israelis, challenging them to send a man to fight him. David heard what Goliath was saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 13:6–7; LEVITICUS 26:36; Isaiah 7:2"> When all the Israeli soldiers saw Goliath, they were terrified and started to ran away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:16; REVELATION 2:7; REVELATION 3:12"> They were saying to each other, “Look at him coming up toward us! And listen to him as he defies us Israelis! The king says that he will give a big reward to whoever kills this man. He also says that he will give his daughter to that man for him to marry her, and that he will no longer require that man’s family to pay taxes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:26; 1 SAMUEL 11:2; 1 SAMUEL 14:6"> David talked to some of the men who were standing near him. He said, “What will be given to whoever kills this Philistine and frees us Israelis from this shame/disgrace? And who does this heathen Philistine think he is, defying/ridiculing the army that serves the all-powerful God?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:25"> They told him the same thing that the other men had said, about what the king would do for anyone who killed Goliath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:36; GENESIS 37:11; PROVERBS 18:19"> But when David’s oldest brother Eliab heard David talking to the men, he was angry. He said to David, “Why have you come down here? Is someone taking care of those few sheep that you left in the desert? I know you are just a ◄smart-aleck/brat►! You just want to watch the battle!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 PETER 3:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:15; PROVERBS 15:1"> David replied, “Have I done something wrong? I was merely asking a question!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:26–27"> Then he walked over to another man and asked him the same question, but the man gave him the same answer. Each time he asked someone, he received the same answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:29"> Finally, someone told king Saul what David had asked, and Saul sent someone to bring David to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:18; DEUTERONOMY 20:1–3; PSALMS 3:6"> David told king Saul, “No one should ◄worry/become a coward► because of that Philistine man. I will go and fight with him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:31; DEUTERONOMY 9:2; REVELATION 13:4"> Saul said to David, “You are only a young man, and he has been a very capable soldier all his life. So you are not able to go and fight with him!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> David replied, “I have been taking care of my father’s sheep for many years. Whenever a lion or a bear came and carried away a lamb,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="AMOS 3:12; 2 TIMOTHY 4:17–18; DANIEL 6:22"> I went after the lion or the bear and attacked it and rescued the lamb from the wild animal’s mouth. Then I grabbed the animal by its jaw/throat and struck it and killed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:10; ACTS 5:38–39; ROMANS 2:28–29"> I have killed both lions and bears. And I will do the same to this heathen Philistine, because he has defied/ridiculed the army of the all-powerful God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:17–18; PSALMS 138:7–8; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:9–10"> Yahweh has rescued me from paws of lions and bears, and he will rescue me from this Philistine!” Then Saul said to David, “All right, go and fight him, and I hope/desire that Yahweh will help you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Then Saul gave to David his own clothes that he always wore in battles, and he gave him a bronze helmet and a coat made of metal plates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 4:6; 2 CORINTHIANS 10:4–5; HOSEA 1:7"> David put these things on. Then he fastened his sword over them, and tried to walk. But he could not walk, because he was not accustomed to wearing those things. So David said to Saul, “I cannot fight wearing all these things, because I am not accustomed to wearing them!” So he took them off.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:27–29; MATTHEW 10:10; JUDGES 15:15–16"> Then he took his walking/shepherd’s stick, and he picked up five smooth stones from the brook/stream. He put them in the pouch of his shoulder-bag. Then he put his sling in his hand and started walking toward Goliath.</span>
</div>
</div>

## David killed Goliath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Goliath walked toward David, with the soldier who was carrying his shield walking in front of him. When he got near David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:12; 1 SAMUEL 17:33; PSALMS 123:3–4"> he looked at David closely. He saw that David had a handsome face and healthy body, but that he was only a young man. So he sneered at David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 9:8; 2 SAMUEL 3:8; 1 SAMUEL 24:14"> He said to David, “Are you coming to me with a stick because you think that I am a dog?” Then he called out to his gods to harm David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:9–10; PROVERBS 18:12; 1 KINGS 20:10–11"> He said to David, “Come here to me, and I will kill you and give your dead body to the birds and wild animals to eat!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 10:4; 2 CHRONICLES 32:8; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:5"> David replied, “You are coming to me with a sword and a dagger and a spear. But I am coming to you ◄in the name/with the authority► of almighty Yahweh. He is the God whom the army of Israel worships, and he is the God whom you have defied/ridiculed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 52:10; JOSHUA 4:24; 2 KINGS 19:19"> Today Yahweh will enable me to defeat you. I will strike you down and cut off your head. And we Israelis will kill many Philistine soldiers and give their bodies to the birds and wild animals to eat. And everyone in the world will hear about it and know that we Israeli people worship an all-powerful God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="PSALMS 44:6–7; ZECHARIAH 4:6; HOSEA 1:7"> And everyone here will know that Yahweh can rescue people without a sword or a spear. Yahweh always wins his battles, and he will enable us to defeat all of you Philistines.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:1; PSALMS 27:1"> As Goliath came closer to attack David, David ran quickly toward him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:27–28; 2 KINGS 9:24; 1 KINGS 22:34"> He put his hand into his shoulder-bag and took out one stone. He put it in his sling and hurled it toward Goliath. The stone hit Goliath on the forehead and penetrated his skull, and he fell face down onto the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="JUDGES 15:15; 1 SAMUEL 17:39; 1 SAMUEL 23:21"> Then David ran and stood over Goliath. He pulled Goliath’s sword from its sheath and killed him with it, and then cut off his head. In that way David defeated the Philistine without having his own sword. He used only a sling and a stone!<br />When the other Philistines saw that their great warrior was dead, they ran away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:11; JOSHUA 15:33–36; JOSHUA 15:45–46"> The Israeli men shouted and ran after them. They pursued them all the way to Gath and to the gates of Ekron city. They struck them as they went, with the result that dead/wounded Philistines were lying on the road all the way from Shaaraim to Gath and Ekron towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:20; 2 KINGS 7:7–16; JEREMIAH 30:16"> When the Israelis returned from chasing the Philistines, they ◄plundered/took everything from► the Philistine camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="EXODUS 16:33; JOSHUA 4:7–8; 1 SAMUEL 21:9"> David later took the head of Goliath to Jerusalem, but he kept Goliath’s weapons in his own tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:21–22; 1 SAMUEL 17:58"> As Saul watched David going toward Goliath, he said to Abner, the commanded of his army, “Abner, whose son is that young man?” Abner replied, “◄As sure as you are alive/Truly► &#91;IDM&#93;, I do not know.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Then the king said, “Find out whose son he is!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:54"> Later, as David returned from killing Goliath, Abner took him to Saul. David was carrying Goliath’s head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:12; 1 SAMUEL 16:18–19"> Saul asked him, “Young man, whose son are you?” David replied, “Sir, perhaps you have forgotten that I am the son of your servant Jesse, who has served you faithfully, who lives in Bethlehem.”</span>
</div>
