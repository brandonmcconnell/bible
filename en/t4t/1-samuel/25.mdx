## Samuel died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 28:3; NUMBERS 20:29; DEUTERONOMY 34:8"> Soon after that, Samuel died, and all the Israeli people gathered and mourned for him. They buried his body outside his home in Ramah. Nabal insulted David<br />Then David and his men moved to the Maon Desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 38:13; 1 SAMUEL 23:24; JOSHUA 15:55"> In Maon town there was a man who owned land in Carmel, a nearby village. He was very rich; he owned 3,000 sheep and 1,000 goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:1; PROVERBS 31:10; Isaiah 32:5–7"> His name was Nabal; he was a descendant of Caleb. His wife Abigail was a wise and beautiful woman, but Nabal was very cruel and treated people very unkindly &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 38:13; 2 SAMUEL 13:23"> One day while David and his men were in the desert, someone told him that Nabal was cutting the wool from his sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 43:23; 1 SAMUEL 17:22"> So David told ten of his men, “Go to Nabal at Carmel and greet him for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 10:5; 1 CHRONICLES 12:18; PSALMS 122:7"> Then tell to him this message from me, ‘I wish/desire that things will go well for you and your family and for everything that you possess.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:21; 1 SAMUEL 25:15–16; 1 SAMUEL 22:2"> ‘ heard people say that you are cutting the wool from your sheep. Previously, when your shepherds were among us, we did not harm them. All the time that your shepherds were among us at Camel, we did not steal any sheep from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 9:19; Nehemiah 8:10–12; LUKE 14:12–14"> You can ask your servants if this is true, and they will tell you that it is true. We have come here at a time when you are celebrating, so I ask you to please be kind to us and give these men whatever extra food you have, for me, David, and my men to eat.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 KINGS 2:15; 2 CHRONICLES 14:7; GENESIS 8:4"> When David’s men arrived where Nabal was, they gave David’s message to him, and they waited for him to reply. But Nabal spoke harshly to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 9:28; Isaiah 32:7; 1 SAMUEL 22:7–8"> He said to them, “Who does this man, this son of Jesse, think that he is? &#91;DOU, RHQ&#93; There are many slaves who are running away from their masters at the present time, and it seems to me that he is just one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDGES 8:6; GALATIANS 6:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:9"> I give bread and water to the men who are cutting the wool from my sheep, and I give them meat from animals that I have slaughtered. Why should I take some of those things and give them to a group of outlaws &#91;RHQ&#93;? ◄Who knows where they have come from?/I do not even know where they have come from.►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:21–22; 2 SAMUEL 24:13; HEBREWS 13:17"> Then David’s men returned and told him what Nabal had said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:13; PROVERBS 25:8; 1 SAMUEL 24:5–6"> When David heard that, he told his men, “We are going to kill Nabal; fasten your swords!” So he fastened on his sword and about 400 men fastened on their swords and went with David. There were 200 of his men who stayed with their supplies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 13:10; MARK 15:29"> One of Nabal’s servants found out what David and his men were planning to do, so he went to Nabal’s wife Abigail and said to her, “David sent some messengers from the desert to greet our master Nabal, but Nabal only yelled at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:7; 1 SAMUEL 25:21; PHILIPPIANS 2:15"> All the time that we were in the fields close to them, those men of David were very kind to us. They did not harm us. They did not steal anything from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 1:10; EXODUS 14:22; ZECHARIAH 2:5"> They protected us during the daytime and during the night. They were like a wall &#91;MET&#93; around us to protect us while we were taking care of our sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 20:7; DEUTERONOMY 13:13; 1 SAMUEL 20:9"> So now you should think about it and decide what you can do. If you do not do something, terrible things will happen to our master and to all his family. Nabal is an extremely wicked man, with the result that he will not heed anyone when that person tries to tell him anything that he should do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 16:1; MATTHEW 5:25; PROVERBS 6:4–5"> When Abigail heard that, she very quickly gathered 200 loaves of bread, and also got two leather bags full of wine, the meat from five sheep, a bushel of roasted grain, 100 packs of raisins, and 200 packs of dried figs. She put all those things on donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 32:20; GENESIS 32:16; PROVERBS 31:11–12"> Then she told her servants, “Go ahead of me. I will follow you.” But she did not tell her husband what she was going to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 KINGS 4:24"> David and his men had left the place where they were staying and were on the road to Nabal’s property. They were coming down a hill when they met Abigail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:13; EPHESIANS 4:31; ROMANS 12:21"> David had been saying to his men, “It was useless for us to protect that man and all his possessions here in this desert. We did not steal anything that belonged to him, but he has done evil to me in return for our good things we did for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:10; 1 SAMUEL 20:13; 1 SAMUEL 3:17"> I hope/desire that God will strike me and kill me &#91;IDM&#93; if he or even one of his men &#91;IDM&#93; is still alive tomorrow morning!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 20:41; JUDGES 1:14; JOSHUA 15:18"> When Abigail saw David, she quickly got down from her donkey and bowed before him, with her face touching the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esther 8:3; 1 SAMUEL 25:28; 2 SAMUEL 14:9"> Then she prostrated herself at David’s feet and said to him, “Sir, I deserve to be punished for what my husband has done. Please listen to what I say to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:17; MALACHI 2:2; 2 SAMUEL 13:33"> Please do not pay attention to &#91;IDM&#93; what this worthless man Nabal has said. His name means ‘fool’, and he surely is a foolish man. But I, who am willing to be your servant, did not see the messengers whom you sent to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 18:32; ROMANS 12:19–20; GENESIS 20:6"> Yahweh has prevented you from getting revenge on anyone and killing anyone. I hope/desire that as surely as Yahweh lives and as surely as you live, your enemies will be cursed like Nabal will be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 33:11; 1 SAMUEL 30:26; 2 KINGS 5:15"> I have brought a gift for you and for the men who are with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 18:17; 2 SAMUEL 7:11; 1 SAMUEL 24:11"> Please forgive me if I have done anything wrong to you. Yahweh will surely reward you by allowing many of your descendants to become kings of Israel, because you are fighting the battles that Yahweh wants you to fight. And I know that throughout all your life you have not done anything wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOHN 17:21; MATTHEW 10:29–30; MALACHI 3:17"> Even when those who are trying to kill you pursue you, you are safe, because Yahweh your God takes care of you. You will be protected as though you were &#91;MET&#93; a bundle that he has safely tied up. But your enemies will disappear as fast as stones that are hurled from a sling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 13:14; PSALMS 89:20; 1 SAMUEL 23:17"> Yahweh has promised to do good things for you, and he will do what he has promised. And he will cause you to become the ruler of the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 40:14; ROMANS 12:19; LUKE 23:42"> When that happens, you will be glad that you did not kill any people in Nabal’s household. You will not think that you deserve to be punished for having taken revenge yourself and killed innocent people. And when Yahweh enables you to successfully become the king, please do not forget to be kind to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 1:68; EXODUS 18:10; PSALMS 72:18"> David replied to Abigail, “I praise Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis worship, because he sent you to talk with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:26; PROVERBS 28:23; PROVERBS 9:9"> I hope/desire that Yahweh will bless you for being very wise. You have prevented me from taking revenge myself and killing many people &#91;MTY&#93; today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 11:11; 1 SAMUEL 25:26; JOSHUA 10:6"> Just as surely as Yahweh the God whom we Israelis worship is alive, he has prevented me from harming you. If you had not come quickly to talk to me, neither Nabal nor even one of Nabal’s men &#91;IDM&#93; would be still alive tomorrow morning.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GENESIS 19:21; 1 SAMUEL 20:42; 2 KINGS 5:19"> Then David accepted the gifts that Abigail had brought to him. He said to her, “I hope/desire that things will go well for you. I have heard what you said, and I will do what you have requested.”</span>
</div>

## Nabal died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:19; 2 SAMUEL 13:23; 2 SAMUEL 13:28"> When Abigail returned to Nabal, he was in his house, having a big celebration like kings have. He was very drunk and feeling very happy. So Abigail did not say anything to him that night about her meeting with David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:28; 1 SAMUEL 25:34; 1 SAMUEL 25:22"> The next morning, when he was no longer drunk, she told him everything that had happened when she talked with David. Immediately ◄he had a stroke/a blood vessel burst in his brain► and he became paralyzed &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:10; 2 KINGS 15:5; 1 SAMUEL 6:9"> About ten days later Yahweh struck him again and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:26; 1 SAMUEL 25:34; PROVERBS 22:23"> After David heard that Nabal was dead, he said, “Praise Yahweh! Nabal insulted me, but Yahweh ◄vindicated me/got revenge for me►. He has prevented me from doing anything wrong. And he has punished Nabal for the wrong that he did.” David married Abigail<br />Then David sent messengers to Abigail, to ask her if she would become his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="GENESIS 24:51; GENESIS 24:37–38"> His servants went to Carmel and said to Abigail, “David sent us to take you to become his wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:10; JOHN 13:3–5; RUTH 2:10"> Abigail bowed down with her face touching the ground. Then she told the messengers to tell David, “I am happy to become your wife. I will be your servant. And I am willing to wash the feet of your slaves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="GENESIS 24:61–67; PSALMS 45:10–11"> Abigail quickly got on her donkey and went with David’s messengers. Five of her maids went with her. When she arrived where David was, she became his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:5; JOSHUA 15:56; 1 SAMUEL 27:3"> David had previously married Ahinoam, a woman from Jezreel town near Carmel. So both Abigail and Ahinoam were now David’s wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:14; 1 SAMUEL 18:27; 1 SAMUEL 18:20"> King Saul’s daughter Michal was also David’s wife, but Saul had given her to Laish’s son Paltiel, who was from Gallim town.</span>
