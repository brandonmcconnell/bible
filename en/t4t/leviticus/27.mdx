## Making promises about gifts to Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:2; 1 SAMUEL 1:28; JUDGES 11:30–31"> “Tell the Israeli people that this is what I am saying to them: If anyone solemnly promises to dedicate another person to work solely for me, the person who is dedicated to me is allowed to be free from having to do that by someone paying to the priest a fixed amount of money. The money that is given must be calculated by comparing it with the official pieces of silver in the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 30:13; LEVITICUS 27:25; NUMBERS 18:16"> These are the amounts that must be paid: 50 pieces of silver for men who are between 20 and 60 years old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:9–10; ZECHARIAH 11:12–13; MATTHEW 26:15"> 30 pieces of silver for adult women who are between 20 and 60 years old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> 20 pieces of silver for young men who are between five and 20 years old, ten pieces of silver for young women who are between five and 20 years old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:40–43; NUMBERS 18:14–16"> five pieces of silver for boys who are between one month and five years old, three pieces of silver for girls who are between one month and five years old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 90:10"> 15 pieces of silver for men who are more than 60 years old, ten pieces of silver for women who are more than 60 years old.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARK 14:7; LEVITICUS 14:21–22; LUKE 21:1–4"> ‘If anyone who has made such a solemn promise is very poor and unable to pay, to free that person from doing what he had promised, he must take that person to the priest. The priest will set the price for freeing him to be what the person who made that solemn promise is able to pay.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> ‘If someone solemnly promises to give an animal that is acceptable to be an offering to me, that animal becomes sacred and belongs to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:15–33; JAMES 1:8"> The person who promised to give it must not give another animal instead of the one that he promised; he must not substitute a bad animal for a good one or even a better one than the one offered. If he would substitute one animal for the other, both animals would belong to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:18; MALACHI 1:14"> If the animal that he wishes to dedicate to me is a kind that is unacceptable to be an offering to me, he must take that animal to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The priest will then decide what its value is, according to the animal’s good and bad points. Whatever value the priests sets will be the value of the animal, and that is the price that the priest must pay for the animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:19; LEVITICUS 27:15; LEVITICUS 22:14"> If the man who gave the animal later decides that he wants to buy it back, he must pay to the priest that price plus an additional 20 percent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:14; LEVITICUS 25:29–31; LEVITICUS 27:21"> ‘Similarly, if someone dedicates his house to be a sacred gift to belong to me, the priest will decide how much it is worth, which will depend on whether the house is in good condition. Whatever the priest says that it is worth, that will be its value and that is the price that the priest must pay for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:13"> If the man who dedicated his house to me later wants to buy it back, he must pay that price plus an additional 20 percent, and then the house will belong to him again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:11–14; HOSEA 3:2; ACTS 4:34–37"> ‘If someone dedicates to me some of the property that belongs to him and his family, its value will be determined by the number of bushels of seed that will be needed to plant seeds on that land: Its value will be ten pieces of silver for each bushel of seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> If he dedicates the land during the Year of Celebration, its full value will be that amount.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:15–16; LEVITICUS 25:51–52; LEVITICUS 25:27"> But if he dedicates the field after the Year of Celebration, the priest will count the number of years until the next Year of Celebration, and if there are not many years that remain, the price will be much lower than the full price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:13"> If the person who dedicated the field later wants to buy it back, he must pay to the priest the price that the priest says it is worth, plus an added one-fifth, and then the field will belong to that man again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> However, if he does not buy it back, or if it has been sold (OR, the priest has sold it) to someone else, that person will never be permitted to buy it back again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:29; NUMBERS 18:14; LEVITICUS 25:31"> In the Year of Celebration, it will become sacred again, and it will be given to the priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:10; LEVITICUS 25:25"> ‘If someone dedicates to me some land that he has bought, land which is not part of the land that his family has always owned,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:18; LEVITICUS 27:12"> the priest will count the number of years until the next Year of Celebration to determine how much it is worth, and the man must pay that amount to the priest on that day, and then that land will belong to that man again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:28; LEVITICUS 27:20"> But in the Year of Celebration, the land will again be owned by the person from whom he bought it, the person whose family had always owned that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 30:13; NUMBERS 18:16; NUMBERS 3:47"> All the silver that is paid must be compared with the official pieces of silver in the Sacred Tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 13:2; EXODUS 13:12–13; NUMBERS 18:17"> ‘No one is permitted to dedicate to me the firstborn of any cow or sheep, because the firstborn already belongs to me &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:11"> If someone gives to me a kind of animal that is not acceptable to me, that person may later buy it back by paying what it is worth plus an additional 20 percent of its value. If he does not buy it back, it must be sold (OR, the priest must sell it) for its standard price.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:17–19; LEVITICUS 27:21; EXODUS 22:20"> ‘However, no slave or animal or family land that someone owns can be sold or bought back after it has been dedicated to me and no price has been paid for it. That kind of gift belongs to me permanently/forever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:18–23; NUMBERS 21:2–3"> ‘No person who has done something that I consider to be very wicked is permitted to be freed from being punished; that person must surely be executed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:23; Nehemiah 13:12; GENESIS 28:22"> ‘One tenth of all the crops and grain or fruit that is produced on anyone’s land is sacred and belongs to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> If anyone wants to buy back any of that tenth, he must pay to the priest what it is worth plus an additional 20 percent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:13; EZEKIEL 20:37; MICAH 7:14"> One of every ten domestic animals belongs to me. When a shepherd counts his animals &#91;MTY&#93; to decide which ones he will give to me, he must mark every tenth one as belonging to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:10"> When he does that, he must not pick out the good ones for himself or leave the bad ones, or substitute bad ones for good ones. If he substitutes one animal for another, both animals will belong to me, and he will not be permitted to buy them back (OR, the shepherd cannot buy them back).’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:46; NUMBERS 1:1; HEBREWS 12:18–25"> Those are the commands that Yahweh gave to Moses/me on Sinai Mountain to tell to the people.</span>
</div>
</div>
