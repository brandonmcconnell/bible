## The fellowship offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:14; LEVITICUS 1:3; ROMANS 5:1–2"> “Also tell the people, ‘When the offering that you bring to Yahweh is to maintain good fellowship with him, you may bring a bull or a cow from your herd of cattle, but what you present to Yahweh must be an animal that has no defects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 29:10; LEVITICUS 1:4–5; ACTS 2:36–38"> You must bring the animal to the entrance to the Sacred Tent. You must lay your hands on its head. Then you must slaughter it and drain some of its blood in a bowl. Then one of Aaron’s sons, one of the priests, will sprinkle the blood against all sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 29:13; LEVITICUS 4:8–9; EXODUS 29:22"> From that offering, you must offer to Yahweh, as a sacrifice that will be burned in the fire, all the fat that covers the inner parts of the animal, or which is attached to them—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:10; LEVITICUS 8:25; EXODUS 29:22"> the kidneys and the fat that is attached to them near the lower back muscle, and the fat that covers the liver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:12; EXODUS 29:13; 1 PETER 2:5"> Then one of the priests will burn those things on the altar, along with the other parts of the animal that will be completely burned to be an offering to Yahweh. And the aroma while it burns will be pleasing to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="TITUS 2:11–12; GALATIANS 3:28; LEVITICUS 1:2"> If that offering to maintain good fellowship with Yahweh is a sheep or a goat, it must also be an animal that has no defects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:62; EPHESIANS 5:12; LEVITICUS 17:8–9"> If you offer a lamb, you must present it to Yahweh at the entrance to the Sacred Tent. You must lay your hands on the lamb’s head and then slaughter it. ‘You must drain some of its blood in a bowl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:5; EPHESIANS 3:12; LEVITICUS 3:13"> Then one of the priests will sprinkle that blood against all sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 29:22; LEVITICUS 9:19; LEVITICUS 8:25"> You must dedicate these things to be a sacrifice to Yahweh that is burned: Its fat, the fat tail that you must cut off close to the backbone, and all the fat that covers the inner parts of the lamb or which is attached to them—</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:4"> the kidneys with the fat that is on them near the lower back muscle, and the fat that covers the liver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:17; LEVITICUS 21:6; LEVITICUS 3:16"> One of the priests will burn those things on the altar to be an offering to Yahweh; it will be as though they will be a special food given to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:15; Isaiah 53:2; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:21"> ‘If your offering is a goat, you must take/present it to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:8; ROMANS 5:15–21; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:21"> You must lay your hands on its head. Then you must slaughter it in front of the Sacred Tent. Then one of Aaron’s sons will sprinkle the blood against all sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:38; MATTHEW 22:37; LEVITICUS 3:9–11"> From that offering you must dedicate these things to be a sacrifice to Yahweh that is burned: All the fat that covers the inner parts of the animal or which is attached to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> the kidneys with the fat that is on them near the lower back muscle, and the fat that covers the liver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:23–25; MATTHEW 22:37; Isaiah 53:10"> The priest will burn those things on the altar to be an offering to Yahweh; it will be as though they will be a special food given to Yahweh. And the aroma while it burns will be pleasing to Yahweh. All the fat of the animals that are sacrificed belongs to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 9:4; MATTHEW 26:28; DEUTERONOMY 15:23"> ‘This is a command that must be obeyed by you and your descendants forever, wherever you live: You must not eat the fat or the blood of any animal.’ ”</span>
</div>
