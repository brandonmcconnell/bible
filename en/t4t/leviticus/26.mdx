## Promised blessings for obeying Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:4; DEUTERONOMY 27:15; NUMBERS 33:52"> Yahweh also said this to Moses/me on Sinai Mountain: “Do not make idols or set up statues of false gods or sacred stones to worship other gods. And do not put on your property a stone that has been shaped to resemble a false god and bow down to worship it. You must worship only me, Yahweh, your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:30"> “Honor the Sabbath days and revere my Sacred Tent, because I, Yahweh, live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; DEUTERONOMY 11:13–15; DEUTERONOMY 28:1–14"> “If you carefully obey all my commands &#91;DOU&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:12; PSALMS 67:6; LEVITICUS 25:21"> I will send rain for you at the proper/right times, in order that crops will grow on your land and there will be plenty of fruit on your trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="AMOS 9:13; JOEL 2:26; JOEL 2:19"> You will continue harvesting and threshing grain until it is time to harvest grapes, and you will continue to harvest grapes until it is time to start planting things in the following year. And you will have all the food that you want to eat, and you will live safely in your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9; ZEPHANIAH 3:13; PSALMS 147:14"> “If you obey all my laws, there will be peace in your country, and when you lie down to sleep, nothing will cause you to be afraid. I will get rid of the dangerous animals in your country, and there will no wars &#91;MTY&#93; in your country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> You will pursue your enemies and kill them with your swords:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:10; DEUTERONOMY 28:7; DEUTERONOMY 32:30"> Five of you will pursue 100 of them, and 100 of you will pursue 1,000 of them and kill them with your swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 17:6–7; Nehemiah 9:23; EZEKIEL 16:62"> “If you obey all my laws, I will bless you and cause you to have many children &#91;DOU&#93;. And I will do what I said that I would do in the agreement that I made with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:22; 2 KINGS 19:29; LUKE 12:17"> When you are still eating food from the harvest from the previous year, it will be necessary for you to throw away some of it to make space for the new harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 25:8; REVELATION 21:3; EXODUS 29:45"> I will live among you in my Sacred Tent, and I will never reject you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:16; JEREMIAH 30:22; JEREMIAH 7:23"> I will walk among you and continue to be your God, and you will continue to be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:27; LEVITICUS 25:38; PSALMS 116:16"> I am Yahweh your God, the one who brought you out of Egypt in order that you would no longer be slaves of the people of Egypt. When you were there, you were treated as though you were animals that worked for the people of Egypt, but &#91;MET&#93; I broke the bars of the ◄yokes/frames that were put around your necks► and enabled you to walk with your heads up.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Threatened punishments for disobeying Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:15–68; LEVITICUS 26:18; JEREMIAH 17:27"> “But if you will not heed me and obey all these commands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:15; DEUTERONOMY 31:16; ZECHARIAH 7:11–13"> and if you reject &#91;DOU&#93; my decrees and laws, and do not do &#91;DOU&#93; what I commanded you to do in the agreement that I made with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 31:8; JEREMIAH 5:17; MICAH 6:15"> these are the things that I will do to you: I will suddenly cause there to be disasters that will ruin you. You will have diseases that cannot be cured, and fevers that will cause you to become blind and will slowly kill you. It will be useless for you to plant seeds, because your enemies will eat the crops that grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 17:10; PROVERBS 28:1; PSALMS 53:5"> I will reject/abandon you &#91;IDM&#93;, with the result that you will be defeated by your enemies. Then those who hate you will rule you, and you will be so terrified that you will run away even when no one is pursuing you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:21; LEVITICUS 26:28; LEVITICUS 26:24"> “If you still will not heed me after all those things happen to you, I will continue to punish you again and again for the sins that you have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:23; 1 KINGS 17:1; DANIEL 4:37"> I will cause you to no longer be stubborn and proud; I will cause no rain to fall; it will be as though &#91;MET&#93; the sky is made of iron; and the ground will be as hard as brass &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 127:1; DEUTERONOMY 11:17; Isaiah 49:4"> You will uselessly work very hard to plant seeds, because crops will not grow in the hard soil in your fields, and fruit will not grow on your trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:18; LEVITICUS 26:27"> “If you continue to oppose me and refuse to heed me, I will cause you to experience disasters again and again, like you deserve to experience because of your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:15; DEUTERONOMY 32:24; JUDGES 5:6"> I will send wild animals to attack you, and they will kill your little children and destroy your cattle. There will be very few of you who will remain alive, with the result that there will be very few &#91;HYP&#93; people walking on the roads in your country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:3; JEREMIAH 2:30; AMOS 4:6–12"> “When you experience those things that I do to punish you, if you still do not pay attention to me, and if you continue to oppose me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 18:26; 2 SAMUEL 22:27; JOB 9:4"> I myself will oppose you, and I will punish you for your sins again and again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:12; EZEKIEL 5:17; JEREMIAH 29:17–18"> I will send foreign armies &#91;MTY&#93; to punish you for not doing the things that I commanded you to do in the agreement that I made with you. If you try to escape from your enemies by shutting yourselves in your cities that have walls around them, I will cause you to experience plagues, and I will allow your enemies to capture &#91;IDM&#93; you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MICAH 6:14; Isaiah 3:1; PSALMS 105:16"> When I cut off your food supply, there will be very little flour with which to make bread. As a result, ten women will be able to bake all their bread in only one oven. When the bread is baked, each woman will divide it among the members of her family, but there will be very little for each one, and when they have eaten all of it, they will still be hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:24; LEVITICUS 26:21"> “After all those things happen, if you still do not heed me, if you still oppose me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:18; JEREMIAH 21:5; Isaiah 66:15"> I will be very angry with you, and I will oppose you, and I myself will punish you for your sins again and again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 5:10; LAMENTATIONS 4:10; MATTHEW 24:19"> You will be extremely hungry, with the result that you will kill your sons and daughters and eat their flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 KINGS 23:20; Isaiah 27:9; EZEKIEL 6:13"> I will cause the shrines where you worship idols on hilltops to be destroyed. I will smash the altars where you burn incense to worship your gods, and I will cause your corpses to be piled on the lifeless statues of your gods. And I will detest you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:3; LAMENTATIONS 1:10; HEBREWS 10:26"> I will cause your cities to become heaps of ruins, and I will cause the buildings that are dedicated to the worship of your gods to be smashed. And I will not be pleased at all with the aroma of your offerings that are burned on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:11; JEREMIAH 19:8; JEREMIAH 18:16"> I will completely ruin your country, with the result that even your enemies who capture it will be shocked/appalled when they see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 7:14; EZEKIEL 20:23; DEUTERONOMY 28:64–66"> I will enable your enemies to kill you with their swords, and I will cause the rest of you to be scattered among other nations. Your country will be ruined and your cities will become ruins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:21; LEVITICUS 26:43; LEVITICUS 25:2–4"> After that happens, as long as you are living in your enemies’ countries, your land will be allowed to rest, like you should have allowed it to rest every seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 24:5–6; ROMANS 8:22"> All the time that your land is desolate, it will be able to rest, like you did not enable it to rest during all the years that you lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EZEKIEL 21:7; Isaiah 30:17; LEVITICUS 26:17"> “As for you people who will remain alive in the countries to which your enemies will have taken you, I will cause you to become so afraid that when you hear the sound of leaves being blown by the wind, you will run away. You will run like &#91;SIM&#93; you are running from a man carrying a sword, and you will fall down, even though no one is pursuing you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:12–13; JUDGES 2:14; JEREMIAH 37:10"> You will stumble over each other. So you will not be able to stand and fight your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:48; Isaiah 27:13; JEREMIAH 42:17–18"> Many of you will die in the countries that belong to your enemies &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:17; EZEKIEL 24:23; EZEKIEL 33:10"> And those of you who remain alive will slowly die and rot in the countries of your enemies because of your sins and because of the sins of your ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:13; Nehemiah 9:2–5; 1 JOHN 1:8–10"> “But your descendants must confess their sins and the sins that their ancestors committed. Their ancestors acted unfaithfully toward me and were hostile to me, with the result that I forced them to go to the countries that belonged to their enemies. But when your descendants humble themselves &#91;SYN&#93; and stop being very stubborn &#91;IDM&#93; and accept being punished for their sins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 106:45; EXODUS 6:5; EZEKIEL 16:60"> I will remember the agreement that I made with your ancestors Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, and what I promised them about this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="ROMANS 8:7; LEVITICUS 26:15; JOHN 7:7"> But before that happens, you will be forced to leave your land, with the result that the land will be able to rest while it is desolate and while the people are being punished for rejecting my laws and hating my decrees &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="ROMANS 11:2; Nehemiah 9:31; DEUTERONOMY 4:29–31"> But when that happens, I will still not reject them or hate them and destroy them completely. I will not cancel the agreement that I made with them; for I am still Yahweh their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:38; EZEKIEL 20:22; EZEKIEL 20:9"> I will ◄remember/never forget► the agreement that I made with your ancestors when I brought them out of Egypt, in order that the people of all nations will know that I, Yahweh, will continue to be your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:34; DEUTERONOMY 12:1; DEUTERONOMY 6:1"> Those are the decrees, the laws, and the regulations &#91;DOU&#93; that Yahweh established on Sinai Mountain between himself and the Israeli people by giving them to Moses/me to tell to them.</span>
</div>
</div>
