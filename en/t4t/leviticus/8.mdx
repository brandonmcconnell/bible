## Aaron and his sons were appointed to be priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 28:2–4; EXODUS 39:41; HEBREWS 7:27"> “Bring Aaron and his sons, and bring their ◄special clothes/clothes that they will wear while they do the work of priests►, the oil for anointing them, the bull to be offered to cause them to become forgiven for the sins they have committed, the two rams to be slaughtered, and the basket containing bread made without yeast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 30:13; ACTS 2:1; Nehemiah 8:1"> Then gather all the people at the entrance to the Sacred Tent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 39:26; EXODUS 39:7; EXODUS 39:5"> Moses/I did what Yahweh told him/me to do, and all the people gathered there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 29:4–37"> Then Moses/I said to the people, “This is what Yahweh has commanded us to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 6:11; EPHESIANS 5:26; EXODUS 29:4"> Then he/I brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 28:4; Isaiah 61:10; GALATIANS 3:27"> He/I put the special tunic/shirt on Aaron, tied the sash/waistband around him, put on him the special robe, and put on him the sacred vest/apron. He/I fastened the sacred vest/apron around him, using the skillfully woven sash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 2:63; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:8; Isaiah 59:17"> He/I put the sacred pouch on his chest and put into it the two stones for him to use to find out what God wants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:9–11; ZECHARIAH 6:11–14; EXODUS 28:36–38"> Then he/I wrapped the turban around Aaron’s head, and fastened on the front of it the gold ornament, the object that showed that he was dedicated to God, like Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 40:9–11; LEVITICUS 8:2; EXODUS 30:23–29"> Then Moses/I took the olive oil and anointed the sacred tent and everything in it, and dedicated them to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="TITUS 3:6; EXODUS 30:29; EXODUS 16:19"> He/I sprinkled some of the oil on the altar seven times. He/I anointed the altar and all the things used with it, and its huge washbasin and its stand, to dedicate them to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 30:30; PSALMS 133:2; EXODUS 29:7"> He/I poured some of the oil on Aaron’s head and anointed him to dedicate him to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 29:8–9; REVELATION 5:10; REVELATION 1:6"> Then he/I brought forward Aaron’s sons. He/I put tunics/shirts on them, tied sashes/waistbands around them, and wrapped turbans around their heads, like Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:19; PSALMS 66:15; 1 PETER 3:18"> Then he/I brought the bull for the offering to cause people to be forgiven for the sins that have committed. Then Aaron and his sons put their hands on the bull’s head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:7; HEBREWS 9:18–23; EZEKIEL 43:19–27"> Then Moses/I slaughtered the bull, caught some of its blood in a bowl, and with his/my finger put some of that blood on the projections at the corners of the altar, to purify the altar. He/I poured the rest of the blood at the base of the altar. By doing that, he/I caused it to be a suitable place for burning sacrifices for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 29:13; LEVITICUS 3:3–5; LEVITICUS 4:8–9"> Moses/I took all the fat that covers the inner parts of the animal, including the liver and kidneys, and burned them on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:11–12; EXODUS 29:14; LEVITICUS 6:30"> He/I took the rest of the bull, including the hide and intestines, and burned them outside the camp, like Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:2; LEVITICUS 1:4–13; EXODUS 29:15–18"> Then he/I brought the ram for the offering that would be completely burned on the altar, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then Moses/I slaughtered the ram and sprinkled its blood on all sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:8"> He/I cut the ram into pieces and washed the inner parts and hind legs.Then he/I put the head, the fat, and the other parts of the ram on the altar. As it burned, the aroma of the smoke was pleasing to Yahweh. It was an offering to Yahweh that was burned, as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:2; LEVITICUS 8:29; EXODUS 29:19–31"> Then he/I brought the other ram, the one for consecrating the priests, and Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 29:20; LEVITICUS 14:14; ROMANS 12:1"> Moses/I slaughtered that ram, drained some of its blood in a bowl, and put some of that blood on the lobes of the right ears, the thumbs of the right hands, and the big toes of the right feet of Aaron and his sons to indicate that what they listened to and what they did and where they went should be directed by Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:9; LEVITICUS 3:3–5; Isaiah 53:10"> He/I picked up all the fat of the ram, its fat tail, the fat that covered the inner parts, including the fat that covered the liver and kidneys, and the right thigh of the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 29:23; 1 TIMOTHY 2:5; ACTS 5:12"> Then from the basket containing the bread that was made without yeast, the bread which had been dedicated to Yahweh, he/I picked up one loaf of bread made without olive oil, and one loaf that was made by mixing the flour with olive oil, and one wafer. He/I put those on top of the portions of fat, and put them into the hands of Aaron and his sons. Then they lifted them up in the presence of Yahweh to indicate that it was an offering that belonged to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 29:25; PSALMS 22:13–14; HEBREWS 10:14–22"> Then Moses/I took those things from their hands and burned them on the altar. That was the offering that was burned to appoint Aaron and his sons as priests. And the aroma while it burned was pleasing to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:31; 1 PETER 4:11; LEVITICUS 7:30–34"> Moses/I also took the breast of the second ram and lifted it up, as Yahweh had commanded. The breast was Moses’/my share of the ram that was sacrificed to dedicate the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 29:21; NUMBERS 3:3; EXODUS 30:30"> Then Moses/I took some of the olive oil for anointing Aaron and his sons, and some of the blood that was on the altar and sprinkled it on Aaron and his sons and on their clothes. By doing that, he/I consecrated Aaron and his sons and their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 29:31–32; GALATIANS 2:20; 1 SAMUEL 2:13–17"> Then Moses/I said to Aaron and his sons, “Boil the meat of the second ram at the entrance of the Sacred Tent, and eat it there with the bread that is in the basket, like I told you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 29:34; EXODUS 12:10; HEBREWS 3:13–14"> Burn up any of the meat and bread that remains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 29:35; NUMBERS 19:12; EZEKIEL 43:25–27"> The time for you to be set apart as priests will be seven days, so do not leave the entrance of the Sacred Tent for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:27; HEBREWS 7:16; HEBREWS 10:11–12"> What we have done today is what Yahweh commanded to cause that you will be forgiven for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:7; DEUTERONOMY 11:1; 1 KINGS 2:3"> You must stay at the entrance of the Sacred Tent for seven days and seven nights and do what Yahweh requires, in order that you will not die because of disobeying him. I am telling you that because that is what Yahweh has commanded me to tell you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:32; DEUTERONOMY 4:2; EXODUS 40:16"> So Aaron and his sons did everything that Yahweh told Moses/me to tell them.</span>
</div>
