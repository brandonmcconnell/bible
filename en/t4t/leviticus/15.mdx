## Discharges from bodies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="AMOS 3:7; LEVITICUS 13:1; LEVITICUS 11:1"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me and Aaron,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:2; 2 SAMUEL 3:29; LEVITICUS 22:4"> “Tell this to the Israelis: When an infected discharge comes out of a man’s sexual organs &#91;EUP&#93;, that fluid should not be touched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:20; EZEKIEL 16:26; LEVITICUS 12:3"> Whether that fluid is blocked or continues to drip, it causes him to be someone who should not be touched.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="TITUS 1:15; EPHESIANS 5:11; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:33"> “And any bed that such a man lies on should not be touched, and anything that he sits on should not be touched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:25; LEVITICUS 16:26; LEVITICUS 17:15"> Anyone who touches that person’s bed must wash his clothes and bathe, and no one should touch him until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JAMES 4:8; Isaiah 1:16"> Anyone who sits on something that man has sat on must wash his clothes and bathe, and no one should touch him until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> “Anyone who touches such a man must wash his clothes and bathe, and no one should touch him until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDE 1:4; 2 PETER 2:1–3; Isaiah 1:16"> “If such a man spits on someone else, that person who is spit upon should not be touched. He must wash his clothes, and no one should touch him until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 31:34"> “Everything that a man who has such a discharge of fluid sits on while he is riding a horse or a donkey should not be touched,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JAMES 4:8; PSALMS 26:6; LEVITICUS 15:8"> and anyone who touches the seat or saddle that he was sitting on also should not be touched until that evening. And anyone who picks up the seat or saddle must wash his clothes and bathe, and no one should touch him until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Anyone whom the man with such a discharge of fluid from his body touches without first rinsing his hands in water must wash his clothes and bathe, and no one should touch him until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:28; LEVITICUS 11:32–33; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:1"> “If such a man touches a clay pot, that pot must be broken into pieces. Anything made from wood that he touches must be rinsed in water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:8; LEVITICUS 8:33; LEVITICUS 15:28"> “If such a man is healed from his flow of fluid, he must wait for seven days. Then he must wash his clothes and bathe in water from a spring. Then he will be able to be with others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 12:8; LEVITICUS 12:6; HEBREWS 10:10"> On the eighth/next day, he must take two doves or two young pigeons and come in front of Yahweh at the entrance of the Sacred Tent, and give them to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:30–31; LEVITICUS 4:26; MATTHEW 3:17"> The priest will sacrifice them. One will be an offering for the man to become acceptable to God, and the other one will be completely burned on the altar. As a result of the priest doing that, the man will be forgiven for having sinned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:10–11; LEVITICUS 22:4; LEVITICUS 15:5"> “Then semen accidentally flows from a man’s sexual organs &#91;EUP&#93;, he must bathe his whole body, and no one should touch him until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Any clothing or leather that has semen on it must be washed, and no one should touch it until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:5; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:3–5; 1 CORINTHIANS 6:12"> When a man has sex with a woman and some semen spills, both of them must bathe, and no one should touch them until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 12:2; MATTHEW 15:19; MARK 5:25"> “Then a woman has her monthly menstrual period, no one should touch her for seven days. If anyone touches her during that time, no one should touch the person who touched her until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:4–9; PROVERBS 2:16–19; PROVERBS 6:35"> No one should touch anything that she lies on or sits on during that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="REVELATION 7:14; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:1; Isaiah 22:14"> Anyone who touches her bed must wash his clothes and bathe, and no one should touch that person until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Anyone who touches something that she has been sitting on, a bed or anything else, must wash his clothes, and no one should touch that person until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:18; 1 PETER 2:11; LEVITICUS 18:19"> “If a man has sex with a woman during that time and some of the menstrual blood touches him, no one should touch him for seven days, and no one should touch the bed that he lies on.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MARK 5:25; MATTHEW 9:20; LUKE 8:43"> “Then a woman has a vaginal discharge of blood for ◄many days/a long time►, which is not her monthly flow of blood, or if her flow of blood continues after her monthly flow ends, no one should touch her until it stops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And no one should touch the bed that she lies on or anything that she sits on while that flow of blood continues, just like during her monthly menstrual period.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:21; HEBREWS 10:22; LEVITICUS 17:15–16"> Anyone who touches those things should not touch anyone else; he must wash his clothes and bathe, and he should not touch anyone else until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:13–15; GALATIANS 3:13; EPHESIANS 1:6–7"> “After a woman’s monthly menstrual flow ends, she must wait seven days before she touches anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> On the eighth/next day, she must take two doves or two pigeons to the priest at the entrance of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The priest will sacrifice one of them to be an offering to enable her to become acceptable to Yahweh again, and completely burn the other one on the altar. By doing that he will cause me, Yahweh, to forgive her for having sinned &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:20; NUMBERS 19:13; EZEKIEL 5:11"> “You must warn the Israeli people not to do things that ◄defile them/cause them to be unacceptable to me►. If they become ◄unacceptable to me/defiled►, they will cause my Sacred Tent which is among them to become an unacceptable place to worship me, and as a result they will die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:46; LEVITICUS 13:59; NUMBERS 6:13"> “Those are the regulations for a man who has a fluid that flows from his sexual organs or who should not be touched because some of his semen spills out while having sex with a woman,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:19; LEVITICUS 20:18; LEVITICUS 15:24–25"> and for any woman during her monthly menstrual period, and for any man who has sex with a woman during her menstrual period.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
