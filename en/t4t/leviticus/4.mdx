## The sin offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 19:12; HEBREWS 9:7; LEVITICUS 4:27"> “Say this to the Israeli people: This is what must be done if someone sins without intending to sin, doing something that is forbidden in any of Yahweh’s commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:27–28; LEVITICUS 4:14; LEVITICUS 9:2"> 'If the Supreme &#91;MTY&#93; Priest sins, and that causes all the people to be guilty, he must bring to Yahweh a young bull that has no defects. That will be an offering for the sin that he has committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:3–4; DANIEL 9:26; Isaiah 53:6"> He must bring the bull to the entrance to the Sacred Tent. He must lay his hands on its head. Then he must slaughter it in front of Yahweh and drain some of the blood into a bowl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:14; NUMBERS 19:4; LEVITICUS 4:16–17"> Then the Supreme Priest must take some of that blood into the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:14; LEVITICUS 14:18; LEVITICUS 26:28"> He must dip one of his fingers into the blood and sprinkle it seven times in the presence of Yahweh, in front of the curtain that separates the Holy Place from the Very Holy Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:15; LEVITICUS 9:9; LEVITICUS 16:18"> Then he must put some of the blood on the projections at the corners of the altar where fragrant incense is burned in the Sacred Tent in the presence of Yahweh. The remaining part of the bull’s blood that is still in the bowl, he must pour out at the base of the altar, where sacrifices are burned, at the entrance to the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:3–5; LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 4:31"> From that offering the Supreme Priest must separate these things from the bull that is to be burned: The fat that covers the inner parts of the bull or which is attached to them—</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:4"> the kidneys and the fat that is attached to them near the lower back muscle, and the fat that covers the liver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then the Supreme Priest must completely burn those things on the altar. That will be just like when the fat is removed from an animal that is sacrificed to maintain good fellowship with Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:5; EXODUS 29:14; LEVITICUS 9:8–11"> But all the other parts of the animal—its skin/hide and all its other meat, its head and its legs, its inner parts and the intestines,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:11; EXODUS 29:14; LEVITICUS 16:27"> he must take outside the camp and throw them in a place that is acceptable to Yahweh, where the ashes are thrown, and he must burn them in a fire on the pile of ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:17; LEVITICUS 5:2–5; HOSEA 5:15"> ‘If all the Israeli people sin without intending to sin, doing something that is forbidden in any of Yahweh’s commands, they will be guilty, even if they do not realize that they have sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:23; LEVITICUS 4:3; LEVITICUS 4:28"> When they realize that they have committed a sin, together they must bring a young bull to be an offering for their sin, to the front of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:4; LEVITICUS 16:21; LEVITICUS 8:14"> The elders must lay their hands on the bull’s head in the presence of Yahweh and slaughter it and catch some of the blood in a bowl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:5–12; HEBREWS 9:12–14"> Then the Supreme &#91;MTY&#93; Priest must take some of that blood into the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:6–7"> ‘He must dip one of his fingers into the blood and sprinkle it seven times in the presence of Yahweh, in front of the curtain that separates the Holy Place from the Very Holy Place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:7"> Then he must put some of the blood on the projections at the corners of the altar that is in the presence of Yahweh in the Sacred Tent. The remaining part of the bull’s blood, he must pour out at the base of the altar where sacrifices are burned, at the entrance of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:6; LEVITICUS 4:26; NUMBERS 15:25"> He must remove all the animal’s fat and burn it on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:25; HEBREWS 10:10–12; LEVITICUS 12:8"> He must do with this bull the same things that he did with the bull that was an offering for his own sins, and they will be forgiven &#123;Yahweh will forgive them&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:15; MATTHEW 20:28; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:21"> Then the priest must take the other parts of the bull outside the camp and burn them, like he does when he himself has sinned. That will be the offering for the sin that all the people have committed, and they will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:2; LEVITICUS 4:13; NUMBERS 31:13"> ‘When one of the leaders sins without intending to sin, doing something that is forbidden in any of the commands of Yahweh his God, he will be guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:14; ROMANS 8:3; NUMBERS 7:22"> When he realizes that he has committed a sin, he must bring as his offering a male goat that has no defects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:13; LEVITICUS 7:2; LEVITICUS 4:3–35"> He must lay his hands on the goat’s head in the presence of Yahweh and slaughter it at the place where they slaughter &#123;are slaughtered&#125; the animals that will be completely burned on the altar. That will be an offering for his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:7; LEVITICUS 4:18; LEVITICUS 4:30"> Then the priest must put some of the animal’s blood into a bowl and dip one of his fingers in it and put some of the blood on the corners of the projections of the altar. Then he must pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:20; LEVITICUS 4:31; LEVITICUS 4:35"> Then he must burn all the fat on the altar, as was done with the fat of the offering to maintain good fellowship with Yahweh. As a result of the priest’s doing that, the leader will no longer be guilty for his sin and he will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:2; NUMBERS 15:27; LEVITICUS 4:13"> ‘If one of the Israeli people who is not a priest sins without intending to sin, and does something that is forbidden in any of the commands of Yahweh his God, he will be guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:23; LEVITICUS 4:3; LEVITICUS 4:32"> When he realizes that he has committed a sin, he must bring as his offering a female goat that has no defects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:4; LEVITICUS 4:24; LEVITICUS 1:4"> He must lay his hands on the goat’s head and slaughter it at the place where they slaughter the animals that will be completely burned on the altar, and catch some of the blood in a bowl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:7; LEVITICUS 4:25; LEVITICUS 4:34"> Then the priest must dip one of his fingers in the blood, and put some of it on the projections at the corners of the altar. Then he must pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 4:35; LEVITICUS 1:9"> Then he must remove all the goat’s fat, and burn all the fat on the altar, like was done with the fat of the offering to maintain fellowship with Yahweh. And the aroma while it burns will be pleasing to Yahweh. As a result of the priest doing that, the person will no longer be guilty for his sin, and he will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:28; 1 PETER 3:18; 1 PETER 2:22"> ‘If that person brings a lamb to be his offering for sin, he must bring a female lamb that has no defects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:29–31; LEVITICUS 4:4"> He must lay his hands on the lamb’s head and slaughter it at the place where they slaughter the animals that will be completely burned on the altar, and catch some of the blood in a bowl.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:30; LEVITICUS 4:25; LEVITICUS 4:7"> Then the priest must dip one of his fingers in the blood and put some of it on the projections at the corners of the altar. Then he must pour out the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 4:20; LEVITICUS 14:18"> Then he must remove all the lamb’s fat, and burn all the fat on the altar, like was done with the fat of the offering to maintain fellowship with Yahweh. He must burn it on top of the other offerings to Yahweh that are being burned. As a result, the priest will request God to forgive that person for his sin, and he will be forgiven.’”</span>
