<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:17; PROVERBS 29:24; LEVITICUS 19:8"> Yahweh also said to tell the people, “If a judge orders any of you to tell in court something that you have seen or something that you heard someone say, if you refuse to say what you know is true, you must ◄pay a penalty/be punished► &#91;MTY&#93; for refusing to tell what you know.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:17; DEUTERONOMY 14:8; Haggai 2:13"> If you accidentally touch something which God considers impure &#91;DOU&#93;, such as the carcass of a wild animal or the carcass of a bull or cow, or of an animal that scurries across the ground, you must ◄pay a penalty/be punished►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:11–16; LEVITICUS 15:1–33; LEVITICUS 12:1–8"> If you touch any human feces, even if you do not intend to do that, when you realize what you have done, you must ◄pay a penalty/be punished►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:2–6; ACTS 23:12; JUDGES 11:31"> If you carelessly make a solemn promise to do something that is good or that is bad, when you realize what you have done, you must ◄pay a penalty/be punished►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:13; NUMBERS 5:7; LEVITICUS 26:40"> If you are guilty of committing any of those sins, you must confess what you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:28; LEVITICUS 4:32; LEVITICUS 4:20"> And for a penalty you must bring to Yahweh a female lamb or female goat, to be an offering for the sin that you have committed, and the priest will sacrifice it, and then you will no longer be guilty for your sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 12:8; LEVITICUS 14:21–22; MATTHEW 3:16"> If you are poor and cannot afford to bring a lamb, you must bring to Yahweh two doves or two young pigeons. One will be an offering for your sin and one will be an offering that will be completely burned on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:15; LEVITICUS 1:17; ROMANS 4:25"> You must bring them to the priest. First he will offer one of them to be an offering for your sin. He will wring/twist its neck to kill it, but he must not pull off its head completely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:30; LEVITICUS 4:34; LEVITICUS 4:18"> Then he must sprinkle some of the blood against the side of the altar. The remaining blood must be drained out at the base of the altar. That will be an offering for your sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:14–17; LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 4:20"> The priest will then do what I have commanded and offer the other bird to be completely burned on the altar. Then you will no longer be guilty for the sin you have committed, and Yahweh will forgive you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:15; LEVITICUS 2:1–2; EXODUS 16:36"> However, if you are very poor and cannot afford two doves or two young pigeons, you must bring to be an offering for your sin ◄2 pounds/1 kg.► of fine flour. You must not put olive oil or incense on it, because it is an offering for sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:35; LEVITICUS 2:9; LEVITICUS 2:2"> You must take it to the priest. He will take a handful of it, to symbolize that the whole offering truly belongs to Yahweh, and burn it on the altar, on top of the other offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:3; LEVITICUS 4:26; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:13"> And by doing that, the priest will enable you to no longer be guilty for any of the sins that you have committed, and you will be forgiven. The part of the offering that is not burned will belong to the priest, like was true for the offerings made from grain.”</span>
</div>

## The guilt offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 30:13; LEVITICUS 4:2; LEVITICUS 6:6"> “When you sin, without intending to, by not giving to Yahweh the things that you are required to give to him, you must pay a penalty by bringing to Yahweh a ram that has no defects. You must determine how much silver it is worth, by using the official standard in the temple. It will be an offering to cause you to no longer be guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:14; NUMBERS 5:7; LEVITICUS 27:27"> But you must also make restitution for what you failed to give to Yahweh, adding one-fifth of its value. You must give that to the priest. He will offer the ram as a sacrifice for the sin that you have committed, and cause you to no longer be guilty; and you will be forgiven.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:27; LEVITICUS 4:13; LEVITICUS 4:2–4"> If you sin by doing something that is forbidden by any of Yahweh’s commands, even if you do not know that you have disobeyed one of my commands, you are guilty and must pay a penalty to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:15–16; 1 TIMOTHY 2:5–6; LEVITICUS 1:4"> When you realize what you have done, you must bring a ram to the Supreme Priest to be an offering in order that you will no longer be guilty. You must bring one that has no defects. He will offer the ram to be a sacrifice to me, and as a result you will no longer be guilty for the sin that you have committed, and you will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 51:4; MALACHI 3:8; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:19–21"> It is an offering to cause you to no longer be guilty for sinning against me.”</span>
</div>
