## Punishments for various sins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said this to Moses/me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:21; LEVITICUS 18:21; LEVITICUS 20:27"> “Tell the Israeli people, ‘This is what Yahweh says: Any Israeli or any foreigner who is living in Israel who sacrifices any of his children to be an offering to the god Molech must be executed. The people of the town must kill him by throwing stones at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:21; EZEKIEL 5:11; EZEKIEL 23:38–39"> I will reject &#91;IDM&#93; such people and command that they no longer be allowed to associate with my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:2–5; ACTS 17:30; 1 KINGS 20:42"> If the people of that man’s town ignore it when he kills one of his children to offer him as a sacrifice to Molech, and if they do not execute that person,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 106:39; HOSEA 2:13; HOSEA 2:5"> I myself will punish that person and his family, and I will command that he no longer be able to associate with my people. And I will do the same thing to any others who do the same thing that he did to worship Molech.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:31; Isaiah 8:19; LEVITICUS 20:27"> ‘ will reject &#91;IDM&#93; those who go to those who ◄consult/talk with► the spirits of dead people and fortune-tellers to seek advice from them. I will command that they no longer be allowed to associate with my people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:44; LEVITICUS 19:2; 1 PETER 1:15–16"> ‘Set yourselves apart and be holy, because I, Yahweh your God, am holy, and I want you to be like me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 31:13; JAMES 1:22; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:23"> Obey carefully &#91;DOU&#93; all my laws. I am Yahweh, the one will cause you to be holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 21:17; MARK 7:10; DEUTERONOMY 27:16"> ‘If anyone curses his father or his mother, he must be executed. He himself will be responsible for his own death &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 8:4–5; DEUTERONOMY 22:22–24; DEUTERONOMY 5:18"> ‘If a man commits adultery with some other man’s wife, both of them, the man and the woman, must be executed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:20; DEUTERONOMY 27:23; AMOS 2:7"> ‘If a man has sex with &#91;EUP&#93; one of his father’s wives, he has disgraced his father. So both that man and that woman must be executed, they will be responsible for their own deaths &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:15; LEVITICUS 18:23; GENESIS 38:18"> ‘If a man has sex with &#91;EUP&#93; his daughter-in-law, both of them must be executed. What they have done is very perverse; and they will be responsible for their own deaths &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 6:9; LEVITICUS 18:22; JUDE 1:7"> ‘If two men have sex together, they have done something detestable. They must both be executed, they will be responsible for their own deaths &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:17; DEUTERONOMY 27:23; JOSHUA 7:15"> ‘If a man marries both a woman and her mother, that is a wicked thing. All three of them must be burned to death, in order that such a wicked practice will not continue to be done among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:23; DEUTERONOMY 27:21; EXODUS 22:19"> ‘If a man has sex with an animal, he must be executed, and you must also kill that animal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 21:32; EXODUS 19:13; HEBREWS 12:20"> ‘Similarly, if a woman has sex with an animal, both the woman and that animal must be killed &#91;DOU&#93;; they are responsible for their own deaths &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:9; DEUTERONOMY 27:22; EZEKIEL 22:11"> ‘If a man marries his sister, the daughter of either his mother or his father, and they have sex together, that is disgraceful. They must not be allowed to continue to associate with my people. And because he has disgraced his sister, he must be punished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:24; LEVITICUS 18:19; EZEKIEL 18:6"> ‘If a man has sex with &#91;EUP, DOU&#93; a woman during her monthly menstrual period, they have both revealed why she was bleeding, so both of them must not be allowed to associate with my people any more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:6; LEVITICUS 18:12–30; EXODUS 6:20"> ‘Do not have sex with the sister of either your father or your mother, because that would be disgracing someone who is a close relative. And you and the woman must both be punished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:14; JOB 18:19; LUKE 23:29"> ‘If a man has sex &#91;EUP&#93; with his aunt, he has dishonored his uncle. I will punish both of them by causing them to die without having any children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:16; MATTHEW 14:3–4"> ‘If a man marries his brother’s wife while his brother is still living, that is disgraceful. He has dishonored his brother; and they both will die without having any children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:25–28; EZEKIEL 36:27; LEVITICUS 18:4–5"> ‘Obey all my laws/decrees &#91;DOU&#93; carefully, in order that you will not be forced to leave &#91;MET&#93; the land to which I am bringing you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:5; LEVITICUS 18:24; LEVITICUS 18:30"> Do not imitate/adopt the customs of the people of the land from which I will expel them as you advance. I hated them because they did all those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:2; EXODUS 6:8; DEUTERONOMY 7:6"> But I said to you, “You will take their land from them; I will give it to you to be yours, a land that is very fertile &#91;IDM&#93;. I am Yahweh your God, who has set you apart from the people of other nations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:3–21; LEVITICUS 11:1–47; ACTS 10:11–15"> ‘So you must distinguish between birds and animals that are unacceptable to me and those that are acceptable to me. Do not defile yourselves by eating birds or animals, or anything that scurries across the ground, that I have said are unacceptable for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 PETER 1:15–16; LEVITICUS 20:7; LEVITICUS 20:24"> You must behave in ways that I consider to be holy, because I, Yahweh, am holy, and I have set you apart from the ungodly people of other nations to belong to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:31; LEVITICUS 20:6; 1 SAMUEL 28:7–9"> ‘You must execute any man or woman among you who ◄consults/talks with► the spirits of dead people or who is a fortune-teller. Kill them by throwing stones at them; but they will be responsible for their own deaths.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
