<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:21; DEUTERONOMY 15:19; LEVITICUS 22:32"> “Tell Aaron and his sons to greatly respect the sacred offerings that the Israeli people bring to me; by doing that you will respect me &#91;MTY&#93;, Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:20–21; NUMBERS 19:13; PSALMS 16:11"> “Tell them that for all future time, if any of their descendants comes near to the sacred offerings that the Israeli people dedicate to me while he is unfit to work for me, that person will no longer be allowed to work for me. I, Yahweh, am commanding this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:39; LEVITICUS 15:2–3; LEVITICUS 15:13–16"> “If any descendant of Aaron has a contagious skin disease or a discharge from his sexual organs, he is not allowed to eat any of the sacred offerings until he is cured. He will also be unfit for his work if he touches anything which has become unacceptable to me because it has touched a corpse, or if he touches anyone who as an emission of semen,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:43–44; LEVITICUS 15:7; LEVITICUS 15:19"> or if he touches anything that crawls on the ground, or if he touches any person who causes him to be unfit for his work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:5; HEBREWS 10:22; Haggai 2:13"> Any priest who touches such things must bathe, and he must not touch anyone else until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:22; DEUTERONOMY 18:3–4; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:13–14"> After the sun sets, he will be permitted to eat some of the sacred offerings that are for him to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 17:15; EXODUS 22:31; EZEKIEL 44:31"> He must not eat anything that is found dead or that has been killed by wild animals, because if he did that, he would be unfit to work for me. I, Yahweh am commanding those things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 28:43; NUMBERS 18:22; NUMBERS 18:32"> “The priests must obey my commandments in order that they will not become guilty and die as a result of ◄despising/not obeying► them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:4; 1 SAMUEL 21:6; EXODUS 29:33"> “Anyone who is not a member of a priest’s family is not permitted to eat any of a sacred offering, and no one who is visiting the priest or whom the priest has hired is permitted to eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 17:13; EXODUS 12:44; NUMBERS 18:11–13"> But if a priest buys a slave, or if a slave is born in his house, that slave is permitted to eat such food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; LEVITICUS 21:3"> If a priest’s daughter marries a man who is not a priest, she no longer is permitted to eat the sacred food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:14; GENESIS 38:11; NUMBERS 18:11–19"> But if a priest’s daughter who has no children becomes a widow or becomes divorced, if she returns to her father’s house to live there like she did when she was young, she may eat the same food that her father eats. But no other person is permitted to eat any of it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:15; LEVITICUS 27:13; LEVITICUS 5:15–19"> “If anyone who is not permitted to eat a sacred offering eats it without realizing that it is sacred, he must pay the priest for the offering, and pay him an extra one-fifth of its value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:32; LEVITICUS 19:8; EZEKIEL 22:26"> When the priests bring offerings to me, the sacred offerings that the Israeli people bring to them, they must not treat those offerings as though they are not sacred</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:9; 1 PETER 2:24; Isaiah 53:11–12"> by allowing those who are not priests to eat any of those offerings. If they did that, they would become guilty and must make a payment. I am Yahweh, the one who causes those offerings to be sacred.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Offerings that are not acceptable

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:2; DEUTERONOMY 12:6; NUMBERS 15:3"> “Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the Israeli people and tell them that I command this: ‘If any of you Israelis or foreigners who live in Israel brings to me an animal that will be completely burned on the altar, either as a result of a solemn promise that you made to me or to be an offering that is given voluntarily,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:10; LEVITICUS 1:3; EPHESIANS 5:27"> it must be an animal that has no defects, in order that it will be accepted by me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:1; MALACHI 1:8; DEUTERONOMY 15:21"> Do not bring any animals that have defects, because I will not accept them as an offering from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:8; NUMBERS 15:3; LEVITICUS 3:6"> Similarly, when someone brings from his cattle or sheep or goats an offering to maintain fellowship with me, either to fulfill a promise that he made to me or to be a voluntary offering, in order that I will accept it, it must have no defects &#91;DOU&#93; or blemishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:20; MALACHI 1:8; LEVITICUS 1:9"> Do not offer to me animals that are blind or injured or maimed/crippled, or any animal that has warts or a festering sore &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:18"> You may present to me to be a voluntary offering an ox or a sheep that is injured or stunted, but it will not be accepted to fulfill a promise made to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:20; DEUTERONOMY 23:1; LEVITICUS 21:20"> You must not offer to me animals whose testicles are bruised or crushed or torn or cut. You must not injure the testicles of any animal in your land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:8; Ezra 6:8–10; NUMBERS 15:14–16"> and you must not accept such animals that are sold/given to you by a foreigner. You must not offer them to me as a sacrifice for me. Such animals will not be accepted by me, because they are deformed or have defects.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 22:30; LEVITICUS 19:23–24; LEVITICUS 22:25"> “When a calf or lamb or goat is born, it must remain with its mother for seven days. After that, it will be acceptable as an offering to me that will be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:6–7; DEUTERONOMY 14:21; EXODUS 34:26"> Do not slaughter a cow or a sheep and its baby on the same day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 116:17; PSALMS 107:22; AMOS 4:5"> “Then you sacrifice an animal to thank me for what I have done, sacrifice it in a way that is acceptable to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:15–18; EXODUS 16:19–20; LEVITICUS 19:7"> The meat must be eaten on that day; do not leave any of it until the next morning. I, Yahweh, am the one who am commanding this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:40; LEVITICUS 19:37; NUMBERS 15:40"> “Obey all my commands &#91;DOU&#93;. I, Yahweh, am commanding them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:21; LUKE 11:2; MATTHEW 6:9"> do not dishonor me &#91;MTY&#93; by disobeying them. You Israeli people must acknowledge that I, Yahweh, am holy, and I am the one who causes you to be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:45; EXODUS 6:7; LEVITICUS 25:38"> And I am the one who brought you out of Egypt in order to show that that I, Yahweh, am and will always be your God.”</span>
</div>
</div>
