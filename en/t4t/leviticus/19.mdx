## Other laws

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:44–45; LEVITICUS 20:7; 1 PETER 1:15–16"> “Speak to all the people of Israel and tell them this: You must be holy, because I, Yahweh your God, am holy, and I want you to be like me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12; EXODUS 20:8; HEBREWS 12:9"> ‘Each of you must respect your father and your mother. And you must ◄honor/treat respectfully► the Sabbath days. I am Yahweh, your God, and that is what I am commanding you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:1; EXODUS 20:23; PSALMS 115:4–7"> ‘Do not worship idols or make metal statues of gods for yourselves. I am Yahweh, your God, and I am the only one you must worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:19; LEVITICUS 22:29; LEVITICUS 7:16"> ‘Then you bring an offering to maintain fellowship with me, offer it in a way that will cause me to accept it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:11–17"> The meat should be eaten on the day that you sacrifice it, but you are permitted to eat some of it on the next day. Anything that remains until the third/next day must be completely burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:18–21; LEVITICUS 22:25; JEREMIAH 16:18"> For any of it to be eaten on the third day is very displeasing to me, and I will not accept that offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:15; LEVITICUS 5:1"> I will punish anyone who eats it after the second day, because he will not have respected that what I say is holy. And that person must no longer be allowed to associate with my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:22; DEUTERONOMY 24:19–22; RUTH 2:2"> ‘Then you harvest your grain, leave the grain at the very edge of the field, and do not pick up the grain that has fallen on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 17:6; LEVITICUS 25:6; Isaiah 24:13"> And when you harvest your grapes, do not go back a second time to try to harvest some more, and do not pick up the grapes that have fallen on the ground. Leave those things for the poor people and for foreigners who are living among you. I, Yahweh your God, am commanding those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 101:7; EPHESIANS 4:25; COLOSSIANS 3:9"> ‘Do not steal anything. ‘Do not tell lies. ‘Do not deceive each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 20:7; DEUTERONOMY 5:11; LEVITICUS 18:21"> ‘Do not show that you do not respect me by using/saying my name to falsely promise that you will do something. Do not forget that I am Yahweh, your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JAMES 5:4; MALACHI 3:5; DEUTERONOMY 24:14–15"> ‘Do not cheat anyone or steal from anyone. ‘If you have agreed to pay your workers at the end of the day, do what you have promised; do not keep those wages until the next day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:18; LEVITICUS 19:32; LEVITICUS 25:17"> ‘Do not curse deaf people, and do not put things in the path of blind people to cause them to stumble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:17; PROVERBS 24:23; DEUTERONOMY 27:19"> ‘Always &#91;LIT&#93; judge people fairly &#91;DOU&#93;. Do not do special favors for either poor people or rich people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 23:1; EXODUS 23:7; PROVERBS 20:19"> ‘Do not spread false rumors about other people. ‘Do not say anything in court that would result in some innocent person being executed. I, Yahweh, am commanding this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 JOHN 2:9; 1 JOHN 2:11; LUKE 17:3"> ‘Do not hate anyone. Instead, honestly rebuke those who ought to be rebuked, in order that you also will not be guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 13:9; MATTHEW 22:39–40; MARK 12:31–34"> ‘Do not try to get revenge against someone or be angry with someone for a long time. Instead, love other people like you love yourself. I, Yahweh your God, am commanding this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:14–17; DEUTERONOMY 22:9–11; MATTHEW 9:16–17"> ‘Obey my laws. ‘Do not allow two different kinds of animals to mate with each other. ‘Do not plant two different kinds of seed in the same field. ‘Do not wear clothing made from two different kinds of material.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:23–24; EXODUS 21:20–21"> ‘If a man has sex with a slave woman who has been promised to marry some other man, but she has not been bought by that man and is still a slave, the man who had sex with her must be punished. But because she is still a slave, she and the man who had sex with her must not be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:6–7; LEVITICUS 5:1–6; LEVITICUS 5:15"> However, that man must bring a ram to be slaughtered at the entrance of the Sacred Tent area, to be an offering in order that he no longer be guilty for his sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:26; LEVITICUS 4:20"> The priest will offer that ram to me in order that the man will be forgiven for the sin that he committed, and I will forgive him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:27; ACTS 7:51; JEREMIAH 9:25–26"> ‘When you enter the land that I have promised to give to you, and when you plant various kinds of fruit trees, you must not eat any of their fruit for three years &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:9; DEUTERONOMY 18:4; NUMBERS 18:12–13"> In the fourth year all of their fruit must be set aside to belong to me; it must be brought to me to be an offering to praise me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Haggai 1:9–11; MALACHI 3:8–10; LEVITICUS 26:3–4"> But in the fifth/next year, you will be permitted to eat their fruit. If you do that, your trees will produce much fruit. I, Yahweh your God, am promising that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:10–14; 2 KINGS 17:17; 2 CHRONICLES 33:6"> ‘Do not eat any meat that still has the animal’s blood in it. ‘Do not consult spirits to find out what will happen in the future, and do not practice sorcery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:5; DEUTERONOMY 14:1; EZEKIEL 44:20"> ‘Do not shave the hair at the sides of your heads like pagan people do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:1; LEVITICUS 21:5; 1 KINGS 18:28"> ‘Do not cut your bodies when you are mourning for people who have died, and do not put tattoos on your bodies. I, Yahweh your God, am commanding this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:17–18; LEVITICUS 21:7; HOSEA 4:12–14"> ‘Do not disgrace your daughters by forcing them to become prostitutes. If you cause them to become prostitutes, soon the land will be filled with prostitutes and all other kinds of people’s wicked behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:2; LEVITICUS 19:3; ECCLESIASTES 5:1"> ‘Honor my Sabbath days and revere my Sacred Tent, because I, Yahweh, live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; LEVITICUS 20:27; LEVITICUS 20:6–7"> ‘Do not seek advice from those who ◄consult/talk with► the spirits of dead people &#91;DOU&#93;, because if you do that, they will defile you. I, Yahweh your God, am the one you should consult.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:1; LAMENTATIONS 5:12; PROVERBS 16:31"> ‘Stand up when old people enter the room, and show that you respect them, and also revere me, your God; that is what I, Yahweh, am commanding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 23:9; EXODUS 22:21; MALACHI 3:5"> ‘When foreigners live among you in your land, do not mistreat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:19; LEVITICUS 19:18; EXODUS 12:48–49"> You must treat them like you treat your fellow-citizens. Love them like you love yourselves, and do not forget that once, when you were foreigners in Egypt, you were badly mistreated by the people of Egypt. I, Yahweh your God, am commanding you to do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:15; DEUTERONOMY 25:13; PROVERBS 16:11"> ‘When you are measuring things, to see how long they are or how much they weigh or how many there are,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:1; EXODUS 20:2; DEUTERONOMY 25:13–15"> use correct measuring sticks and scales and weights on the scales and measuring baskets and other measuring containers. I Yahweh, your God, who brought you out of Egypt, am giving you these laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:1; LEVITICUS 18:4–5; DEUTERONOMY 5:1"> ‘Obey carefully &#91;DOU&#93; all my laws and decrees, because I, Yahweh, am the one who am commanding them.’ ”</span>
