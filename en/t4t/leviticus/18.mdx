## Forbidden sexual behavior

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 6:7; LEVITICUS 11:44; EZEKIEL 20:5"> “Speak to the Israeli people and tell them that I, Yahweh, say this this: I am Yahweh, your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:23; EXODUS 23:24; 1 PETER 4:2–4"> So you must do what I want you to do; you must not do the things that the people in Egypt, where you lived previously, do; and you must not do what is done by the people in Canaan, the land to which I am taking you. Do not imitate their behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:2; LEVITICUS 18:26; EZEKIEL 20:19"> You must obey all of my laws &#91;DOU&#93;, because it is I, Yahweh your God, who am commanding those laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 10:5; EZEKIEL 20:11; GALATIANS 3:12"> If you obey all my laws and decrees, you will continue to remain alive for a long time. I, Yahweh, am the one who is promising that to you. These are some of my laws:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:17–21; LEVITICUS 18:7–19; LEVITICUS 20:11–12"> “Do not have sex with any of your close relatives. It is I, Yahweh, who am commanding that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:11; EZEKIEL 22:10; LEVITICUS 18:8–16"> “Do not disgrace your father by having sex with your mother &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:11; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:1; DEUTERONOMY 27:20"> “Do not have sex with any of your father’s other wives, because that would disgrace your father.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:17; DEUTERONOMY 27:22; EZEKIEL 22:11"> “Do not have sex with your sister or your stepsister; it does not matter whether she was born in your house or somewhere else.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “Do not have sex with your granddaughter, because that would disgrace you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> “Do not have sex with your half-sister, because she is your sister.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:19; EXODUS 6:20"> “Do not have sex with your father’s sister, because she is your father’s close relative.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “Do not have sex with your mother’s sister, because she is your mother’s close relative.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:20"> “Do not disgrace your ◄uncle/father’s brother► by having sex with his wife, because she is your aunt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:12; GENESIS 38:26; EZEKIEL 22:11"> “Do not have sex with &#91;DOU&#93; your daughter-in-law, because she is your son’s wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:5; LEVITICUS 20:21; MATTHEW 22:24"> “Do not have sex with your brother’s wife, because that would disgrace your brother.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:14; AMOS 2:7; DEUTERONOMY 27:23"> “Do not have sex with the daughter or granddaughter of any woman whom you have previously had sex with; they are her (OR, your) close relatives. Having sex with any of them would be a wicked thing to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MALACHI 2:15; GENESIS 30:15; GENESIS 29:28"> “While your wife is still living, do not marry your wife’s sister and have sex with her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:18; LEVITICUS 15:24; EZEKIEL 22:10"> “Do not have sex with any woman while she is having her monthly menstrual period.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 20:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 6:9; HEBREWS 13:4"> “Do not ◄defile yourself/make yourself unacceptable to me► by having sex with someone else’s wife.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:31; LEVITICUS 20:2–5; DEUTERONOMY 18:10"> “Do not give any of your children to be burned to be a sacrifice to the god Molech, because that would show that you do not revere me &#91;MTY&#93;, Yahweh, your God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:13; ROMANS 1:26–27; 1 CORINTHIANS 6:9"> “No man should have sex with another man; that is detestable.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 22:19; LEVITICUS 20:15–16; LEVITICUS 20:12"> “No one, man or woman, should ◄defile himself/cause himself to become unacceptable to me► by having sex with an animal; that is a perverse act.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:12; LEVITICUS 18:30; LEVITICUS 20:22–23"> “Do not ◄defile yourselves/cause yourselves to become unacceptable to me► in any of those ways, because doing those things is how the people of the nations that I expelled as you advanced became ◄unacceptable to me/defiled►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:28; LEVITICUS 20:22–23; JEREMIAH 2:7"> They even caused the land to become defiled, so I punished them for their sins, and it was as though the land vomited out the people who lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:5; LEVITICUS 17:10; LEVITICUS 18:30"> You must all obey my laws and decrees. That includes you people who were born here and the foreigners who live among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:14; LEVITICUS 18:24; DEUTERONOMY 23:18"> All those detestable things were done by the people who lived in this land before you came here, and they caused the land to become defiled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="REVELATION 3:16; LEVITICUS 20:22; LEVITICUS 18:25"> So if you defile the land, I will get rid of &#91;MET&#93; you like I got rid of the people of those nations that were here before you came.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:6; EXODUS 12:15; LEVITICUS 17:10"> “You must not allow people who do any of those detestable things to associate with you who are my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:23; DEUTERONOMY 18:9–12; LEVITICUS 22:9"> Obey everything that I command you to do, and do not defile yourselves by practicing any of the detestable customs that were practiced by the people who were there before you came. I, Yahweh your God, am the one who is commanding these things.”</span>
</div>
</div>
