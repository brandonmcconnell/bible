## Regulations concerning contagious skin diseases

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh said this to Aaron and Moses/me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:8; LEVITICUS 14:56; LEVITICUS 14:3"> “When someone has on his skin a swelling or a rash or a shiny/bright spot that may become ◄contagious/a dreaded skin disease►, he must be brought to Aaron or to one of his sons who are also priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 3:19–20; ACTS 20:28; MALACHI 2:7"> The priest must examine that part of the person’s skin. If the hair in that area has become white and it appears that the sore is deeper than just on the skin, it is a contagious skin disease. When the priest sees that, he must declare that the person must stay away from other people &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 12:15; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:5; 1 TIMOTHY 5:24"> If the spot on the person’s skin is white but it does not appear that the sore is deeper than just on the skin, the priest must tell him to stay away from other people for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then the priest must examine the person again. If the priest sees that the sore has not changed and has not spread, he must tell the person to stay away from people for seven more days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:25; LEVITICUS 14:8; DEUTERONOMY 32:5"> Then the priest must examine him again. If the sore has faded and has not spread, the priest will allow him to be with other people again; it is only a rash. After the person washes his clothes, he will be allowed to be with other people again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 38:3; LEVITICUS 13:35–36; 2 TIMOTHY 2:16–17"> But if the sore spreads after the priest has examined him, he must go to the priest again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:7–8; PHILIPPIANS 3:18–19; ACTS 8:21"> The priest will examine him; and if the sore has spread to more of the skin, it is a contagious skin disease, and the priest will declare that he must stay away from other people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> When anyone has a contagious skin disease, he must be brought to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:27; 2 CHRONICLES 26:19–20; LEVITICUS 13:14–15"> The priest must examine him. And if there is a white swelling in the skin that has caused the hair in that swelling to become white, and if the flesh in that area is painful/sensitive,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> it is a permanent skin disease, and the priest will declare that he must stay away from other people. The priest does not need to tell that person that he must avoid other people, because other people already are wanting to stay away from him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 JOHN 1:8–10; JOB 42:6; ROMANS 7:14"> ‘If the disease spreads all over someone’s body, and the priest examines that person and sees that it is covering his skin from his head to his feet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 9:41; Isaiah 64:6"> and it has caused all his skin to become white which will indicate that the disease has ended, the priest will declare that the person does not have to stay away from other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> But if the person has open sores and they are very painful/sensitive, he has a contagious skin disease,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and when the priest sees that, he will declare that the person must stay away from other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:14–16; ROMANS 7:14–24; PHILIPPIANS 3:6–8"> But if the person’s flesh changes and becomes white, he must go to the priest again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The priest must examine him again; and if the sores have become white, the priest will declare that the person who had been infected is now permitted to be with other people again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 9:9; Isaiah 38:21; PSALMS 38:3–7"> ‘Then someone has a boil on his skin and it has healed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:24"> but in the place where the boil was a white swelling or a bright/shiny spot appears, he must go to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 5:14; LEVITICUS 13:3; 2 PETER 2:20"> The priest must examine it. And if it seems to be deeper than just on the skin, and if the hair in that spot has become white, it is a contagious skin disease that has appeared where the boil had been. And the priest will declare that the person must stay away from other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 5:5"> But when the priest examines it, if there is no white hair in that spot and it is only on the surface of the skin and has become less bright/shiny, then the priest will order him to stay away from other people for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But if it is spreading, it is contagious and the priest will declare that the person must stay away from other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:1; JOB 34:31–32; MATTHEW 26:75"> But if that spot is unchanged and has not spread, it is only a scar from the boil, and the priest will declare that the person is permitted to be with other people again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 3:24"> ‘Then someone has a burn on his skin and a bright/shiny or white spot appears, and the flesh in that area is sensitive/painful,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:18–20; LEVITICUS 13:4"> the priest must examine the spot. If the hair in that spot has turned white and it seems to be deeper than just on the surface of the skin, it is a contagious skin disease that has appeared where the burn was, and that person must stay away from other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:23; LEVITICUS 13:4–5"> But when the priest examines it and sees that there is no white hair in that spot and it is only on the surface of the skin, and has faded, the priest will declare that the person must stay away from people for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> On the seventh day, the priest will examine him again. If the sore is spreading, it is a contagious skin disease, and the priest will declare that the person must stay away from other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> However, if the spot is not changed and has not spread but has faded, it is only a scar from the burn, and the priest will declare that the person is permitted to be with other people again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 9:15; MATTHEW 13:14–15; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:3–4"> ‘If a man or a woman has a sore on his head or on his chin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:34–37; LEVITICUS 14:54"> the priest must examine it. If it appears to be deeper than just on the surface of the skin, and the hair in that spot has thinned out and has become yellowish, it is a contagious skin disease that causes itching. And the priest will declare that the person must stay away from other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> But when the priest examines that kind of sore, if it seems to be only on the surface of the skin and there is no healthy hair in it, the priest will tell the person to stay away from other people for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:30; MATTHEW 23:5; LUKE 18:9–12"> On the seventh day, the priest will examine the sore again. If it has not spread and if there is no yellow hair in that spot and if it appears to be only on the surface of the skin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> the person must shave the hair near the sore but not the hair on the sore. And the priest will tell him to stay away from other people for seven more days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JUDE 1:22; 1 JOHN 4:1; REVELATION 2:2"> On the seventh day, the priest will examine that spot again. If it has not spread and it appears to be only on the surface of the skin, the priest will declare that the person is permitted to be with people again. The person must wash his clothes, and then he can be with other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:27; 2 TIMOTHY 2:16–17; LEVITICUS 13:7"> But if the sore later spreads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> the priest must examine him again. If the itch/sore has spread, the priest does not need to look for yellow hair, because it is clear that the person has a contagious skin disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> However, if the priest thinks that the spot has not changed, and healthy hair is growing in that area, it is clear that the itch has healed, and the priest will declare that the person is permitted to be with other people again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> ‘Then a man or a woman has white spots on the skin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="ROMANS 7:22–25; JAMES 3:2; ECCLESIASTES 7:20"> the priest should examine them. But if the spots are dull white, it is only a rash, and the priest will declare that the person is permitted to be with other people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:13; ROMANS 8:10; SONG OF SONGS 5:11"> ‘If a man loses the hair on any part of his head, he does not need to stay away from other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> But if he gets a bright/shiny sore on his bald head or on his forehead, he has a contagious skin disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> The priest must examine him. If the swollen sore is a bright spot like the spot on someone who has a contagious skin disease,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 PETER 2:1–2; MATTHEW 6:23; Isaiah 1:5"> the priest will declare that the man has a contagious skin disease and must not be with other people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MICAH 3:7; LEVITICUS 10:6; EZEKIEL 24:17"> ‘Anyone who has a contagious skin disease must wear torn clothes and not comb his hair. When he is near other people, he must cover the lower part to his face and call out, “Do not come near me! I have a contagious skin disease!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 KINGS 7:3; 2 KINGS 15:5; 2 CHRONICLES 26:21"> He is not allowed to be with other people as long as he has the disease. He must live alone, outside the camp.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Regulations concerning mildew

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JUDE 1:23; ROMANS 13:12; EZEKIEL 16:16"> “Sometimes a person’s clothing gets mildew on it. It may be clothing that is woven from wool or made from linen or from leather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> If the contaminated/mildewed part is greenish or reddish, it is a spreading mildew, and it must be shown to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:23"> The priest will examine it, and then put it in a separate place by itself for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:44"> On the seventh day he must examine it again. If the mildew has spread, it is clear that it is a type of mildew that destroys clothing, and that clothing must not be worn again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:35; LEVITICUS 11:33; LEVITICUS 14:44–45"> The owner must burn completely the item that has the mildew in it, whatever kind of item it is.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> But when the priest examines it, if the mildew has not spread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> he will tell the person who owns it to wash it. Then he must put it in a separate place for another seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:13; HEBREWS 6:4–8; 2 PETER 2:20–22"> Then the priest will examine it again. If the color of the mildew has not changed, even though it has not spread, that item must not be worn again. It does not matter if the mildew is on the inside of the clothing or on the outside; it must be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> But when the priest examines it after it has been washed, if the mildew has faded, he must tear out that part that had the mildew in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:7; REVELATION 21:8; MATTHEW 3:12"> But if the mildew reappears in that item, it is clear that it is spreading, and the whole item must be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:14; REVELATION 1:5; PSALMS 51:2"> But after the clothing is washed and the mildew disappears, it must be washed again, and then it can be worn again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Those are the regulations concerning mildew on things made of wool or linen or leather, for deciding whether those things can continue to be worn or not.”</span>
</div>
</div>
