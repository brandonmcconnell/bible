## Rituals for those healed of contagious skin diseases

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 17:14; LUKE 5:12–14; MARK 1:40–44"> “These are the regulations for anyone who has been healed of a contagious skin disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:46; LUKE 7:22; LUKE 17:15–19"> The person must be brought to a priest. The priest will take him outside the camp to where that person has been staying, and examine him. If the skin disease has been healed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:6; LEVITICUS 14:6; HEBREWS 9:19"> the priest will say that someone must bring two living birds that are acceptable to Yahweh, along with some cedar wood, some scarlet/red yarn, and some sprigs of ◄hyssop/a very leafy plant►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:50; HEBREWS 2:14; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:1"> Then the priest will command that one of the birds be killed while it is being held over a clay pot containing water from a spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="REVELATION 1:5; ZECHARIAH 13:1; PHILIPPIANS 2:9–11"> Then the priest will dip the other bird, along with the cedar wood, the scarlet/red yarn and the hyssop, into the blood of the bird that was killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:14; EZEKIEL 36:25; Isaiah 52:15"> Then he must sprinkle some of the blood on the person who was healed; he must sprinkle it on him seven times. Then he will declare that the person is permitted to be with other people again. And the priest will release the other bird and allow it to fly away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:25; NUMBERS 8:7; 1 PETER 3:21"> “Then the person who was healed must wash his clothes, shave off all his hair, and bathe. Then he is allowed to return to the camp, but he must stay outside his tent for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:9; LEVITICUS 14:8; NUMBERS 8:7"> On the seventh day, he must again shave off all his hair, including his beard and his eyebrows. Then he must again wash his clothes and bathe, and then he will be allowed to be with other people again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:1; MATTHEW 8:4; LUKE 5:14"> “The next day that person must bring two male lambs and one female lamb that is one year old, all of them with no defects. He must also bring six quarts/liters of fine flour, mixed with olive oil, to be an offering, and ◄0.6 pint/0.3 liter► of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:26–27; EXODUS 29:1–4; NUMBERS 8:21"> The priest who declares that the person’s skin disease is ended must bring that person, and his offerings, to me, Yahweh, at the entrance of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 29:24; LEVITICUS 6:6–7; LEVITICUS 8:27–29"> “Then the priest must take one of the male lambs and lift it up, along with the olive oil, in front of me, to be a guilt offering—an offering for his being guilty for not giving to me the things that he was required to give me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 29:11; LEVITICUS 1:11; LEVITICUS 4:24"> Then the priest must slaughter the lamb in the sacred place where the other sacrifices are offered. Like the offering to enable people to be forgiven, this guilt offering is holy, and belongs to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 29:20; LEVITICUS 8:23–24; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:1"> The priest must take some of the blood of that animal and pour it on the lobe/tip of the right ear and on the thumb of the right hand and on the big toe of the right foot of the one who has been healed of the skin disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 45:7; JOHN 3:34; 1 JOHN 2:20"> Then the priest must take some of the olive oil and pour it in the palm of his own left hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:17; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:31; LEVITICUS 4:6"> Then he must dip the forefinger of his right hand into the oil in his palm, and sprinkle it in front of me seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 PETER 1:2; EZEKIEL 36:27; LEVITICUS 14:14"> Then he must put some of the oil that is still in the palm of his hand on the lobe/tip of the right ear and the thumb of the right hand and on the big toe of the right foot of the person who has been healed of the skin disease. He must put it on top of the blood that he has already put on those places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:26; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:21–22; EPHESIANS 1:17–18"> The remaining oil in his palm must be put on the person’s head, to indicate that I declare that the person has been forgiven for having sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:12; LEVITICUS 5:1; LEVITICUS 12:6–8"> “Then the priest must slaughter one of the other two animals, to show that the one who has been healed of the skin disease has been forgiven for having sinned, and that he has become acceptable to Yahweh. Then the priest will slaughter the animal that will be completely burned on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:10; EPHESIANS 5:2; LEVITICUS 14:8"> He will also put on the altar the offering of grain, to indicate that the person has been forgiven for having sinned. Then that person will be allowed to be with other people again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:7; LEVITICUS 12:8; PROVERBS 17:5"> “But if the person who has been healed of a skin disease is poor and cannot afford to bring all those animals, he must take to the priest one male lamb to be lifted up to be an offering for his not giving to me the things that he was required to give me. He must also take two quarts/liters of fine flour mixed with olive oil to be an offering made from grain, ◄0.6 pint/0.3 liter► of olive oil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 7:16; Isaiah 59:11; JEREMIAH 48:28"> and two doves or two young pigeons, one for him to be forgiven for the sins he has committed, and one to be completely burned on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:10–11"> “On that same day, that person must take those things to the priest at the entrance of the Sacred Tent, to offer them to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:12; LEVITICUS 14:10"> The priest will take the lamb for the offering for that person not giving to me the things that he was required to give me, along with the olive oil, and lift them up in front of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:1; PSALMS 40:6; LEVITICUS 14:14–20"> Then the priest will slaughter that lamb and drain some of the blood in a bowl, and take some of that blood and put it on the lobe/tip of the person’s right ear and on the thumb of his right hand and on the big toe of his right foot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then the priest will pour some of the oil into the palm of his left hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and with his right forefinger he must sprinkle some of the oil from his palm there in my presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He must put some of the oil in his palm on the same places where he put the blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:18; 1 JOHN 2:1–2; EXODUS 30:15–16"> He must put the rest of the oil that is in his hand on the head of the person who has been healed of a skin disease, to indicate that I have forgiven him for having sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:22; ROMANS 8:3; LEVITICUS 12:8"> Then the priest must sacrifice the doves or the pigeons, whichever that person has brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:18; LEVITICUS 5:7"> One will be an offering for sin and the other will be completely burned on the altar, along with the offering of grain. By doing that, the priest will declare that the person is no longer guilty for having sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:10; LEVITICUS 14:21; MATTHEW 11:5"> “Those are the regulations for anyone who has a contagious skin disease and who is poor and cannot afford the usual offerings, in order that he can be with people again.”</span>
</div>

## Instructions regarding mildew in a house

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Yahweh also said to Aaron and Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:49; GENESIS 17:8; NUMBERS 32:22"> “I am about to give you Canaan land to belong to your people permanently. When you enter that land, there will be times when I cause/allow mildew to appear inside one of your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 91:10; DEUTERONOMY 7:26; ZECHARIAH 5:4"> If that happens, the owner of that house must go to the priest and tell him, ‘There is something in my house that looks like mildew.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="REVELATION 18:4; HEBREWS 12:15; 2 TIMOTHY 2:17–18"> “Then the priest will say to him, ‘Take everything out of the house before I enter the house to examine the mildew. If you do not do that, I will declare that everything in the house is contaminated.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:42; LEVITICUS 13:3; LEVITICUS 13:19–20"> After the owner takes everything outside of his house, the priest will go in and inspect the house. If the mildew has caused greenish or reddish spots/depressions on the walls that seem to be deeper than only on the surface of the walls,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:50"> the priest will go outside the house and lock it up for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:36; LEVITICUS 13:27; LEVITICUS 13:22"> On the seventh day, he must go into the house and inspect it again. If the mildew on the walls has spread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="REVELATION 2:20; TITUS 3:10; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:5–6"> the priest will tell someone to tear out and throw in the dump outside the town all the stones in the walls that have mildew on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; REVELATION 22:15; MATTHEW 24:51"> Then the owner must scrape all the walls inside the house, and everything that is scraped off must be thrown into a dump outside the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 2:2; 1 TIMOTHY 5:21–22; 2 CHRONICLES 17:7–9"> Then the owner must get new/other stones to replace the ones that had mildew on them, and take new clay and plaster to cover the stones in the walls of the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:28–30; JUDE 1:12; 2 PETER 2:20"> “If the mildew appears again in the house after that is done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:51–52; ZECHARIAH 5:4"> the priest must go and examine the house again. If the mildew has spread inside the house, it will be clear that the mildew is the kind that destroys houses, and no one will be allowed to live in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 KINGS 10:27; MATTHEW 24:2; 1 KINGS 9:6–9"> It must be completely torn down—the stones, the timber and the plaster—and all those things must be thrown into a dump outside the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:21–22; LEVITICUS 22:6; LEVITICUS 11:28"> “Anyone who goes into that house while it is locked up will not be allowed to be with other people until sunset of that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:8–9; LEVITICUS 11:25"> Anyone who sleeps in that house or eats in that house during that time must wash his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 6:11; JOB 5:18; MARK 5:29"> “But when the priest comes to examine the house after it has been plastered, if the mildew has not spread, he shall declare that people may live in it, because the mildew is gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:4"> But before people are allowed to live in it, the priest must take two small birds and some cedar wood and some red/scarlet yarn and some hyssop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> He must kill one of the birds while holding it over a clay pot containing water from a spring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="PSALMS 51:7"> Then he must take the cedar wood, the hyssop, the red/scarlet yarn, and the other/living bird, and dip them into the blood of the dead bird, and sprinkle some of that blood on the house seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> By doing all those things he will cause the house to be acceptable to be lived in again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:20; LEVITICUS 14:7"> Then he must release the other bird and allow it to fly away. By doing that, he will finish the ritual for causing the house to be acceptable for people to live in it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:32; LEVITICUS 14:32; LEVITICUS 6:9"> “Those are the regulations for contagious diseases, for itching sores,</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:47–59"> for mildew &#91;DOU&#93; on clothes or in a house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:2"> and for swellings, rashes, or bright spots on sores,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:10; EZEKIEL 44:23; JEREMIAH 15:19"> to find out whether a person has a contagious disease or not, and whether people will still be permitted to touch their clothing or their house, or not.”</span>
</div>
