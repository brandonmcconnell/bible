<div className="bp">

## The Sabbath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:3; EXODUS 23:12; EXODUS 35:2–3"> “You may work for six days each week, but on the seventh day you must not do any work. You must rest. It is a sacred/holy day when you must gather together to worship me. Wherever you live, you must rest on that day.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Festivals of Passover and Unleavened Bread

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:2; EXODUS 23:14; LEVITICUS 23:37"> “There are festivals that I have established for you. Those will also be sacred/holy days when you must gather together to worship me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 5:10; DEUTERONOMY 16:1–8; NUMBERS 28:16–17"> The first festival is ◄the Passover/when you remember that I spared your ancestors when I killed all the firstborn Egyptian sons►. That festival will begin at twilight/dusk/sunset on the chosen day ◄each spring/in March or April each year►, and end on the following day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 34:18; DEUTERONOMY 16:8; EXODUS 12:15–16"> The next day, the Festival of Eating Bread That Has No Yeast will begin. That festival will continue for seven days. During that time, the bread that you eat must be made without yeast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:18–25; LEVITICUS 23:8; LEVITICUS 23:21"> On the first day of that festival, you all must stop your regular work and you must gather together to worship me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> On each of the seven days, you must present to me several animals to be an offering/sacrifice to be completely burned on the altar. On the seventh day, you all must again stop your regular work and gather to worship me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Festival of the Beginning of the Harvest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Yahweh also told Moses/me</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 34:26; EXODUS 23:19; DEUTERONOMY 16:9"> to tell the Israeli people about other festivals. He said, “When you arrive in the land which I am giving to you, and you harvest your crops for the first time there, bring to the priest some of the first grain that you harvest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 29:24; LEVITICUS 10:14; LEVITICUS 9:21"> On the day after the next Sabbath/rest day, the priest will lift it up high to dedicate it to me, in order that I will accept it as your gift.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:10–12; 1 PETER 1:19; LEVITICUS 1:10"> That same day, you must sacrifice to me a one-year-old male lamb that has no defects. You must burn it on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:14–16; JOEL 1:9; NUMBERS 28:10"> You must also burn an offering of grain. That offering must consist of ◄three quarts/3.5 liters► of good flour mixed with olive oil. The smell of those things burning will be very pleasing to me. Along with that, you must also offer one quart/liter of wine, which will be a liquid offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 34:26; GENESIS 4:4–5; LEVITICUS 3:17"> Do not eat any bread or any roasted grain or unroasted grain on that day until after you have brought those offerings to me, your God. You must always obey &#91;LIT&#93; those commands, wherever you live.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Festival of the End of the Harvest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:10–11; LEVITICUS 25:8; DEUTERONOMY 16:9–10"> “Seven weeks after the priest offers that grain to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ACTS 2:1; NUMBERS 28:26"> on the next/50th day after he offers it, each family must bring to me an offering from the new crop of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:10; EXODUS 34:26; EXODUS 34:22"> From your homes, bring two loaves of bread to the priest. He will lift them up high to dedicate them as an offering to me. Those loaves must be baked from three quarts/liters of good flour that has yeast mixed with it. That bread will be an offering to me from the first wheat that you harvest each year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:27–31; LEVITICUS 23:12–13; MALACHI 1:13–14"> Along with this bread, you must present to me seven one-year-old lambs with no defects, one young bull, and two rams. They must all be completely burned on the altar. All those offerings, with the grain offering and the wine offering, will be burned, and the smell of all those things burning will be very pleasing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:30; LEVITICUS 4:23–28; LEVITICUS 7:11–18"> Then you must also kill one male goat to be an offering/sacrifice for your sins, and two one-year-old male lambs to be an offering to enable you to maintain fellowship with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:4; EPHESIANS 2:14; NUMBERS 18:8–12"> The priest will lift up these offerings high to dedicate them to me. He will also offer the loaves of bread that were baked from the first wheat that you harvest. Those offerings are sacred to me, but they are for the priests to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:2; LEVITICUS 23:4; NUMBERS 18:23"> On that day, you must stop your regular work and gather to worship me. You must always obey those instructions/commands, and you must obey them wherever you live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:19–21; LEVITICUS 19:9–10; Isaiah 58:10"> “When you harvest the grain in your fields, do not harvest the grain along the edges of the fields, and do not pick up the grain that the harvesters drop. Leave that grain for the poor people and for the foreigners who are living among you. Do not forget that it is I, Yahweh your God, who am commanding those things!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Festival of Trumpets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Yahweh also told Moses/me</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:52; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:16; LEVITICUS 25:9"> to give these instructions/commands to the Israeli people: “Each year in September, on the day that I have chosen, you all must celebrate it by completely resting. You must not do any work on that day. When the priests blow their trumpets loudly, you all must gather together to worship me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:21"> All of you must not do your regular work on that day. Instead, you must present offerings to me that will be burned on the altar.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Festival of Atonement

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:29–31; EXODUS 30:10; LEVITICUS 16:24"> “You must celebrate a day on which you request that I forgive you for the sins that you have committed. That day will be nine days after the festival when the priests blow the trumpets. On that day you must ◄fast/abstain from eating food►. You must gather together to worship me and present offerings to me that will be burned on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 3:9; LEVITICUS 16:34; HEBREWS 10:10"> You must not do any work on that day, because it is the day when the priests will offer sacrifices to me to ◄atone for/ask me to forgive► your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 17:14; NUMBERS 5:2; LEVITICUS 23:27"> You must expel from your group anyone who does not ◄fast/abstain from eating food► on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:3; ZEPHANIAH 2:5; JEREMIAH 15:7"> I will get rid of anyone who does any kind of work on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> You must not work at all! You must always obey those instructions/commands, and you must obey them wherever you live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:31; MATTHEW 11:28–30; PSALMS 69:10–11"> That day will be a day of complete rest for all of you, and on that day you must ◄fast/abstain from eating food► to show that you are sorry for having sinned. That day of rest and ◄fasting/abstaining from eating food► will begin on the evening before the day in which you ask me to forgive you for your sins, and it will end on the evening of the following day.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Festival of Shelters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JOHN 7:2; Nehemiah 8:14; Ezra 3:4"> “Tell the Israeli people that each year they must also celebrate a festival of living in temporary shelters. That festival will begin five days after the day in which they request me to forgive them for their sins. It will last for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:24–25; LEVITICUS 23:7–8"> On the first day of that festival, the people must gather together to worship me, and they must not do any regular work on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:18; JOHN 7:37; 2 CHRONICLES 7:8–11"> On each of the seven days of the festival, they must present to me an offering of animals that will be burned on the altar. On the next day, they must gather again to worship me and present to me another animal that will be burned on the altar. That also will be a sacred gathering, and they must not work on that day, either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:2; LEVITICUS 23:4; ECCLESIASTES 3:1"> “To summarize, those are the festivals that I have appointed. Celebrate those sacred festivals by gathering together to present to me all the various offerings that will be burned on the altar—animals that will be burned completely, and offerings of grain, and offerings to enable the Israeli people to maintain fellowship with me, and offerings of wine. Each offering must be brought on the day that I have indicated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="NUMBERS 29:39; LEVITICUS 19:3; Ezra 2:68–69"> ◄Those festivals must be celebrated/You must celebrate those festivals► in addition to worshiping on the ◄Sabbath days/days of rest►. And all those offerings must be given in addition to the offerings that people personally decide to give, and in addition to the offerings that people make to accompany the solemn promises that they have made, or offerings that people make ◄voluntarily/because they themselves want to►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="EXODUS 23:16; DEUTERONOMY 16:13; LEVITICUS 23:34"> “Returning to my instructions about the Festival of Living in Temporary Shelters, you must celebrate this festival after you have harvested all the crops. On the first day and on the last day of that festival, you must rest completely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:4; REVELATION 7:9; DEUTERONOMY 16:14–15"> But on the first day, you are permitted to pick fruit from trees. Cut palm fronds/branches and other leafy branches from trees or from bushes that grow by the streams, and make shelters/huts to live in for that week. Then rejoice in my presence for those seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="NUMBERS 29:12; Nehemiah 8:18"> You must celebrate this festival for seven days every year. You must never stop obeying my commands/instructions for that festival. You must celebrate it in September.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:14–17; GENESIS 33:17; NUMBERS 24:5"> During the seven days of that festival, all of you people who have been Israelis your entire lives must live in shelters/huts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:10–13; PSALMS 78:5–6; EXODUS 13:14"> That will remind your descendants that their ancestors lived in shelters for many years after I rescued them from Egypt. Do not forget that I, Yahweh your God, am the one who is commanding this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:1–2; MATTHEW 18:20; LEVITICUS 21:24"> So Moses/I gave to the Israeli people all those instructions concerning the festivals that Yahweh wanted them to celebrate each year.</span>
</div>
</div>
