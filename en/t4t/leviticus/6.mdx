<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:9; ACTS 5:4; LEVITICUS 5:15"> “If any one of you you sins against me by deceiving someone—if you refuse to return what someone has lent you, or if you steal something of his, or if you find something and claim that you do not have it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 23:4; LEVITICUS 19:12; EXODUS 22:9–11"> you are guilty. You must return to its owner what you have stolen or what someone has lent you and you have not returned, or what you found that someone else had lost,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:18; AMOS 3:10; LEVITICUS 5:3–4"> or whatever you lied about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:16; LUKE 19:8; NUMBERS 5:7–8"> You must not only return anything like that to its owner, but you must also pay to the owner one-fifth of its value.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:15; LEVITICUS 5:18; Isaiah 53:10–11"> You must also bring to the Supreme Priest a ram to be an offering to me in order that you will no longer be guilty. The ram that you bring must be one that has no defects, one that has the value that has been officially determined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:26; 1 JOHN 1:9; 1 JOHN 2:1–2"> Then he will offer that ram to be a sacrifice that will cause you to no longer be guilty, and you will be forgiven for the wrong things that you did.”</span>
</div>

## Other instructions concerning the burnt offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:12–13; NUMBERS 28:3; EXODUS 29:38–42"> “Tell this to Aaron and his sons: These are the regulations concerning the offerings that will be completely burned on the altar: The offering must remain on the altar all during the night, and the fire on the altar must always be kept burning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 28:39–43; LEVITICUS 16:4; LEVITICUS 1:13"> The next morning the priest must put on his linen under-clothes and linen outer clothes. Then he must remove the ashes of the offering from the fire and put them beside the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:12; EZEKIEL 44:19; HEBREWS 13:11–13"> Then he must take off those clothes and put on other clothes, and take the ashes outside the camp, to a place that is acceptable to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:24; EXODUS 29:38–42; Nehemiah 13:31"> The fire on the altar must always be kept burning; the priest must not allow it to ◄go out/quit burning►. Each morning the priest must put more firewood on the fire. Then he must arrange more offerings on the fire, and burn on the altar the fat of the offerings to be burned to maintain fellowship with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The fire on the altar must be kept burning continually; the priest must not allow it to go out.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Other instructions concerning the grain offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:4; LEVITICUS 2:1–2; JOHN 6:32"> “These are the regulations concerning the offerings made from grain: Aaron’s sons must bring them to me in front of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:2; LEVITICUS 2:9"> The priest must take a handful of fine flour mixed with olive oil and incense and burn that on the altar. That handful will signify that the whole offering truly belongs to me. And the aroma while it burns will be pleasing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:29; LEVITICUS 2:3; LEVITICUS 10:12–13"> Aaron and his sons may eat the remaining part of the grain offering. But they must eat it in a holy place, in the courtyard of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:3; LEVITICUS 2:11; NUMBERS 18:9–10"> It must not have yeast mixed with it. Like the offerings for sin and the offerings to cause people to no longer be guilty of sin, that offering is very holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:29; NUMBERS 18:10; Haggai 2:12–14"> Any male descendants of Aaron are permitted to eat it, because it is forever their regular share of the offerings given to me and burned in the fire on the altar. Anyone else who touches those offerings made from grain will be punished by God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 29:2; EXODUS 16:36; LEVITICUS 2:1–16"> “Tell Aaron and his sons that this is the offering that they must bring to Yahweh on the day that any of them ◄is ordained/becomes a priest►: That person must bring two quarts/liters of fine flour as an offering made from grain. He must bring half of it in the morning and half of it in the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:5; LEVITICUS 7:9; 1 CHRONICLES 9:31"> He must mix it well with olive oil and bake it in a shallow pan. He must then break it into small pieces to be burned on the altar. And the aroma while it burns will be pleasing to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 29:22–25; Isaiah 53:10; LEVITICUS 8:21"> I have commanded that the descendants of Aaron who are appointed in turn to become the Supreme Priests after Aaron dies are the ones who must prepare those things. These offerings must be completely burned on the altar to be sacrifices to me, Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:10; LEVITICUS 6:16–17"> Every offering that a priest gives that is made from grain must be completely burned; none of it is to be eaten.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Other instructions concerning the sin offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:24; LEVITICUS 1:11; LEVITICUS 1:3"> “Tell Aaron and his sons: These are the regulations concerning the offerings that people must bring to me so that I will forgive the people for the sins they have committed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:17–18; EZEKIEL 44:28–29; HOSEA 4:8"> The animals must be slaughtered in my presence in the same place that the animals that are to be completely burned on the altar are slaughtered, in the courtyard in front of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 29:37; Haggai 2:12; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:1"> Any other person who touches any of its meat will be punished by God. And if its blood is splattered on your clothes, you must wash the clothes in a holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:33; LEVITICUS 15:12; HEBREWS 9:9–10"> If the meat is cooked in a clay pot, the pot must be broken afterwards. But if it is cooked in a bronze pot, the pot must be scoured afterwards and rinsed with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:25; LEVITICUS 6:17–18; NUMBERS 18:10"> Any male in a priest’s family may eat some of the cooked meat; that meat is very holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:18; LEVITICUS 16:27–28; HEBREWS 13:11"> But if the blood of those sin offerings is brought into the Sacred Tent to enable the people to be forgiven for having sinned, the meat of those animals must not be eaten. The meat must be completely burned.”</span>
</div>
</div>
