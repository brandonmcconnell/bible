## Where sacrifices are to be offered

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> “Speak to Aaron and his sons and to all the other Israeli people, and say this to them: ‘This is what Yahweh has commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 17:8; LEVITICUS 17:15; DEUTERONOMY 12:5–7"> If you sacrifice an ox or a lamb or a goat, you must bring it to the priest at the entrance of the Sacred Tent area, to present/offer it to me there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 10:7; DEUTERONOMY 12:5–21; Isaiah 66:3"> If you slaughter it to be a sacrifice anywhere else in the camp or outside the camp, you will be guilty of killing an animal in an unacceptable place. As a result you will no longer be allowed to be/associate with my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 22:9; 1 KINGS 14:23; 2 CHRONICLES 28:4"> That rule is in order that you Israeli people, instead of offering sacrifices in the open fields, will offer them to me by taking them to the priest at the entrance of the Sacred Tent area, to be offerings to maintain fellowship with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:17; LEVITICUS 3:2; EXODUS 29:18"> After the animal is slaughtered, the priest must sprinkle some of its blood against the altar at the entrance of the Sacred Tent, and burn its fat to be an aroma that is pleasing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:20; EXODUS 34:15; DEUTERONOMY 32:17"> You people must no longer give sacrifices to the demons that resemble goats which you are worshiping. You people must obey this command forever.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:2–3; LEVITICUS 17:4; LEVITICUS 17:10"> “And tell this to the people: ‘If any Israeli or any foreigner who is living among them brings an offering that is to be completely burned on the altar, or any other sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 17:4"> and does not bring it to the entrance of the Sacred Tent area to be a sacrifice to me, that person will no longer be allowed to associate with my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 17:11; LEVITICUS 3:17; DEUTERONOMY 12:23"> ‘I will reject &#91;IDM&#93; any Israeli or any foreigner who is living among you who eats any blood, and I will command that he no longer be allowed to associate with my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:22; 1 JOHN 1:7; ROMANS 3:25"> That is because the life of every creature is in its blood; I have declared that it is blood that is to be offered on the altar to enable people to be forgiven for their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 12:49"> That is why I say that you Israelis must not eat blood, and the foreigners who live among you must also not eat blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:16; EZEKIEL 24:7; LEVITICUS 7:26"> ‘Any Israeli or any foreigner who is living among you who hunts any animal or bird that is permitted for you to eat, must drain out the blood of the animal or bird that he kills, and cover the blood with dirt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 9:4; DEUTERONOMY 12:23; LEVITICUS 17:11–12"> This is because the life of every creature is in its blood. And that is why I have said &#91;DOU&#93; to you Israelis that anyone who eats blood must no longer be allowed to associate with my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:21; EXODUS 22:31; LEVITICUS 22:8"> ‘Anyone in Israel, including foreigners, who eats any of the meat of any creature that is found dead, or that had been killed by wild animals, must wash his clothes and bathe. Then he must not touch anyone until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:1; NUMBERS 19:19–20; LEVITICUS 7:18"> If he does not do those things, he will be punished for what he did.’”</span>
</div>
