## Rules for priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:25; LEVITICUS 21:11; LEVITICUS 10:6–7"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me, “Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and say to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 4:13; LEVITICUS 18:6"> ‘You priests must not cause yourselves to become unfit to do my work by touching corpses. Priests are permitted to touch only the corpses of close relatives, such as the priest’s mother or father or his son or daughter or his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Priests are also permitted to touch the corpse of a sister if she is not married, because she has no husband to bury her body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:16–17"> Priests must not cause themselves to become unfit to do my work &#91;DOU&#93; by touching corpses of people who were married to one of their close relatives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:1; EZEKIEL 44:20; LEVITICUS 19:27–28"> You priests must not shave your heads or shave the edges of your beards or cut your bodies to show that you are mourning for someone who has died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:11; LEVITICUS 18:21; 1 PETER 2:9"> You must act in ways that I, your God, consider to be holy, and not disgrace my name/reputation. You are the ones who present to me the offerings that are burned. It is as though those offerings are food for me, your God; so you must act in ways that are holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:13–14; EZEKIEL 44:22; LEVITICUS 21:8"> You priests must not marry women who have been prostitutes or who have been divorced from their husbands, because you priests are ◄set apart for me/holy►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:7–8; LEVITICUS 19:2; LEVITICUS 11:44–45"> You must consider that you are holy, because you offer food to me, your God. Consider yourselves to be holy because I, Yahweh, the one who caused you to be priests and the one who enables you to be holy, am holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 38:24; LEVITICUS 19:29; EZEKIEL 9:6"> If a priest’s daughter disgraces herself by becoming a prostitute, she disgraces her father; and she must be killed by being burned in a fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:6–7; LEVITICUS 16:32; MATTHEW 26:65"> The Supreme Priest is the one among his relatives who has been appointed for that work by having his head anointed with olive oil. He is also the one who has been appointed to wear the special garments that priests wear. He must not allow the hair on his head to remain uncombed, and he must not tear his clothes when he is mourning for someone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:14; LEVITICUS 21:1–2; NUMBERS 6:7"> He must not enter some place where there is a corpse. He must not do that and cause himself to become unfit for his work, even if it is his father or his mother who has died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:7; EXODUS 29:6–7; LEVITICUS 8:9–12"> He must not leave the Sacred Tent to join those who are mourning, because he would cause himself to become unfit for his work and would also defile/desecrate the Sacred Tent. He must not leave the Sacred Tent at that time, because by being anointed with olive oil he has been ◄appointed/set apart► to serve his God in the Sacred Tent. I, Yahweh, am the one who am commanding this.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:7; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:2; REVELATION 14:4"> Women whom you priests marry must be virgins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:7; EZEKIEL 44:22"> You priests must not marry widows or prostitutes or divorced women, because if you did that, if you later have sons, they would not be acceptable to be priests among your people. You must marry only virgins from among your own people. I am Yahweh, who sets priests apart to be holy.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 65:4; NUMBERS 16:5; LEVITICUS 10:3"> “Say this to Aaron: 'For all future time, none of your descendants who has any defects on his body will be allowed to come near the altar to offer sacrifices to me which will be like &#91;MET&#93; my food:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 3:2–3; LEVITICUS 22:19–25; TITUS 1:7"> No one who is blind or lame or deformed, or whose face is disfigured,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> no man with a crippled foot or a crippled hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:1; Isaiah 56:3"> no man who is a hunchback or a dwarf, no man whose eyes are defective, no man who has a skin disease &#91;DOU&#93; or whose testicles have been damaged is allowed to brings sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:6; LEVITICUS 21:8; LEVITICUS 21:17"> No descendant of Aaron, the first Supreme Priest, who has any defect is allowed to come to the altar to offer to me, his God, sacrifices that will be burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:3; NUMBERS 18:19; LEVITICUS 24:8–9"> Priests who have defects are permitted to eat the various kinds of holy food offered to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:12; EZEKIEL 44:9–14; EXODUS 30:6–8"> But because of their defects, they must not go near the curtain in the Sacred Tent or near the altar, because if they did that, they would desecrate my Sacred Tent. I am Yahweh, the one who sets those places apart as being holy.' ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 1:18; MALACHI 2:1–7; 2 TIMOTHY 2:2"> So Moses/I told this to Aaron and to his sons and to all the Israeli people.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
