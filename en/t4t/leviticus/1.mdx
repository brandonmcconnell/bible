## The burnt offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 19:3; EXODUS 25:22; EXODUS 29:42"> While Moses/I was standing near the entrance to the Sacred Tent, Yahweh called to him/me from inside the tent. He said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:18–19; ROMANS 12:6; 1 CHRONICLES 16:29"> “Tell this to the Israeli people: ‘When any of you brings an offering to Yahweh, bring one of your sheep or goats or cattle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:21; HEBREWS 9:14; LEVITICUS 22:19–24"> ‘If what you are offering is a bull that will be completely burned on the altar, it must not have any defects. You must take it to the entrance to the Sacred Tent, in order that it will be acceptable to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 29:10; EXODUS 29:15; LEVITICUS 4:20"> You must lay your hands on the head of the bull. When you do that, Yahweh will accept its death in your place to forgive/pardon you for the sins that you have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:11; LEVITICUS 3:8; LEVITICUS 3:2"> You must slaughter the bull in front of Yahweh. Then Aaron’s sons, who are priests, will bring the blood and sprinkle it against all sides of the altar near the entrance to the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:8; GENESIS 3:21"> You must remove the skin of the animal and cut the animal into pieces. You must wash the inner parts and the legs of the bull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:12–13; GENESIS 22:9; 1 CHRONICLES 21:26"> Then Aaron’s sons will put wood on the altar and light a fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:13–14; LEVITICUS 1:12; 1 KINGS 18:33"> Then they will arrange the pieces, including the head and the fat, on the burning wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 8:21; LEVITICUS 1:13; EPHESIANS 5:2"> Then one of the priests will completely burn all of it on the altar. And the aroma will be pleasing to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6–7; LEVITICUS 1:2–3; GENESIS 8:20"> ‘If you are offering a sheep or a goat, it must be a male without any defect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:5; EXODUS 40:22; LEVITICUS 7:2"> You must slaughter it in front of Yahweh, on the north side of the altar, and drain all the blood into a bowl. Then Aaron’s sons will sprinkle the blood against all sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> You must cut the animal into pieces. You must wash the inner parts and the legs of the animal. Then the priests will arrange the pieces, including the head and the fat, on the burning wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:9"> Then one of the priests will take all of it and completely burn all of it on the altar. And the aroma as it burns will be pleasing to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:7; LUKE 2:24; LEVITICUS 12:8"> ‘If what you are offering to Yahweh is a bird, you must offer a dove or a young pigeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:8–9; PSALMS 22:21; 1 JOHN 2:27"> The priest will take it to the altar and wring/twist off its head. Then he will burn the head on the altar. He will drain out the bird’s blood onto the side of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 PETER 1:2; LUKE 1:35; LEVITICUS 16:27"> Then he will remove the bird’s ◄craw/food-grinding pouch► and what is inside it, and throw it on the east side of the altar, where the ashes are thrown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 15:10; JOHN 19:30; LEVITICUS 1:9–10"> Then he will grasp the bird’s wings and partially tear the bird open. Then he will burn it completely in the fire on the altar. And the aroma will be pleasing to Yahweh.’</span>
</div>
