## The guilt offering

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:17; LEVITICUS 5:14–7; LEVITICUS 6:25"> “These are the regulations concerning the offerings to be made by people who are guilty of not giving to me the things that are required to be given to me. Those are very sacred offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:5; LEVITICUS 6:25; LEVITICUS 1:11"> Each animal that is to be offered by such people must be slaughtered in the same place where the animals that will be completely burned on the altar are slaughtered, and their blood must be sprinkled against all sides of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 29:13; LEVITICUS 3:15–16; PSALMS 51:6"> All their fat, the fat tails that are cut close to the backbone, and all the fat that covers the inner parts of the animals or which is attached to them, must be burned on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:4"> That includes the kidneys with their fat near the lower back muscle, and the protruding lobe that is attached to the liver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:13; GALATIANS 5:24; 1 PETER 4:1–2"> The priest must burn them on the altar to be offerings to me, Yahweh. They are an offering for the people to be forgiven for not doing what they were required to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:29; NUMBERS 18:9–10; LEVITICUS 2:3"> All the males in the priest’s family are permitted to eat its meat, but it must be eaten in a sacred place, because it is very sacred.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:25–26; LEVITICUS 14:13; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:13"> “The regulation is the same for the offerings to enable people to be forgiven for the sins they have committed and the offerings for when they are guilty of not giving to me the things that are required to be given to me. The meat of those offerings belongs to the priest who offers them in order that the people will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:11; EXODUS 29:14; ROMANS 13:14"> The priest who slaughters an animal that will be completely burned on the altar is permitted to keep the animal’s hide for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:9; EZEKIEL 44:29; LEVITICUS 2:10"> Offerings of things made from grain that are baked in an oven or cooked in a pan or ◄in a shallow pan/on a griddle► belong to the priest who makes those offerings for another person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 8:14; EXODUS 16:18"> And offerings of things made from grain, whether they were mixed with olive oil or not, also belong to the sons of Aaron.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The offerings to maintain fellowship with Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:18–21; EZEKIEL 45:15; LEVITICUS 3:1–17"> These are the regulations concerning the offerings that people make to maintain fellowship with Yahweh:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:4; NUMBERS 6:15; 1 PETER 2:5"> “If you bring an offering to thank Yahweh, along with the animal that you slaughter you must offer loaves of bread made with olive oil mixed with the flour but without yeast, and wafers that are made without yeast but with olive oil smeared on them, and loaves made from fine flour with olive oil well mixed with the flour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="AMOS 4:5; LEVITICUS 23:17; MATTHEW 13:33"> Along with that offering to thank Yahweh, you must bring an offering of loaves made with yeast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:19; EXODUS 29:27–28; NUMBERS 18:24–32"> You must bring one of each kind for an offering to Yahweh, but they belong to the priest who sprinkles against the altar the blood of the animal that is slaughtered as an offering to maintain fellowship with Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:29–30"> The meat of that offering must be eaten on the day that it is offered; none of it should be left to be eaten on the next day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:5–8; DEUTERONOMY 12:6; PSALMS 116:14"> “However, if your offering is the result of a solemn promise that you made to Yahweh, or if it is an offering that you make ◄voluntarily/without being required to►, you are permitted to eat some of the meat on the day it is offered, but anything that is left may be eaten on the next day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 12:10; HOSEA 6:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:4"> But any meat that is left until the third day must be completely burned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:7–8; LEVITICUS 11:41; NUMBERS 18:27"> If any meat from the offering to maintain fellowship with Yahweh is eaten on the third day, Yahweh will not accept that offering; it will be useless to offer it, because Yahweh will consider that it is worthless. Anyone who eats some of it will have to pay a penalty to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 11:41; TITUS 1:15; ACTS 10:15–16"> “Meat that touches something that God considers to be impure must not be eaten; it must be completely burned. Anyone who has performed the rituals to become acceptable to God is allowed to eat other meat which has been offered as a sacrifice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:2–33; GENESIS 17:14; LEVITICUS 22:3–7"> But if anyone who has not performed those rituals eats some of the meat of the offering to maintain fellowship with Yahweh, meat that belongs to Yahweh, he must no longer be allowed to associate with God’s people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 12:1–59; LEVITICUS 11:20; EZEKIEL 4:14"> If anyone touches something that God considers to be impure and very displeasing to him, whether it is from a human or from an animal, and then he eats any of the meat of the offering to maintain fellowship with Yahweh, meat that belongs to Yahweh, he must no longer be allowed to associate with God’s people.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Eating fat or blood is forbidden

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:16–17; LEVITICUS 17:6; 1 SAMUEL 2:15–17"> “Say this to the Israeli people: ‘Do not eat any of the fat of cattle or sheep or goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 22:31; LEVITICUS 17:15; LEVITICUS 22:8"> The fat of an animal that is found dead or that has been killed by a wild animal may be used for other purposes, but you must not eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Anyone who eats the fat of an animal from which an offering has been made to Yahweh must no longer be allowed to associate with God’s people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 9:4; LEVITICUS 3:17; ACTS 15:20"> And wherever you live, you must not eat the blood of any bird or animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:29; LEVITICUS 7:20–21; LEVITICUS 7:25"> If anyone eats blood, he must no longer be allowed to associate with God’s people.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The portions for the priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:20; 1 JOHN 1:7; LEVITICUS 3:1–17"> “Tell the Israeli people this: ‘Anyone who brings an offering to maintain fellowship with Yahweh must bring part of it to be a sacrifice to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:20; LEVITICUS 3:9; LEVITICUS 3:14"> He himself must bring the offering that will be burned in the fire. He must bring the fat along with the breast of the animal and lift it up in front of Yahweh to indicate that it is an offering to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:5; LEVITICUS 3:11; LEVITICUS 3:16"> The Supreme Priest must burn the fat on the altar, but the breast belongs to Aaron and all his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:20; LEVITICUS 7:34; LEVITICUS 9:21"> You must give to the Supreme Priest the right thigh of the animal that is sacrificed to maintain fellowship with Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:1–46; LEVITICUS 6:1–30; LEVITICUS 7:3"> The son of Aaron who offers the blood and the fat of that sacrifice must be given the right thigh of the animal as his share.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:18–19; LEVITICUS 10:14–15; EXODUS 29:9"> From the offerings that the Israeli people give to maintain fellowship with Yahweh, he has declared that he has given to Aaron and his sons the breast that is lifted up and the right thigh that is offered; those portions must always be their regular share from the Israeli people.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 JOHN 2:20; 1 JOHN 2:27; NUMBERS 18:7–19"> Those are the portions of the offerings brought/given to Yahweh and burned in fire that are allotted to Aaron and his sons on the day that they are set apart to serve Yahweh as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:12; LEVITICUS 8:30; EXODUS 40:13–15"> Yahweh commands that on the day that the priests are appointed &#91;MTY&#93;, the Israeli people must always give those portions to the priests.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="EXODUS 29:1; LEVITICUS 1:1–6; LEVITICUS 6:9–18"> Those are the regulations for the offerings that are to be completely burned on the altar, the offerings made from grain, the offerings to enable people to become acceptable to God again, the offerings for when people are guilty of not giving to Yahweh the things that are required to be given to him, the offerings given when the priests are appointed, and the offerings to maintain fellowship with Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:1–2"> They are regulations that Yahweh gave to Moses/me on Sinai Mountain in the Sinai Desert, on the day that he commanded the Israeli people to start bringing their offerings to him.</span>
</div>
</div>
