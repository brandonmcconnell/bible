## Food that is acceptable to God and food that is not

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh said to Aaron and Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:11; LEVITICUS 11:11; HEBREWS 9:10"> “Tell the Israeli people that this is what Yahweh says: From all the animals that live on the land, these are the ones that you are permitted to eat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:17; DEUTERONOMY 6:6–7"> The ones that have hooves that are completely split and that ◄chew their cuds/bring their food up from their stomachs to chew it again►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> There are some animals that chew their cuds but do not have split hooves, and some animals that have split hooves but do not chew their cuds. You must not eat any of those animals. For example, camels chew their cuds but do not have split hooves, so they are unacceptable for you to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 104:18; PROVERBS 30:26; MATTHEW 7:26"> Rock badgers chew their cuds but do not have split hooves, so they are unacceptable for you to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:7"> Rabbits chew their cuds but do not have split hooves, so they are unacceptable for you to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 66:17; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> Pigs have completely split hooves but they do not chew their cuds, so they are unacceptable for you to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; COLOSSIANS 2:16; HEBREWS 9:10"> All of those animals are unacceptable for you, so you must not eat their meat or even touch their carcasses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:6; JAMES 2:18; ACTS 20:21"> From all the creatures that live in the oceans and the streams, you are permitted to eat any that have fins and scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:27; LEVITICUS 7:18; PSALMS 139:21–22"> But you must detest and not eat those that do not have fins and scales. That includes ones that are very small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> You must despise them, and you must not eat their meat, and you must detest their carcasses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> You must detest everything that lives in the water that does not have fins and scales.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 39:27–30; JEREMIAH 4:22; TITUS 3:3"> There are some birds that you must detest and not eat. They include eagles, bearded vultures, black vultures,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> kites, any kind of falcon/buzzard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 12:24; 1 KINGS 17:6; PROVERBS 30:17"> any kind of raven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 34:11–15; EPHESIANS 5:7–11; EPHESIANS 2:2–3"> horned owls, screech owls, seagulls, any kind of hawk,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> small owls, cormorants, large owls,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> white owls, desert owls, vultures that eat dead animals,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> storks, any kind of heron, hoopoes, and bats.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:10; PHILIPPIANS 3:18–19; LEVITICUS 11:23"> You must detest and not eat flying insects that sometimes walk on the ground &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But you are permitted to eat creatures with wings that sometimes walk on the ground if they have jointed legs for hopping around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:4; MARK 1:6; EXODUS 10:4–5"> They include locusts, crickets, and grasshoppers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> But you must detest and not eat other insects with wings that have four legs.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:33; EPHESIANS 2:1–3; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:17"> ‘There are certain creatures that if you touch their carcasses you will become defiled. Anyone who touches their carcasses must not touch other people until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:5; LEVITICUS 14:8; NUMBERS 31:24"> Anyone who picks up one of their carcasses must wash his clothes and not touch other people until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> ‘The animals whose carcasses you must not touch are those that have hooves that are not completely divided or animals that do not chew their cuds. Anyone who touches the carcasses of any of those animals becomes ◄defiled/unacceptable to me►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:23; LEVITICUS 11:20"> From all the animals that walk on the ground, you must not touch the carcasses of those that have paws to walk on. Anyone who touches one of their carcasses must not touch other people until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:24–25"> Anyone who picks up one of their carcasses must wash his clothes and not touch other people until that evening, because touching their carcasses ◄defiles you/causes you to become unacceptable to me►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:41–42; 2 TIMOTHY 3:2–5; PSALMS 10:3"> ‘From all the animals that walk on the ground, these are the ones that ◄defile you/cause you to become unacceptable to me► if you touch them: Moles, rats, any kind of lizard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> geckos, skinks, and chameleons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:24–25; LEVITICUS 11:8"> Those creatures that scurry across the ground ◄defile you/cause you to become unacceptable to me►; anyone who touches one of their carcasses must not touch other people until the evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:12; TITUS 3:5; TITUS 2:14"> When one of those creatures dies and falls on something, the thing that it falls on, whatever it is used for, will become ◄defiled/unacceptable to me►, whether it is made of wood or cloth or the hide of some animal or from rough cloth. You must put it in water. Then you must not use it until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:28; LEVITICUS 15:12; LEVITICUS 14:45"> If one of them falls into a clay pot, everything in it becomes defiled, and you must break that pot into pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="TITUS 1:15; PROVERBS 21:4; PROVERBS 21:27"> If you pour water from that pot on any food, you must not eat that food. And you must not drink any water from that pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:12; LEVITICUS 6:28; LEVITICUS 11:33"> Anything that one of the carcasses of those creatures falls on becomes ◄defiled/unacceptable to me►; even if it falls on an oven or a cooking pot, anything that it falls on must be broken into pieces. It becomes unacceptable to me and you must not use it again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:1; JOHN 4:14"> If one of their carcasses falls into a spring or a pit for storing water, the water may still be drunk, but anyone who touches one of those carcasses becomes unacceptable to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:37; 1 PETER 1:23; 1 JOHN 5:18"> If one of those carcasses falls on seeds that are to be planted, those seeds are still acceptable to be planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> But if water has been put on the seeds and then a carcass falls on them, the seeds must be thrown away.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:40; LEVITICUS 11:24; LEVITICUS 15:7"> ‘If an animal whose meat you are permitted to eat dies, anyone who touches its carcass must not touch other people until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:8; EZEKIEL 44:31; DEUTERONOMY 14:21"> Anyone who eats some meat from that carcass must wash his clothes, and then he must not touch anyone until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:29; LEVITICUS 11:20; LEVITICUS 11:23"> ‘All creatures that scurry across the ground, including those that move on their bellies and those that crawl, are detestable, and they must not be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:25; LEVITICUS 11:41–42"> Do not ◄defile yourselves/cause you to become unacceptable to me► by eating any of those creatures &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="EXODUS 20:2; 1 PETER 1:15–16; LEVITICUS 19:2"> I am Yahweh your God, and I am holy, so you must consecrate yourselves and be holy. You must avoid eating things that cause you to be unacceptable to me. Do not cause yourselves to become unacceptable to me by eating creatures that scurry across the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:44; EXODUS 20:2; EXODUS 6:7"> I am Yahweh, the one who freed you from being slaves in Egypt, in order to be your God. Therefore, because I am holy, you must be holy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:54; LEVITICUS 15:32; EZEKIEL 43:12"> 'Those are the regulations concerning animals and birds, all the living creatures that live in water or scurry across the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:10; EZEKIEL 44:23; ROMANS 14:13–23"> You must learn what things I say are acceptable to me and what things are not, and learn what things you are permitted to eat and what things you are not permitted to eat.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
