## The reasons for Edom’s punishment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 1:11; MALACHI 1:3–4; GENESIS 27:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">You acted cruel in a cruel way toward your relatives who are descendants of Jacob, the twin brother of your ancestor Esau.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So now you will be disgraced forever; you will be completely destroyed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 137:7; NAHUM 3:10; JOEL 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Foreigners entered the gates of Jerusalem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ◄cast lots/threw small stones that were marked► to decide what valuable things they would take away;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you were as bad as those foreigners,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you just stood there and did not help the Israelis.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 35:15; MICAH 4:11; PROVERBS 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">You should not have ◄gloated/been happy► about the disaster that the Israelis were experiencing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You should not have been happy when their towns were ruined.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You should not have made fun of them when they were suffering.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:15; EZEKIEL 35:5; EZEKIEL 35:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are my people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so you should not have entered their city gates when they were experiencing those disasters</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you should not have laughed at them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And you should not have taken away their valuable possessions.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="AMOS 1:6; PSALMS 31:8; OBADIAH 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You should not have stood at crossroads</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to catch those who were trying to escape.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You should not have captured them and put them into the hands of their enemies</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when they were experiencing those disasters.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOEL 3:17; JEREMIAH 49:12; JEREMIAH 25:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">My people in Jerusalem have been punished</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because I was very angry with them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It is as though &#91;MET&#93; they drank a cup of very bitter liquid on Zion, my sacred hill.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But I will punish &#91;MET&#93; all the other nations even more severely,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cause them to disappear completely.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 12:6; Isaiah 10:17; OBADIAH 1:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The descendants &#91;MTY&#93; of Jacob and his son Joseph will be like &#91;MET&#93; a fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the people &#91;MTY&#93; of Edom will be like &#91;MET&#93; stubble that will be completely burned in that fire.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Not one person will remain alive.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.”</span></span>
