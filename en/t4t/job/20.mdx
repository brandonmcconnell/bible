<div className="bt">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 90:5; PSALMS 73:20; Isaiah 29:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will be forgotten like &#91;SIM&#93; a dream is,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will exist no more.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will vanish, like visions that people see during the night.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 7:8; JOB 7:10; JOB 8:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who saw those people previously will never see them again;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even their families &#91;MTY&#93; will not see them any more.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 15:16; PSALMS 10:7; ECCLESIASTES 11:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Doing wicked things was like having sweet food in their mouths,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they wanted to continue to taste it.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
