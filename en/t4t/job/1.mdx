## Job and his family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JAMES 5:11; EZEKIEL 14:14; EZEKIEL 14:20"> In the land named Uz, there was a man named Job. He was a very godly/righteous &#91;DOU&#93; man, who greatly respected/revered God and always avoided doing evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 127:3–5; JOB 42:13; PSALMS 128:3"> He had seven sons and three daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 29:25; JOB 42:12; JUDGES 6:3"> He owned 7,000 sheep, 3,000 camels, 1,000 oxen, and 500 female donkeys. He also had many servants. He was the richest man in all the area east of the Jordan River.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:1; PSALMS 133:1"> Each year, Job’s sons made feasts in their houses to celebrate their birthdays. Whenever each one made a feast, he would invite all his brothers and his three sisters to come and eat together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 42:8; GENESIS 8:20; 1 SAMUEL 16:5"> When each celebration ended, Job would summon them. He said to himself, “Perhaps one of my sons has sinned and said something evil about God in his heart.”</span>
</div>

## Satan wanted to test Job

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 2:1; JOB 38:7; ZECHARIAH 3:1"> One day, the angels came and gathered together in front of Yahweh, and Satan came too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 PETER 5:8; REVELATION 12:12–17; MATTHEW 12:43"> Yahweh asked Satan, “Where have you come from?”<br />Satan replied, “I have come from the earth, where I been traveling back and forth, seeing what is happening.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 2:3; JOB 1:1; NUMBERS 12:7–8"> Yahweh said to Satan, “Have you ◄noticed/thought about► my faithful servant Job? <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:26; 1 TIMOTHY 4:8; JOB 2:10"> Satan replied to Yahweh, “What you say is true, but Job greatly respects you only because of what you have done for him &#91;RHQ&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 34:7; ZECHARIAH 2:5; 1 PETER 1:5"> You have always &#91;RHQ&#93; protected &#91;MET&#93; him and his family and everything that he owns. You have made him very prosperous/rich. He has livestock all over this land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 2:5; JOB 19:21; REVELATION 16:11"> But if you take away &#91;MTY&#93; everything that he has, he will curse you openly. &#91;IDM&#93;”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 21:34; PROVERBS 27:1; JOB 1:4"> One day after that, Job’s sons and daughters were feasting and drinking wine at the home of their oldest brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 15:13; JEREMIAH 51:31; 1 SAMUEL 4:17"> While they were doing that, a messenger arrived at Job’s home and said to him, “While your oxen were plowing the fields and the donkeys were grazing nearby,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 10:7; JOB 6:19; Isaiah 45:14"> a group of men from the Sheba area came and attacked us. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 KINGS 1:12; GENESIS 19:24; 1 KINGS 18:38"> While he was still talking to Job, another messenger arrived. I am the only one who escaped to come and tell you what happened.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 11:28; HABAKKUK 1:6; GENESIS 11:31"> While he was still talking to Job, another messenger arrived. I am the only one who escaped to come and tell you what happened.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:15; 1 TIMOTHY 6:7; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:18"> He said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“When I was born, I had no clothes on.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When I die, I will not take any clothes with me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh gave me everything that I possessed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and now he has taken it all away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But ◄we should/I will► always praise Yahweh anyway! &#91;MTY&#93;”</span></span>
