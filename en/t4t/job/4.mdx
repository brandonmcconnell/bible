## Eliphaz replied to Job

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 8:1; JOB 22:1; JOB 15:1"> Then Eliphaz, from Teman, replied to Job. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 32:18–20; ACTS 4:20; JEREMIAH 6:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Will you please let me say something to you?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am not &#91;RHQ&#93; able to remain silent any longer.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 35:3; HEBREWS 12:12; PROVERBS 10:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the past, you have instructed/taught many people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you have encouraged those who were weak.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:18; PSALMS 145:14; HEBREWS 12:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">By what you said, you have helped those who ◄needed spiritual help/almost quit trusting in God► &#91;MET&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you have enabled them to become spiritually strong again &#91;MET&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 19:21; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:16; JOB 2:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now, when you experience disasters, you become discouraged.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The disasters hit you, and you are stunned.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:26; PROVERBS 14:26; JOB 1:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">You revere God; ◄does that not cause you to trust in him?/that should cause you to trust in him.► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If you were guiltless, you would &#91;RHQ&#93; be confident that God would not have allowed these disasters to happen to you!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 37:25; 2 PETER 2:9; JOB 36:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Think about this: Do innocent people die while they are still young &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Does God get rid of godly people &#91;RHQ&#93;? No!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:7–8; PROVERBS 22:8; HOSEA 8:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">What I have experienced is this: Just as &#91;MET&#93; farmers who plant bad seeds do not harvest good crops,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">just as those who start trouble for others, later bring trouble on themselves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 15:30; Isaiah 11:4; Isaiah 30:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They die when God angrily blows his breath on them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when he is very angry with them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 58:6; PROVERBS 30:14; PSALMS 3:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even though wicked people may be very powerful like young lions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God will get rid of them &#91;MET&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 34:10; JOB 8:3–4; JOB 1:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will die like fierce lions that starve to death when there are no animals that they can kill and eat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their children will be separated from each other like young lions separate from each other to find food.”</span></span>

## Eliphaz says that he learned a secret

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 26:14; PSALMS 62:11; 1 CORINTHIANS 13:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I heard a message that someone came</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and whispered to me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 33:14–16; GENESIS 2:21; GENESIS 31:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">He spoke to me at night when I was having a bad dream that disturbed/frightened me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while I was fast asleep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="REVELATION 1:17; JOB 33:19; LUKE 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">It caused me to be afraid and tremble;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it caused all my bones to shake.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:7; DANIEL 5:6; PSALMS 104:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">A ghost glided past my face</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and caused the hair on on the back of my neck to stand straight up.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">It stopped, but I could not see what form it had.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But I could sense that there was some being in front of me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it said in a quiet voice,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 9:2; JOB 25:4; ECCLESIASTES 7:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘◄Does God consider anyone to be righteous?/No human beings can be righteous in God’s sight!► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">◄Their creator cannot consider them to be pure./Can their creator consider them to be pure?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 25:5–6; 2 PETER 2:4; JUDE 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">God cannot be sure that his own angels will always do what is right;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he declares that some of them have done what is wrong.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 10:9; GENESIS 2:7; GENESIS 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">So he certainly cannot trust human beings who were made from dust and clay,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who are crushed as easily as moths are crushed!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 20:7; JOB 14:14; JOB 16:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">People are sometimes well in the morning, but in the evening they are dead.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They are gone forever and do not even know it (OR, and no one pays any attention to it).</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 36:12; PSALMS 39:11; JOB 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are like &#91;MET&#93; tents that collapse suddenly:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They die suddenly before they become wise.’”</span></span>
