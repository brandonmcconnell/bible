## Job replied to the three friends again

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Job replied to Eliphaz and the others:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 13:4–5; PSALMS 69:26; JOB 11:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I have heard things like that before;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all of you, instead of helping me, are only causing me to feel more miserable.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 6:26; JOB 15:2; TITUS 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will your speeches, which are only hot air, never end &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Eliphaz, what bothers/irritates you so much that you continue replying to me?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 109:25; PSALMS 22:7; LAMENTATIONS 2:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">If it were you three and not I who were suffering,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I could say the things that you are saying;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I could make great speeches to criticize/condemn you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I could shake my head at you to ridicule you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:9; GALATIANS 6:1; Isaiah 35:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But, unlike all of you, with what I said &#91;MTY&#93; I would encourage you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and try to cause your pain to be less.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 10:1; PSALMS 88:15–18; PSALMS 77:1–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">“But now, if I talk, my pain does not decrease,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and if I am silent, my pain still certainly does not &#91;RHQ&#93; go away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 7:3; JOB 1:15–19; PSALMS 6:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">God has now taken away all my strength,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he has destroyed my family.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 10:17; JOB 19:20; RUTH 1:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">He has shriveled me up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and people think that shows that I am a sinner.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And people see that I am only skin and bones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they think that proves that I am guilty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 35:16; JOB 13:24; LAMENTATIONS 2:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because God is very angry with me and hates me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is as though he is a wild animal that &#91;MET&#93; has gnashed his teeth at me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because he is my enemy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 35:15; PSALMS 22:13; Isaiah 50:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">People gape/stare at me with their mouths open to sneer at me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they have struck me on the face/cheek to ridicule me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they crowd around me to threaten me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 27:12; PSALMS 7:14; PSALMS 31:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though God has handed me over to ungodly people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and turned me over to the wicked &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 7:20; LAMENTATIONS 3:11–12; LAMENTATIONS 3:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Previously, I was living peacefully,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he crushed me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is as though he grabbed my neck and smashed me to pieces.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It is as though &#91;MET&#93; he set me up like a target;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 20:25; JOB 6:4; LAMENTATIONS 2:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">people are surrounding me and shooting arrows at me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His arrows pierce my kidneys</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cause the bile from my liver to spill onto the ground,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and God does not pity me at all.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 15:8; LAMENTATIONS 3:3–5; JOEL 2:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though &#91;MET&#93; I am a wall that he is breaking through;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he rushes at me like &#91;SIM&#93; a soldier attacking his enemies.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 37:34; PSALMS 7:5; PSALMS 75:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Because I am mourning, I wear pieces of rough cloth that I have sewed together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I sit here in the dirt, very depressed/discouraged.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARK 14:34; PSALMS 102:3–5; LAMENTATIONS 1:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">My face is red because I have cried very much,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there are dark circles around my eyes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:8; PSALMS 66:18–19; JOB 27:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">All this has happened to me even though I have not acted violently toward anyone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I always pray sincerely/honestly to God.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 26:21; EZEKIEL 24:7; GENESIS 4:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I die, I want the ground &#91;APO&#93; to act as though I had been murdered and cry out against those who killed me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I do not want anyone to stop me while I am demanding that God act justly toward me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ROMANS 1:9; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">But even now, I know that there is someone in heaven who will testify for me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will say that what I have done is right.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:7; PSALMS 142:2; JOB 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">My three friends scorn/ridicule me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but my eyes are full of tears while I cry out to God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 6:10; Isaiah 45:9; ROMANS 9:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">I pray that the one who knows what I have done would come to plead with God for me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like people plead for their friends.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 14:10; JOB 14:14; JOB 14:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I say this because within a few years I will die;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will walk along the roadto the grave from which I will never return.”</span></span>
</div>
