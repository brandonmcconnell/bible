## Bildad’s first speech to Job

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 2:11"> Then Bildad, from the Shuah area, spoke to Job. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 6:26; JOB 15:2; 1 KINGS 19:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Job, how much longer will you talk like this?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">What you say is only hot air.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; 2 CHRONICLES 19:7; GENESIS 18:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Almighty God certainly never does &#91;RHQ&#93; what is unfair/unjust.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He always does &#91;LIT&#93; what is right/fair.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 1:5; JOB 1:18–19; JOB 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, it is evident that your children have sinned against him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">therefore he has caused them to be punished for evil things that they have done.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JAMES 4:7–10; HEBREWS 3:7–8; JOB 11:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But, if you will now earnestly request &#91;DOU&#93; Almighty God to help you,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 51:9; Isaiah 3:10; PROVERBS 15:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">and if you are pure and honest/righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will surely do something good for you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and reward you by giving your family back to you and enabling you to prosper.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 42:12–13; MATTHEW 13:31–32; PROVERBS 19:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">And even though you think that you were not very prosperous/wealthy before,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">during the last part of your life you will become very wealthy.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:7; DEUTERONOMY 4:32; JOB 15:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I request you to think about what happened long ago</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and consider what our ancestors found out.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 29:15; PSALMS 144:4; JOB 14:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">It seems as though we were born only yesterday</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we know very little &#91;HYP&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">our time here on the earth disappears quickly, like a shadow &#91;MET&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:15; MATTHEW 12:35; JOB 12:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, why do you not allow your ancestors to teach you and tell you something?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Allow them to tell you from what they learned!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 2:3; Isaiah 19:5–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Papyrus can certainly not &#91;RHQ&#93; grow in places where there is no marsh/swamp;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">reeds certainly cannot &#91;RHQ&#93; flourish/grow where there is no water.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:6; PSALMS 129:6–7; MATTHEW 13:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">If they do not have enough water, while they are still blossoming,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they wither more quickly than other plants wither.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 9:17; JOB 11:20; JOB 13:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who do not pay attention to what God says are like those reeds;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">godless people stop confidently expecting that good things will happen to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The things they confidently expect to happen do not happen;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">things they trust will help them are as fragile as &#91;MET&#93; a spider’s web.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 27:18; LUKE 6:47–49; MATTHEW 7:24–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">If they lean against a house (OR, trust in their wealth; OR, lean on a spider web), it does not ◄endure/protect them► &#91;LIT&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they cling to things to be protected, but those things do not remain firm.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 80:11; JOB 21:7–15; JOB 5:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Godless people are like plants &#91;MET&#93; that are watered before the sun rises;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their shoots spread all over the gardens.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:24; MARK 11:20; JUDE 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The roots of those plants twist around piles of stones</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cling tightly to rocks.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 7:10; PSALMS 37:36; JOB 7:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">But if those plants are pulled out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is as though the place where they were planted says ‘They were never here!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And that is what happens to wicked people who do not heed what God says.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 20:5; PSALMS 113:7; 1 SAMUEL 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Truly, evil people &#91;MET&#93; are not joyful &#91;IRO&#93; for a long time;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">other people come and take their places.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 4:7; PSALMS 37:37; PSALMS 37:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">:So, I tell you, Job, God will not reject you if you are truly godly/righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he does not help &#91;IDM&#93; evil people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 126:2; GENESIS 21:6; PSALMS 126:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will enable you &#91;MTY&#93; to continually laugh</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to always shout joyfully.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 132:18; PSALMS 109:29; PSALMS 35:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">But those who hate you will be very ashamed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the homes of wicked people will disappear.”</span></span>
</div>
