## Elihu replied to Job and his three friends

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 10:7; JOB 33:9; JOB 10:2"> Then those three men stopped answering Job, because they could not convince Job that he was wrong in claiming that he had not done anything that was wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 22:21; JOB 40:8; JOB 27:2"> Then Elihu, the son of Barachel, a descendant of Buz, from the clan of Ram, became very angry at Job. He was angry because Job continued to claim that he was righteous/innocent, and that God was wrong to punish him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 8:6; JOB 24:25; JOB 22:5–30"> He was also angry with Job’s three friends, because they had declared that Job must have done many things that were wrong, but they were unable to convince Job that what they said was true. As a result, it seemed to Elihu that God did what was wrong by punishing Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 32:11–12; PROVERBS 18:13"> Elihu was younger than the others, so he waited until they had finished speaking before he replied to Job.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 32:2; EXODUS 32:19"> But when Elihu realized that the three men had no more to say &#91;MTY&#93; to Job, he became angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 15:10; 1 TIMOTHY 5:1; TITUS 2:6"> And this is what he said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I am young, and you all are much older than I am.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So, I was timid, and I was afraid to tell you what I was thinking.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:31; JOB 12:12; HEBREWS 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I thought, ‘Let those &#91;PRS&#93; who are much older speak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because older people should be able to say things that are wise.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 38:36; JOB 33:4; 2 TIMOTHY 3:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the Spirit of Almighty God is within people, and it is he who enables them to be wise.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:25; JOB 12:20; ECCLESIASTES 4:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is not people becoming old that enables them to be wise;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not all old people understand what is right.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 7:25; 1 CORINTHIANS 7:40"> <span className="pl pl-1">“So, I say, ‘Listen to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and allow me to say what I think.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 29:21; JOB 5:27; PROVERBS 28:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I waited for you all to speak;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I wanted to hear the wise things that you would say.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I waited while you thought carefully about what would be the right things to say.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 32:3; 1 TIMOTHY 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I paid attention carefully,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but surprisingly, none of you were able to prove that what Job said was wrong.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:27–29; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, do not say to yourselves, ‘We have discovered what is wise!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It is God who must ◄refute Job/show that what Job said was wrong►, because you three have not been able to do that.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Job was replying to you, not to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but I will not reply to him by saying what you three said.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 6:24–25; MATTHEW 22:46; JOB 29:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">“You three are dismayed because you have not been able to convince him that he was wrong,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so you are not saying any more to him &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JAMES 1:19; JOB 13:5; AMOS 5:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But because you do not speak, I certainly will not wait any longer &#91;RHQ&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you merely stand there and do not reply any more.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 35:3–4; JOB 33:12; JOB 32:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">So now I also will reply to Job</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and say what I think.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 39:3; ACTS 4:20; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have plenty to say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and my spirit compels me to say it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">My inner being is like &#91;SIM&#93; a ◄wineskin/bag that has &lt;new/fermenting&gt; wine in it►,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it will soon burst.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:6–7; JOB 21:3; JOB 13:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I must speak &#91;MTY&#93;, in order that I do not have to keep holding what I want to say;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I must say something &#91;MTY&#93; to reply to you all.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:15; JOB 13:8; MATTHEW 22:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will speak fairly, not favoring any of you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will not try to flatter anyone.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 12:2–3; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:5; PROVERBS 29:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I really do not know how to flatter people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and if I did that, God would soon get rid of me.”</span></span>
</div>
