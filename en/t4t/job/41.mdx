<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 18:8; Isaiah 30:33; HABAKKUK 3:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their breath can cause coals to blaze,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and flames shoot out from their mouths.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> <span className="pl pl-1">They certainly are not afraid of weapons made of straw or rotten wood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they are not even afraid of weapons made of iron or bronze!</span></span>
