## I appeal to you that you present yourselves to God by making yourselves like living sacrifices, which is the appropriate way to serve him. Do not let anything non-Christian determine how you act, but instead let God change your way of thinking.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 PETER 2:5; ROMANS 6:13; HEBREWS 13:15–16"> My fellow believers, since God has acted mercifully toward you in so many ways, I appeal to all of you that you present yourselves &#91;SYN&#93; to him by making yourselves like holy sacrifices &#91;MET&#93;. Make yourselves sacrifices that he is pleased with, sacrifices that are living/alive, not ones that are dead/killed. Since God has done so much for you/us, this is the only appropriate way to serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 PETER 1:14; EPHESIANS 4:22–24; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:17"> Do not let anything non-Christian determine how you should act. Instead, let God change your way of life by making your way of thinking new, in order that you may know what he wants you to do. That is, you will know what is good, and you will know what pleases God, and you will know how to be all that he wants you to be.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Do not think about yourselves more highly than you should. Instead, think about yourselves sensibly, in a way that corresponds to the abilities that God has given to you because you trust in Christ. May we do diligently and cheerfully what God has given us the ability to do.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:3; PHILIPPIANS 2:3–8; PROVERBS 26:12"> Because God has kindly appointed me to be his apostle, which I did not deserve, I say this to every one of you: Do not think about yourselves more highly than what is right for you to think! Instead, think about yourselves in a sensible way that corresponds to the abilities that God has given you because you trust in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:27; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:12–14; EPHESIANS 4:15–16"> Although a person has one body, it consists of many parts. All of the parts are needed for the body, but they do not all function the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:20; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:17; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:27–28"> Similarly we, although we are many, are united into one group because of our relationship with Christ, and we belong to one another. So no one should act as though he is needed by God more than others are!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 PETER 4:10–11; 1 CORINTHIANS 7:7; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:28–31"> Instead, since each one of us can do various things that differ according to the abilities that God has given to us, we should do them diligently and cheerfully! Those whom God has enabled to speak messages from him should speak what corresponds to what they believe God told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:11; 2 TIMOTHY 4:2; ACTS 13:1"> Those whom God has enabled to serve others should do that. Those whom God has enabled to teach his truth should do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 9:7; 1 TIMOTHY 5:17; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:28"> Those whom God has enabled to encourage/exhort his people should do that. Those who share their goods/money with others should do it sincerely/generously. Those who lead the congregation should do it wholeheartedly. Those who help the needy should do it cheerfully.</span>
</div>
</div>

## In the various ways in which you act toward people, love them sincerely.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 34:14; 1 PETER 4:8; 1 TIMOTHY 1:5"> Love others sincerely! Hate what is evil! Continue to eagerly do what God considers to be good!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:3; JOHN 13:34–35; HEBREWS 13:1"> Love one another as members of the same family do; and, you should be ◄the first ones/eager► to honor each other!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:10–11; ACTS 18:25; REVELATION 3:15–16"> Do not be lazy. Instead, be eager to serve the Lord God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 15:13; HEBREWS 10:36; PSALMS 62:8"> Rejoice because you are confidently awaiting what God will do for you! When you suffer, be patient! Keep praying and never give up!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:16; 2 CORINTHIANS 9:12; 1 JOHN 3:17"> If any of God’s people lacks anything, share with them what you have! Readily take care of travelers who need a place to stay!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 PETER 3:9; MATTHEW 5:44; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:15"> Ask God to be kind to those who ◄persecute you/cause you to suffer► because you believe in Jesus! Ask him to be kind to them; do not ask him to cause bad things to happen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:26; HEBREWS 13:3; PSALMS 35:13–14"> If someone is joyful, you should rejoice also! If someone is sad, you should also be sad!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 PETER 3:8; PROVERBS 3:7; JAMES 3:13–17"> Desire for others what you desire for yourselves (OR, Live harmoniously with each other)! Do not do things because you want to be famous! Instead, be content to do tasks that others consider that only unimportant people do (OR, to associate with unimportant people). Do not consider yourselves wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:22; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:15; MATTHEW 5:39"> Do not do something evil to anyone who has done something evil to you. Act in a way that all people will recognize as good!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:14; MATTHEW 5:9; ROMANS 14:19"> Live peacefully with other people whenever it is possible, to the extent that you can influence the situation.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instead of avenging yourselves, allow God to avenge you; and instead of being overcome by something evil done to you, overcome evil deeds by doing good to those who do evil to you, because this is what the Scriptures command.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:35; HEBREWS 10:30; PROVERBS 24:29"> My fellow believers whom I love, do not do something evil in return when people do something evil to you! Instead, allow God to punish them &#91;MTY&#93;, because it is &#123;someone has&#125; written in the Scriptures that the Lord said, “‘It is my responsibility to take revenge; I am the one who will punish people who do something something evil to you &#91;DOU&#93;,’ says the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:21–22; MATTHEW 5:44; LUKE 6:27"> Instead of doing something evil to those who have done something evil to you, do as the Scriptures teach: “If your (sg) enemies are hungry, feed them! If they are thirsty, give them something to drink! By doing that, you (sg) will cause them to feel ashamed and perhaps they will change their attitude toward you &#91;IDM&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 PETER 3:9; LUKE 6:27–30; PROVERBS 16:32"> Do not let evil things that others have done to you overcome you (sg) by making you do evil things to them! &#91;PRS&#93; Instead, overcome the evil things that they have done by doing good deeds to them!</span>
</div>
</div>
