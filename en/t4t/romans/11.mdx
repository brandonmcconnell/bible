## God has certainly not rejected all Jews. I am evidence of that. Just like in the past, there is also at the present time a small group of us Jews who have become believers.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 11:22; 1 SAMUEL 12:22; PHILIPPIANS 3:5"> Because of that, if anyone should ask, “Has God rejected his people the Jews?” I would declare that he certainly has not rejected all of us! You can realize that by remembering that I also belong to the people of Israel. I am a descendant of Abraham, and I belong to the tribe of Benjamin, but God has not rejected me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 94:14; ROMANS 8:29–30; EXODUS 32:1"> No, God has not rejected his people, whom he chose long ago to be people whom he would bless in a special way. Remember &#91;RHQ&#93; what is written &#91;PRS&#93; in the Scriptures about Elijah when he mistakenly complained to God about the people of Israel, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:10–18; 1 KINGS 18:13; Nehemiah 9:26"> “Lord, they have killed the rest of your (sg) prophets, they have destroyed your altars. I am the only one who believes in you who remains alive, and now they are trying to kill me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:18; NUMBERS 25:3; ZEPHANIAH 1:4"> God answered him like this: “You(sg) are not the only one who believes in me! I have protected for myself not just you, but 7,000 other men who have not worshipped the false god Baal. ◄Remember that!/Do you not remember that?► &#91;RHQ&#93;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 9:27; EPHESIANS 1:5–6; ROMANS 9:11"> So, similarly, there is also at this time a small group of us Jews who have become believers. God has chosen us to become believers (OR, his people) only because he acts kindly toward us, in ways that we do not deserve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:21; ROMANS 4:4–5; 2 TIMOTHY 1:9"> Since it is because he acts kindly toward those whom he chooses, it is not because they have done good things that he has chosen them. If God chose people because they did good deeds, then it would not be because he was acting toward them in a way that they did not deserve.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Scriptures confirm that the people of Israel as a whole did not find the way of being declared righteous, although those whom God had chosen did find it.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 9:18; ROMANS 11:5; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:4"> Since God chose only some people of Israel, this is what I conclude: &#91;RHQ&#93; Most of the people of Israel did not find the way for him to erase the record of their sins, even though some of them were earnestly looking for it. The people of Israel whom God had chosen found it, but the rest of them were &#123;God&#125; made spiritually insensitive/unresponsive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:4; Isaiah 29:10; EZEKIEL 12:2"> Our fellow Jews are just like the ones about whom Isaiah wrote,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">God ◄caused their senses to be dull/made them unable to think clearly►. Up to this very day, they have eyes but they cannot see spiritually &#91;MET&#93;. They have ears, but they cannot understand spiritually what they hear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 69:22–23; LUKE 12:20; PSALMS 28:4"> And they remind me of what King David said, when he asked God to cause his enemies’ senses to be dull &#91;MET&#93;,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Make them stupid &#91;MTY&#93;, like animals that are caught in snares!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May they feel secure because of the things that they enjoy but which will catch them like a trap &#91;DOU, MET&#93;, with the result that you will destroy them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 69:23; DEUTERONOMY 28:64–68; 2 PETER 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">May their ability to perceive danger be dulled &#123;Dull their ability to perceive spiritually&#125; &#91;MET&#93;, with the result that they will not become alarmed &#91;MET&#93; when there is danger.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May you cause them to carry heavy loads on their backs continually as slaves do &#91;MTY&#93;.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## My reply to a question as to whether the result of the Jews’ unbelief is a permanent falling away from God is, “No! God is saving many non-Jews to make many Jews envious and seek to be saved.”

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ACTS 13:46–48; ROMANS 11:31; ROMANS 10:19"> Perhaps someone will ask, “When the Jews sinned by not believing in Christ, did it result in their separating themselves from God permanently?” Then I would reply, No, they have certainly not separated themselves from God permanently! What is happening is that because they sinned, God is saving non-Jews in order to cause the Jews to envy the way he blesses non-Jews and so ask Christ to save them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 11:25; COLOSSIANS 1:27; ROMANS 11:15"> When the Jews sinned (OR, rejected Christ), the result was that God abundantly blessed other people in &#91;MTY&#93; the world by offering them the opportunity to believe. And when the Jews failed spiritually, the result was that God abundantly blessed the non-Jews. Since that is true, think how wonderful it will be when the complete number of the Jews whom God has chosen will believe in Christ &#91;RHQ&#93;!</span>
</div>
</div>

## I highly esteem the work that God has called me to do as an apostle among you non-Jews. I hope that I will make my fellow Jews jealous, and as a result, some of them will be saved.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ACTS 9:15; 2 TIMOTHY 1:11–12; EPHESIANS 3:8"> Now it is to you non-Jews that I am saying what follows. I am the one who is the apostle to non-Jews, and I highly esteem this work that God appointed me to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 7:16; 1 TIMOTHY 4:16; ROMANS 9:3"> But I also hope that by my labors I will make my fellow Jews ◄jealous/want what you non-Jews have►, with the result that some of them will believe and be saved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:10; ROMANS 5:10–11; COLOSSIANS 1:20–21"> God has rejected most of my fellow Jews because they refused to believe, with the result that he reconciled many other people in the world &#91;MTY&#93; to himself. But he will accept my fellow Jews again when they trust in Christ! And ◄this is what the result will be./do you know what the result will be?► &#91;RHQ&#93; It will be as though God is making them alive again after they have been dead!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:30; JAMES 1:18; PROVERBS 3:9"> Just like the whole lump of dough will belong to God if people offer to God &#91;MET&#93; the bread baked from the first part of it &#91;MET&#93;, so the Jews will belong to God because their ancestors belonged to God. And just like the branches of a tree will be good if the roots are good &#91;MET&#93;, so the descendants of our great Jewish ancestors who belonged to God will also some day belong to God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## You non-Jews who trust in Jesus must not despise the Jews whom God has rejected. You must not become proud, but instead beware. God will not spare you if you fall away from him, and he will certainly act kindly toward the Jews if they trust in Christ.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:11–13; EPHESIANS 3:6; MATTHEW 21:43"> God has rejected many of the Jews &#91;MET&#93;, like branches of a tree are broken off &#123;like people break off dead branches of a tree&#125;. And each of you non-Jews whom God has accepted is like &#91;MET&#93; a branch of a wild/uncultivated olive tree that was &#123;that someone&#125; grafted among the branches (OR, onto one of the branches) that were left on a cultivated olive tree. God has caused you to benefit from how he blessed our(exc) first Jewish ancestors, as branches benefit from the nutritious sap from the roots of a cultivated olive tree &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:12; ROMANS 11:20; EPHESIANS 2:19–20"> However, you non-Jews must not despise the Jews whom God rejected &#91;MET&#93;, who are like the branches that were broken off from the tree If any of you wants to boast about having received blessings from God, remember this: Branches do not nourish the roots. Instead the roots nourish the branches &#91;MET&#93;. Similarly, you are blessed by God because of what you have received from the Jews! It is not what the Jews have received from you that blesses them &#91;MET&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ROMANS 11:17; ROMANS 11:11–12; ROMANS 11:23–24"> If one of you then says, “God rejected the Jews &#91;MET&#93; like branches &#91;MET&#93; that are broken &#123;that people broke&#125; off a tree and rejected, in order that he might accept us non-Jews, as branches &#91;MET&#93; of a tree are grafted in &#123;as people graft in branches of a tree&#125;,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 12:16; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:24; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:12"> I would reply that this is true. However, it is because the Jews did not believe in Christ that they were rejected &#91;MET&#93; &#123;that God rejected &#91;MET&#93; them&#125;. As for you, it is only because you believe in Christ that God has accepted you! So do not any of you become proud, but instead beware of what could happen to you</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 PETER 2:4–9; JEREMIAH 25:29; JEREMIAH 49:12"> Since God did not spare the unbelieving Jews &#91;MET&#93; who were like a tree’s natural branches, he will not spare any of you if you do not keep trusting in him!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:14; HEBREWS 3:6; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:2"> Note then, that God acts kindly, but he also acts severely. He has acted severely toward the Jews who have stopped trusting in him. On the other hand, he acts kindly toward each of you non-Jews only if you continue to appreciate his acting kindly toward you. However, you will be rejected &#123;he will reject you&#125; if you do not keep trusting in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 3:16; ZECHARIAH 12:10; MATTHEW 23:39"> And if the Jews believe in Christ &#91;LIT&#93;, God also will reunite them to himself as branches are grafted &#123;as people graft branches&#125; into a tree again, because God is able to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ROMANS 11:17–18; ROMANS 11:30"> Each of you non-Jews who were previously separated from God has benefited from the ways in which God blessed the Jews &#91;MET&#93;. That is like taking branches that have been cut &#123;that someone has cut&#125; from a wild olive tree that just grew without being planted &#123;someone planting it&#125; and, contrary to what people usually do, grafting them into a cultivated olive tree. So God will much more readily receive back the Jews because they belonged to him before &#91;MET&#93;! That will be like the original branches that had been cut off being grafted &#123;like grafting the original branches that someone cut off,&#125; back into the olive tree to which they originally belonged!</span>
</div>
</div>

## I want you to know that all the people of Israel will some day be saved, as the Scriptures say will happen. God still loves them because of their ancestors. It is his purpose to act mercifully towards them as well as toward all non-Jews.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 21:24; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:14–16; ROMANS 16:25"> My non-Jewish fellow believers, I certainly want you to understand &#91;LIT&#93; this truth that God has now revealed about my fellow Jews who are refusing to believe. You should not proudly think that God now favors you more than the Jews (OR, that you understand God’s further plans for the Jews). Many people of Israel will continue to be stubborn until all the non-Jews whom God has chosen have believed in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 59:20; JEREMIAH 31:31–37; PSALMS 14:7"> And then all &#91;HYP&#93; the people of Israel will be saved &#123;God will save all &#91;HYP&#93; the people of Israel&#125;. Then these words that are written &#123;that a prophet wrote&#125; in the Scriptures will ◄become true/be fulfilled►:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">The one who sets his people free will originate from the place where God dwells (OR, from the Jews) &#91;MTY&#93;. Then he will remove the guilt of the Israelite people (OR, descendants of Jacob) &#91;MTY&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:31–34; Isaiah 59:21; EZEKIEL 36:25–29"> And as God says, The contract that I will make with them is that I will forgive their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:15; DEUTERONOMY 7:7–8; MATTHEW 21:43"> With regard to the Jews rejecting the good message about Christ, God treats them as enemies, which has benefited you non-Jews. But in regard to their being the people whom God chose, God still loves them because of what he promised their ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:21; NUMBERS 23:19; MALACHI 3:6"> He still loves them, because he never changed his mind about the privileges/blessings he gave to them and about his choosing them to be his people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:7; EPHESIANS 2:1–2; 1 CORINTHIANS 7:25"> You non-Jews once disobeyed God, but now he has acted mercifully towards you because the Jews disobeyed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ROMANS 11:15; ROMANS 10:16; ROMANS 11:25"> Now the Jews have disobeyed God. The result is that by the very same way in which he acted mercifully towards you, he will act mercifully towards them again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:22; ROMANS 3:9; 1 TIMOTHY 2:4–6"> God has declared and proved that all people, both Jews and non-Jews &#91;MET&#93;, disobey ◄him/his laws►. He has declared that because he wants to act mercifully towards us all.</span>
</div>
</div>

## I marvel at how great God’s wisdom and knowledge are, and his decisions and actions toward us!

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOB 5:9; JOB 11:7–9; PSALMS 92:5"> I marvel how vast and great are the wise things that God has done and what he has always known &#91;DOU&#93;! We are completely unable to understand the things that he has decided and the ways in which he acts toward us &#91;DOU&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 40:13; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:16; JOB 15:8"> I remember the Scriptures that say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">◄No one has known what the Lord thinks./Who has known what the Lord thinks?► &#91;RHQ&#93; ◄Absolutely no one has given him advice about what he should do!/Who has advised him about what he should do?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOB 41:11; JOB 35:7; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:7"> And, ◄No one has given anything to God that he did not previously receive from God!/Has anyone given anything to God that he did not previously receive from God?► &#91;RHQ&#93; So God ◄is not obligated to/does not have to► pay back anything to anyone!</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:15–17; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:6; HEBREWS 2:10"> God is the one who created all things. He is also the one who sustains all things.
