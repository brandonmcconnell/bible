## We can draw conclusions from Abraham’s life about how God erases the record of our sins. Abraham could not boast about his accomplishing that because the Scriptures record that it was because he believed what God promised that God erased the record of his sins. God’s doing that was a gift, not a reward.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 51:2; ROMANS 4:16; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:22"> Abraham is the revered ancestor of us Jews. So think about what we can conclude from what happened to Abraham about how God can erase the record of our sins. &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:31; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:30; PHILIPPIANS 3:9"> If it was because of Abraham’s doing good things that the record of his sins was erased &#123;that God erased the record of his sins&#125;, Abraham could then have been able to boast about that to people, but he would not have had any basis to boast to God about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 15:6; JAMES 2:23; GALATIANS 3:6–8"> Remember that in the Scriptures it is written &#123;someone wrote&#125; &#91;PRS&#93; that Abraham believed what God promised &#91;RHQ&#93;, and as a result the record of his sins was erased &#123;God erased the record of his sins&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 11:6; ROMANS 9:32; ROMANS 11:35"> If we receive wages for work that we do, those wages are not considered to be a gift. Instead, they are considered to be what we have earned. Similarly, if God erases the record of our sins because we did things to earn God’s favor, we would not consider that God’s erasing the record of our sin was a gift. Instead, we would consider it as what we had earned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:9; ROMANS 3:22; TITUS 3:3–7"> But suppose that we do not do things to gain God’s acceptance. Suppose that we instead trust in God, who erases the record of sins of wicked people. Then the erasing of the record of our sins because of our trusting in Christ is considered to be a gift to us, not something that we earned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 1:9; PHILIPPIANS 3:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:30"> Similarly, it is as David wrote in the Psalms about people being happy whose record of sins God has erased even though they have not done things to earn it. David wrote:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 32:1–2; PSALMS 130:3–4; LUKE 7:47–50"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is pleased with people whose sins have been &#123;whose sins he has&#125; forgiven, and whose sins he has decided to forget &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 32:2; 1 PETER 2:24; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">God causes to be happy the people whose sins he no longer keeps a record of.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## This happiness of knowing that God has erased the record of our sins is also for the non-Jews. Remember that it was before Abraham was circumcised, when he was still in effect a non-Jew, that God did that. He later received circumcision simply as a sign of God having erased the record of his sins because of his faith. The result was that he became a spiritual father of all who believe in God as he did, whether they are circumcised or not.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:14; COLOSSIANS 3:11; GALATIANS 3:26–28"> As for our being happy because God has erased the record of our sins, ◄it is not something that only we Jews can experience./is it &#91;MTY&#93; something that only we Jews can experience?► &#91;RHQ&#93; No, it is also something that non-Jews can experience &#91;MTY&#93;. What is written in the Scriptures, that it was because Abraham trusted in God that the record of his sins was erased &#123;God erased the record of his sins&#125;, also shows that this is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:15; GALATIANS 5:6; GENESIS 17:10"> ◄Think about when God erased the record of Abraham’s sins./When did God erase the record of Abraham’s sins?► &#91;RHQ&#93; ◄Consider whether it happened after someone circumcised Abraham to mark him as one who belonged to God, or before someone circumcised him./Did it happen after Abraham was circumcised to be marked as one who belonged to God, or before he was circumcised?► &#91;RHQ&#93; It happened before he was circumcised, not after he was circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 3:22; LUKE 19:9; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:22"> Many years later, God commanded that Abraham be circumcised. Abraham’s accepting that ritual simply showed that he knew that God had accepted him. He knew that God had erased the record of his sins because he trusted in God while he was still, in effect, a non-Jew because he had not been circumcised. So we can understand that Abraham became ◄a spiritual ancestor/like an ancestor► to all of us whose record of sins has been erased &#123;whose sins God has erased the record of&#125; because we believe in God’s promise, even though some of us are not circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 1:8; JOHN 8:39–40; PROVERBS 2:20"> Likewise, Abraham is the spiritual ancestor of all us Jews who are not merely circumcised but who, more importantly, believe in God’s promise as our ancestor Abraham did, even before he was circumcised.</span>
</div>
</div>

## It was because Abraham trusted in God that God erased the record of his sins and promised him many blessings. So what God promised is guaranteed to all, both Jews and non-Jews, who trust in God as Abraham did.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:29; GENESIS 22:17–18; GENESIS 28:14"> God promised Abraham and his descendants that they would receive the blessings that he promised to give to the people in the world. But when he promised that, it was not because Abraham obeyed the laws that God later gave to Moses. Instead, it was because Abraham believed that God would do what he promised that he would do. As a result, God erased the record of his sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:18–24; PHILIPPIANS 3:9; ROMANS 4:16"> If we think that it is those who obey God’s laws who will receive what he has promised, it is useless for us to trust in him. And what he promised is worthless. Remember that it is stated in God’s law &#91;PRS&#93; that he will punish people who do not perfectly obey them, and remember that wherever &#91;MTY&#93; laws exist, people disobey them &#91;LIT&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 3:24–26; EPHESIANS 2:8; GALATIANS 3:22"> So it is because we trust in God that we will receive the things that he has promised. It is not because we perfectly obey God’s laws. He wants ◄to erase the record of our sins/to declare us no longer guilty► without our earning it. As a result, what God now promises, he guarantees to give to all people who are Abraham’s spiritual descendants. He promises to do that not only for us Jewish believers, who have God’s laws and trust in him as Abraham did, but also for those non-Jews who do not have God’s laws but who trust in him as Abraham did. Abraham is the spiritual ancestor of all of us believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:28; GENESIS 17:4–5; JOHN 5:21"> What is written &#123;What Moses wrote&#125; in the Scriptures about what God promised Abraham shows that this is true. God said to him, “It is in order that you (sg) may be the ancestor of many ethnic groups that I have chosen you.” It was because Abraham confidently believed God’s promise to give him many descendants, when there was no physical basis for his hoping that this would happen, that God erased the record of his sins. Romans 4:17b-22<br />God guaranteed that he would give Abraham many descendants. Abraham confidently believed that God would do that,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 8:24; GENESIS 15:5–6; ACTS 27:25"> even though there was no physical reason for him to hope that he would have descendants, because he and his wife were too old to bear children. But God is the one who causes dead people to live again, and who talks about things that do not yet exist as already existing. God said to Abraham, “You will have so many descendants that they will be as impossible to count as the stars.” And Abraham believed that, and he believed that he would become the ancestor of many ethnic groups.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:11–19; ROMANS 4:20–21; GENESIS 17:17"> He did not doubt that God would do what he promised, even though he knew that his body was already as incapable of begetting children as if he were dead &#91;MET&#93; because he was about 100 years old. And even though he realized that Sarah had never been able to become pregnant &#91;IDM&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 1:45; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:13; DANIEL 10:19"> he did not doubt at all that God would do what he had promised. Instead, he trusted in God more/very strongly, and he thanked God for what God was going to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 1:37; JEREMIAH 32:27; GENESIS 18:14"> He was also convinced &#123;very sure&#125; that the thing that God promised, God was able to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 4:6; ROMANS 4:3"> And that is the reason that the record of his sins was erased &#123;God erased the record of his sins&#125;.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The words about God erasing the record of Abraham’s sins were written also to assure us who believe in God.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:11; ROMANS 15:4; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:6"> The words in the Scriptures, “The record of his sins was erased &#123;God erased the record of his sins&#125;,” are not only about Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ACTS 2:24; 1 PETER 1:21; ACTS 13:30"> They were also written &#123;They also wrote that&#125; for us whose record of sins would be erased &#123;God would erase&#125;. They were written &#123;They wrote it&#125; for us who believe God, the one who caused our Lord Jesus to become alive again after he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 JOHN 4:9–10; Isaiah 53:5–6; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:21"> Jesus allowed men to execute &#91;MTY&#93; him so that God could forgive our evil deeds. And God caused Jesus to live again because God wanted to show that because of the death of Jesus he was able to erase the record of our sins.</span>
