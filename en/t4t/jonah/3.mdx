## The people of Nineveh repented after Jonah warned them

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JONAH 1:1; JOHN 21:15–17"> Then Yahweh said to Jonah again,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 2:7; JEREMIAH 1:17; JONAH 3:3"> “Go to that great city of Nineveh, and tell them the message that I gave you previously.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:28–29; PSALMS 80:10; GENESIS 22:3"> So this time Jonah obeyed Yahweh, and he went to Nineveh. That city was so big that a person had to walk for three days to completely go through it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:22; JEREMIAH 18:7–10; 2 KINGS 20:1"> On the first day after Jonah arrived, he began walking through the city. He was proclaiming (OR, At the end of that day he started proclaiming) to the people, “Forty days from now, Nineveh will be destroyed &#123;God will destroy Nineveh&#125;!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 11:32; MATTHEW 12:41; JOEL 2:12–17"> The people of Nineveh believed God’s message. They all decided that everyone should begin ◄fasting/abstaining from food►. So everyone, including important people and unimportant people, did that. They also put on coarse cloth, to show that they were sorry for having sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JAMES 4:6–10; Esther 4:1–4; DANIEL 9:3"> The King of Nineveh heard what the people were doing. So he took off his royal robes, and he also put on coarse cloth. He left his palace, and sat down where there were cold ashes, to show that he also was sorry for having sinned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 20:3; JOEL 1:18; JOEL 2:15–16"> Then he sent messengers to proclaim to the people in Nineveh: “My advisors and I have decreed that no one may eat or drink anything. Do not even allow your animals to eat or drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JONAH 1:6; Isaiah 55:6–7; EZEKIEL 18:21–24"> Instead, every person must put on coarse cloth. Put coarse cloth on your animals, too. Then everyone must pray fervently to God. And everyone must stop doing evil actions/things, and stop acting violently toward others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:22; JOEL 2:13–14; PSALMS 106:45"> Perhaps, if everyone does that, God may change his mind and be merciful to us, and stop being very angry with us, with the result that we will not die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:8; 1 KINGS 21:27–29; LUKE 15:20"> When they all did that, God saw what they were doing, and he saw that they had stopped doing evil things. So he pitied them, and he did not get rid of them as he had threatened to do.</span>
</div>
