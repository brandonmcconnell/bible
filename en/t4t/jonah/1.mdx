## Jonah refused to warn the people of Nineveh as Yahweh told him to do

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 11:32; LUKE 11:29–30; 2 KINGS 14:25"> One day Yahweh said to the prophet Jonah, the son of Amittai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 18:20–21; GENESIS 10:11; Isaiah 58:1"> “I have seen how wicked the people of &#91;MTY&#93; the great city of Nineveh are. Therefore go there and warn the people that I am planning to destroy their city because of their sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 139:7–12; GENESIS 4:16; JOSHUA 19:46"> But instead of going to Nineveh, Jonah went in the direction opposite to where Yahweh told him to go. He went down to Joppa city. There he bought a ticket to travel on a ship that was going to Tarshish city, in order to avoid doing what Yahweh told him to do. He got on the ship, and then went down to a lower deck, lay down, and went to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 107:23–31; MATTHEW 8:24–27; EXODUS 10:19"> Then Yahweh caused a very strong wind to blow, and there was such a violent storm that the sailors thought the ship would break apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:28; ACTS 27:18–19; 1 KINGS 18:26"> The sailors were very frightened. Because of that, they each started to pray to their own gods to save them. Then they threw the cargo into the sea to make the ship lighter in order that it would not sink easily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JONAH 3:9; PSALMS 107:28–29; EPHESIANS 5:14"> Then the captain went down to where Jonah was sleeping soundly. He awoke him and said to him, “◄How can you sleep during a storm like this?/You should not be sleeping during a storm like this► &#91;RHQ&#93;! Get up and pray to your god! Perhaps he will pity us and save us, in order that we will not drown!” But Jonah refused to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:33; JOSHUA 7:13–18; 1 SAMUEL 10:20–21"> Then the sailors said to each other, “We need to ◄cast lots/shake from a container small objects that we have all marked►, to determine who has caused all this trouble!” So they did that, and the object with Jonah’s mark fell out of the container.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 47:3; 1 SAMUEL 30:13; JOSHUA 7:19"> So various ones of them asked him, “Are you the one who has caused us all this trouble?” “What work do you do?” “Where are you coming from?” “What country and what people-group do you belong to?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 146:5–6; PSALMS 95:5–6; Nehemiah 9:6"> Jonah replied, “I am a Hebrew. I worship Yahweh God, who lives in heaven. He is the one who made the sea and the land. I am trying to escape from doing what Yahweh told me to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JONAH 1:3; JOB 27:22; JOSHUA 7:25"> After the sailors heard that, they were terrified. So they asked him, “Do you realize what trouble you have caused?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 21:1–6; MICAH 6:6–7; 1 SAMUEL 6:2–3"> The storm kept getting worse and the waves kept getting bigger. So one of the sailors asked Jonah, “What should we do in order to make the sea become calm?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 24:17; 1 CHRONICLES 21:17; JOSHUA 7:12"> He replied, “Pick me up and throw me into the sea. If you do that, it will become calm. I know that this terrible storm is the result of my not doing what Yahweh told me to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:30; JOB 34:29"> But the sailors did not want to do that. Instead, they tried hard to row the ship back to the land. But they could not do that, because the storm continued to get worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 115:3; PSALMS 135:6; EPHESIANS 1:11"> Therefore they prayed to Yahweh, and one of them prayed, “O Yahweh, please do not let us drown because of our causing this man to die. O Yahweh, you have done what you wanted to do. We do not know if this man has sinned. If he has not sinned, do not consider that we are guilty of sinning when we cause him to die!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 8:24; PSALMS 93:3–4; PSALMS 107:29"> Then they picked Jonah up and threw him into the sea. Then the sea became calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 50:14; GENESIS 8:20; DANIEL 6:26"> When that happened, the sailors became greatly awed at Yahweh’s power. So they offered a sacrifice to Yahweh, and they strongly promised him that they would do things that would please him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:40; LUKE 11:30; MATTHEW 16:4"> While they were doing that, Yahweh sent a huge fish that swallowed Jonah. Then Jonah was inside the fish for three days and three nights.</span>
