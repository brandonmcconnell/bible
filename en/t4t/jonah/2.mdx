## Jonah prayed again after God said that he would save Jonah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:53–56; PSALMS 130:1–2; PSALMS 91:15"> While he was inside the huge fish, Jonah prayed to Yahweh God, whom he worshiped. After Yahweh told him that he would answer his prayer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 18:4–6; PSALMS 120:1; PSALMS 34:6"> Jonah said,<br />“Yahweh, when I was greatly distressed here, I prayed to you, and you heard what I prayed. When I was about to descend way down into the place where dead people go, you heard me when I called out for you to help/save me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 42:7; LAMENTATIONS 3:54; PSALMS 69:1–2"> You threw me down into the deep water, into the bottom &#91;DOU&#93; of the sea. The currents of the sea swirled around me, and the huge waves crashed above me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 31:22; PSALMS 5:7; 2 CHRONICLES 6:38"> Then I thought, ‘You have banished me, and I will never be able to enter your presence again. I will never see your holy temple in Jerusalem again!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 69:1–2; LAMENTATIONS 3:54; PSALMS 40:2"> The water surrounded me, and threatened to drown me. Seaweed was wrapped around my head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 30:3; PSALMS 16:10; Isaiah 38:17"> I sank down to where the mountains start rising &#91;MET&#93; from the bottom of the sea. I thought that forever it would be as though my body would be in a prison &#91;MTY&#93; inside the earth below me.<br />But you, Yahweh God, whom I worship, rescued me from going down to the place of the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 18:6; 2 CHRONICLES 30:27; PSALMS 11:4"> When I was almost dead &#91;EUP&#93;, Yahweh, I ◄thought about/prayed to► you. You heard my prayer, up there where you are, in your holy temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 31:6; 2 KINGS 17:15; JEREMIAH 2:13"> All those who worship worthless idols are rejecting you, the one who could act kindly toward them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 3:8; PSALMS 50:14; ECCLESIASTES 5:4–5"> But I will sing to thank you, and I will offer a sacrifice to you. I will surely do what I have solemnly promised to do. Yahweh, you are the one who is able to save us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:26–27; PSALMS 33:9; GENESIS 1:3"> Then Yahweh commanded the huge fish to vomit out Jonah, and the fish did that, and Jonah was able to get to the land.</span>
</div>
</div>
