## Not many of you should become teachers, because God will judge teachers with greater severity than he will judge others. You should all strive to speak what is right, because what you say has a powerful effect on others, and it is often destructive and hypocritical.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 12:47–48; 1 TIMOTHY 1:7; 1 PETER 5:3"> My fellow believers, not many of you should desire to become teachers of God’s truth, because you know that God will judge us (exc), who teach others, more severely than he will judge other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JAMES 1:26; PROVERBS 13:3; 1 PETER 3:10"> It is true that we all in many ways do things that are wrong &#91;MET&#93;. But those who always &#91;LIT&#93; control what they say will be all that God intends/wants them to be. They will be able to control all their actions &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 32:9; PSALMS 39:1; JAMES 1:26"> To illustrate, if we put a small metal bit into the mouth of a horse in order to cause the horse to obey us, we can cause the horse to turn and go where we want it to &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 107:25–27; MATTHEW 8:24; ACTS 27:14–38"> Think also about ships. Although a ship may be very large and although it can be moved by strong winds &#123;strong winds can move it&#125;, by turning a very small ◄rudder/steering paddle► people can direct the ship wherever they want it to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:21; JEREMIAH 9:3–8; PROVERBS 12:18"> Similarly, although our tongues are very small, if we do not control them, we can harm many people by what we proudly say &#91;MTY&#93;. Think also about how just a small flame of fire can cause a large forest (OR, a large area of brushwood) to burn.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 120:2–3; PROVERBS 16:27; MARK 7:15"> Just like a fire damages a forest &#91;MET&#93;, when we say things that are evil, we harm many people. What we say &#91;MTY&#93; reveals that we are very evil. What we say contaminates/defiles everything that we think and do &#91;PRS, MET&#93;. Just like a flame of fire easily causes the whole surrounding area &#91;MET&#93; to burn, what we say &#91;MTY&#93; can cause others to want to do evil. It is the devil himself &#91;MTY&#93; who causes us to say evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 5:4"> Indeed, although people are able to tame/control all kinds of wild animals, birds, reptiles and creatures that live in the water, and people have actually tamed/controlled them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 3:13; PSALMS 140:3; PSALMS 55:21"> no person ◄on his own/by himself► is able to control what he says &#91;MTY&#93;. And when people say evil things, it shows that they are unstable/uncontrolled and wicked &#91;MTY&#93;. As the poison of a snake kills people &#91;MTY&#93;, we harm others &#91;MET&#93; by what we say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 10:7; GENESIS 1:26–27; PSALMS 62:4"> We use our tongue to praise God, who is our Lord and Father, but we also use our same tongue to ask God to say evil things to people. That is very wrong! God made people like himself, so we should speak as respectfully to others as we speak to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 12:14; 1 PETER 3:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:3"> We praise God, but with our same mouth we also ask for evil things to happen to others. My fellow believers, this should not be!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Surely bitter water and good water do not come out of the same spring! &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 6:43–44; MATTHEW 12:33; MATTHEW 7:16–20"> My fellow believers, a fig tree cannot &#91;RHQ&#93; produce olives. Nor can &#91;RHQ&#93; a grapevine produce figs. Neither can a salty spring produce good water. Similarly, we should say only good things, and we should not say evil things &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
</div>
</div>

## If you are jealous of others and self-seeking, do not boast that you are wise. Instead, demonstrate that you are truly wise by doing good things and by acting peaceably and compassionately toward others.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:23; JAMES 3:17; MATTHEW 5:5"> If any of you thinks &#91;RHQ&#93; that you are wise and know a lot, you should always act in a good way to show people that your good actions are the result of your being truly wise. Being wise helps us to act gently toward others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JAMES 3:16; 1 CORINTHIANS 13:4; PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> But if you are very &#91;MET&#93; jealous/unhappy when you see that someone else is succeeding, and always want to have what you want, regardless of what others want, you should not say that you are wise, for by boasting like that, you are saying that ◄God’s message/what God says about you► is not true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JAMES 3:17; JAMES 1:17; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:19"> Those who have such attitudes are not wise &#91;MTY&#93; in the way that God wants them to be. Instead, they are only thinking and acting like ◄ungodly people/people who do not please God►. They think and act according to their own evil desires. They do what the demons want them to do (OR, they think and act as demons do).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JAMES 3:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:33"> Keep in mind that people who have such attitudes ◄are unruly/do not submit to authority► and do all kinds of evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:22–23; PROVERBS 2:6; 1 CORINTHIANS 13:4–7"> But when people are wise &#91;PRS&#93; in the way that God &#91;MTY&#93; wants them to be, they are pure in every way, which God considers to be very important. They also act peaceably towards others, they ◄are considerate of/think about the rights and feelings of► others, they are willing to yield to the wishes of others, they act compassionately toward others, and they do all kinds of good things for others &#91;MET&#93;. How they treat others does not depend on ◄others’ status/whether others are important or not►, and they are sincere in all they do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:9; HOSEA 10:12; PHILIPPIANS 1:11"> Those who act peaceably toward others cause others to also act peaceably, with the result that they all live together acting toward each other in a righteous way &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
</div>
