## The people must act justly and mercifully, not only habitually abstain from eating food

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:1; Ezra 6:14–15; ZECHARIAH 1:1"> When Darius had been the emperor for almost four years, on December 7, Yahweh gave me another message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:21; JEREMIAH 26:19; 1 SAMUEL 13:12"> The people of Bethel city sent two men, Sharezer and Regem-Melech, along with some other men, to the temple of Yahweh, the Commander of the armies of angels, to request that Yahweh bless them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:19; JAMES 4:8–10; MALACHI 2:7"> They also asked the priests at Yahweh’s temple and the prophets this question: “For many years, during the fifth month and during the seventh month of each year, we have mourned and ◄fasted/abstained from eating food►. Should we continue to do that?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16"> Then the Commander of the armies of angels gave me a message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:12; Isaiah 58:4–6; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:31"> He said, “Tell &#91;RHQ&#93; the priests and all the other people of Judah that during the past 70 years, when they mourned and fasted during the fifth and seventh months of each year, it was not really &#91;RHQ&#93; me, Yahweh, whom they were honoring.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 11:20–21; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:26–29; HOSEA 8:13"> And when they ate and drank, it was really &#91;RHQ&#93; to benefit themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–20; JEREMIAH 17:26; JEREMIAH 7:5"> That is certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; what I continually told the former prophets to proclaim to the people, when Jerusalem and the nearby towns were prosperous and filled with people, and people also lived in the desert area to the south and in the foothills to the west.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Yahweh gave another message to me, saying</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MICAH 6:8; PSALMS 82:2–4; PROVERBS 21:3"> “Tell the people that this is what the Commander of the armies of angels says: ‘Do what is just/right, and act kindly and mercifully toward each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:28; Isaiah 1:23; JAMES 5:4"> Do not ◄oppress/treat cruelly► widows or orphans or foreigners or poor people. Do not even think about doing evil to anyone else.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:29; JEREMIAH 8:5; HOSEA 4:16"> But the people refused to pay attention to what Yahweh said. They turned their backs to him, and put their hands over their ears in order to not hear what he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:19; Nehemiah 9:29–30; 2 CHRONICLES 36:16"> They were very stubborn &#91;IDM&#93;, and they would not listen to the laws that God gave to Moses or the messages that the Commander of the armies of angels told his Spirit to give to the prophets who were now dead. So the Commander of the armies of angels was very angry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:24–28; JEREMIAH 14:12; Isaiah 1:15"> The Commander of the armies of angels says, “When I called/spoke to the people, they would not listen. So when they called/prayed to me, I did not listen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:33; DEUTERONOMY 28:64; JEREMIAH 23:19"> And I caused them to be scattered among many nations, where they were strangers. It was as though &#91;MET&#93; a whirlwind picked them up and carried them away from their country. The country/land that they were forced to leave was ruined, with the result that no one could live there or even travel through it. It was previously a delightful land, but they caused it to become ◄desolate/like a desert►.”</span>
</div>
</div>
