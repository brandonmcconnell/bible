## Yahweh summons his people to return to him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:4; ZECHARIAH 7:1; ZECHARIAH 1:7"> I am Zechariah, a prophet, the son of Berekiah and grandson of Iddo. When Darius had been the emperor of Persia for almost two years, during November of that year, Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:6; LAMENTATIONS 5:7; EZEKIEL 22:31"> “I was very angry with your ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MALACHI 3:7; JEREMIAH 3:22; JEREMIAH 4:1"> So tell this to the people: I, the Commander of the armies of angels, say, ‘Return to me, and if you do that, I will help you again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 78:8; PSALMS 106:6–7; JEREMIAH 35:15"> Do not be like your ancestors. Other prophets, who have now died, continually proclaimed to your ancestors that they should stop doing the evil things that they were continually doing. But they refused to pay attention to what I said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:5; JOB 14:10–12; ECCLESIASTES 12:7"> Your ancestors have died and are now in their graves &#91;RHQ&#93;. And the prophets did not &#91;RHQ&#93; live forever, either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:17; DEUTERONOMY 28:15; EZEKIEL 12:25–28"> But I punished &#91;IDM&#93; the people for disobeying the commands and the decrees which I commanded my servants the prophets to tell to the people. Then they ◄repented/turned away from their evil behavior► and said, ‘The Commander of the armies of angels has done to us what we deserved for our evil behavior, just like he said that he would do.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The vision of the horses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:1"> Three months later, on February 15, Yahweh gave another message to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="REVELATION 6:4; Isaiah 55:13; ZECHARIAH 13:7"> During the night I had a vision. In the vision I saw an angel who was on a red horse. He was in a narrow valley among some myrtle trees. Behind him were angels on red, brown, and white horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 4:4–5; ZECHARIAH 5:5; ZECHARIAH 2:3"> I asked the angel who had been talking to me, “Sir, who are those angels on the horses?” He replied, “I will show you who they are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:11; ZECHARIAH 6:5–8; HEBREWS 1:14"> Then the angel who had been under the myrtle trees explained. He said, “They are the angels whom Yahweh sent to ◄patrol/see what is happening in► the entire world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:10; ZECHARIAH 1:15; PSALMS 103:20–21"> Then those angels reported to the angel who was there among the myrtle trees, “We have traveled throughout the world, and we have found out that the army of the emperor of Persia has conquered nations throughout the world, and that those nations are now ◄helpless/unable to resist his army►.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 7:5; JEREMIAH 29:10; PSALMS 102:13"> Then the angel asked, “Commander of the armies of angels, how long will you continue to not be merciful to Jerusalem and the other towns in Judah? You have been angry with them for 70 years!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:10; Isaiah 40:1–2; JEREMIAH 30:10–22"> So Yahweh spoke kindly to the angel who had talked to me, saying things that comforted/encouraged him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOEL 2:18; Isaiah 40:1; ZECHARIAH 1:17"> Then the angel who had been talking with me said to me, “Proclaim this to the people of Jerusalem: The Commander of the armies of angels says that he is very concerned about the people who live on Zion Hill and in the other parts of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:12–17; JEREMIAH 51:24; Isaiah 54:8"> But he is very angry with the nations that are proud and feel safe. He was only a little bit angry with the people of Judah, but he is very angry with the surrounding nations because they caused the people of Judah to experience complete disaster.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:3; Ezra 6:14–15; ZECHARIAH 2:10–11"> Therefore, this is what he says: ‘I will again be merciful to the people of Jerusalem, and they will rebuild my temple. Men will measure all the land in the city before they start rebuilding the houses.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; ZECHARIAH 2:12; ZEPHANIAH 3:15–17"> The Commander of the armies of angels also said this: ‘Tell the people that the people in my towns in Judah will soon be very prosperous again, and I will encourage the people of Jerusalem, and their city will again be my chosen city.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The vision of the four horns

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 5:13; 2 KINGS 24:1–20; ZECHARIAH 5:9"> Then I looked up, and I saw in front of me four animal horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:21; ZECHARIAH 8:14; ZECHARIAH 2:2"> I asked the angel who had been speaking to me, “What are those horns?” He replied, “Those horns represent the armies that forced the people of Jerusalem and other places in Judah and Israel to go to other countries.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:3–5; ZECHARIAH 12:2–6; ZECHARIAH 9:12–16"> Then Yahweh showed me four ◄craftsmen/men who make things from metal►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 75:10; ZECHARIAH 1:19; PSALMS 75:4–5"> I asked, “What are those men coming to do?” He replied, “The armies represented by those horns caused the people of Judah to be scattered, with the result that they suffered greatly. But the craftsmen have come to cause those who attacked &#91;IDM&#93; Judah to be terrified and crushed.”</span>
</div>
</div>
