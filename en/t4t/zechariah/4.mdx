## The vision of the lampstand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:26; DANIEL 8:18; ZECHARIAH 1:9"> Then the angel who had been talking with me returned, and he called to me as though I had been asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="REVELATION 4:5; REVELATION 1:12; MATTHEW 5:14–16"> He asked me, “What do you see?”<br />I replied, “I see a lampstand made completely of gold. There is a small bowl for olive oil at the top, and there are seven small lamps around the bowl, and a place for a wick on each lamp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="REVELATION 11:4; ZECHARIAH 4:14; ROMANS 11:17"> Furthermore, I see two olive trees, one at the right side of the lampstand and one at the left side.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 6:4; ZECHARIAH 1:9; ZECHARIAH 1:19"> I asked the angel who was talking with me, “Sir, what are these?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 4:13; ZECHARIAH 1:9; DANIEL 2:30"> He replied, “Surely you know &#91;RHQ&#93; what they are?”<br />I replied, “No, I do not know.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 32:7–8; 2 CORINTHIANS 10:4–5; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:4–5"> Then he said to me, “This is the message that Yahweh says that you should give to Zerubbabel the governor of Judah: ‘You will do what I want you to do, but it will not be by your own strength or power. It will be done by the power of my Spirit, says the Commander of the armies of angels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:25; ZECHARIAH 4:9; MICAH 4:1"> Zerubbabel, you have many difficult matters to handle. They are like high mountains &#91;MET&#93;. But they will become easy to handle, as though they will become flat land. And you will bring to the temple the final stone to complete the rebuilding of the temple. When you do that, all the people will shout repeatedly, “It is beautiful! May God bless it!”’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then Yahweh gave me another message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezra 6:14–15; ZECHARIAH 6:12–13; ZECHARIAH 6:15"> He said to me, “Zerubbabel &#91;SYN&#93; laid the first stone in the foundation of the temple, and he will put the last stone in its place. When that happens, the people will know that it is I, the Commander of the armies of angels, who have sent you to them.” And he said, “The seven bowls represent the eyes of Yahweh, who looks back and forth at everything that happens on the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 8:7; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:28–29; 2 CHRONICLES 16:9"> No one &#91;RHQ&#93; should think that little things that are done at the beginning of a big project are unimportant. When the temple is almost finished being rebuilt, Zerubbabel will use a ◄plumb line/string with a stone fastened to it► to see if the walls they are building are straight; so when the people see that, they will rejoice.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 4:3; REVELATION 11:4"> Then I asked the angel, “What is the meaning of the two olive trees, one on each side of the lampstand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="REVELATION 11:4; MATTHEW 20:23; Haggai 1:1"> And what isthe meaning of the two olive branches, one alongside each of the gold pipes from which olive oil flows to the lamps?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 4:5; HEBREWS 5:11–12"> He replied, “Surely &#91;RHQ&#93; you know what they are?”<br />I replied, “No, sir, I do not know.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="REVELATION 11:4; Isaiah 61:1–3; DANIEL 9:24–26"> So he said, “They represent the two men who have been appointed &#91;MTY&#93; to serve the Lord who rules the entire earth.”</span>
</div>
