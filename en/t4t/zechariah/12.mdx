## Jerusalem will be rescued

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 42:5; JEREMIAH 51:15; Isaiah 57:16"> This is a message from Yahweh concerning Israel. Yahweh is the one who stretched out the sky, who created the earth, and who ◄gave life to humans/caused humans to be alive►. This is what he says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 14:14; PSALMS 75:8; Isaiah 51:22–23"> “I will soon cause Jerusalem to be like &#91;MET&#93; a cup full of very strong alcoholic drink, and the people of other nations who drink it will stagger around. And they will come and attack Jerusalem and the other towns in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:44; ZECHARIAH 14:2–4; DANIEL 2:34–35"> At that time, the armies of all the nations will gather to attack Jerusalem, but I will cause the leaders of Judah to be like &#91;SIM&#93; a very heavy rock, and all who try to lift it will be badly injured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:28; ZECHARIAH 12:3; ZECHARIAH 12:6"> At that time I will cause every one of their enemies’ horses to panic, and their riders to become crazy. I &#91;IDM&#93; will protect the people of Judah, but I will cause all their enemies’ horses to become blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:12; JOEL 3:16; PSALMS 18:32"> Then the leaders of Judah will say to themselves, ‘The people in Jerusalem are strong because the Commander of the armies of angels is their God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:18; MICAH 5:5–8; ZECHARIAH 2:4"> At that time I will enable the leaders of Judah to be like &#91;SIM&#93; a pan containing hot coals that is put in a woodpile to set it on fire, or like &#91;SIM&#93; a burning torch that is put in a field of ripe grain to set it on fire. The leaders of Judah and their army will destroy the people of the surrounding nations in all directions. But the people of Jerusalem will remain safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:18; ROMANS 3:27; LUKE 1:51–53"> And I, Yahweh, will protect the tents of the soldiers in other places in Judah before I rescue the people of Jerusalem, in order that the leaders of Jerusalem &#91;DOU&#93; will not be honored more than the people in other places in Judah are honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MICAH 5:2–4; ZECHARIAH 9:8; JOEL 3:16–17"> At that time, I will protect &#91;MET&#93; the people of Jerusalem. When that happens, even the weakest people in Jerusalem will be strong like King David was. And the descendants &#91;MTY&#93; of David will rule the people like I would, as though an angel sent from me, Yahweh, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; ZECHARIAH 14:2–3; Haggai 2:22"> At that time, I will prepare to destroy all the nations that attack Jerusalem.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Mourning for the one who will be stabbed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="REVELATION 1:7; EZEKIEL 39:29; JOEL 2:28–29"> “I, Yahweh, will cause the descendants &#91;MTY&#93; of King David to act very kindly and mercifully. They will look at me, the one who has been stabbed, and they will cry bitterly, like people cry for a firstborn son who has died &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:24; 2 KINGS 23:29"> At that time, many people in Jerusalem will be crying bitterly, like people cried when King Josiah was killed in the battle at Hadad-Rimmon on the Megiddo Plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 7:3; LUKE 3:31; 2 SAMUEL 5:14"> Many people in Judah will cry, each family by themselves: the male descendants of David by themselves, and their wives by themselves, the male descendants of David’s son Nathan by themselves, and their wives by themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:7; 2 SAMUEL 16:5; 1 CHRONICLES 23:10"> the male descendants &#91;MTY&#93; of Levi by themselves, and their wives by themselves, the male descendants of Levi’s grandson Shimei by themselves, and their wives by themselves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:12"> and all the other members of the kings’ clans and the priests’ clans by themselves, and their wives by themselves.”</span>
</div>
