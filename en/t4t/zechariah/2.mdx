## The man with a measuring line

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 40:3; ZECHARIAH 1:16; EZEKIEL 47:4"> Then I looked up and saw a man with a measuring line/tape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:39; EZEKIEL 45:6; REVELATION 21:15–17"> I asked him, “Where are you going?” He replied, “I am going to measure Jerusalem, to determine how wide it is and how long it is.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:19; ZECHARIAH 1:8–11; ZECHARIAH 4:5"> Then the angel who had been talking to me started to leave, and another angel walked toward him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:17; JEREMIAH 33:22; EZEKIEL 38:11"> That other angel said to him, “Run and tell that young man that some day there will be very many people and livestock in Jerusalem, with the result that there will be too many to live inside the city walls; many will live outside the walls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 4:5; ZECHARIAH 9:8; PSALMS 3:3"> And Yahweh says that he himself will be like &#91;MET&#93; a wall of fire around the city, and he will be there with his glory.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; EZEKIEL 17:21; Isaiah 52:11–12"> Yahweh declares to the people who were exiled to Babylonia, “Run! Flee from Babylonia &#91;MTY&#93;, the country east of Israel, and flee in all directions from the places where I caused you to be scattered!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; GENESIS 19:17; Isaiah 52:11"> You people who previously lived in Jerusalem and who now live in Babylon, flee from there!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 17:8; DEUTERONOMY 32:10; JOHN 15:21–23"> The armies of the nations that attacked you and took away your valuable possessions have attacked those who are very precious to the Commander of the armies of angels &#91;IDM&#93;. So he says this about me: ‘After I have honored him and sent him to rebuke the nations,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 19:16; Isaiah 14:2; ZECHARIAH 4:9"> it is as though I will strike them with my fist, with the result that those who were their slaves will now take back their possessions from those who caused them to become slaves.’ And when that happens, you will know that the Commander of the armies of angels is the one who sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:20; JOHN 1:14; ZEPHANIAH 3:14–15"> Yahweh says, “You people of Jerusalem &#91;APO&#93;, shout and be happy, because I will be coming to you and I will live among you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="REVELATION 11:15; 1 PETER 2:9–10; ACTS 28:28"> At that time, people of many nations will be united to Yahweh and will become his people. He will live among all of you; and you will know that the Commander of the armies of angels is the one who sent me to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:17; DEUTERONOMY 32:9; JEREMIAH 10:16"> The people of Judah will be a very special part of his sacred country, and Jerusalem will again be the city that he has chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:20; PSALMS 68:5; PSALMS 78:65"> Everyone, everywhere, should be silent in the presence of Yahweh, because he again will come down from the sacred place where he lives in heaven to do great things for us.</span>
</div>
