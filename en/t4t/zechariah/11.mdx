## The parable of the two shepherds

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:7; MATTHEW 23:37; ROMANS 15:8"> This is what Yahweh my God said to me: “I want you to become a shepherd for a flock of sheep that are about to be slaughtered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:7; HOSEA 12:8; 1 TIMOTHY 6:5–10"> The people who are going to buy the sheep will kill the sheep, and they will not be punished. Those who are selling the sheep say, ‘I praise Yahweh, because I will become rich!’ Even the shepherds do not feel sorry for the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 13:14; MATTHEW 22:7; Isaiah 9:19–21"> And similarly, I no longer feel sorry for the people of this country. I am going to allow many of them &#91;HYP&#93; to be captured by other people or by their king. Those who capture them will ruin this country, and I will not rescue any of the people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:14; ZECHARIAH 11:4; Isaiah 11:4"> So I became the shepherd of a flock of sheep that were about to be slaughtered for their meat to be sold to the dealers. I took good care of the sheep, even the ones that were the weakest sheep. Then I took two shepherds’ ◄staffs/walking sticks►. I named the one staff ‘Kindness’ and the other staff ‘Union’. And I took good care of the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 49:7; MATTHEW 23:34–36; JOHN 15:18"> But the three shepherds who had been working with me detested me, and I became impatient with them. Within one month I ◄dismissed/got rid of► those shepherds.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 43:11; MATTHEW 13:10–11; MATTHEW 21:19"> So I said to the dealers, “I will no longer be the shepherd. I will allow the ones that are dying to die. I will allow the ones that are getting lost to get lost. And I will not prevent those that remain from destroying each other.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:7; PSALMS 89:39; HEBREWS 8:8–13"> Then I took the staff that I had named ‘Kindness’ and I broke it. That showed that Yahweh was annulling/canceling the agreement that he had made with all the people-groups.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:12; LUKE 2:38; DEUTERONOMY 31:21"> So that agreement was ended immediately. And the men who bought and sold sheep who were watching me knew by seeing what I was doing that I was giving them a message from Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:15; EXODUS 21:32; LUKE 22:3–6"> I told them, “If you think it is what you should do, pay me for taking care of the sheep. If you do not think that is what you should do, do not pay me.” So they paid me only 30 pieces of silver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:3–10; ACTS 1:18–19; Isaiah 53:2–3"> Then Yahweh said to me, “That is a ridiculously small amount of money that they have paid you! So throw it to the man who makes clay pots!” So I took the silver to the temple of Yahweh, and I threw it in the chest where the offerings/money is kept.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 9:21; Isaiah 11:13; ACTS 23:7–10"> Then I broke my second staff, the one that I named ‘Union’. That indicated that Judah and Israel would no longer be united.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10–12; LUKE 11:40; JEREMIAH 2:26–27"> Then Yahweh said to me, “Take again the things that a foolish shepherd uses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:2–6; JEREMIAH 23:2; MATTHEW 23:2–4"> because I am going to appoint a new king for the people, one who will not take care of my people. He will be like a foolish shepherd: &#91;MET&#93; He will not take care of those who are dying, those who are very young, those who have been injured, or those who do not have enough food. Instead, he will treat them very cruelly, like a shepherd who would &#91;MET&#93; kill and eat the best sheep and tear off their hoofs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:1; EZEKIEL 13:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But terrible things will happen to that foolish/useless king</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who abandons the people &#91;MET&#93; over whom he rules.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His enemies will strike his arm and his right eye with their swords.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The result will be that he will have no strength in his arm,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and his right eye will become completely blind.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>
