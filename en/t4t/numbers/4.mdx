## The work of the clans descended from Kohath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:27; NUMBERS 3:19"> “Write down the names of the men who belong to the clans descended from Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:23; 1 CHRONICLES 23:3–5; 2 KINGS 11:4–12"> Write the names of the men who are between 30 and 50 years old. These will be men who will work at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:19; MARK 13:34; NUMBERS 4:30"> “The work of these descendants of Kohath will be to take care of the sacred items that are used when the people worship at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:51; HEBREWS 9:3; EXODUS 40:3"> When you Israelis move to another location, Aaron and his sons must enter the tent to take down the curtain that separates the Very Holy Place from the other part of the Sacred Tent. They must cover the sacred chest with that curtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 25:13–15; EXODUS 35:19; NUMBERS 4:7–8"> Then they must cover that with a nice covering made from the skins of goats. Over that they must spread a blue cloth. Then they must insert into the rings on the chest the poles for carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:5–8; EXODUS 37:10–16; EXODUS 25:23–30"> “Then they must put a blue cloth over the table on which the priests put the sacred bread. On top of the cloth they must put the bowls for incense, the pans, the other dishes, the jars for the wine that will be offered as a sacrifice, and the sacred bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:6–7; NUMBERS 4:11–13; NUMBERS 4:9"> Over all of this they must spread a scarlet/red cloth. Finally, they must put on top a nice covering made from the skins of goats. Then they must insert into the rings at the corners of the tables the poles for carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 25:31–39; EXODUS 37:17–24; REVELATION 1:20–1"> “Then with another blue cloth they must cover the lampstand, the lamps, the lamp snuffers, the trays, and the special jars of olive oil to burn in the lamps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:12; NUMBERS 4:6"> They must cover the lampstand and all the other items with a nice covering made from the skins of goats. They must place all these things on a frame for carrying them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 40:5; EXODUS 30:1–19; EXODUS 40:26–27"> “Then they must spread another blue cloth over the gold altar which is used for burning incense. Over this they must spread a nice covering made from the skins of goats. Then they must insert into the rings of that altar the poles for carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 9:29; EXODUS 25:9; 2 CHRONICLES 4:22"> “They must take all the other items that are inside the Sacred Tent and wrap them in a blue cloth, cover that with a nice covering made from the skins of goats, and place all that on a frame for carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:12–13; EXODUS 39:1; EXODUS 39:41"> “Then they must remove the ashes from the altar on which they have burned sacrifices. Then they must cover the altar with a purple cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 4:19; EXODUS 38:1–7"> Then they must spread on top of the cloth all the items used at the altar—the pans for carrying the hot coals, the meat forks, the shovels, the bowls for containing the blood to sprinkle on the people, and all the other containers. Then they must spread over all those things a nice covering made from the skins of goats. Then they must insert into the rings at the sides of the altar the poles for carrying it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:6–7; NUMBERS 7:9; DEUTERONOMY 31:9"> “Then Aaron and his sons have finished covering all these sacred things, the Israeli people will be ready to move to a new location. The descendants of Kohath must come and carry all the sacred things to the next place where you Israelis will set up your tents. But the descendants of Kohath must not touch any of these sacred items, because they will immediately die if they touch them. They are the ones who will carry these things, but they must not touch them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 25:6; LEVITICUS 24:2; EXODUS 30:23–38"> “Aaron’s son Eleazar will have the work of taking care of the olive oil for the lamps, the nice-smelling incense, the grain which will be burned on the altar each day, and the olive oil for anointing the priests. Eleazar is the one who will supervise the work that is done at the Sacred Tent and the men who take care of everything that is in it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> “Then Yahweh said to Aaron and Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:6–7; NUMBERS 17:10; NUMBERS 18:5"> “When the descendants of Kohath approach the sacred items in the Sacred Tent to take them to another location, Aaron and his sons must always go in with them and show each of them what work to do and what things to carry. But the descendants of Kohath must not enter the Sacred Tent at any other time and look at the things that are in it. If they do that, I will get rid of all the descendants of Kohath.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The work of the men descended from Gershon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:18; NUMBERS 3:21; NUMBERS 3:24"> “Write down the names of all the men who belong to the clans descended from the Gershon division of the descendants of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:3; 1 TIMOTHY 1:18; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:7"> Write the names of the men who are between 30 and 50 years old. They will be men who will also work at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:19; NUMBERS 4:31–32; NUMBERS 4:47"> “This is the work that they must do and the things that they must carry when you move to a new location:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:25–26; EXODUS 26:14; NUMBERS 7:5–7"> They must carry the curtains of the Sacred Tent; they must carry the Sacred Tent and all the things that cover it, including the nice outer covering made from skins of goats, and the curtain which is at the entrance of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 35:18; EXODUS 27:9"> They must also carry the curtains that form the wall that surrounds the courtyard that surrounds the Sacred Tent and the altar, the curtain that is at the entrance to the courtyard, and the ropes that fasten the curtains. They must also do the packing and loading of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 1:70; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:2"> Aaron and his sons will supervise the work of all the descendants of Gershom. That work includes carrying those things and doing other work that is necessary for moving them. They must tell each of the descendants of Gershom what things they must carry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:33; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:5–6"> Those are the tasks that you must give to the men who belong to the clans descended from Gershom. Aaron’s son Ithamar is the one who will supervise their work.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The work of the men descended from Merari

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:33–35"> Count also the men who belong to the clans descended from Levi’s son Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:3; NUMBERS 4:23; 2 TIMOTHY 2:4"> Write the names of the men who are between 30 and 50 years old. They will be men who will also work at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:36–37; EXODUS 26:15; NUMBERS 7:8–9"> Their work will be to carry the frames that hold up the tent, the crossbars, the posts that hold up the curtains, and the bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 38:21; 1 CHRONICLES 9:29; NUMBERS 3:8"> They must also carry the posts for the curtains that form the walls of the courtyard and the bases for the posts, the tent pegs, and the ropes to fasten the curtains. Tell each man what things he must carry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:28; JOSHUA 3:6; Isaiah 3:6"> Those are the tasks that the descendants of Merari must do at the Sacred Tent. Aaron’s son Ithamar is the one who will supervise them.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Summary of the counting of the descendants of Levi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> So Aaron and Moses/I and the Israeli leaders counted the descendants of Kohath, writing also the names of their clans and family groups.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:24; NUMBERS 4:23; 1 CHRONICLES 23:3"> They/We counted all the men who were between 30 and 50 years old who were able to work at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The total was 2,750 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> They were the descendants of Kohath who were able to work at the Sacred Tent. Aaron and Moses/I counted them just as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> They/We also counted the descendants of Gershon, writing also the names of their clans and family groups.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> They/We counted all the men who were between 30 and 50 years old who were able to work at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:32"> The total was 2,630 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> They were the descendants of Gershon who were able to work at the Sacred Tent. Aaron and Moses/I counted them as Yahweh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> They/We also counted the descendants of Merari, writing also the names of their clans and family groups.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> They/We counted all the men who were between 30 and 50 years old who were able to work at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:13; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:9–10; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:8–12"> The total was 3,200 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:29"> They were the descendants of Merari who were able to work. Aaron and Moses/I counted them as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> So Aaron and Moses/I and the Israeli leaders counted all the descendants of Levi, writing also the names of their clans and family groups.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:4–31; ROMANS 12:6–8; NUMBERS 4:3"> They/We counted all the men who were between 30 and 50 years old. They were ones who were able to work at the Sacred Tent and who carried the tent and everything that was connected with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:39; MATTHEW 20:16; MATTHEW 22:15"> The total was 8,580 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:54; NUMBERS 4:31; NUMBERS 4:15"> They/We completed the counting of all the descendants of Levi, as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me. And they/we told each man what work he was to do and what things he must carry when they moved to a new location.</span>
</div>
