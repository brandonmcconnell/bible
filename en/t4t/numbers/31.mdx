## The Israelis attacked the Midian people-group

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh spoke to Moses/me and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:13; NUMBERS 25:14–18; REVELATION 6:10"> “Before you die, tell the Israeli people that they should ◄pay back/get revenge on► the Midian people-group for what they did to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:25; NUMBERS 25:11; 2 KINGS 10:30"> So Moses/I said to the people, “Get some men prepared for battle. Yahweh will enable them to ◄pay back/get revenge on► the Midian people-group for what they did to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:8; 1 SAMUEL 14:6; JUDGES 7:2"> Select 1,000 men from each tribe to fight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So 12,000 men prepared for fighting in the battle, 1,000 from each tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:44; NUMBERS 10:8–9; 2 CHRONICLES 13:12–15"> When Moses/I sent them to the battle, Phinehas, the son of Eleazar the priest, went with them. He took with him some of the things from the Sacred Tent and the trumpets that would be blown to give the signal to start the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JUDGES 21:11; 1 KINGS 11:15–16; 1 SAMUEL 27:9"> The Israeli men fought the soldiers from the Midian people-group, as Yahweh had told Moses/me to tell them to do, and they killed every man from the Midian people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:21–22; NUMBERS 25:15; REVELATION 2:14"> Among those whom they killed were the five kings of the Midian people-group—Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba. They also killed with a sword Balaam, the son of Beor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:14; 2 CHRONICLES 28:8–10; 2 CHRONICLES 28:5"> They captured all the women and children of the Midian people-group and took away their cattle, their flocks of sheep, and herds of goats, and all their other possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 SAMUEL 30:1; JOSHUA 6:24"> Then they burned down all the houses in the towns and villages where the people of the Midian people-group lived,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:14; JOSHUA 8:2"> but they took home with them all the women and children and animals and possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:1"> They brought all these to Eliezer and Moses/me, and to the rest of the Israeli people who were at their/our camp on the plains where the Moab people-group lived, near the Jordan River, across from Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:2; 1 SAMUEL 30:21; NUMBERS 31:12"> Eleazar and all the leaders of the people and Moses/I went outside the camp to greet/meet with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:19; 1 SAMUEL 15:13–14; NUMBERS 31:48"> But Moses/I was angry with some of the men who had returned from the battle. He/I was angry with the army officers and the men who were commanders of 1,000 men and those who were commanders of 100 men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:3; JEREMIAH 48:10; DEUTERONOMY 2:34"> He/I asked them, “Why did you allow the women to live &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 25:1–9; 2 PETER 2:15; REVELATION 2:14"> They are the ones who did what Balaam suggested and urged/persuaded our people to worship Baal instead of Yahweh. As a result, Yahweh caused a plague to strike his people while they were at Peor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JUDGES 21:11–12; DEUTERONOMY 20:16–18; DEUTERONOMY 7:2"> So, now you must kill all the boys of the Midian people-group, and also kill all the women who have had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with any man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:10–14; 2 CHRONICLES 28:8–10; DEUTERONOMY 20:14"> Spare only the girls who are virgins &#91;EUP&#93;. You can keep them to be your wives or your slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:11–22; NUMBERS 5:2; 1 CHRONICLES 22:8"> All of you who have killed someone or touched the corpse of someone who was killed in the battle must stay outside the camp for seven days. On the third day and on the seventh day, you must perform the ritual to cause you and those whom you have captured to become acceptable to God again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:22; GENESIS 35:2; EXODUS 19:10"> You must also wash your clothes and anything that you took to the battle that is made of leather or goat’s hair or wood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 30:16"> Then Eleazar said to the soldiers who had returned from the battle, “This is what Yahweh has instructed Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> You must put into a fire any gold or silver or bronze or iron or tin or lead things that you brought back from the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:9; NUMBERS 19:17; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:13"> Put everything that will not burn into the fire, and then they will be acceptable for you to use. But also sprinkle those things with the water that causes things and people to become acceptable to God. The things that would burn if you put them in a fire, sprinkle them with that water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:25; LEVITICUS 15:13; LEVITICUS 14:9"> On the seventh day, wash your clothes, and then you will become acceptable to God again. After you do that, you may return to the camp.”</span>

## Dividing the captured goods

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “Tell Eleazar and the leaders of the family groups that they must write down a list of all the goods, the women, and the animals that were captured in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOSHUA 22:8; PSALMS 68:12; 1 SAMUEL 30:24–25"> Then they must divide all those things, half given to the men who fought in the battle and the other half given to the rest of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:26; NUMBERS 31:30; NUMBERS 31:47"> From the men who fought in the battle, take one from every 500 people and from every 500 cattle and donkeys and sheep, to be a tax for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EXODUS 29:27; DEUTERONOMY 12:19; DEUTERONOMY 12:12"> Take these things to Eleazar to ◄be my share/belong to me►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:31; NUMBERS 3:7–8; NUMBERS 3:25"> And from the half that belongs to the ordinary people, take one item from every 50. That includes people, cattle, donkeys, sheep, goats, and other animals. Give those things to the descendants of Levi who take care of my Sacred Tent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Eleazar and Moses/I did what Yahweh commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> There were 675,000 sheep, 72,000 cattle, 61,000 donkeys, and 32,000 virgins that they had captured from the Midian people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The men who fought in the battle took 337,000 sheep from the battle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and they gave 675 of them to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> They took 36,000 cattle and gave 72 of them to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> They took 30,500 donkeys, and they gave 61 of them to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> They took 16,000 virgins, and they dedicated 32 of them to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:8; NUMBERS 18:19; HEBREWS 7:9–12"> Moses/I gave to Eleazar all the animals that had been given to Yahweh, as Yahweh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Moses/I separated what those who had fought in the battle received from what the other people received. The people had taken 337,500 sheep, 36,000 cattle, 30,500 donkeys, and 16,000 virgins,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:30; LUKE 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> From what the people received, Moses/I took one from every 50 items and dedicated them to Yahweh. That included animals and people. As Yahweh commanded, he/I gave them all to the descendants of Levi who took care of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Then the army officers and those who commanded 1,000 men and those who commanded 100 men came to Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="JOHN 18:9; 1 SAMUEL 30:18–19; PSALMS 72:14"> They said, “We, who are your servants, have counted the soldiers whom we command, and we found that none of them ◄is missing/has been killed►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="EXODUS 30:12; EXODUS 30:15–16; PSALMS 116:12"> So to thank Yahweh for that, we have brought to him a gift of the gold items that we found after the battle: Gold arm bands and bracelets and rings and earrings and necklaces. This will enable us to be forgiven for our sins.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:2–6"> So Eleazar and Moses/I accepted the gold items that they brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> The total of it weighed about ◄420 pounds/190 kg.►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:14; NUMBERS 31:32"> Each soldier had taken these things for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="EXODUS 30:16; ZECHARIAH 6:14; ACTS 10:4"> Eleazar and Moses/I accepted these gold items from these commanders and put them in the Sacred Tent to remind the Israeli people about how Yahweh had helped them defeat the Midian people-group.</span>
</div>
