## The Israelis defeated the Canaan people

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 1:16; NUMBERS 33:40; JOSHUA 12:14"> The king of Arad city lived in the area where the Canaan people-group lived, in the desert in the southern part of the land. He heard a report that the Israelis were approaching on the road to Atharim village. So his army attacked the Israelis and captured some of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 11:30; GENESIS 28:20; 1 SAMUEL 1:11"> Then the Israelis solemnly vowed: “Yahweh, if you will help us to defeat these people, we will completely destroy all their towns.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:45; DEUTERONOMY 1:44; PSALMS 10:17"> Yahweh heard what they requested, and he enabled them to defeat the army of the Canaan people-group. The Israeli soldiers killed all the people and destroyed their towns. Ever since that time, that place has been called Hormah which means ‘destruction’.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The bronze snake

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 6:9; JUDGES 11:18; NUMBERS 32:7"> Then the Israelis left Hor Mountain and traveled on the road towards the Red Sea, in order to go around the land of Edom. But the people became impatient along the way,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 78:19; NUMBERS 16:13–14; EXODUS 17:2–3"> and they began to grumble/complain against God and against Moses/me. They said, “Why have you brought us out of Egypt to die here in this desert &#91;RHQ&#93;? There is nothing to eat here, and nothing to drink. And we detest this lousy manna food!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:9; JEREMIAH 8:17; DEUTERONOMY 8:15"> So Yahweh sent poisonous snakes among them. Many of the people were bitten by the snakes and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ACTS 8:24; NUMBERS 11:2; PSALMS 78:34"> Then the people came to Moses/me and cried out, saying, “We now know that we have sinned against Yahweh and against you. Pray to Yahweh, asking that he will take away the snakes!” So Moses/I prayed for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 3:14; PSALMS 106:43–45; PSALMS 145:8"> Then Yahweh told him/me, “Make a model/image of a poisonous snake, and attach it to the top of a pole. If those who are bitten by the snakes look at that model, they will ◄recover/get well►.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 3:14–15; HEBREWS 12:2; JOHN 6:40"> So Moses/I made a snake from bronze and attached it to the top of a pole. Then, when those who had been bitten by a snake looked at the bronze snake, they recovered!</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Israelis traveled to Moab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:43–45"> Then the Israelis traveled to Oboth and ◄camped/set up their tents► there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then they left there, and went to Iye-Abarim, in the desert on the eastern border of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:13–14"> From there they traveled to the valley where the Zered riverbed is, and camped there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 11:18; NUMBERS 22:36; Isaiah 16:2"> Then they traveled to the north side of the Arnon River. That area is in the desert next to the land where the Amor people-group lived. The Arnon River is the boundary between Moab and where the Amor people-group lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:13; 2 SAMUEL 1:18"> That is why in the book called ‘The Book of the Wars of Yahweh’ it tells about<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Waheb town in the Suphah area, and the ravines there;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the Arnon River</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:28; DEUTERONOMY 2:18; DEUTERONOMY 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">and the ravines there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which extend as far as Ar village on the border of Moab.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:8; Isaiah 12:3; Isaiah 41:17–18"> From there, the Israelis traveled to Beer. There was a well there, where Yahweh previously had said to Moses/me, “Gather the people together, and I will give them water.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JAMES 5:13; EXODUS 15:1–2; PSALMS 106:12"> There the Israelis sang this song:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“O well, give us water!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Sing about this well!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 TIMOTHY 6:17–18; JAMES 4:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sing about this well</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which our leaders dug;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they dug out the dirt with their royal scepters and their walking sticks.”</span>Then the Israelis left that desert and went through Mattanah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Nahaliel, and Bamoth villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:28; NUMBERS 23:14; DEUTERONOMY 1:5"> Then they went to the valley in Moab where Pisgah Mountain rises above the desert.</span>
</div>

## The Israelis defeated kings Sihon and Og

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:26–28; NUMBERS 20:14–19; JUDGES 11:19–21"> Then the Israelis sent messengers to Sihon, the king of the Amor people-group. This was the message that they/we gave him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:17"> “Allow us to travel through your country. We will stay on the king’s highway, the main road that goes from the south to the north, until we have finished traveling through your land. We will not walk through any field or vineyard, or drink water from your wells.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JUDGES 11:20; DEUTERONOMY 29:7–8; Isaiah 15:4"> But King Sihon refused. He would not allow them to walk through his land. Instead, he sent his whole army to attack the Israelis in the desert. They attacked the Israelis at Jahaz village.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="AMOS 2:9; JOSHUA 24:8; GENESIS 32:22"> But the Israelis completely defeated them and occupied their land, from the Arnon River in the south to the Jabbok River in the north. They stopped at the border of the land where the Ammon people-group lived, because the Ammon army was defending the border strongly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:33–42; Isaiah 16:8–9; JEREMIAH 48:2"> So the Israelis occupied all the cities and towns where the Amor people-group lived, and some of the Israelis began to live in them. They occupied Heshbon city and the nearby villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Heshbon was the capital of the country. It was the city where King Sihon ruled. His army had previously defeated the army of the king of Moab, and then his people had begun to live in all of the land of Moab as far as the Arnon River in the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:4; NUMBERS 21:14; HABAKKUK 2:6"> For that reason, one of the poets wrote long ago,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Come to Heshbon, the city where King Sihon ruled.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We want the city to be restored/rebuilt.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:15; JEREMIAH 48:45–46; Isaiah 15:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">A fire blazed from Heshbon;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it burned down Ar city in Moab,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it destroyed everything on the hills along the Arnon River.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JUDGES 11:24; 2 KINGS 23:13; JEREMIAH 48:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">You people of Moab, terrible things have happened to you!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You people who worship your god Chemosh have been ◄annihilated/wiped out►!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The men who worshiped &#91;MET&#93; Chemosh have run away and are now refugees,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the women who worshiped him have been captured by the army of Sihon, the king of the Amor people-group.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:18; Isaiah 15:2; JEREMIAH 48:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">But we have defeated Sihon and those descendants of Amor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all the way from Heshbon in the north to Dibon city in the south.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We have completely obliterated/destroyed them as far as Nophah and Medeba towns.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:8–12; NUMBERS 32:33–42; DEUTERONOMY 3:16–17"> So the Israeli people began to live in the land where the Amor people-group lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:32; NUMBERS 32:1; NUMBERS 32:35"> After Moses/I sent some men to explore the area near Jazer city, Israeli people began to live in all the towns in that region and expelled the Amor people-group who lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:12; DEUTERONOMY 1:4; DEUTERONOMY 29:7"> Then they turned north toward the Bashan region, but King Og of Bashan and all his army attacked them at Edrei town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 135:10–11; JOSHUA 8:7; DEUTERONOMY 3:2–3"> Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Do not be afraid of Og, because I am going to enable your men to defeat him and his army, and to take possession of all his land. You will do to him what you did to Sihon, the king of the Amor people-group, who ruled in Heshbon.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:7–8; JOSHUA 13:12; DEUTERONOMY 3:3–17"> And that is what happened. We Israelis defeated Og’s army, and killed King Og and his sons and all his people. Not a person survived! And then we Israelis began to live in their land.</span>
</div>
