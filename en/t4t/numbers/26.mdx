## The people were counted again

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 25:9"> After the plague ended, Yahweh said to Eleazar and Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 38:25–26; NUMBERS 1:2–3; EXODUS 30:11–16"> “Count all the people of Israel again. Write down the names of all the men who are at least 20 years old who are able to fight in battles, along with their family names.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:1; NUMBERS 33:48; NUMBERS 35:1"> So while the Israelis were on the plains where the Moab people-group lived, Eleazar and Moses/I told this to the Israeli leaders:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:1; 1 CHRONICLES 21:1"> “Write down the names of all the Israeli men who are at least 20 years old, as Yahweh has commanded us.” So they did that, and this is a record of all the descendants of Jacob who came out of Egypt who were still alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 5:3; GENESIS 46:8–9; EXODUS 6:14"> They counted 43,730 men from the tribe of Reuben, Jacob’s oldest son. They were in clans descended from Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Pallu’s son was Eliab</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:16; NUMBERS 16:1–35; PSALMS 106:17"> and his grandsons were Nemuel, Dathan and Abiram. Dathan and Abiram were the leaders who joined with Korah to conspire against Aaron and Moses/me and rebelled against Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:38; 2 PETER 2:6; NUMBERS 16:31–35"> But the earth opened up and swallowed them and Korah. Yahweh also sent a fire that burned up 250 men who supported those three men. That was a warning to all the Israeli people that they should respect and obey the leaders whom Yahweh had appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 6:24; PSALMS 42:1; NUMBERS 16:33"> But the descendants of Korah did not die on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 4:24; GENESIS 46:10; EXODUS 6:15"> The Israeli leaders counted 22,200 men from the tribe of Simeon. They were in clans descended from Nemuel, Jamin, Jakin, Zerah, and Shaul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 46:16; NUMBERS 2:14"> They counted 40,500 men from the tribe of Gad. They were in clans descended from Zephon, Haggi, Shuni, Ozni, Eri, Arod, and Areli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 46:12; GENESIS 38:1–10; 1 CHRONICLES 2:3–8"> They counted 76,500 men from the tribe of Judah. They were in clans descended from Shelah, Perez, Zerah, Hezron, and Hamul. Judah’s other two sons, Er and Onan, died in Canaan without having had any children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 46:13; 1 CHRONICLES 7:1; GENESIS 30:17–18"> They counted 64,300 men from the tribe of Issachar. They were in clans descended from Tola, Puvah, Jashub, and Shimron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 46:14; GENESIS 30:19–20"> They counted 60,500 men from the tribe of Zebulun. They were in clans descended from Sered, Elon, and Jahleel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 46:20; GENESIS 48:5; GENESIS 41:51–52"> They counted 52,700 men from the tribe of Manasseh, who was one of the sons of Joseph. They were in clans descended from Machir, from his son Gilead, and from his six grandsons—Jezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Shemida, and Hepher. Hepher’s son Zelophehad did not have any sons, but he had five daughters—Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 7:20–21"> They counted 32,500 men from the tribe of Ephraim, who was Joseph’s other son. They were in clans descended from Shuthelah, Becher, Tahan, and Shuthelah’s son Eran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 8:1; 1 CHRONICLES 7:6–12; GENESIS 46:2"> They counted 45,600 men from the tribe of Benjamin. They were in clans descended from Bela, Ashbel, Ahiram, Shephupham, and Hupham, and from Bela’s two sons—Ard and Naaman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="GENESIS 46:23"> They counted 64,400 men from the tribe of Dan. They were in one clan descended from Shuham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GENESIS 46:17; 1 CHRONICLES 7:30"> They counted 53,400 men from the tribe of Asher. They were in clans descended from Imnah, Ishvi, and Beriah, and from Beriah’s two sons—Heber and Malkiel. Asher also had a daughter named Serah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="GENESIS 46:24; 1 CHRONICLES 7:13"> They counted 45,400 men from the tribe of Naphtali. They were in clans descended from Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, and Shillem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:46; NUMBERS 2:32; EXODUS 12:37"> The total of the Israeli men whom the leaders counted was 601,730.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:23; JOSHUA 14:1; PSALMS 105:44"> “Divide Canaan land among the tribes. Distribute the land according the number of people in each tribe that are on your lists.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:54; NUMBERS 32:3; NUMBERS 32:5"> Decide by ◄casting lots/throwing stones which are marked► to determine which group will get which area, but give the largest areas to the groups with the most people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="GENESIS 46:11; 1 CHRONICLES 6:1; EXODUS 6:16–19"> The leaders also counted the male descendants of Levi. They were in clans descended from Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:1; NUMBERS 3:17–21; EXODUS 6:20"> These also included people from the clans of Libni, Hebron, Mahli, Mushi, and Korah. All of them were descendants of Levi. Kohath was the ancestor of Amram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="EXODUS 6:20; EXODUS 2:1–2; LEVITICUS 18:12"> whose wife was Jochebed. She also was a descendant of Levi, but she was born in Egypt. She and Amram had two sons, Aaron and Moses/me, and their/our older sister Miriam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:2; NUMBERS 3:8"> Aaron’s sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:4; LEVITICUS 10:1–2; 1 CHRONICLES 24:1–2"> But Nadab and Abihu died when they burned incense for a sacrifice to Yahweh in a way that disobeyed what Yahweh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:9; NUMBERS 35:2–8; NUMBERS 3:39"> The leaders counted 23,000 men from the descendants of Levi who were at least one month old. But these men were not counted when the rest of the Israeli people were counted, because they were not given any land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:3"> Those were the men whom Eleazar and Moses/I said should be counted. They counted the Israeli people on the plains where the Moab people-group lived, on the east side of the Jordan River, across from Jericho city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:14–15; DEUTERONOMY 4:3–4; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:5"> None of the men whom they counted were on the lists that were made by Aaron and Moses/me when the Israelis were in the Sinai Desert,</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:5–6; NUMBERS 14:38; ROMANS 11:22"> because Yahweh had said about them, “They will all die in this desert,” and that is what happened. The only ones who were still alive were Jephunneh’s son Caleb and Nun’s son Joshua.</span>
</div>
