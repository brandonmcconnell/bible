<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MICAH 6:5; REVELATION 2:14; GENESIS 49:1"> So yes, I will return to my people, but first, allow me to tell you what will happen to you Moab people in the future.”</span>

## Balaam prophesied a fourth time

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:3–4; NUMBERS 23:7; MATTHEW 13:35"> So Balaam said this to Balak:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I, Balaam, son of Beor, am again giving a prophecy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">speaking as a man who sees what will happen in the future clearly speaks.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:4; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:1; 2 SAMUEL 23:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I hear a message from God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I know things that God, who lives in heaven, has revealed to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I see a vision from him who is all-powerful.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">My eyes are open as I prostrate myself in front of him.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="REVELATION 22:16; LUKE 1:32–33; GENESIS 49:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The things that I see in the vision are not going to happen now;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I see things that God will cause to happen in the future.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A man who is a descendant of Jacob will appear like a star &#91;MET&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a king who holds a scepter will be one of the Israeli people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will crush the heads of you people of Moab;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will wipe out the descendants of Seth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="AMOS 9:12; GENESIS 27:29; 2 SAMUEL 8:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Israelis will occupy Edom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will conquer their enemies who live near Seir Mountain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The Israeli people will be victorious/strong.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 49:10; MICAH 5:2; PSALMS 2:1–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">A ruler who is a descendant of Jacob will come;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will get rid of the people who still live in the city where Balaam first met Balak.”</span></span>
</div>

## Balaam’s final prophecies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 17:14; JUDGES 6:3; 1 CHRONICLES 4:43"> Then Balaam looked out over where the Amalek people-group lived, and he prophesied this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The Amalek people-group were the greatest nation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they will be wiped out.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 15:19; JUDGES 1:16; JOB 29:18"> Then he looked out over the area where the Ken people-group lived, and he prophesied this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You think that the place where you live is secure/safe</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a nest that is made in the cliffs &#91;MET&#93;,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 83:8; GENESIS 10:22; Ezra 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">but you will be wiped out</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when the army of Assyria conquers you.”</span></span>
