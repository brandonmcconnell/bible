## The silver trumpets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also told Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13; JOEL 1:14; PSALMS 81:3"> “Tell someone to make two trumpets by hammering each one from one lump of silver. Blow the trumpets to summon the people to come together and also to signal that they must move their tents to a new location.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:5; JOEL 2:15–16"> If both trumpets are blown, it means that everyone must gather together at the entrance of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:2; EXODUS 18:21; NUMBERS 1:4–16"> If only one trumpet is blown, it means that only the twelve leaders of the tribes must gather together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 2:3–9; NUMBERS 10:14; Isaiah 58:1"> If the trumpets are blown loudly, the tribes that are to the east of the Sacred Tent should start to travel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 10:18; NUMBERS 2:10–16"> When the trumpets are blown loudly the second time, the tribes that are to the south should start to travel. The loud blasts on the trumpet will signal that they should start to travel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOEL 2:1; NUMBERS 10:3–4"> When you want only to gather the people together, blow the trumpets, but do not blow them as loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:4–16; NUMBERS 31:6; 1 CHRONICLES 15:24"> “The priests who are descended from Aaron are the ones who should blow the trumpets. That is a regulation that will never be changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 10:18; JUDGES 2:18; GENESIS 8:1"> When you fight against enemies who attack you in your own land, tell the priests to blow the trumpets loudly. I, Yahweh, your God, will hear that, and I will rescue you from your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 81:3; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:16; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:24–26"> Also tell the priests to blow the trumpets when the people are happy, and at the festivals each year, and at the times when they celebrate the new moon each month. Tell them to blow the trumpets when the people bring offerings that will be completely burned, and when they bring offerings to maintain fellowship with me. If they do that, it will help you to remember that I, Yahweh your God, will help you.”</span>
</div>

## The Israelis traveled

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 40:17; NUMBERS 1:1; NUMBERS 9:11"> On the twentieth day of May in the second year after the/we Israelis left Egypt, the cloud rose up from above the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 21:21; NUMBERS 12:16; NUMBERS 9:5"> So we/the Israelis traveled from the Sinai Desert, and we/they continued traveling north until the cloud stopped in the Paran Desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:6; NUMBERS 9:23"> That was the first time we/they moved, obeying the instructions that Yahweh had given to Moses/me to tell them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 2:3–9; NUMBERS 1:7; NUMBERS 7:12"> The group that went first, carrying their flag/banner, was the group from the tribe of Judah. Nahshon, the son of Amminadab, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:18; NUMBERS 1:8"> The group from the tribe of Issachar followed them. Nethanel, the son of Zuar, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:24; NUMBERS 1:9"> The group from the tribe of Zebulun went next. Eliab, the son of Helon, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:6–8; NUMBERS 1:51; HEBREWS 12:28"> Then they dismantled the Sacred Tent, and the descendants of Gershon and Merari carried it, and they went next.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 2:10–16; NUMBERS 1:5; NUMBERS 26:5–18"> The group from the tribe of Reuben went next, carrying their flag. Elizur, the son of Shedeur, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:6; NUMBERS 7:36"> The group from the tribe of Simeon was next. Shelumiel, the son of Zurishaddai, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:14; NUMBERS 2:14; NUMBERS 7:42"> The group from the tribe of Gad was next. Eliasaph, the son of Deuel, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 10:17; NUMBERS 7:9; NUMBERS 4:4–16"> The group descended from Kohath was next. They carried the sacred items from the Sacred Tent. The Sacred Tent itself was set up at the new location before they arrived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 2:18–24; NUMBERS 1:10; NUMBERS 26:23–41"> The group from the tribe of Ephraim was next, carrying their flag. Elishama, the son of Ammihud, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:10; NUMBERS 7:54"> The group from the tribe of Manasseh went next. Gamaliel, the son of Pedahzur, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:11; NUMBERS 7:60"> The group from the tribe of Benjamin, went next. Abidan, the son of Gideoni, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:9; NUMBERS 1:12; GENESIS 49:16–17"> The ones who went last were the group from the tribe of Dan, carrying their flag. Ahiezer, the son of Ammishaddai, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:13; NUMBERS 7:72"> The group from the tribe of Asher went next. Pagiel, the son of Ocran, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:15; NUMBERS 7:78"> The group from the tribe of Naphtali went last. Ahira, the son of Enan, was their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 2:34; NUMBERS 24:4–5; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:33"> That was the order in which the groups of Israeli tribes traveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 12:7; JUDGES 4:11; EXODUS 3:1"> One day Moses/I said to his/my brother-in-law Hobab, the son of Reuel from the Midian people-group, “We are on the way to the place that Yahweh promised to give to us. Come with us, and we will take good care of you, because Yahweh has promised to do good things for us Israeli people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="RUTH 1:15–17; PSALMS 45:10; MATTHEW 21:29"> But Hobab replied, “No, I will not go with you. I want to return to my own land and to my own family.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOB 29:15; GALATIANS 6:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:14–21"> But Moses/I said, “Please do not leave us. You know the places where we can set up our tents in this desert, and you can guide us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JUDGES 1:16; DEUTERONOMY 10:18; 1 JOHN 1:3"> Come with us. We will share with you all the good things that Yahweh gives to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:33; EXODUS 3:1; PSALMS 95:11"> So Hobab agreed to go with them. The Israelis left Sinai Mountain, which they called Yahweh’s Mountain, and they walked for three days. The men carrying the sacred chest went in front of the other people for those three days, and they kept looking for a place to set up their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:12; Nehemiah 9:19; PSALMS 105:39"> The cloud sent by Yahweh was over them every day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 68:1–2; Isaiah 17:12–14; PSALMS 132:8"> Each morning when the men who were carrying the sacred chest started to walk, Moses/I said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yahweh, arise!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Scatter your enemies!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Cause those who hate you to run away from you!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:10; PSALMS 90:13–17; Isaiah 63:17"> And each time the men stopped to set down the sacred chest, Moses/I said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yahweh, stay close to the thousands of us Israelis!”</span></span>
</div>
