## The sons of Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 6:20; GENESIS 10:1; EXODUS 6:16"> These are some of the things that happened to Aaron and Moses/me when Yahweh spoke to Moses/me on Sinai Mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 6:23; NUMBERS 26:60; EXODUS 28:1"> Aaron’s four sons were Nadab the oldest, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 28:41; LEVITICUS 8:30; LEVITICUS 8:12"> They were appointed &#91;MTY&#93; (OR, Moses/I appointed them) to be priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:1–2; NUMBERS 26:61"> But, while Yahweh was watching, Nadab and Abihu died in the Sinai Desert because they burned incense in a manner that was different from what Yahweh had commanded. They had no sons, so Eleazar and Ithamar were the only sons of Aaron who were left to be priests, along with their father Aaron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:8; NUMBERS 2:33; NUMBERS 1:49–53"> “Bring the men of the tribe of Levi and present them to Aaron, in order that they may assist him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:50; NUMBERS 8:15; NUMBERS 8:11"> They will serve Aaron and all the other Israeli people, while they do their work inside the Sacred Tent and outside it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:33; NUMBERS 10:17; Isaiah 52:11"> They must serve all the Israeli people by taking care of all the things that are inside the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 8:19; NUMBERS 18:6–7; EPHESIANS 4:8"> Appoint them to help Aaron and his two sons. I have chosen them from all the Israeli people to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:51; NUMBERS 3:38; ROMANS 12:7"> Appoint Aaron and his two sons to do the work that priests do. But anyone else who comes close to the Sacred Tent to do that work must be executed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Yahweh also told Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:41; NUMBERS 8:16; NUMBERS 18:6"> “Note that from all the Israelis, I have chosen the men of the tribe of Levi to take the places of all the firstborn sons of the Israeli people. The male descendants of Levi belong to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 2:23; EXODUS 13:12; EXODUS 13:2"> because truly all the firstborn males belong to me. That is because on the day that I killed all the firstborn sons of the people of Egypt, I spared all the firstborn sons of the Israelis and set them apart for myself. I also set apart the firstborn males of your domestic animals. They belong to me, Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Yahweh spoke to Moses/me again in the Sinai Desert. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:62; MARK 10:14; 2 TIMOTHY 3:15"> “Count the male members of the tribe of Levi. Write down their names and the names of their clans and family groups. Count all the males who are at least one month old.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:39; NUMBERS 4:37; NUMBERS 4:49"> So Moses/I counted them, just as Yahweh commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 46:11; EXODUS 6:16–19; NUMBERS 15:5–23"> Levi had three sons, whose names were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:17; 1 CHRONICLES 26:1–32; EXODUS 6:17–19"> Gershon had two sons: Libni and Shimei. The clans that were descended from them had the same names as those two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 6:18; 1 CHRONICLES 6:18; 1 CHRONICLES 15:17–21"> Kohath had four sons: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. The clans that were descended from them had the same names as those four sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 6:19; 1 CHRONICLES 6:19; NUMBERS 3:33"> Merari had two sons: Mahli and Mushi. The clans that were descended from them had the same names as those two sons. Those are the clans that were descended from Levi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The two clans descended from Gershon are those that were descended from his sons Libni and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:38–40"> In those two clans there were 7,500 males who were at least one month old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:53; NUMBERS 2:17"> They were told to set up their tents on the west side of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> The leader of those two clans was Eliasaph, the son of Lael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 25:9; 1 CHRONICLES 23:32; 1 CHRONICLES 9:14–33"> Their work was to take care of the Sacred Tent, including the coverings of the tent, the curtain at the entrance of the tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 35:18; EXODUS 38:9–16; EXODUS 27:9–16"> the ◄curtains/large pieces of cloth► that formed the walls around the courtyard that is around the tent and around the altar, the curtains that were at the entrance of the courtyard, and the ropes for fastening the tent. They also did all the work of taking care of the things outside the Sacred Tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 26:23; NUMBERS 3:19; 1 CHRONICLES 23:12"> The clans that were descended from Kohath were those descended from his four sons: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:31; NUMBERS 4:35–36; NUMBERS 3:7"> In those four clans there were 8,600 males who were at least one month old. The work of the men of the clans was to take care of the things that are inside the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:53; NUMBERS 3:23; NUMBERS 2:10"> They set up their tents on the south side of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> The leader of those four clans was Elizaphan, the son of Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 36:35–36; EXODUS 27:1–8; EXODUS 26:31–33"> The work of the men of those clans was to take care of the sacred chest, the table on which the priest put the sacred bread, the lampstand, the altars, all the items that the priest used in the Sacred Tent, and the curtain that is inside the tent. Their work was also to take care of the things inside the tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:27; NUMBERS 4:16; NUMBERS 20:25–28"> Aaron’s son Eleazar was the leader of all the male descendants of Levi. He supervised all the work that was done at the Sacred Tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:20; 1 CHRONICLES 6:19; 1 CHRONICLES 23:21"> The clans that were descended from Merari were those descended from his sons Mahli and Mushi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="NUMBERS 2:9; NUMBERS 2:11; NUMBERS 1:21"> In those two clans there were 6,200 males who were at least one month old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:53; NUMBERS 2:25; NUMBERS 3:28–29"> They were told to set up their tents on the north side of the Sacred Tent. The leader of those two clans was Zuriel, the son of Abihail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EXODUS 26:32; EXODUS 26:37; EXODUS 26:15–29"> The work of the men of those two clans was to take care of the frames that held up the tent, the crossbars, the pillars, and the bases. They also did all the work that was connected with those items.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Their work was also to take care of the posts that held up the curtains that formed the walls of the courtyard, and all the bases, tent pegs, and ropes that fastened those curtains.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:10; NUMBERS 1:53; NUMBERS 2:3"> Aaron and Moses/I and Aaron’s sons were told to set up their/our tents in the area in front of the Sacred Tent, on the east side. Their/Our work was to supervise the work that would be done in and around the Sacred Tent, for the benefit of the Israeli people. Only the priests were permitted to do that. Yahweh declared that we should execute anyone else who went near the tent to do the work that the priests do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:62; MATTHEW 7:14; NUMBERS 4:47–48"> When Aaron and Moses/I counted all the males who were at least one month old, who belonged to the clans descended from Levi, the total was 22,000.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:15; LUKE 10:20; PSALMS 87:6"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Now count all the other firstborn males in Israel who are at least one month old, and write down their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:12; NUMBERS 3:45; NUMBERS 8:16"> Also, set apart for me the descendants of Levi to be substitutes for the firstborn males of the other Israelis. And set apart for me the livestock of the descendants of Levi to be substitutes for the firstborn livestock of the other Israeli people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> So Moses/I did that. He/I counted the firstborn males of all the Israeli people, as Yahweh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:39"> The total of those who were at least one month old was 22,273.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:12; NUMBERS 3:40–41"> “Set apart the descendants of Levi to be substitutes for the firstborn males of the other Israeli people. The descendants of Levi belong to me, Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="EXODUS 13:13; NUMBERS 18:15–16"> There are 273 more firstborn males of the other Israeli people than of the descendants of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="EXODUS 30:13; LEVITICUS 27:6; NUMBERS 18:16"> To pay for these 273 males, collect five pieces of silver for each of them. Each of those pieces of silver must weigh the same as each of the silver coins that are stored in the Sacred Tent. Give this silver to Aaron and his sons.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> So Moses/I did that. He/I collected the silver from those 273 males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:46–47; 1 PETER 3:18; MATTHEW 20:28"> The total was 1,365 pieces of silver. Each silver coin weighed the same as each of the silver coins stored in the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:48; NUMBERS 16:15; 1 SAMUEL 12:3–4"> Moses/I gave these silver coins to Aaron and his sons, as Yahweh had commanded.</span>
</div>
</div>
