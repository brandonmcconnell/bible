## Rules about special promises to Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:2; DEUTERONOMY 1:13–17; EXODUS 18:25"> Moses/I spoke with the leaders of the Israeli tribes. He/I told them these commands that Yahweh had given to him/me:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 50:14; PSALMS 116:14; ECCLESIASTES 5:4–5"> “If a man solemnly promises Yahweh that he will do something, he must do what he promised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> “If a young woman who is still living with her parents solemnly promises to Yahweh to do something,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and if her father hears about what she promised, and if he does not object, she must do what she promised &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MARK 7:10–13; MATTHEW 15:4–6; EPHESIANS 6:1"> But if her father hears about what she promised and does not allow her to do that, then she does not need to do what she promised. Yahweh will forgive her for not doing what she promised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 56:12"> “If a woman promises Yahweh that she will do something, but then she gets married,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> if her husband hears about what she promised to do, and he does not object, she must do what she promised &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 3:16; EPHESIANS 5:22–24; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:34"> But if her husband hears about it and does not allow her to do that, she does not need to do what she promised, and Yahweh will forgive her for not doing what she promised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 2:37; ROMANS 7:2; LEVITICUS 21:7"> “If a widow or a woman who has been divorced makes a promise, she must do what she promised.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “If a woman who is married promises &#91;DOU&#93; to do something,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and if her husband hears about it but does not object, she must do what she promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 30:8; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:3; NUMBERS 15:25"> But if he hears about it and does not allow her to do that, she does not need to do what she promised, and Yahweh will forgive her for not doing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 11:3; 1 PETER 3:1–6; Isaiah 58:5"> A woman’s husband may require her to do what she has promised, or he may not allow her to do what she has promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If he does not object for several days after he hears about it, she must do what she promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:1; NUMBERS 30:5; NUMBERS 30:8"> But if he waits a long time after she has promised to do something and then he tells her that he will not permit her to do it, if she does not do what she promised, she will not be punished; her husband is the one whom Yahweh will punish.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:54–57; LEVITICUS 11:46–47; NUMBERS 5:29–30"> Those are the rules that Yahweh gave to Moses/me for husbands and wives, and for young women who are still living with their parents.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
