## The duties of the priests and Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 28:38; HEBREWS 13:17; 1 PETER 2:24"> Yahweh said to Aaron, “You and your sons and the other members of your father’s family are the ones ◄who will be punished/whom I will punish► if anything bad happens to the things inside the Sacred Tent. But only you and your sons will be punished if the priests do something bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 29:34; NUMBERS 18:4; EZEKIEL 44:15"> Require that those who belong to your tribe, the tribe of Levi, must assist you and your sons while you perform your work at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:15; NUMBERS 16:40; NUMBERS 4:19–20"> But while they do that work, they must not go near the sacred items inside the tent or near the altar. If they do that, they will die, and you will die, too!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 6:19; NUMBERS 1:51; NUMBERS 3:10"> They may assist you in doing all the work to take care of the Sacred Tent, but no one else is permitted to come near the place where you are working.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 27:21; LEVITICUS 24:3; NUMBERS 16:46"> “You are the ones who will do the sacred work inside the Sacred Tent and at the altar. If you obey these instructions, I will not become very angry with the Israeli people again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:9; NUMBERS 3:12; NUMBERS 3:45"> I myself have chosen the descendants of Levi from the other Israelis in order that they may be your special helpers. They are like a gift that I have given you to work at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 15:15–16; JOHN 3:27; NUMBERS 3:10"> But it is you and your sons, who are the priests, who must perform all the rituals concerning the altar and with what happens inside the Very Holy Place. I am giving to you this work of serving as priests. So anyone else who tries to do that work must be executed.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The offerings for the priests and Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:16; LEVITICUS 7:6; EXODUS 29:29"> Yahweh also said to Aaron, “I myself have appointed you to take care of all the sacred offerings that the Israeli people bring to me. I have given all these sacred offerings to you and to your sons. You and your descendants will get a share of these offerings permanently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:7; LEVITICUS 14:13; LEVITICUS 6:25–26"> The parts of the offerings that are not completely burned on the altar belong to you. Those parts of the sacred offerings, including the offerings of grain, the offerings for sins and for the people to be forgiven for not giving me what they are required to give, are set apart, and are to be given to you and your sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:6; LEVITICUS 6:29; LEVITICUS 6:16"> You must eat those offerings in a sacred place, near the Sacred Tent. You and all of the other males in your clan may eat them, but you must consider those offerings to be holy/dedicated to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:3; EXODUS 29:27–28; LEVITICUS 10:14"> “The priests lift up high the sacred offerings while they are standing in front of the altar. All of those offerings that the Israelis offer to me belong to you and your sons and daughters. They will always be your share. All the members of your family who have performed the rituals to cause them to be acceptable to me are permitted to eat from these offerings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:4; EXODUS 23:19; EXODUS 34:26"> “ am also giving to you the first food that people harvest each year and bring to me—the best olive oil and new wine and grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 22:29; EXODUS 23:19; MICAH 7:1"> All of the first crops that people harvest and bring to me belong to you. Anyone in your family who has performed the ritual to become acceptable to me is permitted to eat that food.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:28; EZEKIEL 44:29"> “Everything in Israel that is set apart to belong to me also belongs to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 34:20; EXODUS 13:2; EXODUS 13:12–13"> The firstborn males, both humans and domestic animals, that are offered to me, will be yours. But people must buy back their firstborn sons and the firstborn animals that may not be used for sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 30:13; NUMBERS 3:47; LEVITICUS 27:2–7"> They must buy them back when they are one month old. The price that they must pay for each one is five pieces of silver. They must weigh the silver on the scales that are in the Sacred Tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 29:16; DEUTERONOMY 15:19–22; LEVITICUS 3:2–5"> “But they are not permitted to buy back the firstborn cattle or sheep or goats. They are holy and have been set apart for me. Slaughter them and sprinkle their blood on the altar. Then completely burn the fat of those animals on the altar to be an offering to me. The smell as they burn will be very pleasing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:31–34; EXODUS 29:26–28"> The meat from those offerings will be yours, just like the breast and right thigh of animals that are presented to me to maintain fellowship with me as the priest lifts them up high in front of the altar are yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 13:5; LEVITICUS 2:13; NUMBERS 18:11"> Anything that the Israeli people present to me as holy/sacred gifts, I am giving to you. They are for you and your sons and daughters to eat. They will always be your share. This is an agreement that I am making with you, an agreement that will ◄last forever/never be changed►. I am also making this agreement with your descendants.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:28; DEUTERONOMY 10:9; JOSHUA 13:33"> Yahweh also said to Aaron, “You priests will not receive any of the land or the property like the other Israeli people will receive. I am what you will receive.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:44; DEUTERONOMY 14:22–29; Nehemiah 10:37"> “Then the Israeli people bring to me a tenth of all the crops and of their newborn animals, I will give that to you descendants of Levi. That will be your payment for the work you do at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:9; NUMBERS 1:51–52; NUMBERS 3:38"> The other Israelis must not go near that tent. If they go near it, I will consider that their doing that is a sin, and they will die for committing that sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:7; NUMBERS 18:20; NUMBERS 18:1"> Only the descendants of Levi are permitted to work at the Sacred Tent, and they will be punished if anything bad happens to it. That is a law that will never be changed. You descendants of Levi will not receive any land among the other Israeli people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:26; MALACHI 3:8–10"> because I will give you one tenth of all the crops and animals that the Israelis bring to be offerings to me. That is why I said that you will not receive any land like the other Israelis will receive.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:38; NUMBERS 18:21"> “Tell this to the descendants of Levi: ‘When you receive one tenth of all the crops and animals from the Israeli people, you must give one tenth of all that to me to be a sacred offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:30; DEUTERONOMY 15:14; 2 KINGS 6:27"> Just like the other Israelis give one tenth of the grain and wine that they produce,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:20–10; GENESIS 14:18"> you must give to me one tenth of all that you receive from them. That will be your sacred offering to me. You must present it to Aaron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Select the best parts of the things that are given to you to give them to me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:9–10; GENESIS 43:11; MATTHEW 6:33"> “Also, tell this to the descendants of Levi: ‘When you present those best portions of grain and wine as your offering to me, I will consider that those gifts are as though they came from your own grain fields and vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 9:10–14; 1 TIMOTHY 5:17–18; LUKE 10:7"> You descendants of Levi and your families are permitted to eat the rest of that food, and you may eat it wherever you want to, because it is your payment for the work that you do at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:8; LEVITICUS 22:2; MALACHI 1:7"> If you give to the priests the best portions of what you receive, you will not be punished by me for accepting one tenth of the gifts that the people bring to me. But you must consider those gifts to be sacred/holy. If you sin by eating those things in ways that are contrary to these regulations that I have given to you, you will be executed.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
