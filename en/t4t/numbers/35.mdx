## The towns given to the Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:1; NUMBERS 31:12; NUMBERS 33:50"> Yahweh told this to Moses/me while the Israeli people were on the plain in the Moab region near the Jordan River, across from Jericho:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 14:3–4; EZEKIEL 48:8; JOSHUA 21:2–42"> “Tell the Israeli people that from the land that they will receive, they must give to the descendants of Levi some cities in which they can live. They must also give them some land around these cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 11:14; JOSHUA 21:11; EZEKIEL 45:2"> Those will be for the descendants of Levi to live in, and around the cities will be land for their cattle and flocks of sheep and goats and other animals.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> “The land that you give them for their animals must extend out for ◄1,500 feet/450 meters► from the walls of the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Also measure ◄3,000 feet/900 meters► in each direction out from the walls of each city. That additional land will be land for their animals outside the walls of the cities.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The cities of safety

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:13; JOSHUA 21:3; PSALMS 9:9"> “Six of the cities that you give to the descendants of Levi will be cities to which people can run to be safe. If someone accidentally kills someone else, the one who killed that person may run to one of those cities to be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:3–42; 1 CHRONICLES 6:54–81"> You must also give to the descendants of Levi 42 other cities and the land around those cities, for their animals,.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:54; NUMBERS 33:54; JOSHUA 21:1–42"> The Israeli tribes that have the most people must give them more cities than the tribes that have fewer people give. Each tribe must give some of its cities to the descendants of Levi, but the tribes that have more land must give more cities, and the tribes that have less land will give fewer cities.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:9; DEUTERONOMY 19:1–2; LEVITICUS 14:34"> “Tell this to the Israeli people: When you cross the Jordan River and enter the Canaan region,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 21:13; JOSHUA 20:2; NUMBERS 35:6"> you must choose some cities to which people can run to be safe/protected. If someone kills another person ◄accidentally/without planning to do that►, the one who killed that person may run to one of those cities and be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 20:3–6; DEUTERONOMY 19:6; JOSHUA 20:9"> One of the relatives of the person who was killed may think he must avenge his relative’s death by killing the murderer. But in that city, the killer will be safe because the people in that city would kill those relatives if they tried to get revenge there. The man who killed someone accidentally must be put on trial in a court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> You must set apart six cities to be cities to which someone who killed another person accidentally may run and be safe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:8–10; DEUTERONOMY 4:41–43; JOSHUA 20:7–9"> There must be three of those cities on the east side of the Jordan River and three that will be on the west side, in the Canaan region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:16; GALATIANS 3:28; ROMANS 3:29"> Those six cities will be cities where Israeli people may run to and be safe, and where foreigners and other people who are living among you can also run to and be safe. Any of those people who accidentally kills someone may run to one of those cities and be safe/protected there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:17; GENESIS 9:5–6; DEUTERONOMY 19:11–13"> “But you must consider that anyone who kills another person with an iron weapon or with a big rock or with a piece of wood, is a murderer, and the one who killed the other person must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 20:3; DEUTERONOMY 19:6; DEUTERONOMY 19:12"> A relative of the person who was murdered must be the one who executes the murderer as soon as he finds him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 21:14; DEUTERONOMY 19:11; 2 SAMUEL 3:27"> If someone shoves another person over a cliff or throws something at another person</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> or hits that person with his hand/fist and causes that person to die, if he did it because he hated that person, then you must consider that he is a murderer, and must be executed. A relative of the person who was killed must be the one who executes the murderer as soon as he finds him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:11; EXODUS 21:13; JOSHUA 20:3"> “But someone might accidentally shove someone else, or accidentally throw something at another person and hit him, not because he hates that person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Or he might drop a rock on someone that he did not see. If the one who does that does not plan to hurt anyone and does not hate the person that was killed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 20:6; NUMBERS 35:12"> the people of that city must decide whether the relative of the dead person has the right to get revenge, or whether the one who killed the other person truly did it ◄accidentally/without planning to do it►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 29:7; LEVITICUS 4:3; ROMANS 3:24–26"> If they decide that the killer planned to kill the other person, they must not allow him to stay in their city. But if they decide that it was done accidentally, they must protect the killer from being killed by the dead person’s relative. They must send the killer to one of the cities where he will be safe/protected, and allow him to stay there until the Supreme Priest dies. After that, the killer may go back to his home, because the dead person’s relative no longer has the right to get revenge.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “But while the Supreme Priest is still living, the person who is in that safe city must not leave that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 22:2; DEUTERONOMY 19:10; DEUTERONOMY 19:6"> If he goes outside the city, and if a relative of the dead person finds him, that relative is permitted to kill that person, and people will not consider that the relative is guilty of murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:14; HEBREWS 10:26–30; HEBREWS 6:4–8"> The killer must stay in that city where he will be safe/protected until the Supreme Priest dies. He will be safe from revenge after that, because the death of the Supreme Priest will be considered to be a sacrifice to atone for that murder. After that, the killer may return to his home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:11; NUMBERS 27:1"> “You must always obey those regulations, wherever you live.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:28; DEUTERONOMY 19:15; MATTHEW 18:16"> “If someone is accused of killing another person, the one who is accused may be executed only if there are people who saw him do it. There must be more than one witness; no one is permitted to be executed if there was only one ◄witness/person who saw him do it►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:11–13; GENESIS 9:5–6; PSALMS 51:14"> “If there is a murderer who truly should be executed, do not spare his life by accepting ◄a ransom/money for him not to be killed►. He must be executed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ACTS 4:12; GALATIANS 3:10–13; GALATIANS 2:21"> “If someone has run to a city where he will be safe/protected, do not allow him to give you money in order that you will permit him to return to his home before the Supreme Priest dies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKE 11:50–51; PSALMS 106:38; HOSEA 4:2–3"> “You must execute people who truly murder others. If you did not do that, you would be causing the people who live in the land to become unacceptable to me. Anyone who deliberately kills an innocent person must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:3; PSALMS 135:21; REVELATION 21:27"> I am Yahweh, and I live among you Israelis, so do not spoil/pollute the land by allowing people to murder others without being punished.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
