## Rules about Nazir-men

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said this to Moses/me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 13:5; AMOS 2:11–12; ROMANS 1:1"> “Tell this to the Israeli people: ‘If any of you wants to make a solemn promise to dedicate himself to belong to me in a special way, after you obey these instructions, you will be called a Nazir-man, which means “dedicated man”.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 1:15; 1 TIMOTHY 5:23; AMOS 2:12"> You must not drink any wine or other alcoholic/fermented drink. You must not drink any vinegar made from wine or from any other alcoholic/fermented drink. You must not drink grape juice or eat grapes or raisins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:5; NUMBERS 6:21; NUMBERS 6:8–9"> You must not eat anything that comes from grapevines, not even the skins or seeds of grapes, during the time that you are a Nazir-man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 1:11; JUDGES 16:17; JUDGES 13:5"> ‘Even your hair will be dedicated to me during the time that you area Nazir-man, so you must never allow anyone to cut your hair. Until the time that your solemn promise to dedicate yourself to me is ended, you must allow your hair to grow long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:11; LEVITICUS 19:28; LEVITICUS 21:1–3"> And you must not go near a corpse during the time that you are a Nazir-man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 9:6; LEVITICUS 21:1–2; LEVITICUS 21:10–12"> Even if the person who died is your father or your mother or your brother or your sister, you must not cause yourself to become unacceptable to me by coming close to the corpse. Your long hair &#91;MTY&#93; shows that you belong to me in a special way, so you must not cut your hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:17–18"> You are required to keep doing this all the time that you are dedicated to me in this special way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:18; ACTS 18:18; LEVITICUS 14:9"> ‘If anyone dies very suddenly when he is near you, then your hair that you have dedicated to me is no longer sacred. So you must wait seven days and then shave it all off. Then you must perform a special ritual to cause yourself to become acceptable to me again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:29; LEVITICUS 1:14; LEVITICUS 15:14"> The next day you must bring two doves or two young pigeons to the priest at the entrance of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:30–31; LEVITICUS 5:8–10"> ‘The priest must kill the birds and offer them as sacrifices. One of them will be an offering to enable me to forgive you for the sins you have committed, and the other will be an offering that is burned completely to please me. After the priest burns them on the altar, I will forgive you for having come close to a corpse, and when your hair grows again it will be dedicated to me again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:6; MATTHEW 24:13; LEVITICUS 14:24"> The amount of time that you were set apart for me the previous time does not count, because you had become unacceptable to me by coming close to a corpse during the time that you were a Nazir-man. So you must again make a solemn promise to dedicate yourself to me for the entire amount of time that you indicated the previous time. And you must also sacrifice a one-year-old lamb for not doing what you were required to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ACTS 21:26"> ‘When the time that you promised to dedicate yourself to me is ended, go to the entrance of the Sacred Tent</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:6; LEVITICUS 4:32; LEVITICUS 4:2–3"> and offer as sacrifices to me three animals that have no defects: Offer a one-year-old ram that will be burned completely, a one-year-old female lamb as a sacrifice to enable me to forgive your sins, and one full-grown ram as a sacrifice to maintain fellowship with me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:4; EXODUS 29:2; NUMBERS 15:10"> ‘When you bring those animals, you must also bring some wine to offer as a sacrifice. And you must also bring a basket of bread that you have made with very good flour and olive oil. But you must not put any yeast in the bread. Also brush/spread some olive oil on some thin wafers and bring them to the priest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> ‘The priest will put the young lamb and the young ram on the altar and completely burn them, in order that I will be pleased and will forgive you for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then he will kill the full-grown ram as an offering to restore fellowship with me, and he will also burn on the altar some of the bread and the grain and wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 21:24; NUMBERS 6:9; ACTS 18:18"> ‘After that, you must stand at the entrance of the Sacred Tent and shave off your hair. Then you must put that hair in the fire that is under the animal that has been sacrificed on the altar to maintain fellowship with me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:15; LEVITICUS 7:30; EXODUS 29:23–28"> ‘The meat from the ram’s shoulder must be boiled. After it is cooked, the priest will take it along with one of the loaves of bread and one wafer which has been brushed with olive oil, and he will put them in your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:7; EXODUS 29:27–28; NUMBERS 5:25"> Then the priest will take them back and lift them up high to dedicate them to me. They now belong to the priest, and he is permitted to eat some of the meat from the ram’s shoulder and from its ribs and from one of its thighs, because that meat is his share of the sacrifice. After that, you will no longer be a Nazir-man, and you will again be permitted to drink wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:16; Ezra 2:69; NUMBERS 5:29"> ‘Those are the regulations about the offerings that Nazir-men solemnly promise to bring to me to end their time of being dedicated to me. They must bring these offerings, but if they want to, they may bring additional offerings. And they must do everything that they solemnly promised to do when they dedicated themselves to me.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The blessing for the people

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:5; 1 CHRONICLES 23:13; GENESIS 48:20"> “Tell Aaron and his sons that when they ask me to bless the people, they must say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:23; PSALMS 121:4–7; PHILIPPIANS 4:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘I desire that Yahweh will bless you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and protect you,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 80:19; PSALMS 119:135; PSALMS 31:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">and that he will smile at you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and act kindly toward you,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 4:6; PSALMS 29:11; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">and that he will be good to you &#91;IDM&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cause things to go well for you.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:14; DANIEL 9:18–19; DEUTERONOMY 28:10"> Then Yahweh said, “If Aaron and his sons ask me to bless the Israeli people, truly I will bless them.”</span>
</div>
</div>
