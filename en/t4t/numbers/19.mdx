## The ritual for removing the guilt of sin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh said to Aaron and Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:20–25; DEUTERONOMY 21:3; 1 SAMUEL 6:7"> “I am now giving to you another regulation. Tell the Israeli people to bring to you one reddish-brown cow that has no defects. It must be an animal that ◄has never been/no one has ever► used for plowing ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:12; LEVITICUS 4:21; LEVITICUS 16:27"> Give it to Eleazar, the priest. He must take it outside the camp and slaughter it and drain the blood in a basin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:17; LEVITICUS 4:6; LEVITICUS 16:14"> He must dip one of his fingers in the blood and sprinkle some of it seven times on the ground near the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 29:14; LEVITICUS 4:11–12; PSALMS 22:14"> Then, while Eleazar watches, the cow must be burned completely—its hide, its meat, the rest of its blood, and even its dung.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:4; LEVITICUS 14:49; PSALMS 51:7"> Eliezer then must take a stick of cedar wood, a stalk of a plant named hyssop, and some scarlet/red yarn, and throw them into the fire where the cow is burning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 11:25; LEVITICUS 11:40; NUMBERS 19:8"> “Then the priest must wash his clothes and bathe. After doing that, he may return to the camp. But he will be unfit for doing any sacred work until that evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The man who burns the cow must also wash his clothes and bathe, and he will also be unacceptable to me until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:13; NUMBERS 19:13; NUMBERS 19:20–21"> “Then someone who has not become unacceptable to me must gather up the ashes of the cow and put them in a ◄sacred place/place that is acceptable to me► outside the camp. The ashes must be kept there for the people of Israel to use when they mix it with water for the ritual to remove the guilt of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 12:49; NUMBERS 19:7–8; COLOSSIANS 3:11"> The man who gathers up the ashes of the cow must also wash his clothes, and he also will be unfit to do any more sacred work until that evening. That is a regulation that will never be changed. It must be obeyed by you Israeli people and by any foreigners who live among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:2; NUMBERS 31:19; LEVITICUS 21:1"> “All those who touch a corpse will be unacceptable to me for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:19; REVELATION 7:14; LEVITICUS 7:17"> On the third day and on the seventh day after touching a corpse, in order to become acceptable to me again, they must have sprinkled on them some of that water for removing the guilt of their sin. If they do not do that on both of those days, they will continue to be unacceptable to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 15:31; LEVITICUS 22:3; LEVITICUS 7:20"> All those who touch a corpse, and do not perform in the correct way the ritual to become acceptable to me again, defile Yahweh’s Sacred Tent. They will no longer be permitted to live among the Israeli people. The water to remove the guilt of sin was not sprinkled on them, so they continue to be unacceptable to me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> “There is another ritual that must be performed when someone dies inside a tent. All those who were inside that tent when that person died or who enter that tent will be unacceptable to me for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:36; LEVITICUS 11:32; NUMBERS 31:20"> Any jars that are inside that tent that are not covered are not permitted to be used.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:19; MATTHEW 23:27; LUKE 11:44"> If someone who is out in a field touches the corpse of someone who was murdered, or who died from natural causes, or if someone touches a bone from some human or touches a grave, that person will be unacceptable to me for seven days.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 7:38; GENESIS 26:19; REVELATION 7:17"> “For someone like that to become acceptable to me again, some of the ashes from the cow that was burned must be taken and put in a jar. Then some fresh water must be poured over the ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:25–27; HEBREWS 9:14; NUMBERS 19:9"> Then someone who is still acceptable to me must take a stalk of a plant named hyssop and dip it into the water. Then that person must sprinkle some of the water on the tent where that person died, on the things that are in the tent, and on the people who were in the tent. He must also sprinkle some of that water on any person who touched a human bone or who touched a person who died, or who touched a grave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 JOHN 1:7; HEBREWS 10:22; EZEKIEL 36:25"> On the third day and on the seventh day after that, the person who is acceptable to me must sprinkle some of that water on those who have become unacceptable to me. On the seventh day, the people who are performing that ritual to become acceptable to me again must wash their clothes and bathe. If they do that, on that evening they will become acceptable to me again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARK 16:16; GENESIS 17:14; NUMBERS 19:13"> “If those who have become unacceptable to me do not become acceptable to me again by doing this, they will no longer be permitted to live among the Israeli people, because they have defiled my Sacred Tent. They did not sprinkle on themselves the water that removes the guilt of their sins, so they remain unacceptable to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:13–14; HEBREWS 10:4; HEBREWS 9:10"> That is a law for the Israeli people that will never be changed. Those who sprinkle that water on themselves must then wash their clothes. And anyone who touches that water which removes guilt for sins will remain unacceptable to God until that evening.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Haggai 2:13; LEVITICUS 7:19; MATTHEW 15:19–20"> “If someone touches a thing or a person that has become unacceptable to me, that person will remain unacceptable to me until that evening.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
