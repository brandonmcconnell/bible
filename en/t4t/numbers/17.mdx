## Aaron’s walking stick budded

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 4:17; EZEKIEL 19:14; GENESIS 49:10"> “Tell the Israeli people to bring to you twelve walking sticks. They should bring one from each of the leaders of the twelve tribes. You should carve each leader’s name on his stick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 6:20; NUMBERS 18:1; NUMBERS 3:2–3"> There must be one stick for the leader of each tribe, so you must carve Aaron’s name on the stick for the tribe of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 30:36; EXODUS 29:42–43; EXODUS 25:16–22"> Put those sticks inside the Sacred Tent, in front of the sacred chest that has in it the tablets on which the Ten Commandments are written. That is the place where I always talk with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:5; NUMBERS 17:8; NUMBERS 16:11"> Buds will sprout on the stick of the man whom I have chosen to be the priest. When the people see that, they will stop their constantly complaining about you because they will realize that you are the one whom I have chosen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So Moses/I told the people what Yahweh had said. Then each of the twelve Israeli leaders, including Aaron, brought his walking stick to Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ACTS 7:44; EXODUS 38:21; NUMBERS 18:2"> Moses/I placed the sticks inside the Sacred Tent in front of the sacred chest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:24; JOHN 15:1–6; PSALMS 132:17–18"> The following morning, when he/I went into the tent, he/I saw that Aaron’s stick, which represented the tribe of Levi, had sprouted, it had produced leaves and blossoms, and it had also produced almonds that were ripe!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Moses/I brought all the sticks out of the Sacred Tent and showed them to the people. Each of the twelve leaders took back his own stick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:4; EPHESIANS 5:6; Isaiah 1:2"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Put Aaron’s stick in front of the sacred chest, and let it stay there permanently. That will be a warning to people who want to rebel against me. Then no more people will die because of complaining against me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> So Moses/I did what Yahweh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:11; Isaiah 57:16; Isaiah 6:5"> Then the Israeli people said to Moses/me, “We are going to die! All of us are surely going to die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:19–22; 2 SAMUEL 6:6–12; GENESIS 3:3"> Everyone who comes close to Yahweh’s Sacred Tent dies. Are the rest of us going to die, also?” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
