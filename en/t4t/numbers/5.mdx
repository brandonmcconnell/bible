## Expelling those who were ritually impure

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:1; LEVITICUS 13:46; NUMBERS 12:14"> “Tell this to the Israeli people: ‘You must send away from your ◄camp/area where you have your tents► any man or woman who has leprosy and anyone who has a discharge of some fluid from his body, and anyone who has become unacceptable to God because of having touched a corpse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:14; LEVITICUS 26:11–12; PSALMS 68:18"> Send them away from there in order that they will not touch people in the camp area where I live among you and cause them to become unacceptable to me.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So Moses/I told that to the Israeli people, and the Israeli people obeyed what Yahweh commanded Moses/me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paying for committing crimes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Yahweh also told Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:1–4; LEVITICUS 5:14–7"> “Tell this to the Israeli people: ‘If someone ◄commits a crime against/does something wrong to► another person, I consider that that person has done wrong to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 5:5; LEVITICUS 26:40; JOSHUA 7:19"> That person must confess that he or she is guilty, and he or she must pay to the person to whom wrong was done what others consider to be a suitable/proper payment for what he has done, and he must pay an extra 20 percent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:6–7; LEVITICUS 7:7; LEVITICUS 25:25–26"> If the person against whom the wrong was done has died and there is no relative to whom the money can be paid, then the money belongs to me, and it must be paid to the priest. In addition, the one who did the wrong must give a male sheep to the priest to sacrifice in order that ◄that person’s sin may be forgiven/I can forgive that person for his sin►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:3–4; LEVITICUS 7:6–14; LEVITICUS 6:17–18"> All the sacred offerings that the Israelis bring to the priest belong to the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:13"> The priest can keep those gifts.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Suspecting a wife of committing adultery

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Yahweh also said this to Moses/me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:19–20; PROVERBS 2:16–17"> “Tell this to the Israeli people: ‘Suppose a man thinks that his wife has slept with another man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:20; LEVITICUS 20:10; PROVERBS 7:18–19"> but neither he nor anyone else knows if it is true or not, because no one saw her doing that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 8:6; PROVERBS 6:34; ZEPHANIAH 3:8"> But if the woman’s husband is jealous, and if he suspects that she has committed adultery, and he wants to know whether that is true or not,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 29:16; 1 KINGS 17:18; LEVITICUS 5:11"> he should take his wife to the priest. He must take along as an offering two quarts/liters of barley flour. The priest must not pour olive oil or incense on it, because this is an offering that the man has brought because he ◄is jealous/wants his wife to sleep only with him►. It is an offering to find out if she is guilty or not. Neither olive oil nor incense should be used because are associated with joy and this is not a time for joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="REVELATION 2:22–23; JEREMIAH 17:10; HEBREWS 13:4"> The priest must tell the woman to stand in front of the altar in my presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 30:18; JOHN 8:6; NUMBERS 19:2–9"> He must put some sacred water in a clay jar, and then he must put some dirt from the floor of the Sacred Tent into the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:4; NUMBERS 5:22; REVELATION 10:9–10"> He must untie/unfasten the woman’s hair. Then he must put in her hands the grain that her jealous husband is offering to determine whether she has committed adultery or not. The priest must hold the bowl that contains bitter water that will cause ◄the woman to be cursed/bad things to happen to the woman► if she is guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ROMANS 7:2; NUMBERS 5:12; MATTHEW 26:63"> The priest must require her to solemnly declare that she will tell the truth. Then he must say to her, “Has another man had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with you? Have you faithfully slept only with your husband or not? If you have not slept with another man, nothing bad will happen to you if you drink the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:12"> But if you have had sex with another man, Yahweh will curse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:26; Nehemiah 10:29; 1 SAMUEL 14:24"> Your womb will shrivel up and your stomach will swell up. You will never be able to give birth to children, and as a result, everyone will curse you and avoid you. If you have committed adultery, when you drink this water, that is what will happen to you.” Then the woman must answer, “If I am guilty, I will not object if that happens.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ACTS 3:19; PSALMS 51:1; PSALMS 51:9"> ‘Then the priest must write with ink on a small scroll these ◄curses/bad things that will happen to her if she is guilty► and then wash the ink off into the bitter water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 5:3–4; MALACHI 3:5"> The priest must take from her the offering of barley flour that she is holding, and lift it up to dedicate it to me. Then he must put it on the altar</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:2; LEVITICUS 2:9; LEVITICUS 5:12"> and burn part of it as a sacrifice. Then the woman must drink the bitter water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:18; ZECHARIAH 8:13; JEREMIAH 29:18"> If the woman has committed adultery, the water will cause her to suffer greatly. Her stomach will swell up and her womb will shrink, and she will be unable to give birth to children. And then her ◄relatives/fellow Israelis► will curse her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MICAH 7:7–10; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:17; PSALMS 113:9"> But if she ◄is innocent/has not committed adultery►, her body will not be harmed, and she will still be able to give birth to children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:19; NUMBERS 5:12; NUMBERS 5:15"> ‘That is the ritual that must be performed when a woman who is married has been unfaithful to her husband,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> or when a man is jealous and suspects that his wife has had sex with another man. The priest must tell that woman to stand at the altar in my presence and obey these instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:10; ROMANS 2:8–9; EZEKIEL 18:4"> Even if the woman has not done what the husband suspected, he will not be punished &#91;MTY&#93; for doing something wrong by bringing his wife to the priest. But if his wife is guilty, she will suffer as a result.’”</span>
</div>
