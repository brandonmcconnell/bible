## Aaron set up the lampstand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 25:37; EXODUS 40:25; LEVITICUS 24:1–2"> “Tell Aaron to put the seven lamps on the lampstand and place them in such a way that they shine toward the front of the lampstand.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So Moses/I told him what Yahweh said, and he did that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 25:31–40; EXODUS 25:9; EXODUS 25:18"> The lampstand had been made from gold that had been hammered from one large lump of gold, from its base to the decorations at the top that resembled flowers. The lampstand was made exactly like Yahweh had told Moses/me that it should be made.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for dedicating the descendants of Levi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 7:1; JAMES 4:8; EXODUS 19:15"> “You must cause the descendants of Levi to be acceptable to me by setting them apart from the other Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:13; NUMBERS 19:7–10; LEVITICUS 8:6"> Do that by sprinkling them with water which will symbolize their being freed/cleansed from the guilt of their sins. Then they must shave off all the hair on their bodies and wash their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:1; EXODUS 29:1; LEVITICUS 16:3"> Then they must bring to the Sacred Tent one bull and some grain mixed with olive oil. Those things will be burned as sacrifices. They must also bring another bull that will enable me to forgive them for the sins they have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 40:12; LEVITICUS 8:3; EXODUS 29:4–37"> Then you must summon all the Israeli people to come together in front of the Sacred Tent, to gather around the descendants of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 1:4; ACTS 6:6; 1 TIMOTHY 4:14"> Then the Israeli people must lay their hands on the descendants of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 7:30; LEVITICUS 8:29; NUMBERS 1:49–53"> Aaron must then present them to me to be a gift from the Israeli people, in order that they can work for me at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:4–10; EXODUS 29:10; LEVITICUS 1:4"> “After that, the descendants of Levi must place their hands on the heads of the two bulls. Then the bulls will be killed and burned on the altar. One will be an offering to enable me to forgive them for the sins they have committed, and the other will be completely burned to please me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 8:21; NUMBERS 8:11; ROMANS 15:16"> The descendants of Levi must stand at the altar in front of Aaron and his sons, and you must then dedicate to me the descendants of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:45; MALACHI 3:17; NUMBERS 18:6"> This ritual will show that the descendants of Levi are set apart from the other Israelis and that they belong to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 8:11; NUMBERS 8:13; NUMBERS 3:23–37"> “After the descendants of Levi have been made acceptable to me, and presented to me like a special offering, they may start to work at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:12; NUMBERS 3:45; NUMBERS 3:9"> They will belong to me. They will work for me as substitutes for the firstborn males of all the Israelis, who also belong to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 13:2; LUKE 2:23; EXODUS 13:12–15"> All the firstborn males in Israel, both the people and the animals, are mine. When I caused all the firstborn sons of the people of Egypt to die, I ◄spared/did not kill► the firstborn males of the Israelis. I set them apart to belong to me. I said that the firstborn males of all their domestic animals would also belong to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But now I have chosen the descendants of Levi to take the places of the firstborn males of other Israelis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:53; NUMBERS 16:46; 2 CHRONICLES 26:16–20"> I have appointed the descendants of Levi to help Aaron and his sons at the Sacred Tent, as Aaron and his sons offer the sacrifices so that the Israeli people’s sins will be forgiven, and to prevent the Israelis from coming close to the tent with the result that a plague would cause many of them to become sick and die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Aaron and Moses/I and the other Israelis helped the descendants of Levi to do everything that Yahweh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 8:7; NUMBERS 19:19; NUMBERS 8:15"> The descendants of Levi sprinkled themselves with water to symbolize that they had been freed/cleansed from the guilt of their sins, and they washed their clothes. Then Aaron brought them to the altar to present them to Yahweh, and he offered sacrifices to cleanse them from the guilt of their sins and cause them to become acceptable to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 31:2; 2 CHRONICLES 35:8–15; NUMBERS 8:15"> After that, the descendants of Levi started to work at the Sacred Tent to assist Aaron and his sons. They did that just as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Yahweh also said this to Moses/me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:3; 1 CHRONICLES 23:3; NUMBERS 4:23"> “The descendants of Levi who are between 25 and 50 years old will work at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:23; 2 TIMOTHY 4:7"> But after they become 50 years old, they must ◄retire/not do that work any more►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:53; NUMBERS 3:32; 1 TIMOTHY 4:15"> They may help the other descendants of Levi to do their work at the Sacred Tent, but they must not do the work themselves. That is what you must tell them about the work they will do.”</span>
</div>
</div>
