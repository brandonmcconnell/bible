## The land east of the Jordan River

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:32; NUMBERS 32:3; GENESIS 13:10–11"> The people of the tribes of Reuben and Gad had much livestock. They saw that the land near Jazer city and the Gilead region east of the Jordan River had good grass for the animals to graze on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> So their leaders came to Eleazar and the other leaders of the people and Moses/me. They said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:34; Isaiah 16:8–9; NUMBERS 32:34–38"> “We have a great amount of livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:34; DEUTERONOMY 2:24–35; NUMBERS 21:24"> Yahweh has enabled us Israelis to capture some land that is very good for animals to graze on—the land near Ataroth, Dibon, Jazer, Nimrah, Heshbon, Elealeh, Sebam, Nebo, and Beon towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="RUTH 2:10; JOSHUA 7:7; JEREMIAH 31:2"> If it pleases you, we would like this land to be ours, instead of land on the other side of the Jordan River.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 13:5; 2 SAMUEL 11:11; PHILIPPIANS 2:4"> Moses/I replied to the leaders of the tribes of Gad and Reuben, “It is not right for &#91;RHQ&#93; your fellow Israelis to go to fight in wars and you stay here!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:27–4; NUMBERS 32:9; DEUTERONOMY 1:28"> If you do that, you will cause the other Israelis to be discouraged, with the result that they will not cross the Jordan River to the land that Yahweh is giving to them &#91;RHQ&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:19–25; NUMBERS 14:2; NUMBERS 13:2–26"> Your ancestors did the same kind of thing. I sent them from Kadesh-Barnea to see what the land of Canaan was like.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:23–10; DEUTERONOMY 1:24–28"> They went as far as Eshcol Valley, but when they saw the huge people in the land, they returned and caused the Israeli people to be discouraged, saying ‘We should not try to enter the land that Yahweh said that he is giving to us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:11; PSALMS 95:11; EZEKIEL 20:15"> So Yahweh became very angry with them, and he solemnly declared this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:28–30; NUMBERS 14:24; NUMBERS 26:2"> ‘From all the people who came out of Egypt, the only ones who are at least 20 years old who will see the land that I promised to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, are Jephunneh’s son Caleb and Nun’s son Joshua, because they trusted me completely. None of the other people who came out of Egypt will even see that land, because they have not completely believed in my power.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:5; NUMBERS 14:33–35; HEBREWS 3:16–19"> So Yahweh was angry with the Israeli people, and as a result he has caused us to wander in this desert for 40 years. Finally, all the people who had sinned against Yahweh by refusing to trust him died, one by one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 11:48; PSALMS 78:57; MATTHEW 23:31–33"> And you are acting like your ancestors did! You sinful Israeli people are going to cause Yahweh to be more angry with you than he was with our ancestors</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 8:11–12; 2 CHRONICLES 15:2; 2 CHRONICLES 7:19–22"> If you stop trusting him, he will cause you and all your fellow Israelis to stay longer in the desert, and he will get rid of all of you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 33:17; NUMBERS 34:22"> Then the leaders of the tribes of Reuben and Gad said to Moses/me, “First we will build pens for our animals and build cities for our families here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOSHUA 4:12–13; NUMBERS 32:29–32; DEUTERONOMY 3:18–20"> Then our families will live in strong cities with walls around them, and they will be safe from the people who live in this land. Then we will get ready to fight battles. We will help the other Israelis to get land on the other side of the river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 22:1–5"> We will not return to our homes until every Israeli has received some land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:8; JOSHUA 12:1–6; NUMBERS 34:15"> We will not take any land on the west side of the Jordan River; our land will be here on the east side.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOSHUA 4:12–13; DEUTERONOMY 3:18–20; JOSHUA 1:13–15"> So Moses/I told them, “Okay, I will tell you what you all must do. You must get ready to fight battles for Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> You all must cross the Jordan River carrying your weapons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOSHUA 22:9; JOSHUA 22:4; DEUTERONOMY 3:20"> After Yahweh helps us to take that land from the people who live there, you will be permitted to return to your homes. You will have done what you have promised Yahweh and the Israeli people that you would do, and you may keep this land to be your own, given to you by Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:12; PROVERBS 13:21; GENESIS 44:16"> “But if you do not do these things, you will be sinning against Yahweh, and he will punish you for that sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:16; NUMBERS 30:2; NUMBERS 32:34–42"> Now you can build cities for your families and pens for your animals, but after doing that, you must do what you have promised.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:13–14"> The leaders of the tribes of Gad and Reuben replied, “We will do what you have asked us to do &#91;MTY&#93;, because you are our leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:14"> Our wives and children and our cattle and sheep and goats will stay here in the cities in the Gilead area,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOSHUA 4:12; NUMBERS 32:17; NUMBERS 12:11"> but we will prepare to go to battle. We will take our weapons and go across the Jordan River and fight for Yahweh, just as you, our leader, have said.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:13"> So Moses/I gave instructions about them to Eleazar, Joshua, and the leaders of the Israeli tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Moses/I said to them, “If the men from the tribes of Gad and Reuben prepare for battle and cross the Jordan River with you, in order to do what Yahweh desires and help you to take that land, give them the Gilead area to belong to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOSHUA 22:19"> But if they do not take their weapons and go with you prepared to fight, they will not receive this land. They will need to accept some land in Canaan, like the rest of you will do.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> The leaders of the tribes of Gad and Reuben replied, “We will do what you have said and what Yahweh has said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We will cross the river into Canaan land, and we will do what Yahweh desires and be prepared for battle. But our land will be here on the east side of the Jordan River.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOSHUA 12:6; DEUTERONOMY 29:8; DEUTERONOMY 3:12–17"> So Moses/I agreed to give that land to the tribes of Gad and Reuben and to half of the tribe of Joseph’s son Manasseh. That land was previously the land where Sihon, the king of the Amor people-group, ruled, and the land where Og, the king of Bashan region, ruled, including its cities and surrounding land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:36; NUMBERS 32:3; Isaiah 17:2"> The people of the tribe of Gad rebuilt Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:1; NUMBERS 32:3"> Atroth-Shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:3"> Beth-Nimrah, and Beth-Haran cities. Those were cities with strong walls around them. And they also built pens for their sheep.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:27; NUMBERS 32:3; Isaiah 15:4"> The people of the tribe of Reuben rebuilt Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; NUMBERS 32:3; JOSHUA 23:7"> Nebo, Baal-Meon, and Sibmah cities. When they rebuilt Nebo and Baal-Meon, they gave new names to those cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GENESIS 50:23; JOSHUA 17:1; NUMBERS 26:29"> The descendants of Manasseh’s son Makir went to the Gilead region and compelled the people of the Amor people-group to leave that area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:1; JOSHUA 13:29–31; DEUTERONOMY 3:13–15"> So Moses/I gave Gilead to the family of Makir, and they started to live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:14; JUDGES 10:4; JOSHUA 13:30"> Jair, who was also a descendant of Manasseh, went and captured the small towns in that region, and he named them the Towns of Jair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 49:11; 2 SAMUEL 18:18"> A man named Nobah went and captured Kenath city and the nearby towns, and then he put his own name to be the new name of that area.</span>
</div>
</div>
