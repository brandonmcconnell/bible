## Miriam and Aaron were jealous of Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 2:21; GENESIS 26:34–35; GENESIS 24:37"> Moses’/My older sister Miriam and his/my older brother Aaron were saying this: “Is Moses the only one to whom Yahweh has spoken messages to tell to us &#91;RHQ&#93;? Does Yahweh not speak messages to us two also?” They told people that they were saying that because Moses/I had married a woman who was a descendant of the Cush people-group, but they were really saying that because they ◄were jealous/did not want Moses/me to be the only leader of the Israeli people►. But Yahweh heard what they were saying.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:25; EXODUS 34:5; PSALMS 99:7"> Then Yahweh descended to the entrance of the tent in a cloud that resembled a huge white pillar. He told Aaron and Miriam to step forward, so they did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 3:5; GENESIS 31:10–11; GENESIS 46:2"> Then he said to them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Listen to me!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When a prophet is among you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I usually reveal myself to him by allowing him to see visions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I speak to him in dreams.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 34:10; 1 CORINTHIANS 13:12; EXODUS 33:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I talk to him face-to-face.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I speak to him ◄clearly/using words that he will understand easily►, not using parables.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He has even seen what I look like.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So you should be afraid to criticize my servant Moses!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 17:22; HOSEA 5:15; NUMBERS 11:1"> Yahweh was very angry with Miriam and Aaron, and he left.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:9; 2 KINGS 5:27; EXODUS 4:6"> When the cloud rose up from the Sacred Tent, Aaron looked at Miriam, and he saw that her skin was as white as snow, because she now had leprosy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 19:19; 2 SAMUEL 24:10; PROVERBS 30:32"> Aaron said to Moses/me, “My master, please do not punish us for this sin that we have foolishly committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 88:4–5; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:8; 1 TIMOTHY 5:6"> Do not allow Miriam to be like a baby that is already dead when it is born, whose flesh is already half decayed!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 12:21; LUKE 6:28; JAMES 5:15"> So Moses/I cried out to Yahweh, saying, “God, I plead with you to heal her!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:9; JOB 30:10; Isaiah 50:6"> But Yahweh replied, “If her father had rebuked her for doing something wrong by spitting in her face, she would have been ashamed for seven days. <span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:8–9; MICAH 7:8–9; MICAH 6:4"> So they sent her outside the camp for seven days. ◄The people/We► did not move to another location until she returned.</span>
