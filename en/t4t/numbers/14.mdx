## The Israeli people continued to complain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:45; NUMBERS 11:1–4"> That night, all the Israeli people cried loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 15:24; EXODUS 17:3; NUMBERS 16:41"> The next day they all complained to Aaron and Moses/me. All the men said, “We wish that we had died in Egypt, or in this desert!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:31–32; JEREMIAH 9:3; PSALMS 78:40"> Why is Yahweh bringing us to this land, where we men will be killed with swords? And our wives and children will be taken away to be slaves. Instead of going to Canaan. It would be better for us to return to Egypt!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ACTS 7:39; Nehemiah 9:16–17; DEUTERONOMY 17:16"> Then some of them said to each other, “We should choose a leader who will take us back to Egypt!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:4; NUMBERS 16:22; NUMBERS 16:45"> Then Aaron and Moses/I bowed down to pray in front of all the Israeli people who had gathered there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:30; NUMBERS 13:30; NUMBERS 14:38"> Joshua and Caleb, two of the men who had explored the land, tore their clothes because they were very dismayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:27; DEUTERONOMY 1:25; DEUTERONOMY 8:7–9"> They said to the Israeli people, “The land that we explored is very good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 22:20; Isaiah 62:4; NUMBERS 13:27"> If Yahweh is pleased with us, he will lead us into that very fertile &#91;IDM&#93; land, and he will give it to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:18; ROMANS 8:31; DEUTERONOMY 31:6"> So do not rebel against Yahweh! And do not be afraid of the people in that land! We will ◄gobble them up/completely destroy them► &#91;MET&#93;! They do not have anyone who will protect them, but Yahweh will be with us and help us. So do not be afraid of them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 17:4; 1 SAMUEL 30:6; LEVITICUS 9:23"> Then all the Israeli people talked about killing Caleb and Joshua by throwing stones at them. But suddenly Yahweh’s glory appeared to them at the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 78:32; PSALMS 106:24; PSALMS 78:22"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me, “How long will these people reject me &#91;RHQ&#93;? I am tired of them not believing in what I can do, in spite of all the miracles I have performed among them &#91;RHQ&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 32:10; NUMBERS 25:9; 2 SAMUEL 24:12–15"> So I will cause a ◄plague/widespread sickness► to strike them and get rid of them. But I will cause your descendants to become a great nation. They will be a nation that is much greater and stronger than these people are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 106:23; EZEKIEL 20:9; DEUTERONOMY 32:27"> But Moses/I replied to Yahweh, “Please do not do that, because the people of Egypt will hear about it! You brought these Israeli people from Egypt by your great power,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 15:14; EXODUS 33:11; DEUTERONOMY 5:4"> and the people of Egypt will tell that to the descendants of Canaan who live in this land. Yahweh, they have already heard about you. They know that you have ◄been with/helped► these people and that they have seen you face-to-face. They have heard that your cloud is like a huge pillar that stays over them, and by using that cloud you lead them during the day, and the cloud becomes like a fire at night to give them light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 6:16"> If you kill these people all at one time, the people-groups who have heard about your power will say,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:28; JOSHUA 7:9; JOSHUA 7:7"> ‘Yahweh was not able to bring them into the land that he promised to give to them, so he killed them in the desert.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:6; MATTHEW 9:8; MICAH 3:8"> “So Yahweh, now show that you are very powerful. You said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 34:6–7; PSALMS 145:8; EXODUS 20:5"> ‘I do not quickly become angry; instead, I love people very much, and I forgive people for having sinned and having disobeyed my laws. But I will always punish &#91;LIT&#93; people who are guilty of doing what is wrong. When parents sin, I will punish them, but I will also punish their children and their grandchildren and their great-grandchildren and their great-great-grandchildren.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 34:9; PSALMS 78:38; PSALMS 106:45"> So, because you love people like that, forgive these people for the sins that they have committed, just like you have continued to forgive them ever since they left Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MICAH 7:18–20; JAMES 5:16; PSALMS 106:23"> Then Yahweh replied, “I have forgiven them, as you requested me to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 72:19; HABAKKUK 2:14; Isaiah 6:3"> But, just as certainly as I live and that people all over the world can see my ◄glory/glorious power►, I solemnly declare this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 19:3; GENESIS 31:7; EXODUS 32:1"> All these people saw my glory and all the miracles that I performed in Egypt and in the desert, but they disobeyed me, and many times they tested whether they could continue to do evil things without my punishing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:11; NUMBERS 26:64; EZEKIEL 20:15"> Because of that, not one of them will see the land that I promised their ancestors that I would give to them. No one who rejected me will see that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:6–9; JOSHUA 14:6–14; EPHESIANS 6:6"> But Caleb, who serves me well, is different from the others. He obeys me completely. So I will bring him into that land that he has already seen, and his descendants will inherit/possess some of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:40; NUMBERS 13:29; PSALMS 81:11–13"> So, since the descendants of Amalek and Canaan who are living in the valleys in Canaan are very strong, when you leave here tomorrow, instead of traveling toward Canaan, go back along the road through the desert towards the Red Sea.”</span>
</div>

## Yahweh punished the Israelis

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then Yahweh said to Aaron and Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 16:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:10; EXODUS 16:28"> “How long will the wicked people of this nation keep complaining about me &#91;RHQ&#93;? I have heard everything that they have grumbled/complained against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:21; HEBREWS 3:17; NUMBERS 14:2"> So now tell them this: ‘Just as certainly as I, Yahweh, live, I will do exactly what you said would happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:45; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:5; HEBREWS 3:17"> I will cause all of you to die here in this desert! Because you grumbled about me, none of you who are more than 20 years old and who were counted when Moses counted everyone</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:65; NUMBERS 14:38; DEUTERONOMY 1:36–38"> will enter the land that I solemnly promised to give to you. Only Caleb and Joshua will enter that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:39; PSALMS 106:24; NUMBERS 26:64"> You said that your children would be taken from you to become slaves, but I will take them into the land, and they will enjoy living in the land that you ◄rejected/said that you could not conquer►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:5; NUMBERS 14:29; HEBREWS 3:17"> But as for you adults, you will die here in this desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:35; NUMBERS 32:13; DEUTERONOMY 2:14"> And like shepherds wander around in the desert as they take care of their sheep, your children will wander around in this desert for 40 years. Because you adults were not loyal/faithful to me, your children will suffer until you all die in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:6; NUMBERS 13:25; JEREMIAH 18:9–10"> You will suffer for your sins for 40 years. That will be one year for each of the 40 days that the twelve men explored Canaan land. And I will be like an enemy to you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:19; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:5; HEBREWS 3:19"> This will certainly happen because I, Yahweh, have said it! I will do these things to every one in this group who conspired against me. They ◄conspired against/decided together to reject► me here in the desert, and they will all die right here in this desert!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:31–33; NUMBERS 13:4–16"> Then the ten men who had explored Canaan and who had urged the people to rebel against Yahweh by giving reports that discouraged the people</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:10; NUMBERS 16:49; HEBREWS 3:17"> were immediately struck with a plague/disease that Yahweh sent, and they died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOSHUA 14:6–10; NUMBERS 26:65"> Of the twelve men who had explored Canaan, only Joshua and Caleb remained alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="EXODUS 33:4; MATTHEW 8:12; HEBREWS 12:17"> When Moses/I reported to the Israeli people what Yahweh had said, many of them were very sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:41; MATTHEW 7:21–23; LUKE 13:25"> So the people got up early the next morning and started to go toward the hilly area in Canaan. They said, “We know that we have sinned, but now we are ready to enter the land that Yahweh promised to give to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 24:20; JOB 4:9; JEREMIAH 2:37"> But Moses/I said, “Yahweh commanded you to return to the desert, so why are you now disobeying him &#91;RHQ&#93;? It will not ◄succeed/be possible►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:42; JOSHUA 7:8; JOSHUA 7:12"> Do not try to enter the land now! If you try, your enemies will defeat you, because Yahweh will not ◄be with/help► you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:29; NUMBERS 14:25; 2 CHRONICLES 15:2"> When you begin to fight the descendants of Amalek and Canaan, they will slaughter you! Yahweh will abandon you, because you have abandoned him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:43; 1 SAMUEL 4:3–11; NUMBERS 31:6"> But even though Moses/I did not leave the camp, and the sacred chest that contained the Ten Commandments was not taken from the camp, the people began to go towards the hilly area in Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:3; JUDGES 1:17; DEUTERONOMY 1:44"> Then the descendants of Amalek and Canaan who lived in that hilly area came down and attacked them and chased them as far south as the town of Hormah.</span>
