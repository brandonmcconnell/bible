## Instructions about offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Yahweh told Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:10; NUMBERS 15:18; LEVITICUS 25:2"> “Tell this to the Israeli people: When you arrive in the land that I am giving to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 29:18; GENESIS 8:21; PHILIPPIANS 4:18"> you must offer to me special sacrifices which will be pleasing to me when the priest burns them on the altar. Some of them may be offerings that will be completely burned on the altar. Some of them may be to indicate that you have made a solemn promise to me. Some of them may be offerings that you yourselves have decided to make. Some of them may be offerings at one of the festivals that you celebrate each year. These offerings may be taken from your herds of cattle or from your flocks of sheep and goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 29:40; LEVITICUS 23:13; LEVITICUS 6:14"> When you give these offerings, you must also bring to me a grain offering of two quarts/liters of nice flour mixed with one quart/liter of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:7; NUMBERS 28:14; JUDGES 9:13"> When you offer a lamb to be a sacrifice to be completely burned, you must also pour on the altar one quart/liter of wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:12–14; NUMBERS 15:4"> ‘When you offer a ram to be a sacrifice, you must also bring an offering of four quarts/liters of finely-ground flour mixed with ◄a third of a gallon/1.3 liters► of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And also pour on the altar ◄a third of a gallon/1.3 liters► of wine. While they are being burned, the smell will be very pleasing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 3:1; LEVITICUS 7:11–18; LEVITICUS 1:3"> ‘Sometimes you will offer a young bull to be completely burned on the altar. Sometimes you will offer a sacrifice to indicate that you have made a solemn promise to me. Sometimes you will offer a sacrifice to maintain fellowship with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:14; NUMBERS 28:12; LEVITICUS 6:14"> When you offer these sacrifices, you must also offer a grain offering of six quarts/liters of finely-ground flour mixed with two quarts/liters of olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:5; NUMBERS 6:15"> Also pour on the altar two quarts/liters of wine to be an offering. While those special gifts are being burned, the smell will be very pleasing to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Each time someone offers a bull or a ram or a male lamb or a young goat to be a sacrifice, it must be done that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> You must obey these instructions for each animal that you bring to me for an offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> ‘All of you people who have been Israelis all of your lives must obey these regulations when you offer sacrifices that will be pleasing to me when they are burned on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> If any foreigners visit you or live among you, if they also want to bring a sacrifice that will be pleasing to me when it is burned on the altar, they must obey these same instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 9:14; NUMBERS 15:29; EXODUS 12:49"> I consider that those who have always been Israelis and those who are foreigners are equal, and so they must all obey the same instructions. All of your descendants must also continue to obey these instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> You Israelis and the foreigners who live among you must all obey the same instructions.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:2; DEUTERONOMY 26:1–15"> “Tell these instructions to the Israeli people: Yahweh says this: ‘When you arrive in the land to which I am taking you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 5:11–12"> and you eat the crops that are growing there, you must set some of them aside to be a sacred offering to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:14; EXODUS 34:26; DEUTERONOMY 26:2–10"> Each year set aside some of the first grain that you gather after you have threshed it. Bake a loaf of bread from the first flour that you grind and bring it to me to be a sacred offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 29:28; NUMBERS 18:26"> Every year, you and your descendants must continue to make and bring to me a loaf of bread baked &#91;MTY&#93; with flour from the first part of the grain that you harvest.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## When people sin unintentionally or deliberately

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:2; LEVITICUS 4:27; LEVITICUS 5:15–17"> “‘There may be times when you Israelis do not obey all these instructions that I have given to Moses to tell you, but not because you intended to disobey them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> There may be times when some of your descendants do not obey all these instructions that I have given to Moses to tell to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:23; LEVITICUS 4:13–21; LEVITICUS 5:15"> If you or they sin by forgetting to obey these instructions and none of the Israeli people realize that they were doing that, one young bull as an offering for all the people must be brought to the priest. That will be pleasing to me when it is burned on the altar. They must also bring to me a grain offering and an offering of wine, and a male goat, to be sacrificed to enable me to forgive them for the sins they have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:20; ROMANS 3:25; HEBREWS 2:17"> By offering these sacrifices, the priest will make atonement for all of you Israeli people. Then, as a result of their bringing to me an offering to be burned on the altar, ◄you will be forgiven/I will forgive you►, because you sinned without realizing that you were sinning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:24"> You Israeli people and the foreigners who are living among you will all be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:27–28; ACTS 17:30; ACTS 3:17"> If one person commits a sin without realizing that he was sinning, that person must bring to me a female goat to be an offering to enable me to forgive that person for the sins that person has committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:35"> The priest will offer it to be a sacrifice to remove the guilt of that person, and that person will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:15; LEVITICUS 16:29; LEVITICUS 17:15"> You Israelis and all the foreigners who live among you must obey these same instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:26; PSALMS 19:13; DEUTERONOMY 1:43"> But those who disobey my commands ◄deliberately/because they want to►, both Israelis and the foreigners who live among you, have sinned against me by doing that. So ◄they must be expelled/you must expel them► from your camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:9; PROVERBS 13:13; EZEKIEL 18:20"> They have despised my commands and deliberately disobeyed them, so they must be punished for their sin by not being allowed to live among you any more.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A man is punished for working on the Sabbath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 35:2–3; EXODUS 31:14–15; EXODUS 20:8–10"> One day, while the Israelis were in the desert, some of them saw a man who was gathering firewood on the Sabbath/rest day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOHN 8:3–20"> Those who saw him doing that brought him to Aaron and Moses/me and the rest of the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:12; NUMBERS 9:8"> They guarded him carefully, because they did not know what to do to punish him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="ACTS 7:58; EXODUS 31:14–15; 1 KINGS 21:13"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me, “The man must be executed. All of you must kill him by throwing stones at him outside the camp.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:25"> So they all took the man outside the camp and killed him by throwing stones at him, as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me that they should do.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Putting tassels on clothing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:5; DEUTERONOMY 22:12; MATTHEW 9:20"> “Tell this to the Israeli people: You and all your descendants must twist threads together to make tassels, and then attach them with blue cords to the bottom edges of your clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 106:39; PROVERBS 28:26; PSALMS 73:27"> When you look at the tassels, you will remember all the instructions that I gave to you, and you will obey them, instead of doing what you desire and as a result causing yourselves to become unacceptable to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="ROMANS 12:1; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:7; LEVITICUS 11:44–45"> Seeing those tassels will help you to remember that you must obey all my commands and that you must be ◄my holy people/dedicated to me►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 PETER 2:9–10; EZEKIEL 36:25–27; HEBREWS 11:16"> Do not forget that I am Yahweh, your God. I am the one who brought you out of Egypt in order that you might belong to me. I am Yahweh, your God.”</span>
</div>
