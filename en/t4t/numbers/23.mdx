<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 46:6; NUMBERS 23:29; Isaiah 1:10–11"> So Balak took Balaam to a field on the top of Pisgah Mountain. There, again he built seven altars and offered a young bull and a ram as a sacrifice on each altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:3; NUMBERS 22:8"> Then Balaam said to the king, “Stand here close to your burned offerings, while I go and talk with Yahweh.”</span>
