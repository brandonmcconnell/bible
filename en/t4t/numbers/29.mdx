## The sacrifices for the Festival of Trumpets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:24–25; 1 CHRONICLES 15:28; Isaiah 27:13"> “Each year, gather together to worship me on the first day of the seventh month, and do not do any work on that day. On that day the priests must blow their trumpets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:27; NUMBERS 28:19; NUMBERS 29:36"> When the offerings on the altar on that day are being completely burned, the smell will be very pleasing to me. The animals that you must bring are one young bull, one male sheep, and seven male lambs that are one-year-old. They must have no defects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> With these animals bring an offering of grain consisting of finely-ground flour mixed with olive oil. With the bull, bring six quarts/liters. With the male sheep, bring four quarts/liters,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and with each of the seven lambs bring two quarts/liters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:30; NUMBERS 28:15; NUMBERS 28:22"> Also offer one male goat in order that you will be forgiven for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:3–8; NUMBERS 28:11–15; NUMBERS 29:18"> All of those animals are in addition to the animals that are completely burned on the altar each morning and on the first day of each month. The offerings of grain and wine must be made exactly as I have told you to do. When these offerings are burned, the smell will be very pleasing to me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The day of Atonement

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:4; LEVITICUS 16:29–31; ZECHARIAH 12:10"> “Each year, on the tenth day of the seventh month, you must gather together to worship me. Do not eat any food or do any work on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:19; NUMBERS 29:13; NUMBERS 29:2"> When you burn offerings on the altar on that day, the smell will be very pleasing to me. The animals that you must bring are one young bull, one male sheep, and seven male lambs that are one-year-old. They must have no defects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:3–12"> With the bull, bring an offering of grain consisting of six quarts/liters of nice flour mixed with olive oil. With the male sheep, bring four quarts/liters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> With each of the lambs, bring two quarts/liters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:3; LEVITICUS 16:5; NUMBERS 28:3–8"> Also bring one male goat to enable me to forgive you for the sins you have committed, and the animals and grain and wine that you burn on the altar each day to enable me to forgive you for the sins you have committed. Those offerings will be in addition to the animals and grain and wine that are completely burned on the altar each day.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Festival of Shelters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:13–14; ZECHARIAH 14:16–19; EXODUS 34:22"> “On the fifteenth day of the seventh month of each year, you must all gather together to worship me. You must not do any work on that day. You must continue to celebrate for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 28:11; Ezra 3:4; NUMBERS 29:2"> When the offerings are burned on the altar, the smell will be very pleasing to me. The animals that you must bring are 13 young bulls, two male sheep, and 14 male lambs that are one-year-old. These animals must have no defects.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> With each of the 13 bulls, bring a grain offering of six quarts/liters of finely-ground flour mixed with olive oil. With each of the male sheep, bring four quarts/liters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> With each of the 14 lambs, bring two quarts/liters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Also bring one male goat to be sacrificed to enable me to forgive you for your sins, in addition to the offerings of animals and grain and wine that are completely burned on the altar each day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:13; PSALMS 50:8–9; PSALMS 69:31"> “For the next six days of the festival, you must also bring animals to sacrifice. On the second day, bring twelve bulls. On the third/next day, bring eleven bulls. One the fourth/next day, bring ten bulls. On the fifth/next day, bring nine bulls. On the sixth/next day, bring eight bulls. On the seventh/last day, bring seven bulls. But on each of those days, all the other sacrifices and offerings that you bring will remain the same.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:36; JOHN 7:37–39; REVELATION 7:9–17"> “On the eighth day of the festival, you must again gather together to worship me, and do not do any work on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> On that day, you must bring to the altar one bull, one full-grown ram, and seven lambs that are one-year-old. These animals must have no defects. They must all be burned on the altar, and their smell will please me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Bring also with the bull and the male sheep and each of the lambs the offerings of grain and wine that ◄are required/I told you to bring►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Also offer one male goat to be sacrificed to enable me to forgive you for your sins. These animals will be in addition to the offerings of animals and grain and wine that are completely burned on the altar each day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 31:3; LEVITICUS 23:2; 1 CHRONICLES 23:31"> “At your festivals, these are the offerings that you must bring to me: The offerings that will be completely burned on the altar, the offerings of grain and wine, and the offerings to maintain fellowship with me. Those are in addition to the offerings that you give to me because you promised to do something, and other special offerings that you want to give to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="ACTS 20:27; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:3; MATTHEW 28:20"> Then Moses/I told to the Israeli people all the things that Yahweh had commanded him/me.</span>
</div>
