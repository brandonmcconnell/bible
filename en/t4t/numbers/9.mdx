## They celebrated Passover again

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:1; EXODUS 40:2"> One year after the Israelis left Egypt, during the month of March, while they were in the Sinai Desert, Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:1–2; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:7–8; 2 CHRONICLES 35:1"> “Tell the Israeli people that they must celebrate the Passover Festival again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 12:6–11; LEVITICUS 23:5; 2 CHRONICLES 30:2"> They must do it on the fourteenth day of this month, early in the evening, and they must obey all the instructions about it that I gave you previously.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So Moses/I told the people what Yahweh had said about celebrating the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 5:10; MATTHEW 28:20; EXODUS 12:1–13"> The people celebrated it, there in the Sinai Desert, in the evening of the fourteenth day of the month, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:2; EXODUS 18:15; NUMBERS 5:2"> But some of the Israeli people had touched a corpse, and as a result they had become unfit to celebrate the Passover. So they asked Aaron and Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:7–8; EXODUS 12:27"> “It is true that we have touched a corpse. But why should that prevent us from celebrating the Passover Festival and offering sacrifices to Yahweh like everyone else &#91;RHQ&#93;?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 85:8; NUMBERS 27:5; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:4"> Moses/I replied, “Wait here until I go into the Sacred Tent and find out what Yahweh says about it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So Moses/I went into the tent and asked Yahweh what he/I should tell the people, and this is what Yahweh said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:1–2; EPHESIANS 2:12–13; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:28"> “Tell this to the Israeli people: If any of you or your descendants touch a corpse and as a result become unacceptable to me, or if you are away from home on a long trip at the time to celebrate the Passover, you will still be permitted to celebrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 30:2–15; EXODUS 12:43–49; JOHN 19:36"> But you must celebrate it exactly one month later, early in the evening of the fourteenth day of that month. Eat the meat of the lamb for the Passover Festival with bread that is baked without yeast and eat bitter herbs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOHN 19:36; EXODUS 12:46; EXODUS 12:10"> Do not leave any of it until the next morning. And do not break any of the lamb’s bones. Obey all the regulations about celebrating the Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 17:14; EXODUS 12:15; NUMBERS 9:7"> But if any of you has not done anything that would make you unfit to celebrate the Passover Festival, and you are not away from home on a long trip, and you do not sacrifice to me at the proper time, you will no longer be allowed to associate with my people. ◄You will be punished/I will punish you►.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 12:48–49; LEVITICUS 24:22; Isaiah 56:3–7"> Foreigners who live among you may also celebrate the Passover Festival, if they obey all my commands concerning it.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A cloud covered the Sacred Tent

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 13:21; EXODUS 40:34; Nehemiah 9:19"> On the day that the Sacred Tent was set up, a cloud covered it. From the time that the sun set until the time that the sun rose the next day, the cloud resembled a huge fire. And that is what happened every day that we/the Israelis were in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 40:36–38; PSALMS 32:8; JOHN 10:9"> When the cloud rose up and started to move to a new location, we/they followed it. When the cloud stopped, we/the Israelis stopped there and set up their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:1; 2 JOHN 1:6; EXODUS 17:1"> We/They traveled when Yahweh, by causing the cloud to move or stop, signaled to us/them when to move and when to stop. When the cloud stayed over the Sacred Tent, we/the Israelis stayed at that place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 3:7; NUMBERS 1:52–53; NUMBERS 3:8"> Sometimes the cloud stayed over the tent for a long time, so when that happened, we/they did not travel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Sometimes the cloud remained over the tent for only a few days. We/The people stopped and set up our/their tents as Yahweh commanded us/them, and we/they traveled to a new location when Yahweh commanded us/them to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; Nehemiah 9:12"> Sometimes the cloud stayed in one place for only one day. When that happened, when the cloud rose up into the sky the next morning, then we/they traveled. Whenever the cloud moved, during the day or during the night, we/they traveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 32:8; EXODUS 40:36–37; EXODUS 40:16"> If the cloud stayed over the Sacred Tent for two days, or for a month, or for a year, during that time we/they stayed where we/they were. But when the cloud rose up into the sky, we/they started to travel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOSHUA 22:3; NUMBERS 9:19; GENESIS 26:5"> When Yahweh commanded us/them to stop and set up our/their tents, we/they did that. When he told us/them to move, we/they moved. We/They did whatever Yahweh told Moses/me that we/they should do.</span>
</div>
</div>
