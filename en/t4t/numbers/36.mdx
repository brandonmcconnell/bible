## Land for Zelophehad’s daughters

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:1; 1 CHRONICLES 7:14–16; JOSHUA 17:2–3"> The family leaders of the clan of Gilead of the tribe of Manasseh went to Moses/me and the other family leaders of the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:3–6; NUMBERS 27:1–7; NUMBERS 33:54"> They said to Moses/me, “Yahweh commanded you, our leader, to apportion the land to the Israeli tribes by throwing ◄lots/stones that have been marked► to decide which group would get which area. Yahweh also commanded you to give the land that belonged to our fellow Israeli Zelophehad to his daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But if his daughters marry men from the other Israeli tribes, that land will no longer belong to our tribe. People of other tribes will get it. So some of our land will no longer belong to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:10–18; Isaiah 61:2; LEVITICUS 25:23"> When the Year of Celebration comes, when all the land that has been bought by someone is returned to its original owners, the land that belonged to Zelophehad will belong to the tribes of the men that his daughters marry. So some of our land, the land that we received from our fathers, will be taken from us, and we will never own it again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:7; DEUTERONOMY 5:28"> Yahweh told Moses/me what to reply to them, so Moses/I said this to them: “These men from the tribe of Manasseh are right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="NUMBERS 36:12; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:14; GENESIS 24:3"> This is what Yahweh is saying to the daughters of Zelophehad: ‘Each of you may marry anyone you want to, but you must marry only someone from your own tribe.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 21:3; NUMBERS 36:9"> In that way, the land that belongs to Israelis will not be passed from one tribe to another tribe. Each Israeli will keep the land in the tribe that belonged to his ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:22"> A woman who inherits her father’s land may get married, but she must marry someone from her own tribe. In that way, every Israeli will keep the land that belonged to his ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The land must not be passed from one tribe to another tribe. Each Israeli tribe must keep the land that it received from its ancestors.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 30:12; MATTHEW 28:20; EXODUS 39:42–43"> Zelophehad’s daughters obeyed what Yahweh told Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:1; NUMBERS 26:33"> The five daughters—Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah and Noah—married their cousins, their father’s relatives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The men whom they married were from the tribe of Manasseh, so their land continued to belong to their father’s family and tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:34; LEVITICUS 26:46; NUMBERS 22:1"> Those were the commands &#91;DOU&#93; that Yahweh gave to Moses/me to tell to the Israelis, while they/we were on the plains of the Moab region, close to the Jordan River, across from Jericho.</span>
</div>
