## A powerful angel descended from heaven and announced that the cities represented by Babylon were about to be completely destroyed because of their immorality and idolatry.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:2; LUKE 17:24; 2 THESSALONIANS 2:8"> After this, in the vision I saw another angel, who had great authority, coming down from heaven. The earth became bright because he was shining so intensely/brightly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="REVELATION 14:8; JEREMIAH 51:8; JEREMIAH 51:37"> He shouted with a very loud voice, “God is about to completely destroy &#91;DOU&#93; all the very evil cities (OR, the very evil city) that Babylon represents. As a result, all kinds of evil spirits &#91;DOU&#93; will live there, and all kinds of foul and detestable &#91;DOU&#93; birds will live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="REVELATION 14:8; REVELATION 17:2; REVELATION 18:9"> God will destroy those cities because their rulers have persuaded the people of &#91;MTY&#93; all nations to act very immorally and idolatrously with the people of &#91;MET&#93; those cities, just like a prostitute persuades men to drink strong wine and then commit fornication with her. The rulers of the earth have also acted immorally and idolatrously with the people of &#91;MTY&#93; those cities. The merchants of the earth have become rich because the people of those cities strongly desired and bought from the merchants many ◄luxuries/expensive things►.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## John heard Jesus tell God’s people to flee from those cities.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:17; JEREMIAH 51:6"> I heard Jesus speak from heaven. He said, “My people, flee from those cities, in order that you do not sin as the people of those cities do. If you sin as they do, I will punish you as I will punish them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 18:20–21; Ezra 9:6; REVELATION 16:19"> It is as though their sins have been piled &#123;have piled&#125; up to heaven, and God remembers them &#91;DOU&#93;, so now he will punish them &#91;MTY&#93;.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus sentenced the cities represented by Babylon to destruction.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:29; PSALMS 137:8; JEREMIAH 51:24"> To the angels whom God assigned to punish those cities, Jesus said, “Pay back/Punish the people of those cities to the same extent that they harmed other people. Cause them to suffer twice as much as they caused other people to suffer &#91;DOU, MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 47:7–9; ZEPHANIAH 2:15; LAMENTATIONS 1:1"> To the same extent that they have boasted about themselves and done the things that they wanted to do, to that extent torment them and cause them to grieve. Do that because in their minds they think, ‘We rule as queens! We are not widows, and we will never mourn as widows do’!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:34; JEREMIAH 50:31; REVELATION 17:16"> So in one day terrible calamities/troubles will come upon them. The people in those cities will die, others will mourn for them, people will be hungry because there will be no food, and their cities will be burned up. The Lord God is able to punish those cities like that, because he is very powerful.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Earth’s kings, merchants, and maritime traders mourned the loss of those cities.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="REVELATION 19:3; REVELATION 18:3; REVELATION 17:2"> “The kings of the earth who have acted immorally with people of those cities and have lived with the people of those cities, doing just what they wanted to do, will weep and mourn &#91;DOU&#93; for them when they see the smoke of the fire that is burning those cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="REVELATION 18:19; REVELATION 18:8; REVELATION 18:15–17"> They will stand far away from those cities, because they will be afraid that they will suffer just like the people of those cities are suffering. They will say, ‘Terrible things will happen to the awesome and mighty cities represented by Babylon! God will punish them suddenly and swiftly &#91;MTY&#93;!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="REVELATION 18:3; REVELATION 18:23; EZEKIEL 27:27–36"> The merchants of the earth will weep and will mourn &#91;DOU&#93; for those cities, because no one will ever again buy the things that they have to sell to the people in those cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="REVELATION 17:4; EZEKIEL 27:5–25; PROVERBS 8:10–11"> They sell ornaments made of gold, silver, precious stones, and pearls. They sell expensive cloth made of fine linen and silk, expensive cloth that is dyed purple and crimson. They sell all kinds of rare wood, all kinds of items made of ivory, costly wood, bronze, iron, and marble. They sell cinnamon, spice, perfume, frankincense, wine, olive oil, fine flour, and grain. They sell cattle, sheep, horses, and chariots. They even sell human beings &#91;SYN, DOU&#93; to become slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:6; PSALMS 106:14; LUKE 16:25"> The merchants will say, ‘The good things you people longed to have are gone! All your luxurious and splendid &#91;DOU&#93; possessions have vanished!! They will be gone forever/You will never have them again &#91;LIT&#93;!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="REVELATION 18:3; ACTS 19:24–27; EZEKIEL 27:31"> The merchants who sold these things and who had become rich by supplying them for those cities will stand far away, because they will be afraid that they will suffer just like the people of those cities are suffering. They will weep and mourn &#91;DOU&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="REVELATION 17:4; LUKE 16:19–31; REVELATION 18:10–11"> and they will say, ‘Terrible things &#91;DOU&#93; have happened to the awesome/great &#91;MET&#93; cities! They were like queens who dressed themselves in clothes made of &#91;MTY&#93; fine linen cloth and expensive cloth dyed purple and crimson, and who were adorned with gold, precious stones, and pearls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:27–36; REVELATION 17:16; Isaiah 23:14"> But suddenly and swiftly &#91;MTY&#93; God has destroyed these expensive things.’ Every ship captain, all people who travel by ship, all ◄sailors/those who work on ships►, and all others who earn their living by traveling on the ocean will stand far away from those cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="REVELATION 13:4; EZEKIEL 27:30–32; REVELATION 18:9–10"> When they see the smoke of the fire that is burning those cities, they will shout, ‘No other cities have ever been so awesome!’ &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="REVELATION 18:10; JOSHUA 7:6; EZEKIEL 27:30"> They will throw dust on their heads to show that they are sad, and they will shout, weep, and mourn &#91;DOU&#93;. They will say, ‘Terrible things have happened to the awesome/great cities, the cities that made all people who own ships that sail on the ocean become rich by carrying their costly ◄things for them to sell/merchandise►! God has suddenly and swiftly destroyed &#91;MTY&#93; those cities!’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## All of God’s people are called to rejoice because God has pronounced judgment on the cities represented by Babylon.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="REVELATION 6:10; REVELATION 19:1–3; LUKE 18:7–8"> Then someone spoke from heaven saying, “All you who live in heaven, rejoice over what has happened to the cities represented by Babylon! You who are God’s people, including you apostles, you ◄prophets/who speak messages that come directly from God►, rejoice, because God has justly punished the people of those cities because they acted in an evil way toward you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:63–64; EZEKIEL 26:21; REVELATION 5:2"> Then a mighty angel picked up a boulder the size of a large stone for grinding grain, and threw it into the ocean. Then he said, “O you people in &#91;APO&#93; the awesome/great cities represented by Babylon, your cities will be destroyed so that they will disappear just like that stone disappeared in the ocean! Your cities will be gone forever &#91;LIT&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:13; JEREMIAH 25:10; JEREMIAH 7:34"> In your cities there will never again be anyone playing harps, singing, playing flutes, and blowing trumpets. There will no longer be any ◄skilled workers/craftsmen► making things. There will never again be people grinding grain at &#91;MTY&#93; the mills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="REVELATION 18:3; Isaiah 23:8; NAHUM 3:4"> No lamplight will ever again shine. There will never again be the happy voices of a bridegroom and his bride. God will destroy your cities because your merchants were the most deceitful men in the world. You persuaded them to deceive people of &#91;MTY&#93; all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="REVELATION 17:6; REVELATION 16:6; JEREMIAH 51:49"> You are also responsible for killing &#91;MTY&#93; the prophets and others of God’s people. Indeed, you are the ones who are guilty for the deaths of all those who have been &#123;whom others have&#125; killed on the earth!”</span>
</div>
