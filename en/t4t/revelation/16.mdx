## Four angels emptied their judgment bowls.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:8; JEREMIAH 10:25; REVELATION 15:1"> In the vision I heard someone in the temple speak in a loud voice to the angels who had the seven bowls. He said, “Go from here and pour out on the earth the wine/liquid in &#91;MTY&#93; the seven bowls. This will show that God is angry with rebellious people and will punish them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="REVELATION 8:7; REVELATION 16:11; REVELATION 13:15–18"> So the first angel went and poured out on the earth the wine/liquid that was in &#91;MTY&#93; his bowl. As a result, horrible and painful sores broke out on God’s enemies who had allowed the beast’s agents to mark them and who worshipped the image of the beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 7:17–21; REVELATION 8:8–9; REVELATION 11:6"> The second angel poured out on the ocean the wine/liquid that was in &#91;MTY&#93; his bowl. As a result, the water in the ocean stank like the blood of a person who has died. And &#91;MTY&#93; every living creature that was in the ocean died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 7:17–20; REVELATION 8:10–11; HOSEA 13:15"> The third angel poured out on the rivers and water springs the wine/liquid &#91;MTY&#93; that was in his bowl. As a result, the water in the rivers and springs turned into blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="REVELATION 1:8; REVELATION 11:17; REVELATION 1:4"> I heard the angel who has authority over the waters say to God, “O God, you exist and have always existed. You are the holy one. You judge people justly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; REVELATION 11:18; REVELATION 6:10–11"> The rebellious people murdered your holy people and prophets. So you are just in punishing them by giving them blood to drink. This is what they deserve!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="REVELATION 19:2; REVELATION 6:9; REVELATION 15:3–4"> Then I heard an angel respond from the altar &#91;PRS&#93;, “Yes, Lord God, you who are almighty, you punish people rightly and justly!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ACTS 2:20; REVELATION 14:18; JONAH 4:8"> Then the fourth angel poured out on the sun the wine/liquid that was in &#91;MTY&#93; his bowl. He enabled the sun to scorch people with fiery heat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="REVELATION 2:21; REVELATION 16:21; REVELATION 11:13"> Because people were severely burned by the intense heat &#123;the intense heat severely burned people&#125;, they said evil things about God &#91;MTY&#93;, because he had the power to strike people with plagues like those. But they refused to stop their evil behavior and refused to honor him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Two more angels emptied their judgment bowls on the earth. Demons gathered armies to fight against God. But Jesus warned his people to watch faithfully for his imminent coming. The battle was ready to begin at Armageddon.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="REVELATION 9:2; MATTHEW 8:12; Isaiah 8:21–22"> When the fifth angel poured out on the throne of the beast the wine/liquid that was in &#91;MTY&#93; his bowl, it became dark in the kingdom that the beast ruled. As a result, the beast and the people whom it ruled were biting their tongues because they were suffering intense pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="REVELATION 16:9; REVELATION 16:2; REVELATION 16:21"> They insulted God who rules in heaven, because their sores were so painful. But they refused to stop doing the evil things that they were doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="REVELATION 9:14; JEREMIAH 51:36; Isaiah 44:27"> The sixth angel poured out on the huge river Euphrates the wine/liquid that was in &#91;MTY&#93; his bowl. As a result, the water in the river was dried up so that the rulers from the eastern &#91;MTY&#93; countries could cross it with their armies &#91;SYN&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="REVELATION 19:20; 2 THESSALONIANS 2:9–11; REVELATION 20:10"> I saw evil spirits that looked like frogs. One came out of the mouth of the dragon, one from the mouth of the beast, and one from the mouth of the false prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="REVELATION 17:14; 1 TIMOTHY 4:1; REVELATION 20:8"> Those spirits were demons who were able to perform miracles. They went out to the rulers of the whole world, in order to gather their armies &#91;SYN&#93; together to the battle that they will fight on the wonderful day when Almighty God punishes his enemies &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:2–3; MATTHEW 24:42–43; 2 PETER 3:10"> The evil spirits will gather the rulers at a place that is called in the Hebrew language Armageddon. I heard the Lord Jesus say, “You must listen carefully to me: I am coming unexpectedly &#91;SIM&#93;, like a thief. So, I will be happy with those who stay alert and keep on living righteously so that they will not be ashamed, just like &#91;MET&#93; a person keeps his clothes on so that he will not be ashamed like a person who is naked.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## After the seventh angel emptied his bowl, God punished the very evil city symbolized by Babylon, and destroyed other cities, and terrible disasters followed.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 19:30; REVELATION 21:6; EPHESIANS 6:12"> The seventh angel poured out into the air the wine/liquid that was in &#91;MTY&#93; his bowl. As a result, someone said with a loud voice from the throne in the temple, “The time for God to finish punishing rebellious people is about to be start.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DANIEL 12:1; REVELATION 4:5; REVELATION 11:19"> When the angel emptied his bowl, lightning flashed, there were rumblings and thunder, and the earth shook. It shook more violently than it has ever shaken since people first lived on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="REVELATION 18:5; REVELATION 17:18; REVELATION 18:21"> And as a result, the very evil city that is symbolized by Babylon split into three parts. God also destroyed cities in other nations. God did not forget that the people of &#91;MTY&#93; the very evil city symbolized by Babylon had sinned very much. So he caused the people with whom he was furiously angry &#91;MET&#93; to drink a cup of very poisonous wine/liquid that represented his punishment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="REVELATION 6:14; REVELATION 20:11; JEREMIAH 4:23–25"> Also as a result of the earthquake, every island disappeared, and the mountains became flat land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="REVELATION 11:19; JOSHUA 10:11; REVELATION 16:9"> Also as a result of the angel’s emptying his bowl, huge hailstones, each weighing about 100 pounds, fell from the sky onto the people. Then people insulted God because he had caused the destructive hail to fall on them in that terrible plague.</span>
</div>
</div>
