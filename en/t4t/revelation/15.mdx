## John saw in the sky a marvelous sign of the seven angels who were responsible for inflicting people with the seven last plagues.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:21; REVELATION 15:6–7; REVELATION 21:9"> Something else very unusual appeared in the sky. I saw seven angels, whose duty it was to punish (OR, inflict hardship upon) rebellious people with seven different plagues (OR, in seven different ways). God is so angry with rebellious people that this is the last time that he will punish them with the purpose of giving them an opportunity to turn away from their sinful behavior.</span>
</div>
</div>

## John saw those who had been victorious over the beast respond to God’s judgment of rebellious people on earth by singing praise to God for his mighty deliverance and righteous judgment.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="REVELATION 4:6; REVELATION 12:11; REVELATION 19:1–7"> I saw what looked like an ocean that was made of glass and mixed with fire. And I saw the people who overcame the beast by not worshipping it or its image, or allowing its agent to mark them with the number that corresponds to the beast’s name &#91;PRS&#93;. They were standing by the ocean that looked like it was made of glass. They had harps for praising God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 111:2; EXODUS 15:1–18; PSALMS 139:14"> They were singing a song like God’s servant Moses sang long ago. They sang like this to praise Jesus, the one who is like a lamb:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lord God Almighty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whatever you do is powerful and marvelous!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You always act righteously and truthfully.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You are king forever!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 86:9; MALACHI 1:11; JEREMIAH 10:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">O Lord, you alone are holy!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">People of &#91;MTY&#93; all nations will come and worship you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you show everyone that you have judged everyone righteously.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So, everyone will fear you and honor you! &#91;RHQ&#93;.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## John saw angels being given the seven bowls of judgment in the heavenly sanctuary, which became filled with the smoke of God’s unapproachable glory.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="REVELATION 11:19; NUMBERS 1:50; MATTHEW 27:51"> After this, in the vision I saw in heaven the temple that ◄corresponds to/was represented by► the tent that the Israelites pitched in the desert. That was the tent that contained the Ten Commandments. The temple door was opened &#123;was open&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="REVELATION 1:13; REVELATION 15:1; EZEKIEL 44:17–18"> The seven angels who had the bowls containing the seven plagues came out of the temple. The angels were dressed in clean, white linen garments, and they wore gold bands around their chests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="REVELATION 10:6; REVELATION 5:8; REVELATION 16:2–1"> One of the four living creatures gave each of the seven angels a golden bowl, filled with wine/liquid. That wine/liquid symbolized that God, who lives forever, would severely punish rebellious people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 6:4; 1 KINGS 8:10–11; EXODUS 40:34–35"> The temple was filled with smoke that symbolized the presence of the glorious and all-powerful God. No one was able to enter the temple until the seven angels finished pouring out the seven plagues.</span>
</div>
