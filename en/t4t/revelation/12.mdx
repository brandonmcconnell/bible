## When a royal woman gave birth to a royal son, she fled to safety and care in a place in the wilderness that was prepared by God.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 21:25; EPHESIANS 5:32; LUKE 21:11"> Then something very unusual appeared in the sky. It was a woman, whose appearance and clothing were &#91;MET&#93; as bright as the sun. The moon was under her feet. On her head was a crown that was made of twelve stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 16:21; GALATIANS 4:19; REVELATION 12:4"> She was pregnant. Then, as she was about to give birth, she cried out because she was suffering pain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="REVELATION 17:12; REVELATION 12:9; DANIEL 7:24"> Something else very unusual appeared in the sky. It was a huge red dragon. It had seven heads and ten horns. On each of its heads was a royal crown.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 8:9–12; REVELATION 12:9; JOHN 8:44"> The dragon’s tail dragged a third of the stars from the sky and threw them to the earth. The dragon set himself in front of the woman who was about to give birth, in order that he might eat her child as soon as it was born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="REVELATION 19:15; REVELATION 2:26–27; REVELATION 12:2"> The woman gave birth to a son, who is destined to rule all the nations with complete authority as if he was using &#91;MET&#93; an iron rod &#91;MET&#93;. God snatched away her child and took him to rule from his throne.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="REVELATION 13:5; REVELATION 11:2–3; REVELATION 12:14"> The woman fled to a desert. She has a place there that God has prepared for her, in order that the angels may take care of her for 1,260 days.</span>
</div>
</div>

## There was a battle in heaven in which Michael and his angels fought the dragon. The dragon and his angels were thrown out of heaven to earth. A loud voice from heaven declared the victory and the sovereignty of God, and lamented the tragic struggle coming to people on earth.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JUDE 1:9; DANIEL 12:1; EPHESIANS 6:12"> In the vision I saw that there was a battle in heaven. Michael and the angels that he commanded fought against the dragon. The dragon and his angels fought back against Michael and his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 37:10; REVELATION 12:11; JUDE 1:6"> But the dragon did not win the battle, so God did not allow the dragon and his angels to stay in heaven any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 10:18; JOHN 12:31; REVELATION 20:10"> The huge dragon was thrown &#123;Michael and his angels threw the huge dragon&#125; out of heaven. The dragon is the ancient serpent, the one who is called the Devil and Satan. He is the one who deceives people all over the earth. He was thrown down to the earth, along with all his angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 22:31; ZECHARIAH 3:1–2; REVELATION 11:15"> Then I heard someone in heaven shout loudly, saying,<br />Now our God has saved his people by his power, and he rules everyone!<br />Now the Messiah, the one who is the supreme ruler whom God appointed,<br />has authority to rule all people, because our God has thrown out of heaven the one who accuses our fellow believers!<br />The dragon is the one who accuses them day and night before our God,<br />saying that they have sinned and that God ought to punish them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 10:3–5; JOHN 16:33; 1 JOHN 5:5"> Our fellow believers overcame the dragon because they never stopped trusting<br />(OR, never stopped telling people about) Jesus,<br />and because they trusted in what Jesus, the one who is like a lamb, accomplished when his blood flowed when he died.<br />Even though those believers wanted to live,<br />they were willing to let people kill them for speaking the truth about him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; REVELATION 8:13; PSALMS 96:11–13"> So, all you angels living &#91;MTY&#93; in heaven, rejoice!<br />But terrible things will happen to you ungodly people who &#91;MTY&#93; live on the earth and on the ocean, because the devil has come down to you!<br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is very angry because he knows that he has only a short time during which he can harm people.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## When the dragon pursued the woman who had given birth to a son, he was thwarted, so he prepared to fight against her other offspring.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 3:15; REVELATION 12:3–5; JOHN 16:33"> When the dragon realized that he had been thrown &#123;that the angels had thrown him&#125; down to the earth, he pursued the woman who had given birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 19:4; Isaiah 40:31; REVELATION 12:6"> But the woman was given two wings like the wings of a very large eagle, in order that she might fly to a desolate place. That is a place that God has prepared for her. There she was taken care of &#123;God’s angels took care of her&#125; for three and a half years. The serpent, that is, the dragon, was not able to reach her there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Isaiah 59:19; PSALMS 18:4"> The serpent spewed water like a river from his mouth in the direction of the woman, in order that the water might sweep her away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 KINGS 17:6; EXODUS 12:35–36; 2 KINGS 8:9"> But the ground helped the woman by opening up and swallowing the river that the dragon spewed out from his mouth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="REVELATION 14:12; GENESIS 3:15; REVELATION 11:7"> Then the dragon was very angry with the woman, so he went away to fight against the people who are like the rest of her descendants. They are the people who obey God’s commandments and who tell other people about Jesus (OR, hold fast to what Jesus taught them).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The dragon stood on the ocean shore.</span>
</div>
