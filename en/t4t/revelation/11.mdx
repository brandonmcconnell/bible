## Christ told John to count his worshipers from the nations, and he appointed two witnesses to prophesy to the nations for a limited time. When they finished witnessing, the beast from the underworld killed them, causing the nations to enjoy temporary victory. God resurrected his witnesses, took them to heaven, and punished the nations. Survivors feared and acknowledged God as awesome.

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 4:11–14; ZECHARIAH 4:2–3; JEREMIAH 11:16"> Those witnesses are the ones that are represented by the two olive trees and the two lampstands that are in the presence of the Lord who rules the earth &#91;SYM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:14; 2 KINGS 1:10–12; HOSEA 6:5"> If enemies try to harm those witnesses, fire will come from the mouths of the witnesses and destroy them. If people want to harm them, the two witnesses will certainly kill them similarly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 17:1; LUKE 4:25; PSALMS 105:26–36"> Those witnesses will have authority to keep rain from falling during the time that they are proclaiming what God reveals to them. <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:14; REVELATION 18:10; REVELATION 18:21"> The dead bodies of the two witnesses will lie in the street of the great city where their Lord was crucified, the city that is symbolically named Sodom or Egypt because its people are very evil, like the people who lived in Sodom and Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 79:2–3; REVELATION 17:15; REVELATION 10:11"> Individuals of many people-groups, tribes, language groups &#91;MTY&#93;, and nations will look at their dead bodies for three and a half days. <span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:17; REVELATION 3:10; ACTS 5:33"> When the rebellious people who live on the earth see that the witnesses are dead, they will rejoice and celebrate. <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 2:7; ROMANS 8:11; ROMANS 8:2"> But after three and a half days, God will cause those two witnesses to breathe again and live. They will stand up, and those who see them will be terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="REVELATION 4:1; 2 KINGS 2:11; ACTS 1:9"> The two witnesses will hear a loud voice which came from heaven saying to them: “Come up here!” Then they will go up to heaven in a cloud. Their enemies will see them as they ascend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="REVELATION 14:7; REVELATION 6:12; JOSHUA 7:19"> At that same time there will be a terrible earthquake, which will cause a tenth of the buildings in &#91;MTY&#93; the city will collapse, and 7,000 people will die. The rest of the people will be afraid and acknowledge that the God who rules in heaven is awesome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="REVELATION 8:13; REVELATION 9:12; REVELATION 15:1"> That will be the second terrible event. Be aware that the third tragic event will happen soon.</span>

## When the seventh angel sounded his trumpet, voices from heaven declared that the Lord God and his Messiah now govern everyone, and the elders declared that the time had come for God’s final judgment. With the heavenly sanctuary open, lightning flashed, thunder reverberated, the earth shook, and large hail fell.
