## Nineveh will be punished

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:11; EZEKIEL 39:4; Isaiah 34:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look at their flashing swords and glittering spears</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as the horsemen race forward!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Many people of Nineveh will be killed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there will be piles of corpses,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that people will stumble over them.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 13:26; EZEKIEL 23:29; JEREMIAH 13:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">So the Commander of the armies of angels says to the people of Nineveh:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“I am your enemy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will cause the people in other nations to see you completely humiliated</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;MET&#93; women who have committed adultery are humiliated by having their skirts lifted high,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that people can see their naked bodies.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MALACHI 2:9; JEREMIAH 51:37; JOB 9:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause rubbish/garbage to be thrown at you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will show others that I despise you very much,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will cause you to be publicly ridiculed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:5; Isaiah 51:19; REVELATION 18:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">All those who see you will turn their backs to you and say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Nineveh is ruined,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but absolutely no one &#91;RHQ&#93; will mourn for it.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">No one will regret Nineveh being destroyed!”</span></span>

## The people of Nineveh will not be able to defend themselves

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="AMOS 6:2; EZEKIEL 30:14–16; JEREMIAH 46:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your city is certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; no safer than Thebes city was.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Thebes was an important city beside the Nile River;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the river was like &#91;SIM&#93; a wall that protected the city &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:5; EZEKIEL 27:10; Isaiah 20:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The rulers of Ethiopia and Egypt helped Thebes;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there was no limit to their power.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The governments of the nearby countries of Put and Libya were also allies of Thebes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; JOEL 3:3; HOSEA 13:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Thebes was captured,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and its people were ◄exiled/forced to go to other countries►.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their babies were dashed to pieces in the streets of the city.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Enemy soldiers ◄cast lots/threw small marked stones► to decide who would get each official in Thebes to become his slave.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All the leaders of Thebes were fastened/tied by chains.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="REVELATION 6:13; Isaiah 28:4; HABAKKUK 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your enemies will cause the walls around your city to fall down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; the first figs that fall from fig trees each year.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your city will be captured easily, like &#91;MET&#93; figs that fall into the mouths &#91;HYP&#93; of those who shake the fig trees.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:30; Isaiah 19:16; JEREMIAH 50:37"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look at your soldiers!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will be as weak/helpless as &#91;MET&#93; women!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The gates of your city will be opened wide</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to allow your enemies to enter them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then the bars of those gates will be burned.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 32:3–4; NAHUM 2:1; 2 CHRONICLES 32:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Store up water now to use when your enemies surround the city!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Repair the forts!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Dig up clay and trample it to make it soft,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and put it into molds to make bricks to repair the walls!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOEL 1:4; NAHUM 2:13; NAHUM 3:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Nevertheless, your enemies will burn your city;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will kill you with their swords;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will kill you like &#91;SIM&#93; locusts destroy crops.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 22:17; Nehemiah 9:23; JEREMIAH 33:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">In your city there are now very many merchants;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it seems that there are more of them than there are stars.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But when your city is being destroyed, those merchants will take the valuable things and disappear</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; locusts that strip the leaves from plants and then fly away.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:17; PSALMS 76:5–6; JEREMIAH 50:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">O King of Assyria, your officials will all be dead &#91;EUP&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your important people will lie down and rest forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your people will be scattered over the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there will no one to gather them together.</span></span>
