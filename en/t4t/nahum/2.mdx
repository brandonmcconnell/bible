## Nineveh will be destroyed

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1–2; 2 PETER 3:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">But, the gates of the dams on the rivers will be thrown open by enemy soldiers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the flood will cause the palace to collapse.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:13; REVELATION 17:1; JEREMIAH 46:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people will rush from Nineveh</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; water rushes from a broken dam.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The officials will shout, “Stop! Stop!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the people will not even look back as they run away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:12; DANIEL 11:8; NAHUM 2:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The enemy attackers say to each other, “Seize the silver!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Grab the gold!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is a huge amount of very valuable things in this city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">more valuable things than anyone can count!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOEL 2:6; PSALMS 22:14; DANIEL 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Soon everything valuable in the city will be seized or ruined.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">People will be trembling, with the result that they will not be able to fight.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their faces will all become pale.</span></span>
</div>
