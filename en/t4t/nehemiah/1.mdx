## Nehemiah’s prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; ZECHARIAH 7:1; Esther 1:2"> I am Nehemiah, the son of Hacaliah. I am writing this account of what I did when I returned to Jerusalem. After King Artaxerxes had been ruling the Persian Empire for almost 20 years, near the end of that year, I was in Susa, the capital of Persia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; EZEKIEL 6:9; JEREMIAH 44:14"> My brother Hanani came to visit me. He and some other men had just returned from Judah. I queried them about the Jews who were living there, whose parents had been ◄exiled/taken forcefully► to Babylonia many years previously. I also asked them about what was happening in Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:17; 2 KINGS 25:10; Nehemiah 7:6"> They said to me, “The Jews in Jerusalem who returned there from Babylonia are living in a very difficult situation. The walls of the city have been broken down, and even the city gates have been burned down.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 9:3; PSALMS 137:1; Ezra 10:1"> When I heard that, I sat down and cried. For several days I mourned, and I ◄fasted/abstained from eating food►, and I prayed to our God, who is/rules in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 20:6; DANIEL 9:4–19; DEUTERONOMY 7:21"> I said, “Yahweh, you who are/rule in heaven, you are a wonderful and awesome God. You keep your promise to faithfully love those who love you and who obey your commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 9:20; DANIEL 9:4; PSALMS 34:15"> Now please look down and listen to what I am praying. I pray during the day and at night for your Israeli people. I confess that we Israeli people have sinned. Even my family and I have sinned against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 9:5–6; DANIEL 9:11; DANIEL 9:13"> We have acted very wickedly. Many years ago your servant Moses gave us your laws and all the things you commanded us to do &#91;DOU&#93;, but we have not obeyed/done them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:64; DEUTERONOMY 4:25–27; LEVITICUS 26:33–46"> “But please remember what you told your servant Moses. You said, ‘If you sin, I will scatter you among the ◄heathen nations/nations that do not believe in me►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:11–14; DEUTERONOMY 30:2–5; DEUTERONOMY 4:29–31"> But if you return to me and obey my commands, even if you have been exiled to very remote/distant places on the earth, I will bring you back here to Israel, the land that I &#91;MTY&#93; have chosen to be honored in.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:29; DANIEL 9:15–27; EXODUS 32:11"> “We are your servants. We are the people whom you brought here by your very great power &#91;MTY, DOU&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Nehemiah 2:8; PROVERBS 21:1"> Yahweh, please hear this prayer of mine, and the prayers of all your people who are delighted to revere you &#91;MTY&#93;. Today I will go to the king, to request that he do me a great favor. Please motivate him to act kindly toward me, in order that I will be successful in obtaining what I want.”<br />At that time, I was the one whose work was to taste all the food and drink before it was served to the king, to be sure that no one had put poison in it.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
