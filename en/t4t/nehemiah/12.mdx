## Nehemiah dedicated the wall

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 15:16; 1 CHRONICLES 15:28; 2 CHRONICLES 29:30"> When we dedicated the wall around Jerusalem, we summoned the descendants of Levi who help the priests from the nearby places where they were living. We asked them to join with us to celebrate when we dedicated the wall. We wanted them to sing songs to thank Yahweh and make music by playing cymbals and harps and other stringed instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 9:16; 1 CHRONICLES 2:54; Nehemiah 6:2"> We summoned the descendants of Levi who habitually sang together. They came to Jerusalem from nearby areas where they had settled, and from places around Netophah town southeast of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:43; 1 CHRONICLES 6:60; Ezra 2:24"> and from three places northeast of Jerusalem—Beth Gilgal city and the areas around Geba and Azmaveth. Those singers had built villages to live in near Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOB 1:5; EXODUS 19:10; 2 CHRONICLES 29:5"> When they came to Jerusalem, the priests and other descendants of Levi who help the priests performed rituals to cause themselves to be acceptable to God, and then they performed similar rituals to purify the other people, the gates, and the wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:13; Nehemiah 12:38; 1 CHRONICLES 13:1"> Then I gathered together the leaders of Judah on top of the wall, and I appointed them to lead two large groups to march around the city on top of the wall, thanking God. As they faced the city, one group walked to the right toward the Dung Gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Behind their leaders marched Hoshaiah and half of the leaders of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:2–7"> Behind them marched a group of priests who were blowing trumpets. They were Azariah, Ezra, Meshullam, Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, and Jeremiah. Next came Zechariah, the son of Jonathan, the son of Shemaiah, the son of Mattaniah, the son of Micaiah, the son of Zaccur, a descendant of Asaph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:24; 2 CHRONICLES 8:14; 1 CHRONICLES 23:5"> Behind them marched other members of Zechariah’s clan: Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah, and Hanani. They all were playing the same kinds of musical instruments that King David had played many years previously. Ezra, the man who knew the Jewish laws very well, marched in front of this group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:26; Nehemiah 2:14; Nehemiah 8:3"> When they reached the Fountain Gate, they went up the steps to David’s City, past his palace, and then to the wall at the Water Gate, on the east side of the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:8; Nehemiah 3:11; Nehemiah 12:31"> The other group of those who were thanking Yahweh marched to the left on top of the wall. I followed them with half of the people. We marched past the Tower of the Ovens to the Broad Wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:3; Nehemiah 8:16"> From there we marched past Ephraim Gate, Jeshanah Gate, the Fish Gate, the Tower of Hananel, the Tower of the Hundred Soldiers, to the Sheep Gate. We finished marching near the gate into the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PSALMS 42:4; PSALMS 134:1–3; Nehemiah 12:31–32"> Both the groups reached the temple while they were thanking God. They stood in their places there. The leaders who were with me also stood with me in our places.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> My group included these priests who were all blowing trumpets: Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, Hananiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 81:1; Nehemiah 11:14; PSALMS 100:1–2"> another Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer. The singers, whose leader was Jezrahiah, sang loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezra 3:13; PSALMS 92:4; JEREMIAH 31:13"> After we went outside the temple, we offered many sacrifices. ## Preparations for worship in the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:12–13; Nehemiah 10:37–39; 2 CHRONICLES 31:11–13"> On that day, men were appointed &#123;they appointed men&#125; to be in charge of the storerooms where they kept the money that people contributed for the temple. They also kept there the other things that the people contributed— the tithes and the first part of the grain and fruit that was harvested each year. Those men collected those things from the fields/farms near the cities, and took them to Jerusalem and gave them to the priests and the other descendants of Levi, as Moses had said in his laws that they should do. All the Israeli people were happy about the work that the priests and the descendants of Levi did,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 25:1–26; 2 CHRONICLES 23:6; 1 CHRONICLES 23:28"> because they performed the rituals to purify things, and the other rituals that God had commanded. The musicians in the temple also did their work as King David and his son Solomon had declared that they should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 29:30; PSALMS 73:1; 1 CHRONICLES 25:1–31"> Ever since the time that David had been the king and Asaph had been his chief musician, the musicians had led the people while they sang songs to praise and thank God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:21–29; Nehemiah 12:26; Nehemiah 13:10–12"> During the years that Zerubbabel and I governed the Israeli people, they all contributed the food that the singers and temple gatekeepers needed each day. They gave ◄a tithe/10%► of their crops to the descendants of Levi who helped the priests, and those descendants of Levi gave ◄a tithe/10%► of that to the priests, who were descendants of Aaron the first Supreme Priest.</span>
