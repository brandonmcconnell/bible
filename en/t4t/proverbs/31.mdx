## Wise sayings from King Lemuel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:20; PROVERBS 1:8; 2 TIMOTHY 1:5"> These are sayings/messages that God gave to King Lemuel’s mother, and which his mother taught him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; 1 SAMUEL 1:11; 1 SAMUEL 1:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are my son; I gave birth to you &#91;RHQ&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you are the son that God gave me in answer to my prayers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:17; Nehemiah 13:26; PROVERBS 7:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not exhaust your energy having sex &#91;EUP&#93; with women to whom you are not married,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with women who ruin kings by having sex with them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:1; ECCLESIASTES 10:17; Isaiah 5:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lemuel, kings should not be constantly drinking wine</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or greatly desire to drink other strong/alcoholic drinks.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">If they do that, they forget the laws that they have made,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they do not do what is right for poor/afflicted people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:23; PSALMS 104:15; 1 SAMUEL 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Give strong/alcoholic drinks to those who are dying</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to those who are ◄greatly distressed/suffering very much►.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">If they drink, they will forget that they are poor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will not think about their distress/troubles any more.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 82:3–4; PROVERBS 24:11–12; Esther 4:13–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Speak &#91;MTY&#93; to defend people who are unable to defend themselves;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">speak to encourage others to do what is right for those who are helpless.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; LEVITICUS 19:15; AMOS 5:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Speak &#91;MTY&#93; ◄on their behalf/to help them► and try to cause judges to decide matters fairly/justly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">try to cause others to do for poor and needy people what should be done for them.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Praise for a good wife

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:14; PROVERBS 12:4; PROVERBS 18:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is very difficult for a man to &#91;RHQ&#93; find a wife who is good and who is capable of doing many things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Any woman who is like that is worth more than jewels.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 PETER 3:1–7; 2 KINGS 4:22–23; 2 KINGS 4:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Her husband completely trusts her,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and because of her, he has everything that he needs &#91;LIT&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:18–22; 1 SAMUEL 25:26–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">She never does anything that would harm him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she does good things for him all the days of her life.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="TITUS 2:5; 1 TIMOTHY 5:10; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">She finds wool and flax in the market,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and she enjoys spinning it to make yarn.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 31:24; 1 KINGS 9:26–28; EZEKIEL 27:3–36"> <span className="pl pl-1">She is like &#91;SIM&#93; a ship that brings from far away goods/merchandise to sell,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because she buys food that comes from far away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 12:11; MARK 1:35; LUKE 12:42"> <span className="pl pl-1">She gets up before dawn to prepare food for her family.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then she plans the work that her servant girls will do on that day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:44; SONG OF SONGS 8:12; JOSHUA 15:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">She goes out and looks at a field that someone wants to sell;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and if it is a good field, she buys it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">She buys grapevines &#91;MTY&#93; with the money that she has earned,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then she plants them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 38:3; 1 PETER 1:13; EPHESIANS 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">She works very hard &#91;IDM&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she makes her arms strong by the work she does.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:9; MATTHEW 25:3–10; PSALMS 127:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">She knows when she is getting a good profit from her business.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When it is necessary, she works &#91;MTY&#93; until it is late at night.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 35:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">She holds the ◄spindle/rod which twists the thread that she is making►,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then she spins the thread &#91;MTY&#93; that she will use.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:28; HEBREWS 13:16; PROVERBS 22:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">She generously helps &#91;MTY&#93; those who are poor and needy &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 45:22; 2 SAMUEL 1:24; PROVERBS 25:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">She is not worried that the people in her house will be cold in the winter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because she has made warm clothes for all of them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="REVELATION 19:8; PROVERBS 7:16; GENESIS 41:42"> <span className="pl pl-1">She makes bedspreads/quilts for the beds.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">She wears fine linen clothes that are dyed purple, like queens wear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:4; RUTH 4:1; DEUTERONOMY 16:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Her husband is well known by the important people of the town;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he sits with the other town leaders in the meetings of the town council.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:16; LUKE 16:19; PROVERBS 31:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">She makes clothes from linen cloth and sells them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">She sells sashes to shop owners.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOB 40:10; ROMANS 13:14; JOB 29:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">She is strong in her character and respected/dignified,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and she ◄laughs at/is not afraid of► what will happen in the future.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:29; PROVERBS 16:24; PROVERBS 10:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">When she speaks, she says what is wise.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When she gives instructions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">she speaks &#91;MTY&#93; kindly (OR, faithfully).</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:10; TITUS 2:4; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">She watches over everything that is done in her household,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and she &#91;IDM&#93; is never lazy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 3:15–17; 2 TIMOTHY 1:5; 1 KINGS 2:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Her children all together speak highly of her,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and her husband also praises her.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 6:8–9; EPHESIANS 5:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">He says to her, “There are many women who do admirable things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you surpass them all!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:7; 1 PETER 3:4–5; PROVERBS 11:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some women who are attractive are not really good women,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they can deceive us regarding what they are really like.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Furthermore, women’s beauty does not last;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but women who revere Yahweh should be honored.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 128:2; HEBREWS 6:10; MATTHEW 7:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Reward women who are like that,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and praise them in public &#91;MTY&#93; for what they have done.</span></span>
</div>
