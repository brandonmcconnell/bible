</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 6:23; Isaiah 3:10–11; HEBREWS 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you are righteous, your reward will be a good life,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but all that sinful people gain is to sin more.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 PETER 1:5–11; LUKE 11:28; PROVERBS 6:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who pay attention when others try to ◄correct them/teach them what they are doing that is wrong► will live ◄happily/for many years►;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but those who reject being rebuked will not find the road to life (OR, cause others to go astray).</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:13; PROVERBS 26:6; MATTHEW 25:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">We do not like a lazy person who refuses to do the job that he is given to do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">just like we do not like vinegar in our mouths or smoke in our eyes.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:11; PROVERBS 3:2; PSALMS 91:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you revere Yahweh, you will live for a long time;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but wicked people die before they become old.</span></span>
