## The purpose of Proverbs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:1; 1 CHRONICLES 22:9; 1 CHRONICLES 28:5"> These are the ◄proverbs/wise sayings► that come from Solomon, the King of Israel, who was the son of King David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:4; DEUTERONOMY 4:5–6; 2 TIMOTHY 3:15–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">◄These proverbs &#91;PRS&#93; can teach people/By studying these proverbs, people can learn► how to be wise</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and how to obey what these proverbs teach them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will also help people to understand which teachings are wise.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:1–9; JOB 22:22; PROVERBS 8:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">These proverbs &#91;PRS&#93; will teach you how to discipline/control yourselves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">how to conduct your lives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and how to do what is right and just &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:5; PROVERBS 8:17; PSALMS 119:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will show people who do not yet know much how to do things that are smart.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will show young people how to become wise and how to make good plans/decisions.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 12:1; PROVERBS 9:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who are wise should also pay attention to these proverbs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order to become more wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and those who understand these teachings will receive good advice/guidance.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 78:2; MARK 4:11; PSALMS 49:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then they will be able to understand the meaning of proverbs and parables/metaphors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">these wise sayings and ◄riddles/sayings that are difficult to understand►.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

## Warnings about sinful people

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:26; PROVERBS 1:18; PROVERBS 12:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They may say, “Come with us! ## Wisdom shouts in the streets

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:3; COLOSSIANS 2:3; LUKE 11:49"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wisdom &#91;PRS&#93; shouts to people in the streets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and calls out to people in the ◄plazas/town squares►.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ACTS 5:20; PROVERBS 9:3; MATTHEW 13:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wisdom calls out in noisy places,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and at the entrances to cities.</span></span>
</div>
