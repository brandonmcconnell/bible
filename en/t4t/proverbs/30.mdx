## Wise sayings from Agur

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 31:1; 2 PETER 1:19–21"> These are sayings/messages that God gave to Agur, the son of Jakeh.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Agur wrote them for Ithiel and Ucal.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 73:22; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:18; Isaiah 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">It seems that I am very stupid;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I do not deserve to be considered to be a human;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I do not have the good sense that humans should have.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="REVELATION 3:7; EPHESIANS 3:18–19; ROMANS 11:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have not learned how to become wise</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I do not know much about God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 3:13; EPHESIANS 4:8–10; JOB 26:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">But let me say this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No one &#91;RHQ&#93; has ascended to heaven to find out what God is like and returned to tell us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No one &#91;RHQ&#93; has gathered/held the wind in his hand.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No one &#91;RHQ&#93; has wrapped the water in the ocean in a piece of cloth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no one &#91;RHQ&#93; has established the boundaries of the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If you know who has done those things, tell me &#91;RHQ&#93; his name, and the names of his children &#91;SAR&#93;!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you do not know who has done those things, so you cannot speak with authority about what God is like.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 18:30; JAMES 3:17; PSALMS 12:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everything that God has said is true;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he is like a shield &#91;MET&#93; for all those who request him to protect them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:32; DEUTERONOMY 4:2; REVELATION 22:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not add to (OR, change) what God has said;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if you do that, he will rebuke you and show that you are lying.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 10:42; 2 KINGS 2:9; PSALMS 27:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, I ask you to do two things for me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">please do them before I die:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 6:6–8; PSALMS 62:9–10; PSALMS 119:37"> <span className="pl pl-1">Help me never to lie or deceive people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do not cause me to become poor or to become rich.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Just give me the food that I need;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HOSEA 13:6; DEUTERONOMY 31:20; EZEKIEL 16:49–50"> <span className="pl pl-1">because if I become rich,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I might say that I do not &#91;RHQ&#93; know you and that I do not need you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and if I become poor,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I might dishonor you by stealing things.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## More wise sayings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 14:4; ECCLESIASTES 7:21; DEUTERONOMY 23:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not ◄slander/say bad things about► a worker to his boss;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if you do that, the worker will curse you, and cause you to have trouble.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:20; PROVERBS 30:17; EXODUS 21:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will list four kinds of evil things that people do:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Some people curse their fathers and do not ask God to bless their mothers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 18:11; PROVERBS 16:2; 2 TIMOTHY 3:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some people think that they are perfect,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but really they have never been cleansed from their guilt for committing disgusting sins.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:17; Isaiah 2:11; PSALMS 101:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some people are very proud;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they think that they are very good and they despise others.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 57:4; PSALMS 3:7; PSALMS 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some people act very cruelly toward others;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is as though &#91;MET&#93; they have teeth that are like sharp knives;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they severely oppress poor people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and try to cause them to disappear from the land.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:21; PROVERBS 30:29; PROVERBS 6:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Leeches are always wanting more blood to suck;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">similarly, greedy people are always saying “Give me some!” or “Give me more!” &#91;MET&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There are four things that are never ◄satisfied/content with what they have►;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they always want more &#91;LIT&#93;:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:20; HABAKKUK 2:5; GENESIS 30:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The place where the dead people are;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">women who do not have any children;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">ground that needs water/rain;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and a fire that always needs more wood.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 23:22; DEUTERONOMY 21:18–21; PROVERBS 30:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who &#91;SYN&#93; make fun of their fathers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or refuse to obey their mothers (OR, despise their aged mothers)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">should die and have their eyes pecked out by crows,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the rest of their corpses should be fed to the vultures.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 42:3; PSALMS 139:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">There are four things that are wonderful to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but I do not understand any of them:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; JOB 39:27; EXODUS 22:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">How eagles fly in the sky,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">how snakes are able to move/crawl across a big rock,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">how ships sail on the seas,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and how a man falls in love with a woman.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:6; PROVERBS 7:13–23; NUMBERS 5:11–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is what a woman who ◄is not faithful to/does not have sex only with► her husband does:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">She commits adultery &#91;EUP&#93;, and then bathes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and says, “I have not done anything that is wrong!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> <span className="pl pl-1">There are four things that no one in the world can tolerate:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:10; ECCLESIASTES 10:7; 1 SAMUEL 25:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">What a slave does who becomes a king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a foolish person eating too much food,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:19; PROVERBS 27:15; PROVERBS 29:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">what a woman who is hated does when she gets married,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and what a female servant does when she becomes the boss instead of her mistress.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ants are not strong,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they store up food during the summer in order to have it during the winter.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 104:18; LEVITICUS 11:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rock badgers also are not strong,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they make their homes among the rocks where they will be safe.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOEL 2:25; JOEL 1:6–7; JOEL 2:7–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Locusts do not have a king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they march like the soldiers in an army.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lizards/Geckos are very small and you can hold them in your hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they are cleverly able to get inside kings’ palaces.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> <span className="pl pl-1">There are four animals that strut around and look very impressive while they walk &#91;DOU&#93;:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:24; JUDGES 14:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lions, which are stronger than all other animals</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and are not afraid of any of them;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DANIEL 3:15–18; PROVERBS 20:2; PROVERBS 16:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">male goats, strutting roosters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and kings who ◄parade/walk proudly back and forth► in front of the people whom they rule.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
