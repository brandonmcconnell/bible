## More wise sayings from Solomon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:1; 1 KINGS 4:32; MICAH 1:1"> Here are more wise sayings/words that Solomon wrote. Some men who worked for Hezekiah, the king of Judah, copied them from a scroll that Solomon had written.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:29; ROMANS 11:33–34; JOB 11:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">We consider God to be great because he ◄acts in mysterious ways/does things that we cannot understand►;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we consider kings to be great because they explain things.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 55:9; PSALMS 103:11; 1 KINGS 4:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is not possible for anyone to measure how high the sky is or how deep the earth/ocean is;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">likewise &#91;SIM&#93;, it is not possible for us to know all that kings are thinking.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 2:20–21; EZEKIEL 22:18; PROVERBS 17:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">If workers burn out the impure bits that are in silver,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a man who makes things from silver can make something beautiful from the silver.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:12; PROVERBS 20:8; PSALMS 101:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Similarly, if wicked advisors are taken away from a king,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">his government will remain secure, because the king will be able to act justly.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:2; PROVERBS 25:27; PROVERBS 16:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you stand in front of a king, do not try to ◄impress him/honor yourself►</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do not ◄act like you are important/ask to sit where important people sit;►</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 14:7–11; LUKE 18:14; 1 PETER 5:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">it is better if someone tells you to sit closer to the king</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than for someone to tell you, while the king is listening, to sit further away in order that someone who is more important may sit closer to the king.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:25; PROVERBS 17:14; PROVERBS 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not quickly go to a court to tell the judge about something that you have seen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because another witness may later say something that proves that you are wrong, and as a result you will be disgraced/ashamed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If that happens, ◄what will you do?/you will not know what to do.► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:5–17; PROVERBS 11:13; PROVERBS 20:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you and someone else think differently about some matter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">settle it between yourselves, and do not tell others any secret that he has told you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 119:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">If others find out that you have told secrets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will be ashamed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and from that time on, ◄you will have a bad reputation/people will think badly about you►.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:23; Isaiah 50:4; ECCLESIASTES 12:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Something that is said that is very appropriate</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">is as delightful as seeing &#91;SIM&#93; gold apples/ornaments in a silver bowl.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 141:5; PROVERBS 15:31–32; PROVERBS 15:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">When a wise person rebukes/warns someone &#91;SYN&#93; who is willing to listen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that is as valuable as &#91;SIM&#93; a gold ring or a gold chain.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:17; PROVERBS 25:25; PHILIPPIANS 2:25–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">A messenger who ◄is reliable/tells someone else exactly what he was told to say► refreshes the spirits of his bosses who sent him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; ◄snow/cold water► refreshes the ground at the time that people harvest crops.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:6; LUKE 14:11; 2 PETER 2:15–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">When someone promises to give a gift to us but never gives it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that disappoints us as much as &#91;SIM&#93; clouds and wind that come but do not bring any rain.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:1; ECCLESIASTES 10:4; PROVERBS 16:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">If someone keeps requesting a ruler long enough to do something, he will often agree to do it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">similarly, by speaking &#91;MTY&#93; gently we can often convince others that what we say is right &#91;IDM&#93;.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:27; EPHESIANS 5:18; PROVERBS 24:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you find some honey, do not eat a lot of it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because doing that may cause you to vomit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 19:2–3; JUDGES 19:18–21; ROMANS 15:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not go to your neighbor’s house very often to talk with him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if you go very often, he will get tired of listening to you and start to hate you.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 57:4; PROVERBS 12:18; JAMES 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">To falsely accuse others in court</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">is like &#91;SIM&#93; attacking them with a war-club or a sword or a sharp arrow.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; EZEKIEL 29:6–7; JOB 6:14–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Depending on unreliable people when you have troubles</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">is as bad as trying to eat when you have a bad/hurting tooth or trying to walk when your foot is crippled.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 12:15; ECCLESIASTES 3:4; Isaiah 58:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Singing to someone who is depressed just causes him to feel worse;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is like &#91;SIM&#93; taking off clothes on a very cold day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or like putting vinegar on a wound.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 12:20–21; MATTHEW 5:44; EXODUS 23:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">If your enemies are hungry, give them something to eat;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if they are thirsty, give them something to drink;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 16:12; MATTHEW 10:13; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">doing that will cause them to feel ashamed &#91;IDM&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Yahweh will reward you for doing that.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 37:22; PSALMS 15:3; ROMANS 1:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">When wind blows from the right direction, it will rain;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">similarly &#91;SIM&#93;, if we gossip about others, that causes them to look at us very angrily.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:19; PROVERBS 21:9; PROVERBS 27:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is better to live ◄alone/by yourself► in the corner of an attic/housetop</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than to live inside the house with a wife who is always nagging.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:30; ROMANS 10:15; PSALMS 63:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Receiving good news from a country far away refreshes our spirits</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; cold water refreshes us when we are very thirsty.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:18–19; 1 SAMUEL 22:14–18; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">When a righteous/good person ◄gives in/yields► to wicked people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that is as bad as &#91;SIM&#93; a spring that becomes muddied or a fountain that becomes polluted.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOHN 5:44; PROVERBS 27:2; PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is not good to eat too much honey,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and trying to get people to praise you is also not good.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:32; PROVERBS 22:24; 2 CHRONICLES 32:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">People who ◄cannot control their tempers/quickly become very angry► are unable to defend their behavior;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that is like having a city without a wall around it, with the result that no one can defend it.</span></span>
</div>
