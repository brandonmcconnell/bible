## Being wise and being foolish

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:18; REVELATION 3:12; 1 KINGS 7:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though wisdom is a woman who has built a big house for herself,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has set up seven columns to support the roof,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:3–14; Isaiah 25:6; LUKE 14:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">and has slaughtered an animal and cooked the meat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has mixed nice spices in the wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and has put the food on the table.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:14; PROVERBS 8:1–3; MATTHEW 22:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though then she sent out her servant women to call out from the highest place in the town,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:32; PROVERBS 9:16; PROVERBS 8:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“You people who need to understand more, come in!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And to those who are ignorant, it is as though she calls out,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 6:27; SONG OF SONGS 5:1; PROVERBS 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Come and eat the food that I have prepared,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and drink the good wine that I have mixed!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:20; PROVERBS 4:11; LUKE 13:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">◄Leave/Go away from► other foolish people, and if you do that, you will continue to live.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Walk on the road that will enable you to ◄have knowledge/know what is true and what is not true►.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:12; PROVERBS 23:9; 2 CHRONICLES 36:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you rebuke someone who will not allow others to correct him, he will insult you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If you reprove/scold an evil man, he will hurt you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 141:5; PROVERBS 13:18; PROVERBS 23:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not rebuke someone who will not allow others to ◄correct him/tell him what he has done is wrong►, because he will hate you for doing that.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But if you rebuke a wise person, he will respect you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:5; MATTHEW 13:11–12; 1 JOHN 5:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you give instruction to wise people, they will become wiser.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And if you teach righteous people, they will learn more.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 111:10; JOB 28:28; PROVERBS 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you want to be wise, you must start by revering Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and if you know God, the Holy One, you will understand which teachings are wise/true.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:27; PROVERBS 3:16; PROVERBS 3:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you become wise, you will live many years &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 22:2–3; 2 PETER 3:3–4; EZEKIEL 18:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you are wise, you are the one who will benefit from it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if you ridicule becoming wise, you are the one who will suffer.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:11; PROVERBS 5:6; 1 TIMOTHY 6:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Foolish women talk loudly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are ignorant and are never ashamed of the wrong things that they do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:3; PROVERBS 7:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They sit at the doors of their houses</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or they sit on the top of the hills in the town,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:25–27; PROVERBS 23:27–28; PROVERBS 7:13–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">and they call out to the men who are passing by,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who are trying to be concerned with their own affairs,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“You people who need to understand more, come into my house!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And to those who are ignorant, they call out,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:17; PROVERBS 23:31–32; JAMES 1:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Just as water which you have stolen tastes very good</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and food that you eat by yourself tastes the best,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">if you have sex secretly with someone to whom you are not married, you will enjoy it very much.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 7:27; PROVERBS 2:18–19; PROVERBS 5:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But men who go to those women’s houses do not know that those who have gone there are now dead;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they have descended down into the deepest parts of the place where dead people are.</span></span>
