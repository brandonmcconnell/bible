</div>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:4; PROVERBS 10:23; PROVERBS 15:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">are as foolish as those who deceive someone else</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then say, “I was only joking.”</span></span>
