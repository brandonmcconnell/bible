## A prayer sung by Habakkuk

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 7:1–17; PSALMS 86:1–17; PSALMS 90:1–17"> Yahweh, I have heard about you;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">I revere you because of all the amazing things that you have done.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">In our time, do again some of those things that you did long ago!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even when you are angry with us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be merciful to us!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:2; PSALMS 4:4; OBADIAH 1:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the vision, I saw God, the Holy One, coming from the Teman district in Edom;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I also saw him coming from the Paran Hills in the Sinai area.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His glory filled the sky,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the earth was full of people who were praising him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:19–20; MATTHEW 17:2; JOB 26:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">His glory was like &#91;SIM&#93; a sunrise;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">rays flashed from his hands</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">where he hides his power.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 12:29–30; NUMBERS 16:46–49; PSALMS 78:50–51"> <span className="pl pl-1">He sent plagues in front of him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and other plagues came behind him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 49:26; DEUTERONOMY 33:15; NAHUM 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">When he stopped, the earth shook.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When he looked at the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all the people trembled.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The hills and mountains that have existed for a very long time &#91;HYP&#93; collapsed and crumbled.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh is the one who exists eternally/forever!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 15:14–16; GENESIS 10:6–7; GENESIS 25:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the vision I saw the people who live in tents in the Cushan region being afflicted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the people in the Midian region trembling.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 68:17; DEUTERONOMY 33:26–27; EXODUS 14:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, was it because you were angry with the rivers and streams that you were angry with them?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Did the seas cause you to be furious,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that you rode through them with horses that were pulling chariots</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that enabled you to defeat them?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 7:12–13; PSALMS 105:41; PSALMS 78:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">It was as though you uncovered your bow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">took arrows from your quiver, and prepared to shoot them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then with lightning you split open the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and streams burst forth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:24; JOSHUA 4:23–24; PSALMS 93:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though the mountains saw you doing that,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they trembled.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Floods rushed by;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it was as though the deep ocean roared and caused its waves to rise up high.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 144:5–6; PSALMS 18:12–14; JOSHUA 10:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sun and moon stopped moving in the sky</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while your lightning flashed past like a swift arrow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your glittering spear flashed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:33; MICAH 4:12–13; NUMBERS 21:23–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Being very angry &#91;DOU&#93;, you walked across the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and trampled the armies of many nations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 110:6; PSALMS 105:15; PSALMS 68:19–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you also went to rescue us, your people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to save the king (Or, people) whom you had chosen &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You struck down the leader &#91;MTY&#93; of those wicked people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and stripped off his clothes, from his head to his feet.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:14; DANIEL 11:40; PSALMS 118:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">With his own spear you destroyed the leader of those soldiers who rushed like &#91;SIM&#93; a whirlwind to attack and scatter us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">thinking that they could conquer us Israelis easily.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 77:19; HABAKKUK 3:8; EXODUS 15:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">You walked through the sea with your horses to destroy our enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and caused the waves to surge.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:9; HABAKKUK 1:5–11; HABAKKUK 3:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I saw that vision,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my heart pounded</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and my lips quivered</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because I became afraid.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My legs became weak</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I shook, because I was terrified.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But I will wait quietly for the people of Babylonia, those who invaded our country, to experience disasters!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:17; JOEL 1:16–18; DEUTERONOMY 28:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even if there are no blossoms on the fig trees,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there are no grapes on the grapevines,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and even if there are no olives growing on the olive trees,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there are no crops in the fields,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and even if the flocks of sheep and goats die in the fields,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there are no cattle in the stalls/barns,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 13:15; PHILIPPIANS 4:4; MICAH 7:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will rejoice because I know Yahweh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will be joyful because Yahweh my God is the one who saves me!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 22:34; PSALMS 18:33; PHILIPPIANS 4:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh the Lord is the one who causes me to be strong;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he enables me to climb ◄safely/without falling► in the rocky mountains, like &#91;SIM&#93; a deer does.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">(This message is for the choir director:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">When this prayer is sung, it is to be accompanied by people playing stringed instruments.)</span></span>
</div>
