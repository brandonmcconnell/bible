## Habakkuk’s first complaint

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 22:1–2; JEREMIAH 14:9; PSALMS 13:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said, “Yahweh, how long must I continue to call to you for help</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">before you respond?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I cry out to you for help, saying that people are acting violently everywhere,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you do not rescue us!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 55:9–11; JEREMIAH 9:2–6; JEREMIAH 20:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">◄Must I/I do not want to► continue seeing people doing what is unjust &#91;RHQ&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Why do you not punish people who are doing what is wrong &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I see people destroying things and acting violently.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There is fighting and quarreling everywhere.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 119:126; Isaiah 1:21–23; JOB 21:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">No one obeys laws &#91;PRS&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and judges &#91;PRS&#93; never do what is fair/just.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Wicked people always defeat righteous people in the courts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that matters are never decided fairly/justly.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh’s reply

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 13:40–41; LAMENTATIONS 4:12; Isaiah 29:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh replied to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I know that what you say is true, but look around at what is happening in the other nations;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if you look, you will be amazed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because I am doing something during this time that you would not believe would happen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even if someone told you about it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 KINGS 24:2; JEREMIAH 5:15; Isaiah 23:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The soldiers of Babylonia are fierce/cruel and swift.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And now I am causing them to become very powerful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will march across the world</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and conquer many other countries.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:5–9; JEREMIAH 52:9–11; DEUTERONOMY 5:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are people whom others fear very much &#91;DOU&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they do whatever they want to,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">paying no attention to the laws of other countries.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:13; ZEPHANIAH 3:3; HOSEA 8:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The horses that pull their chariots go faster than leopards,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they are fiercer than wolves are in the evening.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The horses on which the soldiers ride gallop swiftly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the soldiers riding them come from distant places.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are like &#91;SIM&#93; eagles that swoop down to snatch and kill small animals.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:9; HOSEA 13:15; HOSEA 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">As they ride along,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are determined to act violently.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They advance like &#91;SIM&#93; a wind from the desert,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">gathering prisoners that are as numerous as grains of sand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:6; JEREMIAH 32:24; Isaiah 14:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">They make fun of kings and princes of other countries,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they ridicule all the cities that have high walls around them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They pile dirt outside those walls</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order to climb up and capture those cities.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 5:20; DANIEL 4:30–34; JEREMIAH 4:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They rush past like the wind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then they go to attack other cities.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But they are very guilty,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they think that their own power is their god.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Habakkuk’s second complaint

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; JEREMIAH 30:11; JEREMIAH 46:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said, “Yahweh, you have certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; always/forever been God.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You are my Holy One; you will not die.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So why have you appointed those men from Babylonia to judge and get rid of us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You are like &#91;MET&#93; a huge rock under which we are protected/safe,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so why have you sent them to punish us?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 5:4–5; PSALMS 34:15–16; JEREMIAH 12:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You &#91;SYN&#93; are pure, and you cannot endure looking at what is evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so why are you ignoring men who are treacherous?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why do you do nothing</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to punish those wicked men from Babylonia</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who destroy people who are more righteous than they are?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do you consider that we are like fish in the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or like other creatures in the sea, that have no ruler?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="AMOS 4:2; JEREMIAH 16:16; PSALMS 10:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The soldiers of Babylonia think that we are fish &#91;RHQ&#93; for them to pull out of the sea with hooks</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or to catch in their nets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while they rejoice and celebrate.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:11; DEUTERONOMY 8:17; DANIEL 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">If they catch us, they will worship their weapons &#91;MET&#93; with which they captured us, and offer sacrifices to them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and burn incense in front of them!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will say ‘Those weapons have enabled us to become rich and be able to eat expensive food.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:5–8; EZEKIEL 25:1–17; JEREMIAH 25:9–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will you allow them to continue to conquer people &#91;MET&#93; forever?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will you allow them to destroy people of other nations without their being merciful to anyone?”</span></span>
