## David’s final instructions before he died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 47:29; DEUTERONOMY 31:14; 1 TIMOTHY 1:18"> When David knew that he was about to die, he gave these final instructions to his son Solomon:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:6–7; JOSHUA 23:14; EPHESIANS 6:10"> “I am about to die &#91;EUP&#93;, like everyone else on earth does. Be courageous and conduct yourself as an adult should.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:9; DEUTERONOMY 17:18–20; PROVERBS 3:1–4"> Do what Yahweh our God tells you to do. Conduct yourself as he wants you to do. Obey all of his laws and commands and precepts and instructions that are written in the laws that Moses gave us. Do this in order that you will prosper in all that you do and wherever you go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 KINGS 20:3; 2 SAMUEL 7:25; 1 KINGS 8:25"> If you continually do that, Yahweh will do what he promised me: He said, ‘If your descendants do what I tell them to do, and faithfully obey my commands with all their inner beings, they will always be &#91;LIT&#93; the ones who will rule &#91;MTY&#93; Israel.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:27; 2 SAMUEL 20:10; 2 SAMUEL 18:5"> “There is something else that I want you to do. You know what Joab did to me. He killed my two army commanders, Abner and Amasa. He murdered them violently &#91;MTY&#93; during a time of peace, pretending that it was a time of war. Those two men ◄were innocent/had not done what was wrong►. But Joab killed them and caused their blood to stain his belt and his sandals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:9; Isaiah 57:21; PSALMS 37:37"> Because you are wise, do to him what you think is best for you to do, but do not allow him to become old &#91;MTY&#93; and then die &#91;IDM&#93; peacefully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 17:27–29; 2 SAMUEL 9:7; 2 SAMUEL 19:31–40"> “But be kind to the sons of Barzillai, the man from the Gilead region, and be sure that they always have enough food to eat &#91;MTY&#93;. Do that because Barzillai helped me when I was running away from your older brother Absalom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 16:5–8; 1 KINGS 2:36–46; 2 SAMUEL 19:16–23"> Also, you remember Gera’s son Shimei from Bahurim town in the area where the descendants of Benjamin live. You know what he did to me. He cursed me terribly on the day I left Jerusalem and went to Mahanaim town. But when he later came down to see me at the Jordan River, I solemnly promised, while Yahweh listened &#91;MTY&#93;, that I would not cause him to be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:6; GENESIS 42:38; GENESIS 44:31"> But now you must &#91;LIT&#93; surely punish him. You are a wise man, so you will know what you should do to him. He is an old man &#91;MTY&#93;, but be sure that he loses/sheds a lot of blood when you kill him &#91;MTY&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ACTS 2:29; ACTS 13:36; 1 KINGS 1:21"> Then David died &#91;EUP&#93; and was buried in that part of Jerusalem which was called ‘The City of David’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 5:4–5; 1 CHRONICLES 29:26–27"> David had been the king of Israel for 40 years: He ruled for seven years in Hebron and for 33 years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 1:1; 1 CHRONICLES 29:23–25; PSALMS 132:12"> Solomon became the ruler/king &#91;MTY&#93; to take the place of his father David and took control of all of the kingdom.</span>

## Solomon had Adonijah killed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:4–5; 1 KINGS 1:5–10; LUKE 10:5–6"> One day Adonijah came to Solomon’s mother Bathsheba. She said to him, “Have you come because you want things to go well?” He replied, “Yes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 14:12; LUKE 7:40"> But then he said, “I have something to ask you to do.” She said, “Tell me what you want me to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 22:9–10; 1 CHRONICLES 28:5–7; 1 KINGS 1:25"> He said, “You know that all the Israeli people expected me to be their king because I am David’s oldest surviving son. But that did not happen. Instead, my younger brother became king, because that is what Yahweh wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:7; PSALMS 132:10"> Now I have one thing that I ask you to do. Please do not refuse to do it.” She replied, “Tell me what you want me to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:8; 1 KINGS 1:2–4; 2 SAMUEL 3:7"> He said, “Please ask King Solomon to give to me Abishag, the woman from Shunem town, to be my wife. I am sure that he will not refuse.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:15"> Bathsheba replied, “Okay, I will speak to the king for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 45:9; 1 KINGS 15:13; LEVITICUS 19:32"> So Bathsheba went to King Solomon, to tell him what Adonijah wanted. The king got up from his throne and went to greet her and bowed down to her. Then he sat on his throne again and asked someone to bring a chair for her. So she sat down at the king’s right side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:7–11; MATTHEW 11:24; JOHN 2:3–4"> Then she said, “I have one small thing which I want you to do; please do not say that you will not do it.” The king replied, “Mother, what do you want? I will not refuse you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 KINGS 1:3; 2 SAMUEL 16:21–22"> She said, “Allow Abishag to be given to your older brother Adonijah to be his wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 3:2; 1 CHRONICLES 3:5; MATTHEW 20:22"> The king replied angrily, “What? Are you requesting me to give Abishag to Adonijah? Does he want me to allow him to rule the kingdom, too? Because he is my older brother, does he think that he should be the king? Does he think that Abiathar should be the priest instead of Zadok, and that Joab should be the army commander instead of Benaiah because they supported him when he tried to become the king?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="RUTH 1:17; PSALMS 140:9; PSALMS 64:8"> Then Solomon solemnly promised, requesting Yahweh to listen, “I wish God would strike me and kill me if I do not cause Adonijah to be executed for requesting this!</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 22:10; 1 CHRONICLES 17:10; 1 CHRONICLES 17:17"> Yahweh has appointed me to be the king and placed me here to rule &#91;MTY&#93; as my father David did. He has promised that my descendants &#91;MTY&#93; will be the kings of Israel. So just as surely as Yahweh lives, I solemnly promise that Adonijah will be executed today!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:18; 1 KINGS 2:46; JUDGES 8:20–21"> So King Solomon gave orders to Benaiah to go and kill Adonijah, and Benaiah did that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:18; 1 SAMUEL 22:20–23; JEREMIAH 1:1"> Then Solomon said to Abiathar the priest, “Go to Anathoth town, to your land there. You deserve to be killed, but I will not execute you now, because you were the one who supervised the men who carried Yahweh’s Sacred Chest for David my father, and you endured all the troubles that my father endured.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:27–36; 1 SAMUEL 3:12–14; JEREMIAH 7:12–14"> So Solomon dismissed Abiathar from being the priest of Yahweh. By doing that he caused to happen what Yahweh had said many years previously at Shiloh, that some day he would get rid of the descendants &#91;MTY&#93; of Eli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 KINGS 1:50; 1 KINGS 1:7; 2 SAMUEL 18:2"> Joab had not supported Absalom when he tried to become the king, but he had supported Adonijah. So when Joab heard what had happened, he ran to the Sacred Tent, and he grasped the projections on the corners of the altar, because he knew that no one would kill him there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:25; EXODUS 21:14; 1 KINGS 2:46"> When someone told Solomon that Joab had run to the Sacred Tent and was standing alongside the altar, Solomon told Benaiah, “Go and execute Joab.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> So Benaiah went to the Sacred Tent and said to Joab, “The king commands you to come out!” But Joab replied, “No, I will die here.” So Benaiah went back to the king and reported what he had said to Joab, and what Joab had replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:8–9; NUMBERS 35:33; EXODUS 21:14"> The king replied to him, “Do what he has requested. Kill him and bury his body. If you do that, my descendants &#91;MTY&#93; and I will no longer be punished for what Joab did when he killed &#91;MTY&#93; two men who ◄were innocent/had not done anything that is wrong►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JUDGES 9:24; JUDGES 9:57; PSALMS 7:16"> But Yahweh will punish &#91;MTY&#93; Joab for attacking and killing Abner, the commander of the army of Israel, and Amasa, the commander of the army of Judah, two men who were more righteous and better &#91;DOU&#93; men than he is. My father David did not even know that Joab was planning to murder them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:25; 1 KINGS 2:32; LUKE 2:14"> Joab and his descendants will be punished &#91;IDM&#93; forever for his murdering Abner and Amasa. But things will forever go well for David’s descendants &#91;MTY&#93; who rule &#91;MTY&#93; as King David did.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:1; JOSHUA 15:61; 1 KINGS 2:25"> So Benaiah went into the Sacred Tent and killed Joab. Joab was buried on his property in the desert part of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:27; 1 CHRONICLES 24:3; 1 CHRONICLES 29:22"> Then the king appointed Benaiah to be the commander of the army instead of Joab, and he appointed Zadok to be the priest instead of Abiathar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 16:5–9; 1 KINGS 2:8–9; PROVERBS 20:26"> Then the king sent a messenger to summon Shimei, and the king said to him, “Build a house for yourself here in Jerusalem. Remain there and do not leave the city to go anywhere.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 1:16; 2 SAMUEL 15:23; JOSHUA 2:19"> Be sure that the day that you leave Jerusalem and go across the Kidron Brook, you will be executed, and it will be ◄your own fault/what you deserve►.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:4; 2 KINGS 20:19"> Shimei replied, “Your Majesty, what you say is good. I will do what you have said.” So Shimei remained in Jerusalem for several years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 21:10; 1 SAMUEL 27:2–3"> But three years later, two of Shimei’s slaves ran away. They went to stay with Maacah’s son Achish, the king of Gath city. When someone told Shimei that they were in Gath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKE 12:15; PROVERBS 15:27; 1 TIMOTHY 6:10"> he put a saddle on his donkey and got on it and went to Gath. He found his slaves staying with King Achish and brought them back home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> But someone told King Solomon that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath and had returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 15:4; 1 KINGS 2:36–38; LUKE 15:22"> So the king sent a soldier to summon Shimei and said to him, “I told you to solemnly promise, knowing that Yahweh &#91;MTY&#93; was listening, that you must not leave Jerusalem. I told you &#91;RHQ&#93;, ‘Be sure that if you ever leave Jerusalem, you will be executed.’ And you replied &#91;RHQ&#93; to me, ‘What you have said is good; I will do what you have said.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="ROMANS 13:5; 2 CHRONICLES 30:12; ECCLESIASTES 8:2"> So why did you not do what you solemnly promised to Yahweh? Why did you disobey what I commanded you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 16:5–13; PSALMS 7:16; EZEKIEL 17:19"> The king also said to Shimei, “You know all the evil things that you did to my father David. So Yahweh will now punish you for the evil things that you did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:5; 1 KINGS 2:24; Isaiah 9:6–7"> But Yahweh will bless me, and he will enable David’s descendants &#91;MTY&#93; to rule forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 1:1; 1 KINGS 2:12; 1 KINGS 2:45"> Then the king gave an order to Benaiah, and he went and killed Shimei. So Solomon was completely in control of the kingdom.</span>
</div>
