## Solomon’s governors and officials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:12; 2 CHRONICLES 9:30; 1 KINGS 12:19–20"> Solomon was the king who ruled all of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 18:21; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:28; 2 SAMUEL 8:15–18"> and these were his most important officials:<br />Zadok’s son Azariah was the priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:16; Isaiah 62:6; 1 CHRONICLES 18:15"> Shisha’s sons Elihoreph and Ahijah were the official secretaries. Ahilud’s son Jehoshaphat was the one who announced to the people the king’s decisions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:35; 1 KINGS 2:26–27"> Benaiah was the commander of the army. Zadok and Abiathar were also priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 27:33; 2 SAMUEL 15:37; 2 SAMUEL 16:16"> Nathan’s son Azariah was the administrator of the governors. Another of Nathan’s sons, Zabud, was a priest and the king’s chief advisor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:15; 2 SAMUEL 20:24; 1 KINGS 12:18"> Ahishar supervised the servants who worked in the palace. Abda’s son Adoniram supervised the men who were forced to do work for the government.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 27:1–15"> Solomon appointed twelve men, one to govern each of the regions in Israel. They also were required to provide food for the king and all the others who lived and worked in the palace. Each man was required to provide from his own region the food for one month each year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:33; JUDGES 19:1; JUDGES 17:1"> Their names were:<br />Ben-Hur, for the hilly area of the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:16; 1 SAMUEL 6:20; 1 SAMUEL 6:12"> Ben-Deker, for Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-Shemesh, and Elon-Bethhanan cities;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:35; JOSHUA 12:17; JOSHUA 17:2"> Ben-Hesed, for Arubboth and Socoh towns and the area near Hepher town;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:2; JOSHUA 12:23; JOSHUA 17:11"> Ben-Abinadab, who was married to Solomon’s daughter Taphath, for all the Dor district;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:11; JUDGES 5:19; JOSHUA 3:16"> Ahilud’s son Baana, for Taanach and Megiddo towns, and all the region near Zarethan city, and from Beth-Shan city south of Jezreel as far as Abel-Meholah town and Jokmeam city;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:4; NUMBERS 32:41; 1 KINGS 22:3"> Ben-Geber, for Ramoth city in the Gilead region, and for the villages in Gilead that belonged to Jair, who was a descendant of Manasseh, and the Argob area in the Bashan region. There were 60 large towns in that region altogether, each town with a wall around it and bronze bars across the gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:26; 2 SAMUEL 17:24; 2 SAMUEL 2:8"> Iddo’s son Ahinadab, for Mahanaim city east of the Jordan River;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:32–39; 2 SAMUEL 15:27; 1 SAMUEL 18:18"> Ahimaaz, who had married Solomon’s daughter Basemath, for the territory of the tribe of Naphtali;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:24–31; 2 SAMUEL 15:32"> Hushai’s son Baana, for the territory of the tribe of Asher and for Aloth town;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:17–23"> Paruah’s son Jehoshaphat, for the territory of the tribe of Issachar;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 KINGS 1:8; JOSHUA 18:20–28; ZECHARIAH 12:13"> Ela’s son Shimei, for the territory of the tribe of Benjamin;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:26–17; JOSHUA 13:9–12; NUMBERS 21:21–35"> Uri’s son Geber, for the Gilead region, the land that Sihon the king of the Amor people-group formerly ruled, and the Bashan area, which was the area that Og formerly ruled. In addition to all those, Solomon appointed one governor for the territory of the tribe of Judah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon’s kingdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 22:17; 1 KINGS 3:8; GENESIS 32:12"> There were as many people in Judah and Israel as there are grains of sand &#91;HYP&#93; on the seashore. They had plenty to eat and drink and they were happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 15:18; JOSHUA 1:4; PSALMS 68:29"> Solomon’s kingdom extended from the Euphrates River in the northeast to the Philistia area in the west and to the border of Egypt in the south. The conquered people in those areas paid taxes and were under Solomon’s control for the rest of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> To feed the people in his palace and his guests Solomon needed people to bring to him every day 150 bushels of fine flour and 300 bushels of wheat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:17–18"> ten cattle that were kept in stalls/barns, 20 cattle that were kept in pastures, 100 sheep, and ◄deer and gazelles and roebucks/three kinds of deer►, and poultry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 22:9; PSALMS 72:11; PSALMS 72:3"> Solomon ruled over all the area west of the Euphrates River, from Tiphsah city in the northeast to Gaza city in the southwest. He ruled over all the kings in that area. And there was peace between his government and the governments of nearby countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MICAH 4:4; ZECHARIAH 3:10; JUDGES 20:1"> All during the years that Solomon ruled, the people of Judah and Israel lived safely. Each family had its own grapevines and fig trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 1:14; 2 CHRONICLES 9:25; 2 SAMUEL 8:4"> Solomon had 4,000 stalls for the horses that pulled his chariots and 12,000 men who rode on horses (OR, in the chariots).</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> His twelve governors supplied the food that King Solomon needed for himself and for all those who ate in the palace. Each governor supplied food for one month each year. They provided everything &#91;LIT&#93; that Solomon required.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Esther 8:10; Esther 8:14; MICAH 1:13"> They also brought stalks of barley and wheat for the fast horses that pulled the chariots and for the other work horses. They brought it to the places where the horses were kept.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon’s wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 KINGS 3:12; JAMES 3:17; 1 KINGS 4:20"> God enabled Solomon to be extremely wise and to have great insight/understanding. He understood about more things than the number of grains of sand on the seashore &#91;HYP&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DANIEL 5:11–12; GENESIS 25:6; DANIEL 1:20"> He was wiser than all the wise men in Arabia and Mesopotamia and all the wise men in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:6; 1 CHRONICLES 15:19; 1 KINGS 3:12"> Ethan from Ezrah and Heman and Calcol and Darda and the sons of Mahol were considered to be very wise, but Solomon was wiser than all of them. People in all the nearby countries heard about Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 1:1–17; MATTHEW 13:35; PROVERBS 1:1–31"> He composed/wrote 3,000 ◄proverbs/wise sayings► and more than 1,000 songs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 KINGS 19:23; PSALMS 51:7; PSALMS 92:12"> He talked about various kinds of plants, from the huge cedar trees in Lebanon to the tiny hyssop plants that grow in cracks in walls. He also talked about wild animals and birds and reptiles and fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 9:23; 1 KINGS 10:1; 2 CHRONICLES 9:1"> People came from all over the world to hear the wise things that Solomon said. Many kings sent men to listen to him and then return and tell them what Solomon said.</span>
</div>
</div>
