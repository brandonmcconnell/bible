## Solomon requested wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:24; 1 KINGS 11:1; 1 KINGS 6:1–15"> Solomon went to see the king of Egypt. Solomon made an agreement with him to marry his daughter. They also made an alliance/agreement that their armies would not attack each other. Then Solomon brought the king’s daughter to live in the part of Jerusalem called ‘The City of David’. She lived there until Solomon’s workers had finished building his house and the temple of Yahweh and the wall around Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:43; 2 CHRONICLES 33:17; DEUTERONOMY 12:2–5"> At that time the temple of Yahweh had not yet been built, so the Israeli people were still offering sacrifices at many other places of worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 8:3; PSALMS 31:23; DEUTERONOMY 30:16"> Solomon loved Yahweh, and he obeyed all the instructions that his father David had given him. But he also offered sacrifices and burned incense at various altars.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 16:39; 2 CHRONICLES 1:3; 2 CHRONICLES 7:5"> One day the king went to Gibeon city to offer a sacrifice there, because that was where the most famous/important altar was. In previous years he had offered hundreds of sacrifices on that altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:2; JAMES 1:5–6; NUMBERS 12:6"> That night, Yahweh appeared to him in a dream. He asked Solomon, “What would you like me to give to you?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:4; 1 KINGS 1:48; 2 CORINTHIANS 9:11"> Solomon replied, “You always greatly and faithfully loved my father David, who served you well. You did that because he ◄was faithful to/faithfully obeyed► you and acted righteously and honestly toward you. And you have showed how greatly and faithfully you loved him by giving to him me, his son, and now I am ruling &#91;MTY&#93; as he did before he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 29:1; NUMBERS 27:17; JEREMIAH 1:6"> “Now, Yahweh my God, you have appointed me to be the king like my father was. But I am very young, like a little child &#91;MET&#93;. I do not know how to lead/rule my people at all &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 15:5; GENESIS 13:16; 1 CHRONICLES 21:2"> I am here among the people whom you have chosen. They are a very large group of people; there are very, very many of them, with the result that no one can count them &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JAMES 1:5; PROVERBS 16:16; HEBREWS 5:14"> So please enable me to think clearly, in order that I may rule your people well. Enable me to know what is good and what is evil. If you do not do that, I will never &#91;RHQ&#93; be able to rule this great group of people who belong to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:8"> Yahweh was very pleased that Solomon had requested that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 8:26; JAMES 4:2–3; PROVERBS 16:31"> God said to him, “You did not request that you live for many years or that you become very rich or that you be able to kill all your enemies. Instead, you have requested that I enable you to be wise, in order that you will be able to know and to do what is right while you govern these people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 JOHN 5:14–15; ECCLESIASTES 1:16; MATTHEW 12:42"> So, I will certainly do what you requested. I will enable you to be very wise &#91;DOU&#93;. The result will be that no one who has lived before you or who will live after you will be as wise as you are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EPHESIANS 3:20; MATTHEW 6:33; PROVERBS 3:16"> I will also give you things that you did not request: I will enable you to become very rich and honored, as long as you live. You will be richer and more honored than any other king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:2; PSALMS 91:16; 1 KINGS 15:5"> If you conduct your life &#91;IDM&#93; as I want you to, and if you obey all my laws and commandments, as your father David did, I will enable you to live for many years.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 41:7; 1 KINGS 8:65; Esther 1:3"> Then Solomon awoke, and he realized that God had spoken to him in a dream. Then he went to Jerusalem and stood in front of the Sacred Tent where the Sacred Chest was, and he offered many sacrifices that were completely burned on the altar and offerings to maintain fellowship with Yahweh. Then he made a feast for all his officials.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon made a very wise decision concerning two women

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 18:16; JUDGES 11:1; NUMBERS 27:2"> One day two prostitutes came and stood in front of King Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 13:7; GENESIS 43:20"> One of them said, “Your Majesty, this woman and I live in the same house. I gave birth to a baby while she was there in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Three days after my baby was born, this woman also gave birth to a baby. Only the two of us were in the house; there was no one else there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> “But one night this woman’s baby died because she accidentally rolled on top of her baby while sleeping and smothered it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOHN 3:20; MATTHEW 13:25; PSALMS 139:11"> So she got up at midnight and took my baby boy who was lying beside me while I was sleeping. She carried him to her bed and brought her dead baby and put it in my bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 21:7; 1 SAMUEL 1:23; LAMENTATIONS 4:3–4"> When I awoke the next morning and was ready to nurse my baby, I saw that it was dead. But when I looked at it closely in the morning light, I saw that it was not my baby!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 KINGS 3:23–24"> But the other woman said, “That is not true! The baby that is alive is mine, and the baby that is dead is yours!” Then the first woman said, “No, the dead baby is yours, and the one that is alive is mine!” And they continued to argue in front of the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then the king said, “Both of you are saying, ‘My baby is the one that is alive and the one that is dead is yours.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So he said to one of his servants, “Bring me a sword.” So the servant brought a sword to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:8"> Then the king said to the servant, “Cut the baby that is alive into two parts. Give one part to each of the women.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; JEREMIAH 31:20; 1 JOHN 3:17"> But the woman whose baby was alive loved her baby very much, so she said to the king, “No, Your Majesty! Do not allow him to kill the baby! Give her the child that is alive!” But the other woman said to the king, “No, cut it in half. Then it will not be her baby or my baby.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Then the king said to the servant, “Do not kill the baby. Give the baby to the woman who said ‘Do not cut the baby in half,’ because she is truly the baby’s mother.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DANIEL 2:47; 1 KINGS 3:9–12; COLOSSIANS 2:3"> All the Israeli people heard about what the king had decided, and they revered him. They realized that God had truly enabled him to be very wise, to judge people’s matters fairly.</span>
</div>
</div>
