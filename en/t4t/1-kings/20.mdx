## The Syrian army attacked Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:18; 1 KINGS 15:20; 1 KINGS 22:31"> Ben-Hadad, the king of Syria, gathered all his army, and he persuaded 32 other kings to join him with their armies and horses and chariots. They marched to Samaria city, the capital of Israel, and surrounded it, and prepared to attack it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 36:2–22; 2 KINGS 19:9; Isaiah 37:9–10"> Ben-Hadad sent messengers into the city to King Ahab, to say this to him: “This is what King Ben-Hadad says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 15:9; Isaiah 10:13–14"> ‘You must give to me all your silver and gold, your ◄good-looking/most beautiful► wives/women and strongest children.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 15:11–13; DEUTERONOMY 28:48; 1 SAMUEL 13:6–7"> The king of Israel replied to them, “Tell this to King Ben-Hadad: ‘I agree to do what you requested. You can have me and everything that I own.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The messengers told that to Ben-Hadad, and he sent them back with another message: “I sent a message to you saying that you must give me all your silver and gold and your wives and your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:34; JOEL 3:5; 2 KINGS 18:31–32"> But in addition to that, about this time tomorrow, I will send some of my officials to search your palace and the houses of your officials, and to bring to me everything that pleases them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:7; PROVERBS 6:14; 1 KINGS 8:1"> King Ahab summoned all the leaders of Israel, and said to them, “You can see/realize for yourselves that this man is trying to cause much trouble. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The leaders and all the other people said to him, “Do not pay any attention to him! Do not do what he is requesting!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So Ahab said to Ben-Hadad’s messengers, “Tell the king that I agree to give him the things that he first requested, but I do not agree to allow his officials to take anything that they want from my palace and from the houses of my officials.” So the messengers reported that to King Ben-Hadad, and they returned with another message from Ben-Hadad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:2; EXODUS 11:8; 2 KINGS 19:23–24"> In that message he said, “We will destroy your city completely, with the result that there will not be enough dust and rubble left for each of my soldiers to have one handful! I hope/wish that the gods will strike me dead if we do not do that!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:1; Isaiah 10:15–16; 1 SAMUEL 17:44–47"> King Ahab replied to the messengers, “Tell King Ben-Hadad that a soldier who is putting on his armor preparing to fight a battle &#91;MTY&#93; should not boast at that time; he should wait until after he wins the battle.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:9; 1 KINGS 20:16; PROVERBS 31:4–5"> Ben-Hadad heard that message while he and the other rulers were drinking wine in their temporary shelters. He told his men to prepare to attack &#91;MTY&#93; the city. So his men did that.</span>

## Ahab’s soldiers won their first victory over the Syrians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:28; EZEKIEL 20:22; Isaiah 7:1–9"> At that moment, a prophet came to King Ahab and said to him, “This is what Yahweh says: ‘Do not be at all afraid of &#91;RHQ&#93; the large enemy army that you see! I will enable your army to defeat them today, and you will know that it is I, Yahweh, who have the power to do what I say that I will do.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:50; GENESIS 14:14–16; 1 KINGS 18:44"> Ahab asked, “What group of our army will defeat them?” The prophet replied, “The young soldiers who are commanded by the district governors will do it.” The king asked, “Who should lead the attack?” The prophet replied, “You should!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 7:16; 1 SAMUEL 14:2; JUDGES 7:7"> So Ahab gathered the young soldiers who were commanded by the district governors. There were 232 of those men. Then he also summoned all the Israeli army. There were only 7,000 soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 10:16–17; HOSEA 4:11; 1 KINGS 16:9"> They started to attack at noon, while Ben-Hadad and the other rulers were getting drunk in their temporary shelters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:14–15; 1 KINGS 20:19"> The young soldiers advanced first. Some scouts who had been sent out by Ben-Hadad reported to him, “There are men coming out of Samaria city”!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 KINGS 14:8–12; 1 SAMUEL 14:11–12; PROVERBS 18:12"> He said, “It does not matter whether they are coming to fight against us or to ask for peace. Capture them, but do not kill them</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The young Israeli soldiers went out of the city to attack the Syrian army, and the other soldiers in the Israeli army followed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:16–17; 2 KINGS 19:36; ECCLESIASTES 9:11"> Each Israeli soldier killed a Syrian soldier. The rest of the Syrian army then ran away, and the Israeli soldiers pursued them. But King Ben-Hadad escaped riding his horse, along with some other men riding horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 KINGS 3:18; JUDGES 3:28; 2 KINGS 3:24"> Then the king of Israel went out of the city, and he and his soldiers captured all the other Syrian horses and chariots, and also killed a large number of Syrian soldiers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 11:1; 1 KINGS 20:26; 1 KINGS 20:13"> Then that same prophet went to King Ahab and said to him, “Go back and prepare your soldiers, and think carefully about what will be necessary for you to do, because the king of Syria with attack with his army again in the springtime of next year.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:23; 1 SAMUEL 4:8; PSALMS 50:21–22"> After the Syrian army was defeated, Ben-Hadad’s officials said to him, “The gods that the Israelis worship are gods who live in the hills. Samaria is built on a hill, and that is why their soldiers were able to defeat us. But if we fight against them in the plains/lowlands, we certainly will be able to defeat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:31; PROVERBS 21:30; 1 KINGS 20:16"> So, this is what you should do: You must remove the 32 kings who are leading your troops and replace them with army commanders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 10:3"> Then gather an army like the army that was defeated. Gather an army that has as many horses and chariots as the first army had. Then we will fight the Israelis in the plains/lowlands, and we will surely defeat them.”<br />Ben-Hadad agreed with them, and he did what they suggested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:17; 1 KINGS 20:22; 1 SAMUEL 4:1"> In the spring of the following year, he gathered his soldiers and marched with them to Aphek city east of Galilee Lake, to fight against the Israeli army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JUDGES 6:5; 1 SAMUEL 13:5–8; 1 SAMUEL 14:2"> The Israeli army was also gathered together, and they were equipped with the things that they needed for the battle. Then they marched out and formed two groups facing the Syrian army. Their army was very small; they resembled two small flocks of goats, whereas the Syrian army was very large and spread all over the countryside.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:13; 1 KINGS 17:18; EZEKIEL 12:16"> A prophet came to King Ahab and said to him, “This is what Yahweh says: ‘The Syrians say that I am a god who lives in the hills, and that I am not a god who lives in the valleys. So I will show that they are wrong by enabling your men to defeat this huge army &#91;IDM&#93; in the valley, and you will know that I, Yahweh, have done it.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 13:17; 2 CHRONICLES 28:6; PSALMS 10:16"> The two armies stayed in their tents for seven days, in groups that faced each other. Then, on the seventh day, they started fighting. The Israeli army killed 100,000 Syrian soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:25; 2 CHRONICLES 18:24; 1 KINGS 20:26"> The other Syrian soldiers ran away into Aphek City. Then the wall of the city collapsed and killed 27,000 more Syrian soldiers.<br />Ben-Hadad also escaped into the city, and hid in the back room of a house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:31; GENESIS 37:34; 1 KINGS 20:23"> His officials went to him and said, “We have heard a report that the Israelis act mercifully. So allow us to go to the king of Israel, wearing coarse sacks around our waists and ropes on our heads/necks to indicate that we will be his slaves. Perhaps if we do that, he will allow you to remain alive.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:3–6; JOB 12:17–18; DANIEL 5:20–23"> The king permitted them to do that, so they wrapped coarse sacks around their waists and put ropes on their heads/necks, and they went to the king of Israel and said to him, “Ben-Hadad, who greatly respects you, says, ‘Please do not kill me.’” Ahab replied, “Is he still alive? He is like a brother to me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKE 16:8; 2 KINGS 10:15; PROVERBS 25:13"> Ben-Hadad’s officials were trying to find out if Ahab would act mercifully, and when Ahab said “brother,” they ◄were optimistic/thought that Ahab would be merciful►. So they replied, “Yes, he is like your brother!” Ahab said, “Go and bring him to me.” So they went and brought Ben-Hadad to him. When Ben-Hadad arrived, Ahab told him to get in his chariot and sit with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:20; Isaiah 26:10; 1 KINGS 20:42"> Ben-Hadad said to him, “I will give back to you the towns that my father’s army took from your father. And I will allow you to set up market areas for your merchants in Damascus my capital, just as my father did in Samaria your capital.” Ahab replied, “Because you agree to do that, I will not execute you.” So Ahab made an agreement with Ben-Hadad, and allowed him to go home.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 KINGS 2:15; 1 KINGS 13:17–18; 2 KINGS 4:1"> Then Yahweh spoke to a member of a group of prophets and said to him, “Ask one of your fellow prophets to strike and wound you.” So he did what Yahweh told him to do. He said to one of his fellow prophets, ‘Strike me and wound me.’ But that man refused to do it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 KINGS 13:21–24; 1 KINGS 13:26; 1 SAMUEL 15:22–23"> So the prophet said to him, “Because you refused to obey what Yahweh told you to do, a lion will kill you as soon as you leave me.” And as soon as he left that prophet, a lion suddenly pounced on him and killed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="EXODUS 21:12; 1 KINGS 20:35"> Then the prophet found another prophet, and said to him, “Strike me!” So that man hit him very hard and injured him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:2; MATTHEW 6:16; 1 KINGS 22:30"> Then the prophet put a large bandage/cloth over his face so that no one would recognize him. Then he went and stood alongside the road, waiting for the king to come by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="2 KINGS 10:24; JOB 36:18; 1 PETER 1:18–19"> When the king passed by, the prophet cried out to him, saying “Your majesty, after I was wounded while I was fighting in a battle, a soldier brought to me one of our enemies that he had captured, and said to me, ‘Guard this man! If he escapes, you must pay me 3,000 pieces of silver, and if you do not pay that, you will be executed!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:5–7; MATTHEW 25:24–27; JOB 15:6"> But while I was busy doing other things, the man escaped!” The king of Israel said to him, “That is your problem! You yourself have said that you deserve to be punished.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:26; 2 SAMUEL 13:19; JOB 2:8"> The prophet immediately took off the bandage, and the king of Israel recognized that he was one of the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:31–37; 2 CHRONICLES 18:33–34; 1 KINGS 20:39"> And the prophet said to him, “This is what Yahweh says: ‘You have allowed that man Ben-Hadad to escape after I commanded you to be sure to execute him! Since you did not do that, you will be killed instead. And your army will be destroyed because you allowed some of his army to escape!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 KINGS 21:4; Esther 5:13; Esther 6:12–13"> The king went back home to Samaria, very angry and depressed/dejected.</span>
