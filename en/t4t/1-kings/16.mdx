## When Baasha ruled Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 20:34; 2 CHRONICLES 19:2; 1 KINGS 16:7"> During the time that Baasha was king of Israel, the prophet Jehu, Hanani’s son, gave Baasha this message that he had received from Yahweh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:34; 1 KINGS 14:7; 1 SAMUEL 2:8"> “You were very insignificant/unimportant &#91;IDM&#93; when I caused you to become the ruler of my Israeli people. But you have caused me to become very angry by doing &#91;IDM&#93; the kinds of evil things that King Jeroboam did. You have also caused me to become angry by causing my people to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:10; 1 KINGS 15:29–30; Isaiah 66:24"> So now I will get rid of you and your family &#91;MTY&#93;. I will do to you just like I did to Jeroboam and his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:11; 1 KINGS 21:24"> The bodies of those in your family who die in this city will not be buried; they will be eaten by dogs; and the corpses of those who die in the fields will be eaten by vultures.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:19; 1 KINGS 15:31; 2 CHRONICLES 16:1–6"> The other things that happened during the time that Baasha ruled Israel, and the things that he did, are written &#91;RHQ&#93; in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:17; 1 KINGS 15:21; 1 KINGS 16:13–14"> When Baasha died, he was buried in Tirzah, the capital city. Then his son Elah became king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:14; HOSEA 1:4; PSALMS 115:4"> Yahweh gave that message about Baasha and his family to the prophet Jehu. Baasha had done many things that Yahweh considered to be evil, which caused Yahweh to become angry. Baasha did the same kind of things that King Jeroboam and his family had done previously. Yahweh was also angry with Baasha because he had killed all of Jeroboam’s family.</span>
</div>
</div>

## When Elah ruled Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> After Asa had been the king of Judah for almost 26 years, Elah became the king of Israel. Elah ruled in Tirzah for only two years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:3; GENESIS 39:4; DANIEL 5:1–4"> A man named Zimri was one of Elah’s army officers. He commanded the drivers of half of Elah’s army’s ◄chariots/two-wheeled vehicles pulled by horses►. He made plans to kill Elah while Elah was in Tirzah, getting drunk at the house of a man named Arza. Arza was the man who took care of the things in the king’s palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:31"> Zimri went into Arza’s house and killed Elah. Then Zimri became the king of Israel. That was when Asa had been the king of Judah for 27 years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:22; 1 KINGS 16:3; 1 KINGS 15:29"> As soon as Zimri became king &#91;MTY&#93;, he killed all of Baasha’s family &#91;MTY&#93;. That included every male &#91;IDM&#93; in Baasha’s family and all of Baasha’s male friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:1; 1 KINGS 16:7; 2 CHRONICLES 10:15"> That was just what Yahweh told the prophet Jehu would happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:21; 1 KINGS 15:30; Isaiah 41:29"> Baasha and his son Elah had sinned and led the Israeli people to sin. They caused Yahweh, the God whom the Israeli people worshiped, to become angry, because they both urged the people to worship worthless idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Everything else that Elah did is written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
</div>
</div>

## When Zimri ruled Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:27; JOSHUA 19:44; JOB 20:5"> So Zimri became the king of Israel after Asa had been king of Judah for 27 years. But Zimri ruled in Tirzah for only seven days. The Israeli army was beseiging/surrounding Gibeah, a town which belonged to the Philistine people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 22:2; 2 KINGS 8:26; MICAH 6:16"> The men in the Israeli army camp heard that Zimri had secretly planned to kill King Elah, and then had killed him. So on that day the soldiers chose Omri, the commander of their army, to become the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JUDGES 9:50; LUKE 19:43–44; 2 KINGS 18:9–12"> The Israeli army was camped near Gibbethon city. When they heard what Zimri had done, they left there and went to Tirzah, and surrounded the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 17:23; 1 SAMUEL 31:4–5; JOB 2:9–10"> When Zimri realized that the city was about to be captured, he went into his palace and set it on fire. So the palace burned down, and he died in the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:26; 1 KINGS 14:16; 1 KINGS 12:28"> He died because he had sinned by doing the things that Yahweh considered to be evil. Jeroboam had led the Israeli people to sin, and Zimri sinned just like Jeroboam had sinned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:14; 1 KINGS 16:5; 1 KINGS 16:27"> All the other things that Zimri did, and the record of how he rebelled against King Elah, are written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
</div>
</div>

## When Omri ruled Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:25; EPHESIANS 4:3–5; PROVERBS 28:2"> After Zimri died, the Israeli people were divided. One group wanted Tibni, the son of Ginath, to be their king. The other group wanted Omri to be the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Those who supported Omri ◄were stronger than/defeated► those who supported Tibni. So Tibni was killed, and Omri became king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:8; 1 KINGS 16:29; 2 CHRONICLES 22:2"> Omri became king when Asa had been king of Judah for almost 31 years. Omri ruled Israel for 12 years. For the first six years he ruled in Tirzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 KINGS 13:32; 1 KINGS 16:32; ACTS 8:5–8"> Then he bought a hill from a man named Shemer and paid him about ◄150 pounds/70 kg.► of silver for it. Then Omri ordered his men to build a city on that hill, and he called it Samaria, to honor Shemer, the man who owned it previously.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MICAH 6:16; 1 KINGS 16:30–31; 1 KINGS 14:9"> But Omri did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil. He did more evil deeds than any of the kings who ruled Israel before he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:19; 1 KINGS 16:13; 1 KINGS 12:26–33"> When Jeroboam was previously the king, he had led the Israeli people to sin, and Omri committed the same kind of sins that Jeroboam did. The Israeli people caused Yahweh, the God the Israeli people had worshiped, to become very angry, because they worshiped worthless idols.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:20; 1 KINGS 16:14; 1 KINGS 16:5"> Everything that Omri did, and the record of the victories that his army won, are written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> After Omri died, he was buried in Samaria, and his son Ahab became king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## When Ahab ruled Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Ahab became king of Israel when Asa had ruled Judah for almost 38 years. Ahab ruled in Samaria city for 22 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:25; 1 KINGS 21:25; 1 KINGS 14:9"> Ahab did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil. He did more evil things than any of the kings who ruled Israel before he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:16; 2 KINGS 10:18; JUDGES 18:7"> He committed the same kind of sins that Jeroboam did, but he did things that were worse than the things that Jeroboam did. He married a woman named Jezebel, the daughter of Ethbaal, the king of Sidon city. Then Ahab started to worship Baal, the god that the Canaan people-group worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="2 KINGS 10:21; 2 KINGS 10:26–27"> He built a temple in Samaria in order that the Israeli people could worship Baal there, and he put an altar there for making sacrifices to Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:6; 1 KINGS 21:25; 2 KINGS 21:3"> He also made an idol that represented Asherah, Baal’s wife. He did many more things that caused Yahweh to become angry. He did more evil things than any of the previous kings of Israel had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:26; JOSHUA 23:14–15; MATTHEW 24:35"> During the years that Ahab ruled, Hiel, a man from Bethel city, rebuilt Jericho city. But when he started to rebuild the city, his oldest son Abiram died. And when the city was finished, while Hiel was building the city gates, his youngest son Segub died. They died just like Yahweh had told Joshua would happen to the sons of anyone who would rebuild Jericho.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
