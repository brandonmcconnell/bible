## Yahweh’s reply to Solomon’s prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:11–6; 1 KINGS 9:19; 1 KINGS 7:51"> After Solomon’s workers had finished building the temple and his palace and everything else that Solomon wanted them to build,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 KINGS 3:5; 2 CHRONICLES 7:12; 1 KINGS 11:9"> Yahweh appeared to him in a dream a second time, like he had appeared to him at the city of Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 10:17; 1 JOHN 5:14; 2 KINGS 20:5"> Yahweh said to him,<br />“I heard what you prayed and what you pleaded for me to do. I have set apart/dedicated this temple which your workers have built to be the place where people will worship me forever. I will always watch over it and protect it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:5; 1 KINGS 3:14; 1 KINGS 11:6"> “And as for you, if you conduct your life as I want you to, like your father David did, and if you very sincerely obey all the statutes and laws that I have commanded you to obey,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:4; 1 KINGS 6:12; 2 SAMUEL 7:16"> I will do what I promised your father that I would do: I promised him that Israel would always be ruled by his descendants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:14–16; 1 CHRONICLES 28:9; 2 CHRONICLES 15:2"> “But if you or your descendants turn away from me and disobey the commands and decrees that I have given to you, and if you start to worship other gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 44:14; JEREMIAH 24:9; DEUTERONOMY 4:26"> I will remove my Israeli people from the land that I have given to them. I will also abandon this temple that I have set apart/dedicated to be the place where people should worship me. Then people everywhere will despise the people of Israel and make fun of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:24–26; JEREMIAH 22:8–9; 2 CHRONICLES 7:21"> This temple will become a heap of ruins. Everyone who passes by will be astonished when they see it, and they will be shocked and say, ‘Why has Yahweh done this to this land and to this temple?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:19; JEREMIAH 5:19; DEUTERONOMY 29:25–28"> And other people will reply, ‘It happened because the Israeli people abandoned Yahweh their God, the one who brought their ancestors out of Egypt. They started to accept and worship other gods. And that is why Yahweh has caused them to experience all these disasters.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Other things that Solomon did

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 KINGS 6:37–1; 2 CHRONICLES 8:1–18; 1 KINGS 9:1"> Solomon’s workers labored for 20 years to build the temple and the palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 8:2; 2 CHRONICLES 2:16; 2 CHRONICLES 2:8–10"> Hiram, the king of Tyre had arranged for his workers to give Solomon all the cedar and pine logs and all the gold that he needed for this work. After it was all finished, King Solomon gave to Hiram 20 cities in the Galilee region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:34; JUDGES 14:3"> But when Hiram went from Tyre to Galilee to see the cities that Solomon had given to him, he was not pleased with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:27; AMOS 1:9; 1 KINGS 5:1–2"> He said to Solomon, “My friend, those cities that you gave me are worthless!” So, that area is still called ‘Worthless’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:11; 1 KINGS 10:10; 1 KINGS 9:28"> Hiram paid Solomon only five tons of gold for those cities.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:11; 1 KINGS 9:24; 2 SAMUEL 5:9"> This is a record of the work that King Solomon forced men to do. He forced them to build the temple and his palace and the terraces/landfills on the east side of the city, and the wall around Jerusalem, and to rebuild the cities of Hazor and Megiddo and Gezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 KINGS 3:1; 1 KINGS 9:24; 1 KINGS 7:8"> The reason they needed to rebuild Gezer was that the army of the king of Egypt had attacked Gezer and captured it. Then they had burned the houses in the city and killed all the people of the Canaan people-group who lived there. The king of Egypt gave that city to his daughter as a gift when she married Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOSHUA 16:3; JOSHUA 21:22; 2 CHRONICLES 8:4–18"> So Solomon’s workers also rebuilt Gezer, and they also rebuilt Lower Beth-Horon city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:44; 2 CHRONICLES 8:4"> They also rebuilt Baalath and Tamar towns in the desert in the southern part of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:1; ECCLESIASTES 6:9; EXODUS 1:11"> They also built cities where they kept the supplies for Solomon, the places where his horses and chariots were kept. They also built everything else that Solomon wanted them to build, in Jerusalem and in Lebanon, and in other places in the area over which he ruled.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 23:23; GENESIS 15:19–21; DEUTERONOMY 7:1–3"> There were many people who belonged to the Amor people-group, the Heth people-group, the Periz people-group, the Hiv people-group, and the Jebus people-group who were not killed when the Israelis captured their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:12; JUDGES 1:21; JOSHUA 15:63"> Their descendants still lived in Israel. It was those people whom Solomon forced to become his slaves to build all those places, and they are still slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:39; 1 KINGS 4:1–27; 1 SAMUEL 8:11–12"> But Solomon did not force any Israeli people to become slaves. Some Israelis became soldiers and army officers and commanders and drivers of his chariots and soldiers who rode on horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 KINGS 5:16; 2 CHRONICLES 8:10; 2 CHRONICLES 2:18"> There were 550 officials who supervised the slaves who worked to build all those places.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:8; 2 SAMUEL 5:9; 2 CHRONICLES 32:5"> After Solomon’s wife, who was the daughter of the king of Egypt, moved from the place outside Jerusalem called ‘The City of David’ to the palace that Solomon’s workers built for her, Solomon told his workers to fill in the slopes on the east side of the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 8:12–13; 2 CHRONICLES 8:16; EXODUS 23:14–17"> Three times each year Solomon brought to the temple offerings that were completely burned on the altar and offerings to restore fellowship with Yahweh. He also brought incense to be burned in the presence of Yahweh.<br />And so his men finished building the temple.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:48; NUMBERS 33:35; DEUTERONOMY 2:8"> King Solomon’s workers also built a fleet of ships at Ezion-Geber city, which is near Elath city, on the shore of the Gulf of Aqaba, in the land belonging to the Edom people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 KINGS 10:11; 1 KINGS 5:6; 1 KINGS 5:9"> King Hiram sent some very expert sailors to work on the ships with Solomon’s workers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 8:18; 1 CHRONICLES 29:4; 1 KINGS 10:11"> They sailed to the Ophir region and brought back to Solomon about 16 tons of gold.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
