## Jezebel arranged for Naboth to be killed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 6:33; 2 CHRONICLES 28:22; JOSHUA 19:18"> King Ahab had a palace in Jezreel city. Near the palace was a vineyard owned by a man from that city whose name was Naboth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 8:14; JAMES 1:14–15; 1 TIMOTHY 6:9"> One day, Ahab went to Naboth and said to him, “Your vineyard is close to my palace. I would like to buy it, so that I can plant some vegetables there. In exchange, I will give you a better vineyard somewhere else, or if you prefer, I will pay you for your vineyard.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 36:7; LEVITICUS 25:23; EZEKIEL 46:18"> But Naboth replied, “That land belonged to my ancestors, so I want to keep it. I hope that Yahweh will never allow me to give that land to you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:43; JAMES 1:14; JONAH 4:9"> So Ahab became very sullen and angry because of what Naboth had said. He went home and lay down on his bed. He turned his face toward the wall, and he refused to eat anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:31; GENESIS 3:6; 1 KINGS 19:2"> His wife Jezebel came in and asked him, “Why are you so depressed? Why are you refusing to eat anything?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Esther 5:9–14; JAMES 4:2–7; 1 TIMOTHY 6:9–10"> Ahab replied, “I talked to Naboth, that man from Jezreel. I told him that I wanted his vineyard. I said, ‘I will buy it from you, or I will give you another vineyard for it.’ But he refused to let me have it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 8:14; MICAH 7:3; 2 SAMUEL 13:4"> His wife replied, “You are &#91;RHQ&#93; the king of Israel, so you can get whatever you want Get up, and eat some food and do not worry about what Naboth said. I will get Naboth’s vineyard for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:5; 2 KINGS 10:11; Ezra 4:7–8"> Then Jezebel wrote some letters, and she signed Ahab’s name on them. She used his official seal to seal them. Then she sent them to the older leaders and other important men who lived in Naboth’s town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:4; MATTHEW 23:13; LUKE 20:47"> This is what she wrote in the letters: “Proclaim a day when all the people will gather together and ◄fast/abstain from eating food►. Give to Naboth an important place to sit among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 22:28; ACTS 6:11; LEVITICUS 24:15–16"> Then find two men who always cause trouble. Give them places to sit opposite/facing him. Tell these men to testify that they heard Naboth say things that criticized God and the king. Then take Naboth out of the city and kill him by throwing stones at him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ACTS 4:19; LEVITICUS 19:15; 2 KINGS 10:6–7"> The leaders received the letters and did what Jezebel had written in the letters for them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 58:4; 1 KINGS 21:8–10"> They declared a day on which the people would all ◄fast/abstain from food►. And they gave Naboth a seat in a place where honored people sat, in front of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:26; DEUTERONOMY 22:24; ECCLESIASTES 10:20"> Two men who always caused trouble sat opposite/facing Naboth. While everyone was listening, they stated that they had heard Naboth say things that criticized God and the king. So the people seized Naboth. They took him outside the city and killed him by throwing stones at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 8:14; 2 SAMUEL 11:14–24; ECCLESIASTES 5:8"> Then those leaders sent a message to Jezebel, saying “We have executed Naboth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:10–16; PROVERBS 4:17; 1 KINGS 21:7"> When Jezebel found out that Naboth had been killed, she told Ahab, “Naboth is dead. Now you can go and take possession of the vineyard that he refused to sell to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:12–14; ROMANS 1:32; 2 SAMUEL 11:25–27"> When Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, he got up and went to the vineyard to claim that he now owned it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 9:12; Isaiah 26:21; 2 KINGS 5:26"> Then Yahweh spoke to Elijah the prophet. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 KINGS 13:32; 2 CHRONICLES 22:9"> “Go to Samaria and talk to Ahab, the king of Israel. He is in the vineyard of a man named Naboth. He has gone there to claim that he now owns it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:38; 2 SAMUEL 12:9; HABAKKUK 2:9"> Tell Ahab that this is what I, Yahweh, say to him: ‘You have murdered Naboth and taken his land.’ So I am telling you this: ‘In the same place where Naboth died and dogs came and licked up Naboth’s blood, you will die and the dogs will lick up your blood, too!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:17; ROMANS 7:14; 1 KINGS 21:25"> So when Elijah met with Ahab, Ahab said to him, “You, my enemy, have found me!” &#91;RHQ&#93; Elijah answered, “Yes, I have found you. You have never stopped doing the things that Yahweh says are wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:10; 1 SAMUEL 25:34; 2 KINGS 10:1–7"> So this is what Yahweh says to you: ‘I will soon get rid of you. I will kill you, and I will also kill every male in your household, including those who are slaves and those who are not slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:3; 1 KINGS 14:16; 1 KINGS 16:11"> Your family will all be killed, just like the family of King Jeroboam and like the family of King Baasha were killed. I will get rid of you because you have caused me to become very angry, and you have also led the Israeli people to sin.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:10; 2 KINGS 9:30–37; 1 KINGS 21:25"> Yahweh has also told me that your wife Jezebel will be killed, and dogs in Jezreel will eat her corpse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:4; 1 KINGS 14:11; JEREMIAH 15:3"> The dead bodies of the members of your family who die in this city will not be buried, they will be eaten by dogs, and the corpses of those who die in the fields will be eaten by vultures.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:14; 1 KINGS 21:20; 1 KINGS 16:30–33"> There was no man no man like Ahab who always did things that Yahweh considered to be evil. His wife Jezebel incited/urged him to do many of those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 KINGS 21:11; GENESIS 15:16; LEVITICUS 18:25–30"> The most abominable/detestable thing that Ahab did was to worship idols, just like the Amor people-group had done. And that is why Yahweh took the Amor people’s land from them and gave it to the Israelis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 KINGS 6:30; GENESIS 37:34; 2 SAMUEL 3:31"> After Elijah finished talking to Ahab, Ahab tore his clothes to show that he was sorry for all the sins that he had committed. He put on rough clothes that were made from sacks, and he refused to eat anything. He even wore those rough clothes made from sacks when he slept, to show that he was sorry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then Yahweh said this to Elijah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 PETER 3:9; MICAH 7:18; PSALMS 86:15"> “I have seen &#91;RHQ&#93; that Ahab is now very sorry for all the evil things that he has done. So the things that I promised to do to his family will not happen while he is still alive. I will cause them to happen after his son becomes king. Then those things will happen to his family.” &#91;MTY&#93;</span>
