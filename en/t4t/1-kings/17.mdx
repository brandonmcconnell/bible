## Crows fed Elijah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 4:25–26; JAMES 5:17; LUKE 1:17"> Elijah was a prophet who lived in Tishbe town in the Gilead region. One day he went to King Ahab and said to him, “Yahweh is the God whom we Israelis worship and the God whom I worship and serve. Just as certainly as Yahweh lives, there will be no dew or rain for the next few years, if I do not command rain to fall!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:1; JEREMIAH 7:1; HOSEA 1:1–2"> Then Yahweh said to Elijah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="REVELATION 12:6; JEREMIAH 36:26; PSALMS 83:3"> “Because you have made the king angry with you, escape from the king and, go to the east, to the Kerith Brook, east of where it flows into the Jordan River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 17:9; PSALMS 147:9; JOB 38:41"> You will be able to drink water from the brook, and you will be able to eat what the crows bring to you, because I have commanded them to bring food to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:9; MATTHEW 16:24; JOHN 15:14"> So Elijah did what Yahweh commanded him to do. He went and camped alongside Kerith Brook.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 78:23–24; MATTHEW 6:31–33; JEREMIAH 37:21"> Crows bought bread and meat to him every morning and every evening, and he drank water from the brook.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 54:10; Isaiah 40:30–31"> But after a while, the water in the brook dried up, because rain did not fall anywhere in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 22:14; 1 KINGS 17:2; HEBREWS 13:6"> Then Yahweh said to Elijah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 4:26; OBADIAH 1:20; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:7"> “Go and live in Zarephath village, near Sidon city. There is a widow there who will give you food to eat. I have already told her what to do about that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 4:7; GENESIS 24:17; GENESIS 21:15"> So Elijah did what God said. He went to Zarephath. As he arrived at the gates of the village, he saw a widow who was gathering sticks. He said to her, “Would you please bring me a cup of water?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:35–40; 1 KINGS 18:4; HEBREWS 13:2"> While she was going to get it, he called out to her, “Bring me a piece of bread, too!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:33–34; 1 KINGS 17:1; GENESIS 21:16"> But she replied, “Your God knows that what I am telling you is true: I do not have even one piece of bread in my house. I have only a handful of flour in a jar, and a little olive oil in a jug. I was gathering a few sticks to use these to make a fire and cook one more meal, and then after my son and I eat that, we will die from hunger.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:33; MALACHI 3:10; GENESIS 22:1–2"> But Elijah said to her, “Do not be worried! Go home and do what you said that you were going to do. But first, bake me a little loaf of bread and bring it to me. After you do that, take what is left and prepare some food for you and your son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:36–38; MATTHEW 14:17–20; 2 KINGS 4:42–44"> I know that you will be able to do that, because Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis worship, says this: ‘There will always be plenty of flour and olive oil left in your containers, until the time when I send rain again and the crops grow again!’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:17; MARK 12:43; MATTHEW 15:28"> So the woman did exactly what Elijah told her to do. And she and her son and Elijah had enough food every day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:28–30; JOHN 4:50–51; LUKE 1:37"> because the flour in the jar was never finished, and the jug of oil was never empty. That happened just like Yahweh had told Elijah that it would happen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elijah restored to life the widow’s son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 PETER 1:7; DANIEL 5:23; JOHN 11:14"> Some time later, the woman’s son became sick. He continued to get worse, and finally he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 5:8; JOHN 2:4; 2 KINGS 3:13"> So the woman went to Elijah and said to him, “You are a prophet; so why have you done this to me? Have you come here to punish me for my sins by causing my son to die?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 KINGS 4:32; 2 KINGS 4:10; ACTS 9:37"> But Elijah replied, “Give your son to me.” So she gave her son to him, and he took the boy’s body from her and carried it up the steps to the room where he stayed. He laid the boy’s body on his bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JAMES 5:15–18; MATTHEW 21:22; JOSHUA 7:8–9"> Then Elijah cried out to Yahweh, “O Yahweh my God, this widow has kindly allowed me to stay in her home. So why have you brought this tragedy to her and caused her son to die?” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ACTS 20:10; ACTS 9:40; 2 KINGS 4:33–35"> Then Elijah stretched himself on top of the boy’s body and called out to Yahweh saying, “Yahweh my God, please allow this boy to become alive again!” He did this three times.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:39; REVELATION 11:11; HEBREWS 11:35"> Yahweh heard what Elijah prayed, and he caused the boy to become alive again &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 KINGS 4:36–37; ACTS 9:41; LUKE 7:15"> Elijah carried the boy down the steps and gave him to his mother. He said, “Look, your son is alive!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOHN 16:30; JOHN 2:11; JOHN 3:2"> The woman said to Elijah, “Now I know for certain that you are a prophet and that the words that you speak are truly from Yahweh!”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
