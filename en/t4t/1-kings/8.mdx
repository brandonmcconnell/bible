## The sacred chest was brought to the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 5:2–10; 1 CHRONICLES 15:25; 2 SAMUEL 6:12–17"> Solomon then summoned to Jerusalem all the elders of Israel, all the leaders of the tribes, and the leaders of the clans. He wanted them to join in bringing Yahweh’s Sacred Chest from Zion Hill to the temple, where it was in the part of the city called ‘The City of David’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:34; 2 CHRONICLES 7:8–10; ZECHARIAH 14:16–19"> So all the Israeli leaders came to King Solomon during the Festival of Living in Temporary Shelters, in October.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:9; JOSHUA 3:6; NUMBERS 4:15"> When they had all arrived, the priests lifted up the Sacred Chest</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 3:4; 2 CHRONICLES 1:3; EXODUS 40:2–33"> and brought it to the temple. The descendants of Levi who assisted the priests helped them to carry to the temple the Sacred Tent and all the sacred things that had been in the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:13; 1 CHRONICLES 16:1; 1 KINGS 8:62–63"> Then King Solomon and many of the Israeli people who had gathered in front of Yahweh’s Sacred Chest sacrificed a huge amount of sheep and oxen. No one was able to count the sacrifices because there were so many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 6:19; 2 SAMUEL 6:17; 1 KINGS 6:27"> The the priests then brought the Sacred Chest into the Very Holy Place in the temple, and they placed it under the wings of the statues of the winged creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> The wings of those statues spread out over the Sacred Chest and over the poles by which it was carried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 25:13–15; EXODUS 37:4–5; JOSHUA 4:9"> The poles were so long that the ends of the poles could be seen by people who were standing at the entrance to the Most Holy Place, but they could not be seen by people standing outside the temple. Those poles are still there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 25:21; HEBREWS 9:4; EXODUS 40:20"> The only things that were in the Sacred Chest were the two stone tablets that Moses had put there at Sinai Mountain, where Yahweh made an agreement with the people after they left Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 40:34–35; 2 CHRONICLES 7:1–2"> When the priests came out of the temple, suddenly it was filled with a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 43:4–5; EZEKIEL 44:4; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:18"> It was the glory/radiance of Yahweh that filled the temple, with the result that the priests were not able to continue their work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 97:2; 2 CHRONICLES 6:1–11; LEVITICUS 16:2"> Then Solomon prayed this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yahweh, you have placed the sun in the sky,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you have decided that you would live in very dark clouds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:13; PSALMS 132:13–14; HEBREWS 9:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have built for you a magnificent temple,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a place for you to live in forever.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:18; 1 KINGS 8:55–56; 2 CHRONICLES 30:18–20"> Then, while all the people stood there, the king turned around and faced them, and he asked God to bless them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 1:68; 1 CHRONICLES 29:20; 1 CHRONICLES 29:10"> He said, “Praise Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis belong! By his own power he has done what he promised to give to my father David. What he promised was this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:1; 1 KINGS 8:29; 2 SAMUEL 7:4–8"> ‘From the time that I brought my people out of Egypt, I have never chosen any city in Israel in which a temple should be built for my people to worship me there. But I chose you, David, to rule my people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:2–3; 1 CHRONICLES 22:7; 1 CHRONICLES 28:2"> Then Solomon said, “My father David wanted to build a temple in order that we Israeli people could worship Yahweh our God there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 6:7–9; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:12"> But Yahweh said to him, ‘You have wanted to build a temple for me, and what you wanted to do was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:5; 1 CHRONICLES 22:8–10; 1 CHRONICLES 28:6"> However, you are not the one who I want to build it. It is one of your sons, who I want to build a temple for me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 28:5–6; PHILIPPIANS 1:6; 1 KINGS 8:15"> And now Yahweh has done what he promised to do. I have become the king of Israel to succeed my father, and I am ruling my people, like Yahweh promised. I have arranged for this temple to be built for us Israelis to worship Yahweh, the God, to whom we Israelis belong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:9; DEUTERONOMY 31:26; 1 KINGS 8:5–6"> I have also provided a place in the temple for the Sacred Chest in which are the two stone tablets on which are engraved the Ten Commandments of the agreement that Yahweh made with our ancestors when he brought them out of Egypt.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon’s prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 9:5; 1 KINGS 8:54; EXODUS 9:33"> Then Solomon stood in front of the altar which was facing the Israeli people who had gathered there. He spread out his arms toward heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:22; DEUTERONOMY 7:9; DANIEL 9:4"> and he prayed, “Yahweh, the God whom we Israeli people belong to/worship, there is no god like you up in heaven or down here on the earth. You solemnly promised that you would faithfully love us. And that is what you have done for us who earnestly do what you want us to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:16; 1 KINGS 8:15; 2 SAMUEL 7:12"> You have done the things that you promised my father David, who served you very well, that you would do. Truly, you promised to do these things for him, and today we see that by your power you have done them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:4; 1 KINGS 9:4–6; JEREMIAH 33:17–26"> So now, Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis belong to/worship, please do the things that you promised my father that you would do. You told him that there would always be some of his descendants who would rule Israel, if they would conduct their lives as he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:25–29; Isaiah 41:17; 1 SAMUEL 1:17"> So now, God of us Israeli people, cause what you promised to do for my father David, who served you well, to happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 2:6; Isaiah 66:1; JEREMIAH 23:24"> But God, you cannot really live on the earth. There is surely not enough space for you in the sky, or even in the heaven. So there is surely not enough space for you to live in this temple that my workers have built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 88:1–2; PSALMS 5:1; LUKE 18:1"> But Yahweh, my God, please listen to me while I am praying to you this day,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 6:20; 1 KINGS 8:16; 2 CHRONICLES 7:15–16"> Please keep protecting this temple night and day. This is the place about which you have said, ‘I will always be there.’ I request that you listen to me whenever I turn toward this temple and pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:39; 1 KINGS 8:34; 1 KINGS 8:36"> I request that when I pray to you and your people pray to you while they turn toward this place, that in your home in heaven you will hear us and forgive us for the sins that we have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 22:8–11; 2 CHRONICLES 6:22–23; LEVITICUS 5:1"> If someone is accused of doing something wrong to another person, and they bring him to your altar outside this holy temple, and if he says, ‘I did not do that; may God punish me if I am not telling the truth,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:1; Isaiah 3:10–11; ROMANS 2:6–10"> then you listen from heaven, and decide who is telling the truth. Then punish the person who is guilty as he deserves to be punished, and declare that the other person is innocent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:17; DEUTERONOMY 28:25; JONAH 3:10"> And when your Israeli people are defeated by their enemies in a battle because they have sinned against you, and if they are forced to go to some distant country, if they turn away from their sinful behavior and turn toward this temple and admit that you have justly punished them, and if they plead that you will forgive them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:4–9; DANIEL 9:12; GENESIS 13:15"> listen to them from heaven, and forgive your Israeli people for the sins that they have committed, and bring them back to this land that you gave to our ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:19; DEUTERONOMY 11:17; 1 KINGS 8:33"> And when you do not allow any rain to fall on the land because your people have sinned against you, if they turn toward this temple and admit that you have justly punished them, if they turn away from their sinful behavior and humbly pray to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 27:11; 1 SAMUEL 12:23; JEREMIAH 6:16"> listen to them from heaven, and forgive your Israeli people for the sins that they have committed. Teach them the right way to conduct their lives. Then send rain on this land that you have given to your people to belong to them permanently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:21–22; LEVITICUS 26:16; 2 CHRONICLES 20:9"> And when the people of this land experience famines, or if there is a plague/illness that causes many people to become sick, or if their crops are destroyed by very hot winds or by mildew or by locusts or grasshoppers, or when their enemies surround any of their cities in order to attack them, if any of those bad things happen to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="PSALMS 73:21–22; 1 KINGS 8:22; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> when your Israeli people earnestly plead with you knowing that they are suffering because they have sinned, and if they stretch out their arms toward this temple and pray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 28:9; JEREMIAH 17:10; ACTS 1:24"> listen to them from your home in heaven, and forgive them, and help them. You are the only one who knows what people are thinking, so act toward everyone as they deserve,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PSALMS 130:4; REVELATION 19:5; DEUTERONOMY 6:2"> in order that your people may then have an awesome respect for you, all the years that they live in this land that you gave to our ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 KINGS 10:1–2; ACTS 8:27–40; EXODUS 18:8–12"> There will be some foreigners who do not belong to your Israeli people who have come here from countries far away because they have heard that you are very great and that you perform great miracles. If they come here to this temple to worship you and pray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="PSALMS 102:15; 1 SAMUEL 17:46; PSALMS 22:27"> in your home in heaven, listen to their prayers, and do for them what they request you to do. Do that so that all the people-groups in the world will know about you and revere you, like we your Israeli people do. And then they will know that this temple that I have caused to be built to honor you, is where you should be worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 6:34; PSALMS 78:67–69; 1 KINGS 8:16"> And if you send your people to go to fight against their enemies, if they pray to you, wherever they are, if they turn toward this city that you have chosen and toward this temple that I have caused to be built for you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="GENESIS 18:25; PSALMS 9:4; JEREMIAH 5:28"> listen in heaven to their prayers; listen to what they plead for you to do, and assist them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:20; PROVERBS 20:9; 1 JOHN 1:8–10"> It is true that everyone sins. So, if your people sin against you, and you become angry with them, you may allow their enemies to defeat them and capture them and take them away to their enemies’ country, even to countries that are far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="PSALMS 106:6; Nehemiah 1:6–11; Nehemiah 9:26–30"> If that happens, while they are in those countries to which they were forced to go, if they sincerely repent and plead with you there saying ‘We have sinned and have done things that are very wicked,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="DANIEL 6:10; JEREMIAH 29:12–14; DEUTERONOMY 4:29"> if they very sincerely repent, and turn toward this land that you gave to our ancestors, and toward this city that you have chosen to be the place where we should worship you, and toward this temple that I have caused to be built for you, and if they pray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="2 KINGS 19:19; 1 KINGS 8:45; ZECHARIAH 1:15–16"> then from your home in heaven listen to them while they plead for your help, and assist them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="PSALMS 106:46; 2 CHRONICLES 30:9; ACTS 7:10"> Forgive them for all the sins that they have committed against you, and cause their enemies to be kind to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:10; JEREMIAH 11:4; DEUTERONOMY 4:20"> Do not forget that the Israelis are your people; they are your special possession; you brought our ancestors out of Egypt where they were greatly suffering as though they were in a blazing furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="PSALMS 145:18; 1 KINGS 8:29; PSALMS 86:5"> I request that you always listen to your Israeli people and to me, their king, and heed their prayers whenever they call out to you to help them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="EXODUS 19:5–6; DEUTERONOMY 14:2; DEUTERONOMY 32:9"> You chose them from all the other people-groups in the world to belong to you, which is what you told Moses to tell them when you brought our ancestors out of Egypt.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:1; 2 CHRONICLES 6:12–13; LUKE 22:45"> After Solomon had finished praying and pleading to Yahweh for his help, he stood up in front of the altar where he had been kneeling. He lifted up his arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:14; 2 SAMUEL 6:18; NUMBERS 6:23–26"> Then he asked God to bless all the Israeli people. He prayed loudly, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:14–15; JOSHUA 21:44–45; LUKE 21:33"> “Praise Yahweh, who has given us his people peace, like he promised that he would do. He has done every one of the good things that he promised to Moses, the man who served him very well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:5; DEUTERONOMY 31:6; DEUTERONOMY 31:8"> I pray that our God will be with us like he was with our ancestors, and that he will never abandon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="PSALMS 119:36; EZEKIEL 36:26–27; JEREMIAH 31:33"> I pray that he will cause us to loyally serve him, to conduct our lives as he wants us to, and to obey all his commandments and statutes and laws that he gave to our ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="PSALMS 141:2; JOHN 17:9; JOHN 17:20–24"> I pray that Yahweh our God will never forget these words that I have prayed, pleading for his help; I pray that he will think about them by day and by night. I pray that he will always help us Israeli people and me, giving us the things that we need day by day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:35; JEREMIAH 10:10–12; 1 KINGS 18:39"> If he does that, all the people-groups in the world will know that he is the only one who is God, and that there is no other one who is God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:4; 2 KINGS 20:3; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:1"> I pray that you, his people, will always be fully committed to Yahweh, and that you will obey all his statutes and commands, like you are doing now.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Solomon dedicated the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:17–19; Ezra 6:16–17; 2 CHRONICLES 7:4–10"> Then the king and all the Israeli people who were there offered sacrifices to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Ezra 6:16–17; 2 CHRONICLES 15:11; Nehemiah 12:27"> They sacrificed 22,000 cattle and 120,000 sheep to maintain fellowship with Yahweh. Then the king and all the people dedicated the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 4:1; 2 CHRONICLES 7:7"> On that day, the king also dedicated/set apart the middle part of the courtyard that was in front of the temple. Then he offered sacrifices that would be completely burned in the courtyard, the offerings of grain and the fat of the animals that were sacrificed to maintain fellowship with Yahweh. They sacrificed them there because the bronze altar was not big enough for all those sacrifices to be burned on it that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="NUMBERS 34:5; NUMBERS 34:8; 1 KINGS 8:2"> Then Solomon and all the Israeli people celebrated the Festival of Living in Temporary Shelters for seven days. There was a huge crowd of people there, some of whom had come from distant places like Hamath in the far north and the border of Egypt in the far south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:4; ACTS 2:46; ZECHARIAH 9:17"> On the eighth day, Solomon sent the people to their homes. They all praised him and went home happy because of all the things that Yahweh had done to bless King David and his Israeli people.</span>
