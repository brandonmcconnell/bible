## Jeroboam’s son died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 20:5; 1 KINGS 13:33–34; 1 SAMUEL 31:2"> At that time, Jeroboam’s son Abijah became very sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 28:8; 1 KINGS 11:29–38; 2 SAMUEL 14:2"> Jeroboam said to his wife, “Disguise yourself, in order that no one will recognize that you are my wife. Then go to Shiloh city, where the prophet Ahijah lives. He is the one who predicted/prophesied that I would become the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 9:7–8; 1 KINGS 13:7; 2 KINGS 4:42"> Take with you ten loaves of bread and some small flat cakes, and a jar of honey, and give them to him. Tell him about our son, and he will tell you what will happen to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:29; 1 SAMUEL 3:2; 1 SAMUEL 4:15"> So his wife went to Shiloh, to Ahijah’s house. Ahijah was unable to see, because he was very old and had become blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 KINGS 6:8–12; PROVERBS 21:30; 2 KINGS 4:27"> But before she got there, Yahweh told Ahijah that Jeroboam’s wife was coming to inquire about their son, who was very sick. And Yahweh told Ahijah what he should tell her. When she came to him, she pretended to be another woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 5:17–28; ACTS 5:9–10; LUKE 20:20–23"> But when Ahijah heard her footsteps as she entered the doorway, he said to her, “Come in, wife of Jeroboam! ◄Why do you pretend that you are someone else?/It will not help you to pretend that you are someone else.► &#91;RHQ&#93; Yahweh has given me bad news to tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:2; 2 SAMUEL 12:7–8; 1 SAMUEL 15:16"> Go and tell Jeroboam that this is what Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis worship, says to you: ‘I chose you from among the common people and enabled you to become the king of my Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:5; 1 KINGS 11:33–38; 2 CHRONICLES 28:1"> I took most of the kingdom of Israel away from David’s descendants and gave it to you. But you have not been like David, who served me very well. He obeyed all my commandments very sincerely, doing only things that I considered to be right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:35; Nehemiah 9:26; PSALMS 50:17"> But you have done more evil things/deeds than all those who ruled before you. You have rejected me, and you have caused me to become very angry by making metal images of other gods so that you and others could worship them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:36; 1 KINGS 21:21; 2 KINGS 14:26"> ‘So, I am going to cause terrible things to happen to your family. I will cause all your male descendants to die, young ones and old ones. I will completely get rid of your family &#91;MTY&#93;. I will get rid of your family &#91;MTY&#93; just like a man completely burns dung to cook his food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:4; JEREMIAH 15:3; 1 KINGS 21:23–24"> The corpses of any members of your family who die in cities will be eaten by dogs. And the corpses of any members of your family who die in the open fields will be eaten by vultures. This will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said that it will happen.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:3; 1 KINGS 14:16–17; 2 KINGS 1:6"> So go back home. And as soon as you enter the city, your son will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 19:3; 2 CHRONICLES 12:12; JOB 19:28"> All the Israeli people will mourn for him and bury him. He is the only one of Jeroboam’s family who will be buried properly, because he is the only one of Jeroboam’s family with whom Yahweh is pleased.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:27–29; EZEKIEL 7:2–7; EZEKIEL 12:22–28"> Furthermore, Yahweh will appoint for himself a king to rule over Israel who will get rid of Jeroboam’s descendants. And that will start to happen today!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:15–16; PSALMS 52:5; 2 KINGS 15:29"> Yahweh will punish the people of Israel; he will shake them like the wind shakes the reeds that grow in a stream. He will expel the Israeli people from this good land that he gave to our ancestors. He will scatter them into countries east of the Euphrates River, because they have caused him to become very angry by worshiping statues of the goddess Asherah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:34; 1 KINGS 16:2; 1 KINGS 13:34"> Yahweh will abandon the Israeli people because of the sins that Jeroboam committed, sins which led the Israeli people to commit them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:33; 1 KINGS 16:15; 1 KINGS 16:23"> Jeroboam’s wife returned home to Tirzah city, the new capital of Israel. And just as she entered her house, her son died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:13"> All the Israeli people mourned for him and buried him, which is what Yahweh had told his servant, the prophet Ahijah, would happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 13:2–20; 1 CHRONICLES 27:24; 1 KINGS 16:20"> Everything else that Jeroboam did, and the record of wars that his army fought, and how he ruled, is written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 14:12; PSALMS 3:5; 1 KINGS 11:43"> Jeroboam ruled for 22 years; then he died &#91;EUP&#93; and his son Nadab became king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rehoboam died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 12:13; 1 KINGS 11:36; 1 KINGS 14:31"> Solomon’s son Rehoboam ruled Judah. He was 41 years old when he started to rule, and he ruled for 17 years. He ruled in Jerusalem, which is the city that Yahweh chose out of all the tribes of Israel to be the place where he should be worshiped &#91;MTY&#93;. Rehoboam’s mother’s name was Naamah; she was from the Ammon people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 12:1; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:22; PSALMS 78:58"> The people of Judah did things that Yahweh considered to be evil. They caused him to become angry because they committed more sins than their ancestors had committed: They worshiped many other gods instead of worshiping only Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:2; Isaiah 57:5; 2 KINGS 17:9–10"> They built places to worship those gods; on high hills, and under big trees they set up stone pillars to worship the goddess Asherah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:17; 1 CORINTHIANS 6:9; 1 KINGS 15:12"> Also, there were male prostitutes at these places of worship. The Israeli people did the same disgraceful things that had been done by the people whom Yahweh had expelled while the Israelis were advancing through the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:40; 2 CHRONICLES 12:2–4; 2 CHRONICLES 12:9"> When Rehoboam had been ruling for almost five years, King Shishak of Egypt came with his army to attack Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:18; 2 CHRONICLES 9:15–16; 1 KINGS 7:51"> They took away all the valuable things in the temple and in the king’s palace, including the gold shields that Solomon’s workers had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 8:11; 1 SAMUEL 22:17; 1 KINGS 18:46"> King Rehoboam’s workers made bronze shields to replace them and entrusted them to officers who guarded the entrance to the king’s palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 12:11"> Every time that the king went into the temple, those guards carried those shields; and when he left the temple they returned the shields to the storeroom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:45; 1 KINGS 15:23; 1 KINGS 14:19"> Everything else that Rehoboam did is written &#91;RHQ&#93; in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Judah’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:24; 1 KINGS 12:21; 1 KINGS 15:6–7"> There were wars continually between the armies of Rehoboam and Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 12:16; MATTHEW 1:7; 1 KINGS 15:3"> Then Rehoboam died &#91;EUP&#93;, and he was buried in the part of Jerusalem called ‘The City of David’, where his ancestors were buried. Then his son Abijah became the king.</span>
</div>
