## Jehoshaphat talked with Ahab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:34"> For almost three years there was no war between Syria and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:24; 1 KINGS 22:1; 1 KINGS 22:41"> Then King Jehoshaphat, who ruled Judah, went to visit King Ahab, who ruled Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:43; 1 KINGS 4:13; JOSHUA 21:38"> While they were talking, Ahab said to his officials, “Do you realize that the Syrians are still occupying our city of Ramoth in the Gilead region? And we are doing nothing to retake that city!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 KINGS 3:7; EPHESIANS 5:11; 2 JOHN 1:11"> Then he turned to Jehoshaphat and asked, “Will your army join my army to fight against the people of Ramoth and retake that city?”<br />Jehoshaphat replied, “Certainly I will do whatever you want, and you may command my troops. You may take my horses into battle, also.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:5–6; 2 CHRONICLES 18:4–5; JEREMIAH 42:2–6"> Then he added, “But we should ask Yahweh first, to find out what he wants us to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:19; JEREMIAH 23:14–17; EZEKIEL 13:7–16"> So Ahab summoned about 400 of his prophets together, and he asked them, “Should my army go to fight the people in Ramoth and retake that city, or not?”<br />They answered, “Yes, go and attack them, because God will enable your army to defeat them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 KINGS 3:11–13; 2 CHRONICLES 18:6–7"> But Jehoshaphat asked, “Is there no prophet of Yahweh here whom we can ask?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="AMOS 5:10; JOHN 3:19–21; JOHN 7:7"> The King of Israel replied, “There is one man we can talk to. We can ask him if he can find out what Yahweh wants. His name is Micaiah; he is the son of Imlah. But I hate him, because when he ◄prophesies/tells what God says to him► he never says that anything good will happen to me. He always predicts that bad things will happen to me.”<br />Jehoshaphat replied, “King Ahab, you should not say that!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:32; Isaiah 39:7; 1 KINGS 22:26–27"> So the king of Israel told one of his officers to summon Micaiah immediately.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Micaiah prophesied against Ahab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 5:1; Esther 6:8–9; 1 KINGS 18:29"> The king of Israel and the king of Judah were wearing their ◄royal robes/robes that showed that they were kings►. They were sitting on thrones at the place where people threshed grain, near the gate of Samaria city. All of Ahab’s prophets were standing in front of the kings, ◄prophesying/predicting what was going to happen►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:18–21; DEUTERONOMY 33:17; EZEKIEL 22:27–28"> One of them, whose name was Zedekiah, the son of Kenaanah, had made from iron something that resembled horns of a bull. Then he proclaimed to Ahab, “This is what Yahweh says: ‘With horns like these your army will keep attacking the Syrians like a bull attacks another animal &#91;MET&#93;, until you completely destroy them!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:22; 1 KINGS 22:6–15; 1 KINGS 22:32–36"> All the other prophets of Ahab agreed. They said, “Yes! If you go up to attack Ramoth city in the Gilead region, you will be successful, because Yahweh will enable you to defeat them!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MICAH 2:6–7; PSALMS 11:1; Isaiah 30:10–11"> Meanwhile, the messenger who went to summon Micaiah said to him, “Listen to me! All the other prophets are predicting that the king’s army will defeat the Syrians. So be sure that you agree with them and say ◄what will be favorable/that the king’s army will be successful►.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:13; NUMBERS 22:18; 2 CHRONICLES 18:12–13"> But Micaiah replied, “As surely as Yahweh lives, I will tell him only what Yahweh tells me to say.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:27; JUDGES 10:14; MATTHEW 26:45"> When Micaiah came to Ahab, Ahab asked him, “Micaiah, should we go to fight against the people of Ramoth, or not?”<br />Micaiah replied, “Sure, go! Yahweh will enable your army to defeat them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ACTS 19:13; MATTHEW 26:63; JOSHUA 6:26"> But King Ahab realized that Micaiah was ◄lying/being sarcastic►, so he said to Micaiah, “I have told you &#91;RHQ&#93; many times that you must always tell only the truth when you say what Yahweh has revealed to you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:17; MATTHEW 9:36; 1 KINGS 22:34–36"> So Micaiah said to him, “The truth is that in a vision I saw all the troops of Israel scattered on the mountains. They seemed to be like sheep that did not have a shepherd. And Yahweh said, ‘Their master has been killed. So tell them all to go home peacefully.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:8; PROVERBS 10:24; LUKE 11:45"> Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, “I told you &#91;RHQ&#93; that he never predicts that anything good will happen to me! He always predicts that bad things will happen to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 2:1; JOB 1:6; DANIEL 7:9–10"> But Micaiah continued, saying, “Listen to what Yahweh showed to me! In a vision I saw Yahweh sitting on his throne, with all the armies of heaven surrounding him, on his right side and on his left side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:9; JOB 12:16; JEREMIAH 4:10"> And Yahweh said, ‘Who can persuade Ahab to go to fight against the people of Ramoth, in order that he may be killed there?’<br />Some suggested one thing, and others suggested something else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 2:1; 1 KINGS 22:23; JOB 1:6–7"> Finally one evil spirit came to Yahweh and said, ‘I can do it!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JUDGES 9:23; 1 SAMUEL 19:9; 1 SAMUEL 16:14"> Yahweh asked him, ‘How will you do it?’<br />The spirit replied, ‘I will go and inspire all of Ahab’s prophets to tell lies.’ Yahweh said, ‘You will be successful; go and do it!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:9; MATTHEW 24:24–25; Isaiah 44:20"> So now I tell you that Yahweh has caused all of your prophets to lie to you. Yahweh has decided that something terrible will happen to you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ACTS 23:2; LAMENTATIONS 3:30; MICAH 5:1"> Then Zedekiah walked over to Micaiah and slapped him on his face. He said, “Do you think that Yahweh’s Spirit left me in order to speak to you?” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:30; 2 PETER 2:1; REVELATION 19:20"> Micaiah replied, “You will find out for yourself which of us Yahweh’s Spirit has truly spoken to on the day when you go into a room of some house to hide from the Syrian troops!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> King Ahab commanded his soldiers, “Seize Micaiah and take him to Amon, the governor of this city, and to my son Joash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 16:10; 2 CHRONICLES 18:25–27; REVELATION 2:10"> Tell them that I have commanded that they should put this man in prison and give him only bread and water. Do not give him anything else to eat until I return safely from the battle!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:29; MICAH 1:2; ACTS 13:10–11"> Micaiah replied, “If you return safely, it will be clear that it was not Yahweh who told me what to say to you!” Then he said to all those who were standing there, “Do not forget what I have said to King Ahab!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ahab died in the battle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 18:28; 1 KINGS 22:2–9"> So the King of Israel and the King of Judah led their armies to Ramoth, in the Gilead region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:22; JEREMIAH 23:24; 1 SAMUEL 28:8"> King Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, “I will put on different clothes, in order that no one will recognize that I am the king. But you should wear your ◄royal robe/robe that shows that you are a king►.” So Ahab disguised himself, and they both went into the battle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:24; 2 CHRONICLES 18:30; JEREMIAH 16:6"> The King of Syria told this to his thirty-two men who were driving the chariots: “Attack only the king of Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:20; JONAH 2:1–2; PSALMS 130:1–4"> So when the men who were driving the Syrian chariots saw Jehoshaphat wearing ◄his royal robes/clothes that showed he was the king►, they pursued him. They shouted, “There is the king of Israel!” But when Jehoshaphat cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 76:10; 1 KINGS 22:31"> they realized that he was not the king of Israel. So they stopped pursuing him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:23; MICAH 6:13; 1 SAMUEL 17:49"> But one Syrian soldier shot an arrow at Ahab, without knowing that it was Ahab. The arrow struck Ahab between the places where the parts of his armor joined together. Ahab told the driver of his chariot, “Turn the chariot around and take me out of here! I have been severely wounded!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:28; 1 KINGS 20:42"> The battle continued all the day. Ahab was sitting propped up in his chariot, facing the Syrian troops. The blood from his wound ran down to the floor of the chariot. And late in the afternoon he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 KINGS 14:12; 1 KINGS 22:31; JUDGES 21:24"> Just as the sun was going down, someone among the Israeli troops shouted, “The battle is ended! Everyone should return home!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So king Ahab died, and they took his body in the chariot to Samaria city and buried his body there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 KINGS 21:19; MATTHEW 24:35; JEREMIAH 44:21–23"> They washed his chariot alongside the pool in Samaria, a pool where the prostitutes bathed. And dogs came and licked the king’s blood, just like Yahweh had predicted would happen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="AMOS 3:15; EZEKIEL 27:6; PSALMS 45:8"> The account/record of the other things that happened while Ahab was ruling, and about the palace decorated with much ivory that they built for him, and the cities that were built for him, was written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:12; 2 KINGS 1:17; 2 KINGS 1:2"> When Ahab died, his body was buried where his ancestors were buried. Then his son Ahaziah became king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehoshaphat ruled in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 20:31; 1 KINGS 22:2; 2 CHRONICLES 17:1"> Before King Ahab died, when he had been ruling in Israel for four years, Asa’s son Jehoshaphat started to rule in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="2 KINGS 1:17; 1 KINGS 15:2; 1 KINGS 15:10"> Jehoshaphat was thirty-five years old when he started to rule, and he ruled in Jerusalem for twenty-five years. His mother was Azubah, the daughter of Shilhi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:14; 2 KINGS 12:3; PROVERBS 4:27"> Jehoshaphat was a good king, just like his father Asa had been. He did things that pleased Yahweh. But while he was king, he did not remove all the pagan altars that the people had built on the hilltops. So the people continued to offer sacrifices to idols on those altars and burned incense there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:2; 2 CHRONICLES 19:2; 2 KINGS 8:18"> Jehoshaphat also made a peace agreement with the king of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:29; 1 KINGS 11:41; 2 CHRONICLES 20:34"> All the other things that happened while Jehoshaphat was ruling, and the great things that he did and the victories his troops won, are written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Judah’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:12; 1 KINGS 14:24; DEUTERONOMY 23:17"> Jehoshaphat’s father Asa had tried to expel the male prostitutes that stayed at the pagan shrines, but some of them were still there. Jehoshaphat got rid of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:14; 2 KINGS 3:9; 2 KINGS 8:20"> At that time, there was no king in Edom; a ruler who had been appointed by Jehoshaphat ruled there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:26; 1 KINGS 10:22; 1 KINGS 9:28"> Jehoshaphat ordered some Israeli men to build a fleet/group of ships to sail south to the Ophir region to get gold. But they were wrecked at Ezion-Geber/Elath, so the ships never sailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Before the ships were wrecked, Ahab’s son Ahaziah suggested to Jehoshaphat, “Allow my sailors to go with your sailors,” but Jehoshaphat refused.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 21:1; 1 KINGS 22:40; 1 KINGS 15:24"> When Jehoshaphat died, his body was buried where his ancestors were buried in Jerusalem, the city where King David had ruled. Then Jehoshaphat’s son Jehoram became king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ahaziah ruled in Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 KINGS 1:17; 1 KINGS 22:40; 1 KINGS 15:25"> Before King Jehoshaphat died, when he had been ruling in Judah for 17 years, Ahab’s son Ahaziah began to rule in Israel. Ahaziah ruled in Samaria for two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:26; 1 KINGS 21:25; REVELATION 3:20"> He did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil, doing the evil things that his father and mother had done and the evil things that Jeroboam had done—the king who had led all the Israeli people to sin by worshiping idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:30–32; Isaiah 65:3; 2 KINGS 3:2"> Ahaziah bowed in front of Baal’s idol and worshiped it. That caused Yahweh, the God who was the true God of the Israeli people, to become very angry, just as Ahaziah’s father had caused Yahweh to become angry.</span>
</div>
</div>
