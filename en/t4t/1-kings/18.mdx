## Elijah met with Obadiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 4:25; JAMES 5:17; DEUTERONOMY 28:12"> For almost three years there was no rain in Samaria. Then Yahweh said this to Elijah: “Go and meet with King Ahab and tell him that I will soon send rain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 KINGS 6:25; PROVERBS 28:1; LEVITICUS 26:26"> So Elijah went to talk to Ahab.<br />In Samaria there was almost no food for anyone to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; 1 KINGS 16:9; MATTHEW 10:28"> There was a man there named Obadiah. He was in charge of the king’s palace. He greatly revered Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:13; MATTHEW 10:40–42; REVELATION 17:4–6"> One time when Queen Jezebel had tried to kill all Yahweh’s prophets, Obadiah hid 100 of them in two caves. He put 50 prophets in each cave, and he brought food and water to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:5–6; JOEL 1:18; JOEL 2:22"> By this time, the ◄famine/lack of food and water► had become very severe in Samaria. So Ahab summoned Obadiah and said to him, “We must check/look near every spring and in every valley to see if we can find enough grass to give to some of my horses and mules, so that they will not all die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:3"> So they both started walking throughout the land. Obadiah went in one direction by himself, and Ahab went in another direction by himself.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 KINGS 1:6–8; GENESIS 44:20; Isaiah 60:14"> While Obadiah was walking along, he saw Elijah coming toward him. Obadiah recognized Elijah and bowed down in front of him and said, “Is it really you, Elijah, my master?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ROMANS 13:7; 1 KINGS 18:3; 1 PETER 2:17–18"> Elijah replied, “Yes. Now go and tell Ahab, your master, that I am here.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 5:21; 1 KINGS 18:12; 1 KINGS 17:18"> Obadiah objected. He said, “Sir, I have not &#91;RHQ&#93; harmed you at all. So why are you sending me back to Ahab? He will kill me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 KINGS 17:1; 1 KINGS 1:29; 1 KINGS 18:15"> Yahweh your God knows that I am telling the truth when I solemnly declare that King Ahab has searched in every kingdom all over the world &#91;HYP&#93; to find you. Each time that some king said to him, ‘Elijah is not here,’ Ahab demanded that the king of that country solemnly swear/declare that the king was telling the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:8; 1 KINGS 18:14"> Now you say to me, ‘Go and tell your master that Elijah is here!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 KINGS 2:16; ACTS 8:39; EZEKIEL 8:3"> But as soon as I leave you, the Spirit of Yahweh will carry you away, and I will not know where he will take you. So when I tell Ahab that you are here and he comes to me and he does not find you here, he will kill me! But I do not deserve to die because I have revered Yahweh since I was a boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:4; MATTHEW 25:35; PSALMS 18:21–24"> My master, have you not heard about what I did when Jezebel wanted to kill all of Yahweh’s prophets? I hid 100 of them in two caves and took food and water to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:28"> Now, sir, you say, ‘Go and tell your master that Elijah is here.’ But if I do that, and he comes and you are not here, he will kill me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 KINGS 17:1; 1 KINGS 18:10; LUKE 2:13–14"> But Elijah replied, “Yahweh, the Almighty One whom I serve &#91;IDM&#93;, knows that I am telling the truth as I solemnly declare that I will go to meet with Ahab today.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elijah killed the prophets of Baal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So Obadiah went to tell Ahab that Elijah had come. Ahab went to meet with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:25; 1 KINGS 21:20; ACTS 16:20"> When he saw Elijah, he said to him, “Is that you, the one who causes trouble for the people of Israel?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 15:2; 1 KINGS 9:9; JEREMIAH 2:19"> Elijah replied, “It is not I who have caused trouble for the people of Israel! You and your family are the ones who have caused trouble! You have refused to obey Yahweh’s commands, and you have worshiped the idols of Baal instead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:26; 1 KINGS 16:33; 2 PETER 2:1"> So now, command all the Israeli people to come to Carmel Mountain, and be sure to bring all the 450 prophets who worship Baal and the 400 prophets who worship the goddess Asherah, the ones to whom your wife Jezebel brings food and water.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:9"> So Ahab summoned all his prophets and all the other Israeli people to the top of Carmel Mountain, and Elijah went up there too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:24; JOSHUA 24:15; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:21–22"> Then Elijah stood in front of them and said, “How long &#91;RHQ&#93; are you going to be undecided about who is truly God? If Yahweh is truly God, then worship/serve him. If Baal is truly God, then worship/serve him!” But the people said nothing in reply, because they were afraid of what Jezebel would do to them if they admitted that they worshiped Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:10; 1 KINGS 19:14; 2 TIMOTHY 4:3–4"> Then Elijah said to them, “I am the only true prophet of Yahweh who is left, but Baal has 450 prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Bring two bulls. The prophets of Baal may choose the one that they want. They must kill it and cut it into pieces and lay the pieces on the wood that is on the altar that they have made. But they must not light a fire under the wood. I will kill the other bull and cut it in pieces and lay the pieces on the altar that I make.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:38; 1 CHRONICLES 21:26; 2 CHRONICLES 7:1"> Then they must call to their god &#91;MTY&#93;, and I will call to Yahweh. The god who answers by lighting a fire to the wood that is on that altar is the true God!”<br />Then all the people thought that Elijah’s suggestion was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, “You call to Baal first, because there are many of you. Choose one of the bulls and prepare it, and then call to your god. But do not light a fire under the wood!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:5; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:4; MATTHEW 6:7"> So they killed one of the bulls and cut it up and placed the pieces on the altar. Then they called out to Baal all morning. They shouted, “Baal, answer us!” But no one answered. There was no reply at all &#91;DOU&#93;.<br />Then they danced wildly around the altar that they had made.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 78:65–66; Isaiah 41:23; ECCLESIASTES 11:9"> About noontime, Elijah started to make fun of them. He said, “Surely Baal is a god, so it seems that you must shout louder! Perhaps he is thinking about something, or perhaps he has gone to the toilet. Or perhaps he is traveling somewhere, or perhaps he is asleep and you need to wake him up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:28; DEUTERONOMY 14:1; MARK 5:5"> So they shouted louder. Then, doing one of the things that they frequently did when they worshiped Baal, they slashed/cut themselves with knives and swords until a lot of blood flowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:26; 1 SAMUEL 18:10; EXODUS 29:39"> They continued calling out to Baal all afternoon. But there was no reply, no answer, no god who paid attention.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 33:16; 1 KINGS 19:14; 1 KINGS 19:10"> Then Elijah called to the people saying, “Come closer!” So they all crowded around him. Then Elijah repaired the altar of Yahweh that had previously been ◄ruined/torn down► by the prophets of Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:34; GENESIS 35:10; GENESIS 32:28"> Then he took twelve large stones, each one to represent one of the Israeli tribes. The names of those tribes were given to them by their ancestor Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:17; EXODUS 20:24–25; JUDGES 21:4"> With these stones he rebuilt Yahweh’s altar. Then around the altar he dug a little ditch that was large enough to hold about ◄three gallons/12 liters► of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 22:9; JUDGES 6:20; LEVITICUS 1:6–8"> He piled wood on top of the stones. He killed the bull and cut it in pieces. Then he laid the pieces on top of the wood. Then he said, “Fill four large jars with water, and pour the water on top of the pieces of meat and the wood.” So they did that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 8:21; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:2"> Then he said, “Do the same thing again!” So they did it again. Then he said “Do it a third time!” So they did it again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:32; 1 KINGS 18:38"> As a result, the water flowed down below the altar and filled the ditches.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:29; 2 KINGS 19:19; EXODUS 3:6"> When it was time to offer the evening sacrifices, Elijah walked close to the altar and prayed. He said, “Yahweh, you who are the God that our ancestors Abraham and Isaac and Jacob worshiped, prove today that you are the God whom the Israeli people should worship, and prove that I am your servant. Prove that I have done all these things because you told me to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DANIEL 9:17–19; LUKE 1:16–17; EZEKIEL 36:25–27"> Yahweh, answer me! Answer me in order that these people will know that you, Yahweh God, have the power to do what you say that you will do, and that you have caused them to trust in &#91;IDM&#93; you again!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 21:26; LEVITICUS 9:24; 2 CHRONICLES 7:1"> Immediately a fire from Yahweh flashed down from the sky. The fire burned up the pieces of meat, the wood, the stones, and the dirt that was around the altar. It even dried up all the water in the ditch!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:24; 2 CHRONICLES 7:3; 1 KINGS 18:21"> When the people saw that, they prostrated themselves on the ground and shouted, “Yahweh is God! Yahweh is God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:20; JUDGES 4:7; DEUTERONOMY 13:5"> Then Elijah commanded them, “Seize all the prophets of Baal! Do not allow any of them to escape!” So the people seized all the prophets of Baal, and took them down the mountain to the Kishon Valley, and Elijah killed them all there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elijah prayed for rain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:1; ECCLESIASTES 9:7; 1 KINGS 17:1"> Then Elijah said to Ahab, “Go and get something to eat and drink. But do it quickly, because it is soon going to rain very hard!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKE 6:12; DANIEL 9:7; JOSHUA 7:6"> So Ahab and his men left to prepare a big meal. But Elijah went back up to the top of Carmel Mountain and prayed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:3; LUKE 18:7; LUKE 18:1"> Then he said to his servant, “Go and look out toward the sea, to see if there are any rain clouds.” So his servant went and looked, and came back and said, “I do not see anything.” This happened six times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LUKE 12:54; JOB 8:7; ZECHARIAH 4:10"> But when the servant went the seventh time, he came back and said, “I saw a very small cloud above the sea. As I extend my arm, the cloud is about the size of my hand/fist.”<br />Then Elijah shouted to him, “Go and tell King Ahab to get his chariot ready and go home immediately! If he does not do that, the rain will stop him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:39–40; JOSHUA 17:16; 1 KINGS 21:1"> Very soon the sky was full of black clouds. There was a strong wind, and then it began to rain very hard. Ahab got into his chariot and started to return to Jezreel city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:14; EZEKIEL 1:3; 2 KINGS 9:1"> Yahweh gave extra strength to Elijah. He tucked his cloak into his belt in order to run fast, and he ran ahead of Ahab’s chariot all the way 17 miles/26 km. to Jezreel.</span>
</div>
