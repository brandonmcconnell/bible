## Elijah fled to the desert and then to Sinai Mountain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:40; 1 KINGS 21:25; 1 KINGS 21:5–7"> When Ahab got home, he told his wife Jezebel what Elijah had done. He told her that Elijah had killed all the prophets of Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="RUTH 1:17; 2 KINGS 6:31; 1 KINGS 20:10"> So Jezebel sent this message to Elijah: “By this time tomorrow I will have killed you, just like you killed all those prophets of Baal. If I do not do that, I hope the gods will kill me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 21:31; MATTHEW 26:70–74; MATTHEW 26:56"> When Elijah received her message, he was afraid. So taking his servant with him, he fled in order that he would not be killed. He went far south to Beersheba, in Judah. He left his servant there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JONAH 4:3; NUMBERS 11:15; JONAH 4:8"> Then he went by himself further south into the desert. He walked all day. He sat down under a broom tree and prayed that Yahweh would allow him to die. He said, “Yahweh, I’ve had enough. So allow me to die, because for me to live is no better than being with my ancestors who have died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 34:7; ACTS 12:7; HEBREWS 13:5"> Then he lay down under the broom tree and slept. But while he was sleeping, an angel touched him and woke him up and said to him, “Get up and eat some food!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 17:6; MATTHEW 4:11; MATTHEW 6:32"> Elijah looked around and saw some bread which had been baked on hot stones, and he also saw a jar of water. So he ate some bread and drank some water and lay down to sleep again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 103:13–14; DEUTERONOMY 33:25"> Then the angel who had been sent by Yahweh came again and touched him, and said, “Get up and eat some more food, because you need more strength to go on a long journey.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 34:28; EXODUS 24:18; EXODUS 3:1"> So he got up and ate and drank some more, and because of doing that, he got enough strength to enable him to travel for forty days and nights to Sinai Mountain, the mountain that was dedicated to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:13; EXODUS 33:21–22; JEREMIAH 2:18"> He went into a cave there and slept there that night. Yahweh gave instructions to Elijah The next morning, Yahweh said to him “Elijah, why are you here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:4; 1 KINGS 18:22; EXODUS 34:14"> Elijah replied, “I have zealously served you, Yahweh, God Almighty. But the Israeli people have broken the agreement that they made with you. They have torn down your altars, and they have killed all of your prophets. I am the only one whom they have not killed, and now they are trying to kill me, too. So I am running away from them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:4; PSALMS 50:3; JOB 38:1"> Yahweh said to him, “Go out and stand in front of me on this mountain while I pass by.” Suddenly a strong windstorm struck the mountain. As a result, rocks were loosened from the mountainside. But Yahweh was not in the wind. Then there was an earthquake, but Yahweh was not in the earthquake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOB 4:16; ZECHARIAH 4:6; DEUTERONOMY 4:33"> Then there was a fire, but Yahweh was not in the fire. Then there was a sound like someone whispering quietly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:9; Isaiah 6:2; GENESIS 16:8"> When Elijah heard that, he wrapped his cloak around his face. He went out of the cave and stood at its entrance. And he heard Yahweh speaking to him, saying again, “Elijah, why are you here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:9–10; JEREMIAH 22:9; Isaiah 62:1"> He replied again, “I have zealously served you, Yahweh, God Almighty. But the Israeli people have broken the agreement that they made with you. They have torn down your altars, and they have killed all of your prophets. I am the only one whom they have not killed, and now they are trying to kill me, too. So I am running away from them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 45:1; JEREMIAH 1:10; 2 KINGS 8:7–15"> Then Yahweh told him, “Go back to the desert near Damascus. When you arrive there, anoint with olive oil a man named Hazael, to appoint him to be the king of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:1–14; 1 KINGS 19:19–21; 2 KINGS 2:9"> Then anoint Jehu, the son of Nimshi, to be the king of Israel, and also anoint Elisha the son of Shaphat, from Abel-Meholah town, to become my prophet after you are dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:22; 2 KINGS 8:12; 2 KINGS 13:3"> Hazael’s army will kill many people, and those who escape from being killed by his army will be killed by Jehu’s army, and those who escape from being killed by Jehu’s army will be killed by Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 11:4–5; Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 10:20–22"> But you need to know that there are still 7,000 people in Israel who have never bowed to worship Baal or kissed his idol.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elijah summoned Elisha

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 KINGS 2:8; 2 KINGS 2:13–14; 1 SAMUEL 28:14"> So Elijah went to Syria and found Elisha as he was plowing a field with a team of oxen. There were eleven other men who were in front of him, plowing with teams of oxen in the same field. Elijah went to Elisha, and took off his own coat and put it on Elisha, to show Elisha that he wanted him to take his place as a prophet. Then he started to walk away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:21–22; LUKE 9:61–62; ACTS 20:37"> Elisha left the oxen standing there and ran after Elijah, and said to him, “I will go with you, but first let me kiss my parents goodbye.” Elijah replied, “Okay, go home. But do not forget why I have given my cloak to you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 24:22; 2 TIMOTHY 4:11; PHILEMON 1:13"> So Elisha went back to his oxen. He killed them and cut them in pieces and used the wood from the plow to build a fire to roast the meat. He distributed the meat to the other people in his town, and they all ate some. Then he went with Elijah and became his helper.</span>
</div>
