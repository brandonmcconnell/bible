## The Israelis have not returned to God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 22:12; AMOS 6:1; AMOS 5:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You wealthy women of Samaria have grown fat like &#91;MET&#93; the fat cows of the Bashan region.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You oppress poor people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you cause needy people to suffer.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And you say to your husbands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Bring us more wine to drink!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 16:16; PSALMS 89:35; HABAKKUK 1:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Yahweh our God has said this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Because I am holy, I solemnly promise this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will soon be the time when you all will be taken to another country;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your enemies will take you away as though they are using sharp hooks &#91;DOU&#93; to grab you.</span></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:18–21; PSALMS 81:12; HOSEA 9:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Bring offerings of bread to thank me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and other offerings that are not required/commanded,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and boast about these offerings that you bring,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because that is what you like to do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you do it to impress others, not to please me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That is certainly true, because I, Yahweh, have said it.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:60; LEVITICUS 26:25; JOEL 2:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">I caused you to experience ◄plagues/severe sicknesses►</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like the plagues that I sent to the people of Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I caused many of your young men to die in battles.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I allowed your enemies to capture your horses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Many of your soldiers were killed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I caused your camps to be filled with the smell/stink of their decaying corpses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But in spite of that, you rejected me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That is certainly true, because I, Yahweh, have said it!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 13:19; ZECHARIAH 3:2; GENESIS 19:24–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">I got rid of many of you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like I got rid of the people in Sodom and Gomorrah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those of you who did not die were like &#91;SIM&#93; a burning stick that was snatched from a fire so that it would not burn completely.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But in spite of that, you rejected me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That is certainly true, because I, Yahweh, have said it!</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="AMOS 5:8; MICAH 1:3; AMOS 9:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I created the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I created winds.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I reveal to humans what I am thinking.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I sometimes cause the daylight to become dark like the night.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I rule over everything;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I even walk on the highest mountains on the earth!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am the Commander of the armies of angels!”</span></span>
