## A vision of ripe fruit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="AMOS 7:1; AMOS 7:7; AMOS 7:4"> Yahweh showed me in a vision a basket full of ripe fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="AMOS 7:8; LAMENTATIONS 4:18; JEREMIAH 24:1–3"> He asked me, “Amos, what do you see?” I replied, “A basket of very ripe fruit.” He said, “That indicates that it almost the end for my Israeli people. I will not change my mind again about punishing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 5:23; AMOS 6:9–10; JEREMIAH 9:21–22"> Soon, instead of singing in the temple, they will be wailing. There will be corpses everywhere &#91;HYP&#93;. So, remain silent (OR, Everyone will be silent). These things will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel will be destroyed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:14; PSALMS 14:4; AMOS 5:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though you people trample on needy people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you destroy the poor people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; MICAH 6:10–11; HOSEA 12:7"> You habitually say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We wish that &#91;RHQ&#93; the new moon festival would end soon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order that restrictions on doing business during the festival will be ended</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we can sell grain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We wish that the Sabbath would end soon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order that we will again be permitted to sell our wheat”—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and when you sell it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you charge a big price for it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you cheat people by using scales that do not weigh correctly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="AMOS 2:6; LEVITICUS 25:39–42; Nehemiah 5:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">You sell wheat that you have swept up from the floor, and as a result it is mixed with dirt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Those who are needy and poor &#91;DOU&#93; and who do not have money to buy things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you cause them to become your slaves</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by buying them with the small amount of silver with which you could buy a pair of sandals!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="AMOS 6:8; HOSEA 9:9; HOSEA 8:13"> Yahweh has declared, “Although you are very proud of being descendants &#91;MTY&#93; of Jacob, I solemnly declare that I will not forget the evil things that you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="AMOS 9:5; HOSEA 4:3; JEREMIAH 46:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of those evil things, your country will certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; soon be shaken,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all of you will mourn.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will be as though it will repeatedly rise and fall</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; the Nile River that gets full of water and overflows it banks</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then settles back into its riverbed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARK 15:33; MATTHEW 27:45; JEREMIAH 15:9"> On the day when I punish my people,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cause the sun to set at noontime,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the entire earth will be dark in the daytime.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:26; ZECHARIAH 12:10; JOB 20:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause your religious celebrations to become times when you mourn;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">instead of singing, everyone will be weeping.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because of what I will do, all of you will wear rough sackcloth and shave your heads</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to show that you are sorrowing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cause that time to be like when people mourn after an only son has died.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All of you will be extremely sad all of that day.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 74:9; 1 SAMUEL 3:1; 2 CHRONICLES 15:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And Yahweh our God says this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“It will soon be the time when I will cause something to be very scarce &#91;MET&#93; throughout the country.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But it will not be a time when there is no food or water;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it will instead be a time when there will be no messages from me for anyone to hear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 11:7–10; MATTHEW 24:23–26; DANIEL 12:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">People will stagger from the Dead Sea to the Mediterranean Sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and wander from the north to the east,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">searching for a message from me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but there will not be any.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HOSEA 2:3; LAMENTATIONS 1:18; LAMENTATIONS 2:21"> At that time,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">even beautiful young women and strong young men will faint</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of being very thirsty.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="AMOS 5:5; 1 KINGS 12:28–29; DEUTERONOMY 9:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who ◄make oaths/solemnly promise to do something► using the names of their shameful gods of Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who solemnly promise to do something using the name of the god of Dan,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who solemnly promise to do something using the name of the god of Beersheba,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will all die;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will never live again.”</span></span>
</div>
