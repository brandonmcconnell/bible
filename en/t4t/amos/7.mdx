<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:13; Isaiah 37:4; JEREMIAH 42:2"> And in the vision I saw those locusts come, and they ate everything that was green. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="AMOS 7:1; Isaiah 66:15–16; HEBREWS 1:7"> Then this is what Yahweh our Lord showed me in another vision: He was calling to a fire to come and punish his people. In the vision I saw that the fire dried up the ocean and also burned everything on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 85:4; Isaiah 1:9; JEREMIAH 30:19"> Then I cried out again, “Yahweh our Lord, I plead with you, please stop this! We Israeli people are very helpless; how will we be able to survive &#91;RHQ&#93;?”</span>

## Amaziah criticized Amos

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:4; 1 KINGS 12:31–32; JEREMIAH 26:8–11"> Then Amaziah, the priest at Bethel, sent a message to Jeroboam, the King of Israel. In the message he said, “Amos is plotting against you among the Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93;. I am worried that the people of this country will not be able to refute his words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:6; PSALMS 56:5; AMOS 6:7–8"> This is what he is saying:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Jeroboam will soon be killed by someone using a sword,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the people of Israel will be ◄exiled/forced to leave their land and go to another country►.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 2:14; MATTHEW 8:34; 1 SAMUEL 9:9"> Then Amaziah came to me and said, “You prophet, get out of here! Go back to the country of Judah! Earn money to buy food to be able to live there, and do your prophesying there!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:32; AMOS 2:12; 1 KINGS 12:29"> Do not prophesy any more here at Bethel, because this is where the national temple, the king’s temple, is!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="AMOS 1:1; 2 KINGS 4:38; 2 KINGS 2:5"> I replied to Amaziah, “I was previously not a prophet, and my father was not a prophet; I was a shepherd. I also took care of sycamore-fig trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:8; EZEKIEL 2:3–4; JEREMIAH 1:7"> But Yahweh took me away from taking care of my sheep, and he said to me, ‘Go to Israel and prophesy to my people there!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:46; MICAH 2:6; EZEKIEL 21:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You said to me, ‘Do not prophecy saying terrible things will happen to the people of Israel;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">stop saying those things!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HOSEA 9:3; 2 KINGS 17:6; HOSEA 4:13–14"> Therefore listen to what Yahweh says about you:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Your wife will become a prostitute in this city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your sons and daughters will be killed by your enemies’ swords.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Others will measure your land</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and divide it up among themselves;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you yourself will die in a foreign country.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the people of Israel will certainly be forced to leave their country and go ◄into exile/to live in another country►.’”</span></span>
