<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:14–16; GENESIS 17:1; GENESIS 5:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Two people certainly cannot &#91;RHQ&#93; walk together</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if they have not already agreed about what place they will start walking from.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSEA 11:10; PSALMS 104:21; AMOS 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">A lion certainly does not &#91;RHQ&#93; roar in the bushes/forest</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if it has not killed another animal.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It does not &#91;RHQ&#93; growl in its den</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if it is not eating the flesh of an animal that it has caught.</span></span>

## Yahweh will punish Samaria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="AMOS 4:1; AMOS 6:1; AMOS 1:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said this to the leaders of Samaria (OR, Yahweh said this to me):</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Send this message to the leaders &#91;MET&#93; in Ashdod city and to Egypt:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Come to the hills of Samaria</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and see the way that their ◄leaders/wealthy people► are causing people in that city to be terrified,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the way that they are causing the people to suffer!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:22; ZEPHANIAH 1:9; ZECHARIAH 5:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says that the people there do not know how to do things that are right.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their homes are filled with valuable things that they have stolen or taken violently from others.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:9–11; 2 KINGS 17:3–6; 2 KINGS 15:19"> So Yahweh our God says that soon their enemies will come<br /><span className="pl pl-2">and tear down their ◄defenses/high city walls►</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and take away those valuable things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:34–37; ROMANS 11:4–5; Isaiah 31:4"> Yahweh has declared this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“When a lion attacks a sheep,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">sometimes a shepherd will be able to snatch from the lion’s mouth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">only two legs or an ear of the sheep.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Similarly, only a few people from Samaria will escape,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be able to rescue only a part of a couch and a part of a bed.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:18–19; 2 CHRONICLES 24:19; ACTS 20:21"> The Commander of the armies of angels said this:<br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Proclaim this message about the descendants &#91;MTY&#93; of Jacob:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 KINGS 23:15; HOSEA 10:5–8; HOSEA 10:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I, Yahweh, punish the people of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the sins that they have committed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cause the altars at Bethel town to be destroyed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even the projections at the corners of the altars will be cut off and fall to the ground.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:39; JEREMIAH 36:22; AMOS 6:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause the houses that they live in during the ◄winter/rainy season► and those in which they live during the ◄summer/dry season► to be torn down.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Beautiful big houses and houses that are decorated with ivory will be destroyed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it!”</span></span>
