## Israel will be punished

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; JAMES 5:5; LUKE 12:17–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Terrible things will happen to you people in Jerusalem who are not worried about anything,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and also to you leaders who live on Samaria Hill who think that you are safe.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You think that &#91;IRO&#93; you are the most important people in the world,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people to whom Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93; go to get help.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NAHUM 3:8; GENESIS 10:10; 2 KINGS 18:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">But go to Calneh city and see what happened there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then go to see the great city Hamath and see what happened there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then go down to Gath city in Philistia and see what happened to their city walls.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your land/country is certainly not &#91;RHQ&#93; larger or more powerful than their countries were,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they were all destroyed.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="AMOS 3:12; LUKE 16:19; 1 SAMUEL 25:36–38"> <span className="pl pl-1">You lie on beds decorated with expensive ivory,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and on soft couches.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You eat the tender meat of lambs</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and fat calves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 5:12; AMOS 5:23; AMOS 8:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You create/compose new songs</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and play them on your harps like King David did.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 49:22; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:26; MATTHEW 26:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You drink entire bowlfuls of wine</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you put expensive oils/perfumes on your bodies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you do not grieve about your country of Israel &#91;MTY&#93;, which is about to be destroyed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="AMOS 7:11; DANIEL 5:4–6; AMOS 5:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your feasting and lounging on soft couches will soon end,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will be among the first ones to be forced by your enemies to go ◄into exile/to another country►.</span></span>

## Yahweh detests the Israelis for being very proud

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:14; AMOS 4:2; AMOS 8:7"> Yahweh the Lord has solemnly declared this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I hate the people of Israel because they are very proud;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I detest their fortresses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will enable their enemies to capture their capital city</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and everything in it.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="AMOS 5:3; JOB 1:2; PSALMS 109:13"> When that happens, if there are ten people in one house, they will all die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 8:3; 1 SAMUEL 31:12; AMOS 5:13"> If a relative who ◄has the task of/is responsible for► burning their corpses comes to the house and inquires of anyone who is still hiding there, “Is there anyone here with you?”, and that person replies “No,” the one who inquired will say, “Be quiet! We must not mention the name of Yahweh, lest he cause us also to be killed!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="AMOS 3:15; Isaiah 55:11; 2 KINGS 25:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Terrible things like that will happen because Yahweh has commanded that large houses in Israel must be smashed into pieces,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and small houses must be smashed into tiny bits.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="AMOS 5:7; HOSEA 10:4; AMOS 5:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Horses certainly do not &#91;RHQ&#93; run on big rocks,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and certainly people do not &#91;RHQ&#93; plow the sea with oxen.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you have done things that no one should do:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have distorted what is fair/right and caused it to be considered like poison &#91;MET&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you have changed what is right and consider it to be like things that are bitter.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–15; LUKE 12:19–20; 2 KINGS 14:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are proud because you have captured Lo-Debar town,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you have said, “We captured Karnaim by our own power!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:65; 2 KINGS 14:25; JEREMIAH 5:15–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the Commander of the armies of angels declares,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“I will cause another nation to attack you people &#91;MTY&#93; of Israel;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will ◄oppress you/cause you to suffer► all the way from Hamath Pass in the northwest</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to the Dead Sea in the southeast.”</span></span>
</div>
