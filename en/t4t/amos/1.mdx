## Yahweh will punish Syria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 KINGS 8:12; AMOS 1:9; Isaiah 8:4"> This is also what Yahweh said to me:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will punish the people of Damascus, the capital of Syria, because of the many sins that they have committed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not change my mind about punishing them &#91;MTY&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the cruel things that they did to the people of the Gilead region</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">were like &#91;MET&#93; people threshing grain using sledges that have ◄iron teeth/sharp iron nails► in them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:27; 2 KINGS 6:24; 2 KINGS 13:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause a fire to burn the palace that King Hazael built and lived in,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the fortress where his son King Ben-Hadad also lived.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 KINGS 16:9; AMOS 9:7; JEREMIAH 51:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause the gates of Damascus to be broken down;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will get rid of the king who rules in Aven Valley</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the one who rules in Beth Eden.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And the people of Syria will be captured and taken to the Kir region where they originally lived.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh will punish Philistia

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="AMOS 1:11; 2 CHRONICLES 28:18; OBADIAH 1:11"> Yahweh also said this to me:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will punish the people of the cities in Philistia:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will punish the people of Gaza city because of the many sins that they have committed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not change my mind about punishing them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they captured large groups of people and took them to Edom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and sold them to become the slaves of the people of Edom.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:8; JEREMIAH 47:1; ZEPHANIAH 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause a fire to completely burn the walls of Gaza</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and also destroy its fortresses.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:16; Isaiah 14:29–31; JEREMIAH 47:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will get rid of the king of Ashdod city</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the king who rules &#91;MTY&#93; in Ashkelon city.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will also strike/punish the people of Ekron city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the people of Philistia who are still alive will be killed.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh will punish Tyre

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:11–14; Isaiah 23:1–18; JOEL 3:4–8"> Yahweh also said this to me:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will punish the people of Tyre city because of the many sins that they have committed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not change my mind about punishing them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they also captured large groups of our people and took them to Edom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">disregarding the treaty of friendship that they had made with your rulers.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:4; AMOS 1:7; AMOS 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will cause a fire to completely burn the walls of Tyre</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and also destroy its fortresses.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh will punish Edom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:12–14; 2 CHRONICLES 28:17; MALACHI 1:4"> Yahweh also said this to me:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will punish the people of Edom because of the many sins that they have committed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not change my mind about punishing them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they pursued the people of Israel, who descended from Esau’s brother Jacob, and killed them with swords;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they did not act mercifully toward them at all.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They were extremely angry with the people of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they continued to be angry with them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:7; OBADIAH 1:9–10; JEREMIAH 49:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause a fire to burn Teman district in Edom</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and completely burn the fortresses of Bozrah, the biggest city in Edom.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh will punish Ammon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HOSEA 13:16; EZEKIEL 25:2–7; JEREMIAH 49:1–6"> Yahweh also said this to me:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will punish the people of Ammon because of the many sins that they have committed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not change my mind about punishing them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because their soldiers even ripped open the bellies of pregnant women</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when their army attacked the Gilead region to gain more territory.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:2; AMOS 2:2; DEUTERONOMY 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause a fire to completely burn the walls around Rabbah city</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and completely burn its fortresses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">During that battle, their enemies will shout loudly</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the fighting will be like &#91;MET&#93; a fierce/raging wind/storm.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">After the battle, the king of Ammon and his officials will be ◄exiled/forced to go to another country►.”</span></span>
</div>
