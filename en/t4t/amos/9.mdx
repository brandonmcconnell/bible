## Yahweh promised to restore Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 43:7; Isaiah 65:1; ACTS 15:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">When that happens, your armies will seize/capture the remaining part of Edom region again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will also seize the land in other nations that previously belonged to me &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I, Yahweh, have said that I will do these things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will certainly cause them to happen.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOEL 3:18; JOHN 4:35; LEVITICUS 26:5"> There will be a time when your crops will grow very well.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Very soon after the crops are harvested,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">farmers will plow the ground to plant more crops in it again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and soon after the vineyards are planted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">farmers will harvest grapes and tread on them to make wine.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And because there will be a lot of wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it will seem as though wine is flowing down from the hills &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
