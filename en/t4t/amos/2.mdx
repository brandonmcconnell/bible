## Yahweh will punish Moab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 2:8–9; EZEKIEL 25:8–9; AMOS 1:3"> Yahweh also said this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will punish the people of Moab because of the many sins that they have committed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not change my mind about punishing them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they dug up the bones of the king of Edom and burned them completely,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that the ashes became as white as lime.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:24; JEREMIAH 48:41; AMOS 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will cause a fire to completely burn the fortresses of Kerioth city in Moab.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">People will hear soldiers shouting and blowing trumpets loudly</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while I am causing Moab to be destroyed</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:17; JEREMIAH 48:7; JEREMIAH 48:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">and while I am getting rid of its king and all its leaders.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it!”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh will punish Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:24; EZEKIEL 20:16; EZEKIEL 20:13"> Yahweh also said this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will also punish the people of Judah because of the many sins that they have committed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not change my mind about punishing them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they have rejected what I taught them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they have not obeyed my commands.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have been deceived and persuaded to worship false gods,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the same gods that their ancestors worshiped.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HOSEA 8:14; JEREMIAH 17:27; JEREMIAH 21:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will cause a fire to completely burn everything in Judah, including the fortresses in Jerusalem.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh will punish Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOEL 3:3; MICAH 3:2–3; AMOS 5:11–12"> Yahweh also said this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will punish the people of Israel because of the many sins that they have committed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not change my mind about punishing them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they sell righteous people to get a small amount of silver;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they sell poor people, causing them to become slaves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">getting for each of them only the amount of money with which they could buy a pair of sandals.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="AMOS 5:12; Isaiah 10:2; EZEKIEL 22:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though they trample the poor people into the dirt</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do not allow those who are helpless to be treated fairly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Men and their fathers dishonor me &#91;MTY&#93; by both having sex &#91;EUP&#93; with the same slave girl.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:12–17; EXODUS 22:26–27; AMOS 6:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">When poor people borrow money,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the lenders force those people to give to them a piece of clothing for them to keep until he can pay back the money.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But at the end of each day, instead of returning that garment as Yahweh had commanded them to,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they lie down on that garment at the places where they worship their gods!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They fine people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and with that money they buy wine and drink it in the temples of their gods!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 18:16; MALACHI 4:1; DEUTERONOMY 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Long ago, to assist your ancestors, I got rid of the Amor people-group.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They seemed to be as tall as cedar trees</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and as strong as oak trees,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but I got rid of them completely,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as easily as someone cuts off &#91;MET&#93; the branches of a tree and then digs out all its roots.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:7; EXODUS 12:51; AMOS 3:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I brought your ancestors out of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then I led them through the desert for 40 years.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And then I enabled them to conquer/possess the Amor area.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:25; NUMBERS 6:2–3; 1 KINGS 20:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">I chose some of you Israelis to be prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I chose others to be Nazir-men who were completely dedicated to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You people of Israel certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; know that what I have said is true!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="AMOS 7:13; Isaiah 30:10; JEREMIAH 11:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you commanded the prophets to not speak the messages that I gave to them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you persuaded the Nazir-men to drink wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which I told them never to do.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:14; Isaiah 43:24; EZEKIEL 16:43"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will crush you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; the wheels of a wagon that is loaded with grain crushes whatever it rolls over.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23; PSALMS 33:16; ECCLESIASTES 9:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even if you run fast,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will not escape;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even if you are strong, it will be as though you are weak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and warriors will be unable to save themselves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 33:16–17; EZEKIEL 39:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even if you are able to shoot arrows well,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will be forced to retreat &#91;LIT&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even if you run fast or if you ride away on a horse,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will not be able to save yourself.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MARK 14:52; JEREMIAH 48:41; 2 KINGS 7:8–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even warriors who are very brave will drop their weapons</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when they try to flee on the day that I get rid of them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>
