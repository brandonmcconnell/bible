## Jesus warned the people against the hypocritical behavior of the religious leaders.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 12:1; LUKE 20:45–46; MATTHEW 15:10–20"> Then Jesus said to the crowd and to us his disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 20:46; MARK 12:38; MALACHI 2:7"> “Moses was the renowned teacher who taught the people the laws that God gave him &#91;MTY&#93;. Now, the Pharisees and the men who teach our Jewish laws have made themselves the ones who interpret those laws &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 3:5; TITUS 1:16; ROMANS 2:19–24"> Consequently, you should do whatever they tell you that you must do. But do not do what they do, because they themselves do not do what they tell you that you must do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 11:46; ACTS 15:10; GALATIANS 6:13"> They require you to obey many rules that are difficult to obey. But they themselves do not help anyone obey those rules. It is as if they are tying up loads that are hard to carry and putting them on your shoulders &#91;MET&#93; for you to carry. But they themselves do not desire to help anyone obey those laws. They are like people who do not want to lift even the slightest load that they could lift &#91;MET&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:1–16; JOHN 12:43; PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> with one finger. Whatever they do, they do it to be seen by other people &#123;so that other people will see them doing that&#125;. For example, they make extra wide the tiny boxes containing portions of Scripture that they wear on their arms. They enlarge the tassels on their robes, to make others think that they are pious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 JOHN 1:9; LUKE 11:43–54; MARK 12:38–39"> They want people to honor them. For example, at dinner parties they sit in the seats where the most important people sit. In the places of worship they want to sit in the seats where the most honored people sit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 1:38; JOHN 1:49; JOHN 3:26"> Also, they want people to greet them respectfully in the marketplaces and to honor them by calling them ‘Teacher’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JAMES 3:1; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:12–13; 1 PETER 5:3"> But you who are my disciples should not allow people to address you honorably as ‘Rabbi’, which means ‘teacher’. I am the only one who is really your teacher, which means that you are all equal, like brothers and sisters, and none of you should act superior to another, as the Pharisees do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 JOHN 3:1; HEBREWS 12:9; MALACHI 1:6"> Do not honor anyone on earth by addressing him as ‘Father’, because God, your Father in heaven, is the only spiritual father of all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Do not allow people to call you ‘Instructors’, because I, the Messiah, am the one instructor who teaches all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 9:19; GALATIANS 5:13; JOHN 13:14–15"> In contrast, everyone among you who wants God to consider them to be important must serve others as servants do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 PETER 5:5; JAMES 4:6; PROVERBS 29:23"> God will humble those who exalt themselves. And he will exalt those who humble themselves.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus warned the religious leaders about preventing people from letting God rule their lives.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 11:52; MATTHEW 23:29; MATTHEW 23:23"> Then Jesus said to the religious leaders, “You men who teach the Jewish laws and you Pharisees are hypocrites! There will be terrible punishment for you, because by teaching what is false, you prevent people &#91;MTY/HYP&#93; who are thinking about letting God rule their lives from doing that &#91;MET&#93;. What you are doing is like closing a gate &#91;MET&#93; so that people who are wanting to enter a city cannot go in. As for you, you do not let God rule your lives. Neither do you allow other people to let God rule their lives. What you do is like not going into a house yourselves, and not allowing others to enter it either &#91;MET&#93;.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus also warned the religious leaders about zealously encouraging others to believe what they teach.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:3; ACTS 13:10; ACTS 13:43"> “You are hypocrites, you men who teach the Jewish laws and you Pharisees! Your punishment will be terrible, because you exert yourselves very much to get even one person to believe what you teach. For instance, you travel across seas and lands to distant places in order to do that. And as a result, when one person believes what you teach, you make that person much more deserving to go to hell than you yourselves deserve to.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus rebuked the religious leaders about their silly ideas about supporting their promises with oaths.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:14; MATTHEW 5:33–35; JAMES 5:12"> “You Jewish leaders, your punishment will be terrible! You are like people who are blind &#91;MET&#93; who are trying to lead others. You say, ‘In the case of those who promise to do something and then ask the Temple to affirm what they have promised, it means nothing if they do not do what they have promised. But if they ask the gold that is in the Temple to affirm what they have promised, they must do what they promised.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:19; NUMBERS 16:38–39; PSALMS 94:8"> You are fools, and you are like people who are blind &#91;MET&#93;! The gold that is in the Temple is important, but the Temple is even more important &#91;RHQ&#93;, because it is the Temple that the gold is in that sets the gold apart for a special use for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Also you say, ‘In the case of those who promise to do something and then ask the altar to affirm what they have promised, it means nothing if they do not do what they have promised. But if they ask the gift that they have put on the altar to support what they have promised, they must do what they promised.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 29:37; EXODUS 30:29"> You are like people who are blind &#91;MET&#93;. The gift that is offered on the altar is important, but the altar is even more important &#91;RHQ&#93; because it is the altar on which the offering is offered that sets it apart for a special use for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So, those who promise to do something, and then ask the altar to affirm what they have promised are asking everything on the altar to support what they promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 26:8; 1 KINGS 8:13; 2 CHRONICLES 6:2"> Those who promise to do something, and then ask the Temple to affirm what they promised, are asking that God, to whom the Temple belongs, will affirm what they have promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:34; PSALMS 11:4; Isaiah 66:1"> Those who promise to do something and then ask heaven to affirm what they promised are asking the throne of God and God, who sits on that throne, to affirm what they promised.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus also rebuked the religious leaders about paying attention to unimportant laws and at the same time ignoring important ones.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 11:42; MICAH 6:8; HOSEA 6:6"> “You men who teach the Jewish laws and you Pharisees, your punishment will be terrible! You are hypocrites because, even though you give to God a tenth of the herbs you produce, such as mint, dill, and cummin, you do not obey God’s laws that are more important. For example, you do not act justly toward others, you do not act mercifully toward people, and you are not trustworthy. It is good to give a tenth of your herbs to God, but you should also obey these other more important laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:24; MATTHEW 27:6–8; MATTHEW 15:2–6"> You leaders are like blind people who are trying to lead others. According to your laws you say that touching gnats and camels makes you unacceptable to God. What you are doing is like straining a gnat out of a jar of water so that you do not swallow it and thereby become unacceptable to God, and at the same time swallowing a camel with the water &#91;MET&#93;!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus also rebuked the religious leaders about being greedy and selfish.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 11:39–40; MATTHEW 15:19–20; MARK 7:4–13"> “You men who teach people the Jewish laws and you Pharisees, your punishment will be terrible! You are hypocrites! You are greedy and selfish, but you make yourselves appear holy. You are like cups and plates that have been cleaned on the outside, but on the inside they are very filthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 7:1; JAMES 4:8; HEBREWS 10:22"> You blind Pharisees! First you must stop being greedy and selfish. Then you will be able to do what is righteous. That will be like cleaning the inside of a cup first. Then when you clean &#91;MET&#93; the outside of the cup, the cup will truly be clean.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus rebuked the religious leaders for being hypocrites and pretending to be righteous.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 11:44; ACTS 23:3; NUMBERS 19:16"> “You men who teach the Jewish laws and you Pharisees, your punishment will be terrible! You are hypocrites! You are like tombs &#91;SIM&#93; that are painted white so that people can see them and avoid touching them. The outside surfaces are beautiful, but inside they are full of dead people’s bones and filth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 16:15; PSALMS 51:6; MATTHEW 23:5"> You are like those tombs. When people look at you, they think that you are righteous, but in your ◄inner beings/hearts► you are hypocrites and you disobey God’s commands.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus accused the religious leaders of being guilty of killing all the prophets.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKE 11:47–48; ACTS 2:29"> “You men who teach the Jewish laws and you Pharisees are hypocrites! Your punishment will be terrible! You rebuild the tombs of the prophets whom others killed long ago. You decorate the monuments that honor righteous people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:30; MATTHEW 23:34–35; MATTHEW 21:35–36"> You say, ‘If we had lived when our ancestors &#91;MET&#93; lived, we would not have helped &#91;MET&#93; those who killed &#91;MTY&#93; the prophets.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ACTS 7:51–52; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:15–16; JOSHUA 24:22"> Thus you recognize that you should honor the prophets, but you disregard the word of God that other men whom God has sent teach you. In that way you testify against yourselves that you are descendants and disciples &#91;MET&#93; of those who killed the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 15:16; NUMBERS 32:14; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:16"> You are as ready to murder people as &#91;MET&#93; your ancestors were!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:7; MATTHEW 12:34; MATTHEW 5:22"> You people are so wicked You are as dangerous as poisonous snakes &#91;DOU, MET&#93;! ◄You foolishly think that you will escape being punished in hell!/Do you foolishly think that you will escape when God punishes wicked people in hell?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:16–17; MATTHEW 13:52; COLOSSIANS 1:28"> Take note that this is why I will send prophets, wise men, and other men who will teach you my message. You will kill some of them by nailing them to crosses, and you will kill some in other ways. You will whip some of them in the places where you worship, and you will chase them from city to city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 24:20–22; ZECHARIAH 1:1; HEBREWS 11:4"> As a result, God will consider that you are guilty for killing &#91;SYN&#93; all the righteous people, including killing &#91;MTY&#93; Adam’s son Abel, who was a righteous man &#91;SYN&#93;, and Zechariah, the son of Barachiah, whom you are guilty of killing in the holy place between the Temple and the altar, and for killing all the prophets who lived between the times that those two men lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:23; MATTHEW 24:34; MARK 13:30–31"> Think about this: You people who have observed my ministry, it is you whom God will punish for killing all those prophets!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus expressed his sorrow about what would happen to Jerusalem.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKE 13:34–35; RUTH 2:12; PSALMS 91:4"> “O people of Jerusalem &#91;MTY, APO&#93;, you killed the prophets who lived long ago, and you killed others whom God sent to you. You killed them by throwing stones at them &#91;DOU&#93;! Many times I wanted to gather you together to protect you, like a hen gathers her young chicks under her wings &#91;MET&#93;. But you did not want me to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:5; LUKE 13:35; LUKE 21:24"> So listen to this: Your city &#91;SYN&#93; will soon become an uninhabited place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="PSALMS 118:26; MATTHEW 21:9; ROMANS 11:25"> Keep this in mind: You will see me again only when I return, and you say about me, ‘God is truly pleased with this man who comes with God’s authority &#91;MTY&#93;.’”</span>
</div>
</div>
