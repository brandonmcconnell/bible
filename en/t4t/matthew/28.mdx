## After an angel appeared to several women and showed them the tomb was empty, Jesus appeared to them.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MARK 16:1–8; JOHN 20:1–10; MATTHEW 27:56"> After ◄the Sabbath/the Jewish day of rest► ended, on Sunday morning at dawn, Mary from Magdala and the other Mary went to look at the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ACTS 16:26; JOHN 20:12–13; MARK 16:3–5"> Suddenly there was a strong earthquake. At the same time an angel from God came down from heaven. He went to the tomb and rolled the stone away from the entrance so that everyone could see that the tomb was empty. Then he sat on the stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARK 9:3; ACTS 1:10; MARK 16:5"> His appearance was as bright &#91;SIM&#93; as lightning, and his clothes were as white as snow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="REVELATION 1:17; DANIEL 10:7; MATTHEW 27:65–66"> The guards shook because they were very afraid. Then they became completely motionless, as though they were dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="REVELATION 1:17–18; HEBREWS 1:14; Isaiah 41:10"> The angel said to the two women, “You should not be afraid! I know that you are looking for Jesus, who was &#123;whom they&#125; ◄nailed to a cross/crucified►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MARK 16:6; MATTHEW 12:40; LUKE 24:6–8"> He is not here! God has ◄caused him to be alive again/raised him from the dead►, just like Jesus told you would happen! Come and see the place where his body lay!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:32; LUKE 24:22–24; MATTHEW 28:10"> Then go quickly and tell his disciples, ‘He has risen from the dead! He will go ahead of you to Galilee district. You will see him there.’ Pay attention to what I have told you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 2:11; MARK 16:8; LUKE 24:36–41"> So the women left the tomb quickly. They were afraid, but they were also very joyful. They ran to tell us disciples what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:17; JOHN 20:14–17; MATTHEW 14:33"> Suddenly, as they were running, Jesus appeared to them. He said, “Greetings!” The women came close to him. They knelt down and clasped his feet and worshipped him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 8:29; JOHN 20:17; MATTHEW 28:7"> Then Jesus said to them, “Do not be afraid! Go and tell ◄all my disciples/all those who have been accompanying me► that they should go to Galilee. They will see me there.”</span>
</div>

## What the guards were told to tell people about why Jesus’ body was gone.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:65–66; MATTHEW 28:4"> While the women were going, some of the soldiers who had been guarding the tomb went into the city. They reported to the chief priests everything that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:3–4; ACTS 4:5–22; MATTHEW 27:1–2"> So the chief priests and Jewish elders met together. They made a plan to explain why the tomb was empty. They gave the soldiers a lot of money as a bribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:64"> They said, “Tell people, ‘His disciples came during the night and stole his body while we were sleeping.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:2; ACTS 12:19"> If the governor hears &#91;MTY&#93; about this, we ourselves will make sure that he does not get angry and punish you. So you will not have to worry.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 6:10; MATTHEW 27:8; MATTHEW 26:15"> So the soldiers took the money and did as they were told &#123;as the chief priests and elders told them&#125;. And this story has been told &#123;People have told this story&#125; among the Jews to the very day that I am writing this.</span>
</div>

## Jesus appeared to the apostles in Galilee and gave them final instructions.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:32; MATTHEW 28:10; MATTHEW 28:7"> Later we eleven disciples went to Galilee district. We went to the mountain where Jesus had told us to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 45:11; PSALMS 2:12; JOHN 5:23"> We saw him there and worshipped him. But some of us doubted that it was really Jesus, and that he had become alive again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 3:35; 1 PETER 3:22; MATTHEW 11:27"> Then Jesus came close to us and said, “My Father has given me all authority over everything and everyone in heaven and on earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARK 16:15–16; ACTS 2:38–39; ACTS 1:8"> So go, and using my authority, teach my message to people of all ethnic groups so that they may become my disciples. Baptize them to be under the authority of &#91;MTY&#93; my Father, and of me, his Son, and of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; JOSHUA 1:5; ACTS 18:9–10"> Teach them to obey everything that I have commanded you. And remember that by the Spirit I will be with you always, until the end of this age.”</span>
