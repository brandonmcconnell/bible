## Jesus taught that we should not condemn others for their faults since we have faults ourselves.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 6:37; ROMANS 2:1–2; JAMES 4:11–12"> “Do not talk about how sinfully others have acted, in order that God will not say how sinfully you have acted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JAMES 2:13; MARK 4:24; LUKE 6:38"> If you condemn other people, God will condemn you. To the same extent that you condemn others, you will be condemned &#123;God will condemn you&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 8:7–9; GALATIANS 6:1; LUKE 6:41–42"> ◄None of you should be concerned about someone else’s small faults &#91;MET&#93;!/Why should any of you be concerned about someone else’s small faults?► &#91;MET, RHQ&#93; That would be like noticing a speck in that person’s eye. But you should be concerned about your own big faults. They are like &#91;MET&#93; planks in your own eye, which you do not notice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> ◄You should not say to other people about their minor faults, ‘Let me remove the specks from your eyes!’/Why do you say to other people about their minor faults, ‘Let me remove the specks from your eyes?’► &#91;RHQ&#93; You should not say that if a plank is still in your own eye &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 6:42; PSALMS 51:9–13; MATTHEW 23:13–28"> You hypocrite, stop committing your own sins! That will be like removing the plank from your own eye &#91;MET&#93;. Then, as a result, you will be able to perceive things spiritually so that you can help other people get rid of the faults &#91;MET&#93; that are like specks in their eyes.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught that we should not teach spiritual truth to those who will not appreciate it.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 9:7–8; PROVERBS 23:9; MATTHEW 10:14–15"> “You do not give holy things to dogs that would ignore those things and attack you &#91;CHI&#93;. You do not throw pearls in front of hogs, because they would just ignore them and trample them. Similarly, do not give precious spiritual truth to people you know will not value it but instead will do evil things to you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught that God is ready to give good things to us if we persist in prayer.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:22; 1 JOHN 3:22; JOHN 15:7"> “Keep asking God for what you need. If you do that, he will give it to you. Confidently keep expecting God to give you the things that you need, and he will give them to you. It will be like &#91;MET&#93; looking for what you need and finding it. Keep on praying persistently to God. Then God will answer you. It will be like &#91;MET&#93; knocking on a door in order to enter a room. And the way will be opened &#123;God will open the way&#125; for you to get what you pray for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 81:10; PSALMS 81:16; LUKE 23:42–43"> Remember that God will give things to everyone who continues to ask him for them. He will give things to whoever confidently keeps asking. He will open the way for people to get the things that they keep persistently praying for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 11:11–13"> ◄No man among you would give a stone to his son if his son were to ask for bread./Would anyone among you give a stone to his son if his son were to ask for bread?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Likewise, ◄no man would give a poisonous snake to his son if his son would ask for a fish./would anyone give a poisonous snake to his son if his son would ask for a fish?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 11:11–13; JAMES 1:17; ROMANS 8:32"> You, who are evil, know how to give good things to your children. So God, your Father who is in heaven, will certainly give good things to the people who ask him.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught that we should treat others as we would like them to treat us.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 6:31; ROMANS 13:8–10; GALATIANS 5:13–14"> “So in whatever way you want others to act toward you (pl), that is the way you (pl) should act toward them, because that sums up what Moses wrote in the laws that God gave him and also what the prophets wrote long ago.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught that we should try to walk on the narrow road that leads to heaven.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 13:24–25; PROVERBS 16:25; JOHN 14:6"> “Going to where people live eternally with God in heaven is difficult. It is like going along a narrow road &#91;PRS, MET&#93; that leads to a narrow gate. Not many people find that way. The way that most people take is easy, but it results in their being punished &#123;God punishing them&#125; in hell. That way is like a wide road that people walk on &#91;PRS, MET&#93; until they reach the wide gate, but that road and that gate lead to where they will be destroyed &#123;God will destroy them&#125;. Many people enter that gate. So I am telling you to leave the wide road and enter the narrow gate to heaven.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught about how to recognize false prophets.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 JOHN 4:1; 2 PETER 2:1–3; MATTHEW 24:11"> “Beware of people who come to you and say falsely that they are telling you what God has said. They seem to be harmless, but they are extremely harmful. They are like wolves that have covered themselves with sheepskins to make people believe that they are sheep &#91;MET&#93;, but they actually are wolves that attack people &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 6:43–45; MATTHEW 12:33; JAMES 3:12"> So how will you know that they are false? Well, by seeing what plants produce, you know what kind of plants they are &#91;MET&#93;. For example, thornbushes cannot produce grapes, so ◄no one can pick grapes from thornbushes./can anyone pick grapes from thornbushes?► &#91;RHQ&#93; And thistles cannot produce figs, so ◄no one can pick figs from thistles./can anyone pick figs from thistles?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:33–35; GALATIANS 5:22–24; EPHESIANS 5:9"> Here is another example: All good fruit trees produce good fruit, but all unhealthy trees produce worthless fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:17; 1 JOHN 3:9–10"> No good fruit tree is able to produce worthless fruit, and no unhealthy tree is able to produce good fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:10; HEBREWS 6:8; JOHN 15:2–6"> And all the trees that do not produce good fruit are chopped down and burned in a fire &#123;people chop them down and burn them in a fire&#125; to get rid of them. Similarly, those who falsely claim to be prophets will be thrown &#123;God will throw false prophets&#125; into the fires of hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:16; ACTS 5:38"> By seeing what plants produce, you know what kind of plants they are. Similarly, when you see what the people who come to you do, you will know if they are false prophets.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus warned that he will reject those who do not do what God desires.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 2:13; LUKE 6:46; JAMES 1:22"> “Even though many people habitually call me ‘Lord’, pretending that they have my authority, some of them will not go to heaven where God rules, because they do not do what he desires. Only those people who do what my Father, who is in heaven, desires will go there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKE 13:25–27; 1 CORINTHIANS 13:1–2; MALACHI 3:17–18"> On the day &#91;MTY&#93; that God judges everyone, many people, trying to tell me that they deserve to go to heaven, will say to me, ‘Lord, we(exc) spoke God’s message as your representatives &#91;MTY, RHQ&#93;! As your representatives we(exc) expelled demons from people &#91;RHQ&#93;! And as your representatives, many times we(exc) performed miracles &#91;RHQ&#93;!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:41; PSALMS 6:8; LUKE 13:25"> Then I will declare to them, ‘I have never said that you belonged to me. Go away from me, you who do what is evil!’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus warned that undesirable things would happen to those who did not obey his teaching.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 6:47–49; JOHN 13:17; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:10–11"> “So then, anyone who hears what I say and does what I command, will be like a wise man who built his house on rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JAMES 1:12; PSALMS 125:1–2; 1 PETER 1:7"> Even though the rain came down and the river flooded, and the winds blew and beat against that house, it did not fall down because it had been built &#123;he built it&#125; on a solid foundation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:9; LUKE 6:49; JAMES 2:20"> On the other hand, anyone who hears what I say but does not obey what I say will be like a foolish man who built his house on the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:19–22; 2 PETER 2:20–22; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:13"> When the rain fell and the rivers flooded, and the winds blew and beat against that house, it crashed down and broke completely apart, because it was built on the sand. So it is important for you to obey what I teach you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The crowds were amazed at Jesus, because he taught with his own authority.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 4:32; MARK 1:22; JOHN 7:46"> When Jesus finished saying all those things, the crowdswho had been listening to him were amazed at the new teaching that he had taught them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12–13; LUKE 20:8; DEUTERONOMY 18:18–19"> The reason they were amazed was that he was teaching as a person does who has authority. He was not teaching like the men who taught the Jewish laws, who just repeated what other people had taught them.</span>
</div>
</div>
