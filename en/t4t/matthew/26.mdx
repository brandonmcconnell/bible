## Jesus commended a woman for showing her appreciation of him.

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 12:2–3; LUKE 7:46; LUKE 7:37–38"> a woman came to him. She was carrying a stone jar containing very expensive perfume. To show how much she appreciated Jesus, she poured the perfume on his head as he was eating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 12:4–6; MARK 14:4; EXODUS 5:17"> When we disciples saw that, some of us were angry. One of us said, “◄It is terrible that this perfume was wasted!/Why was this perfume wasted?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="TITUS 3:8; HEBREWS 13:21; 1 TIMOTHY 5:10"> Jesus knew what we were saying. So he said to us, “◄You should not be bothering this woman!/Why are you bothering this woman?► &#91;RHQ&#93; She has done a beautiful thing to me.</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARK 14:13–16; JOHN 7:6; JOHN 17:1"> In reply, he gave instructions to two disciples about where they should go. <span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 2:5; JOHN 15:14; MATTHEW 21:6"> So the two disciples did as Jesus told them. They went and prepared the Passover meal in that man’s house.</span>

## Jesus gave the disciples the bread and wine to symbolize his coming death.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 11:23–29; LUKE 22:18–20; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:16–17"> While we were eating, Jesus took a small loaf of bread and thanked God for it. <span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:16; PSALMS 116:13"> Later he took a cup of wine and thanked God for it. Then he gave it to us, saying, “Drink some of the wine in this cup, all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:4–14; HEBREWS 9:14–22; LEVITICUS 17:11"> The wine in this cup represents my blood, which will soon flow from my body. With my blood I will sign the new agreement that God is making to forgive the sins of many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:2; REVELATION 7:17; MARK 14:25"> Note this carefully: I will not drink wine in this way any more until the time when I drink it with you with a new meaning. That will happen when my Father enables me to begin to rule as king.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 55:20–21; PSALMS 28:3; 2 SAMUEL 3:27"> Judas had previously arranged to give them a signal, saying, “The man whom I will kiss is the one you want. Seize him!”</span>
