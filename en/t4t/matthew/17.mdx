## Jesus’ appearance changed while Moses and Elijah talked with him on a mountain.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 9:28–36; MATTHEW 26:37; MARK 5:37"> A week after Jesus said that, he took Peter, James, and John, the younger brother of James, and led them up a high mountain where they were away from other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 104:2; MATTHEW 28:3; LUKE 9:29"> While they were there, the three disciples saw that Jesus’ appearance was changed. His face shone like the sun, and his clothing shone and became as brilliant as light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 24:44; MARK 9:4; MALACHI 4:5"> Suddenly Moses and Elijah, who were important prophets many years ago, appeared and started talking with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MARK 9:5–6; LUKE 9:33; JOHN 14:8–9"> Peter saw them and said to Jesus, “Lord, it is wonderful for us to be here! If you want me to, I will make three shelters, one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:17; MATTHEW 12:18; DEUTERONOMY 18:15"> While Peter was speaking, a bright cloud appeared and covered them. They heard God speaking about Jesus from inside the cloud. He said to them, “This is my Son. I love him. He pleases me very much. So you must listen to him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ACTS 26:14; 2 PETER 1:18; DANIEL 8:17"> When the three disciples heard God speaking, they were terrified. As a result, they fell prostrate on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 10:18; MATTHEW 14:27; DANIEL 8:18"> But Jesus went to them and touched them and said to them, “Stand up! Do not be afraid any more!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 9:36; MARK 9:8; ACTS 12:10–11"> And when they looked up &#91;MTY&#93;, they saw that Jesus was the only one who was still there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus explained that John the Baptizer fulfilled the prophecy about Elijah returning.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARK 8:30; MARK 9:9–13; MATTHEW 17:23"> When they were walking down the mountain, Jesus commanded them, “Do not tell anyone what you saw on the mountain top until God has caused me, the one who has come from heaven, to become alive again after I die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5–6; MATTHEW 11:14; JOHN 1:21"> Those three disciples had just seen Elijah and he did not do anything to prepare people for the coming of the Messiah, so they asked Jesus, “If what you say is true, why do the men who teach the Jewish laws say that it is necessary for Elijah to come back to earth before the Messiah comes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ACTS 3:21; LUKE 1:16–17; MALACHI 4:6"> Jesus answered them, “It is true that God promised that Elijah would come to prepare many &#91;HYP&#93; people for the Messiah’s coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:3–12; MATTHEW 16:21; ACTS 13:24–28"> But note this: Elijah’s representative has already come, and our leaders have seen him, but they did not recognize him as the one who would come before the Messiah. Instead, they treated him badly, just like they desired. And those same rulers will soon treat me, the one who came from heaven, in the same manner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:14"> Then the three disciples understood that when he was talking about Elijah, he was referring to John the Baptizer.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus healed an epileptic boy, and he rebuked his disciples for not having enough faith to do miracles.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARK 9:14–29; MARK 1:40; ACTS 10:25–26"> When Jesus and the three disciples returned to the rest of us disciples and to the crowd that had gathered, a man approached Jesus and knelt before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:24; JOB 1:10–19; MARK 9:20–22"> He said to him, “Sir/Lord, have mercy on my son and heal him! He has epilepsy and suffers very much. Because of this illness, he has fallen in the fire and in the water many times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:19–20; LUKE 9:40; ACTS 19:15–16"> I brought him to your disciples in order that they might heal him, but they were not able to heal him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:11; MATTHEW 6:30; MARK 16:14"> Jesus responded by saying to everyone who had gathered there, “You who have seen how I help people do not believe that you can do anything! Your minds are distorted! How long do I have to be with you before you are able to do what I do &#91;RHQ&#93;? How long do I have to endure your not believing &#91;RHQ&#93;? Bring the boy here to me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:22; MARK 1:34; MARK 5:8"> When they brought the boy to Jesus, Jesus rebuked the demon that was causing the epilepsy. As a result, the demon came out of the boy, and right then the boy was healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARK 9:28; MARK 4:10"> Later, some of us disciples approached Jesus. We asked him privately, “Why were we (exc) not able to expel the demon?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 17:6; MARK 11:23; LUKE 1:37"> He answered us, “It is because you did not believe very much in God’s power. Think about this: Mustard seeds are very small, but in this area they grow and produce large &#91;MET&#93; plants. Similarly, if your faith grows until you truly believe that God will do what you ask him to, you will be able to do anything &#91;LIT&#93;! You could even say to this hill, ‘Move from here to there!’ and it would go where you told it to go.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus again predicted his death and resurrection.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:21; LUKE 18:31–34; MATTHEW 17:23"> When we disciples had gathered together in Galilee district, Jesus said to us, “I, the one who came from heaven, will soon be handed over &#123;Someone will soon hand me, the one who came from heaven, over&#125; to the authorities &#91;SYN&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:21; Isaiah 53:10–12; MARK 8:31"> They will kill me. But God will cause me to become alive again on the third day after I am killed.” When we heard that, we became very sad.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus arranged for a miracle so that Peter could pay the Temple tax.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 38:26; EXODUS 30:13; MARK 9:33"> When we came to Capernaum city, the men who collect taxes for the Temple approached Peter and said to him, “Your teacher pays the Temple tax, does he not?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 22:21; ROMANS 13:6–7; MATTHEW 22:17"> He answered them, “Yes, he does pay it.” When we came into the house where Jesus was saying, before Peter began to talk about paying taxes, Jesus said to him, “Simon, from whom do you think rulers collect revenue or taxes? Do they collect taxes from the citizens of their own country, or from citizens of countries they have conquered?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Peter answered him, “From citizens of other countries.” Then Jesus said to him, “So citizens of their own country do not need to pay taxes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:19–22"> But even though the Temple is mine, pay the tax for us so that the Temple tax collectors will not become angry with us. In order to get the money to pay it, go to the lake. Cast your fish line and hook, and take the first fish that you catch. When you open its mouth, you will find a silver coin that is worth enough to pay the tax for you and me. Take that coin and give it to the Temple tax collectors.”</span>
</div>
