## Jesus taught a parable about different kinds of soil.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 8:4–10; MATTHEW 13:1–15; MARK 4:1–12"> That same day Jesus, along with us disciples, left the house where he was teaching and went to Galilee lake. He sat down there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 5:3; MATTHEW 4:25; MARK 4:1"> and a very large crowd gathered around him to listen to his teaching. So, in order that they would not jostle him (OR, to speak to the people better), he got into a boat and sat down to teach them. The crowd stood on the shore and was listening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARK 4:2–9; MARK 4:13; PSALMS 78:2"> He was telling them many parables. One of the parables that he told them was this: “Listen! A man went out to his field to sow some seeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:18–19"> As he was scattering them over the soil, some of the seeds fell on the path. Then some birds came and ate those seeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:19; EZEKIEL 36:26; ZECHARIAH 7:12"> Other seeds fell on ground where there was not much soil on top of the rock. Those seeds sprouted very soon, because the sun quickly warmed the shallow soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:21; JAMES 1:11–12; COLOSSIANS 1:23"> But when the young plants came up, they were scorched by the sun, and they withered because they did not have deep roots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 4:18–19; JEREMIAH 4:3–4; GENESIS 3:18"> Other seeds fell on ground that contained roots of thorny weeds. The thorny weeds grew together with the young plants, and they crowded out the plants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:23; LUKE 8:15; GENESIS 26:12"> But other seeds fell on good soil, and the plants grew and produced a lot of grain. Some plants produced 100 times as many seeds as were planted. Some plants produced 60 times as much. Some plants produced 30 times as much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="REVELATION 2:11; MATTHEW 11:15; REVELATION 2:7"> If you want to understand this &#91;MTY&#93;, you should consider carefully what I have just said.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus told the disciples why he taught them using parables.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MARK 4:33–34; MARK 4:10"> We disciples approached Jesus and asked him, “Why do you use parables when you speak to the crowd?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 2:9–10; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:14; MATTHEW 19:11"> He answered us saying, “God &#91;EUP/MTY&#93; is revealing to you what he did not reveal before, about how he wants to rule over people’s lives. But he has not revealed it to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:29; MARK 4:24–25; LUKE 8:18"> Those who think about what I say and understand it, God will enable them to understand more. But those who do not think carefully about what I say will forget even what they already know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:2; JEREMIAH 5:21; Isaiah 42:18–20"> That is why I use parables when I speak to people, because although they see what I do, they do not perceive what it means, and although they hear what I say, they do not really understand what it means.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; EZEKIEL 12:2; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:14"> What these people do completely fulfills what God told the prophet Isaiah to say long ago to the people who did not try to understand what he said, You will hear what I say, but you will not understand it. You will keep seeing what I do, but you will not understand what it means &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10; MARK 4:12; ZECHARIAH 7:11"> God also said to Isaiah, These people have become unresponsive &#91;MTY&#93; to what they see me do and to what they hear me say. They listen unwillingly &#91;MTY&#93; to what I say, and they do not pay attention to &#91;MTY&#93; what I do. If it were not so, they would perceive &#91;MTY&#93; what I am doing, they would understand &#91;MTY&#93; what I say to them, and they would turn away from their sinful lives and turn to me, and I would save them from being punished for their sins &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 10:23–24; EPHESIANS 1:17–18; MATTHEW 16:17"> But as for you, God is pleased with you because you &#91;SYN&#93; have seen what I have done and because you &#91;SYN&#93; understand what I say &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 8:56; HEBREWS 11:13; 1 PETER 1:10–12"> Note this: Many prophets and righteous people who lived long ago longed to see what you are seeing me do, but they did not see it. They longed to hear the things that you have been hearing me say, but they did not hear what you hear me say.” &#91;DOU&#93;</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus explained that the parable about the kinds of soil illustrates four different ways people respond to his teaching.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:11–12; LUKE 8:11–15; MARK 4:13–20"> “Since God wants you to understand what I am teaching you, listen as I explain the parable about the man who sowed seeds in various kinds of soil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 8:11–15; MATTHEW 13:38; HEBREWS 2:1"> The people who hear about how God rules over people’s lives and do not understand what they have heard are like the path where some of the seeds fell. Satan, the Evil One, comes and causes these people to forget &#91;MET&#93; what they have heard &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:31–32; PSALMS 106:12–13; JOHN 5:35"> Some people are like the shallow soil on top of rock. When they hear God’s message, they immediately accept it joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:6; 2 PETER 1:8–9; EPHESIANS 3:17"> But because it does not penetrate deeply into their inner beings, they believe it for only a short time. They are like the plants that did not have deep roots. When they are treated badly and caused to suffer because they believe God’s message, they soon stop believing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 6:17; LUKE 8:14; 1 TIMOTHY 6:9–10"> Some people are like the soil that had the roots of thorny weeds in it. They hear God’s message, but they desire to be rich, so they worry only about &#91;MTY, PRS&#93; material things. As a result, they &#91;PRS&#93; forget God’s message, and they do not do &#91;IDM&#93; the things that God wants them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:11; MATTHEW 12:33; JOHN 15:16"> But some people are like the good soil where some of the seeds fell. Just like the plants that grew in this soil produced a lot of grain, these people hear my message and understand it. Some of them do many things &#91;IDM&#93; that please God, some do even more things that please God, and some do very many things that please God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus told a parable about wheat and weeds.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:1; MATTHEW 13:47; MATTHEW 20:1"> Jesus also told the crowd another parable, by which he tried to explain that although God &#91;MTY/EUP&#93; is a king, he will not immediately judge and punish all the wicked people. Jesus said, “God is like a landowner who sent his servants to sow good wheat seed in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 PETER 5:8; 2 PETER 2:1; ACTS 20:30–31"> While those servants were sleeping and not watching the field, an enemy of the landowner came and scattered weed seeds in the midst of the wheat. Then he left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARK 4:26–29"> After the seeds sprouted and the green plants grew, the heads of grain began to form. But the weeds also grew.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ROMANS 16:17; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:1; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:18–20"> So the servants of the landowner came and said to him, ‘Sir, you &#91;RHQ&#93; gave us good seeds and those are the ones we &#91;RHQ&#93; planted in your field. So where did the weeds come from?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 5:3–7; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:14; LUKE 9:49–54"> The landowner said to them, ‘My enemy did this.’ His servants said to him, ‘So, do you want us to pull up the weeds and put them in a pile?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> He said to them, ‘No, do not do that, because you might pull up some of the wheat at the same time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:12; 1 TIMOTHY 5:24; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:5"> Let the wheat and the weeds grow together until harvest time.’ At that time I will say to the reapers, ‘First gather the weeds, tie them into bundles to be burned. Then gather the wheat and put it into my barns.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus illustrated the effects of people letting God rule their lives.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="LUKE 13:18–19; MATTHEW 17:20; MARK 4:30–32"> Jesus also told this parable: “The number of (OR, God’s influence in the lives of) people whose lives God rules over will continue to grow. It is very much like mustard seeds grow after a man plants them in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:6; DANIEL 4:12; EZEKIEL 17:23–24"> Although mustard seeds are among the smallest of all the seeds that people plant, here in Israel they become large plants. When the plants have fully grown, they are larger than the other garden plants. They become shrubs that are large enough for birds to build nests in their branches.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:6–7; LUKE 13:21"> Jesus also told this parable: “The way people who let God &#91;MTY/EUP&#93; rule their lives &#91;MET&#93; can influence the world is like yeast that a woman mixed with about 50 pounds of flour. That small amount of yeast made the whole batch of dough swell up.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ use of parables fulfilled a prophecy in Scripture.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARK 4:33–34; JOHN 16:25; MATTHEW 13:13"> Jesus told the crowd parables to teach them all these things. When he spoke &#91;HYP&#93; to them, he habitually used such illustrations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 78:2; EPHESIANS 3:9; 1 PETER 1:20–21"> By doing that, he fulfilled what God told one of the prophets to write long ago: I will speak &#91;MTY&#93; in parables; I will tell parables to teach what I have kept secret since I created the world.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus explained the parable about wheat and weeds. The wheat represents those who obey God, and the weeds represents those who disobey God. The reaping represents judgment.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOHN 16:17–20; MATTHEW 13:11; MATTHEW 9:28"> After Jesus dismissed the crowds, he went inside. Then we disciples approached him and said, “Explain to us the parable about the weeds that grew in the wheat field.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 3:5–7; MATTHEW 13:41; LUKE 10:16"> He answered, “The one who sows the good seed represents me, the one who came from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOHN 8:44; PHILIPPIANS 3:18–19; ACTS 13:10"> The field represents this world &#91;MTY&#93;, where people live. The seeds that grew well represent the people who let God rule their lives &#91;MET&#93;. The weeds represent the people who do what the devil, the Evil One, tells them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:49; REVELATION 14:15–19; MATTHEW 25:31"> The enemy who sowed the weed seeds represents the devil. The time when the reapers will harvest the grain represents the time when the world will end. The reapers represent the angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:39"> The weeds are gathered and burned. &#123;The reapers gather the weeds. Then they burn them.&#125; That represents the judging of people, which God will do when the world will end. It will be like this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:31; MATTHEW 18:7; 2 PETER 2:1–2"> I, the one who came from heaven, will send my angels, and they will gather from everywhere the people who cause others to quit believing in me &#91;MET&#93; and all those who disobey God’s commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:50; LUKE 16:23–24; MATTHEW 8:12"> They will throw those people into the fires of hell. There those people will weep and grind their teeth because of the great pain that they are suffering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="DANIEL 12:3; MATTHEW 11:15; LUKE 12:32"> God’s brightness will shine on the people who have lived as he wants them to. It will shine on them as brightly as the sun shines. It will shine on them in the place where God, their Father, rules over them. If you want to understand this &#91;MTY&#93;, you should think carefully about what I have just said.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus illustrated the value of letting God rule our lives.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:2–5; COLOSSIANS 2:3; PHILIPPIANS 3:7–9"> “What people do who begin to allow God &#91;MTY/EUP&#93; to rule their lives is like what a certain man did to acquire a treasure. A treasure was hidden in a field by someone &#123;Someone hid a treasure in a field and never dug it up again&#125;. When another man found it, he hid it by burying it again in order that no one else would find it. Being very happy that he had found something very valuable, he went and sold all his possessions to obtain money to buy the field the treasure was in. He then went and bought the field, and so he was able to acquire that treasure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:13–18; MATTHEW 16:26; MATTHEW 13:24"> Also, what people do who begin to allow God &#91;MTY/EUP&#93; to rule their lives is like what a merchant did who was looking for good quality pearls to buy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="EPHESIANS 3:8; LUKE 18:28–30; PROVERBS 2:4"> When he found one very costly pearl that was for sale, he sold all his possessions to acquire enough money to buy that pearl. Then he went and bought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JOHN 15:6; 2 TIMOTHY 3:2–5; MATTHEW 22:9–10"> What God &#91;MTY/EUP&#93; will do to people who falsely say that they are letting him rule their lives is like what certain fishermen did with the fish they caught in a lake, using a large net. They caught all classes of fish, both useful and worthless fish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:30; MATTHEW 13:40–43; MATTHEW 3:12"> When the net was full, the fishermen pulled it up onto the shore. Then they sat there and put the useful fish into buckets, and threw the worthless ones away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="REVELATION 20:12–15; MATTHEW 24:31; MATTHEW 13:39"> What they did in separating the good fish from the bad ones is like what will happen to people when the world ends. The angels will come to where God is judging people, and will separate the wicked people from the righteous ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:42; MATTHEW 8:12; REVELATION 14:10–11"> They will throw the wicked people into the fire in hell. And those wicked people will weep and gnash their teeth because of the intense pain they are suffering.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus illustrated the value of understanding his parables.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:19; MATTHEW 13:11; MARK 4:34"> Then Jesus asked us, “Do you understand all these parables I have told you?” We said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 JOHN 2:7–8; 2 TIMOTHY 3:16–17; TITUS 1:9"> “Yes, we understand them.” Then he said to us, “Because you understand all these parables, you will understand the following parable: You, along with all others, will teach people what you heard me say about God ruling people’s lives. You will add that to what you formerly learned. You will be like a manager of a household who takes both new things and old things out of his storage room.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus did not do many miracles in Nazareth because people there rejected him as the Messiah.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:28; MARK 4:33–35"> When Jesus had finished telling these parables, he took us and left that area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:28; JOHN 7:15–16; MARK 6:1–6"> We went to Nazareth, his hometown. ◄On the Sabbath/On the Jewish rest day► he began to teach the people in the Jewish worship house. The result was that the people there were astonished. But some said, “This man is just an ordinary person like us. So how is it that he knows so much and understands so much &#91;RHQ&#93;? And how is it that he is able to do such miracles &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="JOHN 6:42; MARK 6:3; LUKE 3:23"> ◄He is just the son of the carpenter!/Isn’t he just the son of the carpenter that lived here?► &#91;RHQ&#93; His mother is Mary, and his younger brothers are James, Joseph, Simon and Judas &#91;RHQ&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> ◄And his sisters also live here in our town./Do not his sisters live in our town?► &#91;RHQ&#93; So how is he able to do all these miracles?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="JOHN 4:44; MATTHEW 11:6; LUKE 4:24"> The people were unable to accept that he was the Messiah. So Jesus said to them, “People honor me and other prophets everywhere else we go, but in our hometowns we are not honored, and even our own families do not honor us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="MARK 6:5–6; ROMANS 11:20; HEBREWS 3:12–19"> Jesus did not perform many miracles there because the people did not believe that he was the Messiah.</span>
</div>
