## Jesus told a parable to illustrate how God will reward those who serve him.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:47; MATTHEW 13:24; SONG OF SONGS 8:11–12"> “In order to illustrate how God rewards people, I will compare the way God &#91;MTY/EUP&#93; rules his people with what the owner of an estate did. Early in the morning the owner of the estate went to the market where people who wanted work gathered. He went there to hire laborers to work in his vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:26; ECCLESIASTES 12:1; MATTHEW 22:19"> He promised the men whom he hired that he would pay them the standard wage for working one day. Then he sent them to his vineyards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:13; ACTS 2:15; MATTHEW 20:6–7"> At nine o’clock that same morning he went back to the market. There he saw more men who did not have work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 6:11; 1 PETER 4:2–3; LUKE 19:7–10"> He said to them, ‘Go to my vineyard as other men have done, and work there. I will pay you whatever is a just wage.’ So they also went to his vineyard and began to work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:24–26; ACTS 10:3; MARK 15:33–34"> At noon and at three o’clock he again went to the market and found other laborers whom he promised to pay a fair wage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:12; JOHN 9:4; ACTS 17:21"> At five o’clock he went to the market again and saw other men standing there who were not working. He said to them, ‘Why are you standing here all day and not working?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:11–12; HEBREWS 6:10; JOHN 9:4"> They said to him, ‘Because no one has hired us.’ He said to them, ‘I will hire you. Go to my vineyard as other men have done, and work there.’ So they went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:15; 1 PETER 4:10; ROMANS 2:6–10"> When evening came, the owner of the vineyard said to his manager, ‘Tell the men to come so that you can give them their wages. First, pay the men who started working last, and pay the men last who started working first.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:6–7; LUKE 23:40–43; MATTHEW 20:2"> The manager paid the standard daily wage to each of the men who did not start working until five o’clock in the afternoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> When the men who had begun working early in the morning went to get their wages, they thought that they would receive more than the standard wage. But they also received only the standard wage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:16; LUKE 19:7; ACTS 22:21–22"> So they complained to the owner of the vineyard because they thought their payment was unfair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 11:5–6; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:23–28; LUKE 12:55"> They said to him, ‘You are not being fair! The men who started working after all of the rest of us started worked for only one hour! You have paid them the same wage as you paid us! But we worked hard all day &#91;IDM&#93;, including working in the hottest part of the day!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:50; MATTHEW 22:12; ROMANS 9:14–15"> The owner of the vineyard said to one of those who complained, ‘Friend, I did not treat you unfairly. You agreed with me to work the whole day for the standard wage &#91;RHQ&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 17:2; LUKE 15:31; MATTHEW 6:6"> Stop complaining to me! Take your wage and go! I desire to give the same wage that I gave you to the men who began working after all of you had begun working.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 9:15–24; DEUTERONOMY 15:9; JONAH 4:1–4"> I certainly have a right to spend my money as I desire &#91;RHQ&#93;, including paying those laborers what I desire to pay them. ◄You should not be envious about my being generous!/Are you angry &#91;MET&#93; because I am generous?► &#91;RHQ&#93;’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:30; MARK 10:31; MATTHEW 22:14"> Then Jesus said to us, “Similarly, God will reward well some people who seem to be less important now, and he will not reward some people who seem to be more important now.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## For the third time Jesus predicted his death and resurrection.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARK 10:32–34; LUKE 18:31–34; MATTHEW 16:13"> When Jesus was walking on the road up to Jerusalem, along with us twelve disciples, he took us to a place by ourselves in order that he could talk to us privately. Then he said to us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:21; ACTS 2:23; MATTHEW 27:1"> “Listen carefully! We are now going up to Jerusalem. While we are there, someone will enable the chief priests and the men who teach the Jewish laws to seize me, the one who came from heaven, and they will put me on trial. They will condemn me and say that I should be killed &#123;the authorities should kill me&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:21; MARK 15:16–20; MATTHEW 27:27–31"> Then they will put me in the hands of non-Jews so that they can make fun of me, whip me, and kill me by nailing me to a cross. But on the third day after that, God will cause me to live again.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught that greatness in God’s sight means humbly serving others as Jesus did.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:21; MARK 10:35–45; MATTHEW 8:2"> Then the mother of James and John brought her two sons to Jesus. She bowed down before Jesus and asked him to do her a favor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:28; MARK 10:36–37; Esther 5:3"> Jesus said to her, “What do you want me to do for you?” She said to him, “Permit these two sons of mine to sit next to you (OR, Permit these two sons of mine to sit next to you at your right side and at your left side) and rule with you &#91;MTY&#93; when you become king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:42; LUKE 22:42; MATTHEW 26:39"> Jesus said to her and her sons, “You do not understand what you are asking for. Can you suffer like I am about to suffer &#91;IDM&#93;?” James and John answered him, “Yes, we are able to do that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="REVELATION 1:9; MATTHEW 25:34; ACTS 12:2"> Then Jesus said to them, “Yes, you will suffer as I will suffer &#91;IDM&#93;. But I am not the one who chooses the ones who will sit next to me and rule with me. God, my Father, will give those places &#91;MTY&#93; to the ones whom he appoints.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 PETER 5:5; PHILIPPIANS 2:3; MARK 10:41"> When we ten other disciples heard what James and John had requested, we became angry with them because we also wanted to rule with Jesus in the highest positions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 22:25–27; MATTHEW 20:25–28; JOHN 13:12–17"> So Jesus called all of us together and said to us, “You know that those who rule the non-Jews enjoy showing them that they are powerful. Their chief rulers enjoy commanding the people under them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARK 10:43; MARK 9:35; MARK 10:45"> You should not be like them. On the contrary, everyone among you who wants God to consider him great must become like a servant for the rest of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 22:26; MARK 9:33–35; MATTHEW 18:4"> And everyone among you who wants God to consider him to be the most important must become like a slave for the rest of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 22:27; HEBREWS 9:28; TITUS 2:14"> You should imitate me. Even though I am the one who came from heaven, I did not come to be served &#123;in order that people would serve me&#125;. On the contrary, I came in order to serve others and to &#91;IDM&#93; allow myself to be killed &#123;other people to kill me&#125;, so that my dying would be like a payment to rescue many people from being punished for their sins.” &#91;MTY&#93;</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus healed two blind men.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARK 10:46–52; LUKE 18:35–43; MATTHEW 9:27–31"> As we were leaving Jericho city, a large crowd of people followed us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MARK 10:46; Isaiah 42:16; MATTHEW 21:9"> As we walked along, we saw two blind men sitting alongside the road. When they heard that Jesus was passing by, they yelled to him, “Lord, Descendant of King David, you are the Messiah Take pity on us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:13; COLOSSIANS 4:2; MATTHEW 7:7–8"> People in the crowd scolded them and told them to be quiet. But the blind men yelled even louder, “Lord, Descendant of King David, you are the Messiah Have pity on us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:37; PHILIPPIANS 4:6; MATTHEW 20:21"> Jesus stopped and called them to come to him. Then he said to them, “What do you want me to do for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 119:18; EPHESIANS 1:17–19"> They said to him, “Lord, heal our eyes &#91;IDM&#93; so that we can see!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:15–16; MATTHEW 9:29; ACTS 26:18"> Jesus felt sorry for them and touched their eyes. Immediately they were able to see, and they went with Jesus.</span>
</div>
