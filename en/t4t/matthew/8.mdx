## Jesus healed a leper and made it possible for him to associate with people again.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:18; MATTHEW 4:25–1; LUKE 5:15"> When Jesus went down from the hillside, large crowds followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:25; LUKE 17:12–19; MATTHEW 18:26"> After he left the crowds, a man who had the skin disease called leprosy unexpectedly came and knelt before him. He said to Jesus, “Lord/Sir, please heal me, because I know you are able to heal me if you are willing to.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 5:13; PSALMS 33:9; 2 KINGS 5:14"> Then Jesus, disregarding the religious law that forbade people to come close to lepers, stretched out his hand and touched the man. He said to him, “I am willing to heal you, and I heal you now!” Immediately the man was healed from his leprosy &#123;he was no longer a leper&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 14:2–32; LUKE 5:14; LUKE 17:14"> Then Jesus said to him, “Go and show yourself to a nearby priest so that he can examine you and verify that you no longer have leprosy. Then, after he tells the local people, they will know that you no longer have leprosy, and you will be able to associate with them again. Make sure that now you do not report about my healing you to anyone other than the priest. Then go to the Temple in Jerusalem and give the priest the offering that Moses commanded that people who have been healed from leprosy should offer as a sacrifice to God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus healed from a distance a Roman officer’s servant because the officer trusted in him.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 7:1–10; ACTS 27:43; ACTS 10:1–33"> When Jesus went to Capernaum city, a Roman officer who commanded 100 soldiers came to him. He begged Jesus to help him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:1; MATTHEW 4:24; ACTS 9:33"> He said to him, “Sir, my servant is lying in bed at home and is paralyzed, and he has severe pain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:18–19; LUKE 7:6; MARK 5:23–24"> Jesus said to him, “I will go to your house and heal him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 107:20; LUKE 7:6–7; PSALMS 33:9"> But the officer said to him, “Lord/Sir, do not bother to go. I am a non-Jew, so I am not worthy for you, a Jew, to come into my house &#91;MTY&#93; and associate with me. Instead, just command that my servant be healed, and he will be healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 4:35–36; TITUS 2:9; COLOSSIANS 3:22"> I believe this because, as for me, I am a man under the authority of others, and I also have soldiers under my authority. When I say to one of them ‘Go!’ he goes. When I say to another ‘Come!’ he comes. When I say to my slave, ‘Do this!’ he does it. And I believe that you speak with a similar kind of authority.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:28; LUKE 7:9; MARK 6:6"> When Jesus heard this, he marveled. He said to the crowd that was walking with him, “Listen to this: I have never before found anyone who has as firm a faith in me as this non-Jewish man has. Not even in Israel, where I would expect people to believe in me, have I found anyone who has the kind of faith in me that he has!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 13:28–29; EPHESIANS 3:6; GALATIANS 3:28–29"> I tell you truly that many other non-Jewish people who will believe in me like this Roman officer will come from distant countries, including those far to the east and far to the west &#91;SYN&#93;, and they will sit down to eat with &#91;MTY&#93; Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in heaven, where God rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:30; LUKE 13:28; MATTHEW 21:43"> But the Jews &#91;IDM&#93; who should have allowed God to rule over them will be sent to hell, where there is total darkness. And as a result, they will weep because of their suffering and will grind their teeth because they will have severe pain &#91;MTY&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:29–30; MARK 9:23; JOHN 4:50"> Then Jesus said to the officer, “Go home. What you believed would happen, that I would heal your servant from a distance, will happen.” Then the officer went home and found out that his servant became well at the exact time that Jesus told him that he would heal him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus healed Peter’s mother-in-law.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 4:38–41; MARK 1:29–34; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:5"> When Jesus and some of his disciples went to the home of Peter, Jesus saw Peter’s mother-in-law. She was lying on a bed because she had a fever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:21; LUKE 4:38–39; LUKE 8:54"> He touched her hand, and as a result, immediately she no longer had a fever &#91;PRS&#93;. Then she got up and served them some food.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus healed many sick people and expelled demons from other people.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 15:26; MATTHEW 14:14; MARK 1:32–34"> That evening when ◄the Sabbath/the Jewish rest day► ended and restrictions about traveling ended, many people who were controlled by demons &#123;whom demons controlled&#125; and people who were sick &#91;MTY&#93; were brought to Jesus. He caused the demons to leave just by speaking to them, and he healed all the people who were sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4; 1 PETER 2:24; MATTHEW 1:22"> By doing this he fulfilled the words that had been said by the prophet Isaiah &#123;that the prophet Isaiah had said&#125; about the Messiah: “He healed people who had sicknesses and he cured them of their diseases.” &#91;DOU&#93;</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus told a man what to expect if he went with Jesus. He told another man to follow Jesus immediately, not to wait until later.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 4:42–43; LUKE 8:22; MARK 4:35"> Jesus saw the crowd around him, but he needed to rest. So he told his disciples to take him by boat to the other side of the lake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 22:33–34; JOHN 13:36–38; LUKE 14:25–27"> As they were walking toward the boat, a man who taught the Jewish laws came to him and said, “Teacher, I will go with you wherever you go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 84:3; LUKE 2:7; Isaiah 53:2–3"> In order that the man might know what he could expect if he went with him, Jesus said to him, “Foxes have holes in the ground in which to live, and birds have nests, but even though I am the one who came from heaven, I do not have a home where I can sleep &#91;MTY&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 9:59–62; LEVITICUS 21:11–12; NUMBERS 6:6–7"> Another man who was one of Jesus’ disciples said to him, “Sir/Lord, permit me first to go home. After my father dies I will bury him, and then I will come with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:13; LUKE 15:32; EPHESIANS 5:14"> But Jesus said to him, “Come with me now. God considers that people who do not have eternal life are dead &#91;MET&#93;. Let those people do the work of burying people who die.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The disciples were amazed when Jesus calmed a storm on the lake.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 8:22–25; MARK 4:36–41; MATTHEW 9:1"> Then Jesus got into the boat. The disciples also got in, and they started sailing close to the shoreline around Lake Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 8:23; PSALMS 107:23–27; MARK 4:37–38"> Suddenly strong winds blew on the lake, and very high waves were splashing into the boat and filling it. But Jesus was sleeping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JONAH 1:6; 2 CHRONICLES 14:11; MARK 4:38–39"> They went and woke him up, and said to him, “Sir/Lord, rescue us! We are about to drown!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; PSALMS 65:7; PSALMS 107:28–30"> He said to them, “◄You should not be terrified!/Why are you terrified?► &#91;RHQ&#93; I am disappointed that in spite of all you have seen me do, ◄you do not believe very much that I can rescue you./do you not believe that I can rescue you?► &#91;RHQ&#93;” Then he got up and rebuked the wind and told the waves to calm down. And the wind stopped blowing and the lake became calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MARK 7:37; MARK 6:51; MATTHEW 14:33"> As a result, they were amazed, and they said to one another, “This man is certainly an extraordinary person &#91;RHQ&#93;! All things are under his control! Even the winds and the waves obey him &#91;PRS&#93;!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus expelled some demons from two men.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 8:26–39; MARK 5:1–20; ACTS 10:38"> When they came to the east side of the lake, they arrived in the region where the Gadarenes lived. Jesus got out of the boat and began walking on the road. Then two men who were controlled by demons &#123;whom demons controlled&#125; came out of the burial caves where they were living. Because they were extremely violent and attacked people, no one dared to travel on the road near where they were living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARK 1:24; LUKE 4:34; JAMES 2:19"> Suddenly they shouted to Jesus, “You are the Son of God! Because you have nothing in common with us &#91;IDM, RHQ&#93;, leave us alone Have you come here &#91;RHQ&#93; to torture us before the time &#91;MTY&#93; God has appointed to punish us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:8; LEVITICUS 11:7; MARK 5:11"> There was a large herd of pigs not far away, digging for food with their noses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="REVELATION 20:1–2; LUKE 8:30–33; MARK 5:12"> So the demons begged Jesus, saying, “You are going to cast us out of the men, so send us into the herd of pigs!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARK 5:13; ACTS 2:23; REVELATION 20:7"> Jesus said to them, “If that is what you want, go!” So the demons left the men and entered the pigs. Suddenly the whole herd of pigs rushed down the steep bank into the lake and drowned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKE 8:34–36; MARK 5:14–16; ACTS 19:15–17"> The men who were tending the pigs became afraid and ran into the town and reported everything that had happened, including what had happened to the two men who had been controlled by demons &#123;whom demons had controlled&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ACTS 16:39; LUKE 8:37–39; LUKE 5:8"> Then it seemed as if all the people &#91;HYP&#93; who lived in that town &#91;MTY&#93; went to meet Jesus. When they saw him and the two men who had been controlled by the demons, they begged Jesus to leave their region because they thought that he would destroy more things than just the pigs (OR, because they realized he must be very powerful).</span>
</div>
