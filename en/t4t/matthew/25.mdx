## Jesus taught in a parable that his disciples should always be ready for his return.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 12:35–36; PHILIPPIANS 2:15–16; MATTHEW 24:42–51"> Jesus continued by saying, “I will tell you something else about the time when I return from heaven as king &#91;MET&#93;. What will happen to many people who say that they know me will be like what happened to ten virgins who took their lanterns and went to the place where they would meet a bridegroom and go to his wedding celebration.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:1–5; MATTHEW 13:19–23"> Five of the virgins were foolish and five of them were wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="REVELATION 3:15–16; 2 TIMOTHY 3:5; Isaiah 48:1–2"> The virgins who were foolish took their lanterns, but they did not take any extra olive oil for the lantern.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 8:9; 1 JOHN 2:27; 1 JOHN 2:20"> But the virgins who were wise took oil in their flasks as well as in their lanterns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:3; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:6–8; 2 PETER 3:4–9"> The bridegroom was delayed, and it became late at night. So all the virgins became sleepy and fell asleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 PETER 3:10; MATTHEW 24:31; MATTHEW 24:44"> In the middle of the night someone woke them up by shouting, ‘Hey! The bridegroom is arriving! Go outside and meet him!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 12:35; REVELATION 3:19–20; 2 PETER 3:14"> So all the virgins got up and trimmed their lantern wicks so that they would burn properly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:9; LUKE 12:35; HEBREWS 4:1"> The foolish virgins said to the wise ones, ‘Give us some of your olive oil, because our lanterns are about to go out!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 49:7–9; REVELATION 3:17–18; Isaiah 55:6–7"> The wise virgins replied, ‘No, because there might not be enough oil for our lanterns and for yours. Go to a store and buy some for yourselves!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 13:25; MATTHEW 24:44; LUKE 12:36–37"> But while the foolish virgins were going to buy some oil, the bridegroom arrived. So the wise virgins, who were ready, went with the bridegroom to the wedding hall where the bride was waiting. Then the door was closed &#123;someone closed the door of that hall&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:21–23; HEBREWS 12:16–17"> Later, the rest of the virgins came to the hall and they called to the bridegroom, ‘Sir, open the door for us!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 8:3; 2 TIMOTHY 2:19; JOHN 10:27"> But he said to them, ‘The truth is that I do not know you, so I will not open the door for you!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:42–44; REVELATION 16:15; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:6"> Then Jesus continued by saying, “So, in order that this will not happen to you, keep expecting that I will return, because you do not know when &#91;MTY&#93; it will be.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus told a parable to teach his disciples about making good use of what God has given them.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARK 13:34; LUKE 19:12–27; 1 PETER 4:9–11"> “When I return from heaven as king, I will deal with those who say that they have been serving me like a man who was about to go on a long journey dealt with his servants. He called them together and gave them each some of his wealth to invest and gain more money for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:24; LUKE 19:13–14; LUKE 12:48"> He gave them money according to their ability to take care of it. For example, he gave one servant 5,000 dollars, he gave another servant 2,000 dollars, and he gave another servant 1,000 dollars. Then he left on his journey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="3 JOHN 1:5–8; 1 TIMOTHY 6:17–18; 2 TIMOTHY 2:6"> The servant who had received 5,000 dollars went immediately and used that money to gain 5,000 dollars more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:9–10; EPHESIANS 5:16; 1 PETER 4:10"> Similarly, the servant who had received 2,000 dollars gained two thousand dollars more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 PETER 1:8; Haggai 1:2–4; MALACHI 1:10"> But the servant who had received one thousand dollars went and dug a hole in the ground and hid the money there to keep it safe.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 3:12–15; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:10; ROMANS 14:7–12"> After a long time the servants’ boss returned. He called them together to find out what they had done with his money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ACTS 20:24; LUKE 19:16–17; COLOSSIANS 1:29"> The servant who had received 5,000 dollars brought two bags, each containing 5,000 dollars. He said, ‘Boss, you gave me 5,000 dollars to take care of. Look, I have gained 5,000 more!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:23; LUKE 16:10; JOHN 12:26"> His boss replied, ‘You are a very good and faithful servant! You have done very well! You have faithfully handled a small amount of money, so I will put you in charge of a lot of things. Come and be happy with me!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 8:12; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:7–8; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:1–3"> The servant who had received 2,000 dollars also came and said, ‘Boss, you gave me 2,000 dollars to take care of. Look, I have gained 2,000 more!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:21; MARK 14:8–9; MARK 12:41–44"> His boss replied, ‘You are a very good and faithful servant! You have done very well! You have faithfully handled a small amount of money, so I will put you in charge of a lot of things. Come and be happy with me!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 19:20–22; LUKE 15:29; MALACHI 3:14–15"> Then the servant who had received 1,000 dollars came. He said, ‘Boss, I was afraid of you. I knew that you are a man who does not do foolish things with his money. You even take from others money that does not really belong to you, like a farmer who harvests grain from another man’s field where he did not even plant the seeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 1:6–7; ROMANS 8:15; REVELATION 21:8"> So because I was afraid of what you would do to me if I did not make a profit from my business, I went out and hid your 1,000 dollars in the ground to keep it safe. Here, this is the money that belongs to you!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:32; JOB 15:5–6"> His boss replied, ‘You wicked, lazy servant! ◄You knew that I take from others money that does not really belong to me, like a farmer who harvests grain from another man’s field where I did not even plant the seeds!/Did you not know that I take from others money that does not really belong to me, like a farmer who harvests grain from another man’s field where I did not even plant the seeds?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:19–20; ROMANS 3:19; JUDE 1:15"> So then, you should have put my money on deposit in a bank, so that when I returned I would get it back with the interest it earned!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 19:24; LUKE 10:42"> Then the boss said to his other servants, ‘Take the 1,000 dollars from him, and give it to the servant who has the 10,000 dollars!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKE 8:18; MARK 4:25; MATTHEW 13:12"> To those who use well what they have received, God will give more, and they will have plenty. But from those who do not use well what they have received, even what they already have will be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:12; REVELATION 3:15–16; MATTHEW 13:50"> Furthermore, throw that worthless servant outside, into the darkness, where he will be with those who are wailing and gnashing their teeth because they are in much pain.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus told a parable about how he will judge between those who have shown kindness to others and those who have not.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="REVELATION 1:7; 2 THESSALONIANS 1:7–8; MATTHEW 19:28"> “When I, the one who came from heaven, come again, I will be very radiant and bring all my angels with me. Then I will sit as a king on my wonderful throne to judge people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MALACHI 3:18; MATTHEW 13:49; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:10"> People from all nations &#91;MTY&#93; will be gathered in front of me &#123;The angels will gather in front of me people from all nations&#125;. Then I will separate them, one from another, as a shepherd separates his sheep from his goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 100:3; JOHN 10:26–28; JOHN 21:15–17"> I will put the righteous people on my right and the unrighteous ones on my left, as a shepherd puts sheep on one side and goats on the other side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 PETER 1:19–20; JAMES 2:5; REVELATION 21:7"> Then I will say to those on my right, ‘You people who have been blessed by my Father &#123;whom my Father has blessed&#125;, come! From the time he created the world, he has been preparing to allow you to receive the blessings that he will give to all those who let him rule their lives. Now is the time for you to receive those blessings</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 JOHN 3:16–19; JAMES 2:15–16; DEUTERONOMY 15:7–11"> They belong to you, because you gave me something to eat when I was hungry. (OR, They belong to you. It is as though you gave me something to eat when I was hungry.) You gave me something to drink when I was thirsty. When I was a stranger in your town, you invited me to stay in your houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JAMES 1:27; 2 TIMOTHY 1:16–18; JAMES 5:14–15"> When I needed clothes &#91;MTY&#93;, you gave me some. When I was sick, you took care of me. When I was in prison, you came to visit me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:3; 1 CHRONICLES 29:14; 1 PETER 5:5–6"> Then the righteous people will reply, ‘Lord, when were you hungry and we saw you and gave you something to eat? When were you thirsty and we gave you something to drink?</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When were you a stranger in our town and we invited you to stay in our houses? When did you need clothes and we gave you some?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> When were you sick or in prison and we went to visit you?’ We do not remember doing any of these things for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:17; PROVERBS 14:31; MATTHEW 10:42"> I will reply, ‘The truth is that whatever you did for any one of your fellow believers, even an unimportant one, it was as though you did it for me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="2 PETER 2:4; JUDE 1:6–7; 2 THESSALONIANS 1:9"> Then I will say to those on my left, ‘You people who have been cursed by God &#123;whom God has cursed&#125;, leave me! Go into the eternal fire that God has prepared for ◄the devil/Satan► and his angels!</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JAMES 2:15–24; JOHN 5:23; MATTHEW 12:30"> It is right for you to go there, because you did not give me anything to eat when I was hungry. You did not give me anything to drink when I was thirsty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> You did not invite me into your homes when I was a stranger in your town. You did not give me any clothes when I needed them &#91;MTY&#93;. You did not take care of me when I was sick or in prison.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:22; LUKE 10:29; MATTHEW 25:24–27"> They will answer, ‘Lord, when were you hungry or thirsty or a stranger or needing clothes or sick or in prison, and we did not help you?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:13; PROVERBS 17:5; PROVERBS 14:31"> I will reply, ‘The truth is that whenever you did not do anything to help any one of my people, even if they were unimportant people, it was as though you did not do anything to help me.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2; JOHN 3:36; JOHN 5:29"> Then those people on my left will go away to the place where they will be punished &#123;God will punish them&#125; eternally, but the righteous people will go to where they will live forever with God.”</span>
</div>
</div>
