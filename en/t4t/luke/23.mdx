## Jesus was accused before Pilate, the Roman governor.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:1–2; MATTHEW 27:11–14; LUKE 22:66"> Then the whole group got up and took Jesus to Pilate, the Roman governor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ACTS 17:6–7; JOHN 19:12; LUKE 23:14"> They began to accuse Jesus, saying, “We (exc) have determined that this fellow has caused political trouble in our country. <span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 1:49; 1 TIMOTHY 6:13; LUKE 22:70"> Pilate asked him, “Do you (sg) claim that you are the King of the Jews?” He replied, “It is as you have just now said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 18:38; LUKE 23:14–15; LUKE 23:22"> Pilate said to the chief priests and the rest of the crowd, “I do not conclude that this man is guilty of any crime.”</span>

## After Pilate sent Jesus to Herod, Jesus refused to answer Herod’s questions.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ACTS 5:37; LUKE 13:1"> When Pilate heard that, he asked, “Is this man from Galilee district?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 3:1; LUKE 13:31; MATTHEW 14:1"> When they told him that Jesus was from Galilee, which was the district that Herod Antipas ruled, he told them to take Jesus to Herod Antipas, because Herod was in Jerusalem at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 9:7–9; MATTHEW 14:1; MARK 6:14"> So they did. When Herod saw Jesus, he was very happy. He had heard about Jesus, and he had been hoping for a long time that he could see Jesus perform a miracle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:14; MATTHEW 7:6; 1 PETER 2:23"> So he asked Jesus many questions, but Jesus did not reply to any of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 11:53; LUKE 23:14–15; LUKE 23:5"> The chief priests and men who taught the Jewish laws stood near Jesus, accusing him very strongly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MARK 15:16–20; MATTHEW 27:27–30; PSALMS 69:19–20"> Then Herod and his soldiers ◄made fun of/ridiculed► Jesus. They put gorgeous clothes on him to mock him as being a king. Then Herod sent him back to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ACTS 4:27; REVELATION 17:13–14; PSALMS 83:4–6"> Until that time Herod and Pilate had been very hostile to each other, but that very day they became friends.</span>
</div>

## Pilate was unsuccessful in his efforts to set Jesus free.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARK 15:14; MATTHEW 27:21–23; JOHN 18:38"> Pilate then gathered together the chief priests and other Jewish leaders and the crowd that was still there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 6:4; HEBREWS 7:26; LUKE 23:1–2"> He said to them, “You brought this man to me, saying that he had caused trouble among the people. <span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Obviously Herod did not conclude that either, because he sent him back to me without punishing him. So it is clear that this man has not done anything for which we (inc) should kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:26; LUKE 23:22; MARK 15:15"> So I will have my soldiers flog him and then release him.”<sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="-17 Pilate had to release one prisoner for the people during the Passover festival each year.">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 21:36; ACTS 3:14; ACTS 22:22"> But the whole crowd shouted, “Execute this man! Release Barrabas for us (exc)!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ACTS 3:14; LUKE 23:5; LUKE 23:2"> Barrabas was a man who had tried to persuade people in the city to rebel against the Roman government. <span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARK 15:15; MATTHEW 14:8–9; MATTHEW 27:19"> Because Pilate wanted to release Jesus, he tried to persuade the crowd again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:22–25; MARK 15:13; JOHN 19:15"> But they kept shouting, “Command your soldiers to kill him by nailing him to a cross!/Have him crucified!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKE 23:14; LUKE 23:20; 1 PETER 3:18"> He spoke to them a third time and asked them, “Why? What crime has he committed? <span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:8; PSALMS 57:4; PSALMS 22:12–13"> But they kept insisting. <span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:26; EXODUS 23:2; PROVERBS 17:15"> Pilate decided to do what the crowd requested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ACTS 3:14; MARK 15:6–7; MARK 15:15"> The man who had been &#123;whom they had&#125; put in prison because he had rebelled against the government and who had murdered someone, he released! That was the man whom the crowd had asked him to release. He handed Jesus over to the soldiers, to do what the crowd wanted.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The soldiers nailed Jesus to a cross.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:32–44; MARK 15:21–32; ACTS 6:9"> As the soldiers were taking Jesus away, they seized a man named Simon, who was from Cyrene city in Africa. Then they put it on Simon’s shoulders, and told him to carry it behind Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 8:52; LUKE 8:2; MARK 15:40"> A large crowd followed Jesus. The crowd included many women who were beating their breasts to show how sad they were, and wailing for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 8:4; SONG OF SONGS 5:8; SONG OF SONGS 1:5"> But Jesus turned to them and said, “You women of Jerusalem, do not cry for me! Instead, cry because of what is going to happen to yourselves and your children!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:19; LUKE 21:23–24; MARK 13:17–19"> I want you to know that there will soon be a time when people will say, ‘Women &#91;SYN&#93; who have never borne children or nursed babies are fortunate!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="REVELATION 6:16; HOSEA 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> Then, in order that they will not have to suffer greatly when their enemies destroy this city, people who are left in the city will say to the mountains, ‘Fall down on us!’ And people will say to the hills, ‘Cover us!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 PETER 4:17–18; JOHN 15:6; PROVERBS 11:31"> I am innocent &#91;MET&#93;. I am like a living tree that people do not try to burn. ## The things that happened when Jesus died.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARK 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; MATTHEW 27:38"> Two other men who were criminals were also being led away &#123;The soldiers also led away two other men who were criminals&#125;. They were going to be executed &#123;The soldiers were going to execute them&#125; with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MARK 15:22–23; JOHN 19:17–18; JOHN 18:32"> When they came to the place which is called ‘The Skull’, they nailed Jesus to a cross after removing his clothes. They did the same thing to the two criminals. They crucified one at the right side of Jesus and one at his left side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:44; ACTS 7:60; 1 PETER 3:9"> Jesus prayed, “My Father, forgive them, because they do not realize whom they are doing this to!” Then the soldiers divided Jesus’ clothing by gambling with something like dice, to decide which piece of clothing each one would get.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PSALMS 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 PETER 2:4"> Many people stood nearby, watching. And the Jewish leaders ◄made fun of/ridiculed► Jesus, saying, “He said &#91;IRO&#93; that he saved other people! If he is the Messiah, the one God has chosen, he should save himself!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PSALMS 69:21; MATTHEW 27:48; MATTHEW 27:34"> The soldiers also ridiculed him because he claimed to be a king. They came up to him and offered him some sour wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> They said to him, “If you (sg) are the King of the Jews, save yourself!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MARK 15:26; MATTHEW 27:37; JOHN 19:19–22"> They also fastened on the cross a sign that stated why they were nailing him to the cross. But all it said was, ‘This is the King of the Jews’.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:44; MARK 15:32; LUKE 23:35"> One of the criminals who was hanging on a cross began to insult Jesus, saying, “You (sg) said that you are the Messiah, so you must be powerful! So save yourself, and save us, too!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:11; LUKE 12:5; REVELATION 15:4"> But the other criminal rebuked him, saying, “◄You (sg) should be afraid of God punishing you!/Are you not afraid of God punishing you?► &#91;RHQ&#93; They are punishing him and us similarly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 PETER 1:19"> They have justly decided that we (inc) two must die. They are punishing us as we deserve for the evil things that we did. But this man has done nothing wrong!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="ROMANS 10:9–14; ACTS 16:31; 1 JOHN 5:11–13"> Then he said, “Jesus, please remember me and take care of me when you become king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="REVELATION 2:7; PSALMS 50:15; 1 TIMOTHY 1:15–16"> Jesus replied, “I want you (sg) to know that today you (sg) will be with me in the place where everyone is happy!” (OR, that today you will be with me in Paradise!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:45; MARK 15:33–34; AMOS 8:9"> Then it was about noontime. It became dark over the whole land, and it stayed dark until three o’clock in the afternoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:19–22; MATTHEW 27:51; HEBREWS 9:3–8"> There was no light from the sun. <span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="PSALMS 31:5; ACTS 7:59; JOHN 19:30"> As that happened, Jesus shouted loudly, “Father, I put my spirit into your care &#91;MTY&#93;!” When he said that, he &#91;EUP&#93; died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:54; MARK 15:39; LUKE 23:41"> When the officer who supervised the soldiers who were executing Jesus saw what happened, he praised God for the way Jesus died, saying, “I am sure that this man had done nothing wrong!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LUKE 18:13; ACTS 2:37; JEREMIAH 31:19"> When the crowd of people who had gathered to see those events saw what happened, they returned to their homes, beating their chests to show that they were sorrowful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LUKE 8:2; PSALMS 38:11; LUKE 23:55"> All of Jesus’ friends, including the women who had come with him from Galilee district, stood at a distance and saw everything that happened.</span>

## Joseph and others buried Jesus while some women watched.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="JOHN 19:38–42; ACTS 11:24; ACTS 10:22"> There was a man named Joseph who came there. He was from the town of Arimathea in Judea. He was a good and a righteous man, and he was a member of the Jewish Council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LUKE 2:25; LUKE 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> But he had not agreed with the other Council members when they decided to kill Jesus and when they planned how to do it. He was waiting expectantly for the time when God would send his king to begin to rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="JOHN 19:38–42"> He went to Pilate and asked Pilate to permit him to take Jesus’ body and bury it. After Pilate allowed him to do that,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; MARK 15:46"> he and some others took Jesus’ body down from the cross. They wrapped it in a linen cloth. Then they put his body in a tomb that he had hired others to dig out of a rock cliff. No one had ever put a body in it before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:62; JOHN 19:31; MARK 15:42"> They had to do it quickly because that was Friday, the day when people prepared things for the Jewish day of rest. ◄The Sabbath/The day of rest► was about to start at sunset, so they had to finish burying Jesus’ body before sunset.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="LUKE 23:49; MATTHEW 27:61; MARK 15:47"> The women who had come with Jesus from Galilee district followed Joseph and the men who were with him. They saw the tomb, and they saw how the men laid Jesus’ body inside it, and saw the men roll a huge stone across the entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="LUKE 24:1; MARK 16:1; DEUTERONOMY 5:14"> Then the women returned to the houses where they were staying. That evening they prepared spices and ointments to put on Jesus’ body, but ◄on the Sabbath/on the Jewish rest day► they rested, according to what Moses had commanded.</span>
</div>
