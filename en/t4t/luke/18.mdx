## Jesus taught about continuing to believe that God will answer prayer.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:2; ROMANS 12:12; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:17"> Jesus told his disciples a parable to teach them that they always ought to pray confidently and not be discouraged if God does not immediately answer their prayers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:7; PSALMS 8:1–4; LUKE 18:4"> He said, “In a certain city there was a judge who did not revere God, and did not care about people, either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; JEREMIAH 5:28; DEUTERONOMY 27:19"> There was a widow in that city who kept coming to him, saying, ‘Please decide what is just in the dispute between me and the man who is opposing me in court!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12–13; LUKE 16:3; LUKE 12:17"> For a long time the judge refused to help her. But later he thought to himself, ‘I do not revere God and I do not care about people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 11:8; LUKE 18:39; JUDGES 16:16"> but this widow keeps bothering me! So I will make sure that she is treated justly. If I do not do that, she will exhaust me by continually coming to me!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 7:13"> Then the Lord Jesus said, “Even though the judge was not a righteous man, think carefully about what he said!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:35–37; 2 PETER 3:9; LUKE 11:13"> Similarly, ◄God will certainly show that what you have done has been right!/will God not show that what you have done has been right?► &#91;RHQ&#93; He will do this for you whom he has chosen. He will do this for you who pray earnestly to him night and day, asking him to help you. He may delay helping you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:23–26; 2 PETER 3:8–9; MATTHEW 24:9–13"> But I tell you, some day he will show that what you did was right, and he will do it quickly. But when I, the one who came from heaven, return to earth, ◄there may not be many people who will still be trusting that I will vindicate them (OR, who will still be trusting in me)./will there be many people who will still be trusting that I will vindicate them (OR, who will still be trusting in me)?► &#91;RHQ&#93;”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught about not thinking that we are better than others in God’s sight.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 16:15; PROVERBS 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> Jesus also told a parable to warn people who mistakenly thought that they were doing things that made them acceptable to God. Besides, they also despised other people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:31–32; 1 KINGS 8:30; 2 KINGS 20:5"> He said this: “Two men went up to the Temple in Jerusalem to pray. One was a Pharisee. The other was a tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; REVELATION 3:17"> The Pharisee stood and prayed silently, ‘God, I thank you that I am not like other men. Some extort money from others; some treat others unjustly; some commit adultery. I do not do such things. And I am certainly not like this tax collector who cheats people!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 11:42; MATTHEW 23:23–24; MATTHEW 9:14"> Our law says that we(exc) should ◄fast/abstain from food► once a week, but I do more than that. I fast twice a week! I give you ten percent of all that I earn!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 JOHN 1:8–10; 1 TIMOTHY 1:15"> But the tax collector stood far from the other people in the Temple courtyard because he felt very unworthy. He would not even look up toward heaven. Instead, he beat on his chest to show that he was sorry for his sin. He said, ‘God, I am a sinner; be merciful to me and forgive me!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 14:11; LUKE 16:15; JAMES 4:10"> Then Jesus said, “I tell you (pl) that as the tax collector went home, the record of his sins was erased &#123;God erased the record of his sins&#125;, not that of the Pharisee. Remember this: Those who exalt themselves will be humbled &#123;God will humble all those who exalt themselves&#125;, but those who humble themselves will be exalted &#123;he will exalt those who humble themselves&#125;.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught about accepting God’s rule in our lives as easily as little children do.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MARK 10:13–16; MATTHEW 19:13–15; 1 SAMUEL 1:24"> One day when many people were coming to Jesus, they were also bringing small children. They wanted him to put his hands on the children and bless them. When the disciples saw that, they rebuked those who were bringing those children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:3–4; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:20; 1 CORINTHIANS 7:14"> But Jesus called the children to come to him. He said to his disciples, “Let the children come to me! Do not stop them! It is people who are humble and trusting like they are who can experience God ruling their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:3; MARK 10:15; PSALMS 131:1–2"> Note this: Those who do not trust God and allow him to direct their lives, as children do, will not enter the &#91;MET&#93; place where God rules.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught that rich people do not easily let God rule their lives.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARK 10:17–31; ACTS 16:30; MATTHEW 19:16–30"> A Jewish leader asked Jesus, “Good teacher, what shall I do in order to have eternal life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 15:14–16; JAMES 1:17; HEBREWS 7:26"> Jesus said to him, “Only God is good! No one else is good! ◄So you(sg) should consider carefully what you are implying by calling me good!/Do you realize that you are implying that I am God by calling me good?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12–17; DEUTERONOMY 5:16–21; ROMANS 13:9"> But to answer your question, you (sg) know the commandments that God gave Moses. He commanded such things as ‘do not commit adultery, do not commit murder, do not steal, do not testify falsely about what you have seen or heard, honor your father and mother.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:6; ROMANS 10:2–3; MARK 10:20–21"> The man said, “I have obeyed all those commandments ever since I was young. So ◄there must be something else I have not done./is that enough?►”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LUKE 12:33; 1 TIMOTHY 6:18–19; MATTHEW 19:21"> When Jesus heard him say that, he replied to him, “There is one thing that you (sg) have not done yet. Sell all that you own. Then give the money to poor people. The result will be that you will have spiritual riches in heaven. Then come and be my disciple!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:31; 1 JOHN 2:15; JOB 31:24–25"> The man became sad when he heard that, because he was very rich and he did not want to give everything away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:9; JAMES 2:5–7; MARK 10:23–27"> Jesus looked at the man as he left, and said, “It is very difficult for those who are wealthy to decide to let God rule &#91;MET&#93; their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:24"> You would say that it is impossible for a camel to go through the eye of a needle. It is almost as difficult &#91;HYP&#93; for rich people to decide to let God rule their lives.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKE 13:23"> The Jews thought that God favored rich people, so they thought that if God did not save rich people, he would not save others, either. So one of the disciples who heard him say that replied, “If that is so, it seems that no one will be saved &#123;that God will not save anyone&#125; &#91;RHQ&#93;!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:17; MATTHEW 19:26; LUKE 1:37"> But Jesus said, “It is impossible for people to save themselves. But God can save them, because God can do anything!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MARK 10:28; PHILIPPIANS 3:7; MATTHEW 19:27"> Then Peter said, “You know that we(exc) have left everything we had and have become your disciples &#91;RHQ&#93;. So what about us? (OR, So will God accept/save us?”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MARK 10:29–31; MATTHEW 10:37–39; LUKE 14:26–28"> He said to them, “Keep this in mind: Those who have left their homes, their wives, their brothers, their parents, their children, or any other family members, to tell others about how God wants to rule &#91;MET&#93; people’s lives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:5–6; JOB 42:10; REVELATION 2:17"> will receive in this life many times as much as they left. And in the future age they will ◄live eternally with God/ have eternal life►.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">

We (inc) are now going up to Jerusalem. While we are there, everything that has been written by the prophets &#123;that the prophets have written&#125; about me, the one who came from heaven, will be fulfilled &#123;will occur&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> I will be put into the hands of &#123;My enemies will hand me over to&#125; non-Jews. The non-Jews will make fun of me and mistreat me and spit on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:3–4; LUKE 24:7; MATTHEW 27:63"> They will whip me, and then they will kill me. But on the third day after that I will become alive again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="LUKE 9:45; MARK 9:32; LUKE 24:25"> But the disciples did not understand any of those things that he said. They were prevented &#123;Something prevented them&#125; from understanding the meaning of what he was telling them.</span>

## Jesus healed a blind man.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:29–34; MARK 10:46–52; LUKE 16:20–21"> As Jesus and his disciples came near to Jericho city, a blind man was sitting beside the road. He was begging for money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:10–11; LUKE 15:26"> When he heard the crowd of people passing by, he asked someone, “What is happening?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:23; JOHN 19:19; JOHN 12:35–36"> They told him, “Jesus, the man from Nazareth town, is passing by.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:27; LUKE 18:39; MATTHEW 15:22"> He shouted, “Jesus, you who are descended from King David, the Messiah, pity me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GENESIS 32:26–28; PSALMS 141:1; LUKE 18:15"> Those who were walking at the front of the crowd scolded the man and told him to be quiet. But he shouted more loudly, “You who are descended from King David, the Messiah, pity me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:31–34; MARK 10:48–52"> Jesus stopped and told people to bring the man to him. When the blind man came near, Jesus asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 KINGS 3:5–15; MATTHEW 20:21–22; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> “What do you (sg) want me to do for you?” He replied, “Lord, enable me to see again!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:3; LUKE 8:48; LUKE 17:19"> Jesus said to him, “Then see! Because you have trusted &#91;PRS&#93; in me, I have healed you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="PSALMS 146:8; 2 THESSALONIANS 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> Immediately he was able to see! And he went with Jesus, praising God. And when all the people who were going with Jesus saw it, they also praised God.</span>
