## The story about four different kinds of soil.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MARK 4:1–9; MATTHEW 13:2–9"> A large crowd of people came to Jesus from many towns. Then he told them this parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:1; JAMES 1:23–24; MARK 4:15"> “A man went out to his field to sow some seeds. As he was scattering them over the soil, some of the seeds fell on the path. Then people walked on those seeds, and birds ate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 3:7–8; LUKE 8:13; MARK 4:5–6"> Some of the seeds fell on rock on which there was a thin layer of moist soil. As soon as the seeds grew, the plants dried up because they had no moisture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:3; LUKE 8:14; LUKE 21:34"> Some of the seeds fell on ground that contained roots of thorny weeds. The thorny weeds grew together with the young plants, and they crowded out those plants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:15; REVELATION 2:11; REVELATION 2:7"> But some of the seeds fell on good soil and grew well. Each seed later produced 100 grains.” Then Jesus called out to them, “If you want to understand &#91;MTY&#93; this, you must consider carefully what I have just said!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus explained that the story represented different ways people respond to his teaching.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARK 4:10; MATTHEW 13:10; MATTHEW 13:18"> Jesus’ disciples later asked him about the meaning of the parable he had just told.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; MARK 4:11; MATTHEW 11:25"> He said, “God is revealing to you what he did not reveal before, about how he wants to rule &#91;MET&#93; over people’s lives. But when I am telling other people about these things, I tell them only in parables. The result will be like a prophet predicted long ago:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will see what I am doing, but will not perceive what it means. They will hear what I am saying, but will not understand it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:19; MARK 4:14–20; JAMES 1:21"> So this is the meaning of that parable: The seeds represent God’s message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 2:9–14; REVELATION 12:9; JAMES 1:23–24"> Some people are like the path on which some of the seeds fell. When these people hear God’s message, the devil comes and causes them to forget what they have heard. As a result, they do not believe it, and will not be saved &#123;God will not save them&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HOSEA 6:4; JOHN 5:35; PROVERBS 12:12"> Some people are like the soil with rock underneath. When they hear God’s message, they accept it joyfully. But because it does not penetrate deeply &#91;MET&#93; into their inner beings, they believe it for only a short time. They are like the plants that did not have deep roots. When difficult things happen to them, they stop believing God’s message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 JOHN 2:15–17; 1 TIMOTHY 6:9–10; 1 TIMOTHY 6:17"> Some people are like the soil that had roots of thorny weeds in it. They are people who accept God’s message, but later on they worry about things, and try to become rich, and enjoy things that give them a lot of pleasure. So they do not become spiritually mature.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:36; GALATIANS 5:22–26; COLOSSIANS 1:10"> But some people are like the good soil. They hear God’s message and accept it. They become good, honest people, and they continue doing things that please God &#91;MET&#93;.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus explained the need to make his truth clear to others.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 11:33; MATTHEW 5:15–16; MARK 4:21–22"> “After people light a lamp, they do not cover it with a basket or put it under a bed. Instead, they put it on a lampstand, so that those who enter their house can see things from its light &#91;MET&#93;. Similarly, you must tell God’s truth to others so that they can know what I can do for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 12:2–3; MATTHEW 10:26; MARK 4:22"> God wants to make clear all of his truth that was not evident previously. He intends that all of the truth that he had not revealed before be told openly &#123;that his people openly tell others all of the truth that he has not revealed before&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:12; LUKE 19:26; MARK 4:23–25"> So listen carefully to what I tell you, because God will enable those who accept my truth to understand even more. But God will cause those who do not accept my truth to not understand even the little that they think they have understood.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>

## Jesus calmed a storm.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:23–27; MARK 4:35–41; MARK 6:45"> One day Jesus got into a boat with his disciples. He said to them, “Let’s go around to the other side of the lake.” So they started to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 93:3–4; PSALMS 124:2–4; PSALMS 148:8"> As they were sailing, he fell asleep. A big windstorm struck the lake. Soon the boat was filling with water, and they were in danger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 107:25–29; LUKE 4:39; PSALMS 104:6–9"> Jesus’ disciples woke him up. They said to him, “Master! Master! We (inc) are going to drown!” He got up and rebuked the wind and told the waves to be calm. The wind immediately stopped blowing and the waves calmed down. The lake became calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:26; PROVERBS 30:4; MARK 4:40–41"> He said to them, “◄I am disappointed that you did not trust me to help you!/Why did you not trust me to help you?►” &#91;RHQ&#93; They were afraid. They were also amazed. They said to each other, “◄What kind of man is this?/This man is not like ordinary men!► &#91;RHQ&#93; He not only commands people, but when he commands the wind and the water, they also obey him!”</span>
</div>

## Jesus expelled many demons from a man.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARK 5:1–20; MATTHEW 8:28–34"> Jesus and his disciples arrived at the region near Gerasa, a town which was on the eastern side of Lake Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MARK 5:2–5; 1 SAMUEL 19:24; NUMBERS 19:16"> There was a man in that region in whom demons lived. He was from the town of Gerasa. (OR, In that area there was a man from that town whom demons controlled.) For a long time he had not worn clothes. He did not live in a house. Instead, he lived in burial caves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:29; 2 PETER 2:4; MARK 5:6–8"> Many times demons attacked him. He was guarded &#123;People tried to guard him&#125; so that he would not injure others. Chains were fastened &#123;They fastened chains&#125; on his hands and shackles on his feet, but he would break the chains and shackles. Then he would be sent away by the demons &#123;the demons would send him away&#125; to some desolate area.<br />When Jesus stepped out of the boat onto the shore, the man saw him. He ran to Jesus and prostrated himself before him. Then Jesus commanded, “You evil spirit, come out of that man!” But it did not leave immediately. Instead, it caused the man to shout very loudly, “Jesus, Son of the great God in heaven, since we (inc) have nothing in common, leave me alone &#91;RHQ&#93;! I beg you, do not torture me now!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:53; LUKE 8:2; MATTHEW 8:29"> In order to expel the demon more easily, Jesus asked the demon, “What is your (sg) name?” He replied, “My name is Crowd/Mob.” He said that because many demons had entered that man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:41; PHILIPPIANS 2:10–11; JOB 2:5"> The demons kept begging Jesus that he would not command them to go into the deep place where God punishes demons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3; REVELATION 20:7"> There was a large herd of pigs ◄grazing/rooting for food► on the hillside. The demons begged Jesus that he allow them to enter the pigs. So he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 PETER 5:8; JOHN 8:44; LUKE 8:22–23"> The demons left the man and entered the pigs. Then the herd of pigs rushed down the steep bank into the lake and drowned.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARK 5:14; MATTHEW 8:33; MATTHEW 28:11"> When the men who were taking care of the pigs saw what happened, they ran! They told people inside the town and outside the town what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LUKE 10:39; ACTS 22:3; 1 JOHN 3:8"> So people went out to see what had happened. <span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:24"> The people who had seen what happened told those who had just arrived how Jesus had healed the man whom demons were controlling before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="ACTS 16:39; LUKE 8:28; LUKE 10:10–11"> Then those people and many other people &#91;HYP&#93; from the area near Gerasa asked Jesus to leave their area. They were very afraid of what else Jesus would do to them. So Jesus and the disciples got into the boat to go back across the lake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ACTS 9:13–16; MARK 5:18–20; PSALMS 145:3–12"> The man from whom he had expelled the demons begged him saying, “Please, let me go with you!” But Jesus refused. Instead, he said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="LUKE 17:15–18; 1 TIMOTHY 5:8; DEUTERONOMY 10:21"> “No, go home and tell people how much God has done for you!” Then he sent him away. So the man went away and told people throughout the nearby town how much Jesus had done for him.</span>
</div>

## Jesus caused a young girl to become alive again, and healed a woman who had been bleeding for years.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:1; MARK 5:21; MARK 6:20"> When Jesus and the disciples returned to Capernaum, a crowd of people who had been waiting for him welcomed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MARK 5:22–43; LUKE 13:14; ACTS 18:8"> Just then a man named Jairus, who was one of the leaders ◄of the synagogue/of the Jewish meeting place► there, came near and prostrated himself at Jesus’ feet. He pleaded with Jesus, “Please come to my house and heal my daughter,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="LUKE 7:12; LUKE 8:45; JOB 1:18–19"> my only daughter, who is about twelve years old, because she is dying!”<br />As Jesus went with him, many people crowded close to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MARK 5:25–26; MATTHEW 9:20–22; LEVITICUS 15:25–33"> Then a woman came near. She had been suffering from constant vaginal &#91;EUP&#93; bleeding for twelve years, but she could not be healed by anyone &#123;no one could heal her&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MARK 5:27–28; DEUTERONOMY 22:12; ACTS 19:12"> She came behind Jesus and touched the edge of his robe in order to be healed. At once her bleeding stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MARK 5:30–32; LUKE 9:13; LUKE 5:5"> Jesus said, “Who touched me?” When everyone said that they had not touched him, Peter said, “Lord, there is a large crowd of people around you (sg) and crowding against you, so any one of them might have touched you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKE 6:19; LUKE 5:17; 1 PETER 2:9"> But Jesus said, “I know that someone deliberately touched me, because my power has healed someone who touched me.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LUKE 7:50; MATTHEW 9:22; HEBREWS 4:2"> Jesus said to her, “◄Ma’am/My dear woman►, because you believed &#91;PRS&#93; that I could heal you, I have healed you. Go from here, and may you experience God’s peace within you.”</span>

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="MARK 5:35–43; Isaiah 7:12; LUKE 7:6"> While he was still talking to her, a man from Jairus’ house came and said to Jairus, “Your daughter has died. <span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="JOHN 11:40; JOHN 11:25; MARK 5:36"> But when Jesus heard that, he said to Jairus, “Do not think that there is nothing more that can be done. Just believe that I can help her. If you(sg) do that, she will live again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="MARK 14:33; MATTHEW 6:5–6; 1 KINGS 17:19–23"> When they arrived outside Jairus’ house, Jesus allowed only Peter, John, James, and the girl’s mother and father to go in Jairus’ house with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="LUKE 23:27; JOHN 11:4; ZECHARIAH 12:10"> All the people there were crying and beating their chests to show that they were sad. <span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="LUKE 16:14; Isaiah 53:3; JOHN 11:39"> The people laughed at him, because they knew that the girl was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:32; JOHN 11:43; MARK 1:31"> But Jesus took hold of her hand and called to her, saying, “Child, get up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="JOHN 11:44; 1 KINGS 17:21–23; MARK 5:43"> At once her spirit returned to her body and she got up. Jesus immediately said, “Give her something to eat!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:4; MATTHEW 9:30; LUKE 5:14"> Her parents were amazed, but Jesus told them not to tell anyone else yet what had happened.</span>
