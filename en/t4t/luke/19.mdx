## Zacchaeus changed his way of life after talking with Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:1–27; LUKE 18:35; 2 KINGS 2:18–22"> Jesus and his disciples entered Jericho and were going through the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 18:24–27; 2 CHRONICLES 17:5–6"> There was a man there named Zacchaeus. He was a chief tax collector, who was rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 23:8; LUKE 9:7–9; JOHN 12:21"> He tried to see Jesus, but he was very short, and there was a big crowd of people near Jesus. So he was not able to see him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 9:10; LUKE 5:19; 1 KINGS 10:27"> So he ran further ahead along the road that Jesus was walking on. He climbed a sycamore-fig tree to see Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="REVELATION 3:20; JOHN 14:23; HEBREWS 13:2"> When Jesus got there, he looked up and said to him, “Zacchaeus, come down quickly, because God wants me to go with you(sg) to your house and stay there tonight”!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 5:29; ACTS 16:34; ACTS 16:15"> So he came down quickly. He took Jesus to his house and welcomed him joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 15:2; LUKE 7:39; LUKE 7:34"> The people who saw Jesus go there grumbled saying, “He has gone to be the guest of a man who is a sinner!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:6; NUMBERS 5:7; LUKE 18:22–23"> Then Zacchaeus stood up while they were eating and said to the Lord Jesus, “Lord, I want you (sg) to know that I am going to give half of what I own to poor people. And as for the people whom I have cheated, I will pay them back four times the amount I have gotten from them by cheating.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:7; LUKE 13:16; ROMANS 4:16"> Jesus said to him, “Today God has forgiven &#91;PRS&#93; you and the other people in &#91;MTY&#93; this house, because you (sg) also have shown that you have trusted in God as your ancestor Abraham did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:16; MATTHEW 1:21; 1 TIMOTHY 1:13–16"> Remember this: I, the one who came from heaven, came to seek and save people like you(sg) who have gone astray from God, just like a shepherd who searches for his lost sheep.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught about being willing to take risks in using what he has given us.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 17:20; ACTS 1:6; 2 THESSALONIANS 2:1–3"> They were coming near to Jerusalem, and the people who were going with Jesus who heard him say these things thought that as soon as he got to Jerusalem he would become their king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:14–30; LUKE 19:12–27; LUKE 20:9"> So he told them this parable: “A prince prepared to go to a distant country in order that the Emperor would make him a king. He intended to return later.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:14–15; JOHN 12:26; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:7–11"> Before he left, he summoned ten of his servants. He gave each of them a coin worth three months’ wages. He said to them, ‘Do business with these coins until I return!’ Then he left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 8:7; ACTS 7:51–52; ACTS 3:14–15"> But many of his fellow-citizens hated him. So after he left they sent some messengers to tell the Emperor, ‘We (exc) do not want this man to be our king!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 4:1–5; ROMANS 14:10–12; PSALMS 2:4–6"> But the Emperor made him king anyway. Later the new king returned. Then he commanded that the servants to whom he had given the coins should be summoned &#123;that someone should summon the servants to whom he had given the coins&#125;. He wanted to know how much they had gained by doing business with the coins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JAMES 2:18–26; 2 TIMOTHY 4:7–8; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:10"> The first man came to him and said, ‘Sir, with your one coin I have earned ten more coins!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 16:10; MATTHEW 25:21; REVELATION 2:26–29"> He said to this man, ‘You are a good servant! You have done very well! Because you have handled faithfully a small amount of money, I will give you authority to rule ten cities.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARK 4:20; MATTHEW 13:23; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:12"> Then the second servant came and said, ‘Sir, with your one coin I have earned five more coins!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:58; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:41–42; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:8"> He said to that servant similarly, ‘Good! I will give you authority to rule five cities.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:24; PROVERBS 26:13–16; JAMES 4:17"> Then another servant came. He said, ‘Sir, here is your coin. I wrapped it in a napkin and put it away, so that nothing would happen to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:24–25; ROMANS 8:7; 1 JOHN 4:18"> I did that because I was afraid of what you would do to me if the business failed. I know you are a man who does not do foolish things with your money. You even take from others money that does not really belong to you, like a farmer who harvests grain from another man’s field where he did not even do the planting.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 1:16; MATTHEW 12:37; JOB 15:5–6"> The king said to that servant, ‘You wicked servant! I will condemn you by the very words &#91;MTY&#93; that you have just now spoken. ◄You know that I do not do foolish things with my money./Did you not know that I do not do foolish things with my money?► &#91;RHQ&#93; You said that I even take from others money that does not really belong to me, like a farmer who harvests grain from another man’s field where he did not even do the planting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 22:25–27; ROMANS 2:4–5; DEUTERONOMY 23:19–20"> So ◄you should at least have given my money to money lenders!/why did you not at least give my money to bankers?► &#91;RHQ&#93; Then when I returned I could have collected that amount plus the interest it would have earned!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 16:2; LUKE 12:20"> Then the king said to those who were standing near, ‘Take the coin from him and give it to the servant who has ten coins!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="LUKE 16:2; 2 SAMUEL 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> They protested, ‘But Sir, he already has ten coins!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 JOHN 1:8; LUKE 8:18; MATTHEW 21:43"> But the king said, ‘I tell you this: To the people who use well what they have received, more will be given &#123;I will give more&#125;. But from the people who do not use well what they have received, even what they already have will be taken away &#123;I will take away even what they already have&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 19:14; PSALMS 21:8–9; MATTHEW 22:7"> Now, as for those enemies of mine who did not want me to rule over them, bring them here and execute them while I am watching!’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus entered Jerusalem humbly, but like a king.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 9:51; HEBREWS 12:2; 1 PETER 4:1"> After Jesus said those things, he continued on the road up to Jerusalem, going ahead of his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ACTS 1:12; MATTHEW 21:17; MATTHEW 21:1–11"> When they got near Bethphage and Bethany villages, near the hill that is called &#123;that they call&#125; Olive Tree Hill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKE 22:8–13; LUKE 19:32; 1 SAMUEL 10:2–9"> he said to two of his disciples, “Go to the village just ahead of you. As you two enter it, you will see a young animal that no one has ever ridden, that has been &#123;someone has&#125; tied up. Untie it and bring it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 24:1; PSALMS 50:10–12; MARK 11:3–6"> If anyone asks you, ‘Why are you untying it?’ say to him, ‘The Lord needs it.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 22:13"> So the two disciples went to the village and found the animal, just like he had told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> As they were untying it, its owners said to them, “Why are you two untying that young animal?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:9; JOHN 10:35; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:9"> They replied, “The Lord needs it.” So the owners said that they could take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MARK 11:7–8; MATTHEW 21:7; JOHN 12:14"> The two disciples brought the animal to Jesus. They threw their cloaks on the animal’s back for him to sit on and helped Jesus get on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:13; MATTHEW 21:8"> Then as he rode along, others spread their cloaks on the road to honor him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JOHN 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; MATTHEW 21:1"> As they came near to Jerusalem, on the road that descends from Olive Tree Hill, the whole crowd of his disciples began to rejoice and praise God loudly for all the great miracles that they had seen Jesus do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:9; LUKE 13:35; PSALMS 118:22–26"> They were saying things like, “May the Lord God bless our king who comes representing &#91;MTY&#93; him!” “May there be peace between God in heaven and us his people!” “May everyone praise God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="ACTS 4:1–2; MATTHEW 23:13; ACTS 4:16–18"> Some of the Pharisees who were in the crowd said to Jesus, “Teacher, rebuke your disciples for saying things like that!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; MATTHEW 3:9"> He replied, “I tell you this: If these people would be silent, the stones themselves would shout to praise me!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus prophesied that Jerusalem would be destroyed.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKE 13:34–35; JOHN 11:35; PSALMS 119:136"> When Jesus came near to Jerusalem and saw the city, he cried about its people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:29; HEBREWS 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> He said, “My disciples know what they need to do to have peace with God; I wish that even today the rest of you people knew it. But now you are unable to know &#91;MTY&#93; it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKE 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; EZEKIEL 4:2"> I want you to know this: Soon your enemies will come and will set up a barricade around your city. They will surround the city and attack it on all sides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="LUKE 21:6; LUKE 13:34–35; JOHN 3:18–21"> They will break through the walls and destroy everything. They will smash you and your people/children. When they finish destroying everything, there will not be one stone left on top of another. All this will happen because you did not recognize the time when God sent his Messiah to save you!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus expelled the merchants from the Temple courtyard, and taught there.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:12–13; MARK 11:15–17; JOHN 2:13–17"> Jesus entered Jerusalem and went into the Temple courtyard. He saw the people who were selling things there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; JEREMIAH 7:11; PSALMS 93:5"> and he began to chase them out. He said to them, “It has been written &#123;A prophet wrote&#125; in the Scriptures that God said, ‘I want my house to be a place where people pray’; but you bandits have made it a cave where you can hide!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MARK 11:18; MATTHEW 26:55; MARK 11:27–33"> Each day during that week Jesus was teaching people in the Temple courtyard. The chief priests and the men who taught the Jewish laws and other Jewish leaders tried to find a way to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:3; ACTS 16:14; JOHN 7:46–49"> But they did not find any way to do it, because all the people there listened eagerly to him and would have resisted them if they had tried to hurt him.</span>
</div>
