## Jesus taught his disciples about prayer.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 19:14; LUKE 22:39–45; ROMANS 8:26–27"> One day Jesus was somewhere praying. When he finished praying, one of his disciples said to him, “Lord, teach us what to say when we (exc) pray, as John the Baptizer taught his disciples!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:6–15; ECCLESIASTES 5:2; PSALMS 72:18–19"> He said to them, “When you pray, say things like this: ‘Father, we want you (sg) &#91;MTY&#93; to be honored/revered. We want people to let you(sg) &#91;MTY, MET&#93; rule over their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:11; MATTHEW 6:34; PROVERBS 30:8"> Give us (exc) each day the food &#91;SYN&#93; that we need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:12–15; MATTHEW 26:41; Isaiah 43:25–26"> Forgive us for the wrong things that we have done, because we forgive people for the wrong things that they do to us. Do not let us do wrong things when we are tempted &#123;someone or something tempts us&#125;.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 18:1–8"> Then he said to them, “Suppose that one of you goes to the house of a friend at midnight. Suppose that you (sg) stand outside and call out to him, ‘My friend, please lend me three buns!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Another friend of mine who is traveling has just arrived at my house, but I have no food ready to give to him!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 13:25; GALATIANS 6:17; LUKE 7:6"> Suppose that he answers you from inside his house, ‘Do not bother me! The door has been locked &#123;We(exc) have locked the door&#125; and all my family are in bed. So I cannot get up and give you (sg) anything!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 32:26; COLOSSIANS 4:12; ROMANS 15:30"> I will tell you that even if he does not want to get up and give you any food, to avoid being ashamed for not helping you because you are his friend (OR, if without being ashamed you continue asking him to do that), he certainly will get up and give you whatever you need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARK 11:24; JOHN 16:23–24; MATTHEW 21:22"> So I tell you this: Keep asking God for what you need. If you do that, he will give it to you (pl). Confidently keep expecting God to give you the things that you need, and he will give them to you &#91;MET&#93;. It will be like looking for what you need and finding it. Keep on praying urgently to God. Then God will answer you. It will be like knocking on a door so that God will open the way for you to get what you pray for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JAMES 4:3; LAMENTATIONS 3:54–58; PSALMS 31:22"> Remember that God will give things to everyone who continues to ask him for them. He will give things to whoever confidently keeps asking. He will open the way for people to get the things that they keep urgently praying for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:9; Isaiah 49:15"> If one of you had a son who asked you (sg) for a fish to eat, ◄you (sg) certainly would not give him a poisonous snake instead!/would you give him a poisonous snake instead?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="REVELATION 9:10; LUKE 10:19; EZEKIEL 2:6"> If he asked you for an egg, ◄you (sg) certainly would not give him a scorpion!/would you give him a scorpion?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:11; Isaiah 44:3–4; ROMANS 8:32"> Even though you people are evil, you know how to give good things to your children. So your Father in heaven will certainly give good things to those who ask him, including giving the Holy Spirit, who is the best gift.”</span>
</div>

## Jesus taught people various things about demons.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:22–24; MATTHEW 9:32–34; MARK 7:32–37"> One day there was a man there who, because a demon controlled him &#91;MTY&#93;, was unable to speak. After Jesus expelled the demon, the man began to talk. Most of the people there were amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:34; MATTHEW 12:24–30; JOHN 8:48"> But some of them said, “It is Beelzebub, the ruler of the demons, who enables this man to expel demons!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 6:30; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:22; MARK 8:11–12"> Other people there asked Jesus to perform a miracle to prove ◄he was the Messiah/that he had come from God► &#91;MTY/EUP&#93;. They wanted to trap him into not being able to perform a miracle or into doing something ridiculous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARK 3:23–27; MATTHEW 12:25–29; Isaiah 19:2–3"> But Jesus knew what they were thinking. So he said to them, “If the people in one nation fight against each other, their nation will be destroyed &#123;they will destroy their nation&#125;. If the people in &#91;MTY&#93; one house are divided, they will cease to remain as one family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:26; MATTHEW 12:31–34; LUKE 11:15"> Similarly, if Satan and his demons were fighting against each other, ◄his rule over them would certainly not last!/how would his rule over them last?► &#91;RHQ&#93; I say this because you are saying that I am expelling demons by the power of the ruler of his own demons</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 9:49; JOB 15:6; ROMANS 3:19"> Furthermore, if it is true that Satan enables me to expel demons, is it also true that your disciples who expel demons do so by Satan’s power &#91;RHQ&#93;? No, that is not true. So they will show that you are not thinking logically.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 8:19; MATTHEW 12:28; LUKE 10:9"> But because it is by the power &#91;MTY&#93; of God that I expel demons, I am showing you that the power of God to &#91;MET&#93; rule people’s lives has come to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARK 3:27; MATTHEW 12:29"> Then, to show that by expelling evil spirits he was making it clear that he was much more powerful than Satan, Jesus said &#91;MET&#93;, “When a strong man who has many weapons guards his own house, no one can steal the things in his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 49:24–25; Isaiah 53:12; 1 JOHN 3:8"> But when someone else who is stronger attacks that man and subdues him, he is able to take away the weapons in which the man trusted. Then he can take from that man’s house anything he wants to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:30; LUKE 9:50; MARK 9:40"> No one can be neutral. Those who do not help me are opposing me, and those who do not gather people to become my disciples are causing those people to go away from me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:43–45; 1 PETER 5:8; JOB 2:2"> Then Jesus said this: “Sometimes when an evil spirit leaves someone, it wanders around in desolate areas seeking someone in whom it can rest. If it does not find anyone, it says to itself, ‘I will return to the person in whom I used to live!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 2:9–12; 2 PETER 2:10–19; JUDE 1:8–13"> So it goes back and finds that the Spirit of God is not in control of that person’s life. The person’s life is like a house that has been &#123;that someone has&#125; swept clean and everything put &#123;put everything&#125; in order, but a house that is empty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 5:14; MATTHEW 12:45; 2 PETER 2:20–22"> Then this evil spirit goes and gets seven other spirits that are even more evil than it is. They all enter that person and begin living there. So, although that person’s condition was bad before, it became much worse.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 1:42; LUKE 1:48; LUKE 23:29"> When Jesus said that, a woman who was listening called out to him, “God is pleased with the woman who gave birth to you (sg) and let you nurse at her breasts!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 6:47–48; LUKE 8:21; PSALMS 112:1"> But he replied, “God is much more pleased with those who hear his message and obey it!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus rebuked the people for wanting more miracles.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKE 11:16; MARK 8:11–12; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:22"> When the group of people around Jesus got larger, he said, “Many of you people who have been observing my ministry are evil. You want me to perform a miracle to prove that I have come from God, but the only miracle that I will perform for you is one like happened to Jonah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:40–42; JONAH 1:17; JONAH 3:2–10"> After Jonah was inside a huge fish for three days, God performed a miracle to restore Jonah. Jonah then went and testified about that to the people in Nineveh city. God will perform a similar miracle for me, the one who came from heaven. When you people have seen that miracle, you will believe my message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 9:1; 1 KINGS 10:1–13; ROMANS 2:27"> Long ago the queen from Sheba, far south of Israel, traveled a long distance to hear Solomon speak many wise things. But now I, a man who is much greater and wiser than Solomon, am here, but you have not listened to what I have told you. Therefore, at the time when God judges all people, the queen from Sheba will stand there, along with you people, and will condemn you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:26; LUKE 11:31; JONAH 3:5–4"> The people who lived in Nineveh city turned from their sinful ways when Jonah preached to them. But now I, who am greater than Jonah, have come and preached to you, but you have not turned from your sinful ways. Therefore, at the time when God judges all people, the people who lived in Nineveh will stand there with you and condemn you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKE 8:16–17; MARK 4:21–22; MATTHEW 5:15–16"> Then, to show them that they did not need more miracles, but that they needed only to understand better what he had already told them, he said to them &#91;MET&#93;, “People who light a lamp do not then hide it, or put it under a basket. Instead, they put it on a lampstand so that those who enter their house can see things from its light. Similarly, I have not concealed God’s truth. I have revealed it to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:22–23; ACTS 26:18; JEREMIAH 5:21"> Your eyes &#91;MET&#93; are like a lamp for your body, because they enable you to see things. If your eyes are healthy, you are able to see everything well &#91;MET&#93;. Similarly, if you(sg) accept my teaching, you will be able to know all that God wants you to know. But if your eyes are bad, you are not able to see anything. It is like being in darkness &#91;MET&#93;. And similarly, if you(pl) do not accept what I teach, you will not be able to know all the things that God wants you to know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:25; 2 PETER 1:9; JAMES 3:13–17"> Therefore, you(pl) do not need to see more miracles. You need to think carefully about what I have already told you, so that the things that you have heard from others do not cause you to remain in spiritual darkness &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:27; PROVERBS 6:23; PROVERBS 4:18–19"> If you live completely according to God’s truth, you will be able to know everything that God wants you to know. It will be like being in a room with a lamp shining brightly, enabling you to see everything clearly.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus rebuked the Jewish leaders for only pretending to be good.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LUKE 7:36; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:19–23; LUKE 14:1"> While Jesus finished saying those things, a Pharisee invited him to eat a meal with him. So Jesus went to his house and ate with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:2–3; MARK 7:2–5; JOHN 3:25"> The Pharisee was surprised when he saw that Jesus did not follow the Pharisees’ ritual by washing his hands before eating. The Pharisees washed their hands in a certain way to be cleansed from anything that might have contaminated them. They were afraid that God might reject them if they had touched something unacceptable to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="TITUS 1:15; PROVERBS 30:12; GENESIS 6:5"> The Lord Jesus said to him, “You Pharisees are concerned about things that are outside your bodies, not with what is in your ◄inner beings/hearts► &#91;MET&#93;. You wash the outside of cups and dishes before you eat because you think that doing that will make you acceptable to God, but within yourselves you are very greedy and wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKE 12:20; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:36; MATTHEW 23:26"> You foolish people! God is concerned about things that are outside our bodies, but ◄he is certainly also concerned about our inner beings!/isn’t he also concerned about our hearts?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="LUKE 12:33; JAMES 1:27; TITUS 1:15"> Give money to those who are poor. Give according to what you know within your ◄inner beings/heart► that you should give. Then you will be surprised to realize that you will be acceptable to God without having to perform all those rituals about washing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:23; LUKE 18:12; PROVERBS 21:3"> But there will be terrible punishment for you Pharisees! You give to God a tenth of all you produce, even the various herbs that you grow, but you do not remember that you must act justly toward others and love God! It is good to give a tenth of your income to God, but you ought to do these other things also!</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="LUKE 20:46; MARK 12:38–39; LUKE 14:7–11"> There will be terrible punishment for you Pharisees, because you like to sit in the best seats in our worship places so that people will think highly of you, and you like people to greet you respectfully in the marketplaces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:27–28; PSALMS 5:9; ACTS 23:3"> There will be terrible punishment for you, because you are like ground where there is no marker to indicate that there is a grave underneath. People walk there, but they cannot see what is rotten down below &#91;MET&#93;! Similarly, people who see you do not realize how polluted you are within yourselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="JOHN 9:40; MATTHEW 22:35; JEREMIAH 6:10"> One of those who taught the Jewish laws replied, “Teacher, by saying this you (sg) are criticizing us also!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKE 11:52; Isaiah 10:1; MATTHEW 23:2–4"> Jesus said, “It will be terrible also for you who teach the Jewish laws! You require people to obey many rules that are difficult to obey &#91;MET&#93;. That is like making them carry heavy burdens on their backs. But you yourselves do not obey the laws that you require others to obey. (OR, you do not do anything to help others to obey the laws.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:15; ACTS 7:51; MATTHEW 23:29–33"> There will be terrible punishment for you! You decorate the tombs of the prophets whom your ancestors killed, but you do not live according to what the prophets taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="JAMES 5:10; MATTHEW 23:31; 2 CHRONICLES 36:16"> So you are declaring that you approve of what your ancestors did. They killed the prophets, and you are not honoring the prophets! You just decorate their tombs!</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:30; COLOSSIANS 2:3; EPHESIANS 4:11"> So God, who is very wise &#91;PRS&#93;, said, ‘I will send prophets and apostles to you Jews. You will kill some of them and cause some of them to suffer greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="GENESIS 9:5–6; REVELATION 18:20–24; 2 KINGS 24:4"> As a result, I will consider that many of you people who have observed my Son’s ministry will be guilty &#91;MTY&#93; of murder, as if you had killed all the prophets that other people have killed, from the time I created the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 24:20–22; MATTHEW 23:35; HEBREWS 11:4"> starting from Adam’s son Cain killing his brother Abel and continuing until they killed the prophet Zechariah in the holy place between the altar and the temple.’ Yes, what I am saying is true &#91;MTY&#93;. God will punish you people who have observed my ministry, you people whom he considers to be guilty &#91;MTY&#93; for killing all those prophets!</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:13; MALACHI 2:7; LUKE 11:45–46"> There will be terrible punishment for you men who teach the Jewish laws, because you have not let people know God’s truth &#91;MET&#93;! It is as though you are taking away a key to a house. You are not going into the house yourselves, and you are not letting other people enter it, either.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 13:5; JEREMIAH 20:10; LUKE 20:20"> After Jesus finished saying those things, he left there. Then the men who taught the Jewish laws and the Pharisees began to act in a very hostile way toward him. They tried to make him say what he thought about many things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="MARK 12:13; MATTHEW 22:15; MATTHEW 22:35"> They kept waiting for him to say something wrong for which they could accuse him.</span>
</div>
