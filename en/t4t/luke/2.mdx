## Jesus was born in Bethlehem.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 3:1; MATTHEW 24:14; ACTS 25:21"> About that time the Emperor Augustus commanded that ◄his officials must write down the names/a census be taken► of all the people who lived in countries controlled by the Roman government &#123;that the Roman government controlled&#125;, so that the government could collect taxes from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 3:1; MATTHEW 4:24; ACTS 26:30"> That was the first time that they ◄wrote down the names/took a census► in Syria province, which included Judea district. They did this while Quirinius was the governor of the province.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Every person had to go to the town where his most famous ancestor had lived, so that the officials could write down their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICAH 5:2; JOHN 7:42; 1 SAMUEL 16:1"> So, because Joseph was a descendant of King David, he went up from Nazareth, the town in Galilee district where he lived, to the town of Bethlehem in Judea district. That was the town where King David grew up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:22–27; MATTHEW 1:18–19"> Joseph went with Mary, who was considered &#123;whom people considered&#125; to be his wife, who was now pregnant. They went so that they could put his name and Mary’s name in the record book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 33:11; REVELATION 12:1–5; MICAH 5:2"> When they arrived in Bethlehem, there was no place for them to stay in the house where travelers stay. So they stayed in a cattle shed. When the time for Mary to give birth arrived, she gave birth to her first son there. She wrapped him in strips of cloth and placed him in a trough where people put food for the animals.</span>

## Responding to the angel’s announcement, shepherds went to see Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 31:39–40; PSALMS 78:70–71; 1 SAMUEL 17:34–35"> That night, there were some shepherds who were taking care of their sheep as usual in the fields near Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:18; 1 KINGS 8:11"> An angel from God appeared to them. A great light ◄from the Lord/from God► shone on them and around them. So they became very afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:9; Isaiah 61:1; Isaiah 52:7"> But the angel said to them, “Do not be afraid! I have come to tell you good news, which will make you very happy! This message is for everyone to hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; MATTHEW 1:21; ACTS 2:36"> The message is that today a baby has been born in Bethlehem, the town where King David grew up. That baby will eventually save you from the guilt of your sins! He is the Messiah, your Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 53:1–2; Isaiah 7:11"> I will tell you how you will recognize him (OR, how you will know that what I say is true): In Bethlehem you will find a baby who has been wrapped &#123;whose mother has wrapped him&#125; in strips of cloth and placed in a trough where people put food for the animals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 103:20–21; REVELATION 5:11; PSALMS 148:2"> Suddenly a large group of angels from heaven appeared and joined the other angel. They all praised God, singing/saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ROMANS 5:1; LUKE 19:38; COLOSSIANS 1:20"> “May all the angels in the highest heaven praise God! And on the earth may the people to whom God has shown his favor have peace with him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 PETER 3:22; MATTHEW 2:1–2; MATTHEW 2:9–11"> After the angels left them and returned to heaven, the shepherds said to each other, “Let’s go to Bethlehem to see this wonderful thing that has happened, which ◄the Lord/God► has told us about!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 19:32; LUKE 2:12; LUKE 2:7"> So they went quickly. They found the place where Mary and Joseph were staying, and they saw the baby lying in the trough where people put food for the animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 66:16; LUKE 8:39; PSALMS 71:17–18"> Then they told Mary and Joseph and others what had been told them &#123;what the angel had told them&#125; about this child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 2:47; LUKE 4:36; LUKE 1:65–66"> Everyone who heard what was said by the shepherds &#123;what the shepherds said&#125; to them was amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 2:51; LUKE 1:66; PROVERBS 4:4"> But Mary did not talk to others about what the angels said; she just kept thinking very much about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 106:48; Isaiah 29:19; ACTS 11:18"> The shepherds returned to their sheep. They kept praising God very much for all the things that they had heard and seen, because everything happened exactly like the angels told them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## They circumcised Jesus and presented him to the Lord.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 1:31; GENESIS 17:12; GALATIANS 4:4–5"> Seven days later, when the priests circumcised the baby, his parents named him Jesus. That was the name that they were told by the angel &#123;the angel told them&#125; to give him before Mary became pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 12:1–8"> It had been written by Moses &#123;Moses had written&#125; long ago in a law that God gave him, that when a woman gave birth to her first son, the parents had to take him to the Temple in Jerusalem in order that they might dedicate him ◄to the Lord/to God►. So when the time came for them to do those rituals so that God would consider them pure again, Mary and Joseph took Jesus there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 12:6–8; LEVITICUS 12:2; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:9"> God had commanded in his laws that if the first child of a couple was a boy, his parents should offer as a sacrifice to God two turtledoves or two young pigeons. So Joseph and Mary gave the priest two birds so he could offer them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Simeon prophesied about what Jesus would do.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 25:9; LUKE 2:38; LUKE 1:6"> At that time there was an old man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon. He habitually did what was pleasing to God and he obeyed God’s laws. The Holy Spirit was directing him as he was waiting for God &#91;MTY&#93; to encourage the Israeli people by sending the Messiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOHN 8:51; HEBREWS 11:5; LUKE 9:27"> It was previously revealed by the Holy Spirit &#123;The Holy Spirit previously revealed&#125; to him that he would see God’s promised Messiah before he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:1; LUKE 2:41; LUKE 2:51"> When Joseph and Mary brought their baby Jesus to the Temple in order to perform the rituals that God had commanded in his laws, the Spirit led Simeon to enter the Temple courtyard, and revealed to him that Jesus was the Messiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 1:64; LUKE 2:20; LUKE 2:13–14"> So he took Jesus up in his arms and praised God, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="REVELATION 14:13; GENESIS 15:15; PHILIPPIANS 1:23"> Lord, you promised me that I would see the one &#91;MTY&#93; who would enable you to save people of all people-groups from the guilt of their sins. <span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="LUKE 1:65–66; Isaiah 8:18; LUKE 2:48"> His parents marveled about what Simeon said about Jesus. Then Simeon blessed them, and said to Mary,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:23; 1 PETER 2:7–8; Isaiah 8:14–15"> “Note what I say: God has determined that because of this child, many Israeli people will turn away from God, and many others will turn to God &#91;MET&#93;. Although he will be like a sign &#91;MET&#93; to warn people, many people will oppose him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:19; MATTHEW 12:24–35"> As a result, the evil thoughts of many people will be made evident &#123;he will reveal the evil thoughts of many people&#125;. <div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:5; PSALMS 23:6; ACTS 14:23"> After that, she lived until she was 84 years old, and she was still a widow. ## What Jesus did in the Temple when he was a boy.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="EXODUS 12:14; NUMBERS 28:16; DEUTERONOMY 12:18"> Every year Jesus’ parents went to Jerusalem to celebrate the Passover festival.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> So when Jesus was twelve years old, they went up to Jerusalem as they always did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 30:21–23; EXODUS 12:15; 2 CHRONICLES 25:17"> When the celebration ended, his parents started to return home, but Jesus stayed in Jerusalem. His parents did not realize that Jesus stayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 122:1–4; Isaiah 2:3; PSALMS 42:4"> They thought that he was with the other people who were traveling with them. They walked all day. Then they started to look for him among their relatives and friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> They did not find him, so they returned to Jerusalem to search for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="LUKE 5:17; Isaiah 49:1–2; Isaiah 50:4"> Two days later, they found him. He was in the Temple courtyard, sitting with the Jewish religious teachers. He was listening to what they said, and he was asking them questions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:28; LUKE 4:32; PSALMS 119:99"> Everyone who heard what he said was amazed at how much he understood and how well he answered the questions that the teachers asked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="LUKE 2:49; MATTHEW 12:46; LUKE 3:23"> When his parents saw him, they were very surprised. His mother said to him, “My son, why have you done this to us? Your father and I have been very worried as we (exc) have been searching for you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="JOHN 6:38; JOHN 8:29; MALACHI 3:1"> He said to them, speaking about the Temple being God his Father’s house, “◄I am surprised that you (dl) did not know where to find me!/Why did you not know where to find me?► &#91;RHQ&#93; ◄You (dl) should have known that it was necessary for me to be in my Father’s house!/Did you not know that it was necessary for me to be in my Father’s house?► &#91;RHQ&#93;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="LUKE 18:34; LUKE 9:45; MARK 9:32"> But they did not understand the meaning of what he said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="LUKE 2:19; DANIEL 7:28; GENESIS 37:11"> Then he returned with them down to Nazareth. He always obeyed them. But his mother kept thinking about all those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="LUKE 2:40; PROVERBS 3:3–4; 1 SAMUEL 2:26"> As the years passed, Jesus continued to become wiser and he grew taller. God and people continued to approve of him more and more.</span>
