## Jesus taught that God is pleased when people turn from their sinful behavior.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 1:15; MATTHEW 9:10–13; LUKE 5:29–32"> Many &#91;HYP&#93; tax collectors and others whom people considered to be habitual sinners kept coming to Jesus to listen to him teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:11; LUKE 19:7; LUKE 7:39"> The Pharisees and men who taught the Jewish laws who were there &#91;SYN&#93; began to grumble, saying, “This man welcomes sinners and he also defiles himself by eating with them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So Jesus told them this parable:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:16; EZEKIEL 34:11–12; MATTHEW 18:12–14"> “Suppose that one of you had 100 sheep. If one of them were lost, ◄you (sg) would certainly leave the 99 sheep in the pasture, and go and search for the one lost sheep until you found it./would you not leave the 99 sheep in the pasture, and go and search for the one lost sheep until you found it?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:2; LUKE 19:9; Isaiah 46:3–4"> When you (sg) found it, you would put it on your shoulders and be happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 15:10; LUKE 15:24; 1 PETER 2:25"> When you brought it home, you would call together your friends and neighbors and say to them, ‘Be happy with me, because I have found my sheep that was lost!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 5:32; LUKE 15:10; LUKE 15:32"> I tell you (pl) that similarly God will be very happy about each and every sinner who turns from doing evil. God is not happy about 99 people who think that they are &#91;IRO&#93; righteous and think that they do not need to turn from doing evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 19:10; EZEKIEL 34:12; JOHN 10:16"> Or, suppose that a woman has ten very valuable silver coins. If she loses one of them, ◄she will certainly light a lamp and sweep the floor and search carefully until she finds it!/will she not light a lamp and sweep the floor and search carefully until she finds it?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 15:6–7"> When she finds it, she will call together her friends and neighbors and say, ‘Be happy with me, because I have found the coin that I lost!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 15:7; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:10; EZEKIEL 33:11"> I tell you that similarly the angels will be happy about even just one sinner who turns from doing evil.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught the Jewish leaders that they also ought to be glad when people turn from their sinful ways.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:23–31"> Then Jesus told them this parable to compare what the Pharisees and teachers of the Jewish law thought about those who turn from their sinful behavior with what God thinks about such people. He said, “A certain man had two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:16–17; MARK 12:44; LUKE 15:30"> One day the younger son said to his father, ‘Father, I do not want to wait until you(sg) die. Give me now the share of your property that belongs to me!’ So the man divided his property between his two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:3; JEREMIAH 2:5; PROVERBS 21:17"> A few days later, the younger son sold his share. He gathered his money and other things together and went to a country far away. There he spent all his money foolishly in reckless/wild living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 33:11; AMOS 8:9–12; HOSEA 2:9–14"> After he had spent all his money, there was a great famine throughout that country. And soon he did not have enough food to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TITUS 3:3; 2 TIMOTHY 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> So he went to one of the landowners in that area and asked for work. The man sent him to work taking care of the pigs in his field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 6:19–21; LAMENTATIONS 4:5; HOSEA 12:1"> Because he was very hungry, he would have been glad to eat the bean pods that the pigs ate. But no one gave him anything to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:28; EPHESIANS 2:4–5; LUKE 15:18–19"> Finally he thought clearly about what he had done. He said to himself, ‘All of my father’s hired servants have plenty of food! They have more &#91;SYN&#93; than they can eat, but here I am dying because I do not have anything to eat &#91;HYP&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:40; 1 JOHN 1:8–10; LUKE 15:21"> So I will leave here and go back to my father. I will say to him, “Father, I have sinned against God &#91;MTY, EUP&#93; and against you (sg).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 42:6; LUKE 5:8; PSALMS 84:10"> I am no longer worthy to be called &#123;of you calling me&#125; your son. Just hire me to be like one of the other hired servants.”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 103:10–13; PSALMS 86:5; PSALMS 86:15"> So he left there and went back to his father’s house. But while he was still some distance from the house, his father saw him. He pitied him. He ran to his son and embraced him and kissed him on the cheek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 51:4; LUKE 15:18–19; JEREMIAH 3:13"> His son said to him, ‘Father, I have sinned against God &#91;MTY/EUP&#93; and against you. I am no longer worthy to be called &#123;of you calling me&#125; your son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 41:42; Esther 8:2; REVELATION 6:11"> But his father said to his servants, ‘Go quickly and bring to me the best robe in the house! Then put it on my son. Put a ring on his finger to show that I am honoring him again as my son! Put sandals on his feet to show that I do not consider him to be a slave!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; MATTHEW 22:2–14; PSALMS 63:5"> Then bring the fat calf and kill it and cook it. We (inc) must eat and celebrate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:5; EZEKIEL 34:16; LUKE 19:10"> because my son has returned! It is as though &#91;MET&#93; he was dead and is alive again! It is as though he was lost and now has been found!’ So they did that, and they all began to celebrate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 150:4; JEREMIAH 31:4; LUKE 15:11–12"> While all that was happening, the man’s older son was out working in the field. When he came near to the house, he heard people playing music and dancing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> He called one of the servants and asked what was happening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ACTS 9:17; PHILEMON 1:16; ACTS 22:13"> The servant said to him, ‘Your younger brother has come home Your father has told us to kill the fat calf to celebrate because your brother has returned safe and healthy.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JONAH 4:9; 1 SAMUEL 18:8; JONAH 4:1–4"> But the older brother was angry. He refused to enter the house. So his father came out and pleaded with him to come in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKE 18:11–12; ZECHARIAH 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> But he replied to his father, ‘Listen to me! For many years I have worked for you like a slave. I always obeyed everything you told me to do. But you never even gave me a young goat, so that I could kill it and cook it and celebrate with my friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:3; LUKE 15:12–13; LUKE 15:32"> But this son of yours spent all the money he got from what you gave him. He spent it to pay for sleeping with prostitutes! Yes, now he has returned home, but it is not fair that you have told your servants to kill the fat calf and cook it for him!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MARK 7:27–28; ROMANS 9:4; ROMANS 11:1"> But his father said to him, ‘My son, you have always been with me, and all my property that I did not give to your brother has been yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:1–10; LUKE 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> But it is as though &#91;MET&#93; your brother was dead and is alive again! It is as though he was lost and now he has been found! So it is appropriate for us to be happy and celebrate!’ ”</span>
</div>
