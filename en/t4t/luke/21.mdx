## Jesus predicted that Jerusalem would be destroyed.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 19:43; DANIEL 9:27; MARK 13:14"> “But when you see that Jerusalem has been surrounded by the armies of your enemies, you will know that it is time for this city to be completely destroyed &#123;them to completely destroy this city&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:1; GENESIS 19:17; LUKE 17:31–33"> At that time those of you who are in Judea district must flee to the higher hills. <span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HOSEA 9:7; Isaiah 65:12–16; Isaiah 63:4"> You must obey what I tell you because, in order that all the things that are written in the Scriptures will be fulfilled, God will very severely punish the people who stay in this city.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ROMANS 11:25; REVELATION 11:2; Isaiah 63:18"> Many of them will be killed &#123;Their enemies will kill many of them&#125; with swords. ## Jesus taught them how to know when his return was near.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:32–35; MARK 13:28–30"> Then Jesus told his disciples this parable: “Think about the fig tree, and all the other trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKE 12:57"> As soon as you see their leaves beginning to sprout, you know that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JAMES 5:9; HEBREWS 10:37; 1 PETER 4:7"> Similarly, when you see these things that I have just described happening, you will know that it is almost time for God to truly rule as king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MARK 13:30; MATTHEW 24:34; MATTHEW 23:36"> Keep this in mind: All the things that I have just now described will happen before all the people who have observed the things that I have done have died.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="REVELATION 16:15; PSALMS 11:6; JEREMIAH 48:43–44"> You need to know that my return will surprise everyone all over the earth.</span>
