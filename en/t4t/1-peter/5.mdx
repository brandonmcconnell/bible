## You elders must care for your congregations with enthusiasm, not being greedy for money or acting like bosses. Instead, be examples to them.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:3–4; LUKE 24:48; ROMANS 8:17–18"> Now I will say this to those among you who are elders in the congregations: I also am an elder. I am one of those who saw Christ when he suffered, and I am also one who will share some of the glory/greatness that Christ has in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TITUS 1:7; 1 TIMOTHY 3:8; TITUS 3:1"> I appeal to you elders to take care of the people who are in your congregations &#91;MET&#93; as shepherds take care of their flocks of sheep. Do this, not because you must do it, but instead do it willingly, as God desires. Do not be greedy to get a lot of money for doing it, but instead do it enthusiastically/wholeheartedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 3:9; PHILIPPIANS 3:17; EZEKIEL 34:4"> Do not act like domineering bosses over the people &#91;MET&#93; whom God has assigned/entrusted to you, but instead be examples to them by the way in which you conduct your lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JAMES 1:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:25; 1 JOHN 3:2"> If you do that, when Jesus, who is like our chief shepherd &#91;MET&#93;, appears, he will give each of you a glorious/wonderful reward &#91;MET&#93;. That reward will be like the wreaths that are given to victorious athletes, but your reward will never wither like wreaths do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JAMES 4:6; PROVERBS 3:34; EPHESIANS 5:21"> Now I will say this to you young men and women: Just like married men and women are ◄to be subject/to submit themselves► to their spouses, you must ◄be subject/submit yourselves► ◄to the older men/to the leaders► of the congregation. And all of you believers should act &#91;MET&#93; humbly toward each other, because this proverb is true:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">God opposes people who are proud, but he acts kindly toward those who are humble.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JAMES 4:10; LUKE 14:11; PROVERBS 29:23"> Therefore, realizing that God has great power &#91;MTY&#93; to punish proud people, humble yourselves, in order that he may honor you at the time that he has determined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 55:22; PHILIPPIANS 4:6; PSALMS 37:5"> Because he takes care of you, let him take care of all the things that you are worried/concerned about.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Resist the devil by always being alert and trusting in Christ’s message, remembering that believers everywhere are also suffering as you are.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:11; JAMES 4:7; EPHESIANS 4:27"> Always be fully alert, because the devil, who is your enemy, is going/sneaking around, looking for people to destroy. He is like &#91;SIM&#93; a lion who roars as it prowls around, seeking someone to kill and devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JAMES 4:7; ACTS 14:22; EPHESIANS 6:11–13"> You must resist him by continuing to firmly trust in Christ and his message, remembering that your fellow believers all over the world are experiencing similar sufferings that the devil is bringing to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 4:17; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:3; 1 PETER 1:6–7"> God is the one who kindly helps us in every situation, and he is the one who chose us to share his eternal glory/greatness in heaven because of our relationship with Christ. And after you have suffered for a while because of things that people do to harm you, he will remove your spiritual defects/imperfections, he will strengthen you spiritually &#91;DOU&#93;, and he will support you emotionally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 PETER 4:11; ROMANS 11:36; REVELATION 1:6"> I pray/desire that he will rule powerfully forever. ◄May it be so!/Amen!►</span>
</div>
</div>

## I have written this short message to encourage you. The believers here, including Mark, greet you. May God give you inner peace.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:22; ACTS 11:23; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:1"> Silas has written this letter for me as I have ◄dictated it to him/told him what to write►. I consider/know that he is a faithful fellow believer. I have written this short letter to you to encourage you, and I want to assure you that what I have written is a true message about the things that God kindly does for us, things that we do not deserve. Continue to firmly believe this message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ACTS 12:12; ACTS 12:25; PSALMS 87:4"> In this city that is sometimes referred to as ‘Babylon’ &#91;MET&#93;, the believers &#91;SYN&#93;, whom God has chosen to belong to him just like he chose you, ◄send you (pl) their greetings/say that they are thinking affectionately about you►. Mark, who is &#91;MET&#93; like a son to me, also ◄sends you his greetings/says that he is thinking affectionately about you►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ROMANS 16:16; EPHESIANS 6:23; ROMANS 8:1"> Greet each other with a kiss on the cheek to show that you love each other. I pray that God will give inner peace to all of you who have a relationship with Christ.</span>
</div>
