## Yahweh’s accusation against Israel

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:22; MICAH 6:2; Isaiah 3:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">You Israeli people, listen to this message from Yahweh!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He is accusing you people who live in this country,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">saying “The people are not faithfully doing what pleases me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are not kind to others,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they do not even know me!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HOSEA 6:9; JEREMIAH 6:7; HOSEA 7:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everywhere in this land they curse others, they murder others, they steal, and they commit adultery.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They act violently toward others</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and commit one murder after another.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:3; Isaiah 24:4–12; NAHUM 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is because of their doing those things that there is no rain &#91;MTY&#93;, the land has become dry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the people are mourning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the people are dying from hunger.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Even the wild animals and the birds and the fish in the sea are dying.</span></span>
</div>

## The people are condemned for idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:27; HOSEA 5:4; HABAKKUK 2:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">“You my people request wooden idols</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to tell you what you should do!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Like prostitutes who have left/abandoned their husbands,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; JEREMIAH 3:6; EZEKIEL 6:13"> <span className="pl pl-2">you have abandoned me your God, and you are chasing after other gods on the tops of hills and mountains;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you burn incense under oak trees, poplar trees, and other trees</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">where there is nice/pleasing shade.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So your daughters have become prostitutes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your daughters-in-law have committed adultery.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HOSEA 4:1; JOHN 8:43; EPHESIANS 4:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">But it is not only your daughters</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whom I will punish for having become prostitutes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it is not only your daughters-in-law</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whom I will punish for committing adultery,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the men also are having sex with prostitutes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they offer sacrifices</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the prostitutes who search for men at the shrines of the idols.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are all foolish people, and they will all be ruined.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="HOSEA 12:11; HOSEA 9:15; AMOS 4:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You Israeli people have abandoned me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;MET&#93; those who commit adultery have abandoned their spouses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But do not cause the people of Judah to sin also.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do not go to Gilgal or go up to Beth-Aven towns</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to worship idols there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not make solemn promises there, saying ‘Just as surely as Yahweh lives, I will do what I am promising.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:24; JEREMIAH 8:5; JEREMIAH 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You people of Israel are as stubborn as &#91;SIM&#93; a young cow.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So there is no way &#91;RHQ&#93; that I, Yahweh, will provide food for them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a shepherd provides food for his lambs</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by leading them to a nice meadow.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 81:12; REVELATION 22:11; HOSEA 13:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of Israel have chosen to worship idols &#91;MET&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so allow them to do what they want to do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MICAH 3:11; AMOS 5:12; 2 KINGS 17:7–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">When their rulers finish drinking their wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they go to find prostitutes;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they love their disgraceful behavior.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:29; JEREMIAH 4:11–12; HOSEA 13:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they will disappear</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as though &#91;MET&#93; they were blown away by a whirlwind.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will become very ashamed because of their offering sacrifices to idols.”</span></span>
