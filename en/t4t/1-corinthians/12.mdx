## It is only God’s Spirit who enables people to say that Jesus is Lord.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:4–11; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:37; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:1–18"> Now, my fellow believers, I will answer your questions about the things God’s Spirit enables believers to do. I want you to know clearly &#91;LIT&#93; about these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 PETER 4:3; 1 THESSALONIANS 1:9; PSALMS 115:5"> You know that before you became Christians, various things led you to worship idols, which are unable to tell you any of God’s message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 JOHN 4:2–3; MATTHEW 16:16–17; ROMANS 10:9"> So the first thing that I will tell you now is that it is not the Spirit of God who would cause anyone to say, “Jesus deserved to die,” and that the Holy Spirit is the only one who can enable anyone to say, “Jesus is truly the Lord.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## God’s Spirit gives believers special abilities as he desires.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 PETER 4:10; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:28; ROMANS 12:4–6"> There are various things that God’s Spirit enables various believers to do, but it is that same Spirit who enables us to do them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:11–12; ROMANS 12:6–8; ROMANS 14:8–9"> There are many different ways to serve the Lord, but it is the same Lord whom we all are serving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 3:7; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:11; JOHN 5:17"> We believers have the power to do various tasks, but it is the same God who gives all of us the power to do these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 PETER 4:10–11; ROMANS 12:6–8; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:19"> God’s Spirit gives his power to each believer in order to benefit all the other believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 2:6; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:6–10; PSALMS 143:10"> To some, the Spirit gives the ability to speak very wise messages. To others, the same Spirit gives the ability to know things that he reveals to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JAMES 5:14–15; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:13; EPHESIANS 2:8"> To others, the same Spirit gives the ability to believe that God will work miracles. To others, the Spirit gives the ability to heal sick people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 14:39; 1 JOHN 4:1; MARK 16:17"> To others, he gives the power to perform miracles. To others, he gives the ability to speak messages that come directly from God. To others, he gives the ability to tell if a message is really from God or not. To others he gives the ability to speak messages in languages &#91;MTY&#93; that they have not learned. To others, he gives the ability to tell the meaning of those messages &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:4; HEBREWS 2:4; 2 CORINTHIANS 10:13"> It is the same Spirit who gives all these abilities. He gives whatever abilities he wants to give, to whatever person he wants to give them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A congregation needs all the abilities that God’s Spirit gives to its members, but you should especially desire the ones that help others the most.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 12:4–5; COLOSSIANS 3:15; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:27"> Although a person’s body has many parts, all the parts form just one body &#91;DOU&#93;. It is like that with those who belong to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:5; COLOSSIANS 3:11; GALATIANS 3:28"> When we were baptized &#123;When people baptized us&#125;, the one Holy Spirit caused us to become one group of believers. It does not matter whether we are Jews or non-Jews. It does not matter whether we are slaves or not slaves. When we believed in Christ, the one Spirit came to live &#91;MET&#93; within all of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:27–28; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:12; EPHESIANS 4:25"> Our bodies do not have only one part. They have many parts with various functions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 9:8–15; 2 KINGS 14:9"> If our foot could talk and say to us, “Because I am not your hand, I am not a part of your body,” it would still be a part of our body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 12:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:22; PHILIPPIANS 2:3"> If our ear could talk and say, “Because I am not your eye, I am not a part of your body,” it would still be a part of our body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:21; PSALMS 139:13–16; PROVERBS 20:12"> If our body were only an eye, ◄we would not be able to hear./how could we hear?► &#91;RHQ&#93; If our body were only an ear, ◄we would not be able to smell anything!/how could we smell anything?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:28; ROMANS 12:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:11"> But the truth is that God has put all the parts of our bodies together exactly as he wanted to put them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> If our body had only one part, it would not be a body. &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:12"> But the truth is that there are many parts in our body, but it is still only one body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 10:1–5; Nehemiah 4:16–21; JOB 29:11"> That is why if our eye could talk, it should never say to our hand, “I do not need you!” And if our head could talk, it should not say to our feet, “I do not need you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:28; ECCLESIASTES 9:14–15; ECCLESIASTES 4:9–12"> Instead, the parts of our body that seem to be weaker are the ones that are absolutely necessary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 3:21; GENESIS 3:7"> And the parts that we do not think highly of are the parts that we clothe carefully. And we protect carefully those parts that we do not show to people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 3:11; GENESIS 2:25"> The parts that are all right to show people, we do not need to protect. Instead, God, who has put all the parts of our bodies together, causes us to care for in a special way the parts that we think are less important.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 13:11; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:10–12; JOHN 17:21–26"> He does that in order that all the parts of our body will work together harmoniously &#91;LIT&#93;, and so that all the parts will care for all the other parts equally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ROMANS 12:15; GALATIANS 6:2; HEBREWS 13:3"> If one part of our body hurts, it is as though all the parts of our body are hurting. If we pay special attention to one part of our body, it is as though all the parts of our body feel pleasure &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:12; COLOSSIANS 1:24; EPHESIANS 5:30"> Everything I have just said about how the parts of our bodies relate to each other applies to how all of you who belong to Christ should relate to each other. Each of you belongs to him, but you have not all been given the same abilities and work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:11–13; ROMANS 12:6–8; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:18"> God has placed apostles in our congregations. Apostles are first in rank. Next in rank are those who speak messages which come directly from God. Next in rank are those who teach spiritual truth. Then there are those who have the power to work miracles, those who have the ability to heal sick people, those who have the ability to help others, those who have the ability to govern the affairs of the congregation, and those who have the ability to speak messages in languages &#91;MTY&#93; that they have not learned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:4–11; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:14–20"> Certainly not all believers are apostles. &#91;RHQ&#93; They all do not have the ability to speak messages that come directly from God. &#91;RHQ&#93; They do not all have the ability to teach spiritual truth. &#91;RHQ&#93; They do not all have the power to work miracles. &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:10"> They do not all have the ability to heal people. &#91;RHQ&#93; They do not all have the ability to speak messages in languages &#91;MTY&#93; they have not learned. &#91;RHQ&#93; They do not all have the ability to tell others the meaning of those messages. &#91;RHQ&#93; Certainly not!</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 13:1; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:39; MATTHEW 5:6"> But you should eagerly desire to have the abilities that will help other believers the most (OR, the abilities that God considers to be the most important). But now I will tell you the best way to use the abilities that God’s Spirit has given you.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
