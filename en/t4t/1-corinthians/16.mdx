## You should set aside funds each week for the offering to be sent to believers in Jerusalem.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ROMANS 12:13; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:1–9; 2 CORINTHIANS 9:12–15"> Now I will reply to another question that you asked. You asked about the money you are collecting to send to God’s people in Jerusalem. Do what I told the congregations in Galatia to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MALACHI 3:9–10; DEUTERONOMY 15:11–14; DEUTERONOMY 8:18"> Every Sunday each of you should set aside at home some funds &#91;EUP&#93; for this purpose, in proportion to how much God has prospered you. Then you should save it up, so that when I arrive there, you will not need to collect any more money. Choose some men whom you approve of, to take this money to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 8:6; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:34; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:19–21"> Then, when I arrive, I will write letters stating that you have authorized these men to take the funds there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 8:4; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:19; ROMANS 15:25"> And if you (OR, I) think that it is appropriate, I will go with them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## I plan to spend some time with you. Treat Timothy respectfully when he arrives. Apollos plans to visit you later.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 19:21; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:19; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:15–17"> Now I plan to travel through Macedonia province. I plan to come to visit you, but I want to go through Macedonia first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ACTS 15:3; ROMANS 15:24; ACTS 20:38"> Perhaps I will stay with you for a short while, or I may stay with you for the whole winter, in order that you yourselves can provide some of the things that I will need &#91;EUP&#93; for my next trip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ACTS 18:21; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:19; JAMES 4:15"> I do not want to see you just for a short time and then continue my trip. I am hoping to stay with you for a while, if the Lord Jesus allows me to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ACTS 2:1; EXODUS 23:16; ACTS 18:19"> But I will stay here in Ephesus city until after the Pentecost festival.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 2:12; ACTS 14:27; COLOSSIANS 4:3"> I want to do that because God has given me a great opportunity &#91;MET&#93; to proclaim his good message here. As a result of my work here, God is producing great results. I also want to stay here because there are many people here who oppose my work, and I need to refute them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:58; 1 THESSALONIANS 3:2; ROMANS 16:21"> When Timothy arrives there in Corinth, treat him respectfully &#91;LIT&#93;, because he is working for the Lord just as I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 4:12; ACTS 15:33; TITUS 2:15"> Do not let anyone despise him. And when he leaves there, give him some of the things that he needs for his trip &#91;EUP&#93; here, and also ask God to bless him. I am waiting for him to come, along with the other fellow believers who have been traveling with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:5; TITUS 3:4"> You also asked about our fellow believer, Apollos. I urged him strongly that he should go back to you with the three fellow believers who came here from Corinth. He was not at all willing to go now, but he will go later, when he has an opportunity.</span>
</div>
</div>

## I give you my final instructions, greetings from others, and my final blessings.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:9; EPHESIANS 6:13–18; EPHESIANS 6:10"> Be on guard against anything that would hinder you spiritually. You have believed the true message; continue believing it firmly. Be courageous. Keep strong in your relationship with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 13:34–35; 1 PETER 4:8; 1 TIMOTHY 1:5"> Act in a loving way in everything that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:16; ROMANS 16:5; ROMANS 16:2"> You know that Stephanas and his family were the first ones there in Achaia province to believe in Christ &#91;IDM&#93;. They have devoted themselves to helping God’s people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:17; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:12; 3 JOHN 1:8"> My fellow believers, I urge you to submit yourselves to them and to people like them who do God’s work and who work hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 11:9; PHILIPPIANS 2:30; PHILEMON 1:13"> I was glad when Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus arrived here from Corinth, because they did things for me that you were not able to do because you were not with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PHILEMON 1:7; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:13; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:12"> They comforted and encouraged me &#91;SYN&#93;, and I expect that this news from me will do the same for you. You should honor them, and you should also honor others like them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ACTS 18:2; PHILEMON 1:2; ROMANS 16:3–5"> The congregations here in Asia province ◄send their greetings to/say that they are thinking fondly of► you. Aquila and his wife Priscilla and the congregation that meets in their house ◄send their warm greetings to/say that they are thinking fondly of► you because both they and you belong to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 16:16; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:26; 1 PETER 5:14"> All your fellow believers here ◄send their greetings to/say that they are thinking of► you. Greet each other affectionately, as fellow believers should.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:18; GALATIANS 6:11; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:17"> Now I, Paul, having taken the pen from the hand of my secretary, write with my own hand to say that I am thinking fondly of you/to give you my greetings. I do this to show you that this letter really comes from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:24; JOHN 14:21; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:14–15"> ◄I pray that God will/May God► curse/cause bad things to happen to ◄anyone who does not love the Lord Jesus. I pray that our Lord will come soon/May our Lord come soon►!</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 14:16; REVELATION 3:19; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:15"> I love all of you who have a close relationship with Christ Jesus.</span>
