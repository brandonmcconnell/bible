## The message about the kings of Egypt and Syria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:5–6; DANIEL 8:26; REVELATION 21:5"> The angel also said, “And what I am going to reveal to you now will truly happen. There will be three more kings to rule Persia, one after the other. Then there will be a fourth king, who will be much richer than the others. As a result of his giving a lot of money to people, they will enable him to become very powerful. Then he will incite/persuade many nations &#91;HYP&#93; to fight against the kingdom of Greece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 11:16; DANIEL 11:36; DANIEL 8:21"> Then a very powerful king will appear in Greece. He will rule over a very large empire, and he will do whatever he wants to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 8:22; DANIEL 8:8; JEREMIAH 12:17"> But when he has become very powerful, he will die. Then his kingdom will be divided into four parts. Kings who are not his descendants will rule, but they will not be as powerful as he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 11:14; DANIEL 11:11; DANIEL 11:25"> Then the King of Egypt &#91;MTY&#93; will become very powerful. But one of his army generals will become more powerful than he is, and he will rule a bigger area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 11:13; DANIEL 11:40; DANIEL 11:7"> Several years later, the King of Egypt and the King of Syria will make an ◄alliance/agreement to help each other► &#91;MTY&#93;. The King of Syria will give his daughter to the King of Egypt to become his wife. But she will not be able to influence him &#91;MTY&#93; very long, and that woman, her husband, her child, and her servants will all be killed/assassinated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 11:38–39; DANIEL 11:19–20; Isaiah 9:14"> Soon after that, one of her relatives &#91;MET&#93; will become King of Egypt. His army will attack the army of Syria. They will enter the fortress of the soldiers of Syria and defeat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 37:19; Isaiah 46:1–2; JEREMIAH 43:12–13"> They will return to Egypt, taking the statues of the gods of the people of Syria and many items made of silver and gold that had been dedicated to those gods. Then for several years his army will not attack the army of the King of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then the army of the King of Syria will invade Egypt, but they will soon return to Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:42; DANIEL 11:7; DANIEL 11:26"> However, the sons of the King of Syria will prepare to start a war, and they will gather a large army. That army will march south and spread all over Israel like a huge flood. They will attack a strong fortress in the south of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 8:7; DANIEL 11:5; PSALMS 33:16–17"> Then the King of Egypt, having become very angry, will march with his army north from Egypt and fight against the army of Syria. The King of Syria will gather together a very large army, but the army of the King of Egypt will defeat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ACTS 12:22–23; 2 KINGS 14:10; Isaiah 10:7–12"> The King of Egypt will become very proud because of his army having killed a very large number of soldiers from Syria, but his army will not continue to win battles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 4:16; DANIEL 12:7; DANIEL 11:6–7"> The King of Syria will again gather together an army that will be bigger than the one that he had before. After a few years, he will again march south on their way to Egypt with a large army and a lot of equipment for fighting battles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="REVELATION 17:17; ACTS 4:25–28"> At that time, many people in Egypt will rebel against their king. In order to fulfill a vision that one of their leaders had seen, some violent/lawless people from your country of Israel will also rebel in order to not be controlled by Egypt any more, but they will be defeated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:6; EZEKIEL 17:17; EZEKIEL 4:2"> Then the King of Syria will come south with his army and pile up dirt against the walls of a city that is well protected, and they will break through those walls and they will capture the city. The soldiers from Egypt who have come to defend that city, even the best troops, will not be strong enough to continue to fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 8:9; DANIEL 11:36; DANIEL 11:3"> So the King of Syria will do whatever he wants to, and no one will be able to oppose him. His army will occupy the glorious land of Israel and completely control/subdue it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 KINGS 12:17; EZEKIEL 4:3; EZEKIEL 4:7"> Then he will decide to march south with all the soldiers from his kingdom. He will make an alliance with the King of Egypt and in order that his own daughter will help him to destroy the kingdom of Egypt, he will give her to the King of Egypt to become his wife. But that plan will fail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HOSEA 12:14; JEREMIAH 31:10; ZEPHANIAH 2:11"> After that, the army of the King of Syria will attack the regions that are close to the Mediterranean Sea, and his army will conquer many of them. But the army of a leader from another country will defeat the army of Syria and will stop their king from continuing to be proud. He will do to the King of Syria what he deserved for being very insolent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:21; PSALMS 37:36; JOB 20:8"> Then the King of Syria will return to the fortresses in his own land. But he will be defeated, and he will ◄die/be assassinated► &#91;EUP&#93; there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 60:17; DANIEL 11:7; DEUTERONOMY 15:2–3"> Then another man will ◄succeed him/become king►. That king will send one of his officers to oppress the people in Jerusalem by forcing them to pay big taxes, in order to get more money for his kingdom. But after a few years &#91;HYP&#93; that king will die, but he will not die as a result of people being angry with him or in a battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 11:34; DANIEL 8:25; JUDGES 9:1–20"> The next King of Syria will be an evil man who, because he will not be the son of the previous king, will not have the right to become king. But he will come when people do not expect it, and he will become king by tricking the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 8:10–11; DANIEL 11:10; DANIEL 9:26"> When his army advances, they will attack any armies that oppose him and destroy those armies. They will also kill God’s Supreme Priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 8:25; 2 THESSALONIANS 2:9; PSALMS 52:2"> By making treaties/alliances with the rulers of other nations, he will deceive them, and he will become very powerful, even though he rules a nation that does not have a lot of people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 7:25; PROVERBS 23:7; MATTHEW 9:4"> Suddenly his army will invade a province that is very wealthy, and they will do things that none of his ancestors did: they will capture in battles all kinds of possessions from the people whom they defeat. Then the king will divide those possessions among his friends. He will also plan for his army to attack fortresses in Egypt, but only for a short time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:18; DANIEL 11:2; PROVERBS 28:25"> He will courageously/boldly conscript/gather a large and powerful army to attack the army of the king of Egypt. But the King of Egypt will prepare to fight against them with a huge and powerful army. However, someone will deceive him, with the result that his plan will not be successful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DANIEL 11:10; DANIEL 11:40; 2 SAMUEL 4:2–12"> Even his most trusted advisors will plan to get rid of him. His army will be defeated and many of his soldiers will be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 12:2; JEREMIAH 9:3–5; HABAKKUK 2:3"> Then the two kings who both want to rule that area will sit down at the same table and eat together, but they will both lie to each other. Neither of them will get what he wants, because it will not be the time that God has determined/set for them to rule Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DANIEL 11:30–32; DANIEL 8:24; ACTS 3:25"> The army of the King of Syria will return to Syria, taking with them all the valuable things that they had captured. The king will be determined to get rid of the Supreme Priest of the Jewish people. He will do what he wants to in Israel, and then return to his own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ACTS 17:26; Isaiah 14:31; DANIEL 11:25"> When it is the time that God has decided, the King of Syria and his army will invade Egypt again. But this time he will not be successful like he was before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 10:4; NUMBERS 24:24; JEREMIAH 2:10"> The army of Rome will come in ships and oppose his army and cause him to be afraid. So he will be very angry, and with his army he will return to Israel and try to get rid of the Supreme Priest. The King of Syria will do what those who have abandoned the Jewish religion advise/want him to do for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DANIEL 12:11; DANIEL 9:27; MATTHEW 24:15"> Some of his soldiers will do things to ◄defile the temple/cause the temple to become unholy for them►. They will prevent the priests from offering sacrifices each day, and they will put in the temple something that is disgusting/abominable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 28:9; PSALMS 9:10; JOHN 17:3"> By deceiving those who have abandoned the Jewish religion, he will persuade them to ◄become his supporters/help him to do what he wants to do►. But those who are devoted to their God will firmly oppose them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:9; JOHN 16:2; MALACHI 2:7"> And wise Israeli leaders will teach others also. But for a while, some of those wise leaders will be killed in battles, and some will be burned to death, and some will be robbed, and some will be put in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:15; ROMANS 16:18; ACTS 20:29–30"> While God’s people are being persecuted, some people will help them a little bit, although some of those who help them will not do it sincerely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:9; MALACHI 3:2–4; REVELATION 2:10"> Some of those wise leaders will be killed &#91;EUP&#93;, but as a result the others will be purified &#91;DOU&#93;. This suffering will continue until it is the time that God has appointed for it to end.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The King of Syria who praises himself

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="REVELATION 13:5–6; 2 THESSALONIANS 2:4; DEUTERONOMY 10:17"> The King of Syria will do what he wants to. He will boast and say that he is greater than any god. He will even revile the Supreme God. He will be able to do what he wants until the time that God punishes &#91;MTY&#93; him. God will accomplish what he has planned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 2:4; Isaiah 14:13; 1 TIMOTHY 4:3"> The King of Syria will ignore the god that his ancestors worshiped and the god that many women love. He will ignore every god, because he will think that he is greater than all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="REVELATION 17:1–5; Isaiah 44:9; REVELATION 13:12–17"> But he will honor the god who people think protects fortresses. That is a god whom his ancestors did not honor. And he will give gold, silver, ◄jewels/very valuable stones► and other expensive gifts to that god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="REVELATION 18:9–13"> He will ask that god (OR, people who worship a god) from another country to help him to defend his fortresses. He will greatly honor those who allow him to be their ruler. He will appoint some of them to important positions in the government; and to reward them, he will give them some land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DANIEL 11:35; DANIEL 11:10; Isaiah 5:28"> But when his time to rule is almost ended, the army of the King of Egypt will attack his army. The army of the King of Syria will fight against them furiously &#91;SIM&#93;. His soldiers will be driving chariots and riding on horses and traveling in many ships. His army will invade many countries and spread all over those countries like a flood &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:47; JEREMIAH 49:6; Isaiah 11:13–15"> They will invade the glorious land of Israel and kill &#91;EUP&#93; tens of thousands of people. But the people of the Edom people-group and the people of the Moab people-group and the people of the Ammon people-group who are still alive will escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 10:10–11; EZEKIEL 29:14; REVELATION 11:8"> When the army of Syria invades other countries, even the people of Egypt will be defeated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 12:3; NAHUM 3:9; EZEKIEL 30:4–5"> The army of Syria will take away from Egypt gold, silver, and other valuable items. The people of Libya and Ethiopia will allow the King of Syria to rule over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="REVELATION 19:19–21; REVELATION 16:12; REVELATION 17:13"> But he will become very frightened/alarmed when he hears reports about what is happening in the east and in the north. So he will become very angry, and send his army to fight furiously and kill many of their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 2:4; DANIEL 8:25; REVELATION 19:19–21"> The King of Syria will set up his royal tents in the area between the Mediterranean Sea and the hill in Jerusalem on which the temple ◄exists/was built►. But he will be killed there, because there will be no one to help him.”</span>
</div>
