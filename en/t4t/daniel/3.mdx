## Nebuchadnezzar had a gold statue made

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:19; JEREMIAH 16:20; Isaiah 46:6"> King Nebuchadnezzar ordered his men to make a gold statue. It was ◄90 feet/27 meters► high and ◄9 feet/2.7 meters► wide. They set it up in the Dura plain in Babylon province.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DANIEL 3:27; PROVERBS 29:12; 1 KINGS 12:32"> Then he sent messages to all the provincial governors, the district governors, the chief advisors, the counselors, the various judges, and all the other officials in the province. He told them to come to the dedication of the statue that he had set up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="REVELATION 17:17; REVELATION 17:13; REVELATION 13:13–16"> When they all arrived, they all stood in front of that statue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 4:1; DANIEL 6:25; MICAH 6:16"> Then an announcer shouted, “You people who come from many countries and many people-groups and who speak many languages, listen to what the king has commanded!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 3:15; DANIEL 3:10; DANIEL 3:7"> When you hear the sounds made by the trumpets, the flutes, the guitars, the harps, the wind instruments, and all the other musical instruments, bow down to worship King Nebuchadnezzar’s gold statue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:50; MATTHEW 13:42; JEREMIAH 29:22"> Anyone who refuses to do that will be thrown into a blazing furnace!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="REVELATION 17:8; REVELATION 13:3; REVELATION 13:8"> So when all those people who had gathered heard the sounds made by the musical instruments, they all bowed down to worship the statue.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 4:12–16; DANIEL 6:12–13; Esther 3:8–9"> But some of the men who studied the stars went to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DANIEL 5:10; DANIEL 6:6; DANIEL 6:21"> They reported to him, “We hope that you, O king, will live a long time!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 3:4–7; DANIEL 6:12; Esther 3:12–14"> You decreed that all the officials whom you had gathered should bow down and worship the gold statue when they heard the sounds made by the musical instruments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> You also decreed that those who refused to do that would be thrown into a blazing furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DANIEL 6:13; DANIEL 2:49; ACTS 17:7"> Sir, there are some men from Judah whom you have appointed to be officials in Babylon province who have not obeyed your decree. Their names are Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. They have refused to worship your god and the gold statue that you have set up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 3:19; DANIEL 2:12; Esther 3:5–6"> Nebuchadnezzar became very angry. He commanded his soldiers to bring in Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. When they were brought to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:2; DANIEL 3:1; Isaiah 46:1"> Nebuchadnezzar said to them, “Is it true that you three men have refused to worship my gods or the gold statue that I have set up?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 5:29; 2 CHRONICLES 32:15–17; EXODUS 5:2"> If that is true, I will give you one more chance. If you bow down to worship the statue that I have set up when you hear the sounds of the musical instruments, fine. But if you refuse, you will be thrown immediately into the blazing furnace. Then ◄what god will be able to rescue you from my power?/no god will be able to rescue you from my power!►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 1:7; DANIEL 3:12"> Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego replied, “Nebuchadnezzar, we do not need to defend our actions to you in this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 8:31; LUKE 1:37; HEBREWS 7:25"> If we are thrown into the blazing furnace, the God whom we worship is able to rescue us. Sir, he will rescue us from your power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 5:29–32; MATTHEW 10:39; REVELATION 2:10–11"> But even if he does not rescue us, you need to know that we will never worship your gods or the gold statue that you have set up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 3:13; LEVITICUS 26:24; PROVERBS 21:24"> Nebuchadnezzar was extremely angry. His being very angry even showed on his face. He commanded that the furnace should be made seven times as hot as usual.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ACTS 16:23; ACTS 16:25; DANIEL 3:15"> After that was done, he commanded some of the very strong men of his army to tie Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, and then to throw them into the blazing furnace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So the soldiers tied them up and threw them into the furnace, while they were wearing their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 12:33; PROVERBS 21:18; PROVERBS 11:8"> Because the fire had been made very hot because the king had very strongly ordered them to do that, the flames leaped out and killed the soldiers as they were throwing the men into the fire!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 6:16–17; JEREMIAH 38:6; PSALMS 34:19"> So Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego fell into the roaring flames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 6:7; DANIEL 6:22; DANIEL 3:9–10"> But as Nebuchadnezzar was watching, he was amazed. He suddenly jumped up and shouted to his advisors, “Did we tie up three men and throw them into the flames, or not?” They replied, “Yes, O king, we did.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; ACTS 28:5; MARK 16:18"> Nebuchadnezzar shouted, “Look! I see four men in the fire! They have no ropes on them, they are walking around, and the flames are not hurting them! And the fourth man looks like a divine being!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DANIEL 3:17; Isaiah 52:12; REVELATION 19:5"> Nebuchadnezzar came closer to the opening of the flaming furnace, and he shouted, “Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, you who worship the Supreme God, come out of there! Come here!” So they stepped out of the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; HEBREWS 11:34; Isaiah 26:11"> Then all the king’s officials crowded around them. They saw that the flames had not harmed them. The flames had not even singed any of the hair on their heads or scorched any of their clothes! There was not even any smell of smoke on them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ROMANS 14:7–8; PSALMS 34:7–8; PSALMS 84:11–12"> Then Nebuchadnezzar said, “Praise the God whom Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego worship! He sent one of his angels to rescue these three men who worship him and trust in him. They ignored my command; they insisted that they would worship only their God, and were willing to die instead of worshiping any other god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DANIEL 2:5; PSALMS 76:10; DEUTERONOMY 32:31"> Therefore, I am now making this decree: ‘If any people, from any country or people-group or any language group, criticize the God whom Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego worship, they will be cut in pieces and their houses will be torn down and made into a pile of rubble. There is no other god who can rescue people like this!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DANIEL 2:49; 1 SAMUEL 2:30; DANIEL 3:12"> Then the king gave Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego more important positions in Babylon province than they had before.</span>
</div>
