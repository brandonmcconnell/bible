## What Nebuchadnezzar dreamed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 4:5; Esther 6:1; JOB 33:15–17"> One night during the second year that Nebuchadnezzar ruled, he had a dream. The dream worried him very much; and as a result he could not sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DANIEL 1:20; DANIEL 4:6; GENESIS 41:8"> The next morning he summoned his men who worked magic, fortune-tellers, those who worked sorcery, and those who studied the stars. Because he had forgotten what he had dreamed, he insisted that they tell him what he had dreamed. As they stood there in front of the king,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 40:8; GENESIS 41:15; DANIEL 4:5"> he said, “I had a dream last night that worries me. Tell me what I dreamed, because I want to know what the dream means.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 3:9; DANIEL 5:10; Ezra 4:7"> The men who studied the stars replied to the king, speaking in the Aramaic language. They said, “King Nebuchadnezzar, we hope that you will live a long time! Tell us what you dreamed, and then we will tell you what it means!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 3:29; Ezra 6:11; 2 KINGS 10:27"> But the king replied, “I have firmly decided that you must tell me the dream, and also tell me what it means. If you do not do that, I will order my soldiers to cut you into pieces, and to cause your houses to become only piles of stones!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 5:7; DANIEL 2:48; DANIEL 5:29"> But if you tell me what I dreamed and what it means, I will reward you. I will give you wonderful gifts and greatly honor you. So tell me what I dreamed and what it means!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 2:4; DANIEL 2:9; ECCLESIASTES 10:4"> But again they said, “Tell us what you dreamed, and then we will tell you what it means.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:5; EPHESIANS 5:16"> The king replied, “I know that you are just trying to get more time, because you know that I will do to you what I said that I would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 41:23; DANIEL 7:25; Esther 4:11"> If you do not tell me what I dreamed, you will be punished. I think that you have all agreed to tell me lies and other wicked things, because you hope that I will change ◄my mind/what I am thinking►. But tell me the dream, and then I will know that you can also tell me what it means.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DANIEL 2:27"> The men who studied the stars replied, “There is no one on the earth who can do what you ask! There is no king, even a great and mighty king, who has ever asked his men who work magic or his fortune-tellers or men who study the stars to do something like that!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 5:11; Isaiah 57:15; GENESIS 41:39"> What you are asking us to do is impossible. Only the gods can tell you what you dreamed, and they do not live among us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DANIEL 3:13; MATTHEW 2:16; DANIEL 3:19"> The king was very angry when he heard that, so he commanded his soldiers that they execute all such wise men in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 1:19–20; Esther 3:12–15; Isaiah 10:1"> And because of what the king commanded, they sent some men to find me and my three friends, to execute us also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 2:24; JEREMIAH 52:12; 2 SAMUEL 20:16–22"> Arioch, the commander of the king’s guards, came to kill us. But I spoke to him very wisely and tactfully/skillfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I asked Arioch, “Why has the king made such a harsh/terrible decree?” So Arioch told me all that had happened because of the king’s dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 1:18–19; DANIEL 2:9–11"> I immediately went to talk to the king and requested that the king give me some time, so that I could find out what the dream was and what the dream meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DANIEL 1:6–7; DANIEL 3:12; DANIEL 1:11"> Then I went home, and I told my friends, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:3; MATTHEW 18:19; MATTHEW 18:12"> I urged them to ask God, who lives/rules in heaven, to have mercy on us by telling us the secret meaning of what the king dreamed, in order that we and the other wise men in Babylon would not be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 12:6; AMOS 3:7; DANIEL 7:7"> And that night God gave to me a vision in which he revealed the secret. Then I praised God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 113:2; PSALMS 145:1–2; REVELATION 5:12"> saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We should praise God &#91;MTY&#93; forever,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because only he truly is wise and powerful.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 75:5–7; PSALMS 31:14–15; PSALMS 113:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">He determines what events will happen through the years.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He removes some kings and gives their authority to new kings.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is the one who causes people to become wise</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and enables those who study to understand many things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 12:22; 1 JOHN 1:5; JEREMIAH 23:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">He reveals things that are very mysterious;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he is surrounded by light,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he knows things that are hidden as though they were in the darkness.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 2:18; EXODUS 3:15; DANIEL 2:29–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, whom my ancestors worshiped,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I thank you and I praise you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you have caused me to be wise and made me strong.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have told me what my friends and I asked you to tell us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you have revealed to us what the king demanded to know.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Daniel told the meaning of the dream

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ACTS 27:24; DANIEL 2:12–14"> Then I went to Arioch, the man whom the king had appointed to execute the wise men in Babylon. I said to him, “Do not kill those wise men. Take me to the king, and I will tell him what his dream means.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:27–28; DANIEL 6:13; DANIEL 5:13"> So Arioch quickly took me to the king. He said to the king, “I have found this man, one of the men whom we brought from Judah who says he can can tell you what your dream means!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DANIEL 1:7; DANIEL 5:16; DANIEL 5:12"> The king said to me, whose new name was Belteshazzar, “Is this true? Can you tell me what I dreamed and what it means?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 47:12–14; DANIEL 2:2; Isaiah 44:25"> I replied, “There are no wise men or fortune-tellers or men who work magic or men who work sorcery who can tell such things to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 40:8; AMOS 4:13; DANIEL 10:14"> But there is a God in heaven who reveals secrets. And he has shown in your dream what will happen in the future. Now I will tell you what you dreamed, the vision you saw as you were lying on your bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DANIEL 2:28; AMOS 4:13; DANIEL 2:47"> O King, while you were sleeping, you dreamed about events that will happen in the future. The one who reveals mysteries has shown you what is going to happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 41:16; ACTS 3:12; DANIEL 2:17–18"> And it is not because I am wiser than anyone else on earth that I know the meaning of this mysterious dream. It is because God wanted you to understand what you were thinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:7; EZEKIEL 28:7; DANIEL 7:3–17"> O King, in your vision you saw in front of you a huge and terrifying statue of a man. It was shining very brightly, and it was frightening and awesome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:7; DANIEL 7:4–6; REVELATION 17:4"> The head of the statue was made of pure gold. Its chest and arms were made of silver. Its belly and thighs were made of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DANIEL 7:19–26; DANIEL 7:7–8; DANIEL 2:40–43"> Its legs were made of iron, and its feet were a mixture of clay and iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="DANIEL 2:44–45; DANIEL 8:25; ZECHARIAH 12:3"> As you watched, something cut a rock from a mountain, but it was not a human who cut it. The rock tumbled down and smashed the feet of the statue, feet that were made of iron and clay. It smashed them to bits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 2:2–3; PSALMS 37:10; REVELATION 20:11"> Then the rest of the statue collapsed into a big heap of iron, clay, bronze, silver, and gold. The pieces of the statue were as small as bits of chaff on the ground where it is threshed, and the wind blew away all the tiny pieces. There was nothing left. But the rock that smashed the statue became a large mountain that covered the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DANIEL 2:23–24"> That was what you dreamed. Now I will tell you what it means.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="EZEKIEL 26:7; JEREMIAH 27:6–7; Ezra 7:12"> You are a king who rules over many other kings. The God who rules in heaven has caused you to rule over them and has given you great power &#91;DOU&#93; and has honored you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DANIEL 4:21–22; PSALMS 50:10–11; JEREMIAH 27:5–7"> He has caused you to be the ruler over all people, and even the animals and birds are controlled by you. So the head of the statue represents you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="DANIEL 5:28–31; Isaiah 44:28–5; DANIEL 2:32"> But after your kingdom/rule ends, there will be another great kingdom, but it will not be as great as yours. The silver parts of the statue represent that kingdom. Then there will be a third great kingdom whose king will rule over the whole earth. The bronze parts of the statue represent that kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="DANIEL 7:7; DANIEL 7:19–26; DANIEL 11:36–45"> After that kingdom ends, there will be a fourth great kingdom. The iron parts of the statue represent that kingdom. The army of that kingdom will smash the previous kingdoms, just like iron smashes everything that it strikes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="REVELATION 17:12; DANIEL 2:33–35; REVELATION 12:3"> The feet and toes of the statue that you saw, that were a mixture of iron and clay, indicate that the kingdom they represent will later be divided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="DANIEL 7:24; REVELATION 13:1"> Some parts of that kingdom will be as strong as iron, but some parts will not remain together, just as iron and clay do not stick together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> The mixture of iron and clay in the statue shows also that the rulers of those kingdoms that separate from each other will try to form alliances with each other as a result of members of the royal families of those kingdoms marrying each other. But that will not succeed, just as iron and clay do not stick together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:18; LUKE 1:32–33; DANIEL 6:26"> But while those kings are ruling, God who rules in heaven will establish a kingdom that will never end. No one will ever defeat its king. He will completely destroy all those kingdoms, but his kingdom will remain forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; GENESIS 41:32; DEUTERONOMY 10:17"> That is the meaning of the rock that something cut from the mountain, the rock that will crush to tiny bits the statue that is made of iron, bronze, silver, and gold. O king, the great God has shown you what will truly happen in the future. And you can trust what I told you about the meaning of the dream.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Daniel was made the chief advisor

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="ACTS 10:25; ACTS 14:13; Ezra 6:10"> Then King Nebuchadnezzar prostrated himself in front of me in great respect. He commanded his people that they burn incense and burn an offering of grain to honor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="DANIEL 2:22; DANIEL 2:28; AMOS 3:7"> The king said to me, “Your God has enabled you to tell me the meaning of this dream/mystery, so now I truly know that your God is greater than all the other gods and all other kings. He reveals to people things that they cannot know.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="DANIEL 2:6; DANIEL 4:9; DANIEL 5:11"> Then the king gave many gifts to me, and he also appointed me to rule over the entire province of Babylon, and also to be the boss of all his wise men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="DANIEL 1:7; Esther 2:19; DANIEL 3:12–30"> I asked the king to appoint Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego to also have important positions in Babylon province, and the king did what I asked him to do. But I did my work while I stayed at the king’s palace.</span>
