## Daniel was thrown into the lions’ den

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 18:21–22; DANIEL 5:31; Esther 1:1"> King Darius decided to divide his kingdom into 120 provinces. He appointed a governor to rule each province.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DANIEL 2:48–49; DANIEL 5:29; Esther 7:4"> He also appointed me and two other men to be administrators, to supervise the governors and to be sure that they did the king’s work properly, in order that the king would not have to worry about anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 5:14; PROVERBS 22:29; GENESIS 41:38–41"> I soon showed that I was ◄more capable/able to do the work better► than all the other administrators and the governors. Because of that, the king planned to appoint me to be in charge of the entire empire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 PETER 3:16; PHILIPPIANS 2:15; 1 PETER 2:12"> Then, the other administrators and the governors became jealous. So they began to try to find something that they could criticize about the way I was working for the king. But I always did my work faithfully and honestly, and was never lazy. So they could not find anything to criticize.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 24:13–16; Esther 3:8; ACTS 24:20–21"> They concluded, “The only way we can find something for which we can criticize Daniel will be something concerning the laws that his god his given him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 2:4; Nehemiah 2:3; DANIEL 6:21"> So the administrators and governors went as one group to the king and said, “◄Your Majesty/O king►, we wish that you will live a long time!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 59:3; PSALMS 62:4; DANIEL 3:27"> We administrators and governors and district governors and advisors and other officials have all agreed that you should make a law that everyone must obey. We want you to command that for the next thirty days people may pray only to you. If anyone prays to anyone else, either to a human or to a god, he must be thrown into a pit of lions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 6:15; Esther 1:19; DANIEL 6:12"> And because laws made by our governments of Media and Persia cannot be changed, we want you, the head of our government, to sign it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 146:3; PSALMS 118:9; PSALMS 62:9–10"> So King Darius wrote the law and signed it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ACTS 5:29; PSALMS 55:17; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:17–18"> But when I found out that the king had written and signed that law, I went home. I knelt down in my upstairs room and prayed. I looked toward Jerusalem, and the windows were open with the result that everyone could see me while I was praying. I prayed three times each day, just as I always did, thanking God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 37:32–33; PSALMS 10:9; DANIEL 6:6"> The officials went together to my house and they saw me praying and requesting God to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DANIEL 6:8; DANIEL 3:8–12; Esther 1:19"> So they returned to the king and said to him, “Do you remember that you wrote a law stating that for the next thirty days people may pray only to you, and if anyone prays to anyone else, either to a human or to a god, he will be thrown into a pit of lions?”<br />The king replied, “Yes, that is the law that I wrote. It is a law of our governments of Media and Persia, which cannot be canceled.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 3:12; ACTS 5:29; DANIEL 2:25"> Then they said to the king, “Well, that man Daniel, one of the men who were brought from Judah, is not paying any attention to you or the law that you signed. He prays to his god three times each day!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MARK 6:26; MATTHEW 27:17–24; 2 SAMUEL 3:28–29"> When the king heard that, he was very distressed. He tried to find a way to save me. All the rest of that day he tried to think of a way to rescue me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 6:12; PSALMS 94:20–21; DANIEL 6:8"> In the evening, many of &#91;HYP&#93; the officials went together to the king and said, “◄Your Majesty/O king►, you know that our governments of Media and Persia have declared that no law that the king signs can be canceled/changed. So Daniel must be thrown to the lions!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 37:39–40; JOB 5:19; PSALMS 91:14–16"> So the king gave the order, and his servants brought me and threw me into a pit where the lions were. Before they threw me in, the king said to me, “I hope/wish that your God, whom you worship regularly, will rescue you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:53; ACTS 12:4; MATTHEW 27:60–66"> They rolled a huge stone across the entrance to the pit. Then the king fastened a string across the entrance and put wax at each end, and stamped the wax with the seal from his ring and the seals of the rings of his officials, in order that no one could secretly rescue me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Esther 6:1; DANIEL 2:1; 2 SAMUEL 12:16–17"> Then the king returned to his palace. That night he refused to eat any food. He would not allow anyone to entertain him because he did not want to be happy, with the result that he would forget about me. And that night he was unable to sleep because he was worried about me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 3:5; MARK 16:2; MATTHEW 28:1"> At dawn the next morning, the king got up and went quickly to the pit where the lions were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 16:11; HEBREWS 7:25; DANIEL 6:27"> When he came near it, he was very worried. He called out, “Daniel, you who serve the all-powerful God! Was your God, whom you worship regularly, able to save you from the lions?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 2:4; DANIEL 6:6; Nehemiah 2:3"> I answered, “◄Your Majesty/O king►, I hope that you will live a long time!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:17; HEBREWS 11:33; ACTS 12:11"> Yes, my God sent his angel to shut the lions’ mouths, in order that they would not harm me! He did that because he knows that I have done nothing that he thinks is wrong. And, ◄Your Majesty/O king►, I never did anything wrong to you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 5:20; MARK 9:23; DANIEL 3:27–28"> The king was extremely happy, and he commanded his servants to lift me out of the pit. When they did that, they saw that the lions had not wounded me at all. God had protected me because I trusted in him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 38:13; Esther 7:10; DEUTERONOMY 24:16"> Then the king commanded that the men who had accused me should be seized and be thrown, along with their wives and children, into the pit where the lions were. When they were thrown into the pit, the lions leaped on them and crushed their bones before they fell onto the bottom of the pit!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DANIEL 4:1; Ezra 1:1–2; Esther 3:12"> Then King Darius wrote this message and sent it throughout his kingdom to the people of every people-group and nation and from all language groups:<br />“I wish/hope that everything is going very well with you!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DANIEL 4:34; LUKE 1:33; DANIEL 3:29"> I command that everyone in my kingdom should fear and revere the God that Daniel worships.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">He is the all-powerful God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will live forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His kingdom will never be destroyed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will rule forever.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DANIEL 4:2–3; PSALMS 18:50; ACTS 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">He rescues and saves his people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He performs all kinds of miracles</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in heaven and on the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He rescued Daniel from the power of the lions!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DANIEL 1:21; 2 CHRONICLES 36:22–2; Isaiah 44:28–1"> So I was successful all during the time that Darius ruled and during the time that Cyrus, the King of Persia, ruled.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
