## Nebuchadnezzar dreamed about a tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 6:25; 1 PETER 1:2; DANIEL 3:4"> Several years after Nebuchadnezzar started to rule, he sent this message to the people of every nation and people-group and all language groups in his empire. He wrote, “I wish/hope that everything is going very well with you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DANIEL 3:26; PSALMS 66:16; PSALMS 71:18"> I want you to know about all the wonderful miracles &#91;DOU&#93; that the Supreme God has performed for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 145:13; Isaiah 25:1; DANIEL 2:44"> <span className="pl pl-1">He performs great miracles,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and does wonderful things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His kingdom will last forever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">his rule over people will never end.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 47:7–8; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:2–3; PSALMS 30:6–7"> I, Nebuchadnezzar, was living in my palace. I was living luxuriantly, and everything was going very well for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 2:1; JOB 7:13–14; DANIEL 2:28–29"> But one night I had a dream that caused me to be very afraid. I saw visions that terrified me as I lay on my bed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 2:2; GENESIS 41:7–8; Isaiah 8:19"> So I summoned all the wise men in Babylon, in order that they would come and tell me what it meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 44:25; DANIEL 2:7; GENESIS 41:8"> All the men who worked magic, the fortune-tellers, the men who worked sorcery, and men who studied the stars came to me. I told them what I had dreamed, but they could not tell me what it meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DANIEL 1:7; DANIEL 5:14; DANIEL 4:18"> Finally, Daniel came to me, and I decided to tell him what I had dreamed. One of my officials had given him a new name, Belteshazzar, to honor my own god. I knew that the spirit of the holy gods was in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DANIEL 2:48; DANIEL 5:11; DANIEL 4:8"> So, using the name that had been given to him, I said, ‘Belteshazzar, you are the most important of all my fortune-tellers. I know that the spirit of the holy gods is in you, and that you can reveal all mysteries. There is none that is too difficult for you. So, tell me what my dream means.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 12:2; DANIEL 4:5; PSALMS 37:35–36"> This is what I dreamed while I was lying on my bed: I saw a large tree growing in the middle of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DANIEL 4:21–22; DEUTERONOMY 9:1; MATTHEW 11:23"> The tree was very strong and had grown very tall; it seemed that its top reached up to the sky, with the result that everyone in the world &#91;HYP&#93; could see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 13:19; LAMENTATIONS 4:20; EZEKIEL 31:6–7"> It had beautiful leaves, and it had produced a lot of fruit for people to eat. Wild animals rested in the shade of that tree, and birds built nests in its branches. All the living creatures everywhere in the world got food from that tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:2; DANIEL 8:13; DANIEL 7:1"> While I was still lying on my bed, I saw another vision. In the vision I saw a holy angel coming down from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:10; DANIEL 4:23; EZEKIEL 31:12–13"> The angel shouted, “Cut down the tree, and cut off its branches! Strip/Shake off all of its leaves, and scatter its fruit. Chase away the animals that are lying in the shade of the tree, and the birds that are in its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 14:7–9; DANIEL 4:25–27; EZEKIEL 29:14–15"> But leave the stump of the tree and its roots in the ground. Fasten a band of iron and bronze around the stump, and allow it to stay there with grass around it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 4:23; REVELATION 12:14; DANIEL 4:25"> That tree seems to have represented a man, because the angel also said, “Cause that man to live out in the fields among the animals and plants. Cause the dew from the sky to make his body damp each morning. Do not allow him to continue to have a mind like humans; instead, cause him to have a mind like animals have, for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DANIEL 4:25; DANIEL 2:21; PSALMS 75:6–7"> The holy angels have decided what must happen. They want to inform everyone that the Supreme God rules over all the kingdoms in this world. He is the one who chooses who will rule these kingdoms. He sometimes allows very unimportant people to become rulers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DANIEL 5:15; GENESIS 41:8; DANIEL 5:8"> Belteshazzar, that is what I, King Nebuchadnezzar, saw in my dream. Now tell me what the dream means. No one else can tell me. I asked all the very wise men in my kingdom to tell me what it means, but they were unable to do that. But you can tell me, because the spirit of the holy gods is in you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Daniel explained the dream

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 8:27; DANIEL 7:28; DANIEL 4:24"> I, whose new name was Belteshazzar, did not say anything for some time, because I was very worried/disturbed about the meaning of the dream. Finally, the king said to me, “Belteshazzar, do not be afraid about the dream and about what it means.” I replied, “Sir, I wish that the events that were predicted in your dream would happen to your enemies, and not to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DANIEL 4:10–12; EZEKIEL 31:3; EZEKIEL 31:16"> In your dream/vision you saw a very strong and very tall tree. It seemed to reach to the sky, with the result that everyone in the world could see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> It had beautiful leaves and it had produced a lot of fruit for people to eat. Wild animals rested in the shade of that tree, and birds built nests in its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 2:37–38; 2 SAMUEL 12:7; JEREMIAH 27:6–8"> ◄Your majesty/O King►, that tree represents you! You have become very powerful. It is as though your greatness reaches up to the sky, and you rule people all over the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 5:21; DANIEL 4:13–16"> Then you saw a holy angel coming down from heaven. That angel said, ‘Cut down the tree, and cut off its branches! Strip/Shake off all of its leaves, and scatter its fruit. But leave the stump of the tree and its roots in the ground. Fasten a band of iron and bronze around the stump and allow it to stay there with grass around it. Each morning cause the dew from the sky to make this man, who was represented by the tree, damp. Cause him to live in the fields with the animals for seven years.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 107:40; JOB 40:11–12; Isaiah 46:10–11"> ◄Your Majesty/O King►, that is what your dream means. That is what the Supreme God has declared will happen to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DANIEL 4:17; JEREMIAH 27:5; PSALMS 83:18"> Your advisors will force you to live away from other people. You will live in the fields with the wild animals. You will eat grass like cows do, and dew from the sky will cause your body to be damp/wet every morning. You will live that way for seven years, until you learn that it is the Supreme God who rules over the kingdoms of the world, and he appoints the ones whom he chooses to rule them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DANIEL 4:15; MATTHEW 5:34; MATTHEW 21:20"> But the stump of the tree and its roots were left in the ground. That means that you will rule your kingdom again when you learn that it is God &#91;MTY&#93; who is really the ruler.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6–7; JONAH 3:9; ACTS 8:22"> ◄Your Majesty/O King►, please do what I am telling you to do. Stop sinning, and do what is right. Turn away from your evil behavior. Act mercifully to poor people. If you do that, perhaps you will continue to be sucessful.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The dream was fulfilled

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:19; ZECHARIAH 1:6; PROVERBS 10:24"> But the king refused to stop sinning. So what was predicted in the dream is what happened to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="REVELATION 2:21; ECCLESIASTES 8:11; 1 PETER 3:20"> Twelve months later, one day he was walking on the flat roof of his palace in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:18; REVELATION 18:10; LUKE 14:11"> As he looked out over/across the city, he said to those around him, “I have built this great city of Babylon to be the place where I rule! I have built it to display my own power, in order to show people my glory, to show them that I am very great!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ACTS 9:3–5; REVELATION 16:7; LUKE 12:20"> While he was still saying this, God &#91;MTY&#93; spoke from heaven and said, “King Nebuchadnezzar, this is what I am saying to you: You are no longer the ruler of this kingdom!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:5; DANIEL 5:21; PROVERBS 8:15–16"> Your advisors will force you to live away from other people. You will live in the fields with wild animals, and you will eat grass like cows do. You will live that way for seven years, until you learn that it is I, the Supreme God, who rules over the kingdoms of this world, and I appoint the ones whom I have chosen to rule them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DANIEL 4:32; DANIEL 4:25; DANIEL 5:5"> Immediately what God predicted happened. Nebuchadnezzar became insane, so his advisors forced him to live away from other people. He ate grass like cows do, and dew from the sky caused his body to be damp/wet every morning. He lived like that until his hair was as long as eagles’ feathers, and his fingernails became like birds’ claws.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="REVELATION 4:10; JEREMIAH 10:10; DANIEL 12:7"> “After those seven years ended, I, Nebuchadnezzar, looked up toward heaven, and I acknowledged that what God said was true. Then I could think correctly again. I praised and worshiped the Supreme God, and I honored him, the one who lives forever.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">He rules forever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">his kingdom will never end.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JOB 42:2; PSALMS 135:6; PSALMS 115:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the people in the world are very insignificant;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we are not like him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He has the power to do whatever he wants to do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">among the angels in heaven and among us people who live on the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So no one can stop him &#91;SYN&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no one can challenge him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">saying to him, ‘Why are you doing these things?’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="DANIEL 4:34; PROVERBS 22:4; DANIEL 4:15–16"> When I was able to think correctly again, I was honored again, and I was able to rule my glorious/great kingdom again. My advisors came to me again to talk about what should be done, and I became greater and more powerful than I was before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JAMES 4:6–7; PSALMS 33:4–5; 1 PETER 5:5–6"> Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and honor &#91;DOU&#93; God, the king who rules in heaven. All of his actions are just and right. And he is able to cause to become humble those who are proud, like I was.”</span>
</div>
</div>
