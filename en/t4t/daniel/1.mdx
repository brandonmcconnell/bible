## Daniel was taken to Babylon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:5–7; 2 KINGS 24:1–2; 2 KINGS 24:13"> After King Jehoiakim had been ruling in Judah for almost three years, King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon came to Jerusalem with his army and surrounded the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:7; ZECHARIAH 5:11; JEREMIAH 27:19–20"> After two years, Yahweh allowed Nebuchadnezzar’s soldiers to capture Jehoiakim, who was the King of Judah. They also took some of the things that were in the temple of God, and took them to Babylonia. There Nebuchadnezzar put them in the temple of his god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 39:7; 2 KINGS 20:17–18; 2 KINGS 24:15"> Then Nebuchadnezzar commanded Ashpenaz, the chief official in his palace, to bring to him some of the Israeli men whom they had brought to Babylon. He wanted men who belonged to important families, including the family of the King of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 5:11; ACTS 7:22; PROVERBS 22:29"> King Nebuchadnezzar wanted only men who were very healthy, handsome/good-looking, wise, well-educated, capable of learning many things, and suitable for working in the palace. He also wanted to teach them the Babylonian language and have them read things that had been written in the Babylonian language.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DANIEL 1:19; 1 KINGS 10:8; DANIEL 1:8"> The king commanded his servants, “Give them the same kind of food and wine that is given to me. Train them for three years. Then they will become my servants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:14; MATTHEW 24:15; DANIEL 2:17"> Among the young Israeli men who were chosen were me, Daniel, and Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, who all came from Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DANIEL 5:12; DANIEL 4:8; DANIEL 2:49"> But Ashpenaz gave us Babylonian names. The name he gave to me was Belteshazzar, the name he gave to Hananiah was Shadrach, the name he gave to Mishael was Meshach, and the name he gave to Azariah was Abednego.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 141:4; EZEKIEL 4:13–14; LEVITICUS 11:45–47"> But I decided that I would not eat the kind of food that the king ate, or drink the wine that he drank, because that would make me ◄ritually defiled/unacceptable to God►. So I asked Ashpenaz to allow me to eat and drink other things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 39:21; PROVERBS 16:7; PSALMS 106:46"> God had caused Ashpenaz to greatly respect me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:16–18; JOHN 12:42–43; PROVERBS 29:25"> but he was worried about what I suggested. He said, “My master, the king, has commanded that you eat the kinds of food and drink that he does. If you eat other things and as a result you become more thin and pale than the other young men who are your age, he will order his soldiers to cut off my head because of what you have done!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Ashpenaz had ordered a guard to watch me, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 14:2; DANIEL 1:16; GENESIS 1:29–30"> So I said to this guard: “Please test us for ten days. During that time give us only vegetables to eat and water to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After ten days, see how we look, and see how the other young men look, the ones who are eating the kind of food that the king eats. Then you can decide about what food you will let us eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The guard agreed to do what I suggested, and he tested us like that for ten days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 23:25; PROVERBS 10:22; 2 KINGS 4:42–44"> After ten days, he saw that my three friends and I looked healthier &#91;DOU&#93; than the young men who had been eating the food that the king wanted them to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 1:12"> So after that, the guard gave us only vegetables to eat; he did not give us the king’s special food and wine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 KINGS 3:12; JAMES 1:5; DANIEL 2:21"> And God gave to us four young men wisdom and the ability to study many things that Babylonians had written and studied. And he also gave to me the ability to understand the meaning of visions and dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DANIEL 1:5"> When those three years that the king had set for training us young men from Judah were ended, Ashpenaz brought all of us to King Nebuchadnezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:29; GENESIS 41:46; DANIEL 1:5"> The king talked with each of us, and realized that none of the other young men were as capable as Hananiah, Mishael, Azariah and I were. So we four became the king’s special advisors/servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 8:19; Isaiah 47:12–14; 1 KINGS 4:29–34"> In all important matters, the king found that what we four men advised was ten times as good as what all the magicians and sorcerers/fortune-tellers in his kingdom advised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DANIEL 6:28; DANIEL 10:1"> I remained there serving the king more than 60 years, until the first year that Cyrus became king.</span>
</div>
