## A psalm written by David for the choir director, to be sung using a tune called ‘Mahalath’

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 14:1–7; PSALMS 10:4; LUKE 12:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Only foolish people say to themselves, “There is no God!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">People who say that are corrupt; they commit terrible sins;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is not one of them who does what is good/right.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; 2 CHRONICLES 15:2; PSALMS 27:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">God looks down from heaven and sees humans;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he looks to see if anyone is very wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that he seeks to know God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; PSALMS 14:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they are all morally corrupt;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no one does what is good/right.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:22; JEREMIAH 10:25; PSALMS 27:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will all these evil people never learn what God will do to them?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They act violently toward Yahweh’s people while eating the food that he provides,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they never pray to Yahweh.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:1; LEVITICUS 26:36; LEVITICUS 26:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But some day those people will become terrified,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like they have never been terrified before,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because God will cause those who are separated from him to die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will disrespect them by scattering their bones.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have rejected God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so he will cause them to be defeated and completely disgraced.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 14:7; JEREMIAH 30:18; PSALMS 126:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I wish/desire that someone from Jerusalem &#91;MTY&#93; would come and rescue the Israeli people!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God, when you bless your people again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all the Israeli people, all the descendants of Jacob, will rejoice.</span></span>
