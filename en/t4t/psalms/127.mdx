## A psalm written by Solomon for people going up to the temple to worship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:7; PROVERBS 21:30–31"> <span className="pl pl-1">If people are building a house without knowing whether it is Yahweh’s will to build it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are building it in vain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Similarly, if Yahweh does not protect a city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is useless for guards/sentries to stay awake to tell people if their enemies come to attack it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:12; PSALMS 4:8; JOB 11:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is also useless to arise very early and go to sleep late at night</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order that you can work hard all day to earn money to buy food,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because Yahweh gives food to those whom he loves.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 128:3–4; DEUTERONOMY 28:4; 1 SAMUEL 1:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Children are a gift that comes to parents from Yahweh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are a reward/blessing from him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:6; PROVERBS 31:28; JEREMIAH 50:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">If a man has sons while he is still young,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when they grow up, they will be able to help him defend his family</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; a soldier can defend himself if he has a bow and arrows in his hand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:11; JOB 42:12–16; GENESIS 50:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">A man who has many sons is very happy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;MET&#93; a soldier who has many arrows in his quiver is very happy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If a man with his many grown sons is taken by his enemies to the place where they decide matters, his enemies will never be able to defeat that man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because his sons will help to defend him.</span></span>
</div>
