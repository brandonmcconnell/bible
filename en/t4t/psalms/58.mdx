## A psalm written by David for the choir director, to be sung using the tune ‘Do not destroy’

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 57:1; DEUTERONOMY 16:18–19; PSALMS 72:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you mighty men speak, you never say what is right;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you never decide things about people justly &#91;RHQ&#93;!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 94:20; Isaiah 10:1; EZEKIEL 22:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">No, in your inner beings you think only about doing what is wrong,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you commit violent crimes in this land of Israel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 51:5; Isaiah 48:8; PROVERBS 22:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wicked people do wrong things and tell lies from the time that they are born &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 140:3; JAMES 3:8; ECCLESIASTES 10:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, show in the heavens how great you are!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And show your glory to people all over the earth!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What wicked people say injures people like the venom of a snake &#91;MET&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They refuse to listen to good advice, as though they were cobras that were deaf &#91;MET&#93;!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:17; Isaiah 19:3; DEUTERONOMY 18:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">As a result, like a snake that does not respond when a charmer plays a flute or when someone sings magic songs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they do not pay attention when others rebuke them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 3:7; JOB 4:10–11; HOSEA 5:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, as for these enemies who want to attack me like young lions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">break their teeth!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 112:10; JOSHUA 7:5; PSALMS 64:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cause them to disappear like water disappears in dry ground!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Cause the arrows that they shoot to have no ◄heads/sharp points►!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 3:16; ECCLESIASTES 6:3; PSALMS 37:35–36"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cause them to become like snails that disappear in the slime;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">cause them to be like babies that are born dead!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 118:12; PROVERBS 10:25; ECCLESIASTES 7:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Get rid of them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as fast as thornbushes are blown away after they are cut (OR, as fast as a fire heats a pot over burning thorns).</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 68:23; PSALMS 91:8; PSALMS 64:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Righteous people will rejoice when they see wicked people being punished as they deserve;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will wash their feet in the blood of wicked people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 67:4; Isaiah 3:10; PSALMS 64:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then people will say, “It is true that there is a reward for righteous people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there is indeed a God who judges people justly here on the earth!”</span></span>
</div>
