## A psalm written by David for the choir director

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 10:4; PSALMS 53:1; ROMANS 3:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Only foolish people say to themselves, “There is no God!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">People who say those things are corrupt/worthless; they do abominable/detestable deeds;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is not one of them who does what is good/right.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 3:11; PSALMS 33:13–14; HEBREWS 11:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh looks down from heaven and sees humans;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he looks to see if anyone is very wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that he desires to know God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 3:10–12; Isaiah 53:6; Isaiah 64:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they are all corrupt/evil;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no one does what is good/right.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 79:6; JEREMIAH 10:25; PSALMS 82:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will those evil people never learn what God will do to punish them &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They act violently toward Yahweh’s people while eating the food that he provides,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they never pray to Yahweh.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 112:2; PSALMS 73:15; PSALMS 24:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But some day they will become very terrified</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because God helps those who act righteously and will punish those who reject him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 9:9; PSALMS 42:10; PSALMS 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who do evil may prevent helpless people from doing what they plan to do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but Yahweh protects those helpless people &#91;MET&#93;.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 53:6; PSALMS 85:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">I wish/desire that Yahweh would come from Jerusalem &#91;MTY&#93; and rescue us Israeli people!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh, when you bless your people again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all of us Israeli people, who are the descendants of Jacob, will rejoice.</span></span>
</div>
