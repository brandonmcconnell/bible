## A psalm written for people going up to the temple to worship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HOSEA 6:11; JOEL 3:1; JEREMIAH 31:8–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">When Yahweh brought us Israeli people back to Jerusalem (OR, enabled us Israelis to prosper again),</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it was wonderful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it seemed as though &#91;SIM&#93; we were dreaming.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 8:21; Isaiah 35:10; ZECHARIAH 8:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">We were extremely happy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and we &#91;SYN&#93; continued shouting joyfully.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then the other people-groups said about us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Yahweh has done great things for them!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 1:49; Isaiah 66:14; Isaiah 51:9–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">And we say, “Yes, Yahweh truly has done great things for us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we are very happy.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 85:4; PSALMS 126:1; Isaiah 41:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, when it rains, water flows in the streams again after they were dry &#91;SIM&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Similarly, enable our nation to become great again like it was before.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:9; Isaiah 35:10; JOHN 16:20–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">We cried when we planted seeds because it was hard work preparing the soil that had not been plowed for many years;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">now we want to shout joyfully because we are gathering a big harvest.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:2–3; GALATIANS 6:7–8; PSALMS 30:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who cried as they carried the bags of seeds to the fields will shout joyfully</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when they bring the crops to their houses at harvest time.</span></span>
