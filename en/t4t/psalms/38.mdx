## A psalm written by David, asking God not to forget him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 6:1; Isaiah 54:8; HEBREWS 12:5–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, when you are angry with me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not rebuke/scold me and punish me!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOB 6:4; PSALMS 32:4; PSALMS 64:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now it is as though you have shot your arrows at me and wounded me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you have struck me and knocked me down.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 6:2; PSALMS 51:8; PSALMS 31:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because you have been angry with me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am suffering great pain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because of my sin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my whole body is diseased.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 40:12; Ezra 9:6; MATTHEW 11:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">All my sins are like a flood that covers my head;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are like a load that is very heavy, with the result that I cannot carry it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 69:5; JEREMIAH 8:22; Isaiah 1:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because I have done foolish things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have sores that have become worse, and they stink.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 30:28; PSALMS 35:14; PSALMS 42:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sometimes I am bent over, and sometimes I lie prostrate;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am sad/cry all day.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 21:18–19; ACTS 12:23; PSALMS 102:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My body is burning with fever,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I am very ill.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 3:24; PSALMS 32:3; JOB 30:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am completely exhausted, and I have no strength.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am very distressed, and I groan with pain.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 10:17; PSALMS 6:6; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, you know that I desire to be healed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you hear me while I am groaning.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 6:7; PSALMS 88:9; PSALMS 69:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My heat is pounding heavily, and all my strength is gone.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am no longer able to see well.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 31:11; JOB 19:13–17; LUKE 23:49"> <span className="pl pl-1">My friends and neighbors stay away from me because of my sores;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even my own family stays away from me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 35:20; PSALMS 140:5; 2 SAMUEL 17:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And those who want to kill me set traps to catch me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those who want to harm me discuss ways to get rid of me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they plot against me all day.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 39:2; Isaiah 53:7; PSALMS 39:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now I act like I’m deaf, and do not listen to what they say.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I act like I cannot talk, so I say nothing to reply to them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="AMOS 5:13; JOHN 8:6; MARK 15:3–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I act like a man who does not answer when people talk to him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because he cannot hear anything.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 39:7; PSALMS 17:6; PSALMS 138:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Yahweh, I trust in you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">My Lord God, you will answer me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 94:18; DEUTERONOMY 32:35; PSALMS 35:24–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said to you, “Do not cause me to die, with the result that my enemies will rejoice!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do not cause me to stumble, with the result that they will be very happy!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 38:6; PSALMS 6:6; PSALMS 35:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">I say that because I am about to fall down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I constantly have pain.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 32:5; PROVERBS 28:13; JOB 33:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">I confess the wrong things that I have done;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am very sorry for the sins that I have committed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 35:19; JOHN 15:18–25; PSALMS 69:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">My enemies are healthy and strong;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there are many people who hate me for no reason.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 35:12; 1 JOHN 3:12; JEREMIAH 18:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who do evil things to me in return for my doing good things to them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">oppose me because I try to do what is right.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 22:19; PSALMS 22:24; PSALMS 22:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, do not abandon me!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">My God, do not stay far from me!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 27:1; PSALMS 40:13; Isaiah 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, you are the one who saves/rescues me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Quickly come and help me!</span></span>
</div>
