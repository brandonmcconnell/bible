## A psalm written by David for the choir director, to be accompanied by musical instruments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:6; PSALMS 4:1; PSALMS 86:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, listen to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and answer my prayer &#91;DOU&#93;!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 18:2; PSALMS 139:9–10; PSALMS 62:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">While I am discouraged and far from my home,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am calling out to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lead me to the place that is like a high rock &#91;MET&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on top of which I will be safe.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:10; PSALMS 62:7; PSALMS 140:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have been my refuge;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you have been like a strong tower &#91;MET&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in which I am safe from being attacked by my enemies.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 23:6; PSALMS 27:4; PSALMS 17:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Allow me to live close to your temple all during my life &#91;HYP&#93;!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Allow me to be safe &#91;MET&#93;, like a little bird is safe under its mother’s wings.</span>(Think about that!)</span>
