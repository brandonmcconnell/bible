## A psalm written by Asaph for Jeduthun, the choir director

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; GENESIS 37:35; Isaiah 26:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whenever have trouble, I pray to the Lord;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all during the night I lift up my hands while I pray,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but nothing causes me to be comforted.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 61:2; PSALMS 142:2–3; JOB 31:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I think about God, I ◄despair/think that he will never help me►;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I meditate about him, I am discouraged.</span>(Think about that!)</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:7; Isaiah 51:9; PSALMS 143:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I think about days that have passed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I remember what happened in previous years.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 42:8; PSALMS 4:4; PSALMS 139:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I spend the whole night thinking about things;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I meditate, and this is what I ask myself:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 85:1; LAMENTATIONS 3:31–32; PSALMS 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Will the Lord always reject us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will he never again be pleased with us?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:19; ROMANS 9:6; 2 PETER 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Has he stopped faithfully loving us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will he not do for us what he promised to do?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 40:11; Isaiah 49:14–15; Isaiah 63:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">God promised to be merciful to us; has he forgotten that?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Because he is angry with us, has he decided to not be kind to us?”</span>(Think about that!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 42:3; MARK 9:24; EXODUS 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said, “What causes me to be very sad is that</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it seems that God, who is greater than any other god, is no longer powerful.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 143:5; PSALMS 105:5; 1 CHRONICLES 16:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">But then, Yahweh, I recall/remember your great deeds;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I remember the wonderful things that you did in the past.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 71:24; PSALMS 145:11; DEUTERONOMY 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-2">I meditate on all that you have done,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I think about your mighty acts.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 73:17; EXODUS 15:11; PSALMS 89:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, everything that you do is holy;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is certainly no god &#91;RHQ&#93; who is great like you are!</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 6:6; DEUTERONOMY 9:29; DEUTERONOMY 9:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">By your power &#91;MTY&#93; you rescued your people from Egypt;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you saved those who were descendants of Jacob and his son Joseph.</span>(Think about that!)</span>

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 97:4; 2 SAMUEL 22:14; REVELATION 11:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thunder crashed in the whirlwind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and lightning ◄lit up/flashed across► the entire sky &#91;HYP&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the earth shook violently &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 14:28; PSALMS 29:10; NAHUM 1:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then you walked through the sea</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on a path that you made through the deep water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but your footprints could not be seen.</span></span>
