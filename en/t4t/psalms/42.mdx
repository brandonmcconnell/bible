## A psalm written by one of the descendants of Korah for the choir director

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 63:1–2; PSALMS 143:6–7; PSALMS 119:131"> <span className="pl pl-1">Deer pant, desiring to drink water from a stream when there is a drought (OR, when they are being pursued by hunters.)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">In the same way &#91;SIM&#93;, God, I need you very much.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 63:1; PSALMS 84:10; JOHN 7:37"> <span className="pl pl-1">I desire to have fellowship with &#91;MET&#93; you, the all-powerful God.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I wonder, “When will I be able to go back to the temple in Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and worship in your presence again?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 79:10; PSALMS 115:2; JOEL 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every day and every night I cry;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is as though the only thing I have to drink is my tears;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and while I do that, my enemies are continually asking me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Why does your god not help you?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 30:29; PSALMS 122:1; 1 SAMUEL 1:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am very distressed &#91;IDM&#93; as I remember</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I went with the crowd of people to the temple in Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">leading them as we walked along;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we were all shouting joyfully and singing to thank God for what he had done;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we were a large group who were celebrating.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 37:7; HEBREWS 10:36–37; PSALMS 61:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I say to myself, “◄Why am I sad and discouraged?/I should not be sad and discouraged!► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I confidently expect God to help me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and again I will praise him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my God, the one who saves me.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 61:2; JONAH 2:7; DEUTERONOMY 3:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now, Yahweh, I am very discouraged &#91;IDM&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so I think about you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even from where the Jordan River gushes out from the bottom of Hermon Mountain and from Mizar Mountain.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JONAH 2:3; PSALMS 69:14–15; PSALMS 88:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But here, the great sorrow that I feel is like water that you send down &#91;MET&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is like a waterfall that tumbles down and floods over me.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 63:6; PSALMS 16:7; PSALMS 149:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh shows me each day that he faithfully loves me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and each night I sing to him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and pray to him, the God who causes me to live.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 38:6; PSALMS 43:2; PSALMS 18:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I say to God, who is like an overhanging rock under which I can hide &#91;MET&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“It seems that you have forgotten me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I &lt;mourn/cry&gt; constantly because my enemies act cruelly toward me” &#91;RHQ&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 42:3; JOEL 2:17; LUKE 2:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">They make fun of me constantly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they continually ask, “Why does your god not help you?” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And when they insult me like that,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is like wounds that I feel even in my bones.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 42:5; JEREMIAH 33:6; PSALMS 43:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I think,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“◄Why am I sad and discouraged?/I should not be sad and discouraged!► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will confidently expect God to help me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will praise him again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">my God, the one who saves me.”</span></span>
</div>
