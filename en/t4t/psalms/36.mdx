## A psalm written for the choir director by David, a man who served God faithfully

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ROMANS 3:18; PROVERBS 16:6; PSALMS 143:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wicked people continually desire to sin.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They consider &#91;IDM&#93; that they do not need to revere God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:19; PSALMS 10:3; PSALMS 49:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because they are very proud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they do not think that God will discover their sins and condemn them (OR, they do not think about their sins and hate themselves for it).</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 55:21; PSALMS 10:7; JEREMIAH 4:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everything that they say is deceitful and full of lies;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they no longer do what is good</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and are no longer wise.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICAH 2:1; PROVERBS 4:16; ROMANS 12:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">While they are lying on their beds, they plan to do things to harm others;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are determined to do things that are not good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they never refuse to do what is evil.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 100:5; PSALMS 108:4; PSALMS 103:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, your faithful love for us reaches as high as the heavens,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you faithfully do what you have promised;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is as though your doing that extends up to the clouds.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 77:19; ROMANS 11:33; PSALMS 71:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your righteous behavior is as permanent as the highest mountains &#91;MET&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your acting justly will continue as long as the deepest oceans exist.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You take care of people and you take care of animals.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 86:5; PSALMS 91:4; RUTH 2:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, your faithful love for us is very precious.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You protect us like birds protect their baby birds under their wings &#91;MET&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 16:11; PSALMS 65:4; Isaiah 58:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You provide for us plenty of food from the abundant supply &#91;IDM&#93; that you have;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your great blessings for us flow like a river.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 8:12; JOHN 4:14; 1 JOHN 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are the one who causes everything to live;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your light is what enables us to see.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:7; HEBREWS 8:11; PSALMS 97:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Continue to faithfully love those who have experienced a relationship with you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and bless those who act righteously/justly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 16:8; PSALMS 17:8–14; PSALMS 119:51"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not allow proud people &#91;SYN&#93; to attack me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or allow wicked people to chase me away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 1:5; PSALMS 58:10–11; PSALMS 55:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look where evil people have fallen on the ground, defeated;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they were thrown down, and they will never rise again.</span></span>
</div>
