## A psalm written by David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 143:7; PSALMS 18:2; PROVERBS 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, I call out to you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you are like an overhanging rock under which I can hide.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not refuse to answer me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because if you are silent, I will soon be with those who are in their graves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 138:2; PSALMS 141:2; PSALMS 140:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to me when I call out for you to help me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when I pray, lifting up my hands as I face your sacred temple.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 55:21; PSALMS 12:2; JEREMIAH 9:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not drag me away with wicked people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with those who do wicked deeds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with those who pretend to act peacefully toward others</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while in their inner beings, they hate them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="REVELATION 22:12; 2 TIMOTHY 4:14; REVELATION 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Punish those people in the way that they deserve for what they have done;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">punish them for their evil deeds &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 31:21–22; PSALMS 107:19–22; PSALMS 118:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise Yahweh</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because he has heard me when I called out for him to help me!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 30:11–12; PSALMS 56:3–4; Isaiah 61:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh makes me strong and protects me like a shield &#91;MET&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I trusted in him, and he helped me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I was glad,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and from my inner being I praised him as I sang to him.</span></span>
