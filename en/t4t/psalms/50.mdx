## A psalm written by Asaph

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 113:3; JOSHUA 22:22; MATTHEW 25:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, the all-powerful one, speaks;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he summons all people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the east to the west.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 48:2; PSALMS 80:1; REVELATION 21:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">His glory shines from Zion Hill in Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">an extremely beautiful city.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DANIEL 7:10; NUMBERS 16:35; LEVITICUS 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our God comes to us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he is not silent.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A great fire is in front of him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and a storm is around him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:1; Isaiah 1:2; DEUTERONOMY 31:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">He comes to judge his people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He shouts to the angels in heaven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to the people on the earth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:28; EXODUS 24:3–8; HEBREWS 13:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">He says, “Summon those who faithfully worship me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those who made an agreement with me by offering sacrifices to me.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 97:6; PSALMS 75:7; PSALMS 89:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The angels in heaven declare,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“God is righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he is the supreme judge.”</span>(Think about that!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 20:2; PSALMS 81:8; JEREMIAH 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">God says, “My people, listen!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You Israeli people, listen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as I, your God, say what you have done that is wrong.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–31; HEBREWS 10:4–10; HOSEA 6:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am not rebuking you for making sacrifices to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for the offerings that you completely burn on the altar.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MICAH 6:6–8; HEBREWS 10:4–6; PSALMS 69:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I do not really need you to sacrifice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the bulls from your barns and the goats from your pens,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:5–6; PSALMS 8:6–8; PSALMS 104:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">because all the animals in the forest belong to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the cattle on 1,000 hills also belong to me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 1:20–22; MATTHEW 6:26; LUKE 12:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I own and know all the birds</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the creatures that move around in the fields.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 19:5; DEUTERONOMY 10:14; PSALMS 24:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, if I were hungry, I would not tell you to bring me some food,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because everything in the world belongs to me!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I do not eat the flesh of the bulls that you sacrifice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I do not drink the blood of the goats that you offer to me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:15; PSALMS 56:12; DEUTERONOMY 23:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sacrifice that I really want is that you thank me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do all that you have promised to do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 107:28; PSALMS 91:15; PSALMS 34:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">And pray to me when you have troubles.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If you do that, I will rescue you, and then you will praise me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 29:13; PSALMS 78:36–38; ROMANS 2:17–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I say this to the wicked people:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">◄Why do you/It does not benefit you at all to► &#91;RHQ&#93; recite my commandments</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or talk about the agreement that I made with you,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26; PROVERBS 12:1; 2 TIMOTHY 4:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">because you have refused to allow me to discipline you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you have rejected what I told you to do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 1:32; 1 TIMOTHY 5:22; HEBREWS 13:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Every time that you see a thief, you become his friend,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you spend much time with those who commit adultery.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 10:7; PSALMS 52:2–4; PSALMS 55:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are always talking &#91;MTY&#93; about doing wicked things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you are always trying to deceive people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:21; PROVERBS 10:18; LEVITICUS 19:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are always accusing members of your own family of doing wrong,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and slandering them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 2:4–5; PROVERBS 29:1; Isaiah 57:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You did all those things, and I did not say anything to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so you thought that I was a sinner just like you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But now I rebuke you and accuse you, right in front of you.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 8:13; PSALMS 9:17; HOSEA 4:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, all you who have ignored me, pay attention to this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because if you do not, I will tear you to pieces,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there will be no one to rescue you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 91:16; JAMES 3:13; PSALMS 24:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sacrifice that truly honors me is to thank me for what I have done;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will save those who always do the things that I want them to.”</span></span>
</div>
