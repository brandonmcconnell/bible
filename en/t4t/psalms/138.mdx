## A psalm written by David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 111:1; PSALMS 95:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, I thank you with all my inner being.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I sing to praise you in front of the idols of false gods to ridicule/belittle them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:18; PSALMS 56:10; MATTHEW 24:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">I bow down while I look towards your sacred temple,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I thank you &#91;MTY&#93; because you faithfully love us and faithfully do all that you have promised.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You have shown that both you &#91;MTY&#93; and what you have promised to do are greater than anything else.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 PETER 5:10; PSALMS 34:4–6; PSALMS 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">On the day when I called out to you, you answered me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you enabled me to be strong and brave.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 102:15; PSALMS 72:11; PSALMS 22:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, some day all the kings of this earth will praise you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they will have heard what you have said.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 21:5; REVELATION 4:11; REVELATION 7:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will sing about what you have done;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will sing and say that you are very great.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 113:5–6; 1 SAMUEL 2:7–8; Isaiah 57:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, you are supreme,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you take care of people who are considered to be unimportant.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And you know what proud people are doing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even though they are far away from you and think that you do not see them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; PSALMS 23:3–4; PSALMS 71:20–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I am in the midst of many troubles/difficulties,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you save/rescue me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With your hand/power &#91;MTY&#93; you rescue me from my enemies who are angry at me.</span><br /></span>
