<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 89:46; PSALMS 74:1; PSALMS 85:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, how long will this continue?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Will you be angry with us forever?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will your being angry destroy us like a burning fire destroys things?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:25; 2 THESSALONIANS 1:8; PSALMS 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Instead of being angry with us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">be angry with the people-groups that do not know/worship you!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Be angry with kingdoms whose people do not pray to you,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:34–35; ZECHARIAH 1:15; Isaiah 64:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-2">because they have killed Israeli people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they have ruined your country.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 64:9; PSALMS 142:6; PSALMS 116:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not punish us because of the sins that our ancestors committed!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Be merciful to us now/quickly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because we are very discouraged.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:7; PSALMS 25:11; 2 CHRONICLES 14:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, you have saved/rescued us many times,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so help us now;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">rescue us and forgive us for having sinned</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order that other people will honor you &#91;MTY&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 42:10; EXODUS 7:5; PSALMS 115:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is not right that &#91;RHQ&#93; other people-groups say about us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“If their God is very powerful, ◄surely he should help them/why does he not help them►?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Allow us to see you punishing the people of other nations in return for their shedding our blood;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they have killed many of us, your people.</span><br /></span>
