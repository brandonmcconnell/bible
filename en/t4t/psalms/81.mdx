<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do that because that is a law for us Israeli people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God commanded it for us descendants of Jacob.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 19:19; NUMBERS 20:13; EXODUS 2:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">When you were greatly distressed, you called out to me, and I rescued you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I answered you out of a thundercloud.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Later I tested whether you would trust me to give you water when you were in the desert at Meribah.</span>(Think about that!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 50:7; JOHN 3:11; Isaiah 1:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">You who are my people, listen while I warn you!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I wish that you Israeli people would pay attention to what I say to you!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 43:12; DEUTERONOMY 32:12; DEUTERONOMY 6:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">You must not have any idols of other gods among you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you must never bow to worship any of them!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 15:7; PSALMS 107:9; EXODUS 20:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am Yahweh, your God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It was not any of those other gods who brought you out of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am the one who did it!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So ask me what you want me to do for you &#91;MTY&#93;, and I will do it.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:18; JEREMIAH 7:23–24; JEREMIAH 2:11–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But my people would not listen to me &#91;SYN&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they would not obey me.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="AMOS 1:8; JUDGES 2:20–23; NUMBERS 14:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">If they did that, I would quickly defeat their enemies;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I would strike/punish all of them &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
