## A psalm written by Asaph

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 10:35; 2 CHRONICLES 19:6–7; PSALMS 82:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">God gathers all the rulers and judges who think they are gods for a meeting in heaven;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he tells them that he has decided this:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:17; PROVERBS 18:5; PSALMS 58:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">“You must &#91;RHQ&#93; stop judging people unfairly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you must no longer make decisions that favor wicked people!</span>(Think about that!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JAMES 1:27; PSALMS 10:18; JEREMIAH 22:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You must defend people who are poor and orphans;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you must act fairly toward those who are needy and those who have no one to help them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:11–12; JOB 5:15–16; JOB 29:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rescue them from the power &#91;MTY&#93; of evil people &#91;DOU&#93;!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 11:3; MICAH 3:1; ROMANS 1:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those rulers do not know or understand anything!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They are very corrupt/evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and as a result of their corrupt/evil behavior,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is as though the foundation of the world is being shaken!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 10:34–36; PSALMS 82:1; EXODUS 22:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">I the all-powerful God, previously said to them, “You think you are gods!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It is as though you are all my sons,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 31:14; PSALMS 49:12; JOB 21:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">but you will die like people do;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your lives will end, like the lives of all rulers end.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="REVELATION 11:15; PSALMS 2:8; PSALMS 96:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, arise and judge everyone on &#91;MTY&#93; the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because all the people-groups belong to you!</span></span>
