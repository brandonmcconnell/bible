## A psalm written by Asaph

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:8; PSALMS 51:10; LUKE 12:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">God truly is good to us Israeli people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to those who totally want to do all that God desires.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 94:18; 1 SAMUEL 2:9; PSALMS 116:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for me, I almost stopped trusting in God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it was as though my feet slipped and I stumbled &#91;MET&#93;,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 21:7; PSALMS 37:7; PROVERBS 23:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">because I envied those who proudly said that they did not need God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I saw that they prospered even though they were wicked.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 21:23–24; ECCLESIASTES 2:16; LUKE 16:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those people do not have any pains;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are always strong and healthy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOB 21:9; PSALMS 73:12; REVELATION 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">They do not have the troubles/difficulties that other people have;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they do not have problems like others do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 109:18; 1 PETER 5:5; DANIEL 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">So they are proud, and their being proud is like a necklace that they show to others,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they show off their violent actions like people show off their beautiful robes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:28; PSALMS 17:10; JOB 15:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">From their inner beings they ◄pour out/do► evil deeds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they are always thinking about more evil things to do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDE 1:16; PSALMS 17:10; PSALMS 10:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They scoff at other people, and they talk about doing evil things to them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are proud while they plan to oppress others.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="REVELATION 13:6; JAMES 3:6; EXODUS 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They say evil things about God who is in heaven &#91;MTY&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they talk boastfully/proudly about things that they have done here on the earth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 75:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The result is that people listen to what they say and praise them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do not think that they have done anything that is wrong.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HOSEA 7:2; ZEPHANIAH 1:12; JOB 22:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wicked people say to themselves, “God will certainly not &#91;RHQ&#93; know what we have done;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people say that he is greater than any other god, but he does not know everything.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 52:7; PSALMS 49:6; HOSEA 12:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">That is what wicked people are like;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they never worry about anything, and they are always becoming richer.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 21:15; PSALMS 26:6; JOB 34:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, God, I think it is useless that I have &#91;RHQ&#93; always done what you want me to,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that I have not sinned.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:5; PSALMS 94:12; JOB 7:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">All day long I have problems,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and every morning you punish me.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:6–7; MALACHI 2:8; 1 PETER 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I had said the things that the wicked people say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I would have been sinning against your people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 8:17; JOHN 16:18–19; PSALMS 36:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And when I tried to think about all this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it was very difficult for me to understand it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 12:20; PSALMS 77:13; ECCLESIASTES 8:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But when I went to your temple, you spoke to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I understood what will happen to the wicked people after they die.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 35:6; JEREMIAH 23:12; DEUTERONOMY 32:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now I know that you will surely put them in slippery places,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will fall down and die.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:1; Isaiah 47:11; DANIEL 5:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will be destroyed instantly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will die in terrible ways.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 20:8; PSALMS 78:65; PSALMS 90:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will disappear as quickly as a dream disappears when a person awakes in the morning;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Lord, when you arise, you will ◄cause them to disappear/forget all about them►.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 37:7; PSALMS 73:3; JOB 16:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I felt sad/bitter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and brokenhearted,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:18; PSALMS 92:6; PSALMS 49:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was stupid and ignorant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I behaved like an animal toward you.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; PSALMS 63:8; MATTHEW 28:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I am always close to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you hold my hand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 32:8; Isaiah 58:11; PROVERBS 3:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You guide me by teaching me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and ◄at the end of my life/when I die►, you will receive me and honor me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:8; PSALMS 16:2; PSALMS 63:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are in heaven and I belong to you &#91;RHQ&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and there is nothing on this earth that I desire more than that.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 12:9–10; Isaiah 40:29–31; PSALMS 138:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">My body and my mind may become very weak,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but God, you continue to enable me to be strong;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I belong to you forever.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JAMES 4:4; PSALMS 119:155; EXODUS 34:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who remain far from you will be destroyed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will get rid of those who abandon you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JAMES 4:8; PSALMS 40:5; HEBREWS 10:19–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me, it is wonderful to be near to God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to be protected by Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to proclaim to others all that he has done for me.</span></span>
</div>
