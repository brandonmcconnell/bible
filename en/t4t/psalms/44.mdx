## A psalm written by one of the descendants of Korah for the choir director

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 78:3–6; EXODUS 13:14–15; DEUTERONOMY 6:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, we ourselves have heard</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">what our parents and grandparents told us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They told us about the miracles</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that you performed long ago.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 78:55; EXODUS 15:17; JOSHUA 11:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">They told us how you expelled the ungodly people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and enabled us to live in their land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They told us that you punished those ungodly people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and enabled your own people to prosper &#91;IDM&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 8:17–18; JOSHUA 24:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">It was not by using their own swords that they conquered the people that lived in that land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it was not by their own power that they were victorious;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it was only by your power &#91;MTY, DOU&#93; that they did those things;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they were sure that you were with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that showed that you were pleased with them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 74:12; PSALMS 42:8; PSALMS 149:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are my King and my God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is you who enable us, your people &#91;MTY&#93;, to defeat our enemies.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 108:13; PSALMS 60:12; PSALMS 118:10–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is by your power that we knock our enemies down and tramp on them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 20:7; HOSEA 1:7; 1 SAMUEL 17:47"> <span className="pl pl-1">I do not trust that I will be saved</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by using my bow and arrows and my sword.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:5; PSALMS 132:18; PSALMS 136:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">No, it is you who have rescued us from our enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is you who have caused those who hate us to become ashamed because they were defeated.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 34:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:29–31; JEREMIAH 9:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">We have continually boasted about what God has done for us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we will thank him &#91;MTY&#93; forever.</span>(Think about that!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 74:1; PSALMS 60:10; PSALMS 60:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now you have rejected us and caused us to be disgraced;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when our armies march out to fight a battle, you no longer go with them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:8; LEVITICUS 26:17; JOSHUA 7:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have caused us to run away from our enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that they captured the things that belonged to us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:27; DEUTERONOMY 28:64; ROMANS 8:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have allowed us to become like &#91;MET&#93; sheep that were ready to be slaughtered;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you scattered us far away among other nations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:13; DEUTERONOMY 32:30; Isaiah 52:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though &#91;MET&#93; you sold us, your people, to our enemies for a very small price,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you did not gain much profit from selling us!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 79:4; PSALMS 80:6; PSALMS 89:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">People who live in nations near us make fun of us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they laugh at us and deride/belittle us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:9; 2 KINGS 19:21; JOB 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">They make jokes using the name of our country,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they shake their heads to indicate that they despise us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 69:7; JEREMIAH 3:25; PSALMS 89:45"> <span className="pl pl-1">All day I feel disgraced;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from seeing my face, people know that I am ashamed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 8:2; PSALMS 74:10; PSALMS 74:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I hear what those who sneer at me and revile me say;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am ashamed in front of my enemies and those who want to harm me.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DANIEL 9:13; DEUTERONOMY 8:14; DEUTERONOMY 6:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All these things have happened to us</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even though we have not forgotten you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we are not the ones who disobeyed the agreement you made with our ancestors.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 119:51; PSALMS 119:157; PSALMS 78:57"> <span className="pl pl-1">We have not stopped being loyal to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we have not stopped doing what you want us to do &#91;IDM&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOB 3:5; PSALMS 23:4; JEREMIAH 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But it is as though you have allowed us to be helpless among wild animals,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and abandoned us in a deep dark ravine.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 81:9; JOB 11:13; DEUTERONOMY 6:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">If we had forgotten to worship &#91;MTY&#93; our God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or if we had spread out our hands to worship a foreign god,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:10; JEREMIAH 23:24; HEBREWS 4:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">you certainly would have known that,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you know even what we secretly think.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 8:36; Isaiah 53:7; PSALMS 44:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">But it is because we belong to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that our enemies are constantly killing us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They act toward us as though we were only sheep to be slaughtered.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 78:65; PSALMS 7:6; PSALMS 35:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, Yahweh, arise! Why are you asleep &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Get up! Do not reject us forever!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOB 13:24; DEUTERONOMY 32:20; PSALMS 42:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why are you not looking at us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Why are you forgetting that we are suffering and being oppressed by our enemies?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 119:25; LAMENTATIONS 4:5; Isaiah 51:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">We are pushed down to the ground</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we cannot get up.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 130:7–8; PSALMS 25:22; PSALMS 26:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Arise, and come and help us!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Rescue us because you faithfully love us!</span></span>
</div>
