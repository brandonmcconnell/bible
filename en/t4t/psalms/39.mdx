<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 90:9–10; PSALMS 144:4; PSALMS 89:47"> <span className="pl pl-1">It seems that you have caused me to live only a short time;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">◄my lifetime/all the time that I have lived► seems like nothing to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The time that all we humans live is as short as &#91;MET&#93; a puff of wind.</span>(Think about that!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 12:20–21; JAMES 4:14; ECCLESIASTES 2:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then we disappear like a shadow does.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It seems that all that we do is for nothing/useless.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We sometimes get a lot of money, but we do not know who will get it after we die.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 44:13; PSALMS 65:3; JOEL 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Save me from being punished for all the sins that I have committed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And do not allow foolish people to make fun of me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOB 2:10; 2 SAMUEL 16:10; LEVITICUS 10:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I did not say anything when you punished me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because I knew that you were the one who caused me to suffer.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 9:34; JOB 13:21; PSALMS 25:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now, please stop punishing me!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If you do not do that, I am about to die because of the ways that you &#91;SYN&#93; have struck/afflicted me.</span></span>
