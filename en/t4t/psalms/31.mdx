## A psalm written by David for the choir director

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 71:2–3; PSALMS 102:2; PSALMS 86:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to me, and save me right now!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Be like a huge rock under which I can hide &#91;MET&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and like a strong fort in which I will be safe.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 23:46; ACTS 7:59; DEUTERONOMY 32:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, you are a God who can be trusted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so I put myself into your care</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you rescued me.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JONAH 2:8; PSALMS 24:4; JEREMIAH 10:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, I hate those who worship useless idols,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but I trust in you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 49:13; Isaiah 63:9; PSALMS 119:153"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will be very glad &#91;DOU&#93; because you faithfully love me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You see me when I have been afflicted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you have known when I have had troubles.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 36:16; DEUTERONOMY 32:30; PSALMS 18:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have not allowed my enemies to capture me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">instead, you have rescued me from danger.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 6:7; PSALMS 88:9; PSALMS 73:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now, Yahweh, be kind to me again</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because I am distressed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because I cry so much, I cannot see well,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I am completely exhausted.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 88:15; PSALMS 32:3–4; PSALMS 38:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have become very weak because I am so miserable,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it is causing my life to become shorter.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have become weak because of all my troubles;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even my bones are becoming weaker.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:10; MATTHEW 27:1; LAMENTATIONS 2:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have heard people slandering me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they have caused me to become terrified.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My enemies are making plans</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order to kill me.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 56:3–4; PSALMS 18:2; PSALMS 43:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Yahweh, I trust in you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You are my God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 1:7; ECCLESIASTES 3:1–8; JOHN 17:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">My whole life &#91;MTY&#93; is in your hands;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">save me from my enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from those who pursue me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 4:6; PSALMS 6:4; DANIEL 9:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be kind to me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and rescue me because you always faithfully love me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 25:2–3; JEREMIAH 20:11; PSALMS 34:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, I call out to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so do not allow me to be disgraced.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I desire that wicked people will be disgraced;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I want them to soon die and go down to the place where the dead people are.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 2:9; Isaiah 64:4; LAMENTATIONS 3:23–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have stored up many blessings for those who revere you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You do those good things for those who go to you to be protected;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">everyone sees you doing that.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 27:5; PSALMS 32:7; PSALMS 91:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You hide people in your presence where it is safe,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and protect them from people who plot to kill them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You hide them in safe places from their enemies &#91;MTY&#93; who accuse/insult them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:7–13; PSALMS 17:7; 1 PETER 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Praise Yahweh!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">When my enemies surrounded the city in which I was living,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he showed me in a wonderful way that he faithfully loves me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 116:11; HEBREWS 5:7; PSALMS 88:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was afraid, and I cried out, “I have been separated from Yahweh!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you heard me and answered my cry for help.</span></span>
