## A psalm written by Asaph for the choir director, to be sung using the tune ‘Do not destroy’

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 145:18; PSALMS 138:2; JEREMIAH 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">We give thanks to you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">our God, we thank you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You are close to us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we proclaim to others the wonderful things that you have done for us.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:17; ACTS 17:31; ACTS 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have said, “I have appointed a time when I will judge people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will judge everyone fairly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:8; Isaiah 24:19; HEBREWS 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the earth shakes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the creatures on the earth tremble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am the one who will ◄keep its foundations steady/prevent it from being destroyed►.</span>(Think about that!)</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 1:21; PROVERBS 1:22; PROVERBS 8:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I say to people who ◄boast/talk proudly about themselves►, ‘Stop bragging!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I say to wicked people, ‘Do not proudly do things to show how great you are &#91;IDM&#93;!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 7:51; EZEKIEL 2:4; Isaiah 48:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not be arrogant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do not speak so boastfully!’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The one who judges people does not come from the east or from the west,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he does not come from the desert.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 1:52; DANIEL 2:21–22; PSALMS 147:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is the one who judges people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he says that some have sinned and must be punished, and that others have not done what is wrong.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 21:20; JEREMIAH 25:15; PSALMS 11:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though Yahweh holds a cup in his hand;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is filled with wine that has spices mixed in it to cause those who drink it to become more drunk;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and when Yahweh pours it out, he will force all the wicked people to drink it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will drink every drop of it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which means that he will give them all the punishment that they deserve.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 9:14; PSALMS 145:1–2; PSALMS 40:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">But as for me, I will never stop saying what the God whom Jacob worshiped has done;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will never quit singing to praise him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 92:10; PSALMS 89:17; JEREMIAH 48:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will destroy the power &#91;IDM&#93; of wicked people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he will cause righteous people to become more powerful.</span></span>
