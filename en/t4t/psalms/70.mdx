## A psalm written by David for the choir director, asking God to help David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 40:13–17; PSALMS 71:12; PSALMS 143:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, please save me!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh, come quickly to help me!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 35:4; PSALMS 35:26; JOHN 18:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Humble those who are happy about my troubles/difficulties, and cause them to be disgraced/ashamed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Chase away those who are trying to kill me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 40:15; ACTS 1:18; EZEKIEL 26:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I hope/desire that you will cause them to become dismayed and ashamed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you have defeated them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:25; PSALMS 40:16; PSALMS 35:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I hope/desire that all those who go to worship you will be very joyful &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I want those who love you because you saved them to shout repeatedly &#91;HYP&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“God is great!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
