## A psalm written by David for the dedication of the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 25:2; DANIEL 4:37; PSALMS 35:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, I praise you because you rescued me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You did not allow me to die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that my enemies could gloat/rejoice.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 103:3–4; 2 KINGS 20:5; PSALMS 147:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, my God, I called out for you to help me when I was ill,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you healed me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 86:13; PSALMS 16:10; JONAH 2:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You saved/restored me when I was dying &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I was nearly dead,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you caused me to get well again.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 97:12; REVELATION 4:8; 1 CHRONICLES 16:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">All you who belong to Yahweh, sing praise to him!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Think about what God, the holy one, has done, and thank him!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 54:7–8; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:17; PSALMS 126:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">When he becomes angry, he is angry for only a very short time &#91;HYP&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he is good to us all of our life.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We may cry during the night,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the next morning we will be joyful.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LUKE 12:19; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:7; PSALMS 119:117"> <span className="pl pl-1">As for me, when I had no troubles, I said to myself,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“No one will defeat me!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 104:29; PSALMS 143:7; PSALMS 30:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, because you were good to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">at first you caused me to be safe as though I was inside &#91;MET&#93; a mountain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But then I became ill, and I thought that you had turned away from me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I became afraid.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:6–7; PSALMS 34:6; PSALMS 130:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I called out to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I pleaded for you to help me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 6:5; PSALMS 115:17–18; Isaiah 38:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said, “Yahweh, ◄what will you gain if I die?/ you won’t gain anything if I die► &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">◄In what way will it benefit you?/it won’t benefit you at all► if I go to the place where the dead people are &#91;RHQ&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Dead people are certainly not able to praise you &#91;RHQ&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they are not able to tell others that you are trustworthy &#91;RHQ&#93;!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 54:4; PSALMS 143:1; PSALMS 143:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, listen to me, and be merciful to me!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh, help me!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:4; JEREMIAH 31:13–14; ECCLESIASTES 3:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now you have healed me, and you have caused me to change from being sad to dancing joyfully.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have taken away the clothes that showed that I was very sad</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you have given me clothes that showed that I was very joyful.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 145:2; PSALMS 146:1–2; PSALMS 44:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will not be silent;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will sing out loudly to praise you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, you are my God,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will thank you forever.</span></span>
</div>
