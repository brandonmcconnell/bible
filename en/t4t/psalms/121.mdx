## A psalm written for people going up to the temple to worship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 120:1; PSALMS 123:1; JEREMIAH 3:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">When we travel toward Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I look up toward the hills and I ask myself, “Who will help me?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 41:13; PSALMS 124:8; HEBREWS 13:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And my answer is that Yahweh is the one who helps me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he is the one who made heaven and the earth.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:26; PROVERBS 3:23; 1 SAMUEL 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will not allow us to fall/stumble;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God, who protects us, will not fall asleep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 127:1; Isaiah 27:3; PSALMS 32:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The one who protects us Israeli people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">never gets sleepy, nor does he sleep &#91;LIT&#93;.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; PSALMS 91:1; Isaiah 4:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh watches over us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he is like the shade &#91;MET&#93; that protects us from the sun.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="REVELATION 7:16; Isaiah 49:10; PSALMS 91:5–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will not allow the sun to harm us during the day,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will not allow the moon to harm us during the night.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:18; PSALMS 91:9–12; PSALMS 41:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will protect us from being harmed in any manner;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will keep us safe.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:6; PROVERBS 2:8; PROVERBS 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will protect us from the time that we leave our houses in the morning until we return in the evening;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will protect us now, and he will protect us forever.</span></span>
</div>
