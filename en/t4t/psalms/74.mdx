## A psalm written by Asaph

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 1:10; PSALMS 79:1; DANIEL 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Walk along and see where everything has been totally ruined;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">our enemies have destroyed everything in the sacred temple.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:7; MATTHEW 24:15; DANIEL 6:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your enemies shouted triumphantly in this sacred place;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they erected their banners to show they had defeated us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 5:6; 2 CHRONICLES 2:14; JEREMIAH 46:22–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">They cut down all the engraved objects in the temple like woodsmen cut down trees.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 6:18; 1 KINGS 6:29; 1 KINGS 6:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then they smashed all the carved wood with their axes and hammers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:9; PSALMS 89:39; EZEKIEL 24:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then they burned your temple to the ground;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they caused that place where you were worshiped to be unfit for people to worship in.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 83:4; 2 KINGS 4:23; Esther 3:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">They said to themselves, “We will destroy the Israelis completely,”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they also burned down all the other places where we gathered to worship God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="AMOS 8:11; EZEKIEL 7:26; 1 SAMUEL 3:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">All our sacred symbols (OR, miracles) are gone;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there are no prophets now/any more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no one knows how long this situation will continue.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 89:50–51; PSALMS 44:16; PSALMS 89:46"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, how long will our enemies make fun of you &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will they insult you &#91;MTY&#93; forever &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 2:3; PSALMS 78:65–66; PSALMS 44:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do you refuse to help &#91;MTY, RHQ&#93; us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Why do you keep your hand inside your cloak instead of using it to destroy our enemies &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 44:4; EXODUS 19:5–6; Isaiah 33:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, you have been our king all the time since we came out of Egypt &#91;HYP&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you have enabled us to defeat our enemies in the land of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 14:21; PSALMS 78:13; EZEKIEL 29:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">By your power you caused the Red Sea to divide;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it was as though you smashed the heads of the rulers of Egypt who were like huge sea dragons &#91;MET&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 27:1; PSALMS 72:9; PSALMS 104:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">It was as though you crushed the head of the king of Egypt &#91;MET&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and gave his body to the animals in the desert to eat.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:11; Isaiah 48:21; PSALMS 105:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">You caused springs and streams to flow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you also dried up rivers that had never dried up previously.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:8; ACTS 17:26; GENESIS 8:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">You determined where the oceans end and the land begins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you created the summer/hot season and the winter/cold season.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 39:8; REVELATION 16:19; DEUTERONOMY 32:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, do not forget that your enemies laugh at you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and that it is foolish people who despise you &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 2:14; MATTHEW 10:16; PSALMS 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not let your helpless people &#91;MET&#93; fall into the hands of their cruel enemies;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">do not forget your suffering/persecuted people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 106:45; GENESIS 17:7–8; HEBREWS 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not forget the agreement that you made with us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">remember that there are violent people in every dark place on the earth.</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 74:18; PSALMS 53:1; Isaiah 52:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, arise and defend yourself by defending your people!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not forget that foolish people laugh at you ◄all day long/continually►!</span><br /></span>
