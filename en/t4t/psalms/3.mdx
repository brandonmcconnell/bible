## A psalm written by David when he fled from his son Absalom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 17:7; 2 SAMUEL 17:11–13; 2 SAMUEL 16:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, I have many enemies &#91;DOU&#93;!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There are many people who oppose/rebel against me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 71:11; PSALMS 42:3; PSALMS 42:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many people are saying about me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“God will certainly not help him!”</span>(Think about that!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 27:6; PSALMS 28:7; GENESIS 15:1"> <span className="pl pl-2">But Yahweh, you are like a shield that protects me &#91;MET&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You greatly honor me, and you encourage me (OR, enable me to triumph over my enemies) &#91;IDM&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 34:4; PSALMS 91:15; Isaiah 65:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I cry out to you, Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you answer me from Zion, your sacred hill.</span>(Think about that!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 4:8; PROVERBS 3:24; Isaiah 26:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">At night I lie down and sleep, and I awake in the morning feeling good/refreshed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you, Yahweh, ◄take care of/protect► me all during the night.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ROMANS 8:31; PSALMS 27:1–3; PSALMS 118:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">There may be thousands of enemy soldiers who surround me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but I am not afraid.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 58:6; JOB 29:17; JOB 16:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, arise!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">My God, come and rescue me again!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You insult my enemies by slapping them on their cheeks;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when you strike them, you break their teeth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that they cannot hurt anyone &#91;MET&#93;.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:11; JONAH 2:9; HOSEA 13:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, you are the one who saves your people from their enemies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Yahweh, bless your people!</span>(Think about that!)</span>
