## A psalm written by one of the descendants of Korah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MICAH 4:1; ZECHARIAH 8:3; PSALMS 96:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh is great, and he deserves to be greatly praised in the city where he lives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which is built on Zion, his sacred hill.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 50:2; LAMENTATIONS 2:15; MATTHEW 5:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">That city, on a high hill, is beautiful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is the city where the true God, the great king, lives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it causes people all over the world to rejoice when they see it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:4–5; PSALMS 125:1; PSALMS 46:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">God is in the strong towers there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he shows that he protects the people in that city.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 10:6–19; REVELATION 17:12–14; REVELATION 20:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many kings gathered with their armies to attack that city,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 14:25; 2 KINGS 7:6–7; 2 KINGS 19:35–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">but when they saw it, they were amazed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they became terrified, and ran away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HOSEA 13:13; Isaiah 13:6–8; Isaiah 21:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because they were very afraid, they trembled</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a woman who is about to give birth to a child;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:17; EZEKIEL 27:25–26; 1 KINGS 22:48"> <span className="pl pl-1">they shook like ships sailing from Tarshish are shaken by a strong wind.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 87:5; PSALMS 78:3–6; PSALMS 46:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">We had heard that this city is glorious,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and now we have seen that it is.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It is the city in which Yahweh, the almighty one, lives.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It is the city which God will preserve/protect forever.</span>(Think about that!)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 26:3; PSALMS 40:10; PSALMS 77:10–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">God, here in your temple we think about how you faithfully love us.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MALACHI 1:11; DEUTERONOMY 28:58; PSALMS 113:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You &#91;MTY&#93; are famous and ◄are praised/people praise you► all over the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you rule powerfully &#91;MTY&#93; and justly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 97:8; REVELATION 16:5–7; REVELATION 18:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people who live near Zion Hill should be happy!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The people in all the cities &#91;MTY&#93; in Judah should rejoice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you judge people fairly.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 33:18–20; Nehemiah 12:31–40; MATTHEW 24:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You Israeli people should walk around Zion Hill</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and count the towers there;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 71:18; PSALMS 122:7; DEUTERONOMY 11:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">notice the walls there and examine the forts</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order that you can tell about them to your children.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 58:11; PSALMS 23:3–4; PSALMS 73:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Say to them, “This is the city that belongs to our God, the one who lives forever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will guide us all of our lives.”</span></span>
