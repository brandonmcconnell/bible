## Philemon 1-3


## I, Paul, am writing this letter to you, Philemon, and to those with you. May God bless you(pl).

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:1; EPHESIANS 3:1; PHILEMON 1:9"> I, Paul, am a prisoner who serves Christ Jesus. I am here with Timothy, our fellow believer. I am writing this letter to you, Philemon, our dear friend and fellow worker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:25; COLOSSIANS 4:17; ROMANS 16:5"> I am also writing to your wife, Apphia, and to Archippus, who is like &#91;MET&#93; our fellow soldier because he serves Christ steadfastly together with us. I am also writing this to the congregation that meets/gathers in your (sg) house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 1:7; EPHESIANS 1:2; 2 CORINTHIANS 13:14"> I pray that God our Father and our Lord Jesus Christ will continue to act kindly toward you all and will continue to cause you to have inner peace.</span>
</div>
</div>

## I thank God and rejoice greatly because you have shown that you love God’s people.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 1:8; EPHESIANS 1:16; 2 THESSALONIANS 1:3"> I always thank my God when I pray for you (sg), because I hear people say that you love all God’s people &#91;CHI&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:4; EPHESIANS 1:15; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:1"> and that you continue to trust in the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:9–11; 2 PETER 1:8; COLOSSIANS 1:9"> I pray that as a result of your knowing all the good things that God/Christ has done for us, you may ◄be effective/influence many others► as you tell others what you believe, in order that they may honor Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 7:13; 1 THESSALONIANS 3:9; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:4"> I have rejoiced greatly and have been greatly encouraged because you, my dear friend, have acted lovingly toward God’s people by encouraging them &#91;SYN&#93;.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Because I know that you love God’s people, I request, rather than command you, to do what you ought to do for my spiritual son Onesimus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:6; 2 CORINTHIANS 10:8; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:12"> So I have a request/favor to ask of you. I am completely confident that I have authority to command you to do what you ought to do, because I am an apostle of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:20; EPHESIANS 4:1; ROMANS 12:1"> But because I know that you love God’s people, I request this instead of commanding you to do it. It is I, Paul, an old man and now also a prisoner because I serve Christ Jesus, who am requesting it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:15; 1 TIMOTHY 1:2"> I request that you do something for someone who has become like my own son because I told him about Christ &#91;MET&#93; while I have been a prisoner &#91;MTY&#93; here. His name is Onesimus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:11; JOB 30:1–2; 1 PETER 2:10"> Although his name, as you know, means ‘useful’, formerly he was useless to you. But now he is useful both to you and to me!</span>
</div>
</div>

## I am sending Onesimus back to you. He is as dear to me as my own self, and he will now be even dearer to you than he is to me.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:14–15; JEREMIAH 31:20; MARK 11:25"> Although ◄he is as dear to me as/I love him as much as I love► &#91;MET&#93; my own self &#91;MTY&#93;, I am sending him back to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:7; EPHESIANS 3:1; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:17"> I would like to have kept him with me, in order that he might serve me on your behalf, while I am a prisoner &#91;MTY&#93; because of my preaching the message about Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 9:7; 1 CHRONICLES 29:17; 1 PETER 5:2–3"> Nevertheless, because I had not yet asked you and you had not yet permitted me to keep him here with me, I decided not to keep him here. I decided that I should not do anything without your permitting it. I decided that you should help me only if you really want to help me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 50:20; GENESIS 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> Perhaps the reason that God permitted Onesimus to be separated from &#123;to leave&#125; you for a little while was that he would believe in Christ, and as a result you would have him ◄back/with you► forever!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:22; MATTHEW 23:8; 1 CORINTHIANS 7:22"> You will no longer have him only as a slave. Instead, you will have him as someone who is more than a slave. You will have him as a fellow believer! He is especially dear to me, but he certainly will be more dear to you than he is to me (OR, I love him very much, but you will certainly love him more than I do), because now he not only belongs to you but he also belongs to the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## If you consider me to be your partner, receive Onesimus as you would receive me. I guarantee to repay you whatever Onesimus owes you.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 8:23; MATTHEW 10:40; PHILEMON 1:12"> So, if you consider me to be your partner in God’s work, receive him as you would receive me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> If he has wronged you in any manner or if he owes you anything, ◄charge that to me/tell me so that I can pay you►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:11; GALATIANS 5:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:15"> I, Paul, am now writing this in my own handwriting: I will repay you what he owes you, although I might mention to you that you owe me even more than Onesimus may owe you, because it was the result of my telling you about Christ that God saved you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Please encourage me in this matter as you encourage other believers in Christ.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 JOHN 1:4; PHILEMON 1:7; PHILEMON 1:12"> ◄Yes/I say it again►, my dear friend, because you and I both have a relationship with the Lord, I want you to do this for me. Encourage me &#91;SYN&#93; by receiving Onesimus kindly, just like you encourage others who believe in Christ.</span>
</div>
</div>

## I have written this letter to you confident of your compliance with my request.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 3:4; 2 CORINTHIANS 2:3; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:16"> I have written this letter to you, confident that you will do what I am requesting you to do. In fact, I know that you will do even more than what I am requesting you to do.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Also, keep a guest room ready for me.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 1:11; PHILIPPIANS 2:24; 2 JOHN 1:12"> Also, keep a guest room ready for me to stay in, because I confidently expect that as a result of your prayers for me, I will be released from prison and will come to you all.</span>
</div>
</div>

## My other fellow workers greet you(sg). May the Lord Jesus Christ bless you(pl) spiritually.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:7; ROMANS 16:7; COLOSSIANS 4:12"> Epaphras, who is suffering with me in prison because of his serving Christ Jesus, ◄sends his greetings to you/wants you to know that he is thinking fondly about you►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:10–11; COLOSSIANS 4:14; COLOSSIANS 4:10"> Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, who are my other fellow workers, also ◄send their greetings to you/want you to know that they are thinking fondly about you►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:22; ROMANS 16:23; ROMANS 16:20"> I pray that the Lord Jesus Christ will ◄continue to work kindly in all your lives/kindly keep accomplishing what he desires within you►.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
