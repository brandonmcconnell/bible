## Joshua’s army captured towns in the north

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:10; PSALMS 2:1–4; JUDGES 4:2"> When King Jabin of Hazor city heard about all those things that had happened, he sent messages to Jobab, the king of Madon city, to the king of Shimron city, and to the king of Acshaph city, requesting them to send their armies to come and help him fight against the Israelis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 12:3; JUDGES 1:27; JOSHUA 17:11"> He also sent messages to the kings in the northern hilly area and to the kings in the Jordan River Valley, south of Galilee Lake and in the western hills. He sent a message to the king of Naphoth-Dor in the west</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDGES 3:3; JOSHUA 18:26; GENESIS 31:49"> and to the kings of the Canaan people-group in the eastern and western parts of the land. He sent messages to the Amor people-group and the Heth people-group and the Periz people-group and the Jebus people-group who lived in the hilly area. He also sent a message to the Hiv people-group who lived below Hermon Mountain in the Mizpah area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 7:12; 1 SAMUEL 13:5; GENESIS 32:12"> So the armies of all those kings gathered together, bringing their horses and chariots. There seemed to be &#91;HYP&#93; as many soldiers as grains of sand on the seashore.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 118:10–12; REVELATION 16:14; Isaiah 8:9"> All of those kings and their armies gathered together at Merom Pond, and they set up their tents there. Then they made plans to fight against the Israelis.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:8; 2 SAMUEL 8:4; JUDGES 20:28"> Then Yahweh said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid of them, because at this time tomorrow I will enable your men to defeat them. Your men must also cripple their horses and burn their chariots.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:9; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:2–3"> So Joshua and his army traveled to Merom Pond and attacked their enemies ◄by surprise/before they were ready to fight►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:6; JOSHUA 19:28; ZECHARIAH 9:2"> Yahweh enabled the Israelis to defeat them. They pursued them to Sidon city in the far north, and to Misrephoth-Maim in the northwest, and to Mizpah Valley in the northeast. The Israelis fought them until ◄they were all dead/there were no survivors►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:6; EZEKIEL 39:9–10"> Then Joshua and his men did what Yahweh told them to do: They crippled their enemies’ horses and burned their chariots.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:1; JUDGES 4:2"> The king of Hazor had ruled the people of all the kingdoms whose armies had fought against the Israelis. So Joshua’s army went back to Hazor and captured that city and killed their king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:40; DEUTERONOMY 20:16"> They killed everyone in Hazor; ◄they did not spare anyone/no one was left alive► &#91;DOU&#93;. Then they burned the city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:16–17; DEUTERONOMY 7:2; NUMBERS 33:50–53"> Joshua’s army captured all of those cities and killed all of their kings. They did that like Moses, the man who served Yahweh well, had commanded them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:18"> Joshua’s men burned Hazor city, but they did not burn any of the other cities that were built on small hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:14; NUMBERS 31:9; JOSHUA 8:27"> The Israelis took for themselves the animals that they found in those cities and everything else that was valuable &#91;DOU&#93;. But they killed all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:7; EXODUS 34:11–13; ACTS 20:20"> Many years before, Yahweh had told Moses to do that, and then Moses told Joshua to do it. So Joshua did everything that Yahweh had commanded Moses to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:41; JOSHUA 11:21; JOSHUA 12:8"> Joshua’s army defeated all the people who were living in that land. They took control over the hilly area and the area in the southern part of Canaan, all the area of Goshen, the western foothills, and the Jordan River Valley. They took control over all the mountains in Israel and all the hills near the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:24; JOSHUA 11:3; JOSHUA 13:5"> They took control of all the land from Halak Mountain in the south up to the Seir hilly area as far as Baal-Gaal in Lebanon Valley in the north, at the bottom of Hermon Mountain. They captured all the kings of those areas and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 14:7–10; JOSHUA 11:23"> Joshua’s men fought against those groups for many years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 9:3–27"> There was only one group that made a peace agreement with the Israelis; they were the people of the Hiv people-group who lived in Gibeon. The Israelis captured all the other towns in battles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 9:18; DEUTERONOMY 20:16–17; EXODUS 4:21"> Yahweh caused the people in all those other groups to be stubborn &#91;IDM&#93;, with the result that they fought against the Israeli army, and the Israelis killed them all. They were not merciful to any of them. That is what Yahweh had commanded Moses to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:2; NUMBERS 13:22–23; JOSHUA 15:13–14"> Joshua’s army also fought the Anak people-group who lived in the hills near Hebron, Debir, and Anah cities. They also fought against the people who lived in the hilly area of Judah and Israel, and they killed all of those people and destroyed their cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:4; 1 CHRONICLES 18:1; JOSHUA 15:46"> As a result, there were no descendants of Anak who remained alive in Israel. Only a few remained alive in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:44–45; NUMBERS 26:52–55; JOSHUA 14:1–15"> Joshua’s army took control of all the land, just as Yahweh long ago had told Moses to do. Yahweh gave the land to the Israelis, because he had promised to give it to them. Then Joshua divided the land among the Israeli tribes. After that, there was ◄peace/no more battles► in the land.</span>
</div>
</div>
