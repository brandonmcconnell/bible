## Joshua encouraged the Israeli people to worship only Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:2; 1 SAMUEL 10:19; GENESIS 33:18–19"> Many years later, Joshua summoned representatives of all the tribes of Israel. He gathered together the elders, the leaders, the judges, and the other officials at Shechem city. He told them to listen to what God wanted to tell them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:15; EZEKIEL 16:3; GENESIS 31:30"> Joshua said to all of them, “This is what Yahweh, the God we Israeli people worship, is saying: ‘Long ago, your ancestors, including Abraham’s father Terah and Abraham’s younger brother Nahor, lived on the east side of the Euphrates River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 12:1–4; GENESIS 21:2–3; ACTS 7:2–3"> But I took your ancestor Abraham from that land east of the Euphrates River, and I led him as he lived in various places in this land of Canaan, and I enabled him to have many descendants. First, I enabled him to have a son, Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 36:8; DEUTERONOMY 2:5; GENESIS 25:24–26"> When Isaac grew up, I enabled him to have twin sons, Jacob and Esau. I enabled Esau to live in the hilly area in Seir region, but many years later Jacob and his sons and their families went down to live in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 3:10; EXODUS 7:1–12; PSALMS 105:26–36"> “Many years later, I sent Moses and his older brother Aaron to help your people, and I caused the people of Egypt to suffer very much because of what I did there. Then I enabled your ancestors to leave Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ACTS 7:36; HEBREWS 11:29; Isaiah 63:12–13"> When I brought your ancestors out of Egypt, they came to the Red Sea. The Egyptian army pursued them, some riding in chariots and others on horses, and they also arrived at the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:34; EXODUS 14:10; EXODUS 14:20"> Then your ancestors pleaded with me to help them. So he caused ◄darkness/a very dark cloud► to come between your ancestors and the Egyptian army, so that the Egyptian army could not see your ancestors. I separated the water in the Red Sea so that your ancestors could cross it, but when the Egyptian army tried to cross in the same way to pursue them, I caused the water to come back and cover them, and the Egyptian soldiers all drowned, as your ancestors watched and were amazed &#91;IDM&#93;. But after that, you lived in the desert for many years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:21–35; PSALMS 135:10–11; Nehemiah 9:22"> “Then I brought your ancestors to the area where the groups who were descendants of Amor lived, east of the Jordan River. They fought against you, but I enabled you to defeat them &#91;IDM&#93;. I enabled you to destroy them so that you could live in their land &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 11:25; MICAH 6:5; NUMBERS 22:2"> Then, Zippor’s son Balak, the King of Moab, ◄decided that his army would fight against/opposed► the Israelis. He summoned Beor’s son Balaam and asked him to curse you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:5; NUMBERS 24:5–10; Isaiah 54:17"> But I would not do what Balaam asked, so he ◄blessed/said that I would do great things for► you four times, and I did not enable the army of Moab to defeat you &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 4:10–12; JOSHUA 3:14–17; PSALMS 114:5"> “Then you all crossed the Jordan River and came to Jericho. The people of Jericho prepared to fight against you, and the armies of the descendants of Amor, Periz, Canaan, Heth, Girgash, Hiv, and Jebus all prepared to do the same thing but I enabled you to defeat &#91;IDM&#93; them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:20; EXODUS 23:28; PSALMS 44:3–6"> I am the one who caused them to panic as you advanced and enabled you to defeat them, as I had enabled you to do earlier to the two kings of the groups who were descendants of Amor. You did not defeat them by using your own bows and arrows and swords; it was I who defeated them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:10–12; DEUTERONOMY 8:7; JOSHUA 21:45"> So I gave you a land that you had not tilled/planted, and I gave you cities that you did not build. Now you live in those cities and you eat the grapes from the grapevines that you did not plant, and you eat olives from trees that you did not plant.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 12:24; DEUTERONOMY 10:12; GENESIS 35:2"> Then Joshua said to the people, “Because of all that Yahweh has done for you, revere him, and serve/worship him very faithfully. Throw away the idols that your ancestors worshiped on the east/other side of the Euphrates River and in Egypt. Serve only Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:21; JOHN 6:67–68; RUTH 1:15–16"> But if you do not want to serve/worship Yahweh, you should decide today what gods you will serve/worship. You should decide whether you will serve/worship the gods that your ancestors who lived on the other/east side of the Euphrates River served, or whether you will serve/worship the gods that the descendants of Amor, who previously lived in the land where you are now living, serve/worship. But as for me and my family &#91;MTY&#93;, we will serve/worship Yahweh!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:38–39; 1 SAMUEL 12:23; ROMANS 6:2"> The Israeli people answered, “We will never quit serving/worshiping Yahweh! We would never think of &#91;IDM&#93; serving/worshiping other gods!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 19:4; Isaiah 63:7–14; Isaiah 46:4"> It was our God, Yahweh, who brought our parents and grandparents up out of Egypt. He rescued them from that land where they were slaves. As he rescued them, they saw him perform great miracles. He protected them all the time when they were traveling. He protected them from all the people-groups through whose territory they traveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MICAH 4:2; EXODUS 10:2; PSALMS 116:16"> As our forefathers advanced, Yahweh expelled the descendants of Amor and the other people-groups who lived in this land. Yahweh is our God, so we are saying that we also will serve/worship him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 5:16; EXODUS 23:21; PSALMS 99:9"> Joshua replied to the people, “I think that you are not able to serve/worship Yahweh, because he is a holy God. He will not forgive your sinning and rebelling against him. He demands that you serve/worship only him &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:26–27; EZEKIEL 18:24; 1 CHRONICLES 28:9"> He has been good to you in the past, but if you turn away from him and serve/worship foreign/other gods, he will turn against you and he will cause you to experience disasters. He will punish &#91;IDM&#93; you severely!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 20:19; Isaiah 44:5; DEUTERONOMY 5:27–28"> But the people replied to Joshua, “No, we will not turn away from worshiping/serving Yahweh We will serve/worship Yahweh!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 119:173; PSALMS 119:11; JOB 15:6"> Then Joshua said, “You yourselves are saying that you have decided to serve/worship Yahweh.” They replied, “Yes, we are saying that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:16–18; JOSHUA 24:14; HEBREWS 12:28–29"> Then Joshua said, “Since you have decided that, you must throw away all the other gods/idols that you have among you. You must also promise that you will wholeheartedly give yourselves to Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis serve/worship.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 24:7; EXODUS 24:3; DEUTERONOMY 5:27–29"> The people replied, “We will serve/worship Yahweh, our God, and obey him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:38; 2 CHRONICLES 23:16; EXODUS 15:25"> That day, Joshua established an agreement between the people and Yahweh. He wrote for them all the laws that they were required to obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JUDGES 9:6; GENESIS 35:4; JOSHUA 4:20–24"> He wrote all those laws on a scroll. He called it ‘The laws of God’. Then he told some men to set up a large stone there at Shechem, under the large oak tree near the place where they worshiped Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 19:40; JOSHUA 22:34; JOSHUA 22:27–28"> He said to all the people, “Look! It is as though this stone has heard everything that Yahweh said to you and that you promised Yahweh. It will serve as a witness against you if you rebel against your God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JUDGES 2:6"> Then Joshua sent the people away, and all of them returned to their own areas/homes.</span>

## Joshua died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JUDGES 2:8; 2 TIMOTHY 4:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 34:5"> Some time after that, Nun’s son Joshua, the faithful servant of Yahweh, died. He was 110 years old when he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:50; JUDGES 2:9; 2 SAMUEL 23:30"> The Israeli people buried his body on his own property in Timnath-Serah town town. It is north of Gaash Mountain in the hilly area of the tribe who were descendants of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JUDGES 2:7; DEUTERONOMY 31:13; DEUTERONOMY 11:2"> The Israeli people served/worshiped Yahweh as long as Joshua was alive. After Joshua died, they continued serving/worshiping Yahweh while the elders who had experienced everything that Yahweh had done for the Israeli people were still alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 50:25; GENESIS 33:19; EXODUS 13:19"> Joseph’s bones, which the Israeli people had brought with them from Egypt, were also buried at Shechem. The people buried them in the piece of land that Jacob had bought long ago for 100 pieces of silver from Hamor, the father of Shechem. That piece of land was in the area that was given to the people who were descendants of Ephraim and Manasseh, Joseph’s sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOSHUA 22:13; EXODUS 6:25; Isaiah 57:1–2"> Eleazar, the Supreme Priest, the son of Aaron, also died. They buried his body at Gibeah, in the area that had been given to Eleazar’s son Phinehas, in the hilly area that belonged to the people who were descendants of Ephraim.</span>
