## The town of Ai is destroyed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:9; DEUTERONOMY 7:18; DEUTERONOMY 1:21"> Then Yahweh said to Joshua, “Do not be afraid or discouraged because of what happened at Ai. Lead all of your soldiers and go there again. I will help you to defeat the king of Ai, his people, and his city, and enable you to take his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:14; JOSHUA 6:21; 2 CHRONICLES 20:22"> Your army will do to the people of Ai and their king like what you did to the people of Jericho and their king. But this time I will permit you to take all their possessions and keep them for yourselves. But first, tell some of your soldiers to hide behind the city and prepare to suddenly attack it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:39; MATTHEW 25:6; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:2"> So Joshua led all his army toward Ai. He chose 30,000 of his best fighters/warriors and prepared to send them out during the night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 20:29; JOSHUA 8:16; JUDGES 20:36"> He said to them, “Listen carefully. Some of you must hide on the other side of the city. Do not go far from the city. Just be ready to attack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:5; MATTHEW 10:16; JUDGES 20:31–33"> I and the men who are with me will march toward the city in the morning. The men in the city will come out to fight us, like they did before. Then we will turn around and start to run away from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDGES 20:32; EXODUS 15:9; ECCLESIASTES 8:11"> They will think that we are running away from them like we did before. So they will chase us away from the city. While we are running away,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:30–31; 2 KINGS 5:1; JOSHUA 8:1"> those of you who are hiding come out and rush into the city and capture it. Yahweh your God will enable you to conquer it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 13:28; JOSHUA 1:16; JOSHUA 1:9"> After you capture the city, burn it. Do what Yahweh has commanded us to do. Those are the orders I am giving to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:2; GENESIS 12:8; Ezra 2:28"> Then Joshua prepared to send some of them to hide and wait between Ai and Bethel, which was west of Ai. But Joshua stayed with his other soldiers that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:12; PSALMS 119:60; GENESIS 22:3"> Early the next morning, Joshua gathered his soldiers together. Then he and the other Israeli leaders led them up to Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They all set up their tents close to Ai, just to the north of the city, where all the people of the city could see them. There was a valley between them and the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:2–3"> Then Joshua chose about 5,000 men and told them to go and hide just west of the city, between Ai and Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:12; JOSHUA 8:8"> So those men did that. The main group of soldiers was north of the city, and the others were hiding west of the city. That night Joshua went down into the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:39; JUDGES 20:34–36; DEUTERONOMY 1:1"> When the king of Ai saw the Israeli army, he and his soldiers got up early the next morning and quickly went out of the city to fight them. They went to a place east of the city, but they did not know that some Israeli soldiers were hiding behind the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:12; JOSHUA 16:1; JUDGES 20:36"> Joshua and the Israeli soldiers who were with him allowed the army of Ai to push them back. They ran toward the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 20:31; JUDGES 20:36–39; JOSHUA 8:5–6"> The men in Ai were ordered to chase after Joshua and his men. So they left the city and started to pursue the Israelis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 5:13; JOSHUA 8:3; Isaiah 19:11–13"> All the men of Ai and the men of Bethel pursued the Israeli army. They did not leave even one man in Ai to defend it. The gates of the city were left wide open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:26; JOB 15:25; 1 SAMUEL 17:41"> Then Yahweh said to Joshua, “Lift up your spear and point it toward Ai, because I am going to enable your soldiers to capture it!” So Joshua pointed his spear toward Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> When the Israeli men who were hiding saw that, they rushed out from the places where they were hiding and ran into the city. They quickly captured it and set it on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 76:5; JOB 11:20; GENESIS 19:28"> When the men of Ai looked back, they saw smoke rising from their city. But they could not escape, because the Israeli troops stopped running away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Joshua and his men saw that the men who had been hiding had captured the city and were burning it, and they saw the smoke rising. So they turned around and started to attack the men of Ai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:2; JOSHUA 10:28; JOSHUA 6:21"> Meanwhile, the soldiers who had captured the city came out and attacked them from the rear. So the men of Ai were caught between the two groups of Israeli soldiers. None of the men of Ai escaped. The Israelis fought until they killed all of them. Only the king of Ai was still alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:8; JOSHUA 10:17; REVELATION 19:20"> Then they seized the king of Ai and brought him to Joshua.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A summary of the battle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:10–14; JOSHUA 10:30–41; NUMBERS 21:24"> While they were fighting, the Israeli army pursued the men of Ai into the fields and into the desert, and killed all of them. Then they went to Ai and killed everyone who was there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:16–18"> They killed 12,000 men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 17:11–12; JOSHUA 8:18"> Joshua continued to point his spear &#91;LIT&#93; toward Ai, until all the people in Ai had been killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:2; NUMBERS 31:26; PSALMS 50:10"> The Israeli soldiers took for themselves the animals and the other things that had belonged to the people of Ai, just like Yahweh had told Joshua that they should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:16; JEREMIAH 49:2; 2 KINGS 19:25"> Joshua and his soldiers burned Ai city and caused it to become a pile of ruins. It is still like that today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:26; 2 SAMUEL 18:17; DEUTERONOMY 21:22–23"> Joshua hanged the king of Ai on a tree and left his corpse hanging there until the evening. At sunset Joshua told his men to take the king’s corpse down from the tree and to throw it on the ground at the city gate. After they did that, they piled a lot of rocks on top of the corpse, and that pile of rocks is still there.</span>
</div>

## Joshua repeated Yahweh’s agreement with the Israeli people

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:4–6; EXODUS 20:24; GENESIS 12:7–8"> Joshua told his men to build on Ebal Mountain an altar for Yahweh, the God who is worshiped by the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:5–7; EXODUS 20:24–25; JOSHUA 8:34–35"> They built it just like Moses, the man who served God well, had written previously in the laws that God had given to him. They made it from stones that had not been cut using iron tools. The Israelis then offered sacrifices to Yahweh that were burned completely on the altar. They also offered sacrifices to restore fellowship with Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:2–3; DEUTERONOMY 27:8"> As the Israelis watched, Joshua wrote on stones the laws that Yahweh had given to Moses previously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:29; DEUTERONOMY 31:12; DEUTERONOMY 31:9"> The Israeli leaders, the officials, the judges, and other Israelis were there, standing nearby. Many people who were not Israelis were also there. Half of the people stood on one side of the valley below Ebal Mountain, and the other half of the people stood on the other side of the valley below Gerizim Mountain. The sacred chest was in the valley between the two groups. That was what Moses had previously commanded that the people should do when Yahweh was about to bless them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:2–3; Nehemiah 13:1; DEUTERONOMY 31:10–12"> Then Joshua read to the people all that Moses had written previously. That included what Yahweh had taught them and the ways that he promised to bless them if they obeyed his commands, or to curse them if they disobeyed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:12; Nehemiah 8:2; JEREMIAH 26:2"> All the Israelis gathered together to listen—the men, the women, and the children. The ◄foreigners/people who were not Israelis► who were living among them also listened, while Joshua read all the commands that Moses had written.</span>
</div>
