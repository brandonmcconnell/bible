## Land for the tribes of Ephraim and Manasseh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:12; JOSHUA 8:15; 2 KINGS 2:19–21"> The land that was allotted to the two tribes of Ephraim and Manasseh that were descended from Joseph started at the Jordan River, east of the springs at Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:13; GENESIS 28:19; 2 SAMUEL 16:16"> It extended west from Jericho to the hilly area near Bethel, which is also called Luz. It extended as far as Ataroth, at the border of the land where the Arki people-group lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:33; 2 CHRONICLES 8:5; JOSHUA 18:13"> From there it extended west to the border of the land where the Japhleti people-group lived, and then it extended west to the area near Lower Beth-Horon. From there it extended west to Gezer city and from there to the Mediterranean Sea.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:14"> That is the land that the tribes of Ephraim and Manasseh were allotted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:13; JOSHUA 16:2"> The border of the land that was allotted to the clans of the tribe of Ephraim started at Ataroth-Addar city in the east. It extended to Upper Beth-Horon</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:7; JOSHUA 18:1"> and from there to the Mediterranean Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 7:28; JOSHUA 6:26; JOSHUA 3:16"> From Michmethath on the north it extended east to Taanath and from there on to Janoah. From there it extended south to Ataroth city and to Naarah town. From there it extended to Jericho and from there to the Jordan River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:8–9; JOSHUA 16:3–6; JOSHUA 12:17"> The northern border extended from Tappuah west to Kanah Ravine, and ended at the Mediterranean Sea. That was the land that was allotted to the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:9"> Many of those towns were really within the area allotted to the tribe of Manasseh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 1:29; DEUTERONOMY 7:1–2; 1 KINGS 9:16"> The people of the tribe of Ephraim could not force the Canaan people-group to leave Gezer, so the Canaan people-group still live among the people of the tribe of Ephraim, but the Israelis forced the people of the Canaan people-group to become their slaves.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
