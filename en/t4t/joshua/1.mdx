## What Yahweh commanded Joshua

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 34:9; DEUTERONOMY 1:38; DEUTERONOMY 31:23"> After Moses, who served Yahweh well, died, Yahweh said to Nun’s son Joshua, who had been Moses’ assistant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:28; DEUTERONOMY 31:7; NUMBERS 27:16–21"> “You know that my servant Moses is now dead. So now get ready to lead all these Israeli people across the Jordan River. Enter the land that I will soon give to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:24; JOSHUA 14:9; TITUS 1:2"> I will give to you all the land that you walk on, as I promised Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 23:31; GENESIS 15:18–21; DEUTERONOMY 3:25"> That land will extend from the desert in the south, to the Lebanon Mountains in the northwest, and to the big Euphrates River in the northeast, and to the Mediterranean Sea on the west. It will include all the land where the descendants of Heth live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ROMANS 8:31; DEUTERONOMY 31:6–8; MATTHEW 28:20"> No group will be able to oppose and defeat you during your life. I will help you as I helped Moses. I will always be with &#91;LIT&#93; you; I will never forsake you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DANIEL 10:19; EPHESIANS 6:10; JOSHUA 1:9"> “Be strong and courageous, because you are the one who will lead these Israeli people and enable them to possess the land that I solemnly promised to their ancestors that I would give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 22:13; DEUTERONOMY 5:32; JOSHUA 1:8"> Just be strong and very courageous. Be sure to obey all the laws that my servant Moses gave to you. In order that you may be successful wherever you go, do not stop obeying any of them &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 119:15; PSALMS 19:14; COLOSSIANS 3:16"> Make sure to habitually read to the people some of the laws that God gave Moses. Meditate on those laws every day and every night. And be sure to obey all those laws. If you do that, you will be successful in everything that you do &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 28:15; JEREMIAH 1:7–8; JOSHUA 1:6–7"> Do not forget &#91;RHQ&#93; that I have commanded you to be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid, and do not be discouraged, because I, Yahweh, your God, will ◄be with/help► you wherever you go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then Joshua commanded the leaders of the people of Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:1; HOSEA 6:2; 2 KINGS 20:5"> “Go throughout the camp and tell the people this: ‘Prepare all the food that you will take with you, because the day after tomorrow you will cross this Jordan River, in order to possess the land that Yahweh, our God, is about to give you.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## What Joshua commanded the eastern tribes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:20–22"> But to the clans that were descendants of Reuben and Gad, and to the half clan that were descendants of Manasseh, Joshua said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:20–28; DEUTERONOMY 3:18–20; JOSHUA 22:1–4"> “Do not forget what Moses, the servant of Yahweh, commanded you: ‘Yahweh, your God, has given you this land in order that you can live here safely/peacefully.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:8; EXODUS 13:18; REVELATION 17:4"> Your wives and your children and your livestock may stay here in this land on the east side of the Jordan River, but all your soldiers must cross the river, ahead of the people of the other tribes. They must take all their weapons with them. You must continue to help your fellow Israelis</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:4; NUMBERS 32:17–22; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:26"> until they are able to live there safely/peacefully, as Yahweh has enabled you to do. You must help them until after they have conquered all the people in the land that Yahweh, your God, is giving them. After that, your soldiers may return to live in this land that Moses gave to you here on the east side of the Jordan River.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TITUS 3:1; DEUTERONOMY 5:27; 1 PETER 2:13–15"> The people answered Joshua, “We will do everything that you have commanded us to do, and we will go wherever you tell us to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:5; 1 KINGS 1:37; JOSHUA 1:9"> As we obeyed all that Moses told us to do, we will obey all that you tell us to do. We pray that Yahweh will ◄be with/help► you as he ◄was with/helped► Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:10; JOSHUA 1:9; Ezra 10:4"> Anyone who ◄rejects what you say/opposes you► and does not obey what you command &#91;DOU&#93;, we will execute. But we want you to be strong and courageous!”</span>
</div>
</div>
