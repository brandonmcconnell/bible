## Towns for the Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 14:1; JOSHUA 19:51; JOSHUA 17:4"> While Eleazar the Supreme Priest and Joshua and the leaders of all the Israeli tribes were at Shiloh, the leaders of the clans of the tribe of Levi went to them and said, “Yahweh commanded Moses that you should give us towns where we can have pasture/fields/grassland for our animals, but you have not done these things yet.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 49:7; DEUTERONOMY 33:8–10; 1 CHRONICLES 6:54–81"> So the Israeli leaders obeyed this command from Yahweh. They agreed to give towns and pasturelands to the tribe of Levi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:54–60; JOSHUA 24:33; JOSHUA 21:8–19"> First they allotted some cities to the Kohath clans, who belonged to the tribe of Levi. To those who were descendants of the first Supreme Priest Aaron, they allotted 13 towns in the areas that had been allotted to the tribes of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:20–26; EXODUS 6:16–25; 1 CHRONICLES 6:66–70"> To the other members of the Kohath clan they allotted ten cities in the areas that had been allotted to the tribes of Ephraim, Dan, and the part of the tribe of Manasseh that lives on the west side of the Jordan River.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:27–33; 1 CHRONICLES 6:71–76; EXODUS 6:16–17"> The people from the Gershon clans were allotted 13 cities in the areas that had been allotted to the tribes of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and the part of the tribe of Manasseh that lives on the east side of the Jordan River.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:34–40; 1 CHRONICLES 6:77–81; EXODUS 6:19"> The people from the Merari clans were allotted twelve cities in the areas that had been allotted to the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:3; JOSHUA 18:6; NUMBERS 33:54"> So the Israeli leaders gave cities and pastureland to the tribe of Levi, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses that they should do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:65; JOSHUA 21:13–18"> These are the names of the cities and surrounding pasturelands that were allotted to them, that were in the areas where the tribes of Judah and Simeon lived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:4; NUMBERS 3:27; EXODUS 6:20–26"> First, they allotted cities to the members of the Kohath clans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:55; LUKE 1:39; 2 SAMUEL 5:1–5"> They allotted to them Kiriath-Arba (which is now named Hebron), in the hilly area of Judah. Arba received its name because Arba was the ancestor of the giants of the Anak people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 14:13–15; 1 CHRONICLES 6:55–57"> But the fields and villages surrounding Arba had already been allotted to Caleb.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:42; JOSHUA 10:29; JOSHUA 15:54"> They allotted to them Hebron, which was one of the cities to which people could run/escape to be safe/protected. They also allotted to the Kohath clan the cities of Libnah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:48; JOSHUA 15:50; 1 SAMUEL 30:27–28"> Jattir, Eshtemoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:49; JOSHUA 15:51; JOSHUA 12:13"> Holon, Debir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:10; 1 CHRONICLES 6:59; JOSHUA 15:55"> Ain, Juttah, and Beth-Shemesh. Altogether they were allotted those nine cities which were located in the areas that had been allotted to the tribes of Judah and Simeon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:24–25; JOSHUA 9:3; 1 CHRONICLES 6:60"> The Kohath clan was also allotted these four cities: Gibeon, Geba,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 10:30; 1 CHRONICLES 6:60; JEREMIAH 1:1"> Anathoth, and Almon. Those cities were in the area that had been allotted to the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Altogether those 13 towns and surrounding pastureland were allotted to the priests who were descended from Aaron.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:66; JOSHUA 21:5"> The other members the Kohath clan were allotted four cities and the surrounding pastureland in the area that had been allotted to the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOSHUA 20:7; JUDGES 9:1; 1 KINGS 12:1"> Those cities were Shechem (which was one of the cities to which people could run/escape to be safe/protected), Gezer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:68; JOSHUA 16:3; JOSHUA 18:13–14"> Kibzaim, and Beth-Horon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:44–45"> They were also allotted four cities and surrounding pastureland in the area that had been allotted to the tribe of Dan. Those cities were Eltekeh, Gibbethon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:42; JOSHUA 10:12; 1 CHRONICLES 6:69"> Aijalon, and Gath-Rimmon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:11; JUDGES 5:19"> They were also allotted two towns and surrounding pastureland that had been allotted to the part of the tribe of Manasseh that lived on the west side of the Jordan River. Those cities were Taanach and Gath-Rimmon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Those were the ten cities and surrounding pastureland that were allotted to the other members of the Kohath clan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:71; DEUTERONOMY 1:4; JOSHUA 20:8"> They also allotted towns and the surrounding pastureland to the Gershon clans. Those clans were also descended from Levi.<br />They were allotted two cities from the area that had been allotted to the tribe of Manasseh that is on the east side of the Jordan River. Those cities were Golan in the Bashan region, which was one of the cities to which people could run/escape to be safe/protected, and Be-Eshtarah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:12; 1 CHRONICLES 6:72–73"> From the area that had been allotted to the tribe of Issachar they were allotted four cities. Those cities were Kishion, Daberath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:23; JOSHUA 12:11; JOSHUA 10:3"> Jarmuth, and En-Gannim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:74–75; JOSHUA 19:25–28"> From the area that had been allotted to the tribe of Asher they were allotted four cities. Those cities were Mishal, Abdon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JUDGES 18:21; 1 CHRONICLES 6:75; JUDGES 1:31"> Helkath, and Rehob.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:76; JOSHUA 20:7; JOSHUA 19:37"> From the area that had been allotted to the tribe of Naphtali they were allotted three cities. Those cities were Kedesh in the Galilee region (it was one of the cities to which people could run/escape to be safe/protected), Hammoth-Dor, and Kartan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> So altogether the Gershon clans were allotted 13 cities and the surrounding pasturelands.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:77; JOSHUA 19:11; JOSHUA 21:7"> The other descendants of Levi, the ones who belonged to the Merari clans, were also allotted towns and their surrounding pasturelands.<br />From the area that had been allotted to the tribe of Zebulun they were allotted four cities. They were Jokneam, Kartah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Dimnah, and Nahalal.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JOSHUA 20:8; DEUTERONOMY 4:43; NUMBERS 21:23"> From the area that had been allotted to the tribe of Reuben they were allotted four cities. They were Bezer, Jahaz,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Kedemoth, and Mephaath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="GENESIS 32:2; 1 KINGS 22:3; 1 CHRONICLES 6:80"> From the area that had been allotted to the tribe of Gad they were allotted four cities. They were Ramoth, which was one of the cities in the Gilead region to which people could run/escape to be safe/protected, Mahanaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 16:8–9; NUMBERS 32:35; NUMBERS 32:1"> Heshbon, and Jazer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Altogether twelve cities were allotted to the Merari clans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:1–8; DEUTERONOMY 33:10; GENESIS 49:7"> So altogether 48 cities were allotted to the tribe of Levi,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> and each city had surrounding pastureland.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GENESIS 13:15; PSALMS 106:42–45; EXODUS 23:27–31"> In that way Yahweh allotted to the Israeli people all the land that he had promised to give to their/our ancestors. The Israeli people took control of most of those areas and started to live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:23; HEBREWS 4:9; DEUTERONOMY 31:3–5"> Just like he had promised our ancestors, Yahweh allowed them/us to have peace with the enemies that surrounded them/us. Yahweh enabled the Israelis/us to defeat their/our enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:56; JOSHUA 23:14–15; NUMBERS 23:19"> Yahweh did all the things that he had promised to the Israeli people/us. Everything that he said would happen did happen.</span>
</div>
</div>
