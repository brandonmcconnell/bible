<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:55–56; JOSHUA 14:2; NUMBERS 33:36–37"> The land that was allotted to the tribe of Judah was divided among its clans. That land extended south along the border of the Edom region, as far as the Zin Desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 14:3; EZEKIEL 47:8; Isaiah 11:15"> The southern border of the land that was allotted to the tribe of Judah started at the south end of the Dead Sea and extended west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 34:4; JUDGES 1:36; NUMBERS 20:1"> It extended south of Scorpion Pass to Zin Desert, and from there west to a place south of Kadesh-Barnea, past Hezron town, to Addar town, and from there it turned northwest to Karka town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 34:5; JOSHUA 13:3; EXODUS 23:31"> From there it continued to Azmon, and from there to the dry riverbed on the border of Egypt, and from there west to the Mediterranean Sea. That was the southern border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 34:12; JOSHUA 18:15–19; NUMBERS 34:10"> The eastern border of the land of the tribe of Judah was the Dead Sea, and it extended north to where the Jordan River ends at the Dead Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:17; JOSHUA 18:19–21"> The northern border started where the Jordan River ends at the Dead Sea. It extended north to Beth-Hoglah town, and from there it extended north of Beth-Arabah town to the big stone set up by Reuben’s son Bohan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 1:9; 2 SAMUEL 17:17; JOSHUA 7:26"> From there the border continued west through Achor Valley to Debir city. From there it turned north to Gilgal city. Gilgal is north of the road that goes through Adummim Pass, on the south side of the valley. From Gilgal the border extended west to the springs at En-Shemesh, and from there to En-Rogel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:16; JOSHUA 15:63; JOSHUA 18:28"> From there it extended through Ben-Hinnom Valley, south of the city where the Jebus people-group lived. (That city is now named Jerusalem.) From there the border extended to the top of the hill on the west side of Hinnom Valley, at the northern end of the valley where the Repha giants lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 13:6; JOSHUA 18:15; JUDGES 18:12"> From there the border extended northwest to Nephtoah Spring, and from there to the cities near Ephron Mountain. From there the border extended west toward Baalah, which is now named Kiriath-Jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 14:1; JUDGES 14:5; GENESIS 38:12–13"> Then the border extended further west to Seir Mountain. Then it continued southwest along the north side of Jearim Mountain, which is also named Kesalon, to Beth-Shemesh city. From there it extended northwest past Timnah city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:45; 2 KINGS 1:2–3; 1 SAMUEL 5:10"> to the hill north of Ekron city. From there it extended west to Shikkeron town and past Baalah Mountain to Jabneel town, then northwest to the Mediterranean Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 34:6–7; JOSHUA 15:47; EZEKIEL 47:20"> The western border of the land that was allotted to the tribe of Judah was the Mediterranean Sea. All the clans of Judah lived inside those borders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:30; DEUTERONOMY 1:34–36; NUMBERS 14:23–24"> Yahweh commanded Joshua to give part of the land for the tribe of Judah to Caleb. So he gave to Caleb Kiriath-Arba city, which is now named Hebron. (Arba was the ancestor of the Anak people-group.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 1:20; JUDGES 1:10; NUMBERS 13:22–23"> Caleb forced the three clans of the Anak people-group to leave Hebron. Those were the Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai clans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:38; JOSHUA 10:3; JUDGES 1:11–13"> Then Caleb left there and went to fight against the people living in Debir city, which was previously named Kiriath-Sepher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 1:12–13; JUDGES 1:6"> Caleb said, “If someone attacks the people in Kiriath-Sepher and captures their city, I will give my daughter Acsah to him to be his wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JUDGES 3:9; JUDGES 1:13; JOSHUA 14:6"> Othniel, the son of Caleb’s brother Kenaz, captured the city. So Caleb gave his daughter to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 24:64; 1 SAMUEL 25:23"> When Caleb’s daughter married Othniel, she told him to ask her father to give her a field. Then Acsah went to talk with her father Caleb. As she got down from her donkey, Caleb asked her, “Do you want something?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JUDGES 1:14–15; 1 SAMUEL 25:27; DEUTERONOMY 33:7"> Acsah replied, “Yes, I want you to do something for me. You have given me some land in the southern part of Canaan, but there is no water there. So please give me some land that has springs.” So Caleb gave her the upper and lower springs near Hebron.</span>

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Kinah, Dimonah, Adadah,</span>

<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 26:33; 1 CHRONICLES 4:28; GENESIS 21:31–33"> Hazar-Shual, Beersheba, Biziothiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:9–11; JOSHUA 19:3; 1 CHRONICLES 4:29"> Baalah, Iim, Ezem,</span>

<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:29; 2 KINGS 8:22; JOSHUA 19:7"> The tribe of Judah was also allotted these towns in the central part of the western foothills: Libnah, Ether, Ashan,</span>

<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:13; GENESIS 23:2; JOSHUA 14:15"> Humtah, Kiriath-Arba (which is now named Hebron), and Zior. There were nine towns altogether with their surrounding villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:2; JOSHUA 15:24; 1 SAMUEL 23:25"> The tribe of Judah was also allotted these towns in the southeastern part of the hilly region: Maon, Carmel, Ziph, Juttah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Jezreel, Jokdeam, Zanoah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:10; GENESIS 38:12; JEREMIAH 14:1"> Kain, Gibeah, and Timnah. There were ten towns altogether with their surrounding villages.</span>

<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:14; 1 SAMUEL 7:1"> The tribe of Judah was also allotted two towns in the northern part of the hilly region, Rabbah and Kiriath-Baal (which is also named Kiriath-Jearim).</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:6; JOSHUA 18:18"> The tribe of Judah was also allotted these towns in the desert near the Dead Sea: Beth-Arabah, Middin, Secacah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:29; 2 CHRONICLES 20:2"> Nibshan, Salt city, and En-Gedi. There were six towns altogether with their surrounding villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="JUDGES 1:21; JUDGES 1:8; 2 SAMUEL 5:6–9"> The army of the tribe of Judah was not able to force the people of the Jebus people-group to leave Jerusalem. So the people of that group are still living among the tribe of Judah.</span>
