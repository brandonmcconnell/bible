## The areas that the Israelis had not yet captured

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOSHUA 14:10; DEUTERONOMY 31:3; JOSHUA 24:29"> When Joshua was very old, Yahweh said to him, “Joshua, you are now an old man, but there is still a lot of land for your army to capture.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:11; 1 SAMUEL 27:8; JOEL 3:4"> Here is a list of the land that remains: The Geshur region and all the area where the Philistia people-group live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:23; JUDGES 3:3; JEREMIAH 2:18"> the area where the Canaan people-group live, from the Shihor River in the south at the eastern border of Egypt, to Ekron city in the north; the rulers of the five cities of Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron; the area where the Avva people-group live,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:30; 1 SAMUEL 4:1; JUDGES 1:34–36"> which is south of the area where the Canaan people-group live;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 5:18; JOSHUA 12:7; PSALMS 83:7"> the area where the Gebal people-group live; and all the Lebanon area east of Baal-Gad city at the bottom of Hermon Mountain, as far as Lebo-Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:8; JOSHUA 23:13; JUDGES 2:21–23"> All the people of the Sidon people-group live in the hilly area from Lebanon to Misrephoth-Maim, but I will force them to leave that area before you Israelis arrive there. Be sure to give that area to the Israeli people when you divide the land among them, like I told you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:53–56; NUMBERS 33:54; EZEKIEL 48:23–29"> Divide all that land among the nine Israeli tribes and half of the tribe of Manasseh, the tribes that do not have land on the east side of the Jordan River.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The land east of the Jordan River

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 12:6; DEUTERONOMY 3:12–17; JOSHUA 22:4"> Half of the tribe of Manasseh and the tribes of Reuben and Gad had already been told what land they would receive, because Moses, the man who served Yahweh well, had already assigned to them the land on the east side of the Jordan River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:16; NUMBERS 21:30; JOSHUA 12:2"> Their land extended from Aroer town at the Arnon River gorge to the town in the middle of the gorge. It included the whole plain from Medeba town south to Dibon town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:24–26"> All the towns ruled by King Sihon were in that area. The land extended to where the Ammon people-group lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:23; JOSHUA 12:2–5; DEUTERONOMY 4:47–48"> The Gilead area was also there, and the regions where the people of the Geshur and Maacah people-groups lived, and all of Hermon Mountain and all the Bashan region south to Salecah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 12:4; JOSHUA 14:3–4; NUMBERS 21:23–35"> All the land ruled by Og, the king who ruled the Bashan region, was in that land. Og was one of the last descendants of Repha; previously he had ruled in Ashtaroth and Edrei cities, but the armies of Moses had defeated their armies and had taken their land from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:12–13; JOSHUA 13:11; NUMBERS 33:55"> But the Israelis did not force the people of the Geshur and Maacah people-groups to leave their land, so they still live among the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:20–24; JOSHUA 14:3–4; DEUTERONOMY 18:1–2"> The tribe of Levi was the only tribe that did not get any land. Instead, just like Yahweh promised, they continued to receive food from the sacrifices that were given to Yahweh, the God whom the Israelis worship, the sacrifices that were burned on the sacred altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Moses had allotted land to each clan in the tribe of Reuben.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:9; JOSHUA 12:2; Isaiah 16:7–9"> Their land extended from Aroer town near the Arnon River gorge to Medeba town. That included all of the plain that is near Medeba and the town in the middle of the gorge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:41; NUMBERS 32:38; NUMBERS 21:19"> It also included Heshbon city and all the nearby towns on the plain—Dibon, Bamioth-Baal, Beth-Baal-Meon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:23; JOSHUA 21:36–37; 1 CHRONICLES 6:78–79"> Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:37–38"> Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-Shahar on the hill overlooking the valley,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOSHUA 12:3; DEUTERONOMY 4:46; EZEKIEL 25:9"> Beth-Peor, the hills in the Pisgah area, and Beth-Jeshimoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:8; DEUTERONOMY 3:10; DEUTERONOMY 2:30–36"> That land included all the towns on the plain and all the area that King Sihon had previously ruled. But the army of Moses had defeated him and the rulers of the Midian people-group: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba. The armies of all those rulers fought battles along with the army of Sihon, and they lived in that country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:8; JUDE 1:11; 2 PETER 2:15"> The Israeli army killed many people during those battles, including Balaam, the prophet from Beor, who tried to use magic to predict/tell what would happen in the future.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The western boundary of the land that was allotted to the clans of the tribe of Reuben was the Jordan River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:34–36"> Moses also allotted some land to each clan in the tribe of Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:26; 2 SAMUEL 11:1; NUMBERS 32:35"> Their land included the Jazer area and all the towns of the Gilead area; half of the land where the Ammon people-group lived, as far as Aroer town which is near Rabbah town;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 17:27; 2 SAMUEL 2:8; JUDGES 10:17"> the area from Heshbon city to Ramath-Mizpah and Betonim towns; the area from Mahanaim town to the Debir region;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:36; NUMBERS 34:11; GENESIS 33:17"> Beth-Haram, Beth-Nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon cities in the valley; and the other area that King Sihon had ruled on the east side of the Jordan River. That area extended north to the southern end of Galilee Lake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> All this land, including the towns and villages, was allotted to the tribe of Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Moses also had allotted some of the land on the east side of the Jordan River to half of the tribe of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:26; DEUTERONOMY 3:13–15; NUMBERS 32:39–41"> Their land extended north from Mahanaim city. It included all the Bashan region, all the land that King Og had ruled, and all the towns in the Jair area in Bashan. Altogether that area included 60 towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOSHUA 12:4; NUMBERS 32:39–40"> Their area also included half of the Gilead region, and the cities of Ashtaroth and Edrei where King Og had ruled. All that area was allotted to the clans descended from Manasseh’s son Makir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Moses had allotted to those tribes the land that was on the plain of the Moab region, on the east side of the Jordan River, across from Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:7; JOSHUA 13:14; DEUTERONOMY 18:1–2"> But Moses did not allot any land to the tribe of Levi because Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis worship, promised that he himself would always provide for them.</span>
</div>
</div>
