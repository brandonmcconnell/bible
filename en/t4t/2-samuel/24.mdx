## David ordered a census to be taken

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 21:1; 1 CHRONICLES 27:23–24; JAMES 1:13–14"> Yahweh was angry with the Israeli people again, so he incited David to cause trouble for them. He said to David, “Send some men to count the people of Israel and Judah.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:10; JUDGES 20:1; 2 SAMUEL 20:23"> So the king said to Joab, the commander of his army, “Go with your officers through all the tribes of Israel, from Dan city in the far north to Beersheba town in the far south, and count the people, in order that I may know how many people there are who are able to be soldiers in the army.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:11; PSALMS 115:14; PROVERBS 14:28"> But Joab replied to the king, “Your Majesty, I wish/desire that Yahweh our God will cause there to be 100 times as many people in Israel as there are now, and I wish/desire that you would see that happen before you die. But why do you want us to do this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ACTS 5:29; ECCLESIASTES 8:4; 1 CHRONICLES 21:4"> But the king commanded Joab and his officers to do it. So they left the king and went out to count the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:9; DEUTERONOMY 2:36; JOSHUA 13:16"> They crossed the Jordan River and set up their tents south of Aroer town, in the middle of the valley, in the territory that was given to the tribe of Gad. From there they went north to Jazer city</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:28; GENESIS 31:47–48; JUDGES 18:28–29"> Then they went north to the Gilead region and to Kadesh city, in the land where the Heth people-group lived. Then they went to Dan city in the far north of Israel, and then further west, to Sidon city near the Mediterranean Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:29; JOSHUA 11:3; GENESIS 21:22–33"> Then they went south to Tyre, a city with high walls around it, and to all the cities where the Hiv and Canaan people-groups live. Then they went east to Beersheba, in the southern part of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> After nine months and 20 days, when they had finished going throughout the land and counting the people, they returned to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 27:23–24; 1 CHRONICLES 21:5–6; NUMBERS 1:44–46"> They reported to the king the number of people that they had counted. There were 800,000 men in Israel and 500,000 men in Judah who were able to become soldiers in the army &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:13; 1 SAMUEL 13:13; PROVERBS 28:13"> But after David’s men had counted the people, David regretted &#91;IDM&#93; that he had told them to do that. One night he said to Yahweh, “I have committed a very big sin. Please forgive me, because what I have done is very foolish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:5; 1 SAMUEL 9:9; 1 CHRONICLES 29:29"> When David got up the next morning, Yahweh gave a message to the prophet Gad. He said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:6–10; REVELATION 3:19; PROVERBS 3:12"> “Go and tell this to David: ‘I am allowing you to choose one of three things to punish you. I will do whichever one you choose.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 21:12; LEVITICUS 26:25; 2 SAMUEL 21:1"> So Gad went to David and told him what Yahweh had said. He said to David, “You can choose whether there will be three years of famine in your land, or three months of your army running away from your enemies, or three days when there will be a ◄plague/very severe illness► in your land. You think about it and choose which one you want, and tell me, and I will return to Yahweh and tell him what your answer is.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 51:1; PSALMS 119:156; 1 CHRONICLES 21:13"> David said to Gad, “All those are very terrible things for me to choose between! But allow Yahweh to punish &#91;MTY&#93; me, because he is very merciful. Do not allow humans to punish me, because they will not be merciful.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 21:14; 1 CHRONICLES 27:24; NUMBERS 25:9"> So Yahweh sent a plague on the Israeli people. It started that morning and did not stop until the time that he had chosen/set. All over the land, from Dan to Beersheba, there were 70,000 Israelis who died because of the plague.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOEL 2:13–14; GENESIS 6:6; EXODUS 12:23"> When Yahweh’s angel stretched out his hand toward Jerusalem to destroy the people by this plague, Yahweh changed his mind about punishing any more people. He said to the angel who was killing them with the plague, “Stop what you are doing &#91;IDM&#93;! That is enough!” When he said that, the angel was standing at the ground where Araunah, from the Jebus people-group, threshed grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 74:1; 1 CHRONICLES 21:16–17; 2 SAMUEL 24:10"> When David saw the angel who was causing the people to become sick and die, he said to Yahweh, “Truly, I am the one who has committed the sin. I have done a very wicked thing, but these people are as innocent as sheep &#91;MET&#93;. They have certainly not &#91;RHQ&#93; done anything that is wrong. So you should punish &#91;IDM&#93; me and my family, not these people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 21:18–30; 2 SAMUEL 24:11"> That day Gad came to David and said to him, “Go up to the place where Araunah threshes grain, and build an altar to worship Yahweh there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:8; Nehemiah 9:26; 2 CHRONICLES 36:16"> So David did what Gad told him to do, which was what Yahweh had commanded, and he went up there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 18:2; RUTH 2:10; 1 CHRONICLES 21:20–21"> When Araunah looked down and saw the king and his officials coming toward him, he prostrated himself on the ground in front of the king, with his face touching the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 21:22; NUMBERS 25:8; 2 SAMUEL 21:3–14"> Araunah said, “Your Majesty, why have you come to me?” David replied, “I have come to buy this ground where you thresh grain, in order to build an altar to Yahweh and offer sacrifices on it, in order that he will stop the plague.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:21; 1 SAMUEL 6:14; 1 CHRONICLES 21:22"> Araunah replied to David, “Your Majesty, offer to Yahweh whatever you wish/want. Here, take my oxen to use for the offering that will be completely burned on the altar. And here, take their yokes and the boards that I use for the threshing, and use them for the wood that you will burn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:40–41; Isaiah 32:8; PSALMS 20:3–4"> I, Araunah, am giving all this to you, the king.” Then he said, “I desire/hope that Yahweh our God will accept your offering.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 21:24–25; MALACHI 1:12–14; GENESIS 23:13"> But the king said to Araunah, “No, I will not take these things as a gift. I will pay you for it. I will not take sacrifices that have cost me nothing, and offer them to Yahweh to be completely burned on the altar.” So he paid 50 pieces of silver to Araunah for the oxen and the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 21:14; LAMENTATIONS 3:32–33; GENESIS 8:20"> Then David built an altar to Yahweh, and he offered the oxen to be completely burned on the altar, and he also offered sacrifices to maintain fellowship with Yahweh. Then, Yahweh answered David’s prayers, and he caused the plague in Israel to end.</span>
