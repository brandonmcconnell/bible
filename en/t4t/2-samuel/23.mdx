## David’s three greatest warriors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 27:2; 1 CHRONICLES 11:11–47; 1 CHRONICLES 27:32"> These are the names of David’s three greatest warriors.<br />The first was Esh-Baal, whose other name was Jashobeam, from the Hachmon clan (OR, the son of Hachmon). He was the leader of the three men. One time he fought against 800 enemies and killed them all with his spear.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 27:4; 1 CHRONICLES 11:12–14; 1 SAMUEL 17:26"> The second/next one of the three greatest warriors was Eleazar, who was the son of Dodo from the clan of Ahoh. One day he was with David when they defied/challenged the soldiers of Philistia who had gathered for the battle. The other Israeli soldiers retreated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 19:5; 1 SAMUEL 11:13; JUDGES 15:14"> but Eleazar stood there and fought the soldiers of Philistia until his arm became very tired, with the result that his hand cramped and he could not stop gripping his sword. Yahweh won a great victory on that day. And afterwards the other Israeli soldiers returned to where Eleazar was, and stripped off the armor from the men whom he had killed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 11:13–14; 1 CHRONICLES 11:27; 2 SAMUEL 23:33"> The third one of the greatest warriors was Shammah, the son of Agee from the clan/town of Harar. One time the Philistia soldiers gathered at Lehi town, where there was a field full of lentils/peas that they wanted to steal. The other Israeli soldiers ran away from the Philistia troops,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 21:31; 2 SAMUEL 23:10; PSALMS 3:8"> but Shammah stood there in the field and did not let the Philistia soldiers steal the crops, and killed them. Yahweh won a great victory on that day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The 30 great warriors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:1; 2 SAMUEL 5:18; JOSHUA 12:15"> Altogether there were 30 special warriors among David’s soldiers. Once, when it was almost time to harvest the crops, three of those 30 men went down to Adullam Cave, where David was staying. A group of men from the Philistia army had set up their tents in Rephaim Valley near Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:4–5; 1 SAMUEL 13:4; 1 SAMUEL 10:5"> David and his soldiers were in the cave because it was safe there, and another group of Philistia soldiers was occupying Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 4:14; PSALMS 42:1–2; Isaiah 41:17"> One day David very much wanted some water to drink, and said “I wish that someone would bring me some water from the well near the gate at Bethlehem!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 35:14; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:14; PHILIPPIANS 2:17"> So his three greatest warriors forced their way through the camp of Philistia soldiers and drew some water from the well, and brought it to David. But he would not drink it. Instead, he poured it out on the ground as an offering to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LEVITICUS 17:10; MARK 14:24; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:30"> He said, “Yahweh, it would certainly not be right for me to drink this water! That would be like &#91;RHQ&#93; drinking the blood of these men who were willing/ready to die for me!” So he refused to drink it.<br />That was one of the things that those three great warriors did.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The list of the great warriors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 10:10; 2 SAMUEL 10:14; 1 CHRONICLES 11:20–21"> Abishai, Joab’s younger brother, was the leader of the 30 greatest warriors. One day he fought against 300 men and killed them all with his spear. As a result, he also became famous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:8; MATTHEW 13:23; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:41"> He was the most famous of the thirty greatest warriors, and he became their leader/commander, but he was not one of the three greatest warriors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:18; JOSHUA 15:21; 2 SAMUEL 20:23"> Jehoiada’s son Benaiah, from Kabzeel town, also did great deeds. <span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:51; 1 CHRONICLES 11:23; COLOSSIANS 2:15"> He also killed a huge soldier from Egypt who carried a spear. <span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Those are some of the things that Benaiah did. As a result, he became famous, like the three greatest warriors were.</span>

</div>
