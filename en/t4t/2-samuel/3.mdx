## David’s sons born at Hebron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 3:1–4; GENESIS 49:3–4; 2 SAMUEL 13:1–29"> David’s wives gave birth to six sons at Hebron. The oldest was Amnon, whose mother was Ahinoam, from Jezreel city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 27:8; 1 CHRONICLES 3:1; DEUTERONOMY 3:14"> The next one was Kileab, whose mother was Abigail, the widow of Nabal, from Carmel city. The next one was Absalom, whose mother was Maacah, the daughter of Talmai, the king of the Geshur region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:13–25; 1 KINGS 1:5–18"> The next one was Adonijah, whose mother was Haggith. The next one was Shephatiah, whose mother was Abital.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The youngest one was Ithream, whose mother was Eglah, another one of David’s wives. Those sons of David were all born in Hebron.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abner decided that David would be king of all of them

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 2:8–9; 2 CHRONICLES 25:8; Isaiah 8:9–10"> While those who wanted Saul’s son to rule over them and those who wanted David to rule over them continued to fight against each other, Abner was becoming more influential among those who wanted Saul’s son to be the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 21:8–11; 1 KINGS 2:21–22; 2 SAMUEL 12:8"> When Saul was alive, he had as one of his wives a slave woman named Rizpah, the daughter of Aiah. But one day Abner had sexual relations with her. So Ishbosheth said to Abner, “Why have you had sexual relations with &#91;EUP&#93; my father’s slave wife?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 9:8; 2 SAMUEL 16:9; MARK 6:18–19"> Abner became very angry about what Ishbosheth said to him. He said to Ishbosheth, “◄Do you think that I am a worthless dog &#91;MET&#93; from Judah?/You are treating me as though I am a worthless dog &#91;MET&#93; from Judah.► &#91;RHQ&#93; From the beginning, I have been loyal to Saul your father, to his brothers, and to his friends. And I have prevented you from being defeated by David’s army. So now ◄why are you criticizing me about what I have done with some woman?/you should not be criticizing me about what I have done with some woman.► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:28; 1 KINGS 19:2; RUTH 1:17"> Yahweh solemnly promised that he would not allow Saul and his descendants to continue to rule. He promised that he would cause David to rule over all the tribes of Israel and Judah, from Dan city far in the north to Beersheba city far in the south. So I hope/desire that God will punish me severely &#91;HYP&#93; if I do not enable that to happen!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Ishbosheth was very afraid of Abner, so he did not say anything in reply to Abner.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 12:38–40; 2 SAMUEL 3:21; LUKE 16:5–8"> Then Abner sent messengers to David when he was at Hebron, to say to him, “Either you or I should be &#91;RHQ&#93; the ruler of this entire nation, but not Isbosheth. However, if you make an agreement with me, I will help you by encouraging all the people of Israel to ◄be want you to be their king/start to support► you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 43:3; 1 SAMUEL 19:11–17; GENESIS 44:23"> David sent back this reply: “Good! I am willing to make an agreement with you. But before that happens, there is one thing that you must do. When you come to see me, you must bring my wife Michal, Saul’s daughter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 18:27; 1 SAMUEL 18:25; 2 SAMUEL 2:10"> Then David sent messengers to Ishbosheth, to say to him, “I had to kill 100 men from Philistia and cut off their foreskins to give to Saul to pay for Michal to be my wife. So now give her back to me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:44"> So Ishbosheth sent some men to take Michal from her husband Palti. But when they took her, her husband followed them all the way to Bahurim town, crying as he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 16:5; 2 SAMUEL 19:16; 1 KINGS 2:8"> Then Abner turned and said to him, “Go back home!”, so he did.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Abner went to the Israeli leaders and talked with them. He said, “For a long time you have wanted David to be your king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:28; PSALMS 89:19–23; 1 SAMUEL 9:16"> So now you have an opportunity to do that. Keep in mind that Yahweh promised this: ‘With the help of David, who serves me well, I will rescue my people from the people of Philistia, and from the power &#91;MTY&#93; of all their other enemies’.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 12:29; 1 SAMUEL 10:20–21; PSALMS 68:27"> Abner also spoke to the people of the tribe of Benjamin. Then he went to Hebron, to tell David what all the people of Israel and the people of the tribe of Benjamin had agreed to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 31:54; GENESIS 26:30; Esther 1:3"> When Abner came with 20 of his soldiers to see David at Hebron, David made a feast for all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:12; 1 KINGS 11:37; 2 SAMUEL 3:10"> Afterwards, Abner said to David, “Sir/Your majesty, I will now go and encourage all the people of Israel to accept you to be their king, as you have desired.” Then Abner left, peacefully.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joab murdered Abner

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Soon after that, Joab and some of David’s other soldiers returned to Hebron after raiding one of their enemies’ villages, bringing with them a lot of things that they had captured. But Abner was not there at Hebron, because David had sent him safely home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> When Joab and the soldiers who were with him arrived, someone told him that Abner had come there and talked with the king, and that the king allowed to go home safely.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:8; 2 SAMUEL 19:5–7; JOHN 18:35"> So Joab went to the king and said, “Why have you done that? Listen to me! Abner is your enemy, but when he came to you, you allowed him &#91;RHQ&#93; to leave!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:6; Isaiah 37:28; NUMBERS 27:17"> Do you not know that he came to you to deceive you and to find out everything that you are doing, and all the places that you go to?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 26:23–26; PROVERBS 27:4–6"> After Joab left David, he sent some messengers to get Abner. They found him at Sirah Well, and brought him back to Hebron, but David did not know that they had done that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:5; 2 SAMUEL 20:9–10; 1 KINGS 2:32"> So when Abner returned to Hebron, Joab met him at the city gate, and took him aside as though he wanted to speak with him privately. Then he stabbed Abner in the stomach with his knife/sword. In that way he murdered Abner because Abner had killed &#91;MTY&#93; Joab’s brother Asahel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:33; EXODUS 21:12; GENESIS 9:6"> Later, after David heard what had happened, he said, “Yahweh knows that I and the people of my kingdom are not at all ◄responsible for/guilty of► murdering &#91;MTY&#93; Abner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 1:16; PSALMS 109:8–19; LEVITICUS 13:44–46"> I hope/desire that Joab and all his family will be punished &#91;MTY&#93; for doing that! I hope/desire that there will always be someone in his family who has sores, or someone who is a leper, or some man who is forced to do women’s work, or someone who is killed in a battle, or someone who does not have enough food to eat!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:17; ACTS 28:4; 2 SAMUEL 2:19–23"> That is how Joab and his brother Abishai murdered Abner, because he had killed their brother Asahel in the battle at Gibeon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 37:34; 2 SAMUEL 1:11; 2 SAMUEL 1:2"> Then David said to Joab and to all Joab’s soldiers, “Tear your clothes and put on coarse cloth to show that you are sad, and mourn for Abner!” And at the funeral, King David walked behind the men who were carrying the coffin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:17; JOB 31:28; 1 SAMUEL 30:4"> They buried Abner’s body at Hebron. And at the grave, the king cried loudly, and all the other people also cried.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 1:17; 2 CHRONICLES 35:25; LUKE 12:19–20"> David sang this sad song to lament for Abner:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“It is not right that &#91;RHQ&#93; Abner died like outlaws die!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JUDGES 16:21; 2 SAMUEL 1:12; JOB 24:14"> <span className="pl pl-2">No one tied his hands or put chains on his feet, like they do to criminals.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No, he was murdered by wicked men!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:17; 2 SAMUEL 1:12; RUTH 1:17"> Then many &#91;HYP&#93; people came to David to tell him to eat some food before sunset, but David refused. He said, “I hope/desire that God will punish me severely &#91;HYP&#93; if I eat any food before the sun goes down!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARK 7:37; PSALMS 62:9; MARK 15:11–13"> All the people saw what David did, and they were pleased. Truly, everything that the king did pleased the people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So all the people realized that the king had not wanted Abner to be killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JOB 32:9; 2 SAMUEL 2:8; 2 SAMUEL 3:12"> The king said to his officials, “◄Do you not realize that a leader and a great man has died today in Israel?/You should realize that a leader and a great man has died today in Israel.► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:33–34; PSALMS 101:8; PSALMS 28:4"> Even though Yahweh appointed me to be the king, today I feel weak. These two sons of Zeruiah, Joab and Abishai, are very violent; I cannot control them. So I hope/desire that Yahweh will punish them severely in return for this wicked thing that they have done!”</span>
</div>
