## Ziba gave food to David and the others

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:18; 2 SAMUEL 15:32; 2 SAMUEL 9:2–13"> When David and the others had gone a little way past the top of the hill, Mephibosheth’s servant Ziba met him. He had with him two donkeys that were carrying 200 loaves of bread, 100 bunches/clusters of raisins, 100 bunches of fresh fruit, and a leather bag full of wine.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 17:29; JUDGES 10:4; JUDGES 5:10"> The king said to Ziba, “What are these for?” Ziba replied, “The donkeys are for your family to ride on, the bread and the fruit are for your soldiers to eat, and the wine is for them to drink when they become exhausted in the desert.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 9:9–10; PSALMS 15:3; 2 SAMUEL 19:24–30"> The king said, “Where is Mephibosheth, the grandson of your former master Saul?” Ziba deceived David by saying, “He stayed in Jerusalem, because he thinks that now the Israeli people will allow him to rule the kingdom that his grandfather Saul ruled.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 18:13; PROVERBS 18:17; PROVERBS 19:2"> The king said to Ziba, “Okay, everything that belonged to Mephibosheth is now yours.” Ziba replied, “Your Majesty, I will humbly serve you; and I desire/hope that you will always be pleased with me.”</span>
</div>

## Shimei cursed David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:16; 1 SAMUEL 17:43; EXODUS 22:28"> When King David and those with him arrived at Bahurim town, a man named Shimei met him. Shimei, whose father was Gera, was one of Saul’s relatives. Shimei was cursing David as he approached.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then he threw stones at David and his officials, even though the officials and David’s bodyguards surrounded David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:13; 1 SAMUEL 25:17; 1 KINGS 21:10"> Shimei cursed David and said to him, “Get out of here, you murderer, you scoundrel!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 1:16; 2 SAMUEL 3:28–29; 1 KINGS 2:32–33"> Yahweh is ◄getting revenge on/punishing► you all for murdering &#91;MTY&#93; many people in Saul’s family. And now he is giving Saul’s kingdom to your son Absalom. You murderer, you are being paid back for the many people that you have killed/murdered &#91;MTY&#93;!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 22:28; 2 SAMUEL 9:8; 1 SAMUEL 24:14"> Then Abishai said to the king, “Your Majesty, this man is as worthless as &#91;MET&#93; a dead dog! ◄Why should he be allowed to curse you?/He should not be allowed to curse you.► &#91;RHQ&#93; Allow me to go over there and cut off his head!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 19:22; ROMANS 9:20; JOHN 18:11"> But the king replied, “You two sons of Zeruiah, ◄do not tell me what to do/this does not concern you► &#91;RHQ&#93;. If he is cursing me because Yahweh told him to do that, certainly no one should say ‘◄Why are you doing that?/You should not be doing that.► &#91;RHQ&#93;’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 17:1–4; GENESIS 45:5; 2 SAMUEL 12:11–12"> Then David said to Abishai and to all his officials, “You know that my own son is trying to kill me. So it is not surprising that this man from the tribe of Benjamin is also trying to kill me. So ignore him, and allow him to curse me, because perhaps he thinks that Yahweh told him to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 23:5; HEBREWS 12:10; PSALMS 25:18"> Perhaps Yahweh will see that I am having all this trouble, and some day he will repay me by ◄blessing/doing good things for► me in return for this man cursing me today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ACTS 23:23; 2 SAMUEL 16:5–6"> Then David and those who were with him walked along the road, and Shimei continued walking along the hillside near him. While he walked along, he cursed David and threw stones and dirt at him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When David and those who were with him arrived at the Jordan River, they were very tired. So they rested there.</span>

## Ahithophel gave advice to Absalom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 15:37; 2 SAMUEL 15:12"> While that was happening, Absalom and all the Israeli people who were with him had arrived in Jerusalem. Ahithophel had also arrived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 KINGS 11:12; 1 SAMUEL 10:24; 1 KINGS 1:25"> When David’s friend Hushai came to Absalom, he said to Absalom, “I desire/hope that the king will live a long time! May you live for many years!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 19:25; 2 SAMUEL 15:32–37; DEUTERONOMY 32:6"> Absalom said to Hushai, “You have been loyal &#91;RHQ&#93; to your friend David for a long time. So why did you not go with him instead of coming to me?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:13; 2 SAMUEL 5:1–3"> Hushai replied, “I will serve the one whom Yahweh and these people and all the other people of Israel have chosen to be their king. So I will stay with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 15:34; PSALMS 55:21; 1 SAMUEL 28:2"> You are my master King David’s son; if I do not serve you, what other man should I serve &#91;RHQ&#93;? I will serve you as I served your father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; PSALMS 37:12–13; Isaiah 29:15"> Then Absalom said to Ahithophel, “What do you advise that we should do?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 20:3; 2 SAMUEL 15:16; ZECHARIAH 8:13"> Ahithophel replied, “Your father left some of his slave wives in the palace to take care of it. Then all those who are with you will be very encouraged.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:11–12; 2 SAMUEL 15:16; 2 SAMUEL 20:3"> So they set up a tent for Absalom on the roof of the palace. And Absalom went into the tent and had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with his father’s slave wives, one by one, and everyone &#91;HYP&#93; could see him going into the tent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 15:12; 2 SAMUEL 17:14; 2 SAMUEL 17:23"> In those days, people accepted what Ahithophel recommended as though he were speaking the words of God. So just as David had always accepted what Ahithophel said, now Absalom did also.</span>
</div>
