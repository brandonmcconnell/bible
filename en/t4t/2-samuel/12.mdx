## Nathan rebuked David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:35–41; PSALMS 51:1–19; 2 SAMUEL 11:10–17"> Yahweh told the prophet Nathan what David had done, and he sent Nathan to tell this story to David: “I heard about two men who were living in a certain city. One was rich and the other was poor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 15:16; JOB 1:3; 2 SAMUEL 5:13–16"> The rich man owned a lot of cattle and sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MICAH 7:5; 2 SAMUEL 11:3; PROVERBS 5:18–19"> But the poor man had only one little female lamb, which he had bought. He raised the lamb, and it grew up with his children. He would give the lamb some of his own food and let it drink from his cup. He let the lamb sleep in his arms. The lamb was like a daughter to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JAMES 1:14; 2 SAMUEL 11:3–4; GENESIS 18:2–7"> One day a visitor came to visit the rich man. The rich man did not want to take one of his own animals and kill it to prepare a meal for the visitor. So instead, he stole the poor man’s lamb and killed it and prepared a meal for his visitor.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 38:24; ROMANS 2:1; 1 SAMUEL 26:16"> When David heard that, he was very angry about what the rich man had done. He said to Nathan, “I solemnly declare that the man who did that should be executed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 22:1; LUKE 19:8; PROVERBS 6:31"> But before he is executed, he should pay back to the poor man four lambs for doing this, and for not having pity on the poor man.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:14; 1 SAMUEL 16:13; 2 SAMUEL 22:1"> Nathan said to David, “You are the man I have been talking about! And this is what Yahweh, the God we Israelis worship, says to you: ‘I appointed you to be king of Israel, after having rescued you from Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 84:11; ROMANS 8:32; 2 SAMUEL 9:7"> I gave you his palace and his wives. I caused you to become the king to rule Israel and Judah. If you had told me that you were not content with what I gave you, I would have given you twice as much!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:19; PSALMS 139:1–2; 1 SAMUEL 15:23"> So why have you despised my commandment about not committing adultery? You have done what I consider to be very evil! You have arranged for Uriah to be killed in a battle by the Ammon people-group’s soldiers, and you have taken his wife to be your wife!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 18:14–15; MATTHEW 26:52; ROMANS 2:4"> You have despised me, and have taken Uriah’s wife to be your wife. So for many generations some of your descendants will die violently. &#91;MTY, PRS&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 16:21–22; DEUTERONOMY 28:30; 2 SAMUEL 15:10"> I solemnly declare to you that I will cause someone from your own family to bring trouble to you. I will take your wives and give them to him, and he will have sexual relations with them in the daytime, where everyone can see it, and you will know all about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 16:22; 2 SAMUEL 11:4–15; ECCLESIASTES 12:14"> What you did, you did secretly, but what I will cause to happen, everyone in Israel will be able to see it or know about it. &#91;IDM&#93;’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 51:4; 2 SAMUEL 24:10; PROVERBS 28:13"> David replied, “I have sinned against Yahweh.” Nathan said to David, “Yahweh has forgiven you for your sin: You will not die because of this sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ROMANS 2:24; Isaiah 52:5; PSALMS 89:31–33"> But, you have shown contempt for Yahweh by doing this. So, your baby will die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:38; PSALMS 104:29; 2 CHRONICLES 13:20"> Then Nathan went home. David’s baby died Then Yahweh caused the baby t o become very sick, the one that Uriah’s wife had given birth to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 13:31; ACTS 9:9; PSALMS 69:10"> So David prayed to God that the child would not die. He ◄fasted/abstained from food►, and he went into the room where he slept and lay all night on the floor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:35; 1 SAMUEL 28:23"> The next morning his advisors stood around him and urged him to get up. But he would not get up, and he would not eat any food with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:15"> One week later the baby died. David’s servants were afraid to tell that to David. They said to each other, “Hey, while the baby was still alive, we talked to him, but he would not answer us. Now, if we tell him that the baby is dead &#91;RHQ&#93;, he may do something to harm himself!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> But when David saw that his servants were whispering something to each other, he realized that the baby must be dead. So he asked them, “Is the baby dead?” They replied, “Yes, he is dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 1:20; RUTH 3:3; LAMENTATIONS 3:39–41"> Then David got up from the floor. He bathed himself, put lotions on his body, and put on other clothes, Then he went into Yahweh’s Sacred Tent and worshiped Yahweh. Then he went home. He asked his servants for some food. They gave him some, and he ate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 2:15"> Then his servants said to him, “We do not understand &#91;RHQ&#93; why you have done this! While the baby was still alive, you cried for him and refused to eat anything. But now that the baby has died, you are not crying any more! You got up and ate some food!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–3; Isaiah 38:5; JONAH 3:9–10"> David replied, “While the baby was still alive, I fasted and cried. I thought, ‘Who knows? Yahweh may be merciful to me and not allow the baby to die’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 37:35; JOB 30:23; LUKE 23:43"> But now the baby is dead. So ◄there is no reason for me to &lt;fast/abstain from eating food&gt; any more./why should I &lt;fast/abstain from eating food&gt; any more?► &#91;RHQ&#93; ◄Can I bring him back to me?/I certainly cannot bring him back to me!► &#91;RHQ&#93; Some day I will go to where he is, but he will not return to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:6; 1 CHRONICLES 28:5–6; 1 CHRONICLES 22:9–10"> Then David comforted his wife, Bathsheba. Then he ◄slept/had sexual relations► with her, and she became pregnant again and gave birth to another son. David named that son Solomon. Yahweh loved that little boy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:4; Nehemiah 13:26; 1 KINGS 1:11"> He told the prophet Nathan to tell David to name the baby boy Jedediah which means ‘loved by Yahweh’, because Yahweh loved him.</span>
</div>

## David’s army captured Rabbah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 20:1–3; DEUTERONOMY 3:11; 2 SAMUEL 11:25"> ◄Meanwhile/While all that was happening►, Joab’s soldiers attacked Rabbah, the capital city of the Ammon people-group; and they captured the king’s fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 21:20; 2 SAMUEL 11:1; DEUTERONOMY 3:11"> Then Joab sent messengers to David, to tell him this: “My troops are attacking Rabbah, and we have captured the city’s water supply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 7:18"> Now gather your troops and come and surround the city and capture it. If you do not do that, my troops will capture the city and it will then be named ‘the City of Joab’.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> So David gathered all his troops. They went with David to Rabbah and attacked it and captured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 20:2"> Then David took the crown from the head of the king of Rabbah (OR, from the head of Milcom the god of Rabbah) and put it on his own head. It was very heavy; it weighed ◄75 pounds/34 kg.►, and it had many very valuable stones fastened to it. His soldiers also took many other valuable things from the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 20:3; PSALMS 21:8–9; AMOS 1:3"> Then they brought the people of Rabbah out of the city and forced them to work for them using saws and iron picks and axes. David’s troops also forced them to make bricks. David’s soldiers did this in all the towns of the Ammon people-group. Then David and all of his army returned to Jerusalem.</span>
