## David was appointed to be the king of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:31; 1 SAMUEL 23:4; GENESIS 32:2"> Some time after that, David asked Yahweh, “Should I go back to live in one of the towns in Judah?” Yahweh replied, “Yes, go up there.” Then David asked, “To which town should I go?” Yahweh replied, “To Hebron.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:42–43; 1 SAMUEL 30:5; LUKE 22:28–29"> So David went up there, taking his two wives, Ahinoam who was from Jezreel city, and Abigail, the widow of Nabal from Carmel city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:1; 1 CHRONICLES 12:1–7; 1 SAMUEL 27:2–3"> He also took the men who had been with him, and their families. They all started to live in villages near Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 5:5; 1 SAMUEL 31:11–13; 2 SAMUEL 5:3"> Then the men of Judah came to Hebron, and one of them poured olive oil on David’s head to show they were appointing him to be &#91;MTY&#93; the king of the tribe &#91;MTY&#93; of Judah. When David found out that the people of Jabesh town in the Gilead region had buried Saul’s body,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 23:21; PSALMS 115:15; RUTH 2:20"> he sent messengers to the men of Jabesh to tell them this: “I desire/hope that Yahweh will bless you for having shown, by burying the body of Saul your king, that you were loyal to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:22; EXODUS 34:6; MATTHEW 5:44"> Now I also desire/hope that Yahweh will faithfully love you and be loyal to you. And I will do good things for you because of what you have done for Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:10; 2 SAMUEL 10:12; GENESIS 15:1"> Now, although Saul your king is dead, be strong and courageous, like the people of Judah, who have appointed me to be their king.”</span>
</div>

## Ishbosheth was appointed to be the king of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 14:50; GENESIS 32:2; 2 SAMUEL 3:7–8"> While this was happening, Ner’s son Abner, the commander of Saul’s army, took Saul’s son Ishbosheth and went across the Jordan River to Mahanaim town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:18; JOSHUA 13:8–11; NUMBERS 1:40"> There Abner proclaimed that Ishbosheth was now the king of the Gilead region and of the tribe of Asher and the region of Jezreel and the tribes of Ephraim and Benjamin. That meant that he was the king of all of the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Ishbosheth was 40 years old when he started to rule over the people of Israel. He ruled them for two years. But the tribe of Judah ◄was loyal to David/wanted David to be their king►,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:11; 2 SAMUEL 5:4–5; 1 CHRONICLES 3:4"> and he ruled them for seven and a half years while he was living in Hebron.</span>
</div>

## The war between the armies of Judah and Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:25; JOSHUA 10:2; JOSHUA 9:3"> One day Abner and the officials of Isbosheth went from Mahanaim across the Jordan River to Gibeon city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:16; 1 CHRONICLES 2:16; 2 SAMUEL 20:23"> Joab, whose mother was Zeruiah, and some of David’s officials went from Hebron to Gibeon, and met at the pool there. They all sat down, the one group on one side of the pool and the other group on the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 2:17; PROVERBS 10:23; PROVERBS 17:14"> Abner said to Joab, “Let’s tell some of our young men to fight each other!” Joab replied, “Okay!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So twelve men from the tribe of Benjamin fought for Ishbosheth, against twelve of David’s soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Each of them grabbed the head of the man against whom he was fighting, and thrust his sword into that man’s side. The result was that all 24 of them fell down dead. So that area in Gibeon is now called ‘Field of Swords’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:1"> Then the others started to fight. It was a very fierce battle. Abner and the men of Israel were defeated by David’s soldiers.</span>
</div>

## Joab’s brother Asahel was killed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 12:8; HABAKKUK 3:19; SONG OF SONGS 8:14"> Zeruiah’s three sons were there on that day: Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. Asahel was able to run very fast; he could run as fast as a wild gazelle/antelope.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 2:21; JOSHUA 1:7; PROVERBS 4:27"> Asahel started to pursue Abner. He ran straight toward Abner, without stopping.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Abner looked behind him, and said “Is that you, Asahel?” Asahel replied, “Yes!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 14:19"> Abner yelled at him, “Turn to one side or the other, and pursue someone else!” But Asahel would not stop pursuing Abner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:27; PROVERBS 29:1; 2 KINGS 14:10–12"> So Abner yelled at him again, “Stop ◄chasing after/pursuing► me! ◄Why should I kill you?/It would not be good for me to kill you!► &#91;RHQ&#93; If I did that, ◄how could I &lt;face/be reconciled with&gt; your brother Joab?/it would be very difficult for me to &lt;face/be reconciled with&gt; your brother Joab.► &#91;RHQ&#93;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:27; 2 SAMUEL 4:6; 2 SAMUEL 5:6"> But Asahel refused to stop pursuing Abner. So Abner suddenly turned and thrust the butt end of his spear into Asahel’s stomach. Because he thrust it very strongly, that end of the spear went though Asahel’s body and came out at his back, and he fell to the ground, dead. All the other soldiers who came to the place where his body was lying stopped and stood there, stunned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> But Joab and Abishai continued to pursue Abner. At sunset they came to Ammah Hill, which is east of Giah, along the road to the desert near Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The men from the tribe of Benjamin gathered around Abner in one group, and stood at the top of a hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:14; JEREMIAH 46:10; ACTS 7:26"> Then Abner called out to Joab, saying “Are we going to continue to fight forever &#91;RHQ&#93;? Do you not realize that if we continue fighting, the result will be very bad &#91;RHQ&#93;? We are all descendants of Jacob. So we should stop fighting each other ◄How long will it be until you tell your soldiers to stop pursuing us?/Tell your soldiers to stop pursuing us.►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:14; 2 SAMUEL 2:14; PROVERBS 15:1"> Joab replied, “Just as surely as God lives, if you had not said that, my soldiers would have continued pursuing your men until tomorrow morning!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So Joab blew a trumpet to signal that they should stop fighting. So all his men did that. They did not pursue the soldiers of Israel any more, and they stopped fighting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 2:8; SONG OF SONGS 2:17"> That night Abner and his soldiers went through the Jordan River Valley. They crossed the Jordan River and marched all the next morning, and they finally arrived at Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Joab and his soldiers stopped pursuing Abner. And when he gathered all his soldiers together, he found out that in addition to Asahel, only 19 of them had been killed in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:1; 1 KINGS 20:11"> But David’s soldiers had killed 360 of Abner’s men, all from the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:13–16; 1 SAMUEL 17:58; 2 CHRONICLES 21:1"> Some of Joab’s soldiers took Asahel’s body and buried it in the tomb where his father had been buried, in Bethlehem. Then they marched all during the night, and at dawn they arrived back home at Hebron.</span>
</div>
