## Queen Athaliah of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 41:1; 2 KINGS 25:25; 2 CHRONICLES 22:10–12"> When King Ahaziah’s mother Athaliah saw that her son had been killed, she commanded that all the members of Ahaziah’s family who might become king must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:26; JEREMIAH 35:2; Isaiah 7:6–7"> So Ahaziah’s sons were all about to be murdered. But Jehosheba, who was King Jehoram’s daughter and Ahaziah’s half-sister, took Ahaziah’s very young son Joash and hid him and ◄his nursemaid/the woman who took care of him► in a bedroom in the temple. So he was not killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 12:8; 2 CHRONICLES 22:12; MALACHI 3:15"> He stayed with Jehosheba for six years. All during that time, he remained hidden in the temple, while Athaliah ruled Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 KINGS 11:19; 2 KINGS 11:9; 2 KINGS 11:17"> But during the next year, Jehoiada the Supreme Priest summoned the officers who supervised the royal bodyguards and the palace guards. He told them to come to the temple. There he required them to solemnly promise that they would do what he told them to do. And he showed King Ahaziah’s son Joash to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 9:25; EZEKIEL 44:2–3; 1 CHRONICLES 23:32"> He gave them these instructions: “There are three groups of you guards. When one group finishes their work on the Sabbath day, divide yourselves into three smaller groups. One group must guard the palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 23:4–5; 1 CHRONICLES 26:13–19"> Another group must guard at the Sur Gate. The other group must guard at the gate behind the other groups.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 23:6; 2 KINGS 11:5"> The two groups that are not working on the Sabbath day must guard the temple to protect little King Joash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:17; 2 CHRONICLES 23:7; 1 KINGS 2:28–31"> You must stand around the king wherever he goes, with your weapons in your hands. You must kill anyone who comes near you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 23:8; 2 KINGS 11:4; 1 CHRONICLES 26:26"> The officers who supervised the guards did what Jehoiada told them to do. Each one brought to Jehoiada the guards that he supervised—the guards who were just finishing their work and those who were about to start their work on the Sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:7; 1 CHRONICLES 18:7; 2 CHRONICLES 5:1"> The priest distributed to the commanders of the guards the spears and shields that had belonged to King David, that were kept/stored in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 KINGS 11:8; EZEKIEL 8:16; 2 KINGS 11:10"> Then he commanded all the guards to stand in their positions, each one with his sword in his hand, all around the young king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 25:16; 1 SAMUEL 10:24; 1 KINGS 1:39"> Then he brought Joash out. He put the crown on his head and gave him a scroll on which were written the rules that the kings needed to obey. Then he poured some olive oil on Joash’s head and proclaimed that he was now the king. The people all clapped their hands and shouted, “We desire/hope that the king will live for many years!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 23:12–15"> When Athaliah heard the noise that was being made by the guards and the other people, she ran to the temple, where the people were gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 34:31; 2 KINGS 23:3; 2 KINGS 9:23"> She saw the new king standing there alongside one of the big pillars, which was the place at the temple where the kings usually stood. She saw that he was surrounded by the temple officers and men blowing trumpets, and that the people were shouting joyfully, and some of them were also blowing trumpets. She tore her clothes to show her distress and shouted, “You are traitors! You have tricked/deceived me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 9:7; 2 CHRONICLES 23:14; 2 CHRONICLES 23:9"> Jehoiada immediately said, “Kill her, but do not kill her here at the temple of Yahweh! Take her away between two rows of guards. And kill anyone who tries to rescue her!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 9:6; 2 CHRONICLES 23:15; MATTHEW 7:2"> She tried to flee, but the guards seized her and took her to the palace, to the place where horses enter the courtyard. They killed her there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 5:3; 2 CHRONICLES 15:12–14; 2 CHRONICLES 34:31"> Then Jehoiada made a solemn agreement between the king and the people, that they would always ◄obey/be faithful to► Yahweh. He also made an agreement requiring the people to be loyal to Joash their king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:3; 2 KINGS 10:26; 1 KINGS 18:40"> Then all the people of Israel who were there went to the temple of Baal and tore it down. They smashed the altars and the statues of Baal. They also killed Mattan, the priest of Baal, in front of the altars. Jehoiada stationed/put guards at the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:25; JEREMIAH 22:4; 2 CHRONICLES 23:19"> Then he and the officers of the temple, the officers who supervised the royal bodyguards, and the king’s bodyguards brought the king from the temple to the palace. All the people followed them. Joash entered the palace at the Guard Gate and sat down on the throne, which showed that he was the new king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:10; PROVERBS 29:2; 2 KINGS 11:14"> All the people of Judah rejoiced. And because Athaliah had been killed, the city was quiet/calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 24:1–14; 2 KINGS 22:1; 2 KINGS 11:4"> Joash was seven years old when he became the king of Judah.</span>
</div>
