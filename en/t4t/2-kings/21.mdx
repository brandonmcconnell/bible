## King Manasseh of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; 2 CHRONICLES 32:33–9; MATTHEW 1:10"> Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to rule. He ruled Judah for 55 years from Jerusalem. His mother was Hephzibah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:31; 2 CHRONICLES 36:14; 2 CHRONICLES 33:2–4"> He did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil. He imitated the disgusting things that were formerly done by the people of the nations that Yahweh had expelled from the land of Israel as his people advanced through the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:4; 2 KINGS 17:16; DEUTERONOMY 17:3"> He commanded his workers to rebuild the shrines for worshiping Yahweh that his father Hezekiah had destroyed because they were not in the place that Yahweh had said they should worship him. He directed his workers to build altars for worshiping Baal. He made a statue of the goddess Asherah, like Ahab the king of Israel had done previously. And Manasseh worshiped &#91;DOU&#93; the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:29; 2 SAMUEL 7:13; JEREMIAH 32:34"> He directed his workers to build altars for worshiping foreign gods in the temple of Yahweh, about which Yahweh had said, “It is here in Jerusalem where I want people to worship &#91;MTY&#93; me, forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:12; 2 KINGS 23:4; 2 KINGS 23:12"> He directed that altars for worshiping the stars be built in both of the courtyards outside the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:17; LEVITICUS 19:31; LEVITICUS 18:21"> He even sacrificed his own son by burning him in a fire. He performed rituals to practice sorcery and magic rituals. He also went to people who consulted the spirits of dead people to find out what would happen in the future. He did many things that Yahweh considered to be extremely evil, things that caused Yahweh to become very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:16; 1 KINGS 9:3; 2 SAMUEL 7:13"> He placed the statue of the goddess Asherah in the temple, the place about which Yahweh had said to David and his son Solomon, “My temple will be here in Jerusalem. This is the city that I have chosen from all the territory of the twelve tribes of Israel, where I want people to worship me, forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:10; DEUTERONOMY 5:28–29; PSALMS 81:11–16"> And if the Israeli people obey all my commands and all the laws that I gave to Moses, the man who served me very well, I will not again force them to leave this land that I gave to their ancestors.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:12; Nehemiah 9:26; 1 KINGS 14:16"> But the people did not heed Yahweh. And Manasseh persuaded them to commit sins that are more evil than the sins that were committed by the people of the nations that Yahweh had expelled from the land as the Israeli people advanced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:34–37; 2 CHRONICLES 33:10; 2 CHRONICLES 36:15"> These are some of the things that the prophets said many times, messages that Yahweh had given them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 KINGS 24:3–4; 1 KINGS 21:26; 2 KINGS 21:9"> “Manasseh, the king of Judah, has done these abominable things, things that are much worse than the things that the Amor people-group did in this land long ago. He has persuaded the people of Judah to sin by worshiping idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 19:3; 1 SAMUEL 3:11; DANIEL 9:12"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh, the God whom you Israeli people worship, say: I will the cause the people of Jerusalem and the rest of Judah to experience great disasters. It will be terrible, with the result that everyone who hears about it will be stunned &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="AMOS 7:7–8; LAMENTATIONS 2:8; Isaiah 34:11"> I will judge and punish &#91;MET&#93; the people of Jerusalem like I punished the family of King Ahab of Israel. I will ◄wipe Jerusalem clean/remove all the people from Jerusalem►, like &#91;MET&#93; people wipe a plate and then turn it upside down after they have finished eating, to show that they are now satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:33; 2 KINGS 19:4; PSALMS 89:38–45"> And I will abandon the people who remain alive, and I will allow their enemies to conquer them and steal everything valuable from their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:30; DEUTERONOMY 31:27; EZEKIEL 20:21"> I will do this because my people have done things that I consider to be very evil, things which have caused me to become very angry. They have caused me to become angry continually, ever since their ancestors left Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:33; 2 CHRONICLES 33:9; 2 KINGS 21:11"> Manasseh commanded his officials to kill many innocent people in Jerusalem, with the result that their blood flowed in the streets. He did this in addition to persuading the people of Judah to sin against Yahweh by worshiping idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 KINGS 20:20–21; 2 CHRONICLES 33:1–20"> If you want to know more about all the things that Manasseh did, they are written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Judah’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 33:20; 2 KINGS 21:26; 2 CHRONICLES 32:33"> Manasseh died &#91;EUP&#93; and was buried in the garden outside his palace, the garden that Uzza had made. Then Manasseh’s son Amon became the king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Amon of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 33:21–23; 1 KINGS 22:51; 1 CHRONICLES 3:14"> Amon was twenty-two years old when he became king. He ruled Judah from Jerusalem for only two years. His mother’s name was Meshullemeth. She was from Jotbah town, and was the granddaughter of Haruz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:32; 2 KINGS 21:11; ACTS 7:51"> Amon did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil, like his father Manasseh had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> He imitated the behavior of his father, and he worshiped the same idols that his father had worshiped &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 KINGS 22:17; 1 KINGS 11:33; 1 CHRONICLES 28:9"> He abandoned Yahweh, the God whom his ancestors had worshiped, and did not behave as Yahweh wanted him to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 KINGS 12:20; 2 KINGS 14:19; 2 CHRONICLES 33:24–25"> Then one day some of his officials plotted to kill him. They assassinated him in the palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 KINGS 14:5; 2 KINGS 11:17; 2 KINGS 14:21"> But then the people of Judah killed all those who had assassinated King Amon, and they appointed his son Josiah to be their king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 KINGS 20:20; 2 KINGS 21:17"> If you want to read about &#91;RHQ&#93; the other things that Amon did, they are written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Judah’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 KINGS 21:18; MATTHEW 1:10; 1 KINGS 13:5"> Amon was also buried in the tomb in the garden that Uzza had made. Then Amon’s son Josiah became the king.</span>
</div>
</div>
