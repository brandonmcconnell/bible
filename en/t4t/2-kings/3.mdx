## The war between Israel and Moab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 KINGS 1:17; 1 KINGS 22:51; 2 KINGS 8:16"> After Jehoshaphat had been ruling Judah for almost 18 years, Ahab’s son Joram became the king of Israel. He ruled in Samaria city for twelve years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 KINGS 10:18; 2 KINGS 10:26–28; 1 KINGS 21:25"> He did things that Yahweh considered to be evil, but he did not do as much evil as his father and mother had done, and he got rid of the pillar for worshiping Baal which his father had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:16; 1 KINGS 15:34; 2 KINGS 15:18"> But he committed the sins that King Jeroboam had committed and which led the Israeli people to sin, and he did not stop committing those sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:2; GENESIS 26:13–14; JOB 1:3"> Mesha, the king of Moab, raised sheep. Every year he was forced to give 100,000 lambs and the wool from 100,000 rams to the king of Israel, because his kingdom was controlled by the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 KINGS 1:1; 2 KINGS 8:20; 2 CHRONICLES 21:8–10"> But after King Ahab died, Mesha rebelled against the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 24:1–25; 1 SAMUEL 11:8; 1 SAMUEL 15:4"> So King Joram left Samaria and gathered together all his soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:4; 2 CHRONICLES 22:10–12; 2 CHRONICLES 18:3"> Then he sent this message to Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah: “The king of Moab has rebelled against me. So will your army join my army and fight against the army of Moab?” Jehoshaphat replied, “Yes, we will help you. We are ready to do whatever you want us to. My soldiers and my horses are ready to help you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MALACHI 1:2–3; NUMBERS 21:4"> He asked, “On which road shall we march to attack them?” Joram replied, “We will go south to Jerusalem, where your army will join us. Then we will all go south of the Dead Sea, and then turn north through the Edom Desert.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:47; NUMBERS 20:2; EXODUS 17:1"> So the king of Israel and his army went with the kings of Judah and Edom and their armies. They marched for seven days. Then there was no water left for their soldiers or for their animals that carried supplies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 78:34–36; Isaiah 51:20; Isaiah 8:21"> The king of Israel exclaimed, “This is a terrible situation! It seems that Yahweh will allow the three of us to be captured by the army of &#91;MTY&#93; Moab!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:7; 1 KINGS 19:21; 2 KINGS 3:3"> Jehoshaphat said, “Is there a prophet here who can ask Yahweh for us what we should do?” One of Joram’s army officers said, “Elisha, the son of Shaphat, is here. He was Elijah’s assistant.” &#91;IDM&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:15; 2 KINGS 2:21; Isaiah 60:14"> Jehoshaphat said, “It will be good to ask him, because he speaks what Yahweh tells him to say.” So those three kings went to Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:6; 1 KINGS 18:19; 1 KINGS 22:10–11"> Elisha said to the king of Israel, “Why do you come to me &#91;RHQ&#93;? Go and ask those prophets that your father and mother consulted!” But Joram replied, “No, we want you to ask Yahweh, because it seems that Yahweh is going to allow us three kings to be captured by the army of Moab.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:16; 1 KINGS 17:1; 2 CHRONICLES 19:3–4"> Elisha replied, “I serve Yahweh, the commander of the armies of angels in heaven. As surely as he lives, if I did not respect Jehoshaphat the king of Judah, I would not even think about doing anything to help you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:23; EPHESIANS 5:18–19; EZEKIEL 1:3"> But, bring a musician to me.” So they did that. And when the musician played on his harp, the power of Yahweh came on Elisha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 2:16–18; 2 KINGS 4:3"> He said, “Yahweh says that he will cause this dry stream-bed to be full of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 107:35; Isaiah 41:17–18; Isaiah 48:21"> The result will be that your soldiers and your animals that carry supplies and your livestock will have plenty of water to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:27; LUKE 1:37; JEREMIAH 32:17"> That is not difficult for Yahweh to do. But he will do more than that. He will also enable you to defeat the army of Moab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:17; DEUTERONOMY 20:19–20; JUDGES 6:16"> You will conquer all their beautiful cities, cities that have high walls around them. You must cut down all their fruit trees, stop water from flowing from their springs, and ruin their fertile fields by covering them with rocks.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 29:39–40; DANIEL 9:21; 1 KINGS 18:36"> The next morning, at the time when they offered the sacrifices of grain, they were surprised to see water flowing from Edom and covering the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:14; 1 KINGS 20:11"> When the people of Moab heard that the three kings had come with their armies to fight against them, all the men who were able to fight in battles, from the youngest men to the oldest ones, were summoned, and they ◄took their positions/prepared to fight► at the southern border of their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But when they rose early the next morning, they saw that the water across from them appeared to be as red as blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JUDGES 5:30; EXODUS 15:9; Isaiah 10:14"> They exclaimed, “It is blood! The three enemy armies must have fought and killed each other! So let’s go and take everything that they have left!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:3–4; JOSHUA 8:20–22; JUDGES 20:40–46"> But when they reached the area where the Israeli soldiers had set up their tents, the Israelis attacked the soldiers from Moab and forced them to retreat. The Israeli soldiers pursued the soldiers from Moab and killed many of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 16:7; JEREMIAH 48:36; JEREMIAH 48:31"> The Israelis also destroyed their cities. And whenever they passed fertile fields, they threw rocks on those fields, until the fields were covered with rocks. They stopped water from flowing from the springs and cut down the fruit trees. Finally, only the capital city, Kir-Hareseth, remained. The Israeli soldiers who threw stones with slings surrounded the city and attacked it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 KINGS 3:9; AMOS 2:1"> When the king of Moab realized that his army was being defeated, he took with him 700 men who fought with swords, and they tried to force a way through the Israeli lines of soldiers to escape to get help from the king of Syria, but they were unable to escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MICAH 6:7; DEUTERONOMY 12:31; JUDGES 11:31"> Then the king of Moab took his oldest son, who would have become the next king, and killed him and offered him for a sacrifice to their god Chemosh, burning him on top of the city wall. The Israeli soldiers were horrified, with the result that they left that city and returned to their own country.</span>
