## The axe head floated

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 KINGS 2:3; 2 KINGS 4:1; JOSHUA 17:14"> One day a group of prophets said to Elisha, “Look, this place where we meet together with you is very small.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 21:3; ACTS 18:3; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:9"> Allow us to go to the Jordan River and cut down some trees to make logs to build a new meeting place.” So Elisha said, “Okay, go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 6:28; JUDGES 19:6; 2 KINGS 5:23"> One of them said to Elisha, “Please come with us.” So Elisha replied, “Okay, I will go with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 19:5; DEUTERONOMY 29:11"> So they went together. When they reached the Jordan River, they cut down some trees.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 37:21; ECCLESIASTES 10:10; 2 KINGS 6:15"> But while one of them was cutting down a tree, suddenly the axe head separated from the handle and fell into the water. He cried out to Elisha, “O, Master, what shall I do? The axe is not mine; I borrowed it!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 KINGS 2:21; MARK 8:23–25; 2 KINGS 4:41"> Elisha replied, “Where did it fall into the water?” After the man showed him the place, Elisha cut off a stick, and threw it into the water, and caused the iron axe head to float on top of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 KINGS 4:36; EXODUS 4:4; 2 KINGS 4:7"> Elisha said, “Take it out of the water.” So the man reached his hand down and picked up the axe head.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elisha tricked the army of Syria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 KINGS 6:24; PROVERBS 21:30; PROVERBS 20:18"> Whenever the king of Syria prepared to send his army to fight against Israel, he first consulted his officers, and then told them where they should set up their tents.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="AMOS 3:7; REVELATION 1:1; 2 KINGS 6:12"> But each time, Elisha would send a message to warn the king of Israel, telling him where the army of Syria was planning to attack them, saying, “Be sure that your army does not go near that place, because the army of Syria has set up their tents there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:14; 2 KINGS 5:14; ACTS 27:24"> So the king of Israel would send messengers to warn the people who lived in that place, and the people would remain on guard. That happened several times.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 18:7–11; Isaiah 57:20–21; MATTHEW 2:3–12"> The king of Syria was very upset/disturbed/angry about that. So he summoned his army officers and said to them, “Obviously one of you is ◄betraying us/revealing our plans► to the king of Israel. Which one of you is doing it?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DANIEL 2:28–30; AMOS 3:7; 2 KINGS 6:9–10"> One of his officers answered, “Your Majesty, it is not one of us. Elisha the prophet knows what we plan to do, and he tells the king of Israel everything. He even knows what you say in your own bedroom!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 37:17; JOHN 11:47–53; 1 SAMUEL 23:22–23"> The king of Syria replied, “Go and find out where he is, and I will send some men there to capture him.” Someone told him, “People say that he is in Dothan town north of Samaria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:17; 1 SAMUEL 24:2; 1 SAMUEL 23:26"> So the king sent a large group of soldiers to Dothan with horses and chariots. They arrived at night and surrounded the town.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:26; 2 CHRONICLES 20:12; PSALMS 53:5"> Early the next morning, Elisha’s servant got up and went outside the house. He saw the soldiers of Syria with their horses and chariots surrounding the town. So he went inside the house and reported it to Elisha and exclaimed, “O, sir! What are we going to do?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10–14; ROMANS 8:31; 1 JOHN 4:4"> Elisha replied, “Do not be afraid! Those who will be helping us will be more than those who will be helping them!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 68:17; PSALMS 34:7; ZECHARIAH 6:1–7"> Then he prayed, “Yahweh, I request that you open my servant’s eyes in order that he can see what is out there!” So Yahweh enabled the servant to look out and see that surrounding the hill on which the town was built was a huge number of horses, and chariots made of fire!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 13:11; JOHN 12:40; GENESIS 19:11"> When the army of Syria prepared to attack Elisha, he prayed again, saying, “Yahweh, cause all these soldiers to become blind!” Yahweh answered his prayer and caused them to be unable to see clearly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 24:16; 2 SAMUEL 16:18–19; LUKE 9:23"> Then Elisha went to them and said, “You are not on the right road; this is not the city that you are searching for. I will take you to the man whom you are searching for.” But he led them to Samaria, the capital of Israel!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 KINGS 6:17; LUKE 24:31; LUKE 16:23"> As soon as they entered Samaria, Elisha prayed again, saying, “Yahweh, now enable these soldiers to see correctly again!” So Yahweh enabled them to see correctly, and they were surprised to see that they were inside Samaria city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:13; 2 KINGS 8:9; 2 KINGS 2:12"> When the king of Israel saw them, he said to Elisha, “Sir, shall I tell my soldiers to kill them? Shall we kill all of them?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ROMANS 12:20–21; GENESIS 48:22; HOSEA 2:18"> Elisha replied, “No, you must not kill them. If your army captured many of your enemies in a battle, you would certainly not &#91;RHQ&#93; kill them. Give these men something to eat and drink, and then allow them to return to their king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:2; 2 KINGS 24:2; LUKE 6:35"> So the king of Israel did that. He told his servants to provide a big feast for them. And when they had eaten and drunk plenty, he sent them away. They returned to the king of Syria and told him what had happened. So for a while after that, soldiers from Syria stopped raiding/attacking towns in Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The army of Syria surrounded Samaria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:1; 1 KINGS 22:31; ECCLESIASTES 9:14"> But some time later, Ben-Hadad, the king of Syria, assembled his entire army, and they went to Samaria and surrounded the city for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 KINGS 6:28–29; JEREMIAH 14:18; JEREMIAH 14:13–15"> Because of that, after a while there was hardly any food left inside the city, with the result that eventually a donkey’s head, which was usually worthless, cost eighty pieces of silver, and ◄one cup/0.3 liter► of dove’s dung cost five pieces of silver.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 14:4; Isaiah 10:3; LUKE 18:3"> One day when the king of Israel was walking on top of the city wall, a woman cried out to him, “Your Majesty, help me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PSALMS 118:8–9; PSALMS 124:1–3; PSALMS 60:11"> He replied, “If Yahweh will not help you, I certainly cannot &#91;RHQ&#93; help you. I certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; do not have any wheat or wine!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 14:5; JUDGES 18:23; LAMENTATIONS 4:10"> What is your problem?” She replied, “Several days ago, that woman over there said to me, ‘Because we have nothing left to eat, let’s kill your son today, in order that we can eat his flesh. Then tomorrow we can kill my son and eat his flesh.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:29; DEUTERONOMY 28:53; DEUTERONOMY 28:57"> So we killed my son and cut his body up and boiled his flesh and ate it. The next day, I said to her, ‘Now give your son to me, in order that we can kill him and cook his flesh and eat it.’ But she has hidden her son!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 KINGS 21:27; 2 KINGS 5:7; 2 KINGS 19:1"> When the king heard what the woman said, he tore his robe to show that he was very distressed. The people who were standing close to the wall were able to see that the king was wearing rough cloth underneath his robe because he was very distressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="RUTH 1:17; 1 KINGS 19:2; JOHN 11:50"> The king exclaimed, “I wish/hope that God will strike me dead if I do not cut off the head of Elisha today, because he is the one who has caused these terrible things to happen to us!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:1; 1 KINGS 18:4; EZEKIEL 8:1"> So the king sent an officer to get Elisha.<br />Before the officer arrived, Elisha was sitting in his house with some Israeli elders who were talking with him. Elisha said to them, “That murderer, the king of Israel, is sending someone here to kill me. Listen: When he arrives, shut the door and do not allow him to come in. And the king will be coming right behind that officer!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOB 2:9; Isaiah 8:21; GENESIS 4:13"> And while he was still speaking, the king and the officer arrived. The king said, “It is Yahweh who has caused us to have all this trouble/suffering. ◄Why should I wait any longer &#91;RHQ&#93; for him to do something to help us?/It is useless for me to wait any longer for him to help us!►”</span>
</div>
