## The widow from Shunem recovered her land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Haggai 1:11; 2 KINGS 4:31–35; RUTH 1:1"> After Elisha caused the son of the woman from Shunem town to become alive again, he told her that she should leave with her family and live somewhere else for a while, because Yahweh was going to ◄send a famine/cause food to become very scarce► in the land. He said that the famine would last for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:8; JUDGES 3:3; 1 SAMUEL 27:1–3"> So the woman did what Elisha told her to do. She and her family went to live in the Philistia region for seven years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:16; LUKE 18:3–5; 2 KINGS 4:13"> After the seven years were ended, they returned to their home. The woman went to the king to request that her house and her land be given back to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:20–27; 2 KINGS 2:14; 2 KINGS 6:32"> When she arrived, the king was talking with Gehazi, Elisha’s servant. The king was saying to him, “Tell me all the great things that Elisha has done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 KINGS 4:35; MATTHEW 10:29–30; 2 KINGS 6:26"> While Gehazi was telling the king that Elisha had caused the son of a woman from Shunem to become alive again, that woman came in and requested the king to enable her to get her house and land back again. Gehazi exclaimed, “Your Majesty, this is the woman whose son Elisha caused to become alive again!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:7; PROVERBS 21:1; 2 SAMUEL 9:7"> When the king asked her about it, she told him that what Gehazi had said was true. The king summoned one of his officials and said to him, “I want everything that this woman possessed previously, including the value of all the crops that have been harvested during these last seven years while she was away from her land, to be given back to her.” So the official did that.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elisha predicted the death of the king of Syria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:24; 2 KINGS 6:24; 1 KINGS 20:1"> Elisha went to Damascus, the capital of Syria, when Ben-Hadad, the king of Syria, was very sick. When someone told the king that Elisha was in Damascus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 KINGS 19:15; 2 KINGS 1:2; 1 SAMUEL 9:7"> the king told one of his officials named Hazael, “Go and talk to that prophet and take a present/gift with you to give to him. Request him to ask Yahweh if I will recover from my illness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:13; 2 KINGS 13:14; 1 KINGS 19:15"> So Hazael went to talk with Elisha. He took with him forty camels that were carrying many kinds of goods that were produced in Damascus. When Hazael met him, he said to him, “Your friend Ben-Hadad, the king of Syria, sent me to ask you whether you think he will recover from his illness.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="AMOS 7:7; GENESIS 2:17; AMOS 7:1"> Elisha said to Hazael, “Go and say to him, ‘Yes, you will certainly not die from this illness,’ but Yahweh has shown me that he will certainly die before he recovers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 19:41; ROMANS 9:2; JEREMIAH 9:18"> Then Elisha stared at him and had a terrified look on his face. That caused Hazael to feel uneasy/embarrassed. Then suddenly Elisha started to cry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HOSEA 13:16; NAHUM 3:10; 2 KINGS 15:16"> Hazael said, “Sir, why are you crying?”<br />Elisha replied, “Because Yahweh has enabled me to know the terrible things that you will do to the people of Israel: You will burn their cities with walls around them, you will kill their fine young men with a sword, you will bash the heads of their children, and you will rip open the bellies of their pregnant women with a sword.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:43; 1 KINGS 19:15; 2 SAMUEL 9:8"> Hazael replied, “I am as insignificant as &#91;MET&#93; a dog; ◄how could I do such things?/I would never do such terrible things!►” &#91;RHQ&#93;<br />Elisha replied, “Yahweh has also revealed to me that you will become the king of Syria.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 KINGS 8:10; MATTHEW 26:16; 2 KINGS 5:25"> Then Hazael left and returned to his master/boss the king, who asked him, “What did Elisha say?”<br />He replied, “He told me that you would certainly recover.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 KINGS 8:13; 1 KINGS 15:28; 1 KINGS 11:26–37"> But the next day, while the king was sleeping, Hazael took a blanket and soaked it in water. Then he spread it on the king’s face in order that he could not breathe, and he died. Then Hazael became the king of Syria instead of Ben-Hadad.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Jehoram of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 KINGS 1:17; 2 KINGS 3:1; 2 CHRONICLES 21:1–20"> After King Joram, the son of Ahab, had been ruling in Israel for almost five years, Jehoshaphat’s son Jehoram became the king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 21:5–10"> He was thirty-two years old when he became the king, and he ruled in Jerusalem for eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 KINGS 8:26; 1 KINGS 11:1–5; 2 CHRONICLES 21:13"> His wife was the daughter of King Ahab. And like everyone in Ahab’s family, he continually did the evil things that the previous kings of Israel had done. He did things that Yahweh considered to be evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:36; 2 SAMUEL 7:12–13; LUKE 1:32–33"> But Yahweh did not want to get rid of the people of Judah, because of what he had promised David, who served him very well. He had promised David that his descendants &#91;MET&#93; would always rule Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:47; 2 KINGS 3:9; 2 KINGS 8:22"> During the time that Jehoram ruled, the king of Edom rebelled against Judah, and they appointed their own king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 18:17"> So Jehoram went with his army and all their chariots to Zair city near the border of Edom. There the army of Edom surrounded them. But during the night, Jehoram and the commanders in their chariots were able to get through the enemy lines and escaped. And all his soldiers also fled to their homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 KINGS 19:8; JOSHUA 21:13; GENESIS 27:40"> So after that, Edom was no longer controlled by Judah, and it is still like that. During that same time, the people of Libnah city also freed themselves from being controlled by Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 KINGS 15:36; 1 KINGS 11:41; 2 CHRONICLES 21:11–20"> If you want to read about the other things that Jehoram did, they are written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Judah’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 22:6; 2 CHRONICLES 25:23; 2 CHRONICLES 21:1"> Jehoram died &#91;EUP&#93; and was buried where the other kings of Judah had been buried in the part of Jerusalem called ‘The City of David’. Then Jehoram’s son Ahaziah became the king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Ahaziah of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:29; 2 KINGS 8:16–17; 2 CHRONICLES 21:20–6"> After Ahab’s son Joram has been ruling in Israel for almost twelve years, Jehoram’s son Ahaziah became the king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 22:2; 2 KINGS 9:21–27; 2 KINGS 11:1"> Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he started to rule. He ruled in Jerusalem for only one year. His mother’s name was Athaliah, the daughter of King Ahab and the granddaughter of King Omri of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 22:3–4; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:14–17; ECCLESIASTES 7:26"> King Ahab conducted his life just like the members of Ahab’s family had done. He did things that Yahweh considered to be very evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 22:5; 1 KINGS 4:13; 2 CHRONICLES 19:2"> Ahaziah’s army joined the army of King Joram of Israel to fight against the army of King Hazael of Syria. Their armies started fighting at Ramoth city in the Gilead region, and the soldiers of Syria wounded Joram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:15–16; 2 KINGS 8:28; 2 CHRONICLES 22:5–7"> King Joram returned to Jezreel city to recover from his wounds. King Ahaziah went to visit him there.</span>
</div>
</div>
