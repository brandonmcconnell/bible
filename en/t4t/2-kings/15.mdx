## King Uzziah of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:3–4; 2 KINGS 14:21; 2 CHRONICLES 26:1"> After Jeroboam &#35;2 had been ruling Israel for almost twenty-seven years, Uzziah, the son of King Amaziah of Judah, began to rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:3–4"> He was 16 years old when he started to rule, and he ruled in Jerusalem for 52 years. His mother was Jecoliah, from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 KINGS 14:3–4; 2 KINGS 12:2–3; 2 CHRONICLES 26:4"> He did things that Yahweh was pleased with, like his father Amaziah had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 KINGS 15:35; 2 KINGS 14:4; 2 KINGS 12:3"> But, the places at the pagan shrines where they worshiped Yahweh were not destroyed, and the people continued to burn incense to honor Yahweh at those places instead of in Jerusalem, the place that Yahweh had appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LEVITICUS 13:46; NUMBERS 12:14; 2 KINGS 7:3"> Yahweh caused Uzziah to become a leper, and he was a leper for the rest of his life. He was not allowed to live in the palace. He lived alone in a house, and his son Jotham ruled the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:5–15; 2 KINGS 14:18"> If you want to know about everything else that Uzziah did, it is written &#91;RHQ&#93; in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Judah’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:23; Isaiah 6:1"> Uzziah died &#91;EUP&#93;, and they buried him in the part of Jerusalem called ‘The City of David’, where his ancestors had been buried. Then his son Jotham officially became the king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Zechariah of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 KINGS 14:16–17; 2 KINGS 14:29–1; 2 KINGS 14:21"> After Uzziah had been ruling Judah for almost thirty-eight years, Zechariah, the son of Jeroboam &#35;2, became the king of Israel. He ruled in Samaria city for only six months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 KINGS 14:24; 2 KINGS 13:2; 2 KINGS 13:11"> He did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil, like his ancestors had done. He committed the same kind of sins that Jeroboam &#35;1 had committed, sins which led the Israeli people to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:28; 2 KINGS 15:14; 2 KINGS 9:24"> Then Shallum, the son of Jabesh, made plans to assassinate Zechariah. He killed him at Ibleam town, and then he became the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 KINGS 14:28"> Everything else that Zechariah did is written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 KINGS 10:30; MARK 13:31; 2 KINGS 13:13"> When Zechariah died, that ended the dynasty of King Jehu. That fulfilled what Yahweh said to King Jehu would happen: “Your son and grandson and great-grandson and great-great-grandson will all be kings of Israel.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Shallum of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 KINGS 15:1; MATTHEW 1:8–9; 2 KINGS 15:8"> Shallum, the son of Jabesh, became the king of Israel after King Uzziah had been ruling Judah for almost thirty-nine years. But Shallum ruled in Samaria for only one month.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:17; 1 KINGS 16:17; 1 KINGS 16:15"> Then Menahem, the son of Gadi, went up from Tirzah city to Samaria and assassinated Shallum. Then Menahem became the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:19; 1 KINGS 14:29; 2 KINGS 15:11"> Everything else that Shallum did, including his killing King Zechariah, is written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 KINGS 8:12; 1 KINGS 4:24; HOSEA 13:16"> While Menahem was ruling in Samaria, he completely destroyed Tappuah village south of Samaria and killed all the people who lived there and in the surrounding territory. He did that because the people in that city refused to surrender to him. With his sword he even ripped open the bellies of pregnant women.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Menahem of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> When King Azariah had been ruling Judah for almost thirty-nine years, Menahem, the son of Gadi, became the king of Israel. He ruled in Samaria for ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil. He committed the same kinds of sins that King Jeroboam &#35;1 had committed, sins that led the people of Israel to sin. He continued to commit those sins for the rest of his life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 KINGS 14:5; 1 CHRONICLES 5:25–26; 2 KINGS 18:16"> Then King Tiglath-Pileser of Assyria came with his army to attack Israel. So Menahem gave him ◄thirty-eight tons/35,000 kg.► of silver in order that Tiglath-Pileser would help Menahem to continue to be king and rule his country more strongly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:14–17; 2 KINGS 23:35; 2 KINGS 17:3–4"> Menahem obtained that money from the rich men in Israel. He compelled each of them to contribute 50 pieces of silver. So Tiglath-Pileser took that money and went back home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If you want to know more about everything that Menahem did, it is written &#91;RHQ&#93; in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Menahem died &#91;EUP&#93; and was buried, and his son Pekahiah became the king of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Pekahiah of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:25; 1 KINGS 22:51; JOB 20:5"> When King Uzziah had been ruling Judah for almost 50 years, Menahem’s son Pekahiah became the king of Israel. He ruled in Samaria for only two years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 KINGS 15:9; 2 KINGS 15:18"> He did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil. He committed the same kind of sins that King Jeroboam &#35;1 had committed, sins which led the people of Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 28:6; 2 KINGS 9:14; 1 KINGS 16:9"> Then one of Pekahiah’s army commanders whose name was Pekah, the son of Remaliah, planned with 50 men from the Gilead region to kill Pekahiah. They assassinated him in a fortified place in the king’s palace in Samaria. Then Pekah became the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Everything else that Pekahiah did is written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Pekah of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:4; Isaiah 7:1; 2 KINGS 15:23"> When King Uzziah had been ruling Judah for almost 52 years, Pekah, the son of Remaliah, became the king of Israel. He ruled in Samaria for 20 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 KINGS 15:18; 2 KINGS 13:6; 2 KINGS 15:9"> He also did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil. He committed the same kind of sins that King Jeroboam &#35;1 had committed, sins that led the people of Israel to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 5:26; 2 KINGS 17:6; 2 KINGS 16:7"> While Pekah was the king, Tiglath-Pileser, the king of Assyria, came with his army and captured the cities of Ijon, Abel-Beth-Maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor, and the regions of Gilead, Galilee, and Naphtali. His army forced the Israeli people to leave their country and go to live in Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:1; 2 CHRONICLES 28:4–6; 2 KINGS 16:1"> Then Hoshea, the son of Elah, plotted to kill Pekah. He assassinated him when Uzziah’s son Jotham had been ruling Judah for almost 20 years. Then Hoshea became the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Everything else that Pekah did is written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Jotham of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 3:12; 2 KINGS 15:7; 2 CHRONICLES 27:1–9"> When Pekah had been ruling Israel for almost two years, Uzziah’s son Jotham began to rule Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 27:1"> He was 25 years old when he started to rule, and he ruled from Jerusalem for 16 years. His mother was Jerusha, the daughter of Zadok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:4–5; 2 KINGS 15:3–4; 2 CHRONICLES 27:2"> He did many things that pleased Yahweh, like his father Uzziah had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 KINGS 12:3; 2 CHRONICLES 23:20; 2 CHRONICLES 27:3–9"> But he did not destroy the places on the tops of hills where the people worshiped Yahweh, and the people continued to burn incense there to honor Yahweh. Jotham’s workers built the Upper Gate of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 27:4–9; 2 KINGS 15:6–7"> If you want to know more about everything else that Jotham did, it is written &#91;RHQ&#93; in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Judah’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 KINGS 16:5; Isaiah 7:1; LUKE 21:28"> It was during the time that Jotham was the king that Yahweh sent King Rezin of Syria and King Pekah of Israel with their armies to attack Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:20; 1 KINGS 14:31; 2 KINGS 16:1"> Jotham died &#91;EUP&#93; and was buried where his ancestors had been buried in the part of Jerusalem called ‘The City of David’. Then his son Ahaz became the king of Judah.</span>
</div>
</div>
