<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:25; 2 KINGS 21:2–11; 2 KINGS 23:26–27"> These things happened to the people of Judah according to what Yahweh commanded. He had decided to get rid of the people of Judah because of the many sins that King Manasseh had committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 KINGS 21:16; PSALMS 106:38; NUMBERS 35:33"> Manasseh had even caused many innocent people in Jerusalem to be killed, and Yahweh would not forgive that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:8; JEREMIAH 26:1–24; JEREMIAH 22:13–17"> The other things that happened while Jehoiakim was king, and all the things that he did, are written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Judah’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:18–19; 2 CHRONICLES 36:6; JEREMIAH 36:30"> When Jehoiakim died, his son Jehoiachin became the king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:5–7; JEREMIAH 46:2; GENESIS 15:18"> The army of the king of Babylon defeated the army of Egypt, and took control of all the area that the Egyptians formerly controlled, from the brook at the border of Egypt in the south to the Euphrates River in the north. So the army of the king of Egypt did not return to attack Judah again.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehoiachin ruled Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:9; 1 CHRONICLES 3:16; JEREMIAH 37:1"> Jehoiachin was 18 years old when he became the king of Judah. His mother’s name was Nehushta; she was the daughter of a man from Jerusalem named Elnathan. Jehoiachin ruled in Jerusalem for only three months.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Jehoiachin did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil, just as his father had done.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:2; DANIEL 1:1–2"> While Jehoiachin was king, some officers of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon came along with the whole Babylonian army to Jerusalem, and they surrounded the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> While they were doing that, Nebuchadnezzar himself came to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:1; JEREMIAH 52:28; 2 KINGS 25:27"> Then King Jehoiachin and his mother and his advisors and important officers and palace officials all surrendered to the Babylonian army.<br />These thngs happened when Nebuchadnezzar had been king for eight years. He arrested Jehoiachin and took him to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 39:6; 2 KINGS 20:17; JEREMIAH 20:5"> Just as Yahweh had said would happen, Nebuchadnezzar’s soldiers took to Babylon all the valuable things from Yahweh’s temple and from the king’s palace. They cut apart all the gold items that King Solomon had put in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:28; 2 KINGS 25:12; 2 KINGS 24:16"> They took from Jerusalem to Babylon 10,000 people, including the important officials and the best soldiers and the people who made and repaired things that were made of metal. Only the very poor people were left in Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:10; JEREMIAH 22:24–28; Esther 2:6"> Nebuchadnezzar’s soldiers also took to Babylon Jehoiachin’s wives and officials, his mother, and all the important people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:28; 2 KINGS 24:14; JEREMIAH 29:2"> They also took to Babylon 7,000 of the best soldiers and 1,000 men who knew how to make and repair things that are made from metal. All of these people whom they took were strong and able to fight in wars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:1; 2 CHRONICLES 36:4; 2 KINGS 23:34"> Then the king of Babylon appointed Jehoiachin’s uncle, Mattaniah, to be the king of Judah, and he changed Mattaniah’s name to Zedekiah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Zedekiah ruled in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 KINGS 23:31; JEREMIAH 52:1–11; JEREMIAH 37:1"> When Zedekiah was twenty-one years old, he became king, and he ruled in Jerusalem for eleven years. His mother’s name was Hamutal; she was the daughter of a man named Jeremiah from Libnah town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 KINGS 23:37; JEREMIAH 24:8; EZEKIEL 21:25"> But Zedekiah did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil, just as Jehoiakim had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:13; 2 THESSALONIANS 2:9–11; JEREMIAH 38:17–21"> Yahweh was very angry. So ◄and finally/after he had been patient a long time► he expelled the people of Jerusalem and the other places in Judah and sent them to Babylon. Jerusalem was destroyed<br />That is what happened when Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.</span>
</div>
</div>
