## Hezekiah prayed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 37:14; PSALMS 91:1–2; PSALMS 123:1–4"> Hezekiah took the letter that the messengers gave him, and he read it. Then he went up to the temple and spread out the letter in front of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; 2 KINGS 5:15; DANIEL 4:34–35"> Then Hezekiah prayed, “Yahweh, the God whom to whom we Israelis belong, you are seated on your throne above the statues of creatures with wings, above the Sacred Chest. Only you are truly God. You rule all the kingdoms on this earth. You are the one who created everything on the earth and in the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 6:40; PSALMS 31:2; 2 KINGS 19:4"> So, Yahweh, please listen to what I am saying, and look at what is happening. And listen to what King Sennacherib has said to insult you, the all-powerful God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 5:26; JOB 9:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:25"> “Yahweh, it is true that the armies of the kings of Assyria have completely destroyed many nations, and ruined their land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 17:29; Isaiah 44:9–20; JEREMIAH 10:3–9"> And they have thrown the idols of those nations into fires and burned them. But that was not difficult to do, because they were not gods. They were only statues made of wood and stone, idols that were shaped by humans, and that is why they were destroyed easily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 83:18; 1 KINGS 8:43; PSALMS 67:1–2"> So now, Yahweh our God, please rescue us from the power &#91;MTY&#93; of the king of Assyria, in order that the people in all the kingdoms of the world will know that you, Yahweh, are the only one who is truly God.”</span>
</div>
