## King Amaziah of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:10; 2 CHRONICLES 25:1–4; 2 KINGS 14:15"> After Jehoash had been ruling Israel for almost two years, Joash’s son Amaziah became the king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He was 25 years old when he started to rule, and he ruled in Jerusalem for 29 years. His mother was Jehoaddin; she was from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 25:2–3; ZECHARIAH 1:4–6; 1 KINGS 11:4"> Amaziah did many things that pleased Yahweh, but he did not do as many things that pleased Yahweh as King David had done. He did some of the good things that his father Joash had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 KINGS 12:3; 2 KINGS 16:4; 2 KINGS 15:35"> But, like his father, he did not tear down the places for worshipping Yahweh at pagan shrines. The people continued to burn incense to honor Yahweh on those hills instead of in Jerusalem, the place that Yahweh had appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 25:3–4; 2 KINGS 12:20–21; NUMBERS 35:33"> As soon as he was completely in control of his kingdom, he caused to be executed the officials who had murdered his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:16; EZEKIEL 18:20; EZEKIEL 18:4"> But he did not execute their children. He obeyed what was written in the laws that God gave to Moses: “Parents must not be executed for the crimes/sins committed by their children, and children must not be executed for crimes/sins committed by their parents. People must be executed only for the sins that they themselves have committed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:13; 1 CHRONICLES 18:12; JOSHUA 15:38"> Amaziah’s soldiers killed 10,000 soldiers of the Edom people-group in the Salt Valley south of the Dead Sea, and they captured Sela city and gave it a new name, Joktheel. That is still its name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 25:17–24; 2 SAMUEL 2:14–17; 2 KINGS 14:11"> Then Amaziah sent messengers to King Jehoash of Israel, saying “Come here and let’s talk together.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 9:8–15; 1 KINGS 4:33; 2 CHRONICLES 33:11"> But King Jehoash replied to King Amaziah: “Once a thornbush growing in the mountains in Lebanon sent a message to a cedar tree, saying, ‘Give your daughter to my son in order that he may marry her.’ But a wild animal in Lebanon passed by the thornbush and trampled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:16; DEUTERONOMY 8:14; 2 CHRONICLES 32:25"> The meaning of what I am saying is that your army has defeated the army of Edom, so now you have become very proud. But you should be content with defeating the people of Edom, and allow your soldiers to stay at home. If you cause trouble by fighting against us, you will surely &#91;RHQ&#93; cause a disaster to happen to you and to your people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:38; 2 CHRONICLES 25:16; JOSHUA 15:10"> But Amaziah refused to heed Jehoash’s message. So he marched with his army to fight against the Israeli army at Beth-Shemesh city in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 18:17; 1 SAMUEL 4:10; 1 KINGS 22:36"> The Israeli army defeated the army of Judah, and all the soldiers of Judah fled and ran back home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:16; Nehemiah 12:39; ZECHARIAH 14:10"> Jehoash’s army also captured King Amaziah there, and they also marched to Jerusalem and tore down the wall that was around the city, from the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate. That was a section that was about ◄200 yards/180 meters► long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:26; 1 KINGS 7:51; 1 KINGS 15:18"> Jehoash’s soldiers seized all the gold and silver that they found, and all the utensils that were in the temple, and all the valuable things that were in the palace, and took them to Samaria. They also took to Samaria some prisoners whom they had captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:12–13; 1 KINGS 14:19–20; 2 KINGS 10:34–35"> If you want to know about all the other things that Jehoash did when he was king, including when he and his army fought against the army of King Amaziah of Judah, they are written in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:9; 2 KINGS 13:13; 1 KINGS 1:21"> Jehoash died &#91;EUP&#93;, and he was buried in Samaria, where the other kings of Israel had been buried. Then his son Jeroboam &#35;2 became the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 25:25–28; 2 KINGS 13:10; 2 KINGS 14:23"> Amaziah, the king of Judah, lived for 15 more years after Jehoash, the king of Israel, died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:41; 1 KINGS 14:29; 2 KINGS 13:8"> If you want to know more about everything else that Amaziah did, it is written &#91;RHQ&#93; in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Judah’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:31; 2 KINGS 15:25; 2 KINGS 12:20–21"> Some people in Jerusalem plotted against Amaziah, so he fled to Lachish city. But they followed him there and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:28; 2 KINGS 12:21; 2 CHRONICLES 33:20"> They took his corpse back to Jerusalem and buried it where his ancestors had been buried, in the part of Jerusalem called ‘The City of David’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 KINGS 21:24; MATTHEW 1:8–9; 2 KINGS 15:13"> Then all the people of Judah appointed Amaziah’s son Uzziah, when he was 16 years old, to be their king</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 KINGS 16:6; 1 KINGS 9:26; DEUTERONOMY 2:8"> After his father died, Uzziah’s workers captured Elath city, and it came under the control of Judah again.</span>
</div>
</div>

## King Jeroboam &#35;2 of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="HOSEA 1:1; AMOS 7:9–11; AMOS 1:1"> When Amaziah had been ruling Judah for almost 15 years, Jeroboam &#35;2 became the king of Israel. He ruled in Samaria city for 41 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:6; 2 KINGS 13:11; GENESIS 38:7"> He did many things that Yahweh considered to be evil. He did not stop committing the same kind of sins that Jeroboam, the son of Nebat, committed, sins which led the Israeli people to sin also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:17; MATTHEW 12:39–40; JOSHUA 19:13"> His soldiers conquered again some of the territory that had previously belonged to Israel, from Hamath city in the north to the Dead Sea in the south. That is what Yahweh, the God whom the Israelis worshiped, promised the prophet Jonah, the son of Amittai, from Gath-Hepher town, would happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:36; EXODUS 3:7; 2 KINGS 13:4"> That happened because Yahweh saw that the Israelis’ enemies were causing the Israelis to suffer very much. And there was absolutely no one &#91;IDM&#93; who would help them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 KINGS 13:23; 2 KINGS 13:5; ROMANS 11:2–12"> But Yahweh said that he would not destroy Israel completely, so he enabled King Jeroboam &#35;2 to rescue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:24; 1 CHRONICLES 18:5–6; 2 SAMUEL 8:6"> If you want to know more about everything else that Jeroboam &#35;2 did, about his fighting courageously in battles, and about his enabling the Israelis to capture again Damascus and Hamath cities, they are written &#91;RHQ&#93; in the scroll called ‘The History of the Kings of Israel’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 KINGS 15:8"> Jeroboam &#35;2 died &#91;EUP&#93;, and was buried where the other kings of Israel were buried, and his son Zechariah became the king.</span>
</div>
</div>
