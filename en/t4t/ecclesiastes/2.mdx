## Pleasure does not bring happiness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 12:19; ECCLESIASTES 8:15; JAMES 5:5"> Then I said to myself, “Okay, I will try to do everything that I enjoy. I will find out whether doing what I enjoy can truly enable me to be happy.” But I found out that doing that was also useless/senseless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 14:13; ECCLESIASTES 7:2–6; 1 PETER 4:2–4"> So I said to myself, “It is foolish to laugh all the time, and continually doing what I enjoy does not seem to bring any lasting benefit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:1; ECCLESIASTES 1:17; PROVERBS 31:4–5"> So, after thinking a lot about it, I decided to ◄cheer myself/cause myself to be happy► by drinking a lot of wine. So while I was still trying to be wise, I decided to do things that many people do to be happy during the short time that they are alive on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:1–12; 2 CHRONICLES 8:11; 2 CHRONICLES 26:10"> I did great things: I caused houses to be built for myself and vineyards to be planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 17:27–29; SONG OF SONGS 4:12–1; JEREMIAH 39:4"> I told my workers to make gardens and parks. Then I told them to fill the gardens with many kinds of fruit trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:14; PSALMS 1:3; JEREMIAH 17:8"> I told them to build reservoirs to store water to irrigate the fruit trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 KINGS 3:4; GENESIS 15:3; 1 KINGS 4:23"> I bought male and female slaves, and babies who later became my slaves were born in my palace. I also owned more livestock than any of the previous kings in Jerusalem had owned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 KINGS 10:10; 1 KINGS 9:28; 2 SAMUEL 19:35"> I also accumulated large amounts of silver and gold that were paid to me from the treasures of kings and rulers of provinces. I hired men and women to sing for me, and I had many ◄concubines/slave wives► who gave me much pleasure &#91;EUP&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 29:25; ECCLESIASTES 1:16; 1 KINGS 10:23"> So, I became greater than anyone else who had ever lived in Jerusalem, and I was very wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:9; ECCLESIASTES 5:18; ECCLESIASTES 3:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I got everything &#91;LIT&#93; that I &#91;SYN&#93; saw and wanted.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I did everything &#91;LIT&#93; that I thought would enable me to be happy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All those things that I &#91;SYN&#93; enjoyed were like a reward for all my hard work.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:14; 1 JOHN 2:16–17; ECCLESIASTES 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">But then I thought about all the hard work that I &#91;SYN&#93; had done to get all those things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and none of it seems to bring any lasting benefit &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It was all like chasing the wind.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Being wise also seemed useless

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:17; ECCLESIASTES 7:25; ECCLESIASTES 1:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I started to think about being wise, and also about being foolish &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I said to myself, “I certainly do not think that &#91;RHQ&#93; the next king will be able to do anything better than I can.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:8; ECCLESIASTES 7:11–12; MATTHEW 6:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I thought, “Surely it is better to be wise than to be foolish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like light is better than darkness,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 49:10; PROVERBS 17:24; ECCLESIASTES 9:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">because wise people walk in the daylight and &#91;IDM&#93; can see where they are going,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but foolish people walk in the darkness and cannot see where they are going.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But I also realized that both wise people and foolish people eventually die.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 6:8; ECCLESIASTES 1:14; ECCLESIASTES 1:2"> So I said to myself,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I am very wise, but I will die at the end of my life, like foolish people do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So ◄how has it benefited me to be very wise?/it certainly has not benefited me to be very wise &#91;RHQ&#93;►.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I do not understand why people consider that it is valuable to be wise.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:11; ECCLESIASTES 9:5; PSALMS 88:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wise people and foolish people all die.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And after we die, we will all eventually be forgotten &#91;DOU&#93;.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:11; JONAH 4:8; ECCLESIASTES 4:2"> So I hated being alive, because everything that we do here on the earth &#91;MTY&#93; distresses me. It all seems to be useless like chasing the wind.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Working hard seemed useless

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 39:6; PSALMS 49:10; LUKE 16:27–28"> I also began to hate all the hard work that I had done, because when I die, everything that I have acquired will belong to the next king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JAMES 3:17; ECCLESIASTES 3:22; ECCLESIASTES 9:13"> And ◄who/no one► knows &#91;RHQ&#93; whether he will be wise or whether he will be foolish. But even if he is foolish, he will acquire all the things that I worked very hard and wisely to get.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 43:14; JOB 17:11–15; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:8–10"> I thought about all the hard work that I had done. It seemed useless, and I became depressed/discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:17–18; 2 CHRONICLES 31:20–21; 2 CHRONICLES 34:2"> Some people work wisely and skillfully, using the things that they have learned. But when they die, they leave everything, and someone who has not worked hard acquires those things. And that also seemed to be senseless and caused me to be discouraged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 127:2; ECCLESIASTES 1:3; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> So, it seems that people do not &#91;RHQ&#93; get much for all the hard work that they do and for worrying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 14:1; JOB 5:7; PSALMS 6:6–7"> Every day the work that they do causes them to experience pain and to be worried. And during the night, their minds are not able to rest. That also is very frustrating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:22; ECCLESIASTES 8:15; 1 TIMOTHY 6:17"> So I decided that the best thing that we can do is to enjoy what we eat and drink, and also enjoy our work. And I realized that those things are what God intends for us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:1–12; 1 KINGS 4:21–24"> There is absolutely no one &#91;RHQ&#93; who is able to enjoy those things if God does not give those things to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:22; JOB 27:16–17; PROVERBS 28:8"> God enables those who please him to be wise, to know many things, and to enjoy many things. But if sinful people work hard and become rich, God can take their money away from them and give it to those who please him. But that also is something that is difficult for me to understand. Their working hard seems useless, like chasing the wind.</span>
</div>
