## Everyone dies

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:3; JOHN 10:27–30; 1 PETER 1:5"> I thought about all those things, and I concluded that God controls what happens to everyone, even those who are wise and those who are righteous. No one knows whether others will love them or whether they will hate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MALACHI 3:18; ECCLESIASTES 2:14–16; MALACHI 3:5"> But we know that some time in the future we will all die;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">it does not matter whether we act righteously or wickedly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whether we are good or whether we are bad,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whether we are acceptable for worshiping God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or whether we have done things to cause us to be unacceptable;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it does not matter if we offer sacrifices to God or if we do not;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it does not matter if we do what we have promised God that we will do or if we do not;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we all die.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The same thing will happen to good people and to sinful people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to those who solemnly promise to do things for God and to those who are afraid to make such promises.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:9; ECCLESIASTES 8:11; ECCLESIASTES 1:17"> It seems wrong that the same thing happens to everyone on this earth: Everyone dies &#91;EUP&#93;. Furthermore, people’s inner beings are full of evil. People do foolish things while they are alive, and then they die and join those who are already dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 14:7–12; Isaiah 38:18; LUKE 16:26–29"> While we are alive, we confidently expect that good things will happen to us. We despise dogs, but it is better to be a dog that is alive than to be a majestic lion that is dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 26:14; ECCLESIASTES 1:11; PSALMS 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">We who are alive know that some day we will die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but dead people do not know anything.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Dead people do not receive any more rewards,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and people soon forget them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 146:3–4; ECCLESIASTES 6:12; JOB 3:17–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">While they were alive, they loved some people, they hated some people, they envied some people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but that all ends when they die.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will never again be a part of anything that happens here on the earth.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:12–13; DEUTERONOMY 12:7; ECCLESIASTES 10:19"> So I say, be joyful &#91;DOU&#93; while you eat your food and drink your wine, because that is what God wants you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="REVELATION 19:14; REVELATION 3:4–5; REVELATION 19:8"> Wear nice &#91;MTY&#93; clothes and make your face look nice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 5:18–19; ECCLESIASTES 2:10; MALACHI 2:15"> Enjoy living with your wife whom you love, all during the time that God has given to you to be alive here on this earth. And even though it is difficult to understand why many things happen, enjoy doing the work that you do here on this earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:23; EPHESIANS 5:16; ROMANS 12:11"> Whatever you are able to do, do it with all your energy, because some time you will die, and in the place of the dead where you are going, no one works or plans to do anything or knows anything or is wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:23; PROVERBS 21:30–31; LAMENTATIONS 3:37–38"> I have seen something else here on the earth:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">The person who runs fastest does not always win the race,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the strongest soldiers do not always win the battle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the wisest people do not always have food,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the smartest people do not always become rich,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and people who have studied a lot are not always ◄honored/treated very specially► by others;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we cannot always control what things will happen to us and where they will happen.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:3; LUKE 21:34–36; ECCLESIASTES 8:5–7"> No one knows when he will die &#91;EUP&#93;;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">fish are cruelly caught in a net,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and birds are caught in snares/traps;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">similarly &#91;SIM&#93;, people experience disasters</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">at times when they do not expect them to happen.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Being wise is better than being foolish

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 8:16; ECCLESIASTES 7:15; ECCLESIASTES 6:1"> Once I saw something that a wise man did that impressed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 20:15–22; 2 KINGS 6:24–20"> There was a small town, where only a few people lived. The army of a great king came to that town and surrounded it. They built dirt ramps up against the walls in order to climb up and attack the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 4:13; GENESIS 40:23; Esther 6:2–3"> In that town there was a man who was poor but very wise. Because of doing what that man suggested, the town was saved; but people soon forgot about him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:19; PROVERBS 24:5; PROVERBS 21:22"> So I realized that although being wise is better than being strong, if you are poor, no one will appreciate what you do, and people will soon forget what you said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JAMES 3:17–18; PROVERBS 28:23; JAMES 1:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Speaking quietly what is very wise is much more sensible</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than a king shouting to foolish people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:11–12; ECCLESIASTES 9:16; JOSHUA 7:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Being wise is more useful</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than a lot of weapons;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but if you do one foolish thing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is possible that because of doing that, you will ruin all the good things that you have done.</span></span>
</div>
