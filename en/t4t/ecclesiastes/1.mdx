## Everything is mysterious

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:8–10; ECCLESIASTES 1:12; ECCLESIASTES 7:27"> I am Solomon, the son of King David. I rule in Jerusalem and people call me ‘The ◄Preacher/Religious Teacher►’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 144:4; ROMANS 8:20; ECCLESIASTES 5:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I say that everything is mysterious;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">everything is hard for me to understand;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is difficult to understand why everything happens.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:9; ECCLESIASTES 5:16; ECCLESIASTES 2:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">◄What do people gain from all the work that they do here on the earth?/It seems that people gain no lasting benefit from all the work that they do here on the earth.► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 104:5; ZECHARIAH 1:5; ECCLESIASTES 6:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Each year old people die and babies are born,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the earth never changes.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 19:4–6; HABAKKUK 3:11; PSALMS 42:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Each morning the sun rises, and each evening it sets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then it hurries around to where it started from.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 3:8; ECCLESIASTES 11:5; PSALMS 107:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wind blows south,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then it turns around to start blowing towards the north.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It goes around and around in circles.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOB 38:10–11; PSALMS 104:6–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the streams flow into the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but the sea is never full.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The water returns to the sky, and when it rains, the water returns to the rivers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and it flows again to the sea.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 27:20; MATTHEW 11:28; ECCLESIASTES 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everything is boring,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that we do not even want to talk about it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We &#91;SYN&#93; see things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but we always want to see more.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We &#91;SYN&#93; hear things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but we always want to hear more.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:15; ECCLESIASTES 6:10; ECCLESIASTES 2:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everything continues to be the same as it has always been;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">things that happen have happened previously, and they will happen again.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">What has been done before will be done again.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There is nothing really new in this world &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ACTS 7:51; LUKE 17:26–30; MATTHEW 23:30–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sometimes people say, “Look at this! This is something new &#91;RHQ&#93;!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But it has existed previously;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it existed before we were born.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:16; PSALMS 9:6; ECCLESIASTES 9:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">People do not remember the things that happened long ago,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and in the future, people will not remember what we are doing now.</span></span>

## What the religious teacher found out about wisdom

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:1; 1 KINGS 4:1–19"> I, the Religious Teacher, have been the king of Israel for many years, ruling in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:10; GENESIS 3:19; 1 TIMOTHY 4:15"> By being wise, I concentrated on understanding everything that was being done on the earth &#91;MTY&#93;. But I found out that God causes all of us to experience things that cause us to be unhappy/miserable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:11; ECCLESIASTES 2:17; ECCLESIASTES 2:26"> It seems that nothing that happens on the earth really enables us to do anything useful. It is like &#91;MET&#93; chasing the wind.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:12–13; Isaiah 40:4; ECCLESIASTES 3:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many things that are crooked cannot be caused to become straight;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we cannot count things that do not exist.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:9; 1 KINGS 4:30; 1 KINGS 10:7"> I said to myself, “Hey, I am wiser than any of the kings that ruled in Jerusalem before I became the king. I am wiser and I know more than any of them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:3; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:21; ECCLESIASTES 1:13–14"> So I determined to learn more about being wise and to learn about knowing about many things, and also to learn about doing things that are very foolish &#91;DOU&#93;. But I found out that trying to understand those things was also useless, like chasing the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOB 28:28; ECCLESIASTES 12:12–13; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:18–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wiser I became, the more disappointed I became.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The more things I knew about, the sadder I became.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
