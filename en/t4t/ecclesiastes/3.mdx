## There is a right time for everything

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:17; MATTHEW 16:3; ECCLESIASTES 8:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is a right/correct time for everything,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a time for everything that we do in this world.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:27; JOB 14:5; MATTHEW 15:13"> <span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to be born, and there is a time to die.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to plant seeds, and there is a time to harvest crops.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HOSEA 6:1–2; 1 SAMUEL 2:6; JEREMIAH 45:4"> <span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to kill people, and there is a time to heal people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to tear things down, and there is a time to build things.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 12:15; PSALMS 30:5; JAMES 4:9"> <span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to cry, and there is a time to laugh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to mourn, and there is a time to dance joyfully.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 KINGS 3:25; JOSHUA 10:27; JOSHUA 4:3–9"> <span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to throw away stones from a field, and there is a time to gather stones to build walls/houses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to embrace people, and there is a time to not embrace people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:7–8; LUKE 9:24–25; MARK 8:35–37"> <span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to search for things, and there is a time to stop searching for things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to keep/save things, and there is a time to throw things away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="AMOS 5:13; MICAH 7:5; LAMENTATIONS 3:28"> <span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to tear our old clothes, and there is a time to mend clothes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There is a time to say nothing, and there is a time when we should speak.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:25; EPHESIANS 5:28–29; LUKE 14:26"> <span className="pl pl-2">There is a time when we should love things that people do, and there is a time when we should hate things that people do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There is a time for war, and there is a time for peace.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:3; ECCLESIASTES 5:16; PROVERBS 14:23"> ◄What do people gain from all the work that they do?/It seems that people gain very little from all the work that they do &#91;RHQ&#93;►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:13–14; GENESIS 3:19; ECCLESIASTES 2:26"> I have seen the work that God has given people to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 11:33; ROMANS 1:19–20; JOB 11:7"> God has appointed a time that is right/correct for everything to happen. He has also caused people to realize that there are things that will endure forever. But in spite of that, no one can completely understand everything that God has done, from the time that he starts doing things until he finishes them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 37:3; ECCLESIASTES 3:22; 1 TIMOTHY 6:18"> I know that the best &#91;LIT&#93; thing for us people to do is to rejoice and to do good things all during the time that we are alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 128:2; ECCLESIASTES 9:7; ECCLESIASTES 2:24"> And I also know that everyone should eat and drink, and enjoy the work that they do. Those are things that God gives to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JAMES 1:17; PSALMS 33:11; ROMANS 11:36"> I also know that what God does endures forever. No one can add to what God does, and no one can take away from the things that God does. God does those things in order that people would revere him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:9–10; ECCLESIASTES 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Things that exist now have already existed previously,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and things that will happen in the future have already happened previously;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">God causes the same things to happen many times.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Injustices in the world

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 4:1; ECCLESIASTES 5:8; PSALMS 94:21–22"> Furthermore, I saw that on this earth &#91;MTY&#93;,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">even in the courts where we expect judges to make right decisions about what people had done,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they did many wicked &#91;DOU&#93; things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:10; ECCLESIASTES 12:14; MATTHEW 16:27"> So I said to myself &#91;SYN&#93;, “God will judge both righteous people and wicked people;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is a time for him to do that,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because there is a time for him to do everything.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 73:22; PSALMS 49:12; HEBREWS 9:27"> And regarding humans, I also said to myself, “God is testing us, to show us that in one way people are no different than animals,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 49:12; PSALMS 49:20; PSALMS 89:47–48"> because what happens to people happens to animals. Animals die, and people die. We all must breathe to remain alive. With regard to that, people have no advantage over animals, so I have a difficult time understanding that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 3:19; JOB 34:15; ECCLESIASTES 12:7"> People and animals all die and are buried. We are all made of soil, and when we die, our corpses become soil again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:7; JOHN 14:3; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:1"> No one knows &#91;RHQ&#93; for sure that when we die, our souls/spirits go up to heaven and the souls/spirits of animals go down to the place where the dead are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:24; ECCLESIASTES 3:11–12; ECCLESIASTES 10:14"> So I concluded that the best thing for us people to do is to be happy about the work that we do, because that is what God has given to us. I say that because no one of us &#91;RHQ&#93; knows what happens to us after we die.</span>
</div>
