## Be careful about making promises

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JAMES 1:19; HEBREWS 12:28–29; PROVERBS 15:8"> When you enter God’s temple, you should be sure to listen carefully. Doing that is better than offering sacrifices to God and then not obeying him, which is foolish.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:7; PROVERBS 10:19; Isaiah 55:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Think carefully before you speak &#91;MTY&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or before you promise God that you will do something &#91;IDM&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not forget that God is in heaven and he is all-powerful</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you are here on the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so think carefully before you make any promises to God.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:2; PROVERBS 10:19; ECCLESIASTES 10:12–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you are continually thinking and worrying about things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will have bad dreams about them and not rest well.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the more you talk,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the more likely it will be that you will say things that are foolish.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 30:2; DEUTERONOMY 23:21–23; PSALMS 76:11"> When you solemnly promise God that you will do something, do not be foolish by delaying in doing it, because God is not pleased with foolish people. Do all the things that you promise God that you will do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 5:4; DEUTERONOMY 23:22; PROVERBS 20:25"> It is better to not promise anything than to promise to do something, and then not doing it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JAMES 1:26; 2 JOHN 1:8; JAMES 3:2"> Do not sin by promising to do something and then not doing it. And when you promise God to do something and do not do it, do not say to God’s priest that it was a mistake for you to promise to do that. If you do that, God will certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; become very angry with you, and he will take from you everything that you &#91;SYN&#93; have worked to acquire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:13; MATTHEW 12:36; ECCLESIASTES 8:12"> Promising to do something and not doing it is like &#91;SIM&#93; a dream that has no value; instead, revere God by doing what you promised him that you would do.</span>
</div>

## Officials cheat each other

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 12:5; ECCLESIASTES 4:1; ECCLESIASTES 3:16"> Do not be surprised if you see poor people being ◄oppressed/treated cruelly► by powerful/influential people, or if you see judges making unjust decisions throughout the land. That happens because the people who do that are supervised and cheated by more important officials &#91;DOU&#93;,</span>

## The futility of wealth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 6:10; MATTHEW 6:24; LUKE 12:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everyone who tries to get as much money as they can</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">will never think that they have enough.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will never be satisfied with the money that they have.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">That also is senseless.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 JOHN 2:16; HABAKKUK 2:13; PSALMS 119:36–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">The more money that we have,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the more people want us to spend our money to buy things for them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So people who have a lot of money do not &#91;RHQ&#93; benefit from it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they see &#91;MTY&#93; it when they get it, but it soon ◄disappears/is completely spent►.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:24; PSALMS 4:8; JEREMIAH 31:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who work hard sleep peacefully at night,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even if they do not have much food to eat.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But rich people do not sleep well,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they worry about their money.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 6:1–2; LUKE 18:22–23; GENESIS 19:14"> I have seen another terrible thing that happens here on the earth &#91;MTY&#93;:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">People save up all their money and become rich,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they are not helped by saving a lot of money,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:19–20; Haggai 2:16–17; PROVERBS 23:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">because something happens that causes their money to be gone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and when they die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is no money for their children to get/inherit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 49:17; JOB 1:21; 1 TIMOTHY 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">When we are born,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we do not bring anything with us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and when we die,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we take nothing with us</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from all that we have earned by our working hard.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:29; ECCLESIASTES 1:3; JOHN 6:27"> That also seems senseless.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">People bring nothing into the world when they are born,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they leave this world taking nothing with them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have worked hard,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they receive no lasting benefit &#91;MET&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 127:2; EZEKIEL 4:16–17; PSALMS 90:7–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Furthermore, rich people are always miserable &#91;MET&#93; and sad,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and depressed/discouraged and often sick and ◄resentful/thinking that what has happened to them is unfair►.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:24; ECCLESIASTES 3:12–13; ECCLESIASTES 3:22"> So, the best thing for people to do here on the earth during the few years that God allows them to be alive is to eat and drink and to enjoy their work, because those are the things that God has given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:18; ECCLESIASTES 3:13; ECCLESIASTES 6:2"> If people are rich and have a lot of possessions, and are able to ◄enjoy/be happy with► the things that they have and to enjoy their work, those things are also gifts from God.</span>
