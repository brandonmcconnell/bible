## Obey the king

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:43; TITUS 3:1; PROVERBS 24:21"> You solemnly promised God that you would obey what the king commands, so do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 10:4; ACTS 5:8–9; DANIEL 4:35"> Do not quickly disobey the king. And do not join those who want to rebel against him, because the king will do what he wants to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOB 9:12; ROMANS 13:1–4; DANIEL 4:35"> We need to obey what the king says more than we need to obey what anyone else says; no one can say to the king, “◄Why are you doing that?/You should not be doing that.► &#91;RHQ&#93;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:14; 1 PETER 3:13–14; PHILIPPIANS 1:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you obey what the king commands,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will not harm you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So be wise, and know the correct/right time to do things and the right way to do them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:1; ECCLESIASTES 3:17; ECCLESIASTES 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Although people experience many troubles/difficulties,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there is a right/correct time to do things, and there is a right/correct way to do them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 10:14; ECCLESIASTES 9:12; PROVERBS 24:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">No one knows what will happen in the future,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so there is no one &#91;RHQ&#93; who can tell us what is going to happen.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 89:48; JOB 14:5; HEBREWS 9:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">We cannot control the wind,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and we cannot control when we will die.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Soldiers are not permitted to go home during a battle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and evil people will not be saved by doing what is evil.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:8; ECCLESIASTES 4:7–8; ECCLESIASTES 5:13"> I thought about all those things, and I thought about all &#91;HYP&#93; the other things that happen on this earth. I saw that sometimes people cause those whom they control to suffer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:5; ECCLESIASTES 2:16; PROVERBS 10:7"> I also saw that sometimes after evil people die, they are highly honored at their funerals by the people in the cities where they had done evil deeds. It seemed to be difficult to understand why that happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 26:10; PSALMS 50:21–22; ROMANS 2:4–5"> If evil people are not immediately punished, it causes other people &#91;SYN&#93; to also want to do evil things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 37:11; 2 PETER 2:9; LUKE 1:50"> But even if sinful people commit 100 crimes, and even if they live for a long time, I know that things will go better for those who greatly respect and revere God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 6:12; Isaiah 3:11; JOB 14:2"> I also know that things will not go well go for those who are evil, because they do not revere God. Shadows do not last a long time. Similarly &#91;SIM&#93;, evil people will not live a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:15; ECCLESIASTES 2:14; MALACHI 3:15"> Another thing that sometimes happens on this earth is that bad things happen to righteous people, and good things happen to evil people. It is difficult to understand why that happens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:24; ECCLESIASTES 5:18; ECCLESIASTES 3:12–13"> So I decided that I would recommend that people be happy while they are alive, because the best thing that people can do here on this earth is to eat and drink and be happy. Enjoying doing those things will help people while they do their work, all the time that God has given to them to remain alive here on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:13; PSALMS 127:2; ECCLESIASTES 2:23"> I thought about being wise and about people who work very hard, working day and night and not taking time to sleep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:11; PSALMS 73:16; ROMANS 11:33"> Then I thought about everything &#91;HYP&#93; that God has done, and I realized that no one can understand everything that happens here on this earth. Truly, people are not able to fully understand everything that God understands, even if they try hard to do that. Even if wise people claim that they understand it all, they cannot.</span>
</div>
