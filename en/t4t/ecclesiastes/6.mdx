<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:13"> I have seen something else here on this earth that troubles people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 5:19; PSALMS 17:14; PSALMS 73:7"> God enables some people to get a lot of money and possessions and to be honored; they have everything &#91;LIT&#93; that they want. But God sometimes does not allow them to continue to enjoy those things. Someone else gets them and enjoys them. That seems senseless and unfair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOB 3:16; JEREMIAH 22:19; ECCLESIASTES 4:3"> Someone might have 100 children and live for many years. But if he is not able to enjoy the things that he has acquired, and if he is not buried properly after he dies, I say that a child that is dead when it is born is more fortunate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 109:13"> That dead baby’s birth is meaningless; it does not even have a name. It goes directly to the place where there is only darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 58:8; JOB 14:1; JOB 3:10–13"> It does not live to see the sun or know anything. But it finds more rest than rich people do who are alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:27; ECCLESIASTES 3:20; JOB 30:23"> Even if people could live for 2,000 years, if they do not enjoy the things that God gives to them, it would have been better for them never to have been born.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">All people who live a long time certainly &#91;RHQ&#93; all go to the same place— to the grave.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 6:27; PROVERBS 16:26; 1 TIMOTHY 6:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">People work hard to earn enough money to buy food to eat &#91;MTY&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but often they never get enough to eat.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:1; ECCLESIASTES 2:14–16; PSALMS 101:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">So it seems that &#91;RHQ&#93; wise people do not receive more lasting benefits</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than foolish people do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And it seems that &#91;RHQ&#93; poor people do not benefit from knowing how to conduct their lives.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 1:14; JOB 31:7; ECCLESIASTES 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is better to enjoy the things that we already have &#91;MTY&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">than to constantly want more things;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">continually wanting more things is senseless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like chasing the wind.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 9:32; Isaiah 45:9–10; JOB 40:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the things that exist on the earth have been given names.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And everyone knows what people are like,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so it is useless to argue with someone (OR, with God)</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who is stronger than we are.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 4:16; ECCLESIASTES 5:7; ECCLESIASTES 2:3–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The more that we talk,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the more often we say things that are senseless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so it certainly does not &#91;RHQ&#93; benefit us to talk a lot.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JAMES 4:14; JOB 14:2; ECCLESIASTES 3:22"> We live for only a short time; we disappear like &#91;SIM&#93; a shadow disappears in the sunlight. No one &#91;RHQ&#93; knows what is best for us while we are alive, and no one &#91;RHQ&#93; knows what will happen to us after we die &#91;EUP&#93;.</span>
</div>
