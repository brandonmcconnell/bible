## Yahweh will punish Syria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; ZECHARIAH 9:1; AMOS 1:3–5"> I received this message from Yahweh about Damascus the capital of Syria:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Listen carefully! <span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:5; JEREMIAH 7:33; NUMBERS 32:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">The towns near Aroer city will be abandoned.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Flocks of sheep will eat grass in the streets and lie down there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there will be no one to chase them away.</span></span>

## The destruction of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 10:16; DEUTERONOMY 32:15–27; Isaiah 24:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">“At that time, Israel will become insignificant.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It will be like &#91;MET&#93; a fat person who has become very thin.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:30; JOEL 3:13; JEREMIAH 51:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">The entire land will be like &#91;SIM&#93; a field where the harvesters have cut all the grain;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there will be nothing left,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; the fields in the Rephaim Valley after all the crops have been harvested.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 24:13; DEUTERONOMY 4:27; ROMANS 9:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Only a few of the Israeli people will remain &#91;MET&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; the few olives that remain on the top of a tree after the workers have caused all the other olives to fall to the ground.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There will be only two or three olives in the top branches,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or four or five olives on the other branches.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That is what the Commander of the armies of angels says.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MICAH 7:7; HOSEA 6:1; HOSEA 3:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then, at that time, you people of Israel will ◄turn for help to/look up to► God, your creator,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the Holy One of Israel.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 27:9; Isaiah 2:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will no longer seek to get help from your idols</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or worship the idols that you have made with your own hands &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will never again bow down in front of the poles where you worship the goddess Asherah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You will never again worship at the shrines that you have built for burning incense.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="AMOS 3:11–15; Isaiah 27:10; Isaiah 24:1–12"> The largest cities in Israel will be abandoned, like the land that the Hiv and Amor people-groups abandoned (OR, like the forests that the Canaan people-group abandoned) when the Israelis attacked them long ago.<br /><span className="pl pl-2">No one will live there.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 106:21; DEUTERONOMY 32:18; Isaiah 26:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">That will happen because you have stopped worshiping God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who is like &#91;MET&#93; a huge rock under which you can be safe.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have forgotten that he is the one who can hide/protect you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So, now you plant very nice grapevines</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and even plant very expensive ones that come from other countries.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOB 4:8; HOSEA 8:7; GALATIANS 6:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">But even if they sprout leaves on the day that you plant them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and even if they produce blossoms on that same morning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">at harvest time, there will not be any grapes for you to pick.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All that you will get is a lot of agony/misery.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:23; LUKE 21:25; PSALMS 18:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen! The armies of many nations will roar like the sea roars.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It will sound like the noise of crashing waves.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 9:5; PSALMS 1:4; Isaiah 41:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">But even though their loud roaring will be like the sound of crashing waves,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when Yahweh rebukes them, they will run far away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will flee like &#91;SIM&#93; chaff on the hills scatters when the wind blows,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like tumbleweeds scatter when a windstorm blows.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 KINGS 19:35; PROVERBS 22:23; JEREMIAH 13:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">And, even though you people of Israel will be terrified,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the morning your enemies will all be gone/dead.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That is what will happen to those who invade our land and then steal our possessions.</span></span>
