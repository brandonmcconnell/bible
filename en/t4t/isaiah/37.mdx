## Hezekiah’s prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 7:8–9; DANIEL 9:3–4; JAMES 5:13"> And then Hezekiah prayed this:</span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 6:40; PSALMS 17:6; Isaiah 37:4"> So, Yahweh, please listen to what I am saying, and look at what is happening! And listen to what Sennacherib has said to insult you, the all-powerful God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NAHUM 2:11–12; 2 KINGS 15:29; 2 KINGS 17:6"> Yahweh, it is true that the armies of the kings of Assyria have completely destroyed many nations and ruined their land.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 46:10; 1 KINGS 18:36–37; PSALMS 83:17–18"> So now, Yahweh our God, please rescue us from the power &#91;MTY&#93; of the King of Assyria, in order that the people in all the kingdoms of the world will know that you, Yahweh, are the only one who is truly God.”</span>
