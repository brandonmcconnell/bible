<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="REVELATION 4:8; EZEKIEL 1:11; PSALMS 104:4"> Above him were standing several winged creatures. Each of them had six wings. <span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 5:8–9; JAMES 3:6–10; JEREMIAH 9:3–8"> Then I said, “Terrible things will happen to me, because everything that I say &#91;MTY&#93; is sinful, and I live among people who constantly say &#91;MTY&#93; sinful things. <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 4:10–13; ACTS 26:16–17; EPHESIANS 3:8"> Then I heard Yahweh asking, “Whom shall I send to be a messenger to my people? Who will go and speak for us?” I replied, “I will. Send me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:14–15; LUKE 8:10; MARK 4:12"> Then he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Okay, go, and say to the Israeli people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘You will listen carefully to what I say, but you will not understand it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will look very carefully at the things that I am doing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you will not understand them.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:15; ACTS 28:27; JEREMIAH 5:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">What you(sg) say will cause these people to become stubborn &#91;IDM&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it will cause them to not be able to hear what I say and see what I do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">As a result, they will not see what I want them to see or hear what I want them to hear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will not understand it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will not turn to me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and be saved from being punished.”</span></span>
