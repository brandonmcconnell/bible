## Warnings to Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HOSEA 7:5; Isaiah 28:7; 2 KINGS 14:25–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Terrible things will happen to Samaria city, the capital of Israel!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It is on a hill above a fertile valley;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the people who live there, who get drunk by drinking too much wine, are very proud;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is a beautiful and glorious city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but some day that beauty will disappear like &#91;MET&#93; a flower that wilts and dries up.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:30; NAHUM 1:8; EZEKIEL 13:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to this: Yahweh will cause a great army to attack Samaria.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their soldiers will be like &#91;SIM&#93; a huge hailstorm or a very strong wind;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will be everywhere, like the water of a huge flood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will smash to the ground the buildings in Samaria.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 26:6; HEBREWS 10:29; REVELATION 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of Samaria are proud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but everything that the drunks who live there think is wonderful/glorious will be trampled on by their enemies.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NAHUM 3:12; MICAH 7:1; Isaiah 28:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yes, Samaria is beautiful, set on a hill above a fertile valley, but that beauty will disappear</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;MET&#93; a flower that wilts and dries up.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Whenever someone sees a good fig at the beginning of the season when figs become ripe, he quickly picks and eats it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">similarly, when the enemies of Israel see all the beautiful things in Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will quickly conquer the city and take away all those things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 PETER 5:4; Isaiah 62:3; Isaiah 60:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">At that time, the Commander of the armies of angels will be like a glorious wreath of flowers for us Israeli people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who are still alive after being exiled.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 18:32–34; JOHN 5:30; JOSHUA 1:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will cause our judges to want to do what is fair/just</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when they decide people’s cases.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will enable the soldiers who stand at the city gates</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to strongly defend the city when our enemies attack it.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 56:10–12; LUKE 21:34; Isaiah 19:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now, our leaders stagger/stumble</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they have drunk a lot of wine and other alcoholic drinks.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The priests and prophets also stagger</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of drinking a lot of wine and other alcoholic drinks.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are not able to think right;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they see visions but they cannot understand what they mean;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are unable to decide things correctly.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:26; PROVERBS 26:11; HABAKKUK 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">All their tables are covered with their vomit;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">filth is everywhere.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 131:2; JEREMIAH 6:10; JOHN 12:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">They ridicule Yahweh saying, “Who does he think that he is teaching?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Why is he talking to us like this?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Does he think that we are little children</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who have recently been weaned?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 28:13; 2 TIMOTHY 3:7; JEREMIAH 11:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">He continually tells us, ‘Do this, do that;’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">first he tells us one rule, then another rule,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he tells us only one line at a time.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 14:21; DEUTERONOMY 28:49; Isaiah 33:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">So now, Yahweh will need to force them to listen to Assyrians</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">speaking to them in a language that they do not understand.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:28–29; Isaiah 30:15; JEREMIAH 6:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh told his people long ago,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“This is a place where you can rest;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you are exhausted from all your travels through the desert,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you will be able to rest in this land.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But they refused to pay attention to what he said.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:14; Isaiah 6:9–10; 2 PETER 3:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">So Yahweh continues to tell the people of Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">one line at a time, “Do this, do that,”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">first one rule and then another rule.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But because of their ignoring what God said, they will be attacked and defeated;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will be wounded and snared and captured.</span></span>
</div>

## Warnings to Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 29:20; Isaiah 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of what will happen in Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you rulers in Jerusalem who make fun of me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">listen to this message from Yahweh:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; EZEKIEL 13:22; AMOS 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You boast saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We have made an alliance with the leaders of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so we will not be killed in battles;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we will never go to the place where the dead people are.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When the army of Assyria attacks us, they will never defeat us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because we have made an agreement with Egypt to protect us!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But that agreement consists of a lot of lies &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 118:22; ACTS 4:11–12; 1 PETER 2:6–8"> Therefore, Yahweh our Lord says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Listen to this! I am going to place in Jerusalem someone who is like &#91;MET&#93; a foundation stone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he is like a stone that has been tested to determine if it is solid.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will be like a valuable cornerstone around which it will be safe to build a house;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and whoever trusts in him will never be disappointed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 KINGS 21:13; AMOS 7:7–9; REVELATION 16:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will test you people of Jerusalem to find out if you will act justly and righteously</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;MET&#93; someone uses a plumb line to determine if a wall is straight and vertical.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But because your agreement with Egypt to protect you was made by leaders lying to each other and deceiving each other,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will be defeated and taken away from your country</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">by an army that will come against you like &#91;MET&#93; a flood.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:7; Isaiah 8:10; Isaiah 28:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will annul/destroy the agreement that you made with the leaders of Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You thought that because of that agreement you would not be killed, and you would not go to the place where the dead are.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But when the vast army of Assyria overwhelms you like a flood,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will trample you into the ground.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:16; Isaiah 36:22; 2 KINGS 24:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their soldiers will come during the morning, at noontime, and at night,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will carry you all away.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And when you understand this message,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will be terrified.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 64:6; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:18–31; ROMANS 9:30–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have heard people say, “Your bed is very short, you will not be able to sleep in it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">your blankets are very narrow; they will not cover you!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">That means for you that your agreement with Egypt is not going to save you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 14:11; JOSHUA 10:12; 2 SAMUEL 5:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will come and cause you to be defeated;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will do to you like he did to the army of Philistia at Perizim Mountain</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and like he did to the Amor people-group at Gibeon Valley.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What he will do will be very strange and unusual &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 10:22–23; Isaiah 24:1–23; MATTHEW 27:44"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Commander of the armies of angels has told me that he is going to destroy the entire land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So do not ridicule what I say any more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because if you do that</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will punish you even more severely.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; JEREMIAH 22:29; REVELATION 2:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen &#91;IDM&#93; to what I say;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">pay attention carefully.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:3; HOSEA 10:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">When a farmer plows some ground, does he never plant seeds &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Does he continue to plow it and never plant anything &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EZEKIEL 4:9; MATTHEW 23:23; EXODUS 9:31–32"> <span className="pl pl-1">No, he makes the ground very level,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then he plants seeds—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">dill and cumin and wheat and barley.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He plants each kind of seed in the correct manner.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 31:3–6; EXODUS 36:2; JOB 39:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">He does that because God has taught him the correct way to do it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 41:15; Isaiah 27:7–8; JEREMIAH 46:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Farmers never thresh caraway/dill with a heavy sledge/club;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">instead, they beat it only with a stick.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Farmers never thresh cumin by driving a cart over it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">instead, they hit it only with a rod.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 9:9–10; MATTHEW 3:12; JOHN 12:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">And grain for baking bread is crushed easily,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so the farmers do not continue to pound it for a long time.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They sometimes cause their horses to pull a cart over it to thresh it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but doing that does not grind the grain.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ROMANS 11:33; JEREMIAH 32:19; JOB 5:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Commander of the armies of angels</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">gives us wonderful advice about how to do things; he causes us to be very wise.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So what the farmers do is very smart/wise, but what your leaders are doing is very stupid.</span></span>
