## Hezekiah’s sickness and his recovery

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 38:1–8; 2 CHRONICLES 32:24; 2 KINGS 20:1–11"> About that time, Hezekiah became very ill and was close to dying. So I went to see him. And I gave him this message: “This is what Yahweh says: ‘You should tell the people in your palace what you want them to do after you die, because you will not recover from this illness. You are going to die’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:6; PSALMS 91:15; 1 KINGS 8:30"> Hezekiah turned his face toward the wall, and prayed this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 5:7; PSALMS 18:20–27; HEBREWS 6:10"> “Yahweh, do not forget that I have always served you very faithfully, and I have done things that pleased you!” Then Hezekiah started to cry loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> I left his room, but Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 34:5–6; 1 JOHN 5:14–15; REVELATION 7:17"> “Go back to Hezekiah and tell him that this is what I, the God to whom your ancestor King David belonged, say: ‘I have heard what you prayed, and I have seen you crying. So listen: I will enable you to live 15 years more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; Isaiah 31:4–5; Isaiah 12:6"> And, I will rescue you and this city from the power &#91;MTY&#93; of the King of Assyria. I will defend this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JUDGES 6:37–39; Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 7:11–14"> And this is what I will do to prove that I will do what I have just now promised. I will cause the shadow of the sun to move ten steps backward on the sundial that was built by King Ahaz.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 KINGS 20:9–11; JOSHUA 10:12–14; MATTHEW 16:1"> So the shadow of the sun on the sundial moved backward ten steps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 18:1; EXODUS 15:1–21; JOB 5:18"> When King Hezekiah was almost well again, he wrote this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PSALMS 102:24; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:9; PSALMS 107:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I thought to myself, “Is it necessary for me to die and go to the place where the dead people are</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">during this time of my life when I am still strong?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Is Yahweh going to rob me of the remaining years that I should live?”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 27:13; PSALMS 116:8–9; PSALMS 31:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said, “I will not see Yahweh again</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in this world where people are alive.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will not see my friends again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or be with others who now are alive in this world.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 73:14; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:1; HEBREWS 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as if my life has been taken away</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; a tent whose pegs have been pulled up by a shepherd and taken away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My time to live has been cut short,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a piece of cloth that a weaver cuts and rolls up after he has finished weaving a cloth.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Suddenly, it seemed that my life was ending.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 51:8; DANIEL 6:24; PSALMS 50:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I waited patiently all during the night,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but my pain was as though &#91;MET&#93; I was being torn apart by lions.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It seemed that my life was finished.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:11; PSALMS 69:3; PSALMS 123:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I was delirious, and I chirped like a swift or a swallow,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and moaned like a dove.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My eyes became tired looking up toward heaven for help.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I cried out, Lord, help me, because I am distressed!’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 KINGS 21:27; JOB 7:11; JOB 10:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">But there was really nothing &#91;RHQ&#93; that I could say and ask him to reply to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because it was Yahweh who sent this illness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So now I will live humbly during my remaining years</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because I am very anguished.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:10–11; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:32; PSALMS 119:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, the sufferings (OR, the promises) that you give are good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because what you do and what you say bring new life and health to us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And you restore/heal me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and allow me to continue to live!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; MICAH 7:18–19; JONAH 2:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Truly, my suffering was good for me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you loved me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and as a result you have rescued me from dying</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and have also forgiven all my sins.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:10; PSALMS 30:9; PSALMS 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Dead people &#91;MTY&#93; cannot praise you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they cannot sing to praise you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those who have descended to their graves</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">cannot confidently expect you to faithfully do things for them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:7; PSALMS 119:175; PSALMS 78:3–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Only people who are still alive, like I am, can praise you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Fathers tell their children how you are faithful,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and if I remain alive, I will do the same thing.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 116:2; PSALMS 150:4; PSALMS 145:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know that Yahweh will fully heal me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so I will sing to praise him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">while others praise him playing musical instruments;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will do that every day of my life, in the temple of Yahweh.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 KINGS 20:7–8; MARK 7:33; JOHN 9:6"> I had previously said to Hezekiah’s servants, “Prepare an ointment from mashed figs, and spread it on his boil, and then he will recover.” So they did that, and Hezekiah recovered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 118:18–19; 2 KINGS 20:8; JOHN 5:14"> And Hezekiah had previously asked, “What will Yahweh do to prove that I will recover and be able to go to his temple?”</span>
