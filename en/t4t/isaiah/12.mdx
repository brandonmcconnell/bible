## Songs that people will sing to praise Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 30:5; Isaiah 54:8; Isaiah 25:1"> At that time, you people of Jerusalem will sing this song:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yahweh, we praise you!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Previously, you were angry with us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you are not angry now</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you have comforted us.</span></span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PSALMS 105:1; 1 CHRONICLES 29:11; PSALMS 145:4–6"> At that time you all will say,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We should thank Yahweh! <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 2:10–11; ZECHARIAH 2:5; PSALMS 9:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">You people of Jerusalem, shout joyfully to praise Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because he is the great Holy One whom we Israeli people worship,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he lives among us!”</span></span>
