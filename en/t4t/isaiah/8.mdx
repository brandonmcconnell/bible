## A warning about the Assyrian attack

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 30:8; JOB 19:23–24; HABAKKUK 2:2–3"> Then Yahweh said to me, “Make a large signboard. And write clearly on it, ‘Maher-Shalal-Hash-Baz’ which means ‘quickly ◄plunder/take by force► and steal everything’.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 KINGS 16:10–11; 2 KINGS 18:2; 2 CORINTHIANS 13:1"> So I requested Uriah the Supreme Priest and Jeberekiah’s son Zechariah, men who were both honest/trustworthy witnesses, to watch me as I was doing that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HOSEA 1:3–9; JUDGES 4:4; Isaiah 7:13–14"> Then I had sex with &#91;EUP&#93; my wife, who was a prophetess, and she became pregnant and then gave birth to a son. Then Yahweh said to me, “Give him the name Maher-Shalal-Hash-Baz (which means ‘suddenly attacked, quickly conquered’),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 9:11; 2 KINGS 16:9; DEUTERONOMY 1:39"> because before he is old enough to say ‘papa’ or ‘mama’, the King of Assyria will come with his army and quickly take away all the valuable things in Damascus and in Samaria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 7:10"> Yahweh spoke to me again and said, “Tell the people of Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 9:7; Nehemiah 3:15; Isaiah 7:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘I have taken good care of you people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you have rejected that, thinking that my help was very little,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;MET&#93; the little canal through which water flows from the Gihon Spring into Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Instead, you have been happy to request help from King Rezin and King Pekah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 17:12–13; GENESIS 6:17; Isaiah 7:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, I will cause the people of Judah to be attacked by the powerful army of the King of Assyria, which will be like a great flood from the Euphrates River.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their soldiers will be everywhere in your country, like a river that overflows all its banks.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 30:28; MATTHEW 1:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those soldiers will go all over Judah—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a river whose water &#91;MET&#93; rises as high as a person’s neck.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their army will spread over the land quickly, like an eagle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will cover your entire land!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But I will be with you!’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:5–6; PROVERBS 11:21; PSALMS 37:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen, all you people in distant countries!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You can prepare to attack Judah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You can prepare for battle, and shout your war-cries,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but your armies will be crushed/shattered!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:37; ROMANS 8:31; JOB 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You can prepare for what you will do to attack Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but what you plan to do will not happen!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will not succeed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because God is with us!</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Isaiah is urged to revere Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:14; PROVERBS 1:15; JEREMIAH 15:19"> Yahweh strongly warned me &#91;MTY&#93; not to act like the other people in Judah did. He said to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 PETER 3:14–15; LUKE 21:9; PSALMS 53:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Do not say that everything that people do is conspiring/rebelling against the government,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like other people say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and do not ◄be afraid of/worry about► the things that other people are afraid of.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 12:5; Isaiah 26:3–4; REVELATION 15:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I, the Commander of the armies of angels, am the one you should consider to be holy.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And I am the one you should fear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the one you should revere.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 PETER 2:8; ROMANS 9:32–33; Isaiah 28:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will protect you(sg).</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But as for the other people &#91;MTY&#93; in Israel and Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will be like &#91;MET&#93; a stone that causes people to stumble,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a rock that causes them to fall down.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And as for the people of Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will be like &#91;MET&#93; a trap or a snare &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:44; LUKE 20:17–18; Isaiah 28:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many people will stumble and fall down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and never get up again.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will experience great troubles;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will be captured by their enemies.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DANIEL 12:4; HEBREWS 3:5; MARK 4:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, I say to you who are my disciples, seal up this scroll</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on which I have written the messages that God has given to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and give his instructions to others who have accompanied me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:36–39; HABAKKUK 2:3; LAMENTATIONS 3:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will wait to see what Yahweh will do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He has rejected the descendants &#91;MTY&#93; of Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but I will confidently expect him to help me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:13–14; LUKE 2:34; ZECHARIAH 8:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I and the children that Yahweh has given to me are like signs to warn the people of Israel;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">we are warnings from the Commander of the armies of angels</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the one who lives in his temple on Zion Hill in Jerusalem.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 10:13; LEVITICUS 20:6; 2 CHRONICLES 33:6"> Some people may urge you(pl) to ◄consult/go and talk with► those who talk with the spirits of dead people or with those who say that they receive messages from those spirits. They whisper and mutter about what we should do in the future. But God is &#91;RHQ&#93; the one whom we should ask to guide us! It is ridiculous &#91;RHQ&#93; for people who are alive to request spirits of dead people to tell us what we should do!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 PETER 1:19; 2 TIMOTHY 3:15–17; Isaiah 30:8–11"> Pay attention to God’s instructions and teaching! If people do not say things that agree with what God teaches us, what they say is worthless. It is as though those people are in darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:20; PROVERBS 19:3; LAMENTATIONS 4:4–5"> They will wander through the land, worried/discouraged and hungry. And when they become very hungry, they will become very angry. They will look up toward heaven and curse God and will also curse their king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 5:30; MATTHEW 22:13; JEREMIAH 13:16"> They will look around the land and see only trouble/distress and darkness and things that cause them to despair. And then they will be thrown into the very black darkness of hell.</span>
</div>
