## A warning about relying on Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 20:7; JEREMIAH 17:5; DEUTERONOMY 17:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Terrible things will happen to those who reply on Egypt to help them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">trusting in their soldiers’ horses and their many chariots and their strong chariot-drivers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">instead of trusting that Yahweh, the Holy One of Israel, will help them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:19; Isaiah 45:7; AMOS 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh is very wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but he also causes people to experience disaster!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And when he decides to do that,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he does not change his mind!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will strike/punish the wicked people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all those who help them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 9:17; EZEKIEL 20:33–34; EZEKIEL 28:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The soldiers of Egypt that you people of Judah are relying on are humans, not God!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And their horses are only horses; they are not powerful spirits!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So when Yahweh raises his fist</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to strike/punish the soldiers of Egypt whom you thought would help you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will also strike you who thought that you would be helped,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you and they will stumble and fall down;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all of you will die together.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSEA 11:10; AMOS 3:8; Isaiah 42:13"> But this is what Yahweh said to me:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“When a huge lion stands over the body of a sheep that he has killed and growls,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even if a large group of shepherds comes to chase/shoo away the lion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even if they shout loudly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the lion will not be afraid and will not leave.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Similarly, I, the Commander of the armies of angels, will come down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to fight my enemies on Zion Hill,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and nothing will hinder me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 91:4; PSALMS 37:40; Isaiah 37:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">I, the Commander of the armies of angels, will protect Jerusalem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; a mother bird protects the baby birds in her nest:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will defend the city</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and rescue it from its enemies.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:22; JEREMIAH 3:14; Isaiah 55:7"> My people, even though you have greatly rebelled against Yahweh, return to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 30:22; Isaiah 2:20; EZEKIEL 36:25"> When you do that, each of you will throw away the idols that you &#91;SYN&#93; have sinned by making, idols that are covered with silver and gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 14:25; GENESIS 49:15; Isaiah 37:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Many of the Assyrian soldiers will be killed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but not by swords that men use.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will be destroyed by the sword of God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who are not killed will ◄panic/be very afraid► and flee.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And some of them will be captured and forced to become slaves.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MALACHI 4:1; Isaiah 29:6; LEVITICUS 6:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even their very strong soldiers &#91;MTY&#93; will be terrified;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will abandon their battle flags and run away!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will cause his enemies who attack Jerusalem to be destroyed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh’s presence on Zion Hill is like a fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like a furnace that blazes in Jerusalem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that is what Yahweh says about what will happen to the Assyrian army!</span></span>
