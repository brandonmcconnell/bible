## Yahweh will destroy Moab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="AMOS 2:1–3; JEREMIAH 48:1–47; Isaiah 11:14"> I received this message from Yahweh about the Moab people-group:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">In one night two of your important cities, Ar and Kir, will be destroyed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:1; LEVITICUS 21:5; Isaiah 3:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of Dibon, your capital city, will go to their temple to mourn/weep;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will go to their shrines on the hilltops to weep.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will wail because of what happened to Nebo and Medeba towns in the south;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will all shave the hair of their heads, and the men will cut off their beards to show that they are grieving.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JONAH 3:6–8; JEREMIAH 19:13; DEUTERONOMY 22:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">In the streets people will wear rough sackcloth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and on their flat rooftops and in the city plazas everyone will wail,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with tears streaming down their faces.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:34; 1 KINGS 19:4; JONAH 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of Heshbon city and Elealeh towns in the north of Moab will cry out;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people as far away as Jahaz town in the south will hear them wailing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore the soldiers of Moab will tremble and cry out</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be very afraid &#91;IDM&#93;.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:5; JEREMIAH 4:20; LUKE 19:41–44"> <span className="pl pl-1">I feel very sorry for the people of Moab;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will flee to Zoar and Eglath-Shelishiyah towns in the far south.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will cry as they walk up to Luhith town.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All along the road to Horonaim town people will mourn</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because their country has been destroyed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOEL 1:10–12; Isaiah 19:5–7; NUMBERS 32:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">The water in Nimrim Valley will have dried up.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The grass there will be withered;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the green plants will all be gone,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there will be nothing left that is green.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:36; NAHUM 2:12–13; Isaiah 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people will pick up their possessions</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and carry them across Willows Brook.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 47:10; Isaiah 15:2–5; JEREMIAH 48:31–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Throughout the country of Moab, people will be crying;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people as far away as Eglaim in the south and Beer-Elim in the north will hear them wailing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:25; LEVITICUS 26:28; LEVITICUS 26:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The stream near Dimon will become red from the blood of people who have been killed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but I will cause the people of Moab to experience even more trouble:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lions will attack those who are trying to escape from Moab</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and will also attack the people who remain in that country.</span></span>
