## The destruction of Ethiopia

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:10; 2 KINGS 19:9; ZEPHANIAH 2:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Terrible things will happen to you people of Ethiopia!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">In your land there are many sailboats at the upper part of the Nile River.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 18:7; GENESIS 10:8–9; 2 CHRONICLES 16:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your rulers send ambassadors that sail quickly down the river in papyrus boats.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You messengers to Ethiopia, go quickly!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Go up the river to people who are tall and who have smooth skin.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">People everywhere are afraid of those people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they conquer and destroy other nations;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they live in a land that is divided by branches of one large river.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Isaiah 26:11; MATTHEW 13:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">You messengers must tell people everywhere,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to people everywhere,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look when I lift up my battle flag on top of the mountain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and listen when I blow the ram’s horn</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to signal that the battle is about to begin.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSEA 5:15; Isaiah 26:21; Isaiah 18:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen because Yahweh has told me this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will watch quietly from where I live.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will watch as quietly as &#91;SIM&#93; the heat waves shimmer as they rises on a hot summer day,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as quietly as the dew forms on the ground during harvest time.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 17:10–11; SONG OF SONGS 2:13; EZEKIEL 17:6–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">But even before the army of Ethiopia starts to attack,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while their plans are slowly forming like &#91;SIM&#93; grapes that are ripening,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will get rid of them like a farmer who &#91;MET&#93; cuts off the new shoots of the grapevines with his shears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and prunes the branches that have become very long.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:33; EZEKIEL 39:17–20; EZEKIEL 32:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the soldiers in the army of Ethiopia will be killed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their corpses will lie in the fields for vultures to eat their flesh in the ◄summer/hot season►.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then wild animals will chew on their bones all during the ◄winter/cold season►.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:10; ACTS 8:27–28; ZECHARIAH 14:16–17"> At that time, the people of that land that is divided by the branches of that one big river will take gifts to Yahweh in Jerusalem.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those tall people who have smooth/dark skin, whom people everywhere are afraid of,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they conquer and destroy many countries,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will take gifts to Jerusalem, the city where the Commander of the armies of angels lives.</span></span>
