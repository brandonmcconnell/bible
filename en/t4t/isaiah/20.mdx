## A message about Egypt and Ethiopia

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:17; JEREMIAH 25:29–30; AMOS 1:8"> One year King Sargon of Assyria sent the chief commander of his army to take his soldiers to capture Ashdod city in Philistia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MICAH 1:8; MATTHEW 3:4; 1 SAMUEL 19:24"> At that time, Yahweh told me, “Take off the rough sackcloth that you have been wearing and take off your sandals.” So I did what he told me to do, and then I walked around naked and barefoot for three years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; NUMBERS 14:34; EZEKIEL 4:5–6"> Then Yahweh said this to the people of Judah: “My servant Isaiah has been walking around naked and barefoot for the past three years. That is to show the terrible disasters that I will cause the people of Egypt and Ethiopia to experience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:4; JEREMIAH 13:26; JEREMIAH 13:22"> What will happen is that the army of the King of Assyria will invade those countries and capture many of the people and take them away as their prisoners. They will force all them, including both the young ones and the old ones, to walk naked and barefoot. They will also force them to have no clothes around their buttocks, which will cause the people of Egypt to be ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 30:5; Isaiah 30:3; 2 KINGS 18:21"> Then the people of other countries who trusted that the armies of Egypt and Ethiopia would be able to help them will be very dismayed/confused and afraid/disappointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:33; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:3; HEBREWS 2:3"> They will say, ‘We trusted that the armies of Egypt and Ethiopia would help us and defend us, but they have been destroyed, so there is no way &#91;RHQ&#93; that we can escape from being destroyed by the army of the King of Assyria!’”</span>
</div>
</div>
