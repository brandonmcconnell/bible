<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 47:1–15; JEREMIAH 50:1–64; Isaiah 1:1"> I, Isaiah, the son of Amoz, received from Yahweh this message about Babylon city:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:58; Isaiah 5:26; JEREMIAH 50:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lift up a flag on the bare top of a hill,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to signal that an army should come to attack Babylon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shout to them and wave your hand to signal to them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that they should march through the city gates into the palaces of the proud rulers of Babylon!</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOEL 3:14; REVELATION 19:11–21; JOEL 2:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to the noise on the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which is the noise of a huge army marching!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is the noise made by people of many people-groups shouting.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The Commander of the armies of angels has summoned this army to gather together.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOEL 1:15; Isaiah 13:9; AMOS 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">You people of Babylon will scream because you will be terrified,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because it will be the time that Yahweh has determined/chosen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the time for the all-powerful God to destroy your city.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 21:7; NAHUM 2:10; NAHUM 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">All of your people will be very afraid &#91;DOU&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that they will be unable even to lift their arms.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="NAHUM 1:6; MALACHI 4:1; Isaiah 13:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to this: The day that Yahweh has appointed/chosen is near,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the day that he will furiously and fiercely punish you because he is very angry with you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will cause your land of Babylonia to be desolate/barren,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he will destroy all the sinners in it.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:17; JEREMIAH 50:16; 1 KINGS 22:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">And all the foreigners in Babylon will run around like &#91;SIM&#93; deer that are being hunted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like sheep that do not have a shepherd.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will try to find other people from their countries,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then they will escape from Babylon and return to their own countries.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DANIEL 5:28–31; JEREMIAH 51:11; Isaiah 41:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look! </div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DANIEL 4:30; ZEPHANIAH 2:9; JEREMIAH 51:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">Babylon has been a very beautiful &#91;MTY&#93; city;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all the people of Babylonia have been very proud of Babylon, their capital city;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but God will destroy Babylon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; he destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:43; Isaiah 14:23; JEREMIAH 50:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">No one will ever live in Babylon again.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It will be deserted forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">◄Nomads/People who travel from place to place to live► will refuse to set up their tents there;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">shepherds will not bring their flocks of sheep to rest there.</span></span>
