## A message of encouragement for King Ahaz

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 KINGS 15:37; 2 KINGS 16:1; Isaiah 1:1"> Ahaz was the son of Jotham and grandson of Uzziah. During the time that Ahaz was the king of Judah, King Rezin of Syria and King Pekah of Israel marched with their armies to attack Jerusalem. But they could not conquer it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 7:13; Isaiah 9:9"> But before they attacked, everyone in the palace heard a report that the armies of Syria and Israel were now ◄allies/joined to attack Jerusalem►. So Ahaz and the people over whom he ruled were extremely afraid; they were shaking from fear like trees shake in a windstorm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:17; Isaiah 36:2; ROMANS 9:27"> Then Yahweh said to me, “Take your son Shear-Jashub, and go to talk with King Ahaz. He is at the end of the ◄aqueduct/water channel► that brings water into the upper reservoir, near the road to the place where women wash clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; LAMENTATIONS 3:26; DEUTERONOMY 20:3"> Tell Ahaz to stop worrying &#91;DOU&#93;. Tell him that he does not need to be afraid of those two kings, Rezin and Pekah. They are very angry with Judah, but they are unable to harm his country any more than completely burned-out coals can harm him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 83:3–4; ZECHARIAH 1:15; PSALMS 2:2"> Yes, they are planning to attack this land and saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> ‘We will attack Judah and conquer it. Then we will appoint Tabeel’s son to be the king of Judah.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; LAMENTATIONS 3:37; Isaiah 46:10–11"> But this is what Yahweh, the Lord, says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘It will not happen;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will not conquer Jerusalem!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1–3; GENESIS 14:15; 2 SAMUEL 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The capital of Syria is Damascus,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but Damascus is ruled only by its unimportant/insignificant king king Rezin.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And as for Israel, within 65 years it will be conquered and completely destroyed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:6; ROMANS 11:20; 2 CHRONICLES 20:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Israel’s capital is Samaria, and Samaria is ruled only by its insignificant/unimportant king king Pekah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So you do not need to be afraid of those two countries!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you must trust me, because if you do not trust me fully,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will be defeated.’ ”</span></span>
</div>

## A promise of a son named Immanuel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 8:5; Isaiah 1:5; Isaiah 10:20"> Later, Yahweh gave me another message to tell to King Ahaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 38:7–8; Isaiah 38:22"> He said to tell him, “Request me, Yahweh your God, to do something that will enable you to be sure that I will help/protect you. What you request can be from a place that is as high as the sky or as low as the place where the dead people are.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:31; 2 CHRONICLES 28:22; MALACHI 3:15"> But when I told that to the king, he refused. He said, “No, I will not request Yahweh to do something to prove that he will help/protect us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 63:10; EZEKIEL 34:18"> Then I said to him, “You people who are descendants of King David, listen! You are causing me to be tired of being patient. Are you also going to cause my God to stop being patient with you &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:23; Isaiah 9:6; LUKE 1:31"> Yahweh himself will do something for you to prove that he will help/protect you. Listen to this: A young woman will become pregnant and give birth to a son. She will name him Immanuel, which means ‘God is with us’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 7:22; AMOS 5:15; MATTHEW 3:4"> And by the time that child is old enough to eat curds/yogurt and honey, he will be able to reject what is evil and choose what is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4; DEUTERONOMY 1:39; Isaiah 9:11"> And before that child is old enough to do that, the lands of the two kings that you(sg) are very ◄afraid of/worried about► will be deserted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 CHRONICLES 28:19–21"> But then Yahweh will cause you and your family and your entire nation to experience terrible disasters. Those disasters will be worse than any disasters that have occurred since the country of Israel separated from Judah. Yahweh will cause the army of the king of Assyria to attack you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; JOSHUA 24:12; DEUTERONOMY 1:44"> At that time, it will be as though Yahweh will whistle to summon the army from the south of Egypt as well as the army of Assyria. They will come and surround your country like &#91;MET&#93; flies and bees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; JEREMIAH 16:16; Isaiah 2:21"> They will all come and settle/live everywhere—in the narrow valleys and caves in the rock cliffs, on land where there are thornbushes as well as on the fertile land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; EZEKIEL 29:18; Isaiah 10:15"> At that time Yahweh will hire the king of Assyria to come with his army from east of the Euphrates River. They will get rid of everything in your land—the crops and the people. They will destroy everything thoroughly; it will be like &#91;MET&#93; a barber shaving not only a man’s hair but his beard and the hair on his legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:17; JEREMIAH 39:10; Isaiah 37:30"> When that happens, a farmer will be able to have only one cow and two goats/sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; MATTHEW 3:4; 2 SAMUEL 17:29"> However, those animals will give plenty of milk, with the result that the farmer will have curds/yogurt to eat. And because there will not be many people left in the land, all the people who remain there will have plenty of milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; SONG OF SONGS 8:11–12; MATTHEW 21:33"> Now there are many areas where there are vineyards that are worth 1,000 pieces of silver, but at that time there will be only briers and thorns &#91;DOU&#93; in those fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 27:3"> There will be only briers and thorns in the entire land, and wild animals, with the result that men will take their bows and arrows and go there to hunt and kill animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Isaiah 13:20–22; Isaiah 5:17"> No one will go to where there previously were gardens on fertile hillsides, because briers and thorns will cover those hillsides. They will be areas where only a few cattle and sheep and goats wander around searching for something to eat.</span>
</div>
