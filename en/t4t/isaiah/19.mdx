## A prophecy about the destruction of Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 12:12; REVELATION 1:7; JOSHUA 2:11"> I received this message from Yahweh about Egypt:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Listen to this! I, Yahweh, am coming to Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">riding on a fast-moving cloud.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The idols in Egypt will tremble when I appear,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the people of Egypt will be extremely afraid &#91;IDM&#93;.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 7:22; 1 SAMUEL 14:20; MATTHEW 10:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will cause the people of Egypt to fight against each other:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">men will fight against their brothers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">neighbors will fight against each other,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people of one city will fight against the people of another city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people of one province will fight against the people of another province.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; DANIEL 2:2; 1 CHRONICLES 10:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of Egypt will become very discouraged,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will cause their plans not to be successful.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will plead with idols and sorcerers and those who talk with spirits of dead people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to tell them what they should do.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; JEREMIAH 46:26; EZEKIEL 29:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will enable someone who will treat them very cruelly to become their king &#91;DOU&#93;.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That is what I, the Commander of the armies of angels, say.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:36; EZEKIEL 30:12; ZECHARIAH 14:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Some day the water in the Nile River will dry up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the riverbed will become very dry &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:25; EXODUS 7:18; 2 KINGS 19:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The branches of the river will all dry up &#91;DOU&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The canals along the river will stink</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of the rotting reeds and ◄bulrushes/tall grass►.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 32:20; JOEL 1:17–18; Isaiah 23:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the plants along the river and all the crops will dry up;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then they will blow away and disappear.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 47:10; HABAKKUK 1:15; NUMBERS 11:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The fishermen will throw into the river lines with hooks on them and nets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and then they will groan and be very discouraged;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will be sad because there will be no fish in the river.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:7; PROVERBS 7:16; 1 KINGS 10:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who weave cloth from flax will not know what to do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because there will be no thread for them to weave.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:10; EXODUS 7:19; EXODUS 8:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will all despair</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and be very discouraged.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:22; ACTS 7:22; 1 KINGS 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">The officials in Zoan city in northern Egypt are foolish.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The advice that they gave to the king was worthless.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why do they continue to tell the king that they are wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that they are descendants of wise kings who lived long ago &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:20; Isaiah 14:24; JUDGES 9:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">King, ◄where are your wise counselors now?/You have no wise counselors now!► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If you had any wise counselors, they could tell you what the Commander of the armies of angels has planned to do to Egypt &#91;SAR&#93;!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:13; JEREMIAH 2:16; JEREMIAH 46:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yes, the officials of Zoan have become foolish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the leaders in Memphis city in northern Egypt have deceived themselves.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All the leaders of the people have ◄caused their people to do wrong things/led their people astray►.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 2:11; 1 KINGS 22:20–23; EZEKIEL 14:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh has caused them to be very foolish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that in everything that they do, it is as though the people of Egypt stagger</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; a drunken person staggers and slips in his own vomit.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:11–12; PROVERBS 14:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">There is no one in Egypt, rich or poor, important or unimportant, who will be able to help them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:30; NAHUM 3:13; Isaiah 11:15"> At that time, the people of Egypt will be as helpless as &#91;SIM&#93; women. They will tremble, being terrified because they know that the Commander of the armies of angels has raised his fist, ready to strike/punish them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:24; DANIEL 4:35; EZEKIEL 29:6–7"> The people of Egypt will be afraid of the people of Judah, and anyone who mentions Judah to them will cause them to be terrified, because that will remind them of what Yahweh, the Commander of the armies of angels, is planning to do to them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:9; ZECHARIAH 2:11; Isaiah 19:21"> At that time, people in five cities in Egypt will solemnly declare that they will serve Yahweh. They will learn to speak the Hebrew language. One of those cities will be called ‘City of the Sun’.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 28:18; JOSHUA 22:10; JOSHUA 22:26"> At that time, there will be an altar for worshiping Yahweh in the center of Egypt, and there will be a ◄pillar/large rock► to honor Yahweh at the border between Egypt and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; TITUS 2:13; Isaiah 45:21–22"> That will be a sign to indicate that the Commander of the armies of angels is worshiped in the land of Egypt. And when the people cry out to Yahweh to help them because others are ◄oppressing them/causing them to suffer►, he will send to them someone who will defend and rescue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; MALACHI 1:11; JOHN 17:3"> Yahweh will enable the people of Egypt to know who he is, and at that time they will have a close relationship with Yahweh and worship him and bring to him offerings of grain and other sacrifices. They will solemnly promise to do things for Yahweh, and they will do what they promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:39; HEBREWS 12:11; HOSEA 14:1"> After Yahweh has punished Egypt, he will cause their troubles to end. The people of Egypt will turn to Yahweh, and he will listen when they plead to him for help, and he will cause their troubles to cease.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; EPHESIANS 2:18–22; Isaiah 35:8–10"> At that time, there will be a highway between Egypt and Assyria. As a result, the people of Egypt will be able to travel easily to Assyria, and the people of Assyria will be able to travel easily to Egypt. And the people of both countries will worship Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:8; GENESIS 12:2; GALATIANS 3:14"> And Israel will be their ally. All three nations will be friendly to each other, and the people of Israel will be a blessing to the people of the entire world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 29:23; HOSEA 2:23; ROMANS 9:24–25"> The Commander of the armies of angels will bless them, saying, “You people of Egypt are now my people. You people of Assyria, I have established your country. You people of Israel are the people whom I have chosen to belong to me.”</span>
</div>
