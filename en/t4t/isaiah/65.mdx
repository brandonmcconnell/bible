<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:12; PSALMS 66:4; MATTHEW 22:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who worship and obey me will sing joyfully,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you evil people will wail loudly</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you will be suffering in your inner beings.</span></span>
