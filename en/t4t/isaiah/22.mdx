## A message about Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:13; PSALMS 125:2; JOEL 3:12"> I received this message from Yahweh about Jerusalem, about the valley where Yahweh showed me this vision.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why is everyone foolishly running up to their flat rooftops?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 32:13; Isaiah 23:7; LAMENTATIONS 2:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everyone in the city seems to be shouting.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There are a lot of corpses in the city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but they were not killed by their enemies’ swords.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They did not die in battles;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">instead, they died from diseases and hunger.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:4–7; Isaiah 3:1–8; JEREMIAH 39:4–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the leaders of the city fled.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">But then they were captured because they did not have bows and arrows to defend themselves.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your soldiers tried to flee while the enemy army was still far away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they also were captured.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:1; MATTHEW 2:18; PSALMS 77:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">That is why I said, “Allow me to cry alone;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not try to comfort me about my people being slaughtered.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MICAH 7:4; Isaiah 37:3; LUKE 23:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Commander of the armies of angels has chosen a time when there will be a great uproar, soldiers marching, and people being terrified</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in the valley where I received this vision.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will be a time when our city walls will be battered down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the people’s cries for help will be heard in the mountains.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 KINGS 16:9; Isaiah 21:2; AMOS 1:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The armies from Elam and Kir in Media will attack,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">driving chariots and carrying shields.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:28–32; JEREMIAH 39:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Our beautiful valleys will be filled with our enemies’ chariots</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the men who drive the chariots will stand outside our city gates.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:2; 1 KINGS 10:17; Isaiah 36:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The walls that protect the cities in Judah will fall down.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You people of Jerusalem will run to get the weapons that are stored in the building called “the Hall of the Forest”.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 KINGS 20:20; Nehemiah 3:16; 2 CHRONICLES 32:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will see that there are many breaks/holes in the walls of Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You will store water in the lower pool in the city.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will inspect the houses in Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and some of them you will tear down to use the stones to repair the city wall.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MICAH 7:7; JEREMIAH 33:2–3; Isaiah 31:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Between the walls of the city you will build a reservoir to store water from the old pool.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you will never request help from the one who made the city;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you have never depended on Yahweh, who planned this city long ago.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOEL 2:17; MICAH 1:16; JOEL 1:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Commander of the armies of angels told you to weep and mourn;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he told you to shave your heads and to wear rough sackcloth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to show that you were sorry for the sins that you had committed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 56:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:32; LUKE 17:26–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">But instead of doing that, you were happy and celebrating;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you slaughtered cattle and sheep,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order to cook their meat and eat it and drink wine.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You said, “Let’s eat and drink all that we want to,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because it is possible that we will die tomorrow!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:13; 1 SAMUEL 3:14; Isaiah 5:9"> So the Commander of the armies of angels revealed this to me: “I will never forgive my people for sinning like this!”</span>
</div>

## A message for Shebna

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 KINGS 18:18; 2 KINGS 19:2"> The Commander of the armies of angels said this to me: “Go to Shebna, the official who supervises the workers in the palace, and give this message to him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:60; 2 CHRONICLES 16:14; 2 SAMUEL 18:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">'Who do you think you are?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Who gave you the authority to build a beautiful tomb where you will be buried,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">chiseling it out of the rocky cliff high above this valley?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:3; JOB 9:24; Esther 7:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">You think that you are &#91;IRO&#93; a great man, but Yahweh is about to hurl you away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It will be as though he will seize you,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 17:13; AMOS 7:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">and roll/crumple you into a ball</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and throw you away into a large distant land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will die and be buried there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your beautiful chariots will stay there in the hands of your enemies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">And because of what happens to you, your master, the king, will be very ashamed/disgraced.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 17:24; PSALMS 75:6–7; JOB 40:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh will force you to quit working in the palace;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will be demoted from your important position.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 36:3; 2 KINGS 18:18; Isaiah 37:2"> Then I will summon Hilkiah’s son Eliakim, who has served serve me well, to replace you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 45:8; Isaiah 9:6–7; GENESIS 41:42–43"> I will allow him to wear your robe, and to fasten your sash around him, and I will give to him the authority that you had. He will be like &#91;MET&#93; a father to the people of Jerusalem and all the other towns in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:18–19; REVELATION 3:7; JOB 12:14"> I will give to him authority &#91;MTY&#93; over what happens in the palace where King David lived; when he decides something &#91;MET&#93;, no one will be able to oppose it; when he refuses to do something, no one will be able to force him to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:8; JOB 36:7; ZECHARIAH 10:4"> I will cause his family to be greatly respected, because I will put him firmly in his position as supervisor of the workers in the palace, like &#91;SIM&#93; a nail that is firmly hammered into a wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DANIEL 6:1–3; JOHN 20:21–23; MATTHEW 28:18"> Others will enable him to have much responsibility, with the result that all the members of his family, even the most insignificant ones, will be honored.' ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 46:11; Esther 9:24–25; PSALMS 146:3"> But the Commander of the armies of angels also says, “Shebna is like &#91;MET&#93; a peg that is firmly fastened to the wall. But there will be a time when I will remove him from his position; he will lose his power/influence, and everything that he promoted &#91;MET&#93; will fail. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.”</span>
</div>
