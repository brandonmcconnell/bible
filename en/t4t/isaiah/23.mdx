## A message about Tyre’s punishment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOEL 3:4–8; AMOS 1:9–10; GENESIS 10:4"> I received this message from Yahweh about Tyre city:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">You sailors on &#91;APO&#93; ships from Tarshish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">weep, because the harbor of Tyre and all the houses in the city have been destroyed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The reports that you heard in Cyprus island about Tyre are true.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 47:5; Isaiah 41:1; EZEKIEL 28:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You people who live along the coast near Tyre, and merchants of Sidon city, mourn silently.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your sailors went across the seas to many places like Tyre.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:18; 1 CHRONICLES 13:5; EZEKIEL 27:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They sailed across deep seas</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to buy grain in Egypt</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and other crops that are grown along the Nile River.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Tyre became the city where people from all nations bought and sold goods.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="REVELATION 18:23; GENESIS 10:15; JEREMIAH 47:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">But now you people in Sidon should be ashamed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you trusted in Tyre, which has been a strong fortress on an island in the sea.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Tyre is like a woman who is saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Now it is as though I have not given birth to any children,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or raised any sons or daughters.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:9–11; EXODUS 15:14–16; REVELATION 18:17–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">When the people of Egypt hear what has happened to Tyre,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will grieve very much.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 23:12; Isaiah 21:15; Isaiah 23:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Sail to Tarshish and tell them what happened;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">weep, you people who live along the coast.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Isaiah 32:13; JOSHUA 19:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people in the very old city of Tyre were &#91;RHQ&#93; previously joyful.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Traders &#91;PRS&#93; from Tyre established colonies in many distant nations.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:8; JEREMIAH 50:44–45; DEUTERONOMY 29:24–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">People from Tyre appointed kings over other places;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">their traders were wealthy;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they were as powerful and wealthy as &#91;MET&#93; kings.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So, who &#91;RHQ&#93; caused the people of Tyre to experience this disaster?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JAMES 4:6; Isaiah 13:11; JOB 40:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">It was the Commander of the armies of angels who did it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he did it in order to cause you people in Tyre not to be proud any more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to humiliate you men who are honored all over the world.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 12:21; Isaiah 23:14; PSALMS 18:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">You people of Tarshish, you must grow crops in your land instead of trading;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">spread out over your land like &#91;SIM&#93; the Nile River spreads over the land of Egypt when it floods,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because there is no harbor in Tyre for your ships now.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 9:3–4; EXODUS 14:21; JEREMIAH 47:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though Yahweh stretched out his hand over the sea</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and shook the kingdoms of the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He commanded that in Phoenicia/Canaan</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all its fortresses must be destroyed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="REVELATION 18:22; EZEKIEL 26:13–14; Isaiah 23:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">He said to the people of Sidon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“You will never rejoice again, because you will be crushed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even if you flee to Cyprus island,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will not escape destruction.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:6; Isaiah 10:5; 2 KINGS 17:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Think about what happened in Babylonia:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the people who were in that land have disappeared.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The armies of Assyria have caused that land to become a place where wild animals from the desert live.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Assyrians built dirt ramps to the top of the walls of the city of Babylon;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">then they entered the city and tore down the palaces</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and caused the city to become a heap of rubble.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 23:1; Isaiah 23:6; EZEKIEL 27:25–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">So wail, you sailors on the ships of Tarshish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because the harbor in Tyre where your ships stop is destroyed!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:22; JEREMIAH 25:9–11; DANIEL 7:14"> For seventy years, which is as long as kings usually live, people will forget about Tyre. But then it will be rebuilt. What will happen there will be like what happened to a prostitute in this song:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:14; PROVERBS 7:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“You harlot, whom people had forgotten,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">play your harp well,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and sing many songs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order that people will remember you again.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NAHUM 3:4; EZEKIEL 27:6–36; REVELATION 19:2"> It is true that after seventy years Yahweh will restore Tyre. Their merchants will again earn a lot of money by buying things from and selling things to many other nations &#91;HYP&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 72:10; DEUTERONOMY 26:12–14; PHILIPPIANS 4:17–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">But their profits will be given to Yahweh.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The merchants will not hoard their money;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">instead, they will give it to Yahweh’s priests</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order that they can buy food and nice clothes.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
