<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 32:15; MATTHEW 19:30; HEBREWS 10:37"> <span className="pl pl-1">Soon the forests in Lebanon will become fertile fields,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and abundant crops will grow in those fields,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that will happen very soon.</span></span>
