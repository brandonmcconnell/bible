## On the basis of all that I have told you, continue to believe firmly in the Lord Jesus Christ according to what I have just taught you, and act accordingly.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:27; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:13; PSALMS 27:14"> My fellow believers, I love you, and I long for you. You make me happy &#91;MTY&#93;, and I am ◄proud of/pleased with► you. Dear friends, on the basis of all that I have told you, continue to believe firmly in the Lord doing what I have just taught you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## I urge Euodia and Syntyche to be reconciled with each other; help them in this since they have both proclaimed the good news faithfully together with me and my other fellow workers.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:10; MARK 9:50"> I urge you(sg), Euodia, and I urge you(sg), Syntyche, to again have a peaceful relationship with each other, because you both have a relationship with the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="REVELATION 3:5; REVELATION 13:8; LUKE 10:20"> And, my faithful comrade/partner, I request that you (sg) help them to again have a peaceful relationship with each other, because they have faithfully proclaimed the message about Christ together with me, even though many people have opposed us (exc). They have faithfully proclaimed that message together with Clement and the rest of my fellow workers, whose names are in the book in which God has written the names of all those people who will live forever.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lord is coming soon. Always rejoice, be gentle to everyone. Do not worry about anything, but pray to God instead. As a result, God will grant you profound peace.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:16–18; ROMANS 12:12; JAMES 1:2–4"> Because you have a relationship with the Lord, always rejoice! I’m saying it again, rejoice!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JAMES 5:8–9; MATTHEW 6:25; TITUS 3:2"> Act in such a way that everyone can know that you are gentle. The Lord is coming soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:5–6; 1 PETER 5:7; MATTHEW 6:25–33"> Do not worry about anything. Instead, in every situation, pray to God, tell him what you need, and ask him to help you &#91;DOU&#93;. Also thank him for what he does for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOHN 14:27; Isaiah 26:3; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:16"> As a result, God will enable you not to worry about anything &#91;MTY&#93; (OR, God will protect your minds in every way) &#91;PRS&#93;. That is, he will cause you to have inner peace because you have a relationship with Christ Jesus. You will not be able to understand &#91;PRS&#93; how you can be so peaceful in such difficult circumstances!!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Continually think about everything that is good and praiseworthy. Continually practice whatever you have learned from me. As a result, God will be with you and give you inner peace.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JAMES 3:17; TITUS 2:7; ROMANS 12:9–21"> My fellow believers, there is one more thing I want you to do. Whatever is true, whatever is worthy of respect, whatever is right, whatever is morally pure, whatever is pleasing, whatever is admirable, whatever is good, whatever deserves praise, those are the things that you should continually think about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JAMES 1:22; PHILIPPIANS 3:17; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:1–8"> Those things that I have taught you and that you have learned from me, those things that you have heard me say and that you have seen me do, those are the things that you yourselves should continually do. As a result of your doing those things, God, the one who causes us to have inner peace, will ◄be with/bless► you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## I rejoice greatly because you have once again demonstrated your concern for me by giving to meet my needs, though it is true that Christ enables me to be content in every situation.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 11:9; GALATIANS 6:6; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:6–7"> I rejoice greatly and thank the Lord because now, after some time, by sending money to me you have once again shown that you are concerned about me &#91;EUP&#93;. Indeed, you were concerned about me all the time, but you had no opportunity to show that you were concerned about me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 6:6–9; HEBREWS 13:5–6; MATTHEW 6:31–34"> I am saying this not because I am concerned that I lack things that I need. In fact, I have learned to be content in whatever situation I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:29; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:7–10; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:27"> Specifically, I know how to be content when I do not have what I need, and I know how to be content when I have plenty. I have learned how to be content in any and every situation &#91;DOU&#93;. Specifically, I have learned how to be content when I have enough to eat, and I have learned how to be content when I do not have enough to eat. I have learned how to be content when I have plenty &#91;DOU&#93; of what I need, and I have learned how to be content when I lack things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 41:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:9–10; COLOSSIANS 1:11"> I am able ◄to cope with/to handle► every situation because Christ gives me the strength to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:16; PHILIPPIANS 1:7; HEBREWS 10:34"> Nevertheless, you did very well by helping me (OR, giving me money) &#91;EUP&#93; while I have been suffering hardship.</span>
</div>
</div>

## You Philippians know that in the early days of preaching the good news in your region you were the only congregation that sent me money. I do not say this because I desire your gifts, but I desire that God would abundantly bless you for aiding me.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:5; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:8–12; ACTS 16:40–5"> My friends there at Philippi, you yourselves know that when I first proclaimed the message about Christ to you, and then left there to go to Macedonia province, you were the only group of believers &#91;LIT&#93; who sent me gifts &#91;EUP&#93; in order that I might proclaim that message to others just like I did for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:9; ACTS 17:1; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:18"> Even when I was in Thessalonica city, you sent money &#91;EUP&#93; to me two different times &#91;IDM&#93; in order to supply what I needed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TITUS 3:14; HEBREWS 6:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 9:9–13"> I say this, not because I desire that you give me money now. Instead, I desire that God will abundantly bless &#91;MET&#93; you as a result of your helping me.</span>
</div>
</div>

## I have received your very generous gift; God is very pleased with this gift, and he will abundantly supply your every need also. Let us praise him forever.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:16; PHILIPPIANS 4:12; 2 CORINTHIANS 9:12"> But I have received a very generous gift &#91;EUP&#93; from you, and as a result, I have plenty. I have an abundant supply of what I need because I have received from Epaphroditus the money &#91;EUP&#93; that you sent to me. God considers that your gift is very acceptable, and he is very pleased with it. Your gift is like a nice-smelling sacrifice &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 9:8–11; PSALMS 84:11; 1 PETER 5:10"> Moreover, God, whom I serve, has an unlimited supply of everything that we(inc) need. And as a result, because of your relationship with Christ Jesus, he will completely supply everything that you need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 11:36; PSALMS 115:1; REVELATION 14:7"> So, praise God our Father forever and ever! Amen!</span>
</div>
</div>

## All of God’s people here, including those who serve God with me and those who work at the emperor’s palace, join me in greeting each one of God’s people there.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:2; PHILEMON 1:23–24; COLOSSIANS 4:10–14"> Greet for me/us(exc) all of God’s people there, that is, all those who have a relationship with Christ Jesus. The fellow believers who serve God together with me here send their greetings to you (OR, say they are thinking fondly of you).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:13; 2 CORINTHIANS 13:13; ROMANS 16:16"> All of God’s people here send their greetings to you. Especially the fellow believers who work in the palace of the emperor send their greetings to you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## May the Lord Jesus Christ bless you.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 13:14; ROMANS 16:20; ROMANS 16:23"> My desire is that our Lord Jesus Christ will continue to act kindly toward you all &#91;SYN&#93;.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
