## Abram and Lot separated

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 12:9–20; JOSHUA 10:40; 2 SAMUEL 24:7"> So Abram and Sarai left Egypt, and they took along all his possessions, and Lot went with them to the Negev Desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 24:35; PSALMS 112:1–3; GENESIS 26:12–13"> Abram was very rich. He owned a lot of livestock, silver and gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 12:8–9; GENESIS 12:6"> They continued traveling from place to place from the Negev Desert toward Bethel town to the place between Bethel and Ai where they had previously set up their tents,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:12; GENESIS 12:7–8; Isaiah 58:9"> and where Abram had made a stone altar and worshiped Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:29; GENESIS 25:27; GENESIS 4:20"> Lot, who was traveling with Abram, also had flocks of sheep and goats and herds of cattle, and tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 36:6–7; ECCLESIASTES 5:10–11; 1 TIMOTHY 6:9"> But they had so many animals that they could not all stay in the same area. There was not enough land to provide water and food for all their animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 26:20; GENESIS 12:6; JAMES 3:16"> Furthermore, the descendants of Canaan and Perizzi were also living in that area, and the land really belonged to them. And the men who took care of Abram’s animals started quarreling with the men who took care of Lot’s animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 6:6–7; ACTS 7:26; PSALMS 133:1"> So Abram said to Lot, “Since we are close relatives, it is not good for us two to quarrel, or for the men who take care of your animals to quarrel with the men who take care of my animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 20:15; GENESIS 34:10; 1 PETER 3:8–12"> There is plenty of land for both of us. So we should separate. You can choose whatever part you want &#91;RHQ&#93;. If you want the area over there, I will stay here. If you want the area here, I will go over there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 34:3; Isaiah 51:3; GENESIS 14:8"> Lot looked around toward Zoar town, and saw that there was plenty of water all over the plain near the Jordan River. It was like the park/garden in Eden, and like the land in Egypt. &#91;That was before Yahweh destroyed the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah which were on that plain.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 13:9; 1 PETER 2:17; HEBREWS 10:25"> So Lot chose the land in the plain of the Jordan River. He left his uncle, Abram, and moved east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 19:29; GENESIS 14:12; GENESIS 19:1"> Abram stayed in the Canaan area, and Lot started to live near the cities in the plain of the Jordan River, and he set up his tents near Sodom city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 PETER 2:10; 2 PETER 2:6–8; JUDE 1:7"> You need to know that people who lived there were extremely wicked.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abram moved to Hebron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:18; DEUTERONOMY 3:27; GENESIS 28:14"> After Abram and Lot separated, Yahweh said to Abram, “Look around at this whole area where you are. Look north and south, look east and west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 20:7; GENESIS 35:12; GENESIS 15:18"> I will give to you and your descendants all the land that you see; I will give it to you forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 28:14; NUMBERS 23:10; GENESIS 32:12"> I will cause your descendants to be as numerous as particles of dust! It will be easier to count the particles of dust than it will be to count your descendants &#91;HYP&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 13:15; NUMBERS 13:17–24"> Walk through the land in every direction, because I am going to give it all to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 18:1; GENESIS 8:20; GENESIS 35:27"> So Abram and his men took down their tents and moved to Hebron and started to live by the huge trees at the town of Mamre. He placed a stone altar there to make sacrifices to Yahweh.</span>
</div>
