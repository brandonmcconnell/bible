## Yahweh caused people to speak many different languages after they started to build a tower at Babel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ACTS 2:6; ZEPHANIAH 3:9; Isaiah 19:18"> At first, all the people in &#91;MTY&#93; the world spoke the same language &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 10:10; DANIEL 1:2; GENESIS 14:1"> As people moved further east, they arrived at a plain in Babylonia region and began to live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 14:10; EXODUS 1:14; EXODUS 2:3"> Then they said to each other, “◄Hey/Come on►, let’s form bricks and bake them to make them hard, for building!” So they used bricks instead of stones, and used tar instead of ◄mortar/a mixture of cement, sand and lime► to hold them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:28; JOHN 5:44; LUKE 1:51"> They said, “Hey, let’s build a city for ourselves! We also ought to build a very high tower that reaches up to the sky! In that way we will become famous! If we do not do this, we will be scattered all over the earth!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 18:21; PSALMS 33:13–14; JOHN 3:13"> So they began to build the city and the tower. Then Yahweh looked down and saw the city and the tower that those puny men were building.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 2:1–4; GENESIS 3:22; GENESIS 11:1"> Yahweh said, “These people are one group that all speak the same language. If they have begun to do this now, then ◄there is nothing that they will decide to do that will be impossible for them/they will be able to do whatever they decide to do► &#91;LIT&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 33:10; PSALMS 55:9; JOB 5:12–13"> So, okay/now, we will go down there and cause there to be many different languages, so that they will not be able to understand each other.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 1:51; DEUTERONOMY 32:8; GENESIS 10:25"> So Yahweh did that. As a result, the people stopped building the city, and Yahweh caused them to disperse all over the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 10:10; GENESIS 10:5; GENESIS 10:20"> The city was called Babel which means ‘confusion’, because there Yahweh caused the people to become confused because the people &#91;MTY&#93; spoke different languages that the others could not understand, not just one language. From there Yahweh caused them to disperse all over the earth.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Shem’s descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:17–27; GENESIS 10:21–22; LUKE 3:34–36"> ◄This is a bigger list/I will now tell you more► of the descendants of Shem: Two years after the flood, when Shem was 100 years old, he became the father of Arpachshad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 9:7; PSALMS 144:12; GENESIS 1:28"> After Arpachshad was born, Shem lived 500 more years and had more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 3:36"> When Arphachshad was 35 years old, he became the father of Shelah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After Shelah was born, Arpachshad lived 403 more years and became the father of more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When Shelah was 30 years old, he became the father of Eber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> After Eber was born, Shelah lived 403 more years and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 10:21; 1 CHRONICLES 1:19; NUMBERS 24:24"> When Eber was 34 years old, he became the father of Peleg.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> After Peleg was born, Eber lived 430 more years and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 3:35"> When Peleg was 30 years old, he became the father of Reu.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> After Reu was born, Peleg lived 209 more years and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 3:35"> When Reu was 32 years old, he became the father of Serug.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> After Serug was born, Reu lived 207 more years and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:2"> When Serug was 30 years old, he became the father of Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> After Nahor was born, Serug lived 200 more years and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 3:34; JOSHUA 24:2"> When Nahor was 29 years old, he became the father of Terah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> After Terah was born, Nahor lived 119 more years and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:2; 1 CHRONICLES 1:26–27; GENESIS 22:20–24"> When Terah was 70 years old, his son Abram was born. Later, two other sons, Nahor and Haran were born.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Terah’s descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 12:4; GENESIS 14:12; GENESIS 11:31"> ◄This is/I will now give► a list of the descendants of Terah: Terah’s sons were Abram, Nahor, and Haran. Haran’s son was named Lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 15:7; Nehemiah 9:7; ACTS 7:2–4"> Haran died before his father died. He died in Ur city in Chaldea land, where he was born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 17:15; GENESIS 22:20; GENESIS 24:15"> Abram and Nahor both married. Abram’s wife was named Sarai, and Nahor’s wife was named Milcah. Milcah and her younger sister Iscah were the daughters of Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 29:31; GENESIS 21:1–2; LUKE 1:7"> Sarai was unable to bear any children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:7; ACTS 7:2–4; GENESIS 24:10"> Terah decided to leave Ur and go to live in Canaan land. So he took his son Abram and his grandson Lot, the son of Haran, and Abram’s wife Sarai with him. But instead of going to Canaan, they stopped at Haran town and lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> When Terah was 205 years old, he died there in Haran.</span>
</div>
</div>
