## God made an agreement with Noah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 9:7; GENESIS 1:28; GENESIS 8:17"> Then God blessed Noah and his sons, saying to them, “I want you to have many children who will live all over the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 8:4–8; GENESIS 1:28; JAMES 3:7"> All the wild animals on the earth and all the birds, all the creatures that scurry across the ground, and all the fish, will be very afraid &#91;DOU&#93; of you. I have put them under your control.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ROMANS 14:14; COLOSSIANS 2:21–22; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:31"> Just as I previously said you could eat green plants for food, now I am saying you can eat everything that lives and moves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 12:23; DEUTERONOMY 15:23; DEUTERONOMY 12:16"> It is blood that causes creatures to be alive, therefore you must not eat meat that still has blood in it after the animal is killed. After you have drained the blood out, you may cook it and eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 21:28–29; EXODUS 21:12; GENESIS 4:9–10"> I insist that murderers must be executed. Animals that kill people must also be executed. The reason that everyone who murders someone else must be executed is that</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:17; MATTHEW 26:52; REVELATION 13:10"> I made people to be like myself in many ways. So someone who murders another human being must be executed by others, because he killed someone who is like me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 1:28; GENESIS 9:1; GENESIS 9:19"> As for you, I want you to produce many children, in order that they and their descendants may live all over the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> God also said to Noah and his sons,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 6:18; Isaiah 54:9–10; ROMANS 1:3"> “Listen carefully. I am now making a solemn promise to you and with your descendants,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 8:1; GENESIS 9:15–16; PSALMS 36:5–6"> and with all the living creatures that are with you—including the birds, the livestock, and the wild animals—every living creature on the earth that came out of the boat with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9; GENESIS 8:21–22; GENESIS 7:21–23"> This is the promise that I am making to you: I will never again destroy all living creatures by a flood, or destroy everything else on the earth by a flood.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 17:11; MATTHEW 26:26–28; EXODUS 12:13"> Then God said to him, “This is the sign to guarantee that I will keep the promise that I am making to you and to all living creatures, a promise that I will keep forever:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:28; REVELATION 4:3; REVELATION 10:1"> From time to time I will put a rainbow in the sky. It will remind me of my promise that I have made to you and everything on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When I cause rain to fall from the clouds, and a rainbow appears in the sky,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:9; EZEKIEL 16:60; Isaiah 54:8–10"> it will remind me about the promise that I have made to you and all living creatures, my promise that there will never again be a flood that will destroy all living creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 54:8–10; JEREMIAH 32:40; 2 SAMUEL 23:5"> Whenever there is a rainbow in the sky, I will see it, and I will think about the promise that I have made to every living creature that is upon the earth, a promise that I will keep forever.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then God said to Noah, “The rainbow will be the sign of the promise that I have made to all the creatures that live on the earth.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 10:1; GENESIS 10:6; GENESIS 9:25–27"> The sons of Noah who came out of the boat were Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Ham later became the father of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:4–28; GENESIS 8:17; GENESIS 5:32"> All the people on the earth are descended from those three sons of Noah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## What happened after Noah got drunk

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:6; PROVERBS 10:11; Isaiah 28:24–26"> Noah was a farmer. He planted grapevines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PROVERBS 20:1; HABAKKUK 2:15–16; PROVERBS 23:31–32"> When they later produced grapes, he made wine from the grapes. One day, when he drank too much of the wine, he became drunk, and he lay naked in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PROVERBS 30:17; GALATIANS 6:1; MATTHEW 18:15"> Ham, the father of Canaan, saw his father lying naked in the tent. So he went outside and told his two older brothers what he had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:1; EXODUS 20:12; 1 TIMOTHY 5:17"> Then Shem and Japheth took a large cloth and placed it across their backs, and walked backwards into the tent. They covered their father’s naked body with the cloth. Their faces were turned away from their father, so they did not see him naked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> When Noah woke up and was sober again, he found out how wrongfully Ham, his youngest son, had behaved toward him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 9:23; DEUTERONOMY 27:16; JUDGES 1:28–30"> He said, “I am cursing Ham’s youngest son, Canaan, and his descendants. They will be like slaves to their uncles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 144:15; GENESIS 12:1–3; LUKE 3:23–36"> I will ask God to enlarge the territory that belongs to Japheth, and allow his descendants to live peacefully among the descendants of Shem &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ROMANS 11:12; ROMANS 15:12; HEBREWS 11:9–10"> And I desire that Canaan’s descendants will be like slaves of Japheth’s descendants.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Noah lived 350 more years after the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 5:27; PSALMS 90:10; GENESIS 5:32"> He died when he was 950 years old.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
