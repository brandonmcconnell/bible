## Yahweh told Abram to go to Canaan and promised to bless him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:8; Nehemiah 9:7; ACTS 7:2–6"> Then Yahweh said to Abram, “Leave this country where you are now living. Leave your father’s clan and his family. Go to a land that I will show you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:9; GENESIS 18:18; GALATIANS 3:14"> I will cause your descendants to become a large nation. I will bless you and cause you to become ◄famous/highly esteemed►. What I do for you will be a blessing to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:8; GENESIS 22:18; GENESIS 27:29"> I will bless those who ◄bless/ask God to do good things for► you, and I will ◄curse/ask God to punish► those who do evil things to you. And because of what you do, groups of people all over the earth will receive blessings (OR, people will wish that God will bless others as much as he has blessed you).”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:8; GENESIS 11:31; GENESIS 11:27"> So Abram left Haran town, as Yahweh told him to do. Abram was 75 years old when he left there along with his wife, Sarai, and his nephew, Lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 14:14; GENESIS 11:31; HEBREWS 11:8–9"> Abram also took along all the possessions and slaves that they had accumulated/acquired in Haran town, and they left there and went to Canaan land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:9; DEUTERONOMY 11:30; GENESIS 35:4"> In Canaan they traveled as far as Shechem town and camped by a huge tree called the tree of Moreh. The Canaan people-group were still living in that land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 17:8; GENESIS 18:1; GENESIS 13:15"> Then Yahweh appeared to Abram and said to him, “I will give this land to your descendants.” Then Abram built a stone altar to offer a sacrifice to Yahweh because Yahweh had appeared to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:31; GENESIS 4:26; GENESIS 21:33"> From Shechem, Abram and his family traveled to the hills that were east of Bethel town. Bethel town was to the west of where they set up their tent, and Ai town was further to the east. There he built another stone altar and offered a sacrifice and worshiped Yahweh there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 13:3; GENESIS 24:62; GENESIS 13:1"> Then they left there and started traveling south to the Negev Desert.</span>

## Abram and Sarai went to Egypt for a while

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 43:1; GENESIS 26:1–3; 1 KINGS 17:1–18"> There was ◄a famine/very little food to eat► in Canaan, so they went south to live in Egypt for a while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 26:7; GENESIS 29:17; GENESIS 12:14"> Just as they were about to enter Egypt, Abram said to his wife Sarai, “Listen, I know that you are a very beautiful woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 20:11; GENESIS 26:7; 1 JOHN 1:8–10"> When the people in Egypt see you, they will say, ‘This woman is his wife!’ and they will kill me in order to get you, but they will not kill you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 26:7; GENESIS 20:12–13; GENESIS 20:2"> So I ask you to tell them that you are my sister, so that because of you telling them that, they will ◄spare my life/not kill me►.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 6:2; MATTHEW 5:28; GENESIS 39:7"> And as soon as they arrived in Egypt, the people in Egypt saw that his wife was indeed very beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 20:2; GENESIS 41:1; 2 KINGS 18:21"> When the king’s officials saw her, they told the king how beautiful she was. And they took her to the king’s palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 20:14; GENESIS 13:2; GENESIS 24:35"> The king treated Abram kindly because of Sarai, and he gave Abram many sheep and cattle and donkeys and male and female slaves and camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 16:21; GENESIS 20:18; PSALMS 105:14–15"> But because the king had taken Sarai, Abram’s wife, Yahweh caused the king and the others in his household to be inflicted with terrible diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 20:9–10; GENESIS 26:9–11; 1 SAMUEL 14:43"> When the king realized why that was happening, he summoned Abram and said to him, “Why did you do this to me? Why did you not tell me that she was your wife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Why did you lie, saying she is your sister, with the result that I took her to be my wife? You should not have done that! So now take your wife, leave here and go!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 105:14–15; PROVERBS 21:1; EXODUS 18:27"> Then the king told his officials/servants to take Abram and his wife and all his possessions out of Egypt.</span>
</div>
