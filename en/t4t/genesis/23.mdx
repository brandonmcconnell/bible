## After Sarah died, Abraham bought a burial place for her

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 17:17"> When Sarah was 127 years old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 23:19; GENESIS 13:18; 1 CHRONICLES 6:57"> she died at Kiriath-Arba town, which is now called Hebron, in the Canaan region. After Abraham mourned for her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 10:15; GENESIS 23:7; 1 SAMUEL 26:6"> he left the body of his wife and went and spoke to some of the descendants of Heth, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 29:15; HEBREWS 11:9; GENESIS 17:8"> “I am a foreigner &#91;DOU&#93; living among you, so I do not own any land here. Sell me some land here so that I can bury my wife’s body.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> They replied to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 24:35; GENESIS 14:14; EXODUS 32:22"> “Sir, you are a powerful prince among us. Choose one of our finest tombs and bury your wife’s body in it. None of us will refuse to sell land to you for a tomb for your relatives’ bodies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:14; GENESIS 19:1; PROVERBS 18:24"> Then Abraham stood up and bowed in respect in front of the people who owned the land, the descendants of Heth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 25:9; 1 JOHN 2:1–2; 1 KINGS 2:17"> He said to them, “If you say that you are willing for me to bury my wife’s body here, listen to me, and ask Ephron, the son of Zohar for me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ROMANS 13:8; ROMANS 12:17"> to sell to me the cave in the Machpelah area, which is at the end of his field. Ask him to sell it to me for the full price he wants, so that I can have a burial ground among you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 34:20; GENESIS 34:24; GENESIS 23:18"> Ephron was sitting among the people near the city gate where many &#91;HYP&#93; descendants of Heth had gathered. He heard what Abraham said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 19:24; Isaiah 32:8; DEUTERONOMY 19:15"> So, just to make the customary reply to start the real discussion about a price, Ephron said, “No, sir, listen to me. I will give to you the field and the cave in it, without charge, with the people here as witnesses.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 23:7; GENESIS 18:2; GENESIS 19:1"> Abraham again bowed before the people who lived in the area,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ROMANS 13:8; 2 SAMUEL 24:24; GENESIS 14:22–23"> and said to Ephron, as all the others were listening, “No, listen to me. If you are willing, I will pay for the field. You tell me what the price is, and I will give it to you. If you accept it, the field will become mine, and I can bury my wife’s body there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Ephron answered Abraham, saying to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 45:12; EXODUS 30:13; EXODUS 30:15"> “Sir, listen to me. The land is worth 400 pieces of silver. But the price is not important to you and me. Give me the money and bury your wife’s body there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:12; JEREMIAH 32:9; EXODUS 30:13"> Abraham agreed with Ephron about the price, and weighed for Ephron the 400 pieces of silver he had suggested, as everyone was listening, using the standard weights for silver used in that area by people who sold things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 25:9; GENESIS 50:13; GENESIS 23:20"> So Ephron’s field in the Machpelah area, near Mamre, and the cave in the field, and all the trees that were in the field inside the boundaries of the land, became Abraham’s possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="RUTH 4:1; JEREMIAH 32:12; GENESIS 34:20"> It became Abraham’s property as all the descendants of Heth were listening there at the city gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 50:13; ECCLESIASTES 12:7; ECCLESIASTES 12:5"> After that, Abraham buried his wife Sarah’s body in the cave in the field in the Machpelah area near Mamre, which is now called Hebron city, in the Canaan region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:10–14; RUTH 4:7–10; GENESIS 49:31–32"> So the field and the cave in it were officially sold to Abraham by the descendants of Heth, to be used as a burial ground.</span>
</div>
