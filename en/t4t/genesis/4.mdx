## God punished Adam and Eve’s oldest son Cain for killing his younger brother Abel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 JOHN 3:12; GENESIS 3:15; GENESIS 4:25"> Adam ◄had sex/slept► with his wife Eve, and she became pregnant and gave birth to a son whom she named Cain, which sounds like the word that means ‘produce’, because, she said, “By Yahweh’s help I have produced a son.” Some time later she gave birth to another son, and she named him Abel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 JOHN 3:15; 1 JOHN 3:12; LUKE 11:51"> After those boys grew up, Abel ◄tended/took care of► sheep and goats, and Cain became a farmer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:12; LEVITICUS 2:1–11; 1 KINGS 17:7"> One day Cain harvested some of the crops he had grown and brought them to Yahweh as a gift for him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:4; PROVERBS 3:9; EXODUS 13:12"> and Abel took from his flock some of the first lambs that had been born and killed them and, as a gift, gave to Yahweh the fatty parts, which were the best parts. Yahweh was pleased with Abel and his offering,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:4; JOB 5:2; LUKE 15:28–30"> but he was not pleased with Cain and his offering. So Cain became very angry, and his face looked dejected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:18; JOB 5:2; LUKE 15:31–32"> Yahweh said to Cain, “◄You should not be angry!/Why are you angry?► &#91;RHQ&#93; ◄You should not scowl like that!/Why do you scowl like that?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JAMES 1:15; ROMANS 2:6–10; Isaiah 3:10–11"> If you had done what was right (OR, if you do what is right) &#91;RHQ&#93;, I would accept your offering. But if you do not do what is right, your desire to sin is ready to attack you like a wild animal that &#91;PRS&#93; crouches outside the doorway, ready to spring on its victim &#91;MET&#93;. Your desire to sin wants to control you, but you must ◄control/not obey► it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:35; 1 JOHN 3:12–15; JUDE 1:11"> But one day, Cain said to his younger brother Abel, “Let’s go for a walk in the fields.” So they went together. And when they were in the countryside, suddenly Cain attacked Abel and killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:13; JOHN 8:44; PSALMS 9:12"> Later, even though Yahweh knew what Cain had done, he said to Cain, “Do you know where Abel, your younger brother, is?”<br />Cain replied, “No, I do not know. ◄My job is not to guard my younger brother!/Am I supposed to take care of my younger brother?►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:24; PSALMS 72:14; HEBREWS 11:4"> Yahweh said, “You have done a terrible thing &#91;RHQ&#93;! So now it is as though your younger brother’s voice is crying to me from the ground, demanding that his death must be avenged.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:10; JOB 16:18; GENESIS 3:14"> You have killed your younger brother, and the ground has soaked up his blood. So now I will expel you from this land and curse your efforts to produce crops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HOSEA 9:17; LEVITICUS 26:36; LEVITICUS 26:20"> You will till the ground to plant crops, but the ground will produce very few &#91;HYP&#93; crops. And you will continually wander around the earth, and not have any place to live permanently.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOB 15:22; REVELATION 16:9; REVELATION 16:11"> Cain replied, “You are punishing me more then I can endure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:19; PSALMS 143:7; JOB 15:20–24"> You are about to expel me from the ground that I have been cultivating, and I will no longer be able to come ◄into your presence/and talk with you►. Furthermore, I will be continually wandering around the earth with no place to live permanently, and anyone who sees me will kill me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EZEKIEL 9:4; PSALMS 79:12; GENESIS 4:24"> But Yahweh said to him, “No, that will not happen. I will put a mark on you to warn anyone who sees you that I will punish him severely if he kills you. I will punish that person seven times as severely as I am punishing you.” Then Yahweh put a mark on Cain’s forehead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:39; JOB 2:7; GENESIS 4:14"> So Cain left Yahweh and went to live in the land called Nod, which means ‘wandering’, which was east of Eden.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The descendants of Cain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 49:11; ECCLESIASTES 2:4–11; DANIEL 4:30"> Some time later, Cain ◄had sex/slept► with his wife, and she became pregnant and gave birth to a son, whom she named Enoch. Then Cain started to build a city, and he named the city ‘Enoch’, the same name that his son had.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 36:2; GENESIS 5:21"> Enoch grew up and married and became the father of a son whom he named Irad. When Irad grew up he became the father of a son whom he named Mehujael. Mehujael grew up and became the father of a son whom he named Methuselah. Methuselah grew up and became the father of Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 2:24; MATTHEW 19:8; GENESIS 2:18"> When Lamech grew up he married two women. The name of one was Adah and the other was Zillah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 4:2; JEREMIAH 35:9–10; JOHN 8:44"> Adah gave birth to a son she named Jabal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 21:12; Isaiah 5:12; GENESIS 31:27"> Later, Jabal was the first person who lived in tents because he traveled from place to place to take care of livestock. His younger brother’s name was Jubal. He was the first person who made/played a ◄lyre/stringed instrument► and a flute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:25; DEUTERONOMY 8:9; 2 CHRONICLES 2:7"> Lamech’s other wife Zillah gave birth to a son whom she named Tubal-Cain. Later Tubal-Cain became a ◄blacksmith/one who made tools from bronze and iron►. Tubal-Cain had a younger sister whose name was Naamah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:18; EXODUS 20:13; LEVITICUS 19:18"> One day Lamech said to his two wives, “Adah and Zillah, listen carefully to what I am saying. A young man struck me and wounded me, so I killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:22; GENESIS 4:15"> Yahweh said long ago that he would avenge and punish anyone who killed Cain seven times as much as he punished Cain for killing his younger brother. So if anyone tries to kill me, I will punish him 77 times as much as Yahweh punished Cain.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Adam and Eve had another son, Seth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 4:8; GENESIS 4:10–11; 1 CHRONICLES 1:1"> Adam continued to ◄have sex/sleep► with &#91;EUP&#93; his wife, and she became pregnant and gave birth to another son, whom she named Seth, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘given’, because, she said, “God has given me another child to take the place of Abel, since Cain killed him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 116:17; 1 KINGS 18:24; JOEL 2:32"> When Seth grew up, he became the father of a son whom he named Enosh. About that time people began to worship Yahweh again.</span>
</div>
</div>
