## Joseph’s brothers went to Egypt to buy grain, but didn’t realize they were buying it from their brother

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ACTS 7:12; JOSHUA 7:10; 2 KINGS 8:3–4"> When someone told Jacob that there was grain in Egypt that people could buy, he said to his sons, “◄Why do you just sit there looking at each other?/Do not just sit there looking at each other!► &#91;RHQ&#93; We need some grain!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 43:8; GENESIS 45:9; GENESIS 43:2"> He said to them, “Someone told me that there is grain for sale in Egypt. Go down there and buy some for us, in order that we will not die!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 42:5; GENESIS 42:13"> So Joseph’s ten older brothers went down to Egypt to buy some grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 42:38; GENESIS 43:29; GENESIS 11:4"> But Jacob did not send Benjamin, Joseph’s younger brother, to go with the others, because he was afraid/worried that something terrible might happen to him like what happened to Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 41:57; ACTS 7:11; GENESIS 12:10"> So Jacob’s other sons went down from Canaan to Egypt to buy grain, and others from there went too, because there was a famine in Canaan also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="REVELATION 3:9; GENESIS 37:7–10; GENESIS 41:40–41"> At that time Joseph was the governor of Egypt. He was the one who sold grain to people who came from all over Egypt and from many other countries &#91;HYP&#93; to buy grain. So when Joseph’s brothers arrived, they were told that it was necessary for them to talk with Joseph. So they went to him and prostrated themselves before him with their faces to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 42:30; GENESIS 42:14–17; MATTHEW 15:23–26"> As soon as Joseph saw his brothers, he recognized them. But he pretended that he did not know them. He spoke harshly to them, saying, “Where do you come from?” One of them replied, “We have come from Canaan, to buy some grain.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 37:2; JOHN 20:14; LUKE 24:16"> Although Joseph recognized his brothers, they did not recognize him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 37:5–9; EXODUS 32:35; JOSHUA 2:1"> And then Joseph remembered what he had dreamed about them many years previously. But he decided not to tell them yet that he was their younger brother. He said to them, “You are spies! You have come to find out whether we will be able to defend ourselves if you attack us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 37:8; 1 SAMUEL 26:17; GENESIS 27:37"> One of them replied, “No, sir! We have come to buy grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 42:19; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:4; JOHN 7:18"> We are all sons of one man. We are honest men, not spies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> He said to them, “I do not believe you. You have come just to see whether we would be able to defend ourselves if we were attacked!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 37:30; GENESIS 42:32; GENESIS 44:20"> But one of them replied, “No, that is not true! Originally there were twelve of us who were brothers, the sons of one man. Our younger brother is with our father. One younger brother has died. &#91;EUP&#93;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:21–28; JOB 13:24; GENESIS 42:9–11"> Joseph replied, “You are lying! I think it is just as I told you. You are spies!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:55; JEREMIAH 5:7; GENESIS 42:16"> But this is how I will determine whether what you are saying is true. I think that as surely as the king lives, you are spies. And you will not leave this place until your youngest brother comes here!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 42:11"> Send one of your group to go and get your younger brother and bring him here. I will put the rest of you in prison, in order that I may test what you have said to find out whether what you are telling me is true. If the one who goes does not bring your younger brother here, then, just as surely as the king lives, it will be clear that you are lying and that you are spies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 40:4; HEBREWS 12:10; GENESIS 40:7"> Then Joseph put them all in prison for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:43; Nehemiah 5:15; LUKE 18:2"> On the third day, Joseph went to the prison and said to them, “I am a man who fears that God will punish me if I do not do what I promise. So do what I tell you, and I will spare your lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 45:23; Isaiah 42:7; GENESIS 40:3"> If you are honest men, let one of your brothers stay here in prison, and the rest of you can take some grain back to your families who are very hungry because of the famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 44:23; GENESIS 43:5; GENESIS 42:34"> But if you come back here again, you must bring your youngest brother to me, so that you can prove that what you told me is true, and as a result I will not have you executed.” So they agreed to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HOSEA 5:15; MARK 9:43–45; GENESIS 37:23–28"> They said to each other, “It is surely because of what we did to our younger brother that ◄we are being punished/God is punishing us►! We saw that he &#91;SYN&#93; was very distressed when he pleaded with us not to harm him. But we did not pay any attention to him, and that is why we are having this trouble!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 24:22; PSALMS 9:12; 1 KINGS 2:32"> Reuben said to them, “I told you not to harm the boy &#91;RHQ&#93;, but you did not pay attention to what I said! Now we are being ◄paid back/punished► for killing him &#91;MTY&#93;!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:20; JOHN 16:13–14"> While they were talking with Joseph, they were speaking through ◄an interpreter/someone who knew their language and the Egyptian language►, but when they said these things among themselves, they were speaking in their own language, and they did not know that Joseph could understand their language, and that he could understand what they were saying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 43:30; JUDE 1:22–23; HEBREWS 4:15"> Because of what they said, Joseph realized that they admitted that what they had done to him many years previously was wrong. He could not keep from crying, and he did not want them to see him crying, so he left them and went outside the room and began to cry. But then he returned to them and talked to them again. Then he took Simeon, and while they were watching, he told his servants to tie him up. He left Simeon in the prison and told the others that they could go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 PETER 3:9; ROMANS 12:17–21; GENESIS 45:21"> Joseph told his servants to fill the men’s sacks with grain, but he also told them to put the money that each one had paid for the grain in the top of his sack. He also told them to give them food to eat along the way. After the servants did those things for Joseph’s older brothers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> his older brothers loaded the sacks of grain on their donkeys and left.</span>
</div>

## Joseph’s brothers returned home, frightened because the money they paid was in their sacks

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 4:24; LUKE 2:7; LUKE 10:34"> At the place where they stopped to sleep that night, one of them opened his sack to get some grain for his donkey. He was amazed to see his money in the top of the sack.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 45:7; LAMENTATIONS 3:37; GENESIS 42:36"> He exclaimed to his brothers, “Someone has returned my money! Here it is in my sack!” They started shaking with fear, and said to each other, “What is this that God has done to us?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> When they returned to their father in Canaan land, they told him all that had happened to them. One of them said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 42:7"> “The man who governs the whole land of Egypt talked very harshly to us. He acted toward us as though we were spying on his country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 42:11"> But we told him, ‘We are honest men! We are not spies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Originally there were twelve of us who were brothers, the sons of one father. One has died &#91;EUP&#93;, and our youngest brother is with our father in Canaan.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 42:19–20; GENESIS 42:15"> The man who is the governor of the land did not believe us, so he said to us, ‘This is how I will know if you are truly honest men: Leave one of your brothers here with me. Then the rest of you can take some grain for your families that are starving from hunger and go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 34:10; GENESIS 34:21; 1 KINGS 10:15"> But when you return, bring your youngest brother to me, in order that I will know that you are not spies, but instead, that you are honest men. Then I will release your brother for you. And then you can buy whatever you want in this country.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GENESIS 43:21; GENESIS 43:12; GENESIS 43:15"> As they were emptying their sacks, they were surprised that in each man’s sack was his pouch of money! When they and their father saw all the pouches of money, they were frightened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GENESIS 43:14; ROMANS 8:31; Isaiah 27:9"> Their father Jacob said to them, “You have caused two of my children to be taken from me! Joseph is dead, and Simeon is gone! And now you want to take Benjamin from me! It is I who am suffering because of all these things that are happening!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="GENESIS 46:9; MICAH 6:7; GENESIS 43:9"> Reuben said to his father, “I will be responsible for Benjamin. I will take him to Egypt and bring him back to you. Let me take care of him. If I do not bring Benjamin back to you, you may kill both of my sons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="GENESIS 37:35; GENESIS 42:4; GENESIS 37:33"> But Jacob said, “No, I will not let my son go down there with you. His older brother is dead, and he is the only one of my wife Rachel’s sons who is left! If something harms him while you are traveling, you would cause me, a gray-haired old man, to die because of sorrow.”</span>
</div>
