<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 31:17; EXODUS 20:11; PSALMS 33:6"> That is the way God created the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:4; EXODUS 31:17; DEUTERONOMY 5:14"> By the time it was the seventh day, God had finished the work of creating everything, so he did not work any more on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:12; MARK 2:27; LEVITICUS 23:3"> God blessed each seventh day, and he set those days apart to be special days, because on the seventh day God did not work any more, after finishing all his work of creating everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 5:1; REVELATION 1:8; GENESIS 1:4"> That is how God created the heavens and the earth. God made the garden of Eden<br />God, whose name is Yahweh, made the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 3:23; HEBREWS 6:7; JOB 5:10"> At first there were no plants growing, because Yahweh God had not yet caused rain to fall on the ground. Furthermore, there was no one to till the ground for planting crops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Instead, mist rose up from the ground, so that it watered the surface of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:7; JOB 33:4; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:45"> Then Yahweh God took some soil and formed a man. He breathed into the man’s nostrils his own breath that gives life, and as a result the man became a living person.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:13; GENESIS 3:24; GENESIS 13:10"> Yahweh God made a park/garden in a place named Eden, which was east of the land of Canaan, and he put there the man that he had created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 3:22; REVELATION 2:7; REVELATION 22:14"> Yahweh God also put there every kind of tree that is beautiful to see and that produced fruit that was good to eat. He also placed in the middle of the park/garden a tree whose fruit would enable those who ate it to live forever. He also placed there another tree whose fruit would enable those who ate it to know what actions were good to do and what actions were evil to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="REVELATION 22:1; PSALMS 46:4"> A river flowed from Eden to provide water for the park/garden. Outside of Eden, the river divided into four rivers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 25:18; GENESIS 10:29; 1 SAMUEL 15:7"> The name of the first river is Pishon. That river flows through all the land of Havilah, where there is gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 28:20; NUMBERS 11:7; EZEKIEL 28:13"> That gold ◄is very pure/has no impurities in it►. There is also a sweet-smelling gum called bdellium, and valuable stones called onyx.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 10:6; Isaiah 11:11"> The name of the second river is Gihon. That river flows through all the land of Cain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DANIEL 10:4; GENESIS 15:18; DEUTERONOMY 1:7"> The name of the third river is Tigris. It flows east of the city of Asshur. The name of the fourth river is Euphrates.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PSALMS 128:2; GENESIS 2:8; EPHESIANS 4:28"> Yahweh God took the man and put him in Eden park/garden to till/cultivate it and take care of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:22; 1 TIMOTHY 4:4; 1 TIMOTHY 6:17"> But Yahweh said to him, “You must not eat the fruit of the tree that will enable you to know what actions are good to do and what actions are evil to do. If you eat any fruit from that tree, on that day your relationship with me will end. But I will permit you to eat the fruit of any of the other trees in the park/garden.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 4:9–12; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:7–12; PROVERBS 18:22"> Then Yahweh God said, “It is not good for this man to be alone. So I will make someone who will be a suitable partner for him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 8:4–8; GENESIS 1:28; GENESIS 9:2"> Yahweh God had taken some soil and had formed from it all kinds of animals and birds, and he brought them to the man to hear what names he would give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 2:18"> Then the man gave names to all the kinds of livestock and birds and wild animals, but none of these creatures was a partner that was suitable for the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 15:12; JOB 4:13; 1 SAMUEL 26:12"> So Yahweh God caused the man to fall into a deep sleep. While the man was sleeping, Yahweh took out one of the man’s ribs. Then he immediately closed the opening in his body and healed it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 11:8–9; 1 TIMOTHY 2:13; HEBREWS 13:4"> Yahweh then made a woman from the rib that he had taken from the man’s body, and he brought her to the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:28–30; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:8–9; 2 SAMUEL 5:1"> The man exclaimed, “Wow! This is truly ◄someone like me/what I was looking for!► She is truly from my bones and from my flesh. So I will call her woman, because she was taken from me, a man.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:3–9; EPHESIANS 5:28–31; MARK 10:6–12"> The first woman was taken from the man’s body, so that is why when a man and a woman marry, they must leave their parents. The man will join very closely to his wife, so that the two of them will be as though they are one person.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 3:7; GENESIS 3:10–11; ROMANS 10:11"> Although the man and his wife were naked, they were not ashamed about that.</span>
</div>
