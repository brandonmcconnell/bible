## Angels warned Lot that Sodom would be destroyed because of the people’s wickedness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 18:22; GENESIS 18:1–5; HEBREWS 13:2"> That evening, the two angels arrived in Sodom. Lot was sitting at the gateway of the city. When he saw them, he got up to greet them, and prostrated himself with his face on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 18:4; LUKE 24:28–29; HEBREWS 13:2"> He said to them, “Gentlemen, please stay in my house tonight. You can wash your feet, and tomorrow you can continue your journey.” But they said, “No, we will just sleep in the city square.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 18:6–8; JUDGES 6:19; 1 SAMUEL 28:24"> But Lot kept insisting strongly that they sleep in his house. So they entered his house with him, and he prepared a meal for them. He baked some bread without yeast, and they ate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 13:13; PROVERBS 4:16; EXODUS 23:2"> After they finished eating, before they went to bed, the men of Sodom city, all of them, from the young ones to the old ones, surrounded the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JUDGES 19:22; Isaiah 3:9; JUDE 1:7"> They called out to Lot, saying, “Where are the men who came to your house this evening? Bring them out, so that we can have sex with them!” &#91;EUP&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDGES 19:23"> Lot went outside the house and shut the door behind him, so that they could not go inside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 6:9–11; JUDGES 19:23; LEVITICUS 20:13"> He said to them, “My friends, don’t do such an evil thing!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 19:24; GENESIS 19:31–38; JUDGES 9:15"> Listen to me. I have two daughters who have never had sex with any man. Let me bring them out to you now, and you can do with them whatever pleases you. But don’t do anything to these men, because they are guests in my house, so I must protect them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 2:14; 2 PETER 2:7–8; GENESIS 13:12"> But they replied, “Get out of our way! You are a foreigner; so you have no right to tell us what is right! If you don’t get out of our way, we will do worse things to you than we will do to them!” Then they lunged towards Lot, and tried forcefully to break down the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> But the two angels opened the door carefully, reached out their hands, and pulled Lot to them inside the house. Then they quickly shut the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 KINGS 6:18; ACTS 13:11; DEUTERONOMY 28:28–29"> Then they caused all the men who were outside the door of the house, young and old, to become blind, so that they could not find the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 7:1; 2 PETER 2:7; REVELATION 18:4"> Then the two angels said to Lot, “Who else is with you here? If you have sons or sons-in-law or daughters or anyone else in the city who is related to you, take them out of the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 18:20; MATTHEW 13:49–50; JUDE 1:7"> because we are the ones who are going to destroy this place. Yahweh considers that these people are so evil that he sent us to destroy the city.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:21; JEREMIAH 51:6; NUMBERS 16:45"> So Lot went and spoke to the men who had pledged to marry his daughters. He said to them, “Hurry! Get out of this city, because Yahweh is about to destroy it!” But his future sons-in-law thought he was joking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:24–27; REVELATION 18:4; GENESIS 19:22"> As it was about to dawn the next morning, the two angels urged Lot, saying, “Get up quickly! Take your wife and your two daughters who are here with you and leave! If you don’t, you will die when we destroy/Yahweh destroys the city!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 9:15–16; TITUS 3:5; JOHN 6:44"> When Lot hesitated, the angels grasped his hand and his wife’s hand and the hands of his two daughters. They led them outside the city safely. The angels did that because Yahweh was acting mercifully toward them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:16–18; LUKE 9:62; PHILIPPIANS 3:13–14"> When they were outside the city, one of the angels said, “If you want to remain alive, run away quickly! Don’t look back! And don’t stop anywhere in the valley! Flee to the hills! If you don’t, you will die!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 13:6–8; ACTS 10:14; GENESIS 32:26"> But Lot said to one of the angels, “No, sir, don’t make me do that!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 27:1; PSALMS 103:1–22; 1 TIMOTHY 1:14–16"> Please, listen. You have been pleased with me and have been very kind to me and spared my life. But I cannot flee to the mountain. If I try to do that, I will die in this disaster &#91;PRS&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 119:175; PROVERBS 3:5–7; GENESIS 19:30"> But listen to me. There is a town nearby. Let me run to that small town. Let me go there now. It is a small town, but our lives will be saved if we go there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 145:19; PSALMS 34:15; PSALMS 102:17"> One of the angels said to Lot, “I will allow you to do what you have requested &#91;MTY&#93;. I will not destroy the little town you are speaking about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 13:10; GENESIS 14:2; GENESIS 32:25–28"> But hurry! Run there, because I cannot destroy anything until you arrive there.” People later called the name of the town Zoar which means ‘small’, because Lot said it was small.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah cities

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> As the sun was rising, Lot and his family arrived in the town which is now called Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:23; Isaiah 13:19; AMOS 4:11"> Then Yahweh caused fire and burning sulfur to fall down on Sodom and Gomorrah like rain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 107:34; GENESIS 14:3; GENESIS 13:10"> In that way, he destroyed those cities and all the people who were living in those cities. He also destroyed everything in the valley, including all the plants/vegetation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKE 17:31–32; GENESIS 19:17; PROVERBS 14:14"> But Lot’s wife stopped and looked back to see what was happening, so she died, and her body later became a pillar of salt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:1; PSALMS 5:3; HEBREWS 2:1"> That morning, Abraham got up and went to the place where he had stood in front of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="REVELATION 18:9; REVELATION 9:2; REVELATION 21:8"> He looked down toward Sodom and Gomorrah, and he was surprised to see that all over the valley, smoke was rising, like the smoke of a huge furnace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 8:1; DEUTERONOMY 9:5; GENESIS 18:23–33"> So, when God destroyed those cities in the valley, he remembered what he had promised Abraham, and he rescued Lot from the disaster that occurred in the cities where Lot had lived.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Lot’s daughters tricked him into making them pregnant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 19:19; Isaiah 15:5; JAMES 1:8"> Lot was afraid to stay in Zoar, so he left there and moved with his two daughters to the mountain, and they lived in a cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:5; Isaiah 4:1; GENESIS 16:2"> One day the older daughter said to ◄the younger daughter/her sister►, “Our father is old, and there is no man in this area who can have sex &#91;EUP&#93; with us, as people all over the earth do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:6–7; GENESIS 11:3; GENESIS 9:21"> Let’s make our father drink wine until he gets drunk. Then we can have sex with him &#91;EUP&#93; without him knowing it. In that way he will cause us to become pregnant and we can bear children.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:15–16; PROVERBS 23:29–35; PROVERBS 20:1"> So that night they caused their father to become drunk. And the older daughter went in and lay in bed with her father, and he had sex with her, but he was so drunk that he did not know when she lay down with him or when she got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:12; JEREMIAH 6:15; Isaiah 3:9"> The next day, his older daughter said to ◄his younger daughter/her sister►, “Listen to me. Last night I slept with our father. Let’s cause him to become drunk again tonight! This time you can go and sleep with him. If he has sex with you, you can become pregnant, and that way you can have a child, too.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:16; PSALMS 8:4; 1 PETER 4:7"> So that night, they caused their father to become drunk with wine again, and then his younger daughter went and slept with him. But again, he was so drunk that he did not know when she lay down with him or when she got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:15; JUDGES 1:7; MATTHEW 7:2"> So Lot caused his two daughters to become pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:9; 2 KINGS 3:1–27; NUMBERS 21:29"> The older one later gave birth to a son, whom she named Moab. He became the ancestor of the Moab people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:19; JUDGES 10:6–40; PSALMS 83:4–8"> The younger one also gave birth to a son, whom she named Ben-Ammi. He became the ancestor of the Ammon people-group.</span>
</div>
