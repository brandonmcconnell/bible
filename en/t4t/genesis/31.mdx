## Jacob’s family fled from Laban

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 4:4; PSALMS 49:16–17; JOB 31:31"> Someone told Jacob that Laban’s sons were complaining and saying, “Jacob has become very rich by taking everything &#91;HYP&#93; that belonged to our father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DANIEL 3:19; 1 SAMUEL 18:9–11; GENESIS 4:5"> And Jacob noticed that Laban was not acting friendly toward him as he had done before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 28:15; GENESIS 32:9; HEBREWS 13:5"> Then Yahweh said to Jacob, “Go back to your country and your relatives, and I will help you there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So Jacob sent a message to Rachel and Leah, telling them to come out to the pastures where his flocks of sheep and goats were.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 31:2–3; GENESIS 31:53; GENESIS 48:15"> When they arrived, he said to them, “I see that your father does not act friendly toward me as he did previously. But God, whom my father worshiped, has helped me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 30:29; EPHESIANS 6:5–8; TITUS 2:9–10"> You two know that I have worked very hard for your father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 37:28; GENESIS 31:29; NUMBERS 14:22"> but many times &#91;HYP&#93; he has cheated me by decreasing my wages. But God has not allowed him to harm me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 30:32"> When Laban said, ‘The speckled animals are the ones that I will give you to be your wages,’ then all the animals gave birth to young ones that were speckled. When he changed and said, ‘The ones that have black and white stripes on them will be your wages,’ then all the animals gave birth to young ones that were striped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 31:1; GENESIS 31:16; PROVERBS 13:22"> In that way, God has taken away the livestock that belonged to your father and has given them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 13:1; GENESIS 30:39; GENESIS 20:6"> “One time, when the animals were mating, I had a dream. In my dream I looked up and was surprised to see that some of the male goats that were mating with the female goats had black and white stripes on them, some were speckled, and some were spotted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; GENESIS 31:13; 1 SAMUEL 3:6"> An angel who was sent by God said to me in the dream, ‘Jacob!’ I replied, ‘I am here!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 3:7; DEUTERONOMY 24:15; GENESIS 31:42"> He said to me, ‘Look up and you will see that all the male goats that are mating have black and white stripes on them, or are speckled or spotted. This is happening because I have seen all that Laban has done to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 31:3; GENESIS 32:9; GENESIS 28:12–22"> I am God who appeared to you at Bethel, where you set up a stone to show that the place was holy, and you poured olive oil on the stone and made a solemn promise to me. So now leave this land immediately, and return to the land where you were born.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="RUTH 4:11; GENESIS 2:24; GENESIS 29:24"> Rachel and Leah replied, “Our father will not give us anything more when he dies &#91;RHQ&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 29:15–20; GENESIS 31:41; GENESIS 29:27–30"> He treats us as though we were foreigners &#91;RHQ&#93;! Your working for him all these years was like a payment that you gave him as a price for us, but we will not inherit any of that money that he got as a price for us. He has spent it all!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 30:35–1; GENESIS 31:9; PSALMS 45:10"> Surely all of the wealth that God took away from our father belongs to us and to our children. So do whatever God has told you to do!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:17; GENESIS 24:61; GENESIS 24:10"> Then Jacob put his children and his wives on camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 27:1–2; GENESIS 27:41; GENESIS 35:27–29"> He made all his livestock go ahead of him. Besides the livestock, he took along all the other things that he had acquired while living in Paddan-Aram. And they prepared to return to his father Isaac, in the Canaan region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 19:13; GENESIS 31:30; HOSEA 3:4"> Before they left, while Laban was shearing his sheep, Rachel stole the small wooden idols that were in her father’s tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Furthermore, Jacob deceived Laban, who belonged to the Aram people-group, by not telling him that they were planning to leave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 KINGS 12:17; GENESIS 37:25; GENESIS 46:28"> So Jacob and his family fled with all their possessions, and they crossed the Euphrates River, and then started traveling south toward the hilly Gilead region.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob rebuked Laban after Laban caught up with him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 5:12–13; GENESIS 30:36; EXODUS 14:5–31"> On the third day after they left, someone told Laban that Jacob and his family had left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 13:8; GENESIS 24:27; EXODUS 2:11"> So he took some of his relatives with him and started to pursue Jacob. They continued walking for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 24:50; GENESIS 31:29; GENESIS 20:3"> Then God appeared to Laban in a dream at night, and said to him, “When you catch up to Jacob, be sure that you do not say anything at all &#91;HYP&#93; to him in an angry manner!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 12:8; GENESIS 33:18; HEBREWS 11:9"> The next day, by the time Laban caught up with Jacob, Jacob and his household had set up their tents in the hilly Gilead region. So Laban and his relatives set up their tents there, too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:2; GENESIS 12:18; GENESIS 20:9–10"> Then Laban went to Jacob and said to him, “Why have you done this? You have deceived me by carrying away my daughters as though you had captured them in a war &#91;MTY&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 15:20; PROVERBS 26:23–26; GENESIS 31:3–5"> Why did you run away and deceive me? Why did you not tell me that you were going to leave, so that we could have rejoiced and sung while people played music on tambourines and harps before I said ‘goodbye’ to you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 31:55; RUTH 1:14; ACTS 20:37"> ◄You did not even let me kiss my grandchildren and my daughters goodbye before they left!/Why did you not allow me to kiss my grandchildren and daughters goodbye before they left?► &#91;RHQ&#93; What you have done was foolish!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 31:53; GENESIS 31:42; GENESIS 31:24"> My relatives and I have the power to harm you, but last night the God whom your father worships said to me in a dream, ‘Be sure that you do not say anything at all to Jacob in an angry way.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:11; GENESIS 31:19; JUDGES 18:24"> Now, I know you have left because you long to go back home. But why did you steal my wooden idols?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:25; GENESIS 31:26–27; GENESIS 20:11"> Jacob replied to Laban, saying, “I did not tell you that we were planning to leave, because I was afraid. I thought that if I told you, you would forcefully take your daughters away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 12:3–5; GENESIS 31:30; GENESIS 31:19"> But if you find anyone here who has your wooden idols, we will execute that person. While our relatives are watching, search for yourself to see if there is anything that belongs to you that is here with me. If you find anything, you can take it!” When Jacob said that, he did not know that Rachel had stolen the wooden idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 24:67; GENESIS 24:28"> Laban went into Jacob’s tent, and then into Leah’s tent, and then into the tents of the two female slaves and searched for the idols, but he did not find them. After he left their tents, he entered Rachel’s tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 31:19; GENESIS 31:17"> But Rachel had previously taken the idols and put them in the saddle of a camel, and she was sitting on the saddle. So when Laban searched all over for them inside Rachel’s tent, he did not find them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:32; EXODUS 20:12; 1 PETER 2:18"> Rachel said to her father, “Do not be angry with me, sir, but I cannot get up in your presence to show respect for you, because I am having my monthly menstrual period. &#91;EUP&#93;” So when Laban searched some more, he did not find the wooden idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:26; GENESIS 49:7; NUMBERS 16:15"> Then Jacob became angry. He rebuked Laban, saying, “What crime did I commit? For what sin that I committed have you pursued me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 PETER 2:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 6:4–5; 1 SAMUEL 12:3–4"> Now you have searched through all my possessions, and what did you find from all these possessions that belongs to you? Put it here in front of my relatives and your relatives, so that they can decide who is right, you or me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="GENESIS 30:27; DEUTERONOMY 28:4; EZEKIEL 34:2–4"> I was with you for 20 years. In all that time, your sheep and goats have ◄not miscarried/always given birth to animals safely►. I have not killed and eaten any rams from your flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="EXODUS 22:31; EXODUS 22:10–13; LEVITICUS 22:8"> When one of your animals was attacked and mauled/killed by a wild animal, I did not bring it to you. I replaced the dead animal with a living one of my own animals. Whenever one of your animals was stolen, during the day or during the night, you demanded that I replace it with one of my own animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="LUKE 2:8; EXODUS 2:19–22; 1 PETER 5:2–4"> I suffered from the heat during the day and from the cold at night. I was often not even able to sleep &#91;PRS&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="GENESIS 31:7; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:26"> I lived in your household for 20 years. I worked for you for 14 years to buy your two daughters, and for six more years to buy some of your sheep and goats. During that time, you changed and reduced my wages many times &#91;HYP&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="PSALMS 31:7; GENESIS 31:53; Isaiah 8:13"> If God, the one whom my grandfather Abraham worshiped and before whom my father Isaac trembled in fear, had not been with me and helped me, you would have sent me away ◄with nothing in my hands/owning nothing►! But God saw how much I was suffering and how hard I was working, so last night he told you that what you have done to me was wrong.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob and Laban made a solemn promise not to harm each other

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Laban replied, “These two women are my daughters, and their children are my grandchildren, and the animals are my animals. Everything you see here is mine &#91;HYP&#93;! But what can I do today to keep my daughters or the children they have given birth to &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GENESIS 31:52; JOSHUA 24:25–27; JOSHUA 22:27"> I cannot do anything in order to keep them, so hey, we should make a peace agreement, you and I, and do something that will remind us about our agreement.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="GENESIS 28:18–22"> So Jacob took a large stone and set it on its end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 3:5; GENESIS 31:23; GENESIS 31:54"> Then Jacob said to his relatives, “You also gather some stones.” So they gathered some rocks and put them in a heap, and they ate some food there near the heap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:1"> Laban gave the heap the Aramaic name Jegar-Sahadutha, which means ‘rock-pile to remind us’, but Jacob gave the rock-pile the Hebrew name Galeed, which has the same meaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:27; JOSHUA 13:8–9; GENESIS 31:23"> Laban said to Jacob, “This pile of rocks we have put here today will help us to remember our agreement.” That is why Jacob called it Galeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="JUDGES 11:29; JUDGES 10:17; 1 SAMUEL 7:5–6"> They also named the place Mizpah, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘watchtower’, because Laban said, “We will ask Yahweh to watch you and me while we are separated from each other, so that we do not try to harm each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:5; JEREMIAH 29:23; MICAH 1:2"> If you mistreat my daughters, or if you take other women to be your wives, even if no one tells me about it, don’t forget that God sees what you and I are doing!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Laban also said to Jacob, “You see this large stone and this pile of rocks that we have set up to be between us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="GENESIS 31:48; GENESIS 31:44–45"> Both this pile of rocks and this large stone will remind us, that I will not go past these rocks to harm you, and you will not go past these rocks to harm me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="GENESIS 31:42; GENESIS 16:5; JOSHUA 24:2"> We will ask the God whom your grandfather Abraham and his father Nahor worshiped to punish &#91;MTY&#93; either one of us, if we harm the other one.” So Jacob solemnly promised to do what they said in their peace agreement. And he asked God, the one before whom his father Isaac trembled, to listen to what they promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="EXODUS 18:12; GENESIS 37:25; GENESIS 26:30"> He offered a sacrifice to God there in the hilly area, and he invited his relatives to eat with him. After they had eaten, they slept there that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="GENESIS 31:28; DEUTERONOMY 23:5; GENESIS 18:33"> The next morning Laban kissed his grandchildren and his daughters goodbye and asked God to bless them. Then he and his men left them and returned home.</span>
</div>
