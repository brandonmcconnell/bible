## Jacob had more sons and a daughter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 5:2; GENESIS 29:31; GENESIS 37:11"> Rachel realized that she was not becoming pregnant and giving birth to any children for Jacob. So she became jealous of her older sister, Leah, because Leah had given birth to four sons. She said to Jacob, “Enable me to become pregnant and give birth to children. If you do not do that, I think I will die!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 16:2; PSALMS 127:3; 2 KINGS 5:7"> Jacob became angry with Rachel and said, “◄I am not God!/Am I God?► &#91;RHQ&#93; He is the one who has prevented you from becoming pregnant!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 50:23; JOB 3:12; RUTH 4:11"> Then she said, “Look, here is my female slave, Bilhah. ◄Sleep with/Have sex with► &#91;EUP&#93; her, so that she may become pregnant and give birth to children for me. In that way it will be as though ◄I have children/her children are mine►.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 35:22; GENESIS 21:10; 2 SAMUEL 12:11"> So she gave him her slave, Bilhah, to be another wife for him, and Jacob had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> She became pregnant and bore Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:59; PSALMS 35:24; PSALMS 43:1"> Rachel said, “God has ◄vindicated me/judged my case and has decided that what I have done is right►. He has also heard my requests and has given me a son.” So she named him Dan, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘he judged’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Later, Rachel’s slave Bilhah became pregnant again and gave birth to another son for Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 32:24–25; GENESIS 23:6; GENESIS 49:21"> Then Rachel said, “I have had a great struggle to have children like my older sister, but truly I have a son.” So she named him Naphtali, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘struggle’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 30:4; GENESIS 30:17; GENESIS 29:35"> When Leah realized that she was not becoming pregnant and giving birth to any more children, she took her female slave, Zilpah, and gave her to Jacob to be another wife for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Zilpah soon became pregnant and gave birth to a son for Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; GENESIS 35:26"> Leah said, “I am truly fortunate!” So she named him Gad, which means ‘fortunate’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Later Leah’s slave, Zilpah, gave birth to another son for Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LUKE 1:48; PROVERBS 31:28; SONG OF SONGS 6:9"> Leah said, “Now I am very happy, and people will call me happy.” So she named him Asher, which means ‘happy.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 7:13; GENESIS 25:30"> When it was time to harvest wheat, Reuben went out into the fields and saw some ◄mandrakes/plants that women eat to help them to become pregnant►. He brought some of them to his mother Leah. But Rachel saw them and said to Leah, “Please give me some of those plants that your son brought to you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:13; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:3; NUMBERS 16:9–10"> But Leah said to her, “No! ◄It was bad that you stole my husband!/Is it not bad enough that you stole my husband?► &#91;RHQ&#93; Now ◄are you going to take my son’s mandrake plants?/You want to take my son’s mandrake plants also, so that you can become pregnant!►” &#91;RHQ&#93; So Rachel said, “All right, Jacob can sleep with you tonight, if you give me some of your son’s mandrake plants.” So Leah ◄agreed/gave her some►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When Jacob returned from the wheat fields that evening, Leah went out to meet him. She said, “You must sleep with me tonight, because I gave Rachel some of my son’s mandrakes that enable women to become pregnant, to pay her for allowing us to do that.” So Jacob slept with her that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 30:6; GENESIS 30:22; EXODUS 3:7"> God answered Leah’s prayers, and she became pregnant and bore a fifth son to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 49:14–15; DEUTERONOMY 33:18; GENESIS 46:13"> Leah said, “God has rewarded me for giving my slave to my husband to be another wife for him.” So she named him Issachar, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘reward’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Leah became pregnant again and bore a sixth son for Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 35:23; MATTHEW 4:13; GENESIS 49:13"> Leah said, “God has given me a precious gift. ◄This time/Now► my husband will honor/respect me, because I have given birth to six sons for him.” So she named him Zebulon, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘accepted gift’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 34:1–3; GENESIS 34:26; GENESIS 46:15"> Later she gave birth to a daughter, and named her Dinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 29:31; PSALMS 113:9; PSALMS 127:3"> Then God thought about what Rachel wanted. He heard her prayers and enabled her to become pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="LUKE 1:25; GENESIS 29:31; LUKE 1:27"> She became pregnant and gave birth to a son. She said, “God has caused that no longer will I be ashamed for not having children.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 49:22–26; GENESIS 35:24; GENESIS 37:2"> She named him Joseph, which sounds like the Hebrew words that mean ‘may he give another’ and she said, ‘ want Yahweh to give me another son.’</span>

## Jacob tricked Laban and became very wealthy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 24:54; GENESIS 24:56; GENESIS 28:15"> After Rachel had given birth to Joseph, Jacob said to Laban, “Now allow me to quit working for you and let me return to my own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 29:30; HOSEA 12:12; GENESIS 31:26"> You know the work that I have done for you for a long time to get my wives. So let me take my wives and my children, and leave.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="RUTH 2:13; ACTS 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> But Laban said to him, “If you are pleased with me, stay here, because I have found out by performing a magic ritual that Yahweh has blessed me because of what you have done for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 29:15; GENESIS 29:19"> Tell me what you want me to pay you for continuing to work for me, and that is what I will pay you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 31:6; GENESIS 31:38–40; 1 PETER 2:18"> Jacob replied, “You know how I have worked for you, and you know that your livestock have increased greatly as I have taken care of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:8; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:14; DEUTERONOMY 11:10"> You had only a few animals before I came here. But now you have very many, and Yahweh has caused them to increase greatly in number wherever I have taken them. But now I need to start taking care of the needs of my own family.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 21:4–6; HEBREWS 13:5; PSALMS 118:8"> Laban replied, “What do you want me to give you?” Jacob replied, “I do not want you to pay me anything. But if you will do this one thing for me, I will continue to take care of your flocks and protect them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 31:8; GENESIS 31:10; GENESIS 30:35"> Allow me to go and look at all of your flocks today and remove from them all the speckled sheep, all the spotted sheep, and every dark-colored lamb, all the goats that are speckled, and all the goats that are spotted, and keep them for myself. They will be my wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 13:14; 2 SAMUEL 22:21; 1 SAMUEL 26:23"> In that way, in the future, you will be able to know whether I have been honest regarding what you have paid me. If any of my goats are neither speckled or spotted, or if any of my lambs are not dark-colored, you will know that I have stolen them from you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 7:7; NUMBERS 22:29; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:5"> Laban agreed and said, “Okay, we will do as you have said.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GENESIS 31:9"> But that same day Laban removed all the male goats that had black and white stripes on them or were spotted, and all the female goats that were speckled or spotted, all the goats that were partly white, and all the dark-colored lambs. He separated them and told his sons to take care of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Then he took these flocks and walked a distance of three days, in order that he could be that far from Jacob. Jacob continued to take care of the rest of Laban’s flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="GENESIS 31:9–13; EZEKIEL 31:8"> Then Jacob cut some branches of ◄poplar, almond, and plane trees/three kinds of trees that had white wood►. He peeled strips of bark from the branches, so that where the bark had been peeled off, the branches were light in color.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Then he placed the peeled branches in the troughs where they put the water for the animals to drink, so that the branches would be in front of the flocks when they came to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="EXODUS 12:35–36; GENESIS 31:9–12; GENESIS 31:40"> The animals also mated in front of the branches, and eventually they gave birth to animals that were speckled, or to animals that were spotted, or to animals that had black and white stripes on them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Furthermore, Jacob separated the female sheep in his flock from the other sheep and goats in Laban’s flock. And when his female sheep mated, he made them look toward the animals that belonged to Laban that had black and white stripes on them, and the dark-colored animals. He did that so that the female sheep would give birth to animals that were striped or dark-colored. By doing that, he made bigger flocks for himself, and he kept them separate from Laban’s flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> In addition, whenever the stronger female sheep were ready to mate, Jacob put some of those peeled branches in the troughs in front of them, so that they would mate in front of the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> But when weak animals were ready to mate, he did not put the branches in their troughs. So the weak ones became part of Laban’s flock, and the strong ones became part of Jacob’s flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GENESIS 24:35; GENESIS 30:30; GENESIS 26:13–14"> As a result, Jacob became very rich. He owned many large flocks of sheep and goats, and many male and female slaves, and many camels and donkeys.</span>
</div>
