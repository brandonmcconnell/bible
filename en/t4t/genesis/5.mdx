## The descendants of Adam

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:24; COLOSSIANS 3:10; GENESIS 1:26–27"> Here is a list of the descendants of Adam. When God created humans, he caused them to be like him in many ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:4; MARK 10:6; GENESIS 2:23"> He created one man and one woman. He blessed them, and on the day that he created them, he called them ‘human beings’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 4:25; JOB 25:4; PSALMS 51:5"> When Adam was 130 years old, he became the father of a son who grew up to be just like him. That was the son he named Seth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:1–3; LUKE 3:36–38; GENESIS 5:26"> After Seth was born, Adam lived 800 more years, and during those years he became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 5:8; GENESIS 5:11; GENESIS 3:19"> Adam lived 930 years altogether, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 4:26"> When Seth was 105 years old, he became the father of Enosh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> After Enosh was born, Seth lived 807 more years, and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Seth lived 912 years altogether, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 3:37; 1 CHRONICLES 1:2"> When Enosh was 90 years old, he became the father of Kenan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> After Kenan was born, Enosh lived 815 more years and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Enosh lived 905 years altogether, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 3:37"> When Kenan was 70 years old, he became the father of Mahalalel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After Mahalalel was born, Kenan lived 840 more years and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Kenan lived 910 years altogether, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:2"> When Mahalalel was 65 years old, he became the father of Jared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> After Jared was born, Mahalalel lived 830 more years and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Mahalalel lived 895 years altogether, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 4:17; 1 CHRONICLES 1:3; JUDE 1:14–15"> When Jared was 162 years old, he became the father of Enoch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Jared lived 800 years after Enoch was born, and he became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Jared lived 962 years altogether, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 3:37"> When Enoch was 65 years old, he became the father of Methuselah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:12; GENESIS 17:1; GENESIS 6:9"> Enoch lived in close fellowship with God for 300 years after Methuselah was born, and he became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Enoch lived 365 years altogether in close fellowship with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:5–6; 1 JOHN 1:7; GENESIS 5:22"> Then one day he disappeared, because God took him away to be with him in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 4:18"> When Methuselah was 187 years old, he became the father of Lamech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Methuselah lived 782 years after Lamech was born, and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Methuselah lived 969 years altogether, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> When Lamech was 182 years old, he became the father of a son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 PETER 2:5; HEBREWS 11:7; MATTHEW 24:37"> whom he named Noah, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘relief’, because he said, “He will bring us relief from all the hard work we have been doing to produce food from the ground that Yahweh cursed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Lamech lived 595 years after Noah was born and became the father of other sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Lamech lived 777 years altogether, and then he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 6:10; GENESIS 10:21; GENESIS 9:18–19"> When Noah was 500 years old, he became the father of a son whom he named Shem. Later he became the father of another son whom he named Ham, and later he became the father of another son whom he named Japheth.</span>
</div>
</div>
