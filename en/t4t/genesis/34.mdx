## Hamor’s son Shechem raped Jacob’s daughter Dinah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 30:21; JEREMIAH 2:36; GENESIS 30:13"> One day Dinah, the daughter of Jacob and Leah, went to visit some of the women in that area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:28; JOB 31:1; JOB 31:9"> Shechem, one of the sons of Hamor, the ruler of that area who was descended from the Hiv people-group, saw her. He wanted her. So he grabbed her and forced her to have sex &#91;EUP&#93; with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HOSEA 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 CHRONICLES 30:22"> He &#91;SYN&#93; was very much attracted to her, and fell in love with her, and he tried to get her to love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 14:2; 2 SAMUEL 13:13; GENESIS 21:21"> So Shechem said to his father Hamor, “Please get this girl for me. I want her to become my wife!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 13:22; LEVITICUS 10:3; 1 SAMUEL 10:27"> Jacob very soon found out that his daughter Dinah had been disgraced/defiled. But his sons were in the fields with his livestock, so he did nothing about it until they returned home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> In the meantime, Shechem’s father Hamor went to talk with Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:15; JUDGES 20:6; DEUTERONOMY 22:21"> While they were still talking, Jacob’s sons came in from the field. When they found out what had happened, they were shocked and very angry. They said, “Shechem has done something that is very disgraceful among us Israeli people, something that never should be done!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:2; PSALMS 119:20; PSALMS 84:2"> But Hamor said to them, “My son Shechem really likes this girl. Please allow him to marry her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:3; GENESIS 26:34–35; GENESIS 19:14"> Let’s make an agreement: You will give your daughters to our young men to be their wives, and we will give our daughters to your young men to be their wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 20:15; GENESIS 47:27; GENESIS 42:34"> You can live among us, and live anywhere in our land that you wish. You can buy and sell things (OR, travel around) and if you find land that you want, then you can buy it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 33:15; GENESIS 18:3"> Then Shechem said to Dinah’s father and brothers, “If you feel good toward me and do what I am asking for, I will give you whatever you ask for.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 22:16–17; DEUTERONOMY 22:28–29; 1 SAMUEL 18:25–27"> Tell me what gifts you want and what bride price you want, and I will give you what you ask for. I just want you to give the girl to me to be my wife.”</span>

## Dinah’s brothers got revenge

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; JOB 13:4"> But because Shechem had done a shameful thing to their sister Dinah, the sons of Jacob deceived Shechem and his father Hamor</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:1–39; 2 SAMUEL 15:7; GENESIS 17:11"> by saying to them, “No, we cannot do that. We cannot give our sister to be the wife of a man who is not circumcised, because that would be a shameful thing for us to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:12"> We will do that only if you do one thing: You must become like us by circumcising all the males that are among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then we will give our daughters to your young men to be your wives, and we will take your daughters to be the wives of our young men. We will live among you, and we will become one people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if you will not agree to being circumcised, we will take our sister and go back to our land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> What they said pleased Hamor and his son Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ACTS 17:12; ACTS 13:50; 1 SAMUEL 22:14"> Shechem was very much in love with Jacob’s daughter, so he quickly agreed to do what they suggested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="RUTH 4:1; PROVERBS 31:23; AMOS 5:12"> Shechem went with Hamor to the meeting place near the city gate, and they spoke to the city leaders, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “These men are friendly toward us. We should let them live here and travel around (OR, buy and sell things) and if they find land that they want, they can buy it. There is plenty of land for them to live here. Our young men can marry their daughters, and their young men can marry our daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But these men will agree to live among us and become one people-group with us only if all our males are circumcised, as they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:12–13; JOHN 6:26–27; JOHN 2:16"> But if we do that, just think! Their livestock and their possessions and their other animals will become ours &#91;RHQ&#93;! So we should agree to do what they suggest, and then they will live among us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 23:10; 1 CORINTHIANS 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> Shechem was the most respected person in his father’s household, so all the men who were there at the city gate agreed to what Hamor and Shechem suggested. So every male in the city was circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 49:5–7; GENESIS 29:33–34; NUMBERS 31:7"> On the third day after that, when the men of the city were still sore because of being circumcised, two of Jacob’s sons, Simeon and Levi, who were Dinah’s brothers, took their swords and entered the city without anyone opposing them, and killed all the men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 2:26; DEUTERONOMY 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> They even killed Hamor and his son Shechem. Then they took Dinah out of Shechem’s house and left the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JOSHUA 7:13; JOSHUA 7:21; EXODUS 2:14"> Then the other sons of Jacob went into the city where all those dead bodies were. They ◄looted/took everything in► the city to get revenge for the shameful thing that had been done to their sister.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 1:15–16; DEUTERONOMY 8:17–18; JOB 20:5"> They took away the people’s sheep and goats, their cattle, their donkeys, and everything else that they wanted from inside the city and from out in the countryside.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They took away everything that was valuable, even the children and the women. They seized and took away everything that was in the houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 105:12; JOSHUA 7:25; EXODUS 5:21"> Then Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have caused a lot of trouble for me! Now the Canaan people-group and the Perizzi people-group and everyone else who lives in this land will ◄hate me/say my name stinks►! I do not have many men to fight for us, so if they all gather together and come to me and attack us, they will destroy us and all our household!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PROVERBS 6:34; GENESIS 34:13; GENESIS 49:7"> But they replied, “◄Should we have allowed Shechem to treat our sister like a prostitute?/We could not just let Shechem treat our sister like a prostitute!►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
