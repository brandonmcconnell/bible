## Joseph had two dreams about himself and his brothers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 17:8; GENESIS 23:4; GENESIS 36:7"> Jacob continued to live in the Canaan region where his father had lived previously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 35:25–26; GENESIS 35:22; 1 SAMUEL 2:22–24"> ◄This is/I will now tell you► what happened to Jacob’s family. When his son Joseph was 17 years old, he was taking care of the flocks of sheep and goats with some of his older brothers. They were sons of his father’s ◄concubines/female slaves that he had taken to be his secondary wives►. Joseph sometimes told his father about bad things that his brothers were doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 3:35; GENESIS 37:32; 2 SAMUEL 13:18"> Jacob loved Joseph more than he loved any of his other children, because Joseph had been born when Jacob was an old man. Jacob made for Joseph a long pretty robe that had long sleeves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 27:41; 1 JOHN 3:12; 1 JOHN 4:20"> When Joseph’s older brothers realized that their father loved him more than he loved any of them, they hated him. They never spoke kindly to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 28:12; GENESIS 42:9; JOHN 17:14"> One night Joseph had a dream. He told his brothers about the dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 44:18; JUDGES 9:7"> He said to them, “Listen to the dream I had!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 44:14; GENESIS 42:6; GENESIS 43:26"> In the dream, we were tying up bundles of wheat in the field. Suddenly my bundle stood up straight, and surprisingly, your bundles gathered around my bundle and bowed down to it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 10:27; ACTS 7:35; HEBREWS 10:29"> His brothers said to him, “Do you think that some day you will rule over us? Are you saying that some day you are going to be our king?” &#91;RHQ&#93; They hated him even more than before because of what he had told them about his dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ACTS 7:9–14; GENESIS 37:7; DANIEL 8:10"> Later he had another dream, and again he told his older brothers about it. He said, “Listen to this! I had another dream. In this dream, the sun and moon and eleven stars were bowing down to me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 27:29; PHILIPPIANS 2:10–11; Isaiah 60:14"> He also told his father about it. His father rebuked him, saying “What are you suggesting by that dream &#91;RHQ&#93;? Do you think it means that your mother and I and your older brothers will some day bow down to the ground in front of you?” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ACTS 7:9; LUKE 2:19; LUKE 2:51"> Joseph’s older brothers were furious/angry with him, but his father just kept thinking about what the dream meant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 37:1; GENESIS 33:18; GENESIS 34:25–31"> One day Joseph’s older brothers went to take care of their father’s sheep and goats that were eating grass near Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:1–3; 1 SAMUEL 3:8; 1 SAMUEL 3:16"> Some time later, Jacob said to Joseph, “Your brothers are taking care of the sheep and goats near Shechem &#91;RHQ&#93;. I am going to send you there to see them.” Joseph replied, “Okay.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 35:27; LUKE 19:42; PSALMS 125:5"> Jacob said, “Go and see if they are doing okay, and if the flocks are doing okay. Then come back and give me a report.” So Jacob sent Joseph from the valley where they were living, the valley where Hebron is located, to go north to find his brothers. When Joseph arrived near Shechem city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 4:27; JUDGES 4:22; GENESIS 21:14"> while he was wandering around in the fields looking/searching for his brothers, a man saw him and asked him, “Whom are you looking/searching for?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 19:10; SONG OF SONGS 1:7"> Joseph replied, “I am looking for my older brothers. Can you tell me where they are taking care of their sheep and goats?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 KINGS 6:13"> The man replied, “They are not here any more. I heard one of them saying, ‘Let’s take the sheep and goats and go to Dothan town.’” Joseph was taken to Egypt after his brothers sold him. So Joseph left there and went north, and found his older brothers near Dothan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 23:12; PSALMS 37:12; PSALMS 37:32"> But they saw him when he was still far away, and they decided to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 28:12; GENESIS 37:11; GENESIS 37:5"> They said to each other things like, “Here comes that dreamer!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="TITUS 3:3; PROVERBS 27:4; MATTHEW 2:2–16"> and “Hey, let’s kill him, and then throw his body into one of the pits/cisterns. Then we will tell people that a ferocious/wild animal attacked and killed him and ate him. And then we will ◄find out whether his dreams come true/make sure that his dreams do not come true►!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 42:22; GENESIS 35:22; JOSHUA 10:28"> Reuben heard what they were saying, so he tried to persuade them not to kill &#91;MTY&#93; Joseph. He said, “No, we should not kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 22:12; DEUTERONOMY 13:9; GENESIS 42:22"> Do not even shed his blood! We can throw him into this pit/cistern in the desert, but we should not harm him &#91;MTY&#93;.” He said that, and then left them, planning to rescue Joseph later and take him back to his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 37:3; MATTHEW 27:28; GENESIS 42:21"> So when Joseph arrived where his older brothers were, they seized him and ripped off his pretty robe with long sleeves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:6; PSALMS 130:1–2; ZECHARIAH 9:11"> Then they took him and threw him into the pit/cistern. But the pit/cistern was dry; there was no water in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 37:28; GENESIS 43:11; JEREMIAH 8:22"> After they sat down to eat some food, they looked up and saw a ◄caravan/group of traders►, descendants of Ishmael, coming from the Gilead area. Their camels were loaded with bags of spices and nice-smelling resins. They were going down to Egypt to sell those things there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 37:20; GENESIS 4:10; GENESIS 25:32"> Judah said to his older and younger brothers, “If we kill our younger brother and hide his body, ◄what will we gain?/we will not gain anything!► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:26; GENESIS 42:21; 1 SAMUEL 18:17"> So, instead of harming him, let’s sell him to these men who are descendants of Ishmael. Don’t forget, he is our own younger brother!” So they all agreed to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ACTS 7:9; PSALMS 105:17; GENESIS 45:4–5"> When those traders from the Midian area came near, Joseph’s brothers pulled him up out of the pit/cistern. Then they sold him to the men from Midian for 20 pieces of silver. The traders then took Joseph to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOB 1:20; GENESIS 37:34; GENESIS 44:13"> When Reuben returned to the pit/cistern, he saw that his younger brother was not there. He was so grieved that he tore his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 42:13; JEREMIAH 31:15; GENESIS 42:32"> He went back to his younger brothers and said, “The boy is not in the pit/cistern! What can I do now?” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 37:23; PROVERBS 28:13; GENESIS 37:3"> Joseph’s brothers did not dare to tell their father what they had done. So, they decided to invent a story about what had happened. They got Joseph’s robe. Then they killed a goat and dipped the robe in the goat’s blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 15:30; GENESIS 37:3; GENESIS 44:20–23"> They took that pretty robe back to their father and said, “We found this robe! Look at it. Is it your son’s robe?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 37:20; GENESIS 44:28; JOHN 13:7"> He recognized it, and he said, “Yes, it is my son’s robe! Some ferocious/wild animal must have attacked and killed him! I am sure that the animal has torn Joseph to pieces!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 37:29; 2 SAMUEL 3:31; PSALMS 69:11"> Jacob was so grieved that he tore his clothes. He put on ◄sackcloth/clothes that people wear when they are mourning for someone who has died►. He mourned/cried for his son for many days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GENESIS 42:38; JOB 2:11; 2 SAMUEL 12:17"> All of his children came to try to comfort him, but he did not pay attention to what they said. He said, “No, I will still be mourning/crying when I die and go to be with my son.” So Joseph’s father continued to cry because of what had happened to his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GENESIS 40:4; GENESIS 37:28; 2 KINGS 25:8"> In the meantime, the men/traders from Midian took Joseph to Egypt and sold him to Potiphar, who was one of the king’s officials. He was the captain of the soldiers who protected the king.</span>
