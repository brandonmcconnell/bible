## Abraham sent a servant to Mesopotamia to find a wife for Isaac

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 112:1–3; GALATIANS 3:9; MATTHEW 6:33"> Abraham was now a very old man. Yahweh had blessed Abraham in many ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 47:29; GENESIS 39:4–6; GENESIS 15:2"> One day Abraham said to the chief servant of his household, the man who was in charge of everything Abraham owned, “Put your hand between my thighs to solemnly promise you will do what I tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:14–17; GENESIS 26:34–35; GENESIS 28:8"> Knowing that Yahweh God who created the heavens and the earth is listening, promise that you will not get a wife for my son, Isaac, from the women here in Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 28:2; GENESIS 22:20–23; GENESIS 12:7"> Instead, go back to my country and to my relatives and get a wife for my son Isaac from among them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:2; EXODUS 9:2; EXODUS 20:7"> The servant asked him, “If I find a woman among your relatives, what if she is not willing to come back with me to this land? Shall I take your son back there to the country you came from, so he can find a wife and live there?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:1; 2 PETER 2:20–22; HEBREWS 10:39"> Abraham replied to him, “No! Be certain that you don’t take my son there!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 33:2; GENESIS 13:15; HEBREWS 1:14"> Yahweh God, who created the heavens, brought me here. He brought me from my father’s household, and from the land where my relatives lived. He spoke to me and made a solemn promise to me, saying, ‘I will give this land of Canaan to your descendants.’ He will send an angel who will go there ahead of you, and enable you to get a wife for my son there and bring her to live here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:17–20; NUMBERS 30:5; NUMBERS 30:8"> But if the woman you find will not come back with you, you are free to disregard the promise you are making. The only thing that you must not do is to take my son to live there!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 24:2"> So the servant put his hand between Abraham’s thighs and made a solemn promise about the matter.</span>

## Abraham’s servant found a wife for Isaac

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 11:31; DEUTERONOMY 23:4; GENESIS 27:43"> Then the servant took ten of his master’s camels and loaded them with all kinds of goods that his master gave him to take along. Then he left to go to Aram-Naharaim, which is in ◄northern Mesopotamia/Syria►. He arrived in Nahor city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 9:11; EXODUS 2:16; JOHN 4:7"> It was late in the afternoon, at the time when the women go to the well to get water, when he arrived there. He made the camels kneel down near the well, which was outside the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 24:27; EXODUS 3:6; GENESIS 26:24"> The servant prayed, “Yahweh God whom my master Abraham worships, enable me to be successful today! Act kindly to my master, Abraham!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 24:43; PSALMS 37:5; EXODUS 2:16"> Listen to me. I am standing near a well of water, and the young women of the city are coming to get water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 6:37; JUDGES 6:17; GENESIS 15:8"> I am asking you this: ‘I will say to some girl, “Please lower your jar so that I may drink some water.” If she says, “Drink some, and I will draw some water from the well for your camels, too,” I will know that she is the woman whom you chose to be a wife for your servant, Isaac, and I will know that you have been kind to my master.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 11:29; GENESIS 24:45; Isaiah 65:24"> Before he finished praying, amazingly, Rebekah arrived there, carrying a jar on her shoulder. She was the daughter of Bethuel, who was the son of Milcah, the wife of Abraham’s younger brother Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 26:7; NUMBERS 31:17–18; GENESIS 39:6"> She was very beautiful and still a virgin. She went down to the edge of the well, filled her jar with water, and then came back up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 4:7; Isaiah 21:14; GENESIS 26:1–35"> Abraham’s servant immediately ran to meet her, and said, “Please give me a little water from your jar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PROVERBS 31:26; 1 PETER 3:8; GENESIS 24:14"> She replied, “Drink some, sir!” She lowered her jar from her shoulder to her hands and gave him a drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 24:14; 1 PETER 4:9; GENESIS 24:45–46"> After she had finished giving him a drink, she said, “I will also get some water for your camels, until they have had enough to drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> She quickly emptied the water in her jar into the trough for giving water to animals, and then ran back to the well, and kept getting water for all the camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 107:43; GENESIS 24:12; 2 SAMUEL 7:18–20"> The servant just watched her, without saying anything. He wanted to know if Yahweh had caused his trip to be successful or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:11–12; EXODUS 32:2–3; GENESIS 23:15–16"> Finally, after the camels finished drinking, the servant took out a gold nose ring that weighed ◄two ounces/6 grams►, and two large gold bracelets for her arms, and gave them to Rebekah and told her to put them on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then he said, “Tell me whose daughter you are. Also, tell me, is there room in your father’s house for me and my men to sleep there tonight?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 24:15; GENESIS 22:23; GENESIS 22:20"> She replied, “My father’s name is Bethuel. He is the son of Nahor and his wife Milcah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 32:8; 1 PETER 4:9; JUDGES 19:19–21"> Yes, we have room where you all can sleep tonight, and we also have plenty of straw and grain to feed the camels.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 24:48; EXODUS 4:31; GENESIS 24:52"> The servant bowed and worshiped Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 24:48; GENESIS 24:12; PSALMS 98:3"> He said, “I thank you, Yahweh God, whom my master Abraham worships. You have continued to be kind and faithful to my master. You led me on this journey straight/directly to the house of my master’s relatives!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 24:67; GENESIS 24:55; GENESIS 31:33"> The girl ran and told everyone in her mother’s household about what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 29:5; GENESIS 29:13; GENESIS 24:55"> Rebekah had a brother whose name was Laban. Laban went quickly to Abraham’s servant, who was still by the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> He was surprised to have seen the bracelets on his sister’s arms and the nose ring and to have heard Rebekah tell what the man had said to her. So he went out and saw the man standing near the camels, close to the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="RUTH 3:10; GENESIS 26:29; PSALMS 115:15"> He said to the man, “You who have been blessed by Yahweh, come with me! ◄Why are standing out here?/You should not keep standing out here!► &#91;RHQ&#93; I have prepared a room for you in the house, and a place for the camels to stay.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JUDGES 19:21; GENESIS 43:24; GENESIS 18:4"> So the servant went to the house, and Laban’s servants unloaded the camels, and brought straw and grain for the camels, and water for him and the men with him to wash their feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 132:3–5; 1 TIMOTHY 6:2; EPHESIANS 6:5–8"> They set food in front of him for him to eat, but he said, “I will not eat until I tell you ◄what I need to tell you/why I have come►.” So Laban said, “Tell us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> So the servant said, “I am Abraham’s servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GENESIS 24:1; GENESIS 13:2; PROVERBS 10:22"> Yahweh has greatly blessed my master so that he has become very rich. Yahweh has given him many sheep and cattle, a lot of gold and silver, male and female servants, camels and donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="GENESIS 25:5; GENESIS 21:10; GENESIS 21:1–7"> My master’s wife, Sarah, bore a son for him when she was very old, and my master has given to his son everything he owns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="GENESIS 27:46; Ezra 9:1–3; GENESIS 24:2–9"> My master made me solemnly promise, saying, ‘Do not get a wife for my son from the women here in Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="GENESIS 24:4; GENESIS 31:19; GENESIS 12:1"> Instead, go back to my father’s family, to my own clan, and get from them a wife for my son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GENESIS 24:5"> Then I asked my master, ‘What shall I do if the woman you show me will not come back with me?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="EXODUS 23:20; PSALMS 16:8; GENESIS 5:24"> He replied, ‘Yahweh, whom I have always obeyed &#91;MTY&#93;, will send his angel with you, and he will cause your journey to be successful, with the result that you can get a wife for my son from my clan, from my father’s family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="GENESIS 24:8; DEUTERONOMY 29:12"> But if my clan refuses to allow her to return with you, you will be freed from being cursed because of not obeying me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; GENESIS 24:12–14; Ezra 8:21"> “When I came today to the well, I prayed, ‘Yahweh God whom my master, Abraham worships, if you are willing, please allow this journey of mine to be successful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GENESIS 24:13–14"> Listen to me. I am standing beside a well. A young woman may come to draw water. I will say to her, “Please, give me a little water to drink from your jar.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GENESIS 24:14; 1 PETER 3:8; GENESIS 2:22"> If she says to me, “Certainly, drink some! And I will also draw some water for your camels,” that will be the woman whom you have chosen for my master’s son!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:7; ACTS 12:12–17; GENESIS 24:15–20"> Before I finished praying, amazingly, Rebekah approached with her water jar on her shoulder. She went down to the well and got some water. I said to her, ‘Please give me a drink!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="GENESIS 24:18–19"> She quickly lowered her jar and said, ‘Drink some! And I will draw water from the well for your camels, too.’ So I drank some water, and she also got water for the camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="PSALMS 45:13–14; GENESIS 24:22–24; Isaiah 62:3–5"> Then I asked her, ‘Whose daughter are you?’ She said, ‘The daughter of Bethuel, the son of Nahor and his wife, Milcah.’ I had a ring and bracelets with me, and I put the ring in her nose and put the bracelets on her arms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PROVERBS 3:5–6; GENESIS 24:26–27; PSALMS 48:14"> Then I bowed and worshiped Yahweh, and I thanked Yahweh God, the one my master Abraham worships, the one who led me on the right road to get the granddaughter of my master’s brother to be a wife for my master’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="GENESIS 47:29; JOSHUA 2:14; PROVERBS 3:3"> Now, if you are kind and faithful to my master, tell me that you will do what I am asking. If you will not do that, tell me that also, so I may know what to do &#91;MTY&#93;.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="PSALMS 118:23; GENESIS 31:24; GENESIS 31:29"> Laban and Bethuel answered, “It is clear that Yahweh has caused this to happen. So we two cannot say anything more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="GENESIS 20:15; GENESIS 24:15; 2 SAMUEL 16:10"> Rebekah is here in front of you. Take her and go, and let her be a wife for your master’s son, just as Yahweh has indicated.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="GENESIS 24:26; GENESIS 24:48; PSALMS 116:1–2"> When Abraham’s servant heard these words, he bowed down to the ground to thank Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="GENESIS 24:22; GENESIS 24:10; EXODUS 12:35"> Then the servant brought out silver and gold jewelry and clothes, and gave them to Rebekah. And he gave gifts to her brother Laban and to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="GENESIS 24:56; GENESIS 24:59; GENESIS 28:5–6"> Then they ate a meal together. The men who were with Abraham’s servant also slept there that night. The next morning, the servant said, “Allow me now to return to my master.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="GENESIS 4:3; LEVITICUS 25:29; JUDGES 14:8"> But her brother and her mother replied, “Let the girl remain with us for about ten days. After that, you may take her and go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="PROVERBS 25:25; JOSHUA 1:8; Isaiah 48:15"> But he replied to them, “Yahweh has made my journey successful, so do not detain me. Let me take her back to my master now!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> They said, “Let’s call Rebekah and ask her what she wants to do.” &#91;MTY&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="LUKE 1:38; PSALMS 45:10–11"> So they summoned Rebekah and asked her, “Will you go with this man now?” She replied, “Yes, I will go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="GENESIS 35:8; NUMBERS 11:12; GENESIS 24:50"> So they allowed Rebekah, a woman who had taken care of her when she was very young, Abraham’s servant, and the men who had come with him. to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="GENESIS 22:17; GENESIS 17:16; RUTH 4:11–12"> Before they all left, they asked God to bless Rebekah, and said to her, “Our sister, we ask that Yahweh will cause you to have millions of descendants, and allow them to completely defeat &#91;MET&#93; all those that hate them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="PSALMS 45:10; GENESIS 2:24; Esther 8:14"> Then Rebekah and her maid servants got ready and got on their camels and went with Abraham’s servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="GENESIS 25:11; GENESIS 16:14; GENESIS 12:9"> At that time, Isaac was living in the Negev desert. He had gone there from Beer-Lahai-Roi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="PSALMS 119:15; PSALMS 1:2; PSALMS 104:34"> One evening he went out into the field to meditate as he walked. He looked up and was surprised to see some camels coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="JUDGES 1:14; JOSHUA 15:18"> Rebekah also looked up and saw Isaac. She got off the camel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 2:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:5–6; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:10"> and asked the servant, “Who is that man who is approaching?” The servant replied, “That is Isaac, my master, the man you will be marrying.” So she took her veil and covered her face, which was the custom for a woman who was about to be married.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="MARK 6:30"> The servant told Isaac everything that had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 8:2; EPHESIANS 5:22–33; GENESIS 23:1–2"> Then Isaac took Rebekah into the tent that belonged to his mother, Sarah, and she became his wife. And he loved her. So Isaac was comforted about his mother’s death.</span>
