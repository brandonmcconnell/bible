## Yahweh changed Abram’s name to Abraham and made an agreement with him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:13; EPHESIANS 3:20; LUKE 1:6"> When Abram was 99 years old, Yahweh appeared to him again and said to him, “I am God Almighty. I want you to live in a way that I will regard you as someone that cannot be criticized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 17:4–6; GENESIS 12:2; PSALMS 105:8–11"> If you do that, I will make an agreement with you in which I will promise to enable you to have very many descendants.” &#91;MTY&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDGES 13:20; GENESIS 17:17; NUMBERS 16:45"> Abram prostrated himself with his face on the ground. Then God said to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 35:11; GALATIANS 3:28–29; GENESIS 12:2"> “Listen to this agreement that I am making with you: People of many nations will be your descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:21–23; Nehemiah 9:7; GENESIS 17:15"> And no longer will your name be Abram. Instead, your name will be Abraham, which means ‘father of many nations,’ because the people of many nations will descend from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 17:4; MATTHEW 1:6–17; GENESIS 35:11"> I will enable you to have very many descendants, and I will cause them to become great nations/people-groups, and some of your descendants will become kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 9:7–9; ACTS 2:39; GENESIS 26:24"> I will make this agreement between me and you and your descendants. It will be an everlasting agreement to be in effect for many generations. This agreement is that I will be your God, and the God of your descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:12; PSALMS 105:11; EXODUS 6:7"> I will give to you and to your descendants the land through which you are now traveling, all of the Canaan region. It will be an everlasting possession for your descendants, and I will be the God that they will worship.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## God told Abraham that all the males must be circumcised as part of that agreement

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:4–5; PSALMS 25:10; PSALMS 103:18"> Then God said to Abraham, “Now you must obey your part of the agreement that I am making with you, and your descendants must also obey it, for all generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:11–12; ROMANS 4:9–11; ACTS 7:8"> This is the agreement that I am making, between myself and you and all your descendants: Every male among you must be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 4:11; ACTS 7:8; DEUTERONOMY 10:16"> ◄Cutting the foreskins of/Circumcising► them will be the sign that you have accepted the agreement I am making with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 12:3; LUKE 2:21; EXODUS 12:48–49"> Every male child among you must be circumcised when he is eight days old, in all future generations. That includes baby boys in your household or those born from slaves that have been bought, and baby boys of foreigners who live among you but do not belong to your household.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 12:44; GENESIS 37:36; Nehemiah 5:8"> It does not matter whether their parents are members of your household or slaves that have been bought; they must all be circumcised. Your bodies will have this mark to show you have accepted this everlasting agreement I am making.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 4:24–26; NUMBERS 15:30–31; EXODUS 12:19"> You must expel from your community any male who has not been circumcised, because by not being circumcised that person has disobeyed my agreement.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## God changed Sarai’s name to Sarah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 12:25; GENESIS 32:28; GENESIS 17:5"> God also said to Abraham, “As for Sarai, your wife, you must not call her Sarai any longer. Since I changed your name, I will change her name also. Her name will now be Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 35:11; GENESIS 17:6; Isaiah 49:23"> I will bless her, and she will surely give birth to a son for you. And I will bless her so much that she will be the ancestor of people of many nations. Kings of some people-groups will be descended from her.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## God promised Abraham and Sarah that they would have a son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="REVELATION 11:16; GENESIS 21:6; GENESIS 18:12"> Abraham prostrated himself with his face on the ground to show respect to God. But then he laughed as he said to himself, “Can a man who is a 100 years old become a father of a son? And since Sarah is 90 years old, how can she bear a child?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 2:39; PSALMS 41:12; PSALMS 4:6"> Then Abraham said to God, “Perhaps you will let Ishmael receive your blessing and inherit all I possess.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 1:13–20; GENESIS 21:2–3; 2 KINGS 4:16–17"> Then God replied, “No! Your wife Sarah will bear a son for you. You must name him Isaac. I will establish my agreement with him, one which will be an everlasting agreement for him and his descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 21:18; GENESIS 21:13; GENESIS 25:12–18"> As for Ishmael, I have heard what you asked me to do for him. I will bless him so that he also will have many descendants. Among his descendants will be rulers of twelve nations. And I will cause his descendants &#91;MTY&#93; to also become a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 21:2–3; GENESIS 26:2–5; GALATIANS 3:29"> But Isaac, the son whom Sarah will give birth to at this time next year, is the one with whom I will establish my agreement.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 18:33; GENESIS 35:9–15; JUDGES 6:21"> When God finished talking to Abraham, God disappeared from his sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; GENESIS 18:19; GENESIS 17:10–14"> On that same day, Abraham took his son Ishmael and all the males that were in his household, including the sons of all the slaves he had bought, and circumcised them. He cut off their foreskins, just as God told him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ROMANS 4:11; GENESIS 17:17; ROMANS 4:19–20"> Abraham was 99 years old when ◄he was circumcised/someone circumcised him►,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and Ishmael was thirteen years old when Abraham circumcised him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; GENESIS 22:3–4; GENESIS 12:4"> On that very day Abraham and his son, Ishmael were circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 18:19"> And all the males in his household, the ones who had been born there and those who had been bought from foreigners, were also circumcised.</span>
