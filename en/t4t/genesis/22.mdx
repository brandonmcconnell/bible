## Yahweh told Abraham to offer Isaac as a sacrifice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:17; JAMES 2:21; DEUTERONOMY 8:2"> Several years later, God tested Abraham to find out whether Abraham really trusted in him and would obey him. He called out to Abraham, and Abraham replied, “I’m here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 3:16; ROMANS 8:32; HEBREWS 11:17"> God said, “Your son, Isaac, whom you love very much, is the only son who is still here with you. But take him and go together to the Moriah region. Offer him as a sacrifice like a burnt offering, on a hill that I will show you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 119:60; MATTHEW 10:37; LUKE 14:26"> So Abraham got up early the next morning, put a saddle on his donkey, and took with him two of his servants along with his son, Isaac. He also chopped some wood to kindle a fire for a burnt offering. Then they started traveling to the place God told him about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 13:32; MATTHEW 17:23; HOSEA 6:2"> On the third day of their travels, Abraham looked up and saw in the distance the place where God wanted him to go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:19; HEBREWS 12:1"> Abraham said to his servants, “You two stay here with the donkey while the boy and I go over there. We will worship God there, and then we will come back to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 19:17; 1 PETER 2:24; Isaiah 53:6"> Then Abraham took the wood to kindle a fire for the burnt offering and placed it on his son Isaac, for him to carry. Abraham carried in his hand a pan containing burning coals to start a fire, and a knife, and the two of them walked along together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 8:15; MATTHEW 26:39; GENESIS 8:20"> Then Isaac spoke to his father Abraham, saying, “My father!” Abraham replied, “Yes, my son, I’m here!” Isaac said, “Look, we have wood and coals to light a fire, but where is the lamb for the burnt offering?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 1:36; JOHN 1:29; REVELATION 5:12"> Abraham replied, “My son, God himself will provide the lamb for the burnt offering.” So the two of them continued walking along together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JAMES 2:21; HEBREWS 11:17–19; PHILIPPIANS 2:7–8"> They arrived at the place God had told him about. There, Abraham built a stone altar and arranged the wood on top of it. Then he tied his son Isaac, and laid him on the altar on top of the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JAMES 2:21–23; HEBREWS 11:17–19; Isaiah 53:6–12"> Then Abraham took the knife and reached out to kill his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 21:17; EXODUS 3:4; GENESIS 16:9–10"> But an angel who was really Yahweh called to him from heaven and said, “Abraham! Abraham!” Abraham replied, “I’m here!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JAMES 2:21–22; JAMES 2:18; 1 SAMUEL 15:22"> The angel said, “Do not harm the boy or do anything to injure him, because now I know that you respect and obey God. I know that because you have not refused to sacrifice your son, the only son who is still with you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:13; PSALMS 40:6–8; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:9–10"> Then Abraham looked up and saw that a ram was nearby, whose horns had been caught in a ◄thicket/clump of bushes►. So Abraham went over and grabbed the ram and killed it, and sacrificed it on the altar as a burnt offering, instead of his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 22:13; EXODUS 17:15; GENESIS 22:8"> Abraham named that place ‘Yahweh will provide’. And to the present day, people say, “On Yahweh’s mountain, he will provide.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> The angel who was really Yahweh called out to Abraham from heaven a second time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:13–14; LUKE 1:73; PSALMS 105:9"> He said, “I, Yahweh your Lord, am telling you that you did what I told you, and you have not refused to sacrifice your only son that is with you. So I say, ‘I solemnly swear, with myself as my witness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 15:5; GENESIS 24:60; JEREMIAH 33:22"> that some day your descendants will be as numerous as the stars in the sky and the grains of sand on the seashore. And your descendants will defeat their enemies and capture their cities &#91;SYN&#93;.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 3:25; GENESIS 12:3; GALATIANS 3:16"> You obeyed me, so by means of your descendants the people of all the nations on the earth will be blessed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 21:31; JUDGES 20:1; JOSHUA 15:28"> Then Abraham and Isaac returned to where his servants were waiting, and they went back home together to Beersheba, and Abraham stayed there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 11:29; GENESIS 24:24; PROVERBS 25:25"> After these things happened, someone told Abraham, “Your brother Nahor’s wife, Milcah, has also given birth to children. She has eight sons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOB 1:1; JOB 32:2; PSALMS 60:1–12"> The oldest son was Uz. The next was Buz. After him was Kemuel, the father of Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> After Kemuel was Kesad, then Hazo, then Pildash, then Jidlaph, then Bethuel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 24:15; GENESIS 28:5; GENESIS 24:51"> who was the father of Rebekah, who later became Isaac’s wife. Those were the eight sons of Milcah, wife of Abraham’s brother, Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 16:3; GENESIS 25:6; PROVERBS 15:25"> Nahor also had a ◄concubine/female slave whom he took as a wife►, whose name was Beumah. She gave birth to four sons: Tebah, Gaham, Tahash, and Maacah.</span>
</div>
