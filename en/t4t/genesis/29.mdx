## Jacob met Laban’s daughter Rachel at Haran

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 6:3; JUDGES 6:33; HOSEA 12:12"> Jacob continued on the road &#91;MTY&#93;, and he arrived at the land that was east of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 4:14; Isaiah 49:10; JOHN 4:6"> There he was surprised to see a well in a field, and three flocks of sheep were lying near the well. It was the well from which shepherds habitually got water for their sheep. There was a large stone covering the top of the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> When all the flocks were gathered there, the shepherds would work together to roll the stone away from the top of the well and get water for the sheep. When they finished doing that, they would put the stone back in its place over the top of the well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 28:10; GENESIS 27:43; ACTS 7:2"> On that day, Jacob asked the shepherds who were sitting there, “Where are you from?” They replied, “We are from Haran.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 24:24; GENESIS 24:29; GENESIS 31:53"> He asked them, “Do you know Laban, the grandson of Nahor?” They replied, “Yes, we know him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 20:9; GENESIS 37:14; EXODUS 18:7"> Jacob asked them, “Is Laban well?” They replied, “Yes, he is well. Look! Here comes his daughter Rachel with the sheep!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:16; GALATIANS 6:9–10"> Jacob said, “Hey! The sun is still high in the sky. It is not time for the flocks to be gathered for nighttime. Give the sheep some water and then take them back to ◄graze/eat grass► in the pastures!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MARK 16:3; LUKE 24:2; GENESIS 34:14"> They replied, “No, we cannot do that until all the flocks are gathered here and the stone is removed from the top of the well. After that, we will give water to the sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 2:21; SONG OF SONGS 1:7–8; EXODUS 2:15–16"> While he was still talking with them, Rachel came with her father’s sheep. She was the one who took care of her father’s sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 2:17"> When Jacob saw Rachel, the daughter of Laban, who was his mother’s brother, Jacob was so excited that he went over and by himself rolled away the stone that covered the top of the well, and he got water for his uncle’s sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 33:4; GENESIS 45:14–15; GENESIS 45:2"> Then Jacob kissed Rachel on the cheek, and he cried loudly because he was so happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 24:28; GENESIS 13:8; GENESIS 14:14–16"> Jacob told Rachel that he was one of her father’s relatives, the son of her aunt Rebekah. So she ran and told that to her father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 24:29; ACTS 20:37; ROMANS 16:16"> As soon as Laban heard that Jacob, his sister’s son, was there, he ran to meet him. He embraced him and kissed him on the cheek. Then he brought him to his home, and Jacob told him all that had happened to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 19:12–13; GENESIS 2:23; JUDGES 9:2"> Then Laban said to him, “Truly, you are part of my family!” Jacob married Leah and Rachel After Jacob had stayed there and worked for Laban for a month,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 30:28; GENESIS 31:7"> Laban said to him “◄You should not work for me for nothing just because you are a relative of mine!/Why should you work for me for nothing just because you are a relative of mine?► &#91;RHQ&#93; Tell me how much you want me to pay you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="RUTH 4:11; GENESIS 49:31; GENESIS 31:4"> Well, Laban had two daughters. The older one was named Leah, and the younger one was named Rachel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 12:11; GENESIS 46:19–22; GENESIS 24:16"> Leah had pretty eyes, but Rachel had a very attractive figure and was beautiful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HOSEA 12:12; EXODUS 22:16–17; GENESIS 34:12"> Jacob ◄was in love with/wanted very much to be married to► Rachel, and he said, “I will work for you for seven years. That will be my payment for your letting me marry your younger daughter, Rachel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; Isaiah 6:5; PSALMS 12:2"> Laban replied, “It is better for me to let you marry her than for her to marry some other man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 13:7; EPHESIANS 5:2; HOSEA 12:12"> So Jacob worked for Laban for seven years to get Rachel, but to him it seemed like it was only a few days, because he loved her so much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 15:1; GENESIS 29:20; GENESIS 4:1"> After the seven years were ended, Jacob said to Laban, “Let me marry Rachel now, because the time we agreed upon for me to work for you is ended, and I want to marry her. &#91;EUP&#93;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="REVELATION 19:9; RUTH 4:10–13; JUDGES 14:10–18"> So Laban gathered together all the people who lived in that area and made a feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MICAH 7:5; GENESIS 38:14–15; GENESIS 24:65"> But that evening, instead of taking Rachel to Jacob, Laban took his older daughter, Leah, to him. But because it was already dark, he could not see that it was Leah and not Rachel, and he had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 16:1; GENESIS 30:9–12; GENESIS 46:18"> (Laban had already given his slave girl Zilpah to his daughter Leah to be her maid/servant.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:2; PROVERBS 11:31; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:13"> The next morning, Jacob was shocked to see that it was Leah who was with him! So he went to Laban and told him very angrily, “◄What you have done to me is disgusting!/What is this that you have done to me?► &#91;RHQ&#93; I worked for you to get Rachel, did I not? So why did you deceive me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Laban replied, “In this land, it is not our custom to give a younger daughter to be married before we let someone marry our firstborn daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LEVITICUS 18:18; JUDGES 14:12; GENESIS 2:2–3"> After we finish this week of celebration, we will let you marry the younger one also. But in return, you must pay for Rachel by working for me for another seven years.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> So that is what Jacob did. After the week of celebration was ended, Laban gave him his daughter, Rachel, to be his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 35:22; GENESIS 29:24; GENESIS 30:3–8"> Laban gave his slave girl, Bilhah, to Rachel to be her maid/servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 31:41; GENESIS 29:20; GENESIS 31:15"> Jacob had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with Rachel also, and he loved Rachel more than he loved Leah. And Jacob worked for Laban for another seven years.</span>
</div>

## Leah gave birth to four sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:21; MATTHEW 10:37; DEUTERONOMY 21:15"> When Yahweh saw that Jacob did not love Leah very much, he enabled her to become pregnant. But Rachel was not able to become pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 26:7; EXODUS 4:31; EXODUS 3:7"> Leah gave birth to a son, whom she named Reuben, which sounds like the Hebrew words that mean ‘Look, a son!’ She said, “Yahweh has seen that I was miserable, and because of that he has given me a son. Now, surely my husband will love me for giving birth to a son for him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 34:25; GENESIS 49:5–6; GENESIS 34:30"> Later she became pregnant again and gave birth to another son. She said, “Because Yahweh has heard that my husband doesn’t love me, he has given me this son, too.” So she named him Simeon, which means ‘someone who hears’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 32:26–29; GENESIS 49:5–7; GENESIS 46:11"> Later she became pregnant again, and gave birth to another son. She said, “Now, finally, my husband will hold me close to him.” So she named him Levi, which means ‘hold close’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:2; 1 CHRONICLES 5:2; GENESIS 49:8–12"> Later she became pregnant again and gave birth to another son. She said, “◄This time/Now► I will praise Yahweh.” So she called his name Judah, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘praise’. After that, she did not give birth to any more children for several years.</span>
</div>
