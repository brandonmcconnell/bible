## Jacob blessed Ephraim and Manasseh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 41:50–52; GENESIS 46:20; PSALMS 128:6"> Some time later, someone told Joseph, “Hey, your father is ill.” When Joseph heard that, he took his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, to see his father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:10; 1 SAMUEL 23:16; DEUTERONOMY 3:28"> When someone told Jacob, “Look, your son Joseph has come to see you!” Jacob sat up on the bed, even though it was difficult for him to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 35:9–12; EXODUS 6:3; GENESIS 28:3"> He said to Joseph, “When I was at Luz in Canaan, God Almighty appeared to me. He blessed me</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 17:8; GENESIS 35:11; GENESIS 26:4"> and said to me, ‘I am going to enable you to become the father of many children. You will have many descendants, and they will become the ancestors of many people-groups. And I will give this land to your descendants to possess forever.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 41:50–52; JOSHUA 14:4; GENESIS 46:20"> “And now I will consider that your two sons, who were born to you here in Egypt before I came here, will ◄belong to me/be as though they are my sons►. Ephraim and Manasseh will be as though they were my sons, and they will inherit my possessions, just like my sons Reuben and Simeon and the others will.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOSHUA 14:4"> If you later become the father of any more children, they will not be considered to be my children, but as my grandchildren, and in Canaan they will receive as part of what they inherit some of the same land that is in the territory that their brothers Ephraim and Manasseh will inherit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 35:16–19; MATTHEW 2:18; GENESIS 35:9"> Many years ago, as I was returning from Paddan-Aram/Mesopotamia, your mother Rachel died in the Canaan region, while we were still traveling, not far from Ephrath town. So I buried her body there alongside the road to Ephrath &#91;which is now called Bethlehem&#93;.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> When Jacob saw Joseph’s sons, he asked, “Who are these boys?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 27:4; GENESIS 33:5; HEBREWS 11:21"> Joseph replied, “They are the sons that God has given to me here in Egypt.” Jacob said, “Bring them close to me so that I can bless them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 27:1; GENESIS 27:27; 1 SAMUEL 3:2"> Jacob was almost blind because he was very old. He could not recognize the boys. So Joseph brought his sons close to his father, and Jacob kissed them and hugged them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EPHESIANS 3:20; GENESIS 45:26; GENESIS 42:36"> Jacob said to Joseph, “I did not expect to see you again, but look at this! God has allowed me to see not only you, but he has allowed me to see your children, too!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob blessed Joseph’s two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim, in a way contrary to what Joseph expected

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 42:6; LEVITICUS 19:32; GENESIS 19:1"> Joseph took the boys from alongside Jacob’s knees. Then he bowed down with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then Joseph took both of the boys, putting Ephraim on his right side toward Jacob’s left hand, and putting Manasseh on his left side toward Jacob’s right hand, and brought them close to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 41:51; PSALMS 118:16; MATTHEW 19:15"> But Jacob did not do what Joseph wanted him to do. Instead, he reached out his right hand and put it on Ephraim’s head, even though he was the younger son. He crossed his arms and put his left hand on Manasseh’s head, even though Manasseh was the older son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 17:1; COLOSSIANS 2:6; Isaiah 33:16"> Then he ◄blessed/asked God to bless► Joseph and his sons, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“My grandfather Abraham and my father Isaac conducted their lives as God desired,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to this very day God has led me and taken care of me as a shepherd leads and cares for his sheep &#91;MET&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 34:22; GENESIS 49:22; GENESIS 48:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The angel whom he sent has kept me from being harmed in any way.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I pray that God will bless these boys.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I pray that people will never forget about me and about Abraham and Isaac because of what God does for these boys.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I pray that they will have many descendants who will live all over the earth.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 48:14; ROMANS 9:7–8; 1 CHRONICLES 21:7"> When Joseph saw that his father had placed his right hand on Ephraim’s head and not on Manasseh’s head, he was distressed/displeased. So he took his father’s hand to move it from Ephraim’s head to Manasseh’s head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 19:18; GENESIS 29:26; GENESIS 43:33"> Joseph said to him, “My father, that is not right! The one on whom you put your left hand is my older son. Put your right hand on his head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:17; NUMBERS 1:33–35; GENESIS 25:28"> But his father refused, saying, “I know that, my son, I know what I am doing. Manasseh’s descendants will also become a people-group, and they will become important. But his younger brother’s descendants will become greater than his will. His descendants will become several nations.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 24:60; RUTH 4:11–12; GENESIS 28:3"> So he blessed them both on that day, saying, “The people in Israel will use your names when they bless people. They will say, ‘We pray that God will help you as he helped Ephraim and Manasseh.’” In that way, Jacob said that Ephraim would become more important than Manasseh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 50:24; GENESIS 46:4; GENESIS 28:15"> Then Jacob said to Joseph, “I am about to die. But I know that God will help/protect you. And some day he will take your descendants back to the land of their ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 4:5; JOSHUA 24:32; 1 CHRONICLES 5:2"> And it is to you, not to your brothers, that I will give the fertile hill in the Shechem area. I captured that land from the Amor people-group, fighting them with my sword and my bow and arrows.”</span>
</div>
</div>
