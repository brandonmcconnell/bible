## God made the earth and the sky

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOHN 1:1–3; HEBREWS 11:3; Isaiah 45:18"> Long, long ago God created the heavens and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:23; PSALMS 104:30; Isaiah 45:18"> When he began to create the earth, it was shapeless and completely desolate/chaotic. Darkness covered the deep water that surrounded the earth. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 4:6; JOHN 1:5; Isaiah 45:7"> God said, “I want light to shine!” And light shone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 2:13; GENESIS 1:18; ECCLESIASTES 11:7"> God was pleased with the light. Then he made the light to shine in some places, and in other places there was still darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 74:16; Isaiah 45:7; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:5"> He gave to the light the name ‘day’, and he gave to the darkness the name ‘night’. After that, there was an evening which was followed by a morning. He called that whole period of time ‘the first day’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:15; PSALMS 33:6; JOB 37:18"> Then God said, “I command that there be an empty space like a huge dome to separate the water that is above it from the water on the earth that is below it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 8:28–29; PSALMS 148:4; MATTHEW 8:27"> And that is what happened. God gave to the space the name ‘sky’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 1:5; GENESIS 1:19; GENESIS 1:10"> Then there was another evening, which was followed by another morning. He called that period of time ‘the second day’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 PETER 3:5; JONAH 1:9; JEREMIAH 5:22"> Then God said, “I want the water that is below the sky to come together, and dry ground to appear and rise above the water.” And that is what happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; PSALMS 104:31; GENESIS 1:4"> God gave to the ground the name ‘earth’, and he gave to the water that came together the name ‘oceans’. God was pleased with the earth and the oceans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 104:14–17; HEBREWS 6:7; PSALMS 147:8"> Then God said, “I want the earth to produce plants, including plants that will produce seeds and trees that will produce fruit. Each kind of tree will bear its own kind of fruit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARK 4:28; Isaiah 55:10–11; MATTHEW 13:24–26"> Then plants grew on the earth; each kind of plant began to produce its own kind of seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then there was another evening which was followed by another morning. God called that period of time ‘the third day’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 104:19–20; DEUTERONOMY 4:19; JEREMIAH 31:35"> Then God said, “I want a sun, a moon, and many stars to shine in the sky. The sun will shine in the daytime and the moon and stars will shine during the nighttime. By the changes in their appearance they will indicate the various seasons (OR, the times for special celebrations), and will enable people to know when days and years begin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I want them also to be like lights in the sky that will shine on the earth.” And that is what happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 136:7–9; DEUTERONOMY 4:19; PSALMS 8:3"> God made two of them that were like big lights. The bigger one, the sun, shines during the day and the smaller one, the moon, shines during the night. He also made the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 8:3; PSALMS 8:1; JOB 38:12"> God set all of them in the sky to shine on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:35; PSALMS 19:6"> to rule the day and the night, and to separate the light of the daytime from the darkness of the nighttime. God was pleased with these things he had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then there was another evening which was followed by another morning. He called that period of time ‘the fourth day’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 2:19; PSALMS 104:24–25; 1 KINGS 4:33"> Then God said, “I want many creatures to live in all the oceans, and I also want birds to fly in the sky above the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 104:24–26; GENESIS 1:25; GENESIS 8:19"> So God created huge sea creatures and every other kind of creature that moves in the water, and caused them to live in all the oceans. He also created many kinds of birds. God was pleased with all those creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 40:15; GENESIS 8:17; PSALMS 107:38"> God blessed them. He said, “Produce offspring and become very numerous. I want the creatures in the water to live in all the oceans, and birds also to become very numerous.” And that is what happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then there was another evening that was followed by another morning. He called that period of time ‘the fifth day’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 50:9–10; GENESIS 6:20; GENESIS 8:19"> Then God said, “I want various kinds of creatures to appear on the earth. There will be many kinds of ◄livestock/domestic animals►, creatures that scurry across the ground, and large wild animals.” And that is what happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:5; JOB 12:8–10; GENESIS 2:19–20"> God made all kinds of wild animals and ◄livestock/domestic animals► and all kinds of creatures that scurry across the ground. God was pleased with all these creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:10; EPHESIANS 4:24; PSALMS 8:4–8"> Then God said, “Now we will make human beings that will be like us in many ways. I want them to rule over the fish in the sea, over the birds, over all the ◄livestock/domestic animals►, and over all the other creatures that scurry across the ground.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:4; EPHESIANS 2:10; GENESIS 5:1–2"> So God created human beings that were like him in many ways. He made them to be like himself. He created some to be male and some to be female.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 9:1; GENESIS 9:7; LEVITICUS 26:9"> God blessed them, saying, “Produce many children, who will live all over the earth and rule over it. I want them to rule over the fish and the birds and over all creatures that scurry across the ground.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 9:3; PSALMS 104:14–15; PSALMS 145:15–16"> God said to the humans, “Listen! I have given you all the plants that produce seeds, all over the earth, and all the trees that have seeds in their fruit. All these things are for you to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 104:14; PSALMS 145:15–16; PSALMS 147:9"> I have given all the green plants to all the wild animals and to the birds and to all the creatures that scurry across the ground, to everything that breathes, for them to eat.” And that is what happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 4:4; PSALMS 104:24; PSALMS 19:1–2"> God was pleased with everything that he had made. Truly, it was all very good. Then there was another evening, that was followed by another morning. He called that period of time ‘the sixth day’.</span>
</div>
