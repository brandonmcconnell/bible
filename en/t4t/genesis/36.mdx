## The descendants of Esau

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 22:17; 1 CHRONICLES 1:35; DEUTERONOMY 23:7"> ◄Here is/I will now give you► a list of the descendants of Esau, whose other name was Edom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 36:25; GENESIS 28:9; GENESIS 9:25"> Esau married three women from the Canaan region: Adah, the daughter of Elon from the Heth people-group; Oholibamah, who was the daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon from the Hiv people-group;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 28:9; GENESIS 25:13"> and Basemath, who was the daughter of Ishmael and sister of Nebaioth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:35; EXODUS 2:18; JOB 2:11"> Esau’s wife Adah gave birth to Eliphaz. Basemath gave birth to Reuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 35:29"> Oholibamah gave birth to Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. All these sons of Esau were born while he was living in the Canaan region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 32:3; GENESIS 12:5; GENESIS 17:8"> Jacob and Esau had very many possessions. The result was that they needed more land for their livestock. The land where they were living was not big enough for both of them. They had too much livestock. So Esau, whose other name was Edom, had taken his wives and sons and daughters and all the other members of his household, his sheep and goats and his other animals, and all the other things he had obtained in Canaan land, and they had moved to an area that was away from Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 32:3; MALACHI 1:3; EZEKIEL 35:2–7"> They went to live in the Seir hilly area.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 19:37"> ◄Here is/I will now give you► another list of the male descendants of Esau. He was the ancestor of the Edom people-group who live in the Seir area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 36:3–4; 1 CHRONICLES 1:35–54"> Esau’s wife Adah gave birth to Eliphaz, and Esau’s wife Basemath gave birth to Reuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 36:15–16; 1 CHRONICLES 1:35–36"> The sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 17:8–16; DEUTERONOMY 25:17–19; 1 SAMUEL 15:2–9"> Esau’s son Eliphaz also had a ◄concubine/female slave whom he took as a secondary wife►. Her name was Timna. She gave birth to Amalek. Those six men were grandsons of Esau’s wife Adah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:37; GENESIS 36:17"> Reuel’s sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. They were grandsons of Esau’s wife Basemath.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 36:2; GENESIS 36:18; 1 CHRONICLES 1:35"> Esau’s wife Oholibamah, who was the daughter of Anah and granddaughter of Zibeon, gave birth to three sons: Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="OBADIAH 1:9; JOB 2:11; GENESIS 36:11–12"> ◄Here is/I will now give you► a list of the people-groups who were descendants of Esau. His oldest son Eliphaz was the ancestor of the Teman people-group, the Omar people-group, the Zepho people-group, the Kenaz people-group,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 15:15"> the Korah people-group, the Gatam people-group, and the Amalek people-group. They were descendants of Eliphaz and of Esau’s wife Adah. They lived in the Edom region.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 36:13; 1 CHRONICLES 1:37; GENESIS 36:4"> The sons of Esau’s son Reuel were ancestors of the Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah people-groups. They were descended from Esau’s wife Basemath. They also lived in Edom.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 36:14; 1 CHRONICLES 1:35; GENESIS 36:5"> The sons of Esau’s wife Oholibamah, whose mother was Anah, were ancestors of the Jeush, Jalam, and Korah people-groups.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> That is the list of the sons of Esau, and the people-groups who were their descendants.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:12; DEUTERONOMY 2:22; GENESIS 14:6"> ◄This is/I will now give you► a list of the descendants of Seir, who belonged to the Hor people-group, who were the first people-group who lived in the Edom region: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. Those seven men became ancestors of people-groups. Each of the people-groups had the same name as the name of their ancestor.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:39"> The sons of Lotan were Hori and Heman and Lotan’s sister was Timna.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:40"> The sons of Shobal were Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:10; 2 SAMUEL 18:9; 1 KINGS 4:28"> The sons of Zibeon were Aiah and Anah. This Anah was the one who discovered the hot springs in the desert while he was taking care of his father Zibeon’s donkeys.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 36:2; GENESIS 36:18; 1 CHRONICLES 1:41"> Anah had two children—a son named Dishon and a daughter named Oholibamah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:41"> Dishon’s sons were Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Keran.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:42; GENESIS 36:21; 1 CHRONICLES 1:38"> Ezer’s sons were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 4:21; JOB 1:1; JEREMIAH 25:20"> Dishan’s sons were Uz and Aran.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 36:20; GENESIS 36:28; 1 CHRONICLES 1:41–42"> The people-groups who were descendants of Hor lived in the Seir/Edom region. The names of the people-groups are Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 17:6; 1 CHRONICLES 1:43–50; GENESIS 17:16"> These are the names of the kings who ruled in Edom before any kings ruled over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Beor’s son Bela became the first king in Edom. The city where he lived was named Dinhabah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:13; JEREMIAH 49:22; AMOS 1:12"> When Bela died, Zerah’s son Jobab/became the king. He was from Bozrah city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 36:11; JOB 2:11; GENESIS 36:15"> When Jobab died, Husham became the king. He was from the region where the Teman people-group lived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> When Husham died, Bedad’s son Hadan became the king. Husham’s army fought the army of the Midian people-group in the Moab region and defeated them. The city where Husham lived was Avith.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:47"> When Hadad died, Samlah became the king. He was from Masrekah town.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:48; GENESIS 10:11"> When Samlah died, Shaul became the king. He was from Rehoboth town along the Euphrates river.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> When Shaul died, Achbor’s son Baal-Hanan became king.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="EXODUS 15:15; 1 CHRONICLES 1:50"> When Achbor’s son Baal-Hanan died, Hadad became king. The city where he lived was named Pau. His wife’s name was Mehetabel. She was the daughter of Matred, who was the daughter of Me-Zahab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:51–54; EXODUS 15:15; GENESIS 36:31"> ◄Here is/I will now give you► a list of all the people-groups that were descendants of Esau: Timna, Alvah, Jetheth, Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, Magdiel, and Iram. They all lived in the Edom region. The land where each people-group lived got the same name as the name of the people-group.</span>
</div>
</div>
