## Jacob and Esau met peacefully

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 32:6–7; GENESIS 27:41–42; GENESIS 32:16"> Then Jacob joined the rest of his family. Later that day Jacob looked up and saw Esau coming, and there were 400 men with him. Jacob was worried because of that, so he separated the children. He put Leah’s children with Leah, Rachel’s children with Rachel, and the two female slaves’ children with their mothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 29:30; GENESIS 30:22–24; GENESIS 37:3"> He put the two female slaves and their children in front. He put Leah and her children next. He put Rachel and Joseph at the rear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 42:6; LUKE 14:11; GENESIS 18:2"> He himself went ahead of them all, and as he continued to approach his older brother, he prostrated himself with his face on the ground seven times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 45:14–15; PSALMS 34:4; LUKE 15:20"> But Esau ran to Jacob. He hugged him, put his arms around his neck, and kissed him on the cheek. And they both cried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 127:3; Isaiah 8:18; GENESIS 48:9"> Then Esau looked up and saw the women and the children. He asked, “Who are these people who are with you?” Jacob replied, “These are the wives and children that God has graciously/kindly given to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then the female slaves and their children came near and bowed in front of Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Leah and her children came and bowed down. Finally Joseph and Rachel came near and bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 32:5; GENESIS 39:5; GENESIS 32:13–20"> Esau asked, “What is the meaning of all the animals that I saw?” Jacob replied, “I am giving them to you, sir, so that you will feel good toward me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 27:39; PHILEMON 1:7; GENESIS 27:41"> But Esau replied, “My younger brother, I have enough animals! Keep for yourself the animals that you have!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 33:26; REVELATION 22:4; GENESIS 32:30"> But Jacob said, “No, please, if you feel good toward me, accept these gifts from me. You have greeted me very kindly. Seeing your smiling face assures me that you have forgiven me. It is like seeing the face of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:27; PHILIPPIANS 4:18; GENESIS 30:43"> Please accept these gifts that I have brought to you, because God has acted kindly toward me, and I still have plenty of animals!” Jacob kept on urging him to accept the animals, and finally he accepted them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then Esau said, “Let’s continue traveling together, and I will show the road to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; EZEKIEL 34:15–16; EZEKIEL 34:23–25"> Jacob had no intention to go with Esau, but he said, “You know, sir, that the children are weak, and that I must take care of the female sheep and cows that are ◄sucking their mother’s milk/nursing their young►. If I force them to walk fast for a long distance in just one day, the animals will all die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 32:3; Isaiah 40:11; ROMANS 15:1"> You go ahead of me. I will lead the animals slowly, but I will walk as fast as the children and animals can walk. I will catch up with you in Seir, in the land where the descendants of Edom live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="RUTH 2:13; GENESIS 47:25; GENESIS 34:11"> Esau said, “Then allow me to leave with you some of the men who came with me, to protect you.” But Jacob replied, “◄Why do that?/There is no need to do that!► &#91;RHQ&#93; The only thing that I want is for you to act friendly toward me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So on that day Esau left to return to Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 60:6; JOSHUA 13:27; JUDGES 8:5"> But instead of going to Seir, Jacob and his family went to a place called Succoth. There he built a house for himself and his family, and built shelters for his livestock. That is the reason they named the place Succoth, which means ‘shelters’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JUDGES 9:1; ACTS 7:16; JOSHUA 24:1"> Some time later, Jacob and his family left Paddan-Aram in Mesopotamia, and they traveled safely to the Canaan region. There they set up their tents in a field near Shechem city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ACTS 7:16; JOHN 4:5; JOSHUA 24:32"> One of the leaders of the people in that area was named Hamor. Hamor had several sons. Jacob paid the sons of Hamor 100 pieces of silver for the piece of ground on which they set up their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 35:7; GENESIS 21:33; GENESIS 32:28"> He built a stone altar there, and named it El-Elohe Israel, which means ‘God, the God of Israel.’</span>
