## Jacob’s body was taken to Canaan and buried there

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 46:4; DEUTERONOMY 6:7–8; ACTS 8:2"> Joseph leaned close to his father’s face and cried over him and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 50:26; 2 CHRONICLES 16:14; JOHN 19:39–40"> Joseph commanded his servants who were morticians to ◄embalm his father’s body/put spices on his father’s body► to ◄preserve it/keep it from decaying►, and then wrap it with strips of cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 20:29; DEUTERONOMY 34:8; GENESIS 50:10"> So the morticians did that. It took 40 days to embalm Jacob’s body, because that is the amount of time that was always required for them to embalm a body. And the people of Egypt mourned for 70 days because of Jacob’s death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 18:3; Esther 4:2"> When the time of mourning was finished, Joseph said to the king’s officials, “If you are pleased with me, please take this message to the king:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 16:14; Isaiah 22:16; MATTHEW 27:60"> ‘When my father was about to die, he told me to solemnly promise that I would bury his body in Canaan, in the tomb that he himself had prepared. So please let me go up to Canaan and bury my father’s body. Then I will return.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 48:21"> After they gave the king the message, he replied, “Tell Joseph, ‘Go up and bury your father’s body, as you ◄swore/solemnly promised► that you would do.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 14:16"> So Joseph went up to Canaan to bury his father’s body. All of the king’s officials, all the king’s advisors, and all the elders in Egypt went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:24–27; EXODUS 10:26; EXODUS 10:8–9"> His own family’s small children and their sheep and goats and their cattle stayed in the Goshen region. But all the rest of Joseph’s family and his older brothers and younger brother and his father’s family went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 14:28; 2 KINGS 18:24; SONG OF SONGS 1:9"> Men riding in chariots &#91;MTY&#93; and on horses also went along. It was a huge group.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOB 2:13; ACTS 8:2; 2 SAMUEL 1:17"> They went to the east side of the Jordan River and arrived at Atad. There was a place there where people ◄threshed/beat the grain to separate the wheat from the chaff.► There they mourned loudly for Jacob for a long time. Joseph performed mourning ceremonies for his father for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 10:15–19; GENESIS 24:6; DEUTERONOMY 11:30"> When the Canaan people-group who lived there saw them mourning like that, they said, “This is a sad mourning place for the people of Egypt!” So they named the place Abel-Mizraim, which sounds like the Hebrew words that mean ‘mourning of the Egyptians.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ACTS 7:16; GENESIS 49:29–32; EPHESIANS 6:1"> Then Jacob’s sons did for him what their father had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 49:29–31; GENESIS 23:16–18; ACTS 7:16"> They crossed the Jordan River and carried Jacob’s body to Canaan. They buried it in the cave in the field at Machpelah, east of Mamre town. That was the field that Abraham had bought from Ephron, who was one of the Heth people-group, to use as a burial place.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> After he had buried his father, Joseph and his older brothers and younger brother and all the others who had gone up to Canaan with him for the funeral returned to Egypt.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph told his brothers that he would not get revenge for what they did to him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:1; GENESIS 42:21–22; PSALMS 53:5"> After Jacob died, Joseph’s brothers became worried. They realized what might happen. They said, “Suppose Joseph hates us and tries to get revenge for all the evil things that we did to him many years ago?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:25"> So they sent someone to tell this to Joseph for them: “Before our father died, he told us this:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:32; MATTHEW 6:14–15; GENESIS 45:8"> ‘Say to Joseph, “Please forgive your older brothers for the evil thing that they did to you, for their terrible sin against you, because what they did to you was very wrong.”’ So now we, who are servants of your father’s God, ask you, please forgive us for what we did to you.” But Joseph just cried when he received their message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 37:7–11; GENESIS 42:6; GENESIS 45:3"> Then his older brothers themselves came and threw themselves on the ground in front of Joseph, and one of them said, “Please listen. We will just be your servants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ROMANS 12:19; GENESIS 45:5; GENESIS 30:2"> But Joseph replied to them, “Do not be afraid! God is the one who punishes people; ◄am I God?/I am not God!► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ROMANS 8:28; PSALMS 119:71; GENESIS 45:5–8"> As for you, yes, you wanted to do something very evil to me. But God caused something good to come from it! He wanted to save many people from dying of hunger, and that is what happened! Today they are alive!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 47:12; 1 PETER 3:9; GENESIS 45:10–11"> So I say again, do not be afraid! I will make sure that you and your children have enough to eat.” In that way he reassured them and made made them feel much better.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph died in Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Joseph lived with his father’s family in Egypt until he was 110 years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:39; GENESIS 30:3; JOB 42:16"> He lived long enough to see Ephraim’s children and grandchildren. The children of Joseph’s grandson Machir, who was Manasseh’s son, were born before Joseph died, and were adopted by Joseph to be his own children &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 3:16–17; GENESIS 35:12; GENESIS 26:3"> One day Joseph said to his older brothers, “I am about to die. But God will certainly ◄help/take care of► you. And some day he will lead your descendants up out of this land and take them to Canaan, the land that he solemnly promised to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 13:19; JOSHUA 24:32; GENESIS 47:29–31"> Then Joseph said, “When God enables you to do that, you must take my body back to Canaan.” He made his older brothers solemnly promise to do that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 50:2–3; GENESIS 47:9; JOSHUA 24:29"> So Joseph died in Egypt when he was 110 years old. His body was embalmed and put in a coffin there.</span>
</div>
</div>
