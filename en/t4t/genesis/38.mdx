## Judah’s daughter-in-law Tamar tricked Judah into making her pregnant

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 22:1; 2 KINGS 4:8; PROVERBS 13:20"> At that time, Judah left his older and younger brothers and went down from the hilly area and stayed with a man whose name was Hiram, who lived in Adullam town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:3; GENESIS 24:3; GENESIS 6:2"> There he ◄met/became acquainted with► a woman who was the daughter of a man from Canaan named Shua. He married her. He had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 46:12; NUMBERS 26:19"> and she became pregnant and later gave birth to a son, whom he named Er.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 46:12; NUMBERS 26:19"> Later she became pregnant again and gave birth to another son whom she named Onan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:20; GENESIS 38:26; GENESIS 46:12"> Many years later, when Judah and his family went to live in Kezib town, Judah’s wife gave birth to another son, whom she named Shelah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 24:3; GENESIS 21:21; MATTHEW 1:3"> When Judah’s oldest son Er grew up, Judah got a wife for him, a woman named Tamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:3; GENESIS 46:12; NUMBERS 26:19"> But Er did something that Yahweh considered to be very wicked, so Yahweh caused him to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 25:5–10; MATTHEW 22:23–27; RUTH 4:5–11"> Then Judah said to Onan, “Your older brother died without having any sons. So marry his widow and have sex &#91;EUP&#93; with her. That is what our customs require that you should do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="TITUS 3:3; DEUTERONOMY 25:6; JAMES 3:16"> But Onan knew if he did that, any children who would be born would not be considered to be his. So every time he had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with his brother’s widow, he spilled his semen on the ground, so that she would not get pregnant and produce children for his older brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 46:12; NUMBERS 11:1; 1 CHRONICLES 21:7"> Yahweh considered that what he did was wicked, so he caused him to die also.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 22:13; RUTH 1:11–13"> Then Judah said to his daughter-in-law Tamar, “Return to your father’s house, but do not marry anyone else. When my youngest son Shelah grows up, he can marry you.” But Judah really did not want Shelah to marry her, because he was afraid that then Shelah would die too, just as his older brothers had died. So Tamar obeyed Judah and went back to live in her father’s house again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:10; 2 SAMUEL 13:39; JUDGES 14:1"> Several years later, Judah’s wife, who was the daughter of Shua, died. When the time of mourning for her was finished, Judah decided to go up to Timnah, to the place where his ◄men were shearing his sheep/sheep were being sheared► His friend Hiram, from Adullam, went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Someone said to Tamar, “Your father-in-law is going to the fields near the city of Timnah to help the men who are shearing his sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 38:26; GENESIS 38:11–13; JEREMIAH 3:2"> She realized that now Shelah was grown up, but Judah had not given her to him to be his wife. So she took off her widow’s clothes, and covered her head with a veil, so that people would not ◄recognize her/know who she was►. Then she sat down at the entrance to Enaim town, which is on the road to Timnah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> When Judah came along and saw her, he thought that she was a prostitute, because she had covered her head like prostitutes often did (OR, and sat where prostitutes often sat).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 6:10; MATTHEW 26:15; 2 SAMUEL 13:11"> Judah did not realize that she was his daughter-in-law. So he said to her, “Hey, let me have sex &#91;EUP&#93; with you!” She replied, “What will you give me for allowing you to have sex &#91;EUP&#93; with me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:33; GENESIS 38:20; PROVERBS 20:16"> He replied, “I will send you a young goat from my flock of goats.” She asked, “Will you give me something now for me to keep until you send the goat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 38:25–26; HOSEA 4:11; JEREMIAH 22:24"> He replied, “What do you want me to give to you?” She replied, “Give me the ring that has your name on it that is tied by a cord around your neck, and give me the walking stick that you are holding in your hand.” So he gave them to her. Then he had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with her, and she became pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 38:14; 2 SAMUEL 14:5; 2 SAMUEL 14:2"> After she left, she took off the veil and put her widow’s clothes on again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 20:9; LEVITICUS 19:17; 2 SAMUEL 13:3"> Judah gave a young goat to his friend from Adullam, for him to take back to the woman, as he had promised. But his friend could not find the woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> So he asked the men who lived there, “Where is the prostitute who was sitting by the road at Enaim?” They replied, “There has never been a prostitute here!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So he went back to Judah and said, “I did not find her. Furthermore, the men who live in that town said, ‘There has never been a prostitute here.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:12; PROVERBS 6:33; REVELATION 16:15"> Judah said, “She can keep the things that I gave to her. If we continued to search for her, people would ridicule us. I tried to send this young goat to her, but you could not find her to give it to her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 21:9; EZEKIEL 23:19; JUDGES 19:2"> About three months later, someone told Judah, “Your daughter-in-law Tamar has become a prostitute and now she is pregnant!” Judah said, “Drag her outside of the city and kill her by burning her!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 37:32; GENESIS 38:18; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:5"> But as they were taking her outside of the city, she gave the ring and walking stick to someone, and told him to take them to Judah, and say to him, “The man who owns these things is the one who caused me to become pregnant.” She also said to tell him, “Look at this ring, and the cord that is attached to it, and this walking stick. Whose are they?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 24:17; JOHN 8:9; TITUS 2:11–12"> When the man did that, Judah recognized the ring and the stick. He said, “She is more righteous than I am. I did not tell my son Shelah to marry her, as I promised that I would.” And Judah did not have sex &#91;EUP&#93; with her again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 25:24"> When it was time for her to give birth, she was surprised that there were twin boys in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> As she was giving birth, one of them put out his hand. So the midwife fastened a scarlet thread around his wrist, saying, “This one came out first.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:4; MATTHEW 1:3; GENESIS 46:12"> But he pulled his hand back inside the womb, and his brother came out first. So she said, “So this is how you break your way out first!” So she named him Perez, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘breaking out.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 9:6; 1 CHRONICLES 2:4; MATTHEW 1:3"> Then his younger brother, the one who had the scarlet thread around his wrist, came out. And he was named Zerah, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘redness of dawn.’</span>
</div>
</div>
