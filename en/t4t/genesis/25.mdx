## Abraham died and was buried near Sarah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:32–33; GENESIS 23:1–2; GENESIS 28:1"> Some time after Sarah died, Abraham married another woman, whose name was Keturah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:32–33; NUMBERS 25:17–18; JOB 2:11"> She later gave birth to six sons: Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 25:13; JEREMIAH 49:8; EZEKIEL 27:20"> Jokshan became the father of two sons, Sheba and Dedan. The descendants of Dedan were the Asshur people-group, the Letush people-group, and the Leum people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 60:6"> The sons of Midian were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. They were all descendants of Keturah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 24:36; HEBREWS 1:2; GALATIANS 3:29"> Abraham declared that after he died, Isaac would inherit everything he owned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 21:14; JUDGES 6:3; ACTS 14:17"> But while Abraham was still living, he gave gifts to the sons of his ◄concubines/slaves that he had taken to be his secondary wives►, and then he sent them away to live in a land to the east, to keep them far from his son Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 12:4"> Abraham lived until he was 175 years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 35:28–29; GENESIS 15:15; 1 CHRONICLES 29:28"> He died at a very old age, joining his ancestors who had died previously &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 50:13; GENESIS 35:29; GENESIS 49:29–30"> His sons Isaac and Ishmael buried his body in the cave at Machpelah area, near Mamre, in the field that Abraham had previously bought from Ephron, one of the descendants of Heth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 23:16; GENESIS 49:31"> Isaac and Ishmael buried his body there, where Abraham previously buried his wife Sarah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 22:17; GENESIS 16:14; GENESIS 24:62"> After Abraham died, God blessed his son Isaac. And Isaac moved to live near Beer-Lahai-Roi.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The descendants of Ishmael

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 83:6; GENESIS 17:20; GENESIS 21:13"> ◄These are/I will now give a list of► the descendants of Abraham’s son, Ishmael, to whom Sarah’s female slave, Hagar from Egypt, had given birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:29–31; Isaiah 60:7; SONG OF SONGS 1:5"> These are their names, in the order in which they were born: Ishmael’s oldest son was named Nebaioth. After him were born Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:11; Isaiah 21:16"> Mishma, Dumah, Massa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 5:19; JOB 2:11"> Hadar, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 17:20; GENESIS 17:23"> The twelve sons of Ishmael became the leaders/chiefs of people-groups that had those names. They each had their own settlement and campsite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 15:15; GENESIS 25:7–8"> Ishmael lived until he was 137 years old. Then he died, &#91;EUP&#93; joining his ancestors who had previously died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 16:12; GENESIS 20:1; 2 KINGS 23:29"> His descendants ◄settled/went to live► in the area between Shur and Havilah, near the border of Egypt as a person travels toward Asshur. All of their camps were close to each other (OR, they all frequently attacked each other).</span>
</div>
</div>

## The birth of Esau and Jacob

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:2; LUKE 3:34; ACTS 7:8"> ◄This is an account of/I will now tell you about► Abraham’s son, Isaac. Abraham became the father of Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 24:29; GENESIS 24:67; GENESIS 22:23"> and when Isaac was 40 years old, he married Rebekah, the daughter of Bethuel. Bethuel was one of the descendants of Aram from Paddan-Aram. Rebekah was the sister of Laban, who belonged to the Aram people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LUKE 1:13; Isaiah 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> Almost 20 years after they were married, Rebekah still had no children. So Isaac prayed to Yahweh concerning his wife, and Yahweh answered his prayer. His wife Rebekah became pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 9:9; 1 SAMUEL 28:6; 1 SAMUEL 30:8"> She was carrying twins in her womb, and they kept jostling each other. So she said, “Why is it that this is happening to me?” So she asked Yahweh about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ROMANS 9:10–13; GENESIS 27:40; GENESIS 27:29"> Yahweh said to her, “The older one of your twins will serve the younger one. The twins will be ancestors of two nations. And those two people-groups will separate from each other.” &#91;CHI&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> When Rebekah gave birth, it was true! Twin boys were born!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 27:11; GENESIS 27:16; GENESIS 27:23"> The first one born was red, and his body had hair all over it, like a garment made of hair. So they named him Esau, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘hairy'.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 27:36; HOSEA 12:3; GENESIS 38:28–30"> Then his brother was born, grasping Esau’s heel. So they named him Jacob, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘heel’. Isaac was 60 years old when the twins were born.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Esau sold his rights as the oldest son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:9; GENESIS 21:20; GENESIS 10:9"> When the boys grew up, Esau became a skilled hunter. He spent a lot of time out in the fields. Jacob was a quiet man who stayed close to the campsite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 27:19; GENESIS 27:31; GENESIS 27:4"> Isaac liked Esau more, because he enjoyed the taste of the meat of the animals that Esau killed. But Rebekah liked Jacob more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:25; Isaiah 40:30–31; 1 SAMUEL 14:28"> One day while Jacob was cooking some stew, Esau came home from the field, very hungry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 36:1; EXODUS 15:15; GENESIS 36:9"> He said to Jacob, “Give me some of that red stew to eat right now, because I am very hungry!” &#91;That is why Esau’s other name was Edom, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘red’.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Jacob said, “I will give you some if you sell me ◄your birthright/the privileges you have because you are the firstborn son►.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MALACHI 3:14; JOB 21:15; JOB 22:17"> Esau replied, “Well, I am about to die from being so hungry. If I die now, ◄my birthright will not benefit me./What good will my birthright be to me?►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:16; GENESIS 27:36; HEBREWS 6:16"> Jacob said, “◄Swear to/Solemnly promise► me that you are giving me the privileges that you will have from being the firstborn son!” So that is what Esau did. He sold his birthright to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:18–19; HEBREWS 12:16–17; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:32"> Then Jacob gave to Esau some bread and some stew made of lentils/beans. Esau ate and drank, and then he got up and left. By doing that, Esau showed that he ◄was not interested in/did not value► the privileges that would be his because of being the firstborn son.</span>
</div>
</div>
