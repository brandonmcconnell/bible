## Those first people sinned by disobeying God

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 11:3; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:14; REVELATION 12:9"> The snake was more cunning than all the other wild animals that Yahweh God had made. One day Satan gave to the snake the ability to talk to the woman. The snake said to her, “Did God really say to you, ‘Do not eat the fruit from any of the trees in the park/garden’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 58:4"> The woman replied, “What God said was, ‘Do not eat the fruit from the tree that is in the middle of the park/garden or touch it. If you do that, you will die. But you can eat fruit from any of the other trees.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 8:44; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:3; 1 TIMOTHY 2:14"> The snake said to the woman, “No, you will certainly not die. God said that</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 28:2; 2 THESSALONIANS 2:4; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:13–15"> only because he knows that when you eat fruit from that tree, you will understand new things. It will be as though your eyes are opened &#91;MET&#93;, and you will know what is good to do and what is evil to do, just as God does.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 JOHN 2:16; JAMES 1:14–15; 1 TIMOTHY 2:14"> The woman saw that the fruit on that tree was good to eat, and it was very beautiful, and she desired it because she thought it would make her wise. So she picked some of the fruit and ate it. Then she gave some to her husband, and he ate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:6; GENESIS 2:25; GENESIS 3:5"> Immediately it was as though their eyes were opened, and they realized that they were naked, so they were ashamed. So they picked some fig leaves and fastened them together to make clothes for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:24; PROVERBS 15:3; HEBREWS 4:13"> Late that afternoon, when a cool breeze was blowing, they heard the sound/footsteps of Yahweh as he was walking in the park/garden. So the man and his wife hid themselves among the bushes in the park/garden, so that Yahweh God would not see them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 4:9; GENESIS 16:8; GENESIS 18:20–21"> But Yahweh God called to the man, saying to him, “Why are you trying to hide from me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 2:25; PSALMS 119:120; Isaiah 47:3"> The man replied, “I heard the sound of your footsteps/walking in the garden, and I was naked, so I was afraid and I hid myself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 3:20; GENESIS 4:10; PSALMS 50:21"> God said, “How did you find out you were naked &#91;RHQ&#93;? It must be because you ate some of the fruit from the tree that I told you, ‘Do not eat its fruit.’” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:13; JAMES 1:13–15; JOB 31:33"> The man said, “You gave me this woman to be with me. She is the one who gave me some of the fruit from that tree, and so I ate it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 11:3; 1 TIMOTHY 2:14; GENESIS 4:10–12"> Then Yahweh God said to the woman, “Why did you do such a thing?” The woman replied, “I ate some of the fruit because the snake deceived me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MICAH 7:17; Isaiah 65:25; PSALMS 72:9"> Then Yahweh God said to the snake, “Because you did this, of all the livestock and the wild animals, I will curse/punish you alone. As a result, you and all other snakes will scurry across the ground on your bellies, and so what you eat will have dirt on it as long as you live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 16:20; 1 JOHN 3:8; REVELATION 12:17"> And I will cause you and the woman to act in a hostile way toward each other, and I will cause your brood and her descendants to act hostilely toward each other. You will strike at the heel of one descendant of hers, but he will strike your head.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="TITUS 2:5; COLOSSIANS 3:18; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:3"> Then Yahweh said to the woman, “You will have great pain when you give birth to children; you will suffer great pain as your children are being born &#91;DOU&#93;. You will greatly desire your husband to show affection for you, but he will rule over you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 8:20–22; GENESIS 5:29; Isaiah 24:5–6"> Then Yahweh said to the man, “You heeded what your wife said, and you ate some of the fruit of the tree concerning which I commanded you saying, ‘Do not eat it.’ So I will make it difficult to grow things in the ground because of what you did. You will have to work hard as long as you live to produce things from the ground to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 13:7; JOB 5:5; Isaiah 7:23"> Thornbushes and thistle plants and other weeds will grow and prevent what you have planted from growing. And for food, you will have to eat things that just grow in your fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:7; JOB 34:15; PSALMS 104:29"> All your life you will sweat as you work hard to produce food to eat. Then you will die &#91;EUP&#93;, and your body will be buried in the ground. I made you from soil, so when you die and are buried your body will decay and become soil again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 2:23; MATTHEW 1:23; GENESIS 5:29"> The man, whose name was Adam, named his wife Eve, which means ‘living’, because she became the ancestor of all living people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:21; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:2–3"> Then Yahweh God killed some animals and made clothes from their skins for Adam and his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="REVELATION 2:7; REVELATION 22:14; PROVERBS 3:18"> Then Yahweh said, “Look! Those two have become like us because they know what is good to do and what is evil to do. So now, it will not be good if they reach out and pick and eat some of the fruit from the tree which enables people who eat it to live forever!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 4:2; GENESIS 3:19; GENESIS 4:12"> So Yahweh God expelled the man and his wife from the garden of Eden. Yahweh God had created Adam from the ground, but now he forced him to till that ground so that he could grow food to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:7; PSALMS 104:4; EXODUS 25:18–22"> After Yahweh expelled them, on the east side of the garden he placed ◄cherubim/creatures with wings► and an angel holding a flaming sword which flashed back and forth. He did that in order to block the entrance to the garden, so that people could not go back to the tree that enables people who eat its fruit to live forever.</span>
