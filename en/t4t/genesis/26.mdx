## Isaac deceived King Abimelech

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 12:10; GENESIS 25:11; GENESIS 21:22–32"> Some time later there was a severe ◄famine/scarcity of food► there. That was different from the famine that occurred when Abraham was alive. So Isaac went southeast to Gerar town, to talk to Abimelech, the king of the Philistine people-group. What happened was this: Isaac considered going to Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 12:7; PSALMS 37:3; GENESIS 17:1"> but Yahweh appeared to him and said, “Do not go down to Egypt! Live in the land where I will tell you to go!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 28:15; PSALMS 105:9; GENESIS 17:8"> Stay in this land for a while, and I will help you and bless you, because it is to you and your descendants that I will give all these lands, and I will do what I solemnly promised to your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ACTS 3:25; GENESIS 15:5; GENESIS 22:17–18"> I will cause your descendants to be as numerous as the stars in the sky. I will give to your descendants all these lands, and I will cause your descendants to be a blessing to the people of &#91;MTY&#93; all nations on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:24; GENESIS 22:18; GENESIS 22:16"> I will do that because your father Abraham obeyed me. He obeyed everything that I told him to do, everything that I declared and all the laws that I gave him.”<br />So Isaac went and asked King Abimelech if he would permit him to live in the Gerar area. The king said, “Okay,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 20:1"> so Isaac stayed there along with his wife and sons.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:25; GENESIS 12:13; GENESIS 20:2"> When the men in Gerar town asked who Rebekah was, Isaac said, “She is my sister.” He said that because he was afraid to say, “She is my wife.” He thought, “Rebekah is very beautiful, so they will want her. If I say that she is my sister, they know they will have to negotiate about a bride price because I am her older brother; but if I say that she is my wife, no negotiation will be possible. They will just kill me to get her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 62:5; PROVERBS 5:18–19; PROVERBS 7:6"> When Isaac had been there a long time, one day Abimelech, the king of the Philistine people-group, looked down from a window in his palace and was surprised to see Isaac caressing his wife Rebekah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So Abimelech summoned Isaac and said to him, “Now I realize that she is really your wife! So why did you say, ‘She is my sister’?” Isaac replied to him, “I said that because I thought that someone here might kill me to get her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 20:9–10; GENESIS 12:18–19"> Abimelech said, “◄You should not have done this to us!/Why did you do this?► &#91;RHQ&#93; One of our people might have ◄had sex with/slept with► &#91;EUP&#93; your wife, and you would have caused us to be guilty of a great sin!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 105:15; GENESIS 20:6; PROVERBS 6:29"> Then Abimelech commanded all his people, saying, “Do not harm/molest this man or his wife! Anyone who does that will surely be executed!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Philistines argued with Isaac about wells

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MARK 4:8; GENESIS 24:1; GENESIS 26:3"> Isaac planted grain in that land that year, and he harvested a very large crop, because Yahweh blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 112:3; PROVERBS 10:22; GENESIS 24:35"> Isaac continued to acquire more and more possessions, until finally he became very wealthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 37:11; GENESIS 13:2; PROVERBS 27:4"> He had large herds of sheep and goats and cattle, and many slaves. Because of that, the Philistine people envied him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 21:30; GENESIS 21:25"> So all the wells that the servants of his father Abraham had dug during the time when he was alive, the people filled up with dirt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 1:9"> Then Abimelech said to Isaac, “You people have become more numerous than we are, so I want you to get out of our area.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Isaac and his family moved from there. They set up their tents in Gerar Valley and started to live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:2; HOSEA 2:17; NUMBERS 32:38"> There were several wells in that area that had been dug when Isaac’s father Abraham was living, but Philistine people had filled them up with dirt after Abraham died. Now Isaac and his servants removed the dirt, and Isaac gave the wells the same names that his father had given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOHN 4:10–11; JOHN 7:38; SONG OF SONGS 4:15"> Isaac’s servants dug in the valley and discovered a well of fresh water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 21:25"> But other men who lived in Gerar Valley who took care of their animals argued/quarreled with the men who took care of Isaac’s animals, and said, “The water in this well is ours!” So Isaac named the well Esek, which means ‘dispute’, because they disputed about who owned it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezra 4:6"> Then Isaac’s servants dug another well, but they quarreled about who owned that one also. So Isaac named it Sitnah, which means ‘opposition’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 17:6; EXODUS 1:7; PSALMS 118:5"> They moved on from there and dug another well, but this time no one quarreled about who owned it. So Isaac named it Rehoboth, which means ‘uninhabited place’, saying, “Yahweh has given us an uninhabited place to live in, a place that is not wanted by other people, and we will become very prosperous here.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JUDGES 20:1; GENESIS 21:31; GENESIS 46:1"> From there Isaac went up to Beersheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 15:1; EXODUS 3:6; Isaiah 43:1–2"> The first night that he was there, Yahweh appeared to him and said, “I am God, whom your father Abraham worshiped. Do not be afraid of anything. I will help you and bless you, and because of what I promised my servant Abraham, I will greatly increase the number of your descendants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 13:18; PSALMS 116:17; GENESIS 12:7–8"> So Isaac built a stone altar there and offered a sacrifice to worship Yahweh. He and his servants set up their tents there, and his servants started to dig a well.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Isaac made a peace treaty with King Abimelech

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 21:22–32; GENESIS 20:3"> While they were digging the well, King Abimelech came to Isaac from Gerar, along with Ahuzzath, his advisor, and Phicol, the commander of his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 26:16; JUDGES 11:7; ACTS 7:14"> Isaac asked them, “You ◄acted in a hostile way toward me/treated me like an enemy► before, and sent me away. So why have you come to me now?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 21:22–23; Isaiah 45:14; Isaiah 61:9"> One of them answered, “We have seen that Yahweh helps you. So we said to each other, ‘We should have an agreement between us and you. We should make a peace treaty with you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 24:31; GENESIS 21:22; GENESIS 22:17"> stating that you will not harm us, in the same way that we did not molest &#91;EUP&#93; you.’ We always treated you well and sent you away peacefully. And now Yahweh is blessing you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 19:3; ROMANS 12:18; HEBREWS 12:14"> So Isaac made a feast for them, and they all ate and drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 20:16–17; HEBREWS 6:16; GENESIS 25:33"> Early the next morning they all ◄swore/solemnly promised► each other that they would do what they had promised. Then Isaac sent them home peacefully.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PROVERBS 10:4; PROVERBS 13:4; PROVERBS 2:4–5"> That day Isaac’s servants came to him and told him about the well that they had finished digging. They said, “We found water in the well!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="GENESIS 21:31; GENESIS 26:28"> Isaac named the well Shibah, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘oath’. To the present time the town there has the name Beersheba which means ‘Friendship Agreement Well’.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Esau married foreign women

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 36:2; GENESIS 36:13; EXODUS 34:16"> When Esau was 40 years old, he married Judith, the daughter of Beeri, and Basemath, the daughter of Elon. Both of those women were descendants of Heth, not from Isaac’s clan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="GENESIS 27:46–2; GENESIS 28:8; GENESIS 6:2"> Esau’s two wives made life miserable for Isaac and Rebekah.</span>
