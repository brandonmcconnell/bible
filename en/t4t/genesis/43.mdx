## Joseph’s brothers returned to Egypt with Benjamin, to buy more grain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 12:10; ACTS 7:11–13; LAMENTATIONS 5:10"> The famine in Canaan got worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 6:6–8; PROVERBS 16:18; PROVERBS 15:16"> Finally, when Jacob and his family had eaten all the grain they had brought from Egypt, Jacob said to them, “Go back to Egypt and buy some more grain for us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 44:23; GENESIS 43:5; 2 SAMUEL 3:13"> But Judah said to him, “The man who sold us the grain warned us sternly, ‘I will not let you see me &#91;SYN&#93; again if you come and your younger brother is not with you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So, if you will send our younger brother with us, we will go down to Egypt and buy some grain for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 20:12; GENESIS 44:26; GENESIS 42:38"> But if you will not send him, we will not go down there, because that man said to us, ‘I will not let you see me again if your younger brother is not with you.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Jacob asked, “Why did you cause me to have this trouble by telling the man that you had a younger brother?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 43:3; GENESIS 42:13; GENESIS 43:27"> One of them replied, “The man asked about us and about our family. He said, ‘Is your father still living? Do you have another brother?’ We had to answer his questions. ◄We could not know that he would say, ‘The next time that you come down here, bring your brother with you!’/How could we know that he would say, ‘The next time that you come down here, bring your brother with you!’►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 42:2; DEUTERONOMY 33:6; GENESIS 45:19"> Then Judah said to his father Jacob, “Send the boy with me, and we will go immediately, in order that we and you and our children may get grain and not die from hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 42:37; PHILEMON 1:18–19; HEBREWS 7:22"> I myself will guarantee that he will return. You can require me to do what I am promising &#91;IDM&#93;. If I do not bring him back to you safely, you can say forever that ◄I am to blame/it was my fault that he did not return to you►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 19:16"> If we had not ◄wasted so much time/waited so long►, by now we could have gone there and returned two times!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 37:25; JEREMIAH 8:22; EZEKIEL 27:17"> Then their father Jacob said to them, “If there is no other way, do this: Put in your sacks some of the best things that are grown in this land, and take them down to the man as a gift. Take some balm/perfume and honey and spices and myrrh/ointment, some pistachio nuts, and almonds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 42:25; GENESIS 42:35; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:6"> Take twice as much money as you took the previous time, because you must return the silver that someone put in the tops of your sacks. Perhaps it was a mistake that it was put in your sacks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 42:38"> Take your younger brother and go back to that man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 4:16; GENESIS 17:1; ACTS 21:14"> I will pray that God Almighty will cause that man to act mercifully toward you, so that he will let your other brother, as well as Benjamin, come back here with you. But as for me, if my sons are taken from me, then I will not have my sons!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So the men took the gifts that Jacob said that they should take, and twice the amount of money that the grain would cost, and they also took Benjamin. They went down quickly to Egypt, and they stood in front of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 44:1; GENESIS 43:19; GENESIS 24:2–10"> When Joseph saw Benjamin with them, he said to the man who ◄was in charge of/supervised► things in his house, “Take these men to my house. Slaughter an animal and prepare a meal, because I want them to eat with me at noon.” And he told him in what order they were to be seated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The man did as Joseph said. And he took them to Joseph’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 42:35; ROMANS 7:8; GENESIS 42:21"> But they were afraid because he was taking them to Joseph’s house. They were thinking, “He is taking us here because of the silver that was put in our sacks the first time that we came here. While we are eating, he will have his servants attack us and seize us and cause us to become his slaves, and also take our donkeys.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph made a feast for his brothers, but they still did not recognize him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> They went with the man who was in charge of things in Joseph’s house. When they arrived at the entrance of the house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 42:3; GENESIS 42:10; GENESIS 42:27"> one of them said to him, “Please, sir, listen to me. We came down here previously and bought some grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 43:12; GENESIS 42:27–35; ROMANS 13:8"> But at the place where we stopped for the night as we were returning home, we opened our sacks. We were astonished to see that in the top of each of our sacks was the exact amount of silver that we had paid for the grain! So we have brought it back with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> We have also brought more silver with us to buy more grain. We do not know who put the silver in our sacks.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 20:26; GENESIS 42:24; JOHN 14:27"> The man replied, “Relax! Do not worry about it! I received the silver that you brought. Your God, the God your father worships, must have put it in your sacks.” And then he brought Simeon to them from the prison.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 18:4; GENESIS 24:32; LUKE 7:44"> Then he took them into Joseph’s house. He gave them water to wash their feet and gave them food for the donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 43:11; GENESIS 43:16"> He told them that they were going to eat with Joseph at noon. So the men prepared their gifts to give to Joseph when he arrived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:10–11; GENESIS 27:29; GENESIS 42:6"> When Joseph came home, they presented to him the gifts that they had brought into the house. Then they bowed down to the ground in front of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 42:13; 1 SAMUEL 25:5; 1 SAMUEL 17:22"> He asked them if they were ◄well/in good health►, and then he asked, “How is the health of your old father, the one that you told me about? Is he still living?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 37:7; GENESIS 43:26; 2 SAMUEL 14:4"> One of them replied, “Yes, our father, who is willing to be your servant, is still alive, and he is well.” Then again they bowed down in front of him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 42:13; NUMBERS 6:25; GENESIS 30:22–24"> Then he saw his younger brother Benjamin, his own mother’s other son. He asked them, “Is this your youngest brother, the one whom you told me about?” After they said “Yes,” he said to Benjamin, “Young man, I pray that God will act kindly toward you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 42:24; JEREMIAH 31:20; 1 KINGS 3:26"> Joseph quickly left the room. He realized that he was about to cry because he ◄was full of emotion about his younger brother/loved his younger brother so much►. He went into his private room and cried there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 45:1; 1 PETER 3:10; Isaiah 42:14"> Then, after he washed the tears from his face, he came out, and controlling his emotions, he said to the servants, “Serve the food!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 46:34; EXODUS 8:26; GENESIS 43:16"> The people of Egypt considered that it was disgraceful for them to eat with Hebrews, so the servants served food to Joseph by himself, and served the other people of Egypt who ate with him by themselves, and they served Joseph’s older brothers and younger brother by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> His brothers were astonished to see that their seats were arranged according to their ages, from the oldest to the youngest!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="GENESIS 45:22; 2 SAMUEL 11:8; 1 SAMUEL 1:5"> And when their portions of food were served to them from Joseph’s table, Benjamin’s portion was five times as much as anyone else’s portion! So they ate food and drank wine with Joseph and became very cheerful.</span>
</div>
</div>
