## God sent a flood to cover the earth

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 24:37–39; 2 PETER 2:5–9; GENESIS 6:9"> Then Yahweh said to Noah, “I have seen that out of everyone who is now living, you alone always act righteously. So you and all your family go into the boat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 8:20; LEVITICUS 10:10; LEVITICUS 11:1–47"> Take with you seven pairs of every kind of animal that I have said I will accept for sacrifices. Take seven males and seven females. Also take a male and a female from every kind of animal that I have said that I will not accept for sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Also take seven pairs of every kind of bird from all over the earth to keep them alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 6:13; GENESIS 7:12; GENESIS 7:17"> Do this because seven days from now I will cause rain to fall on the earth. It will rain constantly for 40 days and nights. By doing that, I will destroy everything that I have made that is on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 6:22; LUKE 8:21; EXODUS 39:32"> Noah did everything that Yahweh told him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 5:32; GENESIS 8:13"> Noah was 600 years old when the flood covered the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 17:27; GENESIS 6:18; MATTHEW 24:38"> Before it started to rain, Noah and his wife and his sons went into the boat to escape from the flood water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Pairs of animals, those that God said that he would accept for sacrifices and those that he would not accept for sacrifices, and pairs of birds and pairs of all the kinds of creatures that move close to the ground,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 7:16; GENESIS 2:19; JEREMIAH 8:7"> males and females, came to Noah and then went into the boat, just as God told Noah that they would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 6:17; GENESIS 7:4; LUKE 17:27"> Seven days later, it started to rain and a flood began to cover the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 8:2; MALACHI 3:10; PROVERBS 8:28–29"> When Noah was 600 years old, ◄on the 17th day of the second month of that year/late in October►, all the water that is under the surface of the earth burst forth, and it began to rain so hard that it was as though a dam &#91;MET&#93; in the sky burst open.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 7:4; MATTHEW 4:2; GENESIS 7:17"> Rain fell on the earth constantly for 40 days and nights.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 6:18; GENESIS 7:1; GENESIS 6:10"> On the day that it started to rain, Noah went into the boat with his wife, and his three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth and their wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 7:8–9; GENESIS 7:2–3"> They had already put in the boat some of every kind of wild animal and every kind of livestock and every kind of bird and every other creature that has wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 6:19–20; Isaiah 11:6"> Pairs of all animals came to Noah and entered the boat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:10; LUKE 13:25; PSALMS 91:1–10"> There was a male and a female of each animal that came to Noah, just as God had said they would do. After they were all in the boat, God shut the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 7:4; GENESIS 7:12"> It rained for 40 days and nights, and the flood increased. It flooded until the water lifted the boat above the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 14:28; PSALMS 69:15; JOB 22:16"> As he water rose higher and higher, the boat floated on the surface of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 104:6–9; PSALMS 46:2–3; 2 PETER 3:6"> The water rose all over the earth until it covered all the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:23; PSALMS 104:6"> Even the highest mountains were covered by more than ◄20 feet/6 meters► of water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 6:13; GENESIS 7:4; GENESIS 6:17"> As a result, every living creature on the surface of the earth died. That included birds and livestock and wild animals and other creatures that scurry across the ground, and all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 2:7; GENESIS 6:17"> On the land, ◄everything that breathed/every living thing► died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 PETER 2:5; 1 PETER 3:20; HEBREWS 11:7"> God destroyed every living creature: People and animals and creatures that scurry across the ground and birds. Only Noah and those who were in the boat with him remained alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 8:3–4"> The flood remained like that on the earth for 150 days.</span>
