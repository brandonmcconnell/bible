## Abram rescued Lot

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 10:10; GENESIS 10:22; Isaiah 37:12"> In a region to the east, there were four kings who were friends/allies. They were King Amraphel of Babylonia, King Arioch of Ellasar, King Chedorlaomer of Elam, and King Tidal of Goiim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 13:10; DEUTERONOMY 29:23; GENESIS 10:19"> In an area to the west, there were five other kings. They were King Bera of Sodom, King Birsha of Gomorrah, King Shinab of Admah, King Shemeber of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, the city that is now called Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOSHUA 3:16; DEUTERONOMY 3:17; NUMBERS 34:12"> Those five kings and their armies gathered together in Siddim Valley, which is also called the Dead Sea Valley, to fight against the four kings and their armies. King Chedorlaomer and his army conquered the armies of those five kings, and demanded that those kings pay him tribute money each year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 9:25–26; EZEKIEL 17:15"> For twelve years he ruled them. But during the thirteenth year they rebelled and refused to keep giving him tribute money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 3:11; GENESIS 15:20; DEUTERONOMY 1:4"> The next year, King Chedorlaomer and the other kings that were his allies took their armies and defeated the Repha people-group in Ashteroth-Karnaim and the Zuz people-group in Ham, and the Emi people-group in Shaveh-Kiriathaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:22; DEUTERONOMY 2:12; GENESIS 21:21"> They also defeated the Hor people-group in the hilly area of Seir as far as El-Paran near the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 20:2; GENESIS 16:14; GENESIS 20:1"> Then they turned back and went to Mishpat city, which is now called Kadesh. They conquered all the land belonging to the Amalek people-group and the Amor people-group who were living in Hazazon-Tamar town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 19:22; GENESIS 13:10; GENESIS 19:20"> Then the armies of the kings of Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim and Bela marched out to fight the armies of the other four kings in Siddim Valley. That valley is close to the Salt/Dead Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> They fought against the armies of Chedorlaomer the king of Elam, Tidal the king of Goiim, Amraphel the king of Shinar, and Arioch the king of Ellasar. The armies of four kings were fighting against the armies of five kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 19:17; GENESIS 19:30; Isaiah 24:18"> The Siddim Valley was full of tar pits. So when the armies of the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah tried to run away, many of the men fell into the tar pits. The others escaped and ran away to the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 14:21; GENESIS 14:16; DEUTERONOMY 28:31"> As the armies of the five kings fled, the armies of the four kings seized all of the valuable things in Sodom and Gomorrah, including all the food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 12:5; GENESIS 11:27; REVELATION 3:19"> They also captured Abram’s nephew Lot and his possessions, since he was living in Sodom at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 13:18; GENESIS 14:24; GENESIS 40:15"> Abram was living near the big trees near Mamre, who belonged to the Amor people-group. Abram had made an agreement with Mamre and his two brothers, Eshcol and Aner, that they would help each other if there was a war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 18:29; GENESIS 15:3; GENESIS 17:27"> One of the men who escaped from the battle told Abram what had happened and that his nephew Lot had been captured and taken away. So Abram summoned 318 men who were his servants, men who had been with Abram since they were born. They all went together and pursued their enemies as far north as Dan city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 9:2; PSALMS 112:5; 1 KINGS 15:18"> During the night, Abram divided the men into several groups, and they attacked their enemies from various directions and defeated them. They pursued them as far as Hobah, which was north of Damascus city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 30:18–19; 1 SAMUEL 30:8; GENESIS 12:2"> Abram’s men recovered all of the goods that had been taken. They also rescued Lot and all his possessions and also the women and others who had been captured by their enemies.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Melchizedek blessed Abram

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 18:18; PROVERBS 19:4; HEBREWS 7:1"> As Abram was returning home after he and his men had defeated the armies of King Chedorlaomer and the other kings who had fought alongside him, the king of Sodom went north to meet him in Shaveh Valley, which people call the King’s Valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:20–3; PSALMS 110:4; HEBREWS 5:6"> The king of Salem city, whose name was Melchizedek, was also a priest who offered sacrifices to the Supreme God. He brought some bread and wine to Abram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="HEBREWS 7:6–7; GENESIS 14:22; PSALMS 24:1"> Then he blessed Abram, saying “I ask the Supreme God, the one who created heaven and earth, to bless you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 28:22; HEBREWS 7:4–9; MALACHI 3:10"> The Supreme God has enabled you to defeat &#91;MTY&#93; your enemies, so we should praise/thank him.” Then Abram gave to Melchizedek a tenth part of all the things he had captured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The king of Sodom said to Abram, “You can keep all the goods you recovered. Just let me take back the people from my city whom you rescued.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 12:7; DANIEL 4:34; DEUTERONOMY 32:40"> But Abram said to the king of Sodom, “I have solemnly promised &#91;MTY&#93; to Yahweh, the Supreme God, the one who created heaven and earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:16; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:9–12; Esther 9:15–16"> that I will not accept even one thread or a thong of a sandal from anything that belongs to you. As a result, you will never be able to say, ‘I caused Abram to become rich.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 14:13; PROVERBS 3:27; MATTHEW 7:12"> The only thing I will accept is the food that my men have eaten. But Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre went with me and fought alongside me, so let them also have a share of the goods we brought back.” So the king of Sodom agreed to what he said.</span>
</div>
</div>
