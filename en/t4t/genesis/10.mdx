## The descendants of Noah’s sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 2:4; GENESIS 9:7; GENESIS 5:1"> ◄This is/I will now give► a list of the descendants of Noah’s sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth. They had many children after the flood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 38:2; EZEKIEL 38:6; REVELATION 20:8"> The sons of Japheth were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:14; JEREMIAH 51:27"> The sons of Gomer were Askenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DANIEL 11:30; Isaiah 23:12; NUMBERS 24:24"> The sons of Javan were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 2:11; Isaiah 42:4; JEREMIAH 2:10"> Those sons and their families who were descended from Javan lived on the islands and on the land close to the Mediterranean Sea. Their descendants became tribes, each with its own language and clans and territory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:8–16; EZEKIEL 27:10; PSALMS 105:23"> The descendants of Ham were Cush, Egypt, Put, and Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:22; EZEKIEL 27:15; Isaiah 21:13"> The descendants of Cush were Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. The descendants of Raamah were Sheba and Dedan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MICAH 5:6"> Another one of Cush’s descendants was Nimrod. Nimrod was the first person on earth who became a mighty warrior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 28:22; GENESIS 6:11; GENESIS 25:27"> Yahweh saw that he had become (OR, caused him to become) a great hunter. That is why people say to a great hunter, “Yahweh ◄sees that you are/has caused you to be► a great hunter like Nimrod.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 11:9; GENESIS 11:2; GENESIS 14:1"> Nimrod became a king who ruled in Babylonia. The first cities over which he ruled were Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MICAH 5:6; PSALMS 83:8; NAHUM 1:1"> From there he went with others to Assyria and built the cities of Nineveh, Rehoboth-Ir, Calah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> and Resen. Resen was a large city between Nineveh and Calah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:11–12; JEREMIAH 46:9; EZEKIEL 30:5"> Ham’s son, Egypt, became the ancestor of the Lud, Anam, Lehab and Naphtuh,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 47:4; 1 CHRONICLES 1:12; DEUTERONOMY 2:23"> Pathrus, Casluh and Caphtor people-groups. The Philistine people were descended from Casluh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 34:2–15; EXODUS 34:11; GENESIS 23:3"> Ham’s youngest son, Canaan, became the father of Sidon, who was his eldest son, and Heth, his younger son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 24:18; JUDGES 1:21; ZECHARIAH 9:7"> Canaan was also the ancestor of the Jebus, Amor, Girgash,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 34:2"> Hiv, Ark, Sin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:22; 2 SAMUEL 8:9; EZEKIEL 27:8"> Arved, Zemar and Hamath people-groups. Later the descendants of Canaan dispersed over a large area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 14:2; GENESIS 19:24–25; JOSHUA 12:7–8"> Their land extended from Sidon city in the north as far south as Gaza town, and then to the east as far as Gerar town, and then farther east to Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim towns, and even as far as Lasha town.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 10:6; GENESIS 11:1–9"> Those are the descendants of Ham. They became groups that had their own clans, their own languages, and their own land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 24:24; GENESIS 11:10–26"> Shem, the older brother of Japheth, became the father of Eber, and the ancestor of all the descendants of Eber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 66:19; JEREMIAH 25:25; GENESIS 14:1–9"> The sons of Shem were Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOB 1:1; JEREMIAH 25:20"> The sons of Aram were Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 3:35; GENESIS 11:12–15"> Arphaxad became the father of Shelah. Shelah became the father of Eber.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:19; GENESIS 10:32; DEUTERONOMY 32:8"> Eber became the father of two sons. One of them was named Peleg, which means ‘division’, because during the time he lived, people on &#91;MTY&#93; the earth became divided and scattered everywhere. Peleg’s younger brother was Joktan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:20–28"> Joktan became the ancestor of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:20–28"> Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 1:20–28; 1 KINGS 10:1; GENESIS 25:3"> Obal, Abimael, Sheba,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:28; 1 CHRONICLES 9:13; GENESIS 2:11"> Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All those people were descended from Joktan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:7"> The areas in which they lived extended from Mesha westward to Sephar, which is in the ◄hill country/area that has a lot of hills►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 10:5; GENESIS 10:20; ACTS 17:26"> They are descendants of Shem. They became groups that had their own clans, their own languages, and their own land.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 9:19; ACTS 17:26; GENESIS 10:1"> All those groups descended from the sons of Noah. Each group had its own ◄genealogy/record of people’s ancestors► and each became a separate ethnic group. Those ethnic groups formed after the flood and spread all around the earth.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
