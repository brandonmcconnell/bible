## Sarah gave birth to Isaac

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 12:6; GENESIS 18:10; GENESIS 18:14"> Yahweh was very kind to Sarah, just as he said he would be. He did for Sarah exactly what he promised to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:22; HEBREWS 11:11; GENESIS 18:10"> She became pregnant and gave birth to a son for Abraham when he was very old, at the time God promised it would happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 17:19; ROMANS 9:7; GENESIS 21:6"> Abraham gave the name ‘Isaac’ (which means ‘he laughs’) to the son Sarah gave birth to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ACTS 7:8; DEUTERONOMY 12:32; JOHN 7:22–23"> Abraham circumcised his son Isaac when his son was eight days old, just as God commanded him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 17:17; ROMANS 4:19; GENESIS 17:1"> Abraham was 100 years old when his son Isaac was born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 126:2; Isaiah 54:1; GENESIS 17:17"> And Sarah said, “Although I was sad before because I did not have any children, God has now enabled me to laugh, and everyone who hears about what God has done for me will laugh with me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 18:11–12; Isaiah 66:8; 2 THESSALONIANS 1:10"> Then she also said, “No one would have said to Abraham that some day Sarah would nurse a child, but I have given birth to a son when Abraham is very old.”</span>

## Abraham sent Hagar and her son Ishmael away

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 1:22; HOSEA 1:8; GENESIS 40:20"> The baby grew and was weaned when he was about three years old. On that day, Abraham prepared a large feast to celebrate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GALATIANS 4:29; GENESIS 16:15; GALATIANS 4:22"> ◄One day/During the feast► Sarah noticed that Hagar’s son Ishmael was ◄making fun of/playing with► Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 8:35; 1 JOHN 2:19; GALATIANS 4:22–31"> So she said to Abraham, “Get rid of that slave woman from Egypt and her son! I do not want the son of that slave woman to be alone (OR, to be an heir along with) my son, Isaac!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:11; GENESIS 17:18; MATTHEW 10:37"> Abraham was very distressed about the matter, because he was concerned about his son Ishmael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:18; ROMANS 9:7–8; GENESIS 17:19"> But God said to Abraham, “Do not be distressed about your son, Ishmael, and about your maidservant, Hagar. Do everything that Sarah tells you to do. Listen to her, because Isaac is the one who will be considered the ancestor of the descendants I promised to give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 16:10; GENESIS 17:20; GENESIS 21:18"> But I will also cause the son of your maidservant to be the ancestor of the people of a great nation &#91;MTY&#93;, because he is also your son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:10; PSALMS 119:60; GENESIS 16:7"> So Abraham got up early the next morning. He got some food ready, put water in a container, and gave them to Hagar. He put them in a bag on her shoulder and sent them away. They wandered in the desert near Beersheba town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 KINGS 3:9; PSALMS 63:1; JEREMIAH 14:3"> After they had drunk all the water in the container, she put her son under one of the bushes there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; GENESIS 29:11; 1 SAMUEL 24:16"> Then she went and sat nearby, about ◄as far as someone can shoot an arrow/100 meters away►, because she thought, “I cannot endure seeing my son die!” As she sat there, she began to cry loudly &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 16:11; PSALMS 50:15; EXODUS 3:7"> The boy was crying too. And God heard the boy crying. So he caused one of his angels to call out from heaven to Hagar, saying, “Hagar, are you worried about something? Do not be afraid, because God has heard the boy crying there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 21:13; GENESIS 16:10; GENESIS 17:20"> Help your son up, and hold his hand as you leave, because I will cause his descendants to become a great nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:31; 2 KINGS 6:17–20; Isaiah 35:5–6"> Then God showed her a well of water. So she went to the well and filled the container with water, and gave the boy a drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 28:15; GENESIS 39:21; GENESIS 16:12"> God helped the boy as he grew up. He lived in the desert and became a good ◄archer/man who hunts with bow and arrows►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 26:34–35; GENESIS 24:3–4; NUMBERS 12:16"> He lived in Paran Desert. While e was there, Hagar got a wife for him from Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOSHUA 3:7; GENESIS 26:26; GENESIS 26:28"> At that time, King Abimelech and Phicol, the commander of his army, said to Abraham, “It is clear that God helps you with everything that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:12; GENESIS 20:14; HEBREWS 6:16"> So now ◄solemnly promise/swear► to me here, as God is listening, that you will ◄not deceive/act fairly to► me and my children and my descendants, in return for my being kind to you. Be kind to me and to all the people here in the country where you are now living.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HEBREWS 6:16; GENESIS 14:13; ROMANS 12:18"> So Abraham promised to do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 26:15–22; GENESIS 29:8; PROVERBS 25:9"> Then Abraham complained to Abimelech about one of Abraham’s wells that Abimelech’s servants had seized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 13:7; 2 KINGS 5:20–24"> But Abimelech said, “I do not know who has done that. You did not tell me previously, and I did not hear about it until today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 14:22–23; Isaiah 32:8; ROMANS 1:31"> So Abraham brought some sheep and gave them to Abimelech, and the two of them made a ◄treaty/peace agreement►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Abraham separated seven female lambs from his flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:14; GENESIS 33:8; EXODUS 12:26"> Abimelech asked Abraham, “What are these seven female lambs that you have separated from the rest of your flock?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 31:52; JOSHUA 24:27; JOSHUA 22:27–28"> Abraham replied, “I want you truly to accept these female lambs from me &#91;SYN&#93;, so that it may be a ◄public witness/proof► that this well belongs to me because I dug it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 26:33; GENESIS 21:14; 1 KINGS 4:25"> So Abimelech accepted the animals, and as a result they called that place Beersheba, which means ‘Friendship Agreement Well’, because there the two of them made that agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 13:17; GENESIS 21:27; JUDGES 13:1"> After they made the agreement at Beersheba, Abimelech and his army commander, Phicol, left, and returned to the land of the Philistine people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 1:17; Isaiah 40:28; PSALMS 90:2"> Abraham planted a ◄tamarisk tree/kind of tree called esel► there, and he worshiped the eternal God there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 29:15; 1 PETER 2:11; HEBREWS 11:9"> Abraham lived in the land of the Philistine people-group for a long time.</span>
