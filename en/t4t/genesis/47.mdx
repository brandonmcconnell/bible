## Jacob blessed the king of Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 45:10; GENESIS 46:31; GENESIS 46:28"> Joseph chose five of his older brothers to go with him to talk to the king. He introduced his older brothers to the king, and then he said, “My father and my older brothers and younger brother have come from the Canaan region. They have brought all their sheep and goats and cattle and everything else that they own, and they are living now in Goshen region.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 46:33–34; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:10; GENESIS 4:2"> The king asked the brothers, “What work do you do?” They replied, “We are shepherds, just as our ancestors were.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 46:34; GENESIS 15:13; DEUTERONOMY 26:5"> They also said to him, “We have come here to live for a while in this land, because the famine is very severe in Canaan, and our animals have no ◄pasture/grass to eat► there. So now, please let us live in the Goshen region.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The king said to Joseph, “I am happy that your father and your older brothers and younger brother have come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 18:21; PROVERBS 22:29; GENESIS 47:11"> They can live wherever you want in the whole country of Egypt. Give your father and your brothers the best part of the land. They can live in Goshen. And if you know that any of them have any special ability to work with livestock, have them be in charge of my own livestock, too.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 47:10; 2 SAMUEL 8:10; 1 SAMUEL 2:20"> Then Joseph brought his father Jacob into the palace and introduced him to the king. Jacob asked God to bless the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the king asked Jacob, “How old are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 39:12; PSALMS 39:5; JOB 14:1"> Jacob replied, “I have been traveling around for 130 years. I have not lived as long as my ancestors, but my life has been full of troubles.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 47:7; PSALMS 129:8; HEBREWS 7:7"> Then Jacob again asked God to bless the king, and left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 47:6; EXODUS 1:11; EXODUS 12:37"> That is how Joseph enabled his father and older brothers and younger brother to start living in Egypt. As the king had commanded, he gave them property in the best part of the land, in Goshen, which is now called Rameses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 45:11; 1 TIMOTHY 5:8; GENESIS 47:1"> Joseph also provided food for all his father’s family. The amounts that he gave them were according to how many children each of them had.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Joseph arranged for people to buy grain from him during the famine

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ACTS 7:11; 1 KINGS 18:5; LAMENTATIONS 4:9"> There were no crops growing in the whole region, because the famine was very severe. The people of Egypt and Canaan &#91;MTY&#93; became weak because they did not have enough food to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 41:56; LUKE 16:10–12; LUKE 16:1–2"> Joseph collected all the money that the people in Egypt and Canaan paid for the grain they were buying from him, and he brought the money to the king’s palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 8:8; PSALMS 37:3; MATTHEW 6:11"> When the people of Egypt and Canaan had spent all their money for grain, they all kept coming to Joseph and saying, “Please give us some food! If you do not give us grain, we will die &#91;RHQ&#93;! We have used all our money to buy food, and we have no money left!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:5; DANIEL 6:5–7; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:32"> Joseph replied, “Since your money is all gone, bring me your livestock. If you do that, I will give you food in exchange for your livestock.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOB 2:4; EXODUS 9:3; Isaiah 31:1"> So they brought their livestock to Joseph. He gave them food in exchange for their horses, their sheep and goats, their cattle, and their donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 KINGS 6:26; JEREMIAH 38:9"> The next year they came to him again and said, “We cannot hide this from you: We have no more money, and now our livestock belongs to you. We have only our bodies and our land to give to you. We have nothing else left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:2–3; JOB 2:4; LAMENTATIONS 1:11"> ◄If you do not give us some food, we will die!/Do you want to watch us die?► &#91;RHQ&#93; If you do not give us seeds, our fields will become useless &#91;IDM&#93;. Buy us and our land in exchange for food. Then we will be the king’s slaves, and he will own the land. Give us seeds that we can plant and grow food, in order that we will not die, and in order that our land will not become like a desert.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So Joseph bought all the farms in Egypt for the king. The people of Egypt each sold their land to him because the famine was very severe, and they had no other way to get money to buy food. So all the farms became the king’s farms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 41:48"> As a result, Joseph caused all the people from one border of the country to the other to become the king’s slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 7:24; Nehemiah 13:10; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:10"> But he did not buy the priests’ land, because they received food from the king regularly, so the food that the king gave them was enough for them. That is the reason they did not sell their land to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 112:5; PSALMS 41:1; ECCLESIASTES 11:6"> Joseph said to the people who sold themselves and their land to him, “Listen to me! Today I have bought you and your land for the king. So here are seeds for you so that you can plant them in the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 41:34; PSALMS 41:1; PSALMS 112:5"> But when you harvest the crop, you must give one-fifth of the crop to the king. The rest of the crop you can keep, to be seed to plant in the fields, and to be food for you and your children and for everyone else in your household to eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 33:15; PROVERBS 11:26–27; GENESIS 18:3"> They replied, “You have saved our lives! We want you to be pleased with us. And we will be the king’s slaves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 47:22; EZEKIEL 7:24"> So Joseph made a law about all the land in Egypt, stating that one-fifth of the crops that are harvested belongs to the king. That law still exists. Only the land that belonged to the priests did not become the king’s land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 46:3; EXODUS 1:7; ACTS 7:17"> Jacob and his family started to live in Egypt, in the Goshen region. They acquired property there. Many children were born to them there. As a result, their population increased greatly.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jacob made Joseph promise to not bury him in Egypt after he died.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 90:10; GENESIS 47:8–9; GENESIS 37:2"> Jacob lived in Egypt 17 years. Altogether he lived 147 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:14; GENESIS 24:49; GENESIS 24:2"> When it was almost time for him to die, he summoned his son Joseph and said to him, “If I have pleased you, make a solemn promise that you will be kind to me and faithfully do what I am now asking you: When I die, do not bury me here in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 49:29–32; GENESIS 23:19; GENESIS 25:9"> Instead, take my body out of Egypt, and bury it in Canaan where my ancestors are buried.” Joseph replied, “I will do that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 KINGS 1:47; HEBREWS 11:21; GENESIS 24:3"> Jacob said, “◄Swear/Solemnly promise► to me that you will do it!” So Joseph swore to do it. Then Jacob turned over in bed, bowed his head, and worshiped God.</span>
</div>
</div>
