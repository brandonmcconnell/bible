## Jacob fled from Esau

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 JOHN 3:12–15; GENESIS 37:4; GENESIS 50:3–4"> So, because his father had given a blessing to Jacob, and not to him, Esau hated his younger brother. Esau thought to himself, “After my father dies and we finish mourning for him, I will kill Jacob!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="GENESIS 37:18–20; 1 SAMUEL 30:5; GENESIS 42:21–22"> But Rebekah found out what her older son, Esau, was thinking. <span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="GENESIS 27:8; GENESIS 11:31; GENESIS 27:13"> So now, my son, listen carefully to what I am telling you. Escape quickly and go and stay with my brother Laban, in Haran town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GENESIS 31:38; GENESIS 31:41"> Stay with him a while, until your older brother is no longer angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:21; GENESIS 4:8–16; GENESIS 9:5–6"> When he forgets what you did to him, I will send a message to you, to tell you to return from there.
