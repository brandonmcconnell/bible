## Joseph tricked his brothers by having his cup put in Benjamin’s sack

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 42:25; GENESIS 43:16; GENESIS 24:2"> When his brothers were ready to return home, Joseph said to the man who was in charge of things in his house, “Fill the sacks of those men with as much grain as they can carry on their donkeys. And put in the top of each man’s sack the silver that he paid for the grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 8:8; GENESIS 42:20; DEUTERONOMY 8:16"> Then put my silver cup in the top of the youngest brother’s sack, along with the silver that he paid for the grain.” So the servant did what Joseph told him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The next morning at dawn the men started on the way home with their donkeys.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOHN 10:32; PSALMS 35:12; PROVERBS 17:13"> When they had not gone far from the city, Joseph said to the servant in charge of things in his house, “Pursue those men immediately. When you catch up to them, say to them, ‘We did good things for you! Why have you paid us back by doing something bad to us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 30:27; LEVITICUS 19:26; 2 KINGS 21:6"> You have stolen the cup that my master drinks from &#91;RHQ&#93;! It is the cup that he uses to find out things that nobody knows! What you did was very wicked!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The servant left immediately and when he caught up with them, he told them what Joseph had told him to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 38:16–18; GENESIS 37:18–32; 2 KINGS 8:13"> But one of them replied to him, “Sir, why do you say such things? We are your servants, and we would never do anything like that!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 43:12; GENESIS 42:35; ROMANS 13:9"> We even brought back to you from Canaan land the silver that we found inside the tops of our sacks! So ◄we certainly would not steal silver or gold from your master’s house!/Why would we steal silver or gold from your master’s house?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 31:32; PSALMS 7:3–5; JOB 31:38–40"> If you discover that any of us has that cup, you can execute him, and the rest of us will become your slaves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 22:3; GENESIS 44:17; GENESIS 44:33"> The man replied, “Okay, I will do what you say. But the one who has the cup will not be executed. Instead, he will become my slave, and the rest of you may return home.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Each of the men quickly lowered his sack down from the donkey to the ground and opened it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 44:26–32; GENESIS 42:36–38; GENESIS 43:14"> Then the servant started to search for the cup in each sack. He started with the oldest brother’s sack and ended with the youngest one’s sack. And he found the cup in Benjamin’s sack and showed it to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 1:11; GENESIS 37:29–34; 2 SAMUEL 1:2"> The brothers tore their clothes because they were so dismayed. They loaded the sacks on the donkeys again and returned to the city.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Back in Egypt, Joseph said that only the one who stole his cup would be his slave

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 50:18; GENESIS 43:25; GENESIS 43:16"> When Judah and his older and younger brothers entered Joseph’s house, Joseph was still there. The servant told Joseph what had happened. Then the brothers threw themselves down on the ground in front of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 4:10; GENESIS 21:26; GENESIS 3:13"> He said to them, “Why did you do this? Do you not know that a man like me can find out things that nobody knows?” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 44:9; DANIEL 9:7; NUMBERS 32:23"> Judah replied, “Sir, what can we say? How can we prove that we ◄are innocent/did not steal the cup►? God has ◄paid us back/punished us► for the sins we committed many years ago. So now we will become your slaves—both we and the one in whose sack the cup was found.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:15; 2 SAMUEL 23:3; GENESIS 18:25"> But Joseph replied, “No, I could never do anything like that. Only the man in whose sack the cup was found will become my slave. The rest of you can return to your father peacefully.”</span>
</div>

## Judah pleaded that he be allowed to become Joseph’s slave instead of Benjamin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 32:22; GENESIS 41:40; GENESIS 18:30"> Then Judah came near to Joseph and said, “Sir, please let me say something to you. You are equal to the king himself, so you could command that I be executed; but do not be angry with me for speaking to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 43:7; GENESIS 43:29; GENESIS 42:7–10"> You asked us, ‘Is your father still living, and do you have another brother?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 37:3; GENESIS 42:38; LUKE 7:12"> We answered, ‘Our father is alive, but he is an old man. He has a young son who was born after our father became an old man. That son had an older brother, who is now dead. So the youngest son is the only one of his mother’s sons who is still alive, and his father loves him very much.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 42:15; GENESIS 42:20; AMOS 9:4"> Then you said to us, ‘The next time you come here, bring your younger brother down to me, so that I can see him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 44:30; GENESIS 42:38"> We said to you, ‘No, we cannot do that, because the boy cannot leave his father. If he leaves his father, his father will die from sorrow.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 43:5; GENESIS 43:3; GENESIS 42:15–20"> But you told us, ‘If your youngest brother does not come back with you, I will not let you see me again!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 42:29–34"> When we returned to our father, we told him what you said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 43:2; GENESIS 43:5"> Months later our father said, ‘Go back to Egypt and buy some more grain!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKE 11:7; GENESIS 43:4–5"> But we said, ‘We cannot go back by ourselves. We will go only if our youngest brother is with us. We will not be able to see the man who sells grain if our youngest brother is not with us.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 46:19; GENESIS 35:16–18; GENESIS 30:22–25"> Our father replied, ‘You know that my wife Rachel gave birth to two sons for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 37:33; GENESIS 37:13–14; GENESIS 42:36"> One of them disappeared, and I said, “A wild animal has surely torn him to pieces.” And I have not seen him since then.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 42:38; GENESIS 43:14; DEUTERONOMY 31:17"> I am an old gray-haired man. If you take this other one from me, too, and something harms him, you would cause me to die because of my sorrow.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 18:1; 2 SAMUEL 18:33; GENESIS 44:31"> “So please listen. My father will remain alive only if his youngest son remains alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 44:29; GENESIS 37:35; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:10"> If he sees that the boy is not with us when we return to him, he will die. We will cause our gray-haired father to die because of his sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 43:8–9; GENESIS 43:16"> I guaranteed/promised to my father that the boy would return safely. I told him, ‘You can require me to do what I am promising. If I do not bring him back to you, you can say forever that ◄I am to blame/it is my fault► for not bringing him back to you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 JOHN 3:16; EXODUS 32:32; HEBREWS 7:22"> “So, please let me remain here as your slave instead of my youngest brother, and let the boy return home with his other older brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Esther 8:6; PSALMS 116:3; PSALMS 119:143"> ◄I cannot return to my father if the boy is not with me!/How can I return to my father if the boy is not with me?► &#91;RHQ&#93; I do not want to see how miserable/sad my father would become!”</span>
