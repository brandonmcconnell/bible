## Jacob and his family returned to Bethel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 31:13; HOSEA 12:4; PSALMS 91:15"> Some time later God said to Jacob, “Go up to Bethel, and live there. Build an altar to worship me, God, who appeared to you when you were fleeing from your older brother Esau.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 19:14; EXODUS 19:10; EZEKIEL 18:31"> So Jacob said to his household and to all the others who were with him, “Get rid of the idols you brought from Mesopotamia. Also, bathe yourselves and put on clean clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; GENESIS 32:7; GENESIS 31:3"> Then we will get ready and go up to Bethel. There I will make an altar to worship God. He is the one who helped me at the time when I was greatly distressed and afraid, and he has been with me wherever I have gone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 9:6; DEUTERONOMY 7:25; EXODUS 32:20"> So they gave to Jacob all the idols that they had brought, and all their earrings. Jacob buried them in the ground under the big oak tree that was near Shechem town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 23:27; 2 CHRONICLES 17:10; JOSHUA 5:1"> As they prepared to leave there, God caused the people who lived in the cities around them to be extremely afraid of Jacob’s family &#91;PRS&#93;, so that they did not pursue and attack them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 28:19; GENESIS 28:22; GENESIS 12:8"> Jacob and all those who were with him came to Luz, which is now called Bethel, in the Canaan region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 35:3; GENESIS 35:1; GENESIS 28:13"> There he built an altar. He named the place El-Bethel, which means ‘God of Bethel’, because it was there that God revealed himself to Jacob when he was fleeing from his older brother Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 24:59; 1 SAMUEL 31:13; JUDGES 2:5"> Deborah, who had taken care of Isaac’s wife Rebekah when Rebekah was a small girl, was now very old. She died and was buried under an oak tree south of Bethel. So they named that place Allon-Bacuth, which means ‘oak of weeping’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 35:1; GENESIS 18:1; GENESIS 26:2"> After Jacob and his family returned from Paddan-Aram/Mesopotamia, while they were still at Bethel, God appeared to Jacob again and blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 17:5; GENESIS 32:27–28; GENESIS 17:15"> God said to him again, “Your name will no longer be Jacob. It will be Israel.” So Jacob was then called ‘Israel’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="GENESIS 17:1; GENESIS 9:1; GENESIS 22:17"> Then God said to him, “I am God Almighty. Produce many children. Your descendants will become many nations, and some of your descendants will be kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 12:7; GENESIS 28:13; GENESIS 26:3–4"> The land that I promised to give to your grandfather Abraham and your father Isaac, I will give to you. I will also give it to your descendants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 17:22; GENESIS 18:33; JUDGES 6:21"> When God finished talking there with Jacob, he left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 28:18–19; 1 SAMUEL 7:12; EXODUS 17:15"> Jacob set up a large stone at the place where God had talked with him. He poured some wine and some olive oil on it to dedicate it to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 28:19"> Jacob named that place Bethel, which means ‘house of God’, because God had spoken to him there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rachel died as Benjamin was born

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MICAH 5:2; GENESIS 48:7; 2 KINGS 5:19"> Jacob and his family left Bethel and traveled south toward Ephrath town. When they were still some distance from Ephrath, Rachel began to have severe childbirth pains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 30:24; 1 SAMUEL 4:19–21"> When her pain was the most severe, the ◄midwife/woman who helped her to give birth► said to Rachel, “Do not be afraid, because now you have given birth to another son!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 12:20; ACTS 7:59; LUKE 23:46"> But she was dying, and with her last breath she said, “Name him Benoni,” which means ‘son of my sorrow’, but his father named him Benjamin, which means ‘son of my right hand’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MICAH 5:2; GENESIS 48:7; RUTH 4:11"> After Rachel died, she was buried alongside the road to Ephrath, which is now called Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 10:2; GENESIS 35:14; 2 SAMUEL 18:17–18"> Jacob set up a large stone over her grave, and it is still there, showing where Rachel’s grave is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MICAH 4:8; LUKE 2:8"> Jacob, whose new name was Israel, continued traveling with his family, and he set up his tents on the south side of the watchtower at Eder town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 5:1; LEVITICUS 18:8; 2 SAMUEL 20:3"> While they were living in that area, Jacob’s son Reuben had sex &#91;EUP&#93; with Bilhah, one of his father’s ◄concubines/female slaves whom he had taken as a secondary wife►. Someone told Jacob about it, and it made him very angry. ◄I will now give you/Here is► a list of Jacob’s twelve sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 30:18–20; GENESIS 46:8–15; GENESIS 29:32–35"> The sons of Leah were Reuben, who was Jacob’s oldest son, then Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar and Zebulon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 30:22–24; GENESIS 46:19–22; GENESIS 35:16–18"> The sons of Rachel were Joseph and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="GENESIS 46:23–25; GENESIS 37:2; GENESIS 30:4–8"> The sons of Rachel’s female slave Bilhah were Dan and Naphtali.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 30:9–13; GENESIS 46:16–18; GENESIS 25:20"> The sons of Leah’s female slave Zilpah were Gad and Asher. All those sons of Jacob, except Benjamin, were born while he was living in Paddan-Aram/Mesopotamia.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Isaac died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="GENESIS 13:18; GENESIS 23:19; GENESIS 18:1"> Jacob had returned back home to see his father Isaac at Mamre, which is also named Kiriath-Arba, and which is now named Hebron. Isaac’s father Abraham had also lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 25:7; GENESIS 50:26; GENESIS 47:28"> Isaac lived until he was 180 years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="GENESIS 15:15; GENESIS 49:33; GENESIS 49:31"> He was very old when he died, joining his ancestors who had died previously. His sons Esau and Jacob buried his body.</span>
</div>
</div>
