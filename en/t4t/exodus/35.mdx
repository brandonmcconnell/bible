## Regulations for the day of rest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 34:32; EXODUS 31:1–11; ROMANS 2:13"> Moses/I gathered all the Israeli people together and said to them, “This is what Yahweh has commanded you to do:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:3; LUKE 13:14–15; EXODUS 20:9–10"> Each week you may work for six days, but on the seventh day you must rest. It is a sacred day, dedicated to Yahweh. Anyone who does any work on the seventh day must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 16:23; NUMBERS 15:32–36; EXODUS 12:16"> Do not even light a fire in your homes on days of rest.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gifts for constructing the Sacred Tent

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 25:1–2"> Moses/I also said to all the Israeli people, “This is also what Yahweh has commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 9:7; PSALMS 110:3; EXODUS 25:2–7"> Make offerings to Yahweh. Everyone who wants to should bring to Yahweh an offering. These are the things that they may offer: Gold, silver, bronze,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 26:7–14; EXODUS 26:31; EXODUS 28:15"> fine white linen, blue or purple or red cloth, cloth made from goats’ hair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> rams’ skins that are ◄tanned/dyed red►, fine leather made from goatskins, wood from acacia trees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 27:20; EXODUS 25:1–40; EXODUS 30:23"> oil for the lamps, spices to put in the olive oil for anointing and in the sweet-smelling incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 39:6–14; EXODUS 28:9; EXODUS 25:5"> valuable onyx stones or other valuable stones to fasten onto the priest’s sacred apron and put on his sacred chest pouch.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 31:1–6; EXODUS 36:1–4"> “All the skilled workers among you should come and make all the things that Yahweh has commanded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 26:1–37; EXODUS 36:8–34; EXODUS 31:7–9"> The Sacred Tent and its covering, its fasteners and its frames, its crossbars, its posts, its bases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 25:10–22; EXODUS 26:31–33; EXODUS 37:1–9"> the sacred chest with its poles and its lid, the curtain that will separate the Holy Place from the Very Holy Place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 25:23–30; LEVITICUS 24:5–6; EXODUS 37:10–16"> the table with the poles for carrying it and all the things that will be used with the table, the sacred bread that will be offered to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 25:31–39; MATTHEW 5:14–15; EXODUS 37:17–24"> the lampstand for the lamps with all the things that will be used to take care of them, the oil for the lamps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 30:1–10; EXODUS 30:22–38; EXODUS 37:25–28"> the altar for burning incense, and the poles for carrying that altar, the oil for anointing and the sweet-smelling incense, the curtain for the entrance of the Sacred Tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 27:1–8; EXODUS 30:18–21; EXODUS 38:1–8"> the altar for offering sacrifices that will be completely burned and its bronze grating, the poles for carrying that altar and all the things that will be used with it, the washbasin and its base,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:2; EXODUS 38:9–20; EXODUS 27:9–19"> the curtains to surround the courtyard and the posts and bases for the posts from which to hang the curtains, the curtain for the entrance to the courtyard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 27:19"> the pegs and ropes for the Sacred Tent and for the courtyard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 31:10; NUMBERS 4:5–15; EXODUS 28:1–43"> and the beautiful clothes that Aaron and his sons are to wear when they do their work in the Holy Place.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then all the Israeli people returned to their tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 25:2; EXODUS 35:26; 1 CHRONICLES 29:14"> Everyone who wanted to brought an offering to Yahweh. They brought some of the things that would be used to make the Sacred Tent and all the other items that would be used in the rituals, and the materials to make the sacred clothes for the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–72; MATTHEW 2:11; Ezra 2:68–69"> All the men and women who wanted to brought gold ornaments, earrings, rings, necklaces, and many other kinds of things made of gold, and they dedicated them to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 29:8; EXODUS 25:2–7; EXODUS 35:6–10"> And many &#91;HYP&#93; people who had blue, purple, or red cloth or fine white linen or cloth made from goats’ hair or rams’ skins that were ◄tanned/dyed red► or leather made from goatskins brought some of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 8:12"> All those who had silver or bronze brought them as offerings to Yahweh. All those who had some acacia wood that could be used for any of the work brought it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 16:12; LUKE 8:2–3; PROVERBS 14:1"> All the women who were skilled to make cloth brought fine linen thread and blue, purple, or red yarn/thread that they had made/spun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 35:21; EXODUS 36:8; EXODUS 35:29"> And all the women who wanted to made/spun thread from goats’ hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezra 2:68; 1 CHRONICLES 29:6; EXODUS 35:9"> All the leaders brought valuable onyx stones and other valuable stones to be fastened to Aaron’s sacred apron and his sacred chest pouch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 30:23–38; EXODUS 35:8"> They also brought spices to put in the sweet-smelling incense, and they brought olive oil for the lamps and for the oil for anointing and for putting in the sweet-smelling incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 9:7; EXODUS 35:21–22; EXODUS 35:4"> All the Israeli men and women who wanted to brought these things to offer them to Yahweh, for doing the work that he had commanded Moses/me to do.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses’ instructions regarding Bezalel and Oholiab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 31:1–6; JAMES 1:17; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:11"> Moses/I said to the Israeli people, “Listen carefully. Yahweh has chosen Bezalel, the son of Uri and grandson of Hur, from the tribe of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JAMES 1:17; Isaiah 11:2–5; Isaiah 61:1–3"> Yahweh has enabled his Spirit to completely control Bezalel and has given him ability and enabled him to know how to do very skilled work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> He can engrave skillful designs in gold, silver, and bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> He can cut ◄jewels/valuable stones► and enclose them in tiny gold frames. He can carve things from wood and do other skilled work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 31:6; 2 CHRONICLES 2:14; Ezra 7:27"> Yahweh has also given to him and to Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, the ability to teach their skills to others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="EXODUS 35:31; 1 KINGS 7:14; Isaiah 28:26"> He has given to them the ability to do all kinds of work that is done by craftsmen—those who create artistic things, those who make fine white linen, those who embroider designs using blue or purple or red yarn/thread, and those who make other cloth. They are able to do many &#91;HYP&#93; kinds of skillful work.</span>
</div>
</div>
