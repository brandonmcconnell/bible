## They made the curtains and coverings for the Sacred Tent

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 KINGS 6:23; EXODUS 26:1–37; EZEKIEL 1:5–28"> All the most skilled men among the workmen made the Sacred Tent. They made it from ten strips of fine linen, and carefully embroidered it using blue, purple, and red yarn/thread to make figures that resembled the winged creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Each strip was ◄14 yards/twelve meters► long and ◄2 yards/1.8 meters► wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:27; EXODUS 26:3; PSALMS 122:3"> They sewed five strips together to make one set, and they sewed the other five strips together to make the other set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 26:4"> For each set, they made loops of blue cloth and fastened them on the outer edge of the strip, at the end of each set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 26:5; EXODUS 26:10"> They put 50 loops on the edge of the first set, and 50 loops on the edge of the second set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 PETER 2:4–5; EPHESIANS 2:20–22; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:20"> They made 50 gold clasps/fasteners, to fasten both of the sets together. In that way, the inside of the Sacred Tent was as though it was one piece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 26:7–13"> They made a cover for the Sacred Tent from eleven pieces of cloth made from goats’ hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Each piece of cloth was ◄15 yards/13.5 meters► long and ◄2 yards/1.8 meters► wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> They sewed five of these pieces of cloth together to make one set, and they sewed the other six pieces of cloth together to make another set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They made 100 loops of blue cloth. They fastened 50 of them to the outer edge of the one set and they fastened 50 to the outer edge of the other set.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> They made 50 bronze clasps/fasteners and joined the two sets together with them. In that way it formed one cover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 26:14"> They made two more covers for the Sacred Tent. They made one from rams’ skins that had been ◄tanned/dyed red►, and they made the top cover from goatskin leather.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 25:5; NUMBERS 25:1; EXODUS 40:18–19"> They made 48 frames from acacia wood and set them up to support the covers for the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Each frame was ◄15 feet/4.5 meters► long and ◄27 in./66 cm.► wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> They made two projections at the bottom of each frame. These were for fastening the frames to the bases underneath them. Each frame had these projections.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The skilled workmen made twenty frames for the south side of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> They made 40 silver bases to go underneath them. Two bases went under each frame. The projections on each frame fit into these bases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Similarly, they made 20 frames for the north side of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> They made 40 silver bases for them also, with two bases for under each frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 26:22; EXODUS 26:27"> For the rear of the Sacred Tent, on the west side, they made six frames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> They also made two extra frames, one for each corner of the rear of the Sacred Tent, to provide extra support.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="ACTS 4:32; EPHESIANS 3:18–19; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:10"> The two corner frames were joined from the bottom to the top (OR, joined at both the bottom and the top). At the top of each of the two corner frames they fastened a gold ring for holding the crossbar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 26:25"> In that way, for the rear of the Sacred Tent there were eight frames, and there were 16 bases, two bases under each frame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 26:26–29; EXODUS 25:28; EXODUS 30:5"> The workmen made 15 crossbars from acacia wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 26:26"> Five of them were for the frames on the north side of the Sacred Tent, five for the south side, and five for the frames at the rear of the Sacred Tent, the west side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> The crossbars on the north, south, and west sides of the Sacred Tent were fastened to the middle of the frames. The two long crossbars extended from one end of the Sacred Tent to the other, and the crossbar on the west side extended from one side of the Sacred Tent to the other side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> The workmen covered the frames with gold and fastened gold rings to the poles. The crossbars were put into the rings. The crossbars were also covered with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 27:51; HEBREWS 10:20; EXODUS 40:21"> They made a curtain from fine white linen. Skilled craftsmen embroidered it with blue, purple, and red yarn/thread, making designs that resembled the winged creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:18"> They suspended/hung the curtain from four posts that were made from acacia wood and covered with gold. They set each post in a silver base.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="EXODUS 26:36–37; EXODUS 40:28"> They made a curtain to cover the entrance of the Sacred Tent. They made it from fine linen, and a skilled weaver embroidered it with blue, purple, and red yarn/thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="EXODUS 27:10"> To support this curtain, they also made five posts from acacia wood and fastened gold clasps/fasteners to them. They completely covered the posts with gold. They also made a bronze base for each of those posts.</span>
