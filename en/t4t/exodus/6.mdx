## Yahweh promised to rescue them

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 12:33; EXODUS 12:31; EXODUS 3:19–20"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Now you will see what I will do to the king and his people. I will powerfully &#91;MTY&#93; compel him to let my people go. In fact, by my power &#91;MTY&#93; I will force him to expel them from his land!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:24; MALACHI 3:6; ACTS 17:24–25"> God also said to Moses/me, “I am Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 83:18; PSALMS 68:4; GENESIS 17:1"> I am the one who appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and told them that I was God Almighty, but I did not tell them that my name was Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 28:4; GENESIS 15:18; GENESIS 17:7–8"> I also made my solemn agreement with them, promising to give them the Canaan region. That was the area in which they were living as foreigners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 2:24; PSALMS 106:44–45; Isaiah 63:9"> Furthermore, I have heard the Israeli people as they were groaning because of the hard work that the Egyptians forced them to do as their slaves. I have thought about that solemn promise that I made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 26:8; 1 CHRONICLES 17:21; EXODUS 7:4"> So tell the Israeli people that I said this: ‘I am Yahweh. I will free you from the burdens of heavy work that the Egyptians have given you. I will free you from being their slaves. With my great power &#91;MTY&#93; and by punishing them &#91;MTY&#93; very severely, I will save you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:6; DEUTERONOMY 29:13; DEUTERONOMY 4:20"> I will cause you to be my own people, and I will be your God, the one you worship. You will truly know that I, Yahweh your God, am the one who has freed you from the burdens of work as slaves of the Egyptians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 26:3; GENESIS 15:18; EZEKIEL 20:42"> And I will bring you to the land that I solemnly promised to give to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob. You will possess it forever. I, Yahweh, am promising this.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 5:21; JOB 21:4; EXODUS 14:12"> Moses/I told that to the Israeli people, but they did not believe what he/I said. They were very discouraged &#91;IDM&#93; because of the hard work that they had been forced to do as slaves.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Aaron and Moses gave God’s message to the king again

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 6:29; EXODUS 5:23; EXODUS 3:10"> “Go and tell the king of Egypt again that he must allow the Israeli people to leave his land!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 4:10; JEREMIAH 1:6; EXODUS 6:30"> But Moses/I said to Yahweh, “Listen to what I am saying. Even the Israeli people have not paid attention to what I told them. But I am a poor speaker. &#91;MET, MTY&#93; So ◄why should the king pay attention to what I tell him?/the king will certainly not pay attention to what I tell him!►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:23; 1 TIMOTHY 6:13; PSALMS 91:11"> But Yahweh spoke to Aaron and Moses/me and commanded them/us, “Tell the Israeli people and the king of Egypt that I have commissioned you two to lead the Israeli people out of Egypt.”</span>
</div>

## The ancestors of Moses and Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 46:9; 1 CHRONICLES 5:3; NUMBERS 26:5–7"> ◄Here is/I will now give you► a list of the ancestors of Moses/me and Aaron: The sons of Reuben, who was Jacob’s oldest son, were Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. They were ancestors of the clans that have those same names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 4:24; GENESIS 46:10; NUMBERS 26:12–13"> The sons of Simeon were Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul. Shaul’s mother was a woman from the Canaan people-group. Those men also were ancestors of clans that have those same names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:17; GENESIS 46:11; 1 CHRONICLES 6:1"> These are the names of the sons of Levi, in the order in which they were born: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. Levi was 137 years old when he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:17; NUMBERS 3:18; 1 CHRONICLES 23:7"> The sons of Gershon were Libni and Shimei. They were ancestors of clans that have those names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:18; 1 CHRONICLES 6:2; NUMBERS 3:19"> The sons of Kohath were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. Kohath was 133 years old when he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:20; 1 CHRONICLES 23:21; 1 CHRONICLES 6:19"> The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi. All of those people whose names I have listed belonged to tribes/clans descended from Levi, in the order in which they were born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:59; EXODUS 2:1–2; EXODUS 6:18"> Amram married his father’s sister, Jochebed. She was the mother of Aaron and Moses/me. Amram was 137 years old when he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:1; 1 CHRONICLES 6:37–38; NUMBERS 16:32"> The sons of Izhar were Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:4; NUMBERS 3:30; Nehemiah 3:20"> The sons of Uzziel were Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="RUTH 4:19–20; NUMBERS 2:3; NUMBERS 1:7"> Aaron married Elisheba. She was the daughter of Amminadab and sister of Nahshon. Elisheba gave birth to four sons: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:22–23; NUMBERS 16:1; 1 SAMUEL 1:1"> The sons of Korah were Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph. They were the ancestors of the Korah people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:33; NUMBERS 25:7–13; JOSHUA 22:31–32"> Aaron’s son Eleazar married one of the daughters of Putiel, and she gave birth to Phinehas. That ends the list of the families and clans that were descended from Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 12:51; EXODUS 12:17; EXODUS 7:4"> Aaron and Moses/I were the ones to whom Yahweh said, “Lead all the families and clans of the Israeli people out of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 6:26; EXODUS 7:10; PSALMS 77:20"> They/We were the ones who spoke to the king of Egypt about bringing the Israeli people out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> On the day that Yahweh spoke to Moses/me in Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EXODUS 6:11; EXODUS 7:2; EXODUS 6:2"> he said, “I am Yahweh. You must tell the king everything that I say to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 6:12; EXODUS 4:10; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:16–17"> But Moses/I said to Yahweh, “Listen to me. I am not a good speaker. &#91;MET, MTY&#93; So ◄the king will certainly not pay attention to what I say/why should the king pay attention to what I tell him?►!” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
</div>
