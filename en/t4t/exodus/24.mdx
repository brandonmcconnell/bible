## Yahweh’s agreement is confirmed

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 24:15; EXODUS 24:13; HEBREWS 10:21–22"> Moses, I will allow you alone to come near to me. The others must not come near, and the rest of the people must not come up the mountain.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GENESIS 28:18; DEUTERONOMY 31:9; JOSHUA 4:8–9"> Then Moses/I wrote down everything that Yahweh had commanded. Early the next morning Moses/I built a stone altar. He/I also set up twelve stones, one for each of the Israeli tribes.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 PETER 1:19; EXODUS 24:8; HEBREWS 9:18"> Moses/I took half of the blood of the animals that were slaughtered and put it in bowls. The other half of the blood he/I threw/splashed against the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 31:11–13; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:27; EXODUS 24:3–4"> Then he/I took the scroll on which he/I had written everything that Yahweh had commanded in the agreement that he had made, and he/I read it aloud, while all the people were listening. Then all the people said, “We will do all that Yahweh has told us to do. We will obey everything.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:4–5; EXODUS 24:6; EZEKIEL 36:25"> Then Moses/I took the blood that was in the bowls and threw/splashed it on the people. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 24:1"> Then Moses/I, along with Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and the 70 Israeli elders/leaders, went up the mountain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 1:18; EZEKIEL 10:1; GENESIS 32:30"> and they/we saw God, the one whom the Israeli people worship. Under his feet was something like a pavement made of expensive blue stones called sapphires. They were as clear as the sky is when there are no clouds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 15:23–24; EXODUS 18:12; GENESIS 31:54"> God did not harm &#91;MTY&#93; those Israeli elders/leaders because of their having seen him. They saw God, and they ate and drank together! Then we all went back down the mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:22; EXODUS 31:18; EXODUS 32:15–16"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Come up to me again on top of this mountain. While you are here, I will give you two stone slabs on which I have written all the laws &#91;DOU&#93; that I have given to you to instruct/teach the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 33:11; NUMBERS 11:28; EXODUS 3:1"> So Moses/I told the elders, “Stay here with the other people until we return! Do not forget that Aaron and Hur will be with you. So if anyone has a dispute while I am gone, he can go to those two men.” Then Moses/I went with his/my servant Joshua part of the way up the mountain that was dedicated to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 19:9; MATTHEW 17:5; EXODUS 19:16"> Then Moses/I went the rest of the way up the mountain. A cloud covered the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 16:10; NUMBERS 16:42; EZEKIEL 1:28"> The ◄glory/brilliant light► of Yahweh came down on the mountain and covered it for six days. On the seventh day, Yahweh called to Moses/me from the middle of the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:29; DEUTERONOMY 4:24; EXODUS 3:2"> When the Israeli people looked at the top of the mountain, the glory of Yahweh was like a big fire that was burning there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:9; EXODUS 34:28; DEUTERONOMY 10:10"> Moses/I went into the cloud on top of the mountain. He/I was there for 40 days and nights.</span>
