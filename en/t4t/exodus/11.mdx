## Yahweh declared that all the oldest sons of the Egyptians would die, and then the king’s officials would plead for the Israeli people to leave Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 15:14; LEVITICUS 26:21; EXODUS 3:20"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me, “I will cause one more disaster to strike the king of Egypt and all his people &#91;MTY&#93;. After that, he will let you leave. In fact, he will expel you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 3:22; EXODUS 12:35–36; Haggai 2:8"> So now speak to all the Israeli people. Tell them to ask all their Egyptian neighbors, both men and women, to give them some silver and gold jewelry.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 3:21; EXODUS 12:36; REVELATION 3:9"> Yahweh made the Egyptians highly respect the Israeli people. In particular, the Egyptian officials and all the rest of the people considered Moses/me to be a very great man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 12:29; PSALMS 60:10; JOB 34:20"> Then Moses/I went to the king and said, “This is what Yahweh says: ‘About midnight tonight I will go throughout Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 78:51; EXODUS 12:12; PSALMS 105:36"> and I will cause all the firstborn/oldest sons to die. That will include your oldest son, the oldest sons of the slave women who grind grain, and the oldest sons of everyone else. I will also kill the oldest males of the Egyptians’ livestock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 12:30; AMOS 5:17; ZEPHANIAH 1:10"> When that happens, people all over Egypt will wail loudly. They have never wailed like that before, and they will never wail like that again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:21; EXODUS 10:23; EXODUS 8:22"> But among the Israeli people it will be so quiet that not even a dog will bark! Then you will know for sure that I, Yahweh, distinguish how I act toward the Egyptians and how I act toward the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 12:31–33; EZEKIEL 3:14; DANIEL 3:19"> Then all these officials of yours will come and bow down before me and will say, “Please get out of Egypt, you and all the Israeli people!”’ After that, I will leave Egypt!” After Moses/I said that, he/I very angrily left the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 7:3–4; EXODUS 3:19; EXODUS 10:1"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me, “The king will not pay any attention to what you say. The result will be that I will perform more plagues in the land of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 4:21; EXODUS 10:20; EXODUS 10:27"> Aaron and Moses/I performed all these miracles in front of the king, but Yahweh made the king stubborn, and he did not let the Israeli people leave his country.</span>
</div>
