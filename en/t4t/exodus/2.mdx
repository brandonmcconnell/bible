## After Moses was born he was later adopted by the king’s daughter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:59; 1 CHRONICLES 23:12–14; EXODUS 6:16–20"> ◄There was a man who/My father► was descended from Jacob’s son Levi. He married a woman who was also descended from Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:23; ACTS 7:20; PSALMS 112:5"> She became pregnant and gave birth to ◄a baby boy/me►. When she saw that he/I was a good-looking baby, she hid him/me for three months, because she was not willing to do what the king commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 1:22; ACTS 7:19; GENESIS 6:14"> When she was unable to ◄hide him/me/keep it a secret► any longer, she got a basket made from tall reeds. She covered the basket with tar ◄to make it waterproof/so water could not get in►. Then she put him/me in the basket and put the basket in the water in the middle of the tall grass at the edge of the Nile River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:59; EXODUS 15:20; MICAH 6:4"> His/My older sister was standing not far away, watching to see what would happen to him/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 7:21; PSALMS 12:5; PSALMS 9:9"> Soon the king’s daughter went down to the river to bathe. Her female servants were walking along the riverbank. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:50; 1 PETER 3:8; ACTS 7:21"> When the servant brought the basket to her, she opened it, and was surprised to see ◄a baby that was/me►, crying. She felt sorry for him/me, and said, “This must be one of the Hebrews’ babies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 2:4; NUMBERS 26:59; NUMBERS 12:1"> Then ◄the baby’s/my► older sister approached the king’s daughter and said, “Do you want me to go and find someone from among the Hebrew women who will be able to nurse the baby for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; PSALMS 27:10; EXODUS 6:20"> The king’s daughter said to her, “Yes, go and find one.” So the girl went and summoned ◄the baby’s/my► mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> The king’s daughter said to her, “Please take this baby and nurse him for me. I will pay you for doing that.” So ◄the woman/my mother► took him/me and nursed him/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:24; GENESIS 16:11; 1 SAMUEL 1:20"> ◄A few years later/when ◄the child/I► grew older►, she brought him/me to the king’s daughter. ## Moses, after murdering an Egyptian, escaped to Midian and later married Zipporah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:24–26; ACTS 7:22–24; EXODUS 1:11"> One day, after Moses/I had grown up, he/I went out of the palace area to see his/my people, the Hebrews. He/I saw how they were being forced to work very hard. He/I also saw an Egyptian man beating one of his/my Hebrew people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ACTS 7:24–26"> He/I looked around to see if anyone was watching. Seeing no one, he/I killed the Egyptian man and buried his body in the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ACTS 7:26–28; 1 CORINTHIANS 6:7–8"> The next day he/I returned to the same place. <span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 12:14; MATTHEW 21:23; GENESIS 19:9"> The man replied, “◄Who made you our ruler and judge?/No one made you our ruler and judge!► &#91;RHQ&#93; You have no right to interfere with us Are you going to kill me just like you killed that Egyptian man yesterday?” Then Moses/I was afraid, because he/I thought, “Since that man knows what I did, surely other people know, too.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 7:29; HEBREWS 11:27; GENESIS 24:11"> And that was correct. The king heard about what he/I had done to that Egyptian. <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="GENESIS 24:11; EXODUS 3:1; 1 SAMUEL 9:11"> The man who was the ◄priest/one who offered the people’s gifts to God► for the Midian people, whose name was Jethro, had seven daughters. One day as Moses/I sat down beside a well, those girls came to the well and got water, and filled the troughs in order to give water to their father’s flock of sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 29:10; GENESIS 21:25; GENESIS 26:15–22"> Some ◄shepherds/men who took care of other sheep► came and started to chase away the girls. But Moses/I helped/rescued the girls, and got water for their sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 3:1; NUMBERS 10:29; EXODUS 4:18"> When the girls returned to their father Jethro, whose other name is Reuel, he asked them, “How is it that you were able to give water to the sheep and come home so quickly today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 50:11; GENESIS 29:10"> They replied, “A man from Egypt kept &#91;MTY&#93; other shepherds from sending us away. He also got water for us from the well and gave water to the flock of sheep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 31:54; GENESIS 43:25; HEBREWS 13:2"> He said to his daughters, “Where is he? ◄Why did you leave him out there?/You should not leave him out there!► &#91;RHQ&#93; Invite him in, so he can have something to eat &#91;MTY&#93;!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="GENESIS 31:38–40; HEBREWS 13:5; HEBREWS 11:25"> So they did, and Moses/I accepted and ate with them. And Moses/I decided to live there. Later Jethro gave him/me his daughter Zipporah to be his/my wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:13–14; ACTS 7:29; EXODUS 22:21"> Later she gave birth to a son, and Moses/I named him Gershom, which sounds like the Hebrew words that mean ‘foreigner’, because he/I said, “I am living as a foreigner in this land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="GENESIS 18:20–21; JAMES 5:4; EXODUS 3:7–9"> Many years later the king of Egypt died. The Israeli people in Egypt were still groaning because of the hard work they had to do as slaves. They called out for someone to help them, and God heard them call out &#91;PRS&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 26:3; PSALMS 105:42; EXODUS 6:5"> He heard them groaning. And he ◄thought about/did not forget► that he had solemnly promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob to bless their descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 4:31; PSALMS 55:22; EXODUS 3:7–8"> God saw how the Israeli people were being badly treated, and he was concerned about them.</span>
