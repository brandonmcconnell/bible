## They set up the Sacred Tent

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 26:30; EXODUS 13:4; NUMBERS 7:1"> “On the first day of the first month next year, tell the people to set up the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 35:12; NUMBERS 4:5; REVELATION 11:19"> Put inside it the sacred chest that contains the stone slabs on which are engraved the Ten Commandments, and hang the curtain in front of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:8; EXODUS 40:22–25; EXODUS 37:10–24"> Bring the table into the Sacred Tent, and place on it all the things that are to be used with it. Bring in the lampstand and fasten the lamps to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:19–22; HEBREWS 9:24; 1 JOHN 2:1–2"> Put the gold altar for burning incense in front of the sacred chest, and set up the curtain at the entrance of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 40:29; 1 JOHN 4:9–10; HEBREWS 13:10"> Put the altar for burning sacrifices in front of the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:1; 1 JOHN 1:7; PSALMS 26:6"> Put the washbasin between the Sacred Tent and the altar, and fill it with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 40:33; EPHESIANS 4:11–12; MATTHEW 16:18"> Hang the curtains that will surround the courtyard, and hang the curtain at its entrance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:10; NUMBERS 7:1; MATTHEW 3:16"> Then take the oil for anointing, and anoint the Sacred Tent and everything that is in it, to ◄set it apart/dedicate it► to me. Then it will be holy/sacred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 29:36–37; LUKE 1:35; Isaiah 61:1"> Also anoint the altar for offering sacrifices that will be completely burned, and all the things that will be used with it, and set it apart. Then it also will be holy/sacred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Also anoint the washbasin and its base, to set it apart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:1–13; LEVITICUS 9:1–24; EXODUS 29:1–35"> Then bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Sacred Tent, and wash them ritually with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 28:41; 1 JOHN 2:20; 1 JOHN 2:27"> Then put on Aaron his sacred clothes to ◄set him apart/dedicate him►, in order that he can serve me as a priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; ROMANS 8:30; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:30"> Also bring his sons there. Put their sacred tunics/gowns on them</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 25:13; HEBREWS 5:1–14; HEBREWS 8:1–10"> and anoint them just as you anointed their father, in order that they also may serve me by working as priests. By anointing them and their descendants, you will cause them and their descendants to be priests throughout all coming generations.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:1; EXODUS 40:17–32; DEUTERONOMY 12:32"> Moses/I did all these things exactly as Yahweh had commanded him/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:1; EXODUS 40:1–2; NUMBERS 9:1"> On the first day of the first month of the next year (OR, second year after they/we left Egypt), the people set up the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:27–28; 1 PETER 1:5; LEVITICUS 26:11"> Following Moses’/my instructions, they set up the Sacred Tent and its bases, set up the frames, attached the crossbars, and put up the posts for the curtains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 26:1–14; EXODUS 36:8–19"> Then they spread out the coverings over the Sacred Tent, exactly as Yahweh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 16:34; DEUTERONOMY 10:5; HEBREWS 10:19–21"> Then he/I took the two stone slabs on which the commandments were written and put them in the sacred chest. He/I put the carrying poles in the rings on the chest and put the lid on top of the chest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 26:33; EXODUS 40:3; EXODUS 35:12"> Then he/I took the chest into the Very Holy Place inside the Sacred Tent and hung the curtain. In that way, he/I prevented the people who were outside from seeing the chest. He/I did all this exactly as Yahweh had commanded him/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 26:35; JOHN 6:53–57; EPHESIANS 3:8"> He/I set the table inside the Sacred Tent, on the north side, outside the curtain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 25:30; EXODUS 40:4; HEBREWS 9:2"> He/I placed on the table the bread that was offered to Yahweh, exactly as Yahweh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 26:35; REVELATION 2:5; JOHN 1:1"> He/I set the lampstand inside the Sacred Tent, on the south side, opposite the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 40:4; EXODUS 25:37; REVELATION 4:5"> Then he/I fastened the lamps to the lampstand in Yahweh’s presence, exactly as Yahweh had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 40:5; HEBREWS 7:25; JOHN 11:42"> He/I set the golden altar for burning incense inside the Sacred Tent, in front of the curtain that separated the Holy Place from the Very Holy Place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EXODUS 30:7"> and he/I burned some sweet-smelling incense on it, exactly as Yahweh had commanded him/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 40:5; HEBREWS 10:19–20; EPHESIANS 2:18"> He/I hung the curtain at the entrance to the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EXODUS 40:6; HEBREWS 13:5–6; HEBREWS 13:10"> At the entrance to the Sacred Tent, he/I set the altar for offering sacrifices that were to be burned completely. Then he/I offered on it the meat that was to be burned completely and the grain offering, exactly as Yahweh had commanded him/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EXODUS 40:7; EXODUS 38:8; EZEKIEL 36:25"> He/I set the washbasin between the Sacred Tent and the bronze altar, and he/I filled the washbasin with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 30:19–20; PSALMS 26:6; JOHN 13:10"> Every time Moses/I and Aaron and his sons went into the Sacred Tent or went to the altar, they/we washed their/our hands and feet ritually, exactly as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 40:8; EXODUS 27:9–16; JOHN 17:4"> Following Moses’/my instructions, they hung the curtains that surrounded the courtyard and the altar, and they hung the curtain at the entrance to the courtyard. So Moses/I finished that work.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh’s brilliant light filled the Sacred Tent

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="REVELATION 15:8; NUMBERS 9:15–23; 2 CHRONICLES 7:2"> Then the tall bright cloud covered the Sacred Tent, and Yahweh’s ◄glory/brilliant light► filled the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:11; LEVITICUS 16:2; 2 CHRONICLES 7:2"> Because that light was very bright, Moses/I was not able to enter the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:19; NUMBERS 9:17; EXODUS 13:21–22"> From that day, whenever the Israeli people wanted to move to another place, they went only when the bright cloud rose from above the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="NUMBERS 9:19–22; PSALMS 31:15"> If the cloud did not rise, they stayed where they were and did not go on until the cloud rose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="PSALMS 78:14; NUMBERS 9:15; EXODUS 13:21"> Wherever they traveled, the bright cloud that indicated Yahweh’s presence was above the Sacred Tent during the day, and a bright fire was inside the cloud at night, with the result that all the Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93; could see it at any time.</span>
</div>
