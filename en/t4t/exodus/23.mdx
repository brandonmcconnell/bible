## Treat people and animals fairly

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="PSALMS 101:5; PROVERBS 19:5; DEUTERONOMY 19:16–21"> “Do not tell to others ◄false rumors/untrue reports► about other people. Do not help someone who is guilty by ◄testifying falsely/lying► about what happened.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 1:10–11; PROVERBS 1:15; LUKE 23:23–24"> Do not join a group of people who are planning to do something evil. Do not testify the way the crowd wants you to, if that will prevent the judge from deciding the case justly/fairly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JAMES 3:17; LEVITICUS 19:15; PSALMS 82:2–3"> When a poor person is on trial, do not testify in his favor just because he is poor and you feel sorry for him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:1–4; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:15; MATTHEW 5:44"> If you see someone’s bull or donkey when it is wandering away loose, take it back to its owner, even if the owner is your enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 22:4"> If you see someone’s donkey that has fallen down because of its heavy load, help the owner to get the donkey up again, even if he is someone who hates you. Do not just walk away without helping him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MALACHI 3:5; JEREMIAH 5:28; LEVITICUS 19:15"> Decide the cases of poor people who are on trial as fairly as you decide the cases of other people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:25; EXODUS 34:7; EXODUS 23:1"> Do not accuse people falsely. Do not decide that innocent and righteous &#91;DOU&#93; people should be executed, because I will ◄punish/not forgive► people who do such an evil thing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:19; PROVERBS 17:23; Isaiah 5:23"> Do not accept money that is a bribe, because officials who accept bribes are not able to &#91;MTY&#93; decide what is right to do, and they do not allow innocent people to be treated fairly.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 27:19; EXODUS 22:21; DEUTERONOMY 24:14–18"> Do not mistreat foreigners who live among you. You know how people often treat foreigners, because the Egyptians did not treat you well when you were foreigners there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:3–4"> Plant seeds in your ground and gather the harvest for six years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:34–35; LEVITICUS 25:2–7; LEVITICUS 25:11–12"> But during the seventh year you must not plant anything. If things grow without your planting seeds, allow poor people to harvest and eat the crops. If there are still crops ◄left over/that they do not harvest►, allow wild animals to eat them. Do the same thing with your grapevines/vineyards and your olive trees.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 13:14; EXODUS 34:21; EXODUS 35:3"> You may work for six days each week, but on the seventh day you must rest and not work. And on the seventh day you must allow your work animals and your slaves and the foreigners who live among you also to rest and be refreshed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:7; ZECHARIAH 13:2; 1 TIMOTHY 4:16"> Make certain that you obey everything that I have commanded you to do. Do not pray to &#91;MTY&#93; other gods. Do not even mention their names.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rules about three annual festivals

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:16; EXODUS 34:22–24; LEVITICUS 23:34"> “Every year you must celebrate three festivals to honor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="LUKE 22:7; EXODUS 34:20; NUMBERS 28:16–25"> The first one is the Festival of Bread That Has No Yeast. Celebrate it in the month that is named Abib. That is the month in which you left Egypt. Celebrate it in the way that I commanded you. And always bring &#91;LIT&#93; an offering when you come to worship me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 34:22; EXODUS 22:29; LEVITICUS 23:9–21"> The second one is the Festival of Harvesting. During that festival you must offer to me the first parts/harvest of your crops that grow from the seeds that you planted. The third one is the Festival of Living in Temporary Shelters. That will be after you finish harvesting your grain and grapes and fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:16; EXODUS 34:23; EXODUS 23:14"> Every year, at each of these times, all the men must gather together to worship me, Yahweh God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 34:25; LEVITICUS 2:11; DEUTERONOMY 16:4"> When you sacrifice an animal and offer it to me, you must not offer bread that has been baked with yeast. When you offer sacrifices, burn the fat from the animals on that same day. Do not allow any fat to remain until the next morning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 34:26; DEUTERONOMY 14:21; DEUTERONOMY 26:10"> Each year, when you harvest your crops, bring to the place where you worship me, Yahweh God, the first part of what you harvest. When you kill a ◄young animal/kid or lamb or calf►, do not prepare to eat it by boiling it in its mother’s milk.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh gave the Israeli people several promises

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 33:14; PSALMS 91:11; EXODUS 32:34"> “Note this: I am going to send an angel ahead of you, to guard you as you travel and to bring you safely to the place that I have prepared for you. Pay attention to what he says and obey him. Do not rebel against him, because he will have my authority &#91;MTY&#93; and he will ◄punish/not forgive► &#91;LIT&#93; you if you rebel against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 12:3; DEUTERONOMY 30:7; ZECHARIAH 2:8"> But if you pay attention to what he says and if you do all that I tell you to do, I will fight strongly &#91;DOU&#93; against all of your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 23:20; Isaiah 5:13; GENESIS 15:19–21"> My angel will go ahead of you, and will take you to where the Amor and Heth and Periz and Canaan and Hiv and Jebus people-groups live, and I will completely get rid of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 20:5; DEUTERONOMY 12:30–31; NUMBERS 33:52"> Do not bow down before their gods/idols or worship them. And do not do the things that they think that their gods want them to do. Completely destroy their gods/idols, and smash to pieces their sacred stones.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:15; EXODUS 15:26; JOSHUA 22:5"> You must worship me, Yahweh God. If you do that, I will bless your (OR, bless you by giving you) food and water, and I will protect you from becoming sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:14; JOB 5:26; DEUTERONOMY 28:4"> No women in your land will have ◄miscarriages/babies that will die after being born prematurely►, and no women will be unable to become pregnant. And I will enable you to live a long time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 2:25; GENESIS 35:5; DEUTERONOMY 11:25"> I will cause the people who oppose you to become very afraid of me. I will cause the people whom you fight against to become very confused. And then I will cause them to turn around and run away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:20; JOSHUA 24:11–12"> I will cause your enemies to become terrified. And I will expel the Hiv, Canaan, and Heth people-groups from your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:22; JOSHUA 17:12–13; JUDGES 3:1–4"> I will not expel all of them in less than one year. If I did that, your land would become deserted, and there would be very many wild animals ◄that would attack you/you would not be able to control►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> I will expel those people-groups slowly, a few at a time, until the number of your people increases and you are able to live everywhere in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="GENESIS 15:18; DEUTERONOMY 11:24; JOSHUA 21:44"> I will cause the borders of your land to extend from the ◄Red Sea/Gulf of Aqaba► in the southeast to the Mediterranean Sea in the northwest, and from the Sinai Desert in the southwest to the Euphrates River in the northeast of the country. I will enable you &#91;MTY&#93; to expel the people who live there, so that you will expel them as you occupy more of the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:2; EXODUS 34:12; EXODUS 34:15"> You must not make any agreement/treaty with those people or with their gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 106:36; DEUTERONOMY 7:16; EXODUS 34:12"> Do not allow those people to live in your land, in order that they do not cause you to sin against me. If you worship their gods, you will not be able to escape from worshiping them and sinning against me, just like someone caught in a trap is unable to escape &#91;MET&#93;.”</span>
</div>
</div>
