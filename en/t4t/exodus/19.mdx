## Yahweh gave instructions to Moses at Sinai Mountain.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 16:1; EXODUS 12:6; LEVITICUS 23:16–18"> The Israeli people then left Rephidim, and exactly two months after leaving Egypt, they/we came to the desert near Sinai Mountain. They/We set up their/our tents at the base of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 3:4; ACTS 7:38; EXODUS 20:21"> Moses/I climbed up the mountain to talk with God. Yahweh called to him/me from the top of the mountain and said, “This is what I want you to say to the Israeli people, the descendants of Jacob:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:11–12; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 40:31"> ‘You have seen what I did to the Egyptians. You have seen what I did for all of you and how I brought you here to me. It was as though I lifted you up as an eagle carries its young eaglets on its wings &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:2; DEUTERONOMY 7:6; DEUTERONOMY 26:18"> So now, if you will do what I tell you and obey all that I command you, you will be my own people. All the people-groups on the earth are mine, but you will be ◄dearer/more special► to me than all the other people-groups.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="REVELATION 5:10; 1 PETER 2:9; 1 PETER 2:5"> You will be people over whom I will rule, and you will all ◄be like priests/represent me to other nations as priests represent me to one nation► &#91;MET&#93;, and you will be completely dedicated to me.’ That is what you must tell the Israeli people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 4:29–30; EXODUS 3:16; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:1"> So Moses/I went down the mountain and summoned the elders/leaders of the people. He/I told them everything that Yahweh had told him/me to tell them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 24:7; EXODUS 24:3; JOSHUA 24:24"> The people all said, “We will do everything that Yahweh has told us to do.” Then Moses/I climbed back up the mountain and reported to Yahweh what the people had said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:36; EXODUS 19:16; EXODUS 24:15–16"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Listen carefully: I am going to appear to you from inside a thick cloud. When I am speaking to you, the people will hear it, and they will always trust in you.” Then Moses/I went down the mountain and reported to the people what Yahweh said. Then he/I went back up the mountain and told Yahweh what the people replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:22; GENESIS 35:2; LEVITICUS 11:44–45"> Then Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Go back down to the people again. Tell them to purify themselves today and tomorrow. Tell them to wash their clothes, too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 19:16; EXODUS 34:5; DEUTERONOMY 33:2"> They must do that to be ready on the day after tomorrow. On that day I will come down to Sinai Mountain to where all the people can see me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:20–21; JOSHUA 3:4; EXODUS 19:21"> You must make a boundary around the base of the mountain, and tell them, ‘Be sure that you do not climb the mountain or even go near it. Anyone who even touches the base of the mountain must be executed.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 4:16; HEBREWS 12:20; EXODUS 19:16"> Do not let anyone touch any person or any animal that touches the mountain. You must kill any person or animal that touches the mountain by throwing stones at it or shooting it with arrows. But when you hear a long loud trumpet sound, the people can come close to the base of the mountain.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 19:10"> So Moses/I went down the mountain again and told the people to purify themselves. They did what Moses/I told them to do, and they also washed their clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 7:5; 1 SAMUEL 21:4–5; EXODUS 19:11"> Then Moses/I said to the people, “Be ready on the day after tomorrow. And you men must not have sex with &#91;EUP&#93; your wives until after then.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:18–19; PSALMS 18:11–14; HEBREWS 12:21"> Two days later, during the morning, there was thunder and lightning, and a very dark cloud on the mountain. A trumpet sounded very loudly, with the result that the people in the camp shook/trembled because they were very afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:10; DEUTERONOMY 5:5"> Then Moses/I led the people outside the camp to meet with God. They stood at the base of the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JUDGES 5:5; EXODUS 3:2; PSALMS 144:5"> All of Sinai Mountain was covered in smoke, because Yahweh had descended on it, surrounded by a fire. The smoke rose up like the smoke from the chimney of a furnace, and the whole mountain shook violently.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="PSALMS 81:7; EXODUS 19:16; Nehemiah 9:13"> As the sound of the trumpet continued to become louder, Moses/I spoke to Yahweh, and Yahweh answered him/me in a loud voice that sounded like thunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:13; EXODUS 34:2; EXODUS 19:11"> Then Yahweh came down again onto the top of Sinai Mountain, and he summoned Moses/me to come up to the top of the mountain. So Moses/I went up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 6:19; EXODUS 3:5; HEBREWS 12:28–29"> Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Go down again and warn the people not to cross the boundary in order to look at me. If they do that, many of them will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 24:5; ACTS 5:10; 1 CHRONICLES 13:9–11"> Also, the priests who habitually come near me must purify themselves. If they do not do that, I will punish them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 19:12; JOSHUA 3:4–5"> Then Moses/I said to Yahweh, “The people will not climb the mountain, because you commanded them, saying, ‘Set a boundary around the mountain, to make it sacred/taboo.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:16; HEBREWS 12:18–25; HEBREWS 12:29"> Yahweh replied, “Go down the mountain, and bring Aaron back up with you. But do not allow the priests or other people to cross the boundary to come up to me. If they cross it, I will punish them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So Moses/I went down the mountain again and told the people what Yahweh had said.</span>
</div>
</div>
