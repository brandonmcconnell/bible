## Instructions for the incense altar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="REVELATION 8:3; EXODUS 37:25–28; EXODUS 40:5"> “Tell the skilled workers to make an altar from acacia wood, for burning incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 27:2"> It is to be square, ◄18 in./45 cm.► on each side. It is to be ◄3 feet/90 cm.► high. Tell them to make a projection that looks like a horn on each of the top corners. The projections must be carved from the same block of wood that the altar is made from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 25:11; EXODUS 25:24"> They must cover the top and the four sides, including the projections, with pure gold. Put a gold border around the altar, near the top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 25:27; EXODUS 26:29; EXODUS 27:7"> They must make two gold rings for carrying the altar. They must attach them to the altar below the border, one on each side of the altar. These rings are for the poles for carrying the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 25:13; EXODUS 25:27"> Tell them to make these two poles from acacia wood and cover them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 25:21–22; EXODUS 30:36; LEVITICUS 16:13"> They must put this altar outside the curtain that hangs in front of the sacred chest and its lid. That is the place where I will talk with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:28; 1 CHRONICLES 23:13; EXODUS 27:20–21"> Aaron must burn sweet-smelling incense on this altar. He must burn some every morning when he takes care of the lamps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:24; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:17; HEBREWS 7:25"> and he must burn some in the evening when he lights the lamps. The incense must be burned continually, throughout all future generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:1"> The priests must not burn on the altar any incense that I have not told you to burn, or burn any animal on it, or any grain offering for me, or pour any wine on it as an offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LEVITICUS 16:18; HEBREWS 9:22–23; LEVITICUS 16:29–30"> One time every year Aaron must perform the ritual for making this altar pure. He must do it by putting on its four projections some of the blood from the animal that was sacrificed ◄to remove the guilt of the people’s sins/so that the people would no longer be guilty for sins►. This ritual is to be done by Aaron and his descendants throughout all future generations. This altar must be completely dedicated to me, Yahweh.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for the money to take care of the Sacred Tent

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:50; 1 CHRONICLES 27:24; EXODUS 38:25–26"> “When your leaders ◄take a census of/count► the Israeli people, each man who is counted must pay to me a price to save his life. They must do this in order that no disaster will happen to them while the people are being counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:25; NUMBERS 3:47; EZEKIEL 45:12"> Every man who is counted must pay to me ◄0.2 ounces/5.7 grams► of silver. They must use the official standard when they weigh the silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:20; NUMBERS 1:18; NUMBERS 1:3"> All the men who are at least 20 years old must pay this amount to me when the people are counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:50; LEVITICUS 17:11; EXODUS 30:12"> Rich men must not pay more than this amount, and poor men must not pay less than this amount, when they pay this money to save their lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:32–33; NUMBERS 16:40; EXODUS 38:25–31"> Your leaders must collect this money from the Israeli people and give it to those who will take care of the Sacred Tent. This money is the payment for the lives of those who take care of the Sacred Tent, ◄to enable me not to forget them/and then I will remember to protect them►.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for the washbasin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 40:7; EXODUS 38:8; 1 KINGS 7:23"> “Tell the skilled workers to make a bronze washbasin and a bronze base for it. They must put it between the Sacred Tent and the altar, and fill it with water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 52:11; EXODUS 40:31–32; PSALMS 26:6"> Aaron and his sons must ritually wash their hands and their feet with this water</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:1–3; HEBREWS 12:28–29; 1 CHRONICLES 13:10"> before they enter the Sacred Tent and before they come to the altar to sacrifice offerings that will be burned on it. If they do that, they will not die because of disobeying my instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 28:43; EXODUS 27:21"> They must wash their hands and their feet, in order that they will not die. They and the males descended from them must obey this ritual throughout all generations.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for the oil for anointing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EZEKIEL 27:19; SONG OF SONGS 4:14; EZEKIEL 27:22"> “Tell the people to collect some of the finest spices— ◄12 pounds/6 kg.► of ◄liquid myrrh/sweet-smelling sap named myrrh►, ◄6 pounds/3 kg.► of sweet-smelling cinnamon, ◄6 pounds/3 kg.► of a sweet-smelling cane/reed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 45:8; EXODUS 29:40; NUMBERS 3:47"> and ◄12 pounds/6 kg.► of ◄cassia/a sweet-smelling bark named cassia►. Be sure that they use the official standard when they weigh these things. Tell an expert perfumer to mix these with ◄one gallon/four liters► of olive oil</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 37:29; 1 CHRONICLES 9:30; EXODUS 40:9"> to make sacred oil for anointing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:1; 1 JOHN 2:20; Isaiah 61:1"> Use this oil for anointing the Sacred Tent, the sacred chest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> the table and all the things that are used with it, the lampstand and all the things that are used to take care of it, the altar for burning incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and the altar for offering sacrifices that will be burned, along with its bases and the washbasin and all the things that are used with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTHEW 23:19; EXODUS 29:37; LEVITICUS 6:18"> Dedicate them by anointing them, in order that they will be completely holy/sacred. Anyone or anything that touches the altar will become taboo.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LEVITICUS 8:12; EXODUS 28:3; LEVITICUS 8:30"> And anoint Aaron and his sons. By doing that, you will dedicate them to serve me by being priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="EXODUS 37:29; LEVITICUS 21:10; PSALMS 89:20"> And tell the Israeli people, ‘This oil will be my sacred anointing oil that must be used throughout all future generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 30:25; EXODUS 30:37–38; LEVITICUS 21:10"> You must not pour it on the bodies of people who are not priests, and you must not make other oil to be like it by mixing the same amount of those things. This oil is sacred, and you must consider it to be sacred.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 30:38; GENESIS 17:14; EXODUS 12:15"> I will consider that anyone who makes ointment like this for any other purpose, and anyone who puts any of this ointment on someone who is not a priest, no longer be allowed to associate with my people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="EXODUS 37:29; EXODUS 25:6; EXODUS 30:23"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me, “Tell the people to take equal parts of several sweet spices—stacte, onycha, galbanum, and pure frankincense—</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:13; EXODUS 30:25; JOHN 12:3"> and tell an expert perfumer to mix them together to make some perfume. Add some salt to keep it pure and make it holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EXODUS 25:22; EXODUS 30:6; EXODUS 16:34"> Beat some of it into a fine powder. Then take some of it into the Sacred Tent and sprinkle it in front of the sacred chest. You must all consider this incense to be very holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:3; EXODUS 29:37; EXODUS 30:32–33"> The people must not mix the same spices to make incense for themselves. This incense must be completely dedicated to me, Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="EXODUS 30:33"> I will consider that anyone who makes incense like this to use it for perfume will no longer be allowed to associate with my people.”</span>
</div>
