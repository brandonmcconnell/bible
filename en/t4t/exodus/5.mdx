<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 3:19; JOB 21:15; 2 KINGS 18:35"> But the king said, “Who is Yahweh? Why should I pay attention to what he says and let the Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93; go? I do not know Yahweh! And furthermore, I will not let the Israeli people go!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 3:18; ZECHARIAH 14:16–19; EZEKIEL 6:11"> They/we replied, “Yahweh God, the one we Hebrews worship, has ◄revealed himself/appeared► to us and told us what to tell you. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 1:11; ACTS 24:5; AMOS 7:10"> But the king of Egypt said to them/us, “Moses and Aaron, ◄stop trying to keep the Israeli people from working/why are you preventing the Israeli people from working?► &#91;RHQ&#93; Tell those slaves to return to work!”</span>

## The king increased the amount of work the Israelis were forced to do

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 5:10; EXODUS 5:19; DEUTERONOMY 16:18"> That same day the king commanded the Egyptian slave bosses and the Israeli men supervising the slaves,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JUDGES 19:19; GENESIS 24:25"> “Do not continue to give the Israeli people straw for making bricks, as you have done previously. Make them go into the fields and gather straw for themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 106:41"> But require them to make the same number of bricks that they did before. Do not decrease the number at all. They ◄are lazy/do not have enough work to do►. That is the reason they are asking me to let them go into the desert to offer sacrifices to their god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:20; JEREMIAH 43:2; JOB 16:3"> Force the men to work harder, so that they will not have time to listen to lies from their leaders!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 1:11; EXODUS 3:7; PROVERBS 29:12"> So the slave bosses and supervisors of the slaves went to where the Israeli people were and said to them, “The king has said that he will no longer give you any straw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 5:13–14"> So you yourselves must go and get straw wherever you can find it. But you must still keep working to make the same number of bricks as before.” &#91;LIT&#93;</span>

## The king refused to pay attention to the complaints of the Israelis

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the Israeli supervisors went to the king and complained, saying “Your Majesty, why are you treating us this way?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now they are not giving us any straw for making bricks, but they keep commanding us to make bricks. And now sometimes they beat us. But it is the fault of your own slave bosses that we cannot make as many bricks as before!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:8; JOHN 6:27; EXODUS 5:8"> But the king said, “You are lazy and do not want to work! That is why you keep saying, ‘Allow us to go to the desert and offer sacrifices to Yahweh.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:18; DANIEL 2:9–13"> So go and get back to work! We are not going to give you any straw, but you must keep making the same number of bricks!”</span>
</div>
