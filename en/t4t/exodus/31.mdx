## Instructions for the work to be done by Bezalel and Oholiab

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me, “Note that I have chosen Bezalel, the son of Uri and grandson of Hur, from the tribe of Judah, for special tasks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 35:31; Isaiah 28:26; 1 KINGS 7:14"> I have caused him to be completely controlled/empowered by my Spirit, and I have given him special ability to make things and have enabled him to know how to do very skilled work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 26:1; EXODUS 25:32–35; 2 CHRONICLES 2:13–14"> He can engrave skillful designs in gold, silver, and bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 28:9–21"> He can cut ◄jewels/valuable stones► and enclose them in tiny gold settings/frames. He can carve things from wood and do other skilled work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 35:10; EXODUS 35:34–1; EXODUS 28:3"> Note that I have also appointed Oholiab, the son of Ahisamach, from the tribe of Dan, to work with him. I have also given special ability to other men, in order that they can make all the things that I have commanded you to be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 36:8–9; EXODUS 25:10–22; EXODUS 26:1–37"> That includes the Sacred Tent, the sacred chest and its lid, all the other things that will be inside the Sacred Tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 37:10–28; EXODUS 30:1–10; EXODUS 25:23–40"> the table and all the things that are used with it, the pure gold lampstand and all the things that are used to take care of it, the altar for burning incense,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 38:1–8; EXODUS 27:1–8; EXODUS 40:11"> the altar for offering sacrifices that will be burned and all the things that will be used with it, the washbasin and its base,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:5–14; EXODUS 28:1–43; EXODUS 39:1–43"> all the beautiful sacred clothes for Aaron and his sons to wear when they work as priests,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 37:29; EXODUS 30:23–38"> the oil for anointing, and the sweet-smelling incense for the Holy Place. The skilled workers must make all these things exactly as I have told you to do.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for the day of rest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Yahweh also said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:12; EZEKIEL 20:20; LEVITICUS 19:30"> “Tell the Israeli people, ‘Obey my instructions regarding the ◄rest days/Sabbath days►. Those days will remind you and your descendants, throughout all future generations, that I, Yahweh, have ◄set you apart/chosen you► to be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 35:2–3; NUMBERS 15:32–36; EZEKIEL 20:21"> You must obey my rules about the Sabbath days because they are holy/sacred. Those who treat those days in an irreverent way by working on those days must be executed to show that I no longer consider them to belong to my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="GENESIS 2:2; EXODUS 16:23; LEVITICUS 23:3"> You may work for six days each week, but the seventh day of each week is a solemn day of rest, dedicated to me, Yahweh. Anyone who does any work on a day of rest is to be executed because I no longer want them to be able to associate with my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:5; GENESIS 9:13; GENESIS 17:11"> You Israeli people must respect the rest day, and you and your descendants must ◄celebrate it/keep it holy► throughout all future generations. It will remind you of the agreement that I have made with you that will last forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 1:31; EXODUS 31:13; GENESIS 2:2–3"> It will remind you Israeli people and remind me of that agreement because I, Yahweh, created the heavens and the earth in six days, and on the seventh day I stopped doing that work and relaxed.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:13; EXODUS 32:15–16; DEUTERONOMY 5:22"> When Yahweh finished talking with Moses/me on the top of Sinai Mountain, he gave him/me the two stone slabs on which he had engraved his commandments with his own fingers.</span>
</div>
</div>
