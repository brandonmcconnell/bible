## Yahweh further rebuked and warned the Israeli people

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 12:7; EXODUS 32:13; EXODUS 32:7"> Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Leave this place and go with the people whom you led out of Egypt. Go to the land that I promised Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob that I would give to their descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 32:34; JOSHUA 24:11; EXODUS 23:27–31"> I will send my angel ahead of you all, and I will expel from that land the Canaan, Amor, Heth, Periz, Hiv, and Jebus people-groups.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 32:9–10; EXODUS 3:8; ACTS 7:51"> You will go to a land that will be very good for raising livestock and growing crops &#91;IDM&#93;. But I will not go with you myself, because if I did that, I might get rid of you while you are traveling, because you are very stubborn &#91;IDM&#93; people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:39; NUMBERS 14:1; EZEKIEL 26:16"> Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Tell the Israeli people, ‘You are very stubborn &#91;IDM&#93;. If I would go with you for even a moment, I would completely ◄get rid of you/wipe you out►. Now take off your jewelry to show that you are sorry for having sinned. Then I will decide how I will punish you (OR, if I should punish you).’” So when Moses/I told to the people what God said, they started to mourn, and they all took off their jewelry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 33:4; EXODUS 32:3; JEREMIAH 2:19"> After the Israeli people left Sinai Mountain, they did not wear jewelry any more.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6–7; EXODUS 29:42–43; PROVERBS 15:29"> When the Israeli people traveled, whenever they stopped and set up their tents, Moses/I had them set up a tent outside the camp, far from the camp. I called it ‘the Sacred Tent where God and I talk together’. Everyone who wanted Yahweh to decide something for them would go out of the camp to the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="NUMBERS 16:27"> Whenever Moses/I went out to the Sacred Tent, all the people would stand at the entrances of their tents and watch him/me until he/I had entered the Sacred Tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 99:7; EXODUS 31:18; EXODUS 13:21–22"> When Moses/I entered the Sacred Tent, the tall cloud that looked like a fire would come down and stay at the entrance of the Sacred Tent, and then Yahweh would talk with Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:22; 1 KINGS 8:14; EXODUS 4:31"> When the people saw the tall cloud at the entrance of the Sacred Tent, they would all prostrate themselves on the ground and worship Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 12:8; DEUTERONOMY 34:10; EXODUS 24:13"> Yahweh would speak to Moses/me directly, like someone speaks to his friend. Then Moses/I would return to the camp. But his/my young helper, Joshua, the son of Nun, stayed in the Sacred Tent.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh promised to be with his people and to let Moses see his glory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 33:17; JEREMIAH 1:5; JOHN 10:14–15"> Moses/I said to Yahweh, “It is true that you have told me, ‘Lead the people to the land that I will show you,’ and you have said that you know me well and that you are pleased with me, but you have not told me whom you will send with me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 119:33; PSALMS 25:4; PSALMS 27:11"> So now, if you are truly pleased with me, I ask you, tell me the things that you intend/plan to do, in order that I will know you better and continue to please you. Also, do not forget that the Israeli people are the people whom you chose to belong to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:44; MATTHEW 11:28; JOSHUA 1:5"> Yahweh replied, “I will go with you, and I will give you inner peace.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 34:9; PSALMS 4:6; EXODUS 33:3"> Moses/I replied, “If you do not go with me, do not force us to leave this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:7; NUMBERS 14:14; 1 KINGS 8:53"> The only way that other people will know that you are pleased with me and with your people is if you go with us &#91;RHQ&#93;! ◄If you go with us, that will show that we are different from all the other people on the earth./If you do not go with us, what will show that we are different from all the other people on the earth?►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 65:24; 1 JOHN 5:14–15; EXODUS 33:12"> Yahweh replied to Moses/me, “What you have asked is exactly what I will do, because I know you well and I am pleased with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="REVELATION 21:23; EXODUS 33:20; TITUS 2:13"> Then Moses/I said, “Please let me see your glorious presence!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ROMANS 9:15–18; EPHESIANS 1:6–8; JEREMIAH 31:14"> Yahweh replied, “I will let you see how great and glorious I am, and I will tell you clearly that my name is Yahweh. I will act very kindly and be merciful to all those whom I choose.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 32:30; JOHN 1:18; 1 TIMOTHY 6:16"> But you are not allowed to see my face, because anyone who sees my face will ◄die/not continue to stay alive►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:31; ZECHARIAH 3:7; JOSHUA 20:4"> But look! Here is a place close to me where you can stand on a large rock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 91:1; Isaiah 2:21; PSALMS 91:4"> When my glorious presence comes past you, I will put you in a large crevice/opening in the rock, and I will cover your face with my hand until I have passed by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 33:20; JOHN 1:18; 1 TIMOTHY 6:16"> Then I will take my hand away, and you will see my back, but you will not see my face.”</span>
</div>
</div>
