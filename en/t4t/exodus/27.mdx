## Instructions for the altar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 38:1–7; EXODUS 20:24–26; EXODUS 40:10"> “Tell them to make an altar from acacia wood. It is to be square, ◄7-1/2 feet/2.2 meters► on each side, and make it ◄4-1/2 feet/1.3 meters► high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 118:27; EXODUS 29:12; LEVITICUS 4:7"> They must make a projection that looks like a horn on each of the top corners. The projections must be carved from the same block of wood as the altar. Tell them to cover the whole altar with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:45; NUMBERS 4:14; 1 CHRONICLES 28:17"> They must make pans in which to put the greasy ashes from the animal sacrifices. Also they must make shovels for cleaning out the ashes, basins and forks for turning the meat as it cooks, and buckets for carrying hot coals/ashes. All of these things must be made from bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 35:16; EXODUS 25:12; EXODUS 38:4–5"> Also tell them to make a bronze grating to hold the wood and burning coals. They must fasten to each of the corners of the altar a bronze ring for carrying the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 38:4"> They must put the grating under the rim that is around the altar. They must make it so that it is inside the altar, halfway down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 35:13–15; NUMBERS 4:44; EXODUS 30:4"> For carrying the altar, they must make poles from acacia wood and cover them with bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 25:28; EXODUS 30:4; NUMBERS 4:13–14"> They must put the poles through the rings on each side of the altar. The poles are for carrying the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 25:40; EXODUS 25:9; HEBREWS 8:5"> The altar will be like a box, made from boards of acacia wood. They must make it according to these instructions that I am giving you here on this mountain.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions for the courtyard

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 38:9–20; EZEKIEL 40:32; EZEKIEL 42:3"> “Around the Sacred Tent there is to be a courtyard. To form the courtyard, tell them to make curtains of fine linen. On the south side, the curtain is to be ◄50 yards/44 meters► long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 26:19–21; JEREMIAH 52:21; EXODUS 36:38"> To support/hang the curtain, tell them to make twenty bronze posts, and one bronze base for each post. To fasten the curtains to the posts, they must make silver hooks, and metal rods covered with silver to fasten the curtains to the hooks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> They must make the same kind of curtains for the north side of the courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> On the west side of the courtyard they must make a curtain ◄25 yards/22 meters► long. The curtains are to be supported by ten posts, with a base under each post.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> On the east side, where the entrance is, the courtyard must also be ◄25 yards/22 meters► wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 27:9; EXODUS 26:36"> Tell them to make a curtain ◄22-1/2 feet/6.6 meters► wide for each side of the entrance to the courtyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 26:36; JUDGES 5:30; EXODUS 28:39"> They must make a curtain ◄30 feet/9 meters► long for the entrance. A skilled weaver must embroider it with blue, purple, and red yarn/thread. It must be ◄supported by/hung from► four posts, each one with a base under it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> All the posts around the courtyard must be connected with metal rods covered with silver. The clasps/fasteners must be made of silver, and the bases must be made of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The whole courtyard, from the east entrance to the west end, must be ◄50 yards/44 meters► long, and the curtains that enclose it must be ◄7-1/2 feet/2.3 meters► high. All the curtains must be made of fine linen, and all the bases under the posts must be made of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EXODUS 35:18; Isaiah 22:23–25; NUMBERS 4:32"> All the things that are not made of gold that are to be used inside the Sacred Tent and in the courtyard, and all the tent pegs to support the Sacred Tent and the curtains, must be made of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 24:1–4; ZECHARIAH 4:11–14; PSALMS 23:5"> “Command the Israeli people that they must bring to you the best kind of olive oil to burn in the lamps. They must bring this oil to you continually, in order that the lamps can burn continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 19:21; NUMBERS 18:23; 1 SAMUEL 3:3"> They must put the lampstand outside of the curtain which is in front of the sacred chest which contains the stone slabs on which I have written my commandments. Aaron must take care of the lamps. After he dies, his descendants must do this work. The lamps are to burn every night, from evening until morning. The Israeli people must obey this regulation throughout all future generations.”</span>
</div>
