<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 3:18; EXODUS 2:14; JEREMIAH 1:6"> Moses/I replied, “But what if the Israeli people ◄do not believe me/are not convinced► or not do what I tell them? What if they say, ‘Yahweh did not appear to you!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 4:17; EXODUS 4:20; Isaiah 11:4"> Yahweh said to him/me, “Look at that thing you are holding in your hand. What is it?” He/I replied, “A ◄walking stick/shepherd’s stick►.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 7:10–15; AMOS 5:19; EXODUS 4:17"> He said, “Throw it down on the ground!” So, he/I threw it on the ground, and it became a snake! And he/I ran/jumped away from it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ACTS 28:3–6; GENESIS 22:1–2; PSALMS 91:13"> But Yahweh said to Moses/me, “Reach down and pick it up by its tail!” So he/I reached down and caught it, and when he/I picked it up, it became a stick in his/my hand again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 19:9; GENESIS 48:3; EXODUS 3:15"> Yahweh said, “Do the same thing in front of the Israeli people, in order that they may believe that I, Yahweh God, the one Abraham and Isaac and Jacob worshiped, truly appeared to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 KINGS 5:27; NUMBERS 12:10"> Yahweh spoke to Moses/me again, saying “Put your hand inside your robe &#91;MTY&#93;!” He/I did that. <span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:10; DEUTERONOMY 32:39; EXODUS 4:30–31"> God said, “You can do that in front of the Israeli people, too. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 7:19–25; EXODUS 1:22; MATTHEW 7:2"> If they do not believe you or do what you say, even after you perform those two miracles, get some water from the Nile River and pour it on the ground. When you do that, the water from the river that you pour on the ground will become blood &#91;MTY&#93; (OR, red like blood).”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:6; EXODUS 6:12; ACTS 7:22"> Moses/I replied, “O Yahweh! I am not an eloquent speaker! <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="AMOS 3:6; PSALMS 146:8; EZEKIEL 3:26–27"> Then Yahweh said to him/me, “You seem to forget who it is that makes people able to speak &#91;RHQ&#93;! <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:9; LUKE 12:11–12; MATTHEW 10:19–20"> So start going to Egypt, and I will help you to speak &#91;MTY&#93;, and I will tell you what you should say.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JONAH 1:3; EXODUS 4:1; MATTHEW 13:41"> But he/I replied, “O, Yahweh, I ask you, please send someone else instead!”</span>

He is actually on his way here right now, and he will be very happy to see you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:16; DEUTERONOMY 18:18; LUKE 21:15"> You can talk to him and tell him what to say &#91;MTY&#93;, and I will help both of you &#91;SYN&#93; to know what to say &#91;MTY&#93;. And I will tell you both what you should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 7:1–2; PSALMS 82:6; JOHN 10:34–35"> He will speak for you to the Israeli people. He will be ◄your spokesman/as though he was your mouth► &#91;MET&#93;, and you will be to him as though you are his god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 4:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:27; EXODUS 7:9–20"> Be sure to take with you the walking/shepherd’s stick that is in your hand, because you will perform miracles with it.”</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:20; EXODUS 2:23; EXODUS 2:15"> Yahweh said to Moses/me before he/I left Midian, “You can safely return to Egypt, because the men who were wanting to kill you &#91;MTY&#93; are now dead.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 17:9; NUMBERS 20:8–9; EXODUS 18:3–4"> So Moses/I took his/my wife and sons and put them on donkeys, and they/we returned to Egypt. And Moses/I took in his/my hand the stick that God told him/me to take along.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ROMANS 9:18; Isaiah 63:17; EXODUS 14:8"> Yahweh said to Moses/me, “When you return to Egypt, be sure to perform all the miracles that I have given you power to do, while the king is watching. <span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HOSEA 11:1; ROMANS 9:4; JEREMIAH 31:9"> Then say to him, ‘This is what Yahweh says: “The Israeli people &#91;MTY&#93; are as dear to me as firstborn sons &#91;MET&#93;.</span>

## Zipporah circumcised her son

<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 17:14; NUMBERS 22:22–23; HOSEA 13:8"> One night, as they were camping on the way to Egypt, Yahweh appeared to Moses/me. He wanted/threatened to kill Moses/me for disobeying his command that boys/sons be circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 5:2–3; GENESIS 17:14; 2 SAMUEL 16:7"> Then his/my wife Zipporah took a flint knife and circumcised her son.
