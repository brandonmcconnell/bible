## Yahweh made bitter water become good water to drink

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 16:7; 1 SAMUEL 15:7; GENESIS 25:18"> Then Moses/I led the Israeli people away from the Red Sea (OR, Gulf of Suez). They/we went to the desert at Shur. They/We walked for three days, but they/we could not find any water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="RUTH 1:20; NUMBERS 33:8"> So they/we went on and came to a place named Marah. There was water there, but they/we could not drink it, because it was bitter. That is why they named the place Marah, which is the Hebrew word that means ‘bitter’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 16:2; EXODUS 14:11; NUMBERS 20:2–5"> The people complained to Moses/me, saying, “What are we going to drink?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 8:2; PSALMS 66:10; JUDGES 3:1"> So Moses/I prayed earnestly to Yahweh. Then Yahweh showed him/me a tree. So he/I cut off one of the branches and threw it into the water, and the water became good to drink. There at Marah, Yahweh gave them various laws to direct their lives. He also tested them there, to determine if they would obey him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 103:3; DEUTERONOMY 7:15; EXODUS 23:25"> by saying, “I am Yahweh, your God. Do not forget that I am the one who heals you.”</span>
