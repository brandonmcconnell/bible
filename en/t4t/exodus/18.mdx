## Moses’ father-in-law, Jethro, came to visit him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 2:16; PSALMS 77:14–15; EXODUS 3:1"> Jethro, who was the priest for the Midian people-group, and who was also Moses’/my father-in-law, heard about all that God had done for the Israeli people. He heard about how Yahweh had brought them out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 2:21; EXODUS 4:25–26"> Moses/I had previously sent his/my wife Zipporah back home when he/I was returning to Egypt. But now Jethro came to him/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 2:22; ACTS 7:29; PSALMS 39:12"> bringing Zipporah and their/our two sons. One son was named Gershom, which sounds like the Hebrew word that means ‘foreigner’, because he/I had said, “I have been a foreigner living in another land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:6; 2 TIMOTHY 4:17; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:8–10"> Her other son was named Eliezer, which sounds like the Hebrew words that mean ‘God helps me’, because he/I had said “God, whom my father worshiped, has helped me and saved me from being killed &#91;MTY&#93; by the king of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 3:1; EXODUS 3:12; EXODUS 24:16–17"> While Moses/I was camped with the Israeli people in the desert near Sinai, God’s sacred/holy mountain, Jethro came to him/me, bringing along Moses’/my wife and our two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Jethro had sent a message to Moses/me, “I, your father-in-law, Jethro, am coming to see you, bringing along your wife and your two sons!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="GENESIS 29:13; GENESIS 14:17; 2 SAMUEL 11:7"> So Moses/I went out of the campsite to meet his/my father-in-law. He/I bowed before him, and kissed him on the cheek. ◄They both/We► asked each other, “Have you been healthy?” Then they/we went into Moses’/my tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 145:4–12; Nehemiah 9:9–15; PSALMS 107:2"> Moses/I told Jethro everything that Yahweh had done to the king and all the other people in Egypt for the sake of the Israeli people. He/I also told him about the troubles/problems they/we had experienced on the way, and how Yahweh had helped them/us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:26; ROMANS 12:15; Isaiah 66:10"> Jethro rejoiced when he heard all that Yahweh had done for the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 14:20; 2 SAMUEL 18:28; LUKE 1:68"> He said, “Praise Yahweh, who has rescued you from the powerful &#91;MTY&#93; Egyptian king and his army!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 95:3; EXODUS 15:11; 2 CHRONICLES 2:5"> Now I realize that Yahweh is greater than all other gods, because he rescued you all from the power &#91;MTY&#93; of the proud Egyptians when they were causing you to suffer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 31:54; DEUTERONOMY 12:7; EXODUS 24:11"> Then Jethro sacrificed an animal by burning it completely on the altar as an offering, and he also offered other sacrifices to God. Aaron and the Israeli elders/leaders went with them/us to eat a sacred meal with Jethro.</span>
</div>

## Jethro gave Moses some good advice about appointing judges

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 16:5; JOB 29:7; JUDGES 5:10"> The next day, Moses/I sat down at the place where he/I settled disputes among the people. The people were continually bringing their disputes to Moses/me, from morning until evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> When Jethro saw everything that Moses/I was doing for the people, he said, “◄Why are you doing all this for the people?/You should not be doing all this for the people!► &#91;RHQ&#93; Why are you doing this by yourself, and all the people are crowding around you, from morning until evening, asking you to make decisions for them &#91;RHQ&#93;?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 15:34; NUMBERS 27:5; NUMBERS 9:8"> Moses/I replied, “I am doing this because the people keep coming to me to find out what God desires.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 24:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 6:1; 2 SAMUEL 15:3"> When they have a dispute about something, they come to me, and they ask me to decide which of them is right. I also tell them all of God’s laws and instructions.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Jethro said to him/me, “What you are doing is not good for you or for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:14–17; ACTS 6:1–4; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:15"> You and these people will ◄wear yourselves out/become exhausted►! This work is too much for one person. You are not able to do it by yourself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:5; DEUTERONOMY 5:5; EXODUS 20:19"> Now listen to some good advice &#91;MTY&#93; I will give to you. If you do what I suggest, God will help you. You should continue to speak to God and tell him about the people’s disputes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 5:1; PSALMS 143:8; DEUTERONOMY 1:18"> You should also teach the people what God has commanded and instructed you. You should also explain to them how they should conduct their lives, and the things that they should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ACTS 6:3; DEUTERONOMY 1:13–17; 2 CHRONICLES 19:5–10"> But in addition, you should choose some other men to help you. Choose men who have reverence for God and who will not accept bribes. Appoint some of them to make decisions for groups of ten people, some for groups of 50 people, some for groups of 100 people, and some for groups of 1,000 people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="NUMBERS 11:17; NUMBERS 27:2; NUMBERS 15:33"> Allow them to serve permanently to settle disputes for the people. The difficult matters the people can bring to you; but the matters that are not difficult, they can decide themselves. That will make the work easier for you, as they help you do that work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 8:6–7; EXODUS 18:18; 1 SAMUEL 8:22"> If you do that, and I think that God is telling you to do it (OR, if God tells you to do it), you will not become exhausted, and all the people will be able to go home feeling satisfied about the decisions, with their disputes settled quickly”.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezra 10:2; EXODUS 18:19; EXODUS 18:2–5"> Moses/I accepted what his/my father-in-law Jethro suggested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:15; EXODUS 18:21; ACTS 6:5"> Moses/I chose capable men from among the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 18:22; EXODUS 18:14–15; DEUTERONOMY 17:8"> They were appointed permanently to make decisions about the people’s disputes. They brought the difficult cases to Moses/me, but they decided the matters that were not difficult by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 10:29–30; JUDGES 19:9; GENESIS 31:55"> Then Moses/I said goodbye to his/my father-in-law, and Jethro returned home.</span>
</div>
