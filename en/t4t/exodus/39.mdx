## They made the sacred clothes for Aaron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="EXODUS 31:10; EXODUS 35:23; EXODUS 35:19"> Bezalel, Oholiab, and the other skilled workmen made the beautiful clothes for Aaron to wear while he did his work as a priest in the Holy Place. They made them from blue, purple, and red cloth, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 28:6–12; LEVITICUS 8:7; EXODUS 25:7"> They made the sacred apron from fine white linen and from blue, purple, and red cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 36:8; EXODUS 26:1"> They hammered some thin sheets of gold and cut them into thin strips which they embroidered into the fine linen and into the blue, purple, and red cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> The apron had two shoulder straps, to join the front part to the back part at the shoulders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="REVELATION 1:13; MATTHEW 28:20; Isaiah 11:5"> A carefully-woven sash, which was made from the same materials as the sacred apron, was sewn onto the sacred apron. This was made exactly as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 28:9; EZEKIEL 28:13; JOB 28:16"> They cut two valuable onyx stones and enclosed them in a tiny gold frame, and a skilled gem-cutter engraved on the stones the names of the twelve sons of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 4:7; EXODUS 28:12; EXODUS 28:29"> They fastened the stones to the shoulder straps of the sacred apron, to represent the twelve Israeli tribes, exactly as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 28:4; LEVITICUS 8:8–9; Isaiah 59:17"> They made the sacred chest pouch. They made it of the same materials as the sacred apron and embroidered it in the same way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> It was square, and the material was folded double, so that it was ◄9 in./22 cm.► long and ◄9 in./22 cm.► wide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 28:21; EXODUS 28:16–17; REVELATION 21:19–21"> They fastened four rows of valuable stones onto the pouch. In the first row, they put a red ruby, a yellow topaz, and a red garnet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 28:18; EZEKIEL 28:13"> In the second row, they put a green emerald, a blue sapphire, and a clear/white diamond.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> In the third row they put a red jacinth, a white agate, and a purple amethyst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> In the fourth row, they put a yellow beryl, a red carnelian, and a green jasper. They set each of the stones in tiny gold frames.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="REVELATION 21:12"> On each of the twelve stones they engraved the name of one of the sons of Jacob, to represent one of the twelve Israeli tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOHN 10:28; JOHN 17:12; 1 PETER 1:5"> They made two chains from pure gold and braided them like cords, to attach the sacred pouch to the sacred apron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 25:12"> They made two gold rings, and they attached them to the upper corners of the sacred pouch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> They fastened one end of each cord to one of the rings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 28:14; SONG OF SONGS 1:10"> They fastened the other end of each cord to the two ◄settings/tiny frames► that enclosed the stones. Then they attached the sacred pouch to the shoulder straps of the sacred apron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then they made two more gold rings and attached them to the lower corners of the sacred pouch, on the inside edges, next to the sacred apron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 26:3"> They made two more gold rings and attached them to the lower part of the front of the shoulder straps, near to where the shoulder straps were joined to the sacred apron, just above the carefully-woven sash/waistband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:24; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:25; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:27"> They tied the rings on the sacred pouch to the rings on the sacred apron with a blue cord, so that the sacred pouch was above the sash/waistband and would not come loose from the sacred apron.</span>
</div>
</div>

## They made other special clothes for the priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 28:31–35"> They made the robe that is to be worn underneath the priest’s sacred apron, using only blue cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> It had an opening through which the priest would put his head. They sewed a border around this opening, to prevent the material from tearing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="EXODUS 28:33; GALATIANS 5:22"> At the lower edge on the robe, they fastened decorations that resembled pomegranate fruit. The decorations were woven from blue, purple, and red yarn/thread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 28:33–34; SONG OF SONGS 4:13; PSALMS 89:15"> Between each of these decorations, they fastened a tiny bell made from pure gold, so there was a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, alternating all around the hem of the robe</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 28:34; SONG OF SONGS 4:13; SONG OF SONGS 6:7"> for Aaron to wear while he did his work as a priest. They made all these things exactly as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EZEKIEL 44:18; 1 PETER 1:13; Isaiah 61:10"> They wove long-sleeved tunics/gowns from fine white linen, for Aaron and his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="EXODUS 28:4; EZEKIEL 44:18; EXODUS 28:39"> They also made a turban of fine linen for Aaron to wear around his head. They made the caps and the undershorts for Aaron’s sons from fine linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EXODUS 28:39"> They made the embroidered sash/waistband for Aaron from fine linen and from blue, purple, and red cloth, and they embroidered designs on it using blue, purple, and red yarn/thread, exactly as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="REVELATION 5:10; HEBREWS 7:26; HEBREWS 1:3"> They also made the tiny ornament of pure gold and had a skilled workman engrave on it the words, ‘Dedicated to Yahweh’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> They fastened this to the front of the turban by a blue cord, just as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 39:42–43; MATTHEW 28:20; EXODUS 25:40"> Finally they finished all the work to make the Sacred Tent. They brought to Moses/me all the things that they had made. They had made them exactly as Yahweh had commanded me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="EXODUS 35:11–19; EXODUS 31:7–11; EXODUS 36:1–38"> They brought to him/me the Sacred Tent and all the things that were used with it: the hooks, the frames, the crossbars, the posts and their bases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> the coverings for the Sacred Tent that were made of tanned/dyed rams’ skins and goatskins, the curtains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:5; EXODUS 25:17; HEBREWS 9:8"> the sacred chest that contained the stone slabs on which the commandments were written, the poles, the lid for the chest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="EXODUS 25:30; 1 KINGS 7:48"> the table with all the things that were used with it, the sacred bread that was offered to God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:15; EXODUS 27:21; MATTHEW 5:14–16"> the lampstand made of pure gold and all its lamps and all the things that were to be used to take care of it, the oil for the lamps,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="EXODUS 30:7; EXODUS 37:29; EXODUS 30:3"> the golden altar for burning incense, the oil for anointing, the sweet-smelling incense, the curtain for the entrance to the Sacred Tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:64; EXODUS 38:30"> the bronze altar for burning sacrifices and its bronze grating, the poles for carrying it, and all the other things that were used with it, the washbasin and its base,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="EXODUS 27:9–16"> the curtains that surrounded the courtyard, the posts and bases that supported them, the curtain for the entrance to the courtyard and its ropes, the tent pegs, and all the other things that would be used in the Sacred Tent,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="EXODUS 28:2; EXODUS 31:10; EXODUS 39:1"> the beautiful sacred clothes for Aaron to wear when he did his work in the Holy Place, and the special clothes for his sons to wear as they did their work as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="EXODUS 35:10; EXODUS 23:21–22; 2 TIMOTHY 2:15"> The Israeli people had done all this work exactly as Yahweh had commanded Moses/me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:23–27; LEVITICUS 9:22–23; 2 SAMUEL 6:18"> Then Moses/I saw all the work that they had done. Truly, they had done everything exactly as Yahweh had commanded that it should be done. Then Moses/I asked God to bless them.</span>
</div>
