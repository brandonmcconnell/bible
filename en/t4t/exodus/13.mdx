## Yahweh gave Moses instructions about dedicating the firstborn sons

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh said to Moses/me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 2:23; NUMBERS 3:13; DEUTERONOMY 15:19"> “Set apart all the firstborn males in order that they may belong to me. The firstborn males of the Israeli people and of their animals will be mine.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Moses instructed them about future celebrations of the Festival of Unleavened Bread

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:18; DEUTERONOMY 5:15; EXODUS 20:2"> Moses/I said to the people, “Do not forget this day! This is the day that you are leaving Egypt. This is the day you are freed from being their slaves. Yahweh has brought you out of Egypt by his great power &#91;MTY&#93;. Do not eat any bread that has yeast in it whenever you celebrate this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 34:18; EXODUS 23:15; EXODUS 12:2"> You are leaving Egypt on this day, which is the first day of the month that is named Abib.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 3:8; JOSHUA 24:11; EXODUS 12:25–26"> Later, when Yahweh brings you into the land where the descendants of Canaan, Heth, Amor, Hiv, and Jebus now live, the land that he solemnly promised to give to you, a land that will be very good for raising livestock and growing crops &#91;MTY&#93;, you must celebrate this festival during this month every year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 12:15–20; EXODUS 34:18; LEVITICUS 23:8"> For seven days the bread that you eat must not have any yeast in it. On the seventh day there must be a festival to honor Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 12:19; MATTHEW 16:6"> For seven days do not eat bread that has yeast in it. You should not have any yeast or bread made with yeast anywhere in your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 13:14; EXODUS 12:26–27; PSALMS 44:1"> On the day the festival starts, you must tell your children, ‘We are doing this to remember what Yahweh did for our ancestors when they left Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 6:8; DEUTERONOMY 11:18–19; NUMBERS 15:39"> This ritual will remind you how Yahweh brought your ancestors out of Egypt with his great power &#91;MTY&#93;. The ritual will be like something you tie on your forehead or on your wrist. It will remind you to recite to others what Yahweh has instructed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 12:14; EXODUS 12:24–25; EXODUS 23:15"> So you must celebrate this festival every year at the time Yahweh has appointed.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 13:5"> Yahweh will bring you into the land where the descendants of Canaan live, just as he promised you and your ancestors that he would do. When he gives that land to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:15; LEVITICUS 27:26; EXODUS 34:19"> you must dedicate to Yahweh the firstborn males of all your animals. These all will belong to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EXODUS 34:20; NUMBERS 18:15–17; REVELATION 14:4"> You may keep the firstborn male donkeys, but you must buy them back by sacrificing a lamb as a substitute for the donkey. If you do not want to buy back the donkey, you must kill it by breaking its neck. You must also buy back every one of your own firstborn sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 13:3; JOSHUA 4:6; PSALMS 145:4"> In the future, when one of your children asks, ‘What does this mean?’, you must say to him, ‘Yahweh brought our ancestors out of Egypt with his great power &#91;MTY&#93;, and freed us from being slaves there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 12:29"> The king of Egypt stubbornly refused to let them leave his country, so Yahweh killed all the firstborn males in Egypt, both the boys and the firstborn of their livestock. That is why we now sacrifice to Yahweh all the firstborn of our livestock, but we buy back our own firstborn sons.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 13:9; DEUTERONOMY 11:18; DEUTERONOMY 6:7–9"> I repeat that this ritual will remind you about how Yahweh brought our ancestors out of Egypt by his great power &#91;MTY&#93;; it will be like something you tie on your wrist or on your forehead to remind you of that.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## God led them to detour through the desert

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EXODUS 14:11–12; Nehemiah 9:17; NUMBERS 14:1–4"> When the king of Egypt let the Israeli people go, God did not lead them to go through the land of the Philistines. That was a shorter way, but God said, “It would be bad if my people change their minds when they realize that they will have to fight the Philistines to take their land, and then decide to return to Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EXODUS 14:2; DEUTERONOMY 32:10; NUMBERS 33:6–8"> Instead, God led them to go around through the desert toward the Red Sea (OR, the Gulf of Suez). When the Israeli people left Egypt, they were carrying weapons to fight their enemies.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 50:24–25; JOSHUA 24:32; ACTS 7:16"> Moses/I had them take along the bones of Joseph with them/us, because Joseph long ago had made the Israeli people promise solemnly that they would do that. He had said to them, “God will enable your descendants to leave Egypt. When that happens, you must carry my bones with you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 33:5–6; EXODUS 12:37"> The Israeli people left Succoth and traveled to Etham, at the edge of the desert, and they set up their tents there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:1–2; Nehemiah 9:12; DEUTERONOMY 1:33"> When they traveled during the daytime, Yahweh went in front of them in a tall white cloud to show them the way. During the night, he went in front of them in a tall cloud that looked like a fire. By doing that, he enabled them to travel in the daytime and also at nighttime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PSALMS 121:5–8; REVELATION 10:1"> The tall cloud did not leave them. It was always in front of them, as a bright white cloud in the daytime and like a fire at night.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
