## The duties of the priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:6; EXODUS 6:23; NUMBERS 26:60"> These are the groups of the descendants of Aaron the first Supreme Priest: Aaron’s four sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:2; NUMBERS 3:4; NUMBERS 26:61"> But Nadab and Abihu died before their father died, and they had no children. So their younger brothers Eleazar and Ithamar became the priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:17; 1 CHRONICLES 24:31; 1 SAMUEL 21:1"> Zadok, who was a descendant of Eleazar, and Ahimelech, who was a descendant of Ithamar, helped David to separate his descendants into two groups. Each group had certain duties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:24; NUMBERS 25:11–13; 1 CHRONICLES 15:6–12"> There were more leaders among the descendants of Eleazar than there were among the descendants of Ithamar. So they appointed 16 leaders from Eleazar’s descendants and eight leaders from Ithamar’s descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 24:31; JOSHUA 18:10; ACTS 4:1"> There were temple officials and priests, including descendants of both Eleazar and Ithamar, to make sure that the work was divided fairly. So they decided what work each person would do by ◄casting lots/throwing marked stones►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 18:16; MATTHEW 8:19; Nehemiah 8:1"> Shemaiah, the son of Nethanel, who was a descendant of Levi, wrote down the names of the leaders of each group while David and his officials were watching. Zadok, the Supreme Priest, and Ahimelech his assistant, and the leaders of the families of the priests and of the families of the other descendants of Levi also watched.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezra 2:36; Nehemiah 11:10; Nehemiah 12:19"> Jehoiarib was the first one whose name was selected by casting lots/throwing marked stones. Next Jedaiah was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 2:39; Nehemiah 7:35; Nehemiah 12:15"> Next, Harim was selected. Next, Seorim was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:17"> Next, Malkijah was selected. Next, Mijamin was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="LUKE 1:5; Nehemiah 12:4; Nehemiah 12:17"> Next, Hakkoz was selected. Next, Abijah was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 2:36; Nehemiah 12:10; Nehemiah 7:39"> Next, Jeshua was selected. Next, Shecaniah was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 12:10"> Next, Eliashib was selected. Next, Jakim was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Next, Huppah was selected. Next, Jeshebeab was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:40; Ezra 2:37; Ezra 10:20"> Next, Bilgah was selected. Next, Immer was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Next, Hezir was selected. Next, Happizzez was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Next, Pethahiah was selected. Next, Jehezkel was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Next, Jakin was selected. Next, Gamul was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Next, Delaiah was selected. Next, Maaziah was selected.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 9:25; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:40; HEBREWS 7:11"> Those were the men who were chosen to be the leaders of the groups that would serve in the temple, obeying the regulations that were set down by Aaron, regulations which Yahweh, the God to whom the Israeli people belonged, had given to him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The other descendants of Levi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 26:24; 1 CHRONICLES 23:16; 1 CHRONICLES 6:18"> This is a list of some of the other descendants of Levi: Amram was the ancestor of Shubael and Jehdeiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:17; 1 CHRONICLES 26:25"> Rehabiah was the ancestor of Isshiah, the oldest son in his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:18; NUMBERS 3:27; NUMBERS 3:19"> Izhar was the father of Shelomoth and the grandfather of Jahath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:19; 1 CHRONICLES 26:31"> Kohath’s son Hebron had four sons: Jeriah and his younger brothers Amariah, Jahaziel, and Jekameam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:20"> Uzziel was the father of Micah and the grandfather of Shamir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 15:20; 1 CHRONICLES 15:18; 1 CHRONICLES 23:20"> Micah’s younger brother was the father of Zechariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:21"> The sons of Merari were Mahli, Mushi, and Jaaziah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:20; 1 CHRONICLES 23:21; 1 CHRONICLES 6:19"> Jaaziah had four sons: Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:22"> Mahli’s son Eleazar did not have any sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> From the descendants of Mahli’s second son Kish there was Jerahmeel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:23; 1 CHRONICLES 6:47"> The sons of Mushi were Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth.<br />Those were descendants of Levi who were listed according to the leaders of their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 24:5–6; 1 CHRONICLES 26:13; 1 CHRONICLES 25:8"> The jobs they would do were decided by ◄casting lots/throwing marked stones►, like their fellow Israelis, the descendants of Aaron, did. They cast lots &#123;The lots were cast&#125; while King David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the leaders of the families of the priests and the other descendants of Levi watched. They gave the same jobs to the families of each oldest brother and each youngest brother.</span>
</div>
</div>
