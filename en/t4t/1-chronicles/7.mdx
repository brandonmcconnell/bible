## The descendants of Issachar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 46:13; NUMBERS 26:23–24"> Issachar’s four sons were Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 24:1–9; 1 CHRONICLES 27:23–24; 1 CHRONICLES 27:1"> Tola’s sons were Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Samuel (OR, Shemuel). They were all leaders of the clans descended from them. In the record of Tola’s descendants were the names of 22,600 men who served in the army during the time that David was the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Uzzi’s son was Izrahiah. Izrahiah’s five sons were Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah. Izrahiah and his sons were all leaders of their clans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 12:32"> In the record of Izrahiah’s descendants were the names of 36,000 men who served in the army because they had many wives and children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> In the records of the clans descended from Issachar were the names of 87,000 men who served in the army.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The descendants of Benjamin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GENESIS 46:21; 1 CHRONICLES 8:1–40; 1 CHRONICLES 7:10–11"> Some of the sons of Benjamin were Bela, Beker, and Jediael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 17:17–18; 1 CHRONICLES 21:1–5"> Bela’s five sons were Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, and Iri. They were all leaders of clans. In the records of the clans descended from Bela there were names of 22,034 men who served in the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Beker’s sons were Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> In the records of the clans descended from Beker were the names of 20,200 men and leaders of the clans who served in the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 3:15–30"> Jediael’s son was Bilhan. Bilhan’s sons were Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Kenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 17:13–19"> They were all leaders of clans descended from them. There were 17,200 of them who served in the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:38–39; GENESIS 46:21; 1 CHRONICLES 7:15"> Shuppim and Huppim were descendants of Ir. One of the descendants of Aher was Hushim.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">

## Manasseh’s descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:30–42; JUDGES 5:14; DEUTERONOMY 3:13–15"> This is a list of Manasseh’s descendants. Manasseh had a slave wife who was from Syria. She was the mother of Asriel and Makir. Makir was the father of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:33; NUMBERS 36:1–12; NUMBERS 27:1–11"> Makir had two wives. They were from the clans of Huppim and Shuppim. One of Makir’s wives was named Maacah. Another descendant of Makir was Zelophehad. Zelophehad had no sons; he had only daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Makir’s wife Maacah gave birth to two sons whom she named Peresh and Sheresh. Sheresh’s sons were Ulam and Rakem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 12:11"> Ulam’s son was Bedan. All those men were considered to be descendants of Gilead, who was the son of Makir and grandson of Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JUDGES 6:24; JUDGES 6:11; JUDGES 8:2"> Makir’s sister name was Hammoleketh who was the mother of Ishdod, Abiezer, and Mahlah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> One son of Gilead was Shemida, whose sons were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Descendants of Ephraim

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="NUMBERS 26:35–36"> This is a list of the descendants of Ephraim. One son of Ephraim’s was Shuthelah. Shuthelah’s son was Bered. Bered’s son was Tahath. Tahath’s son was Eleadah. Eleadah’s son was also named Tahath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Tahath’s son was Zabad. Zabad’s son was Shuthelah. Ephraim’s other sons, Ezer and Elead, went to Gath city to steal some cows and sheep. But they were both killed by some of the men from that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOB 2:11; GENESIS 37:34"> Their father Ephraim cried/mourned for them for many days, and his family came to comfort him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 23:5"> Then he and his wife had sex &#91;EUP&#93; again, and she became pregnant and gave birth to a son. Ephraim named him Beriah which resembles the word ‘trouble’, because of the trouble that his family had experienced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOSHUA 16:3; JOSHUA 16:5; 2 CHRONICLES 8:5"> Ephraim’s daughter was Sheerah. Her workers built three towns: Lower Beth-Horon, Upper Beth-Horon, and Uzzen-Sheerah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Another son of Ephraim was Rephah. Rephah’s son was Resheph. Resheph’s son was Telah. Telah’s son was Tahan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Tahan’s son was Ladan. Ladan’s son was Ammihud. Ammihud’s son was Elishama.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:8; NUMBERS 13:16; EXODUS 17:9–14"> Elishama’s son was Nun. Nun’s son was Joshua, the man who led the Israelis after Moses died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 28:19; JOSHUA 16:2–3; 1 CHRONICLES 6:66–67"> This is a list of the cities and areas where the descendants of Ephraim lived: Bethel and the nearby villages; Naaran to the east; Gezer to the west and the nearby villages; and Shechem and the nearby villages. Those villages extended north as far as Ayyah and the nearby villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JUDGES 1:22–29; ZECHARIAH 12:11; 2 KINGS 9:27"> Along the border of the area where the descendants of Manasseh lived were these towns: Beth-Shan, Taanach, Megiddo, and Dor, and the nearby villages.<br />The people who lived in all those places were descendants of Jacob’s son Joseph.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The descendants of Asher

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 46:17; NUMBERS 26:44–46"> Asher’s sons were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah. Their sister was Serah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Beriah’s sons were Heber and Malkiel. Malkiel was the father of Birzaith</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Heber was the father of Japhlet, Shomer, and Hotham. Their sister was Shua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Japhlet’s sons were Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Japhlet’s younger brother was Shomer. Shomer’s sons were Rohgah, Hubbah, and Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Shomer’s younger brother was Hotham (OR, Helem). Hotham’s sons were Zophah, Imna, Shelesh, and Amal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Zophah’s sons were Suah, Harnepher, Shual, Beri, Imrah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran whose other name was Jether, and Beera.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Jether’s sons were Jephunneh, Pispah, and Ara.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Another descendant of Asher was Ulla, whose sons were Arah, Hanniel, and Rizia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 21:1–5; 2 SAMUEL 24:1–9"> All those men were descendants of Asher, and they were all leaders of their clans. They were brave warriors and excellent leaders. In the record of the clans that are descended from Asher are the names of 26,000 men who served in the army.</span>
</div>
</div>
