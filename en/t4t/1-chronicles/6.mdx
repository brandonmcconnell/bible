## The descendants of Levi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 46:11; EXODUS 6:16; NUMBERS 26:57"> Levi’s sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 6:18; EXODUS 6:21–24; 1 CHRONICLES 23:12"> Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EXODUS 6:20; LEVITICUS 10:1; LEVITICUS 10:12"> Amram’s children were Miriam and her younger brothers Aaron and Moses. Aaron’s sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:50; EXODUS 6:25; Ezra 7:1–5"> Eleazar was the father of Phinehas. Phinehas was the father of Abishua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Abishua was the father of Bukki. Bukki was the father of Uzzi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Uzzi was the father of Zerahiah. Zerahiah was the father of Meraioth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Meraioth was the father of Amariah. Amariah was the father of Ahitub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:17; 2 SAMUEL 15:27; 1 KINGS 1:44"> Ahitub was the father of Zadok. Zadok was the father of Ahimaaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Ahimaaz was the father of Azariah. Azariah was the father of Johanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 3:4; 2 CHRONICLES 3:1; 1 KINGS 6:1–7"> Johanan was the father of Azariah. Azariah was a priest in the temple that Solomon commanded to be built in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezra 7:3"> Azariah was the father of Amariah. Amariah was the father of Ahitub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 11:11; 1 CHRONICLES 9:11"> Ahitub was the father of Zadok. Zadok was the father of Shallum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:8; 2 KINGS 22:12–14; 2 KINGS 22:4"> Shallum was the father of Hilkiah. Hilkiah was the father of Azariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:18; Nehemiah 11:11; ZECHARIAH 6:11"> Azariah was the father of Seraiah. Seraiah was the father of Jehozadak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 2:2; 2 KINGS 25:18; Haggai 1:1"> Jehozadak was forced to leave his home when Yahweh sent King Nebuchadnezzar’s army &#91;MTY&#93; to capture many people in Jerusalem and other places in Judah and compel them to go to Babylonia.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="EXODUS 6:16; 1 CHRONICLES 6:1"> Levi’s sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:7; NUMBERS 3:18; NUMBERS 3:21"> Gershon’s sons were Libni and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:12; 1 CHRONICLES 6:2–3"> Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:21; 1 CHRONICLES 24:26; EXODUS 6:19"> Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi.<br />Here is a list of the descendants of Levi, who became leaders of their clans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:43"> Gershon’s oldest son was Libni. Libni’s son was Jehath. Jehath’s son was Zimmah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Zimmah’s son was Joah. Joah’s son was Iddo. Iddo’s son was Zerah. Zerah’s son was Jeatherai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 6:24; 1 CHRONICLES 6:18; 1 CHRONICLES 6:2"> Kohath’s other son was Amminadab. Amminadab’s son was Korah. Korah’s son was Assir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Assir’s son was Elkanah. Elkanah’s son was Ebiasaph. Ebiasaph’s son was Assir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Assir’s son was Tahath. Tahath’s son was Uriel. Uriel’s son was Uzziah. Uzziah’s son was Shaul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 6:24; 1 CHRONICLES 6:35–36"> Elkanah’s other sons were Amasai and Ahimoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 1:1; 1 CHRONICLES 6:35"> Ahimoth’s son was Elkanah. Elkanah’s son was Zophai. Zophai’s son was Nahath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 1:1; 1 SAMUEL 1:19–20"> Nahath’s son was Eliab. Eliab’s son was Jeroham. Jeroham’s son was Elkanah. Elkanah’s son was Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:33; 1 SAMUEL 8:2"> Samuel’s oldest son was Joel; his other son was Abijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:19; NUMBERS 3:33"> Merari’s oldest son was Mahli. Mahli’s son was Libni. Libni’s son was Shimei. Shimei’s son was Uzzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Uzzah’s son was Shimea. Shimea’s son was Haggiah. Haggiah’s son was Asaiah.</span>

## The temple musicians

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:17; 1 CHRONICLES 15:25–1; 1 CHRONICLES 15:16–22"> After the Sacred Chest was brought to Jerusalem, King David appointed some of the men who were descendants of Levi to be in charge of the music in the Sacred Tent where the people worshiped Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="PSALMS 135:1–3; 1 KINGS 8:6–13; Ezra 6:18"> Those musicians first sang and played their instruments in the Sacred Tent, which was also called the Tent of Meeting, and they continued to do that until Solomon’s workers built the temple of Yahweh in Jerusalem. In all their work, they obeyed the instructions that David had given them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:28; 1 CHRONICLES 15:17; 1 CHRONICLES 25:1–5"> Here is a list of the musicians and their sons: From Kohath’s descendants there was Heman, the leader of the singers. Heman was the son of Joel. Joel was the son of Samuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Samuel was the son of Elkanah. Elkanah was the son of Jeroham. Jeroham was the son of Eliel. Eliel was the son of Toah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Toah was the son of Zuph. Zuph was the son of Elkanah. Elkanah was the son of Mahath. Mahath was the son of Amasai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Amasai was the son of another man whose name was Elkanah. Elkanah was the son of Joel. Joel was the son of Azariah. Azariah was the son of Zephaniah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="PSALMS 45:1; NUMBERS 26:10–11; EXODUS 6:21–24"> Zephaniah was the son of Tahath. Tahath was the son of Assir. Assir was the son of Ebiasaph. Ebiasaph was the son of Korah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="NUMBERS 3:19; 1 CHRONICLES 16:1"> Korah was the son of Izhar. Izhar was the son of Kohath. Kohath was the son of Levi. Levi was the son of Jacob.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:44; Ezra 2:41; PSALMS 75:1"> Heman’s helper was Asaph. His group of singers stood at the right side of Heman. Asaph was the son of Berekiah. Berekiah was the son of Shimea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Shimea was the son of Michael. Michael was the son of Baaseiah. Baaseiah was the son of Malkijah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Malkijah was the son of Ethni. Ethni was the son of Zerah. Zerah was the son of Adaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Adaiah was the son of Ethan. Ethan was the son of Zimmah. Zimmah was the son of Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="EXODUS 6:16; 1 CHRONICLES 6:16–17; GENESIS 46:11"> Shimei was the son of Jahath. Jahath was the son of Gershon, and Gershon was the son of Levi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="PSALMS 89:1; 1 CHRONICLES 15:17; 1 CHRONICLES 25:1"> A group of singers from Merari’s family helped Heman and Asaph. They stood to the left of Heman. The leader of this group was Ethan, the son of Kishi. Kishi was the son of Abdi. Abdi was the son of Malluch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Malluch was the son of Hashabiah. Hashabiah was the son of Amaziah. Amaziah was the son of Hilkiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> Hilkiah was the son of Amzi. Amzi was the son of Bani. Bani was the son of Shemer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:7; EXODUS 6:19; 1 CHRONICLES 23:21"> Shemer was the son of Mahli. Mahli was the son of Mushi. Mushi was the son of Merari, and Merari was the son of Levi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:2; NUMBERS 3:1–4; NUMBERS 18:1–32"> The other descendants of Levi were appointed to do other work in the sacred tent, the place where the people worshiped Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LEVITICUS 4:20; EXODUS 27:1–8; EXODUS 30:1–7"> Aaron and his descendants were the ones who placed on the altar the sacrifices that were to be burned completely, and they burned incense on another altar. Those sacrifices were in order that Yahweh would no longer be angry with the people of Israel for having sinned. Those men also did other work in the Very Holy Place in the sacred tent, obeying the instructions that Moses, who served God well, had given to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:16; 1 CHRONICLES 24:1; NUMBERS 20:26–28"> These were the descendants of Aaron: Aaron’s son was Eleazar. Eleazar’s son was Phinehas. Phinehas’s son was Abishua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Abishua’s son was Bukki. Bukki’s son was Uzzi. Uzzi’s son was Zerahiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> Zerahiah’s son was Meraioth. Meraioth’s son was Amariah. Amariah’s son was Ahitub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:17; EZEKIEL 44:15; 2 SAMUEL 15:35–36"> Ahitub’s son was Zadok, and Zadok’s son was Ahimaaz.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Land for the descendants of Levi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="GENESIS 25:16; JOSHUA 21:10; JOSHUA 21:3–8"> Here is a list of the places where Aaron’s descendants lived. Those who were descendants of Kohath were the first group to be allotted cities to live in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="JOSHUA 14:13; JOSHUA 21:11–13; JOSHUA 15:13"> They were allotted Hebron city in Judah and the pastureland around the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:13; JOSHUA 14:13"> but the fields farther from the city and the villages near the city were given to Caleb, the son of Jephunneh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:13–14; JOSHUA 15:48; JOSHUA 10:29"> The descendants of Aaron who were descendants of Kohath were allotted Hebron, one of the cities to which people could flee and be protected if they accidentally killed someone. They also were allotted the towns and pastureland near Libnah, Jattir, Eshtemoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:3; JOSHUA 15:49; JOSHUA 12:13"> Hilen, Debir,</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:16; JOSHUA 15:42; 1 SAMUEL 6:12–19"> Ashan, Juttah, and Beth-Shemesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:1; 1 KINGS 2:26; JEREMIAH 11:23"> They were also allotted Gibeon, Geba, Alemeth, and Anathoth cities from the tribe of Benjamin.<br />Altogether, these clans descended from Kohath were allotted 13 towns.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:4–5; 1 CHRONICLES 6:18; JOSHUA 21:20–26"> The other clans descended from Kohath were allotted ten towns from the clans of the tribe of Manasseh that lived west of the Jordan River.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:27–33; 1 CHRONICLES 6:71–76; EXODUS 2:22"> The descendants of Gershon were allotted 13 cities and towns from the tribes of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and the part of the tribe of Manasseh that lived in the Bashan region on the east side of the Jordan River.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:7; JOSHUA 21:34–40; NUMBERS 3:20"> The descendants of Merari were allotted twelve cities and towns from the tribes of Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:41–42; JOSHUA 21:3; NUMBERS 35:1–8"> The leaders of Israel allotted those towns and the nearby pasturelands to the descendants of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:57–60"> They also allotted to them the cities and towns from the tribes of Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin that were listed previously.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:20–26"> Some of the descendants of Kohath were allotted towns from the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:21; GENESIS 33:18; GENESIS 35:4"> They were allotted Shechem, which was one of the cities to which people could flee and be protected if they accidentally killed someone, along with the nearby pastureland in the hills of Ephraim. They were also allotted these towns and pastureland near them: Gezer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:22; JOSHUA 16:5; 1 SAMUEL 13:18"> Jokmeam, Beth-Horon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:12; JOSHUA 21:24–25"> Aijalon, and Gath-Rimmon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:25; JOSHUA 17:11"> The other descendants of Kohath were allotted Aner and Bileam towns and the nearby pastureland from the part of the tribe of Manasseh that lived west of the Jordan River.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:27; JOSHUA 9:10; JOSHUA 20:8"> The descendants of Gershon, who were part of the tribe of Manasseh, lived east of the Jordan River. They were allotted the cities and towns and pastureland near them: Golan in the Bashan and Ashtaroth regions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v72" data-v="72" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:28–29; JOSHUA 21:32; JOSHUA 19:37"> From the tribe of Issachar they were allotted cities and towns and pastureland near Kedesh, Daberath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v73" data-v="73"> Ramoth, and Anem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v74" data-v="74" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:26; JOSHUA 21:30"> From the tribe of Asher they were allotted cities and towns and pastureland near Mashal, Abdon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v75" data-v="75" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:31"> Hukok, and Rehob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v76" data-v="76" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:32; JOSHUA 19:37; JUDGES 4:6"> And from the tribe of Naphtali they were allotted cities and towns and pastureland near Kedesh in the Galilee region, and Hammon and Kiriathaim towns.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v77" data-v="77" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:34–35"> The other descendants of Levi, those descended from Merari, were allotted towns and pasturelands from the tribe of Zebulun near Jokneam, Kartah, Rimmono, and Tabor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v78" data-v="78" data-xref="JOSHUA 20:8; JOSHUA 21:36–37; DEUTERONOMY 4:41–43"> From the tribe of Reuben they were allotted cities and towns and pastureland near Bezer in the desert, Jahzah, Kedemoth, and Mephaath. The tribe of Reuben lived east of the Jordan River, across from Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v80" data-v="80" data-xref="GENESIS 32:2; 2 KINGS 9:1; JOSHUA 21:38–39"> From the tribe of Gad, they were allotted cities and towns and pastureland near Ramoth in the Gilead region, the cities of Mahanaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v81" data-v="81" data-xref="JOSHUA 21:39; NUMBERS 21:32; NUMBERS 32:3"> Heshbon, and Jazer.</span>
</div>
