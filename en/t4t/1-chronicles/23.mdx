## The duties of the descendants of Levi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 29:28; 1 CHRONICLES 28:5; 1 KINGS 1:33–39"> David was a very old man &#91;DOU&#93; when he appointed his son Solomon to be the next king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 24:1; 2 CHRONICLES 34:29–30; 1 CHRONICLES 13:1"> David gathered the leaders of Israel and the priests and other descendants of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:24; NUMBERS 4:2–49"> He commanded some of his officials to count the descendants of Levi who were at least thirty years old, and they found out that there were 38,000 of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 19:8; DEUTERONOMY 16:18; 1 CHRONICLES 26:29–31"> Then David said, “From those 38,000 men, I want 24,000 of them to supervise the work at the temple of Yahweh, and I want 6,000 of them to be officials and judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 29:25–26; 2 CHRONICLES 8:14; PSALMS 87:7"> I want 4,000 to be guards at the gates, and 4,000 to praise Yahweh, using the musical instruments that I have provided for them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 8:14; 1 CHRONICLES 6:1; 1 CHRONICLES 6:16"> David divided the descendants of Levi into three family groups; each group consisted of men who were descendants of one of the three sons of Levi—Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The descendants of Gershon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 26:21; EXODUS 6:17; 1 CHRONICLES 6:17–20"> From the descendants of Gershon there were Ladan and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:33–34; 1 CHRONICLES 15:20–21; 1 CHRONICLES 15:7"> There were three sons of Ladan: Jehiel was the oldest, and his younger brothers Zetham and Joel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> There were three of the sons of Shimei: Shelomoth, Haziel, and Haran. They were all leaders of the clans of Ladan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> There were four men who were other sons of Shimei:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Jahath, who was his oldest son, and his younger brothers Ziza, Jeush, and Beriah. Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they were counted as though they were one family.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The descendants of Kohath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EXODUS 6:18; NUMBERS 3:27; NUMBERS 26:58"> Kohath had four sons: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:5; EXODUS 6:20; 1 SAMUEL 2:28"> There were two sons of Amram: Aaron and Moses. Aaron and his descendants were set apart to dedicate the very holy things, to offer sacrifices to Yahweh, to serve in his presence, and to declare to the people what Yahweh &#91;MTY&#93; would do to bless them. They were to do those things forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 33:1; PSALMS 90:1; 1 CHRONICLES 26:23–25"> The sons of Moses, the man who served God well, were counted as part of the tribe of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 18:3–4; EXODUS 2:22; EXODUS 4:20"> The sons of Moses were Gershom and Eliezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 26:24; 1 CHRONICLES 25:20; 1 CHRONICLES 24:20"> The oldest son of Gershom was Shubael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 26:25"> Rehabiah was the oldest son of Eliezer. Eliezer had no other sons, but Rehabiah had many sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 26:26; 1 CHRONICLES 24:22"> The oldest son of Izhar was Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 24:23; 1 CHRONICLES 15:9; 1 CHRONICLES 23:12"> Hebron had four sons. Jeriah was his oldest son, and his younger brothers were Amariah, Jahaziel, and Jekameam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Uzziel had two sons. Micah was the oldest son, and his younger brother was Isshiah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The descendants of Merari

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 6:19; 1 CHRONICLES 6:20; 1 CHRONICLES 24:26–30"> Merari had two sons: Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli were Eleazar and Kish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 24:28; NUMBERS 36:6–8"> Eleazar had no sons; he had only daughters. Their cousins, the sons of Kish, married them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 24:30"> The three sons of Mushi were Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="NUMBERS 10:17; NUMBERS 10:21; 1 CHRONICLES 23:3"> Those were the descendants of Levi, whose names were listed according to their families/clans. They were chosen for special jobs by ◄casting lots/throwing small marked stones►. Each person who was at least 20 years old was listed. They all worked in the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 132:13–14; 1 CHRONICLES 22:18; 2 SAMUEL 7:11"> David had said previously, “Yahweh, the God to whom we Israeli people belong, has enabled us to have peace, and he has come to live in Jerusalem forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:5; NUMBERS 7:9; NUMBERS 4:15"> Therefore, the descendants of Levi no longer need to carry the Sacred Tent and the items used in the work there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:24; PSALMS 72:20; 2 SAMUEL 23:1"> Obeying the final instructions of David before he died, instructions for doing this work at the temple, they counted only the descendants of Levi who were at least 20 years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:3–6; 2 CHRONICLES 31:11; 2 CHRONICLES 35:11–14"> The work of those descendants of Levi was to assist the descendants of Aaron in their work in the temple of Yahweh: To be in charge of the temple courtyards and the side rooms, the ceremonies for purifying all the sacred things, and to do other work at the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:35–36; EXODUS 25:30; LEVITICUS 6:20–23"> They were also in charge of the sacred loaves of bread that were placed each week on the table in the temple, the flour for the grain offerings, the wafers that were made without yeast, and the measuring the ingredients and mixing them and baking that bread and those wafers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="PSALMS 92:1–3; Ezra 3:10–11; 1 CHRONICLES 9:33"> They were also told to stand every morning at the temple and thank Yahweh and praise him. They were also required to do the same thing every evening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 1:13–14; NUMBERS 28:1–29; NUMBERS 10:10"> And they were to do the same thing whenever offerings that were to be completely burned on the altar were presented/offered to Yahweh on Sabbath days and during the new moon celebrations and other religious festivals. They were told how many of them should be there and what they should do each time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="NUMBERS 1:53; NUMBERS 3:38; NUMBERS 3:6–9"> So the descendants of Levi did the work that was assigned to them by their fellow Israelis who were descendants of Aaron. They did that work in the area surrounding the Sacred Tent, and in the Sacred Tent, and later at the temple.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
