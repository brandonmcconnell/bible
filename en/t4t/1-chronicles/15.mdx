## They brought the Sacred Chest to Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 16:1; 1 CHRONICLES 17:1–5; PSALMS 132:5"> David commanded his workers to build some houses for him in Jerusalem. He also told them to set up a tent in which to put the Sacred Chest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 10:8; DEUTERONOMY 31:9; NUMBERS 18:1–8"> He said, “Only the descendants of Levi are permitted to carry God’s Sacred Chest, because they are the ones whom God chose to carry it and to serve him forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:1; 1 CHRONICLES 13:5; 1 CHRONICLES 15:1"> David summoned all the people of Israel to come to Jerusalem. He wanted the Sacred Chest of Yahweh to be put in the place that he had made for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 6:16–22; 1 CHRONICLES 12:26–28; 1 CHRONICLES 6:16–30"> He summoned the descendants of Aaron, who was the first Supreme Priest, and the other descendants of Levi.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:22–24"> 120 descendants of Kohath, who was Levi’s second son, came, with Uriel their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:29–30"> There were 220 descendants of Merari, Levi’s third son, who came, with Asaiah their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 15:11; 1 CHRONICLES 23:8"> There were 130 descendants of Gershon, Levi’s first son, who came, with Joel their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 6:22"> There were 200 people from Elizaphan’s clan who came, with Shemaiah their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 6:18; 1 CHRONICLES 23:19; 1 CHRONICLES 26:30–31"> There were 80 people from Hebron’s clan who came, with Eliel their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 6:18; 1 CHRONICLES 6:18; 1 CHRONICLES 23:12"> And there were 112 people from Uzziel’s clan who came, with Amminadab their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 12:28; 1 KINGS 2:35; 1 SAMUEL 22:20–23"> David summoned the priests Zadok and Abiathar and these other descendants of Levi: Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:6; EXODUS 19:14–15; 1 CHRONICLES 15:14"> David said to them, “You are the leaders of the clans descended from Levi. You and the other descendants of Levi must purify yourselves, in order to be able to do this special work for Yahweh. You must bring the Sacred Chest of Yahweh, the God of us Israelis, up to the place that I have made for it here in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:40; 1 JOHN 1:8–10"> The first time that we tried to bring it, we did not ask Yahweh how we should carry it &#123;it should be carried&#125;. You descendants of Levi were not the ones who carried it, so Yahweh our God punished us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 29:15; JOEL 2:16–17; 2 CHRONICLES 29:34"> Then the priests and the other descendants of Levi performed the rituals to purify themselves, in order that it would be proper for them to do the work of carrying the Sacred Chest of Yahweh, the God of us Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 4:15; NUMBERS 7:9; 2 CHRONICLES 5:9"> The descendants of Levi inserted the poles into the rings on the Sacred Chest so that with them, they could carry the Sacred Chest on their shoulders, like Moses had commanded, and like Yahweh had said that they should.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 13:8; 2 CHRONICLES 5:13; 1 CHRONICLES 16:42"> David told the leaders of the descendants of Levi to appoint some of their relatives to sing joyful songs and play lyres, harps, and cymbals while they were carrying the Sacred Chest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 6:39; 1 CHRONICLES 6:44; 1 CHRONICLES 6:33"> So they appointed Heman and his relatives Asaph and Ethan. Heman was the son of Joel; Asaph was the son of Berekiah. Ethan, a descendant of Merari, was Kushaiah’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 26:4; 1 CHRONICLES 26:8; 1 CHRONICLES 13:14"> There was also another group of descendants of Levi who were appointed: Zechariah, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, and two of the Sacred Tent gatekeepers, Obed-Edom and Jeiel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 25:6; 1 CHRONICLES 16:5; 1 CHRONICLES 13:8"> Heman, Asaph and Ethan sang, and also played bronze cymbals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 46:1–11"> Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah played lyres.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 10:5; 1 CHRONICLES 25:6–7; PSALMS 81:1–2"> Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-Edom, Jeiel and Azaziah played harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 15:27; 1 CHRONICLES 15:16; 1 CHRONICLES 25:7–8"> Kenaniah, the leader of the descendants of Levi, directed the singing because he was very skilled at doing that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 84:10; 2 KINGS 22:4; 1 CHRONICLES 9:21–23"> Berekiah and Elkanah were two of the men who guarded the Sacred Chest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 16:6; NUMBERS 10:8; 1 CHRONICLES 15:28"> The priests Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer were appointed to blow trumpets in front of the Sacred Chest. Obed-Edom and Jehiah also guarded the Sacred Chest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:12–23; MICAH 5:2; PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> David and the Israeli leaders and the officers who commanded 1,000 soldiers went to bring the Sacred Chest back from Obed-Edom’s house. They were very joyful as they brought it to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="NUMBERS 23:29; 1 CHRONICLES 29:14; PSALMS 66:13–15"> God helped the descendants of Levi who carried the Sacred Chest of Yahweh; therefore David and the leaders sacrificed seven bulls and seven ◄rams/male sheep► to thank him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 2:18; 2 SAMUEL 6:14"> All the descendants of Levi who carried the Sacred Chest, all the singers, and Kenaniah, the man who directed those who sang, wore robes of fine white linen and sacred vests made of fine linen. David also wore a sacred vest made of white linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 13:8; 1 CHRONICLES 15:16; 2 CHRONICLES 5:12–13"> So all of the Israeli people joined in bringing the Sacred Chest up to Jerusalem. They shouted joyfully, while the musicians blew horns and trumpets, and played cymbals, lyres, and harps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:20–23; 2 SAMUEL 6:16; DEUTERONOMY 31:26"> While they were bringing the Sacred Chest into Jerusalem, Saul’s daughter Michal watched them, looking out of a window. When she saw King David dancing and celebrating, she despised him.</span>
</div>
