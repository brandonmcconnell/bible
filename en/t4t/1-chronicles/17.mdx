## Yahweh’s promise to David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 6:7–9; 1 CHRONICLES 15:1; 1 CHRONICLES 17:5"> After David began to live in his palace, he said to the prophet Nathan, “It does not seem right that I am here living in a palace made of cedar wood, but Yahweh’s Sacred Chest is kept inside a tent!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 28:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 13:9; ZECHARIAH 8:23"> Nathan replied to David, “Whatever you are thinking about doing, do it, because God is with/guiding you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 3:7; NUMBERS 12:6; Isaiah 30:21"> But that night God spoke &#91;MTY&#93; to Nathan. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 28:2–3; Isaiah 55:8–9; 2 CHRONICLES 6:8–9"> “Go and tell my servant David that this is what I, Yahweh, am saying to him: ‘You are not the one who should build a temple for me to live in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:6; EXODUS 40:2–3; 1 KINGS 8:16"> I have not lived in any building, from the day that I brought the people of Israel up out of Egypt until now. Instead, I have lived in a tent, moving from one place to another with the Israelis when they moved to other places &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:7; NUMBERS 10:33–36; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:16"> Wherever I went with all the Israelis as they traveled, I never &#91;RHQ&#93; said to any of their leaders whom I appointed to lead &#91;MET&#93; them, “Why have you not built me a temple made of cedar wood?”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:21; EXODUS 3:1–10; 1 SAMUEL 17:15"> Therefore, this is what you should say to my servant David: ‘I, the Almighty Commander of the armies of angels, took you from a pasture where you were taking care of sheep, and I appointed you to be the ruler of my Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 18:14; 1 CHRONICLES 17:2; PSALMS 113:7–8"> I have ◄been with/helped► you wherever you have gone, and I have gotten rid of all your enemies as you advanced. And now I will cause you to become very famous, as well-known as the names of the greatest men who have ever lived on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="EXODUS 2:23; EZEKIEL 28:4; EPHESIANS 5:6"> Formerly, during the time that I appointed leaders for my Israeli people, many violent groups oppressed them. But this will not happen any more. I have chosen a place where my Israeli people can live peacefully and no one will disturb them any more. I will give not allow them to be attacked by their enemies. And I will defeat all your enemies. I declare to you that I, Yahweh, will enable your descendants to rule after you die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 132:11; 1 KINGS 1:21; 1 CHRONICLES 28:5"> When your life ends &#91;EUP&#93;, and you die and go to be with your ancestors who have died, I will appoint one of your sons to become king, and I will enable his kingdom to ◄be strong/resist all their enemies►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 KINGS 5:5; DANIEL 2:44; Isaiah 9:7"> He is the one who will arrange for a temple to be built for me. And I will enable his descendants to be kings &#91;MTY&#93; of Israel forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:5; Isaiah 55:3; 1 CHRONICLES 17:12"> I will be like a father to him, and it will be as though he is a son to me. I stopped loving Saul, the one who was the king before you became king, but I will never stop loving your son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 89:36; LUKE 1:32–33; PSALMS 72:17"> I will cause him to rule over my people &#91;MTY&#93;, and his kingdom will endure forever &#91;DOU&#93;.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:28; ACTS 20:27; 2 SAMUEL 7:17"> So Nathan reported to David everything that Yahweh had revealed to him.</span>

## David’s prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:18; GENESIS 32:10; EPHESIANS 3:8"> Then David went into the Sacred Tent and sat in the presence of Yahweh, and prayed this: “Yahweh my God, I am certainly not &#91;RHQ&#93; worthy for you to have done all these things for me, and my family is not worthy, either.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 17:11–15; PHILIPPIANS 2:8–11; Isaiah 49:6"> “And O God, now, in addition to everything else, you have spoken about what will happen to my descendants in the future for many generations. Yahweh my God, you have acted toward me as though I was the most important man on the earth!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JOHN 21:17; 1 SAMUEL 2:30; 2 SAMUEL 7:20–24"> “What more can I, David, say to you for honoring me? Although you know very well what I am like,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 37:35; 2 SAMUEL 7:21; DANIEL 9:17"> Yahweh, for my sake and because it is what you wanted to do, you have done these great things for me, and you have revealed to me these things that you are promising to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; JEREMIAH 10:6–7; EPHESIANS 3:20"> “Yahweh, you are great. There is no one like you. Only you are God, which is what we have always heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:9–10; Isaiah 63:9; Isaiah 48:9"> And there is no nation in the world like Israel &#91;RHQ&#93;. Israel is the only nation on the earth whose people you rescued. You performed great and awesome miracles, rescuing our ancestors from being slaves in Egypt, and expelling the people of other people-groups who were in Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 19:5–6; 1 PETER 2:9; ROMANS 11:1–12"> You have caused us your Israeli people to belong to you forever, and you, Yahweh, have become our God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 119:49; JEREMIAH 11:5; LUKE 1:38"> “And now Yahweh, I pray that you will cause the things that you have promised to do for me and my descendants &#91;MTY&#93; to be fulfilled forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:13; JEREMIAH 31:1; REVELATION 21:3"> When that happens, you &#91;MTY&#93; will be famous forever. And people will exclaim, ‘The Almighty Commander of the armies of angels, is the God who rules Israel!’ And you will cause that forever there will be descendants &#91;MTY&#93; of mine who will rule.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 9:15; EZEKIEL 36:37; PSALMS 10:17"> “You, my God, have revealed to me that you will cause some of my descendants to become kings. So I am brave enough to pray like this to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="EXODUS 34:6–7; HEBREWS 6:18; TITUS 1:2"> Yahweh, you are God! You have promised to do these good things for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:3; ROMANS 11:29; PSALMS 72:17"> And now you, Yahweh, have promised to bless my descendants &#91;MTY&#93;, in order that they will continue to rule forever. That will happen because you, Yahweh, are the one who has blessed them, and you will keep blessing them forever.”</span>
</div>
