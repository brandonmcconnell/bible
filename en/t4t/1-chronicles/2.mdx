<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 32:28; GENESIS 35:22–26; REVELATION 7:5–8"> The sons of Jacob were Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun,</span>

## The descendants of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 38:2–10; GENESIS 46:12; NUMBERS 26:19"> Judah’s sons were Er, Onan, and Shelah. Their mother was the daughter of Shua from the Canaan people-group. When Judah’s oldest son Er grew up, he did something that Yahweh considered to be very wicked, so Yahweh caused him to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:3; GENESIS 38:13–30; Nehemiah 11:4"> Judah and his daughter-in-law Tamar had twin boys named Perez and Zerah. So altogether Judah had five sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 46:12; RUTH 4:18; NUMBERS 26:21"> The sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 4:31; JOSHUA 7:17–18; JOSHUA 7:1"> Zerah had five sons: Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Darda (OR, Dara).</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 6:18; DEUTERONOMY 7:26; JOSHUA 7:25"> One of the sons of Zimri was Carmi. Carmi’s son Achan (OR, Achar) caused the people of Israel to experience much trouble, because he stole some of the things that had been devoted to Yahweh to be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Ethan’s son was Azariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:42; MATTHEW 1:3; RUTH 4:19"> Hezron’s sons were Jerahmeel, Ram, and Caleb (OR, Chelubai).</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:4; NUMBERS 1:7; NUMBERS 2:3"> Ram was the father of Amminadab, and Amminadab was the father of Nahshon. Nahshon was a leader of the tribe of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="RUTH 4:21; MATTHEW 1:4–5; LUKE 3:32"> Nahshon was the father of Salmon, who was the father of Boaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ROMANS 15:12; 1 CHRONICLES 10:14; Isaiah 11:10"> Boaz was the father of Obed, and Obed was the father of Jesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:13; 1 SAMUEL 17:28; 1 CHRONICLES 20:7"> Jesse’s oldest son was Eliab. His other sons were Abinadab, Shimea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Nethanel, Raddai,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:10–11; 1 SAMUEL 17:12–14"> Ozem, and the youngest was David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:6; 2 SAMUEL 3:39; 2 SAMUEL 2:18–23"> Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. Zeruiah’s three sons were Abishai, Joab, and Asahel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 17:25; 1 KINGS 2:32; 2 SAMUEL 20:4–12"> Abigail’s husband was Jether, a descendant of Ishmael, and their son was Amasa.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Descendants of Hezron

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Hezron’s younger son Caleb had two wives. One of them, Azubah, gave birth to three sons: Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:50; 1 CHRONICLES 2:24; 1 CHRONICLES 4:4"> When Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath. Their son was Hur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EXODUS 31:2; EXODUS 36:1–2; EXODUS 37:1"> Hur was the father of Uri, and Uri was the father of Bezalel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 27:1; DEUTERONOMY 3:15; NUMBERS 26:29"> When Hezron was 60 years old, he married &#91;EUP&#93; Makir’s daughter, who was the sister of Gilead. The son of Hezron and Makir was Segub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:30; DEUTERONOMY 3:14; NUMBERS 32:41"> Segub was the father of Jair. Jair’s army controlled twenty-three cities in the region ruled by Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:30; DEUTERONOMY 3:14; NUMBERS 32:41–42"> But the armies of Geshur and Aram captured those towns controlled by Jair. They also captured Kenath city and the nearby towns; altogether they captured 60 towns. The people who lived there were all descendants of Makir, the father of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 4:5; 1 CHRONICLES 2:9; 1 SAMUEL 30:14"> Shortly after Hezron died in Caleb-Ephrath town, his widow Abijah had a son Asshur; Asshur started Tekoa town.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jerahmeel’s descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Hezron’s oldest son was Jerahmeel. His sons were Ram, Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahijah. Jerahmeel’s oldest son was Ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Jerahmeel had another wife named Atarah. Their son was Onam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> The sons of Ram, Jerahmeel’s oldest son, were Maaz, Jamin, and Eker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Onam’s sons were Shammai and Jada. Shammai’s sons were Nadab and Abishur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Abishur’s wife was Abihail. The sons of Abishur and Abihail were Ahban and Molid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Nadab’s sons were Seled and Appaim. Seled did not have any children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:34–35"> Appaim’s son was Ishi; Ishi’s son was Sheshan. One of Sheshan’s daughters was Ahlai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Shammai’s younger brother was Jada. Jada’s sons were Jether and Jonathan. Jether did not have any children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Jonathan’s sons were Peleth and Zaza. Those were the descendants of Jerahmeel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Sheshan did not have any sons; he had only daughters. He had a servant from Egypt whose name was Jarha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Sheshan allowed his daughter to marry Jarha, and their son was Attai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 11:41"> Attai was the father of Nathan. Nathan was the father of Zabad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Zabad was the father of Ephlal. Ephlal was the father of Obed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Obed was the father of Jehu. Jehu was the father of Azariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Azariah was the father of Helez. Helez was the father of Eleasah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Eleasah was the father of Sismai. Sismai was the father of Shallum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Shallum was the father of Jekamiah. And Jekamiah was the father of Elishama.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Caleb’s descendants

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:23–24; HEBREWS 12:23; ROMANS 8:29"> Jerahmeel’s younger brother was Caleb. Caleb’s oldest son was Mesha. Mesha was the father of Ziph. Ziph was the father of Mareshah. Mareshah was the father of Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> Hebron’s sons were Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Shema was the father of Raham. Raham was the father of Jorkeam. Rekem was the father of Shammai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:58"> Shammai was the father of Maon. Maon was the father of Beth-Zur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:18–19; 1 CHRONICLES 2:48"> Caleb had a slave wife who was named Ephah. Caleb and Ephah’s sons were Haran, Moza, and Gazez. Haran had a son whom he also named Gazez.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Ephah’s father was Jahdai. Jahdai was the father of six sons: Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="GENESIS 25:5–6; 1 CHRONICLES 2:46"> Caleb had another slave wife whose name was Maacah. Caleb and Maacah’s sons were Sheber, Tirhanah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:31; 1 CHRONICLES 2:42; Isaiah 10:31"> Shaaph, and Sheva. Shaaph was the father of Madmannah. Sheva was the father of Macbenah and Gibea. Caleb’s daughter was Acsah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 7:1; JOSHUA 15:60; 1 CHRONICLES 2:53"> These people were also descendants of Caleb: Caleb had another wife whose name was Ephrathah. Their oldest son was Hur. Hur’s sons were Shobal, Salma, and Hareph. Shobal started Kiriath-Jearim city. Salma started Bethlehem town. Hareph started Beth-Gader town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 4:2"> Shobal’s descendants were Haroeh, and half of the Manahath people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 23:38; JUDGES 13:25; JUDGES 13:2"> His descendants also included these clans that lived in Kiriath-Jearim: Ithri, Put, Shumath, and Mishra. The Zorath and Eshtaol clans were descendants of the Mishra clan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:26; Ezra 2:22; Nehemiah 12:28"> Salma’s descendants in Bethlehem were the Netophath clan, the Atroth-Beth-Joab clan, the other half of the Manahath clan, and the Zor clan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 KINGS 10:15; JUDGES 1:16; JEREMIAH 35:19"> Salma’s descendants also included the families at Jabez town who wrote and copied important documents. These were the Tirath clan, the Shimeath clan, and the Sucath clan. They were all from the Ken people-group who came from Hammath city, and who had married members of the family &#91;MTY&#93; of Rechab.</span>
</div>
</div>
