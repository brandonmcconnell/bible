## The sons of King David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:2–5; JOSHUA 15:56; 2 SAMUEL 13:1"> Six sons of King David were born in Hebron city. His oldest son was Amnon, whose mother Ahinoam was from Jezreel city. His next son was Daniel, whose mother was Abigail from Carmel city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 19:4–10; 2 SAMUEL 3:4; 1 KINGS 2:24–25"> His next son was Absalom, whose mother was Maacah, the daughter of Talmai, the king who ruled in Geshur town. His next son was Adonijah, whose mother was Haggith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:5"> The next son was Shephatiah, whose mother was Abital. His youngest son was Ithream, whose mother was Eglah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 2:11; 2 SAMUEL 5:4–5; 1 KINGS 2:11"> They were all born in Hebron, where David ruled for 7-1/2 years. After that, David ruled in Jerusalem for 33 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 11:3; 2 SAMUEL 12:24–25; 2 SAMUEL 12:1–15"> Many of David’s children were born in Jerusalem. Bathsheba, the daughter of Ammiel, gave birth to four of his sons: Shammua (OR, Shimea), Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 5:15; 1 CHRONICLES 14:5; 1 SAMUEL 14:5"> Nine other sons of David were also born there. They were Ibhar, Elishua (OR, Elishama), Eliphelet,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 5:15–16"> Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 14:7; 2 SAMUEL 5:14–16"> Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 13:1–20; 2 SAMUEL 5:13"> In addition to all those sons, David’s slave wives also gave birth to sons. David also had one daughter whose name was Tamar.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The kings of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:43; 1 KINGS 14:31–1; 1 KINGS 15:24"> Solomon’s son was King Rehoboam. Rehoboam’s son was King Abijah. Abijah’s son was King Asa. Asa’s son was King Jehoshaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 KINGS 8:24; 2 CHRONICLES 21:17; 2 KINGS 11:21"> Jehoshaphat’s son was King Jehoram (OR, Joram). Jehoram’s son was King Ahaziah. Ahaziah’s son was King Joash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 25:1; 2 KINGS 14:21; 2 KINGS 14:1"> Joash’s son was King Amaziah. Amaziah’s son was King Azariah. Azariah’s son was King Jotham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 KINGS 18:1; 2 KINGS 16:1; 2 CHRONICLES 29:1"> Jotham’s son was King Ahaz. Ahaz’s son was King Hezekiah. Hezekiah’s son was King Manasseh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 KINGS 23:30; 2 CHRONICLES 34:1; 2 KINGS 21:19"> Manasseh’s son was King Amon. Amon’s son was King Josiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 KINGS 23:34"> Josiah’s oldest son was Johanan. His other sons were Jehoiakim, Zedekiah, and Shallum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 KINGS 24:6; MATTHEW 1:11; 2 KINGS 24:17"> Jehoiakim’s sons were Jehoiachin (OR, Jeconiah) and Zedekiah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Other descendants of King David after the people of Judah were exiled

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezra 3:2; Ezra 5:2; MATTHEW 1:12"> King Jehoiachin was captured and taken to Babylon. His sons were Shealtiel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Malkiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezra 2:2; Ezra 3:2; Haggai 2:2"> Pedaiah’s sons were Zerubbabel and Shimei. Two of Zerubbabel’s sons were Meshullam and Hananiah, and their sister was Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Zerubbabel’s five other sons were Hashubah, Ohel, Berekiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-Hesed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:22"> The descendants of Hananiah were Pelatiah and Jeshaiah. Jeshaiah’s son was Rephaiah. Rephaiah’s son was Arnan. Arnan’s son was Obadiah. Obadiah’s son was Shecaniah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezra 8:2"> Shecaniah’s son was Shemaiah. Shemaiah’s five sons were Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Neariah’s three sons were Elioenai, Hizkiah, and Azrikam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Elioenai’s seven sons were Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah, and Anani.</span>
</div>
</div>
