## The death of King Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 31:1–13; 1 CHRONICLES 10:8; 2 SAMUEL 1:6"> The army of Philistia again fought against the Israelis. The Israeli soldiers ran away from them, and many Israelis were killed &#123;the soldiers of Philistia killed many Israelis&#125; on Gilboa Mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 9:39; EXODUS 20:5; 1 SAMUEL 14:6"> The soldiers of Philistia caught up with Saul and his sons, and they killed his sons Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 49:23–24; 1 SAMUEL 31:3–6; 2 SAMUEL 1:4–10"> The fighting was very fierce around Saul, and the ◄archers/men who shot arrows► shot Saul and wounded him severely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 31:4; JUDGES 9:54; 1 SAMUEL 17:26"> Saul said to the man who was carrying his weapons, “Take out your sword and kill me with it, in order that these heathen Philistines will not be able to injure me further and make fun of me while I am dying.” But the man who was carrying Saul’s weapons was terrified and refused to do that. So Saul took his own sword and fell on it and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When the man carrying his weapons saw that Saul was dead, he also threw himself on his own sword and died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HOSEA 13:10–11; 1 SAMUEL 4:10–11; 1 SAMUEL 4:18"> So Saul and three of his sons all died, and none of his descendants ever became king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:36; DEUTERONOMY 28:33; JUDGES 6:2"> When the Israelis who were living in the valley saw that their army had run away and that Saul and his three sons were dead, they left their towns and ran away. Then the soldiers from Philistia came and ◄occupied/lived in► those towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 31:8; 2 KINGS 3:23; 2 CHRONICLES 20:25"> The next day, when the Philistines came to take away the weapons of the dead Israeli soldiers, they found the corpses of Saul and his three sons on Gilboa Mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DANIEL 5:23; JUDGES 16:23–24; MATTHEW 14:11"> They took the clothes off Saul’s corpse and cut off his head and took it and Saul’s armor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 5:2–7; 1 SAMUEL 31:10"> Then they sent messengers throughout their land, to proclaim the news throughout their own area, to their idols and to the other people. They put Saul’s armor in the temple where their idols were, and they hung Saul’s head in the temple of their god Dagon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 31:11–13; 1 SAMUEL 11:1–11; 2 SAMUEL 2:4–7"> All the people who lived in Jabesh in the Gilead region heard what the Philistines had done to Saul’s corpse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 35:8; 2 SAMUEL 3:35; GENESIS 50:10"> So the bravest men/soldiers of Jabesh went and got the corpses of Saul and his sons and brought them back to Jabesh. They buried their bones under a large tree in Jabesh. Then the people of Jabesh ◄fasted/abstained from eating food► for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 20:6; LEVITICUS 19:31; 1 SAMUEL 15:23"> Saul died because he did not faithfully obey what Yahweh told him to do. He even went to a woman who talks to the spirits of dead people and asked her what he should do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:28; 1 SAMUEL 13:14; 1 SAMUEL 28:17"> instead of asking Yahweh what he should do. So Yahweh caused him to die, and he appointed David, the son of Jesse, to be the king of Israel.</span>
