## David became the king

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 5:1–16; 1 CHRONICLES 12:23–40; JUDGES 9:2"> Then the people of Israel came to David at Hebron town and said to him, “Listen, we have the same ancestors &#91;IDM&#93; that you have.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 5:2; JOHN 10:4; PSALMS 78:71"> In the past, when Saul was our king, it was you who led our Israeli soldiers in our battles. <span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:12–13; 1 SAMUEL 16:1; 2 SAMUEL 5:3"> So all the Israeli elders came to David at Hebron. That is what Yahweh had previously told the prophet Samuel would happen.</span>

## David’s men captured Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 1:21; GENESIS 15:21; JOSHUA 15:8"> David and all the Israeli soldiers &#91;SYN&#93; went to Jerusalem. At that time, Jerusalem was called Jebus, and the people who lived there were the Jebus people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 5:2; 1 KINGS 8:1; PSALMS 87:2"> Those people said to David, “Your soldiers will not be able to get inside our city!” But David’s soldiers captured the city, even though it had strong walls around it, and since then it has been called ‘The City of David’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:16; 2 SAMUEL 20:23; 2 SAMUEL 3:27"> What happened was this:: David said to his soldiers, “The one who leads our soldiers to attack the Jebus people-group will become the commander of all my army.” Joab, the son of Zeruiah, led the soldiers, so he became the commander of all the army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 11:5; 2 SAMUEL 5:7; PSALMS 2:6"> After they captured the city which had strong walls around it, David moved there. That is why they named it ‘The City of David’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 9:6; 1 KINGS 11:27; 2 KINGS 12:20"> David’s workers rebuilt the city, starting where the land was filled in and extending to the wall that was around the city. Joab’s men repaired the other parts of the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:1; Isaiah 41:10; 1 CHRONICLES 9:20"> David became more and more powerful/influential, because the Almighty Commander of the armies of angels was with/helping him.</span>

## The list of David’s most mighty warriors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

And all the Israeli people ◄were happy that David was/supported David as► their king. There were many soldiers/warriors who helped David’s kingdom to remain strong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 23:8; 1 CHRONICLES 27:2; 1 CHRONICLES 12:18"> This is a list of the leaders of David’s most mighty warriors: Jashobeam was from the Hacmon clan. He was one of the leaders of David’s most powerful soldiers. One time he fought against 300 enemies and killed them all with his spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 27:4; 2 SAMUEL 23:17–19; 2 SAMUEL 23:23"> Another one was Eleazar, who was the son of Dodo from the clan of Ahoh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 23:11–12; 1 SAMUEL 17:1"> One day he was with David at Pas Dammim when the soldiers of Philistia gathered there for the battle. <span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 144:10; 2 KINGS 5:1; 1 SAMUEL 14:23"> but then David and Eleazar stopped in the middle of the field and fought to defend it and killed many of the soldiers of Philistia. Yahweh enabled them to win a great victory on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 14:9; 2 SAMUEL 5:18; Isaiah 17:5"> One time three of David’s thirty most mighty warriors came to David when he was camping next to the huge rock outside the cave near Adullam. <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 10:5; 1 SAMUEL 13:4; 1 SAMUEL 13:23"> David was in a fortress, and some of the soldiers of Philistia were occupying Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 4:14; Isaiah 12:3; 2 SAMUEL 23:15–16"> One day David was very thirsty and said, “I wish that someone would bring me some water from the well near the gate at Bethlehem!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 21:13; 1 SAMUEL 19:5; ACTS 20:24"> So those three most outstanding warriors forced their way through the camp of Philistia soldiers and drew some water from the well, and brought it to David. But he would not drink it. Instead, he poured it out on the ground to be an offering to Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 23:17; PSALMS 72:14; JUDGES 9:17"> He said, “Yahweh, it would certainly not be right for me to drink this water! That was one of the things that those three most outstanding warriors did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 26:6; 1 CHRONICLES 2:16; 2 SAMUEL 20:6"> Joab’s younger brother Abishai was the leader of the 30 most mighty warriors. One time Abishai fought 300 enemy soldiers with his spear and killed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:41; MATTHEW 13:8"> So he became as famous as those three most outstanding warriors. He became their commander, even though he was not one of those three men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 23:20–23; 2 SAMUEL 8:18; JOSHUA 15:21"> Jehoiada’s son Benaiah was a brave soldier from Kabzeel town who did heroic deeds. <span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 17:7; 1 SAMUEL 17:4; DEUTERONOMY 3:11"> He also killed a soldier from Egypt who was ◄7-1/2 feet/2.3 meters► tall. <span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Those are some of the things that Benaiah did. So he became as famous as the three mighty warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 20:23"> He was more honored than the other members of the group of thirty most mighty warriors, but he did not become a member of the group of three most outstanding warriors. David appointed him to be the leader of his bodyguards.</span>
