## Gifts for building the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 22:5; 1 KINGS 3:7; 2 CHRONICLES 13:7"> Then King David said to all the people who had gathered there, “My son Solomon, the one whom God has chosen to be the next king, is young and does not have much experience. This work of building the temple is great/important, because this glorious building will not be to honor people, but to honor Yahweh our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 22:3–5; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:3; Isaiah 54:11–12"> From all the things that I possess, I have provided what will be needed to build the temple of my God—the gold for the things to be made of gold, the silver for the things to be made of silver, bronze for the things to be made of bronze, iron for the things to be made of iron, wood for the things to be made of wood, and large amounts of onyx and turquoise and other valuable stones of various colors, and marble and all kinds of valuable stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 21:24; PSALMS 27:4; PSALMS 26:8"> In addition to all these things that I have given for the temple, I am giving treasures of gold and silver, because I very much desire that this holy temple for my God be built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:28; 1 CHRONICLES 22:14; JOB 28:16"> I am giving 110 tons of gold from Ophir and 260 tons of refined silver to cover the walls of the buildings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 7:2–3; EXODUS 35:5–9; Ezra 7:15–16"> for making the other items of gold and silver, and for the other work to be done by the craftsmen. So now, I ask you, are there others willing to show by contributing other gifts for the building of the temple &#91;EUP&#93; that they have dedicated themselves to Yahweh?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 9:7; 1 CHRONICLES 27:25–1; Isaiah 60:3–10"> Then the leaders of the families/clans, the leaders of the tribes of Israel, the commanders of 1,000 soldiers and the commanders of 100 soldiers, and the officials who supervised the work that the king wanted done, gave gifts ◄willingly/because they wanted to give them►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:70–71; Ezra 2:69"> For the work at the temple they gave 190 tons and ◄185 pounds/84 kg.► of gold, 375 tons of silver, 675 tons of bronze, and 3,750 tons of iron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 23:8; 1 CHRONICLES 26:21–22"> And any people who owned valuable stones gave them to be put in the storeroom of the temple. Jehiel, a descendant of Gershon, was appointed to be in charge of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 9:7–8; 2 CORINTHIANS 8:3; 1 KINGS 8:61"> The people were happy to see that their leaders wanted to give those things, because they knew that their leaders were happy and enthusiastic to give those things to Yahweh. And King David also was very happy.</span>
</div>

## Solomon was crowned to become the king

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:4–9; NUMBERS 15:7; Ezra 6:17"> The next day the people offered sacrifices to Yahweh. They presented many animals to be completely burned on the altar: 1,000 bulls, 1,000 rams, 1,000 male sheep, plus offerings of wine, and many other sacrifices on behalf of all the people of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:35; 1 CHRONICLES 23:1; EXODUS 24:11"> On that day the people were joyful and ate and drank while Yahweh was watching. Then for the second time they declared that Solomon was now the king. While Yahweh was watching, they anointed him with olive oil to be the king, and they anointed Zadok to be the Supreme Priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:12; 1 CHRONICLES 28:5; ROMANS 13:1"> So Solomon sat on the throne because Yahweh wanted him to be the king to succeed his father David. During the following years Solomon prospered, and all the Israeli people obeyed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 2:3–9; EZEKIEL 17:18; GENESIS 24:2"> King David’s other sons and all the officers and mighty warriors accepted Solomon as their king, and they solemnly promised to obey him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 1:12; 2 CHRONICLES 1:1; ECCLESIASTES 2:9"> Yahweh caused Solomon to be highly respected by all the Israeli people, and they honored him very much. No king of Israel was honored as much as Solomon was.</span>
</div>

## The death of David

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 18:14; PSALMS 78:71–72"> Jesse’s son David was the king who ruled all of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:11; 2 SAMUEL 5:4–5; 1 CHRONICLES 3:4"> He ruled for 40 years: Seven years in Hebron city and 33 years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 15:15; ACTS 13:36; 1 CHRONICLES 23:1"> He became an old man who was very rich and greatly honored by all the people. Then he died, and his son Solomon became the king of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 9:9; 2 SAMUEL 12:1–7; 1 CHRONICLES 21:9–11"> A record of all the things that King David did while he ruled, from the beginning to the end, was put on scrolls written by the prophets Samuel, Nathan, and Gad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DANIEL 4:25; DANIEL 4:23; DANIEL 2:21"> They told about his powerful rule &#91;HEN&#93;, and all the things that happened to him and to the people of Israel and in the nearby kingdoms while he was ruling Israel.</span>
