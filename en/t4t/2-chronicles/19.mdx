<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 18:31–32"> When King Jehoshaphat was returning safely to his palace in Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PSALMS 139:21–22; 2 JOHN 1:10–11; JAMES 4:4"> the prophet Jehu, the son of the prophet Hanani, went out of the city to meet the king, and said to him, “It was not right for you to help a wicked man and to love those who hate Yahweh. Because of what you have done, Yahweh is angry with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 12:12; Ezra 7:10; 1 KINGS 14:13"> But you have done some good things; you got rid of the poles in this country for worshiping the goddess Asherah, and you have strongly determined to do what pleases God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jehoshaphat appointed men to settle disputes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 7:15–17; 1 SAMUEL 7:3–4; 2 CHRONICLES 29:10–11"> Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem. But one time, like he had done once previously, he went out among all the people in the country, from Beersheba city in the far south to the hilly area of the tribe of Ephraim in the far north, and he convinced them to return to worshiping Yahweh, the God whom their ancestors worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 16:18–20; 2 CHRONICLES 19:8; 1 PETER 2:13–14"> He appointed judges throughout Judah, in each of the cities that had walls around them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 1:17; LEVITICUS 19:15; ECCLESIASTES 5:8"> He told them, “Make your decisions carefully, because you are judging cases not in order to please people but to please Yahweh. And he will be watching you whenever you make a decision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:4; GENESIS 18:25; ROMANS 2:11"> So now revere Yahweh, and judge cases carefully, and do not forget that Yahweh our God never acts unjustly, and he never does what people want because of their offering him money; he never accepts bribes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 17:8–9; EXODUS 18:19–26; DEUTERONOMY 17:8–13"> In Jerusalem also, Jehoshaphat appointed some priests and other descendants of Levi and some leaders of Israeli families to be judges. He told them to do what Yahweh’s laws said was right when they settled disputes. Those men lived in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 19:7; 2 SAMUEL 23:3; DEUTERONOMY 1:16–17"> He told them this: “You must always do your work faithfully, revering Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:8–13; EZEKIEL 33:6; EZEKIEL 3:18–21"> In every dispute that your fellow Israelis who live in the cities want you to settle, you must warn them to not sin against Yahweh by telling lies during the trial. If you do not warn them, God will punish both you and your fellow Israelis. If you warn them, you will not be sinning. You must warn them whether someone has accused them of murdering someone, or of disobeying some other law or command or decree of Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 19:8; PSALMS 18:25–26; 2 TIMOTHY 2:1"> Amariah the Supreme Priest will supervise you in any matter that Yahweh is concerned about, and Zebadiah the son of Ishmael, the leader of the tribe of Judah, will supervise you in any matter that I am responsible for. And the descendants of Levi will assist you. Act courageously, and I pray that Yahweh will help those who do their work well.”</span>
</div>
</div>
