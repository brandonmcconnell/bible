<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 5:10; 2 CHRONICLES 5:7; 1 KINGS 8:21"> I have put the Sacred Chest in the temple, in which are the stone tablets on which are engraved the Ten Commandments of the agreement that Yahweh made with us Israeli people.”</span>

## Solomon’s prayer to dedicate the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 63:4; Isaiah 50:10; JOB 11:13"> Then Solomon stood in front of the altar which was in front of the people of Israel who had gathered there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:54; Nehemiah 8:4; PSALMS 95:6"> His workers had built a bronze platform there for him to stand on, which was 7-1/2 feet long and 7-1/2 feet wide and 4-1/2 feet high. They put it in the outer courtyard. Solomon stood on that platform and then knelt down in front of all the people of Israel who had gathered there, and he spread out his arms toward heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 15:11; DEUTERONOMY 7:9; Nehemiah 1:5"> and he prayed,<br />“Yahweh, the God to whom we Israeli people belong, there is no God like you in heaven or on the earth. You solemnly promised that you would faithfully love us, and that is what you have done for us who earnestly do what you want us to do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 22:9–10; 2 SAMUEL 7:12; 1 KINGS 8:24"> “You have done the things that you promised my father David, who served you very well, that you would do. Truly, you promised to do those things for him, and today we see that by your power you have caused it all to happen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:4; JOHN 15:14–15; 2 CHRONICLES 7:18"> “So now, Yahweh, the God we Israelis belong to, please do the things that you promised to my father David that you would do. You said that there would always be some of his descendants to rule Israel, if they faithfully conduct their lives as he did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:5; Isaiah 41:17; 2 CHRONICLES 6:4"> So now, God of us Israeli people, cause what you promised David, who served you well, to happen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 2:6; Isaiah 66:1; PSALMS 139:7–10"> “But, my God, will you really live on earth among people? There is surely not enough space for you in the sky, or even in the heaven! So there is surely not enough space for you to live in this temple that my workers have built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:28; LUKE 18:1–7; JOHN 17:20"> But Yahweh, my God, please listen to my prayer, while I am pleading with you this day and do what I am requesting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 34:15; DANIEL 6:10; PSALMS 121:5"> Please protect this temple day and night. This is the place about which you have said, ‘I will always be there.’ Please listen to my prayer about this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MICAH 7:18; DANIEL 9:19; Isaiah 43:25"> Listen to me when I pray, and listen to your Israeli people when they pray. Listen from heaven, where you live; and when you hear us pray, forgive us.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 22:11; LEVITICUS 5:1; NUMBERS 5:19–22"> “If someone is accused of doing something wrong to another person, and they bring him to testify in front of your altar outside this holy temple, and if he says, ‘I did not do that; may God punish me if I am not telling the truth,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; PROVERBS 17:15; JEREMIAH 51:56"> then you listen from heaven, and you decide who is telling the truth. Then punish the person who is guilty as he deserves to be punished, and declare that the other person is innocent.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:17; 2 KINGS 17:7–18; JOSHUA 7:8"> “And when your Israeli people are defeated by their enemies because they sinned against you, and forced to go to some distant country, if they turn away from their sinful behavior and turn toward this temple and admit that you have justly punished them, and plead that you will forgive them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:6–13; Ezra 1:1–6; GENESIS 13:15"> listen to them from heaven and forgive your Israeli people for the sins that they have committed, and bring them back to this land that you gave to our ancestors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:23; DEUTERONOMY 11:17; LEVITICUS 26:19"> “When you do not allow any rain to fall because your people have sinned against you, if they turn toward this temple and admit that you have justly punished them, and turn away from their sinful behavior and humbly pray to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:35–36; JEREMIAH 5:24; ZECHARIAH 10:1"> hear from heaven and forgive the sins of your Israeli people. Teach them the right way to conduct their lives. Then cause it to rain here on the land that you gave to your people to belong to them permanently.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="REVELATION 9:3–11; JOEL 2:25; RUTH 1:1"> “And when the people of this land experience famines or if there is a plague, or when their crops are destroyed by very hot winds or by mildew or by locusts or grasshoppers, or when their enemies surround any of their cities in order to attack them, if any of those bad things happen to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PSALMS 50:15; PSALMS 32:2–6; 2 CHRONICLES 6:12–13"> when your Israeli people earnestly plead with you, knowing in their inner beings that they are suffering because they have sinned, if they stretch out their hands toward this temple and pray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 28:9; 1 SAMUEL 16:7; REVELATION 2:23"> hear from your home in heaven, and forgive them. You alone know what each person is thinking, so reward each person according to everything that he does,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ACTS 9:31; JOB 28:28; PSALMS 128:1"> in order that they will revere you and conduct their lives as you want them to, all the time that they live in this land that you gave to our ancestors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 56:3–7; 1 KINGS 8:41–43; MATTHEW 8:10–11"> “There will be some foreigners who do not belong to your Israeli people who have come here from countries far away because they have heard that you are very great and that you perform great miracles. If they turn toward this temple and pray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:14; 1 KINGS 8:16; REVELATION 11:15"> from your home in heaven listen to their prayer, and do for them what they request you to do. Do that in order that all the people-groups in the world will know about you and revere you, like we, your own Israeli people do. And then they will know that this is the temple that I have caused to be built for you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 20:6–13; 2 CHRONICLES 18:31; 2 CHRONICLES 20:4"> “When you send your people to go to places to attack their enemies, if they pray to you, wherever they are, if they turn toward this city that you have chosen and toward this temple that I have caused to be built to honor you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="DANIEL 9:17–19; PSALMS 9:3–4; JEREMIAH 5:28"> listen in heaven to their prayers; listen to what they plead for you to do, and assist them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 7:20; JAMES 3:2; 1 JOHN 1:8–10"> “It is true that everyone sins. So, when your people sin against you, and you become angry with them, you may allow their enemies to capture them and take them to their countries, even to countries that are far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:18–20; DANIEL 9:5–11; Isaiah 64:6–12"> When that happens, while they are in the countries to which they were forced to go, if they are sorry for having sinned, if they say, ‘We have sinned; we have done things that are wrong and have done things that are very wicked,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 33:11–13; DANIEL 6:10; DANIEL 9:3–4"> if they repent very sincerely, and if they turn toward this land that you gave to our ancestors, and toward this city that you have chosen to be the place where we should worship you, and toward this temple that I have caused to be built for you and pray,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MICAH 7:18–20; 2 CHRONICLES 6:35; PSALMS 25:18"> then from your home in heaven hear their prayer, and listen to them while they plead for your help, and do what they ask you to do, and forgive your people who have sinned against you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:15; PSALMS 116:2; Nehemiah 1:6"> “Now, my God, look at us and listen to us as we pray to you in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 61:10; 1 CHRONICLES 28:2; PSALMS 132:8–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh our God, come and stay in this place with the Sacred Chest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the chest that shows that you are powerful.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh God, cause your priests to know clearly that you have blessed them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Cause us your people to rejoice because of all the good things that you do for us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Isaiah 55:3; PSALMS 132:1; Isaiah 61:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh God, do not reject me the king whom you have appointed to be the king of Israel;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not forget that you greatly loved David, who served you very well.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>
