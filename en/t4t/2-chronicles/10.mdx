## The people of Israel rebelled against Rehoboam

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 12:6; JOSHUA 20:7; 1 CHRONICLES 12:38"> All the people of Israel went to Shechem city in order to appoint Rehoboam to be their king. So Rehoboam also went there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:40; 1 KINGS 12:2; 1 KINGS 11:28"> Jeroboam, the son of Nebat, had fled to Egypt to escape from King Solomon. But when he heard about the people wanting to appoint Rehoboam to be their king, he returned to Israel from Egypt</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:3"> So the leaders of the northern tribes summoned him, and he went with them to talk to Rehoboam. They said to Rehoboam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:4; EXODUS 2:23; MATTHEW 11:29–30"> “Your father Solomon forced us to work very hard for him. If you do not force us to work that hard, and if you charge us less taxes than we were paying to him, we will serve you faithfully.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:5; PROVERBS 3:28"> He replied, “Come back three days from now and I will give you my answer.” So those leaders and Jeroboam left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOB 32:7; PROVERBS 19:20; PROVERBS 27:10"> Then King Rehoboam consulted his older men who had advised his father Solomon while he was still alive. He asked them, “What shall I say to answer these men?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:1; GENESIS 49:21; 1 KINGS 12:7"> They replied, “If you will be kind to these people and do things that will please them, and if you say kind things to them when you answer them, they will always serve you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 17:14; PROVERBS 1:25; ECCLESIASTES 10:16"> But he ignored what the older men advised him to do. Instead, he consulted the younger men who had grown up with him, who were now his advisors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 10:6; 2 SAMUEL 17:5–6; 1 KINGS 22:6–8"> He said to them, “How should I answer the men who are asking me to reduce the work and taxes that my father required from them?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:23; PROVERBS 13:16; 2 SAMUEL 17:7–13"> The young men who had grown up with him replied, “Those men have said that your father forced them to work very hard for him, so they want you to reduce the work and taxes that your father required from them. But this is what you should tell them: ‘My little finger is thicker than my father’s waist.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:29; Isaiah 47:6; REVELATION 9:10"> What I mean is that my father required you to work hard and pay high taxes. But I will make those loads heavier. It was as though my father whipped you, but I will whip you with whips that have pieces of metal in them.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:12–15; 2 CHRONICLES 10:5"> Three days later, Jeroboam and all the leaders came to King Rehoboam again, which is what he had told them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 10:8; PROVERBS 19:27; 1 SAMUEL 25:10–11"> The king ignored the advice of the older men and spoke harshly to the Israeli leaders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 17:14; PROVERBS 12:5; ECCLESIASTES 10:16"> He told them what the younger men had advised. He said, “My father put heavy burdens of work and taxes on you, but I will put heavier burdens on you. It was as though he beat you with whips, but I will beat you with whips that have pieces of metal in them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:29–39; 1 SAMUEL 2:25; 2 CHRONICLES 25:16–20"> So the king did not pay any attention to the Israeli leaders. But this happened in order that what Yahweh had told the prophet Ahijah about Jeroboam would happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 20:1; 2 CHRONICLES 10:19; PSALMS 76:10"> When the Israeli leaders realized that the king did not pay any attention to what they said, they shouted,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We do not want anything to do with this descendant of King David!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We will not pay attention to what this grandson of Jesse says!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You people of Israel, let’s go home!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">As for this descendant of David, he can rule his own kingdom!”</span>So the Israeli leaders returned to their homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 11:1; 1 KINGS 12:17; 1 KINGS 11:36"> And after that, the only Israeli people whom Rehoboam ruled over were those who lived in the territory of the tribes of Judah and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 KINGS 5:14; 1 KINGS 4:6; ACTS 7:57–58"> Then King Rehoboam went with Adoniram to talk to the Israeli people. Adoniram was the man who supervised all the men who were forced to work for Rehoboam. But the Israeli people killed him by throwing stones at him. When that happened, King Rehoboam quickly got in his chariot and escaped to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:19–20; Ezra 9:7; 2 CHRONICLES 5:9"> Ever since that time, the people of the northern tribes of Israel have been rebelling against the descendants of King David.</span>
</div>
