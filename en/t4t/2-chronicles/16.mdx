## The final years that Asa ruled Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 15:9; 2 CHRONICLES 15:5; 1 KINGS 12:27"> When Asa had been ruling Judah for almost 36 years, King Baasha of Israel went with his army to attack Judah. They captured the town of Ramah north of Jerusalem and started to build a wall around it, in order to prevent any people from entering or leaving the area in Judah that was ruled by King Asa because the only road into Judah from Israel went through Ramah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 KINGS 12:18; 2 CHRONICLES 28:21; 2 KINGS 18:15"> So Asa told his workers to take all the silver and gold that was in the storerooms of the temple and in his own palace, and take and give it to Ben-Hadad, the king of Syria, who was ruling in Damascus. He sent him a message, saying</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 15:4; 2 CHRONICLES 18:3; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:16"> “I want there to be a peace treaty between me and you, like there was between my father and your father. Look, I am sending you a lot of silver and gold. So please cancel the treaty that you have made with Baasha, the king of Israel, in order that he will take his soldiers away from attacking mine, because he will be afraid of your army.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 20:1; 2 CHRONICLES 17:12; 1 KINGS 15:20"> Ben-Hadad agreed to do what King Asa suggested. He sent the commanders of his armies with their soldiers to attack some of the towns in Israel. They captured Ijon, Dan, Abel-Beth-Maacah and all the cities in the area belonging to the tribe of Naphtali where supplies were kept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When Baasha heard about that, he commanded his troops to stop fortifying Ramah and doing other work there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 21:17; JOSHUA 15:38; Isaiah 10:29"> Then King Asa gathered all the men of Judah, and they took away from Ramah all the stones and timber that Baasha’s men had been using to build the wall around that town. They took those materials to the town of Geba and the city of Mizpah north of Jerusalem and built walls around them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 19:2; 1 KINGS 16:1; PSALMS 146:3–6"> At that time the prophet Hanani went to King Asa and said to him, “Because you relied on the king of Syria and not on Yahweh our God, you missed your opportunity to destroy the army of the king of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 12:3; 2 CHRONICLES 13:16; 2 CHRONICLES 16:7"> The huge armies from Ethiopia and Libya with all their chariots and soldiers riding on horses were certainly very powerful. But when you relied on Yahweh, he enabled your army to defeat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 PETER 3:12; PROVERBS 15:3; JEREMIAH 16:17"> That happened because Yahweh sees what is happening all over the earth, and he strengthens those who completely trust him. You have done a very foolish thing, so from now on other armies will be fighting your army.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 18:26; ACTS 16:23–24; 2 SAMUEL 12:13"> Asa was very angry with the prophet because of what the prophet had said. So he commanded his officials to put Hanani in prison. At that same time, he started to treat some of his people very cruelly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 12:15; 2 CHRONICLES 9:29; 2 CHRONICLES 35:27"> All the things that Asa did while he was ruling, from the time he started to rule until he died, are written in the scroll containing the record of the activities of the kings of Judah and Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:5; 2 CHRONICLES 28:22; 1 CHRONICLES 10:14"> When Asa had been ruling for almost 39 years, he was afflicted with a disease in his feet. The disease was very severe, but in spite of that, he did not request help from Yahweh. Instead he sought help only from doctors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:24"> When he had been ruling for almost 40 years, he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GENESIS 50:2; 2 CHRONICLES 21:19; JEREMIAH 34:5"> He was buried in the tomb that his workers had made for him in the part of Jerusalem called ‘The City of David’. They laid his corpse on a bed covered with spices and various perfumes that had been mixed together. They also lit a huge fire to honor him.</span>
</div>
