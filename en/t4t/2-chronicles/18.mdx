## The prophet Micaiah warned King Ahab of Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 17:5; 2 CHRONICLES 21:6; 2 CHRONICLES 19:2"> Jehoshaphat became very wealthy and was greatly honored. But then he arranged for one of his family to marry someone from the family of King Ahab of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:1; 2 CHRONICLES 19:2; 1 KINGS 1:9"> Several years later, he went down from Jerusalem to Samaria to visit Ahab. Ahab welcomed him and the people who had come with him by slaughtering many sheep and cattle for a feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:4; 2 KINGS 3:7; 2 JOHN 1:10–11"> Then he asked Jehoshaphat, “Will you and your army go with my army to attack Ramoth city in the Gilead region?” Jehoshaphat replied, “My soldiers and I will go to the battle when you tell us to go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:2; 2 SAMUEL 5:19; 2 SAMUEL 2:1"> Then he added, “But we should ask Yahweh first, to find out what he wants us to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:3; MICAH 3:11; JEREMIAH 28:1–17"> So the king of Israel gathered all his 400 prophets and asked them, “Should we go to attack the people of Ramoth city, or should we not do that?” They replied, “Yes, go and attack them because God will enable your army to defeat them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:7–9; 2 KINGS 3:11–13"> But Jehoshaphat asked, “Is there no prophet of Yahweh here whom we can ask?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="AMOS 5:10; MICAH 2:7; 1 KINGS 21:20"> The king of Israel replied, “There is still one man here, whom we can ask to find out what Yahweh wants, his name is Micaiah, the son of Imlah. But I hate him because he never says anything good about me. He always predicts that bad things will happen to me.” Jehoshaphat replied, “King Ahab, you should not say that!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:9; Isaiah 39:7; 2 CHRONICLES 18:25–26"> So the king of Israel told one of his officials to summon Micaiah immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9; DANIEL 7:9; MATTHEW 19:28"> The king of Israel and the king of Judah were sitting there on their thrones, wearing their royal robes. They were at the place where people threshed grain, near the gate of Samaria city. All of Ahab’s prophets were standing in front of them, predicting what was going to happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:17; ZECHARIAH 1:18–21; JEREMIAH 28:2–3"> One of them whose name was Zedekiah, the son of Kenaanah, had made from iron something that resembled the horns of a bull. He proclaimed to Ahab, “This is what Yahweh says: ‘With horns like these, your army will keep attacking the army of Syria like a bull attacks another animal, until you completely destroy them.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:24–25; 2 CHRONICLES 18:33–34; JUDE 1:16"> All the other prophets of Ahab agreed. They said, “Yes! If you attack Ramoth city in the Gilead region, you will be successful, because Yahweh will enable you to defeat them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MICAH 2:6; PSALMS 10:11; MICAH 2:11"> The messenger who went to summon Micaiah said to him, “Listen to me! All the other prophets are unitedly predicting that the king will be successful in the battle. So be sure to agree with them and say that the king’s army will be successful!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 22:35; NUMBERS 24:13; NUMBERS 22:18–20"> But Micaiah said, “As surely as Yahweh lives, I will tell the king only what Yahweh tells me to say.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:15; LAMENTATIONS 4:21; AMOS 4:4–5"> When Micaiah arrived, the king of Israel asked him, “Should we go to attack Ramoth, or not?” Micaiah replied, “Sure, go! Yahweh will enable your army to defeat them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:63; 1 SAMUEL 14:24; 1 KINGS 22:16"> But King Ahab realized that Micaiah was lying, so he said to Micaiah, “I have told you many times that you must always tell only the truth when you say what Yahweh has revealed to you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:36; EZEKIEL 34:5–6; NUMBERS 27:17"> Then Micaiah replied, “The truth is that in a vision I saw all the troops of Israel scattered on the mountains. They seemed to be like sheep that did not have a shepherd. And Yahweh said, ‘Their master has been killed. So tell them all to go home peacefully.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 18:7; 1 KINGS 22:18; PROVERBS 29:1"> Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, “I told you that he never predicts that anything good will happen to me! He always predicts that bad things will happen to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="DANIEL 7:9; Isaiah 6:1–5; 1 KINGS 22:19–23"> But Micaiah continued, saying, “Listen to what Yahweh showed to me! In a vision I saw Yahweh sitting on his throne, with all the armies of heaven standing around him, on his right side and on his left side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:9; 2 CHRONICLES 25:19; PROVERBS 11:5"> And Yahweh said, ‘Who can persuade Ahab, the king of Israel, to go to fight against the people of Ramoth, in order that he may be killed there?’ “Some suggested one thing, and others suggested something else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOB 1:6; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:13–15; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:3"> “Finally one spirit, having received a message from Satan, came to Yahweh and said, ‘I can do it!’ “Yahweh asked him, ‘How will you do it?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JOHN 8:44; 1 KINGS 22:21–22; 1 JOHN 4:6"> “The spirit replied, ‘I will go and inspire all of Ahab’s prophets to tell lies.’ Yahweh said, ‘You will be successful; go and do it!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:14; EZEKIEL 14:9; JOB 12:16"> “So now I tell you that Yahweh has caused your prophets to lie to you. Yahweh has decided that something terrible will happen to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARK 14:65; JEREMIAH 20:2; 2 CHRONICLES 18:10"> Then Zedekiah son of Kenaanah walked over to Micaiah and slapped him on his face. He said, “Do you think that Yahweh’s Spirit left me in order to speak to you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:21–22; Isaiah 26:20; JEREMIAH 28:16–17"> Micaiah replied, “You will find out for yourself to which of us Yahweh’s Spirit has truly spoken on the day when you go into a room of some house to hide from the Syrian soldiers!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 18:8; 2 CHRONICLES 34:8; JEREMIAH 38:6–7"> King Ahab commanded his soldiers, “Seize Micaiah and take him to Amon, the governor of this city, and to my son Joash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 16:10; PSALMS 80:5; LUKE 23:2"> Tell them that I have commanded that they should put this man in prison and give him only bread and water. Do not give him anything else to eat until I return safely from the battle!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="LUKE 20:45–46; AMOS 9:10; MICAH 1:2"> Micaiah replied, “If you return safely, it will be clear that it was not Yahweh who told me what to say to you!” Then he said to all those who were standing there, “Do not forget what I have said to King Ahab!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ahab died in the battle at Ramoth-Gilead

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:29–33"> So the king of Israel and the king of Judah led their armies to Ramoth, in the Gilead region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 28:8; JEREMIAH 23:24; PROVERBS 26:25"> King Ahab said to Jehoshaphat, “I will put on different clothes, in order that no one will recognize that I am the king. But you should wear your royal robe.” So the king of Israel disguised himself, and they both went into the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="GENESIS 19:11; DEUTERONOMY 1:17; 1 KINGS 20:42"> The King of Syria told his soldiers who were driving the chariots, “Attack only the king of Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 13:14; PSALMS 118:13; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:9–10"> So when the soldiers who were driving the Syrian chariots saw Jehoshaphat wearing the royal robes, they thought “He must be the king of Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 KINGS 22:33"> So they turned to attack him. But when Jehoshaphat cried out, Yahweh helped him, and they realized that he was not the king of Israel. And God caused them to stop pursuing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:23; 2 SAMUEL 15:11; 1 KINGS 22:34–35"> But one Syrian soldier shot an arrow at Ahab, without knowing that it was Ahab. The arrow struck Ahab between the places where the parts of his armor joined together. Ahab told the driver of his chariot, “Turn the chariot around and take me out of here! I have been severely wounded!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:21; 2 CHRONICLES 18:27; PROVERBS 28:17"> The battle continued all that day. Ahab was sitting propped up in his chariot, facing the Syrian soldiers. And late in the afternoon, when the sun was setting, he died.</span>
</div>
