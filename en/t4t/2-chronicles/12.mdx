## The army of King Shishak of Egypt invaded Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 11:17; 2 CHRONICLES 12:13; 2 CHRONICLES 26:13–16"> After Rehoboam was in complete control of his kingdom, he and all the other people in Judah stopped obeying the laws of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:40; 2 CHRONICLES 7:19–20; 1 CHRONICLES 28:9"> As a result, after Rehoboam had been king for almost five years, Yahweh sent Shishak, the king of Egypt, with his army to attack Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NAHUM 3:9; 2 CHRONICLES 16:8; DANIEL 11:43"> Along with his army he brought 1,200 chariots and 60,000 soldiers riding horses and a very large number of troops from two regions in Libya, and from Ethiopia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 11:5–12; 2 KINGS 18:17; Isaiah 8:8"> They captured many of the cities in Judah that had walls around them, and they came as far as Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 11:2; 2 CHRONICLES 15:2; 1 KINGS 12:22"> Then the prophet Shemaiah came to Rehoboam and the other leaders of Judah who had gathered in Jerusalem because they were afraid of the army of Shishak. Shemaiah said to them, “Yahweh says this: ‘You have abandoned me; so now I am abandoning you, to allow you to be captured by the army of Shishak.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 9:27; DANIEL 9:14; JEREMIAH 13:18"> Then the king and the other Israeli leaders humbled themselves and said, “What Yahweh is doing to us is fair.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 34:25; Isaiah 42:25; 1 KINGS 21:28–29"> When Yahweh realized that they had humbled themselves, he gave this message to Shemaiah: “Because they have humbled themselves, I will not allow them to be destroyed. Instead, I will soon rescue them. I will not use Shishak’s army to completely destroy the people of Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:47–48; Isaiah 26:13; JEREMIAH 10:24"> but they will conquer Jerusalem and force the people there to do what Shishak wants them to do. As a result, the people of Jerusalem will learn that it is better to serve me than to serve the kings of other countries.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 9:15–16; 1 KINGS 10:16–17; 2 KINGS 16:8"> When Shishak’s army attacked Jerusalem, they took/carried away the valuable things that were in the temple of Yahweh and the valuable things that were in the king’s palace. They took everything that was valuable, including the gold shields that Solomon’s workers had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 8:18; 2 SAMUEL 23:23; LAMENTATIONS 4:1"> So King Rehoboam’s workers made bronze shields to be used instead of the gold ones and gave the bronze shields to the commanders of the men who guarded the entrance to his palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> After that, whenever the king went to the temple, the guards went with him, carrying those bronze shields. Then when the king left, they would return the shields to the guards’ room.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 19:3; 2 CHRONICLES 12:6–7; 2 CHRONICLES 33:12–13"> Because Rehoboam humbled himself, Yahweh stopped being angry with him and did not get rid of him. Instead, he caused good things to happen in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 KINGS 14:21; DEUTERONOMY 12:5; 2 CHRONICLES 6:6"> King Rehoboam again was in complete control in Jerusalem and continued to be the king of Judah. He was 41 years old when he became the king. He ruled for 17 years in Jerusalem, which is the city that Yahweh had chosen from all the tribes in Israel to be the place in which people were to worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 19:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:13; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:58"> Rehoboam’s mother’s name was Naamah. She was from the Ammon people-group. Rehoboam did evil things because he did not try to find out what Yahweh wanted him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 9:29; 2 CHRONICLES 12:5; 1 KINGS 12:22"> An account of all the things that Rehoboam did while he was the king, and lists of the members of his family, are in the scrolls written by the prophets Shemaiah and Iddo. The armies of Rehoboam and Jeroboam were constantly fighting each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 3:10; 2 CHRONICLES 13:1; MATTHEW 1:7"> When Rehoboam died, he was buried in the part of Jerusalem called ‘The City of David’. Then his son Abijah became the king.</span>
</div>
