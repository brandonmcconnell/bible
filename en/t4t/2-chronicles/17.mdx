## King Jehoshaphat’s rule in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:24; EPHESIANS 6:10; MATTHEW 1:8"> Asa’s son Jehoshaphat became the king of Judah, and he enabled his army to become very strong, with the result that they could resist attacks from the army of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 15:8; 2 CHRONICLES 11:5; 2 CHRONICLES 11:11–12"> He put soldiers in all the cities in Judah around which they had built walls, and he put soldiers in other places in Judah and in the towns in the area belonging to the tribe of Ephraim that soldiers of his father Asa had captured.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 KINGS 22:2; Isaiah 41:10; 2 TIMOTHY 4:22"> Yahweh helped Jehoshaphat because when he started to rule Judah, he did the things that pleased Yahweh like his ancestor King David had done. He did not worship the idols of Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:28; LUKE 1:6; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:12"> Instead, he sought advice from the God whom his father had worshiped, and he obeyed God’s commands, and did not do the evil things that the kings of Israel continually did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 18:1; MATTHEW 6:33; PSALMS 127:1"> Yahweh enabled him to completely control his kingdom. All the people of Judah brought gifts to him, with the result that he became very rich and was greatly honored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HOSEA 14:9; 2 CHRONICLES 15:17; 2 CHRONICLES 20:33"> He was completely devoted to doing what pleased Yahweh. His workers got rid of the shrines on the hilltops and the poles for worshiping the goddess Asherah throughout Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:3; Nehemiah 8:7–8; ECCLESIASTES 12:9–10"> When he had been ruling Judah for almost three years, he sent some of his officials—Ben-Hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah—to teach the people in various towns in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezra 7:1–6; 2 CHRONICLES 19:8; MALACHI 2:7"> With them he sent several descendants of Levi—Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-Adonijah—and two priests, Elishama and Jehoram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 5:46; ROMANS 3:2; ACTS 8:40"> They took with them a scroll on which were written the laws of Yahweh and taught them to the people in all the towns throughout Judah,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 14:14; EXODUS 15:14–16; PROVERBS 16:7"> The people in all the kingdoms surrounding Judah became very afraid of what Yahweh might do to punish them if they fought against Judah, so they did not start wars with Jehoshaphat’s army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:8; 2 CHRONICLES 9:14; 2 SAMUEL 8:2"> Some people from Philistia brought gifts to Jehoshaphat, and they also brought to him the silver that he demanded that they pay to him. Some Arabs brought to him 7,700 rams and 7,700 goats.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 8:2–6; 2 CHRONICLES 14:6–7; 2 CHRONICLES 32:5"> Jehoshaphat continued to become more powerful/influential. His workers built forts and places to store supplies in various towns in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 27:25–31; 2 CHRONICLES 26:10–15"> Then they put large amounts of supplies in those storehouses.<br />Jehoshaphat also placed in Jerusalem soldiers who were experienced.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 11:1; GENESIS 15:5; NUMBERS 1:18"> The leaders and numbers from each tribe were as follows:<br />From the tribe of Judah, Adnah was the leader of the soldiers, and he commanded 300,000 soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> His assistant was Jehohanan, who commanded 280,000 soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 5:9; JUDGES 5:2; 1 CHRONICLES 29:9"> Next was Zicri’s son Amasiah, who volunteered to serve Yahweh in this way; he commanded 200,000 soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 14:8; 2 SAMUEL 1:21–22"> From the tribe of Benjamin, Eliada, who was a brave soldier, was the leader of the soldiers; he commanded 200,000 men who had bows and arrows and shields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Next was Jehozabad, who commanded 180,000 men who had weapons for fighting battles.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 17:2; 2 CHRONICLES 11:12; 2 CHRONICLES 11:23"> Those were the soldiers who served the king in Jerusalem, in addition to the men whom the king had placed in the other cities in Judah that had walls around them.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
