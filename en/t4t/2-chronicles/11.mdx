## Rehoboam fortified cities in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 8:2–6; 2 CHRONICLES 14:6–7; Isaiah 22:8–11"> Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem, and his workers built walls around several of the cities and towns in Judah to protect them against enemy attacks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nehemiah 3:5; AMOS 1:1; 1 CHRONICLES 4:32"> In the area that belonged to the tribes of Judah and Benjamin they built walls around Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:35; 1 SAMUEL 22:1; JOSHUA 15:58"> Beth-Zur, Soco, Adullam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 54:1; JOSHUA 15:24; 1 SAMUEL 23:19"> Gath, Mareshah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOSHUA 15:39; JOSHUA 15:35; JOSHUA 10:5"> Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="NUMBERS 13:22; JOSHUA 15:33; JOSHUA 14:14"> Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 17:19; Isaiah 22:10–11; 2 CHRONICLES 11:23"> He also appointed an army commander in each of those cities and towns, and gave them supplies of food, olive oil, and wine to keep in storage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 13:22; 2 CHRONICLES 26:14–15; 2 CHRONICLES 32:5"> He put shields and spears in all the cities and made them well-protected. So he continued to rule the tribes of Judah and Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> The priests and other descendants of Levi throughout Israel supported Rehoboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 13:9; NUMBERS 35:2–5; 1 KINGS 12:28–33"> The descendants of Levi who lived outside the territory of Judah and Benjamin abandoned their property and their pastureland, and they came to Jerusalem and to other places in Judah, because Jeroboam and his sons would not allow them to do the work of priests of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:28; 1 KINGS 12:31; 1 KINGS 13:33"> Instead, Jeroboam appointed the priests that he wanted to work at the altars he commanded to be built on the hilltops, to offer sacrifices to the idols that he commanded to be made that resembled goats and calves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 15:9; ACTS 11:23; PSALMS 84:5–7"> And people from every tribe in Israel who wanted to worship Yahweh, the God to whom the Israelis belonged, went with the descendants of Levi to Jerusalem to live there and to offer sacrifices to Yahweh, the God whom their ancestors worshiped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 12:1; 2 CHRONICLES 1:1–12; 2 CHRONICLES 7:17–19"> They caused the kingdom of Judah to be strong, and for three years they were happy that Solomon’s son Rehoboam was the king. During that time they conducted their lives righteously as David and Solomon had done previously.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rehoboam’s family

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 16:6; 1 CHRONICLES 27:18; 1 SAMUEL 17:13"> Rehoboam married Mahalath. She was the daughter of David’s son Jerimoth, and her mother was Abihail, the daughter of Eliab and granddaughter of Jesse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Rehoboam and Mahalath had three sons: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 13:2; 2 CHRONICLES 11:21; 2 CHRONICLES 12:16"> Later Rehoboam married Maacah, the daughter of Absalom, and they had four sons: Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 8:30; DEUTERONOMY 17:17; 1 KINGS 11:3"> Rehoboam loved Maacah more than he loved any other of his wives and slave wives. Altogether he had 18 wives and 60 slave wives, and 28 sons and 60 daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 21:15–17; 1 CHRONICLES 29:1; 1 CHRONICLES 5:1–2"> Rehoboam appointed his son Abijah to be the leader of his older and younger brothers, because he wanted to appoint Abijah to be the next king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 21:3; GENESIS 25:6; LUKE 16:8"> He very wisely sent some of his other sons to other cities in the areas of the tribes of Judah and Benjamin, and to all the other cities that had walls around them. He gave them plenty of supplies and many wives.</span>
</div>
</div>
