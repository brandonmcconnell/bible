## King Ahaziah of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 21:16–17; 2 KINGS 8:24–29; 2 CHRONICLES 36:1"> The people of Jerusalem appointed Jehoram’s youngest son Ahaziah as their king, because the men from Philistia who had invaded Judah with some Arabs had killed all of Jehoram’s other sons. So Ahaziah started to rule Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:28; 2 KINGS 8:26; 2 CHRONICLES 21:6"> Ahaziah was 22 years old when he became king. He ruled in Jerusalem for one year. His mother’s name was Athaliah, a granddaughter of King Omri of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 27:12–13; GENESIS 6:4–5; JUDGES 17:4–5"> King Ahaziah conducted his life just like the members of Ahab’s family had done, because his mother encouraged him to do things that are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 13:20; 2 CHRONICLES 24:17–18; PROVERBS 19:27"> He did things that Yahweh considers to be evil, like the descendants of Ahab had done, because after Ahaziah’s father died, they became his advisors. And Ahaziah died as a result of heeding their bad advice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 KINGS 8:28–29; 2 CHRONICLES 19:2; PSALMS 1:1"> He also did what they advised when he went with Joram, the son of King Ahab of Israel, to fight against the army of Hazael, the king of Syria, at Ramoth-Gilead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 KINGS 9:15; 2 KINGS 8:29; 2 KINGS 10:13–14"> Then King Ahaziah went down to Jezreel city to see Joram, the son of King Ahab, because he had been wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 10:15; 2 KINGS 9:21; MALACHI 4:3"> It was during that visit that God caused Ahaziah to die. When Ahaziah arrived, he went with Joram to meet Jehu, the son of Nimshi, whom Yahweh had appointed to kill all the descendants of Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 KINGS 10:10–14"> While Jehu and the men who were with him were killing the descendants of Ahab, they found the leaders of Judah and the sons of Ahaziah’s relatives who had been working for Ahaziah, and they killed all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 21:17; 2 KINGS 9:27–28; 2 CHRONICLES 21:20–1"> Then Jehu went to find Ahaziah, and his soldiers found Ahaziah while he was hiding in Samaria city. They brought him to Jehu and executed him. Then they buried his corpse, because they said, “He deserves to be buried, because he was a descendant of Jehoshaphat, who tried hard to please Yahweh.” There were no descendants of Ahaziah who were powerful enough to become the kings of Judah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Athaliah and Joash

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 KINGS 11:1–3; 2 CHRONICLES 22:2–4"> When Athaliah, the mother of Ahaziah, saw that her son had been killed, she commanded that all the members of Ahaziah’s family who might become king must be executed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:4; 2 KINGS 11:2; Isaiah 65:8"> But Jehosheba, the daughter of King Jehoram, took Joash, Ahaziah’s very young son, away from the other sons of the king who were about to be murdered, and she hid him and his nursemaid in a bedroom in the temple. Because Jehosheba, who was the daughter of King Jehoram and the wife of the Supreme Priest Jehoiada, was Ahaziah’s sister, she hid the child, with the result that Athaliah could not kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 12:8; PSALMS 73:18–19; JEREMIAH 12:1"> He remained hidden there for six years while Athaliah ruled Judah.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
