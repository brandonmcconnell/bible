## The Sacred Chest was brought to the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 6:12; 2 CHRONICLES 1:4; NUMBERS 10:33"> Then King Solomon summoned to Jerusalem all the elders of Israel, all the leaders of the tribes and of the families/clans. He wanted them to help to bring to the temple Yahweh’s Sacred Chest from Zion Hill, where it was in the part of the city called ‘The City of David’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:2; 2 CHRONICLES 7:8–10; LEVITICUS 23:34–36"> So all the leaders of Israel gathered together along with the king, during the Festival of Living in Temporary Shelters, in October.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:3; JOSHUA 3:6; JOSHUA 6:6"> When they had all arrived, the descendants of Levi lifted up the Sacred Chest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:6; 2 CHRONICLES 1:3; 1 KINGS 8:4"> and they carried it and the Sacred Tent and the sacred things that were inside it. The priests, who were also descended from Levi, carried them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 29:21; 2 SAMUEL 6:13; 1 CHRONICLES 16:1–2"> King Solomon and many of the other people of Israel who had gathered there walked in front of the Sacred Chest. And they sacrificed a huge amount of sheep and cattle. No one was able to count them because there were very many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 132:8; 1 KINGS 6:23–28; 1 KINGS 8:6–7"> The priests then brought the Sacred Chest into the Most Holy Place, the inner room of the temple, and they placed it under the wings of the statues of winged creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 25:12–15; NUMBERS 4:6; EXODUS 37:3–5"> The wings of those statues spread out over the Sacred Chest and over the poles by which it was carried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:8–9"> The poles were very long, with the result that they could be seen by those who were standing at the entrance to the Most Holy Place, but they could not be seen by anyone standing outside the temple. Those poles are still there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:4; 2 CHRONICLES 6:11; DEUTERONOMY 10:2–5"> The only things that were inside the Sacred Chest were the two stone tablets that Moses had put there at Sinai Mountain, where Yahweh made an agreement with the Israeli people after they came out of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 35:4; 2 CHRONICLES 29:5; JOB 1:5"> Then the priests left the Holy Place. All the priests who were there, from every group, had performed the rituals to cause them to be acceptable to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 15:24; PSALMS 68:25; 1 CHRONICLES 6:39"> All the descendants of Levi who were musicians—Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, their sons and their other relatives—stood on the east side of the altar. They were wearing linen clothes, and they were playing cymbals, harps, and lyres. There were 120 other priests who were blowing trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 3:11; JEREMIAH 33:11; 2 CHRONICLES 7:3"> The men blowing trumpets, those playing the cymbals and other musical instruments, and the singers, made music together, praising Yahweh and singing this song:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yahweh is good to us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he faithfully loves us forever.”</span>Then suddenly the temple was filled with a cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EXODUS 40:35; 1 KINGS 8:11; Isaiah 6:1–4"> The glorious radiance of Yahweh filled the temple, with the result that the priests were not able to continue doing their work.</span>
</div>
