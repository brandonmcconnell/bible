## King Asa’s rule in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 KINGS 15:11; 2 CHRONICLES 31:20; 1 KINGS 15:14"> Asa did things that Yahweh his God considers to be right and good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:5; EXODUS 34:13; 2 KINGS 23:14"> His workers got rid of the altars to worship foreign gods that were on the high hills. They smashed the sacred stone pillars and cut down the poles for worshiping the goddess Asherah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 30:19; PSALMS 119:10; PSALMS 101:2–8"> Asa commanded the people of Judah to worship only Yahweh, the God whom their ancestors worshiped, and to obey his laws and commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 34:4; 2 CHRONICLES 34:7"> His workers destroyed all the shrines on the hilltops and the altars for burning incense to idols in every town in Judah. As a result, there was peace while Asa ruled the kingdom of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 15:15; 1 CHRONICLES 22:9; JOB 34:29"> His workers built cities and constructed walls around them. No army attacked Judah during that time, because Yahweh enabled them to have peace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 PETER 3:12; 2 CHRONICLES 14:6; JEREMIAH 29:12–14"> Asa said to the people of Judah, “We should protect these towns by building walls around them, with watchtowers and gates that have bars. This country still belongs to us because we have requested Yahweh our God to help us. We requested him for his help, and he has given us peace in our entire country.” So they built buildings and prospered.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 13:3; 2 CHRONICLES 17:14–19; 2 CHRONICLES 11:1"> Asa had an army of 300,000 men from Judah. They all carried large shields and spears. He also had 280,000 men from the tribe of Benjamin in his army. They carried smaller shields, and bows and arrows. They were all brave soldiers.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 16:8; 2 CHRONICLES 12:2–3; 2 CHRONICLES 11:8"> Zerah, a man from Ethiopia/Sudan, marched with a huge army and 300 chariots to attack the army of Judah and Benjamin. They went as far as the town of Mareshah about 25 miles/southwest of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 1:17; JOSHUA 19:4"> Asa went with his army to fight against them, and both armies took their positions in the Zephathah Valley.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:19; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:9–10; PSALMS 22:5"> Then Asa cried out to Yahweh his God, saying, “Yahweh, there is no one like you who can help those who have very little power to resist a mighty army. Yahweh our God, help us, because we are relying on you; and trusting in you we have come to fight against this huge army. Yahweh, you are our God; do not allow anyone to defeat you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 13:15; PSALMS 136:17–18; DEUTERONOMY 28:7"> Then Yahweh enabled Asa and the army of Judah to defeat the army from Ethiopia. They fled,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GENESIS 10:19; GENESIS 26:1; GENESIS 20:1"> and Asa and his army pursued them to the southwest as far as Gerar. A huge number of the soldiers from Ethiopia were killed, with the result that those who were not killed were unable to fight any more. They were greatly defeated by Yahweh and his army, and the men of Judah carried away a great amount of their possessions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 17:10; GENESIS 35:5; 2 CHRONICLES 20:29"> The men of Judah were able to destroy the people in villages near Gerar because Yahweh had caused the people there to become terrified and unable to fight. The army of Judah took away all the valuable things from those villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="NUMBERS 31:30–47; NUMBERS 31:9; 1 SAMUEL 30:20"> They also attacked the places where the local people who took care of domestic animals had set up their tents, and they took away big herds of sheep and goats and camels. Then they returned to Jerusalem.</span>
</div>
</div>
