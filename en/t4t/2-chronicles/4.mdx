## The furnishings for the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:64; EZEKIEL 43:13–17; 1 KINGS 9:25"> Solomon’s workers made a square bronze altar that was 10 yards wide on each side, and it was 5 yards high.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="TITUS 3:5; ZECHARIAH 13:1; EXODUS 30:18–21"> They also made a very large round tank that was made of metal and cast in a clay mold. It was 10 yards wide/across, and 5 yards high. It was 15 yards around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="EZEKIEL 1:10; EZEKIEL 10:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:9–10"> Below the outer rim there were two rows of small figures of bulls that were part of the metal of the basin. Each row had 300 figures of bulls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:19–20; REVELATION 21:14; MARK 16:15"> The basin was set on twelve statues of bulls. There were three statues that faced north, three that faced west, three that faced south, and three that faced east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:26"> The sides of the tank were 3 inches thick, and its rim was shaped like a cup that curved outward like the petals of flowers. The basin held about 16,500 gallons of water.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:38; EXODUS 30:18–21; 1 KINGS 7:40"> They also made ten basins for washing the animals that were to be sacrificed. The priests used the very large tank for washing themselves.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 25:31–40; 1 KINGS 7:49; JOHN 8:12"> They also made ten gold lampstands according to what Solomon had instructed them. They put them in the temple, five on the south side and five on the north side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:48; MALACHI 1:12; EXODUS 25:23–30"> They made ten tables and put them in the temple, five on the south side and five on the north side. They also made 100 gold bowls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 KINGS 6:36; 2 KINGS 21:5; 1 KINGS 7:12"> They also constructed one courtyard for the priests, and a larger courtyard for the other people. They made doors for the courtyards and covered them with thin sheets of bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:39"> They placed the very large tank on the south side of the temple, at the southeast corner.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:40–51; 1 KINGS 7:14"> They also made pots and shovels for the ashes of the altar, and other small bowls.<br />So Huram and his workers finished the work that King Solomon had given him to do at the temple of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:41; 2 CHRONICLES 3:15–17"> These were the things that they made: the two large pillars, the two bowl-shaped top parts on top of the pillars, the two sets of carvings that resembled chains to decorate the tops of the two pillars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:20; 1 KINGS 7:42; JEREMIAH 52:23"> the 400 carvings that resembled pomegranates that were placed in two rows, that decorated the bowl-shaped tops of the two pillars,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:27–43"> the stands, and the basins that were placed on them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> the very large tank, and the statues of twelve bulls underneath it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 2:13; 1 KINGS 7:13–14; 2 CHRONICLES 4:11"> the pots, shovels, meat forks, and all the other things needed for the work at the altar.<br />All those things that Huram and his workers made for King Solomon were made of polished bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:46"> They made them by pouring melted bronze into the clay molds that Huram had set up near the Jordan river between Succoth and Zarethan cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 KINGS 7:47; JEREMIAH 52:20; 1 CHRONICLES 22:14"> All of those things that Solomon told them to make used a very large amount of bronze; no one tried to weigh it all.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="REVELATION 9:13; DANIEL 5:2–3; EXODUS 25:23–30"> Solomon’s workers also made all these things that were put at the temple: the golden altar, the tables on which the priests put the sacred bread,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 4:7; EXODUS 25:31–37; 1 KINGS 6:5"> the pure gold lampstands and the pure gold lamps, in which the priests put oil to burn in front of the Most Holy Place as God had told Moses that the priests should do,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 KINGS 6:29; EXODUS 37:20; 1 KINGS 6:35"> the pure gold decorations that resembled flowers, and the lamps and tongs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 KINGS 12:13; 2 KINGS 25:14; JEREMIAH 52:18"> the pure gold wick trimmers and bowls for sprinkling, and dishes and lamp snuffers, the gold doors of the temple and the doors to the main hall.</span>
</div>
</div>
