## King Jotham of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 3:12; Isaiah 1:1; MATTHEW 1:9"> Jotham was 25 years old when he became the king of Judah. He ruled from Jerusalem for 16 years. His mother was Jerushah, the daughter of the priest Zadok.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 KINGS 15:34–35; ACTS 5:13; 2 CHRONICLES 26:4"> Jotham did many things that pleased Yahweh like his father Uzziah did. He obeyed Yahweh and did things that are right. He did many things that his father Uzziah had done, but he did not burn incense in the temple, like his father had done. However, the people of Judah continued to sin against Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 33:14; Nehemiah 3:26–27; JEREMIAH 20:2"> Jotham’s workers rebuilt the Upper Gate of the temple, and they did a lot of work to repair the wall near Ophel Hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 11:5–10; 2 CHRONICLES 26:9–10; JOSHUA 14:12–13"> They built towns in the hills of Judah, and they built forts and defense towers in the forests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JUDGES 11:4–33; 2 SAMUEL 10:1–14; JEREMIAH 49:1–6"> During the time that Jotham was the king of Judah, his army attacked and defeated the army of the Ammon people-group. Then, every year during the next three years, he required the Anmon people-group to pay to him four tons of silver, 60,000 bushels of wheat, and 60,000 bushels of barley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:5; 2 CHRONICLES 19:3"> Jotham faithfully obeyed Yahweh his God, and as a result he became a very powerful king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:22–23; 2 KINGS 15:36; 2 CHRONICLES 32:32–33"> A record of everything else that Jotham did during the time that he was the king, including the wars that his army fought, is written in the scroll called ‘the History of the Kings of Israel and Judah’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 27:1"> After Jotham had ruled Judah for 16 years, he died when he was 41 years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 KINGS 15:38"> He was buried in Jerusalem, and his son Ahaz became the king of Judah.</span>
