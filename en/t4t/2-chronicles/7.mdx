## Solomon dedicated the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:38; 1 CHRONICLES 21:26; LEVITICUS 9:23–24"> When Solomon finished praying, fire came down from the sky and burned all the offerings and sacrifices that were on the altar, and the glorious radiance of Yahweh filled the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="REVELATION 15:8; 2 CHRONICLES 5:14; EXODUS 24:17"> The radiance was extremely bright, with the result that the priests could not enter the temple of Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 5:13; 2 CHRONICLES 20:21; LUKE 1:50"> When all the Israeli people who were there saw the fire coming down and the glory of Yahweh above the temple, they prostrated themselves with their faces touching the ground. They worshiped and thanked Yahweh, singing,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yahweh is always good to us;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he faithfully loves us forever.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:62–63"> Then the king and all the people who were there dedicated the temple to Yahweh by offering more sacrifices to him. King Solomon gave 22,000 cattle and 120,000 sheep and goats to be sacrificed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 5:12; 1 CHRONICLES 15:16–21; 2 CHRONICLES 7:3"> The priests stood in their positions, and the other descendants of Levi stood in their positions holding the musical instruments to praise Yahweh, instruments that King David had caused to be made for praising Yahweh and thanking him. They sang, “He faithfully loves us forever.” Facing the other descendants of Levi the priests stood, blowing their trumpets, while all the Israeli people were standing and listening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:64–66; HEBREWS 13:10–12; 2 CHRONICLES 36:14"> Solomon dedicated the middle part of the courtyard in front of the temple. Then he gave offerings to be completely burned there along with the fat of the animals to be sacrificed to maintain fellowship with Yahweh. The priests burned them there in the courtyard because in addition to those things there were offerings of grain, with the result that there was not enough space on the bronze altar to burn all those sacrifices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 15:18; 1 KINGS 8:65; Nehemiah 8:13–18"> Solomon and the other people celebrated the Festival of Living in Temporary Shelters for seven days. There was a huge group of people who celebrated with him. Some of them came from as far away as Lebo-Hamath in the far north and the border of Egypt in the far south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 23:36; 1 KINGS 8:65; JOEL 1:14"> On the eighth/next day they gathered again to worship Yahweh. They had celebrated the dedication of the altar for seven days and the Festival of Living in Temporary Shelters for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:66; PSALMS 100:2; DEUTERONOMY 12:18"> Then on the next day Solomon sent them to their homes. They were very joyful because of all the good things that Yahweh had done for David and Solomon and for all his Israeli people.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh appeared to Solomon again

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:1–9; ECCLESIASTES 2:10–11; 2 CHRONICLES 2:1"> Solomon’s workers finished building the temple and Solomon’s palace. And Solomon finished doing everything else that he had planned to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="GENESIS 17:1; 1 KINGS 9:2; 2 CHRONICLES 7:16"> Then Yahweh appeared to him one night in a dream and said to him, “I have heard your prayer, and I have chosen this temple to be the place where my people will offer sacrifices to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 6:26–28; DEUTERONOMY 11:17; REVELATION 11:6"> “When I prevent any rain from falling, or when I command locusts to eat all the crops, or when I send a plague among my people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:40–41; JAMES 4:9–10; Isaiah 55:6–7"> if the people who belong to me humble themselves and pray, and request me to help them, and if they turn away from their sinful behavior, then I will hear from heaven. I will forgive them for having sinned and I will cause them to prosper again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 PETER 3:12; 2 CHRONICLES 6:40; Nehemiah 1:6"> I &#91;SYN&#93; will see them and I &#91;SYN&#93; will hear them when they pray to me in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:15; 2 CHRONICLES 7:12; 2 CHRONICLES 6:5–6"> I have chosen and have ◄set apart/dedicated► this temple in order that people may worship &#91;MTY&#93; me there forever. I will always watch over &#91;MTY&#93; it and protect &#91;IDM&#93; it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 3:7; EZEKIEL 36:27; 1 KINGS 11:38"> “And as for you, if you obey me as David your father did, and if you do all that I command you to do, and obey all my laws and decrees,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 6:16; JEREMIAH 33:20–21; 2 SAMUEL 7:13–16"> I will make sure that your descendants will always be kings, which is what I promised to David your father, saying, ‘Some of your descendants will always be the kings of Israel.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:14; DEUTERONOMY 28:15; LEVITICUS 26:33–46"> “But if you Israelis turn away from me and disobey the decrees and commands that I have given to you, and you start to worship other gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:20; DEUTERONOMY 28:37; JEREMIAH 24:9"> I will cause you to be expelled from this land that I have given to you, and I will abandon this temple that I have set apart to be the place where people should worship me. I will cause it to be despised and ridiculed by people of all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:8–9; JEREMIAH 16:10–12; JEREMIAH 19:8"> Although this temple is now greatly respected, when that happens, all the people who pass by will be appalled, and they will say, ‘Why has Yahweh done terrible things like this to this country and to this temple?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="DANIEL 9:12; LAMENTATIONS 2:16–17; EZEKIEL 14:23"> And others will reply, ‘It happened because they rejected Yahweh, the God to whom their ancestors belonged, the one who brought their ancestors out of Egypt, and they have been worshiping other gods and trying to please them. And that is why Yahweh has caused them to experience all these disasters.’ ”</span>
</div>
