## King Abijah’s rule in Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 12:16; 1 KINGS 15:1–8"> When Jeroboam had been ruling Israel for almost 18 years, Abijah became the king of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 11:20; 1 KINGS 15:6–7; JUDGES 19:16"> He ruled in Jerusalem for three years. His mother was Micaiah (OR, Maacah), the daughter of Uriel from Gibeah town.<br />There was a war between the armies of Abijah and Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 26:12–13; 1 CHRONICLES 21:5; 1 SAMUEL 17:1–3"> Abijah went into the battle, taking 400,000 of his capable soldiers, and Jeroboam prepared to fight them, taking 800,000 of his capable troops.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:22; 2 CHRONICLES 15:2; GENESIS 10:18"> Abijah stood on the top of Zemaraim Mountain, which is in the hilly area that belonged to the tribe of Ephraim, and he shouted, “Jeroboam and all you other people of Israel, listen to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 18:19; LEVITICUS 2:13; MARK 9:49–50"> You should know that Yahweh, the God to whom all we Israelis belong, has appointed David and his descendants to be the kings of Israel forever. He has confirmed that by making a permanent agreement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:26; 1 KINGS 12:20; 2 CHRONICLES 10:19"> But Jeroboam, who was only an official of David’s son King Solomon, rebelled against his king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JUDGES 9:4; 2 CHRONICLES 12:13; ACTS 17:5"> And when Solomon’s son Rehoboam became king and was still young and inexperienced, a group of worthless scoundrels gathered around you, Jeroboam, and rebelled against Rehoboam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 KINGS 12:28; 2 CHRONICLES 11:15; LUKE 19:14"> “And now you are planning to fight against the kingdom that Yahweh established to be governed by David’s descendants. It is true that you have a huge army, and you and your soldiers have brought with you the golden statues of calves that Jeroboam’s workers made to be gods for all of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 11:14–15; JEREMIAH 2:11; EXODUS 29:35"> But you expelled the priests that Yahweh appointed, men who are descendants of Aaron the first Supreme Priest, and you expelled the descendants of Levi, and you appointed the priests that you wanted, like the people of other countries do. You allow anyone to become a priest of idols that are not gods if he comes to dedicate himself to be a priest by sacrificing a young bull and seven rams.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EXODUS 19:5–6; 2 CHRONICLES 11:16–17; ZECHARIAH 13:9"> “As for us, Yahweh is our God, and we have not abandoned him. Our priests who serve Yahweh are descendants of Aaron, and the descendants of Levi assist them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 2:4; LEVITICUS 24:2–9; EXODUS 29:38"> Every morning and every evening they present to Yahweh offerings to be completely burned on the altar, and they burn fragrant incense. Each week they place the sacred bread on the sacred table, and each morning they light the lamps that are on the gold lampstand. We are obeying what Yahweh our God requires us to do. But you have abandoned him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ACTS 5:39; ROMANS 8:31; NUMBERS 10:8–9"> Yahweh is with us; he is our leader. The priests whom he has appointed will blow their trumpets to signal that we are ready to fight a battle against you. You Israeli men, do not fight against Yahweh, the God to whom your ancestors belonged, because you will not be successful and win the battle against him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:4; JOSHUA 8:9; PROVERBS 21:30"> While he was speaking, Jeroboam sent some of his troops around the army of Judah. So while the soldiers who were with Jeroboam were in front of the army of Judah, the other soldiers of Israel were behind the army of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 14:11; EXODUS 14:10; JOSHUA 8:20"> When the soldiers of Judah turned and saw that they were going to be attacked from the front and from the rear, they cried out to Yahweh. The priests blew their trumpets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 14:12; JOSHUA 11:8; PSALMS 118:4–7"> and the men of Judah shouted a loud battle-cry. Then Yahweh enabled Abijah and the army of Judah to defeat Jeroboam and the army of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 16:8; JUDGES 11:21; JOSHUA 10:12"> The soldiers of Israel fled from the soldiers of Judah, and God enabled the army of Judah to defeat them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 28:6; 2 CHRONICLES 13:3; 2 CHRONICLES 13:12"> Abijah and his troops struck the capable soldiers of Israel and killed 500,000 of them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 5:20; 2 CHRONICLES 14:11; NAHUM 1:7"> So the soldiers of Israel were defeated, and the soldiers of Judah won the battle because they trusted in Yahweh, the God to whom their ancestors belonged.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 15:8; JOSHUA 15:9; JOSHUA 10:39"> Abijah’s army pursued the army of Jeroboam, and they captured from the people of Israel the cities of Bethel, Jeshanah, and Ephron, and the surrounding villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:38; 1 KINGS 14:20; ACTS 12:23"> During the remaining time that Abijah ruled, Jeroboam did not become powerful again. Then Yahweh caused him to become very ill, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 9:5; 2 SAMUEL 5:12–13; JUDGES 10:4"> But Abijah became more powerful. He married 14 wives and had 22 sons and 16 daughters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 9:29; 2 CHRONICLES 12:15; 2 CHRONICLES 24:27"> An account of the other things that Abijah did while he was the king, including what he said and what he did, is in the scroll written by the prophet Iddo.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
