## Pray that more and more people will believe the message about our Lord Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:25; COLOSSIANS 4:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:9"> As for the other matters, our fellow believers, pray for us that more and more people will hear &#91;PRS&#93; our message about our Lord Jesus. Pray that they will believe this message, as you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 4:3–4; ROMANS 15:31; DEUTERONOMY 32:20"> Pray also for us that we will be rescued &#123;that God will rescue us&#125; from those very perverse/evil people here who cause us to suffer. Pray like that because they and many others refuse to believe &#91;LIT&#93; this message.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Our Lord Jesus will cause you to continue to be steadfast and he will protect you from the evil one.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:18; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:24; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:13"> Remember that our Lord Jesus is trustworthy! So we are sure that he will cause you to continue to be steadfast. We are also sure that he will protect you from Satan, the evil one (OR, from evil).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:6; PHILEMON 1:21; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:10"> Because of your close relationship with our Lord Jesus, we are confident that you are obeying what we have commanded you, and we are confident that you will obey what we are commanding you in this letter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 119:36; 1 CHRONICLES 29:18; DEUTERONOMY 30:6"> We pray that our Lord Jesus would enable you to continue knowing that God loves you. We pray also that Christ will cause you to continue being steadfast (OR, being steadfast as he was steadfast).</span>
</div>
</div>

## We command you to disassociate yourselves from every fellow believer who refuses to work.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 3:10–11; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:7; ROMANS 16:17"> Our fellow believers, we command you, with the authority &#91;MTY&#93; that our Lord Jesus Christ has given to us, that you stop associating with every fellow believer who is lazy and refuses to work. That is, you must stay away from those who are not conducting their lives in the manner that we taught you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 3:6; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:16"> We tell you this because you yourselves know that you should behave like we behaved. We ◄did not just sit around without working/were never lazy► while we were living among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 6:11; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:9; EPHESIANS 4:28"> Specifically, we did not eat anyone’s food if we did not pay that person for it. Instead, we worked very hard ◄to support ourselves/to earn the money to buy what we needed►. We worked hard &#91;DOU&#93; during the day and during the night, in order that we would not have to depend on any of you to supply what we needed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 3:7; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:6; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:4–14"> We have always had the right to depend on you for money, but instead we worked hard in order to make ourselves examples for you, in order that you should behave like we behave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="GENESIS 3:19; PROVERBS 13:4; PROVERBS 20:4"> Remember that when we were there with you, we kept commanding you that if any fellow believer refuses to work, you should not give him food to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:13; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:6; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:11"> We are commanding the same thing again now, because someone has told us that some of you are lazy and not working at all, just interfering with what other people are doing!</span>
</div>
</div>

## We command those fellow believers who are not working to support themselves by settling down and working.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EPHESIANS 4:28; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:11; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:8"> We command those fellow believers who are not working, and urge them by the authority &#91;MTY&#93; of our Lord Jesus Christ, that they settle down and ◄support themselves by working/work to earn money to buy what they need►.</span>
</div>
</div>

## You others, do not stop doing what is right because you are discouraged.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:9–10; HEBREWS 12:3; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:1"> Fellow believers! Do not ever get tired of doing what is right!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Publicly identify any fellow believer who does not obey what we have written in this letter and do not associate with him.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="TITUS 3:10; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:6; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:11"> If any fellow believer refuses to work and does not obey what I have written in this letter, ◄publicly identify that person/tell the congregation what that person is doing►. Then do not associate with him, in order that he may become ashamed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="TITUS 3:10; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:5"> Do not think of him as though he was your enemy; instead, warn him as you would warn your other fellow believers.</span>
</div>
</div>

## I pray that our Lord Jesus himself will give peace to you always and in every situation.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 29:11; JOHN 16:33; MATTHEW 1:23"> I pray that our Lord Jesus himself, who gives inner peace to his people, will give inner peace to you always ◄and in every situation/whatever happens►. In closing, I pray that our Lord Jesus will continue to bless you all. I, Paul, am greeting you and I am writing this myself in order that you may know that it is I who have sent this letter. 2 Thessalonians 3:16b-18<br />I pray that our Lord Jesus will continue to help you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 16:21; 2 THESSALONIANS 1:5; COLOSSIANS 4:18"> Now, having taken the pen from my scribe, I, Paul, am sending this greeting to you as I write this myself &#91;SYN&#93;. I do this in all my letters in order that you may know that it is truly I who have sent this letter. This is how I always end my letters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ROMANS 16:20; ROMANS 16:23"> I pray that our Lord Jesus Christ will continue to act kindly to you all.</span>
</div>
</div>
