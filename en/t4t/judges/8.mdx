## Gideon killed Zebah and Zalmunna

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 19:41; JUDGES 12:1–6; JOB 5:2"> Then the descendants of Ephraim said to Gideon, “Why have you acted toward us like this? When you went out to fight against the people of Midian, why did you not summon us to help you?” They rebuked Gideon severely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 13:4–7; PHILIPPIANS 2:2–3; JAMES 3:13–18"> But Gideon replied, “I have done &#91;RHQ&#93; very little compared with what you have done! My small clan of descendants of Abiezer only started the battle, but your very large group of descendants of Ephraim helped me to finish the task very well. It is like the final grapes of the harvest being much better than the first grapes that are picked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:1; JUDGES 7:24–25; JOHN 4:37"> God enabled you to defeat Oreb and Zeeb, the generals of the army from Midian. That is &#91;RHQ&#93; much more important than what I did!” After Gideon told them that, they no longer resented what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="GALATIANS 6:9; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:16; 1 SAMUEL 14:28–29"> Then Gideon and his 300 men went east and crossed the Jordan River. Although they were very tired, they continued to pursue their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 33:17; PSALMS 60:6; 1 SAMUEL 25:18"> When they arrived at Succoth town, Gideon said to the town leaders, “Please give my men some food! They are very tired. We are pursuing Zebah and Zalmunna, the kings of Midian.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:11; PHILIPPIANS 2:21; PROVERBS 18:23"> But the leaders of Succoth replied, “You have not caught &#91;RHQ&#93; Zebah and Zalmunna yet. So why should we give food to your troops now? Catch them first, and then we will give you food.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JUDGES 7:15"> Gideon replied, “Because you said that, after Yahweh enables us to defeat Zebah and Zalmunna, we will return. And then we will make whips from thorns from the desert, and with them we will rip the flesh off your bones!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="GENESIS 32:30–31; 1 KINGS 12:25"> From there, Gideon and his 300 men went to Peniel and asked for food there, but the people gave him the same answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 8:17; 1 KINGS 22:27–28"> So he said to the men of Peniel, “After I defeat those kings, I will return and tear down this tower!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 7:12; JUDGES 20:25; JUDGES 20:2"> By that time, Zebah and Zalmunna had gone to Karkor town with 15,000 troops. They were all that were left of the armies that had come from the east. 120,000 of their men had already been killed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:35; NUMBERS 32:42; JUDGES 18:27"> Gideon and his men went east along the road on which caravans travel. They went past Nobah and Jogbehah villages and arrived at the enemy camp by surprise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 83:11; AMOS 2:14; JOB 12:16–21"> Zebah and Zalmunna fled, but Gideon’s men pursued them and captured them and all their warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After that, Gideon and his men took Zebah and Zalmunna with them and started to return, going through Heres Pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 1:24–25; 1 SAMUEL 30:11–15"> There he captured a young man from Succoth, and demanded that he write down the names of all of the leaders in the town. The young man wrote down seventy-seven names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 8:6–7"> Then Gideon and his men returned to Succoth and said to those leaders, “Here are Zebah and Zalmunna. When we were here before, you made fun of me and said ‘You have not &#91;RHQ&#93; caught Zebah and Zalmunna yet! After you catch them, we will give your exhausted men some food.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 8:7; Ezra 2:6; PROVERBS 10:13"> Then Gideon’s men took the town leaders and whipped them with whips made from briers from the desert, to teach them that they deserved to be punished for not giving them any food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JUDGES 8:9; 1 KINGS 12:25"> Then they went to Peniel and tore down the tower, and killed all the men in the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JUDGES 4:6; JUDE 1:16; PSALMS 89:12"> Then Gideon said to Zebah and Zalmunna, “The men you killed near Tabor Mountain, what did they look like?” They replied, “They were like you; they all looked like they were sons of a king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Gideon replied, “They were my brothers! Just as surely as Yahweh lives, I would not kill you if you had not killed them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 149:9; 1 SAMUEL 15:33; JOSHUA 10:24"> Then he turned to his oldest son, Jether. He said to him, “Kill them!” But Jether was only a boy, and he was afraid, so he did not pull out his dagger to kill them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 83:11; JUDGES 8:26; Isaiah 3:18"> Then Zebah and Zalmunna said to Gideon, “Do not ask a young boy to do the work that a man should do!” So Gideon killed both of them. Then he took the gold ornaments from the necks of their camels.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gideon refused to be their king

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOHN 6:15; JUDGES 9:8–15; 1 SAMUEL 12:12"> Then a group of Israeli men came to Gideon and said to him, “You be our ruler! We want you and your son and your grandsons to be our rulers, because you rescued us from the Midian army.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:22; 1 SAMUEL 12:12; 1 SAMUEL 10:19"> But Gideon replied, “No, I will not rule over you, and my son will not rule over you. Yahweh will rule over you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 25:13; GENESIS 37:25; GENESIS 37:28"> Then he said, “I request only one thing. I request that each of you give me one earring from the things you captured after the battle.” &#91;All the men descended from Ishmael wore gold earrings.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> They replied, “We will be glad to give earrings to you!” So they spread a cloth on the ground, and each man threw on it one gold earring that he had taken from a man he had killed in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="REVELATION 18:16; JOHN 19:2; REVELATION 18:12"> The weight of all the earrings was ◄43 pounds/19.4 kg.►. That did not include other things that they gave to Gideon—the other ornaments or the pendants or the clothes that the kings of Midian wore or the gold chains that were on the necks of their camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JUDGES 17:5; JUDGES 18:14; PSALMS 106:39"> Gideon made/decorated a sacred vest from the gold, and later he put it in his hometown, Ophrah. But soon the Israeli people started to worship the vest. So it became like a trap &#91;MET&#93; for the people, causing them to worship it instead of worshiping only God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gideon died

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JUDGES 5:31; JUDGES 3:11; JUDGES 3:30"> That is how the Israelis defeated the people from Midian. The people of Midian did not become strong enough to attack Israel again. So while Gideon was alive, there was peace in the land for 40 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JUDGES 6:32; JUDGES 7:1; 1 SAMUEL 12:11"> Gideon went back home to live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JUDGES 9:5; JUDGES 9:2; 1 KINGS 11:3"> He had many wives, and they bore him seventy sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JUDGES 9:18; GENESIS 22:24; GENESIS 16:15"> He also had a slave wife in Shechem town, who bore him a son whom he named Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="GENESIS 25:8; JUDGES 6:24; JOB 5:26"> Gideon died when he was very old. They buried his body in the grave where his father Joash was buried, at Ophrah, in the land belonging to the descendants of Abiether.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JUDGES 9:46; JUDGES 2:19; JUDGES 9:4"> But as soon as Gideon died, the Israelis stopped worshiping God and started worshiping the images of the god Baal, like &#91;MET&#93; adultresses leave their husbands and go to sleep with other men. They made a statue of a new god called Baal-Berith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="PSALMS 78:42; PSALMS 106:21; PSALMS 78:11"> They forgot about Yahweh, the one who had rescued them from all their enemies that surrounded them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:14–15; JUDGES 9:5; JUDGES 9:16–19"> And even though Gideon had done many good things for the Israelis, they were not kind to Gideon’s family.</span>
</div>
</div>
