## Samson got revenge on the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="GENESIS 38:17; GENESIS 29:21; LUKE 15:29"> During the time that they harvested wheat, Samson took a young goat to Timnah as a present for his wife. He planned to sleep with &#91;EUP&#93; his wife, but her father would not let him go into her room.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 14:20; JUDGES 14:16; GENESIS 38:14"> He said to Samson, “I really thought that you hated her. So I gave her to the man who had been your best man at the wedding, and she married him. But look, her younger sister is &#91;RHQ&#93; more beautiful than she is. You can marry her!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDGES 14:15"> Samson replied, “No! And this time I have a right to get revenge on you Philistines!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 2:15; PSALMS 63:10; LAMENTATIONS 5:18"> Then he went out into the fields and caught 300 foxes. He tied their tails together, two-by-two. He fastened torches to each pair of tails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 14:30; EXODUS 22:6"> Then he lit the torches and let the foxes run through the fields of the Philistines. The fire from the torches burned all the grain to the ground, including the grain that had been cut and piled in bundles. The fire also burned down their grapevines and their olive trees.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDGES 14:15; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:6; PROVERBS 22:8"> The Philistines asked, “Who did this?” Someone told them, “Samson did it. He married a woman from Timnah, but then his father-in-law gave her to the man who was Samson’s best man at the wedding, and she married him.” So the Philistines went to Timnah and got the woman and her father, and burned them to death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JUDGES 14:4; ROMANS 12:19; JUDGES 14:19"> Samson found out about that, and he said to them, “Because you have done this, I will not stop until I get revenge on you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 63:3; Isaiah 63:6; Isaiah 25:10"> So he attacked the Philistines furiously, and killed many of them. Then he went to hide in a cave in the large rock at a place called Etam.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 15:17; JUDGES 15:19; JUDGES 15:14"> The Philistines did not know where he was, so they went up to where the descendants of Judah lived, set up their tents near Lehi town and then raided the town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> The men there asked the Philistines, “Why have you attacked us?”<br />The Philistines replied, “We have come to capture Samson. We have come to get revenge on him for what he did to us.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDGES 13:1; JUDGES 14:4; DEUTERONOMY 28:47–48"> Someone there knew where Samson was hiding. So 3,000 men from Judah went down to get Samson at the cave in the rock where he was hiding. They said to Samson, “Do you not realize that the people of Philistia are ruling over us? Do you not realize what they will do to us?”<br />Samson replied, “The only thing I did was that I got revenge on them for what they did to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JUDGES 8:21; ACTS 7:25; 1 KINGS 2:25"> But the men from Judah said to him, “We have come to tie you up and put you in the hands of the Philistines.”<br />Samson said, “All right, but promise me that you yourselves will not kill me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They replied, “We will just tie you up and take you to the Philistines. We will not kill you.” So they tied him with two new ropes, and led him away from the cave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 14:19; JUDGES 3:10; PSALMS 118:11"> When they arrived at Lehi, the Philistines came toward him, shouting triumphantly. But Yahweh’s Spirit came upon Samson powerfully. He snapped the ropes on his arms as easily as if they were stalks of burned flax, and the ropes fell off his wrists.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 23:10; LEVITICUS 26:8; JUDGES 3:31"> Then he saw a donkey’s jawbone lying on the ground. It was fresh, so it was hard. He picked it up and killed about 1,000 Philistine men with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then Samson wrote this poem:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“With the jawbone of a donkey</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have made them like a heap of dead donkeys.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With the jawbone of a donkey</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I killed 1,000 men.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> When he finished killing those men, he threw the jawbone away, and later that place was called Jawbone Hill.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 12:7–8; HEBREWS 11:32; JUDGES 8:4"> Then Samson was very thirsty, so he called out to Yahweh, “You have given me strength to win a great victory. So now must I die because of being thirsty, with the result that those heathen Philistines will take away my body and mutilate it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; GENESIS 45:27; PSALMS 34:6"> So God caused water to gush out of a depression in the ground at Lehi. Samson drank from it and soon felt strong again. He named that place ‘The spring of the one who called out’. That spring is still there at Lehi.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JUDGES 13:1; JUDGES 16:31; JUDGES 13:5"> Samson was the leader of the Israeli people for 20 years, but during that time the Philistines were the ones who really ruled over the land.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
