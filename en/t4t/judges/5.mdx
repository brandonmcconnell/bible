<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JUDGES 5:30; JUDGES 1:27; 1 KINGS 4:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“The kings of Canaan fought us at Taanach, near the springs in Megiddo Valley.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But since they did not defeat us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they did not carry away any silver or other treasures from the battle.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

</div>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="PSALMS 37:6; 2 SAMUEL 23:4; PSALMS 19:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But that is not what happened!
