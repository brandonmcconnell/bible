<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 2:21–22; DEUTERONOMY 7:22; PROVERBS 17:3"> At that time there were still many people-groups in Canaan. Yahweh left them there to test the Israeli people. But many of the Israelis in Canaan were ones who had not fought in any of the wars in Canaan. So Yahweh also left those people-groups in Canaan so that the descendants of those who had not fought in any of the wars might learn how to fight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOSHUA 13:3; JOSHUA 13:5; JUDGES 10:7"> This is a list of the people-groups that Yahweh left there: The Philistines and their five leaders, the people living in the area near Sidon city, the descendants of Canaan, and the descendants of Hiv who were living in the mountains of Lebanon between Baal-Hermon Mountain and Lebo-Hamath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 3:1; JUDGES 2:22; EXODUS 15:25"> Yahweh left these people-groups there to test the Israelis, to see if they would obey his commands which he had told Moses to give them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 106:34–38; GENESIS 10:15–18; EXODUS 3:8"> The Israelis lived among the Canaan people-group, the Hiv people-group, the Amor people-group, the Periz people-group, the Hiv people-group, and the Jebus people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:3–4; EXODUS 34:16; 1 KINGS 11:1–5"> Moses had told the people not to associate with any of those people. But the Israelis took daughters of people from those people-groups to be their own wives, and gave their own daughters to men of those groups, to marry them. And as a result they started to worship the gods of those people-groups.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Othniel became their leader

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EXODUS 34:13; 2 KINGS 23:6; 1 KINGS 18:19"> The Israelis did things that Yahweh said were very evil. They forgot about Yahweh, their God, and they started to worship the idols that represented the god Baal and the goddess Asherah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 2:14; HABAKKUK 3:7; JUDGES 4:9"> Yahweh became very angry with the Israelis. So he allowed king Cushan from Mesopotamia to conquer them and rule them for eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 3:15; JUDGES 1:13; PSALMS 22:5"> But when they pleaded to Yahweh to help them, he gave them a leader to rescue them. He was Othniel, the son of Caleb’s younger brother Kenaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 13:25; 1 SAMUEL 11:6; JUDGES 14:19"> Yahweh’s Spirit came upon him, and he became their leader. He led an army that fought against the army of Cushan, and defeated them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDGES 5:31; JUDGES 8:28; JOSHUA 11:23"> After that, there was peace in the land for 40 years, until Othniel died.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ehud became their next leader

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 12:9; JUDGES 2:19; JUDGES 2:14"> After that, the Israelis again did things that Yahweh said were very evil. As a result, he allowed the army of King Eglon, who ruled the Moab area, to defeat the Israelis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 1:16; DEUTERONOMY 34:3; JUDGES 5:14"> Eglon persuaded the leaders of the Ammon and Amalek people-groups to join their armies with his army to attack Israel. They captured Jericho, which was called ‘The City of Palm Trees’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:47–48; DEUTERONOMY 28:40; LEVITICUS 26:23–25"> Then King Eglon ruled the Israelis for eighteen years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 3:9; PSALMS 78:34; Isaiah 36:16"> But then the Israelis again pleaded to Yahweh to help them. So he gave them another leader to rescue them. He was Ehud, a left-handed man, the son of Gera, from the descendants of Benjamin. The Israelis sent him to King Eglon to give him their yearly protection money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="SONG OF SONGS 3:8; REVELATION 2:12; PSALMS 149:6"> Ehud had with him a double-edged dagger, about a foot and a half long. He strapped it to his right thigh, under his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:20; JOB 15:27; JEREMIAH 50:11"> He gave the money to King Eglon, who was a very fat man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Then Ehud started to go back home with the men who had carried the money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 4:20; JUDGES 3:20; ACTS 23:18–19"> When they arrived at the stone carvings near Gilgal, he told the other men to go on, but he himself turned around and went back to the king of Moab. When he arrived at the palace, he said to the king, “Your majesty, I have a secret message for you.” So the king told all his servants to be quiet, and sent them out of the room.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:7; AMOS 3:15; PSALMS 29:1"> Then, as Eglon was sitting alone in the upstairs room of his summer palace, Ehud came close to him and said, “I have a message for you from God.” As the king got up from his chair,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:3; JOB 20:25; 1 SAMUEL 15:33"> Ehud reached with his left hand and pulled the dagger from his right thigh, and plunged it into the king’s belly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> He thrust it in so far that the handle went into the king’s belly, and the blade came out the king’s back. Ehud did not pull the dagger out. He left it there, with the handle buried in the king’s fat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then Ehud left the room. He went out to the porch. He shut the doors to the room and locked them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 24:3"> After he had gone, King Eglon’s servants came back, but they saw that the doors of the room were locked. They said, “The king must be defecating in the inner room.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> So they waited, but when the king did not open the doors of the room, after a while they were worried. They got a key and unlocked the doors. And they saw that their king was lying on the floor, dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Meanwhile, Ehud escaped. He passed by the stone carvings and arrived at Seirah, in the hilly area where the descendants of Ephraim lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JUDGES 6:34; 1 SAMUEL 13:3; JUDGES 5:14"> There he blew a trumpet to signal that the people should join him to fight the people of Moab. So the Israelis went with him from the hills. They went down toward the Jordan river, with Ehud leading them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JUDGES 12:5; JUDGES 7:9; JUDGES 7:15"> He said to the men, “Yahweh is going to allow us to defeat your enemies, the people of Moab. So follow me!” So they followed him down to the river, and they stationed some of their men at the place where people can walk across the river, in order that they could kill any people from Moab who tried to cross the river to escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:15; JUDGES 3:17; PSALMS 17:10"> At that time, the Israelis killed about 10,000 people from Moab. They were all strong and capable men, but not one of them escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JUDGES 3:11; JUDGES 5:31"> On that day, the Israelis conquered the people of Moab. Then there was peace in their land for 80 years.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">
