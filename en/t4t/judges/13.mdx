## Samson was born

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 12:9; JUDGES 6:1; ROMANS 2:6"> Again the Israeli people did things that Yahweh said were very evil. So Yahweh enabled the people of Philistia to conquer them. They ruled over the Israelis for 40 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 19:41; LUKE 1:7; JOSHUA 15:33"> There was a man named Manoah from the descendants of Dan who lived in Zorah town. His wife was unable to become pregnant, so they had no children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="LUKE 1:13; LUKE 1:11; JUDGES 6:11–12"> One day, Yahweh appeared to Manoah’s wife in the form of an angel, and said to her, “Even though you have not been able to give birth to any children until now, you will soon become pregnant and give birth to a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 1:15; JUDGES 13:14; NUMBERS 6:2–3"> From now until he is born, you must not drink any wine or other alcoholic/fermented drink, and you must not eat any food that will make you unacceptable to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="NUMBERS 6:5; 1 SAMUEL 1:11; 1 CHRONICLES 18:1"> After you give birth to your son, you must never allow his hair to be cut. He must be dedicated to God from the day he is born until the day he dies. He is the one who will rescue many of the Israeli people from the people of Philistia.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDGES 13:17–18; 1 SAMUEL 9:6; MATTHEW 28:3–4"> The woman ran and told her husband, “A prophet came to me. He looked awesome, like an angel from God. I did not ask where he came from, and he did not tell me his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JUDGES 13:4"> But he told me, ‘You will become pregnant and give birth to a son. Until then, you must not drink any wine or any alcoholic/fermented drink, and you must not eat any food that would make you unacceptable to God. Your son must be dedicated to God from the day he is born until the day he dies.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ACTS 9:6; JOB 34:32; PROVERBS 3:5–6"> Then Manoah prayed to Yahweh, saying, “O Yahweh, I plead with you, allow that prophet whom you sent to us to come again, and teach us how we should raise the boy who will be born to us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 65:2; MATTHEW 7:7–11"> God did what Manoah asked, and Yahweh again appeared to his wife in the form of an angel, while she was out in the field. But again her husband Manoah was not with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 4:28–29; JOHN 1:41–42"> So she quickly ran and said to her husband, “The man who appeared to me a few days ago has come back again!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Manoah ran back with his wife, and asked, “Are you the man who talked with my wife a few days ago?” He replied, “Yes, I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PROVERBS 22:6; EPHESIANS 6:4; GENESIS 18:19"> Manoah asked him, “When what you promised occurs and my wife gives birth to a son, what rules must he obey, and what work will he do when he grows up?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Yahweh replied, “Your wife must obey all the instructions I gave her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 2:5; JUDGES 13:4; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:4"> Before the baby is born, she must not eat grapes, or drink wine or any other alcoholic/fermented drink, or eat anything that would make her unacceptable to God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 6:18–19; GENESIS 18:3–5; JUDGES 13:3"> Then Manoah said, “Please stay here until we can kill and cook a young goat for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 13:23; JUDGES 6:20; JUDGES 6:26"> Yahweh replied, “I will stay here, but I will not eat anything. However, you may kill an animal and sacrifice it as a burned offering to Yahweh.” (Manoah did not realize that the man he thought was an angel was really Yahweh.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GENESIS 32:29"> Then Manoah asked him, “What is your name? When what you have promised happens, we want to honor you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 32:29; Isaiah 9:6; JUDGES 13:6"> Yahweh replied, “◄Why do you ask me my name?/You should not ask me my name.► &#91;RHQ&#93; It is ◄wonderful/it cannot be understood►.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JUDGES 6:19–21; 1 KINGS 18:30–38"> Then Manoah killed a young goat and burned it on a rock, along with a grain offering, as a sacrifice to Yahweh. And Yahweh did an amazing thing while Manoah and his wife watched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:24; 1 CHRONICLES 21:16; 2 KINGS 2:11"> Flames from the altar blazed up toward the sky, and Yahweh ascended in the flames. When Manoah and his wife saw that, they prostrated themselves on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 6:22; HOSEA 12:4–5; JUDGES 13:16"> Although Yahweh did not appear in the form of an angel to Manoah and his wife again, Manoah realized that the man they thought was an angel was really Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 32:30; DEUTERONOMY 5:26; JOHN 1:18"> So he said, “Now we will die, because we have seen God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 25:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:21; JOHN 15:15"> But his wife said, “No, we will not die, because if Yahweh intended to kill us, he would not have accepted the burned offering and the grain offering. And he would not have appeared to us and told us the wonderful thing that would happen to us, and he would not have performed this miracle.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 1:80; 1 SAMUEL 3:19; HEBREWS 11:32"> When their son was born, they named him Samson. Yahweh blessed him as he grew up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JUDGES 3:10; JOSHUA 15:33; JOHN 3:34"> And while he was in Mahaneh-Dan, which is between Zorah and Eshtaol towns, Yahweh’s Spirit began to control him.</span>
</div>
