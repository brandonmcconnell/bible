## Jephthah’s army defeated the tribe of Ephraim

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 8:1; JAMES 4:1–2; JAMES 3:16"> The men of the tribe of Ephraim summoned their soldiers, and they crossed the Jordan River to the town of Zaphon to talk with Jephthah. They said to him, “◄Why did you not ask us for help?/You should have requested us► to help your army fight the Ammon people-group. So we will burn down your house while you are in it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JUDGES 11:12–33"> Jephthah replied, “The Ammon people-group were oppressing us greatly. When we were prepared to start to attack them, I requested you to come and help us, but you refused. My men and I defeated the Ammon people-group, but you did not help us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 19:5; 1 SAMUEL 28:21; JOB 13:14"> When I saw that you would not help us, I was willing to be killed in the battle against the Ammon people-group. But Yahweh helped us to defeat them. You did not help us when we requested it before, so ◄why have you come here today to fight against me?/you should not have come here today to fight against me.►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 25:10; DEUTERONOMY 3:12–17; PROVERBS 15:1"> Then Jephthah summoned the men of the Gilead region to fight against the men of the tribe of Ephraim. The men of the tribe of Ephraim said, “You men from the Gilead region are men who deserted us. Long ago you left us and moved to the area between our tribe and the tribe of Manasseh.” Because of their saying that, the men of the Gilead region attacked the men of the tribe of Ephraim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JUDGES 3:28; JOSHUA 22:11; JOSHUA 2:7"> The men of Gilead captured that ◄ford/place where people can walk across► at the Jordan River to go to the land where the tribe of Ephraim live. Whenever one of the soldiers from the tribe of Ephraim came to the ford to try to escape, he would say, “Let me cross the river.” Then the men of Gilead would ask him, “Are you from the tribe of Ephraim?” If he said “No,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 69:2; PSALMS 69:15; MARK 14:70"> they would say to him, “Say the word ‘Shibboleth’.” The men of Ephraim could not pronounce that word correctly. So if the person from the tribe of Ephraim said ‘Sibboleth’, they would know that he was lying and that he was really from the tribe of Ephraim, and they would kill him there at the ford. So the men of Gilead killed 42,000 people from the tribe of Ephraim at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Jephthah, the man from the Gilead region, was a leader of the Israeli people for six years. Then he died and was buried in a town in the Gilead region.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ibzan became their leader

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MICAH 5:2; 1 SAMUEL 16:1; MATTHEW 2:1"> After Jephthah died, a man named Ibzan, from Bethlehem, became the leader of the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 10:4; JUDGES 12:14"> He had 30 sons and 30 daughters. He forced all his daughters to marry men who were not in his clan, and brought women from outside his clan to marry his sons. He was the leader of the Israeli people for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> When he died, he was buried in Bethlehem.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elon became their leader

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> After Ibzan died, a man named Elon, from the tribe of Zebulun, became the leader of the Israeli people. He was their leader for ten years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 8:13; 1 CHRONICLES 6:69; JOSHUA 19:42"> Then he died and was buried in Aijalon city in the area where the tribe of Zebulun lives.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abdon became their leader

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> After Elon died, a man named Abdon who was the son of Hillel, from Pirathon city, in the area where the tribe of Ephraim live became the leader of the Israeli people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JUDGES 5:10; JUDGES 10:4"> He had 40 sons and 30 grandsons. Each of them had his own donkey on which to ride. Abdon was the leader of the Israeli people for eight years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JUDGES 5:14; JUDGES 3:13; JUDGES 3:27"> When Abdon died, he was buried in Pirathon, in the hilly area where the descendants of Amalek lived previously, but now it is the area where the tribe of Ephraim lives.</span>
</div>
</div>
