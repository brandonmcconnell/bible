## Deborah became the next leader

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:3; JUDGES 3:12; JUDGES 3:7"> After Ehud died, the Israelis again started doing things Yahweh said were very evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOSHUA 11:1; JUDGES 4:16; JUDGES 4:13"> So he allowed the army of Jabin, one of the kings of Canaan land, who ruled in Hazor city, to conquer the Israelis. The commander of his army was Sisera, who lived in Harosheth-Haggoyim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDGES 1:19; PSALMS 106:42; JUDGES 3:9"> Sisera’s army had 900 chariots. For 20 years he oppressed the Israelis. Then they pleaded to Yahweh to help them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EXODUS 15:20; LUKE 2:36; 2 KINGS 22:14"> At that time Deborah, who was the wife of Lappidoth, was a prophetess who had become the leader in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 18:19; JEREMIAH 31:15; EXODUS 18:26"> She would sit under her palm tree at a place between Ramah and Bethel, in the hilly area where the descendants of Ephraim lived, and people would come to her and ask her to settle disputes between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ACTS 13:47; JUDGES 8:18; HEBREWS 11:32"> One day she summoned Barak, the son of Abinoam, when he was in Kedesh, in the area where the descendants of Naphtali lived. She said to him, “This is what Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis worship, is commanding you to do: ‘Take with you 1,000 men, some from the descendants of Naphtali and some from the descendants of Zebulun, and lead them to Tabor Mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 KINGS 18:40; EZEKIEL 38:10–16; PSALMS 83:9–10"> Yahweh will lure Sisera, the commander of King Jabin’s army, to come with his chariots and his army, to the Kishon River, a few miles away. I will enable your men to defeat them there.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EXODUS 4:10–14; MATTHEW 14:30–31"> Barak replied, “I will go only if you go with me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 2:14; 1 SAMUEL 2:30; JUDGES 4:17–22"> She replied, “Okay, I will go with you. But because that is what you have decided to do, Yahweh will enable a woman to defeat Sisera, and the result will be that ◄you will not be honored/people will not honor you► for doing that.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JUDGES 5:18; JUDGES 5:15; JUDGES 4:14"> So Deborah went with Barak to Kedesh. There he summoned men from Zebulun and Naphtali. Ten thousand men came to him, and then they went together with Deborah to Tabor Mountain.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDGES 1:16; JOSHUA 19:33; NUMBERS 10:29"> At that time Heber, one of the descendants of the Ken people-group, had moved with his wife Jael from the area where that group lived, and set up his tent near the big oak tree at Zaanannim, near Kedesh. Heber was a descendant of Moses’ brother-in-law Hobab.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="PSALMS 89:12; JEREMIAH 46:18; JOSHUA 19:12"> Someone told Sisera that Barak had gone up on Tabor Mountain with an army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 4:2–3; JUDGES 4:7"> So he gathered his troops with their 900 chariots, and they marched from Harosheth-Haggoyim to the Kishon River.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 9:3; 2 SAMUEL 5:24; Isaiah 52:12"> Then Deborah said to Barak, “Get ready! This is the day that Yahweh will enable your army to defeat the army of Sisera. Yahweh is going ahead of you!” So Barak led his men as they descended from Tabor Mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:10; PSALMS 83:9–10; JUDGES 5:20–21"> As they advanced, Yahweh caused Sisera and all his chariots and his army to have great difficulty maneuvering/moving around. So Sisera jumped down from his chariot and ran away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ROMANS 2:12; LEVITICUS 26:7–8; JAMES 2:13"> But Barak and his men pursued the other chariots and the enemy soldiers as far as Harosheth-Haggoyim. They killed all of the men in Sisera’s army. Not one man survived.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 69:22; Isaiah 57:21; AMOS 5:19–20"> But Sisera ran to Jael’s tent. He did that because his boss, King Jabin of Hazor city, was a good friend of Jael’s husband Heber.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 KINGS 6:19"> Jael went out to greet Sisera. She said to him, “Sir, come into my tent! Do not be afraid!” So he went into the tent and lay down, and she covered him with a blanket.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JUDGES 5:25–26; GENESIS 24:43; Isaiah 41:17"> He said to her, “I’m thirsty; can you give me some water?” So she opened a leather container of milk, and gave him a drink. Then she covered him with the blanket again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JOSHUA 2:3–5; 2 SAMUEL 17:20"> He said to her, “Stand in the entrance of the tent. If someone comes and asks ‘Is anyone else here?’, say ‘No.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDGES 5:26–27; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:27; JUDGES 3:21"> Sisera was very exhausted, so he soon was asleep. While he was sleeping, Jael crept quietly to him, holding a hammer and a tent peg. She pounded the peg into his skull, and all the way through his head into the ground, and he died.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 17:10–15; 2 SAMUEL 17:3"> When Barak passed by Jael’s tent to look for Sisera, she went out to greet him. She said, “Come in, and I will show you the man you are searching for!” So he followed her into the tent, and he saw Sisera lying there, dead, with the tent peg through his head.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:24; PSALMS 18:47; PSALMS 18:39"> On that day God enabled the Israelis to defeat the army of Jabin, the king of the people of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 3:12"> From that time, the Israeli people continued to become stronger, and the army of King Jabin continued to become weaker. Finally the Israelis were able to completely subdue the people ruled by the king of Canaan.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
