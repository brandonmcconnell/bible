## Abimelech became king in Shechem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 8:31; PSALMS 83:2–4; 2 SAMUEL 15:6"> Gideon’s son Abimelech went to talk with his mother’s brothers in Shechem city. He said to them and to all his mother’s relatives,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GENESIS 29:14; JUDGES 8:30; HEBREWS 2:14"> “Ask all the leaders of your city: ‘Do you think it would be good for all 70 of Gideon’s sons to rule over you? Or would it be better to have only one of his sons, me, to rule over you?’ And do not forget that I am your relative! &#91;MTY&#93;”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 29:15; PSALMS 10:3; PROVERBS 1:11–14"> So Abimelech’s mother’s brothers spoke to all the leaders of Shechem about what Abimelech had said. They said to each other, “We should allow Abimelech to rule over us, because he is our relative.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JUDGES 8:33; ACTS 17:5; 2 CHRONICLES 13:7"> So the leaders of Shechem took from the temple of their god Baal-Berith ◄almost 2 pounds/0.8 kg.► of silver and gave it to Abimelech. With that silver he paid some worthless troublemakers to help him, and they went with Abimelech wherever he went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 KINGS 11:1–2; 2 CHRONICLES 21:4; JUDGES 6:24"> They went to Ophrah, his father’s town, and murdered 69 of his 70 brothers, the sons of his father Gideon. They killed all those men on one huge rock. But Gideon’s youngest son Jotham hid from Abimelech and his men, and he escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 5:9; 1 KINGS 12:20; 2 KINGS 12:20"> Then all the leaders of Shechem and Beth-Millo gathered under the big sacred tree in Shechem. There they appointed Abimelech to be their leader.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 11:29; JOHN 4:20; DEUTERONOMY 27:12"> When Jotham heard about that, he climbed up Gerizim Mountain. He stood at the top of the mountain and shouted to the people down below, “You leaders of Shechem, listen to me, in order that God will listen to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JUDGES 8:22–23; DANIEL 4:10–18; 2 KINGS 14:9"> One day the trees decided to appoint a king to rule over all of them. So they said to the olive tree, ‘You be our king!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:1; EXODUS 29:7; ACTS 4:27"> “But the olive tree said, ‘No! I will not be your king Men and gods enjoy the oil from my fruit. I will not &#91;RHQ&#93; stop producing olives from which we make that oil, in order to rule over you other trees!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “Then the trees said to the fig tree, ‘You come and be our king!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 13:6–7"> “But the fig tree replied, ‘No! I do not want to &#91;RHQ&#93; stop producing my good sweet fruit, and rule over you other trees!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> “Then the trees said to the grapevine, ‘Come and be our king!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 104:15; ECCLESIASTES 10:19; NUMBERS 15:5"> “But the grapevine replied, ‘No! I will not be your king The new wine that is made from my grapes causes people and gods who drink it to become very happy. I do not want to stop producing grapes and rule over you other trees!’ &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 KINGS 14:9"> “Then all the trees said to the thornbush, ‘Come and be our king!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 30:2; JUDGES 9:20; HOSEA 14:7"> “The thornbush replied, ‘If you truly want to appoint me to be your king, come into the shade of my tiny branches. But if you do not want to do that, I hope/desire that fire will come out from me and burn up all the huge cedar trees in Lebanon country!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 8:35"> “After Jotham finished telling them this parable, he said, “So now I ask you, were you being completely honest and sincere when you appointed Abimelech to be your king &#91;RHQ&#93;? And have you treated Gideon and his family &#91;RHQ&#93; fairly? Have you rewarded Gideon by honoring him as he deserved because of all the good things he did for you? No!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 16:4; ROMANS 5:8; REVELATION 12:11"> “Do not forget that my father fought a battle for you, and he was willing to die for you if that had been necessary, to save you from the Midian people-group.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JUDGES 9:5–6; JUDGES 8:30–31; JUDGES 9:14"> But now you have rebelled against my father’s family, and you have killed 69 of his sons on one huge rock. And you have appointed Abimelech—who is the son of my father’s slave girl, not the son of his wife—to be the king who will rule you people of Shechem. You have done that only because he is one of your relatives!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 8:6; JAMES 4:16; PHILIPPIANS 3:3"> So, if today you have truly acted fairly and sincerely toward Gideon and his family, I hope/desire that he will cause you to be happy and that you will cause him to be happy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JUDGES 9:15; JUDGES 9:56–57; 2 CHRONICLES 20:22–23"> But if what you did was not right, I wish/desire that Abimelech will burn up all of you leaders of Shechem and Beth-Millo with fire! And I also hope/desire that the leaders of Shechem and Beth-Millo will cause fire to burn up Abimelech!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="NUMBERS 21:16; 2 SAMUEL 20:14; JOSHUA 19:8"> After Jotham finished saying that, he escaped from them and ran away to Beer town. He stayed there because he was afraid that his brother Abimelech would try to kill him.</span>
</div>

## Abimelech and his men killed the people of Shechem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Abimelech became the leader of all the people of Israel. He ruled them for three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Isaiah 19:14; Isaiah 19:2"> Then God sent an evil spirit to cause trouble between Abimelech and the leaders of Shechem, with the result that the leaders of Shechem rebelled against Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:32; PSALMS 7:16; Esther 9:25"> The leaders of Shechem had previously helped Abimelech to kill 69 of Gideon’s sons, who were his brothers. So now God sent the evil spirit to punish all of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOSHUA 8:12–13; PROVERBS 1:11–12; JOSHUA 8:4"> The leaders of Shechem ◄set an ambush/sent men to hide► on the hilltops to ambush Abimelech. Those men robbed everyone who passed by. But someone told Abimelech about it, so he did not go near them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="GENESIS 13:8; GENESIS 19:7"> There was a man named Gaal, the son of Ebed, who moved into Shechem city, along with his brothers. Soon the leaders of Shechem started to trust him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JUDGES 9:4; JUDGES 9:46; PSALMS 109:17"> They went out of the city to their vineyards and picked some grapes. They pressed the grapes to make juice, and then they made wine. Then they had a feast in the temple of their god, and they ate a lot of food and drank a lot of wine. Then they cursed Abimelech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="GENESIS 34:2; GENESIS 34:6; 1 SAMUEL 25:10"> Gaal said, “◄Why should we allow Abimelech to rule over us?/We should not allow Abimelech to rule over us!► &#91;RHQ&#93; He is only one of Gideon’s sons so he really does not belong to us &#91;RHQ&#93;! And he appointed Zebul, the governor of our city, to be his deputy! We should ◄serve/be loyal to► one of the descendants of Hamor, the founder of our city, and let him be our leader, not Abimelech &#91;RHQ&#93;!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 15:4; 2 SAMUEL 2:14–17; Isaiah 36:8–9"> If you would appoint me to be your leader, I would get rid of Abimelech. I would say to him, ‘Get your army ready, and then come to fight us!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> When someone told Zebul what Gaal said, he was very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> He secretly sent some messengers to Abimelech. They told him, “Gaal and his brothers have come here to Shechem, and they are causing the people of the city to rebel against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ROMANS 3:15; PSALMS 36:4; PROVERBS 1:11–16"> You and your men should get up during the night and go and hide in the fields outside the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 10:7; ECCLESIASTES 9:10; LEVITICUS 25:26"> As soon as the sun rises in the morning, get up and attack the city. When Gaal and his men come out to fight against you, you can do to them whatever you want to.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> So Abimelech and all the men who were with him got up during the night. They divided into four groups, and hid in the fields near Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> The next morning, Gaal went out and stood at the entrance to the city gate. While he was standing there, Abimelech and his soldiers came out of their hiding places and started walking toward the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARK 8:24; EZEKIEL 7:7"> When Gaal saw the soldiers, he said to Zebul, “Look! There are people coming down from the hills!” But Zebul said, “You are seeing only the shadows of trees on the hills. They are not people; they only resemble people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:14"> But Gaal looked again and said, “Look! There are people coming down from the top of the sacred mountain! There is a group of them coming down from where the tree is where people talk with the spirits of dead people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JUDGES 9:28–29; 2 KINGS 14:8–14; 2 SAMUEL 2:26–27"> Zebul said to Gaal, “Now ◄what good is your bragging?/your bragging is worthless!► &#91;MTY, RHQ&#93; You said, ‘◄Why should we serve Abimelech/We should not allow Abimelech to rule over us►?’ You made fun of these men. So now go out and fight them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> So Gaal led the men of Shechem outside the city to fight Abimelech and his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 KINGS 20:18–21; 1 KINGS 20:30"> Abimelech and his men pursued them, and they killed many of Gaal’s men before they could return safely inside the city gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JUDGES 9:28; JUDGES 9:30"> Abimelech then stayed at Arumah, about five miles away from Shechem, and Zebul’s men forced Gaal and his brothers to leave Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> The next day, the people of Shechem got ready to leave the city and work in their fields. When someone told Abimelech about that,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> he divided his men into three groups, and told them to hide in the fields. So they did that. And when they saw the people coming out of the city, they jumped up and attacked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:15; JUDGES 9:15; JUDGES 9:20"> Abimelech and the men who were with him ran to the city gate. The other two groups ran out to the people in the fields and attacked them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 KINGS 3:25; DEUTERONOMY 29:23; JAMES 2:13"> Abimelech and his men fought all day. They captured the city and killed all the people. They tore down all the buildings, and then they threw salt over the ruins in order that nothing would grow there again.</span>
</div>
</div>

## They killed the people in the fortress

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="JUDGES 8:33; JUDGES 9:4; 1 KINGS 8:26"> When the leaders who lived in the tower/fortress outside of Shechem heard what had happened, they ran and hid inside the fortress, which was also a temple of their god El-Berith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> But someone told Abimelech that all the leaders had gathered there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="PSALMS 68:14; PROVERBS 1:11–12; JUDGES 7:17–18"> So he and all the men who were with him went up Zalmon Mountain which is near Shechem. Abimelech cut some branches of trees with an axe, and put them on his shoulders. Then he said to all the men who were with him, “Quickly, do what I have just done!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:15; JAMES 3:16; JUDGES 9:20"> So his men all cut branches then carried them down the mountain, following Abimelech. They went to the fortress and piled the branches against its walls. Then they kindled a fire, and the fire burned down the fortress and killed all the people who were inside. So all the people who were inside the fortress—about 1,000 men and women—died.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abimelech was killed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Then Abimelech and his men went to Thebes city. They surrounded it and captured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> But there was a strong tower inside the city. So all the men, women, and leaders of the city ran to the tower. When they were all inside, they locked the door. Then they climbed up to the roof of the tower.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="JUDGES 9:48–49; 2 KINGS 14:10; 2 KINGS 15:16"> Abimelech and his men came to the tower and tried to get in the door, but they could not. Then Abimelech prepared to light a fire to burn down the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 11:21; JOB 31:3; JEREMIAH 49:20"> But when Abimelech came near the doorway, a woman who was on the roof dropped a big grinding stone on his head, and crushed his skull.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 31:4–5"> Abimelech quickly called to the young man who carried Abimelech’s weapons, and said, “Pull out your sword and kill me with it! I do not want people to say ‘It was a woman who killed Abimelech.’” So the young man thrust his sword into Abimelech, and Abimelech died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 18:16; 2 SAMUEL 20:21–22; 1 KINGS 22:35–36"> When the Israeli soldiers saw that Abimelech was dead, they all returned to their homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="PSALMS 94:23; JUDGES 9:24; PROVERBS 5:22"> In that way God punished Abimelech for the evil things that he had done to his father and especially for killing all 69 of his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="JUDGES 9:20; JOSHUA 6:26; 1 KINGS 16:34"> God also punished the men of Shechem for the evil things that they had done. And when those things happened, it fulfilled what Gideon’s son Jotham said when he cursed Abimelech and the leaders of Shechem.</span>
</div>
