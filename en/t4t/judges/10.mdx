## Tola ruled the Israelis

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JUDGES 2:16; JUDGES 3:9; JOSHUA 15:48"> After Abimelech died, Tola the son of Puah and grandson of Dodo became the leader to rescue the Israeli people from their enemies. He belonged to the tribe of Issachar, but he lived in Shamir city in the hilly area where the descendants of Ephraim live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> He ruled the Israeli people for 23 years. Then he died and was buried in Shamir.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jair ruled the Israelis

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:29; GENESIS 31:48"> After Tola died, Jair, from the Gilead region, became the Israelis’ leader, and he ruled them for 22 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="NUMBERS 32:41; JUDGES 5:10; DEUTERONOMY 3:14"> He had thirty sons, and each of them had his own donkey to ride on. They each controlled a different town in the Gilead region. That region is still named ‘The Towns of Jair’.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When Jair died, he was buried in Kamon city.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Ammon people-group oppressed the Israelis

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 KINGS 11:33; 1 KINGS 11:7; 2 KINGS 23:13"> Again the Israelis did things that Yahweh said were wrong/evil. They worshiped the idols of their god Baal and their goddess Astarte. They also worshiped the gods of the Aram, Sidon, Moab, and Ammon people-groups, and the gods of the Philistia people-group. They abandoned Yahweh and stopped worshiping him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JUDGES 2:14; DEUTERONOMY 32:16–22; 1 SAMUEL 12:9–10"> So Yahweh was very angry with them, and he allowed the Philistia and Ammon people-groups to defeat &#91;IDM&#93; the Israelis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:3; Isaiah 30:13; JUDGES 10:5"> In that same year, those people started to oppress the Israelis who lived in the Gilead region on the east side of the Jordan River. That was where the Amor people-group also lived. were They caused the Israelis who lived in that region to suffer for 18 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 20:1–2; DEUTERONOMY 28:65; 2 CHRONICLES 15:5"> Then the people of the Ammon people-group crossed the Jordan River to fight against the people of the tribes of Judah, Benjamin, and Ephraim. They caused the Israelis’ lives to be very miserable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 12:10; PSALMS 107:28; PSALMS 107:13"> So the Israelis cried out to Yahweh, saying “We have sinned against you. We have abandoned you, and we have been worshiping the idols of their god Baal.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDGES 3:31; NUMBERS 21:21–25; EXODUS 14:30"> Yahweh answered them, saying, “When the people of Egypt, and the Amor and Ammon and Philistia people-groups</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JUDGES 6:3; 2 CHRONICLES 26:6–7; PSALMS 106:42–43"> and the Sidon and Amalek and Maon people-groups were cruel to you, you cried out to me, and then I rescued you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:13; 1 CHRONICLES 28:9; JUDGES 2:12"> But now you have abandoned me again, and you have been worshiping other gods. So I will not rescue you again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:28; DEUTERONOMY 32:37–38; 1 KINGS 18:27–28"> You have chosen those gods to be the ones that you worship. So call to them to help you. Allow them to rescue you when you have a lot of trouble!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 3:18; 2 SAMUEL 15:26; PROVERBS 28:13"> But the Israeli people said to Yahweh, “Truly we have sinned. Punish us in whatever way you wish to, but please rescue us now!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 63:9; PSALMS 106:44–45; EZEKIEL 18:30–32"> Then the Israelis threw away the idols of the gods that belonged to other people-groups, and they worshiped Yahweh again. He saw that they were suffering very much, and he felt sorry &#91;IDM&#93; for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JUDGES 11:29; GENESIS 31:49; JUDGES 11:11"> The Ammon people-group gathered to fight against the Israelis, and they set up their tents in the Gilead region. The Israeli men also gathered and set up their tents at Mizpah, which was a city in Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JUDGES 11:11; JUDGES 12:7; JUDGES 1:1"> The Israeli leaders said, “Who will lead our soldiers to attack the Ammon people-group? The one who will lead us will become the leader of all us who live in this Gilead region.”</span>
</div>
</div>
