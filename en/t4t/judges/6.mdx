## Gideon became the next leader

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:26–29; JUDGES 2:19–20; JUDGES 2:13–14"> Again the Israelis did things that Yahweh said were very evil. So he allowed the people of Midian to conquer them and rule them for seven years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:38; 1 SAMUEL 13:6; DEUTERONOMY 28:47–48"> The people of Midian treated the Israelis so cruelly that the Israelis fled to the mountains. There they made places to live in caves and animal dens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JUDGES 6:33; JUDGES 3:13; JUDGES 7:12"> Whenever the Israelis planted things in their fields, the people of Midian and Amalek and other groups from the east invaded Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LEVITICUS 26:16; DEUTERONOMY 28:51; MICAH 6:15"> They set up tents in the area, and then destroyed the crops as far south as Gaza. They did not leave anything for the Israelis’ sheep or cattle or donkeys to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JUDGES 7:12; JUDGES 8:10; JEREMIAH 46:23"> They came into Israel with their tents and their livestock like a swarm of locusts. There were so many of them that arrived riding on their camels that no one could count them. They stayed and ruined the Israelis’ crops.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JUDGES 3:9; JEREMIAH 5:17; PSALMS 50:15"> The people of Midian took almost everything the Israelis owned. So finally the Israelis pleaded for Yahweh to help them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> When the Israelis pleaded with Yahweh to help them because of what the people from Midian were doing to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:5–32; Isaiah 63:9–14; PSALMS 136:10–16"> he sent to them a prophet, who said, “Yahweh, the God we Israelis worship, says this: ‘Your ancestors were slaves in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 44:2–3"> But I rescued them from the leaders of Egypt and from all the others who oppressed them. I expelled their enemies from this land, and gave it to your ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:2; 2 KINGS 17:35–39; JEREMIAH 9:13"> I told you all, “I am Yahweh, your God. You are now in the land where the descendants of Amor live, but you must not worship the gods whom they worship.” But you did not pay attention to me.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:2; HEBREWS 11:32; JUDGES 13:3"> One day Yahweh appeared in the form of an angel and sat underneath a big oak tree at Ophrah town. That tree belonged to Joash, who was from the clan of Abiezer. Joash’s son Gideon was threshing wheat in the pit where they pressed grapes to make wine. He was threshing the grain there in order to hide it from the people of Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:5; LUKE 1:28; JUDGES 2:18"> Yahweh went over to Gideon and said to him, “You mighty warrior, Yahweh is helping you!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 77:7–9; 2 CHRONICLES 15:2; Isaiah 59:1–2"> Gideon replied, “Sir, if Yahweh is helping us, why have all these bad things happened to us? We heard about &#91;RHQ&#93; all the miracles that Yahweh performed for our ancestors. We heard people tell us about how he rescued them from being slaves in Egypt. But now Yahweh has abandoned us, and we are ruled by the people from Midian.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:5–9; HEBREWS 11:34; 1 SAMUEL 12:11"> Then Yahweh turned toward him and said, “I will give you strength to enable you to rescue the Israelis from the people of Midian. I am sending you to do that!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 9:21; EXODUS 3:11; EXODUS 4:10"> Gideon replied, “But Yahweh, how can I rescue the Israelis? My clan is the least significant in the whole tribe descended from Manasseh, and I am the least significant person in my whole family!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:5; EXODUS 3:12; ACTS 11:21"> Yahweh said to him, “I will help you. So you will defeat the army of Midian as easily as if you were fighting only one man!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PSALMS 86:17; EXODUS 33:13; Isaiah 7:11"> Gideon replied, “If you are truly pleased with me, do something which will prove that you who are speaking to me are really Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JUDGES 13:15; GENESIS 18:3; GENESIS 18:5"> But do not go away until I go and bring back an offering to you.”<br />Yahweh answered, “Okay, I will stay here until you return.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="GENESIS 18:6–8; JUDGES 13:15–19; LEVITICUS 2:4"> Gideon hurried to his home. He killed a young goat and cooked it. Then he took ◄a half a bushel/18 liters► of flour and baked some bread without yeast. Then he put the cooked meat in a basket, and put the broth from the meat in a pot, and took it to Yahweh, who was sitting under the tree.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JUDGES 13:19; 1 KINGS 18:33–34"> Then Yahweh said to him, “Put the meat and the bread on this rock. Then pour the broth on top of it.” So Gideon did that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="LEVITICUS 9:24; 1 KINGS 18:38; 2 CHRONICLES 7:1"> Then Yahweh touched the meat and bread with the walking stick that was in his hand. A fire flamed up from the rock and burned up everything that Gideon had brought! And then Yahweh disappeared.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 32:30; EXODUS 33:20; DEUTERONOMY 5:26"> When Gideon realized that it was really Yahweh who had appeared in the form of an angel and talked with him, he exclaimed, “O, Yahweh, I have seen you face-to-face when you had the form of an angel! So I will surely die!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="DANIEL 10:19; JOHN 20:26; GENESIS 43:23"> But Yahweh called to him and said, “Do not be afraid! You will not die because of seeing me!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="GENESIS 22:14; EXODUS 17:15; JUDGES 8:32"> Then Gideon built an altar to worship Yahweh there. He named it ‘Yahweh gives us peace’. That altar is still there in Ophrah town, in the land that belongs to the descendants of Abiezer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="EXODUS 34:13; JUDGES 3:7; ACTS 5:29"> That night Yahweh said to Gideon, “Take the second-best/oldest bull from your father’s herd, the bull that is seven years old. Kill it. Then tear down the altar that your father built to worship the god Baal. Also cut down the pole for worshipping the goddess Astarte that is there beside it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 14:40; 2 SAMUEL 24:18; 1 CORINTHIANS 14:33"> Then build a stone altar to worship me, your God Yahweh, here on this hill. Take the wood from the pole you cut down and make a fire to cook the meat of the bull as a burnt offering to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:4; JOHN 2:5; DEUTERONOMY 4:1"> So Gideon and his servants did what Yahweh commanded. But they did it at night, because he was afraid what the other members of his family and the other men in town would do to him if they found out that he had done that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Early the next morning, as soon as the men got up, they saw that the altar to Baal had been torn down, and the pole for worshiping Astarte was gone. They saw that there was a new altar there, and on it was what remained from the bull they had sacrificed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> The people asked each other, “Who did this?” After they investigated, someone told them that it was Gideon, the son of Joash, who had done it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="ACTS 26:9; PHILIPPIANS 3:6; JEREMIAH 50:38"> They went to Joash and said to him, “Bring your son out here! ◄He must be executed/We must kill him►, because he destroyed our god Baal’s altar and cut down the pole for our goddess Astarte!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:11; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:4; EPHESIANS 5:11"> But Joash replied, “Are you trying to defend Baal? Are you trying to argue his case? Anyone who tries to defend Baal should be executed by tomorrow morning! If Baal is truly a god, he ought to be able to defend himself, and to get rid of the person who tore down his altar!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 12:11; JUDGES 7:1; 2 SAMUEL 11:21"> From that time, people called Gideon Jerub-Baal, which means ‘Baal should defend himself’, because he tore down Baal’s altar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JOSHUA 17:16; JUDGES 6:3; ROMANS 8:35–39"> Soon after that, the armies of the people of Midian and of Amalek and the people from the east gathered together. They crossed the Jordan River to attack the Israelis. They set up their tents in Jezreel Valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JUDGES 3:27; 2 CHRONICLES 24:20; JUDGES 3:10"> Then Yahweh’s Spirit took control of Gideon. He blew a ram’s horn to summon the men to prepare to fight. So the men of the clan of Abiezer came to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 30:6–12; JUDGES 4:6"> He also sent messengers throughout the tribes descended from the four tribes of Manasseh, Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali to tell their soldiers to come, and all of them came.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JUDGES 6:14; MATTHEW 16:1; JUDGES 6:17–20"> Then Gideon said to God, “If you are truly going to enable me to rescue the Israeli people as you promised,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="HOSEA 14:5; MATTHEW 10:5–6; MATTHEW 15:24"> confirm it by doing this: Tonight I will put a dry wool fleece on the ground where I thresh the grain. Tomorrow morning, if the fleece is wet with dew but the ground is dry, then I will know that I am the one you will enable to rescue the people of Israel as you promised.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 35:7"> And that is what happened. When Gideon got up the next morning, he picked up the fleece, and squeezed out a whole bowlful of water!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="GENESIS 18:32; Isaiah 50:2; PSALMS 107:33–35"> Then Gideon said to God, “Do not be angry with me, but let me ask you to do one more thing. Tonight I will put the fleece out again. This time, let the fleece remain dry, while the ground is wet with the dew.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> So that night, God did what Gideon asked him to do. The next morning the fleece was dry, but the ground was covered with dew.</span>
</div>
</div>
