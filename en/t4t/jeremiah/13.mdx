<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 13:11; HEBREWS 1:1; JEREMIAH 27:2"> One day Yahweh said to me, “Go and buy a linen waistcloth. Put it on, but do not wash it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EZEKIEL 2:8; JOHN 15:14; PROVERBS 3:5"> So I bought a very nice waistcloth, which is what Yahweh told me to do, and I put it on.</span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICAH 4:10; PSALMS 137:1; JEREMIAH 51:63–64"> He said, “Go to the small Perath Stream near Jerusalem and hide your waistcloth in a crevice/hole in the rocks.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 40:16; EXODUS 39:42–43; 2 TIMOTHY 2:3"> So I went to the stream and did what Yahweh told me to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> A long time later, Yahweh said to me, “Go back to that stream and get the waistcloth that I told you to hide there.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then Yahweh gave me this message:</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 16:12; JEREMIAH 9:14; 2 CHRONICLES 36:15–16"> Those wicked people refuse to pay attention to what I say. <span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EXODUS 19:5–6; JEREMIAH 33:9; PSALMS 81:11"> Just like a waistcloth clings closely/tightly to a man’s waist, I wanted the people &#91;MTY&#93; of Israel and Judah to cling closely to me. I wanted them to be my people, people who would praise me and honor me. But they would not pay attention to me. “</span>
