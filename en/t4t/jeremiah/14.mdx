## The terrible drought

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:8"> After there had been no rain in Judah for a long time, Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 3:26; JEREMIAH 8:21; 1 SAMUEL 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people in Judah are very distressed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">people are sitting on the ground and mourning;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in all of Jerusalem people are crying loudly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 15:30; PSALMS 40:14; 2 KINGS 18:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">The rich people send their servants to wells to get water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but all the wells are dry.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The servants return with empty pitchers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they cover their heads</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they are ashamed and humiliated/disappointed and sad.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JOEL 1:19–20; JOEL 1:11; JEREMIAH 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ground is extremely dry and cracked open</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because there has been no rain.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The farmers are very worried,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so they also cover their heads.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 29:9; JOB 39:1–4; Isaiah 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even the ◄does/female deer► abandon their newborn babies/fawns</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because there is no grass in the fields for them to eat.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:24; JOB 39:5–6; JOEL 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wild donkeys stand on the barren hills,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">panting like &#91;SIM&#93; thirsty jackals/wolves.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They become blind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because there is no grass to eat.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 25:11; HOSEA 5:5; JEREMIAH 14:20–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people say, “Yahweh, we have turned away from you and sinned many times,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and now we know that we are being punished because of our sins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but please help us</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order that everyone can see that you are very great &#91;MTY&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:13; JEREMIAH 50:7; Isaiah 43:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are the one whom we Israelis confidently expect to do good things for us</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when we have many troubles/difficulties.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So, why do you not help us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You act as though you are a stranger in our land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like you are someone who is staying here for only one night.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 63:19; Isaiah 59:1; JEREMIAH 15:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Are you also surprised about the terrible things that are happening to us?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Why do you act like you are unable to save anyone, even though you are a strong warrior?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, you are here among us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and others know that we are your people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so do not abandon us!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HOSEA 9:9; HOSEA 8:13; PSALMS 119:101"> And this is what Yahweh says to those people:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You love to wander away from me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you run &#91;MTY&#93; from one idol to another.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, now I will no longer accept you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will punish you for your sins.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:16; JEREMIAH 11:14; EXODUS 32:32–34"> Then Yahweh said to me, “Do not pray for these people any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:11; EZEKIEL 8:18; PROVERBS 1:28"> When they ◄fast/abstain from food in order to honor me►, I will not pay any attention. When they bring to me their offerings of animals to be completely burned on the altar and their offerings of grain, I will not accept them. Instead, I will get rid of them by wars, by famines, and by diseases.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:14; JEREMIAH 4:10; 2 PETER 2:1"> Then I replied to him, “Yahweh my God, their prophets are telling the people that they will not experience wars &#91;MTY&#93; or famines. They are telling the people that you will surely allow us to have peace in our land for many years.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:6–7; EZEKIEL 12:24; JEREMIAH 29:8–9"> Yahweh replied to me, “Those prophets say that they are speaking what I tell them to say &#91;MTY&#93;, but they are telling lies. I did not send them, so what they are saying is false. They say that they have received visions from me, and that they are telling things that I have revealed to them, but that is not true. They are saying foolish things that they have only thought of themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:12–13; EZEKIEL 14:10; JEREMIAH 23:14–15"> So this is what I say about those prophets who are predicting what will happen, saying that I told them those things: They are saying that we Israelis will not die from wars or famines, but I did not send those prophets. And they themselves will die from wars or from famines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:33; PROVERBS 1:31; JEREMIAH 15:2–3"> And the people to whom they are predicting these things, they and their wives and their sons and their daughters, will also die from wars or from famines. Their corpses will be thrown into the streets of Jerusalem, and there will not be anyone to bury them. I will punish them like they deserve to be punished.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:21; JEREMIAH 9:1; JEREMIAH 13:17"> So, Jeremiah, tell this to them about yourself:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Day and night my eyes are full of tears.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I cannot stop crying.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I cry for my people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who are very precious to me, as if they were &#91;MET&#93; my daughters.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I cry for them because they have been severely wounded;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they have been severely crushed &#91;DOU&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="EZEKIEL 7:15; LAMENTATIONS 1:20; JEREMIAH 8:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">If I go out into the fields,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I see corpses of people who have been slaughtered by our enemies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If I walk along the streets of the city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I see corpses of people who died from hunger.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The prophets and the priests travel through the land, preaching to people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they do not know what they are doing (OR, in a land that they know nothing about).’”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeremiah’s prayer

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 5:22; JEREMIAH 8:15; JEREMIAH 6:30"> Then I prayed this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yahweh, have you completely rejected the people of Judah?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do you really despise the people of Jerusalem &#91;MTY&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why have you &#91;RHQ&#93; wounded us very badly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that we will never be healed?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We hoped that we would have peace,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but there was no peace.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We hoped that there would be a time when we would be healed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but all that we received were things that terrified us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:25; PSALMS 32:5; 1 JOHN 1:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, we admit/know that we are wicked people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that our ancestors also did many wicked things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We have all sinned against you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:7; PSALMS 106:45; JEREMIAH 3:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But Yahweh, in order that we may honor &#91;MTY&#93; you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">do not despise/abandon us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not dishonor the city where &#91;MTY&#93; your glorious throne is.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Please do not forget us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and do not ◄break your agreement with us/stop doing what you agreed to do for us►.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JOEL 2:23; JEREMIAH 5:24; PSALMS 135:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those idols that have been brought from other nations certainly cannot &#91;RHQ&#93; bring rain to us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the sky certainly cannot &#91;RHQ&#93; cause rain to fall.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh our God, you are the only one who can do things like that.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So we will confidently expect you to help us.”</span></span>
</div>
