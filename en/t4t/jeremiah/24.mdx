## Good figs and bad figs

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:2; 2 CHRONICLES 36:10; 2 KINGS 24:12–16"> The army of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon captured Jehoiachin, the king of Judah, and his officials, and all his skilled workers &#91;DOU&#93; and took them to Babylon. After that happened, Yahweh gave me a vision. In the vision I saw two baskets of figs that had been placed in front of the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:7; Isaiah 5:4; MICAH 7:1"> One basket was full of good figs, like the kind that ripen first. The other basket was filled with figs that were bad/rotten, with the result that they could not be eaten.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 8:2; ZECHARIAH 5:5–11; MATTHEW 25:32–33"> Then Yahweh said to me, “Jeremiah, what do you see?”<br />I replied, “I see some figs. Some are very good ones, but some are very bad, with the result that no one would eat them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Then Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:9; NAHUM 1:7; GALATIANS 4:9"> “This is what I, Yahweh, the God whom the Israeli people say that they worship, say: ‘The good figs represent &#91;SIM&#93; the people of Judah whom I exiled to Babylonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:10; JEREMIAH 29:10; JEREMIAH 33:7"> I &#91;SYN&#93; will ◄watch over/take care of► them well, and some day I will bring them back here to Judah. I will establish them and cause them to be strong. I will cause them to be prosperous &#91;MET&#93;, and I will not exile them again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 8:10; JEREMIAH 30:22; EZEKIEL 36:24–28"> I will enable them to desire to know &#91;IDM&#93; that I am Yahweh. They will be my people, and I will be their God, because they will return to me sincerely.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 43:1; JEREMIAH 39:2–9; JEREMIAH 24:2"> But I, Yahweh, also say, ‘The bad figs represent &#91;SIM&#93; Zedekiah, the king of Judah, and his officials, and all the other people who remain in Jerusalem, and those who have gone to Egypt. I will do to them like people do to rotten figs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:4; JEREMIAH 29:18; DEUTERONOMY 28:37"> I will get rid of them, with the result that people in every nation on the earth will be horrified, and will hate them because they are evil people. Wherever I scatter them, people will make fun of them, and say that they are disgraced, and ridicule them, and curse them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; EZEKIEL 5:12–17; JEREMIAH 16:4"> And I will cause them to experience wars and famines and diseases, until they have disappeared from this land which I gave to them and to their ancestors.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
