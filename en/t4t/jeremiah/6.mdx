## Yahweh predicts what will happen to Jerusalem

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 20:19–20; JEREMIAH 32:24"> Yahweh, the Commander of the armies of angels in heaven, says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will command those soldiers to cut down the trees outside Jerusalem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and to build dirt ramps up to the top of the city walls</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order that they can enter the city.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This city must be punished</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because everyone there continually oppresses others.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:8; EZEKIEL 7:23; EZEKIEL 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is as though the wicked things that the people do</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">pour out of the city like &#91;SIM&#93; water flows out of a spring.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The noise from people doing violent and destructive actions is heard everywhere.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I continually see people who are suffering and wounded.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:18; HOSEA 9:12; JEREMIAH 17:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to what I am warning you, you people of &#91;APO&#93; Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because if you do not listen, I will reject you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and cause your land to become desolate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">a land where no one lives.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:9; JEREMIAH 16:16; OBADIAH 1:5–6"> Yahweh, the Commander of the armies of angels in heaven, also says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-2">“I will tell your enemies to cause your country to become as desolate &#91;SIM&#93; as a vineyard from which all the grapes have been completely stripped from the vines.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their soldiers will seize the possessions of those who remain in Israel after the others have been exiled</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; farmers go to the vines again to pick any grapes that were ◄left/not picked►.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ACTS 7:51; JEREMIAH 7:26; JOHN 7:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I said, “If I speak to the Israeli people to warn them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">◄who will listen to me?/No one will listen to me.► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is as though their ears are closed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and as a result they cannot hear what I say.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They scorn Yahweh’s messages;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they do not want to listen to them at all.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:21; JEREMIAH 20:9; MICAH 3:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">So now I am extremely angry, like Yahweh is angry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I cannot restrain it any longer.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So Yahweh said to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Tell everyone that you are very angry with them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Tell the children in the streets and the young men who gather together.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Tell the men and their wives;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">tell the very old people &#91;DOU&#93;, also.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:10; Isaiah 5:25; DEUTERONOMY 28:39–43"> <span className="pl pl-1">Tell the men that I will give their houses to their enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will give their property/fields and their wives to them, also,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I punish &#91;IDM&#93; the people who live in this land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:11; JEREMIAH 8:10; MICAH 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everyone is trying to get money by tricking others,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the most important people to the least important people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even the prophets and the priests are trying to deceive people to get money.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:10; JEREMIAH 23:17; JEREMIAH 4:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They act as though the sins of my people are like &#91;MET&#93; small wounds that they do not need to put bandages on.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They continually greet people by saying ‘I hope things are going well with you,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when things are not going well.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:12; MATTHEW 15:14; JEREMIAH 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">They should be &#91;RHQ&#93; ashamed about the disgusting things that they do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they are not ashamed at all.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They do not even know how to ◄blush/show on their faces that they are ashamed►.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So, they also will be among those who will be killed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will be destroyed when I punish them.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 30:21; DEUTERONOMY 32:7; JEREMIAH 18:15"> This is also what Yahweh said to the Israeli people:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Stand at the crossroads and look at the people who pass by.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Ask them what was the good behavior that their ancestors had long ago.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And when they tell you, behave that way.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">If you do that, you will find rest for your souls.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you replied, ‘We do not want to do that!’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:4; Isaiah 58:1; ZECHARIAH 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I sent my prophets who were like &#91;MET&#93; watchmen.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They said, ‘Listen carefully when we blow the trumpets to warn you that your enemies are approaching,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you said, ‘No, we do not want to listen.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:10; JEREMIAH 31:10; MICAH 6:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, you people in the other nations, listen to this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Pay attention to what is going to happen to the Israeli people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; JEREMIAH 8:9; JEREMIAH 17:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen, all of you!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am going to cause the Israeli people to experience disasters.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">That is what will happen to them because they have refused to listen to what I told them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They have refused to obey my laws.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11; AMOS 5:21–22; Isaiah 60:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You Israeli people, when you burn frankincense that came from far away in Sheba,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and when you offer to me sweet-smelling anointing oil that came from far away,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will not &#91;RHQ&#93; be pleased with your sacrifices.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will not accept the sacrifices that are completely burned on the altar;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am not pleased with any of your sacrifices.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; EZEKIEL 3:20; JEREMIAH 13:16"> Therefore, I will put obstacles on the roads on which my people will travel.<br /><span className="pl pl-2">Men and their sons and people’s neighbors and friends will stumble over those obstacles and fall down;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">everyone will die.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:41–43; JEREMIAH 10:22; JEREMIAH 6:1"> Yahweh also says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You will see a huge army marching towards you from the north.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">An army of a great nation very far away is preparing to attack you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 13:18; JEREMIAH 50:42; LUKE 21:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have bows and arrows and spears;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are very cruel, and do not act mercifully to anyone.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">As they ride along on their horses,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the horses’ feet sound like the roaring of the ocean waves;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are riding in battle formation</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to attack you people of Jerusalem.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:31; JEREMIAH 50:43; JEREMIAH 49:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of Jerusalem say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We have heard reports about the enemy;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so we are very frightened, with the result that we feel weak.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We are very afraid, and worried,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; women who are about to give birth to babies.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:29; JEREMIAH 20:10; PSALMS 31:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">So one person says to another,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Do not go out into the fields! Do not go on the roads,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the enemy soldiers have swords and they are everywhere;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are coming from all directions, and we are extremely afraid.’ ”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:8; ZECHARIAH 12:10; Isaiah 32:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I say, “My dear people, put on ◄sackcloth/rough clothes► and sit in ashes to show that you are sorry for your sins.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Mourn and cry very much,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; a woman would cry if her only son had died,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because your enemies are very near,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they are going to destroy everything.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:18; JEREMIAH 15:20; EZEKIEL 3:8–10"> Then Yahweh said to me,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Jeremiah, I have caused you to become like &#91;MET&#93; someone who heats metal very hot to completely burn the impurities.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You will examine my people’s behavior.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:4; JEREMIAH 5:23; JEREMIAH 18:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will find out that they are very stubborn rebels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are always slandering others.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their inner beings are as hard as bronze or iron;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they all continually deceive others.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:13; JEREMIAH 9:7; 1 PETER 4:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">A metalworker causes the bellows to blow very hard to make the fire very hot to completely burn up the impurities &#91;MET&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But just as a fire does not cause all the waste material to run off,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is impossible to separate the righteous people from the wicked people, and punish only the wicked people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="HOSEA 9:17; PSALMS 119:119; Isaiah 1:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I, Yahweh, have rejected them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I say that they are like &#91;MET&#93; worthless silver.”</span></span>
