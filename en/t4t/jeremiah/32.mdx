## Jeremiah bought some land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:1–2; 2 KINGS 25:1–2; JEREMIAH 25:1"> After Zedekiah had been ruling Judah for almost ten years, Yahweh gave me another message, during the time that Nebuchadnezzar had been ruling Babylonia for almost 18 years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:21; Nehemiah 3:25; JEREMIAH 33:1"> His army was surrounding Jerusalem, and I was in a prison area in the courtyard where the guards of the king’s palace stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 34:2–3; JEREMIAH 21:4–7; JEREMIAH 26:8–9"> King Zedekiah had put me there. I continued to prophesy there about what would happen. I continued to say, “Yahweh says that he is about to allow the army of the king of Babylon to capture this city &#91;DOU&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:4–7; 2 KINGS 25:4–7; JEREMIAH 37:17"> And the soldiers of Babylonia will capture King Zedekiah and ◄take him to/put him in the hands of► the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:7; EZEKIEL 17:9–10; EZEKIEL 17:15"> Then his soldiers will take Zedekiah to Babylon, and he will remain there until I arrange for him to be punished. And if he tries to fight against the soldiers from Babylonia, he will not succeed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> At that time, Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:1; JEREMIAH 11:21; LEVITICUS 25:25"> “Your cousin Hanamel, the son of Shallum your uncle, will come to you. He will say to you, ‘Buy my field at Anathoth, your hometown. Because you are my closest relative, it is written in our laws that you have the right to buy it before I ask if anyone else wants to buy it.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:7; JEREMIAH 32:2; JEREMIAH 33:1"> And just as Yahweh had predicted, my cousin Hanamel came to see me in the courtyard of the palace. He said, “Please buy my field at Anathoth in the area where the descendants of Benjamin live. It is written in our laws that you have the right to buy it before I ask if anyone else wants to buy it.” When he said that, I knew that the message that I had received was truly from Yahweh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 11:12–13; Esther 3:9; 1 KINGS 20:39"> So, I bought the field at Anathoth. I paid Hanamel 17 pieces of silver for it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:44; JEREMIAH 32:12; JEREMIAH 32:25"> I signed the paper/papyrus on which it was written that I was buying it, while others were watching/witnessing it. Then I weighed the silver and gave it to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 2:27; 1 CORINTHIANS 11:16; ACTS 26:3"> Then I took two copies of the paper/papyrus. One was sealed and the other was not sealed. On both of them was written the price and conditions of the purchase. I took both copies</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:16; JEREMIAH 51:59; JEREMIAH 36:4–5"> and I gave them to Baruch, who was the son of Neraiah and the grandson of Mahseiah. I did this while my cousin Hanamel, the other witnesses who had signed the paper/papyrus, and other men of Judah who were there in the courtyard, were watching.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then, while they were all listening, I said to Baruch,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> “This is what the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom we Israelis worship, says: ‘Take both copies of this paper/papyrus and put them in a clay jar, to preserve them for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 3:10; AMOS 9:14–15; JEREMIAH 30:18"> Do that because this is what I, the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom you Israelis worship, say: “Some day people will again own property in this land, and they will buy and sell houses and vineyards and fields.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeremiah’s query about buying the land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 12:1; GENESIS 32:9–12; PHILIPPIANS 4:6–7"> After I had given the papers/papyri to Baruch, I prayed to Yahweh, saying this:</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LUKE 1:37; JEREMIAH 32:27; GENESIS 18:14"> “Yahweh, you are my Lord! You made the sky and the earth by your very great power. &#91;MTY, DOU&#93; Nothing is too difficult for you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:16; DEUTERONOMY 5:9–10; EXODUS 34:7"> You show thousands of people that you faithfully love them, but you punish people for the sins that their parents have committed. You are the great and powerful God, the Commander of the armies of angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:10; MATTHEW 16:27; PSALMS 62:12"> You make wise plans and you do mighty deeds. You see how all people behave &#91;DOU&#93;, and you do to them what they deserve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; 2 SAMUEL 7:23; EXODUS 10:2"> You performed many miracles &#91;DOU&#93; in Egypt, and you continue to perform miracles here in Israel and everywhere else in the world. Because of that, you have ◄become very famous/caused many people to know that you are very great/powerful►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EXODUS 6:6; DEUTERONOMY 26:8; PSALMS 105:43"> You brought our Israeli ancestors out of Egypt by performing many great miracles &#91;DOU&#93;, using your very great power &#91;DOU&#93;, causing our enemies to be terrified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EXODUS 3:8; EXODUS 13:5; JEREMIAH 11:5"> You gave to us Israeli people this land that you solemnly promised to give to our ancestors, a land that is very fertile &#91;IDM&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezra 9:7; PSALMS 78:54–55; DANIEL 9:10–14"> Our ancestors came here and conquered this land and started to live in it, but they refused to obey you or to do what you commanded them to do. Because of that, you have caused us to experience all these disasters.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:36; DEUTERONOMY 4:26; JOSHUA 23:15–16"> And now, the army of Babylonia has built ramps up against our city walls in order to attack our city. Because of our enemies’ swords and because of famines and diseases, they will be able to conquer it easily. The things that you said would happen have now happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:24; PSALMS 97:2; PSALMS 77:19"> And it is evident that the Babylonian army will soon conquer this city. So now, Yahweh my Lord, I do not understand why you told me to buy this field with my silver, while others were watching. It seems that I have just been wasting my money by doing that!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh’s reply about buying the land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 19:26; JEREMIAH 32:17; Isaiah 64:8"> “I am Yahweh, the God who rules all the people in the world. There is absolutely nothing &#91;RHQ&#93; that is too difficult for me to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:3; JEREMIAH 32:24; JEREMIAH 19:7–12"> So, this is what I say: It is true that I will enable the Babylonian army and King Nebuchadnezzar to capture this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:10; JEREMIAH 19:13; JEREMIAH 52:13"> The soldiers of Babylonia who are now outside the walls around the city will enter and burn this city. They will burn down all the houses where people caused me to become angry by burning incense on the tops of their roofs to honor Baal and by pouring out offerings of wine to other gods.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:22–26; DEUTERONOMY 9:7–12; JEREMIAH 25:7"> The people of Israel and Judah have continually done only evil things from the time that they became a nation. They have caused me to become very angry by all their evil deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 KINGS 23:27; JEREMIAH 27:10; 2 KINGS 21:4–7"> From the time that this city was built until now, the people of this city have done only things that caused me to be very angry &#91;DOU&#93;. So now I will destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="DANIEL 9:8; JEREMIAH 2:26; Isaiah 1:4–6"> The people of Israel and Judah, including their kings, their officials, the priests, the false prophets, and all the other people in Jerusalem have committed many sins that have caused me to become angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:13; JEREMIAH 2:27; EZEKIEL 8:16"> My people have turned away from me and have refused to return to me. Even though I taught them things many times, they would not pay attention to what I taught them, and they would not obey me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:30; EZEKIEL 8:5–16; 2 KINGS 21:4–7"> They have set up their detestable idols even in my own &#91;MTY&#93; temple and ◄defiled it/caused it to become a place that is unacceptable for people to worship me►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:31; 2 CHRONICLES 33:6; LEVITICUS 18:21"> They have built shrines to honor Baal in Ben-Hinnom Valley outside Jerusalem, and there they sacrifice their sons and daughters to their god Molech. I never commanded them to do such horrible things. I never even thought that anyone might do such a terrible thing. And by doing it they have caused all the people of Judah to be guilty of having sinned.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The promise to restore Judah and Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:24; JEREMIAH 32:3; JEREMIAH 32:28"> “But now I will say something more about this city. You people of Jerusalem have been saying, ‘The army of the King of Babylon will conquer it, either by their swords or because of famines or diseases.’ But this is what I, Yahweh, the God whom you Israeli people say you belong to, say:</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:3; JEREMIAH 23:6; EZEKIEL 34:12–14"> ‘I will certainly bring my people back here again, from all the countries to which I will force them to go because I am extremely angry &#91;DOU&#93; with them. I will bring them back to this city and allow them to live here safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:7; EZEKIEL 11:19–20; HEBREWS 8:10"> They will be my people, and I will be their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:19–20; ACTS 4:32; JOHN 17:21"> I will cause them to have one way of thinking &#91;IDM&#93; and behaving, in order that they may revere me, for their good and for the good of their descendants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:31–33; EZEKIEL 36:26; JEREMIAH 24:7"> I will make an agreement with them that will last forever: I will never stop doing good things for them, and I will encourage them to revere me and never turn away from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:6; ZEPHANIAH 3:17; Isaiah 62:5"> I will be happy to do good things for them, and I will surely enable them to return to this land and remain here &#91;MET&#93;; I will do that with all my inner being and all my strength.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:28; JOSHUA 23:14–15; JEREMIAH 33:10–11"> And this is also what I, Yahweh, say: ‘I have caused them to experience all these disasters. Similarly, some day I will do for them all the good things that I have promised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="EZEKIEL 37:11–14; JEREMIAH 32:15; JEREMIAH 32:36"> By buying land you, Jeremiah, have predicted that some day people will buy and sell fields in this land about which you people of Jerusalem now say, “The Babylonian soldiers have destroyed it. It is now desolate. It is a land where there are no longer any people or animals.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:26; JEREMIAH 33:26; JEREMIAH 33:11"> But some day people will again buy and sell fields here. People will sign documents about buying those fields, and other people will witness them doing that. That will happen in the land where the descendants of Benjamin live and here in the villages near Jerusalem, in other towns in Judah, in the hilly areas and in the foothills to the west, and in the southern desert area, too. Some day I will cause them to prosper again. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
