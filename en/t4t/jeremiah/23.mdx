## God will punish the evil leaders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:21; ZECHARIAH 11:15–17; EZEKIEL 34:2–10"> Yahweh declares, “Terrible things will happen to the leaders &#91;MET&#93; of my people—those who are like shepherds of the people who are like my sheep—because they have scattered my people and sent them away, and have not taken care of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 32:34; JEREMIAH 21:12; MICAH 7:4"> So, this is what I, Yahweh, the God whom the Israeli people worship, say to those leaders: ‘Instead of taking care of my people and leading them to places where they are safe, like a shepherd does for his sheep, you have scattered them. So I will punish you for the evil things that you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 9:14–15; JEREMIAH 32:37; Isaiah 43:5–6"> But later I will gather those who are still alive, from the countries where I have forced them to go. I will bring them back to their own country, where they will have many children, and their number/population will increase.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:14–15; 1 PETER 1:5; EZEKIEL 34:23–31"> Then I will appoint other leaders for my people, leaders who will take care of them. And my people will never be afraid &#91;DOU&#93; of anything again, and none of them will be like a lost sheep.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="LUKE 1:32–33; Isaiah 4:2; Isaiah 9:7"> Yahweh also says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Some day I will appoint for you a righteous man</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">who will be a descendant of King David.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will be a king who rules wisely.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">He will do what is just and right throughout the land.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:30; JEREMIAH 33:16; PHILIPPIANS 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">At that time, all the Israeli people &#91;DOU&#93; will be saved from their enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be safe.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And his name will be</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Yahweh, the one who vindicates/defends us.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 16:14–15; JEREMIAH 23:3; Isaiah 43:18–19"> Yahweh also says that at that time, people who are solemnly promising to do something will no longer say, “I will do it as surely as Yahweh lives, who rescued the Israeli people from Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; AMOS 9:14–15; EZEKIEL 34:13"> Instead, they will say, “I will do it as surely as Yahweh lives, who brought us Israeli people back to our own land, from the land to the northeast and from all the other countries to which he had exiled us.” And they will live in their own land again.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The false prophets will be punished

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="HABAKKUK 3:16; LAMENTATIONS 3:15; PSALMS 60:3"> I am very shocked because of the sacred message that Yahweh has spoken about what will happen to the false prophets;<br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is as though all my bones shake.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I stagger like a man who is drunk</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after drinking a lot of wine.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HOSEA 4:2–3; PSALMS 107:34; JEREMIAH 9:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The land is full of people who commit adultery;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and Yahweh has cursed the land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even the pastures in the desert are all dried up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because the people do what is evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the false prophets use their power to do things that are not just/fair.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 3:4; JEREMIAH 6:13; MATTHEW 21:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says, “Yes, even the priests and the prophets are ungodly;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they do wicked things even in my temple.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 13:16; JEREMIAH 11:23; PSALMS 35:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, it will be as though the paths that they walk on are slippery.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It will be as though they are being chased in the darkness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there they will fall down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because I will cause them to experience disasters</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">at the time that I will punish them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:8; Isaiah 9:16; 1 KINGS 18:18–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Previously I saw that the prophets in Samaria were doing something that was wrong;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they were prophesying, saying that Baal gave them the messages that they were proclaiming,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they were deceiving my people.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9–10; JEREMIAH 29:23; EZEKIEL 22:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">And now I have seen the prophets in Jerusalem doing terrible things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They commit adultery</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and habitually tell lies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They encourage evil people to continue to do evil things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that the people do not stop sinning.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Those prophets are as wicked as the people in Sodom and Gomorrah were.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:14; JEREMIAH 9:15; LAMENTATIONS 3:5"> So, this is what the Commander of the armies of angels says about those false prophets:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will give those prophets bitter things to eat</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and poison to drink,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because it is because of them that this land is filled with people who do wicked things.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:14; EZEKIEL 13:6; GALATIANS 1:8–9"> This is what the Commander of the armies of angels says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Do not pay any attention to what those false prophets say to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they are just deceiving you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They tell you about visions that they have thought/created only in their own minds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">not about visions that I have given them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MICAH 3:11; JEREMIAH 13:10; AMOS 9:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They habitually say to those who hate me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Yahweh says that you will have peace.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And they say to those who stubbornly do what they want to do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Nothing bad will happen to you because of your doing those things.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:22; Isaiah 40:13–14; AMOS 3:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But none of them has ever been in a council meeting in heaven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">in order to listen to a message from me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">None of them has paid attention to anything that I, Yahweh, have said.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:23; JEREMIAH 25:32; AMOS 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, listen to this: I will punish them; it will be like a great storm;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it will come down like a whirlwind, swirling around the heads of those wicked people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:24; Isaiah 55:11; ZECHARIAH 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will not stop being angry</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until I completely accomplish all that I have planned.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">In the future, you will understand all of this clearly.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:14; JEREMIAH 27:15; JEREMIAH 23:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh also says, “I have not appointed those prophets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they run around telling people their messages.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I did not speak to them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they continue to prophesy.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:18; ZECHARIAH 1:4; JEREMIAH 25:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">If they had been in my council meetings,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they would have been able to speak messages from me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they would have caused people to turn away from committing evil things.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 139:1–10; 1 KINGS 20:23; 1 KINGS 20:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh also says, “Am I a God who is only nearby?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">No, I am a God who is far away, also.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:3; PSALMS 139:7; PSALMS 90:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, no one can hide in some secret place</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that I cannot see him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am everywhere, in heaven and on the earth!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That is what I, Yahweh, say!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:32; JEREMIAH 23:28; JEREMIAH 29:8"> I have heard those prophets prophesy lies, saying that they are telling people messages from me. They say, ‘Listen to me tell you the dream that God gave me last night! I really had this dream!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 4:1–2; JEREMIAH 14:14; 2 THESSALONIANS 2:9–11"> How long will they continue to do this? How long will those lying prophets continue to prophesy things that come only from their own minds?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JUDGES 8:33–34; JUDGES 3:7; ACTS 13:8"> They think that because of the dreams that they tell to each other, people will forget me, like their ancestors forgot about me when they started to worship Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 2:17; 1 TIMOTHY 1:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:12–13"> Allow those false prophets to tell people their dreams, but those who have messages that really come from me should proclaim those messages faithfully. I, Yahweh, say that like straw and grain are certainly very different, my messages and the messages from those false prophets are certainly very different.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12; JEREMIAH 5:14; LUKE 24:32"> It is as though my messages burn like a fire; they strike people’s inner beings like someone strikes a rock with a hammer and smashes it into pieces.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 18:20; EZEKIEL 13:8; JEREMIAH 14:14–15"> Therefore, I, Yahweh say, 'I oppose all those prophets who steal messages from each other and claim that those messages came from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:17; 2 CHRONICLES 18:10–12; 2 CHRONICLES 18:5"> I oppose those prophets who speak their own messages but claim that those messages came from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:7–18; ZEPHANIAH 3:4; LAMENTATIONS 2:14"> I oppose those prophets who falsely say that I told them something in a vision, but they are only telling lies that cause my people to sin. I did not send those prophets. I did not even appoint them to be prophets. And they have no messages that will benefit my people at all.' That is what I, Yahweh, declare.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Messages from false prophets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MALACHI 1:1; JEREMIAH 12:7; HABAKKUK 1:1"> Yahweh said to me, “If one of those prophets or priests or one of the other people asks you, ‘What problem has Yahweh told you about now?’, you must reply, ‘You are the problem! And Yahweh says that he will abandon you!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:3; LAMENTATIONS 2:14"> And if any prophet or priest or anyone else falsely says, ‘I have a prophecy from Yahweh,’ I will punish that person and his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:34; JEREMIAH 42:4; JEREMIAH 33:3"> What you should continually ask each other is, ‘When you spoke to Yahweh, what did he reply? What is he saying to us?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:10; 2 PETER 3:16; Isaiah 3:8"> But stop saying ‘I have a prophecy/message from Yahweh,’ because those who say that are doing it only because they want people to accept their ideas, and by doing that they distort/change the messages that are from our God, the Commander of the armies of angels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> This is what you should ask each prophet: ‘What did Yahweh reply when you talked to him? What is he saying to us?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 11:13–14"> If he replies, ‘What I told you is a prophecy/message from Yahweh,’ you should say to him, ‘You have continued to say, “I received a prophecy/message from Yahweh” even though I told you to not claim that you have received prophecies/messages from Yahweh.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:33; EZEKIEL 8:18; PROVERBS 13:13"> So I , Yahweh, will get rid of you false prophets. I will expel you from my presence. And I will get rid of this city that I gave to you and to your ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:11; EZEKIEL 5:14–15; JEREMIAH 42:18"> I will cause people to make fun of you forever. People will never forget that you were disgraced.”</span>
</div>
</div>
