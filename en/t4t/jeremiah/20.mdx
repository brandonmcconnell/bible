## Pashhur arrested Jeremiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezra 2:37–38; 1 CHRONICLES 24:14; 2 KINGS 25:18"> Pashhur, the son of Immer, was a priest who supervised the temple guards. He heard those things that I had prophesied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:13; ZECHARIAH 14:10; JEREMIAH 1:19"> So he arrested me. Then he commanded guards to whip me and fasten my feet in ◄stocks/a wooden frame► at the Benjamin Gate of Yahweh’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:10; JEREMIAH 46:5; LAMENTATIONS 2:22"> The next day, when Pashhur released me, I said to him, “Pashhur, Yahweh is giving you a new name. From now on, your name will be ‘Surrounded by Terror’,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:21; JEREMIAH 25:9; JEREMIAH 21:4–10"> because Yahweh says this to you: ‘I will cause you and your friends to be terrified. You will watch them being killed by your enemies’ swords. I will enable the army of the king of Babylon to capture the people of Judah. Those soldiers will take some of the people to Babylon, and they will kill others with their swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:13; EZEKIEL 22:25; LAMENTATIONS 1:10"> And I will enable their soldiers to take away other things in Jerusalem. They will take to Babylon all the very valuable things that belonged to your kings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:14–15; LAMENTATIONS 2:14; EZEKIEL 22:28"> And as for you, Pashhur, they will take you and all your family to Babylon.
