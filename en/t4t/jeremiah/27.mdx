## Jeremiah urged them to surrender to Nebuchadnezzar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:3; JEREMIAH 28:1; JEREMIAH 27:12"> Soon after Zedekiah became the King of Judah, Yahweh gave a message to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="AMOS 7:1; AMOS 7:4; JEREMIAH 27:12"> This is what he said to me: “Make a ◄yoke/set of wooden bars to fasten around the neck of an ox►, but fasten it around your own neck with leather straps.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="AMOS 1:9–3; EZEKIEL 25:1–17; EZEKIEL 17:15–21"> Then send messages to the kings of Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyre, and Sidon, by telling those messages to the ambassadors from those countries who have come to Jerusalem to talk to King Zedekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 51:19; JEREMIAH 10:10; JEREMIAH 25:27"> Tell them to give this message to their kings: This is what the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom the Israeli people worship, says:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:17; JEREMIAH 51:15; PSALMS 115:15–16"> ‘With my very great power &#91;MTY, DOU&#93; I created the earth and the people and the animals that are on the earth. And I can give my power to anyone whom I want to.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 28:14; JEREMIAH 25:9; DANIEL 2:37–38"> And now I am going to enable King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon, who does what I want him to do, to control your countries. I am going to enable him to rule everything, even the wild animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:31; REVELATION 13:5–10; DANIEL 5:25–31"> The people of all the nations will work for him, and later for his son, and later for his grandson, until the time for them to rule is finished. Then the armies of many great kings from many nations will conquer Babylon.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:21; EZEKIEL 17:19–21; JEREMIAH 38:17–19"> But now I tell you that you must do what the king of Babylon wants you to do &#91;MET&#93;, like an ox that has a yoke on its neck must do what its master wants it to do. I will punish any nation that refuses to do that. I will cause those people to experience war and famine and diseases, until the armies of Babylon have conquered that nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:14; MALACHI 3:5; JEREMIAH 29:8"> So, do not pay attention to your false prophets and fortune-tellers and people who predict what will happen by working magic or by talking with spirits of dead people. Those people say that the king of Babylon will not conquer your country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:31; JEREMIAH 28:16; JEREMIAH 23:25"> Those people are all liars. If you believe what they say, it will result in your being exiled from your land. I will cause you to be taken from your land, and you will die far away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:8; JEREMIAH 27:2; JEREMIAH 38:2"> But the people of any country who do what the King of Babylon wants them to do will remain in their own country and be able to plant their crops &#91;MTY&#93; as they always have done. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 28:1; JEREMIAH 38:17; 2 CHRONICLES 36:11–13"> After I gave that message to those ambassadors, I gave the same message to King Zedekiah of Judah. I said to him, “If you want to remain alive, do what the King of Babylon and his officials want you to do &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:31; JEREMIAH 27:8; PROVERBS 8:36"> It would be foolish for you not to do that, because the result would be that you and your people would die by your enemies’ swords or by famine or diseases, which Yahweh will cause any nation to experience that refuses to allow the King of Babylon to rule them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:14; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:13–15; JEREMIAH 29:8–9"> Do not pay attention to those prophets who say to you, ‘The King of Babylon will not conquer your country.’ They are liars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 27:10; 2 CHRONICLES 25:16; JEREMIAH 6:13–15"> This is what Yahweh says: ‘I have not appointed those prophets. They are saying that I &#91;MTY&#93; gave them messages, but they are lying. So, if you believe them, I will expel you from this land. And you and all those prophets will die in Babylon!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 KINGS 24:13; JEREMIAH 28:3; DANIEL 1:2"> Then I spoke to the priests and the other people, and I said, “This is what Yahweh says: ‘Do not believe your prophets who tell you that all the gold items that were taken from my temple by soldiers from Babylon will soon be returned from Babylon, because what they are prophesying is a lie.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:17; JEREMIAH 38:23; JEREMIAH 7:34"> Do not pay attention to what they say. Surrender to the king of Babylon. If you do that, you will remain alive. If you do not do that &#91;RHQ&#93;, this entire city will be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 7:8; JEREMIAH 18:20; 1 SAMUEL 12:19"> If they are really prophets who speak messages from me, tell them to plead to me, the Commander of the armies of angels, that the soldiers from Babylon will not be allowed to take away to Babylon the valuable items that still remain in the temple and in the king’s palace and in the other palaces in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:13; JEREMIAH 52:17–23; 2 KINGS 25:17"> I say this because the huge pillars that are in front of the temple and the large water tank and the ten water carts and all the other items that are used for offering sacrifices are still in this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:1; 2 CHRONICLES 36:10; 2 KINGS 24:14–16"> King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon left those things here when he exiled Jehoiachin, the king of Judah, to Babylon, along with all the other leaders of Jerusalem and the leaders of other places in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> I, the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom the Israeli people worship, say this about all those valuable things that are still outside the temple and in the palace of the king of Judah and in other places in Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:10; Ezra 7:19; JEREMIAH 32:5"> They will all be carried away to Babylon. And they will stay there until I say that they should be brought back to Jerusalem. Then they will be bought back here. That is what I, Yahweh, say.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>
