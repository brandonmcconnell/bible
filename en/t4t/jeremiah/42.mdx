## Jeremiah warned them to not go to Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 41:11; JEREMIAH 40:13; JEREMIAH 6:13"> Then Johanan and Azariah the son of Hoshaiah, and all the other leaders of the Israeli soldiers who had not surrendered to the army of Babylonia, and many &#91;HYP&#93; other people, including those who were important and those who were not important, came</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 36:7; LEVITICUS 26:22; ACTS 8:24"> to me. They said, “Please listen to our request and pray to Yahweh our God for all of us. Although we were previously a huge number of people, you can see that now we are only a small number of people who have survived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 86:11; PROVERBS 3:6; PSALMS 25:4–5"> Pray that Yahweh our God will show us what we should do and where we should go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 12:23; 1 SAMUEL 3:17–18; EXODUS 8:29"> I replied, “Okay, I will pray to Yahweh our God, like you have requested, and I will tell you what he says. I will ◄tell you everything/not hide anything from you►.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GENESIS 31:50; JUDGES 11:10; MALACHI 3:5"> They replied to me, “We hope/wish that Yahweh our God will be a faithful witness against us if we refuse to do everything that he tells us to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:23; DEUTERONOMY 5:29; ROMANS 8:7"> We are requesting you to ask Yahweh our God what we should do. When he replies, we will obey him, whether we like what he says or not. We will do that because we know that things will go well for us if we obey him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 27:14; Isaiah 28:16; HABAKKUK 2:3"> So I prayed to Yahweh, and ten days later he gave me his reply.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:1; JEREMIAH 41:11–16; JEREMIAH 43:2–5"> So I summoned Johanan and all his other soldiers and all the other people, including those who were important and those who were not important.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 KINGS 19:4; JEREMIAH 42:2; 2 KINGS 19:6"> I said to them, “You told me to tell Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis worship, what you were requesting. This is what he replied:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:28; EZEKIEL 36:36; JEREMIAH 24:6"> ‘You should stay here in this land. If you do that, I will cause your nation to be strong and not be weak. I will cause you to prosper and not be exiled again. I am sorry about the disasters that I was forced to cause you to experience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ROMANS 8:31; JEREMIAH 1:19; JEREMIAH 1:8"> But do not be afraid of the king of Babylon any more, because I will ◄be with/help► you. I will rescue you &#91;DOU&#93; from his power &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; PSALMS 106:45–46; PROVERBS 16:7"> I will be merciful to you by causing him to act kindly toward you. So as a result, he will allow you to stay here in your land.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:16; EXODUS 5:2; JEREMIAH 42:10"> But if you refuse to obey Yahweh our God, and if you say ‘We will not stay here;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 41:17; JEREMIAH 4:19; Isaiah 31:1"> instead, we will go to Egypt. There we will not experience any wars, we will not hear trumpets signaling our soldiers to prepare for battles, and we will not be hungry,’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:16; JEREMIAH 44:12–14; JEREMIAH 42:17"> then listen to what the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom we Israelis worship, says: ‘If you are determined to go to Egypt, and you go and live there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:27; EZEKIEL 11:8; AMOS 9:1–4"> you will experience those wars and famines that you are afraid of, and you will all die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:22; JEREMIAH 44:28; JEREMIAH 44:13–14"> That is what will happen to all you who are determined to go to Egypt and live there. Some of you will be killed by the swords of your enemies, and others of you will die from famines and from diseases. None of you will escape the disasters that I will bring on you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:20; JEREMIAH 18:16; JEREMIAH 29:18"> And the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom we Israelis worship, also says this: ‘Because I was very angry, I severely punished &#91;MTY&#93; all the people of Jerusalem. I will do the same things to you when you go to Egypt. The result will be that people will curse you. They will be horrified about what has happened to you. They will make fun of you, and you will never see this land again.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 17:16; Isaiah 30:1–7; Nehemiah 9:26"> You little group of people of Judah who are still alive, listen to me: Yahweh has told you, ‘Do not go to Egypt.’ So do not forget what I have warned you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:3–4; EZEKIEL 33:31; MATTHEW 22:15–18"> I know that you were lying when you requested me to pray to Yahweh our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="EZEKIEL 2:7; ZECHARIAH 7:11–12; ACTS 20:26–27"> And today I have told you exactly what he said, but I know that you will not obey Yahweh our God now, just as you have not obeyed him previously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:17; EZEKIEL 6:11; HOSEA 9:6"> You want to go to Egypt and live there. So now, you can be sure of this: All of you will die there. Some of you will be killed by the swords of your enemies and others will die from famines or from diseases.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
