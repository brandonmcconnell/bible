## Israel’s idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:7–17; EZEKIEL 16:31; JEREMIAH 7:24"> One day when Josiah was the king of Judah, Yahweh said to me, “Have you seen what the people of Israel have done? They have turned away from me, like a woman who has abandoned her husband and sleeps with other men. They have gone up on every hilltop and under every big tree and worshiped idols there &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 16:46; HOSEA 6:1–4; HOSEA 14:1"> I thought that they would return to me, so I said to them, ‘Come back to me!’ But they refused. So I sent them away to other countries, like a man writes a note saying that he is divorcing his wife and then sends his wife away because she has committed adultery &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 24:1; HOSEA 4:15–17; Isaiah 50:1"> The people of Judah saw what I did to them. But they are just like the people of Israel. <span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:27; Isaiah 57:6; JEREMIAH 3:2"> They thought that worshiping idols did not matter to me, so they have made the entire land unacceptable to me by worshiping idols of wood and stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="HOSEA 7:14; JEREMIAH 12:2; 2 CHRONICLES 34:33–18"> The people of Judah have pretended to return to me, but they have not really done that. This is true because I, Yahweh, have said it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EZEKIEL 23:11; EZEKIEL 16:51–52; HOSEA 11:7"> Then Yahweh said to me, “The people of Israel have turned away from me, but what the people of Judah have done is worse.</span>
