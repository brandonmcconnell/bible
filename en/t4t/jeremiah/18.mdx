## The potter and the clay

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh gave another message to me. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="HEBREWS 1:1; ACTS 9:6; JEREMIAH 19:1–2"> “Go to the shop of the man who makes clay pots. I will give you a message there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JOHN 15:14; JONAH 1:3; ACTS 26:19"> So I went to that shop, and I saw the man who makes pots. He was working at the wheel that he uses to form pots.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ROMANS 9:20–23; JEREMIAH 18:6; Isaiah 45:9"> But when he finished making one jar, it was not as good as he hoped/wanted it to be. So, he took that clay and formed it into another jar, shaping it as he desired.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; ROMANS 9:20–21; JEREMIAH 18:4"> “Perhaps the people of Israel think that I cannot do to them like this man who makes pots has done &#91;RHQ&#93;. But what they think is wrong. I can control what happens to them like this man controls what he does with the clay in his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:10; JEREMIAH 12:14–17; JEREMIAH 45:4"> There may be a time when I proclaim that I will get rid of a nation or kingdom, like someone pulls up a plant with its roots, and smashes it, and destroys it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:21; JEREMIAH 26:13; JONAH 3:9–10"> But if the people of that nation repent of doing evil things, I will not send to them the disasters that I planned to send.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:28; AMOS 9:11–15; JEREMIAH 1:10"> And there may be a time when I proclaim that I will establish a nation or kingdom and cause it to be strong &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:18; 1 SAMUEL 2:30; EZEKIEL 18:24"> But if the people of that nation start to do evil things and refuse to obey me, then I will not bless them as I said that I would do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 KINGS 17:13; Isaiah 55:6–7; JEREMIAH 35:15"> Therefore, Jeremiah, go and warn all the people in Jerusalem and in other places in Judah. Say to them, ‘This is what Yahweh says: “I am planning to cause you to experience a disaster &#91;DOU&#93;. So, each of you should turn from your evil behavior and start doing what is right, in order that you will not experience that disaster.” ’ ’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:25; JEREMIAH 7:24; DEUTERONOMY 29:19"> So I told that message to the people, but they replied, “It is useless for you to tell us that. We will continue to be stubborn and behave as we want to.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:30; HOSEA 6:10; JEREMIAH 23:14"> So, this is what Yahweh says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Ask the people who live in other nations if they have &#91;RHQ&#93; ever heard of such a thing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">My Israeli people, who have been pure like &#91;MET&#93; virgins, have done a terrible thing!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JOHN 6:68"> <span className="pl pl-1">The snow certainly never &#91;RHQ&#93; completely disappears/melts from the rocky slopes of the mountains in Lebanon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The cold streams that flow down from those distant mountains never &#91;RHQ&#93; cease flowing.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:16; JEREMIAH 2:32; HOSEA 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">But my people are not as reliable as those streams:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They have abandoned me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They burn incense to honor/worship worthless idols.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is as though they have stumbled as they walked along very old roads that are good roads,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and now, instead, they are walking on dirt paths.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 22:7; JEREMIAH 25:9; JEREMIAH 19:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, their land will become desolate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and people who see it will hiss to ridicule it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Everyone who passes by will be appalled;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will shake their heads to show that they are shocked.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 13:24; JEREMIAH 2:27; JOB 27:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will scatter the people when their enemies attack them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like dust is scattered by an east wind.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And when they experience all those difficulties/disasters,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will turn my back on them and refuse to help them.”</span></span>
</div>

## Jeremiah complained to Yahweh again when the people planned to attack him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 52:2; JEREMIAH 11:19; MALACHI 2:7"> Then some of the people said, “Come, let’s plan to attack Jeremiah. We have many priests who teach us God’s laws, and wise men who give us good advice, and prophets who tell us what will happen. We do not need Jeremiah! So, let’s slander him and not pay attention to anything that he says.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9; JEREMIAH 20:12; PSALMS 55:16–17"> Then I prayed, “Yahweh, please listen to me!<br /><span className="pl pl-2">And listen to what my enemies are saying about me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="PSALMS 35:7; PSALMS 57:6; PSALMS 106:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I am doing things that are good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so it is disgusting that &#91;RHQ&#93; they are paying me back by doing evil things to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is as though they have dug a pit for me to fall into and die.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do not forget that one time I stood in front of you and pleaded for you to help them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I tried to prevent you from punishing them &#91;MTY&#93;, even though you were very angry with them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 109:9–20; LAMENTATIONS 5:3; AMOS 4:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">So now, allow their children to die from hunger!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Or cause them to be killed by their enemies’ swords!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cause their wives to become widows, whose children are all dead!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Cause their old men to die in a plague,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and cause their young men to be killed in battles!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:26; PSALMS 140:5; ZEPHANIAH 1:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cause people to scream in their homes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">when enemy soldiers suddenly come into their houses!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cause all those things to happen to them because they want to kill me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is as though they have dug a pit for me to fall into,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they have hidden traps along my path.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PSALMS 35:4; PSALMS 69:22–28; Nehemiah 4:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh, you know all the things that they are planning to do to kill me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not forgive them for their crimes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or blot out the record of their sins.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Cause them to be destroyed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">punish them because of your being angry with them!”</span></span>
