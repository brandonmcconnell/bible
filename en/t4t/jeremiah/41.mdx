## Gedaliah was murdered

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 40:8; JEREMIAH 40:6; PSALMS 41:9"> Ishmael was a member of the king’s family. He had been one of King Zedekiah’s important officials. In October of that year, he went to Mizpah with ten other men to talk with Gedaliah. While they were eating together,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:25; JEREMIAH 40:5; PSALMS 109:5"> Ishmael and the other ten men jumped up, and with their swords they killed Gedaliah—the man whom the king of Babylon had appointed to be their governor!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:25; LAMENTATIONS 1:2; JEREMIAH 41:11–12"> Ishmael and the other men also killed all the Jews and the soldiers from Babylonia who were with Gedaliah at Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 27:11; PSALMS 52:1–2"> The next day, before anyone had found out that Gedaliah had been murdered,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 16:24; DEUTERONOMY 14:1; JOSHUA 18:1"> eighty men from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria cities came to worship at the temple of Yahweh in Mizpah. They had shaved off their beards and torn their clothes and cut themselves to show that they were mourning. And they had brought grain offerings and incense to burn on the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 3:16; JEREMIAH 50:4; 2 SAMUEL 1:2–16"> Ishmael went out of the city to meet them, crying as he went. When he reached them, he said, “Come and see what has happened to Gedaliah!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; PSALMS 55:23; EZEKIEL 22:27"> But as soon as they had all entered the town, Ishmael and his men killed most of them and threw their corpses into a well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOB 2:4; PROVERBS 13:8; PHILIPPIANS 3:7–9"> There were only ten of them whom they ◄spared/did not kill►. They were not killed because they promised to Ishmael that if he allowed them to remain alive, they would bring him lots of wheat and barley and olive oil and honey that they had hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JUDGES 6:2; 1 KINGS 15:17–22; 1 SAMUEL 13:6"> The well where Ishmael’s men had thrown the corpses of the men whom they had murdered was the deep well that King Asa’s men had dug in order that they would have water in the city if the army of King Baasha of Israel would surround the city. Ishmael’s men filled that well with corpses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 40:14; JEREMIAH 40:7; Nehemiah 2:10"> Then Ishmael and his men captured the king’s daughters and some of the other people who had been left in Mizpah by Nebuzaradan in order that Gedaliah would take care of them. Ishmael and his men took those people and started back toward the Ammon area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 40:13–16; JEREMIAH 40:7–8; JEREMIAH 42:1"> But Johanan and all the other leaders of the Israeli soldiers who had not surrendered to the army of Babylonia heard about what Ishmael and his men had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 2:13; GENESIS 14:14–16; 1 SAMUEL 30:1–8"> So they immediately went with all their men to stop them. They caught up with them at the large pool near Gibeon city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When all the people whom Ishmael and his men had captured saw Johanan and the soldiers who were with him, they shouted joyfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So all those who had been captured in Mizpah escaped, and they started to help Johanan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JOB 21:30; PROVERBS 28:17; 1 KINGS 20:20"> But Ishmael and eight of his men escaped and fled to the Ammon area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 43:4–7; JEREMIAH 42:8; JEREMIAH 41:10"> Then Johanan and the men who were with him gathered together all the people whom they had rescued at Gibeon. They included soldiers and women and children and some of the king’s palace officials. They were all people whom Ishmael and his men had captured after they had killed Gedaliah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 19:37–38; JEREMIAH 42:14; JEREMIAH 43:7"> They took them all to Geruth-Kimham village near Bethlehem. And they all prepared to go to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 40:5; LUKE 12:4–5; JEREMIAH 42:16"> They were worried about what the soldiers of Babylonia would do to them when they found out that Ishmael had killed Gedaliah, who had been appointed by the King of Babylonia to be their governor.</span>
