## A message about punishing the evil kings of Judah

<div className="bt">

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 1:32; Isaiah 9:7; JEREMIAH 22:4"> ‘You are the king of Judah. You are the ruler &#91;MTY&#93;, like King David was. You and your officials and your people must listen</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 KINGS 23:34; JEREMIAH 22:18"> He will die in that far-away country and will never see his own country again.”</span>

## A message about King Jehoiakim

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JAMES 5:4; MICAH 3:10; LEVITICUS 19:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">And Yahweh said to me, “Terrible things will happen to King Jehoahaz’s brother, King Jehoiakim.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He unjustly forced men to build his palace.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The rooms on the upper level were built by men who were forced unjustly to do that work;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he forced his neighbors to work for nothing;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he did not pay them anything.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 7:2; Isaiah 5:8–9; Haggai 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He said, ‘I will force my workers to build a huge beautiful palace</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with very large rooms and many windows.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will cover the walls with fragrant cedar panels/timber</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and paint them bright red.’”</span></span>

## God will punish King Jehoiachin

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:1; SONG OF SONGS 8:6; 1 CHRONICLES 3:16"> Yahweh says this: “Jehoiachin, son of King Jehoiakim of Judah, as surely as I am alive, I will punish you. Even if you were the ring on my finger that shows that I am the king, I would pull you off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 34:20–21; JEREMIAH 21:7; 2 KINGS 24:15–16"> You are afraid of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon and his huge army, because they are wanting to kill you. I will enable them to capture you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 KINGS 24:15; JEREMIAH 15:2–4; Isaiah 22:17"> I will expel you and your mother from this land, and you will be taken to another country. Neither of you was born there, but you will both die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:14; 2 KINGS 25:27–30; PSALMS 86:4"> You will never return to this land that you will very much desire to return to.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="PSALMS 31:12; HOSEA 8:8; JEREMIAH 48:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Someone said, “Jehoiachin will be &#91;RHQ&#93; like a broken pot</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that is despised and which no one wants.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He and his children will be ◄exiled/forced to go► to a foreign land.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 36:30; PSALMS 94:20; MATTHEW 1:11–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is what Yahweh says:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“In the record about the kings of Judah, write down that it will be as though this man Jehoiachin had no children,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that he has not been successful during his life,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because none of his children/descendants will ever become king</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to rule over the people of Judah.”</span></span>
