## The people will not admit their sins

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="PROVERBS 24:16; MICAH 7:8; HOSEA 6:1"> Yahweh said to me, “Jeremiah, tell the people that this is what I, Yahweh, am saying to them:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘When people fall down, they get up again, do they not &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">When people are going along a road and find out that they are walking on the wrong road, they go back and find the correct road, do they not &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:6; JEREMIAH 5:3; ZECHARIAH 7:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yes, they do, so why do these people of Judah continue trusting in those idols that have deceived them?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They continue turning away from me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">even though I have warned them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PSALMS 14:2; EZEKIEL 18:28; LUKE 15:17–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have listened carefully &#91;DOU&#93; to what they say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they do not say what they should say.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Not one of them is sorry for having sinned.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">No one says, “I have done &#91;RHQ&#93; wicked things.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are sinning and doing what they want to</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">as fast as &#91;SIM&#93; a horse that is running into a battle.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:3; JEREMIAH 5:4–5; PROVERBS 6:6–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the birds that fly south for the ◄winter/cold season► know the time that they need to fly south,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they all return at the right time the following year.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But my people are not like those birds!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They do not know what I, Yahweh, require them to do.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOHN 9:41; JOB 5:12–13; Isaiah 10:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your men who teach you the laws that Moses wrote have been saying false things about those laws.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">So, why &#91;RHQ&#93; do they continue saying, “We are very wise because we have the laws of Yahweh”?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:15; PSALMS 119:98–100; JOB 5:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those teachers, who think that &#91;IRO&#93; they are wise, will be ashamed/disgraced and dismayed when they are taken to other countries by their enemies</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they sinned by rejecting what I told them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Truly, they were not &#91;RHQ&#93; very wise to do that!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:12–13; EZEKIEL 33:31; Isaiah 56:10–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, I will give their wives to other men;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will give their fields to the enemy soldiers who conquer them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All the people, including those who are important and those who are not important, deceive others in order to obtain their possessions.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Even my prophets and my priests do that.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 6:14; EZEKIEL 13:10–16; LAMENTATIONS 2:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">They act as though the sins of my people are not serious,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;MET&#93; wounds that do not need to be cleaned and bandaged.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They tell the people that everything will go well with them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but that is not true; things will not go well with them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:3; Isaiah 3:9; JEREMIAH 6:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">They should be &#91;RHQ&#93; ashamed when they do disgusting things &#91;RHQ&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they do not even know how to show on their faces that they are ashamed about their sins.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So, they will be killed, and their corpses also will lie among the corpses of others who have been slaughtered by their enemies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will be killed when I punish them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MATTHEW 21:19; JOEL 1:7; JEREMIAH 17:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will allow their enemies to take away the figs and grapes that the people would have harvested from their fields.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their fruit trees will all wither.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will not receive all the blessings that I prepared for them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">This will certainly happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:15; JEREMIAH 9:15; LAMENTATIONS 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the people will say, “◄Why should we wait here in these small towns?/We should not wait here in these small towns.► &#91;RHQ&#93;!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We should go to the cities that have high walls around them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but even if we do that we will be killed there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because Yahweh our God has decided that we must be destroyed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is as though &#91;MET&#93; he has given us a cup of poison to drink,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because we sinned against him.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 14:19; JEREMIAH 4:10; JEREMIAH 8:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">We hoped/desired that things would go well for us,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but things have not gone well.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We hoped that bad things would not happen to us any more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but only things that terrify us are happening to us.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JUDGES 18:29; JUDGES 5:22; JEREMIAH 4:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">People far north in Israel in the city of Dan can already hear the snorting of the horses of their enemies who are preparing to attack us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">It is as though the entire land of Judah is shaking as their army approaches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are coming to destroy our land and everything in it, the people and the cities.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 10:11; PSALMS 58:4–5; DEUTERONOMY 32:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says, “I will send those enemy soldiers to Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be like &#91;MET&#93; poisonous snakes among you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No one will be able to stop them from attacking you &#91;MET&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will attack you like snakes do, and kill you.”</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 39:3; Isaiah 13:5; PSALMS 135:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Throughout our land, the people ask,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Has Yahweh abandoned Jerusalem?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Is he, our city’s king, no longer there?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh replies, “Why do the people cause me to become very angry by worshiping idols and foreign gods?” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span></span>
