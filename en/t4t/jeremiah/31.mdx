## Israel will be restored

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 30:22; JEREMIAH 30:24; ZECHARIAH 13:9"> Yahweh says that at that time, he will be the God who is worshiped by all the clans in Israel, and they will be his people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="NUMBERS 10:33; PSALMS 95:11; MATTHEW 11:28"> This is what Yahweh says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Those people who remained alive and were not killed by their enemies’ swords</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">were blessed by me even in the desert;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I enabled them to have peace.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="PSALMS 103:17; Isaiah 54:8–9; HOSEA 11:4"> Long ago I, Yahweh, said to your ancestors, the Israeli people,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I have loved you and I will continue to love you forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">By faithfully loving you, I have brought you close to myself.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:7; JEREMIAH 31:13; AMOS 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">And now I tell you, my Israeli people whom I love &#91;MET&#93;, that I will cause you to be a nation again.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will joyfully dance as you play your tambourines.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="AMOS 9:14; LEVITICUS 19:23–25; DEUTERONOMY 28:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Again you will plant your vineyards on the hills of Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you will eat the grapes that grow there.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:4–5; MICAH 4:1–3; JEREMIAH 50:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">There will be a time when watchmen will call out from the hills of Samaria &#91;MTY&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Come, let’s go up to Jerusalem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to worship Yahweh, our God!' ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="PSALMS 14:7; PSALMS 28:9; Isaiah 37:31"> And now Yahweh also says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sing joyfully about what I have done for the people of Israel!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shout about your nation, the greatest nation!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Shout joyfully, praising me and saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Yahweh, rescue your people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the ones who are still alive!’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="EZEKIEL 34:16; Isaiah 42:16; JEREMIAH 23:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do that because I will bring them back from the northeast,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from the most distant places on the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Among them will be blind people and lame people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">women who are pregnant and women who are ◄having labor pains/about to give birth to babies►.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They will be a huge group of people!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:4; JEREMIAH 3:4; EXODUS 4:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will be crying as they return,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will be praying to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will guide them along streams of water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">on level paths where they will not stumble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will do this because I am like a father to the Israeli people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it is as though Israel is my oldest son.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; EZEKIEL 34:12; DEUTERONOMY 30:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says, “You people in the nations of the world, listen to this message from me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then proclaim it to people who live along the coasts far away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I scattered my people, but I will gather them again and will take care of them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; a shepherd takes care of his sheep.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; TITUS 2:14; Isaiah 44:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will buy my Israeli people back</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">from those who conquered them because they were more powerful than my people.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Isaiah 58:11; JOEL 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">My people will return to Jerusalem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and shout joyfully on the slopes of Zion Hill.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will rejoice about the things that I have abundantly given to them—</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">grain and new wine and olive oil</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and young sheep and cattle.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They themselves will be like a well-watered garden,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will no longer be sad.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PSALMS 30:11; Isaiah 61:3; Isaiah 51:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The young women will dance joyfully,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the men, young ones and old ones, will join with them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cause them to rejoice instead of mourning;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will comfort them and cause them to be happy instead of being sad.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:25; 1 PETER 2:9; JEREMIAH 33:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The priests will have plenty of things to eat and drink,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all my people will be filled with the good things that I give them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That will certainly happen because I, Yahweh, have said it!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:16–18; JEREMIAH 40:1; PSALMS 77:2"> Yahweh also says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Women were weeping in Ramah town on the border between Israel and Judah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they were mourning and crying very loudly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The women who were the descendants of Ephraim and Manasseh, the two grandsons of Rachel the wife of Jacob, were weeping about their children,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no one could comfort them</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because their children were all dead.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="RUTH 2:12; JEREMIAH 30:3; HEBREWS 6:10"> But now this is what I, Yahweh, say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Do not cry any more,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because I will reward you for the good things you have done for your children.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your children will return from the land where their enemies have taken them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 3:21; JEREMIAH 29:11–16; LAMENTATIONS 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I, Yahweh, am telling you that there are things that you can confidently expect me to do for you in the future.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your children will return to their own land.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:9; PSALMS 80:3; JOB 5:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have heard the people of Israel grieving very much and saying to me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘You punished us severely,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">like &#91;SIM&#93; calves are beaten by their owners to train them for pulling a plow.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So bring us back to obey/worship you again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because we are ready to return to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you alone are Yahweh, our God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:31; JEREMIAH 3:25; EZEKIEL 21:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">We turned away from you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but we repented;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">after you caused us to realize that we were guilty.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We beat our chests to show that we were very ashamed &#91;DOU&#93; of the sins that we committed when we were young.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 55:7; MICAH 7:18–19; Isaiah 63:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I, Yahweh, say this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The Israeli people certainly are &#91;RHQ&#93; still my dear children &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is often necessary for me to threaten to punish them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but I still love them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That is why I have not forgotten them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will certainly act mercifully toward them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 50:5; Isaiah 62:10; PSALMS 84:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">You Israeli people, set up road signs;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">put up posts along the roads</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">to mark the road on which you walked when you were taken from Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My precious/beloved Israeli people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">come back to your towns here.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 49:4; JEREMIAH 7:24; MATTHEW 1:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">You people who have been like &#91;MET&#93; daughters who have forsaken their parents,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">how long will you continue to wander away from me &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I, Yahweh, will cause something to happen on the earth that ◄is new/has not happened before►:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">The women of Israel will be protecting their husbands as they travel back here!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:3; Isaiah 1:26; JEREMIAH 50:7"> This is what the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom we Israeli people worship, says: “When I bring them back from the countries to which they have been exiled, all the people from the towns in Judah will again say, ‘I hope that Yahweh will bless this my home, the holy place where righteous people will live!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:4–8; EZEKIEL 36:10; ZECHARIAH 2:4"> The people of Judah who live in the towns, including the farmers and the shepherds, will all live together peacefully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:6; MATTHEW 11:28; JOHN 4:14"> I will enable weary people to rest, and enable people who are very exhausted to become strong again.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 127:2; ZECHARIAH 4:1–2"> I, Jeremiah, woke up after dreaming all those things, and I looked around. I had slept very delightfully!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="HOSEA 2:23; ZECHARIAH 10:9; EZEKIEL 36:9"> Then Yahweh said to me, “There will be a time when I will greatly increase &#91;MET&#93; the number of people and the number of livestock here in Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 24:6; JEREMIAH 1:10; JEREMIAH 18:7–9"> Previously, I caused their enemies to remove the people from their land and to destroy their land and to cause them to experience many disasters &#91;DOU&#93;. But in the future, I will enable them to build houses and to plant crops here in Israel again. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LAMENTATIONS 5:7; EZEKIEL 18:2–3; JEREMIAH 31:30"> Previously the people often said, ‘The parents have eaten sour grapes, but it is the children’s teeth that ache.’ They meant that it was not fair for them to be punished for their ancestors’ sins. But when I bring them back to their land, they will no longer say that.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="EZEKIEL 18:20; Isaiah 3:11; DEUTERONOMY 24:16"> But now all people will die because of the sins that they themselves have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="HEBREWS 10:16–17; EZEKIEL 37:26; JEREMIAH 31:31–34"> I, Yahweh, say this: ‘There will be a time when I will make a new agreement with the people of Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="EXODUS 24:6–8; DEUTERONOMY 5:3; EXODUS 19:5"> This new agreement will not be like the agreement that I made with their ancestors when I took them by their hands and led them out of Egypt. They broke that agreement, even though I loved them like &#91;MET&#93; husbands love their wives.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 3:3; HEBREWS 10:16; HEBREWS 8:10"> This is what I, Yahweh, say: ‘This is the new agreement that I will make with the people of Israel some day: I will put my laws in their minds and write them on their inner beings. I will be their God, and they will be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; HEBREWS 8:12; 1 JOHN 2:27"> And it will not be necessary for them to teach their neighbors or their relatives and say, “You need to know Yahweh,” because everyone, including unimportant people and very important people, will already know me. And I will forgive them for having been very wicked, and I will never think again about the sins that they have committed.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JEREMIAH 10:16; Isaiah 51:15; PSALMS 136:7–9"> Yahweh is the one who causes the sun to give light during the day,<br /><span className="pl pl-2">and who causes the moon and the stars to give light during the night.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He stirs up the seas, with the result that waves roar.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His name is the Commander of the armies of angels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and this is what he says:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9–10; JEREMIAH 33:20–26; PSALMS 89:36–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will not reject my Israeli people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">any more than I will get rid of the laws that control the universe.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:24–26; Isaiah 40:12; JEREMIAH 33:22"> And this is what I say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘No one can measure the sky,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no one can find out what is supporting the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Similarly, I cannot reject forever the descendants of Jacob</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of all the evil things that they have done.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That is certain, because I, Yahweh, have said it!</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 14:10; 2 KINGS 14:13; JEREMIAH 30:18"> I, Yahweh, also say that there will be a time when everything in Jerusalem will be rebuilt for me, from the tower of Hananel at the northeast corner, west to the gate named the Corner Gate.</span>

<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 15:23; JOEL 3:17; 2 KINGS 11:16"> And the whole area, including the place where corpses and ashes are thrown in the Kidron Valley, and all the fields to the east as far as the Horse Gate, will become set apart for me. And the city of Jerusalem will never again be captured or destroyed.”</span>
