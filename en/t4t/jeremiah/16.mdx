## Jeremiah was warned about the coming disaster

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:2; JEREMIAH 2:1; JEREMIAH 1:4"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 7:26–27; LUKE 23:29; GENESIS 19:14"> “Do not get married and have children in this land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 16:9; JEREMIAH 16:5; JEREMIAH 6:21"> because this is what I, Yahweh, say about the children who are born in this city, and about their mothers and fathers:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:33; PSALMS 83:10; JEREMIAH 9:22"> Many of them will die from terrible diseases. And no one will mourn for them. No one will even bury their corpses; the corpses will lie on the ground, scattered like manure. Others will die in wars or from hunger, and then their corpses will become food for vultures and wild animals.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EZEKIEL 24:16–23; Isaiah 27:11; JEREMIAH 15:1–4"> This is also what Yahweh said to me: “Do not go to funerals to mourn or to show those whose relatives have died that you feel sorry for them, because I have stopped protecting them, and I have caused that things will not go well for them. I have stopped faithfully loving them and acting mercifully toward them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 14:1; JEREMIAH 41:5; JEREMIAH 47:5"> Very many people will die in this land, including those who are important and those who are not important. And no one will mourn for them, or even bury their corpses. No one will cut himself or shave his head to show that he is very sad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HOSEA 9:4; EZEKIEL 24:17; DEUTERONOMY 26:14"> No one will bring food to comfort those who are mourning, not even if it is their father or their mother who has died. No one will give them a cup of wine to cheer them up.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:17; ECCLESIASTES 7:2–4; Isaiah 22:12–14"> And do not go into the houses where people are feasting. Do not eat or drink anything with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:10; JEREMIAH 7:34; EZEKIEL 26:13"> I want you to do this because this is what I, the Commander of the armies of angels, say: ‘While you are still alive and seeing it happen, I will cause there to be no more singing and laughing in this land. There will be no more joyful voices of bridegrooms and brides.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:19; 1 KINGS 9:8–9; DEUTERONOMY 29:24–25"> When you tell these things to the people, they will ask, ‘Why has Yahweh declared that these terrible things will happen to us? What have we done for which we deserve to be punished like this? What sin have we committed against Yahweh our God?’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="PSALMS 106:35–41; DEUTERONOMY 29:25–26; JEREMIAH 22:9"> Then this is what you must tell them that I am replying to them: ‘It is because your ancestors turned away from me, Yahweh. They worshiped other gods and served them. They abandoned me and did not obey my commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 13:10; JEREMIAH 7:26; ECCLESIASTES 9:3"> But you who are living now have done more wicked things than your ancestors did! Each of you stubbornly does the evil things that he desires and refuses to pay attention to what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:36; DEUTERONOMY 4:26–28; JEREMIAH 15:14"> So, I will throw you out of this land, and I will send you to a land that you and your ancestors have never known about. There you will worship other gods day and night. And I will not act mercifully toward you.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:7–8; DEUTERONOMY 15:15; EXODUS 20:2"> But there will be a time when people who are solemnly promising to do something will no longer say, ‘I will do this, just as surely as Yahweh lives—the one who brought the Israeli people here from Egypt.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:18; JEREMIAH 24:6; JEREMIAH 32:37"> Instead, they will say ‘I will do this, just as surely as Yahweh lives—the one who brought us Israeli people back to our own land, from the lands to the north and from all the other lands to which he had ◄exiled us/forced us to go►.’ They will be able to say that because some day I will bring your descendants back to this land that I gave to your ancestors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HABAKKUK 1:14–15; AMOS 4:2; MICAH 7:2"> But now I am summoning your enemies who will seize the people of Judah like &#91;MET&#93; fishermen catch fish. I am summoning those who will search for them on every mountain and hill, and in every cave, like &#91;MET&#93; hunters search for animals to kill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="PROVERBS 15:3; JEREMIAH 23:24; PROVERBS 5:21"> I am watching them carefully. I see every sin that they commit. They will not be able to hide from me &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="NUMBERS 35:33–34; Isaiah 40:2; REVELATION 18:6"> Because of all the wicked things that they have done &#91;DOU&#93;, I will punish them twice as much as I would punish other people. I will do that because they have caused my land to become unacceptable to me because of their worshiping lifeless statues of detestable gods, and also because they have done many other evil things throughout my land.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeremiah prayed again, and Yahweh replied

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 25:4; JEREMIAH 17:17; NAHUM 1:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I prayed, saying, “Yahweh, you are the one who strengthens me and protects me &#91;MET&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you are the one to whom I go when I have troubles.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Some day people from nations all over the world will come to you and say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘Our ancestors left/gave us only something that was false;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they worshiped idols that are completely worthless &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 37:19; PSALMS 115:4–8; GALATIANS 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">No one can &#91;RHQ&#93; make their own gods;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">the gods that they make are only idols; they are not real gods.’ ”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 9:16; PSALMS 83:18; JEREMIAH 33:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then Yahweh said, “Now I will show my power to the people of Judah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will show them that I am truly very powerful &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Then, finally, they will know that I, Yahweh, am the true God.”</span></span>
