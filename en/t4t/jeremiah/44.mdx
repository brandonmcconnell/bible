## Jeremiah predicted disaster would come because of their idolatry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:14; Isaiah 11:11; EXODUS 14:2"> This is the message that Yahweh gave me concerning the Jews who were living in northern Egypt—in Migdol, Tahpenes, and Memphis cities, and in the Pathros region in southern Egypt:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 6:11; JEREMIAH 9:11; JEREMIAH 34:22"> “This is what I, the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom you Israelis say you belong to, say: ‘You saw the disaster that I caused the people in Jerusalem and the other towns in Judah to experience. Those towns are now ruined and deserted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 29:26; DEUTERONOMY 13:6; DEUTERONOMY 32:17"> That happened because I was extremely angry with them on account of their being very wicked. They burned incense to other gods and worshiped them. They were gods that you previously did not know about, and your ancestors also did not know about them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 26:5; JEREMIAH 7:25; JEREMIAH 7:13"> Many times I sent my prophets who served me, to say to them, “Do not do those abominable things that I hate!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:10; JEREMIAH 19:13; 2 CHRONICLES 36:16"> But my people would not pay any attention &#91;DOU&#93; to what I said to them. They would not turn away from their wicked behavior, or stop burning incense to worship/honor other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:20; JEREMIAH 21:5; Isaiah 51:17"> So I poured out my anger &#91;MTY&#93; on them. I punished people on the streets of Jerusalem and on the other towns in Judah. It caused those towns to be ruined and deserted, and they are still like that.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:10; JEREMIAH 51:22; EZEKIEL 33:11"> So now I, the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom you Israelis say you belong to, ask you: ‘Why are you causing yourselves to experience these disasters? Do you not realize that because of what you are doing, soon there will be no more men or women or children or infants left among you who have come here to Egypt from Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:18; JEREMIAH 25:6–7; JEREMIAH 44:3"> Why are you &#91;RHQ&#93; provoking me and causing me to be very angry by burning incense to the idols that you have made here in Egypt? If you continue doing this, you will destroy yourselves, and you will cause yourselves to be people whom all the nations on the earth will curse and despise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:17–18; JOSHUA 22:17–20; JEREMIAH 44:15–19"> Have you forgotten how I punished your ancestors for the wicked things that they did, and how I punished the kings and queens of Judah for what they did, and you and your wives for the sins that you committed in the streets of Jerusalem and the other towns in Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:14; 1 PETER 5:6; REVELATION 15:4"> Up until this day you have not humbled yourselves or revered me. You have not obeyed the laws and decrees &#91;DOU&#93; that I gave to you and your ancestors.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:10; LEVITICUS 26:17; LEVITICUS 20:5–6"> Therefore, this is what I, the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom you Israelis say you belong to, say: ‘I am determined to cause all of you to experience disasters and to get rid of every one of you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:15–18; JEREMIAH 42:22; Isaiah 65:15"> You people from Judah who have survived were determined to come and live here in Egypt. So I will get rid of all of you here in Egypt. Every one of you will die, including those who are important and those who are not important. Some of you will be killed by your enemies’ swords, some will die from famines. You will become people whom others curse, be horrified about, and make fun of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:22; JEREMIAH 43:11; JEREMIAH 44:27–28"> I will punish you here in Egypt like I punished others in Jerusalem, some of whom were killed by their enemies’ swords and some of whom died from famines or diseases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ROMANS 9:27; JEREMIAH 22:26–27; JEREMIAH 42:17"> You people who did not die in Judah fled here to Egypt, hoping that some day you would return to Judah. But very few &#91;HYP&#93; of you will survive and escape. You are longing/wanting to remain alive and return to Judah, but only a very few of you will escape and be able to do that.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PROVERBS 11:21; JEREMIAH 5:1–5; GENESIS 19:4"> Then a large group of the people who had started to live in northern Egypt and southern Egypt, including all the men who knew that their wives had been burning incense to other gods, and all the women who were standing there, said this to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOB 21:14–15; LUKE 19:27; JEREMIAH 16:15–17"> “You are saying that &#91;MTY&#93; Yahweh gave messages to you, but we will not pay any attention to your messages!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:18; Nehemiah 9:34; JEREMIAH 44:21"> We will certainly do everything that we said that we would do. We will burn incense to worship our goddess Astarte, the Queen of Heaven, and we will pour out offerings of wine to her, just like we and our ancestors and our kings and their officials have always done in the streets of Jerusalem and in the other towns in Judah. At that time, we had plenty of food, and we were prosperous and we did not have any troubles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MALACHI 3:13–15; NUMBERS 11:5–6; JOB 21:14–15"> But ever since we stopped burning incense to the Queen of Heaven and giving her offerings of wine, we have had many troubles, and some of our people have been killed by our enemies’ swords and some have died from hunger.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:18; JEREMIAH 44:15; 1 KINGS 21:25"> And the women said, “Furthermore, we burned incense and poured out wine offerings to the Queen of Heaven, and we also made small cakes that resembled her idol, to offer to her. But our husbands certainly knew about and approved of &#91;RHQ&#93; what we were doing!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then I said to all the men and women who had answered me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:17; JEREMIAH 44:9; JEREMIAH 11:13"> “Do not think &#91;RHQ&#93; that Yahweh did not know that you and your ancestors and your kings and their officials and all the other people in Judah were burning incense to worship/honor idols in the streets of Jerusalem and in the other towns in Judah!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:18; Isaiah 7:13; MALACHI 2:17"> It was because Yahweh could no longer endure/tolerate your wicked actions and the detestable things that you were doing that he caused your land to be a place whose name people say when they curse someone, a land that is ruined and which has no one living in it. And your land is still like that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 KINGS 9:9; DANIEL 9:11–12; Nehemiah 13:18"> It is because you burned incense to worship/honor idols and committed other sins against Yahweh that you experienced all those disasters. It is because you have not obeyed him or his laws and decrees and commandments.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 43:7; JEREMIAH 44:15–16; JEREMIAH 42:15"> Then I said to all of them, including the women, “All you people of Judah who are here in Egypt, listen to this message from Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:9; ACTS 23:12–15; Isaiah 28:15"> This is what the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom we Israelis worship, says to you Israeli men: ‘You and your wives have said that you would continue to do what you promised, to burn incense and pour out wine to the goddess whom you call the Queen of Heaven. And you have proved by your actions that you intend to continue to do that. So go ahead and continue doing what you have promised to do for her.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="PSALMS 50:16; EZEKIEL 20:39; HEBREWS 6:13"> But now, all you people from Judah who are now living here in Egypt, listen to this message from Yahweh. He says, ‘I have solemnly declared, using my great name, that soon none of you people from Judah who are here in Egypt will ever again use my name. There will be none of you who, when you solemnly promise to do something, will ever again say, “I will do it as surely as Yahweh lives.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:28; 2 KINGS 21:14; JEREMIAH 1:10"> Because I will be watching over you, not to cause good things to happen to you but to cause things to happen that will harm you. Almost everyone &#91;HYP&#93; from Judah who is now here in Egypt will experience being killed by their enemies’ swords, or dying from famine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:14; Isaiah 27:12–13; Isaiah 10:19"> Only a very few of you will not die, and will return to Judah. When that happens, all those who came to Egypt will find out whose words ◄were true/were fulfilled►, theirs or mine.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="PROVERBS 19:21; MATTHEW 24:15–16; JEREMIAH 44:30"> And Yahweh also says, ‘I will do something that will prove to you that everything that I have said will happen, and that I will punish you here in this place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:4–7; JEREMIAH 43:9–13; JEREMIAH 34:21"> I will cause Hophra, the king of Egypt, to be captured by his enemies who want to kill him, just like I caused King Zedekiah of Judah to be captured by the soldiers of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon.’ ” And that is what happened several years later.</span>
