## God rejected King Zedekiah’s request

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:25; JEREMIAH 37:3; JEREMIAH 52:24"> Yahweh gave me another message when King Zedekiah of Judah sent a man who was also named Pashhur, the son of Malkijah, and a priest named Zephaniah, the son of Maaseiah, to talk to me. They pleaded with me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:3; JEREMIAH 42:4–6; JEREMIAH 37:7"> “The army of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon is attacking Judah. Please speak to Yahweh for us. Ask him if he will help us. Perhaps he will force Nebuchadnezzar’s army to leave by performing a miracle for us, like the miracles he performed previously.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> I replied to them, “Go back to King Zedekiah. Tell him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:5; JEREMIAH 37:8–10; Isaiah 5:5"> ‘This is what Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis worship, says: “I will cause your weapons to be useless in fighting against the king of Babylon and his army that is outside the walls of Jerusalem, attacking. I will enable them to enter into the center of this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 63:10; EXODUS 6:6; Isaiah 5:25"> I myself will fight against your army with my very great power, &#91;MTY, DOU&#93; because I am very angry with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ZEPHANIAH 1:3; EZEKIEL 14:13; JEREMIAH 12:3–4"> I will send a very ◄terrible plague/big sickness► on the people of this city, and on their domestic animals, and many of them will die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 37:17; 2 KINGS 25:5–7; HABAKKUK 1:6–10"> And Yahweh says there are many people in this city who want to kill you. So, he will enable the army of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon and other people in this city to capture you, King Zedekiah, and your officials, and all the other people who do not die from the plague. His army will slaughter your soldiers; they will not act mercifully toward you(pl) or pity you at all. &#91;DOU&#93;’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 30:19; DEUTERONOMY 30:15; DEUTERONOMY 11:26"> And tell this to all the people: ‘Yahweh says that you must decide whether you want to die or to remain alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:2; JEREMIAH 45:5; JEREMIAH 39:18"> Everyone who remains in Jerusalem will die. They will be killed in battles or die from being hungry or from diseases. But those who surrender to the army of Babylon that is surrounding your city will remain alive. They will escape dying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:11; AMOS 9:4; JEREMIAH 44:27"> That will happen because I, Yahweh, have decided to cause this city to experience disasters, not to experience something good. The army of the king of Babylon will capture this city and will destroy it completely by fire.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh will punish Judah’s kings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:20; MICAH 3:1; JEREMIAH 13:18"> Yahweh also told me to say this to the family of the king of Judah: “Listen to this message from Yahweh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 1:17; PROVERBS 31:8–9; JEREMIAH 4:4"> This is what he says to you descendants of King David:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Every day, make fair decisions for the people whom you judge.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Help those who have been robbed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Rescue/Save them from those &#91;SYN&#93; who ◄oppress them/treat them cruelly►.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If you do not do that,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will be angry and punish &#91;MTY&#93; you with a fire that will be impossible to extinguish,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because of all the sins that you have committed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 13:8; LAMENTATIONS 4:12; OBADIAH 1:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will fight against you people of Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you who live on top of a rocky hill above the valley.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will fight against you people who boast, saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“No one can attack us and break through our defenses.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 3:10–11; JEREMIAH 32:19; PROVERBS 1:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will punish you for your wicked deeds like you deserve to be punished;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will be as though I will light a fire in your forests</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that will burn up everything around you!’ ”</span></span>
