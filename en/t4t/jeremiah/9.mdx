<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICAH 7:5–6; PROVERBS 26:24–25; PSALMS 12:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do not trust your neighbors and even your brothers!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They all are as deceitful as Jacob was.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They slander each other and tell lies about each other.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MICAH 6:12; HABAKKUK 2:13; JOB 15:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They deceive their friends</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and never tell the truth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They lie continually and, because of that, they have become skilled liars;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they do one oppressive thing after another, and are unable to stop doing it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HOSEA 4:6; JEREMIAH 13:10; JEREMIAH 8:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They habitually lie and deceive each other,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and no one will admit/say that I am God.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 28:3; PROVERBS 26:24–26; JEREMIAH 9:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">What they say &#91;MTY&#93; injures people like &#91;MET&#93; poisoned arrows do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They say to their neighbors, ‘I hope things will go well for you,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while they are planning to kill them.</span></span>

</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 12:4; HOSEA 4:3; JEREMIAH 12:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, I will weep and wail for the people who live in the mountains and in the ◄pastures/places where the livestock eat the grass►,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because those areas will be desolate, and no one will live there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There will be no cattle there to call to each other,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and all the birds and wild animals will have fled to other places.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 13:22; JEREMIAH 10:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh also says, “I will cause Jerusalem to become a heap of ruins,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and only jackals/wolves will live there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will destroy the towns of Judah, with the result that they will be completely deserted;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">no one will live there.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="HOSEA 14:9; PSALMS 107:43; PSALMS 107:34"> I said, “Only people who are very wise &#91;RHQ&#93; can understand these things.<br /><span className="pl pl-2">Only those who have been taught by Yahweh can &#91;RHQ&#93; explain these things to others.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The wise people are &#91;RHQ&#93; the only ones who can explain why the land will be completely ruined</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that everyone &#91;RHQ&#93; will be afraid to travel through it.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 7:19; JEREMIAH 22:9; Ezra 9:10"> Yahweh replied, “These things will happen because my people have rejected my laws which I gave to them;<br /><span className="pl pl-2">they have not obeyed me or my instructions.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="JEREMIAH 3:17; JEREMIAH 7:24; 1 PETER 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Instead, they have stubbornly done the things that they wanted to do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have worshiped the idols that represent the god Baal,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">which is what their ancestors did.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:14; LAMENTATIONS 3:15; PSALMS 80:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">So now listen to what I, the Commander of the armies of angels, the God of the Israelis, say:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What I will do will be like &#91;MET&#93; giving these people bitter things to eat and poison to drink:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:64; EZEKIEL 5:2; LEVITICUS 26:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will scatter them to many nations which neither they nor their ancestors have known anything about;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will enable their enemies to strike them with swords</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">until I have destroyed them.”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 4:13; EZEKIEL 7:16–18; DEUTERONOMY 28:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to the people of Jerusalem crying/lamenting, saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">‘We have been ruined/destroyed!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">We have experienced a terrible disaster!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now we are very ashamed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because our houses have been destroyed by our enemies, and we are being forced to leave our land.’ ”</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="LUKE 23:27–30; Isaiah 32:9–13; JOB 22:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">You women, listen to what Yahweh says &#91;MTY&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Pay attention to his words &#91;DOU&#93;.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Teach your daughters to wail.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Teach each other how to sing funeral songs,</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:27–29; Isaiah 5:21; ECCLESIASTES 9:11"> Yahweh says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Wise men should not boast about their being wise,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">strong men should not boast about their being strong;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and rich people should not boast about their being rich.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 10:17; GALATIANS 6:14; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Instead, those who want to boast should boast about their knowing me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and about understanding that I am Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that I am kind and just and righteous,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that I faithfully love people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and that I am delighted with people who act that way.</span></span>

<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ROMANS 2:8–9; GALATIANS 5:2–6; ROMANS 2:25–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">There will be a time when I will punish all those people of Egypt and of the Moab people-group and of the Edom people-group and of the Ammon people-group,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all those people who live close to desert areas (OR, who cut their hair short to please their gods) far from Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">all those people who have changed their bodies by circumcising them &#91;MET&#93;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but who have not changed their inner beings.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will punish the people of Judah also,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they have not changed their inner beings, either.”</span></span>
