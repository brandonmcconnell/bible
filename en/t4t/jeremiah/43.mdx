## Jeremiah was taken to Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 26:8; JEREMIAH 51:63; JEREMIAH 42:22"> So I finished telling to the people that message from Yahweh our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:13; Isaiah 7:9; JEREMIAH 40:8"> But then Johanan and Azariah and some other insolent/arrogant men said to me, “You are lying! Yahweh our God has not told us that we should not go to Egypt!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 45:1–3; JEREMIAH 36:4; JEREMIAH 36:26"> We think that Baruch has urged/persuaded you to say this, in order that if we stay here, the soldiers from Babylonia will seize us and kill us or take us to Babylonia.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:5–6; PSALMS 37:3; 2 CHRONICLES 25:16"> So Johanan and the other leaders of the Israeli soldiers and many of &#91;HYP&#93; the other people who were there refused to obey Yahweh’s command to stay in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 40:11–12; JEREMIAH 41:15–16; 1 SAMUEL 26:19"> Johanan and all the other leaders gathered together all the people who had returned from the other countries to which they had been scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:10; JEREMIAH 40:7; JEREMIAH 41:10"> They included men, women, children, the king’s daughters, and all those whom Nebuzaradan had left with Gedaliah, and they also took Baruch and me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:16; JEREMIAH 44:1; Isaiah 30:4"> They refused to obey Yahweh, and they took us all to Egypt, as far as Tahpenes city.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JEREMIAH 2:16; PSALMS 139:7; 2 TIMOTHY 2:9"> While we were at Tahpenes, Yahweh gave me another message. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:2–12; JEREMIAH 19:1–15; JEREMIAH 51:63–64"> “While the people of Judah are watching you, take some large rocks and bury them under the brick pavement at the entrance to the king’s palace there at Tahpenes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 44:28–1; JEREMIAH 1:15; PSALMS 31:20"> Then say to the people of Judah, ‘This is what the Commander of the armies of angels, the God whom you Israelis say you belong to, says: “I will summon Nebuchadnezzar the King of Babylon, who does my work, to come with his army to Egypt. I will set up his throne over these stones that I told Jeremiah to bury. And Nebuchadnezzar will set up his tent there to show that he has become the King of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:2; JEREMIAH 44:13; EZEKIEL 29:19–20"> When his army comes, they will attack Egypt. Then those will die who I have determined must die, those who I have determined must be captured will be captured, and those who I have determined must be killed by swords will be killed by swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 30:13; PSALMS 109:18–19; Isaiah 19:1"> Nebuchadnezzar’s soldiers will burn down the temples of the gods of Egypt. But before they burn the temples, they will take away their idols as souvenirs. His troops will clean Egypt like a shepherd cleans lice from his cloak. They will tear down the sacred pillars that are in the temple of their sun god. And then Nebuchadnezzar’s troops will leave there, without having been harmed.” ’ ”</span>
</div>
