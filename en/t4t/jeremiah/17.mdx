## Keeping the Sabbath day holy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:2; JEREMIAH 26:2; ACTS 5:20"> This is what Yahweh said to me: “Go to the city gates in Jerusalem. First go to the gate where the kings of Judah go in and out of the city, and then go to each of the other gates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 19:3; JEREMIAH 22:2; PSALMS 49:1–2"> Say to the people at each gate, ‘You kings of Judah and everyone else who is living in Jerusalem and all you other people of Judah who enter these gates, listen to this message from Yahweh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:15–21; NUMBERS 15:32–36; JOHN 5:9–12"> He says, “Listen to this warning carefully! Stop doing work on ◄Sabbath/our rest► days! Stop carrying loads through these gates on those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="EZEKIEL 20:12; EXODUS 20:8–10; Isaiah 58:13"> Do not carry loads out of your houses or do any other work on Sabbath days! Instead, cause Sabbath days to be ◄holy/set apart for me►. I commanded your ancestors to do that,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:1; JEREMIAH 11:10; ACTS 7:51"> but they did not listen to me or obey me. When I did things to correct them, they stubbornly &#91;IDM&#93; refused to pay attention to what I said or to accept it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JEREMIAH 17:21–22; DEUTERONOMY 11:13; 2 PETER 1:5–10"> But I say that if you obey me, and if you do not carry loads through these gates on Sabbath days or do any other work on Sabbath days, and if you dedicate the Sabbath days to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:4; Isaiah 9:7; HEBREWS 12:22"> kings of Judah &#91;MTY&#93; and their officials will continue to go in and out of these gates. There will always be someone who is a descendant of &#91;MTY&#93; King David ruling here &#91;MTY&#93; in Jerusalem. Kings and their officials will go in and out of these gates, riding in chariots and on horses, and there will be people living in this city forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 33:13; JEREMIAH 32:44; ZECHARIAH 7:7"> And people will come to Jerusalem bringing offerings to be completely burned on the altar and other offerings. They will bring to the temple grain offerings and incense and offerings to thank me. People will bring those offerings from the towns in Judah and the villages near Jerusalem and from the land where the tribe of Benjamin lives and from the western foothills and from the desert in the south.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 KINGS 25:9; JEREMIAH 7:20; JEREMIAH 21:14"> But if you do not pay attention to what I say, and if you refuse to dedicate the Sabbath days to me, and if you continue to carry loads through these gates into the city on Sabbath days, I will burn these gates completely. The fire will spread to the palaces, and no one will be able to put out that fire.”’”</span>
</div>
