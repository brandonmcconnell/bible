## A message about Philistia

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 25:20; GENESIS 10:19; 1 KINGS 4:24"> Yahweh gave to the prophet Jeremiah a message about the people of Philistia. The message was given to me before Gaza city in Philistia was captured by the army of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 46:20; JEREMIAH 8:16; Isaiah 8:7–8"> This is what Yahweh said:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“An army &#91;MET&#93; will be coming from the northeast</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that will cover the land like a flood.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will destroy the land and everything in it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will destroy people and cities.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">People will scream;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">everyone in the land will wail.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 8:16; JUDGES 5:22; NAHUM 3:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will hear the sound of the hooves of the enemy horses,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they will hear the rumble/noise of the wheels of their enemies’ chariots.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Men will run away;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will not stop to help their children;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will be completely weak and helpless &#91;MTY&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="AMOS 9:7; DEUTERONOMY 2:23; Isaiah 20:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will be the time for all the people of Philistia to be destroyed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the time to prevent the remaining soldiers from helping the people of Tyre and Sidon cities.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I, Yahweh, will get rid of the people of Philistia,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those whose ancestors long ago came from Crete island.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 48:37; JEREMIAH 25:20; JEREMIAH 41:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of Gaza will be humiliated;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will shave off all the hair on their heads to indicate that they are ashamed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The people of Ashkelon city will all be silent because they will be mourning.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All you people who live along the coast of the Mediterranean Sea who are still alive,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">how long &#91;RHQ&#93; will you gash yourselves because you are mourning?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 21:3–5; JEREMIAH 12:12; JOHN 18:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of Philistia say, “Yahweh, when &#91;RHQ&#93; will you tell our enemies to stop killing us with &#91;PRS&#93; their swords?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Tell them to &#91;PRS&#93; put them back into their sheaths and keep them there!”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EZEKIEL 14:17; MICAH 6:9; Isaiah 10:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">But it would not be right &#91;RHQ&#93; for their swords to stay there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because Yahweh has commanded their enemies to do something more;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh intends to tell them to attack all the people living in Ashkelon and in other cities along the coast.</span></span>
</div>
</div>
