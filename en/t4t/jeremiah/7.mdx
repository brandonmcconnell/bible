## Jeremiah spoke at the temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Yahweh gave me another message. He said to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 26:2; REVELATION 3:22; AMOS 7:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Go to the entrance of my temple, and give this message to the people:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You people of Judah who worship here, listen to this message from Yahweh!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 18:11; JEREMIAH 26:13; Isaiah 1:16–19"> The Commander of the armies of angels says to you,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">'If you stop doing evil things and start doing what is right,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will allow you to remain living in your land.'</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MICAH 3:11; MATTHEW 3:9–10; ZEPHANIAH 3:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">But some people are repeatedly saying to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The temple of Yahweh is here,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">so we will be safe;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will not allow us and the temple to be destroyed.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But do not pay attention to what they say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because they are deceiving you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 KINGS 6:12–13; Isaiah 1:19; JEREMIAH 7:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will act mercifully to you only if you change your behavior and stop doing evil things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and if you start to act fairly/justly toward others,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 22:21–24; ZECHARIAH 7:9–12; JEREMIAH 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">and if you stop oppressing foreigners who live in your country, and orphans and widows,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and if you stop murdering people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and if you stop worshiping ◄foreign gods/idols►.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">However, if you continue to do those things, you will be destroyed.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 4:40; JEREMIAH 25:5; JEREMIAH 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">If you do what I have told you, I will allow you to stay in this land that I promised to your ancestors that it would belong to them and their descendants forever.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; EZEKIEL 13:6–16; JEREMIAH 23:14–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">People are repeatedly telling you, ‘The temple is here, so we are safe’,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and you are trusting/believing that what they are saying is true, but it is a lie.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Those people are deceiving you, and what they say is worthless.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:6; EXODUS 20:3; JAMES 4:1–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">You think that &#91;RHQ&#93; you can steal things, murder people, commit adultery, tell lies in court, and worship Baal and all those other gods that you did not know about previously,</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:34; EZEKIEL 23:37; EZEKIEL 23:39"> <span className="pl pl-2">and then come here and stand in front of this temple, which is my temple, and say ‘Nothing bad will happen to us!’,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">while you continue to do all those abominable things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 19:45–46; Isaiah 56:7; MATTHEW 21:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do you realize that you are causing this temple, which is my temple, to become like &#91;MET&#93; a den where bandits hide &#91;RHQ&#93;?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Do you not know that I see all the evil things that you people do there?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="JOSHUA 18:1; JEREMIAH 26:6; 1 SAMUEL 4:10–11"> Long ago I put my Sacred Tent at Shiloh city, to be a place where people would worship me &#91;MTY&#93;.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Think about how I destroyed it because my people, the Israeli people, did many wicked things there.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:12; 2 CHRONICLES 36:15–16; JEREMIAH 7:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">And while you were continually doing those wicked things,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I told you about it many times, but you refused to listen.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I called out to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but you refused to answer me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 78:60; 2 KINGS 25:9; JEREMIAH 7:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, just like I destroyed Shiloh, I will now destroy this temple</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that was built for people to worship me &#91;MTY&#93;,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">this temple that you trust in,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">that is in this place that I gave to you and your ancestors.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 15:1; 2 KINGS 17:23; JEREMIAH 52:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will expel you from this land and send you to other countries far away from me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">just like I did to your relatives, the people of Israel.”</span></span>
</div>

## The warning from Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 11:14; JEREMIAH 14:11–12; JEREMIAH 15:1"> Yahweh said to me, “Jeremiah, do not pray for these people any longer.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not cry for them or plead for me to help them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because I will not pay any attention to you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 8:6–18; EZEKIEL 14:23; JEREMIAH 6:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do you see the wicked things that they are doing in the streets of Jerusalem and in the other towns in Judah?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 19:13; JEREMIAH 44:17–19; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">The children gather firewood</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their fathers use it to make fires on the altars to burn sacrifices.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The women knead/make dough to make cakes to offer to their goddess Astarte who is called the Queen of Heaven.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And on their altars they pour out offerings of wine to their other idols.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">All of those things cause me to become extremely angry!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JEREMIAH 20:11; JEREMIAH 2:19; DANIEL 9:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I am not &#91;RHQ&#93; the one whom they are hurting;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they are really &#91;RHQ&#93; hurting themselves by doing these things for which they should be very ashamed!”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 42:18; LAMENTATIONS 2:3–5; Isaiah 42:25"> So Yahweh the Lord says this:<br /><span className="pl pl-2">“Because I am extremely angry with what happens at this place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will punish these people severely &#91;MTY&#93;;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my being very angry will be like &#91;MET&#93; a fire that will not be extinguished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will destroy the people, their animals, their fruit trees, and their crops.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="HOSEA 8:13; JEREMIAH 6:20; AMOS 5:21–23"> Therefore, this is what the Commander of the armies of angels says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Take away &#91;IRO&#93; your offerings that you bring to burn completely on your altars and your other sacrifices;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">don’t give them to me; eat them yourselves!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="HOSEA 6:6; 1 SAMUEL 15:22; PSALMS 51:16–17"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I led your ancestors out of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">it was not offerings to be completely burned on the altar or other sacrifices that I wanted from them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="EXODUS 15:26; DEUTERONOMY 5:33; EXODUS 19:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">What I told them was, ‘Obey me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">if you do that, I will be your God and you will be my people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If you do the things that I want you to do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">everything will go well for you.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="PSALMS 81:11–12; JEREMIAH 8:5; JEREMIAH 7:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">But your ancestors would not pay any attention &#91;DOU&#93; to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They continued to do the evil things that they wanted to do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">everything that in their stubborn inner beings they desired to do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Instead of coming closer to me, they went further away from me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 36:15; JEREMIAH 25:4; Nehemiah 9:26"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the day that your ancestors left Egypt until now,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I have continued to send my prophets to you.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 16:12; 2 CHRONICLES 30:8; JEREMIAH 7:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, my people, have not listened to me or paid attention to what I said;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you have been stubborn, and you have done more sinful things than your ancestors did.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 26:2; JEREMIAH 1:7; Isaiah 65:12"> Then Yahweh said to me, “When you tell all this to my people,<br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will not listen to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When you call to them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will not answer.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:14–15; PROVERBS 1:7; Isaiah 1:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Say to them, ‘You people of Judah have not obeyed Yahweh, your God;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you have not accepted it when he tried to correct you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No one among you is truthful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you do not say anything that is true; you speak only lies.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JOB 1:20; MICAH 1:16; JEREMIAH 6:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, tell them to cut off their hair to show that they are mourning;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">tell them to go up into the hills and sing a sad funeral song,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because I have completely rejected &#91;DOU&#93; this generation of people who have made me angry.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Valley of Slaughter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:34; EZEKIEL 7:20; DANIEL 9:27"> Yahweh says this: “The people of Judah have done many things that I say are evil.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have set up their disgusting idols in my temple,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">causing it to become an unacceptable place to worship me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 KINGS 23:10; JEREMIAH 32:35; LEVITICUS 18:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">They have built altars at Topheth in Ben-Hinnom Valley outside Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and they sacrifice their sons and daughters on those altars.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I never commanded them to do that;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I never even thought that anyone would do that.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="JEREMIAH 19:11; JEREMIAH 19:6; 2 KINGS 23:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">So they should beware!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">There will be a time when that place will no longer be called Topheth or the Hinnom Valley;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">instead, it will be called the Valley of Slaughter.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There will be a huge number of people who will be buried there,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">with the result that there will be no space to bury more bodies.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="JEREMIAH 12:9; DEUTERONOMY 28:26; REVELATION 19:17–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The corpses of my people that are not buried and are left on the ground will be eaten by vultures and wild animals,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and there will be no one to shoo/chase them away.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="REVELATION 18:23; HOSEA 2:11; EZEKIEL 26:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">There will be no one singing and laughing any more in the streets of Jerusalem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">there will be no more joyful voices of bridegrooms and brides in Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because the land will be completely destroyed.”</span></span>
</div>
