## Jeremiah warned King Zedekiah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:15; DANIEL 2:37–38; 2 KINGS 25:1–9"> King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon came with the armies of all the kingdoms that he ruled, and they fought against Jerusalem and the other towns in Judah. At that time, Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JEREMIAH 21:10; JEREMIAH 34:22; JEREMIAH 37:8–10"> “Go to Zedekiah the King of Judah, and say to him, ‘This is what Yahweh, the God whom we Israelis worship, says: “I am about to enable the army of the King of Babylon to capture this city, and they will burn it down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:4; JEREMIAH 21:7; 2 KINGS 25:4–7"> You will not escape from them; they will capture you and take you to the king of Babylon. And then they will take you to Babylon.” ’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But King Zedekiah, listen to this that Yahweh has promised: ‘You will not be killed in a battle &#91;MTY&#93;;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 22:18; 2 CHRONICLES 16:14; 2 KINGS 22:20"> you will die peacefully. When you die, people will burn incense to honor/remember you just as they did for your ancestors who were kings before you became king. They will mourn for you, crying, “We are very sad that our king is dead!” I, Yahweh, promise that will happen.’ ”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 15:16–24; 1 SAMUEL 3:18; 1 KINGS 21:19"> So I took that message to King Zedekiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="JOSHUA 10:3; JEREMIAH 4:5; 2 KINGS 18:13–14"> At that time the army of Babylonia had surrounded Jerusalem and Lachish and Azekah. Those three cities were the only cities in Judah that had high walls around them that still had not been captured.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeremiah warned them they would be punished for not freeing their slaves

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LEVITICUS 25:10; 2 KINGS 23:2–3; 2 KINGS 11:17"> King Zedekiah had decreed that the people must free their slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:12; LEVITICUS 25:39–46; EXODUS 3:18"> He decreed that the people must free their Hebrew slaves, both the men slaves and the women slaves. No one would be allowed to force a fellow Jew to continue to be his slave.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 26:16; JEREMIAH 26:10; JEREMIAH 38:4"> The officials and the rest of the people had obeyed what the king decreed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 PETER 2:20–22; PROVERBS 26:11; MATTHEW 12:43–45"> but later they changed their minds. They forced the men and women whom they had freed to become their slaves again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> So Yahweh gave me this message to tell to them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JUDGES 6:8; JOSHUA 24:17; DEUTERONOMY 5:27"> “I, Yahweh, the God whom you Israelis say you belong to, ◄made an agreement with/gave this command to► your ancestors long ago, when I rescued them from being slaves in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 15:12; 1 KINGS 9:22; 2 KINGS 17:13–14"> I told them that they must free all their Hebrew slaves after the slaves had worked for them for six years. But your ancestors did not pay any attention to what I said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 34:8; 2 KINGS 23:3; Nehemiah 10:29"> Recently, you obeyed my command and stopped doing what was wrong and did what was right. You made a solemn agreement at my temple that you would free your slaves, and then you freed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:12; EXODUS 20:7; JEREMIAH 34:11"> But now you have disregarded what you solemnly promised, and you have shown contempt for what I &#91;MTY&#93; said by taking back the women and men whom you had freed and said they could live wherever they wanted to. Now you have forced them to be your slaves again.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:2; DEUTERONOMY 28:25; GALATIANS 6:7"> Therefore, this is what I, Yahweh, say: ‘Because you have not obeyed me by freeing your fellow Israelis, I will free you to be destroyed by the swords of your enemies and by famines and diseases. All the nations of the earth will be horrified because of what happens to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="GENESIS 15:10; GENESIS 15:17–18; HOSEA 6:7"> Because you have disregarded what I said in my agreement with you, I will do to you just like you did to the calves that you cut in half to show that you would surely do what you solemnly promised that you would do. I will enable your enemies to cut you into pieces, you officials of Judah and you officials of Jerusalem, and you officials in the palace, and you priests and all you common people. I will do that because you have disregarded what you solemnly promised about freeing your slaves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:33; JEREMIAH 16:4; JEREMIAH 19:7"> I will enable your enemies to capture you, and they will kill you. And your bodies will be food for vultures and wild animals.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JEREMIAH 52:24–27; JEREMIAH 39:6; JEREMIAH 52:10"> I will enable the army of the king of Babylon to capture King Zedekiah and his officials. Although the king of Babylon and his army have left Jerusalem for a short time,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JEREMIAH 9:11; JEREMIAH 39:8; JEREMIAH 39:1–2"> I will summon them back again. This time, they will fight against this city and capture it and burn it down. I will make sure that all the towns in Judah are destroyed, with the result that no one will live there any more.’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
