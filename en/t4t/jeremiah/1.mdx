<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 39:2; 2 CHRONICLES 36:5–8; 2 KINGS 23:34"> Yahweh continued to give me more messages when Josiah’s son Jehoiakim was the king, and he continued to do that until Zedekiah had been the king of Judah for almost eleven years. ## Yahweh chose Jeremiah to be his prophet

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="EZEKIEL 3:16; EZEKIEL 1:3; JEREMIAH 1:2"> One day Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PSALMS 139:16; Isaiah 49:1; GALATIANS 1:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I knew you before I finished forming you in your mother’s womb.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Before you were born, I ◄set you apart/chose you►</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I appointed you to be my prophet</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">whose messages would be for all nations.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EXODUS 6:12; EXODUS 4:10–16; 1 KINGS 3:7–9"> I replied, “O, Yahweh my God, I am not qualified to speak for you, because I am very young!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 16:15–16; EZEKIEL 3:27; NUMBERS 22:20"> Yahweh replied, “Do not say that, because you must go to everyone to whom I will send you, and you must tell them everything that I tell you to say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:5; JOSHUA 1:9; Isaiah 51:7"> And you must not be afraid of the people to whom you will speak, because I will protect you from being harmed by them. This will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="LUKE 21:15; EXODUS 4:11–12; LUKE 12:12"> Then it was as though Yahweh touched my mouth and said, “Listen to me! I am ◄putting my message into your mouth/telling you what you must speak►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:28; 2 CORINTHIANS 10:4–5; Isaiah 44:26–28"> Today I am appointing you to warn nations and kingdoms. You will tell them that I will completely destroy and get rid of &#91;DOU&#93; some of them and that I will establish &#91;MET&#93; others and cause them to be strong.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="AMOS 7:8; AMOS 8:2; ZECHARIAH 5:2"> Then Yahweh said to me, “Jeremiah, what do you see?” I replied, “I see a branch from an almond tree.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 12:28; EZEKIEL 12:25; EZEKIEL 12:22–23"> Yahweh said, “That is correct. And because the word for ‘almond’ resembles the word for ‘watching’, it means that I am watching what will happen, and I will make certain that what I have said to you about destroying nations will happen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="EZEKIEL 11:3; EZEKIEL 11:7; ZECHARIAH 4:2"> Then Yahweh spoke to me again and said, “What do you see now?” I replied, “I see a pot full of boiling water &#91;MTY&#93;. It is tipping toward me from the north.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 41:25; JEREMIAH 4:6; JEREMIAH 10:22"> Yahweh replied, “Yes! It means that from the north great trouble/destruction will spread &#91;MET&#93; over this land, like boiling water &#91;MET&#93; pouring from a pot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="JEREMIAH 43:10; JEREMIAH 25:9; JEREMIAH 9:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to what I say:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I am summoning the armies of kingdoms that will come from the north of Judah to Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their kings will set up their thrones at the gates of this city to indicate that they are now the kings of Judah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Their armies will attack and break down the walls of this city, and they will do the same thing to all the other towns in Judah.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 7:9; JEREMIAH 19:4; ACTS 7:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will punish &#91;MTY&#93; my people because of all the evil things that they have done;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they have abandoned me and they worship gods/idols.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">They worship idols that they have made with their own hands!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JEREMIAH 1:7–8; 1 KINGS 18:46; 1 PETER 1:13"> So, get up and put on your clothes to get ready for action! Then go to the people of Judah and tell them everything that I tell you to say. Do not be afraid of them, because if you are afraid of them, I will cause you to be truly terrified in front of them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7; EZEKIEL 3:8–9; JEREMIAH 15:20"> But listen! I will cause you to be strong, like &#91;MET&#93; a city that has strong walls around it. You will be as strong as &#91;MET&#93; an iron pillar or a bronze wall. None of the kings or officials or priests will be able to defeat you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="JOSHUA 1:9; JEREMIAH 1:8; JEREMIAH 20:11"> They will oppose you, but they will not be able to defeat you, because I will be with you and will protect you. That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it!”</span>
</div>
</div>
