## The promise that God will enable the Israeli people to prosper again

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JEREMIAH 38:28; JEREMIAH 37:21; JEREMIAH 32:2–3"> While I was still being guarded in the courtyard of the palace, Yahweh gave me this second message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="EXODUS 6:3; EXODUS 15:3; Isaiah 37:26"> “This is what I, the one who made the earth, who formed/shaped it and put it in its place, say to the people of Jerusalem: ‘My name is Yahweh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="JEREMIAH 29:12; Isaiah 65:24; EPHESIANS 3:20"> Call out to me, and then I will tell you great and wonderful things that you have not known before.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:24; HABAKKUK 1:10; EZEKIEL 4:2"> This is what I, Yahweh, the God whom you Israeli people say you belong to, say: ‘The men in this city have torn down some of their houses, and even parts of the king’s palace, to get materials to strengthen the walls around the city, in order that the soldiers of Babylonia will not be able to break through the walls after they climb up the ramps that they have built against the walls, and kill the inhabitants with their swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17; JEREMIAH 21:10; JEREMIAH 21:4–7"> You are expecting to fight against the army from Babylonia, but what will happen is that the houses of this city will be filled with the corpses of the men of this city whom I will allow to be killed because I am extremely angry &#91;DOU&#93; with them. I have abandoned them because of all the wicked things that they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="JOHN 10:10; Isaiah 30:26; TITUS 3:5–6"> However, there will be a time when I will cause the people in this city to be healthy and strong again. I will enable them to be prosperous and have peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:26; JEREMIAH 30:3; AMOS 9:14–15"> I will bring the people of Judah and Israel back from the lands to which they were exiled. I will enable them to rebuild their towns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 13:1; MICAH 7:18–19; PSALMS 51:2"> I will get rid of their guilt for all the sins that they have committed against me, and I will forgive them for their sin of rebelling against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 126:2–3; Isaiah 60:5; ZEPHANIAH 3:17–20"> When that happens, all the nations of the world will rejoice, and they will praise me and honor me. They will hear about all the good things that I have done for this city and, because of that, they will revere me, and they will tremble because I have caused the people in this city to have peace and to prosper.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JEREMIAH 32:43; JEREMIAH 32:36; EZEKIEL 37:11"> And this is also what I, Yahweh, say: ‘You people have said that this is a land where there are no longer any people or animals. But in the streets of Jerusalem that are now completely empty &#91;DOU&#93;, and in the other towns in Judah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 16:34; PSALMS 106:1; PSALMS 107:22"> some day people will again be happy and laugh. Brides and bridegrooms will again sing joyfully. And many other people will also sing joyfully as they bring their offerings to me to thank me for what I have done for them. They will sing this song:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“We thank you, the Commander of the armies of angels,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because you are good to us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">You faithfully love us forever.”</span>They will sing that because I will cause the people of this land to be as prosperous as they were before.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 65:10; EZEKIEL 34:12–15; JEREMIAH 36:29"> This land is now desolate. There are no people or animals living here. But I, the Commander of the armies of angels, say this: ‘In this land there will again be pastures/fields where shepherds will lead their sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="LEVITICUS 27:32; LUKE 15:4; JEREMIAH 17:26"> Shepherds will again count their sheep as the sheep walk by, outside the towns in the hilly area, in the western foothills, in the southern desert area, in the land where the descendants of Benjamin live, around Jerusalem, and outside all the other towns in Judah.’ That will surely happen because I, Yahweh, have said it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–9; JEREMIAH 23:5; 1 CHRONICLES 17:13–14"> Listen to this! I, Yahweh, say that there will be a time when I will do for the people of Israel and Judah all the good things that I promised to do for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1–5; JEREMIAH 23:5–6; Isaiah 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">At that time &#91;DOU&#93; I will appoint a righteous man who will be a descendant &#91;MET&#93; of King David.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Throughout the land, he will do what is just and right.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:6; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:30; Isaiah 45:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">At that time, the people of Judah will be rescued from their enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the people of Jerusalem will be safe.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And people will say that the name of the city is ‘Yahweh is the one who vindicates/defends us’.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 KINGS 2:4; PSALMS 89:29–37; LUKE 1:32–33"> And this is also what I, Yahweh, say: ‘There will be descendants of King David ruling &#91;MTY&#93; Israel forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; REVELATION 1:6; DEUTERONOMY 18:1"> And there will always be priests who are descendants of Levi who stand in front of me and offer sacrifices that will be completely burned on the altar and who will burn grain offerings and other sacrifices.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Then Yahweh gave me this message:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GENESIS 8:22; PSALMS 89:37; Isaiah 54:9–10"> “This is what I, Yahweh, say: ‘You certainly cannot annul my promise/agreement to cause nighttime to follow daytime each day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="PSALMS 89:34; 2 CHRONICLES 7:18; 2 SAMUEL 23:5"> Similarly, you cannot annul the promise/agreement that I made with King David, who served me well, that there will always be descendants of his who will rule Judah. The same is true for my agreement with the descendants of Levi who are priests who do work for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="GENESIS 15:5; GENESIS 22:17; HEBREWS 11:12"> No one can count the stars in the sky, and no one can count the grains of sand at the seashore. Similarly, I will cause there to be a huge number of priests who are descendants of David and descendants of Levi who will work for me.’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Yahweh gave another message to me. He said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2–4; PSALMS 83:4; PSALMS 44:13–14"> “Have you noticed/heard that some people are saying, ‘Yahweh chose two groups, the people of Judah and the people of Israel, and later abandoned them.’ They are despising my people and saying that Israel no longer deserves to be considered a nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 74:16–17; JEREMIAH 31:35–36; PSALMS 104:19"> But this is what I say: ‘I will not change my laws that control the day and the night, the sky and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="JEREMIAH 31:37; Isaiah 14:1; HOSEA 2:23"> Similarly, I will never abandon the descendants of David or the other descendants of Jacob, and I will always allow descendants of David to rule the descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. I will bring my people back to their land, and I will act mercifully toward them.’”</span>
</div>
