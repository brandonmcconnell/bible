## The new temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Haggai 2:20; Haggai 2:10"> On October 17 of that year, Yahweh gave me, the prophet Haggai, another message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Nehemiah 8:9; Ezra 2:63"> The message was that I should say this to Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and to the other people who were still alive in Jerusalem:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 3:12; Haggai 2:9; EZEKIEL 7:20"> “Do any of you remember how glorious our former temple was? If you do, what does it look like to you now? It must seem like nothing at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CHRONICLES 28:20; JOSHUA 1:9; ZECHARIAH 8:9"> But now the Commander of the armies of angels says to all of you, to Zerubbabel and Jeshua and the rest of you people who live in this nation, ‘Do not be discouraged; be strong &#91;DOU&#93;, and work to build this new temple, because I will ◄be with/help► you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="EXODUS 34:10; EXODUS 29:45–46; Isaiah 41:13"> My Spirit remains among you, like I promised your ancestors when they left Egypt. So do not be afraid!’ ”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="HEBREWS 12:26–28; Isaiah 10:25; MARK 13:24–26"> This is what the Commander of the armies of angels says: “Soon I will shake the sky and the earth, the oceans and the land again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MALACHI 3:1; 1 KINGS 8:11; JOHN 7:37–39"> I will shake again the people of all the nations, and as a result they will bring their treasures to this temple. I will fill this temple with my glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PSALMS 50:10–12; PSALMS 24:1; Isaiah 60:17"> The silver and the gold that they own are really mine, so they will bring them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="PSALMS 85:8–9; 2 CORINTHIANS 3:9–10; COLOSSIANS 1:19–21"> Then this temple will be more glorious than the former/previous temple was. And I will cause things to go well for you all. That will surely happen because I, the Commander of the armies of angels, have said it.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## What they do now will be acceptable to Yahweh

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 2:20; Haggai 1:1; Haggai 1:15–1"> On December 18 of that year, Yahweh gave another message to me, the prophet Haggai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LEVITICUS 10:10–11; MALACHI 2:7; DEUTERONOMY 33:10"> This is what the Commander of the armies of angels said to me: “Ask the priests this question about what is written in the laws of Moses about sacrifices:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LEVITICUS 6:27; MATTHEW 23:19; LEVITICUS 7:6"> ‘If one of you priests takes from the altar some meat that was sacrificed and is carrying it in his robes, if his robe touches some bread or stew or wine or olive oil or some other food, does that food also become ◄holy/acceptable for a sacrifice►?’” When he said that to the priests, they replied, “No.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="NUMBERS 5:2–3; NUMBERS 19:11–22; LEVITICUS 22:4–6"> Then Haggai asked them, “If someone becomes unacceptable to God by touching a corpse, and then he touches any of those foods, will the food also become unacceptable to God?” The priests replied, “Yes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:9; TITUS 1:15; Isaiah 1:11–15"> Then Haggai replied, “Yahweh says this: ‘It is the same with you people and with this nation. Everything that you do and all the sacrifices that you all offer are unacceptable to me because of the sins that you have committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:5; Ezra 3:10; Ezra 4:24"> Think about what has been happening to you before you began to lay the foundation of my temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; MALACHI 2:2; PROVERBS 3:9–10"> When you expected to harvest 20 bushels of grain, you harvested only ten bushels. When someone went to a big wine vat to get 50 gallons of wine, there were only 20 gallons in the vat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 28:22; Haggai 1:11; Haggai 1:9"> I sent ◄blight/hot winds► and mildew and hail to destroy all your crops. But still you did not return to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 8:9; Haggai 2:15; Ezra 5:1–2"> Starting from this day, December 18, the day when you have laid the foundation of my new temple, continue to think carefully about your situation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MALACHI 3:10; HABAKKUK 3:17–18; PROVERBS 3:9–10"> Is there now &#91;RHQ&#93; any grain seed left in your barns? No, because you have eaten the small amount that you harvested. And there is no fruit on your grapevines and fig trees and pomegranate trees and olive trees. But, from now on, I will bless you!’ ”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Yahweh’s promise to Zerubbabel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> On that same day, Yahweh gave another message to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ZECHARIAH 4:6–10; Haggai 2:6–7; Haggai 1:1"> He said, “Tell Zerubbabel the governor of Judah that I am going to shake the sky and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MICAH 5:10; JUDGES 7:22; ZEPHANIAH 3:8"> I will end the power of the kings of many nations &#91;DOU&#93;. I will cause their chariots and their drivers, their horses and the soldiers who are riding on them to be destroyed. What will happen is that I will cause the soldiers to kill each other with their swords.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; Isaiah 42:1; SONG OF SONGS 8:6"> Zerubbabel, you serve me well. So I, the Commander of the armies of angels, declare that like kings wear signet rings to show that they have authority to rule people, I will appoint you and cause you to have authority to rule. I will do that because I have chosen you. That will surely happen because I, the Commander of the armies of angels, have said it.”</span>
</div>
</div>
