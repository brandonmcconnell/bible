<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EPHESIANS 3:4; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:17; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:2"> I admit that I am not an eloquent speaker, but I do know God’s message. I have made that clear to you by everything that I have done among you and by everything that I have taught you.</span>

## You are mistaken if you think that I do not love you because I refused to accept money from you for my teaching. I will go on doing that to prevent those false apostles among you from boasting that they work just as I do.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 12:13; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:9; 2 CORINTHIANS 10:1"> I refused to accept money from you as pay for teaching you God’s message about Christ. ◄It is ridiculous for you to think that what I did was wrong!/Was that wrong?► &#91;RHQ&#93; I know that by teaching you without pay, I made myself seem &#91;IRO&#93; unimportant to you. But I did it to honor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:18; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:9; PHILIPPIANS 4:14–16"> Some people might say that it was as though &#91;IRO&#93; I was robbing other congregations because I accepted money from them in order that I might work among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 3:8–9; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:9; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:6"> Furthermore, when I was there with you and had no money to buy the things that I needed, I did not cause any of you ◄to have difficulties by my depending on you/by asking you for money/support►, because our (inc) fellow believers who came from here in Macedonia province brought enough money for me to buy everything that I needed. So I have not caused you any difficulty at all by asking you for money, and I never will ask you for money &#91;EUP&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ROMANS 9:1; ROMANS 1:9; ACTS 18:12"> And as sure as you know that everything that Christ says is true, you can be sure that what I say to you now is true. Wherever I go there in Achaia province, I do not let you believers pay me for doing God’s work among you. And no one will be able to prevent me from boasting about that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 12:15; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:11–12; 2 CORINTHIANS 7:3"> No one should wrongly &#91;RHQ&#93; think that it is because I do not love you that I do not take pay from you. God knows that you truly/really are very dear to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 9:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 5:6; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:12"> There are some men there among you who would like to be able to say boastfully that they work among you just as I do. So, in order to make it impossible &#91;IDM&#93; for them to say that, I have refused to accept pay from you, and I will continue doing that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:7; REVELATION 2:2; 1 JOHN 4:1"> Those men are lying when they say that they are apostles. They work deceitfully. Even though they say that they work for God, they are working only for themselves. They try to make people think that Christ sent them as his apostles, but they do not truly represent him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:8; 2 CORINTHIANS 2:11; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:3"> I am not surprised that they act like that, because Satan himself causes people to think that he has come as a shining messenger from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:19; REVELATION 9:11; Isaiah 9:14–15"> So it should not surprise you if these men, who really serve Satan, do good things in order to make you think they serve God. Some day God will punish them according to what they deserve because of the evil things that they do.</span>

## You seem to listen to those who boast, so I too will boast. I will boast about being a Jew (as those others do), and I will boast about having suffered greatly for Christ, and about an extraordinary vision that God gave me. Although God caused me to suffer an affliction, he promised that he would help me and strengthen me.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 11:1; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:6; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:11"> I say again: I do not want any of you to think that my boasting about myself is like foolish people boast. But even if anyone thinks that I am boasting about myself like a foolish person boasts, listen to what I have to say anyway, just like you listen to those false apostles. They really speak foolishly! Listen to me while I also boast a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 7:12; PHILIPPIANS 3:3–6; 2 CORINTHIANS 9:4"> About the things that I am going to tell you, boasting confidently about myself, surely you know that they are not the kind of things that the Lord Jesus would say. Instead, I will speak as a foolish person would speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 4:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:21–23; 2 CORINTHIANS 10:12–18"> Because many among you are boasting about their own work, as unbelievers do, I will also boast about myself and my work &#91;MTY, IRO&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 4:10; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:15; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:1"> I am sure that you, who think that you are so wise, will accept what I say because you gladly accept the false teachers and the foolish things that they say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:4; GALATIANS 4:3; GALATIANS 5:1"> When people treat you as if they were your bosses, you gladly submit to them. You think that it is all right when others force you to provide for their needs. You think that it is all right when people take control over you, when they boast about themselves, or when they insult you by slapping you in the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 10:10; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:17; 2 CORINTHIANS 10:1–2"> I agree that I was not bold enough to treat you in such ways. Ha! Do you think that &#91;IRO&#93; I should feel ashamed because I did not do that? So now, talking like a person who does not have any sense, I will boldly tell you some things about myself, just like those false apostles tell you things about themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 3:5; ROMANS 11:1; ROMANS 9:4"> Do they tell you that they are Hebrews and can speak the Hebrew language? Well, I can say the same thing about myself. Do they tell you that they belong to God’s people, the nation of Israel? Well, I can say the same thing about myself. Do they tell you that they are descendants of our great ancestor Abraham? Well, he is my ancestor, too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 6:4–5; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:9–10; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:9"> Do they tell you how much work they have done for Christ? Now I am talking like a crazy person talks &#91;EUP&#93;. Well, I work for Christ much more than they do. I have been in prison many more times than they have. Many times I have been beaten &#123;people have beaten me&#125; very severely because I believe in and obey Christ. Many times I almost died because of serving Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="MARK 13:9; DEUTERONOMY 25:2–3; MATTHEW 10:17"> On five occasions Jewish religious leaders beat me 39 times with a whip.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ACTS 14:19; ACTS 16:37; HEBREWS 11:37"> On three occasions I was beaten &#123;Roman officials beat me&#125; with wooden sticks. On one occasion a large crowd of people threw stones at me to kill me. On three occasions the ship that I was traveling on was wrecked and sank. On one of those occasions I was floating in the water a night and a day before I was rescued.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ACTS 14:5; ACTS 17:5; ACTS 13:50"> As I have often traveled to other places, I have often risked losing my life. For example, sometimes I have been in danger crossing rivers. I have been in danger traveling in places where there were bandits. At times I have been in danger among people of my own nation, the Jews, and at other times I been in danger among those who are not Jews. I have been in danger in cities, and I have been in dangerous situations in barren areas. I have been in dangerous situations when I was traveling in ships. I have been in danger among people who falsely claim to be fellow believers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:12; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:5; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:9"> I have worked very hard &#91;DOU&#93; to earn ◄a living/what I need►, and often I did not sleep. At times I was hungry and thirsty, and at times I did not have enough time to eat. Sometimes I was cold because I did not have enough clothes to wear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 7:17; ROMANS 15:16; ACTS 20:18–35"> In addition to all that, I am constantly and deeply concerned about all the congregations that I have helped to begin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 8:13; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:22; GALATIANS 4:8–20"> Every time that I hear about fellow believers &#91;RHQ&#93; who are not strong spiritually, I sympathize with them and try to help them. Every time that I learn about a fellow believer &#91;RHQ&#93; who causes another believer to do something wrong, that makes me very angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 12:5–11; PROVERBS 25:27; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:3"> Because it seems that it is necessary for me to continue boasting about myself, I will boast about things that caused some people to think of me as being weak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ROMANS 9:5; EPHESIANS 1:3; JOHN 10:30"> God, who is the Father of our (inc) Lord Jesus, and who is the one whom we should praise forever, knows that I am not lying about this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ACTS 9:24–25; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:26"> One time when I was in Damascus city, the governor whom King Aretas had appointed to rule part of his area, told his soldiers to guard the gates of the city so that they could seize me if I tried to leave the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="ACTS 9:25; 1 SAMUEL 19:12; JOSHUA 2:18"> But my friends helped me to escape by putting me in a large basket and lowering me to the ground by a rope through an opening in the city wall. That was really humiliating!</span>
