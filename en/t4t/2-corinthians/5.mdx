## God will give all of us new heavenly bodies, and he will reward us according to what we did while we were living in our earthly bodies.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="JOB 19:25–26; 1 JOHN 3:2; 2 PETER 1:13–14"> We know that these bodies we live in here in this world are like &#91;MET&#93; tents. They are like temporary living/dwelling places. So we should not be concerned about what happens to our bodies. We know that if we are killed &#123;if someone kills us&#125;, God will give us permanent living places. Those permanent living places &#91;MET&#93; will not be houses that people have made. They will be new bodies in which we will live forever in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 8:23; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:53–54; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:3–4"> While we are here on earth, we suffer. We often groan because we desire to go to heaven where God will give us our new bodies &#91;MET&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GENESIS 3:7–11; REVELATION 16:15; REVELATION 3:18"> When God gives us(inc) our new bodies &#91;MET&#93;, our spirits will have &#91;LIT, MET&#93; bodies to live in that will last forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:53–54; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:2; Isaiah 25:8"> It is true that while we are still living in these bodies &#91;MET&#93; that do not last forever, we often groan &#91;DOU&#93; because we desire to be free from them. We are not longing to be without a body. Instead, we groan because we desire to receive our new bodies &#91;MET&#93; in heaven. We long for this to happen so that these bodies that are going to die some day will suddenly be changed into bodies that will live forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 1:22; EPHESIANS 1:13–14; ROMANS 8:23"> It is God himself who has prepared us to receive these new bodies. He is also the one who has guaranteed/assured us (inc) that this will happen. He has guaranteed it by sending his Spirit to live inside us now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:1; PHILIPPIANS 3:20–21; HEBREWS 11:13"> So, because God’s Spirit lives in us, we are always confident that God will give us new bodies. We know that as long as we have bodies here on earth, we are not yet living together with the Lord Jesus in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:1–27; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:18; ROMANS 8:24–25"> While we (exc) live here, we do not have our new bodies, but we are trusting that God will give them to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:20–24; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:6; JOHN 14:3"> As I said, we (exc) are confident that he will give us new bodies. We (exc) would much prefer to leave these bodies which we have now, and be with the Lord Jesus in our home in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:10; 2 PETER 3:14; ROMANS 14:18"> Because of all that, we always want to please Jesus in everything that we do. Whether we (exc) are still living here in these bodies or whether we have left them and are living in our home in heaven, we want to please him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:27; REVELATION 22:12; EPHESIANS 6:8"> And we need to try hard to do that because each one of us believers must stand before Christ to be judged by him &#123;when he will judge his people&#125;. At that time he will reward each one of us according to what we have done while we lived in this world in these bodies. He will reward us according to whether we have done good things or whether we have done evil things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Christ has shown me how much he loves people. That is why I teach his message with all the strength that I have. Therefore, I plead with you to believe that God will forgive you because of what Christ did for you.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JUDE 1:23; HEBREWS 10:31; 2 TIMOTHY 2:24–26"> It makes me fearful to think that some day I will stand before the Lord for him to judge me. So I do everything that I can to convince people that they should believe (OR, that I teach sincerely) God’s message. God knows very well how I conduct my life and what I teach, and I really believe that you know it, too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 3:1; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:14; GALATIANS 6:12–14"> Once again I say, as I write this, I am not just trying to make you think well/highly of me, as some people will probably say that I am. Instead, I am telling you in this letter why you have good reason to tell those who criticize me that you think highly of me. I am telling you this so that you will know what to say to those teachers of false doctrine among you. They are proud of what they have done, instead of making sure that they are right with God and being pleased with that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 11:16–17; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:1; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:6"> Some of them say that after God has enabled me to see visions, I talk like a crazy person. If that is so, I want you to realize that I talk that way in order to please God. On the other hand, if you think that I speak and act wisely, that is good. I want you to know that I do speak and act wisely in order to help you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:20; ROMANS 14:7–9; MATTHEW 10:37–38"> I speak and act the way I do because the love that Christ has for people influences me in everything that I say and do. When Christ died, he suffered the punishment for the sins of all people. So, we should all think of ourselves as having died with him, being as unresponsive to sinful desires as &#91;MET&#93; a corpse is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ROMANS 14:7–9; 1 PETER 4:2–4; 1 PETER 1:14–15"> When Christ died for the everyone, he died in order that we believers who are alive now should not conduct our lives in a way that will just please ourselves. Instead, we should conduct our lives in a way that will please Christ, because he is the one who died for us and was brought back to life &#123;whom God raised&#125; again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:11; PHILIPPIANS 3:7–8; JOHN 6:63"> So, because I realize how much Christ loves everyone, I no longer think about people in the way that those who do not believe in Christ think about them. Before I was a believer, I thought about Christ in the way that other non-believers did. But I do not think of him that way any more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:26; EPHESIANS 4:22–24; Isaiah 43:18–19"> The fact is that God makes every one of us who trusts in Christ to be completely different than we were before. Our old way of conducting our lives is gone. We now have a completely new way of conducting our lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:20–21; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:19–20; ROMANS 5:10–11"> This complete change in our lives is all something that God does. It is God who made it possible for us to have a peaceful relationship with him. He was able to do that because of what Christ did for us. Now God has sent me, and those who work with me, to tell people that they can have a peaceful relationship with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Isaiah 44:22; 1 JOHN 2:1–2"> That is, he sent us(exc) to tell them that when Christ died, God was making it possible for everyone &#91;MTY&#93; in the world to have a peaceful relationship with him. He no longer keeps a record of the sinful things that we believers have done. The message that God has given to us to tell people is how we can have a peaceful relationship with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="EPHESIANS 6:20; LUKE 14:23; MALACHI 2:7"> So, it is I and my companions who are Christ’s representatives in this world. When we tell people the message about Christ, it is God himself who is pleading with them by means of what we say. So, as true representatives of Christ, we plead with you: Believe God’s true message about Christ in order that you may be reconciled to &#123;have a peaceful relationship with&#125; him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 PETER 2:22–24; 1 PETER 3:18; ROMANS 5:19"> You must believe that even though Jesus never sinned, God punished him for all the sinful things that people do, just as if Jesus had done those sinful things himself. And because of our close relationship with Christ, God has erased the record of our sins/declared us no longer guilty for our sins.</span>
</div>
