## Teach and urge the believers to behave in a manner that is consistent with the correct teachings in order that people will perceive that the teachings about God our Savior are very good.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 1:13; 1 TIMOTHY 6:3; TITUS 2:11–14"> But as for you (sg), teach the believers what agrees with the correct teachings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:23; 1 PETER 4:7; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:8"> Specifically, tell the older men that they should control themselves ◄in all situations/whatever happens►, that they should behave in such a manner that all people will respect them, and that they should control what they say and do. And tell them that they should firmly believe in the correct teachings, that they should sincerely love others, and that they should always be steadfast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="TITUS 2:4; 1 TIMOTHY 3:11; 1 PETER 3:3–5"> Similarly, tell the older women that they should behave ◄in a reverent manner that shows that they are devoted to God/in a manner that honors God►; specifically, they should not ◄slander others/talk evil about others►, and they should not ◄be drinking much/habitually drinking► alcohol. Tell them that they should teach the younger women what is good,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:14; 1 TIMOTHY 5:2; 1 TIMOTHY 5:11"> in order that they may love their husbands and their children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PROVERBS 31:10–31; COLOSSIANS 3:18; 1 TIMOTHY 5:10"> Older women should teach younger women to control what they say and do and to be pure in every way, to be good workers at home and submissive to their own husbands. The younger women should behave like this in order that no one will speak against the message about God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 12:1; 1 PETER 5:5; JOEL 2:28"> As for the younger men, similarly, urge them to control themselves in all that they say and do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 PETER 5:3; 1 TIMOTHY 4:12; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:2"> You yourself must continually do what is good in order that others will see how they should behave. Teach the believers sincerely and seriously;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 PETER 2:12; 1 PETER 2:15; 1 PETER 3:16"> teach what is correct and what no one can justly criticize. Do this in order that when the believers conduct their lives in a proper manner, anyone who opposes ◄us/our message► will be disappointed because there will be nothing bad that they can justly say about us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 PETER 2:18–25; 1 TIMOTHY 6:1–2; COLOSSIANS 3:22–25"> As for slaves, specifically, urge them to be submissive to their masters in everything. They should please their masters; specifically, they should not ◄contradict/refuse to obey► their masters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:16; 1 PETER 2:12; 2 JOHN 1:9"> They should not steal things that belong to their masters. Instead, they should always be completely honest/reliable and thus show their masters that their masters can completely trust them. They should behave like this in order that as a result of all the slaves conducting themselves well, people will realize that the teachings about God our Savior are very desirable.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God very kindly sent Jesus Christ to earth in order that God might save all people and God graciously trains us to behave in a godly manner.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:8; 1 TIMOTHY 2:4; Isaiah 45:22"> The believers should behave in a good manner, since God did for us what we did not deserve, sending Jesus Christ to earth in order that he might save all people &#91;PRS&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EZEKIEL 36:27; MATTHEW 16:24; ROMANS 6:19"> God teaches/tells us &#91;PRS&#93; how to stop doing what he dislikes, and to stop desiring the things that ◄ungodly people/people who habitually do things that do not please God► desire &#91;MTY&#93;. He wants us to control our behavior and to do what is right and to do what pleases him while we live in this present age/time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:8; 1 CORINTHIANS 1:7; MATTHEW 16:27"> Do not forget that we are waiting expectantly for that which will make us very happy indeed &#91;MTY&#93;! We are expectantly waiting for our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ, to come gloriously!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="HEBREWS 9:14; EPHESIANS 2:10; 1 PETER 2:9"> He is the one who willingly gave himself ◄to die/as a sacrifice► on our behalf to set us free from all sinful behavior! He wants ◄us to get rid of all our evil behavior/to make our lives pure► &#91;MET&#93;. He wants us, who are his very own people, to be people who are eager to do what is good.</span>
</div>
</div>

## With full authority, teach these things, urge the believers to do them, and correct those believers who do not follow them.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:2; 1 TIMOTHY 4:12–13; TITUS 1:13"> Teach the believers these things! Urge them to do them! Correct those who do not obey them! As you do this, you have my full authority; so do not allow anyone to disregard you!</span>
</div>
</div>
