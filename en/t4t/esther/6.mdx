## The king honored Mordecai

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="DANIEL 6:18; DANIEL 2:1; Esther 2:23"> That night the king was unable to sleep, so he summoned a servant and told him to bring the records of what had happened during all the time he had been king. So the servant brought the records and read some of them to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Esther 2:21–22"> The servant read about Bigthana and Teresh, the two men who had guarded the entrance to the king’s rooms. He read the account of when Mordecai had found out that they planned to assassinate the king and that he had told the king about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ECCLESIASTES 9:15; GENESIS 40:23; ACTS 28:8–10"> The king asked some of his servants, “What did we do to reward Mordecai or show that we appreciate what he did?” The servants replied, “We did not do anything for him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Esther 5:14; Esther 4:11; Esther 5:1"> At that moment, Haman entered the outer courtyard of the palace. He wanted to talk to the king about hanging Mordecai on the gallows that he had just set up. The king saw him and said, “Who is that out there in the courtyard?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> His servants replied, “That is Haman standing there in the courtyard.” The king said, “Bring him in here!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="PROVERBS 16:18; OBADIAH 1:3; Isaiah 62:4–5"> When Haman came in, the king asked him, “What should I do for a man whom I want to honor?” Haman thought to himself, “◄Whom would the king like to honor more than me?/I think there is no one whom the king would like to honor more than me!►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Esther 6:11; Esther 6:9"> So he replied to the king, “If there is someone you wish to honor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 KINGS 1:33; LUKE 15:22; Esther 1:11"> you should tell your servants to bring him one of the robes you wear you to show that you are the king. Also tell them to bring one of the horses you have ridden. Tell them to put a fancy headdress on the horse’s head to show that it belongs to you, the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="GENESIS 41:43; 1 KINGS 1:33–34; ZECHARIAH 9:9"> Then tell one of your very important officials to put the robe on the man whom you want to honor, and tell that man to sit on the horse, and then lead the horse through the city streets. And tell that official to keep shouting, ‘This is what ◄the king does/is doing► for the man whom he wants to honor!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="REVELATION 18:7; DANIEL 4:37; 2 KINGS 10:10"> The king replied to Haman, “Fine! Go and do what you said immediately! Get my robe and my horse, and do just what you suggested! Do it for Mordecai, the Jew, who is sitting outside at the gate of the palace. Do not forget anything that you suggested!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 1:52; Esther 9:3; REVELATION 3:9"> So Haman did what the king said. He got the robe and the horse. He put the robe on Mordecai, and as Mordecai sat on the horse, Haman led the horse through the city streets proclaiming “This is what the king ◄does/is doing► for the man whom the king wants to honor!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 SAMUEL 15:30; JEREMIAH 14:3–4; JOB 20:5"> Then, Mordecai returned and sat at the gate of the palace. But Haman hurried home, covering his face because he felt completely disgraced/humiliated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="PROVERBS 28:18; HOSEA 14:9; GENESIS 40:19"> He told his wife Zeresh and his friends everything that had happened to him that day. Then his wife and his friends said to him, “Mordecai, the man who has caused you to be completely disgraced/humiliated, is a Jew. It is clear that the God whom the Jews worship is fighting for them. So you will never be able to defeat Mordecai. He will defeat you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Esther 5:8; Esther 5:14; DEUTERONOMY 32:35–36"> And while they were still talking, some of the king’s officials arrived to take Haman quickly to the banquet/feast that Esther had prepared.</span>
</div>
