## Again Jesus provided food miraculously for a great crowd who gathered to hear him and became hungry.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:32–39; MARK 6:34–44"> During those days, a large crowd of people gathered again. After they had been there a couple days, they had no food to eat. So Jesus summoned the disciples to come closer and then he said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="MATTHEW 9:36; MICAH 7:19; PSALMS 103:13"> “This is the third day that these people have been with me, and they have nothing left to eat, so now I feel very sorry for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:31; JUDGES 8:4–6; 1 SAMUEL 30:10–12"> Furthermore, if I send them home while they are still hungry, some of them will faint on the way home, because some of them have come from far away.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 KINGS 4:42–44; 2 KINGS 7:2; MARK 6:52"> Knowing that he was suggesting that they give the people something to eat, one of his disciples replied, “◄We cannot possibly find food to satisfy this crowd, here in this place where no people live!/How can we find food to satisfy this crowd, here in this place where no people live?►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:15–17; MATTHEW 15:34; LUKE 9:13"> Jesus asked them, “How many loaves of bread do you have?” They replied, “We have seven flat loaves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:17; MATTHEW 15:35–36; JOHN 6:10–11"> Jesus commanded the crowd, “Sit down on the ground!” After they sat down, he took the seven loaves, he thanked God for them, he broke them into pieces and started giving them to his disciples in order that they might distribute them to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MATTHEW 14:19; JOHN 21:5; LUKE 24:41–42"> They found that they also had a few small dried fish. So after he thanked God for these, he told the disciples, “Distribute these also.” After they distributed the bread and fish to the crowd,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 KINGS 4:42–44; PSALMS 145:16; MATTHEW 16:10"> the people ate this food and they had plenty to satisfy them. The disciples collected the pieces of food that were left over. They filled seven large baskets full of those pieces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> They estimated that there were about 4,000 people who ate on that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:39"> Then Jesus dismissed the crowd. Immediately after that, he got into the boat along with his disciples, and they went around Lake Galilee to Dalmanutha district.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus rebuked those who insisted on him performing more miracles, and rebuked the disciples when they worried about not having enough food with them.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 10:9; MATTHEW 12:38; DEUTERONOMY 6:16"> In those days some Pharisees came to Jesus. They asked him to perform a miracle that would show that God had sent him. They wanted to find a way by which they could convince the people to reject him. So they started to argue with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:39–40; MARK 7:34; MATTHEW 16:4"> Jesus sighed quietly to himself, and then he said, “I am disgusted that &#91;RHQ&#93;, even though you people have seen how I have healed people, you keep asking me to perform miracles! Note this: Such a miracle will certainly not be shown &#123;I will certainly not show such a miracle&#125; just for you people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 12:36; ACTS 13:45–46; PSALMS 81:12"> Then he left them.<br />He got into the boat again, along with his disciples, and they went further around Lake Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:5"> The disciples had forgotten to bring along enough food. Specifically, they had only one flat loaf of bread with them in the boat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 5:6–8; MATTHEW 16:6; MATTHEW 16:11–12"> As they were going, Jesus warned them against the attitudes the Pharisees and Herod Antipas had, attitudes that were having a bad effect on other people. He did this by telling them a parable. He said, “Be careful! Beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and the yeast of Herod!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 9:46; LUKE 20:5; MATTHEW 16:7–8"> The disciples misunderstood him. So they said to one another, “He must have said that because we have no bread.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARK 6:52; Isaiah 63:17; MARK 2:8"> Jesus knew what they were discussing among themselves. So he said to them, “◄I am disappointed that you are discussing about your not having enough bread!/Why are you discussing about your not having enough bread?► &#91;RHQ&#93; ◄You should understand by now that I can provide miraculously for you if you need food!/Why do you not yet perceive or understand that I can provide miraculously for you if you need food?► &#91;RHQ&#93; ◄You are not thinking!/Why are you not thinking?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 28:26–27; MATTHEW 13:14–15; JEREMIAH 5:21"> ◄I am also disappointed that, although you have eyes, you do not understand what you see!/Why is it that, although you have eyes, you do not understand what you see?► &#91;RHQ&#93; You have ears, but you do not understand what I say &#91;RHQ&#93;!” Then he asked, “Do you not remember what happened</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="LUKE 9:12–17; MATTHEW 14:17–21; JOHN 6:5–13"> when I broke only five loaves and fed the 5.000 people &#91;RHQ&#93;? Not only was everyone satisfied, but there was food left over How many baskets full of pieces of bread that were left over did you collect?” They replied, “We collected twelve baskets full.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:34–38; MARK 8:1–9"> Then he asked, “When I broke the seven loaves in order to feed the 4,000 people, again when everyone had plenty to eat, how many large baskets of pieces of bread that were left over did you collect?” They replied, “We collected seven large baskets full.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="MARK 6:52; MATTHEW 16:11–12; MARK 8:12"> Then he kept saying to them, “◄I am disappointed that you do not understand yet that you should never worry that you do not have enough food!/Why do you not understand yet that you should never worry that you do not have enough food?►” &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus healed a blind man.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 11:21; MARK 6:45; LUKE 10:13"> They arrived in the boat at Bethsaida town. People brought to Jesus a blind man and earnestly requested that Jesus touch him in order to heal him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MARK 7:33; JOHN 9:6–7; MARK 5:23"> Jesus took the hand of the blind man, led him outside the town, he put his saliva on the man’s eyes, he put his hands on the man, and then he asked him, “Do you see anything?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 32:3; Isaiah 29:18; 1 CORINTHIANS 13:9–12"> The man looked up and then he said, “Yes, I see people! They are walking around, but I cannot see them clearly. They look like trees!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 PETER 2:9; 2 PETER 3:18; PROVERBS 4:18"> Then Jesus again touched the eyes of the blind man. The man looked intently and at that moment he was completely healed! He could see everything clearly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:4; MARK 8:23; MARK 5:43"> Jesus said to him, “Do not go into the town! First go straight home and tell the people there about what I did!” Then he sent the man to his home.</span>
</div>
</div>

## On the way to the villages near the city of Caesarea Philippi, Jesus asked his disciples what people were saying about who he was and, then, what they thought.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:13–20; LUKE 9:18–20"> Jesus and the disciples left Bethsaida town and went to the villages near Caesarea Philippi town. On the way he questioned them, “Who do people say that I really am?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOHN 1:21; MATTHEW 14:2; MALACHI 4:5"> They replied, “Some people say that you are John the Baptizer, who has come back to life again. Others say that you are the prophet Elijah who has returned from heaven as God promised. And others say that you are one of the other former prophets who has come back to life again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 JOHN 4:15; MATTHEW 16:15–16; 1 JOHN 5:1"> He asked them, “What about you? Who do you say that I am?” Peter replied to him, “We believe that you are the Messiah!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="LUKE 9:21; MATTHEW 16:20; MARK 9:9"> Then Jesus warned them strongly that they should not tell anyone yet that he was the Messiah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus spoke plainly to his disciples about his coming death and resurrection.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MARK 10:33–34; JOHN 2:19; LUKE 24:26"> Then Jesus began to teach them, “Even though I am the one who came from heaven, it is necessary that I suffer very much. It is also necessary that I be rejected by the elders, the chief priests, and the men who teach the Jewish laws &#123;that the elders, the chief priests, and the men who teach the Jewish laws reject me&#125;, and that I be killed &#123;they kill me&#125;. But on the third day after I am killed I will become alive again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:22; JOHN 16:25; JOHN 16:29"> He said this to them clearly. But Peter took Jesus aside and then, because he assumed that the Messiah would never die, he started to rebuke Jesus for talking about dying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 4:10; PHILIPPIANS 3:19; 1 JOHN 2:15"> Jesus turned around and looked at his disciples. Then he rebuked Peter, saying, “Stop thinking like that! Satan is causing you to talk like that! Instead of wanting what God wants me to do, you are wanting me to do only what most people would want me to do.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus explained to the crowd and his disciples what is required if anyone wants to be Jesus’ disciple.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:38; LUKE 14:26–27; JOHN 10:27"> Then he summoned the crowd along with his disciples so that they might listen to him. He told them, “If any one of you wants to be my disciple, you must not do only what you yourself want to do. Specifically, you must be willing to allow people to hurt you and to disgrace you. That is what they do to criminals who are forced to carry crosses &#91;MET&#93; to the places where they will be crucified. That is what anyone who wants to be my disciple must do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 2:11–13; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:23; LUKE 17:33"> You must do that, because those who try to save their lives by denying that they belong to me when people want to kill them for believing in me will not live eternally, but those who are killed because of being my disciples and because of telling others my good message will live forever with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:26; LUKE 9:25; PSALMS 49:17"> People might get everything they want in this world, but ◄they are really gaining nothing if they do not get eternal life because they do not become my disciples!/what will they gain if they do not get eternal life because they do not become my disciples?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="PSALMS 49:7–8; 1 PETER 1:18–19"> ◄Think carefully about the fact that there is absolutely nothing that people can give to God that would enable them to gain eternal life!/Is there anything that a person can give to God in order to gain eternal life?► &#91;RHQ&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKE 12:8–9; ROMANS 1:16; 1 JOHN 2:23"> And think about this: Those who refuse to say that they belong to me and who reject what I say in these days when many people have turned away from God and are very sinful, I, the one who came from heaven, will also refuse to say that they belong to me when I come back with the holy angels and have the glorious brightness that my Father has!”</span>
</div>
</div>
