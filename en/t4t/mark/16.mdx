## Two days later several women were astonished to find Jesus’ tomb empty.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="LUKE 23:56–12; JOHN 20:1–10; MATTHEW 28:1–10"> On Saturday evening when ◄the Sabbath/the Jewish day of rest► had ended, Mary from Magdala, Mary the mother of the younger James, and Salome bought fragrant ointment. The Jews had a custom of anointing bodies before they buried them, and the women wanted to follow this custom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="JOHN 20:1; LUKE 24:1; MATTHEW 28:1"> So very early on Sunday, just after the sun rose, they took the fragrant ointment and started toward the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MARK 15:46–47; MATTHEW 27:60–66"> While they were going there, they were saying to each other, “Who will roll away for us the stone that blocks the entrance of the tomb?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:2–4; LUKE 24:2; JOHN 20:1"> After they arrived, they looked up and saw that the stone had already been rolled away &#123;that someone had already rolled away the stone&#125;. They were surprised because it would have been difficult to move such a huge stone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JOHN 20:11–12; LUKE 24:3–5; JOHN 20:8"> They entered the tomb and saw an angel who looked like a young man. He was sitting at the right side of the cave. He was wearing a shining white robe. As a result, they were astonished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="REVELATION 1:17–18; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:3–7; LUKE 24:46"> The young man said to them, “Do not be astonished! I know that you are looking for Jesus, the man from Nazareth, who was nailed to a cross &#123;whom they nailed to a cross&#125;. But he has become alive again! He is not here! Look! Here is the place where they placed his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="MARK 14:28; MATTHEW 26:32; MATTHEW 28:10"> But, instead of remaining here, go and tell his disciples. Particularly be sure that you tell Peter. Tell them, ‘Jesus is going ahead of you to Galilee district, and you will see him there, just like he told you previously’!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="LUKE 24:22–24; LUKE 24:9–11; MARK 16:5–6"> The women went outside and ran from the tomb. They were trembling because they were afraid, and they were astonished. But they did not say anything to anyone about this while they were going, because they were afraid.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus rebuked the disciples for not believing the reports of his being alive again.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="JOHN 20:14–19; LUKE 8:2; JOHN 20:12"> &#91;When Jesus became alive again early on Sunday morning, he appeared first to Mary from Magdala town. She was the woman from whom he had previously expelled seven evil spirits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 20:18; LUKE 24:17; JOHN 16:6"> She went to those who had been with Jesus, while they were mourning and crying. She told them what she had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="LUKE 24:11; MARK 16:13–14; MARK 9:19"> But when she told them that Jesus was alive again and that she had seen him, they refused to believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="LUKE 24:13–32; JOHN 21:14; JOHN 21:1"> Later that day, Jesus appeared to two of his disciples while they were walking from Jerusalem to their homes in the surrounding area. But they did not recognize him quickly because he looked very different.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="JOHN 20:25; LUKE 24:33–35; JOHN 20:8"> After they recognized him, those two went back to Jerusalem. They told his other followers what had happened, but they did not believe it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 15:5; LUKE 24:36–43; HEBREWS 3:7–8"> Later he appeared to the eleven apostles while they were eating. He rebuked them because they had stubbornly refused to believe the reports of those who saw him after he had become alive again.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Miracles began to happen after Jesus instructed his disciples to preach the gospel everywhere and he told them that miracles would accompany their ministry.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:19; MARK 13:10; ACTS 1:8"> Later he said to them, “Go into the whole world and preach the good message to everyone!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="JOHN 11:25–26; JOHN 3:36; ROMANS 10:9–14"> Everyone who believes your message and who is baptized will be saved &#123;God will save&#125;. But everyone who does not believe it will be condemned &#123;God will condemn&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ACTS 5:16; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:28; ACTS 8:7"> Those who believe my good message will perform miracles. Specifically, by my power they will expel evil spirits. They will speak in languages that they have not learned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="LUKE 10:19; JAMES 5:14–15; ACTS 28:3–6"> If they pick up snakes accidentally or if they drink any poisonous liquid accidentally, they will not be hurt. Whenever they put their hands on sick people in order that God will heal them, those sick people will become well.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 PETER 3:22; HEBREWS 1:3; HEBREWS 12:2"> After the Lord Jesus had said this to the disciples, he was taken &#123;God took him&#125; up into heaven. Then he sat down on his throne beside God to rule with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 2:4–5; ACTS 14:3; ROMANS 15:19"> As for the disciples, they went out from Jerusalem, and then they preached everywhere. Wherever they went, the Lord enabled them to perform miracles. By doing that, he showed people that God’s message is true.</span>
</div>
</div>
