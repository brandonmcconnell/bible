## Jesus took Peter, James and John up a high mountain where his appearance changed, and Moses and Elijah appeared and talked to Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="LUKE 9:28–36; Isaiah 33:17; MATTHEW 17:1–8"> A week later Jesus took Peter, James and James’ younger brother John and led them up a high mountain. While they were alone up there, he appeared very different to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="MATTHEW 28:3; DANIEL 7:9; REVELATION 7:14"> His clothes became dazzling white. They were whiter than anyone on earth could make them by bleaching them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="LUKE 9:30–31; MATTHEW 17:3–4; ACTS 3:21–24"> Two prophets who had lived long ago, Moses and Elijah, appeared to them. Then the two of them began talking with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 1:23; JOHN 14:8–9; REVELATION 22:3–4"> After a short time, Peter interrupted by exclaiming, “Teacher, it is wonderful to be here! So allow us to make three shelters. One will be for you, one will be for Moses, and one will be for Elijah!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="REVELATION 1:17; DANIEL 10:15–19; MARK 16:5–8"> He said this because he wanted to say something, but he did not know what to say, because he and the other two disciples were very awestruck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 PETER 1:17; MATTHEW 3:17; MARK 1:11"> Then a shining cloud appeared that covered them. God &#91;EUP&#93; spoke to them from the cloud saying, “This is my Son. I love him. Therefore, listen to him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ACTS 10:16; ACTS 8:39–40; LUKE 24:31"> When the three disciples looked around, they saw that suddenly Jesus was alone with them, and that there was no longer anyone else there.</span>
</div>

## Jesus told them that the one like Elijah has already come and been evilly treated, but that the Messiah would also be evilly treated.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:40; MATTHEW 17:9–13; MARK 5:43"> While they were coming down the mountain, Jesus told them that they should not tell anyone yet what had just happened to him. He said, “You may tell them after I, the one who came from heaven, ◄rise from the dead/become alive again► after I die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ACTS 17:18; LUKE 24:7–8; LUKE 18:33–34"> So they did not tell others about it for a long time. But they discussed among themselves what it meant when he said that he would ◄rise from the dead/become alive again► after he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="MALACHI 4:5; MALACHI 3:1; MATTHEW 11:14"> The three disciples had just seen Elijah, but Elijah had not done anything to prepare people to accept Jesus. So is what they are teaching wrong?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 52:14"> Jesus wanted them to know that John the Baptizer was the one who represented Elijah. So he answered them, “It is true that God promised to send Elijah to come first so that people would be ready when I came. Elijah’s representative has already come, and our leaders treated him very badly, just like they wanted to do, just like it was prophesied &#123;the prophets prophesied&#125; long ago. But, ◄I also want you to consider what is written &#123;what the prophets wrote&#125; in the Scriptures about me, the one who came from heaven./why have you not thought about what is written &#123;what the prophets wrote&#125; about me, the one who came from heaven?► &#91;RHQ&#93; It is written &#123;They wrote&#125; that I would suffer much and that I would be rejected &#123;that many people would reject me&#125;.”</span>

## Jesus expelled an evil spirit and later explained to the disciples why they had failed to do that.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="LUKE 9:37–42; MATTHEW 17:14–20; LUKE 11:53–54"> Jesus and those three disciples returned to where the other disciples were. They saw a large crowd around the other disciples and some men who taught the Jewish laws arguing with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EXODUS 34:30; MARK 9:2–3"> As soon as the crowd saw Jesus, they were very surprised to see him come. So they ran to him and greeted him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 5:30–32; MARK 8:11"> He asked them, “What are you arguing about?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="MARK 9:25; LUKE 11:14; JOHN 4:47"> A man in the crowd answered him, “Teacher, I brought my son here in order that you would heal him. There is an evil spirit in him that makes him unable to talk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:19–20; MARK 11:23; MATTHEW 17:16"> Whenever the spirit attacks him, it throws him down. He foams at the mouth, he grinds his teeth together, and he becomes stiff. I asked your disciples to expel the spirit, but they were not able to do it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="NUMBERS 14:11; JOHN 20:27; NUMBERS 14:27"> Jesus replied by saying to those people, “◄I am very disappointed that although you have observed how I help people, you do not believe that you are able to do anything!/Why is it that although you have observed how I help people, you do not believe that you are able to do anything?► &#91;RHQ&#93; ◄I have been with you a long time, and you still cannot do what I do!/How long do I have to be with you before you can do what I do?► &#91;RHQ&#93; Bring the boy to me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARK 1:26; LUKE 4:35; JOHN 8:44"> So they brought the boy to Jesus. As soon as the evil spirit saw Jesus, it shook the boy severely, and the boy fell on the ground. He rolled around, and he foamed at the mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ACTS 9:33; ACTS 4:22; MARK 5:25"> Jesus asked the boy’s father, “How long has he been like this?” He replied, “This started to happen when he was a child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="MATTHEW 15:22–28; MARK 5:19; MATTHEW 20:34"> The spirit does not only do this, but he also often throws him into the fire or into the water in order to kill him. But, pity us and help us, if you can!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 11:40; HEBREWS 11:6; MARK 11:23"> Jesus exclaimed to him, “Do not say &#91;RHQ&#93; ‘If you can’, for God/I can do anything for people who believe in him/me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="LUKE 17:5; HEBREWS 12:2; EPHESIANS 2:8"> Immediately the child’s father shouted, “I believe that you can help me, but I do not believe strongly. Help me to believe more strongly!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ACTS 16:18; LUKE 9:42; MARK 9:15"> Because Jesus wanted to heal the boy before the crowd got bigger, he rebuked the evil spirit, saying, “You evil spirit, you who are causing this boy to be deaf and unable to talk! I command you to come out of him and never enter him again!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="MARK 9:20; MARK 1:26; MARK 9:18"> The evil spirit shouted, it shook the boy violently, and then it left the boy. The boy did not move. He seemed like a dead body. So many of the people there said, “He is dead!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="MARK 1:31; ACTS 3:7; MARK 1:41"> However, Jesus took him by the hand and helped him get up. Then the boy stood up. He was healed!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:19–20; MARK 7:17; MATTHEW 15:15"> Later, when Jesus and his disciples were alone in a house, one of them asked him, “Why were we not able to expel the evil spirit?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="JAMES 5:15; DANIEL 9:3; MATTHEW 17:20"> He said to them, “You can expel this kind of evil spirit only by your praying that God will give you the power to do it. There is no other way that you can expel them.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus was teaching his disciples that he would be handed over to other men, killed, and become alive again; but they did not understand what he was saying.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="MATTHEW 17:22–23; LUKE 9:43–45; MARK 6:31–32"> After Jesus and his disciples left that district, they traveled through Galilee district. Jesus did not want anyone else to know that he was in their area; he did not want</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 16:21; MARK 9:12; LUKE 24:44–46"> anyone to interrupt him as he taught his disciples. He was telling them, “Some day my enemies &#91;SYN&#93; will seize &#91;MTY&#93; me, the one who came from heaven, and I will be handed over to other men. Those men will kill me. But on the third day after I am killed &#123;after they kill me&#125;, I will become alive again!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="LUKE 2:50; LUKE 9:45; LUKE 18:34"> But they did not understand what he was telling them. Also, they were afraid to ask him about what he had said.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught his disciples by illustration about what kind of person God considers important.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:1–5; HEBREWS 4:13; LUKE 9:46–48"> Jesus and his disciples returned to Capernaum town. When they were in the house, he asked them, “What were you discussing while we were traveling on the road?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="MARK 9:50; MATTHEW 20:21–24; MATTHEW 18:1–5"> But they did not reply. They were ashamed to reply because, while they were traveling, they had been arguing with each other about which one of them would be the most important when Jesus became king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="MATTHEW 20:25–28; MARK 10:42–45; LUKE 22:26"> He sat down, he summoned the twelve disciples to come close to him, and then he said to them, “If anyone wants God to consider him to be the most important person of all, he must consider himself to be the least important person of all, and he must serve everyone else.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="MARK 10:16; MATTHEW 19:14–15; MATTHEW 18:2"> Then he took a child and placed him among them. He took the child in his arms and then he said to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:3–5; LUKE 10:16; MATTHEW 10:40–42"> “Those who, because they love me, welcome a child like this one, God considers that they are welcoming me. So it is as though they are also welcoming God, who sent me.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught his disciples about who is for him and who is against him.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="LUKE 9:49–50; NUMBERS 11:26–29; LUKE 11:19"> John said to Jesus, “Teacher, we saw someone who was expelling demons, ◄claiming he had authority from you to do that/while telling people that you have told him to do it►. So we told him to stop doing it because he was not one of us disciples.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 12:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 13:1–2; MATTHEW 13:28–29"> Jesus said, “Do not tell him not to do that! For no one will speak evilly about me just after he performs a miracle while saying that I have told him to do it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="MATTHEW 12:30; LUKE 11:23"> Furthermore, those who are not opposing us are trying to achieve the same goals that we are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="MATTHEW 10:42; MATTHEW 25:40; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:23"> Also think about this: Those who belong to me who help you in any way, even if they just give you a cup of water to drink because you follow me, the Messiah, will certainly be rewarded by God!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught his disciples about sin and its result.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:6; LUKE 17:1–2; MATTHEW 18:10"> Jesus also said, “But if you cause someone who believes in me to sin, God will severely punish you, even if that person is socially unimportant like this little child. If someone tied a heavy stone around your neck and you were thrown &#123;and threw you&#125; into the sea, you would consider that was a severe punishment. But God will punish you more severely than that if you cause a person who believes in me to sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="MATTHEW 5:29–30; MATTHEW 18:8–9; MATTHEW 25:41"> So, if you are wanting to use one of &#91;MTY, PRS&#93; your hands to sin, stop using your hand! Even if you have to cut your hand off and throw it away to avoid sinning, do it &#91;HYP&#93;! It is good that you not sin and that you live eternally, even though you lack one of your hands while you are here on earth. But it is not good that you sin and as a result God throws your whole body into hell. There the fires never go out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:8; MATTHEW 5:22; MARK 9:43"> If you are wanting to use one of &#91;PRS&#93; your feet to sin, stop using your foot! Even if you have to cut off your foot to avoid sinning, do it &#91;HYP&#93;! It is good that you not sin and live eternally, even though you lack one of your feet while you are here on earth. But it is not good that you sin and go to hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="MATTHEW 18:9; MARK 9:43; MATTHEW 5:22"> If because of what you see &#91;MTY, PRS&#93; you are tempted to sin, stop looking at those things! Even if you have to gouge out your eye and throw it away &#91;HYP&#93; to avoid sinning, do it! It is good that you not sin and live eternally, even though you lack one of your eyes while you are here on earth. But it is not good that you sin and, as a result, God puts your whole body in hell.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Isaiah 66:24; MARK 9:43–45; MATTHEW 25:41"> In that place people suffer forever and the fires are never put out &#123;never go out&#125;.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus taught that we must endure trials to remain useful to God.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="LEVITICUS 2:13; EZEKIEL 43:24"> “You must endure difficulties in order that God will be pleased with you. Your difficulties are like a fire that makes things pure &#91;MET&#93;. Your enduring is also like &#91;MET&#93; salt being put on &#123;people putting salt on their&#125; sacrifices to make them pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:6; ROMANS 12:18; MATTHEW 5:13"> Salt is useful to put on food, but you cannot &#91;RHQ&#93; make salt to taste salty again if it becomes flavorless &#91;MET&#93;. Similarly, you must remain useful to God, because no one can make you useful to God again if you become useless. You must also live peacefully with each other.”</span>
