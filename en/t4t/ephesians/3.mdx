## I am in prison because God appointed me to tell non-Jews that Christ is the source of great spiritual blessings for them.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:24; 2 TIMOTHY 1:8; PHILIPPIANS 1:13–16"> Because God has done all this for you non-Jews, I, Paul, pray for you. I want you to know that it is because I serve Christ Jesus for your sake that I am in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:25–27; 1 TIMOTHY 1:11; 2 TIMOTHY 1:11"> I ◄assume that/think that probably► someone has told you how God acted very kindly toward me, appointing me so that I would proclaim the gospel to you non-Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:12; ROMANS 16:25; EPHESIANS 1:9–11"> God revealed to me the message that he had not revealed to others. When you read what I have already written briefly about that,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:1; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:6"> you will be able to understand that I understand clearly that message about Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 PETER 1:10–12; 2 PETER 3:2; TITUS 1:1–3"> Formerly, God did not reveal that message to anyone, but now his Spirit has revealed that message to ◄his holy apostles and prophets/people who tell messages that come directly from God►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="GALATIANS 3:26–29; 1 JOHN 1:3; 1 JOHN 2:25"> That message, which he has now revealed, is that because of our (inc) relationship with Christ Jesus, all of us, non-Jews as well as Jews &#91;MTY&#93;, will receive the great spiritual blessings that God has promised as we all form one group &#91;MET&#93; as a result of our believing the good news about Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:19; EPHESIANS 3:20; EPHESIANS 3:2"> By God powerfully enabling me, and because God acted kindly toward me, doing what I did not deserve, and chose me to do that work, I became someone who tells others this good message.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:27; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:9; EPHESIANS 2:7"> Although I am the least worthy of all God’s people, God kindly appointed me to proclaim to the non-Jews the message about the great spiritual blessings that they can receive from Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:26; MATTHEW 13:35; 2 TIMOTHY 1:9"> and to enable everyone to understand clearly how God accomplished what he planned. God, who created everything, has now revealed this message, which he never revealed to anyone before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 PETER 1:12; EPHESIANS 6:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:7"> What he planned was that all ◄believers/people who belong to Christ► would be the ones who would reveal to all the ranks &#91;DOU&#93; of spiritual beings in heaven that what God had planned is wise in every way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 1:9; EPHESIANS 1:11; EPHESIANS 1:4"> That is what God had always planned, and it is what he accomplished by what our Lord Jesus has done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EPHESIANS 2:18; ROMANS 5:2; HEBREWS 4:14–16"> Because of what he has done and because of our relationship with him, when we pray we can approach God confidently and without being afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:24; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:13; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:6"> So I ask that you do not be discouraged because of my suffering many things for you here in prison. You should feel honored that I am willing to suffer these things for your sake.</span>

## I pray that God’s Spirit will empower you, that you may experience how much Christ loves you, and that God may make you all that he intends for you to be.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="PSALMS 95:6; EPHESIANS 1:16–19; PHILIPPIANS 2:10"> Because God has done all this for you, I kneel and pray &#91;MTY&#93; to God our Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:10; REVELATION 5:8–14; EPHESIANS 1:21"> He is the one who is like a father of all the believers who are now in heaven and those who are still on the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:11; PHILIPPIANS 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> I pray that, using his unlimited resources, he will cause you to be strengthened by his Spirit &#123;cause his Spirit to strengthen you&#125; in your ◄inner beings/hearts► with all God’s power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 2:7; 1 JOHN 4:16; JOHN 14:23"> That is, I pray that because of your trusting in Christ, his Spirit may live in your ◄inner beings/hearts► (OR, he may live in your hearts). And I pray that because you love Christ firmly and faithfully/continually &#91;MET&#93;, you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="PSALMS 103:11–12; JOB 11:7–9; EPHESIANS 1:18–23"> along with all other believers, may be able to know how very &#91;DOU&#93; much Christ loves us(inc).</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:23; COLOSSIANS 2:9–10; COLOSSIANS 1:10"> I want you to experience how very deeply he loves us, even though it is not possible for us to understand fully how much he loves us. And I pray that God will enable you to be filled with &#123;have a full measure of&#125; all the qualities of his character that he himself has.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 9:8; ROMANS 4:21; JEREMIAH 32:27"> God is able to do much ◄greater things/more► than we could ask him to do, or even that we might think that he can do, by his power that is working within us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="JUDE 1:25; ROMANS 11:36; 2 PETER 3:18"> Because of our relationship with Christ Jesus, may all ◄believers/those who belong to him► praise him forever. ◄Amen!/May it be so!►</span>
