## As you always rely on the Lord to strengthen you, make use of every spiritual resource that God provides to resist the devil and all his powerful evil spirits.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 4:13; Isaiah 40:31; JOSHUA 1:9"> Finally, at all times rely/depend completely on the Lord Jesus to strengthen you spiritually by his own mighty power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 10:4; ROMANS 13:12; EPHESIANS 6:13"> Just like a soldier puts on all his ◄armor/things to help him fight► &#91;MET&#93;, you should use every spiritual resource/help that God provides for you, in order that you may successfully resist the devil when he cleverly tries to oppose you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:13; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:4; EPHESIANS 2:2"> You must do this because the fighting that we believers do is not only against human beings &#91;SYN&#93;. Instead, we are also fighting against evil spirits who rule and have authority over all that is evil &#91;MET&#93; in the world. And we are fighting against evil spirits who are in heavenly places (OR, everywhere).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 10:4; LUKE 21:36; EPHESIANS 6:11–17"> Therefore, just like a soldier puts on all his ◄armor/things to help him fight► &#91;MET&#93;, use well all the spiritual resources/helps that God provides for you. Do that in order that you may be able to resist successfully the devil and all his powerful evil spirits &#91;MTY&#93; every time they ◄attack you/tempt you to sin or cause something bad to happen to you►. Do it also in order that when you have done all that you can to resist them when they attack you, you will still be ready to resist them when they attack you the next time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:8; Isaiah 11:5"> You must be ready to firmly resist the devil and his evil spirits &#91;MET&#93;, just like soldiers must be ready to resist the enemy. <span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; ROMANS 10:15; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:18–21"> Hold fast the good message that gives us peace with God, just like soldiers put on their boots firmly &#91;MET&#93; ◄to stand firm against/to firmly resist► their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 PETER 5:8–9; 1 JOHN 5:4–5; PROVERBS 18:10"> In addition, keep trusting firmly in the Lord. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="HEBREWS 4:12; Isaiah 59:17; REVELATION 19:15"> And rely/depend on the fact that God has saved you, in order to protect yourselves against demonic attacks, just like soldiers put on a helmet to protect their heads from attacks by their enemies &#91;MET&#93;. <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:17; COLOSSIANS 4:2; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> As you are doing that, keep praying to God at all times, and requesting him to do things for you and others; and let God’s Spirit direct what you pray. For this purpose, always be spiritually alert/watchful, and ◄always be persistent in/never quit► praying for all God’s people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 4:3; ACTS 4:29; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:1"> And specifically, pray for me. People did not know that message before, but God has now revealed it to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:20; COLOSSIANS 4:4; 2 TIMOTHY 2:9"> I am ◄a representative of Christ/one who speaks on behalf of Christ► as I tell this message to others, and I am in prison &#91;MTY&#93; because of that. Pray that when I tell others about Christ, I may speak without being afraid, because that is how I ought to speak.</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="PHILIPPIANS 2:25; COLOSSIANS 4:7–8; 2 THESSALONIANS 2:17"> That is the reason that I am sending him to you; I want you to know ◄how my companions and I are/what is happening to my companions and me►, and I want him to encourage you &#91;SYN&#93;.</span>
