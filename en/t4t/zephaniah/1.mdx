## Yahweh will punish Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HOSEA 10:5; MICAH 5:13; 2 KINGS 23:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">“These are some of the things that I will do:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will punish the people who live in Jerusalem and other places in Judah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will destroy everything that has been used in the worship of Baal.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cause people to no longer remember the names of the pagan priests</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">or the other priests who have turned away from me.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="JEREMIAH 19:13; 1 KINGS 11:33; 2 KINGS 17:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will get rid of those who go up on the roofs of their houses and worship the sun and moon and stars,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those who claim that they worship me but also worship their god Molech.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; JEREMIAH 2:13; JEREMIAH 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will get rid of all those who previously worshiped me but no longer do,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">those who no longer seek my help or ask me to tell them what they should do.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="HABAKKUK 2:20; ZECHARIAH 2:13; ZEPHANIAH 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be silent in front of Yahweh the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because it will soon be the time/day when Yahweh will judge and punish people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh has prepared to get rid of the people of Judah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">they will be like &#91;MET&#93; animals that are slaughtered for sacrifices,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and he has chosen their enemies to be the ones who will get rid of them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 24:21; Isaiah 3:18–24; JEREMIAH 22:24–30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh says, “On that day when I will get rid of the people of Judah,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">I will punish their officials and the king’s sons,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and I will punish all those who worship foreign gods &#91;MTY&#93;.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 5:5; AMOS 3:10; 1 SAMUEL 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will punish those who show that they revere their god Dagan by avoiding stepping on the threshold of his temple,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and those who do violent things and tell lies in the temples of their gods.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CHRONICLES 33:14; AMOS 8:3; 2 KINGS 22:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yahweh also says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">“On that day, people will cry out at the Fish Gate of Jerusalem.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">People will wail in the newer part of the city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and people will hear a loud crash of buildings collapsing in the hills.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="JAMES 5:1; JEREMIAH 25:34; HOSEA 12:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">So, all you people who live in the market area of Jerusalem should wail,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">because all those who sell goods and weigh out silver will be slaughtered.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="AMOS 6:1; JEREMIAH 48:11; EZEKIEL 8:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will be as though I will light lanterns to search in dark places in Jerusalem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">for those who have become very satisfied with their behavior and complacent about their sins.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They think that I, Yahweh, will do nothing to them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">neither good things nor bad things.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="AMOS 5:11; MICAH 6:15; DEUTERONOMY 28:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I say that their valuable possessions will be plundered/stolen by their enemies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and their houses will be destroyed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will build new homes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they will not live in them;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will plant vineyards,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">but they will never drink any wine made from grapes that grow there.”</span></span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="HOSEA 8:1; AMOS 3:6; Isaiah 32:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">It will be a time when soldiers will blow trumpets to call other soldiers to battle.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">Your enemies will tear down the walls around your cities</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and the high towers at the corners of those walls.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="REVELATION 3:17; PSALMS 83:10; JEREMIAH 2:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because you sinned against Yahweh,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">he will cause you to experience great distress;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">you will walk around groping like &#91;SIM&#93; blind people do.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your blood will flow from your bodies like &#91;SIM&#93; water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-2">and your corpses will lie on the ground and rot &#91;SIM&#93;.</span></span>
