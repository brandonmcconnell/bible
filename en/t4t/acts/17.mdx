## In Thessalonica, Paul convinced many people that Jesus is the Messiah.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 1:1; PHILIPPIANS 4:16; ACTS 20:4"> Paul and Silas traveled through Amphipolis and Apollonia towns and arrived at Thessalonica city. There was a Jewish meeting place there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ACTS 9:20; ACTS 17:17; ACTS 18:4"> ◄On the Sabbath/On the Jewish rest day► Paul went into the meeting house, as he usually did. For three weeks he went there on each Jewish day of rest. Referring to the Scriptures about the Messiah, he spoke to the people who were there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ACTS 18:28; ACTS 9:22; LUKE 24:32"> He explained and showed that the prophets wrote that the Messiah needed to die and ◄to become alive again/to be raised from the dead afterwards►. He told them: “This man Jesus, whom I am telling you about, is our Messiah. He died and became alive again, just like the prophets predicted.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ACTS 17:12; ACTS 13:43; ACTS 14:4"> Some of the Jews there were persuaded by &#123;believed&#125; what Paul had said and began to associate with Paul and Silas. There were also many non-Jewish people there who worshipped God and many important women who also believed the message about Jesus, and they began to associate with Paul and Silas.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Some Jews there incited people to oppose Paul and Silas.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="GALATIANS 5:26; ACTS 17:13; ROMANS 16:21"> But some leaders of the Jews there in Thessalonica became jealous because many people believed what Paul taught. So they went to the public square and persuaded some lazy men who were loitering there to follow them. In this way, the leaders of the Jews gathered a crowd and incited them to become noisy and start a riot against Paul and Silas. Those Jews and others ran to the house of a man named Jason. He was the man who had invited Paul and Silas to stay at his house. They wanted to bring Paul and Silas outside to where the crowd of people was waiting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ACTS 24:5; ACTS 16:19–21; ACTS 28:22"> They discovered that Paul and Silas were not there, but they found Jason and grabbed him. They dragged him and some of the other believers to where the city officials/authorities and many other people were gathered. The men who had brought Jason shouted, “Those two men have caused trouble &#91;IDM&#93; everywhere &#91;HYP&#93; they have gone. Now they have come to our city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="LUKE 23:2; ACTS 16:21; JOHN 19:12"> and this fellow Jason invited them to stay at his house. All the people of this sect oppose what our Emperor has decreed. They say that another person, whose name is Jesus, is the real king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="MATTHEW 2:3; JOHN 11:48"> When the crowd of people that had gathered and the city authorities heard that, they became very angry and excited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ACTS 17:5"> They wanted to put the believers in jail. But instead, the officials made Jason and the other believers pay a fine and told them that they would give the money back to them if Paul and Silas did not cause any more trouble. Then the authorities let Jason and those other believers go.</span>
</div>
</div>

## In Berea, Paul helped many Jews and non-Jews to believe in Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="ACTS 17:2; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:2; ACTS 17:13–14"> So that same night, the believers sent Paul and Silas out of Thessalonica to Berea town. When Paul and Silas arrived there, they went to the Jewish meeting place, on a day when people had gathered there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 34:16; PROVERBS 2:1–5; JOHN 5:39"> The Jews in Thessalonica had not been very willing to listen to God’s message, but the Jews who lived in Berea were very willing to listen, so they listened very eagerly to the message about Jesus. Every day they read the Scriptures for themselves to find out if what Paul said about the Messiah was true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="EPHESIANS 5:14; ACTS 14:1; ACTS 13:50"> As a result, many of the Jewish people believed in Jesus, and also some of the important non-Jewish women and many non-Jewish men believed in him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jews from Thessalonica incited people to oppose Paul.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:14–16; PROVERBS 28:25; ACTS 6:12"> But then the Jews in Thessalonica heard people tell them that Paul was in Berea and that he was preaching the message from God about Jesus. So they went to Berea and told people there that what Paul was teaching was not true. Thus, they caused many of those people to get angry at Paul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ACTS 17:10; MATTHEW 10:23; ACTS 9:25"> So several of the believers in Berea took Paul to the coast to go to another province. But Silas and Timothy stayed in Berea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 18:5; 1 THESSALONIANS 3:1; ACTS 18:1"> When Paul and the other men from Berea arrived at the coast, they got on a ship and went to Athens city. Then Paul said to the men who had come with him, “Tell Silas and Timothy to come to me here in Athens as soon as they can.” Then those men left Athens and returned to Berea.</span>
</div>
</div>

## In Athens, idols distressed Paul, so he talked to many people about Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="PSALMS 119:158; 2 PETER 2:7; PSALMS 119:136"> In Athens, Paul waited for Silas and Timothy to arrive. In the meantime, he walked around in the city. He became very distressed/disturbed because he saw that throughout &#91;HYP&#93; the city there were many idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ACTS 13:16; ACTS 14:1–4; ACTS 17:2–4"> So he went to the Jewish meeting place and talked about Jesus with the Jews, and also with the Greeks who had accepted what the Jews believe. He also went to the public square/center every day and talked to the people whom he met there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## When Paul talked about Jesus, he perplexed some teachers.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:20–21; COLOSSIANS 2:8; LUKE 11:53"> Paul met some teachers who liked to talk about what people should believe. People called some of them Epicureans and they called others Stoics. They told Paul what they believed, and they asked him what he believed. Then some of them said to one another, “This ignorant person is just talking nonsense &#91;RHQ&#93;!” Others said, “We(exc) think that he is teaching people about ◄foreign gods/new gods that we (exc) have not heard about►.” They said that because Paul was telling them that Jesus had died and had become alive again afterwards. They had not heard that message before.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Athens Council members asked Paul to explain what he had been teaching.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="MARK 1:27; ACTS 17:22; ACTS 24:24"> So they took Paul to the place where the city council met. When they arrived there, they said to Paul, “Please tell us, what is this new message that you (sg) are teaching people?</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:23; HOSEA 8:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 2:14"> You are teaching some things that startle us (exc), so we want to know what they mean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 2:16–17; COLOSSIANS 4:5; EPHESIANS 5:16"> They said that, because the people of Athens and also the people from other regions who lived there continually talked about what was new to them, or they listened to others tell what was new.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul told them about the God whom they did not know.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ACTS 25:19; ACTS 19:35; ACTS 17:16"> Then Paul stood up before the men of the city council and said, “Citizens of Athens, I see that you ◄are very religious/think that it is very important to worship many gods►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="JOHN 4:22; GALATIANS 4:8–9; JOHN 17:3"> I say that because, while I was walking along and observing the objects that represent different gods that you worship, I even saw an altar that had these words that someone had carved on it: THIS HONORS THE GOD THAT WE(exc) DO NOT KNOW. So now I will tell you about that God whom you worship but you do not know.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 KINGS 8:27; DEUTERONOMY 10:14; ACTS 7:48"> He is the God who made the world and everything in it. Because he rules over all beings in heaven and on earth, he does not dwell in shrines that people have made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="PSALMS 50:8–13; GENESIS 2:7; Isaiah 42:5"> He does not need to have anything made for him by people &#91;MTY&#93; &#123;to have people &#91;MTY&#93; make &#91;MTY&#93; anything for him&#125;, because everything that exists belongs to him. He is the one who causes ◄us (inc)/all people► to live and breathe, and he gives us all the things that we(inc) need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 32:7–8; GENESIS 3:20; ROMANS 5:12–19"> In the beginning, God created one couple, and from them God produced all ◄the ethnic groups/the nations► that now live everywhere on the earth. He also decided where each ethnic group of people should live and how long they should live there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="JEREMIAH 23:23–24; DEUTERONOMY 4:7; ACTS 14:17"> He wanted people to realize that they need him. Then maybe they would seek him and find him. God wants us to seek him, although he is really close &#91;LIT&#93; to each one of us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="JOB 12:10; COLOSSIANS 1:17; HEBREWS 1:3"> As someone has said, ‘It is only because he enables us that we (inc) live and move and do what we do.’ And, as some of your own poets have said, ‘We (inc) are God’s children.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 40:25; EXODUS 20:4; PSALMS 115:4–8"> Therefore, because we are God’s children and can communicate like God does, we (inc) should not think that he is anything like an image that people have made of gold or silver or stone which cannot communicate. Those images are designed and skillfully made &#123;People design and make those images&#125;, but they are not alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="ACTS 14:16; TITUS 2:11–12; LUKE 24:47"> During the times when people did not know what God wanted them to do, he did not immediately punish them for what they did. But now God commands all people everywhere to turn away from their evil behavior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="MATTHEW 25:31–46; 2 PETER 3:7; ROMANS 2:16"> He tells us that on a certain day that he has chosen he is going to judge all of us(inc) people in &#91;MTY&#93; the world. He has appointed a certain man to judge us, and that man will judge each of us fairly/justly. God has shown to all people that he has appointed that man to judge everyone, because God ◄caused him to become alive again after he had died/raised him from the dead►.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Some Council members believed in Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ACTS 17:18; ACTS 26:8; ACTS 25:19"> When the men of the council heard Paul say that ◄a man had become alive again after he had died/someone had been raised from the dead►, some of them laughed scornfully. But others said, “We (exc) would like you (sg) to tell us more about this, some other time.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> After they said that, Paul left the council meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ACTS 17:19; ROMANS 11:5–6; ACTS 13:48"> However, some of the people there went along with Paul and became believers. Among those who believed in Jesus was a member of the council whose name was Dionysius. Also, an important woman whose name was Damaris and some other people who had heard Paul’s message also believed in Jesus.</span>
</div>
