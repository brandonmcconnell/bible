## In Corinth, Paul helped many people to become believers.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:2; 2 TIMOTHY 4:20; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:1"> After that, Paul left Athens city and went to Corinth city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ROMANS 16:3–4; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:19; 2 TIMOTHY 4:19"> There he met a Jew whose name was Aquila, who grew up in Pontus province. Aquila and his wife Priscilla had recently come from Rome, in Italy. They had previously left Rome because Claudius, the Roman Emperor, had ordered that all the Jews must leave Rome. Paul later went to see Aquila and Priscilla.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 4:12; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:9; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:9"> Those two made tents to earn ◄money/a living►. Paul also made tents, so he stayed with them, and they all worked together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:11; ACTS 17:17; ACTS 28:23"> Every Sabbath, Paul went to the Jewish meeting place, where he spoke forcefully to both Jews and non-Jews. He repeatedly tried to persuade them that Jesus is the Messiah.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul helped many non-Jews become believers.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 5:14; ACTS 18:28; ACTS 20:21"> Then Silas and Timothy arrived there from Macedonia province. After they arrived, Paul did not make tents any more. He used all his time preaching the message about Jesus in the Jewish meeting place. He continued to tell the Jews that the Messiah they had been waiting for was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="EZEKIEL 33:4; 2 SAMUEL 1:16; EZEKIEL 33:8–9"> But the Jews began to oppose Paul and to say evil things about him. So he shook the dust from his clothes to show them that they were displeasing God. Then he said to them, “If God punishes you, it will be your &#91;SYN&#93; own fault &#91;MTY&#93;, not mine! From now on I will go and preach to non-Jewish people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ACTS 16:14; COLOSSIANS 4:11; ACTS 10:2"> So Paul left the Jewish meeting place and went into a house that was next to it, and preached there. Titius Justus, the owner of the house, was a non-Jewish man who had accepted what the Jews believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:13–17; ROMANS 10:14–17; MARK 16:15–16"> After that, the ruler of the Jewish meeting place, whose name was Crispus, and all of his family &#91;MTY&#93; believed in the Lord Jesus. Many other people in Corinth who listened to Paul also believed in Jesus. Then they were baptized. But there were people who still opposed Paul and his preaching.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus commanded Paul to continue speaking about him, so Paul did that.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ACTS 23:11; EPHESIANS 6:19–20; ACTS 27:23–25"> One night Paul had a vision in which the Lord Jesus said to him, “Do not be afraid of those who oppose you. Instead, you should continue speaking to people about me. Do not stop,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 12:9; EXODUS 4:12; Isaiah 41:10"> because I will help you, and no one will be able to harm you (sg) here. Keep telling people about me, because there are many in this city who will believe in me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ACTS 14:3; ACTS 19:10; ACTS 20:31"> So Paul stayed in Corinth for a year and a half, teaching people the message from God about Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Gallio refused to judge Paul.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ACTS 13:50; ACTS 18:27; ACTS 13:7"> When Gallio was the Roman governor of Achaia province, the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; there got together and seized Paul. They took him before Gallio and accused him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ACTS 25:8; ACTS 24:5–6; ACTS 6:13"> saying, “This man is teaching people a false religion, leading them to worship God in ways that are contrary to our Jewish laws.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 PETER 3:14–15; ACTS 25:11; MARK 9:19"> When Paul was about to speak &#91;MTY&#93; to defend himself, Gallio said to the Jews, “If this man had acted deceitfully or disobeyed any of our Roman laws, I would listen patiently to what you Jews want to tell me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 23:29; ACTS 25:19; 1 TIMOTHY 6:4"> However, you are merely arguing about words and names and your own Jewish laws, so you yourselves need to resolve this. I refuse to judge these things!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="REVELATION 12:16; PSALMS 76:10; ROMANS 13:3–4"> After Gallio had said that, he commanded some soldiers/guards that they expel those Jewish leaders from the courtroom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:1; AMOS 6:6; ACTS 17:32"> Then the mob outside grabbed the leader of the Jewish meeting place, Sosthenes because they considered that he was responsible for those accusations against Paul. They beat him, right there in front of the courthouse. But Gallio did nothing about it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## At Ephesus, Paul parted from Priscilla and Aquila.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 21:24; NUMBERS 6:18; NUMBERS 6:5–9"> Paul stayed on with the believers in Corinth for ◄many days/some time►. Then he left the believers there, and went with Priscilla and her husband Aquila. They went down to Cenchrea, a port city. There Paul had his head shaved &#123;someone shave his head&#125; in order to partially complete a vow that he had taken. Then they got on a ship and sailed for Syria province.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:1; 1 TIMOTHY 1:3; 2 TIMOTHY 1:18"> They arrived at Ephesus city, and Priscilla and Aquila stayed there. Before Paul left Ephesus, he entered the Jewish meeting place and lectured to the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="MARK 1:37–38; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:12; ACTS 20:16"> They asked him to stay longer, but he refused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 4:19; JAMES 4:15; ROMANS 15:32"> But, as he left, he told them, “I will come back, if God wills/desires me to do that.” Then, because he wanted to be in Jerusalem to finish completing his vow, he got on a ship that sailed from Ephesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul visited Jerusalem, then he went on to Antioch in Syria and then he returned to visit the believers throughout Galatia and Phrygia.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ACTS 8:40; ACTS 15:4; ACTS 23:23"> When the ship arrived at Caesarea, Paul got off. He went up to Jerusalem and greeted the believers there. Then he went back down to Antioch city in Syria province.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ACTS 14:22; ACTS 16:6; ACTS 15:41"> Paul spent some time with the believers there. Then he left Antioch and traveled to several towns that he had visited previously in Galatia and Phrygia provinces. He taught all of the believers more of the message from God about Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Priscilla and Aquila instructed Apollos about the gospel.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 4:6; TITUS 3:13; 1 CORINTHIANS 3:5–6"> While Paul was traveling in Galatia and Phrygia, a Jewish man whose name was Apollos came to Ephesus. He was a native of Alexandria city. He spoke eloquently and he knew the Scriptures thoroughly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ACTS 19:3; ROMANS 12:11; HOSEA 14:9"> Other believers had taught him some things about how the Lord Jesus desires that people should conduct themselves, and he taught those things very enthusiastically to people. He had heard about some of the things that Jesus did and said, and he taught those facts accurately to people. However, he was teaching incompletely about Jesus, because he knew only what John the Baptizer had taught people whom he baptized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ACTS 18:25; ACTS 14:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:21"> Apollos went to the Jewish meeting place, and he told the people there very confidently the things that he had learned. When Priscilla and Aquila heard what Apollos taught, they invited him to their home. There they explained more accurately to him the way that God gives people eternal life.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Apollos helped believers in Achaia.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 2:13–14; JAMES 1:16–18; 1 PETER 1:2–3"> When Apollos decided that he would like to go to Achaia province, the believers in Ephesus told him that it would be good for him to do that. So they wrote a letter to the believers in Achaia saying that they should welcome Apollos. So Apollos got on a ship to go to Corinth. After he arrived, he greatly helped those whom God had kindly enabled to believe in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ACTS 18:5; 1 CORINTHIANS 15:3–4; ACTS 9:22"> Apollos was vigorously arguing publicly with the leaders of the Jews while many other people listened. By quoting from the Scriptures, he proved to people that Jesus was the Messiah.</span>
</div>
</div>
