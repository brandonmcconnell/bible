## Paul and Barnabas preached the gospel and performed miracles in Iconium.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ACTS 18:4; ACTS 13:51; ACTS 13:46"> At Iconium Paul and Barnabas went as usual into the Jewish meeting place and spoke very convincingly about the Lord Jesus. As a result, many Jews and also non-Jews believed in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="ACTS 13:50; ACTS 21:27–30; ACTS 14:19"> But some of the Jews refused to believe that message, and told the non-Jews not to believe it. They told the non-Jewish people that the message about Jesus was not true. As a result, some of the non-Jews became angry towards the believers there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="HEBREWS 2:4; EPHESIANS 6:18–20; ACTS 20:32"> So Paul and Barnabas spent considerable time there speaking boldly for the Lord, and the Lord Jesus enabled them to do many miracles. In this way he showed people that the message about how the Lord saves us in a way that we do not deserve is true.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Before people could attack the apostles, they escaped to Lystra and Derbe.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ACTS 28:24; ACTS 14:14; LUKE 11:21–23"> The people who lived in Iconium city strongly disagreed with each other concerning the message about Jesus. Some of them agreed with the Jews &#91;SYN&#93; who did not believe that message. Others agreed with Paul and Barnabas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 14:19; 2 TIMOTHY 3:11; ACTS 17:5"> Then the non-Jewish people and the Jewish leaders &#91;SYN&#93; who opposed those two talked among themselves about how they could mistreat them. Some of the important men in that city agreed to help them. Together, they decided that they would kill Paul and Barnabas by throwing stones at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ACTS 14:20–21; MATTHEW 10:23; 2 TIMOTHY 3:11"> But Paul and Barnabas heard about that, so they quickly went away to Lycaonia district. They went to Lystra and Derbe cities in that district and to the area surrounding those cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ACTS 14:21; ACTS 16:10; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:2"> While they were in that area, they continually told people the message about the Lord Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God enabled Paul to heal a lame man.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ACTS 3:2; JOHN 9:1–2; JOHN 5:3–5"> Once while Paul was preaching to people in Lystra, a man was sitting there who was crippled in his legs. When his mother bore him he had crippled legs, so he was never able to walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ACTS 3:4; MATTHEW 15:28; MATTHEW 9:28–29"> He listened as Paul was speaking about the Lord Jesus. Paul looked directly at him and could see in the man’s face that he believed that the Lord Jesus could make him well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 35:6; JOHN 5:8–9; JOHN 14:12"> So Paul called out to him, “Stand up!” When the man heard that, he immediately jumped up and began to walk normally.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The apostles stopped the people from worshipping them.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ACTS 28:6; ACTS 8:10; ACTS 12:22"> When the crowd saw what Paul had done, they thought that Paul and Barnabas ◄were/must be► the gods that they worshipped. So they shouted excitedly in their own Lycaonia language, “Look! These people are the gods that we(inc) worship! They have made themselves look like people and have come down from the sky/heaven to help us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ACTS 19:35"> They began to say that Barnabas was probably the chief god, whose name was Zeus. And they began to say that Paul was Hermes, the messenger/spokesman for the other gods. They mistakenly thought that because Paul was the one who had been speaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="DANIEL 2:46; ACTS 10:25"> Just outside the gates of the city there was a temple where the people worshipped Zeus. The priest who was there heard what Paul and Barnabas had done, so he came to the city gate, where many people had already gathered. He brought two bulls with wreaths of flowers around their necks. The priest and the other people wanted to kill the bulls as part of a ceremony to worship Paul and Barnabas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="MATTHEW 26:65; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:5–6; 2 KINGS 5:7"> But when the apostles, Barnabas and Paul, heard about that, and understood that the people thought that they were gods and wanted to sacrifice the bulls to worship them, they were very distressed, so they tore their own clothes. They rushed among the people, shouting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 10:26; 1 CORINTHIANS 8:4; JAMES 5:17"> “Men, ◄you must not kill those bulls to worship us(dl)!/why are you doing this?► &#91;RHQ&#93; We are not gods! We are just human beings like you! We have come to tell you some good news! We have come to tell you about the God who is all-powerful. He wants you to stop worshipping other gods, because they cannot help you. This true God made the heaven/sky and the earth and the oceans and everything in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ACTS 17:30; PSALMS 81:12; ROMANS 1:28"> In the past, all of you non-Jewish people worshipped whatever gods that you wanted to. God let you worship them, because you did not know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="ROMANS 1:19–20; JOEL 2:23; JOB 5:10"> But he has shown us &#91;LIT&#93; that he acts kindly toward us(inc). He is the one who causes it to rain and causes crops to grow. He is the one who gives you plenty of food, and makes you very happy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="JEREMIAH 44:16–17; GENESIS 19:9; GENESIS 11:6"> The people heard what Paul said, but they still thought that they should sacrifice those bulls to worship Paul and Barnabas. But finally, the people decided not to do it.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Some Jews incited people to stone Paul at Lystra.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 11:25; 2 TIMOTHY 3:11; ACTS 13:50–51"> However, after that, some Jews came from Antioch and Iconium and persuaded many of the people of Lystra that the message Paul had been telling them was not true. The people who believed what those Jews said became angry with Paul. They let the Jews throw stones at him until he fell down, unconscious. They all thought that he was dead, so they dragged him outside the city and left him lying there. But some of the believers in Lystra had followed them outside the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 1:9–10; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:9; ACTS 14:6"> They came and stood around Paul, where he was lying on the ground. And Paul became conscious! He stood up and went back into the city with the believers. The apostles revisited believers and appointed leaders. Acts 14:20b-23<br />The next day, Paul and Barnabas left Lystra and traveled to Derbe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ACTS 14:19; ACTS 14:1; ACTS 13:51"> They stayed there several days and they kept telling the people the good message about Jesus. Many people became believers. After that, Paul and Barnabas started on their way back. They went again to Lystra. Then they went from there to Iconium, and then they went to Antioch in Pisidia province.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 PETER 5:10; 2 TIMOTHY 3:12; JOHN 16:33"> In each city, they helped the believers &#91;SYN&#93; to become strong spiritually, and they urged them to keep on trusting in the Lord Jesus. They told the believers, “It is necessary that we endure people often persecuting us (inc) before we enter the place where God will rule over us forever.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="TITUS 1:5; ACTS 11:30; JAMES 5:14"> Paul and Barnabas appointed leaders for each congregation. And before Paul and Barnabas left each town, they gathered the believers together and spent some time praying and ◄fasting/not eating any food►. Then Paul and Barnabas entrusted the leaders and other believers to the Lord Jesus, in whom they had believed, in order that he would care for them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul and Barnabas reported to the believers at Syrian Antioch.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ACTS 13:13–14; ACTS 15:38"> After Paul and Barnabas had traveled through Pisidia district, they went south to Pamphylia district.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> In that district, they arrived at Perga and preached God’s message about the Lord Jesus to the people there. Then they went down to the seacoast at Attalia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ACTS 15:40; ACTS 11:19; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:12"> There they got on a ship and went back to Antioch in Syria province. That was the place where Paul and Barnabas had been appointed &#123;where believers had sent Paul and Barnabas&#125; to go to other places and preach. Antioch was the place where the believers had asked God to kindly help Paul and Barnabas in the work that they had now completed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 16:9; ACTS 15:12; COLOSSIANS 4:3"> When they arrived in Antioch, they called the believers together. Then Paul and Barnabas told them all that God had helped them to do. Specifically, they told them how God had enabled &#91;IDM&#93; many non-Jewish people to believe in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="ACTS 11:26; ACTS 15:35"> Then Paul and Barnabas stayed in Antioch with the other believers for several months.</span>
</div>
</div>
