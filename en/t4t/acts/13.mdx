<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ACTS 4:36; ACTS 15:35; ACTS 11:22–27"> Among the people in the congregation at Antioch there were ◄prophets/those who spoke messages from God► and those who taught people about Jesus. They were Barnabas; Simeon, who was also called Niger/Blackman; Lucius, from Cyrene city; Manaen, who had grown up with King Herod Antipas; and Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="GALATIANS 1:15; ACTS 9:15; 1 CORINTHIANS 12:11"> While they were worshipping the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said to them, “Appoint Barnabas and Saul to serve me and to go and do the work that I have chosen them to do!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ACTS 6:6; 2 TIMOTHY 2:2; ACTS 13:2"> So they continued ◄to fast/to abstain from eating food► and pray. ## Barnabas and Saul preached the gospel in Jewish meetings place on Cyprus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ACTS 4:36; ACTS 13:2; ACTS 11:19"> Barnabas and Saul, guided by the Holy Spirit, went down from Antioch to Seleucia port. From there they went by ship to Salamis port on Cyprus Island.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 13:14; ACTS 13:46; ACTS 14:1"> While they were in Salamis, they went to the Jewish meeting places. There they proclaimed the message from God about Jesus. John Mark went with them and was helping them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Saul-Paul denounced a sorcerer, and an official believed in Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:15; 1 JOHN 4:1; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:13"> The three of them went across the entire island to Paphos city. There they met a magician whose name was Bar-Jesus. He was a Jew who falsely claimed ◄to be a prophet/to speak messages from God►.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ACTS 19:38; ACTS 13:12; ACTS 18:12"> He often accompanied the governor of the island, Sergius Paulus, who was an intelligent man. The governor sent someone to ask Barnabas and Saul to come to him, because he wanted to hear God’s message. So Barnabas and Saul came and told him about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 3:8; ACTS 13:6–7; 2 TIMOTHY 4:14–15"> However, the magician, whose name was Elymas in the Greek language, was opposing them. He repeatedly tried to persuade the governor not to believe in Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MICAH 3:8; ACTS 4:8; ACTS 2:4"> Then Saul, who now called himself Paul, empowered by the Holy Spirit, looked intently at the magician and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="JOHN 8:44; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:3; MATTHEW 13:38"> “You (sg) are serving the devil and you oppose everything that is good! <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 10:4–5; ACTS 13:7; LUKE 4:22"> When the governor saw what had happened to Elymas, he believed in the Lord Jesus. He was amazed by what Paul and Barnabas were teaching about the Lord Jesus.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ACTS 16:13; ACTS 14:19; ACTS 13:5"> Then Paul and Barnabas traveled by land from Perga, and arrived in Antioch city near Pisidia district in Galatia province. ◄On the Sabbath/On the Jewish rest day► they entered the synagogue/the Jewish meeting place and sat down.</span>

## Paul said that God helped Israel and gave them the land of Canaan.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ACTS 12:17; ACTS 13:26; ACTS 10:2"> So Paul stood up and motioned with his right hand so that the people would listen to him. Then he said, “Fellow Israelis and you non-Jewish people who also worship God, please listen to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:6–8; Isaiah 63:9–14; PSALMS 105:23–24"> God, whom we (inc) Israelis worship, chose our ancestors to be his people, and he caused them to become very numerous while they were foreigners living in Egypt. Then after many years, God helped them &#91;MTY&#93; powerfully and led them out of there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 7:36; PSALMS 95:8–11; PSALMS 106:13–29"> Even though they repeatedly disobeyed him, he cared for them for about 40 years while they were in the desert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="DEUTERONOMY 7:1; PSALMS 78:55; ACTS 7:45"> He enabled the Israelis to conquer seven tribal groups who were then living in Canaan region, and he gave their land to us Israelis for us to possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JUDGES 2:16; 1 SAMUEL 3:20; 2 KINGS 23:22"> Our ancestors began to possess Canaan about 450 years after their ancestors had arrived in Egypt.” Paul told about God’s removing Saul and appointing David to be Israel’s king. <span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 SAMUEL 10:1; 1 SAMUEL 15:1; 1 SAMUEL 12:12–19"> Then, while Samuel was still their leader, the people demanded that he appoint a king to rule them. ## Paul said that God sent David’s descendant, Jesus, to Israel to save them.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="MATTHEW 1:1; PSALMS 132:11; REVELATION 22:16"> “From among David’s descendants, God brought one of them, Jesus, to us Israeli people to save us, just like he had told David and our other ancestors that he would do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ACTS 1:22; JOHN 1:6–8; LUKE 3:2–20"> Before Jesus began his work, John the Baptizer preached to all of our Israeli people who came to him. <span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="MATTHEW 3:11; MARK 1:7; JOHN 1:26–27"> When John was about to finish the work that God gave him to do, he frequently said to the people, ‘Do you think &#91;RHQ&#93; that I am the Messiah whom God promised to send? <div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="LUKE 1:77; ROMANS 1:16; LUKE 1:69"> “Fellow Israelis, you who are descendants of Abraham, and you non-Jewish people who also worship God, please listen! It is to all of us that God has sent the message about how he saves people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ACTS 3:17; LUKE 24:24–27; LUKE 24:20"> The people who were living in Jerusalem and their rulers did not realize that this man Jesus was the one whom God had sent to save them. <span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="LUKE 23:21–25; LUKE 23:14–16; JOHN 19:4"> Many people accused Jesus of doing wicked things, but they could not prove that he had done anything for which he deserved to die. <span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="LUKE 23:53; JOHN 19:28; JOHN 19:30"> They did to Jesus all the things that the prophets long ago had written that people would do to him. <span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="PSALMS 2:7; HEBREWS 5:5; HEBREWS 1:5–6"> He has now done that for us (inc) who are their descendants, and also for you who are not Jews, by causing Jesus to live again.
