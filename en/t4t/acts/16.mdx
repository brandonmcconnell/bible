## Paul chose Timothy to work with him and Silas.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 1:5; 2 TIMOTHY 1:2; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:17"> Paul and Silas went to Derbe city and visited the believers there. Next they went to Lystra city. A believer whose name was Timothy lived there. His mother was a Jewish believer, but his father was a Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 5:10; 2 TIMOTHY 3:15; ACTS 14:21"> The believers in Lystra and Iconium said good things about Timothy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:20; GALATIANS 5:6"> and Paul wanted to take Timothy with him when he went to other places, so he circumcised Timothy. He did that so that the Jews who lived in those places would accept Timothy, because they knew that his non-Jewish father had not allowed him to be circumcised &#123;anyone to circumcise his son&#125;.</span>

## Paul, Silas and Timothy told believers in Galatia what the church leaders had decided.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ACTS 15:28–29; ACTS 15:2; ACTS 11:30"> So Timothy went with Paul and Silas and they traveled to many other towns. In each town they told the believers the rules that had been decided by the apostles and elders in Jerusalem &#123;that the apostles and elders in Jerusalem had decided&#125; that non-Jewish believers should obey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 9:31; ACTS 2:47; ACTS 6:7"> God was helping the believers in those towns to trust more strongly in the Lord Jesus, and every day more people became believers.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Because of a vision in Troas, Paul went to preach in Macedonia.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ACTS 18:23; ACTS 16:7; ACTS 13:2–4"> Paul and his companions wanted/planned to enter Asia province and preach the message about Jesus there, but they were prevented by the Holy Spirit &#123;the Holy Spirit prevented them&#125; from going there. So they traveled through Phrygia and Galatia provinces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="ROMANS 8:9; PHILIPPIANS 1:19; GALATIANS 4:6"> They arrived at the border of Mysia province and they wanted to go north into Bithynia province. But again the Spirit of Jesus showed them that they should not go there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 2:12; 2 TIMOTHY 4:13; ACTS 16:11"> So they went through Mysia province and arrived at Troas, a port city. I, Luke, joined them there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="ACTS 9:10–12; ACTS 19:21; ACTS 18:5"> That night God gave Paul a vision in which he saw a man who was a native of Macedonia province. He was standing some distance away, and he was earnestly calling to Paul, “Please come over here to Macedonia and help us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 2:12–13; ACTS 14:7; ACTS 16:10–17"> The next morning we (exc) immediately got ready to go to Macedonia, because we believed that God had called us to go and preach the good message to the people there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s company went from Troas to Philippi.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ACTS 21:1; ACTS 16:8"> So we (exc) got on a ship in Troas and sailed across the sea to Samothrace Island. We spent the night there, and the next day we sailed again across the sea and arrived at Neapolis port/town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:2; PHILIPPIANS 1:1; ACTS 20:6"> Then we left Neapolis and went by land to Philippi. It was a very important city in Macedonia province, where many Roman citizens lived. We stayed in Philippi several days.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul preached the gospel and Lydia became a believer.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ACTS 13:14; COLOSSIANS 1:23; ACTS 17:2"> On the first ◄Sabbath/Jewish day of rest► after we(exc) arrived, we went outside the city gate down to the river. We had heard someone say that Jewish people gathered to pray there. When we arrived there, we saw some women who had gathered to pray. So we sat down and began to tell them the message about Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="EPHESIANS 1:17–18; LUKE 24:45; Isaiah 50:5"> A woman whose name was Lydia was one of those who were listening to Paul. She was a non-Jewish woman, from Thyatira city, who bought and sold expensive purple cloth. She had accepted what the Jews believe about God. The Lord God caused her to pay attention to the message that Paul preached, and she believed it. The members of her household also heard the good message and believed in Jesus &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 11:14; HEBREWS 13:2; ACTS 16:33"> After Paul and Silas baptized Lydia and the others who lived in her house &#91;MTY&#93; &#123;After Lydia and the others who lived in her house were baptized&#125;, she invited us to go and stay in her home. She said, “You (pl) know that I now believe in the Lord Jesus, so please come and stay in my house.” She persuaded us to do that, so we(exc) stayed there.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul expelled a demon from a slave girl, so officials imprisoned Paul and Silas.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LEVITICUS 19:31; DEUTERONOMY 18:9–11; Isaiah 8:19"> Another day, while we (exc) were going to the place where people regularly gathered to pray, we met a young woman who was a slave. An evil spirit was enabling her to be a ventriloquist and to tell people what would happen to them. People paid a lot of money to the men who were her owners, in return for her telling them things that she said would happen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="JOHN 14:6; 1 PETER 2:16; MARK 5:7"> This young woman followed Paul and the rest of us. She continually shouted, “These men serve the God who is the greatest of all gods! They are telling you how ◄God can save you so that he will not punish you/to be saved►”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="MARK 16:17; LUKE 10:17–19; ACTS 3:6"> She continued to do that for many days. Finally Paul became irritated. So he turned toward the young woman and rebuked the evil spirit that was in her. He said, “By the authority &#91;MTY&#93; of Jesus Christ, I command you (sg) to come out of this young woman!” Right away the evil spirit left her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ACTS 17:6; ACTS 21:30; ACTS 8:3"> And then her owners realized that she could no longer earn money for them because she could no longer predict what would happen to people, so they were angry. They grabbed Paul and Silas and forcefully took them to the public square, to the place where the government authorities and a lot of other people were gathered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="JAMES 4:4; JOHN 15:18–20; ACTS 28:22"> The owners of the young woman brought Paul and Silas to the city officials and told them, “These men are Jews, and they are greatly troubling the people in &#91;MTY&#93; our city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 3:8; ACTS 26:3; JEREMIAH 10:3"> They are teaching that we (inc) should follow customs that our laws do not allow us Romans to consider to be correct or to obey!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:2; 2 CORINTHIANS 6:5; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:23–25"> Many of the crowd joined those who were accusing Paul and Silas, and started beating them. Then the Roman authorities commanded soldiers to tear the shirts off Paul and Silas and to beat them with rods/sticks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="REVELATION 2:10; 2 TIMOTHY 2:9; 1 SAMUEL 23:22–23"> So the soldiers beat Paul and Silas vigorously with rods. After that, they took them and shoved them into the prison. They told the jailer that he should lock them up securely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="JOB 13:27; JOB 33:11; JEREMIAH 29:26"> Because the officials had commanded him to do that, the jailer shoved Paul and Silas into the cell that was farthest inside. There, he made them sit down on the floor/ground and stretch out their legs. Then he fastened their ankles in grooves between two large wooden beams, so that Paul and Silas could not move their legs.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul and Silas helped the jailer and his household to become believers.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:16–18; PSALMS 119:62; JAMES 1:2"> About midnight, Paul and Silas were praying aloud and praising God by singing hymns. The other prisoners were listening attentively to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ACTS 4:31; ACTS 5:19; ACTS 12:10"> Suddenly there was a very strong earthquake. It shook the entire jail &#91;SYN&#93; and its foundation &#91;SYN&#93;. The earthquake caused all the doors of the jail to open suddenly, and caused all the chains that fastened the prisoners to fall off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ACTS 12:19; MATTHEW 27:5; JUDGES 9:54"> The jailer woke up and saw that the doors of the jail were open. He thought that the prisoners had escaped. So he pulled out his sword in order to kill himself, because he knew that the officials would kill him if the prisoners escaped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 5:15; LUKE 23:34; LUKE 6:27–28"> Paul saw the jailer and shouted to him, “Do not harm yourself! We (exc) prisoners are all here!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="REVELATION 3:9; ACTS 24:25; Isaiah 60:14"> The jailer shouted to someone to bring torches/lanterns, and after they brought them, he rushed into the jail and knelt down in front of Paul and Silas. He was very afraid, so much so that he was trembling/shaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="ACTS 2:37; JOHN 6:27–29; Isaiah 58:9"> Then he brought Paul and Silas out of the jail and asked: “Sirs, what do I need to do to be saved from being punished for my sins?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="JOHN 1:12; ACTS 2:38–39; JOHN 3:36"> They answered, “Trust in what the Lord Jesus has done for you, and you will be saved &#123;God will save you&#125;, and the others who live in &#91;MTY&#93; your house will also be saved if they believe in Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="ROMANS 1:16; COLOSSIANS 1:27–28; 1 TIMOTHY 1:13–16"> Then the jailer took Paul and Silas into his house, washed their wounds, and gave them a meal. He woke up all the people in his house, and Paul and Silas told all of them the message about the Lord Jesus. They all believed in him. Immediately after that, the jailer and all his family were baptized &#123;Paul and Silas baptized the jailer and all his family&#125;. They were very happy, because now they all believed in God.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul and Silas encouraged the believers and then left Philippi.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="JEREMIAH 5:22; PSALMS 76:10; ACTS 5:40"> The next morning, the Roman officials commanded some police officers to go to the jail to say to the jailer, “Our bosses say, ‘Let those two prisoners go now!’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ACTS 15:33; JOHN 14:27; 1 SAMUEL 1:17"> After the officers went and told that to the jailer, he went and told Paul, “The Roman authorities have sent a message (sg) saying that I should release you (sg) and Silas from prison. So you two can leave the jail now. Now you can go peacefully!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="ACTS 22:25–29; PSALMS 58:1–2; MATTHEW 10:16"> But Paul said to the police officers, “The authorities commanded men to beat us in front of a crowd before those authorities had learned if we (exc) had done anything wrong! Then they ordered men to shove us into jail! But that was not legal, because we (exc) are Roman citizens! And now they want &#91;RHQ&#93; to send us away secretly! We will not accept that! Those Roman officials must come themselves and tell us that they are sorry, and take us out of jail.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ACTS 22:29; MATTHEW 21:46; MATTHEW 14:5"> So the police officers went and told the city authorities what Paul had said. When those authorities heard that Paul and Silas were Roman citizens, they were afraid that someone would report to more important officials what they had done, and as a result they would be punished &#123;those officials would punish them&#125;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="MATTHEW 8:34; MARK 5:17; DANIEL 6:23"> So the city authorities came to Paul and Silas and told them that they were sorry for what they had done to them. The authorities brought them out of the jail, and repeatedly asked them to leave the city soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 3:2–3; ACTS 16:14; ACTS 14:22"> After Paul and Silas left the jail, they went to Lydia’s house. There they met with her and the other believers. They encouraged the believers to continue trusting in the Lord Jesus, and then the two apostles left Philippi.</span>
</div>
