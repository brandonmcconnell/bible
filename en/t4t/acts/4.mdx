## The Jewish leaders realized that Peter and John had been associating with Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 1:27; JOHN 7:15; MATTHEW 11:25"> The Jewish leaders realized that Peter and John ◄were not afraid of them/spoke boldly►. <div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="ACTS 26:30–32; ACTS 5:34–42; MATTHEW 5:22"> So the Jewish leaders commanded guards to take Peter, John, and the man outside of the room where those leaders were meeting. <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="LUKE 21:15; ACTS 6:10; JOHN 12:18"> Being frustrated, one after another, they said, “◄There is really nothing that we can do to punish these two men!/How can we (inc) do anything to punish these two men?► &#91;RHQ&#93; Almost everyone &#91;HYP&#93; who is living in Jerusalem knows that they have done an amazing miracle, so we cannot tell people that it did not happen!</span>

## Peter and John said that they needed to continue speaking about Jesus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ACTS 5:29; EXODUS 1:17; 1 CORINTHIANS 10:15"> But Peter and John replied, “Would God think that it is right for us two to obey you and not to obey him? We(exc) will let you decide which of those you think is proper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ACTS 22:15; JEREMIAH 20:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:16–17"> But as for us, we cannot obey you. We will not stop telling people about the things that we (exc) have seen Jesus do and what we have heard him teach.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Jewish leaders threatened to punish Peter and John and then released them.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ACTS 5:26; MATTHEW 21:46; LUKE 22:2"> Then the Jewish leaders again told Peter and John not to disobey them. But all the people there were praising God about what had happened to the lame man. The leaders knew that only God could have enabled Peter and John to miraculously heal the man, because the man was more than 40 years old and he was lame when his mother bore him. They also knew that the people would become angry if they punished the two apostles. So, because they could not decide how to punish Peter and John, they finally let them go.</span>
</div>
