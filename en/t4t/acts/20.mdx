## After being in Macedonia and Greece, Paul went to Troas.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 7:5; ACTS 19:21; 1 TIMOTHY 1:3"> After the people at Ephesus had stopped rioting, Paul summoned the believers. He encouraged them to continue to trust in the Lord Jesus. Soon after that, he told them goodbye and left to go to Macedonia province.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:3; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:1; COLOSSIANS 1:28"> After he arrived there, he visited each town where there were believers, and encouraged them. Then he arrived in Greece province, which is also called Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ACTS 20:19; 2 CORINTHIANS 11:26; ACTS 9:23–24"> He stayed there for three months. Then he planned to return to Syria by ship, but he heard that some of the Jews &#91;SYN&#93; in that area were planning to kill him as he traveled. So he decided instead to go by land, and he traveled again through Macedonia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="ACTS 19:29; EPHESIANS 6:21; 2 TIMOTHY 4:20"> The men who were going to travel with him to Jerusalem were Sopater, who was a son of Pyrrhus, who grew up in Berea town; Aristarchus and Secundus, who were from Thessalonica city; Gaius, who was from Derbe town; Timothy, who was from Galatia province; and Tychicus and Trophimus who were from Asia province.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 16:8; ACTS 16:10–11; 2 TIMOTHY 4:13"> Those seven men went ahead of Paul and me, Luke, by ship from Macedonia, so they got to Troas before we did and waited for the two of us there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ACTS 12:3; ACTS 16:12; EXODUS 23:15"> But we two(exc) traveled by land as far as Philippi city. After the Jewish festival when they eat unleavened bread, we got on a ship that was going from the port near Philippi to Troas city. After five days we (exc) arrived at Troas and we met the other men who had traveled there ahead of us. Then we all stayed in Troas for seven days.</span>
</div>
</div>

## At Troas, Paul encouraged believers by resurrecting Eutychus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 16:2; ACTS 2:42; JOHN 20:19"> ◄On Sunday evening/On the evening of the first day of the week►, we (exc) and the other believers there gathered together to celebrate the Lord’s Supper and to eat other food &#91;SYN&#93;. Paul spoke to the believers. He continued teaching them until midnight, because he was planning to leave Troas the next day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ACTS 1:13; LUKE 22:12"> Many oil lamps were burning in the upstairs room in which we (exc) had gathered, so the fumes caused some people to become sleepy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="MARK 13:36; MATTHEW 26:40–41; JONAH 1:5–6"> A young man whose name was Eutychus was there. He was seated on the sill of an open window on the third story of the house. As Paul continued talking for a long time, Eutychus became sleepier and sleepier. Finally, he was sound/really asleep. He fell out of the window from the third story down to the ground. Some of the believers went down immediately and picked him up. But he was dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="MARK 5:39; MATTHEW 9:23–24; 1 KINGS 17:21–22"> Paul also went down. He lay down and stretched out on top of the young man and put his arms around him. Then he said to the people who were standing around, “Do not worry, he is alive again now!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ACTS 20:7; ACTS 20:9"> Then Paul, along with the others, went upstairs again and they ate the Lord’s Supper and other food &#91;SYN&#93;. Afterwards, Paul conversed with the believers until dawn. Then he left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 THESSALONIANS 2:16; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:14; 2 CORINTHIANS 1:4"> The other people took the young man home, and were greatly encouraged because he was alive again.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul and his companions traveled from Troas to Miletus.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="MARK 6:46; MARK 1:35; MARK 6:31–33"> We then went to the ship. Paul did not get on the ship with us in Troas, because he preferred to go more quickly overland to Assos town. The rest of us got on the ship and sailed for Assos.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> We (exc) met Paul in Assos. He got on the ship with us, and we sailed to Mitylene town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:20; ACTS 20:17"> The day after we reached Mitylene, we sailed from there and arrived at a place near Kios Island. The day after that, we sailed to Samos Island. The next day we left Samos and sailed to Miletus town.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 CORINTHIANS 16:8; ACTS 2:1; ACTS 19:21"> Miletus was just south of Ephesus city. Paul had earlier decided that he would not get on a ship that would stop at Ephesus, because he did not want to spend several days in Asia province. If possible, he wanted to arrive in Jerusalem by the time of the Pentecost festival, and the time of that festival was near.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul reminded the Ephesian elders how he had conducted himself and had taught them.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="TITUS 1:5; ACTS 11:30; 1 TIMOTHY 5:17"> When the ship arrived at Miletus, Paul sent a messenger to Ephesus to ask the elders of the congregation to come to talk with him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 1:5–6; ACTS 18:19; ACTS 19:1"> When the elders arrived, Paul said to them, “You personally know how I conducted myself among you the entire time that I was with you, from the first day when I arrived here in Asia province until the day I left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 3:24; 2 CORINTHIANS 2:4; ROMANS 12:11"> You know how I was serving the Lord Jesus very humbly and how I sometimes wept about people. You also know how I suffered because the Jews &#91;SYN&#93; who were not believers often tried to harm me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="ACTS 20:27; 2 TIMOTHY 4:2; ACTS 5:42"> You also know that, as I preached God’s message to you, I never left out anything that would help you. You know that I taught you God’s message when many people were present, and I also went to your homes and taught it there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ACTS 2:38; ACTS 11:18; 1 JOHN 5:11–13"> I preached both to Jews and to non-Jews, telling them all that they must turn away from their sinful behavior. I also told them they should believe in our Lord Jesus.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul told them that they would never see him again.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="JAMES 4:14; 2 CORINTHIANS 5:14; 2 PETER 1:14"> “And now note this: I am going to Jerusalem, because God’s Spirit has clearly shown me that I must go there. I do not know what will happen to me while I am there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ACTS 9:16; 1 THESSALONIANS 3:3; ACTS 21:4"> But I do know that in each city where I have stopped, the Holy Spirit has ◄told me/caused the believers to tell me► that in Jerusalem people will put me in prison &#91;PRS&#93; and will cause me to suffer &#91;PRS&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 4:6–8; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:1; 2 CORINTHIANS 12:10"> But I do not care even if people kill me, if first I am able to finish the work &#91;MET&#93; that the Lord Jesus has told me to do. He appointed me to tell people the good message that God saves us by doing for us what we do not deserve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="ACTS 28:31; ACTS 20:38; MATTHEW 4:23"> I have preached to you the message about how God desires to rule people’s lives. But now I know that today is the last time that you fellow believers will see me &#91;SYN&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="ACTS 18:6; EZEKIEL 3:18–21; EZEKIEL 33:2–9"> So I want you all to understand that if anyone who has heard me preach dies without trusting in Jesus, it is not my fault &#91;MTY&#93;,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ACTS 20:20; 1 THESSALONIANS 2:4; GALATIANS 1:7–10"> because I told you &#91;LIT&#93; everything &#91;HYP&#93; that God has planned for us (inc).</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 PETER 5:2–3; 1 TIMOTHY 4:16; Isaiah 40:11"> You leaders must continue to believe and obey God’s message. You must also help all the other believers &#91;MET&#93; for whom the Holy Spirit has caused you to be responsible &#91;MTY&#93;. Watch over &#91;MET&#93; yourselves and the other believers as a shepherd watches over his sheep. God bought them with the blood that flowed from his Son’s body on the cross.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="MATTHEW 7:15; 2 PETER 2:1; JOHN 10:12"> I know very well that after I leave, people who teach &#91;MET&#93; false doctrines will come among you and will do great harm to the believers. They will be like fierce wolves that kill sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 1:19–20; 2 TIMOTHY 4:3–4; PROVERBS 23:33"> Even in your own group of believers there will be some who will deceive other believers by teaching them messages that are false. They will teach those messages so that some people will believe them and will become their followers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="ACTS 19:8; ACTS 20:19; ACTS 19:10"> So watch out that none of you stops believing the true message about our Lord Jesus. Remember that day and night for about three years I repeatedly taught you that message, and warned you with tears in my eyes not to believe any other message.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul entrusted them to God and to the message about God’s goodness.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="COLOSSIANS 1:12; EPHESIANS 1:14; COLOSSIANS 3:24"> “Now as I leave you, I ask God to protect you and to keep you believing the message that he saves us(inc) by doing for us what we do not deserve. If you continue believing the message that I told you, you will become spiritually mature, and God will give you the blessings that he has promised to give to all of those who belong to him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 CORINTHIANS 11:9; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:12; 1 CORINTHIANS 9:18"> As for myself, I have not desired anyone’s money &#91;MTY&#93; or fine clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="ACTS 18:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 4:12; 2 THESSALONIANS 3:8–9"> You yourselves know that I have worked with my hands &#91;MTY&#93; to earn the money that my companions and I needed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="HEBREWS 13:16; PROVERBS 19:17; 2 CORINTHIANS 9:6–12"> In everything that I did, I showed you that we (inc) should work hard in order to have enough money to give some to those who are needy. We (inc) should remember that our Lord Jesus himself said, ‘You are happy when people give you what you need, but God will be happy with you when you give other people what they need.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul prayed with them and they sadly told him goodbye.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="ACTS 21:5; LUKE 22:41; PHILIPPIANS 4:6"> When Paul had finished speaking, he knelt down with all of the elders and prayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 TIMOTHY 1:4; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:20; 1 THESSALONIANS 5:26"> They all cried a lot, and they hugged Paul and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="ACTS 15:3; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:11; ACTS 20:25"> They were especially sad because he had said that they would never see him &#91;SYN&#93; again. Then they all went with him to the ship.</span>
</div>
</div>
