## Paul defended himself, and the governor Felix promised to judge him.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="ACTS 24:11; ACTS 23:2; ACTS 25:2"> Five days later Ananias the high priest, having heard that Paul was now in Caesarea, went down there from Jerusalem, along with some other Jewish elders and a lawyer whose name was Tertullus. There they formally told the governor what Paul had done that they considered wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="PROVERBS 29:5; PROVERBS 26:28; ACTS 24:26–27"> The governor commanded Paul to be brought &#123;a soldier to bring Paul&#125; in. When Paul arrived, Tertullus began to accuse him. He said to the governor, “Honorable Governor Felix, during the many years that you (sg) have ruled us, we (exc) have lived well/peacefully. By planning wisely, you have improved many things in this province.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="ACTS 23:26; ACTS 26:25; LUKE 1:3"> Therefore, sir, we (exc) always gratefully acknowledge everything that you have done for all of us, wherever you have done those things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="HEBREWS 11:32"> But, so that I will not take up too much of your time, I earnestly request that you kindly listen to me very briefly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="ACTS 28:22; ACTS 24:14; ACTS 26:5"> We (exc) have observed that this man, wherever he goes, causes trouble. Specifically, he causes all the Jews everywhere &#91;HYP&#93; to riot. Also, he leads the entire group whom people call ‘the followers of the Nazarene’, a false sect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="ACTS 24:12; ACTS 21:27–32; JOHN 18:31"> He even tried to do things in the Temple in Jerusalem that would ◄defile it/make it unholy►. So we (exc) seized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> But Lysias, the commander at the Roman fort, came with his soldiers and forcefully took him away from us &#91;SYN&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="ACTS 23:35; ACTS 25:15–16; ACTS 25:5"> Lysias also commanded Paul’s accusers to come here and accuse Paul before you. If you question him yourself, you will be able to learn that all these things about which we are accusing him are true.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 THESSALONIANS 2:16; MICAH 7:2–3; PSALMS 4:2"> When the Jewish leaders who were listening heard that, they told the governor that what Tertullus had said was true.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul disproved what they had said, but admitted that he was a Christian.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 PETER 3:15; 1 SAMUEL 2:25; LUKE 18:2"> Then the governor motioned with his hand to Paul that he should speak. So Paul replied. He said, “Governor Felix, I know that you(sg) have judged this Jewish province for many years. Therefore I gladly defend myself, confident that you will listen to me and will judge me fairly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="ACTS 24:1; ACTS 21:18; ACTS 23:11"> You (sg) can easily ascertain that ◄it has not been more than twelve days since/only twelve days ago► I went up went up to Jerusalem to worship God. That is not enough time to cause a lot of trouble.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="ACTS 25:8; ACTS 28:17; ACTS 24:18"> No one can claim legitimately that they saw me arguing with anyone at the Temple courts because I did not do that. No one can claim legitimately that they saw me causing people to riot in any Jewish meeting place, or causing trouble anywhere else in Jerusalem city, because I did not do that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="ACTS 25:7; 1 PETER 3:16"> So they cannot prove to you the things about which they are now accusing me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="ACTS 9:2; ROMANS 3:21; ACTS 3:13"> But I admit to you (sg) that this is true: I do worship the God that our ancestors worshipped. It is true that I follow the way that Jesus taught us. The Jewish leaders call that a false religion/teaching. I also believe everything that was written by Moses &#123;that Moses wrote&#125; in the laws that God gave him, and everything that was written by the other prophets &#123;that the other prophets wrote&#125; in their books &#91;MTY&#93;.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="DANIEL 12:2; JOHN 5:28–29; 1 THESSALONIANS 4:14–16"> I confidently expect, just like some of these men also expect, that some day God will cause everyone who has died to become alive again. He will ◄cause to become alive again/raise from the dead► both those who were righteous and those who were wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="ACTS 23:1; 1 TIMOTHY 1:5; 2 CORINTHIANS 4:2"> Because I am confidently waiting for that day, I always try to do what pleases God and what other people think is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="GALATIANS 2:10; ROMANS 15:25–28; 1 CORINTHIANS 16:1–4"> After I had been in other places for several years, I returned to Jerusalem. I went there to deliver some money to my fellow Jews who are poor, and to offer sacrifices to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="ACTS 26:21; ACTS 21:26–30; ACTS 24:12"> Some Jews saw me in the temple courts after I had completed the ritual by which a person is made &#123;that makes a person&#125; pure. There was no crowd with me, and I was not causing people to riot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="ACTS 23:30; ACTS 25:16"> But it was some other Jews who had come from Asia province who really caused people to riot. They should be here in front of you (sg) to accuse me, if they thought that I did something wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But if they do not want to do that (OR, But because they are not here), these Jewish men who are here should tell you (sg) what they think I did that was wrong, when I defended myself before their Council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="ACTS 23:6; ACTS 4:2; ACTS 26:6–8"> They might say that one thing that I shouted as I stood before them was wrong. What I said was, ‘You are judging me today because I believe that God will ◄cause all people who have died to become alive again/raise all people from the dead►.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Governor Felix adjourned the trial.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="ACTS 24:6; ACTS 24:24; ACTS 25:26"> Felix already knew quite a lot about what people called ‘the way of Jesus’. But he did not let Paul or his accusers continue to speak. Instead, he said to them, “Later, when Commander Lysias comes down here, I will decide these matters that concern you all.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="ACTS 28:16; ACTS 27:3; PROVERBS 16:7"> Then he told the officer who was guarding Paul to take Paul back to the prison and make sure that he was guarded all the time. But he said that Paul was not to be chained &#123;that the officer was not to fasten chains on him&#125;, and if his friends came to visit him, the officer should allow them to help Paul in any way that they wished.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Felix often talked to Paul, hoping that Paul would give him money.

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="ACTS 20:21; GALATIANS 2:16; MARK 6:20"> Several days later Felix and his wife Drusilla, who was a Jew, came back to Caesarea after having been away for a few days. Felix commanded Paul to be brought in &#123;a soldier to bring Paul in&#125;. Then Felix listened to what Paul said to him. Paul spoke about what Christians believe about the Messiah Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="JOHN 16:8; 1 PETER 3:15; ACTS 10:42"> Paul explained to them about what God requires people to do in order to please him. He also explained about God requiring people to control how they act. Paul also told him that there will be a time when God will judge people. Felix became alarmed after hearing those things. So he said to Paul, “That is all I want to hear now. When there is a time that is convenient I will ask you (sg) to come to me again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 TIMOTHY 6:9–10; PROVERBS 17:23; Isaiah 56:11"> Felix said that because he hoped that Paul would give him some money to allow Paul to get out of prison. So he repeatedly sent for Paul to come, and Paul repeatedly went and talked with him. But he did not give Felix any money, and Felix did not command his soldiers to release Paul from prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="ACTS 25:9; ACTS 25:14; ACTS 12:3"> Felix let Paul remain in prison, because he wanted to please the Jewish leaders and he knew that they did not want him to release Paul. But when two years had passed, Porcius Festus became governor in place of Felix.</span>
</div>
</div>
