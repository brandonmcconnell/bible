<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:13; Isaiah 55:6–7; Jeremiah 35:15"> So now, tell the people of Judah and the citizens of Jerusalem this: The Lord says, ‘I am preparing to bring disaster on you! <span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 7:24; Deuteronomy 29:19"> But they just keep saying, ‘We do not care what you say! We will do whatever we want to do! We will continue to behave wickedly and stubbornly!’”</span>

## Jeremiah Petitions the Lord to Punish Those Who Attack Him

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 52:2; Jeremiah 11:19; Malachi 2:7"> Then some people said, “Come on! Let us consider how to deal with Jeremiah! There will still be priests to instruct us, wise men to give us advice, and prophets to declare God’s word. Come on! Let’s bring charges against him and get rid of him! Then we will not need to pay attention to anything he says.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 6:9; Jeremiah 20:12; Psalms 55:16–17"> Then I said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Lord, pay attention to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Listen to what my enemies are saying.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 35:7; Psalms 57:6; Psalms 106:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Should good be paid back with evil?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet they are virtually digging a pit to kill me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Just remember how I stood before you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">pleading on their behalf</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to keep you from venting your anger on them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 109:9–20; Lamentations 5:3; Amos 4:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">So let their children die of starvation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let them be cut down by the sword.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let their wives lose their husbands and children.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let the older men die of disease</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the younger men die by the sword in battle.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Psalms 140:5; Zephaniah 1:10–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let cries of terror be heard in their houses</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when you send bands of raiders unexpectedly to plunder them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they have virtually dug a pit to capture me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and have hidden traps for me to step into.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 35:4; Psalms 69:22–28; Nehemiah 4:4–5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you, Lord, know</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all their plots to kill me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not pardon their crimes!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not ignore their sins as though you had erased them!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let them be brought down in defeat before you!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Deal with them while you are still angry!</span></span>
