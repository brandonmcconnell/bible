<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 1:32–33; Isaiah 4:2; Isaiah 9:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I, the Lord, promise that a new time will certainly come</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I will raise up for them a righteous branch, a descendant of David.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will rule over them with wisdom and understanding</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will do what is just and right in the land.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:14–15; Jeremiah 23:3; Isaiah 43:18–19"> “So I, the Lord, say: ‘A new time will certainly come. People now affirm their oaths with “I swear as surely as the Lord lives who delivered the people of Israel out of Egypt.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5–6; Amos 9:14–15; Ezekiel 34:13"> But at that time they will affirm them with “I swear as surely as the Lord lives who delivered the descendants of the former nation of Israel from the land of the north and from all the other lands where he had banished them.” At that time they will live in their own land.’”</span>

## Oracles Against the False Prophets

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:16; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 60:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Here is what the Lord says concerning the false prophets:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">My heart and my mind are deeply disturbed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I tremble all over.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I am like a drunk person,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a person who has had too much wine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of the way the Lord</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his holy word are being mistreated.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 4:2–3; Psalms 107:34; Jeremiah 9:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the land is full of people unfaithful to him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They live wicked lives and they misuse their power.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So the land is dried up because it is under his curse.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The pastures in the wilderness are withered.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:4; Jeremiah 6:13; Matthew 21:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Moreover, the Lord says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Both the prophets and priests are godless.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have even found them doing evil in my temple!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:16; Jeremiah 11:23; Psalms 35:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">So the paths they follow will be dark and slippery.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will stumble and fall headlong.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will bring disaster on them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A day of reckoning is coming for them.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Lord affirms it!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:8; Isaiah 9:16; 1 Kings 18:18–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord says, “I saw the prophets of Samaria</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">doing something that was disgusting.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They prophesied in the name of the god Baal</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and led my people Israel astray.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9–10; Jeremiah 29:23; Ezekiel 22:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">But I see the prophets of Jerusalem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">doing something just as shocking.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are unfaithful to me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and continually prophesy lies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So they give encouragement to people who are doing evil,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the result that they do not stop their evildoing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I consider all of them as bad as the people of Sodom,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the citizens of Jerusalem as bad as the people of Gomorrah.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Jeremiah 9:15; Lamentations 3:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">So then I, the Lord who rules over all,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have something to say concerning the prophets of Jerusalem:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I will make these prophets eat the bitter food of suffering</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and drink the poison water of judgment.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the prophets of Jerusalem are the reason</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that ungodliness has spread throughout the land.’”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Ezekiel 13:6; Galatians 1:8–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord who rules over all says to the people of Jerusalem:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Do not listen to what</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those prophets are saying to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are filling you with false hopes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are reporting visions of their own imaginations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">not something the Lord has given them to say.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Jeremiah 13:10; Amos 9:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They continually say to those who reject what the Lord has said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Things will go well for you!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They say to all those who follow the stubborn inclinations of their own hearts,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Nothing bad will happen to you!’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:22; Isaiah 40:13–14; Amos 3:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Yet which of them has ever stood in the Lord’s inner circle</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so they could see and hear what he has to say?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Which of them have ever paid attention or listened to what he has said?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:23; Jeremiah 25:32; Amos 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">But just watch! The wrath of the Lord</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will come like a storm!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Like a raging storm it will rage down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the heads of those who are wicked.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:24; Isaiah 55:11; Zechariah 1:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The anger of the Lord will not turn back</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until he has fully carried out his intended purposes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In days to come</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you people will come to understand this clearly.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">I did not send those prophets.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet they were in a hurry to give their message.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I did not tell them anything.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet they prophesied anyway.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:18; Zechariah 1:4; Jeremiah 25:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">But if they had stood in my inner circle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they would have proclaimed my message to my people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They would have caused my people to turn from their wicked ways</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and stop doing the evil things they are doing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–10; 1 Kings 20:23; 1 Kings 20:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Do you people think that I am some local deity</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and not the transcendent God?” the Lord asks.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 15:3; Psalms 139:7; Psalms 90:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Do you really think anyone can hide himself</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">where I cannot see him?” the Lord asks.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Do you not know that I am everywhere?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the Lord asks.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:32; Jeremiah 23:28; Jeremiah 29:8"> The Lord says, “I have heard what those prophets who are prophesying lies in my name are saying. They are saying, ‘I have had a dream! I have had a dream!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:1–2; Jeremiah 14:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> Those prophets are just prophesying lies. They are prophesying the delusions of their own minds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 8:33–34; Judges 3:7; Acts 13:8"> How long will they go on plotting to make my people forget who I am through the dreams they tell one another? That is just as bad as what their ancestors did when they forgot who I am by worshiping the god Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Timothy 1:12; 1 Corinthians 3:12–13"> Let the prophet who has had a dream go ahead and tell his dream. Let the person who has received my message report that message faithfully. What is like straw cannot compare to what is like grain! I, the Lord, affirm it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Jeremiah 5:14; Luke 24:32"> My message is like a fire that purges dross! It is like a hammer that breaks a rock in pieces! I, the Lord, so affirm it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; Ezekiel 13:8; Jeremiah 14:14–15"> So I, the Lord, affirm that I am opposed to those prophets who steal messages from one another that they claim are from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:17; 2 Chronicles 18:10–12; 2 Chronicles 18:5"> I, the Lord, affirm that I am opposed to those prophets who are using their own tongues to declare, ‘The Lord declares….’</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:7–18; Zephaniah 3:4; Lamentations 2:14"> I, the Lord, affirm that I am opposed to those prophets who dream up lies and report them. They are misleading my people with their reckless lies. I did not send them. I did not commission them. They are not helping these people at all. I, the Lord, affirm it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Malachi 1:1; Jeremiah 12:7; Habakkuk 1:1"> The Lord said to me, “Jeremiah, when one of these people, or a prophet, or a priest asks you, ‘What burdensome message do you have from the Lord?’ Tell them, ‘You are the burden, and I will cast you away. I, the Lord, affirm it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 13:3; Lamentations 2:14"> I will punish any prophet, priest, or other person who says “The Lord’s message is burdensome.” I will punish both that person and his whole family.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; Jeremiah 42:4; Jeremiah 33:3"> So I, Jeremiah, tell you, “Each of you people should say to his friend or his relative, ‘How did the Lord answer? Or what did the Lord say?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:10; 2 Peter 3:16; Isaiah 3:8"> You must no longer say that the Lord’s message is burdensome. For what is ‘burdensome’ really pertains to what a person himself says. You are misrepresenting the words of our God, the living God, the Lord who rules over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Each of you should merely ask the prophet, ‘What answer did the Lord give you? Or what did the Lord say?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:13–14"> But just suppose you continue to say, ‘The message of the Lord is burdensome.’ Here is what the Lord says will happen: ‘I sent word to you that you must not say, “The Lord’s message is burdensome.” But you used the words “The Lord’s message is burdensome” anyway.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:33; Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 13:13"> So I will carry you far off and throw you away. I will send both you and the city I gave to you and to your ancestors out of my sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:11; Ezekiel 5:14–15; Jeremiah 42:18"> I will bring on you lasting shame and lasting disgrace which will never be forgotten!’”</span>
