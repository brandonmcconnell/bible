<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:11; Hebrews 1:1; Jeremiah 27:2"> The Lord said to me, “Go and buy some linen shorts and put them on. Do not put them in water.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:8"> Then the Lord spoke to me again and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 4:10; Psalms 137:1; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> “Take the shorts that you bought and are wearing and go at once to Perath. Bury the shorts there in a crack in the rocks.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:42–43; 2 Timothy 2:3"> So I went and buried them at Perath as the Lord had ordered me to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Many days later the Lord said to me, “Go at once to Perath and get the shorts I ordered you to bury there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 3:12; Isaiah 64:6; Jeremiah 13:10"> So I went to Perath and dug up the shorts from the place where I had buried them. I found that they were ruined; they were good for nothing.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the Lord said to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Leviticus 26:19; Jeremiah 13:15–17; Zephaniah 3:11"> “I, the Lord, say: ‘This shows how I will ruin the highly exalted position in which Judah and Jerusalem take pride.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 9:14; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16"> These wicked people refuse to obey what I have said. They follow the stubborn inclinations of their own hearts and pay allegiance to other gods by worshiping and serving them. So they will become just like these linen shorts which are good for nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Jeremiah 33:9; Psalms 81:11"> For,’ I say, ‘just as shorts cling tightly to a person’s body, so I bound the whole nation of Israel and the whole nation of Judah tightly to me.’ I intended for them to be my special people and to bring me fame, honor, and praise. But they would not obey me.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19"> “So tell them, ‘The Lord, the God of Israel, says, “Every wine jar is made to be filled with wine.”’ And they will probably say to you, ‘Do you not think we know that every wine jar is supposed to be filled with wine?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 63:6; Psalms 75:8; Psalms 60:3"> Then tell them, ‘The Lord says, “I will soon fill all the people who live in this land with stupor. I will also fill the kings from David’s dynasty, the priests, the prophets, and the citizens of Jerusalem with stupor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:20; Psalms 2:9; Jeremiah 19:9–11"> And I will smash them like wine bottles against one another, children and parents alike. I will not show any pity, mercy, or compassion. Nothing will keep me from destroying them,’ says the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 28:14–22; Revelation 2:29; Acts 4:19–20"> Then I said to the people of Judah,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Listen and pay attention! Do not be arrogant!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord has spoken.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 59:9; Isaiah 8:22; Isaiah 5:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Show the Lord your God the respect that is due him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do it before he brings the darkness of disaster.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do it before you stumble into distress</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a traveler on the mountains at twilight.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do it before he turns the light of deliverance you hope for</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">into the darkness and gloom of exile.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Malachi 2:2; Jeremiah 9:1; Jeremiah 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">But if you will not pay attention to this warning,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will weep alone because of your arrogant pride.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will weep bitterly and my eyes will overflow with tears</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you, the Lord’s flock, will be carried into exile.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:26; 2 Kings 24:12; 2 Kings 24:15"> The Lord told me,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Tell the king and the queen mother,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Surrender your thrones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for your glorious crowns</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be removed from your heads.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:27; Jeremiah 39:9; Deuteronomy 28:52"> <span className="pl pl-1">The gates of the towns in southern Judah will be shut tight.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No one will be able to go in or out of them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All Judah will be carried off into exile.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will be completely carried off into exile.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:22; Jeremiah 1:14; Habakkuk 1:6"> Then I said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look up, Jerusalem, and see</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the enemy that is coming from the north.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Where now is the flock of people that were entrusted to your care?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Where now are the ‘sheep’ that you take such pride in?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 13:8; Jeremiah 4:31; Jeremiah 5:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">What will you say when the Lord appoints as rulers over you those allies</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that you, yourself, had actually prepared as such?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then anguish and agony will grip you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like that of a woman giving birth to a baby.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Nahum 3:5; Jeremiah 16:10–11; Jeremiah 5:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will probably ask yourself,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Why have these things happened to me?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why have I been treated like a disgraced adulteress</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whose skirt has been torn off and her limbs exposed?’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is because you have sinned so much.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:22; Jeremiah 9:5; Matthew 19:24–28"> <span className="pl pl-1">But there is little hope for you ever doing good,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you who are so accustomed to doing evil.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Can an Ethiopian change the color of his skin?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Can a leopard remove its spots?</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 1:4; Leviticus 26:33; Ezekiel 5:12"> “The Lord says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘That is why I will scatter your people like chaff</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that is blown away by a desert wind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Job 20:29; Jeremiah 2:32; Psalms 9:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is your fate,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the destiny to which I have appointed you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because you have forgotten me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and have trusted in false gods.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 1:8; Hosea 2:10; Ezekiel 23:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I will pull your skirt up over your face</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and expose you to shame like a disgraced adulteress!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 8:5; Ezekiel 6:13; Ezekiel 24:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">People of Jerusalem, I have seen your adulterous worship,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your shameless prostitution to, and your lustful pursuit of, other gods.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have seen your disgusting acts of worship</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the hills throughout the countryside.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You are doomed to destruction!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How long will you continue to be unclean?’”</span></span>
</div>
