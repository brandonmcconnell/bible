<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:1; Joshua 9:3; Isaiah 9:15"> The following events occurred in that same year, early in the reign of King Zedekiah of Judah. To be more precise, it was the fifth month of the fourth year of his reign. The prophet Hananiah son of Azzur, who was from Gibeon, spoke to Jeremiah in the Lord’s temple in the presence of the priests and all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 3:11; Ezekiel 13:5–16; Jeremiah 27:2–12"> “The Lord God of Israel who rules over all says, ‘I will break the yoke of servitude to the king of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 24:13; 2 Chronicles 36:10; Daniel 1:2"> Before two years are over, I will bring back to this place everything that King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon took from it and carried away to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:24; 2 Kings 25:27–30; Jeremiah 28:2"> I will also bring back to this place Jehoiakim’s son King Jeconiah of Judah and all the exiles who were taken to Babylon.’ Indeed, the Lord affirms, ‘I will break the yoke of servitude to the king of Babylon.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 28:1; Jeremiah 26:2; Jeremiah 19:14"> Then the prophet Jeremiah responded to the prophet Hananiah in the presence of the priests and all the people who were standing in the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 41:13; 1 Kings 1:36; Jeremiah 11:5"> The prophet Jeremiah said, “Amen! May the Lord do all this! May the Lord make your prophecy come true! May he bring back to this place from Babylon all the valuable articles taken from the Lord’s temple and the people who were carried into exile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 22:28"> But listen to what I say to you and to all these people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:26–27; 1 Kings 17:1; 1 Kings 22:8"> From earliest times, the prophets who preceded you and me invariably prophesied war, disaster, and plagues against many countries and great kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:22; Jeremiah 6:14; Jeremiah 4:10"> So if a prophet prophesied peace and prosperity, it was only known that the Lord truly sent him when what he prophesied came true.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:2; Jeremiah 28:4; Malachi 3:13"> The prophet Hananiah then took the yoke off the prophet Jeremiah’s neck and broke it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 14:7; 2 Chronicles 18:22–23; Jeremiah 27:2–12"> Then he spoke up in the presence of all the people. “The Lord says, ‘In the same way I will break the yoke of servitude of all the nations to King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon before two years are over.’” After he heard this, the prophet Jeremiah departed and went on his way.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:2; Daniel 9:2; 2 Kings 20:4"> But shortly after the prophet Hananiah had broken the yoke off the prophet Jeremiah’s neck, the Lord spoke to Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 149:8; Jeremiah 27:15; Lamentations 2:14"> “Go and tell Hananiah that the Lord says, ‘You have indeed broken the wooden yoke. But you have only succeeded in replacing it with an iron one!</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Jeremiah 27:6–7; Daniel 2:38"> For the Lord God of Israel who rules over all says, “I have put an irresistible yoke of servitude on all these nations so they will serve King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon. And they will indeed serve him. I have even given him control over the wild animals.”’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:14–15; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:21"> Then the prophet Jeremiah told the prophet Hananiah, “Listen, Hananiah! The Lord did not send you! You are making these people trust in a lie!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:32; Genesis 7:4; Exodus 32:12"> So the Lord says, ‘I will most assuredly remove you from the face of the earth. You will die this very year because you have counseled rebellion against the Lord.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Zechariah 1:6; Isaiah 44:25–26"> In the seventh month of that very same year the prophet Hananiah died.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
