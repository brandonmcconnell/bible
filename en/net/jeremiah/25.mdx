<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:1; 2 Kings 24:1–2; Daniel 1:1"> In the fourth year that Jehoiakim son of Josiah was king of Judah, the Lord spoke to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah. (That was the same as the first year that Nebuchadnezzar was king of Babylon.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:11; Jeremiah 19:14–15; Mark 7:14–15"> So the prophet Jeremiah spoke to all the people of Judah and to all the people who were living in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:7; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 1:2"> “For the last twenty-three years, from the thirteenth year that Josiah son of Amon was ruling in Judah until now, the Lord has been speaking to me. I told you over and over again what he said. But you would not listen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:3; Jeremiah 26:5; Jeremiah 13:10–11"> Over and over again the Lord has sent his servants the prophets to you. But you have not listened or paid attention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:7; Genesis 17:8; Isaiah 55:6–7"> He said through them, ‘Each of you must turn from your wicked ways and stop doing the evil things you are doing. If you do, I will allow you to continue to live here in the land that I gave to you and your ancestors as a lasting possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:19; Jeremiah 7:6; Jeremiah 35:15"> Do not pay allegiance to other gods and worship and serve them. Do not make me angry by the things that you do. Then I will not cause you any harm.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Kings 21:15; Proverbs 8:36; Deuteronomy 32:21"> So, now the Lord says, ‘You have not listened to me. But you have made me angry by the things that you have done. Thus you have brought harm on yourselves.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> “Therefore, the Lord who rules over all says, ‘You have not listened to what I said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 13:3; Jeremiah 18:16; Jeremiah 1:15"> So I, the Lord, affirm that I will send for all the peoples of the north and my servant, King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon. I will bring them against this land and its inhabitants and all the nations that surround it. I will utterly destroy this land, its inhabitants, and all the nations that surround it and make them everlasting ruins. I will make them objects of horror and hissing scorn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:34; Revelation 18:22–23; Ezekiel 26:13"> I will put an end to the sounds of joy and gladness, to the glad celebration of brides and grooms in these lands. I will put an end to the sound of people grinding meal. I will put an end to lamps shining in their houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Zechariah 1:12; 2 Chronicles 36:21–22"> This whole area will become a desolate wasteland. These nations will be subject to the king of Babylon for seventy years.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 9:2; Isaiah 13:19; Jeremiah 29:10"> “‘But when the seventy years are over, I will punish the king of Babylon and his nation for their sins. I will make the land of Babylon an everlasting ruin. I, the Lord, affirm it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 1:5; Daniel 5:31"> I will bring on that land everything that I said I would. I will bring on it everything that is written in this book. I will bring on it everything that Jeremiah has prophesied against all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:6; Jeremiah 27:7; Jeremiah 50:9"> For many nations and great kings will make slaves of the king of Babylon and his nation too. I will repay them for all they have done!’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judah and the Nations Will Experience God’s Wrath

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 75:8; Isaiah 51:17; Revelation 14:10"> So the Lord, the God of Israel, spoke to me in a vision. “Take this cup from my hand. It is filled with the wine of my wrath. Take it and make the nations to whom I send you drink it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Nahum 3:11; Jeremiah 51:7; Jeremiah 51:39"> When they have drunk it, they will stagger to and fro and act insane. For I will send wars sweeping through them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 25:28; Ezekiel 43:3"> So I took the cup from the Lord’s hand. I made all the nations to whom he sent me drink the wine of his wrath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; Psalms 60:3; Jeremiah 44:22"> I made Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, its kings and its officials drink it. I did it so Judah would become a ruin. I did it so Judah, its kings, and its officials would become an object of horror and of hissing scorn, an example used in curses. Such is already becoming the case!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:13–26; Nahum 3:8–10; Jeremiah 46:2"> I made all of these other people drink it: Pharaoh, king of Egypt; his attendants, his officials, his people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 1:1; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 20:1"> the foreigners living in Egypt; all the kings of the land of Uz; all the kings of the land of the Philistines, the people of Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, the people who had been left alive from Ashdod;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:1–22; Psalms 137:7; Jeremiah 9:26"> all the people of Edom, Moab, Ammon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:4; Amos 1:9–10; Ezekiel 28:22–23"> all the kings of Tyre, all the kings of the coastlands along the sea;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:26; Jeremiah 49:32; Jeremiah 49:8"> the people of Dedan, Tema, Buz, all the desert people who cut their hair short at the temples;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:14; Jeremiah 25:20; Ezekiel 30:5"> all the kings of Arabia who live in the desert;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 11:11; Genesis 10:22; Isaiah 13:17"> all the kings of Zimri; all the kings of Elam; all the kings of Media;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:41; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 50:1–46"> all the kings of the north, whether near or far from one another; and all the other kingdoms which are on the face of the earth. After all of them have drunk the wine of the Lord’s wrath, the king of Babylon must drink it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:16; Habakkuk 2:16; Ezekiel 21:4–5"> Then the Lord said to me, “Tell them that the Lord God of Israel who rules over all says, ‘Drink this cup until you get drunk and vomit. Drink until you fall down and can’t get up. For I will send wars sweeping through you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 34:33; Jeremiah 51:29; Isaiah 14:24–27"> If they refuse to take the cup from your hand and drink it, tell them that the Lord who rules over all says ‘You most certainly must drink it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:21; Proverbs 11:31; 1 Peter 4:17"> For take note, I am already beginning to bring disaster on the city that I call my own. So how can you possibly avoid being punished? You will not go unpunished! For I am proclaiming war against all who live on the earth. I, the Lord who rules over all, affirm it!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Amos 1:2; Joel 3:16; Isaiah 42:13"> “Then, Jeremiah, make the following prophecy against them:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Like a lion about to attack, the Lord will roar from the heights of heaven;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from his holy dwelling on high he will roar loudly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will roar mightily against his land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will shout in triumph like those stomping juice from the grapes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against all those who live on the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hosea 4:1; Joel 3:2; Isaiah 66:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sounds of battle will resound to the ends of the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord will bring charges against the nations.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will pass judgment on all humankind</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will hand the wicked over to be killed in war.’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Lord so affirms it!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:19; Isaiah 34:2; 2 Chronicles 15:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord who rules over all says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Disaster will soon come on one nation after another.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A mighty storm of military destruction is rising up</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the distant parts of the earth.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 66:16; Jeremiah 8:2; Psalms 79:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who have been killed by the Lord at that time</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be scattered from one end of the earth to the other.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will not be mourned over, gathered up, or buried.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their dead bodies will lie scattered over the ground like manure.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; Jeremiah 50:27; Psalms 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Wail and cry out in anguish, you rulers!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Roll in the dust, you who shepherd flocks of people!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The time for you to be slaughtered has come.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will lie scattered and fallen like broken pieces of fine pottery.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 11:20; Amos 2:14; Jeremiah 52:8–11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The leaders will not be able to run away and hide.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The shepherds of the flocks will not be able to escape.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:8; Jeremiah 25:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen to the cries of anguish of the leaders.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Listen to the wails of the shepherds of the flocks.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are wailing because the Lord</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is about to destroy their lands.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 27:10–11; Isaiah 32:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their peaceful dwelling places will be laid waste</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by the fierce anger of the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Jeremiah 5:6; Hosea 13:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord is like a lion who has left his lair.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So their lands will certainly be laid waste</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by the warfare of the oppressive nation</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and by the fierce anger of the Lord.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>
