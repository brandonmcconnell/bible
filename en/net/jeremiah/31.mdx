## Israel Will Be Restored and Join Judah in Worship

<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28"> The Lord says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The people of Israel who survived</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">death at the hands of the enemy</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will find favor in the wilderness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as they journey to find rest for themselves.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Jeremiah 31:13; Amos 5:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will rebuild you, my dear children Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that you will once again be built up.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Once again you will take up the tambourine</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and join in the happy throng of dancers.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:21; Jeremiah 29:11–16; Lamentations 3:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, there is hope for your posterity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your children will return to their own territory.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I, the Lord, affirm it!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 80:3; Job 5:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I have indeed heard the people of Israel say mournfully,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘We were like a calf untrained to the yoke.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You disciplined us and we learned from it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let us come back to you and we will do so,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for you are the Lord our God.</span><br /></span>

## Judah Will Be Restored

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 50:7"> The Lord God of Israel who rules over all says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will restore the people of Judah to their land and to their towns.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When I do, they will again say of Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘May the Lord bless you, you holy mountain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the place where righteousness dwells.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 8:4–8; Ezekiel 36:10; Zechariah 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The land of Judah will be inhabited by people who live in its towns</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as well as by farmers and shepherds with their flocks.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Matthew 11:28; John 4:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will fully satisfy the needs of those who are weary</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and fully refresh the souls of those who are faint.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Zechariah 4:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then they will say, ‘Under these conditions I can enjoy sweet sleep</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I wake up and look around.’”</span></span>

## Israel and Judah Will Be Repopulated

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 10:9; Ezekiel 36:9"> “Indeed, a time is coming,” says the Lord, “when I will cause people and animals to sprout up in the lands of Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 18:7–9"> In the past I saw to it that they were uprooted and torn down, that they were destroyed and demolished. </div>

## The Lord Will Make a New Covenant with Israel and Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 18:2–3; Jeremiah 31:30"> “When that time comes, people will no longer say, ‘The parents have eaten sour grapes, but the children’s teeth have grown numb.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Isaiah 3:11; Deuteronomy 24:16"> Rather, each person will die for his own sins. The teeth of the person who eats the sour grapes will themselves grow numb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Ezekiel 37:26; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> “Indeed, a time is coming,” says the Lord, “when I will make a new covenant with the people of Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 24:6–8; Deuteronomy 5:3; Exodus 19:5"> It will not be like the old covenant that I made with their ancestors when I delivered them from Egypt. For they violated that covenant, even though I was like a faithful husband to them,” says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16; Hebrews 8:10"> “But I will make a new covenant with the whole nation of Israel after I plant them back in the land,” says the Lord. “I will put my law within them and write it on their hearts and minds. I will be their God and they will be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Hebrews 8:12; 1 John 2:27"> “People will no longer need to teach their neighbors and relatives to know me. For all of them, from the least important to the most important, will know me,” says the Lord. “For I will forgive their sin and will no longer call to mind the wrong they have done.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lord Guarantees Israel’s Continuance

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Isaiah 51:15; Psalms 136:7–9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord has made a promise to Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He promises it as the one who fixed the sun to give light by day</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the moon and stars to give light by night.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He promises it as the one who stirs up the sea so that its waves roll.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He promises it as the one who is known as the Lord who rules over all.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9–10; Jeremiah 33:20–26; Psalms 89:36–37"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord affirms, “The descendants of Israel will not</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">cease forever to be a nation in my sight.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That could only happen if the fixed ordering of the heavenly lights</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">were to cease to operate before me.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:24–26; Isaiah 40:12; Jeremiah 33:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord says, “I will not reject all the descendants of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of all that they have done.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">That could only happen if the heavens above could be measured</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or the foundations of the earth below could all be explored,”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">says the Lord.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Jerusalem Will Be Enlarged

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; 2 Kings 14:13; Jeremiah 30:18"> “Indeed a time is coming,” says the Lord, “when the city of Jerusalem will be rebuilt as my special city. It will be built from the Tower of Hananel westward to the Corner Gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1–2; Ezekiel 40:8"> The boundary line will extend beyond that, straight west from there to the Hill of Gareb and then turn southward to Goah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:23; Joel 3:17; 2 Kings 11:16"> The whole valley where dead bodies and sacrificial ashes are thrown and all the terraced fields out to the Kidron Valley on the east as far north as the Horse Gate will be included within this city that is sacred to the Lord. The city will never again be torn down or destroyed.”</span>
</div>
