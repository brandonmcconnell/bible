<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:28; Jeremiah 37:21; Jeremiah 32:2–3"> The Lord spoke to Jeremiah a second time while he was still confined in the courtyard of the guardhouse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 6:3; Exodus 15:3; Isaiah 37:26"> “I, the Lord, do these things. I, the Lord, form the plan to bring them about. I am known as the Lord. I say to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Isaiah 65:24; Ephesians 3:20"> ‘Call on me in prayer and I will answer you. I will show you great and mysterious things which you still do not know about.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:24; Habakkuk 1:10; Ezekiel 4:2"> For I, the Lord God of Israel, have something more to say about the houses in this city and the royal buildings which have been torn down for defenses against the siege ramps and military incursions of the Babylonians:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 8:17; Jeremiah 21:10; Jeremiah 21:4–7"> ‘The defenders of the city will go out and fight with the Babylonians. But they will only fill those houses and buildings with the dead bodies of the people that I will kill in my anger and my wrath. That will happen because I have decided to turn my back on this city on account of the wicked things they have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 10:10; Isaiah 30:26; Titus 3:5–6"> But I will most surely heal the wounds of this city and restore it and its people to health. I will show them abundant peace and security.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 30:3; Amos 9:14–15"> I will restore Judah and Israel and will rebuild them as they were in days of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Micah 7:18–19; Psalms 51:2"> I will purify them from all the sin that they committed against me. I will forgive all their sins which they committed in rebelling against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 126:2–3; Isaiah 60:5; Zephaniah 3:17–20"> All the nations will hear about all the good things which I will do to them. This city will bring me fame, honor, and praise before them for the joy that I bring it. The nations will tremble in awe at all the peace and prosperity that I will provide for it.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:43; Jeremiah 32:36; Ezekiel 37:11"> “I, the Lord, say: ‘You and your people are saying about this place, “It lies in ruins. There are no people or animals in it.” That is true. The towns of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem will soon be desolate, uninhabited either by people or by animals. But happy sounds will again be heard in these places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 16:34; Psalms 106:1; Psalms 107:22"> Once again there will be sounds of joy and gladness and the glad celebrations of brides and grooms. Once again people will bring their thank offerings to the temple of the Lord and will say, “Give thanks to the Lord who rules over all. For the Lord is good and his unfailing love lasts forever.” For I, the Lord, affirm that I will restore the land to what it was in days of old.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 65:10; Ezekiel 34:12–15; Jeremiah 36:29"> “I, the Lord who rules over all, say: ‘This place will indeed lie in ruins. There will be no people or animals in it. But there will again be in it and in its towns sheepfolds where shepherds can rest their sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:32; Luke 15:4; Jeremiah 17:26"> I, the Lord, say that shepherds will once again count their sheep as they pass into the fold. They will do this in all the towns in the southern hill country, the western foothills, the southern hill country, the territory of Benjamin, the villages surrounding Jerusalem, and the towns of Judah.’</span>

## The Lord Reaffirms His Covenant with David, Israel, and Levi

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Haggai 2:6–9; Jeremiah 23:5; 1 Chronicles 17:13–14"> “I, the Lord, affirm: ‘The time will certainly come when I will fulfill my gracious promise concerning the nations of Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 11:1–5; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 4:2"> In those days and at that time I will raise up for them a righteous descendant of David. “‘He will do what is just and right in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:6; 1 Corinthians 1:30; Isaiah 45:17"> Under his rule Judah will enjoy safety and Jerusalem will live in security. At that time Jerusalem will be called “The Lord has provided us with justice.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 2:4; Psalms 89:29–37; Luke 1:32–33"> For I, the Lord, promise: “David will never lack a successor to occupy the throne over the nation of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Revelation 1:6; Deuteronomy 18:1"> Nor will the Levitical priests ever lack someone to stand before me and continually offer up burnt offerings, sacrifice cereal offerings, and offer the other sacrifices.”’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> The Lord spoke further to Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 8:22; Psalms 89:37; Isaiah 54:9–10"> “I, Lord, make the following promise: ‘I have made a covenant with the day and with the night that they will always come at their proper times. Only if you people could break that covenant</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 89:34; 2 Chronicles 7:18; 2 Samuel 23:5"> could my covenant with my servant David and my covenant with the Levites ever be broken. So David will by all means always have a descendant to occupy his throne as king and the Levites will by all means always have priests who will minister before me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17; Hebrews 11:12"> I will make the children who follow one another in the line of my servant David very numerous. I will also make the Levites who minister before me very numerous. I will make them all as numerous as the stars in the sky and as the sands which are on the seashore.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The Lord spoke still further to Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:2–4; Psalms 83:4; Psalms 44:13–14"> “You have surely noticed what these people are saying, haven’t you? They are saying, ‘The Lord has rejected the two families of Israel and Judah that he chose.’ So they have little regard that my people will ever again be a nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 74:16–17; Jeremiah 31:35–36; Psalms 104:19"> But I, the Lord, make the following promise: I have made a covenant governing the coming of day and night. I have established the fixed laws governing heaven and earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:37; Isaiah 14:1; Hosea 2:23"> Just as surely as I have done this, so surely will I never reject the descendants of Jacob. Nor will I ever refuse to choose one of my servant David’s descendants to rule over the descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Indeed, I will restore them and show mercy to them.”</span>
</div>
</div>
