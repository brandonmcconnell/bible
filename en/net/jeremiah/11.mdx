<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord said to Jeremiah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:6; Exodus 19:5; 2 Chronicles 29:10"> “Hear the terms of the covenant I made with Israel and pass them on to the people of Judah and the citizens of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:26; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 29:19–20"> Tell them that the Lord, the God of Israel, says, ‘Anyone who does not keep the terms of the covenant will be under a curse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:12; Jeremiah 7:22–23; Deuteronomy 4:20"> Those are the terms that I charged your ancestors to keep when I brought them out of Egypt, that place which was like an iron-smelting furnace. I said at that time, “Obey me and carry out the terms of the agreement exactly as I commanded you. If you do, you will be my people and I will be your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–13; Psalms 105:9–11; Jeremiah 28:6"> Then I will keep the promise I swore on oath to your ancestors to give them a land flowing with milk and honey.” That is the very land that you still live in today.’” And I responded, “Amen! Let it be so, Lord!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 1:22; Romans 2:13; Jeremiah 7:2"> The Lord said to me, “Announce all the following words in the towns of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem: ‘Listen to the terms of my covenant with you and carry them out!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:15; Ephesians 4:17; 1 Samuel 8:9"> For I solemnly warned your ancestors to obey me. I warned them again and again, ever since I delivered them out of Egypt until this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Jeremiah 7:26; Jeremiah 35:15"> But they did not listen to me or pay any attention to me! Each one of them followed the stubborn inclinations of his own wicked heart. So I brought on them all the punishments threatened in the covenant because they did not carry out its terms as I commanded them to do.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Matthew 26:3–4; Micah 3:11"> The Lord said to me, “The people of Judah and the citizens of Jerusalem have plotted rebellion against me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:16; Ezekiel 16:59; Psalms 78:8–10"> They have gone back to the evil ways of their ancestors of old who refused to obey what I told them. They, too, have paid allegiance to other gods and worshiped them. Both the nation of Israel and the nation of Judah have violated the covenant I made with their ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:18; Proverbs 1:28; Isaiah 1:15"> So I, the Lord, say this: ‘I will soon bring disaster on them which they will not be able to escape! When they cry out to me for help, I will not listen to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; Deuteronomy 32:37; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> Then those living in the towns of Judah and in Jerusalem will go and cry out for help to the gods to whom they have been sacrificing. However, those gods will by no means be able to save them when disaster strikes them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:28; 2 Kings 23:13; Isaiah 2:8"> This is in spite of the fact that the people of Judah have as many gods as they have towns and the citizens of Jerusalem have set up as many altars to sacrifice to that disgusting god, Baal, as they have streets in the city!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:16; Jeremiah 11:11; 1 John 5:16"> So, Jeremiah, do not pray for these people. Do not cry out to me or petition me on their behalf. Do not plead with me to save them. For I will not listen to them when they call out to me for help when disaster strikes them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Proverbs 2:14; Titus 1:15; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> The Lord says to the people of Judah,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“What right do you have to be in my temple, my beloved people?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Many of you have done wicked things.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Can your acts of treachery be so easily canceled by sacred offerings</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that you take joy in doing evil even while you make them?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 11:17–24; Isaiah 27:11; Psalms 52:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I, the Lord, once called you a thriving olive tree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">one that produced beautiful fruit.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But I will set you on fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">fire that will blaze with a mighty roar.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then all your branches will be good for nothing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 5:2; Jeremiah 2:21; Jeremiah 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">For though I, the Lord who rules over all, planted you in the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I now decree that disaster will come on you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the nations of Israel and Judah have done evil</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and have made me angry by offering sacrifices to the god Baal.”</span></span>

## A Plot Against Jeremiah is Revealed and He Complains of Injustice

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:11–12; 2 Kings 6:9–10; Ezekiel 8:6–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord gave me knowledge, that I might have understanding.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then he showed me what the people were doing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; Jeremiah 20:10; Psalms 83:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Before this I had been like a docile lamb ready to be led to the slaughter.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I did not know they were making plans to kill me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I did not know they were saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Let’s destroy the tree along with its fruit!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let’s remove Jeremiah from the world of the living</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so people will not even be reminded of him any more.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:12; Jeremiah 17:10; 1 Samuel 16:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I said to the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“O Lord who rules over all, you are a just judge!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You examine people’s hearts and minds.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I want to see you pay them back for what they have done</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because I trust you to vindicate my cause.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 20:10; Amos 2:12; Luke 13:33–34"> Then the Lord told me about some men from Anathoth who were threatening to kill me. They had threatened, “Stop prophesying in the name of the Lord or we will kill you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:21; 2 Chronicles 36:17; Jeremiah 9:21"> So the Lord who rules over all said, “I will surely punish them! Their young men will be killed in battle. Their sons and daughters will die of starvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:12; Micah 7:4; Hosea 9:7"> Not one of them will survive. I will bring disaster on those men from Anathoth who threatened you. A day of reckoning is coming for them.”</span>
