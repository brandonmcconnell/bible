While they were eating a meal together with him there at Mizpah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 25:25; Jeremiah 40:5; Psalms 109:5"> Ishmael son of Nethaniah and the ten men who were with him stood up, pulled out their swords, and killed Gedaliah, the son of Ahikam and grandson of Shaphan. Thus Ishmael killed the man that the king of Babylon had appointed to govern the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 25:25; Lamentations 1:2; Jeremiah 41:11–12"> Ishmael also killed all the Judeans who were with Gedaliah at Mizpah and the Babylonian soldiers who happened to be there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:11; Psalms 52:1–2"> On the day after Gedaliah had been murdered, before anyone even knew about it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 16:24; Deuteronomy 14:1; Joshua 18:1"> eighty men arrived from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; Jeremiah 50:4; 2 Samuel 1:2–16"> Ishmael son of Nethaniah went out from Mizpah to meet them. He was pretending to cry as he walked along. When he met them, he said to them, “Come with me to meet Gedaliah son of Ahikam.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Psalms 55:23; Ezekiel 22:27"> But as soon as they were inside the city, Ishmael son of Nethaniah and the men who were with him slaughtered them and threw their bodies in a cistern.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Job 2:4; Proverbs 13:8; Philippians 3:7–9"> But there were ten men among them who said to Ishmael, “Do not kill us. For we will give you the stores of wheat, barley, olive oil, and honey we have hidden in a field. So he spared their lives and did not kill them along with the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 6:2; 1 Kings 15:17–22; 1 Samuel 13:6"> Now the cistern where Ishmael threw all the dead bodies of those he had killed was a large one that King Asa had constructed as part of his defenses against King Baasha of Israel. Ishmael son of Nethaniah filled it with dead bodies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:14; Jeremiah 40:7; Nehemiah 2:10"> Then Ishmael took captive all the people who were still left alive in Mizpah. This included the royal princesses and all the rest of the people in Mizpah that Nebuzaradan, the captain of the royal guard, had put under the authority of Gedaliah son of Ahikam. Ishmael son of Nethaniah took all these people captive and set out to cross over to the Ammonites.</span>

## Johanan Rescues the People Ishmael Had Carried Off

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:13–16; Jeremiah 40:7–8; Jeremiah 42:1"> Johanan son of Kareah and all the army officers who were with him heard about all the atrocities that Ishmael son of Nethaniah had committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:13; Genesis 14:14–16; 1 Samuel 30:1–8"> So they took all their troops and went to fight against Ishmael son of Nethaniah. They caught up with him near the large pool at Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When all the people that Ishmael had taken captive saw Johanan son of Kareah and all the army officers with him, they were glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> All those people that Ishmael had taken captive from Mizpah turned and went over to Johanan son of Kareah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 21:30; Proverbs 28:17; 1 Kings 20:20"> But Ishmael son of Nethaniah managed to escape from Johanan along with eight of his men, and he went on over to Ammon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:4–7; Jeremiah 42:8; Jeremiah 41:10"> Johanan son of Kareah and all the army officers who were with him led off all the people who had been left alive at Mizpah. They had rescued them from Ishmael son of Nethaniah after he killed Gedaliah son of Ahikam. They led off the men, women, children, soldiers, and court officials whom they had brought away from Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:37–38; Jeremiah 42:14; Jeremiah 43:7"> They set out to go to Egypt to get away from the Babylonians, but stopped at Geruth Kimham near Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:5; Luke 12:4–5; Jeremiah 42:16"> They were afraid of what the Babylonians might do because Ishmael son of Nethaniah had killed Gedaliah son of Ahikam, whom the king of Babylon had appointed to govern the country.</span>
