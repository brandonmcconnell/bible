## Israel and Judah Will Be Delivered after a Time of Deep Distress

<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> So here is what the Lord has to say about Israel and Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zephaniah 1:10–11; Amos 8:10; Isaiah 5:30"> Yes, here is what he says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You hear cries of panic and of terror;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there is no peace in sight.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Nahum 2:10; Joel 2:6; Jeremiah 4:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ask yourselves this and consider it carefully:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Have you ever seen a man give birth to a baby?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why then do I see all these strong men</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">grabbing their stomachs in pain like a woman giving birth?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And why do their faces</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">turn so deathly pale?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Zechariah 14:1–2; Malachi 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Alas, what a terrible time of trouble it is!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There has never been any like it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is a time of trouble for the descendants of Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but some of them will be rescued out of it.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 3:5; Isaiah 55:3–5; Luke 1:69"> <span className="pl pl-1">But they will be subject to the Lord their God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to the Davidic ruler whom I will raise up as king over them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:27–28; Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 29:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I, the Lord, tell you not to be afraid,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you descendants of Jacob, my servants.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Do not be terrified, people of Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will rescue you and your descendants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from a faraway land where you are captives.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The descendants of Jacob will return to their land and enjoy peace.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will be secure and no one will terrify them.</span><br /></span>

## The Lord Will Heal the Wounds of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:18; Jeremiah 30:15; Jeremiah 14:17"> Moreover, the Lord says to the people of Zion,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Your injuries are incurable;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your wounds are severe.</span><br /></span>

## The Lord Will Restore Israel and Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Haggai 2:7–9; Psalms 102:13; Jeremiah 30:3"> The Lord says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will restore the ruined houses of the descendants of Jacob.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will show compassion on their ruined homes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Every city will be rebuilt on its former ruins.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Every fortified dwelling will occupy its traditional site.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:22; Isaiah 51:11; Isaiah 60:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Out of those places you will hear songs of thanksgiving</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the sounds of laughter and merriment.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will increase their number and they will not dwindle away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will bring them honor and they will no longer be despised.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Jeremiah 2:3; Jeremiah 50:33–34"> <span className="pl pl-1">The descendants of Jacob will enjoy their former privileges.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their community will be reestablished in my favor</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will punish all who try to oppress them.</span><br /></span>
