## The Superscription

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:60; Jeremiah 11:21; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> The following is a record of what Jeremiah son of Hilkiah prophesied. He was one of the priests who lived at Anathoth in the territory of the tribe of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 13:20; Jonah 1:1; Jeremiah 1:11"> The Lord began to speak to him in the thirteenth year that Josiah son of Amon ruled over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:2; 2 Chronicles 36:5–8; 2 Kings 23:34"> The Lord also spoke to him when Jehoiakim son of Josiah ruled over Judah, and he continued to speak to him until the fifth month of the eleventh year that Zedekiah son of Josiah ruled over Judah. That was when the people of Jerusalem were taken into exile.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jeremiah’s Call and Commission

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:16; Ezekiel 1:3; Jeremiah 1:2"> The Lord said to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 139:16; Isaiah 49:1; Galatians 1:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Before I formed you in your mother’s womb I chose you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Before you were born I set you apart.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I appointed you to be a prophet to the nations.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:12; Exodus 4:10–16; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> I answered, “Oh, Lord God, I really do not know how to speak well enough for that, for I am too young.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 16:15–16; Ezekiel 3:27; Numbers 22:20"> The Lord said to me, “Do not say, ‘I am too young.’ But go to whomever I send you and say whatever I tell you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Joshua 1:9; Isaiah 51:7"> Do not be afraid of those to whom I send you, for I will be with you to protect you,” says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Exodus 4:11–12; Luke 12:12"> Then the Lord reached out his hand and touched my mouth and said to me, “I will most assuredly give you the words you are to speak for me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:28; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Isaiah 44:26–28"> Know for certain that I hereby give you the authority to announce to nations and kingdoms that they will be uprooted and torn down, destroyed and demolished, rebuilt and firmly planted.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Visions Confirming Jeremiah’s Call and Commission

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Amos 7:8; Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:2"> Later the Lord asked me, “What do you see, Jeremiah?” I answered, “I see a branch of an almond tree.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:28; Ezekiel 12:25; Ezekiel 12:22–23"> Then the Lord said, “You have observed correctly. This means I am watching to make sure my threats are carried out.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7; Zechariah 4:2"> The Lord again asked me, “What do you see?” I answered, “I see a pot of boiling water; it is tipped toward us from the north.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 41:25; Jeremiah 4:6; Jeremiah 10:22"> Then the Lord said, “This means destruction will break out from the north on all who live in the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 9:11"> For I will soon summon all the peoples of the kingdoms of the north,” says the Lord. “They will come and their kings will set up their thrones near the entrances of the gates of Jerusalem. They will attack all the walls surrounding it, and all the towns in Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:9; Jeremiah 19:4; Acts 7:41"> In this way I will pass sentence on the people of Jerusalem and Judah because of all their wickedness. For they rejected me and offered sacrifices to other gods, worshiping what they made with their own hands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:7–8; 1 Kings 18:46; 1 Peter 1:13"> “But you, Jeremiah, get yourself ready! Go and tell these people everything I instruct you to say. Do not be terrified of them, or I will give you good reason to be terrified of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 50:7; Ezekiel 3:8–9; Jeremiah 15:20"> I, the Lord, hereby promise to make you as strong as a fortified city, an iron pillar, and a bronze wall. You will be able to stand up against all who live in the land, including the kings of Judah, its officials, its priests and all the people of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Jeremiah 1:8; Jeremiah 20:11"> They will attack you but they will not be able to overcome you, for I will be with you to rescue you,” says the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>
