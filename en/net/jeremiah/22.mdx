<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Isaiah 5:8–9; Haggai 1:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He says, “I will build myself a large palace</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with spacious upper rooms.”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He cuts windows in its walls,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">panels it with cedar, and paints its rooms red.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 23:25; Isaiah 3:10; Ecclesiastes 10:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Does it make you any more of a king</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that you outstrip everyone else in building with cedar?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Just think about your father.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He was content that he had food and drink.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He did what was just and right.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So things went well with him.</span><br /></span>

## Jeconiah Will Be Permanently Exiled

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:1; Song of Solomon 8:6; 1 Chronicles 3:16"> The Lord says, “As surely as I am the living God, you, Jeconiah, king of Judah, son of Jehoiakim, will not be the earthly representative of my authority. Indeed, I will take that right away from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20–21; Jeremiah 21:7; 2 Kings 24:15–16"> I will hand you over to those who want to take your life and of whom you are afraid. I will hand you over to King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon and his Babylonian soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 24:15; Jeremiah 15:2–4; Isaiah 22:17"> I will force you and your mother who gave you birth into exile. You will be exiled to a country where neither of you were born, and you will both die there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:14; 2 Kings 25:27–30; Psalms 86:4"> You will never come back to this land to which you will long to return!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Hosea 8:8; Jeremiah 48:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">This man, Jeconiah, will be like a broken pot someone threw away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will be like a clay vessel that no one wants.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why will he and his children be forced into exile?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why will they be thrown out into a country they know nothing about?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:1; Micah 1:2; Jeremiah 6:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">O land of Judah, land of Judah, landof Judah!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Listen to what the Lord has to say!</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:30; Psalms 94:20; Matthew 1:11–16"> The Lord says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Enroll this man in the register as though he were childless.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Enroll him as a man who will not enjoy success during his lifetime.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For none of his sons will succeed in occupying the throne of David</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or ever succeed in ruling over Judah.”</span></span>
