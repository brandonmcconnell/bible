## The Prophecy about Egypt’s Defeat at Carchemish

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 23:29; Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 25:19"> He spoke about Egypt and the army of Pharaoh Necho king of Egypt which was encamped along the Euphrates River at Carchemish. Now this was the army that King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon defeated in the fourth year that Jehoiakim son of Josiah was ruling over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 21:5; Joel 3:9; Nahum 2:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Fall into ranks with your shields ready!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Prepare to march into battle!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:9–11; Jeremiah 51:3; 1 Samuel 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Harness the horses to the chariots!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Mount your horses!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Put on your helmets and take your positions!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Sharpen you spears!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Put on your armor!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:29; Jeremiah 6:25; Jeremiah 46:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">What do I see?” says the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The soldiers are terrified.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are retreating.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have been defeated.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are overcome with terror;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they desert quickly</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">without looking back.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:19; Jeremiah 46:12; Psalms 27:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">But even the swiftest cannot get away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even the strongest cannot escape.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There in the north by the Euphrates River</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they stumble and fall in defeat.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:2; Isaiah 8:7–8; Daniel 11:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Who is this that rises like the Nile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like its streams turbulent at flood stage?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 10:13–16; Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 37:24–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt rises like the Nile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like its streams turbulent at flood stage.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Egypt says, ‘I will arise and cover the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will destroy cities and the people who inhabit them.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:10; Nahum 3:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go ahead and charge into battle, you horsemen!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Drive furiously, you charioteers!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let the soldiers march out into battle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those from Ethiopia and Libya who carry shields,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and those from Lydia who are armed with the bow.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:42; Joel 1:15; Jeremiah 46:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">But that day belongs to the Lord God who rules over all.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is the day when he will pay back his enemies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His sword will devour them until its appetite is satisfied!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will drink their blood until it is full!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the Lord God who rules over all will offer them up as a sacrifice</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the land of the north by the Euphrates River.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:22; Micah 1:9; Nahum 3:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Go up to Gilead and get medicinal ointment,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you dear poor people of Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But it will prove useless no matter how much medicine you use;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there will be no healing for you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Nahum 3:8–10; Isaiah 19:2; Jeremiah 46:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">The nations will hear of your devastating defeat.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your cries of distress will echo throughout the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In the panic of their flight one soldier will trip over another</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and both of them will fall down defeated.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lord Predicts that Nebuchadnezzar Will Attack and Plunder Egypt

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Isaiah 19:1–25"> The Lord spoke to the prophet Jeremiah about Nebuchadnezzar coming to attack the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:10; Jeremiah 44:1; Jeremiah 46:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Make an announcement throughout Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Proclaim it in Migdol, Memphis, and Tahpanhes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Take your positions and prepare to do battle.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For the enemy army is destroying all the nations around you.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 66:15–16; Psalms 68:2; Psalms 18:39"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why will your soldiers be defeated?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will not stand because I, the Lord, will thrust them down.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 26:36–37; Jeremiah 51:9; Jeremiah 46:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will make many stumble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will fall over one another in their hurry to flee.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will say, ‘Get up!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let’s go back to our own people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let’s go back to our homelands</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the enemy is coming to destroy us.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11–16; Exodus 15:9; 1 Kings 20:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">There at home they will say, ‘Pharaoh king of Egypt is just a big noise!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He has let the most opportune moment pass by.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:15; Judges 4:6; Joshua 19:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">I the King, whose name is the Lord who rules over all, swear this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I swear as surely as I live that a conqueror is coming.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will be as imposing as Mount Tabor is among the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as Mount Carmel is against the backdrop of the sea.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:18; Ezekiel 30:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Pack your bags for exile,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you inhabitants of poor dear Egypt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For Memphis will be laid waste.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will lie in ruins and be uninhabited.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hosea 10:11; Jeremiah 47:2; Jeremiah 1:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt is like a beautiful young cow.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But northern armies will attack her like swarms of stinging flies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:5; Obadiah 1:13; 2 Kings 7:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even her mercenaries</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will prove to be like pampered, well-fed calves.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For they too will turn and run away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will not stand their ground</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when the time for them to be destroyed comes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the time for them to be punished.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 29:4; Micah 1:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Egypt will run away, hissing like a snake,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as the enemy comes marching up in force.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will come against her with axes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as if they were woodsmen chopping down trees.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Judges 7:12; Judges 6:5; Joel 2:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">The population of Egypt is like a vast, impenetrable forest.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But I, the Lord, affirm that the enemy will cut them down.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For those who chop them down will be more numerous than locusts.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will be too numerous to count.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:15; Ezekiel 29:1–21; Jeremiah 46:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">Poor dear Egypt will be put to shame.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">She will be handed over to the people from the north.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; Jeremiah 43:12–13; Isaiah 20:5–6"> The Lord God of Israel who rules over all says, “I will punish Amon, the god of Thebes. I will punish Egypt, its gods, and its kings. I will punish Pharaoh and all who trust in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11; Jeremiah 44:30; Ezekiel 29:8–14"> I will hand them over to Nebuchadnezzar and his troops, who want to kill them. But later on, people will live in Egypt again as they did in former times. I, the Lord, affirm it!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Promise of Hope for Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 43:5; Jeremiah 30:10–11; Isaiah 43:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">“You descendants of Jacob, my servants, do not be afraid;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">do not be terrified, people of Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will rescue you and your descendants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from the faraway lands where you are captives.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The descendants of Jacob will return to their land and enjoy peace.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will be secure and no one will terrify them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:32; Revelation 3:19; Jeremiah 10:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I, the Lord, tell you not to be afraid,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you descendants of Jacob, my servant,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I am with you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Though I completely destroy all the nations where I scatter you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will not completely destroy you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will indeed discipline you but only in due measure.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will not allow you to go entirely unpunished.”</span></span>
