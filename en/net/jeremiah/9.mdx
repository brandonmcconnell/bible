## The Lord Laments That He Has No Choice But to Judge Them

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:8; Jeremiah 4:22; Judges 2:10"> The Lord says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“These people are like soldiers who have readied their bows.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their tongues are always ready to shoot out lies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They have become powerful in the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but they have not done so by honest means.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, they do one evil thing after another</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and do not pay attention to me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Micah 7:5–6; Proverbs 26:24–25; Psalms 12:2–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Everyone must be on his guard around his friends.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He must not even trust any of his relatives.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For every one of them will find some way to cheat him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And all of his friends will tell lies about him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 6:12; Habakkuk 2:13; Job 15:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">One friend deceives another</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and no one tells the truth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">These people have trained themselves to tell lies.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They do wrong and are unable to repent.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hosea 4:6; Jeremiah 13:10; Jeremiah 8:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">They do one act of violence after another,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and one deceitful thing after another.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They refuse to pay attention to me,”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">says the Lord.</span></span>
</div>

## The Coming Destruction Calls For Mourning

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:4; Hosea 4:3; Jeremiah 12:10"> I said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will weep and mourn for the grasslands on the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will sing a mournful song for the pastures in the wilderness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they are so scorched no one travels through them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The sound of livestock is no longer heard there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even the birds in the sky and the wild animals in the fields</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">have fled and are gone.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 25:2; Isaiah 13:22; Jeremiah 10:22"> The Lord said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“I will make Jerusalem a heap of ruins.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jackals will make their home there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will destroy the towns of Judah</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that no one will be able to live in them.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 14:9; Psalms 107:43; Psalms 107:34"> I said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Who is wise enough to understand why this has happened?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who has a word from the Lord that can explain it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why does the land lie in ruins?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why is it as scorched as a desert through which no one travels?”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:19; Jeremiah 22:9; Ezra 9:10"> The Lord answered, “This has happened because these people have rejected my laws which I gave them. They have not obeyed me or followed those laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 3:17; Jeremiah 7:24; 1 Peter 1:18"> Instead they have followed the stubborn inclinations of their own hearts. They have paid allegiance to the gods called Baal, as their fathers taught them to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:14; Lamentations 3:15; Psalms 80:5"> So then, listen to what I, the Lord God of Israel who rules over all, say. ‘I will make these people eat the bitter food of suffering and drink the poison water of judgment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 5:2; Leviticus 26:33"> I will scatter them among nations that neither they nor their ancestors have known anything about. I will send people chasing after them with swords until I have destroyed them.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Amos 5:16–17; Ecclesiastes 12:5; 2 Chronicles 35:25"> The Lord who rules over all told me to say to this people,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Take note of what I say.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Call for the women who mourn for the dead!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Summon those who are the most skilled at it!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:1; Isaiah 22:4; Jeremiah 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">I said, “Indeed, let them come quickly and sing a song of mourning for us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let them wail loudly until tears stream from our own eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and our eyelids overflow with water.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Ezekiel 7:16–18; Deuteronomy 28:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the sound of wailing is soon to be heard in Zion.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will wail, ‘We are utterly ruined! We are completely disgraced!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For our houses have been torn down</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and we must leave our land.’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 23:27–30; Isaiah 32:9–13; Job 22:22"> I said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“So now, you wailing women, hear what the Lord says.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Open your ears to the words from his mouth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Teach your daughters this mournful song,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and each of you teach your neighbor this lament.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:11; Ezekiel 9:5–6; Jeremiah 15:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Death has climbed in through our windows.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It has entered into our fortified houses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It has taken away our children who play in the streets.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It has taken away our young men who gather in the city squares.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:2; Jeremiah 25:33; Psalms 83:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Tell your daughters and neighbors, ‘The Lord says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The dead bodies of people will lie scattered everywhere</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like manure scattered on a field.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will lie scattered on the ground</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like grain that has been cut down but has not been gathered.”’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27–29; Isaiah 5:21; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> <br />The Lord says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Wise people should not boast that they are wise.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Powerful people should not boast that they are powerful.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Rich people should not boast that they are rich.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:17; Galatians 6:14; 1 Corinthians 1:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">If people want to boast, they should boast about this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They should boast that they understand and know me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They should boast that they know and understand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that I, the Lord, act out of faithfulness, fairness, and justice in the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and that I desire people to do these things,”</span><br />says the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 2:8–9; Galatians 5:2–6; Romans 2:25–26"> The Lord says, “Watch out! The time is soon coming when I will punish all those who are circumcised only in the flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Leviticus 26:41; Ezekiel 44:7"> That is, I will punish the Egyptians, the Judeans, the Edomites, the Ammonites, the Moabites, and all the desert people who cut their hair short at the temples. I will do so because none of the people of those nations are really circumcised in the Lord’s sight. Moreover, none of the people of Israel are circumcised when it comes to their hearts.”</span>
</div>
