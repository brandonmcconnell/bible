## The Lord Calls on Israel and Judah to Repent

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 86:15; Ezekiel 33:11; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> “Go and shout this message to my people in the countries in the north. Tell them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Come back to me, wayward Israel,’ says the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I will not continue to look on you with displeasure.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I am merciful,’ says the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I will not be angry with you forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:25; Jeremiah 3:6; Deuteronomy 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">However, you must confess that you have done wrong,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and that you have rebelled against the Lord your God.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You must confess that you have given yourself to foreign gods under every green tree,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and have not obeyed my commands,’ says the Lord.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Jeremiah 2:19; Jeremiah 31:32"> “Come back to me, my wayward sons,” says the Lord, “for I am your true master. <span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:7; Micah 4:1–5; Jeremiah 17:12"> At that time the city of Jerusalem will be called the Lord’s throne. <span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:8; Hosea 1:11; Amos 9:15"> At that time the nation of Judah and the nation of Israel will be reunited. Together they will come back from a land in the north to the land that I gave to your ancestors as a permanent possession. ”</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 48:8; Jeremiah 5:11; Hosea 5:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">But, you have been unfaithful to me, nation of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like an unfaithful wife who has left her husband,”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">says the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:10; Jeremiah 31:9; Jeremiah 2:32"> <span className="pl pl-1">“A noise is heard on the hilltops.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is the sound of the people of Israel crying and pleading to their gods.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed they have followed sinful ways;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they have forgotten to be true to the Lord their God.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 14:1; Hosea 14:4; Zechariah 13:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Come back to me, you wayward people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I want to cure your waywardness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Say, ‘Here we are. <span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 121:1–2; Psalms 3:8; Isaiah 12:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">We know our noisy worship of false gods</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on the hills and mountains did not help us.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">We know that the Lord our God</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is the only one who can deliver Israel.</span><br /></span>
