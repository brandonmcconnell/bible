<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:4–5; Jeremiah 27:1; 2 Kings 23:34–36"> The Lord spoke to Jeremiah at the beginning of the reign of Josiah’s son, King Jehoiakim of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:2; Acts 20:27; Matthew 28:20"> The Lord said, “Go stand in the courtyard of the Lord’s temple. Speak out to all the people who are coming from the towns of Judah to worship in the Lord’s temple. Tell them everything I command you to tell them. Do not leave out a single word!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:3; Isaiah 1:16–19; Jeremiah 18:7–10"> Maybe they will pay attention and each of them will stop living the evil way they do. If they do that, then I will forgo destroying them as I had intended to do because of the wicked things they have been doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 9:6; Jeremiah 44:10; Isaiah 1:20"> Tell them that the Lord says, ‘You must obey me! You must live according to the way I have instructed you in my laws.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 11:7; 2 Kings 9:7; Jeremiah 7:13"> You must pay attention to the exhortations of my servants the prophets. I have sent them to you over and over again. But you have not paid any attention to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:9; 2 Kings 22:19; Jeremiah 25:18"> If you do not obey me, then I will do to this temple what I did to Shiloh. And I will make this city an example to be used in curses by people from all the nations on the earth.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:31; Micah 3:11; Acts 5:17"> The priests, the prophets, and all the people heard Jeremiah say these things in the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Lamentations 4:13–14; Acts 7:52; Jeremiah 18:18"> Jeremiah had just barely finished saying all the Lord had commanded him to say to all the people. All at once some of the priests, the prophets, and the people grabbed him and shouted, “You deserve to die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:17–19; Acts 19:24–32; Micah 2:6"> How dare you claim the Lord’s authority to prophesy such things! How dare you claim his authority to prophesy that this temple will become like Shiloh and that this city will become an uninhabited ruin!” Then all the people crowded around Jeremiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:10; Jeremiah 38:4–6; Jeremiah 37:14–16"> However, some of the officials of Judah heard about what was happening and they rushed up to the Lord’s temple from the royal palace. They set up court at the entrance of the New Gate of the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; Matthew 26:66; Deuteronomy 18:20"> Then the priests and the prophets made their charges before the officials and all the people. They said, “This man should be condemned to die because he prophesied against this city. You have heard him do so with your own ears.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Jeremiah 26:15; Acts 4:19"> Then Jeremiah made his defense before all the officials and all the people. “The Lord sent me to prophesy everything you have heard me say against this temple and against this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jonah 3:9; Jonah 4:2; Isaiah 1:19"> But correct the way you have been living and do what is right. Obey the Lord your God. If you do, the Lord will forgo destroying you as he threatened he would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 9:25; 2 Samuel 15:26; Jeremiah 38:5"> As to my case, I am in your power. Do to me what you deem fair and proper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Proverbs 6:17; Matthew 26:25"> But you should take careful note of this: If you put me to death, you will bring on yourselves and this city and those who live in it the guilt of murdering an innocent man. For the Lord has sent me to speak all this where you can hear it. That is the truth!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 5:34–39; Acts 23:29; Acts 25:25"> Then the officials and all the people rendered their verdict to the priests and the prophets. They said, “This man should not be condemned to die. For he has spoken to us under the authority of the Lord our God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:34; Micah 1:1"> Then some of the elders of Judah stepped forward and spoke to all the people gathered there. They said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 1:1; Nehemiah 4:2; Zechariah 8:3"> “Micah from Moresheth prophesied during the time Hezekiah was king of Judah. He told all the people of Judah,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord who rules over all says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Zion will become a plowed field.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jerusalem will become a pile of rubble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The temple mount will become a mere wooded ridge.”’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 32:14; 2 Samuel 24:16; Isaiah 37:15–20"> King Hezekiah and all the people of Judah did not put him to death, did they? Did not Hezekiah show reverence for the Lord and seek the Lord’s favor? Did not the Lord forgo destroying them as he threatened he would? But we are on the verge of bringing great disaster on ourselves.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:2; Joshua 9:17; Joshua 15:60"> Now there was another man who prophesied as the Lord’s representative against this city and this land just as Jeremiah did. His name was Uriah son of Shemaiah from Kiriath Jearim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Matthew 14:5; Jeremiah 36:26"> When the king and all his bodyguards and officials heard what he was prophesying, the king sought to have him executed. But Uriah found out about it and fled to Egypt out of fear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 36:12; 2 Kings 22:12; 2 Kings 22:14"> However, King Jehoiakim sent some men to Egypt, including Elnathan son of Achbor,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:30; Revelation 11:7; Ezekiel 19:6"> and they brought Uriah back from there. They took him to King Jehoiakim, who had him executed and had his body thrown into the burial place of the common people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:5–7; Jeremiah 39:14; Jeremiah 1:18–19"> However, Ahikam son of Shaphan used his influence to keep Jeremiah from being handed over and executed by the people.</span>
</div>
