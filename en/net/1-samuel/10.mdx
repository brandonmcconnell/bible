## Saul Becomes King

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 38:7–8; 1 Samuel 10:2–6; Judges 7:11"> As Saul turned to leave Samuel, God changed his inmost person. All these signs happened on that very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:5–6; 1 Samuel 19:20–24"> When Saul and his servant arrived at Gibeah, a company of prophets was coming out to meet him. Then the spirit of God rushed upon Saul and he prophesied among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:24; John 7:15; Matthew 13:54–55"> When everyone who had known him previously saw him prophesying with the prophets, the people all asked one another, “What on earth has happened to the son of Kish? Does even Saul belong with the prophets?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; John 6:45; James 1:17"> A man who was from there replied, “And who is their father?” Therefore this became a proverb: “Is even Saul among the prophets?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> When Saul had finished prophesying, he went to the high place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:50; 2 Kings 5:25; 1 Samuel 9:3–10"> Saul’s uncle asked him and his servant, “Where did you go?” Saul replied, “To look for the donkeys. But when we realized they were lost, we went to Samuel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Saul’s uncle said, “Tell me what Samuel said to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 29:11; Judges 14:6; 1 Samuel 9:20"> Saul said to his uncle, “He assured us that the donkeys had been found.” But Saul did not tell him what Samuel had said about the matter of kingship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 1 Samuel 7:5–6"> Then Samuel called the people together before the Lord at Mizpah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 6:8–9; Nehemiah 9:27–28; Judges 2:1"> He said to the Israelites, “This is what the Lord God of Israel says, ‘I brought Israel up from Egypt and I delivered you from the power of the Egyptians and from the power of all the kingdoms that oppressed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:12; 1 Samuel 8:19; Joshua 24:1"> But today you have rejected your God who saves you from all your trouble and distress. You have said, “No! Appoint a king over us.” Now take your positions before the Lord by your tribes and by your clans.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 7:16–18; Acts 1:24–26; 1 Samuel 14:41"> Then Samuel brought all the tribes of Israel near, and the tribe of Benjamin was chosen by lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Then he brought the tribe of Benjamin near by its families, and the family of Matri was chosen by lot. At last Saul son of Kish was chosen by lot. But when they looked for him, he was nowhere to be found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; Judges 20:18; 1 Samuel 23:2–4"> So they inquired again of the Lord, “Has the man arrived here yet?” The Lord said, “He has hidden himself among the equipment.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:2; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Samuel 17:4"> So they ran and brought him from there. When he took his position among the people, he stood head and shoulders above them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Kings 1:25; 1 Kings 1:39; 2 Samuel 21:6"> Then Samuel said to all the people, “Do you see the one whom the Lord has chosen? Indeed, there is no one like him among all the people!” All the people shouted out, “Long live the king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11–18; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; Romans 13:1–7"> Then Samuel talked to the people about how the kingship would work. He wrote it all down on a scroll and set it before the Lord. Then Samuel sent all the people away to their homes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:4; Joshua 18:28; 1 Samuel 15:34"> Even Saul went to his home in Gibeah. With him went some brave men whose hearts God had touched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:13; 2 Chronicles 17:5; 1 Kings 10:25"> But some wicked men said, “How can this man save us?” They despised him and did not even bring him a gift. But Saul said nothing about it.</span>
