## David Marries Abigail the Widow of Nabal

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 38:13; 1 Samuel 23:24; Joshua 15:55"> There was a man in Maon whose business was in Carmel. This man was very wealthy; he owned three thousand sheep and a thousand goats. At that time he was shearing his sheep in Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 31:10; Isaiah 32:5–7"> The man’s name was Nabal, and his wife’s name was Abigail. She was both wise and beautiful, but the man was harsh and his deeds were evil. He was a Calebite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 38:13; 2 Samuel 13:23"> When David heard in the desert that Nabal was shearing his sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 43:23; 1 Samuel 17:22"> he sent ten servants, saying to them, “Go up to Carmel to see Nabal and give him greetings in my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 10:5; 1 Chronicles 12:18; Psalms 122:7"> Then you will say to my brother, “Peace to you and your house! Peace to all that is yours!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:21; 1 Samuel 25:15–16; 1 Samuel 22:2"> Now I hear that they are shearing sheep for you. When your shepherds were with us, we neither insulted them nor harmed them the whole time they were in Carmel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Esther 9:19; Nehemiah 8:10–12; Luke 14:12–14"> Ask your own servants; they can tell you! May my servants find favor in your sight, for we have come at the time of a holiday. Please provide us – your servants and your son David – with whatever you can spare.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 2:15; 2 Chronicles 14:7; Genesis 8:4"> So David’s servants went and spoke all these words to Nabal in David’s name. Then they paused.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 9:28; Isaiah 32:7; 1 Samuel 22:7–8"> But Nabal responded to David’s servants, “Who is David, and who is this son of Jesse? This is a time when many servants are breaking away from their masters!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Judges 8:6; Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> Should I take my bread and my water and my meat that I have slaughtered for my shearers and give them to these men? I don’t even know where they came from!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 36:21–22; 2 Samuel 24:13; Hebrews 13:17"> So David’s servants went on their way. When they had returned, they came and told David all these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:13; Proverbs 25:8; 1 Samuel 24:5–6"> Then David instructed his men, “Each of you strap on your sword!” So each one strapped on his sword, and David also strapped on his sword. About four hundred men followed David up, while two hundred stayed behind with the equipment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:10; Mark 15:29"> But one of the servants told Nabal’s wife Abigail, “David sent messengers from the desert to greet our lord, but he screamed at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:7; 1 Samuel 25:21; Philippians 2:15"> These men were very good to us. They did not insult us, nor did we sustain any loss during the entire time we were together in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 1:10; Exodus 14:22; Zechariah 2:5"> Both night and day they were a protective wall for us the entire time we were with them, while we were tending our flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:7; Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Samuel 20:9"> Now be aware of this, and see what you can do. For disaster has been planned for our lord and his entire household. He is such a wicked person that no one tells him anything!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:1; Matthew 5:25; Proverbs 6:4–5"> So Abigail quickly took two hundred loaves of bread, two containers of wine, five prepared sheep, five seahs of roasted grain, a hundred bunches of raisins, and two hundred lumps of pressed figs. She loaded them on donkeys</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 32:20; Genesis 32:16; Proverbs 31:11–12"> and said to her servants, “Go on ahead of me. I will come after you.” But she did not tell her husband Nabal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 4:24"> Riding on her donkey, she went down under cover of the mountain. David and his men were coming down to meet her, and she encountered them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 17:13; Ephesians 4:31; Romans 12:21"> Now David had been thinking, “In vain I guarded everything that belonged to this man in the desert. I didn’t take anything from him. But he has repaid my good with evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 14:10; 1 Samuel 20:13; 1 Samuel 3:17"> God will severely punish David, if I leave alive until morning even one male from all those who belong to him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:41; Judges 1:14; Joshua 15:18"> When Abigail saw David, she got down quickly from the donkey, threw herself down before David, and bowed to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Esther 8:3; 1 Samuel 25:28; 2 Samuel 14:9"> Falling at his feet, she said, “My lord, I accept all the guilt! But please let your female servant speak with my lord! Please listen to the words of your servant!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:17; Malachi 2:2; 2 Samuel 13:33"> My lord should not pay attention to this wicked man Nabal. He simply lives up to his name! His name means ‘fool,’ and he is indeed foolish! But I, your servant, did not see the servants my lord sent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:32; Romans 12:19–20; Genesis 20:6"> “Now, my lord, as surely as the Lord lives and as surely as you live, it is the Lord who has kept you from shedding blood and taking matters into your own hands. Now may your enemies and those who seek to harm my lord be like Nabal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 33:11; 1 Samuel 30:26; 2 Kings 5:15"> Now let this present that your servant has brought to my lord be given to the servants who follow my lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:17; 2 Samuel 7:11; 1 Samuel 24:11"> Please forgive the sin of your servant, for the Lord will certainly establish the house of my lord, because my lord fights the battles of the Lord. May no evil be found in you all your days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 17:21; Matthew 10:29–30; Malachi 3:17"> When someone sets out to chase you and to take your life, the life of my lord will be wrapped securely in the bag of the living by the Lord your God. But he will sling away the lives of your enemies from the sling’s pocket!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:14; Psalms 89:20; 1 Samuel 23:17"> The Lord will do for my lord everything that he promised you, and he will make you a leader over Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 40:14; Romans 12:19; Luke 23:42"> Your conscience will not be overwhelmed with guilt for having poured out innocent blood and for having taken matters into your own hands. When the Lord has granted my lord success, please remember your servant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 1:68; Exodus 18:10; Psalms 72:18"> Then David said to Abigail, “Praised be the Lord, the God of Israel, who has sent you this day to meet me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; Proverbs 28:23; Proverbs 9:9"> Praised be your good judgment! May you yourself be rewarded for having prevented me this day from shedding blood and taking matters into my own hands!</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:11; 1 Samuel 25:26; Joshua 10:6"> Otherwise, as surely as the Lord, the God of Israel, lives – he who has prevented me from harming you – if you had not come so quickly to meet me, by morning’s light not even one male belonging to Nabal would have remained alive!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 19:21; 1 Samuel 20:42; 2 Kings 5:19"> Then David took from her hand what she had brought to him. He said to her, “Go back to your home in peace. Be assured that I have listened to you and responded favorably.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:19; 2 Samuel 13:23; 2 Samuel 13:28"> When Abigail went back to Nabal, he was holding a banquet in his house like that of the king. Nabal was having a good time and was very intoxicated. She told him absolutely nothing until morning’s light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:28; 1 Samuel 25:34; 1 Samuel 25:22"> In the morning, when Nabal was sober, his wife told him about these matters. He had a stroke and was paralyzed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:10; 2 Kings 15:5; 1 Samuel 6:9"> After about ten days the Lord struck Nabal down and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:26; 1 Samuel 25:34; Proverbs 22:23"> When David heard that Nabal had died, he said, “Praised be the Lord who has vindicated me and avenged the insult that I suffered from Nabal! The Lord has kept his servant from doing evil, and he has repaid Nabal for his evil deeds.” Then David sent word to Abigail and asked her to become his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 24:51; Genesis 24:37–38"> So the servants of David went to Abigail at Carmel and said to her, “David has sent us to you to bring you back to be his wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; John 13:3–5; Ruth 2:10"> She arose, bowed her face toward the ground, and said, “Your female servant, like a lowly servant, will wash the feet of the servants of my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 24:61–67; Psalms 45:10–11"> Then Abigail quickly went and mounted her donkey, with five of her female servants accompanying her. She followed David’s messengers and became his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:5; Joshua 15:56; 1 Samuel 27:3"> David had also married Ahinoam from Jezreel; the two of them became his wives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:14; 1 Samuel 18:27; 1 Samuel 18:20"> (Now Saul had given his daughter Michal, David’s wife, to Paltiel son of Laish, who was from Gallim.)</span>
</div>
