<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 7:22; Genesis 31:2; Matthew 20:15"> So Saul was keeping an eye on David from that day onward.</span>

Let it be the hand of the Philistines!”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 119:141; Proverbs 14:20; Ecclesiastes 9:15–16"> So Saul’s servants spoke these words privately to David. David replied, “Is becoming the king’s son-in-law something insignificant to you? I’m just a poor and lightly-esteemed man!”</span>
</div>

</div>
<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
