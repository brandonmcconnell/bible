</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:11; James 1:5; 1 Samuel 12:17"> But this request displeased Samuel, for they said, “Give us a king to lead us.” So Samuel prayed to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 10:16; 1 Samuel 10:19; Exodus 16:8"> The Lord said to Samuel, “Do everything the people request of you. For it is not you that they have rejected, but it is me that they have rejected as their king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 106:34–40; Exodus 32:1; Judges 13:1"> Just as they have done from the day that I brought them up from Egypt until this very day, they have rejected me and have served other gods. This is what they are also doing to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:25; 1 Samuel 8:11–18; Ezekiel 46:18"> So now do as they say. But seriously warn them and make them aware of the policies of the king who will rule over them.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So Samuel spoke all the words of the Lord to the people who were asking him for a king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:52; Deuteronomy 17:14–20; 1 Samuel 10:25"> He said, “Here are the policies of the king who will rule over you: He will conscript your sons and put them in his chariot forces and in his cavalry; they will run in front of his chariot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:7; 1 Kings 4:7; 1 Chronicles 27:1–22"> He will appoint for himself leaders of thousands and leaders of fifties, as well as those who plow his ground, reap his harvest, and make his weapons of war and his chariot equipment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He will take your daughters to be ointment makers, cooks, and bakers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:18; 1 Kings 21:7; 1 Kings 21:19"> He will take your best fields and vineyards and give them to his own servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 1:3; Isaiah 39:7; Genesis 37:36"> He will demand a tenth of your seed and of the produce of your vineyards and give it to his administrators and his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> He will take your male and female servants, as well as your best cattle and your donkeys, and assign them for his own use.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> He will demand a tenth of your flocks, and you yourselves will be his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 3:4; Isaiah 1:15; Proverbs 1:25–28"> In that day you will cry out because of your king whom you have chosen for yourselves, but the Lord won’t answer you in that day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 44:16; Jeremiah 7:13; Isaiah 66:4"> But the people refused to heed Samuel’s warning. Instead they said, “No! There will be a king over us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:5; Psalms 106:35; Deuteronomy 7:6"> We will be like all the other nations. Our king will judge us and lead us and fight our battles.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:11"> So Samuel listened to everything the people said and then reported it to the Lord.</span>

</div>
