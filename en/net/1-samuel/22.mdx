## Saul Executes the Priests

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:9; 1 Samuel 18:10; 1 Samuel 20:33"> But Saul found out the whereabouts of David and the men who were with him. Now Saul was sitting at Gibeah under the tamarisk tree at an elevated location with his spear in hand and all his servants stationed around him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 12:16; 1 Samuel 8:14–15; 1 Samuel 22:13"> Saul said to his servants who were stationed around him, “Listen up, you Benjaminites! Is Jesse’s son giving fields and vineyards to all of you? Or is he making all of you commanders and officers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:21; 1 Samuel 20:13–17"> For all of you have conspired against me! No one informs me when my own son makes an agreement with this son of Jesse! Not one of you feels sorry for me or informs me that my own son has commissioned my own servant to hide in ambush against me, as is the case today!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 52:1–9; 1 Samuel 14:3; Ezekiel 22:9"> But Doeg the Edomite, who had stationed himself with the servants of Saul, replied, “I saw this son of Jesse come to Ahimelech son of Ahitub at Nob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 27:21; 1 Samuel 30:8; 1 Samuel 23:4"> He inquired of the Lord for him and gave him provisions. He also gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 3:15"> Then the king arranged for a meeting with the priest Ahimelech son of Ahitub and all the priests of his father’s house who were at Nob. They all came to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:6; 1 Samuel 22:7; Isaiah 65:1"> Then Saul said, “Listen, son of Ahitub.” He replied, “Here I am, my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:8; Luke 23:2–5; Amos 7:10"> Saul said to him, “Why have you conspired against me, you and this son of Jesse? You gave him bread and a sword and inquired of God on his behalf, so that he opposes me and waits in ambush, as is the case today!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:32; 1 Samuel 19:4–5; 1 Samuel 22:13"> Ahimelech replied to the king, “Who among all your servants is faithful like David? He is the king’s son-in-law, the leader of your bodyguard, and honored in your house!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:36; 1 Peter 3:16–17; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Was it just today that I began to inquire of God on his behalf? Far be it from me! The king should not accuse his servant or any of my father’s house. For your servant is not aware of all this – not in whole or in part!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 28:15; Daniel 3:19–20; 1 Kings 19:2"> But the king said, “You will surely die, Ahimelech, you and all your father’s house!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 1:17; 1 Samuel 14:45; 2 Kings 1:13–14"> Then the king said to the messengers who were stationed beside him, “Turn and kill the priests of the Lord, for they too have sided with David! They knew he was fleeing, but they did not inform me.” But the king’s servants refused to harm the priests of the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:10–11; Hosea 7:3; 1 Samuel 2:18"> Then the king said to Doeg, “You turn and strike down the priests!” So Doeg the Edomite turned and struck down the priests. He killed on that day eighty-five men who wore the linen ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Hosea 10:14; 1 Samuel 21:1"> As for Nob, the city of the priests, he struck down with the sword men and women, children and infants, oxen, donkeys, and sheep – all with the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 23:6; 1 Samuel 30:7; 1 Kings 2:26–27"> But one of the sons of Ahimelech son of Ahitub escaped and fled to David. His name was Abiathar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:1–9; Psalms 44:22"> Then David said to Abiathar, “I knew that day when Doeg the Edomite was there that he would certainly tell Saul! I am guilty of all the deaths in your father’s house!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Kings 2:26; Hebrews 12:1–3; John 10:28–30"> Stay with me. Don’t be afraid! Whoever seeks my life is seeking your life as well. You are secure with me.”</span>
</div>
</div>
