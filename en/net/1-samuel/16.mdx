## David Appears before Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 51:11; 1 Samuel 18:12; Judges 9:23"> Now the Spirit of the Lord had turned away from Saul, and an evil spirit from the Lord tormented him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then Saul’s servants said to him, “Look, an evil spirit from God is tormenting you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 3:15; 1 Kings 10:8; 1 Samuel 19:9"> Let our lord instruct his servants who are here before you to look for a man who knows how to play the lyre. Then whenever the evil spirit from God comes upon you, he can play the lyre and you will feel better.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> So Saul said to his servants, “Find me a man who plays well and bring him to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:19; Genesis 39:23; Genesis 39:2"> One of his attendants replied, “I have seen a son of Jesse in Bethlehem who knows how to play the lyre. He is a brave warrior and is articulate and handsome, for the Lord is with him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:11; 1 Samuel 17:15; Psalms 78:70–72"> So Saul sent messengers to Jesse and said, “Send me your son David, who is out with the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:27; Proverbs 18:16; 1 Samuel 17:18"> So Jesse took a donkey loaded with bread, a container of wine, and a young goat and sent them to Saul with his son David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 41:46; Proverbs 22:29; Psalms 146:3"> David came to Saul and stood before him. Saul liked him a great deal, and he became his armor bearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then Saul sent word to Jesse saying, “Let David be my servant, for I really like him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:14–16; 1 Samuel 18:10–11; Luke 11:24–26"> So whenever the spirit from God would come upon Saul, David would take his lyre and play it. This would bring relief to Saul and make him feel better. Then the evil spirit would leave him alone.</span>
</div>
