## God’s Judgment on Edom &#124; Edom’s Approaching Destruction

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Amos 1:11–12; Joel 3:19; Ezekiel 35:3–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The vision that Obadiah saw.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The Lord God says this concerning Edom:</span>God’s Judgment on Edom&#124;Edom’s Approaching Destruction<br /><span className="pl pl-1">We have heard a report from the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">An envoy was sent among the nations, saying,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Arise! Let us make war against Edom!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:18; Isaiah 23:9; Micah 7:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Lord says, “Look! I will make you a weak nation;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will be greatly despised!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 14:13–15; Proverbs 29:23; Jeremiah 49:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your presumptuous heart has deceived you –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you who reside in the safety of the rocky cliffs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whose home is high in the mountains.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You think to yourself,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘No one can bring me down to the ground!’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Amos 9:2; Job 20:6–7; Habakkuk 2:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even if you were to soar high like an eagle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">even if you were to make your nest among the stars,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I can bring you down even from there!” says the Lord.</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; Jeremiah 49:7; Jeremiah 30:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">All your allies will force you from your homeland!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your treaty partners will deceive you and overpower you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your trusted friends will set an ambush for you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that will take you by surprise!</span><br /></span>

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:13; Amos 1:12; Isaiah 63:1–3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your warriors will be shattered, O Teman,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that everyone will be destroyed from Esau’s mountain!</span></span>

## Edom’s Treachery Against Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 1:11; Malachi 1:3–4; Genesis 27:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Because you violently slaughtered your relatives, the people of Jacob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shame will cover you, and you will be destroyed forever.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 137:7; Nahum 3:10; Joel 3:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">You stood aloof while strangers took his army captive,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and foreigners advanced to his gates.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When they cast lots over Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you behaved as though you were in league with them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:15; Micah 4:11; Proverbs 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">You should not have gloated when your relatives suffered calamity.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You should not have rejoiced over the people of Judah when they were destroyed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You should not have boasted when they suffered adversity.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zechariah 1:15; Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 35:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">You should not have entered the city of my people when they experienced distress.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You should not have joined in gloating over their misfortune when they suffered distress.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You should not have looted their wealth when they endured distress.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:6; Psalms 31:8; Obadiah 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You should not have stood at the fork in the road to slaughter those trying to escape.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You should not have captured their refugees when they suffered adversity.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Coming Day of the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="James 2:13; Habakkuk 2:8; Ezekiel 30:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For the day of the Lord is approaching for all the nations!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Just as you have done, so it will be done to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will get exactly what your deeds deserve.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Jeremiah 49:12; Jeremiah 25:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">For just as you have drunk on my holy mountain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so all the nations will drink continually.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will drink, and they will gulp down;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they will be as though they had never been.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 21:27; Amos 9:11–15; Isaiah 14:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">But on Mount Zion there will be a remnant of those who escape,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it will be a holy place once again.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The descendants of Jacob will conquer</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who had conquered them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Isaiah 10:17; Obadiah 1:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">The descendants of Jacob will be a fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the descendants of Joseph a flame.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The descendants of Esau will be like stubble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will burn them up and devour them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There will not be a single survivor of the descendants of Esau!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, the Lord has spoken it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:44; Zephaniah 2:4–7; Amos 9:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">The people of the Negev will take possession of Esau’s mountain,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the people of the Shephelah will take</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">possession of the land of the Philistines.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will also take possession of the territory of Ephraim and the territory of Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the people of Benjamin will take possession of Gilead.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 17:9–10; Luke 4:26; Jeremiah 32:44"> <span className="pl pl-1">The exiles of this fortress of the people of Israel</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will take possession of what belongs to</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the people of Canaan, as far as Zarephath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will take possession of the towns of the Negev.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 14:9; James 5:20; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Those who have been delivered will go up on Mount Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in order to rule over Esau’s mountain.</span><br />Then the Lord will reign as King!</span>
