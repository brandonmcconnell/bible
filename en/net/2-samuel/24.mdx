<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:11; Psalms 115:14; Proverbs 14:28"> Joab replied to the king, “May the Lord your God make the army a hundred times larger right before the eyes of my lord the king! But why does my master the king want to do this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Ecclesiastes 8:4; 1 Chronicles 21:4"> But the king’s edict stood, despite the objections of Joab and the leaders of the army. So Joab and the leaders of the army left the king’s presence in order to muster the Israelite army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 13:9; Deuteronomy 2:36; Joshua 13:16"> They crossed the Jordan and camped at Aroer, on the south side of the city, at the wadi of Gad, near Jazer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 19:28; Genesis 31:47–48; Judges 18:28–29"> Then they went on to Gilead and to the region of Tahtim Hodshi, coming to Dan Jaan and on around to Sidon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 19:29; Joshua 11:3; Genesis 21:22–33"> Then they went to the fortress of Tyre and all the cities of the Hivites and the Canaanites. Then they went on to the Negev of Judah, to Beer Sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> They went through all the land and after nine months and twenty days came back to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:23–24; 1 Chronicles 21:5–6; Numbers 1:44–46"> Joab reported the number of warriors to the king. In Israel there were 800,000 sword-wielding warriors, and in Judah there were 500,000 soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:13; 1 Samuel 13:13; Proverbs 28:13"> David felt guilty after he had numbered the army. David said to the Lord, “I have sinned greatly by doing this! Now, O Lord, please remove the guilt of your servant, for I have acted very foolishly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:5; 1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Chronicles 29:29"> When David got up the next morning, the Lord had already spoken to Gad the prophet, David’s seer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 12:6–10; Revelation 3:19; Proverbs 3:12"> “Go, tell David, ‘This is what the Lord says: I am offering you three forms of judgment. Pick one of them and I will carry it out against you.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:12; Leviticus 26:25; 2 Samuel 21:1"> Gad went to David and told him, “Shall seven years of famine come upon your land? Or shall you flee for three months from your enemy with him in hot pursuit? Or shall there be three days of plague in your land? Now decide what I should tell the one who sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 51:1; Psalms 119:156; 1 Chronicles 21:13"> David said to Gad, “I am very upset! I prefer that we be attacked by the Lord, for his mercy is great; I do not want to be attacked by men!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:14; 1 Chronicles 27:24; Numbers 25:9"> So the Lord sent a plague through Israel from the morning until the completion of the appointed time. Seventy thousand men died from Dan to Beer Sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:13–14; Genesis 6:6; Exodus 12:23"> When the angel extended his hand to destroy Jerusalem, the Lord relented from his judgment. He told the angel who was killing the people, “That’s enough! Stop now!” (Now the Lord’s angel was near the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; 1 Chronicles 21:16–17; 2 Samuel 24:10"> When he saw the angel who was destroying the people, David said to the Lord, “Look, it is I who have sinned and done this evil thing! As for these sheep – what have they done? Attack me and my family.”</span>

## David Acquires a Threshing Floor and Constructs an Altar There

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:18–30; 2 Samuel 24:11"> So Gad went to David that day and told him, “Go up and build an altar for the Lord on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 11:8; Nehemiah 9:26; 2 Chronicles 36:16"> So David went up as Gad instructed him to do, according to the Lord’s instructions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 18:2; Ruth 2:10; 1 Chronicles 21:20–21"> When Araunah looked out and saw the king and his servants approaching him, he went out and bowed to the king with his face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:22; Numbers 25:8; 2 Samuel 21:3–14"> Araunah said, “Why has my lord the king come to his servant?” David replied, “To buy from you the threshing floor so I can build an altar for the Lord, so that the plague may be removed from the people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Kings 19:21; 1 Samuel 6:14; 1 Chronicles 21:22"> Araunah told David, “My lord the king may take whatever he wishes and offer it. Look! Here are oxen for burnt offerings, and threshing sledges and harnesses for wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:40–41; Isaiah 32:8; Psalms 20:3–4"> I, the servant of my lord the king, give it all to the king!” Araunah also told the king, “May the Lord your God show you favor!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Chronicles 21:24–25; Malachi 1:12–14; Genesis 23:13"> But the king said to Araunah, “No, I insist on buying it from you! I will not offer to the Lord my God burnt sacrifices that cost me nothing.” So David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty pieces of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:14; Lamentations 3:32–33; Genesis 8:20"> Then David built an altar for the Lord there and offered burnt sacrifices and peace offerings. And the Lord accepted prayers for the land, and the plague was removed from Israel.</span>
</div>
