## Shimei Curses David and His Men

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:16; 1 Samuel 17:43; Exodus 22:28"> Then King David reached Bahurim. There a man from Saul’s extended family named Shimei son of Gera came out, yelling curses as he approached.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> He threw stones at David and all of King David’s servants, as well as all the people and the soldiers who were on his right and on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:13; 1 Samuel 25:17; 1 Kings 21:10"> As he yelled curses, Shimei said, “Leave! Leave! You man of bloodshed, you wicked man!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; 2 Samuel 3:28–29; 1 Kings 2:32–33"> The Lord has punished you for all the spilled blood of the house of Saul, in whose place you rule. Now the Lord has given the kingdom into the hand of your son Absalom. Disaster has overtaken you, for you are a man of bloodshed!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 22:28; 2 Samuel 9:8; 1 Samuel 24:14"> Then Abishai son of Zeruiah said to the king, “Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? Let me go over and cut off his head!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:22; Romans 9:20; John 18:11"> But the king said, “What do we have in common, you sons of Zeruiah? If he curses because the Lord has said to him, ‘Curse David!’, who can say to him, ‘Why have you done this?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 17:1–4; Genesis 45:5; 2 Samuel 12:11–12"> Then David said to Abishai and to all his servants, “My own son, my very own flesh and blood, is trying to take my life. So also now this Benjaminite! Leave him alone so that he can curse, for the Lord has spoken to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Hebrews 12:10; Psalms 25:18"> Perhaps the Lord will notice my affliction and this day grant me good in place of his curse.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 23:23; 2 Samuel 16:5–6"> So David and his men went on their way. But Shimei kept going along the side of the hill opposite him, yelling curses as he threw stones and dirt at them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The king and all the people who were with him arrived exhausted at their destination, where David refreshed himself.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Advice of Ahithophel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:37; 2 Samuel 15:12"> Now when Absalom and all the men of Israel arrived in Jerusalem, Ahithophel was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 11:12; 1 Samuel 10:24; 1 Kings 1:25"> When David’s friend Hushai the Arkite came to Absalom, Hushai said to him, “Long live the king! Long live the king!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:25; 2 Samuel 15:32–37; Deuteronomy 32:6"> Absalom said to Hushai, “Do you call this loyalty to your friend? Why didn’t you go with your friend?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:13; 2 Samuel 5:1–3"> Hushai replied to Absalom, “No, I will be loyal to the one whom the Lord, these people, and all the men of Israel have chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:34; Psalms 55:21; 1 Samuel 28:2"> Moreover, whom should I serve? Should it not be his son? Just as I served your father, so I will serve you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; Psalms 37:12–13; Isaiah 29:15"> Then Absalom said to Ahithophel, “Give us your advice. What should we do?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:3; 2 Samuel 15:16; Zechariah 8:13"> Ahithophel replied to Absalom, “Have sex with your father’s concubines whom he left to care for the palace. All Israel will hear that you have made yourself repulsive to your father. Then your followers will be motivated to support you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:11–12; 2 Samuel 15:16; 2 Samuel 20:3"> So they pitched a tent for Absalom on the roof, and Absalom had sex with his father’s concubines in the sight of all Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 17:14; 2 Samuel 17:23"> In those days Ahithophel’s advice was considered as valuable as a prophetic revelation. Both David and Absalom highly regarded the advice of Ahithophel.</span>
</div>
</div>
