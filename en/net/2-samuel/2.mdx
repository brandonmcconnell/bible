## David’s Army Clashes with the Army of Saul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:50; Genesis 32:2; 2 Samuel 3:7–8"> Now Abner son of Ner, the general in command of Saul’s army, had taken Saul’s son Ish-bosheth and had brought him to Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 19:18; Joshua 13:8–11; Numbers 1:40"> He appointed him king over Gilead, the Geshurites, Jezreel, Ephraim, Benjamin, and all Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Ish-bosheth son of Saul was forty years old when he began to rule over Israel. He ruled two years. However, the people of Judah followed David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 2:11; 2 Samuel 5:4–5; 1 Chronicles 3:4"> David was king in Hebron over the people of Judah for seven and a half years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 18:25; Joshua 10:2; Joshua 9:3"> Then Abner son of Ner and the servants of Ish-bosheth son of Saul went out from Mahanaim to Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:16; 1 Chronicles 2:16; 2 Samuel 20:23"> Joab son of Zeruiah and the servants of David also went out and confronted them at the pool of Gibeon. One group stationed themselves on one side of the pool, and the other group on the other side of the pool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:17; Proverbs 10:23; Proverbs 17:14"> Abner said to Joab, “Let the soldiers get up and fight before us.” Joab said, “So be it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> So they got up and crossed over by number: twelve belonging to Benjamin and to Ish-bosheth son of Saul, and twelve from the servants of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> As they grappled with one another, each one stabbed his opponent with his sword and they fell dead together. So that place is called the Field of Flints; it is in Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1"> Now the battle was very severe that day; Abner and the men of Israel were overcome by David’s soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:8; Habakkuk 3:19; Song of Solomon 8:14"> The three sons of Zeruiah were there – Joab, Abishai, and Asahel. (Now Asahel was as quick on his feet as one of the gazelles in the field.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:21; Joshua 1:7; Proverbs 4:27"> Asahel chased Abner, without turning to the right or to the left as he followed Abner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then Abner turned and asked, “Is that you, Asahel?” He replied, “Yes it is!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 14:19"> Abner said to him, “Turn aside to your right or to your left. Capture one of the soldiers and take his equipment for yourself!” But Asahel was not willing to turn aside from following him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; Proverbs 29:1; 2 Kings 14:10–12"> So Abner spoke again to Asahel, “Turn aside from following me! I do not want to strike you to the ground. How then could I show my face in the presence of Joab your brother?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 4:6; 2 Samuel 5:6"> But Asahel refused to turn aside. So Abner struck him in the abdomen with the back end of his spear. The spear came out his back; Asahel collapsed on the spot and died there right before Abner. Everyone who now comes to the place where Asahel fell dead pauses in respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> So Joab and Abishai chased Abner. At sunset they came to the hill of Ammah near Giah on the way to the wilderness of Gibeon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> The Benjaminites formed their ranks behind Abner and were like a single army, standing at the top of a certain hill.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:14; Jeremiah 46:10; Acts 7:26"> Then Abner called out to Joab, “Must the sword devour forever? Don’t you realize that this will turn bitter in the end? When will you tell the people to turn aside from pursuing their brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 17:14; 2 Samuel 2:14; Proverbs 15:1"> Joab replied, “As surely as God lives, if you had not said this, it would have been morning before the people would have abandoned pursuit of their brothers!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then Joab blew the ram’s horn and all the people stopped in their tracks. They stopped chasing Israel and ceased fighting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8; Song of Solomon 2:17"> Abner and his men went through the Arabah all that night. They crossed the Jordan River and went through the whole region of Bitron and came to Mahanaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Now Joab returned from chasing Abner and assembled all the people. Nineteen of David’s soldiers were missing, in addition to Asahel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:1; 1 Kings 20:11"> But David’s soldiers had slaughtered the Benjaminites and Abner’s men – in all, 360 men had died!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 2:13–16; 1 Samuel 17:58; 2 Chronicles 21:1"> They took Asahel’s body and buried him in his father’s tomb at Bethlehem. Joab and his men then traveled all that night and reached Hebron by dawn.</span>
</div>
