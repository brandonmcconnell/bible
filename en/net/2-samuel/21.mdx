</div>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:19; Hebrews 9:22; 1 Samuel 26:19"> David said to the Gibeonites, “What can I do for you, and how can I make amends so that you will bless the Lord’s inheritance?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 49:6–8; 1 Peter 1:18–19"> The Gibeonites said to him, “We have no claim to silver or gold from Saul or from his family, nor would we be justified in putting to death anyone in Israel.” David asked, “What then are you asking me to do for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:1; Daniel 9:26; Matthew 7:2"> They replied to the king, “As for this man who exterminated us and who schemed against us so that we were destroyed and left without status throughout all the borders of Israel –</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:24; Numbers 25:4–5; 1 Samuel 11:4"> let seven of his male descendants be turned over to us, and we will execute them before the Lord in Gibeah of Saul, who was the Lord’s chosen one.” The king replied, “I will turn them over.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:15; 1 Samuel 18:3; 1 Samuel 23:18"> The king had mercy on Mephibosheth son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, in light of the Lord’s oath that had been taken between David and Jonathan son of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:19; 2 Samuel 3:7"> So the king took Armoni and Mephibosheth, the two sons of Aiah’s daughter Rizpah whom she had born to Saul, and the five sons of Saul’s daughter Merab whom she had born to Adriel the son of Barzillai the Meholathite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 1:22; Deuteronomy 21:1–9; 2 Samuel 6:17"> He turned them over to the Gibeonites, and they executed them on a hill before the Lord. The seven of them died together; they were put to death during harvest time – during the first days of the beginning of the barley harvest.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:23; 1 Kings 21:27; Joel 2:23"> Rizpah the daughter of Aiah took sackcloth and spread it out for herself on a rock. From the beginning of the harvest until the rain fell on them, she did not allow the birds of the air to feed on them by day, nor the wild animals by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:4; Ruth 2:11–12"> When David was told what Rizpah daughter of Aiah, Saul’s concubine, had done,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 17:11; 1 Samuel 31:10–13; 2 Samuel 2:5–7"> he went and took the bones of Saul and of his son Jonathan from the leaders of Jabesh Gilead. (They had secretly taken them from the plaza at Beth Shan. It was there that Philistines publicly exposed their corpses after they had killed Saul at Gilboa.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> David brought the bones of Saul and of Jonathan his son from there; they also gathered up the bones of those who had been executed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 18:28; 2 Samuel 24:25; Joshua 7:26"> They buried the bones of Saul and his son Jonathan in the land of Benjamin at Zela in the grave of his father Kish. After they had done everything that the king had commanded, God responded to their prayers for the land.</span>
</div>

## Israel Engages in Various Battles with the Philistines

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 14:10–11; 2 Samuel 5:22; Ecclesiastes 12:3"> Another battle was fought between the Philistines and Israel. So David went down with his soldiers and fought the Philistines. David became exhausted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 2 Samuel 21:20; 1 Samuel 17:4–5"> Now Ishbi-Benob, one of the descendants of Rapha, had a spear that weighed three hundred bronze shekels, and he was armed with a new weapon. He had said that he would kill David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 2 Samuel 18:3; Psalms 132:17"> But Abishai the son of Zeruiah came to David’s aid, striking the Philistine down and killing him. Then David’s men took an oath saying, “You will not go out to battle with us again! You must not extinguish the lamp of Israel!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:29; 2 Samuel 21:20; 2 Samuel 21:16"> Later there was another battle with the Philistines, this time in Gob. On that occasion Sibbekai the Hushathite killed Saph, who was one of the descendants of Rapha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:5; 1 Samuel 17:4–11; 1 Chronicles 11:26"> Yet another battle occurred with the Philistines in Gob. On that occasion Elhanan the son of Jair the Bethlehemite killed the brother of Goliath the Gittite, the shaft of whose spear was like a weaver’s beam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:18; 2 Samuel 21:16; 1 Chronicles 20:6"> Yet another battle occurred in Gath. On that occasion there was a large man who had six fingers on each hand and six toes on each foot, twenty-four in all! He too was a descendant of Rapha.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:10; 1 Samuel 17:36; 1 Samuel 16:9"> When he taunted Israel, Jonathan, the son of David’s brother Shimeah, killed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 20:8; Romans 8:31; Jeremiah 9:23"> These four were the descendants of Rapha who lived in Gath; they were killed by David and his soldiers.</span>
</div>
</div>
