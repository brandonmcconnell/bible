## David Learns of the Deaths of Saul and Jonathan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:6; 1 Samuel 31:6; 1 Samuel 30:17–26"> After the death of Saul, when David had returned from defeating the Amalekites, he stayed at Ziklag for two days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:12; 2 Samuel 4:10; Joshua 7:6"> On the third day a man arrived from the camp of Saul with his clothes torn and dirt on his head. When he approached David, the man threw himself to the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 1:15–19; 2 Kings 5:25"> David asked him, “Where are you coming from?” He replied, “I have escaped from the camp of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:16; 1 Samuel 31:1–6; 1 Chronicles 10:1–6"> David inquired, “How were things going? Tell me!” He replied, “The people fled from the battle and many of them fell dead. Even Saul and his son Jonathan are dead!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 25:2; Proverbs 14:15"> David said to the young man who was telling him this, “How do you know that Saul and his son Jonathan are dead?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Samuel 1:21; 1 Chronicles 10:4–6"> The young man who was telling him this said, “I just happened to be on Mount Gilboa and came across Saul leaning on his spear for support. The chariots and leaders of the horsemen were in hot pursuit of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:12; 2 Samuel 9:6; Judges 9:54"> When he turned around and saw me, he called out to me. I answered, ‘Here I am!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; 1 Samuel 30:13; 1 Samuel 30:1"> He asked me, ‘Who are you?’ I told him, ‘I’m an Amalekite.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> He said to me, ‘Stand over me and finish me off! I’m very dizzy, even though I’m still alive.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 7:2; Judges 9:54; 2 Kings 11:12"> So I stood over him and put him to death, since I knew that he couldn’t live in such a condition. Then I took the crown which was on his head and the bracelet which was on his arm. I have brought them here to my lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:31; Genesis 37:29; 2 Samuel 3:31"> David then grabbed his own clothes and tore them, as did all the men who were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Psalms 35:13–14; Matthew 5:44"> They lamented and wept and fasted until evening because Saul, his son Jonathan, the Lord’s people, and the house of Israel had fallen by the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:8"> David said to the young man who told this to him, “Where are you from?” He replied, “I am an Amalekite, the son of a resident foreigner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 26:9; 1 Samuel 31:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> David replied to him, “How is it that you were not afraid to reach out your hand to destroy the Lord’s anointed?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 2:34; Judges 8:20; 2 Samuel 4:10–12"> Then David called one of the soldiers and said, “Come here and strike him down!” So he struck him down, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Luke 19:22; Leviticus 20:9"> David said to him, “Your blood be on your own head! Your own mouth has testified against you, saying ‘I have put the Lord’s anointed to death.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## David’s Tribute to Saul and Jonathan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Genesis 50:11"> Then David chanted this lament over Saul and his son Jonathan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 10:13; Genesis 49:8; Deuteronomy 4:10"> (He gave instructions that the people of Judah should be taught “The Bow.” Indeed, it is written down in the Book of Yashar.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:27; 2 Samuel 1:25; Deuteronomy 4:7–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The beauty of Israel lies slain on your high places!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How the mighty have fallen!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Micah 1:10; 1 Samuel 18:6; Judges 11:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don’t report it in Gath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">don’t spread the news in the streets of Ashkelon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or the daughters of the Philistines will rejoice,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the daughters of the uncircumcised will celebrate!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 31:1; Isaiah 21:5; 1 Samuel 10:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">O mountains of Gilboa,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">may there be no dew or rain on you, nor fields of grain offerings!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For it was there that the shield of warriors was defiled;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the shield of Saul lies neglected without oil.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:4; Isaiah 34:6–7; 1 Samuel 14:6–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">From the blood of the slain, from the fat of warriors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the bow of Jonathan was not turned away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The sword of Saul never returned empty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Judges 14:18; Lamentations 4:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Saul and Jonathan were greatly loved during their lives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and not even in their deaths were they separated.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They were swifter than eagles, stronger than lions.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Jeremiah 2:32; Judges 5:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">O daughters of Israel, weep over Saul,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who clothed you in scarlet as well as jewelry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who put gold jewelry on your clothes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:19; 2 Samuel 1:27; Judges 5:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">How the warriors have fallen</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the midst of battle!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jonathan lies slain on your high places!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 20:17; 1 Samuel 23:16; 1 Samuel 20:41"> <span className="pl pl-1">I grieve over you, my brother Jonathan!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You were very dear to me.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your love was more special to me than the love of women.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:25; 2 Samuel 1:19; Ezekiel 39:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">How the warriors have fallen!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The weapons of war are destroyed!</span></span>
</div>
