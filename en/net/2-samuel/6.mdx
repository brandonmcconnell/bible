<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:1–4; 1 Kings 8:1; 1 Chronicles 13:6–14"> David again assembled all the best men in Israel, thirty thousand in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 4:4; 1 Samuel 7:1; Psalms 80:1"> David and all the men who were with him traveled to Baalah in Judah to bring up from there the ark of God which is called by the name of the Lord of hosts, who sits enthroned between the cherubim that are on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:7; Numbers 7:4–9; Numbers 4:5–12"> They loaded the ark of God on a new cart and carried it from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill. Uzzah and Ahio, the sons of Abinadab, were guiding the new cart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:7; 1 Samuel 7:1–2"> They brought it with the ark of God up from the house of Abinadab on the hill. Ahio was walking in front of the ark,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:8; Psalms 150:3–5; Amos 5:23"> while David and all Israel were energetically celebrating before the Lord, singing and playing various stringed instruments, tambourines, rattles, and cymbals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 13:9; Numbers 4:15; Numbers 4:19–20"> When they arrived at the threshing floor of Nacon, Uzzah reached out and grabbed hold of the ark of God, because the oxen stumbled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 6:19; 1 Chronicles 15:13; 1 Chronicles 13:10"> The Lord was so furious with Uzzah, he killed him on the spot for his negligence. He died right there beside the ark of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jonah 4:1; 1 Chronicles 13:11–12; Jonah 4:9"> David was angry because the Lord attacked Uzzah; so he called that place Perez Uzzah, which remains its name to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 119:120; 1 Chronicles 13:11–12; Numbers 17:12–13"> David was afraid of the Lord that day and said, “How will the ark of the Lord ever come to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:4–8; 2 Samuel 15:19; 1 Chronicles 15:18"> So David was no longer willing to bring the ark of the Lord to be with him in the City of David. David left it in the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 39:5; Genesis 30:27; Proverbs 3:9–10"> The ark of the Lord remained in the house of Obed-Edom the Gittite for three months. The Lord blessed Obed-Edom and all his family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:25–3; 1 Kings 8:1; Matthew 10:42"> David was told, “The Lord has blessed the family of Obed-Edom and everything he owns because of the ark of God.” So David went and joyfully brought the ark of God from the house of Obed-Edom to the City of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 8:5; 1 Chronicles 15:15; Numbers 4:15"> Those who carried the ark of the Lord took six steps and then David sacrificed an ox and a fatling calf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:18; Exodus 15:20; Psalms 150:4"> Now David, wearing a linen ephod, was dancing with all his strength before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 68:24–27; 1 Chronicles 15:28; 1 Chronicles 15:25"> David and all Israel were bringing up the ark of the Lord, shouting and blowing trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; 1 Chronicles 15:29; Psalms 69:7"> As the ark of the Lord entered the City of David, Saul’s daughter Michal looked out the window. When she saw King David leaping and dancing before the Lord, she despised him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Chronicles 15:1; 2 Chronicles 1:4; 1 Kings 8:5"> They brought the ark of the Lord and put it in its place in the middle of the tent that David had pitched for it. Then David offered burnt sacrifices and peace offerings before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 8:55; 1 Chronicles 16:2; Genesis 14:19"> When David finished offering the burnt sacrifices and peace offerings, he pronounced a blessing over the people in the name of the Lord of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:10; 2 Chronicles 35:7–8; 1 Chronicles 16:3"> He then handed out to each member of the entire assembly of Israel, both men and women, a portion of bread, a date cake, and a raisin cake. Then all the people went home.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 6:16; 2 Samuel 6:14; Judges 9:4"> When David went home to pronounce a blessing on his own house, Michal, Saul’s daughter, came out to meet him. She said, “How the king of Israel has distinguished himself this day! He has exposed himself today before his servants’ slave girls the way a vulgar fool might do!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 13:22; Psalms 89:19–20; 1 Samuel 13:14"> David replied to Michal, “It was before the Lord! I was celebrating before the Lord, who chose me over your father and his entire family and appointed me as leader over the Lord’s people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 4:14; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 51:7"> I am willing to shame and humiliate myself even more than this! But with the slave girls whom you mentioned let me be distinguished!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:25; Isaiah 22:14; Isaiah 4:1"> Now Michal, Saul’s daughter, had no children to the day of her death.</span>
