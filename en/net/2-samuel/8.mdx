He put two-thirds of them to death and spared the other third. The Moabites became David’s subjects and brought tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:47; 2 Samuel 10:16; 2 Samuel 10:19"> David defeated King Hadadezer son of Rehob of Zobah when he came to reestablish his authority over the Euphrates River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:6; Joshua 11:9; 1 Chronicles 18:4"> David seized from him 1,700 charioteers and 20,000 infantrymen. David cut the hamstrings of all but a hundred of the chariot horses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 11:23–25; Isaiah 7:8; Job 9:13"> The Arameans of Damascus came to help King Hadadezer of Zobah, but David killed 22,000 of the Arameans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; 2 Samuel 7:9; Psalms 18:34–46"> David placed garrisons in the territory of the Arameans of Damascus; the Arameans became David’s subjects and brought tribute. The Lord protected David wherever he campaigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 14:26–27; 1 Kings 10:16–17"> David took the golden shields that belonged to Hadadezer’s servants and brought them to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 18:8; 1 Chronicles 22:16; Ezekiel 47:16"> From Tebah and Berothai, Hadadezer’s cities, King David took a great deal of bronze.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 8:65; 2 Chronicles 8:4; 1 Chronicles 18:9"> When King Toi of Hamath heard that David had defeated the entire army of Hadadezer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:10; 1 Chronicles 18:10; Psalms 129:8"> he sent his son Joram to King David to extend his best wishes and to pronounce a blessing on him for his victory over Hadadezer, for Toi had been at war with Hadadezer. He brought with him various items made of silver, gold, and bronze.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 29:2; 1 Chronicles 22:14–16"> King David dedicated these things to the Lord, along with the dedicated silver and gold that he had taken from all the nations that he had subdued,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:14; 2 Samuel 8:2; 1 Chronicles 18:11"> including Aram, Moab, the Ammonites, the Philistines, and Amelek. This also included some of the plunder taken from King Hadadezer son of Rehob of Zobah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 14:7; 2 Samuel 7:9; 1 Chronicles 18:12"> David became famous when he returned from defeating the Arameans in the Valley of Salt, he defeated 18,000 in all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:29; Genesis 25:23; 2 Samuel 8:6"> He placed garrisons throughout Edom, and all the Edomites became David’s subjects. The Lord protected David wherever he campaigned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 5:15; Psalms 89:14; Psalms 72:2"> David reigned over all Israel; he guaranteed justice for all his people.</span>
</div>
