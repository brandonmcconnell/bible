## David’s Warriors

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:2; 1 Chronicles 11:11–47; 1 Chronicles 27:32"> These are the names of David’s warriors:<br />Josheb-Basshebeth, a Tahkemonite, was head of the officers. He killed eight hundred men with his spear in one battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:4; 1 Chronicles 11:12–14; 1 Samuel 17:26"> Next in command was Eleazar son of Dodo, the son of Ahohi. He was one of the three warriors who were with David when they defied the Philistines who were assembled there for battle. When the men of Israel retreated,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:5; 1 Samuel 11:13; Judges 15:14"> he stood his ground and fought the Philistines until his hand grew so tired that it seemed stuck to his sword. The Lord gave a great victory on that day. When the army returned to him, the only thing left to do was to plunder the corpses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:13–14; 1 Chronicles 11:27; 2 Samuel 23:33"> Next in command was Shammah son of Agee the Hararite. When the Philistines assembled at Lehi, where there happened to be an area of a field that was full of lentils, the army retreated before the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 21:31; 2 Samuel 23:10; Psalms 3:8"> But he made a stand in the middle of that area. He defended it and defeated the Philistines; the Lord gave them a great victory.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:1; 2 Samuel 5:18; Joshua 12:15"> At the time of the harvest three of the thirty leaders went down to David at the cave of Adullam. A band of Philistines was camped in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 22:4–5; 1 Samuel 13:4; 1 Samuel 10:5"> David was in the stronghold at the time, while a Philistine garrison was in Bethlehem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 4:14; Psalms 42:1–2; Isaiah 41:17"> David was thirsty and said, “How I wish someone would give me some water to drink from the cistern in Bethlehem near the gate!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:14; 2 Corinthians 5:14; Philippians 2:17"> So the three elite warriors broke through the Philistine forces and drew some water from the cistern in Bethlehem near the gate. They carried it back to David, but he refused to drink it. He poured it out as a drink offering to the Lord</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Mark 14:24; 1 Corinthians 15:30"> and said, “O Lord, I will not do this! It is equivalent to the blood of the men who risked their lives by going.” So he refused to drink it. Such were the exploits of the three elite warriors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:10; 2 Samuel 10:14; 1 Chronicles 11:20–21"> Abishai son of Zeruiah, the brother of Joab, was head of the three. He killed three hundred men with his spear and gained fame among the three.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:8; Matthew 13:23; 1 Corinthians 15:41"> From the three he was given honor and he became their officer, even though he was not one of the three.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:18; Joshua 15:21; 2 Samuel 20:23"> Benaiah son of Jehoida was a brave warrior from Kabzeel who performed great exploits. He struck down the two sons of Ariel of Moab. He also went down and killed a lion in a cistern on a snowy day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:51; 1 Chronicles 11:23; Colossians 2:15"> He also killed an impressive-looking Egyptian. The Egyptian wielded a spear, while Benaiah attacked him with a club. He grabbed the spear out of the Egyptian’s hand and killed him with his own spear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Such were the exploits of Benaiah son of Jehoida, who gained fame among the three elite warriors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 20:23; 1 Chronicles 27:6; 1 Samuel 22:14"> He received honor from the thirty warriors, though he was not one of the three elite warriors. David put him in charge of his bodyguard.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:18; 1 Chronicles 27:7; 1 Chronicles 11:26"> Included with the thirty were the following: Asahel the brother of Joab, Elhanan son of Dodo from Bethlehem,</span>

<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 21:18; 1 Chronicles 11:19; 1 Chronicles 11:28"> Abiezer the Anathothite, Mebunnai the Hushathite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Chronicles 27:13; 2 Kings 25:23; 1 Chronicles 11:30"> Zalmon the Ahohite, Maharai the Netophathite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:30–31; Joshua 18:28; 1 Chronicles 27:15"> Heled son of Baanah the Netophathite, Ittai son of Ribai from Gibeah in Benjamin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Judges 12:15; 1 Chronicles 27:14; Judges 12:13"> Benaiah the Pirathonite, Hiddai from the wadis of Gaash,</span>

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Samuel 11:3; 2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 10:6"> Eliphelet son of Ahasbai the Maacathite, Eliam son of Ahithophel the Gilonite,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:37; Joshua 15:55"> Hezrai the Carmelite, Paarai the Arbite,</span>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:37; 1 Chronicles 11:39; 2 Samuel 4:2"> Zelek the Ammonite, Naharai the Beerothite (the armor-bearer of Joab son of Zeruiah),</span>
