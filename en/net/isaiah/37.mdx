<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 1:9; Isaiah 36:20; Isaiah 10:22"> Perhaps the Lord your God will hear all these things the chief adviser has spoken on behalf of his master, the king of Assyria, who sent him to taunt the living God. </div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> When King Hezekiah’s servants came to Isaiah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 7:4; Isaiah 35:4; Mark 5:36"> Isaiah said to them, “Tell your master this: ‘This is what the Lord says: “Don’t be afraid because of the things you have heard – these insults the king of Assyria’s servants have hurled against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 31:8–9; 2 Chronicles 32:21; Isaiah 33:10–12"> Look, I will take control of his mind; he will receive a report and return to his own land. I will cut him down with a sword in his own land.”’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 10:29; Numbers 33:20–21; 2 Kings 19:8–9"> When the chief adviser heard the king of Assyria had departed from Lachish, he left and went to Libnah, where the king was campaigning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 18:1; Isaiah 20:5; Isaiah 37:7"> The king heard that King Tirhakah of Ethiopia was marching out to fight him. He again sent messengers to Hezekiah, ordering them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 36:15; 2 Kings 18:5; Matthew 27:43"> “Tell King Hezekiah of Judah this: ‘Don’t let your God in whom you trust mislead you when he says, “Jerusalem will not be handed over to the king of Assyria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 36:18–20; 2 Kings 18:33–35; Isaiah 14:17"> Certainly you have heard how the kings of Assyria have annihilated all lands. Do you really think you will be rescued?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; 2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 18:11"> Were the nations whom my predecessors destroyed – the nations of Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the people of Eden in Telassar – rescued by their gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 19:13; 2 Kings 17:24; Isaiah 36:19"> Where are the king of Hamath, the king of Arpad, and the kings of Lair, Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 19:14; Psalms 123:1–4; Joel 2:17–20"> Hezekiah took the letter from the messengers and read it. Then Hezekiah went up to the Lord’s temple and spread it out before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 7:8–9; Daniel 9:3–4; James 5:13"> Hezekiah prayed before the Lord:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 25:22; Psalms 99:1; Psalms 86:10"> “O Lord who commands armies, O God of Israel, who is enthroned on the cherubim! You alone are God over all the kingdoms of the earth. You made the sky and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 6:40; Psalms 17:6; Isaiah 37:4"> Pay attention, Lord, and hear! Open your eyes, Lord, and observe! Listen to this entire message Sennacherib sent and how he taunts the living God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Nahum 2:11–12; 2 Kings 15:29; 2 Kings 17:6"> It is true, Lord, that the kings of Assyria have destroyed all the nations and their lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 44:9–10; Jeremiah 10:11; Hosea 8:6"> They have burned the gods of the nations, for they are not really gods, but only the product of human hands manufactured from wood and stone. That is why the Assyrians could destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 46:10; 1 Kings 18:36–37; Psalms 83:17–18"> Now, O Lord our God, rescue us from his power, so all the kingdoms of the earth may know that you alone are the Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Isaiah 38:3–6; Acts 4:31"> Isaiah son of Amoz sent this message to Hezekiah: “This is what the Lord God of Israel says: ‘Because you prayed to me concerning King Sennacherib of Assyria,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 16:4; Lamentations 2:13; Isaiah 1:8"> this is what the Lord says about him:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The virgin daughter Zion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">despises you – she makes fun of you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">daughter Jerusalem</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shakes her head after you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:7; Daniel 5:20–23; Isaiah 17:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Whom have you taunted and hurled insults at?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">At whom have you shouted</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and looked so arrogantly?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">At the Holy One of Israel!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 14:8; Isaiah 10:18; Exodus 15:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">Through your messengers you taunted the sovereign master,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘With my many chariots I climbed up</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the high mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the slopes of Lebanon.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I cut down its tall cedars</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and its best evergreens.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I invaded its most remote regions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">its thickest woods.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 20:10; Isaiah 36:12; 2 Kings 19:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I dug wells</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and drank water.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With the soles of my feet I dried up</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all the rivers of Egypt.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 4:27–28; Acts 2:23; 1 Peter 2:8"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Certainly you must have heard!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Long ago I worked it out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in ancient times I planned it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and now I am bringing it to pass.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The plan is this:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Fortified cities will crash</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">into heaps of ruins.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 129:6; Psalms 37:2; 1 Peter 1:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their residents are powerless;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they are terrified and ashamed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are as short-lived as plants in the field</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or green vegetation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are as short-lived as grass on the rooftops</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when it is scorched by the east wind.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 2:13; Psalms 139:1–11; Jeremiah 23:23–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">I know where you live</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and everything you do</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and how you rage against me.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:4; Isaiah 30:28; Isaiah 37:34"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because you rage against me</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the uproar you create has reached my ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will put my hook in your nose,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and my bridle between your lips,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will lead you back</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the way you came.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 19:29; Leviticus 25:4–5; 1 Kings 13:3–5"> <br />“This will be your reminder that I have spoken the truth: This year you will eat what grows wild, and next year what grows on its own. But the year after that you will plant seed and harvest crops; you will plant vines and consume their produce.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; 2 Kings 19:30–31; Psalms 80:9"> Those who remain in Judah will take root in the ground and bear fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 9:7; 2 Kings 19:31; Isaiah 59:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">“For a remnant will leave Jerusalem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">survivors will come out of Mount Zion.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The intense devotion of the Lord who commands armies will accomplish this.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 17:12; Isaiah 10:32–34; Isaiah 17:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">So this is what the Lord says about the king of Assyria:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘He will not enter this city,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor will he shoot an arrow here.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will not attack it with his shielded warriors,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor will he build siege works against it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 37:29; Proverbs 21:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will go back the way he came –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he will not enter this city,’ says the Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Kings 20:6; Isaiah 38:6; Isaiah 31:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will shield this city and rescue it for the sake of my reputation and because of my promise to David my servant.”’”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 10:12; 2 Kings 19:35; Isaiah 10:33–34"> The Lord’s messenger went out and killed 185,000 troops in the Assyrian camp. When they got up early the next morning, there were all the corpses!</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jonah 1:2; Jonah 3:3; Genesis 10:11–12"> So King Sennacherib of Assyria broke camp and went on his way. He went home and stayed in Nineveh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 8:4; Jeremiah 51:27; Ezra 4:2"> One day, as he was worshiping in the temple of his god Nisroch, his sons Adrammelech and Sharezer struck him down with the sword. They ran away to the land of Ararat; his son Esarhaddon replaced him as king.</span>
