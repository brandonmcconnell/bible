<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 4:8–9; Psalms 72:19; Numbers 14:21"> They called out to one another, “Holy, holy, holy is the Lord who commands armies! His majestic splendor fills the entire earth!”</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 8:3–5; Hebrews 9:22–26; Ezekiel 10:2"> But then one of the seraphs flew toward me. In his hand was a hot coal he had taken from the altar with tongs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; 1 John 1:7; Isaiah 43:25"> He touched my mouth with it and said, “Look, this coal has touched your lips. Your evil is removed; your sin is forgiven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:10–13; Acts 26:16–17; Ephesians 3:8"> I heard the voice of the sovereign master say, “Whom will I send? Who will go on our behalf?” I answered, “Here I am, send me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 13:14–15; Luke 8:10; Mark 4:12"> He said, “Go and tell these people:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Listen continually, but don’t understand!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Look continually, but don’t perceive!’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:15; Acts 28:27; Jeremiah 5:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Make the hearts of these people calloused;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">make their ears deaf and their eyes blind!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Otherwise they might see with their eyes and hear with their ears,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their hearts might understand and they might repent and be healed.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 24:1–12; Psalms 94:3; Leviticus 26:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">I replied, “How long, sovereign master?” He said,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Until cities are in ruins and unpopulated,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and houses are uninhabited,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the land is ruined and devastated,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:29; Deuteronomy 28:64; 2 Kings 25:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">and the Lord has sent the people off to a distant place,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the very heart of the land is completely abandoned.</span><br /></span>
