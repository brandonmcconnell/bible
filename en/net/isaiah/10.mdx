## The Lord Turns on Arrogant Assyria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:20–24; Isaiah 14:25; Isaiah 30:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Assyria, the club I use to vent my anger, is as good as dead,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a cudgel with which I angrily punish.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 9:17; Jeremiah 25:9; Zechariah 10:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">I sent him against a godless nation,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I ordered him to attack the people with whom I was angry,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to take plunder and to carry away loot,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to trample them down like dirt in the streets.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Micah 4:11–12; Acts 2:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">But he does not agree with this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">his mind does not reason this way,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for his goal is to destroy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to eliminate many nations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Kings 18:24; 2 Kings 19:10; Ezekiel 26:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, he says:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Are not my officials all kings?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 35:20; 2 Kings 16:9; Genesis 10:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Is not Calneh like Carchemish?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Hamath like Arpad?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Samaria like Damascus?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 10:14; 2 Kings 19:17–19; 2 Kings 18:33–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">I overpowered kingdoms ruled by idols,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whose carved images were more impressive than Jerusalem’s or Samaria’s.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:10–13; Isaiah 2:8; Isaiah 36:19–20"> <span className="pl pl-1">As I have done to Samaria and its idols,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so I will do to Jerusalem and its idols.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 19:31; Isaiah 65:7; Jeremiah 50:18"> But when the sovereign master finishes judging Mount Zion and Jerusalem, then I will punish the king of Assyria for what he has proudly planned and for the arrogant attitude he displays.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30; Ezekiel 26:2"> For he says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“By my strong hand I have accomplished this,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">by my strategy that I devised.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I invaded the territory of nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and looted their storehouses.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Like a mighty conqueror, I brought down rulers.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 31:25; Proverbs 21:6–7; Nahum 2:9–1"> <span className="pl pl-1">My hand discovered the wealth of the nations, as if it were in a nest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as one gathers up abandoned eggs,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I gathered up the whole earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">There was no wing flapping,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or open mouth chirping.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 9:20–21; Isaiah 45:9; Isaiah 10:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">Does an ax exalt itself over the one who wields it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or a saw magnify itself over the one who cuts with it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">As if a scepter should brandish the one who raises it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or a staff should lift up what is not made of wood!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:21; Acts 12:23; Psalms 106:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this reason the sovereign master, the Lord who commands armies, will make his healthy ones emaciated. His majestic glory will go up in smoke.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 27:4; Numbers 11:1–3; Isaiah 37:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The light of Israel will become a fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their Holy One will become a flame;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it will burn and consume the Assyrian king’s briers</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his thorns in one day.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 19:23; Isaiah 10:33–34; Isaiah 9:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The splendor of his forest and his orchard</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be completely destroyed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as when a sick man’s life ebbs away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 21:17; Isaiah 37:36"> <span className="pl pl-1">There will be so few trees left in his forest,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a child will be able to count them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 37:31–32; Isaiah 50:10; Isaiah 1:9"> At that time those left in Israel, those who remain of the family of Jacob, will no longer rely on a foreign leader that abuses them. Instead they will truly rely on the Lord, the Holy One of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6; 2 Corinthians 3:14–16; Hosea 7:10"> A remnant will come back, a remnant of Jacob, to the mighty God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 9:27–28; Isaiah 6:13; Revelation 20:8"> For though your people, Israel, are as numerous as the sand on the seashore, only a remnant will come back. Destruction has been decreed; just punishment is about to engulf you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 28:22; Isaiah 24:1–23; Romans 9:28"> The sovereign master, the Lord who commands armies, is certainly ready to carry out the decreed destruction throughout the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22–24; Isaiah 37:6; Isaiah 61:3"> So here is what the sovereign master, the Lord who commands armies, says: “My people who live in Zion, do not be afraid of Assyria, even though they beat you with a club and lift their cudgel against you as Egypt did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 11:36; 2 Kings 19:35; Hebrews 10:37"> For very soon my fury will subside, and my anger will be directed toward their destruction.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Judges 7:25; 2 Kings 19:35"> The Lord who commands armies is about to beat them with a whip, similar to the way he struck down Midian at the rock of Oreb. He will use his staff against the sea, lifting it up as he did in Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 9:4; Luke 4:18; Isaiah 14:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">At that time</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the Lord will remove their burden from your shoulders,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their yoke from your neck;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the yoke will be taken off because your neck will be too large.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:2; 1 Samuel 13:2; 1 Samuel 13:5"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">They attacked Aiath,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">moved through Migron,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">depositing their supplies at Micmash.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 13:23; 1 Samuel 7:17; Joshua 21:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">They went through the pass,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">spent the night at Geba.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Ramah trembled,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Gibeah of Saul ran away.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:44; Jeremiah 1:1; Joshua 21:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shout out, daughter of Gallim!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Pay attention, Laishah!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Answer her, Anathoth!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 15:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Madmenah flees,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the residents of Gebim have hidden.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:1; Isaiah 37:22; Isaiah 19:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">This very day, standing in Nob,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they shake their fist at Daughter Zion’s mountain –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">at the hill of Jerusalem.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Amos 2:9; Daniel 4:37; Isaiah 37:38"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, the sovereign master, the Lord who commands armies,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">is ready to cut off the branches with terrifying power.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The tallest trees will be cut down,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the loftiest ones will be brought low.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 37:24; Daniel 4:23; Jeremiah 48:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">The thickets of the forest will be chopped down with an ax,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and mighty Lebanon will fall.</span></span>
