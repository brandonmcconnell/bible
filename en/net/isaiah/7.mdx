<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 15:37; 2 Kings 16:1; Isaiah 1:1"> During the reign of Ahaz son of Jotham, son of Uzziah, king of Judah, King Rezin of Syria and King Pekah son of Remaliah of Israel marched up to Jerusalem to do battle, but they were unable to prevail against it.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 8:12; Isaiah 7:13; Isaiah 9:9"> It was reported to the family of David, “Syria has allied with Ephraim.” They and their people were emotionally shaken, just as the trees of the forest shake before the wind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 36:2; Romans 9:27"> So the Lord told Isaiah, “Go out with your son Shear-jashub and meet Ahaz at the end of the conduit of the upper pool which is located on the road to the field where they wash and dry cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Lamentations 3:26; Deuteronomy 20:3"> Tell him, ‘Make sure you stay calm! Don’t be afraid! Don’t be intimidated by these two stubs of smoking logs, or by the raging anger of Rezin, Syria, and the son of Remaliah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 83:3–4; Zechariah 1:15; Psalms 2:2"> Syria has plotted with Ephraim and the son of Remaliah to bring about your demise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> They say, “Let’s attack Judah, terrorize it, and conquer it. Then we’ll set up the son of Tabeel as its king.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 8:10; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 46:10–11"> For this reason the sovereign master, the Lord, says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“It will not take place;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it will not happen.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 17:1–3; Genesis 14:15; 2 Samuel 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Syria’s leader is Damascus,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the leader of Damascus is Rezin.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Within sixty-five years Ephraim will no longer exist as a nation.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 11:6; Romans 11:20; 2 Chronicles 20:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">Ephraim’s leader is Samaria,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Samaria’s leader is the son of Remaliah.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">If your faith does not remain firm,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">then you will not remain secure.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 8:5; Isaiah 1:5; Isaiah 10:20"> The Lord again spoke to Ahaz:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 37:30; Isaiah 38:7–8; Isaiah 38:22"> “Ask for a confirming sign from the Lord your God. You can even ask for something miraculous.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 2 Chronicles 28:22; Malachi 3:15"> But Ahaz responded, “I don’t want to ask; I don’t want to put the Lord to a test.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 34:18"> So Isaiah replied, “Pay attention, family of David. Do you consider it too insignificant to try the patience of men? Is that why you are also trying the patience of my God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 1:23; Isaiah 9:6; Luke 1:31"> For this reason the sovereign master himself will give you a confirming sign. Look, this young woman is about to conceive and will give birth to a son. You, young woman, will name him Immanuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 7:22; Amos 5:15; Matthew 3:4"> He will eat sour milk and honey, which will help him know how to reject evil and choose what is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:4; Deuteronomy 1:39; Isaiah 9:11"> Here is why this will be so: Before the child knows how to reject evil and choose what is right, the land whose two kings you fear will be desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7–8; Isaiah 10:5–6; 2 Chronicles 28:19–21"> The Lord will bring on you, your people, and your father’s family a time unlike any since Ephraim departed from Judah – the king of Assyria!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 5:26; Joshua 24:12; Deuteronomy 1:44"> At that time the Lord will whistle for flies from the distant streams of Egypt and for bees from the land of Assyria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 2:19; Jeremiah 16:16; Isaiah 2:21"> All of them will come and make their home in the ravines between the cliffs, and in the crevices of the cliffs, in all the thorn bushes, and in all the watering holes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 8:7; Ezekiel 29:18; Isaiah 10:15"> At that time the sovereign master will use a razor hired from the banks of the Euphrates River, the king of Assyria, to shave the head and the pubic hair; it will also shave off the beard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 5:17; Jeremiah 39:10; Isaiah 37:30"> At that time a man will keep alive a young cow from the herd and a couple of goats.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15; Matthew 3:4; 2 Samuel 17:29"> From the abundance of milk they produce, he will have sour milk for his meals. Indeed, everyone left in the heart of the land will eat sour milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 5:6; Song of Solomon 8:11–12; Matthew 21:33"> At that time every place where there had been a thousand vines worth a thousand shekels will be overrun with thorns and briers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 27:3"> With bow and arrow men will hunt there, for the whole land will be covered with thorns and briers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 7:21–22; Isaiah 13:20–22; Isaiah 5:17"> They will stay away from all the hills that were cultivated, for fear of the thorns and briers. Cattle will graze there and sheep will trample on them.</span>
</div>
