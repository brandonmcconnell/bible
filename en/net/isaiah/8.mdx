## Darkness Turns to Light as an Ideal King Arrives

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 1:19; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Isaiah 30:8–11"> Then you must recall the Lord’s instructions and the prophetic testimony of what would happen. Certainly they say such things because their minds are spiritually darkened.</span>
