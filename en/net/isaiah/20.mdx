<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; Jeremiah 25:29–30; Amos 1:8"> The Lord revealed the following message during the year in which King Sargon of Assyria sent his commanding general to Ashdod, and he fought against it and captured it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 1:8; Matthew 3:4; 1 Samuel 19:24"> At that time the Lord announced through Isaiah son of Amoz: “Go, remove the sackcloth from your waist and take your sandals off your feet.” He did as instructed and walked around in undergarments and barefoot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:18; Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:5–6"> Later the Lord explained, “In the same way that my servant Isaiah has walked around in undergarments and barefoot for the past three years, as an object lesson and omen pertaining to Egypt and Cush,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 19:4; Jeremiah 13:26; Jeremiah 13:22"> so the king of Assyria will lead away the captives of Egypt and the exiles of Cush, both young and old. They will be in undergarments and barefoot, with the buttocks exposed; the Egyptians will be publicly humiliated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 30:5; Isaiah 30:3; 2 Kings 18:21"> Those who put their hope in Cush and took pride in Egypt will be afraid and embarrassed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; 1 Thessalonians 5:3; Hebrews 2:3"> At that time those who live on this coast will say, ‘Look what has happened to our source of hope to whom we fled for help, expecting to be rescued from the king of Assyria! How can we escape now?’”</span>
