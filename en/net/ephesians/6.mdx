## Exhortations for Spiritual Warfare

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:31; Joshua 1:9"> Finally, be strengthened in the Lord and in the strength of his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Romans 13:12; Ephesians 6:13"> Clothe yourselves with the full armor of God so that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 4:4; Ephesians 2:2"> For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the powers, against the world rulers of this darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:4; Luke 21:36; Ephesians 6:11–17"> For this reason, take up the full armor of God so that you may be able to stand your ground on the evil day, and having done everything, to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:8; Isaiah 11:5"> Stand firm therefore, by fastening the belt of truth around your waist, by putting on the breastplate of righteousness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 52:7; Romans 10:15; 2 Corinthians 5:18–21"> by fitting your feet with the preparation that comes from the good news of peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–9; 1 John 5:4–5; Proverbs 18:10"> and in all of this, by taking up the shield of faith with which you can extinguish all the flaming arrows of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Isaiah 59:17; Revelation 19:15"> And take the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:17; Colossians 4:2; Philippians 4:6"> With every prayer and petition, pray at all times in the Spirit, and to this end be alert, with all perseverance and requests for all the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 4:3; Acts 4:29; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> Pray for me also, that I may be given the message when I begin to speak – that I may confidently make known the mystery of the gospel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Colossians 4:4; 2 Timothy 2:9"> for which I am an ambassador in chains. Pray that I may be able to speak boldly as I ought to speak.</span>
</div>
</div>
