## Live in the Light

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 1:18; Colossians 2:8; Matthew 24:4"> Let nobody deceive you with empty words, for because of these things God’s wrath comes on the sons of disobedience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 18:4; Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 5:22"> Therefore do not be partakers with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 8:12; 1 John 1:7; Acts 26:18"> for you were at one time darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of the light –</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 3:9–10; Galatians 5:22–23; 3 John 1:11"> for the fruit of the light consists in all goodness, righteousness, and truth –</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 12:1–2; Hebrews 12:28; Psalms 19:14"> trying to learn what is pleasing to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:12; 1 Timothy 5:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:6"> Do not participate in the unfruitful deeds of darkness, but rather expose them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ephesians 5:3; Jeremiah 23:24; Ecclesiastes 12:14"> For the things they do in secret are shameful even to mention.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 3:20–21; 1 Corinthians 4:5; Hosea 2:10"> But all things being exposed by the light are made evident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 60:1; Romans 13:11–12; Isaiah 26:19"> For everything made evident is light, and for this reason it says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Awake, O sleeper!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Rise from the dead,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and Christ will shine on you!”</span></span>

## Live Wisely

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; James 3:13; Philippians 1:27"> Therefore be very careful how you live – not as unwise but as wise,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 4:5; Amos 5:13; John 12:35"> taking advantage of every opportunity, because the days are evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 12:2; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Colossians 1:9"> For this reason do not be foolish, but be wise by understanding what the Lord’s will is.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Luke 1:15; Romans 13:13"> And do not get drunk with wine, which is debauchery, but be filled by the Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; 1 Corinthians 14:26; Psalms 95:2"> speaking to one another in psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing and making music in your hearts to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Hebrews 13:15"> always giving thanks to God the Father for each other in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; Ephesians 5:24; Ephesians 5:22"> and submitting to one another out of reverence for Christ.</span>
</div>

## Exhortations to Households

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:18–1; 1 Peter 3:1–6; Titus 2:5"> Wives, submit to your husbands as to the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:3–10; Colossians 1:18; Ephesians 1:22–23"> because the husband is the head of the wife as also Christ is the head of the church – he himself being the savior of the body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 5:33; Colossians 3:20; Titus 2:7"> But as the church submits to Christ, so also wives should submit to their husbands in everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Peter 3:7; Colossians 3:19; Ephesians 5:28"> Husbands, love your wives just as Christ loved the church and gave himself for her</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 15:3; Ezekiel 36:25"> to sanctify her by cleansing her with the washing of the water by the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 1:22; Hebrews 9:14; 1 Peter 1:19"> so that he may present the church to himself as glorious – not having a stain or wrinkle, or any such blemish, but holy and blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ephesians 5:25; Matthew 19:5; Genesis 2:21–24"> In the same way husbands ought to love their wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:14–15; Ephesians 5:31; Ecclesiastes 4:5"> For no one has ever hated his own body but he feeds it and takes care of it, just as Christ also does the church,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:15; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; Romans 12:5"> for we are members of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:5; Mark 10:7–8"> For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and will be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Revelation 21:2; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> This mystery is great – but I am actually speaking with reference to Christ and the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Peter 3:2–7; Esther 1:20; Ephesians 5:25"> Nevertheless, each one of you must also love his own wife as he loves himself, and the wife must respect her husband.</span>
</div>
