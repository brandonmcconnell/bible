<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:12; Ephesians 6:12; 1 Corinthians 2:7"> The purpose of this enlightenment is that through the church the multifaceted wisdom of God should now be disclosed to the rulers and the authorities in the heavenly realms.</span>

## Prayer for Strengthened Love

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 95:6; Ephesians 1:16–19; Philippians 2:10"> For this reason I kneel before the Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 1:10; Revelation 5:8–14; Ephesians 1:21"> from whom every family in heaven and on the earth is named.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 1:11; Philippians 4:13; Isaiah 40:29–31"> I pray that according to the wealth of his glory he may grant you to be strengthened with power through his Spirit in the inner person,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Colossians 2:7; 1 John 4:16; John 14:23"> that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith, so that, because you have been rooted and grounded in love,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 103:11–12; Job 11:7–9; Ephesians 1:18–23"> you may be able to comprehend with all the saints what is the breadth and length and height and depth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 1:23; Colossians 2:9–10; Colossians 1:10"> and thus to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled up to all the fullness of God.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:8; Romans 4:21; Jeremiah 32:27"> Now to him who by the power that is working within us is able to do far beyond all that we ask or think,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jude 1:25; Romans 11:36; 2 Peter 3:18"> to him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus to all generations, forever and ever. Amen.</span>
</div>
