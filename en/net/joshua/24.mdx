<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 23:2; 1 Samuel 10:19; Genesis 33:18–19"> Joshua assembled all the Israelite tribes at Shechem. He summoned Israel’s elders, rulers, judges, and leaders, and they appeared before God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:15; Ezekiel 16:3; Genesis 31:30"> Joshua told all the people, “Here is what the Lord God of Israel says: ‘In the distant past your ancestors lived beyond the Euphrates River, including Terah the father of Abraham and Nahor. They worshiped other gods,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 12:1–4; Genesis 21:2–3; Acts 7:2–3"> but I took your father Abraham from beyond the Euphrates and brought him into the entire land of Canaan. I made his descendants numerous; I gave him Isaac,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 36:8; Deuteronomy 2:5; Genesis 25:24–26"> and to Isaac I gave Jacob and Esau. To Esau I assigned Mount Seir, while Jacob and his sons went down to Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 3:10; Exodus 7:1–12; Psalms 105:26–36"> I sent Moses and Aaron, and I struck Egypt down when I intervened in their land. Then I brought you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 7:36; Hebrews 11:29; Isaiah 63:12–13"> When I brought your fathers out of Egypt, you arrived at the sea. The Egyptians chased your fathers with chariots and horsemen to the Red Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Exodus 14:10; Exodus 14:20"> Your fathers cried out for help to the Lord; he made the area between you and the Egyptians dark, and then drowned them in the sea. You witnessed with your very own eyes what I did in Egypt. You lived in the wilderness for a long time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Psalms 135:10–11; Nehemiah 9:22"> Then I brought you to the land of the Amorites who lived east of the Jordan. They fought with you, but I handed them over to you; you conquered their land and I destroyed them from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 11:25; Micah 6:5; Numbers 22:2"> Balak son of Zippor, king of Moab, launched an attack against Israel. He summoned Balaam son of Beor to call down judgment on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:5; Numbers 24:5–10; Isaiah 54:17"> I refused to respond to Balaam; he kept prophesying good things about you, and I rescued you from his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 4:10–12; Joshua 3:14–17; Psalms 114:5"> You crossed the Jordan and came to Jericho. The leaders of Jericho, as well as the Amorites, Perizzites, Canaanites, Hittites, Girgashites, Hivites, and Jebusites, fought with you, but I handed them over to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:20; Exodus 23:28; Psalms 44:3–6"> I sent terror ahead of you to drive out before you the two Amorite kings. I gave you the victory; it was not by your swords or bows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:10–12; Deuteronomy 8:7; Joshua 21:45"> I gave you a land in which you had not worked hard; you took up residence in cities you did not build and you are eating the produce of vineyards and olive groves you did not plant.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:24; Deuteronomy 10:12; Genesis 35:2"> Now obey the Lord and worship him with integrity and loyalty. Put aside the gods your ancestors worshiped beyond the Euphrates and in Egypt and worship the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; John 6:67–68; Ruth 1:15–16"> If you have no desire to worship the Lord, choose today whom you will worship, whether it be the gods whom your ancestors worshiped beyond the Euphrates, or the gods of the Amorites in whose land you are living. But I and my family will worship the Lord!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 10:38–39; 1 Samuel 12:23; Romans 6:2"> The people responded, “Far be it from us to abandon the Lord so we can worship other gods!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Isaiah 63:7–14; Isaiah 46:4"> For the Lord our God took us and our fathers out of slavery in the land of Egypt and performed these awesome miracles before our very eyes. He continually protected us as we traveled and when we passed through nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Micah 4:2; Exodus 10:2; Psalms 116:16"> The Lord drove out from before us all the nations, including the Amorites who lived in the land. So we too will worship the Lord, for he is our God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 5:16; Exodus 23:21; Psalms 99:9"> Joshua warned the people, “You will not keep worshiping the Lord, for he is a holy God. He is a jealous God who will not forgive your rebellion or your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 10:26–27; Ezekiel 18:24; 1 Chronicles 28:9"> If you abandon the Lord and worship foreign gods, he will turn against you; he will bring disaster on you and destroy you, though he once treated you well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Isaiah 44:5; Deuteronomy 5:27–28"> The people said to Joshua, “No! We really will worship the Lord!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 119:173; Psalms 119:11; Job 15:6"> Joshua said to the people, “Do you agree to be witnesses against yourselves that you have chosen to worship the Lord?” They replied, “We are witnesses!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16–18; Joshua 24:14; Hebrews 12:28–29"> Joshua said, “Now put aside the foreign gods that are among you and submit to the Lord God of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 24:7; Exodus 24:3; Deuteronomy 5:27–29"> The people said to Joshua, “We will worship the Lord our God and obey him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:38; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Exodus 15:25"> That day Joshua drew up an agreement for the people, and he established rules and regulations for them in Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Genesis 35:4; Joshua 4:20–24"> Joshua wrote these words in the Law Scroll of God. He then took a large stone and set it up there under the oak tree near the Lord’s shrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 22:34; Joshua 22:27–28"> Joshua said to all the people, “Look, this stone will be a witness against you, for it has heard everything the Lord said to us. It will be a witness against you if you deny your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Judges 2:6"> When Joshua dismissed the people, they went to their allotted portions of land.</span>
</div>

## An Era Ends

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Judges 2:8; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; Deuteronomy 34:5"> After all this Joshua son of Nun, the Lord’s servant, died at the age of one hundred ten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 19:50; Judges 2:9; 2 Samuel 23:30"> They buried him in his allotted territory in Timnath Serah in the hill country of Ephraim, north of Mount Gaash.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Judges 2:7; Deuteronomy 31:13; Deuteronomy 11:2"> Israel worshiped the Lord throughout Joshua’s lifetime and as long as the elderly men who outlived him remained alive. These men had experienced firsthand everything the Lord had done for Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 50:25; Genesis 33:19; Exodus 13:19"> The bones of Joseph, which the Israelites had brought up from Egypt, were buried at Shechem in the part of the field that Jacob bought from the sons of Hamor, the father of Shechem, for one hundred pieces of money. So it became the inheritance of the tribe of Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 22:13; Exodus 6:25; Isaiah 57:1–2"> Eleazar son of Aaron died, and they buried him in Gibeah in the hill country of Ephraim, where his son Phinehas had been assigned land.</span>
