## A New Generation is Circumcised

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:6; Deuteronomy 10:16; Exodus 4:25"> At that time the Lord told Joshua, “Make flint knives and circumcise the Israelites once again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 16:24; Genesis 17:23–27"> So Joshua made flint knives and circumcised the Israelites on the Hill of the Foreskins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; 1 Corinthians 10:5; Deuteronomy 2:16"> This is why Joshua had to circumcise them: All the men old enough to fight when they left Egypt died on the journey through the desert after they left Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:8–9; Hosea 6:6–7; Matthew 12:7"> Now all the men who left were circumcised, but all the sons born on the journey through the desert after they left Egypt were uncircumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:23; Deuteronomy 2:14; Hebrews 3:11"> Indeed, for forty years the Israelites traveled through the desert until all the men old enough to fight when they left Egypt, the ones who had disobeyed the Lord, died off. For the Lord had sworn a solemn oath to them that he would not let them see the land he had sworn on oath to give them, a land rich in milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 14:31; Deuteronomy 1:39"> He replaced them with their sons, whom Joshua circumcised. They were uncircumcised; their fathers had not circumcised them along the way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 34:25"> When all the men had been circumcised, they stayed there in the camp until they had healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 4:19; 1 Samuel 17:26; Genesis 34:14"> The Lord said to Joshua, “Today I have taken away the disgrace of Egypt from you.” So that place is called Gilgal even to this day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 12:6; Numbers 9:1–5; Ezekiel 12:3"> So the Israelites camped in Gilgal and celebrated the Passover in the evening of the fourteenth day of the month on the plains of Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 23:14; Exodus 13:6–7; Leviticus 23:6"> They ate some of the produce of the land the day after the Passover, including unleavened bread and roasted grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 16:35; Isaiah 65:13–14; Proverbs 13:22"> The manna stopped appearing the day they ate some of the produce of the land; the Israelites never ate manna again.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Israel Conquers Jericho

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 18:2; Numbers 22:23; Numbers 22:31"> When Joshua was near Jericho, he looked up and saw a man standing in front of him holding a drawn sword. Joshua approached him and asked him, “Are you on our side or allied with our enemies?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 55:4; Genesis 17:3; Revelation 19:10–14"> He answered, “Truly I am the commander of the Lord’s army. Now I have arrived!” Joshua bowed down with his face to the ground and asked, “What does my master want to say to his servant?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Acts 7:32–33; 2 Peter 1:18"> The commander of the Lord’s army answered Joshua, “Remove your sandals from your feet, because the place where you stand is holy.” Joshua did so.</span>
</div>
