<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 26:29; Genesis 41:51; Genesis 50:23"> The tribe of Manasseh, Joseph’s firstborn son, was also allotted land. The descendants of Makir, Manasseh’s firstborn and the father of Gilead, received land, for they were warriors. They were assigned Gilead and Bashan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:18; Numbers 26:29–32; Judges 6:11"> The rest of Manasseh’s descendants were also assigned land by their clans, including the descendants of Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida. These are the male descendants of Manasseh son of Joseph by their clans.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 26:33; Numbers 36:2–11"> Now Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Makir, son of Manasseh, had no sons, only daughters. These are the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Numbers 27:5–7; Galatians 3:28"> They went before Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the leaders and said, “The Lord told Moses to assign us land among our relatives.” So Joshua assigned them land among their uncles, as the Lord had commanded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 17:2–3; Numbers 32:30–42; Joshua 13:29–31"> Manasseh was allotted ten shares of land, in addition to the land of Gilead and Bashan east of the Jordan,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> for the daughters of Manasseh were assigned land among his sons. The land of Gilead belonged to the rest of the descendants of Manasseh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 21:21; Joshua 20:7; Joshua 24:32"> The border of Manasseh went from Asher to Micmethath which is near Shechem. It then went south toward those who live in Tappuah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 16:8; Joshua 15:34; Joshua 12:17"> (The land of Tappuah belonged to Manasseh, but Tappuah, located on the border of Manasseh, belonged to the tribe of Ephraim.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 16:3; Joshua 19:29; Joshua 16:8–9"> The border then descended southward to the Valley of Kanah. Ephraim was assigned cities there among the cities of Manasseh, but the border of Manasseh was north of the valley and ended at the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Ephraim’s territory was to the south, and Manasseh’s to the north. The sea was Manasseh’s western border and their territory touched Asher on the north and Issachar on the east.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Chronicles 7:29; 2 Kings 9:27; Judges 5:19"> Within Issachar’s and Asher’s territory Manasseh was assigned Beth Shean, Ibleam, the residents of Dor, En Dor, the residents of Taanach, the residents of Megiddo, the three of Napheth, and the towns surrounding all these cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Judges 1:27–28; Exodus 23:29–33; Romans 6:12–14"> But the men of Manasseh were unable to conquer these cities; the Canaanites managed to remain in those areas.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Joshua 16:10; Philippians 4:13; Judges 1:35"> Whenever the Israelites were strong militarily, they forced the Canaanites to do hard labor, but they never totally conquered them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 26:34–37; Genesis 48:19; Genesis 48:22"> The descendants of Joseph said to Joshua, “Why have you assigned us only one tribal allotment? After all, we have many people, for until now the Lord has enabled us to increase in number.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; Genesis 14:5; Luke 12:48"> Joshua replied to them, “Since you have so many people, go up into the forest and clear out a place to live in the land of the Perizzites and Rephaites, for the hill country of Ephraim is too small for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 4:12; Judges 4:3; Judges 1:19"> The descendants of Joseph said, “The whole hill country is inadequate for us, and the Canaanites living down in the valley in Beth Shean and its surrounding towns and in the Valley of Jezreel have chariots with iron-rimmed wheels.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Joshua said to the family of Joseph – to both Ephraim and Manasseh: “You have many people and great military strength. You will not have just one tribal allotment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 13:6; Romans 8:31; Numbers 14:6–9"> The whole hill country will be yours; though it is a forest, you can clear it and it will be entirely yours. You can conquer the Canaanites, though they have chariots with iron-rimmed wheels and are strong.”</span>
</div>
