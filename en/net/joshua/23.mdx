<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 13:1; Joshua 21:44; Joshua 11:23"> A long time passed after the Lord made Israel secure from all their enemies, and Joshua was very old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 24:1; Deuteronomy 31:28; 1 Chronicles 28:1"> So Joshua summoned all Israel, including the elders, rulers, judges, and leaders, and told them: “I am very old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Joshua 10:42; Joshua 10:14"> You saw everything the Lord your God did to all these nations on your behalf, for the Lord your God fights for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 13:6–7; Joshua 18:10; Joshua 13:2"> See, I have parceled out to your tribes these remaining nations, from the Jordan to the Mediterranean Sea in the west, including all the nations I defeated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:23; Joshua 13:6; Exodus 33:2"> The Lord your God will drive them out from before you and remove them, so you can occupy their land as the Lord your God promised you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:32; Deuteronomy 28:14; Proverbs 4:26–27"> Be very strong! Carefully obey all that is written in the law scroll of Moses so you won’t swerve from it to the right or the left,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 23:13; Psalms 16:4; Deuteronomy 7:2–3"> or associate with these nations that remain near you. You must not invoke or make solemn declarations by the names of their gods! You must not worship or bow down to them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:20; Joshua 22:5; Deuteronomy 13:4"> But you must be loyal to the Lord your God, as you have been to this very day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 1:5; Deuteronomy 11:23; Joshua 23:5"> “The Lord drove out from before you great and mighty nations; no one has been able to resist you to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:30; Leviticus 26:8; Deuteronomy 3:22"> One of you makes a thousand run away, for the Lord your God fights for you as he promised you he would.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Joshua 22:5; Deuteronomy 6:5–12; Proverbs 4:23"> Watch yourselves carefully! Love the Lord your God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Ezekiel 18:24; Zephaniah 1:6"> But if you ever turn away and make alliances with these nations that remain near you, and intermarry with them and establish friendly relations with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:16; Numbers 33:55; Exodus 23:33"> know for certain that the Lord our God will no longer drive out these nations from before you. They will trap and ensnare you; they will be a whip that tears your sides and thorns that blind your eyes until you disappear from this good land the Lord your God gave you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 21:33; 1 Kings 2:2; Leviticus 26:3–13"> “Look, today I am about to die. You know with all your heart and being that not even one of all the faithful promises the Lord your God made to you is left unfulfilled; every one was realized – not one promise is unfulfilled!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Leviticus 26:14–46; Judges 3:8"> But in the same way every faithful promise the Lord your God made to you has been realized, it is just as certain, if you disobey, that the Lord will bring on you every judgment until he destroys you from this good land which the Lord your God gave you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:25–26; 2 Kings 24:20"> If you violate the covenantal laws of the Lord your God which he commanded you to keep, and follow, worship, and bow down to other gods, the Lord will be very angry with you and you will disappear quickly from the good land which he gave to you.”</span>
</div>
