<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:31; James 2:25; Matthew 1:5"> Joshua son of Nun sent two spies out from Shittim secretly and instructed them: “Find out what you can about the land, especially Jericho.” They stopped at the house of a prostitute named Rahab and spent the night there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 43:13; Daniel 4:35; Proverbs 21:30"> The king of Jericho received this report: “Note well! Israelite men have come here tonight to spy on the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Samuel 10:3; Genesis 42:9–12; Acts 12:6"> So the king of Jericho sent this order to Rahab: “Turn over the men who came to you – the ones who came to your house – for they have come to spy on the whole land!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 6:19; 2 Samuel 17:19–20; 2 Samuel 16:18–19"> But the woman hid the two men and replied, “Yes, these men were clients of mine, but I didn’t know where they came from.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:20; Ezekiel 47:12; Revelation 21:25"> When it was time to shut the city gate for the night, the men left. I don’t know where they were heading. Chase after them quickly, for you have time to catch them!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; James 2:25; Colossians 3:3"> (Now she had taken them up to the roof and had hidden them in the stalks of flax she had spread out on the roof.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 3:28; Joshua 2:5; Judges 12:5"> Meanwhile the king’s men tried to find them on the road to the Jordan River near the fords. The city gate was shut as soon as they set out in pursuit of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Now before the spies went to sleep, Rahab went up to the roof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 2:11"> She said to the men, “I know the Lord is handing this land over to you. We are absolutely terrified of you, and all who live in the land are cringing before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Exodus 15:14–16; Exodus 14:21–31"> For we heard how the Lord dried up the water of the Red Sea before you when you left Egypt and how you annihilated the two Amorite kings, Sihon and Og, on the other side of the Jordan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Joshua 5:1; Joshua 7:5"> When we heard the news we lost our courage and no one could even breathe for fear of you. For the Lord your God is God in heaven above and on earth below!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joshua 2:18; Joshua 9:18–20; 2 Timothy 1:16–18"> So now, promise me this with an oath sworn in the Lord’s name. Because I have shown allegiance to you, show allegiance to my family. Give me a solemn pledge</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> that you will spare the lives of my father, mother, brothers, sisters, and all who belong to them, and rescue us from death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 5:7; Judges 1:24–25; Genesis 24:49"> The men said to her, “If you die, may we die too! If you do not report what we’ve been up to, then, when the Lord hands the land over to us, we will show unswerving allegiance to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 2 Corinthians 11:33; Joshua 6:20"> Then Rahab let them down by a rope through the window. (Her house was built as part of the city wall; she lived in the wall.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:25; 1 Samuel 23:14; Psalms 11:1"> She told them, “Head to the hill country, so the ones chasing you don’t find you. Hide from them there for three days, long enough for those chasing you to return. Then you can be on your way.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Numbers 30:2; 2 Samuel 21:7; Genesis 24:3–8"> The men said to her, “We are not bound by this oath you made us swear unless the following conditions are met:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 6:23; Joshua 2:21; Joshua 2:12–13"> When we invade the land , tie this red rope in the window through which you let us down, and gather together in your house your father, mother, brothers, and all who live in your father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 27:31; 1 Kings 2:36–42; Exodus 12:13"> Anyone who leaves your house will be responsible for his own death – we are innocent in that case! But if anyone with you in the house is harmed, we will be responsible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Proverbs 11:13"> If you should report what we’ve been up to, we are not bound by this oath you made us swear.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 2:5; Joshua 2:18; Matthew 7:24"> She said, “I agree to these conditions.” She sent them on their way and then tied the red rope in the window.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:10–12; 2 Samuel 17:20; Psalms 32:6–7"> They went to the hill country and stayed there for three days, long enough for those chasing them to return. Their pursuers looked all along the way but did not find them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Then the two men returned – they came down from the hills, crossed the river, came to Joshua son of Nun, and reported to him all they had discovered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 23:31; Joshua 1:8; Joshua 2:9–11"> They told Joshua, “Surely the Lord is handing over all the land to us! All who live in the land are cringing before us!”</span>
