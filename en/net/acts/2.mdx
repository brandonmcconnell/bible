## Peter’s Address on the Day of Pentecost

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 51:7; Isaiah 51:1; Deuteronomy 27:9"> But Peter stood up with the eleven, raised his voice, and addressed them: “You men of Judea and all you who live in Jerusalem, know this and listen carefully to what I say.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:5–8; 1 Samuel 1:15; Matthew 20:3"> In spite of what you think, these men are not drunk, for it is only nine o’clock in the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32"> But this is what was spoken about through the prophet Joel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joel 2:28–32; Isaiah 44:3; Titus 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘And in the last days it will be,’ God says,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘that I will pour out my Spirit on all people,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your sons and your daughters will prophesy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your young men will see visions,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your old men will dream dreams.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Galatians 3:28; Colossians 3:11; 1 Corinthians 7:21–22"> <span className="pl pl-1">Even on my servants, both men and women,&#42;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will pour out my Spirit in those days, and they will prophesy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joel 2:30–31; Zephaniah 1:14–18; Malachi 4:1–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">And I will perform wonders in the sky above&#42;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and miraculous signs on the earth below,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">blood and fire and clouds of smoke.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Amos 8:9; Matthew 24:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sun will be changed to darkness</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the moon to blood</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">before the great and glorious day of the Lord comes.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 10:12–13; Joel 2:32; Psalms 86:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">And then everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 3:2; John 14:10–11; Matthew 12:28"> “Men of Israel, listen to these words: Jesus the Nazarene, a man clearly attested to you by God with powerful deeds, wonders, and miraculous signs that God performed among you through him, just as you yourselves know –</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 22:22; Acts 4:28; Acts 3:18"> this man, who was handed over by the predetermined plan and foreknowledge of God, you executed by nailing him to a cross at the hands of Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:14; 1 Peter 1:21; Acts 2:32"> But God raised him up, having released him from the pains of death, because it was not possible for him to be held in its power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 16:8–11; Isaiah 41:13; Psalms 62:6"> For David says about him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘I saw the Lord always in front of me,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for he is at my right hand so that I will not be shaken.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 16:9; Psalms 63:5; Psalms 71:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore my heart was glad and my tongue rejoiced;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my body also will live in hope,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 16:10; Acts 2:31; Revelation 1:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">because you will not leave my soul in Hades,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor permit your Holy One to experience decay.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 16:11; Psalms 25:4; Psalms 21:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">You have made known to me the paths of life;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will make me full of joy with your presence.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 13:36; 1 Kings 2:10; Acts 7:8–9"> “Brothers, I can speak confidently to you about our forefather David, that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is with us to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 132:11–18; Psalms 89:3–4; Jeremiah 33:14–15"> So then, because he was a prophet and knew that God had sworn to him with an oath to seat one of his descendants on his throne,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 2:27; Psalms 16:10; Acts 13:35"> David by foreseeing this spoke about the resurrection of the Christ, that he was neither abandoned to Hades, nor did his body experience decay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 3:15; Acts 1:8; Acts 1:22"> This Jesus God raised up, and we are all witnesses of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 1:4; Acts 5:31; Acts 2:17"> So then, exalted to the right hand of God, and having received the promise of the Holy Spirit from the Father, he has poured out what you both see and hear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Matthew 22:42–45; Hebrews 1:13"> For David did not ascend into heaven, but he himself says,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at my right hand&#42;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–3; Romans 16:20; Isaiah 60:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet.”’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 5:30–31; Romans 14:8–12; 2 Corinthians 5:10"> Therefore let all the house of Israel know beyond a doubt that God has made this Jesus whom you crucified both Lord and Christ.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 3:10; Acts 22:10; Hebrews 4:12–13"> Now when they heard this, they were acutely distressed and said to Peter and the rest of the apostles, “What should we do, brothers?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 22:16; Mark 16:16; Acts 8:12"> Peter said to them, “Repent, and each one of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 44:3; Genesis 17:7–8; Romans 8:30"> For the promise is for you and your children, and for all who are far away, as many as the Lord our God will call to himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Philippians 2:15; Deuteronomy 32:5; Matthew 17:17"> With many other words he testified and exhorted them saying, “Save yourselves from this perverse generation!”</span>

## The Fellowship of the Early Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Hebrews 10:25; Acts 1:14; 1 John 1:3"> They were devoting themselves to the apostles’ teaching and to fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 7:16; John 14:12; Luke 8:37"> Reverential awe came over everyone, and many wonders and miraculous signs came about by the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 4:32; 1 John 3:16–18; 2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> All who believed were together and held everything in common,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="1 John 3:17; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; James 2:14–16"> and they began selling their property and possessions and distributing the proceeds to everyone, as anyone had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Acts 5:42; Acts 20:7; Acts 2:42"> Every day they continued to gather together by common consent in the temple courts, breaking bread from house to house, sharing their food with glad and humble hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 16:5; Acts 5:13–14; Acts 2:41"> praising God and having the good will of all the people. And the Lord was adding to their number every day those who were being saved.</span>
