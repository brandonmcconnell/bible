## Paul Continues to Minister at Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 28:23; Acts 1:3; Acts 9:20–22"> So Paul entered the synagogue and spoke out fearlessly for three months, addressing and convincing them about the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 19:23; Acts 9:2; Acts 18:6–8"> But when some were stubborn and refused to believe, reviling the Way before the congregation, he left them and took the disciples with him, addressing them every day in the lecture hall of Tyrannus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 20:31; 2 Timothy 1:15; Acts 16:6"> This went on for two years, so that all who lived in the province of Asia, both Jews and Greeks, heard the word of the Lord.</span>

## The Seven Sons of Sceva

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 16:17–20; Acts 5:12; Romans 15:18–19"> God was performing extraordinary miracles by Paul’s hands,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 5:15; 2 Kings 13:20–21; Mark 16:17"> so that when even handkerchiefs or aprons that had touched his body were brought to the sick, their diseases left them and the evil spirits went out of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 12:27; Mark 9:38; Luke 11:19"> But some itinerant Jewish exorcists tried to invoke the name of the Lord Jesus over those who were possessed by evil spirits, saying, “I sternly warn you by Jesus whom Paul preaches.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> (Now seven sons of a man named Sceva, a Jewish high priest, were doing this.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 16:17–18; Luke 4:33–35; Mark 1:34"> But the evil spirit replied to them, “I know about Jesus and I am acquainted with Paul, but who are you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 8:29; Mark 5:3–4; Mark 5:15"> Then the man who was possessed by the evil spirit jumped on them and beat them all into submission. He prevailed against them so that they fled from that house naked and wounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 1:12; Acts 5:5; Philippians 2:9–11"> This became known to all who lived in Ephesus, both Jews and Greeks; fear came over them all, and the name of the Lord Jesus was praised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 10:10; Psalms 32:5; Matthew 3:6"> Many of those who had believed came forward, confessing and making their deeds known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 13:8; Exodus 32:20; Genesis 35:4"> Large numbers of those who had practiced magic collected their books and burned them up in the presence of everyone. When the value of the books was added up, it was found to total fifty thousand silver coins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 12:24; Acts 6:7; Isaiah 55:11"> In this way the word of the Lord continued to grow in power and to prevail.</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Riot in Ephesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 20:22; Acts 20:16; Acts 23:11"> Now after all these things had taken place, Paul resolved to go to Jerusalem, passing through Macedonia and Achaia. He said, “After I have been there, I must also see Rome.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 16:23; 2 Timothy 4:20; Acts 16:3"> So after sending two of his assistants, Timothy and Erastus, to Macedonia, he himself stayed on for a while in the province of Asia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 19:9; Acts 24:22; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> At that time a great disturbance took place concerning the Way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Acts 16:16; Acts 16:19"> For a man named Demetrius, a silversmith who made silver shrines of Artemis, brought a great deal of business to the craftsmen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:19; Hosea 12:7–8; Revelation 18:11–19"> He gathered these together, along with the workmen in similar trades, and said, “Men, you know that our prosperity comes from this business.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 17:29; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 44:10–20"> And you see and hear that this Paul has persuaded and turned away a large crowd, not only in Ephesus but in practically all of the province of Asia, by saying that gods made by hands are not gods at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:11; 1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 23:13"> There is danger not only that this business of ours will come into disrepute, but also that the temple of the great goddess Artemis will be regarded as nothing, and she whom all the province of Asia and the world worship will suffer the loss of her greatness.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 13:4; Revelation 17:13; Acts 16:19–24"> When they heard this they became enraged and began to shout, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 20:4; Colossians 4:10; Acts 27:2"> The city was filled with the uproar, and the crowd rushed to the theater together, dragging with them Gaius and Aristarchus, the Macedonians who were Paul’s traveling companions.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 21:39; 2 Samuel 18:2–3; Acts 17:22–31"> But when Paul wanted to enter the public assembly, the disciples would not let him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:10; Acts 21:12; Acts 16:6"> Even some of the provincial authorities who were his friends sent a message to him, urging him not to venture into the theater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 21:34; Acts 19:40; Matthew 11:7–9"> So then some were shouting one thing, some another, for the assembly was in confusion, and most of them did not know why they had met together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 12:17; 2 Timothy 4:14; 1 Timothy 1:20"> Some of the crowd concluded it was about Alexander because the Jews had pushed him to the front. Alexander, gesturing with his hand, was wanting to make a defense before the public assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 19:28; Matthew 6:7; Revelation 13:4"> But when they recognized that he was a Jew, they all shouted in unison, “Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!” for about two hours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:10–11; 1 Timothy 4:2; Ephesians 2:12"> After the city secretary quieted the crowd, he said, “Men of Ephesus, what person is there who does not know that the city of the Ephesians is the keeper of the temple of the great Artemis and of her image that fell from heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Proverbs 14:29; Acts 5:35–39; Proverbs 25:8"> So because these facts are indisputable, you must keep quiet and not do anything reckless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:3; Romans 2:22; Acts 25:8"> For you have brought these men here who are neither temple robbers nor blasphemers of our goddess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Acts 13:7; 1 Corinthians 6:1; Acts 18:14"> If then Demetrius and the craftsmen who are with him have a complaint against someone, the courts are open and there are proconsuls; let them bring charges against one another there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> But if you want anything in addition, it will have to be settled in a legal assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 17:5–8; Matthew 26:5; Acts 20:1"> For we are in danger of being charged with rioting today, since there is no cause we can give to explain this disorderly gathering.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:17; Psalms 65:7; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10"> After he had said this, he dismissed the assembly.</span>
</div>
</div>
