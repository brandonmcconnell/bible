<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 15:13; Exodus 12:40–41; Galatians 3:17"> But God spoke as follows: ‘Your descendants will be foreigners in a foreign country, whose citizens will enslave them and mistreat them for four hundred years.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 41:54–57; Genesis 45:11; Genesis 42:5"> Then a famine occurred throughout Egypt and Canaan, causing great suffering, and our ancestors could not find food.</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Hebrews 11:23; Exodus 2:2–10; 1 Samuel 16:12"> At that time Moses was born, and he was beautiful to God. For three months he was brought up in his father’s house,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 11:24; Exodus 2:2–10; Deuteronomy 32:26"> and when he had been abandoned, Pharaoh’s daughter adopted him and brought him up as her own son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 19:11; Daniel 1:17–20; 2 Chronicles 9:22"> So Moses was trained in all the wisdom of the Egyptians and was powerful in his words and deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 2:11–12; Proverbs 21:1; Hebrews 11:24–26"> But when he was about forty years old, it entered his mind to visit his fellow countrymen the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Acts 7:28; John 18:25–27"> When he saw one of them being hurt unfairly, Moses came to his defense and avenged the person who was mistreated by striking down the Egyptian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 15:7; Mark 9:32; 2 Kings 5:1"> He thought his own people would understand that God was delivering them through him, but they did not understand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 2:13–15; Proverbs 18:19; 1 John 3:11–15"> The next day Moses saw two men fighting, and tried to make peace between them, saying, ‘Men, you are brothers; why are you hurting one another?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 7:35; Luke 12:14; Acts 7:54"> But the man who was unfairly hurting his neighbor pushed Moses aside, saying, ‘Who made you a ruler and judge over us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> You don’t want to kill me the way you killed the Egyptian yesterday, do you?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 18:2–4; Exodus 2:14–22; Exodus 4:19–20"> When the man said this, Moses fled and became a foreigner in the land of Midian, where he became the father of two sons.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 3:6; Matthew 22:32; Job 4:14"> ‘I am the God of your forefathers, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.’ Moses began to tremble and did not dare to look more closely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 3:5; Joshua 5:15; Ecclesiastes 5:1"> But the Lord said to him, ‘Take the sandals off your feet, for the place where you are standing is holy ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 3:7–10; Exodus 4:31; Psalms 106:44"> I have certainly seen the suffering of my people who are in Egypt and have heard their groaning, and I have come down to rescue them. Now come, I will send you to Egypt&#42;.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Luke 19:14; Psalms 113:7–8"> This same Moses they had rejected, saying, ‘Who made you a ruler and judge?’ God sent as both ruler and deliverer through the hand of the angel who appeared to him in the bush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 12:41; Exodus 14:21; Exodus 33:1"> This man led them out, performing wonders and miraculous signs in the land of Egypt, at the Red Sea, and in the wilderness for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 3:22–23; John 18:37"> This is the Moses who said to the Israelites, ‘God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Romans 3:2; Hebrews 5:12; 1 Peter 4:11"> This is the man who was in the congregation in the wilderness with the angel who spoke to him at Mount Sinai, and with our ancestors, and he received living oracles to give to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 14:3–4; Numbers 11:5; Nehemiah 9:16–17"> Our ancestors were unwilling to obey him, but pushed him aside and turned back to Egypt in their hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 32:1; Exodus 32:23"> saying to Aaron, ‘Make us gods who will go in front of us, for this Moses, who led us out of the land of Egypt – we do not know what has happened to him!’</span>

<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Amos 5:25–26; Ezekiel 20:39; Jeremiah 19:13"> But God turned away from them and gave them over to worship the host of heaven, as it is written in the book of the prophets: ‘It was not to me that you offered slain animals and sacrifices forty years in the wilderness, was it, house of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Amos 5:26–27; Exodus 20:4–5; Deuteronomy 5:8–9"> But you took along the tabernacle of Moloch and the star of the god Rephan, the images you made to worship, but I will deport you beyond Babylon&#42;.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Exodus 38:21; Hebrews 8:5"> Our ancestors had the tabernacle of testimony in the wilderness, just as God who spoke to Moses ordered him to make it according to the design he had seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Psalms 44:2; Joshua 18:1; Joshua 23:9"> Our ancestors received possession of it and brought it in with Joshua when they dispossessed the nations that God drove out before our ancestors, until the time of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 132:1–5; Acts 13:22; 2 Samuel 7:1–5"> He found favor with God and asked that he could find a dwelling place for the house of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 Kings 8:20; 1 Kings 6:37–38; 2 Samuel 7:13"> But Solomon built a house for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 17:24–25; Isaiah 66:1–2; 1 Kings 8:27"> Yet the Most High does not live in houses made by human hands, as the prophet says,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Isaiah 66:1; Psalms 11:4; John 4:21"> <span className="pl pl-1">‘Heaven is my throne,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and earth is the footstool for my feet.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What kind of house will you build for me, says the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">or what is my resting place?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Psalms 33:6–9; Acts 14:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Did my hand not make all these things?’</span></span>
