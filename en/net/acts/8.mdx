## Philip Preaches in Samaria

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 10:23; Acts 11:19; Acts 8:1"> Now those who had been forced to scatter went around proclaiming the good news of the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 6:5; Acts 21:8; Acts 8:35–36"> Philip went down to the main city of Samaria and began proclaiming the Christ to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 4:41–42; 2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 20:15–16"> The crowds were paying attention with one mind to what Philip said, as they heard and saw the miraculous signs he was performing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:1; Acts 14:8–10; Mark 16:17–18"> For unclean spirits, crying with loud shrieks, were coming out of many who were possessed, and many paralyzed and lame people were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 13:52; Acts 13:48; Acts 8:39"> So there was great joy in that city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 13:6; Acts 5:36; John 7:18"> Now in that city was a man named Simon, who had been practicing magic and amazing the people of Samaria, claiming to be someone great.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:34; 2 Peter 2:2; Revelation 13:3"> All the people, from the least to the greatest, paid close attention to him, saying, “This man is the power of God that is called ‘Great.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 8:9; Galatians 3:1; Isaiah 44:25"> And they paid close attention to him because he had amazed them for a long time with his magic.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 1:3; Matthew 28:19"> But when they believed Philip as he was proclaiming the good news about the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, they began to be baptized, both men and women.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:6–7; John 5:20; Acts 19:11"> Even Simon himself believed, and after he was baptized, he stayed close to Philip constantly, and when he saw the signs and great miracles that were occurring, he was amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:2; Galatians 2:9"> Now when the apostles in Jerusalem heard that Samaria had accepted the word of God, they sent Peter and John to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:38; John 16:23–24; John 14:13–14"> These two went down and prayed for them so that they would receive the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 19:2; Matthew 28:19"> (For the Spirit had not yet come upon any of them, but they had only been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 19:6; Acts 6:6; 2 Timothy 1:6"> Then Peter and John placed their hands on the Samaritans, and they received the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:5; Matthew 10:8; 2 Kings 8:9"> Now Simon, when he saw that the Spirit was given through the laying on of the apostles’ hands, offered them money,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Luke 14:7–11; 3 John 1:9; John 5:44"> saying, “Give me this power too, so that everyone I place my hands on may receive the Holy Spirit.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 5:17; Matthew 10:8; Acts 2:38"> But Peter said to him, “May your silver perish with you, because you thought you could acquire God’s gift with money!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; Psalms 36:1; Matthew 6:22–24"> You have no share or part in this matter because your heart is not right before God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:25–26; Daniel 4:27; Hebrews 4:12"> Therefore repent of this wickedness of yours, and pray to the Lord that he may perhaps forgive you for the intent of your heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 58:6; Proverbs 5:22; John 8:34"> For I see that you are bitterly envious and in bondage to sin.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 8:8; Numbers 21:7; Job 42:8"> But Simon replied, “You pray to the Lord for me so that nothing of what you have said may happen to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 1:8"> So after Peter and John had solemnly testified and spoken the word of the Lord, they started back to Jerusalem, proclaiming the good news to many Samaritan villages as they went.</span>
</div>

## Philip and the Ethiopian Eunuch

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 1:3"> Then an angel of the Lord said to Philip, “Get up and go south on the road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza.” (This is a desert road.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:10; Psalms 68:31; Isaiah 56:3–8"> So he got up and went. There he met an Ethiopian eunuch, a court official of Candace, queen of the Ethiopians, who was in charge of all her treasury. He had come to Jerusalem to worship,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Isaiah 1:1; Acts 28:25"> and was returning home, sitting in his chariot, reading the prophet Isaiah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 10:19; Acts 11:12; Acts 20:22–23"> Then the Spirit said to Philip, “Go over and join this chariot.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 24:44–45; John 5:39; Ephesians 5:17"> So Philip ran up to it and heard the man reading Isaiah the prophet. He asked him, “Do you understand what you’re reading?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 10:14; Psalms 73:16–17; Isaiah 29:18–19"> The man replied, “How in the world can I, unless someone guides me?” So he invited Philip to come up and sit with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7–8; 1 Peter 2:21–24; Jeremiah 51:40"> Now the passage of scripture the man was reading was this:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He was led like a sheep to slaughter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and like a lamb before its shearer is silent,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so he did not open his mouth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Isaiah 53:8; Zechariah 13:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">In humiliation justice was taken from him.&#42;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who can describe his posterity?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For his life was taken away from the earth.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 15:15; Matthew 13:36; Matthew 2:2–4"> Then the eunuch said to Philip, “Please tell me, who is the prophet saying this about – himself or someone else?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 24:27; Acts 18:28; Acts 28:23"> So Philip started speaking, and beginning with this scripture proclaimed the good news about Jesus to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 10:47; John 3:5; Titus 3:5–6"> Now as they were going along the road, they came to some water, and the eunuch said, “Look, there is water! What is to stop me from being baptized?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="[[EMPTY]]">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 4:1; John 3:22–23"> So he ordered the chariot to stop, and both Philip and the eunuch went down into the water, and Philip baptized him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 18:12; 2 Kings 2:16; Ezekiel 8:3"> Now when they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord snatched Philip away, and the eunuch did not see him any more, but went on his way rejoicing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Acts 21:8; Acts 23:23; Acts 23:33"> Philip, however, found himself at Azotus, and as he passed through the area, he proclaimed the good news to all the towns until he came to Caesarea.</span>
</div>
