<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 23:33; Matthew 12:34; Acts 28:4"> When Paul had gathered a bundle of brushwood and was putting it on the fire, a viper came out because of the heat and fastened itself on his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:2; Luke 13:4; John 7:24"> When the local people saw the creature hanging from Paul’s hand, they said to one another, “No doubt this man is a murderer! Although he has escaped from the sea, Justice herself has not allowed him to live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 10:19; Mark 16:18; Psalms 91:13"> However, Paul shook the creature off into the fire and suffered no harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 12:22; Acts 14:11–13; Matthew 21:9"> But they were expecting that he was going to swell up or suddenly drop dead. So after they had waited a long time and had seen nothing unusual happen to him, they changed their minds and said he was a god.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 10:40–41; Luke 19:6–9; Acts 28:2"> Now in the region around that place were fields belonging to the chief official of the island, named Publius, who welcomed us and entertained us hospitably as guests for three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 6:5; James 5:14–16"> The father of Publius lay sick in bed, suffering from fever and dysentery. Paul went in to see him and after praying, placed his hands on him and healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 5:12; Mark 6:54–56; Acts 5:15"> After this had happened, many of the people on the island who were sick also came and were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; Philippians 4:11–12; Matthew 6:31–34"> They also bestowed many honors, and when we were preparing to sail, they gave us all the supplies we needed.</span>
</div>

## Paul Finally Reaches Rome

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 27:6; 1 Corinthians 8:4; Isaiah 45:20"> After three months we put out to sea in an Alexandrian ship that had wintered at the island and had the “Heavenly Twins” as its figurehead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> We put in at Syracuse and stayed there three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 27:13"> From there we cast off and arrived at Rhegium, and after one day a south wind sprang up and on the second day we came to Puteoli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 21:4; Acts 20:6; Acts 19:1"> There we found some brothers and were invited to stay with them seven days. And in this way we came to Rome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 13:3; Romans 15:24; Exodus 4:14"> The brothers from there, when they heard about us, came as far as the Forum of Appius and Three Taverns to meet us. When he saw them, Paul thanked God and took courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 27:3; Acts 24:23; Acts 28:30–31"> When we entered Rome, Paul was allowed to live by himself, with the soldier who was guarding him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Addresses the Jewish Community in Rome

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 6:14; Acts 25:10"> After three days Paul called the local Jewish leaders together. When they had assembled, he said to them, “Brothers, although I had done nothing against our people or the customs of our ancestors, from Jerusalem I was handed over as a prisoner to the Romans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:31–32; Acts 23:29; Acts 22:24–25"> When they had heard my case, they wanted to release me, because there was no basis for a death sentence against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 26:32; Acts 25:21; Acts 25:10–12"> But when the Jews objected, I was forced to appeal to Caesar – not that I had some charge to bring against my own people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:20; 2 Timothy 2:9; Acts 26:6–7"> So for this reason I have asked to see you and speak with you, for I am bound with this chain because of the hope of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 54:17; Isaiah 41:11; Isaiah 50:8"> They replied, “We have received no letters from Judea about you, nor have any of the brothers come from there and reported or said anything bad about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; Acts 24:14; 1 Peter 3:16"> But we would like to hear from you what you think, for regarding this sect we know that people everywhere speak against it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 17:2–3; Acts 19:8; Acts 18:28"> They set a day to meet with him, and they came to him where he was staying in even greater numbers. From morning until evening he explained things to them, testifying about the kingdom of God and trying to convince them about Jesus from both the law of Moses and the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 14:4; Acts 17:4–5; Acts 19:8–9"> Some were convinced by what he said, but others refused to believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:7; 2 Peter 1:21; Mark 7:6"> So they began to leave, unable to agree among themselves, after Paul made one last statement: “The Holy Spirit spoke rightly to your ancestors through the prophet Isaiah</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Ezekiel 12:2; Isaiah 42:19–20"> when he said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Go to this people and say,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“You will keep on hearing, but will never understand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you will keep on looking, but will never perceive.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the heart of this people has become dull,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their ears are hard of hearing,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they have closed their eyes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that they would not see with their eyes</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and hear with their ears</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and understand with their heart</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and turn, and I would heal them.”’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:30–32; Acts 26:17–18; Romans 3:29–30"> “Therefore be advised that this salvation from God has been sent to the Gentiles; they will listen!”</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Paul lived there two whole years in his own rented quarters and welcomed all who came to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 4:29; Ephesians 6:19–20; Matthew 4:23"> proclaiming the kingdom of God and teaching about the Lord Jesus Christ with complete boldness and without restriction.</span>
</div>
