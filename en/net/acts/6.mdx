## Stephen is Arrested

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 4:29–30; Acts 7:55; Acts 6:5"> Now Stephen, full of grace and power, was performing great wonders and miraculous signs among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 15:23; Acts 22:3; Acts 15:41"> But some men from the Synagogue of the Freedmen (as it was called), both Cyrenians and Alexandrians, as well as some from Cilicia and the province of Asia, stood up and argued with Stephen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Matthew 10:19–20; 1 Corinthians 2:4"> Yet they were not able to resist the wisdom and the Spirit with which he spoke.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 26:59–60; 1 Kings 21:10–13; 1 Timothy 1:13"> Then they secretly instigated some men to say, “We have heard this man speaking blasphemous words against Moses and God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Acts 14:2; Matthew 26:57"> They incited the people, the elders, and the experts in the law; then they approached Stephen, seized him, and brought him before the council.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 6:11; Acts 21:28; Acts 25:8"> They brought forward false witnesses who said, “This man does not stop saying things against this holy place and the law.</span>
