## Jesus Ascends to Heaven

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 1:3; Luke 24:19; Matthew 4:23–24"> I wrote the former account, Theophilus, about all that Jesus began to do and teach</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 1:9; John 20:21; Isaiah 59:20–21"> until the day he was taken up to heaven, after he had given orders by the Holy Spirit to the apostles he had chosen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5–7; Acts 13:31; John 20:26"> To the same apostles also, after his suffering, he presented himself alive with many convincing proofs. He was seen by them over a forty-day period and spoke about matters concerning the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 24:49; John 14:16; John 15:26"> While he was with them, he declared, “Do not leave Jerusalem, but wait there for what my Father promised, which you heard about from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 3:11; Acts 11:15–16; Luke 3:16"> For John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit not many days from now.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 19:11; Luke 17:20; Daniel 7:27"> So when they had gathered together, they began to ask him, “Lord, is this the time when you are restoring the kingdom to Israel?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:36; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–2; Mark 13:32"> He told them, “You are not permitted to know the times or periods that the Father has set by his own authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:15; Romans 15:19"> But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the farthest parts of the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Luke 24:50–51; Mark 16:19"> After he had said this, while they were watching, he was lifted up and a cloud hid him from their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 24:4; John 20:12; Revelation 7:14"> As they were still staring into the sky while he was going, suddenly two men in white clothing stood near them</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Mark 13:26"> and said, “Men of Galilee, why do you stand here looking up into the sky? This same Jesus who has been taken up from you into heaven will come back in the same way you saw him go into heaven.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## A Replacement for Judas is Chosen

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:52; Matthew 21:1; Luke 24:50"> Then they returned to Jerusalem from the mountain called the Mount of Olives (which is near Jerusalem, a Sabbath day’s journey away).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 20:8; Luke 22:12; Mark 14:15"> When they had entered Jerusalem, they went to the upstairs room where they were staying. Peter and John, and James, and Andrew, Philip and Thomas, Bartholomew and Matthew, James son of Alphaeus and Simon the Zealot, and Judas son of James were there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 6:4; Ephesians 6:18; Colossians 4:2"> All these continued together in prayer with one mind, together with the women, along with Mary the mother of Jesus, and his brothers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:6; Acts 21:20; Revelation 3:4"> In those days Peter stood up among the believers (a gathering of about one hundred and twenty people) and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:18; Psalms 55:12–15"> “Brothers, the scripture had to be fulfilled that the Holy Spirit foretold through David concerning Judas – who became the guide for those who arrested Jesus –</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 1:25; Mark 3:19; John 17:12"> for he was counted as one of us and received a share in this ministry.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:3–10; Matthew 26:14–15; Psalms 55:23"> (Now this man Judas acquired a field with the reward of his unjust deed, and falling headfirst he burst open in the middle and all his intestines gushed out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:22; Matthew 28:15; Acts 21:40"> This became known to all who lived in Jerusalem, so that in their own language they called that field Hakeldama, that is, “Field of Blood.”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 69:25; Psalms 109:8–15; Acts 1:25"> “For it is written in the book of Psalms, ‘Let his house become deserted, and let there be no one to live in it,’ and ‘Let another take his position of responsibility.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 15:27; Luke 10:1–2; Numbers 27:17"> Thus one of the men who have accompanied us during all the time the Lord Jesus associated with us,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:2; Acts 4:33; Acts 1:8–9"> beginning from his baptism by John until the day he was taken up from us – one of these must become a witness of his resurrection together with us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 15:22; Acts 1:26"> So they proposed two candidates: Joseph called Barsabbas (also called Justus) and Matthias.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; Acts 15:8; Hebrews 4:13"> Then they prayed, “Lord, you know the hearts of all. Show us which one of these two you have chosen</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:2; John 6:70–71; John 17:12"> to assume the task of this service and apostleship from which Judas turned aside to go to his own place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:8; Joshua 18:10; 1 Samuel 14:41–42"> Then they cast lots for them, and the one chosen was Matthias; so he was counted with the eleven apostles.</span>
</div>
</div>
