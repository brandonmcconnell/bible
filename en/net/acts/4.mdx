<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Acts 6:12; Acts 23:6–9"> While Peter and John were speaking to the people, the priests and the commander of the temple guard and the Sadducees came up to them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 17:18; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14; Acts 24:14–15"> angry because they were teaching the people and announcing in Jesus the resurrection of the dead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 5:18; Matthew 10:16–17; Acts 16:19–24"> So they seized them and put them in jail until the next day (for it was already evening).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 2:41; 2 Corinthians 2:14–17; 2 Timothy 2:9–10"> But many of those who had listened to the message believed, and the number of the men came to about five thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 4:8; Matthew 27:1–2; Luke 20:1"> On the next day, their rulers, elders, and experts in the law came together in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 3:2; John 18:24; Matthew 26:3"> Annas the high priest was there, and Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and others who were members of the high priest’s family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 11:28; Acts 5:40"> After making Peter and John stand in their midst, they began to inquire, “By what power or by what name did you do this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–20; Acts 2:4"> Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, replied, “Rulers of the people and elders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15–17; Acts 3:7; John 10:32"> if we are being examined today for a good deed done to a sick man – by what means this man was healed –</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:29–32; Acts 3:6; Acts 2:36"> let it be known to all of you and to all the people of Israel that by the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene whom you crucified, whom God raised from the dead, this man stands before you healthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Isaiah 28:16; Mark 12:10–12"> This Jesus is the stone that was rejected by that was rejected by you, the builders, that has become the cornerstone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 14:6; 1 Corinthians 3:11; John 3:36"> And there is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given among people by which we must be saved.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:27; John 7:15; Matthew 11:25"> When they saw the boldness of Peter and John, and discovered that they were uneducated and ordinary men, they were amazed and recognized these men had been with Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 19:36; Acts 3:8–12; Acts 4:21"> And because they saw the man who had been healed standing with them, they had nothing to say against this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 26:30–32; Acts 5:34–42; Matthew 5:22"> But when they had ordered them to go outside the council, they began to confer with one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 21:15; Acts 6:10; John 12:18"> saying, “What should we do with these men? For it is plain to all who live in Jerusalem that a notable miraculous sign has come about through them, and we cannot deny it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 5:28; Romans 15:18–22; Acts 5:24"> But to keep this matter from spreading any further among the people, let us warn them to speak no more to anyone in this name.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:40; Luke 24:46–48; Acts 5:20"> And they called them in and ordered them not to speak or teach at all in the name of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 5:29; Exodus 1:17; 1 Corinthians 10:15"> But Peter and John replied, “Whether it is right before God to obey you rather than God, you decide,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 22:15; Jeremiah 20:9; 1 Corinthians 9:16–17"> for it is impossible for us not to speak about what we have seen and heard.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 5:26; Matthew 21:46; Luke 22:2"> After threatening them further, they released them, for they could not find how to punish them on account of the people, because they were all praising God for what had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 5:5; Acts 9:33; Luke 13:11"> For the man, on whom this miraculous sign of healing had been performed, was over forty years old.</span>
</div>

## The Followers of Jesus Pray for Boldness

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Acts 12:11–12; Psalms 119:63"> When they were released, Peter and John went to their fellow believers and reported everything the high priests and the elders had said to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 20:11; Jeremiah 32:17; Nehemiah 9:6"> When they heard this, they raised their voices to God with one mind and said, “Master of all, you who made the heaven, the earth, the sea, and everything that is in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–6; Acts 1:16; Acts 2:30"> who said by the Holy Spirit through your servant David our forefather,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Why do the nations rage,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the peoples plot foolish things?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 2:2; Revelation 17:12–14; Daniel 9:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">The kings of the earth stood together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the rulers assembled together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against the Lord and against his Christ: &#42;.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Acts 4:30; Luke 9:22"> “For indeed both Herod and Pontius Pilate, with the Gentiles and the people of Israel, assembled together in this city against your holy servant Jesus, whom you anointed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Isaiah 46:10; Genesis 50:20"> to do as much as your power and your plan had decided beforehand would happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:13; Acts 14:3; Philippians 1:14"> And now, Lord, pay attention to their threats, and grant to your servants to speak your message with great courage,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Acts 3:6; Acts 5:12"> while you extend your hand to heal, and to bring about miraculous signs and wonders through the name of your holy servant Jesus.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 21:22; Acts 2:2; Acts 2:4"> When they had prayed, the place where they were assembled together was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak the word of God courageously.</span>
</div>

## Conditions Among the Early Believers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:10; Ephesians 4:2–6; 1 Peter 3:8"> The group of those who believed were of one heart and mind, and no one said that any of his possessions was his own, but everything was held in common.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 1:16; Acts 1:22; Acts 1:8"> With great power the apostles were giving testimony to the resurrection of the Lord Jesus, and great grace was on them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 2:45; Luke 12:33; Luke 16:9"> For there was no one needy among them, because those who were owners of land or houses were selling them and bringing the proceeds from the sales</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 5:2; Acts 2:45; 2 Corinthians 8:20–21"> and placing them at the apostles’ feet. The proceeds were distributed to each, as anyone had need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Galatians 2:9; Galatians 2:13; Galatians 2:1"> So Joseph, a Levite who was a native of Cyprus, called by the apostles Barnabas (which is translated “son of encouragement”),</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 5:1–2; Acts 4:34–35; Matthew 19:29"> sold a field that belonged to him and brought the money and placed it at the apostles’ feet.</span>
