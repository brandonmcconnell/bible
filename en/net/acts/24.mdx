## Paul’s Defense Before Felix

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 3:15; 1 Samuel 2:25; Luke 18:2"> When the governor gestured for him to speak, Paul replied, “Because I know that you have been a judge over this nation for many years, I confidently make my defense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 24:1; Acts 21:18; Acts 23:11"> As you can verify for yourself, not more than twelve days ago I went up to Jerusalem to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 25:8; Acts 28:17; Acts 24:18"> They did not find me arguing with anyone or stirring up a crowd in the temple courts or in the synagogues or throughout the city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 25:7; 1 Peter 3:16"> nor can they prove to you the things they are accusing me of doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 9:2; Romans 3:21; Acts 3:13"> But I confess this to you, that I worship the God of our ancestors according to the Way (which they call a sect), believing everything that is according to the law and that is written in the prophets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 5:28–29; 1 Thessalonians 4:14–16"> I have a hope in God (a hope that these men themselves accept too) that there is going to be a resurrection of both the righteous and the unrighteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 23:1; 1 Timothy 1:5; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> This is the reason I do my best to always have a clear conscience toward God and toward people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Galatians 2:10; Romans 15:25–28; 1 Corinthians 16:1–4"> After several years I came to bring to my people gifts for the poor and to present offerings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 26:21; Acts 21:26–30; Acts 24:12"> which I was doing when they found me in the temple, ritually purified, without a crowd or a disturbance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 23:30; Acts 25:16"> But there are some Jews from the province of Asia who should be here before you and bring charges, if they have anything against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Or these men here should tell what crime they found me guilty of when I stood before the council,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 23:6; Acts 4:2; Acts 26:6–8"> other than this one thing I shouted out while I stood before them: ‘I am on trial before you today concerning the resurrection of the dead.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 24:6; Acts 24:24; Acts 25:26"> Then Felix, who understood the facts concerning the Way more accurately, adjourned their hearing, saying, “When Lysias the commanding officer comes down, I will decide your case.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Acts 28:16; Acts 27:3; Proverbs 16:7"> He ordered the centurion to guard Paul, but to let him have some freedom, and not to prevent any of his friends from meeting his needs.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul Speaks Repeatedly to Felix

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 20:21; Galatians 2:16; Mark 6:20"> Some days later, when Felix arrived with his wife Drusilla, who was Jewish, he sent for Paul and heard him speak about faith in Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 16:8; 1 Peter 3:15; Acts 10:42"> While Paul was discussing righteousness, self-control, and the coming judgment, Felix became frightened and said, “Go away for now, and when I have an opportunity, I will send for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:9–10; Proverbs 17:23; Isaiah 56:11"> At the same time he was also hoping that Paul would give him money, and for this reason he sent for Paul as often as possible and talked with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 25:9; Acts 25:14; Acts 12:3"> After two years had passed, Porcius Festus succeeded Felix, and because he wanted to do the Jews a favor, Felix left Paul in prison.</span>
</div>
