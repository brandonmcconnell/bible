<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 7:2; Acts 28:17; Acts 13:26"> “Brothers and fathers, listen to my defense that I now make to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 21:40"> (When they heard that he was addressing them in Aramaic, they became even quieter.) Then Paul said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:5–6; Acts 9:11"> “I am a Jew, born in Tarsus in Cilicia, but brought up in this city, educated with strictness under Gamaliel according to the law of our ancestors, and was zealous for God just as all of you are today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 22:19–20; 1 Timothy 1:13–15; Acts 26:9–11"> I persecuted this Way even to the point of death, tying up both men and women and putting them in prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 9:1–2; Luke 22:66; Acts 5:21"> as both the high priest and the whole council of elders can testify about me. From them I also received letters to the brothers in Damascus, and I was on my way to make arrests there and bring the prisoners to Jerusalem to be punished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 9:3–8; Acts 26:12–18; Revelation 1:16"> As I was en route and near Damascus, about noon a very bright light from heaven suddenly flashed around me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:9; Matthew 25:45; Genesis 22:11"> Then I fell to the ground and heard a voice saying to me, ‘Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting me?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 26:9; 1 Samuel 8:7; Exodus 16:7–8"> I answered, ‘Who are you, Lord?’ He said to me, ‘I am Jesus the Nazarene, whom you are persecuting.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Daniel 10:7; Acts 26:13"> Those who were with me saw the light, but did not understand the voice of the one who was speaking to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 22:12–16; Acts 2:37"> So I asked, ‘What should I do, Lord?’ The Lord said to me, ‘Get up and go to Damascus; there you will be told about everything that you have been designated to do.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 9:8–9; Acts 13:11; Isaiah 42:16"> Since I could not see because of the brilliance of that light, I came to Damascus led by the hand of those who were with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:22; Acts 6:3; 3 John 1:12"> A man named Ananias, a devout man according to the law, well spoken of by all the Jews who live there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 9:17–18; Philemon 1:16"> came to me and stood beside me and said to me, ‘Brother Saul, regain your sight!’ And at that very moment I looked up and saw him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Corinthians 9:1"> Then he said, ‘The God of our ancestors has already chosen you to know his will, to see the Righteous One, and to hear a command from his mouth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 23:11; Acts 4:20; John 15:27"> because you will be his witness to all people of what you have seen and heard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Acts 2:21; Titus 3:5"> And now what are you waiting for? Get up, be baptized, and have your sins washed away, calling on his name.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:1–4; Acts 10:9–10; Acts 9:26–28"> When I returned to Jerusalem and was praying in the temple, I fell into a trance</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:29; Luke 21:21; Ezekiel 3:6–7"> and saw the Lord saying to me, ‘Hurry and get out of Jerusalem quickly, because they will not accept your testimony about me.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 22:4; Matthew 10:17; Acts 8:3"> I replied, ‘Lord, they themselves know that I imprisoned and beat those in the various synagogues who believed in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 7:58; Acts 8:1; Romans 1:32"> And when the blood of your witness Stephen was shed, I myself was standing nearby, approving, and guarding the cloaks of those who were killing him.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 9:15; 1 Timothy 2:7; Ephesians 3:6–8"> Then he said to me, ‘Go, because I will send you far away to the Gentiles.’”</span>
</div>

## The Roman Commander Questions Paul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 25:24; Acts 21:36; John 19:15"> The crowd was listening to him until he said this. Then they raised their voices and shouted, “Away with this man from the earth! For he should not be allowed to live!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:13; Acts 26:11; Ecclesiastes 10:3"> While they were screaming and throwing off their cloaks and tossing dust in the air,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 21:31–32; Acts 21:34; John 19:1"> the commanding officer ordered Paul to be brought back into the barracks. He told them to interrogate Paul by beating him with a lash so that he could find out the reason the crowd was shouting at Paul in this way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 16:37; Acts 25:16; Acts 27:43"> When they had stretched him out for the lash, Paul said to the centurion standing nearby, “Is it legal for you to lash a man who is a Roman citizen without a proper trial?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 23:27; Acts 22:29"> When the centurion heard this, he went to the commanding officer and reported it, saying, “What are you about to do? For this man is a Roman citizen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> So the commanding officer came and asked Paul, “Tell me, are you a Roman citizen?” He replied, “Yes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> The commanding officer answered, “I acquired this citizenship with a large sum of money.” “But I was even born a citizen,” Paul replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Acts 22:24–26; Acts 16:38–39"> Then those who were about to interrogate him stayed away from him, and the commanding officer was frightened when he realized that Paul was a Roman citizen and that he had had him tied up.</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

He then brought Paul down and had him stand before them.</span>
