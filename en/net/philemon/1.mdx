## Salutation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Colossians 1:1; Ephesians 3:1; Philemon 1:9"> From Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, to Philemon, our dear friend and colaborer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 2:25; Colossians 4:17; Romans 16:5"> to Apphia our sister, to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the church that meets in your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 1:2; 2 Corinthians 13:14"> Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Thanks for Philemon’s Love and Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Ephesians 1:16; 2 Thessalonians 1:3"> I always thank my God as I remember you in my prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:4; Ephesians 1:15; 1 Corinthians 16:1"> because I hear of your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Philippians 1:9–11; 2 Peter 1:8; Colossians 1:9"> I pray that the faith you share with us may deepen your understanding of every blessing that belongs to you in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 7:13; 1 Thessalonians 3:9; 2 Corinthians 7:4"> I have had great joy and encouragement because of your love, for the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, brother.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Request for Onesimus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:6; 2 Corinthians 10:8; 2 Corinthians 3:12"> So, although I have quite a lot of confidence in Christ and could command you to do what is proper,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 4:1; Romans 12:1"> I would rather appeal to you on the basis of love – I, Paul, an old man and even now a prisoner for the sake of Christ Jesus –</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 4:15; 1 Timothy 1:2"> I am appealing to you concerning my child, whose spiritual father I have become during my imprisonment, that is, Onesimus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:11; Job 30:1–2; 1 Peter 2:10"> who was formerly useless to you, but is now useful to you and me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; Jeremiah 31:20; Mark 11:25"> I have sent him (who is my very heart) back to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:7; Ephesians 3:1; 1 Corinthians 16:17"> I wanted to keep him so that he could serve me in your place during my imprisonment for the sake of the gospel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Chronicles 29:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3"> However, without your consent I did not want to do anything, so that your good deed would not be out of compulsion, but from your own willingness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 50:20; Genesis 45:5–8; Isaiah 20:6"> For perhaps it was for this reason that he was separated from you for a little while, so that you would have him back eternally,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Colossians 3:22; Matthew 23:8; 1 Corinthians 7:22"> no longer as a slave, but more than a slave, as a dear brother. He is especially so to me, and even more so to you now, both humanly speaking and in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:23; Matthew 10:40; Philemon 1:12"> Therefore if you regard me as a partner, accept him as you would me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–7"> Now if he has defrauded you of anything or owes you anything, charge what he owes to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Galatians 6:11; Galatians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 4:15"> I, Paul, have written this letter with my own hand: I will repay it. I could also mention that you owe me your very self.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="3 John 1:4; Philemon 1:7; Philemon 1:12"> Yes, brother, let me have some benefit from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:4; 2 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 7:16"> Since I was confident that you would obey, I wrote to you, because I knew that you would do even more than what I am asking you to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 1:11; Philippians 2:24; 2 John 1:12"> At the same time also, prepare a place for me to stay, for I hope that through your prayers I will be given back to you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Concluding Greetings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Colossians 1:7; Romans 16:7; Colossians 4:12"> Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, greets you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10–11; Colossians 4:14; Colossians 4:10"> Mark, Aristarchus, Demas and Luke, my colaborers, greet you too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:22; Romans 16:23; Romans 16:20"> May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.</span>
</div>
</div>
