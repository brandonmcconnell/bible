## The Judge is Coming

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 1:2; Psalms 11:4; Revelation 3:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Listen, all you nations!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Pay attention, all inhabitants of earth!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The sovereign Lord will testify against you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the Lord will accuse you from his majestic palace.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 4:13; Isaiah 26:21; Deuteronomy 32:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, the Lord is coming out of his dwelling place!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will descend and march on the earth’s mountaintops!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Nahum 1:5; Psalms 97:5; Judges 5:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The mountains will disintegrate beneath him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the valleys will be split in two.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The mountains will melt like wax in a fire,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the rocks will slide down like water cascading down a steep slope.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 8:14; Jeremiah 2:19; 1 Thessalonians 2:15–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">All this is because of Jacob’s rebellion</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the sins of the nation of Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">How has Jacob rebelled, you ask?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Samaria epitomizes their rebellion!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Where are Judah’s pagan worship centers, you ask?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are right in Jerusalem!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:14; Micah 3:12; Lamentations 4:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">“I will turn Samaria into a heap of ruins in an open field –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">vineyards will be planted there!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will tumble the rubble of her stone walls down into the valley,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and tear down her fortifications to their foundations.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:18; Hosea 2:12; Hosea 8:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">All her carved idols will be smashed to pieces;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all her metal cult statues will be destroyed by fire.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will make a waste heap of all her images.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Since she gathered the metal as a prostitute collects her wages,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the idols will become a prostitute’s wages again.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Isaiah 20:2–4; Jeremiah 4:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this reason I will mourn and wail;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will walk around barefoot and without my outer garments.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will howl like a wild dog,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and screech like an owl.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:11–15; Isaiah 8:7–8; 2 Chronicles 32:1–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">For Samaria’s disease is incurable.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It has infected Judah;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it has spread to the leadership of my people</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and has even contaminated Jerusalem!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:20; Jeremiah 6:26; Joshua 18:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Don’t spread the news in Gath!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Don’t shed even a single tear!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In Beth Leaphrah sit in the dust!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 48:9; Micah 1:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Residents of Shaphir, pass by in nakedness and humiliation!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The residents of Zaanan can’t leave their city.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Beth Ezel mourns,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“He takes from you what he desires.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 14:19; Micah 1:9; Amos 3:6"> <span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, the residents of Maroth hope for something good to happen,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">though the Lord has sent disaster against the city of Jerusalem.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Kings 18:17; 2 Chronicles 32:9; Joshua 10:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Residents of Lachish, hitch the horses to the chariots!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You influenced Daughter Zion to sin,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for Israel’s rebellious deeds can be traced back to you!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; 2 Kings 16:8; 2 Samuel 8:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore you will have to say farewell to Moresheth Gath.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The residents of Achzib will be as disappointing</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">as a dried up well to the kings of Israel.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 15:44; 1 Samuel 22:1; Joshua 15:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">Residents of Mareshah, a conqueror will attack you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the leaders of Israel shall flee to Adullam.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 22:12; Job 1:20; Jeremiah 7:29"> <span className="pl pl-1">Shave your heads bald as you mourn for the children you love;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">shave your foreheads as bald as an eagle,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for they are taken from you into exile.</span></span>
