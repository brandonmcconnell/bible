## The Purpose of Parables

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 13:10–17; Mark 4:34; Proverbs 13:20"> When he was alone, those around him with the twelve asked him about the parables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; Luke 8:10; 1 John 5:20"> He said to them, “The secret of the kingdom of God has been given to you. But to those outside, everything is in parables,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9–10; Isaiah 44:18; Matthew 13:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">so that although they look they may look but not see,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and although they hear they may hear but not understand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so they may not repent and be forgiven.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 3:19; Matthew 13:51–52; Mark 7:17–18"> He said to them, “Don’t you understand this parable? Then how will you understand any parable?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 8:4; Matthew 13:19; Matthew 13:37"> The sower sows the word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Matthew 13:19; 2 Corinthians 2:11"> These are the ones on the path where the word is sown: Whenever they hear, immediately Satan comes and snatches the word that was sown in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 6:20; Luke 8:13; Matthew 13:20–21"> These are the ones sown on rocky ground: As soon as they hear the word, they receive it with joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Thessalonians 3:3–5; Matthew 12:31"> But they have no root in themselves and do not endure. Then, when trouble or persecution comes because of the word, immediately they fall away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:14; Jeremiah 4:3; Matthew 13:22"> Others are the ones sown among thorns: They are those who hear the word,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; 1 Timothy 6:17"> but worldly cares, the seductiveness of wealth, and the desire for other things come in and choke the word, and it produces nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Luke 8:15; John 15:4–5"> But these are the ones sown on good soil: They hear the word and receive it and bear fruit, one thirty times as much, one sixty, and one a hundred.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:15; Luke 11:33; Luke 8:16"> He also said to them, “A lamp isn’t brought to be put under a basket or under a bed, is it? Isn’t it to be placed on a lampstand?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 8:17; Ecclesiastes 12:14; Matthew 10:26–27"> For nothing is hidden except to be revealed, and nothing concealed except to be brought to light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 4:9; Matthew 11:15; Revelation 2:29"> If anyone has ears to hear, he had better listen!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 2:1; Luke 8:18; Matthew 7:2"> And he said to them, “Take care about what you hear. The measure you use will be the measure you receive, and more will be added to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 13:12; Luke 8:18; Matthew 25:28–29"> For whoever has will be given more, but whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken from him.”</span>
</div>

## The Parable of the Growing Seed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9; Luke 8:11"> He also said, “The kingdom of God is like someone who spreads seed on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 11:5; 2 Thessalonians 1:3; 1 Corinthians 15:37–38"> He goes to sleep and gets up, night and day, and the seed sprouts and grows, though he does not know how.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 4:31–32; Hosea 6:3; Philippians 1:9–11"> By itself the soil produces a crop, first the stalk, then the head, then the full grain in the head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joel 3:13; Matthew 13:30; Matthew 13:40–43"> And when the grain is ripe, he sends in the sickle because the harvest has come.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Mustard Seed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 13:31–32; Luke 13:18–21; Lamentations 2:13"> He also asked, “To what can we compare the kingdom of God, or what parable can we use to present it?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 19:20; Luke 13:18–19; Daniel 2:44–45"> It is like a mustard seed that when sown in the ground, even though it is the smallest of all the seeds in the ground –</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 4:20–22; Daniel 4:10–14; Ezekiel 31:3–10"> when it is sown, it grows up, becomes the greatest of all garden plants, and grows large branches so that the wild birds can nest in its shade.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 16:25; Mark 4:10; Matthew 15:15–20"> He did not speak to them without a parable. But privately he explained everything to his own disciples.</span>

## Stilling of a Storm

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 8:18; Luke 8:22; Luke 8:25"> On that day, when evening came, Jesus said to his disciples, “Let’s go across to the other side of the lake.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 3:9; Mark 4:1; Mark 5:2"> So after leaving the crowd, they took him along, just as he was, in the boat, and other boats were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 8:23–24; Job 1:19; Luke 8:22–23"> Now a great windstorm developed and the waves were breaking into the boat, so that the boat was nearly swamped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 5:7; Hebrews 4:15; Isaiah 54:6–8"> But he was in the stern, sleeping on a cushion. They woke him up and said to him, “Teacher, don’t you care that we are about to die?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 107:29; Psalms 89:9; Psalms 65:7"> So he got up and rebuked the wind, and said to the sea, “Be quiet! Calm down!” Then the wind stopped, and it was dead calm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Matthew 8:26; Matthew 14:31"> And he said to them, “Why are you cowardly? Do you still not have faith?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:25; Job 38:11; Psalms 89:7"> They were overwhelmed by fear and said to one another, “Who then is this? Even the wind and sea obey him!”</span>
