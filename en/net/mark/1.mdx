## The Ministry of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 John 5:11–12; Matthew 4:3; Matthew 3:17"> The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Matthew 11:10; Luke 1:76"> As it is written in Isaiah the prophet,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Look, I am sending my messenger ahead of you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who will prepare your way,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 40:3–5; Matthew 3:3; Luke 3:4–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">the voice of one shouting in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Prepare the way for the Lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">make his paths straight.’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 19:3–4; Matthew 3:6; Acts 13:24–25"> In the wilderness John the baptizer began preaching a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–42; Acts 19:18; Matthew 3:5–6"> People from the whole Judean countryside and all of Jerusalem were going out to him, and he was baptizing them in the Jordan River as they confessed their sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 1:8; Leviticus 11:22; Matthew 3:4"> John wore a garment made of camel’s hair with a leather belt around his waist, and he ate locusts and wild honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 13:25; Luke 3:16; John 1:27"> He proclaimed, “One more powerful than I am is coming after me; I am not worthy to bend down and untie the strap of his sandals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:5; 1 Corinthians 12:13; Acts 19:4–6"> I baptize you with water, but he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit.”</span>

## The Baptism and Temptation of Jesus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 3:13–17; Matthew 2:23; Luke 3:21–22"> Now in those days Jesus came from Nazareth in Galilee and was baptized by John in the Jordan River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 1:31–34; Luke 3:22; Isaiah 42:1"> And just as Jesus was coming up out of the water, he saw the heavens splitting apart and the Spirit descending on him like a dove.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 42:1; Mark 9:7; Matthew 3:17"> And a voice came from heaven: “You are my one dear Son; in you I take great delight.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:1–11; Luke 4:1–13"> The Spirit immediately drove him into the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 2:17–18; Hebrews 4:15; Exodus 34:28"> He was in the wilderness forty days, enduring temptations from Satan. He was with wild animals, and angels were ministering to his needs.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Preaching in Galilee and the Call of the Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Matthew 4:12; Luke 8:1"> Now after John was imprisoned, Jesus went into Galilee and proclaimed the gospel of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 3:2; Acts 2:36–38; Matthew 4:17"> He said, “The time is fulfilled and the kingdom of God is near. Repent and believe the gospel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 4:18–22; John 1:40–42; John 6:8"> As he went along the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew, Simon’s brother, casting a net into the sea (for they were fishermen).</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 5:10; Matthew 4:19–20; Acts 2:38–41"> Jesus said to them, “Follow me, and I will turn you into fishers of people.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:28–30; Luke 5:11; Mark 10:28–31"> They left their nets immediately and followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 5:37; Acts 1:13; Mark 10:35"> Going on a little farther, he saw James, the son of Zebedee, and John his brother in their boat mending nets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26; Mark 10:29"> Immediately he called them, and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired men and followed him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Authority

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:2; Mark 1:39; Matthew 4:23"> Then they went to Capernaum. When the Sabbath came, Jesus went into the synagogue and began to teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54; Luke 4:32"> The people there were amazed by his teaching, because he taught them like one who had authority, not like the experts in the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 9:25; Mark 7:25; Mark 1:34"> Just then there was a man in their synagogue with an unclean spirit, and he cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; James 2:19; Acts 3:14"> “Leave us alone, Jesus the Nazarene! Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are – the Holy One of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 1:34; Luke 4:41; Acts 16:17"> But Jesus rebuked him: “Silence! Come out of him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Mark 9:26; Mark 9:20; Luke 11:22"> After throwing him into convulsions, the unclean spirit cried out with a loud voice and came out of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:31; Mark 7:37; Luke 9:1"> They were all amazed so that they asked each other, “What is this? A new teaching with authority! He even commands the unclean spirits and they obey him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 9:31; Luke 4:37; Mark 1:45"> So the news about him spread quickly throughout all the region around Galilee.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Healings at Simon’s House

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 4:38–39; Matthew 8:14–15; Mark 1:21"> Now as soon as they left the synagogue, they entered Simon and Andrew’s house, with James and John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:5; Mark 5:23; John 11:3"> Simon’s mother-in-law was lying down, sick with a fever, so they spoke to Jesus at once about her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:41; Psalms 116:12; Mark 5:41"> He came and raised her up by gently taking her hand. Then the fever left her and she began to serve them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 8:16; Mark 3:2; Luke 4:40"> When it was evening, after sunset, they brought to him all who were sick and demon-possessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 1:5; Acts 13:44"> The whole town gathered by the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 4:41; Mark 3:12; Matthew 4:23"> So he healed many who were sick with various diseases and drove out many demons. But he would not permit the demons to speak, because they knew him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Praying and Preaching

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Luke 5:16; Luke 22:39–46"> Then Jesus got up early in the morning when it was still very dark, departed, and went out to a deserted place, and there he spent time in prayer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Simon and his companions searched for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:19; Mark 1:5; Zechariah 11:11"> When they found him, they said, “Everyone is looking for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 4:43; John 17:8; Isaiah 61:1–3"> He replied, “Let us go elsewhere, into the surrounding villages, so that I can preach there too. For that is what I came out here to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 4:23; Mark 1:21; Luke 4:43–44"> So he went into all of Galilee preaching in their synagogues and casting out demons.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Cleansing a Leper

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 5:12–14; Matthew 8:2–4; Leviticus 13:1–14"> Now a leper came to him and fell to his knees, asking for help. “If you are willing, you can make me clean,” he said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 4:15"> Moved with compassion, Jesus stretched out his hand and touched him, saying, “I am willing. Be clean!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 15:3; Mark 5:29; John 4:50–53"> The leprosy left him at once, and he was clean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 9:30; Mark 7:36; Luke 8:56"> Immediately Jesus sent the man away with a very strong warning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Leviticus 14:1–32; Luke 17:14; Matthew 8:4"> He told him, “See that you do not say anything to anyone, but go, show yourself to a priest, and bring the offering that Moses commanded for your cleansing, as a testimony to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 2:13; Matthew 9:31; John 6:2"> But as the man went out he began to announce it publicly and spread the story widely, so that Jesus was no longer able to enter any town openly but stayed outside in remote places. Still they kept coming to him from everywhere.</span>
</div>
