<div className="bp">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 16:5"> Now they had forgotten to take bread, except for one loaf they had with them in the boat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:6–8; Matthew 16:6; Matthew 16:11–12"> And Jesus ordered them, “Watch out! Beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and the yeast of Herod!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Luke 20:5; Matthew 16:7–8"> So they began to discuss with one another about having no bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:52; Isaiah 63:17; Mark 2:8"> When he learned of this, Jesus said to them, “Why are you arguing about having no bread? Do you still not see or understand? Have your hearts been hardened?</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
