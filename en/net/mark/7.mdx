## A Syrophoenician Woman’s Faith

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 15:21–28; Matthew 11:21; Ezekiel 28:2"> After Jesus left there, he went to the region of Tyre. When he went into a house, he did not want anyone to know, but he was not able to escape notice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Mark 9:17–23; Acts 10:25–26"> Instead, a woman whose young daughter had an unclean spirit immediately heard about him and came and fell at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Isaiah 49:12; Galatians 3:28"> The woman was a Greek, of Syrophoenician origin. She asked him to cast the demon out of her daughter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 15:23–28; Matthew 7:6; Acts 22:21"> He said to her, “Let the children be satisfied first, for it is not right to take the children’s bread and to throw it to the dogs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Romans 10:12; Romans 3:29; Acts 11:17–18"> She answered, “Yes, Lord, but even the dogs under the table eat the children’s crumbs.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 66:2; Matthew 5:3; 1 John 3:8"> Then he said to her, “Because you said this, you may go. The demon has left your daughter.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 3:8; John 4:50–52"> She went home and found the child lying on the bed, and the demon gone.</span>

## Healing a Deaf Mute

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 5:20; Matthew 15:29–31; Matthew 4:25"> Then Jesus went out again from the region of Tyre to the Sea of Galilee in the region of the Decapolis.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 11:14; Mark 5:23; Matthew 9:32–33"> They brought to him a deaf man who had difficulty speaking, and they asked him to place his hands on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:23; John 9:6–7; Mark 5:40"> After Jesus took him aside privately, away from the crowd, he put his fingers in the man’s ears, and after spitting, he touched his tongue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 11:41; Mark 5:41; Mark 6:41"> Then he looked up to heaven and said with a sigh, “Ephphatha” (that is, “Be opened”).</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Psalms 33:9; Mark 2:12"> And immediately the man’s ears were opened, his tongue loosened, and he spoke plainly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Mark 8:26; Mark 3:12"> Jesus ordered them not to tell anything. But as much as he ordered them not to do this, they proclaimed it all the more.</span>
