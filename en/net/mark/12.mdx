## Paying Taxes to Caesar

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 20:20–26; Luke 11:54; Mark 3:6"> Then they sent some of the Pharisees and Herodians to trap him with his own words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Luke 20:22; John 7:18"> When they came they said to him, “Teacher, we know that you are truthful and do not court anyone’s favor, because you show no partiality but teach the way of God in accordance with the truth. Is it right to pay taxes to Caesar or not? Should we pay or shouldn’t we?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:2; Revelation 2:23; Mark 10:2"> But he saw through their hypocrisy and said to them, “Why are you testing me? Bring me a denarius and let me look at it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 20:24–26; Revelation 3:12; Matthew 22:19–22"> So they brought one, and he said to them, “Whose image is this, and whose inscription?” They replied, “Caesar’s.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 13:7; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Corinthians 5:14–15"> Then Jesus said to them, “Give to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s, and to God the things that are God’s.” And they were utterly amazed at him.</span>
</div>

## Marriage and the Resurrection

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 22:23–33; Acts 4:1–2; Acts 23:6–9"> Sadducees (who say there is no resurrection) also came to him and asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5–10; Ruth 4:5; Ruth 1:11–13"> “Teacher, Moses wrote for us: ‘If a man’s brother dies and leaves a wife but no children, that man must marry the widow and father children for his brother.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 22:25–28; Luke 20:29–33"> There were seven brothers. The first one married, and when he died he had no children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> The second married her and died without any children, and likewise the third.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> None of the seven had children. Finally, the woman died too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In the resurrection, when they rise again, whose wife will she be? For all seven had married her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 20:9; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Matthew 22:29"> Jesus said to them, “Aren’t you deceived for this reason, because you don’t know the scriptures or the power of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 20:35–36; Matthew 22:30; 1 John 3:2"> For when they rise from the dead, they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are like angels in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 3:2–6; Luke 20:37; Genesis 17:7–8"> Now as for the dead being raised, have you not read in the book of Moses, in the passage about the bush, how God said to him, ‘I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 14:9; Romans 4:17; Mark 12:24"> He is not the God of the dead but of the living. You are badly mistaken!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Greatest Commandment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 22:34–40; Luke 10:25–28; Luke 20:39"> Now one of the experts in the law came and heard them debating. When he saw that Jesus answered them well, he asked him, “Which commandment is the most important of all?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:4; Luke 10:27; Galatians 3:20"> Jesus answered, “The most important is: ‘Listen, Israel, the Lord our God, the Lord is one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:5"> Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, with all your mind, and with all your strength.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 13:8–9; Matthew 22:39; Leviticus 19:18"> The second is: ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’ There is no other commandment greater than these.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:39; Deuteronomy 4:35; Isaiah 46:9"> The expert in the law said to him, “That is true, Teacher; you are right to say that he is one, and there is no one else besides him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hosea 6:6; Micah 6:6–8; 1 Samuel 15:22"> And to love him with all your heart, with all your mind, and with all your strength and to love your neighbor as yourself is more important than all burnt offerings and sacrifices.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 20:40; Matthew 22:46"> When Jesus saw that he had answered thoughtfully, he said to him, “You are not far from the kingdom of God.” Then no one dared any longer to question him.</span>
</div>

## The Messiah: David’s Son and Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Matthew 22:41–45; Matthew 26:55"> While Jesus was teaching in the temple courts, he said, “How is it that the experts in the law say that the Christ is David’s son?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 110:1; Hebrews 1:13; Matthew 22:43–45"> David himself, by the Holy Spirit, said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The Lord said to my lord,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Sit at my right hand,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until I put your enemies under your feet.”’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Romans 1:3–4; Matthew 11:25; Matthew 1:23"> If David himself calls him ‘Lord,’ how can he be his son?” And the large crowd was listening to him with delight.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Warnings About Experts in the Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–7; Luke 20:45–47; Luke 11:43"> In his teaching Jesus also said, “Watch out for the experts in the law. They like walking around in long robes and elaborate greetings in the marketplaces,</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="James 2:2–3"> and the best seats in the synagogues and the places of honor at banquets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 23:13; Luke 20:47; Matthew 23:33"> They devour widows’ property, and as a show make long prayers. These men will receive a more severe punishment.”</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 21:1–4; 2 Kings 12:9; John 8:20"> Then he sat down opposite the offering box, and watched the crowd putting coins into it. Many rich people were throwing in large amounts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And a poor widow came and put in two small copper coins, worth less than a penny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Acts 11:29"> He called his disciples and said to them, “I tell you the truth, this poor widow has put more into the offering box than all the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:2–3; 1 John 3:17; Ezra 2:68–69"> For they all gave out of their wealth. But she, out of her poverty, put in what she had to live on, everything she had.”</span>
