## Signs of the End of the Age

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 21:1; Mark 4:34; Mark 14:33"> So while he was sitting on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked him privately,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Matthew 24:3; Daniel 12:6"> “Tell us, when will these things happen? And what will be the sign that all these things are about to take place?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Ephesians 5:6; Jeremiah 29:8"> Jesus began to say to them, “Watch out that no one misleads you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 13:22; Jeremiah 23:21–25; John 8:24"> Many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am he,’ and they will mislead many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 21:9–11; John 14:27; Matthew 24:6–7"> When you hear of wars and rumors of wars, do not be alarmed. These things must happen, but the end is still to come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 24:8; Isaiah 37:3; Psalms 48:6"> For nation will rise up in arms against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. There will be earthquakes in various places, and there will be famines. These are but the beginning of birth pains.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Persecution of Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 10:17–18; John 16:2; Philippians 1:29"> “You must watch out for yourselves. You will be handed over to councils and beaten in the synagogues. You will stand before governors and kings because of me, as a witness to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 24:14; Colossians 1:23; Romans 10:18"> First the gospel must be preached to all nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 21:14–15; Luke 12:11–12; Matthew 10:19–21"> When they arrest you and hand you over for trial, do not worry about what to speak. But say whatever is given you at that time, for it is not you speaking, but the Holy Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 10:21; Luke 12:52–53; Luke 21:16"> Brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his child. Children will rise against parents and have them put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; Matthew 10:22; Matthew 24:13"> You will be hated by everyone because of my name. But the one who endures to the end will be saved.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Abomination of Desolation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Daniel 11:31"> “But when you see the abomination of desolation standing where it should not be (let the reader understand), then those in Judea must flee to the mountains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 17:31–33; Hebrews 11:7; Philippians 3:7–8"> The one on the roof must not come down or go inside to take anything out of his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The one in the field must not turn back to get his cloak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 4:10; Luke 23:29; Hosea 9:14"> Woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing their babies in those days!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Pray that it may not be in winter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 12:1; Joel 2:2; Matthew 24:21"> For in those days there will be suffering unlike anything that has happened from the beginning of the creation that God created until now, or ever will happen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:22; Zechariah 13:8–9; Isaiah 65:8–9"> And if the Lord had not cut short those days, no one would be saved. But because of the elect, whom he chose, he has cut them short.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–25; Matthew 24:5"> Then if anyone says to you, ‘Look, here is the Christ!’ or ‘Look, there he is!’ do not believe him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 24:24; 2 Thessalonians 2:8–14; Mark 13:6"> For false messiahs and false prophets will appear and perform signs and wonders to deceive, if possible, the elect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Peter 3:17; Mark 13:5; John 14:29"> Be careful! I have told you everything ahead of time.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Arrival of the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 13:10; Acts 2:19–20; Revelation 6:12–14"> “But in those days, after that suffering, the sun will be darkened and the moon will not give its light;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 34:4; Revelation 6:13"> the stars will be falling from heaven, and the powers in the heavens will be shaken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 1:7; Matthew 16:27; Matthew 24:30"> Then everyone will see the Son of Man arriving in the clouds with great power and glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Zechariah 2:6; Matthew 24:31; Deuteronomy 30:4"> Then he will send angels and they will gather his elect from the four winds, from the ends of the earth to the ends of heaven.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Parable of the Fig Tree

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 21:29–31; Matthew 24:32–33"> “Learn this parable from the fig tree: Whenever its branch becomes tender and puts out its leaves, you know that summer is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; James 5:9; Ezekiel 12:25–28"> So also you, when you see these things happening, know that he is near, right at the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 24:34; Luke 21:32; Matthew 16:28"> I tell you the truth, this generation will not pass away until all these things take place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 40:8; Isaiah 51:6; Matthew 24:35"> Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Be Ready!

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 1:7; Matthew 24:36–42; Matthew 25:13"> “But as for that day or hour no one knows it – neither the angels in heaven, nor the Son – except the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Luke 12:40; Romans 13:11–12"> Watch out! Stay alert! For you do not know when the time will come.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; John 10:3; Luke 12:36–40"> It is like a man going on a journey. He left his house and put his slaves in charge, assigning to each his work, and commanded the doorkeeper to stay alert.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Matthew 24:42; Matthew 24:44"> Stay alert, then, because you do not know when the owner of the house will return – whether during evening, at midnight, when the rooster crows, or at dawn –</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:6–7; Romans 13:11–14; Matthew 24:48–51"> or else he might find you asleep when he returns suddenly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 13:33; Luke 12:41–46; Mark 13:35"> What I say to you I say to everyone: Stay alert!”</span>
</div>
</div>
