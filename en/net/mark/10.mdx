## The Rich Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 19:16–30; Luke 18:18–30; Romans 2:7"> Now as Jesus was starting out on his way, someone ran up to him, fell on his knees, and said, “Good teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 18:19; Matthew 19:17; Romans 3:12"> Jesus said to him, “Why do you call me good? No one is good except God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Romans 13:9; James 2:11"> You know the commandments: ‘Do not murder, do not commit adultery, do not steal, do not give false testimony, do not defraud, honor your father and mother.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31–32; 2 Timothy 3:5; Philippians 3:6"> The man said to him, “Teacher, I have wholeheartedly obeyed all these laws since my youth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 6:19–21; Revelation 2:4; Luke 18:22"> As Jesus looked at him, he felt love for him and said, “You lack one thing. Go, sell whatever you have and give the money to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 John 2:15–16; 1 Timothy 6:9–10; Luke 12:15"> But at this statement, the man looked sad and went away sorrowful, for he was very rich.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:26; Luke 18:24; Mark 3:5"> Then Jesus looked around and said to his disciples, “How hard it is for the rich to enter the kingdom of God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="James 5:1–3; Proverbs 11:28; Psalms 52:7"> The disciples were astonished at these words. But again Jesus said to them, “Children, how hard it is to enter the kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 19:24–25; Luke 18:25; Matthew 23:24"> It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 16:31; 2 Corinthians 11:23; Romans 10:9–13"> They were even more astonished and said to one another, “Then who can be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:26; Jeremiah 32:27; Job 42:2"> Jesus looked at them and replied, “This is impossible for mere humans, but not for God; all things are possible for God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 19:27–30; Luke 18:28–30; Luke 14:33"> Peter began to speak to him, “Look, we have left everything to follow you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:23; Matthew 5:10–11; Mark 8:35"> Jesus said, “I tell you the truth, there is no one who has left home or brothers or sisters or mother or father or children or fields for my sake and for the sake of the gospel</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 18:30; Philippians 3:8; 2 Corinthians 6:10"> who will not receive in this age a hundred times as much – homes, brothers, sisters, mothers, children, fields, all with persecutions – and in the age to come, eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 19:30; Luke 13:30; Matthew 20:16"> But many who are first will be last, and the last first.”</span>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Luke 18:31–34; Luke 9:51"> They were on the way, going up to Jerusalem. <span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:2; Matthew 20:17–19; Mark 8:31"> “Look, we are going up to Jerusalem, and the Son of Man will be handed over to the chief priests and experts in the law. They will condemn him to death and will turn him over to the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 26:67; Matthew 16:21; Mark 14:65"> They will mock him, spit on him, flog him severely, and kill him. Yet after three days, he will rise again.”</span>

## The Request of James and John

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:20–28; 2 Samuel 14:4–11; 1 Kings 2:20"> Then James and John, the sons of Zebedee, came to him and said, “Teacher, we want you to do for us whatever we ask.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 15:7; 1 Kings 3:5–15; Mark 10:51"> He said to them, “What do you want me to do for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Mark 16:19; Luke 24:26"> They said to him, “Permit one of us to sit at your right hand and the other at your left in your glory.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 12:50; Matthew 20:21–22; Psalms 75:8"> But Jesus said to them, “You don’t know what you are asking! Are you able to drink the cup I drink or be baptized with the baptism I experience?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 12:2; Revelation 1:9; John 17:14"> They said to him, “We are able.” Then Jesus said to them, “You will drink the cup I drink, and you will be baptized with the baptism I experience,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:23; Matthew 25:34; John 17:24"> but to sit at my right or at my left is not mine to give. It is for those for whom it has been prepared.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 20:24; Proverbs 13:10; Romans 12:10"> Now when the other ten heard this, they became angry with James and John.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; Luke 22:25; Matthew 20:25"> Jesus called them and said to them, “You know that those who are recognized as rulers of the Gentiles lord it over them, and those in high positions use their authority over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 23:8–12; Luke 14:11; Mark 9:35"> But it is not this way among you. Instead whoever wants to be great among you must be your servant,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> and whoever wants to be first among you must be the slave of all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Philippians 2:5–8; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> For even the Son of Man did not come to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”</span>
</div>

## Healing Blind Bartimaeus

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Luke 18:35–1; Matthew 20:29–34; John 9:8"> They came to Jericho. As Jesus and his disciples and a large crowd were leaving Jericho, Bartimaeus the son of Timaeus, a blind beggar, was sitting by the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Matthew 9:27; Revelation 22:16"> When he heard that it was Jesus the Nazarene, he began to shout, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:13; Mark 7:26–29; Hebrews 5:7"> Many scolded him to get him to be quiet, but he shouted all the more, “Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 18:40; Matthew 20:32–34; John 11:28"> Jesus stopped and said, “Call him.” So they called the blind man and said to him, “Have courage! Get up! He is calling you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Philippians 3:7–9; Hebrews 12:1"> He threw off his cloak, jumped up, and came to Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 18:41–43; Mark 10:36; Matthew 7:7"> Then Jesus said to him, “What do you want me to do for you?” The blind man replied, “Rabbi, let me see again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 9:22; Acts 26:18; Mark 8:25"> Jesus said to him, “Go, your faith has healed you.” Immediately he regained his sight and followed him on the road.</span>
