## Jesus and Barabbas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:39–40; Acts 25:9; Acts 24:27"> During the feast it was customary to release one prisoner to the people, whomever they requested.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 23:18–19; Matthew 27:16; Luke 23:25"> A man named Barabbas was imprisoned with rebels who had committed murder during an insurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then the crowd came up and began to ask Pilate to release a prisoner for them, as was his custom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 19:14–16; John 19:4–5; John 18:39"> So Pilate asked them, “Do you want me to release the king of the Jews for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 27:4; Titus 3:3; Genesis 4:4–6"> (For he knew that the chief priests had handed him over because of envy.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 3:14; John 18:40; Hosea 5:1"> But the chief priests stirred up the crowd to have him release Barabbas instead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:5–6; Isaiah 9:6–7; Zechariah 9:9"> So Pilate spoke to them again, “Then what do you want me to do with the one you call king of the Jews?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> They shouted back, “Crucify him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 23:41; Luke 23:23–24; Isaiah 53:3"> Pilate asked them, “Why? What has he done wrong?” But they shouted more insistently, “Crucify him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 19:1; Matthew 27:26; Isaiah 50:6"> Because he wanted to satisfy the crowd, Pilate released Barabbas for them. Then, after he had Jesus flogged, he handed him over to be crucified.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus is Mocked

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 19:9; Matthew 27:27–31; John 18:28"> So the soldiers led him into the palace (that is, the governor’s residence) and called together the whole cohort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 27:28–30; John 19:2–5; Luke 23:11"> They put a purple cloak on him and after braiding a crown of thorns, they put it on him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 27:42–43; Mark 15:2; Genesis 37:10"> They began to salute him: “Hail, king of the Jews!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:65; Isaiah 53:3–5; Luke 23:11"> Again and again they struck him on the head with a staff and spit on him. Then they knelt down and paid homage to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 19:16; Matthew 27:31"> When they had finished mocking him, they stripped him of the purple cloak and put his own clothes back on him. Then they led him away to crucify him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Crucifixion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:32; Luke 23:26; Romans 16:13"> The soldiers forced a passerby to carry his cross, Simon of Cyrene, who was coming in from the country (he was the father of Alexander and Rufus).</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 27:33–44; John 19:17–27; Luke 23:27–38"> They brought Jesus to a place called Golgotha (which is translated, “Place of the Skull”).</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 27:34; John 19:28–30; Luke 23:36"> They offered him wine mixed with myrrh, but he did not take it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 23:34; John 19:23–24; Matthew 27:35–36"> Then they crucified him and divided his clothes, throwing dice for them, to decide what each would take.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 19:14; Mark 15:33; Matthew 27:45"> It was nine o’clock in the morning when they crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Matthew 2:2; John 19:18–22"> The inscription of the charge against him read, “The king of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 23:32–33; Matthew 27:38; John 19:18"> And they crucified two outlaws with him, one on his right and one on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="[[EMPTY]]">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Psalms 109:25; Lamentations 1:12"> Those who passed by defamed him, shaking their heads and saying, “Aha! You who can destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> save yourself and come down from the cross!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 12:23–24; Matthew 27:41–43; Luke 23:35–37"> In the same way even the chief priests – together with the experts in the law – were mocking him among themselves: “He saved others, but he cannot save himself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Luke 23:39–43; Zechariah 9:9"> Let the Christ, the king of Israel, come down from the cross now, that we may see and believe!” Those who were crucified with him also spoke abusively to him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Luke 23:44–45; Amos 8:9–10"> Now when it was noon, darkness came over the whole land until three in the afternoon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Psalms 22:1; Matthew 27:46; Hebrews 5:7"> Around three o’clock Jesus cried out with a loud voice, “Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?” which means, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 27:47–49; Mark 9:11–13; Matthew 17:11–13"> When some of the bystanders heard it they said, “Listen, he is calling for Elijah!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Mark 15:23; John 19:28–30"> Then someone ran, filled a sponge with sour wine, put it on a stick, and gave it to him to drink, saying, “Leave him alone! Let’s see if Elijah will come to take him down!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 19:30; Luke 23:46; Matthew 27:50"> But Jesus cried out with a loud voice and breathed his last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–23; Luke 23:45; Hebrews 9:3–12"> And the temple curtain was torn in two, from top to bottom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Luke 23:47–48; Matthew 27:43"> Now when the centurion, who stood in front of him, saw how he died, he said, “Truly this man was God’s Son!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> There were also women, watching from a distance. Among them were Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joses, and Salome.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 8:2–3; Matthew 27:55–56"> When he was in Galilee, they had followed him and given him support. Many other women who had come up with him to Jerusalem were there too.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Burial

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42; John 19:31"> Now when evening had already come, since it was the day of preparation (that is, the day before the Sabbath),</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 2:38; Luke 2:25; Luke 23:51"> Joseph of Arimathea, a highly regarded member of the council, who was himself looking forward to the kingdom of God, went boldly to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 19:31–37"> Pilate was surprised that he was already dead. He called the centurion and asked him if he had been dead for some time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 27:58; John 19:38; Mark 15:39"> When Pilate was informed by the centurion, he gave the body to Joseph.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 22:16"> After Joseph bought a linen cloth and took down the body, he wrapped it in the linen and placed it in a tomb cut out of the rock. Then he rolled a stone across the entrance of the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 15:40; Mark 16:1; Luke 23:55–2"> Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses saw where the body was placed.</span>
</div>
