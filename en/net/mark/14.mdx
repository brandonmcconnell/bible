<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 7:37–39; John 12:1–8; John 11:2"> Now while Jesus was in Bethany at the house of Simon the leper, reclining at the table, a woman came with an alabaster jar of costly aromatic oil from pure nard. <span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 12:4–5; Matthew 26:8–9; Malachi 1:12–13"> But some who were present indignantly said to one another, “Why this waste of expensive ointment?</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 26:57–68; John 18:12–14; Luke 22:54–62"> Then they led Jesus to the high priest, and all the chief priests and elders and experts in the law came together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 18:18; Matthew 26:3; John 18:25"> And Peter had followed him from a distance, up to the high priest’s courtyard. He was sitting with the guards and warming himself by the fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16–18; Matthew 26:59–60; Matthew 5:22"> The chief priests and the whole Sanhedrin were looking for evidence against Jesus so that they could put him to death, but they did not find anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> Many gave false testimony against him, but their testimony did not agree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Acts 6:13–14; Matthew 26:60–61; John 2:18–21"> Some stood up and gave this false testimony against him:</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 2:19; Hebrews 9:11; 2 Corinthians 5:1"> “We heard him say, ‘I will destroy this temple made with hands and in three days build another not made with hands.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Yet even on this point their testimony did not agree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 19:9–10; Matthew 26:62–63; Mark 15:3–5"> Then the high priest stood up before them and asked Jesus, “Have you no answer? What is this that they are testifying against you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Matthew 26:63–64; Mark 15:2; Isaiah 53:7"> But he was silent and did not answer. Again the high priest questioned him, “Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed One?”</span>

<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 10:31–33; John 19:7"> You have heard the blasphemy! What is your verdict?” They all condemned him as deserving death.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Mark 14:29–31; Matthew 26:71–72; John 13:36–38"> But he denied it: “I don’t even understand what you’re talking about!” Then he went out to the gateway, and a rooster crowed.</span>
