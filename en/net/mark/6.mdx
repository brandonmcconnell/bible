## Sending Out the Twelve Apostles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–10; Luke 10:17–20; Matthew 10:1–5"> Jesus called the twelve and began to send them out two by two. He gave them authority over the unclean spirits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:3; Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4"> He instructed them to take nothing for the journey except a staff – no bread, no bag, no money in their belts –</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 12:8; Ephesians 6:15"> and to put on sandals but not to wear two tunics.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 10:7–8; Luke 9:4; Matthew 10:11–13"> He said to them, “Wherever you enter a house, stay there until you leave the area.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 10:14–15; Acts 18:6; Luke 10:10–15"> If a place will not welcome you or listen to you, as you go out from there, shake the dust off your feet as a testimony against them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 4:17; Luke 13:3; Acts 20:21"> So they went out and preached that all should repent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="James 5:14–15; Mark 6:7; Luke 10:17"> They cast out many demons and anointed many sick people with oil and healed them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Mark 6:14–29; Luke 23:7–12"> Now King Herod heard this, for Jesus’ name had become known. Some were saying, “John the baptizer has been raised from the dead, and because of this, miraculous powers are at work in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 16:14; Malachi 4:5; Matthew 21:11"> Others said, “He is Elijah.” Others said, “He is a prophet, like one of the prophets from the past.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 11:10–13; Matthew 27:4; Luke 9:9"> But when Herod heard this, he said, “John, whom I beheaded, has been raised!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 3:19–20; Matthew 11:2; Luke 3:1"> For Herod himself had sent men, arrested John, and bound him in prison on account of Herodias, his brother Philip’s wife, because Herod had married her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 20:21; Leviticus 18:16; 1 Kings 22:14"> For John had repeatedly told Herod, “It is not lawful for you to have your brother’s wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:9; Ephesians 4:26–27; Genesis 39:17–20"> So Herodias nursed a grudge against him and wanted to kill him. But she could not</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 21:26; Matthew 14:5; 2 Chronicles 24:15–22"> because Herod stood in awe of John and protected him, since he knew that John was a righteous and holy man. When Herod heard him, he was thoroughly baffled, and yet he liked to listen to John.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Esther 2:18; Genesis 40:20; 1 Peter 4:3"> But a suitable day came, when Herod gave a banquet on his birthday for his court officials, military commanders, and leaders of Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Esther 1:10–12; Matthew 14:6; Daniel 5:2"> When his daughter Herodias came in and danced, she pleased Herod and his dinner guests. The king said to the girl, “Ask me for whatever you want and I will give it to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Esther 7:2; Esther 5:3; Esther 5:6"> He swore to her, “Whatever you ask I will give you, up to half my kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Job 31:31; Ezekiel 19:2–3; Genesis 27:8–11"> So she went out and said to her mother, “What should I ask for?” Her mother said, “The head of John the baptizer.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Romans 3:15; Numbers 7:19–89; Numbers 7:13"> Immediately she hurried back to the king and made her request: “I want the head of John the Baptist on a platter immediately.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:24–25; Matthew 14:9; Matthew 27:3–5"> Although it grieved the king deeply, he did not want to reject her request because of his oath and his guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 14:10–11"> So the king sent an executioner at once to bring John’s head, and he went and beheaded John in prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> He brought his head on a platter and gave it to the girl, and the girl gave it to her mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 14:12; 2 Chronicles 24:16; Matthew 27:57–60"> When John’s disciples heard this, they came and took his body and placed it in a tomb.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Feeding of the Five Thousand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 9:10; Luke 10:17; Matthew 10:2"> Then the apostles gathered around Jesus and told him everything they had done and taught.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 3:20; John 6:1; Mark 1:45"> He said to them, “Come with me privately to an isolated place and rest a while” (for many were coming and going, and there was no time to eat).</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 8:2–9; Mark 6:45; Mark 4:36"> So they went away by themselves in a boat to some remote place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 6:2; Matthew 15:29–31; Mark 6:54–55"> But many saw them leaving and recognized them, and they hurried on foot from all the towns and arrived there ahead of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 9:36; Luke 9:11; Numbers 27:17"> As Jesus came ashore he saw the large crowd and he had compassion on them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd. So he taught them many things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:5–15; Luke 9:12–17; Matthew 14:15–21"> When it was already late, his disciples came to him and said, “This is an isolated place and it is already very late.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 15:23; Mark 3:21; Mark 5:31"> Send them away so that they can go into the surrounding countryside and villages and buy something for themselves to eat.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Kings 4:42–44; Matthew 15:32–33; Mark 8:2–3"> But he answered them, “You give them something to eat.” And they said, “Should we go and buy bread for two hundred silver coins and give it to them to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:34; Mark 8:5; John 6:9"> He said to them, “How many loaves do you have? Go and see.” When they found out, they said, “Five – and two fish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 Corinthians 14:33; Matthew 15:35"> Then he directed them all to sit down in groups on the green grass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 9:14–15"> So they reclined in groups of hundreds and fifties.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Mark 7:34; Luke 9:16"> He took the five loaves and the two fish, and looking up to heaven, he gave thanks and broke the loaves. He gave them to his disciples to serve the people, and he divided the two fish among them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 9:17; Mark 8:8–9; Matthew 15:37–38"> They all ate and were satisfied,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Mark 8:19–20"> and they picked up the broken pieces and fish that were left over, twelve baskets full.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Now there were five thousand men who ate the bread.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Walking on Water

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 14:22–33; John 6:15–21; Mark 6:32"> Immediately Jesus made his disciples get into the boat and go on ahead to the other side, to Bethsaida, while he dispersed the crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Mark 1:35; Matthew 6:6; Luke 6:12"> After saying good-bye to them, he went to the mountain to pray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 14:23; John 6:16–17"> When evening came, the boat was in the middle of the sea and he was alone on the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 24:28; Luke 12:38; Job 9:8"> He saw them straining at the oars, because the wind was against them. As the night was ending, he came to them walking on the sea, for he wanted to pass by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 14:25–26; Luke 24:37; Job 9:8"> When they saw him walking on the water they thought he was a ghost. They cried out,</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Matthew 14:27; John 6:19–20; Isaiah 43:2"> for they all saw him and were terrified. But immediately he spoke to them: “Have courage! It is I. Do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Psalms 93:3–4; Mark 4:39; John 6:21"> Then he went up with them into the boat, and the wind ceased. They were completely astonished,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Mark 8:17–21; Luke 24:25; Isaiah 63:17"> because they did not understand about the loaves, but their hearts were hardened.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Healing the Sick

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 14:34–36; John 6:24–25; Luke 5:1"> After they had crossed over, they came to land at Gennesaret and anchored there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Psalms 9:10; Philippians 3:10"> As they got out of the boat, people immediately recognized Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 2:1–3; Mark 3:7–11"> They ran through that whole region and began to bring the sick on mats to wherever he was rumored to be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Matthew 9:20; Luke 6:19; Numbers 15:38–39"> And wherever he would go – into villages, towns, or countryside – they would place the sick in the marketplaces, and would ask him if they could just touch the edge of his cloak, and all who touched it were healed.</span>
</div>
