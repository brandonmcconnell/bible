<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Job 2:7–8; 1 Kings 18:28; John 8:44"> Each night and every day among the tombs and in the mountains, he would cry out and cut himself with stones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 2:19; Psalms 66:3; Luke 4:41"> When he saw Jesus from a distance, he ran and bowed down before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 8:29; Acts 16:17; Matthew 26:63"> Then he cried out with a loud voice, “Leave me alone, Jesus, Son of the Most High God! I implore you by God – do not torment me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 1:25; Mark 9:25–26; Acts 16:18"> (For Jesus had said to him, “Come out of that man, you unclean spirit!”)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 8:30; Mark 5:15; Matthew 26:53"> Jesus asked him, “What is your name?” And he said, “My name is Legion, for we are many.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Mark 3:22; Mark 5:13"> He begged Jesus repeatedly not to send them out of the region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 11:7–8; Isaiah 65:4; Isaiah 66:3"> There on the hillside, a great herd of pigs was feeding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:11; Job 1:10–12; Luke 22:31–32"> And the demonic spirits begged him, “Send us into the pigs. Let us enter them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 22:22; 1 Peter 3:22; Job 2:6"> Jesus gave them permission. So the unclean spirits came out and went into the pigs. Then the herd rushed down the steep slope into the lake, and about two thousand were drowned in the lake.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Colossians 1:13; 2 Timothy 1:7; 1 Samuel 6:20–21"> They came to Jesus and saw the demon-possessed man sitting there, clothed and in his right mind – the one who had the “Legion” – and they were afraid.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Those who had seen what had happened to the demon-possessed man reported it, and they also told about the pigs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 8:34; Mark 1:24; Luke 8:37"> Then they asked Jesus to leave their region.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 8:38–39; Philippians 1:23–24; Mark 5:17"> As he was getting into the boat the man who had been demon-possessed asked if he could go with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 66:16; John 4:29; Isaiah 38:9–20"> But Jesus did not permit him to do so. Instead, he said to him, “Go to your home and to your people and tell them what the Lord has done for you, that he had mercy on you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 7:31; Matthew 4:25"> So he went away and began to proclaim in the Decapolis what Jesus had done for him, and all were amazed.</span>

## Restoration and Healing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 9:1; Mark 4:1; Luke 8:40"> When Jesus had crossed again in a boat to the other side, a large crowd gathered around him, and he was by the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 13:14; Acts 18:8; Mark 5:35–36"> Then one of the synagogue rulers, named Jairus, came up, and when he saw Jesus, he fell at his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 13:13; Luke 4:40; Mark 16:18"> He asked him urgently, “My little daughter is near death. Come and lay your hands on her so that she may be healed and live.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 5:31; Mark 3:20; Luke 7:6"> Jesus went with him, and a large crowd followed and pressed around him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 15:25–27; Luke 8:43–44; Matthew 9:20–22"> Now a woman was there who had been suffering from a hemorrhage for twelve years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22; Psalms 108:12"> She had endured a great deal under the care of many doctors and had spent all that she had. Yet instead of getting better, she grew worse.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 19:12; Matthew 14:36; Acts 5:15"> When she heard about Jesus, she came up behind him in the crowd and touched his cloak,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> for she kept saying, “If only I touch his clothes, I will be healed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Psalms 30:2; Mark 3:10"> At once the bleeding stopped, and she felt in her body that she was healed of her disease.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Luke 5:17"> Jesus knew at once that power had gone out from him. He turned around in the crowd and said, “Who touched my clothes?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 8:45; Luke 9:12"> His disciples said to him, “You see the crowd pressing against you and you say, ‘Who touched me?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But he looked around to see who had done it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 8:47; Luke 1:29; Luke 1:12"> Then the woman, with fear and trembling, knowing what had happened to her, came and fell down before him and told him the whole truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 8:48; Matthew 9:22; Luke 7:50"> He said to her, “Daughter, your faith has made you well. Go in peace, and be healed of your disease.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 8:49; John 11:39; Mark 5:22"> While he was still speaking, people came from the synagogue ruler’s house saying, “Your daughter has died. Why trouble the teacher any longer?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Luke 8:50; Matthew 9:28–29; John 11:40"> But Jesus, paying no attention to what was said, told the synagogue ruler, “Do not be afraid; just believe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Acts 9:40; Mark 14:33; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> He did not let anyone follow him except Peter, James, and John, the brother of James.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:23–24; Luke 8:52–53; Mark 5:22"> They came to the house of the synagogue ruler where he saw noisy confusion and people weeping and wailing loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 20:10; 1 Thessalonians 5:10; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–14"> When he entered he said to them, “Why are you distressed and weeping? The child is not dead but asleep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:19; 2 Kings 4:33; Matthew 9:24–25"> And they began making fun of him. But he put them all outside and he took the child’s father and mother and his own companions and went into the room where the child was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Luke 7:14–15; Luke 8:54–55"> Then, gently taking the child by the hand, he said to her, “Talitha koum,” which means, “Little girl, I say to you, get up.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Mark 4:41; Acts 3:10–13; Mark 7:37"> The girl got up at once and began to walk around (she was twelve years old). They were completely astonished at this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 8:4; Matthew 9:30; Mark 1:43"> He strictly ordered that no one should know about this, and told them to give her something to eat.</span>
</div>
</div>
