<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:11; Philippians 1:25"> We pray earnestly night and day to see you in person and make up what may be lacking in your faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Matthew 6:32; 2 Thessalonians 2:16"> Now may God our Father himself and our Lord Jesus direct our way to you.</span>
