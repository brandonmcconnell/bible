## Thanksgiving for Response to the Gospel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:8–9; Ephesians 1:15–16; Philemon 1:4"> We thank God always for all of you as we mention you constantly in our prayers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:13; Revelation 2:19; Galatians 5:6"> because we recall in the presence of our God and Father your work of faith and labor of love and endurance of hope in our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:13; Titus 3:4–5; 2 Peter 1:10"> We know, brothers and sisters loved by God, that he has chosen you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:7–9; Mark 16:20; 1 Corinthians 2:2–5"> in that our gospel did not come to you merely in words, but in power and in the Holy Spirit and with deep conviction (surely you recall the character we displayed when we came among you to help you).</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:1; 1 Corinthians 4:16; Acts 13:52"> And you became imitators of us and of the Lord, when you received the message with joy that comes from the Holy Spirit, despite great affliction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Titus 2:7; 1 Peter 5:3; 1 Timothy 4:12"> As a result you became an example to all the believers in Macedonia and in Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 1:8; Romans 10:14–18; Isaiah 52:7"> For from you the message of the Lord has echoed forth not just in Macedonia and Achaia, but in every place reports of your faith in God have spread, so that we do not need to say anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:10; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Galatians 4:8–9"> For people everywhere report how you welcomed us and how you turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:9; Isaiah 25:8–9; Romans 5:9–10"> and to wait for his Son from heaven, whom he raised from the dead, Jesus our deliverer from the coming wrath.</span>
</div>
</div>
