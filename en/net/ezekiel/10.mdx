<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Ezekiel 9:3; Exodus 40:35"> Then the glory of the Lord arose from the cherub and moved to the threshold of the temple. The temple was filled with the cloud while the court was filled with the brightness of the Lord’s glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Job 40:9; Exodus 19:16"> The sound of the wings of the cherubim could be heard from the outer court, like the sound of the sovereign God when he speaks.</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 13:41–42; Ezekiel 1:13; Ezekiel 41:23–26"> Then one of the cherubim stretched out his hand toward the fire which was among the cherubim. He took some and put it into the hands of the man dressed in linen, who took it and left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:8; Ezekiel 10:21; Isaiah 6:6"> (The cherubim appeared to have the form of human hands under their wings.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:15–17; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 21:20"> As I watched, I noticed four wheels by the cherubim, one wheel beside each cherub; the wheels gleamed like jasper.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Psalms 36:6; Psalms 104:24"> As for their appearance, all four of them looked the same, something like a wheel within a wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:17; Ezekiel 10:22; Ezekiel 1:20"> When they moved, they would go in any of the four directions they faced without turning as they moved; in the direction the head would turn they would follow without turning as they moved,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:18; Revelation 4:8; Revelation 4:6"> along with their entire bodies, their backs, their hands, and their wings. The wheels of the four of them were full of eyes all around.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> As for their wheels, they were called “the wheelwork” as I listened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 4:7; Ezekiel 10:21; 1 Kings 7:36"> Each of the cherubim had four faces: The first was the face of a cherub, the second that of a man, the third that of a lion, and the fourth that of an eagle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:5; Hosea 9:12; Ezekiel 11:22"> The cherubim rose up; these were the living beings I saw at the Kebar River.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:19–21"> When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved beside them; when the cherubim spread their wings to rise from the ground, the wheels did not move from their side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:20–21; Romans 8:2; Ezekiel 1:12"> When the cherubim stood still, the wheels stood still, and when they rose up, the wheels rose up with them, for the spirit of the living beings was in the wheels.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 18:10; Matthew 23:37–39; Psalms 78:60–61"> Then the glory of the Lord moved away from the threshold of the temple and stopped above the cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 1:17–21; Ezekiel 1:26–28"> The cherubim spread their wings, and they rose up from the earth while I watched (when they went the wheels went alongside them). They stopped at the entrance to the east gate of the Lord’s temple as the glory of the God of Israel hovered above them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:1; Ezekiel 10:15; 1 Kings 7:36"> These were the living creatures which I saw at the Kebar River underneath the God of Israel; I knew that they were cherubim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:6; Ezekiel 41:18–19; Ezekiel 10:14"> Each had four faces; each had four wings and the form of human hands under the wings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:11; Ezekiel 1:10; Ezekiel 1:12"> As for the form of their faces, they were the faces whose appearance I had seen at the Kebar River. Each one moved straight ahead.</span>
