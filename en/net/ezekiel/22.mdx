<div className="bible-chapter" lang="en" dir="ltr" data-lang="en" data-translation="net" data-translation-id="eng_net" data-translation-label="NET" data-book="EZK" data-book-slug="ezekiel" data-book-name="Ezekiel" data-chapter="22" data-verses="31">

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 58:1; Ezekiel 24:6; Ezekiel 24:9"> “As for you, son of man, are you willing to pronounce judgment, are you willing to pronounce judgment on the bloody city? Then confront her with all her abominable deeds!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Romans 2:5; 2 Peter 2:3"> Then say, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: O city, who spills blood within herself (which brings on her doom), and who makes herself idols (which results in impurity),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 21:16; Psalms 44:13–14; Ezekiel 22:2"> you are guilty because of the blood you shed and defiled by the idols you made. You have hastened the day of your doom; the end of your years has come. Therefore I will make you an object of scorn to the nations, an object to be mocked by all lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 22:2; Jeremiah 15:2–3"> Those both near and far from you will mock you, you with your bad reputation, full of turmoil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:27; Isaiah 1:23; Zechariah 3:3"> “‘See how each of the princes of Israel living within you has used his authority to shed blood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:16; Exodus 22:21–22; Leviticus 20:9"> They have treated father and mother with contempt within you; they have oppressed the foreigner among you; they have wronged the orphan and the widow within you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:38–39; Ezekiel 22:26; Ezekiel 20:13"> You have despised my holy things and desecrated my Sabbaths!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hosea 4:14; Leviticus 19:16; Hosea 4:10"> Slanderous men shed blood within you. Those who live within you eat pagan sacrifices on the mountains; they commit obscene acts among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 18:19; Leviticus 18:7–8; Ezekiel 18:6"> They have sex with their father’s wife within you; they violate women during their menstrual period within you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 18:15; 2 Samuel 13:14; Leviticus 20:17"> One commits an abominable act with his neighbor’s wife; another obscenely defiles his daughter-in-law; another violates his sister – his father’s daughter – within you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:25; Ezekiel 23:35; Psalms 106:21"> They take bribes within you to shed blood. You engage in usury and charge interest; you extort money from your neighbors. You have forgotten me, declares the sovereign Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:17; Isaiah 33:15; Amos 2:6–8"> “‘See, I strike my hands together at the dishonest profit you have made, and at the bloodshed they have done among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Ezekiel 17:24; Ezekiel 24:14"> Can your heart endure, or can your hands be strong when I deal with you? I, the Lord, have spoken, and I will do it!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:8; Ezekiel 22:22; Deuteronomy 4:27"> I will scatter you among the nations and disperse you among various countries; I will remove your impurity from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Psalms 83:18; Ezekiel 7:24"> You will be profaned within yourself in the sight of the nations; then you will know that I am the Lord.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 119:119; Isaiah 1:22; Jeremiah 6:28–30"> “Son of man, the house of Israel has become slag to me. All of them are like bronze, tin, iron, and lead in the furnace; they are the worthless slag of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:30; Ezekiel 24:3–6; Micah 4:12"> Therefore this is what the sovereign Lord says: ‘Because all of you have become slag, look out! – I am about to gather you in the middle of Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:11–12; Isaiah 54:16; Ezekiel 24:13"> As silver, bronze, iron, lead, and tin are gathered in a furnace so that the fire can melt them, so I will gather you in my anger and in my rage. I will deposit you there and melt you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 64:7; Nahum 1:6; Psalms 50:3"> I will gather you and blow on you with the fire of my fury, and you will be melted in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 20:33; Hosea 5:10"> As silver is melted in a furnace, so you will be melted in it, and you will know that I, the Lord, have poured out my anger on you.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:14–16; Jeremiah 2:30; 2 Chronicles 28:22"> “Son of man, say to her: ‘You are a land that receives no rain or showers in the day of my anger.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hosea 6:9; Jeremiah 2:34; Ezekiel 13:19"> Her princes within her are like a roaring lion tearing its prey; they have devoured lives. They take away riches and valuable things; they have made many women widows within it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10; Ezekiel 44:23; Ezekiel 22:8"> Her priests abuse my law and have desecrated my holy things. They do not distinguish between the holy and the profane, or recognize any distinction between the unclean and the clean. They ignore my Sabbaths and I am profaned in their midst.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:13; Isaiah 1:23; Ezekiel 22:6"> Her officials are like wolves in her midst rending their prey – shedding blood and destroying lives – so they can get dishonest profit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:6–7; Ezekiel 13:10–16; Jeremiah 23:25–32"> Her prophets coat their messages with whitewash. They see false visions and announce lying omens for them, saying, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says,’ when the Lord has not spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:7; Exodus 23:9; Exodus 22:21"> The people of the land have practiced extortion and committed robbery. They have wronged the poor and needy; they have oppressed the foreigner who lives among them and denied them justice.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Isaiah 59:16; Jeremiah 5:1; Psalms 106:23"> “I looked for a man from among them who would repair the wall and stand in the gap before me on behalf of the land, so that I would not destroy it, but I found no one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:43; Ezekiel 7:8–9; Ezekiel 9:10"> So I have poured my anger on them, and destroyed them with the fire of my fury. I hereby repay them for what they have done, declares the sovereign Lord.”</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
