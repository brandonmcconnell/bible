<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 20:1; 2 Kings 6:32"> Then some men from Israel’s elders came to me and sat down in front of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Amos 3:7; 1 Kings 14:4"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:7; Ezekiel 7:19; Ezekiel 20:3"> “Son of man, these men have erected their idols in their hearts and placed the obstacle leading to their iniquity right before their faces. Should I really allow them to seek me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 66:4; Ezekiel 14:7; Isaiah 3:11"> Therefore speak to them and say to them, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: When any one from the house of Israel erects his idols in his heart and sets the obstacle leading to his iniquity before his face, and then consults a prophet, I the Lord am determined to answer him personally according to the enormity of his idolatry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Zechariah 11:8; Romans 1:28"> I will do this in order to capture the hearts of the house of Israel, who have alienated themselves from me on account of all their idols.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:30; Isaiah 30:22; Ezekiel 36:31–32"> “Therefore say to the house of Israel, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: Return! Turn from your idols, and turn your faces away from your abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:48; Isaiah 58:1–2; Leviticus 24:22"> For when anyone from the house of Israel, or the foreigner who lives in Israel, separates himself from me and erects his idols in his heart and sets the obstacle leading to his iniquity before his face, and then consults a prophet to seek something from me, I the Lord am determined to answer him personally.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 15:7; Jeremiah 44:11; Ezekiel 5:15"> I will set my face against that person and will make him an object lesson and a byword and will cut him off from among my people. Then you will know that I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:25; Jeremiah 4:10; Psalms 81:11–12"> “‘As for the prophet, if he is made a fool by being deceived into speaking a prophetic word – I, the Lord, have made a fool of that prophet, and I will stretch out my hand against him and destroy him from among my people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:49; Ezekiel 14:4; Jeremiah 8:11–12"> They will bear their punishment; the punishment of the one who sought an oracle will be the same as the punishment of the prophet who gave it</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 44:10; Revelation 21:7"> so that the house of Israel will no longer go astray from me, nor continue to defile themselves by all their sins. They will be my people and I will be their God, declares the sovereign Lord.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 4:16; Isaiah 24:20"> “Son of man, suppose a country sins against me by being unfaithful, and I stretch out my hand against it, cut off its bread supply, cause famine to come on it, and kill both people and animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:20; Jeremiah 15:1; Ezekiel 28:3"> Even if these three men, Noah, Daniel, and Job, were in it, they would save only their own lives by their righteousness, declares the sovereign Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:17; Leviticus 26:22; Jeremiah 15:3"> “Suppose I were to send wild animals through the land and kill its children, leaving it desolate, without travelers due to the wild animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; James 5:16; Ezekiel 33:11"> Even if these three men were in it, as surely as I live, declares the sovereign Lord, they could not save their own sons or daughters; they would save only their own lives, and the land would become desolate.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Ezekiel 21:3–4; Zephaniah 1:3"> “Or suppose I were to bring a sword against that land and say, ‘Let a sword pass through the land,’ and I were to kill both people and animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Even if these three men were in it, as surely as I live, declares the sovereign Lord, they could not save their own sons or daughters – they would save only their own lives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 5:12; Jeremiah 14:12"> “Or suppose I were to send a plague into that land, and pour out my rage on it with bloodshed, killing both people and animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; 1 John 2:29; Isaiah 3:10"> Even if Noah, Daniel, and Job were in it, as surely as I live, declares the sovereign Lord, they could not save their own son or daughter; they would save only their own lives by their righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:27; Ezekiel 5:17; Ezekiel 14:13"> “For this is what the sovereign Lord says: How much worse will it be when I send my four terrible judgments – sword, famine, wild animals, and plague – to Jerusalem to kill both people and animals!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:16; Ezekiel 20:43; Hebrews 12:6–11"> Yet some survivors will be left in it, sons and daughters who will be brought out. They will come out to you, and when you see their behavior and their deeds, you will be consoled about the catastrophe I have brought on Jerusalem – for everything I brought on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; Revelation 15:4; Deuteronomy 8:2"> They will console you when you see their behavior and their deeds, because you will know that it was not without reason that I have done everything which I have done in it, declares the sovereign Lord.”</span>
</div>
</div>
