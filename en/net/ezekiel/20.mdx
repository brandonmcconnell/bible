<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:1; Ezekiel 30:20; Ezekiel 24:1"> In the seventh year, in the fifth month, on the tenth of the month, some of the elders of Israel came to seek the Lord, and they sat down in front of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Matthew 15:8–9; Psalms 50:15–21"> “Son of man, speak to the elders of Israel, and tell them: ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: Are you coming to seek me? As surely as I live, I will not allow you to seek me, declares the sovereign Lord.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 22:2; Ezekiel 23:36; Ezekiel 14:20"> “Are you willing to pronounce judgment? Are you willing to pronounce judgment, son of man? Then confront them with the abominable practices of their fathers,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:6; Ezekiel 20:15; Exodus 3:8"> and say to them:<br />“‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: On the day I chose Israel I swore to the descendants of the house of Jacob and made myself known to them in the land of Egypt. I swore to them, “I am the Lord your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Jeremiah 32:22; Ezekiel 20:15"> On that day I swore to bring them out of the land of Egypt to a land which I had picked out for them, a land flowing with milk and honey, the most beautiful of all lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 18:3; Ezekiel 20:8; Leviticus 11:44"> I said to them, “Each of you must get rid of the detestable idols you keep before you, and do not defile yourselves with the idols of Egypt; I am the Lord your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:7; Isaiah 63:10; Ezekiel 20:21"> But they rebelled against me, and refused to listen to me; no one got rid of their detestable idols, nor did they abandon the idols of Egypt. Then I decided to pour out my rage on them and fully vent my anger against them in the midst of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:7; Ezekiel 20:22; Exodus 32:12"> I acted for the sake of my reputation, so that I would not be profaned before the nations among whom they lived, before whom I revealed myself by bringing them out of the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 15:22; Exodus 13:17–18; Exodus 14:17–22"> “‘So I brought them out of the land of Egypt and led them to the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 10:5; Leviticus 18:5; Deuteronomy 4:8"> I gave them my statutes and revealed my regulations to them. The one who carries them out will live by them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:20; Ezekiel 37:28; Leviticus 21:23"> I also gave them my Sabbaths as a reminder of our relationship, so that they would know that I, the Lord, sanctify them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:21; Ezekiel 20:8; Isaiah 56:6"> But the house of Israel rebelled against me in the wilderness; they did not follow my statutes and they rejected my regulations (the one who obeys them will live by them), and they utterly desecrated my Sabbaths. So I decided to pour out my rage on them in the wilderness and destroy them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:9; Ezekiel 36:22–23; Ezekiel 20:22"> I acted for the sake of my reputation, so that I would not be profaned before the nations in whose sight I had brought them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 95:11; Psalms 106:26; Hebrews 3:18"> I also swore to them in the wilderness that I would not bring them to the land I had given them – a land flowing with milk and honey, the most beautiful of all lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 15:39; Ezekiel 20:8; Ezekiel 14:3–4"> I did this because they rejected my regulations, did not follow my statutes, and desecrated my Sabbaths; for their hearts followed their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:13; Jeremiah 4:27; Jeremiah 5:18"> Yet I had pity on them and did not destroy them, so I did not make an end of them in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 1:2–4; Psalms 78:6–8; 1 Peter 1:18"> “‘But I said to their children in the wilderness, “Do not follow the practices of your fathers; do not observe their regulations, nor defile yourselves with their idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; Deuteronomy 5:32–8; Ezekiel 37:24"> I am the Lord your God; follow my statutes, observe my regulations, and carry them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Jeremiah 17:22; Exodus 31:13–17"> Treat my Sabbaths as holy and they will be a reminder of our relationship, and then you will know that I am the Lord your God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:13; Numbers 21:5; Ezekiel 20:8"> “‘But the children rebelled against me, did not follow my statutes, did not observe my regulations by carrying them out (the one who obeys them will live by them), and desecrated my Sabbaths. I decided to pour out my rage on them and fully vent my anger against them in the wilderness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:14; Ezekiel 20:9; Jeremiah 14:21"> But I refrained from doing so, and acted instead for the sake of my reputation, so that I would not be profaned before the nations in whose sight I had brought them out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 26:33; Deuteronomy 28:64–68; Deuteronomy 32:26–27"> I also swore to them in the wilderness that I would scatter them among the nations and disperse them throughout the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:16; Ezekiel 6:9; Amos 2:4"> I did this because they did not observe my regulations, they rejected my statutes, they desecrated my Sabbaths, and their eyes were fixed on their fathers’ idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 81:12; Isaiah 66:4; Ezekiel 20:39"> I also gave them decrees which were not good and regulations by which they could not live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Leviticus 18:21; Ezekiel 20:31"> I declared them to be defiled because of their sacrifices – they caused all their first born to pass through the fire – so that I would devastate them, so that they will know that I am the Lord.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Ezekiel 2:7; Ezekiel 3:11"> “Therefore, speak to the house of Israel, son of man, and tell them, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: In this way too your fathers blasphemed me when they were unfaithful to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:13; Nehemiah 9:22–26; Jeremiah 3:6"> I brought them to the land which I swore to give them, but whenever they saw any high hill or leafy tree, they offered their sacrifices there and presented the offerings that provoke me to anger. They offered their soothing aroma there and poured out their drink offerings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:24–25; Ezekiel 16:31"> So I said to them, What is this high place you go to?’” (So it is called “High Place” to this day.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 16:12; Jeremiah 7:26; Judges 2:19"> “Therefore say to the house of Israel, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: Will you defile yourselves like your fathers and engage in prostitution with detestable idols?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:26; Psalms 106:37–39; Jeremiah 7:31"> When you present your sacrifices – when you make your sons pass through the fire – you defile yourselves with all your idols to this very day. Will I allow you to seek me, O house of Israel? As surely as I live, declares the sovereign Lord, I will not allow you to seek me!</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:5; Jeremiah 44:17; Psalms 139:2"> “‘What you plan will never happen. You say, “We will be like the nations, like the clans of the lands, who serve gods of wood and stone.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:5; Ezekiel 8:18; Jeremiah 44:6"> As surely as I live, declares the sovereign Lord, with a powerful hand and an outstretched arm, and with an outpouring of rage, I will be king over you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:38; Amos 9:9–10; Ezekiel 34:16"> I will bring you out from the nations, and will gather you from the lands where you are scattered, with a powerful hand and an outstretched arm and with an outpouring of rage!</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Jeremiah 2:35; Ezekiel 17:20"> I will bring you into the wilderness of the nations, and there I will enter into judgment with you face to face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5–10; Numbers 11:1–35; Ezekiel 20:13"> Just as I entered into judgment with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so I will enter into judgment with you, declares the sovereign Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:13; Leviticus 27:32; Matthew 25:32–33"> I will make you pass under the shepherd’s staff, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Ezekiel 13:9; Amos 9:9–10; Psalms 95:11"> I will eliminate from among you the rebels and those who revolt against me. I will bring them out from the land where they have been residing, but they will not come to the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hosea 4:17; Isaiah 1:13–15; Ezekiel 43:7"> “‘As for you, O house of Israel, this is what the sovereign Lord says: Each of you go and serve your idols, if you will not listen to me. But my holy name will not be profaned again by your sacrifices and your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Malachi 3:4; Isaiah 60:7"> For there on my holy mountain, the high mountain of Israel, declares the sovereign Lord, all the house of Israel will serve me, all of them in the land. I will accept them there, and there I will seek your contributions and your choice gifts, with all your holy things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Ezekiel 11:17; Ezekiel 38:23"> When I bring you out from the nations and gather you from the lands where you are scattered, I will accept you along with your soothing aroma. I will display my holiness among you in the sight of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:13; Ezekiel 38:23; Ezekiel 36:23–24"> Then you will know that I am the Lord when I bring you to the land of Israel, to the land I swore to give to your fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Hosea 5:15; Jeremiah 31:18"> And there you will remember your conduct and all your deeds by which you defiled yourselves. You will despise yourselves because of all the evil deeds you have done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:24; Psalms 115:1; Ezekiel 20:9"> Then you will know that I am the Lord, when I deal with you for the sake of my reputation and not according to your wicked conduct and corrupt deeds, O house of Israel, declares the sovereign Lord.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Prophecy Against the South

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Amos 7:16; Ezekiel 21:2; Jeremiah 13:19"> “Son of man, turn toward the south, and speak out against the south. Prophesy against the open scrub land of the Negev,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Jeremiah 21:14; Isaiah 24:1–6; Ezekiel 17:24"> and say to the scrub land of the Negev, ‘Hear the word of the Lord: This is what the sovereign Lord says: Look here, I am about to start a fire in you, and it will devour every green tree and every dry tree in you. The flaming fire will not be extinguished, and the whole surface of the ground from the Negev to the north will be scorched by it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:2–3; Jeremiah 7:20; Deuteronomy 29:24–28"> And everyone will see that I, the Lord, have burned it; it will not be extinguished.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 16:25; Acts 17:18; Ezekiel 17:2"> Then I said, “O sovereign Lord! They are saying of me, ‘Does he not simply speak in eloquent figures of speech?’”</span>
</div>
