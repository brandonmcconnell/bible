<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:4; Ezekiel 34:14; Ezekiel 37:22"> “As for you, son of man, prophesy to the mountains of Israel, and say: ‘O mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:13; Ezekiel 25:3; Ezekiel 35:10"> This is what the sovereign Lord says: The enemy has spoken against you, saying “Aha!” and, “The ancient heights have become our property!”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 44:13–14; Lamentations 2:15–16; Jeremiah 18:16"> So prophesy and say: ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: Surely because they have made you desolate and crushed you from all directions, so that you have become the property of the rest of the nations, and have become the subject of gossip and slander among the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:28; Deuteronomy 11:11; Ezekiel 36:6"> therefore, O mountains of Israel, hear the word of the sovereign Lord: This is what the sovereign Lord says to the mountains and hills, the ravines and valleys, and to the desolate ruins and the abandoned cities that have become prey and an object of derision to the rest of the nations round about –</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Micah 7:8; Ezekiel 36:3; Psalms 137:7"> therefore this is what the sovereign Lord says: Surely I have spoken in the fire of my zeal against the rest of the nations, and against all Edom, who with great joy and utter contempt have made my land their property and prey, because of its pasture.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 123:3–4; Ezekiel 34:29; Psalms 74:23"> “Therefore prophesy concerning the land of Israel, and say to the mountains and hills, the ravines and valleys, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: Look, I have spoken in my zeal and in my anger, because you have endured the insults of the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5; Amos 1:1–15; Ezekiel 20:15"> So this is what the sovereign Lord says: I vow that the nations around you will endure insults as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 27:6; Hebrews 10:37; Ezekiel 34:26–29"> “‘But you, mountains of Israel, will grow your branches, and bear your fruit for my people Israel; for they will arrive soon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 46:11; Hosea 2:21–23; Ezekiel 36:34"> For indeed, I am on your side; I will turn to you, and you will be plowed and planted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:33; Jeremiah 33:12; Ezekiel 36:37"> I will multiply your people – the whole house of Israel, all of it. The cities will be populated and the ruins rebuilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:12; Jeremiah 23:5–8; Jeremiah 31:27"> I will increase the number of people and animals on you; they will increase and be fruitful. I will cause you to be inhabited as in ancient times, and will do more good for you than at the beginning of your history. Then you will know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:7; Ezekiel 36:13; Jeremiah 32:44"> I will lead people, my people Israel, across you; they will possess you and you will become their inheritance. No longer will you bereave them of their children.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 13:32"> “‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: Because they are saying to you, “You are a devourer of men, and bereave your nation of children,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:25–28; Amos 9:15; Isaiah 60:21"> therefore you will no longer devour people and no longer bereave your nation of children, declares the sovereign Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:29; Psalms 89:50; Ezekiel 36:6"> I will no longer subject you to the nations’ insults; no longer will you bear the shame of the peoples, and no longer will you bereave your nation, declares the sovereign Lord.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:7; Numbers 35:33–34; Micah 2:10"> “Son of man, when the house of Israel was living on their own land, they defiled it by their behavior and their deeds. In my sight their behavior was like the uncleanness of a woman having her monthly period.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Chronicles 34:21; Ezekiel 7:8; Lamentations 2:4"> So I poured my anger on them because of the blood they shed on the land and because of the idols with which they defiled it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:24; Deuteronomy 28:64; Ezekiel 5:12"> I scattered them among the nations; they were dispersed throughout foreign countries. In accordance with their behavior and their deeds I judged them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 2:24; Isaiah 52:5; Jeremiah 33:24"> But when they arrived in the nations where they went, they profaned my holy name. It was said of them, ‘These are the people of the Lord, yet they have departed from his land.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 48:9; Psalms 74:18; Ezekiel 20:9"> I was concerned for my holy reputation which the house of Israel profaned among the nations where they went.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 106:8; Ezekiel 36:32; Psalms 115:1–2"> “Therefore say to the house of Israel, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: It is not for your sake that I am about to act, O house of Israel, but for the sake of my holy reputation which you profaned among the nations where you went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:41; Isaiah 5:16; Ezekiel 39:7"> I will magnify my great name that has been profaned among the nations, that you have profaned among them. The nations will know that I am the Lord, declares the sovereign Lord, when I magnify myself among you in their sight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:21; Ezekiel 34:13; Amos 9:14–15"> “‘I will take you from the nations and gather you from all the countries; then I will bring you to your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; John 3:5; Jeremiah 33:8"> I will sprinkle you with pure water and you will be clean from all your impurities. I will purify you from all your idols.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:19–20; Psalms 51:10; 2 Corinthians 5:17"> I will give you a new heart, and I will put a new spirit within you. I will remove the heart of stone from your body and give you a heart of flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Titus 3:3–6; Hebrews 13:21; 1 Corinthians 3:16"> I will put my Spirit within you; I will take the initiative and you will obey my statutes and carefully observe my regulations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:33; Jeremiah 32:38; Ezekiel 37:27"> Then you will live in the land I gave to your fathers; you will be my people, and I will be your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 1:7–9; Matthew 1:21; Hosea 2:21–23"> I will save you from all your uncleanness. I will call for the grain and multiply it; I will not bring a famine on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Joel 2:26; Deuteronomy 29:23–28"> I will multiply the fruit of the trees and the produce of the fields, so that you will never again suffer the disgrace of famine among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:9; Ezekiel 20:43; Nehemiah 9:26–35"> Then you will remember your evil behavior and your deeds which were not good; you will loathe yourselves on account of your sins and your abominable deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:5; Ezekiel 36:22; 2 Timothy 1:9"> Understand that it is not for your sake I am about to act, declares the sovereign Lord. Be ashamed and embarrassed by your behavior, O house of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Isaiah 58:12; Ezekiel 36:10; Jeremiah 33:10"> “‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: In the day I cleanse you from all your sins, I will populate the cities and the ruins will be rebuilt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:14; Jeremiah 25:9–11; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> The desolate land will be plowed, instead of being desolate in the sight of everyone who passes by.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 51:3; Joel 2:3; Jeremiah 33:9"> They will say, “This desolate land has become like the garden of Eden; the ruined, desolate, and destroyed cities are now fortified and inhabited.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 24:35; Numbers 23:19; Ezekiel 17:24"> Then the nations which remain around you will know that I, the Lord, have rebuilt the ruins and replanted what was desolate. I, the Lord, have spoken – and I will do it!’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 John 5:14; Hebrews 10:21–22; Ezekiel 14:3"> “This is what the sovereign Lord says: I will allow the house of Israel to ask me to do this for them: I will multiply their people like sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Kings 8:63; Acts 2:5–11; John 10:16"> Like the sheep for offerings, like the sheep of Jerusalem during her appointed feasts, so will the ruined cities be filled with flocks of people. Then they will know that I am the Lord.”</span>
</div>
</div>
