<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:49; Matthew 13:35; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> “Son of man, offer a riddle, and tell a parable to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Jeremiah 48:40; Ezekiel 17:7"> Say to them: ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘A great eagle with broad wings, long feathers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with full plumage which was multi-hued,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">came to Lebanon and took the top of the cedar.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 43:14; Isaiah 47:15; Revelation 18:11–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">He plucked off its topmost shoot;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he brought it to a land of merchants</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and planted it in a city of traders.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 44:4; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Jeremiah 37:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">He took one of the seedlings of the land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">placed it in a cultivated plot;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a shoot by abundant water,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a willow he planted it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:14; Proverbs 16:18–19"> <span className="pl pl-1">It sprouted and became a vine,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">spreading low to the ground;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">its branches turning toward him, its roots were under itself.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So it became a vine; it produced shoots and sent out branches.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:4; Ezekiel 17:15; 2 Kings 24:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">“‘There was another great eagle</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with broad wings and thick plumage.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now this vine twisted its roots toward him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and sent its branches toward him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to be watered from the soil where it was planted.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">In a good field, by abundant waters, it was planted</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to grow branches, bear fruit, and become a beautiful vine.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:10; Jeremiah 32:5; Isaiah 30:1–7"> “‘Say to them: This is what the sovereign Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘Will it prosper?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will he not rip out its roots</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and cause its fruit to rot and wither?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All its foliage will wither.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No strong arm or large army</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be needed to pull it out by its roots.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 13:15; John 15:6; Matthew 21:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Consider! It is planted, but will it prosper?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will it not wither completely when the east wind blows on it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will it not wither in the soil where it sprouted?’”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:19; Ezekiel 17:3; Ezekiel 1:2"> “Say to the rebellious house of Israel: ‘Don’t you know what these things mean?’ Say: ‘See here, the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and took her king and her officials prisoner and brought them to himself in Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; 2 Kings 24:15–17; Ezekiel 17:5"> He took one from the royal family, made a treaty with him, and put him under oath. He then took the leaders of the land</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:14; Deuteronomy 28:43; 1 Samuel 2:30"> so it would be a lowly kingdom which could not rise on its own but must keep its treaty with him in order to stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 52:3; Jeremiah 38:18; Ezekiel 17:7"> But this one from Israel’s royal family rebelled against the king of Babylon by sending his emissaries to Egypt to obtain horses and a large army. Will he prosper? Will the one doing these things escape? Can he break the covenant and escape?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 52:11; Ezekiel 17:18–19"> “‘As surely as I live, declares the sovereign Lord, surely in the city of the king who crowned him, whose oath he despised and whose covenant he broke – in the middle of Babylon he will die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 37:7; Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> Pharaoh with his great army and mighty horde will not help him in battle, when siege ramps are erected and siege-walls are built to kill many people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:24; 2 Chronicles 30:8; Lamentations 5:6"> He despised the oath by breaking the covenant. Take note – he gave his promise and did all these things – he will not escape!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:11; Jeremiah 5:9; Jeremiah 5:2"> “‘Therefore this is what the sovereign Lord says: As surely as I live, I will certainly repay him for despising my oath and breaking my covenant!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Ezekiel 32:3; Jeremiah 2:35"> I will throw my net over him and he will be caught in my snare; I will bring him to Babylon and judge him there because of the unfaithfulness he committed against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 25:5; 2 Kings 25:11; Ezekiel 12:14"> All the choice men among his troops will die by the sword and the survivors will be scattered to every wind. Then you will know that I, the Lord, have spoken!</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2; Zechariah 6:12–13; Isaiah 11:1–5"> “‘This is what the sovereign Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘I will take a sprig from the lofty top of the cedar and plant it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will pluck from the top one of its tender twigs;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I myself will plant it on a high and lofty mountain.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:32; Ezekiel 31:6; Isaiah 2:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will plant it on a high mountain of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and it will raise branches and produce fruit and become a beautiful cedar.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Every bird will live under it;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Every winged creature will live in the shade of its branches.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Amos 9:11; 1 Corinthians 1:27–28; Ezekiel 24:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the trees of the field will know that I am the Lord.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I make the high tree low; I raise up the low tree.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I make the green tree wither, and I make the dry tree sprout.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I, the Lord, have spoken, and I will do it!’”</span></span>
