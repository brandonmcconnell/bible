<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:20; Jeremiah 52:5–6; Ezekiel 1:2"> In the eleventh year, in the third month, on the first day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 31:18"> “Son of man, say to Pharaoh king of Egypt and his hordes:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘Who are you like in your greatness?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 10:33–34; Daniel 4:10; Daniel 4:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">Consider Assyria, a cedar in Lebanon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with beautiful branches, like a forest giving shade,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and extremely tall;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">its top reached into the clouds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Revelation 17:15; Proverbs 14:28; Ezekiel 17:5"> <span className="pl pl-1">The water made it grow;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">underground springs made it grow tall.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Rivers flowed all around the place it was planted,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">while smaller channels watered all the trees of the field.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 4:11; Ezekiel 17:5; Psalms 1:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Therefore it grew taller than all the trees of the field;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">its boughs grew large and its branches grew long,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because of the plentiful water in its shoots.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:23; Matthew 13:32; Daniel 4:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the birds of the sky nested in its boughs;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">under its branches all the beasts of the field gave birth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in its shade all the great nations lived.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> <span className="pl pl-1">It was beautiful in its loftiness, in the length of its branches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for its roots went down deep to plentiful waters.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 13:10; Isaiah 51:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">The cedars in the garden of God could not eclipse it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor could the fir trees match its boughs;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the plane trees were as nothing compared to its branches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">no tree in the garden of God could rival its beauty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Daniel 2:37–38; Genesis 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">I made it beautiful with its many branches;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all the trees of Eden, in the garden of God, envied it.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:17; Daniel 5:20; 2 Chronicles 32:25"> “‘Therefore this is what the sovereign Lord says: Because it was tall in stature, and its top reached into the clouds, and it was proud of its height,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:11–12; Nahum 3:18; Daniel 5:18–19"> I gave it over to the leader of the nations. He has judged it thoroughly, as its sinfulness deserves. I have thrown it out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 35:8; Ezekiel 30:11"> Foreigners from the most terrifying nations have cut it down and left it to lie there on the mountains. In all the valleys its branches have fallen, and its boughs lie broken in the ravines of the land. All the peoples of the land have departed from its shade and left it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:4; Ezekiel 29:5; Isaiah 18:6"> On its ruins all the birds of the sky will live, and all the wild animals will walk on its branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 63:9–10; Nehemiah 13:18; 2 Peter 2:6"> For this reason no watered trees will grow so tall; their tops will not reach into the clouds, nor will the well-watered ones grow that high. For all of them have been appointed to die in the lower parts of the earth; they will be among mere mortals, with those who descend to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Malachi 3:4; Nahum 2:8–10; Revelation 18:9–11"> “‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: On the day it went down to Sheol I caused observers to lament. I covered it with the deep and held back its rivers; its plentiful water was restrained. I clothed Lebanon in black for it, and all the trees of the field wilted because of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 14:15; Isaiah 14:8; Ezekiel 26:15"> I made the nations shake at the sound of its fall, when I threw it down to Sheol, along with those who descend to the pit. Then all the trees of Eden, the choicest and the best of Lebanon, all that were well-watered, were comforted in the earth below.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 9:17; Ezekiel 31:6; Ezekiel 31:3"> Those who lived in its shade, its allies among the nations, also went down with it to Sheol, to those killed by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:19; Ezekiel 32:21; Ezekiel 28:10"> Which of the trees of Eden was like you in majesty and loftiness? You will be brought down with the trees of Eden to the lower parts of the earth; you will lie among the uncircumcised, with those killed by the sword! This is what will happen to Pharaoh and all his hordes, declares the sovereign Lord.’”</span>
