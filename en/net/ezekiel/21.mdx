<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Ezekiel 4:7; Ezekiel 25:2"> “Son of man, turn toward Jerusalem and speak out against the sanctuaries. Prophesy against the land of Israel</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Job 9:22; Jeremiah 21:13; Ezekiel 5:8"> and say to them, ‘This is what the Lord says: Look, I am against you. I will draw my sword from its sheath and cut off from you both the righteous and the wicked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 7:2; Ezekiel 6:11–14"> Because I will cut off from you both the righteous and the wicked, my sword will go out from its sheath against everyone from the south to the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:30; Nahum 1:9; 1 Samuel 3:12"> Then everyone will know that I am the Lord, who drew my sword from its sheath – it will not be sheathed again!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 22:4; Ezekiel 6:11; Jeremiah 19:10"> “And you, son of man, groan with an aching heart and bitterness; groan before their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:17; Ezekiel 7:26; Isaiah 13:7"> When they ask you, ‘Why are you groaning?’ you will reply, ‘Because of the report that has come. Every heart will melt with fear and every hand will be limp; everyone will faint and every knee will be wet with urine.’ Pay attention – it is coming and it will happen, declares the sovereign Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 34:5–6; Ezekiel 21:28; Ezekiel 21:3"> “Son of man, prophesy and say: ‘This is what the Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘A sword, a sword is sharpened,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and also polished.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 110:5–6; Ezekiel 20:47; Ezekiel 21:25–27"> <span className="pl pl-1">It is sharpened for slaughter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it is polished to flash like lightning!</span>“‘Should we rejoice in the scepter of my son? No! The sword despises every tree!</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:19; Jeremiah 25:33; Jeremiah 51:20–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">“‘He gave it to be polished,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to be grasped in the hand –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the sword is sharpened, it is polished –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">giving it into the hand of the executioner.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:6; Joel 1:13; Ezekiel 21:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cry out and moan, son of man,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for it is wielded against my people;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">against all the princes of Israel.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They are delivered up to the sword, along with my people.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore, strike your thigh.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; 2 Corinthians 8:2; Ezekiel 21:25"> “‘For testing will come, and what will happen when the scepter, which the sword despises, is no more? declares the sovereign Lord.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 24:1; Numbers 24:10; Leviticus 26:24"> <span className="pl pl-1">“And you, son of man, prophesy,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and clap your hands together.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let the sword strike twice, even three times!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is a sword for slaughter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a sword for the great slaughter surrounding them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:10; Jeremiah 17:27; Ezekiel 21:22"> <span className="pl pl-1">So hearts melt with fear and many stumble.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">At all their gates I have stationed the sword for slaughter.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Ah! It is made to flash, it is drawn for slaughter!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:4; Ezekiel 14:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Cut sharply on the right!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Swing to the left,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">wherever your edge is appointed to strike.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Ezekiel 21:14; Zechariah 6:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I too will clap my hands together,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will exhaust my rage;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I the Lord have spoken.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:1–3; Jeremiah 1:10; Ezekiel 5:1–17"> “You, son of man, mark out two routes for the king of Babylon’s sword to take; both of them will originate in a single land. Make a signpost and put it at the beginning of the road leading to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Amos 1:14; Jeremiah 49:2; Ezekiel 25:5"> Mark out the routes for the sword to take: “Rabbah of the Ammonites” and “Judah with Jerusalem in it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 18:20; Proverbs 16:33; Judges 17:5"> For the king of Babylon stands at the fork in the road at the head of the two routes. He looks for omens: He shakes arrows, he consults idols, he examines animal livers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:2; Ezekiel 26:9; Jeremiah 51:14"> Into his right hand comes the portent for Jerusalem – to set up battering rams, to give the signal for slaughter, to shout out the battle cry, to set up battering rams against the gates, to erect a siege ramp, to build a siege wall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 5:15; Ezekiel 21:24; Ezekiel 29:16"> But those in Jerusalem will view it as a false omen. They have sworn solemn oaths, but the king of Babylon will accuse them of violations in order to seize them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hosea 4:2; Micah 3:10–12; Jeremiah 3:2"> “Therefore this is what the sovereign Lord says: ‘Because you have brought up your own guilt by uncovering your transgressions and revealing your sins through all your actions, for this reason you will be taken by force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 35:5; Ezekiel 21:29; Ezekiel 17:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">“‘As for you, profane and wicked prince of Israel,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whose day has come, the time of final punishment,</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; Psalms 75:7; Jeremiah 13:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">this is what the sovereign Lord says:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Tear off the turban,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">take off the crown!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Things must change!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Exalt the lowly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">bring down the proud!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Jeremiah 23:5–6; Zechariah 9:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">A total ruin I will make it!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will come to an end</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when the one arrives to whom I have assigned judgment.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:20; Ezekiel 21:9–10; Zephaniah 2:8–10"> “As for you, son of man, prophesy and say, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says concerning the Ammonites and their coming humiliation; say:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘A sword, a sword drawn for slaughter,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">polished to consume, to flash like lightning –</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:25; Ezekiel 22:28; Psalms 37:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">while seeing false visions for you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and reading lying omens for you –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to place that sword on the necks of the profane wicked,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">whose day has come,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the time of final punishment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:38; Genesis 15:14; Jeremiah 47:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Return it to its sheath!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">In the place where you were created,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in your native land, I will judge you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nahum 1:6; Jeremiah 6:22–23; Ezekiel 14:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will pour out my anger on you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the fire of my fury I will blow on you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will hand you over to brutal men,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who are skilled in destruction.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 25:10; Malachi 4:1; Ezekiel 20:47–48"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will become fuel for the fire –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your blood will stain the middle of the land;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will no longer be remembered,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for I, the Lord, have spoken.’”</span></span>
