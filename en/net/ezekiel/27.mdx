<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 19:1; Jeremiah 9:10; Ezekiel 28:12"> “You, son of man, sing a lament for Tyre.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:4; Isaiah 23:11; Isaiah 23:2–3"> Say to Tyre, who sits at the entrance of the sea, merchant to the peoples on many coasts, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘O Tyre, you have said, “I am perfectly beautiful.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:5"> <br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your borders are in the heart of the seas;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your builders have perfected your beauty.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:9; Isaiah 14:8; Song of Solomon 4:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">They crafted all your planks out of fir trees from Senir;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they took a cedar from Lebanon to make your mast.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 2:13; Zechariah 11:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">They made your oars from oaks of Bashan;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they made your deck with cypresses from the Kittean isles.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:9; Exodus 25:4; Genesis 10:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">Fine linen from Egypt, woven with patterns, was used for your sail</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to serve as your banner;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">blue and purple from the coastlands of Elishah was used for your deck’s awning.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 10:18; 1 Kings 9:27; Ezekiel 27:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The leaders of Sidon and Arvad were your rowers;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your skilled men, O Tyre, were your captains.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 5:18; Joshua 13:5; Psalms 83:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">The elders of Gebal and her skilled men were within you, mending cracks;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all the ships of the sea and their mariners were within you to trade for your merchandise.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:5; Ezekiel 30:5; Isaiah 66:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">Men of Persia, Lud, and Put were in your army, men of war.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They hung shield and helmet on you; they gave you your splendor.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:3–4"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Arvadites joined your army on your walls all around,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the Gammadites were in your towers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They hung their quivers on your walls all around;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they perfected your beauty.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 23:10; Ezekiel 38:13"> “‘Tarshish was your trade partner because of your abundant wealth; they exchanged silver, iron, tin, and lead for your products.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:2; Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 39:1"> Javan, Tubal, and Meshech were your clients; they exchanged slaves and bronze items for your merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 10:3; Ezekiel 38:6; 1 Chronicles 1:6"> Beth Togarmah exchanged horses, chargers, and mules for your products.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Revelation 18:12; 1 Kings 10:22"> The Dedanites were your clients. Many coastlands were your customers; they paid you with ivory tusks and ebony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 10:6; Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Samuel 10:6"> Edom was your trade partner because of the abundance of your goods; they exchanged turquoise, purple, embroidered work, fine linen, coral, and rubies for your products.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 11:33; 1 Kings 5:9; Jeremiah 8:22"> Judah and the land of Israel were your clients; they traded wheat from Minnith, millet, honey, olive oil, and balm for your merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 7:8; Genesis 15:2; Ezekiel 47:16–18"> Damascus was your trade partner because of the abundance of your goods and of all your wealth: wine from Helbon, white wool from Zahar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 30:23–24; Psalms 45:8; Song of Solomon 4:13–14"> and casks of wine from Izal they exchanged for your products. Wrought iron, cassia, and sweet cane were among your merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 25:3; Ezekiel 27:15"> Dedan was your client in saddlecloths for riding.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 60:7; Genesis 25:13; 1 Kings 10:15"> Arabia and all the princes of Kedar were your trade partners; for lambs, rams, and goats they traded with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 10:7; Isaiah 60:6; Psalms 72:10"> The merchants of Sheba and Raamah engaged in trade with you; they traded the best kinds of spices along with precious stones and gold for your products.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:12; Amos 1:5"> Haran, Kanneh, Eden, merchants from Sheba, Asshur, and Kilmad were your clients.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 2:8"> They traded with you choice garments, purple clothes and embroidered work, and multicolored carpets, bound and reinforced with cords; these were among your merchandise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 2:16; Psalms 48:7; Isaiah 23:14"> The ships of Tarshish were the transports for your merchandise.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘So you were filled and weighed down in the heart of the seas.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 48:7; Ezekiel 26:19; Acts 27:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your rowers have brought you into surging waters.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The east wind has wrecked you in the heart of the seas.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 11:4; Ezekiel 27:24; Ezekiel 27:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your wealth, products, and merchandise, your sailors and captains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your ship’s carpenters, your merchants,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all your fighting men within you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">along with all your crew who are in you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will fall into the heart of the seas on the day of your downfall.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:10; Exodus 15:14; Ezekiel 31:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">At the sound of your captains’ cry the waves will surge;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:16; Ezekiel 32:10; Revelation 18:17–24"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will descend from their ships – all who handle the oar,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the sailors and all the sea captains – they will stand on the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:26; 2 Samuel 1:2; Lamentations 2:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">They will lament loudly over you and cry bitterly.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will throw dust on their heads and roll in the ashes;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:18; Isaiah 22:12; Isaiah 15:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">they will tear out their hair because of you and put on sackcloth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will weep bitterly over you with intense mourning.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:17; Revelation 18:18; Ezekiel 27:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">As they wail they will lament over you, chanting:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Who was like Tyre, like a tower in the midst of the sea?”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Revelation 18:19; Ezekiel 27:3; Ezekiel 28:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">When your products went out from the seas,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you satisfied many peoples;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with the abundance of your wealth and merchandise</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you enriched the kings of the earth.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:26–27; Zechariah 9:3–4; Ezekiel 26:19–21"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now you are wrecked by the seas, in the depths of the waters;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">your merchandise and all your company have sunk along with you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:10; Isaiah 23:6; Ezekiel 26:15–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the inhabitants of the coastlands are shocked at you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their kings are horribly afraid – their faces are troubled.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:16; Psalms 37:10; Zephaniah 2:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The traders among the peoples hiss at you;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you have become a horror, and will be no more.’”</span></span>
