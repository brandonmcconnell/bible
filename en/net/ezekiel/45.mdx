<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:29; Zechariah 14:20–21; Numbers 34:13"> “‘When you allot the land as an inheritance, you will offer an allotment to the Lord, a holy portion from the land; the length will be eight and a quarter miles and the width three and one-third miles. This entire area will be holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 42:16–20; Ezekiel 27:28"> Of this area a square 875 feet by 875 feet will be designated for the sanctuary, with 87½ feet set aside for its open space round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:10"> From this measured area you will measure a length of eight and a quarter miles and a width of three and one-third miles; in it will be the sanctuary, the most holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:19; Numbers 16:5; Ezekiel 40:45"> It will be a holy portion of the land; it will be for the priests, the ministers of the sanctuary who approach the Lord to minister to him. It will be a place for their houses and a holy place for the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:13; Nehemiah 10:38–39; Ezekiel 48:20"> An area eight and a quarter miles in length and three and one-third miles in width will be for the Levites, who minister at the temple, as the place for the cities in which they will live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:15–18; Ezekiel 48:30–35"> “‘Alongside the portion set apart as the holy allotment, you will allot for the city an area one and two-thirds miles wide and eight and a quarter miles long; it will be for the whole house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:21; Ezekiel 34:24; Ezekiel 46:16–18"> “‘For the prince there will be land on both sides of the holy allotment and the allotted city, alongside the holy allotment and the allotted city, on the west side and on the east side; it will be comparable in length to one of the portions, from the west border to the east border</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–5; Ezekiel 46:18; Ezekiel 22:27"> of the land. This will be his property in Israel. My princes will no longer oppress my people, but the land will be allotted to the house of Israel according to their tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:16; Jeremiah 22:3; Ezekiel 44:6"> “‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: Enough, you princes of Israel! Put away violence and destruction, and do what is just and right. Put an end to your evictions of my people, declares the sovereign Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Micah 6:10–11; Amos 8:4–6; Proverbs 16:11"> You must use just balances, a just dry measure (an ephah), and a just liquid measure (a bath).</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 5:10"> The dry and liquid measures will be the same, the bath will contain a tenth of a homer, and the ephah a tenth of a homer; the homer will be the standard measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Numbers 3:47; Leviticus 27:25"> The shekel will be twenty gerahs. Sixty shekels will be a mina for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> “‘This is the offering you must offer: a sixth of an ephah from a homer of wheat; a sixth of an ephah from a homer of barley,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and as the prescribed portion of olive oil, one tenth of a bath from each kor (which is ten baths or a homer, for ten baths make a homer);</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 1:4; Leviticus 6:30; Colossians 1:21"> and one sheep from each flock of two hundred, from the watered places of Israel, for a grain offering, burnt offering, and peace offering, to make atonement for them, declares the sovereign Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 30:14–15; Isaiah 16:1"> All the people of the land will contribute to this offering for the prince of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–12; 2 Chronicles 31:3; Isaiah 66:23"> It will be the duty of the prince to provide the burnt offerings, the grain offering, and the drink offering at festivals, on the new moons and Sabbaths, at all the appointed feasts of the house of Israel; he will provide the sin offering, the grain offering, the burnt offering, and the peace offerings to make atonement for the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 16:16; Exodus 12:2; Ezekiel 43:22"> “‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: In the first month, on the first day of the month, you must take an unblemished young bull and purify the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:20; Ezekiel 43:14; Leviticus 16:18–20"> The priest will take some of the blood of the sin offering and place it on the doorpost of the temple, on the four corners of the ledge of the altar, and on the doorpost of the gate of the inner court.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 19:12; Leviticus 4:27–35; Ezekiel 45:15"> This is what you must do on the seventh day of the month for anyone who sins inadvertently or through ignorance; so you will make atonement for the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 23:5–8; 1 Corinthians 5:7–8; Numbers 9:2–14"> “‘In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, you will celebrate the Passover, and for seven days bread made without yeast will be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 4:14; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Matthew 20:28"> On that day the prince will provide for himself and for all the people of the land a bull for a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Job 42:8; Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 23:1–2"> And during the seven days of the feast he will provide as a burnt offering to the Lord seven bulls and seven rams, all without blemish, on each of the seven days, and a male goat daily for a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 28:12–15"> He will provide as a grain offering an ephah for each bull, an ephah for each ram, and a gallon of olive oil for each ephah of grain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:33–43; Numbers 29:12–38; 2 Chronicles 5:3"> In the seventh month, on the fifteenth day of the month, at the feast, he will make the same provisions for the sin offering, burnt offering, and grain offering, and for the olive oil, for the seven days.</span>
