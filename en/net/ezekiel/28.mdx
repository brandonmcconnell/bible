## A Prophecy Against Sidon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> The word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Genesis 10:15; Jeremiah 25:22"> “Son of man, turn toward Sidon and prophesy against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 39:13; Exodus 14:17; Ezekiel 20:41"> Say, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘Look, I am against you, Sidon,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will magnify myself in your midst.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then they will know that I am the Lord</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I execute judgments on her</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and reveal my sovereign power in her.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:22; Ezekiel 26:6; Ezekiel 25:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will send a plague into the city and bloodshed into its streets;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the slain will fall within it, by the sword that attacks it from every side.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then they will know that I am the Lord.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Joshua 23:13; 2 Corinthians 12:7"> “‘No longer will Israel suffer from the sharp briers or painful thorns of all who surround and scorn them. Then they will know that I am the sovereign Lord.</span>
