<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:14; Ezekiel 11:24; Ezekiel 11:13"> A wind lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the Lord’s temple that faces the east. There, at the entrance of the gate, I noticed twenty-five men. Among them I saw Jaazaniah son of Azzur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, officials of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 30:1; Jeremiah 5:5; Psalms 2:1–2"> The Lord said to me, “Son of man, these are the men who plot evil and give wicked advice in this city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:27; Ezekiel 12:22; Ezekiel 11:7–11"> They say, ‘The time is not near to build houses; the city is a cooking pot and we are the meat in it.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46–47; Ezekiel 21:2; Ezekiel 3:2–15"> Therefore, prophesy against them! Prophesy, son of man!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:10; Jeremiah 16:17; Revelation 2:23"> Then the Spirit of the Lord came upon me and said to me, “Say: This is what the Lord says: ‘This is what you are thinking, O house of Israel; I know what goes through your minds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 7:23; Ezekiel 22:27; Ezekiel 22:12"> You have killed many people in this city; you have filled its streets with corpses.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:3–13; Micah 3:2–3; 2 Kings 25:18–22"> Therefore, this is what the sovereign Lord says: ‘The corpses you have dumped in the midst of the city are the meat, and this city is the cooking pot, but I will take you out of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 10:24; Isaiah 24:17–18; Job 3:25"> You fear the sword, so the sword I will bring against you,’ declares the sovereign Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:8; Psalms 106:41; Deuteronomy 28:36"> ‘But I will take you out of the city. And I will hand you over to foreigners. I will execute judgments on you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Kings 14:25; Jeremiah 52:9–10; 2 Kings 25:19–21"> You will die by the sword; I will judge you at the border of Israel. Then you will know that I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:3; Ezekiel 11:7–10"> This city will not be a cooking pot for you, and you will not be meat within it; I will judge you at the border of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:10; Ezekiel 8:16; Ezekiel 8:14"> Then you will know that I am the Lord, whose statutes you have not followed and whose regulations you have not carried out. Instead you have behaved according to the regulations of the nations around you!’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 9:8; Acts 5:5"> Now, while I was prophesying, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. Then I threw myself face down and cried out with a loud voice, “Alas, sovereign Lord! You are completely wiping out the remnant of Israel!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then the word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:24; Isaiah 65:5; John 16:2"> “Son of man, your brothers, your relatives, and the whole house of Israel, all of them are those to whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, ‘They have gone far away from the Lord; to us this land has been given as a possession.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; Deuteronomy 30:3–4; Psalms 90:1"> “Therefore say: ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: Although I have removed them far away among the nations and have dispersed them among the countries, I have been a little sanctuary for them among the lands where they have gone.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:25; Jeremiah 3:18; Ezekiel 34:13"> “Therefore say: ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says: When I regather you from the peoples and assemble you from the lands where you have been dispersed, I will give you back the country of Israel.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:23; Ezekiel 5:11; Ezekiel 42:7–8"> “When they return to it, they will remove from it all its detestable things and all its abominations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:26–27; Jeremiah 31:33; Psalms 51:10"> I will give them one heart and I will put a new spirit within them; I will remove the hearts of stone from their bodies and I will give them tender hearts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:11; Psalms 105:45; Jeremiah 30:22"> so that they may follow my statutes and observe my regulations and carry them out. Then they will be my people, and I will be their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 9:10; Ecclesiastes 11:9; Jeremiah 1:16"> But those whose hearts are devoted to detestable things and abominations, I hereby repay them for what they have done, says the sovereign Lord.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 10:19; Ezekiel 1:19–20"> Then the cherubim spread their wings with their wheels alongside them while the glory of the God of Israel hovered above them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 14:4; Ezekiel 8:4; Ezekiel 10:4"> The glory of the Lord rose up from within the city and stopped over the mountain east of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:12; Ezekiel 8:3; Ezekiel 11:1"> Then a wind lifted me up and carried me to the exiles in Babylonia, in the vision given to me by the Spirit of God.<br />Then the vision I had seen went up from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 3:4; Ezekiel 3:27; Ezekiel 3:17"> So I told the exiles everything the Lord had shown me.</span>
</div>
