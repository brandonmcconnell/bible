<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> In the tenth year, in the tenth month, on the twelfth day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 46:2–16"> “Son of man, turn toward Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him and against all Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 27:1; Jeremiah 44:30"> Tell them, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘Look, I am against you, Pharaoh king of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the great monster lying in the midst of its waterways,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">who has said, “My Nile is my own, I made it for myself.”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Ezekiel 38:4; Amos 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will put hooks in your jaws</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and stick the fish of your waterways to your scales.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will haul you up from the midst of your waterways,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all the fish of your waterways will stick to your scales.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20; Ezekiel 32:4–6; Jeremiah 8:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will leave you in the wilderness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you and all the fish of your waterways;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will fall in the open field and will not be gathered up or collected.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I have given you as food to the beasts of the earth and the birds of the skies.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6; Exodus 9:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then all those living in Egypt will know that I am the Lord</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because they were a reed staff for the house of Israel;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 17:15–17; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">when they grasped you with their hand, you broke and tore their shoulders,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and when they leaned on you, you splintered and caused their legs to be unsteady.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> “‘Therefore, this is what the sovereign Lord says: Look, I am about to bring a sword against you, and I will kill every person and every animal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Proverbs 16:18; Ezekiel 29:10–12"> The land of Egypt will become a desolate ruin. Then they will know that I am the Lord. Because he said, “The Nile is mine and I made it,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 30:6–9; Exodus 14:2"> I am against you and your waterways. I will turn the land of Egypt into an utter desolate ruin from Migdol to Syene, as far as the border with Ethiopia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:13; Jeremiah 43:11–12; Ezekiel 30:10–13"> No human foot will pass through it, and no animal’s foot will pass through it; it will be uninhabited for forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:19; Ezekiel 30:7; Ezekiel 30:23"> I will turn the land of Egypt into a desolation in the midst of desolate lands; for forty years her cities will lie desolate in the midst of ruined cities. I will scatter Egypt among the nations and disperse them among foreign countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:26; Isaiah 19:22"> “‘For this is what the sovereign Lord says: At the end of forty years I will gather Egypt from the peoples where they were scattered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 11:11; Jeremiah 44:1"> I will restore the fortunes of Egypt, and will bring them back to the land of Pathros, to the land of their origin; there they will be an insignificant kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Ezekiel 30:13; Ezekiel 17:14"> It will be the most insignificant of the kingdoms; it will never again exalt itself over the nations. I will make them so small that they will not rule over the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Isaiah 20:5; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> It will never again be Israel’s source of confidence, but a reminder of how they sinned by turning to Egypt for help. Then they will know that I am the sovereign Lord.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Ezekiel 24:1"> In the twenty-seventh year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:6; Jeremiah 25:9; Ezekiel 26:7–12"> “Son of man, King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon made his army labor hard against Tyre. Every head was rubbed bald and every shoulder rubbed bare; yet he and his army received no wages from Tyre for the work he carried out against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:24–25; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Ezekiel 32:11"> Therefore this is what the sovereign Lord says: Look, I am about to give the land of Egypt to King Nebuchadrezzar of Babylon. He will carry off her wealth, capture her loot, and seize her plunder; it will be his army’s wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Isaiah 10:6–7; Isaiah 45:1–3"> I have given him the land of Egypt as his compensation for attacking Tyre , because they did it for me, declares the sovereign Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; Luke 21:15; Psalms 132:17"> On that day I will make Israel powerful, and I will give you the right to be heard among them. Then they will know that I am the Lord.”</span>
