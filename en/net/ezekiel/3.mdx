## Ezekiel Before the Exiles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 8:3; Acts 8:39; Ezekiel 11:1"> Then a wind lifted me up and I heard a great rumbling sound behind me as the glory of the Lord rose from its place,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:24; Ezekiel 10:16–17; Ezekiel 10:5"> and the sound of the living beings’ wings brushing against each other, and the sound of the wheels alongside them, a great rumbling sound.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:1; Jeremiah 20:7–9; 2 Kings 3:15"> A wind lifted me up and carried me away. I went bitterly, my spirit full of fury, and the hand of the Lord rested powerfully on me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 137:1; Job 2:13; Genesis 50:10"> I came to the exiles at Tel Abib, who lived by the Kebar River. I sat dumbfounded among them there, where they were living, for seven days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:7"> At the end of seven days the word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 52:8; Hebrews 13:17; 2 Chronicles 19:10"> “Son of man, I have appointed you a watchman for the house of Israel. Whenever you hear a word from my mouth, you must give them a warning from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:6; James 5:19–20; Ezekiel 3:20"> When I say to the wicked, “You will certainly die,” and you do not warn him – you do not speak out to warn the wicked to turn from his wicked deed and wicked lifestyle so that he may live – that wicked person will die for his iniquity, but I will hold you accountable for his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:9; Ezekiel 14:20; 1 Timothy 4:16"> But as for you, if you warn the wicked and he does not turn from his wicked deed and from his wicked lifestyle, he will die for his iniquity but you will have saved your own life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:24; Ezekiel 18:26; Ezekiel 33:12–13"> “When a righteous person turns from his righteousness and commits iniquity, and I set an obstacle before him, he will die. If you have not warned him, he will die for his sin. The righteous deeds he performed will not be considered, but I will hold you accountable for his death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; Proverbs 9:9; Titus 2:15"> However, if you warn the righteous person not to sin, and he does not sin, he will certainly live because he was warned, and you will have saved your own life.”</span>
</div>

## Isolated and Silenced

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 9:6; Ezekiel 37:1; Ezekiel 8:4"> The hand of the Lord rested on me there, and he said to me, “Get up, go out to the valley, and I will speak with you there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:28; Acts 7:55; Revelation 1:17"> So I got up and went out to the valley, and the glory of the Lord was standing there, just like the glory I had seen by the Kebar River, and I threw myself face down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 2:2; Daniel 10:19; Daniel 10:8–10"> Then a wind came into me and stood me on my feet. The Lord spoke to me and said, “Go shut yourself in your house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:8; Acts 9:16; Mark 3:21"> As for you, son of man, they will put ropes on you and tie you up with them, so you cannot go out among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:27; Micah 3:6–7; Amos 8:11–12"> I will make your tongue stick to the roof of your mouth so that you will be silent and unable to reprove them, for they are a rebellious house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:2–3; Ezekiel 24:27; Ezekiel 3:11"> But when I speak with you, I will loosen your tongue and you must say to them, ‘This is what the sovereign Lord says.’ Those who listen will listen, but the indifferent will refuse, for they are a rebellious house.</span>
</div>
