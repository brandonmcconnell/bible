</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:13; Jeremiah 16:16; Ecclesiastes 9:12"> “‘This is what the sovereign Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘I will throw my net over you in the assembly of many peoples;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will haul you up in my dragnet.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 18:6; Ezekiel 29:5; Ezekiel 31:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will leave you on the ground,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will fling you on the open field,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will allow all the birds of the sky to settle on you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will permit all the wild animals to gorge themselves on you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will put your flesh on the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and fill the valleys with your maggot-infested carcass.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 34:3; Isaiah 34:7; Revelation 14:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will drench the land with the flow</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">of your blood up to the mountains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the ravines will be full of your blood.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Joel 2:31; Isaiah 13:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I extinguish you, I will cover the sky;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will darken its stars.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will cover the sun with a cloud,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the moon will not shine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:14; Proverbs 20:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will darken all the lights in the sky over you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I will darken your land,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">declares the sovereign Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 18:10–15; Ezekiel 30:26; Jeremiah 25:15–25"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will disturb many peoples,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I bring about your destruction among the nations,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">among countries you do not know.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:16; Ezekiel 27:35; 1 Kings 9:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will shock many peoples with you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and their kings will shiver with horror because of you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When I brandish my sword before them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">every moment each one will tremble for his life, on the day of your fall.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:10; Ezekiel 26:7; Ezekiel 30:22–25"> “‘For this is what the sovereign Lord says:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“‘The sword of the king of Babylon will attack you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:7; Ezekiel 29:19; Ezekiel 31:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">By the swords of the mighty warriors I will cause your hordes to fall –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all of them are the most terrifying among the nations.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will devastate the pride of Egypt,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and all its hordes will be destroyed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:11; Ezekiel 29:8; Ezekiel 30:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will destroy all its cattle beside the plentiful waters;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and no human foot will disturb the waters again,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">nor will the hooves of cattle disturb them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then I will make their waters calm,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will make their streams flow like olive oil, declares the sovereign Lord.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:7; Exodus 7:5; Exodus 14:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">When I turn the land of Egypt into desolation</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the land is destitute of everything that fills it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">when I strike all those who live in it,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">then they will know that I am the Lord.’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:17; 2 Chronicles 35:25; Ezekiel 26:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">This is a lament; they will chant it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The daughters of the nations will chant it.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will chant it over Egypt and over all her hordes,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">declares the sovereign Lord.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:1; Ezekiel 33:21; Ezekiel 1:2"> In the twelfth year, on the fifteenth day of the month, the word of the Lord came to me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:14; Ezekiel 32:2; Jeremiah 1:10"> “Son of man, wail over the horde of Egypt. Bring it down; bring her and the daughters of powerful nations down to the lower parts of the earth, along with those who descend to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:18; Ezekiel 32:29–30; Jeremiah 9:25–26"> Say to them, ‘Whom do you surpass in beauty? Go down and be laid to rest with the uncircumcised!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 28:3; Ezekiel 32:23–26; Ezekiel 32:29–30"> They will fall among those killed by the sword. The sword is drawn; they carry her and all her hordes away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 14:9–10; Ezekiel 32:27; Ezekiel 32:19"> The bravest of the warriors will speak to him from the midst of Sheol along with his allies, saying: ‘The uncircumcised have come down; they lie still, killed by the sword.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:26; Ezekiel 32:29–30; Ezekiel 32:24"> “Assyria is there with all her assembly around her grave, all of them struck down by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:20; Ezekiel 32:24–27; Psalms 27:13"> Their graves are located in the remote slopes of the pit. Her assembly is around her grave, all of them struck down by the sword, those who spread terror in the land of the living.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:18; Ezekiel 26:20; Jeremiah 49:34–39"> “Elam is there with all her hordes around her grave; all of them struck down by the sword. They went down uncircumcised to the lower parts of the earth, those who spread terror in the land of the living. Now they will bear their shame with those who descend to the pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 139:8; 1 Chronicles 10:4; 2 Samuel 1:20"> Among the dead they have made a bed for her, along with all her hordes around her grave. All of them are uncircumcised, killed by the sword, for their terror had spread in the land of the living. They bear their shame along with those who descend to the pit; they are placed among the dead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:13; Genesis 10:2; Ezekiel 38:2–3"> “Meshech-Tubal is there, along with all her hordes around her grave. All of them are uncircumcised, killed by the sword, for they spread their terror in the land of the living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 14:18–19; Job 3:13–15; Ezekiel 32:21"> They do not lie with the fallen warriors of ancient times, who went down to Sheol with their weapons of war, having their swords placed under their heads and their shields on their bones, when the terror of these warriors was in the land of the living.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 2:34–35"> “But as for you, in the midst of the uncircumcised you will be broken, and you will lie with those killed by the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:7–22; Malachi 1:3–4; Obadiah 1:1–9"> “Edom is there with her kings and all her princes. Despite their might they are laid with those killed by the sword; they lie with the uncircumcised and those who descend to the pit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:6; Ezekiel 39:2; Ezekiel 28:21"> “All the leaders of the north are there, along with all the Sidonians; despite their might they have gone down in shameful terror with the dead. They lie uncircumcised with those killed by the sword, and bear their shame with those who descend to the pit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ezekiel 31:16; Ezekiel 14:22; Lamentations 2:13"> “Pharaoh will see them and be consoled over all his hordes who were killed by the sword, Pharaoh and all his army, declares the sovereign Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:27; Genesis 35:5; 2 Corinthians 5:11"> Indeed, I terrified him in the land of the living, yet he will lie in the midst of the uncircumcised with those killed by the sword, Pharaoh and all his hordes, declares the sovereign Lord.”</span>
</div>
