<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 7:37–38; John 6:63; John 4:14"> Every living creature which swarms where the river flows will live; there will be many fish, for these waters flow there. It will become fresh and everything will live where the river flows.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 4:19; Psalms 104:25; Numbers 34:6"> Fishermen will stand beside it; from Engedi to En-eglaim they will spread nets. They will catch many kinds of fish, like the fish of the Great Sea.</span>

Their fruit will be for food and their leaves for healing.”</span>

## Boundaries for the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:4–6; Jeremiah 3:18; Numbers 34:2–12"> This is what the sovereign Lord says: “Here are the borders you will observe as you allot the land to the twelve tribes of Israel. (Joseph will have two portions.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:5–6; Genesis 12:7; Ezekiel 48:29"> You must divide it equally just as I vowed to give it to your forefathers; this land will be assigned as your inheritance.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 34:8; Ezekiel 48:1"> “This will be the border of the land: On the north side, from the Great Sea by way of Hethlon to the entrance of Zedad;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:1; Numbers 13:21; 2 Samuel 8:8"> Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, which is between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath, as far as Hazer-hattikon, which is on the border of Hauran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:1; Numbers 34:9"> The border will run from the sea to Hazar-enan, at the border of Damascus, and on the north is the border of Hamath. This is the north side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Genesis 31:23; Genesis 31:47"> On the east side, between Hauran and Damascus, and between Gilead and the land of Israel, will be the Jordan. You will measure from the border to the eastern sea. This is the east side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 48:28; Deuteronomy 32:51; Numbers 34:5"> On the south side it will run from Tamar to the waters of Meribath Kadesh, the river, to the Great Sea. This is the south side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 34:6; Ezekiel 48:1"> On the west side the Great Sea will be the boundary to a point opposite Lebo-hamath. This is the west side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “This is how you will divide this land for yourselves among the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:11; Ephesians 3:6; Acts 11:18"> You must allot it as an inheritance among yourselves and for the foreigners who reside among you, who have fathered sons among you. You must treat them as native-born among the people of Israel; they will be allotted an inheritance with you among the tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> In whatever tribe the foreigner resides, there you will give him his inheritance,” declares the sovereign Lord.</span>
</div>
