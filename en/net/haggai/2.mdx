<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Haggai 2:20; Haggai 2:10"> On the twenty-first day of the seventh month, the Lord spoke again through the prophet Haggai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Haggai 1:1; Nehemiah 8:9; Ezra 2:63"> “Ask the following questions to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, the high priest Joshua son of Jehozadak, and the remnant of the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezra 3:12; Haggai 2:9; Ezekiel 7:20"> ‘Who among you survivors saw the former splendor of this temple? How does it look to you now? Isn’t it nothing by comparison?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:20; Joshua 1:9; Zechariah 8:9"> Even so, take heart, Zerubbabel,’ says the Lord. ‘Take heart, Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and all you citizens of the land,’ says the Lord, ‘and begin to work. For I am with you,’ says the Lord who rules over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 34:10; Exodus 29:45–46; Isaiah 41:13"> ‘Do not fear, because I made a promise to your ancestors when they left Egypt, and my spirit even now testifies to you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Hebrews 12:26–28; Isaiah 10:25; Mark 13:24–26"> Moreover, the Lord who rules over all says: ‘In just a little while I will once again shake the sky and the earth, the sea and the dry ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; 1 Kings 8:11; John 7:37–39"> I will also shake up all the nations, and they will offer their treasures; then I will fill this temple with glory,’ says the Lord who rules over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 50:10–12; Psalms 24:1; Isaiah 60:17"> ‘The silver and gold will be mine,’ says the Lord who rules over all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 85:8–9; 2 Corinthians 3:9–10; Colossians 1:19–21"> ‘The future splendor of this temple will be greater than that of former times,’ the Lord who rules over all declares, ‘and in this place I will give peace.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Promised Blessing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Haggai 2:20; Haggai 1:1; Haggai 1:15–1"> On the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month of Darius’ second year, the Lord spoke again to the prophet Haggai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 10:10–11; Malachi 2:7; Deuteronomy 33:10"> “The Lord who rules over all says, ‘Ask the priests about the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 6:27; Matthew 23:19; Leviticus 7:6"> If someone carries holy meat in a fold of his garment and that fold touches bread, a boiled dish, wine, olive oil, or any other food, will that item become holy?’” The priests answered, “It will not.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2–3; Numbers 19:11–22; Leviticus 22:4–6"> Then Haggai asked, “If a person who is ritually unclean because of touching a dead body comes in contact with one of these items, will it become unclean?” The priests answered, “It will be unclean.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 28:9; Titus 1:15; Isaiah 1:11–15"> Then Haggai responded, “‘The people of this nation are unclean in my sight,’ says the Lord. ‘And so is all their effort; everything they offer is also unclean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Haggai 1:5; Ezra 3:10; Ezra 4:24"> Now therefore reflect carefully on the recent past, before one stone was laid on another in the Lord’s temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Haggai 1:6; Malachi 2:2; Proverbs 3:9–10"> From that time when one came expecting a heap of twenty measures, there were only ten; when one came to the wine vat to draw out fifty measures from it, there were only twenty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:22; Haggai 1:11; Haggai 1:9"> I struck all the products of your labor with blight, disease, and hail, and yet you brought nothing to me,’ says the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zechariah 8:9; Haggai 2:15; Ezra 5:1–2"> ‘Think carefully about the past: from today, the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, to the day work on the temple of the Lord was resumed, think about it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Malachi 3:10; Habakkuk 3:17–18; Proverbs 3:9–10"> The seed is still in the storehouse, isn’t it? And the vine, fig tree, pomegranate, and olive tree have not produced. Nevertheless, from today on I will bless you.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Zerubbabel the Chosen One

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then the Lord spoke again to Haggai on the twenty-fourth day of the month:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Zechariah 4:6–10; Haggai 2:6–7; Haggai 1:1"> Tell Zerubbabel governor of Judah: ‘I am ready to shake the sky and the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Micah 5:10; Judges 7:22; Zephaniah 3:8"> I will overthrow royal thrones and shatter the might of earthly kingdoms. I will overthrow chariots and those who ride them, and horses and their riders will fall as people kill one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 43:10; Isaiah 42:1; Song of Solomon 8:6"> On that day,’ says the Lord who rules over all, ‘I will take you, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, my servant,’ says the Lord, ‘and I will make you like a signet ring, for I have chosen you,’ says the Lord who rules over all.”</span>
</div>
