## Salutation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 1:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Peter 2:9"> From Jude, a slave of Jesus Christ and brother of James, to those who are called, wrapped in the love of God the Father and kept for Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2; Revelation 1:4–6"> May mercy, peace, and love be lavished on you!</span>
</div>

## Condemnation of the False Teachers

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; Philippians 1:27; Jude 1:20"> Dear friends, although I have been eager to write to you about our common salvation, I now feel compelled instead to write to encourage you to contend earnestly for the faith that was once for all entrusted to the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; Galatians 2:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> For certain men have secretly slipped in among you – men who long ago were marked out for the condemnation I am about to describe – ungodly men who have turned the grace of our God into a license for evil and who deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Hebrews 3:16–2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–12"> Now I desire to remind you (even though you have been fully informed of these facts once for all ) that Jesus, having saved the people out of the land of Egypt, later destroyed those who did not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 25:41; Ephesians 6:12"> You also know that the angels who did not keep within their proper domain but abandoned their own place of residence, he has kept in eternal chains in utter darkness, locked up for the judgment of the great Day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; 2 Peter 2:6; Romans 1:26–27"> So also Sodom and Gomorrah and the neighboring towns, since they indulged in sexual immorality and pursued unnatural desire in a way similar to these angels, are now displayed as an example by suffering the punishment of eternal fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Peter 2:10–12"> Yet these men, as a result of their dreams, defile the flesh, reject authority, and insult the glorious ones.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 3:2; 2 Peter 2:11; Deuteronomy 34:6"> But even when Michael the archangel was arguing with the devil and debating with him concerning Moses’ body, he did not dare to bring a slanderous judgment, but said, “May the Lord rebuke you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:12; Romans 1:21–22"> But these men do not understand the things they slander, and they are being destroyed by the very things that, like irrational animals, they instinctively comprehend.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:15; 1 John 3:12; Revelation 2:14"> Woe to them! For they have traveled down Cain’s path, and because of greed have abandoned themselves to Balaam’s error; hence, they will certainly perish in Korah’s rebellion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17–20; Matthew 15:13; Ezekiel 34:8"> These men are dangerous reefs at your love feasts, feasting without reverence, feeding only themselves. They are waterless clouds, carried along by the winds; autumn trees without fruit – twice dead, uprooted;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17; Philippians 3:19; Isaiah 57:20"> wild sea waves, spewing out the foam of their shame; wayward stars for whom the utter depths of eternal darkness have been reserved.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 50:3–5; Hebrews 11:5–6"> Now Enoch, the seventh in descent beginning with Adam, even prophesied of them, saying, “Look! The Lord is coming with thousands and thousands of his holy ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:16; Ecclesiastes 11:9; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> to execute judgment on all, and to convict every person of all their thoroughly ungodly deeds that they have committed, and of all the harsh words that ungodly sinners have spoken against him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18; 2 Peter 2:10; Philippians 2:14"> These people are grumblers and fault-finders who go wherever their desires lead them, and they give bombastic speeches, enchanting folks for their own gain.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Exhortation to the Faithful

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:2; 1 John 4:6; Ephesians 4:11"> But you, dear friends – recall the predictions foretold by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:1–5"> For they said to you, “In the end time there will come scoffers, propelled by their own ungodly desires.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Proverbs 18:1; Romans 8:9"> These people are divisive, worldly, devoid of the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Romans 8:26–27; 1 Corinthians 14:15"> But you, dear friends, by building yourselves up in your most holy faith, by praying in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Lamentations 3:25–26; Hebrews 9:28"> maintain yourselves in the love of God, while anticipating the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ that brings eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Galatians 6:1; 1 John 5:16–18"> And have mercy on those who waver;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Revelation 3:4"> save others by snatching them out of the fire; have mercy on others, coupled with a fear of God, hating even the clothes stained by the flesh.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Final Blessing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 1:22"> Now to the one who is able to keep you from falling, and to cause you to stand, rejoicing, without blemish before his glorious presence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Revelation 5:13–14; 1 Peter 5:10–11"> to the only God our Savior through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory, majesty, power, and authority, before all time, and now, and for all eternity. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
