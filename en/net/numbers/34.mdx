<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 17:8; Psalms 105:11; Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> “Give these instructions to the Israelites, and tell them: ‘When you enter Canaan, the land that has been assigned to you as an inheritance, the land of Canaan with its borders,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:3; Ezekiel 47:13; Joshua 3:16"> your southern border will extend from the wilderness of Zin along the Edomite border, and your southern border will run eastward to the extremity of the Salt Sea,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Joshua 15:3–4; Numbers 13:26"> and then the border will turn from the south to the Scorpion Ascent, continue to Zin, and then its direction will be from the south to Kadesh Barnea. Then it will go to Hazar Addar and pass over to Azmon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Joshua 15:4; Genesis 15:18; 1 Kings 8:65"> There the border will turn from Azmon to the Brook of Egypt, and then its direction is to the sea.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Western Border of the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Joshua 1:4; Ezekiel 47:20; Ezekiel 47:15"> “‘And for a western border you will have the Great Sea. This will be your western border.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Northern Border of the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:37; Numbers 34:6; Numbers 34:3"> “‘And this will be your northern border: From the Great Sea you will draw a line to Mount Hor;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 13:21; 2 Kings 14:25; Joshua 13:5–6"> from Mount Hor you will draw a line to Lebo Hamath, and the direction of the border will be to Zedad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:17"> The border will continue to Ziphron, and its direction will be to Hazar Enan. This will be your northern border.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Eastern Border of the Land

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> “‘For your eastern border you will draw a line from Hazar Enan to Shepham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 23:33; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 11:2"> The border will run down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east side of Ain, and the border will descend and reach the eastern side of the Sea of Chinnereth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 34:3; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:24–26"> Then the border will continue down the Jordan River and its direction will be to the Salt Sea. This will be your land by its borders that surround it.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 34:1; Joshua 14:1–5"> Then Moses commanded the Israelites: “This is the land which you will inherit by lot, which the Lord has commanded to be given to the nine and a half tribes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 32:33; Numbers 32:23; Joshua 14:2–3"> because the tribe of the Reubenites by their families, the tribe of the Gadites by their families, and half of the tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 32:32"> The two and a half tribes have received their inheritance on this side of the Jordan, east of Jericho, toward the sunrise.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Appointed Officials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The Lord said to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 14:1; Joshua 19:51; Numbers 13:8"> “These are the names of the men who are to allocate the land to you as an inheritance: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Numbers 1:4–16"> You must take one leader from every tribe to assist in allocating the land as an inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 26:65; Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:24"> These are the names of the men: from the tribe of Judah, Caleb son of Jephunneh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 49:5"> from the tribe of the Simeonites, Shemuel son of Ammihud;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Psalms 68:27"> from the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad son of Kislon;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and from the tribe of the Danites, a leader, Bukki son of Jogli.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> From the Josephites, Hanniel son of Ephod, a leader from the tribe of Manasseh;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> from the tribe of the Ephraimites, a leader, Kemuel son of Shiphtan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> from the tribe of the Zebulunites, a leader, Elizaphan son of Parnach;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> from the tribe of the Issacharites, a leader, Paltiel son of Azzan;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> from the tribe of the Asherites, a leader, Ahihud son of Shelomi;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and from the tribe of the Naphtalites, a leader, Pedahel son of Ammihud.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Joshua 19:51; Numbers 34:18"> These are the ones whom the Lord commanded to divide up the inheritance among the Israelites in the land of Canaan.</span>
</div>
</div>
