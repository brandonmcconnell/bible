<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> So Moses spoke to the Israelites, and each of their leaders gave him a staff, one for each leader, according to their tribes – twelve staffs; the staff of Aaron was among their staffs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 7:44; Exodus 38:21; Numbers 18:2"> Then Moses placed the staffs before the Lord in the tent of the testimony.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 17:24; John 15:1–6; Psalms 132:17–18"> On the next day Moses went into the tent of the testimony – and the staff of Aaron for the house of Levi had sprouted, and brought forth buds, and produced blossoms, and yielded almonds!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> So Moses brought out all the staffs from before the Lord to all the Israelites. They looked at them, and each man took his staff.</span>
