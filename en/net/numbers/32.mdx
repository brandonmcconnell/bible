<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ruth 2:10; Joshua 7:7; Jeremiah 31:2"> So they said, “If we have found favor in your sight, let this land be given to your servants for our inheritance. Do not have us cross the Jordan River.”</span>

## Moses’ Response

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:5; 2 Samuel 11:11; Philippians 2:4"> Moses said to the Gadites and the Reubenites, “Must your brothers go to war while you remain here?</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 13:27–4; Numbers 32:9; Deuteronomy 1:28"> Why do you frustrate the intent of the Israelites to cross over into the land which the Lord has given them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:19–25; Numbers 14:2; Numbers 13:2–26"> Your fathers did the same thing when I sent them from Kadesh Barnea to see the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 13:23–10; Deuteronomy 1:24–28"> When they went up to the Eshcol Valley and saw the land, they frustrated the intent of the Israelites so that they did not enter the land that the Lord had given them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 14:11; Psalms 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> So the anger of the Lord was kindled that day, and he swore,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 14:28–30; Numbers 14:24; Numbers 26:2"> ‘Because they have not followed me wholeheartedly, not one of the men twenty years old and upward who came from Egypt will see the land that I swore to give to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:36; Numbers 14:30; Numbers 14:24"> except Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, and Joshua son of Nun, for they followed the Lord wholeheartedly.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:5; Numbers 14:33–35; Hebrews 3:16–19"> So the Lord’s anger was kindled against the Israelites, and he made them wander in the wilderness for forty years, until all that generation that had done wickedly before the Lord was finished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 11:48; Psalms 78:57; Matthew 23:31–33"> Now look, you are standing in your fathers’ place, a brood of sinners, to increase still further the fierce wrath of the Lord against the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:11–12; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 2 Chronicles 7:19–22"> For if you turn away from following him, he will once again abandon them in the wilderness, and you will be the reason for their destruction.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Offer of the Reubenites and Gadites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 33:17; Numbers 34:22"> Then they came very close to him and said, “We will build sheep folds here for our flocks and cities for our families,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Numbers 32:29–32; Deuteronomy 3:18–20"> but we will maintain ourselves in armed readiness and go before the Israelites until whenever we have brought them to their place. Our descendants will be living in fortified towns as a protection against the inhabitants of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Joshua 22:1–5"> We will not return to our homes until every Israelite has his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Joshua 13:8; Joshua 12:1–6; Numbers 34:15"> For we will not accept any inheritance on the other side of the Jordan River and beyond, because our inheritance has come to us on this eastern side of the Jordan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Joshua 4:12–13; Deuteronomy 3:18–20; Joshua 1:13–15"> Then Moses replied, “If you will do this thing, and if you will arm yourselves for battle before the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and if all your armed men cross the Jordan before the Lord until he drives out his enemies from his presence</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 22:9; Joshua 22:4; Deuteronomy 3:20"> and the land is subdued before the Lord, then afterward you may return and be free of your obligation to the Lord and to Israel. This land will then be your possession in the Lord’s sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 59:12; Proverbs 13:21; Genesis 44:16"> “But if you do not do this, then look, you will have sinned against the Lord. And know that your sin will find you out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 32:16; Numbers 30:2; Numbers 32:34–42"> So build cities for your descendants and pens for your sheep, but do what you have said you would do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Joshua 1:13–14"> So the Gadites and the Reubenites replied to Moses, “Your servants will do as my lord commands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 1:14"> Our children, our wives, our flocks, and all our livestock will be there in the cities of Gilead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 4:12; Numbers 32:17; Numbers 12:11"> but your servants will cross over, every man armed for war, to do battle in the Lord’s presence, just as my lord says.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Joshua 1:13"> So Moses gave orders about them to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua son of Nun, and to the heads of the families of the Israelite tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Moses said to them: “If the Gadites and the Reubenites cross the Jordan with you, each one equipped for battle in the Lord’s presence, and you conquer the land, then you must allot them the territory of Gilead as their possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Joshua 22:19"> But if they do not cross over with you armed, they must receive possessions among you in Canaan.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Then the Gadites and the Reubenites answered, “Your servants will do what the Lord has spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> We will cross armed in the Lord’s presence into the land of Canaan, and then the possession of our inheritance that we inherit will be ours on this side of the Jordan River.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Land Assignment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Joshua 12:6; Deuteronomy 29:8; Deuteronomy 3:12–17"> So Moses gave to the Gadites, the Reubenites, and to half the tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph the realm of King Sihon of the Amorites, and the realm of King Og of Bashan, the entire land with its cities and the territory surrounding them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:36; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 17:2"> The Gadites rebuilt Dibon, Ataroth, Aroer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 32:1; Numbers 32:3"> Atroth Shophan, Jazer, Jogbehah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 32:3"> Beth Nimrah, and Beth Haran as fortified cities, and constructed pens for their flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 21:27; Numbers 32:3; Isaiah 15:4"> The Reubenites rebuilt Heshbon, Elealeh, Kiriathaim,</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Isaiah 46:1; Numbers 32:3; Joshua 23:7"> Nebo, Baal Meon (with a change of name), and Sibmah. They renamed the cities they built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Joshua 17:1; Numbers 26:29"> The descendants of Machir son of Manasseh went to Gilead, took it, and dispossessed the Amorites who were in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Joshua 17:1; Joshua 13:29–31; Deuteronomy 3:13–15"> So Moses gave Gilead to Machir, son of Manasseh, and he lived there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:14; Judges 10:4; Joshua 13:30"> Now Jair son of Manasseh went and captured their small towns and named them Havvoth Jair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 49:11; 2 Samuel 18:18"> Then Nobah went and captured Kenath and its villages and called it Nobah after his own name.</span>
</div>
</div>
