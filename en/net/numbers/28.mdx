<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 3:11; Genesis 8:21; Numbers 9:2–3"> “Command the Israelites: ‘With regard to my offering, be sure to offer my food for my offering made by fire, as a pleasing aroma to me at its appointed time.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 6:9; Daniel 11:31; John 1:29"> You will say to them, ‘This is the offering made by fire which you must offer to the Lord: two unblemished lambs one year old each day for a continual burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:21; 1 Kings 18:36; Exodus 12:6"> The first lamb you must offer in the morning, and the second lamb you must offer in the late afternoon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 2:1; Exodus 16:36; Exodus 29:38–42"> with one-tenth of an ephah of finely ground flour as a grain offering mixed with one quarter of a hin of pressed olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 5:25; Leviticus 6:9; Psalms 50:8"> It is a continual burnt offering that was instituted on Mount Sinai as a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 29:42; Numbers 28:14; Isaiah 57:6"> “‘And its drink offering must be one quarter of a hin for each lamb. You must pour out the strong drink as a drink offering to the Lord in the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the second lamb you must offer in the late afternoon; just as you offered the grain offering and drink offering in the morning, you must offer it as an offering made by fire, as a pleasing aroma to the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Weekly Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 58:13; Psalms 92:1–4; Revelation 1:10"> “‘On the Sabbath day, you must offer two unblemished lambs a year old, and two-tenths of an ephah of finely ground flour as a grain offering, mixed with olive oil, along with its drink offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 46:4–5; Numbers 29:22; Numbers 29:31"> This is the burnt offering for every Sabbath, besides the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Monthly Offerings

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 10:10; Ezekiel 46:6; Ezra 3:5"> “‘On the first day of each month you must offer as a burnt offering to the Lord two young bulls, one ram, and seven unblemished lambs a year old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 15:4–12; Ezekiel 46:5–7; Numbers 29:10"> with three-tenths of an ephah of finely ground flour mixed with olive oil as a grain offering for each bull, and two-tenths of an ephah of finely ground flour mixed with olive oil as a grain offering for the ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and one-tenth of an ephah of finely ground flour mixed with olive oil as a grain offering for each lamb, as a burnt offering for a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For their drink offerings, include half a hin of wine with each bull, one-third of a hin for the ram, and one-fourth of a hin for each lamb. This is the burnt offering for each month throughout the months of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:22; Numbers 15:24"> And one male goat must be offered to the Lord as a purification offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Passover and Unleavened Bread

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:1–8; Exodus 12:18; Leviticus 23:5–8"> “‘On the fourteenth day of the first month is the Lord’s Passover.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 23:6; Exodus 23:15; Exodus 13:6"> And on the fifteenth day of this month is the festival. For seven days bread made without yeast must be eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 12:16; Leviticus 23:7–8"> And on the first day there is to be a holy assembly; you must do no ordinary work on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:21; Ezekiel 45:21–25; Numbers 29:8"> “‘But you must offer to the Lord an offering made by fire, a burnt offering of two young bulls, one ram, and seven lambs one year old; they must all be unblemished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And their grain offering is to be of finely ground flour mixed with olive oil. For each bull you must offer three-tenths of an ephah, and two-tenths for the ram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For each of the seven lambs you are to offer one-tenth of an ephah,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 8:3"> as well as one goat for a purification offering, to make atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 28:3; Numbers 28:10"> You must offer these in addition to the burnt offering in the morning which is for a continual burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> In this manner you must offer daily throughout the seven days the food of the sacrifice made by fire as a sweet aroma to the Lord. It is to be offered in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Leviticus 23:8; Numbers 28:18; Exodus 13:6"> On the seventh day you are to have a holy assembly, you must do no regular work.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Firstfruits

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 23:16; Exodus 34:22; Leviticus 23:10"> “‘Also, on the day of the first fruits, when you bring a new grain offering to the Lord during your Feast of Weeks, you are to have a holy assembly. You must do no ordinary work.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 23:18–19; Numbers 28:11; Numbers 28:19"> But you must offer as the burnt offering, as a sweet aroma to the Lord, two young bulls, one ram, seven lambs one year old,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> with their grain offering of finely ground flour mixed with olive oil: three-tenths of an ephah for each bull, two-tenths for the one ram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> with one-tenth for each of the seven lambs,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 28:15; Numbers 28:22; 1 Peter 2:24"> as well as one male goat to make an atonement for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 28:19; Numbers 28:3; Malachi 1:13–14"> You are to offer them with their drink offerings in addition to the continual burnt offering and its grain offering – they must be unblemished.</span>
</div>
</div>
