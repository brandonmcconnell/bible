<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 28:41; Leviticus 8:30; Leviticus 8:12"> These are the names of the sons of Aaron, the anointed priests, whom he consecrated to minister as priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2; Numbers 26:61"> Nadab and Abihu died before the Lord when they offered strange fire before the Lord in the wilderness of Sinai, and they had no children. ## The Assignment of the Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> The Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:8; Numbers 2:33; Numbers 1:49–53"> “Bring the tribe of Levi near, and present them before Aaron the priest, that they may serve him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 1:50; Numbers 8:15; Numbers 8:11"> They are responsible for his needs and the needs of the whole community before the tent of meeting, by attending to the service of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Numbers 4:33; Numbers 10:17; Isaiah 52:11"> And they are responsible for all the furnishings of the tent of meeting, and for the needs of the Israelites, as they serve in the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 8:19; Numbers 18:6–7; Ephesians 4:8"> You are to assign the Levites to Aaron and his sons; they will be assigned exclusively to him out of all the Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Numbers 1:51; Numbers 3:38; Romans 12:7"> So you are to appoint Aaron and his sons, and they will be responsible for their priesthood; but the unauthorized person who comes near must be put to death.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 3:41; Numbers 8:16; Numbers 18:6"> “Look, I myself have taken the Levites from among the Israelites instead of every firstborn who opens the womb among the Israelites. So the Levites belong to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:23; Exodus 13:12; Exodus 13:2"> because all the firstborn are mine. When I destroyed all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I set apart for myself all the firstborn in Israel, both man and beast. They belong to me. I am the Lord.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Numbering of the Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Mark 10:14; 2 Timothy 3:15"> “Number the Levites by their clans and their families; every male from a month old and upward you are to number.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 3:39; Numbers 4:37; Numbers 4:49"> So Moses numbered them according to the word of the Lord, just as he had been commanded.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Summary of Families

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 46:11; Exodus 6:16–19; Numbers 15:5–23"> These were the sons of Levi by their names: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Chronicles 6:17; 1 Chronicles 26:1–32; Exodus 6:17–19"> These are the names of the sons of Gershon by their families: Libni and Shimei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 6:18; 1 Chronicles 6:18; 1 Chronicles 15:17–21"> The sons of Kohath by their families were: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 6:19; 1 Chronicles 6:19; Numbers 3:33"> The sons of Merari by their families were Mahli and Mushi. These are the families of the Levites by their clans.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Numbering of the Gershonites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> From Gershon came the family of the Libnites and the family of the Shimeites; these were the families of the Gershonites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 4:38–40"> Those of them who were numbered, counting every male from a month old and upward, were 7,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:17"> The families of the Gershonites were to camp behind the tabernacle toward the west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> Now the leader of the clan of the Gershonites was Eliasaph son of Lael.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 25:9; 1 Chronicles 23:32; 1 Chronicles 9:14–33"> And the responsibilities of the Gershonites in the tent of meeting included the tabernacle, the tent with its covering, the curtain at the entrance of the tent of meeting,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Exodus 35:18; Exodus 38:9–16; Exodus 27:9–16"> the hangings of the courtyard, the curtain at the entrance to the courtyard that surrounded the tabernacle and the altar, and their ropes, plus all the service connected with these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Numbering of the Kohathites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Chronicles 26:23; Numbers 3:19; 1 Chronicles 23:12"> From Kohath came the family of the Amramites, the family of the Izharites, the family of the Hebronites, and the family of the Uzzielites; these were the families of the Kohathites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 4:35–36; Numbers 3:7"> Counting every male from a month old and upward, there were 8,600. They were responsible for the care of the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 3:23; Numbers 2:10"> The families of the Kohathites were to camp on the south side of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Now the leader of the clan of the families of the Kohathites was Elizaphan son of Uzziel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Exodus 36:35–36; Exodus 27:1–8; Exodus 26:31–33"> Their responsibilities included the ark, the table, the lampstand, the altars, and the utensils of the sanctuary with which they ministered, the curtain, and all their service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 4:27; Numbers 4:16; Numbers 20:25–28"> Now the head of all the Levitical leaders was Eleazar son of Aaron the priest. He was appointed over those who were responsible for the sanctuary.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Numbering of Merari

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 3:20; 1 Chronicles 6:19; 1 Chronicles 23:21"> From Merari came the family of the Mahlites and the family of the Mushites; these were the families of Merari.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 2:9; Numbers 2:11; Numbers 1:21"> Those of them who were numbered, counting every male from a month old and upward, were 6,200.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:25; Numbers 3:28–29"> Now the leader of the clan of the families of Merari was Zuriel son of Abihail. These were to camp on the north side of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 26:32; Exodus 26:37; Exodus 26:15–29"> The appointed responsibilities of the Merarites included the frames of the tabernacle, its crossbars, its posts, its sockets, its utensils, plus all the service connected with these things,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and the pillars of the courtyard all around, with their sockets, their pegs, and their ropes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Numbers 3:10; Numbers 1:53; Numbers 2:3"> But those who were to camp in front of the tabernacle on the east, in front of the tent of meeting, were Moses, Aaron, and his sons. They were responsible for the needs of the sanctuary and for the needs of the Israelites, but the unauthorized person who approached was to be put to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Matthew 7:14; Numbers 4:47–48"> All who were numbered of the Levites, whom Moses and Aaron numbered by the word of the Lord, according to their families, every male from a month old and upward, were 22,000.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Substitution for the Firstborn

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 3:15; Luke 10:20; Psalms 87:6"> Then the Lord said to Moses, “Number all the firstborn males of the Israelites from a month old and upward, and take the number of their names.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:45; Numbers 8:16"> And take the Levites for me – I am the Lord – instead of all the firstborn males among the Israelites, and the livestock of the Levites instead of all the firstborn of the livestock of the Israelites.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> So Moses numbered all the firstborn males among the Israelites, as the Lord had commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 3:39"> And all the firstborn males, by the number of the names from a month old and upward, totaled 22,273.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Numbers 3:40–41"> “Take the Levites instead of all the firstborn males among the Israelites, and the livestock of the Levites instead of their livestock. And the Levites will be mine. I am the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16"> And for the redemption of the 273 firstborn males of the Israelites who exceed the number of the Levites,</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Exodus 30:13; Leviticus 27:6; Numbers 18:16"> collect five shekels for each one individually; you are to collect this amount in the currency of the sanctuary shekel (this shekel is twenty gerahs).</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And give the money for the redemption of the excess number of them to Aaron and his sons.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> So Moses took the redemption money from those who were in excess of those redeemed by the Levites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Numbers 3:46–47; 1 Peter 3:18; Matthew 20:28"> From the firstborn males of the Israelites he collected the money, 1,365 shekels, according to the sanctuary shekel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:48; Numbers 16:15; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> Moses gave the redemption money to Aaron and his sons, according to the word of the Lord, as the Lord had commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
</div>
