## The Response of the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 76:9; Numbers 16:16–21"> The Lord spoke immediately to Moses, Aaron, and Miriam: “The three of you come to the tent of meeting.” So the three of them went.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 11:25; Exodus 34:5; Psalms 99:7"> And the Lord came down in a pillar of cloud and stood at the entrance of the tent; he then called Aaron and Miriam, and they both came forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5; Genesis 31:10–11; Genesis 46:2"> The Lord said, “Hear now my words: If there is a prophet among you, I the Lord will make myself known to him in a vision; I will speak with him in a dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 3:2–6; Joshua 1:1; Psalms 105:26"> My servant Moses is not like this; he is faithful in all my house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:10; 1 Corinthians 13:12; Exodus 33:11"> With him I will speak face to face, openly, and not in riddles; and he will see the form of the Lord. Why then were you not afraid to speak against my servant Moses?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Hosea 5:15; Numbers 11:1"> The anger of the Lord burned against them, and he departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:9; 2 Kings 5:27; Exodus 4:6"> When the cloud departed from above the tent, Miriam became leprous as snow. Then Aaron looked at Miriam, and she was leprous!</span>
</div>

## The Intercession of Moses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Samuel 19:19; 2 Samuel 24:10; Proverbs 30:32"> So Aaron said to Moses, “O my lord, please do not hold this sin against us, in which we have acted foolishly and have sinned!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 88:4–5; 1 Corinthians 15:8; 1 Timothy 5:6"> Do not let her be like a baby born dead, whose flesh is half-consumed when it comes out of its mother’s womb!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Romans 12:21; Luke 6:28; James 5:15"> Then Moses cried to the Lord, “Heal her now, O God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:9; Job 30:10; Isaiah 50:6"> The Lord said to Moses, “If her father had only spit in her face, would she not have been disgraced for seven days? Shut her out from the camp seven days, and afterward she can be brought back in again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:8–9; Micah 7:8–9; Micah 6:4"> So Miriam was shut outside of the camp for seven days, and the people did not journey on until Miriam was brought back in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 11:35; Numbers 33:18; Numbers 10:12"> After that the people moved from Hazeroth and camped in the wilderness of Paran.</span>
