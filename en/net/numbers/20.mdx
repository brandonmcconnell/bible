## Moses Responds

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:5; Numbers 16:4; Numbers 16:19"> So Moses and Aaron went from the presence of the assembly to the entrance to the tent of meeting. They then threw themselves down with their faces to the ground, and the glory of the Lord appeared to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 4:17; Acts 2:1–4; Revelation 22:17"> “Take the staff and assemble the community, you and Aaron your brother, and then speak to the rock before their eyes. It will pour forth its water, and you will bring water out of the rock for them, and so you will give the community and their beasts water to drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 17:10"> So Moses took the staff from before the Lord, just as he commanded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 106:32–33; Acts 14:9–15; Luke 9:54–55"> Then Moses and Aaron gathered the community together in front of the rock, and he said to them, “Listen, you rebels, must we bring water out of this rock for you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:4; Exodus 17:6; Deuteronomy 8:15"> Then Moses raised his hand, and struck the rock twice with his staff. And water came out abundantly. So the community drank, and their beasts drank too.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lord’s Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:37; Numbers 27:14; Ezekiel 36:23"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, “Because you did not trust me enough to show me as holy before the Israelites, therefore you will not bring this community into the land I have given them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Psalms 95:8; Deuteronomy 33:8"> These are the waters of Meribah, because the Israelites contended with the Lord, and his holiness was maintained among them.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Rejection by the Edomites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 23:7; Obadiah 1:10–12"> Moses sent messengers from Kadesh to the king of Edom: “Thus says your brother Israel: ‘You know all the hardships we have experienced,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 12:40; Deuteronomy 26:6; Genesis 46:6"> how our ancestors went down into Egypt, and we lived in Egypt a long time, and the Egyptians treated us and our ancestors badly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 14:19; Exodus 23:20; Exodus 33:2"> So when we cried to the Lord, he heard our voice and sent a messenger, and has brought us up out of Egypt. Now we are here in Kadesh, a town on the edge of your country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:27; Deuteronomy 2:29; Numbers 21:22–24"> Please let us pass through your country. We will not pass through the fields or through the vineyards, nor will we drink water from any well. We will go by the King’s Highway; we will not turn to the right or the left until we have passed through your region.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> But Edom said to him, “You will not pass through me, or I will come out against you with the sword.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:28; Deuteronomy 2:6; Exodus 12:38"> Then the Israelites said to him, “We will go along the highway, and if we or our cattle drink any of your water, we will pay for it. We will only pass through on our feet, without doing anything else.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 11:17; Amos 1:11; Genesis 32:6"> But he said, “You may not pass through.” Then Edom came out against them with a large and powerful force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Judges 11:18; Deuteronomy 2:29; Deuteronomy 2:4–8"> So Edom refused to give Israel passage through his border; therefore Israel turned away from him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Aaron’s Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:14; Numbers 21:4"> So the entire company of Israelites traveled from Kadesh and came to Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 33:37"> And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron in Mount Hor, by the border of the land of Edom. He said:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 25:8; Deuteronomy 32:50; Numbers 31:2"> “Aaron will be gathered to his ancestors, for he will not enter into the land I have given to the Israelites because both of you rebelled against my word at the waters of Meribah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 33:38–39"> Take Aaron and Eleazar his son, and bring them up on Mount Hor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 20:24; Isaiah 22:21–22; Hebrews 7:11"> Remove Aaron’s priestly garments and put them on Eleazar his son, and Aaron will be gathered to his ancestors and will die there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> So Moses did as the Lord commanded; and they went up Mount Hor in the sight of the whole community.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:6; Exodus 29:29–30; Deuteronomy 34:9"> And Moses removed Aaron’s garments and put them on his son Eleazar. So Aaron died there on the top of the mountain. And Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:8; 2 Chronicles 35:24–25; Genesis 1:10"> When all the community saw that Aaron was dead, the whole house of Israel mourned for Aaron thirty days.</span>
</div>
</div>
