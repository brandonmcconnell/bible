<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 38:26; Exodus 30:12; Numbers 1:18"> “Take a census of the entire Israelite community by their clans and families, counting the name of every individual male.</span>

## The Census of the Tribes

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 7:2; Exodus 18:21; Exodus 18:25"> These were the ones chosen from the community, leaders of their ancestral tribes. They were the heads of the thousands of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 10:3; Revelation 7:4–17; Numbers 1:5–15"> So Moses and Aaron took these men who had been mentioned specifically by name,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezra 2:59; Nehemiah 7:61; Hebrews 7:3"> and they assembled the entire community together on the first day of the second month. Then the people recorded their ancestry by their clans and families, and the men who were twenty years old or older were listed by name individually,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 1:2; Numbers 26:1–2; 2 Samuel 24:1–10"> just as the Lord had commanded Moses. And so he numbered them in the wilderness of Sinai.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 26:5–7; Numbers 2:10–11; Genesis 46:9"> And they were as follows:<br />The descendants of Reuben, the firstborn son of Israel: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name individually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 26:7; Numbers 2:10–11"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Reuben were 46,500.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 26:12–14; Genesis 34:25–30; Numbers 2:12–13"> From the descendants of Simeon: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males numbered of them twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name individually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 26:14; Numbers 2:13; Numbers 25:8–9"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Simeon were 59,300.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 26:15–18; Genesis 46:16; Numbers 2:14–15"> <br />From the descendants of Gad: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 26:18; Numbers 2:15"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Gad were 45,650.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:35; Numbers 26:19–22; 2 Samuel 24:9"> From the descendants of Judah: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Samuel 24:9; Numbers 26:22; 2 Chronicles 17:14–16"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Judah were 74,600.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 46:13; Genesis 49:14–15; Genesis 30:18"> From the descendants of Issachar: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 26:25; Numbers 2:6"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Issachar were 54,400.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 26:26–27; Genesis 49:13; Genesis 30:20"> From the descendants of Zebulun: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Numbers 26:27; Numbers 2:8"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Zebulun were 57,400.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Numbers 26:35–37; Deuteronomy 33:17; Genesis 46:20"> From the sons of Joseph:<br />From the descendants of Ephraim: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 26:37; Deuteronomy 33:17; Numbers 2:19"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Ephraim were 40,500.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 26:28–34"> From the descendants of Manasseh: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Numbers 26:34; Genesis 48:19–20; Numbers 2:21"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Manasseh were 32,200.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 49:27; Genesis 44:20; Revelation 7:8"> From the descendants of Benjamin: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 26:41; Judges 20:44–46; 2 Chronicles 17:17"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Benjamin were 35,400.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 46:23; Genesis 30:5–6; Genesis 49:16–17"> From the descendants of Dan: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Numbers 26:43; Numbers 2:26"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Dan were 62,700.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Genesis 30:12–13; Genesis 46:27; Genesis 49:20"> From the descendants of Asher: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 26:47; Numbers 2:28"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Asher were 41,500.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 49:21; Numbers 26:48–50; Genesis 46:24"> From the descendants of Naphtali: According to the records of their clans and families, all the males twenty years old or older who could serve in the army were listed by name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 26:50; Numbers 2:30"> Those of them who were numbered from the tribe of Naphtali were 53,400.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Numbers 26:64; Numbers 1:2–16"> These were the men whom Moses and Aaron numbered along with the twelve leaders of Israel, each of whom was from his own family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> All the Israelites who were twenty years old or older, who could serve in Israel’s army, were numbered according to their families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Exodus 12:37; Numbers 26:51; Exodus 38:26"> And all those numbered totaled 603,550.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Exemption of the Levites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 2:33; Numbers 3:1–49; Numbers 26:57–62"> But the Levites, according to the tribe of their fathers, were not numbered among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> The Lord had said to Moses,</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Numbers 26:62; Numbers 2:33"> “Only the tribe of Levi you must not number or count with the other Israelites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 38:21; Exodus 31:18; Numbers 4:15"> But appoint the Levites over the tabernacle of the testimony, over all its furnishings and over everything in it. They must carry the tabernacle and all its furnishings; and they must attend to it and camp around it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 3:38; Numbers 3:10; 1 Samuel 6:19"> Whenever the tabernacle is to move, the Levites must take it down, and whenever the tabernacle is to be reassembled, the Levites must set it up. Any unauthorized person who approaches it must be killed.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Numbers 2:2; Numbers 2:34; Numbers 10:1–36"> “The Israelites will camp according to their divisions, each man in his camp, and each man by his standard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Numbers 16:46; 1 Chronicles 23:32; Leviticus 10:6"> But the Levites must camp around the tabernacle of the testimony, so that the Lord’s anger will not fall on the Israelite community. The Levites are responsible for the care of the tabernacle of the testimony.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Exodus 40:16; Exodus 39:32"> The Israelites did according to all that the Lord commanded Moses – that is what they did.</span>
</div>
</div>
