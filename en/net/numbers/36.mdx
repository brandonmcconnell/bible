<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; 1 Chronicles 7:14–16; Joshua 17:2–3"> Then the heads of the family groups of the Gileadites, the descendant of Machir, the descendant of Manasseh, who were from the Josephite families, approached and spoke before Moses and the leaders who were the heads of the Israelite families.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Joshua 17:3–6; Numbers 27:1–7; Numbers 33:54"> They said, “The Lord commanded my lord to give the land as an inheritance by lot to the Israelites; and my lord was commanded by the Lord to give the inheritance of our brother Zelophehad to his daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Now if they should be married to one of the men from another Israelite tribe, their inheritance would be taken from the inheritance of our fathers and added to the inheritance of the tribe into which they marry. As a result, it will be taken from the lot of our inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 25:10–18; Isaiah 61:2; Leviticus 25:23"> And when the Jubilee of the Israelites is to take place, their inheritance will be added to the inheritance of the tribe into which they marry. So their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of our ancestral tribe.”</span>

## Moses’ Decision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 27:7; Deuteronomy 5:28"> Then Moses gave a ruling to the Israelites by the word of the Lord: “What the tribe of the Josephites is saying is right.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 36:12; 2 Corinthians 6:14; Genesis 24:3"> This is what the Lord has commanded for Zelophehad’s daughters: ‘Let them marry whomever they think best, only they must marry within the family of their father’s tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 21:3; Numbers 36:9"> In this way the inheritance of the Israelites will not be transferred from tribe to tribe. But every one of the Israelites must retain the ancestral heritage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Chronicles 23:22"> And every daughter who possesses an inheritance from any of the tribes of the Israelites must become the wife of a man from any family in her father’s tribe, so that every Israelite may retain the inheritance of his fathers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> No inheritance may pass from tribe to tribe. But every one of the tribes of the Israelites must retain its inheritance.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:12; Matthew 28:20; Exodus 39:42–43"> As the Lord had commanded Moses, so the daughters of Zelophehad did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 27:1; Numbers 26:33"> For the daughters of Zelophehad – Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah – were married to the sons of their uncles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> They were married into the families of the Manassehites, the descendants of Joseph, and their inheritance remained in the tribe of their father’s family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Leviticus 27:34; Leviticus 26:46; Numbers 22:1"> These are the commandments and the decisions that the Lord commanded the Israelites through the authority of Moses, on the plains of Moab by the Jordan River opposite Jericho.</span>
</div>
