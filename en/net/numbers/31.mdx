<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 27:13; Numbers 25:14–18; Revelation 6:10"> “Exact vengeance for the Israelites on the Midianites – after that you will be gathered to your people.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 26:25; Numbers 25:11; 2 Kings 10:30"> So Moses spoke to the people: “Arm men from among you for the war, to attack the Midianites and to execute the Lord’s vengeance on Midian.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Leviticus 26:8; 1 Samuel 14:6; Judges 7:2"> You must send to the battle a thousand men from every tribe throughout all the tribes of Israel.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> So a thousand from every tribe, twelve thousand armed for battle in all, were provided out of the thousands of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Campaign Against the Midianites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Numbers 10:8–9; 2 Chronicles 13:12–15"> So Moses sent them to the war, one thousand from every tribe, with Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest, who was in charge of the holy articles and the signal trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Judges 21:11; 1 Kings 11:15–16; 1 Samuel 27:9"> They fought against the Midianites, as the Lord commanded Moses, and they killed every male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 13:21–22; Numbers 25:15; Revelation 2:14"> They killed the kings of Midian in addition to those slain – Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba – five Midianite kings. They also killed Balaam son of Beor with the sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; 2 Chronicles 28:5"> The Israelites took the women of Midian captives along with their little ones, and took all their herds, all their flocks, and all their goods as plunder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 1:7; 1 Samuel 30:1; Joshua 6:24"> They burned all their towns where they lived and all their encampments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Joshua 8:2"> They took all the plunder and all the spoils, both people and animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Numbers 22:1"> They brought the captives and the spoils and the plunder to Moses, to Eleazar the priest, and to the Israelite community, to the camp on the plains of Moab, along the Jordan River across from Jericho.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 5:2; 1 Samuel 30:21; Numbers 31:12"> Moses, Eleazar the priest, and all the leaders of the community went out to meet them outside the camp.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Death of the Midianite Women

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 13:19; 1 Samuel 15:13–14; Numbers 31:48"> But Moses was furious with the officers of the army, the commanders over thousands and commanders over hundreds, who had come from service in the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:3; Jeremiah 48:10; Deuteronomy 2:34"> Moses said to them, “Have you allowed all the women to live?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Numbers 25:1–9; 2 Peter 2:15; Revelation 2:14"> Look, these people through the counsel of Balaam caused the Israelites to act treacherously against the Lord in the matter of Peor – which resulted in the plague among the community of the Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 21:11–12; Deuteronomy 20:16–18; Deuteronomy 7:2"> Now therefore kill every boy, and kill every woman who has had sexual intercourse with a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:10–14; 2 Chronicles 28:8–10; Deuteronomy 20:14"> But all the young women who have not had sexual intercourse with a man will be yours.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Purification After Battle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 19:11–22; Numbers 5:2; 1 Chronicles 22:8"> “Any of you who has killed anyone or touched any of the dead, remain outside the camp for seven days; purify yourselves and your captives on the third day, and on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Numbers 19:22; Genesis 35:2; Exodus 19:10"> You must purify each garment and everything that is made of skin, everything made of goat’s hair, and everything made of wood.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Numbers 30:16"> Then Eleazar the priest said to the men of war who had gone into the battle, “This is the ordinance of the law that the Lord commanded Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> ‘Only the gold, the silver, the bronze, the iron, the tin, and the lead,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 19:9; Numbers 19:17; 1 Corinthians 3:13"> everything that may stand the fire, you are to pass through the fire, and it will be ceremonially clean, but it must still be purified with the water of purification. Anything that cannot withstand the fire you must pass through the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 11:25; Leviticus 15:13; Leviticus 14:9"> You must wash your clothes on the seventh day, and you will be ceremonially clean, and afterward you may enter the camp.’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Distribution of Spoils

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then the Lord spoke to Moses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> “You and Eleazar the priest, and all the family leaders of the community, take the sum of the plunder that was captured, both people and animals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 22:8; Psalms 68:12; 1 Samuel 30:24–25"> Divide the plunder into two parts, one for those who took part in the war – who went out to battle – and the other for all the community.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 18:26; Numbers 31:30; Numbers 31:47"> “You must exact a tribute for the Lord from the fighting men who went out to battle: one life out of five hundred, from the people, the cattle, and from the donkeys and the sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 29:27; Deuteronomy 12:19; Deuteronomy 12:12"> You are to take it from their half-share and give it to Eleazar the priest for a raised offering to the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Numbers 3:31; Numbers 3:7–8; Numbers 3:25"> From the Israelites’ half-share you are to take one portion out of fifty of the people, the cattle, the donkeys, and the sheep – from every kind of animal – and you are to give them to the Levites, who are responsible for the care of the Lord’s tabernacle.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> So Moses and Eleazar the priest did as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> The spoil that remained of the plunder which the fighting men had gathered was 675,000 sheep,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> 72,000 cattle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> 61,000 donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and 32,000 young women who had never had sexual intercourse with a man.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> The half-portion of those who went to war numbered 337,500 sheep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> the Lord’s tribute from the sheep was 675.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> The cattle numbered 36,000; the Lord’s tribute was 72.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> The donkeys were 30,500, of which the Lord’s tribute was 61.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> The people were 16,000, of which the Lord’s tribute was 32 people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 18:8; Numbers 18:19; Hebrews 7:9–12"> So Moses gave the tribute, which was the Lord’s raised offering, to Eleazar the priest, as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> From the Israelites’ half-share that Moses had separated from the fighting men,</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> there were 337,500 sheep from the portion belonging to the community,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> 36,000 cattle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> 30,500 donkeys,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and 16,000 people.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Numbers 31:30; Luke 10:1–8; Isaiah 56:10–11"> From the Israelites’ share Moses took one of every fifty people and animals and gave them to the Levites who were responsible for the care of the Lord’s tabernacle, just as the Lord commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> Then the officers who were over the thousands of the army, the commanders over thousands and the commanders over hundreds, approached Moses</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="John 18:9; 1 Samuel 30:18–19; Psalms 72:14"> and said to him, “Your servants have taken a count of the men who were in the battle, who were under our authority, and not one is missing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Exodus 30:12; Exodus 30:15–16; Psalms 116:12"> So we have brought as an offering for the Lord what each man found: gold ornaments, armlets, bracelets, signet rings, earrings, and necklaces, to make atonement for ourselves before the Lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Numbers 7:2–6"> Moses and Eleazar the priest took the gold from them, all of it in the form of ornaments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> All the gold of the offering they offered up to the Lord from the commanders of thousands and the commanders of hundreds weighed 16,750 shekels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:14; Numbers 31:32"> Each soldier had taken plunder for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 30:16; Zechariah 6:14; Acts 10:4"> So Moses and Eleazar the priest received the gold from the commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds and brought it into the tent of meeting as a memorial for the Israelites before the Lord.</span>
</div>
</div>

</div>
