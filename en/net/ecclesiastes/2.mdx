<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Luke 17:27–29; Song of Solomon 4:12–1; Jeremiah 39:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">I designed royal gardens and parks for myself,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I planted all kinds of fruit trees in them.</span><br /></span>

## Wisdom is Better than Folly

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:17; Ecclesiastes 7:25; Ecclesiastes 1:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">Next, I decided to consider wisdom, as well as foolish behavior and ideas.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For what more can the king’s successor do than what the king has already done?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Ecclesiastes 7:11–12; Matthew 6:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">I realized that wisdom is preferable to folly,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">just as light is preferable to darkness:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 49:10; Proverbs 17:24; Ecclesiastes 9:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">The wise man can see where he is going, but the fool walks in darkness.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Yet I also realized that the same fate happens to them both.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 6:8; Ecclesiastes 1:14; Ecclesiastes 1:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I thought to myself, “The fate of the fool will happen even to me!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then what did I gain by becoming so excessively wise?”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So I lamented to myself,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“The benefits of wisdom are ultimately meaningless!”</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 1:11; Ecclesiastes 9:5; Psalms 88:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the wise man, like the fool, will not be remembered for very long,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because in the days to come, both will already have been forgotten.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Alas, the wise man dies – just like the fool!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:11; Jonah 4:8; Ecclesiastes 4:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">So I loathed life because what</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">happens on earth seems awful to me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for all the benefits of wisdom are futile – like chasing the wind.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bp poetry">
