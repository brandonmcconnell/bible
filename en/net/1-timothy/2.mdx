## Conduct of Women

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Peter 3:3–5; Titus 2:3–5; Psalms 149:4"> Likewise the women are to dress in suitable apparel, with modesty and self-control. Their adornment must not be with braided hair and gold or pearls or expensive clothing,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Peter 3:3–5; Revelation 2:19"> but with good deeds, as is proper for women who profess reverence for God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34–35; Ephesians 5:22–24; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> A woman must learn quietly with all submissiveness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 14:34"> But I do not allow a woman to teach or exercise authority over a man. She must remain quiet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 2:18; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; Genesis 1:27"> For Adam was formed first and then Eve.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:3; Genesis 3:6; Genesis 3:12–13"> And Adam was not deceived, but the woman, because she was fully deceived, fell into transgression.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 2:12; Genesis 3:15–16; 1 Peter 4:7"> But she will be delivered through childbearing, if she continues in faith and love and holiness with self-control.</span>
</div>
