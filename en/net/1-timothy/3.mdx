<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Acts 20:28; 1 Peter 5:2"> This saying is trustworthy: “If someone aspires to the office of overseer, he desires a good work.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Titus 1:6–9; 2 Timothy 2:24; Romans 12:13"> The overseer then must be above reproach, the husband of one wife, temperate, self-controlled, respectable, hospitable, an able teacher,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Titus 1:7; Ephesians 5:18; Titus 3:2"> not a drunkard, not violent, but gentle, not contentious, free from the love of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Titus 1:6; Genesis 18:19; 1 Timothy 3:12"> He must manage his own household well and keep his children in control without losing his dignity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Samuel 3:13; Ephesians 5:24; Acts 20:28"> But if someone does not know how to manage his own household, how will he care for the church of God?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:4; Hebrews 5:12–13; 1 Peter 2:2"> He must not be a recent convert or he may become arrogant and fall into the punishment that the devil will exact.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:21; 2 Timothy 2:26; Colossians 4:5"> And he must be well thought of by those outside the faith, so that he may not fall into disgrace and be caught by the devil’s trap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 1:1; 1 Timothy 5:23; Acts 6:3–6"> Deacons likewise must be dignified, not two-faced, not given to excessive drinking, not greedy for gain,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:19; 2 John 1:9–10; 1 Timothy 1:5"> holding to the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:22; 1 Timothy 3:13; Titus 1:6–7"> And these also must be tested first and then let them serve as deacons if they are found blameless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Titus 2:3; Titus 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:2"> Likewise also their wives must be dignified, not slanderous, temperate, faithful in every respect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 3:4–5; Philippians 1:1"> Deacons must be husbands of one wife and good managers of their children and their own households.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:21; Hebrews 6:10; Acts 21:35"> For those who have served well as deacons gain a good standing for themselves and great boldness in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.</span>
