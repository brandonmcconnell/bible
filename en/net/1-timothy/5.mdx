<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; 1 Peter 5:5–6; Titus 2:2"> Do not address an older man harshly but appeal to him as a father. Speak to younger men as brothers,</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:19; Acts 6:1; Deuteronomy 10:18"> Honor widows who are truly in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:1–3; John 19:26–27; Matthew 15:4–6"> But if a widow has children or grandchildren, they should first learn to fulfill their duty toward their own household and so repay their parents what is owed them. For this is what pleases God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3; Luke 2:37; 1 Corinthians 7:32"> But the widow who is truly in need, and completely on her own, has set her hope on God and continues in her pleas and prayers night and day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="James 5:5; Revelation 18:7; Luke 15:24"> But the one who lives for pleasure is dead even while she lives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:11; Titus 2:15; 2 Timothy 4:1"> Reinforce these commands, so that they will be beyond reproach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Galatians 6:10; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Luke 11:11–13"> But if someone does not provide for his own, especially his own family, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 3:2; 1 Timothy 5:11; Luke 2:36–37"> No widow should be put on the list unless she is at least sixty years old, was the wife of one husband,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:18; Titus 3:8; Acts 9:36"> and has a reputation for good works: as one who has raised children, practiced hospitality, washed the feet of the saints, helped those in distress – as one who has exhibited all kinds of good works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:14; Hosea 13:6; James 5:5"> But do not accept younger widows on the list, because their passions may lead them away from Christ and they will desire to marry,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:6; James 3:1; 1 Corinthians 11:34"> and so incur judgment for breaking their former pledge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 20:19; 2 Thessalonians 3:6–11; Acts 20:30"> And besides that, going around from house to house they learn to be lazy, and they are not only lazy, but also gossips and busybodies, talking about things they should not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Titus 2:5; 1 Timothy 6:1; Titus 2:8"> So I want younger women to marry, raise children, and manage a household, in order to give the adversary no opportunity to vilify us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Peter 2:2; Jude 1:4–5; 2 Peter 2:20–22"> For some have already wandered away to follow Satan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:3–5; 1 Timothy 5:8"> If a believing woman has widows in her family, let her help them. The church should not be burdened, so that it may help the widows who are truly in need.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:12–13; Galatians 6:6"> Elders who provide effective leadership must be counted worthy of double honor, especially those who work hard in speaking and teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:4; 1 Corinthians 9:14; 1 Corinthians 9:7–10"> For the scripture says, “Do not muzzle an ox while it is treading out the grain,” and, “The worker deserves his pay.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 18:16; Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> Do not accept an accusation against an elder unless it can be confirmed by two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:2; Titus 1:13; 1 Timothy 1:20"> Those guilty of sin must be rebuked before all, as a warning to the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; 2 Timothy 4:1; 1 Timothy 6:13"> Before God and Christ Jesus and the elect angels, I solemnly charge you to carry out these commands without prejudice or favoritism of any kind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; 1 Timothy 3:10; 1 Timothy 3:6"> Do not lay hands on anyone hastily and so identify with the sins of others. Keep yourself pure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Proverbs 31:4–7; 1 Timothy 4:4"> (Stop drinking just water, but use a little wine for your digestion and your frequent illnesses.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:10; Acts 8:18; Galatians 5:19–21"> The sins of some people are obvious, going before them into judgment, but for others, they show up later.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 10:9; Luke 11:33; Psalms 37:5–6"> Similarly good works are also obvious, and the ones that are not cannot remain hidden.</span>
</div>
