<div className="bp poetry">

Record it legibly on tablets,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so the one who announces it may read it easily.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 27:14; Hebrews 10:36–37; Ezekiel 12:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the message is a witness to what is decreed;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it gives reliable testimony about how matters will turn out.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Even if the message is not fulfilled right away, wait patiently;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for it will certainly come to pass – it will not arrive late.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 1:17; Hebrews 10:38; Galatians 3:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, the one whose desires are not upright will faint from exhaustion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but the person of integrity will live because of his faithfulness.</span><br /></span>

## The Proud Babylonians are as Good as Dead

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 23:7; Micah 2:4; Jeremiah 50:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">“But all these nations will someday taunt him</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and ridicule him with proverbial sayings:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The one who accumulates what does not belong to him is as good as dead</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">(How long will this go on?) –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he who gets rich by extortion!’</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 29:1; Isaiah 47:11; Jeremiah 8:17"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your creditors will suddenly attack;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">those who terrify you will spring into action,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and they will rob you.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 33:1; Habakkuk 2:17; Jeremiah 27:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because you robbed many countries,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all who are left among the nations will rob you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have shed human blood</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and committed violent acts against lands, cities, and those who live in them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:16; Jeremiah 22:13–19; Isaiah 28:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">The one who builds his house by unjust gain is as good as dead.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He does this so he can build his nest way up high</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and escape the clutches of disaster.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Nahum 1:14; 2 Kings 9:26; Jeremiah 36:31"> <span className="pl pl-1">Your schemes will bring shame to your house.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Because you destroyed many nations, you will self-destruct.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:40; Joshua 24:27; Job 31:38–40"> <span className="pl pl-1">For the stones in the walls will cry out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the wooden rafters will answer back.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 3:10; Daniel 4:27–31; Nahum 3:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The one who builds a city by bloodshed is as good as dead –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he who starts a town by unjust deeds.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:58; Isaiah 50:11; Proverbs 21:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Be sure of this! The Lord who commands armies has decreed:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The nations’ efforts will go up in smoke;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their exhausting work will be for nothing.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Psalms 22:27; Psalms 72:19"> <span className="pl pl-1">For recognition of the Lord’s sovereign majesty will fill the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">just as the waters fill up the sea.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:7; Genesis 9:22; Revelation 18:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">“You who force your neighbor to drink wine are as good as dead –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you who make others intoxicated by forcing them to drink from the bowl of your furious anger,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so you can look at their genitals.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 47:3; Nahum 3:5–6; Isaiah 51:21–23"> <span className="pl pl-1">But you will become drunk with shame, not majesty.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Now it is your turn to drink and expose your uncircumcised foreskin!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The cup of wine in the Lord’s right hand is coming to you,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and disgrace will replace your majestic glory!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:8; Psalms 55:23; Zechariah 11:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">For you will pay in full for your violent acts against Lebanon;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">terrifying judgment will come upon you because of the way you destroyed the wild animals living there.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You have shed human blood</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and committed violent acts against lands, cities, and those who live in them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:8; 1 Corinthians 12:2; Isaiah 44:9–10"> <span className="pl pl-1">What good is an idol? Why would a craftsman make it?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">What good is a metal image that gives misleading oracles?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why would its creator place his trust in it</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and make such mute, worthless things?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 135:17; Jeremiah 10:4; 1 Kings 18:26–29"> <span className="pl pl-1">The one who says to wood, ‘Wake up!’ is as good as dead –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he who says to speechless stone, ‘Awake!’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Can it give reliable guidance?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It is overlaid with gold and silver;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it has no life’s breath inside it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:7; Psalms 46:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the Lord is in his majestic palace.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The whole earth is speechless in his presence!”</span></span>
