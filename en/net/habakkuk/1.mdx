## Habakkuk Complains to the Lord

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 22:1"> The following is the message which God revealed to Habakkuk the prophet:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 22:1–2; Jeremiah 14:9; Psalms 13:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">How long, Lord, must I cry for help?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you do not listen!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">I call out to you, “Violence!”</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But you do not intervene!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:9–11; Jeremiah 9:2–6; Jeremiah 20:8"> <span className="pl pl-1">Why do you force me to witness injustice?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why do you put up with wrongdoing?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Destruction and violence confront me;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">conflict is present and one must endure strife.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:126; Isaiah 1:21–23; Job 21:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">For this reason the law lacks power,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and justice is never carried out.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Indeed, the wicked intimidate the innocent.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For this reason justice is perverted.</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## The Lord Reveals Some Startling News

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:40–41; Lamentations 4:12; Isaiah 29:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Look at the nations and pay attention!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will be shocked and amazed!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For I will do something in your lifetime</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that you will not believe even though you are forewarned.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 24:2; Jeremiah 5:15; Isaiah 23:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">Look, I am about to empower the Babylonians,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that ruthless and greedy nation.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They sweep across the surface of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">seizing dwelling places that do not belong to them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5–9; Jeremiah 52:9–11; Deuteronomy 5:27"> <span className="pl pl-1">They are frightening and terrifying;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they decide for themselves what is right.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Zephaniah 3:3; Hosea 8:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">Their horses are faster than leopards</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and more alert than wolves in the desert.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Their horses gallop,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">their horses come a great distance;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like a vulture they swoop down quickly to devour their prey.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Hosea 13:15; Hosea 1:10"> <span className="pl pl-1">All of them intend to do violence;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">every face is determined.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They take prisoners as easily as one scoops up sand.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 32:24; Isaiah 14:16"> <span className="pl pl-1">They mock kings</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and laugh at rulers.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They laugh at every fortified city;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">they build siege ramps and capture them.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:20; Daniel 4:30–34; Jeremiah 4:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">They sweep by like the wind and pass on.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But the one who considers himself a god will be held guilty.”</span></span>
</div>
</div>

## Habakkuk Voices Some Concerns

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Jeremiah 30:11; Jeremiah 46:28"> <span className="pl pl-1">Lord, you have been active from ancient times;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">my sovereign God, you are immortal.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Lord, you have made them your instrument of judgment.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Protector, you have appointed them as your instrument of punishment.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:4–5; Psalms 34:15–16; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">You are too just to tolerate evil;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you are unable to condone wrongdoing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">So why do you put up with such treacherous people?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Why do you say nothing when the wicked devour those more righteous than they are?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 6:7"> <span className="pl pl-1">You made people like fish in the sea,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like animals in the sea that have no ruler.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 4:2; Jeremiah 16:16; Psalms 10:9"> <span className="pl pl-1">The Babylonian tyrant pulls them all up with a fishhook;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he hauls them in with his throw net.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When he catches them in his dragnet,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he is very happy.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:11; Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30"> <span className="pl pl-1">Because of his success he offers sacrifices to his throw net</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and burns incense to his dragnet;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for because of them he has plenty of food,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and more than enough to eat.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5–8; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Jeremiah 25:9–26"> <span className="pl pl-1">Will he then continue to fill and empty his throw net?</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Will he always destroy nations and spare none?</span></span>
</div>
