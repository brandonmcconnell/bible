<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; John 1:14; Romans 1:3"> But when the appropriate time had come, God sent out his Son, born of a woman, born under the law,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:15–17; Romans 5:5; Romans 8:9"> And because you are sons, God sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, who calls “Abba! Father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:16–17; Galatians 3:29–2; Galatians 3:26"> So you are no longer a slave but a son, and if you are a son, then you are also an heir through God.</span>

## Heirs of Promise Are Not to Return to Law

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 2:11–12; 2 Thessalonians 1:8"> Formerly when you did not know God, you were enslaved to beings that by nature are not gods at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Galatians 3:3; Romans 8:3"> But now that you have come to know God (or rather to be known by God), how can you turn back again to the weak and worthless basic forces? Do you want to be enslaved to them all over again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:5; Leviticus 23:1–44"> You are observing religious days and months and seasons and years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Philippians 2:16; Galatians 2:2"> I fear for you that my work for you may have been in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:5; 1 Kings 22:4; Genesis 34:15"> I beg you, brothers and sisters, become like me, because I have become like you. You have done me no wrong!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Personal Appeal of Paul

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:30"> But you know it was because of a physical illness that I first proclaimed the gospel to you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 2:13; Hebrews 13:2"> and though my physical condition put you to the test, you did not despise or reject me. Instead, you welcomed me as though I were an angel of God, as though I were Christ Jesus himself!</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:2; 1 John 3:16–18; Colossians 4:13"> Where then is your sense of happiness now? For I testify about you that if it were possible, you would have pulled out your eyes and given them to me!</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Proverbs 9:8; John 8:45"> So then, have I become your enemy by telling you the truth?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Peter 2:3"> They court you eagerly, but for no good purpose; they want to exclude you, so that you would seek them eagerly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Revelation 3:19; Philippians 1:27"> However, it is good to be sought eagerly for a good purpose at all times, and not only when I am present with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 8:29; Romans 13:14"> My children – I am again undergoing birth pains until Christ is formed in you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17–18; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Thessalonians 3:9"> I wish I could be with you now and change my tone of voice, because I am perplexed about you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## An Appeal from Allegory

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:23–24; Romans 7:5–6"> Tell me, you who want to be under the law, do you not understand the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:15; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–2"> For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by the slave woman and the other by the free woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 4:28–29"> But one, the son by the slave woman, was born by natural descent, while the other, the son by the free woman, was born through the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 21:9–13; Hebrews 10:15–18; Genesis 16:8"> These things may be treated as an allegory, for these women represent two covenants. One is from Mount Sinai bearing children for slavery; this is Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Acts 1:11; Galatians 1:17"> Now Hagar represents Mount Sinai in Arabia and corresponds to the present Jerusalem, for she is in slavery with her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22; Philippians 3:20; Micah 4:1–2"> But the Jerusalem above is free, and she is our mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–5; Psalms 113:9; Ruth 4:14–16"> For it is written:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Rejoice, O barren woman who does not bear children;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">break forth and shout, you who have no birth pains,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">because the children of the desolate woman are more numerous</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">than those of the woman who has a husband.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Romans 9:8–9; Romans 4:13–18"> But you, brothers and sisters, are children of the promise like Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:11"> But just as at that time the one born by natural descent persecuted the one born according to the Spirit, so it is now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 21:10–12; John 8:35; Romans 8:15–17"> But what does the scripture say? “Throw out the slave woman and her son, for the son of the slave woman will not share the inheritance with the son” of the free woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:36; John 1:12–13; Galatians 5:1"> Therefore, brothers and sisters, we are not children of the slave woman but of the free woman.</span>
</div>
</div>
