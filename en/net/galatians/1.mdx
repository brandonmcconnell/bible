## Salutation

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:11–12; 1 Corinthians 1:1; Romans 1:1"> From Paul, an apostle (not from men, nor by human agency, but by Jesus Christ and God the Father who raised him from the dead)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:1; Philippians 4:21; Acts 16:5–6"> and all the brothers with me, to the churches of Galatia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 John 1:3; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Romans 1:7–15"> Grace and peace to you from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Romans 4:25; Galatians 2:20"> who gave himself for our sins to rescue us from this present evil age according to the will of our God and Father,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Romans 11:36; Jude 1:25; Psalms 72:19"> to whom be glory forever and ever! Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Occasion of the Letter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; Galatians 5:7–8; Isaiah 29:13"> I am astonished that you are so quickly deserting the one who called you by the grace of Christ and are following a different gospel –</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 15:24; Romans 16:17–18; Galatians 5:10"> not that there really is another gospel, but there are some who are disturbing you and wanting to distort the gospel of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:13–14; Galatians 1:9; Revelation 22:18–19"> But even if we (or an angel from heaven) should preach a gospel contrary to the one we preached to you, let him be condemned to hell!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Romans 16:17; Proverbs 30:6; Deuteronomy 4:2"> As we have said before, and now I say again, if any one is preaching to you a gospel contrary to what you received, let him be condemned to hell!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:4; Acts 5:29; Ephesians 6:6"> Am I now trying to gain the approval of people, or of God? Or am I trying to please people? If I were still trying to please people, I would not be a slave of Christ!</span>
</div>
</div>

## Paul’s Vindication of His Apostleship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Galatians 1:1; 1 Corinthians 2:9–10; 1 Corinthians 11:23"> Now I want you to know, brothers and sisters, that the gospel I preached is not of human origin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Galatians 1:16; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Galatians 1:1"> For I did not receive it or learn it from any human source; instead I received it by a revelation of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 8:3; 1 Corinthians 15:9; Acts 22:3–5"> For you have heard of my former way of life in Judaism, how I was savagely persecuting the church of God and trying to destroy it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Acts 26:5; Philippians 3:4–6"> I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my contemporaries in my nation, and was extremely zealous for the traditions of my ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:5; Isaiah 49:5; Isaiah 49:1"> But when the one who set me apart from birth and called me by his grace was pleased</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 16:17; Ephesians 3:5–10; Ephesians 6:12"> to reveal his Son in me so that I could preach him among the Gentiles, I did not go to ask advice from any human being,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 9:20–25; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33; Galatians 1:18"> nor did I go up to Jerusalem to see those who were apostles before me, but right away I departed to Arabia, and then returned to Damascus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 9:22–23; Acts 9:26–29; Acts 22:17–18"> Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to visit Cephas and get information from him, and I stayed with him fifteen days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3; Matthew 12:46"> But I saw none of the other apostles except James the Lord’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 11:10–11"> I assure you that, before God, I am not lying about what I am writing to you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Acts 15:41; Acts 9:30; Acts 6:9"> Afterward I went to the regions of Syria and Cilicia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:14; Romans 16:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:1"> But I was personally unknown to the churches of Judea that are in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:8–10; Acts 9:13; 1 Timothy 1:13–16"> They were only hearing, “The one who once persecuted us is now proclaiming the good news of the faith he once tried to destroy.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Acts 11:18; Luke 15:10"> So they glorified God because of me.</span>
</div>
</div>
