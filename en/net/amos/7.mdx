## Amos Confronts a Priest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 38:4; 1 Kings 12:31–32; Jeremiah 26:8–11"> Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent this message to King Jeroboam of Israel: “Amos is conspiring against you in the very heart of the kingdom of Israel! The land cannot endure all his prophecies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; Psalms 56:5; Amos 6:7–8"> As a matter of fact, Amos is saying this: ‘Jeroboam will die by the sword and Israel will certainly be carried into exile away from its land.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Matthew 8:34; 1 Samuel 9:9"> Amaziah then said to Amos, “Leave, you visionary! Run away to the land of Judah! Earn your living and prophesy there!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 12:32; Amos 2:12; 1 Kings 12:29"> Don’t prophesy at Bethel any longer, for a royal temple and palace are here!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Amos 1:1; 2 Kings 4:38; 2 Kings 2:5"> Amos replied to Amaziah, “I was not a prophet by profession. No, I was a herdsman who also took care of sycamore fig trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:8; Ezekiel 2:3–4; Jeremiah 1:7"> Then the Lord took me from tending flocks and gave me this commission, ‘Go! Prophesy to my people Israel!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:46; Micah 2:6; Ezekiel 21:2"> So now listen to the Lord’s message! You say, ‘Don’t prophesy against Israel! Don’t preach against the family of Isaac!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hosea 9:3; 2 Kings 17:6; Hosea 4:13–14"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Therefore this is what the Lord says:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Your wife will become a prostitute in the streets</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and your sons and daughters will die violently.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Your land will be given to others</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and you will die in a foreign land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Israel will certainly be carried into exile away from its land.’”</span></span>
