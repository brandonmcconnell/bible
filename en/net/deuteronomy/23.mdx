## Purity in Personal Hygiene

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Joshua 6:18; Judges 20:26; Revelation 19:11–14"> When you go out as an army against your enemies, guard yourselves against anything impure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 15:16; Numbers 5:2–3; 1 Corinthians 5:11–13"> If there is someone among you who is impure because of some nocturnal emission, he must leave the camp; he may not reenter it immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; Ephesians 5:26–27; Leviticus 14:9"> When evening arrives he must wash himself with water and then at sunset he may reenter the camp.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> You are to have a place outside the camp to serve as a latrine.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 24:6–8"> You must have a spade among your other equipment and when you relieve yourself outside you must dig a hole with the spade and then turn and cover your excrement.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Leviticus 26:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> For the Lord your God walks about in the middle of your camp to deliver you and defeat your enemies for you. Therefore your camp should be holy, so that he does not see anything indecent among you and turn away from you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Respect for Others’ Property

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:25; Leviticus 25:35–37; Psalms 15:5"> You must not charge interest on a loan to your fellow Israelite, whether on money, food, or anything else that has been loaned with interest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:10; Deuteronomy 15:3; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> You may lend with interest to a foreigner, but not to your fellow Israelite; if you keep this command the Lord your God will bless you in all you undertake in the land you are about to enter to possess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:4–5; Psalms 66:13–14; Psalms 116:18"> When you make a vow to the Lord your God you must not delay in fulfilling it, for otherwise he will surely hold you accountable as a sinner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> If you refrain from making a vow, it will not be sinful.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 20:25; Judges 11:30–31; 1 Samuel 1:11"> Whatever you vow, you must be careful to do what you have promised, such as what you have vowed to the Lord your God as a freewill offering.</span>
