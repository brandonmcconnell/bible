## The Ten Commandments

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 20:2–17; Psalms 81:5–10; Leviticus 26:1–2"> “I am the Lord your God, he who brought you from the land of Egypt, from the place of slavery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 20:3; Matthew 4:10; 1 John 5:21"> You must not have any other gods besides me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 4:15–19"> You must not make for yourself an image of anything in heaven above, on earth below, or in the waters beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 20:4–6; Exodus 34:7; Daniel 9:4–9"> You must not worship or serve them, for I, the Lord your God, am a jealous God. I punish the sons, grandsons, and great-grandsons for the sin of the fathers who reject me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 8:28; 1 John 1:7; Jeremiah 32:18"> but I show covenant faithfulness to the thousands who choose me and keep my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Leviticus 19:12; Deuteronomy 6:13"> You must not make use of the name of the Lord your God for worthless purposes, for the Lord will not exonerate anyone who abuses his name that way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 20:8–11; Isaiah 58:13; Isaiah 56:6"> Be careful to observe the Sabbath day just as the Lord your God has commanded you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 20:12; Luke 13:14–16; Exodus 23:12"> You are to work and do all your tasks in six days,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 4:4; Genesis 2:2; Exodus 23:12"> but the seventh day is the Sabbath of the Lord your God. On that day you must not do any work, you, your son, your daughter, your male slave, your female slave, your ox, your donkey, any other animal, or the foreigner who lives with you, so that your male and female slaves, like yourself, may have rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:6; Deuteronomy 15:15; Deuteronomy 16:12"> Recall that you were slaves in the land of Egypt and that the Lord your God brought you out of there by strength and power. That is why the Lord your God has commanded you to observe the Sabbath day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Ephesians 6:1–3; Colossians 3:20"> Honor your father and your mother just as the Lord your God has commanded you to do, so that your days may be extended and that it may go well with you in the land that he is about to give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 20:13; Romans 13:9; Matthew 5:21–22"> You must not murder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 20:14; Luke 18:20; Leviticus 20:10"> You must not commit adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 20:15; Romans 13:9; Ephesians 4:28"> You must not steal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:16; Exodus 23:1; Proverbs 19:9"> You must not offer false testimony against another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Exodus 20:17; Romans 13:9; Luke 12:15"> You must not desire another man’s wife, nor should you crave his house, his field, his male and female servants, his ox, his donkey, or anything else he owns.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Narrative of the Sinai Revelation and Israel’s Response

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 24:12; Exodus 31:18; Deuteronomy 4:12–15"> The Lord said these things to your entire assembly at the mountain from the middle of the fire, the cloud, and the darkness with a loud voice, and that was all he said. Then he inscribed the words on two stone tablets and gave them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 20:18–19; Hebrews 12:18–21"> Then, when you heard the voice from the midst of the darkness while the mountain was ablaze, all your tribal leaders and elders approached me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:33; Exodus 19:19; Exodus 33:20"> You said, “The Lord our God has shown us his great glory and we have heard him speak from the middle of the fire. It is now clear to us that God can speak to human beings and they can keep on living.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:16; Galatians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 3:7–9"> But now, why should we die, because this intense fire will consume us! If we keep hearing the voice of the Lord our God we will die!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:33; Romans 3:20; Matthew 26:63"> Who is there from the entire human race who has heard the voice of the living God speaking from the middle of the fire as we have, and has lived?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Hebrews 12:19"> You go near so that you can hear everything the Lord our God is saying and then you can tell us whatever he says to you; then we will pay attention and do it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:17; Numbers 36:5; Numbers 27:7"> When the Lord heard you speaking to me, he said to me, “I have heard what these people have said to you – they have spoken well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 48:18; Ezekiel 33:31–32; Deuteronomy 11:1"> If only it would really be their desire to fear me and obey all my commandments in the future, so that it may go well with them and their descendants forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Go and tell them, ‘Return to your tents!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Galatians 3:19; Ezekiel 20:11; Deuteronomy 5:1"> But as for you, remain here with me so I can declare to you all the commandments, statutes, and ordinances that you are to teach them, so that they can carry them out in the land I am about to give them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Joshua 23:6; Proverbs 4:27; Joshua 1:7"> Be careful, therefore, to do exactly what the Lord your God has commanded you; do not turn right or left!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:23; Deuteronomy 4:40; Luke 1:6"> Walk just as he has commanded you so that you may live, that it may go well with you, and that you may live long in the land you are going to possess.</span>
</div>
