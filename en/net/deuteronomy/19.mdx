<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 35:15–24; Deuteronomy 4:42; Isaiah 30:33"> Now this is the law pertaining to one who flees there in order to live, if he has accidentally killed another without hating him at the time of the accident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 35:25; Proverbs 27:12; 2 Kings 6:5–7"> Suppose he goes with someone else to the forest to cut wood and when he raises the ax to cut the tree, the ax head flies loose from the handle and strikes his fellow worker so hard that he dies. <span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 35:12; 2 Samuel 14:7; Deuteronomy 21:22"> Otherwise the blood avenger will chase after the killer in the heat of his anger, eventually overtake him, and kill him, though this is not a capital case since he did not hate him at the time of the accident.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Therefore, I am commanding you to set apart for yourselves three cities.</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 59:7; Proverbs 6:17; Matthew 27:4–5"> You must not shed innocent blood in your land that the Lord your God is giving you as an inheritance, for that would make you guilty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 35:16–21; Deuteronomy 27:24; Genesis 9:6"> However, suppose a person hates someone else and stalks him, attacks him, kills him, and then flees to one of these cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 2:28–34; 1 Kings 2:5–6"> The elders of his own city must send for him and remove him from there to deliver him over to the blood avenger to die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 2:31; Deuteronomy 21:9; Deuteronomy 7:16"> You must not pity him, but purge out the blood of the innocent from Israel, so that it may go well with you.</span>

## Laws Concerning Witnesses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:17; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10"> You must not encroach on your neighbor’s property, which will have been defined in the inheritance you will obtain in the land the Lord your God is giving you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 35:30; Deuteronomy 17:6; 2 Corinthians 13:1"> A single witness may not testify against another person for any trespass or sin that he commits. A matter may be legally established only on the testimony of two or three witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 27:12; Psalms 35:11; Exodus 23:1–7"> If a false witness testifies against another person and accuses him of a crime,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 17:9; Deuteronomy 21:5; Malachi 2:7"> then both parties to the controversy must stand before the Lord, that is, before the priests and judges who will be in office in those days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:14; Deuteronomy 17:4; 2 Chronicles 19:6–7"> The judges will thoroughly investigate the matter, and if the witness should prove to be false and to have given false testimony against the accused,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 19:5; Proverbs 19:9; Daniel 6:24"> you must do to him what he had intended to do to the accused. In this way you will purge evil from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:21; Deuteronomy 17:13; Deuteronomy 13:11"> The rest of the people will hear and become afraid to keep doing such evil among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 5:38–39; Exodus 21:23–25; Deuteronomy 19:13"> You must not show pity; the principle will be a life for a life, an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth, a hand for a hand, and a foot for a foot.</span>
