## The Covenant Setting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:1; Joshua 22:7; Numbers 33:17–18"> This is what Moses said to the assembly of Israel in the Transjordanian wastelands, the arid country opposite Suph, between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth, and Di Zahab</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 32:8; Numbers 13:26; Deuteronomy 9:23"> Now it is ordinarily an eleven-day journey from Horeb to Kadesh Barnea by way of Mount Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Deuteronomy 4:1–2; Numbers 20:1"> However, it was not until the first day of the eleventh month of the fortieth year that Moses addressed the Israelites just as the Lord had instructed him to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 21:21–35; Joshua 12:2–6; Joshua 13:10–12"> This took place after the defeat of King Sihon of the Amorites, whose capital was in Heshbon, and King Og of Bashan, whose capital was in Ashtaroth, specifically in Edrei.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:8; Deuteronomy 32:46; Deuteronomy 31:9"> So it was in the Transjordan, in Moab, that Moses began to deliver these words:</span>
</div>
</div>

## Events at Horeb

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 3:1; Numbers 10:11–13; Exodus 19:1–2"> The Lord our God spoke to us at Horeb and said, “You have stayed in the area of this mountain long enough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:24; Joshua 10:40; Amos 2:9"> Get up now, resume your journey, heading for the Amorite hill country, to all its areas including the arid country, the highlands, the Shephelah, the Negev, and the coastal plain – all of Canaan and Lebanon as far as the Great River, that is, the Euphrates.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:18; Genesis 12:7; Genesis 26:3–4"> Look! I have already given the land to you. Go, occupy the territory that I, the Lord, promised to give to your ancestors Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and to their descendants.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 18:18; Numbers 11:17; Numbers 11:11–14"> I also said to you at that time, “I am no longer able to sustain you by myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:22; Exodus 32:13; Genesis 22:17"> The Lord your God has increased your population to the point that you are now as numerous as the very stars of the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 115:14; Genesis 22:17; Numbers 6:27"> Indeed, may the Lord, the God of your ancestors, make you a thousand times more numerous than you are now, blessing you just as he said he would!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; 1 Kings 3:7–9; 2 Corinthians 2:16"> But how can I alone bear up under the burden of your hardship and strife?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 18:21; Acts 1:21–23; Numbers 11:16–17"> Select wise and practical men, those known among your tribes, whom I may appoint as your leaders.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You replied to me that what I had said to you was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:18; Exodus 18:25–26; Ephesians 4:11"> So I chose as your tribal leaders wise and well-known men, placing them over you as administrators of groups of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens, and also as other tribal officials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 7:24; Exodus 22:21; 2 Chronicles 19:6–10"> I furthermore admonished your judges at that time that they should pay attention to issues among your fellow citizens and judge fairly, whether between one citizen and another or a citizen and a resident foreigner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Exodus 18:26; James 2:9"> They must not discriminate in judgment, but hear the lowly and the great alike. Nor should they be intimidated by human beings, for judgment belongs to God. If the matter being adjudicated is too difficult for them, they should bring it before me for a hearing.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Instructions at Kadesh Barnea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:32; Deuteronomy 4:5; Acts 20:20"> So I instructed you at that time regarding everything you should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:2; Deuteronomy 8:15; Jeremiah 2:6"> Then we left Horeb and passed through all that immense, forbidding wilderness that you saw on the way to the Amorite hill country as the Lord our God had commanded us to do, finally arriving at Kadesh Barnea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:7–8"> Then I said to you, “You have come to the Amorite hill country which the Lord our God is about to give us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Joshua 1:9; Psalms 46:1; Numbers 14:8–9"> Look, he has placed the land in front of you! Go up, take possession of it, just as the Lord, the God of your ancestors, said to do. Do not be afraid or discouraged!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Numbers 13:1–20"> So all of you approached me and said, “Let’s send some men ahead of us to scout out the land and bring us back word as to how we should attack it and what the cities are like there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Numbers 13:3–33"> I thought this was a good idea, so I sent twelve men from among you, one from each tribe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 13:21–27; Joshua 2:1–2"> They left and went up to the hill country, coming to the Eshcol Valley, which they scouted out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then they took some of the produce of the land and carried it back down to us. They also brought a report to us, saying, “The land that the Lord our God is about to give us is good.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Disobedience at Kadesh Barnea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Numbers 14:1–4; Acts 7:51; Psalms 106:24–25"> You were not willing to go up, however, but instead rebelled against the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:28; Psalms 106:25; Numbers 14:3"> You complained among yourselves privately and said, “Because the Lord hates us he brought us from Egypt to deliver us over to the Amorites so they could destroy us!</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 13:28–33; Deuteronomy 9:1–2; Joshua 15:14"> What is going to happen to us? Our brothers have drained away our courage by describing people who are more numerous and taller than we are, and great cities whose defenses appear to be as high as heaven itself! Moreover, they said they saw Anakites there.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> So I responded to you, “Do not be terrified of them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 14:14; Nehemiah 4:20; Romans 8:31"> The Lord your God is about to go ahead of you; he will fight for you, just as you saw him do in Egypt</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Isaiah 46:3–4; Acts 13:18; Hosea 11:3–4"> and in the desert, where you saw him carrying you along like a man carries his son. This he did everywhere you went until you came to this very place.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 106:24; Jude 1:5; Hebrews 3:18–19"> However, through all this you did not have confidence in the Lord your God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 78:14; Nehemiah 9:12"> the one who was constantly going before you to find places for you to set up camp. He appeared by fire at night and cloud by day, to show you the way you ought to go.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Judgment at Kadesh Barnea

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Numbers 14:22–30; Psalms 95:11; Ezekiel 20:15"> When the Lord heard you, he became angry and made this vow:</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 95:11"> “Not a single person of this evil generation will see the good land that I promised to give to your ancestors!</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:6–14; Numbers 32:12"> The exception is Caleb son of Jephunneh; he will see it and I will give him and his descendants the territory on which he has walked, because he has wholeheartedly followed me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Numbers 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:21; Deuteronomy 34:4"> As for me, the Lord was also angry with me on your account. He said, “You also will not be able to go there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:28; Numbers 14:30; Deuteronomy 31:7–8"> However, Joshua son of Nun, your assistant, will go. Encourage him, because he will enable Israel to inherit the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Isaiah 7:15–16; Numbers 14:3; Numbers 14:31"> Also, your infants, who you thought would die on the way, and your children, who as yet do not know good from bad, will go there; I will give them the land and they will possess it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Numbers 14:25"> But as for you, turn back and head for the desert by the way to the Red Sea.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Unsuccessful Conquest of Canaan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Numbers 14:39–45; Proverbs 19:3; Numbers 22:34"> Then you responded to me and admitted, “We have sinned against the Lord. We will now go up and fight as the Lord our God has told us to do.” So you each put on your battle gear and prepared to go up to the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Numbers 14:41–43; Joshua 7:8–13; Isaiah 59:1–2"> But the Lord told me: “Tell them this: ‘Do not go up and fight, because I will not be with you and you will be defeated by your enemies.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Numbers 14:44; Isaiah 63:10; Acts 7:51"> I spoke to you, but you did not listen. Instead you rebelled against the Lord and recklessly went up to the hill country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Psalms 118:12; Numbers 14:45; Numbers 21:3"> The Amorite inhabitants of that area confronted you and chased you like a swarm of bees, striking you down from Seir as far as Hormah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Psalms 78:34"> Then you came back and wept before the Lord, but he paid no attention to you whatsoever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Numbers 20:1; Numbers 20:22; Judges 11:16–17"> Therefore, you remained at Kadesh for a long time – indeed, for the full time.</span>
</div>
</div>
