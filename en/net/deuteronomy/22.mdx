## Purity in the Marriage Relationship

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 29:21; Genesis 29:31; Ephesians 5:28–29"> Suppose a man marries a woman, has sexual relations with her, and then rejects her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Proverbs 18:21; Proverbs 18:8"> accusing her of impropriety and defaming her reputation by saying, “I married this woman but when I had sexual relations with her I discovered she was not a virgin!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Then the father and mother of the young woman must produce the evidence of virginity for the elders of the city at the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> The young woman’s father must say to the elders, “I gave my daughter to this man and he has rejected her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Moreover, he has raised accusations of impropriety by saying, ‘I discovered your daughter was not a virgin,’ but this is the evidence of my daughter’s virginity!” The cloth must then be spread out before the city’s elders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Exodus 18:21"> The elders of that city must then seize the man and punish him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:29; Matthew 19:8–9; Deuteronomy 24:1–4"> They will fine him one hundred shekels of silver and give them to the young woman’s father, for the man who made the accusation ruined the reputation of an Israelite virgin. She will then become his wife and he may never divorce her as long as he lives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> But if the accusation is true and the young woman was not a virgin,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:5; Genesis 34:7; Judges 20:6"> the men of her city must bring the young woman to the door of her father’s house and stone her to death, for she has done a disgraceful thing in Israel by behaving like a prostitute while living in her father’s house. In this way you will purge evil from among you.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Ezekiel 23:45–47; John 8:4–5"> If a man is caught having sexual relations with a married woman both the man who had relations with the woman and the woman herself must die; in this way you will purge evil from Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 1:18–19; Deuteronomy 20:7"> If a virgin is engaged to a man and another man meets her in the city and has sexual relations with her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:21–22; Deuteronomy 21:14; 1 Corinthians 5:13"> you must bring the two of them to the gate of that city and stone them to death, the young woman because she did not cry out though in the city and the man because he violated his neighbor’s fiancée; in this way you will purge evil from among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:14"> But if the man came across the engaged woman in the field and overpowered her and raped her, then only the rapist must die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:22"> You must not do anything to the young woman – she has done nothing deserving of death. This case is the same as when someone attacks another person and murders him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Corinthians 13:7"> for the man met her in the field and the engaged woman cried out, but there was no one to rescue her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17"> Suppose a man comes across a virgin who is not engaged and overpowers and rapes her and they are discovered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:19; Deuteronomy 22:24; Deuteronomy 21:14"> The man who has raped her must pay her father fifty shekels of silver and she must become his wife because he has violated her; he may never divorce her as long as he lives.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:20; 1 Corinthians 5:1; Leviticus 18:8"> A man may not marry his father’s former wife and in this way dishonor his father.</span>
</div>
