<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 77:20; Psalms 106:12–48; Psalms 78:14–72"> They did not see what he did to you in the desert before you reached this place,</span>

<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 145:4–6; Psalms 145:12; Psalms 150:2"> I am speaking to you because you are the ones who saw all the great deeds of the Lord!</span>

## The Abundance of the Land of Promise

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ephesians 6:10; Joshua 1:6–7; Ephesians 3:16"> Now pay attention to all the commandments I am giving you today, so that you may be strong enough to enter and possess the land where you are headed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 6:2"> and that you may enjoy long life in the land the Lord promised to give to your ancestors and their descendants, a land flowing with milk and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Zechariah 14:18"> For the land where you are headed is not like the land of Egypt from which you came, a land where you planted seed and which you irrigated by hand like a vegetable garden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Deuteronomy 8:7–9; Psalms 65:12–13"> Instead, the land you are crossing the Jordan to occupy is one of hills and valleys, a land that drinks in water from the rains,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Psalms 33:18; 1 Kings 9:3"> a land the Lord your God looks after. He is constantly attentive to it from the beginning to the end of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:12; Deuteronomy 4:29; Deuteronomy 6:17"> Now, if you pay close attention to my commandments that I am giving you today and love the Lord your God and serve him with all your mind and being,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 5:7; Leviticus 26:4; Ezekiel 34:26"> then he promises, “I will send rain for your land in its season, the autumn and the spring rains, so that you may gather in your grain, new wine, and olive oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 104:14; Deuteronomy 6:11; Joel 2:19"> I will provide pasture for your livestock and you will eat your fill.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Exhortation to Instruction and Obedience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:27; Hebrews 3:12; Hebrews 2:1"> Make sure you do not turn away to serve and worship other gods!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 8:35; Deuteronomy 6:15; 2 Chronicles 6:26"> Then the anger of the Lord will erupt against you and he will close up the sky so that it does not rain. The land will not yield its produce, and you will soon be removed from the good land that the Lord is about to give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Proverbs 3:1"> Fix these words of mine into your mind and being, and tie them as a reminder on your hands and let them be symbols on your forehead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:7; Isaiah 38:19; Deuteronomy 4:9–10"> Teach them to your children and speak of them as you sit in your house, as you walk along the road, as you lie down, and as you get up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:9"> Inscribe them on the doorframes of your houses and on your gates</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 4:10; Proverbs 3:2; Proverbs 9:11"> so that your days and those of your descendants may be extended in the land which the Lord promised to give to your ancestors, like the days of heaven itself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:17; Deuteronomy 11:13; Deuteronomy 10:20"> For if you carefully observe all of these commandments I am giving you and love the Lord your God, live according to his standards, and remain loyal to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:38; Deuteronomy 9:1; Deuteronomy 7:1–2"> then he will drive out all these nations ahead of you, and you will dispossess nations greater and stronger than you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Joshua 14:9; Exodus 23:31; Genesis 15:18–21"> Every place you set your foot will be yours; your border will extend from the desert to Lebanon and from the River (that is, the Euphrates) as far as the Mediterranean Sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:24; Exodus 23:27; Joshua 1:5"> Nobody will be able to resist you; the Lord your God will spread the fear and terror of you over the whole land on which you walk, just as he promised you.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Anticipation of a Blessing and Cursing Ceremony

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 30:1; Deuteronomy 30:15–20; Galatians 3:10"> Take note – I am setting before you today a blessing and a curse:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Deuteronomy 28:1–14; Isaiah 3:10"> the blessing if you take to heart the commandments of the Lord your God that I am giving you today,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 2:8–9; Galatians 3:10"> and the curse if you pay no attention to his commandments and turn from the way I am setting before you today to pursue other gods you have not known.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:12–26; Joshua 8:30–35"> When the Lord your God brings you into the land you are to possess, you must pronounce the blessing on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 12:6; Judges 7:1; Joshua 4:19"> Are they not across the Jordan River, toward the west, in the land of the Canaanites who live in the Arabah opposite Gilgal near the oak of Moreh?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Joshua 1:11; Deuteronomy 9:1; Joshua 3:13–17"> For you are about to cross the Jordan to possess the land the Lord your God is giving you, and you will possess and inhabit it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 28:20; Deuteronomy 12:32; 1 Thessalonians 4:1–2"> Be certain to keep all the statutes and ordinances that I am presenting to you today.</span>
</div>
</div>
