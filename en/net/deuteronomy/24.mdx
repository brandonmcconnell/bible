## Respect for Human Dignity

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2; Mark 1:44; Leviticus 13:1–46"> Be careful during an outbreak of leprosy to follow precisely all that the Levitical priests instruct you; as I have commanded them, so you should do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 12:10–15; 1 Corinthians 10:11; 1 Corinthians 10:6"> Remember what the Lord your God did to Miriam along the way after you left Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:8; Exodus 22:27"> When you make any kind of loan to your neighbor, you may not go into his house to claim what he is offering as security.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> You must stand outside and the person to whom you are making the loan will bring out to you what he is offering as security.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:17; Job 22:6; Job 24:3"> If the person is poor you may not use what he gives you as security for a covering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:25; Daniel 4:27; Exodus 22:26–27"> You must by all means return to him at sunset the item he gave you as security so that he may sleep in his outer garment and bless you for it; it will be considered a just deed by the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 3:5; Proverbs 14:31; Proverbs 22:16"> You must not oppress a lowly and poor servant, whether one from among your fellow Israelites or from the resident foreigners who are living in your land and villages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 19:13; Proverbs 3:27–28; James 5:4"> You must pay his wage that very day before the sun sets, for he is poor and his life depends on it. Otherwise he will cry out to the Lord against you, and you will be guilty of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; 2 Chronicles 25:4; Jeremiah 31:29–30"> Fathers must not be put to death for what their children do, nor children for what their fathers do; each must be put to death for his own sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 16:19; Deuteronomy 27:19; Exodus 23:6"> You must not pervert justice due a resident foreigner or an orphan, or take a widow’s garment as security for a loan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:15; Deuteronomy 15:15; Deuteronomy 16:12"> Remember that you were slaves in Egypt and that the Lord your God redeemed you from there; therefore I am commanding you to do all this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Leviticus 23:22; Leviticus 19:9–10; Deuteronomy 14:29"> Whenever you reap your harvest in your field and leave some unraked grain there, you must not return to get it; it should go to the resident foreigner, orphan, and widow so that the Lord your God may bless all the work you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 19:10"> When you beat your olive tree you must not repeat the procedure; the remaining olives belong to the resident foreigner, orphan, and widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 19:9–10; Deuteronomy 24:19"> When you gather the grapes of your vineyard you must not do so a second time; they should go to the resident foreigner, orphan, and widow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:18; 1 John 4:10–11; Deuteronomy 5:14–15"> Remember that you were slaves in the land of Egypt; therefore, I am commanding you to do all this.</span>
