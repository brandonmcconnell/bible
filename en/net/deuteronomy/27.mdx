## The Covenant Curses

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:11; Deuteronomy 33:9–10; Joshua 8:33"> “The Levites will call out to every Israelite with a loud voice:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 34:17; Exodus 20:4; Deuteronomy 5:8"> ‘Cursed is the one who makes a carved or metal image – something abhorrent to the Lord, the work of the craftsman – and sets it up in a secret place.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Leviticus 19:3; Exodus 21:17; Deuteronomy 21:18–21"> ‘Cursed is the one who disrespects his father and mother.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:14; Proverbs 22:28; Proverbs 23:10–11"> ‘Cursed is the one who moves his neighbor’s boundary marker.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 19:14; Matthew 15:14; Proverbs 28:10"> ‘Cursed is the one who misleads a blind person on the road.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:18; Deuteronomy 24:17; Proverbs 17:23"> ‘Cursed is the one who perverts justice for the resident foreigner, the orphan, and the widow.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 22:30; Leviticus 18:8; 1 Corinthians 5:1"> ‘Cursed is the one who has sexual relations with his father’s former wife, for he dishonors his father.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 18:23; Exodus 22:19; Leviticus 20:15"> ‘Cursed is the one who commits bestiality.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 20:17; Leviticus 18:9; Ezekiel 22:11"> ‘Cursed is the one who has sexual relations with his sister, the daughter of either his father or mother.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 20:14; Leviticus 18:17"> ‘Cursed is the one who has sexual relations with his mother-in-law.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Numbers 35:31; Leviticus 24:17; 2 Samuel 11:15–17"> ‘Cursed is the one who kills his neighbor in private.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 10:17; Exodus 23:7–8; Psalms 15:5"> ‘Cursed is the one who takes a bribe to kill an innocent person.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Romans 10:5"> ‘Cursed is the one who refuses to keep the words of this law.’ Then all the people will say, ‘Amen!’</span>
</div>
