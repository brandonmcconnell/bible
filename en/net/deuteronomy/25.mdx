## Respect for the Sanctity of Others

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 22:24; Luke 20:28; Mark 12:19"> If brothers live together and one of them dies without having a son, the dead man’s wife must not remarry someone outside the family. Instead, her late husband’s brother must go to her, marry her, and perform the duty of a brother-in-law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:19; Psalms 9:5; Psalms 109:13"> Then the first son she bears will continue the name of the dead brother, thus preventing his name from being blotted out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ruth 4:5–6; Ruth 4:1–2"> But if the man does not want to marry his brother’s widow, then she must go to the elders at the town gate and say, “My husband’s brother refuses to preserve his brother’s name in Israel; he is unwilling to perform the duty of a brother-in-law to me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ruth 4:6"> Then the elders of his city must summon him and speak to him. If he persists, saying, “I don’t want to marry her,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ruth 4:7–8; Numbers 12:14; Isaiah 50:6"> then his sister-in-law must approach him in view of the elders, remove his sandal from his foot, and spit in his face. She will then respond, “Thus may it be done to any man who does not maintain his brother’s family line!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> His family name will be referred to in Israel as “the family of the one whose sandal was removed.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; Romans 3:8"> If two men get into a hand-to-hand fight, and the wife of one of them gets involved to help her husband against his attacker, and she reaches out her hand and grabs his genitals,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:13; Deuteronomy 19:21; Deuteronomy 7:2"> then you must cut off her hand – do not pity her.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Proverbs 16:11; Leviticus 19:35–37"> You must not have in your bag different stone weights, a heavy and a light one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> You must not have in your house different measuring containers, a large and a small one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 4:40; Ephesians 6:3"> You must have an accurate and correct stone weight and an accurate and correct measuring container, so that your life may be extended in the land the Lord your God is about to give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Proverbs 11:1; Revelation 21:27; Deuteronomy 22:5"> For anyone who acts dishonestly in these ways is abhorrent to the Lord your God.</span>
</div>

## Treatment of the Amalekites

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 17:8–16; Numbers 25:17–18; Numbers 24:20"> Remember what the Amalekites did to you on your way from Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 3:18; Psalms 36:1; Proverbs 16:6"> how they met you along the way and cut off all your stragglers in the rear of the march when you were exhausted and tired; they were unafraid of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 17:14; 1 Samuel 14:48; Exodus 17:16"> So when the Lord your God gives you relief from all the enemies who surround you in the land he is giving you as an inheritance, you must wipe out the memory of the Amalekites from under heaven – do not forget!</span>
</div>
