## The Exodus, Wandering, and Conquest Reviewed

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 19:4; Exodus 8:12; Joshua 24:5–6"> Moses proclaimed to all Israel as follows: “You have seen all that the Lord did in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh, all his servants, and his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:32–35; Nehemiah 9:9–11; Deuteronomy 7:18–19"> Your eyes have seen the great judgments, those signs and mighty wonders.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 4:18; Isaiah 6:9–10; Proverbs 20:12"> But to this very day the Lord has not given you an understanding mind, perceptive eyes, or discerning ears!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:4; Nehemiah 9:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> I have led you through the desert for forty years. Your clothing has not worn out nor have your sandals deteriorated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Deuteronomy 8:3; Ephesians 5:18; 1 Corinthians 9:25"> You have eaten no bread and drunk no wine or beer – all so that you might know that I am the Lord your God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:24–17; Numbers 32:33–42; Psalms 135:10–12"> When you came to this place King Sihon of Heshbon and King Og of Bashan came out to make war and we defeated them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 3:12–13; Numbers 32:33"> Then we took their land and gave it as an inheritance to Reuben, Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Present Covenant Setting

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Kings 2:3; Deuteronomy 4:6; Luke 11:28"> “Therefore, keep the terms of this covenant and obey them so that you may be successful in everything you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joel 2:16–17; Revelation 20:12; Deuteronomy 31:12–13"> You are standing today, all of you, before the Lord your God – the heads of your tribes, your elders, your officials, every Israelite man,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 12:38; Joshua 9:21–27; Exodus 12:48–49"> your infants, your wives, and the foreigners living in your encampment, those who chop wood and those who carry water –</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Chronicles 15:12–15; 2 Kings 11:17; Deuteronomy 5:2–3"> so that you may enter by oath into the covenant the Lord your God is making with you today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Genesis 17:7; Deuteronomy 28:9"> Today he will affirm that you are his people and that he is your God, just as he promised you and as he swore by oath to your ancestors Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 8:7–12"> It is not with you alone that I am making this covenant by oath,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:39; 1 Corinthians 7:14; Jeremiah 50:5"> but with whoever stands with us here today before the Lord our God as well as those not with us here today.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Results of Disobedience

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Deuteronomy 2:9; Deuteronomy 2:4"> “(For you know how we lived in the land of Egypt and how we crossed through the nations as we traveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:36"> You have seen their detestable things and idols of wood, stone, silver, and gold.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:15; Deuteronomy 11:16–17; Deuteronomy 13:1–15"> Beware that the heart of no man, woman, clan, or tribe among you turns away from the Lord our God today to pursue and serve the gods of those nations; beware that there is among you no root producing poisonous and bitter fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Jeremiah 7:24; Numbers 15:30; Numbers 15:39"> When such a person hears the words of this oath he secretly blesses himself and says, “I will have peace though I continue to walk with a stubborn spirit.” This will destroy the watered ground with the parched.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 74:1; Psalms 79:5; Deuteronomy 9:14"> The Lord will be unwilling to forgive him, and his intense anger will rage against that man; all the curses written in this scroll will fall upon him and the Lord will obliterate his name from memory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Joshua 7:1–26; Ezekiel 13:9"> The Lord will single him out for judgment from all the tribes of Israel according to all the curses of the covenant written in this scroll of the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:8"> The generation to come – your descendants who will rise up after you, as well as the foreigner who will come from distant places – will see the afflictions of that land and the illnesses that the Lord has brought on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zephaniah 2:9; Jeremiah 17:6; Isaiah 34:9"> The whole land will be covered with brimstone, salt, and burning debris; it will not be planted nor will it sprout or produce grass. It will resemble the destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the Lord destroyed in his intense anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 22:8–9; 1 Kings 9:8–9; Romans 2:5"> Then all the nations will ask, “Why has the Lord done all this to this land? What is this fierce, heated display of anger all about?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Hebrews 8:9; Isaiah 47:6; Jeremiah 50:7"> Then people will say, “Because they abandoned the covenant of the Lord, the God of their ancestors, which he made with them when he brought them out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Judges 2:12–13; Judges 5:8"> They went and served other gods and worshiped them, gods they did not know and that he did not permit them to worship.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Daniel 9:11–14; Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Deuteronomy 27:15–26"> That is why the Lord’s anger erupted against this land, bringing on it all the curses written in this scroll.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 7:20; 1 Kings 14:15; Psalms 52:5"> So the Lord has uprooted them from their land in anger, wrath, and great rage and has deported them to another land, as is clear today.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Daniel 2:22; Acts 1:7; Romans 11:33–34"> Secret things belong to the Lord our God, but those that are revealed belong to us and our descendants forever, so that we might obey all the words of this law.</span>
</div>
</div>
