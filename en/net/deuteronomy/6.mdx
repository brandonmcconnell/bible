## The Essence of the Covenant Principles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 12:29–32; Isaiah 44:6; 1 Timothy 2:5"> Listen, Israel: The Lord is our God, the Lord is one!</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 12:30; Matthew 22:37; Luke 10:27"> You must love the Lord your God with your whole mind, your whole being, and all your strength.</span>

## Exhortation to Teach the Covenant Principles

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Colossians 3:16; Proverbs 7:3; Jeremiah 31:33"> These words I am commanding you today must be kept in mind,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:19; Ephesians 6:4; Psalms 78:4–6"> and you must teach them to your children and speak of them as you sit in your house, as you walk along the road, as you lie down, and as you get up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 6:21; Proverbs 3:3; Deuteronomy 11:18"> You should tie them as a reminder on your forearm and fasten them as symbols on your forehead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:20; Isaiah 30:8; Isaiah 57:8"> Inscribe them on the doorframes of your houses and gates.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Exhortation to Worship the Lord Exclusively

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Joshua 24:13; Psalms 105:44; Nehemiah 9:25"> Then when the Lord your God brings you to the land he promised your ancestors Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob to give you – a land with large, fine cities you did not build,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:12–18; Deuteronomy 8:10–20; Ezekiel 16:10–20"> houses filled with choice things you did not accumulate, hewn out cisterns you did not dig, and vineyards and olive groves you did not plant – and you eat your fill,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> be careful not to forget the Lord who brought you out of Egypt, that place of slavery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 4:8; Deuteronomy 10:20; Matthew 4:10"> You must revere the Lord your God, serve him, and take oaths using only his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:6; 1 John 5:21; Deuteronomy 13:7"> You must not go after other gods, those of the surrounding peoples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:24; Exodus 20:5; Deuteronomy 7:4"> for the Lord your God, who is present among you, is a jealous God and his anger will erupt against you and remove you from the land.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Exhortation to Obey the Lord Exclusively

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 17:7; Luke 4:12; Matthew 4:7"> You must not put the Lord your God to the test as you did at Massah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:22; Psalms 119:4; 1 Corinthians 15:58"> Keep his commandments very carefully, as well as the stipulations and statutes he commanded you to observe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:40; Deuteronomy 12:25; Romans 12:2"> Do whatever is proper and good before the Lord so that it may go well with you and that you may enter and occupy the good land that he promised your ancestors,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 33:52–53; Exodus 23:28–30; Judges 3:1–4"> and that you may drive out all your enemies just as the Lord said.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Exhortation to Remember the Past

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; Exodus 12:26; Joshua 4:6–7"> When your children ask you later on, “What are the stipulations, statutes, and ordinances that the Lord our God commanded you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 6:17–18; Ephesians 2:11–12; Deuteronomy 15:15"> you must say to them, “We were Pharaoh’s slaves in Egypt, but the Lord brought us out of Egypt in a powerful way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:34; Psalms 58:10–11; Deuteronomy 7:19"> And he brought signs and great, devastating wonders on Egypt, on Pharaoh, and on his whole family before our very eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:18; Deuteronomy 6:10; Exodus 13:5"> He delivered us from there so that he could give us the land he had promised our ancestors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Psalms 41:2; Deuteronomy 10:12–13"> The Lord commanded us to obey all these statutes and to revere him so that it may always go well for us and he may preserve us, as he has to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 24:13; Romans 10:3; Leviticus 18:5"> We will be innocent if we carefully keep all these commandments before the Lord our God, just as he demands.”</span>
</div>
</div>
