## Provision for Prophetism

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:29–31; Leviticus 18:26–27; Leviticus 18:30"> When you enter the land the Lord your God is giving you, you must not learn the abhorrent practices of those nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; Exodus 22:18; Leviticus 19:26"> There must never be found among you anyone who sacrifices his son or daughter in the fire, anyone who practices divination, an omen reader, a soothsayer, a sorcerer,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 19:31; 1 Samuel 28:11–14"> one who casts spells, one who conjures up spirits, a practitioner of the occult, or a necromancer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:24; Deuteronomy 9:4; Leviticus 18:27"> Whoever does these things is abhorrent to the Lord and because of these detestable things the Lord your God is about to drive them out from before you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 5:48; Genesis 17:1; Genesis 6:9"> You must be blameless before the Lord your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 14:16; Deuteronomy 18:10; Genesis 20:6"> Those nations that you are about to dispossess listen to omen readers and diviners, but the Lord your God has not given you permission to do such things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Acts 7:37; John 1:45"> The Lord your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among you – from your fellow Israelites; you must listen to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 20:19; Deuteronomy 5:23–28; Deuteronomy 9:10"> This accords with what happened at Horeb in the day of the assembly. You asked the Lord your God: “Please do not make us hear the voice of the Lord our God any more or see this great fire any more lest we die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:28"> The Lord then said to me, “What they have said is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15; John 8:28; John 12:49–50"> I will raise up a prophet like you for them from among their fellow Israelites. I will put my words in his mouth and he will speak to them whatever I command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:22–23; Hebrews 2:3; Hebrews 10:26"> I will personally hold responsible anyone who then pays no attention to the words that prophet speaks in my name.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1–5; Jeremiah 27:15; Jeremiah 23:13–15"> “But if any prophet presumes to speak anything in my name that I have not authorized him to speak, or speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet must die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 5:24; Revelation 2:2"> Now if you say to yourselves, ‘How can we tell that a message is not from the Lord?’ –</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:20; 2 Kings 20:1; Jeremiah 28:1–17"> whenever a prophet speaks in my name and the prediction is not fulfilled, then I have not spoken it; the prophet has presumed to speak it, so you need not fear him.”</span>
</div>
