## Conclusion of the Historical Resume

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Numbers 33:38; Numbers 20:23–28; Numbers 33:30–33"> “During those days the Israelites traveled from Beeroth Bene-Yaaqan to Moserah. There Aaron died and was buried, and his son Eleazar became priest in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:32–34"> From there they traveled to Gudgodah, and from Gudgodah to Jotbathah, a place of flowing streams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:5; Deuteronomy 18:5; Numbers 6:23–26"> At that time the Lord set apart the tribe of Levi to carry the ark of the Lord’s covenant, to stand before the Lord to serve him, and to formulate blessings in his name, as they do to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:28; Numbers 18:20–24; Deuteronomy 18:1–2"> Therefore Levi has no allotment or inheritance among his brothers; the Lord is his inheritance just as the Lord your God told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:18–19; Deuteronomy 9:25; Exodus 34:28"> As for me, I stayed at the mountain as I did the first time, forty days and nights. The Lord listened to me that time as well and decided not to destroy you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 33:1; Exodus 32:34"> Then he said to me, “Get up, set out leading the people so they may go and possess the land I promised to give to their ancestors.”</span>

## An Exhortation to Love Both God and People

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Micah 6:8; Deuteronomy 6:5; Luke 10:27"> Now, Israel, what does the Lord your God require of you except to revere him, to obey all his commandments, to love him, to serve him with all your mind and being,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:39; Deuteronomy 6:24; James 1:25"> and to keep the Lord’s commandments and statutes that I am giving you today for your own good?</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 19:5; Psalms 24:1; Psalms 115:16"> The heavens – indeed the highest heavens – belong to the Lord your God, as does the earth and everything in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:37; Deuteronomy 7:7–8; Romans 9:13–23"> However, only to your ancestors did he show his loving favor, and he chose you, their descendants, from all peoples – as is apparent today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:4; Deuteronomy 30:6; Leviticus 26:41"> Therefore, cleanse your heart and stop being so stubborn!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 10:34; Romans 2:11; Daniel 2:47"> For the Lord your God is God of gods and Lord of lords, the great, mighty, and awesome God who is unbiased and takes no bribe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 146:9; Psalms 68:5; Jeremiah 49:11"> who justly treats the orphan and widow, and who loves resident foreigners, giving them food and clothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 22:21; Leviticus 19:33–34; Luke 17:18"> So you must love the resident foreigner because you were foreigners in the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 6:13; Deuteronomy 13:4; Luke 4:8"> Revere the Lord your God, serve him, be loyal to him and take oaths only in his name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:14; Psalms 106:21–22; Exodus 15:2"> He is the one you should praise; he is your God, the one who has done these great and awesome things for you that you have seen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 46:27; Exodus 1:5; Genesis 15:5"> When your ancestors went down to Egypt, they numbered only seventy, but now the Lord your God has made you as numerous as the stars of the sky.</span>
</div>
