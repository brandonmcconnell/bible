## Healing a Leper

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Luke 17:16; Matthew 8:2–4"> While Jesus was in one of the towns, a man came to him who was covered with leprosy. When he saw Jesus, he bowed down with his face to the ground and begged him, “Lord, if you are willing, you can make me clean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:29–30; John 4:50–53; Hosea 14:4"> So he stretched out his hand and touched him, saying, “I am willing. Be clean!” And immediately the leprosy left him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Leviticus 14:2–32; Luke 17:14; Mark 1:44"> Then he ordered the man to tell no one, but commanded him, “Go and show yourself to a priest, and bring the offering for your cleansing, as Moses commanded, as a testimony to them.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 15:30–31; Luke 12:1; Mark 1:45–2"> But the news about him spread even more, and large crowds were gathering together to hear him and to be healed of their illnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 6:12; Mark 1:35–36; Matthew 14:23"> Yet Jesus himself frequently withdrew to the wilderness and prayed.</span>

## Healing and Forgiving a Paralytic

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 6:19; Luke 8:46; Matthew 11:5"> Now on one of those days, while he was teaching, there were Pharisees and teachers of the law sitting nearby (who had come from every village of Galilee and Judea and from Jerusalem), and the power of the Lord was with him to heal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 2:3–12; Matthew 9:2–8; John 5:5–6"> Just then some men showed up, carrying a paralyzed man on a stretcher. They were trying to bring him in and place him before Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 2:4; Matthew 10:27; Matthew 24:17"> But since they found no way to carry him in because of the crowd, they went up on the roof and let him down on the stretcher through the roof tiles right in front of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 14:9; Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5"> When Jesus saw their faith he said, “Friend, your sins are forgiven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Mark 2:6–7; Luke 7:49"> Then the experts in the law and the Pharisees began to think to themselves, “Who is this man who is uttering blasphemies? Who can forgive sins but God alone?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 24:38; Isaiah 66:18; Psalms 139:2"> When Jesus perceived their hostile thoughts, he said to them, “Why are you raising objections within yourselves?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 2:9; Matthew 9:5"> Which is easier, to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Stand up and walk’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Revelation 1:13; Isaiah 53:11; Luke 8:54"> But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins” – he said to the paralyzed man – “I tell you, stand up, take your stretcher and go home.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 9:24; Luke 5:13; Psalms 107:20–22"> Immediately he stood up before them, picked up the stretcher he had been lying on, and went home, glorifying God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Matthew 12:23; Luke 7:16"> Then astonishment seized them all, and they glorified God. They were filled with awe, saying, “We have seen incredible things today.”</span>
</div>

## The Call of Levi; Eating with Sinners

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Mark 2:13–22; Matthew 9:9–17; John 1:43"> After this, Jesus went out and saw a tax collector named Levi sitting at the tax booth. “Follow me,” he said to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 5:11; Matthew 19:22–27; 1 Kings 19:19–21"> And he got up and followed him, leaving everything behind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 2:15; Luke 15:1; 1 Corinthians 10:27"> Then Levi gave a great banquet in his house for Jesus, and there was a large crowd of tax collectors and others sitting at the table with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 21:28–32; Luke 5:17; Mark 7:3"> But the Pharisees and their experts in the law complained to his disciples, saying, “Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 2:17; Matthew 9:12–13; Jeremiah 8:22"> Jesus answered them, “Those who are well don’t need a physician, but those who are sick do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:10; Luke 15:10; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.”</span>
</div>

## The Superiority of the New

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 9:14–17; Mark 2:18–22; John 3:25"> Then they said to him, “John’s disciples frequently fast and pray, and so do the disciples of the Pharisees, but yours continue to eat and drink.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 3:29; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Isaiah 54:5"> So Jesus said to them, “You cannot make the wedding guests fast while the bridegroom is with them, can you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 17:22; Acts 14:23; Acts 3:21"> But those days are coming, and when the bridegroom is taken from them, at that time they will fast.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 9:16–17; Mark 2:21–22; 2 Corinthians 6:16"> He also told them a parable: “No one tears a patch from a new garment and sews it on an old garment. If he does, he will have torn the new, and the piece from the new will not match the old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 9:13; Psalms 119:83; Joshua 9:4"> And no one pours new wine into old wineskins. If he does, the new wine will burst the skins and will be spilled, and the skins will be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 8:8–13; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Ezekiel 36:26"> Instead new wine must be poured into new wineskins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Hebrews 11:39; Mark 7:7–13; Jeremiah 6:16"> No one after drinking old wine wants the new, for he says, ‘The old is good enough.’”</span>
