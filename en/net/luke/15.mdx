## The Parable of the Compassionate Father

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 21:23–31"> Then Jesus said, “A man had two sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 21:16–17; Mark 12:44; Luke 15:30"> The younger of them said to his father, ‘Father, give me the share of the estate that will belong to me.’ So he divided his assets between them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Jeremiah 2:5; Proverbs 21:17"> After a few days, the younger son gathered together all he had and left on a journey to a distant country, and there he squandered his wealth with a wild lifestyle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:11; Amos 8:9–12; Hosea 2:9–14"> Then after he had spent everything, a severe famine took place in that country, and he began to be in need.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Titus 3:3; 2 Timothy 2:25–26; Isaiah 1:5"> So he went and worked for one of the citizens of that country, who sent him to his fields to feed pigs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 6:19–21; Lamentations 4:5; Hosea 12:1"> He was longing to eat the carob pods the pigs were eating, but no one gave him anything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:28; Ephesians 2:4–5; Luke 15:18–19"> But when he came to his senses he said, ‘How many of my father’s hired workers have food enough to spare, but here I am dying from hunger!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Lamentations 3:40; 1 John 1:8–10; Luke 15:21"> I will get up and go to my father and say to him, “Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 42:6; Luke 5:8; Psalms 84:10"> I am no longer worthy to be called your son; treat me like one of your hired workers.”’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 103:10–13; Psalms 86:5; Psalms 86:15"> So he got up and went to his father. But while he was still a long way from home his father saw him, and his heart went out to him; he ran and hugged his son and kissed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 51:4; Luke 15:18–19; Jeremiah 3:13"> Then his son said to him, ‘Father, I have sinned against heaven and against you; I am no longer worthy to be called your son.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 41:42; Esther 8:2; Revelation 6:11"> But the father said to his slaves, ‘Hurry! Bring the best robe, and put it on him! Put a ring on his finger and sandals on his feet!</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 25:6; Matthew 22:2–14; Psalms 63:5"> Bring the fattened calf and kill it! Let us eat and celebrate,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 2:5; Ezekiel 34:16; Luke 19:10"> because this son of mine was dead, and is alive again – he was lost and is found!’ So they began to celebrate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 150:4; Jeremiah 31:4; Luke 15:11–12"> “Now his older son was in the field. As he came and approached the house, he heard music and dancing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So he called one of the slaves and asked what was happening.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Acts 9:17; Philemon 1:16; Acts 22:13"> The slave replied, ‘Your brother has returned, and your father has killed the fattened calf because he got his son back safe and sound.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jonah 4:9; 1 Samuel 18:8; Jonah 4:1–4"> But the older son became angry and refused to go in. His father came out and appealed to him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 18:11–12; Zechariah 7:3; Isaiah 65:5"> but he answered his father, ‘Look! These many years I have worked like a slave for you, and I never disobeyed your commands. Yet you never gave me even a goat so that I could celebrate with my friends!</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 29:3; Luke 15:12–13; Luke 15:32"> But when this son of yours came back, who has devoured your assets with prostitutes, you killed the fattened calf for him!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Mark 7:27–28; Romans 9:4; Romans 11:1"> Then the father said to him, ‘Son, you are always with me, and everything that belongs to me is yours.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Ephesians 2:1–10; Luke 15:24; Isaiah 35:10"> It was appropriate to celebrate and be glad, for your brother was dead, and is alive; he was lost and is found.’”</span>
