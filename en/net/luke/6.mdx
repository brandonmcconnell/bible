## Healing a Withered Hand

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 3:1–6; Matthew 12:9–14; Luke 4:31"> On another Sabbath, Jesus entered the synagogue and was teaching. Now a man was there whose right hand was withered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 20:20; Psalms 38:12; Luke 13:14"> The experts in the law and the Pharisees watched Jesus closely to see if he would heal on the Sabbath, so that they could find a reason to accuse him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 42:4; Matthew 9:4; Luke 5:22"> But he knew their thoughts, and said to the man who had the withered hand, “Get up and stand here.” So he rose and stood there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Mark 3:4; Matthew 12:12–13; Luke 14:3"> Then Jesus said to them, “I ask you, is it lawful to do good on the Sabbath or to do evil, to save a life or to destroy it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 4:6–7; Mark 3:5; John 5:8"> After looking around at them all, he said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” The man did so, and his hand was restored.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–2; Acts 5:33; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> But they were filled with mindless rage and began debating with one another what they would do to Jesus.</span>
</div>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 3:13–19; Matthew 9:36–4; Revelation 21:14"> When morning came, he called his disciples and chose twelve of them, whom he also named apostles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Matthew 4:21; Matthew 4:18"> Simon (whom he named Peter), and his brother Andrew; and James, John, Philip, Bartholomew,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 9:9; Mark 3:18; Acts 1:13"> Matthew, Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, Simon who was called the Zealot,</span>

## The Sermon on the Plain

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 11:21; Matthew 4:23–25; Psalms 107:17–20"> Then he came down with them and stood on a level place. And a large number of his disciples had gathered along with a vast multitude from all over Judea, from Jerusalem, They came to hear him and to be healed of their diseases,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 17:15; Matthew 15:22; Acts 5:16"> and those who suffered from unclean spirits were cured.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Mark 3:10; Acts 5:15–16"> The whole crowd was trying to touch him, because power was coming out from him and healing them all.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 6:35; Matthew 5:6; James 1:12"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed are you who hunger now, for you will be satisfied.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed are you who weep now, for you will laugh.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; Matthew 10:22; 1 Peter 4:12–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Blessed are you when people hate you, and when they exclude you and insult you and reject you as evil on account of the Son of Man!</span></span>

<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 15:7–10; 2 Corinthians 9:6–14; Matthew 6:12"> Give to everyone who asks you, and do not ask for your possessions back from the person who takes them away.</span>

<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 5:44–45; John 13:35; Proverbs 19:17"> But love your enemies, and do good, and lend, expecting nothing back. Then your reward will be great, and you will be sons of the Most High, because he is kind to ungrateful and evil people.</span>

## Do Not Judge Others

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Ephesians 4:32; James 5:9; Matthew 7:1–5"> “Do not judge, and you will not be judged; do not condemn, and you will not be condemned; forgive, and you will be forgiven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:6–8; Deuteronomy 15:10; Mark 4:24"> Give, and it will be given to you: A good measure, pressed down, shaken together, running over, will be poured into your lap. For the measure you use will be the measure you receive.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 15:14; 2 Timothy 3:13; 1 Timothy 6:3–5"> He also told them a parable: “Someone who is blind cannot lead another who is blind, can he? Won’t they both fall into a pit?</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 13:16; Matthew 10:24–25; John 15:20"> A disciple is not greater than his teacher, but everyone when fully trained will be like his teacher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 8:7; James 1:24; Romans 2:1"> Why do you see the speck in your brother’s eye, but fail to see the beam of wood in your own?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 2:21–29; 1 Thessalonians 2:10–12; Psalms 51:9–13"> How can you say to your brother, ‘Brother, let me remove the speck from your eye,’ while you yourself don’t see the beam in your own? You hypocrite! First remove the beam from your own eye, and then you can see clearly to remove the speck from your brother’s eye.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:21; Matthew 12:33; Matthew 7:16–20"> “For no good tree bears bad fruit, nor again does a bad tree bear good fruit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 12:33; James 3:12; Galatians 5:19–23"> for each tree is known by its own fruit. For figs are not gathered from thorns, nor are grapes picked from brambles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 4:23; Matthew 12:34–37; Ephesians 4:29"> The good person out of the good treasury of his heart produces good, and the evil person out of his evil treasury produces evil, for his mouth speaks from what fills his heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; Malachi 1:6; Matthew 7:21–23"> “Why do you call me ‘Lord, Lord,’ and don’t do what I tell you?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="James 1:22–25; Matthew 7:24–27; Luke 11:28"> “Everyone who comes to me and listens to my words and puts them into practice – I will show you what he is like:</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:10–15; Psalms 62:2; Matthew 7:25–26"> He is like a man building a house, who dug down deep, and laid the foundation on bedrock. When a flood came, the river burst against that house but could not shake it, because it had been well built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; James 1:22–26; Matthew 23:3"> But the person who hears and does not put my words into practice is like a man who built a house on the ground without a foundation. When the river burst against that house, it collapsed immediately, and was utterly destroyed!”</span>
</div>
</div>
