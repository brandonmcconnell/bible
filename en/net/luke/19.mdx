## The Parable of the Ten Minas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 17:20; Acts 1:6; 2 Thessalonians 2:1–3"> While the people were listening to these things, Jesus proceeded to tell a parable, because he was near to Jerusalem, and because they thought that the kingdom of God was going to appear immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–30; Luke 19:12–27; Luke 20:9"> Therefore he said, “A nobleman went to a distant country to receive for himself a kingdom and then return.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 25:14–15; John 12:26; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11"> And he summoned ten of his slaves, gave them ten minas, and said to them, ‘Do business with these until I come back.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:7; Acts 7:51–52; Acts 3:14–15"> But his citizens hated him and sent a delegation after him, saying, ‘We do not want this man to be king over us!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:1–5; Romans 14:10–12; Psalms 2:4–6"> When he returned after receiving the kingdom, he summoned these slaves to whom he had given the money. He wanted to know how much they had earned by trading.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="James 2:18–26; 2 Timothy 4:7–8; 1 Corinthians 15:10"> So the first one came before him and said, ‘Sir, your mina has made ten minas more.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 16:10; Matthew 25:21; Revelation 2:26–29"> And the king said to him, ‘Well done, good slave! Because you have been faithful in a very small matter, you will have authority over ten cities.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 4:20; Matthew 13:23; 2 Corinthians 8:12"> Then the second one came and said, ‘Sir, your mina has made five minas.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; 1 Corinthians 15:41–42; 1 Corinthians 3:8"> So the king said to him, ‘And you are to be over five cities.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 25:24; Proverbs 26:13–16; James 4:17"> Then another slave came and said, ‘Sir, here is your mina that I put away for safekeeping in a piece of cloth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 25:24–25; Romans 8:7; 1 John 4:18"> For I was afraid of you, because you are a severe man. You withdraw what you did not deposit and reap what you did not sow.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Matthew 12:37; Job 15:5–6"> The king said to him, ‘I will judge you by your own words, you wicked slave! So you knew, did you, that I was a severe man, withdrawing what I didn’t deposit and reaping what I didn’t sow?</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 22:25–27; Romans 2:4–5; Deuteronomy 23:19–20"> Why then didn’t you put my money in the bank, so that when I returned I could have collected it with interest?’</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 16:2; Luke 12:20"> And he said to his attendants, ‘Take the mina from him, and give it to the one who has ten.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 16:2; 2 Samuel 7:19; Isaiah 55:8–9"> But they said to him, ‘Sir, he has ten minas already!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 John 1:8; Luke 8:18; Matthew 21:43"> ‘I tell you that everyone who has will be given more, but from the one who does not have, even what he has will be taken away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 19:14; Psalms 21:8–9; Matthew 22:7"> But as for these enemies of mine who did not want me to be their king, bring them here and slaughter them in front of me!’”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Triumphal Entry

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 9:51; Hebrews 12:2; 1 Peter 4:1"> After Jesus had said this, he continued on ahead, going up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Matthew 21:1–11"> Now when he approached Bethphage and Bethany, at the place called the Mount of Olives, he sent two of the disciples,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 22:8–13; Luke 19:32; 1 Samuel 10:2–9"> telling them, “Go to the village ahead of you. When you enter it, you will find a colt tied there that has never been ridden. Untie it and bring it here.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Psalms 24:1; Psalms 50:10–12; Mark 11:3–6"> If anyone asks you, ‘Why are you untying it?’ just say, ‘The Lord needs it.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 22:13"> So those who were sent ahead found it exactly as he had told them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> As they were untying the colt, its owners asked them, “Why are you untying that colt?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; John 10:35; 2 Corinthians 8:9"> They replied, “The Lord needs it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 11:7–8; Matthew 21:7; John 12:14"> Then they brought it to Jesus, threw their cloaks on the colt, and had Jesus get on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Kings 9:13; Matthew 21:8"> As he rode along, they spread their cloaks on the road.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 12:12–13; Ezra 3:10–13; Matthew 21:1"> As he approached the road leading down from the Mount of Olives, the whole crowd of his disciples began to rejoice and praise God with a loud voice for all the mighty works they had seen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Luke 13:35; Psalms 118:22–26"> “Blessed is the king who comes in the name of the Lord! Peace in heaven and glory in the highest!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Acts 4:1–2; Matthew 23:13; Acts 4:16–18"> But some of the Pharisees in the crowd said to him, “Teacher, rebuke your disciples.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:11; Isaiah 55:12; Matthew 3:9"> He answered, “I tell you, if they keep silent, the very stones will cry out!”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Weeps for Jerusalem under Judgment

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 13:34–35; John 11:35; Psalms 119:136"> Now when Jesus approached and saw the city, he wept over it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:29; Hebrews 10:26–29; Isaiah 48:18"> saying, “If you had only known on this day, even you, the things that make for peace! But now they are hidden from your eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Luke 21:20–24; Isaiah 29:1–4; Ezekiel 4:2"> For the days will come upon you when your enemies will build an embankment against you and surround you and close in on you from every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Luke 21:6; Luke 13:34–35; John 3:18–21"> They will demolish you – you and your children within your walls – and they will not leave within you one stone on top of another, because you did not recognize the time of your visitation from God.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Cleansing the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 21:12–13; Mark 11:15–17; John 2:13–17"> Then Jesus entered the temple courts and began to drive out those who were selling things there,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 56:7; Jeremiah 7:11; Psalms 93:5"> saying to them, “It is written, ‘My house will be a house of prayer,’ but you have turned it into a den of robbers!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 11:18; Matthew 26:55; Mark 11:27–33"> Jesus was teaching daily in the temple courts. The chief priests and the experts in the law and the prominent leaders among the people were seeking to assassinate him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:3; Acts 16:14; John 7:46–49"> but they could not find a way to do it, for all the people hung on his words.</span>
