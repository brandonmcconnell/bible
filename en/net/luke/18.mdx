## The Parable of the Pharisee and Tax Collector

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 16:15; Proverbs 30:12; Isaiah 65:5"> Jesus also told this parable to some who were confident that they were righteous and looked down on everyone else.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 21:31–32; 1 Kings 8:30; 2 Kings 20:5"> “Two men went up to the temple to pray, one a Pharisee and the other a tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:5; Isaiah 65:5; Revelation 3:17"> The Pharisee stood and prayed about himself like this: ‘God, I thank you that I am not like other people: extortionists, unrighteous people, adulterers – or even like this tax collector.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 11:42; Matthew 23:23–24; Matthew 9:14"> I fast twice a week; I give a tenth of everything I get.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezra 9:6; 1 John 1:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:15"> The tax collector, however, stood far off and would not even look up to heaven, but beat his breast and said, ‘God, be merciful to me, sinner that I am!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 14:11; Luke 16:15; James 4:10"> I tell you that this man went down to his home justified rather than the Pharisee. For everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, but he who humbles himself will be exalted.”</span>
</div>

## The Wealthy Ruler

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Mark 10:17–31; Acts 16:30; Matthew 19:16–30"> Now a certain ruler asked him, “Good teacher, what must I do to inherit eternal life?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 15:14–16; James 1:17; Hebrews 7:26"> Jesus said to him, “Why do you call me good? No one is good except God alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:12–17; Deuteronomy 5:16–21; Romans 13:9"> You know the commandments: ‘Do not commit adultery, do not murder, do not steal, do not give false testimony, honor your father and mother.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Philippians 3:6; Romans 10:2–3; Mark 10:20–21"> The man replied, “I have wholeheartedly obeyed all these laws since my youth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 12:33; 1 Timothy 6:18–19; Matthew 19:21"> When Jesus heard this, he said to him, “One thing you still lack. Sell all that you have and give the money to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 33:31; 1 John 2:15; Job 31:24–25"> But when the man heard this he became very sad, for he was extremely wealthy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Proverbs 30:9; James 2:5–7; Mark 10:23–27"> When Jesus noticed this, he said, “How hard it is for the rich to enter the kingdom of God!</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 23:24"> In fact, it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter the kingdom of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 13:23"> Those who heard this said, “Then who can be saved?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Jeremiah 32:17; Matthew 19:26; Luke 1:37"> He replied, “What is impossible for mere humans is possible for God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 10:28; Philippians 3:7; Matthew 19:27"> And Peter said, “Look, we have left everything we own to follow you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Mark 10:29–31; Matthew 10:37–39; Luke 14:26–28"> Then Jesus said to them, “I tell you the truth, there is no one who has left home or wife or brothers or parents or children for the sake of God’s kingdom</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Hebrews 13:5–6; Job 42:10; Revelation 2:17"> who will not receive many times more in this age – and in the age to come, eternal life.”</span>
</div>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 20:17–19; Isaiah 53:1–12; Psalms 22:1–31"> Then Jesus took the twelve aside and said to them, “Look, we are going up to Jerusalem, and everything that is written about the Son of Man by the prophets will be accomplished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 23:11; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 53:3"> For he will be handed over to the Gentiles; he will be mocked, mistreated, and spat on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Luke 24:7; Matthew 27:63"> They will flog him severely and kill him. Yet on the third day he will rise again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 9:45; Mark 9:32; Luke 24:25"> But the twelve understood none of these things. This saying was hidden from them, and they did not grasp what Jesus meant.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Healing a Blind Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 20:29–34; Mark 10:46–52; Luke 16:20–21"> As Jesus approached Jericho, a blind man was sitting by the road begging.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 21:10–11; Luke 15:26"> When he heard a crowd going by, he asked what was going on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 2:23; John 19:19; John 12:35–36"> They told him, “Jesus the Nazarene is passing by.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 9:27; Luke 18:39; Matthew 15:22"> So he called out, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 32:26–28; Psalms 141:1; Luke 18:15"> And those who were in front scolded him to get him to be quiet, but he shouted even more, “Son of David, have mercy on me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 20:31–34; Mark 10:48–52"> So Jesus stopped and ordered the beggar to be brought to him. When the man came near, Jesus asked him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 Kings 3:5–15; Matthew 20:21–22; Philippians 4:6"> “What do you want me to do for you?” He replied, “Lord, let me see again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Matthew 8:3; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19"> Jesus said to him, “Receive your sight; your faith has healed you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 146:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10–12; Isaiah 43:21"> And immediately he regained his sight and followed Jesus, praising God. When all the people saw it, they too gave praise to God.</span>
