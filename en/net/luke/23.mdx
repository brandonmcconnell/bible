## Jesus Brought Before Herod

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 5:37; Luke 13:1"> Now when Pilate heard this, he asked whether the man was a Galilean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 3:1; Luke 13:31; Matthew 14:1"> When he learned that he was from Herod’s jurisdiction, he sent him over to Herod, who also happened to be in Jerusalem at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 9:7–9; Matthew 14:1; Mark 6:14"> When Herod saw Jesus, he was very glad, for he had long desired to see him, because he had heard about him and was hoping to see him perform some miraculous sign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 27:14; Matthew 7:6; 1 Peter 2:23"> So Herod questioned him at considerable length; Jesus gave him no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 11:53; Luke 23:14–15; Luke 23:5"> The chief priests and the experts in the law were there, vehemently accusing him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Matthew 27:27–30; Psalms 69:19–20"> Even Herod with his soldiers treated him with contempt and mocked him. Then, dressing him in elegant clothes, Herod sent him back to Pilate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 4:27; Revelation 17:13–14; Psalms 83:4–6"> That very day Herod and Pilate became friends with each other, for prior to this they had been enemies.</span>
</div>

## Jesus Brought Before the Crowd

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Mark 15:14; Matthew 27:21–23; John 18:38"> Then Pilate called together the chief priests, the rulers, and the people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 6:4; Hebrews 7:26; Luke 23:1–2"> and said to them, “You brought me this man as one who was misleading the people. When I examined him before you, I did not find this man guilty of anything you accused him of doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Neither did Herod, for he sent him back to us. Look, he has done nothing deserving death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Luke 23:22; Mark 15:15"> I will therefore have him flogged and release him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <sup className="fn"><a href="#fn-a" id="fnref-a" data-fn="[[EMPTY]]">a</a></sup></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 21:36; Acts 3:14; Acts 22:22"> But they all shouted out together, “Take this man away! Release Barabbas for us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Luke 23:5; Luke 23:2"> (This was a man who had been thrown into prison for an insurrection started in the city, and for murder.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 15:15; Matthew 14:8–9; Matthew 27:19"> Pilate addressed them once again because he wanted to release Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 27:22–25; Mark 15:13; John 19:15"> But they kept on shouting, “Crucify, crucify him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 23:14; Luke 23:20; 1 Peter 3:18"> A third time he said to them, “Why? What wrong has he done? I have found him guilty of no crime deserving death. I will therefore flog him and release him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 11:8; Psalms 57:4; Psalms 22:12–13"> But they were insistent, demanding with loud shouts that he be crucified. And their shouts prevailed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 27:26; Exodus 23:2; Proverbs 17:15"> So Pilate decided that their demand should be granted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 3:14; Mark 15:6–7; Mark 15:15"> He released the man they asked for, who had been thrown in prison for insurrection and murder. But he handed Jesus over to their will.</span>
</div>

## The Crucifixion

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 27:32–44; Mark 15:21–32; Acts 6:9"> As they led him away, they seized Simon of Cyrene, who was coming in from the country. They placed the cross on his back and made him carry it behind Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 8:52; Luke 8:2; Mark 15:40"> A great number of the people followed him, among them women who were mourning and wailing for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:4; Song of Solomon 5:8; Song of Solomon 1:5"> But Jesus turned to them and said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for me, but weep for yourselves and for your children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 24:19; Luke 21:23–24; Mark 13:17–19"> For this is certain: The days are coming when they will say, ‘Blessed are the barren, the wombs that never bore children, and the breasts that never nursed!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Revelation 6:16; Hosea 10:8; Isaiah 2:19"> Then they will begin to say to the mountains, ‘Fall on us!’ and to the hills, ‘Cover us!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 4:17–18; John 15:6; Proverbs 11:31"> For if such things are done when the wood is green, what will happen when it is dry?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 15:27; Isaiah 53:12; Matthew 27:38"> Two other criminals were also led away to be executed with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 15:22–23; John 19:17–18; John 18:32"> So when they came to the place that is called “The Skull,” they crucified him there, along with the criminals, one on his right and one on his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:44; Acts 7:60; 1 Peter 3:9"> &#91;But Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they don’t know what they are doing.”&#93; Then they threw dice to divide his clothes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Psalms 22:17; Isaiah 42:1; 1 Peter 2:4"> The people also stood there watching, but the rulers ridiculed him, saying, “He saved others. Let him save himself if he is the Christ of God, his chosen one!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Matthew 27:48; Matthew 27:34"> The soldiers also mocked him, coming up and offering him sour wine,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> and saying, “If you are the king of the Jews, save yourself!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:26; Matthew 27:37; John 19:19–22"> There was also an inscription over him, “This is the king of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Matthew 27:44; Mark 15:32; Luke 23:35"> One of the criminals who was hanging there railed at him, saying, “Aren’t you the Christ? Save yourself and us!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Ephesians 5:11; Luke 12:5; Revelation 15:4"> But the other rebuked him, saying, “Don’t you fear God, since you are under the same sentence of condemnation?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Leviticus 26:40–41; Ezra 9:13; 1 Peter 1:19"> And we rightly so, for we are getting what we deserve for what we did, but this man has done nothing wrong.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Romans 10:9–14; Acts 16:31; 1 John 5:11–13"> Then he said, “Jesus, remember me when you come in your kingdom.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Revelation 2:7; Psalms 50:15; 1 Timothy 1:15–16"> And Jesus said to him, “I tell you the truth, today you will be with me in paradise.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Matthew 27:45; Mark 15:33–34; Amos 8:9"> It was now about noon, and darkness came over the whole land until three in the afternoon,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Hebrews 10:19–22; Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 9:3–8"> because the sun’s light failed. The temple curtain was torn in two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 31:5; Acts 7:59; John 19:30"> Then Jesus, calling out with a loud voice, said, “Father, into your hands I commit my spirit!” And after he said this he breathed his last.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 27:54; Mark 15:39; Luke 23:41"> Now when the centurion saw what had happened, he praised God and said, “Certainly this man was innocent!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Luke 18:13; Acts 2:37; Jeremiah 31:19"> And all the crowds that had assembled for this spectacle, when they saw what had taken place, returned home beating their breasts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 8:2; Psalms 38:11; Luke 23:55"> And all those who knew Jesus stood at a distance, and the women who had followed him from Galilee saw these things.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Burial

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 19:38–42; Acts 11:24; Acts 10:22"> Now there was a man named Joseph who was a member of the council, a good and righteous man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 2:25; Luke 2:38; Isaiah 8:12"> (He had not consented to their plan and action.) He was from the Judean town of Arimathea, and was looking forward to the kingdom of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 19:38–42"> He went to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Matthew 27:59–60; Isaiah 53:9; Mark 15:46"> Then he took it down, wrapped it in a linen cloth, and placed it in a tomb cut out of the rock, where no one had yet been buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:31; Mark 15:42"> It was the day of preparation and the Sabbath was beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47"> The women who had accompanied Jesus from Galilee followed, and they saw the tomb and how his body was laid in it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Luke 24:1; Mark 16:1; Deuteronomy 5:14"> Then they returned and prepared aromatic spices and perfumes. On the Sabbath they rested according to the commandment.</span>
