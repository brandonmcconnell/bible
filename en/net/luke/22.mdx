<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 12:18; Mark 14:12–16; 1 Corinthians 5:7"> Then the day for the feast of Unleavened Bread came, on which the Passover lamb had to be sacrificed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Mark 14:13–16; Acts 4:19; Acts 4:13"> Jesus sent Peter and John, saying, “Go and prepare the Passover for us to eat.”</span>

## A Final Discourse

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 41:9; John 13:26; John 13:21–22"> “But look, the hand of the one who betrays me is with me on the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 2:23; Psalms 109:6–15; Psalms 55:12–15"> For the Son of Man is to go just as it has been determined, but woe to that man by whom he is betrayed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 13:22–25; Mark 14:19; Matthew 26:22"> So they began to question one another as to which of them it could possibly be who would do this.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 9:46; Mark 9:34; Mark 10:37–41"> A dispute also started among them over which of them was to be regarded as the greatest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 20:25–28; Mark 10:41–45"> So Jesus said to them, “The kings of the Gentiles lord it over them, and those in authority over them are called ‘benefactors.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 5:5; Luke 9:48; Matthew 18:3–5"> Not so with you; instead the one who is greatest among you must become like the youngest, and the leader like the one who serves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 20:28; Luke 12:37; John 13:5–16"> For who is greater, the one who is seated at the table, or the one who serves? Is it not the one who is seated at the table? But I am among you as one who serves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Hebrews 2:18; Hebrews 4:15; Acts 1:25"> “You are the ones who have remained with me in my trials.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 25:34; 2 Timothy 2:12; James 2:5"> Thus I grant to you a kingdom, just as my Father granted to me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 19:28; Matthew 8:11; Revelation 2:26–27"> that you may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and you will sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Zechariah 3:1; Job 1:6–12"> “Simon, Simon, pay attention! Satan has demanded to have you all, to sift you like wheat,</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8–10; John 21:15–17; John 17:9–11"> but I have prayed for you, Simon, that your faith may not fail. When you have turned back, strengthen your brothers.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 14:31; Matthew 26:33–35; Mark 14:29"> But Peter said to him, “Lord, I am ready to go with you both to prison and to death!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 13:38; Matthew 26:34; Mark 14:30"> Jesus replied, “I tell you, Peter, the rooster will not crow today until you have denied three times that you know me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 10:9–10; Luke 10:4; Psalms 23:1"> Then Jesus said to them, “When I sent you out with no money bag, or traveler’s bag, or sandals, you didn’t lack anything, did you?” They replied, “Nothing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Matthew 10:22–25; 1 Thessalonians 3:4; 1 Thessalonians 2:14–15"> He said to them, “But now, the one who has a money bag must take it, and likewise a traveler’s bag too. And the one who has no sword must sell his cloak and buy one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Luke 23:32; 2 Corinthians 5:21"> For I tell you that this scripture must be fulfilled in me, ‘And he was counted with the transgressors.’ For what is written about me is being fulfilled.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Luke 22:49; Matthew 26:52–54; John 18:36"> So they said, “Look, Lord, here are two swords.” Then he told them, “It is enough.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## On the Mount of Olives

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 21:37; John 18:1–2; Matthew 21:1"> Then Jesus went out and made his way, as he customarily did, to the Mount of Olives, and the disciples followed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 6:13; Matthew 26:36–46; Luke 22:46"> When he came to the place, he said to them, “Pray that you will not fall into temptation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 26:39; Mark 14:35; Luke 18:11"> He went away from them about a stone’s throw, knelt down, and prayed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:27–28; Matthew 26:39; Matthew 26:42"> “Father, if you are willing, take this cup away from me. Yet not my will but yours be done.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Matthew 4:11; Matthew 26:53; Psalms 91:11–12"> &#91;Then an angel from heaven appeared to him and strengthened him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Lamentations 1:12; Hebrews 5:7; Psalms 22:12–21"> And in his anguish he prayed more earnestly, and his sweat was like drops of blood falling to the ground.&#93;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Mark 14:40–41; Matthew 26:43; Matthew 26:40"> When he got up from prayer, he came to the disciples and found them sleeping, exhausted from grief.</span>

## Betrayal and Arrest

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Mark 14:41–50; John 18:2–11; Luke 22:3–6"> While he was still speaking, suddenly a crowd appeared, and the man named Judas, one of the twelve, was leading them. He walked up to Jesus to kiss him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Mark 14:44–46; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; Proverbs 27:6"> But Jesus said to him, “Judas, would you betray the Son of Man with a kiss?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 22:38"> When those who were around him saw what was about to happen, they said, “Lord, should we use our swords?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:10–11; Mark 14:47; Romans 12:19"> Then one of them struck the high priest’s slave, cutting off his right ear.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Romans 12:21; 2 Corinthians 10:1; 1 Peter 2:21–23"> But Jesus said, “Enough of this!” And he touched the man’s ear and healed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Luke 22:4; Mark 14:48–49; John 17:12"> Then Jesus said to the chief priests, the officers of the temple guard, and the elders who had come out to get him, “Have you come out with swords and clubs like you would against an outlaw?</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 12:27; Ephesians 6:12; Acts 26:18"> Day after day when I was with you in the temple courts, you did not arrest me. But this is your hour, and that of the power of darkness!”</span>
</div>

## Jesus’ Condemnation and Peter’s Denials

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Mark 14:53–54; Matthew 26:57–58; John 18:12–17"> Then they arrested Jesus, led him away, and brought him into the high priest’s house. But Peter was following at a distance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Psalms 26:4–5; 2 Corinthians 6:15–17; Mark 14:66–72"> When they had made a fire in the middle of the courtyard and sat down together, Peter sat down among them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 18:17; Mark 14:6; Mark 14:17"> Then a slave girl, seeing him as he sat in the firelight, stared at him and said, “This man was with him too!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="1 John 1:9; John 18:25; Matthew 26:70"> But Peter denied it: “Woman, I don’t know him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 18:25; Matthew 26:71–72; Mark 14:69–70"> Then a little later someone else saw him and said, “You are one of them too.” But Peter said, “Man, I am not!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 18:26–27; Mark 14:69–70; Matthew 26:73–74"> And after about an hour still another insisted, “Certainly this man was with him, because he too is a Galilean.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Matthew 26:74–75; Luke 22:34; Mark 14:71–72"> But Peter said, “Man, I don’t know what you’re talking about!” At that moment, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Luke 22:34; Isaiah 57:15–18; Jeremiah 31:18–20"> Then the Lord turned and looked straight at Peter, and Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he had said to him, “Before a rooster crows today, you will deny me three times.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Matthew 26:75; 1 Corinthians 10:12; Mark 14:72"> And he went outside and wept bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="John 18:22; Mark 14:55–65; Job 16:9–10"> Now the men who were holding Jesus under guard began to mock him and beat him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 16:25; Judges 16:21"> They blindfolded him and asked him repeatedly, “Prophesy! Who hit you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="Luke 12:10; Matthew 12:31–32; Matthew 27:39"> They also said many other things against him, reviling him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Matthew 27:1; Mark 15:1; Acts 22:5"> When day came, the council of the elders of the people gathered together, both the chief priests and the experts in the law. Then they led Jesus away to their council</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Matthew 11:3–5; John 8:43–45; Matthew 26:63–68"> and said, “If you are the Christ, tell us.” But he said to them, “If I tell you, you will not believe,</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Luke 20:41–44; Luke 20:3–7"> and if I ask you, you will not answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v69" data-v="69" data-xref="Daniel 7:13–14; Psalms 110:1; Mark 16:19"> But from now on the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="Luke 23:3; Matthew 27:11; Matthew 26:64"> So they all said, “Are you the Son of God, then?” He answered them, “You say that I am.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v71" data-v="71" data-xref="Matthew 26:65–66; Mark 14:63–64"> Then they said, “Why do we need further testimony? We have heard it ourselves from his own lips!”</span>
</div>
