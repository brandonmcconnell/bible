<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:2; Luke 5:31; Hebrews 4:16"> But when the crowds found out, they followed him. He welcomed them, spoke to them about the kingdom of God, and cured those who needed healing.</span>

<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 17:2; Mark 9:2–3; Exodus 34:29–35"> As he was praying, the appearance of his face was transformed, and his clothes became very bright, a brilliant white.</span>

<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; John 1:14; Daniel 10:9"> Now Peter and those with him were quite sleepy, but as they became fully awake, they saw his glory and the two men standing with him.</span>

<div className="bp">
