<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 15:28; Romans 3:1–3; Matthew 8:10"> When Jesus heard this, he was amazed at him. He turned and said to the crowd that followed him, “I tell you, not even in Israel have I found such faith!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 4:50–53; Matthew 8:13; Matthew 15:28"> So when those who had been sent returned to the house, they found the slave well.</span>

## Raising a Widow’s Son

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 10:38"> Soon afterward Jesus went to a town called Nain, and his disciples and a large crowd went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 8:42; Luke 8:52; 1 Kings 17:9"> As he approached the town gate, a man who had died was being carried out, the only son of his mother (who was a widow ), and a large crowd from the town was with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 103:13; Isaiah 63:9; Psalms 86:15"> When the Lord saw her, he had compassion for her and said to her, “Do not weep.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 5:25; John 11:43–44; Acts 9:40–41"> Then he came up and touched the bier, and those who carried it stood still. He said, “Young man, I say to you, get up!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 13:21; 2 Kings 4:32–37; 1 Kings 17:23–24"> So the dead man sat up and began to speak, and Jesus gave him back to his mother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 9:8; Luke 7:39; Luke 1:68"> Fear seized them all, and they began to glorify God, saying, “A great prophet has appeared among us!” and “God has come to help his people!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 4:24; Mark 6:14; Matthew 9:26"> This report about Jesus circulated throughout Judea and all the surrounding country.</span>
</div>

## Jesus and John the Baptist

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 3:26; Matthew 11:2–19"> John’s disciples informed him about all these things. So John called two of his disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 9:6–7; John 4:25; Genesis 22:18"> and sent them to Jesus to ask, “Are you the one who is to come, or should we look for another?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 7:19"> When the men came to Jesus, they said, “John the Baptist has sent us to you to ask, ‘Are you the one who is to come, or should we look for another?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 3:10; Psalms 90:7–9; Hebrews 12:6"> At that very time Jesus cured many people of diseases, sicknesses, and evil spirits, and granted sight to many who were blind.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 35:5–6; Isaiah 61:1–3; Luke 4:18"> So he answered them, “Go tell John what you have seen and heard: The blind see, the lame walk, lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised, the poor have good news proclaimed to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 11:6; 1 Peter 2:7–8; Romans 9:32–33"> Blessed is anyone who takes no offense at me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Ephesians 4:14; John 1:23; Luke 1:80"> When John’s messengers had gone, Jesus began to speak to the crowds about John: “What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken by the wind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 59:17; Esther 8:15; Esther 1:3"> What did you go out to see? A man dressed in fancy clothes? Look, those who wear fancy clothes and live in luxury are in kings’ courts!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 1:76; John 5:35; Luke 20:6"> What did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I tell you, and more than a prophet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Malachi 3:1; Luke 1:76; Mark 1:2"> This is the one about whom it is written, ‘Look, I am sending my messenger ahead of you, who will prepare your way before you.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 13:16–17; Matthew 11:11; Colossians 1:25–27"> I tell you, among those born of women no one is greater than John. Yet the one who is least in the kingdom of God is greater than he is.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Luke 3:12; Luke 7:35; Acts 18:25"> (Now all the people who heard this, even the tax collectors, acknowledged God’s justice, because they had been baptized with John’s baptism.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Luke 13:34; 2 Corinthians 6:1; Matthew 22:35"> However, the Pharisees and the experts in religious law rejected God’s purpose for themselves, because they had not been baptized by John. )</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Lamentations 2:13; Mark 4:30"> “To what then should I compare the people of this generation, and what are they like?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Matthew 11:16–19; Jeremiah 5:3–5; Proverbs 17:16"> They are like children sitting in the marketplace and calling out to one another,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘We played the flute for you, yet you did not dance;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">we wailed in mourning, yet you did not weep.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Luke 1:15; Mark 1:6; John 8:52"> For John the Baptist has come eating no bread and drinking no wine, and you say, ‘He has a demon!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Luke 15:2; Luke 7:36; John 12:2"> The Son of Man has come eating and drinking, and you say, ‘Look at him, a glutton and a drunk, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 8:32–36; Matthew 11:19; Hosea 14:9"> But wisdom is vindicated by all her children.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Anointing

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:5–6; Luke 14:1"> Now one of the Pharisees asked Jesus to have dinner with him, so he went into the Pharisee’s house and took his place at the table.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Mark 14:3–9; Matthew 26:6–13; Matthew 21:31"> Then when a woman of that town, who was a sinner, learned that Jesus was dining at the Pharisee’s house, she brought an alabaster jar of perfumed oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Psalms 126:5–6; Psalms 51:17; Genesis 18:4"> As she stood behind him at his feet, weeping, she began to wet his feet with her tears. She wiped them with her hair, kissed them, and anointed them with the perfumed oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 7:16; Luke 15:2; Mark 7:21"> Now when the Pharisee who had invited him saw this, he said to himself, “If this man were a prophet, he would know who and what kind of woman this is who is touching him, that she is a sinner.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 16:19; Luke 5:31; Ezekiel 33:31"> So Jesus answered him, “Simon, I have something to say to you.” He replied, “Say it, Teacher.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 7:47; Romans 5:20; Matthew 18:28"> “A certain creditor had two debtors; one owed him five hundred silver coins, and the other fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Daniel 9:18–19; Colossians 3:13; Romans 3:24"> When they could not pay, he canceled the debts of both. Now which of them will love him more?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Psalms 116:16–18; 1 Timothy 1:13–16; Luke 7:47"> Simon answered, “I suppose the one who had the bigger debt canceled.” Jesus said to him, “You have judged rightly.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:10; Judges 19:21; Genesis 18:4"> Then, turning toward the woman, he said to Simon, “Do you see this woman? I entered your house. You gave me no water for my feet, but she has wet my feet with her tears and wiped them with her hair.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:5; 2 Samuel 19:39; Romans 16:16"> You gave me no kiss of greeting, but from the time I entered she has not stopped kissing my feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Psalms 23:5; Ecclesiastes 9:8; Matthew 6:17"> You did not anoint my head with oil, but she has anointed my feet with perfumed oil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="1 John 4:19; 1 John 3:18; Isaiah 1:18"> Therefore I tell you, her sins, which were many, are forgiven, thus she loved much; but the one who is forgiven little loves little.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Matthew 9:2; Mark 2:5; Luke 5:20"> Then Jesus said to her, “Your sins are forgiven.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Luke 5:20–21; Matthew 9:3; Mark 2:7"> But those who were at the table with him began to say among themselves, “Who is this, who even forgives sins?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Mark 5:34; Luke 8:48; Luke 18:42"> He said to the woman, “Your faith has saved you; go in peace.”</span>
</div>
