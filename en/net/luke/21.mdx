## The Signs of the End of the Age

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 2:20; Matthew 24:1–51; Mark 13:1–37"> Now while some were speaking about the temple, how it was adorned with beautiful stones and offerings, Jesus said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:2; Mark 13:2; Lamentations 5:18"> “As for these things that you are gazing at, the days will come when not one stone will be left on another. All will be torn down!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 1:6–7; Mark 13:14; Mark 13:3–4"> So they asked him, “Teacher, when will these things happen? And what will be the sign that these things are about to take place?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 John 4:1; Mark 13:5–6; Ephesians 5:6"> He said, “Watch out that you are not misled. For many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am he,’ and, ‘The time is near.’ Do not follow them!</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 21:28; Matthew 24:6–8; Mark 13:7–8"> And when you hear of wars and rebellions, do not be afraid. For these things must happen first, but the end will not come at once.”</span>

## Persecution of Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 2:19–20; Mark 13:8; 2 Chronicles 15:5–6"> Then he said to them, “Nation will rise up in arms against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 24:29–30; Luke 21:25–27"> There will be great earthquakes, and famines and plagues in various places, and there will be terrifying sights and great signs from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 4:12–14; Acts 5:40; Acts 22:30"> But before all this, they will seize you and persecute you, handing you over to the synagogues and prisons. You will be brought before kings and governors because of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Philippians 1:12; Philippians 1:28; 2 Thessalonians 1:5"> This will be a time for you to serve as witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 12:11–12; Mark 13:11; Matthew 10:19–20"> Therefore be resolved not to rehearse ahead of time how to make your defense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:9; Ephesians 6:19; Luke 12:12"> For I will give you the words along with the wisdom that none of your adversaries will be able to withstand or contradict.</span>

<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:1; Genesis 19:17; Luke 17:31–33"> Then those who are in Judea must flee to the mountains. Those who are inside the city must depart. Those who are out in the country must not enter it,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hosea 9:7; Isaiah 65:12–16; Isaiah 63:4"> because these are days of vengeance, to fulfill all that is written.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Mark 13:17; Matthew 24:19; Luke 23:29"> Woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing their babies in those days! For there will be great distress on the earth and wrath against this people.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:29; Deuteronomy 28:65–67; 2 Peter 3:10–12"> People will be fainting from fear and from the expectation of what is coming on the world, for the powers of the heavens will be shaken.</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="James 5:9; Hebrews 10:37; 1 Peter 4:7"> So also you, when you see these things happening, know that the kingdom of God is near.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Mark 13:30; Matthew 24:34; Matthew 23:36"> I tell you the truth, this generation will not pass away until all these things take place.</span>
