## The Grateful Leper

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 9:51–52; John 4:3–4"> Now on the way to Jerusalem, Jesus was passing along between Samaria and Galilee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 13:45–46; Luke 5:12; 2 Kings 7:3"> As he was entering a village, ten men with leprosy met him. They stood at a distance,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 15:22; Matthew 20:30–31; Matthew 9:27"> raised their voices and said, “Jesus, Master, have mercy on us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 5:14; Leviticus 13:1–46; 2 Kings 5:14"> When he saw them he said, “Go and show yourselves to the priests.” And as they went along, they were cleansed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 30:11–12; Psalms 103:1–4; Psalms 118:18–19"> Then one of them, when he saw he was healed, turned back, praising God with a loud voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 8:5–25; Matthew 10:5; Revelation 19:10"> He fell with his face to the ground at Jesus’ feet and thanked him. (Now he was a Samaritan.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 1:21; Psalms 106:13; Genesis 3:9"> Then Jesus said, “Were not ten cleansed? Where are the other nine?</span>

## The Coming of the Son of Man

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Luke 5:35; Matthew 9:15; John 8:21–24"> Then he said to the disciples, “The days are coming when you will desire to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you will not see it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 21:8; Matthew 24:23–26; Luke 17:21"> Then people will say to you, ‘Look, there he is!’ or ‘Look, here he is!’ Do not go out or chase after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 24:27; Matthew 24:30; Malachi 4:1–2"> For just like the lightning flashes and lights up the sky from one side to the other, so will the Son of Man be in his day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 16:21; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> But first he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 24:37–39; Luke 17:24; Genesis 7:7–23"> Just as it was in the days of Noah, so too it will be in the days of the Son of Man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Luke 12:19–20; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–3; Luke 16:19–23"> People were eating, they were drinking, they were marrying, they were being given in marriage – right up to the day Noah entered the ark. Then the flood came and destroyed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:49–50; Genesis 18:20–21; James 5:1–5"> Likewise, just as it was in the days of Lot, people were eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting, building;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Peter 2:6; Zephaniah 2:9; Jude 1:7"> but on the day Lot went out from Sodom, fire and sulfur rained down from heaven and destroyed them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:7; 2 Thessalonians 1:7; Revelation 1:7"> It will be the same on the day the Son of Man is revealed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 21:21; Mark 13:14–16; Jeremiah 45:5"> On that day, anyone who is on the roof, with his goods in the house, must not come down to take them away, and likewise the person in the field must not turn back.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 19:26; Hebrews 10:38–39; 2 Peter 2:18–22"> Remember Lot’s wife!</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 8:35–37; Matthew 10:39; John 12:25"> Whoever tries to keep his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life will preserve it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 24:40–41; 2 Peter 2:9; Malachi 3:16–18"> I tell you, in that night there will be two people in one bed; one will be taken and the other left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 24:41; Exodus 11:5; Judges 16:21"> There will be two women grinding grain together; one will be taken and the other left.”</span>
