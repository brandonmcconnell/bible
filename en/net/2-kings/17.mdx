<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 3:2; 2 Kings 13:11; 2 Chronicles 30:5–11"> He did evil in the sight of the Lord, but not to the same degree as the Israelite kings who preceded him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 10:14; 2 Kings 15:19; 2 Kings 19:36–37"> King Shalmaneser of Assyria threatened him; Hoshea became his subject and paid him tribute.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; 2 Kings 24:20; Ezekiel 17:13–19"> The king of Assyria discovered that Hoshea was planning a revolt. Hoshea had sent messengers to King So of Egypt and had not sent his annual tribute to the king of Assyria. So the king of Assyria arrested him and imprisoned him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; 2 Kings 25:1–3; 2 Kings 18:9"> The king of Assyria marched through the whole land. He attacked Samaria and besieged it for three years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:26; Hosea 13:16; Deuteronomy 28:64"> In the ninth year of Hoshea’s reign, the king of Assyria captured Samaria and deported the people of Israel to Assyria. He settled them in Halah, along the Habor (the river of Gozan), and in the cities of the Medes.</span>

## A Summary of Israel’s Sinful History

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 23:16; Psalms 106:35–41; Nehemiah 9:26"> This happened because the Israelites sinned against the Lord their God, who brought them up from the land of Egypt and freed them from the power of Pharaoh king of Egypt. They worshiped other gods;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Leviticus 18:3; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 18:9"> they observed the practices of the nations whom the Lord had driven out from before Israel, and followed the example of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 18:8; Job 31:27; Deuteronomy 13:6"> The Israelites said things about the Lord their God that were not right. They built high places in all their cities, from the watchtower to the fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; Micah 5:14; Exodus 34:13"> They set up sacred pillars and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 28:25; 2 Kings 21:6; Jeremiah 44:17"> They burned incense on all the high places just like the nations whom the Lord had driven away from before them. Their evil practices made the Lord angry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:4; Exodus 20:3–5; Deuteronomy 4:23–25"> They worshiped the disgusting idols in blatant disregard of the Lord’s command.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 35:15; 1 Samuel 9:9; Jeremiah 18:11"> The Lord solemnly warned Israel and Judah through all his prophets and all the seers, “Turn back from your evil ways; obey my commandments and rules that are recorded in the law. I ordered your ancestors to keep this law and sent my servants the prophets to remind you of its demands.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 31:27; Acts 7:51; Psalms 106:24"> But they did not pay attention and were as stubborn as their ancestors, who had not trusted the Lord their God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:30–31; Deuteronomy 32:21; Romans 1:21–23"> They rejected his rules, the covenant he had made with their ancestors, and the laws he had commanded them to obey. They paid allegiance to worthless idols, and so became worthless to the Lord. They copied the practices of the surrounding nations in blatant disregard of the Lord’s command.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 14:23; 1 Kings 16:31; 1 Kings 14:15"> They abandoned all the commandments of the Lord their God; they made two metal calves and an Asherah pole, bowed down to all the stars in the sky, and worshiped Baal.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 21:6; 2 Kings 16:3; Deuteronomy 18:10–12"> They passed their sons and daughters through the fire, and practiced divination and omen reading. They committed themselves to doing evil in the sight of the Lord and made him angry.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 12:20; 1 Kings 11:13; Hosea 11:12"> So the Lord was furious with Israel and rejected them; only the tribe of Judah was left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 16:3; 1 Kings 14:22–23; Ezekiel 16:51–52"> Judah also failed to keep the commandments of the Lord their God; they followed Israel’s example.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 15:29; Jeremiah 6:30; 2 Kings 13:3"> So the Lord rejected all of Israel’s descendants; he humiliated them and handed them over to robbers, until he had thrown them from his presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Kings 11:11; 1 Kings 11:31; 1 Kings 14:16"> He tore Israel away from David’s dynasty, and Jeroboam son of Nebat became their king. Jeroboam drove Israel away from the Lord and encouraged them to commit a serious sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 3:3; 2 Kings 13:6; 2 Kings 13:11"> The Israelites followed in the sinful ways of Jeroboam son of Nebat and did not repudiate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 17:6; 2 Kings 17:13; 2 Kings 17:20"> Finally the Lord rejected Israel just as he had warned he would do through all his servants the prophets. Israel was deported from its land to Assyria and remains there to this very day.</span>
</div>
</div>

## The King of Assyria Populates Israel with Foreigners

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Kings 18:34; 2 Kings 17:30–31; Matthew 10:5"> The king of Assyria brought foreigners from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim and settled them in the cities of Samaria in place of the Israelites. They took possession of Samaria and lived in its cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 17:32; 2 Kings 17:34; 2 Kings 17:41"> When they first moved in, they did not worship the Lord. So the Lord sent lions among them and the lions were killing them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Kings 17:27; 1 Samuel 8:9; Amos 8:14"> The king of Assyria was told, “The nations whom you deported and settled in the cities of Samaria do not know the requirements of the God of the land, so he has sent lions among them. They are killing the people because they do not know the requirements of the God of the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Judges 17:13; 1 Kings 12:31; 1 Kings 13:2"> So the king of Assyria ordered, “Take back one of the priests whom you deported from there. He must settle there and teach them the requirements of the God of the land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Kings 12:29–32; Isaiah 29:13; Matthew 15:14"> So one of the priests whom they had deported from Samaria went back and settled in Bethel. He taught them how to worship the Lord.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 12:31; Micah 4:5; 1 Kings 13:32"> But each of these nations made its own gods and put them in the shrines on the high places that the people of Samaria had made. Each nation did this in the cities where they lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 17:24"> The people from Babylon made Succoth Benoth, the people from Cuth made Nergal, the people from Hamath made Ashima,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Kings 17:24; 2 Kings 17:17; 2 Kings 19:37"> the Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak, and the Sepharvites burned their sons in the fire as an offering to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Kings 12:31; 2 Kings 17:29; 1 Kings 13:33"> At the same time they worshiped the Lord. They appointed some of their own people to serve as priests in the shrines on the high places.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Kings 17:41; Luke 16:13; Matthew 6:24"> They were worshiping the Lord and at the same time serving their own gods in accordance with the practices of the nations from which they had been deported.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 32:28; Genesis 35:10; 2 Kings 17:27–28"> To this very day they observe their earlier practices. They do not worship the Lord; they do not obey the rules, regulations, law, and commandments that the Lord gave the descendants of Jacob, whom he renamed Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Judges 6:10; Joshua 23:16; Joshua 23:7"> The Lord made an agreement with them and instructed them, “You must not worship other gods. Do not bow down to them, serve them, or offer sacrifices to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 6:13; Leviticus 19:32"> Instead you must worship the Lord, who brought you up from the land of Egypt by his great power and military ability; bow down to him and offer sacrifices to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Leviticus 19:37; Deuteronomy 5:31–2; Deuteronomy 4:44–45"> You must carefully obey at all times the rules, regulations, law, and commandments he wrote down for you. You must not worship other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:23; Deuteronomy 6:12; Deuteronomy 8:14–18"> You must never forget the agreement I made with you, and you must not worship other gods.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Luke 1:74–75; 1 Samuel 12:24; Matthew 10:28"> Instead you must worship the Lord your God; then he will rescue you from the power of all your enemies.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Deuteronomy 4:28; 2 Kings 17:8; Jeremiah 13:23"> But they pay no attention; instead they observe their earlier practices.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 6:24; Zephaniah 1:5; 1 Kings 18:21"> These nations are worshiping the Lord and at the same time serving their idols; their sons and grandsons do just as their fathers have done, to this very day.</span>
</div>
</div>
