## Elisha Meets with Hazael

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 11:24; 2 Kings 6:24; 1 Kings 20:1"> Elisha traveled to Damascus while King Ben Hadad of Syria was sick. The king was told, “The prophet has come here.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 19:15; 2 Kings 1:2; 1 Samuel 9:7"> So the king told Hazael, “Take a gift and go visit the prophet. Request from him an oracle from the Lord. Ask him, ‘Will I recover from this sickness?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 5:13; 2 Kings 13:14; 1 Kings 19:15"> So Hazael went to visit Elisha. He took along a gift, as well as forty camel loads of all the fine things of Damascus. When he arrived, he stood before him and said, “Your son, King Ben Hadad of Syria, has sent me to you with this question, ‘Will I recover from this sickness?’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Amos 7:7; Genesis 2:17; Amos 7:1"> Elisha said to him, “Go and tell him, ‘You will surely recover,’ but the Lord has revealed to me that he will surely die.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Romans 9:2; Jeremiah 9:18"> Elisha just stared at him until Hazael became uncomfortable. Then the prophet started crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hosea 13:16; Nahum 3:10; 2 Kings 15:16"> Hazael asked, “Why are you crying, my master?” He replied, “Because I know the trouble you will cause the Israelites. You will set fire to their fortresses, kill their young men with the sword, smash their children to bits, and rip open their pregnant women.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 17:43; 1 Kings 19:15; 2 Samuel 9:8"> Hazael said, “How could your servant, who is as insignificant as a dog, accomplish this great military victory?” Elisha answered, “The Lord has revealed to me that you will be the king of Syria.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 8:10; Matthew 26:16; 2 Kings 5:25"> He left Elisha and went to his master. Ben Hadad asked him, “What did Elisha tell you?” Hazael replied, “He told me you would surely recover.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 8:13; 1 Kings 15:28; 1 Kings 11:26–37"> The next day Hazael took a piece of cloth, dipped it in water, and spread it over Ben Hadad’s face until he died. Then Hazael replaced him as king.</span>
</div>

## Jehoram’s Reign over Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:17; 2 Kings 3:1; 2 Chronicles 21:1–20"> In the fifth year of the reign of Israel’s King Joram, son of Ahab, Jehoshaphat’s son Jehoram became king over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Chronicles 21:5–10"> He was thirty-two years old when he became king and he reigned for eight years in Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 8:26; 1 Kings 11:1–5; 2 Chronicles 21:13"> He followed in the footsteps of the kings of Israel, just as Ahab’s dynasty had done, for he married Ahab’s daughter. He did evil in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 11:36; 2 Samuel 7:12–13; Luke 1:32–33"> But the Lord was unwilling to destroy Judah. He preserved Judah for the sake of his servant David to whom he had promised a perpetual dynasty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Kings 22:47; 2 Kings 3:9; 2 Kings 8:22"> During his reign Edom freed themselves from Judah’s control and set up their own king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 18:17"> Joram crossed over to Zair with all his chariots. The Edomites, who had surrounded him, attacked at night and defeated him and his chariot officers. The Israelite army retreated to their homeland.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 19:8; Joshua 21:13; Genesis 27:40"> So Edom has remained free from Judah’s control to this very day. At that same time Libnah also rebelled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Kings 15:36; 1 Kings 11:41; 2 Chronicles 21:11–20"> The rest of the events of Joram’s reign, including a record of his accomplishments, are recorded in the scroll called the Annals of the Kings of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:6; 2 Chronicles 25:23; 2 Chronicles 21:1"> Joram passed away and was buried with his ancestors in the city of David. His son Ahaziah replaced him as king.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Ahaziah Takes the Throne of Judah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 9:29; 2 Kings 8:16–17; 2 Chronicles 21:20–6"> In the twelfth year of the reign of Israel’s King Joram, son of Ahab, Jehoram’s son Ahaziah became king over Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:2; 2 Kings 9:21–27; 2 Kings 11:1"> Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he became king and he reigned for one year in Jerusalem. His mother was Athaliah, the granddaughter of King Omri of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:3–4; 2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Ecclesiastes 7:26"> He followed in the footsteps of Ahab’s dynasty and did evil in the sight of the Lord, like Ahab’s dynasty, for he was related to Ahab’s family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 22:5; 1 Kings 4:13; 2 Chronicles 19:2"> He joined Ahab’s son Joram in a battle against King Hazael of Syria at Ramoth Gilead in which the Syrians defeated Joram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 9:15–16; 2 Kings 8:28; 2 Chronicles 22:5–7"> King Joram returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds he received from the Syrians in Ramah when he fought against King Hazael of Syria. King Ahaziah son of Jehoram of Judah went down to visit Joram son of Ahab in Jezreel, for he was ill.</span>
</div>
</div>
