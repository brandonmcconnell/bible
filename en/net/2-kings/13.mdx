<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 12:26–33; 1 Kings 14:16; 2 Kings 10:29"> He did evil in the sight of the Lord. He continued in the sinful ways of Jeroboam son of Nebat who had encouraged Israel to sin; he did not repudiate those sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 19:17; 2 Kings 13:24–25; Judges 2:14"> The Lord was furious with Israel and handed them over to King Hazael of Syria and to Hazael’s son Ben Hadad for many years.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 14:26; Exodus 3:7; Psalms 78:34"> Jehoahaz asked for the Lord’s mercy and the Lord responded favorably, for he saw that Israel was oppressed by the king of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:27; 2 Kings 14:25; 2 Kings 14:27"> The Lord provided a deliverer for Israel and they were freed from Syria’s power. The Israelites once more lived in security.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Kings 16:33; 2 Kings 13:2; Deuteronomy 7:5"> But they did not repudiate the sinful ways of the family of Jeroboam, who encouraged Israel to sin; they continued in those sins. There was even an Asherah pole standing in Samaria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 1:3; 2 Kings 10:32; 1 Kings 20:15"> Jehoahaz had no army left except for fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and 10,000 foot soldiers. The king of Syria had destroyed his troops and trampled on them like dust.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:4; 1 Kings 14:29; 1 Kings 14:31"> The rest of the events of Jehoahaz’s reign, including all his accomplishments and successes, are recorded in the scroll called the Annals of the Kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 10:35; 2 Kings 13:13; 2 Kings 14:8"> Jehoahaz passed away and was buried in Samaria. His son Joash replaced him as king.</span>
</div>

## Jehoash’s Reign over Israel

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> In the thirty-seventh year of King Joash’s reign over Judah, Jehoahaz’s son Jehoash became king over Israel. He reigned in Samaria for sixteen years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 13:2; 2 Kings 10:29; 2 Kings 3:3"> He did evil in the sight of the Lord. He did not repudiate the sinful ways of Jeroboam son of Nebat who encouraged Israel to sin; he continued in those sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 13:14–25; 2 Kings 14:8–16; 2 Chronicles 25:17–24"> The rest of the events of Joash’s reign, including all his accomplishments and his successful war with King Amaziah of Judah, are recorded in the scroll called the Annals of the Kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Kings 1:21; 2 Kings 14:28–29; 1 Kings 2:10"> Joash passed away and Jeroboam succeeded him on the throne. Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Elisha Makes One Final Prophecy

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Kings 2:12; Philippians 2:26; Genesis 48:1"> Now Elisha had a terminal illness. King Joash of Israel went down to visit him. He wept before him and said, “My father, my father! The chariot and horsemen of Israel!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> Elisha told him, “Take a bow and some arrows,” and he did so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 4:34; Genesis 49:24; Psalms 144:1"> Then Elisha told the king of Israel, “Aim the bow.” He did so, and Elisha placed his hands on the king’s hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 20:26; 2 Kings 5:10–14; 1 Corinthians 1:18"> Elisha said, “Open the east window,” and he did so. Elisha said, “Shoot!” and<br />he did so. Elisha said, “This arrow symbolizes the victory the Lord will give you over Syria. You will annihilate Syria in Aphek!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Kings 4:6; Exodus 17:11; Isaiah 20:2–4"> Then Elisha said, “Take the arrows,” and he did so. He told the king of Israel, “Strike the ground!” He struck the ground three times and stopped.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Kings 13:25; Numbers 16:15; 2 Kings 4:40"> The prophet got angry at him and said, “If you had struck the ground five or six times, you would have annihilated Syria! But now, you will defeat Syria only three times.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Kings 24:2; 2 Kings 3:7; 2 Kings 6:23"> Elisha died and was buried. Moabite raiding parties invaded the land at the beginning of the year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ezekiel 37:1–10; Acts 19:12; John 5:28–29"> One day some men were burying a man when they spotted a raiding party. So they threw the dead man into Elisha’s tomb. When the body touched Elisha’s bones, the dead man came to life and stood on his feet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Kings 8:12; 2 Kings 13:3–7; Psalms 106:40–42"> Now King Hazael of Syria oppressed Israel throughout Jehoahaz’s reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 105:8; 2 Kings 14:27; Micah 7:18–20"> But the Lord had mercy on them and felt pity for them. He extended his favor to them because of the promise he had made to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He has been unwilling to destroy them or remove them from his presence to this very day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 125:3; Luke 18:7"> When King Hazael of Syria died, his son Ben Hadad replaced him as king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 13:18–19; 2 Kings 10:32; 2 Kings 14:25"> Jehoahaz’s son Jehoash took back from Ben Hadad son of Hazael the cities that he had taken from his father Jehoahaz in war. Joash defeated him three times and recovered the Israelite cities.</span>
</div>
</div>
