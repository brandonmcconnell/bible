<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 30:21; Jeremiah 2:36; Genesis 30:13"> Now Dinah, Leah’s daughter whom she bore to Jacob, went to meet the young women of the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 5:28; Job 31:1; Job 31:9"> When Shechem son of Hamor the Hivite, who ruled that area, saw her, he grabbed her, forced himself on her, and sexually assaulted her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 2:14; Isaiah 40:2; 2 Chronicles 30:22"> Then he became very attached to Dinah, Jacob’s daughter. He fell in love with the young woman and spoke romantically to her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 14:2; 2 Samuel 13:13; Genesis 21:21"> Shechem said to his father Hamor, “Acquire this young girl as my wife.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Samuel 13:22; Leviticus 10:3; 1 Samuel 10:27"> When Jacob heard that Shechem had violated his daughter Dinah, his sons were with the livestock in the field. So Jacob remained silent until they came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Then Shechem’s father Hamor went to speak with Jacob about Dinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 7:15; Judges 20:6; Deuteronomy 22:21"> Now Jacob’s sons had come in from the field when they heard the news. They were offended and very angry because Shechem had disgraced Israel by sexually assaulting Jacob’s daughter, a crime that should not be committed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 11:2; Psalms 119:20; Psalms 84:2"> But Hamor made this appeal to them: “My son Shechem is in love with your daughter. Please give her to him as his wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 7:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 19:14"> Intermarry with us. Let us marry your daughters, and take our daughters as wives for yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 47:27; Genesis 42:34"> You may live among us, and the land will be open to you. Live in it, travel freely in it, and acquire property in it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 33:15; Genesis 18:3"> Then Shechem said to Dinah’s father and brothers, “Let me find favor in your sight, and whatever you require of me I’ll give.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 22:16–17; Deuteronomy 22:28–29; 1 Samuel 18:25–27"> You can make the bride price and the gift I must bring very expensive, and I’ll give whatever you ask of me. Just give me the young woman as my wife!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 25:27–34; Isaiah 59:13; Job 13:4"> Jacob’s sons answered Shechem and his father Hamor deceitfully when they spoke because Shechem had violated their sister Dinah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 23:1–39; 2 Samuel 15:7; Genesis 17:11"> They said to them, “We cannot give our sister to a man who is not circumcised, for it would be a disgrace to us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Galatians 4:12"> We will give you our consent on this one condition: You must become like us by circumcising all your males.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Then we will give you our daughters to marry, and we will take your daughters as wives for ourselves, and we will live among you and become one people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But if you do not agree to our terms by being circumcised, then we will take our sister and depart.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Their offer pleased Hamor and his son Shechem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 17:12; Acts 13:50; 1 Samuel 22:14"> The young man did not delay in doing what they asked because he wanted Jacob’s daughter Dinah badly. (Now he was more important than anyone in his father’s household.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ruth 4:1; Proverbs 31:23; Amos 5:12"> So Hamor and his son Shechem went to the gate of their city and spoke to the men of their city,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> “These men are at peace with us. So let them live in the land and travel freely in it, for the land is wide enough for them. We will take their daughters for wives, and we will give them our daughters to marry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Only on this one condition will these men consent to live with us and become one people: They demand that every male among us be circumcised just as they are circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Proverbs 1:12–13; John 6:26–27; John 2:16"> If we do so, won’t their livestock, their property, and all their animals become ours? So let’s consent to their demand, so they will live among us.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 23:10; 1 Corinthians 7:19; Isaiah 1:10–16"> All the men who assembled at the city gate agreed with Hamor and his son Shechem. Every male who assembled at the city gate was circumcised.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 49:5–7; Genesis 29:33–34; Numbers 31:7"> In three days, when they were still in pain, two of Jacob’s sons, Simeon and Levi, Dinah’s brothers, each took his sword and went to the unsuspecting city and slaughtered every male.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:26; Deuteronomy 32:42; Isaiah 31:8"> They killed Hamor and his son Shechem with the sword, took Dinah from Shechem’s house, and left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Joshua 7:13; Joshua 7:21; Exodus 2:14"> Jacob’s sons killed them and looted the city because their sister had been violated.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Job 1:15–16; Deuteronomy 8:17–18; Job 20:5"> They took their flocks, herds, and donkeys, as well as everything in the city and in the surrounding fields.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> They captured as plunder all their wealth, all their little ones, and their wives, including everything in the houses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 105:12; Joshua 7:25; Exodus 5:21"> Then Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, “You have brought ruin on me by making me a foul odor among the inhabitants of the land – among the Canaanites and the Perizzites. I am few in number; they will join forces against me and attack me, and both I and my family will be destroyed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 6:34; Genesis 34:13; Genesis 49:7"> But Simeon and Levi replied, “Should he treat our sister like a common prostitute?”</span>
