<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Now Rebekah had been listening while Isaac spoke to his son Esau. When Esau went out to the open fields to hunt down some wild game and bring it back,</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 25:28"> Rebekah said to her son Jacob, “Look, I overheard your father tell your brother Esau,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Joshua 6:26; 1 Samuel 24:19; Deuteronomy 33:1"> ‘Bring me some wild game and prepare for me some tasty food. Then I will eat it and bless you in the presence of the Lord before I die.’</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Then you will take it to your father. Thus he will eat it and bless you before he dies.”</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 27:18; Genesis 25:27; Malachi 1:14"> My father may touch me! Then he’ll think I’m mocking him and I’ll bring a curse on myself instead of a blessing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; 1 Samuel 25:24; 2 Samuel 14:9"> So his mother told him, “Any curse against you will fall on me, my son! Just obey me! Go and get them for me!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 27:9; Proverbs 23:2–3; Genesis 25:28"> So he went and got the goats and brought them to his mother. She prepared some tasty food, just the way his father loved it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 27:27; Luke 20:46; Luke 15:22"> Then Rebekah took her older son Esau’s best clothes, which she had with her in the house, and put them on her younger son Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> She put the skins of the young goats on his hands and the smooth part of his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then she handed the tasty food and the bread she had made to her son Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> He went to his father and said, “My father!” Isaac replied, “Here I am. Which are you, my son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 28:15; Matthew 26:70–74; Zechariah 13:3–4"> Jacob said to his father, “I am Esau, your firstborn. I’ve done as you told me. Now sit up and eat some of my wild game so that you can bless me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 20:7; Job 13:7; Genesis 24:12"> But Isaac asked his son, “How in the world did you find it so quickly, my son?” “Because the Lord your God brought it to me,” he replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="James 4:8; Isaiah 57:19; Genesis 27:12"> Then Isaac said to Jacob, “Come closer so I can touch you, my son, and know for certain if you really are my son Esau.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> So Jacob went over to his father Isaac, who felt him and said, “The voice is Jacob’s, but the hands are Esau’s.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 27:16; Hebrews 11:20; Romans 9:11–12"> He did not recognize him because his hands were hairy, like his brother Esau’s hands. So Isaac blessed Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Colossians 3:9; Ephesians 4:25; Proverbs 12:22"> Then he asked, “Are you really my son Esau?” “I am,” Jacob replied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> Isaac said, “Bring some of the wild game for me to eat, my son. Then I will bless you.” So Jacob brought it to him, and he ate it. He also brought him wine, and Isaac drank.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Then his father Isaac said to him, “Come here and kiss me, my son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 6:7; Psalms 65:10; Hebrews 11:20"> So Jacob went over and kissed him. When Isaac caught the scent of his clothing, he blessed him, saying,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Yes, my son smells</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">like the scent of an open field</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">which the Lord has blessed.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:28; Deuteronomy 33:13; Psalms 133:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">May God give you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the dew of the sky</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the richness of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and plenty of grain and new wine.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 12:3; Numbers 24:9; Isaiah 49:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">May peoples serve you</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and nations bow down to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">You will be lord over your brothers,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the sons of your mother will bow down to you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May those who curse you be cursed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and those who bless you be blessed.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Isaac had just finished blessing Jacob, and Jacob had scarcely left his father’s presence, when his brother Esau returned from the hunt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 27:4"> He also prepared some tasty food and brought it to his father. Esau said to him, “My father, get up and eat some of your son’s wild game. Then you can bless me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 27:18"> His father Isaac asked, “Who are you?” “I am your firstborn son,” he replied, “Esau!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 28:3–4; Romans 11:29; Ephesians 1:3"> Isaac began to shake violently and asked, “Then who else hunted game and brought it to me? I ate all of it just before you arrived, and I blessed him. He will indeed be blessed!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Proverbs 1:31; 1 Samuel 30:4"> When Esau heard his father’s words, he wailed loudly and bitterly. He said to his father, “Bless me too, my father!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:6; Job 13:7; Malachi 2:10"> But Isaac replied, “Your brother came in here deceitfully and took away your blessing.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:26; John 1:47; Genesis 32:28"> Esau exclaimed, “‘Jacob’ is the right name for him! He has tripped me up two times! He took away my birthright, and now, look, he has taken away my blessing!” Then he asked, “Have you not kept back a blessing for me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:14; Genesis 27:28–29; Genesis 25:23"> Isaac replied to Esau, “Look! I have made him lord over you. I have made all his relatives his servants and provided him with grain and new wine. What is left that I can do for you, my son?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Hebrews 12:17; Genesis 27:34; Isaiah 65:14"> Esau said to his father, “Do you have only that one blessing, my father? Bless me too!” Then Esau wept loudly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 27:28; Hebrews 11:20; Joshua 24:4"> So his father Isaac said to him,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Indeed, your home will be</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">away from the richness of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and away from the dew of the sky above.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Kings 8:20–22; Genesis 25:23; 2 Chronicles 25:11–12"> <span className="pl pl-1">You will live by your sword</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but you will serve your brother.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">When you grow restless,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">you will tear off his yoke</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">from your neck.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="1 John 3:12–15; Genesis 37:4; Genesis 50:3–4"> So Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing his father had given to his brother. Esau said privately, “The time of mourning for my father is near; then I will kill my brother Jacob!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Genesis 37:18–20; 1 Samuel 30:5; Genesis 42:21–22"> When Rebekah heard what her older son Esau had said, she quickly summoned her younger son Jacob and told him, “Look, your brother Esau is planning to get revenge by killing you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 27:8; Genesis 11:31; Genesis 27:13"> Now then, my son, do what I say. Run away immediately to my brother Laban in Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:38; Genesis 31:41"> Live with him for a little while until your brother’s rage subsides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Genesis 4:8–16; Genesis 9:5–6"> Stay there until your brother’s anger against you subsides and he forgets what you did to him. Then I’ll send someone to bring you back from there. Why should I lose both of you in one day?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:3; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> Then Rebekah said to Isaac, “I am deeply depressed because of these daughters of Heth. If Jacob were to marry one of these daughters of Heth who live in this land, I would want to die!”</span>
