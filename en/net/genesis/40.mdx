<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Genesis 39:20–23; Esther 6:1"> After these things happened, the cupbearer to the king of Egypt and the royal baker offended their master, the king of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Proverbs 16:14; Proverbs 19:12; Psalms 76:10"> Pharaoh was enraged with his two officials, the cupbearer and the baker,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 39:20; Genesis 39:23"> so he imprisoned them in the house of the captain of the guard in the same facility where Joseph was confined.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 39:1; Psalms 37:5; Genesis 37:36"> The captain of the guard appointed Joseph to be their attendant, and he served them.<br />They spent some time in custody.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 41:11; Genesis 40:8; Genesis 37:5–10"> Both of them, the cupbearer and the baker of the king of Egypt, who were confined in the prison, had a dream the same night. Each man’s dream had its own meaning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 41:8; Daniel 2:1–3; Genesis 40:8"> When Joseph came to them in the morning, he saw that they were looking depressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:2; 1 Samuel 1:8; Luke 24:17"> So he asked Pharaoh’s officials, who were with him in custody in his master’s house, “Why do you look so sad today?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 2:47; Genesis 41:15–16; Daniel 2:28"> They told him, “We both had dreams, but there is no one to interpret them.” Joseph responded, “Don’t interpretations belong to God? Tell them to me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Judges 7:13–15; Genesis 37:5–10; Daniel 4:8"> So the chief cupbearer told his dream to Joseph: “In my dream, there was a vine in front of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> On the vine there were three branches. As it budded, its blossoms opened and its clusters ripened into grapes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11–1; 1 Kings 10:5; 2 Chronicles 9:4"> Now Pharaoh’s cup was in my hand, so I took the grapes, squeezed them into his cup, and put the cup in Pharaoh’s hand.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 41:12; Judges 7:14; Genesis 41:25–26"> “This is its meaning,” Joseph said to him. “The three branches represent three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 3:3; Jeremiah 52:31; 2 Kings 25:27"> In three more days Pharaoh will reinstate you and restore you to your office. You will put Pharaoh’s cup in his hand, just as you did before when you were cupbearer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Luke 23:42; 1 Samuel 20:14–15"> But remember me when it goes well for you, and show me kindness. Make mention of me to Pharaoh and bring me out of this prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17–18; Genesis 39:20; 1 Timothy 1:10"> for I really was kidnapped from the land of the Hebrews and I have done nothing wrong here for which they should put me in a dungeon.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 40:1–2"> When the chief baker saw that the interpretation of the first dream was favorable, he said to Joseph, “I also appeared in my dream and there were three baskets of white bread on my head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 49:20; 1 Chronicles 12:20"> In the top basket there were baked goods of every kind for Pharaoh, but the birds were eating them from the basket that was on my head.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 40:12; Genesis 41:26; 1 Corinthians 11:24"> Joseph replied, “This is its meaning: The three baskets represent three days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 40:22; Acts 20:27; 2 Samuel 21:6"> In three more days Pharaoh will decapitate you and impale you on a pole. Then the birds will eat your flesh from you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 40:13; Matthew 14:6"> On the third day it was Pharaoh’s birthday, so he gave a feast for all his servants. He “lifted up” the head of the chief cupbearer and the head of the chief baker in the midst of his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 40:13; Nehemiah 2:1"> He restored the chief cupbearer to his former position so that he placed the cup in Pharaoh’s hand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 40:19; Genesis 41:16; Daniel 2:30"> but the chief baker he impaled, just as Joseph had predicted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 31:12; Job 19:14; Psalms 105:19"> But the chief cupbearer did not remember Joseph – he forgot him.</span>
</div>
</div>
