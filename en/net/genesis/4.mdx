<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">

<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 36:2; Genesis 5:21"> To Enoch was born Irad, and Irad was the father of Mehujael. Mehujael was the father of Methushael, and Methushael was the father of Lamech.</span>

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 116:17; 1 Kings 18:24; Joel 2:32"> And a son was also born to Seth, whom he named Enosh. At that time people began to worship the Lord.</span>
