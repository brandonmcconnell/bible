<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 18:6–8; Judges 6:19; 1 Samuel 28:24"> But he urged them persistently, so they turned aside with him and entered his house. He prepared a feast for them, including bread baked without yeast, and they ate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Proverbs 4:16; Exodus 23:2"> Before they could lie down to sleep, all the men – both young and old, from every part of the city of Sodom – surrounded the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 19:22; Isaiah 3:9; Jude 1:7"> They shouted to Lot, “Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us so we can have sex with them!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 19:23"> Lot went outside to them, shutting the door behind him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:9–11; Judges 19:23; Leviticus 20:13"> He said, “No, my brothers! Don’t act so wickedly!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Judges 19:24; Genesis 19:31–38; Judges 9:15"> Look, I have two daughters who have never had sexual relations with a man. Let me bring them out to you, and you can do to them whatever you please. Only don’t do anything to these men, for they have come under the protection of my roof.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 2:14; 2 Peter 2:7–8; Genesis 13:12"> “Out of our way!” they cried, and “This man came to live here as a foreigner, and now he dares to judge us! We’ll do more harm to you than to them!” They kept pressing in on Lot until they were close enough to break down the door.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> So the men inside reached out and pulled Lot back into the house as they shut the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Kings 6:18; Acts 13:11; Deuteronomy 28:28–29"> Then they struck the men who were at the door of the house, from the youngest to the oldest, with blindness. The men outside wore themselves out trying to find the door.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 7:1; 2 Peter 2:7; Revelation 18:4"> Then the two visitors said to Lot, “Who else do you have here? Do you have any sons-in-law, sons, daughters, or other relatives in the city? Get them out of this place</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 18:20; Matthew 13:49–50; Jude 1:7"> because we are about to destroy it. The outcry against this place is so great before the Lord that he has sent us to destroy it.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 16:21; Jeremiah 51:6; Numbers 16:45"> Then Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law who were going to marry his daughters. He said, “Quick, get out of this place because the Lord is about to destroy the city!” But his sons-in-law thought he was ridiculing them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:24–27; Revelation 18:4; Genesis 19:22"> At dawn the angels hurried Lot along, saying, “Get going! Take your wife and your two daughters who are here, or else you will be destroyed when the city is judged!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Romans 9:15–16; Titus 3:5; John 6:44"> When Lot hesitated, the men grabbed his hand and the hands of his wife and two daughters because the Lord had compassion on them. They led them away and placed them outside the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 24:16–18; Luke 9:62; Philippians 3:13–14"> When they had brought them outside, they said, “Run for your lives! Don’t look behind you or stop anywhere in the valley! Escape to the mountains or you will be destroyed!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 13:6–8; Acts 10:14; Genesis 32:26"> But Lot said to them, “No, please, Lord!</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:1; Psalms 103:1–22; 1 Timothy 1:14–16"> Your servant has found favor with you, and you have shown me great kindness by sparing my life. But I am not able to escape to the mountains because this disaster will overtake me and I’ll die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 119:175; Proverbs 3:5–7; Genesis 19:30"> Look, this town over here is close enough to escape to, and it’s just a little one. Let me go there. It’s just a little place, isn’t it? Then I’ll survive.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 145:19; Psalms 34:15; Psalms 102:17"> “Very well,” he replied, “I will grant this request too and will not overthrow the town you mentioned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 13:10; Genesis 14:2; Genesis 32:25–28"> Run there quickly, for I cannot do anything until you arrive there.” (This incident explains why the town was called Zoar.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> The sun had just risen over the land as Lot reached Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; Isaiah 13:19; Amos 4:11"> Then the Lord rained down sulfur and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah. It was sent down from the sky by the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 107:34; Genesis 14:3; Genesis 13:10"> So he overthrew those cities and all that region, including all the inhabitants of the cities and the vegetation that grew from the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Luke 17:31–32; Genesis 19:17; Proverbs 14:14"> But Lot’s wife looked back longingly and was turned into a pillar of salt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:1; Psalms 5:3; Hebrews 2:1"> Abraham got up early in the morning and went to the place where he had stood before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Revelation 18:9; Revelation 9:2; Revelation 21:8"> He looked out toward Sodom and Gomorrah and all the land of that region. As he did so, he saw the smoke rising up from the land like smoke from a furnace.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 8:1; Deuteronomy 9:5; Genesis 18:23–33"> So when God destroyed the cities of the region, God honored Abraham’s request. He removed Lot from the midst of the destruction when he destroyed the cities Lot had lived in.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 19:19; Isaiah 15:5; James 1:8"> Lot went up from Zoar with his two daughters and settled in the mountains because he was afraid to live in Zoar. So he lived in a cave with his two daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Deuteronomy 25:5; Isaiah 4:1; Genesis 16:2"> Later the older daughter said to the younger, “Our father is old, and there is no man anywhere nearby to have sexual relations with us, according to the way of all the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 18:6–7; Genesis 11:3; Genesis 9:21"> Come, let’s make our father drunk with wine so we can have sexual relations with him and preserve our family line through our father.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:29–35; Proverbs 20:1"> So that night they made their father drunk with wine, and the older daughter came and had sexual relations with her father. But he was not aware that she had sexual relations with him and then got up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:12; Jeremiah 6:15; Isaiah 3:9"> So in the morning the older daughter said to the younger, “Since I had sexual relations with my father last night, let’s make him drunk again tonight. Then you go and have sexual relations with him so we can preserve our family line through our father.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Proverbs 24:16; Psalms 8:4; 1 Peter 4:7"> So they made their father drunk that night as well, and the younger one came and had sexual relations with him. But he was not aware that she had sexual relations with him and then got up.</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:9; 2 Kings 3:1–27; Numbers 21:29"> The older daughter gave birth to a son and named him Moab. He is the ancestor of the Moabites of today.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:19; Judges 10:6–40; Psalms 83:4–8"> The younger daughter also gave birth to a son and named him Ben-Ammi. He is the ancestor of the Ammonites of today.</span>
