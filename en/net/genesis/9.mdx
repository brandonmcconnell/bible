## The Curse of Canaan

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:4–28; Genesis 8:17; Genesis 5:32"> These were the sons of Noah, and from them the whole earth was populated.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Deuteronomy 20:6; Proverbs 10:11; Isaiah 28:24–26"> Noah, a man of the soil, began to plant a vineyard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 20:1; Habakkuk 2:15–16; Proverbs 23:31–32"> When he drank some of the wine, he got drunk and uncovered himself inside his tent.</span>
