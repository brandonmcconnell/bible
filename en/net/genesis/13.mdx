<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:12; Genesis 12:7–8; Isaiah 58:9"> This was the place where he had first built the altar, and there Abram worshiped the Lord.</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 36:6–7; Ecclesiastes 5:10–11; 1 Timothy 6:9"> But the land could not support them while they were living side by side. Because their possessions were so great, they were not able to live alongside one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 26:20; Genesis 12:6; James 3:16"> So there were quarrels between Abram’s herdsmen and Lot’s herdsmen. (Now the Canaanites and the Perizzites were living in the land at that time.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:6–7; Acts 7:26; Psalms 133:1"> Abram said to Lot, “Let there be no quarreling between me and you, and between my herdsmen and your herdsmen, for we are close relatives.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 34:10; 1 Peter 3:8–12"> Is not the whole land before you? Separate yourself now from me. If you go to the left, then I’ll go to the right, but if you go to the right, then I’ll go to the left.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 34:3; Isaiah 51:3; Genesis 14:8"> Lot looked up and saw the whole region of the Jordan. He noticed that all of it was well-watered (before the Lord obliterated Sodom and Gomorrah) like the garden of the Lord, like the land of Egypt, all the way to Zoar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 13:9; 1 Peter 2:17; Hebrews 10:25"> Lot chose for himself the whole region of the Jordan and traveled toward the east. So the relatives separated from each other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 19:29; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 19:1"> Abram settled in the land of Canaan, but Lot settled among the cities of the Jordan plain and pitched his tents next to Sodom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:10; 2 Peter 2:6–8; Jude 1:7"> (Now the people of Sodom were extremely wicked rebels against the Lord.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 49:18; Deuteronomy 3:27; Genesis 28:14"> After Lot had departed, the Lord said to Abram, “Look from the place where you stand to the north, south, east, and west.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 20:7; Genesis 35:12; Genesis 15:18"> I will give all the land that you see to you and your descendants forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 28:14; Numbers 23:10; Genesis 32:12"> And I will make your descendants like the dust of the earth, so that if anyone is able to count the dust of the earth, then your descendants also can be counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 13:15; Numbers 13:17–24"> Get up and walk throughout the land, for I will give it to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 18:1; Genesis 8:20; Genesis 35:27"> So Abram moved his tents and went to live by the oaks of Mamre in Hebron, and he built an altar to the Lord there.</span>
