<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Job 5:2; Genesis 29:31; Genesis 37:11"> When Rachel saw that she could not give Jacob children, she became jealous of her sister. She said to Jacob, “Give me children or I’ll die!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 16:2; Psalms 127:3; 2 Kings 5:7"> Jacob became furious with Rachel and exclaimed, “Am I in the place of God, who has kept you from having children?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 50:23; Job 3:12; Ruth 4:11"> She replied, “Here is my servant Bilhah! Have sexual relations with her so that she can bear children for me and I can have a family through her.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 35:22; Genesis 21:10; 2 Samuel 12:11"> So Rachel gave him her servant Bilhah as a wife, and Jacob had marital relations with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Bilhah became pregnant and gave Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Lamentations 3:59; Psalms 35:24; Psalms 43:1"> Then Rachel said, “God has vindicated me. He has responded to my prayer and given me a son.” That is why she named him Dan.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Bilhah, Rachel’s servant, became pregnant again and gave Jacob another son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:24–25; Genesis 23:6; Genesis 49:21"> Then Rachel said, “I have fought a desperate struggle with my sister, but I have won.” So she named him Naphtali.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 30:4; Genesis 30:17; Genesis 29:35"> When Leah saw that she had stopped having children, she gave her servant Zilpah to Jacob as a wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Soon Leah’s servant Zilpah gave Jacob a son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 49:19; Isaiah 65:11; Genesis 35:26"> Leah said, “How fortunate!” So she named him Gad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then Leah’s servant Zilpah gave Jacob another son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 1:48; Proverbs 31:28; Song of Solomon 6:9"> Leah said, “How happy I am, for women will call me happy!” So she named him Asher.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Song of Solomon 7:13; Genesis 25:30"> At the time of the wheat harvest Reuben went out and found some mandrake plants in a field and brought them to his mother Leah. Rachel said to Leah, “Give me some of your son’s mandrakes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Numbers 16:13; 1 Corinthians 4:3; Numbers 16:9–10"> But Leah replied, “Wasn’t it enough that you’ve taken away my husband? Would you take away my son’s mandrakes too?” “All right,” Rachel said, “he may sleep with you tonight in exchange for your son’s mandrakes.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> When Jacob came in from the fields that evening, Leah went out to meet him and said, “You must sleep with me because I have paid for your services with my son’s mandrakes.” So he had marital relations with her that night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:6; Genesis 30:22; Exodus 3:7"> God paid attention to Leah; she became pregnant and gave Jacob a son for the fifth time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 49:14–15; Deuteronomy 33:18; Genesis 46:13"> Then Leah said, “God has granted me a reward because I gave my servant to my husband as a wife.” So she named him Issachar.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Leah became pregnant again and gave Jacob a son for the sixth time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:23; Matthew 4:13; Genesis 49:13"> Then Leah said, “God has given me a good gift. Now my husband will honor me because I have given him six sons.” So she named him Zebulun.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 34:1–3; Genesis 34:26; Genesis 46:15"> After that she gave birth to a daughter and named her Dinah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Psalms 113:9; Psalms 127:3"> Then God took note of Rachel. He paid attention to her and enabled her to become pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 1:25; Genesis 29:31; Luke 1:27"> She became pregnant and gave birth to a son. Then she said, “God has taken away my shame.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 49:22–26; Genesis 35:24; Genesis 37:2"> She named him Joseph, saying, “May the Lord give me yet another son.”</span>
</div>

## The Flocks of Jacob

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 24:54; Genesis 24:56; Genesis 28:15"> After Rachel had given birth to Joseph, Jacob said to Laban, “Send me on my way so that I can go home to my own country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Hosea 12:12; Genesis 31:26"> Let me take my wives and my children whom I have acquired by working for you. Then I’ll depart, because you know how hard I’ve worked for you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Acts 7:10; Isaiah 61:9"> But Laban said to him, “If I have found favor in your sight, please stay here, for I have learned by divination that the Lord has blessed me on account of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 29:15; Genesis 29:19"> He added, “Just name your wages – I’ll pay whatever you want.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:6; Genesis 31:38–40; 1 Peter 2:18"> “You know how I have worked for you,” Jacob replied, “and how well your livestock have fared under my care.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Timothy 5:8; 2 Corinthians 12:14; Deuteronomy 11:10"> Indeed, you had little before I arrived, but now your possessions have increased many times over. The Lord has blessed you wherever I worked. But now, how long must it be before I do something for my own family too?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:4–6; Hebrews 13:5; Psalms 118:8"> So Laban asked, “What should I give you?” “You don’t need to give me a thing,” Jacob replied, “but if you agree to this one condition, I will continue to care for your flocks and protect them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Genesis 31:8; Genesis 31:10; Genesis 30:35"> Let me walk among all your flocks today and remove from them every speckled or spotted sheep, every dark-colored lamb, and the spotted or speckled goats. These animals will be my wages.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 13:14; 2 Samuel 22:21; 1 Samuel 26:23"> My integrity will testify for me later on. When you come to verify that I’ve taken only the wages we agreed on, if I have in my possession any goat that is not speckled or spotted or any sheep that is not dark-colored, it will be considered stolen.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:7; Numbers 22:29; 1 Corinthians 14:5"> “Agreed!” said Laban, “It will be as you say.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 31:9"> So that day Laban removed the male goats that were streaked or spotted, all the female goats that were speckled or spotted (all that had any white on them), and all the dark-colored lambs, and put them in the care of his sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Then he separated them from Jacob by a three-day journey, while Jacob was taking care of the rest of Laban’s flocks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 31:9–13; Ezekiel 31:8"> But Jacob took fresh-cut branches from poplar, almond, and plane trees. He made white streaks by peeling them, making the white inner wood in the branches visible.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> Then he set up the peeled branches in all the watering troughs where the flocks came to drink. He set up the branches in front of the flocks when they were in heat and came to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 12:35–36; Genesis 31:9–12; Genesis 31:40"> When the sheep mated in front of the branches, they gave birth to young that were streaked or speckled or spotted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> Jacob removed these lambs, but he made the rest of the flock face the streaked and completely dark-colored animals in Laban’s flock. So he made separate flocks for himself and did not mix them with Laban’s flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> When the stronger females were in heat, Jacob would set up the branches in the troughs in front of the flock, so they would mate near the branches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> But if the animals were weaker, he did not set the branches there. So the weaker animals ended up belonging to Laban and the stronger animals to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:35; Genesis 30:30; Genesis 26:13–14"> In this way Jacob became extremely prosperous. He owned large flocks, male and female servants, camels, and donkeys.</span>
</div>
</div>
