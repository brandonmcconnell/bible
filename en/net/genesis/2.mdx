## The Creation of Man and Woman

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 5:1; Revelation 1:8; Genesis 1:4"> This is the account of the heavens and the earth when they were created – when the Lord God made the earth and heavens.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 3:23; Hebrews 6:7; Job 5:10"> Now no shrub of the field had yet grown on the earth, and no plant of the field had yet sprouted, for the Lord God had not caused it to rain on the earth, and there was no man to cultivate the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Springs would well up from the earth and water the whole surface of the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 12:7; Job 33:4; 1 Corinthians 15:45"> The Lord God formed the man from the soil of the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living being.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Genesis 3:24; Genesis 13:10"> The Lord God planted an orchard in the east, in Eden; and there he placed the man he had formed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 3:22; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:14"> The Lord God made all kinds of trees grow from the soil, every tree that was pleasing to look at and good for food. (Now the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil were in the middle of the orchard.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 22:1; Psalms 46:4"> Now a river flows from Eden to water the orchard, and from there it divides into four headstreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 25:18; Genesis 10:29; 1 Samuel 15:7"> The name of the first is Pishon; it runs through the entire land of Havilah, where there is gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:20; Numbers 11:7; Ezekiel 28:13"> (The gold of that land is pure; pearls and lapis lazuli are also there).</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 10:6; Isaiah 11:11"> The name of the second river is Gihon; it runs through the entire land of Cush.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:4; Genesis 15:18; Deuteronomy 1:7"> The name of the third river is Tigris; it runs along the east side of Assyria. The fourth river is the Euphrates.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 128:2; Genesis 2:8; Ephesians 4:28"> The Lord God took the man and placed him in the orchard in Eden to care for it and to maintain it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:22; 1 Timothy 4:4; 1 Timothy 6:17"> Then the Lord God commanded the man, “You may freely eat fruit from every tree of the orchard,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 6:23; James 1:15; Genesis 3:19"> but you must not eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, for when you eat from it you will surely die.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 4:9–12; 1 Corinthians 11:7–12; Proverbs 18:22"> The Lord God said, “It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make a companion for him who corresponds to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 8:4–8; Genesis 1:28; Genesis 9:2"> The Lord God formed out of the ground every living animal of the field and every bird of the air. He brought them to the man to see what he would name them, and whatever the man called each living creature, that was its name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:18"> So the man named all the animals, the birds of the air, and the living creatures of the field, but for Adam no companion who corresponded to him was found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 15:12; Job 4:13; 1 Samuel 26:12"> So the Lord God caused the man to fall into a deep sleep, and while he was asleep, he took part of the man’s side and closed up the place with flesh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 1 Timothy 2:13; Hebrews 13:4"> Then the Lord God made a woman from the part he had taken out of the man, and he brought her to the man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ephesians 5:28–30; 1 Corinthians 11:8–9; 2 Samuel 5:1"> Then the man said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“This one at last is bone of my bones</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and flesh of my flesh;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">this one will be called ‘woman,’</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">for she was taken out of man.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Ephesians 5:28–31; Mark 10:6–12"> That is why a man leaves his father and mother and unites with his wife, and they become a new family.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 3:7; Genesis 3:10–11; Romans 10:11"> The man and his wife were both naked, but they were not ashamed.</span>
</div>
</div>
