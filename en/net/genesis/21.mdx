<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 18:11–12; Isaiah 66:8; 2 Thessalonians 1:10"> She went on to say, “Who would have said to Abraham that Sarah would nurse children? Yet I have given birth to a son for him in his old age!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Galatians 4:29; Genesis 16:15; Galatians 4:22"> But Sarah noticed the son of Hagar the Egyptian – the son whom Hagar had borne to Abraham – mocking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 8:35; 1 John 2:19; Galatians 4:22–31"> So she said to Abraham, “Banish that slave woman and her son, for the son of that slave woman will not be an heir along with my son Isaac!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 12:11; Genesis 17:18; Matthew 10:37"> Sarah’s demand displeased Abraham greatly because Ishmael was his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:18; Romans 9:7–8; Genesis 17:19"> But God said to Abraham, “Do not be upset about the boy or your slave wife. Do all that Sarah is telling you because through Isaac your descendants will be counted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:18"> But I will also make the son of the slave wife into a great nation, for he is your descendant too.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 9:10; Psalms 119:60; Genesis 16:7"> Early in the morning Abraham took some food and a skin of water and gave them to Hagar. He put them on her shoulders, gave her the child, and sent her away. So she went wandering aimlessly through the wilderness of Beer Sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 3:9; Psalms 63:1; Jeremiah 14:3"> When the water in the skin was gone, she shoved the child under one of the shrubs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 49:15; Genesis 29:11; 1 Samuel 24:16"> Then she went and sat down by herself across from him at quite a distance, about a bowshot away; for she thought, “I refuse to watch the child die.” So she sat across from him and wept uncontrollably.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 16:11; Psalms 50:15; Exodus 3:7"> But God heard the boy’s voice. The angel of God called to Hagar from heaven and asked her, “What is the matter, Hagar? Don’t be afraid, for God has heard the boy’s voice right where he is crying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 21:13; Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20"> Get up! Help the boy up and hold him by the hand, for I will make him into a great nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Numbers 22:31; 2 Kings 6:17–20; Isaiah 35:5–6"> Then God enabled Hagar to see a well of water. She went over and filled the skin with water, and then gave the boy a drink.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 24:3–4; Numbers 12:16"> He lived in the wilderness of Paran. His mother found a wife for him from the land of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 3:7; Genesis 26:26; Genesis 26:28"> At that time Abimelech and Phicol, the commander of his army, said to Abraham, “God is with you in all that you do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Joshua 2:12; Genesis 20:14; Hebrews 6:16"> Now swear to me right here in God’s name that you will not deceive me, my children, or my descendants. Show me, and the land where you are staying, the same loyalty that I have shown you.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 6:16; Genesis 14:13; Romans 12:18"> Abraham said, “I swear to do this.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 26:15–22; Genesis 29:8; Proverbs 25:9"> But Abraham lodged a complaint against Abimelech concerning a well that Abimelech’s servants had seized.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 13:7; 2 Kings 5:20–24"> “I do not know who has done this thing,” Abimelech replied. “Moreover, you did not tell me. I did not hear about it until today.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 14:22–23; Isaiah 32:8; Romans 1:31"> Abraham took some sheep and cattle and gave them to Abimelech. The two of them made a treaty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Then Abraham set seven ewe lambs apart from the flock by themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:14; Genesis 33:8; Exodus 12:26"> Abimelech asked Abraham, “What is the meaning of these seven ewe lambs that you have set apart?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:27; Joshua 22:27–28"> He replied, “You must take these seven ewe lambs from my hand as legal proof that I dug this well.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 26:33; Genesis 21:14; 1 Kings 4:25"> That is why he named that place Beer Sheba, because the two of them swore an oath there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 13:17; Genesis 21:27; Judges 13:1"> So they made a treaty at Beer Sheba. Then Abimelech and Phicol, the commander of his army, returned to the land of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Isaiah 40:28; Psalms 90:2"> Abraham planted a tamarisk tree in Beer Sheba. There he worshiped the Lord, the eternal God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Chronicles 29:15; 1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:9"> So Abraham stayed in the land of the Philistines for quite some time.</span>
</div>
