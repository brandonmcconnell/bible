<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Genesis 32:6–7; Genesis 27:41–42; Genesis 32:16"> Jacob looked up and saw that Esau was coming along with four hundred men. So he divided the children among Leah, Rachel, and the two female servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 29:30; Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 37:3"> He put the servants and their children in front, with Leah and her children behind them, and Rachel and Joseph behind them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 42:6; Luke 14:11; Genesis 18:2"> But Jacob himself went on ahead of them, and he bowed toward the ground seven times as he approached his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 45:14–15; Psalms 34:4; Luke 15:20"> But Esau ran to meet him, embraced him, hugged his neck, and kissed him. Then they both wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 127:3; Isaiah 8:18; Genesis 48:9"> When Esau looked up and saw the women and the children, he asked, “Who are these people with you?” Jacob replied, “The children whom God has graciously given your servant.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> The female servants came forward with their children and bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Then Leah came forward with her children and they bowed down. Finally Joseph and Rachel came forward and bowed down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 32:5; Genesis 39:5; Genesis 32:13–20"> Esau then asked, “What did you intend by sending all these herds to meet me?” Jacob replied, “To find favor in your sight, my lord.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 27:39; Philemon 1:7; Genesis 27:41"> But Esau said, “I have plenty, my brother. Keep what belongs to you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Job 33:26; Revelation 22:4; Genesis 32:30"> “No, please take them,” Jacob said. “If I have found favor in your sight, accept my gift from my hand. Now that I have seen your face and you have accepted me, it is as if I have seen the face of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:27; Philippians 4:18; Genesis 30:43"> Please take my present that was brought to you, for God has been generous to me and I have all I need.” When Jacob urged him, he took it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then Esau said, “Let’s be on our way! I will go in front of you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:15–16; Ezekiel 34:23–25"> But Jacob said to him, “My lord knows that the children are young, and that I have to look after the sheep and cattle that are nursing their young. If they are driven too hard for even a single day, all the animals will die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 32:3; Isaiah 40:11; Romans 15:1"> Let my lord go on ahead of his servant. I will travel more slowly, at the pace of the herds and the children, until I come to my lord at Seir.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ruth 2:13; Genesis 47:25; Genesis 34:11"> So Esau said, “Let me leave some of my men with you.” “Why do that?” Jacob replied. “My lord has already been kind enough to me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So that same day Esau made his way back to Seir.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 60:6; Joshua 13:27; Judges 8:5"> But Jacob traveled to Succoth where he built himself a house and made shelters for his livestock. That is why the place was called Succoth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 9:1; Acts 7:16; Joshua 24:1"> After he left Paddan Aram, Jacob came safely to the city of Shechem in the land of Canaan, and he camped near the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 7:16; John 4:5; Joshua 24:32"> Then he purchased the portion of the field where he had pitched his tent; he bought it from the sons of Hamor, Shechem’s father, for a hundred pieces of money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 35:7; Genesis 21:33; Genesis 32:28"> There he set up an altar and called it “The God of Israel is God.”</span>
