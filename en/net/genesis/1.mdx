## The Creation of the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 1:1–3; Hebrews 11:3; Isaiah 45:18"> In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:23; Psalms 104:30; Isaiah 45:18"> Now the earth was without shape and empty, and darkness was over the surface of the watery deep, but the Spirit of God was moving over the surface of the water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:6; John 1:5; Isaiah 45:7"> God said, “Let there be light.” And there was light!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 2:13; Genesis 1:18; Ecclesiastes 11:7"> God saw that the light was good, so God separated the light from the darkness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 74:16; Isaiah 45:7; 1 Thessalonians 5:5"> God called the light “day” and the darkness “night.” There was evening, and there was morning, marking the first day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:15; Psalms 33:6; Job 37:18"> God said, “Let there be an expanse in the midst of the waters and let it separate water from water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 8:28–29; Psalms 148:4; Matthew 8:27"> So God made the expanse and separated the water under the expanse from the water above it. It was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 1:5; Genesis 1:19; Genesis 1:10"> God called the expanse “sky.” There was evening, and there was morning, a second day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Peter 3:5; Jonah 1:9; Jeremiah 5:22"> God said, “Let the water under the sky be gathered to one place and let dry ground appear.” It was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalms 104:31; Genesis 1:4"> God called the dry ground “land” and the gathered waters he called “seas.” God saw that it was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Psalms 104:14–17; Hebrews 6:7; Psalms 147:8"> God said, “Let the land produce vegetation: plants yielding seeds according to their kinds, and trees bearing fruit with seed in it according to their kinds.” It was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Mark 4:28; Isaiah 55:10–11; Matthew 13:24–26"> The land produced vegetation – plants yielding seeds according to their kinds, and trees bearing fruit with seed in it according to their kinds. God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> There was evening, and there was morning, a third day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 104:19–20; Deuteronomy 4:19; Jeremiah 31:35"> God said, “Let there be lights in the expanse of the sky to separate the day from the night, and let them be signs to indicate seasons and days and years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and let them serve as lights in the expanse of the sky to give light on the earth.” It was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Psalms 136:7–9; Deuteronomy 4:19; Psalms 8:3"> God made two great lights – the greater light to rule over the day and the lesser light to rule over the night. He made the stars also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 8:3; Psalms 8:1; Job 38:12"> God placed the lights in the expanse of the sky to shine on the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:35; Psalms 19:6"> to preside over the day and the night, and to separate the light from the darkness. God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> There was evening, and there was morning, a fourth day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 2:19; Psalms 104:24–25; 1 Kings 4:33"> God said, “Let the water swarm with swarms of living creatures and let birds fly above the earth across the expanse of the sky.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 104:24–26; Genesis 1:25; Genesis 8:19"> God created the great sea creatures and every living and moving thing with which the water swarmed, according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to its kind. God saw that it was good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 40:15; Genesis 8:17; Psalms 107:38"> God blessed them and said, “Be fruitful and multiply and fill the water in the seas, and let the birds multiply on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> There was evening, and there was morning, a fifth day.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 50:9–10; Genesis 6:20; Genesis 8:19"> God said, “Let the land produce living creatures according to their kinds: cattle, creeping things, and wild animals, each according to its kind.” It was so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Job 12:8–10; Genesis 2:19–20"> God made the wild animals according to their kinds, the cattle according to their kinds, and all the creatures that creep along the ground according to their kinds. God saw that it was good.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Colossians 3:10; Ephesians 4:24; Psalms 8:4–8"> Then God said, “Let us make<br />humankind in our image, after our likeness, so they may rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air, over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over all the creatures that move on the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 19:4; Ephesians 2:10; Genesis 5:1–2"> <span className="pl pl-1">God created humankind in his own image,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">in the image of God he created them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">male and female he created them.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 9:1; Genesis 9:7; Leviticus 26:9"> God blessed them and said to them, “Be fruitful and multiply! Fill the earth and subdue it! Rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air and every creature that moves on the ground.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 9:3; Psalms 104:14–15; Psalms 145:15–16"> Then God said, “I now give you every seed-bearing plant on the face of the entire earth and every tree that has fruit with seed in it. They will be yours for food.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Psalms 104:14; Psalms 145:15–16; Psalms 147:9"> And to all the animals of the earth, and to every bird of the air, and to all the creatures that move on the ground – everything that has the breath of life in it – I give every green plant for food.” It was so.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:4; Psalms 104:24; Psalms 19:1–2"> God saw all that he had made – and it was very good! There was evening, and there was morning, the sixth day.</span>
</div>
</div>
