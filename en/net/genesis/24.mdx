<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 2:17–20; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> But if the woman is not willing to come back with you, you will be free from this oath of mine. But you must not take my son back there!”</span>

<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Deuteronomy 23:4; Genesis 27:43"> Then the servant took ten of his master’s camels and departed with all kinds of gifts from his master at his disposal. He journeyed to the region of Aram Naharaim and the city of Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:11; Exodus 2:16; John 4:7"> He made the camels kneel down by the well outside the city. It was evening, the time when the women would go out to draw water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:27; Exodus 3:6; Genesis 26:24"> He prayed, “O Lord, God of my master Abraham, guide me today. Be faithful to my master Abraham.</span>

<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 6:37; Judges 6:17; Genesis 15:8"> I will say to a young woman, ‘Please lower your jar so I may drink.’ May the one you have chosen for your servant Isaac reply, ‘Drink, and I’ll give your camels water too.’ In this way I will know that you have been faithful to my master.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:45; Isaiah 65:24"> Before he had finished praying, there came Rebekah with her water jug on her shoulder. She was the daughter of Bethuel son of Milcah (Milcah was the wife of Abraham’s brother Nahor).</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Numbers 31:17–18; Genesis 39:6"> Now the young woman was very beautiful. She was a virgin; no man had ever had sexual relations with her. She went down to the spring, filled her jug, and came back up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:7; Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 26:1–35"> Abraham’s servant ran to meet her and said, “Please give me a sip of water from your jug.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 31:26; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 24:14"> “Drink, my lord,” she replied, and quickly lowering her jug to her hands, she gave him a drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 4:9; Genesis 24:45–46"> When she had done so, she said, “I’ll draw water for your camels too, until they have drunk as much as they want.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> She quickly emptied her jug into the watering trough and ran back to the well to draw more water until she had drawn enough for all his camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:43; Genesis 24:12; 2 Samuel 7:18–20"> Silently the man watched her with interest to determine if the Lord had made his journey successful or not.</span>

<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> “Whose daughter are you?” he asked. “Tell me, is there room in your father’s house for us to spend the night?”</span>

<div className="bp">

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Exodus 4:31; Genesis 24:52"> The man bowed his head and worshiped the Lord,</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 3:10; Genesis 26:29; Psalms 115:15"> Laban said to him, “Come, you who are blessed by the Lord! Why are you standing out here when I have prepared the house and a place for the camels?”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 19:21; Genesis 43:24; Genesis 18:4"> So Abraham’s servant went to the house and unloaded the camels. Straw and feed were given to the camels, and water was provided so that he and the men who were with him could wash their feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 132:3–5; 1 Timothy 6:2; Ephesians 6:5–8"> When food was served, he said, “I will not eat until I have said what I want to say.” “Tell us,” Laban said.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> “I am the servant of Abraham,” he began.</span>

<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 27:46; Ezra 9:1–3; Genesis 24:2–9"> My master made me swear an oath. He said, ‘You must not acquire a wife for my son from the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I am living,</span>

<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Psalms 16:8; Genesis 5:24"> He answered, ‘The Lord, before whom I have walked, will send his angel with you. <span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 24:8; Deuteronomy 29:12"> You will be free from your oath if you go to my relatives and they will not give her to you. Then you will be free from your oath.’</span>

<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:13–14"> Here I am, standing by the spring. When the young woman goes out to draw water, I’ll say, “Give me a little water to drink from your jug.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 2:22"> Then she will reply to me, “Drink, and I’ll draw water for your camels too.” May that woman be the one whom the Lord has chosen for my master’s son.’</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; Acts 12:12–17; Genesis 24:15–20"> “Before I finished praying in my heart, along came Rebekah with her water jug on her shoulder! She went down to the spring and drew water. So I said to her, ‘Please give me a drink.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:18–19"> She quickly lowered her jug from her shoulder and said, ‘Drink, and I’ll give your camels water too.’ So I drank, and she also gave the camels water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 45:13–14; Genesis 24:22–24; Isaiah 62:3–5"> Then I asked her, ‘Whose daughter are you?’ She replied, ‘The daughter of Bethuel the son of Nahor, whom Milcah bore to Nahor.’ I put the ring in her nose and the bracelets on her wrists.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Genesis 24:26–27; Psalms 48:14"> Then I bowed down and worshiped the Lord. I praised the Lord, the God of my master Abraham, who had led me on the right path to find the granddaughter of my master’s brother for his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Joshua 2:14; Proverbs 3:3"> Now, if you will show faithful love to my master, tell me. But if not, tell me as well, so that I may go on my way.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 31:29"> Then Laban and Bethuel replied, “This is the Lord’s doing. Our wishes are of no concern.</span>

</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:48; Psalms 116:1–2"> When Abraham’s servant heard their words, he bowed down to the ground before the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 24:22; Genesis 24:10; Exodus 12:35"> Then he brought out gold, silver jewelry, and clothing and gave them to Rebekah. He also gave valuable gifts to her brother and to her mother.</span>

<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 4:3; Leviticus 25:29; Judges 14:8"> But Rebekah’s brother and her mother replied, “Let the girl stay with us a few more days, perhaps ten. Then she can go.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Proverbs 25:25; Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 48:15"> But he said to them, “Don’t detain me – the Lord has granted me success on my journey. Let me leave now so I may return to my master.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Then they said, “We’ll call the girl and find out what she wants to do.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:38; Psalms 45:10–11"> So they called Rebekah and asked her, “Do you want to go with this man?” She replied, “I want to go.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; Numbers 11:12; Genesis 24:50"> So they sent their sister Rebekah on her way, accompanied by her female attendant, with Abraham’s servant and his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 17:16; Ruth 4:11–12"> They blessed Rebekah with these words:<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Our sister, may you become the mother of thousands of ten thousands!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">May your descendants possess the strongholds of their enemies.”</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 45:10; Genesis 2:24; Esther 8:14"> Then Rebekah and her female servants mounted the camels and rode away with the man. So Abraham’s servant took Rebekah and left.</span>

<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 1:14; Joshua 15:18"> Rebekah looked up and saw Isaac. She got down from her camel</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; 1 Corinthians 11:5–6; 1 Corinthians 11:10"> and asked Abraham’s servant, “Who is that man walking in the field toward us?” “That is my master,” the servant replied. So she took her veil and covered herself.</span>
