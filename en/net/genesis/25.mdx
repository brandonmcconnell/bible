<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:29–31; Isaiah 60:7; Song of Solomon 1:5"> These are the names of Ishmael’s sons, by their names according to their records: Nebaioth (Ishmael’s firstborn), Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Isaiah 21:11; Isaiah 21:16"> Mishma, Dumah, Massa,</span>

<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 17:20; Genesis 17:23"> These are the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names by their settlements and their camps – twelve princes according to their clans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 25:7–8"> Ishmael lived a total of 137 years. He breathed his last and died; then he joined his ancestors.</span>

## Jacob and Esau

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 1:2; Luke 3:34; Acts 7:8"> This is the account of Isaac, the son of Abraham. Abraham became the father of Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 24:29; Genesis 24:67; Genesis 22:23"> When Isaac was forty years old, he married Rebekah, the daughter of Bethuel the Aramean from Paddan Aram and sister of Laban the Aramean.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:13; Isaiah 58:9; Ezra 8:23"> Isaac prayed to the Lord on behalf of his wife because she was childless. The Lord answered his prayer, and his wife Rebekah became pregnant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:9; 1 Samuel 28:6; 1 Samuel 30:8"> But the children struggled inside her, and she said, “If it is going to be like this, I’m not so sure I want to be pregnant!” So she asked the Lord,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Romans 9:10–13; Genesis 27:40; Genesis 27:29"> and the Lord said to her,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“Two nations are in your womb,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and two peoples will be separated from within you.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">One people will be stronger than the other,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the older will serve the younger.”</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> When the time came for Rebekah to give birth, there were twins in her womb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 27:11; Genesis 27:16; Genesis 27:23"> The first came out reddish all over, like a hairy garment, so they named him Esau.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 27:36; Hosea 12:3; Genesis 38:28–30"> When his brother came out with his hand clutching Esau’s heel, they named him Jacob. Isaac was sixty years old when they were born.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hebrews 11:9; Genesis 21:20; Genesis 10:9"> When the boys grew up, Esau became a skilled hunter, a man of the open fields, but Jacob was an even-tempered man, living in tents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 27:19; Genesis 27:31; Genesis 27:4"> Isaac loved Esau because he had a taste for fresh game, but Rebekah loved Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 13:25; Isaiah 40:30–31; 1 Samuel 14:28"> Now Jacob cooked some stew, and when Esau came in from the open fields, he was famished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 36:1; Exodus 15:15; Genesis 36:9"> So Esau said to Jacob, “Feed me some of the red stuff – yes, this red stuff – because I’m starving!” (That is why he was also called Edom.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> But Jacob replied, “First sell me your birthright.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Malachi 3:14; Job 21:15; Job 22:17"> “Look,” said Esau, “I’m about to die! What use is the birthright to me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 12:16; Genesis 27:36; Hebrews 6:16"> But Jacob said, “Swear an oath to me now.” So Esau swore an oath to him and sold his birthright to Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Philippians 3:18–19; Hebrews 12:16–17; 1 Corinthians 15:32"> Then Jacob gave Esau some bread and lentil stew; Esau ate and drank, then got up and went out. So Esau despised his birthright.</span>
</div>
