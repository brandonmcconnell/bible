<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 43:2; Genesis 32:7; Genesis 31:3"> Let us go up at once to Bethel. Then I will make an altar there to God, who responded to me in my time of distress and has been with me wherever I went.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Judges 9:6; Deuteronomy 7:25; Exodus 32:20"> So they gave Jacob all the foreign gods that were in their possession and the rings that were in their ears. Jacob buried them under the oak near Shechem</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 23:27; 2 Chronicles 17:10; Joshua 5:1"> and they started on their journey. The surrounding cities were afraid of God, and they did not pursue the sons of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 28:19; Genesis 28:22; Genesis 12:8"> Jacob and all those who were with him arrived at Luz (that is, Bethel) in the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Genesis 35:3; Genesis 35:1; Genesis 28:13"> He built an altar there and named the place El Bethel because there God had revealed himself to him when he was fleeing from his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 24:59; 1 Samuel 31:13; Judges 2:5"> (Deborah, Rebekah’s nurse, died and was buried under the oak below Bethel; thus it was named Oak of Weeping.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 35:1; Genesis 18:1; Genesis 26:2"> God appeared to Jacob again after he returned from Paddan Aram and blessed him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 17:5; Genesis 32:27–28; Genesis 17:15"> God said to him, “Your name is Jacob, but your name will no longer be called Jacob; Israel will be your name.” So God named him Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 17:1; Genesis 9:1; Genesis 22:17"> Then God said to him, “I am the sovereign God. Be fruitful and multiply! A nation – even a company of nations – will descend from you; kings will be among your descendants!</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 12:7; Genesis 28:13; Genesis 26:3–4"> The land I gave to Abraham and Isaac I will give to you. To your descendants I will also give this land.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 17:22; Genesis 18:33; Judges 6:21"> Then God went up from the place where he spoke with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–19; 1 Samuel 7:12; Exodus 17:15"> So Jacob set up a sacred stone pillar in the place where God spoke with him. He poured out a drink offering on it, and then he poured oil on it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 28:19"> Jacob named the place where God spoke with him Bethel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Genesis 48:7; 2 Kings 5:19"> They traveled on from Bethel, and when Ephrath was still some distance away, Rachel went into labor – and her labor was hard.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 30:24; 1 Samuel 4:19–21"> When her labor was at its hardest, the midwife said to her, “Don’t be afraid, for you are having another son.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 12:20; Acts 7:59; Luke 23:46"> With her dying breath, she named him Ben-Oni. But his father called him Benjamin instead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 5:2; Genesis 48:7; Ruth 4:11"> So Rachel died and was buried on the way to Ephrath (that is, Bethlehem).</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Samuel 10:2; Genesis 35:14; 2 Samuel 18:17–18"> Jacob set up a marker over her grave; it is the Marker of Rachel’s Grave to this day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Micah 4:8; Luke 2:8"> Then Israel traveled on and pitched his tent beyond Migdal Eder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="1 Chronicles 5:1; Leviticus 18:8; 2 Samuel 20:3"> While Israel was living in that land, Reuben had sexual relations with Bilhah, his father’s concubine, and Israel heard about it. Jacob had twelve sons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 30:18–20; Genesis 46:8–15; Genesis 29:32–35"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sons of Leah were Reuben, Jacob’s firstborn, as well as Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 30:22–24; Genesis 46:19–22; Genesis 35:16–18"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sons of Rachel were Joseph and Benjamin.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 46:23–25; Genesis 37:2; Genesis 30:4–8"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sons of Bilhah, Rachel’s servant, were Dan and Naphtali.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 30:9–13; Genesis 46:16–18; Genesis 25:20"> <span className="pl pl-1">The sons of Zilpah, Leah’s servant, were Gad and Asher.</span>These were the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Paddan Aram.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 13:18; Genesis 23:19; Genesis 18:1"> So Jacob came back to his father Isaac in Mamre, to Kiriath Arba (that is, Hebron), where Abraham and Isaac had stayed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 25:7; Genesis 50:26; Genesis 47:28"> Isaac lived to be 180 years old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 15:15; Genesis 49:33; Genesis 49:31"> Then Isaac breathed his last and joined his ancestors. He died an old man who had lived a full life. His sons Esau and Jacob buried him.</span>
