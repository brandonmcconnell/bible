## Cleansing the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 12:46; Matthew 13:55–56; Mark 6:3"> After this he went down to Capernaum with his mother and brothers and his disciples, and they stayed there a few days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 2:41; John 11:55; John 2:23"> Now the Jewish feast of Passover was near, so Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 19:45–46; Mark 11:15; Mark 11:17"> He found in the temple courts those who were selling oxen and sheep and doves, and the money changers sitting at tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 18:6; Zechariah 4:6; 2 Corinthians 10:4"> So he made a whip of cords and drove them all out of the temple courts, with the sheep and the oxen. He scattered the coins of the money changers and overturned their tables.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:13; Jeremiah 7:11; Mark 11:17"> To those who sold the doves he said, “Take these things away from here! Do not make my Father’s house a marketplace!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 69:9; Psalms 119:139"> His disciples remembered that it was written, “Zeal for your house will devour me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 21:23; Mark 8:11; Mark 11:27–28"> So then the Jewish leaders responded, “What sign can you show us, since you are doing these things?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 14:58; Mark 15:29; Matthew 27:40"> Jesus replied, “Destroy this temple and in three days I will raise it up again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Then the Jewish leaders said to him, “This temple has been under construction for forty-six years, and are you going to raise it up in three days?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 6:19; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:20–22"> But Jesus was speaking about the temple of his body.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 12:16; John 16:4; Luke 24:7–8"> So after he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered that he had said this, and they believed the scripture and the saying that Jesus had spoken.</span>
