<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 26:55; Luke 4:20; Luke 21:37"> Early in the morning he came to the temple courts again. All the people came to him, and he sat down and began to teach them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> The experts in the law and the Pharisees brought a woman who had been caught committing adultery. They made her stand in front of them</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and said to Jesus, “Teacher, this woman was caught in the very act of adultery.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Leviticus 20:10; Deuteronomy 22:21–24; Ezekiel 16:38–40"> In the law Moses commanded us to stone to death such women. What then do you say?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:13; Luke 10:25; Ecclesiastes 3:7"> (Now they were asking this in an attempt to trap him, so that they could bring charges against him.) Jesus bent down and wrote on the ground with his finger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 7:1–5; Romans 2:21–25; Romans 2:1–3"> When they persisted in asking him, he stood up straight and replied, “Whoever among you is guiltless may be the first to throw a stone at her.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Then he bent over again and wrote on the ground.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 7:22; Romans 2:15; 1 John 3:20"> Now when they heard this, they began to drift away one at a time, starting with the older ones, until Jesus was left alone with the woman standing before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 41:11–12"> Jesus stood up straight and said to her, “Woman, where are they? Did no one condemn you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 3:17; John 5:14; Isaiah 1:16–18"> She replied, “No one, Lord.” And Jesus said, “I do not condemn you either. Go, and from now on do not sin any more.”&#93;&#93;</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus as the Light of the World

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 12:46; John 9:5; John 12:35"> Then Jesus spoke out again, “I am the light of the world. The one who follows me will never walk in darkness, but will have the light of life.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:31–47"> So the Pharisees objected, “You testify about yourself; your testimony is not true!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:28; John 13:3; John 8:42"> Jesus answered, “Even if I testify about myself, my testimony is true, because I know where I came from and where I am going. But you people do not know where I came from or where I am going.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 16:7; John 7:24; John 12:47"> You people judge by outward appearances; I do not judge anyone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Acts 17:31; John 8:29; John 16:32"> But if I judge, my evaluation is accurate, because I am not alone when I judge, but I and the Father who sent me do so together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; Deuteronomy 17:6; Hebrews 10:28"> It is written in your law that the testimony of two men is true.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 8:38; John 8:58; John 8:25"> I testify about myself and the Father who sent me testifies about me.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:3; John 8:54–55; John 14:6–9"> Then they began asking him, “Who is your father?” Jesus answered, “You do not know either me or my Father. If you knew me you would know my Father too.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 7:30; John 7:8; Mark 12:41"> (Jesus spoke these words near the offering box while he was teaching in the temple courts. No one seized him because his time had not yet come.)</span>
</div>
</div>

## Where Jesus Came From and Where He is Going

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:34; John 8:24; 1 Corinthians 15:17–18"> Then Jesus said to them again, “I am going away, and you will look for me but will die in your sin. Where I am going you cannot come.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 8:52; Psalms 22:6; John 8:48"> So the Jewish leaders began to say, “Perhaps he is going to kill himself, because he says, ‘Where I am going you cannot come.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 3:31; John 17:14; John 17:16"> Jesus replied, “You people are from below; I am from above. You people are from this world; I am not from this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 3:36; Mark 16:16; John 3:18"> Thus I told you that you will die in your sins. For unless you believe that I am he, you will die in your sins.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 5:17–29; Luke 22:67; John 1:22"> So they said to him, “Who are you?” Jesus replied, “What I have told you from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:15; John 12:47–50; John 8:40"> I have many things to say and to judge about you, but the Father who sent me is truthful, and the things I have heard from him I speak to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 6:9; John 8:43; 2 Corinthians 4:3–4"> (They did not understand that he was telling them about his Father.)</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 5:19; John 5:30; John 6:38"> Then Jesus said, “When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am he, and I do nothing on my own initiative, but I speak just what the Father taught me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 6:38; John 16:32; John 4:34"> And the one who sent me is with me. He has not left me alone, because I always do those things that please him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 7:31; John 2:23; John 11:45"> While he was saying these things, many people believed in him.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Abraham’s Children and the Devil’s Children

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 John 1:9; John 15:4–9; 2 Timothy 3:14"> Then Jesus said to those Judeans who had believed him, “If you continue to follow my teaching, you are really my disciples</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Romans 8:2; John 8:36; John 17:17"> and you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 3:9; John 8:39; Genesis 15:13"> “We are descendants of Abraham,” they replied, “and have never been anyone’s slaves! How can you say, ‘You will become free’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Romans 6:16; 2 Peter 2:19; Proverbs 5:22"> Jesus answered them, “I tell you the solemn truth, everyone who practices sin is a slave of sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Galatians 4:4–7; Luke 15:31; Romans 8:15–17"> The slave does not remain in the family forever, but the son remains forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:17; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2"> So if the son sets you free, you will be really free.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; John 8:45–47; John 7:1"> I know that you are Abraham’s descendants. But you want to kill me, because my teaching makes no progress among you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:19; John 8:44; John 8:41"> I am telling you the things I have seen while with the Father; as for you, practice the things you have heard from the Father!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Galatians 3:7; John 8:37; Romans 9:7"> They answered him, “Abraham is our father!” Jesus replied, “If you are Abraham’s children, you would be doing the deeds of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 8:26; Galatians 4:29; Galatians 4:16"> But now you are trying to kill me, a man who has told you the truth I heard from God. Abraham did not do this!</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Isaiah 64:8; John 8:38; Isaiah 63:16"> You people are doing the deeds of your father.”<br />Then they said to Jesus, “We were not born as a result of immorality! We have only one Father, God himself.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 12:49; John 14:10; John 17:8"> Jesus replied, “If God were your Father, you would love me, for I have come from God and am now here. I have not come on my own initiative, but he sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="John 12:39–40; Romans 8:7–8; John 7:17"> Why don’t you understand what I am saying? It is because you cannot accept my teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Acts 13:10; 1 John 3:12; 1 John 3:8–10"> You people are from your father the devil, and you want to do what your father desires. He was a murderer from the beginning, and does not uphold the truth, because there is no truth in him. Whenever he lies, he speaks according to his own nature, because he is a liar and the father of lies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:3–4; Galatians 4:16; John 18:37"> But because I am telling you the truth, you do not believe me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Hebrews 4:15; John 16:8; 1 Peter 2:22"> Who among you can prove me guilty of any sin? If I am telling you the truth, why don’t you believe me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 10:26–27; 2 John 1:9; John 18:37"> The one who belongs to God listens and responds to God’s words. You don’t listen and respond, because you don’t belong to God.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 7:20; John 8:52; Matthew 10:5"> The Judeans replied, “Aren’t we correct in saying that you are a Samaritan and are possessed by a demon?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Peter 2:23; Isaiah 49:3; John 13:31–32"> Jesus answered, “I am not possessed by a demon, but I honor my Father – and yet you dishonor me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 5:41; John 8:54; John 7:18"> I am not trying to get praise for myself. There is one who demands it, and he also judges.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="John 11:25–26; John 5:24; John 3:15–16"> I tell you the solemn truth, if anyone obeys my teaching, he will never see death.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:24; John 8:48; Hebrews 2:9"> Then the Judeans responded, “Now we know you’re possessed by a demon! Both Abraham and the prophets died, and yet you say, ‘If anyone obeys my teaching, he will never experience death.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="John 4:12; Romans 9:5; Hebrews 7:1–7"> You aren’t greater than our father Abraham who died, are you? And the prophets died too! Who do you claim to be?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 8:50; Proverbs 25:27; 2 Peter 1:17"> Jesus replied, “If I glorify myself, my glory is worthless. The one who glorifies me is my Father, about whom you people say, ‘He is our God.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 8:19; John 8:44; John 7:28–29"> Yet you do not know him, but I know him. If I were to say that I do not know him, I would be a liar like you. But I do know him, and I obey his teaching.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Hebrews 11:13; Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:7–9"> Your father Abraham was overjoyed to see my day, and he saw it and was glad.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Then the Judeans replied, “You are not yet fifty years old! Have you seen Abraham?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Exodus 3:14; Colossians 1:17; John 17:5"> Jesus said to them, “I tell you the solemn truth, before Abraham came into existence, I am!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 11:8; John 10:30–33; John 10:39–40"> Then they picked up stones to throw at him, but Jesus hid himself and went out from the temple area.</span>
</div>
</div>
