<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 50:6; Matthew 20:19"> Then Pilate took Jesus and had him flogged severely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 15:16–20; Luke 23:11; Matthew 27:27–31"> The soldiers braided a crown of thorns and put it on his head, and they clothed him in a purple robe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 27:29; Matthew 26:49; Luke 1:28"> They came up to him again and again and said, “Hail, king of the Jews!” And they struck him repeatedly in the face.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 18:38; John 19:6; Luke 23:4"> Again Pilate went out and said to the Jewish leaders, “Look, I am bringing him out to you, so that you may know that I find no reason for an accusation against him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 19:2; Hebrews 12:2; Isaiah 7:14"> So Jesus came outside, wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe. Pilate said to them, “Look, here is the man!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 18:31; John 19:4; Acts 2:23"> When the chief priests and their officers saw him, they shouted out, “Crucify him! Crucify him!” Pilate said, “You take him and crucify him! Certainly I find no reason for an accusation against him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Leviticus 24:16; John 5:18; Matthew 26:63–66"> The Jewish leaders replied, “We have a law, and according to our law he ought to die, because he claimed to be the Son of God!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 19:13; Acts 14:11–19"> When Pilate heard what they said, he was more afraid than ever,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 18:33; Psalms 38:13–15; Philippians 1:28"> and he went back into the governor’s residence and said to Jesus, “Where do you come from?” But Jesus gave him no answer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 5:19; John 18:39; Daniel 3:14–15"> So Pilate said, “Do you refuse to speak to me? Don’t you know I have the authority to release you, and to crucify you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 13:1; John 3:27; Acts 2:23"> Jesus replied, “You would have no authority over me at all, unless it was given to you from above. Therefore the one who handed me over to you is guilty of greater sin.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 17:6–7; John 18:33–36; Mark 6:16–26"> From this point on, Pilate tried to release him. But the Jewish leaders shouted out, “If you release this man, you are no friend of Caesar! Everyone who claims to be a king opposes Caesar!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 82:5–7; Matthew 27:19; Amos 4:7"> When Pilate heard these words he brought Jesus outside and sat down on the judgment seat in the place called “The Stone Pavement” (Gabbatha in Aramaic).</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 27:62; John 19:5; Mark 15:25"> (Now it was the day of preparation for the Passover, about noon. ) Pilate said to the Jewish leaders, “Look, here is your king!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 23:18; Acts 21:36; John 19:6"> Then they shouted out, “Away with him! Away with him! Crucify him!” Pilate asked, “Shall I crucify your king?” The high priests replied, “We have no king except Caesar!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Luke 23:24–25; Matthew 27:26–31; Mark 15:15–20"> Then Pilate handed him over to them to be crucified. The Crucifixion<br />So they took Jesus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:33; Luke 14:27; Luke 23:26"> and carrying his own cross he went out to the place called “The Place of the Skull” (called in Aramaic Golgotha).</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:12; Mark 15:24–27; Galatians 3:13"> There they crucified him along with two others, one on each side, with Jesus in the middle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 27:37; Luke 23:38; Mark 15:26"> Pilate also had a notice written and fastened to the cross, which read: “Jesus the Nazarene, the king of the Jews.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 26:14; Revelation 16:16; Acts 22:2"> Thus many of the Jewish residents of Jerusalem read this notice, because the place where Jesus was crucified was near the city, and the notice was written in Aramaic, Latin, and Greek.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:19"> Then the chief priests of the Jews said to Pilate, “Do not write, ‘The king of the Jews,’ but rather, ‘This man said, I am king of the Jews.’”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Psalms 76:10; Psalms 65:7; Proverbs 8:29"> Pilate answered, “What I have written, I have written.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Mark 15:24; Matthew 27:35"> Now when the soldiers crucified Jesus, they took his clothes and made four shares, one for each soldier, and the tunic remained. (Now the tunic was seamless, woven from top to bottom as a single piece.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 22:18; John 19:28; John 19:36–37"> So the soldiers said to one another, “Let’s not tear it, but throw dice to see who will get it.” This took place to fulfill the scripture that says, “They divided my garments among them, and for my clothing they threw dice.” So the soldiers did these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 23:49; Matthew 27:55–56; John 20:1"> Now standing beside Jesus’ cross were his mother, his mother’s sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 21:24; John 20:2; John 2:4"> So when Jesus saw his mother and the disciple whom he loved standing there, he said to his mother, “Woman, look, here is your son!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 12:48–50; John 16:32; 1 Timothy 5:2–4"> He then said to his disciple, “Look, here is your mother!” From that very time the disciple took her into his own home.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Death

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 69:21; Psalms 22:15; John 19:30"> After this Jesus, realizing that by this time everything was completed, said (in order to fulfill the scripture), “I am thirsty!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Numbers 19:18; Luke 23:36; Mark 15:36"> A jar full of sour wine was there, so they put a sponge soaked in sour wine on a branch of hyssop and lifted it to his mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 17:4; Philippians 2:8; Colossians 2:14–17"> When he had received the sour wine, Jesus said, “It is completed!” Then he bowed his head and gave up his spirit.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 19:14; John 19:42; Mark 15:42"> Then, because it was the day of preparation, so that the bodies should not stay on the crosses on the Sabbath (for that Sabbath was an especially important one), the Jewish leaders asked Pilate to have the victims’ legs broken and the bodies taken down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 19:18; Luke 23:39–43"> So the soldiers came and broke the legs of the two men who had been crucified with Jesus, first the one and then the other.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> But when they came to Jesus and saw that he was already dead, they did not break his legs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 John 5:6; Zechariah 12:10; Zechariah 13:1"> But one of the soldiers pierced his side with a spear, and blood and water flowed out immediately.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 21:24; Hebrews 2:3–4; 1 John 1:1–3"> And the person who saw it has testified (and his testimony is true, and he knows that he is telling the truth), so that you also may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Psalms 34:20; Numbers 9:12; Exodus 12:46"> For these things happened so that the scripture would be fulfilled, “Not a bone of his will be broken.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Revelation 1:7; Psalms 22:16–17"> And again another scripture says, “They will look on the one whom they have pierced.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Burial

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Mark 15:42–47; Matthew 27:57–61; Proverbs 29:25"> After this, Joseph of Arimathea, a disciple of Jesus (but secretly, because he feared the Jewish leaders ), asked Pilate if he could remove the body of Jesus. Pilate gave him permission, so he went and took the body away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 7:50–52; Song of Solomon 4:14; John 3:1–21"> Nicodemus, the man who had previously come to Jesus at night, accompanied Joseph, carrying a mixture of myrrh and aloes weighing about seventy-five pounds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:44; John 20:5–7; Acts 5:6"> Then they took Jesus’ body and wrapped it, with the aromatic spices, in strips of linen cloth according to Jewish burial customs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Luke 23:53; Matthew 27:60; John 20:15"> Now at the place where Jesus was crucified there was a garden, and in the garden was a new tomb where no one had yet been buried.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 19:31; John 19:14; Isaiah 53:9"> And so, because it was the Jewish day of preparation and the tomb was nearby, they placed Jesus’ body there.</span>
</div>
