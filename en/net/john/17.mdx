## Jesus Prays for the Disciples

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 17:26; John 14:21–24; 1 John 5:20"> “I have revealed your name to the men you gave me out of the world. They belonged to you, and you gave them to me, and they have obeyed your word.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 7:16–17; John 16:15; John 14:7–10"> Now they understand that everything you have given me comes from you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 17:14; John 15:15; John 16:30"> because I have given them the words you have given me. They accepted them and really understand that I came from you, and they believed that you sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Hebrews 9:24; John 17:2; John 17:6"> I am praying on behalf of them. I am not praying on behalf of the world, but on behalf of those you have given me, because they belong to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 10:30; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 5:8–14"> Everything I have belongs to you, and everything you have belongs to me, and I have been glorified by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 6:9; Proverbs 18:10; John 17:12–18"> I am no longer in the world, but they are in the world, and I am coming to you. Holy Father, keep them safe in your name that you have given me, so that they may be one just as we are one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 109:6–19; John 10:27–28; John 18:9"> When I was with them I kept them safe and watched over them in your name that you have given me. Not one of them was lost except the one destined for destruction, so that the scripture could be fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 12:2; Galatians 5:22; John 15:11"> But now I am coming to you, and I am saying these things in the world, so they may experience my joy completed in themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:16; John 15:18–21; 1 John 5:19–20"> I have given them your word, and the world has hated them, because they do not belong to the world, just as I do not belong to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 121:7; 1 John 5:18; Matthew 6:13"> I am not asking you to take them out of the world, but that you keep them safe from the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 17:14"> They do not belong to the world just as I do not belong to the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:3; Psalms 119:160; Ephesians 5:26"> Set them apart in the truth; your word is truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:20; John 20:21; John 17:3"> Just as you sent me into the world, so I sent them into the world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Titus 2:14; Hebrews 2:11; John 17:17"> And I set myself apart on their behalf, so that they too may be truly set apart.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus Prays for Believers Everywhere

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:6–11; Acts 2:41; Acts 4:4"> “I am not praying only on their behalf, but also on behalf of those who believe in me through their testimony,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:12; Colossians 3:11–14; John 17:11"> that they will all be one, just as you, Father, are in me and I am in you. I pray that they will be in us, so that the world will believe that you sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:18; John 14:20; Mark 16:17–20"> The glory you gave to me I have given to them, that they may be one just as we are one –</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 17:21; John 16:27; 1 John 3:1"> I in them and you in me – that they may be completely one, so that the world will know that you sent me, and you have loved them just as you have loved me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 17:5; John 12:26; John 14:3"> “Father, I want those you have given me to be with me where I am, so that they can see my glory that you gave me because you loved me before the creation of the world .</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:11; John 7:29; John 17:8"> Righteous Father, even if the world does not know you, I know you, and these men know that you sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 17:23; John 17:6; John 15:9"> I made known your name to them, and I will continue to make it known, so that the love you have loved me with may be in them, and I may be in them.”</span>
