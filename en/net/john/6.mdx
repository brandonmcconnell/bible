<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 1:53; Nehemiah 9:25; Nehemiah 8:10"> When they were all satisfied, Jesus said to his disciples, “Gather up the broken pieces that are left over, so that nothing is wasted.”</span>

## Walking on Water

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Now when evening came, his disciples went down to the lake,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Mark 6:45; John 6:24–25; John 2:12"> got into a boat, and started to cross the lake to Capernaum. (It had already become dark, and Jesus had not yet come to them.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Psalms 107:25; Psalms 135:7; Matthew 14:24"> By now a strong wind was blowing and the sea was getting rough.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Mark 6:47–49; Matthew 14:25–26; Job 9:8"> Then, when they had rowed about three or four miles, they caught sight of Jesus walking on the lake, approaching the boat, and they were frightened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 14:27–31; Isaiah 43:1–2; Isaiah 44:8"> But he said to them, “It is I. Do not be afraid.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Mark 6:51; Matthew 14:32–33; Revelation 3:20"> Then they wanted to take him into the boat, and immediately the boat came to the land where they had been heading.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 6:45; Matthew 14:22; John 6:2"> The next day the crowd that remained on the other side of the lake realized that only one small boat had been there, and that Jesus had not boarded it with his disciples, but that his disciples had gone away alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:1; John 6:11–12"> But some boats from Tiberias came to shore near the place where they had eaten the bread after the Lord had given thanks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 6:17; Luke 8:40; Mark 6:53"> So when the crowd realized that neither Jesus nor his disciples were there, they got into the boats and came to Capernaum looking for Jesus.</span>
</div>
</div>

## Jesus’ Discourse About the Bread of Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 1:38–39; Matthew 23:7"> When they found him on the other side of the lake, they said to him, “Rabbi, when did you get here?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="James 4:3–4; Philippians 3:19; Philippians 2:21"> Jesus replied, “I tell you the solemn truth, you are looking for me not because you saw miraculous signs, but because you ate all the loaves of bread you wanted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Colossians 3:2; Isaiah 55:2; John 6:54"> Do not work for the food that disappears, but for the food that remains to eternal life – the food which the Son of Man will give to you. For God the Father has put his seal of approval on him.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Acts 16:30; Acts 9:6; Matthew 19:16"> So then they said to him, “What must we do to accomplish the deeds God requires?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 John 3:23; Acts 16:31; John 3:36"> Jesus replied, “This is the deed God requires – to believe in the one whom he sent.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 12:37; John 10:38; 1 Corinthians 1:22"> So they said to him, “Then what miraculous sign will you perform, so that we may see it and believe you? What will you do?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:15; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24–25"> Our ancestors ate the manna in the wilderness, just as it is written, ‘He gave them bread from heaven to eat.’”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:1; John 6:58; Exodus 16:4"> Then Jesus told them, “I tell you the solemn truth, it is not Moses who has given you the bread from heaven, but my Father is giving you the true bread from heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 8:42; John 17:8; John 3:13"> For the bread of God is the one who comes down from heaven and gives life to the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="John 4:15; John 6:26; Psalms 4:6"> So they said to him, “Sir, give us this bread all the time!”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="John 6:48–58; Revelation 22:17; John 6:41"> Jesus said to them, “I am the bread of life. The one who comes to me will never go hungry, and the one who believes in me will never be thirsty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8–9; Luke 16:31; John 12:37"> But I told you that you have seen me and still do not believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Matthew 11:28; John 6:39; John 17:24"> Everyone whom the Father gives me will come to me, and the one who comes to me I will never send away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 5:30; John 4:34; John 3:31"> For I have come down from heaven not to do my own will but the will of the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:40; John 18:9"> Now this is the will of the one who sent me – that I should not lose one person of every one he has given me, but raise them all up at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 6:54; John 5:24; Romans 6:23"> For this is the will of my Father – for everyone who looks on the Son and believes in him to have eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 6:51–52; John 6:48; John 6:33"> Then the Jews who were hostile to Jesus began complaining about him because he said, “I am the bread that came down from heaven,”</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Mark 6:3; Matthew 13:55–56"> and they said, “Isn’t this Jesus the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How can he now say, ‘I have come down from heaven’?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Hebrews 4:13; John 6:64; Mark 9:33"> Jesus replied, “Do not complain about me to one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 6:65; John 6:39–40; Matthew 11:25–27"> No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him, and I will raise him up at the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Isaiah 54:13; Jeremiah 31:33–34; Hebrews 10:16"> It is written in the prophets, ‘And they will all be taught by God.’ Everyone who hears and learns from the Father comes to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 1:18; John 7:29; Matthew 11:27"> (Not that anyone has seen the Father except the one who is from God – he has seen the Father.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="John 3:36; Colossians 3:3–4; Romans 5:9–10"> I tell you the solemn truth, the one who believes has eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="John 6:51; 1 Corinthians 11:24–25; 1 Corinthians 10:16–17"> I am the bread of life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3–5; Jude 1:5; Zechariah 1:5"> Your ancestors ate the manna in the wilderness, and they died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 6:33; John 6:51; Romans 8:10"> This is the bread that has come down from heaven, so that a person may eat from it and not die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Luke 22:19; John 6:33; Hebrews 10:5–12"> I am the living bread that came down from heaven. If anyone eats from this bread he will live forever. The bread that I will give for the life of the world is my flesh.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 9:16; John 10:19; John 6:41"> Then the Jews who were hostile to Jesus began to argue with one another, “How can this man give us his flesh to eat?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="1 John 5:12; Matthew 26:26–28; John 6:55"> Jesus said to them, “I tell you the solemn truth, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you have no life in yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 6:39–40; John 6:27; Philippians 3:7–10"> The one who eats my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up on the last day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="John 15:1; John 8:31; John 6:32"> For my flesh is true food, and my blood is true drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="1 John 3:24; John 15:4–5; John 14:20"> The one who eats my flesh and drinks my blood resides in me, and I in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="Galatians 2:20; John 5:26; John 17:21"> Just as the living Father sent me, and I live because of the Father, so the one who consumes me will live because of me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="John 3:36; John 6:47–51; John 6:41"> This is the bread that came down from heaven; it is not like the bread your ancestors ate, but then later died. The one who eats this bread will live forever.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Many Followers Depart

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="John 6:24; Proverbs 8:1–3; Luke 4:31"> Jesus said these things while he was teaching in the synagogue in Capernaum.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="John 6:66; John 8:43; John 8:31"> Then many of his disciples, when they heard these things, said, “This is a difficult saying! Who can understand it?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="John 2:24–25; John 6:64; John 21:17"> When Jesus was aware that his disciples were complaining about this, he said to them, “Does this cause you to be offended?</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Mark 16:19; John 3:13; John 17:4–5"> Then what if you see the Son of Man ascending where he was before?</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Galatians 5:25; Hebrews 4:12; Psalms 119:93"> The Spirit is the one who gives life; human nature is of no help! The words that I have spoken to you are spirit and are life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="John 10:26; 2 Timothy 2:19; Hebrews 4:13"> But there are some of you who do not believe.” (For Jesus had already known from the beginning who those were who did not believe, and who it was who would betray him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="John 6:44–45; John 6:37; John 3:27"> So Jesus added, “Because of this I told you that no one can come to me unless the Father has allowed him to come.”</span>
</div>

<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Ruth 1:11–18; John 6:70; Luke 14:25–33"> So Jesus said to the twelve, “You don’t want to go away too, do you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v68" data-v="68" data-xref="Psalms 73:25; John 5:24; 1 John 5:11–13"> Simon Peter answered him, “Lord, to whom would we go? You have the words of eternal life.</span>

<span className="v" id="v70" data-v="70" data-xref="John 13:27; John 13:2; John 17:12"> Jesus replied, “Didn’t I choose you, the twelve, and yet one of you is the devil?”</span>
