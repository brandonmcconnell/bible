<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; John 12:17; 2 Kings 20:1–12"> Now a certain man named Lazarus was sick. He was from Bethany, the village where Mary and her sister Martha lived.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 12:3; Mark 14:3; Matthew 26:6–7"> (Now it was Mary who anointed the Lord with perfumed oil and wiped his feet dry with her hair, whose brother Lazarus was sick.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="John 11:5; Revelation 3:19; 2 Timothy 4:20"> So the sisters sent a message to Jesus, “Lord, look, the one you love is sick.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 11:40; John 9:3; 1 Peter 4:14"> When Jesus heard this, he said, “This sickness will not lead to death, but to God’s glory, so that the Son of God may be glorified through it.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 15:9–13; John 16:27; John 17:26"> (Now Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 30:18; Isaiah 55:8–9; Genesis 45:1–5"> So when he heard that Lazarus was sick, he remained in the place where he was for two more days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="John 10:40–42; Luke 9:51; Acts 20:22–24"> Then after this, he said to his disciples, “Let us go to Judea again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 10:31; John 8:59; Acts 21:12–13"> The disciples replied, “Rabbi, the Jewish leaders were just now trying to stone you to death! Are you going there again?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="John 9:4; John 12:35; Jeremiah 31:9"> Jesus replied, “Are there not twelve hours in a day? If anyone walks around in the daytime, he does not stumble, because he sees the light of this world.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:10–11; Proverbs 4:18–19; Jeremiah 13:16"> But if anyone walks around at night, he stumbles, because the light is not in him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 12:2; John 11:13; John 11:43–44"> After he said this, he added, “Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep. But I am going there to awaken him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Then the disciples replied, “Lord, if he has fallen asleep, he will recover.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 9:24"> (Now Jesus had been talking about his death, but they thought he had been talking about real sleep.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 16:25; John 16:29; John 10:24"> Then Jesus told them plainly, “Lazarus has died,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; John 14:10–11; Isaiah 54:15"> and I am glad for your sake that I was not there, so that you may believe. But let us go to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 13:37; Matthew 10:3; John 21:2"> So Thomas (called Didymus ) said to his fellow disciples, “Let us go too, so that we may die with him.”</span>

## Speaking with Martha and Mary

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 11:39; Hosea 6:2; Acts 2:27–31"> When Jesus arrived, he found that Lazarus had been in the tomb four days already.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Revelation 14:20; Revelation 21:16; John 6:19"> (Now Bethany was less than two miles from Jerusalem,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Job 2:11; John 11:31; 1 Thessalonians 5:11"> so many of the Jewish people of the region had come to Martha and Mary to console them over the loss of their brother.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 10:38–42; 1 Thessalonians 4:17; Matthew 25:1"> So when Martha heard that Jesus was coming, she went out to meet him, but Mary was sitting in the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 11:32; 1 Kings 17:18; Matthew 9:18"> Martha said to Jesus, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:31; John 11:41–42; Matthew 28:18"> But even now I know that whatever you ask from God, God will grant you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 11:43–44"> Jesus replied, “Your brother will come back to life again.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 12:2–3; Acts 24:15; John 5:28–29"> Martha said, “I know that he will come back to life again in the resurrection at the last day.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 4:14; John 3:36; 1 John 5:11–12"> Jesus said to her, “I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me will live even if he dies,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 10:28; John 5:24; Romans 8:13"> and the one who lives and believes in me will never die. Do you believe this?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 16:16; John 6:69; John 6:14"> She replied, “Yes, Lord, I believe that you are the Christ, the Son of God who comes into the world.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 11:20; John 13:13; Mark 10:49"> And when she had said this, Martha went and called her sister Mary, saying privately, “The Teacher is here and is asking for you.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Proverbs 15:23; Psalms 27:8; Song of Solomon 3:1–4"> So when Mary heard this, she got up quickly and went to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 11:20"> (Now Jesus had not yet entered the village, but was still in the place where Martha had come out to meet him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 11:19; Genesis 37:35; 2 Samuel 12:16–18"> Then the people who were with Mary in the house consoling her saw her get up quickly and go out. They followed her, because they thought she was going to the tomb to weep there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 11:21; Luke 17:16; Luke 5:8"> Now when Mary came to the place where Jesus was and saw him, she fell at his feet and said to him, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="John 11:38; John 12:27; Mark 3:5"> When Jesus saw her weeping, and the people who had come with her weeping, he was intensely moved in spirit and greatly distressed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Mark 15:47; Matthew 28:6; John 1:39"> He asked, “Where have you laid him?” They replied, “Lord, come and see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Luke 19:41; Hebrews 4:15; John 11:33"> Jesus wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="John 11:3; 1 John 3:1; 1 John 4:9–10"> Thus the people who had come to mourn said, “Look how much he loved him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="John 9:6–7; John 11:32; Luke 23:39"> But some of them said, “This is the man who caused the blind man to see! Couldn’t he have done something to keep Lazarus from dying?”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Lazarus Raised from the Dead

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="John 11:33; Luke 24:2; Matthew 27:60"> Jesus, intensely moved again, came to the tomb. (Now it was a cave, and a stone was placed across it.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 11:17; Mark 16:3; Psalms 49:7"> Jesus said, “Take away the stone.” Martha, the sister of the deceased, replied, “Lord, by this time the body will have a bad smell, because he has been buried four days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="John 11:4; John 11:23–26; Romans 4:17–25"> Jesus responded, “Didn’t I tell you that if you believe, you would see the glory of God?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Matthew 11:25; John 17:1; Psalms 123:1"> So they took away the stone. Jesus looked upward and said, “Father, I thank you that you have listened to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="John 3:17; John 17:21; 1 John 4:14"> I knew that you always listen to me, but I said this for the sake of the crowd standing around here, that they may believe that you sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Kings 4:33–36; Acts 9:40; Acts 9:34"> When he had said this, he shouted in a loud voice, “Lazarus, come out!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="John 5:25; John 20:7; John 11:25–26"> The one who had died came out, his feet and hands tied up with strips of cloth, and a cloth wrapped around his face. Jesus said to them, “Unwrap him and let him go.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## The Response of the Jewish Leaders

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="John 2:23; John 11:19; John 10:41"> Then many of the people, who had come with Mary and had seen the things Jesus did, believed in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="John 9:13; Acts 5:25; John 12:37"> But some of them went to the Pharisees and reported to them what Jesus had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 12:19; Acts 4:5–6"> So the chief priests and the Pharisees called the council together and said, “What are we doing? For this man is performing many miraculous signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:15–16; Matthew 23:35–38; Matthew 27:25"> If we allow him to go on in this way, everyone will believe in him, and the Romans will come and take away our sanctuary and our nation.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Matthew 26:3; John 11:51; John 18:13–14"> Then one of them, Caiaphas, who was high priest that year, said, “You know nothing at all!</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="John 18:14; Luke 24:46; Romans 3:8"> You do not realize that it is more to your advantage to have one man die for the people than for the whole nation to perish.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Daniel 9:26; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Exodus 28:30"> (Now he did not say this on his own, but because he was high priest that year, he prophesied that Jesus was going to die for the Jewish nation,</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="John 10:16; Isaiah 49:6; 1 John 2:2"> and not for the Jewish nation only, but to gather together into one the children of God who are scattered.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Acts 5:33; Acts 9:23; Mark 14:1"> So from that day they planned together to kill him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="John 7:1; John 7:4; 2 Chronicles 13:19"> Thus Jesus no longer went around publicly among the Judeans, but went away from there to the region near the wilderness, to a town called Ephraim, and stayed there with his disciples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="2 Chronicles 30:17–20; Hebrews 9:13–14; John 6:4"> Now the Jewish feast of Passover was near, and many people went up to Jerusalem from the rural areas before the Passover to cleanse themselves ritually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="John 7:11; John 11:7–8"> Thus they were looking for Jesus, and saying to one another as they stood in the temple courts, “What do you think? That he won’t come to the feast?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57" data-xref="John 9:22; John 8:59; John 5:16–18"> (Now the chief priests and the Pharisees had given orders that anyone who knew where Jesus was should report it, so that they could arrest him.)</span>
</div>
