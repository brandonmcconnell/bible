<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:19; Jeremiah 1:5–7; Ephesians 2:10"> You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you to go and bear fruit, fruit that remains, so that whatever you ask the Father in my name he will give you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 15:12; 1 John 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:17"> This I command you – to love one another.</span>

## The World’s Hatred

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:13; John 7:7; Mark 13:13"> “If the world hates you, be aware that it hated me first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 John 4:4–5; John 17:14–16; Ephesians 1:4–11"> If you belonged to the world, the world would love you as its own. However, because you do not belong to the world, but I chose you out of the world, for this reason the world hates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 3:12; John 13:16; Matthew 10:24"> Remember what I told you, ‘A slave is not greater than his master.’ If they persecuted me, they will also persecute you. If they obeyed my word, they will obey yours too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Matthew 10:22; John 16:3; Matthew 10:18"> But they will do all these things to you on account of my name, because they do not know the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 9:41; James 4:17; Acts 17:30"> If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not be guilty of sin. But they no longer have any excuse for their sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 8:40–42; 2 John 1:9; 1 John 2:23"> The one who hates me hates my Father too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 5:36; John 14:9; John 7:31"> If I had not performed among them the miraculous deeds that no one else did, they would not be guilty of sin. But now they have seen the deeds and have hated both me and my Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Psalms 109:3; Psalms 69:4; Psalms 35:19"> Now this happened to fulfill the word that is written in their law, ‘They hated me without reason.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 14:26; John 14:16–17; John 16:13–15"> When the Advocate comes, whom I will send you from the Father – the Spirit of truth who goes out from the Father – he will testify about me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 John 4:14; John 21:24; Acts 10:39–42"> and you also will testify, because you have been with me from the beginning.</span>
