## Teaching in the Temple

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 7:28; Luke 19:47; John 7:37"> When the feast was half over, Jesus went up to the temple courts and began to teach.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 4:22; Matthew 7:28–29; Matthew 13:54"> Then the Jewish leaders were astonished and said, “How does this man know so much when he has never had formal instruction?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:10; John 14:24; John 17:8"> So Jesus replied, “My teaching is not from me, but from the one who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 8:31–32; John 8:43; Jeremiah 31:33–34"> If anyone wants to do God’s will, he will know about my teaching, whether it is from God or whether I speak from my own authority.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 5:41; 1 Corinthians 10:31–33; John 13:31–32"> The person who speaks on his own authority desires to receive honor for himself; the one who desires the honor of the one who sent him is a man of integrity, and there is no unrighteousness in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 1:17; John 11:53; Matthew 12:14"> Hasn’t Moses given you the law? Yet not one of you keeps the law! Why do you want to kill me?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 10:20; John 8:48; John 8:52"> The crowd answered, “You’re possessed by a demon! Who is trying to kill you?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 7:23; John 5:2–11"> Jesus replied, “I performed one miracle and you are all amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Genesis 17:10–14; Romans 4:9–11"> However, because Moses gave you the practice of circumcision (not that it came from Moses, but from the forefathers), you circumcise a male child on the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 12:5; John 5:14–16; Matthew 12:2"> But if a male child is circumcised on the Sabbath so that the law of Moses is not broken, why are you angry with me because I made a man completely well on the Sabbath?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 11:3–4; James 2:9; John 8:15"> Do not judge according to external appearance, but judge with proper judgment.”</span>
</div>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">

<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:7–8; John 7:48; John 12:42"> Yet here he is, speaking publicly, and they are saying nothing to him. Do the rulers really know that this man is the Christ?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Micah 5:2; John 7:41–42; John 6:42"> But we know where this man comes from. Whenever the Christ comes, no one will know where he comes from.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 8:42; John 8:19; John 8:26"> Then Jesus, while teaching in the temple courts, cried out, “You both know me and know where I come from! And I have not come on my own initiative, but the one who sent me is true. You do not know him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 8:55; Matthew 11:27; John 17:18"> but I know him, because I have come from him and he sent me.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 8:20; John 10:39; John 7:6"> So then they tried to seize Jesus, but no one laid a hand on him, because his time had not yet come.</span>
