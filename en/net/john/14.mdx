<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 4:12; John 10:9; Ephesians 2:18"> Jesus replied, “I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; John 8:19; Luke 10:22"> If you have known me, you will know my Father too. And from now on you do know him and have seen him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 33:18–23; Revelation 22:3–5; John 1:43–46"> Philip said, “Lord, show us the Father, and we will be content.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Colossians 1:15; John 12:45; John 14:7"> Jesus replied, “Have I been with you for so long, and you have not known me, Philip? The person who has seen me has seen the Father! How can you say, ‘Show us the Father’?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 5:19; John 10:38; John 10:30"> Do you not believe that I am in the Father, and the Father is in me? The words that I say to you, I do not speak on my own initiative, but the Father residing in me performs his miraculous deeds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 10:38; John 10:25; John 5:36"> Believe me that I am in the Father, and the Father is in me, but if you do not believe me, believe because of the miraculous deeds themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Matthew 21:21; Mark 16:17; Luke 10:17–19"> I tell you the solemn truth, the person who believes in me will perform the miraculous deeds that I am doing, and will perform greater deeds than these, because I am going to the Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:7; Mark 11:24; Matthew 7:7"> And I will do whatever you ask in my name, so that the Father may be glorified in the Son.</span>

## Teaching on the Holy Spirit

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 John 2:3–5; 1 John 5:2–3; John 14:21–24"> “If you love me, you will obey my commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 14:26; John 15:26; John 16:7–15"> Then I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Advocate to be with you forever –</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:27; John 16:13; 1 John 2:27"> the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot accept, because it does not see him or know him. But you know him, because he resides with you and will be in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 14:3; Psalms 23:4; Matthew 18:20"> “I will not abandon you as orphans, I will come to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 16:16; John 7:33; Hebrews 7:25"> In a little while the world will not see me any longer, but you will see me; because I live, you will live too.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 15:5–7; Galatians 2:20; John 17:21–23"> You will know at that time that I am in my Father and you are in me and I am in you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 14:15; 1 John 5:3; 1 John 2:5"> The person who has my commandments and obeys them is the one who loves me. The one who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him and will reveal myself to him.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 1:13; Luke 6:16; Matthew 10:3"> “Lord,” Judas (not Judas Iscariot) said, “what has happened that you are going to reveal yourself to us and not to the world?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 14:21; John 14:15; Revelation 3:20–21"> Jesus replied, “If anyone loves me, he will obey my word, and my Father will love him, and we will come to him and take up residence with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 7:16; John 14:10; John 8:42"> The person who does not love me does not obey my words. And the word you hear is not mine, but the Father’s who sent me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 17:6–8; John 13:19; John 16:12"> “I have spoken these things while staying with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 15:26; John 14:16; 1 John 2:27"> But the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you everything, and will cause you to remember everything I said to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:7; John 16:33; Isaiah 41:10"> “Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you; I do not give it to you as the world does. Do not let your hearts be distressed or lacking in courage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 10:29–30; 1 Corinthians 15:24–28; 1 Corinthians 11:3"> You heard me say to you, ‘I am going away and I am coming back to you.’ If you loved me, you would be glad that I am going to the Father, because the Father is greater than I am.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="John 13:19; John 16:4–31; Matthew 24:24–25"> I have told you now before it happens, so that when it happens you may believe.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 John 5:19; 1 John 4:4; Revelation 12:9"> I will not speak with you much longer, for the ruler of this world is coming. He has no power over me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 10:18; Philippians 2:8; John 12:49"> but I am doing just what the Father commanded me, so that the world may know that I love the Father. Get up, let us go from here.”</span>
</div>
