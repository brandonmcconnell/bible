<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 7:53; John 16:32"> So the disciples went back to their homes.</span>

<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 24:3–7; Revelation 3:4; Mark 16:5–6"> And she saw two angels in white sitting where Jesus’ body had been lying, one at the head and one at the feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:15; John 2:4; 1 Samuel 1:8"> They said to her, “Woman, why are you weeping?” Mary replied, “They have taken my Lord away, and I do not know where they have put him!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 21:4; Matthew 28:9"> When she had said this, she turned around and saw Jesus standing there, but she did not know that it was Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 20:13; John 18:7; John 1:38"> Jesus said to her, “Woman, why are you weeping? <span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 10:3; John 1:38; Acts 10:3"> Jesus said to her, “Mary.” She turned and said to him in Aramaic, “Rabboni” (which means Teacher).</span>

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="John 20:26; John 14:27; John 20:21"> On the evening of that day, the first day of the week, the disciples had gathered together and locked the doors of the place because they were afraid of the Jewish leaders. <span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 16:22; John 16:20; John 20:27"> When he had said this, he showed them his hands and his side. Then the disciples rejoiced when they saw the Lord.</span>

## The Response of Thomas

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="John 11:16; John 6:66–67; Hebrews 10:25"> Now Thomas (called Didymus), one of the twelve, was not with them when Jesus came.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="John 6:30; Mark 16:11; Hebrews 3:12"> The other disciples told him, “We have seen the Lord!” But he replied, “Unless I see the wounds from the nails in his hands, and put my finger into the wounds from the nails, and put my hand into his side, I will never believe it!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="John 20:19; Isaiah 54:10; John 20:21"> Eight days later the disciples were again together in the house, and Thomas was with them. Although the doors were locked, Jesus came and stood among them and said, “Peace be with you!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="John 20:25; 1 John 1:1–2; John 20:20"> Then he said to Thomas, “Put your finger here, and examine my hands. Extend your hand and put it into my side. Do not continue in your unbelief, but believe.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="John 1:1; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 7:14"> Thomas replied to him, “My Lord and my God!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Peter 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:7; Hebrews 11:1"> Jesus said to him, “Have you believed because you have seen me? Blessed are the people who have not seen and yet have believed.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="John 21:25; 2 Timothy 3:15–17; Romans 15:4"> Now Jesus performed many other miraculous signs in the presence of the disciples, which are not recorded in this book.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 10:43; John 3:15–16; 1 John 5:1"> But these are recorded so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name.</span>
