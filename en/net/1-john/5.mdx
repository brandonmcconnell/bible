<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 6:23; 1 John 2:25; John 3:36"> And this is the testimony: God has given us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.</span>

## Assurance of Eternal Life

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 20:31; John 1:12; 2 Peter 1:10–11"> I have written these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God so that you may know that you have eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 14:13; Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:12–13"> And this is the confidence that we have before him: that whenever we ask anything according to his will, he hears us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 11:9–10; Mark 11:24; Proverbs 15:29"> And if we know that he hears us in regard to whatever we ask, then we know that we have the requests that we have asked from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:4–6; 1 Samuel 2:25; Jeremiah 11:14"> If anyone sees his fellow Christian committing a sin not resulting in death, he should ask, and God will grant life to the person who commits a sin not resulting in death. There is a sin resulting in death. I do not say that he should ask about that.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 John 3:4; 1 John 5:16; James 4:7–10"> All unrighteousness is sin, but there is sin not resulting in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 John 3:9; 1 John 5:4; 1 John 5:1"> We know that everyone fathered by God does not sin, but God protects the one he has fathered, and the evil one cannot touch him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:4; 1 John 4:4–6; Ephesians 2:2"> We know that we are from God, and the whole world lies in the power of the evil one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 17:3; Luke 24:45; John 14:9"> And we know that the Son of God has come and has given us insight to know him who is true, and we are in him who is true, in his Son Jesus Christ. This one is the true God and eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:14; 1 Corinthians 10:7; Exodus 20:3–4"> Little children, guard yourselves from idols.</span>
