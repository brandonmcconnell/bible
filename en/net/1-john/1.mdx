## The Prologue to the Letter

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 4:20; John 20:27; 1 John 4:14"> This is what we proclaim to you: what was from the beginning, what we have heard, what we have seen with our eyes, what we have looked at and our hands have touched (concerning the word of life –</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 1:4; 1 John 5:20; 1 Timothy 3:16"> and the life was revealed, and we have seen and testify and announce to you the eternal life that was with the Father and was revealed to us).</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:9; John 17:3; John 17:21"> What we have seen and heard we announce to you too, so that you may have fellowship with us (and indeed our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ).</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 15:11; John 16:24; 2 John 1:12"> Thus we are writing these things so that our joy may be complete.</span>
</div>
</div>

## God Is Light, So We Must Walk in the Light

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="John 8:12; James 1:17; Isaiah 60:19"> Now this is the gospel message we have heard from him and announce to you: God is light, and in him there is no darkness at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 John 4:20; 1 John 2:4; John 3:19–21"> If we say we have fellowship with him and yet keep on walking in the darkness, we are lying and not practicing the truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 5:8; Romans 13:12; Revelation 1:5"> But if we walk in the light as he himself is in the light, we have fellowship with one another and the blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us from all sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 3:23; Ecclesiastes 7:20; James 3:2"> If we say we do not bear the guilt of sin, we are deceiving ourselves and the truth is not in us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 32:5; Proverbs 28:13; Jeremiah 33:8"> But if we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous, forgiving us our sins and cleansing us from all unrighteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 1:8; 1 John 5:10; 1 John 2:4"> If we say we have not sinned, we make him a liar and his word is not in us.</span>
</div>
</div>
