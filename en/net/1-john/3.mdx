<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Philippians 3:21; Colossians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 13:12"> Dear friends, we are God’s children now, and what we will be has not yet been revealed. We know that whenever it is revealed we will be like him, because we will see him just as he is.</span>

<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 9:28; 1 Peter 3:18"> And you know that Jesus was revealed to take away sins, and in him there is no sin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="3 John 1:11; 1 John 2:4; 1 John 3:9"> Everyone who resides in him does not sin; everyone who sins has neither seen him nor known him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 John 2:29; 1 Peter 1:15–16; Romans 2:13"> Little children, let no one deceive you: The one who practices righteousness is righteous, just as Jesus is righteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 2:14; John 8:44; Colossians 2:15"> The one who practices sin is of the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. For this purpose the Son of God was revealed: to destroy the works of the devil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 John 5:18; 1 Peter 1:23; John 1:13"> Everyone who has been fathered by God does not practice sin, because God’s seed resides in him, and thus he is not able to sin, because he has been fathered by God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 4:8; 3 John 1:11; Luke 6:35"> By this the children of God and the children of the devil are revealed: Everyone who does not practice righteousness – the one who does not love his fellow Christian – is not of God.</span>

## God Is Love, So We Must Love One Another

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="John 15:12; 2 John 1:5; 1 John 4:7"> For this is the gospel message that you have heard from the beginning: that we should love one another,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Hebrews 11:4; Genesis 4:4–15; Proverbs 29:27"> not like Cain who was of the evil one and brutally murdered his brother. And why did he murder him? Because his deeds were evil, but his brother’s were righteous.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 15:18–19; John 17:14; Luke 21:17"> Therefore do not be surprised, brothers and sisters, if the world hates you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 13:35; 1 John 5:2; 1 John 4:20–21"> We know that we have crossed over from death to life because we love our fellow Christians. The one who does not love remains in death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 5:21–22; Proverbs 26:24–26; Revelation 21:8"> Everyone who hates his fellow Christian is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life residing in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 John 4:9–11; Ephesians 5:2; John 13:34"> We have come to know love by this: that Jesus laid down his life for us; thus we ought to lay down our lives for our fellow Christians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; Isaiah 58:7–10; Deuteronomy 15:7–11"> But whoever has the world’s possessions and sees his fellow Christian in need and shuts off his compassion against him, how can the love of God reside in such a person?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="James 2:15–16; Romans 12:9; 1 Peter 1:22"> Little children, let us not love with word or with tongue but in deed and truth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 10:22; 1 John 3:21; Hebrews 6:10–11"> And by this we will know that we are of the truth and will convince our conscience in his presence,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 139:1–4; Jeremiah 17:10; Hebrews 4:13"> that if our conscience condemns us, that God is greater than our conscience and knows all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:28; Hebrews 4:16; 2 Corinthians 1:12"> Dear friends, if our conscience does not condemn us, we have confidence in the presence of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="John 15:7; John 9:31; 1 John 5:14"> and whatever we ask we receive from him, because we keep his commandments and do the things that are pleasing to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="John 6:29; John 13:34; 1 Peter 1:22"> Now this is his commandment: that we believe in the name of his Son Jesus Christ and love one another, just as he gave us the commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 John 4:15–16; 1 John 4:12–13"> And the person who keeps his commandments resides in God, and God in him. Now by this we know that God resides in us: by the Spirit he has given us.</span>
</div>
