## Daniel Finds Favor in Babylon

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:5–7; 2 Kings 24:1–2; 2 Kings 24:13"> In the third year of the reign of King Jehoiakim of Judah, King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon advanced against Jerusalem and laid it under siege.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:7; Zechariah 5:11; Jeremiah 27:19–20"> Now the Lord delivered King Jehoiakim of Judah into his power, along with some of the vessels of the temple of God. He brought them to the land of Babylonia to the temple of his god and put the vessels in the treasury of his god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 39:7; 2 Kings 20:17–18; 2 Kings 24:15"> The king commanded Ashpenaz, who was in charge of his court officials, to choose some of the Israelites who were of royal and noble descent –</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 5:11; Acts 7:22; Proverbs 22:29"> young men in whom there was no physical defect and who were handsome, well versed in all kinds of wisdom, well educated and having keen insight, and who were capable of entering the king’s royal service – and to teach them the literature and language of the Babylonians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 1:19; 1 Kings 10:8; Daniel 1:8"> So the king assigned them a daily ration from his royal delicacies and from the wine he himself drank. They were to be trained for the next three years. At the end of that time they were to enter the king’s service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:14; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 2:17"> As it turned out, among these young men were some from Judah: Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 5:12; Daniel 4:8; Daniel 2:49"> But the overseer of the court officials renamed them. He gave Daniel the name Belteshazzar, Hananiah he named Shadrach, Mishael he named Meshach, and Azariah he named Abednego.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 141:4; Ezekiel 4:13–14; Leviticus 11:45–47"> But Daniel made up his mind that he would not defile himself with the royal delicacies or the royal wine. He therefore asked the overseer of the court officials for permission not to defile himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 39:21; Proverbs 16:7; Psalms 106:46"> Then God made the overseer of the court officials sympathetic to Daniel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 6:16–18; John 12:42–43; Proverbs 29:25"> But he responded to Daniel, “I fear my master the king. He is the one who has decided your food and drink. What would happen if he saw that you looked malnourished in comparison to the other young men your age? If that happened, you would endanger my life with the king!”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Daniel then spoke to the warden whom the overseer of the court officials had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 14:2; Daniel 1:16; Genesis 1:29–30"> “Please test your servants for ten days by providing us with some vegetables to eat and water to drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Then compare our appearance with that of the young men who are eating the royal delicacies; deal with us in light of what you see.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So the warden agreed to their proposal and tested them for ten days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 23:25; Proverbs 10:22; 2 Kings 4:42–44"> At the end of the ten days their appearance was better and their bodies were healthier than all the young men who had been eating the royal delicacies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 1:12"> So the warden removed the delicacies and the wine from their diet and gave them a diet of vegetables instead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 3:12; James 1:5; Daniel 2:21"> Now as for these four young men, God endowed them with knowledge and skill in all sorts of literature and wisdom – and Daniel had insight into all kinds of visions and dreams.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 1:5"> When the time appointed by the king arrived, the overseer of the court officials brought them into Nebuchadnezzar’s presence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 22:29; Genesis 41:46; Daniel 1:5"> When the king spoke with them, he did not find among the entire group anyone like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, or Azariah. So they entered the king’s service.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 8:19; Isaiah 47:12–14; 1 Kings 4:29–34"> In every matter of wisdom and insight the king asked them about, he found them to be ten times better than any of the magicians and astrologers that were in his entire empire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 6:28; Daniel 10:1"> Now Daniel lived on until the first year of Cyrus the king.</span>
</div>
