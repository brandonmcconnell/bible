<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 9:1; Daniel 5:31; Daniel 10:18"> And in the first year of Darius the Mede, I stood to strengthen him and to provide protection for him.)</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezra 4:5–6; Daniel 8:26; Revelation 21:5"> Now I will tell you the truth. The Angel Gives a Message to Daniel “Three more kings will arise for Persia. Then a fourth king will be unusually rich, more so than all who preceded him. When he has amassed power through his riches, he will stir up everyone against the kingdom of Greece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Daniel 11:16; Daniel 11:36; Daniel 8:21"> Then a powerful king will arise, exercising great authority and doing as he pleases.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 8:22; Daniel 8:8; Jeremiah 12:17"> Shortly after his rise to power, his kingdom will be broken up and distributed toward the four winds of the sky – but not to his posterity or with the authority he exercised, for his kingdom will be uprooted and distributed to others besides these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 11:14; Daniel 11:11; Daniel 11:25"> “Then the king of the south and one of his subordinates will grow strong. His subordinate will resist him and will rule a kingdom greater than his.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 11:13; Daniel 11:40; Daniel 11:7"> After some years have passed, they will form an alliance. Then the daughter of the king of the south will come to the king of the north to make an agreement, but she will not retain her power, nor will he continue in his strength. She, together with the one who brought her, her child, and her benefactor will all be delivered over at that time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 11:38–39; Daniel 11:19–20; Isaiah 9:14"> “There will arise in his place one from her family line who will come against their army and will enter the stronghold of the king of the north and will move against them successfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 37:19; Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 43:12–13"> He will also take their gods into captivity to Egypt, along with their cast images and prized utensils of silver and gold. Then he will withdraw for some years from the king of the north.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then the king of the north will advance against the empire of the king of the south, but will withdraw to his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:42; Daniel 11:7; Daniel 11:26"> His sons will wage war, mustering a large army which will advance like an overflowing river and carrying the battle all the way to the enemy’s fortress.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 8:7; Daniel 11:5; Psalms 33:16–17"> “Then the king of the south will be enraged and will march out to fight against the king of the north, who will also muster a large army, but that army will be delivered into his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 12:22–23; 2 Kings 14:10; Isaiah 10:7–12"> When the army is taken away, the king of the south will become arrogant. He will be responsible for the death of thousands and thousands of people, but he will not continue to prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 4:16; Daniel 12:7; Daniel 11:6–7"> For the king of the north will again muster an army, one larger than before. At the end of some years he will advance with a huge army and enormous supplies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 17:17; Acts 4:25–28"> “In those times many will oppose the king of the south. Those who are violent among your own people will rise up in confirmation of the vision, but they will falter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 6:6; Ezekiel 17:17; Ezekiel 4:2"> Then the king of the north will advance and will build siege mounds and capture a well-fortified city. The forces of the south will not prevail, not even his finest contingents. They will have no strength to prevail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 8:9; Daniel 11:36; Daniel 11:3"> The one advancing against him will do as he pleases, and no one will be able to stand before him. He will prevail in the beautiful land, and its annihilation will be within his power.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 12:17; Ezekiel 4:3; Ezekiel 4:7"> His intention will be to come with the strength of his entire kingdom, and he will form alliances. He will give the king of the south a daughter in marriage in order to destroy the kingdom, but it will not turn out to his advantage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hosea 12:14; Jeremiah 31:10; Zephaniah 2:11"> Then he will turn his attention to the coastal regions and will capture many of them. But a commander will bring his shameful conduct to a halt; in addition, he will make him pay for his shameful conduct.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 26:21; Psalms 37:36; Job 20:8"> He will then turn his attention to the fortresses of his own land, but he will stumble and fall, not to be found again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 60:17; Daniel 11:7; Deuteronomy 15:2–3"> There will arise after him one who will send out an exactor of tribute to enhance the splendor of the kingdom, but after a few days he will be destroyed, though not in anger or battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 11:34; Daniel 8:25; Judges 9:1–20"> “Then there will arise in his place a despicable person to whom the royal honor has not been rightfully conferred. He will come on the scene in a time of prosperity and will seize the kingdom through deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 8:10–11; Daniel 11:10; Daniel 9:26"> Armies will be suddenly swept away in defeat before him; both they and a covenant leader will be destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 8:25; 2 Thessalonians 2:9; Psalms 52:2"> After entering into an alliance with him, he will behave treacherously; he will ascend to power with only a small force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:25; Proverbs 23:7; Matthew 9:4"> In a time of prosperity for the most productive areas of the province he will come and accomplish what neither his fathers nor their fathers accomplished. He will distribute loot, spoils, and property to his followers, and he will devise plans against fortified cities, but not for long.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Proverbs 15:18; Daniel 11:2; Proverbs 28:25"> He will rouse his strength and enthusiasm against the king of the south with a large army. The king of the south will wage war with a large and very powerful army, but he will not be able to prevail because of the plans devised against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 11:10; Daniel 11:40; 2 Samuel 4:2–12"> Those who share the king’s fine food will attempt to destroy him, and his army will be swept away; many will be killed in battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 12:2; Jeremiah 9:3–5; Habakkuk 2:3"> These two kings, their minds filled with evil intentions, will trade lies with one another at the same table. But it will not succeed, for there is still an end at the appointed time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Daniel 11:30–32; Daniel 8:24; Acts 3:25"> Then the king of the north will return to his own land with much property. His mind will be set against the holy covenant. He will take action, and then return to his own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 17:26; Isaiah 14:31; Daniel 11:25"> At an appointed time he will again invade the south, but this latter visit will not turn out the way the former one did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:24; Jeremiah 2:10"> The ships of Kittim will come against him, leaving him disheartened. He will turn back and direct his indignation against the holy covenant. He will return and honor those who forsake the holy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Daniel 12:11; Daniel 9:27; Matthew 24:15"> His forces will rise up and profane the fortified sanctuary, stopping the daily sacrifice. In its place they will set up the abomination that causes desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:9; Psalms 9:10; John 17:3"> Then with smooth words he will defile those who have rejected the covenant. But the people who are loyal to their God will act valiantly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Matthew 24:9; John 16:2; Malachi 2:7"> These who are wise among the people will teach the masses. However, they will fall by the sword and by the flame, and they will be imprisoned and plundered for some time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 7:15; Romans 16:18; Acts 20:29–30"> When they stumble, they will be granted some help. But many will unite with them deceitfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Malachi 3:2–4; Revelation 2:10"> Even some of the wise will stumble, resulting in their refinement, purification, and cleansing until the time of the end, for it is still for the appointed time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Revelation 13:5–6; 2 Thessalonians 2:4; Deuteronomy 10:17"> “Then the king will do as he pleases. He will exalt and magnify himself above every deity and he will utter presumptuous things against the God of gods. He will succeed until the time of wrath is completed, for what has been decreed must occur.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Isaiah 14:13; 1 Timothy 4:3"> He will not respect the gods of his fathers – not even the god loved by women. He will not respect any god; he will elevate himself above them all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Revelation 17:1–5; Isaiah 44:9; Revelation 13:12–17"> What he will honor is a god of fortresses – a god his fathers did not acknowledge he will honor with gold, silver, valuable stones, and treasured commodities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Revelation 18:9–13"> He will attack mighty fortresses, aided by a foreign deity. To those who recognize him he will grant considerable honor. He will place them in authority over many people, and he will parcel out land for a price.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Daniel 11:35; Daniel 11:10; Isaiah 5:28"> “At the time of the end the king of the south will attack him. Then the king of the north will storm against him with chariots, horsemen, and a large armada of ships. He will invade lands, passing through them like an overflowing river.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Jeremiah 48:47; Jeremiah 49:6; Isaiah 11:13–15"> Then he will enter the beautiful land. Many will fall, but these will escape: Edom, Moab, and the Ammonite leadership.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Zechariah 10:10–11; Ezekiel 29:14; Revelation 11:8"> He will extend his power against other lands; the land of Egypt will not escape.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:3; Nahum 3:9; Ezekiel 30:4–5"> He will have control over the hidden stores of gold and silver, as well as all the treasures of Egypt. Libyans and Ethiopians will submit to him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Revelation 19:19–21; Revelation 16:12; Revelation 17:13"> But reports will trouble him from the east and north, and he will set out in a tremendous rage to destroy and wipe out many.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:4; Daniel 8:25; Revelation 19:19–21"> He will pitch his royal tents between the seas toward the beautiful holy mountain. But he will come to his end, with no one to help him.</span>
