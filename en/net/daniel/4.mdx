## Nebuchadnezzar Dreams of a Tree Chopped Down

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 47:7–8; 1 Thessalonians 5:2–3; Psalms 30:6–7"> I, Nebuchadnezzar, was relaxing in my home, living luxuriously in my palace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 2:1; Job 7:13–14; Daniel 2:28–29"> I saw a dream that frightened me badly. The things I imagined while lying on my bed – these visions of my mind – were terrifying me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 2:2; Genesis 41:7–8; Isaiah 8:19"> So I issued an order for all the wise men of Babylon to be brought before me so that they could make known to me the interpretation of the dream.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 44:25; Daniel 2:7; Genesis 41:8"> When the magicians, astrologers, wise men, and diviners entered, I recounted the dream for them. But they were unable to make known its interpretation to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 1:7; Daniel 5:14; Daniel 4:18"> Later Daniel entered (whose name is Belteshazzar after the name of my god, and in whom there is a spirit of the holy gods). I recounted the dream for him as well,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 2:48; Daniel 5:11; Daniel 4:8"> saying, “Belteshazzar, chief of the magicians, in whom I know there to be a spirit of the holy gods and whom no mystery baffles, consider my dream that I saw and set forth its interpretation!</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Jeremiah 12:2; Daniel 4:5; Psalms 37:35–36"> Here are the visions of my mind while I was on my bed.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">While I was watching,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there was a tree in the middle of the land.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It was enormously tall.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 4:21–22; Deuteronomy 9:1; Matthew 11:23"> <span className="pl pl-1">The tree grew large and strong.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Its top reached far into the sky;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">it could be seen from the borders of all the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 13:19; Lamentations 4:20; Ezekiel 31:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">Its foliage was attractive and its fruit plentiful;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on it there was food enough for all.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Under it the wild animals used to seek shade,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and in its branches the birds of the sky used to nest.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All creatures used to feed themselves from it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Daniel 8:13; Daniel 7:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">While I was watching in my mind’s visions on my bed,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">a holy sentinel came down from heaven.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 3:10; Daniel 4:23; Ezekiel 31:12–13"> <span className="pl pl-1">He called out loudly as follows:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘Chop down the tree and lop off its branches!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Strip off its foliage</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and scatter its fruit!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let the animals flee from under it</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the birds from its branches!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Job 14:7–9; Daniel 4:25–27; Ezekiel 29:14–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">But leave its taproot in the ground,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">with a band of iron and bronze around it</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">surrounded by the grass of the field.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Let it become damp with the dew of the sky,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let it live with the animals in the grass of the land.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Daniel 4:23; Revelation 12:14; Daniel 4:25"> <span className="pl pl-1">Let his mind be altered from that of a human being,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let an animal’s mind be given to him,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and let seven periods of time go by for him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 4:25; Daniel 2:21; Psalms 75:6–7"> <span className="pl pl-1">This announcement is by the decree of the sentinels;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">this decision is by the pronouncement of the holy ones,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">so that those who are alive may understand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that the Most High has authority over human kingdoms,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and he bestows them on whomever he wishes.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He establishes over them even the lowliest of human beings.’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 5:15; Genesis 41:8; Daniel 5:8"> “This is the dream that I, King Nebuchadnezzar, saw. Now you, Belteshazzar, declare its interpretation, for none of the wise men in my kingdom are able to make known to me the interpretation. But you can do so, for a spirit of the holy gods is in you.”</span>
</div>
</div>

## Daniel Interprets Nebuchadnezzar’s Dream

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 8:27; Daniel 7:28; Daniel 4:24"> Then Daniel (whose name is also Belteshazzar) was upset for a brief time; his thoughts were alarming him. The king said, “Belteshazzar, don’t let the dream and its interpretation alarm you.” But Belteshazzar replied, “Sir, if only the dream were for your enemies and its interpretation applied to your adversaries!</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 4:10–12; Ezekiel 31:3; Ezekiel 31:16"> The tree that you saw that grew large and strong, whose top reached to the sky, and which could be seen in all the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> whose foliage was attractive and its fruit plentiful, and from which there was food available for all, under whose branches wild animals used to live, and in whose branches birds of the sky used to nest –</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 2:37–38; 2 Samuel 12:7; Jeremiah 27:6–8"> it is you, O king! For you have become great and strong. Your greatness is such that it reaches to heaven, and your authority to the ends of the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 5:21; Daniel 4:13–16"> As for the king seeing a holy sentinel coming down from heaven and saying, ‘Chop down the tree and destroy it, but leave its taproot in the ground, with a band of iron and bronze around it, surrounded by the grass of the field. Let it become damp with the dew of the sky, and let it live with the wild animals, until seven periods of time go by for him’ –</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 107:40; Job 40:11–12; Isaiah 46:10–11"> this is the interpretation, O king! It is the decision of the Most High that this has happened to my lord the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 4:17; Jeremiah 27:5; Psalms 83:18"> You will be driven from human society, and you will live with the wild animals. You will be fed grass like oxen, and you will become damp with the dew of the sky. Seven periods of time will pass by for you, before you understand that the Most High is ruler over human kingdoms and gives them to whomever he wishes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Daniel 4:15; Matthew 5:34; Matthew 21:20"> They said to leave the taproot of the tree, for your kingdom will be restored to you when you come to understand that heaven rules.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6–7; Jonah 3:9; Acts 8:22"> Therefore, O king, may my advice be pleasing to you. Break away from your sins by doing what is right, and from your iniquities by showing mercy to the poor. Perhaps your prosperity will be prolonged.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 23:19; Zechariah 1:6; Proverbs 10:24"> Now all of this happened to King Nebuchadnezzar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Revelation 2:21; Ecclesiastes 8:11; 1 Peter 3:20"> After twelve months, he happened to be walking around on the battlements of the royal palace of Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 16:18; Revelation 18:10; Luke 14:11"> The king uttered these words: “Is this not the great Babylon that I have built for a royal residence by my own mighty strength and for my majestic honor?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Acts 9:3–5; Revelation 16:7; Luke 12:20"> While these words were still on the king’s lips, a voice came down from heaven: “It is hereby announced to you, King Nebuchadnezzar, that your kingdom has been removed from you!</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:5; Daniel 5:21; Proverbs 8:15–16"> You will be driven from human society, and you will live with the wild animals. You will be fed grass like oxen, and seven periods of time will pass by for you before you understand that the Most High is ruler over human kingdoms and gives them to whomever he wishes.”</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Daniel 4:32; Daniel 4:25; Daniel 5:5"> Now in that very moment this pronouncement about Nebuchadnezzar came true. He was driven from human society, he ate grass like oxen, and his body became damp with the dew of the sky, until his hair became long like an eagle’s feathers, and his nails like a bird’s claws.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Revelation 4:10; Jeremiah 10:10; Daniel 12:7"> But at the end of the appointed time I, Nebuchadnezzar, looked up toward heaven, and my sanity returned to me.<br /><span className="pl pl-1">I extolled the Most High,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and I praised and glorified the one who lives forever.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For his authority is an everlasting authority,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and his kingdom extends from one generation to the next.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Job 42:2; Psalms 135:6; Psalms 115:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">All the inhabitants of the earth are regarded as nothing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He does as he wishes with the army of heaven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and with those who inhabit the earth.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">No one slaps his hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and says to him, ‘What have you done?’</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Daniel 4:34; Proverbs 22:4; Daniel 4:15–16"> At that time my sanity returned to me. I was restored to the honor of my kingdom, and my splendor returned to me. My ministers and my nobles were seeking me out, and I was reinstated over my kingdom. I became even greater than before.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="James 4:6–7; Psalms 33:4–5; 1 Peter 5:5–6"> Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and exalt and glorify the King of heaven, for all his deeds are right and his ways are just. He is able to bring down those who live in pride.</span>
</div>
</div>
