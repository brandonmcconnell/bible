<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 11:1; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 5:31–1"> In the first year of Darius son of Ahasuerus, who was of Median descent and who had been appointed king over the Babylonian empire –</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:10; Jeremiah 25:11–12; 2 Chronicles 36:21"> in the first year of his reign I, Daniel, came to understand from the sacred books that, according to the word of the LORD disclosed to the prophet Jeremiah, the years for the fulfilling of the desolation of Jerusalem were seventy in number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:3; Jeremiah 29:10–13; James 5:16–18"> So I turned my attention to the Lord God to implore him by prayer and requests, with fasting, sackcloth, and ashes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Romans 8:28; Exodus 20:6; Deuteronomy 5:10"> I prayed to the LORD my God, confessing in this way:<br />“O Lord, great and awesome God who is faithful to his covenant with those who love him and keep his commandments,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Malachi 3:7; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:15"> we have sinned! We have done what is wrong and wicked; we have rebelled by turning away from your commandments and standards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:10; 2 Chronicles 36:15–16; Luke 20:10–12"> We have not paid attention to your servants the prophets, who spoke by your authority to our kings, our leaders, and our ancestors, and to all the inhabitants of the land as well.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 44:15; Jeremiah 3:25; Jeremiah 2:26–27"> “You are righteous, O Lord, but we are humiliated this day – the people of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem and all Israel, both near and far away in all the countries in which you have scattered them, because they have behaved unfaithfully toward you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 9:6–7; Lamentations 1:7–8; Lamentations 1:18"> O LORD, we have been humiliated – our kings, our leaders, and our ancestors – because we have sinned against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ephesians 1:6–8; Daniel 9:7; Daniel 9:5"> Yet the Lord our God is compassionate and forgiving, even though we have rebelled against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Daniel 9:6; 2 Kings 18:12; Nehemiah 9:13–17"> We have not obeyed the LORD our God by living according to his laws that he set before us through his servants the prophets.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:5–10; Isaiah 1:4–6; Deuteronomy 28:15–68"> “All Israel has broken your law and turned away by not obeying you. Therefore you have poured out on us the judgment solemnly threatened in the law of Moses the servant of God, for we have sinned against you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:9; Lamentations 1:12; Job 12:17"> He has carried out his threats against us and our rulers who were over us by bringing great calamity on us – what has happened to Jerusalem has never been equaled under all heaven!</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Isaiah 9:13; Daniel 9:11"> Just as it is written in the law of Moses, so all this calamity has come on us. Still we have not tried to pacify the LORD our God by turning back from our sin and by seeking wisdom from your reliable moral standards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 9:7; Nehemiah 9:33; Psalms 51:14"> The LORD was mindful of the calamity, and he brought it on us. For the LORD our God is just in all he has done, and we have not obeyed him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:10; Exodus 32:11; Nehemiah 1:10"> “Now, O Lord our God, who brought your people out of the land of Egypt with great power and made a name for yourself that is remembered to this day – we have sinned and behaved wickedly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Joel 3:17; Psalms 87:1–3"> O Lord, according to all your justice, please turn your raging anger away from your city Jerusalem, your holy mountain. For due to our sins and the iniquities of our ancestors, Jerusalem and your people are mocked by all our neighbors.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Psalms 80:19; Lamentations 5:18; 2 Corinthians 1:20"> “So now, our God, accept the prayer and requests of your servant, and show favor to your devastated sanctuary for your own sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 37:17; Jeremiah 36:7; 2 Kings 19:16"> Listen attentively, my God, and hear! Open your eyes and look on our desolated ruins and the city called by your name. For it is not because of our own righteous deeds that we are praying to you, but because your compassion is abundant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 9:18; Ephesians 1:12; Amos 7:2"> O Lord, hear! O Lord, forgive! O Lord, pay attention, and act! Don’t delay, for your own sake, O my God! For your city and your people are called by your name.”</span>
</div>

## Gabriel Gives to Daniel a Prophecy of Seventy Weeks

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 145:18; Daniel 9:3–4; Romans 3:23"> While I was still speaking and praying, confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel and presenting my request before the LORD my God concerning his holy mountain –</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:19; Daniel 8:16; 1 Kings 18:36"> yes, while I was still praying, the man Gabriel, whom I had seen previously in a vision, was approaching me in my state of extreme weariness, around the time of the evening offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 9:24–27; Daniel 10:21; Daniel 8:16"> He spoke with me, instructing me as follows: “Daniel, I have now come to impart understanding to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 10:19; Matthew 24:15; Daniel 10:11–12"> At the beginning of your requests a message went out, and I have come to convey it to you, for you are of great value in God’s sight. Therefore consider the message and understand the vision:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Hebrews 9:11–14; Romans 5:10; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> <span className="pl pl-1">“Seventy weeks have been determined</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">concerning your people and your holy city</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to put an end to rebellion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to bring sin to completion,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to atone for iniquity,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to bring in perpetual righteousness,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">to seal up the prophetic vision,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and to anoint a most holy place.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Ezra 4:24; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 55:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">So know and understand:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">From the issuing of the command to restore and rebuild</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Jerusalem until an anointed one, a prince arrives,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there will be a period of seven weeks and sixty-two weeks.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will again be built, with plaza and moat,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but in distressful times.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Isaiah 53:8; Nahum 1:8; Mark 13:2"> <span className="pl pl-1">Now after the sixty-two weeks,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">an anointed one will be cut off and have nothing.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">As for the city and the sanctuary,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the people of the coming prince will destroy them.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But his end will come speedily like a flood.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Until the end of the war that has been decreed</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">there will be destruction.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 24:15; Luke 21:20; Daniel 12:11"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will confirm a covenant with many for one week.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But in the middle of that week</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he will bring sacrifices and offerings to a halt.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">On the wing of abominations will come one who destroys,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">until the decreed end is poured out on the one who destroys.”</span></span>
</div>
