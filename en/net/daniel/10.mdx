<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 6:28; Daniel 1:21; Daniel 8:26"> In the third year of King Cyrus of Persia a message was revealed to Daniel (who was also called Belteshazzar). This message was true and concerned a great war. He understood the message and gained insight by the vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:4; Daniel 9:24–27; Ezra 9:4–5"> In those days I, Daniel, was mourning for three whole weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Matthew 6:17; Daniel 11:8; 1 Corinthians 9:27"> I ate no choice food; no meat or wine came to my lips, nor did I anoint myself with oil until the end of those three weeks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 2:14; Daniel 8:2; Ezekiel 1:3"> On the twenty-fourth day of the first month I was beside the great river, the Tigris.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Revelation 1:13–15; Daniel 12:6–7; Ezekiel 9:2"> I looked up and saw a man clothed in linen; around his waist was a belt made of gold from Upaz.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 19:12; Revelation 1:13–17; Matthew 17:2"> His body resembled yellow jasper, and his face had an appearance like lightning. His eyes were like blazing torches; his arms and feet had the gleam of polished bronze. His voice thundered forth like the sound of a large crowd.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 9:7; Acts 22:9; Genesis 3:10"> Only I, Daniel, saw the vision; the men who were with me did not see it. On the contrary, they were overcome with fright and ran away to hide.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Habakkuk 3:16; Daniel 8:27"> I alone was left to see this great vision. My strength drained from me, and my vigor disappeared; I was without energy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Daniel 8:18; Job 4:13; Genesis 15:12"> I listened to his voice, and as I did so I fell into a trance-like sleep with my face to the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 1:17; Jeremiah 1:9; Daniel 8:18"> Then a hand touched me and set me on my hands and knees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 26:16; Ezekiel 2:1; Daniel 8:16–17"> He said to me, “Daniel, you are of great value. Understand the words that I am about to speak to you. So stand up, for I have now been sent to you.” When he said this to me, I stood up shaking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 10:30–31; Daniel 9:20–23; Daniel 10:19"> Then he said to me, “Don’t be afraid, Daniel, for from the very first day you applied your mind to understand and to humble yourself before your God, your words were heard. I have come in response to your words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jude 1:9; Daniel 12:1; Daniel 10:20–21"> However, the prince of the kingdom of Persia was opposing me for twenty-one days. But Michael, one of the leading princes, came to help me, because I was left there with the kings of Persia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 8:26; Daniel 2:28; Daniel 12:4"> Now I have come to help you understand what will happen to your people in the latter days, for the vision pertains to future days.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Luke 1:20; Ezekiel 24:27; Daniel 10:9"> While he was saying this to me, I was flat on the ground and unable to speak.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 6:7; Daniel 8:15; Jeremiah 1:9"> Then one who appeared to be a human being was touching my lips. I opened my mouth and started to speak, saying to the one who was standing before me, “Sir, due to the vision, anxiety has gripped me and I have no strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 6:1–5; Exodus 24:10–11; Mark 12:36"> How, sir, am I able to speak with you? My strength is gone, and I am breathless.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 10:16; Luke 22:43; Daniel 8:18"> Then the one who appeared to be a human being touched me again and strengthened me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 35:4; Joshua 1:9; Judges 6:23"> He said to me, “Don’t be afraid, you who are valued. Peace be to you! Be strong! Be really strong!” When he spoke to me, I was strengthened. I said, “Sir, you may speak now, for you have given me strength.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 8:21; Daniel 11:2–4; Daniel 8:5–8"> He said, “Do you know why I have come to you? Now I am about to return to engage in battle with the prince of Persia. When I go, the prince of Greece is coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 10:13; Daniel 12:1; Revelation 12:7"> However, I will first tell you what is written in a dependable book. (There is no one who strengthens me against these princes, except Michael your prince.</span>
