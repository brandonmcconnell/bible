<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Daniel 1:17; Amos 3:7; Daniel 5:1"> In the first year of King Belshazzar of Babylon, Daniel had a dream filled with visions while he was lying on his bed. Then he wrote down the dream in summary fashion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Revelation 7:1; Revelation 17:15"> Daniel explained: “I was watching in my vision during the night as the four winds of the sky were stirring up the great sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Daniel 7:17; Daniel 2:32–33"> Then four large beasts came up from the sea; they were different from one another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:7; Isaiah 14:13–17; Ezekiel 17:3"> “The first one was like a lion with eagles’ wings. As I watched, its wings were pulled off and it was lifted up from the ground. It was made to stand on two feet like a human being, and a human mind was given to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Daniel 2:39; Isaiah 13:17–18; Proverbs 17:12"> “Then a second beast appeared, like a bear. It was raised up on one side, and there were three ribs in its mouth between its teeth. It was told, ‘Get up and devour much flesh!’</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 13:2; Daniel 8:20–22; Daniel 8:5–8"> “After these things, as I was watching, another beast like a leopard appeared, with four bird-like wings on its back. This beast had four heads, and ruling authority was given to it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 13:1; Revelation 12:3; Revelation 17:12"> “After these things, as I was watching in the night visions a fourth beast appeared – one dreadful, terrible, and very strong. It had two large rows of iron teeth. It devoured and crushed, and anything that was left it trampled with its feet. It was different from all the beasts that came before it, and it had ten horns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 13:5–6; Revelation 13:1; Psalms 12:3"> “As I was contemplating the horns, another horn – a small one – came up between them, and three of the former horns were torn out by the roots to make room for it. This horn had eyes resembling human eyes and a mouth speaking arrogant things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Revelation 1:14; Mark 9:3; Daniel 7:13"> “While I was watching,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">thrones were set up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the Ancient of Days took his seat.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His attire was white like snow;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">the hair of his head was like lamb’s wool.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His throne was ablaze with fire</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and its wheels were all aflame.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 30:27; Deuteronomy 33:2; Revelation 20:11–15"> <span className="pl pl-1">A river of fire was streaming forth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and proceeding from his presence.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Many thousands were ministering to him;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Many tens of thousands stood ready to serve him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The court convened</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the books were opened.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 19:20; Revelation 20:10; 2 Thessalonians 2:8"> “Then I kept on watching because of the arrogant words of the horn that was speaking. I was watching until the beast was killed and its body destroyed and thrown into the flaming fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Daniel 8:7; Daniel 7:4–6"> As for the rest of the beasts, their ruling authority had already been removed, though they were permitted to go on living for a time and a season.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 26:64; Revelation 1:7; Luke 21:27"> I was watching in the night visions,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">“And with the clouds of the sky</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">one like a son of man was approaching.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He went up to the Ancient of Days</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and was escorted before him.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Revelation 11:15; Daniel 7:27; John 3:35"> <span className="pl pl-1">To him was given ruling authority, honor, and sovereignty.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">All peoples, nations, and language groups were serving him.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His authority is eternal and will not pass away.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His kingdom will not be destroyed.</span></span>

## An Angel Interprets Daniel’s Vision

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Daniel 7:28; Jeremiah 17:16; Genesis 40:7–8"> “As for me, Daniel, my spirit was distressed, and the visions of my mind were alarming me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Revelation 7:13–14; Zechariah 2:3; Revelation 5:5"> I approached one of those standing nearby and asked him about the meaning of all this. So he spoke with me and revealed to me the interpretation of the vision:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Daniel 8:19–22; Daniel 2:37–40; John 18:36"> ‘These large beasts, which are four in number, represent four kings who will arise from the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Isaiah 60:12–14; Revelation 5:10"> The holy ones of the Most High will receive the kingdom and will take possession of the kingdom forever and ever.’</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Daniel 7:7; Daniel 2:40–43"> “Then I wanted to know the meaning of the fourth beast, which was different from all the others. It was very dreadful, with two rows of iron teeth and bronze claws, and it devoured, crushed, and trampled anything that was left with its feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Daniel 7:23; Daniel 7:8; Daniel 7:11"> I also wanted to know the meaning of the ten horns on its head, and of that other horn which came up and before which three others fell. This was the horn that had eyes and a mouth speaking arrogant things, whose appearance was more formidable than the others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 17:14; Daniel 8:24"> While I was watching, that horn began to wage war against the holy ones and was defeating them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Daniel 7:18; Revelation 20:4; Revelation 5:10"> until the Ancient of Days arrived and judgment was rendered in favor of the holy ones of the Most High. Then the time came for the holy ones to take possession of the kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Daniel 2:40; Daniel 7:7; Luke 2:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">“This is what he told me:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">‘The fourth beast means that there will be a fourth kingdom on earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">that will differ from all the other kingdoms.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">It will devour all the earth</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and will trample and crush it.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Daniel 7:20; Daniel 11:36; Revelation 13:1"> <span className="pl pl-1">The ten horns</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">mean that ten kings will arise from that kingdom.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Another king will arise after them,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">but he will be different from the earlier ones.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will humiliate three kings.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Daniel 12:7; Revelation 12:14; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> <span className="pl pl-1">He will speak words against the Most High.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">He will harass the holy ones of the Most High continually.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His intention will be to change times established by law.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">They will be delivered into his hand</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For a time, times, and half a time.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Revelation 20:10–11; 2 Thessalonians 2:8; Revelation 11:13"> <span className="pl pl-1">But the court will convene, and his ruling authority will be removed –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">destroyed and abolished forever!</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 72:11; Luke 1:33; Revelation 11:15"> <span className="pl pl-1">Then the kingdom, authority,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and greatness of the kingdoms under all of heaven</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">will be delivered to the people of the holy ones of the Most High.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His kingdom is an eternal kingdom;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">all authorities will serve him and obey him.’</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Luke 2:19; Luke 2:51; Daniel 10:8"> “This is the conclusion of the matter. As for me, Daniel, my thoughts troubled me greatly, and the color drained from my face. But I kept the matter to myself.”</span>
