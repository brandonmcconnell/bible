<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:13; Zechariah 1:15; Isaiah 18:3–4"> Then he cried out to me, “Look! The ones going to the northland have brought me peace about the northland.”</span>

## A Concluding Oracle

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 8:1; Zechariah 7:1; Zechariah 1:1"> The word of the Lord came to me as follows:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezra 7:14–16; Ezra 8:26–30; Isaiah 66:20"> “Choose some people from among the exiles, namely, Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah, all of whom have come from Babylon, and when you have done so go to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 3:1; Psalms 21:3; Song of Solomon 3:11"> Then take some silver and gold to make a crown and set it on the head of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 3:8; Isaiah 4:2; Jeremiah 33:15"> Then say to him, ‘The Lord who rules over all says, “Look – here is the man whose name is Branch, who will sprout up from his place and build the temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 110:4; Isaiah 49:5–6; Hebrews 3:1"> Indeed, he will build the temple of the Lord, and he will be clothed in splendor, sitting as king on his throne. Moreover, there will be a priest with him on his throne and they will see eye to eye on everything.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 26:13; Exodus 12:14; Mark 14:9"> The crown will then be turned over to Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, and Hen son of Zephaniah as a memorial in the temple of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 60:10; Zechariah 3:7; Isaiah 58:10–14"> Then those who are far away will come and build the temple of the Lord so that you may know that the Lord who rules over all has sent me to you. This will all come to pass if you completely obey the voice of the Lord your God.”’”</span>
