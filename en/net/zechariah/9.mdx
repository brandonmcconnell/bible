<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:23; Ezekiel 28:12; Ezekiel 28:2–5"> as are those of Hamath also, which adjoins Damascus, and Tyre though they consider themselves to be very wise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:33; Job 27:16; Ezekiel 28:4–5"> Tyre built herself a fortification and piled up silver like dust and gold like the mud of the streets!</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:18; Ezekiel 26:17; Isaiah 23:1–7"> Nevertheless the Lord will evict her and shove her fortifications into the sea – she will be consumed by fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 8:26; Revelation 18:9–17; Jeremiah 47:1"> Ashkelon will see and be afraid; Gaza will be in great anguish, as will Ekron, for her hope will have been dried up. Gaza will lose her king, and Ashkelon will no longer be inhabited.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 1:8; Zephaniah 2:10; 1 Peter 5:5"> A mongrel people will live in Ashdod, for I will greatly humiliate the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Amos 3:12; Ezekiel 16:57–61; Isaiah 60:14–16"> I will take away their abominable religious practices; then those who survive will become a community of believers in our God, like a clan in Judah, and Ekron will be like the Jebusites.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 54:14; Isaiah 60:18; Isaiah 52:1"> Then I will surround my temple to protect it like a guard from anyone crossing back and forth; so no one will cross over against them anymore as an oppressor, for now I myself have seen it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 21:4–7; Zephaniah 3:14–15; John 12:13–16"> <span className="pl pl-1">Rejoice greatly, daughter of Zion!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Shout, daughter of Jerusalem!</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Look! Your king is coming to you:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">he is legitimate and victorious,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">humble and riding on a donkey –</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">on a young donkey, the foal of a female donkey.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 1:7; 2 Corinthians 10:4–5; Hosea 2:18"> <span className="pl pl-1">I will remove the chariot from Ephraim</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the warhorse from Jerusalem,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and the battle bow will be removed.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Then he will announce peace to the nations.</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">His dominion will be from sea to sea</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">and from the Euphrates River to the ends of the earth.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:14; Exodus 24:8; Psalms 102:19–21"> Moreover, as for you, because of our covenant relationship secured with blood, I will release your prisoners from the waterless pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Job 42:10; Isaiah 61:7; Lamentations 3:21–22"> Return to the stronghold, you prisoners, with hope; today I declare that I will return double what was taken from you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Revelation 17:14; Zechariah 10:3–7; Isaiah 49:2"> I will bend Judah as my bow; I will load the bow with Ephraim, my arrow! I will stir up your sons, Zion, against yours, Greece, and I will make you, Zion, like a warrior’s sword.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Psalms 18:14; Isaiah 66:15; Psalms 144:5–6"> Then the Lord will appear above them, and his arrow will shoot forth like lightning; the Lord God will blow the trumpet and will sally forth on the southern storm winds.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 12:6; Zechariah 10:7; Zechariah 10:5"> The Lord who rules over all will guard them, and they will prevail and overcome with sling stones. Then they will drink, and will become noisy like drunkards, full like the sacrificial basin or like the corners of the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 62:3; Luke 12:32; Haggai 2:23"> On that day the Lord their God will deliver them as the flock of his people, for they are the precious stones of a crown sparkling over his land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 33:17; Isaiah 62:8–9; 1 John 4:8–11"> How precious and fair! Grain will make the young men flourish and new wine the young women.</span>
