## Vision Seven: The Ephah

<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 1:9; Zechariah 1:14; Zechariah 2:3"> After this the angelic messenger who had been speaking to me went out and said, “Look, see what is leaving.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Amos 8:5; Ezekiel 44:10–11"> I asked, “What is it?” And he replied, “It is a basket for measuring grain that is moving away from here.” Moreover, he said, “This is their ‘eye’ throughout all the earth.”</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:1–49; Revelation 17:1–18; Jeremiah 3:1–2"> Then a round lead cover was raised up, revealing a woman sitting inside the basket.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 15:16; Amos 9:1–4; Proverbs 5:22"> He then said, “This woman represents wickedness,” and he pushed her down into the basket and placed the lead cover on top.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:49; Hosea 8:1; Daniel 9:26–27"> Then I looked again and saw two women going forth with the wind in their wings (they had wings like those of a stork) and they lifted up the basket between the earth and the sky.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> I asked the messenger who was speaking to me, “Where are they taking the basket?”</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 11:2; Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2"> He replied, “To build a temple for her in the land of Babylonia. When it is finished, she will be placed there in her own residence.”</span>
